### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A certification symbol placed on products by the British Standards Institute, based on the letters B, S, and V.

   **Usage:** UK

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any similar certification or, in colloquial terms, as a benchmark intended to indicate safety or good quality.
