### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To detach (an artillery piece) from its limber to deploy for firing.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To clumsily put into employ (a large weapon or object).

   **Usage:** broadly; transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To unsling (something, as a backpack, carried on the body with a strap); to bring (something carried) into the hands for use.

   **Usage:** broadly; transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To stretch (limbs, muscles, etc., that have been cramped or unused for some time).

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive
