### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Barely able; having difficulty doing something.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; usually

   **Example:** Although they are still available, I think we would be hard-pressed to find one on short notice.

   **Source context (verbatim):** with to-infinitive

   **Source synonyms:** hard pushed; hard put

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Experiencing financial difficulty or difficulty in surviving.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** The earthquake left the residents hard-pressed.

   **Source synonyms:** in dire straits
