### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A mental disorder (in some classifications of such disorders, including ICD-11) characterized by a persistent preoccupation with having or developing a serious illness, despite the absence of significant physical symptoms or evidence of disease.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** illness anxiety disorder; hypochondria
