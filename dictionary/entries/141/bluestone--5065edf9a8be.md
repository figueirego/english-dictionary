## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

- bluestones — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of several bluish-grey varieties of stone used for construction

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of several bluish-grey varieties of stone used for construction&#58;
   1. A form of dolerite which appears blue when wet or freshly broken.

      **Usage:** UK; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of several bluish-grey varieties of stone used for construction&#58;
   1. Any of the several (massive) kinds of non-local stone (particularly dolerite) used to construct Stonehenge.

      **Usage:** UK; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any of several bluish-grey varieties of stone used for construction&#58;
   1. A feldspathic sandstone found in the US and Canada.

      **Usage:** Canada; US; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Any of several bluish-grey varieties of stone used for construction&#58;
   1. A form of limestone found in the Shenandoah Valley and some other places.

      **Usage:** US; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Any of several bluish-grey varieties of stone used for construction&#58;
   1. A bluish-grey basalt or olivine basalt.

      **Usage:** Australia; New-Zealand; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Any of several bluish-grey varieties of stone used for construction&#58;
   1. Slate, such as comes from quarries in or near Adelaide.

      **Usage:** Australia; South; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Either of two related copper- and sulfur-based bright blue stones&#58;
   1. Copper(II) sulfate, CuSO₄(H₂O)ₓ where x is 0-5, used as a coloring agent in glass-making and pottery and for other purposes.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. Either of two related copper- and sulfur-based bright blue stones&#58;
   1. Chalcanthite, a water-soluble sulfate mineral, CuSO₄·5H₂O.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. Lapis lazuli, or its core constituent, lazurite.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
