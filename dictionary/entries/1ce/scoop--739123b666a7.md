### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To lift, move, or collect with a scoop or as though with a scoop.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He used both hands to scoop water and splash it on his face.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To make hollow; to dig out.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I tried scooping a hole in the sand with my fingers.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To report on something, especially something worthy of a news article, before (someone else).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The paper across town scooped them on the City Hall scandal.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To begin a vocal note slightly below the target pitch and then to slide up to the target pitch, especially in country music.

   **Usage:** often

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

   **Source context (verbatim):** with "up"

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To pick (someone) up

   **Usage:** slang

   **Example:** You have a car. Can you come and scoop me?

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To win the entire pot in a hand in which the pot was split.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** card-games; poker
