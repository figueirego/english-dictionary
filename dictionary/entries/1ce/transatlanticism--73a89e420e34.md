## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Support for the relationship between Britain and America, especially in literature and culture, traditionally based upon a shared history, a common language, shared principles of legal philosophy, a commonality of religious beliefs, and kinship ties.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An ideology which advocates a close economic, political and military alliance between certain nations in North America, particularly the United States and Canada, and those of Western Europe.

   **Usage:** broadly; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** Atlanticism
