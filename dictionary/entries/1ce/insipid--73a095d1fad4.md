## Adjective

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ɪnˈsɪp.ɪd/ — source labels: UK; US

### Forms

- more insipid — comparative
- most insipid — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Unappetizingly flavorless.

   **Example:** The diners were disappointed with the plain, insipid soup they were served.

   **Source synonyms:** tasteless; bland; vapid; wearish

   **Source antonyms:** savory; savorous; sapid; flavoury; gustable; saporific; saporous; tastable; tasteable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Flat; lacking character or definition.

   **Example:** The textbook had a most insipid presentation of the controversy.

   **Source synonyms:** boring; vacuous; dull; bland; vapid; characterless; colourless
