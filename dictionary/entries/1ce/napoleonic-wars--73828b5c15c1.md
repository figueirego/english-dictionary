## Proper noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The series of wars (1803–1815) between France (ruled by Napoleon) and other European countries.

   **Usage:** plural; plural-only

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The entire series of wars (1792–1815) between France and other European countries between the French Revolution and the fall of Napoleon.

   **Usage:** broadly; plural; plural-only
