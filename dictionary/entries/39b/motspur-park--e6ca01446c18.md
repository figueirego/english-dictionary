## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A railway station now in the borough of Merton, Greater London, England, added in a rural pocket known as West Barnes in the Merton and Morden Urban District in Surrey, England in 1925 to an existing railway (opened 1859) to encourage use of local land for housing (OS grid ref TQ225676).

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A suburb now partly in the borough of Kingston upon Thames, and partly in the borough of Merton, both in Greater London, England, but prior to local government reorganisation in 1965, partly in the Merton and Morden Urban District and partly in the Municipal Borough of Malden and Coombe, both then in Surrey, England (OS grid ref TQ2267).
