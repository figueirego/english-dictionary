### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A bright point that appears at the centre of a circular object's shadow due to Fresnel diffraction.

   **Example:** The Arago spot played an important role in the discovery of the wave nature of light and is a common way to demonstrate that light behaves as a wave.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; natural-sciences; optics; physical-sciences; physics
