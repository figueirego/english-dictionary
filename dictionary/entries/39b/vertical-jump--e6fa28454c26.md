### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A standard measure of explosive power that quantifies lower-body strength by finding the difference between a person's standing reach and the highest point they can touch while jumping; athletes perform a squat and explosive upward jump, swinging their arms for momentum, with the score revealing their power; often using chalk or a Vertec device for measurement.

   **Source synonyms:** vertical leap; Sargent Jump
