### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To push or pump (something, especially fluids) into a cavity or passage.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The nurse injected a painkilling drug into the veins of my forearm.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To introduce (something) suddenly or violently.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Punk injected a much-needed sense of urgency into the British music scene.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To administer an injection to (someone or something), especially of medicine or drugs.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Now lie back while we inject you with the anesthetic.

   **Example:** to inject the blood vessels

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To take or be administered something by means of injection, especially medicine or drugs.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** It's been a week since I stopped injecting, and I'm still in withdrawal.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To introduce (code) into an existing program or its memory space, often without tight integration and sometimes through a security vulnerability.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To cast or throw; used with on.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To introduce or add (something that is different or foreign).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** inject something into orbit

   **Example:** inject some spice into my life
