<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of various historical medicines, typically an electuary compounded with various poison, believed to derive from Mithridates VI and to serve as a universal antidote.

   **Usage:** countable; historical; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; pharmacology; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
