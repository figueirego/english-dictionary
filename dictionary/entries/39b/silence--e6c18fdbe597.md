### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The absence of any sound.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** When the motor stopped, the silence was almost deafening.

   **Example:** deadly silence

   **Example:** in silence

   **Example:** There was a dead silence for a few seconds.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The act of refraining from speaking.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** "You have the right to silence," said the police officer.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Refraining from speaking, for purposes of prayer or meditation; especially, a form of worship practiced by the Society of Friends (Quakers) during meetings.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** During silence a message came to me that there was that of God in every person.
