### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who, or that which, accumulates.

   **Usage:** literal

   **Example:** He is a great accumulator of bad jokes.

   **Source synonyms:** collector

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A wet-cell storage battery.

   **Usage:** UK

   **Example:** Looks like it's time to recharge the accumulator again.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A collective bet on successive events, with both stake and winnings being carried forward to accumulate progressively.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** gambling; games

   **Source synonyms:** parlay

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A system of elastic springs for relieving the strain upon a rope, as in deep-sea dredging.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; mechanical-engineering; mechanics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A vessel containing pressurized hot water ready for release as steam.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; manufacturing

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A container which stores hydraulic power for release, in the form of a pressurized fluid (often suspended within a larger tank of fluid under pressure).

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; hydraulics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

   **Source synonyms:** shock absorber

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A register or variable used for holding the intermediate results of a computation or data transfer.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; programming; sciences

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A derivative contract under which the seller commits to sell shares of an underlying security at a certain strike price, which the buyer is obligated to buy.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance

   **Source synonyms:** share forward accumulator

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. One who takes two higher degrees simultaneously, to reduce their length of study.

   **Usage:** UK; historical

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** education

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. A one way membership function.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; cryptography; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences
