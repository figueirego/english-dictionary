### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A male given name from Gothic, equivalent to Koine Greek Θευδέριχος (Theudérikhos) or English Derek; variant form Theodoric.
   1. A male given name of historical usage, notably borne by Flāvius Theodericus Magnus (Theoderic the Great) (c. 454–526 CE), King of the Ostrogoths (from 475), of the Ostrogothic Kingdom of Italy (from 493), and of the Visigoths (from 511).

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A male given name from Gothic, equivalent to Koine Greek Θευδέριχος (Theudérikhos) or English Derek; variant form Theodoric.
   1. A male given name of historical usage, notably borne by Theoderic I (c. 390–451 CE), King of the Visigothic Kingdom from 418 to 451.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A male given name from Gothic, equivalent to Koine Greek Θευδέριχος (Theudérikhos) or English Derek; variant form Theodoric.
   1. A male given name of historical usage, notably borne by Theoderic II (c. 426–466 CE), King of the Visigothic Kingdom from 453 to 466.
