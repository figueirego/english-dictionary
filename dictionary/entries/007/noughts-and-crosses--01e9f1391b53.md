## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A two-player game played on a three-by-three grid, in which players take it in turns to place their respective symbol (either a nought or a cross) in a cell of the grid, the objective being to form a row, column or diagonal of three of one's own symbol.

   **Usage:** Commonwealth; Ireland; UK; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** games
