## Adverb

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In or to the southern part of an area, country, etc.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** They sent him down south, but he came back home the first chance he got.

   **Source antonyms:** up north

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In England.

   **Usage:** Scotland; not-comparable

   **Example:** Once we heard his accent, we knew he was from down south.
