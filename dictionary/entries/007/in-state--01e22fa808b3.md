### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Existing or occurring within a single state.

   **Usage:** Australia; India; Malaysia; Nigeria; US; also; not-comparable

   **Example:** in-state travel

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of or from the state where an institution is located, especially a public or state-funded college or university.

   **Usage:** Australia; India; Malaysia; Nigeria; US; also; not-comparable

   **Example:** an in-state student

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of or from the state where an institution is located, especially a public or state-funded college or university.
   1. Applicable to or available to individuals of or from the state.

      **Usage:** Australia; India; Malaysia; Nigeria; US; also; not-comparable

      **Example:** in-state tuition
