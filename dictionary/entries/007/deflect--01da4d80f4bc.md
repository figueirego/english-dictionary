### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make (something) deviate from its original path or position.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To touch the ball, often unwittingly, after a shot or a sharp pass, thereby making it unpredictable for the other players.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The defender deflected the cross into his own net.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To deviate from an original path or position.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To avoid addressing (questions, criticism, etc.).

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** The Prime Minister deflected some increasingly pointed questions by claiming he had an appointment.

   **Source synonyms:** elude

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To divert (attention, etc.).

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To redirect culpability to avoid it.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; psychology; sciences
