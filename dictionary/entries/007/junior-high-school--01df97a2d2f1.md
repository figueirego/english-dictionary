### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A school for children who have completed elementary school or grade school, but are not yet old enough for high school. It often includes grades 7 and 8, and possibly also 6 or 9.

   **Usage:** US; countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** education

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A secondary education school for grades 7–10 (a continuation of elementary school). It is succeeded by senior high school.

   **Usage:** Philippines; countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** education

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A secondary education school for grades 7–10 (a continuation of primary school), found in rural areas. It is succeeded by high school, though high schools also serve these grade levels in their immediate catchment areas.

   **Usage:** Shetland; countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** education
