### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A flash of light produced by short-duration, high-voltage discharge of electricity within a cloud, between clouds, or between a cloud and the earth.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** Although we did not see the lightning, we did hear the thunder.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A discharge of this kind.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** The lightning was hot enough to melt the sand.

   **Example:** That tree was hit by lightning.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Anything that moves very fast.

   **Usage:** figuratively; uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Gin.

   **Usage:** archaic; slang; uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Obsolete form of lightening.

   **Usage:** alt-of; obsolete; uncountable; usually

   **Alternative form of:** [lightening](../0a6/lightening--2995dbaa601b.md)
