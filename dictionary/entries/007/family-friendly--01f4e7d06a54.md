### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Suitable for families consisting of parents and minor children.

   **Example:** This family friendly paradise - with its giant snow slides, outdoor amphitheatre, and traditional winter activities - is the ideal destination for the young and old alike.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Promoting the welfare of the family structure.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Devoid of material deemed inappropriate for children such as sexually-explicit text or images, use of profanity, or references to the use of illegal drugs.

   **Usage:** US
