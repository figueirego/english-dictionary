### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Somewhat reddish or reddish-brown in colour (especially of hair or skin colouring).

   **Example:** gingery hair / freckles; the gingery fur of a fox

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Having reddish-brown hair. (of a person)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Having a flavour or aroma of the spice ginger; containing that spice.

   **Example:** a gingery broth / stir-fry

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Energetic, vigorous, lively, peppy, zippy.

   **Usage:** dated

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Ginger, inhibited, cautious.

   **Usage:** US; dated
