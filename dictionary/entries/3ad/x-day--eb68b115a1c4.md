## Proper noun · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. 1 November 1945, the planned date for the Allied invasion of Kyushu in southwestern Japan in World War II (cancelled following Japan's unconditional surrender in September 1945).

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An annual celebration in the Church of the SubGenius parody religion, falling on July 5 and commemorating the prophesied end of the world on that date in 1998, when alien beings from "Planet X" were to have invaded.
