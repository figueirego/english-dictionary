**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To pursue life’s opportunities boldly and energetically.

   **Extractor diagnostics (not usage labels):** no-gloss

**Supplemental definition (evidence reviewed; source gap preserved):** The publisher’s description of its own motivational book applies the exact phrase to brave life choices; the original published play quotation also connects it to active effort before death. The definition is based on these uses, not solely on analogy with taking a bull by the horns.
