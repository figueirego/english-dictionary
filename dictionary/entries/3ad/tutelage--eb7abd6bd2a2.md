### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of guarding, protecting, or guiding.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** the king's right of seigniory and tutelage

   **Source synonyms:** guardianship; protection

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The state of being under a guardian or a tutor; the care or protection enjoyed; being a ward or a tutee.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Instruction; teaching; guidance; being a tutor.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** tutorage; tutorship
