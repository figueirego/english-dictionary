### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A yard to the front of a house or similar residence, typically having a lawn and often trees, shrubs, and/or flowers.

   **Usage:** Canada; US

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An enclosed and often primarily utilitarian area to the front of a house or similar residence, typically having a hard surface or paving.

   **Usage:** UK
