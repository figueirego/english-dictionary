### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act or process in which an agent (person or computer) parses something (a text, a program).

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Parsing an Old English text can be difficult.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An instance of that act or process; a parse.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** A parsing of a sentence about a horse can yield several possible results.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any result of such an instance; a parse; an acceptation.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** Upon review, for the word horse, the parsing "animal" was rejected in favor of the parsing "piece of gymnastic equipment".
