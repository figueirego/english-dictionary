### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The moon in its first or last quarter when only half the visible face is illuminated; a quarter moon.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Anything shaped like a crescent, especially the base of a fingernail.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A semicircular or crescent-shaped Filipino butter cookie.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Synonym of black and white (“type of cookie”).
