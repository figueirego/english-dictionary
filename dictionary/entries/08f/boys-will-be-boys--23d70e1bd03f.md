### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. It is hard, often fruitless, to attempt to curb the natural playfulness and tendency to mischief of most growing boys.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Even grown men usually remain somewhat boyish at heart.

   **Example:** Boys will be boys, grinned Grandpa as he watched his adult son playing with the fancy train-set he had given his grandson for Christmas.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any potentially objectionable behavior (especially predatory sexual behavior) that the speaker considers to be commonly practiced by many boys and men should be viewed as excusable because it comes naturally to them.

   **Usage:** informal; offensive
