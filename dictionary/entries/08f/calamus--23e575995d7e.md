**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Sweet flag (Acorus calamus).

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A quill; the hard, horny, hollow, and more or less transparent part of the stem or scape of a feather.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences; ornithology

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually
