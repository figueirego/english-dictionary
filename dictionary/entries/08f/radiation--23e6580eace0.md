### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The shooting forth of anything from a point or surface, like diverging rays of light.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The radiation of love from the crowd was addictive to the young singer-songwriters.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The shooting forth of anything from a point or surface, like diverging rays of light.
   1. Ellipsis of electromagnetic radiation.

      **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; countable; ellipsis; often; uncountable

      **Example:** heat radiation

      **Source synonyms:** EMR

      **Alternative form of:** [electromagnetic radiation](../014/electromagnetic-radiation--051d2d9d2b3d.md)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The process of radiating waves or particles.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The transfer of energy via radiation.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Radioactive energy.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A rapid diversification of an ancestral species into many new forms.

   **Usage:** countable; evolutionary theory
