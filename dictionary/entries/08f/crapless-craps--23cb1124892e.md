## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A variety of the dice game craps, differing in placing the shooter at a disadvantage and in allowing players to bet on rolling a 2, 3, 11 or 12 before a 7 is thrown.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** bastard craps
