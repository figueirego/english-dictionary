<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To perform a musical piece while reading it for the first time, without rehearsal.

   **Example:** I can sight read really simple music, but hard pieces need a lot of practice so I don't have to stop and figure out what I'm doing.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music
