### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To glide along without adding energy; to allow a vehicle to continue moving forward after disengaging the engine or ceasing to apply motive power.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** When I ran out of gas, fortunately I managed to coast into a nearby gas station.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To sail along a coast.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

   **Source synonyms:** hug the coastline

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To make a minimal effort; to continue to do something in a routine way, without initiative or effort.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To draw near to; to approach; to keep near, or by the side of.

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To sail by or near; to follow the coastline of.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To conduct along a coast or river bank.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

**Source sense R2.S7**
