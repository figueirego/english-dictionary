## Noun

### Forms

- timewaster — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Someone who wastes their own time or the time of others.

   **Example:** I showed him round the property thinking he was a prospective buyer, but in fact he was just a time-waster.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Something unproductive.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; informal

   **Example:** Email was supposed to improve business efficiency, but all too often it turns out to be a time-waster.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Something which passes the time that may or may not be seen positively.

   **Example:** This puzzle book is a good time-waster when my train is late.
