## Noun · Record 2

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. The act or process of turning (becoming) black in colour.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** nigrescence

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. The result or an instance of such an act or process; a black stain or mark.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. Any of various (dark-colored) surface treatments for metal substrates, to inhibit corrosion.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**
