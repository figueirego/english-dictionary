### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A fruit of the coconut palm (not a true nut), Cocos nucifera, having a fibrous husk surrounding a large seed.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A hard-shelled seed of this fruit, having white flesh and a fluid-filled central cavity.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The edible white flesh of this fruit.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The coconut palm.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A member of any of various dark-skinned ethnic groups (e.g. Hispanic, South Asian, or black) who is considered to have overly assimilated to white culture&#58;
   1. A Hispanic or other dark-skinned person considered to have overly assimilated to white culture.

      **Usage:** US; countable; derogatory; ethnic; slur; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A member of any of various dark-skinned ethnic groups (e.g. Hispanic, South Asian, or black) who is considered to have overly assimilated to white culture&#58;
   1. A black person considered to have overly assimilated to white culture, a black race traitor.

      **Usage:** Australia; Kenya; South-Africa; countable; derogatory; ethnic; slur; uncountable

      **Source synonyms:** Oreo; Uncle Tom
