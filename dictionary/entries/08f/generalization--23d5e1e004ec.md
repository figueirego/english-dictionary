## Noun

### Forms

- generalizations — plural
- generalisation — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An act or instance of generalizing; concluding that something true of a subclass is true of the entire class.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The formulation of general concepts from specific instances by abstracting common properties.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** [universalization](../265/universalization--9979a9e3a919.md)

   **Source antonyms:** specialization; concretization; individualization; particularization; singularization; specialization

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Inductive reasoning from detailed facts to general principles.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An oversimplified or exaggerated conception, opinion, or image of the members of a group.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A proof, axiom, problem, or definition that includes another's cases, and also some additional cases; a conclusion reached by inferring from specific cases to more general cases or principles.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** A hypersphere is a generalization of a sphere across more than three dimensions.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences
