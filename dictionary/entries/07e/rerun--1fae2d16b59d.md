**Source sense R1.S1**

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A television program shown after its initial presentation, particularly many weeks after its initial presentation; a repeat.

   **Usage:** US

   **Example:** I'm not a fan of the new Simpsons episodes, but the old reruns have me in stitches, although I've seen them countless times.

   **Source synonyms:** repeat

**Source sense R1.S3**

**Source sense R1.S4**

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; politics

**Source sense R1.S5**
