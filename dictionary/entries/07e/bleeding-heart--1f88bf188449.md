## Noun

### Forms

- bleeding-heart — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of various perennial plants, of the family Papaveraceae, subfamily Fumarioideae, often having pink heart-shaped flowers, including&#58;
   1. An Asian bleeding heart (Lamprocapnos spectabilis, syn. Dicentra spectabilis), a popular garden plant with arching sprays of pendent red and white (or pure white) flowers.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of various perennial plants, of the family Papaveraceae, subfamily Fumarioideae, often having pink heart-shaped flowers, including&#58;
   1. Any member of the genus Dicentra (bleedinghearts), native to eastern Asia and North America, some species with colors and shape similar to Lamprocapnos spectabilis.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of various perennial plants, of the family Papaveraceae, subfamily Fumarioideae, often having pink heart-shaped flowers, including&#58;
   1. Dactylicapnos, a genus native to Himalayas and mountainous areas of South China, Burma and Vietnam, usually with yellow flowers of similar shape.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Certain species of genus Clerodendrum of flowering shrubs, lianas, or small trees of the mint family Lamiaceae.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Any member of the species Homalanthus populifolius (bleeding heart tree), an Australian rainforest plant.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Any member of the species Homalanthus novoguineensis (bleeding heart tree), a rainforest plant of Australia, New Guinea, and nearby islands.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Any member of the genus Gallicolumba of ground-dwelling doves of east Asian islands.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A person who is overly sympathetic to the plight of the underprivileged, exploited or helpless, or to casualties of circumstance.

   **Usage:** derogatory; figuratively
