### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of adopting.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The state of being adopted; the acceptance of a child of other parents as if they were one's own child.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** A Chinese baby girl was given away for adoption.

   **Example:** Another Chinese boy was put up for adoption.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An admission to an institution, for example a hospital, clinic, mental asylum.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** the adoption of people into hospitals or monasteries

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The choosing and making that to be one's own which originally was not so; acceptance.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** the adoption of opinions

   **Example:** the adoption of words from another language

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The choosing and making that to be one's own which originally was not so; acceptance.
   1. The act of accepting and putting into effect or practice (a method, proposal, technique, etc.)

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Example:** Our company is considering the adoption of a four-day week.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The transfer from an old system to another (usually better) system.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. An act of divine grace by which the redeemed in Christ are admitted to the privileges of the sons of God.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** lifestyle; religion; theology

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**
