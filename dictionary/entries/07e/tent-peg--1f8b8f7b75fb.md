### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A peg, driven into the ground, to hold a rope that supports a tent.

   **Example:** Upon arrival at the campsite, they discovered that they had forgotten the tent pegs; they had to use rocks and logs to tether the tent instead.

   **Source synonyms:** spike; stake

<a id="r1-s2"></a>
