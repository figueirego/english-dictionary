### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To achieve a balance, usually by increasing something, e.g., revenue.

   **Example:** By raising taxes, we can balance up until the deficit is gone, instead of decreasing tax rates and therefore revenue, which would be balancing down.

   **Example:** We have to provide a certain amount every month, so if the price of one option increases we will balance up by buying from other sources.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To calculate a balance (a total).

   **Example:** In December, we balance down by adding up the payments for the entire year, so we can report the balance to the government.
