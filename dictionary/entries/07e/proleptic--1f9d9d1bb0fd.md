## Noun · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /pɹoʊˈlɛptɪk/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Forms

- proleptics — plural

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. An instance of prolepsis;
   1. The placement of an element in a syntactic unit before that to which it would logically correspond.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. An instance of prolepsis;
   1. Information about the outcome of a story placed near the beginning.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. An instance of prolepsis;
   1. A lateral branch that develops from a lateral meristem, after the formation of a bud or following a period of dormancy, when the lateral meristem is split from a terminal meristem.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. Something that predicts or implies the future or outcome.
