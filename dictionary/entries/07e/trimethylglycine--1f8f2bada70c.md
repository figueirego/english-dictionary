## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An organic compound related to choline, with the difference that the terminal carboxylic acid group of trimethylglycine has been reduced to a hydroxyl group in choline.

   **Usage:** uncountable
