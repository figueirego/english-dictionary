### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The topographical boundary dividing two adjacent catchment basins, such as a ridge or a crest.

   **Usage:** Australia; Ireland; New-Zealand; UK

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; hydrology; natural-sciences

   **Source synonyms:** water parting; drainage divide; water divide; divide

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A region of land within which water flows down into a specified body, such as a river, lake, sea, or ocean; a drainage basin.

   **Usage:** Canada; US

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; hydrology; natural-sciences

   **Source synonyms:** catchment basin; catchment; catchment area; drainage area; river basin; water basin

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A critical point marking a change in course or development.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** watershed moment — watershed event — watershed ruling

   **Source synonyms:** turning point; crossroads

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The time of day after which material of a more adult nature (e.g., violence, swear words, or nudity) may be broadcast on television or radio, either one permitted by law or one contrived from convention (e.g. when children are not watching).
