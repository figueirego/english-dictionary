### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To act decisively on the basis of information received or deduced.

   **Example:** The policeman decided to act on the tip from his informant.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To act accordingly to a (usually sublimated) desire or impulse.

   **Example:** He keeps telling me about wanting to make a move, but he hasn't worked up to acting on it yet.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To take action against something.

   **Example:** World leaders have repeatedly stated that we must act on climate change.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To affect something.

   **Example:** Different drugs act on different parts of our body in many different ways.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see act, on.
