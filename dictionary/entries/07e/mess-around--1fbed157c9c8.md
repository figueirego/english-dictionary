## Verb

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To fiddle idly.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** The kids sat there messing around with leaves and blades of grass because they were bored.

   **Example:** These guys don't mess around! They finished the whole job in ten minutes.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To joke, kid, or play.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** Hey, don't take it personally. I was only messing around.

   **Example:** Don't mess around with electricity if you don't understand it.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To have a sexual relationship, especially one which is noncommittal.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** Rumor has it that he's messing around with his secretary.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To dance.

   **Usage:** dated; slang; African-American Vernacular
