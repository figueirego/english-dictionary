### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A pattern, a way of doing something; especially a pattern of thought, a system of beliefs, a conceptual framework.

   **Example:** Thomas Kuhn's landmark “The Structure of Scientific Revolutions” got people talking about paradigm shifts, to the point the word itself now suggests an incomplete or biased perspective.

   **Source synonyms:** style; model; worldview

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An example serving as the model for such a pattern; an exceptionally good or prototypical example of a pattern or group.

   **Source synonyms:** template; exemplar; archetype; poster child; archetype; byspel (rare); case; beau ideal; case in point; embodiment; epitome; eponym (archaic); exemplar; exemplification; example; exemplificator; Exhibit A; illustration; incarnation; instance; paradigm; prototype; quintessence; representative; sample (obsolete); specimen; symbol; ur-type; archetype; epitome; example; exemplar; icon; model; paradigm; paragon; pattern; quintessence; template

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A set of all forms which contain a common element, especially the set of all inflectional forms of a word or a particular grammatical category.

   **Example:** The paradigm of "to sing" is "sing, sang, sung". The verb "to ring" follows the same paradigm.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** paradigma
