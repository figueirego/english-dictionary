## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A ring of clear liquid, forming into drops, near the top of a glass of fortified wine; the result of differential evaporation of alcohol and subsequent changes in surface tension; an example of the Marangoni effect.

   **Usage:** plural; plural-only
