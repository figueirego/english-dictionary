## Verb · Record 1

### Forms

- goes to — present; singular; third-person
- going to — participle; present
- went to — past
- gone to — participle; past

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see go, to.

   **Example:** All the jewelry went to her heirs.

   **Example:** Don't go to all that bother for me.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To attend an event or a sight.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** We went to a concert for my birthday.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To attend classes at a school as a student.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** He went to the University of Kansas for almost two years before he dropped out.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To tend to support.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The study goes to the point I was making earlier about subsidies.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To get to work; (imperatively) come on.

   **Usage:** archaic; intransitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Used imperatively to express protest or surprise; "come, now!".

   **Usage:** archaic; intransitive
