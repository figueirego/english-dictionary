## Noun · Record 2

### Forms

- prediseases — plural
- pre-disease — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A condition, albeit consisting of only one or two signs, that is identifiable as, or believed to be, a subclinical prelude to a disease, with greatly increased predisposition thereto (for example, prediabetes or prehypertension); the operational definition of such a state or the clinical value of asserting it may be contentious (as with purported preobesity).

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences
