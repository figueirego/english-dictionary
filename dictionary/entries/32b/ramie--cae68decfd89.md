### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A tall, tropical Asian perennial herb, of species Boehmeria nivea, cultivated for its fibrous stems.

   **Usage:** countable; usually

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The fibre extracted from this plant known especially for its ability to hold shape, reduce wrinkling, and introduce a silky lustre used to make textiles, fishing nets, upholstery, etc.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually
