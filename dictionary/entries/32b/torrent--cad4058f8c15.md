**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A violent flow, as of water, lava, etc.; a stream suddenly raised and running rapidly, as down a precipice.

   **Example:** Rain fell on the hills in torrents.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** They endured a torrent of inquiries.
