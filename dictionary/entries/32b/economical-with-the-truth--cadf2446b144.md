## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Not telling the whole truth, especially in order to present a false image of a situation; untruthful; lying. Often used with sarcasm or satire.

   **Usage:** euphemistic; idiomatic

   **Example:** I would be acting economical with the truth if I were to tell you that I was enjoying myself.
