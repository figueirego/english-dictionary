**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A disorder characterized by sudden and uncontrollable attacks of deep sleep, often brief, sometimes accompanied by paralysis and hallucinations.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Excitement-induced narcolepsy caused him to sleep through the most important events of his life.
