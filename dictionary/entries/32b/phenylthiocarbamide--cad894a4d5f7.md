### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The N-phenyl derivative of carbamide; it is either tasteless or has a bitter taste, depending on the genetic makeup of the person tasting it, who is either a nontaster or a taster, respectively.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
