## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In excess of the normal; above the normal.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Characterized by, or pertaining to, a personality disposition in which an individual exerts considerable effort to present themselves as socially normative to others.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; psychology; sciences; social-science; sociology

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of an accelerated version of reality where the outlook is bleak, but there is no ability to change course.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; psychology; sciences; social-science; sociology

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**
