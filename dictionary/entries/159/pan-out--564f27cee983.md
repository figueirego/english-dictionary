### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To separate and recover (valuable minerals) by swirling dirt or crushed rock in a pan of water, in the manner of a traditional prospector seeking gold.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To proceed according to plan; to result or end up with an acceptable outcome or in an acceptable form.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

   **Example:** My hopes for a white Christmas didn't pan out this year.

   **Example:** We'll see what pans out from the arbitration process.

   **Source synonyms:** shape up; work out

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To fall asleep.

   **Usage:** Ireland; intransitive; slang
