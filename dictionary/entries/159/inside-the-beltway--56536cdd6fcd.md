### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. within the portions of Washington D.C., Maryland and Virginia encircled by Interstate 495 (the Washington Beltway)

   **Usage:** US; not-comparable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. of or relating to things that occur in Washington, D.C., usually in relation to United States national politics.

   **Usage:** US; not-comparable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. out of touch with the sentiments of people living outside of Washington, D.C.; elitist, insular

   **Usage:** US; derogatory; not-comparable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

   **Usage:** New-Zealand; not-comparable
