### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To return, restore (a thing to its original owner or location etc.).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Give me back my book!

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To contribute money, goods, or, especially, services for charitable purposes, as if in return for one's own success.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** We organize this Thanksgiving dinner at the old-age home to give back to the community.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To recede; to retreat.

   **Usage:** archaic
