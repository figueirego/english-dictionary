### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An experience or set of experiences which is intellectually challenging or stimulating.

   **Usage:** informal

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A disorienting, exciting experience, especially one consisting mainly of striking sensory impressions.

   **Usage:** informal

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A state of mind in which one is distracted, disturbed, or unnerved, whether self-induced or resulting from ill-treatment by others.

   **Usage:** informal
