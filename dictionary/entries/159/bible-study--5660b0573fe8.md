## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The intentional, regular habit of reading, analyzing, and applying biblical scripture, either personally or in a group setting.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The study of the Christian Bible in an informal group setting; a group of people who meet for that purpose.

   **Usage:** broadly; uncountable

   **Example:** Are you coming to Bible study tonight?

   **Example:** Your friends can join our Bible study if they want.

   **Source synonyms:** Bible group; cell group; homegroup
