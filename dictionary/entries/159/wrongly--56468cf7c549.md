### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In violation of a moral or other standard, code, or convention; in an unfair, unjust, dishonest, or immoral manner; wrongly convicted is synonymous with wrongful conviction and miscarriage of justice.

   **Source synonyms:** unfairly; unjustly; dishonestly; immorally; wrongfully; illicitly; immodestly; immorally; improperly; incontinently; indecently; reprehensibly; unchastely; unseemly; unvirtuously; wickedly; wrongfully; wrongly

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Incorrectly, mistakenly; by error.

   **Example:** I wrongly assumed that it would be an easy job.

   **Source synonyms:** incorrectly; mistakenly; erroneously

   **Source antonyms:** correctly; rightly
