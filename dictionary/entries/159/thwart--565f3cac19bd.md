## Noun · Record 5

### Pronunciation

- enPR: thwôrt — source labels: General-American
- IPA: /θwɔɹt/ — source labels: General-American

### Forms

- thwarts — plural

### Senses

<a id="r5-s1"></a>

**Source sense R5.S1**

1. A seat across a boat on which a rower may sit.

   **Example:** The fisherman sat on the aft thwart to row.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

   **Source synonyms:** thaught; thawt; thoft (Britain; dialectal)

<a id="r5-s2"></a>

**Source sense R5.S2**

2. A brace, perpendicular to the keel, that helps maintain the beam (“breadth”) of a marine vessel against external water pressure and that may serve to support the rail.

   **Example:** A well-made dugout canoe rarely needs a thwart.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r5-s3"></a>

**Source sense R5.S3**

3. An act of thwarting; something which thwarts; a hindrance, an obstacle.

   **Usage:** rare
