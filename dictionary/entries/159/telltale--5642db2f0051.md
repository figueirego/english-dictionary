### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who divulges private information with intent to hurt others.

   **Source synonyms:** blabbermouth; gossip; rumormonger; squealer; talebearer; tattletale; carrytale; blab; blabbermouth; blabtale (obsolete); blabtongue (obsolete); busybody; buzzer (obsolete); gossip; gossiper; gossipmonger; meddler; mouth; mumblenews (obsolete); newsmonger; rumormonger; rumourmonger; scandalmonger; sleazemonger; tabby (archaic); talebearer; talecarrier; tattletale; telltale; telltale tit (childish); titivil (obsolete); tittle-tattle; town crier; whisperer

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An indicator, such as a warning light, that serves to warn of a hazard or problem.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Something that serves to reveal something else.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** The telltale was the lipstick on his shirt collar.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A movable piece of ivory, lead, or other material, connected to the bellows of an organ, whose position indicates when the wind is exhausted.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A length of yarn or ribbon attached to a sail or shroud etc to indicate the direction of the flow of the air relative to the boat.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A mechanical attachment to the steering wheel, which, in the absence of a tiller, shows the position of the helm.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A compass in the cabin of a vessel, usually placed where the captain can see it at all hours, and thus inform himself of the vessel's course.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A machine or contrivance for indicating or recording something, particularly for keeping a check upon employees (factory hands, watchmen, drivers, etc.) by revealing to their employers what they have done or omitted.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A bird, the tattler.

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**
