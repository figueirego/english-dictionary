### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To misstep; to walk or run clumsily; to trip or fall.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** He stumbled over a rock.

   **Example:** Last night, you drunkenly stumbled to the front door, waking up the whole house.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To make mistakes, to have trouble.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** I always stumble over verbs in Spanish.

   **Source synonyms:** blunder; err

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To cause to stumble or trip.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** trip up

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To mislead, to cause to err or fall.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** confound

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To make a discovery unexpectedly or accidentally; to chance, come, or happen (upon a person or thing) (with onto, across, on, upon).

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** We stumbled onto the answer without even trying.
