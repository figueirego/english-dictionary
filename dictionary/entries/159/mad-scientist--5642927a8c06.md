## Noun

### Forms

- mad scientists — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A bumbling or eccentric scientist working on questionable projects, or a villainous scientist bent on destruction; sometimes, a complex character in whom these archetypes seem to vie for supremacy.
   1. A stock character in fiction who embodies such an archetype.

      **Example:** The Nutty Professor franchise explores the light comedic potential of a mad scientist.

      **Example:** Dr Strangelove explores the darker comedic potential of a mad scientist.

      **Example:** Her new novel involves a mad scientist, but there's more to it than just bubbling flasks and lightning bolts.

      **Source synonyms:** Doctor Frankenstein; Dr. Frankenstein; Dr Frankenstein; Frankenstein; frankenstein; mad professor

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A bumbling or eccentric scientist working on questionable projects, or a villainous scientist bent on destruction; sometimes, a complex character in whom these archetypes seem to vie for supremacy.
   1. A real-life scientist who is asserted to embody such an archetype.

      **Example:** The mad scientists at Acme Labs have done it again! \[said either approvingly or disparagingly\]

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A bumbling or eccentric scientist working on questionable projects, or a villainous scientist bent on destruction; sometimes, a complex character in whom these archetypes seem to vie for supremacy.
   1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; a scientist who is insane, power-mad, or similarly deranged.

      **Example:** In the 1890s, some of the locals viewed the chemical plant as a folly run by mad scientists and venal aristocrats.
