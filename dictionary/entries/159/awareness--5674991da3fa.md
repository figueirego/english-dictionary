### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The state or level of consciousness where sense data can be confirmed by an observer.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** I gradually passed from sleep to full awareness.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The state or quality of being aware of something.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** raise awareness of a topic

   **Example:** The awareness of one type of idea naturally fosters an awareness of another idea
