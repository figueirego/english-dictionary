## Proverb

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Literally, what is good for a female goose is equally good for a male goose (gander); or, what is good for a woman should be equally good for a man.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. If something is good for one person, it should be equally good for another person; someone who treats another in a certain way should not complain if the same is done to them.
