## Noun

### Forms

- rip-offs — plural
- ripoff — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A bad deal; an unfair or exorbitant price or rate.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** In those days, a hamburger was fifteen cents, and if someone was charging a quarter for a bowl of soup, it was regarded as a rip-off.

   **Example:** Eighty-seven bucks for a three-ounce piece of fish? What a rip-off! Haute cuisine or not!

   **Source antonyms:** bargain; good value; value for money; steal

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A theft or robbery.

   **Example:** He noticed that the window was ajar, and right away he knew in his gut that a rip-off had probably occurred.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A scam.

   **Example:** He promised to mail me a refund, but it was just a rip-off.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A copy, especially one that is illegal or inferior.

   **Example:** Are you sure that's a real Rolex and not just a cheap rip-off?

   **Source synonyms:** knockoff
