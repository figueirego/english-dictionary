### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see on, side.

   **Example:** He was sleeping on my side of the bed.

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Supporting or advocating for someone.

   **Example:** No matter what happens, we will always be on your side.
