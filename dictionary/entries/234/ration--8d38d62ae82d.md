**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Example:** Jack gathered his meager ration of food.

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To supply with a ration; to limit (someone) to a specific allowance of something.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** We rationed ourselves to three sips of water a day until we were rescued.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To portion out (especially during a shortage of supply); to limit access to.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** By the third day on the raft, we had to ration our water.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To restrict (an activity etc.)

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I've rationed myself to one cup of coffee a day.

   **Example:** Our present health care system is rationed only to those who can afford it because of unnecessary high cost, lack of insurance coverage by 47 million people, and exorbitant prescription prices.
