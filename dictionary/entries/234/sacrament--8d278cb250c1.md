**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A sacred act and the attendant ceremony, considered (theology) an outward sign of divine grace, instituted by Jesus Christ.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity

**Source sense R1.S4**

   **Usage:** broadly
