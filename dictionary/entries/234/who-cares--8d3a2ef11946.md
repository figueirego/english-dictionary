## Phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A reply to an unimportant or irrelevant statement, indicating indifference on the part of the speaker.

   **Usage:** colloquial; rhetoric

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A reply that dismisses the importance of another speaker's immediately preceding statement.

   **Usage:** colloquial; rhetoric
