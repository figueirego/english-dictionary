### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A deciduous oak tree, Quercus velutina, native to eastern North America; its wood.

   **Usage:** countable; usually

   **Source synonyms:** quercitron

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A related oak tree native to western North America, Quercus kelloggii, often considered the best oak for acorn mush by American Indians in its native range; the wood of this tree.

   **Source synonyms:** California black oak
