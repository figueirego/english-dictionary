### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To call upon (a person, a god) for help, assistance or guidance.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** invocate (obsolete)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To solicit, petition for, appeal to a favorable attitude.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The envoy invoked the King of Kings's magnanimity to reduce his province's tribute after another drought.

   **Source synonyms:** solicit; appeal; petition

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To call another ship.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

   **Source context (verbatim):** of one ship

   **Source synonyms:** signal

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To call to mind (something) for some purpose.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** evoke; bring to mind; call to mind; enmind

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To appeal for validation to a (notably cited) authority.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** In certain Christian circles, invoking the Bible constitutes irrefutable proof.

   **Source synonyms:** cite; reference; appeal

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To conjure up with incantations.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** This satanist ritual invokes Beelzebub.

   **Source synonyms:** summon

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To bring about as an inevitable consequence.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Blasphemy is taboo as it may invoke divine wrath.

   **Source synonyms:** bring about; incite; abet (obsolete); actuate; goose; fuel; light a fire under; send; sting; bring about; cheer (obsolete); draw; drive; encourage; egg on (idiomatic); engender; evoke; excite; foment; goad; grill (Scotland; US; obsolete); ignite (figuratively); impel; incite; induce; inflame (figuratively); instigate; invoke; lash; motive; motivate; move (obsolete); obligate; oblige; push; promote; prompt; provoke; rouse; set off; spark off; spur; spur on; stimulate; stir; stir up; stoke (figuratively); tempt; urge; whet (obsolete); whip up

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To cause (a program or subroutine) to execute.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Interactive programs let the users enter choices and invoke the corresponding routines.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** call; execute; launch; run
