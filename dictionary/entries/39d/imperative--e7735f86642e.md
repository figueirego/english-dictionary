### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. The grammatical mood expressing an order (see jussive). In English, the imperative form of a verb is the same as that of the bare infinitive.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** The verbs in sentences like "Do it!" and "Say what you like!" are in the imperative.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** grammar; human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** imperative mood

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. A verb in the imperative mood.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** grammar; human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. An essential action, a must&#58; something which is imperative.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** Visiting Berlin is an imperative.
