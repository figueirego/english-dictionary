### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A young toad or frog in its larval stage of development that lives in water, has a tail and no legs, and, like a fish, breathes through gills.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The aquatic larva of any amphibian.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** salamander tadpole

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A type of cargo bike that has two wheels in front and one in back.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A child's basic drawing of a human being, having a detailed head but only sticks for the body and limbs.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; psychology; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A small child.

   **Usage:** informal

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

   **Usage:** derogatory; informal
