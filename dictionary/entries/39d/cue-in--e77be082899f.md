### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To add elements (dialogue, music, special effects) to a specific spot in a script.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** We should cue in the music at the end of this scene.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To signal (someone) to join a performance or to begin a task at a particular moment.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The conductor cued me in at the wrong time.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To identify and understand signals, emotions, body language, subtext, etc.; to connect with someone.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** A good therapist can cue in to even the toughest of clients.
