<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The office of a pope.
   1. The office of the pope of Rome, who is the head of the Roman Catholic Church.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable; usually

      **Example:** The Vatican defends the primacy of the papacy.
