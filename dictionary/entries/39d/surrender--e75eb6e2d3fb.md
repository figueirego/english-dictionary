### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To give up into the power, control, or possession of another.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** hand over; betray; overgive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To yield (a town, a fortification, etc.) to an enemy.

   **Usage:** broadly; transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To give oneself up into the power of another, especially as a prisoner; to submit or give in.

   **Usage:** intransitive; reflexive

   **Example:** Don't shoot! I surrender!

   **Source synonyms:** strike one's flag; wave the white flag; chuck up the sponge

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To give up possession of; to yield; to resign.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to surrender a right, privilege, or advantage

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To yield (oneself) to an influence, emotion, passion, etc.

   **Usage:** reflexive

   **Example:** to surrender oneself to grief, to despair, to indolence, or to sleep

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To abandon (one's hand of cards) and recover half of the initial bet.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** blackjack; games

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. For a policyholder, to voluntarily terminate an insurance contract before the end of its term, usually with the expectation of receiving a surrender value.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; insurance
