### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To go to bed; to go to sleep (especially at, or around midnight).

   **Usage:** colloquial; idiomatic

   **Example:** I'm about ready to turn into a pumpkin. You can stay up later if you want.

   **Example:** Tell her I'm turning into a pumpkin if she stays out too late. She won't be getting a lift from me.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used to indicate a curfew, or the time by which one must depart.

   **Usage:** colloquial; idiomatic

   **Example:** My daughter turns into a pumpkin at 10&#58;00, so you need to have her home before then.

   **Example:** I'm turning into a pumpkin at 4&#58;00, so we need to finish this.

   **Example:** "I need to get back now!" "Why? Are you going to turn into a pumpkin?"

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To cease to be special or extraordinary.

   **Usage:** colloquial; idiomatic
