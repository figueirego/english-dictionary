### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A specialist in marketing (promoting and selling products and services).

   **Usage:** US

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One who promotes and sells products or services; called a marketer in US English.

   **Usage:** UK

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Someone who works in or supports a certain kind of system of selling and buying, for example free marketeer, black marketeer.
