### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To inflate by means of a pump.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** air up

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To inflate by means of a pump.
   1. To do so with a pumping action.

      **Usage:** transitive

      **Example:** For someone who is in shape, it is not difficult to pump up a car tire with a bicycle pump; it is merely tedious. But you can get the job done if you need it bad enough.

      **Source synonyms:** air up

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To excite a person or group to a frenzy.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** The singer has been pumping up the audience.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To cause one's muscles to swell by means of focused weightlifting.

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** I want to pump you up.

   **Example:** He's gonna help me pump up.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** bodybuilding; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To cause (something) to increase in intensity, salience or other traits beyond what is reasonable.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** You have to pump up the relatability of the characters in your screenplay.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To increase the volume of (something).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** pump up the jam

   **Example:** pump up the jukebox
