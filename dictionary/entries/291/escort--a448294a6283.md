## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈɛs.kɔɹt/ — source labels: General-American
- IPA: /ˈɛs.kɔːt/ — source labels: Received-Pronunciation

### Forms

- escorts — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A group of people or vehicles, generally armed, who go with a person or people of importance to safeguard them on a journey or mission.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An accompanying person in such a group.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A guard who travels with a dangerous person, such as a criminal, for the protection of others.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A group of people attending as a mark of respect or honor.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An accompanying person in a social gathering, etc.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Protection, care, or safeguard on a journey or excursion.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A sex worker who does not operate in a brothel, but with whom clients make appointments; a call girl or male equivalent; a pimp.

## Verb · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ɪsˈkɔɹt/ — source labels: General-American
- IPA: /ɪsˈkɔːt/ — source labels: Received-Pronunciation

### Forms

- escorts — present; singular; third-person
- escorting — participle; present
- escorted — participle; past
- escorted — past

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To attend to in order to guard and protect; to accompany as a safeguard (for the person escorted or for others); to give honorable or ceremonious attendance to.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To accompany (a person) in order to compel them to go somewhere (e.g. to leave a building).

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To go with someone as a partner, for example on a formal date.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To work as an escort (sex worker).

   **Usage:** intransitive
