**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Serving as an ornament; having no purpose other than to make more beautiful.

   **Example:** Some think it most ornamental to wear their bracelets on their wrists; others, about their ankles.

   **Source synonyms:** beautifying; embellishing; decorative

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Bred for aesthetic or decorative purposes.

   **Usage:** usually

   **Example:** Those cranberry bushes are ornamental, so they're not edible.
