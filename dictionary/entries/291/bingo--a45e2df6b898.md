## Noun · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈbɪŋ.ɡəʊ/ — source labels: UK
- IPA: /ˈbɪŋ.ɡoʊ/ — source labels: US

### Forms

- bingos — plural
- Bingo — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A game of chance for two or more players, who mark off numbers on a grid as they are announced by the caller; the game is won by the first person to call out "bingo!" or "house!" after crossing off all numbers on the grid or in one line of the grid.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** He plays bingo with his family on Saturdays.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A similar game or amusement in which participants tick off themed words, phrases or pictures as these are called out, or as they are mentioned, for example during a speech or performance

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A win in such a game.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** There were two bingos in the last game, so the players split the prize money.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A play where all seven of a player's letter tiles are played, awarding a score bonus.

   **Usage:** US; countable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Scrabble; board-games; games

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Great, sudden, typically hard-to-predict success; the jackpot.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** housey-housey — game of chance; lotto — game of chance
