### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To pass (one) unnoticed so that one is unaffected by it.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The news of the actor's death just washed over me, as I had never particularly cared for him.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To affect the emotions of (a person) suddenly and overwhelmingly.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Embarrassment washed over me as I realized I had forgotten the notes for my presentation.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To surge over the banks, or other retaining structure.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The river washed over the embankment at several places.
