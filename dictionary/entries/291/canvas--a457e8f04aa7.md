### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A type of coarse cloth, woven from hemp (traditionally) or from cotton and polyesters, useful for making sails, tents, and overcoats or as a surface for paintings.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A piece of such cloth stretched across a frame on which one may paint an artwork.

   **Usage:** painting

   **Example:** She is painting a moonlight scene on the canvas.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A mesh of loosely woven cotton strands or molded plastic to be decorated with needlepoint, cross-stitch, rug hooking, or other crafts.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A basis for creative work.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** The author takes rural midwestern life as a canvas for a series of tightly woven character studies

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A region on which graphics can be rendered.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computer-graphics; computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Sails in general.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A tent.

   **Example:** He spent the night under canvas.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A rough draft or model of a song, air, or other literary or musical composition; especially one to show a poet the measure of the verses he is to make.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. Athletic shoes.
