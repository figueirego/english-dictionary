## Adjective · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- enPR: răn'dəm — variety unspecified in the source labels
- IPA: /ˈɹændəm/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Forms

- more random — comparative
- most random — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Occurring for no particular reason; haphazard, unpredictable.

   **Example:** Our city is plagued by random acts of violence.

   **Example:** random acts of kindness

   **Source synonyms:** desultory

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Involving an outcome which is impossible to predict, but which may be represented by a probability distribution; in the ideal case, involving outcomes which are equally likely.

   **Example:** The flip of a fair coin is purely random.

   **Example:** The newspaper conducted a random sample of five hundred American teenagers.

   **Example:** The results of the field survey look random by several different measures.

   **Example:** A toss of a loaded die is still random, though biased.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences; statistics

   **Source synonyms:** aleatory; stochastic

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. Pseudorandom; mimicking the result of random selection.

   **Example:** The rand function generates a random number from a seed.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. Selected for no particular reason; arbitrary; unspecified.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** A random American off the street couldn't tell the difference.

   **Example:** You're just going to trust the word of random people on the Internet?

   **Example:** I didn't have time to peruse the resturant's menu, so I just ordered some random dish.

   **Example:** Whatever random hobby you're into, there's an Internet community for it.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. Being (part of) a varied, unrelated, and apparently arbitrary collection of things; diverse, heterogeneous.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** My notebook has turned into a random collection of thoughts.

   **Example:** I brought a bunch of random snacks, but nothing nutritious.

   **Example:** I mixed a bunch of random vegetables into a salad, and it actually turned out pretty good.

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. Apropos of nothing; lacking context, relevance, or any connection to the previous situation; unexpected.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** This random guy just came up to me to say that he was a fan of my work.

   **Example:** Thank you for that completely random comment... now, let's get back to our actual topic of discussion.

   **Example:** The teacher's bartending story was interesting, but very random.

   **Example:** I feel like pineapple is a very random thing to put on a pizza.

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

**Source sense R2.S7**

7. Of a person&#58; characterized by or often saying random things; habitually using non sequiturs.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** You're so random! I never know what you're going to do next.

<a id="r2-s8"></a>

**Source sense R2.S8**

8. Being out of the ordinary; unusual or unexpected; odd, strange, bizarre.

   **Usage:** UK; slang

   **Example:** That's a rather random fact!

   **Example:** I can't believe he would say that. That's so random!
