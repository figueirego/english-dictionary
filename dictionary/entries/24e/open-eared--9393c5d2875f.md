### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Listening closely to something; attentive.

   **Example:** Companies should be open-eared to feedback from their customers.

   **Source antonyms:** closed-eared

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Being receptive or open-minded to new styles of music.

   **Example:** Many people tend to listen to what they know, but others are more open-eared.

   **Source antonyms:** closed-eared

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Synonym of open-back (“partially covering the ear”).
