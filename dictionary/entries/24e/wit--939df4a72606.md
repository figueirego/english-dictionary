### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Sanity.

   **Usage:** countable; plural-normally; uncountable

   **Example:** He's gone completely out of his wits.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The senses.

   **Usage:** countable; plural-normally; uncountable

   **Example:** Keep your wits about you.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Intellectual ability; faculty of thinking, reasoning.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Where she has gone to is beyond the wit of man to say.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The ability to think quickly; mental cleverness, especially under short time constraints.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** My father had a quick wit and a steady hand.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Intelligence; common sense.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The opportunity was right in front of you, and you didn't even have the wit to take it!

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Humour, especially when clever or quick.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The best man's speech was hilarious, full of wit and charm.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A person who tells funny anecdotes or jokes; someone witty.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Your friend is quite a wit, isn't he?
