## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A method of comparing, or measuring, forces, electric currents, etc., by so opposing them that the pointer of an indicating apparatus, or the needle of a galvanometer, remains at, or is brought to, zero, as contrasted with methods in which the deflection is observed directly.
