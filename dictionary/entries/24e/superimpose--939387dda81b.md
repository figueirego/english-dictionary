### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To place an object over another object, usually in such a way that both will be visible.

   **Example:** He superimposed the company logo over the image.

   **Source synonyms:** overlay; superpose

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
