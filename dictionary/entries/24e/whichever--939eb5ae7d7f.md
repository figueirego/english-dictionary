## Determiner · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /wɪt͡ʃˈɛvɚ/ — source labels: General-American
- IPA: /ʍɪt͡ʃˈɛvɚ/ — source labels: General-American

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Which ever; emphatic form of 'which'.

   **Usage:** interrogative

   **Example:** Good heavens! Whichever button did you press to make that happen?!

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Regardless of the ... that; no matter which.

   **Example:** It won't work, whichever buttons you press.

   **Example:** Whichever book you borrow, be sure to return it to the library on time.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any or either ... that; the ... that.

   **Example:** Choose whichever card you like.

   **Example:** Borrow whichever books interest you.

   **Example:** I'll buy whichever shirt is cheapest.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. According to or depending upon which.

   **Example:** I'll buy the red shirt or the blue shirt, whichever one is cheaper.

   **Example:** If the work is of joint authorship, the copyright expires 50 years after the death of the author who first dies, or upon the death of the author who dies last, whichever period is longer.

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Which ever; emphatic form of 'which'.

   **Usage:** interrogative

   **Example:** These chocolates all look delicious. Whichever shall I choose?

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Irrespective of the one(s) that; no matter which one(s).

   **Example:** Whichever you pick, be content with it.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. Any or either one(s) that; the one(s) that.

   **Example:** I have a few books; please borrow whichever interest you.

   **Example:** They have lots of shirts, but I'll buy whichever is cheapest.

   **Example:** Whichever of you gets here first will get the prize.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. Any or either one(s).

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** I don't mind which you have. Take whichever.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. According to or depending upon which one(s).
