### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see go, west.

   **Example:** Go west until you hit the ocean, then turn left.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Synonym of set&#58; to move toward the western horizon, to go down.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of the sun

   **Source synonyms:** set (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Synonym of die.

   **Usage:** dated; figuratively

   **Example:** Och, your ma's gone west, dearie. You'll hear her voice no more.

   **Source synonyms:** die (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To be lost, destroyed, or otherwise ruined.

   **Usage:** dated; figuratively

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To move to the American West, particularly (historical) during the 19th century era.

   **Usage:** US

   **Example:** Go west, young man!
