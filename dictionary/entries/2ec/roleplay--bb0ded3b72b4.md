### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To act out a scenario or fantasy with another person or other people.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** To spice up their sex life, they like to roleplay.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To act as a character as part of a fantasy, especially with a group.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He likes to roleplay a vampire.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To act out a social role, especially one that one does not truly belong to.

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** He talks like he's some scrappy bootstrap entrepreneur but he's just some old money rich kid roleplaying.
