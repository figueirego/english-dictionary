**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Pertaining to or relating to the church, its government, forms, or ceremonies; ecclesiastical.

   **Source synonyms:** churchical; churchlike; churchical; churchish (rare); churchlike; churchly; churchy; ecclesial; ecclesiastic; ecclesiastical

<a id="r1-s3"></a>
