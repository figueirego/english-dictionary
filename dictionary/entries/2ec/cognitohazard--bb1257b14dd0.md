**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A sensory signal (such as an image or sound) that directly causes harmful or undesired physiological effects to one who senses or perceives it.

   **Example:** The symbol on that piece of paper is a cognitohazard which puts everyone who reads it in a week-long coma.
