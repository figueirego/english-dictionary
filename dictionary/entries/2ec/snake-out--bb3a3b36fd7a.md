## Verb

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To move outwards like a snake.

   **Usage:** literally

   **Example:** His hand was snaking out from the window.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To remove by dragging out, as removing a snake from its hole.

   **Usage:** US; archaic; slang; transitive

   **Example:** Then snake out the item from its container.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To crack a whip so as to wrap around a target.

   **Usage:** US; archaic; slang; transitive

   **Example:** Snake out the horse's legs.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To unclog a drain using a plumbing snake.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The plumber snaked out the drain.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To inform; to rat

   **Usage:** Multicultural-London-English; African-American Vernacular
