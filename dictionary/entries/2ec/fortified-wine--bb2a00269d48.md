### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A type of wine to which a distilled spirit, typically brandy or a neutral grape spirit, has been added in order to elevate its alcohol content.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Fortified wines are usually drunk as aperitifs, digestifs, or liqueurs.

   **Source synonyms:** liqueur wine
