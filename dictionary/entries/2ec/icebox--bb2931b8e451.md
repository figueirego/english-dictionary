## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A box or compartment containing ice, typically used to keep provisions cool.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A compartment in a refrigerator that is colder than the rest of the refrigerator and is used as a freezer.

   **Usage:** UK

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A refrigerator.

   **Usage:** US; dated

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A city with a cold or snowy climate.

   **Usage:** US; slang

   **Example:** I moved here from the icebox of the upper Midwest.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A prison.

   **Usage:** US; slang

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A morgue.

   **Usage:** US; slang
