<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The people spoken, or written to, as an object.

   **Usage:** nominative; objective; plural; second-person; singular; object pronoun

   **Example:** Both of you should get ready now.

   **Example:** I'll look up you and your sister.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. (To) yourselves, (to) yourself.

   **Usage:** US; colloquial; nominative; objective; plural; pronoun; reflexive; second-person; singular

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The person spoken to or written to, as an object. (Replacing thee; originally as a mark of respect.)

   **Usage:** nominative; objective; plural; second-person; singular; object pronoun

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The people spoken to or written to, as a subject. (Replacing ye.)

   **Usage:** nominative; objective; plural; pronoun; second-person; singular; subjective

   **Example:** You are all supposed to do as I tell you.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The person spoken to or written to, as a subject. (Originally as a mark of respect.)

   **Usage:** nominative; objective; plural; pronoun; second-person; singular; subjective

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A person's favorite sports team.

   **Usage:** colloquial; nominative; objective; plural; pronoun; second-person; singular; subjective

   **Example:** I get that you're from Southeast Michigan, but I'm still surprised that you're a Detroit Lions fan. You have been on the receiving end of losing seasons for a while now.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Anyone, one; an unspecified individual or group of individuals (as subject or object).

   **Usage:** indefinite; nominative; objective; personal; plural; pronoun; second-person; singular

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A dummy pronoun used in certain constructions, usually with verbs of receiving (such as get or find) or sensing (such as see or hear), typically stating the existence or typicality of something.

   **Usage:** indefinite; nominative; objective; personal; plural; pronoun; second-person; singular

   **Example:** You get a lot of rude people in London.

   **Example:** It was one of those small-town dive bars you often read about.
