### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Without hope; despairing; not expecting anything positive.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Giving no ground of hope; promising nothing desirable; desperate.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Without talent, not skilled.

   **Example:** He's a hopeless writer, but can draw very well.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Of an adverse condition, incurable.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** She is a hopeless romantic.

   **Example:** He is a hopeless idler.
