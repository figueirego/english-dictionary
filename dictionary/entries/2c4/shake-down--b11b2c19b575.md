### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To cause something to fall down by shaking it, or something it is attached to.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** shake down apples from an apple tree.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To shake someone so money falls from their pockets.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To extort money from (someone) by means of threats.

   **Usage:** broadly; slang; transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To search exhaustively.

   **Usage:** slang; transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**
