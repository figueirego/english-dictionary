### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To rebel, particularly against authority.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The farmers had to revolt against the government to get what they deserved.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To repel greatly.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Your brother revolts me!

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To be disgusted, shocked, or grossly offended; hence, to feel nausea; used with at.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The stomach revolts at such food; his nature revolts at cruelty.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To cause to turn back; to roll or drive back; to put to flight.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To turn away; to abandon or reject something; specifically, to turn away, or shrink, with abhorrence.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. to perform a revolution in Tycoon, reversing the card hierarchy

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** card-games; games

   **Source antonyms:** counterrevolt
