### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An English surname from the Celtic Parisi tribe of Yorkshire, the name of uncertain origin. Koch proposes derivation from Proto-Celtic \*kʷert (“make, cause”), from Proto-Indo-European \*kʷer- (compare Welsh peri).

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An English habitational surname, a variant of Paris.
