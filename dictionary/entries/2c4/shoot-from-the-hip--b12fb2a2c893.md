**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To discharge a firearm while it is held near the hip, without taking time to aim via the gunsights.

   **Usage:** literally

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To speak or act quickly based on first impressions, without carefully studying the background information, wider context, etc.

   **Usage:** figuratively
