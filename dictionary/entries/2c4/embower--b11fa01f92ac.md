### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To enclose something or someone as if in a bower; shelter with foliage.

   **Usage:** poetic; transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To lodge or rest in or as in a bower.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** But the small birds in their wide boughs embowring / Chaunted their sundrie tunes with sweete consent;
