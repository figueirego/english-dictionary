### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The stock of an item on hand at a particular location or business.

   **Usage:** operations

   **Example:** Due to an undersized inventory at the Boston outlet, customers had to travel to Providence to find the item.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A detailed list of all of the items on hand.

   **Usage:** operations

   **Example:** The inventory included several items that one wouldn't normally think to find at a cheese shop.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The process of producing or updating such a list.

   **Usage:** operations

   **Example:** This month's inventory took nearly three days.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A space containing the items available to a character, especially in a video game, for immediate use.

   **Example:** You can't get through the underground tunnel if there are more than three items in your inventory.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The total set of a (specified) linguistic feature (within a language etc.)

   **Usage:** especially

   **Example:** Germanic languages have a marked tendency towards large vocalic inventories.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; phonology; sciences
