## Verb · Etymology 2 · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈænɛks/ — source labels: UK
- IPA: /əˈnɛks/ — source labels: US
- IPA: /ˈænɛks/ — source labels: US

### Forms

- annexes — present; singular; third-person
- annexing — participle; present
- annexed — participle; past
- annexed — past
- annexe — alternative; UK
- anex — alternative; nonstandard

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To add something to another thing, especially territory; to incorporate.

   **Example:** The ancient city of Petra was annexed by Rome.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To attach or connect, as a consequence, condition, etc.

   **Example:** to annex a penalty to a prohibition, or punishment to guilt

   **Example:** Annexed to my feeling of guilt was a sense of having let my familiy down.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To join; to be united.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To take something without permission (informal)

   **Example:** I returned to resume my place but my assistant had already annexed my chair.

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** underjoin

**Source antonyms:** separate
