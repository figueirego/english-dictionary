**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act or process of making somebody or something proletarian.

   **Usage:** British; English; Oxford; US; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The act or process of making somebody or something proletarian.
   1. The social process whereby people move from being either employers, unemployed, or self-employed to being employed as wage labour by employers.

      **Usage:** British; English; Marxism; Oxford; US; countable; uncountable

      **Source synonyms:** proletarization

      **Source antonyms:** bourgeoisification; embourgeoisement
