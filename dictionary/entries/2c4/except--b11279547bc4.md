## Conjunction · Record 3

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ɪkˈsɛpt/ — source labels: Received-Pronunciation
- IPA: /ɛkˈsɛpt/ — source labels: Received-Pronunciation

- IPA: /əkˈsɛpt/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Forms

- excepte — alternative; archaic; rare

### Senses

<a id="r3-s1"></a>

**Source sense R3.S1**

1. Used to introduce a clause, phrase, verb infinitive, adverb or other non-noun complement forming an exception or qualification to something previously stated.

   **Example:** You look a bit like my sister, except (that) she has longer hair.

   **Example:** I never made fun of her except teasingly.

   **Example:** To survive, I did everything except steal.

   **Example:** Come any time except between ten and twelve.

<a id="r3-s2"></a>

**Source sense R3.S2**

2. Loosely, used to introduce a contrastive statement explaining why something wasn't successful, didn't happen, etc.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** They fired tear gas at us, except the wind was blowing the wrong way.

   **Example:** I almost walked out, except I remembered the promise I had made.

<a id="r3-s3"></a>

**Source sense R3.S3**

3. Unless; used to introduce a hypothetical case in which an exception may exist.

   **Usage:** archaic
