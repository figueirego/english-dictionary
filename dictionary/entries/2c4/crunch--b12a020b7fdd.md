### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To crush something, especially food, with a noisy crackling sound.

   **Example:** When I came home, Susan was watching TV with her feet up on the couch, crunching a piece of celery.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To be crushed with a noisy crackling sound.

   **Example:** Beetles crunched beneath the men's heavy boots as they worked.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To calculate or otherwise process (e.g. to crunch numbers&#58; to perform mathematical calculations). Presumably from the sound made by mechanical calculators.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Example:** That metadata makes it much easier for the search engine to crunch the data for queries.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To grind or press with violence and noise.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To emit a grinding or crunching noise.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To cause the gears to emit a crunching sound by releasing the clutch before the gears are properly synchronised.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** automotive; transport; vehicles

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To compress (data) using a particular algorithm, so that it can be restored by decrunching.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences
