### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The state of being difficult, or hard to do.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** My mom is old and has a lot of difficulty (in) remembering all her grandchildren's names.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An obstacle that hinders achievement of a goal.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** We faced a difficulty in trying to book a flight so late.

   **Source synonyms:** trouble

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Physical danger from the environment, especially with risk of drowning

   **Usage:** countable; in-plural; sometimes; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An objection.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. That which cannot be easily understood or believed.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. An awkward situation or quarrel.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
