### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To apply pressure (to something) from two or more sides at once.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** I squeezed the ball between my hands.

   **Example:** Please don't squeeze the toothpaste tube in the middle.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To embrace closely; to give a tight hug to.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To fit into a tight place.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** I managed to squeeze the car into that parking space.

   **Example:** Can you squeeze through that gap?

   **Example:** I squeezed myself through the crowd.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To remove something with difficulty, or apparent difficulty.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He squeezed some money out of his wallet.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To barely manage to.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** I managed to squeeze through the exam with a D.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To put in a difficult position by presenting two or more choices.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I'm being squeezed between my job and my volunteer work.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To oppress with hardships, burdens, or taxes; to harass.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To attempt to score a runner from third by bunting.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Jones squeezed in Smith with a perfect bunt.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; baseball; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports
