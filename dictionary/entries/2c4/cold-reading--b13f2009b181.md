### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The technique, or an instance, of using likely guesses and assumptions, then narrowing in on any positive responses, in order to give the impression of having information about a person or event.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The self-described psychic was merely using cold reading.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see cold, reading.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Acting auditions often include cold reading.
