### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Corn (maize) harvested before reaching full maturity.

   **Usage:** uncountable; originally

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A street food dish originating in Central America, but now also popular in the United States, consisting of corn on the cob which is grilled or boiled and served on a stick, typically with toppings such as cheese, chili powder, mayonnaise, and lime juice.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** street corn
