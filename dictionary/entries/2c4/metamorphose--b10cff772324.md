### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of a moth or insect, to undergo metamorphosis.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To undergo some transformation.

   **Usage:** broadly; intransitive

   **Source synonyms:** metamorphize; metamorphosize; metamorphize; metamorphose; metamorphosize (nonstandard); transform; transmogrify; transmute
