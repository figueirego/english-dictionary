**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A member of the racial classification of humanity composed of peoples native to North Asia, East Asia, Southeast Asia, Pacific Oceania, the Americas, and parts of Central Asia and South Asia (and sometimes eastern Fennoscandia) – as well as their descendants in other parts of the world.

   **Usage:** dated; offensive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anthropology; human-sciences; sciences
