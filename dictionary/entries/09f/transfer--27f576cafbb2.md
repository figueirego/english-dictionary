### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To move or pass from one place, person or thing to another.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to transfer the laws of one country to another; to transfer suspicion

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To convey the impression of (something) from one surface to another.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to transfer drawings or engravings to a lithographic stone

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To exit one mass transit vehicle and board another (typically one belonging to a different line or mode of transportation) to continue a journey.

   **Example:** transfer to the Blue Line

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** transport

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a traveler

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To be or become transferred.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To arrange for something to belong to or be officially controlled by somebody else.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The title to land is transferred by deed.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To 'carry' or apply anything (a concept, a name...) from one type of thing to a different type of thing.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The ancient Romans named the planet Mars by transferring that name from a deity of their pantheon to the planet appearing in the night sky.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To move from a wheelchair to another seating surface, or to a wheelchair from another seating surface.

   **Usage:** intransitive
