### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A valve used to turn something off.

   **Example:** The fire raged for hours because they were unable to reach the shutoff for the gas line.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The act of turning something off.

   **Example:** The letter warned of an impending shutoff if payment was not made promptly.
