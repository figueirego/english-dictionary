### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A large criminal organization, or a group of such organizations, that deals in illegal goods and services.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Organized crime is largely responsible for the nation’s illicit drug trafficking.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The crimes perpetrated by those organizations.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Organized crime has dramatically increased in our neighbourhood.
