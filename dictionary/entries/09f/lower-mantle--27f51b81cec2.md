## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The part of the Earth's mantle from 660 to 2890 km (410 to 1800 miles) below Earth's surface, between the transition zone and the outer core; it occupies about 56% of the total volume of the Earth.

   **Source synonyms:** inner mantle; mesosphere

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any similar layer in an exoplanet.

   **Source synonyms:** inner mantle
