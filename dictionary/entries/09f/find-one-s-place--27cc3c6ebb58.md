### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To discover one's vocation, purpose, and/or sense of belonging to or passion for something.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** He's apparently found his place at the monastery.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To locate where (in a work of print) (one) had most recently stopped reading, in order to resume reading.

   **Example:** I wanna pick up where I left off, but I can't seem to find my place.

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** find one's calling — to discover a sense of belonging or passion; find purpose; find one's spot — to return to one's stopping point

**Source antonyms:** lose one's place — to become desynchronized from a last-read passage
