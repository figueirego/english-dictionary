### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An act or instance of annulling.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** marriage annulment

   **Example:** grant an annulment

   **Example:** petition for annulment

   **Example:** The marriage was declared void by an official annulment.

   **Example:** They sought an annulment instead of a divorce.

   **Source synonyms:** abolition; cancellation; nullification; abolition; abrogation; annulment; cancellation; deauthentication; devalidation; invalidation; nullification; renunciation; revocation; termination; vitiation

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The state of having been annulled.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An invalidation of something, especially a legal contract.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A legal (notably judicial) declaration that a marriage is invalid; the procedure leading to it.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Catholicism; Christianity; law
