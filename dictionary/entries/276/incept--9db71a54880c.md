<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Usage:** transitive

2. To begin.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The company was incepted in 2006.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To accept or to be accepted to the Master of Arts degree at Oxford or Cambridge University.

   **Usage:** UK; ambitransitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To put an idea into a person's mind so deeply that they believe it was their own.

   **Usage:** transitive
