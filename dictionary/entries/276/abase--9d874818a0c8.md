### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To lower, as in condition in life, office, rank, etc., so as to cause pain or hurt feelings; to degrade, to depress, to humble, to humiliate.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** degrade; demean; depress; discredit; disgrace; dishonor; humble; humiliate; sink; abase; bring down to size (idiomatic); cut down to size (idiomatic); debase (archaic); defame; degrade; demean; demote; depress; discredit; disgrace; dishonour; disparage; downgrade; embase (archaic); give a bad name; humble; humiliate; knock down to size (idiomatic); put someone in their place (idiomatic); reduce; relegate; sink (figuratively); slur; trim down to size (idiomatic); whittle down to size (idiomatic)

   **Source antonyms:** aggrandise; dignify; elevate; exalt; extol; honor; promote; raise; uplift; advance; aggrandize; dignify; elevate; ennoble; exalt; honor; nobilitate; prefer (dated); promote; raise; uplift

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To lower physically; to depress; to cast or throw down; to stoop.

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive

   **Example:** to abase the eye

   **Source synonyms:** bring down; lower; reduce; abase (archaic); abate; belittle; besmall (rare); bring down; embase (obsolete); cut; decrease; decrement; diminish; foreshorten; lessen; lilliputianize; lower; mince; minimize; reduce; shrink; smallify

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To lower in value, in particular by altering the content of alloys in coins; to debase.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** adulterate; debauch; degrade; devalue; downgrade; abase (obsolete); adulter (rare); adulterate; adulterize (uncommon); alloy; balderdash (archaic); corrupt; bastardize; dash (dated); dilute; embase (archaic); sophisticate (figuratively); abase; avile (obsolete); debase; debauch; degrade; depreciate; devalue; devaluate; dirty; elevate (Latinism; obsolete); vitiate
