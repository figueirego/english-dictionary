## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The remains of a shipwreck still floating in the water.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. That which has been discharged from a ship or boat, especially on the ocean or a sea, (flotsam unintentionally and jetsam intentionally).

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A collection of miscellaneous items or fragments of little importance.

   **Usage:** figuratively; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. People considered to be of little worth.

   **Usage:** figuratively; uncountable

   **Example:** The flotsam and jetsam of society were at the night club.
