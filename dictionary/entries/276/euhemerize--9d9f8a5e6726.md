### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To invent a plausible but fanciful historical origin for something in order to rationalize mythology and legends.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive; derogatory

   **Example:** Confucian scholars frequently euhemerized the myths used to explain religious rituals to the public.

   **Source synonyms:** demythologize; undeify\[personage\]\]s are the direct objects&gt;

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To create mythology and legends from ancient historical figures and events.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** The Yellow Emperor was perhaps euhemerized from a prehistoric tribal leader.

   **Source synonyms:** mythologize; deify\[personage\]\]s are the direct objects&gt;

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** rationalize — fanciful creation of rational explanations for the supernatural
