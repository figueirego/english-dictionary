### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A term formed from the initial letters of several words or parts of words, which is itself pronounced letter by letter.

   **Example:** The term BBC is an initialism for the British Broadcasting Corporation.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** alphabetism

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
