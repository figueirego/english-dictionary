### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Sharp and harsh, or bitter and not to the taste.

   **Source synonyms:** pungent; acrimonious (archaic)

   **Source antonyms:** delectable; delicious; tasteful

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Causing heat and irritation.

   **Example:** The bombardier beetle sprays acrid secretions to defend itself.

   **Source synonyms:** corrosive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Caustic; bitter; bitterly irritating.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** That man has an acrid temper.

   **Source synonyms:** acerbic; acrimonious
