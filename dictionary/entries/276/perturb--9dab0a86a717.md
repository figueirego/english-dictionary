### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To cause (something) to be physically disordered or disturbed; to cause confusion.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** derange; disturb; unsettle; addle; baffle; vex; lose; embroil; stick; stump; baffound; bamboozle; bedevil; befuddle; throw; bemud (figuratively); bemuse; bewilder; bumfuzzle; complicate; confound; confuddle (slang); confuse; confuzzle (childish; slang); dazzle; discombobulate; [disconcert](../05f/disconcert--17d1192b726d.md); blind; dumbfound; entangle (figuratively); ferhoodle; flabagast; flabbergast; flabbergaster; flurry; fluster; flummox; fuddle; jumble; knot (obsolete); mix up; muddle; muddy (figuratively); ravel; perplex; puzzle

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To disturb (someone, their mind, etc.) mentally; to bother, trouble, upset.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** agitate; distress; unsettle; agitate; freak; sting; bother; bring down; discompose; unstring; [disconcert](../05f/disconcert--17d1192b726d.md); enturbulate (nonstandard); disturb; mortify (obsolete); perturb; put out; ruffle; throw off balance; unsettle; upset; flurry

   **Source antonyms:** besoothe; cheer; comfort; console; solace; soothe

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of a celestial body&#58; to modify the motion or orbit of (another celestial body) by exerting a gravitational force; hence (physics), to slightly modify (the motion of an object).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astronomy; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To slightly modify (a set of equations or their solutions), producing deviations from a simple, easily solvable problem, in order to find an approximate solution to a problem that is more difficult to solve or otherwise unsolvable.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To influence (a process or system) so that it deviates from its normal state.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To bother, to disturb, to trouble.

   **Usage:** intransitive
