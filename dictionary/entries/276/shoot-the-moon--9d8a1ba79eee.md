### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To attain great heights, a high value, or a numerically high measurement.

   **Usage:** broadly; figuratively

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To achieve the lowest or highest score possible, such that the player is usually rewarded with bonus points.

   **Example:** In our last hand of pinochle, Leon and Janet shot the moon, taking all 50 tricks.

   **Example:** When Randy took the queen of spades on the last trick in our game of hearts, he shot the moon.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** card-games; games

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To abscond without paying one's rent.

   **Usage:** slang

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see shoot, moon.
