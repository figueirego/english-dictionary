## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A time of vacation from school that occurs during the spring.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The week of vacation from school that occurs during the winter-spring semester, which does not necessarily fall within the official season of spring.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; uncountable
