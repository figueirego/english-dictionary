### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To halt something temporarily.

   **Example:** The meeting was suspended for lunch.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To hold in an undetermined or undecided state.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To discontinue or interrupt a function, task, position, or event.

   **Example:** to suspend a thread of execution in a computer program

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To hang freely; underhang.

   **Example:** to suspend a ball by a thread

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To bring a solid substance, usually in powder form, into suspension in a liquid.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To make to depend.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To debar, or cause to withdraw temporarily, from any privilege, from the execution of an office, from the enjoyment of income, etc.

   **Example:** to suspend a student from college; to suspend a member of a club

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To support in a liquid, as an insoluble powder, by stirring, to facilitate chemical action.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To remove the value of an unused coupon from an air ticket, typically so as to allow continuation of the next sectors' travel.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** aeronautics; aerospace; aviation; business; engineering; lifestyle; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; tourism; transport; travel
