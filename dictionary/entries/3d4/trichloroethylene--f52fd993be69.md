**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The unsaturated chlorinated hydrocarbon CCl₂=CHCl used as an industrial solvent and formerly as an anaesthetic.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The nurse gave the woman in labour trichloroethylene, a chemical also used to clean metal parts.
