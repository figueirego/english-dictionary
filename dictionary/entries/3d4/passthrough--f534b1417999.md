### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act or process of passing through.
   1. As of a signal through a device or network.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; computing; electrical-engineering; electricity; electromagnetism; electronics; energy; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; networking; physical-sciences; physics; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The act or process of passing through.
   1. As of increased costs through a business entity to its customers through increased prices.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Example:** The airlines called the fuel surcharge a passthrough, but did not rescind it until well after fuel prices had returned to previous levels.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; economics; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The act or process of passing through.
   1. Allowing the use of some resource unchanged from one system to another.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A wall opening intended to allow something to be passed through it.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A legal entity intended to not incur (income) taxation at the entity, but solely at the beneficiaries.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Partnerships, limited-liability companies, and Subchapter S corporations are the main US passthroughs.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** economics; government; sciences; taxation

   **Source synonyms:** flowthrough
