2. In the shape of, or moving in, a circle.

   **Source synonyms:** annular; ring-shaped; O-shaped; oval; round; toroid; ringlike; round-like

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Circuitous or roundabout.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Referring back to itself, so as to prevent computation or comprehension; infinitely recursive.

   **Example:** circular reasoning

   **Example:** Your dictionary defines "brave" as "courageous", and "courageous" as "brave". That's a circular definition.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Distributed to a large number of persons.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Perfect; complete.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Adhering to a fixed circle of legends; cyclic; hence, mean; inferior.

   **Usage:** archaic

### Related words
