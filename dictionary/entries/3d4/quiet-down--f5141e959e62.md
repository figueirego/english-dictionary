### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To become quieter.

   **Usage:** US; intransitive

   **Example:** As the lights dimmed the general noise quieted down.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To make someone or something become quieter.

   **Usage:** US; transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To diminish in intensity.

   **Usage:** US; intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** Diplomacy can only begin when the violence quiets down.
