### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To attempt to complete a puzzle in which dots on a paper must be connected in a specific order so that the joining lines form a picture.

   **Usage:** literally

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To make connections in one's mind, in order to arrive at a more holistic understanding of a situation.

   **Usage:** broadly; idiomatic

   **Example:** He joked in class that "Hitler's killer was his hero," but the teacher didn't connect the dots so his comment went unpunished.
