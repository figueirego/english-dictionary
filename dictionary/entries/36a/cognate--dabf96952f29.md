## Noun · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈkɒɡn(e)ɪt/ — source labels: UK
- IPA: /ˈkɑɡn(e)ɪt/ — source labels: US

### Forms

- cognates — plural
- cogn. — alternative; abbreviation

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. One of a number of things allied in origin or nature.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. One who is related to another on the female side.

   **Usage:** dated

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. One who is related to another, both having descended from a common ancestor through legal marriages.

   **Usage:** dated

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. A word either descended from the same base word of the same ancestor language as the given word, or judged to be a regular reflex of the same reconstructed root of proto-language as the given word.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences
