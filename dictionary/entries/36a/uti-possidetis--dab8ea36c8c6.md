## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The principle of a treaty which leaves belligerents mutually in possession of the acquisitions they have gained by force during the war.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A kind of interdict granted to one person who claimed possession of a land, in order that he be declared the legal possessor, and that the other party in the suit must not take the land by force.

   **Usage:** Ancient-Rome; historical

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law
