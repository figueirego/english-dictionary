### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of the genus Naja of venomous snakes that can spread their neck ribs into a broad hood when threatened; a true cobra.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Certain other snakes of the family Elapidae.
   1. A ring-necked spitting cobra or rinkhals (Hemachatus haemachatus).

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Certain other snakes of the family Elapidae.
   1. A king cobra (Ophiophagus hannah).

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Certain other snakes of the family Elapidae.
   1. A tree cobra (genus Pseudohaje).

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Certain other snakes of the family Elapidae.
   1. A shield-nosed cobra/coral cobra (genus Aspidelaps).

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Certain other snakes of the family Elapidae.
   1. A black desert cobra (genus Walterinnesia).

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Certain other snakes of the family Elapidae.
   1. An American cobra (Micrurus fulvius).

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A false water cobra, Hydrodynastes gigas.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A type of lanyard knot, thought to resemble a snake in its shape.
