### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any plant that, although not belonging to the grass family, produces seeds that are used to make flour.

   **Example:** Buckwheat, quinoa and amaranth are pseudocereals.

   **Source synonyms:** pseudograin
