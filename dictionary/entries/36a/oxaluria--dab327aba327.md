### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The presence of oxalic acid and oxalate in the urine (which is often true, in some amount or other).

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; physiology; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The presence of oxalic acid and oxalate in the urine (which is often true, in some amount or other).
   1. The presence of excess oxalic acid and oxalate in the urine (as can happen with an oxalate-rich diet and/or metabolic problems).

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; physiology; sciences

      **Source synonyms:** hyperoxaluria
