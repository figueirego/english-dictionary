### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of various species of Anthospermum (Rubiaceae), a succulent evergreen shrub whose leaves release a fragrant odour when bruised, especially Anthospermum aethiopicum

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Hymenaea verrucosa (Fabaceae), a New World tree producing copal.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
