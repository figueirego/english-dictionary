### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Really, in effect; not just hypothetically.

   **Example:** The new tax should have helped the farming community. But in practice farmers generally appear to be worse off than before.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Regularly practising an activity so as to remain skilled in it.
