**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** I need some help with my homework.

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Something or someone which provides assistance with a task.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** He was a great help to me when I was moving house.

   **Usage:** countable; usually

   **Example:** I've printed out a list of math helps.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. One or more people employed to help in the maintenance of a house or the operation of a farm or enterprise.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** The help is coming round this morning to clean.

   **Example:** Most of the hired help is seasonal, for the harvest.

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To provide assistance to (someone or something).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He helped his grandfather cook breakfast.

   **Source synonyms:** see through

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To assist (a person) in getting something, especially food or drink at table; used with to.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** It is polite to help your guests to food before serving yourself.

   **Example:** Help yourself to whatever's in the fridge.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To contribute in some way to.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The white paint on the walls helps make the room look brighter.

   **Example:** If you want to get a job, it helps to have some prior experience.

   **Example:** I've already taken three pills, but they don't seem to help my headache.

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To provide assistance.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** She was struggling with the groceries, so I offered to help.

   **Example:** Please, help!

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To avoid; to prevent; to refrain from; to restrain (oneself). Usually used in nonassertive contexts with can.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Usage:** Hong-Kong; Singapore
