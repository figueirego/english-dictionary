## Noun

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˌswɪsˈɑɹmiˌnaɪf/ — source labels: General-American

### Forms

- Swiss Army knives — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of a class of multi-function pocket knives or multitools, having a blade and various tools, such as screwdrivers and can openers.

   **Example:** She carries a Swiss Army knife for the convenience of having dozens of small tools handy at any moment.

   **Source synonyms:** Swiss Army penknife

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A tool or approach that has many functions, one for every perceivable need.

   **Usage:** broadly; figuratively

   **Example:** This software has been a good Swiss Army knife for us as we rebuild our systems.
