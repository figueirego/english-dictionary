### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The interior or inner part.

   **Example:** The inside of the building has been extensively restored.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The left-hand side of a road if one drives on the left, or right-hand side if one drives on the right.

   **Example:** On a motorway, you should never pass another vehicle on the inside.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The side of a curved road, racetrack etc. that has the shorter arc length; the side of a racetrack nearer the interior of the course or some other point of reference.

   **Example:** The car in front drifted wide on the bend, so I darted up the inside to take the lead.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The interior organs of the body, especially the guts.

   **Usage:** colloquial; in-plural

   **Example:** Eating that stuff will damage your insides.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A passenger within a coach or carriage, as distinguished from one upon the outside.

   **Usage:** UK; colloquial; dated

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The inside scoop; information known only to certain involved people.

   **Usage:** slang
