### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of various plants with shaggy tomentose leaves
   1. species of Culcitium and Espeletia in South America

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of various plants with shaggy tomentose leaves
