## Noun

### Forms

- death grips — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An extremely tight grip.

   **Example:** The walker held with a death grip to his stick lest he drop it down the hill.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An extremely tight grip.
   1. The grip of something dead or dying.

      **Example:** I gently tore the bloodied piece of paper out from her death grip.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An extremely tight grip.
   1. A grip that kills.

      **Example:** The wolf held the chicken in a death grip until it stopped moving.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An extremely tight grip.
   1. A tight grip on the penis during masturbation.

      **Usage:** slang

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A perilous situation or stalemate where failure or death is imminent.

   **Example:** The armies came together in a death grip at the bottom of the valley.
