## Noun

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈpɪmpənɛl/ — source labels: UK

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A plant of the genus Pimpinella, especially burnet saxifrage (Pimpinella saxifraga).

   **Usage:** archaic

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of various plants of the genus Anagallis, having small red, white or purple flowers, especially the scarlet pimpernel (Anagallis arvensis).

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Sanguisorba spp.
   1. Great burnet (Sanguisorba officinalis)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Sanguisorba spp.
   1. Salad burnet (Sanguisorba minor).

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A yellow pimpernel (Taenidia integerrima)

   **Usage:** Canada; US

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Someone resembling the fictional Scarlet Pimpernel; a gallant dashing resourceful man given to remarkable feats of bravery and derring-do in liberating victims of tyranny and injustice.

   **Usage:** figuratively
