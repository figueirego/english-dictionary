### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To retrace one's steps.

   **Example:** I dropped my sunglasses and had to backtrack to find them.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To repeat or review work already done.

   **Example:** If we backtrack through this problem, maybe we can figure out where we went wrong.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To taxi down an active runway in the opposite direction to that being used for takeoff.

   **Example:** Speedbird One&#58; enter and backtrack Runway 27 Left.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** aeronautics; aerospace; aviation; business; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To exercise a racehorse around the racetrack in the opposite direction to that in which races are run.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To go back on or withdraw a statement.

   **Example:** After enormous public outrage, the government backtracked on its proposed ban.

   **Source synonyms:** backpedal
