### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A system in broadcasting and recording studios that enables the director to communicate with others (host, presenter, anchor) during performance.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Communication from members of the public to the makers of a broadcast transmission while it is happening.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; uncountable
