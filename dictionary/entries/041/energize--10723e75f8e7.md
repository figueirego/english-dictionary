### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To invigorate; to make energetic.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To supply with energy, especially electricity; to turn on power to (something).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Whenever we energize that circuit we blow a fuse.
