### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A large and heavy saucepan or frying pan with legs and, usually, a long handle designed for heating food in a fireplace.

   **Usage:** UK; historical

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A pan for frying, generally large and heavy.

   **Usage:** US

   **Example:** Heat some oil in a cast-iron skillet and add the onions.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A dish or meal cooked in such a pan.

   **Usage:** US; attributive; sometimes

   **Example:** This is an easy skillet with potatoes and bacon.

   **Example:** Can you make up a quick skillet hash?
