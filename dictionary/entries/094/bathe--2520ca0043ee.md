### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To clean oneself by immersion in water or using water; to take a bath, have a bath.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To immerse oneself, or part of the body, in water for pleasure or refreshment; to swim.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To clean a person by immersion in water or using water; to give someone a bath.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** We bathe our baby before going to bed; other parents do it in the morning if they have time.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To apply water or other liquid to; to suffuse or cover with liquid.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** She bathed her eyes with liquid to remove the stinging chemical.

   **Example:** The nurse bathed his wound with a sponge.

   **Example:** The incoming tides bathed the coral reef.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To cover or surround.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** The room was bathed in moonlight.

   **Example:** A dense fog bathed the city streets.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To sunbathe.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The women bathed in the sun.
