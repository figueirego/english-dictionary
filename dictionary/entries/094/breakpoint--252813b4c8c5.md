## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A point in a program at which operation may be interrupted during debugging so that the state of the program at that point can be investigated.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; programming; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A location referred to when issuing watches, warnings, or advisories for specific areas.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** climatology; meteorology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The point where surface water waves are breaking in, e.g., oceans or lakes.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**
