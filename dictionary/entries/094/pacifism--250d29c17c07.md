### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The support of peace, specifically&#58;
   1. The conviction that it is morally wrong to settle disputes (especially between countries) by war or other violent means.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The support of peace, specifically&#58;
   1. The ethical avoidance of inflicting harm on others in one's daily life.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source synonyms:** ahimsa; nonkilling

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
