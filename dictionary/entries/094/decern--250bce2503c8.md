### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To decide or determine (a matter disputed or doubtful), with simple object, with infinitive or object clause, or intransitive.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To decree (something) by judicial sentence.
   1. To decree by judicial sentence that something be done.

      **Usage:** intransitive; sometimes; transitive; Scots law

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To decree (something) by judicial sentence.
   1. To decree a person etc. to be or to do something by judicial sentence. (in the phrase “to decern in”, obsolete) To mulct in by decree of court.

      **Usage:** intransitive; sometimes; transitive; Scots law

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To discern; to distinguish or separate by differences (things that differ, or one thing from another).

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To discern; to distinguish or separate by differences (things that differ, or one thing from another).
   1. To distinguish; to discriminate between.

      **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To see distinctly (with the eyes or the mind); distinguish (an object or fact); discern.
