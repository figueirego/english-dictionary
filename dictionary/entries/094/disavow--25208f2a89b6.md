### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To strongly and solemnly refuse to own or acknowledge; to deny responsibility for, approbation of, and the like.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He was charged with embezzlement, but he disavows the crime.

   **Source synonyms:** abjure; deny; disclaim; disown; reject

   **Source antonyms:** accept; own up

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To deny; to show the contrary of; to deny legitimacy or achievement of any kind.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Because of her dissatisfaction, she now disavows the merits of socialism.

   **Source synonyms:** disprove; deny; impugn; reject; repudiate

   **Source antonyms:** accept; prove
