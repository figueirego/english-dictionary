## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

- good deals — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Large number, amount, or extent.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; often; singular; usually

   **Example:** He made a good deal of trouble for us.

   **Example:** We have a good deal of territory to cover.

   **Example:** The audience is generally unaware of the good deal of work that goes into its creation.

   **Source context (verbatim):** with indefinite article

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see good, deal.

   **Example:** I can offer you a good deal on that van.

   **Example:** There are plenty of good deals on flights.
