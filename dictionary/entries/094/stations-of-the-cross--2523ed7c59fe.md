## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Collectively, the series of (normally 14) icons or statues in a church which depict the last days of Jesus and his crucifixion.

   **Usage:** plural; plural-only

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity
