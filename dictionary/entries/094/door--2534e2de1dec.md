### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A portal of entry into a building, room, or vehicle, typically consisting of a rigid plane movable on a hinge. It may have a handle to help open and close, a latch to hold it closed, and a lock that ensures it cannot be opened without a key.

   **Example:** bathroom door, bedroom door, doorkey, exterior door, interior door

   **Example:** I knocked on the vice president's door.

   **Example:** That's the door to the bathroom.

   **Example:** I caught my finger in the door.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** architecture

   **Source synonyms:** gate; portal

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A building with a door, especially a house.

   **Usage:** in-plural; metonymically

   **Example:** His house is three doors down.

   **Example:** He went five doors up the road to the bank.

   **Example:** You brought this problem to my door.

   **Source synonyms:** home; house; place

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any flap, etc., that opens like a door.

   **Example:** cupboard door

   **Example:** the 24 doors in an Advent calendar

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An entry point.

   **Usage:** immigration

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A means of approach or access.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** Learning is the door to wisdom.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A possibility.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** to leave the door open

   **Example:** all doors are open to somebody

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A barrier.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** Keep a door on your anger.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A software mechanism by which a user can interact with a program running remotely on a bulletin board system. See BBS door.

   **Usage:** dated

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. The proceeds from entrance fees and/or ticket sales at a venue such as a bar or nightclub, especially in relation to portion paid to the entertainers.

   **Example:** The bar owner gives each band a percentage of the door and charges customers more to get in.
