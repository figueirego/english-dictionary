   1. A clever verbal solution to an embarrassing verbal mistake.

      **Usage:** colloquial; countable; uncountable

      **Example:** When I realized I'd called my teacher "Mom" again, I made a quick recovery saying that she loves me so much that she reminds me of my mother.
