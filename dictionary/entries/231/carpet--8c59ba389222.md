### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To lay carpet, or to have carpet installed, in an area.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** After the fire, they carpeted over the blackened hardwood flooring.

   **Example:** The builders were carpeting in the living room when Zadie inspected her new house.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To substantially cover something, as a carpet does; to blanket something.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** Popcorn and candy wrappers carpeted the floor of the cinema.

**Source sense R2.S3**
