### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An arterial road in western Silicon Valley, California, running through Palo Alto, Menlo Park, and Woodside, notable for its concentration of venture capital firms' offices.

   **Source synonyms:** Sand Hill; SHR

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Source synonyms:** Sand Hill; SHR
