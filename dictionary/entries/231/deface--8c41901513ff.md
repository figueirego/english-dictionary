### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To damage or vandalize something, especially a surface, in a visible or conspicuous manner.

   **Example:** After the painting was defaced a decade ago, it went viral and has been a tourist attraction ever since.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To void or devalue; to nullify or degrade the face value of.

   **Example:** He defaced the I.O.U. notes by scrawling "void" over them.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To alter a coat of arms or a flag by adding an element to it.

   **Usage:** flags

   **Example:** You get the Finnish state flag by defacing the national flag with the state coat of arms placed in the middle of the cross.
