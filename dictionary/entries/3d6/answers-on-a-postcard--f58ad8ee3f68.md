## Phrase · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Emphasising that the speaker has no idea of the answer to a question, especially an outlandish question (but with no expectation that answers will literally be sent by mail).

   **Usage:** UK; humorous

   **Example:** I haven't got a clue why there was a goat in the corner of the room. Answers on a postcard for that one, please.
