### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To telephone, to call on the telephone.

   **Example:** The written instructions weren't clear, so I dialed her up and asked her to explain what she meant.

   **Source synonyms:** give a ring; phone; ring; ring up

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To connect a computer to a modem.

   **Example:** In those days it took three minutes just to dial up, and every minute you were connected was costing you a higher phone bill.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To increase the level (of something) by turning a dial, or (figuratively) as if by turning a dial.

   **Example:** These comments are guaranteed to dial up the animosity.

   **Source synonyms:** ratchet up

   **Source antonyms:** dial back
