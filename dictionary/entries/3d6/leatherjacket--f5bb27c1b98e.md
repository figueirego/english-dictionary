### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The larva of certain crane flies.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of several brightly coloured tropical fishes, of the family Balistidae, including the filefish and the triggerfish; leatherfish.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of several Atlantic fishes, of the genus Oligoplites, that have a leathery skin; leatherjack.
