## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who has a tendency to be angry or to feel offended.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Don't be a sorehead! Forgive, forget and get on with your life.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A politician who is dissatisfied through failure, lack of recognition, etc.

   **Usage:** US; countable; derogatory; uncountable; political slang

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Infection in sheep by the nematode Elaeophora schneideri; elaeophorosis.

   **Usage:** uncountable

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Fowlpox.
