## Adverb

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Even; suggests a minimum, especially regarding what might be expected.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Without so much as a by your leave, he walked into the office and started digging through my files. Then off he went, without so much as a ‘goodbye’.

   **Example:** If she so much as looked at another man, I’d divorce her!

   **Source synonyms:** as much as

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. But rather.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** I’m not enjoying the meal so much as admiring the atmosphere.
