### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The policy of forcefully extending a nation's authority by territorial gain or by the establishment of economic and political dominance over other nations.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The means of forming, maintaining, or expanding an empire.

   **Usage:** countable; historical; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any undue extension of political, intellectual, or other forms of authority, particularly in regards to systems that benefit the Western world at the cost of the Global South.

   **Usage:** countable; derogatory; figuratively; uncountable
