### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An expression indicating a lack of enthusiasm or willingness (in something or to do something).

   **Usage:** no-past-participle; no-present-participle

   **Example:** I can't be bothered to clean the house today.

   **Example:** No, I simply can't be bothered with that.
