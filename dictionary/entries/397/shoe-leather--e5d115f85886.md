### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Leather that is used to make shoes.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Leather from which shoes are made that is worn out through walking.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The sweat of one's brow; effort; investigatory effort.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; idiomatic; uncountable

   **Example:** They expended a lot of shoe-leather in hunting down every lead.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Tough meat, especially cheap meat.

   **Usage:** countable; derogatory; figuratively; slang; uncountable

   **Example:** The school certainly wasn't breaking the bank by feeding us — when it wasn't gruel it was shoe-leather.
