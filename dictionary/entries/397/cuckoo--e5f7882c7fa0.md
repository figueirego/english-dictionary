### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of various birds, of the family Cuculidae within the order Cuculiformes, famous for laying its eggs in the nests of other species; but especially a common cuckoo (Cuculus canorus), that has a characteristic two-note call.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The sound of that particular bird.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The bird-shaped figure found in cuckoo clocks.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The cuckoo clock itself.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A person who inveigles themselves into a place where they should not be (used especially in the phrase a cuckoo in the nest).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Someone who is crazy.

   **Usage:** countable; slang; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Alternative form of coo-coo (Barbadian food).

   **Usage:** alt-of; alternative; countable; uncountable

   **Alternative form of:** [coo-coo](../2b8/coo-coo--ae065cb17730.md)

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Alternative form of ku ku jiao (the penis)

   **Usage:** Manglish; Singlish; alt-of; alternative; colloquial; countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** cuckoo bird; ku ku bird

   **Alternative form of:** [ku ku jiao](../277/ku-ku-jiao--9dcdf8188053.md)
