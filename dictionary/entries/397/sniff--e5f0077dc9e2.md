### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make a short, audible inhalation, through the nose, as when smelling something; to smell (something).

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** The dog sniffed around the park, searching for a nice scent.

   **Example:** I sniffed the meat to see whether it had gone off.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To make a short, audible inhalation, through the nose, as when smelling something; to smell (something).
   1. To say (something) while sniffing, such as in case of illness or unhappiness, or in contempt.

      **Usage:** ambitransitive; transitive

      **Example:** "He's never coming back, is he?" she sniffed while looking at a picture of him.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To make a short, audible inhalation, through the nose, as when smelling something; to smell (something).
   1. To inhale drugs (usually cocaine) through the nose, usually in powder form.

      **Usage:** UK; ambitransitive; slang

      **Source synonyms:** snort

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To make a short, audible inhalation, through the nose, as when smelling something; to smell (something).
   1. To be dismissive or contemptuous of something; used with at.

      **Usage:** ambitransitive; figuratively

      **Example:** This opportunity is not to be sniffed at.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To make a short, audible inhalation, through the nose, as when smelling something; to smell (something).
   1. To perceive vaguely.

      **Usage:** ambitransitive; broadly; transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To pry; to investigate in an interfering manner.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To pry; to investigate in an interfering manner.
   1. To intercept and analyse packets of data being transmitted over a network.

      **Usage:** intransitive

      **Example:** packet sniffer

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences
