### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of various species of marine bivalve shells formerly belonging to the genus Avicula (no longer accepted), now a synonym of Pteria, with species assigned to several genera, in which the hinge border projects like a wing.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any marine gastropod shell formerly in the genus Strombus, with species now assigned to several genera, principally in the family Strombidae.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any pteropod shell.

   **Usage:** archaic
