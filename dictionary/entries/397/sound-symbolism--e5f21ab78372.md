2. The phenomenon by which some speech sounds are associated with the meanings they convey, either intrinsically or by a recurring phono-semantic association, or an instance thereof; an ideophone.
   1. speech sound that is neither arbitrary with respect to meaning nor an onomatopoeia that imitates a real sound

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable; more specifically

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; phonology; sciences
