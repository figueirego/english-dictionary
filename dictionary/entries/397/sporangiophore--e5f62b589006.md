### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A receptacle in ferns which bears the sporangia, usually a stalk, but sometimes a scale (as in horsetails).

   **Example:** The cones of Equisetum are sporangiophores.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A special type of hypha that bears sporangia on the tip.
