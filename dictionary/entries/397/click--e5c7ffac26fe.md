### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A brief, sharp, not particularly loud, relatively high-pitched sound produced by the impact of something small and hard against something hard, such as by the operation of a switch, a lock, or a latch.

   **Example:** As I turned the key, the lock gave a click and the door opened.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The act of snapping one's fingers.

   **Usage:** UK

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An ingressive sound made by coarticulating a velar or uvular closure with another closure.

   **Example:** tsk is a click in English.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; phonetics; phonology; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** click consonant

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The sound made by a dolphin.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The act of operating a switch, etc., so that it clicks.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The act of pressing a button on a computer mouse or similar input device, both as a physical act and a reaction in the software.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; graphical-user-interface; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A single instance of content on the Internet being accessed.

   **Usage:** broadly

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A pawl or similar catch.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A knock or blow.

   **Usage:** UK; obsolete; slang

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**
