### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Apt to win favour; pleasing.

   **Usage:** UK

   **Example:** The candidate wearing the business suit made a favourable impression.

   **Source synonyms:** good; pleasing

   **Source antonyms:** bad; displeasing; unfavorable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Expressing or indicating favour or goodwill; approving, encouraging.

   **Usage:** UK

   **Example:** The proposal has received many favourable comments.

   **Source synonyms:** approving; encouraging; positive

   **Source antonyms:** disapproving; discouraging; negative; unfavourable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Useful or helpful.

   **Usage:** UK

   **Example:** We made quick progress, due to favourable winds.

   **Source synonyms:** advantageous; helpful; useful; propitious

   **Source antonyms:** unfavourable; unhelpful

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Convenient or at a suitable time; opportune.

   **Usage:** UK

   **Example:** The rain stopped at a favourable time for our tennis match.

   **Source synonyms:** convenient; good; handy; opportune; suitable

   **Source antonyms:** bad; inconvenient; inopportune; unfavourable; unsuitable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Auspicious or lucky.

   **Usage:** UK

   **Example:** She says that she was born under a favourable star.

   **Source synonyms:** auspicious; fortunate; lucky

   **Source antonyms:** inauspicious; unfavourable; unlucky
