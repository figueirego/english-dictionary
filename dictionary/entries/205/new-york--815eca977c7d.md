### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The largest city in the state of New York, and the largest city in the United States, a metropolis extending into neighboring New Jersey in addition to Pennsylvania and Connecticut.

   **Example:** New York is a former capital of the USA.

   **Source synonyms:** Big Apple; City of New York; New Amsterdam; New York City; NY; NYC; NY, NY; Gotham

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A state of the United States.

   **Example:** The capital of New York State is Albany, not New York City.

   **Source synonyms:** the Empire State; New York State; NY; NYS; State of New York

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. New York County, a county of New York, coterminous with the Borough of Manhattan.

   **Example:** The New York County District Attorney only has jurisdiction in Manhattan; each of the five boroughs of New York City elects its own DA.

   **Source synonyms:** Borough of Manhattan; Manhattan

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A former colony of England, from 1664 to 1707, and of Great Britain, from 1707 to 1776, which became the US state of the same name.

   **Usage:** historical

   **Source synonyms:** Province of New York

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A settlement in Toretsk urban hromada, Bakhmut Raion, Donetsk Oblast, Ukraine (co-ordinates&#58; 48°19′55″N 37°50′11″E).

   **Source synonyms:** Niu-York; Novhorodske

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

   1. A village in Wildmore parish, East Lindsey district, Lincolnshire (OS grid ref TF2455).

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A few places in England&#58;
   1. A settlement in Hartwith cum Winsley parish, North Yorkshire, previously in Harrogate borough (OS grid ref SE1962).

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A few places in England&#58;
