### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see send, up.

   **Example:** Fears of war sent oil prices up by 10%.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To imitate (someone or something) for the purpose of satirical humour.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The programme accurately sends up the British Civil Service system at Whitehall.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To put in prison.

   **Usage:** US; slang; transitive

   **Example:** The judge sent him up for three years.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To cause (something, e.g. sparks) to erupt.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Where the metal touched the asphalt, it sent up a shower of sparks.
