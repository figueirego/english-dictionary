   **Source synonyms:** adorn; grace; adore (obsolete); adorn; apparel; array; invest (literary); detail; beautify; bedeck; betrim; commend (dated); decorate; decore; deck; deck out; do up; dress up; dub; embellish; enrich; garnish; grace; mangonize (obsolete); ornament; prettify; pretty up

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** advance; elevate; ennoble; exalt; honor; prefer; advance; aggrandize; dignify; elevate; ennoble; exalt; honor; nobilitate; prefer (dated); promote; raise; uplift
