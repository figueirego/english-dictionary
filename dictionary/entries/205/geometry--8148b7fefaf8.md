### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The branch of mathematics dealing with spatial relationships.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A mathematical system that deals with spatial relationships and that is built on a particular set of axioms; a subbranch of geometry which deals with such a system or systems.

   **Usage:** countable; often qualified in combination

   **Example:** 1975 \[Addison-Wesley\], Eugene F. Krause, Taxicab Geometry, 1986, Dover, page 64, Entire new geometries are also suggested by real-world cities.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The observed or specified spatial attributes of an object, etc.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A mathematical object comprising representations of a space and of its spatial relationships.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** algebraic-geometry; geometry; mathematics; sciences
