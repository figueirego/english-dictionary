### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Easy; contemptibly easy.

   **Usage:** derogatory; usually

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Amiable, flexible, easy to get along with.

   **Usage:** archaic

   **Example:** His facile disposition made him many friends.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Effortless, fluent (of work, abilities etc.).

   **Example:** Her writing was facile and articulate.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Lazy, simplistic, superficial (especially of explanations, discussions etc.).

   **Example:** He arrived with a facile understanding of her works.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Of a reaction or other process, taking place readily.

   **Example:** Decarboxylation of beta-keto acids is facile.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences
