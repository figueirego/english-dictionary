## Noun · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /teɪst/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Forms

- tast — alternative; obsolete

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One of the sensations produced by the tongue in response to certain chemicals; the quality of giving this sensation.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** He had a strange taste in his mouth.

   **Example:** Venison has a strong taste.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The sense that consists in the perception and interpretation of this sensation.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** His taste was impaired by an illness.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A small sample of food, drink, or recreational drugs.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A person's implicit set of preferences, especially esthetic, though also culinary, sartorial, etc.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Dr. Parker has good taste in wine.

   **Source synonyms:** palate

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Personal preference; liking; predilection.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** I have developed a taste for fine wine.

   **Source synonyms:** palate

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A small amount of experience with something that gives a sense of its quality as a whole.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

   **Example:** Such anecdotes give one a taste of life on a trauma ward.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A kind of narrow and thin silk ribbon.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. The least amount or portion of something; a jot.

   **Usage:** countable; dialectal; uncountable

## Verb · Etymology 1 · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /teɪst/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Forms

- tastes — present; singular; third-person
- tasting — participle; present
- tasted — participle; past
- tasted — past
- tast — alternative; obsolete

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To sample the flavor of something orally.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To have a taste; to excite a particular sensation by which flavor is distinguished.

   **Usage:** copulative; intransitive

   **Example:** The chicken tasted great, but the milk tasted like garlic.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To identify (a flavor) by sampling something orally.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I can definitely taste the marzipan in this cake.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To experience.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** I tasted in her arms the delights of paradise.

   **Example:** They had not yet tasted the sweetness of freedom.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To take sparingly.

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To try by eating a little; to eat a small quantity of.

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

**Source sense R2.S7**

7. To try by the touch; to handle.

   **Usage:** obsolete
