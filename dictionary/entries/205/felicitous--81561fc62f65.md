### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Characterized by felicity.
   1. Appropriate, apt, fitting.

      **Source synonyms:** opportune; wealful (dialectal; obsolete)

      **Source antonyms:** infelicitous; unfelicitous

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

   1. Causing happiness or pleasure.
