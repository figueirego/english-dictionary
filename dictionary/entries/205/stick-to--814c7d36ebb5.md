### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To persist; to continue (to use, do, etc.)

   **Example:** I have seen all the fancy electric toothbrushes, but I'm going to stick to the old-fashioned kind.

   **Example:** If you stick to your studies, you will continue to improve.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To follow and remain close to.

   **Example:** The suspect doubled back through an alley, but the detective stuck to him.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see stick, to.
