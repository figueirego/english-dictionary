### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A type of acne that develops in newborns, characterised by open and closed comedones on the cheeks, chin and forehead.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** neonatal acne; acne infantum; neonatal cephalic pustulosis
