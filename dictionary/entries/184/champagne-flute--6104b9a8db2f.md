### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A piece of stemware with a long stem and a tall, narrow bowl on top, designed to keep champagne desirable during its consumption by preventing heat from the drinker's hand from warming the champagne while retaining its carbonation.

   **Source synonyms:** champagne glass
