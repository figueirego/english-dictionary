## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A minor river in south-east Greater London, England, which joins the Thames at Deptford. Ravensbourne railway station is near this river.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A minor river in the borough of Havering, north-east Greater London, which joins the River Rom (or Beam).

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A locality and national park in the Toowoomba Region, Queensland, Australia.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A suburb of Dunedin, Otago, New Zealand, beside Otago Harbour.
