### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An evil spirit supposed to oppress people while asleep, especially to have sex with women as they sleep.

   **Usage:** mediaeval folklore

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

   **Source synonyms:** nightmare
