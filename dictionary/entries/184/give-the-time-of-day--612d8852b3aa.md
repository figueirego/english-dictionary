### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To acknowledge someone; to give someone respect or attention; to tip one's hat.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** If you're lucky, she might give you the time of day.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To not acknowledge someone; to snub someone; to disrespect someone; to ignore someone.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** He won't give the time of day to someone like you or me.

   **Example:** I tried to say "hi", but she wouldn't give me the time of day.

   **Source context (verbatim):** in the negative

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To tell someone the time of day by looking at one's wristwatch, etc.

   **Usage:** dated; literally
