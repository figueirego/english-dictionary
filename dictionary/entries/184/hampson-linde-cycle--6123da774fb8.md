### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A cycle employed in the liquefaction of gases, especially for air separation. It is essentially the same as the Siemens cycle, except in the expansion step&#58; where the Siemens cycle has the gas do external work to reduce its temperature, the Hampson-Linde cycle relies solely on the Joule-Thomson effect, so the cold side needs no moving parts.

   **Source synonyms:** Linde process
