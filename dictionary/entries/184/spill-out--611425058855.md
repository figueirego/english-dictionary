### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. to exit (outside) in large quantities

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** People were spilling out of the stadium after the game.

   **Source context (verbatim):** said of people

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. to be released without constraint

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Abuse spilled out of her mouth.

   **Source context (verbatim):** said of emotions or feelings

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see spill, out.

   **Example:** Be careful with that jug! The water is spilling out.
