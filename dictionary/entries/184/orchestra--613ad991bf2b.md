### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A large group of musicians who play together on various instruments, usually including some from strings, woodwind, brass and/or percussion; the instruments played by such a group.

   **Example:** The orchestra plays music for the dancers to dance to in the 19th century-styled dance hall.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A semicircular space in front of the stage used by the chorus in Ancient Greek and Hellenistic theatres.

   **Usage:** Ancient Greece

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; theater

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The area in a theatre or concert hall where the musicians sit, immediately in front of and below the stage, sometimes (also) used by other performers.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A group of crickets.

   **Usage:** collective
