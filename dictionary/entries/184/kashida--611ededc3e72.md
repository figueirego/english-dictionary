### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A type of justification used in some cursive scripts, particularly (Perso)-Arabic, where characters are elongated like a string rather than separated by spaces.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A character representing this elongation.

   **Usage:** countable
