## Noun · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Forms

- middle names — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A name between the first given name and the family name or surname; a second or subsequent given name.

   **Example:** I go by David but it's my middle name; my full name is William David Mason.

   **Example:** María is a common middle name in Spanish, as in José María García.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A trait, activity, or action closely associated with or aptly describing a person.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** Think twice about playing for cash because "Poker" is her middle name.

   **Example:** I won't tell anyone. "Discretion" is my middle name.
