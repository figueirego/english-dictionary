**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A non-invasive MRI-based diagnostic technique used to visualize the bile ducts, pancreatic duct, and gallbladder. It produces detailed images without an endoscope or ductal contrast injection and is commonly used as a non-invasive alternative or preliminary test before ERCP, for diagnosis only and not treatment.
