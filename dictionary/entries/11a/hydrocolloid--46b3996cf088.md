### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any material that forms a colloid (especially a gel) when mixed with water.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any material that forms a colloid (especially a gel) when mixed with water.
   1. Any of a broad subclass of such materials that are of biologic origin or importance, most being biopolymers such as polysaccharides or proteins.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any material that forms a colloid (especially a gel) when mixed with water.
   1. Any of a broad subclass of such materials that are of biologic origin or importance, most being biopolymers such as polysaccharides or proteins.
      1. Such a material, made from agar, used to make dental impressions.
