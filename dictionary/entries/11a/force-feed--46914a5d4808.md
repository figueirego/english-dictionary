### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To force a person or animal to ingest food, for example by stuffing food down the throat, or using a tube passed into the stomach.

   **Example:** They were ordered to force-feed the prisoners on hunger strike.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. More broadly, also referring to forcing the drinking of a beverage or other liquid

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To force someone to take in information or accept an ideology.

   **Example:** The teachers at this school tend to force-feed their students information, rather than encourage critical thinking and debate.
