### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An understanding between entities to follow a specific course of conduct.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** to enter an agreement

   **Example:** The UK and US negotiators were nearing agreement.

   **Example:** He nodded his agreement.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A state whereby several parties share a view or opinion; the state of not contradicting one another.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** The results of my experiment are in agreement with those of Michelson and with the law of General Relativity.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A legally binding contract enforceable in a court of law.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Rules that exist in many languages that force some parts of a sentence to be used or inflected differently depending on certain attributes of other parts.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** grammar; human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** concord; concordance

   **Usage:** countable; in-plural; obsolete; uncountable
