### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Incorporeal; not possessed of a body.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; spirit; or other such essence so conceived; spirit; or other such essence so conceived

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a soul

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Not expressed or exhibited in material or concrete form; wholly abstract.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; ideas; theories; or the like

   **Source context (verbatim):** of principles

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Not incorporated into a coherent system; conceptually disconnected.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; ideas; theories; or the like

   **Source context (verbatim):** of principles

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

   **Usage:** especially; not-comparable

   **Source context (verbatim):** of armed multitudes

**Source sense R1.S5**
