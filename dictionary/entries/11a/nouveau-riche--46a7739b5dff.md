### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. New money; wealthy persons whose fortunes are newly acquired, and who are therefore perceived to lack the refinement of those who were raised wealthy.

   **Usage:** countable; derogatory; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** parvenu; arriviste; neo-rich

   **Source antonyms:** vieux riche; nouveau pauvre
