### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make deep or deeper

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** They deepened the well by 200 feet.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To make darker or more intense; to darken

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The event deepened the prevailing gloom.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To make more poignant or affecting; to increase in degree

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** News of her death deepened my sorrow.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To make lower in tone

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The tuner deepened the tone of the organ.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To make more thorough or extensive.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The class deepened my understanding of the subject.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To make more intimate.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The shared experiences deepened our relationship.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To make more sound or heavy.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The hypnotist then proceeded to deepen his trance.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To become deeper

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The water deepens as you go toward the middle of the channel.

   **Example:** The crisis deepened in the following weeks.

   **Usage:** intransitive
