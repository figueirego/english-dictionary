## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A period of history generally seen as occurring before the Middle Ages, that is, before the fall of the Roman Empire. Includes Ancient Egypt, Ancient Greece and Ancient Rome.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. That which happened a long time ago and not worth discussing any more.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; uncountable

   **Example:** Who cares if he said those things at the beginning of the year? That's ancient history now.
