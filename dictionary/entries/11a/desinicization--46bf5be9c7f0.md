### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The removal; elimination or suppression of Chinese influence and culture or process of stripping down one's Chineseness.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** The Spanish colonial government in the Philippines implemented desinicization policies on the Chinese immigrants in order to be assimilated into the mainstream Filipino society.
