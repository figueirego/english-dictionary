### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Physically close.

   **Example:** I can't see near objects very clearly without my glasses.

   **Example:** Stay near at all times.

   **Source synonyms:** close

   **Source antonyms:** remote

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Close in time.

   **Example:** The end is near.

   **Example:** We're getting near to Christmas.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Closely connected or related.

   **Example:** The deceased man had no near relatives.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Close to one's interests, affection, etc.; intimate; dear.

   **Example:** It is a matter of near consequence to me.

   **Example:** a near friend

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Close to anything followed or imitated; not free, loose, or rambling.

   **Example:** a version near to the original

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. So as barely to avoid or pass injury or loss; close; narrow.

   **Example:** a near escape

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Approximate, almost.

   **Example:** The two words are near synonyms.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. On the side nearest to the kerb (the left-hand side if one drives on the left).

   **Usage:** UK

   **Example:** The near front wheel came loose.

   **Source context (verbatim):** in relation to a vehicle

   **Source antonyms:** off

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. Next to the driver, when he is on foot; (US) on the left of an animal or a team.

   **Usage:** dated

   **Example:** the near ox; the near leg

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. Immediate; direct; close; short.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. Stingy; parsimonious.

   **Usage:** archaic

   **Example:** Don't be near with your pocketbook.

<a id="r1-s12"></a>

**Source sense R1.S12**

12. Within the currently selected segment in a segmented memory architecture.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** a near pointer

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; programming; sciences

   **Source antonyms:** far
