### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The state or characteristic of being just or fair.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** the justice of a description

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The ideal of fairness, impartiality, etc., especially with regard to the punishment of wrongdoing.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Justice was served.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Judgment and punishment of a party who has allegedly wronged another.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** to demand justice

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The civil power dealing with law.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Ministry of Justice

   **Example:** the justice system

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A title given to judges of certain courts (usually supreme courts); capitalized when placed before a name.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Mr. Justice Krever presides over the appellate court

   **Source synonyms:** justiciar; justiciary; doomsman (archaic); judg (obsolete); judge; justice; justicer (archaic); justiciar (historical); justiciary (historical)

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Correctness, conforming to reality or rules.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
