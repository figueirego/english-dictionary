### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To rely on (someone).

   **Usage:** US; archaic; transitive

   **Example:** He's dependable enough that you can always tie to him.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To connect one thing to another; to prove that a connection exists between two things.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** That evidence ties him to the boathouse at the time of the murder.

   **Example:** His GPS records tie to the dead woman.
