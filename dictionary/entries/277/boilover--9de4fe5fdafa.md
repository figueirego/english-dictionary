### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A hazardous situation where an attempt is made to extinguish semi-enclosed oil- or petrochemical-fuelled fire with water, generating steam which propels the burning fuel outwards.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A sporting event with an unexpected outcome, especially a horse race where the favourite fails to win.

   **Usage:** Australia; New-Zealand; countable; slang
