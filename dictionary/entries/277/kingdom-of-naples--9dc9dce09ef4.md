### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A former kingdom in southern Italy with its capital at Naples between 1282 and 1816.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Synonym of Parthenopean Republic, a short-lived French puppet state, or inclusive of it when ignoring its separate existence in 1799

   **Usage:** historical; inexact

   **Source synonyms:** Parthenopean Republic (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Synonym of Kingdom of the Two Sicilies, properly the union of the kingdoms of Naples and Sicily but with its capital at Naples from 1817 to 1861

   **Usage:** historical; inexact

   **Source synonyms:** Kingdom of the Two Sicilies (synonym; synonym-of); properly the union of the kingdoms of Naples and Sicily but with its capital at Naples from 1817 to 1861 (synonym; synonym-of)
