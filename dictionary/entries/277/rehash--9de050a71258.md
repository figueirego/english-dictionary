### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To hash (chop food into small pieces) again.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To repeat with minor variation.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Today's parliamentary session only rehashed last week's arguments.

   **Example:** The CEO of the company only rehashed a speech for the news conference.

   **Example:** The general rehashed plans for the war.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To analyze a prior contentious or embarrassing event.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Let's not rehash what we did last night.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To recompute the structure of a hash table, taking into account any newly added items.
