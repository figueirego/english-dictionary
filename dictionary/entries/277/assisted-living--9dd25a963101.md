### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of the various residential arrangements under which people with special needs reside in a facility, such as a group home or supportive apartment building, where trained staff specialize in aiding residents with at least some of the activities of daily living.

   **Usage:** uncountable
