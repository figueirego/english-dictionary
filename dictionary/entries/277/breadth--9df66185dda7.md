### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The extent or measure of how broad or wide something is; width.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The breadth of the corridor is 4.5 metres.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A piece of fabric of standard width.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Scope or range, especially of knowledge or skill.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** expand one’s breadth of marketing

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A style in painting in which details are strictly subordinated to the harmony of the whole composition.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** art; arts

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The length of the longest path between two vertices in a graph.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** graph-theory; mathematics; sciences
