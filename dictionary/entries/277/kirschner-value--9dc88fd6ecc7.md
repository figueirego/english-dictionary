### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A value indicating how much volatile fatty acid can be extracted from a given fat through saponification, in terms of the number of millilitres of 0.1 normal sodium hydroxide necessary for the neutralization of water-soluble silver salts made from the water-soluble volatile fatty acids distilled from 5 grams of the fat.

   **Source synonyms:** Kirschner number
