### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A member of a group of Han Chinese whose traditional ancestral homes are in southern Fujian, South China.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A member of a Southern Min-speaking Chinese people who inhabit or originate from eastern Guangdong; (loosely) Teochew.

   **Usage:** obsolete

**Source synonyms:** Hokkien; Fukien; Fukienese
