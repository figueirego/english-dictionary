## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A worker who is considered crucial to a particular sector.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A worker considered necessary for the community at large; especially first responders, other medical, education and transport personnel.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see essential, worker.
