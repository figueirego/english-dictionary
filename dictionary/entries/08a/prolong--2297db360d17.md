### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To extend in space or length.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To lengthen in time; to extend the duration of.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Complaining prolongs one’s pain.

   **Source synonyms:** [draw out](../230/draw-out--8c1fcdd084f6.md)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To put off to a distant time; to postpone.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The government shouldn't prolong deciding on this issue any further.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To become longer; lengthen.

   **Usage:** intransitive
