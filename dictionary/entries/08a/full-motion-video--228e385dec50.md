### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Video marketed as being of sufficient quality to make motion appear continuous to humans, considered to require at least 16 frames per second.

   **Usage:** countable; dated; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In-game footage that is pre-rendered, often using real-world scenes, as opposed to being rendered in-engine or in real time.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** video-games
