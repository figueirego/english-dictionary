1. To tighten or secure with screws.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To raise by turning a rotary handle.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To raise (rent, fees, etc.) to extortionate levels.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To raise or summon up.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** trying to screw up enough courage to ask her out

5. To twist into a contorted state.

   **Usage:** transitive
