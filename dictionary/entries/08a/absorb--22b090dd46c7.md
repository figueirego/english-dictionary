### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To include so that it no longer has separate existence; to overwhelm; to cause to disappear as if by swallowing up; to incorporate; to assimilate; to take in and use up.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To engulf, as in water; to swallow up.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To suck up; to drink in; to imbibe, like a sponge or as the lacteals of the body; to chemically take in.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To be absorbed, or sucked in; to sink in.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The drops of water slowly absorbed into the dry sponge.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To take in energy and convert it.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Heat, light, and electricity are absorbed in the substances into which they pass.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To take in energy and convert it.
   1. in receiving a physical impact or vibration without recoil.

      **Usage:** transitive

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To take in energy and convert it.
   1. in receiving sound energy without repercussion or echo.

      **Usage:** transitive

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To take in energy and convert it.
   1. taking in radiant energy and converting it to a different form of energy, like heat.

      **Usage:** transitive

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To engross or engage wholly; to occupy fully.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. To occupy or consume time.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. To assimilate mentally.

   **Usage:** transitive
