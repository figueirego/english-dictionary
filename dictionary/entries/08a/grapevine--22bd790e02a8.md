### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The plant, a vine of genus Vitis, on which grapes grow.

   **Example:** Although many grapevines have geographical names, these rarely reflect their real origin, if known at all.

   **Source synonyms:** winetree (rare)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An informal person-to-person means of circulating information or gossip.

   **Example:** I heard through the grapevine that Jim will be leaving soon.

   **Source synonyms:** jungle drums; bush telegraph; jungle telegraph; mulga wire; rumor mill

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A rumor.

   **Usage:** rare; apparently primarily Indian English

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A move in which the feet are alternately placed in front of each other, while both remaining on the ice or ground, incorporating half-turns.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; skating; sports

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A leglock.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; hobbies; lifestyle; martial-arts; military; politics; sports; war; wrestling

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A dance figure in partner dancing that includes sidesteps and steps across the support foot. See Grapevine (dance move).
