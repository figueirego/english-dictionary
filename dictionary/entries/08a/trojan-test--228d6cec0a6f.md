### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A test of binarity. A test to determine if a two-body system is a primary and satellite, or a binary system. A test to determine if the L4 and L5 Trojan points (Lagrange points) are stable or not. If it is determined they are stable, the system is that of a primary and satellite. (where one component is at least 25x times more/less massive than the other) If the points are not stable, then the system is that of two components in a binary relationship. (where the two components have less than 25x the difference in mass with the other)

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astronomy; natural-sciences
