## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A facility designed for firearms practice.

   **Example:** He enjoys going to the shooting range, but these days the price of cartridges and the price of range time have dampened his enthusiasm for the hobby.

   **Source synonyms:** firing range; range; target range; gun range; gunnery range; shooting ground; shootery

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A distance that allows for shooting&#58; one that is not so far as to make shooting infeasible, unlikely, or impossible.

   **Example:** within shooting range

   **Example:** You'll need to be patient and wait for those deer to come within shooting range.

   **Source synonyms:** firing range
