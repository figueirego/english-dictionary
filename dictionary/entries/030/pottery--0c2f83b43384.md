### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Fired ceramic wares that contain clay when formed.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** The shelves were lined with pottery of all shapes and sizes.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A potter's shop or workshop, where pottery is made.

   **Usage:** countable; usually

   **Example:** I visited the old potteries and saw the pots being made.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The potter's craft or art&#58; making vessels from clay.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** Bernard Leach was skilled at pottery.
