### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A river, the River Westbourne in London, England, which is now placed underground.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An area of the borough of the City of Westminster, Greater London near Bayswater and Paddington (OS grid ref TQ2581). Another name used here is Westbourne Park.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A suburb of Bournemouth, Dorset, England (OS grid ref SZ0791).

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A suburb of Ipswich, Suffolk, England (OS grid ref TM1445).

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A village and civil parish in Chichester district, West Sussex, England (OS grid ref SU7507).

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A community in the Municipality of WestLake-Gladstone, Manitoba, Canada.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. An unincorporated community in Campbell County, Tennessee, United States.
