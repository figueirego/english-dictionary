## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A ruler of other rulers.

   **Example:** The various tribal chieftains met each spring to elect an overlord that would lead them in time of war.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A feudal lord directly owed rent or personal service by a tenant of subinfeudated land, a tenant's liege lord.

   **Usage:** historical

   **Example:** The tenant owed a variety of obligations to his overlord—usually including military service or serjeanty—but these were gradually converted into monetary rents.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Anyone with overarching power or authority in a given domain.

   **Usage:** typically derogatory

   **Example:** The crusty old professor was overlord of the history department, and few dared to cross his will.
