## Verb

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To step sideways to make a space for someone else.

   **Example:** Stand aside, please, so the doctor can get through.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To resign a job or position voluntarily so that someone else can have it instead.

   **Example:** It's time he stood aside and let a more qualified person do the job.

   **Source synonyms:** stand down

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To temporarily recuse oneself from action or decision-making in some domain.

   **Example:** The minister will stand aside during the investigation.

   **Source synonyms:** stand down
