### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The phenomenon in some dialects of English, especially Canadian English, in which /aɪ/ is realized as \[ʌɪ\] and/or /aʊ/ is realized as \[ʌʊ\] or \[ɛʊ\] before underlying unvoiced consonants.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** dialectology; human-sciences; linguistics; phonology; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A similar but more restricted phenomenon, found in most dialects of American English, that applies only to /aɪ/, which is realized as \[ʌɪ\] in certain contexts, especially before underlying unvoiced consonants but with significant speaker-to-speaker variation.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** dialectology; human-sciences; linguistics; phonology; sciences
