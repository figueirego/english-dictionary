### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. At the same time, in the same place; in close association or proximity.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** We went to school together.

   **Example:** We can't all talk together; let's take turns politely.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Into one place; into a single thing; combined.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** He put all the parts together.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. In a relationship or partnership, for example a business relationship or a romantic partnership.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Bob and Andy went into business together. Jenny and Mark have been together since they went on holiday to Mexico.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Without intermission or interruption; continuously; uninterruptedly.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** It has been raining four days together

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** at the same time — at the same time; concurrently; collectively — in a relationship or partnership; jointly
