### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An attitude of consideration or high regard; a feeling of admiration or esteem.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** He is an intellectual giant, and I have great respect for him.

   **Source synonyms:** deference; esteem; consideration; regard; fealty; reverence; aught

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Good opinion, honor, or admiration.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** admiration; esteem; reverence; regard; recognition; veneration; honor

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Polite greetings, often offered as condolences after a death.

   **Usage:** uncountable; always plural

   **Example:** The mourners paid their last respects to the deceased poet.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A particular aspect, feature or detail of something.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** This year's model is superior to last year's in several respects.

   **Source synonyms:** aspect; dimension; face; facet; side

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Good will; favor.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
