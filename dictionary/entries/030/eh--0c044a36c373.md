### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used as a tag question, to emphasise what goes before or to request that the listener express an opinion about what has been said.

   **Usage:** Australia; Canada; Commonwealth; Hong-Kong; India; Ireland; New-Zealand; Singapore; South-Africa; UK; informal; Upper Midwestern US

   **Example:** These hot dogs are pretty good, eh?

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In isolation, a request for repetition or clarification of what has just been said.

   **Example:** —I've been studying Graphiurus murinus. —Eh? Studying what?

   **Source synonyms:** what; pardon; [sorry](../121/sorry--484aab2f2cd0.md); come again

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. In isolation, expressing surprise or confusion.

   **Example:** —You're going to be a dad! —Eh? You're joking!

   **Source synonyms:** what; wha; huh; za; [sorry](../121/sorry--484aab2f2cd0.md); come again

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An interjection used to ascertain the continued attention of someone addressed by the speaker.

   **Usage:** Canada

   **Example:** I went to the restaurant, eh, but my friends didn't show up.

   **Source synonyms:** y'know

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Expressing apathy or lack of enthusiasm; meh.

   **Example:** —Do you feel like going out tonight? —Eh, I don't know.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Expressing apprehension following or preceding a reasoning or excuse; uh.

   **Usage:** Philippines

   **Example:** —Why is it like this? —Eh, It's like that eh.
