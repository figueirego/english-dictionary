### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A term which appears to be (and may be thought of by speakers as) a loanword from another language, even being formed from elements found in that language, but which does not exist as a word in that language with the same meaning.

   **Source synonyms:** pseudo-borrowing; false loan; false borrowing
