### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A certain principle or any of several aphorisms expressing it&#58; If something cannot go on forever, it will eventually stop.

   **Example:** Leaving aside the point that what goes up must come down, Stein's law says that if infinite height is not possible then the rising will eventually stop.
