### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A valve or tap for the drainage of accumulated fluid (saliva) from musical instruments such as trombones or trumpets.

   **Source synonyms:** spit valve

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of several kinds of key (wrench) used to open and close water valves of certain kinds.
   1. A rod used to open and close a buried quarter-turn water valve, as in a water main.

      **Source synonyms:** curb key

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of several kinds of key (wrench) used to open and close water valves of certain kinds.
