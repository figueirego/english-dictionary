### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To begin working.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** As soon as he got home, he went to his desk and set to work.

   **Example:** Every day he sets to work at the new assignments.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To begin working at.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** As soon as I could, I set to work making the entries.

   **Source context (verbatim):** with gerund

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To cause to begin working.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I will immediately set them to work.

   **Example:** I was setting the children to work cleaning their rooms.

   **Example:** I set the pump to work getting the water out of the basement.

   **Source synonyms:** put to work
