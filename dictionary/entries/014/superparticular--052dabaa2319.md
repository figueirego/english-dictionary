### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of a natural number, being larger than another natural number by a unit fraction of that smaller number.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; obsolete

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences
