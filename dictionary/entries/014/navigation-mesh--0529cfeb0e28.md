**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A data structure used in artificial intelligence applications to aid agents in pathfinding through large spaces. It is a collection of two-dimensional convex polygons.

   **Example:** We used a navigation mesh to describe the paths that can be followed by computer-controlled characters in our video game.
