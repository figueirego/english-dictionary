### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Half-hourly&#58; occurring every thirty minutes.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; properly

   **Example:** The warship rang its semihourly bell, announcing it was now half past 10 o'clock.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Roughly (but not exactly) hourly&#58; occurring around every sixty minutes.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; inexact

   **Example:** Samples were collected semihourly (on the hour and half hour) throughout the study.
