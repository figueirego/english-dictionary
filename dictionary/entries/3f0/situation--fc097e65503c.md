### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The combination of circumstances at a given moment; a state of affairs.

   **Example:** The United States is in an awkward situation with debt default looming.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The way in which something is positioned vis-à-vis its surroundings.

   **Example:** The Botanical Gardens are in a delightful situation on the river bank.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The place in which something is situated; a location.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Position or status with regard to conditions and circumstances.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A position of employment; a post.

   **Usage:** UK

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A difficult or unpleasant set of circumstances; a problem.

   **Example:** Boss, we've got a situation here…

   **Source synonyms:** ass in a sling (vulgar); bind; bucket of syrup (idiomatic); calvary; cauldron; cross (figuratively); cross to bear (idiomatic); difficulty; dilemma; dire straits; doghouse; fix; hole; jam; kettle (idiomatic); kettle of fish (idiomatic); ordeal; pickle; pinch; plight; predicament; quandary; quagmire; situation; spot; spot of bother; sticky situation; sticky wicket; tight spot; trouble

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. An individual movie theater.

   **Usage:** US; film industry

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. An outfit, garment, or look.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Example:** She is working some sort of amazing burgundy chiffon situation with gold piping.
