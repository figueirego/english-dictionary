### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To stay after others or other parts have been removed or otherwise disappeared.

   **Example:** After three rounds of interviews, only 5 candidates remained.

   **Example:** I like to make more than enough food if I have people round for dinner, so I can eat my way through what remains in the following days.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To be left after a number or quantity has been subtracted or cut off; to be left as not included or comprised.

   **Example:** If you divide 20 apples between three people, each gets six and two remain.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To continue unchanged in place, form, or condition, or undiminished in quantity; to abide; to stay; to endure; to last.

   **Example:** Promise me you will always remain my good little girl.

   **Example:** She remained a force in local politics until her death.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To await; to be left to.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To continue in a state of being.

   **Usage:** copulative

   **Example:** There was no food in the house, so I had to remain hungry.

   **Example:** The light remained red for two full minutes.
