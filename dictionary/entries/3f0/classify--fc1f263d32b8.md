### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. to identify by or divide into classes; to categorize

   **Example:** Should we classify "make up" as an idiom or as a phrasal verb?

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. to declare something a secret, especially a government secret

   **Example:** They decided to classify that information.
