### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used to introduce one reason or example among potentially several reasons for something that has been stated or is about to be explained.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; sequence

   **Example:** Well, I’m having difficulties with the heating, for one thing.

   **Source synonyms:** for a start; for one
