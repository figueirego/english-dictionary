### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A transformation that maps any point P ≠ O to the point P’ on the ray from O through P for which OP × OP’ = r², and which maps O to the point at infinity.

   **Usage:** inversive geometry

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences

   **Source context (verbatim):** for a given circle with centre O and radius r
