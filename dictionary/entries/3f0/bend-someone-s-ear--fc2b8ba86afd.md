### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To bore; to talk too long, especially to one particular person.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** Sorry to bend your ear with the whole story, but I think you ought to know.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To call someone's attention to an important matter.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I managed to bend his ear about the current crisis. I hope he will act now.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To put a hand behind one's ear and cup it around so as to hear better.

   **Usage:** reflexive
