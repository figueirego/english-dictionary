**Source sense R1.S1**

1. (chemical nomenclature) The formal diimidic acid HO–C(=NH)–(CH2)4–C(=NH)–OH, the parent acid of adipimidate esters.

   **Extractor diagnostics (not usage labels):** no-gloss

**Supplemental definition (evidence reviewed; source gap preserved):** The parent name is explicitly attested in two authoritative chemical-name records. Removing the two ester methyl groups is a documented nomenclatural derivation, not an asserted experimental isolation of the free acid.
