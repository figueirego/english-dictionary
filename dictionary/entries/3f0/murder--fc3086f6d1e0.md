## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈmɜːdə(ɹ)/ — source labels: Received-Pronunciation
- IPA: /ˈmɝ.dɚ/ — source labels: General-American

### Forms

- murders — plural
- murther — alternative; obsolete

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An act of killing a person unlawfully, especially with predetermination.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** Throwing that punch was a murder whether you meant for it to happen or not.

   **Example:** There have been ten unsolved murders this year alone.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An act of killing a person unlawfully, especially with predetermination.
   1. The crime of killing a person unlawfully, especially with predetermination.

      **Usage:** uncountable

      **Example:** The defendant was charged with murder.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An act of killing a person unlawfully, especially with predetermination.
   1. Short for felony murder, the act of committing or abetting a crime that results in the killing of a person, regardless of intent, even if the perpetrator did not directly kill the victim.

      **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; uncountable

      **Example:** Both the arsonist and her partner were charged with murder, even though they had intended for it to be a victimless crime.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

      **Alternative form of:** [felony murder](../384/felony-murder--e1114a4756c6.md)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An act of killing a person unlawfully, especially with predetermination.
   1. The act of killing any being in an immoral manner; (countable) an instance of this.

      **Usage:** uncountable

      **Example:** Meat is murder.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An act of killing a person unlawfully, especially with predetermination.
   1. Synonym of murderer , a person who has unlawfully killed another.

      **Usage:** broadly; countable; nonstandard; uncountable

      **Source synonyms:** murderer (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Something difficult or terrible to endure.

   **Usage:** figuratively; uncountable

   **Example:** I heard this class is murder.

   **Example:** This headache is murder.

   **Source context (verbatim):** used as a predicative noun

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Something remarkable or impressive.

   **Usage:** countable; dated; figuratively; slang; uncountable

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A flock of crows or sometimes ravens.

   **Usage:** collective; countable

   **Example:** A murder of crows dotted my backyard.

   **Example:** Every morning we woke up to the squawking of what seemed like a hundred murders in the trees above our campsite.

   **Source synonyms:** conspiracy; unkindness

## Verb · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈmɜːdə(ɹ)/ — source labels: Received-Pronunciation
- IPA: /ˈmɝ.dɚ/ — source labels: General-American

- murders — present; singular; third-person
- murdering — participle; present
- murdered — participle; past
- murdered — past

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To illegally kill (a person or persons) with intent, especially with predetermination

   **Example:** The woman found dead in her kitchen was murdered by her husband.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To kill violently.

   **Usage:** colloquial; excessive

   **Example:** My uncle’s dog murdered my cat.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To defeat decisively.

   **Usage:** colloquial; excessive; figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** Our team is going to murder them.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To kick someone's ass or chew someone out (used to express one’s anger at somebody).

   **Usage:** colloquial; excessive; figuratively

   **Example:** He's torn my best shirt. When I see him, I'll murder him!

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To botch or mangle.

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To devour, ravish.

   **Usage:** UK; colloquial; figuratively

   **Example:** I could murder a hamburger right now.
