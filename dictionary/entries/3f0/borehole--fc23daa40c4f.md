## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

- boreholes — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A hole bored into the ground to collect samples for analysis or to extract oil or water.

   **Example:** The state of the groundwater is monitored at various boreholes in the area.

   **Source synonyms:** well

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any other hole that has been bored into something.

   **Example:** There were little boreholes in the oak beam, indicating that insects had once taken some interest in it.

   **Source synonyms:** bore
