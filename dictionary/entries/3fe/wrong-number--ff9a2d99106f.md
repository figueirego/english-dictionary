## Noun

### Forms

- wrong numbers — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A telephone number dialled by mistake, for example, by transposing digits.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A telephone call received from an unfamiliar caller, due to a mistake in the number dialled.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** \- Who was that on the phone? - It was just a wrong number.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An incorrect notion or understanding concerning a person or situation.

   **Usage:** dated; idiomatic

   **Source synonyms:** misapprehension; misinterpretation; misunderstanding
