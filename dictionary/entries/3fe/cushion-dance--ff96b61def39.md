### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A dance in which one person carries a cushion around the room, placing it before someone he or she wishes to kiss; after which kiss, the chosen person takes up the cushion and continues the dance.

   **Usage:** historical

   **Source synonyms:** kissing dance
