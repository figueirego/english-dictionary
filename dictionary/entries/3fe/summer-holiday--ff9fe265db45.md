## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A vacation taken during the summer.

   **Usage:** Commonwealth; Ireland; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A vacation taken during the summer.
   1. A school break in summer between school years and the break in the school academic year.

      **Usage:** Commonwealth; Ireland; countable; uncountable

      **Source synonyms:** summer holidays (Commonwealth; Ireland); summer vacation (Canada; US)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An easy and pleasant experience.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

   **Example:** This year's inventorying project was a summer holiday in comparison with how it used to go.

   **Source synonyms:** walk in the park; piece of cake; bagatelle; waltz; walk (US; informal); breeze; cake and gingerbread (obsolete); cakewalk; child's play; cinch; doddle; doss; duck soup; easy pickings; gimme; layup; low-hanging fruit; no-brainer; open and shut case; picnic; piece of cake; piece of piss (slang; vulgar); plain sailing; plane sailing (archaic); slam dunk; snap; walk in the park; walkover

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see summer, holiday&#58; a holiday (holy day or comparable) that falls during the summer; especially, one that celebrates summer or, most specifically, the summer solstice.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Various religions have a summer holiday; scholars point out that even Christianity's Saint John's Eve partly reflects the Christianization of pre-Christian midsummer festivals.
