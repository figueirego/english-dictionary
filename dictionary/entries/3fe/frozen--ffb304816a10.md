**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having undergone the process of freezing; in ice form.

   **Example:** The mammoth has been frozen for ten thousand years.

   **Example:** frozen pizza

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Immobilized.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** I just stood frozen in terror as the robber pointed at me with his gun.

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of an account or assets, in a state such that transactions are not allowed.
