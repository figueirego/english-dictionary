### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Having very strong opinions.

   **Example:** Her dad is so opinionated that when he's around it is hard to simply relax and have a light conversation.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a person

   **Source antonyms:** unopinionated

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Holding to one's own opinion obstinately and unreasonably.

   **Example:** Her dad is so opinionated that the others' preferences were overridden and no one had any fun on the outing.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a person

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. Infused with opinions rather than wholly objective.

   **Example:** His essay was opinionated, which was a problem because the assignment had been to write a factual analysis.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of statements or content

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. Enforcing one particular approach instead of offering options and flexibility.

   **Example:** an opinionated framework

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences; software
