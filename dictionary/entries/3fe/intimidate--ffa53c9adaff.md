### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make timid or afraid; to cause to feel fear or nervousness; to deter, especially by threats of violence.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He's trying to intimidate you. If you ignore him, hopefully he'll stop.

   **Source synonyms:** abash
