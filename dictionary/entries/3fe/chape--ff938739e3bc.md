### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The lower metallic cap at the end of a sword's scabbard.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Alternative form of chappe (“rainguard”) (piece fitted to a sword's crossguard).

   **Usage:** alt-of; alternative

   **Alternative form of:** [chappe](../058/chappe--160fa9ddd06b.md)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The piece by which an object is attached to something, such as the frog of a scabbard or the metal loop at the back of a buckle by which it is fastened to a strap.

   **Usage:** archaic

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The tip of a fox's tail.
