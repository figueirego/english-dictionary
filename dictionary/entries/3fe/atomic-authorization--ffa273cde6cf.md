## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of securing authorization rights independently from the intermediaries that use them and the parties to which they apply

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. (A document containing) published rights with a guarantee of authenticity which may be verified independently of the intermediaries that use them and the parties to which they apply.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. (A digitally signed document containing) published rights with cryptographically verifiable integrity, secured independently of the applications that use them and entities to which they apply.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
