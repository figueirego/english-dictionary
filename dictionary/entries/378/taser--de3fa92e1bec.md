### Senses

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To shock a person, thing, individual, or animal with a handheld device with the electric shock that it delivers; to stun with a stun gun.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

   **Usage:** figuratively
