### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A form of insurance that is not contingent on the death or survival of a person. Sometimes excludes health insurance as well. Common personal forms include car insurance, home insurance, and travel insurance, alongside a range of commercial types of insurance.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually
