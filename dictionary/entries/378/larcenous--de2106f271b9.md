### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Given to larceny, tending to thievery.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Resembling theft, often applied to legal actions that seem unfair.

   **Example:** These late fees are larcenous. I should have read the fine print before signing.
