### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Physical contamination, now especially the contamination of the environment by harmful substances, or by disruptive levels of noise, light etc.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** Pollution levels are almost always higher in cities rather than the countryside, what with the cars, industry and so on.

   **Source synonyms:** defedation; defilement; soilage; adulteration; contamination; defedation; defilement; pollution; sophistication (archaic)

   **Source antonyms:** conservation

   **Source synonyms:** adulterant; contaminant; contamination; impurity; sophisticant; adulterant; adulterator; contaminant; contamination; contaminator; impurity; pollutant; pollution; sophisticant (archaic); taint
