### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To attempt or try.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** Would you like to take a stab at explaining the theory?

   **Source synonyms:** take a crack at; take a run at; give something a go; give something a try

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To guess about.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I'll take a stab at the answer, but I don't really know for sure.
