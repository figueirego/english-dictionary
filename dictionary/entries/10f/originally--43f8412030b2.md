### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. As it was in the beginning; at first.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Originally, this paper was white, but over the years it has yellowed with age.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In an original manner.

   **Example:** They had to think originally to come up with those creative ideas.
