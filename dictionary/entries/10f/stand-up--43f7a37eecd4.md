### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To rise from a lying or sitting position.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Stand up, then sit down again.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To bring something up and set it into a standing position; to set something up.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Laura stood the sofa up on end.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. (stand someone up) To avoid a prearranged meeting, especially a date, with (a person) without prior notification; to jilt or shirk.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** John stood Laura up at the movie theater.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To last or endure over a period of time.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a thing

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To continue to be believable, consistent, or plausible.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a person or narrative

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To stand immediately behind the wicket so as to catch balls from a slow or spin bowler, and to attempt to stump the batsman.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; cricket; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a wicket-keeper

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To launch, propel upwards

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To formally activate and commission (a unit, formation, etc.).

   **Usage:** US; transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To make one's voice heard, to speak up.

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. To serve in a role during a wedding ceremony.

   **Usage:** formal; intransitive

   **Example:** Will you stand up for me (as my best man) at the wedding?

   **Example:** The bridesmaids will stand up over there during the ceremony.
