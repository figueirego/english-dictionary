### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To predict or forecast, especially through the application of skill.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Examining the tea-leaves, she prognosticated dark days ahead.

   **Source synonyms:** foretell; prophesy

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To presage, betoken.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The bluebells may prognosticate an early spring this year.

   **Source synonyms:** foretoken; foreshadow; portend
