### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To close one's eyes in sleep.

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To close one's eyes.

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Usually followed by at&#58; to look the other way, to turn a blind eye.

   **Usage:** archaic; intransitive

   **Source synonyms:** connive (obsolete); shut one's eyes

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To close one's eyes quickly and involuntarily; to blink.

   **Usage:** archaic; intransitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To blink with only one eye as a message, signal, or suggestion, usually with an implication of conspiracy. (When transitive, the object may be the eye being winked, or the message being conveyed.)

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** He winked at me. She winked her eye. He winked his assent.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To gleam fitfully or intermittently; to twinkle; to flicker.

   **Usage:** intransitive
