### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The specific compound in a natural or man-made mixture that is responsible for the mixture's activity.

   **Usage:** especially

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; pharmacology; sciences

   **Source antonyms:** inactive ingredient

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
