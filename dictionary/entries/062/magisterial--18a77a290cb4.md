### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Befitting the status or skill of a magister or master; authoritative, masterly.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of or pertaining to a master, magistrate, the magisterium, or one in authority.

**Source sense R1.S3**
