## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any individual piece of music, normally separated from the others by a short pause, on an LP, CD, etc.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
