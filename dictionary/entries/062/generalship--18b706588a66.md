## Noun

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The position or office of a general.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war

   **Source synonyms:** generalcy

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The term of office of a military general.

   **Example:** George Washington's generalship was marked by both amazing victories and stunning blunders, neither of which would have happened to someone with more formal officer training.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The skills or performance of a good general; military leadership, strategy.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. By extension, leadership, good management.

   **Example:** Under my generalship my fine troop of brats picked up every scrap of litter in that lot.
