### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One vertex of a Steiner tree.

   **Example:** I created a Steiner point in my layout design at the point where I want my net's routing to branch.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; electrical-engineering; electricity; electromagnetism; electronics; energy; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An extra vertex that is not a member of the input.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** graph-theory; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The point on the circumcircle of a triangle that lies at the intersection of three lines through the vertices of the triangle, where those lines are parallel to the corresponding sides of the first Brocard triangle.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The geometric centroid of the system obtained by placing a mass equal to the magnitude of the exterior angle at each vertex of the system.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Any of the 20 points located at the 3 by 3 intersections of the 60 Pascal lines which are produced by extending the triplets on opposite sides of a conic section in Pascal's theorem for all permutations of verices.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences
