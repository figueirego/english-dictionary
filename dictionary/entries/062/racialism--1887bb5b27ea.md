## Noun

### Forms

- racialisms — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The belief that humans can be categorized as belonging to distinct races, each race being characterized by fixed and heritable traits.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Racism, the belief in the existence of different races and that some are superior to others; policies or practices which promote the dominance of one or more races over others.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Tribalism, nationalism.

   **Usage:** UK; countable; dated; uncountable
