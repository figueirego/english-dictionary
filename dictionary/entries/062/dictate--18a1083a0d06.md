## Verb · Etymology 2 · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈdɪkˌteɪt/ — source labels: General-American

### Forms

- dictating — participle; present
- dictated — participle; past
- dictated — past

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To order, command, control.

   **Example:** Etiquette dictates that wedding invitations should be acknowledged in writing.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To speak in order for someone to write down the words.

   **Example:** She is dictating a letter to a stenographer.

   **Example:** The French teacher dictated a passage from Victor Hugo.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To determine or decisively affect.

   **Example:** We issue replacements as necessity dictates.
