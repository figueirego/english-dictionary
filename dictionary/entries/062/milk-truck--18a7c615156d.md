### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of several types of truck used for road transportation of liquid fresh milk; usually, a tanker truck for bulk transport; formerly often a stakebody truck to pick up milk churns (milk cans); sometimes a truck to deliver milk bottles for home delivery.

   **Usage:** US
