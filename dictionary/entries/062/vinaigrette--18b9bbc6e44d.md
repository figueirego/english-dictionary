### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A sauce, made of an acidic liquid such as vinegar or lemon juice; oil; and other ingredients, used as a salad dressing, or as a marinade for cold meats.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** vinegarette (archaic)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A small perforated box for holding aromatic vinegar contained in a sponge, or a smelling bottle for smelling salts

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** vinegarette

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A small, two-wheeled vehicle, like a litter, to be drawn or pushed by a person. They were used in France until the late 18th century.

   **Usage:** countable; historical; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Alternative form of vinegret (“type of Russian salad”).

   **Usage:** alt-of; alternative; countable; uncountable

   **Alternative form of:** [vinegret](../3b8/vinegret--ee335d3acdcc.md)
