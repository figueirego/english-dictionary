### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Wisdom which comes after the event.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source antonyms:** forewit
