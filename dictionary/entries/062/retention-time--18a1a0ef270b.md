### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A measure of the amount of time required for a solute to pass through a chromatography column after it has been injected.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; analytical chemistry

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; chromatography; natural-sciences; physical-sciences
