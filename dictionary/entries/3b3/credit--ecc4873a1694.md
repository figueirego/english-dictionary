### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Reliance on the truth of something said or done; faith; trust.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Recognition, respect and admiration.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** I give you credit for owning up to your mistake.

   **Example:** He arrived five minutes late, but to his credit he did work an extra ten minutes at the end of his shift.

   **Source synonyms:** due

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. Acknowledgement of a contribution, especially in the performing arts.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** She received a singing credit in last year's operetta.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. Written titles and other information about the TV program or movie shown at the beginning and/or end of the TV program or movie.

   **Usage:** countable; plural-normally; uncountable

   **Example:** They kissed, and then the credits rolled.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** broadcasting; film; media; television

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. A privilege of delayed payment extended to a buyer or borrower on the seller's or lender's belief that what is given will be repaid.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** In view of your payment record, we are happy to extend further credit to you.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance; law

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. The time given for payment for something sold on trust.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** a long credit or a short credit

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

**Source sense R2.S7**

7. A person's credit rating or creditworthiness, as represented by their history of borrowing and repayment (or non payment).

   **Usage:** US; uncountable

   **Example:** What do you mean my credit is no good?

<a id="r2-s8"></a>

**Source sense R2.S8**

8. An addition to certain accounts; the side of an account on which payments received are entered.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** accounting; business; finance
