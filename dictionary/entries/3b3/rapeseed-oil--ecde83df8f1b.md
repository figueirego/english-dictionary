**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A thick brownish-yellow oil expressed from rapeseed, used chiefly for cooking, lubricating and in the manufacture of soap and india rubber.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** colza oil; rape oil
