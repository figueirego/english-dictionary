### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To not accept (something on offer).

   **Example:** It seems odd that the union left the tuition assistance program on the table when they negotiated this latest contract.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To fail to seize (an opportunity that is apparent).

   **Example:** They really left a lot on the table when they designed this hampered user interface.
