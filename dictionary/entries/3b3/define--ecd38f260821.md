### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To determine with precision; to mark out with distinctness; to ascertain or exhibit clearly.

   **Example:** the defining power of an optical instrument

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To settle, decide (an argument etc.).

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To express the essential nature of something.

   **Example:** I define myself as a techno-anarchist.

   **Example:** Your past mistakes do not define who you are.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To state the meaning of a word, phrase, sign, or symbol.

   **Example:** The textbook defined speed as velocity divided by time.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To describe, explain, or make definite and clear; used to request the listener or other person to elaborate or explain more clearly his or her intended meaning of a word or expression.

   **Example:** Person 1&#58; Is she good at math? Person 2&#58; Define "good." If you mean if she is faster than the average middle schooler at multiplication, then yes. If you mean if she is able to do multivariable calculus, then no.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To demark sharply the outlines or limits of an area or concept.

   **Example:** to define the legal boundaries of a property

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To establish the referent of a term or notation.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences
