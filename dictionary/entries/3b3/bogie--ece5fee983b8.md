### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A low, hand-operated truck, generally with four wheels, used for transporting objects or for riding on as a toy; a trolley.

   **Usage:** Northern-England

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One of two sets of wheels under a locomotive or railcar; also, a structure with axles and wheels under a locomotive, railcar, or semi which provides support and reduces vibration for the vehicle.

   **Usage:** also; attributive; broadly

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** rail-transport; railways; transport

   **Source synonyms:** railroad truck (US)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A set of wheels attached to one of an aircraft's landing gear, or the structure connecting the wheels in one such set.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** Although most A320s have two wheels on each of their main gear, a few built for the Indian market have four-wheel bogies, halving the amount of weight on each wheel and allowing the aircraft to use runways that couldn't withstand the ground pressure from a standard A320.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** aeronautics; aerospace; aviation; business; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A railway carriage.

   **Usage:** India; UK

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** rail-transport; railways; transport

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A soapbox racing vehicle.

   **Usage:** dated
