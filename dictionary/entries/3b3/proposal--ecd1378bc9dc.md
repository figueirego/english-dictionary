### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Something which is proposed, or offered for consideration or acceptance.
   1. A scheme or design.

      **Example:** proposals for the construction of a new building

      **Source synonyms:** overture; proffer; proposition; plot; blueprint (informal); design; plan; plot; project; proposal; scheme

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Something which is proposed, or offered for consideration or acceptance.
   1. The terms or conditions proposed.

      **Example:** to make proposals for a treaty of peace

      **Source synonyms:** overture; proffer; proposition

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Something which is proposed, or offered for consideration or acceptance.
   1. The document on which such a thing is written.

      **Source synonyms:** overture; proffer; proposition

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Something which is proposed, or offered for consideration or acceptance.
   1. The act of asking someone to be one's spouse; an offer of marriage.

      **Source synonyms:** overture; proffer; proposition

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Something which is proposed, or offered for consideration or acceptance.
   1. The offer by a party of what they have in view as to an intended business transaction, which, with acceptance, constitutes a contract.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

      **Source synonyms:** overture; proffer; proposition; tender
