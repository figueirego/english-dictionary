## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Vision that works, to one degree or another, in the low-light conditions of night.
   1. Such vision provided naturally by the biological systems of an eye.

      **Usage:** uncountable

      **Example:** The field workers doing the nighttime field work had a rule that artificial lights were to be kept doused or shaded so as not to blind their night vision after their eyes had spent many minutes adjusting to the dark.

      **Source synonyms:** scotopia

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Vision that works, to one degree or another, in the low-light conditions of night.
   1. Such vision provided by technology or (in fiction) by superpowers.

      **Usage:** uncountable

      **Example:** night vision goggles

      **Example:** night vision devices

      **Example:** night vision equipment

      **Example:** The special forces operating in the area were relying heavily on night vision and smart weapon systems.
