## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The focusing on the text of written law to the exclusion of the intent of law, elevating strict adherence to law over justice, mercy, grace and common sense.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** casuistry

   **Source antonyms:** antilegalism; nonlegalism

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A belief that salvation is attained or maintained by adherence to the requirements of moral law; salvation by works rather than by faith alone.

   **Usage:** derogatory; often; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** lifestyle; religion

   **Source antonyms:** antilegalism; antinomianism; antinomism; solifidianism; nonlegalism

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A legal axiom, term or rule.

   **Usage:** countable
