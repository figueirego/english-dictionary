## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A 19th-century process of reproducing steel-engraved designs for printing. The design is engraved on a steel block, then hardened and used to transfer a raised-image version to a steel roller under heavy pressure. The roller is then hardened and used as a die to impress duplicate images on printing plates for transferring to paper by the intaglio method.

   **Usage:** historical; uncountable
