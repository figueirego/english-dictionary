### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A specialized animal tissue with high lipid content, used for long-term storage of energy&#58; fat tissue.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** Mammals that hibernate have plenty of fat to keep them warm during the winter.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. A specialized animal tissue with high lipid content, used for long-term storage of energy&#58; fat tissue.
   1. Such tissue as food&#58; the fatty portion of (or trimmings from) meat cuts.

      **Usage:** uncountable; usually

      **Example:** Ask the butcher for a few pounds of fat for our greens.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. A lipid that is solid at room temperature, which fat tissue contains and which is also found in the blood circulation; sometimes, a refined substance chemically resembling such naturally occurring lipids.

   **Usage:** countable; usually
