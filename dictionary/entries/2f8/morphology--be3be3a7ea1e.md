### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A scientific study of form and structure, usually without regard to function. Especially&#58;
   1. The study of the internal structure of morphemes (words and their semantic building blocks).

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A scientific study of form and structure, usually without regard to function. Especially&#58;
   1. The study of the form and structure of animals and plants.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

   1. The study of the structure of rocks and landforms.
