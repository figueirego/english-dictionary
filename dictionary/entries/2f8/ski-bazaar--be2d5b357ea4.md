### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A used ski equipment sale event organized by a ski hill, ski club, ski team, ski store, or ski enthusiasts, where old ski equipment, be it remaindered end of line stock, second hand, former team equipment, or former rental equipment, is put on sale to the public. Typically, the public can also consign their old equipment for sale; and equipment on sale has been inspected for good condition and in-warranty status.
