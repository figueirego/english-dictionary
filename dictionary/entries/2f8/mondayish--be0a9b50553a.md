## Adjective · Record 2

### Forms

- more Mondayish — comparative
- most Mondayish — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. feeling ill, especially used of clergymen having worked all day Sunday

   **Usage:** informal

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. hungover after a weekend of drinking (as a presumed contributing explanation for a clergyman feeling Mondayish)

   **Usage:** informal

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. grumpy and disheartened on returning to work on a Monday after the weekend

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** I feel a bit Mondayish this week at the start of a long project.
