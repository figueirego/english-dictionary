### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The practice of keeping domestic cats within a defined area, such as indoors, in an enclosed yard, or within a cat-proof structure, to prevent them from roaming freely, typically implemented to protect native wildlife and reduce the spread of diseases.
