### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The drawing of air into the lungs, accomplished in mammals by elevation of the chest walls and flattening of the diaphragm, as part of the act of breathing.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; physiology; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** inhalation

   **Source antonyms:** expiration; exhalation

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A single inward breath (intake of air).

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source synonyms:** inhalation

   **Source antonyms:** expiration; exhalation

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A supernatural divine influence on the prophets, apostles, or sacred writers, by which they were qualified to communicate moral or religious truth with authority; a supernatural influence which qualifies people to receive and communicate divine truth; also, the truth communicated.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The act or process of an elevating or stimulating influence upon the intellect, emotions or creativity.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** She was waiting for inspiration to write a book.

   **Source synonyms:** afflatus (formal); motivation; spark; flash; eureka

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A person, object, or situation which quickens or stimulates an influence upon the intellect, emotions or creativity.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The trip was an inspiration to her for writing a book.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A new idea, especially one which arises suddenly and is clever or creative.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
