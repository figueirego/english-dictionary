### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In sheet-metal roofing, a joint formed by bending up the edge of a sheet and folding it downward over the turned-up edge of the next sheet.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A joint allowing movement in every direction except axial rotation.
