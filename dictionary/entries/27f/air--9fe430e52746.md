<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To bring (something) into contact with the air, so as to freshen or dry it.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I'll hang these clothes on the rack to air them.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To let fresh air into (a room or a building), to ventilate.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** It's getting quite stuffy in this room&#58; let's open the windows and air it.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To make public (an opinion, concern, issue, secret, differences, etc); to present to public view (and sometimes discussion).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Residents used the public meeting to air their concerns about the poor state of the roads.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To broadcast (a television show etc.).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The BBC decided not to air the controversial episode.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To be broadcast.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** This game show first aired in the 1990s and is still going today.

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To ignore (a person).

   **Usage:** Multicultural-London-English; UK; slang; transitive

   **Example:** Why is this girl airing me?
