## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A small, buoyant board used to support a swimmer's upper body and arms while allowing free kicking movements.

   **Example:** The introductory swimming instructor carried a pile of kickboards to the pool for the children.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. On pianos and organs, a vertical panel located above the pedals.

   **Example:** A mouse had gnawed a hole through the kickboard to nest inside the organ; she refused to use the pedals nearby.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Synonym of kickplate (“strip at base of door to prevent scuffing”).

   **Source synonyms:** kickplate (synonym; synonym-of)
