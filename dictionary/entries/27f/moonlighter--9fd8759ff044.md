### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who moonlights, working a second job on the side.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One who serenades by moonlight.

   **Usage:** US; regional

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A moonshiner, who makes illicit alcohol.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

   **Usage:** Ireland; historical
