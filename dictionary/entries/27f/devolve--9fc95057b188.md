### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To be inherited by someone else; to pass down upon the next person in a succession, especially through failure or loss of an earlier holder.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source synonyms:** pass down

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To delegate (a responsibility, duty, etc.) on or upon someone.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** pass down; pass on; hand down

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To delegate (a responsibility, duty, etc.) on or upon someone.
   1. To transfer authority and responsibility for (something) to (another entity).

      **Usage:** especially; transitive; usually; a federal one to a federated one

      **Example:** Despite being a unitary state, the Government of the United Kingdom devolved many matters to the Governments of Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland.

      **Source context (verbatim):** of a central government to a local one

      **Source synonyms:** pass down; pass on; hand down

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To fall as a duty or responsibility on or upon someone.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To fall as a duty or responsibility on or upon someone.
   1. To shift or to be transferred from a central government to a local one, a federal one to a federated one, etc.

      **Usage:** especially; intransitive

      **Example:** Many legislative powers devolved to the new Scottish parliament in 1999.

      **Source context (verbatim):** of government authority

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To degenerate; to break down.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** A discussion about politics may devolve into a shouting match.

   **Source synonyms:** deteriorate; atrophy; break down; crack (archaic); decline; deteriorate; degenerate; devolve; confound; fade; fall off; go bad; go downhill; go left; go off; go pear-shaped; go sideways; go south; go to hell; go to pot; go to seed; go to the dogs; go wrong; lapse; molder; pejorate; retrograde; suffer; spend; sweal; shrivel; tabefy; wither; wither away; wane; wanse; worsen

   **Source antonyms:** evolve; improve

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To roll (something) down; to unroll.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** roll down; unwind; unfurl
