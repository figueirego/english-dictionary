### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To convert a representation of language, typically speech but also sign language, etc., to a written representation of it. The term now usually implies the conversion of speech to text by a human transcriptionist with the assistance of a computer for word processing and sometimes also for speech recognition, the process of a computer interpreting speech and converting it to text.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To make such a conversion from live or recorded speech to text.

   **Example:** The doctor made several recordings today which she will transcribe into medical reports tomorrow.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To transfer data from one recording medium to another.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To adapt a composition for a voice or instrument other than the original; to notate live or recorded music.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To cause DNA to undergo transcription.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biochemistry; biology; chemistry; microbiology; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To represent speech by phonetic symbols.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences
