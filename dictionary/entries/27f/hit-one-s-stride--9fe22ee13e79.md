### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. when walking or running, to reach a full or comfortable pace

   **Example:** I usually start running slowly for a warm-up and then hit my stride a few minutes into the run.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. to reach a full level of efficiency, competence, comfort, etc.; to get going

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** They had a baby just as she was hitting her stride professionally.
