### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The international mile&#58; a unit of length precisely equal to 1.609344 kilometers established by treaty among Anglophone nations in 1959, divided into 5,280 feet or 1,760 yards.

   **Example:** Turn left in 1.2 miles.

   **Example:** You need to go about three mile down the road.

   **Example:** 3.6 km is about 2 miles.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of several customary units of length derived from the 1593 English statute mile of 8 furlongs, equivalent to 5,280 feet or 1,760 yards of various precise values.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of many customary units of length derived from the Roman mile (mille passus) of 8 stades or 5,000 Roman feet.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The Scandinavian mile&#58; a unit of length precisely equal to 10 kilometers defined in 1889.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Any of many customary units of length from other measurement systems of roughly similar values, as the Chinese mile or Arabic mile.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. An airline mile in a frequent flyer program.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** lifestyle; tourism; transport; travel

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Any similarly large distance.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** The shot missed by a mile.

   **Example:** My legs felt stiff and leaden from miles of walking.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A race of 1 mile's length; a race of around 1 mile's length (usually 1500 or 1600 meters)

   **Example:** The runners competed in the mile.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** athletics; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. One mile per hour, as a measure of speed.

   **Usage:** colloquial

   **Example:** five miles over the speed limit

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. A prominent road or street, often roughly a mile long.

   **Usage:** usually in place names

   **Example:** The Royal Mile in Edinburgh. The Millennium Mile (London). Magnificent Mile (Chicago).

   **Example:** I'm staying near millionaire's mile.
