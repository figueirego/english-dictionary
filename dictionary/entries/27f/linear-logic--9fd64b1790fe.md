### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A logic in which two structural rules are missing from its sequent calculus&#58; those for weakening and contraction; which has some extra logical connectives, so that it has both "additive" and "multiplicative" versions of the typical binary connectives and truth constants; and which has a pair of modal, "exponential" operators for resource management, to help make up for the loss of the two structural rules.
