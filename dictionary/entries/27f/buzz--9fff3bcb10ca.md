   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Usage:** countable; informal; uncountable

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To make a low, continuous, humming or sibilant sound, like that made by bees with their wings.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To make a low, continuous, humming or sibilant sound, like that made by bees with their wings.
   1. To utter a murmuring sound; to speak with a low, humming voice.

      **Usage:** broadly; intransitive

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To make a low, continuous, humming or sibilant sound, like that made by bees with their wings.
   1. Of a group of people, to talk about some interesting topic excitedly.

      **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To make a low, continuous, humming or sibilant sound, like that made by bees with their wings.
   1. To fly while making such a sound.

      **Usage:** intransitive

      **Source context (verbatim):** of an insect

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To show a high level of activity and haste, energization or excitement, to be busy as a bee in one’s actions but perhaps mentally charged.

   **Usage:** colloquial

   **Example:** The room was buzzing with excitement.

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To whisper; to communicate, as tales, in an undertone; to spread, as a report, by whispers or secretly.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

**Source sense R2.S7**

7. To talk to incessantly or confidentially in a low humming voice.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s8"></a>

**Source sense R2.S8**

8. To fly at high speed and at a very low altitude over (an area), as to make a surprise pass.

   **Usage:** also; figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** buzzing around in small cars that dodged through traffic

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** aeronautics; aerospace; aviation; business; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r2-s9"></a>

**Source sense R2.S9**

9. To cut (the hair) in a close-cropped military style, or buzzcut.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s10"></a>

**Source sense R2.S10**

10. To throw swiftly.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s11"></a>

**Source sense R2.S11**

11. To drink to the bottom.

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive

<a id="r2-s12"></a>

**Source sense R2.S12**

12. To communicate with (a person) by means of a buzzer.

   **Usage:** transitive
