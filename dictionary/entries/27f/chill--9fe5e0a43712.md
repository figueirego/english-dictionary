### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A moderate, but uncomfortable and penetrating coldness.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** There was a chill in the air.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A sudden penetrating sense of cold, especially one that causes a brief trembling nerve response through the body; the trembling response itself; often associated with illness&#58; fevers and chills, or susceptibility to illness.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Close the window or you'll catch a chill.

   **Example:** I felt a chill when the wind picked up.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An uncomfortable and numbing sense of fear, dread, anxiety, or alarm, often one that is sudden and usually accompanied by a trembling nerve response resembling the body's response to biting cold.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Despite the heat, he felt a chill as he entered the crime scene.

   **Example:** The actor's eerie portrayal sent chills through the audience.

   **Example:** His menacing presence cast a chill over everyone.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An iron mould or portion of a mould, serving to cool rapidly, and so to harden, the surface of molten iron brought in contact with it.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The hardened part of a casting, such as the tread of a carriage wheel.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A lack of warmth and cordiality; unfriendliness.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Calmness; equanimity.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A sense of style; trendiness; savoir faire.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A chilling effect; an atmosphere of this.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
