### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To prevent or restrain (someone or something) from; to refrain or cause to refrain from.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To make and cause to remain secret.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** What else did your brother keep from me?

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To cause to be excluded or not present.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He could not keep the sadness from his trembly voice.

   **Example:** I don't want to keep you from school.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To protect or preserve from.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** As a mother, I wanted to keep my son from harm.
