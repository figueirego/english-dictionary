### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To put (something) in its usual storage place; to place out of the way, clean up.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Please put away the tools when you are finished.

   **Example:** I put the clothes away so as to neaten the room.

   **Source synonyms:** store; stow

   **Source antonyms:** take out

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To store, add to one's stores for later use.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** putting a little away for a rainy day

   **Example:** Preparing for the worst, they put away food for the winter.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To consume (food or drink), especially in large quantities.

   **Usage:** colloquial; transitive

   **Example:** You wouldn't think such a small person could put away so much food.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To send (someone) to prison or mental asylum.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** After he was convicted, they put him away for 10 years.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To kill someone.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To knock out an opponent.

   **Usage:** broadly; transitive; combat sports

   **Example:** He put away his opponent in the first round.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To discard, divest oneself of.

   **Usage:** formal; literary; transitive

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To fend off, deflect; to dismiss.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To divorce.

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. To take a large lead in a game, especially enough to guarantee victory or make the game no longer competitive.

   **Example:** They put the game away by scoring three touchdowns in the fourth quarter.
