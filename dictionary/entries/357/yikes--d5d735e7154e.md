### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Expression of shock and alarm.

   **Usage:** humorous; informal; often

   **Example:** Yikes! A monster!

   **Example:** Then she told me I was ‘beautiful, for a black girl’. —Yikes!

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Expression of empathy with unpleasant or undesirable circumstances.

   **Usage:** humorous; informal; often

   **Example:** John has lost his job and can't pay his mortgage; yikes!
