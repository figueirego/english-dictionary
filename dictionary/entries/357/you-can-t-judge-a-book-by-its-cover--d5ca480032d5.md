### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. It is not possible to make reliable judgments about things or people by considering external appearances alone.

   **Usage:** figuratively; idiomatic

   **Source synonyms:** clothes don't make the man; the cowl does not make the monk; all that glitters is not gold; looks can be deceiving; appearances are deceptive; don't just scratch the surface; Anything essential is invisible to the eyes; One sees clearly only with the heart; Other things besides cream float to the top
