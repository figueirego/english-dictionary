**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of or relating to the Granadine Confederation, a short-lived federal republic established in 1858 as a result of a constitutional change replacing the Republic of New Granada, and consisting of the present-day nations of Colombia and Panama and parts of northwestern Brazil.
