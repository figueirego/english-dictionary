### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A more extreme form of patriotism; the idea of a more extreme support for one's country, people or culture.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Communists, socialists, and Islamists are against nationalism.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Support for the creation of a sovereign nation (which does not currently exist).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Basque nationalism

   **Example:** Kurdish nationalism

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Support for the union of Northern Ireland and the Republic of Ireland.

   **Usage:** Ireland; UK; countable; uncountable
