<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A senior secretary or official with administrative or legal duties, sometimes in charge of some area of government such as finance or justice.

   **Example:** Chancellor of the Duchy of Lancaster

   **Example:** Lord Chancellor

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A senior secretary or official with administrative or legal duties, sometimes in charge of some area of government such as finance or justice.
   1. Ellipsis of Chancellor of the Exchequer.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; politics
