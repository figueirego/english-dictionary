### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The selection of one thing or person over others (with the main adposition being "for" in relation to the thing or person, but possibly also "of")

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** He has a preference for crisp wines.

   **Source synonyms:** druthers; way

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The option to so select, and the one selected.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Can I keep my preferences when I upgrade to the new version of this application?

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The state of being preferred over others.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A strong liking or personal valuation.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
