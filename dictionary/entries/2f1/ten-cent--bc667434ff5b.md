### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. two-bit (insignificant or worthless); cheap (both inexpensive and poorly made).

   **Usage:** dated; idiomatic; not-comparable; usually

   **Example:** Forget that frickin' ten-cent supplier. From now on, we'll buy only from trusted partners.

   **Example:** Enough of these ten-cent tools. From now on, we'll buy only the good stuff that doesn't break so easily.

   **Source synonyms:** poor man's; dollar-store; Wish.com; Poundland; pound-shop; Tesco Value; BTEC

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see ten, cent&#58; costing 10¢.

   **Usage:** dated; not-comparable; usually
