## Noun

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈpɪŋ.ə(ɹ)/ — source labels: UK
- IPA: /ˈpɪŋ.ɚ/ — source labels: US

### Forms

- pingers — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A device that emits a short, high-pitched sound burst, such as in sonar or other echo location systems.

   **Example:** We couldn't take the boat into the shallows near the reef because our sonar pinger was broken.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A device that periodically emits a signal that can be monitored to permit movement tracking.

   **Example:** The scientist attached a pinger to the carapace of the sea turtle to track its migration.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A computer program that sends a ping message over a network.

   **Example:** Some people like fancy pingers with extra features, but I always use the default ping application.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A user of a ping program; one who pings.

   **Example:** Every time we replace the main page, we get flooded with pingers making sure our web site is still there.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An illicit drug, especially ecstasy, taken for recreational purposes.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Example:** That was a great party. Everyone took a pinger or two.
