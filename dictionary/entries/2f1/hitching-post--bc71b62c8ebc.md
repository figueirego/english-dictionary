## Noun

### Forms

- hitching posts — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A post to which a horse (or other animal) may be tethered to prevent it from straying.

   **Example:** Joe tied his horse to the hitching post before going into the bank.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A main shelter at a recreation place (e.g. a campground) where people go to socialize, eat, and purchase small items.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Example:** The two girls who were camping went to the hitching post to buy some hot dogs and buns for the wiener roast.
