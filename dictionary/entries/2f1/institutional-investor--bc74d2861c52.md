## Noun

### Forms

- institutional investors — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A (usually large) organization that invests (usually large amounts of) money, often on behalf of others for whom it is a fiduciary (such as workers building their savings toward their retirement).

   **Example:** The state employees' pension fund is the main institutional investor with a stake in that company.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of the individual persons whose money is invested by such an organization.

   **Usage:** sometimes

   **Example:** This level of risk does not appeal to institutional investors who are worried about a nosedive of their 401(k) balances.
