### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In the same way, each to the other; reciprocally.

   **Example:** The contract was mutually binding.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In a shared manner; equally; affecting all parties the same way.

   **Example:** The adventure was mutually beneficial.
