### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. On a wind instrument, to articulate a note by starting the air with a tap of the tongue, as though by speaking a 'd' or 't' sound (alveolar plosive).

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** Playing wind instruments involves tonguing on the reed or mouthpiece.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To manipulate with the tongue.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To lick, penetrate or manipulate with the tongue during flirting or oral sex.

   **Usage:** slang; sometimes; transitive; vulgar

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To protrude in relatively long, narrow sections.

   **Example:** a soil horizon that tongues into clay

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To join by means of a tongue and groove.

   **Example:** to tongue boards together

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To talk; to prate.

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

**Source sense R2.S7**

7. To speak; to utter.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r2-s8"></a>

**Source sense R2.S8**

8. To chide; to scold.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r2-s9"></a>

**Source sense R2.S9**

9. To flense

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive
