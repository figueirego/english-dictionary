### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To be so presumptuous as (to do something) without proper authority or permission \[with to (+ infinitive)\].

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I don't want to pretend to be an authority on this line of business, so I would never presume to tell you how to run your own company.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To perform, do (something) without authority; to lay claim to without permission.

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive

   **Example:** Don't make the decision yourself and presume too much.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To assume or suggest to be true (without proof); to take for granted, to suppose.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Paw-prints in the snow allow us to presume a visit from next door's cat.

   **Example:** Dr. Livingstone, I presume?

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To take as a premise; to assume for the sake of argument.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To impose (on) for one's advantage; to be presumptuous; to take advantage (of); to take liberties (with) \[with on or upon\].

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Thanks, but I can't accept the money; I would not want to presume on the generosity of a stranger'
