### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A gap or space between two adjacent teeth, especially the upper front incisors (in humans).

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; pathology; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A gap between teeth of different types, e.g. incisors and molars, which is a feature of some rodents and ungulates.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; biology; medicine; natural-sciences; sciences; zoology

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any abnormal space, fissure, or cleft in an organ or other part of the body.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; pathology; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The modified protoplasm at the equator of a cell, existing before mitotic division.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; cytology; medicine; natural-sciences; sciences
