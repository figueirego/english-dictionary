### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A pardon of a sin; (chiefly historical, also figuratively) the forgiveness of an offence, or relinquishment of a (legal) claim or a debt.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** acceptilation

   **Source antonyms:** irremission

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A lessening of amount due, as in either money or work, or intensity of a thing.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A lessening of amount due, as in either money or work, or intensity of a thing.
   1. A reduction or cancellation of the penalty for a criminal offence; in particular, the reduction of a prison sentence as a recognition of the prisoner's good behaviour.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

      **Source synonyms:** remitment

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A lessening of amount due, as in either money or work, or intensity of a thing.
   1. An abatement or lessening of the manifestations of a disease; a period where the symptoms of a disease are absent.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Example:** Her cancer was in remission.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

      **Source synonyms:** anesis; remittence

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An act of remitting, returning, or sending back.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. An act of remitting, returning, or sending back.
   1. A referral of a case back to another (especially a lower or inferior) court of law; a remand, a remittal.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Reflection or back-scattering of light by a material; (to send back)

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; spectroscopy
