### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A small triangular flag, especially one attached to a knight's lance; a pennon.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Alternative form of pavane.

   **Usage:** alt-of; alternative

   **Alternative form of:** [pavane](../3e8/pavane--fa2b4096baf0.md)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

3. The peacock bass

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any of various birds with ornate plumage, similar to a peacock.

   **Usage:** South-America

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A tropical butterfly, Doxocopa pavon, noted for the iridescent purple coloration of the male.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A subtropical plant of genus Peperomia.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**
