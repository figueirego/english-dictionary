### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A line down the center of a road, generally one that divides traffic moving in opposite directions.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A line, either demarcated or imagined, representing the precise center (middle) of something (such that what lies on one side of it is precisely equal in size to what lies on the other side); the central axis represented by this line.

   **Usage:** trades
