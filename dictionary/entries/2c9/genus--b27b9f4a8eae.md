### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A category in the classification of organisms, ranking below family (Lat. familia) and above species.

   **Example:** All magnolias belong to the genus Magnolia.

   **Example:** Other species of the genus Bos are often called cattle or wild cattle.

   **Example:** There are only two genera and species of seadragons.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences; taxonomy

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A category in the classification of organisms, ranking below family (Lat. familia) and above species.
   1. A taxon at this rank.

      **Usage:** by ellipsis

      **Example:** In most cases, at subsequent mentions of the binomial name you can abbreviate the genus with no danger of confusing the reader.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences; taxonomy
