## Adverb

### Forms

- most yawningly — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In a yawning way; accompanied by yawns.

   **Example:** She stumbled downstairs and yawningly prepared breakfast.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. So as to open wide; gapingly.

   **Example:** A chasm opened yawningly before them.
