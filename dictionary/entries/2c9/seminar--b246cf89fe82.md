### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A class held for advanced studies in which students meet regularly to discuss original research, under the guidance of a professor.

   **Example:** Students attended a seminar on climate change.

   **Example:** She gave a guest lecture during the philosophy seminar.

   **Source synonyms:** institute

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A meeting held for the exchange of useful information by members of a common business community.

   **Example:** The company hosted a training seminar for new employees.
