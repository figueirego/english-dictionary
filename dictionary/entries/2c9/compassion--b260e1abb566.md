## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /kəmˈpæʃ.ən/ — source labels: UK; US

### Forms

- compassions — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Deep awareness of the suffering of others that people have to the point of them being motivated to relieve such states.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Deep awareness of the individual experiences of suffering of others or themselves that people have to the point of them being motivated to relieve such experiences, as reflected by the psychological term self-compassion.

   **Usage:** broadly; uncountable; usually

   **Example:** True leadership lies in compassion, not in coercion.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; psychology; sciences
