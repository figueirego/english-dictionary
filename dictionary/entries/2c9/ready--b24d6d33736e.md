### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Prepared for immediate action or use.

   **Example:** I made ready to set out on my journey.

   **Example:** The troops are ready for battle.

   **Example:** The porridge is ready to serve.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Prepared for immediate action or use.
   1. first only used predicatively, freely used from the end of the 17th century

      **Example:** a loaf of ready-sliced bread

      **Example:** The cave was like a ready-made home for us.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Inclined; apt to happen.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Liable at any moment.

   **Example:** The seed is ready to sprout.

   **Source synonyms:** fit

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Not slow or hesitating; quick in action or perception of any kind.

   **Example:** a ready apprehension

   **Example:** ready wit

   **Example:** a ready writer or workman

   **Source synonyms:** dexterous; prompt; [easy](../25f/easy--97c10efe01d5.md); expert

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Offering itself at once; at hand; opportune; convenient.
