## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A city in northeastern Albania, located in the north of the Luma between the rivers Black Drin and White Drin just upstream from their confluence into the river Drin; it is the seat of its eponymous county, municipality and municipal unit.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A county of Albania.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A municipality of the county of Kukës, Albania.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A municipal unit of the municipality of Kukës in the county of Kukës, Albania.
