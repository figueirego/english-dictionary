## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A valley and district in north-west Aberdeenshire council area, Scotland; the valley is also spelt Strath Bogie.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A town in the Strathbogie Ranges and the shire in Victoria, Australia.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A local government area in northern Victoria, Australia; in full, the Shire of Strathbogie.
