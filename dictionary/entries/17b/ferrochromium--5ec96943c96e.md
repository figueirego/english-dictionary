## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An alloy of iron and chromium, with 50% to 65% chrome; naturally occurring versions of it serve as an ore for chromium metal; it is also made industrially from chromite as a feedstock for the making of other alloys.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** ferrochrome
