### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To coat with a layer of metal by electrochemical means.

   **Usage:** broadly; dated; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** electrogalvanize; electroplate

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To coat with a layer of metal by electrochemical means.
   1. To coat with a layer of zinc (for rust resistance) by electrochemical means.

      **Usage:** broadly; dated; transitive; usually

      **Example:** We then galvanize the steel so that the zinc coating will sacrificially take the corrosion for many years.

      **Source synonyms:** electrogalvanize; electroplate; electrogalvanize; zinc

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To shock or stimulate into sudden activity, as if by electric shock.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** The girl’s picture helped galvanize public opinion against the administration’s policy.

   **Example:** Republicans are hoping a proposed gas-tax repeal will galvanize their voters.
