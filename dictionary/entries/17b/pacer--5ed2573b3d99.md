## Noun · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who paces.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In harness racing, a horse with a gait in which the front and back legs on one side take a step together alternating with the legs on the other side; as opposed to a trotter.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A horse whose natural gait is a pace (where the legs of the same side are lifted together).

   **Usage:** archaic

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A pacemaker (one who sets the pace in a race).

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A timer in an electric toothbrush that indicates how long the user should spend brushing in each part of the mouth.
