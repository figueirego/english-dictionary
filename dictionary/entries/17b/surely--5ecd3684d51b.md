### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Without fail.

   **Example:** slowly but surely

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Certainly, undoubtedly.

   **Usage:** sentence adverb

   **Example:** Surely, you must be joking.

   **Example:** You don’t suppose we're going to pay for you, surely?

   **Example:** She’s surely got the letter by now.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

   **Example:** His feet were planted surely on the ground.
