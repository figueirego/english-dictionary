## Adjective · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Not fenced.
   1. Not enclosed by a fence and thus free to roam over a wider area.

      **Usage:** not-comparable

      **Source context (verbatim):** of animals or people

      **Source synonyms:** free-range

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Not fenced.
   1. Not enclosed by a fence and thus more easily approachable, visitable, encroachable, attackable, and so on.

      **Usage:** not-comparable; usually

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; real-estate

      **Source context (verbatim):** of lands

      **Source synonyms:** fenceless

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Not fenced.
   1. Without protection.

      **Usage:** figuratively; not-comparable

      **Source synonyms:** fenceless; undefended; defenceless; defenseless
