**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A widely cultivated plant, Solanum lycopersicum, having edible fruit.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The savory fruit of this plant, most often red when ripe, treated as a vegetable in horticulture and cooking.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** He was chopping a tomato to put in the salad.

   **Example:** He was eating a tomato when his boss called him.

   **Source synonyms:** golden apple (archaic); love apple (archaic); wolf's peach (obsolete)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

3. A shade of red, the colour typical of a ripe tomato.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

**Source sense R1.S4**

   **Usage:** countable; slang; uncountable
