### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Synonym of egg white (“(uncountable) the clear fluid part of an egg surrounding the yolk, being mostly the protein albumin and water, which turns stiff and white when beaten or cooked; (countable) the white of a particular egg”).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** glair; white; egg white (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A colourless, soluble protein found in blood plasma, egg white, etc.; albumin; (countable) chiefly preceded by a descriptive word&#58; one form of such a protein.

   **Usage:** archaic; broadly; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biochemistry; biology; chemistry; microbiology; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The endosperm of seeds of most flowering plants, especially when found in mature seeds.

   **Usage:** archaic; broadly; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Synonym of blood plasma (“the clear fluid portion of blood in which the blood cells are suspended”).

   **Usage:** broadly; obsolete; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hematology; medicine; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** blood plasma (synonym; synonym-of)
