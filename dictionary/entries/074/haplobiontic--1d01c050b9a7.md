## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of a plant or fungus&#58; having either a haploid or a diploid phase (but not both) in its life cycle, and thus lacking any alternation of generations.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The algal ancestors of land plants were probably haplobiontic.
