### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An individual weapon for war, especially a mechanical one such as a siege engine or tank.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The military resources of a belligerent country considered as a whole.

   **Example:** The German war machine was more powerful than that of the allies at the start of the war.

   **Source synonyms:** military-industrial complex
