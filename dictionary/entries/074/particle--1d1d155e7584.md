### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A very small piece of matter, a fragment; especially, the smallest possible part of something.

   **Source synonyms:** bit; chip; chunk; tranche; clip; crumb; crumbling; cutting; flake; fleak (dialectal; obsolete); fragment; hunk; lump; mammock (obsolete); ort; piece; piecemeal; portion; scrap; scraplet; slice; snead; snippet; steck (dialectal; obsolete)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of various physical objects making up the constituent parts of an atom; an elementary particle or subatomic particle.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A part of speech that has no inherent lexical definition but must be associated with another word to impart meaning, often a grammatical category&#58; for example, the English word to in a full infinitive phrase (to eat) or O in a vocative phrase (O Canada), or as a discourse marker (mmm).

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** grammar; human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** p-word

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A part of speech which cannot be inflected.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. In the Roman Catholic church, a crumb of consecrated bread; also the smaller breads used in the communion of the laity.
