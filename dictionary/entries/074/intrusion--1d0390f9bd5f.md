### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The forcible inclusion or entry of an external group or individual; the act of intruding.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** She viewed sales calls as an unwelcome intrusion.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The forcible inclusion or entry of an external group or individual; the act of intruding.
   1. A penetration by hackers, a computer virus, etc.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Example:** The intrusion of the virus rendered our other security efforts useless.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The forcible inclusion or entry of an external group or individual; the act of intruding.
   1. The insertion of a phoneme into the pronunciation of a word despite its absence from the spelling. (e.g. intrusive r)

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; phonology; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Something that is intrusive or that has arisen from an act of intruding.
   1. A feeling of being intruded upon; the state or condition of being intruded.

      **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Something that is intrusive or that has arisen from an act of intruding.
   1. Magma forced into other rock formations; the rock formed when such magma solidifies.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; geology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Something that is intrusive or that has arisen from an act of intruding.
   1. A structure that lies within a historic district but is nonhistoric and irrelevant to the district.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Something that is intrusive or that has arisen from an act of intruding.
   1. An involuntarily arising idea or memory that is nuisant and falsifies an accurate impression of the world.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; psychology; sciences

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Something that is intrusive or that has arisen from an act of intruding.
   1. A swarm or nest of cockroaches, infesting a structure.

      **Usage:** collective; countable; rare; uncountable

      **Example:** When I turned on the light, scattered mats of cockroach intrusions scurried into the walls.

      **Example:** The sight of an intrusion had me running for an exterminator.
