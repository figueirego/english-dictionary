### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Thoroughly; including every detail; with all particulars.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I do not understand it; please explain it to me in detail.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. By engaging small sections of an enemy army rather than fighting a single pitched battle against the entire enemy force.

   **Example:** Darius defeated the rebels in detail.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war
