### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A harbor, river, or offshore area that can accommodate a ship at anchor, either for quarantine, queuing, or discharge.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A fee charged for anchoring.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Anchorage is five pounds a night outside the harbour.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. That into which something is anchored or fastened.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** the anchorages of the Brooklyn Bridge

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The surgical fixation of prolapsed organs.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The act of anchoring, or the condition of lying at anchor.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The set of anchors belonging to a ship.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The retreat of a hermit, or anchorite.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Something on which one may depend for security; ground of trust.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable
