## Adverb

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈpɹæk.tɪ.k(ə.)li/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Forms

- more practically — comparative
- most practically — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In practice; in effect or in actuality, though possibly not officially, technically, or legally.

   **Example:** Stalin was not technically or legally the head of state but was practically so.

   **Source synonyms:** de facto; effectively; as much as

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Almost completely; almost entirely.

   **Example:** He was practically uneducated, barely able to read and write a simple sentence.

   **Source synonyms:** effectively; nearly; virtually

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. With respect to practices or a practice.

   **Example:** He wasn't very good with words or numbers; he was more of a practically minded person

   **Example:** He was practically educated and lacked theoretical depth.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. In a way that is practical&#58; feasibly; unchallengingly.

   **Example:** practically achievable

   **Source synonyms:** practicably
