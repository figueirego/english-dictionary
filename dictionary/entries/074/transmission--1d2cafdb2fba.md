### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of transmitting, e.g. data (signals) or electric power.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The fact of being transmitted.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Something that is transmitted, such as a message, picture, or a disease; the sending of such a thing.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The passage of a nerve impulse across synapses.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A device, usually an assembly of gears (gearbox) plus a clutch or torque converter, through which power is sent from the engine to the driveshaft in an automobile (motor car or motor truck).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; trucking; heavy equipment; trucking; heavy equipment

   **Example:** Any vehicle with an internal combustion engine needs a transmission that can alter the gear ratio (engine RPM to wheel RPM) as the vehicle's speed changes.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** automotive; transport; vehicles

   **Source synonyms:** gearbox\[Appendix&#58;Glossary; tranny

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The right possessed by an heir or legatee of transmitting to his successor(s) any inheritance, legacy, right, or privilege, to which he is entitled, even if he should die without enjoying or exercising it.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The passing of a pathogen or communicable disease from an infected host individual or group to a conspecific individual or group.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; medicine; natural-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. The passing down of a text through time.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences; textual-criticism
