### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A machine for cutting up stalks of corn for cattle feed.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A machine for harvesting corn.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An implement consisting of a long blade attached to a handle at nearly a right angle, used for cutting down the stalks of Indian corn.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>
