## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

- Morrisites — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A follower of Joseph Morris, the English-born American leader of a schismatic Mormon movement called Church of the Firstborn that expected an imminent Second Coming.

   **Usage:** Mormonism; historical

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A follower of Lewis Morris, an American revolutionary and Federalist politician.

   **Usage:** historical

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; politics

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A follower of William Morris, a British anarchist socialist and Romantic artist associated with the Arts and Crafts movement.

   **Usage:** historical
