### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To remove tangles or knots from.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** With gentle combing, untangle your hair.

   **Source synonyms:** disentangle; unravel; tangle; enmesh; ensnare (figuratively); ensnarl; entangle; foul; implicate (archaic); imply (archaic); involve (figuratively); mat; ravel; snarl; tangle

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To remove confusion or mystery from.

   **Usage:** broadly; figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** It took a while, but he finally untangled the problem.

   **Source synonyms:** demystify
