### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To do something insufficiently; especially, to undercook.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Sorry to say, I've underdone the pot roast. I think we'd better put it back in the oven for a bit.

   **Example:** Sorry to say, I've underdone the downspout repair. I think we'd better hire someone to replace the whole thing.

   **Source antonyms:** overdo; overcook

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To do something insufficiently; especially, to undercook.
   1. To do less than is requisite.

      **Usage:** transitive

      **Example:** We warned him not to underdo it. This customer expects attention to detail.

      **Source synonyms:** half-ass; shortchange; phone in

      **Source antonyms:** overdo; overcook

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To do something insufficiently; especially, to undercook.
   1. To act below one's abilities; do less than one can.

      **Usage:** intransitive; transitive; uncommon

      **Source synonyms:** underachieve; slack off

      **Source antonyms:** overdo; overcook

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To put under, subject.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive
