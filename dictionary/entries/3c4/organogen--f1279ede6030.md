### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of the four elements — carbon, hydrogen, oxygen, and nitrogen — that are especially characteristic of organic compounds.

   **Usage:** dated

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
