### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To behave or act naturally, without regard to how this behavior is perceived by others.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** All you have to do is be yourself.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To be as one usually is, as opposed to being sick, depressed, etc.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** What's wrong? You're not yourself today.
