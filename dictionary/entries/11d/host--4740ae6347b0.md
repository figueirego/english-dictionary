### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One which receives or entertains a guest, socially, commercially, or officially.

   **Example:** A good host is always considerate of the guest’s needs.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One that provides a facility for an event.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A person or organization responsible for running an event.

   **Example:** Our company is host of the annual conference this year.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A moderator or master of ceremonies for a performance.

   **Example:** The host was terrible, but the acts themselves were good.

   **Source synonyms:** presenter

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The primary member of a system, typically the member who fronts most often.

   **Usage:** multiplicity

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Any computer attached to a network.

   **Usage:** Internet

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A cell or organism which harbors another organism or biological entity, usually a parasite.

   **Example:** Viruses depend on the host that they infect in order to be able to reproduce.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; ecology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. An organism bearing certain genetic material, with respect to its cells.

   **Usage:** evolution

   **Example:** The so-called junk DNA is known, so far, to provide no apparent benefit to its host.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; genetics; medicine; natural-sciences; sciences
