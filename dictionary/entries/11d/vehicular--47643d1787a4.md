### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of or pertaining to a vehicle or vehicles, usually specifically cars and trucks; involving a vehicle.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** vehicular homicide

   **Example:** Ernest had a fear of vehicular travel, and ended up walking everywhere.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. That is used between two groups who do not share a common native tongue nor a common culture.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** vehicular language

   **Example:** English is used as a vehicular language in many business settings.

   **Example:** Latin was a vehicular language for natural philosophers and others from the late Renaissance to the late 18th century.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a language
