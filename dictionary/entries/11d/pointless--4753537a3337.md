### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having no point or sharp tip; terminating squarely or in a rounded end.

   **Example:** a pointless sword

   **Example:** a pointless knife

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Having no prominent or important feature, as of an argument, discourse, etc.

   **Example:** a pointless remark

   **Example:** The sequel to the film was even more pointless than the original

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Having no purpose; purposeless; unable to effect an aim.

   **Example:** Since the decision has already been made, further discussion seems pointless.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Having no points

   **Example:** After four matches and four defeats, United remain pointless at the foot of the table.
