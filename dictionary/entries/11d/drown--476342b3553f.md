### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To die from suffocation while immersed in water or other fluid.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** When I was a baby, I nearly drowned in the bathtub.

   **Source synonyms:** drench (obsolete)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To kill by suffocating in water or another liquid.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The car thief fought with an officer and tried to drown a police dog before being shot while escaping.

   **Source synonyms:** drench (obsolete)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To be flooded&#58; to be inundated with or submerged in (literally) water or (figuratively) other things; to be overwhelmed.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** We are drowning in information but starving for wisdom.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To inundate, submerge, overwhelm.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** He drowns his sorrows in buckets of chocolate ice cream.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To obscure, particularly amid an overwhelming volume of other items.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive; usually passive voice

   **Example:** The answers intelligence services seek are often drowned in the flood of information they can now gather.

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** noyade — to kill by suffocating in water or another liquid; flood — to cover, as with water; inundate — to cover, as with water
