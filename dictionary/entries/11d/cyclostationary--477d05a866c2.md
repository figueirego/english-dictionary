## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having statistical properties that vary cyclically with time.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The maximum daily temperature in New York City can be modelled as a cyclostationary process&#58; the maximum temperature on July 21 is statistically different from the temperature on December 20, but it is a reasonable approximation that the temperature on December 20 of different years has identical statistics.
