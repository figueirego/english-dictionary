<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences

   **Source synonyms:** fleur-de-lis

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The contractile membrane perforated by the pupil, which adjusts to control the amount of light reaching the retina, and which forms the colored portion of the eye (See Iris (anatomy) on Wikipedia.Wikipedia ).

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A diaphragm used to regulate the size of a hole, especially as a way of controlling the amount of light reaching a lens.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** arts; broadcasting; cinematography; film; hobbies; lifestyle; media; photography; television

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

   **Usage:** poetic

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; electrical-engineering; electricity; electromagnetism; electronics; energy; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics
