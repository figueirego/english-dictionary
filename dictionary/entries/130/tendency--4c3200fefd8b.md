### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A likelihood of behaving in a particular way or going in a particular direction; a tending toward.

   **Example:** Denim has a tendency to fade.

   **Example:** I have a tendency to get bored after the first half an hour of a movie.

   **Example:** There's a common tendency among first-game visitors to a casino to bet overcautiously.

   **Source synonyms:** inclination; disposition; propensity; penchant; trend

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
