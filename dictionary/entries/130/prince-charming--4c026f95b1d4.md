### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A fictional heroic prince in various fairy-tales.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A heterosexual woman's prototypical ideal romantic partner, especially but not necessarily if he embodies common characteristics of the fictional princes, such as being courageous or chivalrous.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** She's still unmarried because she is waiting for her Prince Charming... and he may not exist.
