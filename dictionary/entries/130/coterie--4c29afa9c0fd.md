### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A circle of individuals who associate with one another for a common purpose.

   **Example:** The new junior employee joined our merry after-hours coterie.

   **Example:** A tightly knit coterie of executive powerbrokers made all the real decisions in the company.

   **Source synonyms:** clique

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A communal burrow of prairie dogs.

   **Example:** The coterie was located in the middle of our wheat field.
