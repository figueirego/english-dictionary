## Noun

### Forms

- street names — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The slang term for a drug when it is traded as a street drug.

   **Example:** They said they'd never heard of medetomidine, but when the cop referred to it by its street name — rhino tranq — their ears perked up.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person’s nickname on the street, such as a gangster or mafia name; (loosely) any nickname.

   **Example:** They said they'd never heard of Mister Dzhugashvili, but when the cop referred to him by his street name — Stalin — their ears perked up.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see street, name.

   **Example:** I know where Eric's house is, but I've forgotten the street name.
