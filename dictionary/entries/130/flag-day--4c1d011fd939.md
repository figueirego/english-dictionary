## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A day on which a registered charity raises money, usually by selling small lapel flags.

   **Usage:** Ireland; UK

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A day on which a registered charity raises money, usually by selling small stickers.

   **Usage:** Hong-Kong

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
