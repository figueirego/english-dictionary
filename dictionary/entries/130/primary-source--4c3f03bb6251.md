### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An original work, especially one by a notable author whose work is now the subject of commentaries (secondary sources).

   **Usage:** scholarly studies

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A historical object, especially a written document, that was created at or near the time of the events studied.
