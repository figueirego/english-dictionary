### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Affected with scabs; full of scabs.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Diseased with the scab (mange)&#58; mangy.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Having a blotched, uneven appearance.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** media; printing; publishing

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Injured by the attachment of barnacles to the carapace of a shell.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Working against union policies, working to bust unions; in particular, being a scab (worker who crosses a union picket line).

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. stingy; scrounging.

   **Usage:** Ireland; UK; slang

   **Example:** The chipper was a bit scabby on the vinegar today.

   **Example:** I lent you a fiver last week and you still haven’t paid me back, you scabby bastard!

   **Example:** You never bring you’re own pen but always ask to borrow mine, you scabby git!
