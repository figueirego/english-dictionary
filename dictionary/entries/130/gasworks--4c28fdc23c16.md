### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A factory where gas is generated or distributed (whether for industrial consumption or for residential distribution).

   **Source synonyms:** gas plant

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A factory where gas is generated or distributed (whether for industrial consumption or for residential distribution).
   1. One where coal is converted to coal gas and coke (via gasification).

      **Source synonyms:** gas plant

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A factory where gas is generated or distributed (whether for industrial consumption or for residential distribution).
   1. One where water gas was generated for distribution.

      **Usage:** historical

      **Source synonyms:** gas plant

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A factory where gas is generated or distributed (whether for industrial consumption or for residential distribution).
   1. One that formerly generated coal gas or water gas and now distributes natural gas from a pipeline.

      **Source synonyms:** gas plant
