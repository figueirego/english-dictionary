<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any spherical (or nearly spherical) object.
   1. A part of a device, often a lamp.

      **Example:** the globe of a lamp

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

4. A light bulb.

   **Usage:** Australia; South-Africa; dated

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A circular military formation used in Ancient Rome, corresponding to the modern infantry square.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

   **Usage:** in-plural; slang; quite uncommon

   **Usage:** obsolete
