<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make something holy by religious rite, sanctify.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To invoke divine favor upon.

   **Example:** In some countries, priests bless farm animals on St. Anthony's Day.

   **Example:** In many families, a grace is said before dinner to give thanks for and bless the food.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To honor as holy, glorify; to extol for excellence.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To esteem or account happy; to felicitate.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To make the sign of the cross upon, so as to sanctify.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To wave; to brandish.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To turn (a reference) into an object.

   **Usage:** transitive; Perl programming; past tense only blessed; Perl programming; past tense only blessed

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To secure, defend, or prevent from.

   **Usage:** archaic

   **Source context (verbatim):** with from

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To give or send.
