## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see not, go, anywhere.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Able to be disregarded and dealt with later.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** You shouldn't get too anxious about your vacation next month. The beach is not going anywhere.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Dependable; not inclined to leave or bail out unexpectedly.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** You can count on me. I'm not going anywhere.

**Source sense R1.S4**

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** My research is not going anywhere.
