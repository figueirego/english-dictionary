## Noun

### Forms

- Shakespearianism — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The devotion to or the teaching of the works and terminology of William Shakespeare.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** I thee tell, Woman&#58; our Son is become lost to Shakespeareanism and Latin, all but unwilling to be a Typesetter as his Father.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A term coined by Shakespeare, or otherwise derived from his works.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** It is said that the English language has over 1,700 Shakespereanisms.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Shakespeare's works collectively.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** A small number of persons has ever perused Shakespeareanism in its entirety.

   **Example:** Dr. Hallsworth is a scholar of literature, with an expertise on the Shakespeareanism.
