### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A condition characterised by a significant limitation in a person's physical or intellectual powers or ability.

   **Usage:** countable; medical model of disability

   **Example:** people with disabilities

   **Source synonyms:** handicap

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The state of having such a condition; the state of being disabled.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** disabledness; incapacity

   **Source antonyms:** ability; able-bodiedness

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A limitation in function (the things that a person can do or achieve) that is driven by any combination of their impairments (the medical facts about them) and the imperfect state of society's inaccessibility that sometimes fails to accommodate their needs.

   **Usage:** countable; social model of disability

   **Source synonyms:** handicap

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An inability.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete

   **Source synonyms:** impotence

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A lack of legal qualification to do something; legal incapacity or incompetency.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

   **Source synonyms:** disqualification

   **Source antonyms:** capacity; competency; qualification

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A lack of legal qualification to do something; legal incapacity or incompetency.
   1. A duty to refrain from altering a person's legal relations; correlative of an immunity.

      **Usage:** countable; jurisprudence

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

      **Source synonyms:** disqualification

      **Source antonyms:** capacity; competency; qualification

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Regular payments received by a disabled person, usually from the state.

   **Usage:** informal; uncountable

   **Example:** I had to go on disability after the accident.

   **Example:** Did you get your disability this month?

   **Source synonyms:** disability benefits
