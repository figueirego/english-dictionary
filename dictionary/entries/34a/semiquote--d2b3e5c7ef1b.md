## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

- semiquotes — plural
- semi-quote — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A single quotation mark, ('). This is often used for a quote within a quote, as in "Tom said 'What?'"

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A punctuation mark to indicate that the text is a semi-quote, i.e. a close paraphrase that uses some of the author's original words.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A partly paraphrased quotation.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** "A fanatic is someone who will not change his mind or the subject of discussion" is a semiquote of Winston Churchill.
