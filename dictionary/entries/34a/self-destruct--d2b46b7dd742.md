### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To destroy oneself or itself.

   **Usage:** figuratively; usually

   **Example:** The submarine was primed to self-destruct.

   **Source synonyms:** autodestruct

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To destroy or greatly damage one's position in a game, in one's work or career, in one's financial or social position, etc.

   **Example:** If you attack with an energy level of only 30 you are going to self-destruct.

   **Example:** Dating the boss is a bad move&#58; if it goes badly, you could self-destruct.
