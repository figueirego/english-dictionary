### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The study of oneself.

   **Usage:** countable; literary; uncommon; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The property of a word or phrase which describes itself.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The words multisyllabic and sesquipedalian display autology&#58; multisyllabic is a multisyllabic word, and sesquipedalian is a sesquipedalian word.
