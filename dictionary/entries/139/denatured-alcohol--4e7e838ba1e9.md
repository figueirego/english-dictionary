### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Reasonably pure ethyl alcohol for industrial purposes that has been made unfit for consumption by the addition of a poisonous substance such as acetone or methyl alcohol.

   **Usage:** US; uncountable; usually

   **Source synonyms:** methylated spirit; methylated spirits
