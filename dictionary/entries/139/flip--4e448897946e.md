   **Source synonyms:** flip over; turn; turn over; turn upside down

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** toss

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

   **Usage:** US; transitive

4. To turn state's evidence; to agree to testify against one's co-conspirators in exchange for concessions from prosecutors.

   **Usage:** US; intransitive

   **Example:** The mafioso flipped on his superiors to get a lighter sentence.

**Source sense R2.S5**

   **Usage:** US; transitive
