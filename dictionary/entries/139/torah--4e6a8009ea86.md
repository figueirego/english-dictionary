### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The first five books of the Hebrew Scriptures, traditionally attributed to Moses and therefore also known as the Five Books of Moses.

   **Example:** Tradition holds that the Torah was handed down to Moses on Mount Sinai.

   **Source synonyms:** Five Books of Moses; Books of Moses; Chumash; Law; Pentateuch

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The full body of written Jewish law, including the Tanakh, the Talmud, the Mishnah and the midrashic texts.

   **Usage:** Judaism

   **Example:** It says in the Torah that both gossip and murder cause irreparable damage.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The whole of Jewish law, both written and unwritten.

   **Usage:** Judaism

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The encompassing philosophy of Judaism.

   **Usage:** Judaism
