## Verb · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To gradually or steadily flow upwards or outwards so as to begin to fill or overflow something.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Oil was welling up out of the borehole.

   **Example:** As soon as I heard news of his death, tears started to well up in my eyes.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To accumulate within one, to the point of overwhelming one.

   **Usage:** broadly; figuratively

   **Example:** Emotion welled up inside me.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of an emotion
