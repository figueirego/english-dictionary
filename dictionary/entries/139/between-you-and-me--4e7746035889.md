## Prep\_phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In confidence; used to impose an obligation to keep something being discussed secret.

   **Example:** Between you and me, I think the boss is crazy.

   **Example:** Let’s keep this conversation between you and me.

   **Example:** This is just between you and me.

   **Source synonyms:** between ourselves; between you and I; between you and me and these four walls; between you, me, and the bedpost
