### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To be quiet; to keep quiet.

   **Usage:** intransitive; onomatopoeic

   **Example:** He wouldn't shush so I kicked him.

   **Example:** Shush, my parents are back!

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To ask someone to be quiet, especially by saying shh.

   **Usage:** intransitive; onomatopoeic; transitive

   **Example:** The boy in front of us was making too much noise, so we shushed him.
