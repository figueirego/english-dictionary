### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The office or role of president.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** After many years as a party leader, she finally ascended to the presidency.

   **Example:** The candidate finally declared for the presidency earlier this morning.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The bureaucratic organization and governmental initiatives devolving directly from the president.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Because of the president's lame duck status, the presidency was often hampered by congressional actions.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The time during which one is president; a president's term of office.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** In France, a presidency lasts for five years.

   **Example:** Ernest was a historian specializing in the presidency of Herbert Hoover.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A supreme local council made up of three persons; (usually capitalized; also First Presidency) the highest authority.

   **Usage:** Mormonism; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. One of the three original provinces of British India.

   **Usage:** India; UK; countable; historical; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The role of ordained clergy in the celebration of the Eucharist in some denominations.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity
