### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Made of wood.

   **Example:** a wooden boat

   **Example:** On a recent windy day, hundreds of visitors climbed wooden stairs to take pictures in front of the glacier.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. As if made of wood; moving awkwardly, or speaking with dull lack of emotion.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** wooden acting
