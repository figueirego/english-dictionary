### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of the days of a week on which work is done; any day in a workweek.

   **Usage:** US

   **Example:** It will take five workdays to process your application.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. That part of a day in which work is done.

   **Usage:** US

   **Example:** My workday is 8 hours.

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** working day (UK; mainly); 9 to 5 — part of the day; nine to five — part of the day; weekday — day on which work is done; business day — day on which work is done in legal and official usage; jobday
