### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A country in southern Europe that existed until 1870, comprising the territories of Italy (and some enclaves in France) over which the pope was the ruler in a civil as well as a spiritual sense.

   **Usage:** historical; plural-normally

   **Source synonyms:** States of the Church (dated); Pontifical States; Ecclesiastical State (archaic)
