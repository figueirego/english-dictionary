## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A situation in which progress is more than offset by adverse developments.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** We got all the roofing removed, but the chimney was damaged and we discovered that some rafters had to be replaced. One step forward, two steps back. Or maybe three.
