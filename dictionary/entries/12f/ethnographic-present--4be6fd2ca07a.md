### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The present tense formerly used in ethnographies, even for cultures that no longer existed by the time they were written.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** When he said they hunt buffalo, he was using the ethnographic present- there are no buffalo on the reservation.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anthropology; human-sciences; sciences
