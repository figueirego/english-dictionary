## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A rare congenital medical condition in which blood vessels and/or lymph vessels fail to form properly. The three main features are nevus flammeus, venous and lymphatic malformations, and soft-tissue hypertrophy of the affected limb.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences
