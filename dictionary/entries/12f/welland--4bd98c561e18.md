### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A placename&#58;
   1. A river in Northamptonshire, Leicestershire, Rutland, Cambridgeshire and Lincolnshire, which flows into the Wash, a large bay on the North Sea.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A placename&#58;
   1. A village and civil parish (served by Little Malvern and Welland Parish Council) in Malvern Hills district, Worcestershire, England (OS grid ref SO7940).

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A placename&#58;
   1. A river in southern Ontario, Canada, which joins the Niagara River and was named after the English river.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A placename&#58;
   1. A city on the Welland River in Niagara regional municipality, Ontario.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   1. An unincorporated community in Mendota Township, LaSalle County, and Brooklyn Township, Lee County, Illinois, United States.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable
