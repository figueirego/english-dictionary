## Noun

### Forms

- totalisms — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A social, economic and/or political system in which some authority (e.g. the state or "the market") wields absolute power; totalitarianism.

   **Usage:** rare; uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A philosophy, ideology or belief system that is total in its scope, one that covers everything.

   **Usage:** countable; rare

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Totality; (the) entirety (of something).

   **Usage:** rare; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Totalness, absoluteness; the characteristic of being absolute in nature or scope.

   **Usage:** rare; uncountable
