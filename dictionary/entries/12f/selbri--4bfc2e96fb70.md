### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A word acting like a predicate, which is immediately preceded by one sumti (“argument”), x\_1, and usually followed by one or more sumti (“arguments”)&#58; x\_2, x\_3... up to no further than x\_5. It is analogous to a verb in natural languages (non-constructed languages).

   **Usage:** Lojban

   **Example:** A selbri together with its associated sumti (if any) forms a bridi (“sentence/predication”). A selbri without any sumti is called an observative bridi (“sentence/predication”).
