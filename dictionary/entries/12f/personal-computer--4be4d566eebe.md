### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A computer for use by one person at a time (using a microprocessor).

   **Example:** He is working on an important project on his personal computer.

   **Source synonyms:** PC; desktop computer; microcomputer

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A computer for use by one person at a time (using a microprocessor).
   1. The IBM Personal Computer.

      **Usage:** historical

      **Source synonyms:** PC; desktop computer; microcomputer
