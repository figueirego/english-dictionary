### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An item of clothing, covering all or part of the hand and fingers, but usually allowing independent movement of the fingers.

   **Example:** I wore gloves to keep my hands warm.

   **Example:** The boxing champ laced on his gloves before the big bout.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A baseball mitt.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The ability to catch a hit ball.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** Frederico had a great glove, but he couldn't hit a curveball, so he never broke into the pros.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; baseball; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

**Source sense R1.S4**

   **Usage:** slang

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A challenge from one to another.

   **Usage:** with-definite-article

   **Example:** to throw down the glove, i.e. to offer a challenge; to take up the glove, to accept it
