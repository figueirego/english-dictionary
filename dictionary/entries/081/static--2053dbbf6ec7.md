### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Interference on a broadcast signal caused by atmospheric disturbances; heard as crackles on radio, or seen as random specks on television.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** The World Series was on, but there was so much static that we could barely even follow the action.

   **Source synonyms:** shash; snow

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Interference or obstruction from people.

   **Usage:** broadly; figuratively; uncountable

   **Example:** I was getting a lot of static from the bean counters whenever I tried to proceed.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. Verbal abuse.

   **Usage:** US; slang; uncountable

   **Example:** Don't you be giving me any static over it. You know the rules.

   **Source synonyms:** flak

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. Static electricity.

   **Usage:** uncountable
