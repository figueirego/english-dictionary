## Prep\_phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In a precarious position, with one's resources nearly exhausted.

   **Example:** Our job as social workers is to help families on the edge.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In a state of excitement due to taking risks.

   **Example:** living on the edge

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. With figurative or temporal proximity to (an event, state of mind, etc.).

   **Usage:** with-of

   **Example:** The stock market was on the edge of collapse.

   **Example:** on the edge of lunacy
