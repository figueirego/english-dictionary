### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The plea or mode of defense under which a person on trial for a crime proves or attempts to prove being in another place when the alleged act was committed.

   **Example:** to set up an alibi

   **Example:** to prove an alibi

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
