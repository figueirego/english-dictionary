### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A line running from the spritsail topmast of a sailing ship to the middle of the forestay in order to steady the mast.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A small line joining the backstay of a sailing ship, or a small line used to prevent the lee backstays from chafing against the yards.
