### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A sink in a kitchen used for washing crockery, cutlery, utensils, food, etc., and disposing of waste.

   **Example:** The kitchen sink was piled high with dirty dishes.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A miscellaneous item or a miscellany, especially exemplifying an indiscriminate profusion.

   **Usage:** also; attributive; figuratively

   **Example:** They threw the kitchen sink at the problem, but still couldn’t fix it.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. In chained or tied to the kitchen sink, etc.&#58; domestic chores or housework, especially when regarded as menial and tedious.

   **Usage:** figuratively; metonymically

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A thing regarded as defiled due to corruption, immorality, etc.

   **Usage:** figuratively; obsolete
