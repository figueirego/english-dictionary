### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To slowly move away from a point, especially if on water.

   **Example:** Watch the seafoam drift away from your feet.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To lose connection to a person, philosophy, or group over time.

   **Example:** I used to be close to it, but then I began to drift away.
