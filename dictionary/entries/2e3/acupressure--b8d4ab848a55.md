### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An alternative medicine technique, derived from acupuncture, in which physical pressure is applied to acupoints.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An act or instance of applying an acupressure technique.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A mode of arresting hemorrhage resulting from wounds or surgical operations, by passing under the divided vessel a needle, the ends of which are left exposed externally on the cutaneous surface.

   **Usage:** countable; dated; uncountable
