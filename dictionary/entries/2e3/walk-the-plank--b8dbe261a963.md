### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. On an early naval vessel or pirate ship&#58; to be forced to walk off the end of a gangplank (a plank of wood extending outwards from the side of the vessel) and plunge into the ocean and drown, used as a method of killing.

   **Usage:** also; figuratively; historical

   **Source synonyms:** walk the gangplank

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To be forced to resign from a position in an organization.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** After he was caught selling company secrets, it’s not surprising they made him walk the plank.

   **Source synonyms:** walk the gangplank
