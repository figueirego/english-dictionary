### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To depart on a voyage by boat or ship.

   **Example:** We set sail for the Caribbean.

   **Source synonyms:** put out

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To depart on a voyage by boat or ship.
   1. To spread and arrange the sails of a sailboat or sailing ship for the purpose of putting out.

      **Usage:** literal

      **Example:** The sailors of the regatta are going to wait out this weather before they set sail.

      **Source synonyms:** put out

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To depart on a voyage by boat or ship.
   1. To do the analogous counterpart on a vessel with any other means of propulsion, such as steaming.

      **Usage:** figuratively

      **Example:** A Navy task force is expected to set sail shortly.

      **Source synonyms:** put out
