### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Made a saint; saint-like, reverenced.

   **Example:** Pope John Paul II, of sainted memory, is a candidate for beatification.

   **Source synonyms:** blessed; holy

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Used to mark a beloved person mentioned in conversation as being deceased.

   **Example:** My sainted mother never had an evening dress.

   **Source synonyms:** of blessed memory; late; late lamented

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. Pious, saintly.

   **Source synonyms:** devoutful; godfearing; godly; Allah-fearing; devout; devoutful (obsolete); frum (Judaism); godfearing; heavenly-minded; inward (obsolete); godly; observant; pious; piteous (obsolete); practicing; religionary (obsolete); sainted; sanctimonious (archaic)

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. Much admired.

   **Source synonyms:** illustrious; prestigeful
