### Pronunciation

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of a place&#58; not having tracks or paths; pathless, untrodden; also, having had all tracks removed.

   **Example:** a trackless desert a trackless forest

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Leaving no track or trace when moving; also, not following any track or path.

   **Usage:** literary

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of a train, tram, etc.&#58; not running on tracks.

   **Example:** a trackless trolley

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** rail-transport; railways; road; transport

   **Source synonyms:** railless
