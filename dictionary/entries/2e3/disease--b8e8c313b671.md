### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An abnormal condition of a human, animal or plant that causes discomfort or dysfunction; distinct from injury insofar as the latter is usually instantaneously acquired.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The tomato plants had some kind of disease that left their leaves splotchy and their fruit withered.

   **Example:** The exposed workers had some kind of disease that left their skin splotchy and their faces withered.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** disorder; illness; sickness; ailment; malady

   **Source antonyms:** health; healthiness; healthfulness; wellness

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any abnormal or harmful condition, as of society, people's attitudes, way of living etc.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A lack of ease; uneasiness; trouble; vexation; disquiet.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
