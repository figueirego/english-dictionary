## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A clinical finding in which percussion of the skull near the junction of the frontal, temporal, and parietal bones produces a cracked-pot sound, indicative of separated sutures.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A clinical finding, suggestive of alcoholic coma, in which the pupils are contracted but an external painful stimulation causes dilation followed by slow constriction.
