### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Lacking flow or motion, and thus usually not fresh or healthy; decaying through stillness.

   **Example:** a stagnant pool stagnant water

   **Source synonyms:** motionless; stale; standing; still

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Without activity, change or progress, or excitement in an unhealthy manner; inactive, stale.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** a stagnant economy stagnant prices

   **Example:** Their love had turned stagnant.
