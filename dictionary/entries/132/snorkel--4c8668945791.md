### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A hollow tube, held in the mouth, or mounted on and opening into a diving mask, used by swimmers for breathing underwater.

   **Example:** They wore snorkels while exploring the coral reef.

   **Example:** She used a snorkel to breathe while swimming underwater.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** diving; hobbies; lifestyle; sports; underwater-diving

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A retractable tube fitted in diesel-engine submarines to allow sufficient ventilation that the engines may be used at periscope depth.

   **Source synonyms:** snort

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A snorkel parka.
