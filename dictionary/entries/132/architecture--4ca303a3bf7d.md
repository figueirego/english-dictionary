### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The art and science of designing and managing the construction of buildings and other structures, particularly if they are well proportioned and decorated.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The architecture throughout NYC is amazing.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any particular style of building design.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Construction, in a more general sense; frame or structure; workmanship.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A unifying structure.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A family of CPUs sharing a common instruction set and having partial or full compatibility with software built on each other.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A specific model of a microchip or CPU.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The Intel architectures have more software written for them.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The structure and design of a system or product.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The architecture of the company's billing system is designed to support its business goals.
