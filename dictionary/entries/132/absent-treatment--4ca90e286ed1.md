### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A (nonscientific or pseudoscientific) form of (alleged) medical therapy in which the practitioner brings about healing despite their being physically absent, through telepathy, telekinesis, prayer, or a combination of those.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** teletherapy

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Interpersonal treatment (way of interacting), usually mistreatment, that ignores the presence of a person.

   **Usage:** countable; uncommon; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** silent treatment; cold shoulder
