### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Hortative of go, particularly as a cheer expressing encouragement.

   **Usage:** form-of; hortative

   **Example:** Let's go to the beach next Saturday.

   **Example:** Let's go, team! C'mon!

   **Form of:** [go](../133/go--4cd0e21a9a07.md); particularly as a cheer expressing encouragement

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Let us begin or get started. (a call to put a plan into action)

   **Source synonyms:** let's roll

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Hurry up; be quick.

   **Usage:** colloquial

   **Example:** Let's go, practice starts in five minutes.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Bring it on. (an incitement to fight)

   **Usage:** colloquial

   **Example:** You think you're so tough, huh? Let's go then!

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Yay. (an expression of pleasure, joy, relief, or satisfaction)

   **Usage:** colloquial

   **Example:** Our team is up two points already, let's go!
