### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of or pertaining to a First Nation or First Nations collectively.
   1. Of or pertaining to the indigenous peoples of Canada (typically not including the Inuit or Metis); Indian.

      **Usage:** Canada; not-comparable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of or pertaining to a First Nation or First Nations collectively.
   1. Of or pertaining to the indigenous peoples of any country or region.

      **Usage:** broadly; not-comparable; uncommon

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of or pertaining to a First Nation or First Nations collectively.
   1. Of or pertaining to the indigenous peoples of any country or region.

         **Usage:** Australia; broadly; not-comparable; uncommon
