## Adjective · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of, relating to, or characteristic of a proceeding where one of the involved parties is not present.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The judge issued a temporary ex parte injunction.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Concerning only one side of a matter; one-sided.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

## Adverb · Record 2

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. In the manner of a proceeding where one of the involved parties is not (or sometimes may not be) present.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Grand juries are conducted ex parte; neither the suspect nor his attorney may attend.
