### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To attain success, especially from humble origins; achieve a great deal.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** Keep studying and you'll go far.

   **Source synonyms:** go a long way

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To cover significant expenses.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** A ten-pound note doesn't go far these days.

   **Source context (verbatim):** in negative constructions
