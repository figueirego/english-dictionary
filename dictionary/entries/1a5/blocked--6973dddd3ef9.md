### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Obstructed, so that through movement or flow is prevented or impeded.

   **Example:** This road is blocked, so we must find another route.

   **Example:** Call a plumber to unclog the blocked drain.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Unable to move owing to an obstruction.

   **Example:** The argument was over a blocked car in a driveway.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Banned or barred from connecting or logging on.

   **Usage:** numeral; usually; IP address

   **Example:** Blocked users will be unable to access their accounts indefinitely.

   **Example:** The phone will reject calls from blocked numbers.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** communications; electrical-engineering; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; telecommunications; telephone; telephony

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a person

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Disabled or disconnected, so as to prevent access or from performing certain actions.

   **Example:** All of this user's accounts are now blocked.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a computer account or similar

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Drunk.

   **Usage:** Ireland; slang

**Source sense R1.S6**
