**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To position (something) ahead of time. (The objects thus positioned are usually supplies, tools, instruments, weapons, or troops.)

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** She pre-positioned the surgical instruments in trays on a numbered rack.

   **Example:** The officers deemed it necessary to pre-position at least three battalions in case of a breakout.
