## Noun

### Forms

- queen mothers — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A widowed queen consort (a queen dowager) whose son or daughter from that marriage is the reigning monarch.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A woman, especially an older woman, with authority, preeminence, impressive presence, and/or a founding role in a specified area.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** the queen mother of Canadian literature
