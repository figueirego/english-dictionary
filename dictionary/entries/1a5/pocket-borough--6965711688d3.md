### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A borough which could effectively be controlled by a single person who owned at least half of the burgage tenements (whose occupants had the right to vote in the borough's parliamentary elections), since he could populate these tenements with his own supporters.

   **Usage:** historical

   **Source synonyms:** close borough
