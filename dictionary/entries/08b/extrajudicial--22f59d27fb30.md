**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Out of or beyond the power or authority of a court or judge; beyond jurisdiction.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** An extrajudicial conveyance is effected by the act of the parties and does not involve, as in the fine and recovery, judicial proceedings.
