## Noun

### Forms

- lockages — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Materials for locks in a canal.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The works forming a canal lock or locks.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A toll or fee paid for transporting a vessel through the locks of a canal.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The total height difference (elevation and descent) made by the locks of a canal.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** lift; canal lift

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The use or transit of a canal lock.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A situation where things lock together.

   **Usage:** colloquial; countable; uncountable
