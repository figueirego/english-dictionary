## Interjection

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Indicates a lack of confidence or certainty about the activity about to be tried, but that it will be attempted nonetheless.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** Well, I checked everything and I think it's wired up correctly, so I guess all that's left is to turn it on. Here goes nothing!
