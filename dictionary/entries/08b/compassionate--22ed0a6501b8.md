### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having, feeling or showing compassion (to or toward someone).

   **Example:** The Compassionate, the All-Compassionate

   **Source synonyms:** empathetic; sympathetic; ruthful

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Given to someone as an exception because of a family emergency or a death in their family.

   **Example:** compassionate leave; a compassionate visa

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Inviting or asking for pity.

   **Usage:** obsolete

   **Source synonyms:** piteous; pitiable; ruthful; compassionate (obsolete); pathetic; pathetisad (rare); piteous; pitiable; pitiful; pitisome; sad (slang); poor; rueful; ruthful; snivelly; sorry-ass (slang; vulgar)
