### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The word fuck (or inflected forms like fucking) or fucker.

   **Usage:** euphemistic

   **Example:** Oh, f-word, I've lost the keys.

   **Example:** The dialogue was littered with f-words.

   **Source synonyms:** f-bomb

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The word fag or faggot.

   **Usage:** euphemistic

   **Source synonyms:** f-bomb; f-slur

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The word friends (as opposed to lovers, where a romantic relationship is sought but refused).

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any word beginning with f that is not normally taboo but is considered (often humorously) to be so in the given context.

   **Example:** She has no luck with diets. Don't mention the f-word (by which I mean "fat").
