### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A dessert made by boiling gelatine (or a plant-based alternative such as agar or carrageenan), sugar and some flavouring (often derived from fruit) and allowing it to set.

   **Usage:** Commonwealth; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** His mother prepared jelly for him and his friends for dessert.

   **Source synonyms:** jello

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A clear or translucent fruit preserve, made from fruit juice and set using either naturally occurring, or added, pectin.

   **Usage:** Canada; US; countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** jam

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Clipping of jelly coconut.

   **Usage:** Caribbean; Jamaica; abbreviation; alt-of; clipping; countable; uncountable

   **Alternative form of:** [jelly coconut](../25c/jelly-coconut--97147b067c06.md)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A savoury substance, derived from meat, that has the same texture as the dessert.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Any substance or object having the consistency of the dessert or preserve.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** calf's-foot jelly

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

**Source sense R1.S8**

   **Usage:** US; countable; slang; uncountable
