### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A noisy boaster; a swaggering fellow.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A dry, thin, crispy baked bread (usually salty or savoury, but sometimes sweet, as in the case of graham crackers and animal crackers).

   **Source synonyms:** biscuit (Australia; UK)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A prawn cracker.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The final section of certain whips, which is made of a short, thin piece of unravelled rope, or which is a short piece of twisted string tied to the end of the whip, which produces a distinctive cracking sound when the whip is cracked.

   **Source synonyms:** popper; snapper

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A firecracker.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A Christmas cracker.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A northern pintail, a dabbling duck of species Anas acuta.

   **Usage:** UK

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A person or thing that breaks a thing (e.g., nutcracker).
   1. Refinery equipment used to pyrolyse organic feedstocks. If catalyst is used to aid pyrolysis it is informally called a cat-cracker

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

   1. A pair of fluted rolls for grinding caoutchouc.

      **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. A person or thing that breaks a thing (e.g., nutcracker).
   1. One who cracks (i.e. overcomes) computer software or security restrictions.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

      **Source synonyms:** black hat; hacker

<a id="r1-s12"></a>

**Source sense R1.S12**

12. A fine, great thing or person (crackerjack).

   **Usage:** Australia; Ireland; New-Zealand; UK; slang

   **Example:** She's an absolute cracker!

   **Example:** The show was a cracker!

   **Example:** A cracker of a day.

**Source sense R1.S13**

13. An ambitious or hard-working person (i.e. someone who arises at the 'crack' of dawn).
