## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈɪn.flu.əns/ — source labels: Canada; General-American; Received-Pronunciation
- IPA: /ɪnˈflu.əns/ — source labels: US; nonstandard

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The power to affect, control or manipulate something or someone; the ability to change the development of fluctuating things such as conduct, thoughts or decisions.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** I have absolutely no influence over him.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An action exerted by a person or thing with such power on another to cause change.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** I'm not able to exercise influence over him.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A person or thing exerting such power or action.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** He has been a great influence on the voters during the elections.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An element believed to determine someone's character or individual tendencies, caused by the position of the stars and planets at the time of one's birth.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astrology; human-sciences; mysticism; philosophy; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The action of flowing in; influx.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Electrostatic induction.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; electrical-engineering; electricity; electromagnetism; energy; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics
