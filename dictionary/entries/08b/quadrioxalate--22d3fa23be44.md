### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A salt containing four equivalents of oxalic acid to one equivalent of the basic element (or to one equivalent of a univalent basic radical).

   **Usage:** dated

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences
