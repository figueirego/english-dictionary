## Noun · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A sending or mission (to do or accomplish something).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An official charge or authority to do something, often used of military officers.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** David received his commission after graduating from West Point.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The thing to be done as agent for another.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** I have three commissions for the city.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A body or group of people, officially tasked with carrying out a particular function.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** the European Commission

   **Example:** the Electoral Commission

   **Example:** the Federal Communications Commission

   **Example:** The company's sexual harassment commission made sure that every employee completed the on-line course.

   **Source synonyms:** committee

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A fee charged by an agent or broker for carrying out a transaction.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** a reseller's commission

   **Example:** The real-estate broker charged a four percent commission for their knowledge on bidding for commercial properties; for their intellectual perspective on making a formal offer and the strategy to obtain a mutually satisfying deal with the seller in favour of the buyer.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A bonus in pay awarded to a salesperson for selling a particular, secretly sponsored product, for which the sponsor will reimburse the employer.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; commerce

   **Source synonyms:** spiff; percentage (informal)

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A payment by a fan to an artist, after which the artist will produce a work of art requested by the fan.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** art; arts

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. The act of committing (e.g. a crime or error).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** the commission, preparation or instigation of an act of terrorism

   **Source antonyms:** omission

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To send or officially charge someone or some group to do something.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** James Bond was commissioned with recovering the secret documents.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To place an order for (often a piece of art).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He commissioned a replica of the Mona Lisa for his living room, but the painter gave up after six months.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The aircraft carrier was commissioned in 1944, during WWII.
