### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The point, in an orbit about the Earth, that is farthest from the Earth&#58; the apoapsis of an Earth orbiter.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astronomy; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The point, in an orbit about any planet, that is farthest from the planet&#58; the apoapsis of any satellite.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astronomy; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The point, in any trajectory of an object in space, where it is farthest from the Earth.

   **Usage:** possibly archaic outside astrology

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The highest point.

   **Usage:** figuratively
