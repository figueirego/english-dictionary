### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Meat from a dog eaten as food.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** We visited South Korea this time around, where we ate dog meat for the first time.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Meat prepared to be given to a dog as food.

   **Usage:** uncountable

**Source sense R1.S3**
