### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Moving with speed, rapidity or swiftness, or capable of doing so; rapid; fast.

   **Example:** I ran to the station – but I wasn't quick enough.

   **Example:** He's a quick runner.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Occurring in a short time; happening or done rapidly.

   **Example:** That was a quick meal.

   **Source synonyms:** ready

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Lively, fast-thinking, witty, intelligent.

   **Example:** You have to be very quick to be able to compete in ad-lib theatrics.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Mentally agile, alert, perceptive.

   **Example:** My father is old but he still has a quick wit.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Easily aroused to anger; quick-tempered.

   **Example:** She has a very quick temper.

   **Example:** He is wont to be rather quick of temper when tired.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of people or tempers

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Alive, living.

**Source sense R1.S7**

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a foetus

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Pregnant, especially at the stage where the foetus's movements can be felt; figuratively, alive with some emotion or feeling.

   **Usage:** archaic

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. Flowing, not stagnant.

   **Usage:** archaic

   **Source context (verbatim):** of water

10. Burning, flammable, fiery.
