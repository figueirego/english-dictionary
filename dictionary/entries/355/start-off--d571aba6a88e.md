### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To begin.

   **Example:** She started off with a lullaby.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To set out on a trip.

   **Example:** They started off on horseback.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To instigate someone to laugh or cry or to begin talking about something they often talk about.

   **Example:** Don't start him off about that again.
