## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An arithmetic notation in which numbers precede the operators to be applied to them.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Back in my day, we had reverse Polish notation calculators&#58; You had to write "2 4 3 \* +" instead of "2 + 4 \* 3".
