### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. the process of making rural.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. the change in a country or region when its population migrates from urban to rural areas

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. the process of the formation of villages and the decline of big cities

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. the proportion of a region's population that live in villages and small communities; the rate at which this proportion is growing

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually
