## Noun

### Forms

- three-quarter siblings — plural
- 3/4 sibling — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A sibling with whom one shares about 37.5% of one's genes, as opposed to 25% for half-siblings and 50% for full siblings.
   1. The offspring of a horse's sire/dam that is by/out of a sibling of its other parent.

      **Usage:** horse breeding

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A sibling with whom one shares about 37.5% of one's genes, as opposed to 25% for half-siblings and 50% for full siblings.
   1. The offspring of a horse's dam and a son of its sire.

      **Usage:** horse breeding

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A sibling with whom one shares about 37.5% of one's genes, as opposed to 25% for half-siblings and 50% for full siblings.
   1. The offspring of a horse's sire and a daughter of its dam.

      **Usage:** horse breeding
