### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of or relating to Adam Sedgwick (1785–1873), one of the founders of modern geology, who proposed the Devonian and Cambrian periods.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of or relating to Eve Kosofsky Sedgwick (1950–2009), American writer on gender studies, queer theory, and critical theory.
