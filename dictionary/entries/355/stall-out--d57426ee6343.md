### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To stall completely; to come to an end or quit through stalling

   **Usage:** intransitive; usually; motor; aircraft; motor; aircraft

   **Example:** My car was running fine and then, for no reason, it stalled out.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of an engine

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To slow, come to a halt, and remain; to linger

   **Usage:** intransitive; usually; hurricane

   **Example:** The hurricane is expected to make landfall and then stall out, dumping several inches of rain over several days.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a storm
