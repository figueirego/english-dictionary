## Prep\_phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Actively doing something, or working to solve a (specified) problem.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** —Jack, could you take a look at my computer? It won't boot up. —I'm on it!

   **Source synonyms:** on the case

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Performing at a high level; on good form.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** Verstappen was lacklustre yesterday, but today he's really on it, posting the fastest lap time by half a second.
