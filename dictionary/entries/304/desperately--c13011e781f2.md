### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In a desperate manner; without regard to danger or safety; recklessly.

   **Example:** She desperately grabbed at the nearby branches as she heard the branch she was standing on start to snap.

   **Example:** I could desperately do with some coffee right now.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Extremely.

   **Example:** The race for silver medal was desperately close, they needed a photo finish to separate the runners.
