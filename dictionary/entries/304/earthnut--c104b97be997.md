### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of various roots, tubers, or pods that grow underground&#58;
   1. A tuber belonging to the species Conopodium majus (syns. Bunium flexuosum, Conopodium denudatum), Bunium bulbocastanum (syn. Carum bulbocastanum), or Apios americana (syn. Apios tuberosa).

      **Source synonyms:** earth chestnut; hawknut; pignut

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of various roots, tubers, or pods that grow underground&#58;
   1. A dwarf ginseng&#58; Panax trifolius.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of various roots, tubers, or pods that grow underground&#58;
   1. Synonym of peanut (Arachis hypogaea).

      **Source synonyms:** peanut (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any of various roots, tubers, or pods that grow underground&#58;
   1. An underground tuber of a plant of the genus Tuber.

      **Source synonyms:** truffle
