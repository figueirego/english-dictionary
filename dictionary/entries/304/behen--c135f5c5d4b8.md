## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of various plants imported into Europe for the pharmaceutical trade in dried form, so of uncertain identity.
   1. Behen rubrum or red behen&#58; said in some sources to be Salvia haematodes (now Salvia pratensis subsp. haematodes), but in other sources to be Statice limonium, which is a synonym for Limonium vulgare.

      **Usage:** obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of various plants imported into Europe for the pharmaceutical trade in dried form, so of uncertain identity.
   1. Behen album or white behen&#58; a thistle native to western and central Asia, Centaurea behen

      **Usage:** obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of various plants imported into Europe for the pharmaceutical trade in dried form, so of uncertain identity.
   1. Behen officinarum&#58; Cucubalis behen or Oberna behen, synonyms for Silene vulgaris

      **Usage:** obsolete; uncountable
