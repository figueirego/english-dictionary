### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To receive, especially with a consent, with favour, or with approval.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To admit to a place or a group.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The Boy Scouts were going to accept him as a member.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To regard as proper, usual, true, or to believe in.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I accept the notion that Christ lived.

   **Example:** I can't accept nothing being done about the problem and your standing idly by.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To receive as adequate or satisfactory.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To receive or admit to; to agree to; to assent to; to submit to.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I accept your proposal, amendment, or excuse.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To endure patiently.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I accept my punishment.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To acknowledge patiently without opposition or resistance.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** We need to accept the fact that restaurants are closed due to COVID-19 and that no amount of wishing or screaming will make them reopen any sooner.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To agree to pay.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; law

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To receive officially.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to accept the report of a committee

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. To receive something willingly.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. To do a service done by an establishment.

   **Usage:** Philippines

   **Example:** We accept repairs.

   **Example:** We accept bookbinding.
