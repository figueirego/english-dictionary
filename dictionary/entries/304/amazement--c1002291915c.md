### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The condition of being amazed; a state of overwhelming wonder, as from surprise or sudden fear, horror, or admiration; a feeling of intense astonishment or awe, as from witnessing something incredible and impressive.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** We stared in amazement at the skills on display.

   **Example:** The amazement that painting sparked in me lasted until the tour guide jolted me out of it.

   **Source synonyms:** awe; astonishment; astoundment; fascination; stupefaction

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A particular feeling of wonder, surprise, fear, or horror.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Something which amazes.

   **Usage:** countable; dated
