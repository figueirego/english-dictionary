### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The fruit of the wax myrtle shrub; or the plant itself (Morella cerifera), with aromatic, leathery leaves and waxy berries.

   **Usage:** Canada; US

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The fruit of the wax myrtle shrub; or the plant itself (Morella cerifera), with aromatic, leathery leaves and waxy berries.
   1. Other species in the family Myricaceae, especially in genus Myrica; bay-rum tree, candleberry.

      **Usage:** Canada; US

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The fruit of the bay laurel (Laurus nobilis).

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. West Indian bay tree (Pimenta racemosa), a tropical American shrub with aromatic leaves that are used in the preparation of bay rum.
