## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. News of unpleasant, unfortunate or sad events.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Yes, we just heard the bad news yesterday. We're so sorry for your loss.

   **Source antonyms:** good news

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An irritating, troublesome or harmful object, situation or individual.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; uncountable

   **Example:** Mark my words&#58; that guy is bad news.

   **Source synonyms:** bad business; bad apple; rotten apple; bad seed; bad egg

   **Source antonyms:** good news; breath of fresh air; good egg
