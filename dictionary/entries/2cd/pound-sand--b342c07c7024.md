### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To engage in a simple, useless activity that anyone would reasonably know how to perform.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; informal

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To engage in a futile activity.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; informal

   **Source synonyms:** get blood out of a stone; piss up a rope; pound dirt; pound salt; pound sand into a rathole

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To go away; get lost; go to hell; to waste one's own time (with some other meaningless task) rather than waste the speaker's time with something equally irritating or pointless.

   **Usage:** derogatory; idiomatic; slang; dismissal

   **Example:** All you do is complain. Why don't you go pound sand up your ass and stop bothering the line staff.

   **Source synonyms:** get lost; go fly a kite; take a hike; kick rocks; fuck off
