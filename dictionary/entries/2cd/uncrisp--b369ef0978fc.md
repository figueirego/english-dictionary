### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Not crisp.
   1. Not possessing firmness and freshness or brittleness (especially of foods).

      **Source synonyms:** limp; soft

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Not crisp.
   1. Not quick, precise, accurate or well-defined.

      **Source synonyms:** messy; sloppy; blurry

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Not crisp.
   1. Not curling in stiff curls or ringlets (of hair).

      **Usage:** dated

      **Source synonyms:** straight
