## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of carpeting, that covers all of the floor of a room.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Pervasive, ubiquitous, or unremitting.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; informal; not-comparable

   **Example:** The TV showed wall-to-wall coverage of the bombing.

   **Example:** I am fed up with wall-to-wall pop music.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of a space, full or crowded.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; informal; not-comparable

   **Example:** The airport was wall-to-wall with impatient passengers.
