### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To argue or promote an idea.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I suppose the people who wrote that stuff on the wall were trying to make a point, but they mainly made a mess.

   **Example:** A restaurant my family made a point of going to.

   **Source synonyms:** make a statement

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To take care in doing something; to pay attention or ensure that something is done.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** Make a point to carry \[or of carrying\] your calendar with you at all times.

   **Example:** My wallet is missing! I know I had it earlier&#58; I made a point of bringing it.

   **Example:** He made a point of mentioning that embarrassing incident in front of me whenever he saw me.
