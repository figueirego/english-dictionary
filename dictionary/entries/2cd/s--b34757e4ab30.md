## Suffix · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Added to the end of a singular noun (which normally uses the suffix -s to become plural) to indicate that the noun represents an unknown number which may be one or more than one.

   **Usage:** morpheme

   **Example:** The dog(s) got into the garden.

   **Example:** The singer(s) will perform live.

## Suffix · Etymology 2 · Record 2

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Added to the end of a verb in place of the suffix -s when the subject of the verb may be one or more than one.

   **Usage:** morpheme

   **Example:** The dog(s) get(s) into the garden.

   **Example:** The singer(s) perform(s) live.
