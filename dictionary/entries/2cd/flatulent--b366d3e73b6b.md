### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Affected by gas in the intestine; likely to break wind.

   **Example:** Sorry, I'm a bit flatulent at the moment - just eaten some curry.

   **Source synonyms:** farty (informal)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Causing flatulence.

   **Example:** a flatulent food

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Reminiscent of flatulence.

   **Example:** a flatulent smell

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

   **Usage:** obsolete
