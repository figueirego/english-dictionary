### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Identical pitch between two notes or sounds; the simultaneous playing of notes of identical pitch (or separated by one or more octaves).

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually; acoustics

   **Example:** The unison has a pitch ratio of 1&#58;1.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A sound or note having the same pitch as another, especially when used as the base note for an interval; a unison string.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually; acoustics

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The state of being in harmony or agreement; harmonious agreement or togetherness, synchronisation.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** Everyone moved in unison, but the sudden change in weight distribution capsized the boat.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Two or more voices speaking or singing the same words together.

   **Usage:** broadly; uncountable; usually
