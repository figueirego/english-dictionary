### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An Irish surname, a variant of Herlihy.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An English surname from Old English, a variant of Harley.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A locale in England. Probably from Old English hyrne (“corner, hirn”) + lēah (“woodland; clearing”).
   1. A village and civil parish in Windsor and Maidenhead borough, Berkshire (OS grid ref SU8283).
