### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Woody trees in family Rutaceae
   1. Chloroxylon swietenia (Ceylon or East Indian satinwood)

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Woody trees in family Rutaceae
   1. Zanthoxylum flavum (Jamaican satinwood or West Indian satinwood).

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   1. Murraya paniculata, from southeast Asia and Australia

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable
