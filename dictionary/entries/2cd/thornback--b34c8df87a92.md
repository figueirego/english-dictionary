## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any animal with a thorny back, especially marine animals, such as&#58;
   1. The thornback guitarfish (Platyrhinoidis triseriata), a species of ray in the north-eastern Pacific Ocean.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any animal with a thorny back, especially marine animals, such as&#58;
   1. The thornback ray or thornback skate (Raja clavata), a species of ray in the Atlantic Ocean.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

   1. The thornback skate (Dentiraja lemprieri), a fish endemic to Australia.
