### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Cut in the edge into points or inequalities, like teeth; dented on the surface; jagged; notched; stamped in.

   **Source synonyms:** erose; serrated; dentate; dented; dentilated; erose; gimped; gnawed; indented; jagged; jaggy; nicked; notched; notchy; saw-toothed; serrate; serrated; serriform; toothed

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Having an irregular, uneven border; sinuous; undulating.

   **Example:** a heavily indented coastline

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Notched like the part of a saw consisting of the teeth; serrated.

   **Example:** an indented border or ordinary

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; heraldry; hobbies; lifestyle; monarchy; nobility; politics

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Bound out by an indenture; apprenticed; indentured.

   **Example:** an indented servant

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**
