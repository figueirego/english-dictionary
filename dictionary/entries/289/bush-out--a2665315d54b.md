### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To be bushy; to protrude in a thick tuft.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To become bushy; to grow into the form of a thick tuft.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Removing the shoots on the side of the plant will encourage it to grow upward instead of bushing out.
