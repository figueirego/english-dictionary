### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Alternative letter-case form of Sophist, certain teachers in Ancient Greece, particularly skilled orators.

   **Usage:** alt-of; historical

   **Alternative form of:** [Sophist](../156/sophist--5599f3bf0ba8.md); certain teachers in Ancient Greece; particularly skilled orators

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A teacher who uses plausible but fallacious reasoning.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Source synonyms:** sophister (obsolete)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. One who is captious, fallacious, or deceptive in argument.

   **Usage:** broadly; figuratively

   **Source synonyms:** logic chopper; pilpulist; sophister (obsolete)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Synonym of sophister (“university student who has completed at least one year”).

   **Usage:** dated

   **Source synonyms:** sophister (synonym; synonym-of)
