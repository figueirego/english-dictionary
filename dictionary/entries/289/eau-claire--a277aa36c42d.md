### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A number of places in the United States
   1. A city, the county seat of Eau Claire County, Wisconsin, United States; an on the in the Eau Claire River. Named after the river.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A number of places in the United States
   1. Ellipsis of Eau Claire River&#58; A tributary of the Chippewa River in Wisconsin, United States. Named for its waters.

      **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; ellipsis

      **Alternative form of:** Eau Claire River

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A number of places in the United States
   1. Ellipsis of Eau Claire County&#58; A county of Wisconsin, United States. County seat&#58; Eau Claire. Named after the city, in turn named after the river, named for its waters.

      **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; ellipsis

      **Alternative form of:** [Eau Claire County](../1df/eau-claire-county--77d6f7f9009b.md)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A number of places in Canada

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A number of places in Canada
   1. A neighbourhood of Calgary, Alberta, Canada. Named after the Eau Claire Sawmill from Wisconsin that opened at that location, itself named after the city and river in Wisconsin where the sawmill was founded.
