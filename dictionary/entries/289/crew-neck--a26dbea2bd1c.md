### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Referring to the collar of pullover garments and usually of sweaters and T-shirts, when \[the collar\] circles the neck.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source synonyms:** crewnecked

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Referring to a garment's collar whose front is meant to ride up onto the neck, sometimes to Adam's apple level.

   **Source synonyms:** high crew
