### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An unusual item, such as a painting, or a tabletop knick-knack which encourages comment from visitors, and so helps to break the ice.

   **Example:** I don't think much of the sculpture in their front yard, but I guess it makes a good conversation piece.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A painting of a group of people engaged in conversation or some other activity, popular in the 18th century.

   **Usage:** painting
