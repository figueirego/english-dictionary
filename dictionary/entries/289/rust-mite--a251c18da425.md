## Noun

### Forms

- rust mites — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of various small mites of family Eriophyidae that burrow in the surface of leaves or fruits usually producing brown or reddish patches, including&#58;
   1. A mite which, by puncturing the rind, causes the rust-coloured patches on citrus fruit.
      1. A pink citrus rust mite (Aculops pelekassi)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of various small mites of family Eriophyidae that burrow in the surface of leaves or fruits usually producing brown or reddish patches, including&#58;
   1. A mite which, by puncturing the rind, causes the rust-coloured patches on citrus fruit.
      1. A citrus rust mite (Phyllocoptruta oleivora).

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of various small mites of family Eriophyidae that burrow in the surface of leaves or fruits usually producing brown or reddish patches, including&#58;
   1. A mite of species Aceria anthocoptes, found on Cirsium arvense, (Canada thistle).
