## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A former country in Central Europe, distinguished from the German Democratic Republic, commonly known as East Germany. Official name&#58; Federal Republic of Germany.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A geographic region of Germany, consisting of the states formerly part of the Cold War-era West Germany, also known as Western Germany.

   **Source context (verbatim):** since 1990
