### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Synonym of bad luck.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** The worst tour I have ever had the misfortune to experience.

   **Source synonyms:** bad luck (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An instance of bad luck, an undesirable event such as an accident.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** She had to come to terms with a number of misfortunes.

   **Source synonyms:** adversity; mishap; misadventure; disadventure

   **Source antonyms:** fortuity
