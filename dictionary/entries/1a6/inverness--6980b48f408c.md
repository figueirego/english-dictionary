### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A type of overcoat constructed without sleeves and having a shoulder cape extending to the wrists. The best examples are quite heavy, made from Harris tweed; often associated with Sherlock Holmes.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. A variation of this coat, the "coachman's cape"&#58; the shoulder cape does not extend around the back but is made of two segments, the length of each being sewn into the seam of the coat on either side.
