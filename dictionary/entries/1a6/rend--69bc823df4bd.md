### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To separate into parts with force or sudden violence; to split; to burst.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Powder rends a rock in blasting.

   **Example:** Lightning rends an oak.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To violently disturb the peace of; to throw into chaos.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** a scream that rent the air

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To part or tear off forcibly; to take away by force; to amputate.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To be rent or torn; to become parted; to separate; to split.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Relationships may rend if tempers flare.

   **Source synonyms:** rive
