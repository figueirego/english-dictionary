## Adverb

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used for emphasis after an interrogative word.

   **Usage:** euphemistic

   **Example:** What the heck are you doing?

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Intensifies a phrasal verb; goes between the base verb and its modifier.

   **Usage:** euphemistic

   **Example:** Elevators make me throw the heck up.
