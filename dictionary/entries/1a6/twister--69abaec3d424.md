### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who twists.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One who twists.
   1. One whose occupation is to twist or join the threads of one warp to those of another, in weaving.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An instrument used in twisting or making twists.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A ball delivered with a twist, as in cricket or billiards.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A tornado.

   **Usage:** colloquial

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A girder.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; carpentry; construction; manufacturing

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The inner part of the thigh, the proper place to rest upon when on horseback.

   **Usage:** dated

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A crook, a villain.

   **Usage:** UK; colloquial

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. Any member of the species Tholymis tillarga of libellulid dragonfly, of tropical West Africa to Asia, Australia, and the Pacific Islands.

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. The party game Twister, usually capitalized, or a variant.
