### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An unleavened bread, usually made with barleymeal, wheatmeal, or oatmeal; sometimes of peasemeal or otherwise.

   **Usage:** Northern-England; Scotland; especially; uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A biscuit bread made of wheat flour or cornmeal, fat, and sometimes baking powder, typically baked over a fire, wrapped around a stick or in a pan.

   **Usage:** Canada; uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A biscuit bread made of wheat flour or cornmeal, fat, and sometimes baking powder, typically baked over a fire, wrapped around a stick or in a pan.
   1. This or any similar traditional bread when made by indigenous Americans, originally from native sources such as maize or pseudocereals and plants with starchy roots

      **Usage:** Canada; specifically; uncountable; usually

      **Source synonyms:** Indian bread; alatiq; skaan
