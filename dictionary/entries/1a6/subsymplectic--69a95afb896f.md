### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. (of a finite abelian group with an alternating pairing into Q/Z) Embeddable, with its pairing preserved, into a standard symplectic module B × B\*, where the order of B equals the size of a Lagrangian subgroup.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences
