## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A sales system under which the salesperson receives a commission on his or her own sales and a smaller commission on the sales from each person he or she convinces to become a salesperson.

   **Usage:** uncountable
