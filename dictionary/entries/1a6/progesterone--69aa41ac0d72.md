### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A steroid hormone, secreted by the ovaries, whose function is to prepare the uterus for the implantation of a fertilized ovum and to maintain pregnancy.

   **Usage:** uncountable; steroids

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biochemistry; biology; chemistry; microbiology; natural-sciences; physical-sciences
