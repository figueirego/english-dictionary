### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Salt prepared by evaporating seawater.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** I always keep a tin of sea salt in the pantry.

   **Source synonyms:** rock salt

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see sea, salt&#58; salt in the sea.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** This is a saltwater fish. It needs ambient sea salt to survive.
