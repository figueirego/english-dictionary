### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The state of being relative to something else; the absence of universally applicable rules or standards; relativism; (countable) an instance of this.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Ellipsis of principle of relativity (“the principle that the laws of physics should be the same for all observers”).

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; ellipsis; uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

   **Alternative form of:** [principle of relativity](../097/principle-of-relativity--25cd9b031590.md)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Ellipsis of principle of relativity (“the principle that the laws of physics should be the same for all observers”).
   1. Also Einsteinian relativity&#58; the reliance of the nature of physical phenomena (such as gravity, light, mass, and time) on the relative motion between an observer and the thing observed, as developed by Albert Einstein in two theories, special relativity and general relativity.

      **Usage:** specifically; uncountable; usually

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An evaluation of the similarities and differences between things; a comparison; hence, a difference in position or status between things; a disparity.

   **Usage:** countable; in-plural; usually

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An evaluation of the similarities and differences between things; a comparison; hence, a difference in position or status between things; a disparity.
   1. The difference in pay or positions between different employees in a business (internal relativity), or between different businesses (external relativity); a differential.

      **Usage:** countable; in-plural; specifically; uncountable; usually

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** economics; sciences
