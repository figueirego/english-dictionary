### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A substance at a temperature and pressure above its own thermodynamic critical point that can diffuse through solids like a gas and dissolve materials like a liquid.

   **Example:** The most commonly used supercritical fluids are carbon dioxide and water.
