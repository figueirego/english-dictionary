### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A smaller, self-contained part of a larger entity. Often refers to a manufactured object that is part of a larger device.

   **Example:** A CPU is a component of a computer.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>
