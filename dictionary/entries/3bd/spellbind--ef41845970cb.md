**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To place under the influence of a magical spell; to enchant or bewitch with a spell.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** bespell; ensorcell; enspell; bechat (UK; dialectal); becry; beglammer (UK; dialectal); bespell; bewitch; conjure (archaic); enchant; englamour; ensorcell; enspell; forspeak (obsolete); glamour; hex; jinx; maleficiate (obsolete); mojo; spell; spellbind; spellcast; witch
