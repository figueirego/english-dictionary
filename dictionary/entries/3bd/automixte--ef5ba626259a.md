## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A system of driving automobiles employing a gasoline engine and an auxiliary reversible dynamo. When there is an excess of power, the dynamo is driven by the engine so as to charge a small storage battery; when there is a deficiency of power, the dynamo reverses and acts as an auxiliary motor.

   **Usage:** attributive; uncountable
