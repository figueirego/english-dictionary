## Adjective · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈstɪltɪd/ — source labels: General-American; Received-Pronunciation

### Forms

- more stilted — comparative
- most stilted — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Making use of or possessing a stilt or stilts, or things resembling stilts; raised on stilts.

   **Source antonyms:** unstilted

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Elevated or raised in a contrived or unnatural way; stiff and artificially formal or pompous; also, depending on redundant, unnecessary elements.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** He gave a stilted bow and left.

   **Source synonyms:** awkward; laboured; affected; theatrical; affected; effete; fey; recherché; hoity-toity; overrefined; pansified (derogatory); precious (derogatory); pretentious; smug (obsolete); stagy; stilted (figuratively); theatrical; Woosterian

   **Source antonyms:** humble; natural; unstilted

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of a building or architectural feature such as an arch or vault&#58; supported by stilts (“supporting pillars or posts”); also (generally) having the main part raised above the usual level by some structure.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** architecture

   **Source antonyms:** unstilted
