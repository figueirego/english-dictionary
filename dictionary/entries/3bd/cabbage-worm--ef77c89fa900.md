### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of various lepidopterans whose larvae feed on cabbages and other cole crops.
   1. Any of several species of Pieris (family Pieridae) butterflies&#58;
      1. A small white (Pieris rapae), found in most temperate regions.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of various lepidopterans whose larvae feed on cabbages and other cole crops.
   1. Any of several species of Pieris (family Pieridae) butterflies&#58;
      1. A large white (Pieris brassicae), of Eurasia and north Africa.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of various lepidopterans whose larvae feed on cabbages and other cole crops.
   1. Any of several species of Pieris (family Pieridae) butterflies&#58;
      1. A southern cabbage butterfly (Pieris protodice), of North America.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any of various lepidopterans whose larvae feed on cabbages and other cole crops.
   1. Any of several species of Pieris (family Pieridae) butterflies&#58;
      1. A green-veined white (Pieris napi) of Europe and North America.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Any of various lepidopterans whose larvae feed on cabbages and other cole crops.
   1. A cabbage looper (Trichoplusia ni, family Noctuidae), of much of the northern hemisphere.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Any of various lepidopterans whose larvae feed on cabbages and other cole crops.
   1. A cabbage webworm (Hellula undalis, family Crambidae) native to southern Europe or Asia that also injures cabbages and other vegetables in the Gulf states of the United States.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Any of various lepidopterans whose larvae feed on cabbages and other cole crops.
   1. A diamondback moth (Plutella xylostella, syn. Plutella maculipennis, family Plutellidae).
