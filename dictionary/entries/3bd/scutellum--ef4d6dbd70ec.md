## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of several shield-shaped structures in insects, grasses etc
   1. A rounded apothecium having an elevated rim formed of the proper thallus, the fructification of certain lichens.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; lichenology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of several shield-shaped structures in insects, grasses etc
   1. The third of the four pieces forming the upper part of a thoracic segment of an insect. It follows the scutum, and is followed by the small postscutellum.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences; zoology

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of several shield-shaped structures in insects, grasses etc
   1. One of the transverse scales on the tarsi and toes of birds.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences; zoology

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any of several shield-shaped structures in insects, grasses etc
   1. A grass seed cotyledon that is highly modified for absorption.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences
