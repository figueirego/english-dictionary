### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A flute that is smaller than a Western concert flute and pitched nearly an octave higher.

   **Source synonyms:** piccolo

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A flute stop in an organ pitched an octave higher than the standard pitch.
