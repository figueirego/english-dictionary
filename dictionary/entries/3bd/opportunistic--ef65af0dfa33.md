### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Taking advantage of situations that arise.

   **Example:** The danger now isn't so much from the AIDS virus itself as from opportunistic infections.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Taking advantage of situations to advance one's own interests without regard for moral principles.

   **Example:** You can't trust somebody that opportunistic&#58; he'll stab you in the back the first chance he gets.
