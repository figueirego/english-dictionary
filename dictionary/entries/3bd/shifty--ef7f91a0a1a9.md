### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Subject to frequent changes in direction.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Moving from one object to another; not looking directly and steadily at the person with whom one is speaking.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a person's eyes

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Having the appearance of being dishonest, criminal, or unreliable.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; informal

   **Example:** He was a shifty character in a seedy bar, and I checked my wallet was still there after talking to him.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Resourceful; full of, or ready with, shifts or expedients.
