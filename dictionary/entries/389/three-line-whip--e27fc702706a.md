### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A directive to MPs from party leaders to attend a parliamentary meeting and vote in a particular way, despite what the MP might believe or what his or her constituents might like.

   **Usage:** UK

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; politics

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An imperative order.

   **Usage:** broadly; humorous

   **Example:** I have to go shopping with the wife, I'm afraid. She's given me a three-line whip.
