### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Resembling a hawk in appearance or behaviour.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Supportive of warlike foreign policy; bellicose; inclined toward military action.

   **Example:** The Prime Minister could count on the support of a hawkish majority in Parliament to support the invasion.

   **Source synonyms:** bellicose

   **Source antonyms:** dovish

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Favouring increasing interest rates; inclined towards increasing interest rates.

   **Example:** The Federal Reserve's recent statement on the slowing of inflation was interpreted as hawkish by the market.

   **Source antonyms:** dovish
