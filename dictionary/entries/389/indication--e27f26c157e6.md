## Noun

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An act of pointing out or indicating.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A fact that shows that something exists or may happen.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** There's no indication that the fire was caused by criminals.

   **Example:** All the indications point to drink-driving as the cause of the accident.

   **Source synonyms:** symptom; evidence; tip-off

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A mark or another symbol used to represent something.

   **Usage:** countable; rare; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** mark; token; [sign](../02d/sign--0b6f395ca14a.md)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A discovery made; information.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An explanation; a display.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Any symptom or occurrence in a disease that serves to direct to suitable remedies; the problem that warrants and prompts the use of a diagnostic test, imaging mode, or treatment (e.g., medication, surgical procedure).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Influenza and suspected influenza are FDA-approved indications for oseltamivir.

   **Example:** In the United States, major depressive disorder is an off-label indication for clomipramine, but in various other countries, it is an approved indication.

   **Example:** The theme of treatment failure of previous treatments plus continuing impairment (pain and poor function) appears among the indications for many types of joint replacement surgery.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A declared approximation of the price at which a traded security is likely to commence trading.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance
