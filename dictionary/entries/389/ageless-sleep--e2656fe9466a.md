## Noun

### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A magical state of suspended animation, where-in the recipient is placed in a state of ageless, deathless, everlasting sleep. Well-known examples are Endymion (the lover of the Greek moon goddess, Selene), and the princess Sleeping Beauty.

   **Usage:** uncountable
