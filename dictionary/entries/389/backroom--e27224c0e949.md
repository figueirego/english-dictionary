### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A room near the rear of a premises.
   1. Especially, one that is only accessible to a privileged few and can be used as an inconspicuous meeting place, where secrecy, anonymity, and even crooked dealings may occur.

      **Usage:** attributive; figuratively; often

      **Example:** backroom deals

      **Example:** backroom bargaining

      **Source synonyms:** smoke-filled room\[insider\]\]s&gt;

      **Source antonyms:** [front room](../2ba/front-room--aea0e36bcefc.md); foreroom; anteroom; antechamber

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A room near the rear of a premises.
   1. A storeroom in the rear of the premises.

      **Example:** We had thought that they were out of the old model, but somebody checked in the backroom and found a case of them.
