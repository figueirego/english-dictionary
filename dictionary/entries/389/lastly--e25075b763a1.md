### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used to mark the beginning of the last in a list of items or propositions.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; sequence

   **Example:** Firstly, that's illegal; secondly, it has serious disadvantages; thirdly, it's not necessary; and lastly, did I mention that it's illegal?

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used to signal that the speaker is about to yield control of the conversation.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; discourse marker
