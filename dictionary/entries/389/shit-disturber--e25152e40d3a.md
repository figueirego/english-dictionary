## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who causes needless difficulties or distress for others; a troublemaker.

   **Usage:** Canada; vulgar

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person who voices or encourages a viewpoint opposed to the status quo; an iconoclast.

   **Usage:** Canada; vulgar

   **Example:** 1969&#58; Canadian Press, “Anti-religious&#58; CYC trainee kicked out because of ‘allegiance with God,’ in Ottawa Citizen, 1969-11-14, p 5&#58; “We were to be ‘shit disturbers.’” Those who suggested conventional approaches were told to get out of the CYC.
