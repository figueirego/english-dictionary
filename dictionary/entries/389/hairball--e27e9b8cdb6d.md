## Noun

### Forms

- hairballs — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A trichobezoar; a small wad of fur or mass of hair formed in the digestive system of a cat or other animal, from hair ingested while grooming.

   **Source synonyms:** furball

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A messy, tangled, intractable issue.

   **Usage:** figuratively; slang

   **Example:** The contract negotiations are turning into a real hairball.
