## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A town in northeastern Albania, located east of the river Black Drin and southwest of the Mali i Gramës in the Korab mountain range; it is the seat of the county of Dibër, the municipality of Dibër and the former seat of the defunct Dibër district.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An administrative unit of the municipality of Dibër in the county of Dibër, Albania.
