### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who or that which synchronizes.
   1. Part of a synchromesh manual transmission that allows the smooth engagement of gears.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** automotive; transport; vehicles

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One who or that which synchronizes.
   1. A device that permits an automatic weapon to fire between the blades of a revolving propeller.

      **Usage:** aerial warfare

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. One who or that which synchronizes.
