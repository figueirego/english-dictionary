## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A provision of a law which says the law or something under it, as an agency or benefit established by it, will come to an end on a particular date or after a particular period of time.

   **Example:** A sunset clause in this act says the tax exemption will expire on the thirtieth day after the effective date of the act.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

   **Source synonyms:** sunset provision

   **Source antonyms:** sunrise clause
