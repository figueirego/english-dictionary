### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used to shift focus to an overriding point or impediment.

   **Usage:** colloquial; idiomatic

   **Example:** I'd like to come, but the thing is, I just can't afford it.

   **Example:** We've discussed lots of options. Thing is, we only have an hour left.

   **Example:** It's true that the stock market might go down, but the the thing is that with this investment you can make a profit regardless!
