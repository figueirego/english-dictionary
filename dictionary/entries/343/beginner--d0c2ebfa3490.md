### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Someone who is just starting at something, or has only recently started.

   **Example:** I'm new to learning Finnish&#58; I'm just a beginner.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Someone who sets something in motion.

   **Example:** The beginner of the games lit the ceremonial torch.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An actor who is present on stage in the first moments of a play.
