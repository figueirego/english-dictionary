### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of, from or relating to Cythera, an island now part of Greece, at which the goddess Aphrodite came ashore on a seashell according to Greek mythology. Thus, referring to the goddesses Aphrodite or Venus.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of or pertaining to the planet Venus; Venusian.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; uncommon
