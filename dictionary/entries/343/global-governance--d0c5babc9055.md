### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Supranational integration of national actors aimed at solving global problems that cannot be solved through national decision making alone.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Global governance would be essential in tackling mankind's common challenges but it is hard to achieve among extremely different kinds of actors.
