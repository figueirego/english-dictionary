### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To plan, control and record the position and course of a vehicle, ship, aircraft, etc., on a journey; to follow a planned course.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He navigated the bomber to the Ruhr.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To give directions, as from a map, to someone driving a vehicle.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** You drive. I'll navigate.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To travel over water in a ship; to sail.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** We navigated to France in the dinghy.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To move between web pages, menus, etc. by means of hyperlinks, mouse clicks, or any other mechanism.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** It was difficult to navigate back to the home page.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To find a way through a difficult situation or process.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive; figuratively
