### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A game mode where the player character is invulnerable to damage, typically activated by entering a cheat code.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** video-games

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A mode of play in (mostly) roguelike games, allowing the player to create objects on demand, to be resurrected in the case of death, etc.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** video-games

   **Source synonyms:** wizard mode

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A temporary state of extreme skill (in a sport, video game, etc.).

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; slang; uncountable

   **Example:** She went god mode and effortlessly defeated her opponents, leaving them in awe of her superhuman skills.
