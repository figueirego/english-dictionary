<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of a characteristic&#58; extreme or very high or strong in degree; severe; also, excessive, towering.

   **Source synonyms:** maximal; ultimate; utmost; violent; extreme; ferocious; exquisite (formal); intense; pluperfect (informal); almighty (informal); greatest; highest; maximal; maximum; passing (literary; rare); raging; ultimate; utmost; uttermost; devilish; excessive; horrendous (informal); de trop (literary); extravagant; extreme; immoderate; intemperate; lavish; nimious (Scottish; obsolete); obscene; overmuch; overweening; over the top; prodigal; undue; unmeasured; unreasonable; unrestrained; wanton

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of a thing&#58; possessing some characteristic to an extreme or very high or strong degree.

   **Source synonyms:** hot

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of feelings, thoughts, etc.&#58; strongly focused; ardent, deep, earnest, passionate.

   **Example:** intense study

   **Example:** intense thought

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Of a person&#58; very emotional or passionate.

   **Example:** The artist was a small, intense man with piercing blue eyes.
