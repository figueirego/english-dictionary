## Adjective · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˌdɪskəˈnɛktəd/ — source labels: UK

### Forms

- more disconnected — comparative
- most disconnected — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. That is no longer connected.

   **Example:** There's no use trying to make a call on the disconnected phone.

   **Source synonyms:** deconnected

   **Source antonyms:** connected

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Feeling a lack of empathy or association with something.

   **Example:** I just feel so disconnected from people living on the other side of the world.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Incoherent; disjointed.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. That can be partitioned into two nonempty subsets which are both open and closed.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a topological space
