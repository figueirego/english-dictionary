## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The property of an electric circuit by which a voltage is induced in it by a changing magnetic field.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The power cable itself has enough inductance to disrupt the digital signal of the video output cable, due to poor shielding.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The quantity of the resulting electromagnetic flux divided by the current that produces it, measured in henries (SI symbol&#58; H.)

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** What is the inductance of that power supply's main inductor?
