### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A polynomial over a given field that has distinct roots in the algebraic closure of said field (the number of roots being equal to the degree of the polynomial).

   **Usage:** field theory

   **Example:** Over a perfect field, the separable polynomials are precisely the square-free polynomials.

   **Example:** The study of the automorphisms of splitting fields of separable polynomials over a field is referred to as Galois theory.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** algebra; mathematics; sciences
