### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To be capable of throwing a strong punch.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** Lord, that boy sure can pack a punch.

   **Source synonyms:** pack a wallop

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To be capable of having a swift and powerful effect.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** Lord, that moonshine sure does pack a punch.
