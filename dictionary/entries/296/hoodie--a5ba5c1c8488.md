### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A sweatshirt with an integral hood and, sometimes, a large kangaroo pocket at the front.

   **Example:** You can buy hoodies on the cheap in summer.

   **Source synonyms:** kangaroo; kangaroo jacket; bunny hug

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A young person wearing such a sweatshirt, usually a male, stereotypically associated with antisocial behaviour.

   **Usage:** UK; derogatory; often; slang

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A foreskin.

   **Usage:** slang

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The hooded crow, Corvus cornix.
