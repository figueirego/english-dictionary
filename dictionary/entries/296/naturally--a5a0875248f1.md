### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In a natural manner.

   **Example:** Although he was unused to the situation, he tried to act naturally.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Inherently or by nature.

   **Example:** Boys are naturally aggressive.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Surely or without any doubt.

   **Example:** I shall naturally protest at that decision.

   **Example:** Would you like a glass of champagne, sir? - Naturally!
