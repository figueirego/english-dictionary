## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. (real analysis, of a positive-valued function f on real arguments) Satisfying f(xy) ≥ f(x)f(y) whenever x, y and xy belong to its domain.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

   **Extractor diagnostics (not usage labels):** empty-gloss; no-gloss
