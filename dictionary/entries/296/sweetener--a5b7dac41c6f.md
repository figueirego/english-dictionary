## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Something added to food to sweeten its taste, especially an artificial substitute for sugar.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Something given or added to a deal to sweeten another's attitude, especially a bribe or kickback.

   **Usage:** informal

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A shill bidder at an auction.

   **Usage:** slang
