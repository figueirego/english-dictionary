### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To reject or repudiate (an authority, a person, a court judgment, etc.).

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To reject or repudiate (an authority, a person, a court judgment, etc.).
   1. Of a judge, juror, or prosecutor&#58; to declare (oneself) unable to participate in a court case due to an actual or potential conflict of interest or lack of impartiality.

      **Usage:** transitive; reflexive or intransitive \[sometimes proscribed\]

      **Example:** The judge recused herself from that case, citing a possible conflict of interest as one of the parties was a personal friend.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To reject or repudiate (an authority, a person, a court judgment, etc.).
   1. To object to (a judge, juror, or prosecutor) participating in a court case due to an actual or potential conflict of interest or lack of impartiality.

      **Usage:** Canada; US; transitive

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

      **Source synonyms:** inhibit (Philippines)

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive
