### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To not believe; to exercise disbelief.

   **Example:** If you disbelieve such people, then keep disbelieving them for as long as you live or want.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To actively deny (a statement, opinion or perception).

   **Example:** He chose to disbelieve the bad news as inconceivable.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To cease to believe.
