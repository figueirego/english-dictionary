### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The process of refreshing; a means of restoring strength, energy or vigour.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** reinvigoration; revival; restoration; renewal; renovation; reanimation; repair

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. That which refreshes, such as a light snack or drink.

   **Usage:** countable; plural-normally; usually

   **Example:** All refreshments will be provided by the hosts.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The result of being refreshed.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
