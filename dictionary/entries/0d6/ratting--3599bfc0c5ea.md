### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. The blood sport of setting a dog upon rats confined in a pit to see how many he will kill in a given time.

   **Usage:** archaic; uncountable; usually

   **Source synonyms:** rat-baiting

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. A vocation involving the pest control of rats, typically using a working terrier.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually
