### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A vertical partition dividing the hull into separate compartments; often made watertight to prevent excessive flooding if the ship's hull is breached.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A similar partition in an aircraft or spacecraft, or some rail vehicles.

   **Usage:** aircraft; spacecraft; aircraft; spacecraft

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** rail-transport; railways; transport

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A partition or panel through which connectors pass, or a connector designed to pass through a partition.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; mechanical-engineering; mechanics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**
