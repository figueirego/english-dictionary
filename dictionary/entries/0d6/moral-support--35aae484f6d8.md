## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Assistance given to a person or cause, that is psychological rather than physical.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** I was very grateful to my sister who came along with me to the psychologist as moral support.

   **Example:** Although I'm not passionate about this issue, I'm willing to attend the rally to give moral support.
