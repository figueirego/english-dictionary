### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A duplicate or counterpart that acts in a contrary, nefarious, or insidious manner.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** As a ploy to discredit the reformist politician, the opposing party had hired a lookalike to act as his evil twin and be seen in a variety of compromising situations.

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A rogue wireless access point installed near a legitimate one for purposes of eavesdropping or phishing.
