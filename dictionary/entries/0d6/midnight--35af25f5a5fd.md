<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The middle of the night&#58; the sixth temporal hour, equidistant between sunset and sunrise.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Thanks to its sonar, the narwhal can remain active even at midnight, unhindered by the darkness.

   **Source antonyms:** noon; midday
