## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An outgoing telephone call.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** communication; communications

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An instance of being summoned to visit a certain place in order to provide assistance; an instance of summoning someone who is on call.

   **Example:** I had to pay for the callout of the plumber after the pipe burst.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A meeting or rally held in order to find interested participants, e.g. for an activity or sports team.

   **Usage:** US

   **Example:** So many people attended the basketball callout that the coach decided to form two teams.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An invitation to fight.

   **Usage:** slang

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A request for people to join or take part.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A pull quote&#58; an excerpt from an article (such as in a news magazine) that is duplicated in a large font alongside the article so as to grab a reader's attention and indicate the article's topic.

   **Usage:** graphic layout

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** media; publishing; typography

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. An annotation that pertains to a specific location in a body of text or a graphic, and that is visually linked to that location by a mark or a matching pair of marks.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. The act of calling out from work, i.e. announcing that one cannot attend; the act of calling in sick.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A form of verbal abuse with the intention of making the victim feel guilty.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

10. The invocation of an external third-party component.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; programming; sciences
