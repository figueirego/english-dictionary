## Adjective · Record 1

### Forms

- day to day — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Ordinary, mundane, or everyday.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** One has to deal with the day-to-day chores.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Happening every day, everyday.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** I would like to know about the day-to-day workings of the business.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Subject to daily redetermination.

   **Usage:** US; not-comparable; often

   **Example:** He has an ankle sprain and his status is day-to-day.
