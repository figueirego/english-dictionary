### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A swirling vortex.

   **Usage:** literary; poetic

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A circular or spiral motion; also, a circle described by a moving body; a revolution, a turn.

   **Usage:** literary; poetic

   **Source synonyms:** circuit; whirl

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Synonym of gyrus (“a fold or ridge on the cerebral cortex of the brain”).

   **Usage:** archaic

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; biology; medicine; natural-sciences; sciences; zoology; zootomy

   **Source synonyms:** gyrus (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An ocean current caused by wind which moves in a circular manner, especially one that is large-scale and observed in a major ocean.

   **Example:** Most ships that tried to cross the Pacific in the past would get stuck in the gyres and never make it out.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; natural-sciences; oceanography
