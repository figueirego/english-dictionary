**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Usage:** Commonwealth; Ireland; UK; slang

   **Source synonyms:** annoyance; bother; faff (British; slang); grievance; hassle; inconvenience; irritation; nuisance; palaver (British); peeve; plague; trouble; vexation

   **Usage:** typically 'in a faff'

   **Example:** She's in a total faff about tonight's dinner party.

   **Source synonyms:** flap
