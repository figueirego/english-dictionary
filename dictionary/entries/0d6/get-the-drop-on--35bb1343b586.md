### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To point one's firearm at another person, thereby gaining dominance in a situation such as a historic duel, where pistols would be held vertically until time to shoot.

   **Usage:** informal; transitive

   **Example:** He got the drop on them and proceeded to shoot and kill them before they could fire a shot or respond.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To obtain an advantage over another person, by acting before that person.

   **Usage:** broadly; informal; transitive

   **Example:** We’re always getting the drop on our competitors because the owner really knows the business and can act fast.

   **Source synonyms:** beat to the punch; get the jump on
