### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To take (fish or other marine animals) with a trawl.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** The fisherman went out to trawl the deep sea for shrimp.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To fish from a slow-moving boat.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** They used a large net to trawl for fish along the coast.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To make an exhaustive search for something within a defined area.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** We need to trawl through the data to find meaningful patterns.

   **Source synonyms:** rummage; forage; rifle
