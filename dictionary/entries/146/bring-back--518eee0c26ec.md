### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To fetch something.

   **Usage:** ditransitive; transitive

   **Example:** I brought back the groceries.

   **Example:** If you're going to the store, could you bring me back some chocolate?

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To cause someone to remember something from the past.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The smell of the magnolia brought back sweet memories of my childhood.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To reenact an old rule or law.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** In the UK it is customary to hold a vote every few years on whether to bring back the death penalty.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To revive, to resurrect; to cause something dead to be alive once again.

   **Usage:** transitive
