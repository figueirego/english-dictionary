<a id="r1-s4"></a>

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

5. A rocky coastal district now in in South Ayrshire, Scotland and part of the Carrick, Cumnock and Doon Valley Scottish Parliament constituency and the eponymous UK Parliament constituency.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

7. A locality in Meander Valley council area, northern Tasmania, Australia.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A Scottish earldom bestowed on the heir apparent to the reigning monarchs first of Scotland (since Robert the Bruce in 1292) and later of the United Kingdom.
