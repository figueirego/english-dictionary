### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A point of view resulting in a misleading or inaccurate representation of a person, situation, or fact.

   **Usage:** countable; idiomatic

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A cause of action arising under the common law where a person is portrayed in a way which, while not technically false, is misleading and likely to cause embarrassment to that person.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law
