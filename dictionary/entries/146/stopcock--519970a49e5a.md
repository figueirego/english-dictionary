### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A valve, tap or faucet which regulates the flow of liquid or gas through a pipe.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A main shutoff for water to a home from a municipal supply. Usually these valves exist in pairs, one outside the property boundary and one inside the property boundary.

   **Usage:** UK

   **Example:** If you have a burst pipe, turn the water off at the stopcock.
