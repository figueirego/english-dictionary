### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Expressed in more words than are necessary.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** He wrote a periphrastic love letter to his wife to patch up their relationship.

   **Example:** She was simply asked to express how she felt, but her response was periphrastic.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Indirect in naming an entity; circumlocutory.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Characterized by periphrasis.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** “The daughter of the man” may be used as a periphrastic synonym for “the man’s daughter”.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** grammar; human-sciences; linguistics; sciences
