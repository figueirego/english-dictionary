### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A garment knitted from wool, worn over the upper body.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source synonyms:** jumper; pullover; sweater

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A shirt worn by a member of an athletic team, usually oversized, typically depicting the athlete's name and team number as well as the team's logotype.

   **Usage:** US; countable

   **Example:** A young boy was wearing a Manchester City Erling Haaland jersey.

   **Source synonyms:** guernsey (Australian); shirt (UK)
