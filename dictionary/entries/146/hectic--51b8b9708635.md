**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Very busy with activity and confusion.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** The city center is so hectic at 8 in the morning that I go to work an hour beforehand to avoid the crowds

   **Source synonyms:** feverish

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Denoting a type of fever accompanying consumption and similar wasting diseases, characterised by flushed cheeks and dry skin.

   **Usage:** obsolete

   **Example:** hectic fever
