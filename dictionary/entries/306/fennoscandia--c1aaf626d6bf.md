## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A peninsula in Northern Europe, comprising, besides the Scandinavian Peninsula, also Finland, the Kola Peninsula and Karelia (and sometimes also including Denmark, although it is not part of the Scandinavian Peninsula).

   **Source synonyms:** Fennoscandinavia; Fenno-Scandinavia

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. the Fennoscandian Shield underlying Norway, Sweden, Finland, and Northern Denmark; Baltic Shield.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; geology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. the culture group comprising the Finnic, Sami, and Scandinavian cultures.

   **Usage:** culture
