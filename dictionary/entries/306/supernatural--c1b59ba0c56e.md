### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Above nature; beyond or added to nature, often so considered because it is given by a deity or some force beyond that with which humans are born.

   **Example:** In Roman Catholic theology, sanctifying grace is considered to be a supernatural addition to human nature.

   **Example:** Stephen King's first novel is about a girl named Carrie dealing with supernatural powers.

   **Source synonyms:** supranatural

   **Source antonyms:** ordinary; normal; typical

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Not of the usual; not natural; altered by forces that are not understood fully, if at all.

   **Example:** The house is haunted by supernatural forces.

   **Example:** Many humans believe in the existence of supernatural forces; some do not.

   **Source synonyms:** supranatural

   **Source antonyms:** ordinary; normal; typical
