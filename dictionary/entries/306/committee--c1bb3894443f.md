### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A body of one or more persons convened for the accomplishment of some specific purpose, typically with formal protocols.

   **Example:** My uncle is on the committee.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A guardian; someone in charge of another person deemed to be unable to look after themselves.

   **Usage:** archaic
