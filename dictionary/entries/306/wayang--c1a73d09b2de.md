### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A theatrical performance of mythological events employing puppets or human dancers in Malaysia and Indonesia (especially Java).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A shadow theatre performance, or such performances in general.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any traditional theatrical work, combining drama, music, song and sometimes dance.

   **Usage:** Malaysia; Singapore; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

4. Any traditional theatrical work, combining drama, music, song and sometimes dance.
   1. Traditional Chinese opera, performed outdoors. The equivalent term in Mandarin is 街戲 /街戏 (jiēxì, literally “street opera”).

      **Usage:** Malaysia; Singapore; countable; uncountable
