### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Drying or roasting; especially an anaerobic heat treatment to make wood waterproof.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The act or process of subjecting something to heat or to highly acidic substances.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An angry outburst; vituperation.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable
