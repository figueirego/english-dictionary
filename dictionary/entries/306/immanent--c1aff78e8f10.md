### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Naturally part of something; existing throughout and within something; intrinsic.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of something which has always already been.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; philosophy; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Restricted entirely to the mind or a given domain; internal; subjective.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Existing within and throughout the mind and the world; dwelling within and throughout all things, all time, etc. Compare transcendent.

   **Usage:** metaphysics

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; lifestyle; philosophy; religion; sciences; theology

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a deity

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Taking place entirely within the mind of the subject and having no effect outside it. Compare emanant, transeunt.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; philosophy; sciences

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a mental act

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**
