## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A postmodern approach to analyzing intersubjective discourses, based on the ways that authority is rooted in individuals' knowledge and perspectives, and the power that such authority exerts.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** standpoint epistemology
