## Verb

### Forms

- carries off — present; singular; third-person
- carrying off — participle; present
- carried off — participle; past
- carried off — past

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To transport away.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I need a truck to carry off all this furniture.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To steal or kidnap

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Bandits carried off most of the money.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To act convincingly; to succeed at giving the impression of (e.g.) knowledge, confidence, or familiarity.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** The actress carried off a difficult performance with dash.

   **Example:** He is carrying off his act, convincing them he was a nuclear scientist.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To achieve (something); to succeed at (something).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Despite a slow start in the race, he carried it off in the end.

   **Example:** We didn't think they'd finish by the deadline, but they carried it off.

   **Source synonyms:** pull off

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To cause the death of.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Malaria carried off many people.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To win (a prize, etc.).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** After a closely-fought match, Oxford carried off the trophy.
