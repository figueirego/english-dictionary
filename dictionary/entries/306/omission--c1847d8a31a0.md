### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of omitting.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** preterition (formal)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The act of neglecting to perform an action one has an obligation to do.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** E&amp;O insurance (for errors and omissions) covers both errors of commission and errors of omission.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An instance of those acts, or the thing left out thereby; something deleted or left out.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** The suspicious omissions in the new edition of the book attracted claims of censorship.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Something not done or neglected.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The lack of a sponge count was an omission by the surgical team.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The shortening of a word or phrase, using an apostrophe ( ' ) to replace the missing letters, often used to approximate the sound of speech or a specific dialect.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** grammar; human-sciences; linguistics; sciences
