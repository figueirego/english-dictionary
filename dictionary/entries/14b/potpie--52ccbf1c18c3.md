### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A pie, having pastry sides and bottom and (usually also) top, and filled with savory fillings, such as meat, root vegetables, or (often) both.

   **Usage:** UK; US; sometimes

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A pie, having pastry sides and bottom and (usually also) top, and filled with savory fillings, such as meat, root vegetables, or (often) both.
   1. Especially, such a pie that is small, that has crust made entirely of flaky pastry, and that is baked in its own pie tin, usually for one person (single-serve).

      **Usage:** UK; US; sometimes

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A dish of meat and vegetable stew with dumplings.

   **Usage:** US; regional
