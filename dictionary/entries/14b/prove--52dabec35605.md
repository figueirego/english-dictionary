### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To demonstrate that something is true or viable; to give proof for; to bear out; to testify.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I will prove that my method is more effective than yours.

   **Source synonyms:** substantiate

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To turn out; to manifest.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** It proved to be a cold day.

   **Example:** A faulty connection proved to be the culprit.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To turn out to be.

   **Usage:** copulative

   **Example:** Have an exit strategy should your calculations prove incorrect.

   **Example:** The hope that he’d react proved utterly unfounded.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To put to the test, to make trial of.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** They took the experimental car to the proving-grounds.

   **Example:** The exception proves the rule.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To ascertain or establish the genuineness or validity of; to verify.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to prove a will

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To experience.

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To take a trial impression of; to take a proof of.

   **Usage:** dated; transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** media; printing; publishing

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Alternative form of proof (“allow (dough) to rise; test the activeness of (yeast); pressure-test (a firearm)”).

   **Usage:** alt-of; alternative

   **Alternative form of:** [proof](../307/proof--c1cda2636282.md)

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To determine by experiment which effects a substance causes when ingested.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** homeopathy; medicine; sciences
