**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The seasonal freeze-thaw action upon waterlogging topsoils which induces downslope movement.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Gelifluction is prominent in periglacial regions where snow falls during six to eight months of the year.
