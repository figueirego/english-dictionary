### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Something left behind, saved or remaining from an earlier time.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** That policy is a holdover from days of punch card data entry.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The distance (at target) by which a rifle scope is aimed higher than the intended point of impact in order to compensate for bullet drop over the distance to the target.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** This rangefinder not only measures the distance to the target, but also provides a digital readout of the inches of holdover at that distance.
