### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An exciting or extraordinary scene, exhibition, performance etc.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** The horse race was a thrilling spectacle.

   **Source synonyms:** pageant; show

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Drama and excitement in a display, product, performance, exhibition, etc.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** I enjoyed her presentation, but it needs more spectacle.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An embarrassing or unedifying scene or situation.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** He made a spectacle out of himself.
