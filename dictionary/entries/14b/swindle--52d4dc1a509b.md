### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To defraud.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The two men swindled the company out of $160,000.

   **Source synonyms:** chisel; diddle

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To obtain (money or property) by fraudulent or deceitful methods.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** She swindled more than £200 out of me.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. For a player in a losing position to play a clever move that provokes an error from the opponent, thus achieving a win or a draw.
