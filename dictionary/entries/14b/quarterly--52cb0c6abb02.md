## Adjective · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Occurring once every quarter year (three months); taking place quarter-yearly.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** quarterly rent payments

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. (of a coat of arms) Divided into four parts crosswise.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The arms of Hohenzollern is quarterly argent and sable.
