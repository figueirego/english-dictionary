### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A sense organ of a fish running lengthwise down each of its sides from the vicinity of the operculum to the base of the tail, used to detect movement and vibration in the surrounding water.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; ichthyology; natural-sciences; zoology; zootomy

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An equivalent organ found in tadpoles and aquatic salamanders that runs down the head and trunk.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; herpetology; natural-sciences; zoology; zootomy
