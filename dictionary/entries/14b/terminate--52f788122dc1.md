### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To end something, especially when left in an incomplete state.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to terminate a process before its completion

   **Example:** to terminate an effort, or a controversy

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To conclude.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To set or be a limit or boundary to.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to terminate a surface by a line

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To form an appropriate end on (a wire, cable, hose, pipe, etc), such as by applying a cable terminal or a hose ferrule.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** We'll rough them all in before we start terminating any of them.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To end the employment contract of an employee; to fire, lay off.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To kill someone or something.

   **Usage:** euphemistic; transitive

   **Example:** The enemy must be terminated by any means possible.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To end, conclude, or cease; to come to an end.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Of a mode of transport, to end its journey; or, of a railway line, to reach its terminus.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** This train terminates at the next station.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To issue or result.

   **Usage:** intransitive
