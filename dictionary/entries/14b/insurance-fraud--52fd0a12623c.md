### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Receiving insurance policy-based compensation by deceitfully causing an event that, if it were accidental, would lead to a rightful claim for said compensation.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Some people have committed insurance fraud by setting their houses on fire and claiming that the fire was an accident.
