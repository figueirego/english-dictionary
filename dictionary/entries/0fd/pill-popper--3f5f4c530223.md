## Noun

### Forms

- pill poppers — plural
- pill-popper — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A syringelike device for administering medicinal pills to animals.

   **Source synonyms:** pill gun

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person who enjoys or frequently takes medication in pill form.

   **Usage:** humorous; informal

   **Example:** Well, you know, once Pop got into his sixties, he came to be a bit of a pill popper, what with the blood pressure and the blood sugar and the cholesterol and the arthritis.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A person who frequently takes any other kind of pill, such as vitamins or illicit drugs.

   **Usage:** derogatory; informal; often

   **Example:** It's great when people are enthusiastic about health and wellness, but you just have to watch out for how hard the vitamin people sell you on the pricey supplements — they'll try to turn you into a pill popper.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A person who frequently takes any other kind of pill, such as vitamins or illicit drugs.
   1. Especially, someone with a frank substance use disorder who consumes a large amount of illicit pills, especially opioid street drug pills&#58; synonym of pillhead.

      **Usage:** derogatory; informal; often

      **Example:** I wouldn't hang around in that part of town after dark, what with all the methheads and pill poppers. I'm not looking to get robbed.
