### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Mounted higher than one or more other turrets within its firing arc, allowing it to fire over the top of the lower-mounted turret(s).

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; nautical; politics; transport; war
