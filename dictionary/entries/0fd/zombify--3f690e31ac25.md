### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To turn into a zombie (a member of the living dead or undead).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** fiction; literature; media; publishing

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To take control of (a computer) in order to use it covertly and illicitly.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** These trojans can zombify your machine.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To deprive of authenticity or vitality; to render someone brainless or unthinking.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive
