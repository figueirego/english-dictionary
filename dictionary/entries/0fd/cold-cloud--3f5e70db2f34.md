### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. (astronomy) A cloud of interstellar gas at a low temperature, such as a cold neutral hydrogen cloud with a temperature of a few tens of kelvin.

   **Extractor diagnostics (not usage labels):** no-gloss

**Supplemental definition (evidence reviewed; source gap preserved):** The original derived terms Local Ribbon of Cold Clouds and Local Lynx of Cold Clouds establish an interstellar context. The primary observational study directly identifies the Local Leo Cold Cloud as a cold neutral medium cloud and measures 15–30 K; that example is not imposed as a universal temperature cutoff.
