### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A potential bias in epidemiologic studies, involving the fact that subjects who voluntarily enroll in a clinical trial and actually follow the experimental regimen are likely to be healthier than the general population.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
