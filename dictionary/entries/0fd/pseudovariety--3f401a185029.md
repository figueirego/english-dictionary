### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A class of algebras of a given signature, closed under the taking of homomorphic images, subalgebras and finitary direct products.

   **Example:** Pseudovarieties are of particular importance in the study of finite semigroups and hence in formal language theory.
