### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Able to withstand adverse conditions.

   **Source synonyms:** sturdy

   **Source antonyms:** fragile

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Evincing strength and health; strong; (often, especially) both large and healthy.

   **Example:** He was a robust man of six feet four.

   **Example:** robust health

   **Example:** A robust wall was put up.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Requiring strength or vigor.

   **Example:** robust employment

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Sensible (of intellect etc.); straightforward, not given to or confused by uncertainty or subtlety.

   **Example:** robust findings

   **Example:** robust proof

   **Example:** robust explanation

   **Example:** robust lesson

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Rough; rude.

   **Usage:** euphemistic

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Designed or evolved in such a way as to be resistant to total failure despite partial damage.

   **Usage:** systems engineering

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**
