## Noun

### Forms

- political footballs — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A contentious political issue or problem that is often debated or discussed, but that remains unresolved; an issue or problem which is avoided by authorities and handed off to others.

   **Usage:** countable; idiomatic

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Ongoing unproductive wrangling or posturing between political factions, resulting in failure to deal with an issue or problem in a decisive or appropriate way.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; uncountable
