### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To swing the back of a vehicle (originally an aircraft) from side to side.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** As we approached the runway, the pilot fishtailed slightly to reduce landing speed.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

   **Usage:** transitive
