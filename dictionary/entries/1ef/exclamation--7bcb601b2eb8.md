### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of loudly calling or crying out, for example, as in anger, grief or pain, joy, surprise, etc.; (countable) an instance of this.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The act of loudly complaining or protesting; (countable) an instance of this; a complaint, an outcry, a protest.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Synonym of interjection (“a phrase or word with no particular grammatical relation to a sentence but which serves to express emotion or as a filled pause”).

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** grammar; human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** interjection (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Synonym of exclamation mark (“the punctuation mark "!", generally used to denote excitement, shock, or surprise”).

   **Usage:** archaic; countable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** communications; journalism; literature; media; orthography; publishing; writing

   **Source synonyms:** exclamation mark (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Synonym of ecphonesis (”(uncountable) the making of an emotional utterance; (countable) an instance of this; an exclamatory phrase”); also, synonym of epiphonema (“(countable) an exclamatory phrase or reflection used to round off or summarize an argument or discourse”).

   **Usage:** countable; rhetoric; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** ecphonesis (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Followed by of&#58; the act of formally declaring; (countable) an instance of this; a declaration, a proclamation, a pronouncement.

   **Usage:** obsolete; uncountable
