## Noun

### Forms

- yellow pines — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of various trees with wood of a yellow color.
   1. In genus Pinus of pines (family Pinaceae)
      1. Any southern yellow pine of five species Pinus.

         **Usage:** Southern-US; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of various trees with wood of a yellow color.
   1. In genus Pinus of pines (family Pinaceae)
      1. Any western yellow pine, either a ponderosa pine or a Jeffrey pine.

         **Usage:** US; Western; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of various trees with wood of a yellow color.
   1. In species of family Podocarpaceae, of podocarps
      1. A coniferous tree endemic to New Zealand, of species Halocarpus biformis.

         **Usage:** New-Zealand; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any of various trees with wood of a yellow color.
   1. In species of family Podocarpaceae, of podocarps
      1. A tree of the species Podocarpus elatus, of Australia.

         **Usage:** Australia; countable; uncountable

         **Source synonyms:** Australian plum; brown pine; Illawarra plum; Illawarra plum pine

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Wood from any of these trees.

   **Usage:** uncountable
