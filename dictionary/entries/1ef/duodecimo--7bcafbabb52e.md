### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A size of paper, so called because it is originally made by folding and cutting a single sheet from a printing press into 12 leaves; (5 by 7¾ inches)&#58; 6.5 to 7.5 inches high, approximately 4.5 inches wide.

   **Usage:** paper

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** media; printing; publishing

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A sheet or page of that size.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A book having pages of that size.
