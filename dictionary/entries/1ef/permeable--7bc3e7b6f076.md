### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Able to be permeated; absorbing or allowing the passage of fluids.

   **Example:** permeable strata

   **Example:** Rainwater sinks through permeable rock to form an underground reservoir.

   **Source synonyms:** water-permeable

   **Source antonyms:** impermeable; impervious; nonpermeable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Allowing the passage of persons, information, or other things, especially when such passage occurs despite hopes or expectations otherwise.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** permeable borders

   **Example:** permeable boundaries

   **Source synonyms:** penetrable

   **Source antonyms:** impermeable; impenetrable; nonpermeable
