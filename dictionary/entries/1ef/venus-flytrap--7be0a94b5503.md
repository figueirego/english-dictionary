**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An insectivorous plant (Dionaea muscipula) of the savannas of North and South Carolina, with hinged leaves bordered with stiff hairs that close when an insect alights on them.

   **Source synonyms:** tipitiwitchet; dionaea
