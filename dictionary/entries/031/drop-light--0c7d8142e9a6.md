**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A lamp that is suspended, for example, by a cable.
   1. Such a lamp on a retractable cable, suitable for workshops and garages, where the light can easily be taken down underneath cars, trucks, locomotives, machinery, and so on, then easily put away in an overhead position.

      **Source synonyms:** trouble light; rough service light
