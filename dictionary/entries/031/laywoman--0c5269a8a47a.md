## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A woman who is a layperson, one who has not taken a religious oath (such as becoming a nun).

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A woman who is not a professional in a given field.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** Carmen is not a professional anthropologist, but strictly a laywoman.
