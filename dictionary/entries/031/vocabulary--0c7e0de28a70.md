### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A usually alphabetized and explained collection of words e.g. of a particular field, or prepared for a specific purpose, often for learning.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The collection of words a person knows and uses.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** My Russian vocabulary is very limited.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The stock of words used in a particular field.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The vocabulary of social sciences is often incomprehensible to ordinary people.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The words of a language collectively; lexis.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The vocabulary of any language is influenced by contacts with other cultures.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A range of artistic or stylistic forms or techniques.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; uncountable
