## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A Christmas tradition where a group of people give anonymous gifts to each other, with each person randomly selected to give a gift to one other person.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The person who buys a present for another in a secret-Santa exchange.

   **Example:** I could tell Mary was my secret Santa.

   **Source synonyms:** Kris Kringle
