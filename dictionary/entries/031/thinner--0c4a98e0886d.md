### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A liquid substance used to thin the consistency of another liquid.

   **Example:** Turpentine or mineral spirits can be used as a thinner for oil-based paints.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Something that thins or thins out (another thing).

   **Example:** Pomegranate juice and exercise are great natural blood thinners.
