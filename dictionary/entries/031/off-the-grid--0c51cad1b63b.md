## Adjective · Record 1

### Forms

- off-the-grid — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of a home, business, or the occupants thereof, not using electricity from the public electrical supply system.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Not reachable by means of a publicly available communication system, such as the Internet or a mobile telephone network.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Isolated; in a remote location; in seclusion; not participating in some official process or system.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Source synonyms:** off-grid; incommunicado

## Adverb · Record 2

### Forms

- off-the-grid — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. In or into a situation or place in which electricity from the public electricity system is not used.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. In or into a clandestine or isolated situation or place, especially one in which public communication is curtailed.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. Secretly; in a clandestine manner.

   **Usage:** idiomatic
