### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A neurotropic nematode parasite (Parelaphostrongylus tenuis).

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any parasitic, worm-like species that inhabits the brain of another organism, typically altering its behaviour or giving it special abilities.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** literature; media; publishing; science-fiction

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
