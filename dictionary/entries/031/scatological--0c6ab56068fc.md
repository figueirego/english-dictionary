### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Relating to the research area of scatology, the particulate study of biological excrement, feces, or dung.

   **Usage:** formal

   **Example:** The scientist could read the dinosaur dung to see what it had eaten. He was an expert in scatological studies.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Relating to scatology, the use of obscenities.

   **Example:** His interest in scatological reading gained him very few friends.
