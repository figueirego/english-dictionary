### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who practises headhunting, the taking and preserving of a person's head after killing them.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anthropology; human-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One who recruits skilled personnel, especially executive-level ones, for a company.

   **Example:** A headhunter called me out of the blue with an offer I couldn't refuse.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business

   **Source synonyms:** recruiter; talent scout

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A pitcher who throws at the batter's head.

   **Example:** Thankfully, there aren't many headhunters in the modern game.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; baseball; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A player who uses a hockey stick to attempt to strike an opponent's head.

   **Example:** He is known as a goon and a headhunter.
