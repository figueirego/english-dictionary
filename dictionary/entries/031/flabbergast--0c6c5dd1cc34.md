### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To overwhelm with bewilderment; to amaze, confound, or stun, especially in a ludicrous manner.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He was flabbergasted to find that his work had been done for him before he began.

   **Example:** Her stupidity flabbergasts me, and I have to force myself to keep a straight face while she explains her beliefs.

   **Example:** The oddity of the situation was so flabbergasting I couldn’t react in time for anyone to see it.

   **Source synonyms:** flabbergaster
