### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A style of Japanese pottery, considered the traditional style for the pottery used in the Japanese tea ceremony; (especially capitalized) such pottery made by the Raku family.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ceramics; chemistry; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The English transliteration of a Japanese surname; specifically, that of the family traditionally licensed to manufacture the pottery.
