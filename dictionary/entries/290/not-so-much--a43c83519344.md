## Phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Indicates that the speaker identifies with a prior statement to a much lesser degree (or not at all) than the other person.

   **Example:** My mom really likes spinach; me, not so much.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Indicates that one factor is much more relevant or important than another.

   **Example:** It's not so much that he's a bad person, but that his political views are too extreme.
