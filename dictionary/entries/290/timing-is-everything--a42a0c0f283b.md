## Proverb

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The consideration of other events can greatly influence some desired outcome (such as whether a joke is well received).

   **Example:** \- Telling the old joke about a butt-crack was not a good idea, just as the plumber arrived, Bob. - You know what they say&#58; "timing is everything." I'm sure we can find another plumber before the house floods.
