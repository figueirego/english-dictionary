## Noun · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A phenomenon where in a vacuum, two clean oxide-less uncoated metal surfaces will fuse, weld, or merge together when they come into contact with each other, without any other action required (without fusion heating).

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** contact welding
