### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of liberating or the state of being liberated.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The liberation of American slaves was accomplished by the Department of War, that of British slaves by the Exchequer.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Synonym of conquest or theft.

   **Usage:** countable; euphemistic; ironic; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** conquest or theft (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The achievement of equal rights and status, particularly as seen as freedom from historic and structural oppression.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** women's liberation... black liberation... gay liberation...

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; politics
