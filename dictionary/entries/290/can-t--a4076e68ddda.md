### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Cannot (negative modal auxiliary); be unable to; not have the ability to (whether due to lacking relevant strength or skill or knowledge or supplies, or due to logical impossibility, etc).

   **Example:** I can’t quite get it to work.

   **Example:** Shut up, can’t you?

   **Example:** The butler can’t be the murderer — he can't have killed her — because he was in London that evening.

   **Source antonyms:** be able to; can

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To be forbidden to; to be not permitted to.

   **Example:** You can’t enter the hall without a ticket.

   **Source antonyms:** be able to; can; have to; must

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To be unwilling to; to not have the desire of; to don’t want to.

   **Usage:** euphemistic; slang

   **Example:** Will you come to dinner with us tonight? Can't; already have plans this evening.
