## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A theorised flood, 5.33 million years ago, of the Mediterranean basin, then dry due to the Messinian salinity crisis, triggered by its reconnection with the Atlantic Ocean, that marks the transition from the Miocene epoch (Messinian age) to the Pliocene epoch (Zanclean age).

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; geology; natural-sciences

   **Source synonyms:** Zanclean deluge; terminal Messinian flood
