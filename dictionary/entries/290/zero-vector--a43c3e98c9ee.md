### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A vector 0 in a vector space V such that for any v∈V, 0+v=v; a vector whose value in every dimension is zero; i.e., ⃑0=&lt;0,0,…,0&gt;.

   **Example:** The zero vector of a given vector space is the space's additive identity.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** linear-algebra; mathematics; sciences
