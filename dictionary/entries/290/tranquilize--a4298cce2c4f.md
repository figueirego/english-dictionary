### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To calm (a person or animal) or put them to sleep using a tranquilizer dart.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The escaped lion was finally tracked down, tranquilized, and safely returned to the zoo.

   **Source synonyms:** dart; sedate

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To make (something or someone) tranquil.

   **Usage:** literary; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** appease; calm; pacify

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To become tranquil.

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete; rare

   **Source synonyms:** calm down; relax

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To dart (a person or animal) with a sedative

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The bear should go down several minutes after being tranquilized.
