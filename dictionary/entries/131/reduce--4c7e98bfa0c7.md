### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To bring down the size, quantity, quality, value or intensity of something; to diminish, to lower.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to reduce weight, speed, heat, expenses, price, personnel etc.

   **Source synonyms:** cut; decrease; lower; abase (archaic); abate; belittle; besmall (rare); bring down; embase (obsolete); cut; decrease; decrement; diminish; foreshorten; lessen; lilliputianize; lower; mince; minimize; reduce; shrink; smallify

   **Source antonyms:** enlarge; increase; add; amplify; augment; dilate; eke / eke out; elevate; embiggen (humorous; nonstandard; usually); enhance; enlarge; enlargen (nonstandard); enrich; greaten; grow; increase; increment; largen (UK; literary); magnify; propagate (obsolete); raise; strengthen; swell; up

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To lose weight.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To bring to an inferior rank; to degrade, to demote.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to reduce a sergeant to the ranks

   **Source synonyms:** disparage; humble; relegate; sink; abase; bring down to size (idiomatic); cut down to size (idiomatic); debase (archaic); defame; degrade; demean; demote; depress; discredit; disgrace; dishonour; disparage; downgrade; embase (archaic); give a bad name; humble; humiliate; knock down to size (idiomatic); put someone in their place (idiomatic); reduce; relegate; sink (figuratively); slur; trim down to size (idiomatic); whittle down to size (idiomatic)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To humble; to conquer; to subdue; to capture.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to reduce a province or a fort

   **Source synonyms:** overcome; surmount; vanquish; beat; bring down; convince (obsolete); chew up; eat someone for breakfast (slang); eat someone's lunch (US; slang); flog (Australia; New-Zealand); humble; kick ass; malavogue (Ireland; dated); murdelize; outfox; outguess; outrun; outsmart; outwit; overcome; overpower; overwin; own; pulverise; pwn; rape (offensive; slang); reduce; spank; steal the show; subdue; subvert; surmount; thrash; trounce; vanquish

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To bring to an inferior state or condition.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to reduce a city to ashes

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To be forced by circumstances (into something one considers unworthy).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** reduced to silence

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To decrease the liquid content of (a food) by boiling much of its water off.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** cooking; food; lifestyle

   **Source synonyms:** inspissate; condense; engross (obsolete); incrassate (rare); inspissate; reduce; thick (archaic); thicken

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Formaldehyde can be reduced to form methanol.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; metallurgy; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

**Source sense R1.S10**

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

11. To express the solution of a problem in terms of another (known) algorithm.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computer; computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; science; sciences
