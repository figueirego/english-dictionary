## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A piano placed in a public area to encourage passers-by to stop and play.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A mechanical piano used by street entertainers, operated with a crank that turned a wooden cylinder from which leather hammers were thrown against the strings.

   **Usage:** historical

   **Source synonyms:** barrel piano
