## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The unusually loud hearing of a person's own voice, breathing or other self-generated sounds.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** autophonia; tympanophony; tympanophonia

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An auscultatory process which consists of noting the tone of the observer's own voice, while he speaks, holding his head close to the patient's chest.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences
