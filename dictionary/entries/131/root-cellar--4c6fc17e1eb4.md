## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

- root cellars — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A structure built underground or partially underground and used to store vegetables, fruits, nuts, and other foods; it may be under a house or near one.

   **Example:** Potatoes can keep if they are in a root cellar.
