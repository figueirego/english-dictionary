### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. indicates that a document has no place of publication shown on the document and that none has been inferred from other evidence

   **Source context (verbatim):** in bibliographies

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. indicates that an anterior subject does not belong in a particular place or situation.

   **Usage:** often preceded by "had" or "has"

   **Example:** Terrorism and mass murder have no place in this world.
