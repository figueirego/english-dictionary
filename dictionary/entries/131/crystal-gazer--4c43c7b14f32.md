### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Someone who claims to be able to see the future by gazing into a crystal ball.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Someone who makes predictions about the future, especially when these are seen as speculative.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** According to the bank's crystal-gazers, interest rates are going to rise again soon.
