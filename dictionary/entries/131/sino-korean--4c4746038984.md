### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of or pertaining both to China and to Korea; being both Chinese and Korean.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** the state of Sino-Korean relations

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Etymologically borrowed or otherwise derived from an eighth-century dialect of Middle Chinese which greatly influenced the Korean language.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; specifically

   **Example:** Until the fifteenth century, Koreans had a native word for the numeral "hundred", but it has since been replaced by a Sino-Korean word.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences
