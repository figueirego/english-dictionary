**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Your definition of "elephant" needs to be more precise than "a big animal with large ears".

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; lexicography; linguistics; sciences; semantics
