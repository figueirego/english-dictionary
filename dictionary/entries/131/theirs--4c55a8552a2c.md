### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. That or those belonging to them; the possessive case of they, used without a following noun.

   **Example:** This luggage is theirs. These passports are also theirs.

   **Example:** Theirs cost a lot more than ours.

   **Example:** That cat of theirs got into our garden again.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Their house or home.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** Let's go over to theirs.
