## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A strand of yarn etc. as used to guide one through a labyrinth; something which points the way, a guide.

   **Usage:** archaic

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Information which may lead one to a certain point or conclusion.

   **Example:** Give me a clue because the question is too vague.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An object or a kind of indication which may be used as evidence.

   **Example:** The detectives were looking for some clues at the scene of the crime.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Insight or understanding ("to have a clue \[about\]" or "to have clue". See have a clue, clue stick)

   **Example:** I had little clue that I was being carefully monitored by the CCTV.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The text that indicates an answer in a crossword puzzle.

   **Usage:** crosswording
