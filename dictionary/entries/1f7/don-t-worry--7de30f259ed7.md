## Phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Indicates to the interlocutor not to worry (feel anxiety) about something.

   **Example:** A&#58; What about the late fee? B&#58; Don't worry about it — it's only a little bit.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Synonym of none of your business.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; informal; oblique

   **Example:** A&#58; How much did you make on that side gig with Julio and those guys? B&#58; Don't worry about it — it's only a little bit.

   **Source synonyms:** none of your business (synonym; synonym-of)
