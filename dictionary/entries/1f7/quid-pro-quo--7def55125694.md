### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Something which is understood as something else; an equivocation.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Substitution of one drug for another.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Something which is offered or asked for in exchange for something else.

   **Example:** To deny that the arrangement was a quid pro quo would be laughably disingenuous.

   **Example:** It was quite obviously a quid pro quo arrangement.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A usually non-monetary exchange transaction, or series or process of exchange transactions.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A usually non-monetary exchange transaction, or series or process of exchange transactions.
   1. An equal or fair transaction or series of process of exchange transactions; tit for tat.
