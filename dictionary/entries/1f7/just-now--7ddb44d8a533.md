### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Very close to the present moment; just a moment ago.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; set phrase

   **Example:** I was talking to my friend on the phone just now.

   **Example:** I('ve) just now noticed that we're out of bread.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. At present; now.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** We do not have that item in stock just now.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. In a little while.

   **Usage:** Caribbean; Scotland; South-Africa; not-comparable

   **Example:** I'll be coming just now.
