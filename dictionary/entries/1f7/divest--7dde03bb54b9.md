### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To strip, deprive, or dispossess (someone) of something (such as a right, passion, privilege, or prejudice).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** You shall never divest me of my right to free speech.

   **Example:** When I wake up, I make a point to divest myself of all my prejudices, ready to start the day.

   **Example:** The report divested the organization of its mystique.

   **Source synonyms:** deprive; dispossess

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To sell off or be rid of through sale, especially of a subsidiary.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** In 2011, the company divested an 81% majority stake in its foreign subsidiary.

   **Example:** Glasgow will become the first university in Europe to divest from fossil fuels.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance

   **Source synonyms:** sell off

   **Source antonyms:** invest

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To undress.

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** undress; disrobe

   **Source antonyms:** dress
