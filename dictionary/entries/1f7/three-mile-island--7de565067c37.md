## Proper noun · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An island of Londonderry Township, Dauphin County, Pennsylvania, United States, on Lake Frederick, Susquehanna River; the site of a nuclear power plant accident in 1979.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A nuclear power plant located on the island; the site of a nuclear accident in 1979.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A nuclear reactor core partial meltdown in 1979, at the nuclear power plant's reactor unit 2

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An island of the town of Meredith, Belknap County, Lakes Region, New Hampshire, United States, on Lake Winnipesaukee.
