## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who is very intelligent, but socially dull.

   **Usage:** derogatory

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person who is very intelligent in one limited sphere, but inept in other areas.

   **Usage:** derogatory

   **Example:** It is also acceptable to say that the computer is a dumb genius.
