**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A result about injectivity and surjectivity of polynomials, often given as this special case&#58; If P is an injective polynomial function from an n-dimensional complex vector space to itself then P is bijective. The full theorem generalizes to any algebraic variety over an algebraically closed field.
