### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A container, typically made of glass or plastic and having a tapered neck, used primarily for holding liquids.

   **Example:** Beer is often sold in bottles.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The contents of such a container.

   **Example:** I only drank a bottle of beer.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A container with a rubber nipple used for giving liquids to infants, a baby bottle.

   **Example:** The baby wants a bottle.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. (originally bottle and glass as rhyming slang for "arse") Nerve, courage.

   **Usage:** UK; informal

   **Example:** You don’t have the bottle to do that!

   **Example:** He was going to ask her out, but he lost his bottle when he saw her.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A container of hair dye, hence with one’s hair color produced by dyeing.

   **Usage:** attributive

   **Example:** Did you know he’s a bottle brunette? His natural hair color is strawberry blonde.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a person with a particular hair color

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Intoxicating liquor; alcohol.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** to drown one’s troubles in the bottle

   **Example:** to hit the bottle
