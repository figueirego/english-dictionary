<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

   **Usage:** Australia; South-Asia; informal

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. A pedlar or hawker, especially one selling cheap or substandard goods.

   **Usage:** archaic

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. Cheap or substandard goods sold by a duffer.

   **Usage:** archaic

<a id="r2-s5"></a>
