## Adverb · Record 1

### Forms

- more by accident — comparative
- most by accident — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Accidentally; unintentionally; without meaning to; as the result of an event not expected, aided, or willingly planned.

   **Example:** Sorry. I knocked over the vase by accident.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. As the result of some undesigned, unintended, unrelated, natural, or random occurrence; as the unexpected and unforeseen result of formerly unrelated factors.

   **Example:** The car got totaled by accident; a giant snowdrift fell off the church roof.

   **Example:** The only way that project still exists today is by accident; I thought we closed it years ago.

   **Example:** The scholar thinks the quote and the parable were spoken on different occasions and only by accident were brought together here.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. By chance; unexpectedly; as the result of an unrelated series of events.

   **Example:** I hadn't seen her for years, but I ran into her by accident at the store.

   **Example:** She overheard the information by accident.

## Adjective · Record 2

### Forms

- more by accident — comparative
- most by accident — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Accidental; as the result of some unforeseen happening or series of events; unexpected; resulting from an event without the foresight, expectation, aid, or design of the person by whose agency it was caused; unintentional.

   **Example:** The victim's death was by accident.

   **Example:** That the injury was by accident is not questioned.

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** causeless; incidental; unintended
