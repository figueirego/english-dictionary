### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Other; in addition to previously mentioned items.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; postpositional

   **Example:** The instructor is busy. Can anyone else help me?

   **Example:** Whatever else she may be, she is not a surgeon.

   **Example:** When all else fails, read the instructions.
