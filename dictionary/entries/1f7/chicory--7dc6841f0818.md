### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Either of two plants of the Asteraceae family.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Either of two plants of the Asteraceae family.
   1. Common chicory (Cichorium intybus), source of Belgian endive, radicchio, and certain other cultivars with other names.

      **Usage:** UK; countable; uncountable

      **Example:** sugarloaf chicory

      **Source synonyms:** blue daisy; blue dandelion; blue sailor; blue weed; chicory button; coffeeweed; cornflower; hendibeh; horseweed; ragged sailor; succory; wild bachelor's button; wild endive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Either of two plants of the Asteraceae family.
   1. Endive (Cichorium endivia), the source of escarole and frisée.

      **Usage:** Canada; US; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A coffee substitute made from the roasted roots of the common chicory, sometimes used as a cheap adulterant in real coffee.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** cooking; food; lifestyle
