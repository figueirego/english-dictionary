### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A friend who supports someone; a person who has someone's back; a backer, a supporter.

   **Usage:** UK; archaic; dialectal

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Synonym of hangnail (“a loose, narrow strip of nail tissue protruding from the side edge and anchored near the base of a fingernail or toenail”).

   **Usage:** UK; dialectal

   **Source synonyms:** hangnail (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A person who pretends to be someone's friend; a false friend, a secret enemy.

   **Usage:** UK; dialectal; obsolete
