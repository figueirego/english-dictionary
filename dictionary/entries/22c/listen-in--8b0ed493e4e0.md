### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To listen without participating.

   **Example:** Do you mind if I listen in on your meeting tomorrow?

   **Example:** We invite you to listen in to our radio broadcast.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To eavesdrop; to listen secretly.

   **Example:** You should close the door for conversations like that. You never know who might be listening in.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To listen to a radio broadcast.

   **Usage:** dated

   **Example:** Did you listen in to the concert last night?
