## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A skill for dealing with people and society.

   **Usage:** countable; in-plural; often

   **Example:** His lack of mastery of the social graces made it obvious he had not been raised in upper-class society.

   **Source synonyms:** social skills; soft skill; people skills

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The ability to fit into polite society and behave properly and with etiquette.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** His lack of social grace made him anathema at dinner parties.

   **Source synonyms:** refinement
