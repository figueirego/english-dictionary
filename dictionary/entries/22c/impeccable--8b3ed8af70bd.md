### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Perfect, without faults, flaws or errors

   **Example:** He grew up in Norway, but he writes impeccable English.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Incapable of wrongdoing or sin; immaculate

   **Example:** It was easy for James V to imprison Lady Glamis, but actually convicting her was far more difficult; her character was impeccable and she was highly respected by all who knew her.
