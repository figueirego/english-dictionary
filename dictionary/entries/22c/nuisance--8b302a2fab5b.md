### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A minor annoyance or inconvenience.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The neighbor's dog barking throughout the night is a right nuisance - I'm going to complain.

   **Example:** What a nuisance to have to go now!

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person or thing causing annoyance or inconvenience.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** You can be such a nuisance when you don't get your way.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Anything harmful or offensive to the community or to a member of it, for which a legal remedy exists.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** a public nuisance

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law
