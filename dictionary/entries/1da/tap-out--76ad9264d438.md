## Verb

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To produce (a message, rhythm, or other thing) by tapping.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To submit to an opponent by tapping one's hand repeatedly either on the arena or the opponent's body.

   **Usage:** intransitive; combat sports

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To force (an opponent) to submit.

   **Usage:** transitive; combat sports

   **Example:** He tapped his opponent out in what seemed like ten seconds.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To relieve a person of duty, such as a casino worker or wrestler in a tag-team match.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To deplete, especially of a liquid; to finish the last of a drink.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** After a month of sugaring, the local maples were all tapped out.

   **Example:** It's nice that the ATM is open 24-7, but it doesn't help if your account is tapped out.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To run out of money in a gambling establishment.

   **Usage:** intransitive

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** tap — to submit; tap — to force to submit
