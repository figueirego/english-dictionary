### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The quality or state of being fast (in various senses).
   1. The quality or state of being strongly attached; firmness, secureness, tenacity.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The quality or state of being fast (in various senses).
   1. The quality or state of being strongly attached; firmness, secureness, tenacity.
      1. The ability of a dye to withstand fading.

         **Usage:** countable; specifically; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The quality or state of being fast (in various senses).
   1. The quality or state of moving quickly; quickness, rapidity, swiftness.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The quality or state of being fast (in various senses).
   1. The quality or state of having an extravagant lifestyle or immoral habits.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The quality or state of being fast (in various senses).
   1. Of a fortress or other place&#58; the quality or state of being secure from access or attack; safety, security.

      **Usage:** archaic; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The quality or state of being fast (in various senses).
   1. The quality or state of being firm, hard, or solid; firmness, hardness, solidity.

      **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Something that is fast, or that causes someone or something to be fast (in various senses).
   1. A fortified or secure place; a fortress, a stronghold.

      **Usage:** also; countable; figuratively; uncountable

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Something that is fast, or that causes someone or something to be fast (in various senses).
   1. Something used to fasten or tie; a fastener or fastening.

      **Usage:** countable; regional; uncountable; obsolete except UK
