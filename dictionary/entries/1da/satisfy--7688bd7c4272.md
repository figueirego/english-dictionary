### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To be enough (for)

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** That brief explanation will never satisfy.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To be enough (for)
   1. To meet the needs of, to fulfill the wishes or requirements of (someone).

      **Usage:** ambitransitive; transitive

      **Example:** The final rehearsal of the day seemed to satisfy the director.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To be enough (for)
   1. To provide what is wanted or required for (something).

      **Usage:** ambitransitive; transitive

      **Example:** Even three giant helpings of turkey and ham didn't satisfy her appetite.

      **Source synonyms:** supply; appease; gratify

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To pay to the extent of what is claimed or due.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to satisfy a creditor

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To answer or discharge (a claim, debt, legal demand, etc.); to give compensation for.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to satisfy a claim or an execution

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To cause (a sentence) to be true when the sentence is interpreted in one's universe.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The complex numbers satisfy \#92;existsx\#58;x²\#43;1\#61;0.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; logic; mathematics; philosophy; sciences

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To convince by ascertaining; to free from doubt.

   **Usage:** dated; literary; transitive
