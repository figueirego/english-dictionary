## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The conversion of a formula of first-order logic which involves the following steps&#58; (1) replace free variables with constants, (2) replace any variable bound by an existential quantifier which lies in the scope of an even number of logical negations with a Skolem function, and (3) replace any variable bound by a universal quantifier which lies in the scope of an odd number of negations with a Skolem function.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; logic; mathematics; philosophy; sciences
