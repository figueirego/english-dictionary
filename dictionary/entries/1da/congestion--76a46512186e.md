### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The hindrance or blockage of the passage of something, for example a fluid, mixture, traffic, people, etc. (due to an excess of this or due to a partial or complete obstruction), resulting in overfilling or overcrowding.
   1. An accumulation or buildup, the act of gathering into a heap or mass.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Example:** network congestion

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The hindrance or blockage of the passage of something, for example a fluid, mixture, traffic, people, etc. (due to an excess of this or due to a partial or complete obstruction), resulting in overfilling or overcrowding.
   1. Blocking up of the capillary and other blood vessels, etc., in any locality or organ (often producing other morbid symptoms); local hyperemia, active or passive.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Example:** arterial congestion

      **Example:** venous congestion

      **Example:** congestion of the lungs

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An excess or accumulation of something.
   1. An excess of traffic; usually not a complete standstill of traffic, so usually not synonymous with traffic jam.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Example:** traffic congestion

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An excess or accumulation of something.
   1. An excess of mucus or fluid in the respiratory system; congestion of the lungs, or nasal congestion.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An excess or accumulation of something.
   1. Edema, water retention, swelling, enlargement of a body part because of fluid retention in tissues and vessels.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable
