### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A flat-bottomed craft able to float and drift on water, used for transport or as a waterborne platform.

   **Example:** They floated down the river on an inflatable raft

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any flattish thing, usually wooden, used in a similar fashion.

   **Usage:** broadly

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A thick crowd of aquatic birds, particularly a group floating on the surface of the water.

   **Usage:** collective

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A thick crowd of aquatic birds, particularly a group floating on the surface of the water.
   1. A group of aquatic mammals, especially otters and especially when visible on the surface of the water.

      **Usage:** broadly; collective

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A thick crowd of aquatic birds, particularly a group floating on the surface of the water.
   1. A group of alligators or crocodiles, especially when floating in water with their backs visible above the surface.

      **Usage:** collective

      **Source synonyms:** congregation

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A collection of logs, fallen trees, etc. which obstructs navigation in a river.

   **Usage:** US

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A slice of toast.

   **Usage:** US; slang; when ordering food

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A square array of sensors forming part of a large telescope.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>
