### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To expel air from the lungs through the nose or mouth by action of the diaphragm, to breathe out.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To expel (something, such as tobacco smoke) from the lungs by action of the diaphragm.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To pass off in the form of vapour; to emerge.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To emit (a vapour, an odour, etc.).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The earth exhales vapor; marshes exhale noxious effluvia.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To draw out; to cause to be emitted in vapour.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The sun exhales the moisture of the earth.
