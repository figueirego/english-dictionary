## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A line that is a base for measurement or for construction.

   **Example:** A laser level generates a convenient baseline for interior work.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A datum used as the basis for calculation or for comparison.

   **Example:** We used the last doctor visit to provide baselines for vital statistics.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A line used as the basis for the alignment of glyphs.

   **Example:** Several characters typically have descenders below the lower baseline.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** media; publishing; typography

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The line at the farthest ends of the court indicating the boundary of the area of play.

   **Example:** The umpire missed the call. The ball hit the baseline.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports; tennis

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A configuration of software, hardware, or a process that is established and documented as a point of reference.

   **Example:** The baseline configuration includes unsupported components.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. An initial measurement or observation used as a reference point to compare future changes.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences
