### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of dubious authenticity, legitimacy or legality.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Example:** He's selling new CD players for £20 each – that sounds a bit iffy to me.

   **Source synonyms:** dodgy; dubious; fishy

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Uncertain or chancy; risky.

   **Example:** The weather is still iffy for Saturday's rocket launch.

   **Example:** That potato salad smells a bit iffy. Don't chance it — it's not worth salmonellosis.
