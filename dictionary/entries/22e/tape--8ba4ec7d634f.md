**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Flexible material in a roll with a sticky surface on one or both sides; adhesive tape.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** I sealed up the box with clear shipping tape.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** We made some decorative flowers out of the tape we bought.

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Finishing tape, stretched across a track to mark the end of a race.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Jones broke the tape in 47.77 seconds, a new world record.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Magnetic or optical recording media in a roll; videotape or audio tape.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Did you get that on tape?

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Any video or audio recording, regardless of the method used to produce it.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; informal; uncountable
