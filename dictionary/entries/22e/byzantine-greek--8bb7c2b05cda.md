### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Collectively, the continuum of forms of the Greek language as written and spoken during the time of the Byzantine Empire (C.E. 330–1453), preceded by Koine Greek and Early Medieval Greek and succeeded by Late Medieval Greek to Modern Greek. (ISO 639-3 code&#58; grc shared with Classical Greek.)
