### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Source synonyms:** waterfast; waterproof; impervious; impermeable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. So devised or planned as to be impossible to defeat, evade or nullify.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** a watertight alibi
