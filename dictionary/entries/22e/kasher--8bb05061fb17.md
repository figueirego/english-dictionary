### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To render kosher.
   1. to remove excess blood from by washing and salting

      **Usage:** Judaism; transitive

      **Example:** “Kosher salt” is called thus, not because it is any more kosher than other types of salt, but because it is used in the kashering process of meat.

      **Source context (verbatim):** of meat

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To render kosher.
   1. to remove traces of non-kosher substances from by cleaning, heating, etc.

      **Usage:** Judaism; transitive

      **Example:** David moved into his new apartment, put up mezuzahs, and kashered his new kitchen.

      **Source context (verbatim):** of kitchenware
