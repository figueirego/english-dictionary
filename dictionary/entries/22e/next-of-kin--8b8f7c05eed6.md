## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Closest blood relative, heir to inheritance.

   **Example:** Lord Bentley has passed away; notify the next of kin.

   **Example:** Shall we inform the next of kin of his passing?

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Close friend, spouse, or partner (for example on a hospital admission form), one of someone's close ones.

   **Source synonyms:** loved one; near relative; close one
