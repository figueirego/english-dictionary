## Adjective · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈkʌstəm/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Made or done in a way adjusted to fit the needs of a particular person or group (e.g., customer, health care patient, do-it-yourselfer), and thus specialized and, in some cases, unique.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** My feet are very large, so I need custom shoes, specially made by a custom tailor.

   **Source synonyms:** bespoke; custom-made; tailored; tailor-made; custom-built; purpose-built; custom-made; made-to-measure; made-to-order; tailor-made

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Own, personal, not standard or premade.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** We can embroider a wide range of ready designs or a custom logo.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. Done on a for-hire basis, as contrasted with being done only for oneself.

   **Usage:** especially; not-comparable

   **Example:** custom threshing

   **Example:** custom baling

   **Example:** The amount of high-end equipment that he maintains is justified by the fact that he does a lot of custom tillage and combining.

   **Example:** My grandfather did his own threshing, plus a bunch of custom threshing on the side.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** agriculture; business; lifestyle

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

   **Usage:** archaic; not-comparable
