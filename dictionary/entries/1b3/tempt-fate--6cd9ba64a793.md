### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To take a risk.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** Don't tempt fate by driving without a seatbelt.

   **Source synonyms:** court disaster; play Russian roulette

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To take an imagined or superstitiously believed risk of provoking an adverse outcome by mentioning something or making a prediction.

   **Example:** I shouldn't tempt fate, but it looks like it's going to rain.

   **Example:** "I'm sure everything will work out fine" — "Don't tempt fate!"
