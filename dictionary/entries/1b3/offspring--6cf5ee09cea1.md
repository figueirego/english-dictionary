### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person's daughter or son; a person's child.

   **Example:** We are the offspring of Italian immigrants.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of a person's descendants, including of further generations.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An animal or plant's progeny or young.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Anything produced; the result of an entity's efforts.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** Artists often treasure their works as their immortal offspring.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**
