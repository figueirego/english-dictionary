## Noun

### Forms

- club cars — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A railroad car that serves in the manner of a club (lounge), offering drinks, snacks, background music, and sometimes meals.

   **Usage:** dated

   **Example:** I saw Charlie last night in the club car. When we get to Chicago, he's going to telephone Mr. Brown for further instructions.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** rail-transport; railways; transport

   **Source synonyms:** buffet car; refreshment car

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A golf cart.

   **Usage:** broadly; informal

   **Example:** We'll take the ferry to the island, and when we get off the ferry, we'll rent a club car and drive into town.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see club, car; for example, one of the cars owned by a car club member.

   **Example:** Several club cars were in the cruise on Sunday. Clubmembers Joe and Jane Worthington brought their Packard, and Tom Smith brought his Plymouth.
