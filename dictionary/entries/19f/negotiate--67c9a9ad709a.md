### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To confer with others in order to come to terms or reach an agreement.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source synonyms:** treat (especially; formal); **Source context (verbatim):** in order to reach a settlement

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To arrange or settle something by mutual agreement.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** We negotiated the contract to everyone's satisfaction.

   **Example:** The client and server computers must first negotiate a network protocol to be used.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To succeed in coping with, getting over or navigating a hazard or obstacle.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** We negotiated the mountain track with difficulty.

   **Example:** Although the car was quite rickety, he could negotiate the curves very well.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To transfer to another person with all the rights of the original holder; to pass, as a bill.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To transact business; to carry on trade.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To intrigue; to scheme.

   **Usage:** obsolete
