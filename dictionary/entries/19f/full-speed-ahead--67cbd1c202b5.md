**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A command to move forward at maximum speed.

   **Usage:** usually

   **Example:** Damn the torpedoes, full speed ahead!

**Source sense R2.S1**

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** Then he gave it full speed ahead, and shot us into the inlet.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport
