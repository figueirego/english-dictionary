### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Inflammation of a mucous membrane.
   1. Especially, that of the nose and throat.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

      **Source synonyms:** coryza

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Inflammation of a mucous membrane.
   1. The discharge (fluid) associated with this condition.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Example:** He coughed violently and spat out the catarrh irritating his throat.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences
