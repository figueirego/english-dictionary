## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Synonym of Mongolia, (country); viewed as a region south of Siberia, west of Manchuria and north of Inner Mongolia (with closely related native Mongolian population), well north of the Great Wall, which never became an integral part of the Chinese empire, and later achieved autonomy and full independence from China, as the republic of Mongolia.

   **Usage:** historical

   **Source synonyms:** Mongolia (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any proverbially distant or remote place.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Source synonyms:** Timbuktu
