### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To sit or stand with a leg on each side of something; to sit astride.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** stride

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To be on both sides of something; to have parts that are in different places, regions, etc.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** arch; traverse

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To consider or favor two apparently opposite sides; to be noncommittal.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Wanting to please both sides, he straddled the issue.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To form a disorderly sprawl; to spread out irregularly.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** This weed straddles the entire garden.

   **Example:** Conservation and Management of Straddling Fish Stocks and Highly Migratory Fish Stocks

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To fire successive artillery shots in front of and behind of a target, especially in order to determine its range (the term "bracket" is often used instead).

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To place a voluntary raise prior to receiving cards (only by the first player after the blinds).

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** card-games; poker

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To stand with the ends staggered; said of the spokes of a wagon wheel where they join the hub.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To execute a commodities market spread.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** economics; sciences
