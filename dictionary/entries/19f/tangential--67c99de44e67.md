### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Referring to a tangent, moving at a tangent to something.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Merely touching, positioned as a tangent.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Only indirectly related.

   **Example:** That subject is tangential to our discussion, and we cannot let it distract us.

   **Source synonyms:** tangent; incidental
