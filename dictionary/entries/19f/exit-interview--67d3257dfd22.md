## Noun

### Forms

- exit interviews — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A final meeting between a person who is leaving his or her employment and a representative of the employing organization, in order to gather feedback about employment conditions and reduce future turnover.

   **Usage:** human resource management

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A similar meeting held when a person leaves some other type of organization, such as an educational institution or voluntary association.
