### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The leader of an Arab village, family or small tribe.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An Islamic religious cleric; the master of a Sufi order.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An official title for members of the royal family as well as some prominent families.

   **Usage:** some Arab Gulf countries

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A romantic lover.

   **Usage:** archaic; slang

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An Arab, especially one dressed in traditional clothing.

   **Usage:** slang

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A member of one of the three hereditary, traditionally endogamous Yazidi castes, taking certain spiritual and leadership roles.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. An honorific for specialists in spirituality, for example in Sufism.
