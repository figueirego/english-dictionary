**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A (hypothetical) prime number p such that p² divides F\_π(p), where F\_n is the Fibonacci sequence and π(p) is the pth Pisano period (the period length of the Fibonacci sequence reduced modulo p).

   **Example:** Wall-Sun-Sun primes are conjectured to exist, but no example has yet been found.
