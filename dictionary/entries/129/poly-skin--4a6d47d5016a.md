### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A type of hair replacement system that uses a base made from a thin, flexible polyurethane material; the polyurethane membrane is often translucent and designed to mimic the appearance of human skin or epidermis.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A shorthand for treatments involving polynucleotides, which are injectable products used for skin rejuvenation.
