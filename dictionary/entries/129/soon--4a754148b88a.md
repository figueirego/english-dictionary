### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Short in length of time from the present.

   **Example:** I need the soonest date you have available.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Early.

   **Usage:** US; dialectal

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used as an alternative to express 'to be going to' in the form 'to be soon to'.

   **Example:** Hurry up, the wedding is soon to start!

   **Example:** A new shop is soon to be opened in this street.
