### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The property of a body that resists any change to its uniform motion; equivalent to its mass.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In a person, unwillingness to take action.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Lack of activity; sluggishness; said especially of the uterus, when, in labour, its contractions have nearly or wholly ceased.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences
