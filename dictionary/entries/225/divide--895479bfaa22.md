   **Example:** a wall divides two houses; a stream divides the towns

   **Source synonyms:** cut up; disunite; partition; split; split up

   **Source antonyms:** combine; merge; unify; unite

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A thing that divides.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>
