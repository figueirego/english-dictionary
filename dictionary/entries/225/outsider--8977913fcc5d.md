### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Someone who is not part of a particular community, profession, organization etc.; a person coming from elsewhere, having no special knowledge.

   **Example:** While the initiated easily understand the symbols, they are wholly inaccessible to outsiders.

   **Source synonyms:** outlier; stranger; alien; barbarian (derogatory); firangi (UK); foreign; foreigner; fremd; fremdling; furriner (derogatory; dialectal); gaijin (Japan; derogatory); metœcus (rare); nonlocal; noninhabitant; nonnational; nonresident; non-resident; outcomeling; outener (dialectal); outlander; outsider; Paradesi (India); peregrine (dated); strangeling; stranger; uitlander (South-Africa); abject; castaway; deviant; exile; fremd (dialectal; rare); leper (figuratively); misfit; offscouring (figuratively); outcast; outsider; overcast (obsolete); pariah; reject (derogatory; slang); untouchable (figuratively)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Someone who does not fit into conventional society; a misfit; an iconoclast.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A competitor or contestant who has little chance of winning; a long shot.

   **Example:** Johnny was an outsider at this year's karate tournament, but he still managed to win second place out of sheer determination.

   **Source synonyms:** dark horse; little guy; long shot; underdog

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A type of jaunting car with the passenger seats facing outwards.

   **Usage:** obsolete; rare

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The heel or end piece of a loaf of bread.

   **Usage:** rare
