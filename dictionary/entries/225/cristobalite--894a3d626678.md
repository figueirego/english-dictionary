### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A mineral of volcanic rocks that solidified at a high temperature. Cristobalite is chemically identical to quartz, with the chemical formula SiO₂, but has a different crystal structure. Crystals are always microscopic, and usually form small rounded masses.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; geography; geology; mineralogy; natural-sciences; physical-sciences
