### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The process where tiny fog droplets adhere to the surfaces of trees and other objects, coalesce into larger drops, and then fall to the ground, providing a crucial water source in areas with low rainfall or in areas that are seasonally dry.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** climatology; meteorology; natural-sciences
