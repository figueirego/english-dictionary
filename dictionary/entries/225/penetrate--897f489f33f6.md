### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To enter into; to make way into the interior of; to pierce.

   **Example:** Light penetrates darkness.

   **Source synonyms:** ransack

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To achieve understanding of, despite some obstacle; to comprehend; to understand.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** I could not penetrate Burke's opaque rhetoric.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To affect profoundly through the senses or feelings; to move deeply.

   **Example:** to penetrate one's heart with pity

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To infiltrate an enemy to gather intelligence.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To insert the penis into an orifice, such as a vagina, mouth, or anus.

   **Example:** a male elephant comes up and penetrates the female

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To move a piece past the defending pieces of one's opponent.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** board-games; chess; games
