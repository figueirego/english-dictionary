### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Indicating degree, quantity or frequency greater than zero&#58; to the slightest degree, in any way, at any time or in any circumstance.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; not-comparable

   **Example:** You mustn't speak to her at all.

   **Example:** Can you see anyone at all?

   **Example:** Are you at all bothered by the noise?

   **Example:** Let me know if you are at all concerned.

   **Example:** Were you angry that he was laughing too hard, or that he was laughing at all?

   **Example:** My speech didn't seem to go down well at all.

   **Example:** I'm glad that she didn't stay long, but sorry that she came at all.

   **Example:** This is the only one of the issues for which I am at all responsible.

   **Example:** If it’s at all possible, I'll try and do it.

   **Source synonyms:** whatsoever; in the least; any
