### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A chess opening characterized by the moves 1.e4 e5, in which both White and Black move their king's pawn to the fourth rank with their first move.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of the set openings which begin in this way. (Ruy Lopez, Scotch Game, etc.)

   **Usage:** countable
