**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Usage:** Canadian; Australian rules football

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** American-football; ball-games; football; games; hobbies; lifestyle; rugby; sports

   **Usage:** Australia; derogatory; slang
