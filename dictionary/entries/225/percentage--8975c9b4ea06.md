### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The amount, number or rate of something, regarded as part of a total of 100; a part of a whole.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** A high percentage of secondary school leavers take a gap year.

   **Source synonyms:** centage

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A share of the sales, profits, gross margin or similar.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** She gets a percentage for every vacuum cleaner sold.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Benefit or advantage.

   **Usage:** countable; informal; uncountable

   **Example:** There was no percentage in staying at home.
