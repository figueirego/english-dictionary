### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A typically annual or semiannual public event for exhibition of privately owned automobiles, especially classic cars.

   **Example:** They went to the car show to see some vintage iron.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** automotive; transport; vehicles

   **Source synonyms:** cruise

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An auto show.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** They went to the car show to see the new models for this coming year.
