## Noun · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A town containing merchants who have exclusive right, under royal authority, to purchase or produce certain goods for export; also, the body of such merchants seen as a group.

   **Usage:** countable; historical; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Place of supply; source.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The principal commodity produced in a town or region.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A basic or essential supply.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Rice is a staple in the diet of many cultures.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A recurring topic, character, or item.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Lurid stories are a staple of tabloid journalism.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Short fiber, as of cotton, sheep’s wool, or the like, which can be spun into yarn or thread.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Tow is flax with short staple.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Unmanufactured material; raw material.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

## Noun · Etymology 2 · Record 4

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈsteɪ.pəl/ — source labels: Received-Pronunciation; US

### Forms

- staples — plural

### Senses

<a id="r4-s1"></a>

**Source sense R4.S1**

1. Any of several types of fastener comprising a bent piece of wire.
   1. A wire fastener, made of thin wire, used to secure stacks of paper by penetrating all the sheets and curling around.

      **Example:** They stapled the documents with an office stapler, putting a staple in the top left corner of each one.

<a id="r4-s2"></a>

**Source sense R4.S2**

2. Any of several types of fastener comprising a bent piece of wire.
   1. A wire fastener, in any of various sizes, used to secure something else by penetrating and curling.

      **Example:** They stapled the housewrap with a staple gun firing large staples.

<a id="r4-s3"></a>

**Source sense R4.S3**

3. Any of several types of fastener comprising a bent piece of wire.
   1. A U-shaped wire fastener, made of thick wire, used to attach fence wire or other material to posts or structures.

      **Example:** The rancher used staples to attach the barbed wire to the fence posts.

<a id="r4-s4"></a>

**Source sense R4.S4**

4. One of a set of U-shaped metal rods hammered into a structure, such as a piling or wharf, which serve as a ladder.

   **Example:** Fortunately, there were staples in the quay wall, and she was able to climb out of the water.

<a id="r4-s5"></a>

**Source sense R4.S5**

5. A metal loop used to fasten doors in conjunction with a hasp and a padlock.

<a id="r4-s6"></a>

**Source sense R4.S6**

6. A shaft, smaller and shorter than the principal one, joining different levels.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; mining

<a id="r4-s7"></a>

**Source sense R4.S7**

7. A small pit.

<a id="r4-s8"></a>

**Source sense R4.S8**

8. A district granted to an abbey.

<a id="r4-s9"></a>

**Source sense R4.S9**

9. A post; prop; support

   **Usage:** obsolete
