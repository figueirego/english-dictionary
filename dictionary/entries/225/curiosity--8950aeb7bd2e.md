### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Inquisitiveness; the tendency to ask and learn about things by asking questions, investigating, or exploring.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** inquisitiveness

   **Source antonyms:** ignorance

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A unique or extraordinary object which arouses interest.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** He put the strangely shaped rock in his curiosity cabinet.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Careful, delicate construction; fine workmanship, delicacy of building.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable
