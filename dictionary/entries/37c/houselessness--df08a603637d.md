## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Lack of a house and any other roof; by extension, being out of house and home.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** homelessness

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Lack of a house but not of a home.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** He said that his houselessness was not homelessness because he went to school in that community, was registered to vote there, and had been living in the teepee for seventeen years. He said that teepee was his home.
