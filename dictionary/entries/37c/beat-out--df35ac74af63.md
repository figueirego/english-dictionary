### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To sound a rhythm on a percussion instrument such as a drum.

   **Example:** The drummer beat out a steady slow march.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To extinguish.

   **Example:** He managed to beat the flames out with a blanket.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To defeat by a narrow margin.

   **Usage:** US

   **Example:** She beat out three other contenders to claim the prize.

**Source sense R1.S4**
