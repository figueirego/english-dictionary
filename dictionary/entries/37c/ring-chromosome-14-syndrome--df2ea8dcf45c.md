## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A rare human chromosome abnormality occurring when one or both of the telomeres that mark the ends of chromosome 14 are lost, allowing the uncapped ends to fuse together. It causes intellectual disability, recurrent seizures, etc.

   **Usage:** uncountable
