### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The inability of a person to recognize their own illness or handicap.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A failure to be aware of a defect or deficit resulting from disability, due to a brain injury (e.g. Anton-Babinski syndrome).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
