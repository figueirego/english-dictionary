### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Arising or originating from a figure of authority.

   **Example:** The authoritative rules in this school come not from the headmaster but from the aged matron.

   **Source synonyms:** formal; proper; ex cathedra

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Highly accurate or definitive; treated or worthy of treatment as a scholarly authority.

   **Example:** This book is the world's most authoritative guide to insect breeding habits.

   **Source synonyms:** definitive; of record; precise

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Having a commanding style.

   **Example:** He instructed us in that booming, authoritative voice of his.

   **Source synonyms:** grave; magisterial; masterful; oracular; sterling
