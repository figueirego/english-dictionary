### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The bulk transfer or release of a large amount of raw information from one database, system, or person to another.
   1. The act of exporting the contents and structure of an entire database into a single file to create a backup or migrate to a new system.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences
