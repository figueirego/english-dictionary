## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A lawyer, solicitor, etc, who acts on behalf of a client in legal matters.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

   **Source synonyms:** attorney; counselor; abogado; advocate; attorney; avocat; counsel; counselor; latitat (obsolete; slang); lawman (rare); lawyer; legal representative; limb of the law (archaic; slang); man of law (archaic)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One who represents or acts on behalf of another under authority recognised by law, such as a parent or guardian of a minor, a manager of a legal person, etc.
