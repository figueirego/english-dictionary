### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To eject something violently (such as lava or water, as from a volcano or geyser).

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The volcano erupted, spewing lava across a wide area.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To burst forth; to break out.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The third molar tooth erupts late in most people, and sometimes does not appear at all.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To spontaneously release pressure or tension.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

   **Example:** The crowd erupted in anger.

   **Example:** The whole table erupted in laughter.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. (Of birds, insects, etc.) To suddenly appear in a certain region in large numbers.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences
