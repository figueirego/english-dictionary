### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A large mist/smoke-filled black iron pot, stereotypically used by European witches to brew potions and cast spells.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Sarcosoma globosum&#58; a fist-sized non-toxic inedible mushroom, globular with a hole on its top, filled with a jelly; found in circumboreal spurce forest habits near water sources, primarily in Northern Europe.
