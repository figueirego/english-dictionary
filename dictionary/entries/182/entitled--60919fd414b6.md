### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having a title.

   **Usage:** literally

   **Example:** Her book is entitled 'My Autobiography'.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Having a legal or moral right or claim to something.

   **Example:** As a natural-born citizen I am entitled to run for president.

   **Example:** If you were injured at work you may be entitled to compensation.

   **Example:** He feels entitled to other people's respect.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Convinced of one's own righteousness (self-righteousness) or the justifiability of one's actions or status, especially wrongly so; demanding and pretentious.

   **Usage:** figuratively
