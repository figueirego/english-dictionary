### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A watch kept on some premises, or on a town, by guards during the night.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One or more of the guards on such a watch.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. One of the watches kept on a marine vessel.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The sailors standing such a watch.
