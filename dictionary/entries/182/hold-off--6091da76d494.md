### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To delay (someone or something) temporarily; to keep at bay.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** Let's try to hold off the lawyers until we are ready for them.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To delay commencing (an action until some specified time or event has passed).

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** Hold off (on) the decision one more day so I can answer your question.

   **Example:** Hold off (on) baking until I get there.
