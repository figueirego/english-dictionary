## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. specific impedance; especially the ratio of a pressure phasor associated with a wave at any given point in a medium to the velocity phasor at that point.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. bulk impedance; a measurement of the electrical impedance per unit distance in a bulk material or a solution

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
