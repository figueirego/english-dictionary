### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The white root of a yellow-flowered plant, Brassica rapa, grown as a vegetable and as fodder for cattle.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The yellow root of a related plant, the swede or Brassica napus.

   **Usage:** Atlantic-Canada; Cornwall; Ireland; Northern-England; Scotland; Manitoba

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The white root of Raphanus sativus var. longipinnatus, also known as a daikon.

   **Usage:** Hong-Kong

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The root of the yam bean, Pachyrhizus erosus; jicama.

   **Usage:** Malaysia; Singapore; broadly

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Clipping of turnip watch

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; clipping; dated

   **Alternative form of:** [turnip watch](../3bf/turnip-watch--efe64d421f8c.md)

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A fool or simpleton.

   **Usage:** slang
