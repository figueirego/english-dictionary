### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The last course of a meal, consisting of fruit, sweet confections etc.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** I ordered hummus for a starter, a steak as the main course, and chocolate cake for dessert.

   **Example:** Can I see the dessert menu, please?

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A sweet dish or confection served as the last course of a meal.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Trifle is a favourite dessert of the English, but rivalled by pavlova in Australia and New Zealand.
