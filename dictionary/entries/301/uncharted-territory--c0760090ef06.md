### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An area of land never before explored, mapped, or surveyed.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** terra incognita

   **Source antonyms:** home ground; homeland
