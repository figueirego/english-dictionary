### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One after the other; one at a time; in succession; successively.

   **Example:** The teacher answered the students each in turn.

   **Example:** Each member of the group, in turn, rose to share a personal story, until nearly everyone in the room had spoken.

   **Example:** Eventually, each of the countries in turn would be brought to submission by the invading force.

   **Source synonyms:** in order; step by step

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In due order; in proper sequence; in a determined or measured sequence, as a waiting line or queue.

   **Example:** You are next in turn to bat.

   **Example:** The boy was next in turn to sit on Santa's lap.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. In response; in return.

   **Example:** The musical ambassadors finished their performance, and the local musicians in turn played for them a traditional ballad.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Having a relationship sequentially comparable to one just mentioned; accordingly or similarly, with respect to sequence, precedence, or hierarchy.

   **Example:** The vassals were subject to their lords, who in turn were subject to barons or kings.

   **Example:** My car was hit by a truck, which in turn was hit by the van.

   **Example:** Mr. X leaked the secret to Mr. Y who in turn leaked it to Mr. Z.
