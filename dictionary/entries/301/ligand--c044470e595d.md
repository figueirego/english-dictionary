### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A molecule or moiety (ion, functional group, or radical) that binds to another chemical entity to form a larger complex; as, especially&#58;
   1. Such an entity that binds to a central metal atom to form a coordination complex.

      **Usage:** physical; coordination chemistry

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; inorganic-chemistry; natural-sciences; organic-chemistry; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A molecule or moiety (ion, functional group, or radical) that binds to another chemical entity to form a larger complex; as, especially&#58;
   1. Such an entity that forms a complex with a biomolecule to serve a biological purpose.

      **Usage:** physical

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biochemistry; biology; chemistry; inorganic-chemistry; medicine; microbiology; natural-sciences; organic-chemistry; pharmacology; physical-sciences; physiology; sciences
