## Adverb · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈmiːnwaɪl/ — variety unspecified in the source labels
- IPA: /ˈmiːnʍaɪl/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. During the time that something is happening, often specifically in a different place.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** There was a party going on downstairs. Meanwhile, I was reading a book.

   **Source synonyms:** in the meantime; meantime; meanwhilst; the while

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. During an intervening time; from now until a future time.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** We’re going on holiday next month; meanwhile, keep working on the project.

   **Source synonyms:** meantime; until then; for now

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. In contrast to aspects previously mentioned.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Some songs on the album are beautiful. Meanwhile, others are quite forgettable.

   **Source synonyms:** at the same time; on the other hand
