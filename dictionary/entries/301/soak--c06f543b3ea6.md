### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To be saturated with liquid by being immersed in it.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** I'm going to soak in the bath for a couple of hours.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To immerse in liquid to the point of saturation or thorough permeation.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Soak the beans overnight before cooking.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To penetrate or permeate by saturation.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The water soaked into my shoes and gave me wet feet.

   **Source synonyms:** imbue

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To allow (especially a liquid) to be absorbed; to take in, receive. (usually + up)

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** A sponge soaks up water; the skin soaks in moisture.

   **Example:** I soaked up all the knowledge I could at university.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To overcharge or swindle out of a large amount of money.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** fleece

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To drink intemperately or gluttonously.

   **Usage:** dated; slang

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To heat (a metal) before shaping it.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; metallurgy; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

**Source sense R1.S8**
