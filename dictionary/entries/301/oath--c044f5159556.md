## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈəʊθ/ — source labels: Received-Pronunciation
- IPA: /ˈoʊθ/ — source labels: General-American

### Forms

- oaths — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A solemn pledge or promise that invokes a deity, a ruler, or another entity (not necessarily present) to attest the truth of a statement or sincerity of one's desire to fulfill a contract or promise.

   **Example:** take an oath

   **Example:** swear an oath

   **Example:** break one's oath

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A statement or promise which is strengthened (affirmed) by such a pledge.

   **Example:** After taking the oath of office, she became the country's forty-third premier.

   **Example:** The generals swore an oath of loyalty to the country.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A light, irreverent or insulting appeal to a deity or other entity.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A curse, a curse word.

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** pledge; vow; avowal
