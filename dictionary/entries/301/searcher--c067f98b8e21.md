1. One who searches.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

   **Usage:** UK; historical

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

3. An officer who apprehended idlers on the street during church hours in Scotland.

   **Usage:** UK; historical

<a id="r1-s4"></a>
