## Interjection

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An expression of bashfulness or modesty, especially in response to a compliment or when giving thanks.

   **Usage:** US; colloquial

   **Example:** Aw shucks, you're going to make me blush.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used to express disappointment, embarrassment or annoyance.

   **Usage:** US; colloquial

   **Example:** Aw shucks, it looks like we're going to lose the game after all.
