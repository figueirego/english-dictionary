### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Opening of an organ by its own means (such as an anther or a seed pod) to release its contents.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A rupture, as with a surgical wound opening up, often with a flow of serous fluid.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences
