## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The phenomenon by which the Late Proto-Slavic "liquid diphthongs", formed from a vowel followed by a liquid \*r or \*l, become disyllabic -oro-, -ere- and -olo- reflexes in East Slavic languages.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; phonology; sciences
