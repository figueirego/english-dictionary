## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

- Communists — plural
- Commie — alternative
- Commo — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A member of a formally constituted Communist Party.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A proponent of the kind of communism formally espoused by such a party, typically either Marxism, or more commonly, Leninism or Maoism.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any member of an organization, especially a military force, controlled and led by a formally constituted Communist Party, even if most individual members of the organization are not party members.

   **Usage:** inexact

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Alternative letter-case form of communist, a proponent of the general philosophy of communism.

   **Usage:** alt-of; proscribed; sometimes; inexact

   **Alternative form of:** [communist](../0b5/communist--2d7c4738117f.md)
