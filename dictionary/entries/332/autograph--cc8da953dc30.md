### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person’s own handwriting, especially the signature of a famous or admired person.

   **Example:** Some autograph-hunters were pestering the players after the game.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person's signature used as a mark of formal approval.

   **Usage:** broadly; colloquial

   **Example:** If you could just put your autograph on the ol’ contract, please…

**Source sense R1.S3**
