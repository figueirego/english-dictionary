### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To cease fighting or arguing; to reach an agreement, or at least a truce.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** They need to calm down and bury the hatchet before someone gets hurt.

   **Source antonyms:** take up the hatchet

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
