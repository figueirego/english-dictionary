### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To maintain order among spectators encircling participants in a prizefight or a performance and keep them from coming too near the participants.

   **Usage:** obsolete

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; hobbies; lifestyle; sports; theater

   **Source synonyms:** keep the ring

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To be a spectator at a fight; (figuratively) to watch other people argue.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To oversee a situation while attempting to remain uninvolved in it.

   **Usage:** UK; idiomatic

   **Example:** Police held the ring during the protests.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Of a prizefighter&#58; to hold one's own during a fight; to continue winning a series of fights.
