**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A rewriting of computer code to improve its readability or structure without affecting its meaning or behaviour, thus making it easier to maintain; a refactoring.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** An objective of refactorization is to clear up the global scope of all the library variables, functions and objects.
