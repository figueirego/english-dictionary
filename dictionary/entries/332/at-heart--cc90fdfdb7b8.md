### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In spirit; according to one's beliefs, views or feelings; deep down, fundamentally, really.

   **Example:** I’m a vegan at heart – the thought of the death of animals for my food is abhorrent.

   **Example:** My dad may be old on the outside, but he’s young at heart; he plays video games every now and then to improve his coordination.

   **Source synonyms:** at bottom; essentially

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Wished earnestly for; held in high esteem.

   **Example:** I always have your best interests at heart.
