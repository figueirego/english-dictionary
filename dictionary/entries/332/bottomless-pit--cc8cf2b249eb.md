### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A pit with no visible bottom and apparently infinite depth.

   **Usage:** literally

   **Source synonyms:** abyss

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biblical; lifestyle; religion

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

   **Usage:** figuratively
