### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An employee of a hotel who attends to the requirements and wishes of guests.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An employee of a hotel who attends to the requirements and wishes of guests.
   1. One who offers a bespoke level of service in any of various industries.

      **Usage:** attributive; broadly

      **Example:** concierge banking (that is, private banking) concierge medicine concierge service

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A nurse who provides support during fertility treatment.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; healthcare

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. One who attends to the maintenance of a building and provides services to its tenants and visitors.

   **Usage:** US; often

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A senior official in charge of a fortress, palace, etc.; also, a custodian of a house; a warden of a prison.

   **Usage:** obsolete; except historical

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Synonym of conciergerie (“the office of a concierge; also, the lodge or residence of a concierge”).

   **Usage:** obsolete

   **Source synonyms:** conciergerie (synonym; synonym-of)
