## Proper noun · Record 1

### Forms

- the Immaculate Conception — canonical

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The doctrine, in the Roman Catholic Church, that the Virgin Mary was conceived biologically but free from original sin.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Catholicism; Christianity

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. December 8th (The Feast of the Immaculate Conception of Mary).

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Catholicism; Christianity; Roman-Catholicism

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The conception or incarnation of Jesus as viewed by those who believe that he was conceived without sin.

   **Usage:** proscribed

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A title of Mary, in particular reference to her unique status with regard to the aforementioned doctrine.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Catholicism; Christianity; Roman-Catholicism

## Noun · Record 2

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Any virgin birth or an event likened to one.

   **Usage:** informal; lowercase; often; proscribed; uncountable

   **Example:** I didn't think the cat had gotten out, but either she did or those kittens are a case of immaculate conception.
