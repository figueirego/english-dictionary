## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈpɑːspɔːt/ — source labels: Received-Pronunciation
- IPA: /ˈpæspɔɹt/ — source labels: US

### Forms

- passports — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An official document normally used for international journeys, which proves the identity and nationality of the person for whom it was issued.

   **Example:** You will have to bring your passport to prove who you are.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any document that allows entry or passage.

   **Usage:** broadly; informal

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Something which enables someone to do or achieve something.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** The tenor's voice was his passport to the international concert circuit.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An identification document used by firefighters, collected when they enter a dangerous area and returned when they leave, to keep track of who is still in the area.
