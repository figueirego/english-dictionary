### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The length of a single cycle of a wave, as measured by the distance between one peak or trough of a wave and the next; it is often designated in physics as λ, and corresponds to the velocity of the wave divided by its frequency.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person's attitude and way of thinking as compared to another person's.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** I think you and I are on a different wavelength.
