### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A type of concordia diagram, used in the context of uranium–lead dating, whose axes are the ratios ²⁰⁶Pb/²³⁸U and ²³⁷Pb/²³⁵U.

   **Example:** The "x" and "y" axes of the Wetherill diagram are the quantities of the respective end (lead) isotopes of the actinium series and uranium series decay chains, each adjusted and expressed as a ratio to the amount remaining of the corresponding start (uranium) isotope.

   **Example:** In the Wetherill diagram, no isotope is found on both axes, allowing the two decay schemes to be evaluated independently.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; geology; natural-sciences
