2. To become entangled.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To place in an embarrassing situation; to ensnare; to make overly complicated.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To be congested in traffic, or to make traffic congested.

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>
