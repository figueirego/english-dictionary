**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Example:** I seek wisdom.

   **Source synonyms:** look for; search for

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To try to acquire or gain; to strive after.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I sought my fortune on the goldfields.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To try to reach or come to (a location).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** When the alarm went off I sought the exit in a panic.
