### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To touch something with one's body.

   **Example:** She accidentally rubbed up against the painted door and got green paint on her blouse.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To touch another person with one's body in a sexually stimulating manner.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To touch a person's body in a friendly manner, seeking attention. (of animals)

   **Example:** Tibbles the cat rubbed up against my leg, asking for a piece of my cheese.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To come into conflict with someone or something.
