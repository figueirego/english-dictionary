### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Usage:** dialectal

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The top part of a boot or shoe, above the sole and welt and in front of the ankle seam, that covers the instep and toes; the front part of an upper.

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Something added to give an old thing a new appearance.

   **Source synonyms:** patch

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Something patched up, pieced together, improvised, or refurbished.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A repeated and often improvised accompaniment, usually consisting of one or two measures, often a single chord or simple chord progression, repeated as necessary, for example, to accommodate dialogue or to anticipate the entrance of a soloist.
