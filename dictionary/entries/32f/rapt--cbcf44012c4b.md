   **Usage:** archaic; not-comparable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

2. Lifted up into the air; transported into heaven.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Very interested, involved in something, absorbed, transfixed; fascinated or engrossed.

   **Usage:** comparable

   **Example:** The children watched in rapt attention as the magician produced object after object from his hat.

   **Usage:** comparable

   **Example:** He was rapt with his exam results.
