## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A belief that property is an absolute right, sometimes to the extreme of considering it to overrule the human rights of others.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Advocacy of proprietary colonies and proprietary government in colonial America.

   **Usage:** historical; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A philosophy and belief that very small businesses are generally more humane than large corporations.

   **Usage:** uncountable
