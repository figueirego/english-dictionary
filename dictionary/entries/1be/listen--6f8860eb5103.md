### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To use one's sense of hearing and auditory cognition in an intentional way; to make deliberate use of one's ears; to pay attention to or wait for a specific sound.

   **Usage:** intransitive except in archaic usage

   **Example:** Please listen carefully as I explain.

   **Example:** I could hear you talking upstairs, but I didn’t really listen to your conversation.

   **Example:** (transitive, archaic) Listen the watchman’s cry upon the wall.

   **Example:** \[with to\] I like to listen to music.

   **Example:** \[with for\] You should listen for the starting gun.

   **Source synonyms:** heed; mind; note; pay attention; attend

   **Source antonyms:** ignore

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To accept advice or obey instruction; to agree or assent.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Listen, the only reason I yelled at you was because I was upset, OK?

   **Example:** Good children listen to their parents.

   **Source synonyms:** mind

   **Source antonyms:** disregard
