## Noun

### Forms

- horse powers — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of a class of machines that used draft horses as a source of power to an output shaft or sheave. Treadmill types and merry-go-round–like types existed.

   **Usage:** dialectal; historical

   **Example:** In that era, they powered their corn sheller and their thresher with a horse power.

   **Source synonyms:** horse engine

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Alternative spelling of horsepower (“unit of power equaling 745 watts or similar figure”).

   **Usage:** alt-of; alternative; archaic

   **Alternative form of:** [horsepower](../13e/horsepower--4f85a3bef18c.md)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; motive effort supplied by one or more horses.

   **Example:** In the 1920s through 1940s, the push for power farming aimed to replace horse power with tractor power on the farm.
