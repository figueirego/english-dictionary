### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. With a lack of formality&#58;
   1. In a casual or informal manner, as with a close acquaintance; especially when such behavior may be considered inappropriate.

      **Example:** He spoke familiarly to the hotel porter.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. With a lack of formality&#58;
   1. In a casual or informal manner, as with a close acquaintance; especially when such behavior may be considered inappropriate.
      1. In an inappropriately sexual or romantic manner.

         **Usage:** dated

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. With a lack of formality&#58;
   1. Colloquially; of common or vernacular speech, as opposed to scholarly language.

      **Usage:** alt-of; colloquial

      **Example:** The giant panda, familiarly known as a "panda bear", is not actually a bear at all.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

      **Alternative form of:** common or vernacular speech; as opposed to scholarly language

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. With knowledge or experience&#58;
   1. In a manner prompting recognition or recollection.

      **Example:** The voice from downstairs sounded familiarly like a foghorn, and right away he knew that Uncle Fred had arrived.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. With knowledge or experience&#58;
   1. In a manner expressing comprehensive or intimate knowledge.

      **Example:** The instructor spoke familiarly about the dangerous conditions on the mountain during the winter.
