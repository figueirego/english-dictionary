### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To walk, move or stand unsteadily or falteringly; threatening to fall.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The baby tottered from the table to the chair.

   **Example:** The old man tottered out of the pub into the street.

   **Example:** The car tottered on the edge of the cliff.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To be on the brink of collapse.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

   **Source synonyms:** topple

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To collect junk or scrap.

   **Usage:** archaic; intransitive
