## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A day which is a term (as for payment of rent), or is a day in a term, as of the sitting of a court.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One of a series of special days, designated by scientists of different nations or stations, for making synoptic magnetic, meteorological, or other physical observations.
