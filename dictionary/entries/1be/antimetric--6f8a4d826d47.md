## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The converse of symmetric in electrical network theory, especially filter theory. A network where the input and output image impedances are the dual, or inverse, of each other as opposed to symmetric networks where they are equal.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** A Butterworth filter with an odd number of elements is symmetric while one with an even number of elements is antimetric.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; electrical; electrical-engineering; electricity; electromagnetism; energy; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In mechanics, relating to a pair of forces, a pair of oscillations or other motion acting in the opposite direction to symmetric.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Going off the rhythm, especially during a transition from one time signature to another.

   **Usage:** especially; not-comparable; sound engineering

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music
