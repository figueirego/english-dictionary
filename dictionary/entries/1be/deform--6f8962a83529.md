### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To change the form of (something), usually thus making it disordered or irregular; to give (something) an abnormal or unusual shape.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** contort; distort

   **Source antonyms:** undeform

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To change the form of (something), usually thus making it disordered or irregular; to give (something) an abnormal or unusual shape.
   1. To alter the shape of (something) by applying a force or stress.

      **Usage:** transitive

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

      **Source synonyms:** contort; distort

      **Source antonyms:** undeform

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To change the look of (something), usually thus making it imperfect or unattractive; to give (something) an abnormal or unusual appearance.

   **Usage:** also; figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** a face deformed by bitterness

   **Source synonyms:** deface; disfigure; distort; mar

   **Usage:** transitive
