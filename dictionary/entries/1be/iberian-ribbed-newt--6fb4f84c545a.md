### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A large (15-30 cm) salamander (Pleurodeles waltl) with orange tubercles along each side, through which its sharp ribs can puncture through its skin as a defence mechanism.

   **Source synonyms:** Spanish ribbed newt; sharp-ribbed newt; sharp-ribbed salamander; gallipato (rare)
