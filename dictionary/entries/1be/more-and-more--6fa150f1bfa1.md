## Adverb · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Progressively more.

   **Usage:** degree

   **Example:** The road gets more and more steep.

   **Example:** He started calling more and more frequently.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In a manner that progressively increases.

   **Example:** The wound hurt more and more as we walked on.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** manner

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Indicates that the statement is becoming progressively more true.

   **Usage:** modal

   **Example:** More and more, children are among the first to take up new technologies.
