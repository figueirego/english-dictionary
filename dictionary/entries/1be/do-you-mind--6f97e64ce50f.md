## Phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used to politely ask someone for a favor.

   **Example:** Do you mind if I turn on the radio?

   **Example:** Do you mind coming into the office for a moment?

   **Example:** I'd like to borrow a plate. Do you mind?

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A rhetorical question used to inform someone that they are being intrusive or annoying.

   **Example:** Do you mind? I'm trying to work here!
