### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The casualty or advantage which formerly fell to the superior when the heir of a deceased vassal failed to renew the investiture, the superior being then entitled to the rent of the feu.

   **Usage:** countable; historical; uncountable; Scots law

   **Source synonyms:** nonentres
