### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any type of money that is permitted to be circulated as a medium of exchange as directed by the laws of a nation.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** banking; business; finance

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any type of money which is recognized in a community as a legal medium of exchange.
