### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. All the characteristics, conventions and use of colour and language of a house (company, institution, website, etc.) that serve to make it self-consistent and (in some cases also) distinct from others.

   **Example:** Don't take it personally if your editor imposes the Oxford comma — it's merely an aspect of house style.
