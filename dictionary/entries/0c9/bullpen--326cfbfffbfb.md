## Noun

### Forms

- bullpens — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An enclosed area used to hold bulls.

   **Example:** Magnus and Big Dick are over in the bullpen, ready for tonight's rodeo.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An enclosed area for pitchers to warm up in during a game.

   **Example:** After that hit, there is activity in the bullpen.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; baseball; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The relief pitchers of a team collectively.

   **Example:** Their bullpen is worn out after yesterday's doubleheader where both games went 12 innings.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; baseball; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A place for someone or something to get ready.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** If this team of consultants fails, I have another warming up in the bullpen.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An open seating arrangement where project teams can communicate openly with minimal effort.

   **Example:** We have our sales team in a bullpen setup to keep their energy flowing.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A military prison or its enclosing stockade.

   **Usage:** US; slang

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war
