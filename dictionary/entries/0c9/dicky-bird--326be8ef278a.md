### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A small bird.

   **Usage:** familiar

   **Example:** The Swan and the Dickey-bird&#58; A Fable.

   **Example:** Simple Simon went a hunting, He thought he could not fail, For to catch a Dickie Bird, For he’d got a little salt to put upon his tail.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A word; a brief chat.

   **Usage:** UK; informal

   **Example:** We've not heard a dicky-bird about anything relating to his birthday.

   **Source context (verbatim):** from Cockney rhyming slang; used especially in negative constructions

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A small thing.

   **Usage:** UK; informal

   **Example:** I peeked into the cellar but there was nothing there. Not a dicky-bird.
