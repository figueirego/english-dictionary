### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An explosion or sudden report made by the near-instantaneous decomposition or combustion of unstable substances. Specifically, combustion that spreads supersonically via shock compression.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** the detonation of gun cotton

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An explosion or sudden report made by the near-instantaneous decomposition or combustion of unstable substances. Specifically, combustion that spreads supersonically via shock compression.
   1. Any sudden explosion made by an exothermic process (whether chemical or not) that produces a supersonic shock wave.

      **Usage:** broadly; countable; uncountable

      **Example:** a nuclear detonation

      **Example:** carbon detonation

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Engine knocking, a type of improper combustion in gasoline piston engines.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; mechanical; mechanical-engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences
