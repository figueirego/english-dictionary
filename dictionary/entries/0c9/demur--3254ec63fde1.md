### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Chiefly followed by to, and sometimes by at or on&#58; to object or be reluctant; to balk, to take exception.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** I demur to that statement.

   **Example:** The personnel demurred at the management’s new scheme.

   **Source synonyms:** oppose; scruple

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To submit a demurrer (“motion by a party to a legal action for the immediate or summary judgment of the court on the question of whether, assuming the truth of the matter alleged by the opposite party, it is sufficient in law to sustain the action or defence, and hence whether the party bringing the motion is required to answer or proceed further”).

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To endure, to last.

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To linger, to tarry.

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To remain, to stay.

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete

   **Source synonyms:** abide

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To suspend judgment or proceedings because of a difficulty or doubt; to put off the conclusion or determination of a matter; to delay, to hesitate, to pause.

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To have doubts; to be doubtful.

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete; rare

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Followed by upon&#58; to be captivated or fixated; to dwell on, to linger.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive; obsolete

<a id="r1-s9"></a>
