### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To try not to meet or communicate with (a person); to shun.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To stay out of the way of (something harmful).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I avoided the slap easily.

   **Example:** One town was flooded from the storm, while the other town avoided the storm.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To keep away from; to keep clear of; to stay away from.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I try to avoid the company of gamblers.

**Source sense R1.S4**
