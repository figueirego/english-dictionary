### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A rebirth of a soul, in a physical life form, such as a body.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** He is believed to be the reincarnation of Jesus.

   **Example:** Do you believe in reincarnation?

   **Source synonyms:** metempsychosis; transmigration\[Appendix&#58;Glossary

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A fresh embodiment.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A new, considerably improved, version.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** a reincarnation of an old video game
