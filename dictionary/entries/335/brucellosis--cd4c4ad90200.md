### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Infection by a bacterium of the genus Brucella, which is carried by ruminants and other mammals, with symptoms including recurring fevers, sweating, weakness, anorexia, headaches, depression, and generalized aches and pains.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
