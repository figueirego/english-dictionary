## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Acronym of jet-assisted takeoff, a method of using booster rockets to allow heavily-loaded aircraft to lift off more quickly and use less runway length for takeoff.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; acronym; alt-of; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** RATO (UK)

   **Alternative form of:** jet-assisted takeoff

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One of the rockets used for the above purpose.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** An enduring urban legend claims that someone in Arizona strapped a JATO to their car and crashed it into a cliff.

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** RATO; RATOG (Britain)
