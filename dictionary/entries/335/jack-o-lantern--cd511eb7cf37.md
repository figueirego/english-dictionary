### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A carved pumpkin whose top and stem have been cut out and interior removed, leaving a hollow shell that is then decorated to represent a face, illuminated from within by a candle. It is a symbol of Halloween.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A carved pumpkin whose top and stem have been cut out and interior removed, leaving a hollow shell that is then decorated to represent a face, illuminated from within by a candle. It is a symbol of Halloween.
   1. A similar item made from a turnip.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A will o' the wisp.

   **Usage:** archaic

   **Source synonyms:** ignis fatuus; hinkypunk; Joan the wad; hobby lantern; peg-a-lantern; Will the Smith; Pinket; Will o' the Wikes; Jack-with-a-lantern
