### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The quality that renders something desirable or valuable; worth.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** There is tremendous value in a good education.

   **Example:** An abacus is of little value when you have an electronic calculator.

   **Source synonyms:** worth

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The degree of importance given to something.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** The value of my children's happiness is second only to that of my wife.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. That which is valued or highly esteemed, such as one's morals, morality, or belief system.

   **Usage:** countable; often; plural; uncountable

   **Example:** He does not share his parents' values.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The amount (of money or goods or services) that is considered to be a fair equivalent for something else.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The value of the stolen painting is estimated to be around four million pounds.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The relative duration of a musical note.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The value of a crotchet is twice that of a quaver.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The relative darkness or lightness of a color in (a specific area of) a painting etc.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** art; arts

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Any definite numerical quantity or other mathematical object, determined by being measured, computed, or otherwise defined.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The exact value of pi cannot be represented in decimal notation.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics; sciences

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Precise meaning; import.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** the value of a word; the value of a legal instrument

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. The valuable ingredients to be obtained by treating a mass or compound; specifically, the precious metals contained in rock, gravel, etc.

   **Usage:** countable; in-plural; uncountable

   **Example:** The vein carries good values.
