## Adverb

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In a very close one-on-one situation.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In the same level as someone else.

   **Usage:** figuratively; not-comparable; usually; product features

   **Example:** comparing head to head

   **Source context (verbatim):** of skill

   **Source synonyms:** toe to toe

   **Source antonyms:** apples to oranges

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Against, in direct opposition.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** competing head to head

   **Source synonyms:** toe to toe
