## Verb

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To grasp or take hold of (an object) suddenly, forcibly, or tightly.

   **Example:** The child seized upon the cat's tail as soon as it was within reach.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To take up, embrace, enact, or turn eagerly to (a plan, idea, ideology, cause, practice, method, etc.); to grasp, understand, and accept quickly; to adopt wholeheartedly or vigorously.

   **Example:** I heard her ideas and seized upon them immediately.

   **Example:** The committee seized upon the new plan at once.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To take possession of or claim (an idea, person, event, etc.) as one's own; to assimilate, absorb, annex, co-opt.

   **Example:** After the controversy of her public statement, she has been the darling of right-wing groups who have seized upon her as the poster child of their cause.
