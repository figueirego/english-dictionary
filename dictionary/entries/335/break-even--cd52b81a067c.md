### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To neither gain nor lose a quantity of something, (especially) money.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** After an entire night playing poker, he nearly broke even.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To stay the same; to neither advance nor regress.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** It's a lot of work just to break even and keep the weeds down.
