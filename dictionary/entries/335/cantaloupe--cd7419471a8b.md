### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Cucumis melo Cantalupensis Group, a cultivar group melon with sweet aromatic orange flesh, including two main types&#58;
   1. Cantalupensis Group sensu stricto, with non- or slightly netted rind, also known as true cantaloupe or European cantaloupe, found in the Middle East and Europe.

      **Usage:** Ireland; UK; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Cucumis melo Cantalupensis Group, a cultivar group melon with sweet aromatic orange flesh, including two main types&#58;
   1. Reticulatus Group (now merged into Cantalupensis Group), with strongly netted rind, also known as American cantaloupe, muskmelon or rockmelon.

      **Usage:** Australia; Canada; US; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An orange colour, like that of cantaloupe flesh.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
