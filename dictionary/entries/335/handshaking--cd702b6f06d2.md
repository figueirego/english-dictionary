## Noun · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A greeting by clasping hands.

   **Example:** A round of handshakings followed the introductions.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A step in a protocol in which information is exchanged between computing processor devices; often as part of the initiation process for communications.

   **Example:** I waited for the modems to finish their handshaking so I could start transferring the file.
