### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A condemned prisoner walking to a death chamber or other place of execution.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Someone who is soon to die.

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

   **Usage:** figuratively
