### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A female relative of one's generation, separated by one degree of marriage&#58;
   1. The sister of one's spouse.

      **Example:** Though they are not twins, my sister-in-law resembles my wife in almost every way.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A female relative of one's generation, separated by one degree of marriage&#58;
   1. The wife of one's sibling.

      **Example:** My sister-in-law and my brother met while they were both on vacation in Jamaica.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Co-sister-in-law&#58; The wife of one's sibling-in-law.
   1. The wife of the sibling of one's spouse.

      **Usage:** uncommon

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Co-sister-in-law&#58; The wife of one's sibling-in-law.
   1. The sister of the spouse of one's sibling.

      **Usage:** uncommon
