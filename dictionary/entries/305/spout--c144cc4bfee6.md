## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /spʌʊt/ — source labels: Canada

### Forms

- spouts — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A tube or lip through which liquid or steam is poured or discharged.

   **Example:** I dropped my china teapot, and its spout broke.

   **Example:** I put a spout in the maple tree to collect its sap

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A waterspout (“channel through which water is discharged, especially from the gutters of a roof”).

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A stream or discharge of liquid, typically with some degree of force.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A stream of water that falls from higher to lower; a (typically thin) waterfall.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A similar stream or fall of earth, rock, etc.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A waterspout (“whirlwind or tornado that forms over water”).

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The mixture of air and water thrown up from the blowhole of a whale.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A hollow stump formed when a tree branch breaks off.

   **Usage:** Australia

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To gush forth in a jet or stream

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Water spouts from a hole.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To eject water or liquid in a jet.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** The whale spouted.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To speak tediously or pompously.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To utter magniloquently; to recite in an oratorical or pompous manner.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To pawn; to pledge.

   **Usage:** dated; slang; transitive

   **Example:** to spout a watch
