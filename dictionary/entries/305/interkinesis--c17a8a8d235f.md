### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The second stage of interphase, during which there is no DNA replication, but the spindles of the first meiotic division disassemble and the microtubules reassemble into two new spindles for the second meiotic division.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences
