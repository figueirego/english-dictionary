### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of accepting; the receiving of something offered, with acquiescence, approbation, or satisfaction; especially, favourable reception; approval.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** the acceptance of a gift, office, doctrine, etc.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An instance of that act.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Belief in something; agreement, assent.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The state of being accepted.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The usual or accepted meaning of a word or expression.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. An assent and engagement by the person on whom a bill of exchange is drawn, to pay it when due according to the terms of the acceptance; the bill of exchange itself when accepted.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. An agreeing to the action, proposals, or terms of another by some act which results in the conclusion of a legally binding contract; the reception or taking of a thing bought as that for which it was bought, or as that agreed to be delivered, or the taking of possession of a thing as owner.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. The act of an authorized representative of the government by which the government assents to ownership of existing and identified supplies, or approves specific services rendered, as partial or complete performance of a contract.

   **Usage:** US; countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A list of horses accepted as starters in a race.

   **Usage:** Australia; New-Zealand; countable; plural; plural-only; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; horse-racing; horseracing; horses; lifestyle; pets; racing; sports

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. Synonym of etendue.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; natural-sciences; optics; physical-sciences; physics
