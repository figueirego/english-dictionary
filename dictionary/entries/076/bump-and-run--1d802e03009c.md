## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A technique for passing another vehicle, mainly used in stock car and touring car racing, in which the trailing car intentionally strikes the rear bumper of the car in front, forcing the driver to slow down or adjust the steering.

   **Usage:** attributive; often; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; motor-racing; racing; sports

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A strategy used by defensive backs, in which a defender lined up directly in front of a wide receiver attempts to impede him with arms, hands, or the entire body and disrupt his intended route.

   **Usage:** attributive; often; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** American-football; ball-games; football; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports
