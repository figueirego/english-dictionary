### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A sweetened spreadable jam or jelly made of any of a variety of fruits, such as strawberry, peach, or apple.

   **Usage:** countable; in-plural; often; uncountable

   **Example:** I like blueberry preserves even better than grape jam on buttered toast.

   **Source synonyms:** jam; jelly; conserve; marmalade

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Reservation, nature preserve.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An exclusive, often protected, domain or area of activity.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Kids regard their tree houses as their own preserve.

   **Source synonyms:** territory; turf; prerogative

## Verb · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /pɹəˈzɜːv/ — source labels: Received-Pronunciation
- IPA: /pɹəˈzɝv/ — source labels: General-American

### Forms

- preserves — present; singular; third-person
- preserving — participle; present
- preserved — participle; past
- preserved — past
- præserve — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To protect; to keep from harm or injury.

   **Example:** Let's pray that we'd be preserved from danger.

   **Example:** Every people has the right to preserve its identity and culture.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To save from decay by the use of some preservative substance, such as sugar or salt; to season and prepare (fruits, meat, etc.) for storage.

   **Example:** to preserve peaches or grapes

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To maintain throughout; to keep intact.

   **Example:** to preserve appearances; to preserve silence

   **Example:** I preserved a composed manner throughout the interrogation.
