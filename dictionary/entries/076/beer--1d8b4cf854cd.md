### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An alcoholic drink fermented from starch material, commonly barley malt; often with hops or some other substance (like gruit) to impart a bitter flavor.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Beer is brewed all over the world.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A fermented extract of the roots and other parts of various plants, as spruce, ginger, sassafras, etc.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A solution produced by steeping plant materials in water or another fluid.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A glass, bottle, or can of any of the above beverages.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** I bought a few beers from the shop for the party.

   **Example:** Can I buy you a beer?

   **Example:** I'd like two beers and a glass of white wine.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A variety of the above beverages.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** Pilsner is one of the most commonly served beers in Europe.

   **Example:** I haven't tried this beer before.

   **Example:** I had two beers yesterday at the restaurant.
