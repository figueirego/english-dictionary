2. A failed or reverse climax
   1. An unsatisfying resolution to a narrative, usually owing to a deus ex machina or similarly trivial resolution of the main conflict.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; narratology; sciences

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A failed or reverse climax

      **Usage:** countable; rhetoric; uncountable
