### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act or process of qualifying for a position, achievement etc.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Qualification for this organization is extraordinarily difficult.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An ability or attribute that aids someone's chances of qualifying for something; specifically, completed professional training.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** What are your qualifications for this job?

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A certificate, diploma, or degree awarded after successful completion of a course, training, or exam.

   **Usage:** UK; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A clause or condition which qualifies something; a modification, a limitation.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** I accept your offer, but with the following qualification.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A quality or attribute.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable
