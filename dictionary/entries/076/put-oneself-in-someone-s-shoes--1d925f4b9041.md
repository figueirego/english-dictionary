### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To try to look at a situation from a different point of view; as if one were the other person; to empathise.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** If you could just put yourself in his shoes for a moment, perhaps you would understand why it is not as easy as you seem to think.
