### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A projecting lamina of bone on the inferior surface of the petrous portion of the temporal bone that is continuous with the tympanic plate and surrounds the base of the styloid process.

   **Usage:** archaic

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An outpouching of peritoneum through the inguinal canal, from which the serous membrane (tunica vaginalis) covering the testicle is derived.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** embryology; medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
