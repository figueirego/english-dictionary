### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To provide informational feedback to.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** His employees fed back a lot more than he wanted.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To convey by means of specialized communications channel.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Customers fed back their complaints and some praise.
