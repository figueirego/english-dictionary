**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Usage:** dated

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To turn another way; to divert.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** divert; steer; veer

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To corrupt; to cause to be untrue; corrupted or otherwise impure

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** How could stopping someone from killing himself or herself "pervert the course of justice"?

   **Source synonyms:** corrupt; lead astray; seduce

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To misapply, misuse, use for a nefarious purpose

   **Example:** He has perverted his talents to dishonest gain.

   **Source synonyms:** misapply; misuse

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. to misinterpret designedly.

   **Example:** pervert one's words

   **Source synonyms:** bias; distort; skew; twist

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To become perverted; to take the wrong course.

   **Usage:** intransitive
