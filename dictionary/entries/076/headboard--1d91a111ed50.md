## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A vertical panel, either plain or upholstered, attached to the head of a bed.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A panel, usually of metal, attached to the head of a fore-and-aft sail for additional strength.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A board on the front of a train, carrying the train's name or that of the service it is on.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** rail-transport; railways; transport

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**
