### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To cause (something) to stick out or swell; to extrude; also, to cause (someone or something) to be covered in swellings.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To make (a design on a coin, an ornament on an object, etc.) stand out from a surface.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To represent (a subject) on an object in relief; also, of a design or subject&#58; to stand out on (an object) in relief.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To decorate or mark (something) with a design or symbol in relief.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The papers weren’t official until the seal had been embossed on them.

   **Example:** The title was embossed in gold lettering on the cover.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To decorate (something) with bosses (“ornamental convex protuberances”); to boss; hence, to decorate (something) richly.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To cause (something) to be prominent or stand out.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To make (speech, etc.) unduly bombastic or grand.

   **Usage:** figuratively; obsolete; transitive
