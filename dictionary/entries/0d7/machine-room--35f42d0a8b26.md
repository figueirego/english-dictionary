## Noun

### Forms

- machine rooms — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A limited-access room in a larger building dedicated to HVAC equipment and other machinery.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A dedicated room for computers and related data processing equipment, often having a raised floor, abundant and dedicated air conditioning, and specialized power supply and fire suppression systems.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A room for other types of machinery.
