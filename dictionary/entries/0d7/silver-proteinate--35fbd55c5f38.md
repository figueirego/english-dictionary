## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A substance made of silver and albumin, used in carbohydrate electron microscopy and historically to treat gonorrhea before the discovery of antibiotics.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** argentoproteinum (archaic); protargol
