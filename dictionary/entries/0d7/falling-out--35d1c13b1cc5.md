### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A rift between people or groups, often following a disagreement or quarrel

   **Usage:** countable; idiomatic; uncountable

   **Example:** Since the falling out with his neighbor, they have kept their distance.

**Source sense R1.S2**
