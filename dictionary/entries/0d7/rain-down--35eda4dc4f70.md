## Verb

### Forms

- rains down — present; singular; third-person

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To fall from the sky as, or like, rain.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** I'm soaking wet because it rained down on me.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To let fall as, or like, rain.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** In the latest supernatural incident, the sky rained down frogs.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To strike (literally or figuratively) many times and/or very intensely.

   **Usage:** intransitive; with on or upon

   **Example:** My wrath will rain down upon you.

   **Example:** The jeers rained down on me throughout my middle school years.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To appear suddenly or inexplicably in large numbers.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive
