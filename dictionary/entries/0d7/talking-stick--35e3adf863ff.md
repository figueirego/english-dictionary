### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A ceremonial stick that grants the holder the right to speak in a group discussion.

   **Source synonyms:** speaker's staff

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A piece of wood which is used by aboriginal Australians as part of or a form of communication either by being carved or by another method.

   **Usage:** Australia

   **Source synonyms:** message stick; stick letter
