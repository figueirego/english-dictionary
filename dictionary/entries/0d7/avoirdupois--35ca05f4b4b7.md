### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The official system of weights used in the UK between 1856 and 1963. It had been the customary system in London since 1300.

   **Usage:** historical; uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The official system of weights used in the USA between 1866 and 1959.

   **Usage:** historical; uncountable; usually
