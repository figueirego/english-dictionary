### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Tea leaves which have been "fermented" (actually, oxidized) before drying to enhance their flavour (cf. green tea).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** cha; char; Rosie (Cockney; slang); Rosie Lee (Cockney; slang); Rosy (Cockney; slang); Rosy Lee (Cockney; slang); tay; tea; wet and warm; Chinese tea

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A cup of tea served without milk.

   **Usage:** UK; countable; uncountable
