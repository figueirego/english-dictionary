## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A diminutive of the female given name Cassandra of medieval origin, later also used for Cassidy, Cassiopeia, and Catherine with its variant forms.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A surname originating as a matronymic.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A male given name transferred from the surname.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A diminutive of the male given name Cassius.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A number of places&#58;
   1. A township and unincorporated community therein, in Sullivan County, Indiana, United States.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A number of places&#58;
   1. A census-designated place in Pocahontas County, West Virginia, United States.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A number of places&#58;
   1. A number of other townships in the United States, listed under Cass Township.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A number of places&#58;
   1. A locality in Selwyn District, Canterbury, New Zealand, named after Thomas Cass.
