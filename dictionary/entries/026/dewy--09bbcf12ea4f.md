**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Covered by dew.

   **Example:** The dewy grass was too slick for football.

   **Source synonyms:** bedewed; rory; bedewed; dewy; roric; rorid; rory (obsolete); roscid

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Having the quality of bearing droplets of water.

   **Example:** In the dewy fog, it was cold and damp.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>
