### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The part of a baseball field which is beyond the infield and to the left of a person standing on home plate and facing the pitcher.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; baseball; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; baseball; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

3. An unexpected, bizarre, or unwatched source (especially in the phrases out of left field and from left field).

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

   **Example:** Some of her comments really came from left field. I have no idea what she was thinking.
