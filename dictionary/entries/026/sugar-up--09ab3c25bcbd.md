### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To ingest a large amount of sugar, e.g. in candy.

   **Usage:** informal; intransitive

   **Example:** The kids sugared up good last night.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To add sugar to (something).

   **Usage:** informal; transitive

   **Example:** I sugared up your coffee.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To give sugary foods to others to eat.

   **Usage:** informal; transitive

   **Example:** Please don't sugar up the kids tonight.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To flirt with and give signals of romantic intent for the purposes of seduction

   **Usage:** informal; transitive

   **Example:** She's been sugaring him up for months.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To make (something) more attractive; to touch up to add appeal.

   **Usage:** informal; transitive

   **Example:** I sugared up my stories to get those guys on my side.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Synonym of suck up (to adulate or flatter).

   **Usage:** informal; intransitive

   **Example:** I don't wanna have to sugar up to Mr. Snyder.

   **Source synonyms:** suck up (synonym; synonym-of)
