## Prep\_phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. At any rate&#58; anyhow, regardless (emphasizing that a point doesn't matter or hardly matters).

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** The product is duly prohibited by law, but in any event, it is still available on the black market.

   **Source synonyms:** at all events; in any case; anyway; nonetheless; nevertheless; come what may

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. At any rate&#58; by any measure, any way one slices it, in every case, no matter what (emphasizing that a point matters so much that there is no case in which it does not matter).

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** You can grumble all you like, but in any event, you'd better be there at the appointed time.

   **Source synonyms:** at all events; in any case; no matter what

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see in, any, event.

   **Example:** Kinetics might be described as a series of events, and in any event, there is some instant at which some change begins.
