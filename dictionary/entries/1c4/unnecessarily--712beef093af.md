## Adverb

### Forms

- more unnecessarily — comparative
- most unnecessarily — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In an unnecessary way; not by necessity.

   **Example:** He unnecessarily repeated much of what others had covered.

   **Source synonyms:** needlessly; pointlessly; uselessly; for no reason

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To an extent beyond what is needed.

   **Example:** The food provided was unnecessarily generous, especially for an event meant to raise money for the hungry.

   **Source synonyms:** unduly
