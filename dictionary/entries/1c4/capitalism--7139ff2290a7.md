### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** economics; government; politics; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

      **Usage:** Marxism; countable; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** economics; government; politics; sciences
