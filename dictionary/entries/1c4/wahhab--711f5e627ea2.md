### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. (in Johan Galtung's writing, attested as the plural Wahhabs) A follower of the Arabian religious movement associated with Muhammad ibn Abd al-Wahhab.

   **Extractor diagnostics (not usage labels):** no-gloss

**Supplemental definition (evidence reviewed; source gap preserved):** The original count-noun record has no restrictive quotation, domain or etymology. Newly captured authorial prose by Johan Galtung uses the exact plural Wahhabs in grammatical parallel with Puritans for people carrying a religious message in Arabia; this is neither a surname genitive nor a truncation of Wahhabis. The independent bilingual Aceh encyclopedia's English paragraph expressly associates its plural Wahhabs with Muhammad ibn Abdul Wahab ibn Sulaiman in Saudi Arabia. Taken together, the sources support a narrowly attributed follower reading, rather than deriving a noun mechanically from Wahhabi or treating ambiguous singular names as proof. The supplement is explicitly limited to the attested plural use and does not claim that the singular was independently found, that this spelling is standard, or that the unknown original intended sense has been recovered.
