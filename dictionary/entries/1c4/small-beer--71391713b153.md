### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Beer with a low alcoholic content, usually between 0.5% and 2.8%.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Something that is of relatively little importance.

   **Usage:** UK; figuratively; uncountable

   **Example:** The income from standard widgets is small beer compared to the income from the gold-plated ones.

   **Source synonyms:** peanuts; small potatoes; bagatelle; bag of shells; bric-a-brac; dicky (British; idiomatic); dicky-bird (British; idiomatic); fico (archaic); fig; inconsequential; flamfew (Scotland; obsolete); flyspeck; minuity; niff-naff; picayune (US); scuddick (obsolete); sop; ticky-tack; toy; trick; trifle; triviality; whiffle; white chip
