### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A charge taken on bets, as by a bookie or gambling establishment.

   **Usage:** slang; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** cut; house take; house cut; house advantage; juice; overround; rake; rake-off; vig

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The interest on a loan of money, especially for loans made by a usurer or loan shark.

   **Usage:** slang; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** vig; rake off; cut

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An amount owed on account of or payment of a bookie's charge or of interest.

   **Usage:** countable; slang

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A commission or similar extra charge.

   **Usage:** countable; slang; uncountable
