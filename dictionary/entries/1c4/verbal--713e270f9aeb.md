### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A verb form which does not function as a predicate, or a word derived from a verb. In English, infinitives, participles and gerunds are verbals.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** grammar; human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** non-finite verb

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. A spoken confession given to police.

   **Usage:** Ireland; UK; countable

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. Talk; speech, especially banter or scolding.

   **Usage:** Ireland; UK; colloquial; uncountable
