### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Expressing that, having begun something (that involves significant risk or effort), one intends to see it to completion rather than stopping short.

   **Usage:** UK

   **Example:** Having tripped the bank's alarms, he simply uttered&#58; "in for a penny, in for a pound", and continued on.
