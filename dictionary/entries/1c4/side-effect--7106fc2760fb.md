### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An unintended consequence, or secondary result, of any action, in addition to the main or intended consequence of that action.

   **Source synonyms:** by-product

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An unintended consequence, or secondary result, of any action, in addition to the main or intended consequence of that action.
   1. An unintended consequence of a drug or therapy; usually an adverse effect, and not a beneficial one.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

      **Source synonyms:** by-product

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An unintended consequence, or secondary result, of any action, in addition to the main or intended consequence of that action.
   1. A change a in state outside of the local environment of the function being called.

      **Example:** The Eiffel programming language is somewhat purist in insisting that functions have no side effects.
