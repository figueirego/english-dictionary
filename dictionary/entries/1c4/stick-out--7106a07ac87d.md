### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To protrude.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He stuck out his tongue at me.

   **Source synonyms:** obtrude

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To protrude; to extend beyond.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** His tongue was sticking out.

   **Example:** I can fit the pipe in my car, but one end will stick out from the back.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To be prominent, noticeable, or obtrusive.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** The one red wall really sticks out among all the creamy ones.

   **Source synonyms:** stand out; obtrude; outstand; stand out (idiomatic); stick out (idiomatic); stick out like a sore thumb

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To persist until the end; stick it out.

   **Source synonyms:** abide (archaic); aby (obsolete); astand (obsolete); endure; go on; lie; prevail; hold on; last; live; persist; remain; stick; stick it out; stick out
