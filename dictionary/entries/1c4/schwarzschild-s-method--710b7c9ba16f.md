### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A method to develop triaxial equilibrium kinematic models of actual galaxies (without knowledge of the motions of the component elements of the actual galaxies) using a grid and assigning masses and orbits, simulating gravitational evolution with the resulting estimated distributions, to compare with the actual galaxy, until a model results in similarity with the actual galaxy.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astronomy; astrophysics; natural-sciences
