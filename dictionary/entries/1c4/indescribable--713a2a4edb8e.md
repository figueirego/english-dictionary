### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Impossible (or very difficult) to describe.

   **Example:** He proved it with indescribable mathematics.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Exceeding all description.

   **Example:** Our hotel had an indescribable view of the Bay of Naples.
