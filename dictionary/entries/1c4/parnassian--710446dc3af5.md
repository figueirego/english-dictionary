## Adjective · Record 1

### Forms

- more Parnassian — comparative
- most Parnassian — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of or relating to Parnassus, as the source of literary (especially poetic) inspiration; (hence) of or belonging to poetry.

   **Source synonyms:** poetic

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of or relating to a style of poetry or language which can only be created by poets, but not in the language of inspiration.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** communications; journalism; literature; media; poetry; publishing; writing

   **Source context (verbatim):** from Gerard Manley Hopkins' writings

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of or relating to the Parnassianism movement of French poetry in the years 1850 to 1900, whose adherents rejected Romanticism and instead favored classicism with its formal structure and emotional detachment.

   **Usage:** historical

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** literature; media; publishing
