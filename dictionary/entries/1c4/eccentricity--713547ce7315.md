### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The quality of being eccentric or odd; any eccentric behaviour.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The ratio, constant for any particular conic section, of the distance of a point from the focus to its distance from the directrix.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** For an ellipse, the eccentricity is the ratio of the distance from the center to a focus divided by the length of the semi-major axis.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The eccentricity of the conic section (usually an ellipse) defined by the orbit of a given object around a reference object (such as that of a planet around the sun).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astronomy; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The farthest distance from a vertex to any other vertex.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** graph-theory; mathematics; sciences
