### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any semi-aquatic, carnivorous mammal in the Mustelinae subfamily, similar to weasels, with dark fur, native to Europe and America, of which two species in different genera are extant&#58; the American mink (Neogale vison) and the European mink (Mustela lutreola).

   **Usage:** plural mink or minks

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The fur or pelt of a mink, used to make apparel.

   **Usage:** plural mink

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An article of clothing made of mink.

   **Usage:** plural minks

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A person with poor personal hygiene; a smelly person.

   **Usage:** Scotland; derogatory; slang; plural minks
