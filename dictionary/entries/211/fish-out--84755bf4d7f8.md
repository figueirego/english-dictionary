## Verb

### Forms

- fishes out — present; singular; third-person

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To catch all or nearly all the fish in (a given body of water), so that few or none remain.

   **Example:** Settlers came and soon fished out the lake.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To pick up or pull something or someone from (a body of water).

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To search for by rummaging, and then extract (an item from e.g. a container); to obtain.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I fished out my keys from the bottom of my bag.

   **Example:** I fished out some information from some old copies of the local gazette.
