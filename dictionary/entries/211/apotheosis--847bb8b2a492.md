### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The fact or action of becoming or making into a god; deification.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** deification; theogenesis (Mormonism); exaltation

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Glorification, exaltation; crediting someone or something with extraordinary power or status.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The turn of the century saw the apotheosis of digital technology.

   **Source synonyms:** exaltation; glorification

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A glorified example or ideal; the apex or pinnacle (of a concept or belief).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** apex; paragon

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The best moment or highest point in the development of something, for example of a life or career; the apex, culmination, or climax (of a development).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The apotheosis of her career was her appointment as chairman.

   **Source synonyms:** apex; climax; culmination; peak; pinnacle

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Release from earthly life, ascension to heaven; death.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** death

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The latent entity that mediates between a person's psyche and their thoughts. The id, ego and superego in Freudian Psychology are examples of this.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; psychology; sciences
