### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The age at which the rights and privileges of an adult are legally granted or recognized; i.e.; the age where a person ceases to be a minor legally and becomes a legal adult.

   **Example:** In Indiana the age of majority is 18 years.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law
