### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The most primitive part of the brain; the brain stem.

   **Usage:** informal

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any part of a person's psyche or personality dominated by instinct or impulse rather than rational thought.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Source synonyms:** id; reptilian brain

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any part of a person's mind concerned with automatic, repetitive tasks rather than emotion.

   **Usage:** broadly
