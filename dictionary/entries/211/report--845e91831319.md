### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To relate details of (an event or incident); to recount, describe (something).

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To repeat (something one has heard), to retell; to pass on, convey (a message, information etc.).

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To take oneself (to someone or something) for guidance or support; to appeal.

   **Usage:** obsolete; reflexive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To notify someone of (particular intelligence, suspicions, illegality, misconduct etc.); to make notification to relevant authorities; to submit a formal report of.

   **Usage:** formal; transitive

   **Example:** For insurance reasons, I had to report the theft to the local police station.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To make a formal statement, especially of complaint, about (someone).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** If you do that again I'll report you to the boss.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To show up or appear at an appointed time; to present oneself.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To write news reports (for); to cover as a journalist or reporter.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** Andrew Marr reports now on more in-fighting at Westminster.

   **Example:** Every newspaper reported the war.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To be accountable to or subordinate to (someone) in a hierarchy; to receive orders from (someone); to give official updates to (someone who is above oneself in a hierarchy).

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The financial director reports to the CEO.

   **Example:** Now that I've been promoted, I report to Benjamin, whom I loathe.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To return or present as the result of an examination or consideration of any matter officially referred.

   **Usage:** dated

   **Example:** The committee reported the bill with amendments, or reported a new bill, or reported the results of an inquiry.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; politics

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. To take minutes of (a speech, the doings of a public body, etc.); to write down from the lips of a speaker.

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. To refer.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s12"></a>

**Source sense R1.S12**

12. To return or repeat, as sound; to echo.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive; obsolete; rare

<a id="r1-s13"></a>

**Source sense R1.S13**

13. Of a ship, to return to a port.

   **Example:** Our ship leaves port on Monday and will re-port on Friday.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport
