### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To tilt back and forth on an edge.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** He teetered on the brink of the precipice.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To totter (move unsteadily).

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To be indecisive; to hesitate.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** We teetered on the fence about buying getaway tickets and missed the opportunity.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To be close to becoming a typically negative situation.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** Despite appearances, the firm was teetering on the edge of bankruptcy.
