### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Containing sufficient poison to be dangerous to touch or ingest.

   **Example:** While highly poisonous to dogs, this substance is completely harmless if ingested by humans.

   **Source synonyms:** poisoned; toxic; venomous; attery (archaic; dialectal)

   **Source antonyms:** nonpoisonous; unpoisonous

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of an animal such as a snake or spider, or parts of its body&#58; producing a toxin intended for defensive or offensive use which is usually injected into an enemy or prey by biting or stinging; hence, of a bite or sting&#58; injecting poison.

   **Usage:** proscribed; sometimes

   **Example:** Poisonous snakes should only be handled by experienced professionals.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Negative, harmful.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Source synonyms:** toxic
