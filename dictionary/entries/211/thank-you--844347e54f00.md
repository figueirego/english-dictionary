### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An expression of gratitude or politeness in response to something done or given.

   **Example:** “Thank you!” said the girl after her mom gave her a gift.

   **Example:** “And thank you for being so sweet,” replied her mom.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used as a polite affirmative to accept an offer.

   **Usage:** often

   **Example:** —May I help you? —Yes, thank you.

   **Example:** —May I help you? —Thank you.

   **Source context (verbatim):** with yes

   **Source synonyms:** please

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A polite dismissal; usually used in professional contexts.
