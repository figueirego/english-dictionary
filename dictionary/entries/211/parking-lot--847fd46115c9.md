## Noun

### Forms

- parking lots — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A designated area, usually outdoors and divided into marked spaces, intended for the temporary parking of motor vehicles.

   **Usage:** Canada; Malaysia; Philippines; US

   **Example:** Should we park in that parking lot, or in the garage up the street?

   **Source synonyms:** parking area; car park; carpark

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A road blocked by stop-start traffic.

   **Usage:** US; figuratively; humorous

   **Example:** The 101 was a parking lot again today.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A holding area in which to set aside some question or concern for likely future consideration.

   **Usage:** especially; figuratively

   **Example:** Let's put this question in the parking lot for now, and we'll follow up on it later this week.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; management

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A place in a state of complete destruction, especially one achieved through military means.

   **Usage:** US; figuratively
