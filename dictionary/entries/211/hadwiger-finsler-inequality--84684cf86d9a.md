**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A result on the geometry of triangles in the Euclidean plane, stating that, if a triangle in the plane has side lengths a, b and c and area T, then a²+b²+c²≥(a-b)²+(b-c)²+(c-a)²+4√3T.
