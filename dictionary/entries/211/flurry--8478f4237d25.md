### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A light, brief snowfall; a shower of snow.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A sudden and brief blast or gust; a light, temporary breeze.

   **Example:** a flurry of wind

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A shower of dust, leaves etc. brought on by a sudden gust of wind.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any sudden activity; a stir.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** The day before the wedding was a flurry of preparations.

   **Example:** a flurry of excitement.

   **Example:** a flurry of speculation.

   **Source synonyms:** sprint

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A snack consisting of soft ice cream mixed with small pieces of fruit, cookie crumbs, etc.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The violent spasms of a dying whale.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. An occurrence of something (countable instances) in large numbers, happening suddenly or in a short period of time.

   **Example:** The fencer landed a flurry of hits on her opponent.

   **Example:** The think piece provoked a flurry of media responses for the remainder of the week.

   **Source synonyms:** volley; barrage
