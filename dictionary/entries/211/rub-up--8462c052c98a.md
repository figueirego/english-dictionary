### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To polish or scrub; to cover (something with a substance) by rubbing.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I rubbed up the brass buttons on my jacket to make them shine.

   **Example:** The pitcher rubs up the new baseball with dirt to get a better grip.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To rub (a body part)&#58; to massage, give a massage to.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To rub (a body part)&#58; to massage, give a massage to.
   1. To rub on someone or something.

      **Usage:** intransitive; transitive; with-on

      **Example:** The cat rubbed up on my legs.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To create (something) by rubbing.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to rub up a lather

   **Example:** The new shoe rubbed up a blister on the back of his foot.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To revive one's knowledge of (something); to renew (a skill).

   **Usage:** ambitransitive; dated; informal

   **Source synonyms:** bone up; brush up; review; revise

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To assault (someone).

   **Usage:** US; slang; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** rough up

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To reduce (something) to a powder or paste using friction (with a mortar and pestle, for example); to mix (with something) using friction.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

   **Example:** to rub up pigments with water or oil

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To excite or awaken (something); to revive or reawaken (something).

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

   **Example:** to rub up the memory; to rub up old sores
