## Adverb

### Forms

- e'en so — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In spite of that (preceding a remark (or fact) within a given discourse).

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The colors are a bit bright. Even so, I love this room's design.

   **Source synonyms:** nonetheless; nevertheless; be that as it may; having said that; that said; all that said; still; all that said; anyhow; all the same (idiomatic); be it as it may (idiomatic); be that as it may (idiomatic); but (Australia; Geordie); even so; non obstante (formal); having said that; howbeit (archaic); however; just the same; maugre (obsolete); natheless (archaic); nevertheless; nonetheless; notwithstanding; still; still and all; that being said; that said; though; withal (archaic); yet

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Exactly thus&#58; in exactly such a manner (as said or surmised); of precisely such a nature; precisely so.

   **Usage:** archaic; not-comparable

   **Example:** —You don't mean to tell me that you're the very man whom they've been seeking? —Even so. 'Tis I.

   **Source synonyms:** just so
