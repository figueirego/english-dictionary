### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Resembling or having the consistency of mush; semiliquid, pasty, or granular.

   **Example:** I don't especially like mushy oatmeal.

   **Source synonyms:** grumous; mushlike; pulpous; curdy; grumous; mushlike; mushy; paplike; pappy; pobby; porridgelike; puddingish; puddinglike; puddingy; pulplike; pulpous; pulpy; pultaceous; slushy

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Soft; squishy.

   **Example:** The brake pedal is mushy sometimes when I step on it.

   **Source synonyms:** smushy; spongy; squdgy; squitchy; squooshy

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Overly sappy, corny, or cheesy; sentimental, especially in a romantic way; maudlin.

   **Example:** Skip the mushy, romantic scenes and get to the action.

   **Source synonyms:** sappy; slushy; soppy; bathetic; cheesy; squishy; corny; drippy; gooey (figuratively); hokey (US; informal); [icky](../20c/icky--833a6825b0c5.md); kitsch; kitschy; maudlin; mawkish; mushy; overemotional; oversentimental; overwrought; sappy (US; figuratively); schmaltzy; sickly sweet; slushy; soppy (UK; figuratively); soupy; syrupy (figuratively); treacly (figuratively)
