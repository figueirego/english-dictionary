### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One's opinion or thoughts.

   **Usage:** colloquial; idiomatic; plural; plural-only

   **Example:** That's just my two cents, you can believe what you like.

   **Source synonyms:** two Abe Lincolns; two pennies' worth; tuppence worth; two penn'orth

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A nearly worthless amount, alluding to placing a copper penny on each of the eyelids of a pauper's or slave's body before burial.

   **Usage:** Australia; Canada; Ireland; New-Zealand; US; colloquial; idiomatic; plural; plural-only

   **Example:** That car won't last a week, it's not worth two cents.

   **Source synonyms:** farthing
