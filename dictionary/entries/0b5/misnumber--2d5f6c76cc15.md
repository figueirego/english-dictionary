### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To number incorrectly.
   1. To assign numbers nonsequentially where sequential numbering was due.

      **Usage:** transitive

      **Example:** The pages in this report are misnumbered. There are two "page 7"s, and there is no "page 6"!

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To number incorrectly.
   1. To ascribe singular where plural was due, or vice versa.

      **Usage:** transitive
