### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The notional line on either side of which opposing forces are arrayed, and at which they meet in battle; a fortified line, short of the line where battle is expected, and behind which forces are arrayed; a forward line of infantry or other force, prepared for battle.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A line of battle.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; navy; politics; war

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of those parts of a contest which, as a result of strategic decisions on both sides, are most closely fought and will likely determine the contest's course and outcome.

   **Usage:** figuratively; plural-normally
