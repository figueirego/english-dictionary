### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A children’s school, typically older than toddlers and younger than adolescents. In the U.S., elementary schools cover grades 1 through 5, and the ages of the children are usually 6-11 years. At a minimum, elementary schools will teach basic reading, writing, arithmetic, and history.

   **Usage:** Canada; US; countable; uncountable; historically UK
