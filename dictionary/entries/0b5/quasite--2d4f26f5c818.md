### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A theoretical type of artificial satellite equipped with large solar sails for maneuvering. It is capable of maintaining a stable solar orbit at speeds lower than standard Keplerian velocity, though it lacks the necessary solar sail area to completely hold a stationary position.

   **Usage:** space flight
