### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having the faculty of sensation; pertaining to the senses.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Responsive to stimuli.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Easily offended, upset, or hurt.

   **Example:** Max is very sensitive; he cried today because of the bad news.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a person

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Capable of offending, upsetting, or hurting.

   **Usage:** usually; topic

   **Example:** Religion is often a sensitive topic of discussion and should be avoided when dealing with foreign business associates.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of an issue

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Meant to be concealed or kept secret.

   **Example:** These are highly sensitive documents.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Being aware of the feelings of others and taking care not to offend them.

   **Example:** Thank you for being sensitive.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Important, intricate, and requiring great delicacy.

   **Example:** The president's untimely statement disrupted some very sensitive negotiations.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Accurate; able to register small changes in some property.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of an instrument

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. Having paranormal abilities that can be controlled through mesmerism.

   **Usage:** archaic
