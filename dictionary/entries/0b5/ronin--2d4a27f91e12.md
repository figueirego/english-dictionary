**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A masterless samurai (who often becomes a mercenary to make ends meet).

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A student who has failed the entrance examination for the high school or university of their choice&#58; if the student does not wish to take the exam the following year or is unable to, the student becomes a ronin, assuming full responsibility for their own training and survival.
