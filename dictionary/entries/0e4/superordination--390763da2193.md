### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The ordination of a person to fill a position already occupied; especially, the ordination by an ecclesiastical official, during his lifetime, of his successor.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The relation of a universal proposition to a particular proposition in the same terms.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; logic; mathematics; philosophy; sciences
