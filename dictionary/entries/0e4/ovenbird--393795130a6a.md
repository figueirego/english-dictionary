### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of several birds&#58;
   1. Originally, any of several small European birds that build dome-shaped nests; such as the long-tailed tit, willow warbler and chiffchaff.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of several birds&#58;
   1. An American warbler, Seiurus aurocapilla, that builds such a nest of vegetation.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of several birds&#58;
   1. Any bird of the Central and South America family Furnariidae.
