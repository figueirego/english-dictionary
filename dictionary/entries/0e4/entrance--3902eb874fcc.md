### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The action of entering, or going in.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** make a grand entrance

   **Example:** Her entrance attracted no attention whatsoever.

   **Source synonyms:** ingress; entry

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The act of taking possession, as of property, or of office.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** the entrance of an heir upon his inheritance, or of a magistrate into office

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The place of entering, as a gate or doorway.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** Place your bag by the entrance so that you can find it easily.

   **Source synonyms:** entryway

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The right to go in.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** You'll need a ticket to gain entrance to the museum.

   **Example:** to give entrance to friends

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The entering upon; the beginning, or that with which the beginning is made; the commencement; initiation.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The causing to be entered upon a register, as a ship or goods, at a customhouse; an entering.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** His entrance of the arrival was made the same day.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The angle which the bow of a vessel makes with the water at the water line.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

9. The beginning of a musician's playing or singing; entry.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
