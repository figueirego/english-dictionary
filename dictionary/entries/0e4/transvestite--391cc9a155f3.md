### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who sometimes wears clothes traditionally worn by and associated with the opposite sex; typically a male who cross-dresses occasionally by habit or personal choice.

   **Usage:** dated

   **Example:** Even though Steven used to dress up in his sister's clothes, it still came as a surprise he ended up as a transvestite.

   **Source synonyms:** cross-dresser; TV; tranny; transy
