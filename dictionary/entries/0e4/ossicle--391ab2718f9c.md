### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A small bone (or bony structure), especially one of the three of the middle ear.

   **Example:** The incus is one of the three auditory ossicles.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; medicine; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** auditory bone; auditory ossicle; bonelet; ossicular chain; ossicule; ossiculum; otic bone

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Bone-like joint or plate.
   1. One of numerous small calcareous structures forming the skeleton of certain echinoderms, as the starfishes.

      **Example:** The skeleton of echinoderms is made of ossicles, linked to each other via muscles and connective tissue.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences; zoology

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Bone-like joint or plate.
   1. One of the hard articuli or joints of the stem or branches of a crinoid or encrinite.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences; zoology

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Bone-like joint or plate.
   1. One of the several small hard chitinous parts or processes of the gastric skeleton of crustaceans, as in the stomach of a lobster or crawfish.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences; zoology
