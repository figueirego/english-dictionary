### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To bring peace to (a place or situation), by ending (or suppressing) war, fighting, violence, anger or agitation.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Their orders were to pacify the province by destroying resistance.

   **Source synonyms:** peacify; repacify; allay; locarnize

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To mollify, placate or soothe (someone or something).

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To appease (someone).

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** placate; see others there
