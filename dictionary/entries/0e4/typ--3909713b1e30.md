## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Abbreviation of typical&#58; used to label a feature that is to be interpreted as exactly the same as nearby comparable features.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; engineering drawings

   **Example:** Four equally spaced bolt holes on a bolt circle can be dimensioned at one hole with the notation (TYP) following it.

   **Alternative form of:** [typical](../26f/typical--9bfac98c5b65.md)
