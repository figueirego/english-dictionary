### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The weight of a product (especially food) without the (tare) weight of its packaging.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The weight of a vehicle without that of its fuel, cargo, personnel etc.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
