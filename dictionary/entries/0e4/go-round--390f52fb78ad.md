## Verb

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To physically swirl or rotate.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To rotate, to move in a circle.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To travel around (something) physically.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To circumvent, evade or outmanoeuvre.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To circulate, to move aimlessly.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To pass around, to circulate.

   **Example:** They say a new deadly virus is going round in South America.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To be sufficient to be shared, to be enough for everyone.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To go to another person's home or a public event.

   **Example:** I went round and told him what I thought of him.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To live behaving in a certain way, doing something regularly (followed by specification).

   **Example:** You can't go round stealing from unlocked cars!
