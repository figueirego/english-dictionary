   **Example:** trans- + national → transnational

   **Example:** trans- + regional → transregional

   **Example:** trans- + American → transamerican

2. To or on the other side of, beyond; outside of.

   **Usage:** morpheme

   **Example:** trans- + alpine → transalpine

   **Example:** trans- + uranic → transuranic

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A compound in which two atoms or groups are situated on opposite sides of some plane of symmetry passing through the compound. (Also used without the hyphen as an adjective; see trans.)

   **Usage:** morpheme; physical
