### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To allow or permit without explicit approval, usually if it is perceived as negative.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The party tolerated corruption within its ranks.

   **Source synonyms:** allow

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To bear, withstand.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I can tolerate working on Saturday, but not Sunday.

   **Example:** The elevator can tolerate up to 360 kilograms.

   **Source synonyms:** take; endure; withstand; bear; live with; put up with; stand
