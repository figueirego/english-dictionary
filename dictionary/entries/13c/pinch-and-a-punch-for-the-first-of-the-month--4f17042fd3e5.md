## Phrase

### Forms

- pinch punch first of the month — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Said on the first day of a new month, while pinching and punching someone as a prank (especially by children). The victim may respond with "a flick and a kick for being so quick" and the original attacker may reply with "a punch in the eye for being so sly".

   **Usage:** Australia; Canada; Ireland; New-Zealand; South-Africa; UK

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. More generally, a pair of attacks or setbacks at the start of something.

   **Usage:** Australia; Ireland; New-Zealand; UK
