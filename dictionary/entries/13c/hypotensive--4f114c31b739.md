## Noun · Record 2

### Forms

- hypotensives — plural

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Any drug that lowers the blood pressure.

   **Example:** a hypotensive agent such as an opioid

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. A drug that lowers the blood pressure as its principal intended effect; a drug taken to treat hypertension.

   **Example:** a hypotensive agent such as an ARB or an ACEI

   **Source synonyms:** antihypertensive

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. A person with hypotension.
