### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The point in the launch of a rocket or an aircraft where it leaves contact with the ground.

   **Usage:** space flight

   **Example:** we have liftoff \[in narration of launches\]

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** aeronautics; aerospace; aviation; business; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; rocketry

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The point at which a person or animal leaves the ground, as for example when jumping.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The point of launching into success or action.

   **Usage:** figuratively
