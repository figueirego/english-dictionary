### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To throw away; to get rid of; to dispose of (something, especially food that has gone bad).

   **Usage:** informal; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** toss; throw out; junk; 86; bin; can; cast aside; cast off; cast away; chuck; chuck away; chuck out; discard; dispose of; ditch; dump; get rid of; jettison; pitch; scrap; shift; throw aside; throw away; throw out; toss; trash

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To remove or eject from a group; to kick out.

   **Usage:** informal; transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To reject or refuse to consider as evidence.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The judge tossed out her testimony when it came to light that she was getting paid as a contractor by the defendant.

   **Source antonyms:** allow; adduce
