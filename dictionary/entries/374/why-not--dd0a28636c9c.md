## Adverb

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Why is that so? (for a negative statement)

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** I don't want to go out today. — Why not?

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used to state that one has no objection to and approves of a proposal.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; rhetorical

   **Example:** Let's have pizza for lunch. — Yeah, why not?

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used to introduce a proposal.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; rhetorical

   **Example:** Why not try turning the handle in the other direction to see if that opens the window?

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** why? — why is that not true; how come? — why is that not true; all right — to show one has no objection; OK — to show one has no objection; how about — to introduce a proposal; what if — to introduce a proposal
