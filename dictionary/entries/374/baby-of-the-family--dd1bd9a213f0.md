## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The youngest member of an immediate family.

   **Source synonyms:** lastborn

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A member of an immediate family who behaves as, or is treated as though they were the youngest member of a family.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An adult member of an immediate family who is treated by others in the family, especially the parents, as though they were a child.
