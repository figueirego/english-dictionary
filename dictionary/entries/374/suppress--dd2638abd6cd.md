### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To put an end to, especially with force, to crush, do away with; to prohibit, subdue.

   **Example:** Political dissent was brutally suppressed.

   **Source synonyms:** abolish; clamp down; eliminate; bury; quash; squash

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To restrain or repress, such as laughter or an expression.

   **Example:** I struggled to suppress my smile.

   **Source synonyms:** curtail; stifle; behedge; guard; govern (formal); smother; bind (figuratively); chasten; choke back; check; inhibit; catch (reflexive); contain; vanquish; cork (figuratively); curb; quench; quell; bury; curtail; dampen; hamshackle (figuratively); hold back (idiomatic); hold down (idiomatic); limit; repress; restrain; restrict; shackle (figuratively); stay; stifle; stop; straiten; subdue; suppress; throttle (figuratively); underbring; withhold; withstrain (archaic)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To exclude undesirable thoughts from one's mind.

   **Example:** He unconsciously suppressed his memories of abuse.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; medicine; psychiatry; psychology; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To prevent publication.

   **Example:** The government suppressed the findings of their research about the true state of the economy.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To stop a flow or stream.

   **Example:** The rescue team managed to suppress the flow of oil by blasting the drilling hole.

   **Example:** Hot blackcurrant juice mixed with honey may suppress cough.

   **Source synonyms:** clamp off; dam; stanch; stem

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To forbid the use of evidence at trial because it is improper or was improperly obtained.

   **Usage:** US

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To reduce unwanted frequencies in a signal.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; electrical-engineering; electricity; electromagnetism; electronics; energy; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To stop or prevent the enemy from executing unwanted activities like firing, regrouping, observation or others.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war
