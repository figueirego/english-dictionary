## Adjective · Record 1

### Forms

- more shorthanded — comparative
- most shorthanded — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Lacking sufficient staff or people, as for normal or efficient operations.

   **Example:** The tiny restaurant usually got by with three workers on that shift, but found itself shorthanded when the tour bus pulled in.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Having less than a quorum.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Having, or occurring during an interval with, fewer than a full complement of players, often as the result of a penalty.

   **Usage:** team sports

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Having fewer than the standard number of players.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** card-games; poker

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a table
