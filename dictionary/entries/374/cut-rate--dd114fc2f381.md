## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Offered for sale or rent at a lower than usual rate.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Sometimes cut-rate airfares are available because the seat would otherwise go empty.

   **Source synonyms:** discount

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Cheaply made.

   **Usage:** broadly; not-comparable

   **Example:** For this jobsite, we'll have none of those shoddy, cut-rate materials, please. Send us the good stuff for this job.

   **Source synonyms:** discount; second-rate; third-rate; fourth-rate; fifth-rate

   **Source antonyms:** first-rate
