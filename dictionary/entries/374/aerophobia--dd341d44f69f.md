## Noun

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˌɛə.ɹoʊˈfoʊ.bi.ə/ — source labels: General-American

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A pathological aversion or sensitivity to air or the movement of air, especially as a symptom of rabies.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** The patient was diagnosed after displaying both aerophobia and acute hydrophobia.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An aversion to fresh air, drafts or breezes.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** I can't understand your aerophobia on days this warm.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Fear of flying

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Many first-time flyers suffer from aerophobia, though some hide it better than others.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** lifestyle; tourism; transport; travel
