### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Lying on its back.

   **Source synonyms:** reclined

   **Source antonyms:** prone; prostrate

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of the hand, forearm or foot, turned facing toward the body or upward&#58; with the thumb outward (palm up), or with the big toe raised relative to the little toe.

   **Example:** when one is washing one’s face, the hand is in the supine position; and then the forearm is also in the supine position; when the foot is resting on the outer side of the sole, it is in the supine position

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Reluctant to take action due to indifference or moral weakness; apathetic or passive towards something.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** Such corruption is commonplace in a world of supine civil servants and underfunded ministries.

   **Source synonyms:** passive; peaceful; lazy; lethargic; listless; adiaphroistic; apathetic; apathistical; blasé; blithe; deaf; cool; shiftless; dispassionate; dull; enervated; gallionic; halfhearted; heartless (rare); impassive; ineffervescible; insouciant; indifferent; insensible; jaded; languid; languorous; Laodicean (archaic); lethargic; listless; lackadaisical; lotophagous; lukewarm; meh; mopey; nonchalant; nonplussed (US); otiose; phlegmatic; pococurante; spiritless; supine; tepid; torpid; torpedinous (archaic); unaroused; uncaring; unconcerned; unenthusiastic; unimpressible; uninterested; unlively; unmoved; untouched; unzealous; world-weary

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Inclining or leaning backward; inclined, sloping.

   **Usage:** poetic; rare

   **Source synonyms:** inclined; sloping
