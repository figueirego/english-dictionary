### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. (statistics) A method of modelling relationships among variables that distinguishes direct effects from effects transmitted through other variables.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences; statistics

   **Extractor diagnostics (not usage labels):** empty-gloss; no-gloss

**Supplemental definition (evidence reviewed; source gap preserved):** The original quotation explicitly concerns the statistical method. Both captured research papers distinguish direct and indirect relationships; the gloss does not imply that an observational path model proves causation.
