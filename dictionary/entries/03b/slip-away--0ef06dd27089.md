### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To leave without being noticed.

   **Example:** I'm going to try to slip away from work early, if I can.

   **Source synonyms:** slide off; [slip off](../0c7/slip-off--31d774433731.md); sneak off

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To pass quickly, almost unnoticed.

   **Example:** The months slipped away and became years.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of time

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To disappear; to elude one; to recede; to dwindle.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To die peacefully.

   **Usage:** euphemistic

   **Example:** He slipped away quietly in his sleep.
