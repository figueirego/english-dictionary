### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To produce by writing.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The supervisor wrote up the personnel report about him.

   **Source synonyms:** write down; record; transcribe

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To document officially the faults, offenses, or wrongdoing of.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** His supervisor wrote him up after their last argument.

   **Source synonyms:** write down; cite

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To write about positively; to praise in writing.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The paper wrote up the team's prospects.

   **Source synonyms:** talk up

   **Source antonyms:** write down

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To make an upward adjustment in the value of an asset.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The company decided to write up its property assets after an independent appraisal showed their market value had increased significantly.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** accounting; business; finance

   **Source antonyms:** write down; devalue

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To annotate a stamp album page with details about the stamp, such as die used or colour variety.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** She carefully wrote up each stamp in her collection, noting the Scott catalog number, perforation gauge, and printing plate variety.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; philately

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see write, up.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I can't write up in the attic; it's too cold there in the winter and too hot there in the summer.
