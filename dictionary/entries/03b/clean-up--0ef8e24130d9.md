### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make an area or a thing clean; to pick up a mess; to tidy.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Clean up your room.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To become clean, handsome, smart in appearance, e.g. for a special occasion, especially when it is out of character to be seen as such.

   **Usage:** colloquial; idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** He sure cleans up nice.

   **Source synonyms:** scrub up

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To make something less corrupt or unseemly.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** clean up one's act

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To make a large profit; to win by a large margin, or to win a large amount, especially in gambling. Also clean house.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** Man, he sure cleaned up last night at the blackjack table.

   **Example:** The investors cleaned up when the stock hit the roof last year.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To intervene in a fight between two players at low health and easily kill both of them or the winner.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** video-games
