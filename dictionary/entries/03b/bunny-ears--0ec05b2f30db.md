## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A hand gesture in which the index and middle fingers are raised and parted, while the other fingers are clenched, used behind the head of a subject of a photograph.

   **Usage:** plural; plural-only

   **Example:** I only realized I had been given the bunny ears after the pictures were developed.
