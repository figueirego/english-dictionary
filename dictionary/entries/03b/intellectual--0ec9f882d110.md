### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Pertaining to, or performed by, the intellect; mental or cognitive.

   **Example:** intellectual powers, activities, etc.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Endowed with intellect; having a keen sense of understanding; having the capacity for higher forms of knowledge or thought; characterized by intelligence or cleverness

   **Example:** an intellectual person

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Suitable for exercising one's intellect; perceived by the intellect

   **Example:** intellectual employments

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Relating to the understanding; treating of the mind.

   **Example:** intellectual philosophy, sometimes called "mental" philosophy

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Spiritual.

   **Usage:** archaic; poetic
