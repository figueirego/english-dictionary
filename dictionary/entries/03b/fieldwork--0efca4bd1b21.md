### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Work done out in the fields as opposed to that done elsewhere on the farm (e.g., barn, house, outbuildings, office).

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Besides the bookkeeping and marketing, there's a ton of fieldwork to get done this week.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** agriculture; business; lifestyle

   **Source synonyms:** outwork

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Work done out in the real world rather than in controlled conditions.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** That well Bob set up last weekend was some good fieldwork.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The collection of raw data in the field.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** I thought the fieldwork in the course I was taking was going to be hard, but it was just interviewing people.

   **Source context (verbatim):** in scientific research

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A temporary fortification built by troops in the field; a defensive earthwork in the field.

   **Usage:** countable; rare

   **Example:** Billy's out building some fieldwork so that our enemies don't walk right over us.
