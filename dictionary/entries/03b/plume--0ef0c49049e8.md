### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A feather of a bird, especially a large or showy one used as a decoration.

   **Usage:** archaic; literary; poetic

   **Source synonyms:** aigrette

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A cluster of feathers worn as an ornament, especially on a helmet; a hackle.

   **Usage:** archaic; literary; poetic

   **Source synonyms:** aigrette

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A token of honour or prowess; that on which one prides oneself; a prize or reward.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Source synonyms:** feather in one's cap

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The vane (“flattened, web-like part”) of a feather, especially when on a quill pen or the fletching of an arrow.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Ellipsis of plume moth (“a small, slender moth of the family Pterophoridae”).

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; ellipsis

   **Alternative form of:** [plume moth](../157/plume-moth--55ed6005f703.md)

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To adorn, cover, or furnish with feathers or plumes, or as if with feathers or plumes.

   **Usage:** also; figuratively; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** feather; fledge

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Chiefly of a bird&#58; to arrange and preen the feathers of, specifically in preparation for flight; hence (figurative), to prepare for (something).

   **Usage:** reflexive; transitive

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To congratulate (oneself) proudly, especially concerning something unimportant or when taking credit for another person's effort; to self-congratulate; to preen.

   **Usage:** broadly; reflexive; transitive

   **Example:** He plumes himself on his skill.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To strip (a bird) of feathers; to pluck.

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** unplume

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To strip (a bird) of feathers; to pluck.
   1. To peel, to strip completely; to pillage; also, to deprive of power.

      **Usage:** archaic; broadly; transitive

      **Source synonyms:** unplume

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To strip (a bird) of feathers; to pluck.
   1. Of a hawk&#58; to pluck the feathers from prey.

      **Usage:** archaic; obsolete; transitive

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** falconry; hobbies; hunting; lifestyle

      **Source synonyms:** unplume

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

**Source sense R2.S7**

7. Of a dispersed substance such as dust or smoke&#58; to fan out or spread in a cloud.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Smoke plumed from his pipe, then slowly settled towards the floor.
