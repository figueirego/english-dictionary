## Noun

### Forms

- countershocks — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A shock that travels in the opposite direction to an applied impulse.
   1. Such a shock that propagates through an object that was struck with a blow, such as a stone being chiseled or the Earth's crust undergoing an earthquake.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A shock that travels in the opposite direction to an applied impulse.
   1. Such a shock in the form of an electric impulse, as may occur for various reasons in electrical systems, for either desirable or unanticipated reasons (for example, some defibrillation involves countershocks).
