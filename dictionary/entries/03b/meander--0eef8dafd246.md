### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One of the turns of a winding, crooked, or involved course.

   **Usage:** often; plural

   **Example:** the meanders of an old river, or of the veins and arteries in the body

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One of a series of regular sinuous curves, bends, loops, turns, or windings in the channel of a river, stream, or other watercourse

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A tortuous or winding journey.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Synonym of Greek key, a decorative border; fretwork.

   **Source synonyms:** Greek key (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A self-avoiding closed curve which intersects a line a number of times.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A path on which the directions, distances, and elevations are noted, as a part of a land survey.

   **Usage:** obsolete; possibly

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A decorative border consisting of a repeated linear motif, particularly of intersecting perpendicular lines.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** architecture

**Source sense R2.S1**

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The stream meandered through the valley.

   **Source synonyms:** wind; worm; wander

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**
