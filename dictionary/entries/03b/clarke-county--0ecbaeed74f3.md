## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A county, one of 67 in Alabama, United States. County seat&#58; Grove Hill. Named after John Clarke.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A county, one of 159 in Georgia, United States. County seat&#58; Athens. Named after Elijah Clarke. It is now consolidated with Athens as Athens-Clarke County.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A county, one of 99 in Iowa, United States. County seat&#58; Osceola. Named after James Clarke.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A county, one of 82 in Mississippi, United States. County seat&#58; Quitman. Named after Joshua G. Clarke.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A county, one of 95 in Virginia, United States. County seat&#58; Berryville. Named after George Rogers Clark.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Clark County, Washington, revised in 1925. Named after William Clark.
