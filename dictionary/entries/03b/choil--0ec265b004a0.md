### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An unsharpened portion of a knife blade at the base of the blade, near the handle of the knife.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The region of a knife where such a portion is, or would be if it existed; the region may be arranged as a grip, guard, or combination thereof (a finger choil), or it may be a notch demarking the end of the sharpened edge (a sharpening choil).

   **Example:** This knife's large finger choil helps compensate for the short handle.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The region of a knife where such a portion is, or would be if it existed; the region may be arranged as a grip, guard, or combination thereof (a finger choil), or it may be a notch demarking the end of the sharpened edge (a sharpening choil).
   1. The portion where the heel meets the bolster.

      **Usage:** on fixed-blade knives

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The region of a knife where such a portion is, or would be if it existed; the region may be arranged as a grip, guard, or combination thereof (a finger choil), or it may be a notch demarking the end of the sharpened edge (a sharpening choil).
   1. The indentation of a pocket-knife blade where it joins the tang.

      **Usage:** on folding knives
