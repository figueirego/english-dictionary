### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person living on adjacent or nearby land; a person situated adjacently or nearby; anything (of the same type of thing as the subject) in an adjacent or nearby position.

   **Usage:** UK

   **Example:** My neighbour has two noisy cats.

   **Example:** They′re our neighbours across the street.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One who is near in sympathy or confidence.

   **Usage:** UK

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A fellow human being.

   **Usage:** UK

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biblical; lifestyle; religion

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Anything located directly adjacent to something else.

   **Usage:** UK

   **Example:** The flood fill algorithm fills an area with colour by starting at one pixel and recursively visiting its neighbours.
