### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To put on the throne in a formal installation ceremony called enthronement, equivalent to (and often combined with) coronation or other ceremonies of investiture.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** ceremonially enthrone

   **Example:** enthrone a monarch

   **Example:** The bishop came to enthrone the new archbishop.

   **Source synonyms:** coronate; crown

   **Source antonyms:** dethrone

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To help a candidate to the succession of a monarchy (as a kingmaker does), or by extension in any other major organization.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Source antonyms:** dethrone

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To exalt, to praise.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The novel seemed to enthrone love as the highest virtue.
