## Phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Indicates something that will happen very soon; indicates action that is imminent; ready to; went to

   **Usage:** idiomatic; verb; with-infinitive

   **Example:** He's standing at the edge, and I think he's about to jump.

   **Example:** She seemed about to say something.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Indicates that one has no intention of doing the stated thing at any time in the future.

   **Usage:** Canada; informal

   **Example:** I'm not about to let the lockdown stop me from going to the beach every day.

   **Example:** I'm not about to wander around at night to find it.

   **Source context (verbatim):** as negative 'not about to'

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Indicates that one is preparing or planning to do the stated thing at some time in the future, not necessarily imminently.

   **Usage:** obsolete

   **Source context (verbatim):** in affirmative

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** bound to; fixing to; on the point of
