## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of the branch of the Uralic language family that includes Finnish, Estonian, Karelian, Veps, Livonian, Ingrian and Votic.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source synonyms:** Baltic-Finnic; Baltic-Fennic; Balto-Finnic; Balto-Fennic; Fennic

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of the hypothetical Finno-Volgaic subgrouping of the Uralic languages that includes the Baltic-Finnic, Sami, Mordvinic and Mari languages.

   **Usage:** dated; not-comparable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of the hypothetical Finno-Permic subgrouping of the Uralic languages that includes the Finno-Volgaic and Permic languages.

   **Usage:** dated; not-comparable

   **Source synonyms:** Finno-Permic; Finno-Permian; Fenno-Permic; Fenno-Permian; Fennic

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Finnish.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; rare
