## Noun · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Forms

- wait-a-bits — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of certain plants with sharp hooked thorns found in southern and eastern Africa.
   1. One of numerous acacias and mimosas and relatives.
      1. Senegalia brevispica (wait-a-bit acacia)

         **Usage:** South-Africa

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of certain plants with sharp hooked thorns found in southern and eastern Africa.
   1. One of numerous acacias and mimosas and relatives.
      1. Biancaea decapetala (syn. Caesalpinia decapetala, Mauritius thorn, Mysore thorn)

         **Usage:** South-Africa

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of certain plants with sharp hooked thorns found in southern and eastern Africa.
   1. Ziziphus mucronata (buffalo thorn, Cape thorn)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any of certain plants with sharp hooked thorns found in southern and eastern Africa.
   1. The grapple plant (Uncarina leptocarpa).

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Any of certain plants with sharp hooked thorns found in southern and eastern Africa.
   1. Asparagus spp. of South Africa (Asparagus capensis, Asparagus suaveolens, Asparagus burchellii, Asparagus mariae, Asparagus flavicaulis, Asparagus spinescens)
