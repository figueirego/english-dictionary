## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One of 75 counties in Arkansas, United States. County seat&#58; Prescott. It was so named as it is roughly the shape of the state of Nevada when inverted.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One of 58 counties in California, United States. County seat&#58; Nevada City. Named after the city, in 1851, not the state of Nevada which was created in 1861.
