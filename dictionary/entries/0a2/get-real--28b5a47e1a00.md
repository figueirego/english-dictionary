## Interjection · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Expresses disbelief, or urges someone to face reality.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** You want me to stay here with you in this dump? Get real!

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To stop pretending or dreaming; to start facing up to some aspect of reality (usually, an unpleasant one).

   **Example:** let's get real

   **Example:** Some fans are asking what this plot twist might mean for the next sequel, but let's get real&#58; there's probably not going to be another sequel in this movie franchise, if the viewership stays so low.

   **Example:** He finally got real and stopped going on about how his side gig was going to make him rich.
