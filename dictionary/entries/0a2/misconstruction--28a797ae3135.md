## Noun

### Forms

- misconstructions — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Mistaken or faulty construction; an instance of it.
   1. A misunderstanding or misinterpretation resulting from a different meaning than intended of a word that has multiple meanings.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Mistaken or faulty construction; an instance of it.
   1. Other misconstrual or misinterpretation not necessarily hinging on missense of any particular word.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Example:** a misconstruction of the law

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Mistaken or faulty construction; an instance of it.
