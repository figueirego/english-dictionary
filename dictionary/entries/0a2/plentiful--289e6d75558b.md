### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Existing in large number or ample amount.

   **Example:** a plentiful harvest

   **Example:** a plentiful supply of water

   **Example:** She accumulated a plentiful collection of books.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Yielding abundance; fruitful.

   **Example:** Some years, the tree is a plentiful source of apples.

**Source sense R1.S3**
