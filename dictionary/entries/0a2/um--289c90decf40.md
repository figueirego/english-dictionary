### Pronunciation

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Expression of hesitation, uncertainty or space filler in conversation.

   **Example:** Um, I don’t know.

   **Example:** Let’s see... um... how about this?

   **Source synonyms:** er; hmm; uh; eh

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Dated spelling of mmm.

   **Usage:** US

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An expression to forcefully call attention to something wrong.

   **Example:** Um, excuse me!

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An expression of shocked disapproval used by a child who witnesses forbidden behavior.

   **Usage:** childish
