### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Administration; the use of limited resources combined with forecasting, planning, leadership and execution skills to achieve predetermined specific goals.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The executives of an organisation, especially senior executives.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Judicious use of means to accomplish an end.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** Excellent time management helped her succeed in all facets of her life.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The prosecuting side in an impeachment trial (as opposed to the respondent or defending side).

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually
