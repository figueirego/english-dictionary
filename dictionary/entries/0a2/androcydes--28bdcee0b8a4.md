### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of certain male historical figures of Ancient Greece&#58;
   1. Androcydes of Cyzicus, a Greek painter of the 4th century BCE.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of certain male historical figures of Ancient Greece&#58;
   1. A Greek physician and writer of the 4th century BCE, who according to Pliny advised Alexander the Great to moderate his drinking.
