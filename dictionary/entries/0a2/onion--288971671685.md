### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A monocotyledonous plant (Allium cepa), allied to garlic, used as vegetable and spice.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The bulb of such a plant.

   **Example:** My eyes are stinging from the chopped onions.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of various plants, mostly of the genus Allium, that are more or less similar to Allium cepa.

   **Example:** Many onions are delicious; domesticated onions such as Allium cepa and Allium fistulosum are especially good, but even wild onions such as Allium canadense and Allium validum are nice in small doses.
