## Noun

### Forms

- misadventures — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Synonym of misfortune, bad luck or mishap, an instance of bad luck.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** All under the sun are subject at times to misery and misadventure.

   **Source synonyms:** misfortune (synonym; synonym-of); bad luck or mishap (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Synonym of mistake, a misguided and/or failed enterprise, a risky action that turns out badly.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** yet another American military misadventure

   **Source synonyms:** mistake (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A fatal mishap, especially one voluntarily risked by the victim, bad luck causing an accidental death without attaching any liability to any other involved parties.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law
