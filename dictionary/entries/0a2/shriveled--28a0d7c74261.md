### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Wrinkled because the volume has reduced while the surface area of the outer layer has remained constant.

   **Usage:** US

   **Example:** A prune is a shriveled plum.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Collapsed in size.

   **Usage:** US

   **Example:** Her lips looked too shriveled to close over her teeth.
