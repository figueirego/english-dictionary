### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Title used before an adult woman's name or surname, especially when it is not desired or possible to indicate her marital status with Miss or Mrs.

   **Example:** Ms Jane Doe; Ms. Roe

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Title used for a woman before a place, name, thing, or description that she is supposed to typify or represent.

   **Example:** Ms Efficiency and Ms Right

   **Source synonyms:** Mrs.
