### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A branch of biology concerned with the scientific study of plants.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The plant life of a geographical area; flora.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** the botany of Greenland

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The properties and life phenomena exhibited by a plant, plant type, or plant group.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A botanical treatise or study, especially of a particular system of botany or that of a particular place.

   **Usage:** countable
