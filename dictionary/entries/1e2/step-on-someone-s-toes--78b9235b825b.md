### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To cause someone offense, especially by trying to control something that is under their authority, or interfering in their private affairs.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I didn't think I'd be stepping on my sister's toes when I told my nephew to sit up straight at the dinner table.

   **Source synonyms:** tread on someone's toes
