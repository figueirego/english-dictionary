### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A non-dispersive wave in the ocean or atmosphere that balances the Earth's Coriolis force against a topographic boundary such as a coastline, or a waveguide such as the equator.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** climatology; geography; meteorology; natural-sciences; oceanography

<a id="r1-s2"></a>
