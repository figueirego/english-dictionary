## Noun

### Forms

- bad tastes in one's mouth — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A feeling that something is morally despicable as to cause nausea.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** The more I thought of my orders, the more it left a bad taste in my mouth.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A feeling of guilt, responsibility, or embarrassment as to cause nausea.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** Knowing that I had inadvertently aided a killer, left me with a bad taste in my mouth.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A feeling of disappointment and frustration.

   **Usage:** idiomatic
