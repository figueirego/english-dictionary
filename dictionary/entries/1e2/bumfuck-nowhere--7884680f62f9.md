**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The middle of nowhere; a nondescript, isolated, and underdeveloped locale whose precise location may be difficult to ascertain.

   **Usage:** derogatory; slang; uncountable; vulgar

   **Example:** I can't believe we're lost in the middle of bumfuck nowhere with a broken-down car!
