### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A stopper, alternative to a cork, often made of rubber, used to prevent fluid passing through the neck of a bottle, vat, a hole in a vessel etc.

2. The cecum or anus, especially of livestock but sometimes (slang) of humans.

3. A bribe.

   **Usage:** slang

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The orifice in the bilge of a cask through which it is filled; bung-hole.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A sharper or pickpocket.

   **Usage:** obsolete; slang

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

   **Usage:** UK; obsolete; slang
