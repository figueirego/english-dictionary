### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A lake in west-central Saskatchewan, Canada, bordered by the Rural Municipality of Tramping Lake, Rural Municipality of Mariposa, Rural Municipality of Grandview and Rural Municipality of Reford, No. 379.

**Source sense R1.S2**
