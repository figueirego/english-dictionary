**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Given a group G and a commutative ring R, a tuple of maps (a,d,x), where a,d&#58;G→R and x&#58;GxG→R, are a pseudorepresentation if they satisfy the relations one would expect if a(g) and d(g) were the diagonal entries of a two dimensional representation \|a(g)b(g)\|\\\|c(g)d(g)\| and if x was given by x(g,g^′)=b(g)c(g^′).
