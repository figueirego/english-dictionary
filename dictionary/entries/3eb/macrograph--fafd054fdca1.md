## Noun

### Forms

- macrographs — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An image of an object as seen by the naked eye, without magnification.

   **Example:** a macrograph of a metallic fracture

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An image of an object that is at least as large as the object.
