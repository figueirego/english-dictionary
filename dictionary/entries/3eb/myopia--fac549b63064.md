### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A disorder of the vision where distant objects appear blurred because the eye focuses their images in front of the retina instead of on it.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; pathology; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** shortsightedness; nearsightedness

   **Source antonyms:** hyperopia

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A lack of imagination, discernment or long-range perspective in thinking or planning.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable
