<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A long rope with a sliding loop on one end, generally used in ranching to catch cattle and horses.

   **Example:** He managed to catch the runaway bull with a lasso.

   **Example:** She threw the lasso skillfully around the post.
