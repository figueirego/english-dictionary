### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To interpret (a picture, story, or other form of communication) to reveal a hidden, broader message about real-world issues and occurrences.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To create an allegory from (a character, an event or situation, etc.); also, to use one or more symbols to depict (a hidden, broader message about real-world issues and occurrences).

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Followed by away&#58; to treat (something) as allegorical or symbolic rather than as truth.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** lifestyle; religion

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To interpret an allegory.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To create or use allegory.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** For his explanation he will allegorize the problem.
