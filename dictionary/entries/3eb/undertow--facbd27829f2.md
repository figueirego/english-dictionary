## Noun · Record 2

### Forms

- undertows — plural

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A short-range flow of water returning seaward from the waves breaking on the shore.

   **Example:** A strong undertow may sweep a returning swimmer off their feet but it does not carry them far from the shore.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. A feeling that runs contrary to one's normal one.

   **Usage:** broadly
