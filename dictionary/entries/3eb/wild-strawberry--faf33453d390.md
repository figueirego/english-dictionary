## Noun

### Forms

- wild strawberries — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A plant of the species of strawberry that grows naturally throughout much of the Northern Hemisphere, Fragaria vesca.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of several species of plants resembling true strawberries, such as Indian strawberry (Potentilla indica, syn. Fragaria indica).

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A berry of such plants.
