## Determiner

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. All, except for a negligible set, i.e. finite, countable or of measure zero.

   **Example:** Rational numbers are a countable set, so almost all numbers are irrational.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

   **Source antonyms:** almost no

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In non-mathematical use, see almost, all.

   **Example:** We've eaten almost all of the cake.
