### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Nervous and worried.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Having a feeling of anxiety or disquietude; extremely concerned, especially about something that will happen in the future or that is unknown.

   **Example:** She was anxious to hear how her test results were.

   **Example:** I could tell she was anxious as she was biting her nails.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Accompanied with, or causing, anxiety; worrying.

   **Example:** anxious labor

   **Example:** There was an anxious wait before the results were revealed.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of things

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Earnestly desirous.

   **Example:** He is anxious to please, so you can count on him.

   **Example:** All the voters were anxious to hear the election result.
