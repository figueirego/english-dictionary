### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A type of stock market or financial derivative order, signifying that the order is to be presented for execution, and if not executed ("filled") immediately (traditionally within a few seconds) is to be cancelled.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance; stock-market

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A type of stock market or financial derivative order, signifying that the order is to be presented for execution, and if not executed ("filled") immediately (traditionally within a few seconds) is to be cancelled.
   1. An order-handling policy stating that if a supplier cannot fulfill an entire order immediately, the vendor ships what is currently in stock and automatically cancels ("kills") the remaining balance.
