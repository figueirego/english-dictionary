### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The year in school that comes after twelfth grade in some schools in the US, and that came after twelfth grade before 1988 in Ontario, Canada.

   **Usage:** US

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** education

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A reference to college as nothing more than a continuation of high school, since secondary education typically ends after the twelfth grade.

   **Usage:** US; derogatory; humorous

   **Example:** You went to community college? That's nothing but the thirteenth grade.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** education
