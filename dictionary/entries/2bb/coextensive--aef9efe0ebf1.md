### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having the same spatial limits or boundaries; sharing the same area.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The city and county of San Francisco are coextensive.

   **Source synonyms:** coterminous

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Occurring over the same period of time; contemporaneous.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
