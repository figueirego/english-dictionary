### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The achievement of one's aim or goal.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** His third attempt to pass the entrance exam was a success.

   **Example:** a glowing success

   **Source synonyms:** hit

   **Source antonyms:** failure

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Financial profitability.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Don't let success go to your head.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. One who, or that which, achieves assumed goals.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Scholastically, he was a success.

   **Example:** The new range of toys has been a resounding success.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The fact of getting or achieving wealth, respect, or fame.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** She is country music's most recent success.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Something which happens as a consequence; the outcome or result.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable
