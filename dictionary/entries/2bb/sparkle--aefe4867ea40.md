   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

## Verb · Etymology 2 · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈspɑɹ.kəl/ — source labels: General-American

- sparkled — participle; past
- sparkled — past
- sparckle — alternative
- sparcle — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To emit sparks; to throw off ignited or incandescent particles.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The wood was sparkling in the bonfire.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To shine as if throwing off sparks; to emit flashes of light; to scintillate; to twinkle.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** The stars sparkle in the sky.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To manifest itself by, or as if by, emitting sparks; to glisten; to flash.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To emit little bubbles, as certain kinds of liquors; to effervesce.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** sparkling wine

   **Example:** sparkling water

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To emit in the form or likeness of sparks.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To disperse.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

**Source sense R2.S7**

7. To scatter on or over.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive
