### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A standardized form of some task or process (that is, a standardized way of doing it); the formalised set of step-by-step instructions for completing such a task or process, mandated to ensure consistency, efficiency, and (especially) quality, safety, and (often) regulatory compliance.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; particularly manufacturing

   **Source synonyms:** SOP
