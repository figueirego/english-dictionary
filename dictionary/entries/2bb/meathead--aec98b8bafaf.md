### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An ungainly, dull, or stupid person; someone who is lazy, disrespectful, and/or whose beliefs and philosophies clash with those of another.

   **Usage:** slang

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A large, muscular, stupid man, especially an athlete.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Example:** Gary was a hulking meathead who, when he wasn't playing football, was either hunting, fishing or getting drunk and rowdy in some topless bar.

   **Source synonyms:** brute; jock
