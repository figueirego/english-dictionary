### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The fact of knowing about something; general understanding or familiarity with a subject, place, situation etc.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** His knowledge of Iceland was limited to what he'd seen on the Travel Channel.

   **Example:** He has a lot of knowledge on the Indus Valley Civilization.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Awareness of a particular fact or situation; a state of having been informed or made aware of something.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Intellectual understanding; the state of appreciating truth or information.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** Knowledge consists in recognizing the difference between good and bad decisions.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Familiarity or understanding of a particular skill, branch of learning etc.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** Does your friend have any knowledge of hieroglyphs, perchance?

   **Example:** A secretary should have a good knowledge of shorthand.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Justified true belief

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually; philosophical

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Sexual intimacy or intercourse (now usually in phrase carnal knowledge).

   **Usage:** archaic; uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Information or intelligence about something; notice.

   **Usage:** obsolete; uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. The total of what is known; all information and products of learning.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** His library contained the accumulated knowledge of the Greeks and Romans.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. Something that can be known; a branch of learning; a piece of information; a science.

   **Usage:** countable; usually

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. Acknowledgement.

   **Usage:** obsolete; uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. Notice, awareness.

   **Usage:** obsolete; uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s12"></a>

**Source sense R1.S12**

12. The deep familiarity with certain routes and places of interest required by taxicab drivers working in London, England.

   **Usage:** UK; informal; uncountable; usually
