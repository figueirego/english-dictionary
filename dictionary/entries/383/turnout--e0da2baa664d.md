### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of coming forth.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The number or proportion of people who attend or participate in an event (especially an election) or are present at a venue.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A place to pull off a road.

   **Usage:** US; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** When towing a trailer, use the turnouts to let faster traffic pass.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A place where moveable rails allow a train to switch tracks; a set of points.

   **Usage:** US; countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** rail-transport; railways; transport

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A quitting of employment for the purpose of forcing increase of wages; a strike.

   **Usage:** countable; dated; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A striker.

   **Usage:** countable; dated; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. That which is prominently brought forward or exhibited; hence, an equipage.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** A man with a showy carriage and horses is said to have a fine turnout.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Net quantity of produce yielded.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. The act of putting out to pasture.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Duties include feeding and turnout of horses.

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. Rotation of the leg at the hips which causes the feet and knees to turn outward, away from the front of the body.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; ballet
