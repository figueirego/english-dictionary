### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A chain of events that, once initiated, cannot be halted; especially one in which the final outcome is undesirable or precarious.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** I think basing your actions off an assumption like that is a slippery slope that is going to get you in trouble.

   **Example:** She's gotten herself on a slippery slope to being dismissed.

   **Source synonyms:** slippy slope; thin edge of the wedge; thin end of the wedge

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A logical fallacy in which a party asserts that a particular result will probably follow from a given decision or circumstance, without necessarily providing any rational argument for the likelihood of the assumed consequence.

   **Usage:** broadly

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A logical fallacy in which a party asserts that a particular outcome has resulted from a given decision or circumstance by referencing other events that are inconsequentially interconnected.

   **Usage:** broadly
