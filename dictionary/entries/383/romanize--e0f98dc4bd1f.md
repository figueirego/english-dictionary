### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To put letters or words written in another writing system into the Latin (Roman) alphabet.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The author romanizes Chinese names using the Wade–Giles system rather than the Pinyin system.

   **Source synonyms:** Latinize; transliterate

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To bring under the authority or influence of Rome.

   **Usage:** capitalized; historical; transitive; usually

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To make or become Roman in character or style.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive; capitalized; usually

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To make or become Roman Catholic in religion (by conversion), character or style.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive; capitalized; usually

   **Source synonyms:** Latinize

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To fill with Latin words or idioms.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** Latinize
