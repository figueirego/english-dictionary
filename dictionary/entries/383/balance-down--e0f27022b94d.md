### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To achieve a balance, usually by decreasing something, e.g., revenue.

   **Example:** By lowering costs, we can balance down to eliminate the deficit, instead of trying to increase revenue, which would be balancing up.

   **Example:** The costs of buying from that source have gone up, so let's balance down on that by buying from another source.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To calculate a balance (a total).

   **Example:** Balance up his account so he knows how much he owes.
