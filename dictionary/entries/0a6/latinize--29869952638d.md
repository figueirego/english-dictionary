### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To translate something into the Latin language; or make a word similar in appearance or form to a Latin word.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Guglielmus is a Latinized form of William.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To transliterate something into the characters of the Latin script; to Romanize

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The Cyrillic letter Л can be Latinized as L.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To render or become Roman Catholic in form or style or to diffuse Roman Catholic ideas in something.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to Latinize the Church of England
