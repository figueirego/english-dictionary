### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To support from below with props or masonry.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** You should underpin the mine roof to prevent further collapse.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To give support to; to form a basis of; to corroborate.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** Public confidence in politicians must underpin our democracy.

   **Source synonyms:** underlie
