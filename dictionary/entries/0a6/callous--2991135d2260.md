## Adjective · Record 1

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Emotionally hardened; unfeeling and indifferent to the suffering/feelings of others.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** She was so callous that she could criticise a cancer patient for wearing a wig.

   **Source synonyms:** heartless; insensitive; brutal; callous; cold; insensate (formal); hardhanded; cold-eyed; cruel; dour; thick-skinned; flint-hearted; forbidding; frosty; grim; hard; hard-boiled; hardened; hardhearted; heartless; harsh; humorless; icy; iron-hearted; insensitive; marble-hearted; rigorous; rockhearted; severe; stern; stonehearted; stony; stonyhearted; strict; tough; unsympathetic; unmoved

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Having calluses, or relating to calluses.

   **Usage:** literally

   **Source synonyms:** callousy; callusy; calloused; callused
