### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Behaviorally erratic or uncontrollable, especially of a machine or mechanical process.

   **Example:** It was working fine until it went haywire and wouldn't stop printing blank sheets.

   **Example:** Those kids go haywire when they don't get what they want.
