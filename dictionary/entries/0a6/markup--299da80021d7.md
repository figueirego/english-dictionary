### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The percentage or amount by which a seller hikes up their buy-in price when determining their selling price.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** You don’t make much money selling gas because the markup is so low.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An increase in price.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** There will be a markup on those products next week; better buy them now.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The notation that is used to indicate the meaning of the elements in an electronic document, or to dictate how text should be displayed.
