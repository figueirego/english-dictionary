### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A highway with grade-separated crossings (rather than level crossings) and designed (and only permitted) for high-speed motor-traffic (in Europe motor vehicles with a higher speed limit than 40 km/h) running in two directions on one separate carriageway each.

   **Usage:** Ireland; New-South-Wales; New-Zealand; UK

   **Source synonyms:** freeway; expressway; highway; autoroute

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A racetrack venue designated especially for the sport of auto racing.

   **Usage:** US

   **Source synonyms:** speedway
