### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Excessive; going beyond that what is natural or sufficient.

   **Example:** To individuals who despise killings in any form, death penalty is undue punishment.

   **Source synonyms:** immoderate; unrestrained; devilish; excessive; horrendous (informal); de trop (literary); extravagant; extreme; immoderate; intemperate; lavish; nimious (Scottish; obsolete); obscene; overmuch; overweening; over the top; prodigal; undue; unmeasured; unreasonable; unrestrained; wanton

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Which ought not to be done; illegal; unjustified.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Not owing or payable.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a payment etc
