### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To leave a building by swiping an identification badge; to give up or cause to give up an identification badge when it is no longer needed.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** He badges out at five every day.

   **Example:** Since it's your last day, badge out with HR before you go home.
