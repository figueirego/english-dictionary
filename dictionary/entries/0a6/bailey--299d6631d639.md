### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The outer wall of a feudal castle.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The space immediately within the outer wall of a castle or fortress.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

   **Source context (verbatim):** in certain proper names

<a id="r1-s4"></a>
