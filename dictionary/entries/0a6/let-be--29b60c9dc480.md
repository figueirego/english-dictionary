### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To not disturb or meddle with; to leave (someone or something) alone (see let it be).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Will you never let me be?

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To stop, to stop doing something; to leave off (now used alone, formerly also + infinitive).

   **Usage:** archaic; intransitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used to assign a value to a symbol.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences
