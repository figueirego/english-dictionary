### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To use a needle to pass thread repeatedly through (pieces of fabric) in order to join them together.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I sewed a button on.

   **Example:** Balls were first made of grass or leaves held together by strings, and later of pieces of animal skin sewn together and stuffed with feathers or hay.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To use a needle to pass thread repeatedly through pieces of fabric in order to join them together.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Followed by into&#58; to enclose by sewing.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to sew money into a bag
