### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To inquire into or study in order to ascertain facts or information.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to investigate the causes of natural phenomena

   **Example:** He was asked to investigate a robbery.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To examine, look into, or scrutinize in order to discover something hidden or secret.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to investigate an unsolved murder

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To conduct an inquiry or examination.

   **Usage:** intransitive
