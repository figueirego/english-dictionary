### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To spin, turn, or revolve.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** He rotated in his chair to face me.

   **Example:** The earth rotates.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To advance through a sequence; to take turns.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The nurses' shifts rotate each week.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To lift the nose during takeoff, just prior to liftoff.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The aircraft rotates at sixty knots.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of aircraft

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To spin, turn, or revolve something.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Rotate the dial to the left.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To advance something through a sequence; to allocate or deploy in turns.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To replace older materials or to place older materials in front of newer ones so that older ones get used first.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The supermarket rotates the stock daily so that old foods don't sit around.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To grow or plant (crops) in a certain order.

   **Usage:** transitive
