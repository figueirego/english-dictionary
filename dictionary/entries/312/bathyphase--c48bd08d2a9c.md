### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The time period in a cycle during which the cycle is low, especially the lower part of a sine wave fitted to a measurement of a circadian pattern or other biological rhythm.

   **Example:** The bathyphase of the metabolic process ran from late evening until shortly after the subject awoke, with the lowest point recorded in at three o'clock in the morning.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; physiology; sciences
