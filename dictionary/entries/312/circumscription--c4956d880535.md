### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of circumscribing or the quality of being circumscribed.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Anything that circumscribes or a circumscribed area.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The definition of what does and does not belong to a given taxon, from a particular taxonomic viewpoint or taxonomic system.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The circumscription in the APG system of the family Malvaceae includes the former families Bombacaceae, Sterculiaceae and Tiliaceae.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences; taxonomy

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An electoral district; used often in texts treating electoral systems in Romance countries.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
