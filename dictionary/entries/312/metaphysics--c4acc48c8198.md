## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The branch of philosophy which studies fundamental principles intended to describe or explain all that is, and which are not themselves explained by anything more fundamental; the study of first principles; the study of being insofar as it is being (Latin&#58; ens in quantum ens).

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Philosophers sometimes say that metaphysics is the study of the ultimate nature of the universe.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; philosophy; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The view or theory of a particular philosopher or school of thinkers concerning the first principles which describe or explain all that is.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** The metaphysics of Thomas Aquinas holds that all real beings have both essence and existence.

   **Example:** In Aristotelian metaphysics physical objects have both form and matter.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; philosophy; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The metalogic of physics; the logical framework of physics.

   **Usage:** broadly; uncountable

   **Example:** Even other universes should be a result of different physics. Without rules, these universes wouldn't exist, because they will have an undefined, thus impossible, nature. We will never understand or guess all possible forms of physics. That's why we have to understand the generic metaphysics.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; logic; mathematics; philosophy; sciences

   **Source context (verbatim):** from the philosophical sense

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any fundamental principles or rules.

   **Usage:** broadly; uncountable

   **Source context (verbatim):** from the philosophical sense

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The study of a supersensual realm or of phenomena which transcend the physical world.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** I have a collection of books on metaphysics, covering astral projection, reincarnation, and communication with spirits.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Displeasingly abstruse, complex material on any subject.

   **Usage:** derogatory; uncountable

   **Example:** This political polemic strikes me as a protracted piece of overwrought, fog-shrouded metaphysics!
