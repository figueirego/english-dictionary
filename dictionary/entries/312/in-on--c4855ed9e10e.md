**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Being or made privy to, a participant in, involved in, included in, or informed of (usually something private or secret, especially a plan or plot).

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** She was in on the surprise from the beginning.

   **Example:** He didn't hold the gun, but he got in on the robbery after he overheard some of us talking.
