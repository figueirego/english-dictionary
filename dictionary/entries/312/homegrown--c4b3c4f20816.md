## Adjective · Record 1

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Raised or cultivated at home, on one's own land, or in one's own country; domestic; indigenous.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Each spring they planted a garden and each summer they enjoyed homegrown vegetables.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of produce

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Produced or coming from one's own country or a particular place; local; native; self-produced.

   **Usage:** broadly; not-comparable

   **Example:** The young politician is a homegrown talent we are all proud of here.
