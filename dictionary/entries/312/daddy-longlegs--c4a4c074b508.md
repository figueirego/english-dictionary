**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The cranefly; any insect of the suborder Tipulomorpha.

   **Usage:** Ireland; UK

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

2. Any (non-spider) arachnid of the order Opiliones, mostly with long thin legs.

   **Usage:** Australia; Canada; New-Zealand; US

   **Source synonyms:** harvestman
