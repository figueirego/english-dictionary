### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A type of trap consisting of a concealed pit in the ground, into which the victim is supposed to fall and not be able to get out from.

   **Usage:** literal

   **Source synonyms:** trapping pit

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A potential, unsuspected, hidden problem, hazard, or danger that is easily encountered but not immediately obvious.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** It's usually a simple task, but you should know the pitfalls before you attempt it yourself.

   **Source synonyms:** trap

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An antipattern.

   **Source synonyms:** antipattern; dark pattern

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**
