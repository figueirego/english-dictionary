### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The capacity, condition, or state of acting or of exerting power.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** action; activity; operation

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The capacity of individuals to act independently and to make their own free choices.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** moral agency

   **Example:** individual agency

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; philosophy; psychology; sciences; social-science; sociology

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A medium through which power is exerted or an end is achieved.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** instrumentality; means

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The office or function of an agent; also, the relationship between a principal and that person's agent.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** authority of agency

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An establishment engaged in doing business for another; also, the place of business or the district of such an agency.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** management

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A department or other administrative unit of a government; also, the office or headquarters of, or the district administered by, such unit of government.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Agency for Toxic Substances and Disease Registry

   **Example:** Central Intelligence Agency

   **Source synonyms:** organ (formal); service
