## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A male given name from Hebrew, of Jewish usage, variant of Arieh (also transcribed as Aryeh, Arye).

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A diminutive of the female given names Arianna, Ariana, Ariel, Ariella, and Arielle, also used as a formal given name.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A Papuan language spoken in Western Province, Papua New Guinea.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A town in Abruzzo, Italy.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**
