### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Often followed by to or towards&#58; faithfulness or loyalty towards a person, a group, one's civic or moral duties, etc.; allegiance; (countable) an instance of this.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** the fidelity of the civil servants to their country

   **Source antonyms:** infidelity

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Often followed by to or towards&#58; faithfulness or loyalty towards a person, a group, one's civic or moral duties, etc.; allegiance; (countable) an instance of this.
   1. Faithfulness or loyalty to one's partner or spouse, including restraint from romantic or sexual relationships with third parties.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source antonyms:** infidelity; infidelity

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Often followed by to or towards&#58; faithfulness or loyalty towards a person, a group, one's civic or moral duties, etc.; allegiance; (countable) an instance of this.
   1. Faithfulness to God and one's religion.

      **Usage:** archaic; countable; uncountable

      **Source synonyms:** faith

      **Source antonyms:** infidelity; infidelity; irreligion; nullifidianism

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Accuracy, or exact correspondence, to some given fact or quality; exactness.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; uncountable

   **Source antonyms:** infidelity

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The closeness of association between an organism and a plant community.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; ecology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Chiefly in high fidelity, low fidelity, etc.&#58; the degree to which a system accurately reproduces an input such as an image or sound.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; technology

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete
