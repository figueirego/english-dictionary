**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To stop (an action); cease, desist.

   **Usage:** archaic; intransitive

   **Source synonyms:** anchor; astand (obsolete); blin (archaic; dialectal); brake; cease; desist; draw up; grind to a halt (idiomatic); halt; hold; immobilize; leave off; rest; stall; stay (obsolete); stint (archaic); stop; swike (archaic; dialectal)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To stop speaking or talking (of a subject).

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To be sparing or mean.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The next party you throw, don't stint on the beer.

   **Source synonyms:** skimp

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To restrain within certain limits; to bound; to restrict to a scant allowance.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To assign a certain task to (a person), upon the performance of which he/she is excused from further labour for that day or period; to stent.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To impregnate successfully; to get with foal.
