### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To talk to (someone) in a friendly, open, or casual manner, sometimes also in a charming or affected manner, usually to curry favor, and sometimes flirtatiously with the intention of establishing a romantic or sexual encounter or relationship with that person.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** Have you been chatting up my girlfriend?

   **Example:** He spent all evening chatting her up.
