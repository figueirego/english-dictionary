### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A dance from the big band era, a variation of the conga, in which dancers made small jumps with feet together.

   **Usage:** US; idiomatic

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A jump made where both wheels leave the ground.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** cycling; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A ground ball that hops along the field instead of rolling.

   **Example:** The first baseman misplays the bunny hop, so all runners are safe.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; baseball; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An exercise whereby the person crouches on their haunches and then extends their legs and springs up, jumping vertically into the air like a rabbit hopping.
