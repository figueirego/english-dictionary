**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To turn a device off and then back on again, usually to restore proper function by forcing a malfunctioning or unresponsive device to reset or reinitialize, or as a step in troubleshooting the device.

   **Example:** If the computer's frozen, try power-cycling it.
