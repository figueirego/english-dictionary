**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A main-sequence star of spectral type G and luminosity class V, such as the Sun.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astronomy; natural-sciences

   **Source synonyms:** G star; G-type star
