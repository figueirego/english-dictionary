**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** lifestyle; religion

   **Source synonyms:** nullifidian

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person who does not believe in deities or gods.
   1. A person who is certain that no deities exist or who thinks that the existence of deities can be disproven.

      **Usage:** strictly

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** lifestyle; religion

      **Source synonyms:** nullifidian; positive atheist; strong atheist; gnostic atheist

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A person who does not believe in deities or gods.
   1. A person who doubts the existence of deities (therefore, an agnostic may be considered an atheist).

      **Usage:** broadly

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** lifestyle; religion

      **Source synonyms:** nullifidian; weak atheist

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A person who does not believe in deities or gods.
   1. Any person lacking belief in deities (including children who are unaware of religion).

      **Usage:** very broadly

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** lifestyle; religion

      **Source synonyms:** nullifidian

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A person who does not believe in a particular deity (but may believe in another deity).

   **Usage:** uncommon

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A person who does not believe in any religion (not even a religion without gods); a nonreligious person.

   **Usage:** proscribed; sometimes
