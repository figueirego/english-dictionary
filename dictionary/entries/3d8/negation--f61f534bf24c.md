### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of negating something.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A denial or contradiction.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A proposition which is the contradictory of another proposition and which can be obtained from that other proposition by the appropriately placed addition, or insertion, of the word "not". (Or, in symbolic logic, by prepending that proposition with the symbol for the logical operator "not".)

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; logic; mathematics; philosophy; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The logical operation which obtains such (negated) propositions.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; logic; mathematics; philosophy; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A negative particle; a word, particularly an adverb, with a negative meaning&#58; for instance, no and not in English, nōn, haud and nē in Latin, οὐ and μή in ancient Greek.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences
