## Verb

### Forms

- reaches out — present; singular; third-person

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To extend one's hand(s) forward; to reach for something.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** He reached out to catch the falling snowflake.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To ask for help.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** His drug overdose was just him reaching out to you.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To make more friends, to increase one's group of friends or acquaintances.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Volunteering projects can be good ways to reach out.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To try to contact someone, especially in order to give or get information, help, support, etc.

   **Usage:** intransitive; with-to

   **Example:** Feel free to reach out if you need someone to talk to.

   **Example:** We're reaching out to key clients to inform them of our new promotional package.
