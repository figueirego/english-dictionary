**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To leave (a person, usually a man) sexually frustrated by denying them an orgasm or abruptly ending sexual contact.

   **Usage:** slang; transitive; vulgar

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To lead someone on or tease them with the promise of something desirable without actually delivering it, causing them to feel frustrated and unsatisfied.

   **Usage:** broadly; idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** Stop blue-balling me and tell me what happened!

   **Source synonyms:** tease; lead on; tantalize
