### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To conduct (oneself) well, or in a given way; to conform.

   **Usage:** reflexive

   **Example:** You need to behave yourself, young lady.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To act, conduct oneself in a specific manner; used with an adverbial of manner.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** He behaves like a child whenever she's around.

   **Example:** How did the students behave while I was gone?

   **Example:** My laptop has been behaving erratically ever since you borrowed it.

   **Source synonyms:** deport; tread; acquit

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To conduct, manage, regulate (something).

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To act in a polite or proper way.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** His mother threatened to spank him if he didn't behave.
