### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To collapse heavily or helplessly.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Exhausted, he slumped down onto the sofa.

   **Source synonyms:** plump

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To decline or fall off in activity or performance.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Real estate prices slumped during the recession.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To slouch or droop.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To lump; to throw together messily.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To fall or sink suddenly through or in, when walking on a surface, as on thawing snow or ice, a bog, etc.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To cause to collapse; to hit hard; to render unconscious; to kill.

   **Usage:** slang; transitive

## Noun · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /slʌmp/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Forms

- slumps — plural

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A heavy or helpless collapse; a slouching or drooping posture; a period of poor activity or performance, especially an extended period.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. A heavy or helpless collapse; a slouching or drooping posture; a period of poor activity or performance, especially an extended period.
   1. A period when a person goes without the expected amount of sex or dating.

      **Usage:** broadly; slang

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. A period of poor performance.

   **Example:** If she doesn't pull out of her slump, her job may be in jeopardy.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. A measure of the fluidity of freshly mixed concrete, based on how much the concrete formed in a standard slump cone sags when the cone is removed.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. A form of mass wasting in which a coherent mass of loosely consolidated materials or a rock layer moves a short distance down a slope.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; geology; natural-sciences

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. A crater or depression (an area where the ground slumps) which forms as a result of such wasting. (A large crater is colloquially called a megaslump.)

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; geology; natural-sciences

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

**Source sense R2.S7**

7. A boggy place.

   **Usage:** UK; dialectal

<a id="r2-s8"></a>

**Source sense R2.S8**

8. The noise made by anything falling into a hole, or into a soft, miry place.

   **Usage:** Scotland

<a id="r2-s9"></a>

**Source sense R2.S9**

9. The gross amount; the mass; the lump.

   **Usage:** Scotland

<a id="r2-s10"></a>

**Source sense R2.S10**

10. A cobbler-like dessert cooked on a stove.

   **Example:** a blackberry slump
