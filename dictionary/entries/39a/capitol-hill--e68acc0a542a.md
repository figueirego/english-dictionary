## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The hill in Washington, D.C., on which the Capitol is located, where Congress holds its sessions.

   **Example:** She spent a great deal of time culturing new contacts on Capitol Hill.
