### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To rip away from; to pull a piece from forcibly.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Do not tear off the price tag if you want to return that shirt.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To become detached by tearing or ripping.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To leave or depart rapidly.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The fugitive tore off down the alley.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To remove all of a roof (shingles, etc.) down to the wood deck.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; construction; manufacturing; roofing

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** detach — to rip away from; remove; rip off; flee — to leave or depart rapidly
