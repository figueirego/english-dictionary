### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Standing or set apart.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The Leaning Tower of Pisa is a freestanding bell tower.

   **Source synonyms:** self-standing; stand-alone; isolated; insulated; detached; individuated; separated

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Not attached to anything.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Not supported by or on anything; independent.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** a freestanding hospital not associated with any university medical school

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Of a computer program&#58; that runs directly on a computer's hardware, without being managed by an operating system.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; programming; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** stand-alone
