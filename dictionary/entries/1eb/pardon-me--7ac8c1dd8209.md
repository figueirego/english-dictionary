## Phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Sorry; said as an apology.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** Pardon me for stepping on your foot.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Polite expression used to soften a contradiction.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** Pardon me, but that is not true.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Polite expression to ask someone to repeat an utterance.

   **Usage:** Canada; US; idiomatic

   **Example:** Pardon me, I didn't hear you.

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** come again — polite expression to get someone to repeat; pardon; what
