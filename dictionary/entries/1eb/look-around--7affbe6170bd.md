## Verb

### Forms

- looks around — present; singular; third-person
- looking around — participle; present
- looked around — participle; past

- look round — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To inspect a building or area.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** We're interested in buying this house. Can we look around tomorrow?

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To search a place.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I can't find my keys, so I'll look around.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To turn one's head to see what is behind oneself.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** He heard a voice and looked around to see a man wearing dark clothes.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To take note of the situation; to make oneself aware.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see look, around.
