## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

- not guilties — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A formal plea by a defendant of not being culpable for the crime with which the defendant is charged.

   **Example:** If you do not plead, a plea of not guilty will be entered for you.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

   **Source synonyms:** innocent

   **Source antonyms:** guilty

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A verdict or formal finding by the legal system that a defendant is not culpable for the crime with which the defendant was charged.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

   **Source synonyms:** acquittal; exoneration; quittance; absolution (obsolete); acquittance (obsolete); acquitment (obsolete); acquittal; assoilment (archaic); compurgation (historical); disculpation; exculpation; exoneration; not guilty; quittance; vindication

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A member of a jury or tribunal supporting acquittal, or a vote cast in support of acquittal.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A person who has been acquitted of a crime.

## Adjective · Record 2

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Of a person, legally innocent of a crime which they have been accused of in a court of law.

   **Example:** He was found not guilty because there was a reasonable doubt.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Innocent.

   **Usage:** nonstandard

   **Example:** The jury said he was not guilty. I knew he wouldn't have done something like that.
