## Noun

### Forms

- flour moths — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of several small moths of the Pyralidae family whose larvae feed on flour.
   1. a cosmopolitan pyralid moth whose larvae are pests on milled plant products.

      **Source synonyms:** mealmoth; meal moth

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of several small moths of the Pyralidae family whose larvae feed on flour.
   1. The Indian meal moth (Plodia interpunctella).

      **Source synonyms:** mealmoth; meal moth

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of several small moths of the Pyralidae family whose larvae feed on flour.
   1. The Mediterranean flour moth or Indian flour moth (Ephestia kuehniella).

      **Source synonyms:** mealmoth; meal moth
