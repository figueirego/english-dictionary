### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To be vigilant and aware often as an imperative to alert a person to danger.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** While you're in the city center, look out for the dodgy street vendors.

   **Source synonyms:** take care; watch out

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To find by looking&#58; to hunt out.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To be facing.

   **Example:** A spacious room that looks out on the sea.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see look, out.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Ellipsis of look out for (someone)

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; ellipsis; informal; intransitive

   **Example:** Hey man, I know it costs four dollars but I only have three. Can you please look out?

   **Alternative form of:** look out for
