### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A discussion about one's trade, business, or employment that only others in the same field can understand.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any language considered (or alleged to be) acceptable in a single-sex workplace (typically a workshop), but not in mixed or polite company.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** locker room talk
