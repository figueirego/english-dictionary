- spiraled — US; past
- spiralled — UK; participle; past
- spiralled — UK; past

### Senses

<a id="r3-s1"></a>

**Source sense R3.S1**

1. To move along the path of a spiral or helix.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The falling leaves spiralled down from the tree.

   **Source synonyms:** billow; gyrate; spire

<a id="r3-s2"></a>

**Source sense R3.S2**

2. To cause something to spiral.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** You need to learn how to spiral a ball.

<a id="r3-s3"></a>

**Source sense R3.S3**

3. To increase continually.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

   **Example:** Her debts were spiralling out of control.

<a id="r3-s4"></a>

**Source sense R3.S4**

4. To decline mentally.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** She spiralled into a deep depression.
