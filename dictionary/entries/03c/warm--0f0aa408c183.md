### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of a somewhat high temperature, often but not always connoting that the high temperature is pleasant rather than uncomfortable.

   **Example:** The tea is still warm.

   **Example:** This is a very warm room.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Friendly and with affection.

   **Example:** We have a warm friendship.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Having a color in the part of the visible electromagnetic spectrum between red and yellow-green.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Close to a goal or correct answer.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** Earlier you were way off, but now you're getting warmer.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Fresh, of a scent; still able to be traced.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Communicating a sense of comfort, ease, or pleasantness.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** a warm piano sound

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Ardent, zealous.

   **Usage:** archaic

   **Example:** a warm debate, with strong words exchanged

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Well off as to property, or in good circumstances; prosperous.

   **Usage:** dated; informal

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. Requiring arduous effort.

   **Usage:** archaic
