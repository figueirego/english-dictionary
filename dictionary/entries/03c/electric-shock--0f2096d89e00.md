### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An instance of subjecting (someone or something) to an electric shock.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The newer taser delivered a more powerful electric shock to the target than the older one did.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The physical reaction or shock caused by the flow of electricity through the body.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
