### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To separate (oneself); to dissolve one's association with a person, group, or situation.

   **Example:** After the scandal, the political party disassociated itself from the questionable candidate.

   **Example:** If the whole membership disassociates, the result will be disestablishment.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To separate into smaller discrete units, as with analysis.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The problem is easier to understand if you disassociate the variables.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a whole or of its parts

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To separate; to disunite; to disintegrate; to dissolve.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The fibers of this nonwoven textile disassociated when I tried to wash it.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of linked components

4. Alternative form of dissociate.

   **Usage:** alt-of; alternative; proscribed
