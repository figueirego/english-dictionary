### Pronunciation

- IPA: /lʊmp/ — source labels: Northern-England

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Something that protrudes, sticks out, or sticks together; a cluster or blob; a mound or mass of no particular shape.

   **Example:** Stir the gravy until there are no more lumps.

   **Example:** a lump of coal; a lump of clay; a lump of cheese

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A swelling or nodule of tissue under the skin or in an internal part of the body.

   **Example:** Lumps in the breasts are an indicator of breast cancer.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A group, set, or unit.

   **Example:** The money arrived all at once as one big lump sum payment.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A small, shaped mass of sugar, typically about a teaspoonful.

   **Example:** Do you want one lump or two with your coffee?

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A dull or lazy person.

   **Example:** Don't just sit there like a lump.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A fat person.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A beating or verbal abuse.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** He's taken his lumps over the years.

   **Source context (verbatim):** as plural

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A projection beneath the breech end of a gun barrel.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A kind of fish, the lumpsucker.

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. Food given to a tramp to be eaten on the road.

   **Usage:** obsolete; slang

<a id="r1-s11"></a>
