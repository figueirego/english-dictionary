### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To throw up or vomit; to eject what has previously been swallowed.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To cough up from the gut to feed its young, as an animal or bird does.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The young gulls were fed by their mother’s regurgitated food.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To repeat (information) verbatim or by rote, typically after learning it without actual comprehension.

   **Usage:** derogatory; figuratively; sometimes; transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To be thrown or poured back; to rush or surge back.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Food may regurgitate from the stomach into the mouth.
