### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The ruler of an absolute monarchy or the head of state of a constitutional monarchy.

   **Source synonyms:** sovereign

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A monarch butterfly (Danaus plexippus) and others of genus Danaus, found primarily in North America.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A police officer.

   **Usage:** Aboriginal English

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A stag which has sixteen or more points or tines on its antlers.

   **Usage:** capitalized; often

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The chief or best thing of its kind.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

   **Source synonyms:** monarch flycatcher
