**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To omit (some portion of program logic) through optimization, when it is found to be unused or unnecessary.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The loop that counts from 1 to 1,000,000 is optimized out by the compiler, because nothing happens inside the loop and the value is not used afterwards.
