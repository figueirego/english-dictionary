### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person to whom property is legally committed in trust, to be applied either for the benefit of specified individuals (beneficiaries), or for public uses; one who is intrusted with property for the benefit of another.

   **Usage:** trust law

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person in whose hands the effects of another are attached in a trustee process.
