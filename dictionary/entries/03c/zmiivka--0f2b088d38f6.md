**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A village in Beryslav urban hromada, Beryslav Raion, Kherson Oblast, Ukraine, founded in 1804 but incorporating an older settlement (Verbivka) founded in 1782.

   **Source synonyms:** Schlangendorf

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

<a id="r1-s3"></a>
