### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To interfere with.

   **Usage:** colloquial

   **Example:** Don't mess with the controls. I just got everything adjusted correctly.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To diss; to put down.

   **Usage:** colloquial

   **Example:** Hey! Quit messing with my brother.

   **Source synonyms:** tangle

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To joke around with or dupe someone, in either a friendly or unfriendly manner.

   **Usage:** colloquial

   **Example:** Don't worry&#58; I'm just messing with you.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To admire, enjoy, or approve of.

   **Usage:** colloquial; especially African-American Vernacular

   **Example:** You know I mess with you heavy.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see mess, with. To eat with.
