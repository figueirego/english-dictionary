### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. After.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; temporal

   **Example:** The discussion will be continued on the other side of the commercial break.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Older than (a specified age).

   **Usage:** temporal

   **Example:** She was on the other side of 70 and had had a hard life.

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see on, the, other, side, of.
