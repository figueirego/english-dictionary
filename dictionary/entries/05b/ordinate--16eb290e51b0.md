## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈɔː(ɹ)dɪnət/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Forms

- ordinates — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The second of the two terms by which a point is referred to, in a system of fixed rectilinear coordinate (Cartesian coordinate) axes.

   **Example:** The point (3,2) has 3 as its abscissa and 2 as its ordinate.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The vertical line representing an axis of a Cartesian coordinate system, on which the ordinate (sense above) is shown.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences
