## Adjective · Record 1

### Forms

- most on time — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Punctual or according to schedule; at the planned time.

   **Example:** Unless our train's not on time, we'll be home at three.

## Prep\_phrase · Record 2

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Punctually or according to the schedule.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The train arrived on time for the first time this week.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. By installments, payments over a period of time.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** They bought their dining-room furniture on time.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. By a player's running out of time.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Smith beat Jones on time.
