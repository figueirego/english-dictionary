### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any black or brown straight-winged insect of the order Blattodea that is not a termite.

   **Source synonyms:** roach (US)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Term of abuse.

   **Usage:** derogatory; slang

   **Example:** I want a divorce, you cockroach!

   **Source synonyms:** roach; scum

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A person or a member of a group of people regarded as undesirable and rapidly procreating.

   **Usage:** derogatory; offensive; slang

   **Example:** During the Rwandan genocide, Hutu genocidaires labelled Tutsis as cockroaches and called for their extermination.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A person from New South Wales.

   **Usage:** Australia; derogatory; especially; humorous; slang

   **Source context (verbatim):** used in rugby league

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A hard lump of brown sugar.

   **Usage:** Australia; obsolete; slang
