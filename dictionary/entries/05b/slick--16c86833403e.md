## Adjective · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Pronunciation

### Forms

- slickest — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Slippery or smooth due to a covering of liquid; often used to describe appearances.

   **Example:** This rain is making the roads slick.

   **Example:** The top coating of lacquer gives this finish a slick look.

   **Example:** His large round head was shaved slick.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Sleek; smooth.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Appearing expensive or sophisticated.

   **Example:** They read all kinds of slick magazines.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Superficially convincing but actually untrustworthy.

   **Example:** That new sales rep is slick. Be sure to read the fine print before you buy anything.

   **Source synonyms:** glib

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Clever, making an apparently hard task look easy.

   **Usage:** often; sarcastic

   **Example:** Our new process for extracting needles from haystacks is extremely slick.

   **Example:** That was a slick move, locking your keys in the car.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Extraordinarily great or special.

   **Usage:** US; West Coast slang

   **Example:** That is one slick bicycle&#58; it has all sorts of features!
