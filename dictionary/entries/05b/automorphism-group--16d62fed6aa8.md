### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The group of automorphisms of a given set or other mathematical object, with the group operation being function composition.

   **Example:** The automorphism group of a set is the set's symmetric group.

   **Example:** The automorphism group of a finite-dimensional vector space (respectively, projective space) is the space's general linear group (respectively, projective linear group).
