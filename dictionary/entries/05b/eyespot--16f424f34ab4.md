### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of various primitive light-sensitive organs or regions in many diverse organisms.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An eye-like marking on the tail of a peacock or the wing of a butterfly.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source synonyms:** ocellus

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences
