### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A statement or claim of questionable accuracy, from no known reliable source, usually spread by word of mouth.

   **Usage:** US; countable

   **Example:** There's a rumor going round that he's going to get married.

   **Example:** vile rumor

   **Example:** a rumor going round

   **Example:** vicious rumors

   **Example:** spread a rumor

   **Source synonyms:** word

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Information or misinformation of the kind contained in such claims.

   **Usage:** US; uncountable

   **Example:** They say he used to be a thief, but that's just rumor.

   **Source synonyms:** noise (informal)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Report, news, information in general.

   **Usage:** US; archaic; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Fame, reputation.

   **Usage:** US; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Clamor, din, outcry.

   **Usage:** US; obsolete; uncountable
