### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Reduction from a better to a worse state.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** decline; deterioration; worsening

   **Source antonyms:** amelioration; betterment; improvement
