   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

2. State or condition of not meeting a desirable or intended objective, opposite of success.
   1. A condition in which a specified organ does not function well enough to support life.

      **Example:** heart failure

      **Example:** congestive heart failure

      **Example:** liver failure

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
