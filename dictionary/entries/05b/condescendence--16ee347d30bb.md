### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of condescending; voluntary descent from one's rank or dignity in intercourse with an inferior; courtesy toward inferiors, condescension.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; Scots law
