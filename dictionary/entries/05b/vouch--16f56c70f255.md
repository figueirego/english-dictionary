### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To call on (someone) to be a witness to something.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To cite or rely on (an authority, a written work, etc.) in support of one's actions or opinions.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** obtest (archaic)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To affirm or warrant the correctness or truth of (something); also, to affirm or warrant (the truth of an assertion or statement).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** attest; avouch; certify

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To bear witness or testify to the nature or qualities (of someone or something).

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To back, confirm, or support (someone or something) with credible evidence or proof.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Synonym of vouchsafe (“to condescendingly or graciously give or grant (something)”).

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** vouchsafe (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To assert, aver, or declare (something).

   **Usage:** archaic; obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. In full vouch to warrant or vouch to warranty&#58; to summon (someone) into court to establish a warranty of title to land.
