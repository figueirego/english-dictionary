### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An uncultivated cherry tree of species Prunus avium, native to Europe and western Asia.

   **Source synonyms:** sweet cherry; [gean](../243/gean--90c780c17255.md)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A tree of species Prunus serotina, native to North America.

   **Source synonyms:** black cherry

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A tree of species Antidesma bunius, native to Southeast Asia and northern Australia

   **Source synonyms:** bignay
