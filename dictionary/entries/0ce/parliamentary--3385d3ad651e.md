### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of, relating to, or enacted by a parliament.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Parliamentary procedures are sometimes slow.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Having the supreme executive and legislative power resting with a cabinet of ministers chosen from, and responsible to a parliament.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The UK is a parliamentary democracy.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of a class of train which, by an act of parliament, ran both ways along a line, at least once each day, at the rate of one penny per mile.

   **Usage:** UK; historical; not-comparable
