**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To concentrate toward the essence by making more close, compact, or dense, thereby decreasing size or volume.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** An abridged dictionary can be further condensed to pocket size.

   **Example:** Boiling off water condenses a thin sauce into a soupier mixture.

   **Source synonyms:** thicken; simplify; reduce; **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** cooking; food; lifestyle; astringe (obsolete); compact; telescope; compactify (humorous); condense; densitize (uncommon); pack; press; mash; scrunch; smush; squoosh; squash; squish; squeeze; thring (British; dialectal); thrutch (British; dialectal)

   **Source antonyms:** dilute
