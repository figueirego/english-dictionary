## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A bacterial infection of the nails resulting in green or black discoloration, often linked to the hands being constantly moist or exposed to chemicals.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** chloronychia; Goldman-Fox syndrome
