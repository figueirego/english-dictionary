### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A scaly multiple fruit resulting from the ripening of an ament in certain plants, such as the hop or pine.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences

   **Source synonyms:** strobilus; cone

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An individual asexually producing sexual individuals differing from itself also in other respects, such as a tapeworm or a jellyfish; one of the forms that occur in metagenesis.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences

   **Source synonyms:** strobilus

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
