## Noun

### Forms

- mineralizers — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An element which is combined with a metal to form an ore.

   **Example:** In galena, or lead ore, sulphur is a mineralizer; in hematite, oxygen is a mineralizer.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A volatile or other substance, such as water, that facilitates the recrystallization of rocks.
