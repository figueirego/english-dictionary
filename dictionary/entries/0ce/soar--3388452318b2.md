### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To fly high with little effort, like a bird.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To mount upward on wings, or as on wings, especially by gliding while employing rising air currents.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To remain aloft by means of a glider or other unpowered aircraft.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To rise, especially rapidly or unusually high.

   **Example:** The pump prices soared into new heights as the strike continued.

   **Source synonyms:** aspire (archaic)

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To rise in thought, spirits, or imagination; to be exalted in mood.

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To increase rapidly in number, volume, size, or amount.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source synonyms:** balloon
