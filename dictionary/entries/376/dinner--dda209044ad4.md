### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The main meal of the day, often eaten in the evening.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** I had an early dinner because the breakfast in this hostel is superb.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An evening meal.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** I had some friends to dinner two nights ago.

   **Example:** Do I have to change for dinner?

   **Example:** We're about to start dinner. Phone again later.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A midday meal (in a context in which the evening meal is called supper or tea).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A meal given to an animal.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Give the dog its dinner.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A formal meal for many people eaten for a special occasion.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** My family gathers twice a year, namely at Christmas and Thanksgiving dinners.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The food provided or consumed at any such meal.

   **Usage:** uncountable
