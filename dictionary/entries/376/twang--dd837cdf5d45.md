### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The sharp, quick sound of a vibrating tight string, for example, of a bow or a musical instrument.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A particular sharp vibrating sound characteristic of electric guitars.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A trace of a regional or foreign accent in someone's voice.

   **Example:** Despite having lived in Canada for 20 years, he still has that Eastern European twang in his voice.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The sound quality that appears in the human voice when the epilaryngeal tube is narrowed.

   **Example:** nasal twang

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; phonetics; phonology; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** nasality

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A sharp, pungent taste or flavor; sometimes, a disagreeable one specifically.

   **Example:** spicy twang

   **Source synonyms:** tang

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. An annoying or stupid person; especially, a recalcitrant.

   **Usage:** slang; vulgar
