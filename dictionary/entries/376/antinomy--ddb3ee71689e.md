### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A contradiction within a law, or between different laws; also, a contradiction between authorities.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any contradiction or paradox.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** antinome (archaic; rare)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any contradiction or paradox.
   1. In the thought of the German philosopher Immanuel Kant (1724–1804)&#58; an apparent contradiction between valid conclusions; a paradox.

      **Usage:** broadly; countable; specifically; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** epistemology; human-sciences; logic; mathematics; philosophy; sciences
