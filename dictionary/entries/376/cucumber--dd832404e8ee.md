### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A vine in the gourd family, Cucumis sativus.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The edible fruit of this plant, having a green rind and crisp white flesh.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** cuke (informal); cumber (colloquial)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A person who is calm and self-possessed.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; informal; uncountable
