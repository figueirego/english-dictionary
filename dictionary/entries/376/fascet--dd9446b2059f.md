### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A wire basket on the end of a rod to carry glass bottles, etc., to the annealing furnace.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; glassmaking; manufacturing
