### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To eat (parts of) another of one's own species.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The female black widow spider is known to cannibalise the male.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To remove parts of (a machine, etc) for use in other similar machines.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to cannibalize an old laptop for parts

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To reduce sales or market share (for one of one's own products) by introducing another.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business
