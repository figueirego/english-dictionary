### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A specialized gantry crane for loading and unloading cargo ships that attaches to and moves along guides on the ship's hatchway.

   **Usage:** plural "cavallettos"

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A long pole placed horizontally at a height of 4 to 20 inches from the ground, used in training horses.

   **Usage:** plural "cavalletti"

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The punishment of being placed in a pillory and whipped.
