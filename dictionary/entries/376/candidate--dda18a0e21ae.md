### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who seeks to be elected or appointed to a position or privilege.

   **Example:** Smith announced he was the party's candidate for the next election.

   **Example:** All candidates who miss the deadline or make a spelling mistake in their applications are automatically rejected.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person who is thought likely or worthy to gain a position or privilege.

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A participant in an examination.

   **Example:** Candidates must remain silent for the entirety of the exam.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Someone or something likely or suited to undergo or be chosen for a purpose.

   **Example:** After being presented with various suitors, she decided none of the candidates were the kind of man she was looking for.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A student taking a degree who has finished the coursework but has other remaining requirements such as a dissertation.

   **Example:** a Ph.D. candidate

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** education

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The recipient of certain academic degrees, now mainly awarded in Scandinavia.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** education

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A gene which may play a role in a given disease.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; genetics; medicine; natural-sciences; sciences
