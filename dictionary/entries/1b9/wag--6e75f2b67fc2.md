### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To swing from side to side, as an animal's tail, or someone's head to express disagreement or disbelief.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** The dog wagged its tail and jumped at its owner.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To play truant from school.

   **Usage:** Australia; New-Zealand; UK; slang

   **Source synonyms:** see Thesaurus&#58;play truant

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To go; to proceed; to move; to progress.

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To move continually, especially in gossip; said of the tongue.

   **Example:** She's a real gossip&#58; her tongue is always wagging.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To leave; to depart.

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Of the tail (lower order of the batting lineup)&#58; to score more runs than expected.

   **Usage:** intransitive; slang

   **Example:** The tail wagged.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; cricket; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports
