### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To continue functioning; to maintain.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The grandfather clock keeps going for over a week without winding.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To proceed; to make progress, physically or figuratively.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To see that (a person) is continually supplied with something, or that they can progress or carry on.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I didn't think I could run any longer, but fear kept me going.

   **Example:** I'll put all this meat in my freezer, and it'll keep me going for many months.
