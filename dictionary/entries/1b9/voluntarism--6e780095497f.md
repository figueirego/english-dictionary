### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A reliance on volunteers to support an institution or achieve an end; volunteerism.

   **Usage:** US; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A doctrine that assigns the most dominant position to the will rather than the intellect.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; philosophy; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The political theory that a community is best organized by the voluntary cooperation of individuals, rather than by a government, which is regarded as being coercive by nature.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
