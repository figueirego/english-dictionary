### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The legal capacity to participate in a testamentum (will), be it as a testator, a heres (heir), or a signator (witness).
   1. The legal capacity to compose a testamentum as a testator, available to any Roman citizen sui juris who had attained puberty (i.e. males of fourteen years or older and females of twelve years or older).

      **Usage:** Roman; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

      **Source synonyms:** testamenti factio activa

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The legal capacity to participate in a testamentum (will), be it as a testator, a heres (heir), or a signator (witness).
   1. The legal capacity to receive a legatum (bequest) as a heres, unavailable to unenlisted peregrini (foreigners and Roman free provincial subjects) or, following the Lex Junia Norbana of AD 19, to Latini Juniani (freedmen whose manumissions had failed to satisfy the conditions of the Lex Aelia Sentia of AD 4).

      **Usage:** Roman; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

      **Source synonyms:** testamenti factio passiva

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The legal capacity to participate in a testamentum (will), be it as a testator, a heres (heir), or a signator (witness).
   1. The legal capacity to witness a testamentum as a signator, unavailable to women.

      **Usage:** Roman; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The legal capacity to make a will (under Scots law, anyone may be a beneficiary under a testator’s settlement).

   **Usage:** uncountable; Scots law
