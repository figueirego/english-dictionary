### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A letter that is written generically with blanks to be filled in with individual details, often sent in bulk, and frequently regarded as impersonal.

   **Example:** For my trouble, they sent me a form letter with my name misspelled.
