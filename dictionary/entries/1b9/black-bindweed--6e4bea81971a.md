### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any member of the species Fallopia convolvulus of the knotweed family, native to Eurasia, now widespread in temperate climates in western Eurasia and North America, also found elsewhere, considered noxious in agriculture.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** buckwheat; bear-bind; bind-corn; climbing bindweed; climbing buckwheat; corn-bind; corn bindweed; devil's tether; wild buckwheat

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
