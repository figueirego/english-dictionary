### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Slenderly; delicately.

   **Example:** He was slightly built, but tall.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To a small extent or degree.

   **Usage:** degree

   **Example:** He weighed slightly less than his wife who was a foot shorter.

   **Source synonyms:** a little; marginally; somewhat; not much

   **Source antonyms:** quite; very
