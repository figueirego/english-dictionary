### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An estimate that is hardly any better than a guess, often because it is based on insufficient or unreliable data.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** I'm not sure how many people were at the festival. My best guesstimate is 50,000.

   **Source synonyms:** educated guess
