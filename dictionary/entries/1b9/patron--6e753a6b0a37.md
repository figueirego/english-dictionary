### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who protects or supports; a defender or advocate.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One who protects or supports; a defender or advocate.
   1. A guardian or intercessor; synonym of patron saint.

      **Example:** St. Joseph is the patron of many different places.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An influential, wealthy person who supported an artist, craftsman, a scholar or a noble.

   **Source synonyms:** friend

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A customer, as of a certain store or restaurant.

   **Example:** This car park is for patrons only.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A protector of a dependent, especially a master who had freed a slave but still retained some paternal rights.

   **Usage:** Roman; historical

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. One who has gift and disposition of a benefice.

   **Usage:** UK

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ecclesiastical; lifestyle; religion

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A padrone.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A property owner, a landlord, a master. (Compare patroon.)

   **Usage:** historical; obsolete
