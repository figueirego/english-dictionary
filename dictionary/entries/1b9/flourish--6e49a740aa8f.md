### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To thrive or grow well.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The barley flourished in the warm weather.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To prosper or fare well.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The town flourished with the coming of the railway.

   **Example:** The cooperation flourished as the customers rushed in the business.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To be in a period of greatest influence.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** His writing flourished before the war.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To develop; to make thrive; to expand.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To make bold, sweeping movements with.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** They flourished the banner as they stormed the palace.

   **Example:** The squirrel flourished its fluffy tail about as an alarm signal after its narrow escape from the cat.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To make bold and sweeping, fanciful, or wanton movements, by way of ornament, parade, bravado, etc.; to play with fantastic and irregular motion.

   **Usage:** intransitive

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To use florid language; to indulge in rhetorical figures and lofty expressions.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To adorn with beautiful figures or rhetoric; to ornament with anything showy; to embellish.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. To execute an irregular or fanciful strain of music, by way of ornament or prelude.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. To boast; to vaunt; to brag.

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete

<a id="r1-s12"></a>

**Source sense R1.S12**
