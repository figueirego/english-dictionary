### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A mouth or aperture, such as of a tube, pipe, etc.; an opening.

   **Example:** the orifice of an artery or vein; the orifice of a wound; he entered the cave's gaping orifice

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One of the natural external openings of the human or animal body.

   **Example:** the vagina and other orifices

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A stupid or objectionable person.

   **Usage:** derogatory; slang
