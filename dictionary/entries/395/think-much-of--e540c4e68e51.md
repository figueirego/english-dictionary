### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To like; to hold in high esteem; to look upon favourably.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I don't think much of her new book.

   **Source synonyms:** think highly of; appreciate; like; care for; appreciate; bedear; cherish; esteem; hold dear; prize; think much of; treasure; value; adore; care for; cling to; cherish; dote; fancy; love; esteem; honor; idolize; lay store by; look up to; prize; hallow; put store by; put store in; [rate](../315/rate--c549779d79e5.md) (informal); regard (obsolete); respect; revere; set store by; value; venerate; worthy

   **Source antonyms:** think little of

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To consider deeply.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** When he asked to have a look at my bank statements, I didn't think much of it, but now I'm certain he was up to something no good.

   **Source context (verbatim):** in the negative
