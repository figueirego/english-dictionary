## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

- tailspins — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The rapid, uncontrollable descent of an aircraft in a steep spiral.

   **Example:** The loss of the third engine threw the plane into a tailspin.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** aeronautics; aerospace; aviation; business; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A severe mental or emotional collapse; emotional breakdown.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** Just hours after leaving the institution, she suffered another tailspin.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any sharp, sustained, often uncontrollable descent or decline.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** The present stock tailspin proves bankruptcy is imminent.
