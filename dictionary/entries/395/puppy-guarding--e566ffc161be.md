### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In various sports, playground games, etc.&#58; spending the entire game defending a certain objective and thus preventing the normal progression of play.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** In capture the flag, a player might get accused of puppy guarding if they are constantly standing on top of their own flag.

   **Source synonyms:** monkey guarding; babysitting
