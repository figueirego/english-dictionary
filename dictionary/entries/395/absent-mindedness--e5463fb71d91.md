## Noun

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The characteristic or state of being easily distracted or preoccupied.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** I don't mean to make mistakes; it's just absent-mindedness.

   **Example:** For six years they had taken care of him and loved him—watched over his outgoings and his incomings and forgiven all his absent-mindednesses.

   **Source synonyms:** absence of mind

   **Source antonyms:** presence of mind; mindfulness
