## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A philosophical school or individual accepting certain tenets fundamental to Hinduism, by various definitions&#58;
   1. The supreme authority of the Vedas.

      **Usage:** Hinduism; uncountable

      **Source antonyms:** nastika

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A philosophical school or individual accepting certain tenets fundamental to Hinduism, by various definitions&#58;
   1. The existence of atman (roughly "soul").

      **Usage:** Hinduism; uncountable

      **Source antonyms:** nastika

<a id="r1-s3"></a>
