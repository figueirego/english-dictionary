### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To be similar or the same.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Unfortunately, none of the numbers on my lottery ticket matched up with the ones drawn.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To put together, or in a pair.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** After matching up all the socks in the draw, I threw away those without a pair.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To matchmake; to set up a romantic meeting between two people.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** She thought James asked her on a date because he was interested in her, but it turned out he wanted to match her up with his brother.
