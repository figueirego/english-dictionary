### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To take apart from; to take off.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to detach the tag from a newly purchased garment

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To separate for a special object or use.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to detach a ship from a fleet, or a company from a regiment

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To come off something.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Now that the zipper has detached, my winter coat won't keep me very warm.
