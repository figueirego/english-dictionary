### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A coin placed on the eyelid of a deceased person to keep the eye closed.

   **Usage:** historical; plural-normally

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Synonym of Charon's obol (“a coin placed in or on the mouth of a deceased person as a bribe or payment for Charon, the ferryman of Hades”).

   **Usage:** Greek; Roman

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; mysticism; mythology; philosophy; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** Charon's obol (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The memorial plaque, a circular bronze plaque presented to the next of kin of British Empire personnel killed in World War I.

   **Usage:** UK; World-War-I; informal

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; numismatics

   **Source synonyms:** dead man's penny; widow's penny
