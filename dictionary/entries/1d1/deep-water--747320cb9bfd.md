### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having a great depth of water.

   **Example:** a deep-water harbour

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Carried out at great depth.

   **Example:** deep-water drilling

<a id="r1-s3"></a>
