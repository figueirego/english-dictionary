### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To pound or beat rapidly or violently.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Her heart began to throb faster as the moment approached.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To pulse (often painfully) in time with the circulation of blood.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** I have a throbbing headache.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a body part

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To exhibit an attitude, trait, or affect powerfully and profoundly.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** The bass in the song made the entire room throb with energy.

   **Source context (verbatim):** with "with"
