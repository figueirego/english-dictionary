### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The national debt incurred by a regime for purposes that do not serve the best interests of the nation, and thus considered to be a personal debt of the regime and not a debt of the state.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; international law
