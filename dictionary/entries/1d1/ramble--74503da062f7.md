### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To move about aimlessly, or on a winding course.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To walk for pleasure; to amble or saunter.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To lead the life of a vagabond or itinerant; to move about with no fixed place of address.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To talk or write incessantly, unclearly, or incoherently, with many digressions.

   **Usage:** often; with-on; about

   **Example:** Francine has a tendency to ramble when it gets to be late in the evening.

   **Example:** She often rambles about bees' behavior, both in the hive and in the field.

   **Example:** Usually I don't mind listening to her ramble on a bit; it can be soothing.

   **Example:** Last night she was rambling on about horticultural methods, which was so soothing that I nodded off.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To follow a winding path or course.

   **Example:** The river rambled through the mountains.
