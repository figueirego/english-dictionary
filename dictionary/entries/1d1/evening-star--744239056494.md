### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Any star seen in the evening, especially the planets Mercury and Jupiter.

   **Source synonyms:** Hermes

   **Source antonyms:** morning star; Apollo

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. An evening primrose (Oenothera biennis).
