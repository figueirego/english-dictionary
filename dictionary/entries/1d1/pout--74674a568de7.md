### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To push out one's lips; especially, to do so in a gesture of dismay, either serious or playful.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To thrust itself outward; to be prominent.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To be or pretend to be ill-tempered; to sulk.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To say while pouting.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** "Don't you love me any more?" she pouted.
