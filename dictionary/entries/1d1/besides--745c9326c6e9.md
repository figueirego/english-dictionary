### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Also; in addition.

   **Usage:** conjunctive; not-comparable

   **Example:** We've paid for your whole education and plenty more besides!

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Used to emphasize an additional point, especially an important or stronger reason; moreover; furthermore.

   **Usage:** conjunctive; not-comparable

   **Example:** I don't feel like going out tonight. Besides, I have to work tomorrow morning anyway.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. Otherwise; else.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** I have been to Spain but nowhere besides.

**Source sense R2.S4**

   **Usage:** not-comparable; obsolete
