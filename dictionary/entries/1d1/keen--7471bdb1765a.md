<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Often with a prepositional phrase, or with to and an infinitive&#58; showing a quick and ardent responsiveness or willingness; eager, enthusiastic, interested.

   **Usage:** Commonwealth

   **Example:** I’m keen on computers.

   **Example:** She’s keen to learn another language.

   **Example:** “Do you want to go on holiday with me?” “Yes, I’m keen.”

   **Source synonyms:** ardent; eager; prompt; hot on

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Fierce, intense, vehement.

   **Example:** This boy has a keen appetite.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Having a fine edge or point; sharp.

   **Source synonyms:** sharp

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Acute of mind, having or expressing mental acuteness; penetrating, sharp.

   **Source synonyms:** acute; penetrating; shrewd; astute; brainy; bright; brilliant; clever; educated; genius; gifted; hivey (US; slang); ingenious; intellectual; intelligent; keen; knowledgeable; piquant; quick; quick on the uptake; quick-witted; rational; sagacious; savvy; scholared; sharp; smart; talented; whip-smart; wise

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Acrimonious, bitter, piercing.

   **Example:** keen satire or sarcasm

   **Source synonyms:** biting; cutting; piercing

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Cutting, penetrating, piercing, sharp.

   **Usage:** usually; wind

   **Example:** a keen wind

   **Example:** the cold is very keen

   **Source context (verbatim):** of cold
