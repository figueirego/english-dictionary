### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of the sensory receptors of both the auditory system and the vestibular system in all vertebrates.

   **Example:** In mammals, the auditory hair cells are located within the organ of Corti on a thin basilar membrane in the cochlea of the inner ear.
