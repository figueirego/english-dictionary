### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A wall separating two cavities; a partition.
   1. Ellipsis of nasal septum (“the cartilaginous center wall of the nose separating the two nostrils”).

      **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; ellipsis

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; biology; medicine; natural-sciences; sciences

      **Source synonyms:** dissepiment

      **Alternative form of:** [nasal septum](../0cc/nasal-septum--3312f962745b.md)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A wall separating two cavities; a partition.
   1. Either of the two walls that separate the atria or ventricles of the heart into left and right chambers.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; biology; medicine; natural-sciences; sciences

      **Source synonyms:** dissepiment

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
