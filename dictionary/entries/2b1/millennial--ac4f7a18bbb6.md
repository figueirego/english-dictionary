## Adjective · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /mɪˈlɛn.ɪ.əl/ — source labels: Received-Pronunciation
- IPA: /məˈlɛn.i.əl/ — source labels: Canada; General-American

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Thousand-year-old; also (by extension, loosely) thousands of years old.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source synonyms:** millenarian

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Occurring every thousand years.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Occurring at, or relating to, the beginning or end of a millennium.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Referring to the thousandth anniversary of an event or happening.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** a millennial fair

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Often capitalized&#58; of or relating to, or characteristic of, people born in the last two decades of the 20th century from around the early 1980s to the mid 1990s (with 1996 births cited as the last Millennial year), and who reached adulthood early in the third millennium C.E.

   **Usage:** alt-of; broadly; capitalized; not-comparable; often

   **Example:** the millennial generation

   **Example:** He was suffering from a typical millennial problem&#58; Which is the correct emoji to use?

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** demographics; demography

   **Alternative form of:** or relating to; or characteristic of; people born in the last two decades of the 20th century from around the early 1980s to the mid 1990s

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Synonym of millenarian (“pertaining to the belief in an impending period of one thousand years of peace and righteousness associated with the Second Coming of Christ and his reign on earth”).

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** the millennial judgment

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity

   **Source synonyms:** millenniary (rare); millenarian (synonym; synonym-of)
