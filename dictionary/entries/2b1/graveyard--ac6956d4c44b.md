### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A tract of land in which the dead are buried.

   **Source synonyms:** boneyard (informal); Boot Hill (US; informal); burial ground; camposanto; cemetery; churchyard; crypt; [God's acre](../355/god-s-acre--d56f5ccd244b.md) (poetic); gravestead; graveyard; kirkyard (Scotland); lichyard (literary); marble orchard (slang); memorial park (US); urupa

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A final storage place for collections of things that are no longer useful or useable.

   **Usage:** broadly; figuratively

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A final storage place for collections of things that are no longer useful or useable.
   1. The discard pile, in some trading card games.

      **Usage:** broadly; figuratively; collectible card games

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A final storage place for collections of things that are no longer useful or useable.
   1. A team where players are sent when they are not useful, or a team where players become useless if sent there.

      **Usage:** broadly; figuratively

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A period very early in the morning in which there is very little activity.

   **Usage:** attributive

   **Example:** graveyard shift, graveyard slot, graveyard watch

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Synonym of suicide (“beverage combining all available flavors at a soda fountain”).

   **Usage:** US; slang

   **Source synonyms:** suicide (synonym; synonym-of)
