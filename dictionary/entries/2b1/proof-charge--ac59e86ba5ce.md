### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A charge of powder and ball, greater than the service charge, fired in an arm, as a gun or cannon, to test its strength.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; firearms; government; military; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; politics; tools; war; weaponry
