### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A custard; any of several bland, gelatinous foodstuffs, usually made from stewed fruit and thickened with oatmeal, cornstarch or flour.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A custard; any of several bland, gelatinous foodstuffs, usually made from stewed fruit and thickened with oatmeal, cornstarch or flour.
   1. A slightly tart, jelly-like food of Welsh origin, made from extensively boiling oats, then boiling down the liquid extracted from it.

      **Usage:** countable; historical; specifically; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Empty or meaningless talk, especially when used to flatter.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** jive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
