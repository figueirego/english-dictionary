### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The nearness of objects with little or no delimiter.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** apposition

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The nearness of objects with little or no delimiter.
   1. An absence of linking elements in a group of words that are listed together.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** grammar; human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

      **Source synonyms:** apposition

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The nearness of objects with little or no delimiter.
   1. An absence of operators in an expression.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Example:** Using juxtaposition for multiplication saves space when writing longer expressions. a\#92;timesb collapses to ab.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

      **Source synonyms:** apposition

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The extra emphasis given to a comparison when the contrasted objects are close together.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** There was a poignant juxtaposition between the boys laughing in the street and the girl crying on the balcony above.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The extra emphasis given to a comparison when the contrasted objects are close together.
   1. Two or more contrasting sounds, colours, styles etc. placed together for stylistic effect.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Example:** The juxtaposition of the bright yellows on the dark background made the painting appear three dimensional.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** art; arts

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The extra emphasis given to a comparison when the contrasted objects are close together.
   1. The close placement of two ideas to imply a link that may not exist.

      **Usage:** countable; rhetoric; uncountable
