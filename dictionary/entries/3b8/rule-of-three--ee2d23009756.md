<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A rule stating that, if two ratios are equal, then the denominator of the second equals its numerator times the reciprocal of the first ratio&#58; if a/b=c/d then d=(bc)/a.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** education; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A rule for clinical trials used for determining the rate of adverse side effects when no such side effects present during the course of the trial.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A rule which states that things which come in groups of three are inherently funnier or more effective than things which come in groups of other sizes.

   **Usage:** rhetoric

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** comedy; communications; entertainment; journalism; lifestyle; literature; media; publishing; writing

4. A rule which states that things which come in groups of three are inherently funnier or more effective than things which come in groups of other sizes.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Wicca; lifestyle; religion

**Source sense R1.S6**

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; programming; sciences
