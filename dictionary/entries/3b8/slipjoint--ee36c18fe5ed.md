## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A type of joint for a folding knife, involving a spring that helps hold the blade open against some pressure but does not lock it open in the sense of any positive engagement by the parts of any lock mechanism; it is thus a nonlocking type of joint.

   **Usage:** attributive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A knife or other tool with such a joint; usually a pocketknife.

   **Usage:** metonymically
