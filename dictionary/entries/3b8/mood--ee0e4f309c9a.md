### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A mental or emotional state, composure.

   **Example:** I've been in a bad mood since I was dumped by my ex-boyfriend.

   **Source synonyms:** composure; humor; spirit; temperament

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Emotional character (of a work of music, literature, or other art).

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A sullen, gloomy or angry mental state; a bad mood.

   **Example:** He's in a mood with me today.

   **Source synonyms:** huff; pet; temper

   **Source antonyms:** good humour; good mood; good spirits

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A disposition to do something, a state of mind receptive or disposed to do something.

   **Example:** I'm not in the mood for running today.

   **Source synonyms:** huff; frame of mind

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A prevalent atmosphere, attitude, or feeling.

   **Example:** A good politician senses the mood of the crowd.

   **Example:** A novel that catches the mood of prewar Berlin.

   **Source synonyms:** vibe

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A familiar, relatable feeling, experience, or thing.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Source synonyms:** big mood
