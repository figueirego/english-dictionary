### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A sheep that has been shorn for the first time

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A sheepskin or lambskin that has gone through a limited shearing process so that the fibers are of uniform depth

   **Usage:** US

   **Example:** Her coat was lined with shearling.
