### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A pigmented filament of keratin which grows from a follicle on the skin of humans and other mammals.

   **Usage:** countable; singular; usually

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The collection or mass of such growths growing from the skin of humans and animals, and forming a covering for any part or the whole body.

   **Usage:** singular; uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Specifically, the collection of hairs on the top and sides of the human head, growing from the scalp.

   **Usage:** singular; uncountable; usually

   **Example:** In the western world, women usually have long hair while men usually have short hair.

   **Source synonyms:** thatch; mop; locks

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A slender outgrowth from the chitinous cuticle of insects, spiders, crustaceans, and other invertebrates. Such hairs are totally unlike those of vertebrates in structure, composition, and mode of growth.

   **Usage:** countable; singular; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences; zoology

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A cellular outgrowth of the epidermis, consisting of one or of several cells, whether pointed, hooked, knobbed, or stellated.

   **Usage:** countable; singular; usually

   **Example:** Internal hairs occur in the flower stalk of the yellow frog lily (Nuphar).

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Any slender, flexible outgrowth, filament, or fiber growing or projecting from the surface of an object or organism.

   **Usage:** countable; singular; usually

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A locking spring or other safety device in the lock of a rifle, etc., capable of being released by a slight pressure on a hair-trigger.

   **Usage:** countable; singular; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; firearms; government; military; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; politics; tools; war; weaponry

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Any very small distance, or degree; a hairbreadth.

   **Usage:** countable; singular; usually

   **Example:** Just a little louder please—turn that knob a hair to the right.

   **Example:** I won by a hair.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. Complexity; difficulty; the quality of being hairy.

   **Usage:** singular; slang; uncountable; usually
