### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A surgical operation involving cutting into a lobe of a body organ, specifically (neurosurgery), a procedure, now largely disused, involving severing connections between the prefrontal cortex and the thalamic region of the frontal lobe of the brain to treat certain mental illnesses.

   **Usage:** historical

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences; surgery

   **Source synonyms:** leucotomy

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A surgical operation involving cutting into a lobe of a body organ, specifically (neurosurgery), a procedure, now largely disused, involving severing connections between the prefrontal cortex and the thalamic region of the frontal lobe of the brain to treat certain mental illnesses.
