## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Not having been sanitized; unsanitary.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The dirty dishes are unsanitized.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of information&#58; which has not been censored or cleaned up.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The leaked inter-office memo was unsanitized.
