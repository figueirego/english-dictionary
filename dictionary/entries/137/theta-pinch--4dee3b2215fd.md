**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A pinch (magnetic compression of an electrically-conducting filament) where the magnetic field runs down the axis of the cylinder, while the electric field is in the azimuthal direction.

   **Usage:** nuclear physics

   **Source synonyms:** thetatron; θ-pinch
