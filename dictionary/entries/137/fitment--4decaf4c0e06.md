## Noun

### Forms

- fitments — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A thing fitted to another in order to accomplish a specific purpose.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An item of permanent furniture or equipment.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The act of furnishing with fitments; an instance of such an act.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The proper positioning and orientation of a thing for it to serve its designed purpose.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The categorisation of an employee, for the purpose of calculating salary or allowances.

   **Usage:** India; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Something that suits or fits.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; uncountable
