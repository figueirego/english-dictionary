<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make broad or broader.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** broaden one’s knowledge

   **Example:** broaden the canal

   **Example:** Travel broadens the mind.

   **Source synonyms:** widen; extend

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To become broad or broader.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The roads gradually broadened as we drove into the countryside.

   **Example:** Her knowledge broadened after spending a year in South America.
