<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An area of decayed vegetation (particularly sphagnum moss) which forms a wet spongy ground too soft for walking.

   **Source synonyms:** marsh; swamp; mire; fen; slough; moor

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An area of decayed vegetation (particularly sphagnum moss) which forms a wet spongy ground too soft for walking.
   1. An acidic, chiefly rain-fed (ombrotrophic), peat-forming wetland. (Contrast an alkaline fen, and swamps and marshes.)

      **Usage:** specifically; wetland science

      **Source synonyms:** marsh; swamp; mire; fen; slough; moor

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An area of decayed vegetation (particularly sphagnum moss) which forms a wet spongy ground too soft for walking.
   1. Boggy ground.

      **Usage:** uncountable

      **Source synonyms:** marsh; swamp; mire; fen; slough; moor

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Confusion, difficulty, or any other thing or place that impedes progress in the manner of such areas.

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A place to defecate&#58; originally specifically a latrine or outhouse but now used for any toilet.

   **Usage:** Australia; Ireland; New-Zealand; UK; slang

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. An act or instance of defecation.

   **Usage:** Australia; New-Zealand; slang

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A little elevated spot or clump of earth, roots, and grass, in a marsh or swamp.

   **Usage:** US; dialectal

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

   **Usage:** US
