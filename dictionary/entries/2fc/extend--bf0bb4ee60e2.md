   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The desert extended for miles in all directions.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to extend sympathy to the suffering
