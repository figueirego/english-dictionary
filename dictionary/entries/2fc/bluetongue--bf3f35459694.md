### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A disease of ruminants, caused by Bluetongue virus of the genus Orbivirus, carried by mosquitos, midges etc.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The blue-tongue lizard.

   **Usage:** Australia; countable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Someone who is as sleepy as a lizard; a lazy person.

   **Usage:** Australia; New-Zealand; colloquial; countable
