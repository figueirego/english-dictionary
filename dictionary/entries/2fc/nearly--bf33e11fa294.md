### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In close approximation; almost, virtually.

   **Example:** He left a nearly full beer on the bar.

   **Example:** I nearly didn't go to work yesterday.

   **Example:** He was (so/very) nearly over the worst part of his college degree.

   **Example:** I've nearly completely finished reading it.

   **Example:** There's not nearly enough drink for all these people!

   **Source synonyms:** environ (obsolete)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** archaic

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

**Source sense R1.S4**
