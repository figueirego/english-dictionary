### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To bewail; to weep bitterly over; to feel sorrow for.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I deplore my neighbour for having lost his job.

   **Example:** I deplore not having listened to your advice.

   **Source synonyms:** bewail; lament; afterthink; becry; begrieve (archaic; rare); bemoan; bemourn (rare); bewail; beweep; condole (India); deplore; elegize; grieve; grieven; keen; lament; mean (Ireland; UK); mourn; pine; repent; sigh; sorrow; wail; wayment (obsolete)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To condemn; to express strong disapproval of.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I deplore how you treated him at the party.

   **Example:** The UNHCR deplores the recent events in Sudan.

   **Example:** Many people deplore the actions of the corrupt government.

   **Source synonyms:** censure; condemn; admonish; animadvert; bash; bawl out; tilt; clobber (informal); cannonade; slash; lash; blame; scorch (informal); blast; berate; berisp (obsolete); castigate; censure; charge; chastise; chew out; chide; condemn; criticize; denounce; denunciate; deprecate; derogate; dispraise; drag (slang); dress down; eat someone alive; excoriate; find fault; fulminate; get at; get down; get up (Australia; Britain); give somebody a piece of one's mind; give somebody what-for; inveigh; lace into; lambaste; lay into; lecture; light into; objurgate; pooh-pooh; put someone on blast (slang); rate; read somebody the riot act; rebuke; reprehend; reprimand; reproach; reprove; rip into; rubbish (Australia; Britain); savage; scold; skewer (figuratively); slam; slate; tear into; tell off; threap; tick off (Britain); tosh (Britain); keelhaul; upbraid; vilify; vituperate
