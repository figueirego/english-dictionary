### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Anything (originally a piece of armour) that protects the nose.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An animal's noseband.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The bridge between spectacle lenses that rests on the nose.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The part of a microscope that holds the objectives.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**
