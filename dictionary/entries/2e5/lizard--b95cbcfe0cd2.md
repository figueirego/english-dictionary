### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any reptile of the order Squamata that is not a snake or mosasaur—typically characterised by a rounded torso, a short neck with an elevated head, a long tail and four limbs, although some species are legless.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Lizard skin, the skin of these reptiles.

   **Usage:** attributive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An unctuous person.

   **Usage:** colloquial

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A coward.

   **Usage:** colloquial

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A hand forming a "D" shape with the tips of the thumb and index finger touching (a handshape resembling a lizard), that beats paper and Spock and loses to rock and scissors in rock-paper-scissors-lizard-Spock.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** games; rock-paper-scissors

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A person who idly spends time in a specified place, especially a promiscuous woman.

   **Usage:** in-compounds

   **Example:** lounge lizard; lot lizard; beach lizard; truck stop lizard
