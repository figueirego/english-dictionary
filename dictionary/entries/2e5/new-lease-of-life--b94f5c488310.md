## Noun

### Forms

- new leases of life — plural
- new lease on life — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An opportunity to live longer than was expected, due to some kind of reprieve.

   **Usage:** UK

   **Example:** The heart transplant has given him a new lease of life.

   **Example:** The facelift proposed for the old theatre may well give it a new lease of life.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An opportunity for an improved quality of life, due to some change in circumstances.

   **Usage:** UK; figuratively

   **Example:** The latest developments in drugs to treat patients with bipolar disorders have given many a new lease of life.
