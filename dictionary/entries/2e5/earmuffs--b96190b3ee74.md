### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A headphone-like device designed to cover a person's ears for protection against cold or noise. They consist of a thermoplastic or metal head-band, that fits over the top of the head, and a pad at each end, to cover the external ears.

   **Usage:** plural; plural-normally
