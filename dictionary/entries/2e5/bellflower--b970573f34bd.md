## Noun

### Forms

- bellflowers — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of many plants that produce flowers that are bell-like.
   1. Any flowering plants in the genus Campanula.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of many plants that produce flowers that are bell-like.
   1. Flowering plants in the genera, Campanulastrum, Codonopsis, or Platycodon, in the family Campanulaceae.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of many plants that produce flowers that are bell-like.
   1. Any flowering plant in genus Uvularia.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A flower of one of these plants, which is shaped like a bell.
