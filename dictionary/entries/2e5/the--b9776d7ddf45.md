### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. With a comparative or with more and a verb phrase, establishes a correlation with one or more other such comparatives.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The more I think about it, the weaker it looks.

   **Example:** The more money donated, the more books purchased, and the more happy children.

   **Example:** It looks weaker and weaker, the more I think about it.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. With a comparative, and often with for it, indicates a result more like said comparative. This can be negated with none.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** It was a difficult time, but I’m the wiser for it.

   **Example:** It was a difficult time, and I’m \{none - not any\} the wiser for it.

   **Example:** I'm much the wiser for having had a difficult time like that.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. Beyond all others.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** We went the furthest under her leadership.

   **Example:** They trusted him the most.

   **Source context (verbatim):** with a superlative adjective
