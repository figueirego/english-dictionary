### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Payment, such as property or money, paid by the bride's family to the groom or his family at the time of marriage.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source antonyms:** bride price; reverse dowry; dower; see others at bride price

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Payment by the groom or his family to the bride's family.

   **Usage:** countable; uncommon; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** bride price; reverse dowry; dower; see others at bride price

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Inheritance from a deceased husband to his widow.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** dower

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
