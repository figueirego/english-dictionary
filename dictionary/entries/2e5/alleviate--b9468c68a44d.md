### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To reduce or lessen the severity of a pain or difficulty.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** alleviate his pain

   **Example:** Alcohol is often a cheap tool to alleviate the stress of a hard day.

   **Source synonyms:** address; allay; ameliorate; assuage; ease; mitigate; quell; relieve

   **Source antonyms:** aggravate; exacerbate; intensify

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. (finance) to pay down partially.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** There are different ways to alleviate your financial debt.
