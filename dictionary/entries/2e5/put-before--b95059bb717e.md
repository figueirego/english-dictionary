### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To treat (someone or something) as more important than (someone or something else).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I'd never put your brother before you.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To display a proposal or idea to (someone) to be considered.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I'd like to put my proposal before the committee.
