### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An emergency maneuver by a submarine in which it suddenly descends, especially to considerable depth, usually in order to escape an attack or avoid a collision.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A rapid descent by a person or thing, ending in a violent collision.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
