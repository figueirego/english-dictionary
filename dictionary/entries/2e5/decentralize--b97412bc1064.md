### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To cause to change from being concentrated at one point or site to being distributed across several.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** decentralize operations

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To reduce the authority of (a governing body) by distributing that authority among several bodies.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** decentralize authority

   **Example:** decentralize decision-making

   **Example:** The government planned to decentralize power to local councils.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To become decentralized.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To bring (a suspect) to the ground by use of directed force; to tackle.

   **Usage:** US

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; law-enforcement
