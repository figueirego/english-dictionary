## Prep\_phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. able to talk with someone, either in general or about the particular topic

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** They weren't on talking terms last time we saw them.

   **Example:** It is more professional to accept differences, no matter what the grievance, and walk away on talking terms.

   **Example:** We are on talking terms with him about his business.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. in agreement or harmony with (something)

   **Usage:** figuratively; idiomatic

   **Example:** Reality and I were simply not on talking terms.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see on, talking, terms.

   **Usage:** rare

   **Example:** After completing the last lesson, the student should be on “talking terms” with anyone who discusses the subject of algebra.
