### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To throw a pitch at a much slower than expected speed.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** He changed up on Wilson after three fastballs and struck him out.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; baseball; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To confuse someone by doing something unexpected or in an unexpected way.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** I thought that we were going to study the project some more, but then he came back from the weekend and changed up, making it a top priority.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To vary.

   **Usage:** informal; transitive

   **Example:** change it up

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To change to a higher gear in a vehicle.

   **Source synonyms:** upshift
