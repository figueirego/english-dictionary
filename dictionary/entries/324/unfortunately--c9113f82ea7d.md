### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Happening through bad luck, or because of some unfortunate event.

   **Example:** He unfortunately placed his hand on a loose brick.

   **Example:** There were an unfortunately large number of misprints.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used (as a parenthetical word, often a sentence adverb) to express disappointment, compassion, sorrow, regret, or grief.

   **Example:** The houses which burned down could have been saved. Unfortunately, the fire truck had broken down on the way.
