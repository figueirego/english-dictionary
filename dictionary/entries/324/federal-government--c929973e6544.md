## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of several forms of government of federations.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The national government of the United States, in contrast to government at the state or local level.

   **Usage:** US; countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** fedgov

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The national governments of Canada and Pakistan, in contrast to government at the provincial or territorial level.

   **Usage:** Canada; Pakistan; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The national governments of Australia, Malaysia, and Nigeria, in contrast to government at the state or territorial level.

   **Usage:** Australia; Malaysia; Nigeria; countable; uncountable
