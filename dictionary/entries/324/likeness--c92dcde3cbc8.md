### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The state or quality of being like or alike.

   **Example:** I bear no likeness to my parents whatsoever.

   **Source synonyms:** similitude; resemblance; similarity

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Appearance or form; guise.

   **Example:** A foe in the likeness of a friend

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. That which closely resembles; a portrait.

   **Example:** How he looked, the likenesses of him which still remain enable us to imagine.
