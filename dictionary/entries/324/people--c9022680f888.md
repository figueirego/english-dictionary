### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. plural of person&#58; a body of persons considered generally or collectively; a group of two or more persons.

   **Usage:** countable; form-of; plural; uncountable

   **Example:** There were so many people at the restaurant last night.

   **Example:** These six people are waiting for the bus.

   **Example:** Under dictatorship, people suffered and died.

   **Source synonyms:** peeps (slang); lede; leod

   **Form of:** [person](../0e2/person--38a81e87e796.md)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Persons forming or belonging to a particular group, such as a nation, class, ethnic group, country, family, etc.

   **Usage:** collective; countable

   **Example:** a people apart

   **Example:** an industrious people

   **Example:** the indigenous peoples of Europe

   **Example:** the native peoples of Borneo

   **Source synonyms:** collective; community; congregation; folk

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A group of persons regarded as being servants, followers, companions or subjects of a ruler or leader.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Moses said, "Let my people go."

   **Example:** his people were weary

   **Source synonyms:** fans; groupies; supporters

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. One's colleagues or employees.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** I'll have my people call your people.

   **Example:** I have my people working on it.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A person's ancestors, relatives or family.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** My people lived through the Black Plague and the Thirty Years War.

   **Example:** His people live out west.

   **Source synonyms:** kin; kith; folks

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The mass of a community as distinguished from a special class (elite); the commonalty; the populace; the vulgar; the common crowd; the citizens.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The people have successfully stood up against tyranny and totalitarianism.

   **Example:** The election is over and the people have spoken.

   **Example:** The people won't tolerate so much corruption any more!

   **Source synonyms:** populace; commoners; citizenry

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. People in general, humans, by extension sentient beings real or fictional.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** People don't like it when you tweak their noses.

   **Example:** Teachers are people too.
