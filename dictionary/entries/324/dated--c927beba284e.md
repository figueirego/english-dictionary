## Adjective · Record 1

### Forms

- most dated — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Marked with a date.

   **Example:** The first dated entry in the diary was from October 1922.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Outdated.

   **Example:** "Omnibus" is a dated term for a bus.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Anachronistic; being obviously inappropriate for its present context.

   **Example:** Calling a happy person gay seems awfully dated nowadays; people will assume you mean something else.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. No longer fashionable.

   **Example:** Slang can become dated very quickly.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Alotted a span of days.

   **Usage:** obsolete
