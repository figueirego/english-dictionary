### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A sketch or account of anything in words; a portraiture or representation in language; an enumeration of the essential qualities of a thing or species.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** give a verbal description of the events

   **Example:** a realistic description

   **Source synonyms:** portrait

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The act of describing; a delineation by marks or signs.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A set of characteristics by which someone or something can be recognized.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The zoo had no lions, tigers, or cats of any description.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Sort, kind.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** A dealership with cars of every description

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A scientific documentation of a taxon for the purpose of introducing it to science.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The type description of the fungus was written by a botanist.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences; taxonomy

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The act or practice of recording and describing actual language usage in a given speech community, as opposed to prescription, i.e. laying down norms of language usage.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences
