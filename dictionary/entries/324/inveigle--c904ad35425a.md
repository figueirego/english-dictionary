### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To convert, convince, or win over with flattery or wiles.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** entice; induce; put up to

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To obtain through guile or cunning.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He inveigled an introduction to my sister from me.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To fool, to delude, to blind in judgement.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To ensnare, to entangle.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive
