### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A low soft shoe that can be slipped on and off easily.

   **Usage:** footwear

   **Source synonyms:** pantofle

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A low soft shoe intended for indoor use; a bedroom slipper or house slipper.

   **Usage:** footwear

   **Example:** Get out of bed, put on your slippers, and come downstairs.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A flip-flop (type of rubber sandal).

   **Usage:** Hawaii; India; Kenya; Malaysia; Philippines; Singapore; US; footwear

   **Source synonyms:** flip-flop; sandal; thong

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A person or creature that slips.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A kind of apron or pinafore for children.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A kind of brake or shoe for a wagon wheel.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A piece, usually a plate, applied to a sliding piece, to receive wear and permit adjustment; a gib.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A form of corporal punishment where the buttocks are repeatedly struck with a plimsoll; "the slipper".

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. The plimsoll or gym shoe used in this form of punishment.

   **Usage:** euphemistic

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. A kind of bedpan urinal shaped somewhat like a slipper.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences
