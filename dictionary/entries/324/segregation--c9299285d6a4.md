### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of setting apart and organizing things based upon their characteristics.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The separation of people based upon race, sex, religion, or other identity in institutions.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; politics

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The setting apart in Mendelian inheritance of alleles, such that each parent passes only one allele to its offspring.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Separation from a mass, and gathering about centers or into cavities at hand through cohesive or adhesive attraction or the crystallizing process.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; geography; geology; mineralogy; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The separation of people (geographically, residentially, or in businesses, public transit, etc) into racial or other categories (e.g. religion, sex).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; public policy

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; politics

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The separation of people (geographically, residentially, or in businesses, public transit, etc) into various categories which occurs due to social forces (culture, etc).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; sciences; social-science; sociology

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The separation of a pair of chromatids or chromosomes during mitosis and meiosis

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; genetics; medicine; natural-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Separation for practical reasons, by necessity.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. The separation of a subset of prisoners from the general prison population, possibly solitary confinement.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
