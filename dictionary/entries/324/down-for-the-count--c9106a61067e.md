## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Decisively beaten; defeated; rendered irrelevant for the long term.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; not-comparable

   **Example:** As the company grew, one after another of its smaller competitors ended up down for the count.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Soundly unconscious, either because asleep or because otherwise knocked out.

   **Usage:** figuratively; not-comparable

   **Example:** A&#58; Look at him, he's out like a light! B&#58; Yeah, he had a long day! And now he's down for the count.

   **Source synonyms:** out cold

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see down, for, the, count&#58; lying upon the mat of a boxing ring while the referee counts the fighter out.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Liston was down for the count more than once before rising up to regain command of the fight.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a boxer
