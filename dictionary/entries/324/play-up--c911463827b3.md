### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see play, up.

   **Example:** Our school team is playing up at the old recreation ground.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To misbehave.

   **Usage:** UK; idiomatic

   **Example:** Children who do not receive enough attention may begin to play up.

   **Example:** The radar system is playing up again.

   **Source synonyms:** act up

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To make or attempt to make something appear more important, likely, or obvious; to showcase or highlight.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** For the audition, Nicole will play up her skill as a pianist by playing Jesu, Joy of Man's Desiring by Bach.

   **Source synonyms:** overemphasize; overstate; stretch; big up (slang); cut it fat (UK; obsolete; slang); exaggerate; hyperbolize (archaic); overcharge (dated); overdo; overemphasize; overstate; play up; stretch (figuratively); three-sheet

   **Source antonyms:** play down

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To play (e.g. sport or music) more vigorously.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** I can't hear the ukuleles at the back. Play up please!

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To be unfaithful to a lover; to cheat; used with on.

   **Usage:** slang; transitive
