### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Genuine, honest, and free from pretense or deceit; heartfelt.

   **Example:** I believe he is sincere in his offer to help.

   **Source synonyms:** earnest

   **Source antonyms:** insincere

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Meant truly or earnestly.

   **Example:** She gave it a sincere if misguided effort.

   **Source synonyms:** devout (formal)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Clean; pure.

   **Usage:** archaic
