### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A verbal or written request, or a scheduled time, for actors or models to be available for the application of cosmetics, hairstyling, etc. prior to a performance or photography session.

   **Usage:** modeling

   **Example:** The director scheduled the makeup call for 30 minutes before the play started.

   **Example:** The model had to rush to get to her 8&#58;30 makeup call.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** acting; broadcasting; entertainment; film; lifestyle; media; television; theater
