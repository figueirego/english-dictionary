### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having the duty of taking care of something; answerable for an act performed or for its consequences; accountable; amenable, especially legally or politically.

   **Usage:** postpositional

   **Example:** Parents are responsible for their child's behaviour.

   **Source context (verbatim):** followed by "for"

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Being a primary cause of a situation or action and thus able to be blamed or credited for it.

   **Usage:** postpositional

   **Example:** Who is responsible for this mess?

   **Source context (verbatim):** followed by "for"

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Answerable to (a superior).

   **Source context (verbatim):** followed by "to"

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Involving important duties; involving a degree of personal accountability on the part of the person concerned.

   **Example:** She has a responsible position in the firm.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a job or position

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Having good judgment in decision-making.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Able to be trusted; reliable; trustworthy.

   **Example:** He looks like a responsible guy.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Capable of rational conduct and thus morally accountable for one's behavior.
