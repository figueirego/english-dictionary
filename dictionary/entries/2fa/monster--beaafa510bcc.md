   1. A badly behaved person, especially a child; a brat.

      **Usage:** figuratively; humorous

      **Source synonyms:** beast

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A terrifying and dangerous creature, especially one of an imaginary or fictional kind.
   1. A prodigy; someone very talented in a specific domain.

      **Usage:** informal

      **Example:** That dude playing guitar is a monster.

      **Source synonyms:** beast

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A creature of unnatural or highly unusual shape or form (which makes a frightening or unpleasant impression).
   1. Something very or unusually large.

      **Usage:** informal

      **Example:** Have you seen those powerlifters on TV? They're monsters.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A creature of unnatural or highly unusual shape or form (which makes a frightening or unpleasant impression).
   1. A deformed animal or person (especially, a severely deformed one); in previous centuries often taken as an ill omen at the time of its birth. (Offensive when applied to humans in modern usage.)

      **Usage:** archaic

      **Example:** The villagers were worried because the weather had been strange and several monsters had been born among the flocks and people.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**
