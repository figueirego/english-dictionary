### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To continue; to proceed; to go on.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** After spending the night resting in an abandoned church, the group decided to move on in their quest.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To start dealing with something else.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** The best revenge is always to just happily move on and let karma do the rest.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To attempt a seduction, often in a way seen as disloyal or rude to another.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; informal; transitive

   **Example:** I think Shane is going to move on my ex tonight.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To physically move something or someone.

   **Example:** The police moved the protestors on.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To assault or attack.

   **Usage:** transitive; African-American Vernacular
