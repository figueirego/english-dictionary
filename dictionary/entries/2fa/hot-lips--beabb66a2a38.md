## Noun · Record 2

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A person who kisses or is said to kiss passionately.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. An attractive or sexy person.
