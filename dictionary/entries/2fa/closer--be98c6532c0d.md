## Noun · Etymology 2 · Record 3

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈkləʊ.zə/ — source labels: UK
- IPA: /ˈkloʊ.zɚ/ — source labels: General-American

### Forms

- closers — plural

### Senses

<a id="r3-s1"></a>

**Source sense R3.S1**

1. Someone or something that closes.

   **Example:** In our organization, the VP of Sales usually acts as the closer.

<a id="r3-s2"></a>

**Source sense R3.S2**

2. Someone or something that concludes.

   **Example:** The DJ chose a fantastic track as his closer at the end of the night.

<a id="r3-s3"></a>

**Source sense R3.S3**

3. Synonym of close (“the point at the end of a sales pitch when the consumer is asked to buy”).

   **Usage:** sales

   **Source synonyms:** [close](../0c4/close--310ff200149b.md) (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r3-s4"></a>

**Source sense R3.S4**

4. The last stone in a horizontal course, if smaller than the others; a piece of brick finishing a course.

<a id="r3-s5"></a>

**Source sense R3.S5**

5. A relief pitcher who specializes in getting the last three outs of the game.

   **Example:** They brought their closer in for the ninth.
