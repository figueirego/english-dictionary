### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To have an absolute requirement for.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Living things need water to survive.

   **Example:** You do not always need to go to the library to study. You may use the Internet.

   **Example:** She needed convincing that I was what she really needed.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To want strongly; to feel that one must have something.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** After ten days of hiking, I needed a shower and a shave.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To be obliged or required (to do something).

   **Usage:** modal

   **Example:** I need not go if I don't want to, need I?

   **Example:** I was wondering if I need fill in more forms. - No, that's the only one you need fill out.

   **Example:** I need never have packed my set of sunglasses - it rained all day every day.

   **Usage:** intransitive
