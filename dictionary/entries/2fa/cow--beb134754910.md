### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An adult female of the species Bos taurus, especially one that has calved.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; strictly

   **Example:** The whole herd is out to pasture right now — the cows, calves, and heifers are out back, and the bulls are down by the creek.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any member of the species Bos taurus that is not of a bucking breed or fighting breed, regardless of sex or age, including steers and calves.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; informal; uncountable

   **Example:** We saw lots of cows at the farm show, including some surprisingly gigantic bulls.

   **Source synonyms:** cattle beast

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Beef&#58; the meat of cattle as food.

   **Usage:** uncommon; uncountable

   **Example:** The only meat I eat is cow.

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any bovines or bovids generally, including yaks, buffalo, etc.

   **Usage:** countable; uncommon; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A female member of other large species of mammal, including the bovines, moose, whales, seals, hippos, rhinos, manatees, and elephants.
