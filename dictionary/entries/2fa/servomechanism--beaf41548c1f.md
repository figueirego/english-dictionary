### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A mechanical device for controlling large amounts of power by means of smaller amounts of power and correcting the performance of the device using feedback.

   **Source synonyms:** servo

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any system which controls motion automatically using feedback.
