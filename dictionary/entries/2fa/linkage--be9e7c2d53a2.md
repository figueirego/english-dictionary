### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A mechanical device that connects things.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** A linkage in my car's transmission is broken so I can't shift out of first gear.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A connection or relation between things or ideas.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** association; bond; connection; link; relationship

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The act or result of linking&#58; the combination of multiple object files into one executable, library, or object file.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences; software-compilation

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The property of genes of being inherited together.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; genetics; medicine; natural-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A set of definitely related languages for which no proto-language can be derived, typically a group of languages within a family that have formed a sprachbund.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A United States foreign policy, during the 1970s détente in the Cold War, of persuading the Soviet Union to co-operate in restraining revolutions in the Third World in return for nuclear and economic concessions.

   **Usage:** US; countable; historical; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; politics
