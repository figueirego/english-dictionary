### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. At an angle to the horizontal; slanted or sloped.

   **Example:** The take-off ramp was inclined at 20 degrees.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Having a tendency, preference, likelihood, or disposition.

   **Example:** She's inclined to lose her temper.
