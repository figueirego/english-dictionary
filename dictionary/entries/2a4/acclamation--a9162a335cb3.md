### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A shout of approbation, favor, or assent; eager expression of approval; loud applause.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** acclaim

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The act of winning an election to a post because there were no other candidates.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** With no one running against her, she won by acclamation.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A representation, in sculpture or on medals, of people expressing joy.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** art; arts

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An oral vote taken without formal ballot and with much fanfare; typically an overwhelmingly affirmative vote.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; politics

   **Source synonyms:** voice vote
