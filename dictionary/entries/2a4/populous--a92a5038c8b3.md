### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having a large population.

   **Example:** China is the most populous country in the world.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Spoken by a large number of people.

   **Example:** Chinese is the most populous language.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a language

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Densely populated.

   **Example:** The Nile delta is a populous region.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Crowded with people.

   **Example:** Airport departure halls are often populous places during the rush hours.
