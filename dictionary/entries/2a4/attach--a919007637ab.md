### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To fasten, to join to (literally and figuratively).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** You need to attach the carabiner to your harness.

   **Example:** An officer is attached to a certain regiment, company, or ship.

   **Source synonyms:** connect; annex; affix; unite; accouple (obsolete); affix; assemble; associate; attach; bewed; bind; clasp; clinch; combine; conglomerate; conglutinate; conjoin; connect; construct; couple; entwine (figuratively); fay; fix; graft; hitch; hook; inosculate; interlink; join; joint; knit (figuratively); knot; link; marry (figuratively); piece together; piecen; pinion; put together; splice; unite; wed (figuratively); yoke

   **Source antonyms:** detach; unfasten; disengage; separate; decouple; detach; disconnect; disengage; rip off; tear off; unconnect; uncouple; unfasten; unhitch

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To adhere; to be attached.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source synonyms:** cling; stick; adhere; attach; bond; cleave; cling; cohere (formal); conglutinate; glue (figuratively); hug; stick; stick together; stick with

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To include an attachment with a communication (especially an email or other electronic communication).

   **Example:** I've attached the contract to this email.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To come into legal operation in connection with anything; to vest.

   **Example:** Dower will attach.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To win the heart of; to connect by ties of love or self-interest; to attract; to fasten or bind by moral influence; with to.

   **Example:** attached to a friend; attaching others to us by wealth or flattery

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To connect, in a figurative sense; to ascribe or attribute; to affix; with to.

   **Example:** to attach great importance to a particular circumstance

   **Example:** Some of the guilt must attach to the parents.

   **Source synonyms:** chalk up to; draw; impute; accredit; ascribe; attach (figuratively); attribute; chalk up to (idiomatic); charge; draw (figuratively; obsolete); impute; lay; put down to (idiomatic); repose (obsolete)

   **Usage:** obsolete
