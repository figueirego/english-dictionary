### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Promoting the good of one division, department or subgroup over that of the whole.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Promoting the good of one region over that of the nation.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Sectionalism is tearing the country apart. Everyone wants the other regions to do the work and pay the taxes while they reap the benefits.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; politics
