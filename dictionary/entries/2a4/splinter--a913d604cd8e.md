**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To come apart into long sharp fragments.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The tall tree splintered during the storm.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To cause to break apart into long sharp fragments.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** His third kick splintered the door.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To break, or cause to break, into factions.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** The government splintered when the coalition members could not agree.
