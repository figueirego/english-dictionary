## Noun · Etymology 2 · Record 2

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A hard slap on bare skin with a hand that leaves a red, fully hand-shaped mark.

   **Example:** He still owes me a five-star so I try not to let him catch me with my shirt off.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. A mark from such a slap.

   **Example:** Look at that five-star on my back! That bastard who gave it to me really has it coming.
