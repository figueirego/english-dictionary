### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An exchange of goods or services without the use of money.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** We had no money so we had to live by barter.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The goods or services used in such an exchange.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** The man used his watch as barter to pay for his tab.
