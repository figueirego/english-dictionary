## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An architectural style most commonly used in platforms, temples, and pyramids in pre-Columbian Mesoamerica, consisting of an inward-sloping surface or panel (the talud) with a panel or structure perpendicular to the ground sitting upon the slope (the tablero).

   **Usage:** uncountable
