### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A series of bones situated at the back from the head to the pelvis of a human, or from the head to the tail of an animal, enclosing the spinal cord and providing support for the thorax and abdomen.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; biology; medicine; natural-sciences; sciences; zoology; zootomy

   **Source synonyms:** backbone; spinal column; vertebral column

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A series of bones situated at the back from the head to the pelvis of a human, or from the head to the tail of an animal, enclosing the spinal cord and providing support for the thorax and abdomen.
   1. Courage or assertiveness.

      **Usage:** figuratively

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; biology; medicine; natural-sciences; sciences; zoology; zootomy

      **Source synonyms:** backbone; spinal column; vertebral column

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Something resembling a backbone, such as a ridge, or a long, central structure from which other structures radiate.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The narrow, bound edge of a book that encloses the inner edges of the pages, facing outwards when the book is on a shelf and typically bearing the title and the author's and publisher's name.

   **Source synonyms:** back

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A pointed, fairly rigid protuberance or needlelike structure on an animal, shell, mushroom or plant. The botanical term technically refers to such a structure derived from a leaf or part of a leaf.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; mycology; natural-sciences; zoology; zootomy

   **Source synonyms:** needle; prickle; quill; spicule; spike; thorn; virgula (obsolete)

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences
