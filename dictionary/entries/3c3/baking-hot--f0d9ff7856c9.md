## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see baking, hot&#58; very hot in a way that involves baking (in an oven or as if in an oven).

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Watch out for that exhaust pipe! It's baking hot!

   **Example:** Watch out — the chrome parts of the car are baking hot from the sun.

   **Source synonyms:** baking; roasting; baked
