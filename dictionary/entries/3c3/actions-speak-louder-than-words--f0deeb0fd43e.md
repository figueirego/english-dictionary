### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. It is more effective to act directly than to speak of action.

   **Source antonyms:** the pen is mightier than the sword

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An individual's actions indicate their true sentiments more reliably than their claims do.
