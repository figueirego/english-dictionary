### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Anticlockwise, counter-clockwise.

   **Usage:** archaic; not-comparable; now chiefly fantasy or witchcraft

   **Example:** It is unlucky to walk widdershins around a church.

   **Source antonyms:** clockwise; sunwise; deasil

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The wrong way.

   **Usage:** Scotland; not-comparable
