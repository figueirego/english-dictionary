## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A village and civil parish in Cotswold district, Gloucestershire, England, adjacent to the River Evenlode (OS grid ref SP2229). It was in a detached part of Worcestershire until 1931.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A river with its source in Gloucestershire, England, which flows into the River Thames in Oxfordshire.
