## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A process by which an amalgam of gold is applied to metallic surfaces, the mercury being subsequently volatilized, leaving a film of gold or an amalgam containing from 13 to 16% of mercury.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** amalgam gilding; mercury gilding
