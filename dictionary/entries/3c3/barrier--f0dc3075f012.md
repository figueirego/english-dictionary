### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A structure that bars passage.

   **Example:** The bus went through a railway barrier and was hit by a train.

   **Example:** The bomber had passed through one checkpoint before blowing himself up at a second barrier.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An obstacle or impediment.

   **Example:** Even a small fee can be a barrier for some students.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A boundary or limit.

   **Example:** Few marathon runners break the three-hour time barrier.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A node (in government and binding theory) said to intervene between other nodes A and B if it is a potential governor for B, c-commands B, and does not c-command A.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A separation between two areas of the body where specialized cells allow the entry of certain substances but prevent the entry of others.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; physiology; sciences
