### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To constrain someone by force or by social, moral or legal means.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Your carelessness obliges firmness on my part.

   **Example:** I am obliged to report to the police station every week.

   **Source synonyms:** necessitate (formal)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To do (someone) a service or favour (hence, originally, creating an obligation).

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** He obliged me by not parking his car in the drive.

   **Example:** The singer obliged(us) with an encore.

   **Source synonyms:** accommodate

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To cause somebody to feel indebted.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I am much obliged to you for your recent help.
