### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To sink or fall to the bottom; to settle, as lees.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To fall downward; to become lower; to descend; to sink.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To fall into a state of calm; to be calm again; to settle down; to become tranquil.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The sea subsides.

   **Example:** The tumults of war will subside.

   **Example:** The fever has subsided.

   **Source synonyms:** abate

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To cease talking.

   **Usage:** colloquial; intransitive
