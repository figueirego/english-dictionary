### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The status of a revolutionary class where it continues to push for and achieve its interests despite the political dominance of those with opposing interests.

   **Usage:** Marxism; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The theory that socialism can be obtained without the country first experiencing a period of capitalism. The revolution must furthermore become an agent for other anticapitalist revolutions around the globe, in part to secure the revolution abroad.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** communism; government; human-sciences; ideology; philosophy; politics; sciences

   **Source antonyms:** stagism
