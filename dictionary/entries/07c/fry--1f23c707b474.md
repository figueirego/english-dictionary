<a id="r1-s4"></a>

   1. To suffer because of too much heat.

      **Usage:** colloquial; intransitive

      **Example:** You'll fry if you go out in this sun with no sunblock on.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To affect or be affected by extreme heat or current.
   1. To execute, or be executed, by the electric chair.

      **Usage:** US; ambitransitive; slang

      **Example:** He's guilty of murder&#58; he's going to fry.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To affect or be affected by extreme heat or current.
   1. To destroy (something, usually electronic), often with excessive heat, voltage, or current.

      **Usage:** informal; transitive

      **Example:** If you apply that much voltage, you'll fry the resistor.

      **Example:** Taking drugs for years fried his brain.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To make someone laugh a lot, to crack someone up.

   **Usage:** slang; transitive

   **Example:** This joke is absolutely frying me.
