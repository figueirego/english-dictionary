### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Not containing animal products (meat, eggs, milk, leather, etc) or inherently involving animal use.

   **Usage:** especially; not-comparable

   **Example:** He eats a completely vegan diet.

   **Example:** This chocolate cake is vegan.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** food; lifestyle

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a product or practice

   **Source synonyms:** plant-based

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Committed to avoiding any product or practice that inherently involves animal use.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** She is not vegan as she eats eggs and wears leather.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a person

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Relating to vegans or veganism.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Yesterday I went to a vegan party.

   **Example:** She is interested in vegan philosophy.

## Noun · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈviːɡən/ — variety unspecified in the source labels
- IPA: /ˈveɪɡən/ — source labels: nonstandard; rare

### Forms

- vegans — plural

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A person who does not eat, drink or otherwise consume any animal products

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. A person committed to avoiding products and practices that inherently involve animal use, including all foods containing animal products, and to abstaining from direct and intentional harm to animals as far as possible; an adherent to veganism.

   **Example:** Our vegan-friendly shaving brush is made with synthetic bristles instead of badger hair.
