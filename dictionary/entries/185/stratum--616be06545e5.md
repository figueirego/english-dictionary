### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Source synonyms:** tier

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A layer of sedimentary rock having approximately the same composition throughout.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; geology; natural-sciences

   **Source synonyms:** bed; layer

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

<a id="r1-s4"></a>
