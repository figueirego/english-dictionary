### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To waste time doing pointless or silly things

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** What are you pratting about with now? Get your homework done.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To fail to do something correctly, and so consider the time to be wasted.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** I spent the weekend pratting about with the central heating and got absolutely nowhere with it!
