### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A sandwich consisting of a piece of boneless, sometimes fried chicken served in a bun, typically hot (called a chicken sandwich in the US).

   **Usage:** Ireland; UK

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A sandwich consisting of a patty made from ground chicken, served in a bun, typically hot and often with other ingredients.

   **Usage:** US

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The chicken patty used in such a sandwich.
