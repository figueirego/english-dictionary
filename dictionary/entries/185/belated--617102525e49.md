### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Affected or overtaken by the lateness of the time; hence, synonym of benighted (“overtaken by night; caught out by oncoming night before reaching one's destination”).

   **Source synonyms:** lated; nighted

   **Source antonyms:** unbelated

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Later in relation to the proper time which something should have happened; specifically, delayed beyond the proper time.

   **Example:** A belated happy birthday to you!

   **Source synonyms:** lated; tardive; tardy; late; [backward](../11e/backward--47823b565d4a.md); behindhand; belated; late; lated (obsolete); overdue; tardy

   **Source antonyms:** unbelated

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Slow to learn or to adopt a trend; hence, lacking education or sophistication; benighted, unenlightened, unsophisticated.

   **Usage:** figuratively
