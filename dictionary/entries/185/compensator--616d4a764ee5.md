## Noun

### Forms

- compensators — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A party or thing which compensates, pays or otherwise provides a compensation

   **Usage:** literally

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any device used to compensate for something, notably to achieve a better balance.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A building control device used to deliver the compensation relationship or curve between the air temperature outside a building and the temperature of water or air provided to spaces or zones within the building for purposes of heating or cooling.

   **Example:** Compensators ensure less heat is provided to a room on a warmer day. They may be implemented mechanically, electronically or in software.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. One of several small magnets placed in the binnacle to neutralize the effect of the ship's metal on the compass

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport
