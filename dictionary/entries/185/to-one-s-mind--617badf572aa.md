### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. From one's point of view, in one's opinion.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** To my mind if you’ve talked to Stacy then you’ve talked to Beth, since they tell each other everything.

   **Example:** To her mind, the only thing worse than public speaking is public dancing.

   **Source synonyms:** in my view; in someone's eyes; in one's book; in one's opinion

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. According to one's desire or preference.

   **Usage:** archaic; idiomatic

   **Source synonyms:** to someone's liking; to someone's taste
