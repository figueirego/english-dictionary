### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A deity or other supernatural being said to have been worshipped by certain West African peoples; an idol.

   **Usage:** countable; historical; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any object of superstition; religious words and/or actions which are seen as superstitious or fraudulent.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Rev. Quack's healing services are little more than mumbo jumbo.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** lifestyle; religion

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any confusing or meaningless speech; nonsense, gibberish.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Football lingo is mumbo jumbo to me.
