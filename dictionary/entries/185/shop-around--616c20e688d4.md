### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To actively search and compare options before making a selection or choice of vendor.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** He's gone to shop around for a new car.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To actively make a search for an alternative choice of something one has, owns, or wants, without it necessarily involving buying or selling anything.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** She's shopping around for a new boyfriend.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To put something out on offer to multiple parties in the hope one will choose it.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He's shopping around his book to several studios. He hopes to get a movie deal.
