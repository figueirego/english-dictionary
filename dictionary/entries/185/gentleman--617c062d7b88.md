### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any well-bred, well-mannered, or charming man.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any man.

   **Usage:** broadly; polite; term-of-address

   **Example:** Please escort this gentleman to the gentlemen's room.

   **Source synonyms:** sahib

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A man of gentle but not noble birth, particularly a man of means (originally ownership of property) who does not work for a living but has no official status in a peerage; (UK law) an armigerous man ranking below a knight.

   **Usage:** historical

   **Example:** Being a gentleman, Robert was entitled to shove other commoners into the gongpit but he still had to jump out of the way of the knights to avoid the same fate himself.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An effeminate or oversophisticated man.

   **Usage:** derogatory

   **Example:** Well, la-di-da, aren't you just a proper gentleman?

   **Source synonyms:** cockney; puss-gentleman; sissy; bading (Philippines); big girl's blouse (Australia; Ireland; New-Zealand; UK; derogatory); cockney (obsolete); cot-quean (derogatory; obsolete); cupcake (slang); dudelet (archaic; derogatory); fakaleiti; fairy (derogatory); femboy; gentleman (derogatory); ginch (Australia; Ireland; New-Zealand; UK; slang); girl's blouse (derogatory; slang); girlyman (derogatory; slang); janegirl (rare); jessie (dated); nancy (derogatory; slang); nancyboy (derogatory; slang); nancy pants; pansy (derogatory); Percy (derogatory; slang); poof (Commonwealth; derogatory); poofter (Commonwealth; dated; derogatory); posy sniffer (derogatory; slang); pretty boy; puss-gentleman (obsolete); quiche-eater (idiomatic); sissy (derogatory); softling (archaic); soy boy (derogatory; slang); swish (derogatory; slang); twink (derogatory; slang); zenana (South-Asia)

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An amateur or dabbler in any field, particularly those of independent means.

   **Usage:** derogatory; historical; sometimes; usually

   **Source synonyms:** dilettante

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. An amateur player, particularly one whose wealth permits him to forego payment.
