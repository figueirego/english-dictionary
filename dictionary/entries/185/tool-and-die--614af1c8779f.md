## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An area of activity (concentration or specialty) in manufacturing, comprising toolmaking, diemaking, and (usually also) moldmaking.

   **Usage:** attributive; uncountable; usually

   **Example:** The toolmakers are employed in tool and die shops.

   **Example:** They work in tool and die.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The tools and dies made by this specialty.

   **Usage:** sometimes; uncountable; usually

   **Example:** Wage rates for tool and die makers differ in various regions of the country.
