### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One-twentieth of a ream of paper; a collection of twenty-four or twenty-five sheets of paper of the same size and quality, unfolded or having a single fold.

**Source sense R1.S2**
