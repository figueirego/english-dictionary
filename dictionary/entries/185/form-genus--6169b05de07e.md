### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A collection of organisms that is given formal recognition at the rank of genus with a taxonomic name, but which is known to be an artificial group rather than a natural one.

   **Example:** All fossilized roots of scale trees are assigned to the form genus 'Stigmaria'.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences; taxonomy

   **Source synonyms:** morphogenus
