### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of deterring, or the state of being deterred.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The policy relies on nuclear deterrence.

   **Example:** Strict penalties act as a deterrence against theft.

   **Example:** effective deterrence

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An action taken by states or alliances of nations against equally powerful alliances to prevent hostile action.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** policy of deterrence

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The art of producing in one's enemy the fear of attacking.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The art of producing in one's enemy the fear of attacking.
   1. Nuclear deterrence&#58; discouraging nuclear attack through the threat of nuclear retaliation.

      **Usage:** countable; specifically; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The action of discouraging an action or event by instilling doubt or fear of the penalty or punishment.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
