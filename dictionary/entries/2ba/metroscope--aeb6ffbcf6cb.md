### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A modification of the stethoscope, for directly auscultating the uterine souffles (not the dessert) during pregnancy. One part is inserted into the vagina, and the other into the ear.

   **Usage:** historical

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences
