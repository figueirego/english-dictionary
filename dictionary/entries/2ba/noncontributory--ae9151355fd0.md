## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Describing a pension plan in which the members do not provide their own direct contributions (that is, the employer makes all monetary contributions; the employees make no monetary contributions, whereas their service toward the company's ability to operate is their indirect contribution, for which the pension is remuneration).

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Not being a contributing factor (to a decision, analysis, or situation), such as not contributing to a medical diagnosis.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Although medical history is always relevant to diagnosis, it is sometimes noncontributory, shedding no light on possible causes of symptoms.
