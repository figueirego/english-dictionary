### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Silly, especially at an inappropriate time or in an inappropriate manner; lacking a good reason for being, or for doing what one does; due to or moved by a caprice or whim.

   **Example:** trifling ((literary)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of little weight or importance; not worth notice; slight; trivial.

   **Source synonyms:** base; contemptible; floccinaucinihilipilificatious (humorous; rare); foolish; for the birds (informal); futile; frivolous; idle; ignorable; immaterial; inconsequential; inconsiderable; indifferent; insignificant; like a fart in a windstorm; meager; mean; minor; miserable; negligible; nothingburger (informal); nondescript; null; paltry; petty; picayune; picayunish; piddling; piddly; pissant; pissass (idiomatic; vulgar); piteous (obsolete); pitiful; pointless; silly; simple (archaic); slight; ticky-tacky (US); tin-pot (informal); trifling; trivial; two-bit (US; slang); unimportant; unweighty; vain; [worthless](../377/worthless--ddda5e25a150.md); wretched

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Having no reasonable prospect of success because its claim is without merit, lacking a supporting legal or factual basis, while the filing party is, or should be, aware of this.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law
