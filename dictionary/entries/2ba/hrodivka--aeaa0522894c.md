### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A rural settlement, the administrative centre of Hrodivka settlement hromada, Pokrovsk Raion, Donetsk Oblast, Ukraine, founded in 1750.
   1. A former volost of Bakhmut povit, Katerynoslav governorate, Russian Empire, established in the late-1890s, abolished a. 1923.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A rural settlement, the administrative centre of Hrodivka settlement hromada, Pokrovsk Raion, Donetsk Oblast, Ukraine, founded in 1750.
   1. A former selrada of Pokrovsk Raion, Donetsk Oblast, Ukraine, merged into Hrodivka settlement hromada in June 2020.
