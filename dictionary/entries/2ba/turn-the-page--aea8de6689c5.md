### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To move on to new involvements or activities; to make a fresh start.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** You've been divorced for three years. It's time to turn the page and start looking for somebody else.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; To proceed to the next page in a book.
