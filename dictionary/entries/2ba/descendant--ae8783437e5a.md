## Noun · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /dɪˈsɛndənt/ — source labels: Canada; UK; US
- IPA: /dɪˈsendənt/ — source labels: Australia

### Forms

- descendants — plural

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. One of the progeny of a specified person, at any distance of time or through any number of generations.

   **Example:** The patriarch survived many descendants&#58; five children, a dozen grandchildren, even a great grandchild.

   **Example:** a lineal descendant of Charlemagne

   **Source antonyms:** ancestor; progenitor; forefather; foremother

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. A thing that derives directly from a given precursor or source.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** This famous medieval manuscript has many descendants.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. A later evolutionary type.

   **Example:** Dogs evolved as descendants of early wolves.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. A language that is descended from another.

   **Example:** English and Scots are the descendants of Old English.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. A word or form in one language that is descended from a counterpart in an ancestor language.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** reflex; derivative

   **Source antonyms:** etymon

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. The intersection of the western (setting) horizon and the ecliptic, its ecliptical longitude; the astrological sign it corresponds to.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astrology; human-sciences; mysticism; philosophy; sciences
