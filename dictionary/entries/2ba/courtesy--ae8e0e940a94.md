## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈkɜːtəsi/ — source labels: Received-Pronunciation
- IPA: /ˈkɝɾəsi/ — source labels: General-American

### Forms

- courtesies — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Polite behavior.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Please extend them the courtesy of your presence.

   **Example:** He was always known for his grace, kindness, and courtesy to people of every rank.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A polite gesture or remark, especially as opposed to an obligation or standard practice.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** I offered them a ride simply as a courtesy.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Consent or agreement in spite of fact; indulgence.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** They call this pond a lake by courtesy only.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Willingness or generosity in providing something needed.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** They received free advertising through the courtesy of the local newspaper.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A curtsey.

   **Usage:** countable; dated; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The life interest that the surviving husband has in the real or heritable estate of his wife.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law
