### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A sweet smell, usually likened to that of flowers, said to be emitted by the bodies of saints during their life, or especially at or after death.

   **Usage:** Middle-Ages; UK; especially; historical; uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person's reputation for, or state of, holiness.

   **Usage:** UK; figuratively; uncountable; usually

   **Example:** to die in odour of sanctity

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A (supposed) general aura of goodness or virtue.

   **Usage:** UK; figuratively; humorous; ironic; uncountable; usually
