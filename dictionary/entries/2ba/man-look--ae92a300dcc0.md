### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A cursory search for something present that fails to locate it, stereotypically performed by men.

   **Usage:** colloquial; humorous

   **Example:** My Nigel says there was no brandy cream in the supermarket, but I think he only had a man look.
