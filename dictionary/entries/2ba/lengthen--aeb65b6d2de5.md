### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make longer, to extend the length of.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** You need to lengthen the story if you want to have it published.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To become longer.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The shadows on the lawn lengthened as the sun began to set.
