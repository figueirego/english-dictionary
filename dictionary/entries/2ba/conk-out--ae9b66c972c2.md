### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To fall fast asleep; to sleep soundly.

   **Usage:** informal; intransitive

   **Example:** He wasn't there, just the dog, conked out on the porch.

   **Source synonyms:** zonk out

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To stop functioning.

   **Usage:** informal; intransitive

   **Example:** The old car conked out halfway up the hill.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To die.

   **Usage:** informal; intransitive

   **Example:** He lived to be 90, then conked out in his sleep.
