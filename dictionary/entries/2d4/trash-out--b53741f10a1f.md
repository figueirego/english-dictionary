### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To criticize a person in a ranting way or manner.

   **Usage:** informal; transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To stop working; to crap out.

   **Usage:** informal; intransitive

   **Example:** My computer trashed out again.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To remove trash and other items from an abandoned house.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The bank hired me to trash this house out.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To ruin (a place) with vandalism, garbage, etc.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The rock band trashed out the hotel room.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To make (a place or thing) trashy or low-class.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** They're going to trash out the neighborhood.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To insult, demean and criticize (someone).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** You shouldn't trash people out like that.
