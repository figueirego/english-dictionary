## Noun

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈlʌnt͡ʃˌɹum/ — source labels: General-American

### Forms

- lunchrooms — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A room designated as a place to eat lunch.

   **Example:** Company policy was to eat snacks only in the lunchroom.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A diner or small restaurant that serves lunch.

   **Example:** I don't know how they stay in business serving only lunch, but the lunchroom on the corner is cheap and fast so I like it.
