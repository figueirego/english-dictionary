### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A deep-bowled spoonlike utensil with a long, usually curved, handle.

   **Source synonyms:** dipper

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A container used in a foundry or steel mill to transport and pour out molten metal.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; metallurgy; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The float or paddle on a mill wheel.

   **Source synonyms:** ladle board

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An instrument for drawing the charge of a cannon.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A ring, with a handle or handles fitted to it, for carrying shot.
