### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A combining form relating to England or, by extension, the United Kingdom.

   **Usage:** morpheme

   **Example:** Anglo-Indian rule brought railroads, but also some of the worst famines in history.

   **Example:** Anglo-German naval rivalry was a contributing factor to the First World War.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A combining form relating to the English or British people, culture, or livestock.

   **Usage:** morpheme

   **Example:** The Anglo-Indian Cliff Richard's 1952 "Move It" is sometimes credited as the first British rock hit.

   **Example:** T.S. Eliot was an anglophile.

   **Example:** Anglo-Kirghiz mares, now better known as Novokirghiz, produce more milk than representatives of either separate lineage.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A combining form relating to the English language.

   **Usage:** morpheme

   **Example:** Japanese anglophones—but not Chinese ones—often struggle with distinguishing /r/ and /l/.
