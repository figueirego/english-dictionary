### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. From a given time onwards; longer, again.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** They don't make repairable radios any more.

   **Source context (verbatim):** in negative or interrogative constructions

   **Source synonyms:** any longer; no longer; no more

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. at this point (expressing exacerbation in the face of the futility of a situation)

   **Usage:** not-comparable; rhetoric

   **Example:** What are we even doing any more.

   **Source context (verbatim):** with even

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Now, from now on.

   **Usage:** Northern-Ireland; US; colloquial; not-comparable

   **Example:** I eat fish any more.

   **Source context (verbatim):** in positive constructions

   **Source synonyms:** going forward; nowadays; anymore; any more; as of now; from now on; going forward; hereafter; henceforth; henceforward; henceforwards (archaic); in future (UK); in the future (US); moving forward; anymore; any more; at present; at the minute (Britain); at the moment; at this moment in time; atm (Internet); currently; just now; now; nowadays; presently; right now; these days

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see any, more.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Alice doesn't like Braque any more than she likes Picasso.

   **Example:** If we laugh any more in class, we're gonna get expelled.

   **Example:** (as determiner or pronoun) I can't see any more people arriving; I don't think any more (of them) want to come.
