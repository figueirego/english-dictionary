### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make deaf, either temporarily or permanently.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The head injury deafened her for life.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To make soundproof.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to deafen a wall or a floor

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

   **Usage:** dialectal; figuratively; rare; sometimes; transitive
