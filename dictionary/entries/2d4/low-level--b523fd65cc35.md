## Adjective

### Forms

- lowest-level — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Relatively unimportant.

   **Example:** The low-level bureaucrat couldn't help us and his boss could have but wouldn't.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Not intense.

   **Example:** The low-level radiation released won't kill you immediately, but avoid long term exposure.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of, or relating to a program, or to code in which each statement corresponds to a single machine instruction.

   **Example:** The assembler generates low-level code.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Detailed.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Situated or occurring at a lower level.
