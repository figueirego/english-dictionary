### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A nonexistent top-level domain that is assigned a meaning in certain specialized contexts.

   **Usage:** Internet

   **Example:** The anonymity network Tor has a top-level pseudodomain .onion for hidden services, which can only be reached with a Tor client.
