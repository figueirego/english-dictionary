**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act or practice of lingering or remaining in a place aimlessly or without an obvious purpose.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** NO LOITERING \[sign posted where hanging out and causing trouble is unwelcome\]
