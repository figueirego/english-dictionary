### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A branching, hair-like structure that grows on the bodies of birds, used for flight, swimming, protection and display.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Long hair on the lower legs of a dog or horse, especially a draft horse, notably the Clydesdale breed. Narrowly only the rear hair.

   **Source synonyms:** feathers; feathering; horsefeathers

   **Source antonyms:** spats

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. One of the fins or wings on the shaft of an arrow.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A longitudinal strip projecting from an object to strengthen it, or to enter a channel in another object and thereby prevent displacement sideways or rotationally but permit motion lengthwise.

   **Source synonyms:** spline

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Kind; nature; species (from the proverbial phrase "birds of a feather").

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. One of the two shims of the three-piece stone-splitting tool known as plug and feather or plug and feathers; the feathers are placed in a borehole and then a wedge is driven between them, causing the stone to split.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The angular adjustment of an oar or paddle-wheel float, with reference to a horizontal axis, as it leaves or enters the water.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Anything petty or trifling; a whit or jot.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. Partridges and pheasants, as opposed to rabbits and hares (called fur).

   **Usage:** in-plural

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; hunting; lifestyle

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. A junction indicator attached to a colour-light signal at an angle, which lights up, typically with four white lights in a row, when a diverging route is set up.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** rail-transport; railways; transport

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. A faint edge.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; cricket; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports
