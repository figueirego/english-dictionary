### Pronunciation

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One of a pair of elongated pointed teeth (either cuspids or incisors) that extend outside the mouth of an animal such as walrus, elephant or wild boar, and which continue to grow throughout the animal's life.

   **Example:** Until the CITES sales ban, elephant tusks were the 'backbone' of the legal ivory trade.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A small projection on a (tusk) tenon.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A tusk shell.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A projecting member like a tenon, and serving the same or a similar purpose, but composed of several steps, or offsets, called teeth.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; carpentry; construction; manufacturing

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A sharp point.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The share of a plough.
