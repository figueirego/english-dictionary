### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A playing card with a face, properly a jack, queen, or king but (inexact, sometimes proscribed) sometimes also an ace or joker.

   **Usage:** US

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** card-games; games

   **Source synonyms:** court card; monkey; paint

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** Internet

   **Example:** My face card never declines.
