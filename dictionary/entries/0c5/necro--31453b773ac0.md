**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make a new post to a forum discussion that has been dormant for a long time, making the thread visible in the list of active topics; to bump.

   **Usage:** Internet

   **Example:** Sorry to necro a comment from three years ago, but …

   **Source synonyms:** necropost; necro-bump
