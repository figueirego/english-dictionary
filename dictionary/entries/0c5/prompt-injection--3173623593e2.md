## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A method of manipulating an artificial intelligence system by embedding malicious instructions (prompts) within input data in order to trick the model into overriding its intended behaviour or safeguards.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; artificial intelligence; computer security; artificial intelligence; computer security

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A method of causing an artificial intelligence to ignore its initial instructions (often ethical restrictions) by directly giving it a particular prompt; a jailbreak.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; artificial intelligence; computer security; artificial intelligence; computer security
