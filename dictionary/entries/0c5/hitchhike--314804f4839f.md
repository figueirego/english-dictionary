### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To try to get a ride in a passing vehicle while standing at the side of a road, generally by either sticking out one's finger or thumb or holding a sign with one's stated destination.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To try to get (a ride) in this manner.

   **Usage:** US; transitive

   **Example:** Pamela hitchhiked a ride to New York.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To be carried along with something else.

   **Usage:** broadly; intransitive

   **Example:** In genetic hitchhiking, a gene is propagated because it occurs in conjunction with a favourable mutation.

   **Example:** In cultural hitchhiking, a cultural trait spreads with a technologically advanced population.
