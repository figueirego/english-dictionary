### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A cowboy; specifically, a working cowboy who generally does not participate in rodeos.

   **Usage:** US; also; attributive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One who adopts a distinctive style of cowboy attire and heritage.

   **Usage:** US; broadly

   **Example:** Many cowboy poets have a buckaroo look and feel about them.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A style of cowboy boot with a high heel tapered at the back.

   **Usage:** US; broadly

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A headstrong, reckless person; a hothead.

   **Usage:** US; Western; figuratively

   **Example:** Don’t run in looking for a fight like some kind of buckaroo.
