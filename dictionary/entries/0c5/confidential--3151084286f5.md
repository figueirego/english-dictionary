### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Kept, or meant to be kept, secret within a certain circle of persons; not intended to be known publicly.

   **Example:** The newspaper claims a leaked confidential report by the government admits to problems with corrupt MPs.

   **Source synonyms:** private; classified; off the record; privileged; secret; dern

   **Source antonyms:** public; on the record

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Inclined to share confidences; (of things) making people inclined to share confidences; involving the sharing of confidences.

   **Usage:** dated

   **Example:** Sitting in front of the fire, they became quite confidential, and began to gossip.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Having someone's confidence or trust; having a position requiring trust; worthy of being trusted with confidences.

   **Usage:** dated
