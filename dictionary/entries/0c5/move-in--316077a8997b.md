### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To start living or working in a new place; to transport one's belongings to a new home or workplace; to make one's home or workplace into a suitable environment.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** We move in to our new place in August.

   **Example:** Those two moved in together when they got engaged.

   **Example:** We are still moving in, so the house doesn't feel like home yet.

   **Source antonyms:** move out

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To bring to a new place to live.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I moved my mom in when she got sick.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To move closer.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The lions moved in for the kill.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To attempt to take control (of something) forcibly.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Troops will move in if the civil unrest continues.

   **Example:** Regulatory officials may move in on the company soon.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see move, in.
