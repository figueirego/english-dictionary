### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The process of assembling or bringing together.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** the assemblage of words and recordings

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A collection of things which have been gathered together or assembled.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** group

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A gathering of people.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** assemblage; congregation; crowd; flock; horde; huddle; legion; mass; multitude; press; slew; throng

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A group of different artifacts found in association with one another.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** archaeology; history; human-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A visual art form similar to collage, which combines two-dimensional and three-dimensional, often found, elements into works of art.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
