### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having no guard or protection; vulnerable.

   **Example:** The unguarded gatehouse was soon taken by the invading army.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Displaying a lack of caution or thought.

   **Example:** I let out her secret in an unguarded moment.
