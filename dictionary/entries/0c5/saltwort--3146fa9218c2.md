### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Certain plants of various genera that grow in dry, alkali soils, including the following&#58;
   1. Those of the family Amaranthaceae, including&#58;
      1. Salsola spp. (Russian thistles) and related genera within the subfamily Salsoloideae.

         **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Certain plants of various genera that grow in dry, alkali soils, including the following&#58;
   1. Those of the family Amaranthaceae, including&#58;
      1. Salicornia spp. (syn. Sarcocornia) spp.) (pickleweeds).

         **Usage:** countable; uncountable

         **Source synonyms:** glassworts; swampfires; marsh samphires

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Certain plants of various genera that grow in dry, alkali soils, including the following&#58;
   1. Those of the family Amaranthaceae, including&#58;
      1. Tecticornia spp. (samphires, glassworts, etc.)

         **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   1. Those of the family Amaranthaceae, including&#58;

         **Usage:** countable; uncountable

7. Certain plants of various genera that grow in dry, alkali soils, including the following&#58;
   1. Those of the family Bataceae of turtleweeds&#58;
      1. Batis spp.,especially those of the species Batis maritima of plants distributed in the southwestern United States, Caribbean, and South America in coastal saltmarshes.
