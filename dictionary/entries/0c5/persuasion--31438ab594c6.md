### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of persuading, or trying to do so; the addressing of arguments to someone with the intention of changing their mind or convincing them of a certain point of view, course of action etc.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An argument or other statement intended to influence one's opinions or beliefs; a way of persuading someone.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An argument or other statement intended to influence one's opinions or beliefs; a way of persuading someone.
   1. All activity attempting to influence or control others' behavior or profession of beliefs, from convincing to threatening, assault, or battery.

      **Usage:** countable; euphemistic; uncountable

      **Example:** Some of the shopowners were resistant to the extortion, so the mafia did some persuasion.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A strongly held conviction, opinion or belief.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** It is his persuasion that abortion should never be condoned.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. One's ability or power to influence someone's opinions or feelings; persuasiveness.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A specified religious adherence, a creed; any school of thought or ideology.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Another personal, animal or inanimate trait that is not (very) liable to be changed by persuasion, such as sex, gender, ethnicity, origin, profession or nature.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; humorous; often; uncountable
