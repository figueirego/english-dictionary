<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person who knocks.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A critic; one who disparages.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

4. A person who knocks (denigrates) something.

   **Usage:** derogatory; informal

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A person's breast, especially if large.

   **Usage:** plural-normally; slang

6. A dwarf, goblin, or sprite imagined to dwell in mines and to indicate the presence of ore by knocking.

   **Usage:** archaic; especially Cardigan

   **Source context (verbatim):** in South Wales

   **Usage:** dated; slang
