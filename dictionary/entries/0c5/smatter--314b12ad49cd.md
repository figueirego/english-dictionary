### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make (someone or something) dirty; to bespatter, to soil.

   **Usage:** also; figuratively; obsolete; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** assoil (obsolete); beclart (UK; dialectal); bedaub (obsolete); bedirty; imbrue (especially with blood) (archaic; literary); bedizen (UK; dialectal); befile (obsolete); befilth (rare); befoul; begrime; beray (archaic; obsolete); beshit (figuratively; vulgar); besmear; besmirch; besully; bewray (archaic; obsolete); blacken; blot; blur (obsolete); clat; contaminate; darken; defile; dirten (Uganda); dirty; elt (Northern-England; Scotland); file (archaic); foil (obsolete); foul; gunk; gunk up; inquinate; scunge; sling mud; slotter; slur; smear; smirch; smudge; smut; smutch; soil; soss (UK; dialectal); sowl (obsolete); spot; stain; sullow; sully; tarnish; uggle (UK; dialectal; obsolete)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To hit (someone or something) with a liquid; to splash, to spatter.

   **Usage:** US; broadly; transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To approach or study (something, such as a subject) superficially; to dabble in.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To speak (a language or words) with only a superficial knowledge of it.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To hit with a liquid; to splash, to spatter.

   **Usage:** US; intransitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To have a slight, superficial knowledge of something; to dabble.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To talk ignorantly or superficially; to babble, to chatter.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive; obsolete
