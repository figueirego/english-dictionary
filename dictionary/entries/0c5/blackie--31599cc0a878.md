### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A surname.

   **Example:** Mr Blackie, please state for the record your job title and chief duties.

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A nickname for someone surnamed Black.

   **Example:** Go ask Blackie if he wants to join us for beers after work tonight.

**Source sense R1.S3**
