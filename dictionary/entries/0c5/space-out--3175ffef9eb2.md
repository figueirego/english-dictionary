### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To position (objects, people, time slots, etc.) at regular intervals with a calculated space between them.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** She spaced out the yoga mats as much as she could while leaving room for some walkways.

   **Example:** She spaced out her scheduled events as much as she could while leaving time for question-and-answer sessions.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To become distracted or disoriented; to lose attention or focus.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** She spaced out when the speaker started droning about the fine-print details of convertible debentures.

   **Source synonyms:** daydream; tune out; zone out

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To increase the white space between letters, words or lines.

   **Usage:** dated; transitive; typing

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** media; printing; publishing
