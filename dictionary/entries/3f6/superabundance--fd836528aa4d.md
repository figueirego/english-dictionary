### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An extreme abundance; abundance to a vast degree that seems almost excessive.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** We have not merely an abundance, but a superabundance of enemies at this time; they will need to stand in line to disagree with us.
