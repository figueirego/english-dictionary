### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. (of a chromosome) having the centromere between one end and the middle, and thus having a long arm, and a short arm

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Human chromosome 2 is submetacentric.
