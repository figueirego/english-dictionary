## Noun

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The action of mocking; ridicule, derision.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Something so lacking in necessary qualities as to inspire ridicule; a laughing-stock.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Something insultingly imitative; an offensively futile action, gesture etc.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Mimicry, imitation, now usually in a derogatory sense; a travesty, a ridiculous simulacrum.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The defendant wasn't allowed to speak at his own trial - it was a mockery of justice.
