### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An edited or censored version of a document; a product resulting from the process of editing or censoring.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** The government supplied only the redaction to the reporters; the original was kept secret.

   **Example:** The content of this codex is a redaction of information from various earlier works. \[≈ edited selection\]

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The change or changes made while editing.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The process of editing or censoring.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** The Expense Claims made by Members of Parliament must be subject to redaction before publication under the Freedom of Information Act. \[≈ censoring\]
