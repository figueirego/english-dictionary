### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Intending to, resulting in, or capable of disposition (disposing, disposing of, or settling a matter).

   **Example:** We were unable to produce any dispositive evidence to support our case.

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** Euro-English or non-native speakers' English

   **Source antonyms:** cogent
