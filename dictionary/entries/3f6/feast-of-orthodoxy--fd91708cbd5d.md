**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A celebration on the first Sunday of Great Lent in the Eastern Orthodox Church and other churches using the Byzantine rite to commemorate, originally, only the final defeat of iconoclasm on the first Sunday of Lent in 843, and later also opposition to all heterodoxy.
