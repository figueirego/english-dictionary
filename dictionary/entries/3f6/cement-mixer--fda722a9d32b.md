### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A device used in construction in which cement, aggregate and water are slowly mixed together in a revolving drum to form concrete.

   **Source synonyms:** concrete mixer

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A truck mounted with a rotating drum for mixing, transporting, and pouring concrete.
