## Noun

### Forms

- supreme courts — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A court of law which represents the highest legal authority within a jurisdiction.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A superior court; a court of general competence which typically has unlimited jurisdiction with regard to civil and criminal legal cases.

   **Example:** After the establishment of the Supreme Court of the United Kingdom, the original Supreme Court was renamed as the Senior Courts.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. In certain provinces and states, a superior court of original jurisdiction, which however is inferior to said region's court of appeals.

   **Usage:** Canada; US

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** high court; court of last resort
