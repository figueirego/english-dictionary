### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Resembling a star.

   **Example:** Pluto's starlike appearance in the telescope was an early clue that it was not the large Planet X astronomers had hoped for.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Resembling or characteristic of a celebrity or leading actor.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Convex; having the property that any line segment joining any mapped point to a specified point in the domain lies entirely in the domain.

   **Example:** the growth theorem for holomorphic starlike mappings
