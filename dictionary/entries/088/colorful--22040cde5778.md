### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Possessing prominent and varied colors.

   **Usage:** US

   **Example:** a colourful display of flowers

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Interesting, multifaceted, energetic, distinctive.

   **Usage:** US

   **Example:** He had a colourful career in the music industry.

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Profane, obscene, offensive (usually in the phrase colourful language).

   **Usage:** US; euphemistic
