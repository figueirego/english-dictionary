**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A function from (state, input symbol) to state describing what state to move to on receiving a given input in a given state.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; computing-theory; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A homeomorphism that bijects between the subsets of the images of two overlapping coordinate charts that are shared in the preimage&#58; For (U\_1,φ₁) and (U\_2,φ₂) coordinate charts with U\_1∩U\_2≠∅, the transition functions φ₁₂=φ₂∘φ₁⁻¹ and its inverse φ₂₁=φ₁∘φ₂⁻¹ may be constructed.
