### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A retail store selling inexpensive items, especially one in which all items have a price of one dollar. (The latter type, whose name has the literal meaning, were formerly common and are now rare.)

   **Usage:** Canada; US

   **Source synonyms:** five-and-dime; five-and-ten; dime store; pound shop; $2 shop; ten-dollar store

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A government-owned shop in Cuba that sold goods mainly to foreigners and solely in exchange for hard currency.

   **Usage:** historical
