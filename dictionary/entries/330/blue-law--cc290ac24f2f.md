### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A restrictive religious-based law in colonial New England, especially preventing leisure activities on a Sunday.

   **Usage:** Canada; US; historical

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any puritanical or restrictive law, especially one that prohibits or restricts commercial activity on a Sunday.

   **Usage:** Canada; US; broadly
