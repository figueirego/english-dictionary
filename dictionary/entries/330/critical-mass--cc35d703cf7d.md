### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The minimum amount of fissile material needed to support a self-sustaining nuclear chain reaction.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; nuclear physics

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The minimum amount of something or the minimum number of people needed to trigger a phenomenon.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; figuratively; uncountable
