### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To manage to produce, deliver, or present (something) by inventing, creating, thinking of, or obtaining it.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** How can you come up with such brilliant ideas?

   **Example:** Unless Geoff can come up with the money for that train ticket, he'll be stuck in Des Moines for the weekend.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To reach or overtake.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** catch up with

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see come, up, with.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** He came up with a bag of rice, having it carried it up from downstairs.
