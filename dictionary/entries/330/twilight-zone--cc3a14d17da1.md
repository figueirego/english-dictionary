## Noun · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A moving line that divides the daylit side and the dark night side of a planetary body.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astronomy; astrophysics; natural-sciences

   **Source synonyms:** terminator

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A region or context located in between others and therefore not subject to their norms.

   **Usage:** broadly; figuratively

   **Source synonyms:** shadowland

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A deteriorating area surrounding a central business district.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Synonym of mesopelagic zone (“the part of the ocean where there is very minimal light, located below the photic epipelagic zone but above the aphotic bathypelagic zone.”).

   **Source synonyms:** mesopelagic zone (synonym; synonym-of)
