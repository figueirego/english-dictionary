### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To repel or drive back.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to repulse an assault; to repulse the enemy

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To reject or rebuff.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to repulse a suitor

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To cause revulsion in; to repel.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The smell of rotting food repulsed me.

   **Example:** I find your conduct reprehensible, disgusting, and it repulses me, the way a mongoose repulses a snake.
