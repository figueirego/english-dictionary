### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A title awarded to a town in the United Kingdom which, before letters patent, held city status by virtue of having a cathedral within its bounds.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A city in a former British colony which resembles an English cathedral city in that it has a relatively small population, an Anglican cathedral, and also holds the title of city.
