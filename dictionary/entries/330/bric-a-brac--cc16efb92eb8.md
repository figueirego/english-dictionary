### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Small ornaments and other miscellaneous display items of little value.

   **Usage:** also; attributive; uncountable; usually

   **Source synonyms:** brickety-brack; knick-knacks; novelty item; bauble; novelty; bric-a-brac; fandangle; fangle; fizgig (archaic); folderol; gewgaw; kickshaw; knick-knack; mathom; tat; tchotchke (US); trangam (obsolete); trinket; whim-wham (obsolete)

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any collection containing a variety of miscellaneous items; a hodgepodge, an olio.

   **Usage:** also; attributive; broadly; uncountable; usually
