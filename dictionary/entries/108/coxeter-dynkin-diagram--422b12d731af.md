### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A graph with numerically labelled edges (called branches) representing the spatial relations between a collection of mirrors (or reflecting hyperplanes).

   **Example:** A Coxeter-Dynkin diagram encodes the information in a Coxeter matrix, which in turn encodes the presentation of a Coxeter group.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** algebra; geometry; mathematics; sciences
