### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who possesses, or appears to possess, extrasensory abilities such as clairvoyance, precognition and telepathy, as well as potentially interact externally using their minds or souls through telekinesis or teleportation, or who appears to be susceptible to paranormal or supernatural influences.

   **Source synonyms:** psych

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person who supposedly contacts the dead; a medium.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** parapsychology; pseudoscience

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. In gnostic theologian Valentinus' triadic grouping of man, the second-highest type; a person focused on intellectual reality (the other two being hylic and pneumatic).

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Gnosticism; lifestyle; religion
