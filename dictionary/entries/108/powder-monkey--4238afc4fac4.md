## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An explosives expert; a person who sets explosives.

   **Usage:** informal

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person employed to carry gun powder from the ship's magazine to the gun deck during a battle; in the 18th century Royal British and U.S. Navies, this task (also carrying water) during battles became a permanent nickname for the ship's cabin boys and apprentice seamen.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A skier or snowboarder who avidly seeks out the “powder” (light, dry, fluffy snow).

   **Usage:** informal

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; skiing; snowboarding; sports
