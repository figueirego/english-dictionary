### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A former viscounty in the southwestern French Pyrenees, which became part of the French royal domain before it was united with the French crown, together with Navarra, as a single feudal province of the French realm.
