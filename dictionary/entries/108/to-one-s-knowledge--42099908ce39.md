### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To the extent of one's limited knowledge of the facts.

   **Example:** John will be starting his new job next Monday, to my knowledge.

   **Example:** There won't be any other new hires starting at the same time, to his knowledge.

   **Example:** He's the best candidate that they possibly could have landed, to our knowledge.
