### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Struggle that is followed by death.

   **Usage:** in-plural

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The final movements or sounds, often erratic, of a living being as it dies.

   **Usage:** in-plural

   **Example:** In her death throes, she made a sound and reached outward.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The final actions or output of a machine or other object before it ceases to function.

   **Usage:** figuratively; in-plural

   **Example:** As part of its death throes, the printer made a grinding sound and then never worked again.
