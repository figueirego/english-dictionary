## Noun · Record 2

### Forms

- New Englishes — plural
- New English — plural

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A variety of English that has come into being in a region where English was a former colonial language or is a foreign language.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. New Englanders.

   **Usage:** historical; in-plural; uncommon

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. English settlers who arrived in Ireland in the 16th and 17th centuries, as distinguished from previous Anglo-Norman invaders (the Old English).

   **Usage:** historical; in-plural

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** World English — variety of English
