**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A journey made to a sacred place, or a religious journey.

   **Example:** In the Muslim faith, the pilgrimage to Mecca is known as the Hajj.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A visit to any site revered or associated with a meaningful event.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** Each year we made a pilgrimage to New York City to visit the pub where we all first met.
