## Phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see nothing, to, see, here.

   **Example:** This town is very unattractive to tourists, since there's nothing to see here.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The event or situation is unremarkable or unimportant, and people should not pay undue attention to it.

   **Example:** After the press conference about the financial scandal, the spokesperson reassured the public that there was nothing to see here.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A phrase used to dismiss or downplay an event, frequently implying that there is, in fact, something significant being hidden or covered up.

   **Usage:** figuratively; ironic; often; sarcastic

   **Example:** The politician's vague response to corruption allegations was essentially a nothing-to-see-here moment.

   **Example:** A factory discharged waste into the river, but officials insisted it posed no danger. There was a sentiment of "nothing to see here".
