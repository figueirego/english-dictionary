   **Source synonyms:** Wu; Gusu; Helu City

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Synonym of Suzhou Creek&#58; A river in Shanghai municipality and Jiangsu, China, between Lake Tai and the Huangpu River; despite its name, it is 78 miles long.

   **Source synonyms:** Wusong; Suzhou Creek (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Su, a former imperial prefecture in modern Jiangsu, China, surrounding the city.

   **Usage:** historical
