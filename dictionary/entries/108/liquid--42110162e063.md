### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A substance that is flowing, and keeping no shape, such as water; a substance of which the molecules, while not tending to separate from one another like those of a gas, readily change their relative position, and which therefore retains no definite shape, except that determined by the containing receptacle; an inelastic fluid.

   **Usage:** countable; physical; uncountable

   **Example:** A liquid can freeze to become a solid or evaporate into a gas.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; phonetics; phonology; sciences

## Adjective · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: \[ˈlɪk.wɪd\] — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Flowing freely like water; fluid; not solid and not gaseous; composed of particles that move freely among each other on the slightest pressure.

   **Usage:** physical

   **Example:** liquid nitrogen

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Easily sold or disposed of without losing value.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance

   **Source context (verbatim):** of an asset

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. Having sufficient trading activity to make buying or selling easy.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a market

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. Flowing or sounding smoothly or without abrupt transitions or harsh tones.

   **Example:** a liquid melody

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. Belonging to a class of consonants comprising the laterals and the rhotics, which in many languages behave similarly.

   **Example:** /l/ and /r/ are liquid consonants.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; phonology; sciences

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. Fluid and transparent.

   **Example:** the liquid air
