## Interjection

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. May God bless you; said as a short prayer, benediction, or valediction for the recipient; usually religiously; sometimes nonliterally but rhetorically or culturally.

   **Example:** I heard you helped them with clearing away the downed tree. God bless you! The Lord provides!

   **Example:** You got that whole area cleaned up in one day? God bless you! I'll buy your coffee today!

   **Source synonyms:** God bless; bless you; God be with you

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Said to somebody who has sneezed, as a rhetorical response; alternative form of bless you.
