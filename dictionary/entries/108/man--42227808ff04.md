### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Someone (possibly implied male) who is an expert in an area or who takes part in an activity.

   **Usage:** morpheme

   **Example:** horse + -man → horseman

   **Example:** sports + -man → sportsman

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Someone (possibly implied male) who is employed or holds a position in an area.

   **Usage:** morpheme

   **Example:** law + -man → lawman

   **Example:** news + -man → newsman

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Someone (possibly implied male) who has special characteristics relating to a topic or area.

   **Usage:** morpheme

   **Example:** free + -man → freeman

   **Example:** ice + -man → iceman

   **Example:** super + -man → superman

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Someone (possibly implied male) who has a particular nationality.

   **Usage:** morpheme

   **Example:** Scots + -man → Scotsman

   **Example:** Dutch + -man → Dutchman

   **Example:** China + -man → Chinaman

   **Example:** Yorkshire + -man → Yorkshireman

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A man whose name begins with the preceding letter.

   **Usage:** morpheme

   **Example:** H + -man → H-man (“man whose name starts with H, e.g. Harry”)

   **Example:** J + -man → J-man (“man whose name starts with an J”)

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A ship which has special characteristics relating to a trade or area.

   **Usage:** morpheme

   **Example:** Guinea + -man → Guineaman (“ship of the West African slave trade”)

   **Example:** East India + -man → East Indiaman

   **Example:** merchant + -man → merchantman

   **Example:** Greenland + -man → Greenlandman (“a whaling ship”)

   **Example:** China + -man → Chinaman (“sailing ship engaged in the Old China Trade”)
