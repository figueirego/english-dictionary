### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Female first cousin of one's parent; one's female first cousin once removed in a higher generation.

   **Example:** Mary is my mother's first cousin. That makes Mary my cousin-aunt. Judy is my dad's first cousin, so for me she is another cousin-aunt.
