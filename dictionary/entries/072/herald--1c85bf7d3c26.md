**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A messenger, especially one bringing important news.

   **Example:** The herald blew his trumpet and shouted that the King was dead.

   **Source synonyms:** bode

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A harbinger, giving signs of things to come.

   **Example:** Daffodils and robins are heralds of Spring.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An official whose speciality is heraldry, especially one between the ranks of pursuivant and king-of-arms

   **Example:** Rouge Dragon is a herald at the College of Arms.

   **Source synonyms:** pursuivant
