### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To fight for superiority; to contend; to compete eagerly so as to gain something.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Her suitors were all vying for her attention.

   **Source synonyms:** compete; battle; contest; oppose

   **Source antonyms:** concede; give in; give up; reconcile

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To rival (something), etc.

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To do or produce in emulation, competition, or rivalry; to put in competition; to bandy.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To stake; to wager.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To stake a sum of money upon a hand of cards, as in the old game of gleek. See revie.
