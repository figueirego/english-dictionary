### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To lash out; to strike or hit at someone or something, particularly something in arm's length of the striker and at or near the level of the striker's head.

   **Usage:** intransitive; often

   **Source context (verbatim):** with at

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To strongly criticize or make a verbal attack, particularly as a response to previous criticism or provocation.

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To draw a line through some text such as a printed or written sentence, with the purpose of deleting that text from the rest of the document.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. to treat something as settled

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Of a batter, to be retired after three strikes (missed swings, as opposed to any other way of becoming "out"); of a pitcher, to cause this to happen to the batter.

   **Usage:** ergative

   **Example:** Jones struck out on a nasty slider.

   **Example:** The pitcher struck out Jones with a nasty slider.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; baseball; games; hobbies; lifestyle; softball; sports

   **Source synonyms:** punch out

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To fail; to be refused a request or to have a proposal not be accepted, in particular a request for a (hopefully romantic) date.

   **Usage:** colloquial; figuratively; intransitive

   **Example:** Dave asked the new girl to the dance but he struck out.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To begin to make one's way.

   **Example:** The travellers struck out towards the line of mountains.

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** cross out — to draw a line through a text; strike through — to draw a line through a text
