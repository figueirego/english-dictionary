## Noun

### Forms

- jokemen — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A clown, someone who says or does idiotic things.

   **Usage:** Multicultural-London-English

   **Example:** Did you hear Ilyas got suspended? He's such a jokeman.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Someone who takes serious things as jokes.

   **Usage:** Multicultural-London-English

   **Example:** I can't believe you laughed during the memorial, you jokeman!

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A comedian, someone who makes a lot of jokes.

   **Usage:** Multicultural-London-English

   **Example:** What a jokeman, he's so funny.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Someone who likes to play pranks.

   **Usage:** Multicultural-London-English

   **Example:** Stop being such a jokeman; we're in school!
