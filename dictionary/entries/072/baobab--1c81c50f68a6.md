### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A tree, Adansonia digitata, native to tropical Africa, having a broad swollen trunk and edible gourd-like hanging fruits.

   **Source synonyms:** African baobab; cream of tartar tree; monkey bread; sour gourd; tabaldi; tebeldi; upside-down tree

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any tree of the genus Adansonia.
