## Noun

### Forms

- lateralisms — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An organizational structure in which competing interests of equal power negotiate with each other.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A scheme for how nations work together or compete, such as multilateralism, bilateralism, unilateralism, etc.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Dominance or specialization by one side of the body; lateralization.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Lateral thinking; the process of approaching a subject from multiple sides, or an instance of this process.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
