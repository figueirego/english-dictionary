## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The peninsula leading from mainland Alaska, to the Aleutian Islands, lying to the north of the Kodiac Island archipelago.

   **Source synonyms:** Aleutian Peninsula; Aleutic Peninsula; Aleut Peninsula

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
