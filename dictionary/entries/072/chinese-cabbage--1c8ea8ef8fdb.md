### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any East Asian leafy vegetable related to the Western cabbage, especially subspecies or varieties of Brassica rapa.
   1. Bok choy (Brassica rapa subsp. chinensis).

      **Usage:** uncountable; usually
