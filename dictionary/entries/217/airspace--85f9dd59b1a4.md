### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A specified portion of the atmosphere, especially that under the technical aviation control and/or jurisdiction of a particular state over which territory (and territorial waters) it lies.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Various European countries transferred the aviation control of their adjoining airspace to Eurocontrol, while each retaining legal jurisdiction.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** aeronautics; aerospace; aviation; business; engineering; government; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; politics

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. That part of the sky designated for the sole use of aircraft.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** aeronautics; aerospace; aviation; business; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Space (i.e. a few neighboring frequencies) available for broadcasting within a particular frequency band.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The legalisation of free radios caused a bitter fight for airspace.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** broadcasting; media

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The portion of an enclosed area which contains air, especially breathable air.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The cavity in a cavity wall containing air for insulation.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; construction; manufacturing
