### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An expression that gives information about the roots of a polynomial; for example, the expression D = b² - 4ac determines whether the roots of the quadratic equation ax² + bx + c = 0 are real and distinct (D &gt; 0), real and equal (D = 0) or complex (D &lt; 0).

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** algebra; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The invariant (on the vector space of forms of degree d in n variables) that vanishes exactly when the corresponding hypersurface in Pⁿ⁻¹ is singular.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences
