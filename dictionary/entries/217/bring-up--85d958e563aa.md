### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see bring, up&#58; To bring from a lower to a higher position.

   **Example:** When you're in the basement, can you bring up the paints?

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To mention.

   **Example:** Don't bring up politics if you want to have a quiet conversation with that guy.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To raise or rear (children).

   **Example:** She did well enough bringing up two sons and a daughter on her own.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To uncover, to bring from obscurity; to resurface (e.g. a memory)

   **Example:** A short Internet search brought up some amazing details of this story.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To turn on power or start, as of a machine.

   **Example:** Wait a minute while I bring up my computer.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To check (a newly-assembled printed circuit board) for errors.

   **Example:** Anyone who's been tasked with bringing up a board knows how trying it is.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; electrical-engineering; electricity; electromagnetism; electronics; energy; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To vomit.

   **Example:** I was very ill today; I kept bringing up everything I ate.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To stop or interrupt a flow or steady motion.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To reach a particular score, especially a milestone.

   **Example:** Warner smacked a four over midwicket to bring up his century.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; cricket; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. To legally charge and put on trial; to position (someone) for judgement or examination by authority.

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. To level type or make it ready for printing by overlaying it.

   **Usage:** obsolete

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** media; printing; publishing

<a id="r1-s12"></a>

**Source sense R1.S12**

12. To prepare a vein for an injection.

   **Usage:** transitive
