## Noun

### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A Japanese dessert made with bread, preferably pain de mie, cut into cubes, brushed with a butter and honey mixture and baked until golden, and filled with fruit, nuts, syrup, cream, etc., and often topped with ice cream.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** brick toast; Shibuya toast
