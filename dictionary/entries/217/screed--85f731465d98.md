### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A piece of writing (such as an article, letter, or list) or a speech, especially if long.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Source synonyms:** harangue; polemic; rant; tirade; diatribe; admonition; condemnation; diatribe; dithyramb; harangue; impugnation; j'accuse; jeremiad; philippic; polemic; rant; screed; slanging match; tirade

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Chiefly in the plural form screeds&#58; a large quantity.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Senses relating to building construction and masonry.
   1. A tool, usually a long strip of wood or other material, placed on a floor to be covered with concrete, a wall to be plastered, etc., as a guide for producing a smooth, flat surface.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; construction; manufacturing; masonry

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Senses relating to building construction and masonry.
   1. A tool such as a long strip of wood or other material which is drawn over a wet layer of concrete, plaster, etc., to make it smooth and flat; also, a machine that achieves this effect; a screeder.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; construction; manufacturing; masonry

      **Source synonyms:** strickle

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Senses relating to building construction and masonry.
   1. A smooth, flat layer of concrete, plaster, or similar material, especially if acting as a base for paving stones, tiles, wooden planks, etc.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; construction; manufacturing; masonry

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A piece or narrow strip cut or torn off from a larger whole; a shred.

   **Usage:** Ireland; Newfoundland; Scotland; dated

   **Source synonyms:** scrid

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A piece of land, especially one that is narrow.

   **Usage:** Scotland; UK; dated; regional

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A rent, a tear.
