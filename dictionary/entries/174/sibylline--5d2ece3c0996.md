## Adjective · Record 1

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of or pertaining to or resembling a sibyl or female oracle, especially the Cumaean Sibyl and the Sibylline Books.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source synonyms:** sibyllic

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of or pertaining to or resembling a sibyl or female oracle, especially the Cumaean Sibyl and the Sibylline Books.
   1. Having oracle-like predicting powers, clairvoyant.

      **Usage:** broadly; not-comparable

      **Source synonyms:** sibyllic

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of or pertaining to or resembling a sibyl or female oracle, especially the Cumaean Sibyl and the Sibylline Books.
   1. Occult, mysterious.

      **Usage:** broadly; not-comparable

      **Source synonyms:** sibyllic; enigmatic

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Excessively and exorbitantly expensive. (In allusion to the Sibyl who sold three books to Tarquinius Superbus at the price of the original nine.)

   **Usage:** not-comparable
