### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To collapse or burst inward violently.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The submarine imploded as it sank below its crush depth.

   **Source antonyms:** explode

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To collapse or burst inward violently.
   1. To suddenly lose support in all areas of a campaign simultaneously.

      **Usage:** broadly; intransitive
