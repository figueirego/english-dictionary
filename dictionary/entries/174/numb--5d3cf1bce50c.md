### Pronunciation

- IPA: /nʌm/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Forms

- numbing — participle; present
- numbed — participle; past
- numbed — past

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To cause to become numb (physically or emotionally).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The dentist gave me novocaine to numb my tooth before drilling, thank goodness.

   **Example:** When I first heard the news, I was numbed by the shock.

   **Source synonyms:** benumb

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To cause (a feeling) to be less intense.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He turned to alcohol to numb his pain.

   **Source synonyms:** dull

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To cause (the mind, faculties, etc.) to be less acute.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** dull

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To become numb (especially physically).

   **Usage:** intransitive
