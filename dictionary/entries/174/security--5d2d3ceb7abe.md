### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The condition of not being threatened, especially physically, psychologically, emotionally, or financially.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Chad always carries a gun for his own security.

   **Example:** The pink blanket gives little Mary lots of security.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Something that secures.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An organization or department responsible for providing security by enforcing laws, rules, and regulations as well as maintaining order.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Pamela works in security.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Something that secures the fulfillment of an obligation or law.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Freedom from apprehension.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A tradeable financial asset, such as a share of stock or any of various classes of bond.

   **Usage:** countable; in-plural; often; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Proof of ownership of stocks, bonds or other investment instruments.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Property etc. temporarily relinquished to guarantee repayment of a loan.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance

   **Source synonyms:** collateral

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A guarantee.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable
