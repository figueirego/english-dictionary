### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In dire need (of something); having a dire need or desire.

   **Example:** I hadn't eaten in two days and was desperate for food.

   **Example:** desperate to eat; desperate for attention

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Being filled with, or in a state of, despair; hopeless.

   **Example:** I was so desperate at one point, I even went to see a loan shark.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Beyond hope, leaving little reason for hope; causing despair; extremely perilous.

   **Example:** a desperate disease; desperate fortune

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Involving or employing extreme measures, without regard to danger or safety; reckless due to hopelessness.

   **Example:** He dove into the rushing waters in a desperate effort to save her life.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Extremely bad; outrageous, shocking; intolerable.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Intense; extremely intense.
