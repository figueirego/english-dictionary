### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of complaining.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** customer complaints

   **Example:** noise complaints

   **Example:** file complaints

   **Example:** The company received numerous complaints about the product.

   **Example:** He filed complaints with the housing authority.

   **Source synonyms:** grouch (informal)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A grievance, problem, difficulty, or concern.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** I have no complaints about the quality of his work, but I don't enjoy his company.

   **Source synonyms:** plaint; moan (informal); noise (informal)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. In a civil action, the first pleading of the plaintiff setting out the facts on which the claim is based.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. In criminal law, the preliminary charge or accusation made by one person against another to the appropriate court or officer, usually a magistrate.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A bodily disorder or disease; the symptom of such a disorder.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Don't come too close; I've got this nasty complaint.
