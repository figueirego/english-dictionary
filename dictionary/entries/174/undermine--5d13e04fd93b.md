### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To dig underneath (something), to make a passage for destructive or military purposes; to sap.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To weaken or work against; to hinder, sabotage.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** The war efforts were undermined by the constant bickering between the allies.

   **Source synonyms:** corrode; undercut

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To erode the base or foundation of something, e.g. by the action of water.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To regard an object as the sum of the parts that compose it, in object-oriented ontology.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; philosophy; sciences
