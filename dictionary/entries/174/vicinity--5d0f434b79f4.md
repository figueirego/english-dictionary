### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Proximity; the state of being near.

   **Example:** There was a crackling sound in the vicinity of my right ear.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Neighbourhood; nearby region; surrounding area.

   **Example:** There is a hurricane in the vicinity of the Bahamas.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Approximate size or amount.

   **Example:** I weigh in the vicinity of 80kg.
