## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈɛb.ən.i/ — source labels: UK; US

### Forms

- ebonies — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A hard, dense, deep black wood from various subtropical and tropical trees, especially of the genus Diospyros.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A tree that yields such wood.

   **Usage:** countable; usually

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A deep, dark black colour.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; usually

   **Source synonyms:** onyx; raven

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Dark skin colour.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A black key on a piano or other keyboard instrument (as opposed to ivory).

   **Usage:** countable; usually
