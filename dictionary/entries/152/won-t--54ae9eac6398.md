### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. will not (negative auxiliary); used to indicate a future non-occurring action.

   **Example:** Sam won't be doing any work this afternoon.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. doesn't want to

   **Example:** He either can't or won't do anything about it.
