### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A configuration of RNA, left-hand twisting double helix, that takes up a zigzagging form due to the natural right-hand twisting tendency of double-stranded RNA.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

   **Usage:** countable
