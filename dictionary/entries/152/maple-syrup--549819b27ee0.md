### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Syrup made from the sap of the sugar maple, Acer saccharum.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** maple honey; maple molasses; pancake syrup

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Syrup flavored with artificial maple flavoring; though legally in the United States cannot be labelled as such when for sale.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
