### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person or thing that immediately follows another in holding an office or title.

   **Example:** George W. Bush was successor to Bill Clinton as President of the US.

   **Example:** They haven't yet named her successor.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The next heir in order or succession.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A person who inherits a title or office.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The integer, ordinal number or cardinal number immediately following another.

   **Example:** A limit ordinal is not the successor of any ordinal.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** arithmetic; mathematics; sciences; set-theory
