### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To absorb (fluid).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The dry soil sucked up water like a sponge.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To adulate or flatter (someone) excessively, generally to obtain some personal benefit or favour.

   **Usage:** often; slang; with-to

   **Example:** Jimmy sucked up to the English teacher hoping he would get an A.

   **Source synonyms:** brown-nose; butter up; kiss up

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To fellate (someone).

   **Usage:** slang
