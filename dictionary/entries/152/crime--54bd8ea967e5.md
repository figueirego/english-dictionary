### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A specific act committed in violation of the law, especially criminal law.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** the commission of a crime

   **Source synonyms:** criminal offence; unlawfulness

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any great sin or wickedness; iniquity.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** Those methods of saving money should be a crime.

   **Source synonyms:** delinquency; wrongdoing; crime; delict; delinquency; error; malfeasance; misdeed; misdemeanor; misdoing; offense; peccadillo; sin; tort; tortfeasance; transgression; veniality; villainy; wrong; wrongdoing

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. That which occasions crime.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Criminal acts collectively.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** an effort by the police to tackle crime in the city

   **Source synonyms:** criminality; delinquency

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The habit or practice of committing crimes.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Crime doesn’t pay.
