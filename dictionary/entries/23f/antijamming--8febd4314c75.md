## Adjective

### Forms

- anti-jamming — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Designed to prevent being jammed (communications interference).

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Most military radios have some antijamming capability so they can be used in spite of the enemy's efforts to render them inoperative.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Designed not to jam (become stuck).

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The new copier had an antijamming design to keep the paper from getting stuck.
