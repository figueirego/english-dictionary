**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Several species of trees in the genus Sorbus, especially Sorbus domestica and Sorbus torminalis.

   **Usage:** Europe

   **Source synonyms:** service tree

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Several species of trees in the genus Sorbus, especially Sorbus domestica and Sorbus torminalis.
   1. The berries of those trees

      **Usage:** Europe

      **Source synonyms:** service tree

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any plant of the genus Amelanchier of small deciduous trees and large shrubs in the family Rosaceae.

   **Usage:** Canada; US

   **Source synonyms:** currant-tree; Juneberry; shad-blossom; shadbush

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

   1. The berry of such a plant.
