### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A spacecraft which cycles between Earth and Mars, in its own orbit which intercepts both their orbits at times when the planets are at those points in their orbits; which allows transport from Earth to Mars, or vice versa.

   **Usage:** space flight
