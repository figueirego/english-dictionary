### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The state or condition of being a human being.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** He feared the speedy decline of all manhood.

   **Source synonyms:** humanity

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The state or condition of being a man, as contrasted with being a boy, woman, girl, or nonbinary.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** He was a strapping youth poised on the brink of manhood.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. All of the men of a given place, area, or any human subgroup (ethnicity, nation, race, religion, family, work crew or ship's crew, etc.) regarded collectively.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** During the Second World War, Russia lost a great percentage of its young manhood, giving rise to a great wave of widowhood in the country.

   **Source synonyms:** mankind

   **Source antonyms:** womanhood; womankind

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The idealized nature of a man&#58; all of the characteristics traditionally and ideally ascribed to manliness regarded collectively.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** My goodness, James certainly is a prime example of manhood, isn't he?

   **Example:** Traditionally, men sought to enhance the manhood of their young sons, so that they would become manly men, but in general, men no longer do so.

   **Source synonyms:** manfulness; manliness; mannishness

   **Source antonyms:** womanhood; womanishness; womanliness

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The self-concept of a man being with respect to his possession of the various qualities traditionally and ideally ascribed to manliness; a man's sense or view of himself as being more or less manly.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The failure of William's business seems to have been quite damaging to his manhood.

   **Example:** I feel that when you boss me around, you insult my manhood.

   **Source antonyms:** womanhood

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The male genitalia, especially the penis.

   **Usage:** countable; euphemistic; uncountable

   **Example:** She spied on him in the shower and glimpsed his manhood.

   **Source antonyms:** womanhood
