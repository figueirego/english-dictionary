### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Easily transmitted to others.

   **Example:** Last year's flu strain was so contagious that everybody in town got sick!

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a disease

   **Source antonyms:** noncontagious; noncommunicable; nontransmissible

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Easily passed on to others.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** contagious enthusiasm; infectious laughter

   **Example:** Wearing jeans was a contagious fad at that time.

   **Source synonyms:** infectious; spreadable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Having a disease that can be transmitted to another person.

   **Example:** They were highly contagious, spreading bacteria to other people.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a person

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Contiguous.
