### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An exercise in which the abdominal muscles are contracted, bringing the torso of a person lying down to a more upright position. The degree of difficulty can be controlled by choosing from partial-range or full-range versions.

   **Example:** Susan's doing sit-ups to strengthen her muscles.
