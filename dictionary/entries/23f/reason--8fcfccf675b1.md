### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To deduce or come to a conclusion by being rational.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To perform a process of deduction or of induction, in order to convince or to confute; to argue.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source synonyms:** ratiocinate

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To converse; to compare opinions.

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To arrange and present the reasons for or against; to examine or discuss by arguments; to debate or discuss.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** I reasoned the matter with my friend.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To support with reasons, as a request.

   **Usage:** rare; transitive

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To persuade by reasoning or argument.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to reason one into a belief; to reason one out of his plan

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

**Source sense R2.S7**

   **Usage:** transitive; with-down

   **Example:** to reason down a passion
