### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Colocasia esculenta, raised as a food primarily for its corm, which distantly resembles potato.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source synonyms:** colocasia; elephant ears; eddo; old cocoyam; yam (Malaysia; Singapore)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of several other species with similar corms and growth habit in Colocasia, Alocasia etc.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Food from a taro plant.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source synonyms:** dasheen; madumbi; eddo
