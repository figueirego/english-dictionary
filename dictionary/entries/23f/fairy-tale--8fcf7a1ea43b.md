### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A folktale or literary story featuring fairies or similar fantasy characters.

   **Example:** I used to love reading fairy tales when I was a child.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The literary genre of these stories.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A fabricated or misleading account meant to deceive, such as an exaggerated excuse.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** He's telling us another fairy tale about how great the software will be.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A delightful and ideal situation of a kind attained by very few.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** I've been living in a fairy tale since I met Joe.
