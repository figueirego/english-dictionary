## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A parliamentary rule in the Polish-Lithuanian Commonwealth, allowing any dissenting member of the legislature to force an immediate end to the current session and nullify any legislation that had already been passed.

   **Usage:** historical

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; law; politics

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any system in which a single dissenting member can unilaterally block an action.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** The US, Russia, UK, France, and China have liberum veto power in the UN Security Council, allowing them to shield themselves and their allies from UN oversight.
