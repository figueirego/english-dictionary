## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- enPR: smĕl — source labels: General-American; Received-Pronunciation
- IPA: /smɛl/ — source labels: General-American; Received-Pronunciation

### Forms

- smells — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A sensation, pleasant or unpleasant, detected by inhaling air (or, the case of water-breathing animals, water) carrying airborne molecules of a substance.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** I love the smell of fresh bread.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The sense that detects odours.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; physiology; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A conclusion or intuition that a situation is wrong, more complex than it seems, or otherwise inappropriate.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To sense a smell or smells.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I can smell fresh bread.

   **Example:** Smell the milk and tell me whether it's gone off.

   **Source synonyms:** detect; sense

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To sense a smell or smells.
   1. To detect or perceive; often with out.

      **Usage:** broadly; transitive

      **Source synonyms:** detect; sense

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. Followed by like or of if descriptive&#58; to have a particular smell, whether good or bad.

   **Usage:** copulative; intransitive

   **Example:** The roses smell lovely.

   **Example:** Her feet smell of cheese.

   **Example:** The drunkard smelt like a brewery.

   **Source synonyms:** pong (informal); reek; stink; whiff

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. Followed by like or of if descriptive&#58; to have a particular smell, whether good or bad.
   1. To smell of; to have a smell of

      **Usage:** broadly; copulative; intransitive

      **Source synonyms:** pong (informal); reek; stink; whiff

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. Followed by like or of if descriptive&#58; to have a particular smell, whether good or bad.
   1. To smell bad; to stink.

      **Usage:** copulative; intransitive

      **Example:** Ew, this restroom smells (loathsomely).

      **Source context (verbatim):** without a modifier

      **Source synonyms:** pong (informal); reek; stink; whiff

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. Followed by like or of if descriptive&#58; to have a particular smell, whether good or bad.
   1. To have a particular tincture or smack of any quality; to savour.

      **Usage:** copulative; figuratively; intransitive

      **Example:** A report smells of calumny.

      **Source synonyms:** pong (informal); reek; stink; whiff
