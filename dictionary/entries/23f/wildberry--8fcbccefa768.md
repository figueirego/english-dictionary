### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A combination of wild cherries and strawberries, raspberries, blackberries or other berries. Used in ice cream and other food products, as well as in cosmetics.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any undomesticated species of berry that is found in the wild, often one that is edible and cultivated on a small scale; a wild berry. Wildberries tend to be smaller than the related domesticated varieties.

   **Usage:** countable
