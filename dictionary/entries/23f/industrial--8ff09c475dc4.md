   **Example:** This is an industrial product—it's much too strong for home use.

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Massive in scale or quantity.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Employed as manpower by such industry.

**Source sense R1.S7**
