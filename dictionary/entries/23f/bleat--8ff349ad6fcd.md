**Source sense R2.S1**

   **Usage:** onomatopoeic

   **Source synonyms:** blat; baa

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Of a person, to complain.

   **Usage:** derogatory; informal

   **Example:** The last thing we need is to hear them bleating to us about organizational problems.

   **Source synonyms:** kvetch; moan; whinge; whine; holler
