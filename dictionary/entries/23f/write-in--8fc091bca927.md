### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To write a letter to, e.g. a publication.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** If you have any questions, you can write in to the following address.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To fill in something required, by writing.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Don't forget to write in your details on the paper.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To vote for a candidate not on the ballot by writing their name.
