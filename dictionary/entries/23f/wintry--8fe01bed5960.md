<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Suggestive or characteristic of winter; cold, stormy.

   **Example:** wintry weather

   **Source synonyms:** brumal; hibernal; hiemal

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Containing sleet or snow.

   **Example:** It will be cloudy overnight, with outbreaks of heavy rain at times. The rain may turn wintry over higher ground.
