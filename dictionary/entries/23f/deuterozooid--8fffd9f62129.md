### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One of the secondary, and usually sexual, zooids produced by budding or fission from the primary zooids, in animals having alternate generations.

   **Example:** In the tapeworms, the joints are deuterozooids.
