### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To stun; to surprise.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I was knocked back by the sheer size of the hall.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To drink an alcoholic beverage swiftly or often; to finish a beverage.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** My friends were already knocking back drinks when I arrived at the bar.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To push or strike someone or something backward with force.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The impact of the collision knocked him back several feet.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To withstand or endure a difficult situation or setback.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Despite facing numerous challenges, she managed to knock them back and keep moving forward.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To achieve a significant accomplishment or success.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Their latest album really knocked it back and received critical acclaim.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To reject; to refuse.

   **Usage:** Australia; UK; transitive

   **Example:** How many job seekers did you knock back today?

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To press or knead (dough) so as to remove air bubbles.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** baking; cooking; food; lifestyle
