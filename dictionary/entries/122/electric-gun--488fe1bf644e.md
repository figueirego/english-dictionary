## Noun

### Forms

- electric guns — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An electroshock gun; a taser; a stun gun.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A gun with electrically powered components, such as the reloader or barrel rotator.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A gun that uses electricity to vaporize water as the propulsive force on the ballistic projectile.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A gun that uses electromagnetic, electric or magnetic force to propel a projectile.
   1. A railgun (electric propulsion gun).

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A gun that uses electromagnetic, electric or magnetic force to propel a projectile.
   1. A coilgun (magnetic propulsion gun).

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A type of ray gun
