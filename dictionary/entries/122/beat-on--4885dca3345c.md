### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To talk at length; to go on and on; to rant or expound.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

   **Example:** She's beating on about poor service.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To criticize, impugn or shed a bad light on (a group or topic) for political gain.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** She's unpopular enough that photos of her are used in elections to beat on liberal candidates.

   **Example:** You guys are always beating on immigration, but it won't win you this election.
