### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Relevant or pertinent; succinct; specific. (The article "the" may be replaced by a possessive form denoting whose point is being acknowledged.)

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** His letter was short and to the point.

   **Example:** But, more to the point, we should be asking whether this requirement is even necessary in the first place.

   **Example:** Furthermore, to Alice's point, it would be best to get input from the marketing team before finalizing this.

   **Source synonyms:** on point; pithy

   **Source antonyms:** beside the point

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see to, point.

   **Example:** confident to the point of imperiousness
