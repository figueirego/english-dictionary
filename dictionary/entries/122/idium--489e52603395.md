## Suffix

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Forms names of body parts, mainly of invertebrate animals, fungi and plants.
   1. reproductive organs, gametes, and early life stages

      **Usage:** morpheme; most common

      **Example:** basidium, spermidium, planidium, meconidium, glochidium

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Forms names of body parts, mainly of invertebrate animals, fungi and plants.
   1. sensory organs, mainly of arthropods

      **Usage:** morpheme

      **Example:** scolopidium, ommatidium, (not arthropodan) sphaeridium

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Forms names of body parts, mainly of invertebrate animals, fungi and plants.
   1. various segments (arthropods) and functional processes

      **Usage:** morpheme

      **Example:** stethidium, chilidium, limbidium, phyllidium, gonidium

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Forms names of body parts, mainly of invertebrate animals, fungi and plants.
   1. respiratory organs

      **Usage:** morpheme

      **Example:** ctenidium, taenidium

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. cellular compartments

   **Usage:** morpheme; marginal

   **Example:** chromidium, cytoophidium
