### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A group of musicians who play for the recording of an album but are not considered part of the recording artist's normal band.

   **Example:** She performed on 12 albums as a soul singer in a session band before she was able to begin her solo career.
