## Proper noun

### Forms

- Mischief Nights — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A particular night during which young people play pranks and do mischief in their neighborhoods.
   1. The night before May Day.

      **Usage:** Canada; UK; US; dated; originally

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A particular night during which young people play pranks and do mischief in their neighborhoods.
   1. The 30th of October, the night before Halloween

      **Usage:** Canada; UK; US

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A particular night during which young people play pranks and do mischief in their neighborhoods.
   1. The 4th of November, the night before Guy Fawkes Night.

      **Usage:** Canada; UK; US

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A particular night during which young people play pranks and do mischief in their neighborhoods.
   1. The night of the 30th to the 31st of October (the night before Halloween).

      **Usage:** Canada; New-Jersey; New-York; Pennsylvania; UK; US; especially; Maryland; Delaware; Massachusetts; Rhode Island; Connecticut; Maryland; Delaware; Massachusetts; Rhode Island; Connecticut
