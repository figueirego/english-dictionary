<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Ellipsis of Agrippina the Elder (Latin&#58; Agrippina Maior), frequently, Vipsania Agrippina. A woman of the Roman Empire, a member of the Julio-Claudian dynasty. The granddaughter of the first Emperor, Augustus, and, mother of Caligula, Julio-Claudian Emperor of Rome.

   **Usage:** Ancient-Rome; abbreviation; alt-of; countable; ellipsis; uncountable

   **Alternative form of:** Agrippina the Elder

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Ellipsis of Agrippina the Younger (Latin&#58; Agrippina Minor), frequently, Julia Agrippina. A woman of the Roman Empire, a member of the Julio-Claudian dynasty. The great-granddaughter of the first Emperor, Augustus, and, mother of Nero, the final Julio-Claudian Emperor of Rome.

   **Usage:** Ancient-Rome; abbreviation; alt-of; countable; ellipsis; uncountable

   **Alternative form of:** Agrippina the Younger
