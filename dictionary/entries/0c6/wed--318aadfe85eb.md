### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To perform the marriage ceremony for; to join in matrimony.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The priest wed the couple.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To take as one's spouse.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** She wed her first love.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To take a spouse.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To take each other as a spouse.

   **Usage:** reciprocal

   **Example:** They will wed in the summer.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To join or commit to, more or less permanently, as if in marriage.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** I'm not wedded to this proposal; suggest an alternative.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To take to oneself and support; to espouse.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To wager, stake, bet, place a bet, make a wager.

   **Usage:** Northern-England; Scotland

   **Example:** I'd wed my head on that.
