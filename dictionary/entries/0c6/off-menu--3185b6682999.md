### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Not listed in the menu but available if a customer requests it.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; usually

   **Example:** The cheesesteak is off menu.

   **Example:** All these off menu orders — there are so many that they're getting out of hand and bogging down the kitchen.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a dish
