### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Indicates something or someone whose current state is unknown, mixed or depends on some future action.

   **Usage:** humorous; not-comparable

   **Example:** My husband packed the hamper, so until we get to the park, this is Schrödinger's picnic.
