### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To cause (a person or animal) to be fat or fatter.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** We must fatten the turkey in time for Thanksgiving.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To become fat or fatter.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** He gradually fattened in the five years after getting married.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a person or animal

   **Source synonyms:** gain weight; put on weight

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To make thick or thicker (often something containing paper, especially money).

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To become thick or thicker.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** enrich
