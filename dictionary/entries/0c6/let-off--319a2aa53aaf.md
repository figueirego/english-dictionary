### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To cause to explode or come out; to release.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Stand back when you let off fireworks.

   **Example:** to let off a fart

   **Source synonyms:** detonate; [let out](../2b4/let-out--ad1b92a6855a.md); set off; touch off

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To forgive and not punish.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** The boss let me off for breaking the office window, when in theory she could have fined me about 30 dollars.

   **Source synonyms:** excuse; pardon

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Synonym of leave off.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Source synonyms:** leave off (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To ease off.

   **Example:** You let off the throttle while you're shifting, then you resume once you've caught the next gear.

   **Source synonyms:** let up
