### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of the two extant elongated fish of the genus Amia, native to North America, previously thought to be the same species.
   1. especially, a ruddy bowfin (Amia calva).

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of the two extant elongated fish of the genus Amia, native to North America, previously thought to be the same species.
   1. an eyespot bowfin (Amia ocellicauda).
