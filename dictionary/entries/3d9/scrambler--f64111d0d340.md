### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Someone or something that scrambles (in various senses).

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A vine that does not attach itself to its supports.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A device that makes messages intentionally, but reversibly, unintelligible for reasons of privacy or security.

   **Example:** In the movies spies are always talking over cell phones with built-in scramblers.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**
