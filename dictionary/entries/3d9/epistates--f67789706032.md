### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A superintendent or overseer in Ancient Greece.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A man positioned behind a protostates in an Ancient Greek phalanx. For example, in a file of eight men, the protostates were in positions 1, 3, 5 and 7 and the epistates in 2, 4, 6 and 8.

   **Usage:** historical
