### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An integral domain in which every proper ideal factors into a product of prime ideals which is unique (up to permutations).

   **Usage:** ring theory

   **Example:** It can be proved that a Dedekind domain (as defined above) is equivalent to an integral domain in which every proper fractional ideal is invertible.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** algebra; mathematics; sciences
