### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A decorative knot of hair on the crown of the head, sometimes having ribbons or feathers.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A decorative headdress.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A crest of feathers upon the head of a bird.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A crested pigeon, or, a topknot pigeon.

   **Usage:** Australia; informal

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The wool shorn from the crown of a sheep.

   **Usage:** Australia

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Any fish in one of several genera&#58;
   1. The New Zealand topknot, Notoclinus fenestratus

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Any fish in one of several genera&#58;
   1. The Norwegian topknot, Phrynorhombus norvegicus

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Any fish in one of several genera&#58;
   1. The brown topknot, Notoclinus compressus

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. Any fish in one of several genera&#58;
   1. Several species in the genus Zeugopterus

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. Any fish in one of several genera&#58;
   1. A small European flounder (Phrynorhombus regius, syn. Rhombus punctatus).
