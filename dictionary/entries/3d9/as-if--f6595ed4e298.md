## Conjunction · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˌæz‿ˈɪf/ — source labels: General-American; Received-Pronunciation
- IPA: /əz‿ˈɪf/ — source labels: unstressed

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. As though; in a manner suggesting.

   **Usage:** formal

   **Example:** The old man stumbled, as if he were about to fall.

   **Example:** It looks as if it's going to rain.

   **Example:** She jumped all of a sudden, as if stung.

   **Source synonyms:** like

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In mimicry of.

   **Example:** When the teacher’s back was turned, the class clown would hold his stomach as if he were ill.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Indicating something that the speaker deems very unlikely, or is adamant that he or she would not do (see also interjection sense below).

   **Example:** How can you even think that? As if I would inform on you to the police!

## Interjection · Record 2

- IPA: /əz‿ˈɪf/ — source labels: unstressed

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Indicates that the speaker deems something highly unlikely.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** "I'm going to clean your whole house." — "As if!"

   **Example:** Me, take up yoga and become a vegetarian? As if!
