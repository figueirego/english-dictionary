## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Opposing or countering tipping motions.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The wheelchair was fitted with an antitipping device.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Opposing or countering tipping (the dumping of rubbish).

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The antitipping laws will introduce fines for leaving goods by the roadside.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Opposing or countering the paying of gratuities.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Many restaurants in tourist areas failed to comply with the antitipping legislation.
