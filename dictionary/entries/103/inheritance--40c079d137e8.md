### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The passing of title to an estate upon death.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. That which a person is entitled to inherit, by law or testament, such as the part of an estate (i.e., a portion).

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The act or mechanism of inheriting; the state of having inherited.

   **Usage:** especially; uncountable

   **Example:** The Indo-European languages share various similarities as a result of their inheritance from a common ancestor.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; human-sciences; linguistics; natural-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The biological attributes passed hereditarily from ancestors to their offspring.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; genetic algorithms

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The mechanism whereby parts of a superclass are available to instances of its subclass.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; object-oriented programming

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; programming; sciences
