### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A crucible, or similar pot, used to fuse mixtures of metals, etc.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A place where many divergent things (often races or cultures, but also talents) come together and are homogenized.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** The festival is a true racial melting pot; people from dozens of countries take part speaking many languages.
