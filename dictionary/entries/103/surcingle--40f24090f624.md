### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A long unpadded strap to pass over and keep in place a blanket, pack or saddle on an animal.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A piece of tack wrapped around the belly of a horse, to use when longeing.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>
