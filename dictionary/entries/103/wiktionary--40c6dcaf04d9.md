### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A collaborative project run by the Wikimedia Foundation to produce a free and complete dictionary in every language; the dictionaries, collectively, produced by that project.

   **Example:** He had logged in to Wiktionary two months ago.

   **Source synonyms:** Wikt

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of the editions of the dictionary, written in a certain language.

   **Source synonyms:** Wikt
