### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make or attempt to make something seem less important, likely, or obvious.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** The essay attempts to play down the role slavery had in the Civil War.

   **Example:** The senator played down the threat of a recession.

   **Example:** During the wave of anti-immigrant sentiment, Miguel played down his immigrant status by removing Chicano patois from his speech and adopting the nickname "Mike."

   **Source synonyms:** de-emphasize; downplay; bagatellize; banalize; belittle; cut down (idiomatic); de-emphasize; denigrate; degrade; deprecate; derogate; disparage; dispraise; downplay; lilliputianize; make little of (idiomatic); make nothing of (idiomatic); minimize; play down (idiomatic); set at nought (archaic); superficialize; trivialize; underestimate; underplay; understate; vilipend (dated; formal)
