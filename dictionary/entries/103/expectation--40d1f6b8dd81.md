### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act or state of expecting or looking forward to an event as about to happen.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. That which is expected or looked for.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. That which is expected or looked for.
   1. An implicit obligation or duty held by another in someone's view.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Example:** He struggled with his parents' expectations.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The prospect of the future; grounds upon which something excellent is expected to occur; prospect of anything good to come, especially of property or rank.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The value of any chance (as the prospect of prize or property) which depends upon some contingent event.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The first moment; the expected value; the long-run average value of a variable over many independent repetitions of an experiment.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences; statistics

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The arithmetic mean.

   **Usage:** colloquial; countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences; statistics

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. The leaving of a disease principally to the efforts of nature to effect a cure.

   **Usage:** countable; rare; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences
