**Source sense R1.S1**

1. (linguistics) Emphasis on a proposition’s truth value, often marked in English by stress on an auxiliary or negation.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences
