### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The block of programming on television during the middle of the evening, usually between 7&#58;00 pm and 11&#58;00 pm.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** The station is moving the new comedy to a prime time slot next month.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** broadcasting; media; radio; television

   **Source synonyms:** power hour

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The busiest or most important period.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Summer is prime time for road construction.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Maturity; the state at which a person or product will be accepted by the mainstream.

   **Usage:** figuratively; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Spring.

   **Usage:** obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A new period or time of youthfulness; the beginning of something.

   **Usage:** obsolete; uncountable
