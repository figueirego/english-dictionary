**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In the International System of Units, the derived unit of electric charge; the amount of electric charge carried by a current of 1 ampere flowing for 1 second. Symbol&#58; C

   **Example:** He is charged up with enough coulombs to make his hair stand on end.
