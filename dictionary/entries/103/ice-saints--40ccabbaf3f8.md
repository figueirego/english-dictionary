**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The saints Pancras of Rome, Servatius of Tongeren, Mamertus of Vienne and/or Boniface of Tarsus, and sometimes Sophia of Rome, whose feast days are on May 11th though 15th.

   **Usage:** capitalized; plural; plural-normally; sometimes
