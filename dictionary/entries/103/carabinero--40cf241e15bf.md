## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A frontier guard (or similar) in Spain or South America.
   1. A member of the uniformed Chilean national police force and gendarmerie, the "Carabineros de Chile".

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A frontier guard (or similar) in Spain or South America.
   1. A member of an armed force, in Spain between 1829 and 1940, whose mission was to patrol the coasts and borders of the country.

      **Usage:** historical
