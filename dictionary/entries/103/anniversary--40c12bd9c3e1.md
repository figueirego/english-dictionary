### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A day that is an exact number of years (to the day) since a given significant event occurred. Often preceded by an ordinal number indicating the number of years.

   **Example:** Today is the fiftieth anniversary of the end of the war.

   **Example:** A 'millennium' is a thousand-year anniversary.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A day that is an exact number of years (to the day) since a given significant event occurred. Often preceded by an ordinal number indicating the number of years.
   1. Such a day that commemorates a wedding.

      **Usage:** especially

      **Example:** We are celebrating our tenth anniversary today.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A day subsequent in time to a given event by some significant period other than a year (especially as prefixed by the amount of time in question).

   **Usage:** broadly
