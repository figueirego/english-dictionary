### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To progress cyclically through various phases, such as growth and senescence.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To alternate; to increase and diminish in turn.

   **Example:** The manifestation of such philosophy seemed to wax and wane, being the most common one time, but virtually without followers at another, apparently disappearing.
