### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An optional light meal consumed shortly before going to bed.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any meal eaten in the evening; dinner eaten in the evening, rather than at noon.

   **Usage:** Atlantic-Canada; Boston; Canadian-Prairies; Midwestern-US; South-Africa; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** We normally have supper at 7.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A meal from a chip shop consisting of a deep-fried food with chips.

   **Usage:** Northern-England; Northern-Ireland; Scotland; countable; slang; uncountable

   **Example:** a fish supper
