### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A form (expression) that does not use limits, implicitly or explicitly.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** There is no known representation of π in closed form&#58; each expression for it includes an integral, an infinite series, or an infinite continued fraction.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences
