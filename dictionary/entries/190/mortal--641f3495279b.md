### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Susceptible to death by aging, sickness, injury, or wound; not immortal.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Causing death; deadly, fatal, killing, lethal (now only of wounds, injuries etc.).

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Punishable by death.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Fatally vulnerable.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Of or relating to the time of death.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Affecting as if with power to kill; deathly; related to a life-and-death struggle.

   **Example:** mortal enemy

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Human; belonging or pertaining to people who are mortal.

   **Example:** mortal wit or knowledge; mortal power

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Very painful or tedious; wearisome.

   **Example:** a sermon lasting two mortal hours

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. Very drunk.

   **Usage:** Geordie; Scotland; slang

   **Source synonyms:** mortalled

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. Causing spiritual death (the destruction of charity in the soul) and thus, a disruption of one's relationship with God.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** lifestyle; religion

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a sin

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. An intensifier.

   **Usage:** colloquial; dated
