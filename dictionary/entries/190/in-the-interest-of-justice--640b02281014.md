## Prep\_phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The legal principle which allows judges to exercise discretion to ensure fairness and equity in a case, rather than adhere to rigid procedural rules.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The reason of case dismissal used when the judge decides that justice will be best served when the case is dismissed. This reason can also be used with other than dismissal motions.

   **Usage:** US; idiomatic

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law
