### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An edible clam with a hard shell found along the Atlantic Coast of North America, from species Mercenaria mercenaria, formerly Venus mercenaria.

   **Source synonyms:** hard clam; round clam; Mercenaria mercenaria

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An edible clam with a hard shell found along the Atlantic Coast of North America, from species Mercenaria mercenaria, formerly Venus mercenaria.
   1. The largest size of such an edible clam, generally considered only fit for use in chowders and other clam dishes.

      **Source synonyms:** hard clam; round clam; Mercenaria mercenaria; chowder clam

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A similar edible clam, of species Arctica islandica, found along coasts around the North Atlantic, generally in deeper waters.

   **Source synonyms:** ocean quahog; black quahog; mahogany clam; Icelandic cyprine
