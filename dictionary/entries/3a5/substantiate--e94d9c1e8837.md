<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To verify something by supplying evidence; to authenticate or corroborate.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** substantiate allegations

   **Example:** substantiate a claim

   **Example:** The lawyer was asked to substantiate her claims.

   **Example:** He could not substantiate the rumors with evidence.

   **Source synonyms:** vouch
