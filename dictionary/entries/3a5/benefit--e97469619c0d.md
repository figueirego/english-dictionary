   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The whole scene was staged for his benefit, and it completely fooled him.

   **Example:** Since my wife is Canadian, whenever we have dinner with my family, they keep bringing up anything they've heard about Canada lately for her benefit.
