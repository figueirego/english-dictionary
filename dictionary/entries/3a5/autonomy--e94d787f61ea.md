### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The right or condition of self-government; freedom to act or function independently.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source antonyms:** dependency; heteronomy; servitude; nonautonomy; inoperability

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A self-governing country or region.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The capacity to make an informed, uncoerced decision.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; philosophy; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The capacity of a system to make a decision about its actions without the involvement of another system or operator.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; mechanical-engineering; mechanics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

   **Source antonyms:** heteronomy; incapacity

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The status of a church whose highest-ranking bishop is appointed by the patriarch of the mother church, but which is self-governing in all other respects. Compare autocephaly.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity
