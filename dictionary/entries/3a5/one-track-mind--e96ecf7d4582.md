### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. That said to be possessed by someone who is obsessed with something or only able to think of one thing.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** He has a one-track mind. All he ever talks about is trains and railroads.

   **Source synonyms:** obstinacy; doggedness; conviction; insistence; pertinacity; self-will; recalcitrance; resolve; stubbornness; temerity; tenacity; vehemence; intransigence; pigheadedness
