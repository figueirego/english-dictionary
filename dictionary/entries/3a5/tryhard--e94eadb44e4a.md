### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person usually of little talent who tries hard to succeed, especially through imitation, usually to gain fame or popularity.

   **Usage:** derogatory; informal

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
