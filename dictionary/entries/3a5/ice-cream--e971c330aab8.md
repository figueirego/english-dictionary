### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A dessert made from frozen sweetened cream or a similar substance, usually flavoured.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Strawberries are particularly delicious with ice cream.

   **Example:** We were having chocolate ice cream.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A portion of ice cream on a stick, in a cup or in a wafer cone.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** Can I have some money for an ice cream, Mum?
