### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make somebody able (to do, or to be, something); to give sufficient ability or power to do or to be; to give strength or ability to.

   **Source synonyms:** empower; endow

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To affirm; to make firm and strong.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To qualify or approve for some role or position; to render sanction or authorization to; to confirm suitability for.

   **Source synonyms:** let; permit; authorize

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To yield the opportunity or provide the possibility for something; to provide with means, opportunities, and the like.

   **Source synonyms:** allow

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To imply or tacitly confer excuse for an action or a behavior.

   **Example:** His parents enabled him to go on buying drugs.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To put a circuit element into action by supplying a suitable input pulse.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; electrical-engineering; electricity; electromagnetism; electronics; energy; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To activate, to make operational (especially of a function of an electronic or mechanical device).

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; computing; electrical-engineering; electricity; electromagnetism; electronics; energy; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** activate; turn on

   **Source antonyms:** disable
