### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Unified; sticking together; making up a whole.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Orderly, logical and consistent.

   **Source antonyms:** disjointed

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Aesthetically ordered.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Having a natural or due agreement of parts; harmonious&#58; a coherent design.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Of waves having the same direction, wavelength and phase, as light in a laser.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Attaching or pressing against an organ of the same nature.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Belonging to a specific class of sheaves having particularly manageable properties closely linked to the geometrical properties of the underlying space. See Coherent sheaf on Wikipedia.Wikipedia

   **Usage:** sheaf theory

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a sheaf

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. In a technical sense, determined by the (topology of) the subsets. Formally, Such that 𝒯 is the finest topology on T for which the inclusion maps ιₐ&#58;C\_a→T are continuous, where each C\_a is considered with its subspace topology.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences; topology

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a topology 𝒯 on a set T with respect to a family C\_a of subsets of T

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. Finitely generated and such that all finitely generated submodules are finitely presented.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** algebra; mathematics; sciences

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a module

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. Such that every finitely generated (left) ideal is finitely presented.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** algebra; mathematics; sciences

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a ring
