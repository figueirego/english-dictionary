### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The point where the clutch engages when the pedal is released.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** automotive; transport; vehicles

   **Source synonyms:** bite point

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The precise position at which a control mechanism as been moved just enough to cause a device to operate.

   **Usage:** broadly

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The point or set of circumstances when a particular action can begin or a critical transition can occur.

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The level of aggravation that causes someone or something to lash out or bite.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The position of the jaw where the upper and lower teeth meet during a bite

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The location where something is bitten.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The point of a blade or similar item where it begins to bite or penetrate.
