## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A dirt road, created by the passage of thousands of tobacco casks being rolled, mainly by people or mules, from plantations to river steamboats or trucks.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A region of North Carolina historically associated with the production of tobacco.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The four North Carolina schools in the Atlantic Coast Conference of the National Collegiate Athletic Association.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports
