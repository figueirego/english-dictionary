### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A task assigned to a group of people, especially schoolchildren or army recruits, where they move around an area and pick up rubbish from the ground.

   **Usage:** Australia; countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** emu parade; emu patrol

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any activity which involves repetitive bending down over a wide area, such as searching for clues relating to somebody's disappearance.

   **Usage:** Australia; countable; uncommon; uncountable
