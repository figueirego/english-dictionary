### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To say (something) unexpectedly.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He came out with a very dubious excuse.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To say (something) in a plainspoken way instead of euphemistically or obliquely, after reluctantly hesitating to do so.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Just come out with it already — you think you're too good for me!

   **Source synonyms:** spit out

   **Source antonyms:** conceal; hide; obfuscate; sugarcoat

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To make (something) public or available to the public.

   **Example:** We're coming out with the new product on Tuesday.

   **Source synonyms:** announce; introduce; release; reveal
