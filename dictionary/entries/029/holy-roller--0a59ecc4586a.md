### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A member of any Christian church characterized by ecstatic behaviour; especially of the Pentecostal Church.

   **Usage:** derogatory; informal; usually

   **Example:** I attended a worship service where I was astounded to see holy rollers convulsing on the floor and speaking in tongues.

   **Source synonyms:** Holy Jumper

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A devoutly religious Christian person.

   **Usage:** derogatory; informal

   **Example:** She's such a holy roller that she steers every conversation around to the joys of religion.
