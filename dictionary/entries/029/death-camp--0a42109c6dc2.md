### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A camp destined to cause the death of its inmates.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A camp destined to cause the death of its inmates.
   1. Such a camp built during the Holocaust by the Nazis to kill mainly Jews, generally by means of gassing.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A prison camp in which a large number of prisoners die, ascribed to various reasons such as starvation, disease, brutality, and neglect, but not necessarily thought to be intentional.
