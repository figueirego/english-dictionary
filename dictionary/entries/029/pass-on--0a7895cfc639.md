   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source synonyms:** proceed

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To convey or communicate.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Can you pass on the information next time you see him?

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To transfer (something) to someone, especially by handing or bequeathing it to the next person in a series.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To skip or decline.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I'll pass on dessert, thanks.
