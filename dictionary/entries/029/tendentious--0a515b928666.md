### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having a tendency, written or spoken, with a partisan, biased or prejudiced purpose, especially a controversial one; implicitly or explicitly slanted; biased.

   **Example:** That he was a supporter of the cause was clear, because his reports from the front were tendentious.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of persons or their words

   **Source synonyms:** partisan; slanted; partial; prejudiced

   **Source antonyms:** objective; impartial; unbiased; fair; balanced
