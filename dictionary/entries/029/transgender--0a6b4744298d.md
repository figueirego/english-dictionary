### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of a person&#58; having a gender (identity) which is different from one's assigned sex; that is, the identity of a trans man, trans woman, or someone non-binary, for example, agender, bigender, or third-gender.

   **Usage:** broadly; not-comparable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of a person&#58; having a gender (identity) which is different from one's assigned sex; that is, the identity of a trans man, trans woman, or someone non-binary, for example, agender, bigender, or third-gender.
   1. Of a person&#58; having a gender (identity) which is opposite from the sex one was assigned at birth&#58; being assigned male but having a female gender, or vice versa (that is, not including a non-binary identity).

      **Usage:** broadly; not-comparable; strictly

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of a person&#58; transgressing or not identifying with culturally conventional gender roles and categories of male or female.

   **Usage:** broadly; not-comparable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Of or pertaining to transgender people (adjective sense 1), or their experiences or identity.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Of a space&#58; intended primarily for transgender people.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Of a space&#58; available for use by transgender people, rather than only non-transgender people.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Synonym of crossgender (“across multiple genders”).

   **Usage:** dated; not-comparable

   **Source synonyms:** crossgender (synonym; synonym-of)
