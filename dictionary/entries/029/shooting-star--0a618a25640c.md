### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A meteor, especially a streak of light in the night sky, caused by a meteoroid burning up as it enters the Earth's atmosphere.

   **Source synonyms:** falling star; meteor; faxed star

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of several wild flowering plants in the genus Primula (synonym Dodecatheon), mostly found in Western North America.

   **Source synonyms:** shootingstar

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
