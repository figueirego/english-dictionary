### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To remain silent.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Granny is asleep, so keep quiet while you play.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To refrain from talking about something; to keep a secret.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** Remember it's a surprise party, so keep quiet about it. — Okay. I'll keep it still

   **Example:** I'm quitting my job by the end of the month, but my boss doesn't know. Please keep it quiet.
