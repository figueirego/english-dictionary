### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To do something for someone, or to someone, similar to what they did for you or to you (whether good or bad).

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I did some snow shoveling for him when he was ill, and he was kind enough to return the favor this year. \[positive valence\]

   **Example:** They ignored my request, so I was happy to return the favor when I got the chance. \[negative valence produced by sarcasm\]
