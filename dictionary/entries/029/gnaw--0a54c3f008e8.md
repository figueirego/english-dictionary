### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To bite something persistently, especially something tough.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** The dog gnawed the bone until it broke in two.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To produce excessive anxiety or worry.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Her comment gnawed at me all day and I couldn't think about anything else.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To corrode; to fret away; to waste.
