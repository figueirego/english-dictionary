**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A full-body costume of a cartoon character (typically a mascot of cartoonish proportions) originating in Japan.

   **Source synonyms:** kig; kigu

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A full-body cosplay of a human cartoon character with realistic proportions paired with an anime-style mask, originating in Japan; also (metonymic), the anime-style mask used in such a cosplay.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anime; broadcasting; film; media; television
