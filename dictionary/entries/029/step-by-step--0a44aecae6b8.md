## Adverb

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. From one stage to the next in sequence.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** She assembled the toy step by step, following the instructions carefully.

   **Example:** We were shown the process step by step.

**Source sense R1.S2**
