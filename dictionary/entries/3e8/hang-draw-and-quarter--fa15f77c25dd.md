### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To execute (someone) by hanging until nearly dead, followed by castration, disembowelling, beheading and quartering; especially as a punishment for high treason.

   **Usage:** historical; transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To savagely attack, or attempt to utterly destroy.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** hanged, drawn and quartered by the press
