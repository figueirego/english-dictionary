## Adjective

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /bʌɪˈkaməɹəl/ — source labels: UK

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Being or having a system with two, often unequal, chambers or compartments; of, signifying, relating to, or being the product of such a two-chambered system.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** the bicameral anatomy of the brain

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of, having, or relating to two separate legislative chambers or houses.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government

   **Source antonyms:** unicameral; tricameral

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of a script or typeface&#58; having two cases, upper case and lower case.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** media; publishing; typography

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Relating to the functions of the two cerebral hemispheres in the history of human beings ‘hearing’ the speech of gods or idols, according to Julian Jaynes's theory of the bicameral mind.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; psychology; sciences
