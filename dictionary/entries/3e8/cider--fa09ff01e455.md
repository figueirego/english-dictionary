### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An alcoholic, often sparkling (carbonated) beverage made from fermented apples.

   **Usage:** Australia; Canada; Ireland; New-Zealand; UK; countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** hard cider; apple cider

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. This or any similar alcoholic drink made from similar fruit, such as pears.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A non-alcoholic uncarbonated beverage consisting of the juice of early-harvest apples, usually unfiltered and still containing pulp; without pulp such a beverage is called apple juice.

   **Usage:** Canada; US; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** I picked up a jug of cider for the kids.

   **Source synonyms:** sweet cider; apple cider

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A non-alcoholic carbonated beverage made from apples or pears.

   **Usage:** Australia; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A non-alcoholic, lemon-lime flavored carbonated beverage.

   **Usage:** Japan; South-Korea; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A cup, glass, or serving of any of these beverages.

   **Usage:** countable
