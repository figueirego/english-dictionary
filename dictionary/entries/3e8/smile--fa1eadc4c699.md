### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A facial expression comprised by flexing the muscles of both ends of one's mouth, often showing the front teeth, without vocalisation, and in humans is a common involuntary or voluntary expression of happiness, pleasure, amusement, goodwill, or anxiety.

   **Example:** She's got a perfect smile.

   **Example:** He has a sinister smile.

   **Example:** She had a smile on her face.

   **Example:** He always puts a smile on my face.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Favour; propitious regard.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** the smile of the gods

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A drink bought by one person for another.

   **Usage:** dated; slang

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To have (a smile) on one's face.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** When you smile, the whole world smiles with you.

   **Example:** I don't know what he's smiling about.

   **Example:** She smiles a beautiful smile.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To express by smiling.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to smile consent, or a welcome

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To express amusement, pleasure, or love and kindness.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To look cheerful and joyous; to have an appearance suited to excite joy.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The sun smiled down from a clear summer sky.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To be propitious or favourable; to countenance.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The gods smiled on his labours.

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. Of ackee fruit&#58; to open fully, indicating that it is no longer toxic, and ready to be picked.

   **Usage:** intransitive
