### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An Asian vine (several species in the genus Pueraria, but mostly Pueraria montana var. lobata, syn. Pueraria lobata in the US), grown as a root starch, and which is a notorious invasive weed in the United States.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source synonyms:** Chinese arrowroot; East Asian arrowroot; Japanese arrowroot; kudzu vine; mile-a-minute

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A starch extracted from the root that is used in traditional East Asian medicine and cuisine.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** cooking; food; lifestyle; medicine; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** kudzu powder
