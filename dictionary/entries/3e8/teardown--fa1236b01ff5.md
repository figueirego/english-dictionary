## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

- teardowns — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A well-maintained structure purchased and torn down to make way for a new structure.

   **Example:** The downtown redevelopment involved numerous teardowns.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; real-estate

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The process of opening and disassembling a device to inspect, showcase, or refurbish its components.

   **Example:** Every new shop manual that our team writes is based on a complete teardown.

   **Source synonyms:** stripdown

   **Source antonyms:** build; buildup
