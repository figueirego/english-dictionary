### Senses

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Usage:** figuratively; not-comparable

## Noun · Record 3

### Senses

<a id="r3-s1"></a>

**Source sense R3.S1**

1. The entirety of something of duration.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** The man hated every second of the ten years he served in prison for a crime he didn't commit.

   **Example:** After enduring traumatic experiences, many people are likely to remember every second of them.
