**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** espionage; government; military; politics; war

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To gain widespread acceptance in a group or society, especially where there was not any before.

   **Usage:** figuratively; idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** Long an outsider in Western politics, Portugal came in from the cold after the 1974 Carnation Revolution.
