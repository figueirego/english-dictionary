### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To have as one's origin, birthplace or nationality.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Most tourists in Mallorca come from England. My girlfriend comes from Sweden.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To be derived from.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To derive one's opinion or argument from; to take as a conceptual starting point.

   **Usage:** transitive; slightly informal

   **Example:** Even though I have a more progressive philosophy, I can understand where he's coming from. There was a time in my life when it was hard for me to adapt to change, myself.

   **Source antonyms:** drive at; get at
