### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To interpret erroneously, to understand incorrectly; to misunderstand.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** His words were misconstrued as an insult.

   **Example:** It’s easy to misconstrue her silence as agreement.

   **Example:** misconstrue the meaning

   **Example:** misconstrue someone’s intentions
