### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Immunity from the local laws of a certain area, especially due to diplomatic negotiation.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A territory or space that is under the power of a certain area but not defined by its norms.

   **Usage:** countable; usually
