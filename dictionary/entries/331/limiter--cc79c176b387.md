### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. That which limits or confines.

   **Example:** The limiter automatically put on the brakes to keep the machine from going too fast.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A circuit that allows signals below a specified input threshold to pass unaffected while attenuating the peaks of stronger signals.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; electrical-engineering; electricity; electromagnetism; electronics; energy; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics
