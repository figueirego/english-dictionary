### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Similar or identical in value, meaning or effect; virtually equal.

   **Example:** To burn calories, a thirty-minute jog is equivalent to a couple of hamburgers.

   **Source synonyms:** on a par; equipollent

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of two sets, having a one-to-one correspondence.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** equinumerous

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Relating to the corresponding elements of an equivalence relation.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Of two categories, (informally) such that one is essentially a relabeling of the other; (formally) related by a pair of functors such the composition of the one with the other is naturally isomorphic to the identity functor.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** category-theory; computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Having the equal ability to combine.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Of a map, equal-area.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** cartography; geography; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Equal in measure but not admitting of superposition; applied to magnitudes.

   **Example:** A square may be equivalent to a triangle.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences
