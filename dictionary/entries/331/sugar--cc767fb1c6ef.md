**Source sense R2.S1**

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** John heavily sugars his coffee.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To make (something unpleasant) seem less so.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** She has a gift for sugaring what would otherwise be harsh words.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

3. In making maple sugar, to complete the process of boiling down the syrup till it is thick enough to crystallize; to approach or reach the state of granulation; with the preposition off.

   **Usage:** Canada; US; regional

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; entomology; natural-sciences

   **Usage:** transitive
