### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A mathematical statement of some importance that has been proven to be true. Minor theorems are often called propositions. Theorems which are not very interesting in themselves but are an essential part of a bigger theorem's proof are called lemmas.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A mathematical statement that is expected to be true.

   **Usage:** colloquial; nonstandard

   **Example:** Fermat's Last Theorem was known thus long before it was proved in the 1990s.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A syntactically correct expression that is deducible from the given axioms of a deductive system.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; logic; mathematics; philosophy; sciences
