### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. to give one's time, focus one's efforts, commit oneself, etc. entirely for, on, or to a certain matter; to consecrate.

   **Example:** They devoted their lives to following Jesus Christ.

   **Example:** I devoted this afternoon to repainting my study, and nothing will get in my way.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. to consign over; to doom

   **Example:** to devote one to destruction

   **Example:** The city was devoted to the flames.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. to execrate; to curse
