### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An individual sheet of glass or other transparent material in a window, door, etc.

   **Example:** We need doubling glazing as this window pane lets out lots of heat.

   **Example:** Perspex panes

   **Source synonyms:** windowpane

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A portion of a user interface that typically makes up part of a larger window and may be docked or snapped into position.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; graphical-user-interface; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A division; a distinct piece or compartment of any surface.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A square of a checkered or plaid pattern.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. One of the openings in a slashed garment, showing the bright colored silk, or the like, within; hence, the piece of colored or other stuff so shown.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A compartment of a surface, or a flat space; hence, one side or face of a building.

   **Example:** metal panes on an outhouse

   **Example:** An octagonal tower is said to have eight panes.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** architecture

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A subdivision of an irrigated surface between a feeder and an outlet drain.

   **Example:** way-panes

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. One of the flat surfaces, or facets, of any object having several sides.

   **Example:** wooden panes of an insulation box

   **Source synonyms:** panel

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. One of the eight facets surrounding the table of a brilliant-cut diamond.

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. A set of postage stamps of the same kind, as part of a single (printed) sheet. An individual pane might be sent to a post office for public purchase.

   **Example:** pane of stamps

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; philately
