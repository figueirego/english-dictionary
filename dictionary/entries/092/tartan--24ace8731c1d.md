### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Woven woollen fabric with a distinctive pattern of coloured stripes intersecting at right angles originally associated with Scottish Highlanders, now with different clans (though this only dates from the late 18th century) and some Scottish families and institutions having their own patterns; (countable) a particular type of such fabric.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** plaid; Scotch plaid

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Woven woollen fabric with a distinctive pattern of coloured stripes intersecting at right angles originally associated with Scottish Highlanders, now with different clans (though this only dates from the late 18th century) and some Scottish families and institutions having their own patterns; (countable) a particular type of such fabric.
   1. A pattern used on such fabric.

      **Usage:** countable

      **Source synonyms:** plaid; Scotch plaid

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Woven woollen fabric with a distinctive pattern of coloured stripes intersecting at right angles originally associated with Scottish Highlanders, now with different clans (though this only dates from the late 18th century) and some Scottish families and institutions having their own patterns; (countable) a particular type of such fabric.
   1. Clothing made from this fabric.

      **Usage:** uncountable

      **Source synonyms:** plaid; Scotch plaid
