## Verb

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To lie down to sleep for the night, usually of livestock or machinery, or of people when sleeping somewhere worse than normal, often camping or in prison.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The horses will bed down once they get in the stable.

   **Example:** The tent was an uncomfortable place to bed down.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To put an animal to rest for the night.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** We'll bed down the cattle and head into town.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To sleep with or sleep alongside (a person or pet).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Who has she been bedding down with?

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To make or become permanent, fixed or wedded to a particular setup, way of thinking, style or manner.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive; figuratively

   **Example:** A habit can easily become bedded down.

   **Example:** The new batch of employees soon bedded down.
