## Noun

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A four-base hit, allowing the batter to circle the bases and reach home safely in one play without any errors being committed by the defensive team.

   **Example:** The first baseman hit a home run to lead off the ninth.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; baseball; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

   **Source synonyms:** homer; longball

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A three-base hit, a legal hit where the batter gets to third base without stopping illegally and without the opposing team attempting to put the batter out on earlier bases, scoring one run immediately.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; games; hobbies; lifestyle; pesäpallo; sports

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The portion of a journey that ends at home.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** I'm on the home run.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A success, especially a popular success.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** The product was a home run.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Sexual intercourse.

   **Usage:** euphemistic; figuratively; slang

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A connection from a central distribution point or circuit breaker to a destination.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; electrical; electrical-engineering; electricity; electromagnetism; energy; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics
