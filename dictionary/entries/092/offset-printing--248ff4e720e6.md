## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Printing by the offset process, in which ink is carried from a metal plate to a rubber blanket (“offset cylinder”) and from there to the printing surface.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** offset; offset lithography
