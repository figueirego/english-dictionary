## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

- crybullies — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who engages in intimidation, harassment, or other abusive behaviour while claiming to be a victim, often using the perceived victimization to induce others to engage in bullying of the intended victim.

   **Usage:** derogatory
