## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A certain degree of strength in alcoholic liquor, as formerly ascertained by the floating or sinking of glass globules of different specific gravities thrown into it, and later by more accurate meters.

   **Usage:** obsolete; uncountable; distilling

   **Example:** a brandy above bead proof

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A degree of strength in alcoholic liquor as shown by beads (small bubbles) remaining on its surface, or at the side of the glass, when shaken.

   **Usage:** obsolete; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** glass proof
