### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A split or separation within a group or organization, typically caused by discord.

   **Example:** A schism broke the organization into two rivals.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A movement or organization that splits off from a larger body.

   **Example:** He founded a schism that eventually rivaled the original organization in size.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A formal division or split within a religious body.

   **Example:** The schism between Sunnis and Shias happened quite early in Islamic history.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** lifestyle; religion

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A split within Christianity whereby a group is no longer in communion with the Church or its ecclesiastical authorities, but shares essentially the same beliefs; in other words, a political split without the introduction of heresy.

   **Usage:** Eastern Orthodoxy

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Catholicism; Christianity
