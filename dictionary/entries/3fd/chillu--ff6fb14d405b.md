### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A type of letter used in Malayalam which does not have an inherent vowel, used to represent a pure consonant without the use of a virama.

   **Example:** There are five chillus currently in use&#58; ൺ (ṇ), ൻ (ṉ), ർ (ṟ), ൽ (l) and ൾ (ḷ). There are also four archaic ones&#58; ൿ (k), ൔ (m), ൕ (y) and ൖ (ḻ).

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** grammar; human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** chillaksharam
