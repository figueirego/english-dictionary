### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To grope; to explore an area with one's hands, usually in search of something.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** I felt around in my pockets for my keyring in vain.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To gather information sneakily; to seek out information without revealing that one is actively searching for it.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; informal
