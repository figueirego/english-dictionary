## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of several groupings of elements that share traits with iron in one way or another.
   1. Row 4 (period 4) of the periodic table, or a large subset thereof.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of several groupings of elements that share traits with iron in one way or another.
   1. Row 4 (period 4) of the periodic table, or a large subset thereof.
      1. A smaller subset thereof comprising iron, cobalt, and nickel.

         **Source synonyms:** iron triad

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of several groupings of elements that share traits with iron in one way or another.
   1. Group 8 of the periodic table, comprising iron, ruthenium, osmium, and hassium.
