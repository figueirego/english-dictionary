<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To drop or throw trash without properly disposing of it (as discarding in public areas rather than trash receptacles).

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** By tossing the bottle out the window, he was littering.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To scatter carelessly about.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To strew (a place) with scattered articles.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To give birth to, in the manner of animals.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To produce a litter of young.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To supply (cattle etc.) with litter; to cover with litter, as the floor of a stall.

   **Usage:** transitive
