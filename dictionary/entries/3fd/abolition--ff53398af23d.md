### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of abolishing; an annulling; abrogation.

   **Example:** the abolition of debts; the abolition of laws; the abolition of slavery; the abolition of police; the abolition of taxes

   **Example:** The campaign for the abolition of slavery changed the course of history.

   **Source synonyms:** annulment; devalidation; revocation; abolition; abrogation; annulment; cancellation; deauthentication; devalidation; invalidation; nullification; renunciation; revocation; termination; vitiation

   **Source antonyms:** establishment; foundation

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The state of being abolished.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The ending of the slave trade or of slavery.

   **Usage:** UK; US; capitalized; historical; often

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The ending of convict transportation.

   **Usage:** Australia; capitalized; historical; often

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An amnesty; a putting out of memory.

   **Usage:** obsolete
