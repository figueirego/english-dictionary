### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To share a border with.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The town borders on the forest, so there are often deer on the road.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To be almost like some other thing; to verge on.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** His behaviour borders on insanity.
