### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A crab, especially the blue crab, that has recently molted and whose new exoskeleton has not yet hardened, rendering the entire animal soft and edible.

   **Source synonyms:** soft-shelled crab
