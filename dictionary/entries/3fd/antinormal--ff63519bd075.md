## Noun · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈæn.tiˌnɔɹm(ə)l/ — source labels: US

### Forms

- antinormals — plural

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A normal that points opposite to convention. For example, in solid geometry it is conventional for normals to point outwards, so an antinormal in solid geometry refers to a normal pointing inwards.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences
