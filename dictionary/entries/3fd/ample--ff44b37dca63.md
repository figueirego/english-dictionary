## Determiner · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈæm.pəl/ — source labels: General-American; Received-Pronunciation

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A fully sufficient or abundant quantity of; enough or more than enough.

   **Example:** We have ample time to finish the task.

   **Example:** It is a large house with ample space for all of us.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A quantity (of something) that is fully sufficient; plenty.

   **Example:** We don't need any more. We already have ample.

## Adjective · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈæm.pəl/ — source labels: General-American; Received-Pronunciation

### Forms

- ampler — comparative
- amplest — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Large; great in size, extent, capacity, or bulk; for example spacious, roomy or widely extended.

   **Example:** We have an ample supply of water

   **Example:** She has a very ample bosom.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Very adequate.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. Not contracted or brief; not concise; extended; diffusive

   **Example:** an ample story
