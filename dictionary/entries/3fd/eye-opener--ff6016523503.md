### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Something that causes the eyes to open, or that opens the eyes.

   **Usage:** literally

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Something that provides a sudden insight, or makes something clear that was previously mysterious.

   **Usage:** informal

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A startling or shocking revelation.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; informal

   **Example:** A visit to the slaughterhouse was a real eye-opener to anyone who thought they understood where their food came from.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A strong alcoholic beverage, especially one consumed in the morning.

   **Usage:** euphemistic; informal

   **Example:** As soon as I got on the plane I asked the flight attendant for an eye-opener, but all he had to offer was orange juice.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An attractive woman.

   **Usage:** informal; sometimes objectifying

   **Source synonyms:** eye candy
