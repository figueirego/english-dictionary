### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A triangle.

   **Usage:** countable; rare

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An ancient triangular harp of Oriental origin which had four strings and was often used for banquet music. Also called sabbeka, sackbut, sambuca.

   **Usage:** countable; historical

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A division consisting of three star signs.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** The Cancer trigon is composed of Cancer, Scorpio and Pisces

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astrology; human-sciences; mysticism; philosophy; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A trine; an aspect of two planets distant 120 degrees from each other.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astrology; human-sciences; mysticism; philosophy; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An old ball game played by three people standing in a triangular formation. (See Wikipedia's entry for the game)

   **Usage:** historical; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The cusp (cutting region) of the crown of an upper molar, usually the anterior part.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences; zoology
