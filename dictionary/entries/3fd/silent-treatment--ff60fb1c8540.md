### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A form of communication refusal where one person deliberately ignores or withholds interaction from another, often in order to express anger, disapproval, or to exert control.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** to give someone the silent treatment

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; psychology; sciences; social-science; sociology
