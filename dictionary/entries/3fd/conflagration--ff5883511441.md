### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A large fire extending to many objects, or over a large space; a general burning.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** It took sixty firefighters to put out the conflagration.

   **Source synonyms:** firestorm; inferno

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable
