### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any magic trick performed with a hat, especially one involving pulling an object (traditionally a rabbit) out of an apparently empty hat.

   **Usage:** literal

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An achievement of a bowler taking three wickets in three consecutive balls.
   1. Three achievements in a single game, competition, season, etc., such as three consecutive wins.

      **Usage:** broadly

      **Example:** Ever heard of a Gordie Howe hat trick? It comprises a goal, an assist, and a fighting major penalty.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; cricket; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports
