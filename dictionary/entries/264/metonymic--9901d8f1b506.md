### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of, or relating to, a word or phrase that names an object from a single characteristic of it or of a closely related object.

   **Example:** The British government is often referred to by the metonymic expression "Downing Street".

   **Source synonyms:** metonymical; metonymous
