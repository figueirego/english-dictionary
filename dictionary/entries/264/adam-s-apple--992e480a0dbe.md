### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The lump in the throat, usually more noticeable in men than in women; the laryngeal prominence.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; medicine; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** Adam's morsel; apple; apple of Adam; laryngeal prominence; goozle

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any member of the species Tabernaemontana divaricata of fragrant houseplants, native to southeast Asia.

   **Source synonyms:** crape jasmine; pinwheel flower; Nero's crown; butterfly gardenia; carnation of India; East Indian rosebay; Tabernaemontana coronaria; coffee rose; crepe gardenia

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

3. Any of hybrid species Musa × paradisiaca, of cultivated bananas.
