## Phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used either to end a discussion, or to imply that further discussion is not needed.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** “Your new computer is super expensive!” “Yeah, but it rocks! ’Nuff said!”
