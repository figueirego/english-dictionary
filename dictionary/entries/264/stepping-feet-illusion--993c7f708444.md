### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An optical illusion of motion perception involving two "buses" (rectangles), one blue and one yellow, moving horizontally across a "street" of black and white stripes. Although both buses move at a constant speed, their perceived speed varies dramatically, because of differing contrast levels.
