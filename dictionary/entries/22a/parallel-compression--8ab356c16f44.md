### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A dynamic range compression technique used in sound recording and mixing, achieved by mixing an unprocessed "dry" or lightly compressed signal with a heavily compressed version of the same signal.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** New York compression
