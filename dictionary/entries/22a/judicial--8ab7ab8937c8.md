## Adjective · Record 1

### Pronunciation

### Forms

- most judicial — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of or relating to the administration of justice.

   **Source synonyms:** justiciary

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of or relating to the court system or the judicial branch of government.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. specified by a civil bill court under the terms of the Land Law (Ireland) Act, 1881

   **Usage:** Ireland; historical

   **Example:** judicial rent, judicial lease

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Of or relating to judgeship or the judiciary, the collective body of judges.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Of or relating to sound judgment; judicious (but see Usage notes).
