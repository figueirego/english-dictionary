**Source sense R1.S1**

1. (differential geometry) Describing a geometry in which a smoothly varying inner product is defined on a bracket-generating horizontal subbundle of the tangent bundle.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

   **Extractor diagnostics (not usage labels):** empty-gloss; no-gloss

**Supplemental definition (evidence reviewed; source gap preserved):** The original quotation concerns geometric structures on nilpotent groups. The article explicitly defines subriemannian manifolds through a horizontal distribution and smoothly varying inner products in that setting.
