### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To renovate or to convert to a different use, particularly houses, offices, or rooms within them.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** We're going to make over the garage into a guest suite.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. (of a person, particularly a woman) To give a new physical look to, especially with a new hairstyle, cosmetics, or clothes.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** Cindy is going to make over Karen tomorrow.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. (of a career, public image, etc.) To improve upon or take in a new direction.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** The senator needs to make over his image.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To transfer ownership (or care) of, especially by means of a legal document.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To praise (someone) excessively.

   **Usage:** dated; slang; African-American Vernacular
