### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One of several kinds of bird in the family Corvidae, especially Pica pica.

   **Source synonyms:** haggister (dialectal); maggie; Eurasian magpie; European magpie; common magpie; pica (obsolete); pie (regional); piet

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A superficially similar Australian bird, Gymnorhina tibicen, in the family Artamidae.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Someone who displays a magpie-like quality such as hoarding or stealing objects.

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A fan or member of Newcastle United F.C.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Usage:** UK

   **Usage:** UK; obsolete; slang
