### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One person's working time for a day, or the equivalent, used as a measure of how much work or labor is required or consumed to perform some task.

   **Example:** They estimated that it would take at least four people working a total of at least 80 person-days to build the foundation.

   **Source synonyms:** man-day
