### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A small cavern or hollow place in the side of a hill, or among rocks; especially, a cave used by a wild animal for shelter or concealment.

   **Example:** a den of robbers

   **Example:** Daniel was put into the lions’ den.

   **Source synonyms:** lair; luster; Wiktionary appendix of animal terms, including their homes

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A squalid or wretched place; a haunt.

   **Example:** a den of vice

   **Example:** an opium den; a gambling den

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A comfortable room not used for formal entertaining.

   **Source synonyms:** family room; sunroom; Florida room; Arizona room; rec room; playroom

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Synonym of fort (“structure improvised from furniture, etc. for playing games.”).

   **Example:** Our little girls love using bedsheets and other stuff around the house to make dens in the living room and pretending they're on adventures.

   **Source synonyms:** fort (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A narrow glen; a ravine; a dell.

   **Usage:** Scotland; UK; obsolete

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A group of Cub Scouts of the same age who work on projects together.
