## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

- devil's advocates — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who debates from a viewpoint which one may not actually hold, usually to determine its validity or simply for the sake of argument.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I don't really believe all that – I was just playing devil's advocate.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A canon lawyer appointed by the Church to argue against the canonization of the proposed candidate.

   **Usage:** historical

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Catholicism; Christianity; Roman-Catholicism
