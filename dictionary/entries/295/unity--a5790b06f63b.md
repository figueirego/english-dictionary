### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Oneness&#58; the state or fact of being one undivided entity.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** union; unitedness; onehead (obsolete); oneness; oneship; singleness; unicity; unitude (archaic); unity; unification; unionization; integration

   **Source antonyms:** disunity; multiplicity; plurality; manyness; manifoldness; manyhood (rare); manyness; morefold (nonstandard; rare); multeity (rare); multipleness; multiplicity; nonunity; numerousness

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Agreement; harmony.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A single undivided thing, seen as complete in itself.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any of the three classical rules of drama&#58; unity of action (nothing should be admitted not directly relevant to the development of the plot), unity of place (the scenes should be set in the same place), and unity of time (all the events should be such as might happen within a single day).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** broadcasting; drama; dramaturgy; entertainment; film; lifestyle; media; television; theater

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The number 1 or any element of a set or field that behaves under a given operation as the number 1 behaves under multiplication.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The peculiar characteristics of an estate held by several in joint tenancy.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The form of consensus in a Quaker meeting for business which signals that a decision has been reached. In order to achieve unity, everyone who does not agree with the decision must explicitly stand aside, possibly being recorded in the minutes as doing so.

   **Usage:** Quakerism; countable; uncountable
