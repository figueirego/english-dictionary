### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Upon or above the notochord; applied especially to a vertebral column which develops upon the dorsal side of the notochord, as distinguished from a perichordal column, which develops around it.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; medicine; sciences
