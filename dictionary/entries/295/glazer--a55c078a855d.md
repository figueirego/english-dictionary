### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who applies glaze, as in pottery, etc.; one who gives a glasslike or glossy surface to anything; a calenderer or smoother of cloth, paper, etc.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person who is prone to endless monologuing; derived from the practice of speaking until the listener's eyes "glaze over".

   **Usage:** slang

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Alternative form of glazier&#58; one who fits glass.

   **Usage:** alt-of; alternative

   **Alternative form of:** [glazier](../1a6/glazier--69b916d42ece.md)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Someone who glazes (compliments or praises someone excessively in a cringeworthy way); an ass-kisser or sycophant.
