## Verb · Record 1

### Pronunciation

### Forms

- parched — participle; past

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To burn the surface of, to scorch.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The sun today could parch cement.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To roast, as dry grain.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To dry to extremity; to shrivel with heat.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The patient’s mouth is parched from fever.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To be very thirsty.

   **Usage:** colloquial; intransitive

   **Example:** We're parched, hon. Could you send up an ale from the cooler?

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To boil something slowly (Still used in Lancashire in parched peas, a type of mushy peas).

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To become superficially burnt; to become sunburned.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The locals watched, amused, as the tourists parched in the sun, having neglected to apply sunscreen or bring water.
