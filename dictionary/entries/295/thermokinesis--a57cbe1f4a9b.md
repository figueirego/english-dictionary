### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The ability to manipulate the motion of molecules, slowing them down or speeding them up, thus changing temperature in the immediate vicinity with the power of one's mind. This is a wide spectrum ability than simply pyrokinesis or cryokinesis.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** fiction; literature; media; publishing
