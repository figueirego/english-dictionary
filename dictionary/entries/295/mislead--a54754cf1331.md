### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To lead astray, in a false direction.

   **Usage:** literally; transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To deceive by telling lies or otherwise giving a false impression.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To deceptively trick into something wrong.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The preacher elaborated Satan's ways to mislead us into sin

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To accidentally or intentionally confuse.

   **Usage:** broadly; transitive
