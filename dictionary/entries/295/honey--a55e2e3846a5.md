### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A sweet, viscous, gold-colored fluid produced from plant nectar by bees, and often consumed by humans.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** The honey in the pot should last for years.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A variety of this substance.

   **Usage:** countable; usually

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Nectar.

   **Usage:** rare; uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Something sweet or desirable.

   **Usage:** figuratively; uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A term of affection.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** Honey, would you take out the trash?

   **Example:** Honey, I'm home.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A woman, especially an attractive one.

   **Usage:** countable; informal; usually

   **Example:** Man, there are some fine honeys here tonight!

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A spectrum of pale yellow to brownish-yellow color, like that of most types of (the sweet substance) honey.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Precum; pre-ejaculate.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually; vulgar; African-American Vernacular
