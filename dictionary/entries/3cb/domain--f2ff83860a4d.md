### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A geographic area owned or controlled by a single person or organization.

   **Example:** The king ruled his domain harshly.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A field or sphere of activity, influence or expertise.

   **Example:** Dealing with complaints isn't really my domain&#58; get in touch with customer services.

   **Example:** His domain is English history.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A group of related items, topics, or subjects.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**
