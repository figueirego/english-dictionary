## Proper noun · Record 2

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A saint born in Cyrus who became an anchorite in Chalcis and died in 388.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. A minor fourth century Greek geographer from Heraclea (an ancient city on the coast of Bithynia).

   **Usage:** Ancient Greece

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. Roman emperor of the East from 450 to 457, born 392.

   **Usage:** Ancient-Rome

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. Son of Anthemius, grandson of the Emperor Marcian, who led an unsuccessful rebellion in 479.

   **Usage:** Ancient-Rome
