### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An early style of telephone with a mouthpiece (transmitter) mounted at the top of its stand, and a receiver that was held by the user to the ear during a call. When not in use, the receiver rested in the fork of the switch hook protruding to the side of the stand, thereby disconnecting the audio circuit from the network.

   **Usage:** historical
