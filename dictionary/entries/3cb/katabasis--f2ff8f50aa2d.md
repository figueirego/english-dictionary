### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A mytheme or trope in which the hero embarks on a journey to the underworld.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; literature; media; mysticism; mythology; philosophy; publishing; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any journey downwards or fall.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; humorous; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A retreat, especially a military one.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source antonyms:** anabasis

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A journey from the interior of a country to the coast.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The presence of downward (drainage or katabatic) winds.

   **Usage:** countable; rare; uncountable
