### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To clarify something by giving, or serving as, an example or a comparison.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** We illustrate our definitions by including quotations or simple examples.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To provide a book or other publication with pictures, diagrams or other explanatory or decorative features.

   **Example:** The economics textbook was illustrated with many graphs.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To shed light upon.

   **Usage:** obsolete

   **Source synonyms:** illuminate; alight (archaic); belight; beshine (dialectal); bright; enlighten; fire; illume (archaic); illuminate; illumine; illustrate (obsolete); irradiate; light; lighten; light up; luster (obsolete); lustrate (obsolete); shed light on

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To give renown or honour to; to make illustrious.

   **Usage:** obsolete

   **Source synonyms:** glorify
