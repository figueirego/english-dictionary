## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of a compound, containing chlorine with a lower oxidation number than equivalent chloric compounds; especially having an oxidation number of 3.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Relating to, or resembling, the electronegative character of chlorine; hence, electronegative; opposed to basylous or zincous.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; obsolete
