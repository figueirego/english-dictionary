### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A grappling hold around a person's neck, especially one in which the neck is grasped tightly from behind with an arm, cutting off the flow of blood to the brain and restricting breathing.

   **Example:** He put his opponent in a chokehold.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; hobbies; lifestyle; martial-arts; military; politics; sports; war; wrestling

   **Source synonyms:** neckhold

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A powerful and restrictive control or influence over something.

   **Usage:** broadly; figuratively

   **Example:** One country has a chokehold on another country's economy.

   **Source synonyms:** stranglehold; throttlehold
