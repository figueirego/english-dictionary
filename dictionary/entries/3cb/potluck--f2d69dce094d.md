### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A meal, especially one offered to a guest, consisting of whatever food is available.

   **Usage:** also; attributive; countable; dated; uncountable

   **Example:** Here are some leftover beans and meat; we can make a good potluck stew from them.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Whatever is available in a particular situation.

   **Usage:** also; attributive; broadly; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A shared meal consisting of whatever guests have brought (sometimes without prior arrangement); a potlatch; also, a dish of food brought to such a meal.

   **Usage:** US; also; attributive; countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** fuddle (Britain; dialectal)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The last draft or portion of an alcoholic beverage in a pot or other drinking vessel.

   **Usage:** also; attributive; countable; obsolete; uncountable
