**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A round lump made up of or coated with slime or a slime-like substance such as mucus.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A round lump made up of or coated with slime or a slime-like substance such as mucus.
   1. A mucus-coated lump containing the cercariae (“parasitic larvae”) of a liver fluke (of the phylum Platyhelminthes).

      **Usage:** specifically

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences

**Source sense R1.S3**

   **Usage:** colloquial; derogatory; figuratively
