## Noun

### Pronunciation

### Forms

- black-and-tan — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of various breeds of dog having black and tan colouring.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A bar, nightclub etc. which had both black and white customers, especially one that featured black entertainers.

   **Usage:** US; countable; historical; slang

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An alcoholic drink made from a dark and a light malt, typically stout (black) and ale (tan).

   **Usage:** colloquial; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Not only did the Dublin barkeeps not make a shamrock in the foam, they claimed to not even know what a black and tan was.
