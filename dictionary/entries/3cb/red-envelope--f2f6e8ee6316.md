### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In Chinese communities, a decorated paper envelope, traditionally red, containing a gift of money (lucky money) presented during special occasions such as birthdays, weddings, and the Chinese New Year; also, the gift of money presented in such an envelope.

   **Source synonyms:** red bag; red packet; angpao; ang pow; hongbao; lai see
