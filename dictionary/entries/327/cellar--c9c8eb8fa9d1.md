## Noun · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈsɛlə/ — source labels: Received-Pronunciation
- IPA: /ˈsɛləɹ/ — source labels: General-American

### Forms

- cellars — plural
- seller — alternative; obsolete

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An enclosed underground space, often under a building, used for storage or shelter.

   **Example:** The farmhouse has several additions, added over many decades; it has three cellars, and one of them is older than the other two.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A wine collection, especially when stored in a cellar.

   **Example:** The insurance company valued his cellar at $27,000, largely on the strength of his bottles of 1972 Château Hypothetica.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Last place in a league or competition; some rank near last place.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Example:** The Tigers have been in the cellar all year long, and I'm tired of it.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A basement.

   **Usage:** Northeastern; UK; US

   **Example:** Most of my tools and hardware are in the garage, but I keep some tools in the cellar, too, mainly for convenience.
