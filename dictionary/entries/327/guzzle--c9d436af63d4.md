### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To drink or eat quickly, voraciously, or to excess; to gulp down; to swallow greedily, continually, or with gusto.

   **Usage:** informal; transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To consume alcoholic beverages, especially frequently or habitually.

   **Usage:** dated; informal; intransitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To consume anything quickly, greedily, or to excess, as if with insatiable thirst; often said of gas-powered vehicles.

   **Usage:** broadly; informal; transitive

   **Example:** This car just guzzles petrol.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To flow copiously; to spray out.

   **Usage:** informal; intransitive; rare

   **Example:** Blood guzzled from the wound.

   **Example:** The barrel guzzled out beer from the hole.

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** swig — to drink quickly, voraciously; swill — to drink quickly, voraciously
