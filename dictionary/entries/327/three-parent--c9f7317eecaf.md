### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having three genetic parents; that is, having been produced by a process in which the nucleus of one egg is inserted into the cytoplasm of another egg, which contains mitochondrial DNA, and then the hybrid egg is fertilized by a sperm.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a person
