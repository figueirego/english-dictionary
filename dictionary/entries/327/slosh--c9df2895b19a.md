### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To shift chaotically; to splash noisily.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The water in his bottle sloshed back and forth as he ran.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a liquid

   **Source synonyms:** slush

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To cause to slosh.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The boy sloshed water over the edge of the bath.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a liquid

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To make a sloshing sound.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** His boots were so completely soaked that they sloshed when he walked.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To pour noisily, sloppily or in large amounts.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The coffee was nice and hot, so she sloshed some into a cup and went back to her desk.

   **Example:** He really sloshed on the sauce- they were a bit strong for my taste.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a liquid

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. to move noisily through water or other liquid.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The streets were flooded, but they still managed to slosh their way to school.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To punch (someone).

   **Usage:** UK; colloquial; transitive
