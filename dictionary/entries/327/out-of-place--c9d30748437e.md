### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Not in the proper arrangement or situation.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** No wonder I couldn’t find it—it was out of place.

   **Example:** She came in out of the storm with not a hair out of place.

   **Example:** Amongst all those horsey people I felt quite out of place.

   **Source synonyms:** dislocated; dystopic; ectopic; heterotopic; malpositioned; misplaced; out of keeping with

   **Source antonyms:** in keeping with; in place

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Inappropriate for the circumstances.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** That remark was out of place.

   **Source synonyms:** unseemly; atopic

   **Source antonyms:** appropriate; seemly
