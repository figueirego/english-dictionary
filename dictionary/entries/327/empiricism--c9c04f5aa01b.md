## Noun

### Forms

- empiricisms — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A doctrine which holds that the only or, at least, the most reliable source of human knowledge is experience, especially perception by means of the physical senses. (Often contrasted with rationalism.)

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; philosophy; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A pursuit of knowledge purely through experience, especially by means of observation and sometimes by experimentation.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Research methodology shaped from empirical philosophy (see above), e.g. surveys, statistics, etc.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; political-science; sciences; social-science; social-sciences; sociology

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Medicine as practised by an empiric, founded on mere (personal or anecdotal) experience, without the aid of science or a knowledge of principles.

   **Usage:** countable; historical; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** folk medicine; quackery; charlatanry
