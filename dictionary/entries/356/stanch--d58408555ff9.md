### Senses

<a id="r3-s1"></a>

**Source sense R3.S1**

1. To stop the flow of (water or some other liquid).

   **Usage:** US; especially; poetic; transitive; otherwise archaic

   **Source synonyms:** estop; stopple

<a id="r3-s2"></a>

**Source sense R3.S2**

2. To stop the flow of (water or some other liquid).
   1. To stop the flow of (blood); also, to stop (a wound) from bleeding.

      **Usage:** US; especially; poetic; transitive; otherwise archaic

      **Example:** A small amount of cotton can be stuffed into the nose to stanch the flow of blood if necessary.

      **Source synonyms:** estop; stopple

<a id="r3-s3"></a>

**Source sense R3.S3**

3. To stop the flow of (water or some other liquid).
   1. To make (a building or other structure) watertight or weatherproof.

      **Source synonyms:** estop; stopple

<a id="r3-s4"></a>

**Source sense R3.S4**

4. To check or stop, or deter (an action).

   **Usage:** US; especially; transitive

<a id="r3-s5"></a>

**Source sense R3.S5**

5. To check or stop, or deter (an action).
   1. To stop the progression of (an illness); also, to alleviate (pain); often followed by of&#58; to relieve (someone's) pain.

      **Usage:** US; especially; transitive

<a id="r3-s6"></a>

**Source sense R3.S6**

   **Usage:** US; especially; obsolete; transitive

   **Usage:** US; also; especially; intransitive; reflexive
