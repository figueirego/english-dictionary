### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A tune that keeps replaying in one's head or that one keeps thinking about, especially if unwanted.

   **Source synonyms:** brainworm

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Ellipsis of corn earworm (“larva of the moths Helicoverpa zea (syn. Heliothis zea) and Helicoverpa armigera, which are agricultural pests”).

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; ellipsis

   **Alternative form of:** [corn earworm](../0c7/corn-earworm--31dd43626ed6.md)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An earwig; an insect of the order Dermaptera.

   **Usage:** archaic
