### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To store, or place in reserve; to save (money, etc.) for later use.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Our family has been putting food by for generations.

   **Example:** I have put by a few hundred pounds for a rainy day.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To remove (something) from the present context and into its proper place; (figurative) to reject or disregard.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** When the lesson was finished, she put by her books and papers.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To perform an action without attracting the attention of.

   **Usage:** ditransitive

   **Example:** I managed to put that transaction by accounts payable.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Of a ship&#58; to be run aground intentionally to avoid a collision

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The Bow Spring put by to avoid colliding with the Manzanillo II.
