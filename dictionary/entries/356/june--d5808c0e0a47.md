### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The sixth month of the Gregorian calendar, following May and preceding July, containing the northern solstice.

   **Example:** this glad June day

   **Source synonyms:** Sixth Month

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A female given name transferred from the month name \[in turn from English\], often for a girl born in June, used since the end of the 19th century.
