### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To form (something) or for (something) to be formed into cakes (a block of any of various dense materials).

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** He caked up the dirt.

   **Example:** The concrete then cakes up through natural processes.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To cake entirely (of blood or other liquid, to dry out and become hard).

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** All the blood had caked up.

   **Example:** An hour of drying out caked up the blood puddle.
