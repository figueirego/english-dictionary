### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Remaining in its original, pristine state, undamaged; not altered.

   **Example:** There are practically no untouched forests left in Europe.

   **Source synonyms:** unaltered; unscathed; unspoiled; unsullied; virgin; maiden

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Not eaten.

   **Example:** He left his vegetables untouched.

   **Source synonyms:** unconsumed; uneaten; undevoured; unscarfed; unscoffed

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Not influenced, affected or swayed.

   **Example:** She was untouched by his tale of woes.

   **Source synonyms:** indifferent; unmoved; adiaphroistic; apathetic; apathistical; blasé; blithe; deaf; cool; shiftless; dispassionate; dull; enervated; gallionic; halfhearted; heartless (rare); impassive; ineffervescible; insouciant; indifferent; insensible; jaded; languid; languorous; Laodicean (archaic); lethargic; listless; lackadaisical; lotophagous; lukewarm; meh; mopey; nonchalant; nonplussed (US); otiose; phlegmatic; pococurante; spiritless; supine; tepid; torpid; torpedinous (archaic); unaroused; uncaring; unconcerned; unenthusiastic; unimpressible; uninterested; unlively; unmoved; untouched; unzealous; world-weary

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Not having come in contact.

   **Example:** Our chocolates are untouched by human hands.

   **Source synonyms:** unhandled

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Not read or examined.

   **Source synonyms:** unexamined; unread; unscanned

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Not discussed or talked about.

   **Example:** Politics was an untouched topic in our family.

   **Source synonyms:** indiscussed; unaddressed; undiscoursed; undiscussed
