### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A deliberately untrue statement which is intended to produce a favorable result; especially, one which is judged to be better than an overhonest alternative, as for example to remain polite in a superficial social exchange, or to pick one's battles.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Source synonyms:** fib
