### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A candidate unified theory of all physical forces and particles; a theory which suggests that subatomic particles are one-dimensional strings rather than zero-dimensional points. It also suggests that space-time can have up to nine dimensions, plus the dimension of time.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** String theory denies the existence of a hypothetical eleventh dimension.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics
