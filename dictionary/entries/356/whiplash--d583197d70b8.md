**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An injury to the upper spine connected to a violent jerk of the head in either a backward or forward or side to side direction, resembling the motion of a whip.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** whiplash injury; whiplash-associated disorder

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An abrupt and unexpected change, or the resulting feeling of shock.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

   **Example:** tonal whiplash
