### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The aspects of one's life that are personal, such as family, friends and other personal relationships, as opposed to the elements that make up one's public or professional life.

   **Example:** The Russians have at least three faces&#58; one for the political regime, one for business dealings with anybody, and the real face, which is for one's private life and family reserved. Put another way, how to seem something different to different kinds of people in the public life.

   **Source synonyms:** personal life

   **Source antonyms:** public life; work life
