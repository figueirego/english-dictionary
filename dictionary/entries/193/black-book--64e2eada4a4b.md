## Proper noun · Record 1

### Forms

- the Black Book — canonical
- black book — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of several books or documents of a political character, published at different times of history and for different purposes.
   1. The Black Book of the Exchequer, a book compiled in the 12th century, containing a description of the court of exchequer of England, an official statement of the revenues of the crown, etc.

      **Usage:** historical or current

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of several books or documents of a political character, published at different times of history and for different purposes.
   1. The Compendium Compertorum, a document containing details of the abuses practiced in the English monasteries and religious houses, compiled by order of their visitors under Henry VIII, to hasten their dissolution.

      **Usage:** historical or current

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of several books or documents of a political character, published at different times of history and for different purposes.
   1. The Black Book of the Admiralty, a book on admiralty law written in Old French, containing important decisions of the High Court of Admiralty, compiled in the reign of Edward III.

      **Usage:** historical or current

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any of several books or documents of a political character, published at different times of history and for different purposes.
   1. A 19th century book detailing the corruption and financial abuses of the British government.

      **Usage:** historical or current

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Any of several books or documents of a political character, published at different times of history and for different purposes.
   1. The Sonderfahndungsliste G.B., a list compiled by the Schutzstaffel of prominent British citizens and residents to be arrested in the event of a Nazi invasion of Britain.

      **Usage:** historical or current

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Any of several books or documents of a political character, published at different times of history and for different purposes.
   1. A book or document kept in the football briefcase of the United States President, describing nuclear retaliation options.

      **Usage:** historical or current
