### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. From side to side, often in an oblique manner; across or over.

   **Example:** Above, the stars appeared to move slowly athwart.

   **Example:** We placed one log on the ground, and another athwart, forming a crude cross.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

   **Source synonyms:** overthwart (obsolete); transversely

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Across the path of something, so as to impede progress.

   **Example:** a fleet standing athwart our course

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Against the anticipated or appropriate course of something; improperly, perversely, wrongly.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

   **Source synonyms:** awry; crosswise
