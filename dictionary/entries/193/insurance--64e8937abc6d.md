### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A means of indemnity against a future occurrence of an uncertain event.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** The car was totalled, but fortunately I had insurance.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The business of providing insurance.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** After five years in banking, I switched to insurance.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any attempt to forestall an unfavorable event.

   **Usage:** figuratively; uncountable; usually

   **Example:** The sky was clear, but I took my umbrella for insurance.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A bet made after the deal, which pays off if the dealer has blackjack.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** I only take insurance if the count is right.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** blackjack; games

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An insurance policy.

   **Usage:** countable; usually

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A company providing insurance policies; an insurer.

   **Usage:** countable; usually
