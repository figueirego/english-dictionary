## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A marriage motivated by some reason other than love; for example, one conducted to obtain residence rights in a country, for financial gain, or for political purposes.

   **Source synonyms:** mariage de convenance

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A big tent alliance of disparate factions (such as political factions).

   **Usage:** figuratively
