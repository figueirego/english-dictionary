### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make a lot of percussive noise while moving around.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** She banged around with those tools all night.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To spend time in a place; to linger.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** After meeting up with Simon, the two of us banged around the neighborhood.

   **Example:** This issue has been banging around in the popular imagination for a while.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To beat up, often over time or repeatedly.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He's been banging her around since they got married.
