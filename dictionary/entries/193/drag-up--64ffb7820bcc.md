### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To remind people of (something, usually unpleasant, from the past).

   **Example:** I don't know why John had to drag up the incident of the car accident. It was really embarrassing.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To educate reluctant pupils.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To raise a child with insufficient discipline or instillment of social etiquette.

   **Usage:** UK; figuratively; transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Of a man&#58; to dress in women's clothing for entertainment.
