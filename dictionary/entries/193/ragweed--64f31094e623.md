### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A plant of the genus Ambrosia, native to North America and a common invasive species elsewhere, particularly noted for producing pollen that triggers hay fever.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** ambrosia

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Synonym of ragwort.

   **Usage:** Ireland; Scotland; countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** ragwort (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
