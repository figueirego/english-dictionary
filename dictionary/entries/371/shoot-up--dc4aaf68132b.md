### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To grow taller or larger rapidly.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive; sometimes

   **Example:** Our operating costs have shot up due to the fuel shortage.

   **Example:** He was a small child, but shot up when he reached his teenage years.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To fire many bullets or shells at.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The terrorists decided to shoot up a convenience store.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To use up (ammunition) by shooting.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Most of our ammunition has now been shot up.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To inject (a drug) intravenously.

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** This place is always full of smackheads shooting up.

   **Source synonyms:** jack up
