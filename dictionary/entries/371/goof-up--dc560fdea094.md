### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make a mistake.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Example:** If I go near a skateboard, I'm sure I'm just going to goof up and fall off.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To introduce problems to or damage through incompetence.

   **Usage:** slang; transitive

   **Example:** I tried to fix it, but I think I just goofed it up some more.
