### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To lie in a position directly beneath something.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To lie under or beneath.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** A stratum of clay underlies the surface gravel.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To serve as a basis of; form the foundation of.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** underpin

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

   **Usage:** transitive
