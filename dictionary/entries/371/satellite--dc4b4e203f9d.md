**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An attendant on an important person; a member of someone's retinue, often in a somewhat derogatory sense; a henchman.

   **Usage:** archaic

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A moon or other smaller body orbiting a larger one.

   **Example:** The Moon is a natural satellite of the Earth.

   **Example:** A spent upper stage is a derelict satellite.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A man-made apparatus designed to be placed in orbit around a celestial body, generally to relay information, data, etc. to Earth.

   **Example:** Many telecommunication satellites orbit at 36000km above the equator.

   **Source synonyms:** artificial satellite

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Satellite TV; reception of television broadcasts via services that use man-made satellite technology.

   **Usage:** colloquial; uncountable

   **Example:** Do you have satellite at your house?

5. A country, state, office, building etc. which is under the jurisdiction, influence, or domination of another body.
