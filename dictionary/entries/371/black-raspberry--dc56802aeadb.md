### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Either of two particular species of raspberry that are native to North America; a plant or berry of the species.
   1. Rubus leucodermis

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source synonyms:** blackcap raspberry; blue raspberry; whitebark raspberry; blackcap; black cap; black cap raspberry

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Either of two particular species of raspberry that are native to North America; a plant or berry of the species.
   1. Rubus occidentalis

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source synonyms:** wild black raspberry; thimbleberry; scotch cap

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Rubus coreanus, native to Korea, Japan, and China; a plant or berry of the species.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A flavor (of candy, beverage, chewing gum, or dessert) that is inspired by and named for this fruit and is conventionally dyed black or nearly black.

   **Usage:** uncountable
