### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In a poor manner or condition; without plenty, or sufficiency, or suitable provision for comfort.

   **Example:** to live poorly

   **Example:** He uses his employees poorly.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. With little or no success; indifferently; with little profit or advantage.

   **Example:** to do poorly in business

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Meanly; without spirit.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Without skill or merit.

   **Example:** He plays tennis poorly.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. In a negative manner; with disapproval; unfavorably.

   **Example:** I think poorly of them.

   **Example:** She looked poorly on that suggestion.
