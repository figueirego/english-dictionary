### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A building or institution where money (originally, only coins) is produced under government licence.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A vast sum of money; (by extension) a large amount of something.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** That house is worth a mint.

   **Example:** It must have cost a mint to produce!

   **Example:** to make a mint

   **Source synonyms:** bundle (informal); pile (slang); small fortune (colloquial)

**Source sense R1.S3**
