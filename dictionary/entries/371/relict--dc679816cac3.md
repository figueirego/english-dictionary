### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Something that, or someone who, survives or remains or is left over after the loss of others; a relic.
   1. The surviving member of a married couple after one or the other has died; a widow or widower.

      **Usage:** archaic; formal

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Something that, or someone who, survives or remains or is left over after the loss of others; a relic.
   1. A species, organism, or ecosystem that has survived from a previous age&#58; one that was once widespread but is now found only in a few areas.

      **Usage:** formal

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; ecology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Something that, or someone who, survives or remains or is left over after the loss of others; a relic.
   1. A structure or other feature that has survived from a previous age.

      **Usage:** formal

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; geology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Something that, or someone who, survives or remains or is left over after the loss of others; a relic.
   1. A survival of an archaic word, language or other form.

      **Usage:** formal

      **Example:** A small number of linguists believe that Cimbrian is not an Austro-Bavarian dialect but a relict of Lombardic.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences
