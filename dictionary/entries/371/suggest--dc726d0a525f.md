### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To imply but stop short of explicitly stating (something).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Are you suggesting that I killed my wife?

   **Source synonyms:** point

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To cause one to suppose (something); to bring to one's mind the idea (of something).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The name "hamburger" suggests that hamburgers originated from Hamburg.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To explicitly mention (something) as a possibility for consideration, often to recommend it.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** This meeting venue was suggested to us by our boss.

   **Example:** He suggests that we celebrate with dinner at Bellissimo. He suggests our celebrating with dinner at Bellissimo.

   **Example:** The guidebook suggested that we visit the local cathedral, so that's what we did.

   **Source synonyms:** venture; hazard

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To seduce; to prompt to evil; to tempt.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive
