### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A tree of the genus Carpinus, having smooth grey bark and a ridged trunk, the wood being white and very hard, found in temperate regions of the Northern Hemisphere.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A tree of the genus Carpinus, having smooth grey bark and a ridged trunk, the wood being white and very hard, found in temperate regions of the Northern Hemisphere.
   1. Especially, the American hornbeam (Carpinus caroliniana), which is common along the banks of streams in the United States.

      **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A tree of the genus Carpinus, having smooth grey bark and a ridged trunk, the wood being white and very hard, found in temperate regions of the Northern Hemisphere.
   1. Especially, the common hornbeam or European hornbeam (Carpinus betulus).

      **Usage:** countable

      **Source synonyms:** hardbeam

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The wood of these trees or one species of them; (countable) a sample of such wood.

   **Usage:** uncountable
