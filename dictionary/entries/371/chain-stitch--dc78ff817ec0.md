## Noun

### Forms

- chain stitches — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A stitch like the links of a chain, used in crocheting.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An ornamental stitch like the links of a chain, used in sewing and embroidery.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A stitch in which the looping of the thread or threads forms a chain on the underside of the work; the loop stitch, as distinguished from the lock stitch.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; machine sewing
