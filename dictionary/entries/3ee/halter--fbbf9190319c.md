### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A bitless headpiece of rope or straps, placed on the head of animals such as cattle or horses to lead or tie them.

   **Source synonyms:** headstall; headpiece; headcollar (British)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A rope with a noose, for hanging criminals; the gallows rope.

   **Source synonyms:** collar; Bridport dagger (colloquial; obsolete); collar (archaic); halter; hangman's knot; hangman's noose; hempen collar (archaic; euphemistic; poetic); hempen cravat (archaic; euphemistic)

**Source sense R1.S3**
