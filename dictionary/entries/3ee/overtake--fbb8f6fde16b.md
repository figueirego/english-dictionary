### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To pass a slower moving object or entity (on the side closest to oncoming traffic).

   **Example:** The racehorse overtook the lead pack on the last turn.

   **Example:** The car was so slow we were overtaken by a bus.

   **Source synonyms:** forge

   **Source antonyms:** undertake\[curbside\]\]&gt;

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To become greater than something else in quantity, worth, etc.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Grocery sales in the north have overtaken those in the south.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To take by surprise; surprise and overcome; carry away.

   **Example:** Our plans were overtaken by events.
