### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An apparatus for gymnastic exercises with a cylindrical body covered with leather and two upright pommels, as hand grips, near the centre; held upright with adjustable legs.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>
