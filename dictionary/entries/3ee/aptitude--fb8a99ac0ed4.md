### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A natural ability to acquire knowledge or skill.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** She showed an early aptitude for mathematics.

   **Example:** The test measures a student’s aptitude for language learning.

   **Source synonyms:** talent; knack; ability; technique; aptitude; capacity; craft; capability; competency; competence; cunning; dexterity; ease; facility; hand; knack; list; prowess; moxie; potential; proficiency; skill; talent; tekkers (Multicultural-London-English); touch

   **Source antonyms:** ineptitude

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The condition of being suitable.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** appropriateness; fitness; suitability

   **Source antonyms:** ineptitude
