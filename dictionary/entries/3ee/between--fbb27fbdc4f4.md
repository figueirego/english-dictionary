### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In the position or interval that separates (two things), or intermediate in quantity or degree. (See Usage notes below.)

   **Example:** John stood between Amy and Mary. Let’s meet between two and three.

   **Example:** I want to buy one that costs somewhere between forty and fifty dollars.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Done together or reciprocally.

   **Example:** conversation between friends

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Shared in confidence.

   **Example:** Between you and me, I think the boss is crazy. Let's keep this between ourselves.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. In transit from (one to the other, or connecting places).

   **Example:** He's between jobs right now. The shuttle runs between the town and the airport.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Combined (by effort or ownership).

   **Example:** Between us all, we shall succeed. We've only got £5 between us.

   **Example:** Between the leaky taps and the peeling wallpaper, there isn't much about this house to appeal to a buyer.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. One of (representing a choice).

   **Example:** You must choose between him and me.

   **Example:** Some colour-blind people can't distinguish between red and green.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Taking together the combined effect of.

   **Example:** Between the food and the card games, this proved to be the best birthday party I have ever had.
