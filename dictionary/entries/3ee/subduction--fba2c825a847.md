### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The action of being pushed or drawn beneath another object.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The process of one tectonic plate moving beneath another and sinking into the mantle at a convergent plate boundary.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; geology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>
