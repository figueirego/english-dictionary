### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Disposition to do good.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** gesture of benevolence

   **Example:** spirit of benevolence

   **Example:** She smiled with benevolence at the children.

2. Charitable kindness.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Usage:** countable
