### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Tenacious, energetic, spunky.

   **Example:** spirited and feisty

   **Example:** She gave a feisty response during the debate.

   **Example:** The feisty puppy barked at everyone.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Belligerent; prepared to stand and fight, especially in spite of relatively small stature or some other disadvantage.

   **Example:** small but feisty

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Easily offended and ready to bicker.

   **Example:** a feisty attitude

   **Source synonyms:** testy
