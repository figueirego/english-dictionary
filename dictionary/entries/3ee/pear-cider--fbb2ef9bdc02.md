### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An alcoholic cider made from pears, especially the commercial product made from imported pear juice.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** perry; pear wine

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An apple-based alcoholic cider infused with pear or artificial pear flavour.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
