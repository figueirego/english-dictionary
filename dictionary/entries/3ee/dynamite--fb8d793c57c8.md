### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A class of explosives made from nitroglycerine in an absorbent medium such as kieselguhr, used in mining and blasting.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A stick of trinitrotoluene (TNT).

   **Usage:** informal; proscribed; uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Anything exceptionally dangerous, exciting or wonderful.

   **Usage:** figuratively; slang; uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A strong drug, in particular heroin, cocaine or potent marijuana.

   **Usage:** slang; uncountable; usually
