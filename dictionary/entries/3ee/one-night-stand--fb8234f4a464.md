## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An occasion when a performer or team of them (especially in vaudeville) expects to perform at a theater for a single evening.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A single sexual encounter between two individuals, where at least one of the partners has no immediate intention or expectation of establishing a longer-term sexual or romantic relationship.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Source synonyms:** hit and run; pump and dump; wham, bam, thank you ma'am

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Either of the two partners involved in such a single sexual encounter.

   **Usage:** idiomatic
