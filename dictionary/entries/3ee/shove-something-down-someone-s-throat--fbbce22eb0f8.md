**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To aggressively and persistently force an opinion, belief or idea upon a person (especially if they lack interest or have an opposing viewpoint).

   **Usage:** derogatory; idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** I disagreed with his opinion, but he continued to shove it down my throat.
