### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The action of the verb to wash.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Clothing, bedlinen or soft furnishings that have been, are currently being, or are to be washed; laundry.

   **Usage:** Australia; New-Zealand; UK; uncountable

   **Example:** My mother used to do the washing on a Monday.

   **Source synonyms:** laundry

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The residue after an ore, etc, has been washed.

   **Usage:** countable; in-plural; often

   **Example:** The washings have a higher concentration of metal.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The liquid used to wash an ore.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A place where a precious metal found in gravel is separated from lighter material by washing.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** the gold-washings

   **Example:** silver-washings

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A thin covering or coat.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** a washing of silver
