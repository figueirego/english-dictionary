<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A computerized interactive system for storing, processing and reproducing verbal messages left through a conventional telephone network.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** voice mailbox; voicemailbox
