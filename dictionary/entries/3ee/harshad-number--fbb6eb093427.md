### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A positive integer which is divisible by the sum of all of its digits.

   **Example:** The sum of digits of 24 is 2 + 4 = 6, and 24 is divisible by 6, so 24 is a Harshad number.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** Niven number
