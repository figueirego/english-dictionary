### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Causing harm in a stealthy, often gradual, manner.

   **Source synonyms:** subtle

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Intending to entrap; alluring but harmful.

   **Example:** Hansel and Gretel were lured by the witch’s insidious gingerbread house.
