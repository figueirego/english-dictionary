### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The maximum number of passengers that can physically fit within a transit vehicle without requiring severe contortions by said passengers.

2. The maximum number of passengers that can physically fit within a transit vehicle without requiring severe contortions by said passengers.
   1. A situation in which one or more transit vehicles are carrying the maximum number of passengers that they can physically accommodate without requiring severe contortion of passengers.

      **Example:** The frequent occurrence of crush loads is indicative of a severely overloaded system requiring immediate improvements to increase its passenger capacity.
