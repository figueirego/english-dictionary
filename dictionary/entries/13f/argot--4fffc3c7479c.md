### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A secret language or conventional slang peculiar to thieves, tramps and vagabonds.

   **Source synonyms:** cant; jargon; slang

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The specialized informal vocabulary and terminology used between people with special skill in a field, such as between doctors, mathematicians or hackers.

   **Example:** The conversation was in the argot of the trade, full of acronyms and abbreviations that made no sense to the uninitiate.

   **Source synonyms:** jargon

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A strongly marked style of speaking.
