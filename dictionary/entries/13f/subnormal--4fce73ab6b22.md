## Noun · Record 2

### Forms

- subnormals — plural

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A person whose abilities are less than normal.

   **Example:** the IQ scores of mental subnormals

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. That part of the axis of a curved line which is intercepted between the ordinate and the normal.
