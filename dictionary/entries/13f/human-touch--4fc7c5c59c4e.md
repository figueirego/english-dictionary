## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The ability of a person to deal with others in a personable and empathetic way.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; uncountable

   **Example:** Some politicians lack the human touch to connect with voters.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The positive qualities of human beings manifested in a place or object.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; uncountable

   **Example:** Before the renovation the hotel lacked the human touch to attract visitors.
