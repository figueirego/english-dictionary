### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A dimensionless number&#58; the ratio of heat transfer irreversibility to total irreversibility due to heat transfer and fluid friction&#58; Be=(̇S'\_(gen,,ΔT))/(̇S'\_(gen,,ΔT)+̇S'\_(gen,,Δp)), where ̇S'\_(gen,,ΔT) is the entropy generation contributed by heat transfer and ̇S'\_(gen,,Δp) is the entropy generation contributed by fluid friction.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics; thermodynamics

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A dimensionless number&#58; the pressure drop along the fluid path length in both external flows and internal flows&#58; Be\_L=(Δp,L²)/(μν), where μ is the dynamic viscosity, ν is the momentum diffusivity (or Kinematic viscosity) and L is fluid path length.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** fluid-dynamics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics
