## Noun

### Forms

- rubythroats — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of several species of birds, the males having a brilliant patch of metallic red on the throat.
   1. A ruby-throated hummingbird (Archilochus colubris).

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of several species of birds, the males having a brilliant patch of metallic red on the throat.
   1. A Siberian rubythroat (Calliope calliope) or white-tailed rubythroat (Calliope pectoralis),of genus Calliope (syn. Luscinia).

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of several species of birds, the males having a brilliant patch of metallic red on the throat.
   1. A ruby-throated bulbul (Rubigula dispar, syn. Pycnonotus dispar).
