### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see do, well.

   **Example:** He tries hard to tell a good joke but, alas, he does not do it well.

   **Example:** I did well on the first part of the exam, but totally messed up on the essay question.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To take the prudent approach; to be advised.

   **Usage:** with-infinitive

   **Example:** He would do well to listen more carefully to his wife.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To benefit, to favour.

   **Example:** The company did well by his family after he died.

   **Source context (verbatim):** with a prepositional phrase headed by by or for

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To succeed; to flourish.
