## Interjection

### Forms

- if truth be told — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used when admitting something one might otherwise lie about, e.g. to keep up appearances or be polite.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I'm not sure if I can do this, truth be told.

   **Example:** Actually, truth be told, I just don't like you.

   **Source synonyms:** admittedly; frankly; to be honest; to tell the truth; truth to tell
