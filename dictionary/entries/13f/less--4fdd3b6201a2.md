## Adverb · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Pronunciation

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. comparative degree of little

   **Usage:** comparative; diminutive; form-of

   **Example:** I slept little last night, but even less the night before.

   **Example:** I like him less each time I see him.

   **Example:** All my cares grew less until completely gone.

   **Form of:** [little](../060/little--180ca01b95f0.md)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used for constructing syntactic diminutive comparatives of adjectives and adverbs.

   **Usage:** comparative; diminutive

   **Example:** This gadget is less useful than I expected.

   **Example:** I'm not any less happy for being on my own.

   **Example:** Demanding? I've never known a less demanding patient!

   **Example:** Please do your work a little less hurriedly.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To a smaller extent or degree.

   **Usage:** comparative; diminutive

   **Example:** The grammar book was less than (that is, not at all) helpful.

   **Example:** That this is a positive quality makes it no less a stereotype, and therefore unacceptable.

**Source antonyms:** more
