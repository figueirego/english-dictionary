## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Actions permissible by the rules.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; uncountable

   **Example:** Pretending to be slow is fair game. Pretending to be injured is not.

   **Example:** The referee ruled the unprecedented play fair game.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A goal or an object that may legitimately be sought.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; uncountable

   **Example:** After the middle sister's call from a friend's house, her slice of cake was fair game.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An acceptable subject or target for criticism, scrutiny, mockery, or attack.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; uncountable

   **Example:** Anyone running for office is fair game for criticism.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see fair, game.&#58; a game that is fair, that does not involve cheating, etc.

   **Usage:** uncountable
