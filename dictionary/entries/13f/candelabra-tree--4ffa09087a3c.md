## Noun

### Forms

- candelabra trees — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Several species of Euphorbia, including Euphorbia ingens, Euphorbia candelabrum, Euphorbia ammak, Euphorbia cooperi, Euphorbia lacti, and Euphorbia antiquorum.

   **Source synonyms:** naboom

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A Brazilian tree, Araucaria angustifolia.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A South American tree, Cecropia pachystachya.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An African native, Senna didymobotrya, whose yellow flowers look like lit candles.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Any of various individual trees that resemble a candelabrum.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A decorative motif of a stylized tree with symmetric branches having the appearance of a candelabrum.
