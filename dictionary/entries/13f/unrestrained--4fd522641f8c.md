## Adjective · Record 1

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Immoderate; not restrained or held in check; inordinate (literary).

   **Example:** The party was a scene of unrestrained debauchery.

   **Source synonyms:** intemperate; lavish; wanton; devilish; excessive; horrendous (informal); de trop (literary); extravagant; extreme; immoderate; intemperate; lavish; nimious (Scottish; obsolete); obscene; overmuch; overweening; over the top; prodigal; undue; unmeasured; unreasonable; unrestrained; wanton

   **Source antonyms:** moderate; restrained

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Spontaneous, natural and informal; unconstrained.

   **Example:** Their meeting was one of unrestrained joy.

   **Source synonyms:** genuine; heartfelt; sincere

   **Source antonyms:** forced; insincere; restrained; rigid; stiff

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Not subject to physical restraint.

   **Source synonyms:** unbound; unconstrained; incontinent (literary)

   **Source antonyms:** bound; constrained; restrained
