### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Very much; to a large extent.

   **Usage:** informal; not-comparable

   **Example:** He may like you a ton, but don’t confuse that with love.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Often; frequently.

   **Usage:** informal; not-comparable

   **Example:** It doesn’t happen a ton, but when it does it is great.
