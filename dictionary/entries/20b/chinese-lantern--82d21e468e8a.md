### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A paper lantern in bright colours used for decorative purposes, commonly painted with Chinese art and calligraphy motifs; some float in the air like hot-air balloons, and some float on water.

   **Source synonyms:** sky lantern

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A bladder cherry (Alkekengi officinarum, syn. Physalis alkekengi).

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

<a id="r1-s4"></a>
