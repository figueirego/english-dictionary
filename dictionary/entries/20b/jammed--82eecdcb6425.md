### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Stuck.

   **Example:** The window is jammed shut.

   **Example:** How to treat a jammed finger or toe.

   **Example:** The printer is still jammed. Make sure that all the jammed paper is removed.

   **Example:** The ship was jammed between two rocks.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Overcrowded; congested.

   **Example:** Certain routes of the city are heavily jammed.

   **Example:** These roads are hopelessly jammed with cars and mobile homes during holidays.

   **Example:** A jammed Oktoberfest beer tent.

   **Source synonyms:** jam-packed; jam rammed; packed; packed to the gills; rammed

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Rendered unusable by intentional interference.

   **Example:** All their broadcasts are jammed.

   **Example:** The jammed programming is being distributed on USB sticks instead.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of radio signals
