### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person whose job is cleaning cesspools or sewers, or emptying privies by night.

   **Usage:** historical

   **Source synonyms:** night soil man; nightsoiler; shit-carter

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** dated

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
