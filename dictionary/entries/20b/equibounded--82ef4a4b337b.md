### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having the property that there exists a positive number M such that for all functions f in the family of functions F defined on X, \|f(x)\| ≤ M for all x in X.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a family of functions
