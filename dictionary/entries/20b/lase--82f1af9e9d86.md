### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To use a laser beam on, as for cutting.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The surgeon lased the elongated soft palate, cutting off the excess tissue and stopping the blood flow in one swipe.

   **Example:** The physical chemist lased the atoms as they passed between the electrodes to study their motion.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To use a laser beam on, as for targeting or rangefinding.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The commander lased the target across the valley.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To operate as a laser; to release coherent light due to stimulation.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Once enough of the gas particles are in a higher energy state, they will begin to lase and give off a coherent beam.
