## Noun · Record 2

### Forms

- el cheapos — plural

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A mock-honorific title given to a particular person who is stingy.

   **Usage:** capitalized; often; slang

   **Example:** Don't expect a big tip from El Cheapo over there.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. A person who is stingy; a miser.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Example:** He's such an el cheapo that his wallet hasn't been opened for days.

   **Source synonyms:** see Thesaurus&#58;miser

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. On the game show The Price Is Right, used in reference to the lowest two-digit number on a board of (initially) nine numbers in the pricing game Money Game.

   **Usage:** slang
