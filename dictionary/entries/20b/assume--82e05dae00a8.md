### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To authenticate by means of belief; to surmise; to suppose to be true, especially without proof.

   **Example:** We assume that, as her parents were dentists, she knows quite a bit about dentistry.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To take on a position, duty or form.

   **Example:** Mr. Jones will assume the position of a lifeguard until a proper replacement is found.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To adopt a feigned quality or manner; to claim without right; to arrogate.

   **Example:** He assumed an air of indifference.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To receive, adopt (a person).

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To adopt (an idea or cause).
