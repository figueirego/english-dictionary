## Noun · Etymology 2 · Record 4

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /kwæk/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Forms

- quacks — plural

### Senses

<a id="r4-s1"></a>

**Source sense R4.S1**

1. A fraudulent healer, especially a bombastic peddler in worthless treatments, a doctor who makes false diagnoses for monetary benefit, or an untrained or poorly trained doctor who uses fraudulent credentials to attract patients

   **Usage:** derogatory

   **Example:** That doctor is nothing but a lousy quack!

<a id="r4-s2"></a>

**Source sense R4.S2**

2. Any similar charlatan or incompetent professional.

   **Usage:** derogatory; figuratively

<a id="r4-s3"></a>

**Source sense R4.S3**

3. Any doctor.

   **Usage:** derogatory; humorous; mildly; slang

   **Example:** That quack wants me to quit smoking, eat less, and start exercising. The nerve!

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** snake-oil salesman; medicaster; quacksalver; pharmacopole (archaic)
