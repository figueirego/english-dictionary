### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Someone involved in a contest or battle (as in an agon), protagonist.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The muscle that contracts while the other relaxes.

   **Example:** When bending the elbow, the biceps is the agonist.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A molecule that can combine with a receptor on a cell to produce a physiological reaction.

   **Example:** Acetylcholine is an agonist at the cholinergic receptor.
