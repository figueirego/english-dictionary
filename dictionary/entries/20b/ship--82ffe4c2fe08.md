**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Appended to a noun to form a new noun denoting a property or state of being, time spent in a role, or a specialised union. In particular, the way a set of social duties associated with a particular role shapes or develops one's character.

   **Usage:** countable; morpheme; uncountable

   **Example:** fellow + -ship → fellowship

   **Example:** owner + -ship → ownership
