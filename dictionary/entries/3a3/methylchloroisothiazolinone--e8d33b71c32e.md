<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A synthetic preservative used in many cosmetics, personal care products and cleaners to stop bacteria, mold and fungi. It is effective at very low concentrations but is also a known cause of skin irritation and allergic contact dermatitis in some people. It is often used together with methylisothiazolinone.
