### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To fasten with a button or buttons.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Button up your shirt cuff. It has come undone.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To fasten all the buttons on a coat, or similar item of clothing, to keep warm.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Button up well. It's freezing outside today.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To conclude; to finalize; to put the finishing touches (on).

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** It's time to button up the meeting.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Alternative form of button up one's lip.

   **Usage:** alt-of; alternative

   **Example:** He needs to button up.

   **Alternative form of:** [button up one's lip](../322/button-up-one-s-lip--c8b8d2dd60cd.md)

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To seal off (a military vehicle or facility).

   **Usage:** US; ergative; slang; transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war
