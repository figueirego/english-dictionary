### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A situation where if one of the two players wins the next point, he or she will win the game (but not the set).

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports; tennis

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A juncture within a game when a player, or team, only needs to win the next rally to win the game.

   **Usage:** racket sports
