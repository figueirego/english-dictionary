## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The start of the third hour of the day; 2&#58;00 a.m. (02&#58;00).

   **Usage:** In both the 12-hour clock and the 24-hour clock

   **Example:** The bar closes at two o'clock (= "The bar closes at 2&#58;00 a.m.").

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The start of the fifteenth hour of the day; 2&#58;00 p.m. (14&#58;00).

   **Usage:** Only in the 12-hour clock

   **Example:** The next train comes at two o'clock (= "The next train comes at 14&#58;00").

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A position ahead and rightward (horizontal clock orientation) or above and rightward (vertical clock orientation) (from the location of the 2 mark on a clock face)

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** We've got a bogey at two o'clock.
