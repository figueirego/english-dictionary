### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A small case, often flat and often made of leather, for keeping money (especially paper money), credit cards, etc.

   **Usage:** UK; US

   **Example:** The thief stole all the money and credit cards out of the old man's wallet.

   **Example:** I forgot my wallet and left it at home.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person's bank account or assets.

   **Usage:** broadly; informal

   **Example:** It's unknown if the pro running back's recent sex scandal will hit him in the wallet or not.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An e-wallet or digital wallet.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; computing; engineering; finance; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A thick case or folder with plastic sleeves in which compact discs may be stored.

   **Example:** I won an auction online for a cheap CD wallet.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A bag or pouch.

   **Usage:** archaic

   **Example:** He brought with him a large wallet with some provisions for the road.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A person's buttocks (the area of the body nearest where one keeps one's wallet).

   **Usage:** slang

   **Example:** He fell down and landed on his wallet.
