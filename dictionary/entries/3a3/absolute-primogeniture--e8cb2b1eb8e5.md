### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A form of primogeniture in which sex is irrelevant for inheritance; the oldest surviving child without regard to sex becomes the heir.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** equal primogeniture; lineal primogeniture
