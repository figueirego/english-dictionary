### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The fine particles which are the result of reducing a dry substance by pounding, grinding, or triturating, or the result of decay; dust.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The fine particles which are the result of reducing a dry substance by pounding, grinding, or triturating, or the result of decay; dust.
   1. Cocaine in powder form (as opposed to crack).

      **Usage:** countable; slang; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A mixture of fine dry, sweet-smelling particles applied to the face or other body parts, to reduce shine or to alleviate chaffing.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** cosmetics; lifestyle

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An explosive mixture used in gunnery, blasting, etc.; gunpowder.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Ellipsis of powder snow; light, dry, fluffy snow.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; countable; ellipsis; informal; uncountable

   **Alternative form of:** [powder snow](../398/powder-snow--e613f86fcd23.md)

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; countable; ellipsis; uncountable
