### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The base after home plate in a counter-clockwise path around a baseball infield.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The runner took his lead off first base.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; baseball; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports
