### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see lay, foundation.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To elicit information from a witness to show that the witness has knowledge of a matter before the witness testifies to the matter itself.

   **Example:** In order to lay a foundation for the witness to describe the murder, the prosecutor first had to show that the witness was present when the murder occurred.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To make a start.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** You should lay a foundation, even if you can't finish the project.
