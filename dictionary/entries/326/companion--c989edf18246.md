### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A friend, acquaintance or partner; someone with whom one spends time or accompanies

   **Example:** His dog has been his trusted companion for the last five years.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person employed to accompany or travel with another.

   **Usage:** dated

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The framework on the quarterdeck of a sailing ship through which daylight entered the cabins below.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The covering of a hatchway on an upper deck which leads to the companionway; the stairs themselves.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A knot in whose neighborhood another, specified knot meets every meridian disk.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences; topology

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A person, thing or phenomenon that is closely associated with another person, thing or phenomenon.

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. An appended source of media or information, designed to be used in conjunction with and to enhance the main material.

   **Usage:** attributive
