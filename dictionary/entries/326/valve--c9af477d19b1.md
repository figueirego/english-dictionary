### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A device that controls the flow of a gas or fluid through a space, such as a pipe, manifold, or plenum.

   **Example:** open the valve

   **Example:** close the valve

   **Example:** shut off the valve

   **Example:** An oil refinery has an impressively vast number and variety of valves.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A device that controls the flow of a gas or fluid through a space, such as a pipe, manifold, or plenum.
   1. Such a device that admits fuel and air into the cylinder of an internal combustion engine, or one that allows combustion gases to exit.

      **Example:** In nearly all internal combustion engines, the valves are of the poppet type.

      **Source synonyms:** poppet valve

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. One or more membranous partitions, flaps, or folds, which permit the passage of the contents of a vessel or cavity in one direction, but stop or control the flow in the opposite direction.

   **Example:** the ileocecal valve

   **Example:** the atrioventricular valves

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. One of the leaves of a folding door or of a window sash.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A vacuum tube.

   **Usage:** UK

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. One of the pieces into which certain fruits naturally separate when they dehisce.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A small portion of certain anthers, which opens like a trapdoor to allow the pollen to escape, such as in the barberry.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. One of the pieces or divisions of bivalve or multivalve shells.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. One of the two similar portions of the shell of a diatom.
