### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A hat with bottle corks dangling from strings attached to the brim, traditionally worn in the bush to keep flies away from one's face.

   **Usage:** Australia

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A covering for the head made of cork similar in shape to a pith helmet.

   **Usage:** historical
