### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Lexical borrowings from English that do not correspond directly to English word usage.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A structured artificial language that uses English words in order to be more user friendly for English speakers.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Nonsense text or speech that resembles English in some way.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. English-language jargon or dialect that does not reflect the way most people speak.

   **Usage:** derogatory; uncountable
