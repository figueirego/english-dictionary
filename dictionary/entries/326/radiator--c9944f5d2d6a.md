### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Anything which radiates or emits rays.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A device that lowers engine coolant temperature by conducting heat to the air, through metal fins.

   **Example:** The car overheated because of a broken radiator.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** automotive; engineering; mechanical; mechanical-engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; transport; vehicles

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A finned metal fixture that carries hot water or steam in order to heat a room.

   **Example:** The radiator kept the apartment warm during winter.

   **Example:** Steam hissed from the old iron radiator.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of buildings

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**
