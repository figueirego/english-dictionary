### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. That which howls, especially an animal such as a wolf or a howler monkey.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person hired to howl in mourning at a funeral.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A painfully obvious mistake.

   **Usage:** slang

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A hilarious joke.

   **Usage:** slang

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A bitterly cold day.

   **Usage:** slang

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A person who expresses aggression openly in the form of threats.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; psychology; sciences

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A heavy fall.

   **Usage:** figuratively; sometimes

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A serious accident (especially to come a howler or go a howler; compare come a cropper).

   **Usage:** slang

   **Example:** Our hansom came a howler.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A tremendous lie (especially an obvious one); a whopper.

   **Usage:** slang

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

   **Usage:** dated; slang
