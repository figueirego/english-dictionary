<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Crowded with business or activities; having a great deal going on.

   **Example:** Be careful crossing that busy street.

   **Source synonyms:** heavy; bustling

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Engaged with or preoccupied by an activity or person.

   **Example:** The director cannot see you now&#58; he's busy.

   **Example:** Her telephone has been busy all day.

   **Example:** He is busy with piano practice.

   **Example:** Ramzi is busy getting ready for meetings.

   **Example:** Go away, I’m busy sleeping!

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Having much work to do; having much to get done.

   **Source synonyms:** swamped

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Having a lot going on; complicated or intricate.

   **Example:** Flowers, stripes, and checks in the same fabric make for a busy pattern.

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Officious; meddling.
