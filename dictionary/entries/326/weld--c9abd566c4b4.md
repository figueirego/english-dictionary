### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To join two materials (especially two metals) together by applying heat, pressure and filler, either separately or in any combination.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To make something by welding.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I've welded a metal sculpture.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To bind together inseparably; to unite closely or intimately.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The arrows pierced through the welded ranks of the opposing army.

   **Example:** to weld an alliance
