<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Synonym of punching bag.
   1. A tough bag, usually cylindrical, filled with air, sand, or other material, and suspended so that swings freely, which is punched or kicked when practising sports such as boxing or martial arts.

      **Usage:** UK
