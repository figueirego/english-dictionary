## Adverb

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /əˈlɑːm.ɪŋ.li/ — source labels: UK
- IPA: /əˈlɑɹm.ɪŋ.li/ — source labels: US

### Forms

- more alarmingly — comparative
- most alarmingly — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In an alarming way, frighteningly.

   **Example:** He sneezed alarmingly.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Causing fear or concern.

   **Example:** Alarmingly, he went into a severe sneezing fit.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To an extent that causes alarm.

   **Example:** He went into an alarmingly severe sneezing fit.
