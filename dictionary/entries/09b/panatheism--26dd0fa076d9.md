## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The assertion that no god or gods exist and that then nothing can be correctly termed holy or be considered sacred.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The belief in "all atheism"; the position that all people are atheists to one or more god models.

   **Usage:** uncountable
