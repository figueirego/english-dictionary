### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Essential or decisive for determining the outcome or future of something; extremely important; vital.

   **Example:** The battle of Tali-Ihantala in 1944 is one of the crucial moments in the history of Finland.

   **Example:** A secure supply of crude oil is crucial for any modern nation, let alone a superpower.

   **Source synonyms:** big; central; consequential; critical; crucial; epochful; essential; extraordinary; fateful; fatefraught (archaic; rare); fatesome (rare); heavy; pivotal; high on the totem pole; historic; innegligible; key; life-or-death; meaningful; necessary; noted; noticeable; observable; outstanding; paramount; pertinent; relevant; remarkable; salient; significant; unignorable; valuable; vital; weighty

   **Source antonyms:** noncrucial

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Cruciform or cruciate; cross-shaped.

   **Usage:** archaic

   **Source synonyms:** noncruciform

**Source sense R1.S3**

   **Usage:** Jamaica; especially; slang; Bermuda
