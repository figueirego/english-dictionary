### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A play of usually three acts which can be performed with a small cast and practically no sets or costumes in a small space.

   **Example:** Chamber plays became popular in the early 20th century, with leading exponents being Max Reinhardt and August Strindberg.
