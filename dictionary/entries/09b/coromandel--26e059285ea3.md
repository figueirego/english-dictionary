## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A town on the Coromandel Peninsula, Waikato, North Island, New Zealand, named after HMS Coromandel.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A mountain range in the Coromandel Peninsula, North Island, New Zealand; in full, Coromandel Range.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A geographic region of southeast coastal India, bounded by the Krishna River to the north, the Bay of Bengal to the east, the Kaveri delta to the south, and the Eastern Ghats to the west; located in Tamil Nadu and Andhra Pradesh; in full, Coromandel Coast.
