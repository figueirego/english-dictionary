### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To continue or proceed as before.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** I’ll be gone for a few days, but I hope you will carry on in my absence.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To continue, maintain or pursue (an activity or enterprise).

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** to carry on commerce in a market

   **Example:** Carry on the good work.

   **Example:** It is difficult to carry on a conversation with so many distractions.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To persist, persevere.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** keep calm and carry on

   **Source synonyms:** keep on; hang in; hang on; keep on keeping on

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To talk continuously or prolongedly (about something), often in an excessively excited way.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** He’s always carrying on about his stupid aquarium.

   **Source synonyms:** go on

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To act or behave; especially, to misbehave so as to attract attention; to make a fuss; to behave ostentatiously.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** I really wish you wouldn't carry on like that in public!

   **Example:** I could hear some protestors hollering, but I wasn't close enough to make out what was happening. What were they carrying on about?

   **Source synonyms:** see misbehave § Synonyms

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To have an illicit sexual or flirtatious relationship.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** I thought he was my friend, but all the time he was carrying on with my wife!
