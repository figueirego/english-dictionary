### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A thin membrane resembling a veil or curtain.
   1. The soft palate.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A thin membrane resembling a veil or curtain.
   1. A thin membrane partially covering the cluster of sporangia near the leaf base in quillworts and their extinct relatives.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A thin membrane resembling a veil or curtain.
   1. A veil-like membrane of immature mushrooms extending from the margin of the cap to the stem and is torn by growth, to reveal the gills.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; mycology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A thin membrane resembling a veil or curtain.
   1. A locomotory and feeding organ provided with cilia found in the larval stage of bivalves.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; malacology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A thin membrane resembling a veil or curtain.
   1. An annular membrane, typically bordering a cavity, especially in certain molluscs, medusae, and other invertebrates.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences; zoology

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A thin membrane resembling a veil or curtain.
   1. A delicate membrane found on certain protists.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. An accessory cloud resembling a veil extending over a large distance; normally associated with cumulus and cumulonimbus.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** climatology; meteorology; natural-sciences
