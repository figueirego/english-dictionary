### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Formed or occurring beneath a glacier or other body of ice.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source antonyms:** supraglacial

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Moving at a rate allegedly slower than a glacier; extremely slow.

   **Usage:** excessive; informal; not-comparable

   **Source synonyms:** glacial; crawling; slack; slow-moving; languid; languorous; draggy; tardy (literary); creeping; dreich (Midlands; North; Northern-England; Northern-Ireland; Scotland); gastropodous; glacial; heavy-footed; inching; inert; labored; leaden; lead-footed; lethargic; low-speed; lumbering; plodding; pokey; ponderous; slothlike; slow; slow as a snail; slow as Christmas; slow as molasses; slow as molasses in January; slowfooted; sluggish; stately; steady; subglacial (excessive; informal); sullen; tardigrade; tedious; testudineous; torpid; turtleish; turtlesque; unspeedy
