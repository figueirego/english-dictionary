## Noun

### Forms

- headrooms — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The vertical clearance above someone's head, as in a tunnel, doorway etc; room (space) for one's head.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The vertical measurement, top to bottom, for example for clearance under a bridge.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The ability of a system to reproduce loud sounds free of distortion; dynamic headroom.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; electrical-engineering; electricity; electromagnetism; electronics; energy; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The distance between the actual performance of an algorithm and its maximum possible performance.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Synonym of headspace (“space between the top of the contents of a container and its seal”).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** headspace (synonym; synonym-of)
