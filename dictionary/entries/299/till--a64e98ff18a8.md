- IPA: /tɪl/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r3-s1"></a>

**Source sense R3.S1**

1. A cash register.

   **Usage:** UK

<a id="r3-s2"></a>

**Source sense R3.S2**

2. A removable box within a cash register containing the money.

   **Example:** Pull all the tills and lock them in the safe.

   **Example:** When you've finished serving that customer could you jump off please? We need to take the till.

<a id="r3-s3"></a>

**Source sense R3.S3**

3. The contents of a cash register, for example at the beginning or end of the day or of a cashier's shift.

   **Example:** My count of my till was 30 dollars short.

<a id="r3-s4"></a>

**Source sense R3.S4**

4. A cash drawer in a bank, used by a teller.

<a id="r3-s5"></a>

**Source sense R3.S5**
