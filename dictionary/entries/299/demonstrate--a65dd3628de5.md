### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To show how to use (something).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Can you demonstrate the new tools for us?

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To show the steps taken to create a logical argument or equation.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To participate in or organize a demonstration.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Those people outside are demonstrating against the election results.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To show, display, or present; to prove or make evident

   **Usage:** transitive
