## Verb · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To hold onto something securely or closely.

   **Usage:** literally

   **Example:** Hold tight to that vase, and don't drop it.

   **Example:** Hold tight to me&#58; the bus is going round a sharp bend.

   **Example:** Hold me tight and kiss me.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To remain patient, until a specified event occurs.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** Hold tight, I will go and look for your orders in our back room.
