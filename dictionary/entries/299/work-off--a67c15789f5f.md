### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To lose (weight) by doing physical work; to burn off the calories gained from eating something.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** We need to get moving to work off these donuts.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To end (a debt) by doing labor for the person owed money.

   **Usage:** broadly; transitive

   **Example:** After thirteen years, I've worked off my student debt.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To produce (printed matter etc.) by some process of work.

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To ferment.

   **Usage:** colloquial
