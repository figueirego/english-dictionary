### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any fish thought to resemble a toad.
   1. Any fish of the genus Lophius.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any fish thought to resemble a toad.
   1. Any of the ray-finned fishes of family Batrachoididae of benthic ambush predators.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

   1. Certain species in family Tetraodontidae.
      1. Banded toadfish, of Australia (Torquigener pleurogramma).

         **Usage:** countable; uncountable
