### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A pole tool used for handling things too far away to reach.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Influence; far-reaching power.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** the long arm of the law

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A gun with a long barrel, such as a rifle or shotgun.

   **Source synonyms:** long gun

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**
