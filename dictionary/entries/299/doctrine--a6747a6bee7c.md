### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A belief or tenet, especially about philosophical or theological matters.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** The Incarnation is a basic doctrine of Christianity.

   **Example:** The Four Noble Truths summarise the main doctrines of Buddhism.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The body of teachings of an ideology, most often a religion, or of an ideological or religious leader, organization, group, or text.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** What is the understanding of marriage and family in orthodox Marxist doctrine?

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A self-imposed policy governing some aspect of a country's foreign relations, especially regarding what sort of behavior it will or will not tolerate from other countries.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** the Monroe Doctrine the Brezhnev Doctrine the Negroponte Doctrine
