### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A representation of the tombs of Hasan and Husayn (the grandsons of Muhammad), carried in the South Asian and Indo-Caribbean Shiite processions of Muharram.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source synonyms:** taboot

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Alternative form of ta'ziyeh (“a dramatic art form of Iran performed by Shiites and reenacting the martyrdom of Husayn ibn Ali at the Battle of Karbala, usually performed in the month of Muharram”)

   **Usage:** alt-of; alternative; uncountable

   **Alternative form of:** [ta'ziyeh](../1b4/ta-ziyeh--6d307a5e164f.md)
