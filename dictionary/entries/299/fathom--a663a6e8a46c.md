### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A man's armspan, generally reckoned to be six feet (about 1.8 metres). Later used to measure the depth of water, but now generally replaced by the metre outside American usage.

   **Usage:** US; historical

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A man's armspan, generally reckoned to be six feet (about 1.8 metres). Later used to measure the depth of water, but now generally replaced by the metre outside American usage.
   1. An internationally standardized version of this unit, the international fathom (= 1.8288 metres = 6 feet).

      **Usage:** US; historical

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A measure of distance to shore&#58; the nearest point to shore at which the water depth is the value quoted.

   **Usage:** US

   **Example:** After we'd rowed for an hour, we found ourselves stranded ten fathoms from shore.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An unspecified depth.

   **Usage:** figuratively; in-plural

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Depth of insight; mental reach or scope.

   **Usage:** archaic; figuratively; obsolete

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The act of stretching out one's arms away from the sides of the torso so that they make a straight line perpendicular to the body.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

   **Usage:** also; figuratively; transitive
