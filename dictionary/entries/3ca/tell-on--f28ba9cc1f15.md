### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To inform on, to rat out; to tell a person in authority that someone else has done something wrong.

   **Usage:** childish; usually

   **Example:** My sister told on me when I didn't go to school.

   **Source synonyms:** bewray (archaic)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To cause visible strain to.

   **Example:** Years of drinking started to tell on his body.
