### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Something that refreshes.

   **Example:** The nap was a welcome refresher.

   **Source synonyms:** refreshant

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A review to refresh or renew knowledge.

   **Example:** They recommend a refresher class every few years.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A fee paid to counsel to continue with a case, especially one that was adjourned.
