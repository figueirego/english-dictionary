### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Plainly; clearly; manifestly; evidently.

   **Usage:** rare

   **Source synonyms:** obviously; plainly; clearly; evidently; visibly; apparently; blatantly; baitly (Multicultural-London-English); clearly; evidently; eyely (archaic; rare); glaringly; manifestly; noticeably; obvi (often; sarcastic; slang); obviously; openly; observably; overtly; plainly; visibly

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Seemingly; in appearance.

   **Example:** A man may be apparently friendly, yet malicious in heart.

   **Source synonyms:** ostensibly; seemingly; apparently; at first blush (idiomatic); at first glance; at first sight; on the face of it (idiomatic); on the surface; ostensibly; outwardly; prima facie; seemingly; superficially; surfacely (nonstandard); to all appearances

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. According to what the speaker has read or heard.

   **Example:** Apparently you are quite a good dancer.

   **Source synonyms:** allegedly; reportedly

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. According to what one can deduce.

   **Usage:** often; sarcastic
