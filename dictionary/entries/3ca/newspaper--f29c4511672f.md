### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A publication, usually published daily or weekly, containing news and other articles. Traditionally a print publication typically printed on cheap, low-quality paper; today usually digital and often also available in print.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** Many major newspapers are paywalled because they need the revenue.

   **Example:** The paper boy delivers her newspaper to her front porch every weekday.

   **Source synonyms:** paper; rag; masthead

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The news organization that produces such a publication.

   **Usage:** countable; metonymically

   **Example:** Some newspapers are afraid to do hard-hitting investigative journalism. But this newspaper doesn't run from a fight.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A quantity of or one of the types of paper on which newspapers are printed.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** newsprint
