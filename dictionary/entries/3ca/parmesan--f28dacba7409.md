### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A hard, full-fat Italian cheese from Parma or its neighboring province Reggio Emilia.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source synonyms:** Parmigiano-Reggiano; reggiano

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A similar cheese produced elsewhere.

   **Usage:** broadly; uncountable; usually

   **Example:** Most of the parmesan in our grocery store comes from Wisconsin.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A breaded and fried cutlet, usually of chicken or veal, topped with tomato sauce and cheese.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Money.

   **Usage:** slang; uncountable; usually

   **Source synonyms:** cheddar
