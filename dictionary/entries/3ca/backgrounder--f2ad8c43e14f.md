### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A briefing or document typically provided prior to a meeting or other event that gives basic details on subjects that are likely to be mentioned.

   **Usage:** US

   **Example:** One of the President's aides gave us a ten-minute backgrounder on the dairy industry before the meeting.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person employed to draw backgrounds for a comic or cartoon.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A cattle farmer or rancher who runs a backgrounding operation.
