### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Ruthlessly acquisitive or competitive; of a business or other set of circumstances, trying to succeed at the expense of other people.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; informal; not-comparable

   **Example:** dog-eat-dog world

   **Example:** In this company it's dog eat dog. If you don't do better than the rest, you're on the street in next to no time.

   **Source synonyms:** every man for himself; kill or be killed
