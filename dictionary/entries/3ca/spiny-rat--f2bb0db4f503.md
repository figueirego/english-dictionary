### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of certain caviomorph rodents of the family Echimyidae of Neotropical spiny rats, especially of genus Proechimys, that resemble rats and have stiff, pointed hairs, or spines.

   **Source synonyms:** hedgehog rat; porcupine rat

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of certain genera of family Muridae of rodents&#58;
   1. Any member of two species of genus Rattus.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of certain genera of family Muridae of rodents&#58;
   1. Any member of the species of genus Maxomys (sometimes considered a subgenus of Rattus).

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any of certain genera of family Muridae of rodents&#58;
   1. A Ryukyu spiny rats (Tokudaia).

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Any of certain genera of family Muridae of rodents&#58;
   1. A Sulawesi spiny rats (Echiothrix).
