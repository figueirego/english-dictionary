### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Money in the form of notes or bills and coins, as opposed to checks, credit or electronic transactions.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** How are you off for cash? After you bounced those checks last time, they want to be paid in cash.

   **Source synonyms:** cash money; cold hard cash

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Liquid assets, money that can be traded quickly, as distinct from assets that are invested and cannot be easily exchanged.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Money.

   **Usage:** informal; uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Cash register, or the counter in a business where the cash register is located.

   **Usage:** Canada; countable; usually

   **Example:** Let me just bring these to the cash for you.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An instance of winning a cash prize.

   **Usage:** countable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** gambling; games

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A place where money is kept, or where it is deposited and paid out; a money box.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; usually
