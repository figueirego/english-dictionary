### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Work (especially that which is heavy, repetitive or mindless) that is considered undesirable and therefore delegated to underlings.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; uncountable

   **Example:** He plans to hire somebody to do the grunt work of digging the trenches for the pipes.
