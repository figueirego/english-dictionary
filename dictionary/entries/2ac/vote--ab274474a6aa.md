### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A formalized choice on legally relevant measures such as employment or appointment to office or a proceeding about a legal dispute.

   **Example:** The city council decided the matter should go to public vote.

   **Example:** Parliament will hold a vote of confidence regarding the minister.

   **Example:** One occasion indicative votes were used was in 2003 when MPs were presented with seven different options on how to reform the House of Lords.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An act or instance of participating in such a choice, e.g., by submitting a ballot.

   **Example:** The Supreme Court upheld the principle of one person, one vote.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An ardent wish or desire; a vow; a prayer.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

4. A formalized petition or request.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Any judgment of intellect leading to a formal opinion, a point of view.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Any judgment of intellect leading not only to a formal opinion but also to a particular choice in a legally relevant measure, a point of view as published.

   **Example:** dissenting vote (i.e. in particular the differing opinion published with a judicial judgment considered as a source of information)

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To cast a vote; to assert a formalized choice in an election.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Q&#58; Did you vote last month? A&#58; Yes. I voted for John Smith, and it's a shame that he lost; you can't blame me for the mess we're in now.

   **Example:** The directors voted on the question of whether to increase the R&amp;D spending.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To choose or grant (some objective) by means of a vote, or by general consent.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The directors voted an increase in R&amp;D spending.

   **Example:** The meal was voted a great success.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To exercise one's voting right upon (a ballot or a share of stock).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** vote your ballot

   **Example:** vote your shares

   **Example:** Please vote your ballot using black or blue ink, then seal your ballot inside the privacy envelope.

   **Example:** The depository may vote shares on behalf of investors who have not submitted instruction to the bank.
