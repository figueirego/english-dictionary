**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Example:** a constitutional infirmity constitutional ardour or dullness

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Example:** a constitutional walk

   **Example:** a constitutional right constitutional reforms

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law
