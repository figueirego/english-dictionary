### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A blow that causes the recipient to fall or move backwards, a knock back; a recoil.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Something that impedes or reverses progress; a setback.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A rejection; a refusal.

   **Usage:** Australia; UK; informal

   **Example:** He got a few knockbacks today when he tried to give his resume out at the local shops.
