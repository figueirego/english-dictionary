### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The expected value of the nth largest value (for some n) in a standard normal distribution sample of a particular size, used in normality testing.

   **Example:** For a sample size of 6, the rankits are approximately −1.2672, −0.6418, −0.2016, 0.2016, 0.6418, and 1.2672. This means, for example, that the second largest value of a sample is likely to be around 0.6148.
