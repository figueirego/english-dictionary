## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A rare syndrome caused by a genetic disorder, usually involving coloboma of the eye, heart defects, atresia of the nasal choanae, retardation of growth and/or development, genital and/or urinary abnormalities, and ear abnormalities and deafness.

   **Usage:** uncountable
