### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To cross; to move from one side (of something) to the other, literally or figuratively.

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** In heavy rain, the cavalry couldn't get across the river.

   **Example:** It's impossible to get across with all checkpoints closed.

   **Example:** Bridge the digital divide. Let the poor get across.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To make an idea evident; to successfully explain a thought or feeling; put over.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I couldn't get across to the board this simple economic fact.

   **Example:** Better organized writing helps to get the point across.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To succeed; to do well.

   **Usage:** dated; intransitive; slang; African-American Vernacular

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To annoy (someone); to get on someone's bad side.

   **Usage:** slang; transitive
