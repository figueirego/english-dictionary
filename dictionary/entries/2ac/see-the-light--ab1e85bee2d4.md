### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To undergo a spiritual conversion.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive; religious

   **Example:** Once I was lost in darkness, but now I have seen the light.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To gain an understanding of something previously not understood, especially in a sudden insight.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** Finally, near the end of the meeting, John saw the light and withdrew his objections.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To come into the world or to public notice.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** Never had his book seen the light …until now.

   **Source synonyms:** see the light of day
