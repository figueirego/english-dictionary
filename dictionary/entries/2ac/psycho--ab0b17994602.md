- IPA: /ˈsaɪ̯.koʊ̯/ — source labels: Canada; US

### Forms

- psychos — plural
- syko — alternative; slang

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A person who is psychotic, psychopathic, or (broadly) otherwise insane.

   **Usage:** derogatory; slang

   **Example:** The loony bin up on the hill is full of psychos.

   **Source synonyms:** freak; loony; nutcase; wacko

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. A person who acts in a bizarre or dangerous manner.

   **Usage:** broadly; derogatory; slang

   **Example:** She complained that he was a psycho for driving at such a high speed in heavy traffic.

   **Source synonyms:** creep; freak; loose cannon; weirdo
