2. A word having the same spelling as another, but with a different meaning and (often) pronunciation.

   **Example:** The word lead (/ˈlɛd/), for the metal, and lead (/ˈliːd/), for a leash, are heteronyms.

   **Example:** The word agape, "astonished" or "wide open," is a heteronym of agape, "Christian love."

   **Source synonyms:** heterophone; homograph
