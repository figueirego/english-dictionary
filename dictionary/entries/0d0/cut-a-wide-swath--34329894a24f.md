### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To clear a broad track through a grassland, woodland, geographical region, or other area, either by natural means or by human action.

   **Usage:** US

   **Source synonyms:** blaze a trail; cut a swath

**Source sense R1.S2**
