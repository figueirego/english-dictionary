### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To live in isolation&#58; to live hermetically (especially, hermitically); thus, to be unaware of or be oblivious to common knowledge; to be isolated from knowledge of current events.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** Of course I know about Looney Tunes, SpongeBob SquarePants, and The Simpsons. I do not live under a rock.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see live, under, rock.

   **Example:** Eventually their misanthropy got so bad that they built a ferroconcrete underground home in the farthest boondocks of North Dakota so that they could literally live under a rock.
