### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of various small bagpipes having a soft sound, especially with a bellows, which were popular in France in the 17th and early 18th century.

   **Usage:** historical

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

   **Source synonyms:** pastoral oboe; shepherd's pipe

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of various small bagpipes having a soft sound, especially with a bellows, which were popular in France in the 17th and early 18th century.
   1. A pastoral air or tune that has a drone imitating such an instrument; also, a dance performed to this music.

      **Usage:** broadly; historical

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

      **Source synonyms:** pastoral oboe; shepherd's pipe

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An organ stop using reed pipes with cone-shaped resonators, found in organs in France in the 17th and 18th centuries.

   **Usage:** historical

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A small oboe without a cap for its reed, which evolved from the chanter or pipe of bagpipes; a piccolo oboe.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

   **Source synonyms:** oboe musette; piccoloboe

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. In full musette bag&#58; a small bag or knapsack with a shoulder strap, formerly used by soldiers, and now (cycling) chiefly by cyclists to hold food and beverages or other items.

   **Usage:** US
