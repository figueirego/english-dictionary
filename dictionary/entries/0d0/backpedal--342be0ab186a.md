### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To pedal backwards on a bicycle.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To step backwards.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The player had to backpedal before catching the ball.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To distance oneself from an earlier claim or statement; back off from an idea.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** Though initially adopting a hard-line stance, the politician soon started to backpedal.
