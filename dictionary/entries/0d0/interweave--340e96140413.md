### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To combine (things) through weaving.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The artist interwove strands of gold into the fabric.

   **Example:** The branches interweave to form a dense canopy.

   **Example:** interweave threads

   **Source synonyms:** interlace; interthread; intertwine; betwine; braid; entwist; entwine; interlace; interthread; intertwine; intertwist; interweave; lace; lock; raddle; ruddle; twine; twist

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To intermingle.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The novel interweaves several personal stories with the history of the region.

   **Example:** The documentary interweaves interviews with archival footage.

   **Example:** interweave stories

   **Example:** interweave different elements

   **Source synonyms:** blend; commingle; mingle
