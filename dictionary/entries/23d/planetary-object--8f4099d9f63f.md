## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any secondary body in the Solar system that is geologically differentiated or in hydrostatic equilibrium and thus has a planet-like geology&#58; a planet, dwarf planet, or the larger moons and asteroids.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astronomy; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any substantial body in orbit around the Sun or other star&#58; a planet, minor planet, or comet.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astronomy; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
