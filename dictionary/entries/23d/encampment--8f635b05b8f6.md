### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A place where people (such as displaced people, soldiers, or travellers) encamp, that is, stay in tents or other temporary structures; a camp or campsite.

   **Usage:** countable; often; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A place where people or things stay temporarily.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An enclosed or fortified prehistoric site, especially a European Iron Age hill-fort.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** archaeology; history; human-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A meeting (especially an annual meeting or important conference) of an outdoorsy group of people, such as military veterans or scouts of the scouting movement.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A meeting of Freemasons.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; obsolete; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Freemasonry; freemasonry; lifestyle

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The act of encamping or setting up a camp; also, the state of being encamped or in a camp.

   **Usage:** uncountable
