## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A computer vision technique that detects connected regions ("blobs") in an image or video frame and follows their position, size, shape, and movement from frame to frame.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A visual style that overlays digital boxes, crosshairs, ID numbers, and tracking lines onto moving subjects in a video or image.

   **Usage:** broadly; uncountable; aesthetic
