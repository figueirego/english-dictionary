### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The total mass of a living thing or a part thereof (such as a cell).

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The total mass of all, or a specified category of, living things within a specific area, habitat, etc.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Organic matter derived from living things that were recently alive (especially vegetation) used as a fuel or source of energy, especially if cultivated for that purpose; also, fuel produced from such organic matter; biofuel.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually
