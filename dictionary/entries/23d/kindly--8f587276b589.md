## Adjective · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Pronunciation

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having a kind personality; kind, warm-hearted, sympathetic.

   **Example:** A kindly old man sits on the park bench every afternoon feeding pigeons.

   **Source synonyms:** openhearted

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Favourable, gentle, pleasant, tidy, auspicious, beneficent.

   **Usage:** dated

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Lawful.

   **Usage:** archaic

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Natural; inherent to the kind or race.

   **Usage:** obsolete

## Adverb · Etymology 2 · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈkaɪn(d)li/ — source labels: General-American; Received-Pronunciation

### Forms

- kindlier — comparative
- more kindly — comparative
- kindliest — superlative
- most kindly — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. In a kind manner, out of kindness.

   **Example:** He kindly offered to take us to the station in his car.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. In a favourable way.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. Used to make a polite request&#58; please.

   **Example:** Kindly refrain from walking on the grass.

   **Example:** May we request that patrons kindly take their seats.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. With kind acceptance; used with take.

   **Usage:** US

   **Example:** I don't take kindly to threats.

   **Example:** Aunt Daisy didn't take it kindly when we forgot her anniversary.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. Kind of, somewhat.

   **Usage:** dialectal

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. Readily.

   **Usage:** archaic

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

**Source sense R2.S7**

7. Naturally.

   **Usage:** obsolete

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** thoughtfully — in a kind manner; be so kind as to — used to make polite requests; please — used to make polite requests
