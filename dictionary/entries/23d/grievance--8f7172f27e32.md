### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Something which causes grief.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source synonyms:** hardship; sorrow; affliction; aggrievance; distress; grief; grievance; hardship; misery; ruth (obsolete); sadness; sorrow; undelight; vexation; woe; wrong

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A wrong or hardship suffered, which is the grounds of a complaint.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Feelings of being wronged; outrage; resentment, bitterness or anger.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A complaint or annoyance.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source synonyms:** bother; vexation; annoyance; bother; faff (British; slang); grievance; hassle; inconvenience; irritation; nuisance; palaver (British); peeve; plague; trouble; vexation

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A formal complaint, especially in the context of a unionized workplace.

   **Usage:** countable; human resources

   **Example:** If you want the problem fixed, you'll have to file a grievance with the city.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Violation of regulations or objectionable behavior.

   **Usage:** uncountable
