## Verb · Etymology 1 · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /dʌl/ — source labels: General-American; Received-Pronunciation
- IPA: /dʊl/ — source labels: Northern-England

### Forms

- dulls — present; singular; third-person
- dulling — participle; present
- dulled — participle; past
- dulled — past
- dul — alternative
- dulle — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To render dull; to remove or blunt an edge or something that was sharp.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Years of misuse have dulled the tools.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To soften, moderate or blunt; to make dull, stupid, or sluggish; to stupefy.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He drinks to dull the pain.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To lose a sharp edge; to become dull.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** A razor will dull with use.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To render dim or obscure; to sully; to tarnish.
