### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To grow teeth.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Babies typically start teething at about six months.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To bite on something to relieve discomfort caused by growing teeth.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** She'll teethe on anything that she can get into her mouth.
