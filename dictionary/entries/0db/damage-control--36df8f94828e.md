## Noun

### Forms

- damage controls — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The limiting of damage resulting from an action when damage cannot be avoided.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A department or group, as aboard a naval vessel, responsible for taking action to control damage caused by fire, collision, etc.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any efforts, as by a company or organization, to curtail losses, counteract unfavorable publicity, etc.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; public relations

   **Example:** As news of the impending layoffs spread faster, the company finally began damage control.
