### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A water-soluble pigment or paint.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** Jim's aunt uses only watercolours to paint her pictures.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A painting made by using such pigment.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** Jim's aunt paints beautiful watercolours.

   **Source synonyms:** [aquarelle](../2de/aquarelle--b7b416443cf9.md)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. This kind of painting as a genre.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** I'm not a big fan of watercolour; I much prefer oil painting.
