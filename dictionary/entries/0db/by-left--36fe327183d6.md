### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Done without abiding by proper rules and procedures, as it may be more convenient to ignore them entirely, bend the rules or use a workaround.

   **Usage:** Singapore; Singlish; idiomatic; slang

## Prep\_phrase · Record 2

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Used to refute a previous statement of by right by demonstrating that something is, in practice, done in an unofficial manner, without much regard for proper procedures and protocols.

   **Usage:** Singapore; Singlish; humorous; idiomatic; often; slang

   **Example:** By right, we’re supposed to lock the room every night. By left, we only do it every Friday since no-one bothers to check.
