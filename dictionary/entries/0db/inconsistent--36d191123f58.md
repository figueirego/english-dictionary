### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Not consistent&#58;
   1. Not compatible (with another thing); incompatible, discrepant, at odds.

      **Example:** His account of the evening was inconsistent with the security-camera footage.

      **Source antonyms:** consistent

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Not consistent&#58;
   1. Lacking internal consistency; self-contradicting; not compatible with itself.

      **Example:** He gave an inconsistent account of the evening, saying he called her before eight, but later that he had not talked to her until after nine.

      **Source synonyms:** spotty; mixed

      **Source antonyms:** consistent

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
