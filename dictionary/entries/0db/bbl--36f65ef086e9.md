### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Abbreviation of barrels and occasionally also of barrel (singular).
   1. Volumes of crude oil pumped, stored, or sold (in bulk).

      **Usage:** especially; most often

      **Example:** measured in 1000-bbl units

      **Source context (verbatim):** in the 20th and 21st centuries

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Abbreviation of barrels and occasionally also of barrel (singular).
   1. Barrels (casks) transported or sold, or the volume that they represent.

      **Usage:** especially

      **Example:** 5 bbls. flour

      **Source context (verbatim):** in previous centuries

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Abbreviation of barrels and occasionally also of barrel (singular).
   1. Venturis in carburetion.

      **Usage:** internal combustion engines

      **Example:** 1-bbl carburetor

      **Example:** 4-bbl carburetor

      **Example:** three 2-bbl carbs

      **Source synonyms:** v; V
