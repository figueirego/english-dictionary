### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of dramatizing.
   1. The act of adapting a literary work for the theatre.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The act of dramatizing.
   1. The act of presenting something in a dramatic or melodramatic manner.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>
