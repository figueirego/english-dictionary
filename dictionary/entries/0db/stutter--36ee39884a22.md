### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To speak (words) with a spasmodic repetition of vocal sounds.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** He stuttered a few words of thanks.

   **Source synonyms:** hacker (Britain; archaic; dialectal); stammer

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To expel a gas with difficulty.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** I was stuttering after the marathon.

   **Example:** The engine of the old car stuttered going up the slope.
