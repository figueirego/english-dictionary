### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Very tired; exhausted.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** After hours on the airplane and a long drive, I'm bushed.

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Mentally unwell due to isolation, especially due to working in a remote mine or camp; experiencing cabin fever.

   **Usage:** Canada

   **Example:** After three weeks up there I was bushed and needed to get out.
