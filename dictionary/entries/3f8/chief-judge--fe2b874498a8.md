### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The principal, presiding, or most senior judge of especially a lower level court or of a circuit

   **Usage:** US

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One of the judges who preside over the sessions and deliberations of a court, while also overseeing the administration of the court
