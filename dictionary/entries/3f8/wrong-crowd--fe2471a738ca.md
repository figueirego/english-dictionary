### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A group of people deemed to have a negative influence on a person's behavior; a bad crowd.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** to fall in with the wrong crowd

   **Example:** to hang out with the wrong crowd

   **Example:** Jimmy's mother was concerned that he might have fallen in with the wrong crowd.
