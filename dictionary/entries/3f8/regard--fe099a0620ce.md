<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One's concern for another; esteem; relation, reference.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A particular aspect or detail; respect, sense.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; preceded by “in” or “with”

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The worth or estimation in which something or someone is held.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** He is held in great regard in Whitehall.

   **Source synonyms:** esteem; repute
