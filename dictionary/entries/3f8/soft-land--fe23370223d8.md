### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To land (a vehicle or other payload) on the surface of a body in such a manner that it survives the landing mostly or entirely intact and is usable or able to operate, at least briefly, following touchdown.

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** The Spirit and Opportunity rovers were soft-landed on Mars in 2004.

   **Example:** The crate of supplies soft-landed in the drop zone under its parachute, allowing the soldiers to replenish their food and ammo for the next few days of fighting.
