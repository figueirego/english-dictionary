## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A holiday or observance commemorating a female monarch and observing her birth, including&#58;
   1. the Queen's Official Birthday (observed on the Saturday falling between 11 and 17 June during the reign of Queen Elizabeth II (died 2022))

      **Usage:** UK

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A holiday or observance commemorating a female monarch and observing her birth, including&#58;
   1. the Queen's actual birthday (21 April in the case of Queen Elizabeth II)

      **Usage:** UK

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
