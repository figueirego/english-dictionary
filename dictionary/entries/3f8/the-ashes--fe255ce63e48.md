## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A trophy played for by England and Australia in Test cricket; in the form of a small urn containing the ashes of a cremated cricket stump or set of bails.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; cricket; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; cricket; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports
