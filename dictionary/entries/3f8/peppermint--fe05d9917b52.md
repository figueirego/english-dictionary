### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A hybrid herb of the mint family (Mentha × piperita), formed by crossing watermint and spearmint, which has a high menthol content and a sharp flavor and is used in cooking, especially in herb teas and in confections.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of various eucalypts of southeastern Australia whose leaves yield peppermint-like essential oils.

   **Usage:** Australia; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of various sweets containing extract of the peppermint herb; peppermint confectionery.

   **Usage:** countable
