### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A problem, or unpleasant or painful situation or person that one has to accept and deal with, although one finds it very difficult, or it causes trouble or worry over a long period of time.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** We all have our crosses to bear.

   **Example:** The loss was a heavy cross for her to bear.
