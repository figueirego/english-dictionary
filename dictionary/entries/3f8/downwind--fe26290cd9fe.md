### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. in the same direction as the wind is blowing

   **Usage:** not-comparable; usually

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. positioned relative to something in such a way that it can be smelled in the wind

   **Usage:** not-comparable; usually

   **Example:** I don't want to live downwind from a pig farm.

   **Source context (verbatim):** followed by from
