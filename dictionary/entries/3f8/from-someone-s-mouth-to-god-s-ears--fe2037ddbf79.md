### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Synonym of God willing&#58; may what someone just said be (or be as if) a prayer to God; may the Lord hear our prayer.

   **Example:** Person A&#58; That guy might get his comeuppance someday. Person B&#58; From your mouth to God's ears.
