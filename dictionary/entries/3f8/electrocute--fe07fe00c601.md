### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To kill by electric shock.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Her hairdryer fell into the tub while she was bathing, and she was electrocuted.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To execute by electric shock, often by means of an electric chair.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The executioner threw the switch on Old Sparky, and the condemned prisoner was electrocuted for his crimes.

   **Source synonyms:** fry (slang)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To inflict a severe electric shock (not necessarily fatal) upon.

   **Usage:** informal; proscribed; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** electrify; shock; tase; zap
