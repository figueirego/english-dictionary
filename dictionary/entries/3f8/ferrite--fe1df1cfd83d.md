### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The interstitial solid solution of carbon in body-centered cubic iron.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** ferrite transition zone

   **Source synonyms:** mul&#58;α-Fe; alpha iron

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of a class of metal oxides which show ferrimagnetism; used in transformers, inductors, antennas, recording heads, microwave devices, motors and loudspeakers.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** ferrite magnets

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The anion FeO₂²⁻, and any of the salts (formally derived from the unknown ferrous acid) derived from it.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** sodium ferrite

   **Example:** calcium ferrite

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; inorganic-chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences
