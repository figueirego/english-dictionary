### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Ideology that regards cis people as superior to other people (including trans people, nonbinary people, and genderfluid people), or diminishes the rights or interests of those who are not cis.

   **Usage:** neologism; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Behavior (individual or systemic) in which cis people are privileged over other people in society (trans people, nonbinary people, genderfluid people).

   **Usage:** uncountable
