### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A concealed hole in the ground designed to kill, trap, or severely injure a target.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A natural trap where ancient animals became mired and died en masse, resulting in dense, highly concentrated fossil deposits.
