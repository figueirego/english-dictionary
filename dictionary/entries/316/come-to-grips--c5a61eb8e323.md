### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To confront each other decisively.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The armies finally came to grips at Stalingrad.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of two opponents

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To attempt to face or resolve an internal contradiction or difficulty.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** It was not until five years after graduation that she finally came to grips and began a career.

   **Example:** The country is coming to grips and accepting its responsibilities.
