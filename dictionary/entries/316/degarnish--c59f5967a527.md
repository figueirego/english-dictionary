### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To strip or deprive entirely, as of furniture, ornaments, etc.; to disgarnish.

   **Example:** to degarnish a house

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To deprive of a garrison, or of troops necessary for defence.

   **Example:** to degarnish a city or fort
