### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In an emotional manner; displaying emotion.

   **Example:** "For a long time, I thought I'd never live to see this day," he said emotionally. "Thank you all for everything you did in my behalf."

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Regarding emotions.

   **Example:** Physically, it was easy, but emotionally it was the hardest thing I've ever done.
