### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To move forward and into view, to emerge, to appear.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Suddenly a man appeared and came forth out of the fog.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To volunteer to provide something.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Betty came forth with verification of his claims.
