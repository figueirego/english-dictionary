### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To travel past without stopping.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** I noticed my wife pass by with a bag of shopping.

   **Source synonyms:** go by; overgo; pass

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To elapse.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Nearly a year had passed by since I first laid eyes on him.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a period of time

   **Source synonyms:** go by; go past; pass

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To travel past (something) without stopping.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** It was hard to stick to my diet all weekend, especially when I passed by the pastry shop.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. to ignore; to disregard.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** I had the distinct worry that happiness would pass me by.

   **Example:** He explained the idea in great detail, but still, his boss passed by the idea without a mention.

   **Source synonyms:** discount; disregard; ignore; misregard; pass; unheed

   **Source antonyms:** examine; pay attention; recognize

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To drop by.

   **Usage:** Philippines
