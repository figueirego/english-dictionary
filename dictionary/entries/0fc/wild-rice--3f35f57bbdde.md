### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Rice of any of four species of grasses which form the genus Zizania, which have edible grains and edible stems.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Edible grain (edible rice) harvested from these grasses.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually
