### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Initialism of received signal strength indication, an arbitrary number roughly corresponding to a given power absorbed by a receiving system when receiving a signal, with greater numbers corresponding to greater power (and lesser to lesser), but with no further definition of relationship.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; initialism

   **Alternative form of:** received signal strength indication
