### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Absence of responsibility for a crime, tort, etc.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Her attorney managed to convince the jury of her innocence.

   **Source synonyms:** unguilt

   **Source antonyms:** guilt; blameworthiness; culpability; guilt; guiltiness; noninnocence; uninnocence

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Lack of understanding about sensitive subjects such as sexuality and crime.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** In his innocence, he offered the stranger to bring the package to Paris, never suspecting it contained drugs.

   **Source synonyms:** naivety; unworldliness; purity

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Lack of ability or intention to harm or damage.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Tests have demonstrated the innocence of this substance.

   **Source synonyms:** harmlessness; innocuity

   **Source antonyms:** harmfulness; nocence

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Imbecility; mental deficiency.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** idiocy; moronity
