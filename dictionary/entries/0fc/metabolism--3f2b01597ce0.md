### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The chemical processes that occur within a living organism in order to maintain life.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The metabolisms of aerobic bacteria require oxygen

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; physiology; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The rate at which these processes occur for a given organism

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Small animals have a much higher metabolism than large animals because they lose more body heat

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; physiology; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The processes that maintain any dynamic system.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; figuratively; uncountable
