### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Able to be represented by a map.

   **Example:** Satellite imagery has made many inaccessible areas much more mappable.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Able to be associated with something else by means of a mapping.

   **Example:** Computers on the Internet are mappable to IP addresses.
