### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used to indicate the timing and contingency of some obligation in contracts, especially financial.

   **Example:** The dividend on the preferred stock shall be payable in equal amount to the dividend on the common stock when, as, and if the Company declares a dividend on the common stock.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance; law
