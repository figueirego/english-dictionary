<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To lengthen by pulling.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I stretched the rubber band until it almost broke.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To lengthen when pulled.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The rubber band stretched almost to the breaking point.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To pull tight.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** First, stretch the skin over the frame of the drum.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To extend one’s limbs or another part of the body, for example in order to improve the elasticity of one's muscles.

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** I always stretch my muscles before exercising.

   **Example:** When the cat woke up, it yawned and stretched.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To extend physically, especially from a limit point and/or to a limit point.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The beach stretches from Cresswell to Amble.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To get more use than expected from a limited resource.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** I managed to stretch my coffee supply a few more days.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To make inaccurate by exaggeration.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** To say crossing the street was brave is stretching the meaning of "brave" considerably.

   **Example:** To say he's been to this park a million times is stretching the numbers. The true number is around 30 or 40.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Her bizarre explanation really stretches credulity.

**Source sense R1.S9**

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. To increase, to grow.

   **Example:** As I sat in the waiting room, the minutes stretched into hours.
