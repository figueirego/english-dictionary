## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One of the short ends of a rectangular table, considered to be the most prominent place and often reserved for the host(s).

   **Usage:** dated

   **Source synonyms:** [head of the table](../3ab/head-of-the-table--ead1107fb3ee.md)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. At a school, the senior pupil in charge of those assigned to a particular meal table; a similar position in other institutions.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The end of an examining, operating or imaging table on which the patient’s head rests.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences
