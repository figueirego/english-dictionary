### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A metallic-green beetle, Cetonia aurata, with a distinct V-shaped scutellum, native to Eurasia.

   **Source synonyms:** green rose chafer; rose beetle; rose bug

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any beetle of the subfamily Cetoniinae; a flower chafer.

   **Usage:** formerly

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A tan-colored beetle, Macrodactylus subspinosus, found in North America.

   **Source synonyms:** rose beetle; rose bug
