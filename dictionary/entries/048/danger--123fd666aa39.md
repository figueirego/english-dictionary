### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Exposure to likely harm; risk of death or serious injury.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** There's plenty of danger in the desert.

   **Source synonyms:** peril

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An instance or cause of likely serious harm.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Mischief.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The stop indication of a signal (usually in the phrase "at danger").

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; mainly outside US

   **Example:** The north signal was at danger because of the rockslide.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** rail-transport; railways; transport

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Ability to harm; someone's dominion or power to harm or penalise. See in one's danger, below.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Liability.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Difficulty; sparingness; hesitation.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A contemptible person, especially one seen as perverted or mentally ill.

   **Usage:** UK; countable; derogatory; uncountable
