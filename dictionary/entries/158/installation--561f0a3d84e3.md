### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An act of installing.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The installation of the new software took only a few minutes.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Something installed, especially the whole of a system of machines, apparatus, and accessories, when set up and arranged for practical working, as in electric lighting, transmission of power, etc.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A work of installation art.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The museum is displaying a modern art installation.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A grouping of facilities that constitute a permanent military base.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** A military installation was built near the border.
