### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A 20th-century philosophical movement emphasizing the uniqueness of each human existence in freely making its self-defining choices; also (generally), a philosophy which emphasizes existence over essence.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** The heyday of existentialism occurred in the mid-twentieth century.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; philosophy; sciences

   **Source antonyms:** noumenalism

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The philosophical views of a particular thinker associated with the existentialist movement.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** Sartre’s existentialism is atheistic, but the existentialism of Marcel is distinctly Christian.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; philosophy; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Synonym of structuralism (“a school of thought that focuses on exploring the individual elements of consciousness, how they are organized into more complex experiences, and how these mental phenomena correlate with physical events”).

   **Usage:** historical; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; philosophy; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** structuralism (synonym; synonym-of)
