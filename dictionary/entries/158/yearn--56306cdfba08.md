### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To have a strong desire for something or to do something; to long for or to do something.

   **Usage:** also; figuratively; intransitive

   **Example:** All I yearn for is a simple life.

   **Source synonyms:** pant; salivate; burn

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To have a strong desire for something or to do something; to long for or to do something.
   1. To long for something in the past with melancholy or nostalgia.

      **Usage:** also; figuratively; intransitive; specifically

      **Source synonyms:** pant; salivate; burn

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of music, words, etc.&#58; to express strong desire or longing.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To have strong feelings of affection, love, sympathy, etc., toward someone.

   **Usage:** dated; intransitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To be distressed or pained; to grieve; to mourn.

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Often followed by out&#58; to perform (music) which conveys or say (words) which express strong desire or longing.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To have a strong desire or longing (for something or to do something).

   **Usage:** archaic; poetic; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** earn (obsolete)

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To cause (someone) to have strong feelings of affection, love, sympathy, etc.; also, to grieve or pain (someone).

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** earn (obsolete)
