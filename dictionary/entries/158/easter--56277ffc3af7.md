### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A Christian feast commemorating the resurrection of Jesus Christ. It is celebrated on the first Sunday (and Monday) following the full moon that occurs on or next after the vernal equinox, ranging in most of Western Christianity (such as Protestantism and Roman Catholicism) from March 22 to April 25, and in Eastern Christianity (such as the Coptic Church and Eastern Orthodox Church) from April 4 to May 8.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** We spent each of the past five Easters together as a family.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity

   **Source synonyms:** Easter Day; Easter Sunday

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Eastertide (“the period from Easter to Whitsun”).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Usually preceded by an inflection of make&#58; the act of receiving the Eucharist during Easter.

   **Usage:** countable; dated; figuratively; specifically; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Catholicism; Christianity; Roman-Catholicism

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Ellipsis of Easter term.

   **Usage:** Ireland; UK; abbreviation; alt-of; countable; ellipsis; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** education; law

   **Alternative form of:** [Easter term](../220/easter-term--88218e399dcd.md)

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A festival held in honour of the goddess Eostre or Ostara, celebrated at the vernal equinox or within the month of April; Eostre, Ostara.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** lifestyle; paganism; religion

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**
