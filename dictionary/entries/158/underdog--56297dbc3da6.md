### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A competitor thought unlikely to win.

   **Example:** They were the underdogs in the basketball competition.

   **Source synonyms:** little guy; rank outsider

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Somebody at a disadvantage.

   **Source synonyms:** bottom dog

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A high swing wherein the person pushing the swing runs beneath the swing while the person being pushed is at the forward limit of the arc.
