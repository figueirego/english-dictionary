### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Greatly; to a large degree, especially one large enough to be frankly appreciable.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** grossly inflated expectations

   **Example:** grossly enlarged hepatic lobes, evident on palpation

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To a serious, severe, or offensive degree.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** grossly misjudged

   **Example:** grossly inflamed

   **Source synonyms:** gravely; flagrantly

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. In a gross (disgusting) manner; without delicacy.

   **Usage:** informal; not-comparable

   **Example:** The men were talking grossly about their sexual conquests.

   **Source synonyms:** disgustingly

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Roughly; approximately; inexactly; sketchily.

   **Usage:** archaic; not-comparable
