**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To (cause to) float easily or gently through the air.

   **Usage:** ergative

   **Example:** A breeze came in through the open window and wafted her sensuous perfume into my eager nostrils.

   **Source synonyms:** breathe

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

   **Usage:** intransitive

3. To give notice to by waving something; to wave the hand to; to beckon.
