### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The grouping or juxtaposition of things, especially words or sounds.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Such a specific grouping.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A sequence of words or terms that co-occur more often than would be expected by chance (i.e., the statistically significant placement of particular words in a language), often representing an established name for, or idiomatic way of conveying, a particular semantic concept.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences; translation-studies

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A method of finding an approximate solution of an ordinary differential equation L\[y\]=0 by determining coefficients in an expansion y(x)=y\_0(x)+∑ₗ₌₀^qαₗy\_l(x) so as to make L\[y\] vanish at prescribed points; the expansion with the coefficients thus found is the sought approximation.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences
