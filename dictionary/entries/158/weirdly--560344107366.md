### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In a weird manner.

   **Example:** The teacher suspected he was on drugs because he was behaving weirdly.

   **Example:** She wore a necklace strung with weirdly-shaped pearls.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** manner

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Contrary to expectations.

   **Usage:** evaluative

   **Example:** Weirdly, Alice showed up 10 minutes early. She was usually late for everything.

   **Example:** The roughness of the wool was weirdly pleasant.
