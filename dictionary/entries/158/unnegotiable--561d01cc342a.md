   **Extractor diagnostics (not usage labels):** no-gloss

**Supplemental definition (evidence reviewed; source gap preserved):** The original empty count noun is independently supported by plural nominal usage in a Canadian regulator’s hearing transcript. Paragraphs 9475–9477 contrast principles that could not be waived with the details to be negotiated. This is stronger evidence of the general nominal meaning than the previously examined Sarah Kane literary usage; it does not copy the sibling adjective or turn that literary interpretation into a general meaning.
