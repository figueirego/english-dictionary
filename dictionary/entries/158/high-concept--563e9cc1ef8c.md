### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of a work such as a book, film, or television programme&#58; based on an appealing and easily communicable idea; also, of or relating to such a work.

   **Usage:** derogatory; sometimes

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** broadcasting; film; literature; media; publishing; television

   **Source antonyms:** low-concept

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Based on an idea or theme, especially one regarded as highly artistic or intellectual.

   **Usage:** proscribed; sometimes

   **Example:** I’m not too sure about that new molecular gastronomy restaurant. It seems too high-concept and snooty for me.
