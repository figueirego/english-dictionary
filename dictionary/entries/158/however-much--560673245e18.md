## Determiner · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Whatever amount of; regardless of the amount of (something stated or implied).

   **Example:** It's not for sale, however much money you're offering.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Emphatic form of how much, expressing shock, surprise, etc.

   **Usage:** emphatic; form-of; interrogative

   **Example:** However much did you pay for that?

   **Form of:** [how much](../1ad/how-much--6b6d33f066ac.md); expressing shock; [surprise](../020/surprise--0832c0adf054.md); [etc](../204/etc--812de6e718f8.md)

## Adverb · Record 2

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To whatever extent; regardless of the extent to which.

   **Example:** You have to go to school however much you may dislike it.
