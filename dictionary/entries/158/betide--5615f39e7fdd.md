### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Often used in a prediction (chiefly in woe betide) or a wish&#58; to happen to (someone or something); to befall.

   **Usage:** dated; literary; transitive; usually

   **Example:** Whether good or ill betide you, trust in yourself.

   **Source context (verbatim):** used in the subjunctive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Chiefly in the third person&#58; to happen; to take place; to bechance, to befall.

   **Usage:** dated; intransitive; literary

   **Source synonyms:** betime (archaic); come to pass; occur; tide (obsolete); transpire; bechance; befall; behap; belimp (obsolete); betide (dated; literary); betime (archaic); beworth; chance (archaic); come about; come to pass; crop up; evene (obsolete); eventuate; go on; hap (literary); happen; hold; occur; pass; take place; tide (obsolete); transpire
