## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The start of the tenth hour of the day; 9&#58;00 a.m. (09&#58;00).

   **Usage:** uncountable; In both the 12-hour clock and the 24-hour clock

   **Example:** The breakfast buffet opens at nine o'clock (= "The breakfast buffet opens at 9&#58;00 a.m.").

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The start of the twenty-second hour of the day; 9&#58;00 p.m. (21&#58;00).

   **Usage:** uncountable; Only in the 12-hour clock

   **Example:** Supper was served at nine o'clock (= "Supper was served at 9&#58;00 p.m.").

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To the left (horizontal clock orientation) (from the location of the 9 mark on a clock face)

   **Usage:** informal; uncountable

   **Example:** We've got a bogey at nine o'clock.
