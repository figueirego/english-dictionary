## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Physical damage caused to an object due to ordinary use over time; specifically (accounting, law), as justifying a write-down of the object's value in a set of accounts, or exclusion of compensation for the object under a warranty of quality.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** The washing machine flooded the basement due to wear and tear, so Margaret was not eligible to claim compensation from the manufacturer.

   **Source synonyms:** tear and wear

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Physical damage caused to an object due to ordinary use over time; specifically (accounting, law), as justifying a write-down of the object's value in a set of accounts, or exclusion of compensation for the object under a warranty of quality.
   1. The process by which such physical damage is caused.

      **Usage:** uncountable

      **Example:** The structural integrity of the bridge has been compromised by constant wear and tear.

      **Source synonyms:** tear and wear

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Physical damage caused to an object due to ordinary use over time; specifically (accounting, law), as justifying a write-down of the object's value in a set of accounts, or exclusion of compensation for the object under a warranty of quality.
   1. The cost payable for such physical damage.

      **Usage:** uncountable

      **Source synonyms:** tear and wear

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Damage or deterioration caused to something intangible due to harsh conditions, the passing of time, etc.; also, the process by which such damage or deterioration is caused.

   **Usage:** figuratively; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. One's ability to endure harsh conditions; resilience, toughness.

   **Usage:** obsolete; uncountable
