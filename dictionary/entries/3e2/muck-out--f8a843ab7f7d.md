### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To clean the excrement and other rubbish from the area where an animal is kept, such as a horse stable or a dog kennel.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Could you muck out Toby's stable, please?

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To financially ruin a gambler; to win against a gambler; to clean out a gambler.

   **Usage:** dated; transitive
