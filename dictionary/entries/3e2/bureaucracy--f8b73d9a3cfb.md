### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Government by bureaus or their administrators or officers.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A system of administration based upon organisation into bureaus, division of labour, a hierarchy of authority, etc., designed to dispose of a large body of work in a routine manner.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; organizational theory

   **Example:** At that time the administration replaced the system of patronage in the civil service with a bureaucracy.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The body of officers and administrators, especially of a government.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The bureaucracy is expanding to meet the needs of the expanding bureaucracy. (apocryphal quip)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Excessive red tape and routine in any administration, body or behaviour.

   **Usage:** countable; derogatory; uncountable

   **Example:** The head of the civil service promised to clamp down on bureaucracy.
