### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any fish of the genus Lophius.

   **Usage:** countable; uncommon; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of several benthic anglerfish, of the family Antennariidae, having a frog-like mouth with a lure.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of the benthic ray-finned fish of the family Batrachoididae (the sole family of order Batrachoidiformes), which are ambush predators and have a toad-like appearance.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
