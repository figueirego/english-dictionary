### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In the French language usage of the letter h at the start of a word which allows liaison with a preceding consonant.

   **Example:** The French word homme ("man") begins with a mute h. Consequently we can say l'homme /l‿ɔm/ and les hommes /le‿zɔm/, but never /lə ɔm/ and /le ɔm/, like we do with the aspirated h.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; phonology; sciences

**Source sense R1.S2**
