### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Under normal conditions or circumstances; usually; most of the time.

   **Example:** Normally, I eat breakfast at 6am, but today, I got up late and didn't eat until 9.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In the expected or customary manner.

   **Example:** Lisa ate normally, until she realised that she was late for choir, when she sped up.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To a usual or customary extent or degree.

   **Example:** He was abnormally agitated, she only normally so.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. In the manner of a variable with a Gaussian distribution.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences; statistics
