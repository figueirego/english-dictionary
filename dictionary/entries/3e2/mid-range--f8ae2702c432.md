### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. The middle portion of a particular range.

   **Example:** the mid-range of frequency

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. A disc usually thrown for distances between fifty and one hundred meters, depending on the player.

   **Usage:** disc golf

   **Example:** She's so strong, she can throw a mid-range whilst the other ones have to throw a driver.

   **Source synonyms:** mid (colloquial)
