### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Promising but empty talk; words without action or intention.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; uncountable

   **Example:** The candidate gave lip service to fixing the problems, but it is doubtful that he will do much.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Cunnilingus (sometimes also referred to as giving lip).

   **Usage:** slang; uncountable; vulgar

   **Example:** Jack gave Samantha lip service.

   **Source synonyms:** brain (vulgar); dome; head (vulgar); mouth music; neck; Kirk
