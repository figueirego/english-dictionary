## Adverb

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. As much as one wishes; to any extent; used to emphasize the preceding verb.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Scream all you want, but it is not going to change the fact that the museum will be closed for the entire month.

   **Example:** He can complain all he wants but it won't fix anything.

   **Source synonyms:** all one likes
