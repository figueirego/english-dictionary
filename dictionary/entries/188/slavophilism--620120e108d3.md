## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. ideological commitment to maintaining traditional Slavic culture and resisting non-Slavic influences (as first formulated in 19th-century imperial Russia).

   **Usage:** uncountable; more specifically
