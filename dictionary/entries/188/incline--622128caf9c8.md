### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To bend or move (something) out of a given plane or direction, often the horizontal or vertical.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He had to incline his body against the gusts to avoid being blown down in the storm.

   **Example:** The people following the coffin inclined their heads in grief.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To slope.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Over the centuries the wind made the walls of the farmhouse incline.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To tend to do or believe something, or move or be moved in a certain direction, away from a point of view, attitude, etc.

   **Usage:** intransitive; chiefly passive voice

   **Example:** He inclines to believe anything he reads in the newspapers.

   **Example:** I'm inclined to give up smoking after hearing of the risks to my health.

   **Source synonyms:** dispose
