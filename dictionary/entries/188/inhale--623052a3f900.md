### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To draw air into the lungs, through the nose or mouth by action of the diaphragm.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source synonyms:** breathe in; inbreathe; inspire

   **Source antonyms:** breathe out; outbreathe; exhale; expire

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To draw air or any form of gas (either in a pure form, or mixed with small particles in the form of aerosols/smoke, sometimes stemming from a medicament) into the lungs, through the nose or mouth by action of the diaphragm.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** breathe in; inbreathe; inspire; fetch

   **Source antonyms:** breathe out; outbreathe; exhale

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To eat very quickly.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

### Related words
