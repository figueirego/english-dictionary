## Noun

### Forms

- travel documents — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An identity document issued by a government or international treaty organization to facilitate the movement of individuals or small groups of persons across international boundaries, such as a passport.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An identity document similar to a passport, but which does not confer proof of citizenship from the issuing country.

   **Source synonyms:** certificate of identity; laissez-passer
