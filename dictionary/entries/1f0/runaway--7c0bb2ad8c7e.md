<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person or animal that runs away or has run away; a person, animal, or organization that escapes captivity or restrictions.

   **Example:** Runaways are vulnerable to criminal exploitation.

**Source sense R1.S2**

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

   **Example:** A trim runaway can make an airplane almost impossible to fly.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** The home side won in a runaway.
