### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of recognizing or the condition of being recognized (matching a current observation with a memory of a prior observation of the same entity).

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** He looked at her for ten full minutes before recognition dawned.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Acceptance as valid or true.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** The law was a recognition of their civil rights.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Official acceptance of the status of a new government by that of another country.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Honour, favourable note, or attention.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** The charity gained plenty of recognition for its efforts, but little money.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The propriety consisting for antibodies to bind to some specific antigens and not to others.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** immunology; medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A return of the feu to the superior.

   **Usage:** historical; uncountable; usually; Scots law
