### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A type of movable bridge over a body of water with one or two spans that can be raised to allow the passage of water traffic whenever necessary; the lifting movement may be either hinged or elevating.

   **Source synonyms:** lift bridge; hoist bridge; bascule bridge
