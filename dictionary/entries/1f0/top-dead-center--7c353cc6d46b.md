## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A position in the cycle of a piston engine wherein the piston is at the topmost point in its stroke. In a four-stroke cycle, TDC occurs twice&#58; at the end of the compression stroke and at the end of the exhaust stroke.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; mechanical; mechanical-engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences
