### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An instrument with which anything is scraped.
   1. An instrument by which the soles of shoes are cleaned from mud by drawing them across it.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An instrument with which anything is scraped.
   1. An instrument drawn by oxen, horses or a tractor, similar to a plow, that is used for scraping up earth in making or repairing roads, digging cellars, building canals, etc.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An instrument with which anything is scraped.
   1. An instrument having two or three sharp sides or edges for cleaning the planks, masts, or decks of a ship.

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An instrument with which anything is scraped.
   1. In the printing press, a board or blade, the edge of which is made to rub over the tympan sheet, thus producing an impression.
