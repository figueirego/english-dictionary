## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈsændˌbæɡ/ — source labels: General-American

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A sturdy sack filled with sand, generally used in large numbers to make defensive walls against flooding, bullets, or shrapnel.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A small bag filled with sand and used as a cudgel.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An engraver's leather cushion, etc.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A deceptive play whereby a player with a strong hand bets weakly or passively.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** card-games; poker

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A bag of illicit drugs.

   **Usage:** slang

## Verb · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈsændˌbæɡ/ — source labels: General-American

### Forms

- sandbags — present; singular; third-person
- sandbagging — participle; present
- sandbagged — participle; past
- sandbagged — past

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To construct a barrier of sandbags (around).

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** We sandbagged the basement windows against the floodwaters.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To strike (someone) with a sandbag or other object to disable or render unconscious.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To conceal or misrepresent one's true position, potential, or intent in order to gain an advantage; (originally poker) to pretend to have a weak hand, as a strategy.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

   **Source synonyms:** downplay

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To blindside; to deceive; to undermine.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To premake dishes (prepare them in advance) (intransitive); to premake (dishes) (transitive).

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive; restaurant kitchen jargon

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To pretend to drink alcohol early on so that, as the night draws on, one can drink everybody else "under the table".
