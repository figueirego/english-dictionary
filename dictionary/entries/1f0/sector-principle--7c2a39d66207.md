## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A principle in international law where international boundaries are extended over sea areas in the Arctic towards the North Pole following meridians (lines of longitude). The same principle is used over land in the Antarctic to the South Pole.

   **Usage:** uncountable
