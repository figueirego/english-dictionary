### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The setting of an automobile's headlights adjusted to brighten a longer distance in front of the car, than when on the normal (low-beam) setting.

   **Usage:** plural-normally

   **Example:** Turn off your high-beams - you're blinding the oncoming traffic.

   **Source synonyms:** full-beam; upper-beam; main beam; brights (plural)

   **Source antonyms:** low-beam; dim light; dipped headlight; dimmed headlights

<a id="r1-s2"></a>
