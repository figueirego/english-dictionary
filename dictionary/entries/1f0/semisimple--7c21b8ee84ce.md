## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In any of several technical senses, decomposable into sub-objects that have a simple structure.
   1. Containing a collection of simple objects such that all objects in the category are direct sums of these simple objects.

      **Usage:** not-comparable; most generally

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** algebra; category-theory; computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

      **Source context (verbatim):** of an abelian category; of an algebraic structure

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In any of several technical senses, decomposable into sub-objects that have a simple structure.
   1. In which each submodule is a direct summand; equivalently, equal to a direct sum of simple submodules.

      **Usage:** not-comparable; module theory

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** algebra; mathematics; sciences

      **Source context (verbatim):** of a module; of an algebraic structure

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. In any of several technical senses, decomposable into sub-objects that have a simple structure.
   1. Semisimple as a module over itself; equivalently, such that all (left) modules are semisimple.

      **Usage:** not-comparable; ring theory

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** algebra; mathematics; sciences

      **Source context (verbatim):** of an algebra or ring; of an algebraic structure

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. In any of several technical senses, decomposable into sub-objects that have a simple structure.
   1. Semisimple as a module over itself; equivalently, such that all (left) modules are semisimple.
      1. Semiprimitive&#58; having trivial Jacobson radical.

         **Usage:** not-comparable; proscribed; ring theory

         **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** algebra; mathematics; sciences

         **Source context (verbatim):** of a ring; of an algebraic structure; of an algebra or ring

         **Source synonyms:** Jacobson semisimple

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. In any of several technical senses, decomposable into sub-objects that have a simple structure.
   1. For which every invariant subspace has an invariant complement, equivalent to the minimal polynomial being squarefree.

      **Usage:** not-comparable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** algebra; linear-algebra; mathematics; sciences

      **Source context (verbatim):** of an operator or matrix; of an algebraic structure

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. In any of several technical senses, decomposable into sub-objects that have a simple structure.
   1. Being a direct sum of simple Lie algebras.

      **Usage:** not-comparable; Lie theory

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** algebra; mathematics; sciences

      **Source context (verbatim):** of a Lie algebra; of an algebraic structure

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. In any of several technical senses, decomposable into sub-objects that have a simple structure.
   1. Being a direct sum of simple representations (also known as irreducible representations).

      **Usage:** not-comparable; representation theory

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** algebra; mathematics; sciences

      **Source context (verbatim):** of a linear representation of a group or algebra; of an algebraic structure

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. In any of several technical senses, decomposable into sub-objects that have a simple structure.
   1. Being a linear algebraic group whose radical of the identity component is trivial.

      **Usage:** not-comparable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** algebra; group-theory; mathematics; sciences

      **Source context (verbatim):** of an algebraic group; of an algebraic structure
