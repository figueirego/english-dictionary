### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To get (someone) out of a difficult or embarrassing situation; to free (someone) from the embarrassment (of a situation); (often reflexive) to relieve (someone of a burden, item of clothing, etc.).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** disburden; disencumber; extricate

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To free (something) from complication.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To disentangle (two things); to distinguish.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive
