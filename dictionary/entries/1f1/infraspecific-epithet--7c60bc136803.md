### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The third word in the scientific name of an infraspecific taxon, following the name of the species. This applies only to formal names of plants and fungi, and not to the formal names of bacteria or animals.

   **Example:** In the name "Cannabis sativa subsp. indica", the word indica is the infraspecific epithet.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences

   **Source synonyms:** subspecific epithet
