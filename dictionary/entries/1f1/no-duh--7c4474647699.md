### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An expression or exclamation that something is exceedingly obvious or apparent, or that one would have to be extremely foolish not to realize something immediately.

   **Usage:** US; colloquial; sarcastic

   **Example:** It says here not to drive with the sun shade covering the windshield of your car. No duh!
