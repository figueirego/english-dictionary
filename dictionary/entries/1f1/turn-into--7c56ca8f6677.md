### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To become.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** all things turn into dust

   **Example:** Iron turns into rust in the presence of air and water.

   **Source synonyms:** turn to; convert; change; transform; get (intransitive); turn; go; come; fall; grow; wax; make; make into

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To cause (something or someone) to become.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He turned water into wine.

   **Source synonyms:** turn to; convert; change; transform; make (transitive); make into
