## Noun

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈhɛɹˌlaɪn/ — source labels: US

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The line along one's forehead where hair starts growing.

   **Example:** It's just a receding hairline — it's not like you're going bald.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A very thin line in writing, drawing, or typography.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A very thin fissure.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** While inspecting the new house, we found a few hairlines in one of the walls.

   **Example:** There was a hairline crack in the airplane's wing.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A fishing line made from hair.
