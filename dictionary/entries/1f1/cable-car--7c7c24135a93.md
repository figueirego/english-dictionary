### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A streetcar moved by gripping to rotating loop of cable running along the track.

   **Usage:** Philippines; US

   **Source synonyms:** cable tram; cable tramway

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any passenger vehicle attached to a moving cable for propulsion, and guided by some form of rails, on a specially-built track not in a street.

   **Source synonyms:** people mover; cable tramway

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A hanging carriage or car for transporting people or cargo.

   **Source synonyms:** tram; aerial tram; ropeway; gondola; gondola lift; telpher; cable tramway
