### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who attends; one who works with or watches over someone or something.

   **Example:** Give your keys to the parking attendants and they will park your car for you.

   **Source synonyms:** attender

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A servant or valet.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A visitor or caller; one who attends (is present at or regularly goes to) some event or place.

   **Usage:** archaic

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. That which accompanies or follows.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**
