### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Able to agree; possible to be agreed.

   **Example:** an agreeable contract

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Pleasant to the senses or the mind; pleasing, satisfying, palatable.

   **Example:** a man with agreeable manners

   **Example:** not completely agreeable remarks

   **Example:** She’s quite an agreeable person.

   **Example:** This fruit has an agreeable taste.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Willing; ready to agree or consent.

   **Usage:** dated

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Agreeing or suitable; followed by to, or rarely by with.

   **Source synonyms:** conformable; correspondent; concordant

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Pursuant, conformant, accordant.

   **Usage:** adverbial

   **Example:** Agreeable to the order of the day, the House took up the report.

   **Source synonyms:** sympathetic
