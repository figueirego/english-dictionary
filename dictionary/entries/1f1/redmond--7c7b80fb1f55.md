### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A surname from Irish \[in turn originating as a patronymic\], an anglicization of Mac Réamoinn (“son of Raymond”).

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A male given name transferred from the surname.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A city in Washington; named for early settler Luke McRedmond.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A city in Oregon; named for early settler Frank T. Redmond.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A town in Utah; apparently named for red mounds near the town.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A town in Western Australia; named for Irish nationalist leader John Redmond.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Microsoft, a technology company headquartered in Redmond, Washington.
