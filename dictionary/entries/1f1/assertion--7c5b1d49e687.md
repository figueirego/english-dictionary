<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of asserting; positive declaration or averment.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Something which is asserted; a declaration; a statement asserted.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** You're a man of strong assertions!

   **Source synonyms:** contention

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A statement or declaration which lacks support or evidence.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
