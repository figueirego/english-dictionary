### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The first meal of the day, usually eaten in the morning.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** You should put more protein in her breakfast so she will grow.

   **Example:** I had an early dinner because the breakfast in this hostel is superb.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A meal consisting of food normally eaten in the morning, which may typically include eggs, sausages, toast, bacon, etc.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** We serve breakfast all day.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The celebratory meal served after a wedding (and occasionally after other solemnities e.g. a funeral).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A meal eaten after a period of (now often religious) fasting.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; largely obsolete outside religion
