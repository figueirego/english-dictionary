### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To secure the release of an arrested person by providing bail money.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To remove water from a boat by scooping it out.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To rescue, especially financially.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** Once again, the industry got itself in trouble and government had to bail it out.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To exit an aircraft while in flight.

   **Usage:** intransitive; usually; with-of

   **Example:** Make sure your parachute harness is securely fastened before you bail out!

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To leave (or not attend at all) a place or a situation, especially quickly or when the situation has become undesirable; to abandon (something).

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive; slang

   **Example:** I'm going to bail out of class today.

   **Example:** She's bailing out on the project.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To abandon (someone) when that person faces difficulties.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive; slang; with-on

   **Example:** When I got arrested, she bailed out on me.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To sell all or part of one's holdings in stocks, real estate, a business, etc.

   **Usage:** colloquial; idiomatic; intransitive; with-of

   **Example:** I'm going to bail out of stocks and buy gold instead.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To make an unscheduled voluntary termination of an underwater dive, usually implying the use of an alternative breathing gas supply.

   **Usage:** intransitive; with-of

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To fail badly; to fall off a skateboard.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; skateboarding; sports

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. To abandon one's surfboard when faced with a large or perilous wave.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports; surfing
