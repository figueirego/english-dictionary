### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A dressing made from vegetable oil, raw egg yolks, vinegar or lemon juice, and seasoning, used on salads, with French fries, in sandwiches, etc.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any cold dish with that dressing as an ingredient.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** We served a lobster mayonnaise as a starter.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any cream, for example for moisturizing the face or conditioning the hair, for which the base is egg yolks and oil.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** hair mayonnaise

   **Example:** facial mayonnaise

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Exaggeration.

   **Usage:** Australia; countable; informal; uncountable
