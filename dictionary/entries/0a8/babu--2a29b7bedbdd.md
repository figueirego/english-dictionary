### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A Hindu title of respect, equivalent to Mr., usually appended to the surname of a Hindu man.

   **Usage:** Hinglish; India; North

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Originally, a Hindu gentleman employed to work as a clerk for the colonial administration; now, a clerk or low-ranking government official.

   **Usage:** India; historical

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. an archetypical Indian bureaucrat, perceived as being corrupt, lazy or arrogant.

   **Usage:** India; derogatory; chiefly social media
