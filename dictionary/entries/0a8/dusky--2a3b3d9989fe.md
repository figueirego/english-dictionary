### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Dimly lit, as at dusk (evening).

   **Example:** I like it when it is dusky, just before the street lights come on.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Having a shade of color that is rather dark.

   **Example:** The dusky rose was of a muted color, not clashing with any of the other colors.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Dark-skinned.

   **Usage:** dated; literary

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Ashen; having a greyish skin coloration.

   **Example:** The patient was in shock and had a dusky skin tone.
