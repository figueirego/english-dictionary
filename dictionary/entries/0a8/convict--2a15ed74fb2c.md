<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

   **Source synonyms:** assigned servant; con; government man; public servant (historical)

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

   **Source synonyms:** penal colonist

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. The convict cichlid (Amatitlania nigrofasciata), also known as the zebra cichlid, a popular aquarium fish, with stripes that resemble a prison uniform.
