### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The point through which the resultant of all buoyant forces on an immersed hull are assumed to act; the centre of mass of the displaced water.

   **Usage:** Canada; UK

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport
