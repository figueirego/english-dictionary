### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make known in a formal manner; to proclaim; to announce; to declare.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** herald; make known; promulgate; report; sound

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To criticize or speak out against (someone or something); to point out as deserving of reprehension, etc.; to openly accuse or condemn in a threatening manner; to invoke censure upon; to stigmatize; to blame.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** attack; charge; condemn; criticize; damn; decry; discredit; inveigh against; proscribe; accuse; atwite (obsolete); blame; bumble (obsolete); challenge (obsolete); cop out (idiomatic; slang); decry; defame (historical); denounce; fault; finger; inculpate; lay at someone's door (idiomatic); lay on; lay something at the feet of (idiomatic); patronize (obsolete); point the bone (Australia; idiomatic); put it to; put the blame on; shend (archaic); twit; wite (Scotland); admonish; animadvert; bash; bawl out; tilt; clobber (informal); cannonade; slash; lash; blame; scorch (informal); blast; berate; berisp (obsolete); castigate; censure; charge; chastise; chew out; chide; condemn; criticize; denounce; denunciate; deprecate; derogate; dispraise; drag (slang); dress down; eat someone alive; excoriate; find fault; fulminate; get at; get down; get up (Australia; Britain); give somebody a piece of one's mind; give somebody what-for; inveigh; lace into; lambaste; lay into; lecture; light into; objurgate; pooh-pooh; put someone on blast (slang); rate; read somebody the riot act; rebuke; reprehend; reprimand; reproach; reprove; rip into; rubbish (Australia; Britain); savage; scold; skewer (figuratively); slam; slate; tear into; tell off; threap; tick off (Britain); tosh (Britain); keelhaul; upbraid; vilify; vituperate

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To make a formal or public accusation against; to inform against; to accuse.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to denounce a confederate in crime

   **Example:** to denounce someone to the authorities

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To proclaim in a threatening manner; to threaten by some outward sign or expression; make a menace of.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

   **Example:** to denounce war; to denounce punishment

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To announce the termination of; especially a treaty or armistice.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To claim the right of working a mine that is abandoned or insufficiently worked.

   **Usage:** US; historical
