### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A planet with a moon large enough to be considered a planet, were the planet not there, having a substantial portion of the system's overall mass.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astronomy; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A pair of planemos in orbit around each other, where the barycenter of their orbits lies outside of both planemos, which in turn is in orbit about a star.
