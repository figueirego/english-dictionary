### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Unfairly discriminatory against someone based on their age.

   **Example:** Upon hearing that his employer would soon require yearly physical examinations and vision screening, my father exclaimed that the policy was ageist.
