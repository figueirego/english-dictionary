### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A thick, sweet syrup drained from sugarcane, especially (Canada, US) the still thicker and sweeter syrup produced by boiling down raw molasses.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any similarly thick and sweet syrup produced by boiling down fruit juices, tree saps, etc., especially concentrated maple syrup.

   **Usage:** US; uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Anything considered figuratively sweet, especially sweet words.

   **Usage:** figuratively; uncountable; usually

   **Example:** He really poured on the molasses, charming his audience and changing more than a few votes.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Something which moves or works extremely slowly.

   **Usage:** figuratively; uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. plural of molass&#58; whiskey made from molasses.

   **Usage:** Scotland; form-of; obsolete; plural; uncountable; usually

   **Form of:** [molass](../2de/molass--b7baab1cfec9.md)

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Synonym of molass&#58; whiskey made from molasses.

   **Usage:** Scotland; obsolete; rare; uncountable; usually

   **Source synonyms:** [molass](../2de/molass--b7baab1cfec9.md) (synonym; synonym-of)
