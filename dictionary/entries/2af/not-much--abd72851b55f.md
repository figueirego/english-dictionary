## Phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see not, much.

   **Example:** There's not much meat in this chicken curry.

   **Example:** This manager is not much better than the last one.

   **Example:** "What have you been up to?" — "Oh, not much."

   **Example:** The house is not much to look at, but it's very comfortable.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used sarcastically with opposite meaning to express strong agreement or assertion of truth; a lot.

   **Example:** "She's rich, is she?" — "Huh, not much!" \[meaning yes, she is very rich\]
