### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An organic acid of the tartaric acid series, distinct from lithofellic acid, but, like it, obtained from certain bile products, such as bezoar stones.

   **Usage:** obsolete; uncountable; rganic compound
