### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To perceive things as different, or to perceive their difference itself; to differentiate, distinguish, discriminate.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** They are identical twins, and if they dress the same, everybody has trouble telling them apart.

   **Example:** Can you tell me (apart) from my twin brother?
