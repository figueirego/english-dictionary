### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To retract or withdraw (an earlier statement).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** No, you are not fat; I take it all back.

   **Source synonyms:** recant; unsay; abjure; backpedal; disavow; disown; eat one's words (figuratively); recall; recant; retract; revoke; swallow; take back; unsay; unspeak (rare); walk back; withcall (rare); withdraw

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To cause (someone) to remember some past event or time.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** That tune takes me back to my childhood.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To resume a relationship with (someone).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** She has forgiven him, and taken him back.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To regain possession of (something).
   1. Physically so.

      **Usage:** transitive

      **Example:** The wedding is off, and he has taken back the ring.

      **Source synonyms:** reclaim

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To regain possession of (something).
   1. Culturally so.

      **Usage:** transitive

      **Example:** The Queer community took back the word from their disparagers.

      **Source synonyms:** reappropriate; reclaim

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To return (something) to a vendor for a refund.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Take back faulty goods to the shop where you bought them.
