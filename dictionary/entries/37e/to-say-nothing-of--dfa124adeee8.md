**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An apophasis used to mention another important, usually related, point&#58; not taking into account, not to mention, without considering.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** She had already eaten a large lunch, to say nothing of a full cooked breakfast that morning.
