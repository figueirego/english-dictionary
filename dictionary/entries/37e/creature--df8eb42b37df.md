### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A living being, such as an animal, monster, or alien.

   **Example:** insects and other creatures

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An unidentified, mysterious, and often monstrous animal or being.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A human.

   **Usage:** derogatory; sometimes

   **Example:** He's a creature of habit.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

   **Usage:** uncommon
