## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The length of time that a thing or person remains in a given state.
   1. The period of time that a system or element of a system remains in a given state.

      **Usage:** uncountable; usually

      **Example:** The drone has a dwell time of about six hours when operating from an in-theater airfield.

      **Example:** The part needs a total dwell time in the autoclave of 95 minutes.

      **Example:** The twist drill needs a total dwell time of 500 milliseconds at the bottom of the hole.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

**Source sense R1.S2**
