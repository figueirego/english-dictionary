### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The outermost celestial sphere of the heavens in Ptolemaic astronomy, which was believed to cause all the inner spheres to rotate.

   **Usage:** historical

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astronomy; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The prime mover or first cause (“an initial cause from which all other causes and effects follow”).

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; lifestyle; philosophy; religion; sciences; theology

   **Source synonyms:** primus motor

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The person or thing that is the main impetus for some action; a driving force.

   **Usage:** broadly
