## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having some regular characteristics.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. That is semiregular with regular faces of precisely two types that alternate around each vertex.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a polyhedron or tessellation

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Such that 1 − r is a unit (has a multiplicative inverse).

   **Usage:** not-comparable; ring theory

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

   **Source context (verbatim):** of an element r of a ring

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Having certain properties in common with holomorphic functions of a single complex variable.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematical-analysis; mathematics; sciences

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a mapping from a multi-dimensional space or manifold to an equivalent space

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. That is the result of a required adjustment of an induced representation that would, unadjusted, give rise to (only) a quasi-invariant measure.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; representation theory; topological algebra; representation theory; topological algebra

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a representation
