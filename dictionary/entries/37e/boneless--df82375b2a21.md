### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Without bones, especially as pertaining to meat or poultry prepared for eating.

   **Example:** boneless chicken

   **Example:** boneless, skinless chicken

   **Example:** boneless wings

   **Source synonyms:** boned; deboned

   **Source antonyms:** unboned; bone-in
