## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A kitchen utensil with a handle and a head appropriate for drizzling a viscous liquid, such as honey.

   **Example:** I used a honey drizzler to add honey to the mixture.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any pouring device intended to allow liquids to be drizzled.
