### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A monetary system where the value of circulating money is linked to the value of gold.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** economics; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The highest or most reliable benchmark against which all others in a category are judged or measured.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** The OED is the gold standard for English dictionaries.
