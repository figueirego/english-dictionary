### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To push or throw over.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The massed crowds toppled the statue of the former dictator.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To overturn.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To overthrow something.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** to topple one's rival

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To totter and fall, or to lean as if about to do so.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The pile of pennies began to topple.
