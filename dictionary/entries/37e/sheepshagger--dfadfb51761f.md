### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A man who engages in sexual intercourse with sheep; usually used as a slur for a rural person seen as unsophisticated.

   **Usage:** UK; slang

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A term of abuse for inhabitants of various countries or regions which have large populations of sheep.
   1. A person from Wales.

      **Usage:** UK; derogatory; slang

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A term of abuse for inhabitants of various countries or regions which have large populations of sheep.

      **Usage:** UK; derogatory; slang

<a id="r1-s4"></a>
