### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To move (something) in regular procession through a system.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** develop; progress

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To change or transform (something).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Over several years the author evolved the story originally drafted as a novella into a real epic.

   **Source synonyms:** develop; convert; develop; draw (figuratively; obsolete); evolve; metamorphize; metamorphose; reconvert; shift (figuratively); transfigurate; transfigure; transform; transmogrify; transmute; transume (archaic)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To cause (something) to come into being or develop.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** contrive; generate; bring forth; cause; contrive; create; ferment; forge; develop; engender; evolve; generate; gin up (North-America); innate; form; forthbring (obsolete); make; produce; spawn

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To acquire or develop (a trait) in the process of biological evolution.

   **Usage:** transitive; usually; a species

   **Example:** How long ago did birds evolve beaks?

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a population

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To change genetic composition over successive generations through the process of evolution.

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive; usually; a species

   **Example:** A hundred thousand years from now, will Homo sapiens have evolved into beings unrecognizable to their ancestors?

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a population

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To give off (a gas such as carbon dioxide or oxygen) during a chemical reaction.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to evolve odours

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To wind or unwind (something).

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To move in regular procession through a system.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To change, to transform.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** What began as a few lines of code has now evolved into a million-line behemoth.

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. Of a trait; to develop within a population through biological evolution.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** How long ago did beaks evolve?

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences
