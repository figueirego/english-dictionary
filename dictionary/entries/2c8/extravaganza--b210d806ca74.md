### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An extravagant or eccentric piece of music, literature, or drama, originally associated with Victorian England.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An instance of fantastical, unrestrained, lavish, or chaotic behaviour or conduct.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** The bride had her heart set on a simple wedding, but her parents insisted on a three-hundred-guest extravaganza.
