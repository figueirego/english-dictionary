### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The practice of intentionally killing a human being or animal in a humane way, especially in order to end suffering.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** Euthanasia is the most difficult part of a veterinarian's job.

   **Source synonyms:** mercy killing

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The practice of killing a human being who is considered a liability to society, especially a disabled person.

   **Usage:** Nazism; especially; euphemistic; uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An easy death, or the means to bring about such a death.

   **Usage:** obsolete; uncountable; usually
