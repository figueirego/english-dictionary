### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An aside, an act of straying from the main subject in speech or writing.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The lectures included lengthy digressions on topics ranging from the professor's dog to the meaning of life.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The act of straying from the main subject in speech or writing, (rhetoric) particularly for rhetorical effect.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; generally uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A deviancy, a sin or error, an act of straying from the path of righteousness or a general rule.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A deviation, an act of straying from a path.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An elongation, a deflection or deviation from a mean position or expected path.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astronomy; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics
