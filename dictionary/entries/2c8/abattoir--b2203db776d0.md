### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A public slaughterhouse for cattle, sheep, etc.

   **Example:** Once the cows reach maturity, they're sent to the abattoir.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A place or event of great carnage or bloodshed.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** The army's raid on the enemy turned into a major abattoir.

   **Source synonyms:** bloodbath
