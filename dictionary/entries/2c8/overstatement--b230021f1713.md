## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An exaggeration; a statement in excess of what is reasonable.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The story he gave was something of an overstatement of the facts.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The tendency to overstate.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** She had a knack for overstatement.
