### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A sport involving the performance of sequences of movements requiring physical strength, flexibility, and kinesthetic awareness.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Gymnastics was a significant part of the physical education curriculum.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Complex intellectual or artistic exercises or feats of physical agility.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** His mental gymnastics are legendary.
