### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An opening, possibly covered by a grate or grill, possibly ducted, that allows air to circulate.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A device or valve that allows to air to be evacuated from a radiator or pipe in a heating system.

   **Source synonyms:** bleed valve; bleed nipple
