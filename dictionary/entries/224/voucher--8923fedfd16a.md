### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A piece of paper or other token that entitles the holder to a discount, or that can be exchanged for goods and services.

   **Example:** a voucher for a free kettle

   **Example:** This voucher entitles the bearer to a 20% discount on frozen goods.

   **Source synonyms:** coupon

**Source sense R1.S2**
