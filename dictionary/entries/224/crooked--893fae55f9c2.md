### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Not straight; having one or more bends or angles.

   **Example:** We walked up the crooked path to the top of the hill.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Set at an angle; not vertical or square.

   **Example:** That picture is crooked - could you straighten it up for me?

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. Dishonest or illegal; corrupt.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** He was trying to interest me in another one of his crooked deals.

   **Example:** He's so crooked that everyone avoids interacting with him as much as they can help it.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. Ill-tempered; grumpy; cranky.

   **Usage:** Newfoundland; dialectal

   **Example:** I wouldn't try to talk to him just now, b'y! He's some crooked today!
