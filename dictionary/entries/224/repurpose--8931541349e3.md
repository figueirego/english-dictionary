### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To reuse for a different purpose, on a long-term basis, with or without alteration.

   **Example:** The town common was repurposed as a practice field.

   **Example:** The church was repurposed as a nightclub by lighting changes and removing the pews, but it never opened.
