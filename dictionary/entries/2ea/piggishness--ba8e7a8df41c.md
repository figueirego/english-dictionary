### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The quality of being like a pig in one's character; greediness, boorishness, or obstinacy.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** The man was a glutton; his piggishness was legendary.

   **Example:** The old man's stubborn piggishness kept him from acknowledging that his wife was a better driver than he, even after his license was revoked.
