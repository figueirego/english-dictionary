### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Not allowing or affording rest.

   **Example:** The night before his wedding was a restless one.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Without rest; unable to be still or quiet; uneasy; continually moving.

   **Example:** He was a restless child.

   **Example:** She sat, restless and nervous, and tried to concentrate.

   **Source synonyms:** antsy

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Not satisfied to be at rest or in peace; averse to repose; eager for change; discontented.

   **Example:** A restless ambition.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Deprived of rest or sleep.

   **Example:** They remained restless, sitting by the window the entire night.
