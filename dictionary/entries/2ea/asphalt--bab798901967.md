### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A sticky, black to brown and highly viscous liquid or semi-solid, composed almost entirely of bitumen with small mineral particles, that is present in most crude petroleums and in some natural deposits.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The mining of asphalt was big business in this area in the early twentieth century.

   **Source synonyms:** bitumen

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Ellipsis of asphalt concrete, a hard ground covering used for roads and walkways.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; countable; ellipsis; uncountable

   **Example:** Please hose off the asphalt near the top of the driveway if the trucks get lots of mud on it.

   **Source synonyms:** tarmac; tarmacadam; blacktop; bitumen

   **Alternative form of:** [asphalt concrete](../156/asphalt-concrete--5597251e14c3.md)
