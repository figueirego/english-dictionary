### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To develop an amicable relationship (with), especially in a sudden manner during an initial encounter.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** I really hit it off with Jack, and hired him without needing an interview.

   **Example:** Those two are really hitting it off. I wouldn't be surprised if they started dating soon.
