### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Being a commutative reduced ring in which, whenever x, y satisfy x³=y², there is s with s²=x and s³=y.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** algebra; mathematics; sciences

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a ring

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of a subgroup A of a group G, having a subgroup B such that AB = G, and for any proper subgroup C of B, AC is a proper subgroup of G.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** group-theory; mathematics; sciences
