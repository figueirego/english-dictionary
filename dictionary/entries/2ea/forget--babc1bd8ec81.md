### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To lose remembrance of.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I have forgotten most of the things I learned in school.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To unintentionally not do, neglect.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I forgot to buy flowers for my wife at our 14th wedding anniversary.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To unintentionally leave something behind.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I forgot my car keys in the living room.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To cease remembering.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Let's just forget I ever told you anything about it.

   **Example:** He forgot having already visited this city.

   **Source synonyms:** leave behind

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To not realize something (regardless of whether one has ever known it).

   **Usage:** broadly; informal; transitive

   **Example:** People forget how much work goes into what we do.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Euphemism for fuck, screw (a mild oath).

   **Usage:** slang

   **Example:** Forget this, I'm done!

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** obliviate; overlook; pass over; disremember; forget
