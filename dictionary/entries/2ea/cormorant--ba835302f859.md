### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of various medium-large black seabirds of the family Phalacrocoracidae which dive into water for fish and other aquatic animals, found throughout the world except for islands in the centre of the Pacific Ocean; specifically, the great cormorant (Phalacrocorax carbo).

   **Source synonyms:** corvorant (obsolete); sea crow (UK; dialectal); sea raven

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A voracious eater; also, a person who, or thing which, is aggressively greedy for wealth, etc.

   **Usage:** also; archaic; attributive; figuratively

   **Source synonyms:** glutton; animal; belly-god (obsolete); big back; buzgut (UK; archaic); cormorant; dog; epicure; gannet; glutton; gorger; gourmand; gormandizer; greedyguts; guttle (Britain; archaic; dialectal); hog; Homer Simpson; helluo; pig; slow belly; trencherman
