**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of several legendary heroes in various Polynesian mythologies, notably associated with canoe building; in the context of comparative mythology, these figures are frequently identified with each other, resulting in as few as one or two distinct identities.

   **Usage:** Polynesian mythology

   **Source synonyms:** 'Aka; Laka; Lasa; Raka
