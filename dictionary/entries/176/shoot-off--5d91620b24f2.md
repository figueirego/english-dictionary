### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To leave quickly.

   **Usage:** colloquial

   **Example:** I have to shoot off&#58; my interview starts in under an hour.

   **Source synonyms:** shoot

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To do (something, usually a written or verbal communication) quickly, and without hesitation or forethought.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To overshoot the bounds of a set when filming, showing part of what lies outside it.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** broadcasting; media; television
