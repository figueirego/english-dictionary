### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A greaser or biker.

   **Usage:** UK; West-Midlands; countable; dated; slang

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A member of an alternative subculture, typically one based on rock music, as opposed to a chav or townie.

   **Usage:** UK; West-Midlands; countable; slang

   **Source synonyms:** emo; goth; greeber; mosher; rocker

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A member of a United Kingdom subculture of the late 1980s and early 1990s, musically affiliated with garage rock and electronica, typically wearing baggy clothes and hair in high ponytails.

   **Usage:** UK; countable; slang

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A short-lived subgenre of alternative rock music in the late 1980s and early 1990s, with influences from punk rock, electronic dance music, hip-hop, and psychedelia.

   **Usage:** uncountable
