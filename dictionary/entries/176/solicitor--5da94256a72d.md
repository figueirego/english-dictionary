### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who solicits
   1. A lawyer offering most legal services to clients apart from representation in court, for which the lawyer typically solicits and instructs a barrister instead, although rights of audience vary by jurisdiction.

      **Example:** Traditional solicitors are still found in the United Kingdom, Ireland, New Zealand, India, Hong Kong, Singapore, and parts of Australia.

      **Source synonyms:** transactional attorney

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One who solicits
   1. A lawyer now performing both the role of a traditional solicitor and that of a barrister.

      **Usage:** Australia; Canada

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. One who solicits
   1. The chief legal officer of a city, town, or similar jurisdiction in some American states.

      **Usage:** US

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. One who solicits
   1. A customer, a requester of goods and services, especially johns, the clients of prostitutes.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. One who solicits
   1. A door-to-door salesperson, a person soliciting sales by visiting others' houses.

      **Usage:** Canada; US

      **Example:** Did you see the sign that says 'No Solicitors'?

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. One who solicits
   1. An agent or proxy in other contexts, especially a petitioner, an intercessor, a go-between.

      **Usage:** archaic

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. One who solicits
   1. A catalyst, a thing prompting some action or behavior.

      **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. One who solicits
   1. A tempter, an inciter.

      **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. One who solicits
   1. A military recruiter.

      **Usage:** obsolete; rare
