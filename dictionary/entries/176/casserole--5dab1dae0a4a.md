### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A dish of glass or earthenware, with a lid, in which food is baked and sometimes served.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Food, such as a stew, cooked in such a dish.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** a chicken casserole

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any type of food that fills the high-walled dish or pan in which it was cooked.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; uncountable

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** casserole dish — glass or earthenware dish; hotpot (UK) — food cooked in such a dish; hotdish — both senses
