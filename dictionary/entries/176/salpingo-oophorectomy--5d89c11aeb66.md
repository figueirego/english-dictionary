### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A surgical procedure in which one or both fallopian tubes and ovaries are removed, typically performed to treat or prevent conditions such as tubal or ovarian cancer, severe infection, or other diseases affecting the adnexal structures of the female reproductive system.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** bilateral salpingo-oophorectomy
