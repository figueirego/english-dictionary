### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of limiting or the state of being limited.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A restriction; a boundary, real or metaphorical, caused by some thing or some circumstance.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Getting into his wheelchair after his amputation, it felt like a limitation you could roll in.

   **Example:** He understood the exam material, but his fear was a limitation he could not overcome.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An imperfection or shortcoming that limits something's use or value.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A time period after which some legal action may no longer be brought.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The lawyer obtained impunity by dragging his obviously guilty client's case beyond the ten-year limitation.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law
