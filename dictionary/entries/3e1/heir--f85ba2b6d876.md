### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Someone who inherits, or is designated to inherit, the property of another.

   **Example:** My brother is the heir to our childhood house and yet has no interest in it.

   **Source synonyms:** beneficiary; inheritor

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One who inherits, or has been designated to inherit, a hereditary title or office.

   **Example:** As the heir to the British throne, the Prince of Wales is a very public figure.

   **Source synonyms:** inheritor

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A successor in a role, representing continuity with the predecessor.

   **Source synonyms:** aftercomer; heir; succeeder; successor; successour (obsolete)
