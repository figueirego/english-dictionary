### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. to operate (surgically) again (on the same thing as a previous operation).

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** When a problem reoccurs after surgery, it might be necessary to reoperate on a patient.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences; surgery

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. to begin to operate or function again.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** As soon as the spaceship was completely repaired, it started reoperating.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. to bring back (something) into operation.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Antoní and Consuela reoperated the factory's candy machine that had been stored in the storage room for over a decade.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; manufacturing
