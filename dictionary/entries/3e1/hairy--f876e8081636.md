### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having a lot of body hair.

   **Example:** a hairy man

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a person

   **Source synonyms:** hirsute; furry; hairful

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Having a lot of fur.

   **Example:** hairy mammoth

   **Source context (verbatim):** of an animal

   **Source synonyms:** long-haired; furry; fluffy

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Having hair growing from it.

   **Example:** hairy chest; hairy back

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a human body part other than the head

   **Source synonyms:** villous; hirsute; furry

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Long-haired.

   **Usage:** uncommon

   **Example:** yelling about dirty, hairy hippies

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Seeming as if hair-covered or as if consisting of hair.

   **Example:** hairy cell leukemia

   **Example:** hairy moss

   **Source synonyms:** hairlike

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Difficult, complex, intricate, or intimidating.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** It’s a hairy problem, and will probably take several weeks to sort out.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Causing anxiety or fright; terrifying, scary.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Source synonyms:** hair-raising
