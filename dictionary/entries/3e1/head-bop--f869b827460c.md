**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A rhythmic, repetitive movement of the head up and down or side to side, usually done automatically or intentionally in time with a catchy musical beat.
   1. A simple, casual dance move or part of a viral internet dance trend.

      **Usage:** colloquial; informal
