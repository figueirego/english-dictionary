### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A value associated with a metric space, being one less than the Helly number of the family of metric balls in that space.

   **Example:** Helly's theorem implies that the Helly dimension of a Euclidean space equals its dimension as a real vector space.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** combinatorics; mathematics; sciences
