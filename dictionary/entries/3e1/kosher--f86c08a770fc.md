### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Fit for use or consumption, in accordance with Jewish law (especially relating to food).

   **Usage:** Judaism

   **Example:** Only in New York can you find a good, kosher hamburger!

   **Example:** David's mother kept a kosher kitchen, with separate sets of dishes for meat and for dairy.

   **Example:** In order for a suit to be kosher, it cannot contain both wool and linen together.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Observant of the rules of kashrut (of a person or establishment).

   **Example:** "Are you kosher?" "No, I love to eat scallops."

   **Example:** "Is this restaurant kosher?" "No, we serve pork."

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. In accordance with standards or usual practice.

   **Usage:** broadly; figuratively

   **Example:** Is what I have done kosher with Mr. Smith?

   **Source synonyms:** by the book; proper; rightful; halal (figuratively); homodox (uncommon); kosher (figuratively); orthodox; by the book; allowed; conventional; customary; natural; official; proper; routine; traditional

### Related words

**Source antonyms:** nonkosher; possul — the state of being prohibited from consumption; treyf — food that is prohibited; unkosher
