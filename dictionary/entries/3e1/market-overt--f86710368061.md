## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A rule in English law that overrode the general rule that the purported sale of stolen goods would not transfer ownership, if the goods were openly sold at a designated market between sunrise and sunset.

   **Usage:** England; Wales; historical; uncountable
