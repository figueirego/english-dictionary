### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see black, beetle.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A black scarab beetle, later specifically the African black beetle (Heteronychus arator), a serious pest of grasses in New Zealand.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The oriental cockroach (Blatta orientalis), a large roach from the family Blattidae.

   **Usage:** UK

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A similar roach native to New Zealand, of species Maoriblatta novaeseelandiae (syn. Platyzosteria novaeseelandia); the Māori bug.

   **Usage:** New-Zealand

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Any of various other species of beetle that are black in color, including&#58;
   1. An Asiatic rhinoceros beetle (Oryctes rhinoceros), a pest of palm trees in tropical Asia.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Any of various other species of beetle that are black in color, including&#58;
   1. A member of the European species Pterostichus nigrita (syn. Feronia nigrita).
