### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To be immobilized by water too shallow to allow it to float.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The ship ran aground and had to be towed back out to sea.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a vessel

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To cause (a vessel) to be immobilized by water too shallow to allow it to float.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The drunken helmsman ran the ship aground.
