### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To consider it appropriate or worthy to do or give (something), often when it is seen as beneath one's dignity; to condescend, to vouchsafe.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source context (verbatim):** in the negative

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To consider it appropriate or worthy to accept or take (something).

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

   **Source antonyms:** disdain

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Often followed by of&#58; to consider (someone) as worthy of something; to dignify.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To consider it appropriate or worthy to do something, often when it is seen as beneath one's dignity; to condescend, to think fit, to vouchsafe.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** He didn’t even deign to give us a nod of the head; he thought us that far beneath him.
