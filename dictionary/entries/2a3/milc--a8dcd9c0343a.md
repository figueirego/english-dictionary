**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Abbreviation of mirrorless interchangeable lens camera. A digital camera similar to a DSLR, with a lens mount that can change out and accept different lenses, without the reflexive mirror mechanism of SLRs.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of
