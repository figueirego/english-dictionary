### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A warbler, of the species Acrocephalus scirpaceus in the family Acrocephalidae, native to Eurasia and Africa.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of various similar Old World birds of the same genus, especially great reed warbler (A. arundinaceus) and clamorous reed warbler (A. stentoreus).
