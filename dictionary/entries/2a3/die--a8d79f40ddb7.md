### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To stop living; to become dead; to undergo death.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To stop living; to become dead; to undergo death.
   1. followed by of as an indication of direct cause; general use

      **Usage:** intransitive

      **Example:** He died of malaria.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To stop living; to become dead; to undergo death.
   1. followed by from as an indication of direct cause; general use, though somewhat more common in the context of medicine or the sciences

      **Usage:** intransitive

      **Example:** He died from heart failure.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To stop living; to become dead; to undergo death.
   1. followed by for; often expressing wider contextual motivations, though sometimes indicating direct causes

      **Usage:** intransitive

      **Example:** He died for the one he loved.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To stop living; to become dead; to undergo death.
   1. followed by with as an indication of direct cause

      **Usage:** archaic; intransitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To stop living; to become dead; to undergo death.
   1. followed by to as an indication of direct cause (like from)

      **Usage:** intransitive; proscribed; sometimes

      **Example:** I can't believe I just died to a turret!

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To stop living; to become dead; to undergo death.
   1. followed by with as an indication of manner

      **Usage:** intransitive; still current

      **Example:** She died with dignity.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To stop living; to become dead; to undergo death.
   1. to die in a certain form.

      **Usage:** intransitive

      **Example:** Will I die a happy man?

      **Source context (verbatim):** in bare form

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To (stop living and) undergo (a specified death).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He died a hero's death.

   **Example:** They died a thousand deaths.

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. To lose or be eliminated from a game, particularly with a deathlike animation.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Example:** Whenever my brother dies, he ragequits.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** video-games

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. To yearn intensely.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

   **Example:** I'm really dying to eat in that new restaurant.

   **Example:** I'm dying for a piss.

<a id="r1-s12"></a>

**Source sense R1.S12**

12. To be or become hated or utterly ignored or cut off, as if dead.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive; uncommon

   **Example:** The day our sister eloped, she died to our mother.

<a id="r1-s13"></a>

**Source sense R1.S13**

13. To become spiritually dead; to lose hope.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

   **Example:** He died a little inside each time she refused to speak to him.

<a id="r1-s14"></a>

**Source sense R1.S14**

14. To be mortified or shocked by a situation.

   **Usage:** colloquial; excessive; intransitive

   **Example:** If anyone sees me wearing this ridiculous outfit, I'll die.

<a id="r1-s15"></a>

**Source sense R1.S15**

15. To be so overcome with emotion or laughter as to be incapacitated.

   **Usage:** excessive; figuratively; intransitive

   **Example:** When I found out my two favorite musicians would be recording an album together, I literally planned my own funeral arrangements and died.

<a id="r1-s16"></a>

**Source sense R1.S16**

16. To stop working; to break down or otherwise lose "vitality".

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** My car died in the middle of the freeway this morning.

   **Example:** Sorry I couldn't call you. My phone died.

   **Example:** My battery died and my charger was at home.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a machine

<a id="r1-s17"></a>

**Source sense R1.S17**

17. To abort, to terminate (as an error condition).

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a computer program

<a id="r1-s18"></a>

**Source sense R1.S18**

18. To expire at the end of the session of a legislature without having been brought to a vote.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The proposed gas tax died after the powerful rural senator refused to let it out of committee.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a legislative bill or resolution

<a id="r1-s19"></a>

**Source sense R1.S19**

19. To perish; to cease to exist; to become lost or extinct.

<a id="r1-s20"></a>

**Source sense R1.S20**

20. To sink; to faint; to pine; to languish, with weakness, discouragement, love, etc.

<a id="r1-s21"></a>

**Source sense R1.S21**

21. To become indifferent; to cease to be subject.

   **Usage:** often

   **Example:** to die to pleasure or to sin

   **Source context (verbatim):** with "to"

<a id="r1-s22"></a>

**Source sense R1.S22**

22. To disappear gradually in another surface, as where mouldings are lost in a sloped or curved face.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** architecture

<a id="r1-s23"></a>

**Source sense R1.S23**

23. To become vapid, flat, or spiritless, as liquor.

<a id="r1-s24"></a>

**Source sense R1.S24**

24. To fail to evoke laughter from the audience.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Example:** Then there was that time I died onstage in Montreal...

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a stand-up comedian or a joke
