### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of or relating to vapour; also, having the characteristics or consistency of vapour.

   **Source synonyms:** halitous (obsolete); halituous (obsolete); vaporiform; vaporlike; vapourlike; vaporose (obsolete); vapory; vapoury; aeriform; aereous; aerial (historical); airy; ethereal; gaseous; pneumatic; vaporlike; vaporous; wafty

   **Source antonyms:** nonvaporous

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Breathing out or giving off vapour.

   **Source synonyms:** vaporescent (rare); vapourescent (rare)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of a place&#58; filled with vapour; foggy, misty.

   **Source synonyms:** fumid; vapored (rare); vapoured (rare); vaporiferous (rare); vaporsome

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Of a thing&#58; covered or hidden by vapour, fog, or mist.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Lacking depth or substance; insubstantial, thoughtless, vague.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Source synonyms:** frothy; vain; vaporsome

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Of clothes or fabric&#58; thin and translucent; filmy, gauzy.

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Feeling melancholy; experiencing the vapors.
