## Verb

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To sketch or mark with pencil, crayon, etc., on a given surface.

   **Usage:** literally

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To appeal to, make a demand of, rely on; to utilize or make use of, as a source.

   **Usage:** also draw upon

   **Example:** Without the proper resources, the young manager drew on his imagination to solve the crisis.

   **Example:** The reporter drew heavily on interviews with former members of the secretive group.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To advance, continue; to move or pass slowly or continuously, as under a pulling force.

   **Example:** As the day draws on, the oxen will begin to show fatigue.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To approach, come nearer, as evening.

   **Example:** Evening is drawing on; we'd better call it a day.

   **Example:** In his bones, he sensed winter was drawing on sooner than usual.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To put on (a garment).

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To draw the trigger of a gun, so as to fire upon (something).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I drew on the pheasant as it rose.
