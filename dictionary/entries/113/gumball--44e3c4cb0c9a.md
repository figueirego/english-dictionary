### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Chewing gum sold as a ball usually coated with colored hard sugar.

   **Example:** Remember those clear globe dispensers with gumballs for a penny?

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A silly, laughable person.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Example:** When he drinks too much he's a gumball.

   **Source synonyms:** goofball

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The flashing lights on top of a police car.

   **Usage:** US; slang

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The spiky seedpod of the sweet gum tree.

   **Source synonyms:** monkey ball
