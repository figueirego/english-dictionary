**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Rustic, pastoral, country-styled.

   **Example:** The countryside was filled with charming, bucolic scenery, complete with rolling hills, fields of wildflowers, and quaint farmhouses.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Relating to the pleasant aspects of rustic country life.

   **Example:** The cozy bed and breakfast was located in a picturesque, bucolic setting, offering guests a chance to escape the city and enjoy the simple pleasures of the countryside.

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Pertaining to herdsmen or peasants.
