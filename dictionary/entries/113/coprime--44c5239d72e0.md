## Adjective

### Forms

- co-prime — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having no positive integer factors in common, aside from 1.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** 24 and 35 are coprime.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; number-theory; sciences

   **Source context (verbatim):** of two or more positive integers

   **Source synonyms:** relatively prime

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Having no positive integer factors, aside from 1, in common with one or more specified other positive integers.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** 24 is coprime to 35.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; number-theory; sciences

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a positive integer

   **Source synonyms:** relatively prime

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Whose greatest common divisor is a nonzero constant (i.e., polynomial of degree 0).

   **Usage:** broadly; not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** algebra; mathematics; sciences

   **Source context (verbatim):** of two or more polynomials

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. (of two or more ideals of a ring) Whose sum is the whole ring; (of two or more elements of a ring) generating the whole ring.

   **Usage:** broadly; not-comparable; ring theory
