### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A large number of insects or other bugs (e.g. centipedes), especially when in motion or (for bees) migrating to a new colony.

   **Usage:** collective

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A mass of people, animals or things in motion or turmoil.

   **Example:** a swarm of meteorites

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A group of nodes sharing the same torrent in a BitTorrent network.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A number of small earthquakes (or other seismic events) occurring, with no clear cause, in a specific area within a relatively short space of time.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; geology; natural-sciences; seismology
