### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The largest positive integer (respectively polynomial, element of a given ring) that is a divisor of each of a given set of integers (respectively polynomials, elements of a given ring).

   **Example:** The greatest common divisor of 66, 30 and 18 is 6.
