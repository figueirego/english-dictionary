## Noun

### Forms

- dry eyes — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An eye disease caused by decreased tear production; short for dry eye syndrome.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An eye which is affected by this disease.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An eye which is not crying, i.e. someone emotionally unmoved.

   **Usage:** countable; idiomatic; uncountable

   **Example:** There wasn't a dry eye in the audience after her heart-wrenching performance.
