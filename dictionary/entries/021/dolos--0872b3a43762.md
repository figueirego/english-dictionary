### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The bones that are thrown when throwing the bones for divination.

   **Usage:** South-Africa; uncommon

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The ankle bones of sheep or goats formerly used by children as playthings.

   **Usage:** South-Africa; uncommon

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Interlocking blocks of concrete, used for protection of seawalls and to preserve beaches from erosion, formerly known as Merryfield blocks.
