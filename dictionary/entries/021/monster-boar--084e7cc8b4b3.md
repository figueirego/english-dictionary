### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A Eurasian wild boar.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A folkloric or exaggerated perception of the Entelodont, a massive, extinct family of omnivorous mammals that roamed Eurasia and North America; their ferocity, immense size, and deadly tusks elevated them to mythological status.

   **Source synonyms:** terminator pig; hell pig
