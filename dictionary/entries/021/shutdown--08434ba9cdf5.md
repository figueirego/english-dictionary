## Noun

### Forms

- shut-down — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The action of stopping operations; a closing, of a computer, business, event, etc.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** You need to enter your password at startup, but it's not required at shutdown.

   **Example:** There is a plastic molly-guard covering the escalator's shutdown button to prevent little kids from pushing it and stopping the escalator.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A statement, insult, etc. that prevents the opponent from replying further.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** When he insulted me, my reply was such a shutdown that he left the room.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An autistic response to stress or sensory overload, in which the individual freezes up and becomes silent, motionless, and unresponsive.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; autism

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; psychology; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A defensive ploy that prevents the opposing players from attacking.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A big success; a hyped atmosphere.

   **Usage:** UK; countable; slang; uncountable
