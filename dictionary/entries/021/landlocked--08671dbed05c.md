### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of a country, region, etc., surrounded by land (having no borders with the sea).

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Switzerland is landlocked and obviously will never be a great sea power and must always trade overland.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Living in freshwater, such as landlocked salmon.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of a property or parcel, surrounded by other property and having no access to a public road.

   **Usage:** US; not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; real-estate
