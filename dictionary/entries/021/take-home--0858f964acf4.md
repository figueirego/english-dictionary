## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

- take-homes — plural
- takehome — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Anything that one is given to bring home from an institutional setting.
   1. An examination or assignment to be completed outside the classroom.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Anything that one is given to bring home from an institutional setting.
   1. A supply of methadone that someone under treatment is allowed to take home instead of coming in to a treatment center every day.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Ellipsis of take-home pay.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; countable; ellipsis; uncountable

   **Example:** Yes, he grosses more than I do, but my take-home is comparable because of tax differences.

   **Alternative form of:** [take-home pay](../3ad/take-home-pay--eb562b15649c.md)
