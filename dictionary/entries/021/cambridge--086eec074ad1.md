### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A city and local government district with borough status in Cambridgeshire, England, famous for its university.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Ellipsis of University of Cambridge.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; ellipsis

   **Example:** He went to Cambridge, you know.

   **Alternative form of:** University of Cambridge

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A village in Slimbridge parish, Stroud district, Gloucestershire, England, situated on the local River Cam (OS grid ref SO7403).

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A city in Washington County, Idaho, United States.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A village, the county seat of Henry County, Illinois, United States.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

**Source sense R1.S7**
