## Adjective · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Not specially prepared or designated for skiing, hence viewed as more exciting but also more dangerous.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Please stay away from the off-piste slopes.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of snow or ski-runs

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Deviating from what is seen as correct, usual or conventional.

   **Usage:** figuratively; not-comparable

   **Example:** His ideas are totally off-piste.
