### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To keep or preserve for the winter.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** It is best to overwinter tender plants indoors.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To spend the winter (in a particular place).

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Insects may overwinter in fallen fruit if it is not removed.
