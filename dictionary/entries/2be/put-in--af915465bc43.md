### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To place inside.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Just put in the key for the ignition and turn it.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To place inside.
   1. To imprison or place in a prison cell.

      **Usage:** ditransitive; transitive

      **Example:** They put me in the state pen for a year for that.

      **Example:** They put me in with an absolute maniac for a cellmate.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To apply, request, or submit.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** I'm going to the bank to put in for a transfer.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To contribute.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I put in an extra hour at work today.

   **Example:** Despite his success, the comedian liked to put in appearances at some of the smaller venues.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To call at (a place or port), especially as a deviation from an intended journey.

   **Usage:** dated; intransitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To declare or make official.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The defendant has put in a plea of not guilty.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To plant a crop.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** We've put in carrots in the east field.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To make (a telephone call).

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To fill in on a form or questionnaire; to use as an answer on a form or questionnaire.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Put in my name as your emergency contact.

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. To install or deliver.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I'm putting in a new water heater in the spring.

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. To injure the body of (someone).

   **Usage:** slang; transitive; African-American Vernacular

   **Example:** She put six shots in him.

<a id="r1-s12"></a>

**Source sense R1.S12**

   **Usage:** obsolete
