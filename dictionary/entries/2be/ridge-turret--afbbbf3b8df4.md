### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Ellipsis of turret; an architectural element primarily found on rooftops; an often ornamental tower constructed directly onto the ridge or apex where two or more sloping roof planes meet; occasionally functioning as ventilation or to house clocks, bells, or observation platforms.
