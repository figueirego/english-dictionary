### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A preparation of barium sulfate (a radioopaque compound) which the patient ingests for the purpose of producing clear radiographs of the esophagus, stomach, and duodenum.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>
