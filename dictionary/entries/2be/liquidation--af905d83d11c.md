### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act or process of liquidating or of being liquidated.
   1. The act of exchange of an asset of lesser liquidity with a more liquid one, such as cash.
      1. The act or process of winding up the affairs of a company; the selling of the assets of a business as part of the process of dissolving the business.

         **Usage:** countable; uncountable

         **Example:** The store is having a liquidation sale&#58; everything must go as they go out of business.

         **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The act or process of liquidating or of being liquidated.
   1. The act of exchange of an asset of lesser liquidity with a more liquid one, such as cash.
      1. The clearing off or settling of a debt.

         **Usage:** countable; uncountable

         **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The act or process of liquidating or of being liquidated.
   1. The act or process of abolishing or eliminating; the murder of dehumanized victims.

      **Usage:** countable; euphemistic; uncountable

      **Example:** When Stalinists spoke of the liquidation of the kulaks as a class, they intentionally created ambiguity regarding reeducation versus murder.

      **Source synonyms:** devitalization; fatality; death; end; tomb (literary); celestial transfer (slang); decease; decomposition; defunction (obsolete); dematerialization; demise; dirt nap (slang); doom; exit (euphemistic; often); expiration; fatality; liquidation; mortality (figuratively); passing; quietus; repose; sleep of the just (dated; euphemistic); sunset; the big sleep; crash; downfall; fall; (the) bane; (the) end; killing

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** board-games; chess; games
