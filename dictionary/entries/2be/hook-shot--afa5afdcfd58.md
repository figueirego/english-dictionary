### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A shot in which the offensive player gently throws the ball with a sweeping motion of his arm in an upward arc with a follow-through which ends over his head.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; basketball; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

2. A type of shot played by swinging the bat in a horizontal arc, hitting the ball high in the air to the leg side, often played to balls which bounce around head height; a hook.
