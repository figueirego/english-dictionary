### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who followed the calculations of William Miller or his followers that the world would end on October 22, 1844; when it did not, the Great Disappointment occurred. The subsequent denominations his movement spawned include the Adventists, Jehovah's Witnesses and the (Campbellite) Restorationists.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A resident of any community known as Miller; often specifically a resident of Miller Beach, in Gary, Indiana
