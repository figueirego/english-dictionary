### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A city, the administrative centre of Drohobych urban hromada and Drohobych Raion, Lviv Oblast, Ukraine, founded in 1091.
   1. A former oblast of the Ukrainian Soviet Socialist Republic, established in December 1939, annexed to Lviv Oblast in May 1959.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A city, the administrative centre of Drohobych urban hromada and Drohobych Raion, Lviv Oblast, Ukraine, founded in 1091.
   1. A former miskrada of Odesa Oblast, Ukraine, merged into Drohobych urban hromada in June 2020.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A city, the administrative centre of Drohobych urban hromada and Drohobych Raion, Lviv Oblast, Ukraine, founded in 1091.
   1. A raion of Lviv Oblast, Ukraine, established in January 1940, considerably enlarged in July 2020.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A city, the administrative centre of Drohobych urban hromada and Drohobych Raion, Lviv Oblast, Ukraine, founded in 1091.
