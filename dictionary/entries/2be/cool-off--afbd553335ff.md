## Verb

### Forms

- cools off — present; singular; third-person

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To decrease in temperature, activity, or temper.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** You are getting a little too angry; you need to cool off.

   **Example:** The metal will take about three hours to cool off after the molding.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To cause to decrease in temperature, activity or temper.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** At first he was very angry, but his friends managed to cool him off.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To lose interest or enthusiasm \[with on\].

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To be imprisoned.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** He's cooling off in the state pen.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To kill (someone).

   **Usage:** slang; transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To wait for a furor to die down; to hide during a police manhunt.

   **Usage:** intransitive; slang

   **Example:** After doing the job, he went to the safehouse to cool off.
