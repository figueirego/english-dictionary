**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** particular (noun)

2. A distinctive characteristic or quality; a peculiarity.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** differentia; discrimen
