### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To remove the limbs of.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Death by drawing and quartering usually dismembered the condemned person.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To cut or otherwise divide something into pieces.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The killer methodically dismembered the bodies of his victims.

   **Example:** The American Civil War nearly dismembered the Union.
