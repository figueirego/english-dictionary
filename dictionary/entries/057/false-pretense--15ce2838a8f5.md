### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A representation made of untrue assertions in order to convey a false impression.

   **Usage:** US

   **Example:** She enrolled at the university under false pretenses, giving an invented birth date and lying about her qualifications.
