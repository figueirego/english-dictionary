## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In conformity to statutes regarding size, quality etc.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** statutable measures

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Made or introduced by statute; proceeding from an act of legislature.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** a statutable provision or remedy

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Describing a crime or offence regulated by statute; legally punishable.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source synonyms:** disciplinable; penal; castigable; chastisable; disciplinable; penal; punishable; statutable; subpenal (obsolete)
