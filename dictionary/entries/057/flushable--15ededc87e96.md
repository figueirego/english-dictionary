### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of a toilet, that has a flushing mechanism.

   **Example:** It's such a relief to find a flushable toilet in this bathroom.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of a product, designed to be disposed of by flushing down the toilet.

   **Example:** Dispose of these pads carefully; they're not flushable.
