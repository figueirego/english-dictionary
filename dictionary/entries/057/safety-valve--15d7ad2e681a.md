### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A valve set to open at a pressure below that at which a container holding a gas, vapour, etc. (such as a boiler or pressure cooker), would burst, thus reducing the pressure; a relief valve.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A valve set to close a container holding a gas or vapour to prevent excessive loss of pressure.

   **Usage:** broadly

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any mechanism offering relief from physical or emotional pressure or tension.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Source synonyms:** outlet; release

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any mechanism offering relief from physical or emotional pressure or tension.
   1. A United States law, codified at 18 United States Code §3553(f), authorizing a judge to disregard mandatory minimum sentences for some criminals with few or no prior offenses.

      **Usage:** US; figuratively

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law
