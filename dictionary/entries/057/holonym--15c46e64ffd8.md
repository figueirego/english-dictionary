### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A term that denotes the whole of which another term's referent is a part.

   **Example:** The word "face" is a holonym of the word "eye", which is a holonym of the word "retina".

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; philosophy; sciences; semantics

   **Source antonyms:** meronym
