## Noun · Record 3

### Forms

- thrustings — plural

### Senses

<a id="r3-s1"></a>

**Source sense R3.S1**

1. The motion by which someone or something thrusts.

<a id="r3-s2"></a>

**Source sense R3.S2**

2. The act of squeezing curd by hand, to expel the whey.

   **Usage:** dialectal; obsolete; rare

   **Source synonyms:** pressing

<a id="r3-s3"></a>

**Source sense R3.S3**

3. The white whey, or that which is last pressed out of the curd by the hand, and of which butter is sometimes made.

   **Usage:** dialectal; obsolete; rare; usually pluralised

   **Example:** In the process of making whey butter,..the ‘thrustings’, or white whey, is set in ‘cream mugs’, to ‘carve’, and acidulate for churning.
