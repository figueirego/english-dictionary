### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To frighten or cause hesitation; to daunt, put off (usually used in the negative); to disconcert, to perturb.

   **Usage:** informal; transitive

   **Example:** Jumping out of an airplane does not faze him, yet he is afraid to ride a roller coaster.
