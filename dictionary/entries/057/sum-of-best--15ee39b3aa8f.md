### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The total combined time of the fastest segments or splits achieved in a speedrun of a video game, representing the theoretical fastest time that a speedrunner could achieve at their current level of skill.

   **Example:** The runner's final time in the record-breaking speedrun was even faster than her previous sum of best.
