### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. At what time? At which time? Upon which occasion or circumstance? Used to introduce direct or indirect questions about time.

   **Usage:** interrogative; not-comparable

   **Example:** When will they arrive?

   **Example:** Do you know when they arrived?

   **Example:** I don't know when they arrived.

   **Example:** When they arrived is unknown.

   **Example:** When we've asked them, our students say Spanish isn't difficult.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. At what time? At which time? Upon which occasion or circumstance? Used to introduce direct or indirect questions about time.
   1. Used after a noun or noun phrase in isolation to express impatience with an anticipated future event.

      **Usage:** Internet; humorous; interrogative; not-comparable; often

      **Example:** The site's all bugged. Fix when?

      **Example:** Tank class buff when?

      **Example:** My fridge even restocks itself these days. Glorious AI overlords when?

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. At an earlier time and under different, usually less favorable, circumstances.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** He's mister high and mighty now, but I remember him when.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. At which, on which, during which&#58; often omitted or replaced with that.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; relative

   **Example:** That was the day when the Twin Towers fell.

   **Example:** Not a week goes by when I don't mourn that loss.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The time at, on or during which.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; fused relative

   **Example:** I recall when they were called the Greys.

   **Example:** Next year is when we elect a new mayor.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A circumstance or situation in which.

   **Usage:** informal; not-comparable

   **Example:** Love is when you can't get enough of someone.

   **Source context (verbatim):** in definitions or explanations

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. At (or as soon as) that time that; at the (or any and every) time that; if.

   **Example:** Pavlov's dogs salivate when \[i.e. at any and every time that\] they hear a bell.

   **Example:** When \[i.e. at any and every time that\] he speaks to her, he is always polite.

   **Example:** Put your pencil down when \[i.e. as soon as, at the moment that\] the timer goes off.

   **Example:** A player wins when \[as soon as, or at any time that, if\] she has four cards of the same suit.

   **Example:** A student is disqualified when \[as soon as, if\] they cheat.

   **Example:** Don't forget to signal when (you are) turning right.

   **Example:** When she'd written her letters, she did some gardening.

   **Example:** Jose, when he finally managed to go to sleep, had a series of bad dreams.

   **Example:** I said nothing about doing that when I had the time.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. During the time that; at the time of the action of the following clause or participle phrase.

   **Example:** They dream when \[i.e. during the time that\] they sleep.

   **Example:** I'm happiest when \[during the time that, or at any time that\] I’m working.

   **Example:** It was raining when I came yesterday.

   **Example:** The game is over when the referee says it is.

   **Example:** Be careful when crossing the street.

   **Example:** When (you are) angry, count to ten before speaking or acting.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. At what time; at which time.

   **Example:** I am here till Friday, when \[i.e. at which time\] I leave for Senegal.

   **Example:** I was just walking down the street, when \[i.e. at which time\] all of a sudden it started to rain.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. Since; given the fact that; considering that.

   **Example:** I don't see the point of putting up Christmas decorations when I am the only person who is going to see them.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. Whereas; although; at the same time as; in spite of the fact that.

   **Example:** You're picking at your scabs when you should be letting them heal.

   **Example:** He keeps changing things when the existing system works perfectly well.
