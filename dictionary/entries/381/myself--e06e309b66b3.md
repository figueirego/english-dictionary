### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Me, as direct or indirect object the speaker as the object of a verb or preposition, when the speaker is also the subject.

   **Usage:** pronoun; reflexive

   **Example:** I taught myself.

   **Example:** (I) don’t think much of your new car, myself.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Personally, for my part; used in apposition to I, sometimes for simple emphasis and sometimes with implicit exclusion of any others performing the activity described.

   **Example:** I myself have witnessed the event.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. In my normal state of body or mind.

   **Example:** I feel like myself.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Me, as the object of a verb or preposition without a reflexive trigger. Sometimes used for intensifying the pronoun of oneself.

   **Usage:** proscribed

   **Example:** Give the ball to John or myself.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. I (as the subject of a verb).

   **Usage:** archaic; proscribed

   **Example:** My wife and myself want to go on vacation.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. my name is...

   **Usage:** India; Pakistan; nonstandard

   **Example:** Myself John.
