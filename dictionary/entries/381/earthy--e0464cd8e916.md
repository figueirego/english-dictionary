### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Resembling dirt or soil (i.e. earth).

   **Example:** The earthy smell of fresh turned loam told me the farmer had started plowing this morning, the definitive sign of spring for me.

   **Source synonyms:** terreous

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Composed, or largely composed, of soil.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Down-to-earth, not artificial, natural.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** She was an earthy soul, the salt of the earth as they say of such rural folk, untarnished by false civilization.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Coarse and unrefined, crude.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** earthy humour

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Like or resembling the earth or of the earth.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Covered with earth (mud, dirt).
