**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Mrs Smith, née Jones

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used when giving a former name. Originally known as.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Since the name change, Butch (née Frances) seems more tough and self-assured.
