### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To cause pain to someone.

   **Example:** Her elbow has been giving her grief.

   **Source synonyms:** hurt; trouble; ache (literary; rare); afflict; aggrieve; agonize; anguish; dere (British; dialectal); excruciate; hurt; irritate; pang; pine; rack; throe (obsolete); torment; torture; wring (figuratively)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To hassle or abuse someone.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** Fred was giving me grief over the money I owed him.

   **Source synonyms:** bother; harass; pester; trouble; badger; bait; bedevil; beleaguer; [persecute](../3ab/persecute--eac7ff79919f.md); dog; bullyrag; besiege; bug; devil (informal); beset (figuratively); chevy (figuratively); give someone grief (idiomatic); harass; harry; hound; pester; pick at (idiomatic); plague; vex (archaic)
