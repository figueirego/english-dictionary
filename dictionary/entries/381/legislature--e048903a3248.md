### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A governmental body with the power to make, amend and repeal laws.

   **Example:** state legislature

   **Example:** bicameral legislature

   **Example:** colonial legislature

   **Example:** members of the legislature

   **Example:** The state legislature passed a new education bill.

   **Example:** Members of the legislature debated late into the night.

   **Example:** The law cannot take effect until approved by the legislature.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A governmental body with the power to make, amend and repeal laws.
   1. The legislative assembly of a province or territory, as opposed to the federal parliament.

      **Usage:** Canada
