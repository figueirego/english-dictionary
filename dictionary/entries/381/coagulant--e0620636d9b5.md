### Forms

- coagulants — plural

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A substance that causes coagulation.

   **Example:** Magnesium sulfate is the coagulant used in making tofu. It causes the liquid soy milk to separate into solid tofu and water.

   **Source synonyms:** coagulator

   **Source antonyms:** anticoagulant
