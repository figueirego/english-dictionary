### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The ability, when walking aboard a ship, to anticipate the motion of the deck so as to walk steadily without losing balance.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; plural; plural-only

   **Example:** to get/find one's sea legs

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The ability to travel by ship without becoming seasick.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; plural; plural-only

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Synonym of crab sticks.

   **Usage:** US; plural; plural-only; rare

   **Source synonyms:** crab sticks (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>
