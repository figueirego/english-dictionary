### Senses

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To discard.

   **Usage:** US

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

   **Usage:** US

   **Example:** The burglars trashed the house.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

   **Usage:** US

   **Usage:** transitive
