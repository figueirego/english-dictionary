**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any trans-Neptunian object whose orbit is wholly within the scattered disc.

   **Example:** Short-period comets (those with periods up to 200 years) are thought to be scattered disc objects perturbed from their orbits towards the inner solar system by the gravitational pull of Neptune.
