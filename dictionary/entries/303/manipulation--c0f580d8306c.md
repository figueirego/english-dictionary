### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The practice of manipulating or the state of being manipulated.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The dealer's manipulations could have removed cards from the deck.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The skillful use of the hands in, for example, chiropractic.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** After a few minutes of manipulation each week, she obtained days of relief from her neck pain.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The usage of psychological influence over a person, event, or situation to gain a desired outcome.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The counselor was able to reach the disturbed teen through positive psychological manipulation.

   **Example:** Through subtle manipulations he orchestrated the downfall of his rival.
