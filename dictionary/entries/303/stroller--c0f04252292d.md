### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A seat or chair on wheels, pushed by somebody walking behind it, typically used for transporting babies and young children.

   **Usage:** Australia; Canada; US

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One who strolls.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>
