### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Physically secure and certain, non-failing, reliable.

   **Example:** This investment is a sure thing.

   **Example:** The bailiff had a sure grip on the prisoner's arm.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Certain in one's knowledge or belief.

   **Example:** Are you sure to succeed, as polls say? —Well, I am at least certain of making it to the podium this time.

   **Example:** He was sure of being a finalist.

   **Example:** They aren't sure who will attend for sure.

   **Example:** You seemed sure that the car was his. Yeah. I’m not sure what is the point of spending hours and hours discussing this.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Certain to act or be a specified way.

   **Example:** Be sure to lock the door when you leave.

   **Source context (verbatim):** followed by a to infinitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Certain to obtain something.

   **Example:** Many people book early in order to be sure of the best seats.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Free from danger; safe; secure.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Betrothed; engaged to marry.

   **Usage:** obsolete
