### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The surface of coal exposed by mining, or a part of a coal seam that is currently being mined.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; mining

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The end of a drift, entry, or room in a coal mine.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; mining

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

   **Usage:** UK; figuratively
