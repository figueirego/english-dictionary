## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

- dead weights — plural
- deadweight — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Unremitting heavy weight that does not move.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The largest weight of cargo a ship is able to carry; i.e., the weight of a ship when fully loaded minus its weight when empty.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A dead load.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; construction; manufacturing

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. That which is useless or excess; that which slows something down.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

   **Example:** She wants to shed the dead weight of so many stacks of old clutter.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The total (unpowered) weight of a train to be propelled (or hauled) by the locomotive; another locomotive being hauled dead as part of the train is a dead weight.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** rail-transport; railways; transport

## Adverb · Record 2

### Forms

- deadweight — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. With just enough weight for a ball to drop into a pocket, or come to rest in a precisely determined position.

   **Example:** He rolled the red in dead weight, and now he's nicely on the blue.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a shot in snooker or other cue sports
