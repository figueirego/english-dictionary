1. To dance the waltz (with).

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** They waltzed for twenty-one hours and seventeen minutes straight, setting a record.

   **Example:** While waltzing her around the room, he stepped on her toes only once.

2. To move briskly and unhesitatingly, especially in an inappropriately casual manner, or when unannounced or uninvited.

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive; usually; into; around; into; around

   **Example:** He waltzed into the room like he owned the place.

   **Example:** You can't just waltz him in here without documentation!
