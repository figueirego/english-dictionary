### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To reverse the process of hearing, so that (a sound, etc.) was never heard.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I wish I could unhear that terrible song!

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To forget a sound.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** They could never unhear what they heard that night.
