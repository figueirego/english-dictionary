## Adjective · Record 1

### Forms

- more apolitical — comparative
- most apolitical — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having no interest or involvement in politics.

   **Example:** The Queen is an apolitical figure.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Having no political relevance or function.

   **Example:** The PM's visit to the school was entirely apolitical.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Politically neutral, unbiased, nonaligned and free from party politics.

   **Example:** My colleagues and I come to this task with open minds, we are apolitical and independent of any political party.
