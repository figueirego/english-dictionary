### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A cocktail made with gin or vodka and vermouth.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Will you join us at six o’clock for martinis?

   **Example:** My boss is out on another of his three-martini lunches.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any cocktail served in a cocktail glass, often sweet or fruity and aimed at women.

   **Usage:** countable; proscribed; sometimes; uncountable

   **Example:** I love the chocolate martinis at Trendy Bar!
