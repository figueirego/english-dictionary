### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The side of a fabric, often with a more visible color or pattern, that is intended to face outward on a finished project.

   **Example:** Pin fabric A and fabric C together, right sides facing.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; manufacturing; sewing; textiles

   **Source synonyms:** finished side

   **Source antonyms:** wrong side
