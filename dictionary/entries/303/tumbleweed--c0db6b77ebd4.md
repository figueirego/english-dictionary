### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any plant which habitually breaks away from its roots once dry, forming a light, rolling mass which is driven by the wind from place to place; as Russian thistle, wild indigo, witch grass, Amaranthus albus, etc.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** roly-poly (Australia); steppe-witch; wind-witch

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any plant which habitually breaks away from its roots once dry, forming a light, rolling mass which is driven by the wind from place to place; as Russian thistle, wild indigo, witch grass, Amaranthus albus, etc.
   1. One of the dried-out, seed-dispersing, globular forms of such plants.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source synonyms:** roly-poly (Australia); steppe-witch; wind-witch

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Describing unwanted silence and inactivity. Often used of a situation when one makes a statement that is ignored or ill-received by one's audience, as the resultant silence is likened to that of a desolate desert with rolling tumbleweeds.

   **Usage:** attributive; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A tan colour, like that of a dried out tumbleweed.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
