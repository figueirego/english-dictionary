## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Something that is completed or accomplished only once, never to be done again.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; informal

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A basketball player who plays college basketball for a single year and then declares for the NBA draft.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Example:** John Calipari's Kentucky squad was made up of one-and-done players.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; basketball; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The act of a team making the postseason playoffs but losing the very first game played in it.

   **Usage:** US; informal

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A family in which the parents decide to have only one child.
