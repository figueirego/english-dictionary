<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A fuss, an uproar, a commotion or stir; hype; brouhaha, hullabaloo.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** There was a big hoo-ha about it in the papers, and then the world completely forgot the matter.
