## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An ice cream variety, popular in Chicago and the American Midwest in the late 19th and early 20th century, consisting of a layer of orange sherbet or ice cream, a layer of vanilla ice cream, and a layer of caramel (or sometimes coffee, chocolate, or maple) ice cream.

   **Usage:** uncountable
