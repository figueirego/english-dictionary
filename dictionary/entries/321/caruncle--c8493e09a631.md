### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A small, fleshy excrescence that is a normal part of an animal's anatomy.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; biology; medicine; natural-sciences; sciences; zoology; zootomy

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Clipping of lacrimal caruncle&#58; the red prominence at the inner corner of the eye.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; clipping

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; medicine; sciences

   **Alternative form of:** [lacrimal caruncle](../17b/lacrimal-caruncle--5efabd18348b.md)

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A colored waxy or oily outgrowth near the micropyle of some seeds, attractive to ants which aid the seed's dispersal.
