### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To shrivel, droop or dry up, especially from lack of water.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The flowers began to wither in the hot sun without enough water.

   **Example:** She watched as the old tree slowly withered with time.

   **Example:** Without care, the crops will wither and fail to grow.

   **Source synonyms:** sear

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To cause to shrivel or dry up.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** sear

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To lose vigour or power; to languish; to waste away; to pass away.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To become helpless due to emotion.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To make helpless due to emotion.

   **Usage:** transitive
