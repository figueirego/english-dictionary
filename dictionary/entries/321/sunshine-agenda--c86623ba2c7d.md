### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A public agenda.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An agenda on which an item has been placed for which the decision-making body will not entertain discussion or commentary.

   **Usage:** US

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; law

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A public agenda on which an item for decision has been placed for which the decision-making body will not entertain commentary, discussion or influence during a certain defined time-frame prior to the decision, in order to give the body "a period of repose" in which to make decisions.

   **Usage:** US
