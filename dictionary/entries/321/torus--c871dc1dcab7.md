### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The standard representation of such a space in 3-dimensional Euclidean space&#58; a surface or solid formed by rotating a closed curve, especially a circle, about a line which lies in the same plane but does not intersect it (e.g. like a ring doughnut).
   1. A topological space which is a product of two circles.

      **Example:** A 4-variable Karnaugh map can be thought of, topologically, as being a torus.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences; topology

      **Source synonyms:** toroid
