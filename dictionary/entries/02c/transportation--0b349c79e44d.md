### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of transporting, or the state of being transported; conveyance, often of people, goods etc.

   **Usage:** US; uncountable; usually

   **Example:** We have to get people out of their cars and encourage them to use alternative forms of transportation.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Deportation to a penal colony.

   **Usage:** historical; uncountable; usually

   **Example:** Mulligan's sentence was commuted from death to transportation.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A means or manner of conveyance; a system of conveyance.

   **Usage:** US; uncountable; usually

   **Example:** Nice transportation, dude, but your brake lights are busted.

   **Example:** The hotel offers ground transportation to the airport.

   **Example:** Public transportation is useful for everybody.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A ticket or fare.

   **Usage:** US; uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Punishment by sending to a penal colony, such as Australia.

   **Usage:** historical; uncountable; usually
