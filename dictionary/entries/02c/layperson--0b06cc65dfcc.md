### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who is not a cleric.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One who is not intimately familiar with a given subject or activity.

   **Example:** The book was written for professionals, but an intelligent layperson could understand most of it.
