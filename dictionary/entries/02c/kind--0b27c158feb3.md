### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A type, race or category; a group of entities that have common characteristics such that they may be grouped together.

   **Example:** What kind of (a) person are you?

   **Example:** What kind of ice-cream would you like? There's chocolate, strawberry, and lemon.

   **Example:** Neither of them is the marrying kind.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A makeshift or otherwise atypical specimen.

   **Example:** The opening served as a kind of window.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. One's inherent nature; character, natural disposition.

   **Usage:** archaic

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Family, lineage.

   **Usage:** archaic

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Manner.

   **Usage:** archaic

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Goods or services used as payment, as e.g. in barter.

   **Example:** I don't have any money but I can pay in kind by washing the dishes.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Equivalent means used as response to an action.

   **Example:** I'll pay in kind for his insult.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Each of the two elements of the communion service, bread and wine.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. The type of a type constructor or a higher-order type operator.

   **Usage:** type theory

   **Example:** The kind of any primitive data type is \*, corresponding to a nullary constructor.

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. Food in a particular category.

   **Usage:** Caribbean; in-compounds
