## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An island and local government area located in Bass Strait, on the north coast of Tasmania, Australia, named after Philip Gidley King.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A small island near Cape Melville in northern Queensland, Australia.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An island on the coast of British Columbia, Canada, named after James King (Royal Navy officer).

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An island in Qikiqtaaluk Region, Nunavut, Canada.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A small island in Waskesiu Lake, Saskatchewan, Canada, named after William Lyon Mackenzie King.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A small island in the Bering Sea, Alaska, United States, named after James King (as in British Columbia).

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. An island in the Sacramento–San Joaquin River Delta, in San Joaquin County, California, United States.
