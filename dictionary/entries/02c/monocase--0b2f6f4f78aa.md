### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of a script or typeface&#58; having only one case, making no distinction between upper and lower case.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; media; publishing; sciences; typography

   **Source synonyms:** unicameral; unicase

2. Supporting or consisting of only one case of letters; either all uppercase or all lowercase; (also), case insensitive, treating upper and lower case as being the same.
