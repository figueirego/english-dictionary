**Source sense R1.S1**

1. No problem, the solution is simple, there you have it, it's that easy, you have what you want, all will be well&#58; indicates a desirable conclusion has been reached with ease.

   **Usage:** Commonwealth; Ireland; UK; idiomatic

   **Example:** Insert the plug, press the switch, and Bob's your uncle.
