## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Being equivalent to the object whose constituents or parameters are reversed in order and mapped by some involution, particularly the inversion operator of a quasigroup.
   1. Of a polynomial, being equivalent to the polynomial with reversed and additively inverted coefficients&#58; ∑ᵢ₌₀ⁿa\_ixⁱ is antipalindromic iff ∑ᵢ₌₀ⁿa\_ixⁱ=∑ᵢ₌₀ⁿ-a\_n-ixⁱ⟺a\_i=-a\_n-i.

      **Usage:** not-comparable

      **Example:** x²-1 is an antipalindromic polynomial.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Being equivalent to the object whose constituents or parameters are reversed in order and mapped by some involution, particularly the inversion operator of a quasigroup.
   1. Of a natural number, with respect to base b, being equivalent to the natural number whose digits are reversed and subtracted from b-1&#58; ∑ᵢ₌₀ⁿa\_ibⁱ is antipalindromic iff ∑ᵢ₌₀ⁿa\_ibⁱ=∑ᵢ₌₀ⁿ(b-1-a\_n-i)bⁱ⟺a\_i=b-1-a\_n-i.

      **Usage:** not-comparable

      **Example:** 1728\#95;\#123;10\#125; is an antipalindromic number with respect to base 10.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences
