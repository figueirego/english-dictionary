### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see hold, one's, breath. To keep air in one's lungs, pausing noticeably before exhaling after previously inhaling.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To wait expectantly for something to happen soon.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; usually in a negative

   **Example:** He might pay back the money he borrowed without you reminding him, but I wouldn't hold my breath.
