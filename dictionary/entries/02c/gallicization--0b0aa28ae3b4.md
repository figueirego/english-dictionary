### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Synonym of Frenchification, the act or process of making French of more French-like, especially in formal and academic contexts.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The Gallicization of Algeria—at least the cities on its northern coast—was a national project for over a century.

   **Source synonyms:** Frenchification (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The act of translation into the French language.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A translation into the French language.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
