### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A practitioner or adherent of serialism, an approach to composing which emphasizes the use of a series of notes, especially scales, to achieve coherence.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music
