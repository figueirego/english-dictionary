### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A passage; a way through.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; now rare except in certain set phrases

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A road open at both ends or connecting one area with another; a highway or main street.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The act of going through; passage; travel, transit.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** The sign leading to the other carriage reads&#58; No thoroughfare.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An unobstructed waterway allowing passage for ships.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
