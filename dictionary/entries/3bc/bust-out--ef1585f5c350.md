### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To escape (from); break out.

   **Usage:** Canada; US; informal; intransitive

   **Example:** to bust out of prison

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To free from captivity.

   **Usage:** Canada; US; informal; transitive

   **Example:** They tried to bust the prisoner out, but were thwarted by the police.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To reveal, to show; to appear.

   **Usage:** US; informal; intransitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To bring out; to take out.

   **Usage:** US; informal; transitive

   **Example:** They decided it was time to bust out the good hooch.

   **Source synonyms:** break out

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To burst out (laughing).

   **Usage:** US; informal; intransitive; nonstandard

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To lose by going bust.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** blackjack; games

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To buy an interest in (a business) and then take out a large amount of debt, strip (the business) of assets and then cash out before (the business) goes into bankruptcy.

   **Usage:** dated; slang; transitive

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To have sex with (a woman); to bring (a woman) to orgasm.

   **Usage:** slang; transitive; African-American Vernacular
