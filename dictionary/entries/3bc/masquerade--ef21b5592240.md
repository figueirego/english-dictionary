### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An assembly or party of people wearing (usually elaborate or fanciful) masks and costumes, and amusing themselves with dancing, conversation, or other diversions.

   **Usage:** also; attributive

   **Example:** I was invited to the masquerade at their home.

   **Source synonyms:** masque (obsolete); costume party

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The act of wearing a mask or dressing up in a costume for, or as if for, a masquerade ball.

   **Usage:** also; attributive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An act of living under false pretenses; a concealment of something by a false or unreal show; a disguise, a pretence; also, a pretentious display.

   **Usage:** also; attributive; figuratively

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An assembly of varied, often fanciful, things.

   **Usage:** also; attributive; figuratively

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A cosplay event at which costumed attendees perform skits.

   **Usage:** also; attributive; slang

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** lifestyle

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A dramatic performance by actors in masks; a mask or masque.

   **Usage:** also; attributive; obsolete

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

   **Usage:** also; attributive; obsolete; rare

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To take part in a masquerade; to assemble in masks and costumes; (loosely) to wear a disguise.

   **Usage:** also; attributive; intransitive

   **Example:** I’m going to masquerade as an old-fashioned pilot. What are you going to dress up as?

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To pass oneself off as a different person or a person with qualities that one does not possess; also, to make a pretentious show of being what one is not.

   **Usage:** also; attributive; figuratively; intransitive

   **Example:** He masqueraded as my friend until the truth finally came out.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To appear as something else.

   **Usage:** also; attributive; broadly; figuratively; intransitive

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To conceal (someone) with, or as if with, a mask; to disguise.

   **Usage:** also; attributive; rare; transitive
