### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To fly or glide downwards suddenly; to plunge (in the air) or nosedive.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The lone eagle swooped down into the lake, snatching its prey, a small fish.

   **Source synonyms:** stoop

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To move swiftly, as if with a sweeping movement, especially to attack something.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The dog had enthusiastically swooped down on the bone.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To fall on at once and seize; to catch while on the wing.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To seize; to catch up; to take with a sweep.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To pass with pomp; to sweep.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To search the ground for discarded cigarette butts that can be made into new cigarettes.

   **Usage:** UK; slang
