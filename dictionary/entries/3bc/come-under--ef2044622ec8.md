## Verb

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To come underneath (something).

   **Usage:** literally

   **Example:** Why don't you come under my umbrella? There's plenty of room.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To be included or classified under (a title, specified class etc.).

   **Example:** To sympathisers, though, all of his crimes came under the general heading of misdemeanors.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To be subjected to, be under the auspices of.

   **Example:** Eventually I was promoted, and a small team of recruits came under my authority.
