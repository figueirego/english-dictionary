### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A pure quality with the power to cure or to turn one substance into another; also, a substance such as a philosopher's stone able to turn one substance into another.

   **Usage:** also; countable; figuratively; historical; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** alchemy; pseudoscience

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The product of such a transformation.

   **Usage:** also; countable; figuratively; historical; obsolete; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** alchemy; pseudoscience

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A fine substance deposited by precipitation, formerly applied to certain white precipitates from metallic solutions.

   **Usage:** countable; historical; uncountable

   **Example:** magistery of bismuth (BiONO₃·H₂O)

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A concentrated extract of a substance.

   **Usage:** countable; historical; obsolete; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An art or a skill.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Synonym of magistracy (“the dignity or office of a magistrate; the collective body of magistrates”).

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete

   **Source synonyms:** magistracy (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A medicine prepared for a specific use.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; medicine; natural-sciences; pathology; pharmacology; sciences; veterinary; zoology

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. The quality possessed by a master; authority, mastership, mastery; also, the exercise of authority.

   **Usage:** obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. Synonym of magisterium (“the teaching authority or office of the Roman Catholic Church”).

   **Usage:** obsolete; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Catholicism; Christianity; Roman-Catholicism

   **Source synonyms:** magisterium (synonym; synonym-of)
