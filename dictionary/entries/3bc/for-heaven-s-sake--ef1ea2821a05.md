### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Expresses frustration, exasperation, annoyance, or the urgency of an instruction.

   **Usage:** euphemistic; idiomatic

   **Source synonyms:** FFS; for Christ's sake; for cripes' sake; for crying in the beer; for crying out loud; for fucking out loud; for fuck's sake; for God's sake; for goodness' sake; for heaven's sake; for mercy's sake; for Pete's sake; for pity's sake; for the love of all that is holy; for the love of Betsy; for the love of God; for the love of Mike; for the love of Moses; for the love of fuck; for the love of Pete

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Expresses surprise, sometimes pleasant.

   **Usage:** euphemistic; idiomatic

   **Example:** Well, for heaven's sake! Look who's here! Good to see you again!
