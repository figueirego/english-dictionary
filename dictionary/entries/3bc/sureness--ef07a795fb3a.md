### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The property of being sure, certainty.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** His sureness was born of having looked it up in a reputable reference book.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The property of being sure, deft, confident of ability.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** The surgeon's sureness was the result of long study in school and long practice in the operating room, never did he hesitate and never was there a mis-move.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The result or product of being sure.

   **Usage:** countable; rare
