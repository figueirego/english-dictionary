### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. specifically characteristic or indicative of a particular disease or condition.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** A tetrad of rash, arthralgia, abdominal pain and kidney disease in a child is pathognomonic for Henoch-Schönlein purpura.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a sign or symptom
