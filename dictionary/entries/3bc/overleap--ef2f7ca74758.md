### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To leap over, to jump over, to cross by jumping.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To pass over; to exceed; to omit, leave out.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To make too much effort in leaping; to leap too far.

   **Usage:** dated; reflexive

   **Example:** I overleapt myself and stumbled.
