**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A method of cooking in which food is heated in airtight bags for an extended period of time at relatively low, but tightly controlled temperatures, in order to maintain the integrity of ingredients.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**
