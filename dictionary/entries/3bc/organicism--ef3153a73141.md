### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The theory that disease is a result of structural alteration of organs.

   **Usage:** countable; historical; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The concept that everything is organic, or forms part of an organic whole.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The treatment of society or the universe as if it were an organism.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; philosophy; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The mereological idea that living things are the only composite objects.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; philosophy; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The theory that the total organization of an organism is more important than the functioning of its individual organs.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
