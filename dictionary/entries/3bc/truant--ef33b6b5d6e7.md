### Senses

<a id="r3-s1"></a>

**Source sense R3.S1**

1. Also used with the impersonal pronoun it (dated)&#58; to shirk or wander from business or duty; (specifically) of a student&#58; to be absent from school without permission; to play truant.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The number of schoolchildren known to have truanted from this school has been unusually high.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive
