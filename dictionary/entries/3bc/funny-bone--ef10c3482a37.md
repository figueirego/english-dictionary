### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The ulnar nerve in the elbow, which, when hit, causes a tingling sensation.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** Ouch, I just hit my funny bone.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Nickname for the humerus, so named because of its being homophonic with "humorous".

   **Usage:** mnemonic

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. One's sense of humor.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** His wisecracks always tickle my funny bone.
