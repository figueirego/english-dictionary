## Verb · Record 1

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To cut downward.

   **Usage:** intransitive; literally

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To adulterate a drug.

   **Usage:** literally; transitive

   **Example:** We need to cut the cocaine down.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To bring down by cutting.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** They want to cut down several trees to make room for the parking lot.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To insult, to belittle.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To reduce the amount of something.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** He wants to cut down on extra steps.

   **Example:** Please don't put the candy jar right next to my desk. I'm trying to cut down on sugar.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To slay, often in great numbers.

   **Usage:** dated; idiomatic

   **Example:** I will cut you down where you stand.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To shoot a gun; to shoot (someone or many people), usually to kill them.

   **Usage:** intransitive; slang; transitive

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To challenge (someone); to prove superiority to (someone).

   **Usage:** slang; transitive; African-American Vernacular
