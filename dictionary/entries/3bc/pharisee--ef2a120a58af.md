### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A member of an ancient Jewish political party, a social movement, and a school of thought among Jews that flourished during the Second Temple Era (536 B.C.E.–70 C.E.). The movement was ultimately the basis for most contemporary forms of Judaism.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person who values the letter of the law over its spirit or intention.

   **Usage:** broadly; derogatory; figuratively
