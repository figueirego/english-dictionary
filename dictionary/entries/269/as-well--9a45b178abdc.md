### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In addition; also.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; not-comparable

   **Example:** Wearing his hat and coat, he looked outside and decided he should take an umbrella as well.

   **Source synonyms:** additionally; too; in the bargain

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To the same effect.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** They might as well walk as drive in this traffic.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Me too.

   **Usage:** South-Africa; not-comparable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An intensifier. Actually; really.

   **Usage:** UK; colloquial; not-comparable

   **Example:** ―Would you though? ―I would as well.

   **Example:** ―She looks so much like her mum, don't you think? ―Oh my god, she does as well!

   **Example:** ―Good morning Mr Brown! ―Good morning! I see you've got little Molly \[pet dog\] out for a stroll as well!
