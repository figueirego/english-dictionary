### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A display, particularly one that presents numerical data.

   **Example:** Her company retrofitted its manual machine tools with digital readouts.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The information, usually a measurement, shown on such a display.

   **Example:** The density is measured by the readout from a hydrometer.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The information output from a computer in a readable form.

   **Example:** The computer readout was inaccurate.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An account of the topics discussed in a meeting, especially in diplomatic or political contexts.

   **Example:** Most of us first found out when we heard the readout from the summit meeting.
