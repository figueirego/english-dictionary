### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To end (something).
   1. To stop (fire, etc.) from burning; also, to stop (light, etc.) from shining; to put out, to quench.

      **Usage:** transitive

      **Source synonyms:** douse; extinct; smother

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To end (something).
   1. To eclipse or obscure (someone or something).

      **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

      **Example:** A beauty that extinguishes all others by comparison

      **Example:** The rays of the sun were extinguished by the thunder clouds.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To end (something).
   1. To kill (someone).

      **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

      **Source synonyms:** extinct
