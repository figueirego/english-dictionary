**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Describing a junction or intersection where two or more transport axes cross at the same level (or grade).

   **Usage:** US; not-comparable

   **Example:** An at-grade intersection may require a traffic-control device such as a stop sign, traffic light or railway signal to manage conflicting traffic.
