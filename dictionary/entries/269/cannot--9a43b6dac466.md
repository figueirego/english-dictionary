### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Can not; be unable to (whether due to physical inability, logical impossibility, etc.).

   **Example:** I cannot open the window. It is stuck.

   **Example:** The butler cannot be the murderer, he cannot have killed her, because he was in another city at the time.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Be forbidden or not permitted to.

   **Example:** You cannot enter the hall without a ticket.
