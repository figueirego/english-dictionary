## Adverb

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used to introduce a suggestion or proposal for a future event.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** What if we arrange it for 9&#58;00 instead of 10&#58;00?

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used to introduce a speculation about a future event.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** What if we could learn to get along? What would happen then?

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used to introduce a speculation about a past event. Often the basis for counterfactual historical interpretations.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** What if Abraham Lincoln had not been assassinated? How might the Reconstruction of the Southern States have been handled?

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** what about — introducing a suggestion; how about — introducing a suggestion
