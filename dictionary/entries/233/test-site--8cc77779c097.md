## Noun

### Forms

- test sites — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A place where products or weapons are tested.

   **Example:** The Nevada test site is contaminated with radiation after years of nuclear testing.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A clinic or office where medical testing takes place.

   **Example:** The free teen clinic is a very welcoming HIV test site.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A room dedicated to the administration of exams, especially for certifications.

   **Example:** Connie took her bar exam at the University of Texas test site.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** education
