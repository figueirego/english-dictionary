### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A type of rennet cheese or cheese produced under employment of a similar clotting enzyme characterized by being firm owing the curds not only having been comminuted to a large degree but having been stirred with the whey before being put to ripen for a longer time.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** stirred curd cheese
