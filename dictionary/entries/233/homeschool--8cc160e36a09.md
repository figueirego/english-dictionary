### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To educate children at home, that is, at a private domestic place, in lieu of sending them to a public school or private educational institution.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I chose to homeschool my children.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To be educated at home.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** I only started regular school when I was ten; before that I homeschooled.

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A school within a private domestic place, rather than in a public facility or private institution.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Synonym of boarding school.

   **Usage:** US; countable; dated; uncountable
