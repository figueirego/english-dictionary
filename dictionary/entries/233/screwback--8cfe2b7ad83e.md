## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The period of screw rotation during injection molding when the plastic can flow into the mold.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A hook-shaped attachment for an earring that sits behind the earlobe and tightens with a screw. Also, an earring that uses this type of attachment.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A mechanism for attaching a small item to clothing that operates by a post which goes through the cloth and a small backing plate that screws to the post. Also, the medal, tie pin, button, etc. that uses this type of attachment.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A type of case for a pocket watch where the mechanism is accessed by a removable back plate that screws onto the back of the case. Also, a watch that has such a case.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The effect of putting backspin on the cue ball.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; billiards; games; hobbies; lifestyle; pool; snooker; sports

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A powerful shot that imparts a spin to the ball.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** golf; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A half-volley executed by holding the racket loosely and swinging it straight at the ball.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** architecture

**Source sense R1.S9**

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s10"></a>
