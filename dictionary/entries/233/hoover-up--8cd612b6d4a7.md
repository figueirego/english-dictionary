### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To suck (something) into a vacuum cleaner, irrespective of brand.

   **Usage:** informal; transitive

   **Example:** Don't worry about the mess on the carpet – I'll hoover it up later.

   **Source synonyms:** vacuum up

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To do the vacuuming.

   **Usage:** informal; intransitive

   **Example:** I'll hoover up after I've finished washing the dishes.

   **Source synonyms:** hoover; vacuum up

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To avidly absorb or consume (something), as if by a vacuum cleaner.

   **Usage:** figuratively; informal; transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To avidly absorb or consume (something), as if by a vacuum cleaner.
   1. To consume (food or drink) quickly, especially by taking it into the mouth directly from the plate rather than using cutlery.

      **Usage:** figuratively; informal; transitive

      **Source synonyms:** bolt; scarf down; wolf down
