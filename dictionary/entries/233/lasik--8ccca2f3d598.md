**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Abbreviation of laser in situ keratomileusis&#58; corrective eye surgery for various refractive disorders in which an excimer laser reshapes the cornea.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; uncountable

   **Example:** In LASIK surgery, a computer-controlled laser is used to reshape the cornea.
