   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** During Christmas time my dad loves to eat mince pies.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

   **Usage:** Cockney; countable; in-plural; slang

   **Usage:** UK; slang; uncountable
