### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A village, the administrative centre of Pishchiv rural hromada, Zviahel Raion, Zhytomyr Oblast, Ukraine, founded in 1650.
   1. A former volost of Novohrad-Volynskyi povit, Volyn governorate, Russian Empire, established in 1867, abolished in 1923.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A village, the administrative centre of Pishchiv rural hromada, Zviahel Raion, Zhytomyr Oblast, Ukraine, founded in 1650.
   1. A former silrada of Novohrad-Volynskyi Raion, Zhytomyr Oblast, Ukraine, established in 1923, amalgamated into Pishchiv rural hromada in August 2017.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A village, the administrative centre of Pishchiv rural hromada, Zviahel Raion, Zhytomyr Oblast, Ukraine, founded in 1650.
   1. Former name of Yarun Raion&#58; a former raion of Zhytomyr Oblast, Ukrainian Soviet Socialist Republic; known by this name March 1923–September 1925.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A village, the administrative centre of Pishchiv rural hromada, Zviahel Raion, Zhytomyr Oblast, Ukraine, founded in 1650.
   1. A rural hromada of Zviahel Raion, Zhytomyr Oblast, Ukraine, established in August 2017.
