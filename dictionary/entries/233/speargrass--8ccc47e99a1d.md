### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of several not closely related grasses in some way reminiscent of a spear.
   1. Species of Aristida (three-awn wiregrass)

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of several not closely related grasses in some way reminiscent of a spear.
   1. Heteropogon contortus (tanglehead)

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of several not closely related grasses in some way reminiscent of a spear.
   1. Imperata cylindrica (cogon grass)

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any of several not closely related grasses in some way reminiscent of a spear.
   1. Species of Poa (bluegrass etc)

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Any of several not closely related grasses in some way reminiscent of a spear.
   1. Species of Stipa (feathergrass), typically Stipa calamagrostis

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable
