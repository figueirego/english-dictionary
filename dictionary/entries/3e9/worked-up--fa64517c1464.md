### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Excessively emotional, excited, aroused or angry.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; informal

   **Example:** I know it's difficult when things get this busy, but there's really no need to get so worked up over it.

   **Example:** Brother Wu gets worked up when you banter him off in any way, shape or form. Particularly his shoddy brand, football opinions or taste in clothes.
