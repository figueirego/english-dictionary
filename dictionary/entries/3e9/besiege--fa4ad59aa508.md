### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To beset or surround with armed forces for the purpose of compelling to surrender, to lay siege to, beleaguer.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To beleaguer, to vex, to lay siege to, to beset.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To assail or ply, as with requests or demands.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The box office was besieged by fans wanting tickets.
