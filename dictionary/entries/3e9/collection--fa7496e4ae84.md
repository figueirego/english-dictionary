### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A set of items or amount of material procured, gathered or presented together.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The attic contains a remarkable collection of antiques, oddities, and random junk.

   **Example:** The asteroid belt consists of a collection of dust, rubble, and minor planets.

   **Example:** This year's Summer Collection will include a wide range of evening wear.

   **Example:** He has a big collection of stamps.

   **Example:** He has a superb coin collection.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A set of pitch classes used by a composer.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The activity of collecting.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Collection of trash will occur every Thursday.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A set of sets; used because such a thing is in general too large to comply with the formal definition of a set.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematical-analysis; mathematics; sciences; set-theory; topology

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A gathering of money for charitable or other purposes, as by passing a contribution box for donations.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Debt collection.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

   **Usage:** UK; countable; uncountable
