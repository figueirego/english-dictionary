## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A 2011 controversy around the broadcast by Nine News of fake footage of a helicopter supposedly flying near Beerwah, Queensland, where the remains of murdered schoolboy Daniel Morcombe had been found earlier that month.

   **Usage:** Australia; informal

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An Australian political scandal of 2014 involving Speaker of the Australian House of Representatives Bronwyn Bishop flying from Melbourne to a party fundraiser in Geelong, at a cost of AUD$5227.

   **Usage:** Australia; informal

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A 2013 controversy around alleged bribery and corruption in the purchase of a fleet of helicopters for Indian state officials.

   **Usage:** India; informal
