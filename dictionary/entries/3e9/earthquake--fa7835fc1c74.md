### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A shaking of the ground, caused by volcanic activity or movement around geologic faults.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** planetary geology

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
