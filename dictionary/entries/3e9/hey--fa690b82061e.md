   **Usage:** informal

   **Usage:** informal

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** Hey? How's that?

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Used as a tag question, to emphasise what goes before or to request that the listener express an opinion about what has been said.

   **Usage:** informal

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A meaningless beat marker or extra, filler syllable in song lyrics.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** The chorus is "nana na na, nana na na hey hey hey, goodbye".
