### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A kind of rational decision-making, different from the widely-accepted game-theoretical one. A player whose opponent is also superrational will cooperate in the prisoner's dilemma problem, whereas a game-theoretically rational player will defect.

   **Usage:** uncountable
