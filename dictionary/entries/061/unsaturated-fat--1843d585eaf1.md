### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A type of dietary fat where the fatty acid chains contain one or more double bonds between carbon atoms, making them less saturated with hydrogen, causing them to typically remain liquid at room temperature.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
