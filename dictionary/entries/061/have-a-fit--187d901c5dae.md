### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To experience an epileptic seizure.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** She needs to keep her sugar level low or she'll have a fit.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To become suddenly enraged.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** She'll have a fit when she finds out a younger woman got the job she was hoping for.
