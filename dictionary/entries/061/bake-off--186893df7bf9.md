### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A cooking contest, especially one where competition is head-to-head, not limited to preparing food involving baking.

   **Example:** The winner of the Pillsbury bake-off is obviously a very good cook and is respected for it.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An occasion of the preparation of crack cocaine.

   **Usage:** Multicultural-London-English; slang
