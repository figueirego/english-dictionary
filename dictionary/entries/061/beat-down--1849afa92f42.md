## Verb · Record 1

### Forms

- beats down — present; singular; third-person
- beating down — participle; present
- beat down — past
- beaten down — participle; past

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see beat, down.

   **Example:** The transmission tower was beaten down by the storm.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To forcefully diminish the power or influence of; to quell; to squash.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The government tried to beat down the opposition movement.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. (of the sun) To shine brightly and radiate with intense heat.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** We had to leave the beach because the sun was really beating down.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. (of rain) To strike with great force.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** It was a ghastly morning, with the rain beating down in sheets.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To wear (someone) out by repeated actions that overwhelm one's patience or strength.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** She continued to badger until his friend did what he said. Eventually, she beat him down and he gave in.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To haggle with (someone) to sell at a lower price.

   **Usage:** informal; transitive

   **Example:** I managed to beat him down to half his original asking price.

   **Example:** We beat the price down to 50 dollars.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To severely beat someone up.

   **Usage:** slang; transitive
