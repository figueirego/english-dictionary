### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person with poor judgment or little intelligence.

   **Usage:** derogatory

   **Example:** You were a fool to cross that busy road without looking.

   **Example:** The village fool threw his own shoes down the well.

   **Source synonyms:** ignoramus; naif; simpleton; simp; goofus; doofus; dimwit; idiot; imbecile; stultus; buffoon; cuckoo; moron; lunatic; maniac

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A jester; a person whose role was to entertain a sovereign and the court (or lower personages).

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A stock character typified by unintelligence, naïveté or lucklessness, usually as a form of comic relief; often used as a source of insight or pathos for the audience, as such characters are generally less bound by social expectations.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** literature; media; publishing

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Someone who has been made a fool of or tricked; dupe.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Someone who derives pleasure from something specified.

   **Usage:** informal

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. An informal greeting akin to buddy, dude, or man.

   **Usage:** slang; chiefly African-American Vernacular

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A particular card in a tarot deck, representing a jester.

   **Usage:** often capitalized Fool

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; mysticism; philosophy; sciences; tarot
