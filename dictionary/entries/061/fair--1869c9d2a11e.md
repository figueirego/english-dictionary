### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Beautiful, of a pleasing appearance, with a pure and fresh quality.

   **Usage:** archaic; literary; original sense

   **Example:** Monday's child is fair of face.

   **Example:** There was once a knight who wooed a fair young maid.

   **Example:** fair sex

   **Source synonyms:** beautiful; pretty; lovely

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Unblemished (figuratively or literally); clean and pure; innocent.

   **Example:** one's fair name

   **Example:** After scratching out and replacing various words in the manuscript, he scribed a fair copy to send to the publisher.

   **Source synonyms:** pure; clean; neat

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Light in color, pale, particularly with regard to skin tone but also referring to blond and red hair.

   **Example:** She had fair hair and blue eyes.

   **Source synonyms:** pale

   **Source antonyms:** swarthy

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Just.

   **Example:** He must be given a fair trial.

   **Source synonyms:** honest; equitable; rightful; clean

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Adequate, reasonable, or decent, but not excellent.

   **Example:** Their performance has been only fair.

   **Example:** The patient was in a fair condition after some treatment.

   **Source synonyms:** OK; okay

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Favorable to a ship's course.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a wind

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Favorable, pleasant.

   **Example:** The weather was fair today.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Favorable, pleasant.
   1. Not overcast; cloudless; clear.

      **Example:** a fair sky

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. Favorable, pleasant.
   1. Free from obstacles or hindrances; unobstructed; unencumbered; open; direct; said of a road, passage, etc.

      **Example:** a fair mark; in fair sight; a fair view

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. Without sudden change of direction or curvature; smooth; flowing; said of the figure of a vessel, and of surfaces, water lines, and other lines.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; manufacturing; shipbuilding

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. Between the baselines.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; baseball; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s12"></a>

**Source sense R1.S12**

12. Taken direct from an opponent's foot, without the ball touching the ground or another player.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; games; hobbies; lifestyle; rugby; sports

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a catch

<a id="r1-s13"></a>

**Source sense R1.S13**

13. Not a no ball.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; cricket; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a ball delivered by the bowler

<a id="r1-s14"></a>

**Source sense R1.S14**

14. Of a coin or die, having equal chance of landing on any side, unbiased.

   **Example:** A fair coin has a 50% chance of landing on heads.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences; statistics
