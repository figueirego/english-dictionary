### Senses

<a id="r3-s1"></a>

**Source sense R3.S1**

1. To compute the average of, especially the arithmetic mean.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** If you average 10, 20, and 24, you get 18.

<a id="r3-s2"></a>

**Source sense R3.S2**

2. Over a period of time or across members of a population, to have or generate a mean value of.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The daily high temperature last month averaged 15°C.

   **Example:** I averaged 75% in my examinations this year.

<a id="r3-s3"></a>

**Source sense R3.S3**

3. To divide among a number, according to a given proportion.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to average a loss

**Source sense R3.S4**

   **Usage:** intransitive
