## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The time of year when the visible of activity in ants spike drastically as temperatures warm up; when ant colonies wake from winter dormancy, experience a surge in population, and become highly active in their search for food, water, and nesting sites; generally considered by homeowners and pest control professionals to be early spring through summer (typically March to August in the Northern Hemisphere).

   **Usage:** informal
