### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A lower-case letter, ('a, b, c, as opposed to A, B, C), normally used in a text.

   **Source synonyms:** minuscule

   **Source antonyms:** capital letter

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A character that was once stored in the bottom half of a compositor's typecase.

   **Usage:** historical

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** media; printing; publishing
