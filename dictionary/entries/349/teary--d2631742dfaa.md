### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of a person, having eyes filled with tears; inclined to cry.

   **Example:** No wonder Ryan was teary; he missed his parents.

   **Source synonyms:** cryey; sobful; weepy; cryey (rare); crysome; lachrymate (literary; rare); lachrymose; larmoyant (rare); maudlin (obsolete); moist; sobful (archaic); tearful; teary; watery; weepy

   **Source antonyms:** dry-eyed

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of eyes, filled with tears.

   **Example:** Wade looked into his girlfriend's teary eyes.

   **Source synonyms:** moist; tear-filled; wet

   **Source antonyms:** dry
