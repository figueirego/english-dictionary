## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A tiny, uninhabited hamlet in Korea, in the center of the demilitarized zone, where the North and South Korean governments meet.

   **Example:** The Armistice meetings held at Panmunjom from 1951 to 1953 resulted in the cessation of hostilities in the peninsula.
