### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To emit heat and light without a flame.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Iron glows red hot when heated to near its melting point.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of a fire&#58; to emit heat and light.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The fire was still glowing after ten hours.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To emit light brightly and steadily as if heated to a high temperature; to shine.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To be very hot; also, to be on fire; to burn.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Of a colour&#58; to be bright; also, of a thing&#58; to have a bright colour.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

   **Example:** The new baby’s room glows with bright, loving colours.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Of a person&#58; to display intense emotion.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

   **Example:** a healthy glow

   **Example:** The zealots glowed with religious fervor.

   **Example:** You are glowing from happiness!

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Of a person's body or a part of it&#58; to feel hot and often to flush (“become suffused with a reddish colour”) as well, due to an emotional response, exertion, etc.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

   **Example:** After their workout, the gymnasts’ faces were glowing.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To be involved in an (chiefly online) undercover sting operation, especially by American federal agencies.

   **Usage:** Internet; US; figuratively; intransitive

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To create a threatening online post that may involve violence, and look suspicious enough to attract a police investigation.

   **Usage:** Internet; US; figuratively; intransitive

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. To emit (flame).

   **Usage:** archaic; obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. To expose (someone) to the authorities.

   **Usage:** Internet; figuratively; transitive

<a id="r1-s12"></a>

**Source sense R1.S12**

12. To make (something) hot; to heat.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

## Noun · Etymology 1 · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ɡləʊ/ — source labels: Received-Pronunciation
- IPA: /ɡloʊ/ — source labels: General-American

### Forms

- glows — plural

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A state of heat and light being emitted by a hot object.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. A state of heat being emitted by a person or an animal's body.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. A state of light being emitted by something (for example, a bioluminescent animal or fungus, or a mineral) which is not hot; luminescence.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. A state of brightness or warmth of colour; specifically, a reddish colour on a person's face indicating health or youth; a flush.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

   **Example:** He had a bright red glow on his face.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. A condition of being passionate or having warm feelings; an ardour.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable
