## Noun

### Forms

- statute miles — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The mile of 5,280 feet or 1,760 yards, distinguished from the traditional mile of 5,000 feet employed before the 1593 Weights and Measures Act.

   **Usage:** UK

   **Source synonyms:** international mile; survey mile

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The US survey mile, distinguished from the international mile adopted by treaty in the 1950s.

   **Usage:** US

   **Source synonyms:** survey mile; international mile

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The land mile in its various forms, distinguished from the nautical mile.

   **Source synonyms:** land mile
