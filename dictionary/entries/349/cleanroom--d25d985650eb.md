### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A room or environment that is controlled in such a way as to minimize airborne particulate matter, typically for the purpose of fabricating sensitive electronic or other devices.

   **Source synonyms:** white room

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The method of copying a design by reverse engineering and then recreating it without infringing any copyrights.

   **Usage:** attributive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences
