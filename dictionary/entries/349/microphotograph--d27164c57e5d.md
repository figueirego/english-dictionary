### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A photograph so reduced in size that it must be viewed through a lens or a microscope.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A photograph taken through a microscope, an enlarged picture of a very small item or area&#58; a photomicrograph.
