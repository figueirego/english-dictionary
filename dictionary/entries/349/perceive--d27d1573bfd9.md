### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To become aware of, through the physical senses, to see; to understand.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To interpret something in a particular way.

   **Example:** John was perceived to be a coward by his comrades
