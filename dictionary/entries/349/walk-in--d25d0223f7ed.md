### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A facility or room which may be walked into&#58;
   1. A relatively small room (such as a closet or pantry) or refrigerator or freezer that is spacious enough to walk into.

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A facility or room which may be walked into&#58;
   1. A relatively larger room or (especially) an apartment that is entered directly, not via an intervening passage or lobby.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A facility or room which may be walked into&#58;
   1. A facility or an event that principally handles customers who do not have an appointment.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A facility or room which may be walked into&#58;
   1. A facility accessed on foot rather than by car, usually contrasted to drive-in.

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Someone who walks in (to a place, etc)&#58;
   1. A customer, job applicant or similar who visits a restaurant, medical facility, car dealership, etc. without a reservation, appointment, or referral.

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Someone who walks in (to a place, etc)&#58;
   1. A defector (or similar) who walks into an embassy (etc) unannounced.

7. A demonstration or protest in which the participants assemble outside a facility, gain media exposure, and enter the facility in unison.
