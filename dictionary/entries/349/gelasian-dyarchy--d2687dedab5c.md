### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The doctrine, traced back to Pope Gelasius I, that there are two authorities governing human affairs, the spiritual authority of the church and the temporal authority of the empire or secular government.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Catholicism; Christianity; Roman-Catholicism; government; politics
