### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The chemical element (symbol O) with an atomic number of 8 and relative atomic mass of 15.9994. It is a colorless and odorless gas. Sometimes called elemental oxygen to distinguish it from molecular oxygen.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** By molar fraction, calcium oxide contains equal parts calcium and oxygen.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

   **Source synonyms:** sourstuff; E948\[packaging gas\]\]&gt;

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Molecular oxygen (O₂), a colorless, odorless gas at room temperature.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; loose but very common; even in formal use; loose but very common; even in formal use

   **Example:** About 21% of the air you breathe is oxygen.

   **Source synonyms:** dioxygen; mul&#58;O₂

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A mixture of oxygen and other gases, administered to a patient to help them breathe.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An atom of this element.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A condition or environment in which something can thrive.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

   **Example:** Silence is the oxygen of shame.

   **Example:** They hoped to starve the terrorists of the oxygen of publicity.
