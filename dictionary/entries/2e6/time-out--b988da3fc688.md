### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A short break in the action of a sport, for substitution, consultation, etc.

   **Example:** We're being beaten! We need a time-out!

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A break from a tense, heated or stressful situation (often enforced, sometimes as a disciplinary measure); a cooling-off period.

   **Usage:** childish

   **Example:** I'm going to take a brief time-out from this job.

   **Example:** Hitting people is not acceptable! Go to your room and take a time-out!

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A perhaps temporary break in a relationship.

   **Usage:** informal

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The abortion of an incomplete task after a time limit considered long enough for it to end normally.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** communication; communications
