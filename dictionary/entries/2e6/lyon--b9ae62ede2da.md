### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The third largest city in France; the capital of Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A metropolitan authority in Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes, France, outside of Rhône department, which includes the city of Lyon and most of its suburbs; in full, the Metropolis of Lyon.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
