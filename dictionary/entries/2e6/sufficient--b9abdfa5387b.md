### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An adequate quantity of; enough.

   **Example:** We have sufficient supplies to last the winter.

   **Example:** There is not sufficient access to the Internet in many small rural villages.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A quantity (of something) that is as large as is needed.

   **Example:** We don't need any more; we already have sufficient.

   **Example:** Sufficient of us are against this idea that we should stop now.

   **Source context (verbatim):** as pronoun

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Of a type or kind that suffices, that satisfies requirements or needs.

   **Example:** This is a necessary condition but not a sufficient one.
