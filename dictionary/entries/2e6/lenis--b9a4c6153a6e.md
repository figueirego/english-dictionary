**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Relatively weakly articulated, hence unaspirated and possibly voiced; especially as compared to the others of a group of homorganic consonants.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Unaspirated \[p\] is lenis in Danish, the corresponding fortis consonant being \[pʰ\]. The same sound is fortis in French, the corresponding lenis consonant being \[b\].
