## Adjective · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Situated or lying across; side to side, relative to some defined "forward" direction; perpendicular or slanted relative to the "forward" direction; identified with movement across areas.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source antonyms:** longitudinal; crosswise

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Made at right angles to the long axis of the body.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. (of an intersection) Not tangent, so that a nondegenerate angle is formed between the two things intersecting. (For the general definition, see Transversality (mathematics) on Wikipedia.Wikipedia )

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Not in direct line of descent; collateral.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; obsolete
