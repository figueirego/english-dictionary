## Adjective · Record 3

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈnɔɹmən/ — source labels: General-American
- IPA: /ˈnɔːmən/ — source labels: Received-Pronunciation

### Senses

<a id="r3-s1"></a>

**Source sense R3.S1**

1. Of or pertaining to Normandy or its inhabitants (present or past).

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r3-s2"></a>

**Source sense R3.S2**

2. Relating to the Norman language or the dialect of French spoken in Normandy.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r3-s3"></a>

**Source sense R3.S3**

3. Relating to the Romanesque architecture developed by the Normans after the Norman Conquest, characterized by large arches and heavy columns.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r3-s4"></a>

**Source sense R3.S4**

4. Having a counterintuitive design that confuses users about proper operation; after Don Norman, author of The Design of Everyday Things (1988).

   **Usage:** attributive; not-comparable

   **Example:** Darn Norman door! I thought I had to push, but I actually had to pull.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** arts; design
