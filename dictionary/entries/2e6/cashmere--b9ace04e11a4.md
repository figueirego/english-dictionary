### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Fine, downy wool from beneath the outer hair of the Cashmere goat.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A soft fabric made of this wool.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** a cashmere sweater

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A rich kind of shawl made from this wool.

   **Usage:** countable; usually

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any similar shawl.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; usually
