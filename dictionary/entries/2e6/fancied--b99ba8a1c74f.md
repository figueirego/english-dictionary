## Adjective · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈfænsɪd/ — source labels: General-American; Received-Pronunciation

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Existing only in the fancy (“imagination”) or by social convention; imaginary, imagined; supposed; perceived rather than real.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The plant was named for its fancied resemblance to a Roman spear.

   **Source synonyms:** conceptual; fanciful; ideal; notional

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Organized, or (especially of attire) designed, to suit one's fancy or whim.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Having a fancy (“inclination, liking, or love”) for; desired, favourite.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Predicted to win or succeed.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** the fancied team in the tournament

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports

   **Source antonyms:** unfancied

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Designed in an artistic manner.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; obsolete
