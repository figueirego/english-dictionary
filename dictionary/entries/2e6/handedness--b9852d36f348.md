**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A preference (usually innate) for using one hand rather than the other.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The property that distinguishes an asymmetric object from its mirror image. For example, the essential difference between a left and right glove.
