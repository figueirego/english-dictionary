### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A місце́ва ра́да (mistseva rada) centred in a village (село́) or, historically, a rural-type settlement (се́лище \[сільсько́го ти́пу\]); a Ukrainian selsoviet.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A місце́ва ра́да (mistseva rada) centred in a village (село́) or, historically, a rural-type settlement (се́лище \[сільсько́го ти́пу\]); a Ukrainian selsoviet.
   1. A Ukrainian collegial body charged with the administration of one or more rural inhabited localities and its or their associated territory; a Ukrainian rural council.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A місце́ва ра́да (mistseva rada) centred in a village (село́) or, historically, a rural-type settlement (се́лище \[сільсько́го ти́пу\]); a Ukrainian selsoviet.
   1. The municipal building in which a Ukrainian rural council holds its meetings and conducts its business; a Ukrainian village hall.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A місце́ва ра́да (mistseva rada) centred in a village (село́) or, historically, a rural-type settlement (се́лище \[сільсько́го ти́пу\]); a Ukrainian selsoviet.
   1. The administrative–territorial unit headed by such a Ukrainian rural council.

      **Usage:** historical outside the Crimea
