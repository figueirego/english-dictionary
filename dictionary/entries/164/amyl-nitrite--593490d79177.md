### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A yellowish volatile synthetic liquid C₅H₁₁NO₂ of commercial amyl alcohol and nitrous acid that is used medicinally as a vasodilator. It is rapidly absorbed by the body on inhalation, and is sometimes used recreationally especially as an aphrodisiac.

   **Usage:** uncountable
