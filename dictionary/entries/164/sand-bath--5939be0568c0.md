## Noun

### Forms

- sand baths — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A piece of laboratory equipment consisting of a container filled with heated sand, that is used to provide an even source of heat.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A bath in which the body is immersed in hot sand.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The rolling of birds in sand, by which they dust themselves over to cleanse the skin and feathers; the act of pulverizing.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of many birds and mammals
