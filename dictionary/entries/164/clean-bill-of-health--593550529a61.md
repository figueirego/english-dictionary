## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A bill of health that states that there is no infectious disease present in a ship, or its port of departure.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A statement that indicates that there are no obvious problems.

   **Usage:** broadly; figuratively

   **Example:** Now that we have changed the brake pads, the oil and the spark plugs, we can give this car a clean bill of health.
