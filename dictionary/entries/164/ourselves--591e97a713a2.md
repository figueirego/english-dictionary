### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Us; the group including the speaker as the object of a verb or preposition when that group also is the subject.

   **Usage:** reflexive

   **Example:** We should keep this for ourselves.

   **Example:** Let's not beat ourselves up over this.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Intensifies the subject as the group including the speaker, especially to indicate that no one else satisfies the predicate or needs to be included.

   **Usage:** emphatic

   **Example:** We did it ourselves.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Referring to everyone being addressed.

   **Usage:** colloquial

   **Example:** Come on everyone! Let's get ourselves up and out of bed, please.

   **Example:** Now then children, I'm not going to help you with the next problem. We should be able to work this one out ourselves.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Referring to an individual person being addressed, especially a person in the speaker's care; also ourself.

   **Usage:** colloquial; may seem patronising

   **Example:** Hello Mrs Miggins. Did we manage to wash ourselves this morning?

   **Example:** Well done Timmy! Did we make that model ourselves?

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** usself (dialectal; obsolete); weself (Jamaican; Scottish)
