## Noun · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈɹɪp(ə)l/ — source labels: UK; US

### Forms

- ripples — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A moving disturbance, or undulation, in the surface of a fluid.

   **Example:** I dropped a small stone into the pond and watched the ripples spread.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One of a series of corrugations in flat surface.

   **Example:** The ebbing tide had left ripples in the sand.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A sound similar to that of undulating water.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A style of ice cream in which flavors have been coarsely blended together.

   **Example:** I enjoy fudge ripple ice cream, but I especially like to dig through the carton to get at the ripple part and eat only that.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A small oscillation of an otherwise steady signal.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; electrical-engineering; electricity; electromagnetism; electronics; energy; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A small spreading change, impact, or effect produced by a larger or more consequential action.

   **Usage:** figuratively
