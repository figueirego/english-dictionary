## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

- countersinks — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A conical recess, typically machined at the mouth of a hole to admit a screw (with a matching taper) so that the screw sits flush with a surface.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The cutter used to cut such a recess. (Also used, at less depth, for edge-breaking/deburring.)

   **Source synonyms:** countersinker

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To create such a conical recess.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to countersink a hole for a screw

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To cause to sink even with or below the surface.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** to countersink a screw or bolt into woodwork
