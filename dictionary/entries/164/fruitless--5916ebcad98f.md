### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Bearing no fruit; barren.

   **Source antonyms:** fruitful

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Unproductive, useless.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** The unskilled man’s attempt at fixing his car engine was fruitless.

   **Source synonyms:** bootless; barren; futile; sterile; fruitless; gainless; idle; dry; ineffectual; inefficacious; inutile (obsolete); no good; otiose; pointless; profitless; purposeless; tired (African-American-Vernacular-English; derogatory); unavailing; unprofitable; unuseful; unutilizable; useless; vain; void

   **Source antonyms:** fruitful; effective; efficacious; productive; useful

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of a person&#58; unable to have children; barren, infertile.

   **Usage:** archaic; figuratively

   **Example:** The fruitless woman desperately wanted to have children.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Of a diet, etc.&#58; without fruit.

   **Usage:** rare
