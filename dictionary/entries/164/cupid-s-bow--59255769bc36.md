## Noun

### Forms

- Cupid's bows — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The archery bow borne by Cupid in artistic representations, which curves inward at the centre and often outward at the ends.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The curve of the upper lip which resembles such a bow; the philtrum.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A similar curve carved in woodwork, etc.
