<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Sharp, barbed; not dull.

   **Usage:** comparable

   **Example:** The warrior brandished a pointed spear.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Having a certain number of points.

   **Usage:** in-compounds

   **Example:** a five-pointed star

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. Having a relevance to the matter at hand&#58; pertinent, relevant.

   **Usage:** comparable

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. In animals, having a coat pattern with points, that is, darkening of the extremities.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The Siamese is a pointed breed of cat.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

   **Usage:** comparable

   **Example:** a pointed remark
