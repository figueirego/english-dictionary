### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To proceed with; to begin or continue, especially after an interruption.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Turn off the television and get on with your homework.

   **Example:** Now that the technical difficulties have been taken care of, let's get on with the show!

   **Example:** I have to get on with my spring cleaning.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To have a good relationship with.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Peter is not easy to get on with.

   **Source synonyms:** get along with

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To successfully use or adapt to.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I just can't get on with these new glasses.
