### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To pinch and pull with a sudden jerk and twist; to twitch.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to tweak the nose.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To adjust slightly; to fine-tune.

   **Usage:** informal; transitive

   **Example:** If we tweak the colors towards blue, it will look more natural.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To tease, to annoy; to get under the skin of (someone, typically so as to irritate them, or by extension to enamor, frighten, etc).

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To abuse methamphetamines, especially crystal meth.

   **Usage:** US; intransitive; slang

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To exhibit extreme nervousness, evasiveness when confronted by authorities, compulsiveness, erratic motion, excitability, etc, due to or mimicking the symptoms of methamphetamine abuse.

   **Usage:** US; intransitive; slang

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To be extremely confused; to have no clue what is happening.

   **Usage:** broadly; intransitive; slang

   **Example:** Am I tweaking or does 2020 feel like 6 months ago?

   **Source synonyms:** trip

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. From a catapult, to strike a target with a missile.

   **Usage:** uncommon

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; government; military; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; politics; tools; war; weaponry
