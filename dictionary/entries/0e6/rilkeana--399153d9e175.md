### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Belongings, manuscripts, and other items reminding of the Austrian poet Rainer Maria Rilke.

   **Usage:** plural; plural-only

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Poetry composed in a manner reminiscent of Rainer Maria Rilke, or else dealing with themes similar to those of Rilke's poetry.

   **Usage:** plural; plural-only

   **Example:** 'Rilkeana&#58; variações elegíacas' ('Rilkeana&#58; Elegiac Variations') is a 1999 collection of poems by Ana Hatherly first published in Lisbon by Assírio &amp; Alvim.
