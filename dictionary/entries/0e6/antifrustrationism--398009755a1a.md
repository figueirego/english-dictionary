### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The axiological view that frustrated preferences create a negative moral 'debit' which can be canceled out through their satisfaction. Thus, satisfying a desire that already exists is good, but creating a new satisfied desire is not.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; philosophy; sciences
