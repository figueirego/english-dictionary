### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To manage or organise the operation of a theatrical stage; to act as stage manager.

   **Usage:** literally

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To carefully control or direct (an event, operation, etc.) from the background, especially in a calculating manner, so as to achieve a particular desired appearance or outcome.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** Bad practices were rife at the factory, but the boss stage-managed the inspectors' visit so that they did not see anything amiss.
