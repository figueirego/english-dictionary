### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A gossip.

   **Usage:** derogatory; informal

   **Example:** If it weren't for that blabbermouth Sally, the principal would never have known we did it.

   **Source synonyms:** carrytale; blab; blabbermouth; blabtale (obsolete); blabtongue (obsolete); busybody; buzzer (obsolete); gossip; gossiper; gossipmonger; meddler; mouth; mumblenews (obsolete); newsmonger; rumormonger; rumourmonger; scandalmonger; sleazemonger; tabby (archaic); talebearer; talecarrier; tattletale; telltale; telltale tit (childish); titivil (obsolete); tittle-tattle; town crier; whisperer

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person who talks excessively.

   **Usage:** derogatory; informal

   **Source synonyms:** airbag; babblemouth; babbler; bag of wind; blab; blabberer; blatherer; blabbermouth; blatherskite; bloviator; chatterbag; chatter-basket; chatterbox; chatterer; Chatty Cathy; chewet (obsolete); gabbler; gasbag; jabberer; jangler; Mag; motormouth; patterer; prater; prattler; rattle; tongue-pad (obsolete); twaddler; windbag; yapper; yakker
