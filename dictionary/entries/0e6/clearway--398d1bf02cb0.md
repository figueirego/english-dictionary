### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A road or other path that is kept free of obstructions, or on which stopping is strictly regulated, in order to maintain free flow of movement.

   **Usage:** Australia; New-Zealand; UK

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An area projecting from the end of a runway that is kept clear of obstructions exceeding a height that increases with distance from the end of the runway.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** aeronautics; aerospace; aviation; business; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences
