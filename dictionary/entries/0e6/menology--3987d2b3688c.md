### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The study of months; the names and system of months within a given calendar.

   **Usage:** uncommon

   **Example:** The Romans themselves believed Romulus had given them a 10-month lunar calendar, but modern scholars consider the actual original state of Roman menology uncertain.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Synonym of menologium, a monthly record, particularly in Assyriology and certain Christian contexts.

   **Usage:** capitalized; often

   **Example:** The Menology of St Symeon mostly repeats earlier accounts but includes some small additions as well.

   **Source synonyms:** menologium (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The content of a menologium, a liturgical calendar or hagiography.

   **Usage:** rare

   **Example:** The menologies of St Symeon mostly repeat earlier accounts but include some small additions as well.
