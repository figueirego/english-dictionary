### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The details of the food to be served at a banquet; a bill of fare.

   **Example:** After the wedding celebration was over the menu was displayed for dinner.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A list containing the food and beverages served at a restaurant, café, or bar. Menus may be printed on paper sheets provided to customers, put on a large poster or display board inside the establishment, displayed outside the restaurant, or digital.

   **Example:** The server handed us the menu.

   **Example:** We selected duck pâté and chicken goujons from the menu.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The food or drinks that are available in a restaurant, café, or bar.

   **Example:** They took the chicken sandwich off their menu.

   **Example:** We've added a few new dishes to our menu.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A list from which the user may select an operation to be performed, often done with a keyboard, mouse, or controller under a graphical user interface.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; games; gaming; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A list or agenda.

   **Example:** What's on the menu for today's meeting?
