## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Experienced, well-informed investors, gamblers, etc. considered as a group.

   **Usage:** collective; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance

   **Source antonyms:** dumb money

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The money invested or bet by such people; by extension, the opinions of such people.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** The smart money is on a half percent cut in the basic bank lending rates before the end of the week.

   **Source antonyms:** dumb money

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Money paid by a person to buy himself off from some unpleasant engagement or some painful situation.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Money allowed to soldiers or sailors, in the English service, for wounds and injuries received; also, a sum paid by a recruit, previous to being sworn in, to procure his release from service.

   **Usage:** historical; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Vindictive or exemplary damages; damages beyond a full compensation for the actual injury done.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law
