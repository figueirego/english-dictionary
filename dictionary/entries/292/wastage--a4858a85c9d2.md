### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The amount or proportion of something that is wasted or lost by deterioration or other natural process.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** The average wastage is 1.5% in the grocery department.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The periodical turnover of personnel in an organisation by death, retirement or resignation, as perceived by those aspiring to promotion or appointment in the organisation.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Anything lost by wear or waste.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Goods that are damaged, out of date, reduced, or generally unsaleable, which are destined to be thrown away and which are written off as a loss.

   **Usage:** uncountable
