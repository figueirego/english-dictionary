<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. plural of ephemeron
   1. Manmade objects that are short-lived (under usual conditions), usually because their design intent is to be inexpensively made and thus affordably bought, a goal that often is an engineering trade-off versus durability.
      1. Especially, such objects that are collectibles, such as (originally) cheap publications (e.g., pamphlets, brochures), single-use packaging from packaged goods, and giveaway or throwaway trinkets.

         **Example:** vintage ephemera

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. plural of ephemeron
   1. Manmade objects that are short-lived (under usual conditions), usually because their design intent is to be inexpensively made and thus affordably bought, a goal that often is an engineering trade-off versus durability.
      1. Published single-sheet or single-page documents which are meant to be thrown away after one use.

         **Usage:** library science

4. plural of ephemeron
   1. Manmade objects that are short-lived (under usual conditions), usually because their design intent is to be inexpensively made and thus affordably bought, a goal that often is an engineering trade-off versus durability.
      1. Transitory audiovisual matter not intended to be retained or preserved.

         **Usage:** broadly
