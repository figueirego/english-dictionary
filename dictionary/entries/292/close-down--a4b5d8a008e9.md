### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To stop trading as a business.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** The local factory will close down soon, unless sales pick up.

   **Example:** They had to close the mine down as it was in a dangerous condition.

   **Source synonyms:** close one's doors; close up; shut down; shutter; shut up

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To surround someone, as to impede their movement.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** If anyone passes to Smith, close him down.

   **Source synonyms:** shut down
