### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Rapid and without delay.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** a snappy response

   **Source synonyms:** fast; immediate; quick

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Irritable.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** You're snappy this morning; did you not sleep well?

   **Source synonyms:** peevish; testy; tetchy

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Tidy; well-dressed; sharp.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** Here he is, looking snappy in his brand-new suit.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Chilly, brisk, sharp.

   **Example:** snappy weather; snappy pace; snappy rejoinder

   **Source synonyms:** nippy; parky

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Tending to snap or bite.

   **Example:** a snappy dog

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Clever, catchy.

   **Example:** a snappy motto
