## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A process similar to wattle and daub, once common in Lincolnshire, involving a simple frame of upright studs joined by cross rails at the tops and bottoms, then having thin staves of ash attached, and daubed with a mixture of mud, straw, hair and dung.

   **Usage:** historical; uncountable
