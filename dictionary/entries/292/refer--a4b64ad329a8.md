### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To direct the attention of (someone toward something).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The shop assistant referred me to the help desk on ground floor.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To submit to (another person or group) for consideration; to send or direct elsewhere.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He referred the matter to the principal.

   **Example:** The doctor may refer patients to a psychiatrist.

   **Example:** I'll refer the objections back to management for reconsideration.

   **Source synonyms:** send

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To place in or under by a mental or rational process; to assign to, as a class, a cause, source, a motive, reason, or ground of explanation.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He referred the phenomena to electrical disturbances.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To mention (something); to direct attention (to something).

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** To explain the problem, the teacher referred to an example in another textbook.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To make reference to; to be about; to relate to; to regard; to allude to.

   **Usage:** intransitive; stative

   **Example:** The recipe referred to several unusual ingredients.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To be referential to another element in a sentence.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** grammar; human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To point to either a specific location in computer memory or to a specific object.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** In C, the pointer obtained by &amp;a refers to the variable a.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; programming; sciences

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To require to resit an examination.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Smith's marks in the finals were unsatisfactory and he was referred.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** education

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To have the meaning of, to denote.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The nickname "Big Apple" refers to the city of New York.

   **Example:** In programming, a "memory leak" refers to a situation where memory is or stays unnecessarily allocated.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a term or name

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. To cause (pain) to be felt elsewhere.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. To apparently relocate; to be felt in another place than where it is actually caused.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

   **Source context (verbatim):** of the sensation of pain
