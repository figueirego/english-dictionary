**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A box used to provide a soiling area for (usually) pet cats, generally filled with sand, clay chips, etc.

   **Example:** Pregnant cat owners are often advised to have someone else clean the cat box to reduce the risk of contracting toxoplasmosis.
