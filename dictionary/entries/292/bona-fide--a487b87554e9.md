### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. In good faith; sincere; without deception or ulterior motive.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Although he failed, the prime minister made a bona fide attempt to repair the nation's damaged economy.

   **Source synonyms:** sincere

   **Source antonyms:** mala fide

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Genuine; not counterfeit.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** This is a bona fide Roman coin.
