### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To cover with a hard crust.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** iron encrusted with rust

   **Example:** a doughnut encrusted with sugar

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To form a crust.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To inset or affix decorative materials upon (a surface); to inlay into, as a piece of carving or other ornamental object.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The jeweller encrusted the ring with gems.
