### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To give up or cravenly abandon something difficult.

   **Usage:** intransitive; slang

   **Example:** When the going got tough, he punked out.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To treat as a punk (either as a worthless person or the bottom in a male-male relationship, especially a nonconsenting prison relationship).

   **Usage:** slang; transitive; almost always used in the passive voice

   **Example:** He knew if he didn't stand up for himself, he'd be punked out by someone stronger.
