### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of colliding or crashing.

   **Example:** The skateboarder had a nasty wipeout as she came off the ramp.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Total destruction or elimination.

   **Example:** The wipeout of this industry could be catastrophic.
