### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The first element of a full name, particularly when it could also be surname in East Asian and other cultural traditions.

   **Example:** In a structure typical for East Asia cultures, first names are usually used as family names. It usually identifies that person is a one of the members of a group (typically a family or clan) who have a common family name.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Synonym of given name, particularly when it forms the first element of a full name.
