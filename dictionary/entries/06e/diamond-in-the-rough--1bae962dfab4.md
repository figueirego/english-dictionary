## Noun

### Forms

- diamonds in the rough — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An uncut diamond.

   **Example:** He wants to go try his luck at that state park in Arkansas where you can look for diamonds in the rough and you can keep what you find.

   **Source synonyms:** rough diamond

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person or thing whose goodness or other positive qualities are hidden by a harsh or unremarkable surface appearance.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** Her auto mechanic is a diamond in the rough&#58; tough-talking but honest, even generous.

   **Source synonyms:** rough diamond
