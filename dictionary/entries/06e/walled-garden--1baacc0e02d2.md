## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A garden enclosed by walls for purposes of shelter or decoration.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any closed platform. A closed platform is any computing system where the manufacturer retains monopolistic control over installation, distribution, and use of software and services.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A closed platform for content or services controlled by a single carrier or service provider, typically requiring subscription to access.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; media; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; technology

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**
