### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To refuse or (intentionally) fail to adhere to (a rule) or to obey an order from (somebody).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** disobey the rules

   **Example:** disobey your parents

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To refuse or (intentionally) fail to obey.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Under an authoritarian regime, people have an obligation to disobey.
