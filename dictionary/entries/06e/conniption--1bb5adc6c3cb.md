### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A fit of anger or panic; conniption fit.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** When she came downstairs and saw what her children were eating, she had a conniption.

   **Source synonyms:** tantrum; tantrum; catfit (US; dialectal); conniption; conniption fit; dummy spit; headfit (British; slang); hissy (US); hissy fit (US); meltdown (figuratively); melty; paddy; shit fit (US); snit fit (US); wobbler (British; slang); wobbly (British; slang)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A fit of laughing; convulsion.

   **Example:** The joke was not that funny, but he went into conniptions laughing.

   **Source synonyms:** the giggles
