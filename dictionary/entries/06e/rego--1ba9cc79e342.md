### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Registration for a motor vehicle.

   **Usage:** Australia; New-Zealand; colloquial; uncountable; usually

   **Example:** The police pulled me over for driving with an expired rego.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The fee required for such registration.

   **Usage:** Australia; New-Zealand; colloquial; uncountable; usually

   **Example:** David couldn′t drive his car as he hadn′t paid his rego.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The registration number of a motor vehicle, used by police to access registration details such as the identity of the owner.

   **Usage:** Australia; New-Zealand; colloquial; countable; usually
