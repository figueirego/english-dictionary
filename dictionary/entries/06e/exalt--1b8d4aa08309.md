<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To honor; to hold in high esteem; to praise or worship.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The LORD is exalted, for he dwells on high.

   **Example:** They exalted their queen.

   **Source synonyms:** extol; laud; worthy; aggrandize; applaud; enthrone (literary); appraise; belaud (rare); bepraise; commend; cry up; eulogize; exalt; extol; glorify; honor; idolize; laud; lionize; magnify; panegyrize; praise; puff; revere; speak well of; transfigure; venerate; worthy; adore; exalt; glorify; praise; worship

2. To raise in rank, status etc., to elevate.

   **Source synonyms:** aggrandize; dignify; ennoble; nobilitate; upgrade; advance; aggrandize; dignify; elevate; ennoble; exalt; honor; nobilitate; prefer (dated); promote; raise; uplift
