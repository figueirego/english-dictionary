### Pronunciation

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Insane, crazy.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** You're out of your mind if you think you can jump that far.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Temporarily mentally unstable; very distressed.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** When you didn't phone home, we were out of our minds with worry.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Away from the focus of one's thoughts.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** Try to put the day's troubles out of your mind and relax.
