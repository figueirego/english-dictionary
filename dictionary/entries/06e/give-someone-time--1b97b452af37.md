### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To grant an individual a sufficient amount of time to finish doing something; especially, to modify their viewpoint or attitude.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** They'll come around. Just give them time.

   **Example:** A&#58; I'll get there. Just give me some time. B&#58; Take all the time you need. I'll be here when you're ready.

   **Example:** When you feed these animals their grain, you have to give them enough time to eat it before you turn them all out into the paddock.
