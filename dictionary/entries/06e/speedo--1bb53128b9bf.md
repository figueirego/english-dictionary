### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A tight-fitting swimsuit, especially commonly worn by competitive swimmers and divers. Usually implies a brief or bikini style swimsuit.

   **Usage:** US

   **Example:** I could tell that she was a serious swimmer, because every time I saw her at the pool she was wearing a Speedo of one design or another.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. (by restriction) A male swimsuit, very tight fitting and small, brief-style, and not a thong-type.

   **Usage:** US

   **Example:** She called down from upstairs, "He'll be out in a minute&#58; he's just putting his Speedo on."
