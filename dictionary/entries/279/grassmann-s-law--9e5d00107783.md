### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A dissimilatory phonological process in Ancient Greek and Sanskrit, according to which an aspirated consonant loses its aspiration if it is followed by another aspirated consonant in the next syllable.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; phonology; sciences
