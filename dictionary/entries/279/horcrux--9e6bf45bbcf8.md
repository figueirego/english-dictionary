<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In Harry Potter, an object in which a wizard has concealed a part of their soul through magic, rendering them immortal until the object is irreparably damaged or destroyed.

   **Source synonyms:** phylactery

<a id="r1-s2"></a>
