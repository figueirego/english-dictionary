### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who traffics in commodities for profit.

   **Source synonyms:** trader

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The owner or operator of a retail business.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A trading vessel; a merchantman.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Someone who is noted for a stated type of activity or behaviour.

   **Usage:** derogatory; informal; sometimes

   **Example:** He's some kind of speed merchant — he drives way too fast.

   **Example:** Goal merchant Smith scored twice again in the match against Mudchester Rovers.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A supercargo.

   **Usage:** obsolete
