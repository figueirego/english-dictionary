## Noun

### Forms

- originalists — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who has, or tends to have, original ideas.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One who aims to discover how the writers of a document intended it to be interpreted, and to interpret it in that way.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. One who aims to discover how the writers of a document intended it to be interpreted, and to interpret it in that way.
   1. Specifically, one who aims to discover how the writers of the Jewish and Christian scriptures intended them to be interpreted, and to interpret them in that way.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** lifestyle; religion; theology

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. One who aims to discover how the writers of a document intended it to be interpreted, and to interpret it in that way.
   1. Specifically, one who aims to discover how the writers of the Constitution of the United States intended it to be interpreted, and to interpret it in that way.

      **Usage:** US

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; politics
