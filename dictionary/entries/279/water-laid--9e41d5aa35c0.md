### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Consisting of three or more hawser-laid ropes twisted together in the opposite direction from that in which the hawser-laid ropes are twisted; cable-laid.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; rope-making
