### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To do (something) quickly, in a slipshod, or unprofessional manner, especially performing or composing music or a piece of writing.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** I've got this paper due tomorrow, but I think I can bang it out in one night.

   **Example:** The band were banging out a vaguely recognisable version of the Star Spangled Banner.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To eject.

   **Usage:** intransitive; slang

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** aeronautics; aerospace; aviation; business; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To celebrate (a fellow printing or newspaper industry worker) to mark their completion of an apprenticeship or their retirement by (formerly) hitting metal furniture in the printing room or (more recently) hitting one's desk in the newsroom.

   **Usage:** UK; transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** media; printing; publishing

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To beat up (someone).

   **Usage:** transitive
