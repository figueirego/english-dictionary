### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To give up, surrender, or yield (e.g. a weapon), usually by placing it on the ground.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The police urged the gunman to lay down his weapon.

   **Example:** Lay down your arms.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To place or install on the ground, e.g. a railway on a trackbed.

   **Example:** lay down a mat; lay down asphalt; lay down a road

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To intentionally take a fall while riding a motorcycle, in order to prevent a more serious collision.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He laid down his brand-new Harley-Davidson to avoid the oncoming bus.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To specify, institute, enact, assert firmly, state authoritatively, establish or formulate (rules or policies).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Let's lay down the rules right at the beginning, so we are consistent.

   **Example:** You've got to lay down the law with that boy.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To stock, store (e.g. wine) for the future. See also lay by.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To kill (someone).

   **Usage:** dated; transitive; African-American Vernacular

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To euthanize an animal.

   **Usage:** euphemistic; transitive

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To sacrifice, especially in the phrase "to lay down one's life."

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To lie down.

   **Usage:** intransitive; proscribed

   **Example:** I feel a bit ill, so I'm going to go lay down for a while.

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. To draw the lines of a ship's hull at full size, before starting a build.

   **Usage:** dated

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. To place a sheet in a printing press for printing.

   **Usage:** obsolete

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** media; printing; publishing
