### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To have confidence in someone.
   1. To trust or have faith (in).

      **Usage:** archaic; intransitive

      **Example:** I confide in her judgement in this matter.

      **Example:** You can confide in him doing the right thing.

      **Source context (verbatim):** with in

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To have confidence in someone.
   1. To take (someone) into one's confidence; to speak in secret with.

      **Usage:** intransitive

      **Example:** I could no longer keep this secret alone; I had to confide in my brother.

      **Example:** She confided in me about her plan to rob the bank.

      **Source context (verbatim):** with in

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To entrust (something) to the responsibility of someone.

   **Usage:** dated; transitive

   **Example:** I confide this mission to you alone.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To say (something) in confidence.

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** After several drinks, I confided my problems to the barman.

   **Example:** She confided that her marriage had been in trouble for some time.
