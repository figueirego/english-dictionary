### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. The flowering Andean root vegetable Mirabilis expansa, which was important to the Incas and which survives in cold, windy places several thousand meters above sea level.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**
