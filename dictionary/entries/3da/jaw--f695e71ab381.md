### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One of the bones, usually bearing teeth, which form the framework of the mouth.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The part of the face below the mouth.

   **Example:** His jaw dropped in amazement.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Anything resembling the jaw (sense 1) of an animal in form or action; the mouth or way of entrance.

   **Usage:** especially; figuratively; in-plural

   **Example:** the jaws of a pass; the jaws of darkness; the jaws of death.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A notch or opening.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A notched or forked part, adapted for holding an object in place.

   **Example:** the jaw of a railway-car pedestal.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. One of a pair of opposing parts which are movable towards or from each other, for grasping or crushing anything between them.

   **Example:** the jaws of a vise; the jaws of a stone-crushing machine.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The inner end of a boom or gaff, hollowed in a half circle so as to move freely on a mast.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Impudent, abusive, or worthless talk.

   **Usage:** dated; slang

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. An axle guard.

   **Usage:** slang

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. The curved part of the cushion marking the entry to the pocket.
