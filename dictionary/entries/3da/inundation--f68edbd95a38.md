### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of inundating; an overflow; a flood; a rising and spreading of water over grounds.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The state of being inundated; flooding.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An overflowing or superfluous abundance; a flood; a great influx.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

   **Example:** There is an inundation of tourists in summer, but in winter the town is almost deserted.
