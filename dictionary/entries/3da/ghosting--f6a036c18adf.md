## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

- ghostings — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A method of ending a personal relationship by stopping any contact with the other party and not providing an explanation.

   **Usage:** colloquial; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Stories of ghosting often deal with broken-off romances, but ghosting can end a friendship as well.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The blurry appearance of a television picture resulting from interference caused by multipath reception.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** broadcasting; business; electrical-engineering; electricity; electromagnetism; electronics; energy; engineering; media; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics; television

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A form of identity theft in which someone steals the identity, and sometimes even the role within society, of a specific dead person (the "ghost") who is not widely known to be deceased.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The phenomenon of the writing on one side of a page in a notebook being partly visible on the other side.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** bleed-through; bleeding; print-through

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Ghost imaging.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The practice of hiding prisoners from inspection by outside inspectors.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Their hastily fabricated Potemkin village was practically deserted, what with all the ghosting.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The act or habit of exhaling vape smoke slowly, to prevent others from noticing.

   **Usage:** countable; slang; uncountable

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A problem with a keyboard where certain simultaneous keypresses trigger the action of a further key that was not in fact pressed.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences
