### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. The fifth decade of a century&#58; the 1840s, 1940s, 2040s, etc.

   **Usage:** plural; plural-only

   **Example:** My ma was born in the forties and my pa was born in the seventies.

   **Source synonyms:** '40s; 40s

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. The decade of one's life from age 40 through age 49.

   **Usage:** plural; plural-only

   **Example:** They're both in their forties.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. The range between 40 and 49.

   **Usage:** plural; plural-only; rates

   **Example:** What to wear when the weather is in the forties really depends on whether you're talking Fahrenheit or Celsius.
