### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A thing or animal that makes a chirping sound.

   **Example:** The electronic smoke alarm includes a chirper which sounds when the battery is low.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person who speaks with a distinct English or Welsh accent.

   **Usage:** Canada; derogatory; mildly; slang

   **Example:** I could barely understand a word that chatty chirper said.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
