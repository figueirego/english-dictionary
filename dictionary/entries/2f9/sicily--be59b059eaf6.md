### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The largest island in the Mediterranean Sea, an autonomous region of Italy, close to Africa and separated from Tunisia and Libya by the Strait of Sicily.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Synonym of Kingdom of Sicily, a former kingdom controlling the island and some of southern Italy in the medieval and early modern period

   **Usage:** historical

   **Source synonyms:** Kingdom of Sicily (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Synonym of Kingdom of Naples, a former kingdom controlling southern Italy and claiming sovereignty over the island in the early modern period

   **Usage:** historical

   **Source synonyms:** Kingdom of Naples (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**
