### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The hour being what it is; the hour being so advanced; considering that it is so far in time already; now that so much time has elapsed; if not before, now at the latest.

   **Example:** My parents ought to've arrived by now. I wonder what's keeping them.

   **Example:** Totally overwhelmed by now, I started to hand out the fliers willy-nilly.

   **Example:** By now an angry crowd had collected outside.

   **Example:** No doubt you will have heard the news by now.

   **Source synonyms:** already; at this stage; at this point
