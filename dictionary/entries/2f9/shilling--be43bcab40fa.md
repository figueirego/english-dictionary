## Noun · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈʃɪlɪŋ/ — source labels: General-American; Received-Pronunciation

### Forms

- shillings — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A coin formerly used in the United Kingdom, Ireland, Malta, Australia, New Zealand and many other Commonwealth countries worth twelve old pence, or one twentieth of a pound sterling.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The currency of Kenya, Somalia, Tanzania and Uganda.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A currency in the United States, differing in value between states.

   **Usage:** US; historical

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The Spanish real, formerly having the value of one eighth of a dollar.

   **Usage:** US; historical; New York and some other states

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Alternative form of schilling, a coin formerly used in Germany and German states, worth twelve pfennig.

   **Usage:** alt-of; alternative; historical

   **Alternative form of:** [schilling](../251/schilling--94401aeb0e35.md)

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Alternative form of skilling, a coin formerly used in Denmark, Norway or Sweden, worth twelve penning.

   **Usage:** alt-of; alternative; historical

   **Alternative form of:** [skilling](../261/skilling--98672d899365.md)
