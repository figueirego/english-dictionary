### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A scheme for the distribution of prizes by lot or chance, especially a gaming scheme in which one or more tickets bearing particular numbers draw prizes, the other tickets are blanks.

   **Example:** Dave plays the lottery almost every week, but always picks different numbers.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Something decided by chance.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** It's a bit of a lottery whether we'll get a good table in that diner.
