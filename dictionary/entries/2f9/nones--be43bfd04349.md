## Noun · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /nəʊnz/ — source labels: UK

### Forms

- nones — plural
- Nones — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The notional first-quarter day of a Roman month, occurring on the 7th day of the four original 31-day months (March, May, Quintilis or July, and October) and on the 5th day of all other months.

   **Usage:** capitalized; historical; often

   **Example:** The third day before the nones of March is March 5th; the third nones of August is August 3rd; and the third of the nones of November is November 3rd.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The ninth hour after dawn (about 3 pm).

   **Usage:** capitalized; historical; sometimes

   **Source synonyms:** none; [noon](../2c6/noon--b198f3cd8e70.md) (obsolete)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The divine office appointed to the hour.

   **Example:** The Greek monks always listen to their reader recite Psalms 83, 84, and 85 from the Septuagint at nones.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity

   **Source synonyms:** none

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Alternative form of noon&#58; the sixth hour after dawn; midday (12 pm).

   **Usage:** alt-of; alternative; obsolete

   **Alternative form of:** [noon](../2c6/noon--b198f3cd8e70.md)

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Synonym of lunch&#58; a meal eaten around noon.

   **Usage:** obsolete

   **Source synonyms:** lunch (synonym; synonym-of)

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** midday — sixth hour of daylight; [noon](../2c6/noon--b198f3cd8e70.md); noontide
