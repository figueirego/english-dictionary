### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A flight past a celestial object in order to make observations.

   **Example:** The upcoming flyby of Halley's comet is expected to provide new information on the nature of the comet.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A low-level ceremonial flight, typically in connection with an airshow or a military parade.

   **Usage:** US

   **Source synonyms:** flypast (UK); flyover (US)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A brief visit.

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A sailby performed by a hydrofoil vessel while hydrofoiling.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A three-dimensional animated sequence in which the camera moves past an object or scenery.
