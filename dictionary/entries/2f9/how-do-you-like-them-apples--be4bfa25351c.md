## Phrase

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A question directed jestingly or mockingly at someone who has received surprising information, ridiculing or making light of the situation.

   **Usage:** colloquial; rhetoric

   **Example:** "Our governor has just vetoed a bill that would offer more money to our schools. How do you like them apples?"

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used after an actual or proposed action with which the listener might be displeased, and also used after refuting an argument.

   **Usage:** Ireland; US; colloquial; rhetoric

   **Example:** "I can't give you a raise now; if I did, this whole company would go bankrupt, and you wouldn't have a job at all. Now how do you like them apples?"
