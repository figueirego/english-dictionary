## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A procedure to arrange for clearing customs or immigration in advance.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The customs service announced new preclearance regulations last week.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Advance approval by a federal court or the Department of Justice for changes to voting regulations in certain states under the 1965 Voting Rights Act.

   **Usage:** US; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The great state of Alabama believes that preclearance is a relic of the past.
