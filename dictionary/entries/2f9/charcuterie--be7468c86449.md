### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The practice of cooking and preparing ready-to-eat meat products, especially pork.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Cured meat that is ready to be eaten, especially pork.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

   **Usage:** countable
