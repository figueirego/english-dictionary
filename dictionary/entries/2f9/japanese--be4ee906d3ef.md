## Noun · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˌdʒæp.əˈniːz/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Forms

- Japanese — plural
- Japaneses — plural

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A citizen or national of Japan (incl. Ryukyuans and Ainu people).

   **Usage:** countable; in-plural

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. A member of the predominant ethnic group of Japan, also found throughout the world in diaspora.

   **Usage:** countable; in-plural

   **Example:** A Japanese will typically have black hair, brown eyes, and pale skin.

   **Source synonyms:** Yamato

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. Ellipsis of Japanese food.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; ellipsis; uncountable

   **Example:** Let’s go out to eat. I’m in the mood for Japanese.

   **Alternative form of:** Japanese food

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** Japanee (archaic; person); Japanner (obsolete; person); Japonian (obsolete; person); Nipponese (dated; person)
