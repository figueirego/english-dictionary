### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A combination of diffraction grating and prism used to obtain spectra from selected parts of an image.

   **Example:** The advantage of grisms is that the same camera can be used both for imaging (without the grism) and spectroscopy (with the grism) without having to be moved.

   **Source synonyms:** grating prism
