### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person whose spouse is absent&#58;
   1. A person who has lost a spouse and hasn't remarried&#58;
      1. A woman whose spouse (traditionally husband) has died (and who has not remarried); a woman in relation to her late spouse; feminine of widower.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person whose spouse is absent&#58;
   1. A person who has lost a spouse and hasn't remarried&#58;
      1. Any person whose spouse has died (and who has not remarried).

         **Usage:** uncommon

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A person whose spouse is absent&#58;
   1. A woman whose husband is often away pursuing a hobby, career, etc.

      **Usage:** broadly; humorous; in-compounds; informal; often; sarcastic

      **Example:** My aunt is a football widow in the fall and a basketball widow in the winter and early spring.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An additional hand of playing cards dealt face-down in some card games, to be used by the highest bidder.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** card-games; games

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** media; publishing; typography
