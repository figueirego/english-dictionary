## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A semantic relation that exists between a term denoting a whole (the holonym) and a term denoting a part that pertains to the whole (the meronym).

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** The relation between the terms ‘foot’ and ‘heel’ is that of holonymy, since one of the parts of a foot is a heel.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; philosophy; sciences; semantics

   **Source antonyms:** meronymy

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The quality or nature of being the whole in a holonymous relation.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences; semantics
