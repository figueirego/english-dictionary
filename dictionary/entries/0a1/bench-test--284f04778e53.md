## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

- bench tests — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An individual test of a component, such as an electrical or mechanical device, that is performed with the component removed from its normal operating environment, notionally on a workshop bench.
