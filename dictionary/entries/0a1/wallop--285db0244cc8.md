**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A heavy blow, a punch.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** he gave him a mighty wallop

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** this guy's got some wallop

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An emotional impact, a psychological force.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** that film has some serious wallop

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A thrill, an emotionally excited reaction.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Anything produced by a process that involves boiling; beer, tea, or whitewash.

   **Usage:** slang; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

   **Usage:** Scotland; UK; countable; dialectal; uncountable
