### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of reduction or depletion (including through withdrawal or utilization).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The government initiated a drawdown of forces in the border region.

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The act of reduction or depletion (including through withdrawal or utilization).
   1. The withdrawal of weapons, ammunition and other supplies from US stocks, and provision of it to another nation.

      **Usage:** US; countable; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; law; military; politics; war

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

3. The act of reduction or depletion (including through withdrawal or utilization).
   1. A lowering in water level, as in a well or a reservoir.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

4. The result of reduction, utilization, or depletion.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** economics; sciences
