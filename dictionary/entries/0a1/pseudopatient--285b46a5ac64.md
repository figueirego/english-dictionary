### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who pretends to be ill, especially to gain some benefit.

   **Usage:** dated

   **Source synonyms:** malingerer

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person who poses as a patient so that research may be conducted or staff trained.

   **Example:** Most of the pseudopatients were falsely diagnosed with mental disorders.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences
