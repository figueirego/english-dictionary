### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of reflecting or the state of being reflected.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The property of a propagated wave being thrown back from a surface (such as a mirror).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Something, such as an image, that is reflected.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The dog barked at his own reflection in the mirror.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Careful thought or consideration.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** After careful reflection, I have decided not to vote for that proposition.

   **Source synonyms:** cogitation; contemplation; deliberation; mulling; musing; pondering; meditation; reflection; rethinking; reconsideration; rumination; thought

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A representative manifestation or outcome of a condition, trend or trait.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Our recent results are a reflection of the progress we've made as a team.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A representative manifestation or outcome of a condition, trend or trait.
   1. Used to make an implied criticism.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The process or mechanism of determining the capabilities of an object at run-time.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences
