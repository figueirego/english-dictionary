### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Mental acceptance of a claim as true.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** It's my belief that the thief is somebody known to us.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Faith or trust in the reality of something; often based upon one's own reasoning, trust in a claim, desire of actuality, and/or evidence considered.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** My belief is that there is a bear in the woods. Bill said he saw one.

   **Example:** Based on this data, it is our belief that X does not occur.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Something believed.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** The ancient people have a belief in many deities.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The quality or state of believing.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** My belief that it will rain tomorrow is strong.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Religious faith.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** She often said it was her belief that carried her through the hard times.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. One's religious or moral convictions.

   **Usage:** countable; in-plural; uncountable

   **Example:** I can't do that. It's against my beliefs.
