### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A period of time when a person occupies a position only conditionally and may be removed if certain conditions are not met.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** You'll be on probation for first six months. After that, if you work out, they'll hire you permanently.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A type of sentence where convicted criminals are permitted to continue living in a community but will automatically be sent to jail if they violate certain conditions.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** He got two years probation for robbery.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A testing period of time.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The act of testing; proof.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; uncountable
