### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The long fine silky filaments excreted by several mollusks (particularly Pinna nobilis) by which they attach themselves to the sea bed, and from which sea silk is manufactured.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually
