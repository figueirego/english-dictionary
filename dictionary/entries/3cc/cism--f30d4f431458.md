### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Added to names of primarily Greek letters to indicate a phonetic phenomenon relating to the corresponding phoneme&#58;
   1. Excessive usage of the phoneme.

      **Usage:** morpheme

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Added to names of primarily Greek letters to indicate a phonetic phenomenon relating to the corresponding phoneme&#58;
   1. A sound change towards the phoneme.

      **Usage:** morpheme

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Added to names of primarily Greek letters to indicate a phonetic phenomenon relating to the corresponding phoneme&#58;
   1. The retention of a phoneme when related varieties lose it.

      **Usage:** morpheme

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Added to names of primarily Greek letters to indicate a phonetic phenomenon relating to the corresponding phoneme&#58;
   1. A speech disorder marked by either excessive or defective use of the phoneme.
