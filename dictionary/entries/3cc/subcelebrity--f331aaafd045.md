### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The state or quality of being somewhat known but not enough to be considered truly famous.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person who is somewhat known but not famous enough to be considered a true celebrity.
