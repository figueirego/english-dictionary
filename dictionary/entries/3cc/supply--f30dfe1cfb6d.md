### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To provide (something), to make (something) available for use.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to supply money for the war

   **Source synonyms:** fulfill

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To furnish or equip with.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to supply a furnace with fuel; to supply soldiers with ammunition

   **Source synonyms:** line

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To fill up, or keep full.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Rivers are supplied by smaller streams.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To compensate for, or make up a deficiency of.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To serve instead of; to take the place of.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To act as a substitute.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To fill temporarily; to serve as substitute for another in, as a vacant place or office; to occupy; to have possession of.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to supply a pulpit

## Noun · Etymology 1 · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- enPR: səplīʹ — variety unspecified in the source labels
- IPA: /səˈplaɪ/ — variety unspecified in the source labels
- IPA: \[sʌˈpʰɫaɪ\] — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Forms

- supplies — plural

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. The act of supplying.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. An amount of something supplied.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** A supply of good drinking water is essential.

   **Example:** She said, "China has always had a freshwater supply problem with 20 percent of the world’s population but only 7 percent of its freshwater".

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. The market force that causes sellers to be both willing and able to sell a good or service, as measured by the amount of that good or service that is currently available to be bought at any given price point; the amount itself.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Supply and demand ebb and flow in a complex interplay.

   **Example:** The supply of timber to the region's mills was lower than expected this month, owing to transport problems posed by wildfires.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** economics; sciences

   **Source antonyms:** demand

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. Provisions.

   **Usage:** countable; in-plural; uncountable

   **Example:** They are busy laying in supplies for the coming winter.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. An amount of money provided, as by Parliament or Congress, to meet the annual national expenditures.

   **Usage:** countable; in-plural; uncountable

   **Example:** to vote supplies

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. Somebody, such as a teacher or clergyman, who temporarily fills the place of another; a substitute.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
