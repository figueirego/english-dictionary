### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An investment of labour, typically by the owner and often his or her family, usually in a small business or personal residence that increases the value of the business or residence.

   **Usage:** countable; idiomatic; uncountable

   **Example:** My wife and I have put a lot of sweat equity into our home&#58; I did the renovations and she did the redecorating.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; real-estate
