### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A large inscribed stone found near Rosetta, Egypt, in 1799, later used as a basis for understanding many previously undecipherable examples of hieroglyphic writing.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any source of information crucial to interpreting.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** My friend’s carefully-compiled notebook was the Rosetta Stone that opened up understanding of the physics lectures.
