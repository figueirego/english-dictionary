### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Treating serious issues with (often deliberately) inappropriate humour; flippant.

   **Example:** Robbie's joke about Heather's appearance was just him being facetious.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Pleasantly humorous; jocular.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

   **Usage:** Of an idea or statement
