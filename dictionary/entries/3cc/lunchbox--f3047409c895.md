### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A container for transporting meals, especially lunch.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The penis when enclosed in clothing.

   **Usage:** UK; slang

   **Source synonyms:** basket (US); packet; package

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A lunch packaged in a disposable box to be taken away to eat.

   **Usage:** especially Asia

   **Example:** Jack was carrying his lunchbox with him.

   **Source synonyms:** box lunch

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A luggable; an early laptop computer, usually a unit with a handle and a fold-out keyboard.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A simple portable transmitter sometimes used in phreaking.

   **Usage:** slang

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A cocktail made with beer, orange juice, and almond liqueur.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A person seen as weak and disposable who could easily be sacrificed without much guilt or afterthought.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; slang
