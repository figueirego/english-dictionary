### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A block or slab upon which a column, pedestal, statue or other structure is based.

   **Example:** The queen placed the vase on the plinth so the cupbearer could fill it with flowers.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The bottom course of a wall.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
