### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who is easily made to bleed, or who bleeds in unusually large amounts, particularly a hemophiliac.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A blood vessel that requires cauterization etc. to stop it from bleeding during surgery.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences; surgery

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Anything that saps a resource produced by something else.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A valve designed to release a small amount of excess pressure from a system.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A troublesome fellow or thing; a blighter.

   **Usage:** Commonwealth; Ireland; UK; derogatory; slang

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Synonym of scratch hit.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; baseball; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports
