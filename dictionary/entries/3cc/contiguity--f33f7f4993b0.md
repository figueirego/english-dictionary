### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A state in which two or more physical objects are physically touching one another or in which sections of a plane border on one another.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The two regions share a border and are connected by geographical contiguity.

   **Example:** The study examined the contiguity of urban areas across the border.

   **Example:** geographical contiguity

   **Example:** spatial contiguity

   **Example:** physical contiguity
