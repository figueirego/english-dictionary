### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Somebody whose wealth is at least one quadrillion (10¹⁵) currency units (which is possible only in places with hyperinflated currencies).

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An extremely wealthy person.

   **Usage:** broadly; figuratively

   **Source synonyms:** bazillionaire; gazillionaire; affluent; capitalist; fat cat (slang); HNWI; moneybags; rich person
