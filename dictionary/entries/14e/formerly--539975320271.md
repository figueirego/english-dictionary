### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. At some time in the past.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Xenia Robertson, formerly known as Xenia Isaamov, is from Ukraine.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Previously; once.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** This ranch was formerly owned by slaveholders.

   **Source synonyms:** at one time
