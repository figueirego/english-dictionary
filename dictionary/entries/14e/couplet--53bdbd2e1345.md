**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A set of two things, particularly.
   1. A pair of lines, typically with rhyming end words.
      1. Ellipsis of antithetical couplet, Chinese couplet, or duilian couplet (“duilian”).

         **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; ellipsis

         **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** literature; media; publishing

         **Alternative form of:** antithetical couplet; Chinese couplet; or duilian couplet

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A set of two things, particularly.
   1. A pair of one-way streets which carry opposing directions of traffic through gridded urban areas.

      **Example:** 5th Street is one-way west only and 6th Street is one-way east only. Together, they form a couplet in Downtown Los Angeles.

      **Source synonyms:** one-way pair
