### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having the faculty of reason; rational, reasoning.

   **Usage:** archaic

   **Source synonyms:** reasonous

   **Source antonyms:** irrational; non-rational; unreasonable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Just; fair; agreeable to reason.

   **Source synonyms:** equitable; reasonous; righteous; upright

   **Source antonyms:** absurd; arbitrary; unjust; unreasonable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Not excessive or immoderate; within due limits; proper.

   **Example:** a reasonable demand, amount, or price

   **Source synonyms:** reasonous; temperate; measured; mellow; middle of the road (idiomatic); mild; moderate; nonexcessive; nonextreme; nonfanatic; proper; reasonable; reasonous; sober; temperate; tempered; unexcessive; unextreme

   **Source antonyms:** unreasonable; devilish; excessive; horrendous (informal); de trop (literary); extravagant; extreme; immoderate; intemperate; lavish; nimious (Scottish; obsolete); obscene; overmuch; overweening; over the top; prodigal; undue; unmeasured; unreasonable; unrestrained; wanton

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Not expensive; fairly priced.

   **Example:** $20 a bottle is very reasonable for a good wine at a restaurant.

   **Source synonyms:** affordable; competitive; affordable; cheap at the price (UK); gainworthy; inexpensive; middle-end (rare); mid-range; purchasable; reasonable

   **Source antonyms:** expensive; extortionate; unreasonable; 5-star; big-ticket; chargeful (obsolete); cheap at half the price (humorous); costful (archaic); costly; cost-prohibitive (euphemistic); fancy; dear (Ireland; UK); dearthful (Scotland; obsolete); dispendious (archaic); ex (Singapore); expenny (slang); expensive; exy (Australia; slang); high-cost; lickpenny (obsolete); million-dollar (figuratively); high-priced; princely; out of sight; overdear; pricey; ruinous; spendy (US; regional); spenno; spenny (Australia; UK; informal); splurgy (US; slang); steep
