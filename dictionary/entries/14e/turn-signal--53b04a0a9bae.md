### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Each of the flashing lights on each side of a vehicle which is used to indicate the vehicle is moving left or right.

   **Usage:** US

   **Example:** The hazard lights turn on all the turn signals.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** automotive; transport; vehicles

   **Source synonyms:** blinker (informal); indicator (Australia; Britain; New-Zealand); directional; directional signal; direction indicator; trafficator; turn indicator; winker (informal)
