<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To kill one-tenth of (a group), (historical, specifically) as a military punishment in the Roman army selected by lot, usually carried out by the surviving soldiers.

   **Usage:** archaic

   **Source synonyms:** tithe

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To destroy or remove one-tenth of (something).

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

3. To devastate&#58; to reduce or destroy significantly but not completely.

   **Usage:** broadly

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To exact a tithe or other 10% tax.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

5. To tithe&#58; to pay a 10% tax.

   **Usage:** obsolete; rare

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To divide into tenths; to decimalize.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

   **Usage:** proscribed
