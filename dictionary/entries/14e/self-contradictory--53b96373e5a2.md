## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Containing multiple claims or ideas that are inherently in disagreement; of something or someone that contradicts itself.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The statement “We agree to disagree on everything” is self-contradictory.

   **Source antonyms:** self-consistent
