### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The doctrine of the oneness and unity of reality, despite the appearance of diversity in the world.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; lifestyle; philosophy; religion; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** one-thingism

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The doctrine that there is a single source of political authority, especially that the church is subordinate to the state or vice versa.

   **Usage:** countable; historical; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; politics

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The legal doctrine that international law forms part of domestic law automatically after ratification or accession.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; international law
