### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To close, terminate, or end; to stop operating, either permanently or temporarily.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The store was shut down at the end of the year.

   **Example:** The company shuts down underperforming lines.

   **Example:** The project will be shutting down before the transfers come through.

   **Example:** They are planning to shut down the entire building at the end of the month.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To close, terminate, or end; to stop operating, either permanently or temporarily.
   1. To turn off or stop.

      **Usage:** ergative; transitive

      **Example:** It's a good idea to shut down the machine before you leave.

      **Example:** My computer is shutting down as we speak.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To close, terminate, or end; to stop operating, either permanently or temporarily.
   1. To stop offering a service, such as a bar; to perform the necessary tasks to stop a service or close a function.

      **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

      **Example:** The restaurant shuts down at two o'clock in the morning.

      **Example:** Mary will shut down the breakfast buffet at ten-thirty.

      **Example:** The last step to finish shutting down the bar is to take the cash drawer to the safe.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To close, terminate, or end; to stop operating, either permanently or temporarily.
   1. To become dark.

      **Usage:** dated; intransitive; transitive

      **Example:** Once the night shut down, I couldn't see my way home.

      **Source context (verbatim):** of a night

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To suppress, block out, or reject something.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** She made a proposal to reorganize the department, but management shut her down.

   **Example:** I asked her out, but she shut that down.

   **Example:** The head office shut the project down as soon as I presented it to the board.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To suppress, block out, or reject something.
   1. To render (something) ineffective.

      **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

      **Example:** We shut down the other team's strategy.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To suppress, block out, or reject something.
   1. To emotionally withdraw into oneself as a defense mechanism; to block out external stressors.

      **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive; transitive

      **Example:** I can't talk to him about the accident; he just shuts down anytime I try.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To pass (another vehicle), especially quickly.

   **Usage:** slang; auto racing; car culture; auto racing; car culture

   **Example:** I opened that throttle and shut him down.
