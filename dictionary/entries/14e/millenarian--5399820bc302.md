### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Pertaining to the belief in an impending period of one thousand years of peace and righteousness associated with the Second Coming of Christ and his reign on earth.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity

   **Source synonyms:** millennial

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Pertaining to any of various religious or social movements which believe in a coming or ongoing radical change to existing world order.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Lasting or expected to last a thousand years.
