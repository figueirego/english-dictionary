### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of assimilating or the state of being assimilated.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The metabolic conversion of nutrients into tissue.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The absorption of new ideas into an existing cognitive structure.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A sound change process by which the phonetics of a speech segment becomes more like that of another segment in a word (or at a word boundary), so that a change of phoneme occurs.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; phonology; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The adoption, by a minority group, of the customs and attitudes of the dominant culture.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; cultural studies

   **Example:** After centuries of British cultural assimilation, a majority of Irish now speak English instead of Irish.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; sciences; social-science; sociology
