### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Initialism of European Article Numbering&#58; a barcode symbology whose main variant, EAN-13, encodes thirteen digits, differing from UPC-A in that three of the digits in the left half are reversed so as to encode an additional digit.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; initialism

   **Alternative form of:** European Article Numbering
