### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A box used to provide a soiling area for (usually) pet cats, generally filled with cat litter, sand, clay chips, etc.

   **Example:** Pregnant women are often advised to have someone else clean the litter box to reduce the risk of contracting toxoplasmosis.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A toilet.

   **Usage:** humorous; usually
