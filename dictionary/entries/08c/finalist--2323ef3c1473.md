## Noun

### Forms

- finalists — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An adherent of finalism; someone who believes that a given process or situation is in its final state, with no further change possible.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Someone who believes that natural processes (especially evolution) are tending towards some specific final state; a teleologist.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Somebody or something that appears in the final of a competition.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A university student taking their final examinations, or in the year of study when these are taken.
