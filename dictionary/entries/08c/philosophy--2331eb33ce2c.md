### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An academic discipline that seeks truth through reasoning rather than empiricism, often attempting to provide explanations relating to general concepts such as existence and rationality.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Philosophy is often divided into five major branches&#58; logic, metaphysics, epistemology, ethics and aesthetics.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A view or outlook regarding fundamental principles underlying some domain.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** a philosophy of government; a philosophy of education

   **Source synonyms:** worldview; ethos

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A general principle (usually moral).

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A comprehensive system of belief.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source synonyms:** doctrine; ideology

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The love of wisdom.

   **Usage:** archaic; uncountable; originally

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A calm and thoughtful demeanor; calmness of temper.

   **Usage:** dated; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Synonym of small pica (especially in French printing).

   **Usage:** countable; dated; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** media; printing; publishing

   **Source synonyms:** small pica (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A broader branch of (non-applied) science.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable

   **Example:** natural philosophy
