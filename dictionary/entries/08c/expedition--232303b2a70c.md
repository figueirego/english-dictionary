## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ɛkspəˈdɪʃən/ — source labels: UK; US

### Forms

- expeditions — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A military journey; an enterprise against some enemy or into enemy territory.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An important or long journey, for example a march or a voyage.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A trip, especially a long one, made by a person or a group of people for a specific purpose.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** a naval expedition

   **Example:** a scientific expedition

   **Example:** an expedition across the Alps

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The group of people making such excursion.

   **Usage:** collective; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The process or activities of performing expediter tasks.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The quality of being expedite; speed, quickness.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The act of expediting something; prompt execution.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable
