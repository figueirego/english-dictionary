### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Extremely offensive or unpleasant; very annoying, contemptible, or odious.

   **Example:** Throwing stones at the bus is another example of your obnoxious behaviour.

   **Source synonyms:** aggravating; aggy (African-American-Vernacular-English; Britain; slang); annoying; wretched; displeasing; bothersome; chagrining; cruddy (slang); douchetastic (US; slang); douchey (derogatory; slang; vulgar); drippy; gittish (Britain; slang); honery (US; slang); irksome; irritating; obnoxious; pesky; plaguy; troublesome; twattish (derogatory; slang); vexatious; abhorrant; abhorrent; bad; sickly; mean; infernal; bum; horrid; vile; demonic; disagreeable; disgusting; distasteful; foul; rancid; fulsome; gooey (archaic); grim; gro; grody; gross; grotty; harsh; loathsome; munted (Australia; New-Zealand; slang); nasty; nauseous; offensive; obnoxious; obscene; rank (informal); repugnant; repulsive; revolting; scabrous; sickening; strident; unpleasant

   **Source antonyms:** unobnoxious; acceptable; agreeable; amiable; charming; comely (obsolete); nice; pleasant; pleasing; welcome

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Unjustly disagreeable, argumentative or objectionable; brazenly rude.

   **Example:** He was an especially obnoxious and detestable specimen of a man.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a person

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Exposed or vulnerable to something, especially harm or injury.

   **Usage:** archaic; obsolete

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Causing harm or injury; harmful, hurtful, injurious.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Deserving of blame or punishment; blameworthy, guilty.

   **Usage:** obsolete

   **Source synonyms:** reprehensible

**Source sense R1.S6**

   **Usage:** obsolete
