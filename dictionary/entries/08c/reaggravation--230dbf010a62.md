## Noun

### Forms

- reaggravations — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The state or act of reaggravating.

   **Example:** She has a reaggravation of her brain injury because she fell down the stairs again.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A state resulting from a reaggravation.

   **Example:** That looks like a reaggravation on his leg; he injured it two weeks ago as well.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
