### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. The act of grading again, thus changing the grade of something.
   1. A recontouring of land.

      **Example:** This is the second regrading of this lot, because the drainage plan has been revised yet again.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. The act of grading again, thus changing the grade of something.
   1. A rejudging or other reassessment.

      **Example:** There was a regrading of all the exams in light of the errors belatedly discovered in some of the test questions.
