### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To strike; to hit; to knock; to give a blow to.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Solid bodies, if they be very softly percussed, give no sound.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To impact.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Falling on the roof of the caravan, the hailstones percussed noisily.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To attempt to pinpoint the location or other quality of something by tapping on an overlying surface.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The doctor percussed his chest to determine whether he had pneumonia.

   **Example:** Percussing a patient's spleen is best done while he is on his back.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences
