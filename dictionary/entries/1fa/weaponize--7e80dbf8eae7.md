### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make into a weapon.

   **Usage:** British; English; Oxford; US; transitive

   **Example:** Anything can be weaponized. A big enough rock, dropped from a sufficient height, is a very good weapon.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To make more effective as a weapon.

   **Usage:** British; English; Oxford; US; transitive

   **Example:** To weaponize anthrax it is made more distributable, not more virulent.
