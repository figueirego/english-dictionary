### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To walk up a set of stairs as exercise.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To use a strategy for big city walking to avoid staying on the same street; it involves turning at a corner, walking to the end of the block, crossing the street, then repeating

   **Example:** I'm going to stair-step from East 3rd and 50th down to West 44th and Seventh Avenue.
