### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A North American elm tree of species Ulmus rubra, with a mucilaginous and slightly aromatic inner bark.

   **Source synonyms:** red elm; gray elm; soft elm; moose elm; Indian elm

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
