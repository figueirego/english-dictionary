### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Partial or biased in favour of one faction or demographic group.

   **Example:** We were robbed! That referee was definitely one-sided.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. With one competitor dominant over the other.

   **Example:** It was a swift, one-sided fight, all over in the second round.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Out of proportion or lopsided.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Having only one side, like a Möbius strip.
