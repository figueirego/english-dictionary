**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The state of being normal or usual; normalcy.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Jessie was going to wear pants to school, but her brother persuaded her to wear shorts to preserve normality.

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
