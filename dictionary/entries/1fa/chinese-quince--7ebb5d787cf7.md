### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An Asian tree of species Pseudocydonia sinensis, similar and related to the quince, with astringent, quince-like fruit.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Flowering quince (Chaenomeles lagenaria, syn. Chaenomeles speciosa), native to Japan and Korea, introduced elsewhere as an ornamental.
