**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Concerned with human or earthly matters, physical as opposed to spiritual.

   **Source synonyms:** material; mundane; sublunar; terrene (literary)

   **Source antonyms:** otherworldly; spiritual

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Concerned with secular rather than sacred matters.

   **Source synonyms:** lay; profane; temporal; areligious; civil; earthly; irreligious; laicist; laicistic; mundane; nonreligious; profane; secular; temporal; unconsecrated; unsanctified; worldly

   **Source antonyms:** clerical; religious; sacred

**Source sense R1.S3**
