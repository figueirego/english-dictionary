### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The phenomenon of making an unplanned, fortunate discovery through a combination of unexpected circumstances and insightful recognition.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source antonyms:** Murphy's law; perfect storm

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An unsought, unintended or unexpected, but fortunate, discovery or learning experience that occurs by accident.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** chance; luck; adventure (obsolete); accident; casualty; chance; chance-medley (obsolete); fortune; hap (archaic); hazard; joss; luck; serendipity; weird (archaic)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The occurrence and development of events by chance in a happy or beneficial way.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
