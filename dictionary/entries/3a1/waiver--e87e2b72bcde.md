## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈweɪ.və(ɹ)/ — source labels: UK
- IPA: /ˈweɪ.vɚ/ — source labels: US

### Forms

- waivers — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of waiving, or not insisting on, some right, claim, or privilege.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A legal document removing some requirement, such as waiving a right (giving it up) or a waiver of liability (agreeing to hold someone blameless).

   **Example:** I had to sign a waiver when I went skydiving, agreeing not to sue even if something went wrong.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. relating to waivers

   **Usage:** Canada; US; noun adjunct

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Something that releases a person from a requirement.

   **Example:** I needed a waiver from the department head to take the course because I didn't technically have the prerequisite courses.

   **Example:** I needed a waiver from the zoning board for the house because the lot was so small, but they let me build because it was next to the park.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The process of waiving or outlawing a person.

   **Usage:** obsolete
