### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An informal social gathering, usually in a natural outdoor setting, to which the participants bring their own food and drink.

   **Example:** We went out for a picnic in the forest.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The meal eaten at such a gathering.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An easy or pleasant task.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** We remind the guests that dealing with this problem is no picnic, and to be patient.

   **Source synonyms:** piece of cake

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An entertainment at which each person contributed some dish to a common table.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A cut of pork from the shoulder area (above the front leg) of a pig.
