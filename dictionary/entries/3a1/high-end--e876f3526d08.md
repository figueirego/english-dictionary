### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Most sophisticated or expensive.

   **Example:** I wouldn't put a high-end stereo in your car in this neighborhood if I were you.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Appealing to sophisticated or discerning tastes.

   **Example:** She shops at discount department stores, but looks as if she patronized high-end boutiques.
