## Noun

### Forms

- connecting rods — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A rod, inside a reciprocating engine, that connects a piston to the crankshaft.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; usually

   **Example:** throw a connecting rod \[ = when the reciprocating assembly flies apart, rarely\]

   **Example:** The engine threw a connecting rod at six thousand RPM and "ventilated" the engine block, as they say.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see connecting, rod&#58; any rod that connects two other parts.

   **Usage:** sometimes

   **Example:** There's a tiny connecting rod that links the actuator to this valve.
