## Noun · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈstændaʊt/ — source labels: General-American

### Forms

- standouts — plural

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. An exceptional or noteworthy person or thing.

   **Example:** This building is one of the city's architectural standouts.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. The distance a self-retracting metal tape measure can be extended before collapsing under its own weight.

   **Example:** This tape measure has a standout of 14 feet.
