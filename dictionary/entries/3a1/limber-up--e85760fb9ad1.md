### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To stretch one's muscles to make them more limber, usually as a preparation for physical exercise.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The swimmer limbered up for a few minutes before the final.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To make (someone or something) more limber or flexible.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** You need to limber up your wits before the exam.

   **Example:** A good massage will limber up the neck and arms.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To prepare; to make oneself ready.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To attach a limber.
