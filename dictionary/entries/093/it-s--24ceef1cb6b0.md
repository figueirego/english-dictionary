   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; contraction

   **Example:** it’s too expensive; it’s coming right for us!; it's a free country

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Contraction of it + was.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; contraction; proscribed

   **Example:** When I was a kid, it's the thought that counted but, now, it's all about the Benjamins.

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. There's, there is; there're, there are.

   **Usage:** contraction; dialectal; nonstandard

   **Example:** It's a package for you by the door.
