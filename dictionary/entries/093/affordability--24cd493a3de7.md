## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The extent or degree to which something is affordable, as measured by its cost relative to the amount that the purchaser is able to pay.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** First-time home buyers are feeling the squeeze of higher interest rates with mortgage affordability at its worst level for 16 years.
