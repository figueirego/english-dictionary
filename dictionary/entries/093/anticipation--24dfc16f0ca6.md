### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of anticipating, taking up, placing, or considering something beforehand, or before the proper time in natural order.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Often the anticipation of a shot is worse than the pain of the stick.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The eagerness associated with waiting for something to occur.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** He waited with great anticipation for Christmas to arrive.

   **Example:** He waited in anticipation of the arrival of Christmas.

   **Example:** I was giddy with anticipation.

   **Source synonyms:** electricity

**Source sense R1.S3**
