### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An unknown location; (by extension) apparent disappearance or nonexistence.

   **Usage:** figuratively; humorous; idiomatic; uncountable; usually

   **Example:** Where did you think the exhaust would go? Into thin air?

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see thin, air, (especially, low-density air as a result of high altitude).

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** You'll need to stay mindful of taking breaks, drinking water, and avoiding overexertion up here, because you're dealing with thin air at this altitude.
