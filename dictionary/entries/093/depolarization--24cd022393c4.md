### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of reducing polarity, or the result of such action; reduction to an unpolarized or less polarized condition.
   1. An intracellular shift in electrical charge distribution, resulting in less negative charge inside the cell compared with outside; it is essential to the function of many cells, communication between cells, and the overall physiology of an organism.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; medicine; natural-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
