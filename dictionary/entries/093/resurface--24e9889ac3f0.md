### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To come once again to the surface.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** His body finally resurfaced after three years underwater.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To surface again; to reappear or re-occur.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To make something reappear.

   **Usage:** rare; transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To provide a new surface, to replace or remodel the surface of something, or to restore a surface. To put a new coating or finish on a surface.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** A zamboni is a big machine that resurfaces ice at a rink so it is smooth as glass for the skaters.
