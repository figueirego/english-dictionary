## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A laboratory technique used to evaluate the binding of an antibody to its antigen, similar to immunodiffusion, but with the addition of an applied electrical field across the diffusion medium, usually an agar or polyacrylamide gel.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** immunology; medicine; sciences
