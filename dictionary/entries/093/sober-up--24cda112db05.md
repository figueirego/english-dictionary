### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To become sober; to cause to become sober (usually in terms of alcohol or drugs; sometimes figuratively).

   **Example:** She's in no state to go home by herself, so make sure she doesn't drink anything else, and she'll soon sober up.

   **Example:** A pot of black coffee ought to sober him up.

   **Example:** When the irrational exuberance finally ended and the central bank took away the punch bowl, the business world belatedly sobered up.
