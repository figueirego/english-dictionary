### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A movement from one location to another.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I've been stuck in this house for too long. I could use a change of scenery. Maybe I'll go to the beach.

   **Source synonyms:** change of scene; change of air; change of pace
