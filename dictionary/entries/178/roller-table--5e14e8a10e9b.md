### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A worktable whose tabletop is composed of multiple rollers (either cylindrical or spherical), easing the movement of large or heavy workpieces, especially in a factory or workshop. The rollers often ride on roller bearings or are such bearings. Conveyor systems sometimes incorporate such tables as components.
