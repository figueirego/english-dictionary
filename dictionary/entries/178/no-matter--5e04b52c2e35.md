## Interjection · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. It doesn't matter, it's unimportant, it doesn't make any difference.

   **Usage:** US; dated

   **Example:** It seems that Doug's coat has gone missing. Oh, well, no matter&#58; we can always buy a new one, now can't we?

   **Example:** "Did you fail your exam?" "Yes, but no matter. I'll just study harder next time."
