## Noun

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of ascending; a motion upwards.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** He made a tedious ascent of Mont Blanc.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The way or means by which one ascends.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** There is a difficult northern ascent from Malaucene of Mont Ventoux.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An eminence, hill, or high place.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The degree of elevation of an object, or the angle it makes with a horizontal line; inclination; gradient; steepness

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The road has an ascent of 5 degrees.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The ascender height in a typeface.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** media; publishing; typography

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. An increase, for example in popularity or hierarchy

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
