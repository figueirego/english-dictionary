### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A gecko with a tail in the shape of a leaf.
   1. Any of 14 species of geckos in the genus Uroplatus found in Madagascar.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A gecko with a tail in the shape of a leaf.
   1. Any of 9 species of geckos in the genus Phyllurus in Australia.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A gecko with a tail in the shape of a leaf.
   1. Any of 7 species of geckos in the genus Saltuarius found in Australia.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A gecko with a tail in the shape of a leaf.
   1. Long-necked northern leaf-tailed gecko, Orraya occultus in Australia.
