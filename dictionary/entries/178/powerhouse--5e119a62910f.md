### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A power station; the building housing the core parts of a power station, such as the dynamo.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any source of power, energy or strength; a source of influence or inspiration.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** The company has been a powerhouse in its industry for many years.

   **Example:** The mitochondrion is the powerhouse of the cell.

   **Example:** She's a powerhouse of ideas.
