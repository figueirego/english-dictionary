**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A very small amount; a hint; a trace, slight idea; an inkling.

   **Example:** Add a soupçon of red pepper.

   **Example:** coffee with a soupçon of cognac

   **Example:** No one is so depraved that a soupçon of goodness cannot be found in them.
