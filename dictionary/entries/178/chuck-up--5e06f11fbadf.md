### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To vomit; throw up.

   **Usage:** New-Zealand; UK; intransitive; slang; transitive

   **Example:** She got ridiculously drunk last night and chucked up in the back of the minicab on the way home.

   **Example:** Take it easy on the roller coasters or you're going to chuck up your lunch.

   **Source synonyms:** upchuck; throw up

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To chuck up the sponge; to give up; to admit defeat; to break a contract; to abandon or quit (something).

   **Usage:** dated; intransitive; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** throw in the towel; chuck in; jig up; jack up
