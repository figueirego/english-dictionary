### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Suitable for some particular purpose.

   **Example:** This would be an opportune spot for a picnic.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. At a convenient or advantageous time.

   **Example:** The opportune arrival of the bus cut short the boring conversation.
