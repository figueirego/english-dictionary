### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make a whimpering or sniffing sound when breathing, because of a runny nose.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Her dad heard her sniffling and passed her a handkerchief.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To utter with a whimpering or sniffing sound.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He sniffled a pathetic apology.

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**
