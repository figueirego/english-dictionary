## Adjective · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /sləʊ/ — source labels: UK
- IPA: /slaː/ — source labels: Northumbria
- IPA: /sloʊ/ — source labels: US

### Forms

- slower — comparative
- slowest — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Taking a long time to move or go a short distance, or to perform an action; not quick in motion; proceeding at a low speed.

   **Example:** a slow train; a slow computer

   **Example:** Turkey should always be cooked in a slow oven.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Not happening in a short time; spread over a comparatively long time.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of reduced intellectual capacity; not quick to comprehend.

   **Usage:** derogatory; informal

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Not hasty; not tending to hurry; acting with deliberation or caution.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Behind in time; indicating a time earlier than the true time.

   **Example:** That clock is slow.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a clock or the like

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Lacking spirit; deficient in liveliness or briskness.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Not busy; lacking activity.

   **Example:** It was a slow news day, so the editor asked us to make our articles wordier.

   **Example:** I'm just sitting here with a desk of cards, enjoying a slow afternoon.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a period of time

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** slow the process
