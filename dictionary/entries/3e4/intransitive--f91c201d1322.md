### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Not transitive&#58; not having, or not taking, a direct object.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The word "drink" is a transitive verb in "they drink wine", but an intransitive one in "they drink often."

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** grammar; human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a verb

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Not transitive or passing further; kept; detained.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; rare

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of a set of dice&#58; containing three dice A, B, and C, with the property that A rolls higher than B more than half the time, and B rolls higher than C more than half the time, but lacking the property that A rolls higher than C more than half the time. See intransitive dice and intransitive game.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; probability; sciences
