### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To write (a document) in large, aesthetic, and legible lettering; to make a finalized copy of.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To buy up wholesale, especially to buy the whole supply of (a commodity etc.).

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business

   **Source synonyms:** corner the market

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To monopolize; to concentrate (something) in the single possession of someone, especially unfairly.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To completely engage the attention of; to involve.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** She seems to be completely engrossed in that book.

   **Source synonyms:** enwrap; wrap
