### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A binary function, written as δ with two subscripts, which evaluates to 1 when its arguments are equal, and 0 otherwise.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A unary function, written as δ with a single index, which evaluates to 1 at zero, and 0 elsewhere.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences
