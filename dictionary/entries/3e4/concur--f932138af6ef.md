### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To agree (in action or opinion); to have a common opinion; to coincide; to correspond.

   **Example:** The jury concurs with the case put forward by the defence lawyer, that the defendant is undoubtedly innocent.

   **Example:** I do not concur that this is the best way forward.

   **Example:** We do not concur in that particular point.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To meet in the same point; to combine or conjoin; to contribute or help towards a common object or effect.

   **Example:** concurring plans

   **Example:** concurring lines of thought

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To run together; to meet.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To converge.

   **Usage:** rare
