### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An unspillable electrochemical battery whose cells use electrolyte soaked sand – typically used to test telegraph cables at sea.

   **Usage:** historical

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; electrical-engineering; electricity; electromagnetism; electronics; energy; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

   **Usage:** historical

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war
