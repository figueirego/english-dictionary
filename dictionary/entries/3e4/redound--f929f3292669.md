### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To swell up (of water, waves etc.); to overflow, to surge (of bodily fluids).

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To contribute to an advantage or disadvantage for someone or something.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To contribute to the honour, shame etc. of a person or organisation.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To reverberate, to echo.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To reflect (honour, shame etc.) to or onto someone.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To attach, come back, accrue to someone; to reflect back on or upon someone (of honour, shame etc.).

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** His infamous behaviour only redounded back upon him when he was caught.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To arise from or out of something.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To roll back; to be sent or driven back.

   **Usage:** intransitive; usually; flood

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a wave
