<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** My heart is hers, and hers only.

   **Example:** I threw out only him.

   **Example:** The cat sat only on the mat. It kept off the sofa.

   **Example:** The only scientist to have won three Nobel prizes.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. No more than; just.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The cat only sat on the mat. It didn't scratch it.

   **Example:** If there were only one more ticket!

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. As recently as.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** He left only moments ago.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. Emphasizing something that is just or necessary.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** It's only fair to let him borrow your kite, after you've had his toys all day.

   **Example:** He'd been belittling her for years. It's only natural that she left.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. Used to express surprise or consternation at an action.

   **Usage:** UK; not-comparable

   **Example:** She's only gone and run off with the milkman!

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. Just, simply, undoubtedly.

   **Usage:** Ireland; informal; not-comparable

   **Example:** Sure you're only gorgeous!

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

**Source sense R2.S7**

7. Introduces a disappointing or surprising outcome that renders futile something previously mentioned.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** They rallied from a three-goal deficit only to lose in the final two minutes of play.

   **Example:** I spent years studying a degree only to learn there was no requirement for such a certification.

   **Example:** I helped him out only for him to betray me.

<a id="r2-s8"></a>

**Source sense R2.S8**

8. Above all others; particularly.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; obsolete
