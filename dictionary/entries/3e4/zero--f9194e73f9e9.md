   **Example:** The temperature outside is ten degrees below zero.

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. A value of the independent variables of a function, for which the function is equal to zero.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The zeroes of a polynomial are its roots by the fundamental theorem of algebra.

   **Example:** The derivative of a continuous, differentiable function that twice crosses the axis must have a zero.

   **Example:** The nontrivial zeros of the Riemann zeta function may all lie on the critical line.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

**Source sense R2.S7**

7. The additive identity element of a monoid or greater algebraic structure, particularly a group or ring.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Since a commutative zero is the inverse of any additive identity, it must be unique when it exists.

   **Example:** The zero (of a ring or field) has the property that the product of the zero with any element yields the zero.

   **Example:** The quotient ring over a maximal ideal is a field with a single zero element.
