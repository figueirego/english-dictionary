### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To slap or strike someone on the head.

   **Usage:** dialectal; obsolete

   **Example:** He will cornobble you very hard if he finds out.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To hit upside the head, specifically with a dead fish.

   **Usage:** archaic; dialectal

   **Example:** The dead trout caught last week was used by Trevor to cornobble Will.
