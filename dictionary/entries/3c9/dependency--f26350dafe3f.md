### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A state of dependence; a refusal to exercise initiative.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Frank's sullen dependency was driving his father nuts.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Something dependent on, or subordinate to, something else

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** In the United States' governmental structure, the military is conceived as a dependency under the executive branch.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The status of being a legal dependent; the relationship between a dependent and their provider.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A colony, or a territory subject to rule by an external power.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** colony; protectorate; province; dominion

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A dependence on a habit-forming substance such as a drug or alcohol.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** addiction

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Reliance on the functionality provided by some other, external component.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** This library has dependencies on a lot of other libraries. We have to compile all of those other libraries first.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. An external component whose functionality is relied on.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** One of this library's dependencies is very finicky. It can be hard to get it to compile on some systems.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences
