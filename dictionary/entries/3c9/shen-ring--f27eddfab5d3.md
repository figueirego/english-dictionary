### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A circle with a line at a tangent to it, represented in hieroglyphs as a stylized loop of rope, representing eternal protection.

   **Example:** In its elongated form, the shen ring became the cartouche which enclosed and protected a royal name.
