### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A type of bond that does not require an asset as surety up front, but which instead incurs a penalty if one defaults when it is due.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** released on nonsurety release

   **Example:** nonsurety court bond
