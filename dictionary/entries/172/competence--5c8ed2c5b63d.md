### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The quality or state of being competent, i.e. able or suitable for a general role.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The quality or state of being able or suitable for a particular task; the quality or state of being competent for a particular task or skill.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The system of linguistic knowledge possessed by native speakers of a language, as opposed to its actual use in concrete situations (performance), cf. linguistic competence.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A sustainable income.

   **Usage:** countable; dated; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. the legal authority to deal with a matter.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** The bill was denied royal assent because the Scottish Parliament does not have legislative competence on the bill's matter.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; law; politics

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The degree to which a rock is resistant to deformation or flow.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; geology; natural-sciences
