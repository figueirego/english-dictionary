### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A potentially life-threatening complication of acute promyelocytic leukemia, first thought to be specifically associated with tretinoin treatment, and characterized by dyspnea, fever, weight gain, hypotension, and pulmonary infiltrates.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** RAS (initialism); differentiation syndrome
