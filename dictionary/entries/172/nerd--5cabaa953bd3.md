### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who is intellectual but generally introverted.

   **Usage:** derogatory; slang; sometimes

   **Example:** The bullies used to call him a nerd at school.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One who has an intense, obsessive interest in something.

   **Usage:** derogatory; informal; sometimes

   **Example:** a computer nerd

   **Example:** a comic-book nerd

   **Source synonyms:** geek; dag (Australia); propeller head

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A member of a subculture revolving around intellectualism, technology, video games, fantasy and science fiction, comic books and assorted media.

   **Usage:** derogatory; informal; sometimes

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. One who is stupid and socially inept or unattractive; a social outcast.

   **Usage:** dated; derogatory; informal; sometimes
