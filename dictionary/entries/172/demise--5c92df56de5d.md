### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The conveyance or transfer of an estate, either in fee for life or for years, most commonly the latter.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Transmission by formal act or conveyance to an heir or successor; transference; especially, the transfer or transmission of the crown or royal authority to a successor.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Death; decease.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source synonyms:** fatality; quietus; death; end; tomb (literary); celestial transfer (slang); decease; decomposition; defunction (obsolete); dematerialization; demise; dirt nap (slang); doom; exit (euphemistic; often); expiration; fatality; liquidation; mortality (figuratively); passing; quietus; repose; sleep of the just (dated; euphemistic); sunset; the big sleep; crash; downfall; fall; (the) bane; (the) end

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The end of something, in a negative sense; downfall.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** The lack of funding ultimately led to the project's demise.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The atmospheric disintegration of a satellite or satellite component upon re-entering Earth's atmosphere.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Relocating components to places where they receive more heating effect earlier in the reentry and even triggering a partial break-up of the satellite structure during reentry to aid demise.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; technology
