### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A group of attendants or servants, especially of someone considered important.

   **Usage:** collective

   **Example:** the queen’s retinues

   **Source synonyms:** entourage; retain (obsolete; rare); cortege

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A group of warriors or nobles accompanying a king or other leader; comitatus.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>
