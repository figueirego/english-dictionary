### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The ratio of an object's longest dimension to its next-longest dimension.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The ratio of width to height in a flat surface or 2-dimensional abstract construction, such as an image, character, or pixel.
