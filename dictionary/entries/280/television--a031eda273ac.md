**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An electronic communication medium that allows the transmission of real-time visual images, and often sound.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** She watched television for over five hours last night.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** broadcasting; media

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An electronic home entertainment device equipped with a screen and a speaker for receiving television signals and displaying them in audio-visual form.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** I have an old television in the study.

   **Source synonyms:** television set; TV set; idiot box
