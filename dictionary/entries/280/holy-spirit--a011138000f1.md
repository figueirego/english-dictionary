## Proper noun · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Forms

- the Holy Spirit — canonical

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The person (hypostasis) of the Trinity or Godhead corresponding to divine essence present in the faithful (particularly inspired prophets) and considered to proceed either from (Eastern Orthodoxy) God the Father alone or (Roman Catholicism) from Him together with God the Son.

   **Example:** In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The spirit of God, especially the gifts of wisdom and prophecy.

   **Usage:** Judaism

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The spirit of God who delivers messages to humanity in the form of Manifestations of God.
