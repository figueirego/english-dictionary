## Noun

### Forms

- netherworlds — plural
- nether world — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The place to which one's spirit descends upon death, conceived as below the surface of the earth.

   **Example:** In some religions, one's soul departs to a netherworld, instead of the heavens.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The locale of the spirit world or afterlife, whether deemed to be situated below the world of the living or not.

   **Example:** While I was astrally projecting, I felt my soul pass through many insubstantial netherworlds.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Specifically, a location of punishment in the afterlife; a hell.

   **Example:** The missionary was motivated by a sincere desire to rescue souls from eternal torment in the netherworld.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A hidden, shadowy, or sinister subculture, such as that of organized crime.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** Richard had been initiated into the netherworld of dog racing by his father, and knew all the popular greyhound performance-enhancing drugs.
