### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To purchase (something) that (somebody else) intends to buy.

   **Usage:** ditransitive

   **Example:** We better make an offer before somebody buys the house out from under us.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To make a payment to escape from or cancel (a problem).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He bought out from under his legal problems.
