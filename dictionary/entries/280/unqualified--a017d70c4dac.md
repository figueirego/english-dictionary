### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Not qualified&#58; ineligible; unfit for a position or task.

   **Example:** His lack of a high school diploma renders him unqualified for the job.

   **Source synonyms:** ineligible; nonqualified

   **Source antonyms:** competent; eligible; qualified

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Not elaborated upon; undescribed; unrestricted.

   **Example:** The right to free speech is well established as not being an unqualified right to violent incitement nor to free amplification.

   **Source synonyms:** undescribed

   **Source antonyms:** described; qualified

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Outright; thorough; utter; unhampered.

   **Example:** an unqualified success
