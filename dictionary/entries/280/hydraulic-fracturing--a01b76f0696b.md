## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A technique in which a mixture of water and sand is forced down an oil well (or similar) in order to create fractures in the oil-bearing rock and thus release more oil.

   **Usage:** uncountable; oil industry

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** fracing; fracking; hydrofracking
