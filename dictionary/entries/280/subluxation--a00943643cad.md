### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A partial dislocation of a joint, in which the articulating surfaces remain in partial contact.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The patient experienced subluxations of his right ulna, his lower right rear molar, and the lens of his left eye.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; pathology; sciences
