### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Owed or owing.

   **Example:** He is due four weeks of back pay.

   **Example:** The amount due is just three quid.

   **Example:** The due bills total nearly seven thousand dollars.

   **Example:** He can wait for the amount due him.

   **Source synonyms:** needed; owing; to be made; required

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Appropriate.

   **Example:** With all due respect, you're wrong about that.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Scheduled; expected.

   **Example:** Rain is due this afternoon.

   **Example:** The train is due in five minutes.

   **Example:** When is your baby due?

   **Source synonyms:** expected; forecast

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Having reached the expected, scheduled, or natural time.

   **Example:** The baby is just about due.

   **Source synonyms:** expected

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Owing; ascribable, as to a cause.

   **Example:** The dangerously low water table is due to rapidly growing pumping.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. On a direct bearing, especially for the four points of the compass.

   **Example:** The town is 5 miles due North of the bridge.
