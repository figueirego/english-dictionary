### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A change in nature, form, or quality.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The spread of something harmful to another location, such as the metastasis of a cancer.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A sudden or rapid transition from one point, topic or argument to another, often to evade an uncomfortable subject or to redirect the discussion.

   **Usage:** countable; rhetoric; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The transference of a bodily function or disease to another part of the body, specifically the development of a secondary area of disease remote from the original site, as with some cancers.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; oncology; sciences
