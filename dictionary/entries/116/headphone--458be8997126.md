<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. singular of headphones&#58; a cushioned speaker placed on, over, or inside the ear, normally used in left and right pairs.

   **Usage:** form-of; plural-normally; singular

   **Example:** Some joggers wear only one headphone in their ears, to enjoy music while paying attention to their environment.
