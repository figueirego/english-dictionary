## Noun

### Forms

- lowriders — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A vehicle, usually a passenger car, with its suspension system modified so that it rides as low to level ground as possible without dragging.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The driver or a frequent passenger of such a vehicle.

   **Usage:** slang

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Attributive form of lowriders.

   **Usage:** attributive; form-of

   **Form of:** [lowriders](../2b4/lowriders--ad395017b551.md)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Used to describe a dog known for its short legs (e.g. a dachshund, corgi, basset hound, etc.)

   **Usage:** informal
