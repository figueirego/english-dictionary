### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To disrupt someone's composure or comfort with acting publicly or freely.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The crowd's laughter and jeers embarrassed him.

   **Source synonyms:** abase; abash; bring low; confuse; confound; debase; degrade; demean; depress; disconcert; discomfit; dishonour; embarrass; humble; humiliate; lower; mortify; put to shame; shame

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To hinder from liberty of movement; to impede; to obstruct.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The motion was advanced in order to embarrass the progress of the bill.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To involve in difficulties concerning money matters; to encumber with debt; to beset with urgent claims or demands.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** A man or his business is embarrassed when he cannot meet his pecuniary engagements.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To perplex mentally; confuse, disconcert; catch off guard.

   **Usage:** formal; transitive
