### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The property or quality of being repeatable.
   1. The absence of variation amongst multiple measurements taken under the same conditions.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The property or quality of being repeatable.
   1. The degree to which a machine can move its parts to the same position (location) repeatedly, which is closely (and mathematically) related to its precision and accuracy.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable
