### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An establishment that sells goods or services to the public; originally only a physical location, but now a virtual establishment as well.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A place where things are manufactured or crafted; a workshop.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A large garage where vehicle mechanics work.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The car's in the shop right now.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Workplace; office. Used mainly in expressions such as shop talk, closed shop and shop floor.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Discussion of business or professional affairs.

   **Usage:** figuratively; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A variety of classes taught in junior or senior high school that teach vocational skills.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. An establishment where a barber or beautician works.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** a barber shop

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. An act of shopping, especially routine shopping for food and other domestic supplies.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** This is where I do my weekly shop.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. The collective items bought (or to be bought) on a shopping trip.

   **Usage:** UK; colloquial; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** I’ve got a big shop, so feel free to go in front.

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To visit stores or shops to browse or explore merchandise, especially with the intention of buying such merchandise.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Why don’t you come shopping for gifts with me, early before the Christmas rush?

   **Example:** He’s (gone) shopping for clothes.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To browse or purchase products from (a catalog, an internet website, etc.), mostly from home.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Shop our new arrivals.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To report the criminal activities or whereabouts of someone to an authority.

   **Usage:** UK; slang; transitive

   **Example:** He shopped his mates in to the police.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To imprison.

   **Usage:** UK; slang; transitive

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To photoshop; to digitally edit a picture or photograph.

   **Usage:** Internet; transitive

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To dismiss from employment.

   **Usage:** archaic; slang; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** fire; sack

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

**Source sense R2.S7**

7. To investigate or evaluate as a mystery shopper.

   **Usage:** colloquial; transitive

<a id="r2-s8"></a>

**Source sense R2.S8**

8. To pitch (a book, script, etc.) to a publisher or studio.
