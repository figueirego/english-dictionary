### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having the same relationship, each to each other.

   **Example:** They were mutual enemies.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

2. Collective, done or held in common.

   **Example:** Mutual insurance.

3. Reciprocal.

   **Example:** They had mutual fear of each other.
