### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A young plant grown from seed.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences

   **Source synonyms:** sprout; chit

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any young plant, especially&#58;
   1. one grown in a nursery for transplanting.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any young plant, especially&#58;
   1. a tree smaller than a sapling.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A variety that originated from a volunteer that grew ferally from a seedling (sense 1) and was discovered, named, and subsequently propagated via grafting. (In some cases the parent variety is known.)

   **Example:** a chance seedling

   **Example:** a seedling apple

   **Example:** The Ambrosia is a seedling of Jonagold.
