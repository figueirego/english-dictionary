### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A railroad station shared by more than one railroad company, usually to spread the costs and ease the logistics of serving a particular town by rail.

   **Usage:** Canada; US

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** rail-transport; railways; transport
