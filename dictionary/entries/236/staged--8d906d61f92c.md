### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of an event, intentionally performed while being falsely contrived to appear authentic or coincidental.

   **Example:** The car accident was actually staged so he could collect the insurance money.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Intended for the stage as in a theater.

   **Example:** The staged performance was good, but I liked the book better.
