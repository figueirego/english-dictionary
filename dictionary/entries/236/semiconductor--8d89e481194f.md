### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A substance with electrical properties intermediate between a good conductor and a good insulator.

   **Example:** Integrated circuits are made from semiconductors, especially silicon.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Ellipsis of semiconductor device.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; ellipsis; metonymically

   **Example:** The company supplies semiconductors to laptop, server, and smartphone manufacturers.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business

   **Source synonyms:** chip; microchip; integrated circuit
