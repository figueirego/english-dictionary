### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Prone to cause obsession.

   **Example:** The idea is too tempting, it's obsessive.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Having one thought or pursuing one activity (an obsession) to the absolute or nearly absolute exclusion of all others.

   **Example:** Hardcore fans' obsessive behavior may take over their lives.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Excessive, as results from obsession.

   **Example:** A workaholic's obsessive zeal may lead to success or burnout.
