## Noun

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˌtɹɛp.ɪˈdeɪ.ʃən/ — source labels: Received-Pronunciation; US

### Forms

- trepidations — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Anxiety over the uncertain future or possible ill-occurrence.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** I decided, with considerable trepidation, to let him drive my car without me.

   **Source synonyms:** agitation; apprehension; consternation; fear; hesitation; worry

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An involuntary trembling, sometimes an effect of paralysis, but usually caused by terror or fear.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** quaking; quivering

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A libration of the starry sphere in the Ptolemaic system; a motion ascribed to the firmament, to account for certain small changes in the position of the ecliptic and of the stars.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astronomy; natural-sciences
