### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A motorist who occupies the right (especially rightmost) lane (passing lane) of a multi-lane road or highway, without moving over to allow faster-moving traffic to pass.

   **Source synonyms:** right lane hogger; right lane hugger; right lane camper
