## Prep\_phrase

### Forms

- on the streets — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Without a home; without the means to afford good shelter.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** We haven't been able to pay rent for five months. Next month we'll be on the street.

   **Source synonyms:** homeless; houseless; unhoused; unsheltered

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In public, including daily life and the crime that sometimes occurs there; especially, in the world of street crime and those whose daily life is immersed in it.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** At that time, a greenback was worth more on the street than it was at the foreign exchange office.

   **Example:** Their attitude is that the police commissioner's bluster isn't worth much on the street.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. In actual practice, as opposed to in a laboratory, classroom, etc.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** These students are eager to test their knowledge on the street instead of in the exam book.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Working as a sex worker.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** Times got a little desperate and she was on the street for a while.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Publicly known

   **Example:** She put it on the street, so now everyone knows.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see on, the, street.

   **Example:** The pavement markings on the street were confusing to foreigners.
