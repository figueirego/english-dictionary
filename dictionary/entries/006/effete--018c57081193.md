### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. exhausted, spent, worn-out.

   **Usage:** obsolete; quantities etc

   **Source context (verbatim):** of substances

   **Source synonyms:** low; used up; consumed; depleted; out; done up (obsolete); effete (obsolete); exhausted; all (regional); expended; gone; finished; forworn (archaic); low; spent; used up

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Lacking strength or vitality; feeble, powerless, impotent.

   **Source synonyms:** low; puny; anemic; breakable; broken-down; damaged; debilitated; decrepit; delicate; dickless; effete; enervated; enfeebled; exhausted; faint; feeble; flimsy; fragile; frail; harmed; hurt; impaired; impotent; infirm; injured; languid; low; powerless; prostrated; prostrate; puny; raddled; resistless; sapped; slightsoft; softened; susceptible; tender; tired; torpid; unresistant; vincible; vulnerable; wasted; weak; weakened; weary; weedy (Britain; Ireland); wimpy; worn out; yielding

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Decadent, weak through self-indulgence.

   **Source synonyms:** dissipated; incontinent
