### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make (someone or something) neat; to arrange (people or things) in an orderly, tidy way; to tidy.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** She made a frantic attempt to neaten her hair.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To engage in activity that arranges someone or something in an orderly, tidy way.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Don't mind me if I neaten while you talk.
