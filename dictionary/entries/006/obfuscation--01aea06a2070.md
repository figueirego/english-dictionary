### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act or process of obfuscating, or obscuring the perception of something; the concept of concealing the meaning of a communication by making it more confusing and harder to interpret.

   **Usage:** formal; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The act or process of obfuscating, or obscuring the perception of something; the concept of concealing the meaning of a communication by making it more confusing and harder to interpret.
   1. The alteration of computer code to preserve its behavior while concealing its structure and intent.

      **Usage:** formal; uncountable

      **Example:** You need to turn on obfuscation for these classes.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Confusion, bewilderment, or a baffled state resulting from something obfuscated, or made more opaque and muddled with the intent to obscure information.

   **Usage:** formal; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A single instance of intentionally obscuring the meaning of something to make it more difficult to grasp.

   **Usage:** countable; formal

   **Example:** During the debate, the candidate sighed at his opponent's obfuscations.
