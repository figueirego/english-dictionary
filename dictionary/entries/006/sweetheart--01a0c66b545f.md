### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who is always very kind.

   **Example:** She is such a sweetheart&#58; she never complains about me being late.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person very much liked or loved by someone, especially when both partners are young.

   **Example:** John married his high-school sweetheart in 1981.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A term of endearment, especially for a lover or child.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A female member of a college or university fraternity.

   **Usage:** US
