### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Expression signifying a pause or hesitation.

   **Usage:** informal

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Expression used to imply meaning, rather than say it, such as when a person is embarrassed.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** A&#58; I can’t go to the party because... you know... B&#58; Your mom is still mad at you, huh? A&#58; Yeah...

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used as a rhetorical question to confirm agreement, knowing or understanding at the end of a statement.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** But I guess that's life, you know?

   **Example:** I just wasn't into it, you know?

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Used to introduce information.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** You know, he's actually pretty nice.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Used as a generic, conversational discourse marker, for casual emphasis.

   **Usage:** Singapore; Singlish

   **Source synonyms:** know

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see you, know.
