## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An obsolete scientific theory, chiefly developed by René Descartes, which attempted to explain celestial mechanics and the phenomena now described as gravitation by positing a system of fluid vortices governed by centrifugal forces and extending outwards from the sun.

   **Usage:** historical; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The vortex theory of the atom&#58; a theory by Lord Kelvin, to explain why there are relatively few types of atom, but a huge number of each type.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see vortex, theory.
