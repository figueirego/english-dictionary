## Interjection

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A general reassurance not to be concerned further with a subject.
   1. Be reassured that it does not bother me; I am not concerned.

      **Usage:** Australia; Canada; Malaysia; New-Zealand; Singapore; UK; colloquial; especially

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A general reassurance not to be concerned further with a subject.
   1. It was no problem; not at all; you're welcome (particularly, though not strictly, when the favour being acknowledged was requested or in response to a difficulty, rather than spontaneous).

      **Usage:** Australia; Canada; Malaysia; New-Zealand; Singapore; UK; colloquial; especially

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A general reassurance not to be concerned further with a subject.
   1. Do not worry, I will do it.

      **Usage:** Australia; Canada; Malaysia; New-Zealand; Singapore; UK; colloquial; especially
