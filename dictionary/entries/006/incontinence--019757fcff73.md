### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Lack of self-restraint, an inability to control oneself; unchastity.

   **Usage:** countable; dated; uncountable

   **Example:** verbal incontinence

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The inability of any of the physical organs to restrain discharges of their contents; involuntary discharge or evacuation (of urine or feces).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences; urology
