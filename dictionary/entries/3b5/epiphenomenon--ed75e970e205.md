### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An activity, process, or state that is the result of another; a by-product, a consequence.

   **Source synonyms:** by-product; unintended consequence; side effect

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A mental process or state that is an incidental by-product of physiological events in the brain or nervous system.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; philosophy; psychology; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A symptom that develops during the course of a disease that is not connected to the disease.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; pathology; sciences
