### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A game or confrontation in which two individuals intently gaze at each other, until the other individual is forced to blink or turn away.

   **Usage:** informal

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A confrontation in which each party waits for the other to yield.

   **Usage:** idiomatic
