## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A type of single bed with a frame in three sections in order to raise the head, middle or foot as required.

   **Example:** The family bought a hospital bed from a DME supplier so that their grandmother could continue living at home with her family.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One inpatient bed, as a unit of measure of hospital capacity and utilization thereof.

   **Example:** The region is expected to add 800 hospital beds over the next 5 years.
