### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Elision, suppression, or complete loss of a letter or sound (syllable) from the beginning of a word, such as the development of special from especial.

   **Usage:** Canada; US; countable; rhetoric; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; phonetics; phonology; prosody; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** pheresis; procope

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The removal of blood from a patient, and the removal of certain components (such as platelets) from that blood, followed by the transfusion of the filtered blood back to the donor (patient).

   **Usage:** Canada; US; countable; specific; uncountable; still current

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** pheresis; hemapheresis

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Extirpation or extraction of a superfluity (especially a pathological one) from the body, especially blood.

   **Usage:** Canada; US; countable; general; obsolete; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences
