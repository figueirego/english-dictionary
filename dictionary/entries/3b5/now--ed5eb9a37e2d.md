## Adverb · Etymology 1 · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /naʊ/ — source labels: General-American; Ireland; Received-Pronunciation
- IPA: /nuː/ — source labels: Northumbria
- IPA: /næʊ/ — variety unspecified in the source labels
- IPA: /nɛʊ/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Forms

- noo — alternative; Geordie; Scotland; dialectal

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. At the present time.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Now I am six.

   **Example:** Stop that now, Jimmy!

   **Example:** I could have been Secretary of State now if I hadn’t left politics five years ago.

   **Example:** We can’t phone now – they will certainly have gone to bed.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Used to introduce a point, a qualification of what has previously been said, a remonstration or a rebuke.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Now, we all want what is best for our children.

   **Example:** Now, Jimmy, stop that.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. Differently from the immediate past; differently from a more remote past or a possible future; differently from all other times.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Now I am ready.

   **Example:** We all now want the latest toys for our children.

   **Example:** We all want what is now best for our children.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. At the time reached within a narration.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Now he remembered why he had come.

   **Example:** He now asked her whether she had made pudding.

   **Example:** The pudding was now ready to be served.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. Used to indicate a context of urgency.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Now listen, we must do something about this.

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. At the present point of a recurring cycle or event.

   **Usage:** informal; not-comparable

   **Example:** I always used to do my shopping now, to avoid the rush.

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

**Source sense R2.S7**

7. Very recently; not long ago; up to the present.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; obsolete

   **Source context (verbatim):** as but now

<a id="r2-s8"></a>

**Source sense R2.S8**

8. Used to address a switching side, or sharp change in attitude from before.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; usually emphasized

   **Example:** Now, you want to protect me. An hour ago, you were mercilessly bullying me!

<a id="r2-s9"></a>

**Source sense R2.S9**

9. Sometimes; occasionally; used to list a series of often assumed states.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** His face fit his roles&#58; now smiling, now earnest, now glowering, now raging.
