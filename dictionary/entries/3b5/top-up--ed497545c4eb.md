### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To refill (or recharge) something.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** I shall top up with petrol before the long trip.

   **Example:** Please top up the tank tonight.

   **Source synonyms:** top off

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To refill (or recharge) something.
   1. To add to a balance in an account (e.g., mobile payment, debit card, bank account).

      **Usage:** ambitransitive; idiomatic

      **Example:** I shall top up the mobile phone at the supermarket.

      **Example:** Users can top up their wallet balance and start sending and receiving money between mobile phones.

      **Source synonyms:** top off; add value

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To refill (or recharge) something.
   1. To refill a drink.

      **Usage:** UK; ambitransitive

      **Example:** The waitress topped up my coffee every few minutes.

      **Source synonyms:** top off; top off
