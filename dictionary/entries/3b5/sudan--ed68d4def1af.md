### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A country in North Africa and East Africa.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A country in North Africa and East Africa.
   1. Turkish Sudan; autonym Turkiyyah; Sudan under the Eyalet of Egypt, from 1820 to 1885

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A country in North Africa and East Africa.
   1. Mahdist State, also known as Mahdist Sudan or the Sudanese Mahdiyya, from 1885 to 1898

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A country in North Africa and East Africa.
   1. Anglo-Egyptian Sudan, a condominium of the United Kingdom and Egypt from 1899 to 1955

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A country in North Africa and East Africa.
   1. Republic of the Sudan, since 1955

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A climatic zone characterized by savannas and mosaic forests running in a belt just south of the Sahel in West and East Africa

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Mali (when it was under French colonial rule as Soudan).

   **Usage:** countable; historical; uncountable

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A minor city in Lamb County, Texas, United States, apparently named after the African region.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
