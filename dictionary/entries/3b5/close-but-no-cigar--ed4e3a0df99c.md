**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used to indicate that one is almost correct or has almost succeeded, but not quite.

   **Usage:** colloquial; idiomatic

   **Example:** Betty ran all out in the sprint race; yet, it was close, but no cigar.

   **Source synonyms:** almost doesn't count; a miss is as good as a mile
