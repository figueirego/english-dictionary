## Noun · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A trolley pole; a single-pole device for collecting electrical current from an overhead electrical line, normally for a tram/streetcar or a trolleybus.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A streetcar or light train.

   **Usage:** US

   **Source synonyms:** tram (UK); trolley car

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A light rail, tramway, trolleybus or streetcar system.

   **Usage:** US; colloquial

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A truck from which the load is suspended in some kinds of cranes.

   **Source synonyms:** crane trolley; traveling trolley

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A truck which travels along the fixed conductors in an electric railway, and forms a means of connection between them and a railway car.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A cart or shopping cart; a shopping trolley.

   **Usage:** Australia; Ireland; New-Zealand; UK

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Clipping of flatbed trolley.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; clipping

   **Alternative form of:** [flatbed trolley](../260/flatbed-trolley--982df87562fe.md)

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A hand truck.

   **Usage:** UK

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A soapbox car.

   **Usage:** UK

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. A gurney, a stretcher with wheeled legs.

   **Usage:** UK

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. A handcar.
