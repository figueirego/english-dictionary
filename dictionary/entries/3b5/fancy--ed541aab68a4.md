### Senses

<a id="r4-s1"></a>

**Source sense R4.S1**

1. To appreciate without jealousy or greed.

   **Usage:** formal

   **Example:** I fancy your new car, but I like my old one just fine.

<a id="r4-s2"></a>

**Source sense R4.S2**

2. Would like; have a desire for.

   **Usage:** UK

   **Example:** I fancy a burger tonight for dinner.

   **Example:** Do you fancy going to town this weekend?

   **Source synonyms:** feel like

<a id="r4-s3"></a>

**Source sense R4.S3**

3. To be sexually, aesthetically or romantically attracted to.

   **Usage:** UK; informal

   **Example:** I fancy that girl over there.

   **Source synonyms:** like (US)

<a id="r4-s4"></a>

**Source sense R4.S4**

4. To imagine, suppose.

   **Usage:** dated

   **Example:** \[dated\] I fancy you'll want something to drink after your long journey.

   **Example:** I fancy this is an error.

   **Example:** Fancy meeting you here!

   **Example:** Fancy that! I saw Elizabeth Bennett and Mr. Darcy kissing in the garden.

   **Example:** You won't get the gig just because you fancy yourself a musician.

   **Source context (verbatim):** in a set phrase

<a id="r4-s5"></a>

**Source sense R4.S5**

5. To form a conception of; to portray in the mind.

   **Source synonyms:** imagine

<a id="r4-s6"></a>

**Source sense R4.S6**

6. To have a fancy for; to like; to be pleased with, particularly on account of external appearance or manners.

<a id="r4-s7"></a>

**Source sense R4.S7**

   **Usage:** transitive
