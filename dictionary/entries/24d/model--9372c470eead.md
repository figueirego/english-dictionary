### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who poses as a subject or visual reference for an artist.

   **Example:** Leonardo da Vinci chose a 15th century criminal as a model for Judas in his painting The Last Supper.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person who wears and showcases clothing, accessories, or cosmetics to promote or display them to an audience.

   **Example:** The supersize model displayed the latest summer shorts fashion, tactfully representing similarly overweight teenagers.

   **Example:** My mother was a hand model when she was young.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A person, usually attractive, who is hired to show items or goods to the public, such as items that are given away as prizes on a TV game show.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A person who performs or poses in erotic or pornographic media.

   **Usage:** adult entertainment

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A representation of a physical object.

   **Example:** The boy played with a model of a World War II fighter plane.

   **Example:** The biology teacher passed a large plastic model of the atomic structure of DNA around the classroom.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A simulation used to analyze the workings of a real world system or event.

   **Example:** The computer weather model did not correctly predict the path of the hurricane.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A set of parameters trained on data to perform artificial intelligence tasks such as prediction, classification, or generation.

   **Example:** machine learning model

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A style, type, or design.

   **Example:** we service all makes and models

   **Example:** He decided to buy the turbo engine model of the sports car.

   **Example:** This year’s model features four doors instead of two.

   **Example:** Q&#58; What make and model of car do you drive? A&#58; It’s a Buick LaCrosse.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. The structural design of a complex system.

   **Example:** The team developed a sound business model.

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. A successful example to be copied, with or without modifications.

   **Example:** He was a model of eloquence and virtue.

   **Example:** British parliamentary democracy was seen as a model for other countries to follow.

   **Source synonyms:** role model

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. An interpretation function which assigns a truth value to each atomic proposition.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; logic; mathematics; philosophy; sciences

<a id="r1-s12"></a>

**Source sense R1.S12**

12. An interpretation which makes a set of sentences true, in which case that interpretation is called a model of that set.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; logic; mathematics; philosophy; sciences

<a id="r1-s13"></a>

**Source sense R1.S13**

13. An organism or species that is used to study an aspect of physiology or pathophysiology or a particular disease; often, a nonhuman one used to approximate processes in humans.

   **Example:** They studied fasting and glycemia in a mouse model.

   **Example:** They studied Mendelian inheritance in a fruit fly model.

   **Example:** They studied plasmid transfer in a mycobacterial model.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; medicine; natural-sciences; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** model organism

<a id="r1-s14"></a>

**Source sense R1.S14**

14. Any copy, or resemblance, more or less exact.

<a id="r1-s15"></a>

**Source sense R1.S15**

15. In software applications using the model-view-controller design pattern, the part or parts of the application that manage the data.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** architecture; computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences; software

### Senses

<a id="r3-s1"></a>

**Source sense R3.S1**

1. To create or modify something with the traits of an example.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Many countries have modeled their constitutions on the U.S. Constitution.

<a id="r3-s2"></a>

**Source sense R3.S2**

2. To create from a substance such as clay.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The sculptor modelled the clay into the form of a dolphin.

<a id="r3-s3"></a>

**Source sense R3.S3**

3. To make a miniature model of.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He takes great pride in his skill at modeling airplanes.

<a id="r3-s4"></a>

**Source sense R3.S4**

4. To make a model or models.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r3-s5"></a>

**Source sense R3.S5**

5. To devise a simulation of.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** They modelled weather patterns using a computer.

   **Source synonyms:** pattern

<a id="r3-s6"></a>

**Source sense R3.S6**

6. To display an item on one's body for others to see the potential effect on their own bodies, especially in regard to wearing clothing while performing the role of a fashion model.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** She modelled the shoes for her friends to appreciate.

<a id="r3-s7"></a>

**Source sense R3.S7**

7. To work as a model in art or fashion.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The actress used to model before being discovered by Hollywood.
