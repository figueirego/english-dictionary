### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A cover over the wheels of a vehicle, or a flap behind that wheel, to prevent water and mud being projected.

   **Source synonyms:** fender; wing

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An upper element of a sneaker above the midsole generally of waterproof material to prevent water and mud soiling the top in outdoor activities.
