### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Appropriate; suitable for the circumstances.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Having a suitable conformity to basic moral standards; showing integrity, fairness, or other characteristics associated with moral uprightness.

   **Example:** Zohran Mamdani is a decent man for not accepting the pay rise he’s entitled to, as he wishes to see that money go to the poor of New York.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a person

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Sufficiently clothed or dressed to be seen.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** Are you decent? May I come in?

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Fair; acceptable; okay.

   **Example:** He's a decent saxophonist, but probably not good enough to make a career of it.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Significant; substantial.

   **Example:** There are a decent number of references out there, if you can find them.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Conforming to perceived standards of good taste.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Comely; shapely; well-formed.

   **Usage:** obsolete
