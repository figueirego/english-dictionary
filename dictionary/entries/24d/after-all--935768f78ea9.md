## Prep\_phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Anyway, in any case; indicates a statement is true regardless of other considerations; used to reinforce or explain a point.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** After all, they never come home for Christmas.

   **Example:** Of course he won't give you credit. After all, his first and last concern is his company's profit margin.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In the end, however; used in referring to something that was believed to be the case, but is not; or to an outcome that is not what was expected or predicted.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** They won't be coming home for Christmas after all.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Introduces information that supports the preceding statement.

   **Example:** Of course he's coming to the party. After all, he did promise.
