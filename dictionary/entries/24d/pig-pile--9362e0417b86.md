### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To cause a group of people to lie in a pile upon another, originally as a punishment to the victim on the bottom.

   **Usage:** US; colloquial; transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To jump into such a pile.

   **Usage:** US; ambitransitive; colloquial

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

   **Usage:** US; ambitransitive; colloquial; figuratively
