### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Based on unanimity, assent or agreement.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The debate went on for hours, but in the end the decision was unanimous.

   **Source synonyms:** solid

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Sharing the same views or opinions, and being in harmony or accord.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** We were unanimous&#58; the President had to go.
