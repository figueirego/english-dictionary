**Source sense R1.S1**

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A written or printed announcement.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** A series of notices signpost patients to the X-ray department.

   **Example:** A notice was attached to the building reminding the residents about the five hour water shortage that was happening today.

   **Example:** I always read the death notices in the paper.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A formal notification or warning.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** The sidewalk adjacent to the damaged bridge stonework shall be closed until further notice.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Advance notification of termination of employment, given by an employer to an employee or vice versa.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** I gave her her mandatory two weeks' notice and sacked her.

   **Example:** I can't work here any longer. I'm giving notice.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A published critical review of a play or the like.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Prior notification.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** I don't mind if you want to change the venue; just give me some notice first, OK?

   **Usage:** countable; dated; uncountable
