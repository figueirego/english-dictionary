### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To give money or other compensation to in exchange for goods or services.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** pay cash or pay in cash, pay by credit/debit card

   **Example:** pay in dollars/euros, etc.

   **Example:** How much will the job pay?

   **Example:** She is paying him to clean the place up.

   **Example:** He doesn't have money, but he pays rent in chores and housework.

   **Example:** He was paid off the books and in kind where possible.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To deposit into an account.

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** I pay a portion of each paycheck into a savings account.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To discharge, as a debt or other obligation, by giving or doing what is due or required.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** to pay compensation, to pay a fine

   **Example:** She offered to pay the bill.

   **Example:** He is paying his debt to society.

   **Example:** He was allowed to go as soon as the kidnappers were paid.

   **Source synonyms:** pay off; repay

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To be profitable; to be worth it.

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** It didn't pay him to keep the store open any more.

   **Example:** Crime doesn’t pay.

   **Example:** It will pay to wait.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To cover, as a cost.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Those investments paid for her college education.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To yield as a benefit.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to pay dividends; to pay interest

   **Example:** You will get paid from this investment yearly.

   **Source synonyms:** pay out; yield; return

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To give (something else than money).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to pay attention, to pay tribute, to pay a visit

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To suffer consequences, especially justly.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** He paid for his fun in the sun with a terrible sunburn.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To admit that a joke, punchline, etc., was funny.

   **Usage:** transitive
