### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having existed for so great a period of time that its longevity cannot be expressed.

   **Usage:** relative to past

   **Example:** The ageless pyramids stood prominently against the sunset.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Continuing infinitely or indefinitely.

   **Usage:** relative to future

   **Example:** This memorial will show our ageless respect to those who died.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Always appearing youthful; never seeming to age.

   **Example:** her ageless face
