### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A network of criss-crossing lines, strands, cables or pipes.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The students studied the reticulation of the veins in the leaf.

   **Source synonyms:** graticulation

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
