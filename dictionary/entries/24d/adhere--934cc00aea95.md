### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To stick fast or cleave, as a glutinous substance does; to become joined or united.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Wax adhered to his finger.

   **Source synonyms:** cleave; cling; stick

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To be attached or devoted by personal union, in belief, on principle, etc.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To be consistent or coherent; to be in accordance; to agree.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To fasten by adhesion.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To affirm a judgment.

   **Usage:** intransitive; Scots law

### Related words
