<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To untruthfully speak of another in a belittling, deprecative, slighting manner, either in person or in absentia.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; slang

   **Source synonyms:** slander; talk shit; trash-talk; assassinate (figuratively); asperse; backbite; badmouth; befoul (figuratively); belibel (dated); besmirch; besmear; bewray (archaic); blacken; blame (obsolete); blaze; blemish; blot; blur; calumniate; calumny; chat shit (Commonwealth; slang; vulgar); chat shite (Ireland; UK; slang; vulgar); cloud; defame; demonize (figuratively); denigrate; deprave; depreciate; detract; dirty; discredit; disgrace; disparage; disrepute; diss (slang); drag through the mud (idiomatic); forgab (obsolete); forspeak (obsolete); foul; infamize; lampoon; libel; malign; monsterize; muddy (figuratively); reproach; revile; scandal (obsolete); shit-talk (slang; vulgar); slag (UK; slang); slander (obsolete); smear; smirch (archaic; figuratively); spatter (figuratively; obsolete); spot; speak ill of; stab (figuratively); stain; sully; talk shit (slang; vulgar); talk shite (UK; slang; vulgar); talk trash (US; slang); tarnish; torch (figuratively); traduce; trash-talk (US; slang); unflatter; vilify; vilipend (dated; formal); villainize; wrong
