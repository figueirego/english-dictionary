### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A highly cytotoxic vesicant, C₄H₈Cl₂S, used in World War I that is known to cause skin burns, blisters, inflammation, edema, and potentially other respiratory effects.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The man who survived mustard gas and pepper spray is now a seasoned veteran.
