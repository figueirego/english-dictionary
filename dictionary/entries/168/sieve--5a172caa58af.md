### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A device with a mesh, grate, or otherwise perforated bottom to separate, in a granular material, larger particles from smaller ones, or to separate solid objects from a liquid.

   **Example:** Use the sieve to get the pasta from the water.

   **Source synonyms:** sifter; strainer; temse

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A device with a mesh, grate, or otherwise perforated bottom to separate, in a granular material, larger particles from smaller ones, or to separate solid objects from a liquid.
   1. A kind of coarse basket.

      **Usage:** obsolete

      **Source synonyms:** sifter; strainer; temse

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A process, physical or abstract, that arrives at a final result by filtering out unwanted pieces of input from a larger starting set of input.
   1. One of several techniques for counting integers.

      **Example:** Given a list of consecutive numbers starting at 1, the Sieve of Eratosthenes algorithm will find all of the prime numbers.
