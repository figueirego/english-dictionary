### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of or pertaining to; an adjectival suffix appended to various words, often nouns, to make an adjective form. (Often added to words of Latin origin, but also used with words of other origins. When a word ends in a, -n is used instead.)

   **Usage:** morpheme

   **Example:** Rome + -an → Roman

   **Source synonyms:** -al; -ar; -ese; -esque; -ic; -id; -ish; -like; -oid; -ous; -y

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One from, belonging to, relating to, or like.

   **Usage:** morpheme

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Appended to nouns to form an occupation. (When males with a profession are distinguished from females, males are -an, females -(i)enne.)

   **Usage:** morpheme

   **Example:** comedy + -an → comedian

   **Example:** history + -an → historian

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Appended to words indicating sugars to form a polysaccharide.

   **Usage:** morpheme

   **Example:** glucose + -an → glucan

   **Example:** dextrose + -an → dextran
