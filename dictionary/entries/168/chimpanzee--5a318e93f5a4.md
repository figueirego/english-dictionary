### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Pan troglodytes, a species of great ape native to the savannas and forests of Africa, and believed by biologists to be the closest extant relative to humans along with the bonobo.

   **Source synonyms:** chimp

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A great ape of the genus Pan, including the bonobo which was formerly known as the pygmy chimpanzee.

   **Usage:** dated
