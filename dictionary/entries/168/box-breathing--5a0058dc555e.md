## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A breath-based relaxation technique that involves visualizing a journey around the four sides of a square, pausing while travelling horizontally and breathing in while travelling up the square and out while travelling down it.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** square breathing
