### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To faint; to become unconscious.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** I pass out at the sight of blood.

   **Example:** I passed out on the train after drinking a bottle of vodka.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To fall asleep irrespective of wilful action, as a result of the consumption of alcohol or other drugs or another form of physiological exhaustion.

   **Usage:** slang

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To distribute.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** We'll pass out copies of the agenda.

   **Source synonyms:** hand out; give out

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To graduate, usually marked by a ceremony at the end of training.

   **Usage:** usually; fire-fighters

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law-enforcement; police

   **Source context (verbatim):** of soldiers

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To become proficient in a particular job or task.

   **Usage:** broadly

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To graduate from university.

   **Usage:** India

   **Example:** He passed out of the Madras University in 1990.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To end (a round) by having passes as the first four bids.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** bridge; games

<a id="r1-s8"></a>
