### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In a critical manner; with, or in terms of, criticism.

   **Example:** I looked critically at the frayed carpet of the hotel room.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In terms of critique, review, of or by critics.

   **Example:** a critically applauded film

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. With close discernment; accurately; exactly.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. At a crisis or critical time; in a situation, place, or condition of decisive consequence.

   **Example:** a fortification critically situated

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Requiring immediate attention; likely to cause a collapse.

   **Example:** critically ill

   **Example:** critically injured

   **Example:** critically endangered
