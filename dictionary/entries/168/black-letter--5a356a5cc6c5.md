**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A highly calligraphic Western European script style used from approximately 1150.
   1. A Northern European style of type based on the script, with contrasting thick-and-thin, angular strokes forming upright letterforms, and usually set with a dark typographic colour on the page.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable
