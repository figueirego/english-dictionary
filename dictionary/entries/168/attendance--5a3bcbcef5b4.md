### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of attending; the state of being present; presence.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Attendance at the meeting is required.

   **Example:** All those in attendance are to sign this slip.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A tally or listing of the persons present.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The class sat down so that the teacher could take attendance.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The frequency with which one has been present for a regular activity or set of events.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** John's attendance for the conventions was not good.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Attention paid to something; careful regard.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Maid service.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
