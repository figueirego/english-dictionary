### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To receive pleasure or satisfaction from something.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Enjoy your holidays! I very much enjoy dancing.

   **Example:** I've really enjoyed you and your sister showing me around.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To have the use or benefit of something.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I plan to go travelling while I still enjoy good health.

   **Source synonyms:** rejoice

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To be satisfied or receive pleasure; to have fun.

   **Usage:** India; intransitive

   **Example:** I enjoyed a lot.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To have sexual intercourse with.

   **Usage:** transitive
