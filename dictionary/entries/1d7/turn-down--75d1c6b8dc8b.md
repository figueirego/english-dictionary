### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To refuse, decline, or deny.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** He turned down all our offers of help.

   **Source antonyms:** take up

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To reduce the power, etc. of something by means of a control, such as the volume, heat, or light.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** Turn down the television so I can hear myself think.

   **Example:** When it starts to boil, turn down the heat to a simmer.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To reposition by turning, flipping, etc. in a downward direction; to double or fold down.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** Turn down the blankets to let them air out.
