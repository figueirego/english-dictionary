## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of reading data back from a location where it has been stored.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The repetition of a message one has received, in order to acknowledge it and to demonstrate accurate comprehension (especially of commands).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** aeronautics; aerospace; aviation; business; communications; electrical-engineering; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; telecommunications

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The reading aloud by a transcriptionist, court reporter, or stenographer of draft correspondence or of testimony previously taken down in dictation, usually at the request of the presiding judge or parties involved in a deposition (courtroom contexts) or of the manager dictating the correspondence (business contexts).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** OK, now let's do a readback before you send this off.

   **Example:** The judge ordered a readback of a portion of the testimony.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** communications; journalism; literature; media; publishing; stenography; writing
