**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To have.

   **Usage:** UK; especially; idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** I'd got her by the collar.

   **Example:** Jim's got his eyes closed, so you don't have to worry about him seeing you undressed.

   **Example:** José's got a car, hasn't he? Neither you nor I have.

   **Example:** I have got to do my homework.
