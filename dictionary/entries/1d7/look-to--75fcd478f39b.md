### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see look, to.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To seek inspiration or advice or reward from someone.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** Whenever I'm upset, I look to Mary to cheer me up.

   **Source synonyms:** run to; turn to

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

   **Usage:** idiomatic

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To intend to; to prepare to; to seem likely to.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I think that cat was looking to bite me if I hadn't run away!

   **Example:** A storm looks to be on the way.
