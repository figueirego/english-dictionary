### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To execute (a person) by nailing to a cross.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To punish or otherwise express extreme anger at, especially as a scapegoat or target of outrage.

   **Usage:** excessive; transitive

   **Example:** After his public gaffe, he was crucified in the media.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To thoroughly beat at a sport or game.

   **Usage:** excessive; informal; transitive

   **Example:** West Ham beat Manchester City five nil–they crucified them!
