### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Something which is done or is to be done; business of any kind, commercial, professional, or public.

   **Usage:** in-plural; often

   **Example:** a difficult affair to manage

   **Source synonyms:** matter; concern

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any proceeding or action which it is wished to refer to or characterize vaguely.

   **Example:** The house was a ramshackle affair.

   **Source synonyms:** goings-on; happening; affair; befalling; episode; instance; event; goings-on (plural; plural-only); happening; hap (archaic; slang); incident; occasion (obsolete); occurrence; proceeding

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An action or engagement not of sufficient magnitude to be called a battle.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A material object (vaguely designated).

   **Example:** He used a hook-shaped affair with a long handle to unlock the car.

   **Example:** This garden is a ramshackle affair.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An adulterous relationship, chiefly of a married person. (from affaire de cœur, affair of the heart).

   **Example:** When Martin's wife found out about his affair with her best friend, she asked for a divorce.

   **Example:** Mary had an affair with a woman from the gym.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. An otherwise illicit romantic relationship, such as with someone who is not one's regular partner (boyfriend, girlfriend).

   **Example:** Jerry's girlfriend said she wanted to go steady, but she was in an affair with one of his team-mates.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A person with whom someone has an adulterous relationship.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A party or social gathering, especially of a formal nature.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. The (male or female) genitals.

   **Usage:** archaic; slang
