### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person or thing that is helpless, inefficient, or disabled.

   **Usage:** colloquial

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An elected official who has lost the recent election or is not eligible for reelection and is marking time until leaving office.

   **Usage:** US

   **Example:** Congressman Jones was a lame duck and did not vote on many issues that were important to his constituents.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; politics

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A person who cannot fulfill their contracts.

   **Usage:** dated; slang

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance

   **Source synonyms:** duck

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A server that continues to serve ongoing requests while refusing new ones, allowing for graceful shutdown.
