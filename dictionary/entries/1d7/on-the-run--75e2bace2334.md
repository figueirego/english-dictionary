## Prep\_phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Fleeing.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** The suspect in the robbery is still on the run.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. At a disadvantage; forced to abandon a position.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** The corruption charges against her aides have her on the run.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Constantly traveling or moving from place to place.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** She's a busy executive and always on the run.

   **Example:** He had to eat on the run.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see on, run.

   **Example:** The skier's wife had the baby while he was still on the run.
