## Noun · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈɹaʊndəˌbaʊt/ — variety unspecified in the source labels
- IPA: /ˈɹuːndəˌbuːt/ — source labels: Northumbria

### Forms

- roundabouts — plural

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A road junction at which traffic streams circularly around a central island.

   **Usage:** Australia; Canada; Ireland; New-England; New-Zealand; UK; US; especially; sometimes

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. A horizontal wheel which rotates around a central axis when pushed and on which children ride, often found in parks as a children's play apparatus.

   **Usage:** UK

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. A fairground carousel.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. A detour.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. A short, close-fitting coat or jacket worn by men or boys, especially in the 19th century.

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. A round dance.

   **Usage:** archaic
