### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Leptoptilos crumeniferus, a large wading bird native to Africa, with a naked head and neck adapted for scavenging.

   **Source synonyms:** adjutant stork; marabou stork

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person having five-eighths black ancestry; the offspring of a mulatto and a griffe.

   **Usage:** Louisiana; US; dated

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A kind of thrown raw silk, naturally nearly white, but capable of being dyed without scouring.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; manufacturing; textiles

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A thin fabric made from this silk, as for scarves.
