## Adjective · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Taking place when one is an employee.

   **Usage:** UK; not-comparable

   **Source context (verbatim):** of training etc.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Relating to or being a full-time employee.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** We offer both in-service and full-time employee training.
