### Senses

<a id="r3-s1"></a>

**Source sense R3.S1**

1. Used to form possessional adjectives from nouns, in the sense of having the object represented by the noun.

   **Usage:** morpheme

   **Example:** point + -ed → pointed

   **Example:** horn + -ed → horned

   **Example:** hoof + -ed → hooved

   **Example:** chagrin + -ed → chagrined

   **Source antonyms:** -less

<a id="r3-s2"></a>

**Source sense R3.S2**

2. As an extension of the above, used to form possessional adjectives from adjective-noun pairs.

   **Usage:** morpheme

   **Example:** red + hair + -ed → red-haired

   **Example:** left + hand + -ed → left-handed

   **Example:** two + prong(s) + -ed → two-pronged
