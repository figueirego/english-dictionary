## Phrase · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Do not discharge a weapon. Used originally for weapons needing a spark or lighting of a fuse to ignite gunpowder; now sometimes used to mean any weapon launching a projectile.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Wait, don't retaliate, calm down, be quiet.

   **Usage:** colloquial; idiomatic

   **Example:** Hold your fire and let me explain.
