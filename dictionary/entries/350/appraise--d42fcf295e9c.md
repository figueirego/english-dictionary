### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To determine the value or worth of (something), particularly as a person appointed for this purpose.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to appraise goods and chattels

   **Source synonyms:** apprise (archaic); apprize (archaic); evaluate

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To consider comprehensively.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** interpret; look upon; think of; account (formal); appraise; count; esteem; see; censure (obsolete); consider; deem; hold; dub; find; interpret; judge; look upon; regard as; respect; think of; view as

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To judge the performance of someone, especially a worker.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** At the end of the contract, you will be appraised by your line manager.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To estimate; to conjecture.

   **Usage:** transitive
