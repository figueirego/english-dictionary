### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To be physically capable of going into a space.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Source synonyms:** fit into; squeeze in; squeeze into

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To find or make a place for&#58;
   1. To designate a job, role, or task for.

      **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

      **Example:** Just show up ready for work, and they'll fit you in no matter what your skills are.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To find or make a place for&#58;
   1. To incorporate into a schedule; to create an appointment, slot, or time for.

      **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

      **Example:** The dentist said he can't fit me in until Thursday.

      **Example:** I don't have time to work on your house today, but I'll fit it in tomorrow.

      **Source synonyms:** squeeze in; work in

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To be or become of a like type with others; (especially) to be or become socially accepted.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

   **Example:** The recluse did not fit in at the party.

   **Source synonyms:** belong

   **Source antonyms:** stick out

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. (followed by with) To conform, to be suitable.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

   **Example:** I'm afraid that does not fit in with our plans.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. (used alone or followed by with) To be consistent or reconcilable (with other facts or ideas).

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

   **Example:** We felt confident about our previous theory, but the results of the latest experiments don't fit in.

   **Example:** He was the top suspect in the downtown crime spree, and these robberies fit in with the pattern.

   **Source synonyms:** accord; agree; cohere; comport; conform; correspond; fit; harmonize; jibe (informal); match; tally
