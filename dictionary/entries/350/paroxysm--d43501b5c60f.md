### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A period (especially one of several recurring periods) during the course of an illness when symptoms worsen; a sudden attack of a disease symptom, such as a bout of coughing or a seizure.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** exacervation; fit

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Chiefly followed by of&#58; a sudden outburst of violent activity or feeling; also, the most severe part of an activity or incident; the climax.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable

   **Source synonyms:** high point; acme; apex; zenith; apogee; apotheosis; ascendance; capstone (figuratively); climax; coronation (figuratively); crown (figuratively); culmination; extreme; fastigium; high note (figuratively); high point; high tide (figuratively); high-water mark (figuratively); midday (figuratively); Mount Everest (figuratively); outblossom; paroxysm; perihelion (figuratively); pinnacle; summa (figuratively); summit (figuratively)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An outburst of a violent argument or disagreement.

   **Usage:** archaic; broadly; countable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A violent occurrence of a natural phenomenon, such as an earthquake, thunderstorm, or volcanic eruption; specifically (volcanology), the most explosive event during a series of volcanic eruptions.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; geology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Disastrous or sudden change.

   **Usage:** broadly; rare; uncountable
