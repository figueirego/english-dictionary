### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A state of nervousness about performing some action in front of a group of people, on or off of a stage; nerves; a lack of self-assurance before an audience.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** I did some guest-speaking at the middle school last year, and I experienced some stage fright before and during my first presentation.

   **Example:** I got stage fright while doing a dance show for my school last year.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Synonym of paruresis.

   **Usage:** euphemistic; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** paruresis (synonym; synonym-of)
