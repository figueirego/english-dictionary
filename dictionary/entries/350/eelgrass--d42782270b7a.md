### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of several species of aquatic plant, with very long and narrow leaves
   1. Zostera spp. in marine environments

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of several species of aquatic plant, with very long and narrow leaves
   1. Zostera spp. in marine environments
      1. especially Zostera marina, growing abundantly in shallow bays along the coasts of the Northern Hemisphere.

         **Usage:** countable; uncountable
