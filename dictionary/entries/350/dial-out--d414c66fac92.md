### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make a call using an outbound line.

   **Usage:** literally

   **Example:** To dial out, press 9 and then the phone number.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To start an online meeting or conference call by calling or otherwise alerting the participants.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

   **Source antonyms:** dial in

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To ignore (someone).

   **Usage:** figuratively; slang

   **Example:** You have to dial him out to get any work done.
