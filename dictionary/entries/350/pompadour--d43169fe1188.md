### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A women's hairstyle in which the hair is swept upwards from the face and worn high over the forehead.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A men's hairstyle of the 1950s.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A crimson or pink colour.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A corsage with low square neck.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A pattern for silk, with leaves and flowers in pink, blue, and gold.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A member of the 56th (West Essex) Regiment of Foot, an infantry regiment in the British Army, active from 1755 to 1881.
