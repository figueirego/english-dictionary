### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The month of Adar when it occurs in a leap year, preceded by a 30-day intercalary month (which is then itself called Adar, or Adar Rishon).

   **Usage:** Judaism

   **Source synonyms:** Adar Bet; Adar Sheni; Second Adar
