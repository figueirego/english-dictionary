### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of, containing, or being a notion; mental or imaginary.

   **Example:** The idea that a rooster says cock-a-doodle-doo rather than ooh ooh-ooh ooh-ooh is socially conventional even though its sound correspondence is at heart notional.

   **Source synonyms:** conceptual; fancied; fanciful; ideal

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** Maine
