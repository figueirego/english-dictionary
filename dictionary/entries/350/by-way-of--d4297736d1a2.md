### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. By the route of.

   **Example:** From Shakespeare to Milton by way of the English Bible, our language has been shaped by humanist ideas.

   **Source synonyms:** through; via

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. For the purpose of; as a means of; as an instance of; as a type or form of.

   **Example:** By way of apology, he tried to be gallant.

   **Example:** By way of qualification, I should point out that…
