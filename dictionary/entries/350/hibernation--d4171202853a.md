<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A state of minimum power consumption, inactivity and metabolic depression in some animals during winter.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; sleep

   **Example:** The bears are in hibernation.

   **Example:** The bears come out of hibernation in March.
