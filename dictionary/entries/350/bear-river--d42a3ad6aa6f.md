## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A number of rivers in Canada, including British Columbia and Nova Scotia.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A number of rivers in the United States, including California, Colorado, Georgia, Maine, Michigan, Minnesota, Utah, and Washington.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A village in Annapolis County and Digby County, Nova Scotia, Canada, on the river of the same name.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An unincorporated community in Bearville Township, Itasca County and Morcom Township, Saint Louis County, Minnesota, United States, and on a river of the same name.
