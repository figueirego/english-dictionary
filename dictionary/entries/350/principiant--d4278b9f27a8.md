### Senses

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. (invariant theory, historical) A reciprocant invariant under homographic substitutions.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

   **Extractor diagnostics (not usage labels):** empty-gloss; no-gloss

**Supplemental definition (evidence reviewed; source gap preserved):** The source gap is a mathematical noun, distinct from the historical noun meaning a beginner. MacMahon explicitly gives this mathematical name and definition in his 1898 Royal Society obituary of Sylvester.
