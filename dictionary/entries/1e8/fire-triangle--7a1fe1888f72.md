### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The three elements necessary and sufficient to sustain a fire&#58; fuel (e.g. wood or petrol/gasoline), oxidizer (e.g. oxygen), and sufficient heat (e.g. reaction temperature or activation energy).
