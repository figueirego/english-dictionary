### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To spread (something) over or through as in air, water, or other matter, especially by fluid motion or passive means.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The small window diffuses light throughout the room.

   **Example:** Ideas are easily diffused with the help of technology.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To be spread over or through as in air, water, or other matter, especially by fluid motion or passive means.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Food coloring diffuses in water.

   **Example:** The riot diffused quite suddenly.
