### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To cover or enclose (something) by folding and securing a covering entirely around it.

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** He wrapped up the parcel with brown paper.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To conclude or finish completely.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** Let me wrap up this project before I begin a new one.

   **Source synonyms:** finish off; finish up

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To summarize or recapitulate.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** The newscaster wrapped up the day's events.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To put on abundant clothing as protection from the cold; to bundle up.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** It's a cold, snowy day and I'm going to wrap up thoroughly before I go sledding.

   **Source synonyms:** muffle up

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To cocoon; to surround protectively.

   **Usage:** figuratively; idiomatic; intransitive; transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To tie up; to make too busy to respond.

   **Usage:** figuratively; idiomatic; transitive

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To combine, incorporate or encapsulate into one thing.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To hide or cover up.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To curl into a more compact form.

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive
