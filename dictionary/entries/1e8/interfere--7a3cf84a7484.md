### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To get involved or involve oneself, causing disturbance.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** I always try not to interfere with other people’s personal affairs.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of waves, to be correlated with each other when overlapped or superposed.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Correlated waves interfere to produce interesting patterns, while uncorrelated waves overlap without interfering.

   **Example:** Where the radio-wave signals of the two radio stations interfere the listener hears nothing but noise.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of a horse, to strike one foot against the opposite foot or ankle by using the legs.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To sexually molest, especially of a child.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The investigation found the girls had been interfered with.

   **Source context (verbatim):** with with
