## Verb

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A response to can't wait that negates the phrase.

   **Usage:** informal; no-past-participle; no-present-participle; sarcastic

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used to dismiss a concern considered urgent by another party.

   **Usage:** no-past-participle; no-present-participle

   **Example:** Could you move, please? I need to get by! I'm not done yet. You can wait.
