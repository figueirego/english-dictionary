### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To skim the surface of a body of water while moving at high speed.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. For a car or similar vehicle to slide along the road on a thin film of water between the road and the tyres. This occurs when a car has some speed and comes to somewhere with more water on the road than the weight of the car and the grooves in the tyre tread pattern (if any) can push away. The result is almost no traction at all for steering or braking.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Don't drive too fast on wet roads or the car may hydroplane and cause you to lose control of the vehicle.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** automotive; transport; vehicles

   **Source synonyms:** aquaplane (UK)
