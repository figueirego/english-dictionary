## Noun

### Forms

- deadbeat dads — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A man, especially one who is divorced or estranged from his partner, who fails to provide monetary child support when he is legally required to do so.

   **Usage:** Canada; US; derogatory; idiomatic

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A man who is voluntarily absent and uncaring towards his children, especially one who leaves them entirely in their mother's care.

   **Usage:** broadly; derogatory; idiomatic; informal
