### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. to tap one's knuckles on an object such as a desk or wall to attract attention or as a signal of commmunication.

   **Usage:** literally

   **Example:** Just rap your knuckles on the wall when you're ready to go.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. to give someone a punishment.

   **Usage:** dated; idiomatic

   **Example:** If the sales department doesn't get organized soon, the CEO is going to rap their knuckles.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. to strike someone on the back of the hand usually as a method of punishment.

   **Usage:** archaic; literally

   **Example:** In the old days the teacher would rap someone's knuckles with a ruler if they misbehaved in class.
