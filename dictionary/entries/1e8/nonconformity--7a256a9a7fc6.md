### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A refusal to conform to the doctrine, discipline, or practice of a state religion, especially refusal by other Protestants to conform to the doctrine or discipline of the Church of England; the principles or practice of nonconformists; nonconformists as a class.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** dissent; dissentism (rare); inconformity; nonconformism; nonconformitancy (obsolete; rare)

   **Source antonyms:** conformity

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A rejection of, or failure to conform to, a practice, standard, rule, or law.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** disconformity; inconformity; nonconformism

   **Source antonyms:** conformity

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A type of unconformity in which a nonsedimentary rock intrudes in sedimentary layers.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; geology; natural-sciences
