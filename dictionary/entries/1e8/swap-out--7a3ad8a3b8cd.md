### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To transfer (memory contents) into a swap file.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** outswap

   **Source antonyms:** swap in

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To exchange (something or someone) for an unused (or less-used) equivalent.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** Those men are tired; let's swap that unit out.

   **Example:** I can't keep going; is it okay if I swap out?

   **Source synonyms:** switch; switch out

   **Source antonyms:** swap in

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To exchange (something) for (something else). (usually followed by with or for)

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I'll swap out the dead battery for a new one.

   **Source synonyms:** switch; switch out; replace

   **Source antonyms:** swap in
