### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who survives, especially one who survives a traumatic experience.

   **Example:** From this moment on, I choose to live not as a victim, but as a survivor.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person who is able to endure hardship.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Source synonyms:** trooper; tough cookie

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. One who knew a specific decedent.

   **Example:** She was from a large family and had many friends, so the funeral was crowded with mourning survivors.
