### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To end work; to officially record a time when one terminates a period of work.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** If we clock out now we can be at the bar by happy hour.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To officially record a work-termination time for.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I've got to beat the rush. Do me a favor and clock me out.

   **Example:** I clocked myself out as soon as I could.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To transmit individual bits of data under the control of a clock.

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; electrical-engineering; electricity; electromagnetism; electronics; energy; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To die.

   **Usage:** intransitive; slang
