### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The process of constructing.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Construction is underway on the new bridge.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Anything that has been constructed.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The engineer marvelled at his construction.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The trade of building structures.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** He had worked in construction all his life.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A building, model or some other structure.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The office was a construction of steel and glass.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A (usually non-representational) structure, such as a collage etc.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** "Construction in string and clockwork" took first prize.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** art; arts

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The manner in which something is built.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** A thing of simple construction.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A group of words arranged to form a meaningful phrase.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** grammar; human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. The act or result of construing the meaning of something.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** American conservatives tend to favor strict construction of the Constitution.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. The meaning or interpretation of a text, action etc.; the way something is viewed by an observer or onlooker.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. A geometric figure of arcs and line segments that is drawable with a straightedge and compass.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences
