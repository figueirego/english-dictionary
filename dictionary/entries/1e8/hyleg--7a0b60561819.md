**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In Hellenistic astrology, the planet with the greatest essential dignity in five important natal chart positions&#58; the degree of the Sun; the degree of the Moon; the Ascendant; the Lot of Fortune; and the prenatal syzygy (that is, New Moon or Full Moon, whichever most closely preceded the birth).
