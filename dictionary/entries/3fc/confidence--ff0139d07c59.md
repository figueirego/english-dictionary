### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Self-assurance.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** He entered the ring with confidence.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A feeling of certainty; firm trust or belief; faith.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** She had confidence it would soon end.

   **Example:** I reposed a great deal of confidence in his judgment.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Information held in secret; a piece of information shared but to thence be kept in secret.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Boldness; presumption.

   **Usage:** countable; dated; uncountable
