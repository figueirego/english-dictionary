<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A building material created by mixing cement, water, and aggregate such as gravel and sand.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Various types of concrete have been used in the construction of this highway.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. A building material created by mixing cement, water, and aggregate such as gravel and sand.
   1. Such a material whose cement is Portland cement or a similar limestone derivative.

      **Usage:** countable; especially; uncountable

      **Example:** The sidewalk was made of concrete that had been poured in large slabs.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. A term designating both a quality and the subject in which it exists; a concrete term.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; logic; mathematics; philosophy; sciences

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

   **Usage:** US; countable; uncountable

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. An extract of herbal materials that has a semi-solid consistency, especially when such materials are partly aromatic.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; perfumery

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. Sugar boiled down from cane juice to a solid mass.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; possibly; uncountable

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

**Source sense R2.S7**

7. Any solid mass formed by the coalescence of separate particles; a compound substance, a concretion.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable
