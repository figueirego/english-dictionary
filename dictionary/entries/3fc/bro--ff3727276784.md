### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Brother (a male sibling).

   **Usage:** slang

   **Example:** My mom took my lil' bro to soccer practice now and she wanted me to pick him up.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Brother (a comrade or friend; one who shares one’s ideals).

   **Usage:** slang

   **Example:** Bro, you good? You've been lookin' kinda out of it lately.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Brother, my man, good sir; a friendly term of address for typically men.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Example:** don't tase me, bro

   **Example:** Hey bro, sorry to bother you but I think I accidentally backed up into your car — can I make it up to you?

   **Example:** Bro said he finna go off today!

   **Source synonyms:** man

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A frat boy or someone who espouses the fraternity bro culture.

   **Usage:** slang

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Someone, usually male, who aggressively evangelizes a person, concept or technology.

   **Usage:** derogatory; in-compounds; slang; usually

   **Example:** AI bro
