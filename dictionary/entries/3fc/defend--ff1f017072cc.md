### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To ward off attacks against; to fight to protect; to guard.
   1. To ward off attacks against (something or someone).

      **Usage:** ditransitive; transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To ward off attacks against; to fight to protect; to guard.
   1. To ward off attacks from (something or someone).

      **Usage:** ditransitive; intransitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To support by words or writing; to vindicate, talk in favour of.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To make legal defence of; to represent (the accused).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To focus one's energies and talents on preventing opponents from scoring, as opposed to focusing on scoring.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Mostly in tests. The action of not putting force into hitting a shot, but to conserve energy and wear down bowlers so they can attack later.

   **Example:** The teams strategy was to defend but score runs as well for the first 20 overs.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; cricket; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To attempt to retain a title, or attempt to reach the same stage in a competition as one did in the previous instance of that competition.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To call a raise from the big blind.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** card-games; poker

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To ward off, repel (an attack or attacker).

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. To prevent, to keep (from doing something).

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. To prohibit, forbid.

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete; transitive
