## Determiner · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Such an amount of.

   **Example:** I didn't realise he'd lost as much money.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In comparisons, often in the pattern 'as much ~ as ~'.
   1. The same amount of; no less.

      **Example:** I have as much right to park here as you do.

      **Example:** I'm earning more money now, but I don't have as much free time.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. In comparisons, often in the pattern 'as much ~ as ~'.
   1. Used with a modifier to indicate a relative amount.

      **Example:** I have twice as much money as my sister, but only half as much as my brother.

## Pronoun · Record 2

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. As determiner, above, but with the complement omitted and stated elsewhere or understood from context.

   **Example:** There used to be a lot of sand on this beach, but now there isn't as much.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. With the complement indicated with 'of'.

   **Example:** I devote as much of my life as I can to my family.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. That same thing (or something amounting to it), referring to something previously mentioned.

   **Example:** I think she's overweight, but when I said as much to her, she got very upset.

   **Example:** I doubt you have any claim against the bank; it says as much in the terms and conditions.

## Adverb · Record 3

### Senses

<a id="r3-s1"></a>

**Source sense R3.S1**

1. In comparisons, often in the pattern 'as much ~ as ~'.
   1. To the same extent.

      **Usage:** not-comparable

      **Example:** I like Katie a lot, but I don't like Hannah as much.

      **Example:** It is as much your fault as mine.

<a id="r3-s2"></a>

**Source sense R3.S2**

2. In comparisons, often in the pattern 'as much ~ as ~'.
   1. Used with a modifier to indicate a relative extent.

      **Usage:** not-comparable

      **Example:** I like you twice as much as I like Lucy.

<a id="r3-s3"></a>

**Source sense R3.S3**

3. Precisely that.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** I think she's overweight, but when I said as much to her, she got very upset.

   **Example:** Spending on health is half again as much as last year.

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** so much — emphasizing smallness
