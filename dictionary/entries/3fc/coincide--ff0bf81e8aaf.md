### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To occupy exactly the same space.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The two squares coincide nicely.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To occur at the same time.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The conference will coincide with his vacation.

   **Source synonyms:** concur

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To correspond, concur, or agree.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Our ideas coincide, except in certain areas.
