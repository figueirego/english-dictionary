### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A paved surface; a hard covering on the ground.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; computing; engineering; in-technical-contexts; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A paved path, for the use of pedestrians, located at the side of a road.

   **Usage:** Canada; Ireland; Mid-Atlantic; South-Africa; UK; US; countable; usually

   **Source synonyms:** sidewalk; footpath (Australia; India; New-Zealand); trottoir

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A paving (paved part) of a road or other thoroughfare; the roadway or road surface.

   **Usage:** Canada; US; uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The paved part of an area other than a road or sidewalk, such as a cobblestone plaza, asphalt schoolyard or playground, or parking lot.

   **Usage:** Canada; US; uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually
