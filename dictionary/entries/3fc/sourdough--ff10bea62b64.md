### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A type of bread dough leavened with yeast and lactobacilli that produce acids giving a sour taste.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** sourdough bread

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An old-timer, especially in Alaska.

   **Usage:** countable; slang

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A permanent resident of the territory. Someone who has lived in the Yukon during all four seasons.

   **Usage:** Yukon; countable

   **Source antonyms:** cheechako

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A 49er, a California Gold Rush miner.

   **Usage:** countable; historical

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Leaven; an agent that makes dough rise.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable
