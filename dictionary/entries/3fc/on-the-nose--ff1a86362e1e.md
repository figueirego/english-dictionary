## Prep\_phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Exact; precise; appropriate.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** His estimate that they would consume 23 boxes was on the nose.

   **Example:** His choice of decor was on the nose, considering the theme of the party.

   **Source synonyms:** on the button; on the dot

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Unimaginative; overliteral; lacking nuance; unsophisticated.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** Wearing that floral dress to a garden party was a little on the nose, wouldn't you say?

   **Source synonyms:** heavy-handed

   **Source antonyms:** nuanced; subtle

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Smelly; malodorous.

   **Usage:** Australia; figuratively; often; slang

   **Example:** That bucket of raw prawns you left in the sun is a bit on the nose.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Being a bet on a horse to finish first.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** gambling; games

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. On the lookout.

   **Usage:** obsolete; slang

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see on, nose.
