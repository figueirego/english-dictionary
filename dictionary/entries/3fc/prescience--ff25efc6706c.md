### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Knowledge of events before they take place.
   1. Such knowledge that is supernatural or paranormal in nature, including the prediction of things that nobody could have known by the ordinary senses.

      **Usage:** especially; uncountable; usually

      **Source synonyms:** precognition; foresight; foreknowledge; clairvoyance; premonition; divination; prophecy; psychicness

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Knowledge of events before they take place.
   1. Such knowledge that comes from wise and thorough forethought (for example, careful planning).

      **Usage:** sometimes; uncountable; usually

      **Source synonyms:** foresight; forethought
