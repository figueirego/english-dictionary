## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈɹeɪˌdɑɹ/ — source labels: General-American

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In full primary radar&#58; a method of detecting a distant object and determining its position, velocity, or other characteristics by analysing radio waves (usually microwaves) which are sent towards the object and which reflect off its surfaces; also, the field of study of this method.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** They were able to find the enemy fleet using radar.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In full primary radar&#58; a method of detecting a distant object and determining its position, velocity, or other characteristics by analysing radio waves (usually microwaves) which are sent towards the object and which reflect off its surfaces; also, the field of study of this method.
   1. In full secondary radar&#58; a method of detecting a distant object and determining its position, velocity, or other characteristics by analysing signals transmitted by the object in response to radio waves sent towards the object.

      **Usage:** broadly; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. In full primary radar&#58; a method of detecting a distant object and determining its position, velocity, or other characteristics by analysing radio waves (usually microwaves) which are sent towards the object and which reflect off its surfaces; also, the field of study of this method.
   1. Often preceded by a descriptive word&#58; a system using one of the above detection methods, differentiated by configuration or platform, frequency, power, and other technical attributes.

      **Usage:** countable

      **Example:** The Ministry of Defence funded the development of an entirely new radar and improved avionics.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. In full primary radar&#58; a method of detecting a distant object and determining its position, velocity, or other characteristics by analysing radio waves (usually microwaves) which are sent towards the object and which reflect off its surfaces; also, the field of study of this method.
   1. An installation of the apparatus for operating one of the above detection systems.

      **Usage:** countable

      **Example:** Each of these aircraft has been upgraded with a new radar and a more powerful engine.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Often preceded by a descriptive word&#58; a natural (for example, in an animal such as a bat) or human-made detection method based on the analysis of reflected signals other than radio waves, as light waves or sound waves; (countable) an instance of this.

   **Usage:** broadly; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A superior ability to detect something; an awareness, an intuition.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively

   **Example:** His sensitive radar for hidden alliances keeps him out of trouble.
