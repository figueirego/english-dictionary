### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Held with two hands as in "port arms", but carried well above the head. The high port is often the position taught for running (at the double) or charging.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a weapon

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

   **Usage:** broadly; idiomatic

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Held in front of the body, especially in an authoritative or aggressive way.

   **Usage:** broadly; idiomatic

   **Source context (verbatim):** not just a weapon; of anything
