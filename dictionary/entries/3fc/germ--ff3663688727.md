### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The small mass of cells from which a new organism develops; a seed, bud, spore, or zygote.
   1. The embryo of a seed, especially of a seed used as a cereal or grain. See Wikipedia article on cereal germ.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The small mass of cells from which a new organism develops; a seed, bud, spore, or zygote.
   1. The small mass of cells from which a part of an organism develops, or a macroscopic but immature form of that part; a bud.

      **Example:** surgical removal of germs of wisdom teeth

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A pathogen&#58; a pathogenic microorganism, such as a bacterium or virus.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The origin or earliest version of an idea or project.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** the germ of civil liberty

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An equivalence class that includes a specified function defined in an open neighborhood.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences
