### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To repeat (an activity) as a way of improving one's skill in that activity.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** You should practise playing piano every day.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To repeat an activity in this way.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** If you want to speak French well, you need to practise.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To perform or observe in an habitual fashion.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** They gather to practise religion every Saturday.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To pursue (a career, especially law, fine art or medicine).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** She practised law for forty years before retiring.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To conspire.

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To put into practice; to carry out; to act upon; to commit; to execute; to do.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To make use of; to employ.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To teach or accustom by practice; to train.
