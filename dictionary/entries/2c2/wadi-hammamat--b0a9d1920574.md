## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The modern name for Ro-Henu, currently a dry river bed between the Red Sea and the Nile; in ancient times the first leg of Ancient Egypt's Mu-qed route (a pilgrimage and maritime trade route) to ancient Punt (Horn of Africa's northern coast).
