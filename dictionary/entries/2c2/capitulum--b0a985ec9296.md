### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A densely clustered inflorescence composed of a large number of individual florets arising from a platform-like base.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences

   **Source synonyms:** capitellum; sphaeridium

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The head-like mouthpart apparatus of a tick, including the palpi, mandibles, and hypostome.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** arachnology; biology; natural-sciences; zoology

   **Source synonyms:** capitellum

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; medicine; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** capitellum
