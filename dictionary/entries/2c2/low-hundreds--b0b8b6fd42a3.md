### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An unspecified number between 100 and 200 that is rather closer to 100 than 200, as opposed to mid hundreds and high hundreds.

   **Usage:** plural; plural-only

   **Example:** A normal blood sugar is in the low to mid hundreds.
