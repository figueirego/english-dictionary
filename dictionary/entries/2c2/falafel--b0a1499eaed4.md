### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A Middle Eastern food in the form of balls made from chickpeas or broad beans and other ingredients, often served in a pita.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** There's a stall at the market that sells fantastic falafel.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A pita with falafel balls as a filling.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A single falafel ball.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** The stallholder puts salad into an open pita bread, followed by the four falafels, and then liberally covers them with hummus.
