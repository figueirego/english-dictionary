### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having the necessary powers or the needed resources to accomplish a task.

   **Example:** She is able to lift the box without assistance.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Free from constraints preventing completion of task; permitted to; not prevented from.

   **Example:** In a democratic world you'd be able to say what you thought wherever you were.

   **Example:** I'll see you as soon as I'm able.

   **Example:** With that obstacle removed, I am now able to proceed with my plan.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Gifted with skill, intelligence, knowledge, or competence.

   **Example:** The chairman was also an able sailor.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Legally qualified or competent.

   **Example:** He is able to practice law in six states.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Capable of performing all the requisite duties; as an able seaman.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Having the physical strength; robust; healthy.

   **Usage:** dialectal; obsolete

   **Example:** After the past week of forced marches, only half the men are fully able.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Easy to use.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. Liable to.

   **Usage:** dialectal; obsolete

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

   **Usage:** obsolete

   **Example:** He was born to an able family.
