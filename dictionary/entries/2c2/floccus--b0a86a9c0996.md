### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A cloud species which consists of rounded tufts of cloud, often formed by dissipation from larger cloud species. Associated with cirrus, cirrocumulus, altocumulus, and stratocumulus genera.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** climatology; meteorology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A flock or tuft of wool or wool-like hairs

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
