### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To take (a child, heir, friend, citizen, etc.) by choice into a relationship.
   1. To take voluntarily (a child of other parents) to be in the place of, or as, one's own child.

      **Usage:** transitive

      **Example:** A friend of mine recently adopted a Chinese baby girl found on the streets of Beijing.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To take (a child, heir, friend, citizen, etc.) by choice into a relationship.
   1. To obtain (a pet) from a shelter or the wild.

      **Usage:** transitive

      **Example:** We're going to adopt a Dalmatian.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To take (a child, heir, friend, citizen, etc.) by choice into a relationship.
   1. To contribute towards the upkeep of (a child or animal), in exchange for occasional stories, pictures, etc.

      **Usage:** transitive

      **Example:** We adopted an elephant at the local zoo.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To take (a child, heir, friend, citizen, etc.) by choice into a relationship.
   1. To take by choice into the scope of one's responsibility.

      **Usage:** transitive

      **Example:** This supermarket chain adopts several families every Yuletide, providing them with money and groceries for the holidays.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To take or receive as one's own what is not so naturally.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He adopted a new look in order to fit in with his new workmates.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To select and take or approve.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to adopt the view or policy of another

   **Example:** These are resolutions that were adopted.

   **Source synonyms:** espouse; embrace

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To beat an opponent ten times in a row.

   **Usage:** slang; transitive

   **Example:** The match was not even close; the IM made amateurish blunders and ended up getting adopted.
