### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A large extent or tract of land; for example a region, country or district.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One of three of Canada's federal entities, located in the country's Arctic, with fewer powers than a province and created by an act of Parliament rather than by the Constitution&#58; Yukon, Northwest Territories, and Nunavut.

   **Usage:** Canada; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. One of three of Australia's federal entities, located in the country's north and southeast, with fewer powers than a state and created by an act of Parliament rather than by the Constitution&#58; Northern Territory, Australian Capital Territory and Jervis Bay Territory.

   **Usage:** Australia; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A geographic area under control of a single governing entity such as state or municipality; an area whose borders are determined by the scope of political power rather than solely by natural features such as rivers and ridges.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An area that an animal of a particular species consistently defends against its conspecifics.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; ecology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The part of the playing field or board over which a player or team has control.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A geographic area that a person or organization is responsible for in the course of work.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A location or logical space which someone owns or controls.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A market segment or scope of professional practice over which an organization or type of practitioner has exclusive rights.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
