### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Attached as an appendage.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Annexed; concomitant.

   **Example:** a seal appendant to a paper

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Appended by prescription, that is, a personal usage for a considerable time; said of a thing of inheritance belonging to another inheritance which is superior or more worthy; as, an advowson, common, etc., which may be appendant to a manor, common of fishing to a freehold, a seat in church to a house.
