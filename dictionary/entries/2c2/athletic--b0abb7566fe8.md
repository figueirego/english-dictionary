### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having to do with athletes.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Are you a member of the American Athletic Association?

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Physically active.

   **Example:** Since you're such an athletic person, you may wish to consider joining.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Having a muscular, well developed body, being in shape.

   **Example:** You have such an athletic build—you must work out regularly.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An attribute of a motion or play which requires fine physical ability.

   **Example:** The center fielder made an athletic play to snatch the ball from over the fence.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Of a level in a Super Mario game&#58; with an emphasis on platforming challenge, often involving precise jumps between floating platforms above a bottomless pit, and having upbeat background music.
