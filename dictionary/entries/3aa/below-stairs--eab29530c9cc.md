### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. On a floor lower than the one a speaker currently occupies; below the main floor of a multi-floor building.

   **Usage:** dated

   **Source synonyms:** downstairs

   **Source antonyms:** above stairs; upstairs

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In or pertaining to the lowest levels of a large house where the house staff work and are accommodated, contrasted with above stairs where the owning family reside.

   **Usage:** UK; historical

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Common, vulgar.

   **Usage:** broadly

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The areas of a large house in which house staff work, or the staff that work there.

   **Usage:** UK; historical

   **Source context (verbatim):** as noun
