**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To extend the utmost hospitality; to treat someone as an honored guest; to welcome or host, especially in a showy or extravagant manner.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; informal

   **Example:** The mayor of the little town rolled out the red carpet for new businesses by calling on them personally.
