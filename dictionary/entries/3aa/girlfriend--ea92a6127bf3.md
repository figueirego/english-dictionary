### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A female partner in an unmarried romantic relationship.

   **Example:** Marc went to the park with his girlfriend and watched the sunset with her.

   **Example:** I was playing the acoustic guitar and my girlfriend was playing the drums.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A female friend of a woman or girl.

   **Usage:** dated

   **Example:** Mary always enjoyed hanging out with her girlfriend Jessica.

   **Source synonyms:** chickfriend (slang)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A fellow gay man, especially a friend as opposed to a sexual partner.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A term of address for a female friend or among gay men.
