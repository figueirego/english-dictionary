### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To wait a moment.

   **Usage:** figuratively; idiomatic; imperative

   **Example:** Hang on. Let me check.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To hold, grasp, or grip.

   **Usage:** figuratively; idiomatic

   **Example:** Hang on to the handle so you don't drop it.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To keep; to store something for someone.

   **Usage:** figuratively; idiomatic

   **Example:** Hang on to my jacket until I get back.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To pay close attention to, or regard with (possibly obsequious) admiration.

   **Usage:** figuratively; idiomatic

   **Example:** The audience hangs on his every word.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To continually believe in something; to have faith in.

   **Usage:** figuratively; idiomatic

   **Example:** He's got a philosophy he hangs on to.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To persevere.

   **Usage:** figuratively; idiomatic

   **Example:** Just hang on and keep going; this pain won't last forever.

   **Source synonyms:** hang in

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To depend upon.

   **Usage:** figuratively; idiomatic

   **Example:** Everything hangs on whether the boss agrees.

   **Source synonyms:** hang upon

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To weigh down or oppress.

   **Usage:** figuratively; idiomatic

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see hang, on.

   **Usage:** figuratively; idiomatic
