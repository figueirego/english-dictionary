### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To perplex or puzzle.

   **Source synonyms:** puzzle; vex

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To stun or amaze.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To fail to see the difference; to mix up; to confuse right and wrong.

   **Source synonyms:** confuse; mix up; addle; baffle; vex; lose; embroil; stick; stump; baffound; bamboozle; bedevil; befuddle; throw; bemud (figuratively); bemuse; bewilder; bumfuzzle; complicate; confound; confuddle (slang); confuse; confuzzle (childish; slang); dazzle; discombobulate; disconcert; blind; dumbfound; entangle (figuratively); ferhoodle; flabagast; flabbergast; flabbergaster; flurry; fluster; flummox; fuddle; jumble; knot (obsolete); mix up; muddle; muddy (figuratively); ravel; perplex; puzzle

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To make something worse.

   **Usage:** proscribed; sometimes

   **Example:** Don't confound the situation by yelling.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To combine in a confused fashion; to mingle so as to make the parts indistinguishable.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To cause to be ashamed; to abash.

   **Example:** His actions confounded the skeptics.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To defeat, to frustrate, to thwart.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

   **Usage:** dated

   **Example:** Confound you!

   **Example:** Confound the lady!
