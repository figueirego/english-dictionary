### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To interact with others, especially personally, informally and off the clock rather than formally and officially.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** hoping to socialize a bit at the company holiday party

   **Source synonyms:** hang out

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To instruct (somebody), usually subconsciously and mostly by example, in the etiquette of a society.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** depending on how children are socialized regarding racial and ethnic biases

   **Source synonyms:** acculturate

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To take something into collective or governmental ownership.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** fears about potential attempts to socialize medicine

   **Source synonyms:** nationalize
