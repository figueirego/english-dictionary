### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A heavy release of blood within or from the body.

   **Usage:** US; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** We got news that he died of a hemorrhage.

   **Source synonyms:** bleed; bleeding; hemorrhea

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A sudden or significant loss.

   **Usage:** US; countable; figuratively; uncountable

   **Example:** the fiscal hemorrhage that has resulted from financial globalization

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To bleed copiously.

   **Usage:** US; intransitive

   **Example:** The patient is hemorrhaging!

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To lose (something) in copious and detrimental quantities.

   **Usage:** US; figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** The company hemorrhaged money until eventually it went bankrupt.
