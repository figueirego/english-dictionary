### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The state of forgetting completely, of being oblivious, unconscious, unaware, as when sleeping, drunk, or dead.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** He regularly drank himself into oblivion.

   **Example:** Only the oblivion of sleep can heal the greatest traumas.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The state of being completely forgotten, of being reduced to a state of non-existence, extinction, or nothingness, including through war and destruction. (Figuratively) for an area like hell, a wasteland.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** Due to modern technology, many more people and much more information will not slip into oblivion, contrary to what happened throughout history until now.

   **Example:** They tried to bomb them into oblivion.

   **Example:** I will cast them into oblivion!

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A form of purgatory.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Amnesty.

   **Usage:** obsolete; uncountable; usually
