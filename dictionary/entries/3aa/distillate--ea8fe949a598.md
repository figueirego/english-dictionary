### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The liquid that has been condensed from vapour during distillation; normally a purified form or a fraction of an original liquid.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Usage:** Australia; countable; uncountable
