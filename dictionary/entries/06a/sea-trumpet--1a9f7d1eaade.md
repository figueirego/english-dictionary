### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Ecklonia buccinalis, a large blackish seaweed of the Southern Ocean, having a hollow and expanding stem and a pinnate frond.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** trumpetweed

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
