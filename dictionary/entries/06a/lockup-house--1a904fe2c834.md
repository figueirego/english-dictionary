### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A small house where people are kept in temporary custody after arrest, especially where debtors are held before transfer to a debtors' prison.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A lodging house where men are kept and forced to enlist in the army or navy.

   **Usage:** colloquial; historical; obsolete
