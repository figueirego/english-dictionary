### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who, in trying to offer help or advice, says something that aggravates the distress.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** Only a Job's comforter would try to argue that yesterday's stock fall announcement could bring anything good.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A boil.

   **Usage:** obsolete; slang
