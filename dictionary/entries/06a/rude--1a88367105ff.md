1. Lacking in refinement or civility; bad-mannered; discourteous.

   **Example:** This girl was so rude towards the cashier by screaming at him for no apparent reason.

   **Example:** Karen broke up with Fred because he was often rude to her.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>
