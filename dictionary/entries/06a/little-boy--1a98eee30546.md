## Noun

### Forms

- little boys — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A male child, especially one younger than ten years of age.

   **Example:** She has three little boys, so she's very busy these days.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A small saveloy often consumed with tomato sauce at parties.

   **Usage:** Australia; New-Zealand

   **Source synonyms:** cocktail sausage; cheerio

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A male who is weak or subservient.

   **Usage:** derogatory

   **Example:** You're too much of a little boy to be suited for hard work like this.

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** boykin — male child; young boy — male child
