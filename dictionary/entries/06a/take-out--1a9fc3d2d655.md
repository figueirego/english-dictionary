### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To remove.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To escort someone on a date.

   **Example:** Let me take you out for dinner.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To immobilize with force; to subdue; to incapacitate.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To obtain by application by a legal or other official process.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** take out a loan; take out medical insurance; take out a membership; take out a patent

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To kill or destroy.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; slang

   **Example:** The soldiers were instructed to take out the enemy base by any means necessary.

**Source sense R1.S6**

   **Usage:** idiomatic; slang

   **Example:** These photos are taking me out.
