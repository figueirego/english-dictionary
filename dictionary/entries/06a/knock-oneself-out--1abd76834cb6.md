### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To go ahead; to do as one pleases; to enjoy oneself; to endeavor very hard at a task.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; informal

   **Example:** "These cookies are my favorite! Can I have some?" "Sure, knock yourself out." (i.e., have as many as you want)

   **Example:** (sarcastically, indicating lack of interest) "I'm going to the store." / "Knock yourself out."
