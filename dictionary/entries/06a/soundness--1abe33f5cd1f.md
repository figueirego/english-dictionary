## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The state or quality of being sound.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The result or product of being sound.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The property (of an argument) of not only being valid, but also of having true premises.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; logic; mathematics; philosophy; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The property of a logical theory that whenever a wff is a theorem then it must also be valid. Symbolically, letting T represent a theory within logic L, this can be represented as the property that whenever T⊢φ is true, then T⊨φ must also be true, for any wff φ of logic L.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; logic; mathematics; philosophy; sciences
