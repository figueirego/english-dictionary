### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Pertaining to or having the characteristics of prose.

   **Example:** The tenor of Eliot's prosaic work differs greatly from that of his poetry.

   **Source antonyms:** poetic

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Straightforward; matter-of-fact; lacking the feeling or elegance of poetry.

   **Example:** I was simply making the prosaic point that we are running late.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of writing or speaking

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Overly plain, simple or commonplace, to the point of being boring.

   **Usage:** usually; main usage

   **Example:** His account of the incident was so prosaic that I nodded off while reading it.

   **Example:** She lived a prosaic life.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of writing or speaking but also figurative

   **Source synonyms:** humdrum; dull; unimaginative; banal; basic; mindless; beat (slang); blah; commonplace; bland; boring; cold (obsolete); colourless; corny; drab; stuffy; dreich (Midlands; North; Northern-England; Northern-Ireland; Scotland); drowsy; dull; dull as dishwater; flat; heavy going; ho-hum; humdrum; insipid; lackluster; lame; languid; lifeless; longsome (UK; dialectal); monotonous; mundane; no fun at parties; plain; plodding; prosaic; prosy; pedantic; pedestrian; static; straightforward; snoozeworthy; soporific; tedious; uncool; wan; weaksauce (informal); wooden; uninteresting; stodgy; vapid; vanilla

   **Source antonyms:** turgid; bombastic; flowery
