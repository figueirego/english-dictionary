## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A feeling of nervousness or anxiety causing one to reconsider a decision about an upcoming event.

   **Usage:** informal; plural; plural-only; frequently in the phrase 'get cold feet'

   **Example:** The groom got cold feet before his wedding.

   **Example:** The sale was almost agreed, but now the prospective purchasers seem to be suffering from a bad case of cold feet.
