### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used for the digit nine to form systematic element names of elements whose existence has been predicted, and which have not yet been given a trivial name.

   **Usage:** morpheme

   **Example:** un- (“1”) + un- (“1”) + enn- (“9”) + -ium (element suffix) → ununennium (“element 119”)
