### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To take care of; to attend to; to check up on.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I'll see about that when the time comes.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To inform oneself about; to come to know of; to look for.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** We'll see about that!

   **Example:** I have to see about a good doctor.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** See your supervisor about the incident!
