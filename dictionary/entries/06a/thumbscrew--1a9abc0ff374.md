### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A screw that is designed to be turned with the thumb and fingers, thus requiring no screwdriver or spanner (wrench).

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An instrument of torture used to crush the fingers.

   **Source synonyms:** pilliwinks

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A weakness that can be taken advantage of.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** Law 33&#58; Discover Each Man's Thumbscrew ― Robert Greene, The 48 Laws of Power
