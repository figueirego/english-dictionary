### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To stand about without any aim or purpose; to stand about idly.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** For some reason, they discourage loitering outside the store, but encourage it inside.

   **Source synonyms:** lepak (Malaysia; Singapore); linger; hang around

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To stroll about without any aim or purpose, to ramble, to wander.

   **Usage:** archaic; intransitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To remain at a certain place instead of moving on.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Of an aircraft, to remain in the air near a target.

   **Usage:** intransitive
