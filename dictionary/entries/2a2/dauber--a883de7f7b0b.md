### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who, or that which, daubs; especially, a coarse, unskillful painter.

   **Usage:** derogatory

   **Source synonyms:** alcatote (British; obsolete); auf (obsolete); botcher; bumbler; bungler; blunderer; blunderhead; butterfingers; chucklehead; clodhopper (British); dauber; duffer; dumbshit (vulgar); fuckup (vulgar); fumblefingers (colloquial; rare); fumbler; galoot; gaum (Irish); gawk; gobaloon (Irish); goon; gype (British); trapes; klutz; lomperd; looby; lubber; lummox; lumpkin; maladroit; mess-up; messup; muff; oaf; palooka; peabrain; pisher; sad sack; schlemiel; schmuck (US); screwup (vulgar); shit bird (vulgar); slangrill (obsolete); spaz (sometimes; vulgar); stumblebum; turkey; weapon (British); yutz

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A pad or ball of rags, covered with canvas, for inking plates.

   **Usage:** copperplate printing

   **Source synonyms:** dabber; inkball

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A type of thick marker pen used to mark a bingo card.

   **Source synonyms:** dabber; dobber

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A low and gross flatterer.

   **Usage:** archaic

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The mud wasp; the mud dauber.
