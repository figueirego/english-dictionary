## Noun · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˌɹɛp.ɹə.zɛnˈteɪ.ʃən/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Forms

- representations — plural
- repræsentation — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. That which represents something else.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The Venus of Willendorf was an early representation of the female body.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The act of representing.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** We are no longer happy with your representation of our company at trade events.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The appearance(s) of a particular demographic group in a piece of media, particularly in regards to how such appearances are treated.

   **Usage:** broadly; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The lawyers and staff who argue on behalf of another in court.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** People who cannot afford representation are eligible for government assistance.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The ability to elect a representative to speak on one's behalf in government; the role of this representative in government.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The lack of representation in the British parliament was one of the main factors behind the American Revolution.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; politics

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. An action of some algebraic structure (typically a group or algebra, particularly a Lie algebra) on a vector space, such that each element acts by a linear endomorphism.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A figure, image or idea that substitutes reality.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A theatrical performance.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A statement; a presentation of opinion or position, or an utterance made to influence the opinions or actions of others

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
