### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To forbid, disallow, or proscribe officially; to make illegal or illicit.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The restaurant prohibits smoking on the patio.

   **Example:** I was prohibited to come.

   **Example:** Crossing the border without permission is prohibited.

   **Source synonyms:** ban; disallow; forbid; proscribe; veto

   **Source antonyms:** allow; authorize; permit
