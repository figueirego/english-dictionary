### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A grain used extensively in Europe for making bread, beer, and (now generally) for animal fodder.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** They bought a sack of rye and a sack of wheat with the intent to try their hand at milling and baking.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The grass Secale cereale from which the grain is obtained.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** This field will be planted to rye next spring.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Ellipsis of rye bread.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; countable; ellipsis; uncountable

   **Example:** Customer&#58; A tuna sandwich, please. Waiter&#58; Sure, hon. You want that on white, wheat, or rye?

   **Alternative form of:** [rye bread](../208/rye-bread--822fe866036c.md)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Ellipsis of rye whiskey, whiskey made mainly or wholly from rye grain.

   **Usage:** Canada; US; abbreviation; alt-of; countable; ellipsis; uncountable

   **Example:** He likes any whiskey, but his favorite is rye.

   **Alternative form of:** rye whiskey; whiskey made mainly or wholly from rye grain

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A drink (serving) of rye whiskey.

   **Usage:** Canada; US; countable

   **Example:** I'll have a rye, neat, please.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Caraway (from the mistaken assumption that the whole seeds, often used to season rye bread, are the rye itself)

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; proscribed; sometimes; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Ryegrass, any of the species of Lolium.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; proscribed; sometimes; uncountable

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A disease of hawks.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A young man.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; gypsy slang
