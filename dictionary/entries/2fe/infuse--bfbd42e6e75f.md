### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To cause to become an element of something; to insert or fill.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To steep in a liquid, so as to extract the soluble constituents (usually medicinal or herbal).

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To inspire; to inspirit or animate; to fill (with).

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To instill as a quality.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** breathe

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To undergo infusion.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Let it infuse for five minutes.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To make an infusion with (an ingredient); to tincture; to saturate.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To pour in, as a liquid; to pour (into or upon); to shed.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive
