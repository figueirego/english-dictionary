### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A humorous verbal "award" given to a batter who has struck out four times in a single game.

   **Example:** Jones got a golden sombrero last night; he had a hat trick (three strikeouts) going into the ninth inning, then struck out for the fourth time.
