## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A recessed carving on an Ancient Egyptian tomb wall representing a palace façade with a door at its center, intended to serve as a threshold between the worlds of the living and the dead, and in front of which offerings were placed.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Egyptology; history; human-sciences; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** ka-door

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A comparable architectural element in tombs of other cultures, such as Etruscan or ancient Sardinian tombs.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** archaeology; history; human-sciences; sciences
