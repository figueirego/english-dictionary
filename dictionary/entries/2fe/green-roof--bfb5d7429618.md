### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A roof of a house or other building which is covered with soil and grass, plants, trees, or other landscaping materials.

   **Source synonyms:** ecoroof

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A roof of a house or other building on which solar panels, solar hot water collectors, wind powered turbines, or other natural energy collectors are placed.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A roof of a house or other building which is designed to collect rainfall for use in gardens, lawns, or as drinking water.
