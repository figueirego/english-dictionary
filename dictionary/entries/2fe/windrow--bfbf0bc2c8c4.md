### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A row of cut grain or hay allowed to dry in a field.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A line of leaves etc heaped up by the wind.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A similar streak of seaweed etc on the surface of the sea formed by Langmuir circulation.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A line of gravel left behind by the edge of a grader’s blade.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

   1. A ridge or berm at a perimeter

      **Usage:** broadly

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A line of snow left behind by the edge of a snowplow’s blade.

   **Usage:** Canada

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A line of snow left behind by the edge of a snowplow’s blade.
   1. A long snowbank along the side of a road.

      **Usage:** Canada; broadly
