### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having a fast pace, tempo, or beat.

   **Example:** The notes are easy, but it's an upbeat tune and should be played fairly quickly.

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Example:** Though he had bad news, he ended with an upbeat forecast for the future.

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. An unaccented beat at the start of a musical phrase.

   **Example:** The conductor said&#58; I give you three beats for nothing and then you come in on the upbeat.
