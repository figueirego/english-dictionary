### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Inclined to find fault or criticize.

   **Example:** A good teacher is fair but critical.

   **Source synonyms:** fastidious; captious; censorious; exacting; judgmental; slashing

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Pertaining to, or indicating, a crisis or turning point.

   **Example:** This is a critical moment.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Extremely important.

   **Example:** It's critical that you deliver this on time.

   **Source synonyms:** crucial; imperative

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Relating to criticism or careful analysis, such as literary or film criticism.

   **Example:** The movie was a critical success, but bombed at the box-office.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Relating to criticism or careful analysis, such as literary or film criticism.
   1. Employing or related to textual criticism, particularly through a stemmatological comparison of all extant texts and reconstruction of the original.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences; textual-criticism

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Of a patient condition involving unstable vital signs and a prognosis that predicts the condition could worsen; or, a patient condition that requires urgent treatment in an intensive care or critical care medical facility.

   **Example:** The patient's condition is critical.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. In such a condition.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** The patient is critical.

   **Example:** Two critical after fatal Wimbledon school crash

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Likely to go out of control if disturbed, that is, opposite of stable.

   **Example:** The political situation was so critical that the government declared the state of siege.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. Of the point (in temperature, reagent concentration etc.) where a nuclear or chemical reaction becomes self-sustaining.

   **Example:** The reaction was about to become critical.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. Of a temperature that is equal to the temperature of the critical point of a substance, i.e. the temperature above which the substance cannot be liquefied.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. Needing great discrimination to be correctly classified; easily confused.

   **Example:** critical plants

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences
