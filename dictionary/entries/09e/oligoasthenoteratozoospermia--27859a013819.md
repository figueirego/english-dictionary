**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A male fertility condition characterized by low sperm count, poor sperm, and abnormal sperm shape occurring together. These abnormalities can reduce the likelihood of natural conception and are a common cause of male infertility.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; pathology; sciences
