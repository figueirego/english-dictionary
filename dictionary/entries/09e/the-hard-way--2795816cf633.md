### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In a way that is more difficult than necessary; by adverse experience.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** He never asks for advice; he always does things the hard way.

   **Example:** They'll find out the hard way that there's no such thing as a free lunch.

   **Example:** I don't know how well seatbelts protect you, but I certainly don't want to find out the hard way, so I use them either way.

   **Example:** You can't assume that someone else will call an ambulance. I learnt that the hard way after crashing my bike.
