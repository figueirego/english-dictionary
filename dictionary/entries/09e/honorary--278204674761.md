## Adjective · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈɑn.əˌɹɛɹ.i/ — source labels: General-American
- enPR: ŏnʹə-rĕr'ē — source labels: General-American

### Forms

- honourary — alternative; archaic

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Given as an honor, with no duties attached, and without payment.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** honorary degree; honorary citizen

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Voluntary.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Unofficial; practically. Describes the holder of an unofficial position or title that is assigned as a special honor rather than by normal channels.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Megsie is an honorary employee because she helps other customers while she shops here.

   **Example:** I consider you an honorary member of our family because you've been with us for so long.
