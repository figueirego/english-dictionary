## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The categorical opposition to the belief in any deity.

   **Usage:** secular; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Alternative form of misotheism.

   **Usage:** alt-of; alternative; uncountable; chiefly theistic

   **Usage:** alt-of; alternative; secular; uncountable
