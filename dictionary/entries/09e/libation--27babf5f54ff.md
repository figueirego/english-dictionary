### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of pouring a liquid, most often wine, in sacrifice on the ground, on a ritual object, or on a victim, in honor of some deity.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** tip; tipping
