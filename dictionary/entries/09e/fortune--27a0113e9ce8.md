### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Destiny, especially favorable.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** She read my fortune. Apparently I will have a good love life this week, but I will have a bad week for money.

   **Source synonyms:** doom; fate; chance (archaic); destin (obsolete); destiny; doom; fatality; fate; foredoom; foreordination; fortune; kismet; lot; orlay (Germanic); portion; predestination; predestiny (obsolete); preordination; qadar; weird (archaic); wyrd (Old-Norse)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A prediction or set of predictions about a person's future provided by a fortune teller.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A small slip of paper with wise or vaguely prophetic words printed on it, baked into a fortune cookie.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The arrival of something in a sudden or unexpected manner; chance; accident.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** hap; luck; adventure (obsolete); accident; casualty; chance; chance-medley (obsolete); fortune; hap (archaic); hazard; joss; luck; serendipity; weird (archaic)

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Good luck.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** fame and fortune

   **Example:** Fortune favors the brave.

   **Source synonyms:** fortuity; felicity (rare); fortune; fortuitousness; fortuity; good cess; good fortune; good luck; happiness (archaic); luck

   **Source antonyms:** doom; fate; misfortune

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. One's wealth; the amount of money one has, especially if it is vast.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** He's amassed a small fortune working in the Middle East.

   **Example:** My vast fortune was a result of inheritance and stock market nous.

   **Example:** Her fortune is estimated at 3 million dollars.

   **Source synonyms:** riches; abundance; affluence; cheese (slang); ease; fortune; substance (literary); Mammon; opulence; plenty; richdom; riches; wealth

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A large amount of money.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** That car must be worth a fortune! How could you afford it?
