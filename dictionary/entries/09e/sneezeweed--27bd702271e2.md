### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A plant of the genus Helenium, especially Helenium autumnale.

   **Usage:** US; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A plant of the genus Centipeda; either of the species Centipeda cunninghami or Centipeda minima, which induce sneezing and are known as a folk remedy for colds and allergic reactions.

   **Usage:** Australia; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Synonym of sneezewort (Achillea ptarmica).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
