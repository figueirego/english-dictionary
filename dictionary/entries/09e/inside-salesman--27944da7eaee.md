### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A sales representative employed by a retail or wholesale vendor who works on the premises of his or her employer, transacting with customers directly on a sales floor or by telephone, online messaging, etc.

   **Usage:** sales

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
