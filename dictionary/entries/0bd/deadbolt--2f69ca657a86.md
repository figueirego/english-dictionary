## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈdɛdˌboʊ̯lt/ — source labels: General-American

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The part of the lock which is moved when the key is engaged.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A kind of lock in which the bolt (moving portion) is held in position by the cylinder rather than by a spring and so cannot be retracted except by turning the cylinder.

   **Source synonyms:** deadlock
