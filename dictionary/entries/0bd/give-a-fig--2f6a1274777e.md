### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To care what someone else thinks or does, particularly in relation to what they think or say about the speaker.

   **Usage:** euphemistic; informal; negative

   **Example:** I couldn’t give a fig!

   **Source synonyms:** becare; bother; care; concern oneself; heed (archaic); mind; reck (archaic); trouble oneself; worry
