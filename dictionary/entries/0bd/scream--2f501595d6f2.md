### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A loud, emphatic, exclamation of extreme emotion, especially horror, fear, excitement, or anger; it may comprise a word or a sustained, high-pitched vowel sound.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A loud vocalisation of many animals, especially in response to pain or fear.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A form of singing associated with the metal and screamo styles of music. It is a loud, rough, distorted version of the voice; rather than the normal voice of the singer.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Used as an intensifier.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** We had a real scream of a time at the beach.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An exclamation mark.

   **Usage:** printers' slang

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To cry out with a shrill voice; to utter a sudden, sharp outcry, or shrill, loud cry, as in fright or extreme pain; to screech, to shriek.

   **Usage:** also; figuratively; intransitive

   **Source synonyms:** call; cry; holler; halloo; roar; howl; roop; scream; shout; yell

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To move quickly; to race.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

   **Example:** He almost hit a pole, the way he came screaming down the hill.

   **Source synonyms:** speed; zoom; hightail; move quickly; skedaddle; fly; scramble; get a move on; hasten; haul; hurtle; bustle; haul ass; haul one's ashes; hie; hump it; hurry; hustle; make haste; make tracks; move it; rattle one's hocks; rush; scream; shift; skedaddle (US); swap (obsolete); speed; zip; zoom

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To be very indicative of; clearly having the characteristics of.

   **Usage:** figuratively; informal; intransitive

   **Example:** Do you know what screams "I’m obnoxious"? People who feel the need to comment on every little thing they notice.
