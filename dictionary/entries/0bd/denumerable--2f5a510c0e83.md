## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Capable of being assigned a bijection to the natural numbers. Applied to sets which are not finite, but have a one-to-one mapping to the natural numbers.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The empty set is not denumerable because it is finite; the rational numbers are, surprisingly, denumerable because every possible fraction can be assigned a natural number and vice versa.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** countable; countably infinite
