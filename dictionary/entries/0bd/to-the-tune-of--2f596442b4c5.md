### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Fitting the melody of.

   **Usage:** literally

   **Example:** The lyrics were written to the tune of "Amazing Grace".

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Roughly; about; to the approximate sum or extent of.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** The damage that he did to his car was to the tune of two grand.
