## Verb

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To follow with the eyes; to look in the direction of (someone or something departing).

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To seek out, to look for.

   **Usage:** regional; transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To care for; to keep safe.

   **Usage:** also; reflexive; transitive

   **Example:** He asked me to look after his daughter while he was away.

   **Source synonyms:** take care of

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To have as one's business; to manage, be responsible for.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** keep

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To expect, look forward to.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive
