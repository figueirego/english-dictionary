### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In the surrounding ambience.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** As soon as we entered we could feel the excitement and tension in the air.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In the generally prevailing feeling, idea, atmosphere, or mood.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Prevalent without traceable origin or authority.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** There was a rumour about them in the air.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Not in a fixed or stable position; unsettled; up in the air.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Unsupported and liable to be turned or taken in flank.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** The army had its wing in the air.
