### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The inflorescence of the Cannabis sativa plant, smoked or ingested for euphoric effect.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** After smoking a bowl of that fine marijuana, they ate some brownies.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The hemp plant itself, Cannabis sativa.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually
