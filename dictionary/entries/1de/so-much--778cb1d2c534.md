## Determiner · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈsəʊ ˈmʌtʃ/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A particular amount of (something), often a large or excessive amount.

   **Example:** How could you eat so much ice-cream?

   **Example:** There is only so much information you can remember.

   **Example:** I've got so much work to do, but so little time.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A particular amount of (something), often a large or excessive amount.
   1. Making a comparison with another situation; as much.

      **Usage:** usually

      **Example:** There isn't so much sand on this beach as there used to be.

      **Source context (verbatim):** with negatives

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A particular amount of (something), often a large or excessive amount.
   1. An amount of (something) sufficient to bring about a given consequence.

      **Example:** There is so much snow, I can't get out of the door.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A demonstrated amount of (something).

   **Example:** "Just so much flour," he replied, sprinkling a small pile on the table.

## Adverb · Record 3

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈsəʊ ˈmʌtʃ/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Senses

<a id="r3-s1"></a>

**Source sense R3.S1**

1. To a great degree or extent; very much.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** I love you so much.

<a id="r3-s2"></a>

**Source sense R3.S2**

2. To such a degree or extent.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Will you please stop complaining so much!

   **Example:** It wasn't so much that I don’t want to, but I just didn't have the time.

<a id="r3-s3"></a>

**Source sense R3.S3**

3. To such a degree or extent.
   1. Making a comparison with another situation; as much.

      **Usage:** not-comparable; usually

      **Example:** I used to like her a lot, but now I don't like her so much.

      **Source context (verbatim):** with negatives

<a id="r3-s4"></a>

**Source sense R3.S4**

4. To such a degree or extent.
   1. To a (usually large) extent sufficient to bring about a given consequence.

      **Usage:** not-comparable

      **Example:** Identical twins are so much alike, it is difficult to identify them.

<a id="r3-s5"></a>

**Source sense R3.S5**

5. To a demonstrated or implied degree or extent; this much, that much.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Don't over-whisk the mixture – only whisk it so much.
