### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A plate or shield with a set of holes that fits over a keyboard.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A feature on a mobile phone, etc. that prevents keys from being pressed by accident.

   **Example:** Remember to turn on the keyguard before you put the phone in your pocket.
