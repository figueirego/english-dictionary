### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To have or give off a strong, unpleasant smell.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** You reek of perfume.

   **Example:** Your fridge reeks of egg.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To be evidently associated with something unpleasant.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

   **Example:** The boss appointing his nephew as a director reeks of nepotism.

   **Source synonyms:** smack

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To be emitted or exhaled, emanate, as of vapour or perfume.

   **Usage:** archaic; intransitive

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To emit smoke or vapour; to steam.

   **Usage:** archaic; intransitive

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To cause (something) to smell.

   **Usage:** rare; transitive

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To fall in such a way (e.g. particularly finely or heavily) as to resemble smoke.

   **Usage:** archaic

   **Source context (verbatim):** of rain or snow
