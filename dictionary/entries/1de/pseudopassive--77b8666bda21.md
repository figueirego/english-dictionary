### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A construction where the object of a preposition has been promoted to the role of subject, as in The problem was talked about.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** grammar; human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A construction that removes the subject from an intransitive verb, as in the Dutch sentence Er wordt gefloten ("\[Someone\] whistled").
