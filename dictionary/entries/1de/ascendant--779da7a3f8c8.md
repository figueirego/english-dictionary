## Noun · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /əˈsɛnd(ə)nt/ — source labels: Received-Pronunciation
- IPA: /əˈsɛndənt/ — source labels: General-American

### Forms

- ascendants — plural
- ascendent — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. The degree of the zodiac or point of the ecliptic which rises in an eastern direction above the horizon at a particular moment (especially the moment of a person's birth), which is supposed to have a commanding influence on a person's fortune and life; a horoscope.

   **Usage:** also; figuratively

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astrology; human-sciences; mysticism; philosophy; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** significator of life

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Chiefly in in the ascendant&#58; an act of ascending or rising.

   **Usage:** broadly

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. Synonym of ascendancy (“commanding influence; dominant control; superiority, supremacy”).

   **Usage:** broadly; figuratively

   **Example:** One man has the ascendant over another.

   **Source synonyms:** ascendancy (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. An ancestor.

   **Usage:** broadly; genealogy

   **Source antonyms:** descendant

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. A person who ascends or goes up; specifically (usually followed by to), a person who ascends to a throne or assumes some other position of power.

   **Usage:** broadly; obsolete

   **Example:** Given his father’s ghastly demise, one would not expect such glee from the ascendant to his throne.

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. Something which is higher than the things around it; a peak, a summit; specifically (typography), synonym of ascender (“the portion of a lowercase letter that extends above the midline”).

   **Usage:** broadly; obsolete

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

**Source sense R2.S7**

7. Something which leads or slopes upwards, such as a flight of stairs or an upward incline.

   **Usage:** broadly; obsolete

<a id="r2-s8"></a>

**Source sense R2.S8**

8. A person who supports a policy of ecclesiastical or national supremacy.

   **Usage:** broadly; obsolete; rare
