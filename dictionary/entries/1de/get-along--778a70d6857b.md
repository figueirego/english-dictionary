### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To interact or coexist well, without argument or trouble.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive; often

   **Example:** I wish the kids would get along better.

   **Example:** She never did get along with her brother.

   **Source context (verbatim):** followed by with

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To survive; to do well enough.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** She didn’t have a lot of money, but she had enough to get along.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To go; to move; to leave.

   **Usage:** intransitive; progressive

   **Example:** Driver, get along there.
