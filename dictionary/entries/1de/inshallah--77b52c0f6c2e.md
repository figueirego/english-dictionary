### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Expressing the speaker’s wish for a given future event to occur, and also generally their emphasis on its contingency upon the divine will.

   **Example:** We will sign the contract tomorrow, inshallah.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Islam; lifestyle; religion

   **Source synonyms:** God willing; Deo volente; hopefully

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Expressing a skeptical affirmative, to indicate the unlikeliness of events, as if they need a divine intervention to come about.

   **Usage:** colloquial; sarcastic

   **Source synonyms:** yeah, right
