## Verb

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To assume a sitting position from a standing position.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Sit down! We have work to do.

   **Example:** I sat down in the armchair, and my sis sat down on the floor.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To cause to be seated or in a sitting posture; to furnish a seat to.

   **Usage:** reflexive; transitive

   **Example:** Sit your ass down and shut up.

   **Example:** I sat him down and explained to him how things truly work around here.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To meet formally at a conference table.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To meet with someone for discussions, negotiations or interrogation.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

   **Example:** I sat down with him about the allegations.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To assume a low or sunken position.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The ball scooted off the fairway and sat down in the thick rough.

   **Example:** As we all climbed aboard, the little boat sat down low in the water.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. In Aboriginal use, to live or stay at (a place).

   **Usage:** Australia; intransitive

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To take up residence in or settle (an area).

   **Usage:** Australia; intransitive; obsolete
