### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An isotropic scaling transformation of an affine space with a single fixed point.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A homomorphism from a module M over a ring A to itself of the form ν&#58;x↦ax for some fixed a∈A (especially when M=A; a is said to be the ratio of the homothety, by analogy with the geometric case).

   **Usage:** commutative algebra
