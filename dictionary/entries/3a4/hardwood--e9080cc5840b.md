## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈhɑɹdˌwʊd/ — source labels: General-American

### Forms

- hardwoods — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The wood from any dicotyledonous tree, without regard to its hardness.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** Balsa is a hardwood, but a soft hardwood.

   **Source context (verbatim):** in botany and forestry

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. (in more general use) As the preceding, but limited to those that are commercial timbers, and are at least average in hardness.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** Ash, hickory and oak are some of the most prominent domestic hardwoods.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The tree or tree species that yields the preceding.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** This hardwood has been planted extensively throughout the hills here.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; forestry

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A joint term for the commercial timbers, without distinguishing which.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** You should have used hardwood for this window sill instead of this junk.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The sport of basketball, in particular, an indoor basketball court; so named because the floor of an indoor basketball court is normally made of hardwood.

   **Usage:** countable; slang; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports
