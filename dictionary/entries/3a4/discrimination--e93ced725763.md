### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Discernment, the act of discriminating, discerning, distinguishing, noting or perceiving differences between things, with the intent to understand rightly and make correct decisions.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** differentiation

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Differential treatment of an individual or group to their disadvantage; treatment or consideration based on class or category rather than individual merit; partiality; prejudice; bigotry.

   **Usage:** countable; sometimes; uncountable

   **Example:** sexual or racial discrimination

   **Example:** reverse discrimination

   **Source context (verbatim):** with "against"

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The quality of being discriminating; acute discernment, especially in matters of good taste.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** tastefulness

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. That which discriminates; a distinguishing mark, a characteristic.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete
