## Noun · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ɪkˈspliːtɪv/ — source labels: UK
- IPA: /ˈɛksplətɪv/ — source labels: US

### Forms

- expletives — plural

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A profane, vulgar term, notably a curse or obscene oath.

   **Example:** If we don't take advantage of any \[expletive\] in any way, then it's our loss.

   **Source synonyms:** swear word; bad word; oath

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. A word without meaning added to fill a syntactic position.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. A word that adds to the strength of a phrase without affecting its meaning (such as fucking in "there's no fucking way he's gonna get away with it").

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** intensifier

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. An obscene or otherwise offensive interjection (such as shit, fuck, or damn it).
