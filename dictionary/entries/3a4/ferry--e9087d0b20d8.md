   **Usage:** transitive

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A boat or ship used to transport people, smaller vehicles and goods from one port to another, usually on a regular schedule.

   **Example:** Islanders normally have to queue for half an hour to get on the ferry.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

   **Source synonyms:** ferryboat

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. A place where passengers are transported across water in such a ship.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. The service constituted by this watercraft's operation; the business (company) that operates such a service.

   **Example:** In those days there was a ferry at Sleepytown. Modern roads and bridges for motor vehicles have rendered such local river ferries obsolete.
