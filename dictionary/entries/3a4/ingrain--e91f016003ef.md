### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To dye with a fast or lasting colour.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To make (something) deeply part of something else.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** The dirt was deeply ingrained in the carpet.

   **Example:** The lessons I learned at school were firmly ingrained in my mind.

   **Source synonyms:** breed in the bone; embed; infix; instill; radicate
