### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To give up, resign, surrender.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to renounce a title to land or to a throne

   **Source synonyms:** atsake (obsolete)

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To cast off, repudiate.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To decline further association with someone or something, disown.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** disown; repudiate; abjure; deny; disavow; disclaim; disown; forsay (archaic); forswear; renounce; repudiate; wash one's hands of

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To abandon, forsake, discontinue (an action, habit, intention, etc), sometimes by open declaration.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To make a renunciation of something.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To surrender formally some right or trust.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s7"></a>
