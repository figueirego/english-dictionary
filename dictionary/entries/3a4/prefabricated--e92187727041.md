## Adjective · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. manufactured in advance, usually to a standard format, and then assembled on site

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** A prefabricated home

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. invented or constructed in advance

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** They both told the police a prefabricated version of events.
