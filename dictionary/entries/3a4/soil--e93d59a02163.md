**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A mixture of mineral particles and organic material, used to support plant growth.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** We bought a bag of soil for the houseplants.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The unconsolidated mineral or organic material on the immediate surface of the earth that serves as a natural medium for the growth of land plants.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The unconsolidated mineral or organic matter on the surface of the earth that has been subjected to and shows effects of genetic and environmental factors of&#58; climate (including water and temperature effects), and macro- and microorganisms, conditioned by relief, acting on parent material over a period of time. A product-soil differs from the material from which it is derived in many physical, chemical, biological, and morphological properties and characteristics.

   **Usage:** uncountable

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Country or territory.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. That which soils or pollutes; a stain.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A marshy or miry place to which a hunted boar resorts for refuge; hence, a wet place, stream, or tract of water, sought for by other game, as deer.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Dung; compost; manure.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** night soil

<a id="r1-s8"></a>
