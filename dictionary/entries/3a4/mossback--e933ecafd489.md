**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Synonym of mossyback (“a person with old-fashioned views; hence, one who is very conservative or reactionary”).

   **Usage:** US; informal

   **Source synonyms:** conservative; reactionary; retrograde; traditionalist; mossyback (synonym; synonym-of)

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person who stayed hidden to evade conscription (especially by the Confederate States Army) during the American Civil War (1861–1865); a mossyback.

   **Usage:** US; historical; informal
