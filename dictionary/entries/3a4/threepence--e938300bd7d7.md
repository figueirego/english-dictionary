**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A former (pre-decimalisation) British or Irish coin worth three old pence.

   **Usage:** countable; historical

   **Source synonyms:** threepenny bit; thruppence
