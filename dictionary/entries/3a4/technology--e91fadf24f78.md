### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The combined application of science and art in practical ways in industry, as for example in designing new machines.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Humankind relies on technology to keep average standard of living higher than it would otherwise be.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Machines or equipment thus designed.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** We went to the trade show to see the latest technology on display.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any useful skill or mechanism that humans have developed or invented (including in prescientific eras).

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** the incipient metalworking technology of the Bronze Age

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any useful trait that has evolved in any organism.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The study of or a collection of techniques.

   **Usage:** literary; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A discourse or treatise on the arts.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; uncountable
