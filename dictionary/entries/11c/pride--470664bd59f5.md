### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The quality or state of being proud.
   1. A sense of one's own worth; reasonable self-esteem and satisfaction (in oneself, in one's work, one's family, etc).

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Example:** He swelled with pride as he held the trophy. His family watched with pride from the bleachers.

      **Example:** He takes great pride in his work.

      **Example:** He had pride of ownership in his department.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The quality or state of being proud.
   1. An unreasonable overestimation of one's own superiority in terms of talents, or looks, wealth, importance, etc., which comes across as being haughty, lofty, and often showing contempt of others; exaggerated self-worth.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Proud or disdainful behavior or treatment that reflects such an attitude (of haughtiness); arrogance.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** disdain; hubris

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Something or someone of which one is proud; that which is the source of self-congratulation and self-esteem (whether reasonable or arrogant), for example

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Her long ginger hair is her pride.

   **Example:** The 18th century table is the pride of Mark's house/

   **Example:** My children's accomplishments are my pride.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Show; ostentation; glory.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Highest level or rank; (figurative) elevation reached; loftiness or glory.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Consciousness of power; fullness of animal spirits; mettle; wantonness.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Lust or heat; sexual desire (especially in a female animal)

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A group of lions or other large felines or similar animals.

   **Usage:** collective; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** A pride of lions often consists of a dominant male, his harem and their offspring, but young adult males 'leave home' to roam about as a bachelors' pride until they are able to seize/establish a family pride of their own.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences; zoology
