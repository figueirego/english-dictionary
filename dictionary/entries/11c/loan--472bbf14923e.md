- IPA: /loʊn/ — source labels: US

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An act or instance of lending, an act or instance of granting something for temporary use.

   **Example:** Because of the loan that John made to me, I was able to pay my tuition for the upcoming semester.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** banking; business; finance; law

   **Source synonyms:** loaning; lending

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A sum of money or other property that a natural or legal person borrows from another with the condition that it be returned or repaid over time or at a later date (sometimes with interest).

   **Example:** All loans from the library, whether books or audio material, must be returned within two weeks.

   **Example:** He got a loan of five thousand pounds.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** banking; business; finance; law

   **Source synonyms:** principal

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The contract and array of legal or ethical obligations surrounding a loan.

   **Example:** He made a payment on his loan.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The permission to borrow any item.

   **Example:** Thank you for the loan of your lawn mower.
