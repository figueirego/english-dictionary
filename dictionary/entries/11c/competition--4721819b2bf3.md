### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The action of competing.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** The competition for this job is strong.

   **Example:** Several firms are in competition for the contract.

   **Source synonyms:** contention

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A contest for a prize or award.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** The newspaper is featuring a competition to win a car.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The competitors in such a contest.

   **Usage:** collective; uncountable

   **Example:** The new stain remover was ten times more effective than the competition.

### Related words

**Source antonyms:** cooperation — antonym(s) of “action of competing”
