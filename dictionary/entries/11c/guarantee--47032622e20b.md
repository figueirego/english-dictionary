### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Anything that assures a certain outcome.

   **Example:** Can you give me a guarantee that he will be fit for the match?

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A legal assurance of something, e.g. a security for the fulfillment of an obligation.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A written declaration that a certain product will be fit for a purpose and work correctly; a warranty.

   **Usage:** specifically

   **Example:** The cooker comes with a five-year guarantee.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The person to whom a guarantee is made.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A person who gives such a guarantee; a guarantor.

   **Usage:** colloquial
