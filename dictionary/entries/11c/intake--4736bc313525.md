### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The place where water, air or other fluid is taken into a pipe or conduit; opposed to outlet.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The beginning of a contraction or narrowing in a tube or cylinder.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The quantity taken in.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** the intake of air

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An act or instance of taking in.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** an intake of oxygen or food

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A nostril, especially a large one.

   **Usage:** countable; derogatory; slang; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The people taken into an organization or establishment at a particular time.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** the new intake of students

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The process of screening a juvenile offender to decide upon release or referral.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A tract of land enclosed.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

   **Usage:** UK; countable; dialectal; uncountable
