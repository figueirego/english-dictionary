### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To bring about by urging or encouraging.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to instigate a riot

   **Example:** to instigate a change

   **Source synonyms:** foment; provoke

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To goad or urge (a person) forward, especially to wicked actions.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to instigate someone to a crime

   **Source synonyms:** animate; encourage; impel; incite; provoke; spur; stimulate; tempt; urge

   **Source antonyms:** halt; prevent; stop
