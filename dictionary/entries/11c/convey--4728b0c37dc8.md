### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To move (something) from one place to another.

   **Example:** Air conveys sound. Water is conveyed through the pipe.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To take or carry (someone) from one place to another.

   **Usage:** dated

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To communicate; to make known; to portray.

   **Example:** to convey an impression; to convey information

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To transfer legal rights (to).

   **Example:** He conveyed ownership of the company to his daughter.

   **Example:** The refrigerator, washer, and dryer do not convey unless it's specifically laid out in the contract.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To manage with privacy; to carry out.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To carry or take away secretly; to steal; to thieve.

   **Usage:** obsolete
