### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The action of pretending; false or simulated show or appearance; false or hypocritical assertion or representation.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** He visited the king under the pretense of friendliness.

   **Example:** "Lady Little", the title that she used, was just a pretense.

   **Example:** She appeared to weep uncontrollably, but it was all pretense.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Affectation or ostentation of manner.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** She was a plain-speaking woman without a hint of pretense.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Intention or purpose not real but professed.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** with only a pretense of accuracy

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An unsupported claim made or implied.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An insincere attempt to reach a specific condition or quality.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Intention; design.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable
