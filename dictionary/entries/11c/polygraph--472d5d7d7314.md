<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Source synonyms:** lie detector

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** dated

   **Source synonyms:** manifold writer; autopen

3. A collection of different works, either by one or several authors.

   **Usage:** archaic
