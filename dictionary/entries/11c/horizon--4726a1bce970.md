### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The visible horizontal line (in all directions) where the sky appears to meet the earth in the distance.

   **Example:** A tall building was visible on the whole sweep of the horizon.

   **Example:** The sky and sea seem to merge at the horizon.

   **Source synonyms:** skysill; skyline

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The range or limit of one's knowledge, experience or interest; a boundary or threshold.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** Some students take a gap year after finishing high school to broaden their horizons.

   **Example:** With clinical researchers hard at work, a new treatment is on the horizon.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The range or limit of any dimension in which one exists.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A specific layer of soil, or stratum

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; geology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A cultural sub-period or level within a more encompassing time period.

   **Usage:** US

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** archaeology; history; human-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Any level line or surface.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**
