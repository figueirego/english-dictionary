### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A technique for creating the appearance that the object of a drawing is extending into space by shortening the lines with which that object is drawn.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** art; arts
