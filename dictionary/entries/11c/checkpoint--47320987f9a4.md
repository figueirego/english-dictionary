## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈt͡ʃɛkˌpɔɪnt/ — source labels: General-American

### Forms

- checkpoints — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A point or place where a check is performed, especially a point along a road or on a frontier where travellers are stopped for inspection.

   **Example:** The travellers were stopped at the checkpoint.

   **Example:** Since this was rebel country, checkpoints were set up along the road.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A situation, often represented by a point in time, at which the state of a database system is known to be valid, and to which it can be returned in the event of a crisis by using a combination of backups and logs; the data stored at this event.

   **Example:** After the crash, we rolled back the database to the last checkpoint.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A predetermined point in a map, level or scenario that the player may resume from if they die or restart.

   **Example:** You can't finish the race if you haven't passed all of the checkpoints on the track.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** video-games

   **Source synonyms:** savepoint

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A biological control mechanism ensuring correct progression through the cell cycle.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences
