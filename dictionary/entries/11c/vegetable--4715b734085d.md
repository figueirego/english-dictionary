### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any plant.

   **Usage:** archaic

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A plant raised for some edible part of it, such as the leaves, roots, fruit or flowers, but excluding any plant considered to be a fruit, grain, herb, or spice in the culinary sense, and usually including non-plants lumped into the same culinary category, such as edible mushrooms and seaweed.

   **Source synonyms:** veg (informal); veggie (informal)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The edible part of such a plant.

   **Source synonyms:** veg (informal); veggie (informal)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A person whose brain (or, infrequently, whose body) has been damaged to the point that they cannot interact with the surrounding environment; a person in a persistent vegetative state.

   **Usage:** derogatory; figuratively; often

   **Source synonyms:** cabbage

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A mine (explosive device).

   **Usage:** historical; slang; RAF
