### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To detect with the senses, especially with the eyes.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To perceive, recognize, or comprehend with the mind; to descry.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To distinguish something as being different from something else; to differentiate or discriminate.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He was too young to discern right from wrong.

**Source sense R1.S4**

   **Usage:** intransitive
