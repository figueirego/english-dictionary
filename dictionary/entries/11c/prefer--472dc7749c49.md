### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To be in the habit of choosing something rather than something else; to favor; to like better.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I prefer tea to coffee.

   **Example:** I'd prefer it if you didn't do it.

   **Example:** I'd prefer to stay home rather than drive/driving in the blizzard.

   **Source synonyms:** fain; favor; forechoose

   **Source antonyms:** disprefer

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To advance, promote (someone or something).

   **Usage:** dated; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** elevate; raise; uplift; advance; aggrandize; dignify; elevate; ennoble; exalt; honor; nobilitate; prefer (dated); promote; raise; uplift

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To present or submit (something) to an authority (now usually in "to prefer charges").

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To put forward for acceptance; to introduce, recommend (to).

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive
