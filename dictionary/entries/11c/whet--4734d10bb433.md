   **Source synonyms:** steel; strop

### Senses

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. An act of sharpening something by rubbing on an object, especially a whetstone.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

   **Usage:** broadly; dialectal; archaic except UK

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

   **Usage:** broadly; dialectal; archaic except UK

   **Usage:** figuratively

**Source sense R2.S5**
