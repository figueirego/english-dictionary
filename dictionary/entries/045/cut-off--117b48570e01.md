### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To remove by cutting.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** prune; trim

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To isolate or remove from contact.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To stop the provision or supply of something, e.g. power, water.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To exclude (something) from the field of view.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Half of his head has been cut off by the edge of the photo.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a boundary

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To stop providing funds or something else to (someone).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** His parents cut him off to encourage him to find a job.

   **Example:** You're drunk, Jerry, I'm cutting you off.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To end abruptly.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** My phone call was cut off before I could get the information.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To interrupt (someone speaking).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** That dingbat cut me off as I was about to conclude my thesis.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To swerve in front of (another car) while driving.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** cut up

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To move so as to block someone else's movement in a direction.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I ran to the house, but Sally is quicker and cut me off.

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. To turn off or switch off (an electrical device).

   **Usage:** Southern-US; transitive

   **Example:** Cut off the lamp so I can get some sleep.
