### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To break or separate into pieces.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Break up the cheese and put it in the salad.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To break or separate into pieces; to disintegrate or come apart.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** It broke up when it hit the ground.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To upset greatly; to cause great emotional disturbance or unhappiness in.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To end a (usually romantic or sexual) relationship.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** She broke up with her boyfriend last week.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To end a (usually romantic or sexual) relationship with each other.

   **Usage:** intransitive; reciprocal

   **Example:** Jane and Stephen broke up.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To dissolve; to part.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** The meeting finally broke up after a three-hour discussion.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Of a school, to close for the holidays at the end of term.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Of a conversation, to cease to be understandable because of a bad connection; of a signal, to deteriorate.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** You're breaking up. Can you repeat that? Well, it got cut off.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** communications; electrical-engineering; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; telecommunications

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To stop a fight; to separate people who are fighting.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** The police came in to break up the disturbance.

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. To become disorganised.

   **Usage:** figuratively; idiomatic; intransitive

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. To cut or take to pieces for scrap.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s12"></a>

**Source sense R1.S12**

12. To be or cause to be overcome with laughter.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive; slang; transitive
