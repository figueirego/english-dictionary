### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A great flood or rain.

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An overwhelming amount of something; anything that overwhelms or causes great destruction.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** The rock concert was a deluge of sound.
