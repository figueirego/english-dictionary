### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Everything someone uses or does fails or becomes worse.

   **Usage:** derogatory; idiomatic; vulgar

   **Example:** This irresponsible artist turned everything he touched into shit, wrecking whatever projects he was assigned into with such a shoddy performance.

   **Source antonyms:** everything one touches turns to gold
