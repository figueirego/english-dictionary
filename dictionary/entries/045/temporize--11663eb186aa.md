### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To deliberately act evasively or prolong a discussion in order to gain time or postpone a decision, sometimes so that a compromise can be reached or simply to make a conversation more temperate; to stall for time.

   **Usage:** British; English; Oxford; US; intransitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To discuss, to negotiate; to reach a compromise.

   **Usage:** British; English; Oxford; US; broadly; intransitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To apply a temporary piece of dental work that will later be removed.

   **Usage:** British; English; Oxford; US; transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** dentistry; medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To comply with the occasion or time; to humour, or yield to, current circumstances or opinion; also, to trim (“fluctuate between parties, so as to appear to favour each”).

   **Usage:** British; English; Oxford; US; archaic; intransitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To delay, especially until a more favourable time; to procrastinate.

   **Usage:** British; English; Oxford; US; intransitive; obsolete

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To take temporary measures or actions to manage a situation without providing a definitive or permanent solution.

   **Usage:** British; English; Oxford; US; transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences
