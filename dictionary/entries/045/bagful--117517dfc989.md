### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The amount that fills a bag.

   **Example:** She carried a bagful of groceries into the house.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A large assortment.

   **Example:** The politician had a bagful of humorous anecdotes she could interject into any spur of the moment stump speech.
