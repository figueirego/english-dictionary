### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see take, it.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To start doing.

   **Example:** take it from the top

   **Example:** I began the project; now you take it from here.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To suppose; to assume.

   **Example:** I take it on trust that you won't tell my secret.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To suppose; to assume.
   1. To assume based on evidence or observation; to gather.

      **Usage:** first-person; usually

      **Example:** I take it that she didn't like the cake. (e.g. on seeing the cake thrown in the bin)

      **Source context (verbatim):** with a clause

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To endure affliction, criticism, punishment or torment.

   **Example:** She finally left her abusive husband. She couldn't take it any more.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To taste or sample some food or drink.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To accept something even though it may not be perfect or ideal; to acquiesce.

   **Example:** Today's kinda hot and humid, but at least the heat wave is over and there's a breeze&#58; I'll take it.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To submit to passive sexual intercourse.
