## Verb

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. to request that an owner borrows or gives something of theirs one is in need, under the Bubuti system, which makes the request unrefusable.

   **Usage:** ditransitive

   **Example:** I bubuti you for your flip-flops.
