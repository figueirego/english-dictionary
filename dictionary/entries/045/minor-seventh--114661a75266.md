### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A musical interval in Western music that is equal to or approximates a ratio of 9/5 or 16/9, depending on the context. In twelve-tone equal temperament, it spans ten semitones and seven degrees of the diatonic scale.

   **Example:** The minor seventh has a pitch ratio close to 57&#58;32.
