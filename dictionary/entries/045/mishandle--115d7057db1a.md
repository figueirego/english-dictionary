### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To manipulate something roughly, causing physical damage.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** If you mishandle a fragile package, the contents may break.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To deal with a situation incorrectly or ineffectively; to make a mistake in handling a situation.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The prime minister was accused of mishandling the diplomatic crisis.
