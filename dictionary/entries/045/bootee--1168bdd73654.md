### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A soft, woolen shoe, usually knitted, for a baby or small pet.

   **Example:** Take off the baby's bootees before you put her in the crib.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A thick sock worn under a wetsuit.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An overshoe or sock worn to cover dirty shoes or feet.

   **Example:** Surgeons often slip on bootees before entering the operating room.
