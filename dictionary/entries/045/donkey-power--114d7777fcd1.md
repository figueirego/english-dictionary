## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A unit of power equal to 250 watts, or about one third of a horsepower.

   **Usage:** humorous

   **Example:** The Yugo car was famous for how anemic its engine was, but its output sounded more impressive when reported in donkey power.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; motive power provided by one or more donkeys.

   **Example:** In that era, most of the equipment in this mine was run by donkey power.
