### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Alternative form of know one's place (“to understand one's rank or status and behave accordingly; to act humbly”).

   **Usage:** alt-of; alternative; idiomatic

   **Example:** My grandfather believed that life was simpler when he was young, when everybody knew their place.

   **Alternative form of:** [know one's place](../344/know-one-s-place--d1276627bf1f.md)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Alternative form of know one's place (“to understand one's rank or status and behave accordingly; to act humbly”).
   1. To accept your position within society, an organization, your family, etc. and to not want to improve it.

      **Usage:** idiomatic

      **Example:** I just get on with my job and do as I'm told - I know my place.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Alternative form of know one's place (“to understand one's rank or status and behave accordingly; to act humbly”).
   1. To be aware of the limit of one's role in a particular place or situation.

      **Usage:** idiomatic

      **Example:** I know my place, so I don't bother the CEO when I see him around the office. I'm just an intern, after all.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Alternative form of know one's place (“to understand one's rank or status and behave accordingly; to act humbly”).
   1. To understand that you are less important than other people.

      **Usage:** idiomatic; insulting

      **Example:** You ought to know your place, filthy commoner!
