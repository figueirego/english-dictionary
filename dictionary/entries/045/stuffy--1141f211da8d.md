### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Poorly ventilated; partially plugged.

   **Example:** I can’t smell very well today – I have a stuffy nose.

   **Example:** Let’s go outside – it’s getting stuffy in here.

   **Source synonyms:** fusty

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Uncomfortably warm without sufficient air circulation.

   **Source synonyms:** oppressive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Stout; mettlesome; resolute.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Angry and obstinate; sulky.

   **Usage:** US

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Boring, old-fashioned, uninteresting, over-formal, pompous, very conventional.

   **Example:** The stuffy professor droned on as the class lost interest.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Stout; sturdy.

   **Usage:** Scotland
