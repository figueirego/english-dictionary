## Phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An expression of contempt or dismissiveness directed at someone, particularly in the case of disagreement.

   **Usage:** colloquial; dated; idiomatic

   **Example:** I don't care what he thinks! He can just sit on a tack!

   **Example:** "Go sit on a tack!" she said, angrily.

**Source sense R1.S2**
