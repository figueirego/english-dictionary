## Noun · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /pɪθ/ — source labels: General-American; Received-Pronunciation

### Forms

- piths — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The soft, spongy substance inside plant parts; specifically, the parenchyma in the centre of the roots and stems of many plants and trees.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences

   **Source synonyms:** marrow (archaic); medulla

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The albedo (“whitish inner portion of the rind”) of a citrus fruit.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Senses relating to humans and animals.
   1. The soft tissue inside a human or animal body or one of their organs; specifically, the spongy interior substance of a horn or the shaft of a feather.

      **Usage:** broadly; uncountable; usually

      **Source synonyms:** medulla

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Senses relating to humans and animals.
   1. Chiefly of animals&#58; the soft tissue inside a spinal cord; the spinal marrow; also, the spinal cord itself.

      **Usage:** broadly; uncountable; usually

      **Source synonyms:** medulla

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Senses relating to humans and animals.
   1. Synonym of diploe (“the thin layer of soft, spongy, or cancellate tissue between the bone plates which constitute the skull”).

      **Usage:** broadly; obsolete; uncountable; usually

      **Source synonyms:** diploe (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Senses relating to humans and animals.
   1. The soft tissue of the brain.

      **Usage:** broadly; obsolete; rare; uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The soft inner portion of a loaf of bread.

   **Usage:** Ireland; Southern-England; West-Country; broadly; uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. The central or innermost part of something; the core, the heart.

   **Usage:** figuratively; uncountable; usually

   **Source synonyms:** kernel; marrow

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. The essential or vital part of something; the essence.

   **Usage:** figuratively; uncountable; usually

   **Example:** The pith of my idea is that people should choose their own work hours.

   **Source synonyms:** crux; gist; heart; heart and soul; inwardness; kernel; marrow; meat; medulla (obsolete); nitty-gritty; nub; quintessence; [soul](../2b8/soul--ae0facccf0a3.md); spirit; substance; bowels; sum; brass tacks; core; crux; essence; gist; drift; guts; gravamen; heart; heart and soul; heartstring (archaic); heartstrings (archaic); inwardness (obsolete); kernel; marrow; meat; moral of the story (idiomatic); nitty-gritty; nub; nuts and bolts (idiomatic); pith (figuratively); punctum saliens; quintessence; [soul](../2b8/soul--ae0facccf0a3.md); spirit; stuff; substance; tenor

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. Physical power or strength; force, might.

   **Usage:** figuratively; uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. A quality of courage and endurance; backbone, mettle, spine.

   **Usage:** figuratively; uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s12"></a>

**Source sense R1.S12**

12. The energy, force, or power of speech or writing; specifically, such force or power due to conciseness; punch, punchiness.

   **Usage:** figuratively; uncountable; usually
