### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A war waged despite not being necessary, such as for the acquisition of land, resources, or power.

   **Example:** This war we find to be a war of choice, not of necessity, and hence call for its immediate discontinuation and for the restoration of antebellum relations.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; politics

   **Source antonyms:** war of necessity
