<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A small opening; a small pit or depression, especially in bone.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A small opening; a small pit or depression, especially in bone.
   1. A space visible between cells, allowing free passage of light.

      **Usage:** microscopy

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A small blank space; a gap or vacancy; a hiatus.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A small blank space; a gap or vacancy; a hiatus.
   1. An absent part, especially in a book or other piece of writing, often referring to an ancient manuscript or similar.

      **Example:** Long lacunae in this inscription make interpretation difficult.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A small blank space; a gap or vacancy; a hiatus.
   1. Any gap, break, hole, or lack in a set of things; something missing.

      **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A small blank space; a gap or vacancy; a hiatus.
   1. A language gap, which occurs when there is no direct translation in the target language for a lexical term found in the source language.
