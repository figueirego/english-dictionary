### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Indicating duty or obligation.

   **Usage:** auxiliary; dialectal; formal

   **Example:** I ought to vote in the coming election.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Indicating advisability or prudence.

   **Usage:** auxiliary; dialectal; formal

   **Example:** You ought to always stand back from the edge of the platform.

   **Example:** Do you think we ought to leave now?

   **Source synonyms:** should

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. Indicating desirability.

   **Usage:** auxiliary; dialectal; formal

   **Example:** He ought to read the book; it was very good.

   **Source synonyms:** should

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. Indicating likelihood or probability.

   **Usage:** auxiliary; dialectal; formal

   **Example:** We ought to arrive by noon if we take the motorway, shouldn't we?
