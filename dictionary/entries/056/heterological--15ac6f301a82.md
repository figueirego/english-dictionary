## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of a word, not describing itself.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The words ultrashort and onomatopoeia are heterological&#58; ultrashort might be called short but is not an ultrashort word, and onomatopoeia is not an onomatopoeic word.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** grammar; human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

   **Source antonyms:** autological

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Not true of itself.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; philosophy; sciences

   **Source antonyms:** autological

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Not of shared ancestral origin.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; tissues; or body parts; tissues; or body parts

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences

   **Source context (verbatim):** of biomolecules

   **Source synonyms:** heterologous

   **Source antonyms:** homologous; homological

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

   **Usage:** not-comparable
