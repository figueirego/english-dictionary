### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An early form of biometric identification used on criminals, usually by the French police, making use of distinguishing anthropometric measurements, such as head size, arm span and scars.

   **Usage:** historical; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** broadly; uncountable
