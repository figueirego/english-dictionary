## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The poisonous gas, used domestically for cooking, etc. and formerly for lighting, prepared by heating coal with steam (gasification); it is a mixture of hydrogen and carbon monoxide; sometimes synonymous with synthesis gas or town gas.

   **Usage:** uncountable
