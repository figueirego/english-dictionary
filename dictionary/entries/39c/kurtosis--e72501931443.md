### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A measure of "heaviness of the tails" of a probability distribution, defined as the fourth cumulant divided by the square of the variance of the probability distribution.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences; statistics

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Excess kurtosis&#58; the difference between a given distribution's kurtosis and the kurtosis of a normal distribution.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences; statistics
