### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To discipline (one's body, appetites etc.) by suppressing desires; to practise abstinence on.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Some people seek sainthood by mortifying the body.

   **Source synonyms:** macerate

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To injure the dignity of; to embarrass; to humiliate.

   **Usage:** transitive; usually

   **Example:** I was so mortified I could have died right there; instead I fainted, but I swore I'd never let that happen to me again.

   **Source context (verbatim):** used passively

   **Source synonyms:** demean; humiliate; shame

   **Source antonyms:** dignify; honor
