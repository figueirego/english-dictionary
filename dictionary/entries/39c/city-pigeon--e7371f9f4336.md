### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A feral pigeon living in an urban environment (Columba livia forma urbana).

   **Example:** Despite their lowly status, all city pigeons come from domestic pigeons, which were once popular as both pets and as messengers.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
