### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Wearing crocs and flipping the heel strap from the frontward position to the back, securing it around the ankle for a snug, supportive fit; the alternative to "relaxed mode" where the strap rests on the shoe's top.

   **Usage:** Internet; humorous; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A feature that sharpens a car's performance by making it more responsive, primarily by adjusting the throttle, transmission, and sometimes stiffening the suspension and steering, giving a sportier, more engaged driving feel for spirited driving.

   **Usage:** uncountable
