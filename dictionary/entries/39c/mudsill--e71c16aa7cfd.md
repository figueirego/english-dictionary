### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The lowest sill of a structure, usually placed in or on the ground.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A particularly low or dirty place/state; the nadir of something (see rock bottom)

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** The Pre-Historic Era was the mudsill of human development.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A person of low status or humble provenance.

   **Usage:** Southern-US; dated
