### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To successfully convince (someone) to agree to, accept, or do something, usually through reasoning and verbal influence.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** That salesman was able to persuade me into buying this bottle of lotion.

   **Source synonyms:** convince; draw; coax; wheedle

   **Source antonyms:** deter; dissuade

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To convince of by argument, or by reasons offered or suggested from reflection, etc.; to cause to believe (something).

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To urge, plead; to try to convince (someone to do something).

   **Usage:** archaic; regional; transitive
