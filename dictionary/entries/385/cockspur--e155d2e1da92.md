## Noun

### Forms

- cockspurs — plural
- cock's-spur — alternative; obsolete

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A blade for tying to the foot of a gamecock.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source synonyms:** gaff

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Barnyard grass (Echinochloa crus-galli).

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** barnyard millet; cockspur grass; common barnyard grass; Japanese millet; water grass

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Cockspur hawthorn (Crataegus crus-galli).

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source synonyms:** cockspur thorn

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Richard grass (Dactylis glomerata), a widely grown tall stout hay and pasture grass of Eurasia.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Pullback (Pisonia aculeata), a small West Indian tree.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Any member of species, formerly all of the genus Acacia, of several spiny trees, such as Vachellia cookii (cockspur acacia).

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Burgrass (Cenchrus echinatus).

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Maltese star thistle (Centaurea melitensis), an annual European weed, naturalized in North and South America.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. Any member of the species Casearia aculeata of shrubs and trees, of dry biomes of the tropical Americas.

   **Usage:** countable
