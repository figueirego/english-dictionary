### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Referring to a perspective or view from the context of a fictional world, in contrast to a perspective from the real world.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Referring to Lord Voldemort as the most feared person in the world is an in-universe classification in the Harry Potter series.

   **Source synonyms:** intradiegetic; diegetic; Watsonian

   **Source antonyms:** out-of-universe; extradiegetic; Doylist
