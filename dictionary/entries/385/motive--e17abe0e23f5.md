### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An idea or communication that makes one want to act, especially from spiritual sources; a divine prompting.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An incentive to act in a particular way; a reason or emotion that makes one want to do something; anything that prompts a choice of action.

   **Source synonyms:** motivation; sake

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Something which causes someone to want to commit a crime; a reason for criminal behaviour.

   **Example:** What would his motive be for burning down the cottage?

   **Example:** No-one could understand why she had hidden the shovel; her motives were obscure at best.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A limb or other bodily organ that can move.

   **Usage:** obsolete; rare

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Alternative form of motif.

   **Usage:** alt-of; alternative; fine arts

   **Example:** If you listen carefully, you can hear the flutes mimicking the cello motive.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** architecture; entertainment; lifestyle; music

   **Alternative form of:** [motif](../1f4/motif--7d0c43beb087.md)

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A party, gathering, or get-together.

   **Usage:** UK

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. One's plans for the day or night.

   **Usage:** UK

   **Example:** What's the motive for tonight, lads?
