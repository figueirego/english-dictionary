### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An expression of remorse or regret for having said or done something that harmed another&#58; an instance of apologizing (saying that one is sorry).

   **Example:** What he said really hurt my feelings, but his apology sounded so sincere that I couldn't help but forgive him.

   **Example:** The CEO made a public apology for the scandal, and promised full cooperation with the authorities.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A formal justification, defence.

   **Example:** the Apology of Socrates

   **Source synonyms:** apologia

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Anything provided as a substitute; a makeshift.

   **Example:** a poor apology for a hotel room
