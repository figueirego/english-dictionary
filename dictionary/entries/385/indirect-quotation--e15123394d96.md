## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An utterance in which one person states what someone else said, but framed as an object within a larger clause without quotation marks (when in written form) and sometimes, but not necessarily, repeating their exact words verbatim.

   **Example:** She relayed his words as an indirect quotation&#58; she said, "He told us that he wants stronger action to be taken."

   **Example:** He told us that he wants stronger action to be taken; there are no quote marks in this sentence because it contains an indirect quotation.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>
