1. Denoting the middle part.

   **Usage:** morpheme

   **Example:** He's in his mid-thirties — meaning he is roughly around the age of 33-37, as opposed to one's early thirties (aged roughly 30–33) and one's late thirties (aged roughly around 37-39).
