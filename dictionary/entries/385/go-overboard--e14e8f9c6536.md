### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To fall out of a vessel.

   **Example:** Attach the buoys so they don't go overboard.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To go too far; to exceed reasonable bounds.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** You can decorate the new room, but don't go overboard with surreal paintings.

   **Source synonyms:** get carried away; gild the lily; overegg; over-egg the pudding; overdo; take (something) too far
