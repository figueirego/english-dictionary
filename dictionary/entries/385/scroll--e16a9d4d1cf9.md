### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To change one's view of data on a computer's display by moving in gradual increments, typically using an input device or touch screen.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** She scrolled the offending image out of view.

   **Example:** She kept scrolling until she found the desired paragraph.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; graphical-user-interface; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To move in or out of view horizontally or vertically.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The rising credits slowly scrolled off the screen.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To continuously and aimlessly consume content on social media, especially on websites or apps with a scrolling feed, such as Instagram, TikTok, or X.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To flood a chat system with numerous lines of text, causing legitimate messages to scroll out of view before they can be read.

   **Usage:** Internet; dated; intransitive

   **Example:** Hey, stop scrolling!

**Source sense R2.S5**

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive
