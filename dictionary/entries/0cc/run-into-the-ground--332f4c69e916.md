### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To mismanage to the point of ruin.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; informal; transitive

   **Example:** Although he has an MBA from Harvard, he still ran the company into the ground.

   **Example:** Even though that was a good joke, you really did run it into the ground.

   **Source synonyms:** drive into the ground

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To wear out, especially through excessive use.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; informal; transitive

   **Example:** I figure this car has a few more years left in it and I intend to run it into the ground before purchasing another.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To discuss ad nauseam.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; informal; transitive

   **Example:** You’ve run the upcoming election into the ground and I simply won’t listen to another word about it.
