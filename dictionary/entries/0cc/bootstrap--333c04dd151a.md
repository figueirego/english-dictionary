### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A loop (leather or other material) sewn at the side or top rear of a boot to help in pulling the boot on.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** He used his business experience as a bootstrap to win voters.
