### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having deteriorated, degraded or fallen from normal, coherent, balanced and desirable to undesirable and typically abnormal.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of qualities

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Having lost good or desirable qualities; hence also having bad character or habits, base, immoral, corrupt.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a person or system

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Having lost functionality in general.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Having multiple domain elements correspond to one element of the range.

   **Example:** The genetic code is degenerate because a single amino acid can be coded by one of several codons.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of an encoding or function

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Qualitatively different, usually simpler, than typical objects of its class.

   **Example:** A degenerate circle, having radius zero, consists of a single point.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Having multiple different (linearly independent) eigenvectors.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

   **Source context (verbatim):** of an eigenvalue
