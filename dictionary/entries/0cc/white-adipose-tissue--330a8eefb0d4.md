### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One of the two types of adipose tissue present in mammals, providing heat insulation, mechanical cushion, and a source of energy, and comprising as much as 20% of the body weight in men and 25% of the body weight in women.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
