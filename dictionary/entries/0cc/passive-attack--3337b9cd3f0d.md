## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An attack in which the cryptanalyst cannot interact with any of the parties involved, attempting to break the system solely based upon observed data such as ciphertext.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; cryptography; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A damaging effect that is caused by an object or character when another character interacts with it.

   **Example:** The dungeon fern has a passive attack that releases spores.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** games; gaming

   **Source synonyms:** passive
