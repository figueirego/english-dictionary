## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A cyclone within the tropics that has a certain range of wind speeds, typically having its thunderclouds organized in a spiral shape with wind speeds on the surface between about 30 and 75 miles (60–120 km) per hour.

   **Example:** Janice was a tropical depression 10 days ago, a tropical storm 5 days ago, and a hurricane yesterday and today.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any tropical cyclone.

   **Usage:** broadly
