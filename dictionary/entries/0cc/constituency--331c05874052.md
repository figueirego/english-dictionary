### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A district represented by one or more elected officials.

   **Example:** John was elected to parliament from the Bedford constituency.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; politics

   **Source synonyms:** division (Australia); electoral district; electorate (New-Zealand); precinct; riding (Canada)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The voters within an electoral district.

   **Usage:** collective

   **Source synonyms:** electorate

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The residents of an electoral district.

   **Usage:** collective

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The voters of a candidate.

   **Usage:** collective

   **Example:** people outside his usual constituency of young married couples

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An interest group or fan base.

   **Usage:** collective
