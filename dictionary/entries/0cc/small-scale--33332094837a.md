### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Concerning a lower order of magnitude rather than a higher one.

   **Example:** small-scale aspects of fluid dynamics

   **Source synonyms:** microlevel; micro

   **Source antonyms:** large-scale; macrolevel; macro

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Having a modest scope or extent.

   **Example:** small-scale business operations

   **Source antonyms:** large-scale

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Drawn or constructed at a small size (at low scale), without much detail.

   **Example:** a small-scale inset orienting the viewer, implying "you are here"

   **Source antonyms:** large-scale
