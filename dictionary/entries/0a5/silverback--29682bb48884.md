### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A mature male gorilla leading a troop, so named from the silver streaking on its back.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; mammalogy; natural-sciences; zoology

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A dominant or powerful older human male.

   **Usage:** broadly; informal

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of various ferns of the genus Pityrogramma.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**
