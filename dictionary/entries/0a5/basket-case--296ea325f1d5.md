## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Someone (especially a soldier) who has lost all four limbs.

   **Usage:** colloquial; historical; offensive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An institution or country in a bad condition or difficult situation (economically, financially or otherwise).

   **Usage:** colloquial

   **Example:** This country is a financial basket case, a country so broke that it should be a perfect warning to lenders.

   **Example:** Some countries are breadbaskets, others basket cases.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. One made powerless or ineffective, as by nerves, panic, stress or exhaustion; someone considered mentally unstable.

   **Usage:** colloquial; potentially offensive

   **Example:** She was a complete basket case the morning of her wedding.

   **Source synonyms:** emotional cripple

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An item in a disassembled state that is brought to be repaired.

   **Usage:** colloquial
