## Adjective · Record 1

### Forms

- more Northern — comparative
- most Northern — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Characteristic of the North of England (the North) (usually capitalised).

   **Usage:** UK

   **Example:** Les Dawson was a famous Northern comedian.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Characteristic of the northern part of the United States (the North) (usually capitalised).

   **Usage:** US

   **Example:** She's a Northern girl, so she had to ask what hush puppies and mudbugs are. We were happy to serve her the answer on a platter!

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r3-s1"></a>

**Source sense R3.S1**

1. A steam locomotive of the 4-8-4 wheel arrangement.

   **Example:** It takes something big, like a Northern, to pull a train of this size.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** rail-transport; railways; transport

   **Source synonyms:** Northern Pacific

<a id="r3-s2"></a>

   **Usage:** Canada

   **Example:** I decided to kick back and lose myself in a good Northern for a while.
