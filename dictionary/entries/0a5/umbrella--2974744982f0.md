### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A cloth-covered frame used for protection against rain or sun.

   **Example:** Quick, grab that umbrella before you get rained on!

   **Source synonyms:** brelly

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Anything that provides similar protection.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** The fighters provide a defensive air umbrella over the battle group.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Anything that provides similar overarching coverage of a range of concepts, purposes, groups, etc.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** The test facility was established under the umbrella of the company's quality program.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A strategy in which five players in the offensive zone form an umbrella-shaped formation around the opposition's goal net.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; ice-hockey; lifestyle; skating; sports

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The main body of a jellyfish, excluding the tentacles.

   **Example:** Jellyfish are composed of more than 90% water and most of their umbrella mass is made up of gelatinous material.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. An umbrella-shaped reflector with a white or silvery inner surface, used to diffuse a nearby light.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** arts; broadcasting; hobbies; lifestyle; media; photography; television
