### Pronunciation

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The quality of being resonant.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** resound

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A resonant sound, echo, or reverberation, such as that produced by blowing over the top of a bottle.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The sound produced by a hollow body part such as the chest cavity upon auscultation, especially that produced while the patient is speaking.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Something that evokes an association, or a strong emotion; something that strikes a chord.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

   **Example:** emotional resonance

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The increase in the amplitude of an oscillation of a system under the influence of a periodic force whose frequency is close to that of the system's natural frequency.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A short-lived subatomic particle or state of atomic excitation that results from the collision of atomic particles.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; nuclear physics

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. An increase in the strength or duration of a musical tone produced by sympathetic vibration.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. The property of a compound that can be visualized as having two structures differing only in the distribution of electrons.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

   **Source synonyms:** mesomerism

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. An influence of the gravitational forces of one orbiting object on the orbit of another, causing periodic perturbations.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astronomy; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. The condition where the inductive and capacitive reactances have equal magnitude.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; electrical-engineering; electricity; electromagnetism; electronics; energy; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. A quality of human relationship with the world.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; sciences; social-science; sociology
