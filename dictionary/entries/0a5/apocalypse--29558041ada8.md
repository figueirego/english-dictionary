## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /əˈpɒkəlɪps/ — source labels: UK
- IPA: /əˈpɑkəlɪps/ — source labels: US

### Forms

- apocalypses — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A revealing, especially a prophecy of, or the unfolding of, supernatural events.

   **Example:** The early development of Perl 6 was punctuated by a series of apocalypses by Larry Wall.

   **Source synonyms:** revelation

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A huge disaster; a cataclysmic event; destruction or ruin of large scope and scale.

   **Example:** A nuclear apocalypse would have been possible if tensions went out of control during the Cuban Missile Crisis.

   **Source synonyms:** cataclysm; catastrophe; holocaust; armageddon; doomsday; end times; eschaton; judgement day; judgment day

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The unveiling of events prophesied in the Revelation; the second coming and the end of life on Earth; global destruction.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity

   **Source synonyms:** armageddon; doomsday; end times; eschaton; Ragnarok

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The Book of Revelation.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity
