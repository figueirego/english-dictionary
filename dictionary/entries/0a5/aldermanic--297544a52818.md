### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of or pertaining to an alderman (“member of a municipal legislative body in a city or town”) or group of aldermen.

   **Source synonyms:** aldermanical; aldermanlike; aldermanly

   **Source antonyms:** nonaldermanic

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Having the (supposed) lifestyle or qualities of an alderman.

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Having the (supposed) lifestyle or qualities of an alderman.
