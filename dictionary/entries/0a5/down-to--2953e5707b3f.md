## Preposition

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see down, to.

   **Example:** I had to walk down to the ground floor.

   **Example:** Go down to the shops.

   **Example:** Her hair came down to her waist.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Ready or willing to (do something specified).

   **Example:** Are you down to go to the cinema?

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. With no one/nothing remaining but.

   **Example:** I'm down to my last few sleeping pills.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Within the responsibility or influence of.

   **Example:** What type of person you become is down to you.

   **Example:** So I guess the cooking is now down to me.

   **Source synonyms:** up to

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Due to; because of.

   **Example:** The failure was down to total neglect.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Including even the smallest parts.

   **Example:** We checked everything down to the last/smallest detail.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Until, up to.
