## Preposition

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Based on what is said or stated by; on the authority of; by the order of.

   **Example:** According to the directions, the glue takes 24 hours to dry.

   **Example:** According to Captain Smith, we are not to spend more than 20 minutes on this task.

   **Source synonyms:** as per; per; following

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In a manner conforming or corresponding to; in proportion to; in accordance with.

   **Example:** You might expect him to balk, according to his temperament.

   **Source synonyms:** as per; per; following; as to; on the basis of

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Depending on.

   **Example:** They might come or not, according to their whim.

   **Example:** The young’s chances of getting a job vary according to whether they live in the Northern or the Southern Europe..

   **Source synonyms:** with
