### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A man — typically urban, heterosexual, and affluent — who is concerned with personal appearance, such as personal grooming, fashion, and aesthetics in general.

   **Usage:** dated

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** dated
