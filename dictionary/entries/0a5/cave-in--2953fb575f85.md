## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

- cave-ins — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of something collapsing or caving in.

   **Example:** We were warned away from the rickety old mine because of the danger of cave-ins.

   **Source synonyms:** breakage; collapse; rockfall

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The location where something has caved in.

   **Example:** We couldn't get past the cave-in blocking the tunnel.

   **Source synonyms:** blockage; collapse; rockfall

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The act of relenting.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** It was a cave-in, but I let my child have a candy to shut her up.

   **Source synonyms:** acquiescence; consent; sellout
