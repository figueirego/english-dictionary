## Adverb · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Truthfully; honestly.

   **Usage:** informal; not-comparable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Unambiguously; bluntly; plainly and directly.

   **Usage:** informal; not-comparable

   **Example:** She told me straight up that she didn't want to go, then surprised me by coming.

   **Example:** She straight up hates him.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To the utmost degree; really.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; slang

   **Example:** Wow, this food is straight up delicious.

   **Source context (verbatim):** modifying an adjective

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Of an alcoholic drink, chilled (stirred or shaken over ice) and strained before being served in a cocktail glass, with no ice.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Gimme a margarita, straight up.

   **Source synonyms:** up; straight
