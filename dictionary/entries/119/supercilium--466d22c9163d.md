<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

   1. The region of the eyebrows.

      **Usage:** rare

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; medicine; sciences

      **Usage:** rare

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; medicine; sciences

      **Usage:** rare
