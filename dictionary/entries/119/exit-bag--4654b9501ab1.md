## Noun

### Forms

- exit bags — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A bag designed to be sealed around a person’s head in order to commit suicide, with a tube or orifice for the introduction of an inert gas.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any bag used to commit suicide by suffocation.

   **Usage:** broadly

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Child-resistant packaging for cannabis products, required by law in many U.S. states for customers to carry their merchandise out of a dispensary.
