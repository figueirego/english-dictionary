### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. As an auxiliary verb&#58;
   1. Used with past participles of verbs to form the passive voice.

      **Usage:** auxiliary

      **Example:** The dog was saved by the boy.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. As an auxiliary verb&#58;
   1. Used with present participles of verbs to form the continuous aspect.

      **Example:** The woman is walking.

      **Example:** I shall be writing to you soon.

      **Example:** We liked to chat while we were eating.

      **Example:** I can't always be catering to your whimsies.

      **Example:** I was being deadly serious when I made that suggestion.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. As an auxiliary verb&#58;
   1. Used with to-infinitives of verbs to express intent, obligation, appropriateness, or relative future occurrence.

      **Example:** I am to leave tomorrow.

      **Example:** They are to stay here until I return.

      **Example:** I'd been to've been ambassador, but fell seriously ill.

      **Example:** How were they to know the whole exercise was a ruse?

      **Example:** They were to have been married overseas but COVID forced a change of plans.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. As an auxiliary verb&#58;
   1. Used with past participles of certain intransitive verbs to form the perfect aspect.

      **Example:** He is finished.

      **Example:** He is gone.

      **Example:** He is come.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. As an auxiliary verb&#58;
   1. To tend to do, often do; marks the habitual aspect.

      **Source context (verbatim):** not conjugated

**Source sense R1.S6**
