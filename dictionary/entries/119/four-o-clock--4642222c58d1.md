## Noun

### Forms

- four-o'clock — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The start of the fifth hour of the day; 4&#58;00 a.m. (04&#58;00).

   **Usage:** uncountable; In both the 12-hour clock and the 24-hour clock

   **Example:** The breakfast buffet opens at four o'clock (= "The breakfast buffet opens at 4&#58;00 a.m.").

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The start of the seventeenth hour of the day; 4&#58;00 p.m. (16&#58;00).

   **Usage:** uncountable; Only in the 12-hour clock

   **Example:** The next train comes at four o'clock (= "The next train comes at 16&#58;00").

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A position to the right and slightly behind (horizontal clock orientation) (from the location of the 4 mark on a clock face)

   **Usage:** informal; uncountable

   **Example:** We've got a bogey at four o'clock.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any of several plants, of the genus Mirabilis, whose funnel-shaped flowers open in late afternoon.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The friarbirds (Philemon spp.); so called from its cry, which resembles these words.

   **Usage:** uncountable
