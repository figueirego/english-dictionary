### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act, process, or result of reducing.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The amount or rate by which something is reduced, e.g. in price.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** A 5% reduction in robberies

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A reaction in which electrons are gained and valence is reduced; often by the removal of oxygen or the addition of hydrogen.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The process of rapidly boiling a sauce to concentrate it.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** cooking; food; lifestyle

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The rewriting of an expression into a simpler form.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A transformation of one problem into another problem, such as mapping reduction or polynomial-time reduction.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computer; computing; computing-theory; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; science; sciences

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. An arrangement for a far smaller number of parties, e.g. a keyboard solo based on a full opera.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A philosophical procedure intended to reveal the objects of consciousness as pure phenomena. (See phenomenological reduction.)

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; phenomenology; philosophy; sciences

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A medical procedure to restore a fracture or dislocation to the correct alignment, usually with a closed approach but sometimes with an open approach (surgery).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** closed reduction

   **Example:** open reduction and internal fixation

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** taxis

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. A reduced price of something by a fraction or decimal.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; paying

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. The ratio of a material's change in thickness compared to its thickness prior to forging and/or rolling.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** arts; crafts; engineering; hobbies; lifestyle; metallurgy; metalworking; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s12"></a>

**Source sense R1.S12**

12. A religious settlement created during a mission by Spanish or Portuguese colonists with the intent of evangelizing Christianity to the local population.

   **Usage:** countable; historical; uncountable

   **Example:** Spanish reductions in Mexico were common during the 18th century.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Catholicism; Christianity; Roman-Catholicism
