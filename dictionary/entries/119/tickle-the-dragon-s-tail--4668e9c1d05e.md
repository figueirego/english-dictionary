### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To do something that has a risk of going catastrophically wrong.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** Climbing so close to an erupting volcano was tickling the dragon's tail.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To annoy an irritable person.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** Don't tell him the headline's misprinted; you don't want to tickle the dragon's tail.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To bring two subcritical masses close together in order to find the edge of criticality.

   **Usage:** nuclear physics

   **Example:** The laboratory head tickles the dragon's tail whenever we have guests as a show of machismo.
