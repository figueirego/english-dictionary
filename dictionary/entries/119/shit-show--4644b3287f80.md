### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A situation characterized by chaos, confusion, and incompetence.

   **Usage:** slang; vulgar

   **Example:** The government's enforcement crackdown derailed, morphing from a firm hand into a shit show.

   **Source synonyms:** crapshow; shitstorm; clown show; clusterfuck; debacle; fiasco; hot mess; omnishambles; perfect storm

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A messy situation, especially involving drunkenness and partying.

   **Usage:** slang; vulgar

   **Example:** We won't be going to those parties anymore if they're not going to be anything better than a shit show full of shitfaced debauchery.
