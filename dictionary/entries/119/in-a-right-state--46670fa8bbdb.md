### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. in a mess or in a state of chaos; very unkempt, in poor condition.

   **Usage:** UK; idiomatic

   **Example:** The COVID-19 pandemic had the world in a right state.

   **Example:** At the end of a long day at work, I’m usually in a right state.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. in a state of panic or confusion.

   **Usage:** UK; idiomatic

   **Example:** The dancers were in a right state as their track wouldn’t play minutes before their performance.

   **Example:** Trigonometry lectures always had me in a right state!
