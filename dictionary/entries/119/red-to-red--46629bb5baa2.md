## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Describing the condition in which two sea-going ships travelling in opposite directions pass each other on their port sides, making their red navigation lights face each other.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Describing the connection between two batteries' positive terminals, for instance when jump-starting a car.

   **Usage:** not-comparable
