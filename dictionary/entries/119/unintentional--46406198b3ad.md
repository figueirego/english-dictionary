### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Not intended or deliberate; without intention or with not enough intention.
   1. Inadvertent; unwitting.

      **Example:** His omission of her name from the acknowledgments section was unintentional; it was fixed for the second print run.

      **Source synonyms:** nonintentional; unintended; nonintentional; incidental; accidental; accidentary (obsolete); adventitious; by accident; by the by; casual; causeless; chanceable (obsolete); fortuitous; incidental; lippening (UK; dialectal); occasional; random; serendipitous; unforeseen; unintended; unintentional; unplanned

      **Source antonyms:** intentional; intentional; intended

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Not intended or deliberate; without intention or with not enough intention.
   1. Unmindful.

      **Example:** She kindly remonstrated us for allowing our yoga to be so unintentional; she reminded us that it is supposed to be more than mere absent-minded stretching.

      **Source synonyms:** nonintentional

      **Source antonyms:** intentional
