### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Matching each member of one set with exactly one member of another set.

   **Example:** There is a one-to-one relationship between days with large cash shortages and his workdays.

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Involving direct communication between two people.
