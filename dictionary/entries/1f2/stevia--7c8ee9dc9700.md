### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of the sweet herbs of genus Stevia, native to tropical and subtropical regions of South America and western North America.

   **Source synonyms:** sweetleaf (US)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A sweetener, many times sweeter than an equal amount of sugar, extracted from Stevia rebaudiana, that can be substituted for sugar for some purposes.
