### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Over a great length or distance.

   **Example:** He is a long-distance runner.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Referring to a nonlocal telephone call; a toll call.

   **Example:** The hotel does not allow for long-distance phone calls.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Referring to a romantic relationship&#58; between two people with a considerable distance between them.

   **Example:** They are in a long-distance relationship.
