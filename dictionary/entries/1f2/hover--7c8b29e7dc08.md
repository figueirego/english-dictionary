### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To keep (something, such as an aircraft) in a stationary state in the air.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of a bird&#58; to shelter (chicks) under its body and wings; (by extension) of a thing&#58; to cover or surround (something).

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of a bird or insect&#58; to flap (its wings) so it can remain stationary in the air.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To remain stationary or float in the air.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The hummingbird hovered by the plant.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Sometimes followed by over&#58; to hang around or linger in a place, especially in an uncertain manner.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

   **Example:** His pen hovered above the paper.

   **Example:** The strange man hovered outside the gents’ toilet.

   **Example:** The visitors were hovering at the door, seemingly unwilling to enter.

   **Example:** Some helicopter parents weren’t so much dropping off their kids as hovering over them until the event started.

   **Source synonyms:** wasp around

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To be indecisive or uncertain; to vacillate, to waver.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

   **Example:** Filling in the voting form, I hovered between Labour and Liberal Democrat.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Chiefly followed by over&#58; to use a mouse or other device to place a cursor over something on a screen such as a hyperlink or icon without clicking, so as to produce a result (such as the appearance of a tooltip).

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** A tooltip appears when you hover over this link.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To travel in a hovercraft as it moves above a water surface.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport
