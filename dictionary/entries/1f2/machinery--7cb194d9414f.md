### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The machines constituting a production apparatus, in a plant etc., collectively.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The working parts of a machine as a group.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The collective parts of something which allow it to function.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** All of the machinery of the law was brought to bear on the investigation.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The literary devices used in a work, notably for dramatic effect.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable
