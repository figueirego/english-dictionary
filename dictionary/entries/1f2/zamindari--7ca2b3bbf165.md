### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In British India, a system used to collect revenues from the ryots (cultivators of agricultural land) indirectly through the zamindars, as opposed to ryotwari, where revenues were collected directly.

   **Usage:** historical; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The office or jurisdiction of a zamindar.

   **Usage:** countable; historical

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The land possessed by a zamindar.

   **Usage:** countable; historical; uncountable
