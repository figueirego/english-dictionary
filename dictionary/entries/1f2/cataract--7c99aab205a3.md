### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A (large) waterfall, specifically one flowing over the edge of a cliff.

   **Example:** The cataracts on the Nile helped to compartment Upper Egypt.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A flood of water; specifically, steep rapids in a river.

   **Usage:** broadly

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An overwhelming downpour or rush; a flood.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** His cataract of eloquence

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A type of governor used in single-acting steam engines, where a flow of water through an opening regulates the stroke.

   **Usage:** historical

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; mechanical-engineering; mechanics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Synonym of waterspout (“a whirlwind that forms over water”).

   **Usage:** also; figuratively; obsolete

   **Source synonyms:** waterspout (synonym; synonym-of)
