### Pronunciation

### Forms

- chist — alternative; obsolete

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A box, now usually a large strong box with a secure convex lid.

   **Example:** The clothes are kept in a chest.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A coffin.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The place in which public money is kept; a treasury.

   **Example:** You can take the money from the chest.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A chest of drawers.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The portion of the human body from the base of the neck to the top of the abdomen; the homologous area in some other animals.

   **Example:** She had a sudden pain in her chest.

   **Example:** An anteroposterior radiograph found opacities throughout her chest.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The front (anterior) surface of this portion of the torso.

   **Example:** He has a tattoo on his chest, and another on his upper back.

   **Example:** The wild gorilla was beating its chest.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A female human's breasts.

   **Usage:** euphemistic

   **Example:** He avoided being seen gazing at her chest, although he dearly longed to stare.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A hit or blow made with one's chest.

   **Example:** She scored with a chest into the goal.

**Source synonyms:** breast — the thorax; trunk — box
