### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An intermediate floor or storey in between the main floors of a building; specifically, one that is directly above the ground floor which does not extend over the whole floorspace of the building, and so resembles a large balcony overlooking the ground floor; an entresol.

   **Example:** On our way to the top floor, we stopped at the mezzanine.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** architecture

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An apartment, room, etc., on such an intermediate floor.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** architecture

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The lowest balcony in an auditorium, cinema, theatre, etc.; the dress circle.

   **Usage:** Canada; US

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** architecture

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Additional flooring laid over a floor to bring it up to some height or level.

   **Usage:** obsolete

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** architecture

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. In full mezzanine window&#58; a small window at the height of a mezzanine floor (sense 1.1) or an attic, used to light these floors.

   **Usage:** obsolete

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** architecture

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A floor under the stage, from which contrivances such as traps are worked.

   **Usage:** obsolete

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; theater
