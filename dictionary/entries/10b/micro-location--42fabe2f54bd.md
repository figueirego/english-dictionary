## Noun

### Forms

- micro-locations — plural
- microlocation — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A location of something within a larger area or region, such as a part of a city or a section of a river.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A location specified on a microscopic scale, such as on a biochip or on a DNA molecule.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The process of electronically determining the location of something with a level of precision that locates it within a building or room.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A specific location detected or targeted by micro-location technology.

   **Usage:** countable
