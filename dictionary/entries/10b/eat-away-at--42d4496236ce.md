### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To erode.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The river is eating away at the river bank.

   **Example:** The battery acid ate away at the metal until a large hole appeared.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To consume or use up, erode or wear away gradually.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The expenses ate away at our profits.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To cause to feel uneasy, troubled, worried, guilty, or vexed; to bother about something.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Not confessing that I had stolen the money ate away at me until I could no longer sleep.

   **Source synonyms:** devour (literary)
