### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The reflexive case of they, the third-person plural personal pronoun. The group of people, animals, or objects previously mentioned, as the object of a verb or following a preposition (also used for emphasis).

   **Usage:** pronoun; reflexive; third-person

   **Example:** They’ve hurt themselves.

   **Example:** They fought among themselves.

   **Example:** They are going to try climbing Mount Everest themselves.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The reflexive case of they, the third-person singular personal pronoun. The single person previously mentioned, as the object of a verb or following a preposition (also used for emphasis).

   **Usage:** pronoun; reflexive; third-person

   **Example:** Would whoever stole my phone please make themselves known.

   **Example:** I don't want anyone to fight among themselves.

   **Example:** Everyone must do it themselves.
