### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of the 15 radioactive elements of the periodic table from actinium to lawrencium that are positioned under the lanthanides, to which they have similar chemistry.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; inorganic-chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of the 14 elements from actinium to nobelium (lawrencium is excluded by some authors as it is a d-block rather than an f-block element).

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; inorganic-chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of the 14 elements from thorium to lawrencium (actinium having been mistakenly excluded because it was thought to be a d-block element in the past).

   **Usage:** dated

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; inorganic-chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences
