### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A recidivist; one who backslides, especially in a religious sense; an apostate.

   **Example:** She married him thinking to change his ways, and for a while he got religion, but he was ever a backslider; she soon began finding bottles stashed about the house.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One who will not do his work or shoulder his responsibilities.
