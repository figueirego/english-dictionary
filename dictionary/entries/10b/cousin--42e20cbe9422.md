      **Usage:** specifically

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Chiefly with a qualifying word&#58; Any relation (especially a distant one) who is not a direct ancestor or descendant but part of a person's extended family; a kinsman or kinswoman.
   1. When used without a qualifying word&#58; the child of a person's parent's brother (that is, an uncle) or sister (an aunt); a cousin-german, a first cousin.

      **Usage:** specifically

      **Example:** Although we were cousins, we grew up like sisters.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A person of an ethnicity or nationality regarded as closely related to someone of another ethnicity or nationality.

   **Usage:** in-plural

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Used as a term of address for someone whom one is close to; also, (preceding a first name, sometimes capitalized as Cousin) a title for such a person.

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Used by a monarch to address another monarch, or a noble; specifically (UK) in commissions and writs by the Crown&#58; used in this way to address a viscount or another peer of higher rank.
