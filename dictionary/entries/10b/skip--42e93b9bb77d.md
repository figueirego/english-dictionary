   **Example:** She will skip from one end of the sidewalk to the other.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The rock will skip across the pond.

**Source sense R1.S4**

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I bet I can skip this rock to the other side of the pond.

   **Usage:** transitive
