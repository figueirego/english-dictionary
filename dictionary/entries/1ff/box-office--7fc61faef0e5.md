### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A place where tickets are sold in a theatre/theater or cinema.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** The movie tanked at the box office.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** broadcasting; entertainment; film; lifestyle; media; television; theater

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The total amount of money paid by people worldwide to watch a movie at cinemas/movie theaters.

   **Usage:** broadly; uncountable

   **Example:** box office receipts

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** broadcasting; film; media; television

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Quality of an entertainment or spectacle that makes it very popular with the public, or likely to be so.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** His performance last night was pure box office.

   **Example:** A show that makes great box office.
