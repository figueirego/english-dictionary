### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To take the sum of; to total.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Add up the prices and find out how much it will cost.

   **Example:** When they added up the vaccine doses from all known and anticipate sources, they didn't have enough.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a numerical amount

   **Source synonyms:** sum up; tally

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To accumulate; to amount to.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** If you can save even a couple of dollars per day, it will add up to a lot over a year.

   **Source synonyms:** accrue; mount up

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To make sense; to be reasonable or consistent.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** His story just doesn't add up. Why would he have been at the restaurant the day before the event?

   **Source synonyms:** compute; hang together; check out; make sense; track; figure
