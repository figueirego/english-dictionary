### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To improve, usually applied to technology, generally by complete replacement of one or more components.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** When you upgrade your standard of living, you become addicted to that standard of living.

   **Source antonyms:** downgrade

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To replace with something better.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I had to upgrade my anti-virus software to protect my computer from the newer threats.

   **Source antonyms:** downgrade

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To improve the equipment or furnishings of or services rendered to.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** They upgraded him to the latest model.

   **Example:** They were upgraded to first class.

   **Source antonyms:** downgrade

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To improve in condition or status.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** She upgraded to a more successful husband.

   **Source antonyms:** downgrade

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To replace a program with a later version of itself, a version having a higher version number or marketed under a more recent product name.
