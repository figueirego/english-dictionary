### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A therapeutic treatment administered, especially for chronic diseases of the circulatory system, at the city of Bad Nauheim in Germany, consisting of baths in the natural mineral waters of that region, which are charged with carbonic acid, and a graduated course of rest, physical exercise, massage, etc.
