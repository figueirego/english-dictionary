### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The angular distance north or south from a planet's equator, measured along the meridian of that particular point.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astronomy; geography; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An imaginary line (in the form of a circumference) around a planet running parallel to the planet's equator.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** snow showers in the northerly latitudes

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; natural-sciences

   **Source synonyms:** parallel

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The relative freedom from restrictions; scope to do something.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

   **Example:** His parents gave him a great deal of latitude.

   **Source synonyms:** leeway

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The angular distance of a heavenly body from the ecliptic.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astronomy; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The extent to which a light-sensitive material can be over- or underexposed and still achieve an acceptable result.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** arts; hobbies; lifestyle; photography

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Extent or scope; e.g. breadth, width or amplitude.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
