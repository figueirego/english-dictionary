### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Spacious and convenient; roomy and comfortable.

   **Example:** Our house is much more commodious than our old apartment.

   **Source synonyms:** spacious; capacious; ample; commonious

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Convenient, serviceable, suitable.

   **Usage:** archaic

   **Source synonyms:** becoming; fit; proper; handy; useful

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Advantageous, profitable, beneficial.

   **Usage:** archaic

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Of life or living, endowed with conveniences; comfortable; free from hardship.

   **Usage:** archaic

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Of a person, accommodating, obliging, helpful.

   **Usage:** obsolete
