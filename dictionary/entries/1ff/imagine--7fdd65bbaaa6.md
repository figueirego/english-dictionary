### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To form a mental image of something; to envision or create something in one's mind.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Try to imagine a pink elephant.

   **Example:** Imagine yourself in a packed airport lounge.

   **Example:** Imagine sitting on a tropical beach with a cool mimosa.

   **Source synonyms:** project; see; fancy

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To believe in something created by one's own mind, often something false.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** She imagined that the man wanted to kill her.

   **Example:** There's nothing there; you're just imagining things.

   **Source synonyms:** fancy

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To assume; to suppose.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I imagine that he will need to rest after such a long flight.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To conjecture; to guess.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I cannot even imagine what you are up to!

   **Example:** The board imagines the merger will increase profits by 25%

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To use one's imagination.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Imagine that we were siblings.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To contrive in purpose; to scheme; to devise.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Used to mock an idea by suggesting that it is ridiculous or ill thought through.

   **Usage:** Internet; sarcastic; transitive; rhetorical

   **Example:** Imagine thinking that would work.
