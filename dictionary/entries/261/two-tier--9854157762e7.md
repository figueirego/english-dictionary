## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having or consisting of two tiers.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Relating to the two-tier system, a payroll system in which one group of workers receives lower wages and/or benefits than another.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; labour

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Giving preferential treatment to one group over another.

   **Usage:** UK; derogatory; not-comparable

   **Example:** two-tier policing
