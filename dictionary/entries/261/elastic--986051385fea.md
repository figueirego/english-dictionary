### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Capable of stretching; particularly, capable of stretching so as to return to an original shape or size when force is released.

   **Example:** The rope is somewhat elastic, so expect it to give when you pull on it.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Made of elastic.

   **Example:** elastic band

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of clothing, elasticated.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Sensitive to changes in price.

   **Example:** Demand for entertainment is more elastic than demand for energy.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** economics; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. springy; bouncy; vivacious

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Pervasive, all-encompassing.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Able to return quickly to a former state or condition, after being depressed or overtaxed; having power to recover easily from shocks and trials.

   **Example:** elastic spirits; an elastic constitution
