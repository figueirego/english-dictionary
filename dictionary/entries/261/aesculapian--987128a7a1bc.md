### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of or relating to Aesculapius, the Greco-Roman god of medicine.

   **Usage:** literally

   **Example:** The Aesculapian sanctuary on Kos island was also a center of medical learning and practice

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Medical, concerning healing.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** Water therapy at famous springs is an Aesculapian method which recently came back in fashion, as when Bath reemerged from archaelogy to high-society spa
