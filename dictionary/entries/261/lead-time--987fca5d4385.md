## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The amount of time between the initiation of some process and its completion, e.g. the time required to manufacture or procure a product; the time required before something can be provided or delivered.

   **Usage:** countable; idiomatic; uncountable

   **Example:** It's an excellent device, but it has a 10-week lead time, so be sure to order it in advance.
