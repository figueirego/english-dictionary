## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Enlisted soldiers in a military organization, as would be arrayed in a grid pattern with ranks and files.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The members of an organization, community, etc. who are not in leadership positions.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; uncountable

   **Example:** The executives attend meetings in exotic locations while the rank and file stays at headquarters doing the bulk of the work.
