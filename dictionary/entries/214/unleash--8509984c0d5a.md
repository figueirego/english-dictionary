### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To free from a leash, or as from a leash.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He unleashed his dog in the park.

   **Source antonyms:** leash; leash up

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To let go; to release.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** He unleashed his fury.
