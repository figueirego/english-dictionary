## Noun

### Forms

- city blocks — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The smallest part of a town enclosed by streets.

   **Example:** The store is huge, occupying an entire city block.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The distance from one urban street to the next, or an approximately comparable distance.

   **Example:** In Lawrence, a mile is generally 12 city blocks east-west or 8 blocks north-south.

   **Example:** Go along that road for about a city block, and you should see an old barn.
