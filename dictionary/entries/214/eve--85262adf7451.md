### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The day or night before, usually used for holidays, such as Christmas Eve.

   **Example:** She died on the eve of her 100th birthday.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Evening, night.

   **Usage:** archaic; poetic

   **Example:** on a cold winter's eve.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The period of time when something is just about to happen or to be introduced

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** the eve of a scientific discovery

   **Source antonyms:** evening; winter
