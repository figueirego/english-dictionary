### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Causing itself to continue to exist.

   **Example:** The committee was self-perpetuating&#58; all it did was hold a meeting to decide when the next meeting was, and consume funds to do so.

   **Example:** It's a self-perpetuating error, now that we've made it, we can't fix it without breaking things that depend on it.
