### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To not feel good; to experience an unpleasant physical sensation.

   **Usage:** intransitive; literally

   **Example:** It feels really bad to get hit with a baseball bat.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To not feel good; to experience an unpleasant physical sensation.
   1. To feel ill or sick.

      **Usage:** intransitive; literally

      **Example:** After chemo, I felt bad.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To feel remorse or shame for one's actions.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** I feel bad about what I did. I won't do it again.

   **Example:** I felt bad that I was leaving my home again.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To feel pity for, to empathize with.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** With all the things he went through, I felt pretty bad for him.
