### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To destroy by fire.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To become extinguished due to lack of fuel.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The candle finally burned out.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To become nonfunctional (especially of lightbulbs or similar light-producing devices).

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To tire due to overwork; to overwork to one's limit.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** After six months of twelve-hour workdays, most people just burn out and quit.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To cause (someone) to tire due to overwork; to cause (someone) to overwork to one's limit.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To end one's shift at a job.

   **Usage:** intransitive; slang; uncommon

   **Example:** I start my shift at three in the afternoon, and get to burn out at midnight.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To have one's tires skid against the ground; to peel off, peel out.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** automotive; transport; vehicles

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To make (someone) unavailable for work involving exposure to ionizing radiation by employing (the person) in such work until the person's accumulated exposure reaches the maximum permitted for an administrative period, typically a year.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** The repairs on this nuclear reactor have burned out every welder in the province.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To use up too much energy when first bowled and to therefore not finish strongly.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** bowling; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a ball

   **Source synonyms:** burn up; roll out

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. To be destroyed by fire.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** An abandoned tank was burning out at the edge of the battlefield.
