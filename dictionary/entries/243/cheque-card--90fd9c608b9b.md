### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A small plastic card issued by a bank or similar which is a guarantee of payment of cheques written by the cardholder (up to a specified maximum amount).

   **Usage:** UK

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** banking; business

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A card issued by a bank or similar which behaves like a credit card but is in fact a type of debit card.

   **Usage:** South-Africa
