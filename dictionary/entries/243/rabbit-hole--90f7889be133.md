### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A bizarre world, where everyday rules do not apply.

   **Usage:** figuratively; usually

   **Source context (verbatim):** with an article

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A way into such a world.

   **Usage:** figuratively; usually

   **Example:** These mushrooms will take you down the rabbit hole, man.

   **Source context (verbatim):** with an article

   **Source synonyms:** looking-glass

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A time-consuming tangent or detour, often one from which it is difficult to extricate oneself.

   **Usage:** broadly; figuratively

   **Example:** I'm also a fan of a really obscure book series, but that's a rabbit hole that we won't get into.

   **Example:** While writing my paper, my research went down several rabbit holes that were only marginally related and wasted a lot of my time.

   **Source synonyms:** wild-goose chase; rabbit trail\[regiolect\]\]ic&gt;

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The initial clue that leads to an alternate reality game.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** games; gaming

   **Source synonyms:** trailhead

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see rabbit, hole. The entrance to a rabbit warren or burrow; the whole warren or burrow.
