### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A sheltered place where bees, usually honey bees (genus Apis), live.
   1. Such a home prepared by the bees themselves, in which some species of honey bees live and raise their young.

      **Source synonyms:** hive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A sheltered place where bees, usually honey bees (genus Apis), live.
   1. A man-made structure in which honey bees are kept so that humans can harvest their honey.

      **Source synonyms:** hive; apiary; hive; skep

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any place full of activity, or in which people are very busy.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Source synonyms:** hive of activity

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A women's hairstyle, popular in the 1960s, in which long hair is styled into a hive-shaped form on top of the head and usually held in place with lacquer.

   **Source synonyms:** B-52

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A particular style of hat.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A type of anti-personnel ammunition round containing flechettes, and characterised by the buzzing sound made as they fly through the air.
