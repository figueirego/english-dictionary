### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To give or supply; to make or create (implies trying or striving).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to put forth an effort

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To extend forward (a body part or something held).

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To advance, offer, propose (often verbally).

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To send (someone) out, remove (someone) from a place.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To emit, send out, give off (light, odour, etc.).

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To grow, shoot, bud, or germinate.

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. (of a ship) To leave (a port or haven).

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive
