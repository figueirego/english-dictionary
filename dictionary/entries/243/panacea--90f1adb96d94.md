### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A remedy believed to cure all disease and prolong life that was originally sought by alchemists; a cure-all.

   **Source synonyms:** heal-all

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A solution to all problems.

   **Example:** A monorail will be a panacea for our traffic woes.

   **Source synonyms:** chicken soup

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
