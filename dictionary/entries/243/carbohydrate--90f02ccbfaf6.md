### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A sugar, starch, or cellulose that is a food source of energy for an animal or plant.

   **Usage:** nutrition

   **Example:** These microbes are primarily responsible for breaking down cellulose and other carbohydrates into volatile fatty acids (VFAs).

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; organic-chemistry; physical-sciences

   **Source synonyms:** carb; saccharide

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any food rich in starch or other carbohydrates.

   **Usage:** broadly; colloquial; metonymically

   **Example:** I'm cutting down on carbohydrates like bread and pasta.
