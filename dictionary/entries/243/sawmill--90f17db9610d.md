### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A machine, building, or company used for cutting (milling) lumber or (rarely) other hard materials such as stone.

   **Example:** He brought his portable sawmill and turned the old beams into interesting flooring.

   **Example:** The old sawmill still has its waterwheel but they took the saw away years ago.

   **Example:** The sawmill sells lumber to carpenters and sawdust to gardeners.
