## Noun · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˌkævəˈlɪɚ/ — variety unspecified in the source labels
- IPA: /ˈkæ.vəˌlir/ — source labels: US

### Forms

- cavaliers — plural

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A military man serving on horse, (chiefly) early modern cavalry officers who had abandoned the heavy armor of medieval knights.

   **Usage:** historical

   **Source synonyms:** chevalier

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. A gallant&#58; a sprightly young dashing military man.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. A gentleman of the class of such officers.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. Alternative letter-case form of Cavalier, a royalist partisan of Charles I of England during the English Civil War, especially from the upper classes.

   **Usage:** alt-of; historical

   **Alternative form of:** [Cavalier](../1be/cavalier--6fadc20e9f71.md)

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. Someone with an uncircumcised penis.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Source antonyms:** roundhead

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**
