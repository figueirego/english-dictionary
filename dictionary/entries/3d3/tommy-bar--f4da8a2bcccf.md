### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A cylindrical iron or steel bar used as a lever.
   1. A short bar put through the hole in a socket spanner or box spanner to provide leverage.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; machining; mechanical-engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

      **Source synonyms:** tommy

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A cylindrical iron or steel bar used as a lever.
   1. A bar used to loosen or tighten some types of chucks and tool holders on machine tools such as lathes or milling machines.

      **Example:** A set of two tommy bars is the standard tooling for the spindle nose threads of some minilathes and minimills.
