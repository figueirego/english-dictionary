### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Synonym of baguette&#58; a long thin loaf of bread with a thick crust and often having large bubbles of air inside, popular in and associated with France.

   **Usage:** informal; uncountable; usually

   **Source synonyms:** freedom bread; baguette (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see French, bread&#58; any of many types of bread made in France or said to be made faithfully in the style thereof.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually
