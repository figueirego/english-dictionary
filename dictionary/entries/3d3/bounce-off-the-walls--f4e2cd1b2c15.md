### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To be overly active relative to the enclosed space in which one is.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Source synonyms:** bounce around; jump around; jump about

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To be brimful of agitation from excitement, indignation, or anxiety.

   **Usage:** broadly; figuratively

   **Example:** Acme Inc was our best customer, but they're hopping mad now. They're bouncing off the walls over there.

   **Example:** These kids need to get outside for recess. They're bouncing off the walls in here.
