## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

- mile-a-minutes — plural
- mile a minute — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of several species of fast-growing plants, some of which are extremely invasive outside their native range and undesirable.
   1. Asiatic tearthumb, kudzu of the North (Persicaria perfoliata), native to eastern Asia, introduced to northeastern US.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of several species of fast-growing plants, some of which are extremely invasive outside their native range and undesirable.
   1. Kudzu (Pueraria lobata; Pueraria montana var. lobata), native to eastern Asia, highly invasive elsewhere.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of several species of fast-growing plants, some of which are extremely invasive outside their native range and undesirable.
   1. mile-a-minute vine (Ipomoea cairica), native from tropical Africa to the Mediterranean, invasive elsewhere.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any of several species of fast-growing plants, some of which are extremely invasive outside their native range and undesirable.
   1. Mikania micrantha, native to the tropical and subtropical Americas.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Any of several species of fast-growing plants, some of which are extremely invasive outside their native range and undesirable.
   1. Russian vine (Fallopia baldschuanica), native to central Asia, widely naturalized.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Any of several species of fast-growing plants, some of which are extremely invasive outside their native range and undesirable.
   1. Okie bean (Dipogon lignosus), native to South Africa, introduced elsewhere.
