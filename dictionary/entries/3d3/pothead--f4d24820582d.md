### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who smokes cannabis frequently, or to excess.

   **Usage:** derogatory; slang

   **Example:** That pothead Shane has a nasty bong.

   **Source synonyms:** grasshopper; stoner; weedhead

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An electric motor's terminal connection or wiring box.

   **Usage:** Canada

   **Source synonyms:** pecker; T-box; peckerhead

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
