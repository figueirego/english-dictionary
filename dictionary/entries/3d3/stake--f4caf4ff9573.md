## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /steɪk/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Forms

- stakes — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A piece of wood or other material, usually long and slender, pointed at one end so as to be easily driven into the ground as a marker or a support or stay.

   **Example:** We have surveyor's stakes at all four corners of this field, to mark exactly its borders.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A piece of wood driven in the ground, placed in the middle of the court, that is used as the finishing point after scoring 12 hoops in croquet.

   **Usage:** croquet

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A stick or similar object (e.g., steel channel or angle stock) inserted upright in a lop, eye, or mortise, at the side or end of a cart, flat car, flatbed trailer, or the like, to prevent goods from falling off; often connected in a grid forming a stakebody.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The piece of timber to which a person condemned to death was affixed to be burned.

   **Usage:** with-definite-article

   **Example:** Thomas Cranmer was burnt at the stake.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A share or interest in a business or a given situation.

   **Example:** The owners let the managers eventually earn a stake in the business.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. That which is laid down as a wager; that which is staked or hazarded; a pledge.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A small anvil usually furnished with a tang to enter a hole in a bench top, as used by tinsmiths, blacksmiths, etc., for light work, punching hole in or cutting a work piece, or for specific forming techniques etc.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A territorial division comprising all the Mormons (typically several thousand) in a geographical area.

   **Usage:** Mormonism

## Verb · Record 2

### Pronunciation

### Forms

- staked — participle; past
- staked — past

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To fasten, support, defend, or delineate with stakes.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to stake vines or plants

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To pierce or wound with a stake.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To put at risk upon success in competition, or upon a future contingency.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** bet; hazard; wager

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To provide (another) with money in order to engage in an activity as betting or a business venture.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** John went broke, so to keep him playing, Jill had to stake him.

   **Example:** His family staked him $10,000 to get his business started.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To deposit and risk a considerable amount of cryptocurrency in order to participate in the proof of stake process of verification.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; cryptocurrencies; cryptocurrency; finance

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To stretch leather skin over a piece of iron held knee-height, in preparation to use the skin in the creation of gloves.

   **Usage:** obsolete
