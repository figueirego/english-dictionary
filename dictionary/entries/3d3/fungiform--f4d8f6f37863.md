### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having the shape of a mushroom; especially, the archetypical such shape, suggesting a cap and stem.

   **Example:** the fungiform papillae of the tongue

   **Source synonyms:** mycomorphic; mushroom-shaped
