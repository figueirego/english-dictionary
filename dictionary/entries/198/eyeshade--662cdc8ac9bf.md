### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A type of headgear for shielding the eyes from glaring light, usually consisting of a visor and a headband, more popular among indoor workers in the late-nineteenth and early-twentieth centuries than today.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A cosmetic product which may be applied to the upper eyelid and to the area near the eye to change skin coloration.

   **Usage:** uncountable
