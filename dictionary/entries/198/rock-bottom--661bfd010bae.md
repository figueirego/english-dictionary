## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The very lowest possible level.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; uncountable

   **Example:** Pork belly futures have hit rock bottom.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The lowest possible state in one’s life, whether mentally, socially, or otherwise; often, a period of extreme mental stress, homelessness, or rejection by all friends and family.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; uncountable

   **Example:** Some people believe that mental illnesses can't be treated unless the person hits rock bottom first.
