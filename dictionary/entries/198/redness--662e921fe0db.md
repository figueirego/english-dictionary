### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The state or quality of being red.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Nancy Reagan is famous for the redness of her wardrobe.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A red discoloration, especially of skin.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Redness and swelling are associated with certain superficial infections.
