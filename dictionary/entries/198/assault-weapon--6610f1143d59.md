## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any select-fire firearm that allows semi-automatic and fully automatic operation, and is used or was once used by a military organization.

   **Usage:** US

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A semi-automatic firearm that resembles a military weapon.

   **Usage:** US

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A semi-automatic firearm that resembles a military weapon.
   1. A semi-automatic weapon that has a folding stock, a muzzle flash suppressor, a bayonet mount, or a pistol grip.

      **Usage:** US; New York State

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A rocket launcher designed to attack fortified structures, such as a Mk 153 Shoulder-Launched Multipurpose Assault Weapon (aka SMAW).

   **Usage:** US
