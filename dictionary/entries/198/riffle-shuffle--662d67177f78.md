**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A technique of shuffling a deck of playing cards by first separating the deck into two parts.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** card-games; games

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A permutation that models the outcome of a riffle shuffle; the operation of dividing an ordered set into two equal subsets and then alternately listing sequences of elements from each subset that have a common specified length.
