### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Someone who is hostile to, feels hatred towards, opposes the interests of, or intends injury to someone else.

   **Example:** under enemy duress

   **Example:** He made a lot of enemies after reducing the working hours in his department.

   **Example:** You may not want any enemies, but sometimes, your enemies choose you.

   **Source synonyms:** foe; unfriend; adversary; nemesis; backfriend

   **Source antonyms:** ally; friend

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A hostile force or nation; a fighting member of such a force or nation.

   **Example:** Rally together against a common enemy.

   **Source synonyms:** foe; adversary; nemesis

   **Source antonyms:** ally; friend

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Something harmful or threatening to another

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Of, by, relating to, or belonging to an enemy.

   **Usage:** attributive

   **Example:** The building was destroyed by enemy bombing.
