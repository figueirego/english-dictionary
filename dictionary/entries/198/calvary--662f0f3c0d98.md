### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A life-size representation of the crucifixion of Jesus Christ on a piece of raised ground.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A series of representations of Christ’s Passion in a church.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A type of monumental public Christian cross, sometimes encased in an open shrine.

   **Example:** Calvaries are typical of Galicia (Spain) and Portugal.
