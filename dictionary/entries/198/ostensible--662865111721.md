### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Apparent, evident; meant for open display.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Appearing as such; being such in appearance; professed, supposed (rather than demonstrably true or real).

   **Example:** The ostensible reason for his visit to New York was to see his mother, but the real reason was to get to the Yankees game the next day.
