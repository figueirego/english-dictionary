**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of the defensive players who are in position on each side farthest laterally from the ball and whose principal responsibility is to defend against passes.

   **Usage:** Gaelic football; hurling; Gaelic football; hurling

   **Example:** The cornerback tipped the ball away from the receiver.
