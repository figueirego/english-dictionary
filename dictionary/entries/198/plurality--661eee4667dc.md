### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The state of being plural.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The holding of multiple benefices.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ecclesiastical; lifestyle; religion

   **Source synonyms:** pluralism

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A state of being numerous.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source synonyms:** multiplicity

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A number or part of a whole which is greater than any other number or part, but not necessarily a majority.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A number of votes for a single candidate or position which is greater than the number of votes gained by any other single candidate or position voted for, but which is less than a majority of valid votes cast.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source synonyms:** relative majority

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A margin by which a number exceeds another number, especially of votes.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A group of many entities&#58; a large number.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** A plurality of ideas were put forth at the meeting, most of which were rejected out of hand.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A group composed of more than one entity.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source context (verbatim):** of spouses
