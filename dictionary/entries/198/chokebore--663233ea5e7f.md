## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In a shotgun, a bore which is tapered to a slightly smaller diameter at a short distance to the rear of the muzzle, in order to prevent the rapid dispersion of the shot.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A shotgun that is made with such a bore.
