### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person skilled at using swords in sport or combat; a fencer.

   **Example:** He is a remarkable swordsman.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person who fights with a sword.

   **Example:** Archers attempted to take down an unknown swordsman.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A man who is a skillful or enthusiastic practitioner of sexual intercourse.

   **Usage:** informal
