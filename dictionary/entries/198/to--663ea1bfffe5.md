### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A particle used for marking the following verb as an infinitive.

   **Example:** I want to leave.

   **Example:** He asked me what to do.

   **Example:** I have places to go and people to see.

   **Example:** To err is human.

   **Example:** Who am I to criticise? I've done worse things myself.

   **Example:** Precisely to get away from you was why I did what I did.

   **Example:** I need some more books to read and friends to go partying with.

   **Example:** Those to avoid death ended up being exiled.

   **Example:** He fled to India, never to return.

   **Example:** If he lets you down, he'll have me to reckon with.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. As above, with the verb implied.

   **Example:** "Did you visit the museum?" "I wanted to, but it was closed."

   **Example:** If he hasn't read it yet, he ought to.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used to indicate an obligation on the part of, or a directive given to, the subject.

   **Example:** You are to go to the store and buy a bottle of milk.
