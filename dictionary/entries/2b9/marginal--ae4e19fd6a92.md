### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of, relating to, or located at or near a margin or edge; also figurative usages of location and margin (edge).

   **Usage:** not-comparable; usually

   **Example:** The marginal area at the edge of the salt-marsh has its own plants.

   **Example:** In recent years there has been an increase in violence against marginal groups.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of, relating to, or located at or near a margin or edge; also figurative usages of location and margin (edge).
   1. Written in the margin of a book.

      **Usage:** not-comparable; usually

      **Example:** There were more marginal notes than text.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of, relating to, or located at or near a margin or edge; also figurative usages of location and margin (edge).
   1. Sharing a border; geographically adjacent.

      **Usage:** not-comparable; usually

      **Example:** Monmouthshire is a Welsh county marginal to England.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Determined by a small margin; having a salient characteristic determined by a small margin.
   1. Of a value, or having a characteristic that is of a value, that is close to being unacceptable or leading to exclusion from a group or category.

      **Usage:** comparable; not-comparable; usually

      **Example:** His writing ability was marginal at best.

      **Example:** Having reviewed the test, there are two students below the required standard and three more who are marginal.

      **Example:** The pilots lacked experience flying in marginal weather conditions.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Determined by a small margin; having a salient characteristic determined by a small margin.
   1. Barely productive.

      **Usage:** comparable; not-comparable; usually

      **Example:** He farmed his marginal land with difficulty.

      **Source context (verbatim):** of land

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Determined by a small margin; having a salient characteristic determined by a small margin.
   1. Subject to a change in sitting member with only a small change in voting behaviour, this usually being inferred from the small winning margin of the previous election.

      **Usage:** Australia; New-Zealand; UK; comparable; not-comparable; usually

      **Example:** In Bristol West, Labour had a majority of only 1,000, so the seat is considered highly marginal this time around.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; politics

      **Source context (verbatim):** of a constituency

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Pertaining to changes resulting from a unit increase in production or consumption of a good.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** economics; sciences

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Marginalized.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; sciences; social-science; sociology
