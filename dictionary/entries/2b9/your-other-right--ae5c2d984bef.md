## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Your left-hand side. (Used to correct oneself after saying "your right", having intended "your left".)

   **Usage:** humorous

   **Source antonyms:** your other left

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Your right-hand side. (Used to correct another person who has been directed to move (or look, etc) to "your right" but has mistakenly moved left.)

   **Source antonyms:** your other left
