## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A particularly special or compatible person or thing.

   **Usage:** colloquial

   **Example:** I knew as soon I met him that John was the one for me and we were married within a month.

   **Example:** That car's the one — I'll buy it.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A particularly special or compatible person or thing.
   1. A particular person destined to save the universe or world; the chosen one.

      **Usage:** colloquial

      **Example:** Jesus Christ is the One, the savior.

      **Example:** He (Neo) is the One.

      **Example:** They, like me, believe him (Anakin Skywalker) to be The One.
