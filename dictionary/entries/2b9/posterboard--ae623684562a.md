### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Cardboard material suitable for making posters.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** I used a piece of posterboard for my project.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** I used the posterboard for my project.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
