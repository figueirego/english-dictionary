### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any room in which soundproofing shields the occupants from loud noises originating outside.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A room in a mental hospital in which sensory deprivation provides patients with an environment free of distractions, often with padding on the walls and soft furniture similar to that of occupational therapy.
