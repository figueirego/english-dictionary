### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A small difference between the energy levels 2S\_½ and 2P\_½ of the hydrogen atom, not predicted by the Dirac equation, whose detection in the Lamb–Retherford experiment of 1947 foreshadowed the development of quantum electrodynamics (in which the discrepancy is explained to be an effect of the emission and re-absorption of virtual photons); such a difference between otherwise expectedly equal energy levels of hydrogen or any other type of atom.

   **Usage:** quantum mechanics; quantum electrodynamics; quantum mechanics; quantum electrodynamics
