### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One more further, in addition to the quantity by then; a second or additional one, similar in likeness or in effect.

   **Example:** Yes, I'd like another slice of cake, thanks.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Not the same; different.

   **Example:** Do you know another way to do this job?

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any or some other, similar in likeness or in effect, instead.

   **Example:** One gold ingot is valued the same as another, but gemstones are valued individually.
