### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A succulent annual, Mesembryanthemum crystallinum, of the fig marigold family, having pale pink flowers and fleshy leaves covered with glistening water vesicles.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of several plants in fig marigold family, Aizoaceae, which were formerly classified in the genus Mesembryanthemum along with Mesembryanthemum crystallinum.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of several plants in fig marigold family, Aizoaceae, which were formerly classified in the genus Mesembryanthemum along with Mesembryanthemum crystallinum.
   1. Especially, Carpobrotus edulis.
