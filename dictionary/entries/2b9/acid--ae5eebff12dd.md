### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Sour, sharp, or biting to the taste; tart; having the taste of vinegar.

   **Example:** acid fruits or liquors

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Sour-tempered.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** His response was harsh and acid.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of or pertaining to an acid; acidic.

   **Example:** acid soils

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Denoting a musical genre that is a distortion (as if hallucinogenic) of an existing genre, as in acid house, acid jazz, acid rock.

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A sour substance.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Any compound which yields H+ ions (protons) when dissolved in water; an Arrhenius acid.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. Any compound that easily donates protons to a base; a Brønsted acid.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. Any compound that can accept a pair of electrons to form a covalent bond; a Lewis acid.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences
