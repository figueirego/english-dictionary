   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** reprimand; chide; admonish; animadvert; bash; bawl out; tilt; clobber (informal); cannonade; slash; lash; blame; scorch (informal); blast; berate; berisp (obsolete); castigate; censure; charge; chastise; chew out; chide; condemn; criticize; denounce; denunciate; deprecate; derogate; dispraise; drag (slang); dress down; eat someone alive; excoriate; find fault; fulminate; get at; get down; get up (Australia; Britain); give somebody a piece of one's mind; give somebody what-for; inveigh; lace into; lambaste; lay into; lecture; light into; objurgate; pooh-pooh; put someone on blast (slang); rate; read somebody the riot act; rebuke; reprehend; reprimand; reproach; reprove; rip into; rubbish (Australia; Britain); savage; scold; skewer (figuratively); slam; slate; tear into; tell off; threap; tick off (Britain); tosh (Britain); keelhaul; upbraid; vilify; vituperate

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To advise against wrongdoing; to caution; to warn against danger or an offense.

   **Usage:** transitive; with of or against

   **Source synonyms:** caution; admonish; adjure; advise; advocate; counsel; encourage; urge; exhort; prompt; recommend; suggest; tip; warn
