### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A contributing factor.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A substance, especially a coenzyme or a metal, that must be present for an enzyme to function.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biochemistry; biology; chemistry; microbiology; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A molecule that binds to and regulates the activity of a protein.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biochemistry; biology; chemistry; microbiology; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The result of a number being divided by one of its factors.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The signed determinant of the submatrix produced by removing the row and column containing a specified element; primarily used in the recursive definition and calculation of the determinant of a matrix.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** linear-algebra; mathematics; sciences
