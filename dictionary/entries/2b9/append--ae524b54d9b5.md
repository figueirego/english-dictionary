### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To hang or attach to, as by a string, so that the thing is suspended.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** a seal appended to a record

   **Example:** An inscription was appended to the column.

   **Example:** notes appended to a book chapter
