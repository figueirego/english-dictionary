### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To provide someone with an incentive to do something; to encourage; to actuate.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The weekly staff meeting was meant to motivate employees.

   **Source synonyms:** drive; spur; abet (obsolete); actuate; goose; fuel; light a fire under; send; sting; bring about; cheer (obsolete); draw; drive; encourage; egg on (idiomatic); engender; evoke; excite; foment; goad; grill (Scotland; US; obsolete); ignite (figuratively); impel; incite; induce; inflame (figuratively); instigate; invoke; lash; motive; motivate; move (obsolete); obligate; oblige; push; promote; prompt; provoke; rouse; set off; spark off; spur; spur on; stimulate; stir; stir up; stoke (figuratively); tempt; urge; whet (obsolete); whip up

   **Source antonyms:** demotivate; discourage; dissuade

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To animate; to propel; to cause to take action.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He was motivated purely by self-interest.

   **Example:** Steam-motivated pumps are used in manufacturing.

   **Source synonyms:** drive; move; push

   **Source antonyms:** demotivate; halt; stop
