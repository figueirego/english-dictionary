### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To claim or demonstrate that someone is a bastard, or illegitimate.

   **Usage:** British; English; Oxford; US

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To reduce from a higher to a lower state, such as by removing refined elements or introducing debased elements; to debase.

   **Usage:** British; English; Oxford; US

   **Example:** The simplified word processor is a less-functional, bastardized version of the full program.

   **Source synonyms:** butcher; debase; mongrelize; sophisticate; abase (obsolete); adulter (rare); adulterate; adulterize (uncommon); alloy; balderdash (archaic); [corrupt](../047/corrupt--11d510e067d2.md); bastardize; dash (dated); dilute; embase (archaic); sophisticate (figuratively)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To beget out of wedlock.

   **Usage:** British; English; Oxford; US
