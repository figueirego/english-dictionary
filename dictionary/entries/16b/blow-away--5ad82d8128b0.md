### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see blow, away.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To cause to go away by blowing, or by wind.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He blew away the dust which had collected on the book.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To disperse or to depart on currents of air.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** I didn't have to rake. The leaves just blew away.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To leave, depart.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To kill (someone) by shooting them with a firearm.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** The kid just blew the clerk away.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To utterly destroy, especially with overwhelming force.

   **Usage:** broadly; idiomatic; transitive

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To flabbergast; to impress greatly.

   **Usage:** US; idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** The critics were blown away by their latest album.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To overwhelm.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To cause to go away; to get rid of.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. To delete (data, files, etc.).

   **Usage:** informal; transitive
