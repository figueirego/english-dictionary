## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The total number of all available workers; the workforce.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** phased increases in manpower

   **Source synonyms:** humanpower; human resources; personnel

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The power exerted by a single person.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** canals dug entirely by manpower

   **Source synonyms:** humanpower
