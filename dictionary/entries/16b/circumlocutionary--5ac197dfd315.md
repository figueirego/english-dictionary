### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Articulated in a roundabout manner; tautological or with repetitive language.

   **Example:** The old man's rambling yarn was circumlocutionary.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
