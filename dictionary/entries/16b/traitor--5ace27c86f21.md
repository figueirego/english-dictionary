### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Someone who violates an allegiance and betrays their country; someone guilty of treason; one who, in breach of trust, delivers their country to an enemy, or yields up any fort or place entrusted to their defense, or surrenders an army or body of troops to the enemy, unless when vanquished.

   **Example:** After World War I, the communists and Jews were accused to be traitors by the German right wing (the "stab-in-the-back myth"), ultimately culminating in their persecution and massacre.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Someone who takes arms and levies war against their country; or one who aids an enemy in conquering their country.

   **Source synonyms:** turncoat; withersake; apostate; balimbing (Philippines); butterfly (Philippines); changeling (archaic; figuratively); defector; runagate; traitor; transfuge (dated); turncloak (rare); turncoat; turntippet (obsolete); withersake (archaic)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. One who betrays any confidence or trust.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Source synonyms:** betrayer; fink; snake; snake in the grass; backstabber; betrayer; cockatrice (archaic); double-crosser; quisling; rat; ratter; recreant; renegade; serpent; snake; snake in the grass; swike (dialectal; obsolete); traitor; treacher (archaic); treacherer (archaic; uncommon); treasonist; turntippet (obsolete); withersake (archaic); wolf in sheep's clothing
