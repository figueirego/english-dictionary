## Adjective · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Pronunciation

### Forms

- absenter — comparative
- absentest — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Being away from a place; withdrawn from a place; existing but not present; (sometimes) missing.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Owing to his own illness and then his family's, Ramzi has often been absent from class this month. We will help him catch up with his studies.

   **Example:** When they were able to return to the clinic some months later, certain pieces of equipment were absent.

   **Source antonyms:** present

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Not existing.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The body part was rudimentary or absent in 1% of specimens.

   **Example:** Empathy seemed to be absent from the messages.

   **Example:** Signs of forced entry were absent.

   **Source antonyms:** present; existing; extant

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Inattentive to what is passing; absent-minded; preoccupied.

   **Usage:** comparable

   **Example:** Tom was there, but he seemed absent and withdrawn. Normally he is quite present \[= engaged\] during a meeting.

   **Source antonyms:** present
