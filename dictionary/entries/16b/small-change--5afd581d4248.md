## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Coins of little value kept in one's pocket or bag, ready for small purchases.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** pocket change; loose change; spare change; pocket shrapnel; shrapnel

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A minor or insignificant amount of money.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; uncountable

   **Example:** The cost of toothpaste is small change compared to the cost of dental work.

   **Source synonyms:** pocket change; loose change; spare change; chump change; pittance; coffee money

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A person or thing of little importance or value.

   **Usage:** broadly; idiomatic; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** small-timer; small fry; small potatoes; nobody
