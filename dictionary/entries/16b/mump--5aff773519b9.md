1. To mumble, speak unclearly.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

2. To move the lips with the mouth closed; to mumble, as in sulkiness.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To beg, especially if using a repeated phrase.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

   **Source synonyms:** bum; cadge; sponge; beg; blag (Britain); bum; cadge; leech; mooch; mump; scrounge; scunge; sponge; tap; wheedle
