### Forms

- most glacial — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of, or relating to glaciers.

   **Example:** We examined the glacial deposits.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Very slow.

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Cold and icy.

   **Example:** After the rain and frost, the pavements were glacial.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Having the appearance of ice.

   **Example:** On cold days, glacial acetic acid will freeze in the bottle.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Cool and unfriendly.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** He gave me a glacial stare.
