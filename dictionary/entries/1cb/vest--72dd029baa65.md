## Verb · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /vɛst/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Forms

- vests — present; singular; third-person
- vesting — participle; present

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To clothe with, or as with, a vestment, or garment; to dress; to robe; to cover, surround, or encompass closely.

   **Usage:** passive

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To clothe with authority, power, etc.; to put in possession; to invest; to furnish; to endow; followed by with and the thing conferred.

   **Example:** to vest a court with power to try cases of life and death

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To place or give into the possession or discretion of some person or authority; to commit to another; with in before the possessor.

   **Example:** The power of life and death is vested in the king, or in the courts.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To clothe with possession; also, to give a person an immediate fixed right of present or future enjoyment of.

   **Example:** to vest a person with an estate

   **Example:** an estate is vested in possession

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. (of an inheritance or a trust fund) To devolve upon the person currently entitled when a prior interest has ended.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Upon the death of the Sovereign the Crown automatically vests in the next heir without the need of coronation or other formality.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To become vested, to become permanent.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** My pension vests at the end of the month and then I can take it with me when I quit.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

**Source sense R2.S7**

7. To invest; to put.

   **Usage:** obsolete

   **Example:** to vest money in goods, land, or houses
