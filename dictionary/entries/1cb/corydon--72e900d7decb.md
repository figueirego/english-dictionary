## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A male given name from Greek, notably used as a stock name for a shepherd in pastorals, in particular a shepherd whose love for a boy is described in Virgil's Eclogues.

   **Usage:** literary

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A place name used for several towns in the United States.
   1. A town, the county seat of Harrison County, Indiana, also located in Harrison Township.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A place name used for several towns in the United States.
   1. A small city, the county seat of Wayne County, Iowa.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A place name used for several towns in the United States.
   1. A town in Henderson County, Kentucky.
