### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The quality or state of being uniform.
   1. The quality or state of having the same characteristics or form as other things, and lacking variety; (countable) an instance of this.

      **Usage:** uncountable; usually

      **Source synonyms:** sameness

      **Source antonyms:** disuniformity; nonuniformity

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The quality or state of being uniform.
   1. Often followed by of&#58; the quality or state of a thing always having the same characteristics or form; consistency or regularity in appearance or operation.

      **Usage:** uncountable; usually

      **Example:** The uniformity of the decor

      **Source antonyms:** disuniformity; nonuniformity

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The quality or state of being uniform.
   1. Often followed by of&#58; especially of one's life&#58; lack of variety; tedious sameness; monotony.

      **Usage:** uncountable; usually

      **Source antonyms:** disuniformity; nonuniformity

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The quality or state of being uniform.
   1. The condition or state described by uniformitarianism (“the scientific principle that natural laws and processes operated in the past in the same way and at the same rates that they operate today, and sometimes in the same way everywhere in the universe as well”).

      **Usage:** uncountable; usually

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; geology; natural-sciences

      **Source antonyms:** disuniformity; nonuniformity

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The quality or state of being uniform.
   1. Adherence to or conformity with one viewpoint, set of observances, etc.

      **Usage:** especially; uncountable; usually

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** lifestyle; religion

      **Source antonyms:** disuniformity; nonuniformity
