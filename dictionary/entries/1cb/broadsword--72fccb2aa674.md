### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A type of early modern sword that has a broad double-edged blade for cutting (as opposed to the more slender thrust-oriented rapier) and typically a basket hilt.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** history; human-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person armed with such a sword.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Synonym of longsword.

   **Usage:** colloquial; often

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** fantasy

   **Source synonyms:** longsword (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**
