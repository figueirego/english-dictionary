### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To plant too many plants (of any particular crop).

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** With choice hybrid seeds being so expensive, it pays not to overplant if you can help it; pick your seed plates carefully.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To plant too many of (some kind of plant), whether by sowing or by transplanting.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Growers in the region had overplanted their wheat in a desperate attempt to mitigate slim margins by "making it up on volume".

   **Example:** He overplanted tobacco in his eagerness for a cash crop with higher margins.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To plant (a place) with too many plants.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The entire breadbasket region was overplanted with cultivars that were more disease-prone but had the dubious advantage of lower seed cost.
