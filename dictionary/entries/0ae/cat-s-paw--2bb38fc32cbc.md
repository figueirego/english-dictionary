### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A paw of a cat.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Someone who acts in another's interest, (properly) unknowingly or through trickery.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Source synonyms:** tool; dupe

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A minor breeze that ripples the surface of a body of water.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A twisting variant of the lark's-foot hitch which forms two small bights used to hook a pair of tackles to a rope.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Someone or something that comes down quickly upon a victim in the manner of a cat's paw.

   **Usage:** figuratively; uncommon

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A small crowbar with a handle at a right angle to a blade with a V-shaped notch, principally used by carpenters to remove nails.

   **Usage:** US

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; carpentry; construction; manufacturing

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A metal set of claws worn over the hand or wielded in the hand used to remove skin and flesh.

   **Usage:** historical; torture

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; government; military; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; politics; tools; war; weaponry

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A supervisor whose reliance on a subordinate's analysis is so complete as to render him or her liable for the subordinate's animus or other misconduct towards a third employee.

   **Usage:** US

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. Any of several species of North American freshwater mussels of the genus Epioblasma, especially E. obliquata.

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. Any of several species of Australian bloodworts of the genus Anigozanthos, especially A. humilis.
