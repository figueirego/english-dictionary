**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A rocky material made of calcium carbonate or other calcareous materials, usually derived from the skeletons of dead coral, which houses various marine animals, algae and bacteria and is used as an important part of aquascaping and biological filtration in marine aquaria.

   **Usage:** uncountable; aquarium
