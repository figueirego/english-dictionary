<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

3. The top cooking surface on a cooker. It typically comprises several cooking elements (often four), usually called rings.

   **Usage:** Commonwealth; Ireland; UK

   **Source synonyms:** cooktop (Canada; US); stovetop; range

**Source sense R1.S4**

**Source sense R1.S5**

   **Source synonyms:** hob-ferret
