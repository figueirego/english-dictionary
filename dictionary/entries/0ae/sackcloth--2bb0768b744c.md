### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A coarse hessian style of cloth used to make sacks.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Garments worn as an act of penance.

   **Usage:** also; countable; figuratively; uncountable; usually

   **Example:** After he realised the gravity of his crime he spent some time wearing sackcloth and ashes.

   **Source context (verbatim):** with “and ashes”

   **Source synonyms:** hairshirt; cilice
