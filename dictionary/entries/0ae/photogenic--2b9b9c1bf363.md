### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Generated or caused by light.

   **Example:** The sunbather developed a photogenic melanoma on her back.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Producing or emitting light; luminescent.

   **Example:** The photogenic bacteria were visible in the dark room.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Looking good when photographed.

   **Example:** The company hired the spokesperson for his photogenic face.
