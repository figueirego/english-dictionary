### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To defeat in combat; to subjugate.

   **Source synonyms:** overrun

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To become a leader, decisive factor or champion in an area.

   **Example:** With her successes, she has truly conquered the committee, all of whose members now defer to her on most issues.

   **Example:** It was his campaign manager's strategy that truly conquered the election for him.

   **Example:** By the end of the season, he had conquered the sport of baseball.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To acquire by force of arms, win in war; to become ruler of; to subjugate.

   **Example:** In 1453, the Ottoman Empire conquered Constantinople.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To overcome an abstract obstacle.

   **Example:** Today I conquered my fear of flying by finally boarding a plane.

   **Example:** to conquer difficulties or temptations

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To gain, win, or obtain by effort.

   **Usage:** dated

   **Example:** to conquer freedom; to conquer a peace
