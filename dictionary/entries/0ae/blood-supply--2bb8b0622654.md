### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Donated blood which is stored in a blood bank.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The local blood drive will replenish the area's blood supply.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The volume of blood flowing to part of the body over a particular time period.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** circulation
