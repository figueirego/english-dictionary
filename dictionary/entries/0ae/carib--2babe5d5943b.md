### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A member of one of a number of Amerindian peoples who inhabit the coast of Central and South America and the Lesser Antilles.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A member of one of a number of Amerindian peoples who inhabit the coast of Central and South America and the Lesser Antilles.
   1. A member of the mainland or ‘proper’ Caribs (Kari'na), a Cariban people who inhabit the north coast of South America, in parts of Venezuela, Guyana, Suriname, and French Guiana.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A member of one of a number of Amerindian peoples who inhabit the coast of Central and South America and the Lesser Antilles.
   1. A member of the Kalinago people of the Lesser Antilles, an Arawakan people who took heavy cultural influence from the mainland Caribs and so were formerly known as Island Caribs.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A member of one of a number of Amerindian peoples who inhabit the coast of Central and South America and the Lesser Antilles.
   1. A member of the Garifuna people of Honduras, historically known as Black Caribs, descendants of mixed Kalinago people and Africans who were deported to the Central American mainland in the 18th century.
