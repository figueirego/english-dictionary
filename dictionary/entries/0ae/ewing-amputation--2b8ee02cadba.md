### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A type of below-the-knee amputation, which reconstructs agonist-antagonist myoneural interfaces that are left hanging unpaired in traditional amputations; to preserve normal nerve signaling between muscle and brain; reducing incidences of phantom limb syndrome.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences; surgery

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** broadly
