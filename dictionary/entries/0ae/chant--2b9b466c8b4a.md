<a id="r1-s2"></a>

2. To sing or intone sacred text.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

   **Example:** The football fans chanted insults at the referee.

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To sell horses fraudulently, exaggerating their merits.

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive
