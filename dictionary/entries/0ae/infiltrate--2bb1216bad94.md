### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To surreptitiously penetrate, enter or gain access to.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** The spy infiltrated the high-tech company and stole many secrets.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To cause to penetrate in this way.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The agency infiltrated several spies into the company.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To pass through something by filtration.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a liquid

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To cause (a liquid) to pass through something by filtration.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To invade or penetrate a tissue or organ.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** High-grade tumors often infiltrate surrounding structures.

   **Example:** In certain conditions, immune cells may infiltrate into the cerebrospinal fluid.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences
