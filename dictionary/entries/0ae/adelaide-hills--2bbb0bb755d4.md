## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The portion of the southern Mount Lofty Ranges that is east of the city of Adelaide, South Australia.

   **Example:** The Adelaide Hills form the eastern boundary of the Adelaide Plains, separating them from the extensive plains that surround the Murray River and stretch eastwards to Victoria.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A local government area in the Adelaide Hills of South Australia; in full, Adelaide Hills Council.
