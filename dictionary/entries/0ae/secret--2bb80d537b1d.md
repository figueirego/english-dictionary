**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A piece of knowledge that is hidden and intended to be kept hidden.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** "Can you keep a secret?" "Yes." "So can I."

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The key or principle by which something is made clear; the knack.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The secret to a long-lasting marriage is compromise.

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Something not understood or known.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Private seclusion.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** The work was done in secret, so that nobody could object.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The genital organs.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; in-plural; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A form of steel skullcap.

   **Usage:** countable; historical; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Any prayer spoken inaudibly and not aloud; especially, one of the prayers in the Tridentine Mass, immediately following the "orate, fratres", said inaudibly by the celebrant.
