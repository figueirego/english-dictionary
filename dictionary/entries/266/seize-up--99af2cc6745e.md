### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To stop functioning; to come to a halt.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

   **Example:** Iceland's foreign currency market has seized up after the three largest banks collapsed.

   **Source synonyms:** freeze; freeze up; grind to a halt

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To stiffen or become tight and difficult to move.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** It was hard to write as my fingers had seized up with the cold weather.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of muscles

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To stop working suddenly, and become impossible to start again.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** My car seized up this morning. So I had to catch the bus.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a machine
