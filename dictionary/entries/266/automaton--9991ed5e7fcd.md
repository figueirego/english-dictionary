### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A machine or robot designed to follow a precise sequence of instructions.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person who acts like a machine or robot, often defined as having a monotonous lifestyle and lacking in emotion.

   **Example:** Due to her strict adherence to her daily schedule, Jessica was becoming more and more convinced that she was an automaton.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A formal system, such as a finite-state machine or cellular automaton.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A toy in the form of a mechanical figure.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The self-acting power of the muscular and nervous systems, by which movement is effected without intelligent determination.

   **Usage:** dated
