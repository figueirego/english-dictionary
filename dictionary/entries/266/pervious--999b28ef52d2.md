### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Often followed by to&#58; capable of being penetrated by another body or substance, such as air or water; admitting passage.

   **Example:** a pervious soil

   **Source synonyms:** penetrable; permeable; perviable; porous

   **Source antonyms:** impervious; impenetrable; impermeable; unpervious

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Capable of being seen through; open to being examined; patent, unconcealed.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Source synonyms:** perspicuous; transparent

   **Source antonyms:** impervious; unpervious

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Capable of being penetrated mentally; intelligible, understandable.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Source synonyms:** perspicuous; transparent

   **Source antonyms:** impervious; unpervious

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Of a person, etc.&#58; susceptible to being influenced by arguments, ideas, etc.; impressionable, tractable.

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Capable of penetrating or permeating.

   **Usage:** obsolete

   **Source synonyms:** pervading

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Of a body structure (especially the nostril of a bird)&#58; having a hole, perforate; also, wide open.

   **Usage:** obsolete

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences; zoology
