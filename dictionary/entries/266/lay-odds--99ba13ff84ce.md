### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To offer a bet at stated odds.

   **Example:** Bookies are already laying odds on the outcome of the next general election.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To express a high level of confidence in an outcome by offering odds whereby one would lose substantially in the event of an unfavourable result.

   **Usage:** figuratively; often

   **Example:** I'll lay odds that they're divorced by Christmas.
