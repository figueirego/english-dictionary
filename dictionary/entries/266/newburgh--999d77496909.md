## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A town in Fife council area, Scotland, situated on the Firth of Tay (OS grid ref NO2318).

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A coastal village in Aberdeenshire council area, Scotland, near the mouth of the Ythan (OS grid ref NJ9925).

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A hamlet in north Aberdeenshire council area (OS grid ref NJ9659).

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A village and civil parish in West Lancashire district, Lancashire, England (OS grid ref SD4810).

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A locality and civil parish (without a council) in North Yorkshire, England, previously in Hambleton district (OS grid ref SE5476).

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A community and former village in Stone Mills, Lennox and Addington County, Ontario, Canada.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A town on the Ohio River in Warrick County, Indiana, United States.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A town in Penobscot County, Maine, United States.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A city and town in Orange County, New York, United States, named after Newburgh, Fife.

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. A former township, now part of Cleveland, Ohio, United States.
