## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

- footplates — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The metal plate which forms the base platform of a steam locomotive and supports the boiler and the driver's cab, particularly in the United Kingdom.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** rail-transport; railways; transport

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The engineer's cab in any kind of train.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The flat portion at the base of the stapes; pedicel.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The part of a roller skate that attaches to the boot.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A timber in a wood frame that distributes a concentrated load.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; construction; manufacturing

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A platform on which the driver's foot rests but which, unlike a pedal, has no effect.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** automotive; transport; vehicles
