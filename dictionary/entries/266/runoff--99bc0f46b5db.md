### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. That portion of precipitation or irrigation on an area which does not infiltrate or evaporate, but instead is discharged from the area.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Dissolved chemicals, etc, included in such water.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The runoff of nitrates is poisoning the lake.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A second or further round of a competition, after other competitors (often all but the last two) have been eliminated.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A second or further round of an indecisive election, after other candidates (often all but the last two) have been eliminated.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** There will now be a runoff as neither front runner received more than 50% of the vote.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; politics
