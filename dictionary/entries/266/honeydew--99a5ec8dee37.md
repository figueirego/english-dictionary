### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A sweet, sticky substance deposited on leaves and other plant parts by insects (especially aphids and scale insects) feeding on plant sap, or by fungi.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** melligo (archaic)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A sweet liquid substance resembling the substance mentioned in sense 1, such as honey, nectar, or manna in the Bible.

   **Usage:** broadly; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** ambrosia

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A blackish mould (often called sooty mould) produced by fungi of the order Dothideales, feeding on the substance mentioned in sense 1.

   **Usage:** broadly; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. In full honeydew tobacco&#58; a fine sort of tobacco moistened with a sweet substance (originally molasses).

   **Usage:** broadly; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Ellipsis of honeydew melon (“a melon from the Cucumis melo Inodorus cultivar group, with sweet, light green or white flesh and a smooth greenish-white or yellow rind”).

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; broadly; countable; ellipsis

   **Alternative form of:** [honeydew melon](../0d7/honeydew-melon--35e06e42afa8.md)

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Ellipsis of honeydew melon (“a melon from the Cucumis melo Inodorus cultivar group, with sweet, light green or white flesh and a smooth greenish-white or yellow rind”).
   1. A light green colour, like the flesh of some honeydew melons.

      **Usage:** broadly; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Something that is enjoyable or pleasant.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; figuratively
