### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In a double manner; with twice the severity or degree.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; usually; quantity or measure

   **Example:** My mother was always doubly careful when winding the grandfather clock.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of degree; of relative importance

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In two ways.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Stealing and then lying about it is doubly wrong.

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. With duplicity.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; obsolete
