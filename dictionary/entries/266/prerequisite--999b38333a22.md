### Forms

- prerequisites — plural

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Something that is required as necessary or indispensable, or as a prior condition of something else.

   **Example:** A degree is a prerequisite for entry into this profession.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. A course or topic that must be completed before another course or topic can be started.

   **Example:** Algebra is typically a prerequisite for physics.
