### Senses

<a id="r8-s1"></a>

**Source sense R8.S1**

1. To find by chance.

   **Example:** I lit upon a rare book in a second-hand bookseller's.

<a id="r8-s2"></a>

**Source sense R8.S2**

2. To stop upon (of eyes or a glance); to notice.

<a id="r8-s3"></a>

**Source sense R8.S3**

3. To alight; to land or come down.

   **Usage:** archaic

   **Example:** She fell out of the window but luckily lit on her feet.
