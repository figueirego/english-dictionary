### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Silica based glass extruded into fibers that possess a length at least 1000 times greater than their width.

   **Usage:** UK; uncountable; usually

2. Ellipsis of fibreglass wool.

   **Usage:** UK; abbreviation; alt-of; ellipsis; uncountable; usually

   **Alternative form of:** fibreglass wool

<a id="r1-s3"></a>
