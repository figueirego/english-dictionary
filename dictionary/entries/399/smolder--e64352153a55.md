### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To burn slowly, giving off smoke but with little to no flame.

   **Usage:** US; intransitive

   **Example:** The remains of the bonfire were left to smolder for hours.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To show signs of repressed anger or suppressed mental turmoil or other strong emotion, such as passion.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To exist in a suppressed or hidden state.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive
