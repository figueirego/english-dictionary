### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To express appreciation or gratitude toward (someone or something).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** She thanked him for the lift.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. In the future tense in the form one will thank someone to do something, chiefly expressing a command or criticism&#58; to request that (someone) do something.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I’ll thank you not to smoke in my house!

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To express appreciation or gratitude for (something).

   **Usage:** rare; transitive

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To respond to (someone) out of, or as if out of, appreciation or gratitude.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. Chiefly followed by for&#58; to credit or hold (someone or something) responsible, especially for something negative; to blame.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** We can thank global warming for this freak weather.

   **Example:** We have two sick newborns to thank for our lack of sleep lately.

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To express gratitude.

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete
