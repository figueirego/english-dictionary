### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In a horrible way; very badly.

   **Example:** The beginning art students displayed their horribly executed paintings with hopeful faces.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** manner

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To an extreme degree or extent.

   **Usage:** often; degree

   **Example:** Then everything went horribly wrong.

   **Example:** The man was horribly nice, yet she still wouldn't marry him.

   **Source context (verbatim):** modifying a negative adverb or adjective

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. With a very bad effect.

   **Usage:** evaluative

   **Example:** Horribly, as he was dying, his eyes reddened.
