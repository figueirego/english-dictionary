### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A form of a language that is institutionally promoted, regarded as the most correct or neutral variety, and is used by a population for public and formal purposes.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences; social-science; sociolinguistics; sociology

   **Source synonyms:** standard; standard dialect; standard variety

   **Source antonyms:** nonstandard variety

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A language that has a standard form as one of its varieties; a language that has undergone standardization.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences; social-science; sociolinguistics; sociology
