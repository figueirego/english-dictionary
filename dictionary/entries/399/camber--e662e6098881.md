### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A slight convexity, arching or curvature of a surface of a road, beam, roof, ship's deck etc., so that liquids will flow off the sides.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The slope of a curved road created to minimize the effect of centrifugal force.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** crossfall

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An upward concavity in the underside of a beam, girder, or lintel; also, a slight upward concavity in a straight arch.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** architecture

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The alignment on the roll axis of the wheels of a road vehicle, where positive camber signifies that the wheels are closer together at the bottom than the top.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** automotive; transport; vehicles

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The curvature of an airfoil.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** aeronautics; aerospace; aviation; business; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A small enclosed dock in which timber for masts (etc.) is kept to weather.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport
