### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. That which one is morally or legally obligated to do.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Do your duty by me! -No, we don't have a duty to keep you abreast.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The state of being at work and responsible for or doing a particular task.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** I’m on duty from 6 pm to 6 am.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An excise tax; especially, one on imports or exports.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** customs duty

   **Source synonyms:** excise duty

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. One's due, something one is owed; a debt or fee.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Respect; reverence; regard; act of respect; homage.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The efficiency of an engine in work done per unit of fuel; particularly, the number of pounds of water which a steam pumping engine can raise one foot by burning one bushel or hundredweight of coal.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The act of urination or defecation, especially for a dog.

   **Usage:** countable; euphemistic; uncountable
