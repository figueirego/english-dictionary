### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A glass or phial, shaped like an inverted funnel, with a very thin convex bottom that can be made concave or convex by sucking out or blowing in air, used to demonstrate the malleability of glass.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>
