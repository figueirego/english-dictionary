## Phrase

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An introduction to a statement that will be brief, indicating that further discussion would be futile, too lengthy, too private, etc.

   **Example:** Suffice it to say that we will not be buying you any more cars in the future.

   **Example:** There are different theories as to what may have happened precisely. Suffice it to say that we’ve never seen him again.
