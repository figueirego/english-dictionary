A hypothetical form of energy thought to be spread uniformly throughout space (and time) and to have anti-gravitational properties&#58; it represents a possible mechanism for the cosmological constant, and thus is one of the possible explanations for the current accelerating rate of expansion of the universe; and it is estimated to account for about 74% of the mass-energy of the universe.
