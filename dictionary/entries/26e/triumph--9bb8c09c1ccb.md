### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A conclusive success following an effort, conflict, or confrontation of obstacles; victory; conquest.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** the triumph of knowledge

   **Example:** After being defeated in three previous finals, Roger finally tasted triumph at this year's competition.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A magnificent and imposing ceremonial performed in honor of a victor.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any triumphal procession; a pompous exhibition; a stately show or pageant.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A state of joy or exultation at success.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A trump card.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A card game, also called trump.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. a ceremony held to publicly celebrate and sanctify the military achievement of an army commander.

   **Usage:** Ancient-Rome; countable; historical; uncountable

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A work of art, cuisine, etc. of very high quality.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Scorsese's latest film is a triumph.

   **Example:** This wedding cake is a triumph.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A card trick in which the cards are shuffled with half face-up and half face-down, then laid out so that only the observer's chosen card is facing upward.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
