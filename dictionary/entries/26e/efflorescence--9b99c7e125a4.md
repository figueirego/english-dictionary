### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The production of flowers.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Rapid flowering of a culture or civilisation etc.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The formation of a powdery surface on crystals, as a hydrate is converted to anhydrous form by losing loosely bound water of crystallization to the atmosphere.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An encrustation of soluble salts, commonly white, deposited on the surface of stone, brick, plaster, or mortar; usually caused by free alkalies leached from mortar or adjacent concrete as moisture moves through it.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; construction; manufacturing

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An encrustation of soluble salts, deposited on rock or soil by evaporation; often found in arid or geothermal environments.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; geology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A redness, rash, or eruption on the skin.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; pathology; sciences
