## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈkaʊntəˌpɑːt/ — source labels: UK
- IPA: /ˈkaʊntɚˌpɑɹt/ — source labels: US

### Forms

- counterparts — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Either of two parts that fit together or otherwise complement one another.

   **Example:** Those brass knobs and their hollow counterparts interlock perfectly.

   **Source synonyms:** complement; mate

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A duplicate of a legal document.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. One who or that which resembles another.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. One who or that which has corresponding functions or characteristics.

   **Example:** Her French counterpart attended the meeting via video call.

   **Example:** The two companies are working together with their respective counterparts in Asia.

   **Source synonyms:** opposite number; counterparty; opponent; homolog; homotype; equivalent; partner; vis-à-vis

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Either half of a flattened fossil when the rock has split along the plane of the fossil.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; history; human-sciences; natural-sciences; paleontology; sciences
