   **Source context (verbatim):** of two solutions

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Having the same concentration of solutes as human blood.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Use an isotonic saline solution in your neti pot to prevent irritation of your nasal passages.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a medical solution

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source context (verbatim):** of two muscles

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**
