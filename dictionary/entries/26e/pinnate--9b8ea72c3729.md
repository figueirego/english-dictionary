### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Resembling a feather.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Having two rows of branches, lobes, leaflets, or veins arranged on each side of a common axis

   **Example:** Mimosa is a tree with pinnate leaves.

   **Example:** The trunk is unbranched, often much shortened, and bears a crown of feathery or pinnate fronds.

**Source sense R1.S3**
