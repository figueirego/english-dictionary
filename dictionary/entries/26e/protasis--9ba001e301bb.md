### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The first part of a play, in which the setting and characters are introduced.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A clause that expresses a contingent element in a conditional sentence.

   **Example:** In "I will be coming if this weather holds up", "if this weather holds up" is the protasis, and "I will be coming" is the apodosis.
