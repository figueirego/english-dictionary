### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To miss an experience or lose an opportunity, etc. that should not be missed.

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** Anybody who does not try the homemade ice cream is really missing out.

   **Example:** He missed out on the chance to date her in high school. Now, she's married.

   **Source context (verbatim):** with 'on'

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To leave out, to omit.

   **Usage:** UK; transitive

   **Example:** People often miss out the "l" in "vulnerable".

   **Example:** He made a list of children in the class but missed out Sally.
