### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A company, business, organization, or other purposeful endeavor.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The government sponsored enterprises (GSEs) are a group of financial services corporations which have been created by the United States Congress.

   **Example:** A micro-enterprise is defined as a company or business having 5 or fewer employees and a low seed capital.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An undertaking, venture, or project, especially a daring and courageous one.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Biosphere 2 was a scientific enterprise aimed at the exploration of the complex web of interactions within life systems.

   **Source synonyms:** pursuit; undertaking; emprise (formal); activity; doings; endeavor; enterprise; pursuance; pursuit; undertaking; venture

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A willingness to undertake new or risky projects; energy and initiative.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** He has shown great enterprise throughout his early career.

   **Source synonyms:** initiative

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Active participation in projects.

   **Usage:** uncountable
