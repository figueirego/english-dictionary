**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Ellipsis of tomato ketchup (“a tomato-vinegar-based sauce, sometimes containing spices, onion or garlic, and (especially in the US) sweeteners”).

   **Usage:** Canada; UK; US; abbreviation; alt-of; ellipsis; uncountable

   **Example:** tomato ketchup

   **Example:** This diner serves ketchup in red bottles, and mustard in yellow ones.

   **Source synonyms:** tomato catsup

   **Alternative form of:** [tomato ketchup](../378/tomato-ketchup--de29a3938d7c.md)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Such a sauce more generally (not necessarily based on tomatoes, but with mushrooms, fish, etc.). This is the older meaning.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable
