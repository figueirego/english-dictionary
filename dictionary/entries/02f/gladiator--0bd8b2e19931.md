<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person (professional or slave) who entertained the public by engaging in mortal combat with another, or with a wild animal.

   **Usage:** Ancient-Rome

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A disputant in a public controversy or debate.

   **Usage:** broadly
