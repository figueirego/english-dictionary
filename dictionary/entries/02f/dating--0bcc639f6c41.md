### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A form of romantic courtship typically between two individuals with the aim of assessing the other's suitability as a partner in an intimate relationship or as a spouse.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Janet was surprised at how much casual dating had changed since she was last single.

   **Example:** We met on a popular dating app.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
