### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To collapse inward or downward.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The roof caved in under the weight of the snow.

   **Source synonyms:** [cave](../2e2/cave--b89a199ea81b.md); break; fall in; give way

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To cause to collapse inward or downward.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He caved in the side of the barrel with a single well-placed kick.

   **Source synonyms:** break; crush; stave; stave in

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To relent; to grant approval against one's initial will.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

   **Example:** After he asked me a few times, I finally caved in and had a slice of cake.

   **Source synonyms:** [cave](../2e2/cave--b89a199ea81b.md); assent; give in; give up; relinquish; yield; comply; acquiesce
