### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The property of being stupid.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** I suppose you can put it down to gross stupidity, but that's not much of an excuse.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An act that is stupid.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** Jimmy's stupidity cost him his car.
