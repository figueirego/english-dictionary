## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of several species of tufted grasses of the genus Lolium.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** Perennial ryegrass is an important species for lawn seed mixes.

   **Source synonyms:** lolium

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A collection of plants of any of the species, as in a lawn or field.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** We seeded the meadow with ryegrass to provide better forage.
