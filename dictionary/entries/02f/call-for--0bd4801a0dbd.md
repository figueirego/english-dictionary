## Verb

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To shout out in order to summon (a person).

   **Example:** I leant out of the back door and called for Lucy.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To ask for in a loud voice.

   **Example:** We finished the main course in short order and called for more wine.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To request, demand.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** The government has called for an end to hostilities in the region.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To necessitate, demand, exact; to make appropriate

   **Example:** This situation calls for a high degree of courage.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To stop at a place and ask for (someone).

   **Example:** I'll call for you just after midday.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To anticipate, predict.

   **Usage:** US; informal

   **Example:** The forecast calls for rain.
