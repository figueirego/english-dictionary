### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of relying (on or in someone or something); trust.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Your reliance on his expertise may be misplaced.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The condition of being reliant or dependent.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The industry is working to phase out its reliance on fossil fuels.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Anything on which to rely; ground of trust.

   **Usage:** countable; dated; uncountable

   **Example:** The boat was a poor reliance.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A person or thing which relies on another.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
