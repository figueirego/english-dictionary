## Verb · Record 3

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈkaʊn.tɚˌfɪt/ — source labels: General-American
- IPA: /ˈkawntəˌfɪt/ — source labels: British; Southern; Standard

### Forms

- counterfeits — present; singular; third-person
- counterfeiting — participle; present
- counterfeited — participle; past
- counterfeited — past

### Senses

<a id="r3-s1"></a>

**Source sense R3.S1**

1. To falsely produce what appears to be official or valid; to produce a forged copy of.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to counterfeit the signature of another, coins, notes, etc.

<a id="r3-s2"></a>

**Source sense R3.S2**

2. To produce a faithful copy of.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r3-s3"></a>

**Source sense R3.S3**

3. To feign; to mimic.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

   **Example:** to counterfeit the voice of another person

<a id="r3-s4"></a>

**Source sense R3.S4**

4. Of a turn or river card, to invalidate a player's hand by making a better hand on the board.

   **Usage:** transitive; usually "be counterfeited"

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** card-games; poker
