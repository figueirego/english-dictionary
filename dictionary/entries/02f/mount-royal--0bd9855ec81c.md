## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A large hill in the centre of Montreal Island, Quebec, Canada, the origin of the name of Montreal.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A suburban town in Montreal, Quebec, named after the hill.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A large neighbourhood of Calgary, Alberta, Canada.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A neighbourhood of Saskatoon, Saskatchewan, Canada.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A locality in the Singleton council area, eastern New South Wales, Australia.

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A mountain in the Mount Royal Range, New South Wales, Australia.
