### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. That suspends, temporarily stops.

   **Example:** Effect of an appeal – In principle, an appeal has a suspensive effect (Art. 51(1) VRP). The term suspensive effect denotes the fact that the legal consequence prescribed by the ruling does not become effective until the conclusion of the appeal procedure.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law
