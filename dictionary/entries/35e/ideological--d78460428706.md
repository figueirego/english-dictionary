### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of or pertaining to one or more ideologies.

   **Example:** There are economists from all over the ideological spectrum.

   **Example:** ideological fervor

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Irrational; supported by misinformation and social reinforcement, as opposed to credible evidence.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a claim or idea

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Characterized by strict, uncompromising adherence to a particular political ideology, rather than what’s necessarily considered popular or practical.

   **Example:** The most ruthless leaders in history were often ideological ones with utopian visions.

   **Example:** The regime, ideological in governance, continued its agenda despite experts’ criticism.

   **Example:** ideological trade policy

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; politics
