**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A medieval feudal principality centered on Vienne and its region (Viennois) in southeastern France, ruled by the Dauphins of Viennois from the 12th century until its cession to the French crown in 1349; corresponding roughly to the later province of Dauphiné.
