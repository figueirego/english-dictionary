### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To experience sympathy for, to sympathise with.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I feel for him&#58; he’s lost two family members in two months.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To search for something by touching (as with one’s hand in a bag).

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** He felt for his purse.
