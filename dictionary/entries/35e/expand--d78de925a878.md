### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To change (something) from a smaller form or size to a larger one; to spread out or lay open.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** You can expand this compact umbrella to cover a large table.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To increase the extent, number, volume or scope of (something).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** A flower expands its leaves.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To express (something) at length and/or in detail.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To rewrite (an expression) as a longer, yet equivalent, sum of terms.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Use the binomial theorem to expand \#123;(x\#43;1)\#125;⁴.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** algebra; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To become, by rewriting, a longer, yet equivalent, sum of terms.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The expression \#123;(x\#43;1)\#125;⁴ expands to x⁴\#43;4x³\#43;6x²\#43;4x\#43;1.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** algebra; mathematics; sciences

   **Source context (verbatim):** of an expression

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To multiply both the numerator and the denominator of a fraction by the same (non-zero) number (which yields a fraction of equal value).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Expanding 4\#47;7 by 3 gives 12\#47;21.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** arithmetic
