### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make (something) diverse or varied in form or quality; to give variety to (something) to distinguish by numerous aspects or differences.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** diverse; variegate

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To make (something) diverse or varied in form or quality; to give variety to (something) to distinguish by numerous aspects or differences.
   1. To make the scope of (business, investments, etc.) diverse, especially so as to balance and mitigate risks.

      **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

      **Source synonyms:** diverse; variegate; diverse
