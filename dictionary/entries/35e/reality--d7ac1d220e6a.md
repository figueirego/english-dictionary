**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The state of being actual or real; realness.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** The reality of the crash scene on TV dawned upon him only when he saw the victim was no actor but his friend.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The real world.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A real entity, event, or other fact.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** The ultimate reality of life is that it ends in death.

   **Example:** There are several new realities that this team now needs to accept&#58; our old project is dead, and our new project is now the priority.
