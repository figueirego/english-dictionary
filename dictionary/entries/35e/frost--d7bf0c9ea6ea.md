### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A cover of minute ice crystals on objects that are exposed to the air. Frost is formed by the same process as dew, except that the temperature of the frosted object is below freezing.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The cold weather that causes these ice crystals to form.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Coldness or insensibility; severity or rigidity of character.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The act of freezing; the congelation of water or other liquid.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A shade of white, like that of frost.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A disappointment; a cheat.

   **Usage:** countable; dated; slang; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A kind of light diffuser.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** broadcasting; media; television

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

   **Usage:** transitive
