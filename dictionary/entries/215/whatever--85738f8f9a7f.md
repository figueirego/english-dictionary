### Senses

<a id="r3-s1"></a>

**Source sense R3.S1**

1. What ever; emphasised form of 'what', used to ask which thing, event, circumstance, etc.

   **Usage:** interrogative

   **Example:** Whatever do you mean?

<a id="r3-s2"></a>

**Source sense R3.S2**

2. Regardless of anything that.

   **Example:** Whatever he does, he will still lose the game.

   **Example:** Whatever happens, stay calm.

<a id="r3-s3"></a>

**Source sense R3.S3**

3. Anything that; all that.

   **Usage:** fused relative

   **Example:** Do whatever works.

   **Example:** I’ll do whatever I can.

<a id="r3-s4"></a>

**Source sense R3.S4**

4. Anything; thing(s) of unspecified kind, or no matter what kind; sometimes used to indicate that the speaker does not care about options.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** I don't need any of this stuff, so take whatever.

### Senses

<a id="r5-s1"></a>

**Source sense R5.S1**

1. A holophrastic expression used to dismiss something that the speaker doesn't care about, doesn't think important, or doesn't want to consider or discuss any further. Sometimes conceived as an ellipsis of whatever you say, implying feigned deference&#58; whatever you say \[apparently must be true\].

   **Usage:** colloquial

   **Example:** So you broke it? Whatever! Get a grip.

   **Example:** Go brush your teeth. – Whatever!

   **Source synonyms:** whatev; whevs; what the fuck ever; whatever you say; whoopee do; if you say so; meh; so what
