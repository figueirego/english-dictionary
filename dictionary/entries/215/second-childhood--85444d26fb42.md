### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The period or state of cognitive decline of an elderly person, characterized by childlike judgment and behavior.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Source synonyms:** dotage; senescence; senility

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A childlike state in any adult, resulting from mental illness, trauma, or other conditions.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Source synonyms:** dementia
