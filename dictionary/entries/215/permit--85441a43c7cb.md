### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To allow (something) to happen, to give permission for.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To allow (someone) to do something; to give permission to.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To allow for, to make something possible.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To allow, to admit (of).

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To grant formal authorization for (something).

   **Usage:** transitive; pronounced like noun

   **Example:** The Building Department permitted that project last week.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To attempt to obtain or succeed in obtaining formal authorization for (something).

   **Usage:** transitive; pronounced like noun

   **Example:** We've been busy permitting the State Street development.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To hand over, resign (something to someone).

   **Usage:** archaic; rare
