<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A forceful emission of something, especially in small drops or particles; a spluttering or sputtering.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** a splutter of water from a pipe

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

3. A noisy commotion.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively

   **Source synonyms:** controversy

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Chaotic and forceful speaking, verbal exchange, etc.; (countable) an instance of this.

   **Usage:** figuratively; uncountable

   **Example:** a splutter of rage from the old man

   **Source synonyms:** sputter
