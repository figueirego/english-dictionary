### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Someone who threads something; one who makes or provides strings, especially for bows.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Someone who strings someone along.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A horizontal timber that supports upright posts, or supports the hull of a vessel.

   **Source synonyms:** string; stringboard; stringpiece

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The side rail supporting the rungs of a ladder or the steps of a flight of stairs.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; carpentry; construction; manufacturing

   **Source synonyms:** stile; rail; string; stringboard; stringpiece

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** journalism; media
