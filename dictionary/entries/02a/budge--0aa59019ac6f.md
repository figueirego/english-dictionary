### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To move; to be shifted from a fixed position.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** I’ve been pushing this rock as hard as I can, but it won’t budge an inch.

   **Source context (verbatim):** in the negative

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To move; to shift from a fixed position.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I’ve been pushing this rock as hard as I can, but I can’t budge it.

   **Source context (verbatim):** in the negative

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To yield in one’s opinions or beliefs.

   **Example:** The Minister for Finance refused to budge on the new economic rules.

   **Source context (verbatim):** in the negative

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To cut or butt (in line); to join the front or middle rather than the back of a queue.

   **Usage:** Canada; Western; Upper Midwestern US; Indiana; Upper Midwestern US; Indiana

   **Example:** Hey, no budging! Don't budge in line!

   **Source context (verbatim):** in the negative

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To try to improve the spot of a decision on a sports field.

   **Source context (verbatim):** in the negative
