## Noun

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ʌn.ɪmˈplɔɪ.mənt/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Forms

- unemployments — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The state of having no job; joblessness.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Unemployment made Jack depressed.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The phenomenon of joblessness in an economy.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Unemployment has been considered a cause of crime.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The level of joblessness in an economy, often measured as a percentage of the workforce.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Unemployment was reported at 5.2% in May, up from 4.9% in April.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A type of joblessness due to a particular economic mechanism.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** All unemployments, seasonal, frictional, cyclical, classical, whatever, mean that you're out of work.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An instance or period of joblessness.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** Until then his life had consisted of low-paying jobs, numerous unemployments, and drug use.

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** joblessness; worklessness; unwork

**Source antonyms:** employment; reemployment
