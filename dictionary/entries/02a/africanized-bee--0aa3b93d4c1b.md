### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A hybrid of the western honey bee (Apis mellifera), produced originally by crossbreeding of the East African lowland honey bee (A. m. scutellata) with various European honey bee subspecies such as the Italian honey bee (A. m. ligustica) and the Iberian honey bee (A. m. iberiensis), and known for being defensive and aggressive.

   **Source synonyms:** Africanized honey bee; killer bee (colloquial)
