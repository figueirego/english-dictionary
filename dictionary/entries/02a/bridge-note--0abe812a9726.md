### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Synonym of bridge loan; a short-term financing tool that provides immediate cash to bridge the gap between two financial events (e.g., helping a startup get quick funds before a big investment round, often converting to equity later).

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance

   **Source synonyms:** bridge loan (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A Folgezettel; a note that connects two seemingly separate ideas or clusters of notes by explaining the why and how of their relationship, acting as a conceptual bridge to reveal new insights, rather than just linking one to the other.
