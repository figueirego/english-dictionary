### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To open (something that was clenched).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The baby stopped crying and unclenched her fists.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To relax, especially one's muscles.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Bill, take some deep breaths and unclench.
