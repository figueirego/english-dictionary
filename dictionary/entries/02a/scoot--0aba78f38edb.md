### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To walk or travel fast; to go quickly.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To run away hastily; scram.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** You'd better scoot before my mother gets home and sees that broken window!

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To move with the forelegs while sitting, so that the floor rubs against its rear end.

   **Example:** The dog was scooting all over our new carpet.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of an animal

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To move sideways (especially along a seat for multiple people), usually to make room for someone else (to sit, stand, etc.).

   **Usage:** intransitive; often

   **Example:** Do you mind scooting a bit to the left?

   **Example:** Would you mind scooting over for me?

   **Source context (verbatim):** with over

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To dispatch someone or something at speed; Synonym of shoo.

   **Usage:** transitive
