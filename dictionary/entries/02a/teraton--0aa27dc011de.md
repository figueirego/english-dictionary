### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A measure of the strength of an explosion or a bomb based on how many trillion tons of TNT would be needed to produce the same energy.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A measure of the weight of an object based on how many trillions of tons it weighs.
