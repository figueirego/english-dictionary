## Adjective · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of or relating to a residue; residual; left over, when the main portion has been removed.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. With respect to a will, relating to the portion of an estate which was not disposed of in the will, or for which the directions in the will could not be carried out.

   **Usage:** not-comparable
