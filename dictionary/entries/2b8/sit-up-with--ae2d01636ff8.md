### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To stay awake past one's usual time in order to care for or provide company to (someone).

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To remain with (someone) through the night, especially with an ill or troubled person.

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To remain with (a recently deceased person) all night, ostensibly to keep animals from the corpse.
