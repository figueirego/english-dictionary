### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make a humble request to (someone, especially a person in authority); to beg, to beseech, to entreat.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** implore; obsecrate; petition; plead; pray (obsolete); sifflicate; thig (obsolete)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To make a humble request to (someone, especially a person in authority); to beg, to beseech, to entreat.
   1. Of a member of the university, or an alumnus or alumna of another university seeking a degree ad eundem&#58; to make a formal request (to the university) that an academic degree be awarded to oneself.

      **Usage:** archaic; specifically; transitive; Oxford University

      **Source synonyms:** implore; obsecrate; petition; plead; pray (obsolete); sifflicate; thig (obsolete)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To make a humble request to (someone, especially a person in authority); to beg, to beseech, to entreat.
   1. To make a humble request to (a deity or other spiritual being) in a prayer; to entreat as a supplicant.

      **Usage:** specifically; transitive

      **Example:** to supplicate the Deity

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** lifestyle; religion

      **Source synonyms:** implore; obsecrate; petition; plead; pray (obsolete); sifflicate; thig (obsolete)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To ask or request (something) humbly and sincerely, especially from a person in authority; to beg or entreat for.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to supplicate blessings on Christian efforts to spread the gospel

   **Source synonyms:** solicit

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To humbly request for something, especially to someone in a position of authority; to beg, to beseech, to entreat.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source synonyms:** sifflicate; thig (obsolete)
