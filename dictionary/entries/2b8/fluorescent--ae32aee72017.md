### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of or relating to fluorescence.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Exhibiting or produced by fluorescence.

   **Example:** The fluorescent plants shimmered in the darkness.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Emitting visible light as a result of the excitation of phosphors by ultraviolet photons produced by the passage of an electrical current through an inert gas infused with mercury.

   **Example:** The quality of fluorescent lighting technology has improved dramatically in recent years.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Vivid, as if fluorescing; neon.

   **Example:** Her shirt was fluorescent orange.
