### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. That or those belonging to us.
   1. Excluding the person(s) being addressed (exclusive ours)

      **Example:** You can't take that! It's ours!

      **Example:** Could we ask you to move, please. These seats are ours.

      **Example:** This beach is a favourite of ours.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. That or those belonging to us.
   1. Including the person(s) being addressed (inclusive ours).

      **Example:** We should ask for it back. After all, it is ours.

      **Example:** Don't you think that ours looks much nicer than theirs?

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. That or those belonging to any entity that the speaker is a part of or identifies with, such as place of employment or education, nation, region, language, etc.

   **Example:** French food is better than ours.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. That or those belonging to people in general.

   **Example:** As we get older, maintaining an active social life should be a priority of ours.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. That or those belonging to everyone being addressed.

   **Usage:** colloquial

   **Example:** Good evening everyone. How are we all tonight? I know I've got my happy face on; have we all got ours on too?

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. That or those belonging to an individual being addressed; used especially to a person in the speaker's care, or to whom advice or instruction is being given.

   **Usage:** colloquial; may seem patronising

   **Example:** Hello Mrs Miggins. How are we feeling this morning? Oh, what's that bottle of tablets under our chair? Is that ours?

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Used to imply connection between the speaker's experiences or activities and a group of listeners.

   **Usage:** colloquial

   **Example:** Hey guys, in this video I'll show you how I make my upside-down sponge cake. There are lots of recipes for these, but ours is the best.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Our house or home.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** You can stay the night at ours if you like.
