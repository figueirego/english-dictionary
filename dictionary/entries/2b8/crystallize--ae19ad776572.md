### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make something form into crystals.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I crystallized the copper sulfate by slowly cooling a saturated solution.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To assume a crystalline form.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The copper sulphate crystallized from solution.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To give a definite or precise form to (something).

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** After some thought, I crystallized my ideas for the paper.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To take a definite form.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

   **Example:** My ideas crystallized overnight.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To coat something with crystals, especially with sugar.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** We crystallized the fruit by coating in sugar.
