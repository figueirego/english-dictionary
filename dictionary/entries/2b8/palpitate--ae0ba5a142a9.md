### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To beat strongly or rapidly; said especially of the heart.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** When he just looks at me, my heart begins to palpitate with excitement.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To cause to beat strongly or rapidly.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The allergy medicine palpitates my heart.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To shake tremulously

   **Usage:** intransitive
