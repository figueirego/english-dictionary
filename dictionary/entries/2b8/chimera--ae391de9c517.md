### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Alternative letter-case form of Chimera, a supposed monster in Lycia with the head of a lion, body of a goat, and tail of a dragon or serpent, killed by the hero Bellerophon.

   **Usage:** Greek; alt-of

   **Example:** The game includes a bunch of chimeras, with the lion heads shooting fire and the snake tails whipping around trying to bite the player.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; mysticism; mythology; philosophy; sciences

   **Alternative form of:** [Chimera](../0b0/chimera--2c16e3683d77.md)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any fantastic creature combining parts from different animals.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** art; arts; human-sciences; mysticism; mythology; philosophy; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A foolish, incongruous, or vain thought or product of the imagination.

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Anything composed of very disparate parts.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** He built his car one piece at a time, producing a chimera with the head of a Volkswagen, the body of a Geely, and the tail of an aftermarket Porsche.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A grotesque like a gargoyle, but without a spout for rainwater.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** architecture

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. An organism with genetically distinct cells originating from two or more zygotes.

   **Example:** The DNA test returned a false negative because the killer was a chimera, having absorbed her twin in utero.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; genetics; medicine; natural-sciences; sciences

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Alternative form of chimaera, a cartilaginous marine fish in the subclass Holocephali and especially the order Chimaeriformes, with a blunt snout, long tail, and a spine before the first dorsal fin.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences; zoology
