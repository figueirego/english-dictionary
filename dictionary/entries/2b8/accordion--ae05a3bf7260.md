<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A box-shaped musical instrument played by compressing or expanding its bellows while pressing its buttons or its keys, causing pallets to open, which allow air to flow across strips of brass or steel (reeds).

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A vertical list of items that can be individually expanded and collapsed to reveal their contents.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; graphical-user-interface; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Something, or set of things (concepts, etc), which can be expanded (or extended) and collapsed, like the musical instrument's pleated, folding bellows.

   **Usage:** attributive; figuratively
