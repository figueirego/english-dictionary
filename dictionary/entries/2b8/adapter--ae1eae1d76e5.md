### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who is capable of adapting to differing situations.

   **Example:** He was an able adapter, and could easily adjust to the differences when the company changed ownership.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One who adapts a thing, e.g. a play.

   **Example:** The critic gave rave reviews to the adapter of the ancient play, who worked to give the text more relevance to the modern day.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A device or application used to achieve operative compatibility between devices that otherwise are incompatible.

   **Example:** He had an adapter that let him plug his phone into the car's cigarette lighter for power.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A device or application used to achieve operative compatibility between devices that otherwise are incompatible.
   1. Specifically, a device that permits two or more plugs to be used at a single electrical power point.

      **Example:** The wall outlet sprouted an electrical monstrosity of adapters plugged into adapters that sparked ominously.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A device or application used to achieve operative compatibility between devices that otherwise are incompatible.
   1. Specifically, a device that allows one format of plug to be used with a different format of socket.

      **Example:** We bought adapters to use our three-prong plugs in the two-prong, unpolarized outlets of the old house.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A device or application used to achieve operative compatibility between devices that otherwise are incompatible.
   1. Specifically, an AC adapter&#58; a device that reduces voltage and converts AC to DC to allow a battery-powered device to use mains power.

      **Example:** I lost my cellphone's adapter so I couldn't recharge it.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A collection of low-rank matrices which, when added to a base model, produces a fine-tuned model; a LoRA.

   **Usage:** machine learning; artificial intelligence; machine learning; artificial intelligence
