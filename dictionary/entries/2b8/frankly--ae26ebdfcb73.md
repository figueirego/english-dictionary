### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In a frank or candid manner, especially in a way that may seem too open, excessively honest, or slightly blunt.

   **Example:** to speak frankly

   **Example:** He spoke frankly about the economy.

   **Source synonyms:** candidly; forthcomingly; honestly; truthfully; bluntly; candidly; outright; earnestly; for real; for realsies (slang); forthcomingly; frankly; genuinely; honestly; legitimately; no cap (African-American-Vernacular-English); openly; simply; sincerely; singly; straight up; truly; truthfully

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In truth, to tell the truth.

   **Usage:** sentence adverb

   **Example:** Most of what they said was, frankly, a pack of lies.

   **Source synonyms:** as a matter of fact; to be frank; truth to tell; above a bit; actually; all things considered (idiomatic); as a matter of fact; by right (obsolete); certainly; come to think of it (idiomatic); de facto; definitely; de jure; fact is (idiomatic); forsooth (archaic; poetic); frankly; honestly; in actuality; in all honesty; indubitably; in fact; in faith (archaic); in point of fact; in reality; in truth; indeed; legitimately; literally; not gonna lie; properly; really; right; simply; soothfast (obsolete); soothly (archaic); surely; to be frank; to be honest; TBH; tbh; to tell the truth (idiomatic); truly; truthfully; undoubtedly; verily (archaic); very much; wis (dialectal)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To a degree large enough as to be plainly evident.

   **Example:** frankly septic

   **Example:** frankly psychotic

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences
