## Adjective

### Pronunciation

### Forms

- most disdainful — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Showing contempt or scorn; having a pronounced lack of concern for others viewed as unworthy.

   **Example:** He was disdainful of those he thought of as the little people. He openly sneered at them. They mocked him behind his back.

   **Example:** She glimpsed at the people whom she had left behind, and smirked in the most disdainful manner towards them.
