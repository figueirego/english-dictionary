**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Abbreviation of and company "X &amp; Co." means X and their companions or associates, commonly as the proper name of a corporation but also informally as a group of people.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of

   **Example:** It might be time for Rishi &amp; Co. to start looking for new jobs.
