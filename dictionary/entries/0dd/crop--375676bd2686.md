### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A plant, grown for it, or its fruits or seeds, to be harvested as food, livestock fodder, or fuel or for any other economic purpose.

   **Example:** The farmer had to decide which crop to grow as his main bet for the coming year. Would it be barley, oats, or something else?

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** agriculture; business; lifestyle

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The production amount of such an output for a specific season or year, particularly of plants.

   **Example:** It was a good crop of oats this year. What a nice change after last year's crop!

   **Source synonyms:** harvest; yield

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A group, cluster, or collection of things occurring at the same time.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** The decade produced a whole crop of ideas about space travel.

   **Example:** The university had an exceptional crop of graduates in 1892, including three who went on to win Nobel Prizes.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A group of vesicles at the same stage of development in a disease.

   **Example:** The patient had a crop of bumps indicative of chicken pox.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The lashing end of a whip.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. An entire short whip, especially as used in horse-riding.

   **Source synonyms:** hunting crop; riding crop; whip; bat

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A rocky outcrop.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. The act of cropping.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A photograph or other image that has been reduced by removing the outer parts.

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

**Source sense R1.S11**
