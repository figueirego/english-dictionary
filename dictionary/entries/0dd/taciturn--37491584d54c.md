### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Silent; temperamentally untalkative; disinclined to speak.

   **Example:** The two sisters could hardly have been more different, one so boisterous and expressive, the other so taciturn and calm.

   **Source synonyms:** reticent; untalkative

   **Source antonyms:** garrulous; loquacious; talkative; prolix
