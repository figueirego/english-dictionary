### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To flip over; to rotate top to bottom.

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** Turn the box upside down and look at the bottom.

   **Source synonyms:** flip over; turn over

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To thoroughly examine.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** They turned the house upside down looking for the car keys.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To alter (something) significantly, often in a negative way.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive
