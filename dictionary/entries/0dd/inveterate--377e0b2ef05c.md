### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Firmly established from having been around for a long time; of long standing.

   **Example:** an inveterate disease

   **Example:** an inveterate habit

   **Source synonyms:** long-standing; deep-rooted; ingrained; ineradicable; indelible; radicated; chronic; permanent

   **Source antonyms:** transient; temporary; momentary; fleeting; impermanent; shifting; acute; novel

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Having had a habit (usually a bad habit) for a long time.

   **Example:** an inveterate idler; an inveterate gambler; an inveterate smoker

   **Example:** an inveterate traveller

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a person

   **Source synonyms:** hardened; chronic; dyed-in-the-wool

   **Source antonyms:** casual; sometime; occasional; dilettante

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Malignant; virulent; spiteful.
