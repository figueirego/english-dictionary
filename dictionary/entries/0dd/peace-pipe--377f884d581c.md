### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A tobacco pipe smoked ceremonially in certain Native American cultures that symbolizes peace among those who smoke it, and among whom it is passed around from person to person.

   **Example:** to smoke the peace pipe

   **Source synonyms:** calumet
