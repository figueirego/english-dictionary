### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A device composed of an odd number of NOT gates whose output oscillates between two voltage levels, representing true and false. The NOT gates, or inverters, are attached in a chain; the output of the last inverter is fed back into the first.
