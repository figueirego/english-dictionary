### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To heat (a substance) to remove its impurities and refine it.

   **Usage:** historical; transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** alchemy; pseudoscience

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To heat (a substance) without melting in order to drive off water, etc., and to oxidize or reduce it; specifically, to decompose (carbonates) into oxides, and, especially, to heat (limestone) to form quicklime.

   **Usage:** physical; transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

   **Source synonyms:** calcinate (obsolete); chark

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To heat (something) to dry and sterilize it.

   **Usage:** broadly; transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To purify or refine (something).

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To burn up (something) completely; to incinerate; hence, to destroy (something).

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** calcinize (obsolete)

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Of a substance&#58; to undergo heating so as to oxidize it.

   **Usage:** intransitive; physical

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences
