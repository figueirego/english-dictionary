## Adjective · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Affected by dampness or mould; damp, mildewed, mouldy.

   **Source synonyms:** fusty

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Having an odour or taste of mould; also (generally), having a stale or unfresh odour or taste.

   **Example:** musty food musty furniture a musty odour

   **Source synonyms:** fusty

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Characteristic of or relating to mould or mouldiness.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Of attitudes, ideas, writing, or other abstract things&#58; no longer fresh or interesting; outdated, stale.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Source synonyms:** outmoded

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Of a person&#58; boring and unadventurous; also, old-fashioned, stuck in the past.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Source synonyms:** fogeyish

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Bad-tempered, grumpy, irritable.

   **Usage:** figuratively; archaic except Northern England (northwest)
