## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The property of being sedentary.
   1. The habit of spending most of one's time physically at rest, sitting or lying down, etc. (Includes circumstances of being mentally busy but not physically busy).

      **Usage:** uncountable

      **Source context (verbatim):** of a person

      **Source synonyms:** sedentarism

      **Source antonyms:** activeness; activity; hustle; busyness; exercise
