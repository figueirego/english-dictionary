### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An astringent salt, usually occurring in the form of pale crystals, much used in the dyeing and tanning trade and in certain medicines, and now understood to be a double sulphate of potassium and aluminium (K₂SO₄·Al₂(SO₄)₃·24H₂O).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any similar double sulphate in which either or both of the potassium and aluminium is wholly or partly replaced by other univalent or tervalent cations.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; inorganic-chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences
