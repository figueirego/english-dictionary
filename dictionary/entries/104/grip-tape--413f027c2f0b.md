### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Sandpaper with an adhesive on one side, used to provide added friction for skateboards, sports clubs and bats, etc.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
