### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A woman with school-age children, likely boys, especially one who spends time transporting them between sporting activities.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A stereotypical woman, typically middle-class, who is overprotective of her children and often overambitious and bragging about their success in sports.
