## Noun · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /əˈpɛln̩t/ — source labels: UK

### Forms

- appellants — plural
- appellaunt — alternative; obsolete

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A litigant or party that is making an appeal in court.

   **Example:** The appellant made her submissions to the court.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

   **Source synonyms:** plaintiff in error

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. One who makes an earnest entreaty of any kind.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. One who challenges another to single combat.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. One of the clergy in the Jansenist controversy who rejected the bull Unigenitus issued in 1713, appealing to a pope "better informed", or to a general council.

   **Usage:** historical
