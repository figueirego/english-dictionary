## Adverb

### Forms

- an all — alternative; informal; regional
- n'all — alternative; informal; regional
- anaw — alternative; informal; regional

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Including every object, attribute, or process associated with preceding item or series of items.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; not-comparable

   **Example:** He ate the whole fish, bones and all.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used to suggest certain unstated relevant implications of what has been stated.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; informal; not-comparable

   **Example:** What with you saying he was sick and all, I figured neither of you were coming.

   **Source synonyms:** and all that; and that; and stuff; and so on; et cetera

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used to add emphasis.

   **Usage:** Northern-England; Scotland; not-comparable

   **Example:** He starts yelling and we come running to help, but a fat load of thanks we get and all!

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. As well; in addition.

   **Usage:** UK; informal; not-comparable

   **Example:** I'll have some of the red ones, some green ones, and them yellow ones and all.
