### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To puzzle, perplex, baffle, bewilder (somebody); to afflict by being complicated, contradictory, or otherwise difficult to understand.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** It confused me when I went to the office and nobody was there, but then I realised it was Sunday.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To mix up, muddle up (one thing with another); to mistake (one thing for another).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** People who say "hola" to Italians are confusing Italian with Spanish.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To mix thoroughly; to confound; to disorder.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The dense fog utterly confused traffic on the highway.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To make uneasy and ashamed; to embarrass.

   **Usage:** dated; transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To rout; discomfit.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To be confused.

   **Usage:** intransitive
