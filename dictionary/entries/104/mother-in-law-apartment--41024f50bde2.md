### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A small apartment attached to or carved out of a nominally single-family house, ostensibly intended for occupancy by a mother-in-law or other relative, but potentially also rented out to a stranger.

   **Example:** "Accessory dwelling units are commonly referred to as mother-in-law apartments. Accessory dwelling units (ADUs) are a second dwelling on a piece of property where a primary residence already exists." —King County (Washington)

   **Source synonyms:** in-law unit; granny annexe; granny flat; granny room; secondary suite; casita; garden apartment; ADU
