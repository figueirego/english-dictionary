### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Consisting of air.

   **Example:** an airy substance; the airy parts of bodies

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Relating or belonging to air; high in air; aerial.

   **Example:** an airy flight

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Open to a free current of air; exposed to the air; breezy.

   **Example:** an airy situation

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Resembling air; thin; unsubstantial; not material; airlike.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Relating to the spirit or soul; delicate; graceful.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Not based on reality; having no solid foundation

   **Source synonyms:** empty; trifling; visionary

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Light-hearted; vivacious

   **Source synonyms:** sprightly; flippant; superficial

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Having an affected manner; being in the habit of putting on airs; affectedly grand.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. Having the light and aerial tints true to nature.

   **Usage:** painting

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. Spacious, well lit, well ventilated.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a room or building
