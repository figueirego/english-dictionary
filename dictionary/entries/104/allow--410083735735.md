### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To let one have as a suitable share of something.

   **Usage:** ditransitive

   **Example:** to allow a servant his liberty; to allow a free passage; to allow one day for rest

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To present something as possible or reasonable.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The event allows of only one interpretation.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To permit, to give permission to.

   **Usage:** catenative; transitive

   **Example:** I will allow my son to be absent.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To not bar or obstruct.

   **Example:** Although I don't consent to their holding such meetings, I will allow them for the time being.

   **Example:** Smoking (is) allowed only in designated areas.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To acknowledge, accept the truth of; to concede; to accede to an opinion; to say something one agrees on in the context of a larger disagreement or reluctance.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to allow a right; to allow a claim; to allow an appeal

   **Example:** You must allow that disagreeing about politics puts a strain on a relationship.

   **Example:** "Well, that rug does match the wallpaper," he allowed.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To grant (something) as a deduction or an addition; especially to abate or deduct.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to allow a sum for leakage

   **Example:** How much will you allow on my old jalopy?

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To make an allowance, to take into account when making plans.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** When calculating a budget for a construction project, always allow for contingencies.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To render physically possible.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To praise; to approve of; hence, to sanction.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. To sanction; to invest; to entrust.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. To like; to be suited or pleased with.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s12"></a>

**Source sense R1.S12**

12. To decide (a request) in favour of the party who raised it; to grant victory to a party regarding (a request).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s13"></a>

**Source sense R1.S13**

13. To forgo bothering with, to let slide.

   **Usage:** Multicultural-London-English; transitive
