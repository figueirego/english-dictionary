## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The electronic noise generated by the thermal agitation of the charge carriers (usually the electrons) inside an electrical conductor at equilibrium, which happens regardless of any applied voltage.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** Johnson noise; Nyquist noise; thermal noise
