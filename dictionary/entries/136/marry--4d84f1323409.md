### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To enter into the conjugal or connubial state; to take a husband or a wife.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Neither of her daughters showed any desire to marry.

   **Example:** I forfeited my inheritance and married outside my parents' faith.

   **Source synonyms:** get married; wed

   **Source antonyms:** divorce

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To enter into marriage with one another.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Jack and Jenny married soon after they met.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To take as husband or wife.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** In some cultures, it is acceptable for an uncle to marry his niece.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To arrange for the marriage of; to give away as wife or husband.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He was eager to marry his daughter to a nobleman.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To unite in wedlock or matrimony; to perform the ceremony of joining spouses; to bring about a marital union according to the laws or customs of a place.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** A justice of the peace will marry Jones and Smith.

   **Example:** His daughter was married some five years ago to a tailor's apprentice.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To acquire by marriage.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I wanted to marry wealth and power, and got both.

   **Source synonyms:** marry into

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To join or connect. See also marry up.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

   **Example:** There’s a big gap here. These two parts don’t marry properly.

   **Example:** I can’t connect it, because the plug doesn’t marry with the socket.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of inanimate or abstract things

**Source sense R1.S8**

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** The attempt to marry medieval plainsong with speed metal produced interesting results.
