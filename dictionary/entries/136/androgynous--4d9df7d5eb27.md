### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Possessing the sex organs of both sexes.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Pertaining to a feature or characteristic that is not definitively of either sex.

   **Example:** Nicknames like Pat, Sam, and Chris are androgynous, used by both men and women.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Possessing qualities of both sexes.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Of a connector or docking port, able to connect to another connector or port of the same type, rather than being restricted to connecting to solely male or solely female connectors or ports.

   **Example:** APAS is an androgynous docking system, so – in principle, at least – any APAS-equipped vehicle should be able to hook up with any other, without having to worry about matching male with female ports.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computer-hardware; computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences
