### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of a blade, such as a sword or knife, sharp on both edges.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Beneficial and harmful at the same time; assisting or contributing in one manner while also creating risk or negative consequences.

   **Usage:** comparable; figuratively

   **Example:** Slander is a double-edged weapon&#58; you can hurt the reputation of another but it diminishes your own reputation that you debase yourself so much.
