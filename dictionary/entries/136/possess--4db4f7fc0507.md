### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To have (something) as, or as if as, an owner; to have, to own.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He does not even possess a working telephone.

   **Source synonyms:** inhold

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of an idea, thought, etc.&#58; to dominate (someone's mind); to strongly influence.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of a supernatural entity, especially one regarded as evil&#58; to take control of (an animal or person's body or mind).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** They thought he was possessed by evil spirits.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Of a person&#58; to control or dominate (oneself or someone, or one's own or someone's heart, mind, etc.).

   **Usage:** also; literary; poetic; reflexive; transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Of a person&#58; to control or dominate (oneself or someone, or one's own or someone's heart, mind, etc.).
   1. To dominate (a person) sexually; to have sexual intercourse with (a person).

      **Usage:** also; literary; poetic; reflexive; transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To cause an idea, thought, etc., to strongly affect or influence (someone); to inspire, to preoccupy.

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive

   **Example:** What on earth possessed you to go walking by the quarry at midnight?

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To occupy the attention or time of (someone).

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To obtain or seize (something); to gain, to win.

   **Usage:** also; archaic; literary; transitive

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. Chiefly followed by of or with&#58; to vest ownership of something in (oneself or someone); to bestow upon, to endow.

   **Usage:** also; archaic; reflexive; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** seise

   **Source antonyms:** dispossess; unpossess

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**
