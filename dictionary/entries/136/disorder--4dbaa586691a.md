### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Absence of order; state of not being arranged in an orderly manner.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** After playing the children left the room in disorder.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A disturbance of civic peace or of public order.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The class was thrown into disorder when the teacher left the room

   **Example:** The army tried to prevent disorder when claims the elections had been rigged grew stronger.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A physical or mental malfunction.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** Bulimia is an eating disorder.
