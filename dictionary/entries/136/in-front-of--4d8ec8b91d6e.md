## Preposition

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. At or near the front part of (something).

   **Example:** There’s a bus stop in front of the faculty entrance, right on the same side of the road.

   **Example:** I jerked the little girl back from in front of a speeding car just in time.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In the presence of, in view of (someone).

   **Example:** Don't fight in front of the children!

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Located before, ahead of, previous to (someone or something).

   **Example:** I'll take the one in front of the black one.

   **Example:** Write ‘Professor’ in front of her name.
