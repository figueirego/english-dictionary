### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To strike or pierce with the beak or bill (of a bird).

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** The birds pecked at their food.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To form by striking with the beak or a pointed instrument.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to peck a hole in a tree

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To strike, pick, thrust against, or dig into, with a pointed instrument, especially with repeated quick movements.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To seize and pick up with the beak, or as if with the beak; to bite; to eat; often with up.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To do something in small, intermittent pieces.

   **Example:** He has been pecking away at that project for some time now.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To type by searching for each key individually.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To type in general.

   **Usage:** rare

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To kiss briefly.
