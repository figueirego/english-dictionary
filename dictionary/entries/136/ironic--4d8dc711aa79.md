### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Characterized by or constituting (any kind of) irony.

   **Example:** It's somewhat ironic to have a wave of smog right on Earth Day.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a situation

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Characterized by or constituting (any kind of) irony.
   1. Odd or coincidental; strange.

      **Usage:** proscribed

      **Example:** It's ironic that we are eating a sandwich in Sandwich, Massachusetts.

      **Source context (verbatim):** of a situation

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Acting in an unserious and teasing manner.

   **Usage:** preceded with "being"

   **Example:** Don't take it personally. We're just being ironic.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Done in an insincere and mocking manner; satirical.

   **Usage:** usually; action

   **Example:** You should assume that everything this guy posts is ironic.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a statement
