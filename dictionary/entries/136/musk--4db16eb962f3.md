### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A greasy secretion with a powerful odour, produced in a glandular sac of the male musk deer and used in the manufacture of perfumes.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A similar secretion produced by the otter and the civet.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A synthetic organic compound used as a substitute for the above.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The odour of musk.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Body odor, especially when aroused or unwashed.

   **Usage:** countable; slang; uncountable; vulgar

   **Example:** I was so excited I could smell my own musk.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A musk deer (genus Moschus).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A muskflower (Erythranthe moschata).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A plant of the genus Erodium (Erodium moschatum); the musky heronsbill.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A plant of the genus Muscari; grape hyacinth.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. A small tree or tall shrub, Olearia argophylla, of south-eastern mainland Australia and Tasmania, with leaves that produce a musky aroma.

   **Usage:** Australia; countable; uncountable
