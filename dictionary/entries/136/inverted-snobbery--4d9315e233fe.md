## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A form of snobbery where the practitioner deprecates the target for attributes that would normally be considered desirable (particularly wealth).

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** His dislike of the residents of the wealthier neighbourhood was simply inverted snobbery.

   **Source synonyms:** inverse snobbery
