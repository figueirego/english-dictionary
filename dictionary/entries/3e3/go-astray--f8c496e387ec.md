### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To develop bad habits; to behave improperly or illegally.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** If you keep hanging out with that gang you'll go astray.

   **Source synonyms:** break bad; go bad

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To behave in an adulterous manner.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The woman thought her husband had gone astray.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To come to believe an untruth.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To become lost or mislaid.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source context (verbatim):** of an object

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To be undesirable or unhelpful.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** A pinch of salt in this dish wouldn't go astray.

   **Source context (verbatim):** in the negative

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see go, astray.

   **Example:** I went astray and ended up lost.
