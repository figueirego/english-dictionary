### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A (permanent) enclosure from which water can be expelled, in order to give access to underwater areas for engineering works etc.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A structure with similar function that is temporary.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

   **Source synonyms:** cofferdam; coffer

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A coffer (sunken panel).

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** architecture

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport
