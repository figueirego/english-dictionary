### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To hit; to successfully land a hit.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** His opponent danced and dodged so nimbly that he couldn’t strike a blow.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To do something to support or defend (for) or to hinder or harm (at / against) something.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** The Supreme Court ruling struck a blow for gender equality.

   **Example:** It is hoped that the new vaccine will strike a blow against the spread of tuberculosis.
