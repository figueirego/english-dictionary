### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A medical condition caused by advanced infection with the human immunodeficiency virus (HIV), characterized by a low CD4⁺ cell count or a range of clinical presentations (such as specific opportunistic infections and cancers).

   **Usage:** uncountable
