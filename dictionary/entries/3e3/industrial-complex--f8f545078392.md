## Noun · Etymology 2 · Record 2

### Forms

- industrial complexes — plural

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A network of entwined interests between individuals and institutions in the private sector and the public sector resulting in coordination in order to achieve mutually beneficial results, especially to the detriment of broader society.

   **Usage:** in-compounds; usually

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**
