### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A gravity-based device used to store and rotate round food and drink in a first-in-first-out manner.

   **Example:** After collecting them each day from the chicken coop, she put the eggs in her skelter until she needed them.
