## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Based on plants; especially, of a diet, consisting mostly or wholly of foods derived from plants; vegan.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** I only eat plant-based food.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Made from plants other than the traditional source.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** plant-based pizza crust

   **Example:** plant-based pasta
