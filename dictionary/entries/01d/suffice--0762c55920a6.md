### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To be enough or sufficient; to meet the need (of anything); to be adequate; to be good enough.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** For this plum cake, two eggs should suffice.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To satisfy; to content; to be equal to the wants or demands of.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** A joint of lamb sufficed even his enormous appetite.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To furnish; to supply adequately.

   **Usage:** archaic

   **Example:** During the festival, the temple suffices food to the beggars.

   **Example:** The king sufficed his army with food and weapons before the great battle.
