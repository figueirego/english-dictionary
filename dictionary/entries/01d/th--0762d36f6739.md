   **Example:** the 4th/Fourth of July

   1. Used to form a term denoting the ordinal numeral corresponding to the value, being a natural number, of a mathematical expression.

      **Usage:** morpheme

      **Example:** The nth term of a geometrical progression whose first term is a and common ratio is r is given by ar\#123;n-1\#125;.
