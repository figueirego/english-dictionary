## Adjective · Record 2

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Consisting of loose leaves (pages), often collected in a binder, rather than being bound like a book or stapled.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** All the pages fell out of my looseleaf notebook.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Of tea&#58; supplied as whole tea leaves rather than teabags.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. Of lettuce&#58; having loosely bunched leaves.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Looseleaf lettuce is popularly used in salads.
