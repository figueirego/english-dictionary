### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A popular explanation for the origin of a term which has been rejected as false by expert etymologists.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Many English folk etymologies involve backronyms.

   **Example:** The spelling in 'sovereign' was influenced by folk-etymology association with 'reign'.

   **Source synonyms:** etymythology; pseudoetymology; paraetymology; paretymology

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A modification of a word or its spelling resulting from a misunderstanding of its etymology, as with island, belfry, and hangnail.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** popular etymology
