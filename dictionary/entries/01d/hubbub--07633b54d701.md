<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A confused sound of a crowd of people shouting or speaking simultaneously; an uproar.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source synonyms:** hubbuboo; hue and cry; racket; tumult

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Noisy confusion; commotion, uproar; (countable) an instance of this; an ado, a commotion.

   **Usage:** broadly; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** hubbuboo; tumult; ado; argy-bargy (UK); bangarang; bobbery (dated); brouhaha; bunfight; bustle; bust-up; chaos; ferment; clamour; clatter; commotion; disruption; disturbance; flap; flutter; foofaraw; fracas; furor; fuss; hobbleshaw (obsolete); hoo-ha; hoo-hah; hubbub; hullabaloo; hurly-burly; kerfuffle; mayhem; merry hell; outcry; palaver; pandemonium; pother; quarrel; riot; rowdydow (archaic); ruckus; rumpus; shemozzle; shindy (slang); splatterdash; stir; stooshie (Scotland); three-ring circus; to-do; tomfoolery; trouble; tumult; turmoil; unrest; uproar; upstir

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A sound of people making a battle cry or war cry.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete
