### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An Italian soup consisting of a meat broth and small shreds of an egg-based mixture, prepared by drizzling the mixture into boiling broth and stirring.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A type of Italian gelato (milk-based ice cream) with chocolate shavings.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of several types of cheese often pulled into shreds.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any of several types of cheese often pulled into shreds.
   1. Ellipsis of stracciatella di bufala.
