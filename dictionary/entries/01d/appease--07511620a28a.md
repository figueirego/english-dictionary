### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make quiet; to calm; to reduce (something) to a state of peace; to dispel (anger, hatred, etc.).

   **Example:** to appease the tumult of the ocean

   **Source synonyms:** calm; pacify; quell; quiet; still; lull

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To make conciliatory offerings or concessions to (someone) in an attempt to dispel their anger, aggression, etc.; to adapt to the demands of; to come to terms with.

   **Example:** They appeased the angry gods with burnt offerings.

   **Source synonyms:** mollify; placate; propitiate
