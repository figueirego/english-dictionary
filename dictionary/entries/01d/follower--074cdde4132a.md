### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who follows, comes after another.

   **Usage:** literally

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Something that comes after another thing.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. One who is a part of master's physical group, such as a servant or retainer.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. One who follows mentally, adherer to the opinions, ideas or teachings of another, a movement etc.

   **Source synonyms:** adherent; disciple

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An imitator, who follows another's example.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A pursuer.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. An account holder who subscribes to see content from another account on a social media platform.

   **Usage:** social media

   **Example:** I have over 50 followers on Twitter, but all my tweets only get 10 likes.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A machine part receiving motion from another.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A machine part receiving motion from another.
   1. A tappet.

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. A man courting a maidservant; a suitor.

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. Young cattle.

<a id="r1-s12"></a>

**Source sense R1.S12**

12. A metal piece placed at the top of a candle to keep the wax melting evenly.

<a id="r1-s13"></a>

**Source sense R1.S13**

13. Any of the three players (the ruckman, ruck rover, and rover) who usually follow the ball around the ground rather than occupying a fixed position.
