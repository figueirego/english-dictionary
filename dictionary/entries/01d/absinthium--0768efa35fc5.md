### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Common wormwood (Artemisia absinthium), an intensely bitter herb used in the production of absinthe and vermouth, and as a tonic.

   **Usage:** archaic; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
