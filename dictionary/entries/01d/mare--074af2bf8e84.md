<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A type of evil spirit formerly thought to sit on the chest of a sleeping person; also, the feeling of suffocation felt during sleep, attributed to such a spirit (likely a folkloric interpretation of sleep paralysis).

   **Usage:** historical; obsolete

**Source sense R2.S2**

   **Usage:** Commonwealth; Ireland; UK; colloquial
