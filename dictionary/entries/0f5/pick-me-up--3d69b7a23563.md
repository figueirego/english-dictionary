## Noun

### Forms

- pick-me-ups — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A drink, often an alcoholic one, taken as a stimulant or sometimes as a hangover cure; a restorative, tonic or bracer.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** Strong tea is just the pick-me-up that I need right now.

   **Example:** He brought a hip flask along, just in case he needs a little pick-me-up later.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Something that improves one's mood.

   **Example:** A trip into town for lunch with my friend was a nice pick-me-up today.

**Source synonyms:** picker-upper
