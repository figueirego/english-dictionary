### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈblaɪn.dəd/ — source labels: UK; US
- IPA: /ˈblɑɪn.dɪd/ — source labels: Australia

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Deprived of sight; thus&#58;
   1. Deprived of sight in a way that is or may be permanent, by damage to the eyes or brain.

      **Usage:** not-comparable

      **Example:** blinded by perinatal vertical transmission of gonorrhea

      **Source synonyms:** [blind](../396/blind--e5b84c89f624.md); sightless

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Deprived of sight; thus&#58;
   1. Deprived of sight temporarily, by being either dazzled or blindfolded.

      **Usage:** not-comparable

      **Example:** blinded by the light

      **Source synonyms:** dazzled; blindfolded

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. Lacking intellectual discernment, as for example because of greed or stupidity.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. Willingly prevented from knowing certain information that, were it known, might bias an outcome or decision (either consciously or unconsciously).

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** sciences

   **Source synonyms:** masked; [blind](../396/blind--e5b84c89f624.md)

   **Source antonyms:** nonblinded; open-label

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. Covered by blinds.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** blinded windows

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. Displaying a particular destination or route number on the blinds.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The bus was blinded for route 100 to the city centre.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a bus
