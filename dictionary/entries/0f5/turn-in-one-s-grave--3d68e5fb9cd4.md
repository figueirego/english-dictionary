### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To be appalled, offended or disgusted by something, despite being deceased.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** Beethoven is probably turning in his grave at the way that rock group mangled his Ninth Symphony.

   **Example:** The new scientific discovery about the gravitation particle could make Isaac Newton turn in his grave.

   **Source synonyms:** roll in one's grave; roll over in one's grave; spin in one's grave
