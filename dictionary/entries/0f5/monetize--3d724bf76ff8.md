### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To convert something (especially a security) into currency.

   **Usage:** British; English; Oxford; US

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To mint money.

   **Usage:** British; English; Oxford; US

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To establish a currency as legal tender.

   **Usage:** British; English; Oxford; US

   **Source antonyms:** demonetize

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To make an activity profit-generating, particularly in computer and Internet-related activities.

   **Usage:** British; English; Oxford; US; transitive

   **Example:** We considered monetizing our computer help forums by inserting ad banners.
