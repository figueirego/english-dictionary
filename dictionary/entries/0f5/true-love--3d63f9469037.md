## Noun

### Forms

- true loves — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Faithful love.
   1. Feelings of love that are adhered to faithfully, or the faithful manifestation of such feelings.

      **Usage:** uncountable; originally

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Faithful love.
   1. A lover who is faithful to the object of his or her affection or faithfully loved themselves.

      **Usage:** countable; originally

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Love that is in some sense purer or more unique than ordinary love.
   1. The form of romantic affection that is considered pure and wholly positive, not just based on feelings of lust and sex.

      **Usage:** uncountable; by later reinterpretation

      **Example:** Some believe that true love doesn’t exist.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Love that is in some sense purer or more unique than ordinary love.
   1. The unique individual for whom one feels such affection.

      **Usage:** countable; by later reinterpretation

      **Example:** He was my one true love.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Love that is in some sense purer or more unique than ordinary love.
   1. The situation in which a couple is perfectly compatible and there is no better relationship, as set by a greater force such as God or fate.

      **Usage:** uncountable; by later reinterpretation

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A thing for which a person feels an intense love.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** Cinematography is my only true love.
