### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The speed of light viewed as an uncrossable barrier to acceleration from subluminal and superluminal speeds in special relativity.

   **Example:** Tachyons exist on the other side of the Einstein barrier.
