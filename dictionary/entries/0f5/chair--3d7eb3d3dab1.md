### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An item of furniture used to sit on or in, comprising a seat, legs or wheels, back, and sometimes arm rests, for use by one person.

   **Example:** All I need to weather a snowstorm is hot coffee, a warm fire, a good book and a comfortable chair.

   **Example:** I sat on my chair to have dinner.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Clipping of chairperson.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; clipping; often; with-definite-article; also written Chair

   **Example:** Under the rules of order adopted by the board, the chair may neither make nor second motions.

   **Example:** She will be the first lady to serve as chair of the committee.

   **Alternative form of:** [chairperson](../395/chairperson--e57854055350.md)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The post or position of chairperson.

   **Usage:** usually; with-definite-article

   **Example:** The meeting began, with Mr Roberts in the chair.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The seating position of a particular musician in an orchestra.

   **Example:** My violin teacher used to play first chair with the Boston Pops.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

**Source sense R1.S5**

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** education
