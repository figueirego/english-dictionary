### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The fact or quality of having a position in space.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The features or surroundings of a particular place.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** a rural locality

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The condition of being local.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The situation or position of an object.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An area or district considered as the site of certain activities; a neighbourhood.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Limitation to a county, district, or place.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A built-up area, i.e. a city, town or village.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; natural-sciences

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; quantum mechanics
