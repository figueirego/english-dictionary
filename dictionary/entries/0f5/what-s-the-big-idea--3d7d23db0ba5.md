## Phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used to express surprise or dissatisfaction with an action or statement of another person, especially to that person.

   **Usage:** US; colloquial; often preceded by hey

   **Example:** I heard you been chatting up my girl. What's the big idea?

   **Example:** Hey, what's the big idea, you telling everybody I'd left town?
