### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A sewer.

   **Usage:** figuratively; sometimes

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The opening in reptiles, amphibians and birds, as well as elasmobranchians, lobe-finned fishes, and monotreme mammals, which serves as the common outlet for the urogenital ducts and rectum.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; biology; medicine; natural-sciences; sciences; zoology
