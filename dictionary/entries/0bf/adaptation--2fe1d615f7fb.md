### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The process of adapting something or becoming adapted to a situation; adjustment, modification.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A change that is made or undergone to suit a condition or environment.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The process of replacing a given morpheme of a source lexeme with an equivalent morpheme of the target lexeme, especially where the two morphemes are cognates.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The process of change that an organism undergoes to be better suited to its environment.

   **Usage:** uncountable; evolutionary theory

   **Source antonyms:** maladaptation

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An instance of an organism undergoing change, or the structure or behavior that is changed.

   **Usage:** countable; evolutionary theory

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The process of adapting an artistic work from a different medium.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. An artistic work that has been adapted from a different medium.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** authorship; broadcasting; communications; film; journalism; literature; media; publishing; television; writing

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. The means by which social groups adapt to different social and physical environments.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; sciences; social-science; sociology
