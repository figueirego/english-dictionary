### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A plant or flower of the repeat-blooming Chinese rose species Rosa chinensis.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A plant or flower of one of the class of hybrids developed from Rosa chinensis.
