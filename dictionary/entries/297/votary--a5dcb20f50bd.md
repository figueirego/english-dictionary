## Noun · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈvəʊtəri/ — source labels: UK
- IPA: /ˈvoʊdəri/ — source labels: US

### Forms

- votaries — plural

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A person, such as a monk or nun, who lives a religious life according to vows they have made.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. A devotee of a particular religion or cult.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. A devout or zealous worshipper.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. Someone who is devoted to a particular pursuit etc.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Source synonyms:** enthusiast; addict; buff; aficionado; amateur; enthusiast; devotee; fancier; fan; fiend; freak; junkie; maniac; votary; zealot

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. A loyal supporter or devoted admirer of a person or institution.
