### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An approach shot made from a location a short distance from the green in which the ball is launched into the air with only low or moderate force, so that it will land on the green and roll toward the hole.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** golf; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A relatively easy field goal, made from a short distance.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** American-football; ball-games; football; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A shot in which the ball is kicked from underneath with accuracy but with less than maximum force, to launch it high into the air in order either to pass it over the heads of opponents or to score a goal.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; games; hobbies; lifestyle; soccer; sports

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A takeout shot aimed at hitting a rock at an angle.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; curling; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s5"></a>
