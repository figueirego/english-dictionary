### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A period into which a year is divided, historically based on the phases of the moon.

   **Example:** July is my favourite month.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A period of 30 days, 31 days, or some alternation thereof.

   **Example:** We went on holiday for two months.

   **Example:** I lived there (for) over a month.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
