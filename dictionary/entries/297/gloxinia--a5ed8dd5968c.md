### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of the three species of South American plants of the genera Gloxinia in family Gesneriaceae.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Brazilian gloxinia (Sinningia speciosa), a plant species formerly classified in the genus Gloxinia, in the family Gesneriaceae.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Creeping gloxinia (Lophospermum erubescens), in the family Plantaginaceae.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**
