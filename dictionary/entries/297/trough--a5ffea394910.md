### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A long, narrow container, open on top, for feeding or watering animals.

   **Example:** One of Harriet's chores was to slop the pigs' trough each morning and evening.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any similarly shaped container.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any similarly shaped container.
   1. A rectangular container used for washing or rinsing clothes.

      **Usage:** Australia; New-Zealand

      **Example:** Ernest threw his paint brushes into a kind of trough he had fashioned from sheet metal that he kept in the sink.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A short, narrow canal designed to hold water until it drains or evaporates.

   **Example:** There was a small trough that the sump pump emptied into; it was filled with mosquito larvae.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An undivided metal urinal (plumbing fixture)

   **Usage:** colloquial

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A gutter under the eaves of a building; an eaves trough.

   **Usage:** Canada

   **Example:** The troughs were filled with leaves and needed clearing.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A channel for conveying water or other farm liquids (such as milk) from place to place by gravity; any ‘U’ or ‘V’ cross-sectioned irrigation channel.

   **Usage:** Australia; New-Zealand

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** agriculture; business; lifestyle

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A long, narrow depression between waves or ridges; the low portion of a wave cycle.

   **Example:** The buoy bobbed between the crests and troughs of the waves moving across the bay.

   **Example:** The neurologist pointed to a troubling trough in the pattern of his brain-waves.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A low turning point or a local minimum of a business cycle.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** economics; sciences

   **Source antonyms:** peak
