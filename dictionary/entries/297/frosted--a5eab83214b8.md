## Adjective · Record 1

### Forms

- more frosted — comparative
- most frosted — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Covered in frost; frosty.

   **Example:** Eiswein is made from frosted grapes.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Translucent, appearing to be covered in frost.

   **Example:** She installed frosted windows, since there was a clear view of her bathroom from her neighbor's bedroom.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Damaged by frost.

   **Example:** Frosted fruit will be unsalable.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Extremely intoxicated.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Example:** I was so frosted after drinking that 12-pack.
