## Adverb

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. By others; through others. Said of a proposition held to be true because it follows from other true propositions.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** If humans are mortal, and Socrates is a human, then per alia Socrates is mortal.
