### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An internal combustion engine with eight cylinders arranged in two banks of four in a V-shaped configuration, usually as part of a single engine block.

   **Example:** He spent extra for a V8 because he doubted that a straight-six would be enough for towing his trailer.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A vehicle with such an engine.

   **Usage:** dated; metonymically

   **Example:** He rolled up in a brand-new V8.
