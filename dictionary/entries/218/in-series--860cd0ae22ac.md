### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One after another. Sequentially.

   **Example:** The experiments were done in series.

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of components&#58; connected in a chain, instead of in parallel.

   **Example:** The total resistance of two resistors connected in series is easily calculated as the simple sum.
