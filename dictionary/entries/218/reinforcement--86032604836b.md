### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act, process, or state of reinforcing or being reinforced.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A thing that reinforces.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** There's a website that can be a good learning reinforcement.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Additional troops or materiel sent to support a military action.

   **Usage:** countable; in-plural; uncountable

   **Example:** Send in the reinforcements!

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The process whereby a behavior with desirable consequences comes to be repeated.

   **Usage:** uncountable; behavioral psychology

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A small round white sticker placed around a punched hole in a piece of paper to prevent the binder's rings from tearing through the paper.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
