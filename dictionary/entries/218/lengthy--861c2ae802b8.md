### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having length; long and overextended, especially in time rather than dimension.

   **Example:** a lengthy text

   **Example:** a lengthy discussion

   **Example:** a laborious and lengthy process

   **Example:** She died last night after a lengthy illness.

   **Example:** They received lengthy prison terms.
