### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. According to what is right, or what one is legally or morally entitled to; justly, properly, rightfully.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** The post of vice-president should, by right, have been given to John.

   **Source synonyms:** fairly; in fairness; in justice

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In reality; correctly, to tell the truth, truthfully.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; obsolete
