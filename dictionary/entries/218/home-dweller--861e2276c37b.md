### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A resident or occupant of a specific household.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A homebody; someone who enjoys spending time in their household or staying in.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A long-term inhabitant or permanent settler of a property, rather than a traveler, temporary lodger, or a vagrant.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>
