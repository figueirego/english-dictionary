### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The natural light that is ambient in daytime, being mostly sunlight (both direct and indirect, on either sunny days or cloudy days).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** In those days the entire factory was lit only by daylight and was closed at night.

   **Source synonyms:** sunlight; sunshine

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A light source that simulates daylight.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The intensity distribution of light over the visible spectrum generated by the Sun under various conditions or by other light sources intended to simulate natural daylight.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The period of time between sunrise and sunset.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** burning daylight

   **Example:** wasting daylight

   **Example:** We should get home while it's still daylight.

   **Source synonyms:** daytime; day; daylight; daylength; daytime; photoperiod

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** We had only two hours to work before daylight.

**Source sense R1.S9**

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

   **Example:** We completely agree. There's no daylight between us on the issue.
