### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An absorbent garment worn around the crotch that retains the wearer's urine and feces, often worn by a baby or young child who is not yet toilet trained, by an adult who is incontinent (who wets the bed), or under extreme working conditions without access to a toilet; a nappy.

   **Usage:** Canada; India; Philippines; US; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** cloth diaper, disposable diaper, nighttime diaper, swim diaper

   **Source synonyms:** nappy; napkin; pampers; diapie

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A container that absorbs and collects (retains) liquid or waste material, similar to a diaper (noun sense 1).

   **Usage:** Canada; US; colloquial; countable; humorous; sometimes; uncountable

   **Example:** engine diaper, meat diaper, period diaper, rubber diaper

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A piece of clothing that resembles the shape of a diaper (noun sense 1) but lacks the absorbency.

   **Usage:** Canada; US; colloquial; countable; derogatory; humorous; possibly; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A textile fabric having a repeating pattern, especially of diamonds or flowers, formed by alternating directions of thread.

   **Usage:** countable; historical; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** diamond twill

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A repeating geometrical or (often stylized) floral pattern, usually of small diamonds evenly spaced, that decorates a flat surface, sometimes in bas-relief; diaperwork.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; decorative arts; embroidery; decorative arts; embroidery

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** architecture; art; arts

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A repeating geometrical or floral pattern, used to cover the surface of a shield and forming the ground for any charges.

   **Usage:** countable; historical; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; heraldry; hobbies; lifestyle; monarchy; nobility; politics

   **Source synonyms:** fur

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A towel, napkin, or tablecloth made from the diaper fabric (noun sense 4).

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable
