### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The property of being false.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A false statement, especially an intentional one; a lie.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** Don't tell falsehoods.

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Mendacity, deceitfulness; the trait of lying.
