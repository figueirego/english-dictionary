## Noun · Record 3

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈɛkstɹə/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Forms

- extras — plural

### Senses

<a id="r3-s1"></a>

**Source sense R3.S1**

1. Something additional, such as an item above and beyond the ordinary school curriculum, or added to the usual charge on a bill.

   **Source synonyms:** addition; supplement

<a id="r3-s2"></a>

**Source sense R3.S2**

2. An extra edition of a newspaper, which is printed outside of the normal printing cycle, for example to report an important late-breaking event.

   **Example:** Extra, extra! Read all about it!

<a id="r3-s3"></a>

**Source sense R3.S3**

3. A run scored without the ball having hit the striker's bat - a wide, bye, leg bye or no ball.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; cricket; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

   **Source synonyms:** sundry

<a id="r3-s4"></a>

**Source sense R3.S4**

4. A supernumerary or walk-on in a film or play.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** acting; broadcasting; entertainment; film; lifestyle; media; television; theater

<a id="r3-s5"></a>

**Source sense R3.S5**

5. The state or trait of being over the top, of behaving in an overly dramatic manner.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Example:** Stop! I can't deal with all your extra today!

**Source sense R3.S6**

6. Something of an extra quality or grade.
