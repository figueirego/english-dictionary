### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The part of the day from noon or lunchtime until sunset, evening, or suppertime or 6pm.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The later part of anything, often with implications of decline.

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A party or social event held in the afternoon.

   **Usage:** informal
