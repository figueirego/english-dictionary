## Noun

### Forms

- copperheads — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of certain of snakes having a copper-colored head in three families within suborder Serpentes.
   1. A venomous pit viper of species Agkistrodon contortrix, found in parts of North America.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of certain of snakes having a copper-colored head in three families within suborder Serpentes.
   1. A water moccasin (Agkistrodon piscivorus)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of certain of snakes having a copper-colored head in three families within suborder Serpentes.
   1. A Chinese copperhead (Deinagkistrodon acutus), a venomous pit viper species found in Southeast Asia

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any of certain of snakes having a copper-colored head in three families within suborder Serpentes.
   1. Any Australian copperhead (Austrelaps spp.), a venomous elapid found in southern Australia and Tasmania.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Any of certain of snakes having a copper-colored head in three families within suborder Serpentes.
   1. A copperhead rat snake (Coelognathus radiatus, syn. Elaphe radiata), a non-venomous colubrid species found in southern Asia.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Someone with ginger hair.

   **Usage:** derogatory
