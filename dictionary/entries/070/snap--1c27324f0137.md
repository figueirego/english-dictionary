## Interjection · Record 3

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /snæp/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Forms

- snap! — canonical

### Senses

<a id="r3-s1"></a>

**Source sense R3.S1**

1. The cry used in a game of snap when winning a hand.

<a id="r3-s2"></a>

**Source sense R3.S2**

2. "I've got one the same!", "Me too!"

   **Usage:** Australia; UK; broadly

   **Example:** Snap! We've both got pink buckets and spades.

<a id="r3-s3"></a>

**Source sense R3.S3**

3. Used to express agreement.

   **Usage:** UK; broadly

<a id="r3-s4"></a>

**Source sense R3.S4**

4. Used in place of an expletive to express surprise, usually in response to a negative statement or news; often used facetiously.

   **Usage:** Canada; US

   **Example:** I just ran over your phone with my car. —Oh, snap!

<a id="r3-s5"></a>

**Source sense R3.S5**

5. Used after something is said by two people at exactly the same time.

   **Usage:** Australia; New-Zealand; UK

   **Example:** —Wasn't that John? —Wasn't that John? —Snap!
