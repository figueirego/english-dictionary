### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Pertaining to the reduction of a decree etc.; rescissory.

   **Usage:** archaic; Scots law

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Causing the physical reduction or diminution of something.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. That reduces a substance etc. to a more simple or basic form.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; chemistry; economics; engineering; metallurgy; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. That can be derived from, or referred back to, something else.

   **Usage:** archaic; historical

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. That reduces an argument, issue etc. to its most basic terms; simplistic, reductionist.

   **Usage:** now frequently derogatory
