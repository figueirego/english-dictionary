### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of equipping, or the state of being equipped, as for a voyage or expedition.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any items used in equipping something or someone, for example things needed for an expedition or voyage.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** We need more gardening equipment if we want to properly landscape our yard, not just a rake and some gloves.

   **Example:** The sports shop sells different kits of famous clubs, and equipment from all kind of sports.
