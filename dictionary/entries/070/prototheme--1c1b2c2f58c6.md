## Noun

### Forms

- protothemes — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The first element of a name composed of two traditional name roots.

   **Example:** The Old Norse given names Þorsteinn and Þorgeirr both contain the prototheme Þor- ("Thor-").

### Related words

**Source antonyms:** deuterotheme — antonym(s) of “first element of a name”
