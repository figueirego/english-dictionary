### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A projector of a bright beam of light for use in theatres and studios; a flood

   **Example:** The floodlights bathed her in a pool of light.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
