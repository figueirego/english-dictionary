### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Formerly and habitually or repeatedly, but possibly no longer, did.

   **Usage:** no-past-participle; no-present-participle

   **Example:** You never used to like soap operas, did you? I used to like that band long ago, and I was surprised I still do. I used to know a guy from the UK who pronounced "mother" with the "r". It used to be me who ran the company. There used to be open fields here. Now it's a shopping mall.

   **Example:** I always used to go to the seaside in August as a child.

   **Example:** He used to live here, but moved away last year.

   **Example:** The club used to be frequented by locals; then, after the "incident", it used to get raided by the cops.

   **Example:** My wife used not to be so hot-tempered.
