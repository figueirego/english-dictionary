### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Synonym of excursus.
   1. A fuller discussion (usually in a separate section at the end of a chapter or book) of a particular part of the text of a work, especially a Bible commentary or classical Greek or Latin text.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Synonym of excursus.
   1. An act of departing from the main narrative in speech or writing to discuss an incidental issue; a digression.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An act of moving or rushing forward or out; specifically (military) an attack or incursion by troops; a foray, a sally, a sortie.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A chance or way to move out from a place.

   **Usage:** obsolete
