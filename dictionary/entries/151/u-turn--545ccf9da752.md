## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

- U-turns — plural
- u-turn — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A turn in a vehicle carried out by driving in a semicircle in order to travel in the opposite direction.

   **Source synonyms:** uey

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A reversal of policy or opinion.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** The government have done a U-turn and are now supporting greater European integration instead of opposing it.

   **Source synonyms:** about-face; flipflop; flip-flop; backflip; turnaround; turnabout; row-back
