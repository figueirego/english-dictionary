### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person, especially a child, who moves slowly.

   **Usage:** Ireland; UK; informal

   **Example:** Hey, you slowcoaches at the back! Get a move on!

   **Source synonyms:** plodder; sluggard; slowpoke (North-America)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person who is slow on the uptake; one who does not comprehend new ideas quickly.

   **Usage:** Ireland; UK; informal
