### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A knot, knob, protuberance or swelling.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The point where the orbit of a planet, as viewed from the Sun, intersects the ecliptic. The ascending and descending nodes refer respectively to the points where the planet moves from South to North and N to S; their respective symbols are ☊ and ☋.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astronomy; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A leaf node.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A computer or other device attached to a network.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; networking; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The point at which the lines of a funicular machine meet from different angular directions.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

   **Source synonyms:** knot

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The point at which a curve crosses itself, being a double point of the curve. See crunode and acnode.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A similar point on a surface, where there is more than one tangent-plane.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A vertex or a leaf in a graph of a network, or other element in a data structure.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** graph-theory; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A hard concretion or incrustation which forms upon bones attacked with rheumatism, gout, or syphilis; sometimes also, a swelling in the neighborhood of a joint.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. A point along a standing wave where the wave has minimal amplitude.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. The knot, intrigue, or plot of a dramatic work.

   **Usage:** rare

<a id="r1-s12"></a>

**Source sense R1.S12**

12. A hole in the gnomon of a sundial, through which passes the ray of light which marks the hour of the day, the parallels of the Sun's declination, its place in the ecliptic, etc.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; technical

<a id="r1-s13"></a>

**Source sense R1.S13**

13. The word of interest in a KWIC, surrounded by left and right cotexts.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computational; computing; engineering; human-sciences; linguistics; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s14"></a>

**Source sense R1.S14**

14. A region of an electric circuit connected only by (ideal) wires (i.e. the voltage between any two points on the same node must be zero).

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; electrical-engineering; electricity; electromagnetism; electronics; energy; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s15"></a>

**Source sense R1.S15**

15. A point in a parse tree that can be assigned a syntactic category label.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences; syntax

<a id="r1-s16"></a>

**Source sense R1.S16**

16. A point in a cladogram from which two clades branch, representing the presumed ancestor.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences
