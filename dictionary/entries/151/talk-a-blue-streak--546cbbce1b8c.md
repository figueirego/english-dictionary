### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To talk for a long duration of time, at a rapid pace without giving others a chance to speak, or to the point of tedium.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; informal

   **Example:** She's normally a quiet person, but she will talk a blue streak about fashion, if you let her.
