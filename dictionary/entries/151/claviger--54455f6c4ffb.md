**Source sense R1.S1**

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A group within genus Lasius (formerly considered a subgenera Acanthomyops) of bright yellow to orange species of ant that have 3 maxillary palps and give off a distinctive citronella odor when disturbed. The group name comes from their club-shaped palps.
