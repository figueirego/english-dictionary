### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person or thing that causes something to circulate.

   **Example:** Who is the circulator of this rumor?

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A local transit system that provides regular service within a closed loop.

   **Example:** You can ride the airport circulator to the next terminal.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** transport

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A passive electronic component with three or more ports, in which the ports can be accessed in such a way that when a signal is fed into any port it is transferred to the next port only.
