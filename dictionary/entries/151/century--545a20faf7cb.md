### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A period of one hundred consecutive years; often specifically a numbered period with conventional start and end dates, e.g., the twentieth century, which stretches from (strictly) 1901 through 2000, or (informally) 1900 through 1999. The first century AD was from 1 to 100.

   **Source synonyms:** age; centennium; yearhundred; centenary

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A unit in ancient Roman army, originally of one hundred army soldiers as part of a cohort, later of more varied sizes (but typically containing sixty to seventy or eighty) soldiers or other men (guards, police, firemen), commanded by a centurion.

   **Source synonyms:** centuria

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A political division of ancient Rome, meeting in the Centuriate Assembly.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A hundred things of the same kind; a hundred.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A hundred runs scored either by a single player in one innings, or by two players in a partnership.

   **Example:** He has scored 44 limited-overs centuries.

   **Example:** They put on a century stand to help their team recover from 57–4 to 198–4.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; cricket; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A score of one hundred points.

   **Example:** That was his tenth professional century.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; games; hobbies; lifestyle; snooker; sports

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A race a hundred units (as meters, kilometres, miles) in length.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A banknote in the denomination of one hundred dollars.

   **Usage:** US; informal
