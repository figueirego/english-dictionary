## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A city in Charente-Maritime department, Nouvelle-Aquitaine, southeastern France, the former capital of the Saintonge province.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A French Catholic diocese named after the above, its see.

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A French arrondissement named after the above, its capital.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**
