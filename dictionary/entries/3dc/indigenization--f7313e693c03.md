### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of making something or someone more indigenous; adaptation to native or local culture.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
