### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The point of complete depletion, of the state of being used up.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** We worked the mine to exhaustion, there's nothing left to extract.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Supreme tiredness; having exhausted energy.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** I ran in the marathon to exhaustion, then I collapsed and had to be carried away.

   **Source synonyms:** fatigue; tiredness; exhaustedness; exhaustion; frazzle; wornness; weariness

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The removal (by percolation etc) of an active medicinal constituent from plant material.

   **Usage:** dated; uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The removal of all air from a vessel (the creation of a vacuum).

   **Usage:** dated; uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An exhaustive procedure

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences
