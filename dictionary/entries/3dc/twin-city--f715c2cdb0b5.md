## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One of two cities in close proximity to each other.

   **Example:** Texarkana, Arkansas, and Texarkana, Texas, are twin cities forming the Texarkana metropolitan area.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A city that has been twinned with another more distant city.

   **Source synonyms:** sister city; twin town
