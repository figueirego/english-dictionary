### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of deflecting or something deflected.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Russell's goalbound shot took a deflection off a defender and went out for a corner.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Deviation (of a needle or other indicator or mechanism) from a previous position.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The act of refusing to address something (questions, criticism, etc.).

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The act of rejecting culpability by redirecting blame elsewhere.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; psychology; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Bending or deformation under load.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A tactic that forces an opposing piece to leave the square, rank or file it occupies, thus exposing the king or a valuable piece

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** board-games; chess; games
