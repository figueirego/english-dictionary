## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /tɛstɪˈməʊni.əl/ — source labels: UK
- IPA: /tɛstɪˈmoʊni.əl/ — source labels: General-American

### Forms

- testimonials — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A statement, especially one given under oath; testimony.

   **Example:** The witness provided a written testimonial to the court.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A (usually written) recommendation of someone's worth or character.

   **Example:** The company website features a customer testimonial.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A tribute given in appreciation of someone's service etc.

   **Example:** He gave a moving testimonial at the retirement dinner.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A match played in tribute to a particular player (who sometimes receives a proportion of the gate money).
