### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To separate or break down (something) into its components.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Various fungi can decompose wood.

   **Example:** Our team decomposed this task into several subtasks that could be successfully distributed.

   **Source synonyms:** disintegrate; fragment

   **Source antonyms:** compose; assemble; recompose

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To rot, decay or putrefy.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Plastics can take centuries to decompose.

   **Source synonyms:** break down
