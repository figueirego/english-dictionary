## Adverb

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Even if that is the case; whether that is true or not; nevertheless.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; not-comparable

   **Example:** "He doesn't earn much money." "Be that as it may, he's been successful in what he set out to do."
