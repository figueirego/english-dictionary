## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The holiday commemorating (the beginning of) the new year; the holiday time that includes New Year's Eve and New Year's Day.

   **Example:** We spent last New Year's at her parent's house.

   **Example:** We'll be in Paris for New Year's.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The holiday commemorating (the beginning of) the new year; the holiday time that includes New Year's Eve and New Year's Day.
   1. New Year's Eve.

      **Usage:** often; specifically

      **Example:** We love the fireworks and the ball drop on New Year's.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The holiday commemorating (the beginning of) the new year; the holiday time that includes New Year's Eve and New Year's Day.
   1. New Year's Day.

      **Usage:** often; specifically

      **Example:** We love to host an early dinner on New Year's with prime rib.
