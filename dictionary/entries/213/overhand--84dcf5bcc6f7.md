### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Executed with the hand brought forward and down from above the shoulders.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** an overhand shuffle of a deck of cards

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Sewn with close, vertical stitches that draw the edges of a seam together.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; manufacturing; sewing; textiles

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. With the working part on top of the standing part.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a loop in rope

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Laid such that the surface of the wall to be jointed is on the opposite side of the wall from the mason, requiring the mason to lean over the wall to complete the work.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; construction; manufacturing; masonry

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Done from below upward.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; mining
