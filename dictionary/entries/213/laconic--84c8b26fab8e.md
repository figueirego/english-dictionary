### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of speech or writing, communicative through the use of as few words as possible.

   **Source synonyms:** terse; succinct; concise; pithy; lapidary; brief

   **Source antonyms:** verbose; prolix; loquacious; long-winded; bombastic

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of a speaker or writer, communicating through the use of as few words as possible.

   **Source synonyms:** taciturn; untalkative; terse; quiet; spartan

   **Source antonyms:** talkative; chatty; verbose; prolix; loquacious; long-winded; bombastic

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Laid-back; casual; not intense.

   **Usage:** Australia; proscribed; sometimes
