### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of, or relating to microscopes or microscopy; microscopal

   **Example:** We supply all microscopic stains and other materials.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. So small that it can only be seen with the aid of a microscope.

   **Example:** The water was full of microscopic organisms.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Very small; minute

   **Example:** Compared to the galaxy, we are microscopic in scale.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Carried out with great attention to detail.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** The police carried out a microscopic search of the crime scene.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Able to see extremely minute objects.
