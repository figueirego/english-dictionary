### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To fail to care for or attend to something.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to neglect duty or business; to neglect to pay debts

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To omit to notice; to forbear to treat with attention or respect; to slight.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to neglect strangers

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To fail to do or carry out something due to oversight or carelessness.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To ignore for the sake of simplifying calculations without significantly affecting accuracy.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** We can neglect this term, as it approaches zero in the limit anyway.

   **Example:** This problem says to neglect air resistance.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences
