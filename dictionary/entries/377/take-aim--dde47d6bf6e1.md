### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To position oneself and/or one's weapon so as to be aimed specifically at a chosen mark or target (which is indicated after 'at')

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** He took aim at the prize bird meticulously and shot the cock from the distant flock with a single shot.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To direct criticism (towards).

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** He needed someone to blame for the bus accident, so took aim at the mechanics who failed to carry out the checks.
