### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Such as one may avail oneself of; capable of being used for the accomplishment of a purpose.

   **Example:** We have an available candidate.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Readily obtainable.

   **Example:** The list shows the available products in the store.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Valid.

   **Example:** This is an available plea.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Having sufficient power, force, or efficacy to achieve the purpose; availing, effective.

   **Usage:** archaic

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Free to meet someone, speak on the telephone, enter a romantic relationship, or the like.

   **Example:** I have a question for you when you're available.

   **Example:** Hi, this is Mark Smith calling. Is your mother available?

   **Example:** I asked her if she was available, but she said she had a boyfriend.
