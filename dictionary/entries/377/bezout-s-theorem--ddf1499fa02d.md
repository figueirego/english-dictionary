### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A statement in algebraic geometry concerning the number of common zeros of n polynomials in n indeterminates. In its original form, the theorem states that in general the number of common zeros equals the product of the degrees of the polynomials.
