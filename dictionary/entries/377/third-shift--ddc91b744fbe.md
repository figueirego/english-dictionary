### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A regularly scheduled period of work, and the third one in the standard working day of any particular company&#58; usually from midnight to 8&#58;00 a.m.

   **Source synonyms:** graveyard shift; night shift
