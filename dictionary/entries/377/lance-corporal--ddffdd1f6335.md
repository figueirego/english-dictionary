### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An enlisted rank of grade E-3 in the United States Marine Corps, ranking above private first class and below corporal. Abbreviated as LCpl.

   **Usage:** US

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A non-commissioned officer in the British Army and Royal Marines, ranking above private and below corporal. A rank with NATO code OR-3.

   **Usage:** UK
