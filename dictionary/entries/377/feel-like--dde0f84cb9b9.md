### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To have a desire for something, or to do something.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I didn't feel like working yesterday, so I called in sick.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To perceive oneself to resemble (something); to have the sense of being (something).

   **Usage:** idiomatic

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To feel that something is likely to happen; to predict.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I feel like it will rain all week.

   **Example:** She felt like the dog could start biting at any moment.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To feel as though.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To give a perception of something; to appear or to seem.

   **Usage:** impersonal

   **Example:** It felt like rain, but it barely drizzled.

   **Example:** It feels like Gerald is the likely suspect.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Denotes the apparent temperature.

   **Usage:** impersonal

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** climatology; meteorology; natural-sciences
