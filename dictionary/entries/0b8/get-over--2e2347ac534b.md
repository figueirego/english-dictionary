### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To overcome.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** I'm trying to get over my fear of flying.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To recover (from).

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** I'm having problems getting over a bad cold.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To forget and move on; to calm down regarding something.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** She was in love with me for 10 years, and still hasn't got over the fact that the feeling wasn't mutual.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To manipulate (someone); to get around (someone); to improve one's own standing at another's expense.

   **Usage:** slang; transitive

   **Example:** We need a plan to get over Mr. Smith.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To successfully communicate; to get across.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** In our lectures we need to get over the importance of online safety.
