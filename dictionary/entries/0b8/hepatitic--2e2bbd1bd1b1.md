### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Related to hepatitis and other liver diseases.

   **Example:** Hepatitic diseases such as cirrhosis and hepatitis A are preventable through a healthy lifestyle including vaccination and good diet.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Related to the laboratory samples and enzyme levels in the liver.

   **Example:** HIV+ patients really have to be careful and get their hepatitic levels checked every four months.
