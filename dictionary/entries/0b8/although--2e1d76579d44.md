### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Though, even though, in spite of or despite the fact that&#58; introducing a clause that expresses a concession.

   **Example:** Although it was very muddy, the football game went on.

   **Example:** The patients, (al)though getting stronger, will not come off their medications.

   **Example:** Although proven, the accusation was ignored by the court.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. But, except.

   **Example:** It was difficult, although not as difficult as we had expected.
