### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An outline, boundary or border, usually of curved shape.

   **Example:** the low drag contour of a modern automobile

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A line on a map or chart delineating those points which have the same altitude or other plotted quantity&#58; a contour line or isopleth; (metonymically) the landform or its surface.

   **Source synonyms:** contour line

<a id="r1-s3"></a>
