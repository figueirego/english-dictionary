### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An upright board across the foot of a bedstead.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A board or small raised platform on which to support or rest the feet, such as that found in a carriage.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>
