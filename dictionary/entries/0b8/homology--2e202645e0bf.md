### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The relationship of being homologous; a homologous relationship.
   1. specifically, such relationship in the context of the geometry of perspective.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable; projective geometry

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The relationship of being homologous; a homologous relationship.
   1. An automorphism of the projective plane (representing a perspective projection) that leaves all the points of some straight line (the homology axis) fixed and maps all the lines through some single point (the homology centre) onto themselves.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable; projective geometry

      **Example:** If the homology centre lies on the homology axis, the homology is said to be singular or parabolic; otherwise, it is called non-singular or hyperbolic.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The relationship of being homologous; a homologous relationship.
   1. A general way of associating a sequence of algebraic objects, such as abelian groups or modules, to a sequence of topological spaces; also used attributively&#58; see Usage notes below.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** algebraic-topology; mathematics; sciences; topology

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The relationship of being homologous; a homologous relationship.
   1. Given a chain complex \{Gₙ\} and its associated set of homomorphisms \{Hₙ\}, the rule which explains how each Hₙ maps Gₙ into the kernel of Gₙ₊₁.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Example:** Because of their connection with both homology and cohomology, chain complexes are an important topic of study in homological algebra.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** algebra; mathematics; sciences
