### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To configure or set up (particularly a complex machine with many configuration options \[especially knobs and dials).

   **Example:** Before starting the recording, make sure you've dialled in the right synthesiser settings.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To connect (to a system or service) by telephone.

   **Example:** I tried dialling in to the server, but I couldn't connect.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To join or participate in (a conference call).

   **Example:** Did you dial in to that earnings announcement?

   **Source synonyms:** call in

   **Source antonyms:** dial out

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To apprehend and align oneself (to an idea, circumstance, way of thinking, etc.).

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** The company quickly dialled in to the fact that customers wanted greater convenience.

   **Example:** Nowadays most people are dialled in to the idea that smoking is bad for you.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To reduce (something) to an appropriate level.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** He was pushy, but he dialed that in until they found him tolerable.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To achieve a perfect result in brewing coffee by manipulating grind size, water amount, etc.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** cooking; food; lifestyle
