### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who overhelps; a person who helps so excessively or intrusively that they hinder or smother the one being helped.

   **Example:** She meant well, but as an overhelper she left her son unable to do anything for himself.

   **Source synonyms:** smotherer; meddler
