### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. to make something inactive or no longer effective

   **Usage:** ergative

   **Example:** deactivate an account

   **Example:** deactivate a device

   **Example:** The technician deactivated the alarm system before repairs began.

   **Example:** You should deactivate your old account to protect your privacy.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. to prevent the action of a biochemical agent (such as an enzyme)

   **Example:** chemically deactivate

   **Example:** The chemical agent was deactivated by exposure to heat.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. to remove a person or piece of hardware from active military service

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. to commit suicide

   **Usage:** Internet; euphemistic; neologism
