### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The residential area near one's home.

   **Usage:** US; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** He lives in my neighborhood.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The inhabitants of a residential area.

   **Usage:** US; collective; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The fire alarmed the neighborhood.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A formal or informal division of a municipality or region.

   **Usage:** US; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** We have just moved to a pleasant neighborhood.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An approximate amount.

   **Usage:** US; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** He must be making in the neighborhood of $200,000 per year.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The quality of physical proximity.

   **Usage:** US; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The slums and the palace were in awful neighborhood.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The quality of being a neighbor, of living nearby, next to each other; proximity.

   **Usage:** US; countable; obsolete; uncountable

   **Example:** Our neighborhood was our only reason to exchange hollow greetings.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Close proximity; nearness.

   **Usage:** US; countable; dated; uncountable

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. The disposition becoming a neighbor; neighborly kindness or good will.

   **Usage:** US; countable; obsolete; uncountable
