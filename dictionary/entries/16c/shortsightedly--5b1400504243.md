## Adverb

### Forms

- more shortsightedly — comparative
- most shortsightedly — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. With short sight; myopically.

   **Example:** The teacher peered shortsightedly around the classroom.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In a manner that fails to consider the big picture or future effects of an action or decision.

   **Example:** He shortsightedly decided not to go to college and instead entered the workforce; so he made more money the first few years, but his earning power was never as great as it could have been.
