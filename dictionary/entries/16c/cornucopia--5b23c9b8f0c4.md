### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A goat's horn endlessly overflowing with fruit, flowers and grain; or full of whatever its owner wanted&#58; or, an image of a such a horn, either in two or three dimensions.

   **Usage:** Greek; countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; mysticism; mythology; philosophy; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A hollow horn- or cone-shaped object, filled with edible or useful things.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An abundance or plentiful supply.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The store provided a veritable cornucopia of modern gadgets.
