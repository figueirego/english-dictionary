## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A feeding strategy where a predator hunts other predators who have recently fed on prey, thus eating both the prey animal and the prey's own undigested prey/food. The predator chooses prey who have fed recently, and ignores those who have not fed recently.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; ecology; natural-sciences
