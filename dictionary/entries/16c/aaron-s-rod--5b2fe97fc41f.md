### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of various plants with a tall flowering stem, especially&#58;
   1. Verbascum thapsus, the great mullein, common mullein, or hag-taper.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source synonyms:** torchwort; hag-taper; common mullein; great mullein; Adam's flannel; Jacob's staff; Jupiter's staff; cow's lungwort

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of various plants with a tall flowering stem, especially&#58;
   1. Goldenrod, the Solidago genus of North American plants with yellow flowers.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of various plants with a tall flowering stem, especially&#58;
   1. Hylotelephium telephium (syn. Sedum telephium; orpine, livelong, or live-forever).

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A rod-shaped molding decorated with an entwined snake, and sometimes leaves, vines, or scrolls.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** architecture

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A rod with one serpent twined around it, as used by Aaron (differing from the caduceus of Mercury, which has two serpents).

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The penis.

   **Usage:** countable; dated; slang; uncommon; uncountable
