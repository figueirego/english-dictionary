### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The trait of being willing to donate money, resources, or time.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** charity; largesse; liberality; generousness

   **Source antonyms:** stinginess

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A desire to give or share without expecting payment; charity; an eager willingness to demonstrate openness and empathy or to provide a charitable outlook on something; a desire to share either emotionally or in resources, time, etc.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A generous act.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source synonyms:** beau geste

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The trait of being abundant, more than adequate.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** He ate everything, despite the generosity of the portions.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Good breeding; nobility of stock.

   **Usage:** archaic; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** nobility
