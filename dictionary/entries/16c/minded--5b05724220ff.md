### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having or exemplifying a mind of the stated type, nature or inclination.

   **Usage:** in-compounds; usually hyphenated

   **Example:** a fair-minded decision by a traditionally minded Pope.

   **Example:** literary-minded/literature-minded/two-minded

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Having a preference for doing something; having a likelihood, or disposition to carry out an act.

   **Example:** I am minded to refuse the request.

   **Example:** Order another drink if you are so minded

   **Example:** He seems minded to go ahead with the plan.
