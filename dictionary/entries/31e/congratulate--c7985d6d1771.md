### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To express one’s sympathetic pleasure or joy to the person(s) it is felt for

   **Example:** Remind me to congratulate Dave and Lisa on their wedding.

   **Example:** We must congratulate Dave and Lisa on getting married.

   **Source synonyms:** felicitate; compliment

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To consider oneself fortunate in some matter.

   **Usage:** reflexive

   **Example:** I congratulated myself on the success of my plan.
