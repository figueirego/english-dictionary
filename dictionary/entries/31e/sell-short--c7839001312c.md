### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To engage in the process of short selling (selling a stock one does not own on the premise of the stock's price going down).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To underestimate the value of something; to understate its value.

   **Usage:** reflexive; sometimes; transitive

   **Example:** You're selling it short by not mentioning its bonus features.

   **Source synonyms:** underprice; understate; misunderestimate; sell short; underapproximate; underestimate; underguess; underjudge; underrate; underreckon; understate; undersell

   **Source antonyms:** overstate; overvalue; overapproximate; overestimate; overhold; overrate; overreckon; overstate; overthink (rare)
