## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An assumption that the speaker considers very unlikely to be true but temporarily accepts in order to make a hypothetical argument.

   **Example:** Even if we make the heroic assumption that the governor makes this his top priority, it isn't feasible to have new regulation in effect in six months.
