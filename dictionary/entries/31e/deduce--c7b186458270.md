### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To reach (a conclusion) by applying rules of logic or other forms of reasoning to given premises or known facts.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** conclude; infer

   **Source antonyms:** induce

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To examine, explain, or record (something) in an orderly manner.

   **Usage:** transitive; uncommon

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To obtain (something) from some source; to derive.

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To be derived or obtained from some source.

   **Usage:** archaic; intransitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To take away (something); to deduct, to subtract (something).

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

   **Example:** to deduce a part from the whole

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To lead (something) forth.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive
