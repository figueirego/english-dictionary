### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Lying face-down.

   **Example:** prone position

   **Source synonyms:** prostrate; pronated; neveling; nuel

   **Source antonyms:** supine

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of the hand, forearm or foot&#58; turned facing away from the body; with the thumb inward or big toe downward.

   **Example:** The hand is in the prone position typically when using a keyboard; and the forearm is then also in the prone position; when the foot is resting on the inner side of the sole, it is in the prone position.

   **Source synonyms:** pronated

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Having a downward inclination or slope.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Shooting from a position while lying down.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; video-games; war

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Predisposed, liable, inclined, given (to something) (most often, specifically to being befallen by an unsought bad outcome, rather than to undertaking a willful behavior or to having a happy accident).

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** prone to failure
