### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A small café or restaurant typically selling light refreshments along with coffee-based drinks.

   **Example:** He went to the nearby coffee shop with his friends.

   **Source synonyms:** café; coffeehouse; diner

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A café-like establishment selling marijuana in the Netherlands.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Synonym of kopitiam (“traditional eatery”).

   **Usage:** Malaysia; Singapore

   **Source synonyms:** kopitiam (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**
