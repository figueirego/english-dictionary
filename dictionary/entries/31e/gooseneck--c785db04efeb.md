## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Anything with a slender curved shape, resembling the neck of a goose, such as the shaft of some lamps.

   **Usage:** attributive; often

   **Example:** A gooseneck lamp can be swiveled around an object, allowing a good examination of all its surfaces.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The swivel connection on a sailboat located near the bottom of the mast that the boom attaches to. When a sailboat performs a tack or a jibe the gooseneck swings the boom from one side of the boat to the other.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport
