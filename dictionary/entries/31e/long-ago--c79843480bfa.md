## Adverb · Record 1

### Forms

- longer ago — comparative
- longest ago — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. At a time in the past, especially the distant past.

   **Example:** There was a Roman fort here long ago.

   **Example:** How long ago did she die? It's not that long ago that she did.

   **Example:** Long ago, in a far country, there lived a queen who had three princes.
