### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To become steamy.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To become coated with condensation.

   **Example:** The bathroom mirror steamed up when he opened the shower door.

   **Source synonyms:** fog up

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To bring (a steam boiler) up to working steam pressure&#58; to build up a head of steam inside (a boiler).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** It takes about an hour to steam up the locomotive.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. to build up a head of steam.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Engine 99 had been steaming up since 21&#58;00.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To cook by steaming.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I'll steam up a bunch of dumplings.
