## Verb · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈfɪzəl/ — source labels: UK; US

### Forms

- fizzles — present; singular; third-person
- fizzling — participle; present
- fizzled — participle; past
- fizzled — past

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To sputter or hiss.

   **Example:** The soda fizzled for several minutes after it was poured.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To decay or die off to nothing; to burn out; to end less successfully than previously hoped.

   **Usage:** figuratively; informal

   **Example:** The entire project fizzled after the founder quit.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To fail to generate the expected yield when exploded during testing.

   **Example:** The shot fizzled, generating only 200 tons rather than the 30 kilotons they were aiming for.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a nuclear weapon
