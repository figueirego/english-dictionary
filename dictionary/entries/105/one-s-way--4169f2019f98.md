### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Preceded by a verb and followed by an adverbial prepositional phrase to express a successfully completed action in a literal or figurative space, where the verb denotes the means or manner of the action, while the prepositional phrase specifies the direction or goal of the action&#58;
   1. Used to express a physical movement performed by the subject (especially in a forward direction).

      **Example:** to force one's way in

      **Example:** If necessary, I'll bull my way into the reception.

      **Example:** We had to slog our way through muddy fields.

      **Example:** She joked her way into the meeting.

      **Example:** They stumbled their way to the back of the room.
