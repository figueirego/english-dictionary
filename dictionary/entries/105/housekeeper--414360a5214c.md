### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Someone (traditionally a woman) employed to look after the home, typically by managing domestic servants or superintending household management; also someone with equivalent duties in a hotel, institution etc.

   **Example:** She was their third housekeeper, but after a month or so she also gave up.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Someone who manages the running of a home, traditionally the female head of the household.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Someone who keeps to their house; someone who rarely ventures away from home; an unadventurous person, a homebody.

   **Usage:** archaic; colloquial

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Someone who owns a house as a place of residence; a householder.

   **Usage:** archaic
