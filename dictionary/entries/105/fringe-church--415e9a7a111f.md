## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A Christian denomination espousing doctrines or worship customs rejected by the majority of Christians.

   **Usage:** colloquial; derogatory

   **Example:** Millions of people attend fringe church affiliated worship services each day.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A place of worship affiliated with this type of religious denomination.

   **Usage:** colloquial; derogatory

   **Example:** Congregants drove to attend services at a fringe church on Sunday morning.
