### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The incorporation of water molecules into a complex with those of another compound.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; construction; manufacturing
