**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To adhere to an object, without being affixed, in such a way as to follow its contours. Used especially of fabrics and films.

   **Source synonyms:** cleave; stick; adhere; attach; bond; cleave; cling; cohere (formal); conglutinate; glue (figuratively); hug; stick; stick together; stick with

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To cause to adhere to, especially by twining round or embracing.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** cleave; stick; adhere; attach; bond; cleave; cling; cohere (formal); conglutinate; glue (figuratively); hug; stick; stick together; stick with

**Source sense R2.S4**

   **Usage:** transitive
