## Noun

### Forms

- miscomputations — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The incorrect performance of a computation.

   **Example:** His miscomputation of the amount of tax he owed increased his tax bill.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A wrong or incorrect computation.

   **Example:** The miscomputation of the library's weight was the result of not accounting for the weight of the books.
