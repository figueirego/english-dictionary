### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To satisfy, carry out, bring to completion (an obligation, a requirement, etc.).

   **Usage:** US; transitive

   **Example:** You made a promise, son, and now you must fulfill it.

   **Source synonyms:** realize; perform

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To emotionally or artistically satisfy; to develop one's gifts to the fullest.

   **Usage:** US; transitive

   **Example:** This is the most fulfilling work I've ever done.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To obey, follow, comply with (a rule, requirement etc.).

   **Usage:** US; transitive

   **Example:** Unfortunately, you don't fulfill the criteria for extra grants at the present time.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To package, distribute, or ship goods.

   **Usage:** US

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To fill full; fill to the utmost capacity; fill up.

   **Usage:** US; archaic

   **Example:** My lady is positively fulfilled of grace.
