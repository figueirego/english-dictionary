### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The feeling or state of being disappointed&#58; a feeling of sadness or frustration when something is not as good as one hoped or expected, or when something bad unexpectedly happens.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Even a trip to beautiful California can cause disappointment.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An example or the act of disappointing&#58; a circumstance in which a positive expectation is not achieved.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** The disappointment with our trip to California caused bickering.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Something or someone that disappoints&#58; that which causes disappointment.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** Even a trip to beautiful California can be a disappointment.

   **Example:** What a disappointment!

**Source synonyms:** let-down; setback
