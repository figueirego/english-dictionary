### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Advent, the period from Advent Sunday (inclusive) through the start of Epiphany.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Christmastide, Yuletide; the Twelve Days of Christmas, starting on Christmas Day and ending on Epiphany, January 6.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>
