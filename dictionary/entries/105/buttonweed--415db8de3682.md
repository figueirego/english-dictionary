### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of numerous species of plants with button-like flowers, especially&#58;
   1. Any of several plants of the genera Diodia and Hexasepalum (syn. Diodella), of the madder family.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of numerous species of plants with button-like flowers, especially&#58;
   1. False buttonweed (Spermacoce spp.).

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of numerous species of plants with button-like flowers, especially&#58;
   1. Velvetleaf, Indian mallow (Abutilon theophrasti).

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any of numerous species of plants with button-like flowers, especially&#58;
   1. Certain knapweeds (Centaurea spp.).

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Any of numerous species of plants with button-like flowers, especially&#58;
   1. Clustered bluet (Oldenlandia uniflora).

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Any of numerous species of plants with button-like flowers, especially&#58;
   1. Dwarf mallow, common mallow (Malva neglecta).

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Any of numerous species of plants with button-like flowers, especially&#58;
   1. Water buttons (Cotula spp.).
