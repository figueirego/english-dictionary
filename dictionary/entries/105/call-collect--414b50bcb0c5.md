### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make a telephone call in which the cost of the call is paid by the person receiving the call; to place a collect call.

   **Usage:** US; intransitive

   **Example:** In the days of payphones, if you had no coins, you could call collect and hope that the callee would accept the charges.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To call (someone) in this way.

   **Usage:** US; transitive

   **Example:** Call me what you want — just don’t call me collect.

   **Example:** Please call me to let me know you’re safe — you’re welcome to call me collect.
