### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To twist one's body with snakelike motions.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The prisoner managed to squirm out of the straitjacket.

   **Source synonyms:** writhe; wriggle

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To twist in discomfort, especially from shame or embarrassment.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** I recounted the embarrassing story in detail just to watch him squirm.

   **Example:** A tough question that made the press office squirm.

   **Source synonyms:** fidget

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To evade a question, an interviewer etc.
