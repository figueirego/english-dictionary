### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A dummy or other crude representation of a person, group or object that is hated.

   **Example:** In England on Bonfire Night, an effigy is often burned.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A likeness of a person.

   **Example:** tomb effigy

   **Source synonyms:** figure; image; picture
