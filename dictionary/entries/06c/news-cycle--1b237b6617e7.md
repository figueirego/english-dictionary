### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The reporting of a particular media story, or news in general, from the first instance (breaking news) to the last, often including reporting on public and other reactions to the earlier reports, now especially via social media; (originally) the reporting for a particular schedule or deadline in broadcast or print, as in a daily paper.

   **Example:** 24-hour news cycle

   **Example:** to dominate the news cycle
