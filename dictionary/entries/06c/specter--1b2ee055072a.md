### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A ghostly apparition, a phantom.

   **Usage:** US

   **Example:** A specter haunted the cemetery at the old Vasquez manor.

   **Source synonyms:** wraith; spook

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A threatening mental image; an unpleasant prospect

   **Usage:** US; figuratively

   **Example:** The specter of an election-vote recount is this year's political bogeyman.
