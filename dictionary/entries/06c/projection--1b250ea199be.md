**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Something which projects, protrudes, juts out, sticks out, or stands out.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The face of the cliff had many projections that were big enough for birds to nest on.

   **Source synonyms:** protuberance; appendix

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The action of projecting or throwing or propelling something.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The crisis or decisive point of any process, especially a culinary process.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The display of an image by devices such as movie projector, video projector, overhead projector or slide projector.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>
