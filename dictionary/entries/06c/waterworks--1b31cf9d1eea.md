### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The water supply system of a district, town, city, or other place, including reservoirs, pipes, and pumps.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any single facility, such as a filtration plant or pumping station, within such a system.

   **Usage:** treated as singular

   **Example:** She is employed by a waterworks at the outskirts of the city.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Often in the form turn on the waterworks&#58; crying or tears, especially in a way that is considered manipulative or over-emotional.

   **Usage:** figuratively; informal

   **Example:** She turned on the waterworks when we told her the old man was dead, but she was asking questions about the will soon enough.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Rain.

   **Usage:** figuratively; informal

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The genitourinary system.

   **Usage:** UK; euphemistic; figuratively

   **Source synonyms:** plumbing (euphemistic; informal)

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A hydraulic apparatus by which a supply of water is furnished for ornamental purposes; also, an ornamental fountain or waterfall.

   **Usage:** historical; treated as singular

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Engineering works relating to the conveyance and flow of fluids (principally water), such as the collection and distribution of water, drainage, irrigation, etc.

   **Usage:** archaic

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; construction; manufacturing
