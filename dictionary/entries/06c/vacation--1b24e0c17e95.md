### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An extended trip or journey away from home for rest or pleasure.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** We were on vacation in Florida this year.

   **Source synonyms:** holiday

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An extended period of leisure time away from work or school.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Schoolchildren are on vacation this week.

   **Source synonyms:** holiday

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A period during which official activity or business is formally suspended; an official holiday from university, law courts etc.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The act of vacating something; moving out.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The Conservative Party’s vacation of the centre ground gave an opportunity to its opponents.

   **Source synonyms:** departure; moveout

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The act of making legally void.

   **Usage:** US; countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Freedom from some business or activity.

   **Usage:** countable; general; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>
