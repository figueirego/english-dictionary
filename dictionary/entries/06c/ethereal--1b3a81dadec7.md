### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Pertaining to the presupposition of an invisible air-like element permeating all of space, or to the higher regions beyond the earth or beyond the atmosphere.

   **Example:** ethereal space

   **Example:** ethereal regions

   **Source synonyms:** aetheric; etheric; aereous; celestial

   **Source antonyms:** earthly

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Pertaining to the immaterial realm, as symbolically represented by, or (in earlier epochs) conflated with, such atmospheric and extra-atmospheric concepts.

   **Source synonyms:** celestial; incorporeal; insubstantial; intangible; spiritual; uncorporeal; mystical; otherworldly; transcendental; cosmic; universal

   **Source antonyms:** corporeal; corporal; earthly

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Consisting of ether; hence, exceedingly light or airy; tenuous; spiritlike; characterized by extreme delicacy, as form, manner, thought, etc.

   **Source synonyms:** ethereous; delicate; light; aeriform; aereous; aerial (historical); airy; ethereal; gaseous; pneumatic; vaporlike; vaporous; wafty; airy; aery; ethereal; diaphanous (formal); immaterial; insubstantial; tenuous; unreal; unsubstantial (archaic); subtle; tricky; nice; delicate; knotty
