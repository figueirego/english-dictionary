### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To come together; to meet; to unite.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To come together, as in one body or for a public purpose; to meet; to assemble.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To cause to assemble; to call together; to convoke; to summon.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To summon judicially to meet or appear.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To make a convention; to declare a rule by convention.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** To forestall any problems, we convened on the rule that all the database records would avoid containing certain literal strings.

   **Source context (verbatim):** with "on" or "upon"
