### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The expulsion from the womb of a foetus or embryo before it is fully developed, with loss of the foetus.
   1. A spontaneous abortion; a miscarriage.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable; now uncommon in general use

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The expulsion from the womb of a foetus or embryo before it is fully developed, with loss of the foetus.
   1. An induced abortion.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Example:** Mary decided to have an abortion because she was too young to raise a baby.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An aborted foetus; an abortus.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A misshapen person or thing; a monstrosity.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Failure or abandonment of a project, promise, goal etc.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Arrest of development of any organ, so that it remains an imperfect formation or is absorbed.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The cessation of an illness or disease at a very early stage.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
