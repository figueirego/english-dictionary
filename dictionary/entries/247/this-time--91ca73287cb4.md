## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. On or near the same date (in the past or in the future); by this time.

   **Example:** (By) this time next week I'll be lying on the beach.

   **Example:** This time next year, I shall've finished university.
