**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A non-Archimedean ordered field whose every member a can be constructed as a formal series of the form a=∑\_(q∈ℚ)a\_qε^q, where a\_q are real numbers, ℚ is the set of rational numbers, and ε is to be interpreted as a fixed positive infinitesimal.
