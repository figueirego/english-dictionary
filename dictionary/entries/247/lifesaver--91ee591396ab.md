### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Someone or something that saves lives.
   1. A lifeguard or other rescuer.

      **Example:** My dad loves his job as a surf lifesaver.

      **Example:** Paramedics are not just ordinary workers but lifesavers with a very important job.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Someone or something that saves lives.
   1. A life buoy.

      **Example:** They threw him a lifesaver and then hauled him up onto the boat.

      **Source synonyms:** life preserver; life ring

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Someone or something that saves lives.
   1. Any lifesaving tool or item of supplies.

      **Example:** When I got lost in the woods, my Swiss Army knife was a lifesaver.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Someone or something that is very useful or helpful.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** Thank you so much for fixing my car; you're a real lifesaver!

   **Example:** This shop is a lifesaver on a hot day when you're craving a cold drink.

   **Source synonyms:** boon; godsend
