### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see move, back.

   **Example:** They've moved the estimate of its date of origin back a hundred years.

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To reschedule (something) to a later date or time.

   **Usage:** US; transitive

   **Example:** The report still hasn't been completed, so the meeting will have to be moved back again.
