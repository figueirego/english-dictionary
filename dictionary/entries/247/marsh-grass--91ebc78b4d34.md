### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any coarse grass growing in marshes of genus Spartina.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source synonyms:** cord grass, swamp grass, meadow grass, swamp hay, thatch grass

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any wetland grass of genus Hymenachne.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Barnyard millet, jungle ricegrass (Echinochloa colonum)

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source synonyms:** jungle rice, shama millet, Deccan grass, small barnyard grass, awnless barnyard grass
