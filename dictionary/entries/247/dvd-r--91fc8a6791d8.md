### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Initialism of Digital Versatile Disc recordable&#58; a form of digital media that is similar to a CD, but has far denser storage and thus greater capacity. It can only be written to once, but can be read from an indefinite number of times.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; initialism
