### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The action of extenuating; extenuated condition.
   1. The action or process of making or becoming thin; an instance of this; a shrunken condition; leanness, emaciation.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The action of extenuating; extenuated condition.
   1. Making less dense; rarefaction.

      **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

      **Source context (verbatim):** of air

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The action of extenuating; extenuated condition.
   1. The action or process of making slender or diminishing in bulk; an instance of this.

      **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The action of extenuating; extenuated condition.
   1. The action of making less or weak; and instance of this; a weakening, impoverishment. Also, mitigation (of blame or punishment).

      **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The action of extenuating; extenuated condition.
   1. The action of representing (something) as slight and trifling; underrating; an instance of this, a plea to this end; a modification in terms.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The action of extenuating; extenuated condition.
   1. The action of representing (something) as slight and trifling; underrating; an instance of this, a plea to this end; a modification in terms.
      1. A figure in which a term is used which, in contrast with the more fitting term it supplants, understates or seeks to diminish the significance of something.

         **Usage:** countable; obsolete; rhetoric; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The action of extenuating; extenuated condition.
   1. The action of lessening, or seeking to lessen, the guilt of (an offence or fault) by alleging partial excuses; and instance or means of doing this; a plea in mitigation of censure.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. The action of extenuating; extenuated condition.
   1. Thin garments.

      **Usage:** US; countable; humorous; in-plural; uncountable

      **Source context (verbatim):** as “extenuations”
