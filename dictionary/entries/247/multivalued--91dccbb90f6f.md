## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of a function, associating one or more values of its range with each value of its domain.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The inverse sine function is multivalued unless it is restricted to a suitable range.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences
