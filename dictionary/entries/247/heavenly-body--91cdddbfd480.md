### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A natural celestial object, visible in the sky, such as a star, planet, natural satellite, asteroid, comet, the Moon or the Sun. Objects flying or moving in the atmosphere are not usually considered as heavenly bodies.

   **Source synonyms:** celestial body; wanderstar

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An astronomical object.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The recreated, angel-like body that people will have in heaven.
