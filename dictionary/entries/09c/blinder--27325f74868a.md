### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Something that blinds, literally or figuratively.
   1. A bag or cloth put over the head of a difficult horse while it is being handled or mounted.

      **Source synonyms:** blinker; winker

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Something that blinds, literally or figuratively.
   1. A screen attached to a horse's bridle preventing it from being able to see things to its side.

      **Usage:** often; plural; plural-only

      **Source synonyms:** blinker; winker

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. Something that blinds, literally or figuratively.
   1. Something that impairs visual or mental perception in a way figuratively compared to horses' blinders.
