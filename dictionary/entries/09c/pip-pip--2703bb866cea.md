### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Goodbye; cheerio, toodle-oo, toodle-pip (mostly used by the upper classes).

   **Usage:** UK; colloquial

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A general greeting (mostly used by the upper classes)..

   **Usage:** UK; colloquial

   **Example:** Pip-pip! What's going on here?

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used to create enthusiasm (mostly used by the upper classes).

   **Usage:** UK; colloquial

   **Example:** Pip-pip! Let's get out there and knock the stuffing out of 'em!
