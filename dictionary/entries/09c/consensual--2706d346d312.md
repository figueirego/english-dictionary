### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. With permission, with consensus, without coercion; allowed without objecting or resisting.

   **Example:** consensual sex

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Existing, or made, by the mutual consent of two or more parties.

   **Example:** a consensual contract

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Excited or caused by sensation, sympathy, or reflex action, and not by conscious volition.

   **Example:** consensual motions

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Excited or caused by sensation, sympathy, or reflex action, and not by conscious volition.
   1. Contralaterally corresponding rather than ipsilaterally induced.

      **Example:** direct and consensual pupillary reflexes produce bilateral constriction from a unilateral stimulus

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences
