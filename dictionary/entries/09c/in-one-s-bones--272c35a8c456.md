### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. (of a feeling, belief, etc.) Derived from instinct, intuition, or an ingrained inclination.

   **Usage:** figuratively; idiomatic

   **Example:** He understood in his bones that the odds were not in his favor.

   **Example:** She could feel the rhythm in her bones.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see in, one's, bones&#58; especially, referring to rheumatic aches sensitive to atmospheric pressure, temperature, and humidity.

   **Example:** As his aging hands became increasingly arthritic, he would often tell us that he could feel it in his bones when a snowstorm was coming.

   **Example:** She could feel the rhythm in her bones.
