**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To cause (someone) to go insane or become deranged.

   **Usage:** transitive; chiefly passive voice

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To cause disorder in (something); to distort from its ideal state.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To cause to malfunction or become inoperative.

   **Usage:** transitive
