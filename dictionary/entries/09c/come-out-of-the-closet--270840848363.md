### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To disclose to others about one's sexual orientation, romantic orientation, or gender identity, where previously this had been kept secret.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** He came out of the closet as gay to his friends.

   **Source synonyms:** come out

   **Source antonyms:** remain in the closet; woodwork

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To self-disclose about one's minority or disapproved-of identity, belief, preference, etc.

   **Usage:** humorous; idiomatic; informal; intransitive; sometimes

   **Example:** She finally came out of the closet to her religious family regarding her atheism.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see come, out, of, the, closet.
