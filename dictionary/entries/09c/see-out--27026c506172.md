### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To accompany a guest to the door when he or she leaves (also used figuratively).

   **Usage:** formal; transitive

   **Example:** Please see our guest out.

   **Example:** I saw that boy into the world and I will see him out.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To continue something until completion; to watch an activity develop to a conclusion.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I'll never give up. I'm going to see this thing out.

   **Source synonyms:** see through
