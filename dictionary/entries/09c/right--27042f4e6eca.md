### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Designating the side of the body which is positioned to the east if one is facing north, the side on which the heart is not located in most humans. This arrow points to the reader's right&#58; →

   **Example:** After the accident, her right leg was slightly shorter than her left.

   **Source synonyms:** right-hand; dexter; dextral; starboard

   **Source antonyms:** left; left-hand; sinister; sinistral

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Clockwise, particularly when describing a change in direction or orientation.

   **Example:** The road up ahead contains a right bend.

   **Example:** Rotate the bolt to the right to tighten it.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Complying with justice, correctness, or reason; correct, just, true. See also the interjection senses below.

   **Example:** That's not the right thing to do.

   **Example:** So I was right all along? C'mon. I want to hear you say it.

   **Source synonyms:** correct; just

   **Source antonyms:** wrong; incorrect; unjust

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Appropriate, perfectly suitable; fit for purpose.

   **Example:** Is this the right software for my computer?

   **Source antonyms:** wrong

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Healthy, sane, competent.

   **Example:** I'm afraid my father is no longer in his right mind.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Real; veritable (used emphatically).

   **Example:** You've made a right mess of the kitchen!

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Of an angle, measuring 90 degrees, or one quarter of a complete rotation; the angle between two perpendicular lines.

   **Example:** The kitchen counter formed a right angle with the back wall.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Of a geometric figure, incorporating a right angle between edges, faces, axes, etc.

   **Example:** a right triangle a right prism a right cone

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. Designating the bank of a river (etc.) on one's right when facing downstream (i.e. facing forward while floating with the current); that is, the south bank of a river that flows eastward. If this arrow&#58; ⥴ shows the direction of the current, the tilde is on the right side of the river.

**Source sense R1.S10**

   **Source synonyms:** right-wing; conservative

   **Source antonyms:** left; left-wing; liberal

### Senses

<a id="r3-s1"></a>

**Source sense R3.S1**

1. Yes, that is correct; I agree.

   **Example:** — United's the best team in the country. — Right. And they'll go all the way for sure. — Damn right they will.

<a id="r3-s2"></a>

**Source sense R3.S2**

2. I have listened to what you just said and I acknowledge your assertion or opinion, regardless of whether I agree with it (opinion) or can verify it (assertion).

   **Example:** — United's the best team in the country, so they'll come up with something. — Right. And do you think they'll go all the way?

<a id="r3-s3"></a>

**Source sense R3.S3**

3. Signpost word to change the subject in a discussion or discourse.

   **Example:** — After that interview, I don't think we should hire her. — Right. Who wants lunch?

<a id="r3-s4"></a>

**Source sense R3.S4**

4. Used to check listener engagement and (especially) agreement at the end of an utterance or each segment thereof.

   **Example:** You're going, right?

   **Example:** I went downstairs, right, and I was going to call her, but I found this note, right, so what am I supposed to do now?

<a id="r3-s5"></a>

**Source sense R3.S5**

5. Used to add seriousness or decisiveness before a statement.

### Senses

**Source sense R5.S1**

1. To correct.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Righting all the wrongs of the war immediately will be impossible.

<a id="r5-s2"></a>

**Source sense R5.S2**

2. To set upright.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The tow-truck righted what was left of the automobile.

<a id="r5-s3"></a>

**Source sense R5.S3**

3. To return to normal upright position.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** When the wind died down, the ship righted.
