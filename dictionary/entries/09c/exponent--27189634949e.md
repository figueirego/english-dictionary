<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who expounds, represents or advocates.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The number by which a value (called the base) is said to be raised to a power in exponentiation&#58; for example, the 3 in 2³=8.
