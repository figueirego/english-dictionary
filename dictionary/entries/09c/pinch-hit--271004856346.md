### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To bat in place of another player

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Jones pinch-hit for Smith in the sixth.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; baseball; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To do something in the place of another person who is not able to perform or is less skilled; to substitute or stand in for somebody.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** Jones pinch-hit for Smith during today's presentation.
