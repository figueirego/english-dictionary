### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A profound and persistent unhappiness caused by having characteristics (body parts, clothing, etc) of, or being perceived as, a certain gender or sex, experienced especially by transgender people.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source synonyms:** GD; gender identity disorder (dated)

   **Source antonyms:** gender euphoria
