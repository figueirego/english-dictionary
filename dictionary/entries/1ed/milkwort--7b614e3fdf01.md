### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of more than five hundred species of flowering plants of the genus Polygala that are used as a food source by the larvae of some members of the Lepidoptera order.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** Rogation-flower
