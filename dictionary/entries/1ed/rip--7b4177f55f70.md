**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Initialism of requiescat in pace, requiescant in pace, or rest in peace (a blessing or expression of hope that a deceased person is at peace).

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; initialism

   **Example:** RIP, Pope Francis.

   **Alternative form of:** [requiescat in pace](../17a/requiescat-in-pace--5e8a8c923c1c.md); [requiescant in pace](../0a7/requiescant-in-pace--29eb17b3beea.md); or rest in peace

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Initialism of requiescat in pace, requiescant in pace, or rest in peace (a blessing or expression of hope that a deceased person is at peace).
   1. Expressing disappointment at the fact that someone or something has come to an end or gone away.

      **Usage:** broadly; informal; ironic; often

      **Example:** RIP Vine. Only real ones remember.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Initialism of requiescat in pace, requiescant in pace, or rest in peace (a blessing or expression of hope that a deceased person is at peace).
   1. Expressing concern about something bad happening to someone or something.

      **Usage:** excessive; humorous; ironic; often; slang

      **Example:** That jumpscare is so loud! RIP headphone users.

      **Example:** RIP your DMs.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Initialism of requiescat in pace, requiescant in pace, or rest in peace (a blessing or expression of hope that a deceased person is at peace).
   1. Expressing mild disappointment or embarrassment.

      **Usage:** broadly; slang

      **Example:** I applied to dozens of companies and didn't get a single job offer, RIP.
