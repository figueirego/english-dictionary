### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Not comfortable; causing discomfort.

   **Example:** The class squirmed and fidgeted in the uncomfortable new chairs.

   **Source antonyms:** ergonomic

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Experiencing discomfort physically.

   **Source synonyms:** ill at ease

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Experiencing discomfort mentally and emotionally.
   1. Uneasy or anxious.

      **Example:** Sharing a house with them made me uncomfortable.

      **Source synonyms:** ill at ease

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Experiencing discomfort mentally and emotionally.
   1. Put off or disgusted.

      **Example:** It was clear that the film's apparent celebration of psychopathy made many audience members uncomfortable.
