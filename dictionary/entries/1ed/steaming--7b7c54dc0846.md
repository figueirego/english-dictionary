## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈstiː.mɪŋ/ — source labels: UK

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The action of steam on something.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Steaming is fine for this fabric, as long as you keep the iron moving.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The method of cooking by immersion in steam.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Their favorite way to cook clams is steaming.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The act or process of seafaring under steam power.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Steaming was their preferred way to travel until airliners came along.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The raising of steam by a steam locomotive etc; the maintenance of a head of steam during operation; (metonymic) operation under load.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Two 4-4-0s were steaming through the scenic foothills, giving rides to tourists.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A form of robbery in which a large gang moves swiftly and violently through a bus or train.

   **Usage:** UK; countable; informal; uncountable
