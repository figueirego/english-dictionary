### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An instance of spinning 180 degrees, so that one is facing in the opposite direction.

   **Example:** He did a 180 off the diving board.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The exact opposite.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** The friendly welcome was a 180 from what she had been expecting.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A complete change of direction or opinion.

   **Usage:** broadly; figuratively

   **Example:** She used to like turnips, but she did a 180 and now hates them.

   **Source synonyms:** U-turn; about-face; about-turn

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A turn where all three darts hit the treble 20 sector, scoring 180 points (the maximum possible).

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** darts; games
