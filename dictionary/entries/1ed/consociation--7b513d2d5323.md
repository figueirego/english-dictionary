   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A confederation of Christian churches or organizations.
   1. A voluntary, permanent council made up of representatives of neighbouring Congregational churches for mutual advice and co-operation in ecclesiastical matters; also, a meeting of pastors and delegates from churches thus united.

      **Usage:** US; countable; specifically; uncountable; Congregationalism

      **Example:** In Connecticut some of the Congregational churches are associated in consociations and the others in associations.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity
