### Forms

- illiterates — plural
- illitterate — alternative; obsolete

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. An illiterate person, one either not able to read and write or not knowing how.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. A person ignorant about a given subject. (The relevant subject is usually named as a noun adjunct.)

   **Example:** Their government is run by business illiterates.

   **Example:** Sometimes a mathematical illiterate is called an innumerate.
