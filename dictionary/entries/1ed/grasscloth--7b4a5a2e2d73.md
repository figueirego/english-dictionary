### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A wall covering made from the fibres of jute, rushes or other grasses.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of various coarse cloths, the fibre of which is rarely that of a grass; especially, the Chinese summer-cloth made from ramie, Boehmeria nivea, properly a nettle.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
