### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Tall and awkward. (of a person’s body)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Awkward; especially, involving the awkward protrusion of the elbows. (of a person’s movement)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Having bends that resemble elbows. (of a tree or branches)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**
