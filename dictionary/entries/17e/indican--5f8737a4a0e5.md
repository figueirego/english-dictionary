### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A glycoside obtained from plants of genus Indigofera, the source of natural indigo dye.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; organic-chemistry; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An indigo-forming substance found in urine and other animal fluids, and convertible into red and blue indigo (urrhodin and uroglaucin); an indoxyl sulphate of potash.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; organic-chemistry; physical-sciences
