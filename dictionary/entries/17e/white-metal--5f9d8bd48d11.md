### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any tin- or lead-based alloy (commonly used to make bearings, jewellery, toy soldiers, fusible plugs, some medals and metal type).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Silver that cannot be labeled as silver in the UK because it does not bear an Assay Office hallmark.

   **Usage:** UK; countable; uncountable; auctions

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
