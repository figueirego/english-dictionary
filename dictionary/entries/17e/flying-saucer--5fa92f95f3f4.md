## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A disc-shaped unidentified flying object or UFO; originally in reference to sightings by aviator Kenneth Arnold in Washington in 1947.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** Some connect the recent spate of flying saucer sightings with a rumored top-secret military aviation project.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An alien interplanetary vessel, typically disc-shaped and of metallic construction.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** literature; media; publishing; science-fiction

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A form of confectionery, a small spheroidal capsule of rice paper filled with sherbet.
