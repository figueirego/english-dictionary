## Adverb

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In a straight line distance between two locations, as opposed to the road distance or over land distance.

   **Usage:** Ireland; UK; idiomatic; not-comparable

   **Example:** It is 240 miles as the gull flies.

   **Source synonyms:** as the crow flies
