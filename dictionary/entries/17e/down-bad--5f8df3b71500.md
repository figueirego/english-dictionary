**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In a difficult situation, despondent.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; slang; African-American Vernacular

   **Example:** Walter was down bad after his wife had left him.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

   **Usage:** not-comparable; slang
