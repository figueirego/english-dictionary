### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A class of two-handed double-trapeze dinghy (from its maximum length of 16 feet, approx. 4900 millimetres).

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A miner or other person who took part in the California Gold Rush of 1849.

   **Usage:** historical

   **Source synonyms:** argonaut

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A player for the San Francisco 49ers, an NFL team.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** American-football; ball-games; football; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s4"></a>
