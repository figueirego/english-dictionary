### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A type of deformed growth occurring on the skin caused by the human papillomavirus (HPV).

   **Example:** A wart has appeared on my toe.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; pathology; sciences
