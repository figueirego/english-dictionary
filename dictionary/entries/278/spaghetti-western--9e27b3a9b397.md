**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A motion picture depicting a story of cowboys and desperadoes set in the American Old West, but produced by an Italian-based company and filmed in Europe, notably in Italy.

   **Example:** “A Fistful of Dollars” and “Once Upon a Time in the West” are famous spaghetti westerns.
