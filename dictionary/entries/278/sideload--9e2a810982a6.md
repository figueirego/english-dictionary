### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To transfer data between two local devices, rather than to or from a remote device.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I'm going to sideload my computer's address book onto my mobile phone.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To install an app on a mobile device by using any app store that doesn't come preinstalled like Google Play or Apple App Store. Or installing an app from an installation package. (.apk/.ipa)

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Developer&#58; I'm not paying 25$ to Google to put my free app on Google Play. User&#58; I'm fine sideloading it from F-Droid. Thank you for the free app!

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences
