**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astrophysics

   **Source synonyms:** noodle effect

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** rare; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
