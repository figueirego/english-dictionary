## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The start of the ninth hour of the day; 8&#58;00 a.m. (08&#58;00).

   **Usage:** In both the 12-hour clock and the 24-hour clock

   **Example:** The breakfast buffet opens at eight o'clock (= "The breakfast buffet opens at 8&#58;00 a.m.").

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The start of the twenty-first hour of the day; 8&#58;00 p.m. (20&#58;00).

   **Usage:** Only in the 12-hour clock

   **Example:** Supper was served at eight o'clock (= "Supper was served at 8&#58;00 p.m.").

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A position to the left and behind (horizontal clock orientation) (from the location of the 8 mark on a clock face)

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** We've got a bogey at eight o'clock.
