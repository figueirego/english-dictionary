## Noun

### Forms

- whitetips — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of various animals having a white tip or tips on the body.
   1. The oceanic whitetip shark, Carcharhinus longimanus.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of various animals having a white tip or tips on the body.
   1. The whitetip reef shark, Triaenodon obesus.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of various animals having a white tip or tips on the body.
   1. Any of the hummingbirds of the Urosticte genus, characterized by the white tips on the males' rectrices.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any of various animals having a white tip or tips on the body.
   1. Any of various species of damselfly of the genus Episynlestes, endemic to Australia.
