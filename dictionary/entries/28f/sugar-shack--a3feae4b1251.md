### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A building where sap from a sugarbush is boiled down to make maple syrup.

   **Source synonyms:** sugarhouse

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Such a facility that receives visitors for traditional meals and entertainment during the period when maple sap is being collected.
