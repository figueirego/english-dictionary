   **Example:** After stepping off the bus, my wallet fell on the floor.

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To cover or furnish with a floor.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** floor a house with pine boards

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To strike down or lay level with the floor; to knock down.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To hang (a picture on exhibition) near the base of a wall, where it cannot easily be seen.

   **Usage:** dated; informal

   **Source antonyms:** sky

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To push (a pedal) down to the floor, especially to accelerate.

   **Usage:** slang; transitive; driving

   **Example:** our driver floored the pedal

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To silence by a conclusive answer or retort.

   **Usage:** informal; transitive

   **Example:** floor an opponent

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To amaze or greatly surprise.

   **Usage:** informal; transitive; usually passive voice

   **Example:** We were floored by his confession.

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

**Source sense R2.S7**

7. To finish or make an end of.

   **Usage:** colloquial; transitive

   **Example:** floor a college examination

<a id="r2-s8"></a>
