### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A very young human, particularly from birth to a couple of years old or until walking is fully mastered.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A very young human, particularly from birth to a couple of years old or until walking is fully mastered.
   1. A very young human, even if not yet born.

      **Example:** When is your baby due?

      **Example:** Her baby had always been active, even before he was born, when he would kick her bladder.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any very young animal, especially a vertebrate; many species have specific names for their babies, such as kittens for the babies of cats, puppies for the babies of dogs, and chicks for the babies of birds. See &#58;Category&#58;Baby animals for more.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A person who is immature, infantile, or feeble.

   **Example:** Stand up for yourself – don't be such a baby!

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A person who is new to or inexperienced in something.

   **Example:** I only qualified as an architect this summer, so I'm still a baby.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The lastborn of a family; the youngest sibling, irrespective of age.

   **Example:** Adam is the baby of the family.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A person's romantic partner; a term of endearment used to refer to or address e.g. one's girlfriend, boyfriend, or spouse.

   **Example:** Too busy thinking about my baby, and I ain't got time for nothing else.

   **Example:** Baby, don't cry.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A form of address to a person considered to be attractive.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** Hey baby, what are you doing later?

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A thing that is comparatively small of its kind.

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. A concept or creation endeared by its creator.

   **Example:** This test program I've designed is my new baby.

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. A pet project or responsibility.

   **Example:** You need to talk to John about that – it's his baby.

<a id="r1-s12"></a>

**Source sense R1.S12**

12. An affectionate term for anything.

   **Example:** See my new car here? I can't wait to take this baby for a drive.

<a id="r1-s13"></a>

**Source sense R1.S13**

13. A small image of an infant; a doll.

   **Usage:** archaic

<a id="r1-s14"></a>

**Source sense R1.S14**

14. One who is new to an identity or community.

   **Usage:** attributive; often

   **Example:** baby queer; baby trans
