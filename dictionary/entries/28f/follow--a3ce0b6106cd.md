### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To go after; to pursue; to move behind in the same path or direction, especially with the intent of catching.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** Follow that car!

   **Example:** She left the room and I followed.

   **Source synonyms:** trail; tail

   **Source antonyms:** guide; lead

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To go or come after in a sequence.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** B follows A in the alphabet.

   **Example:** We both ordered the soup, with roast beef to follow.

   **Source synonyms:** succeed; come after; ensue; follow; follow on; succeed

   **Source antonyms:** precede; antecede; come before; forego; precede

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To carry out (orders, instructions, etc.).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Follow these instructions to the letter.

   **Source synonyms:** pursue

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To live one's life according to (religion, teachings, etc).

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To understand, to pay attention to.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Do you follow me?

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To watch, to keep track of (reports of) some event or person.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I followed the incumbent throughout the election.

   **Example:** My friends don't regularly follow the news.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To be a supporter of (a sports team).

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To subscribe to see content from an account on a social media platform.

   **Usage:** transitive; social media

   **Example:** If you want to see more of our articles, follow us on Twitter.

   **Source antonyms:** unfollow

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To be a logical consequence of something.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** It follows that if two numbers are not equal then one is larger than the other.

   **Example:** If you don't practise proper hygiene, illness is sure to follow.

   **Source synonyms:** ensue

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. To walk in, as a road or course; to attend upon closely, as a profession or calling.

   **Usage:** transitive
