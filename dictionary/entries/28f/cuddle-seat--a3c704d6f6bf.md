### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A fabric sling or soft carrier worn by an adult, allowing the baby to be carried against the chest or hip.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A pair of adjoining seats in a cinema without an intervening armrest, allowing couples to cuddle during the entertainment.
