## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A knit fabric in which intermeshing loops are positioned in warp, or lengthwise, direction. The fabric has a flatter, closer, less elastic structure than most welt knits and is run-resistant.

   **Usage:** uncountable
