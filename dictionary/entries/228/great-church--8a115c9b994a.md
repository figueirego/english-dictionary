### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The orthodox Christian church of antiquity, after 380 CE the established church of the Roman Empire, especially as distinct from smaller Christian movements or heresies.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Hagia Sophia before the fall of the Byzantine Empire or one of the churches that previously occupied its site, the Great Church of Constantinople.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
