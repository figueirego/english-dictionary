### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A well-formed formula (wff) that has a constant term in place of a variable; expressed as Q(a/x), where the resulting wff is identical to Q except that the constant a takes the place of every free occurrence of the variable x.
