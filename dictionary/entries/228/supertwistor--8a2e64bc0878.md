### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A supersymmetric extension of twistors, having n degrees of freedom (where n is the number of supersymmetries) in addition to those of a standard twistor.

   **Example:** The superconformal algebra can be realized on supertwistor space.
