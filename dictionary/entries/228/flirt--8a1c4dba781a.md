### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To throw (something) with a jerk or sudden movement; to fling.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** They flirt water in each other's faces.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To jeer at; to mock.

   **Usage:** archaic; intransitive

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To dart about; to move with quick, jerky motions.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To blurt out.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To play at courtship; to talk with light affection, to imply sexual attraction in a playful (especially conversational) way.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To experiment, or tentatively engage, with; to become involved in passing with.

   **Usage:** intransitive
