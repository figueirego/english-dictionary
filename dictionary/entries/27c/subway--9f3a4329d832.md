## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈsʌbˌweɪ/ — source labels: General-American; Received-Pronunciation
- IPA: \[ˈsʌbˌweɪ̯\] — source labels: General-American; Received-Pronunciation

### Forms

- subways — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An underground railway, especially for mass transit of people in urban areas.

   **Usage:** Canada; Scotland; South-Africa; US

   **Example:** He is going to Westminster by subway.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A train that runs on such an underground railway.

   **Usage:** Canada; Scotland; South-Africa; US

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A rapid transit system, regardless of the elevation of its right of way; a metro system.

   **Usage:** Canada; US

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An underground walkway, tunnel for pedestrians (called pedestrian underpass in US).

   **Usage:** UK

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An underground route for pipes, sewers, etc.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. An underground route for creatures (e.g. bats).

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** metro; Métro (Paris); tube; Tube (London); underground; underground railway; rapid transit; underpass
