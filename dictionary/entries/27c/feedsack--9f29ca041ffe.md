## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A sack (bag) for feed, usually of cotton or polymer cloth or of heavy paper.

   **Usage:** animal husbandry

   **Example:** He brought us his fruitwood sawdust in an old feedsack, ready to be used in our crafting project.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Sackcloth. (In the nineteenth and twentieth centuries, feedsack cloth was often reused for making clothing, quilts, or rags, and this tradition continues to some extent today.)

   **Example:** Let's be honest&#58; the outfit hardly matters — you could put her in a dress made of feedsack and she'd still look fabulous.

   **Source synonyms:** hessian; burlap
