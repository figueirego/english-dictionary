### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Belonging to the sex which typically produces eggs (ova), or to the gender which is typically associated with it.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; usually

   **Example:** female authors, the leading male and female artists, a female bird cooing at a male

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Characteristic of this sex/gender. (Compare feminine, womanly.)

   **Usage:** not-comparable; usually

   **Example:** stereotypically female pastimes, an insect with typically female coloration

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Tending to lead to or regulate the development of sexual characteristics typical of this sex.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; usually

   **Example:** the female chromosome

   **Example:** estrogen, the primary female sex hormone, is produced by both females and males

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Feminine; of the feminine grammatical gender.

   **Usage:** feminine; form-of; not-comparable; usually; less common than 'feminine'

   **Form of:** the feminine grammatical gender

**Source sense R1.S5**

   **Usage:** not-comparable; usually

**Source sense R1.S6**
