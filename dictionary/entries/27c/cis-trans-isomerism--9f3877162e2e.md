## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A form of isomerism in which, in one isomer two atoms or groups are on the same side of the plane of a double bond or ring, whereas in the other isomer they are on opposite sides; these are cis- and trans- isomers, respectively.

   **Usage:** physical; uncountable
