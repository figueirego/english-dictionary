## Noun

### Forms

- split ends — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A hair which has split at the end, usually from dryness or poor nutrition.

   **Example:** I've got so many split ends – I think I must be working too hard.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The condition of having hairs with split ends.

   **Usage:** in-plural

   **Example:** Help! I've got split ends!

   **Source synonyms:** schizotrichia; trichoptilosis

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The position which lines up wide and on the offensive line of scrimmage, whose primary job is to serve as a wide receiver.

   **Example:** Jones played split end.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** American-football; ball-games; football; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

   **Source synonyms:** wideout

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

   **Example:** The split end ran deep.
