### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The Gospel canticle of Zechariah (Luke 1&#58;68–79), with the incipit Benedictus Dominus Deus Israel (“Blessed be the Lord God of Israel”).

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity; Western-Christianity

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The second part of the Sanctus, beginning, Benedictus qui venit in nomine domini (“Blessed is he who comes in the name of the Lord”), and historically often sung as a separate piece of music.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity; Western-Christianity

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The music that accompanies either of the above.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music
