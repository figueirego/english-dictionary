### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The action of opposing or of being in conflict.

   **Example:** The two politicians are in opposition to the bill.

   **Example:** The proposal met with strong opposition from local residents.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An opposite or contrasting position.

   **Example:** These ideas are in opposition to each other.

   **Source synonyms:** oppositeness

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The apparent relative position of two celestial bodies when one is at an angle of 180 degrees from the other as seen from the Earth.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astronomy; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A political party or movement opposed to the party or government in power.

   **Example:** leader of the opposition

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; politics

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. In United States intellectual property law, a proceeding in which an interested party seeks to prevent the registration of a trademark or patent.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A position in which the player on the move must yield with his king allowing his opponent to advance with his own king.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** board-games; chess; games

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The difference of quantity or quality between two propositions having the same subject and predicate.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; logic; mathematics; philosophy; sciences
