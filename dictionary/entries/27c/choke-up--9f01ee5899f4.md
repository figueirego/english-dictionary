### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To (temporarily) lose one's power of speech, because of strong emotion.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Hearing that song always makes me choke up.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To cause (a person) temporarily to lose the power of speech, because of strong emotion.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** It chokes me up to think about how he died.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To cease talking.

   **Usage:** imperative; intransitive; often

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To block up; to cause something to be blocked.

   **Usage:** figuratively; sometimes; transitive
