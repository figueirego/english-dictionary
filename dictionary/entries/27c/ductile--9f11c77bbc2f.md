### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Capable of being pulled or stretched into thin wire by mechanical force without breaking.

   **Example:** ductile material

   **Example:** ductile shape

   **Example:** ductile alloy

   **Example:** ductile state

   **Example:** Gold is highly ductile, allowing it to be drawn into very fine wires.

   **Example:** The ductile nature of copper makes it essential in electrical wiring.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
