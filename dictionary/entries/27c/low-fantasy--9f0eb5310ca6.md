## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

- low fantasies — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A subgenre of fantasy fiction set in the primary or real world as opposed to a secondary or fantasy world.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A subgenre of fantasy fiction that focuses on more grounded and realistic fantasy with more focus on the daily lives and practical goals of the characters.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A work in this subgenre.

   **Usage:** countable
