## Noun · Record 2

- IPA: /ˈsɝː.fɪt/ — source labels: General-American

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. An excessive amount of something.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** A surfeit of wheat is driving down the price.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Overindulgence in either food or drink; overeating.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. A sickness or condition caused by overindulgence.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** King Henry I is said to have died of a surfeit of lampreys.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. Disgust caused by excess; satiety.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. A group of skunks.

   **Usage:** countable

## Verb · Record 3

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈsɜː.fɪt/ — source labels: Received-Pronunciation
- IPA: /ˈsɝː.fɪt/ — source labels: General-American

### Forms

- surfeiting — participle; present
- surfeited — participle; past

### Senses

<a id="r3-s1"></a>

**Source sense R3.S1**

1. To fill (something) to excess.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** stuff

<a id="r3-s2"></a>

**Source sense R3.S2**

2. To feed (someone) to excess (on, upon or with something).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** She surfeited her children on sweets.

   **Source synonyms:** glut; overfeed; stuff

<a id="r3-s3"></a>

**Source sense R3.S3**

3. To make (someone) sick as a result of overconsumption.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r3-s4"></a>

**Source sense R3.S4**

4. To supply (someone) with something to excess; to disgust (someone) through overabundance.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** cloy; glut

<a id="r3-s5"></a>

**Source sense R3.S5**

5. To satisfy (someone's appetite) to excess.

   **Usage:** figuratively; sometimes; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** glut

<a id="r3-s6"></a>

**Source sense R3.S6**

6. To overeat or feed to excess (on or upon something).

   **Usage:** intransitive; reflexive

   **Source synonyms:** glut; indulge; overfeed; overindulge

<a id="r3-s7"></a>

**Source sense R3.S7**

7. To indulge (in something) to excess.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive; reflexive

<a id="r3-s8"></a>

**Source sense R3.S8**

8. To become sick from overindulgence.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive; reflexive; sometimes
