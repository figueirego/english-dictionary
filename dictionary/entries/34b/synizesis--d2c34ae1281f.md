**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Usage:** countable; rhetoric; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** communications; human-sciences; journalism; linguistics; literature; media; phonetics; phonology; poetry; prosody; publishing; sciences; writing

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; cytology; medicine; natural-sciences; sciences
