### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of several species of fish in Salmonidae, closely related to salmon, and distinguished by spawning more than once.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Many anglers consider trout to be the archetypical quarry.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An objectionable elderly woman.

   **Usage:** UK; countable; derogatory; uncountable; plural trouts

   **Example:** Look, you silly old trout, you can't keep bringing home cats! You can't afford the ones you have!
