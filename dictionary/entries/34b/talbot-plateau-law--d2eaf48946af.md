**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An experimental observation related to the psychophysics of vision. If a light flickers so rapidly that it appears continuous, then its perceived brightness will be determined by the relative periods of light and darkness&#58; the longer the darkness, the weaker the light.

   **Source synonyms:** Talbot's law
