**Source sense R2.S1**

   **Usage:** Sri-Lanka; dated

   **Source synonyms:** carrying pole; milkmaid's yoke; shoulder pole

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. A measure of weight equivalent to that which can be carried using a pingo, equal to around 25 kilograms (55 lbs).

   **Usage:** Sri-Lanka; dated
