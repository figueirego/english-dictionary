### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Characterized by research, especially the serious investigation of a subject.

   **Usage:** colloquial; humorous; nonstandard

   **Example:** Michelle's new job is researchy.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Having little or no known practical application.

   **Usage:** colloquial; derogatory; nonstandard

   **Example:** The committee agreed that the project had merit, but was too researchy and should have lower priority than more practical projects.
