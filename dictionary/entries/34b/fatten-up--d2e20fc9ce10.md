### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To cause to gain weight by means of feeding.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** My cat looks underweight, so I'll fatten him up with higher-calorie cat food.

   **Example:** I visited my mother for a month, and she managed to fatten me up 20 pounds.
