### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of various means of limiting trade (especially imports) without charging a tariff in the form of taxes or duties (for example, technical requirements, inspections, licenses, quotas); a nontax import restriction.

   **Source synonyms:** NTM

   **Source antonyms:** tariff barrier; tariff
