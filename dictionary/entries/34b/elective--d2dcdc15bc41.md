### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of, or pertaining to voting or elections; involving a choice between options.

   **Source synonyms:** electoral

   **Source antonyms:** appointive; hereditary

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Open to choice; freely chosen; (also, usually) unnecessary; minor.

   **Example:** After accounting for all of my required courses, there is hardly any room in my schedule for any elective ones.

   **Source synonyms:** discretionary; optional; voluntary

   **Source antonyms:** compulsory; mandatory; obligatory; required; involuntary

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Scheduled and nonemergent (regardless of whether necessary or unnecessary and whether minor or serious).

   **Usage:** US health care system

   **Example:** It was very confusing hearing my cancer surgery being classified as "elective surgery". Am I "electing" to live rather than die?

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; technical

   **Source antonyms:** emergent; unscheduled
