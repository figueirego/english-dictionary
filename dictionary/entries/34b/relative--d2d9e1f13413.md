**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Connected to or depending on something else; comparative.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** We've been discussing the relative merits of various methods of transportation.

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Expressed in relation to another item, rather than in complete form.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; path; or similar; path; or similar

   **Example:** The relative URL /images/pic.jpg, when evaluated in the context of http&#58;//example.com/index.html, corresponds to the absolute URL http&#58;//example.com/images/pic.jpg.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Depending on an antecedent; comparative.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The words “big” and “small” are relative.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** grammar; human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

**Source sense R1.S4**

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

**Source sense R1.S5**

   **Usage:** archaic; not-comparable; rare

   **Example:** relative to your earlier point about taxes, ...
