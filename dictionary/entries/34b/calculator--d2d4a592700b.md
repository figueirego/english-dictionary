### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A mechanical or electronic device that performs mathematical calculations; (now usually) an electronic one specifically.

   **Example:** electronic calculator; pocket calculator

   **Example:** The calculator on a bookkeeper's desk in the 1950s was an adding machine with mechanical guts.

   **Example:** I told him to hand me a calculator and he jokingly placed an adding machine at my side.

   **Source synonyms:** electronic calculator

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person who performs mathematical calculation.

   **Usage:** historical

   **Example:** During the war, his grandmother had worked at the Bletchley Park decoding station as a calculator.

   **Source synonyms:** computer; reckoner

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A person who calculates (in the sense of scheming).

   **Example:** He revealed himself to be a cold-blooded calculator out to sabotage his colleagues.

   **Source synonyms:** schemer; plotter; manipulator

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A set of mathematical tables.

   **Usage:** obsolete

   **Source synonyms:** ready reckoner
