### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In humans, the joint or the region of the joint in the middle part of the leg between the thigh and the shank.

   **Example:** Penny was wearing a miniskirt, so she skinned her exposed knees when she fell.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In the horse and allied animals, the carpal joint, corresponding to the wrist in humans.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. In birds&#58;
   1. The tarsal joint, uniting the tarsometatarsus and the tibiotarsus (homologous to the human ankle).

      **Usage:** non-technical

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. In birds&#58;
   1. The joint uniting the tibiotarsus and the femur, typically hidden beneath plumage.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The part of a garment that covers the knee.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A piece of timber or metal formed with an angle somewhat in the shape of the human knee when bent.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; manufacturing; shipbuilding

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. An act of kneeling, especially to show respect or courtesy.

   **Usage:** archaic

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Any knee-shaped item or sharp angle in a line; an inflection point.

   **Example:** the knee of a graph

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A blow made with the knee; a kneeing.

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. The presence of a parent etc., where a young child acquires early knowledge.

   **Usage:** figuratively
