### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A thick white liquid produced by soaking the white inner flesh of the seed of the coconut in water and straining. It is traditionally used in Southeast Asian cuisine in curry and other gravy.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually
