### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The quality of being different.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** You need to learn to be more tolerant of difference.

   **Source antonyms:** identity; sameness

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A characteristic of something that makes it different from something else.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** There are three differences between these two pictures.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A disagreement or argument.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** We have our little differences, but we are firm friends.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Significant change in or effect on a situation or state.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** It just won't make much difference to me.

   **Example:** It just won't make much of a difference to anyone.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The result of a subtraction; sometimes the absolute value of this result.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** The difference between 3 and 21 is 18.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Choice; preference.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. An addition to a coat of arms to distinguish two people's bearings which would otherwise be the same. See augmentation and cadency.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; heraldry; hobbies; lifestyle; monarchy; nobility; politics

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. The quality or attribute which is added to those of the genus to constitute a species; a differentia.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; logic; mathematics; philosophy; sciences

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A Boolean operation which is true when the two input variables are different but is otherwise false; the XOR operation ( scriptstyle A◌̅B+◌̅AB).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; logic circuits

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. The set of elements that are in one set but not another ( scriptstyle A◌̅B).

   **Usage:** countable; relational; uncountable
