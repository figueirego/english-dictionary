### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To seem at least vaguely familiar.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; informal

   **Example:** His face rings a bell. I wonder if I know him from somewhere.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To spark a previously forgotten memory.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; informal

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see ring, a, bell.
