## Adverb

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˌdɪs.ɪnˈd͡ʒɛn.ju.əs.li/ — source labels: UK; US

### Forms

- most disingenuously — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In a manner that is not frank or open; deceptively.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In an unnoble manner; in a manner unbecoming of true honor or dignity; unworthily.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. In a manner that adopts a pose of naïveté, possibly to make a point or to deceive.
