### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Very eager (to do something).

   **Example:** I was bursting to tell him the secret.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Urgently needing to urinate.

   **Usage:** often

   **Example:** Can you tell me where the toilets are? I'm bursting.

   **Example:** The kid is bursting to go to the toilet.

   **Source context (verbatim):** followed by "to go to..."

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. So full (with something) as almost to erupt.

   **Example:** The newly stocked refrigerator is bursting with fresh vegetables.

   **Example:** Her heart was bursting with repressed emotion.
