## Phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Not for no reason; not without reason; (often) for a good reason.

   **Example:** Not for nothing did he wait until a moment when Mommy wasn't home to ask for Daddy's permission to watch an R-rated movie.

   **Source synonyms:** with good reason

   **Source antonyms:** for nothing; for no good reason

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used to soften the impact of what is said next, implying that it is worthwhile or important.

   **Example:** A&#58; It's ridiculous how much they charge for insurance premiums around here. B&#58; Not for nothing, when one out of four homeowners in an area has filed a claim within the past five years, premiums aren't gonna be cheap.

   **Source context (verbatim):** as a disjunct

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see not, for, nothing.
