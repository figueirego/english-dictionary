**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Example:** Smith is the principal architect of this design.

   **Example:** The principal cause of the failure was poor planning.

   **Source synonyms:** chief; main; primary; capital

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

   **Usage:** Latinism; obsolete

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Chosen or assumed among a branch of possible values of a multi-valued function so that the function is single-valued.

   **Example:** Two is the principal square root of 4. Both −2 and +2 are square roots of 4.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences
