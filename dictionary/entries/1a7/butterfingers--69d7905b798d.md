### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Someone who tends to drop things; (more generally) someone who is clumsy or uncoordinated; a klutz.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; informal

   **Example:** I am such a butterfingers. That’s the third drink I’ve spilled today.

   **Source synonyms:** butterfinger (uncommon)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One's fingers which tend to drop things, or are clumsy or uncoordinated.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; informal
