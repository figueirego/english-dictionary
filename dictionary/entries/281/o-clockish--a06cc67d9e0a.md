## Adverb

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. After a numeral, denotes a time of around the start of that hour.

   **Usage:** informal; not-comparable

   **Example:** We'll have dinner at seven o'clockish, depending on how soon Keith arrives.
