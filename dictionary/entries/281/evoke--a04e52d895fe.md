### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To call out; to draw out or bring forth.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To cause the manifestation of something (emotion, picture, etc.) in someone's mind or imagination.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Being here evokes long forgotten memories.

   **Example:** Seeing this happen equally evokes fear and anger in me.

   **Example:** The book evokes a detailed and lively picture of what life was like in the 19th century.

   **Source synonyms:** invoke; excite; inspire; put someone in mind of; call up

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To elicit a response.
