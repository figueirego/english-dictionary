### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An edible holder, usually sweet and usually a baked good, for ice cream.

   **Example:** They've still got plenty of ice cream left, but they're out of ice cream cones, so they're serving only in paper cups right now.

   **Source synonyms:** cone; cornet; ice-cream cornet; poke

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An edible holder, usually sweet and usually a baked good, for ice cream.
   1. A thin, conical confection baked from crepe batter, sealed at its apex, used to hold ice cream while it is being eaten.

      **Usage:** strictly

      **Source synonyms:** cone; cornet; ice-cream cornet; poke

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An edible holder, usually sweet and usually a baked good, for ice cream.
   1. Any sweet, edible cone or half-open cylinder which can be used in this fashion; often made with wafer or waffle techniques.

      **Usage:** broadly

      **Source synonyms:** cone; cornet; ice-cream cornet; poke

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Such a holder filled with ice cream.

   **Example:** We bought some ice cream cones from the ice cream van in the park.

   **Source synonyms:** cone; ice cream\[Appendix&#58;Glossary; cornet; ice-cream cornet; poke
