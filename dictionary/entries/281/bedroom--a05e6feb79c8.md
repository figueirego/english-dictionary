### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A room in a house, apartment, hotel, or other dwelling where a bed is kept for sleeping.

   **Example:** Please don't enter my bedroom without knocking.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The location of sexual activity.

   **Usage:** euphemistic

   **Example:** "How are things in the bedroom?" asked the marriage therapist.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The room atop of a bed suitable for sleeping on.

   **Usage:** obsolete
