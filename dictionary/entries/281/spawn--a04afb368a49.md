**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** A frog spawns thousands of tadpoles every year.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To generate, bring into being, especially nonmammalian beings in very large numbers.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To bring forth in general.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The web server spawns a new process to handle each client's request.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To induce (aquatic organisms) to spawn.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To plant with fungal spawn.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To deposit (numerous) eggs in water.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To reproduce, especially in large numbers.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To appear, or cause (something or someone) to appear, spontaneously in a game world at a particular place and time.

   **Usage:** ergative

   **Example:** We put down torches to stop creepers from spawning.

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To appear, or cause (something or someone) to appear, unexpectedly and seemingly out of nowhere.

   **Usage:** Internet; ergative; figuratively
