### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of magnifying; enlargement; exaggeration.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** His story included a magnification of the events.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
