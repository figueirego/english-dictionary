## Phrase

### Forms

- who's to say — canonical

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. According to whose authority (especially in defiance)

   **Example:** Who's to say what clothes I can and can't wear in my free time?

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Nobody knows; it is difficult to judge

   **Example:** In the closest contest for years, who's to say who will win the title?
