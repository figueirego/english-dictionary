### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. That conforms, especially to the shape of something.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. That preserves angles between intersecting curves.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a transformation

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. That preserves relative angles over small scales, at all but a limited number of distinct points.

   **Example:** On conformal map projections, the scale depends on location only and not direction.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** cartography; geography; natural-sciences

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a map projection
