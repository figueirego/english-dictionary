### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Serving hard liquor, instead of only beer and wine.

   **Usage:** US; not-comparable

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a bar or restaurant

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Provisioned with standard liquors (vodka, gin, whisky, rum, tequila), together with mixers, but of a middle (inoffensive but inexpensive) grade. Contrasted with premium bar and super-premium bar, which carry higher grades of liquor.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; catering
