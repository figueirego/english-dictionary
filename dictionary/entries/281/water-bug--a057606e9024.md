### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any member of the infraorder Nepomorpha of true water bugs.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of various other true bugs that live around water.
