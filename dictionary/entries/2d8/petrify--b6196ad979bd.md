### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To turn to stone&#58; to harden organic matter by permeating with water and depositing dissolved minerals.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To produce rigidity akin to stone.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To immobilize with fright.

   **Source synonyms:** alarm; affright (literary); appall; dismay; fray (archaic); frighten; grill (Scotland; obsolete); give someone the shits (idiomatic; vulgar); harrow; horrify; make someone's blood run cold (idiomatic); mortify; petrify; scare; scare straight; scare the bejeebers out of (slang); scare the hell out of (idiomatic; slang); scare the pants off of (idiomatic; slang); scare the shit out of (idiomatic; slang); terrorize; terrify; spook

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To become stone, or of a stony hardness, as organic matter by calcareous deposits.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To become stony, callous, or obdurate.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To make callous or obdurate; to stupefy; to paralyze; to transform; as by petrification.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive
