### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A wheel that transmits power to other elements of a mechanism.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One of the wheels of a motor vehicle that drives (in the engineering sense), that is, one of the wheels that takes power from the drivetrain and applies it to the ground so as to propel the vehicle.

   **Usage:** industrial

   **Example:** You should have tire chains on your drive wheels in this weather.
