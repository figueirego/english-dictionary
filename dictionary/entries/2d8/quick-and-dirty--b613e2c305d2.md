**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Done or constructed in a hasty, approximate, temporarily adequate manner, but not exact, fully formed, or reliable for a long period of time.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I can do a quick-and-dirty market analysis in time for the meeting tomorrow.

### Senses

**Source sense R2.S1**

   **Usage:** US; slang

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. A quick, temporary fix, estimate, or the like.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** The car broke down but we managed to do a quick-and-dirty and were back on the road in fifteen minutes.
