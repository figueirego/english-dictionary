**Source sense R1.S1**

3. A commission agent.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A person or business organization that provides money for another's new business venture; one who finances another's business.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A business organization that lends money on accounts receivable or buys and collects accounts receivable.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. One of the elements, circumstances, or influences which contribute to produce a result.

   **Example:** The greatest factor in the decision was the need for public transportation.

   **Example:** The economy was a factor in this year's budget figures.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Any of various objects multiplied together to form some whole.

   **Example:** 3 is a factor of 12, as are 2, 4 and 6.

   **Example:** The factors of the Klein four-group are both cyclic of order 2.

   **Example:** The formula adjusts for region and season by multiplying by a prescribed factor for each.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Influence; a phenomenon that affects the nature, the magnitude, and/or the timing of a consequence.

   **Usage:** causal analysis

   **Example:** The launch temperature was a factor of the Challenger disaster.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A resource used in the production of goods or services, a factor of production.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** economics; sciences
