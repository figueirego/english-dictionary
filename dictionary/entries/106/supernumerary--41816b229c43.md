### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who works in a group, association, or public office without forming part of the regular staff (the numerary).

   **Example:** The judge was a supernumerary, helping the regular judges whenever there was a surplus amount of work.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An extra or walk-on, often non-speaking, in a film or play; a spear carrier.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** broadcasting; entertainment; film; lifestyle; media; television; theater

   **Source synonyms:** supe; super

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Something which is beyond the prescribed or standard amount or number.

   **Example:** They have a supernumerary hair whorl on their head, since they have three hair whorls.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An animal which has not formed a pair bond and is therefore single.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences; zoology

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A married man or woman who is a secular member of Opus Dei, a Roman Catholic religious institution.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Catholicism; Christianity; Roman-Catholicism
