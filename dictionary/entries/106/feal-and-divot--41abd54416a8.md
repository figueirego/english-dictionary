## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A predial servitude (a legal interest by a non-owner connected with a piece of land) that grants the dominant tenant the right to cut out portions of turf for the construction or repair of sod structures such as houses, fences, or roofs.

   **Usage:** uncountable; Scots law

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The construction technique for building sod structures by building walls out of feal and covering them with divot.

   **Usage:** Scotland; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Sod that is used for construction.

   **Usage:** Scotland; uncountable
