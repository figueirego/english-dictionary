### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A channel or pathway through which something is conducted, carried, etc.
   1. A pipe or channel for conveying water, etc.

      **Example:** This channel is a conduit to send the excess water back to the millpond.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A channel or pathway through which something is conducted, carried, etc.
   1. A duct or tube into which electrical cables may be pulled&#58; electrical conduit.

      **Example:** Last week the electricians were running conduit, and this week they'll be pulling cable.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A channel or pathway through which something is conducted, carried, etc.
   1. Any pattern, typically composed of still lifes or oscillators, used to transfer an active region to another location without being destroyed or permanently modified in the process.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** cellular-automata; computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A channel or pathway through which something is conducted, carried, etc.
   1. A means by which something is transmitted.

      **Usage:** figuratively

      **Example:** The medium considered herself a conduit for messages from the spirit world.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**
