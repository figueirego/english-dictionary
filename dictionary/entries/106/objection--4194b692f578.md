### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of objecting.

   **Example:** last-minute objection

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A statement expressing opposition, or a reason or cause for expressing opposition (generally followed by the adposition to).

   **Example:** I have no objection to any person's religion.

   **Source synonyms:** quarrel; complaint; grievance (formal)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
