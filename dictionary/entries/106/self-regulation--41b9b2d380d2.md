## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The regulation by a person of their own behaviour without external control, governance, or monitoring.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** self-command; self-control; self-discipline; self-mastery; willpower

   **Source antonyms:** undisciplinedness

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The regulation by an organization of its own operation without external control, governance, or monitoring.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** self-governance; autonomy

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The regulation by a device or machine of its own operation without external control, governance, or monitoring.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** automation
