### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A normally regular beat felt when arteries near the skin (for example, at the neck or wrist) are depressed, caused by the heart pumping blood through them; the qualitative nature of this beat.

   **Example:** Her pulse was thready and weak.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; physiology; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The rate of this beat as an indication of a person's health.

   **Usage:** broadly; metonymically

   **Example:** Her pulse was 110 at 8 a.m.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; physiology; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** heart rate; HR

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A beat or throb; also, a repeated sequence of such beats or throbs.

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The focus of energy or vigour of an activity, place, or thing; also, the feeling of bustle, busyness, or energy in a place; the heartbeat.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** You can really feel the pulse of the city in this district.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An (increased) amount of a substance (such as a drug or an isotopic label) given over a short time.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A setting on a food processor which causes it to work in a series of short bursts rather than continuously, in order to break up ingredients without liquidizing them; also, a use of this setting.

   **Usage:** attributive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** cooking; food; lifestyle

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The beat or tactus of a piece of music or verse; also, a repeated sequence of such beats.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; human-sciences; lifestyle; linguistics; music; phonology; prosody; sciences

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A brief burst of electromagnetic energy, such as light, radio waves, etc.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics
