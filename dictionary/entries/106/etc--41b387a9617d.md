### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Abbreviation of et cetera&#58; and so on; used to note that the rest of a list or piece of information has been left out on the assumption that it is similar or already known.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of

   **Example:** The grocery shop sells cucumbers, lettuce, radishes, etc.

   **Example:** I like video games of strategy (Civilization, SimCity, etc.) and speed (such as Neverball).

   **Source synonyms:** and so forth; and so on; and the rest; blah blah blah; et al.; you name it; mul&#58;...

   **Alternative form of:** [et cetera](../2d0/et-cetera--b40bfd1382a4.md)
