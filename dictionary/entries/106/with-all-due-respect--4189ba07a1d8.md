## Prep\_phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A phrase used before disagreeing with someone or saying something they may find offensive, usually considered polite.

   **Usage:** formal; idiomatic

   **Example:** With all due respect, Sir, I don't think that's the case.

   **Example:** It seems that you are, with all due respect, wrong in this particular case — as you see the murder took place at three o'clock.

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** with due respect; no offense
