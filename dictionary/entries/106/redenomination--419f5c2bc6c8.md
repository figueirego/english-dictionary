### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The process of redenominating.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The recalibration of the face value of a currency, most frequently by multiplying that value by a power of 10, sometimes followed by the demonetization of any cash issued with the original face value.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** After the redenomination, the new peso was worth 100 old pesos.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; economics; finance; sciences
