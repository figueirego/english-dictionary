## Noun

### Forms

- sergeant majors — plural
- sergeants major — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A senior, noncommissioned appointment in the Army of a company or unit, directly responsible to the officer in charge for their soldiers' performance, standards, discipline, morale, welfare, et al, that can be held by a Warrant Officer or above.

   **Usage:** Canada

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The most senior rank of non-commissioned officer in the United States Army, ranking above a first sergeant. In the United States Marine Corps it ranks above First Sergeant and is equal in grade to Master Gunnery Sergeant.

   **Usage:** US; plural sergeants major

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An appointment (not a rank) in the British Army and Royal Marines, denoting the most senior non-commissioned officer in a battalion, regiment or company.

   **Usage:** UK; plural sergeant majors

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An appointment (not a rank) in the Singapore Army and National Cadet Corps, denoting the most senior non-commissioned officer in a battalion, regiment, company or unit.

   **Usage:** Singapore; plural sergeant majors

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A pintano, (Abudefduf saxatilis), a species of damselfish.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Abrota ganga, a butterfly of Southeast Asia.
