### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To express feelings of pain, dissatisfaction, or resentment.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Joe was always complaining about the noise made by his neighbours.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To state the presence of something negative; to indicate that one is suffering from something; to report (e.g.) a symptom.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The patient complained of headache, nausea, and joint pain.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To make a formal accusation or bring a formal charge.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** They've complained about me to the police again.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To creak or squeak, as a timber or wheel.

   **Example:** the complaining bed-springs
