## Noun · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The 49-day period from the second day of Passover to Shavuot, begun and ended with an offering of a sheaf of barley and noted nightly during evening prayer.

   **Usage:** Judaism; uncountable; usually

   **Example:** The counting of the Omer—the Sefiras HaOmer—is a mitzvah.

   **Source context (verbatim):** with 'the'

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Alternative form of omer&#58; the sheaf of barley offered on the second day of Passover.

   **Usage:** Judaism; alt-of; alternative; uncountable
