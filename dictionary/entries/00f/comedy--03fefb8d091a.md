**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Usage:** countable; historical

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. a light, amusing play with a happy ending

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** A Midsummer Night's Dream is among Shakespeare's most famous comedies.

   **Usage:** Europe; Medieval; countable
