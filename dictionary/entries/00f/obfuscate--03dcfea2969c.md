### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make dark; to overshadow.

   **Example:** The squid's ink obfuscated its presence.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To deliberately make more confusing in order to conceal the truth.

   **Example:** obfuscate facts

   **Example:** Can weakness be really obfuscated?

   **Example:** Before leaving the scene, the murderer set a fire in order to obfuscate any evidence of his identity.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To alter code while preserving its behavior but concealing its structure and intent.

   **Example:** We need to obfuscate these classes before we ship the final release.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences
