### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The method by which the field umpire restarts play at a neutral contest after a stoppage within the field of play, involving the throwing or bouncing of the ball up between two players, known as rucks, who then attempt to win possession for their teams.

   **Usage:** Australian rules football
