## Adjective · Record 2

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Coterminous&#58; having the same scope or range, or meeting at the ends.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Differing only by a whole number of complete circles.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** When you try to draw two coterminal angles on a circle, you will find that you only get one line.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences

   **Source context (verbatim):** of two angles

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. Describing an object in a category, such that there is precisely one morphism that maps that object to every object in the category.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** category-theory; computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences
