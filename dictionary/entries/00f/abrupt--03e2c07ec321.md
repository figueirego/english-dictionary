### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Broken away (from restraint).

   **Usage:** obsolete; rare

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Without notice to prepare the mind for the event; sudden; hasty; unceremonious.

   **Example:** The party came to an abrupt end when the parents of our host arrived.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Curt in manner.

   **Source synonyms:** brusque; rude; uncivil; impolite; terse; brisk

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Having sudden transitions from one subject or state to another; unconnected; disjointed.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Broken off.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Extremely steep or craggy as if broken up; precipitous.
