### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A non-specific rule or principle that provides direction to action or behaviour.

   **Example:** He considered the Ten Commandments more as a guideline than a requirement.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A plan or explanation to guide one in setting standards or determining a course of action.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
