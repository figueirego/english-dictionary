### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Something added; especially, such an addition added to make up for a deficiency.

   **Source synonyms:** accessory; accompaniment; addition; add-on; adjunct; affix; annex; infusion; apanage; appendage; appendix (obsolete); extra; increase; increment; supplement

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An extension to a document or publication that adds information, corrects errors, or brings up to date.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An additional section of a newspaper devoted to a specific subject; an additional section of a journal issue, or an additional issue, for a similar purpose.

   **Example:** the literary supplement

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** journalism; literature; media; publishing; sciences; scientific

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An angle that, when added to a given angle, makes 180°; a supplementary angle.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A vitamin, herbal extract, or chemical compound ingested to meet dietary deficiencies or enhance muscular development; the dosage form that conveys or embodies it (e.g., tablet, capsule, pill, powder, beverage).

   **Usage:** nutrition

   **Example:** Vitamins, minerals, and essential fatty acids must come from either the diet or supplements.

   **Example:** Menstruators are likelier to need an iron supplement than non-menstruators, whose diet alone is usually sufficient.

   **Example:** She wasn't getting enough iron, so they prescribed a supplement.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** bodybuilding; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A surcharge, additional cost, especially for food in a restaurant.

   **Example:** There is a £2 supplement if you choose the steak.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. An adjunct that is nonessential, especially a supplementary relative clause.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** grammar; human-sciences; linguistics; sciences
