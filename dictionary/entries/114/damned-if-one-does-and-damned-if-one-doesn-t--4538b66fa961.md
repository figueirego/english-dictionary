### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of a situation where either choice results in a negative outcome.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; informal; not-comparable

   **Example:** If John leaves for New York to follow his career, his father will disinherit him. He's damned if he does and damned if he doesn't.

   **Example:** Damned if I do and damned if I don't.

   **Example:** Damned if you do and damned if you don't.
