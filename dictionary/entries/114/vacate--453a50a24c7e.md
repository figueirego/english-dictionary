### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To move out of a dwelling or other property, either by choice or by eviction.

   **Example:** I have to vacate my house by midday, as the new owner is moving in.

   **Example:** You are hereby ordered to vacate the premises within 14 days.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To leave an office or position.

   **Example:** He vacated his coaching position because of the corruption scandal.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To have a court judgement set aside; to annul.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The judge vacated the earlier decision when new evidence was presented.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To leave an area, usually as a result of orders from public authorities in the event of a riot or natural disaster.

   **Example:** If you do not immediately vacate the area, we will make you leave with tear gas!
