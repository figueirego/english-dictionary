### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Being a generically manufactured product, rebranded with the marketer's or retailer's trade dress&#58; synonym of private label.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; marketing

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Unbranded; being a brand name product which has had a white label applied to remove branding so that it can be resold for cheaper without undercutting the value of the brand.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Being a vinyl record with a white label or white background (rather than artwork), typically used for test records or promotion.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music
