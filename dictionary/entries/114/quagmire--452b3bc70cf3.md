### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A swampy, soggy area of ground.

   **Example:** That quagmire regularly ‘swallows’ caught-up hikers’ boots

   **Source synonyms:** marsh; marshland; mire; quag

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A perilous, mixed up and troubled situation; a hopeless tangle.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** The paperwork got lost in a quagmire of bureaucracy.

   **Example:** Those election results are a quagmire for any coalition except one of national union.
