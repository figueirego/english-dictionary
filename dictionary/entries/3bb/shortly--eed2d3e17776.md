### Pronunciation

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In a short or brief time or manner; quickly.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In or after a short time; soon.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The past perfect progressive is used for actions and situations which had continued up to a past moment or shortly before it.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

   **Usage:** not-comparable
