### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To fix the mind upon (something, or something to be accomplished); be intent upon

   **Usage:** ambitransitive; usually; or "on"/"upon" + noun

   **Example:** He intends to go to university.

   **Example:** They evidently intended some mischief.

   **Example:** After I've finished my contract I never intend to teach teenagers again.

   **Source context (verbatim):** followed by particle "to" + verb

   **Source synonyms:** mean; purport (formal); design; plan; think; propose (Northern-England; Scotland); purpose; have a mind to; foremind; mint; mean to

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To fix the mind on; attend to; take care of; superintend; regard.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To stretch to extend; distend.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To strain; make tense.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To intensify; strengthen.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To apply with energy.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To bend or turn; direct, as one’s course or journey.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To design mechanically or artistically; fashion; mold.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**
