## Noun · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Forms

- cyclers — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Anything with a cyclic (repetitious) behaviour. Something that cycles between different states.

   **Example:** The battery cycler allows us to switch between batteries as each one runs out.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An orbit that approaches two astronomical bodies on a regular basis.

   **Example:** The Earth-Mars cyclers could potentially be used for transporting people between the two planets.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astronomy; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A, usually man-made, object that follows such an orbit.

   **Usage:** broadly; space flight

   **Example:** If we had more than one cycler then the length of time between the outbound and inbound journeys could be reduced to a few months.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A person with bipolar disorder, often used when comparing the speed of mood swings.

   **Usage:** euphemistic

   **Example:** My son's a fast cycler which is much harder for people to understand.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; psychology; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A device for performing dialysis; for mechanically purifying blood.

   **Example:** The only issue I have had with my cycler was when the cat started chewing on the connecting line.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Ellipsis of thermal cycler, a machine for creating multiple copies of DNA sequences.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; ellipsis

   **Example:** We took samples from the tubes in the cycler at regular intervals

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biochemistry; biology; chemistry; microbiology; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

   **Alternative form of:** [thermal cycler](../2f6/thermal-cycler--bda2125a0473.md)

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A task in a cycle of tasks where the status of being active task is passed around the loop.

   **Example:** When the last cycler has run for 200ms we pass control back to the first one and begin again.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences
