### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An instruction to play gradually more loudly, denoted by a long, narrow angle with its apex on the left ( &lt; ), by musicians called a hairpin.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A gradual increase of anything, especially to a dramatic climax.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** Their fighting rose in a fearsome crescendo.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The climax of a gradual increase.

   **Usage:** figuratively; nonstandard

   **Example:** Their arguing rose to a fearsome crescendo.
