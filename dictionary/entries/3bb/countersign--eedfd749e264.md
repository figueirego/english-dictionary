## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A second signature added to a document to affirm the validity of the signature of the first person.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The response to a sign or signal.

   **Example:** Only those who give the countersign may enter the Masonic Lodge.
