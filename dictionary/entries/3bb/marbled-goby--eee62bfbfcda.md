### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A prized freshwater food fish of species Oxyeleotris marmorata, having a large head, symmetrical patterning on its dorsal fins, and rounded, outstretched pectoral fins.

   **Source synonyms:** marble goby; soon hock (Singapore)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A goby native to the eastern Atlantic, of species Pomatoschistus marmoratus.

   **Source synonyms:** marble goby
