### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of unknown origin.

   **Example:** He received a mysterious phone call this morning.

   **Source synonyms:** untraceable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Having unknown qualities.

   **Example:** Our boss is a mysterious man who hardly ever meets with us.

   **Source synonyms:** esoteric; uncertain; undefined

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Difficult to understand.

   **Example:** Why he left without saying goodbye is quite mysterious.

   **Source synonyms:** abstruse; Chinese; dense; enigmatic; fathomless; bottomless; Greek; inapprehensible; incognizable; incomprehensible; ineffable; inenubilable (formal; literary); inexplicable; impenetrable; insensible; inscrutable; insoluble; insolvable; mysterious; nonunderstandable; obscure; retruse (obsolete); tenebrose (figuratively); thick; undecipherable; undiscoverable; unexplainable; unfathomable; ungraspable; unintelligible; unknowable; ununderstandable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Deliberately evasive or enigmatic.

   **Example:** Stop being mysterious and just tell me what you want.

   **Source synonyms:** abstruse; behind the scenes; catlike; backroom; backstairs; stealth (slang); clandestine; cloak-and-dagger; concealed; confidential; covert; deep-laid; dern; disguised; furtive; huggermugger; hush-hush; inconspicuous; incognito; low profile; lurksome; lurky; mousy; obfuscated; off-the-books; on the down-low; on the sly; secret; secretive; slinky; sly; sneaky; stealthy; sub rosa; surreptitious; under-the-table; under the radar; under wraps; undercover; underground; unperceived
