## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A pitted, now usually metal, cup-shaped cap worn on the tip of a finger, which is used in sewing to push the needle through material.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; manufacturing; sewing; textiles

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. As much as fills a thimble (sense 1); a thimbleful.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An object which resembles a thimble (sense 1) in shape or size.
   1. A thimble or similar object used in thimblerig (“a game of skill which requires the bettor to guess under which of three thimbles or small cups a pea-sized object has been placed after the person operating the game rapidly rearranges them”).

      **Usage:** also; attributive

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** games

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An object which resembles a thimble (sense 1) in shape or size.
   1. A socket in machinery shaped like a thimble.

      **Usage:** also; attributive

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; technology

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A metal ring which a cable or rope intended for attaching to other things is looped around as a protection against chafing.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A ring- or tube-shaped component such as a ferrule.
