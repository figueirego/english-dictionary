### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. used to indicate a paraphrase or the chance of an error in the details of reported speech.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** When he hit his finger with the hammer, he said "ouch" or words to that effect.
