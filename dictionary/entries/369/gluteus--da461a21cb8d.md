### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of the several muscles of the nates, each of which arises from the pelvis and inserts into a femur.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Short for gluteus maximus, either of a pair of large muscles in the human buttocks.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; informal; slang

   **Example:** I'm going to kick your gluteus.

   **Alternative form of:** [gluteus maximus](../139/gluteus-maximus--4e623af1125d.md); either of a pair of large muscles in the human buttocks
