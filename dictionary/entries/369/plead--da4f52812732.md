### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To present (an argument or a plea), especially in a legal case.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive; copulative

   **Example:** The defendant has decided to plead not guilty.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To beg, beseech, or implore, especially emotionally.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** He pleaded with me not to leave the house.

   **Example:** He was pleading for mercy.

   **Source synonyms:** appeal; ask; beg; request; entreat

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To offer by way of excuse.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Not wishing to attend the banquet, I pleaded illness.

   **Example:** It is no defence to plead that you were only obeying orders.

   **Source synonyms:** allege; claim; cry; maintain

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To discuss by arguments.

   **Usage:** transitive
