## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The principle by which property rights extend vertically upwards and, sometimes, downwards from a property; it is used to describe air rights or no-fly zones, as well as rights to resources under the soil, such as mineral rights, among others.
