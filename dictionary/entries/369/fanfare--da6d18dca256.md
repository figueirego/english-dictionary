### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A flourish of trumpets or horns as to announce; a short and lively air performed on hunting horns during the chase.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** They played a short fanfare to announce the arrival of the king.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A show of ceremony or celebration.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The town opened the new library with fanfare and a speech from the mayor.
