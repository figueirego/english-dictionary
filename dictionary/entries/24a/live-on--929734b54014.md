### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To endure.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** Her memory lives on in our hearts.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To survive solely by consuming a certain thing.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** When he was in the rainforest, he lived on bugs and rainwater.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To rely on financially.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** She lives on a stipend from her parents.

   **Example:** I'm still living on the royalties from my first novel.
