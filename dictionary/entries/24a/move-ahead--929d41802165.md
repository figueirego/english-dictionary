### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To continue without being distracted; to keep going.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To proceed; to make or signify progress

   **Usage:** broadly; figuratively

   **Example:** the construction project is moving ahead

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To go on to something else.

   **Example:** the committee moved ahead to the next item on the agenda
