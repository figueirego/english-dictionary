**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of or related to the metal lead, particularly
   1. Made of lead.

      **Usage:** archaic

      **Source synonyms:** lead; plumbic; plumbous; leady (uncommon); saturnine (archaic)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of or related to the metal lead, particularly
   1. Resembling the color of lead, metallic or dark gray; (of daylight) dim, overcast.

      **Usage:** figuratively

      **Example:** The sky was dark and overcast, a leaden pall producing a deep gloom.

      **Source synonyms:** lead; plumbic; plumbous; leady (uncommon); saturnine (archaic); bland; colourless; dim; dingy; dull; faint; lackluster; leaden; muddy; sad (figuratively); sober; uncolorful; unholiday; wan
