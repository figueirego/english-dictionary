### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In a fabulous manner.
   1. In a fashionable or glamorous manner.

      **Example:** That girl is fabulously beautiful.

      **Source synonyms:** fantastically; incredibly; fashionably; glamorously

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In a fabulous manner.
   1. In an apocryphal or mythical manner; in fables.

      **Example:** Burying its head in the sand is fabulously attributed to the ostrich.

      **Source synonyms:** fantastically; incredibly; mythically; legendarily; parabolically; apocryphally
