### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An old French copper coin equal to one twentieth of a livre or twelve deniers; one sou is to the livre as one shilling is to the pound.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Cent; pocket money.

   **Usage:** dated; slang

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A thing of the smallest value; a whit; a jot.

   **Usage:** dated

   **Example:** I do not care a sou for your excuses.
