### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A board or table used to openly hold and display silver plate and other dishware; a sideboard; a buffet.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Things displayed on a sideboard; dishware, particularly valuable plate.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A cabinet, closet, or other piece of furniture with shelves intended for storing cookware, dishware, or food; similar cabinets or closets used for storing other items.

   **Example:** Put the cups back into the cupboard.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A closet for storing coats.

   **Usage:** Pennsylvania; UK; Western

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Things stored in a cupboard; particularly food.

   **Usage:** obsolete
