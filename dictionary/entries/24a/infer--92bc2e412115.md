**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To introduce (something) as a reasoned conclusion; to conclude by reasoning or deduction, as from premises or evidence.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** conclude; deduce; educe; construe

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Source synonyms:** imply; suggest; entail
