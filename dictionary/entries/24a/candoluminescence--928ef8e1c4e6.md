## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The light given off by certain materials at high temperatures (usually when exposed to a flame) that has an intensity at some wavelengths which can be higher than the blackbody emission expected from incandescence at the same temperature.

   **Usage:** uncountable
