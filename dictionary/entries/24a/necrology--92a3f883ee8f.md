### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A church register containing the names of those connected with the church who have died.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; church history

   **Source synonyms:** obituary (historical)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A listing of people who have died during a specific period of time.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A notice of death.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** obituary

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

   **Usage:** uncountable
