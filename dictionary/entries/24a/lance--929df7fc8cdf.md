### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A weapon of war, consisting of a long shaft or handle and a steel blade or head; a spear carried by horsemen.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A wooden spear, sometimes hollow, used in jousting or tilting, designed to shatter on impact with the opposing knight’s armour.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A whaling implement consisting of a long wooden shaft with a projecting iron shank tipped with a broad, leaf-shaped cutting blade, used to kill harpooned whales by repeated thrusts.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A soldier armed with a lance; a lancer.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war
