### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In an uncertain or unspecified location.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** I must have left my glasses somewhere.

   **Example:** I've hidden candy somewhere in this room.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To an uncertain or unspecified location.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** He plans to go somewhere warm for his vacation.

   **Example:** I have to go somewhere at lunch. Can I meet you at 2?

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. At some unspecified point.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** I don't remember the exact number, but it was somewhere between 200 and 300.
