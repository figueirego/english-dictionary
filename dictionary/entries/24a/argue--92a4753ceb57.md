### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To show grounds for concluding (that); to indicate, imply.

   **Example:** The new increase in crime argues for even tougher jail sentences, according to some.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To debate, disagree, or discuss opposing or differing viewpoints; to controvert; to wrangle.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** He also argued for stronger methods to be used against China.

   **Example:** He argued as follows&#58; America should stop Lend-Lease convoying, because it needs to fortify its own Army with the supplies.

   **Example:** The two boys argued over a disagreement about the science project.

   **Source synonyms:** submit; spar

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To have an argument, a quarrel.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source synonyms:** altercate; bicker; wrangle; remonstrate

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To present (a viewpoint or an argument therefor).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He argued his point.

   **Example:** He argued that America should stop Lend-Lease convoying because it needed to fortify its own Army with the supplies.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To prove.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To accuse.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive
