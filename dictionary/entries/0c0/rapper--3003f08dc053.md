### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who, or that which, raps or knocks.
   1. The knocker of a door.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One who, or that which, raps or knocks.
   1. A swinging knocker for making signals at the mouth of a shaft.

      **Usage:** historical

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; mining

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. One who, or that which, raps or knocks.
   1. A spiritualistic medium who claims to receive communications from spirits in the form of knocking sounds.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A performer of rap music, or someone who raps in any form of music.

   **Example:** He rose from the ghetto to become a successful rapper.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A sword.

   **Usage:** Scotland; obsolete

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**
