### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To disallow or reject.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I wanted to go to the party, but I was denied.

   **Source antonyms:** allow; grant

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To assert that something is not true.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to deny a rumour to deny an allegation

   **Example:** I deny that I was at the party.

   **Example:** Everyone knows he committed the crime, but he still denies it.

   **Source synonyms:** contradict; disclaim; gainsay; refute; withsay

   **Source antonyms:** affirm; confirm

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To refuse to give or grant something to someone.

   **Usage:** ditransitive

   **Example:** My father denied me a good education.

   **Source antonyms:** grant

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To take something away from someone; to deprive of.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To prevent from scoring.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To disclaim connection with, responsibility for, etc.; to refuse to acknowledge; to disown; to abjure; to disavow.

   **Example:** Jesus prophesied that by the time the cock crowed, Peter would have denied him three times.

   **Source synonyms:** disclaim; forswear; renounce; abjure; deny; disavow; disclaim; disown; forsay (archaic); forswear; renounce; repudiate; wash one's hands of

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To refuse (to do or accept something).

   **Usage:** obsolete
