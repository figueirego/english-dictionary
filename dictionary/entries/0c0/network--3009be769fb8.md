### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any interconnected group or system.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** A network of roads crisscrossed the country.

   **Example:** The many species that live in the forest canopy constitute a network of life via their complex interactions.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any interconnected group or system.
   1. A set of people with sociocultural connections to one another; especially, such a set consisting of occupational or business connections.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Example:** To get a job in today's economy, it is important to have a strong network.

      **Example:** For people with mental health challenges, having a support network of family and friends can improve the chances of maintaining successful mental health care.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any interconnected group or system.
   1. A group of affiliated television stations that broadcast common programs from a parent company.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** broadcasting; media

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any interconnected group or system.
   1. A computer network&#58; multiple computers and other devices connected together to share information.

      **Usage:** Internet; countable; uncountable

      **Example:** The copy machine is connected to the network so it can now serve as a printer.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

**Source sense R1.S5**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; literal

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To interact socially for the purpose of getting connections or personal advancement.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Many people find it worthwhile to network for jobs and information.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To connect two or more computers or other computerized devices.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** If we network his machine to the server, he will be able to see all the files.

   **Usage:** transitive
