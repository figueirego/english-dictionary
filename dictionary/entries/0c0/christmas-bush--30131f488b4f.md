### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Plants, of mostly unrelated species, that bloom around Christmas time.
   1. A small tree or shrub that occurs in all Australian states of species Bursaria spinosa.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Plants, of mostly unrelated species, that bloom around Christmas time.
   1. A New South Wales Christmas bush), a tall shrub from Australia of species Ceratopetalum gummiferum.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Plants, of mostly unrelated species, that bloom around Christmas time.
   1. A tropical shrub of species Chromolaena odorata, of the sunflower family.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Plants, of mostly unrelated species, that bloom around Christmas time.
   1. A poisonous shrub of species Comocladia dodonaea, of Caribbean islands.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Plants, of mostly unrelated species, that bloom around Christmas time.
   1. A New Zealand Christmas bush of species Metrosideros excelsa and Metrosideros kermadecensis)

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Plants, of mostly unrelated species, that bloom around Christmas time.
   1. A Victorian Christmas bush (Prostanthera lasianthos), a shrub of Australia.
