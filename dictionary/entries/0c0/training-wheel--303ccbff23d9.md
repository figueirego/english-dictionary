### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A wheel forming part of the machinery of a cannon or a similar firearm which is turned to train or aim the weapon at a target.

   **Usage:** historical

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Synonym of trailing wheel (“on a steam locomotive, an unpowered wheel or axle located behind the driving wheels”).

   **Usage:** archaic

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** rail-transport; railways; transport

   **Source synonyms:** trailing wheel (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. One of a pair of small wheels, each attached to one side of a bicycle's rear wheel to provide support for new riders.

   **Usage:** Canada; US; in-plural

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** cycling; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

   **Source synonyms:** stabiliser; stabilizer
