## Noun

### Forms

- treasure chests — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A chest filled with treasure, especially one used by pirates, etc.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A treasury, especially a state or royal treasury.

   **Usage:** metonymically

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any source of something valuable or beautiful.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** a treasure chest of poems

   **Source synonyms:** gold mine; treasure trove
