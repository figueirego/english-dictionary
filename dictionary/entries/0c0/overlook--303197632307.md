### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To offer a view (of something) from a higher position.

   **Example:** Our hotel room overlooks the lake.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To fail to notice; to look over and beyond (anything) without seeing it.

   **Example:** These errors were overlooked by the proofreaders.

   **Source synonyms:** misheed; ignore; misheed; misregard; miss; overlook; overpass; oversee (obsolete)

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To pretend not to have noticed (something, especially a mistake or flaw); to pass over (something) without censure or punishment.

   **Example:** I’m not willing to overlook such bad behaviour.

   **Source synonyms:** take no notice of; brush off (idiomatic); discount; disregard; dissemble; despise; ignore; misheed; misregard; neglect; pass; pass by (idiomatic); pay no heed (idiomatic); put aside (idiomatic); set at nought (archaic); shrug off (idiomatic); slough; take no notice of (idiomatic); tune out (idiomatic); unheed; unmind

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To look down upon from above or from a higher location.

   **Example:** The hill overlooks the valley.

   **Source synonyms:** survey; look over; luster; lustrate

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To supervise, oversee; to watch over.

   **Usage:** archaic; sometimes non-native speakers' English

   **Example:** to overlook a gang of laborers

   **Example:** to overlook one who is writing a letter

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To observe or watch (someone or something) surreptitiously or secretly.

   **Usage:** archaic

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

**Source sense R2.S7**

7. To inspect (something); to examine; to look over carefully or repeatedly.

   **Usage:** archaic

   **Source synonyms:** scrutinize; analyze; check; canvass; examine; inspect; thresh; look over; luster (obsolete); lustrate (obsolete); overlook (archaic); probe; scrutinate; scrutinize; study; survey

<a id="r2-s8"></a>

**Source sense R2.S8**

8. To look upon with an evil eye; to bewitch by looking upon; to fascinate.

   **Usage:** archaic
