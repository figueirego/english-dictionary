   1. A small pellet containing aromatic substances, burned to diffuse a fragrance or to disinfect or fumigate.

      **Usage:** historical; specifically

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A medicinal pill, originally made of compressed herbs.

   **Usage:** historical; pharmacy

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A candy- or sweet-like lozenge, which, when sucked, releases substances that soothe a sore throat, and sometimes vapours to help unblock the nose or sinuses.
