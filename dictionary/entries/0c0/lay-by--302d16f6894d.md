### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To put away for future use; save; to build up as savings.

   **Example:** He had managed to lay by money for college through his years as a paperboy.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. (of a sailing vessel) To remain stationary while heading into the wind; to come to a standstill; heave to; lay to.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r2-s3"></a>
