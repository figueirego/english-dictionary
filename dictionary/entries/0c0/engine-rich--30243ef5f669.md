### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Pertaining to a condition where a rocket engine is being consumed and expelled as part of the rocket exhaust, where it is not a part of normal operations.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Pertaining to a condition where there is excessive fuel in the fuel-to-air ratio, in the running of an internal combustion engine.
