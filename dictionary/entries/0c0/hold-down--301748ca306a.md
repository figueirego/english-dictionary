### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To restrain; to check.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** Please hold the noise down.

   **Example:** You hold him down while I search him.

   **Source synonyms:** restrain; hold back; behedge; guard; govern (formal); smother; bind (figuratively); chasten; choke back; check; inhibit; catch (reflexive); contain; vanquish; cork (figuratively); curb; quench; quell; bury; curtail; dampen; hamshackle (figuratively); hold back (idiomatic); hold down (idiomatic); limit; repress; restrain; restrict; shackle (figuratively); stay; stifle; stop; straiten; subdue; suppress; throttle (figuratively); underbring; withhold; withstrain (archaic)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To continue, to hold and to manage well; to keep; maintain; support.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** I held down that job for years.

   **Example:** Thank you to everyone who's been holding us down while we've been going through this difficult time.
