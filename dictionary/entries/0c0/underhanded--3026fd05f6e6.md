### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Done by moving the hand (and arm) from below.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Sly, dishonest, corrupt, cheating.

   **Example:** His underhanded trick backfired and he was disqualified.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Insincere; sarcastic.

   **Example:** An underhanded compliment is actually criticism.

4. Secret; surreptitious.

   **Source synonyms:** sneaky
