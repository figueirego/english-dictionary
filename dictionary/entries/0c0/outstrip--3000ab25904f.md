### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To move more quickly than (someone or something) so as to outrun or leave it behind.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** We quickly outstripped the amateur runners.

   **Source synonyms:** overgo; overhaul; overtake

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To exceed or surpass (someone or something).

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** This year’s production has already outstripped last year’s.

   **Source synonyms:** outdo; transcend; exceed; excel (rare); forpass (obsolete); go beyond; breach; cap; outreach; outcompass; outstep; outstrip; overgo (obsolete); overpass; overreach; overstep; overstride; surpass; transgress; transcend; trespass (obsolete)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To exceed or overstep (a boundary or limit); to transgress.

   **Usage:** archaic; rare; transitive
