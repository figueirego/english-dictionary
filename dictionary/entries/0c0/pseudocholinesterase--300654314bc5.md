### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One of the two types of cholinesterase (the other being acetylcholinesterase), found primarily in the liver where it hydrolyses butyrylcholine.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biochemistry; biology; chemistry; microbiology; natural-sciences; physical-sciences
