### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An infectious disease caused by the bacterium Mycobacterium leprae, gradually producing nerve damage and patches of skin necrosis and historically handled by permanently quarantining its sufferers.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; pathology; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Similar contagious skin diseases causing light patches of scaly skin, particularly psoriasis, syphilis, vitiligo, scabies, and (biblical) the various diseases considered "tzaraath" in the Old Testament.

   **Usage:** dated; proscribed; uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Anything considered similarly permanent, harmful, and communicable, particularly when such a thing should be handled by avoidance or isolation of its victims.

   **Usage:** figuratively; uncountable; usually

   **Example:** Sin is a spiritual leprosy.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A contagious disease causing similar effects in animals, particularly
   1. Ellipsis of murine leprosy or feline leprosy (“diseases caused in rodents and cats by Mycobacterium lepraemurium”).

      **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; ellipsis; uncountable; usually; veterenary medicine

      **Alternative form of:** murine leprosy or feline leprosy

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A contagious disease causing similar effects in animals, particularly
   1. Synonym of mange and glanders in horses.

      **Usage:** obsolete; uncountable; usually; veterenary medicine

      **Source synonyms:** mange and glanders in horses (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Synonym of leprosarium&#58; a place for the housing of lepers in isolation from the rest of society.

   **Usage:** obsolete; rare; uncountable; usually

   **Source synonyms:** leprosarium (synonym; synonym-of)
