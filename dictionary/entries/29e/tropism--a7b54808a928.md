### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The turning of an organism (chiefly a plant) or part of an organism either towards or away from a stimulus; (countable) an instance of this.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A capability or tendency for a pathogen (chiefly a virus) to infect a type of cell, tissue, organ, or host organism.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable

   **Example:** host tropism tissue tropism

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; microbiology; natural-sciences; virology

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of a person&#58; an instinctive predilection or tendency; also (generally), a liking, a preference.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively
