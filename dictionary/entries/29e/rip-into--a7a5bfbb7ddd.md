### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To verbally attack or criticise.

   **Usage:** colloquial; idiomatic

   **Example:** The teacher ripped into Johnny when he found out he had plagiarised.

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To start to eat something.
