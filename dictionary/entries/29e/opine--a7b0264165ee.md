### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To express an opinion; to state as an opinion; to suppose, consider (that).

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** I opined that matters would soon become considerably worse.

   **Example:** "Your decisions," she opined, "have been unfailingly disastrous for this company."

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To give one's formal opinion (on or upon something).

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** I had to opine on the situation because I thought a different perspective was in order.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To suppose, consider as correct, or entertain, an opinion.

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive
