### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To cause something or someone to pass from a higher to a lower place.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To bowl.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; cricket; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To commit (someone) to a prison term.

   **Usage:** slang; transitive

   **Example:** Eventually she was caught, and sent down for twelve years.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To demote a player within the levels of professional baseball.

   **Usage:** US

   **Example:** After performing poorly for the first month of the season, he was sent down to the minor leagues.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; baseball; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To suspend or expel (an undergraduate) from university.

   **Usage:** Ireland; UK; dated; transitive; usually Oxbridge slang

   **Example:** He was sent down from Oxford for theft.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To send a person to the countryside to become a peasant-farmer for re-education; to get in touch with the fundamental values of peasant-based communism (as opposed to urban worker-class proletariat-based communism).

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** communism; government; human-sciences; ideology; philosophy; politics; sciences
