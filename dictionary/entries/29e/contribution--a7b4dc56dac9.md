### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Something given or offered that adds to a larger whole.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An amount of money given toward something.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** He made a contribution of $100 for the victims of the flood.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The act of contributing.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The taking part, often with the idea that it has led to (scientific etc.) progress.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
