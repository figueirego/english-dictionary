**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An initialism used to introduce an illustrative example or short list of examples&#58; for the sake of an example; for example.

   **Example:** Continents (e.g., Asia) contain many large bodies of water (e.g., lakes and inland seas) and many large flowing streams of water (i.e., rivers).
