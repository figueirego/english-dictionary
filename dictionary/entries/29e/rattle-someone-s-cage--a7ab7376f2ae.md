### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To demand attention from someone; to nag, nudge, or remind someone.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** Nobody has corrected the problem yet, so it's time to rattle their cage.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To anger or to annoy someone.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** If you really want to rattle his cage, ask him about his family.
