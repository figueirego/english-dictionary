### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Something that happens to someone by chance, a chance occurrence.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** The raffle is just a matter of luck.

   **Example:** Gilbert had some bad luck yesterday — he got pick-pocketed and lost fifty dollars.

   **Source synonyms:** chance; fortune

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Something that happens to someone by chance, a chance occurrence.
   1. A favourable chance occurrence.

      **Usage:** especially; uncountable; usually

      **Example:** Sometimes it takes a bit of luck to get success.

      **Example:** I couldn't believe my luck when I found a fifty dollar bill on the street.

      **Source synonyms:** chance; fortune; good luck; hap; fortune; fortuitousness; felicity (rare); fortune; fortuitousness; fortuity; good cess; good fortune; good luck; happiness (archaic); luck

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A superstitious feeling that brings fortune or success.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** He blew on the dice for luck.

   **Example:** I wish you lots of luck for the exam tomorrow.

   **Source synonyms:** fortune

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Success.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** I tried for ages to find a pair of blue suede shoes, but didn't have any luck.

   **Example:** He has a lot of luck with the ladies. Perhaps it's because of his new motorbike.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The results of a random number generator.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** The creators of tool-assisted speedruns often manipulate luck to get the most favorable results in order to save the most time.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences; video-games
