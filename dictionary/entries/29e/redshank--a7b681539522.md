### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of two species of Old World wading bird in the genus Tringa that have long red legs.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any member of the species Ceratodon purpureus of moss.

   **Source synonyms:** fire moss; purple horn toothed moss

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Lady's thumb or redleg (Persicaria maculosa), an herb in the buckwheat family.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A bare-legged person; one of the Scottish Highlanders, who wore kilts.

   **Usage:** derogatory; obsolete
