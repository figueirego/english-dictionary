### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. One who acts to confound or obfuscate; an obscurantist.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. A person who seeks to prevent or hinder enquiry and the advancement of knowledge or wisdom; an agent of endarkenment.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**
