### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A baseball game in which many runs are scored, especially by home runs.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Example:** The game turned into a 15-9 slugfest.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; baseball; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A game or match in which heavy blows are exchanged.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Example:** The championship bout was a slugfest; both boxers were bloodied.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports
