### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A beefsteak cut from the primal rib area of a bovine, with the rib bone attached to the steak cut. A bone-in rib steak.

   **Source synonyms:** cowboy steak; tomahawk steak; entrecôte

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A similar cut from another animal.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A bone-in or boneless cut of steak, either the rib or ribeye steak.

   **Usage:** broadly
