### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The people living within a political or geographical boundary.

   **Example:** The population of New Jersey will not stand for this!

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The people with a given characteristic.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** India has the third-largest population of English-speakers in the world.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A count of the number of residents within a political or geographical boundary such as a town, a nation or the world.

   **Example:** The town’s population is only 243.

   **Example:** population explosion; population growth
