### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To spend (time) in idleness; to waste; to consume.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To lose or spend time doing nothing, or without being employed in business.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** to idle in an IRC channel

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. Of an engine&#58; to run at a slow speed, or out of gear; to tick over.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To cause (an engine) to idle (run at a slow speed, or out of gear).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The driver idled his engine until the car finally stalled.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To make (workers, students, etc) idle; to leave without work.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a layoff or a labor strike
