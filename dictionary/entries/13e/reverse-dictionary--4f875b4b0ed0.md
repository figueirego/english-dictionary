## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A dictionary in which entries are sorted alphabetically by their last letter, then second-to-last, and so on&#58; Nausicaa would precede Hecuba, which would precede orca; influenza would precede cab, and so on.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A dictionary that lists words under the concepts they describe.

   **Source synonyms:** inverted dictionary
