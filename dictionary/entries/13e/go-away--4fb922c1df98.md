### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To depart or leave a place.

   **Example:** I'm not going to buy it. Please go away and don't come back.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To travel somewhere, especially on holiday or vacation.

   **Example:** Will you be going away at the weekend?

   **Example:** Are you going away this year?

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**
