### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To utter words indistinctly or unintelligibly; to utter inarticulate sounds

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The men were babbling, so we couldn't make sense of anything.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To talk incoherently; to utter meaningless words.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To talk too much; to chatter; to prattle.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** She babbled on for hours about the importance of some new gadget.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To make a continuous murmuring noise, like shallow water running over stones.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Hounds are said to babble, or to be babbling, when they are too noisy after having found a good scent.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To utter in an indistinct or incoherent way; to repeat words or sounds in a childish way without understanding.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To reveal; to give away (a secret).

   **Usage:** transitive
