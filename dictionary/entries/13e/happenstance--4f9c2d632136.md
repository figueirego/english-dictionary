### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The chance or random quality of an event or circumstance.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** by happenstance

   **Source synonyms:** accident; chance; coincidence; happenchance

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A chance or random event or circumstance.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** Our meeting today was a happenstance, but I'm glad we crossed paths.

   **Example:** the happenstances of life
