## Verb

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To set off an area or shape with chalk.

   **Usage:** literally

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To record a score or event, as if on a chalkboard.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To disallow or cancel

   **Usage:** idiomatic

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To chalk (someone) up dismissively or jeeringly.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** We had chalked him off as an idiot!

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To dismiss or be dismissive towards (someone or something); to reject (someone or something).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He seemed like an idiot, so I chalked him off.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To deduct from something; to separate from something.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The funds are going to be chalked off from the events fund.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To mark off (something).

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To kill (someone).

   **Usage:** slang; transitive
