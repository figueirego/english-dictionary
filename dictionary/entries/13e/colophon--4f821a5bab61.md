### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In manuscripts (typically before the invention of printing), the note, usually at the end, left by the scribe who copied it, giving information on his exemplar, where and when the copy was made, and sometimes, his own name.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** media; printing; publishing

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An inscription found at the end of, or interspersed with the contents of, Chinese paintings or scrolls, usually left by later literati, appreciators, or collectors.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A printer's or publisher's identifying inscription or logo appearing at the front or end of a book, or the same appearing on the spine or dust jacket.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** media; printing; publishing

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A page on a website identifying the details of its creation, such as the author's name and the technologies used.

   **Usage:** Internet; broadly

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A finishing stroke or crowning touch.

   **Usage:** obsolete
