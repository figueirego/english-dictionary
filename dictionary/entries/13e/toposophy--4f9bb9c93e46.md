## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A branch of ethnology that emphasizes the relationship between people and the place in which they live or from which they come.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anthropology; ethnology; human-sciences; sciences

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; philosophy; sciences
