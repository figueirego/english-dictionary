### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Written or printed in black letter.

   **Example:** a black-letter manuscript or book

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** media; printing; publishing

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Given to the study of books in black letter; old-fashioned.

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of or relating to the days in the calendar not marked with red letters as saints' days; unlucky; inauspicious.

   **Example:** black-letter days
