### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In theaters, or in terms of the theater.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The film premiered on 3 August before opening theatrically two days later.

   **Example:** The story was a great success in novel form, but did not perform well theatrically.
