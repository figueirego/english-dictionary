### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An enclosed area, attached to a chicken coop, in which poultry are allowed to run around and peck.

   **Source synonyms:** chicken pen; chickenyard; chookyard

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The act of "gapping it", i.e. emigrating from Rhodesia as a white inhabitant during the country's transition to independence.

   **Usage:** historical; slang
