### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Composed of or covered in slop; very wet.

   **Example:** The dog tracked sloppy mud through the kitchen!

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Careless, messy; not neat, elegant, or careful.

   **Example:** The carpenter did a sloppy job of building the staircase.

   **Source synonyms:** careless; cursory; derelict (US); heedless; irregardless; irrespective; lash (obsolete); lax; incautious; neglectful; negligent; reckless; regardless; remiss; slack; slapdash; sloppy; slovenly; slipshod; unfaithful

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Imprecise or loose.

   **Example:** a sloppy measurement; a sloppy fit

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Characteristic of or resembling AI slop.

   **Usage:** derogatory
