### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who believes that human beings have free will.

   **Source antonyms:** determinist

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person who exercises free will.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A person belonging to a sect that rejected the doctrine of predestination.

   **Usage:** derogatory; historical

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity; Protestantism

   **Source synonyms:** Arminian; Pelagian; Remonstrant; Semipelagian

   **Source antonyms:** Calvinist; predestinarian

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An immigrant to the United States who, upon arrival, voluntarily became an indentured servant.

   **Usage:** US; historical

   **Source synonyms:** redemptioner
