### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make sad or unhappy.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** It saddens me to think that I might have hurt someone.

   **Example:** I was saddened that you felt so little for me.

   **Source synonyms:** deject; depress

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To become sad or unhappy.

   **Source synonyms:** get a sad on; unhappy; aggrieve (obsolete); besorrow; condole (obsolete); darken; engrieve (archaic); erme (obsolete); gloom; grieve; grieven; bleed; lament; moan (poetic); [mourn](../265/mourn--99507470e0a5.md); remorse; repent; regret; rue (archaic); sigh; sorrow; wayment (obsolete); yearn (obsolete)

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To darken a color during dyeing.

   **Usage:** rare; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** shade; tinge

**Source sense R1.S4**
