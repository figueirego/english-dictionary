### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To try to see, to pay attention to with one’s eyes.
   1. As an intransitive verb, often with "at".

      **Usage:** intransitive

      **Example:** They kept looking at me.

      **Example:** Don’t look in the closet.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To try to see, to pay attention to with one’s eyes.
   1. As a transitive verb, often in the imperative; chiefly takes relative clause as direct object.

      **Usage:** colloquial; transitive

      **Example:** Look what you did to him!

      **Example:** Look who's back!

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To appear, to seem.

   **Example:** It looks as if it’s going to rain soon. or It looks like it’s going to rain soon. or It looks like rain \[is coming\].

   **Example:** Our new boss looks to be friendly.

   **Example:** It looks as if \[or like\] I'm stuck with you.

   **Example:** (UK) He always looks like scoring a goal if not two.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To give an appearance of being.

   **Usage:** copulative

   **Example:** What a fright you looked in that hideous outfit.

   **Example:** You’re looking worried. Whaddya thinking about?

   **Example:** You looked a fool when you slipped on a banana peel.

   **Example:** You didn't use to look so dismal, but now you look/are looking positively unhappy.
