### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A unit of distance equal to one-eighth of a mile (220 yards, or 201.168 metres), now mainly used in measuring distances in farmland and horse racing.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A unit of land area one furlong (sense 1) square (ten acres, or about four hectares).

   **Usage:** dated

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An undefined portion of an unenclosed field.

   **Usage:** UK; dated; dialectal

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Synonym of stadion (“a Greek unit of distance based on standardized footraces, equivalent to about 185.4 metres”).

   **Usage:** historical

   **Source synonyms:** stade; stadium; stadion (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Synonym of headland (“unploughed boundary of a field”).

   **Usage:** dialectal; obsolete except UK

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** agriculture; business; lifestyle

   **Source synonyms:** headland (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Synonym of land (“the ground left unploughed between furrows”).

   **Usage:** dialectal; obsolete except UK

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** agriculture; business; lifestyle

   **Source synonyms:** land (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**
