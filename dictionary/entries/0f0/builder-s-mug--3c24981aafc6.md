### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Usage:** UK

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A unit of volumetric measure equivalent to the typical builder's mug, around 1/2-pint (10 oz.; 300 cc; 300 mL; 30 cL; 1 cup)

   **Usage:** UK; informal
