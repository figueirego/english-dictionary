### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To rise suddenly.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** He started up when he heard the scream.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To commence the operation of; to activate; to launch.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The four of them started up a law practice.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To begin to operate.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The engine started up right away.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To begin.

   **Usage:** colloquial; intransitive

   **Example:** The band started up playing.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To begin to make a sound

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** First a solitary blackbird started up, but soon the whole forest was alive with birdsong.
