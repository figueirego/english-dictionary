**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To touch by means of a mutual border, edge or end; to border on; to lie adjacent (to); to be contiguous (said of an area of land)

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** It was a time when Germany still abutted upon Russia.

   **Example:** His land abuts on the road.
