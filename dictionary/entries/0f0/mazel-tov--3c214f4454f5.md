### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used to express congratulations or good wishes, especially at weddings.

   **Source context (verbatim):** in a Jewish context

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Said after a glass has been accidentally broken, in reference to the tradition of breaking a glass as part of a Jewish wedding, after which "mazel tov" is sometimes said.

   **Usage:** humorous
