## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Someone whose job is to speak for someone's case in a court of law; a counsel.

   **Source synonyms:** attorney; counselor; abogado; advocate; attorney; avocat; counsel; counselor; latitat (obsolete; slang); lawman (rare); lawyer; legal representative; limb of the law (archaic; slang); man of law (archaic)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Anyone who argues the case of another; an intercessor.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A person who speaks in support of something, or someone; proponent

   **Source synonyms:** exponent

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A person who supports others to make their voices heard, or ideally for them to speak up for themselves.

   **Example:** Since she started working with her advocate, she has become much more confident.

   **Source synonyms:** friend

## Verb · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈæd.və.keɪt/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Forms

- advocates — present; singular; third-person
- advocating — participle; present
- advocated — participle; past
- advocated — past

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To plead in favour of; to defend by argument, before a tribunal or the public; to support, vindicate, or recommend publicly.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To encourage support for something.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I like trees, but I do not advocate living in them.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To engage in advocacy.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** We have been advocating for changes in immigration law.

   **Source context (verbatim):** with for

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To appeal from an inferior court to the Court of Session.

   **Usage:** Scots law

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To call a case before itself for decision.

   **Usage:** Scots law

   **Source context (verbatim):** in higher courts
