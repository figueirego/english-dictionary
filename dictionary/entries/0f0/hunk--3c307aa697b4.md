## Noun · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /hʌŋk/ — source labels: UK; US

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A large or dense piece of something.

   **Example:** a hunk of metal

   **Source synonyms:** chunk; lump; bit; chip; chunk; tranche; clip; crumb; crumbling; cutting; flake; fleak (dialectal; obsolete); fragment; hunk; lump; mammock (obsolete); ort; piece; piecemeal; portion; scrap; scraplet; slice; snead; snippet; steck (dialectal; obsolete)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An attractive man, especially one who is muscular.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Source synonyms:** beefcake; stud; beefcake; bona omi (Polari; dated); Chad (slang); chicken (Polari); chick magnet (US); dimber cove (obsolete); dreamboat (dated; humorous; informal); himbo (derogatory; slang; sometimes); hunk; lady-killer; man-meat (slang); pretty boy (slang); rizzler; spunk (Australia); stud; stud muffin (idiomatic); superhunk; twink (slang)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A record of differences between almost contiguous portions of two files (or other sources of information). Differences that are widely separated by areas which are identical in both files would not be part of a single hunk. Differences that are separated by small regions which are identical in both files may comprise a single hunk. Patches are made up of hunks.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A honyock.

   **Usage:** US; slang

   **Source synonyms:** hick; yokel; bogger (Ireland; UK); bogtrotter (Ireland); boor; bucolic; bydlo (Eastern; Europe; Russia); culchie (Ireland); carl; churl; corncob (US); cornhusker (US); country bumpkin; country cousin; clodhopper; dubbo (Australia); joskin; hayseed (US); hick; hob (obsolete); hobnail (obsolete); hog-rubber (obsolete); honyock (US); hunk (US); lob (obsolete); promdi (Philippines); provincial; rube (US); rustic; woolly back (UK); yokel

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**
