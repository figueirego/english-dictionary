### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. At the time referred to (by the present discussion), which is in the past for many discussions but is in the future for others.

   **Example:** You have to keep in mind that there were no computers at that time.

   **Example:** You have to remember that this database field is asking for your best estimate of what the exchange rate will be at that time — not today's exchange rate.

   **Source synonyms:** at the time
