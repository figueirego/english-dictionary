### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Synonym of supracargo
   1. An officer on board a merchant ship who is in charge of the cargo and its sale; if there are two such officers, the senior one, the other being the subcargo.

      **Usage:** historical

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** economics; sciences; shipping; transport

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Synonym of supracargo
   1. An agent or representative of a company in charge of its overseas business.

      **Usage:** obsolete

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** economics; sciences; shipping; transport

<a id="r1-s3"></a>
