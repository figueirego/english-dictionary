### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An organism that lives on or in another organism of a different species, deriving benefit from living on or in that other organism, while not contributing towards that other organism sufficiently to cover the cost to that other organism.

   **Example:** Lice, fleas, ticks, and mites are widely spread parasites.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An organism that lives on or in another organism of a different species, deriving benefit from living on or in that other organism, while not contributing towards that other organism sufficiently to cover the cost to that other organism.
   1. An internal parasite that is an animal or protist.

      **Usage:** informal; specifically

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A person who lives on other people's efforts or expense and gives little or nothing back.

   **Usage:** derogatory

   **Source synonyms:** bloodsucker; bludger (Australia; New-Zealand; slang); bummer (US; dated; slang); freeloader; free rider; gadfly (slang); leech; mooch; moocher; parasite; scrounge; scrounger; scunge (slang); smell-feast (archaic); sponge; sponger; sucker; trencher mate (archaic)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A sycophant or hanger-on.

   **Usage:** derogatory

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A climbing plant which is supported by a wall, trellis etc.

   **Usage:** literary; poetic

<a id="r1-s6"></a>
