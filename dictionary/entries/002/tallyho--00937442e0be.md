### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used to urge on a fox hunt, especially when the fox is sighted.

   **Usage:** UK

   **Example:** There he is! Coming out of that coppice! Tallyho tallyho tallyho!

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A cry used to signify encouragement when embarking on any somewhat daring endeavour.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A simple greeting, primarily used by the upper classes.

   **Example:** Tallyho, chaps! What's the time, anybody?

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Target sighted.
