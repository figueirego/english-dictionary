### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A problem or puzzle devised to exercise the brain and mind.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** She bought me a book of brainteasers, which is both entertaining and also supposedly good for decreasing dementia risk.

   **Source synonyms:** brain-tickler

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any difficult problem or puzzle.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** Trying to figure out how to square this circle is a brainteaser to say the least.

   **Source synonyms:** stumper; puzzler; enigma
