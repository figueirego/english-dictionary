### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Composed of inherently different or distinct elements; incongruous.

   **Example:** The board of the company was decidedly disparate, with no two members from the same social or economic background.

   **Source synonyms:** incongruous; mismatched; uncoordinated

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Essentially different; of different species, unlike but not opposed in pairs.

   **Source synonyms:** different; dissimilar; unalike

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Utterly unlike; incapable of being compared; having no common ground.

   **Source synonyms:** incommensurable
