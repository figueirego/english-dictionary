### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Intentionally, or after deliberation; not accidentally.

   **Example:** He deliberately broke that, didn't he?

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Taking one's time, slowly and carefully.

   **Example:** After being called upon, he strode deliberately up to the blackboard.
