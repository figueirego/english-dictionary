### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The same height at all places; parallel to a flat ground.

   **Example:** This table isn't quite level; see how this marble rolls off it?

   **Source synonyms:** flush

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. At the same height as some reference; constructed as level with.

   **Example:** We tried to hang the pictures so that the bottom of the frames were level with the dark line in the wallpaper.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Unvaried in frequency.

   **Example:** His pulse has been level for 12 hours.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Unvaried in volume.

   **Example:** His voice has been unchanged. It has been level for 12 hours.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Calm.

   **Example:** He kept a level head under stress.

   **Example:** He kept a level gaze.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. In the same position or rank.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Straightforward; direct; clear.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Well balanced; even; just; steady; impartial.

   **Example:** a level head; a level understanding

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. Of even tone; without rising or falling inflection; monotonic.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; phonetics; phonology; sciences
