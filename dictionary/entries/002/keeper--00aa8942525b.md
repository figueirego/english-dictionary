### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who keeps (retains) something.

   **Example:** Finders keepers; losers weepers.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One who remains or keeps in a place or position.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A fruit or vegetable that keeps (remains good) for some time without spoiling.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A person or thing worth keeping.

   **Usage:** informal

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A person charged with guarding or caring for, storing, or maintaining something; a custodian, a guard; sometimes a gamekeeper.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The player charged with guarding a goal or wicket; a goalkeeper or wicketkeeper.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. At Eton College, a student who is the captain of a sport or an activity such as drama.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A part of a mechanism that catches or retains another part, for example the part of a door lock that fits in the frame and receives the bolt.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A thin, flexible tress or tongue of material (e.g. leather) at the end of a crop opposite the handle, which is broad enough to prevent the horse's skin from being marked as it might be by a whip.

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. An offensive play in which the quarterback runs toward the goal with the ball after it is snapped.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** American-football; ball-games; football; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. Synonym of armature (“piece of metal connecting the poles of a magnet to preserve its strength by forming a circuit”).

   **Source synonyms:** armature (synonym; synonym-of)
