## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A cooked dish made by standing a prepared chicken upright on a partially filled can of beer and cooking it slowly in a barbecue or oven, so that the beer evaporates and permeates the meat.

   **Usage:** US; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** cooking; food; lifestyle
