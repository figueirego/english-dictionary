### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A kind of electronic noise that occurs in semiconductors and ultra-thin gate oxide films, consisting of sudden, unpredictable step-like transitions between two or more discrete voltage or current levels.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source synonyms:** bistable noise; impulse noise; popcorn noise; random telegraph noise
