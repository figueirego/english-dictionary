### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A feature of some cameras and other optical systems that allows a correct focus on the subject to be obtained automatically

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** This camera has autofocus, so it's more expensive.
