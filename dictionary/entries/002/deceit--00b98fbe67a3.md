### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An act or practice intended to deceive; a trick.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The whole conversation was merely a deceit.

   **Source synonyms:** trick; fraud

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An act of deceiving someone.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** deception; trickery; deception; imposition (formal); hype; deceit; dupery; fiddle; fetch; hocus-pocus; jugglery; legerdemain; list; mislead (noun); prestidigitation; Punic faith (archaic); rinky-dink (archaic); ruse; sleight of hand (idiomatic); subterfuge; swack (UK; dated; slang); trickery

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The state of being deceitful or deceptive.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** deceptiveness; deceitfulness; dissimulation; fraudulence; trickery; underhandedness

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The tort or fraudulent representation of a material fact made with knowledge of its falsity, or recklessly, or without reasonable grounds for believing its truth and with intent to induce reliance on it; the plaintiff justifiably relies on the deception, to his injury.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Usage:** collective; countable; rare; uncountable
