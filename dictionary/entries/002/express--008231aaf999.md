### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Moving or operating quickly, as a train not making local stops.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source synonyms:** fast; crack

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Specific or precise; directly and distinctly stated; not merely implied.

   **Usage:** comparable

   **Example:** I gave him express instructions not to begin until I arrived, but he ignored me.

   **Example:** This book cannot be copied without the express permission of the publisher.

   **Source synonyms:** explicit; plain; clear; direct; explicit; express; monosemic; monosemous; patent; plain; specific; unambiguous; unequivocal; univocal

   **Source antonyms:** implied

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Truly depicted; exactly resembling.

   **Example:** In my eyes it bore a livelier image of the spirit, it seemed more express and single, than the imperfect and divided countenance.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Providing a more limited but presumably faster service than a full or complete dealer of the same kind or type.

   **Usage:** postpositional

   **Example:** Tesco Express

   **Example:** McDonald's Express

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; commerce; retail
