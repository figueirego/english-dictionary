### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In a condition of poverty or debility, especially as a result of experiencing a financial or personal setback.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; not-comparable

   **Example:** People who are down and out need some place to turn.
