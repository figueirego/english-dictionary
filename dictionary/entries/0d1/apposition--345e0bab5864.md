**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** grammar; human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The relationship between such nouns or noun phrases.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** grammar; human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The quality of being side by side, apposed instead of opposed, next to each other.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
