### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To move or swing from side to side; or backward and forward; to rock.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** sway to the music

   **Example:** The trees swayed in the breeze.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To move or wield with the hand; to swing; to wield.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to sway the sceptre

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To influence or direct by power, authority, persuasion, or by moral force; to rule; to govern; to guide. Compare persuade.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Do you think you can sway their decision?

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To cause to incline or swing to one side, or backward and forward; to bias; to turn; to bend; to warp.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** reeds swayed by the wind

   **Example:** judgment swayed by passion

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To hoist (a mast or yard) into position.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to sway up the yards

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To be drawn to one side by weight or influence; to lean; to incline.

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

**Source sense R2.S7**

7. To have weight or influence.

<a id="r2-s8"></a>

**Source sense R2.S8**

8. To bear sway; to rule; to govern.
