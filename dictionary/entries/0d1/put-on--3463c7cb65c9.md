### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To don (clothing, equipment, or the like).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Why don't you put on your jacket. It's cold.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To decorate or dress (something) onto another person or a surface.

   **Usage:** ditransitive; transitive

   **Example:** I put a coat on my daughter.

   **Example:** I put tinsel on the Christmas tree.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To fool, kid, deceive.

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** You must be putting me on.

   **Example:** She's putting on that she's sicker than she really is.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To assume, adopt or affect; to behave in a particular way as a pretense.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Why are you putting that silly voice on?

   **Example:** He's just putting on that limp -- his leg's actually fine.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To play (a recording).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I'll put your favorite record on.

   **Example:** Can you put on The Sound of Music? I'd like to see it again.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To play (a recording) on (a sound system).

   **Usage:** ditransitive; transitive

   **Example:** I'll put blues on the stereo.

   **Example:** We usually put the Beatles on on my boombox.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To initiate cooking or warming, especially on a stovetop.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I'll put on some coffee for everybody.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To perform for an audience.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The actors will put Macbeth on only one more time.

**Source sense R1.S9**

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The theatre company is putting on Into the Woods this season.

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. To provide.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

   **Usage:** ditransitive; transitive
