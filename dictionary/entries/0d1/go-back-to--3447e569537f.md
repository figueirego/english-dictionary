### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To stem from; to originate in.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The awqaf system in India goes back to the early days of the Islamic presence in the subcontinent.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To know each other since long from.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Bill and I go back to college.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of two or more persons

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see go back, to.

   **Example:** It was time to go back to the hotel.

   **Example:** A package that cannot be delivered will go back to the sender.
