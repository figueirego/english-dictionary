**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A compound tense used in literary French, Spanish, Portuguese and Italian, in function a pluperfect, and in form composed of the conjunction of the simple past tense of an auxiliary verb (avoir, être) with the past participle. Example&#58; Après que la femme et l'homme l'eurent remercié = After the woman and the man had thanked him
