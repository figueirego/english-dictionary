### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of a wide range of symptoms associated with the discontinuance of a drug by an addicted individual, or the medically controlled discontinuance by a patient of a prescribed drug.

   **Usage:** especially; in-plural

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** drug withdrawal

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of a wide range of symptoms associated with the discontinuance of certain foods, beverages, or other nondrug substances.
