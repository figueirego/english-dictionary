### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The physical space closely surrounding a person, which can lead to discomfort, anger or anxiety if it is encroached.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; psychology; sciences; social-science; sociology
