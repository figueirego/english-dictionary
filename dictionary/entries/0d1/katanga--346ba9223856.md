## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A region in the southeastern Democratic Republic of the Congo, originally conquered by the Congo Free State (owned by the Belgian king, not his European state) but administered as a corporate mining concession.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Since 1910, an autonomous part of the Belgian colony Congo.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Since 1933, a regular province, until 1947 however named after its capital&#58; Elisabethstad in Dutch or Elisabethville in French.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. In 1960 a unilaterally declared independent state; in 1961-63 reconquered by Congo and reorganized in three provinces until 1966; renamed Shaba 1972-1997 as part of president Mobutu's Zaireanization.
