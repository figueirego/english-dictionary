### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The rights and privileges enjoyed by the freemen of medieval cities (their "citizens"), but not by outsiders, bondsmen, or others.

   **Usage:** historical; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An honor bestowed by certain cities, towns, and (UK) local councils, including those of rural counties.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** No, your freedom of the city doesn't allow you to ignore traffic fines or drive sheep through rush-hour traffic.
