## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Not attended; without persons present.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The meeting went largely unattended, since it took place on Christmas Eve.

   **Example:** System administrators may schedule an unattended installation of the software.

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Not attended to; not receiving attention.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The fire was caused by an unattended electric blanket.
