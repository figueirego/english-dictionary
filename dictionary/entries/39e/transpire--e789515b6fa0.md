### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To give off (vapour, waste matter etc.); to exhale (an odour etc.).

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To perspire.

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete

   **Source synonyms:** sweat

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of plants, to give off water and waste products through the stomata.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To become known; to escape from secrecy.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** It eventually transpired that the murder victim had been a notorious blackmailer.

   **Source synonyms:** come to light; emerge; develop

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To happen, take place.

   **Usage:** broadly; intransitive

   **Source synonyms:** come about; come to pass; occur; bechance; befall; behap; belimp (obsolete); betide (dated; literary); betime (archaic); beworth; chance (archaic); come about; come to pass; crop up; evene (obsolete); eventuate; go on; hap (literary); happen; hold; occur; pass; take place; tide (obsolete); transpire
