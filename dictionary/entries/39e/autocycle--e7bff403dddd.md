### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A vehicle somewhat like a moped, having a small motor where the pedals would be, and a petrol tank located within the frame.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A three-wheeled vehicle controlled with a steering wheel and foot pedals for acceleration and braking, where passengers ride in seats instead of astride the body.
