## Adjective · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of or pertaining to a specific period of time in the past, not extending to the present.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** He was the one-time president of the club.

   **Source synonyms:** former; past; sometime; antecedent; anterior; earlier; erstwhile; ex; foregone; former; hitherto (archaic); old; olden; past; preceding; precedent; previous; prior; quondam; sometime; used-to-be; whilom

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Occurring or used in a single instance and then never again.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** one-time charge/payment

   **Example:** He was an eloquent speaker, and his slip of the tongue was a one-time error.

   **Source synonyms:** one-off
