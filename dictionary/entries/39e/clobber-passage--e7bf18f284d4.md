### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of a number of passages in the Bible that may be interpreted as clear condemnations of homosexuality&#58; usually Genesis 19&#58;1-38; Leviticus 18&#58;22 and 20&#58;13; 1 Corinthians 6&#58;9-11; 1 Timothy 1&#58;9-10; Jude 6-7; and Romans 1&#58;25-27.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Source synonyms:** clobber text; clobber verse

<a id="r1-s2"></a>
