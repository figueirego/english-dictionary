## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An administrative county of Suffolk, England, created in 1889, along with East Suffolk. Both counties were abolished in 1974, being merged as Suffolk once again.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A local government district in Suffolk formed on 1 April 2019 on the merger of Forest Heath and St Edmundsbury districts. Its headquarters are in Bury St Edmunds.
