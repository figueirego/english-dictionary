### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of or pertaining to veins.

   **Usage:** relational

   **Example:** Her venous circulation was poor, leading to varicose veins.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Having passed through the capillaries and given up oxygen for the tissues and become charged with carbon dioxide.

   **Usage:** relational

   **Source context (verbatim):** of blood

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Possessing veins.

   **Example:** It was a sample of venous tissue.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Having numerous veins.

   **Example:** The tree has highly serrated and venous leaves.
