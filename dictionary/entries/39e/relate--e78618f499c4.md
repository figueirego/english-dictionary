### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To tell in a descriptive way.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The captain related an old yarn.

   **Example:** Please relate the circumstances of your journey here today.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To be relevant specifically to.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** These regulations relate only to exported goods.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To bring into a relation, association, or connection (between one thing and another).

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To have a connection.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The patterns on the screen relate to the pitch and volume of the music being played.

   **Source synonyms:** ally; refer; pertain

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To interact.

   **Usage:** intransitive; jargon

   **Example:** Children who find it hard to relate end up being inadequately socialized.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To respond through reaction.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To identify with; to understand.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** I find it difficult to relate to others because I'm extremely introverted.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To bring back; to restore.

   **Usage:** obsolete
