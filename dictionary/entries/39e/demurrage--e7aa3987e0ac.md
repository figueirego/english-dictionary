### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. the detention of a ship or other freight vehicle, during delayed loading or unloading

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** economics; sciences; shipping; transport

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. a charge made for exchanging currency for bullion

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
