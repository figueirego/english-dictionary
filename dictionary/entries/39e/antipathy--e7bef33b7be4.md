### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Often followed by against, between, for, or to&#58; a (deep) feeling of dislike or repugnance, normally towards a person and less often towards a thing, often without any conscious reasoning; aversion, distaste, hostility; (countable) an instance of this.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** contrariety; disgust; enmity; hatred; ill feeling; ill will; opposition

   **Source antonyms:** sympathy

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Natural contrariety or incompatibility between things, as a result of which they negatively affect or oppose each other; (countable) an instance of this.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Oil and water have antipathy.

   **Source antonyms:** sympathy

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A person or thing that one has a (deep) feeling of dislike or repugnance towards; an anathema, a bête noire, a bugbear.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A person or thing that has a (deep) feeling of dislike or repugnance towards another person or thing; a hater.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The quality of being antipathetic&#58; not easily united by grafting.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences
