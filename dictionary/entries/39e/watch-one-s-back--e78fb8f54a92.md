### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To remain wary of being attacked from behind by surprise; to remain watchful to prevent a predictable attack from being a surprise.

   **Usage:** literally

   **Example:** the kind of dive bar where you better watch your back

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To be wary of betrayal or an otherwise unexpected attack.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** the kind of office gossip that prompts you to watch your back
