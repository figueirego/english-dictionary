### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To score six points against (a bowler) by hitting the ball over the boundary.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; cricket; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To affect in a devastating way by some unexpected news.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; informal

   **Example:** When I heard about the accident, it hit me for six.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To hit another person very hard.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; informal

   **Example:** When he swore at me again, I couldn't hold back. So, I hit him for six.
