### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To perceive sounds through the ear.

   **Usage:** intransitive; stative

   **Example:** I was deaf, but now I can hear.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To perceive (a sound, or something producing a sound) with the ear, to recognize (something) in an auditory way.

   **Usage:** stative; transitive

   **Example:** I could hear them quarreling upstairs, but didn’t really listen to their argument.

   **Source synonyms:** [catch](../27e/catch--9f925cdc4fee.md)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To exercise this faculty intentionally; to listen to.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To listen favourably to; to grant (a request etc.).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Eventually the king chose to hear her entreaties.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To receive information about; to come to learn of.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To be contacted by.

   **Example:** I haven't heard from you in a while; how have you been?

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To listen to (a person, case) in a court of law; to try.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Your case will be heard at the end of the month.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

   **Source synonyms:** adjudge; rule on; try; adjudge; adjudicate; arbitrate; deem (obsolete); [doom](../244/doom--910ecd3e43c7.md) (obsolete); hear; judge; rule on; settle; try

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To sympathize with; to understand the feelings or opinion of.

   **Usage:** informal; transitive

   **Example:** You're tired of all the ads on TV? I hear ya.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To study under.

   **Usage:** Greek; transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; philosophy; sciences
