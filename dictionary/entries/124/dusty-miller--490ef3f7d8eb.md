### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A formulaic phrase for a miller, related to the dust generated in the milling process.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of several species of plants with leaves of a dusty appearance&#58; Centaurea cineraria, Jacobaea maritima, Artemisia stelleriana, Spyridium parvifolium, and Silene coronaria, syn. Lychnis coronaria.
