### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To cause or force a price, quantity, rate, etc. to decrease.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; informal

   **Example:** The wind chill drives down the temperature.

   **Source antonyms:** drive up

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To drive to a location that is south of one's current location.
