### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A postsecondary school that prepares its students for military service as an officer.

   **Source synonyms:** service academy

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A secondary school or postsecondary school that incorporates military-like discipline into its educational program, typically leading many of its students to join the military.
