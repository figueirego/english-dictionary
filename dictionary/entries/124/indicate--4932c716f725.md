### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To point out; to discover; to direct to a knowledge of; to show; to make known.

   **Usage:** sometimes; transitive

   **Example:** The guard blew his whistle to indicate imminent departure.

   **Source context (verbatim):** with 'of'

   **Source synonyms:** betoken; index; speak of; bespeak

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To show or manifest by symptoms.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Marked weakness unresponsive to dietary change and exercise may indicate an underlying neurologic or metabolic problem.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** suggest

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To point to as the proper remedies.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** This type of arrhythmia indicates the use of a beta-blocker.

   **Example:** Amiodarone is not indicated in this type of arrhythmia.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** warrant

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To signal in a vehicle the desire to turn right or left.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To investigate the condition or power of, as of steam engine, by means of an indicator.

   **Usage:** transitive
