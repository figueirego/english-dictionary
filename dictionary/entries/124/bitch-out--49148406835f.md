### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To not do something out of fear; chicken out.

   **Usage:** intransitive; slang; vulgar

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To back out of an agreement for any reason.

   **Usage:** intransitive; slang; vulgar

   **Example:** My dad promised he would take me to a restaurant for dinner today, but much later, he was unexpectedly bitching out.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To complain.

   **Usage:** intransitive; slang; transitive; vulgar

   **Example:** My mom bitches out every time she sees bad grades on my school report card.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To yell at; to reprimand; to chew out.

   **Usage:** slang; transitive; vulgar

   **Example:** My dad bitched me out when he found out what I did to his car.
