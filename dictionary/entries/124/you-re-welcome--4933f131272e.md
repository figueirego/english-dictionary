### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used to acknowledge thanks; ritual reply to “thank you”.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used (often preemptively) to imply that the speaker ought to be thanked.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see you're, welcome.
