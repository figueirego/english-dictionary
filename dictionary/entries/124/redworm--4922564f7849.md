### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A type of small, reddish earthworm, Lumbricus rubellus, used as bait in angling; also, in later use, the brandling, Eisenia foetida.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A parasitic worm of the family Strongylidae, which causes anaemia in many vertebrates.
