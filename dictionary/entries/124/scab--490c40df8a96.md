## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

### Forms

- scabs — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An incrustation over a sore, wound, vesicle, or pustule, formed during healing.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The scabies.

   **Usage:** colloquial; countable; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The mange, especially when it appears on sheep.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any of several different diseases of potatoes producing pits and other damage on their surface, caused by streptomyces bacteria (but formerly believed to be caused by a fungus).

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Common scab, a relatively harmless variety of scab (potato disease) caused by Streptomyces scabies.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Any one of various more or less destructive fungal diseases that attack cultivated plants, forming dark-colored crustlike spots.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; medicine; natural-sciences; pathology; phytopathology; sciences

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A slight irregular protuberance which defaces the surface of a casting, caused by the breaking away of a part of the mold.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; founding

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A mean, dirty, paltry fellow.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** antimodel; bad guy; baddie; bastard; beggar; blackguard; bounder; cad; cockatrice (archaic); coistril (obsolete); evildoer; fustilarian (obsolete); harlot (obsolete); heavy (informal); knave; lidderon (archaic; dialectal; rare); makeshift (obsolete); miscreant; ne'er-do-well; niding (archaic); nithing (archaic); rapscallion; rascal; rogue; scab; scamp; scapegrace; scoundrel; scum; scumbag; thug; undesirable; varlet; villain

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A worker who acts against trade union policies; any picket crosser (strikebreaker), and especially one with devotion to union busting.

   **Usage:** countable; derogatory; slang; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** blackleg; blacklegger; knobstick; scalie
