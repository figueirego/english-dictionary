### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A plate protecting a fighting person's back.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A plate harnessed to a diver's back, to which the gas cylinders are attached.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** diving; hobbies; lifestyle; sports; underwater-diving

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** automotive; transport; vehicles
