### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To stray, rove, or wander from a normal course and others of its kind.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** He straggled away from the crowd and went off on his own.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To act in a disorderly and irregular way.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Grow or hang messily.

   **Example:** The roses had been allowed to straggle across the path.

   **Example:** A few gray wisps straggled from underneath his cap.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To move along slowly so as to remain some distance behind the person or people in front.
