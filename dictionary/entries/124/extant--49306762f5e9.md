### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Still in existence; not having disappeared.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** extant manuscripts of the Old Testament

   **Source synonyms:** existent; existing; essential; existent; existing; extant

   **Source antonyms:** inextant

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Still alive; not extinct.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** birds are the only extant dinosaurs

   **Source synonyms:** alive and kicking; living; vital; above ground (figuratively); alive; alive and kicking (idiomatic); alive and well; animate; animated; extant; kicking (slang); live; lively (archaic); livesome; living; quick (archaic); still with us; surviving; undeceased; vital; vitalic
