### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A non-SI unit of energy, equivalent to 4.184 kilojoules, that can raise the temperature of a kilogram of water by one degree Celsius. This is the unit normally used in food labeling.

   **Usage:** nutrition

   **Source synonyms:** large calorie; kilocalorie; Calorie; kilogram calorie

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A non-SI unit of energy, equivalent to 4.184 joules, that can raise the temperature of a gram of water by one degree Celsius. This unit was formerly widely used in chemistry and physics.

   **Usage:** physical

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

   **Source synonyms:** small calorie; gram calorie; thermochemical calorie
