### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of or relating to long chain organic compounds that have multiple double bonds; polyunsaturated fatty acids are essential to human nutrition.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; organic-chemistry; physical-sciences

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** not-comparable; nutrition
