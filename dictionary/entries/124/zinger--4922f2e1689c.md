### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A very rapidly moving object, especially one that is thrown.

   **Usage:** Canada; US; informal

   **Example:** The pitcher threw a real zinger and struck him out.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A surprising or unusually pointed, humorous and impressive insult or insulting quip.

   **Usage:** Canada; US; informal

   **Source synonyms:** one-liner

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An event that when experienced leaves the witness dazed, either physically or metaphorically.

   **Usage:** Canada; US; informal

   **Example:** I was still reeling from the zinger of seeing my ex on a date with my best friend.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An outstanding, energetic and surprising thing or person.

   **Usage:** Canada; US; informal

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A piece of food or drink that is tart and stimulating.

   **Usage:** Canada; US
