**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Graphism of symbols such as letters that express some meaning.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

2. Something written, such as a document, article or book.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

3. The process of representing a language with symbols or letters.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A work of an author.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** Congrats, you did an interesting piece of writing.

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The style of writing of a person.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** I can't read your writing.
