## Noun

### Forms

- open secrets — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Information that is widely known, but not acknowledged openly.

   **Example:** At that time, the corruption in the mayor's office was an open secret.

   **Source synonyms:** secret of Polichinelle

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Information that is not widely known, despite being freely available.

   **Usage:** dated

   **Example:** The wonderful dining options in this city are hidden gems — an open secret.
