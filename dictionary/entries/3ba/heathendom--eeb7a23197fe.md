### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The state of being heathen.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** The priest argued to the king that unless clerics accompanied the colony ship, the colony would soon descend into heathendom and barbarity.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. From one religion's or creed's perspective, the rest of the world that does not follow that creed or religion.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** My aunt considered all of Europe to be heathendom, and refused to visit us in Amsterdam on religious grounds.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Specifically, the non-Christian world; territories where Christianity is not the dominant religion.

   **Usage:** dated; uncountable; usually

   **Example:** The Crusaders meant to wrest Jerusalem from heathendom, but they managed to pillage a number of lands in Christendom along the way.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The collection of people who follow Heathenry, a modern pagan faith inspired by the pre-Abrahamic religions of Germanic tribes, Anglo-Saxons and Norse peoples.

   **Usage:** Germanic; capitalized; sometimes; uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** lifestyle; paganism; religion
