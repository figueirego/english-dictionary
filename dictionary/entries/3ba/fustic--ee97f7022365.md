### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A tropical American tree (Maclura tinctoria), whose wood produces a yellow dye.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A Eurasian smoketree (Cotinus coggygria), whose wood produces an orange dye.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually
