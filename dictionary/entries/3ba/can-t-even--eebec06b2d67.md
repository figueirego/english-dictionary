### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see can't, even.

   **Usage:** no-past-participle; no-present-participle

   **Example:** He’s nine years old, and can’t even tie his own shoelaces!

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Without a following verb, as an expression of aporia&#58; to be unable to express one's feelings about an overwhelming situation.

   **Usage:** no-past-participle; no-present-participle; slang

   **Example:** Oh my God, I got cheap tickets to the Beyoncé concert! I can’t even!
