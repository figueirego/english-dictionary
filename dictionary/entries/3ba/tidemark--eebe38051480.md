### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. a line (of seaweed or differently coloured sand etc) on the shore showing the level of high or low tide

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. any mark showing the limit of some past activity

   **Usage:** broadly

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
