### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The form of a Boolean formula that the formula has if the formula is a conjunction of disjunctions of literals, such as “(A or B or C) and (D or E or not F)”.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; logic; mathematics; philosophy; sciences
