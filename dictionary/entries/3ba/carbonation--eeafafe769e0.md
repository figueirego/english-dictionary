## Noun

### Forms

- carbonations — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The state of having carbon dioxide gas dissolved in a liquid; the act or process of creating that state.

   **Usage:** countable; physical; uncountable

   **Example:** Soda pop is basically sugar water with a little bit of flavoring and added carbonation.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

   **Source antonyms:** decarbonation

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The amount or level of dissolved carbon dioxide remaining in solution.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Celia didn't eat at the diner anymore because she thought the carbonation in their fountain drinks was off.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The replacement of calcium hydroxide with calcium carbonate triggered by a chemical reaction.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; geography; geology; mineralogy; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

   **Source synonyms:** carbonatation

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Synonym of carbonization.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** carbonization (synonym; synonym-of)

   **Source antonyms:** decarbonation; decarbonization
