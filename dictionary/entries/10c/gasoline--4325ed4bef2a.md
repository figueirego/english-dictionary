### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A flammable liquid consisting of a mixture of refined petroleum hydrocarbons, mainly used as a motor fuel; petrol.

   **Usage:** Canada; Philippines; US; uncountable; usually

   **Example:** You can't put gasoline in this car. This car takes diesel fuel.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any specific kind of this fuel.

   **Usage:** countable; usually

   **Example:** He was trying to invent a cleaner-burning gasoline.

   **Example:** The refinery produces a wide range of gasolines.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

   **Usage:** slang; uncountable; usually

   **Source synonyms:** gas
