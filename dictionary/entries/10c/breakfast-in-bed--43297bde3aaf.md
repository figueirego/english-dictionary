## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Breakfast served to one in bed upon waking, commonly to show appreciation for the receiver.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The children made their mother breakfast in bed on her birthday.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see breakfast, in, bed.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** I ate breakfast in bed this morning.
