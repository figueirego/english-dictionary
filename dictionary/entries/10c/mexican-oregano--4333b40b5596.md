### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A Mexican shrub, Lippia graveolens, not closely related to true oregano, but with leaves that smell and taste like like it.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A Mexican shrub, Poliomintha longiflora, with leaves that smell and taste like oregano

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A Mexican and southwestern-US herb related to bee balm, Mt. Pima oregano, Monarda austromontana, which smells and tastes like oregano

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually
