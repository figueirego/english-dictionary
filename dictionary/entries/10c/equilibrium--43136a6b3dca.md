### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The condition of a system in which competing influences are balanced, resulting in no net change.

   **Source synonyms:** balance; stability

   **Source antonyms:** disequilibrium; imbalance; instability

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Mental balance.

   **Source synonyms:** sanity

   **Source antonyms:** insanity; instability; madness

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The state of a reaction in which the rates of the forward and reverse reactions are the same.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The state of a body at rest or in uniform motion in which the resultant of all forces on it is zero.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

   **Source antonyms:** disequilibrium; nonequilibrium
