## Adjective · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ɪkˈsklu.sɪv/ — source labels: General-American; Received-Pronunciation
- IPA: /ɪkˈsklu.zɪv/ — source labels: General-American; Received-Pronunciation

### Forms

- more exclusive — comparative
- most exclusive — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Excluding items or members that do not meet certain conditions.

   **Usage:** literally

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Referring to a membership organisation, service or product&#58; of high quality and/or renown, for superior members only.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Source synonyms:** sole

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A snobbish usage, suggesting that members who do not meet requirements, which may be financial, of social status, religion, skin colour etc., are excluded.

   **Example:** Exclusive clubs tend to serve exclusive brands of food and drinks, in the same exorbitant price range, such as the 'finest' French châteaux.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Exclusionary.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Whole, undivided, entire.

   **Example:** The teacher's pet commands the teacher's exclusive attention.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Of or relating to the first-person plural pronoun when excluding the person being addressed.

   **Example:** The pronoun in "We're going to a party later, but you aren't invited" is an exclusive "we".

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Having a romantic or sexual relationship with one another, to the exclusion of others.

   **Example:** They decided to no longer be exclusive.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of two people in a romantic or sexual relationship

### Related words

**Source antonyms:** inclusive; non-exclusive

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ɪkˈsklu.zɪv/ — source labels: General-American; Received-Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Information (or an artefact) that is granted or obtained exclusively.

   **Example:** The editor agreed to keep a lid on a potentially disastrous political scoop in exchange for an exclusive of a happier nature.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. A member of a group who excludes others from their society.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. A word or phrase that restricts something, such as only, solely, or simply.
