**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To transport toward somebody/somewhere.

   **Example:** Waiter, please bring me a single malt whiskey.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To supply or contribute.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** The new company director brought a fresh perspective on sales and marketing.

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To occasion or bring about.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Let's bring our differences to an issue.

   **Example:** The controversial TV broadcast brought a storm of complaints.

   **Example:** Seeing her brought the memories flooding back and tears to my eyes.

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To raise (a lawsuit, charges, etc.) against somebody.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To persuade; to induce; to draw; to lead; to guide.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>
