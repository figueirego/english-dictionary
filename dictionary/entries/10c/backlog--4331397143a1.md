### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A large log to burn at the back of a fire.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A reserve source or supply.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An accumulation or buildup, especially of unfilled orders, unconsumed products or unfinished work.

   **Example:** He went to work on Saturday to try to work through the backlog of papers on his desk.

   **Example:** Kyle purchased a new one despite having a backlog of video games that he hasn't played.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A log containing text previously read, as in text-based video games or chat rooms.
