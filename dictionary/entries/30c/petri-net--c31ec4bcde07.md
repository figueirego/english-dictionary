### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One of several mathematical representations of discrete distributed systems, a 5-tuple (S,T,F,M\_0,W)!, where
   1. S is a set of places.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One of several mathematical representations of discrete distributed systems, a 5-tuple (S,T,F,M\_0,W)!, where
   1. T is a set of transitions.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. One of several mathematical representations of discrete distributed systems, a 5-tuple (S,T,F,M\_0,W)!, where
   1. S and T are disjoint, i.e. no object can be both a place and a transition

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. One of several mathematical representations of discrete distributed systems, a 5-tuple (S,T,F,M\_0,W)!, where
   1. F is a set of arcs known as a flow relation. The set F is subject to the constraint that no arc may connect two places or two transitions, or more formally&#58; F⊆(S×T)∪(T×S).

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. One of several mathematical representations of discrete distributed systems, a 5-tuple (S,T,F,M\_0,W)!, where
   1. M\_0&#58;S→ℕ is an initial marking, where for each place s∈S, there are n\_s∈ℕ tokens.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. One of several mathematical representations of discrete distributed systems, a 5-tuple (S,T,F,M\_0,W)!, where
   1. W&#58;F→ℕ⁺ is a set of arc weights, which assigns to each arc f∈F some n∈ℕ⁺ denoting how many tokens are consumed from a place by a transition, or alternatively, how many tokens are produced by a transition and put into each place.
