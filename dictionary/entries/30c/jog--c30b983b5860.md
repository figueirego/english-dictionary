### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To push slightly; to move or shake with a push or jerk, as to gain the attention of; to jolt.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** jog one's elbow

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To shake, stir or rouse.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I tried desperately to jog my memory.

   **Source synonyms:** refresh

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To walk or ride forward with a jolting pace; to move at a heavy pace, trudge; to move on or along.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To move at a pace between walking and running, to run at a leisurely pace.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** I saw her jogging in the forest yesterday.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** exercise; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To cause to move at an energetic trot.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to jog a horse

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**
