### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A portable telephone (for example, one installed in a car) that connects with the telephone network, typically by means of radio transmissions.

   **Usage:** dated

   **Source synonyms:** mobile telephone; portable phone; portable telephone; radio telephone; radio-telephone; radiotelephone

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A portable (handheld) telephone that connects with the cellular telephone network.

   **Source synonyms:** cell; cell phone; cellular; cellular mobile; cellular phone; cellular telephone; cellphone//handphone; mobile; mobile telephone
