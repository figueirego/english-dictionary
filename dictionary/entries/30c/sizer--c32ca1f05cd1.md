## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An instrument or contrivance to size articles, or to determine their size by a standard, or to separate and distribute them according to size.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An instrument or tool for bringing anything to an exact size.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An object used at the airport to test to see if a carry on or personal item is the correct size allowed by the airline.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Alternative form of sizar (“undergraduate who received an allowance”).
