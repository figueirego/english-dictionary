## Noun

### Forms

- saddle reefs — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A mineral deposit associated with the crest of an anticlinal fold and following the bedding planes, usually found in vertical succession, especially the gold-bearing quartz veins of Australia.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; geography; geology; mineralogy; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An opening at the crest of a sharp fold in sedimentary rocks, occupied by ore.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; geography; geology; mineralogy; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A saddle-shaped bedded mineral (usually gold-bearing quartz) vein occurring along the crest of an anticline or (less common) a syncline (an inverted saddle).

   **Usage:** Australia

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; geography; geology; mineralogy; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

   **Source synonyms:** saddle
