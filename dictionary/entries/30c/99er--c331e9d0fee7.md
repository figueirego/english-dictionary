## Noun

### Forms

- 99ers — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A long-term unemployed person who has exhausted his or her entitlement to welfare benefits.

   **Usage:** US; plural

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Someone in the bottom 99% of the population by wealth.

   **Usage:** US; plural

   **Source antonyms:** one-percenter

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Someone associated in any other way with the number 99 or a year ending in 99.

   **Example:** The class of 1999 are referred to as the 99ers.
