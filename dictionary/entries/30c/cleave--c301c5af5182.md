**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To split or sever something with, or as if with, a sharp instrument.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The wings clove the foggy air.

   **Source synonyms:** rive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To break a single crystal (such as a gemstone or semiconductor wafer) along one of its more symmetrical crystallographic planes (often by impact), forming facets on the resulting pieces.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; geography; geology; mineralogy; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To make or accomplish by or as if by cutting.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The truck clove a path through the ice.

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. (chemistry) To split (a complex molecule) into simpler molecules.

   **Usage:** transitive
