### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of the small carnivorous catlike mammals encompassing certain species from the families Viverridae, Eupleridae, and Nandiniidae, native to tropical Africa and Asia.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** civet cat

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The musky perfume produced by the animal; civetone.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A spotted skunk.

   **Usage:** US; countable; regional; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**
