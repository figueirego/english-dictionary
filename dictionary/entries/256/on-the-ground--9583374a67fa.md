## Prep\_phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In an area (location) of interest or active operations, versus outside that area (and especially, far away from it).

   **Example:** You might feel differently about this policy if you were on the ground in a war zone.

   **Source synonyms:** in the field; on the spot; on location; in situ

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In the daily life of the general public, or at least in that of some group of people who aren't out-of-touch elites.

   **Example:** Evidently, senior management has no idea how things get done by those of us on the ground.

   **Source synonyms:** on the street; in the streets; in reality; real world

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see on, the, ground.

   **Example:** I'm still surprised that I found a hundred-dollar bill just lying on the ground.
