### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Slender and long of limb; lanky.

   **Example:** For an accountant, Robert had a rangy appearance.

   **Example:** Her African violets weren't getting enough sun and became rangy.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Prone to roaming around.

   **Example:** rangy cattle

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Having or permitting range or scope; roomy; commodious.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Requiring a large vocal range; having a great difference from the highest to the lowest notes.

   **Example:** The Star Spangled Banner has been criticized as too rangy for many people to sing along to comfortably.
