### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A peninsula in Turkey, north of the Dardanelles, between the Aegean and Marmara seas.
   1. The Gallipoli Campaign (1915–1916) in World War I.

      **Usage:** historical; by ellipsis

      **Source synonyms:** Chersonese; Chersonesus Thracica; Thracian Chersonese

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A peninsula in Turkey, north of the Dardanelles, between the Aegean and Marmara seas.
   1. Synonym of Gelibolu&#58; a town and municipality of Turkey, located on the Gallipoli peninsula (sense 1).

      **Usage:** dated

      **Source synonyms:** Chersonese; Chersonesus Thracica; Thracian Chersonese; Gelibolu (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A town in Lecce, Apulia, Italy.

   **Usage:** also; attributive

   **Example:** Gallipoli oil Gallipoli soap
