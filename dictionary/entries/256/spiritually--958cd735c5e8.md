## Adverb

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈspɪɹɪt͡ʃʉwəlɪj/ — source labels: British; Southern; Standard

### Forms

- more spiritually — comparative
- most spiritually — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In a manner affecting, relating or pertaining to the spirit or the soul.

   **Example:** physically and spiritually abused

   **Example:** spiritually sanctified

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Identical (or similar) in vibes to; best understood through the connotations of.

   **Usage:** Internet; transitive

   **Example:** Oxford is spiritually London.
