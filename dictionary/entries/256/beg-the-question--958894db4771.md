### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To engage in the logical fallacy of begging the question (petitio principii).

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; law; logic; mathematics; philosophy; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To sidestep or fail to address a question.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To raise or prompt a question.

   **Usage:** proscribed; sometimes

   **Example:** Three people were hurt in the fire at the warehouse last night, which begs the question&#58; what were they doing there in the first place?
