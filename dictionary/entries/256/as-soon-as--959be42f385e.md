### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see as, soon.

   **Example:** I came as soon as I could.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. At the moment when; immediately (after); instantly; directly.

   **Example:** As soon as he arrived, everyone gasped.

   **Source synonyms:** no sooner than; immediately; once

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used to indicate that the first action mentioned is as likely as, or is preferred to, the second action mentioned.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I'd jump out the window as soon as not.
