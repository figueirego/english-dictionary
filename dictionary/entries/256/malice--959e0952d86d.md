### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Intention to harm or deprive in an illegal or immoral way. Desire to take pleasure in another's misfortune.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** Your voice positively drips malice.

   **Source synonyms:** evil; ill will; wickedness
