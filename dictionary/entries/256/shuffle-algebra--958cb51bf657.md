### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A Hopf algebra with a basis corresponding to words on some set, whose product is given by the shuffle product X ⧢ Y of two words X, Y&#58; the sum of all ways of interlacing them. The interlacing is given by the riffle shuffle permutation.
