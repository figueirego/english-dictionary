### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of congregating or collecting together.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A gathering of faithful in a temple, church, synagogue, mosque or other place of worship, especially those present at a devotional service or who regularly attend such services, particularly in contrast to the leadership, choir or others.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** flock; fold; congregation; flock; fold; laity

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A Roman Congregation, a main department of the Vatican administration of the Catholic Church.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A corporate body whose members gather for worship, or the members of such a body.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Any large gathering of people.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A flock of birds.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** flock

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A group of alligators or crocodiles.

   **Usage:** collective; countable; noun; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** raft

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. The main body of university staff, comprising academics, administrative staff, heads of colleges, etc.

   **Usage:** UK; countable; uncountable; Oxford University
