## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A form of the game of musical statues in which the dancing players must stay on a sheet of newspaper, or (in another version) dance around the sheet and step onto it as the music stops. The sheet is reduced in size after each round.

   **Usage:** uncountable
