### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To retell (something) real (e.g., an event or series of events) as if it were fiction; especially, to do so in a way that departs from reality in any extent from mild to extensive (from minor details to essential substance).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** a lightly fictionalized account of \[X\]

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To retell (something) real (e.g., an event or series of events) as if it were fiction; especially, to do so in a way that departs from reality in any extent from mild to extensive (from minor details to essential substance).
