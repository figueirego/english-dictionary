### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An article of clothing that covers the part of the body between the waist and the ankles or knees, and is divided into a separate part for each leg.

   **Usage:** plural; plural-only

   **Example:** The trousers need to be shortened.

   **Source synonyms:** breeches; britches; kecks (Britain; dialectal); pants (US); strides (Australia)
