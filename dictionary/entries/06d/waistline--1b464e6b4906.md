## Noun

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈweɪstˌlaɪn/ — source labels: General-American

### Forms

- waistlines — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. a line around the body at the waist; its measurement

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. the narrowest part of a garment, usually at the waist, but may be above or below depending on the dictates of fashion or the whim of the designer

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. person having a large waistline

   **Usage:** slang

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The horizontal line just below the windows of a car or other vehicle.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** automotive; transport; vehicles
