## Adjective

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Including (almost) everything within its scope.

   **Example:** an inclusive list of data formats

   **Source synonyms:** exhaustive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Including the extremes as well as the area between.

   **Example:** numbers 1 to 10 inclusive

   **Source antonyms:** exclusive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of, or relating to the first-person plural pronoun when including the person being addressed.

   **Example:** The pronoun in "If you want, we could go back to my place for coffee" is an inclusive "we".

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

   **Source antonyms:** exclusive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Including or accepting those belonging to a particular group.

   **Example:** trans-inclusive feminism

   **Source synonyms:** inclusionary

   **Source antonyms:** exclusionary
