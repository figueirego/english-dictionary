### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A point on an engineering drawing that has been added to make the layout of the part easier. It is usually the intersection of the tangent lines of a curve.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A location that is worth visiting because it is a tourist attraction or the base for some type of service such as a restaurant or shop.
