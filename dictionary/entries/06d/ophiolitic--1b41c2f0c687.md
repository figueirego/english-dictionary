### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of or pertaining to sections of the oceanic crust and the subjacent upper mantle that have been uplifted or emplaced to be exposed within continental crustal rocks

   **Example:** The shore of Newfoundland displays many ophiolitic deposits.
