## Verb · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /θʌmp/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Forms

- thumps — present; singular; third-person
- thumping — participle; present
- thumped — participle; past
- thumped — past

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To hit (someone or something) as if to make a thump.

   **Usage:** UK; transitive

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To cause to make a thumping sound.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The cat thumped its tail in irritation.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To thud or pound.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To throb with a muffled rhythmic sound.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Dance music thumped from the nightclub entrance.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. (of a rabbit) to hit the ground with the back legs to signal agitation.

   **Usage:** intransitive
