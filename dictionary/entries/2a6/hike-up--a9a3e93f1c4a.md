### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To lift; to tug or pull upwards.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** He hiked up his sagging trousers.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To raise or increase sharply; to hike.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** They tried to make more money by putting lots of salt in the popcorn and hiking up the price of drinks.
