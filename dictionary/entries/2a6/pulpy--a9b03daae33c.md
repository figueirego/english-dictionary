### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having the characteristics of pulp.

   **Example:** the pulpy texture of overripe fruit

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Having the characteristics of pulp fiction; thus, having a garish focus on sex and violence.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** a pulpy TV thriller
