### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A comparison of items that, however categorically similar, is inapt; an incompatible analogy, or the items so compared; a disanalogy.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; plural; plural-only

   **Example:** It's apples and oranges. You can't convert square meters to linear inches.

   **Source synonyms:** chalk and cheese; day and night; night and day
