### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A piece of dining room furniture having drawers and shelves for linen and tableware; originally for serving food.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** furniture; lifestyle

   **Source synonyms:** buffet; cupboard (obsolete)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

<a id="r1-s3"></a>
