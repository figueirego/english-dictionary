## Phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Indicating that nothing that could be said would add to or improve the situation.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** A&#58; But it's written right there in the instructions! B&#58; What can I say? We're not dealing with people who are good at reading, here.

   **Source synonyms:** what can I tell you; what can you do; it is what it is
