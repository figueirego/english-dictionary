### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A liquid byproduct from a grain distillery, consisting of the liquid portion of draff after the spent grain is removed, plus washings from tuns (spent wash). Over time its disposition has varied&#58; mostly as dumped waste, sometimes sold for part of the ration for pigs and cattle, when such a market is available.

   **Usage:** uncountable
