**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Despite that; however.

   **Usage:** conjunctive; not-comparable

   **Example:** I'm not paid to do all this paperwork for you. I will do it this once, though.

   **Example:** So they got married. That, though, was not the end of the story.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; degree

   **Example:** "Man, it's hot in here." — "Isn't it, though?"
