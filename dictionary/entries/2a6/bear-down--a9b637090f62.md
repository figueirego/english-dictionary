### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see bear, down.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To steer away from the wind; to approach from windward.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To push (someone) to the ground; to defeat, overcome.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To maintain one's position against (someone) in a debate; to stand one's ground against.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To intensify one's efforts.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** It's 9&#58;41, 58 degrees, and I'm flunking out. Time to bear down.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To approach in a determined manner.

   **Usage:** intransitive; with-on

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To exert downward pressure on one's abdomen, as in giving birth, forcing out feces, and some similar bodily maneuvers.

   **Usage:** intransitive
