**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An imaginary device that measures cringeworthiness (the extent to which something makes one cringe; that is, flinch or recoil with embarrassment).

   **Usage:** humorous; informal

   **Example:** My cringeometer went off the scale as I watched his excruciating performance.
