### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make loose.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to loosen a knot; to loosen one's grip / hold on something

   **Example:** After the Thanksgiving meal, Bill loosened his belt.

   **Source synonyms:** ease; relax; untighten

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To become loose.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** I noticed that my seatbelt had gradually loosened during the journey.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To disengage (a device that restrains).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** undo; unfasten

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To become unfastened or undone.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To free from restraint; to set at liberty.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** liberate; release; set free

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To relieve (the bowels) from constipation; to promote defecation.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To create a breach or rift between (two parties).

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To sail away (from the shore).

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete

   **Source synonyms:** put out
