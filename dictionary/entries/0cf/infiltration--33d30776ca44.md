### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act or process of infiltrating, as of water into a porous substance, or of a fluid into the cells of an organ or part of the body.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The act or process of infiltrating, as of water into a porous substance, or of a fluid into the cells of an organ or part of the body.
   1. process by which water on the ground surface enters the soil.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable; soil science

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; hydrology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
