### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A large piece of machinery that digs holes or trenches.

   **Source synonyms:** excavator

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A tool for digging.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A spade (playing card).

   **Usage:** slang

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. One who digs.

**Source sense R1.S5**

   **Usage:** Australia; obsolete

   **Usage:** Australia; informal

   **Usage:** Australia; broadly; dated
