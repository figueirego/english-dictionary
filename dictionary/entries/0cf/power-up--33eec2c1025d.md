### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To turn the electrical power on to a device as a precondition to making it operational.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Every morning he powered up his electronic devices, even before making coffee.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To become ready for operation as a result of the provision of electrical power.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The office systems power up automatically at 7&#58;30am on workdays.
