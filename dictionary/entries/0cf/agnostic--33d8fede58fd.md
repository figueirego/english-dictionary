### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Doubtful or uncertain about the existence or demonstrability of God or other deity.

   **Example:** She left the church when she had become agnostic.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Having no firmly held opinions on something.

   **Usage:** informal; usually

   **Example:** I'm agnostic on whether ethanol is a green fuel.

   **Example:** He says he's agnostic concerning the Secretary's claims.

   **Source context (verbatim):** with a prepositional phrase

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of or relating to agnosticism or its adherents.

   **Example:** His agnostic viewpoint is summarized in his book.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Unaware or noncommittal regarding the specific nature of the components or input with which it interacts.

   **Example:** The socket communications layer is agnostic with regard to its underlying transport mechanism—it is “transport-agnostic”.

   **Example:** The software's registration key is platform agnostic and will work on both x86 and ARM processors.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a software component etc.
