## Adverb · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Two steps in the vertical direction and one step in the horizontal direction, or vice versa, with each move, in the manner of a chess knight.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; pertaining to moving over a square grid

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. (Moving) with slope 2; (taking) 2n vertical steps and n horizontal steps (with n being some positive integer), or vice versa, with each move.

   **Usage:** broadly; not-comparable; pertaining to moving over a square grid
