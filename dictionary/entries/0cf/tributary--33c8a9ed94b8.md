### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A natural water stream that flows into a larger river or other body of water.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; hydrology; natural-sciences

   **Source synonyms:** affluent; influent

   **Source antonyms:** distributary

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A vein which drains into another vein.

   **Example:** The great saphenous vein is a tributary of the femoral vein.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
