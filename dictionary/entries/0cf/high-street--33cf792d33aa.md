### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The main street of any town.

   **Usage:** Ireland; UK; parts of New England

   **Source synonyms:** main street; Main Street; high road

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Mainstream shops, banks, etc. that can be found on such a street, in contrast to more specialist shops and services.

   **Usage:** Ireland; UK

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Physical, bricks and mortar shops, in contrast to Internet shops.

   **Usage:** Ireland; UK

   **Source synonyms:** bricks and mortar
