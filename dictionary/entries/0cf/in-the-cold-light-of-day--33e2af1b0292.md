### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Viewed calmly and dispassionately after allowing emotions to cool or after having sobered up.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** In the cold light of day, I felt I had overreacted.

   **Source antonyms:** in the heat of the moment

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see in, the, cold, light, of, day; during the day; in daylight.
