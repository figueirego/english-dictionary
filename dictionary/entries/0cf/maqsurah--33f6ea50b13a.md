### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An enclosure, box, or screen near the mihrab in a mosque, typically reserved for a Muslim ruler and his entourage, and originally designed to shield him from potential assassins during prayer.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
