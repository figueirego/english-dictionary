## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The goddess and personification of justice; one of the Horae, she is a daughter of Jupiter and Themis, and her sisters are Pax (Peace) and Disciplina / Eunomia (Order of Law); her Greek counterpart is Dike.

   **Usage:** Roman

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; mysticism; mythology; philosophy; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A female given name from Latin.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. justice, order and judgement.

   **Usage:** poetic
