## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any specific feature, part, or element of something.

   **Example:** Japan's aging population is an important aspect of its economy.

   **Source synonyms:** component; facet; feature

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The way something appears when viewed from a certain direction or perspective.

   **Example:** The stone has a greenish aspect in this light.

   **Source synonyms:** hue

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The way something appears when considered from a certain point of view.

   **Example:** in certain aspects \[ = in certain respects\]

   **Source synonyms:** respect; front

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A phase or a partial, but significant view or description of something.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. One's appearance or expression.

   **Source synonyms:** appearance; look; blee (obsolete)

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Position or situation with regard to seeing; that position which enables one to look in a particular direction; position in relation to the points of the compass.

   **Example:** The house has a southern aspect, i.e. a position which faces the south.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Prospect; outlook.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A grammatical quality of a verb which determines the relationship of the speaker to the internal temporal flow of the event which the verb describes, or whether the speaker views the event from outside as a whole, or from within as it is unfolding.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** grammar; human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. The relative position of heavenly bodies as they appear to an observer on earth; the angular relationship between points in a horoscope.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astrology; human-sciences; mysticism; philosophy; sciences

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. The personified manifestation of a deity that represents one or more of its characteristics or functions.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; lifestyle; mysticism; mythology; philosophy; religion; sciences

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. The act of looking at something; gaze.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s12"></a>

**Source sense R1.S12**

12. Appearance to the eye or the mind; look; view.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s13"></a>

**Source sense R1.S13**

13. In aspect-oriented programming, a feature or component that can be applied to parts of a program independent of any inheritance hierarchy.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; programming; sciences

<a id="r1-s14"></a>

**Source sense R1.S14**

14. The visual indication of railway signal as displayed to the driver. With three-aspect colour light signals this would be red, yellow or green, and on four-aspect signals, double-yellow also; a two-aspect signal displays red or green.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** rail-transport; railways; transport
