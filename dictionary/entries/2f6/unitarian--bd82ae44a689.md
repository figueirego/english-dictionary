## Noun · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˌjuːnɪˈtɛəɹi.ən/ — source labels: Received-Pronunciation
- IPA: /ˌjunəˈtɛɹi.ən/ — source labels: General-American

### Forms

- unitarians — plural

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A Christian who denies the doctrine of the Trinity, usually stressing some distinctions between God the Father and God the Son without denying the divinity of Jesus.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity

   **Source antonyms:** trinitarian; Trinitarian

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Synonym of Muwahhid, a Muslim advocating God's strict unity.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Islam; lifestyle; religion

   **Source synonyms:** Muwahhid (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. Synonym of monotheist, a believer in the unity of God, particularly non-Christians (Muslims, Jews, etc.) discussed from a Christian perspective.

   **Usage:** uncommon

   **Source synonyms:** monotheist (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. A person who rejects dualism in other contexts.

   **Source antonyms:** dualist

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** unipersonalist — nontrinitarian Christian; Unitarian — nontrinitarian Christian; Arian (pejorative; usually) — nontrinitarian Christian
