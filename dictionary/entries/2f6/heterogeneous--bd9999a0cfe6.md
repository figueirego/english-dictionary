### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Diverse in kind or nature; composed of diverse parts.

   **Example:** He had a large and heterogeneous collection of books.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Incommensurable because of different kinds.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Having more than one phase (solid, liquid, gas) present in a system or process.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Visibly consisting of different components.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Of a network comprising different types of computers, potentially with vastly differing memory sizes, processing power and even basic underlying architecture; alternatively, of a data resource with multiple types of formats.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences
