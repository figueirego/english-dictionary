## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Action on the part of one party which will be treated by a court as equivalent to the use of force in determining whether that party has acted improperly with respect to another.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** A civilian who impersonates a police officer in order to convince a debtor to turn over physical possession of collateral owed to a creditor has used constructive force to acquire the collateral.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law
