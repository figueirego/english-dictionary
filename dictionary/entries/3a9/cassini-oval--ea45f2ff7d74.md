### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A plane curve defined as the set (or locus) of points in the plane such that the product of the distances to two fixed points is constant (related to an ellipse, for which the sum of the distances is constant).

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences
