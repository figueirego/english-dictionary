**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To introduce (something) little by little, either via discrete phases of increase or (by extension) by continuous gradations; to introduce in phases, or as if by phases (gradually); to begin (something) gradually.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The government will phase in the new education system over a period of three years.

   **Example:** The program phased in last year.

   **Source antonyms:** phase out
