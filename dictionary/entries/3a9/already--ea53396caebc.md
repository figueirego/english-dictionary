### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Prior to some specified time, either past, present, or future; by this time; previously.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** I was surprised that she hadn’t already told me the news.

   **Example:** Much of what he said I knew already.

   **Example:** I still haven't seen that movie. I hope you haven't already seen it either.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. So soon.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Are you quitting already?

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An intensifier used to emphasize impatience or express exasperation.

   **Usage:** Canada; US; not-comparable

   **Example:** I wish they'd finish already, so we can get going.

   **Example:** Enough already!

   **Example:** Be quiet already!

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Indicates the completion of an action (whether past or hypothetical), or more generally, perfective aspect.

   **Usage:** Singapore; Singlish; not-comparable

   **Source synonyms:** liao (Singlish)

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Marks a change in state, more or less in the same manner as the inchoative aspect.

   **Usage:** Singapore; Singlish; not-comparable

   **Source synonyms:** liao (Singlish)
