### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The surroundings, circumstances, environment, background or settings that determine, specify, or clarify the meaning of an event or other occurrence.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** —In what context did your attack on him happen? —We had a pretty tense relationship at the time, and when he insulted me I snapped.

   **Source synonyms:** frame; framework; background; backdrop; setting; purlieus; milieu

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The text in which a word or passage appears and which helps ascertain its meaning.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Without context, I can't tell if dish refers to a plate, or to the food served thereon.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The surroundings and environment in which an artifact is found and which may provide important clues about the artifact's function and/or cultural meaning.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** archaeology; history; human-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The trama or flesh of a mushroom.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; mycology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. For a formula&#58; a finite set of variables, which set contains all the free variables in the given formula.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; logic; mathematics; philosophy; sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The data (register contents, program counter value, etc.) needed to switch to another thread of execution.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; programming; sciences
