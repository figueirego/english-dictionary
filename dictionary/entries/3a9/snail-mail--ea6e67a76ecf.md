### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Mail (physical material) sent in the post, especially as compared with email.

   **Usage:** retronym; uncountable

   **Example:** I came home to a mailbox stuffed with snail mail.

   **Source synonyms:** smail

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Mail (postal service), especially as compared with email.

   **Usage:** retronym; uncountable

   **Example:** The official notice will be sent via both email and snail mail owing to legal requirements.

   **Source synonyms:** smail
