### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Synonym of heart (“the seat of the affections or love”).

   **Source synonyms:** heart (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Also in the plural form heartlands&#58;
   1. The interior part of a region, especially when contrasted with coastal parts or when regarded as particularly powerful or significant.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Also in the plural form heartlands&#58;
   1. In the geopolitical theory of the English geographer Halford John Mackinder (1861–1947)&#58; the interior of the world island comprising north-central Eurasia, regarded as politically powerful.

      **Usage:** specifically

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Also in the plural form heartlands&#58;
   1. Residential districts and planning areas outside the city centre; the new towns of Singapore collectively.

      **Usage:** Singapore; in-plural; specifically

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Also in the plural form heartlands&#58;
   1. The states in the centre of the United States, chiefly regarded as politically and socially conservative; also, the people living in such states collectively.

      **Usage:** US; attributive; often; specifically

      **Source synonyms:** Middle America

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Also in the plural form heartlands&#58;
   1. A region or part of a region particularly associated with or significant for a characteristic, such as an activity, a faith, support for a political party or other organization, etc.

      **Example:** The home counties are the Conservative heartland.

      **Example:** We stayed in the heartland of the French wine-growing regions.
