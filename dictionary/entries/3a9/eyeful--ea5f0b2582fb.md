## Noun · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Forms

- eyesful — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A full or complete view; a good look.

   **Example:** Get an eyeful of this!

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A remarkable sight or a very attractive person.

   **Usage:** ironic; sometimes

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Enough material to fill one's eye.

   **Example:** I could barely see through two eyefuls of dust.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A brief period of sleep; forty winks.

   **Usage:** informal
