**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To remove from an official register or list.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** When the company failed to file its financial statements, it was delisted by the Stock Exchange.

   **Example:** When the property had failed to sell for months on end, it was delisted by the owner, who will try again next spring.

   **Source synonyms:** unlist

   **Source antonyms:** list; relist
