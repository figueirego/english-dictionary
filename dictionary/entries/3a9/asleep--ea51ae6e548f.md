## Adjective

### Pronunciation

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In a state of sleep; also, broadly, resting.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; predicative

   **Example:** I was asleep when you called.

   **Example:** Never disturb a man asleep.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Inattentive.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; predicative; slang

   **Example:** How could you miss that? Were you asleep?

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Having a numb or prickling sensation accompanied by a degree of unresponsiveness.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; predicative

   **Example:** My arm fell asleep. You know, like pins and needles.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a body part

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Dead.

   **Usage:** euphemistic; not-comparable; predicative
