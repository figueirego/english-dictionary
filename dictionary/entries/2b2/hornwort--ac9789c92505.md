### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of certain bryophytes, of phylum Anthocerotophyta, with a leafless thallus characterized by a dominant gametophyte stage of the life cycle and a sporophyte stage shaped like a horn.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An aquatic flowering plant in the genus Ceratophyllum

   **Source synonyms:** coontail

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Vernal grasses (Anthoxanthum spp.).
