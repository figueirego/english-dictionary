**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To cause to fall by shooting.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The carnival game involved shooting down tin cans.

   **Example:** My grandfather shot down several fighter planes in the war.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive
