**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Unable to speak; lacking power of speech.

   **Usage:** dated

   **Example:** deaf, dumb, and blind

   **Source synonyms:** mute; speechless; wordless

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

2. Not talkative; taciturn or unwilling to speak.

   **Usage:** archaic

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Having no input or voice in running things.

   **Usage:** dated

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Unaccompanied by words or speech, silent, wordless.

   **Usage:** dated; usually; actions

   **Example:** dumb show

   **Source context (verbatim):** of things

**Source sense R1.S5**

   **Usage:** dated
