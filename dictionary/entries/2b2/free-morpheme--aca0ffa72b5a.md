### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A morpheme that can carry meaning on its own, and does not require a prefix, suffix, or infix to give it meaning.

   **Example:** Book is a free morpheme, but the -s on books is not, because -s carries no meaning until joined to a free morpheme.
