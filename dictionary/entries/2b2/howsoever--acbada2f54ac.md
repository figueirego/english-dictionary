## Adverb

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˌhaʊsoʊˈɛvəɹ/ — source labels: US

### Forms

- howsever — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Regardless of the way in which.

   **Example:** Howsoever they pleaded, their pleas went unheard.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In any manner whatsoever.

   **Example:** The party to this agreement may use the property whensoever and howsoever he or she pleases.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To whatever degree or extent.

   **Example:** Their evidence, howsoever true it may have been, was not believed.
