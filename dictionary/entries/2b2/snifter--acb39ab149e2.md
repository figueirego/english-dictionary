### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A sniff.

   **Usage:** Northern-England; Scotland

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. A strong or severe wind.

   **Usage:** Northern-England; Scotland; figuratively

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. A small, wide, pear-shaped glass used for drinking aromatic liquors such as bourbon and brandy.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Source synonyms:** brandy bowl; brandy glass; brandy snifter; cognac glass; whiskey glass

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. Synonym of nip (“a small amount of an alcoholic beverage, especially one equivalent to what a snifter (noun sense 2.2) might hold”)

   **Usage:** figuratively; informal

   **Source synonyms:** nip (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. Synonym of cocaine addict; a sniffer.

   **Usage:** US; figuratively; slang

   **Source synonyms:** cocaine addict (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. A small amount of cocaine taken by inhaling through the nose.

   **Usage:** US; figuratively; slang

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

**Source sense R2.S7**

   **Usage:** US; figuratively; slang
