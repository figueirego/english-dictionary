### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A portion of land or territory as defined by its landform, its geographical (and architectural) features.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A portion of land which the eye can comprehend in a single view, including all the objects thus seen.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A sociological aspect of a physical area.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A picture representing a real or imaginary scene by land or sea, the main subject being the general aspect of nature, as fields, hills, forests, water, etc.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The pictorial aspect of a country.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. a mode of printing where the horizontal sides are longer than the vertical sides.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; media; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; printing; publishing; sciences

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A space, indoor or outdoor and natural or man-made (as in "designed landscape").

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. a situation that is presented, a scenario.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

   **Example:** The software patent landscape has changed considerably in the last years
