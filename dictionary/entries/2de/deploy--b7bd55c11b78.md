### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To prepare and arrange (originally military unit or units, i.e., to array troops) for use.

   **Usage:** ergative; transitive

   **Example:** "Deploy two units of infantry along the enemy's flank," the general ordered.

   **Example:** deploy some lifeguards on the beach

   **Example:** Teachers can deploy a wide range of resources in their classrooms.

   **Source synonyms:** field

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To unfold, open, or otherwise become ready for use.

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** He waited tensely for his parachute to deploy.

   **Example:** deploy the airbag

   **Example:** The airbag will deploy on collision.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To install, test and implement a computer system or application.

   **Example:** The process for the deployment scenario includes&#58; building a master installation of the operating system, creating its image and deploying the image onto a destination computer.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences
