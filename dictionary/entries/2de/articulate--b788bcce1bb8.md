### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make clear or effective.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To speak clearly; to enunciate.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** I wish he’d articulate his words more clearly.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To explain; to put into words; to make something specific.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I like this painting, but I can’t articulate why.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To bend or hinge something at intervals, or to allow or build something so that it can bend.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** an articulated bus

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To attack a note, as by tonguing, slurring, bowing, etc.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Articulate that passage heavily.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To form a joint or connect by joints.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The lower jaw articulates with the skull at the temporomandibular joint.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To treat or make terms.
