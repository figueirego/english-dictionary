### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A string representing an encoded piece of text.

   **Example:** Bob cannot get any information out of the codeword since he doesn't know what transmission bases Alice used.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; cryptography; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A word or phrase (or by extension a concept) used to make a (concealed) reference to another word or concept.

   **Source synonyms:** dogwhistle

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A type of crossword puzzle where the letters of the alphabet are represented by numbers and the solver must identify them by their position and frequency.

   **Usage:** crosswording
