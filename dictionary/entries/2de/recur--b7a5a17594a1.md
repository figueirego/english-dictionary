### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of an event, situation, etc.&#58; to appear or happen again, especially repeatedly.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The theme of the prodigal son recurs later in the third act.

   **Source synonyms:** recrudesce; recourse (obsolete); recur; rehappen (rare); reoccur; repeat

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of an event, situation, etc.&#58; to appear or happen again, especially repeatedly.
   1. Of a disease or symptom&#58; to happen again, especially repeatedly or after a remission or an apparent recovery.

      **Usage:** intransitive

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

      **Source synonyms:** recrudesce; recourse (obsolete); recur; rehappen (rare); reoccur; repeat

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of a memory, thought, etc.&#58; to come to the mind again.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To speak, think, or write about something again; to go back or return to a memory, a subject, etc.

   **Usage:** dated; intransitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Followed by to, or (Scotland, obsolete) on or upon&#58; to have recourse to someone or something for assistance, support, etc.; to appeal, to resort, to turn to.

   **Usage:** archaic; intransitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Synonym of recurse (“to execute a procedure recursively”).

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** recurse (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Often in the form recurring following a number&#58; of a numeral or group of numerals in a decimal fraction&#58; to repeat indefinitely.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** One-third can be written in decimal form as 0.3333 …, or point three-recurring.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Followed by into or to&#58; to go to a place again; to return.

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. Followed by into or to&#58; To go back to doing an activity, or to using a thing; to return.

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. Followed by to&#58; to go to a place; to resort.

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete; rare

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. Followed by from&#58; to move or run back from something; to recede, to withdraw.

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete; rare
