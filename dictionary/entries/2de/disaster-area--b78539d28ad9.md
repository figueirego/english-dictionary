## Noun

### Forms

- disaster areas — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An area that has been the site of a natural disaster that receives an official designation entitling residents and organizations within that area to receive relief and benefits.

   **Example:** Much of the central Gulf Coast was declared a disaster area after hurricane Katrina.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An area that looks like one that has suffered a natural disaster.

   **Usage:** also; figuratively

   **Example:** Your room is a disaster area.
