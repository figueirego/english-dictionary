### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A children's play area consisting of a box filled with sand.

   **Usage:** Canada; US

   **Example:** Her children were playing in the sandbox.

   **Source synonyms:** sandpit

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A box filled with sand that is shaped to form a mould for metal casting.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A container for sand or pounce, used historically before blotting paper.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An animal's litter box.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A box carried on locomotives, from which sand runs onto the rails in front of the driving wheels, to prevent slipping.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** rail-transport; railways; transport

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. An isolated area where a program can be executed with a restricted portion of the resources available.

   **Example:** Running a program in a sandbox can prevent it from doing any damage to the system.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A page on a wiki where users are free to experiment without destroying or damaging any legitimate content.

   **Usage:** wiki jargon

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. The Middle East.

   **Usage:** US; slang; usually "The Sandbox"

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war
