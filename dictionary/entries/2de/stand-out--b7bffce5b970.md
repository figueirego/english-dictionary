### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To jut out, protrude (from a surface etc.).

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To persist in opposition or resistance (against something); to refuse to comply (with someone).

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To sail in a direction away from shore.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To maintain, to persist in claiming (that).

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To be clearly distinguishable; to be obvious, to be easily noticeable, in contrast to the surroundings.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Tourist guides often carry umbrellas so that they stand out in a crowd.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To be prominent or conspicuous; to have special significance.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** She stood out from the other candidates and was offered the job.

   **Source context (verbatim):** used figuratively
