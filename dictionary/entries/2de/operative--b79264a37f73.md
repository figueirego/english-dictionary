### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Effectual or important.

   **Example:** He's usually in a good mood — the operative word there being "usually". Today was a disaster.

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Functional, in working order.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Having the power of acting; hence, exerting force, physical or moral; active in the production of effects.

   **Example:** an operative motive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Producing the appropriate or designed effect; efficacious.

   **Example:** an operative dose, rule, or penalty

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Based upon, or consisting of, a surgical operation or operations.

   **Example:** operative surgery
