### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A type of gallows consisting of a wooden triangle supported by three wooden legs, used for public hangings.

   **Usage:** UK; historical

   **Example:** The original three-legged mare was the Tyburn tree, which stood near a major road junction in Middlesex (present-day Greater London); another well-known example was the York Tyburn at Knavesmire, Yorkshire.
