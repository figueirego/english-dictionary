## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The process of or results of a second tally, for purposes of accuracy and/or safety.

   **Example:** For controlled substances' prescriptions pharmacists do a double-count as a matter of course.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An error in accounting etc. where something is counted more than once.
