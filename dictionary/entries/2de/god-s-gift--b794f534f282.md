## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Said of someone with an inflated sense of self-importance, or (occasionally) something that is overvalued.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; uncountable; ironic or meiosis

   **Example:** He thinks he's God's gift to music lovers.

   **Example:** They're acting as if his new album is God's gift to all music lovers.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see God's, gift&#58; especially, any blessing duly appreciated.

   **Usage:** endearing; uncountable; occasionally literal

   **Example:** Our darling baby was God's gift to us this year.
