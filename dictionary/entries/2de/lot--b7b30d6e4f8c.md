### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A large quantity or number; a great deal.

   **Example:** win the whole lot (of money); i.e. jackpot

   **Example:** lots of people think so

   **Source synonyms:** load; [mass](../102/mass--4084a32b9bba.md); pile

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A separate, appropriated portion; a quantized, subdivided set consisting a whole.

   **Example:** a lot of stationery

   **Example:** The Lord divided the land to the tribes, each according to his lot.

   **Source synonyms:** batch; collection; group; set

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. One or more items auctioned or sold as a unit, separate from other items.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A number of people taken collectively.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** a sorry lot

   **Example:** a bad lot

   **Example:** you lot

   **Source synonyms:** crowd; gang; group

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A distinct portion or plot of land, usually smaller than a field.

   **Example:** a building lot in a city

   **Source synonyms:** allotment; parcel; plot

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. That which happens without human design or forethought.

   **Source synonyms:** chance; accident; destiny; fate; fortune; chance (archaic); destin (obsolete); destiny; doom; fatality; fate; foredoom; foreordination; fortune; kismet; lot; orlay (Germanic); portion; predestination; predestiny (obsolete); preordination; qadar; weird (archaic); wyrd (Old-Norse)

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Anything (as a die, pebble, ball, or slip of paper) used in determining a question by chance, or without human choice or will.

   **Example:** to cast lots

   **Example:** to draw lots

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. The part, or fate, that falls to one, as it were, by chance, or without one's planning.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A prize in a lottery.

   **Source synonyms:** prize

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. Allotment; lottery.

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. All members of a set; everything.

   **Usage:** definite; the lot

   **Example:** The table was loaded with food, but by evening there was nothing but crumbs; we had eaten the lot.

   **Example:** If I were in charge, I'd fire the lot of them.

<a id="r1-s12"></a>

**Source sense R1.S12**

12. An old unit of weight used in many European countries from the Middle Ages, often defined as 1/30 or 1/32 of a (local) pound.

   **Usage:** historical
