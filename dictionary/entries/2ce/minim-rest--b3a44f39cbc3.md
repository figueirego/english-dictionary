## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A pause or interval of silence equal in duration to two quarter rests or one half of a whole rest. In common or 4/4 time, its duration is two beats.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A symbol used in musical notation drawn as a solid rectangle directly above the middle line of a staff whose height is half the distance between lines.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music
