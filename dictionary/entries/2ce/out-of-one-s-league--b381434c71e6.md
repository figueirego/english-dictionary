# out of one's league

## Prep\_phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In a situation in which one is mismatched with one or more others whose accomplishments, preparedness, or other characteristics are on a significantly higher or lower level than one's own.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I can't believe she agreed on a date with him. She's totally out of his league.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Out of one's depth; in a situation which one is poorly prepared to handle.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** These questions are out of my league, please let me ask a higher-up.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Too good or too expensive for one.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** An expensive car is out of your league right now, don't you think?
