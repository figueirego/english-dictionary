## Noun

### Forms

- sterilisation — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The process of treating something to kill or inactivate microorganisms.

   **Usage:** British; English; Oxford; US; uncountable

   **Example:** Heat sterilization is used during canning so the food can be safely stored for long periods.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A procedure to permanently prevent an organism from reproducing.

   **Usage:** British; English; Oxford; US; uncountable

   **Example:** Spaying a cat is a form of sterilization to limit the population growth.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An instance of a sterilization procedure.

   **Usage:** British; English; Oxford; US; countable

   **Example:** The vet performed several sterilizations this week.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A monetary policy operation used to offset a foreign exchange intervention.

   **Usage:** British; English; Oxford; US; uncountable

   **Example:** The Federal Reserve is responsible for foreign exchange sterilization.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The process of permanently rendering a planet like Earth completely uninhabitable to all life, including even microbes, leading to their complete extinction.

   **Usage:** British; English; Oxford; US; uncountable

   **Example:** In about 1 billion years, the increasing heat from the Sun as it gradually ages, by triggering a runaway greenhouse effect that will boil away the oceans, will lead to the complete sterilization of all life on Earth.
