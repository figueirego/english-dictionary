## Adverb · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Upward and downward, alternately.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** She looked me up and down and hinted that I could do with losing some weight.

   **Example:** I quickly eyed the building up and down.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Forwards and backwards alternately along a path.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; not-comparable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Staunchly, unwaveringly, obstinately.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; not-comparable

   **Example:** He swore up and down that he hadn't taken the money.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. In various places, e.g., in a country.

   **Usage:** figuratively; not-comparable
