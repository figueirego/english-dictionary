### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The extent to which a resource is used for the intended purpose.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source antonyms:** inefficiency; wastefulness

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The extent to which a resource is used for the intended purpose.
   1. The ratio of useful work to energy expended. Often given the symbol η.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Example:** The efficiency of this loudspeaker is 40%.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

      **Source antonyms:** inefficiency; wastefulness

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The extent to which a resource is used for the intended purpose.
   1. The extent to which time is well used for the intended task.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Example:** The efficiency of the planning department is deplorable.

      **Source antonyms:** inefficiency; wastefulness

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

   **Usage:** countable; dated; uncountable

   **Usage:** US; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** I have an efficiency available June through July.
