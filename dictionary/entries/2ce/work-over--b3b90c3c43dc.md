### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To improve a prototype, or first draft.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The estimated figures are not bad, but somebody will have to work them over.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To physically attack in order to cause injury.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; slang; transitive

   **Example:** He'll talk, once we work him over.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To subject (a person) to a severe scolding, interrogation, beating, etc.
