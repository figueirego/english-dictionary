### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A particular buddha; an artistic depiction of this buddha.
   1. In Pure Land Buddhism, a branch of Mahāyāna Buddhism, the name of the principal buddha regarded as of celestial origin.

      **Usage:** uncountable; usually

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Buddhism; lifestyle; religion

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A particular buddha; an artistic depiction of this buddha.
   1. In Vajrayāna Buddhism, the name of the western buddha, one of the Five Dhyani Buddhas representing the five qualities of the Buddha.

      **Usage:** uncountable; usually

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Buddhism; lifestyle; religion

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A class of deities.

   **Usage:** Hinduism; uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A male given name from Sanskrit used in India.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually
