### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To seduce, persuade or fascinate someone or something.

   **Example:** He charmed her with his dashing tales of his days as a sailor.

   **Source synonyms:** delight; enchant; enamor; entrance; fetch; charm (literary)

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To use a magical charm upon; to subdue, control, or summon by incantation or supernatural influence; to ensorcel or exert a magical effect on.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** After winning three games while wearing the chain, Dan began to think it had been charmed.

   **Source synonyms:** bewitch; enchant; ensorcel; enspell

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To protect with, or make invulnerable by, spells, charms, or supernatural influences.

   **Example:** She led a charmed life.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To make music upon.

   **Usage:** obsolete; rare

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To subdue or overcome by some secret power, or by that which gives pleasure; to allay; to soothe.
