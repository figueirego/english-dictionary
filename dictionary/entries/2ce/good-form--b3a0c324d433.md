## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Behavior that is both morally and socially correct; proper manner, decorum or etiquette.

   **Usage:** UK; uncountable

   **Example:** The Queen is a model of good form.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see good, form — especially, a high level of performance in an activity (often in the expression on good form; see also on form).

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Jones showed good form in the match today, and Smith was on good form too.
