### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A rhotic sound which is emphatic or strongly enunciated; in English accents especially a postvocalic consonantal r, as distinguished from its absence in a nonrhotic accent or from an R-coloured vowel.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; phonetics; phonology; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The hard r at the end of the word nigger when spoken with a General American accent and considered more offensive than the African-American Vernacular English pronunciation conventionally spelled nigga, whose final sound is sometimes called soft a.

   **Usage:** US
