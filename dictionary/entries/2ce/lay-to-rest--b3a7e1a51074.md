### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To perform funerary services, especially burial, for someone who has died.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** He was laid to rest beneath the old oak tree in 1825.

   **Source synonyms:** entomb; inhume; inter; bury

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To close (a matter of dispute).

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** Payment of the court-imposed fine should finally lay this matter to rest.

   **Source synonyms:** put to rest; put to bed; put to sleep
