### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To bring (the body or part of it) into contact with a substance that causes illness (a pathogen), so that the pathogen begins to act on the body; (of a pathogen) to come into contact with (a body or body part) and begin to act on it.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Not everyone will be infected when an epidemic strikes.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To contaminate (an object or substance) with a pathogen.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To make somebody enthusiastic about one's own passion, or to communicate a feeling to others, or a feeling communicating itself to others.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Her passion for dancing has infected me.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Of a computer virus&#58; to insert its own code into (another executable file).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences
