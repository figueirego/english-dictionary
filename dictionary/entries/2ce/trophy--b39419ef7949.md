## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An object, usually in the form of a statuette, cup, or shield, awarded for success in a competition or to mark a special achievement.

   **Example:** He won the trophy in a running competition.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An object taken as a prize by a hunter, or a conqueror or belligerent, especially one that is displayed.

   **Example:** The set of antlers which hung on the wall was his prized trophy.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any emblem of success; a status symbol.

   **Example:** His trophies included his second wife, his successful children, the third and fourth homes in Palm Beach and Malibu, and his three yachts.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An object taken by a serial killer or rapist as a memento of the crime.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** criminology; human-sciences; law; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A tropæum.

   **Usage:** Ancient-Rome; historical

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A display of weaponry and other militaria, often captured from a defeated enemy, as an ornament designed for the purpose of triumphalist display by a victor or as a show of military prowess by a monarch.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** architecture; art; arts

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. An artifact or artwork that has been stolen by a criminal and traded on the black market.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. An animal killed by a trophy hunter that usually has its parts sold on the black market.
