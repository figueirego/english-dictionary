## Adverb · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /(h)wɛɹˈɛvɚ/ — source labels: General-American
- IPA: /wɛəˈɹɛvə/ — source labels: Received-Pronunciation

### Forms

- whereever — alternative; obsolete

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Where ever; an emphatic form of where.

   **Usage:** interrogative; not-comparable

   **Example:** Wherever have you been all my life?

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In, at or to any place that one likes or chooses.

   **Usage:** informal; not-comparable

   **Example:** I don't care what you do with it; just put it wherever.

   **Example:** Where do you want to have lunch? — Wherever; I don't mind.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The place (no matter where) in, at or to which.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; fused relative

   **Example:** He's back from wherever he's been hiding.

   **Example:** Go home to wherever you belong.

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** everwhere (Appalachia; US; dialectal)

## Conjunction · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /(h)wɛɹˈɛvɚ/ — source labels: General-American
- IPA: /wɛəˈɹɛvə/ — source labels: Received-Pronunciation

### Forms

- whereever — alternative; obsolete

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Regardless of the place in, at or to which.

   **Example:** Wherever you go, I’ll find you.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. In, at or to any place (that); anywhere.

   **Example:** You can sit wherever you like.

   **Example:** I'd follow you wherever you went.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. In, at or to all places or situations (that); everywhere.

   **Example:** Add quotations wherever they are needed.

   **Example:** I see mistakes wherever I look.

   **Example:** Our charity has limited funds, but we help wherever we can.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. Used to show that one does not know where someone or something is located.

   **Example:** She lives in Puddletown, wherever that is.
