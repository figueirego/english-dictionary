### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A public office which acts as a trustee, usually when a sum is required to be deposited as security by legislation, when courts remove another trustee, or for estates where either no executor is named by will or the testator elects wishes to appoint one.
