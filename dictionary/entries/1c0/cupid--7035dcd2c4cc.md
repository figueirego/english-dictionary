### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The god of love, son of Venus; sometimes depicted as a putto (a naked, winged boy with bow and arrow). The Roman counterpart of Eros.

   **Usage:** Roman

   **Example:** The myth of Cupid and Psyche was one of the most influential romances in European culture.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; mysticism; mythology; philosophy; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Sudden love or desire; the personification of falling in love.

   **Usage:** poetic

   **Example:** He was just walking down the street minding his own business when Cupid struck.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A moon of Uranus.

   **Example:** Cupid is so small and far away that it can only be seen with the Hubble Space Telescope.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astronomy; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**
