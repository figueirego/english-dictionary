### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Infectious disease; pestilence, plague; (countable) sometimes used in curses such as a murrain on someone&#58; an outbreak of such a disease; a plague.

   **Usage:** archaic; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A widespread affliction, calamity, or destructive influx, especially when seen as divine retribution; a plague.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; figuratively

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of several highly infectious diseases of cattle or other livestock, such as anthrax, babesiosis, or rinderpest; or a particular epizootic thereof; also, an infectious disease affecting other animals, such as poultry.

   **Usage:** countable; historical

   **Example:** water murrain

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; medicine; natural-sciences; pathology; sciences; veterinary; zoology

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An infectious disease affecting plants.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable

   **Example:** potato murrain

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; medicine; natural-sciences; pathology; phytopathology; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A poor-quality green-salted animal hide.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Death, especially from an infectious disease.

   **Usage:** obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Rotting flesh, especially of an animal which has died from a disease; carrion.

   **Usage:** obsolete; uncountable
