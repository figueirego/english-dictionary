## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The difference (variation) constituted by having a brain that processes, learns, or behaves differently from what is societally considered typical.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Having autism is merely one form of neurodifference; being gifted and talented is another form of neurodifference.

   **Source synonyms:** neurodivergence; neurodiversity

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any particular facet or instance of such differentness.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** The researchers sought some way to classify and quantify these neurodifferences.
