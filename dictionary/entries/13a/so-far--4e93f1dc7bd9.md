## Adverb

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Until now; previously; yet.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** So far, nothing unusual has happened.

   **Example:** ―How are your driving lessons? ―So far, pretty good.

   **Source synonyms:** thus far; to date; hitherto; as yet (idiomatic); as of yet (idiomatic); erenow; herebefore (archaic); hereto (archaic); heretofore; heretoforetime (obsolete); hereunto (archaic); hereuntofore (rare); hitherto; hithertofore (rare); so far; thus far; to date (idiomatic); up to now; yet

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A limited distance, literal or metaphorical.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** Don’t expect a long walk. You can only go so far in that direction.
