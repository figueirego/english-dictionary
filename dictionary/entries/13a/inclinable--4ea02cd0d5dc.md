### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Capable of being inclined or tilted.

   **Example:** an inclinable desk

   **Source antonyms:** uninclinable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Capable of being inclined, or given a tendency.

   **Example:** He seemed inclinable to peace, if we negotiated skilfully.

   **Source antonyms:** uninclinable
