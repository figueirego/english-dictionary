### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make use of, to apply, especially of something nonmaterial; to bring to bear.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He considered exerting his influence on John to gain an advantage for himself.

   **Source synonyms:** exercise; utilize; assert; operate

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To put in vigorous action.

   **Usage:** reflexive

   **Example:** I exerted myself in today's training.

   **Source synonyms:** extend
