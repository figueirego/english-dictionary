### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A musical instrument of the brass family, slightly smaller than a trumpet, usually in the musical key of B-flat.

   **Source synonyms:** cornet-à-piston; cornet-à-pistons

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A piece of paper twisted to be used as a container.

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An ice cream cone (the shell itself).

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An ice cream cone (the unit comprising the shell and the ice cream).

   **Usage:** UK; broadly; dated

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A troop of cavalry; so called from its being accompanied by a cornet player.

   **Usage:** obsolete
