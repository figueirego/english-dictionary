## Prep\_phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. After a long time; eventually.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** Now that the dog has stopped barking, perhaps we can at last get some rest.

   **Example:** After three hundred years had passed, at last the vampire's soul was free.

   **Source synonyms:** in due course; sooner or later

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In the end; finally; ultimately.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** After all their troubles, at last they lived happily ever after.

   **Example:** After exhausting all possibilities, Holmes was at last satisfied the problem was unsolvable.

   **Source synonyms:** lastly; ultimately
