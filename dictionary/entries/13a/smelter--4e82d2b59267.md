### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person employed to do smelting.

   **Example:** He works as a smelter.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A machine used to smelt metal.

   **Example:** Throw the scrap in the smelter so we can melt it down and reuse it.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A place where smelting is done.

   **Example:** We'll take the scrap to the smelter and get some money for it.
