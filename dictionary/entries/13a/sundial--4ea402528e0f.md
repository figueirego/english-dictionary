### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A device measuring the time of day by the position of a shadow cast by a pole or plate (gnomon) upon an engraved series of marks.

   **Example:** The birdbath includes a small gnomon that acts as a sundial with markings on the basin if it's aligned correctly with the sun.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A sea snail of the genus Heliacus.
