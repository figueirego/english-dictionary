### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An edible plant, Lactuca sativa and its close relatives, having a head of green or purple leaves.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** sleepwort

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The leaves of the lettuce plant, eaten as a vegetable; as a dish often mixed with other ingredients, dressing etc.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** I’ll have a ham sandwich with lettuce and tomato.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** food; lifestyle; vegetable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. United States paper currency; dollars.

   **Usage:** US; slang; uncountable

   **Example:** Twenty dollars an hour? That's a lot of lettuce!

   **Source synonyms:** cabbage; greenbacks

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A strong yellow-green color, like that of lettuce (also called lettuce green).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

   **Usage:** slang; uncountable
