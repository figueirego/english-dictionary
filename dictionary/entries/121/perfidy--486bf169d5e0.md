### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A state or act of violating faith or allegiance; violation of a promise or vow, or of trust.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** betrayal; treachery; perfidiousness; betrayal; breach of promise; breach of trust; double-dealing; Judas kiss; perfidiousness (rare); perfidy; prodition (obsolete); Punic faith (archaic); stab in the back; swike (dialectal; obsolete); tergiversation; traitory (obsolete); treacherousness; treachery; treason

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An illegitimate act of deception, such as using symbols like the Red Cross or white flag in a false claim of surrender to gain proximity to an enemy for purposes of attack.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; international law

   **Source context (verbatim):** in warfare

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
