### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A litter pulled or borne by one horse or borne on poles borne by two or more horses.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Synonym of horsecart, particularly in contexts for carrying the injured or deceased in early cart ambulances or hearses.

   **Usage:** uncommon

   **Source synonyms:** horsecart (synonym; synonym-of); particularly in contexts for carrying the injured or deceased in early cart ambulances or hearses (synonym; synonym-of)

3. Straw or similar litter used for horses' rest or, once mixed with horse urine and excrement, as a waste product or fertilizer.
