## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Often used to refer to a place moderately far away. It generally is used in place of a city or town name when that name is unknown or cannot be recalled.

   **Usage:** US; slang

   **Example:** I don't get to see Jim much since he moved. Now he lives somewhere out in East Overshoe.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A placeholder for a fictitious municipality in sample documents or hypothetical situations. (This usage is similar to the way John Doe is sometimes used as a placeholder for the name of a person.)
