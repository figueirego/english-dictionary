### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A non-commissioned officer in the Royal Australian Navy and Royal Navy, ranking above able seaman and below petty officer.

   **Usage:** Australia; Navy; UK

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A non-commissioned officer in the Canadian Forces Maritime Command, ranking above able seaman and below master seaman.

   **Usage:** Canada; Navy
