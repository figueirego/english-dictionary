### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Expresses regret, remorse, or sorrow.

   **Example:** Sorry! I didn't see that you were on the phone.

   **Example:** Sorry about yesterday. — No worries.

   **Example:** Sorry I’m so late.

   **Source synonyms:** soz (Britain; slang)

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Said as a request to excuse one's unintentional behaviors.

   **Example:** Oops! Sorry!

   **Source synonyms:** excuse me

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. Said as a request to pass somebody.

   **Example:** Sorry! Coming through!

   **Source synonyms:** excuse me

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. Used as a request for someone to repeat something not heard or understood clearly.

   **Example:** Sorry? What was that? The phone cut out.

   **Source synonyms:** I beg your pardon?; I'm sorry?; say again; come again; excuse me? (US)

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. Used to correct oneself in speech.

   **Example:** There are four—sorry, five—local branches of the store.

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. Used as a hedge.

   **Example:** Sorry, but I don't care what you think.
