### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see put, it, to.

   **Example:** I will put it to you very candidly.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To put up for consideration.

   **Example:** put it to a vote

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To overload with work or obligations.

   **Example:** You really put it to him, making him do all the heavy-lifting.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To blame on.

   **Example:** They did not have an easy scapegoat, so they put it to the leader.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To exploit an unfair advantage; to cheat; to swindle.

   **Example:** That mechanic put it to Sam.

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Alternative form of stick it to.

   **Usage:** alt-of; alternative
