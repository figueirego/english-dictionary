### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A logical fallacy in which a premise of an argument contains a direct or indirect assumption that the conclusion is true; offering a circular argument; circular reasoning.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** It is an instance of begging the question to argue that God can only do good deeds because God is good.

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** circular argument; circular reasoning; hysteron proteron; petitio principii
