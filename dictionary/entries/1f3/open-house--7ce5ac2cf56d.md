## Noun

### Forms

- open houses — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A house habitually kept open to callers.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An event, often in one's home, at which there are few restrictions regarding who and how many may attend.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An event during which a facility or institution is open to the public for inspection or tour.

   **Source synonyms:** open day

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A period of time, usually several hours in a day, during which a house which is for sale is open for inspection or tour by interested parties.

   **Usage:** Australia; Canada; US

   **Example:** We′re holding an open house this weekend at 77 Parkway Road.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A casual school event where the school invites parents or guardians to tour their child's school and meet the teachers before the school year begins

   **Usage:** US

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** education
