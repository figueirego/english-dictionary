## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who lives in the bush, especially a member of a community or ethnic group who lives in the bush.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A member of one of the foraging ethnic groups native to southwestern Africa, mostly the Kalahari.

   **Usage:** offensive; sometimes
