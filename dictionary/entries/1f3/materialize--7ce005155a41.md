### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To cause to take physical form, or to cause an object to appear.

   **Usage:** British; English; Oxford; US; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** incorporate

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To take physical form, to appear seemingly from nowhere.

   **Usage:** British; English; Oxford; US; intransitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To become real (of a plan, idea, etc.); to come to fruition.

   **Usage:** British; English; Oxford; US; intransitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To regard as matter; to consider or explain by the laws or principles which are appropriate to matter.

   **Usage:** British; English; Oxford; US; transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To perform materialization; to save the results of a database query as a temporary table or materialized view.

   **Usage:** British; English; Oxford; US; transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; databases; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences
