### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In the initial position in a sequence whose elements are numbered starting at zero; the ordinal number corresponding to zero.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The zeroth order polynomial approximation is constant.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Corresponding to a position preceding the first.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** A and C are in equilibrium following the Zeroth Law of Thermodynamics.
