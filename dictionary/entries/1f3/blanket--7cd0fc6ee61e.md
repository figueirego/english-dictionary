### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A heavy, loosely woven fabric, usually large and woollen, used for warmth while sleeping or resting.

   **Example:** The baby was cold, so his mother put a blanket over him.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A covering layer of anything.

   **Example:** The city woke under a thick blanket of fog.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A thick rubber mat used in the offset printing process to transfer ink from the plate to the paper being printed.

   **Example:** A press operator must carefully wash the blanket whenever changing a plate.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A streak or layer of blubber in whales.
