### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To put aside, break all relations with, or reject finally.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** After finding out he'd been cheating, I had to finish with him.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To complete; to end the use of.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** After finishing with his homework, he was allowed to use his game machine.

   **Example:** After finishing with his game machine, he put it away and went to sleep.
