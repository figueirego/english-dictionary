## Suffix · Etymology 5 · Record 5

### Senses

<a id="r5-s1"></a>

**Source sense R5.S1**

1. Added to the end of words ending in ⟨d⟩, representing an AAVE pronunciation as /t/ rather than /d/, now generally with intensifying force.

   **Usage:** morpheme; slang; African-American Vernacular

   **Example:** period + -t → periodt

   **Example:** good + -t → goodt

   **Example:** Lord + -t → Lordt
