### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having two or more equally applicable meanings; capable of double or multiple interpretation.

   **Example:** equivocal words

   **Example:** an equivocal sentence

   **Source synonyms:** ambiguous; indeterminate

   **Source antonyms:** unequivocal; univocal

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Capable of being ascribed to different motives, or of signifying opposite feelings, purposes, or characters; deserving to be suspected.

   **Example:** His actions are equivocal.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Uncertain, as an indication or sign.

   **Source synonyms:** uncertain; doubtful; incongruous

   **Source antonyms:** certain
