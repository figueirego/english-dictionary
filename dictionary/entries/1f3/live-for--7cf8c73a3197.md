### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To deeply enjoy or be fulfilled by something esteemed as very important in one's life.

   **Example:** I live for knowledge.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To find a reason to continue having the will to live.

   **Example:** He has been chronically depressed and feels he has nothing to live for.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To eagerly await or hope for (a significant life event).

   **Example:** I live for the day I can finally break free from my servitude.
