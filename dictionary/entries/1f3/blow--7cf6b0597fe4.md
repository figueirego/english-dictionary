### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To produce an air current.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To propel by an air current (or, if under water, a water current), usually with the mouth.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Blow the dust off that book and open it up.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To be propelled by an air current.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The leaves blow through the streets in the fall.

   **Example:** What stormy weather —the roofs are blowing off!

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To direct or move, usually of a person to a particular location.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To create or shape by blowing.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to blow bubbles

   **Example:** to blow glass

   **Example:** Joe puffed on his pipe and blew a couple of smoke rings.

**Source sense R1.S6**

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to blow the fire

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To clear of contents by forcing air through.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to blow an egg

   **Example:** to blow one’s nose

   **Example:** The submarine blew its main ballast tanks.

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To cause to make sound by blowing (as a musical instrument).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Blow your horn! That car is about to hit us!

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To make a sound as a result of being blown.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** In the harbor, the ships’ horns blew.

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. To exhale visibly through the spout the seawater which it has taken in while feeding.
