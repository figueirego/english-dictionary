### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The amount of a certain phenomenon (such as stuff, things, energy, or otherwise) in a given area (surface area) or volume (amount of space).
   1. A measure of the mass of matter contained by a unit volume.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

      **Source synonyms:** denseness; mass density

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The amount of a certain phenomenon (such as stuff, things, energy, or otherwise) in a given area (surface area) or volume (amount of space).
   1. The ratio of one quantity, representing something of interest, to another quantity representing space, area, or extent in which the thing of interest is distributed.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Example:** The number of particles per unit volume of a specified volume can be considered to be the particle density for the specified volume.

      **Source synonyms:** denseness; number density

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The probability that an outcome will fall into a given range, per unit of that range; the relative likelihood of possible values of a continuous random variable.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences; statistics

   **Source synonyms:** probability density

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Stupidity.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** There's only so much density that pedagogy can penetrate.

   **Source synonyms:** denseness; thickheadedness; blockheadedness
