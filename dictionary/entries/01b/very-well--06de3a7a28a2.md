## Adverb · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see very, well.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** She plays very well almost any instrument that you can think of.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used to weaken the effect of certain modal verbs.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; not-comparable

   **Example:** It may very well rain this afternoon.

   **Example:** I can't very well talk to you and concentrate on sanding this at the same time.

## Interjection · Record 2

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Indicating acceptance, often with resignation or acquiescence, of a statement or situation.

   **Usage:** formal; idiomatic

   **Example:** "Jeeves, fetch the wine from the cellar." "Very well, sir."

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Synonym of fair enough.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Source synonyms:** fair enough (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. A standard response by a superior to a report or confirmation.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; naval; navy; politics; war
