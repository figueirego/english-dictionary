## Noun · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈhoʊmˌspʌn/ — source labels: General-American

### Forms

- homespuns — plural

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Fabric made from homespun yarn; clothing made from it. Also, machine made fabrics (usually cottons) similar to homespun fabrics in that solids, plaids, or stripes are created by weaving dyed threads (rather than printing), so that both sides of the fabric look the same.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. An unpolished, rustic person.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable
