### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Somebody with whom one works.

   **Example:** He heard from a coworker that the company planned to merge those departments.

   **Source synonyms:** colleague; workmate; adjunct; associate; business partner; cohort; colaborer /colabourer; collaborator; colleague; compeer; confrere; consociate; coworker /co-worker; fellow; jobmate; workfellow; workmate

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person in the lowest common denominator, especially regarding popular culture.

   **Usage:** Internet; attributive; derogatory

   **Example:** This is the most coworker movie I've ever seen.

   **Source synonyms:** normie
