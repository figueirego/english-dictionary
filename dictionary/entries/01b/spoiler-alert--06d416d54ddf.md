## Interjection · Record 2

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. An indication, placed in a text, that the plot of a drama is about to be exposed.

   **Example:** Spoiler alert&#58; The main character, Mark, succumbs to an illness at the end.

   **Source synonyms:** spoiler ahead; spoiler warning

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Indicates that the speaker is about to reveal something unanticipated or unpleasant.

   **Usage:** humorous; often

   **Example:** Spoiler alert, these measures have not proved to be very helpful at mitigating the effects of this crisis.

   **Source synonyms:** spoiler ahead; spoiler warning
