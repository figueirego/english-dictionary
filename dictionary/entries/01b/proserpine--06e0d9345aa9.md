## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Alternative form of Proserpina (“Roman goddess of the seasons and spring vegetation, daughter of Ceres and Jupiter; queen of the Underworld as wife of Pluto; equivalent to Greek Persephone and old Italic deity Libera”).

   **Usage:** Roman; alt-of; alternative

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; mysticism; mythology; philosophy; sciences

   **Alternative form of:** [Proserpina](../317/proserpina--c5fa7fa0d4e6.md)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A river in Queensland, Australia, named for the goddess in 1859.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A town in Whitsunday Region, Queensland, founded in the 1890s, so it would have been named after the river.
