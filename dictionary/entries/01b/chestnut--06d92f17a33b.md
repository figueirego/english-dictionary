### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An edible nut (technically a fruit) of the Spanish chestnut or sweet chestnut tree (Castanea sativa); also (chiefly preceded by a descriptive word), a nut from a related shrub or tree; or a similar nut from an unrelated plant.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source synonyms:** Spanish chestnut; sweet chestnut

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In full chestnut tree&#58; the shrub or tree that bears this nut, the Spanish chestnut or sweet chestnut (Castanea sativa); also (chiefly preceded by a descriptive word), a shrub or tree of the genus Castanea.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source synonyms:** Spanish chestnut; sweet chestnut

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. In full chestnut tree&#58; the shrub or tree that bears this nut, the Spanish chestnut or sweet chestnut (Castanea sativa); also (chiefly preceded by a descriptive word), a shrub or tree of the genus Castanea.
   1. Wood of a chestnut tree.

      **Usage:** uncountable

      **Source synonyms:** Spanish chestnut; sweet chestnut

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. In full chestnut tree&#58; the shrub or tree that bears this nut, the Spanish chestnut or sweet chestnut (Castanea sativa); also (chiefly preceded by a descriptive word), a shrub or tree of the genus Castanea.
   1. Short for horse chestnut (“any of several tree species of the genus Aesculus, especially Aesculus hippocastanum; the fruit of such a tree”).

      **Usage:** UK; abbreviation; alt-of; countable

      **Source synonyms:** Spanish chestnut; sweet chestnut

      **Alternative form of:** [horse chestnut](../36d/horse-chestnut--db4a3243a91b.md)

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Things resembling a chestnut fruit in appearance or colour.
   1. A dark, reddish-brown colour, like that of chestnut fruit (noun sense 1).

      **Usage:** broadly; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Things resembling a chestnut fruit in appearance or colour.
   1. A horse with a reddish-brown coat, mane and tail.

      **Usage:** broadly; countable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Things resembling a chestnut fruit in appearance or colour.
   1. An oval or round horny plate located on the inner side of the leg of a horse or other equines, which is thought by some people to correspond with the thumbnail of other animals.

      **Usage:** broadly; countable

      **Source synonyms:** night eye

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Chiefly in old chestnut&#58; a joke, meme, phrase, ploy, etc. which has been repeated so often as to have grown ineffective or tiresome; a cliché.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively

   **Source synonyms:** platitude
