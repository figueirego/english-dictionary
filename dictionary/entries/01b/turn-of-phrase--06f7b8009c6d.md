## Noun

### Forms

- turns of phrase — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An expression which is worded in a distinctive way, especially one which is particularly memorable or artful.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A distinctive way of wording things, a distinctive way of writing or speaking.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** This author has a beautiful turn of phrase.

   **Source synonyms:** style; prose
