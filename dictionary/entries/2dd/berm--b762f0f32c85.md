### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A narrow ledge or shelf, as along the top or bottom of a slope.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A raised bank or path, especially the bank of a canal opposite the towpath.

   **Source synonyms:** heelpath

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. One of the flat terraces on the slope of an open-pit mine.

   **Usage:** Australia

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; mining

   **Source synonyms:** bench; bench floor

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A terrace or shelf of sand along a beach, formed above the high tide water level by wave action.

   **Example:** On some beaches, the berm grows higher in summer and flattens out in the rougher winter seas.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A long mound or bank of earth, used especially as a barrier or to provide insulation.

   **Example:** A berm separates the Moroccan-controlled and Polisario-controlled parts of Western Sahara.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A small wall along the edge of a bench of an open-pit mine, intended to prevent items falling over the crest.

   **Usage:** Canada; US

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; mining

   **Source synonyms:** windrow
