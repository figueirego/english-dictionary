### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any psychoactive cannabinoid that is synthetically or semisynthetically derived from delta-9-THC and is not naturally present in cannabis except in negligible amounts.

   **Usage:** US

   **Source synonyms:** altnoid; noid; alt-cannabinoid; hemp-derived cannabinoid; derived psychoactive cannabis product; DPCP; gas station weed
