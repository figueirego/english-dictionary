### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Senses relating to the metal.
   1. A silvery-coloured, metallic chemical element with the atomic number 80; it is liquid at room temperature, and toxic.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source synonyms:** mul&#58;Hg; mul&#58;☿; azoth; hydrargyrum; quicksilver

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Senses relating to the metal.
   1. Preceded by the&#58; mercury (noun sense 1) as used in the column of a barometer or thermometer, its fall or rise thus indicating the decrease or increase of ambient pressure or temperature.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Example:** The mercury there has averaged 37.6°C, 2.3°C above the February norm.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Senses relating to the metal.
   1. A preparation containing mercury (noun sense 1), especially calomel (mercurous chloride) or corrosive sublimate (mercuric chloride), formerly used as a medicine to treat syphilis, etc.

      **Usage:** countable; historical; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Senses relating to the metal.
   1. One of the elemental principles formerly thought to be present in all metals.

      **Usage:** countable; historical; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Senses relating to the metal.
   1. Liveliness, volatility.

      **Usage:** countable; figuratively; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Senses relating to plants.
   1. An annual plant, now generally called annual mercury (Mercurialis annua), chiefly native to central and southern Europe which was formerly grown for its medicinal properties; French mercury, herb mercury.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source synonyms:** mercurial

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Senses relating to plants.
   1. Chiefly with a descriptive word.
      1. Any of several plants of the genus Mercurialis; specifically (obsolete), dog's mercury or wild mercury (Mercurialis perennis).

         **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Senses relating to plants.
   1. Chiefly with a descriptive word.
      1. Any of several plants resembling Mercurialis plants but of a different genus, or having similar medicinal or toxic qualities as annual mercury or dog's mercury.
         1. Allgood or Good King Henry (Blitum bonus-henricus), a species of goosefoot native to central and southern Europe formerly cultivated as a vegetable but now generally regarded as a weed; English mercury, false mercury (obsolete).

            **Usage:** Northern-England; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. Senses relating to plants.
   1. Chiefly with a descriptive word.
      1. Any of several plants resembling Mercurialis plants but of a different genus, or having similar medicinal or toxic qualities as annual mercury or dog's mercury.
         1. Poison ivy or poison oak (genus Toxicodendron, especially the eastern poison ivy (Toxicodendron radicans) or Atlantic poison oak (Toxicodendron pubescens)).

            **Usage:** Northeastern; US; countable; uncountable
