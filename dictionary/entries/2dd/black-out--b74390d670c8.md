### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To censor or cover up by writing over with black ink.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To censor or cover up.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To lose consciousness; to suffer a blackout.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source synonyms:** faint; pass out

   **Source antonyms:** black in; come to

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To cause (someone) to lose consciousness.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To obscure in darkness.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** darken

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To be in a state of blackout, as a building, a city, a ship.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The countryside blacked out to avoid helping enemy aircraft.
