### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To throw with a sudden effort or thrust; to hurl or launch.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To send forth suddenly or rapidly; to emit; to shoot.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** As the sun darted forth his beams, she darted a meaningful glance at me.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To shoot with a dart, especially a tranquilizer dart.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** They had to dart the animal to get close enough to help

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To fly or pass swiftly, like a dart; to move rapidly in one direction; to shoot out quickly.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The flying man darted eastward.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To start and run with speed; to shoot rapidly along.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The deer darted from the thicket.

   **Example:** The fish darted under a stone.

   **Source synonyms:** dash; steam
