### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Synonym of surname&#58; a name designating a person as a member of a family.

   **Example:** In the Chinese name Mao Zedong, Mao is the family name and Zedong is the given name.

   **Source synonyms:** surname (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A family’s honor or reputation.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A given name common within a family or given because of another family member.

   **Usage:** uncommon

   **Example:** Yeah, we named her Mildred. It's an old family name. Don't worry, though. She also has four middle names to choose from.
