### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A decision reached by a large number of people within the context of a society. Usually, this consists of a single choice from a pool of mutually exclusive options (as in an election, etc.).

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
