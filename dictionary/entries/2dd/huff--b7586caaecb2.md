## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /hʌf/ — source labels: General-American; Received-Pronunciation
- IPA: /hɐf/ — source labels: Australia; New-Zealand
- IPA: /hʊf/ — source labels: Northern-England

### Forms

- huffs — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A heavy breath; a grunt or sigh.

   **Example:** With a huff, he lifted the box onto the back of the truck.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A condition of anger, annoyance, disgust, etc.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** in a huff

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. One swelled with a false sense of importance or value; a boaster.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The act of removing an opponent's piece as a forfeit for deliberately not taking a piece (often signalled by blowing on it).

   **Usage:** draughts

## Verb · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /hʌf/ — source labels: General-American; Received-Pronunciation
- IPA: /hɐf/ — source labels: Australia; New-Zealand
- IPA: /hʊf/ — source labels: Northern-England

### Forms

- huffs — present; singular; third-person
- huffing — participle; present
- huffed — participle; past
- huffed — past

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To breathe heavily.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The run left him huffing and puffing.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To say in a huffy manner.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To enlarge; to swell up.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Bread huffs.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To bluster or swell with anger, arrogance, or pride; to storm; to take offense.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To treat with arrogance and insolence; to chide or rebuke rudely; to bully, to hector.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To vex; to offend.

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

**Source sense R2.S7**

7. To inhale psychoactive inhalants.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s8"></a>

**Source sense R2.S8**

8. To remove an opponent's piece as a forfeit for deliberately not taking a piece (often signalled by blowing on it).

   **Usage:** transitive; draughts
