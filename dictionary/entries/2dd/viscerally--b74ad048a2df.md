## Adverb

### Forms

- more viscerally — comparative
- most viscerally — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In a manner that affects the viscera or gut.

   **Usage:** literally

   **Example:** The soldier was wounded viscerally and was expected to die of gangrene.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In a visceral manner; in a way that affects one's inner core or produces overwhelming emotions.

   **Example:** The shock of my friend's decapitation affected me viscerally, and I became paralyzed with dread.
