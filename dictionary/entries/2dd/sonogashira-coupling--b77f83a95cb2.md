### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A crosscoupling reaction used in organic synthesis to form carbon–carbon bonds. It employs a palladium catalyst as well as copper co-catalyst to form a carbon–carbon bond between a terminal alkyne and an aryl or vinyl halide.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; organic-chemistry; physical-sciences
