## Adjective · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ɪˈtalɪk/ — source labels: UK
- IPA: /ɪˈtælɪk/ — source labels: US

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of or relating to the Italian peninsula.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Pertaining to a subfamily of the Centum branch of the Indo-European language family, that includes Latin and other languages (as Oscan, Umbrian) spoken by the peoples of ancient Italy

   **Usage:** Indo-European-studies; not-comparable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Pertaining to a subfamily of the Centum branch of the Indo-European language family, that includes Latin and other languages (as Oscan, Umbrian) spoken by the peoples of ancient Italy
   1. Osco-Umbrian; an extinct branch of such language family, which excludes the Latino-Faliscan languages

      **Usage:** Indo-European-studies; dated; not-comparable

      **Example:** The ancient Italic languages that are now extinct include Oscan, Umbrian, and South Picene.

      **Source synonyms:** Osco-Umbrian; Sabellic; Sabellian

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Pertaining to various peoples that lived in Italy before the establishment of the Roman Empire, or to any of several alphabet systems used by those peoples.

   **Usage:** historical; not-comparable

   **Example:** There were several Italic alphabets, one being the Etruscan alphabet.
