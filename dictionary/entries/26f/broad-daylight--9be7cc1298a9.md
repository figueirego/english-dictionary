### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Abundant natural illumination in daytime, producing clear visibility; hence, daytime.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** open daylight

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Chiefly preceded by in&#58; the full view of observers during the day.

   **Usage:** figuratively; uncountable

   **Example:** The bank robbery took place in broad daylight.
