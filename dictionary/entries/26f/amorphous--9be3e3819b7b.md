### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Lacking a definite form or clear shape.

   **Example:** amorphous blob

   **Example:** The enormous pile of spaghetti landed on the floor in an amorphous heap.

   **Source synonyms:** formless; shapeless

   **Source antonyms:** structured; well-defined

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Being without definite character or nature.

   **Usage:** broadly

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Lacking organization or unity.

   **Usage:** broadly

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. In the non-crystalline solid state of, or being a non-crystalline counterpart to, a crystalline solid.

   **Example:** Glass is an amorphous solid, but some glass is called "crystal" because it looks crystalline and has been called crystal since before humans even understood molecular structure at all.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

   **Source synonyms:** noncrystalline

   **Source antonyms:** crystalline; crystal

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Infinite and not the disjoint union of two infinite subsets.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences; set-theory

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a set
