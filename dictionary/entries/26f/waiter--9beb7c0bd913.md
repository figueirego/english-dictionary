### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A male or female attendant who serves customers at their tables in a restaurant, café or similar.

   **Example:** Waiter! There's a fly in my soup.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Someone who waits for somebody or something; a person who is waiting.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A person working as an attendant at the London Stock Exchange.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A vessel or tray on which something is carried, as dishes, etc.; a salver. (See etymology of dumbwaiter.)

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A custom house officer; a tide waiter.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A watchman.

   **Usage:** obsolete
