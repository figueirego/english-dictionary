### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of or pertaining to the coronal and apical aspects of a tooth (the crown and the root apex); as (usually, more specifically) being on the longitudinal axis of the tooth, defined by those poles.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; dentistry; medicine; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** apicocoronal
