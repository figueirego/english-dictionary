## Phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Expression of anger or contempt because the speaker believes the interlocutor is overestimating their own power, authority, or superiority.

   **Usage:** rhetoric

   **Example:** Who do you think you are, telling me how to live my life?

   **Example:** Oh, so you're not gonna give us a refund? Who do you think you are?

   **Example:** Who do you think you are? My boss? My dad?
