### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A dish consisting of French fries topped with cheese curds and gravy, eaten primarily in Canada.

   **Usage:** Canada; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Jean made an eight-hour trip across the border into Quebec just to satisfy his craving for poutine.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Chiefly with a qualifying word&#58; any of a number of variations on the basic poutine dish.

   **Usage:** Canada; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** In Italian poutine, gravy is replaced with spaghetti sauce.
