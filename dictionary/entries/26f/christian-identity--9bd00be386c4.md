## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An interpretation of Christianity according to which only Celtic and Germanic peoples, such as the Anglo-Saxon, Nordic nations, or Aryan people and people of kindred blood, are the descendants of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob and are therefore the descendants of the ancient Israelites.
