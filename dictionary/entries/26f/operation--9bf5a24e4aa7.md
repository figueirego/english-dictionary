### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The method by which a device performs its function.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** It is dangerous to look at the beam of a laser while it is in operation.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The method or practice by which actions are done.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The act or process of operating (verb)&#58; agency; the exertion of power, physical, mechanical, or moral.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** operating

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A planned undertaking.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** The police ran an operation to get vagrants off the streets.

   **Example:** The Katrina relief operation was considered botched.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A business or organization.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** We run our operation from a storefront.

   **Example:** They run a multinational produce-supply operation.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A surgical procedure.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** She had an operation to remove her appendix.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A procedure for generating a value from one or more other values (the operands).

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** The number of operands associated with an operation is called its arity; an operation of arity 2 is called a binary operation.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; human-sciences; logic; mathematics; natural-sciences; philosophy; physical-sciences; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** function; transformation

   **Usage:** countable
