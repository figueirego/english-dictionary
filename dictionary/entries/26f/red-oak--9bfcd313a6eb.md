## Noun

### Forms

- red oaks — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An oak tree with wood of a red tint, with leaves that have pointed lobes, Quercus rubra, native to eastern North America.

   **Usage:** countable; usually

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A similar tree of any of the many species in Quercus subg. Quercus, section Lobatae, found mostly in North America.

   **Usage:** countable; usually

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Carnarvonia araliifolia, a rainforest tree from Australia.

   **Usage:** Australia; countable; usually

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Wood from such trees.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Agkistrodon contortrix, a venomous snake species found in North America.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

### Related words
