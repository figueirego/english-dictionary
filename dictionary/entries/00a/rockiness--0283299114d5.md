## Noun

### Forms

- rockinesses — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The state or quality of being rocky.
   1. Being full of rocks.

      **Usage:** uncountable; usually

      **Example:** rockiness of soil

      **Source synonyms:** stoniness

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The state or quality of being rocky.
   1. Turbulence physically.

      **Usage:** uncountable; usually

      **Example:** rockiness of an airline flight through a thunderstorm

      **Source synonyms:** choppiness

      **Source antonyms:** smoothness; stability

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The state or quality of being rocky.
   1. Turbulence emotionally or societally.

      **Usage:** uncountable; usually

      **Example:** rockiness of a relationship or the economy

      **Source synonyms:** drama; dramaticness

      **Source antonyms:** stability

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The state or quality of being rocky.
   1. Uncertainty and changeability of trends or outlooks.

      **Usage:** uncountable; usually

      **Example:** rockiness of one's state of health

      **Source synonyms:** fragility; shakiness; unsteadiness

      **Source antonyms:** stability

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The state or quality of being rocky.
   1. The degree of rock-and-roll sound that a piece or subgenre of music has.

      **Usage:** uncountable; usually

      **Example:** rockiness of some types of jazz
