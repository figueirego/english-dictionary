### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A machine or device that converts other energy forms into mechanical energy, or imparts motion.

   **Example:** The door can close by itself because there's a small motor built into it.

   **Source synonyms:** drive; engine

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A motor car, or automobile, sometimes extended to other powered vehicles, such as goods vehicles.

   **Usage:** now colloquial outside attributive use

   **Example:** Nice motor!

   **Example:** Motor insurance is expensive for youngsters.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A source of power for something; an inspiration; a driving force.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Source synonyms:** engine

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any protein capable of converting chemical energy into mechanical work.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The controller or prime mover of the universe; God.

   **Usage:** archaic; poetic

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The fermenting mass of fruit that is the basis of pruno, or "prison wine".

   **Usage:** slang

   **Source synonyms:** kicker

### Senses

<a id="r3-s1"></a>

**Source sense R3.S1**

1. To make a journey by motor vehicle; to drive.

   **Usage:** dated; transitive

   **Example:** On Saturday we motored down to Plymouth.

   **Source synonyms:** moto

<a id="r3-s2"></a>

**Source sense R3.S2**

2. To rotate a jet engine or turboprop using the engine's starter, without introducing fuel into the engine.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** During startup, the engine should be motored for 20 to 30 seconds to allow the shafts to straighten out, as they may have become bowed under their own weight while the airplane was sitting on the ground.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** aeronautics; aerospace; aviation; business; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r3-s3"></a>

**Source sense R3.S3**

3. To progress at a brisk pace.

   **Usage:** figuratively; informal; intransitive

   **Example:** Sales were slow at first, but now things are really motoring.

<a id="r3-s4"></a>

**Source sense R3.S4**

4. To leave.

   **Usage:** intransitive; slang

   **Example:** I gotta motor.
