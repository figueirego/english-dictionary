### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A carved screen that separated the chancel and nave in a medieval church; it originally carried a large crucifix; in continental Europe it often featured a balcony from which the Gospel and sermons could be read.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity; architecture
