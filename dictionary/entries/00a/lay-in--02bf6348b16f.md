### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To put (something) aside for future use.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to lay in a stock / store / supply of something

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To add (something) to a painting, especially directly onto the blank canvas in the early stages of the work.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** She uses a round brush to lay in the background.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** art; arts
