### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To grow roots into soil.

   **Usage:** intransitive; literally

   **Example:** Those tulip bulbs have taken root.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To become established, to take hold.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

   **Example:** The new regulations have yet to take root.
