### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A neuromuscular condition where a ring of muscles is unable to relax fully, causing obstruction of a lumen, as&#58;
   1. Such obstruction in the esophagus&#58; esophageal achalasia.

      **Usage:** countable; especially; uncountable; usually

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A neuromuscular condition where a ring of muscles is unable to relax fully, causing obstruction of a lumen, as&#58;
   1. Such obstruction in the esophagus&#58; esophageal achalasia.
      1. Such esophageal achalasia of primary (idiopathic) nature&#58; primary esophageal achalasia.

         **Usage:** countable; especially; uncountable; usually; strictly

         **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A neuromuscular condition where a ring of muscles is unable to relax fully, causing obstruction of a lumen, as&#58;
   1. Such obstruction due to impairment of other gastrointestinal tract smooth muscles.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable; occasionally

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences
