### Forms

- grumbling — participle; present
- grumbled — participle; past

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To make a low, growling or rumbling noise, like a hungry stomach or certain animals.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The distant thunder grumbles.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To complain; to murmur or mutter with discontent; to make ill-natured complaints in a low voice and a surly manner.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** He grumbles about the food constantly, but has yet to learn to cook.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To utter in a grumbling fashion.

   **Usage:** transitive
