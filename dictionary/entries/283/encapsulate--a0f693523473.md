### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To enclose something in, or as if in, a capsule.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To epitomize something by expressing it as a brief summary.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To enclose objects in a common interface in a way that makes them interchangeable, and guards their states from invalid changes.

   **Usage:** object-oriented programming

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences; software

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**
