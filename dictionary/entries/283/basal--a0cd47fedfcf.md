## Adjective · Record 1

- IPA: \[ˈbeɪzəɫ\] — source labels: US; also

### Forms

- most basal — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Basic, elementary; relating to, or forming, the base, or point of origin.

   **Example:** This is the basal idea.

   **Example:** The organism needs more energy on basal maintenance.

   **Example:** A basal reader is a kind of book that is used to teach reading.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Associated with the base of an organism or structure.

   **Example:** In the spring, basal leaves emerge from a stout taproot.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of a minimal level that is necessary for maintaining the health or life of an organism.

   **Example:** a basal diet

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. In a phylogenetic tree, being a group, or member of a group, which diverged earlier. The earliest clade to branch in a larger clade.

   **Usage:** chiefly systematics

   **Example:** Hittite is a basal Indo-European language.

   **Example:** A magnolia is a basal angiosperm.
