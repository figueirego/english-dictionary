### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Not even or balanced; not the same on one side as on the other.

   **Example:** Carrying a heavy suitcase, he walked with a lopsided gait.

   **Source synonyms:** one-sided

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. biased; not balanced between points of view
