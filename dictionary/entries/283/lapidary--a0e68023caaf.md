### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who cuts and polishes, engraves, or deals in gems and precious stones.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** gemsmith; lapidarist; lapidist

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The field in which such a person works, a subfield of gemology.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An expert in gems and precious stones; a connoisseur of lapidary work.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** gemmologist; gemologist; lapidist

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Gems and precious stones collectively; jewellery.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A treatise on (precious) stones.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable; except historical

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Of or pertaining to gems and precious stones, or the art of cutting or otherwise working them.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Of or pertaining to monumental stones, and particularly to the art of inscribing them.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. engraved on stone.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source context (verbatim):** relating to an inscription

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. Senses relating to speech or writing.
   1. Pertaining to a way of speaking which is circumlocutional, is excessively precise, and is multifaceted in the construction of propositions.

      **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. Senses relating to speech or writing.
   1. Of a piece of writing or a writing style&#58; characteristic of or suitable for an inscription; embodying the precision and refinement of inscriptions on monuments; concise and stately.

      **Usage:** not-comparable

      **Source synonyms:** brief; concise; crisp; laconic; lapidary; short-winded; succinct; tight; to the point; taut; terse
