### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A flight manoeuvre that uses opposing rudder and aileron inputs to move the aircraft sideways without turning it.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** aeronautics; aerospace; aviation; business; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

2. A flight manoeuvre that uses opposing rudder and aileron inputs to move the aircraft sideways without turning it.
   1. A flight maneuver to rapidly lose speed and/or altitude whilst not altering the aircraft's ground track.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** aeronautics; aerospace; aviation; business; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences
