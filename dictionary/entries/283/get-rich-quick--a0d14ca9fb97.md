### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of a scheme, claiming to provide large profits quickly, with no realistic chance of success, in order to lure gullible investment victims.

   **Usage:** derogatory; idiomatic; not-comparable; often

   **Example:** Five companies offering get-rich-quick schemes to property investors have been shut down.
