## Verb · Record 1

### Forms

- crashing out — participle; present

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To fall asleep from exhaustion.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** He crashed out after a long day of work.

   **Source synonyms:** crash

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To terminate with an unhandled error; to crash.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** Harry didn't know why but the computer crashed out all of a sudden.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To be eliminated from a competition.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** He crashed out at the second round of the tournament.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To produce or create rapidly; to bang out.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To rapidly precipitate.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To become uncontrollably angry or upset; to rage, snap.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Example:** Losing six games in a row was enough to make my friend start crashing out.

   **Example:** If I had a boss like that, I'd crash out too.

   **Source synonyms:** lose one's cool; fly off the handle; go apeshit; throw a fit; erupt; snap

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To break out of a prison.

   **Usage:** US; slang

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To act out recklessly and violently, often in a way that leads to self-destruction or serious consequences, typically in enraged reaction to something.

   **Usage:** Internet; African-American Vernacular
