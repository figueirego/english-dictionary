### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To become too big in size or too mature in age or outlook to continue to want, need, use, experience, or accept some object, practice, condition, belief, etc.

   **Usage:** figuratively; often; transitive

   **Example:** Poorer children often have to wear whatever an older sibling has outgrown.

   **Example:** I used to have allergies but I outgrew all of them.

   **Example:** Some people blame God for their troubles, but one must outgrow such notions.

   **Source synonyms:** grow out of

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To grow faster or larger than.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The best adapted plant varieties tend to outgrow those less adapted.
