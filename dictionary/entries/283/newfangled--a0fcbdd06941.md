### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. New and often needlessly novel or gratuitously different; recently devised or fashionable, especially when not an improvement.

   **Usage:** derogatory; disapproving; humorous; not-comparable; usually

   **Example:** newfangled electronic gadgets that cost a lot and do little

   **Source antonyms:** oldfangled; old-fashioned

<a id="r1-s2"></a>
