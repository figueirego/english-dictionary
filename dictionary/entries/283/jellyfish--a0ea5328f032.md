### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An almost transparent aquatic animal; any one of the acalephs, especially one of the larger species, having a jellylike appearance.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** jelly; sea jelly

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An almost transparent aquatic animal; any one of the acalephs, especially one of the larger species, having a jellylike appearance.
   1. Any of various species of cnidarians in the subphylum Medusozoa, including box jellyfish (class Cubozoa), true jellyfish (class Scyphozoa), stalked jellyfish (class Staurozoa), and certain hydrozoans.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source synonyms:** jelly; sea jelly

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An almost transparent aquatic animal; any one of the acalephs, especially one of the larger species, having a jellylike appearance.
   1. Any of various species of cnidarians in the subphylum Medusozoa, including box jellyfish (class Cubozoa), true jellyfish (class Scyphozoa), stalked jellyfish (class Staurozoa), and certain hydrozoans.
      1. The medusa phase of these animals rather than a younger life stage.

         **Usage:** countable; especially; uncountable

         **Source synonyms:** jelly; sea jelly

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An almost transparent aquatic animal; any one of the acalephs, especially one of the larger species, having a jellylike appearance.
   1. A ctenophore, a member of the phylum Ctenophora (comb jellies).

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source synonyms:** jelly; sea jelly

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

5. A sudoku technique involving possible cell locations for a digit, or pair, or triple, in uniquely four rows and four columns only. This allows for the elimination of candidates around the grid.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
