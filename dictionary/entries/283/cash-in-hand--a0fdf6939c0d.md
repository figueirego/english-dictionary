## Adjective · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Receiving wages or salary directly in the form of cash, often without deducting tax.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Many high school students get cash in hand jobs for extra pocket money.

## Adverb · Record 2

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. In a manner receiving direct payment by cash, often without paying tax.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** I heard he's working at that restaurant cash in hand to pay the bills.
