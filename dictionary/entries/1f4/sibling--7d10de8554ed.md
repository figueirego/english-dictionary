### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who shares a parent; one's brother or sister who one shares a parent with.

   **Example:** None of my siblings are married yet.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A node in a data structure that shares its parent with another node.
