### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A vector quantity that denotes the rate of change of position with respect to time, combining speed with a directional component.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** A car racing in a circle may retain the same speed while continually changing its velocity.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A rapidity of motion.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The train was travelling at a slower velocity than usual.

   **Source synonyms:** speed; velocity

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The rate of occurrence.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The number of times that an average unit of currency is spent during a specific period of time.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** economics; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The value corresponding to how hard a key is struck on a MIDI controller or keyboard.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; music hardware and software
