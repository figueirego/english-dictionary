## Noun

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A day during which the public schools are open, usually synonymous with weekday during the school year.

   **Example:** In modern China, Sunday is not usually a schoolday except around major holidays.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The part of a day when students attend school.

   **Example:** I couldn't contact my child during the schoolday because of the school's no-cellphone policy.

   **Example:** The schoolday starts at 9&#58;00 am and ends at 3&#58;00 pm.
