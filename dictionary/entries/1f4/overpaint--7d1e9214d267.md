### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To color or describe too strongly.

   **Example:** OK, you've painted me a picture well enough; you don't need to overpaint it.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To apply the final layers of paint over underpainting.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To paint over (something).

   **Example:** This antique chair's inlays have been overpainted, but we can restore its finish.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To paint too much&#58; with too thick a coating of paint.

   **Example:** This antique chair has been overpainted to the point that you can't discern the carved details.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To erase by drawing graphics over the top.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computer-graphics; computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences
