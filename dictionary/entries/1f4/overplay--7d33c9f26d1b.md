### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To overdo or overact one's effect or role.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** When Chris overacted his part again, the director warned that anyone overplaying would be barred from the next production.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** acting; broadcasting; entertainment; film; lifestyle; media; television; theater

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To play (a song or record) too frequently, often to the point of causing weariness and annoyance.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To overestimate one's strength in a game or event, which ultimately may end in a defeat.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive; figuratively

   **Example:** Jack won the last match of blackjack; Theo overplayed.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To accidentally hit (one's golf ball) beyond "the green".

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The first few shots went wonderfully, but Robin overplayed the last and lost.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** golf; hobbies; lifestyle; sports
