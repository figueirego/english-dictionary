## Proper noun · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The oldest person whose age is recorded in the Hebrew Bible, having reportedly lived 969 years.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A planet in the PSR B1620-26 star system, Messier 4 globular cluster, Milky Way Galaxy, Scorpius constellation; a circumbinary planet, officially named PSR B1620-26 b, orbiting a pulsar (“PSR B1620-26 A”) and white dwarf (“WD B1620-26 B”).

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astronomy; natural-sciences

   **Source synonyms:** Planet Methuselah; Methuselah Planet; Genesis; Planet Genesis; Genesis Planet
