### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To stay or remain in a place or situation, especially as if unwilling to depart or not easily able to do so.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source synonyms:** abide; loiter; tarry; abide (archaic); eld (archaic; poetic); linger; retard (obsolete); stay; tarry; wait

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To remain alive or existent although still proceeding toward death or extinction; to die gradually.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To consider or contemplate for a period of time; to engage in analytic thinking or discussion.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive; often

   **Source context (verbatim):** followed by on
