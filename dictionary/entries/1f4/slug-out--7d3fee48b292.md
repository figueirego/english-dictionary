### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To engage in a match, fight, battle, argument or competition.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** They slugged out two wins last month.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To be fought or competed.

   **Usage:** intransitive; usually; fight; battle; fight; battle

   **Example:** It was a victory slugged out by the French.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a match

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To hit a ball.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Example:** He slugged out a homerun in the second inning.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; baseball; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To write down with a typewriter.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To print (something) using slugs (A piece of type metal imprinted by a linotype machine).

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** media; printing; publishing

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To add a slug (A block of text at the beginning of a scene), write a scene with a slug or write many slugs in a script.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To add directions (such as for sound effects) to an animation script.

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive
