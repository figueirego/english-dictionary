      **Example:** It is vital that I communicate this information to you.

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To impart.
   1. To impart or transmit (an intangible quantity, substance); to give a share of.

      **Usage:** transitive

      **Example:** to communicate motion by means of a crank

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To impart.
   1. To pass on (a disease) to another person, animal etc.

      **Usage:** transitive

      **Example:** The disease was mainly communicated via rats and other vermin.
