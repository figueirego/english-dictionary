## Pronoun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An intentionally vague thing, used in answers to leading questions which the speaker wishes to deflect or evade.

   **Usage:** euphemistic

   **Example:** How much did that cost you? Five grand? Something like that, yeah...

   **Example:** So are you two an item yet? Eh, something like that.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see something, like, that.
