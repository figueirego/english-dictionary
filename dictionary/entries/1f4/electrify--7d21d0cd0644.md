### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To supply electricity to; to charge with electricity.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to electrify a cable

   **Source synonyms:** electricalize; electricize; electrize; energize; galvanize

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To cause electricity to pass through; to affect by electricity; to give an electric shock to.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to electrify a limb, or the body

   **Source synonyms:** electricalize; electricize; electrize; galvanize; shock; zap

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To adapt (a home, farm, village, city, industry, vehicle, railroad) for electric power.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To strongly excite, especially by something delightful or inspiring; to thrill.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Her performance in the play electrified the audience.

   **Source synonyms:** galvanize; vitalize; animate; awaken; electrify (figuratively); enflame (figuratively); enkindle (figuratively); enthrill; exagitate (obsolete); excite; exhilarate; exsuscitate (obsolete); fillip; fire (figuratively); fire the blood; galvanize (figuratively); gin up; ignite (figuratively); inflame (figuratively); intoxicate (figuratively); kindle (figuratively); quicken; rouse; send (slang); set fire to (figuratively); set on fire (figuratively); sharpen (figuratively); stir the blood; suscitate (obsolete); thrill; vitalise; wake; warm the blood; whet (figuratively); wind up; work up; yerk (Scotland; obsolete)

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To make electric.

   **Usage:** intransitive
