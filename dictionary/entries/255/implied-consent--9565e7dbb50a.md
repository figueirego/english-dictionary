## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The assumed agreement that a person would approve a course of action if asked, when that person is not presently able to be asked.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** The man was unconscious, but the paramedics had his implied consent to treat him.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Consent given by virtue of another arrangement.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Traveling by air gives security officials implied consent to search your bags.
