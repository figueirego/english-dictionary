### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Unable to walk properly because of a problem with one's feet or legs.

   **Usage:** especially

   **Example:** With the years, this horse has little by little gone lame.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of an animal

   **Source synonyms:** crippled

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Unable to walk properly because of a problem with one's feet or legs.
   1. That cannot be moved properly.

      **Usage:** especially; especially a foot or leg

      **Source context (verbatim):** of a limb; of an animal

      **Source synonyms:** crippled; gammy

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Unable to walk properly because of a problem with one's feet or legs.
   1. Physically disabled.

      **Usage:** dated; especially; offensive

      **Example:** a lame man

      **Source context (verbatim):** of an animal

      **Source synonyms:** crippled

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Hobbling; limping; inefficient; imperfect.

   **Usage:** broadly; dated

   **Source antonyms:** efficient; perfect

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Unconvincing or unbelievable.

   **Usage:** colloquial

   **Example:** He had a really lame excuse for missing the birthday party.

   **Source synonyms:** weak

   **Source antonyms:** convincing; believable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Uncool, uninteresting, or unfunny.

   **Usage:** colloquial

   **Example:** He kept telling these extremely lame jokes all night.
