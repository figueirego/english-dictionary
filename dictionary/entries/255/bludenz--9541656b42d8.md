### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A town in the state of Vorarlberg, Austria, located on the river Ill at the meeting point of five valleys&#58; Walgau, Montafon, Brandnertal, Klostertal and Großes Walsertal, and is surrounded by the Bregenz Forest Mountains in the north, the Rätikon in the southwest and the Silvretta in the southeast; it is best known for its nearby ski areas and producing Milka chocolate; the town is the administrative centre of its eponymous district.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An administrative district in the state of Vorarlberg, Austria, comprising the state's southern half.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A municipality of Bludenz district in the state of Vorarlberg, Austria, comprising the town of the same name.
