### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The duration for which something is visible on film or television.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The wise old monk had little screen time compared with his buxom young disciple.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The amount of time a person spends using screen-accessed technology (such as a television, a computer, a smartphone, etc.).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The editorial advised parents of small children to limit their kids' screen time.
