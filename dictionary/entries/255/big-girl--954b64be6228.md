## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see big, girl.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An older girl; a girl who is no longer an infant; a female child considered mature enough for a specified purpose.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** You can tie your own shoelaces – you're a big girl now.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A timid, weak or effeminate boy or man.

   **Usage:** derogatory; informal

   **Example:** Stop crying, you big girl!

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An adult woman; a woman behaving as a responsible adult.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; informal

   **Example:** Just put on your big-girl panties and make a choice.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A woman who is overweight.
