### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To become involved in a situation, so as to alter or prevent an action.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The police had to be called to intervene in the fight.

   **Source synonyms:** interfere; step in

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To occur, fall, or come between, points of time, or events.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** An instant intervened between the flash and the report.

   **Source synonyms:** elapse

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To occur or act as an obstacle or delay.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Nothing intervened to prevent the undertaking.

   **Example:** The weather intervened before the contest could get under way.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To say (something) in the middle of a conversation or discussion between other people, or to respond to a situation involving other people.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Source synonyms:** interrupt

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To come between, or to be between, persons or things.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** The Mediterranean intervenes between Europe and Africa.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. In a suit to which one has not been made a party, to put forward a defense of one's interest in the subject matter.

   **Example:** an application for leave (i.e. permission) to intervene

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law
