### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The quality of being incoherent.
   1. The quality of not making logical sense or of not being logically connected.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The quality of being incoherent.
   1. The quality of not holding together physically.

      **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Something incoherent; something that does not make logical sense or is not logically connected.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**
