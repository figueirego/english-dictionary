## Adjective · Record 1

### Forms

- more intermittent — comparative
- most intermittent — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Stopping and starting, occurring, or presenting at intervals; coming after a particular time span.

   **Example:** The day was cloudy with intermittent rain.

   **Example:** Intermittent bugs are most difficult to reproduce.

   **Source synonyms:** periodic; periodical; patchy; spasmodic; broken; fitful; [ragged](../197/ragged--65e50ce38fad.md); catchy; discontinuous; intermissive; intermittent; patchy; spasmodic; stop-start

   **Source antonyms:** steady; constant; continual

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Existing only for certain seasons; that is, being dry for part of the year.

   **Usage:** specifically

   **Example:** The area has many intermittent lakes and streams.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; geology; natural-sciences

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a body of water
