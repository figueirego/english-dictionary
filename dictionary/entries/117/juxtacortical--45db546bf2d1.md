### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Next to a cortex; thus&#58;
   1. Abutting the cortical layer of bone, as with juxtacortical chondroma.

      **Usage:** not-comparable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; medicine; sciences
