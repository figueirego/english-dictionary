## Noun · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The duties or taxes imposed on imported or exported goods.

   **Usage:** countable; in-plural; uncountable

   **Example:** Customs of £200 were due on all the wine we took back from France.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The government department or agency that is authorised to collect the taxes imposed on imported goods.

   **Usage:** singular; uncountable

   **Example:** Customs stopped us in the airport for an inspection.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The process of going through customs checks at a border checkpoint.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** They usually don't care about customs, but she was clearly planning on selling most of it.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The location at a border checkpoint where customs checks are carried out.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
