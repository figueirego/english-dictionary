## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A method of finding an approximate value for a definite integral, based on dividing the area under the graph of the function into a series of strips, each of which is approximately a trapezoid, and summing the area of all the trapezoids to approximate the area.

   **Usage:** US

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences
