## Prep\_phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. On a level with the eye of the observer, as of a picture hung in on a wall.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. At risk, as in a contest or enterprise.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** The survival of the company is on the line with this project.

   **Source synonyms:** at stake; on the rocks

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Currently calling on the telephone.

   **Example:** I have John on the line for you.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. On one of the lines marked on a playing field or court; (by extension) within bounds.

   **Example:** The ball was on the line but the umpire ruled it out.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports
