### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In spite of what preceded; yet.

   **Usage:** conjunctive; not-comparable

   **Example:** He saw a monstrosity of a dog trodding on the sidewalk, unleashed, with a haughty man besides it; nevertheless, he continued his treacherous plight.
