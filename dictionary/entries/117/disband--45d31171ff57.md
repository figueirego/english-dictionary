**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To break up or (cause to) cease to exist; to disperse.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** The president wanted to disband the scandal-plagued agency.

   **Example:** I used to be in a punk band, but we disbanded in the early 1980s.
