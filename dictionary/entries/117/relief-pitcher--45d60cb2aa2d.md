### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A pitcher who enters the game after the starting pitcher or another relief pitcher is removed due to injury, ineffectiveness, ejection from the game or fatigue.

   **Example:** The team's relief pitchers have not allowed a run in the past six games.
