### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A birth (or vegetal reproduction) after spontaneous, unfertilized gamete-development in a virginal mother, parthenogenesis.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. (often capitalized and used absolutely&#58; the Virgin Birth) The miraculous birth of Christ from the Virgin Mary.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity; lifestyle; religion; theology

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. (often capitalized and used absolutely&#58; the Virgin Birth) The miraculous birth of Christ from the Virgin Mary.
   1. Any similar birth of a deity.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity; lifestyle; religion; theology

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Childbirth in which the offspring is said to have been conceived without sexual intercourse.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Many religions and traditions have myths about a virgin birth.

   **Example:** Oh, you never let the cat out? Then I suppose those kittens were a virgin birth.

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Childbirth in which the mother conceived the child through artificial insemination.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
