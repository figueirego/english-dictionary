### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. That which is rolled up; a summation; an aggregation; a total.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A food preparation made by wrapping ingredients in another food, e.g. meat and vegetables wrapped in flatbread.

   **Example:** She ate a chicken rollup and a salad.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** cooking; food; lifestyle

   **Source synonyms:** pinwheel; roll; swirl

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A kind of flat, pectin-based, fruit-flavored snack rolled into a tube.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A self-made cigarette of tobacco wrapped in rolling paper.

   **Example:** I smoke rollups because they are cheaper than buying cigarettes.

   **Source synonyms:** rollie

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A business technique where multiple small companies in the same market are acquired and merged.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A collection of software updates distributed as a single package.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences
