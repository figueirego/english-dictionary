## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The long line of people, in a primitive firefighting technique, who pass buckets of water hand-to-hand from the nearest water source to the site of a fire, in an attempt to douse it.

   **Source synonyms:** human chain

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any process in which something is passed along a chain.

   **Usage:** broadly; figuratively

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A discrete-time analogue delay line that moves a signal along a line of capacitors, one step at each clock cycle.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; electrical-engineering; electricity; electromagnetism; electronics; energy; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics
