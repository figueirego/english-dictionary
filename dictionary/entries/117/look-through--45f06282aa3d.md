### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see look, through.

   **Example:** He looked through the binoculars at the bird.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To take a view of the contents of; search in, either with the eyes or by hand.

   **Example:** I looked through the magazine, but it didn't seem very interesting.

   **Example:** Airport security looked through her purse and confiscated her nail scissors.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To penetrate with the understanding; to see through.

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To appear or pretend not to see something or someone who is clearly visible.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; often

   **Example:** I tried to get Michele's attention at the party, but she looked right through me!

   **Source context (verbatim):** with "right"
