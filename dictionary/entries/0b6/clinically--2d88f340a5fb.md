### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In a clinical manner&#58; dispassionately or analytically.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In the setting of a medical clinic; in a clinical setting; (of a health condition) to a degree high enough to be relevant to clinical practice.

   **Example:** clinically apparent

   **Example:** Hypokalemia may manifest clinically as fatigue and muscle cramping.

   **Example:** Mild electrolyte disturbances are usually not clinically apparent, but if they worsen, symptoms begin.

   **Source antonyms:** nonclinically

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. At a person's bedside.

   **Example:** a person baptised clinically
