### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The property of two or more appearances of being of the selfsame underlying object; sameness as regards self or identity; identity; identicality.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** There is no doubt about the selfsameness of the morning Sun with the evening Sun.
