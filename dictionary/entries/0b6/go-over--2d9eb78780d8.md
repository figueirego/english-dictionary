### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see go, over.

   **Example:** I'm going over to my friend's house because I've already gone over my weekly quota.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To look at carefully; to scrutinize; to analyze.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** Please go over the reports to make sure we haven't missed anything.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To create a response or impression.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** Playing a radio in the office did not go over well with his coworkers.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To spray paint graffiti over someone else's graffiti.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** arts; graffiti; visual-arts

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To encompass or cover (a subject).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** In first period, we're going over the basics of cell biology.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To score a try.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; games; hobbies; lifestyle; rugby; sports

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To pat down (someone) and then rob them.

   **Usage:** UK; dated; slang; transitive

   **Example:** That's who went over the butcher.

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To turn out successful; to be well-received.

   **Usage:** dated; intransitive; slang

   **Example:** That song really went over with the audience.

   **Usage:** dated; intransitive
