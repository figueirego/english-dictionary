### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of the poisonous plants belonging to the genus Solanum, especially black nightshade or woody nightshade.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any plant of the wider Solanaceae family, including the nightshades as well as tomato, potato, eggplant, and deadly nightshade.

   **Usage:** colloquial

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Belladonna or deadly nightshade (Atropa belladonna).

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any of several plants likened to nightshade, usually because of similar dark-colored berries.
