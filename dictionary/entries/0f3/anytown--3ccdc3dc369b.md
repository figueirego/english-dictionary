## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any non-specific, non-notable town in the USA.

   **Usage:** US

   **Example:** Then we rolled into Anytown, Arkansas. A real jerkwater.

   **Source synonyms:** dullsville

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A substitute placename for an inhabited place, a city or town, with an unknown or unspecified name.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** 123 Fake Street, Anytown, ST 98765, USA

   **Source synonyms:** Whateverville
