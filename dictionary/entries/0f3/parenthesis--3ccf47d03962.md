### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A clause, phrase, or word which is inserted into a grammatically complete passage of text for explanation or as an aside, and usually marked off with brackets, commas, or dashes.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source synonyms:** parentheme

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A clause, phrase, or word which is inserted into a grammatically complete passage of text for explanation or as an aside, and usually marked off with brackets, commas, or dashes.
   1. An aside serving as an explanation; also, an afterthought.

      **Usage:** countable; generally

      **Source synonyms:** parentheme

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A clause, phrase, or word which is inserted into a grammatically complete passage of text for explanation or as an aside, and usually marked off with brackets, commas, or dashes.
   1. The making of asides or explanations in grammatically complete passages of text.

      **Usage:** rhetoric; uncountable

      **Source synonyms:** parentheme

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Either of a pair of brackets, chiefly the round brackets "(" and ")" but occasionally also the square brackets "\[" and "\]", which are used together to enclose parenthetical material in a text.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** communications; journalism; literature; media; orthography; publishing; writing

   **Source synonyms:** paren; parenthesis-point; bracket

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Either of a pair of brackets, chiefly the round brackets "(" and ")" but occasionally also the square brackets "\[" and "\]", which are used together to enclose parenthetical material in a text.
   1. Either of a pair of curved lines or shapes resembling a round bracket.

      **Usage:** broadly; countable; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** communications; journalism; literature; media; orthography; publishing; writing

      **Source synonyms:** paren; parenthesis-point; bracket

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Either of a pair of brackets, chiefly the round brackets "(" and ")" but occasionally also the square brackets "\[" and "\]", which are used together to enclose parenthetical material in a text.
   1. Either of a pair of brackets used to clarify an expression by grouping terms affected by a common operator, or to enclose the components of a vector or the elements of a matrix.

      **Usage:** broadly; countable; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** communications; human-sciences; journalism; literature; logic; mathematics; media; orthography; philosophy; publishing; sciences; writing
