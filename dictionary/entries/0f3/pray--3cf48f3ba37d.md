### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To direct words, thoughts, or one's attention to a deity or any higher being, for the sake of adoration, thanks, petition for help, etc.

   **Example:** Muslims pray in the direction of Mecca.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** lifestyle; religion

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To humbly beg a person for aid or their time.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To ask earnestly for; to seek to obtain by supplication; to entreat for.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To wish or hope strongly for a particular outcome.

   **Example:** She is praying that the Red Sox will win tonight.
