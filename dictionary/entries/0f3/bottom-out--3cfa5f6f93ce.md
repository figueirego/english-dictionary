### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To reach the bottom; to reach the nadir or lowest point.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of a vehicle, to touch or drag along the ground or other surface.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** Going over that speed bump, my car bottomed out.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** automotive; transport; vehicles

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of a vessel, to touch the bottom of a water body.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Of a suspension or similar device, to strike the lower mechanical stop and lose the ability to buffer further downward motion.

   **Example:** The hard landing caused the oleos to bottom out and the gear struts collapsed.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; mechanical; mechanical-engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To fully anally penetrate.

   **Usage:** intransitive; slang

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** LGBT

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To be, at a basic level.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** The problem bottoms out into a management issue.
