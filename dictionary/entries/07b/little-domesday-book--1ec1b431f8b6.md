### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One of the two volumes comprising the Domesday Book, being a record of the Domesday survey returns from Essex, Norfolk and Suffolk; in a similar format to the detailed survey summaries used to compile the Great Domesday Book, such as the Exon Domesday, but no corresponding entries were ever made in Great Domesday.
