### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A fuller discussion (usually in a separate section at the end of a chapter or book) of a particular part of the text of a work, especially a bible commentary or classical Greek or Latin text.

   **Source synonyms:** excurse

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An act of departing from the main narrative in speech or writing to discuss an incidental issue; a digression.

   **Source synonyms:** excurse

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Chiefly followed by into or to.
   1. A brief journey or trip, especially one out of the usual way; an excursion.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Chiefly followed by into or to.
