### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. So named; commonly called.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** the so-called Ising mode

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Commonly called by such a name, but false or unfitting.

   **Usage:** ironic; not-comparable

   **Example:** These so-called "assistants" are making our jobs harder, not easier!
