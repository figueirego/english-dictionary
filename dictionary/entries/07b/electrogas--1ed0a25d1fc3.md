### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A continuous vertical position arc welding process in which an arc is struck between a consumable electrode and the workpiece, sometimes with the use of a shielding gas, but without application of pressure.

   **Usage:** attributive; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** EGW (initialism)
