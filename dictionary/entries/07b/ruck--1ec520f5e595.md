### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In Australian rules football
   1. A contest in games in which the ball is thrown or bounced in the air and two players from opposing teams attempt to give their team an advantage, typically by tapping the ball to a teammate.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. In Australian rules football
   1. A player who competes in said contests; a ruckman or ruckwoman.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. In Australian rules football
   1. Either of a ruckman or a ruck rover, but not a rover.

      **Usage:** archaic

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. In Australian rules football
   1. Any one of a ruckman, a ruck rover or a rover; a follower.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The situation formed when a player carrying the ball is brought to the ground and one or more members of each side are engaged above the ball, trying to win possession of it; a loose scrum.

   **Usage:** rugby union

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The common mass of people or things; the ordinary ranks.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. An argument or fight.

   **Usage:** colloquial
