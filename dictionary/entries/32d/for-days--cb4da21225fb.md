### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see for, days.

   **Example:** for days on end

   **Example:** The argument went on for days&#58; they just couldn't agree.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. For a large amount or distance; a lot.

   **Usage:** Canada; US; informal

   **Example:** Her legs went on for days.

**Source sense R1.S3**

   **Usage:** Canada; US; informal
