### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Something that is slightly different from a type or norm.

   **Example:** All breeds of dog are variants of the species “Canis lupus familiaris”.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. A different sequence of a gene (locus).

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; genetics; medicine; natural-sciences; sciences

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. A variable that can hold any of various unrelated data types.

**Source sense R2.S4**
