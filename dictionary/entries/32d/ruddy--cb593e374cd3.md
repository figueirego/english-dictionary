## Adjective · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈɹʌdi/ — source labels: UK; US

### Forms

- ruddier — comparative
- ruddiest — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Reddish in color, especially of the face, fire, or sky.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Robust and vigorous, like a person with a red complexion (as compared to a pale one).

   **Example:** in ruddy health

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A mild intensifier, expressing irritation.

   **Usage:** Australia; UK; not-comparable; slang

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** rosy — reddish in color; bally (emphatic; intensifier); bleeding (emphatic; intensifier); blimming (emphatic; intensifier); bloody (emphatic; intensifier); blooming (emphatic; intensifier)
