**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To avoid, especially a duty, responsibility, etc.; to stay away from.

   **Usage:** transitive

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To evade an obligation; to avoid the performance of duty, as by running away.

   **Usage:** intransitive
