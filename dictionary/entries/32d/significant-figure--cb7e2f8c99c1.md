### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A digit that is meaningful with respect to the precision of a measurement; that is, excluding any leading zeroes, trailing rounding or placeholder zeroes, and non-zero digits not warranted by the accuracy of a derivation or measurement.

   **Example:** The speed was calculated by dividing 2314 metres by 71 seconds and reported as 32.5915 m/s, but since both the distance and time were measured only to the nearest whole number, there were actually only two significant figures in this result, i.e. 32.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** significant digit

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see significant, figure.
