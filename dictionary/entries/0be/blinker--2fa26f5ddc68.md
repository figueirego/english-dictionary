## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈblɪŋkə/ — source labels: Received-Pronunciation
- IPA: /ˈblɪŋkəɹ/ — source labels: General-American

### Forms

- blinkers — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Anything that blinks.

   **Example:** She was a frequent blinker, always on the verge of tears.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The turn signal of an automobile.

   **Usage:** Australia; Northern-US; informal

   **Source synonyms:** directional (Australia; Britain; New-Zealand); directional signal; direction indicator; indicator; trafficator; turn indicator; turn signal (US); winker (informal)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A shield attached to the bridle of a horse or other domesticated animal to prevent it from seeing things behind it and to its side.

   **Usage:** in-plural

   **Source synonyms:** blinder; winker

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Whatever obstructs sight or discernment.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The eyelid.

   **Usage:** rare

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A black eye.

   **Usage:** slang

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. In Conway's Game of Life, an arrangement of three cells in a row that switches between horizontal and vertical orientations in each generation.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** cellular-automata; computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A situation where the light of a dab pen or vape starts blinking, which happens when the user takes an extremely long hit.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Example:** a double blinker (hitting blinkers on two pens simultaneously)

   **Example:** I just hit a blinker.
