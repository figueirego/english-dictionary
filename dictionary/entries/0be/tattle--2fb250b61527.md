   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Often said of children&#58; to report incriminating information about another person, or a person's wrongdoing in an annoying fashion, usually to a person in a position of authority over the accused person; to tell on somebody.

   **Usage:** Canada; US; derogatory; intransitive
