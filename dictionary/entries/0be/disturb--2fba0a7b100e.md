### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. to confuse a quiet, constant state or a calm, continuous flow, in particular&#58; thoughts, actions or liquids.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The noisy ventilation disturbed me during the exam.

   **Example:** The performance was disturbed twice by a ringing mobile phone.

   **Example:** A school of fish disturbed the water.

   **Source synonyms:** perturb; unsettle; agitate; freak; sting; bother; bring down; discompose; unstring; disconcert; enturbulate (nonstandard); disturb; mortify (obsolete); perturb; put out; ruffle; throw off balance; unsettle; upset; flurry; agitate; muddle; roil; trouble

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. to divert, redirect, or alter by disturbing.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** A mudslide disturbed the course of the river.

   **Example:** The trauma disturbed his mind.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. to have a negative emotional impact; to cause emotional distress or confusion.

   **Usage:** intransitive
