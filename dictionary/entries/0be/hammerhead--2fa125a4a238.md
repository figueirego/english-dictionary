### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The portion of a hammer containing the metal striking face (also including the claw or peen if so equipped).

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any organism with head shaped like that of a hammer&#58;
   1. Any shark of the family Sphyrnidae, which only includes the genera Sphyrna and Zygaena, with eyes set on projections from the sides of the head.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences; zoology

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any organism with head shaped like that of a hammer&#58;
   1. A hogsucker, a fresh-water fish of species Hypentelium nigricans, in the sucker family Catostomidae.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences; zoology

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any organism with head shaped like that of a hammer&#58;
   1. A hammer-headed fruit bat, a fruit bat of species Hypsignathus monstrosus, with a large blunt nozzle.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences; zoology

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**
