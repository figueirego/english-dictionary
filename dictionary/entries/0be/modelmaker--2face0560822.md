### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who builds models, especially scale models.
   1. One who does so avocationally as a hobby.

      **Example:** His father was a locally renowned modelmaker whose model cars and airplanes were on display in his barbershop for many years.

      **Source synonyms:** scale modeler; modeler

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One who builds models, especially scale models.
   1. One who does so vocationally in industry.

      **Usage:** dated

      **Example:** His grandfather was a modelmaker for a heavy engineering firm.

      **Source synonyms:** scale modeler
