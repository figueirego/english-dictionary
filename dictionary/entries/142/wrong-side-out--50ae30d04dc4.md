### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of a garment, etc, having its inner or hidden side on the outside and vice versa.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Reversed, changed diametrically, by analogy with a garment that is wrong side out.

   **Usage:** figuratively; not-comparable
