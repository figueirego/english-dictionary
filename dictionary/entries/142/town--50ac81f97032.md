### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A settlement; an area with residential districts, shops and amenities, and its own local government; especially one larger than a village and smaller than a city, historically enclosed by a fence or walls, with total populations ranging from several hundred to more than a hundred thousand (as of the early 21st century)

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** This town is really dangerous because these youngsters have Beretta handguns.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any more urbanized centre than the place of reference.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** I'll be in Yonkers, then I'm driving into town to see the Knicks at the Garden tonight.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A rural settlement in which a market was held at least once a week.

   **Usage:** UK; countable; historical; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The residents (as opposed to gown&#58; the students, faculty, etc.) of a community which is the site of a university.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Used to refer to a town or similar entity under discussion.

   **Usage:** colloquial; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Call me when you get to town.

   **Example:** They put up "lost dog" posters all over town.

   **Example:** My apartment is five minutes from the center of town.

   **Example:** It's quiet in this part of town.

   **Source context (verbatim):** used without an article

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A major city, especially one where the speaker is located.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. An interconnected maze of tunnels in which prairie dogs live.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences; zoology

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A townhouse.

   **Usage:** countable; informal; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; real-estate

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

9. A municipal organization, such as a corporation, defined by the laws of the entity of which it is a part.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law
