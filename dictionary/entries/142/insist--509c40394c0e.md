### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To hold up a claim emphatically.

   **Usage:** with on or upon

   **Example:** The defendant insisted on his innocence.

   **Example:** I insist that my secretary dresses nicely.

   **Source context (verbatim):** with (that + indicative) or intransitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To demand continually that something happen or be done; to reiterate a demand despite requests to abandon it.

   **Usage:** sometimes; with on or upon

   **Example:** The Prime Minister insisted on his Chancellor's resignation.

   **Example:** The Prime Minister insisted that his Chancellor resign.

   **Example:** I insist that my secretary dress nicely.

   **Example:** I know I promised to pay you back tomorrow, but it's not very convenient for me. Can we put it off to Friday? —I'm afraid I have to insist on what we agreed.

   **Source context (verbatim):** with (that + subjunctive) or intransitive

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To stand (on); to rest (upon); to lean (upon).

   **Usage:** obsolete
