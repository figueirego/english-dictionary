### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To evoke an intense interest or attraction in someone.

   **Example:** The flickering TV fascinated the cat.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To make someone hold motionless; to spellbind.

   **Example:** We were fascinated by the potter's skill.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To be irresistibly charming or attractive to.

   **Example:** Her gait fascinates all men.
