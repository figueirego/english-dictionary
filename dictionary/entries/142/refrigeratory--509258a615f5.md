### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Something which refrigerates or cools.
   1. a vessel filled with cold running water, surrounding the worm. The vapour within is thereby condensed.

      **Usage:** dated; distillation

      **Source synonyms:** worm-tub
