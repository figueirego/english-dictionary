### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Not concerned or worried (about).

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Not giving sufficient attention or thought, especially concerning the avoidance of harm or mistakes.

   **Example:** Jessica was so careless that she put her shorts on backwards.

   **Source synonyms:** negligent; reckless

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Free from care; unworried, without anxiety.

   **Usage:** archaic

   **Example:** Jessica was careless about putting her shorts on properly.
