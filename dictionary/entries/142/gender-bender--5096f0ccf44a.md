### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who dresses in the clothes of another sex, or in clothes which make their gender identity ambiguous.

   **Usage:** colloquial; idiomatic

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An event where people are encouraged to dress in the clothes of another sex, even if they would normally not.

   **Usage:** broadly

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A person capable of changing their physical sex.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** fantasy

<a id="r1-s4"></a>
