### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A garland or circlet for the head.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

   **Usage:** archaic

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A set of repetitive prayers, other than the Rosary, typically prayed with a string of beads.

   **Example:** The Chaplet of the Seven Sorrows of Mary consists of seven sets of Hail Marys.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Catholicism; Christianity
