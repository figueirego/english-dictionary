### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A group of people; a crowd, a throng, a troop; in particular (archaic), a group of people accompanying or travelling with someone.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete

   **Source synonyms:** company; gathering

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A group of animals, especially one which is lively or unruly, or made up of wild animals such as wolves; a flock, a herd, a pack.

   **Usage:** archaic; collective; countable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A group or assortment of snails.

   **Usage:** collective; countable

   **Source synonyms:** escargatoire

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A group of disorganized things.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A group of (often violent) criminals or gangsters; such people as a class; (more generally) a disorderly and tumultuous crowd, a mob; hence (archaic, preceded by the), the common people as a group, the rabble.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A fashionable assembly; a large evening party, a soirée.

   **Usage:** countable; dated

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A noisy disturbance; also, a disorderly argument or fight, a brawl; (uncountable) disturbance of the peace, commotion, tumult.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. An illegal assembly of people; specifically, three or more people who have come together intending to do something illegal, and who have taken steps towards this, regarded as more serious than an unlawful assembly but not as serious as a riot; the act of assembling in this manner.

   **Usage:** countable; historical

<a id="r3-s1"></a>

**Source sense R3.S1**

1. The act of completely defeating an army or other enemy force, causing it to retreat in a disorganized manner; (by extension) in politics, sport, etc.&#58; a convincing defeat; a thrashing, a trouncing.
