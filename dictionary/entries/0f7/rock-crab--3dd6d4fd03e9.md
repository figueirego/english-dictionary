## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of several kinds of crabs often found on rocky coasts, especially
   1. Hemigrapsus sexdentatus, a large-eyed marine crab found off the coast of New Zealand, the common rock crab

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of several kinds of crabs often found on rocky coasts, especially
   1. Cancer irroratus, the Atlantic rock crab, a crab with large marginal teeth, found along the Atlantic coast of North America

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of several kinds of crabs often found on rocky coasts, especially
   1. Romaleon antennarium, the Pacific rock crab of parts of the Pacific coast of North America.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any of several kinds of crabs often found on rocky coasts, especially
   1. A red rock crab.
