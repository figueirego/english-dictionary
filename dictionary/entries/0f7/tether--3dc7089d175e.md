### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A rope, cable etc. that holds something in place whilst allowing some movement.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The limit of one's abilities, resources, patience, etc.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** Since his hours have increased, I feel that he is at the end of his tether.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An attachment to a place, time, entity or person.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** Despite moving, he maintained a strong tether to his culture back home.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A strong rope or line that connects a sailor's safety harness to the boat's jackstay.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; sailing; transport
