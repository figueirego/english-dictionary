**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Under the influence of a magical spell; enchanted, bewitched, or supernaturally constrained.

   **Source synonyms:** ensorcelled; forspoken; under a spell; bespelled; bewitched; charmed; cursed; enchanted; ensorcelled; forspoken (obsolete); maleficiate; spellbound; under a spell (idiomatic)

   **Example:** The story left the children spellbound.
