### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A feeling of general bodily discomfort, fatigue or unpleasantness, often at the onset of illness.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** unease; doldrums; ill at ease

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An ambiguous feeling of mental or moral depression.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** melancholy; weltschmerz; angst

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Ill will or hurtful feelings for others or someone.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
