**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An intermediary, agent between two (or more) parties.

   **Example:** act as a middleman

   **Example:** cut out the middleman

   **Example:** financial middleman

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An intermediate dealer between the manufacturer and the retailer or customer.

   **Example:** The farmer sold his produce directly to stores without a middleman.

   **Example:** Online platforms often eliminate the need for a middleman.

**Source sense R1.S3**
