### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who, or that which, injects something.
   1. One who injects (himself or herself with) psychoactive drugs.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One who, or that which, injects something.
   1. Any of various devices that are used to inject something, as&#58;
      1. An apparatus by which a stream of water is forced into a steam boiler.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. One who, or that which, injects something.
   1. Any of various devices that are used to inject something, as&#58;
      1. Any of the one or more devices in a fuel injection system that create the spray of fuel.
