**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of a heavenly body, to be at the highest point, reach its greatest altitude.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astronomy; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To reach the (physical or figurative) summit, highest point, peak etc.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The tower culminates in a point.

   **Source synonyms:** peak

3. To reach a climax; to come to a decisive point, especially an end or conclusion.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

   **Example:** Their messy breakup culminated in a restraining order.

   **Example:** The class will culminate with a rigorous examination.
