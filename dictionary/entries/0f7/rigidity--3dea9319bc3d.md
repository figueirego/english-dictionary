### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The quality or state of being rigid; lack of pliability; the quality of resisting change of physical shape.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** moral rigidity

   **Example:** The metal’s rigidity made it hard to bend.

   **Example:** She disliked the rigidity of the rules.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The amount of resistance with which a body opposes change of form.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** structural rigidity

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Stiffness of appearance or manner; want of ease or elegance.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** speak with excessive rigidity

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. stickiness (of prices/wages etc.). Describing the tendency of prices and money wages to adjust to changes in the economy with a certain delay.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** economics; sciences
