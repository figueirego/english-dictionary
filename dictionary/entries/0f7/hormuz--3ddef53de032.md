### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The strait that connects the Persian Gulf, with the Gulf of Oman, Indian Ocean.

   **Source synonyms:** Hormuz Strait; Strait of Hormuz; Straits of Hormuz

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An island of Iran, in the strait of the same name, five miles off the Iranian coast.

   **Source synonyms:** Hormuz Island; Island of Hormuz

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A district of Qeshm, Hormozgan Province, Iran; on the island of the same name.

   **Source synonyms:** Hormuz District; District of Hormuz

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A city in Iran; capital of the district of the same name.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Synonym of Hormozabad.

   **Source synonyms:** Hormozabad (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Alternative spelling of Hormud.

   **Usage:** alt-of; alternative

   **Alternative form of:** Hormud

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Alternative spelling of Ormus (“former Persian kingdom”).

   **Usage:** alt-of; alternative

   **Alternative form of:** [Ormus](../368/ormus--da208e561cff.md)
