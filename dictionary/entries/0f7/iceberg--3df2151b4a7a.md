### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The seaward end of a glacier.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A huge mass of ocean-floating ice which has broken off a glacier or ice shelf

   **Example:** The Titanic hit an iceberg and sank.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An aloof person.

   **Usage:** US; slang

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An impending disastrous event whose adverse effects are only beginning to show.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Source context (verbatim):** after an adjective

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A topic that is more convoluted and fractal than it may seem.

   **Usage:** Internet; figuratively; slang

   **Example:** Do some digging into that politician's past life and associates, and you'll see that the iceberg runs deep.

   **Example:** There's a massive iceberg of increasingly crazy fanfics out there based around those two characters.

   **Source synonyms:** rabbit hole

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A topic that is more convoluted and fractal than it may seem.
   1. A list, infographic, or other enumeration of such a topic and its subcomponents, often ordered into groups sorted by obscurity and bizarreness.

      **Usage:** Internet; broadly; figuratively; slang

      **Example:** Check out the gaming iceberg on this post! There's some really freaky titles there that I've never heard of.

      **Example:** Today we're gonna talk about the Star Wars iceberg.

      **Source synonyms:** rabbit hole
