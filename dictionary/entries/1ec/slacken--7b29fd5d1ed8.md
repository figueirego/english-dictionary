### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To gradually decrease in intensity or tautness; to become slack; to lag.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The pace slackened.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To make slack, less taut, or less intense.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** slacken the rope

   **Source synonyms:** ease; loosen

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To deprive of cohesion by combining chemically with water; to slake.

   **Example:** to slacken lime
