### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To reach by moving down or reducing.

   **Example:** Wait for the temperature to come down to a reasonable level before touching the lid.

   **Example:** Come down to my place someday and have lunch.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To entirely depend upon a single factor; basically, ultimately or in essence.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** The decision comes down to whether you really want to pay that much for a little extra convenience.

   **Example:** The game is going to come down to the last five seconds.
