### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A mass or collection of baked goods such as biscuits and bread.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The baking of biscuits, bread, and similar items.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The business of manufacturing biscuits.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An individual business that manufactures biscuits and similar items; a biscuit factory.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The process of inserting biscuits (thin ovals of wood or other material) into mating slots in order to provide a gluing surface.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** arts; business; carpentry; construction; crafts; hobbies; lifestyle; manufacturing; woodworking
