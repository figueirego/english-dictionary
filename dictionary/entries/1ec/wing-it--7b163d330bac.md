### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To improvise; to make things up or figure things out as one goes; to perform with little or no preparation.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** I don't know their system, but I think I can wing it.

   **Example:** I didn't study for today's test, so I'll have to wing it.
