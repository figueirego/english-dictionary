### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The glow seen in the sky after sunset.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** afterlight

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The light emitted by an incandescent object while cooling.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The light emitted by a phosphor after excitation.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The mildly euphoric feeling experienced after a pleasurable experience, especially after an orgasm or drug-induced high.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An afterparty.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** There's an afterglow with singing after the evening worship.
