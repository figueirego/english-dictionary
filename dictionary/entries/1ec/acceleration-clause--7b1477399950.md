### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. a provision in a contract that makes all obligations to be performed under the contract due immediately upon a specific occurrence, such as a breach

   **Example:** Joe was mortified to learn that his failure to make a scheduled car payment triggered an acceleration clause requiring that the entire balance due on the car be paid immediately.
