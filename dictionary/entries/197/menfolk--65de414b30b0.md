### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Male people in general.

   **Usage:** plural; plural-only

   **Example:** The old woman was surprised when the soldiers stopped to look at her camouflage quilts, since menfolk are rarely interested in needlework.

   **Usage:** plural; plural-only
