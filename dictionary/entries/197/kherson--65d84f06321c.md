### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A port city, a municipal and regional administrative centre of Kherson raion, Kherson oblast, Ukraine, located on the Dnieper/Dnipro.
   1. A raion of Kherson, Ukraine. Seat&#58; Kherson. It is also illicitly claimed by Russia.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A port city, a municipal and regional administrative centre of Kherson raion, Kherson oblast, Ukraine, located on the Dnieper/Dnipro.
   1. An oblast in southern Ukraine. Seat&#58; Kherson.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A port city, a municipal and regional administrative centre of Kherson raion, Kherson oblast, Ukraine, located on the Dnieper/Dnipro.
   1. An urban hromada of Kherson Raion, Kherson Oblast, Ukraine, established in June 2020.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A port city, a municipal and regional administrative centre of Kherson raion, Kherson oblast, Ukraine, located on the Dnieper/Dnipro.
   1. A former governorate of the Russian Empire, the Ukrainian People's Republic and Ukrainian State, established in 1803, abolished in 1920.
