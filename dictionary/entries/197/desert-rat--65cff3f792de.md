### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One of various mouselike rodents species inhabiting deserts&#58;
   1. A gerbil (subfamily Gerbillinae) of the deserts of Africa and Asia

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One of various mouselike rodents species inhabiting deserts&#58;
   1. A kangaroo rat (genus Dipodomys) from North America

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. One of various mouselike rodents species inhabiting deserts&#58;
   1. A Natal multimammate mouse (Mastomys natalensis) from Africa

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. One of various mouselike rodents species inhabiting deserts&#58;
   1. A jerboa, including those of species Jaculus orientalis and Jaculus jaculus (lesser Egyptian jerboa), which inhabit the desert regions of North Africa.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A British or Australian soldier who served in North Africa with the British 7th Armoured Division in 1941-42.

   **Usage:** historical; slang
