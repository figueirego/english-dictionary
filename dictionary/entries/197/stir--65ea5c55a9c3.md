### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To disturb the relative position of the particles (of a liquid or similar) by passing an object through it.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** She stirred the pudding with a spoon.

   **Example:** He stirred his coffee so the sugar wouldn't stay at the bottom.

   **Source synonyms:** agitate

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To disturb the content of (a container) by passing an object through it.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Would you please stir this pot so that the chocolate doesn't burn?
