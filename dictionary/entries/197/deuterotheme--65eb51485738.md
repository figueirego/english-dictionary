## Noun

### Forms

- deuterothemes — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The second element of a name composed of two traditional name roots.

   **Example:** The Slavic given names Jaromir and Ludomir both contain the deuterotheme -mir ("peace").

   **Example:** The Old English name Beowulf comprises of the prototheme Beo- and the deuterotheme -wulf.
