## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Four bodies of water that metaphorically made up the boundaries of ancient China&#58; Qinghai Lake (to the west), the East China Sea (east), Lake Baikal (north), and the South China Sea (south).

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The four seas bordering the United Kingdom&#58; the English Channel, North Sea, Irish Sea, and Atlantic Ocean.
