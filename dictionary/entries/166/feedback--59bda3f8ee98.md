**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Critical assessment of a process or activity or of their results.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source synonyms:** estimation; assessment; critique; evaluation

**Source sense R1.S3**

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually; amplification
