### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To move into.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Under some circumstances police are allowed to invade a person's privacy.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To enter by force, usually in order to conquer.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Argentinian troops invaded the Falkland Islands in 1982.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To infest or overrun.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The picnic was invaded by ants.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To attack; to infringe; to encroach on; to violate.

   **Example:** The king invaded the rights of the people.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To make an unwelcome or uninvited visit or appearance, usually with an intent to cause trouble or some other unpleasant situation.
