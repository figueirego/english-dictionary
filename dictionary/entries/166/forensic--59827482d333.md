### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Relating to the use of science and technology in the investigation and establishment of facts or evidence in a court of law.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** forensic analysis; forensic examination; forensic evidence; forensic investigation; forensic pathologist

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Relating to, or appropriate for, courts of law; suitable or adapted to legal argumentation.

   **Usage:** dated; not-comparable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Precise, thorough, or highly meticulous, by analogy with a scientific legal investigation.

   **Usage:** informal; not-comparable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Relating to forms of disability associated with criminal risk.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** a forensic learning disability

   **Example:** patients with forensic needs

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Relating to, or used in, debate or argument.

   **Usage:** archaic; not-comparable
