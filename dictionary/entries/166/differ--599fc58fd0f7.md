**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Not to have the same traits or characteristics; to be unalike or distinct.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** These shoes only differ from those ones in having slightly longer laces.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Not to have the same traits or characteristics; to be unalike or distinct.
   1. To be separated in quantity.

      **Usage:** intransitive

      **Example:** The numbers 3 and 21 differ by 18.
