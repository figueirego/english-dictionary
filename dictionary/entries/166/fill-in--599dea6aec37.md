### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To fill; to replace material that is absent or has been removed.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** After you're done laying the pipe, fill in the trench.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To inform somebody, especially to supply someone missing or missed information.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** If you know anything about this, maybe you can fill me in.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To substitute for somebody or something.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** He can't go on vacation very often because there is nobody to fill in for him.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To complete a form or questionnaire with requested information.

   **Usage:** UK; idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** I've got a form that is to be filled in in ink.

   **Source synonyms:** fill out

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To beat up; to physically assault.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; slang

   **Example:** Talk to me like that again and I'll fill you in!
