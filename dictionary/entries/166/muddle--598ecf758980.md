### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To mix together, to mix up; to confuse.

   **Example:** Young children tend to muddle their words.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To mash slightly for use in a cocktail.

   **Example:** He muddled the mint sprigs in the bottom of the glass.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To dabble in mud.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To make turbid or muddy.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To think and act in a confused, aimless way.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To cloud or stupefy; to render stupid with liquor; to intoxicate partially.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To waste or misuse, as one does who is stupid or intoxicated.
