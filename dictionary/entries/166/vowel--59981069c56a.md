### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A sound produced by the vocal cords with relatively little restriction of the oral cavity, forming the prominent sound of a syllable.

   **Example:** Hawaiian has either five or twenty-five vowels, depending on how they are counted.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; phonetics; phonology; sciences

   **Source antonyms:** consonant

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A letter or diacritic representing the sound of a vowel; in English, the vowels are a, e, i, o, u, y (sometimes), and w (rarely).

   **Example:** The word facetiously is spelled with all six vowels in alphabetical order.

   **Example:** The word cwm, borrowed from Welsh, is an example of the letter w serving as a vowel in English.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** communications; journalism; literature; media; orthography; publishing; writing
