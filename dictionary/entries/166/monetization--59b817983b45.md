### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The conversion of something (especially metal) into money.

   **Usage:** British; English; Oxford; US; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The process of making a business activity profit-generating, particularly in computer and Internet-related activities.

   **Usage:** British; English; Oxford; US; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The conversion of an economic system based mainly on barter and payment in kind into one that uses currency for most transactions.
