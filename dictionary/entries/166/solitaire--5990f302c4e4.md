### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who lives alone; a recluse or hermit.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** hermit

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A game for one person, played on a board with pegs or balls, in which the object is, beginning with all the places filled except one, to remove all but one of the pieces by "jumping", as in draughts.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; board games

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of various card games that can be played by one person.

   **Usage:** Canada; US; countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** card-games; games

   **Source synonyms:** patience

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Ellipsis of Rodrigues solitaire (Pezophaps solitaria), an extinct bird related to the dodo.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; countable; ellipsis; uncountable

   **Alternative form of:** [Rodrigues solitaire](../09c/rodrigues-solitaire--27039f113a9b.md)

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Ellipsis of Réunion solitaire (Raphus solitarius, now Threskiornis solitarius), an extinct bird formerly believed to be related to the dodo.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; countable; ellipsis; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** Réunion ibis

   **Alternative form of:** Réunion solitaire

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. One of several American species of bird in the genus Myadestes in the thrush family.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A single gem, usually a diamond, mounted in a piece of jewellery by itself.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** jewelry; lifestyle

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable
