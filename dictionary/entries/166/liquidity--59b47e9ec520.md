### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The state or property of being liquid.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source antonyms:** illiquidity; nonliquidity

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The degree of which something is in high supply and demand, making it easily convertible to cash.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance

   **Source antonyms:** illiquidity; nonliquidity

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An asset's property of being able to be sold without affecting its value; the degree to which it can be easily converted into cash.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** Some stocks are traded so rarely that they lack liquidity.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** economics; sciences

   **Source antonyms:** illiquidity; nonliquidity

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Availability of cash over short term&#58; ability to service short-term debt.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance

   **Source antonyms:** illiquidity; nonliquidity
