### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any plant of the genus Hoya, especially those which are commonly cultivated as houseplants.
   1. Hoya carnosa, native to East Asia.

      **Source synonyms:** honey plant; waxflower; porcelainflower

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

      **Source synonyms:** honey plant; waxflower; porcelainflower
