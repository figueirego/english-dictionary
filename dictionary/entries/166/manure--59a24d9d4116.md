### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Animal excrement, especially that of common domestic farm animals and when used as fertilizer. Generally speaking, from cows, horses, sheep, pigs and chickens.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** dung

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Any fertilizing substance, whether of animal origin or not; fertiliser.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** vegetable manure \[⁠= green manure⁠\]

   **Example:** livestock manures

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

   **Usage:** countable; euphemistic; uncountable
