### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A stochastic process for which the conditional expectation of future values given the sequence of all prior values is bounded above by the current value.

   **Example:** If a gambler repeatedly plays a game with negative expectation, his payoff over time is a supermartingale.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences
