### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A small corner formed by two walls; an alcove.

   **Example:** There was a small broom for sweeping ash kept in the nook between the fireplace bricks and the wall.

   **Source synonyms:** alcove; ancona; recess

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A hidden or secluded spot; a secluded retreat.

   **Example:** The back of the used book shop was one of her favorite nooks; she could read for hours and no one would bother her or pester her to buy.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A recess, cove or hollow.

   **Source synonyms:** niche

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An English unit of land area, originally ¹⁄₄ of a yardland but later 12+¹⁄₂ or 20 acres.

   **Usage:** historical

   **Source synonyms:** fardel

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A corner of a piece of land; an angled piece of land, especially one extending into other land.

   **Usage:** Northern-England; archaic

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**
