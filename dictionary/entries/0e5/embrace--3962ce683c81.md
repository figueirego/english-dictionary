   **Usage:** also; figuratively; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** entwine; surround

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To enfold, to include (ideas, principles, etc.); to encompass.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** Natural philosophy embraces many sciences.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

   **Usage:** obsolete; rare; transitive
