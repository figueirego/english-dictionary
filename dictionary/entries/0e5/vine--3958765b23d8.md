### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any plant whose stem requires support and which climbs by tendrils or twining.

   **Source synonyms:** climber

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any plant the stem of which creeps along the ground.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A stem of such plants, especially when thick and rope-like; a liana.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A climbing plant that produces grapes.

   **Usage:** UK; especially

   **Example:** They picked the grapes off the vine.

   **Source synonyms:** grapevine; winetree

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**
