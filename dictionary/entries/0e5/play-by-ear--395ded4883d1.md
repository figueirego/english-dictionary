### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To play guided by one's musical perception and intuition, rather than from a written score.

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

   **Source context (verbatim):** of music

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To do by guessing, intuition, or trial and error; to react to events as they occur, often with risk of negative outcomes; to improvise.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** If they ask for something we didn't prepare, we will have to play it by ear.
