### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A sequence of bass notes at intervals of one perfect fifth lower than the previous note. In twelve-tone equal temperament the sequence repeats every twelve tones, forming a cycle.

   **Usage:** music theory

   **Example:** The composition modulates upwards through the cycle of fifths, eventually returning to the original key.
