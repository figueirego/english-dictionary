### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A field of study or work that is related to one aspect, but not the whole, of a broader field of study or work.

   **Example:** Social psychology is a subdiscipline of psychology.

   **Example:** Phonology is a subdiscipline of linguistics.
