### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A fungus tissue that resembles parenchyma found in plants, but is actually tangled hyphae.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; mycology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Algal tissue that resembles parenchyma found in plants, but consists of appressed filaments.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences; phycology
