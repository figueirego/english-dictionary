### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of several oaks that grow on dry, sandy barrens.
   1. Quercus laevis, of the southeastern U.S.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of several oaks that grow on dry, sandy barrens.
   1. Quercus incana, of the southern U.S., from Texas through the Piedmont to Virginia
