### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To enter service or become active.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** It will be some time before the new factory comes online, and until then we can't fulfill demand.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To log in to an Internet communication system, such as an instant messenger, online game or forum.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** Come online later; I want to talk to you.
