### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of relaxing or the state of being relaxed; the opposite of stress or tension; the aim of recreation and leisure activities.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A diminution of tone, tension, or firmness; specifically in pathology&#58; a looseness; a diminution of the natural and healthy tone of parts.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** relaxation of the soft palate

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Remission or abatement of rigor.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Remission of attention or application.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** relaxation of efforts

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Unbending; recreation; a state or occupation intended to give mental or bodily relief after effort.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The transition of a nucleus, atom or molecule from a higher energy level to a lower one; the opposite of excitation

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>
