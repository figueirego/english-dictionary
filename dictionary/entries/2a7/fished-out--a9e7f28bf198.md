## Adjective · Record 2

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Having had all or nearly all its fish caught, so that few or none remain.

   **Example:** The lake is completely fished out.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a body of water

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Mostly or entirely caught, so that stocks are highly depleted or exhausted.

   **Example:** Cod and halibut are now fished out.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a type of fish
