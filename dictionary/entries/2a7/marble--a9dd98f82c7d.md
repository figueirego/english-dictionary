   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** marbleize

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To cause meat, usually beef, pork, or lamb, to be interlaced with fat so that its appearance resembles that of marble.

   **Usage:** transitive
