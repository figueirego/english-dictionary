### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To strike (a person), originally with something flat or heavy, but now specifically in such a way as to discolour the skin without breaking it; to contuse.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To damage the skin of (fruit or vegetables), in an analogous way.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of fruit or vegetables, to gain bruises through being handled roughly.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Bananas bruise easily.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To become bruised.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** I bruise easily.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To fight with the fists; to box.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To harm or injure somebody's feelings or self-esteem.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** Her thoughtless remarks bruised my ego.

   **Example:** I was bruised by such wanton criticism.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To impair (gin) by shaking rather than stirring.

   **Usage:** transitive
