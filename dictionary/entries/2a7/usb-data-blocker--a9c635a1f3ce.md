### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A piece of hardware through which a USB cable and a USB hub are connected designed to only transmit electrical energy but not data in order to avert juice jacking.

   **Source synonyms:** data blocker; USB blocker; USB condom
