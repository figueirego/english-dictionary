### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An answer or reply, or something in the nature of an answer or reply.

   **Example:** When pushed for an answer, she gave the stock response.

   **Source synonyms:** antiphon (literary)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The act of responding or replying; reply&#58; as, to speak in response to a question.

   **Example:** A ready response to questions about wrongdoing

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An oracular answer.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A verse, sentence, phrase, or word said or sung by the choir or congregation in sequence or reply to the priest or officiant.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A versicle or anthem said or sung during or after a lection; a respond or responsory.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A reply to an objection in formal disputation.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

**Source sense R1.S8**
