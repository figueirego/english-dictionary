### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having curves in alternate directions; meandering.

   **Example:** We followed every bend of the sinuous river.

   **Source synonyms:** curvy; serpentine; wavy; windy

   **Source antonyms:** flat; straight

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Moving gracefully and in a supple manner.

   **Example:** We were entranced by her sinuous dance.

   **Source synonyms:** fluid

   **Source antonyms:** abrupt; broken

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
