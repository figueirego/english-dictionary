### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A pair of straps or pieces of string tied around the leg below the knee to raise trousers above the level of farmyard dirt.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The same to prevent the ingress of dust and vermin up the trouser leg.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>
