### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Tending to ask questions, or to want to explore or investigate; inquisitive; (with a negative connotation) nosy, prying.

   **Example:** Young children are naturally curious about the world and everything in it.

   **Source synonyms:** enquiring, inquiring; exquisitive (obsolete); investigative; peery (rare)

   **Source antonyms:** incurious; noncurious; uncurious

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Caused by curiosity.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Leading one to ask questions about; somewhat odd, out of the ordinary, or unusual.

   **Example:** The platypus is a curious creature, with fur like a mammal and a beak like a bird.
