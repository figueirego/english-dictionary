## Verb · Etymology 2 · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ɡɑːd/ — source labels: Received-Pronunciation
- IPA: /ɡɑɹd/ — source labels: General-American

### Forms

- guarding — participle; present
- guarded — participle; past
- guarded — past
- gard — alternative; obsolete
- garde — alternative; obsolete
- guarde — alternative; obsolete

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To protect from danger; to secure against surprise, attack, or injury; to keep in safety; to defend.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** All of the mountain passes are guarded by troops.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To keep watch over, in order to prevent escape or restrain from acts of violence, or the like.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Guard the prisoner.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To watch by way of caution or defense; to be cautious; to be in a state or position of defense or safety.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** Careful people guard against mistakes.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To protect the edge of, especially with an ornamental border; hence, to face or ornament with lists, laces, etc.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To fasten by binding; to gird.

   **Usage:** transitive
