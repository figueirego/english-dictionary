### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. The expected time of arrival for participants in a performance event, e.g., a play or concert.

   **Usage:** performing arts

   **Example:** The performance starts at 2pm; call time is 11am for crew, noon for musicians.
