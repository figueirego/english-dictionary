### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An opaque white tape that can be placed against paper and transferred by pressure in order to blank out a mistake; an alternative to correction fluid that does not need time to dry.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
