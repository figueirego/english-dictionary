## Verb

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /səbˈskɹaɪb/ — source labels: US

### Forms

- subscribes — present; singular; third-person
- subscribing — participle; present
- subscribed — participle; past
- subscribed — past

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To write (one’s name) at the bottom of a document; to sign (one's name).

   **Usage:** archaic outside law

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To sign; to mark with one's signature as a token of consent or attestation.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Parties subscribe a covenant or contract; a man subscribes a bond.

   **Example:** Officers subscribe their official acts, and secretaries and clerks subscribe copies or records.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To sign up to have copies of a publication, such as a newspaper or a magazine, delivered for a period of time.

   **Usage:** ergative

   **Example:** Would you like to subscribe or subscribe a friend to our new magazine, Lexicography Illustrated?

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To pay for the provision of a service, such as Internet access, a cell phone plan, or a streaming service.

   **Example:** I subscribed to this streaming service years ago.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To believe or agree with a theory or an idea \[with to\].

   **Example:** I don’t subscribe to that theory.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To pay money to be a member of an organization.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To contribute or promise to contribute money to a common fund.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To promise to give, by writing one's name with the amount.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Each man subscribed ten dollars.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To agree to buy shares in a company.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. To sign away; to yield; to surrender.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. To yield; to admit to being inferior or in the wrong.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s12"></a>

**Source sense R1.S12**

12. To declare over one's signature; to publish.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s13"></a>

**Source sense R1.S13**

13. To indicate interest in the communications made by a person or organization.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Please like this video, and subscribe to my YouTube channel.

<a id="r1-s14"></a>

**Source sense R1.S14**

14. To register for notifications about an event or similar.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** If you subscribe to the MouseClick event, your application can react to the user clicking the mouse.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; programming; sciences
