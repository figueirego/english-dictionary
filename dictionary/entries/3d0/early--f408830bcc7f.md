### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. At a time in advance of the usual or expected event.

   **Example:** at eleven, we went for an early lunch; she began reading at an early age; his mother suffered an early death

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Arriving a time before expected; sooner than on time.

   **Example:** You're early today! I don't usually see you before nine o'clock.

   **Example:** The early guests sipped their punch and avoided each other's eyes.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. After but close to the start of a period of time.

   **Example:** The play "Two Gentlemen of Verona" is one of Shakespeare's early works.

   **Example:** Early results showed their winning 245 out of 300 seats in parliament. The main opponent locked up only 31 seats.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. In the starting hours of the day.

   **Example:** It's too early for this sort of thing. I'm not awake yet.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Having begun to occur; in its early stages.

   **Example:** early cancer

   **Example:** harvesting early peaches

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Of a star or class of stars, hotter than the sun.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astronomy; natural-sciences

   **Source antonyms:** late
