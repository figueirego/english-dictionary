### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The condition of being free.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The army is here, your liberty is assured.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The condition of being free from imprisonment, slavery or forced labour.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The prisoners gained their liberty from an underground tunnel.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The condition of being free to act, believe or express oneself as one chooses.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Everyone has the right to life, liberty and security of person.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Freedom from excessive government control.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A short period when a sailor is allowed ashore.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** We're going on a three-day liberty as soon as we dock.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A breach of social convention.

   **Usage:** countable; often; plural; uncountable

   **Example:** You needn't take such liberties.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A local division of government administration in medieval England.

   **Usage:** countable; historical; uncountable

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. An empty space next to a group of stones of the same color.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; go
