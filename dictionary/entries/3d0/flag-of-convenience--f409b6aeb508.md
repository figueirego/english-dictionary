## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Registration of a ship in a country chosen for its low taxes, permissive regulations, etc.

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The flag flown by a ship so registered (corresponding to the country of registration).

   **Usage:** literally

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Use of a particular location or jurisdiction as one's nominal base of operations in order to take advantage of favorable laws and regulations or to avoid restrictions on one's operations.

   **Usage:** broadly
