### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Often preceded by in the&#58;
   1. The state of not being owned by anyone, and thus freely usable by everyone.

      **Usage:** also; attributive; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Often preceded by in the&#58;
   1. The state of not being owned by anyone, and thus freely usable by everyone.
      1. The realm of creative works to which no exclusive intellectual property rights apply, that is, that are no longer protected by copyright or patents.

         **Usage:** also; attributive; specifically; uncountable

         **Example:** As the copyright in this book has lapsed, it is now in the public domain.

         **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Often preceded by in the&#58;
   1. The state of not or no longer being confidential or secret.

      **Usage:** also; attributive; uncountable

      **Example:** Very little information about the treaty is in the public domain.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Land regarded as owned by the public, and not subject to a land patent or other forms of private ownership (for example, unowned prairie in the southwestern and western United States).

   **Usage:** US; especially; uncountable
