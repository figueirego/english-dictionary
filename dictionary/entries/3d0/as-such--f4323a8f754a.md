## Prep\_phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In the role or manner of the thing mentioned.

   **Example:** This is a valuable artefact and should be treated as such.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In a true or literal sense.

   **Usage:** usually

   **Example:** I don't have a library as such, but I do have quite a large collection of books.

   **Source context (verbatim):** in negative constructions

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Per se, by itself, in itself, in and of itself.

   **Example:** Money as such is not the root of evil.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Being as previously mentioned or described.

   **Example:** I'm a mother of four young children; as such, I'm always rushed off my feet.
