### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Either of two small leather cases containing scrolls with passages from the Torah, traditionally worn by a Jewish man (one on the arm (usually the left) and one on the forehead) and now sometimes by a woman at certain morning prayers as a reminder to obey the law as set out in the Bible; a tefilla.

   **Usage:** Judaism

   **Source synonyms:** phylacter

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Either of two small leather cases containing scrolls with passages from the Torah, traditionally worn by a Jewish man (one on the arm (usually the left) and one on the forehead) and now sometimes by a woman at certain morning prayers as a reminder to obey the law as set out in the Bible; a tefilla.
   1. A profession of faith, or religious or traditional observance; also, a reminder.

      **Usage:** Judaism; derogatory; figuratively

      **Source synonyms:** phylacter

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A fringe which an Israelite was required to wear as a reminder to obey the law as set out in the Bible; (by extension) any fringe or border.

   **Usage:** Judaism; archaic

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Synonym of amulet (“a protective charm or ornament”).

   **Usage:** archaic
