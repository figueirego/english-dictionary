### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A Eurasian sparrowhawk, Accipiter nisus, a small, short-winged European hawk that preys on smaller birds.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A Eurasian sparrowhawk, Accipiter nisus, a small, short-winged European hawk that preys on smaller birds.
   1. A female such bird, a male being a musket.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** falconry; hobbies; hunting; lifestyle

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of numerous other relatively small birds of Old World species of Accipiter, that prey on smaller birds or otherwise resemble A. nisus.
