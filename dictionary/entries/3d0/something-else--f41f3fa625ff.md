## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Another unspecified item, perhaps not yet known or decided upon.

   **Usage:** literally; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Something exceptional, out of the ordinary, unusual.

   **Usage:** informal; uncountable

   **Example:** My mother's cooking is something else!

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A person or thing that is outstandingly unfavorable.

   **Usage:** informal; uncountable

   **Example:** You mean to tell me you let those kids walk all the way home? At night in the rain? Man, you're really something else.
