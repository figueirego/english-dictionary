### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A description of an event or system that is considered to be accurate.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A coherent statement or set of ideas that explains observed facts or phenomena and correctly predicts new facts or phenomena not previously observed, or which sets out the laws and principles of something known or observed; a hypothesis confirmed by observation, experiment etc.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The underlying principles or methods of a given technical skill, art etc., as opposed to its practice.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source antonyms:** praxis

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A field of study attempting to exhaustively describe a particular class of constructs.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Knot theory classifies the mappings of a circle into 3-space.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A set of axioms together with all statements derivable from them; or, a set of statements which are deductively closed. Equivalently, a formal language plus a set of axioms (from which can then be derived theorems). The statements may be required to all be bound (i.e., to have no free variables).

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** A theory is consistent if it has a model.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; logic; mathematics; philosophy; sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The standardization and study of fixed sequences of moves, especially in the opening phase of a game.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; chess and similar games

   **Source synonyms:** joseki

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Mental conception; reflection, consideration.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A hypothesis or conjecture.

   **Usage:** countable; informal; uncountable
