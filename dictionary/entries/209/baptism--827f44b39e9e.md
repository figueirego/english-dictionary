### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A Christian sacrament, by which one is received into a church and sometimes given a name, generally involving the candidate to be anointed with or submerged in water.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The child’s baptism was held at the old church.

   **Example:** The ceremony of baptism symbolizes spiritual rebirth.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity

   **Source synonyms:** christening

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A similar ceremony of initiation, purification or naming.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The moment at which an object is first assigned a proper name.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; philosophy; sciences
