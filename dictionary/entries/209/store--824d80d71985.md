### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A place where items may be accumulated or routinely kept.

   **Example:** This building used to be a store for old tires.

   **Source synonyms:** storeroom; stockroom; warehouse; magazine

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A supply held in storage.

   **Example:** They keep a store of canned goods in their basement.

   **Example:** They could eat from their stores for a month or two if need be.

   **Source synonyms:** stock; supply; cache; stash

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A building (or portion thereof) where items may be purchased.

   **Usage:** North-American; mainly

   **Example:** Dad went to the store to get milk and bread.

   **Source synonyms:** shop; store; shop; retail store; retail outlet

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Memory.

   **Usage:** dated

   **Example:** The main store of 1000 36-bit words seemed large at the time.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A great quantity or number; abundance.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Ellipsis of store cattle beast&#58; a head of store cattle (feeder cattle to be sold to others for finishing).

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; ellipsis

   **Example:** heifers and stores
