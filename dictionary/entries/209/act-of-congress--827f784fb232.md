### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A statute enacted by the United States Congress or Congress of the Philippines.

   **Usage:** Philippine; US; literally

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; politics

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An authorization that is extremely difficult to obtain, especially in a timely fashion.

   **Usage:** US; colloquial; idiomatic

   **Example:** Does it take an act of Congress just to get a stop sign on a corner?
