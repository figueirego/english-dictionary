### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make less heavy; to lighten; to alleviate, to relieve.

   **Usage:** also; figuratively; obsolete; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** alighten (archaic)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Often followed by from or off&#58; to get off an animal which one has been riding; to dismount; to descend or exit from a vehicle; hence, to complete one's journey; to stop.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** He alighted from his horse.

   **Example:** Passengers are alighting from the carriage.

   **Source synonyms:** alighten (archaic); light (archaic); disembark; debark; get off; get out; unlight

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Often followed by at, on, or upon&#58; of something aloft&#58; to descend and settle; to land, to lodge, to rest.

   **Usage:** also; figuratively; intransitive

   **Example:** A flying bird alights upon a tree.

   **Example:** Snow alights on a roof.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To come down or go down; to descend.

   **Usage:** archaic; intransitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Often followed by on or upon&#58; of a blow, something thrown, etc.&#58; to land heavily.

   **Usage:** archaic; intransitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Often followed by on or upon&#58; to find by accident; to chance upon, to come upon.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To arrive.

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete
