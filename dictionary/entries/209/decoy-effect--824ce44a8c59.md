### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The phenomenon whereby consumers will tend to have a specific change in preference between two options when also presented with a third option that is asymmetrically dominated&#58; that is, the third option is inferior in all respects to one option, but, in comparison to the other, it is inferior in some respects and superior in others.
