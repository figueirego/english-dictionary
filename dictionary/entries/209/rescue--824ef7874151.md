### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To save from any violence, danger or evil.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The well-trained team rescued everyone after the avalanche.

   **Source synonyms:** free; deliver; pull out of the fire

   **Source antonyms:** endanger; imperil

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To free or liberate from confinement or other physical restraint.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to rescue a prisoner from the enemy

   **Source synonyms:** liberate; release

   **Source antonyms:** arrest; capture; kidnap

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To recover forcibly, especially from a siege.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** liberate; recapture; retake

   **Source antonyms:** besiege; capture

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To remove or withdraw from a state of exposure to evil and sin.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** Traditionally missionaries aim to rescue many ignorant heathen souls.

   **Source synonyms:** redeem; save

   **Source antonyms:** corrupt; deprave

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To achieve something positive under difficult conditions.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To restore a particular trait in an organism that was lost or altered, especially where this loss was as the consequence of some experimental manipulation.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; genetics; medicine; natural-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To salvage and restore something that has been discarded.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I rescued a set of antique dining chairs.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To fix a mistake made while preparing something, especially in cooking.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The cook rescued the sauce after it began to curdle.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To adopt (an animal).

   **Usage:** transitive
