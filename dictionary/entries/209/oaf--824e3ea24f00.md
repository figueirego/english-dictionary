### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person, especially a large male, who is clumsy or a simpleton.

   **Usage:** derogatory

   **Example:** Ouch! You dropped that box on my feet, you lumbering oaf!

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An ill-mannered person; a boor.

   **Usage:** dated; derogatory

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An elf's child; a changeling left by fairies or goblins, hence, a deformed or foolish child.

   **Usage:** obsolete
