### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A forefather, any of a person's direct ancestors.

   **Source synonyms:** ancestor; forefather

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person from whom one or more people (dynasty, tribe, nation…) are descended.

   **Example:** Abraham, alias Ibrahim, is the presumed progenitor of both the Jewish and Arab peoples.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An ancestral form of a species.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A predecessor of something, especially if also a precursor or model.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** ARPANET was the progenitor of the Internet.

   **Example:** Are neural progenitor cells infected by Zika virus?

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Someone who originates something.

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A founder.
