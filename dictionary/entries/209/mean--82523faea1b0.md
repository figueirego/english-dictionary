### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To intend.
   1. To intend, to plan (to do); to have as one's intention.

      **Usage:** transitive

      **Example:** I didn't mean to knock your tooth out.

      **Example:** I mean to go to Arévalo in Spain this summer; I’ve been meaning to tell you for weeks, but I’ve just found the time.

      **Example:** I meant to take the car in for a smog check, but it slipped my mind.

      **Example:** The authors meant a challenge to the status quo.

      **Example:** Do you mean to say you regret it after all?

      **Source synonyms:** intend

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To intend.
   1. To have as intentions of a given kind.

      **Usage:** intransitive

      **Example:** Don't be angry; she meant well.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To intend.
   1. To intend (something) for a given purpose or fate; to predestine.

      **Usage:** passive; regional; transitive

      **Example:** Actually this desk was meant for the subeditor.

      **Example:** Man was not meant to question such things.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To intend.
   1. To intend an ensuing comment or statement as an explanation.

      **Usage:** transitive

      **Example:** Your reasoning seems needlessly abstruse, complex, and verbose for me. I mean, could you dumb it down for my sake?

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To convey (a meaning).
   1. To convey (a given sense); to signify, or indicate (an object or idea).

      **Usage:** transitive

      **Example:** The sky is red this morning—does that mean we're in for a storm?

      **Source synonyms:** convey; indicate; signify

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To convey (a meaning).
   1. Of a word, symbol etc&#58; to have reference to, to signify.

      **Usage:** transitive

      **Example:** What does this hieroglyph mean?

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To convey (a meaning).
   1. Of a person (or animal etc)&#58; to intend to express, to imply, to hint at, to allude.

      **Usage:** transitive

      **Example:** I’m afraid I don’t understand what you mean.

      **Example:** He’s a little different, if you know what I mean.

      **Source synonyms:** hint; imply; get at

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To have conviction in (something said or expressed); to be sincere in (what one says).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Does she really mean what she said to him last night?

   **Example:** Say what you mean and mean what you say.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To cause or produce (a given result); to bring about (a given result).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** One faltering step means certain death.

   **Example:** This breakthrough will mean that we spend less on electricity bills.

   **Source synonyms:** bring about; cause; lead to; result in

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. To be of some level of importance.

   **Usage:** usually

   **Example:** That little dog meant everything to me.

   **Example:** Formality and titles mean nothing in their circle.

   **Source context (verbatim):** with to

**Source sense R1.S11**
