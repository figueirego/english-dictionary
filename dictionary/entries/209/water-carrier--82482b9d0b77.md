### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who carries water from a spring or well, especially in antiquity and pre-modern era when it was a common job.

   **Usage:** historical; usually

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An arrangement of wires on which a bucket of water, raised from a well, etc., may be conveyed wherever required, as to a house.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A pipe or tube that conveys water.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A transportation ship that is water-based.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

   **Usage:** colloquial; figuratively
