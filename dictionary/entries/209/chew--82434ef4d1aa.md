### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To crush with the teeth by repeated closing and opening of the jaws; done to food to soften it and break it down by the action of saliva before it is swallowed.

   **Example:** Make sure to chew thoroughly, and don't talk with your mouth full!

   **Example:** The steak was tough to chew as it had been cooked too long.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To grind, tear, or otherwise degrade or demolish something with teeth or as with teeth.

   **Example:** He keeps his feed in steel drums to prevent the mice from chewing holes in the feed-sacks.

   **Example:** The harsh desert wind and sand had chewed the stump into ragged strips of wood.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The condition of something being torn or ground up mechanically.

   **Usage:** informal; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To think about something; to ponder; to chew over.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** The professor stood at the blackboard, chalk in hand, and chewed the question the student had asked.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To chew tobacco.

   **Example:** To reduce cancer risk, it is best that you neither chew nor smoke.

   **Example:** He'd been chewing for many years by the time he first noticed some white plaques on his oral mucosa.
