### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To create by chiseling.

   **Usage:** literally; transitive

   **Example:** He chiseled up the details on the sculpture.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To cast off splinters or pieces like might be created by chiseling; to (figuratively) cause many or small weak results.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The sight chiseled up old anxieties.

   **Example:** When the car crashed into the deck, it chiseled up sharp splinters of wood.
