### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To fall from an upright or standing position to a horizontal or prone position.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of an argument, to fail to be valid.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of a computer program or system, to crash.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; informal; intransitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To break down; to become inoperable.

   **Usage:** South-Africa; idiomatic; informal; intransitive

   **Example:** We'll have to walk; my car has fallen over.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To revolt or change allegiance.

   **Usage:** archaic
