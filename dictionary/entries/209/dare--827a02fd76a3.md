### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To have enough courage (to do something).

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** I wouldn't dare (to) argue with my boss.

   **Example:** No one dares (to) criticize his decisions.

   **Example:** They daredn't tell me the truth, even though I dared (to) acknowledge my mistake to them.

   **Example:** I daren't tell him I've had an affair with another man.

   **Source synonyms:** venture

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To defy or challenge (someone to do something).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I dare you to kiss that girl.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To have enough courage to meet or do something, go somewhere, etc.; to face up to.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Will you dare death to reach your goal?

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To terrify; to daunt.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To drive larks to the ground by scaring them (for instance, with mirrors or hawks) so they can be caught in nets.

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive
