### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In a fair manner; fair; not biased or skewed or favouring a certain party.

   **Example:** A jury is expected to ensure that the accused is fairly judged.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** manner

   **Source synonyms:** justly; frankly; cleanly

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Favorably; auspiciously; commodiously.

   **Example:** a town fairly situated for foreign trade

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Honestly; properly.

   **Example:** Consumer laws aim to have consumers fairly treated.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

4. Softly; quietly; gently.
