<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make difficult to accomplish; to act as an obstacle; to frustrate.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** A drought hinders the growth of plants.

   **Source synonyms:** delay; frustrate; hamper; impede; obstruct; prevent; thwart

   **Source antonyms:** further; assist; expedite; facilitate; help

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To delay or impede; to keep back, to prevent.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** She hindered a man from committing suicide.

   **Source synonyms:** bar; block; delay; hamper; impede; obstruct; restrain; stop

   **Source antonyms:** aid; assist; help

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive
