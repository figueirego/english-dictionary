## Noun · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A support or foundation, especially as a structure of masonry that supports a wall.

   **Example:** This house's underpinning is not up to standard.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A basis for something.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** I would like to know more about the underpinning of this political theory.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

   **Usage:** figuratively; humorous
