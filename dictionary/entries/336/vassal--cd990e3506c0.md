### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The grantee of a fief, a subordinate granted use of a superior's land and its income in exchange for vows of fidelity and homage and (typically) military service.

   **Usage:** historical

   **Example:** The manor's vassals owed first fruits and a tithe to the parish church, another 10% to the lord (including at least 50 eels), a week or two each year of service in the manor's upkeep, and service in the local fyrd.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

   **Source synonyms:** feudatory; feudal tenant

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any direct subordinate bound by such vows to a superior.

   **Usage:** historical

   **Example:** The king ordered his vassals to join him on the crusade unless they had a written note signed by the archbishop or pope.

   **Source synonyms:** subject; dependant; bondsman; villein; serf; helot; thrall; servant; slave

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any subordinate bound by similar close ties.

   **Usage:** figuratively
