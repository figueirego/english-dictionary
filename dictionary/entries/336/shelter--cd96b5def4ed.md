### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Somewhere one can find protection.
   1. A place where a person or animal may find protection from the elements (especially rain, storms, or cold) sometimes as a place to sleep (such as a cave).

      **Usage:** countable

      **Example:** The band of explorers found a shelter behind the waterfall, which they rested at for three days.

      **Example:** rock shelter

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Somewhere one can find protection.
   1. A bare-bones above-ground structure meant to provide temporary cover from the elements; usually small and with no door to close.

      **Usage:** countable; usually

      **Example:** bus shelter

      **Example:** waiting shelter

      **Source context (verbatim):** as the head in compounds

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Somewhere one can find protection.
   1. A place for protection from deadly conditions outside, including military assault; usually partly or fully underground.

      **Usage:** countable

      **Example:** tornado shelter

      **Example:** fallout shelter

      **Example:** air-raid shelter

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Somewhere one can find protection.
   1. A refuge, sanctuary, or (by metonymy) institution that provides temporary housing or a place to sleep for those in need or in danger.

      **Usage:** countable

      **Example:** women's shelter

      **Example:** homeless shelter

      **Source synonyms:** home

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Somewhere one can find protection.
   1. A refuge, sanctuary, or (by metonymy) institution that provides temporary housing or a place to sleep for those in need or in danger.
      1. An animal shelter.

         **Usage:** countable; often

         **Example:** I wish more people would look for pets at the shelter before turning to breeders!

         **Example:** kill shelter

         **Source context (verbatim):** with the definite article

         **Source synonyms:** home

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. That which provides protection or cover.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Along with air, water, and food, shelter is often recognized as a human necessity.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The state of being protected or shielded.

   **Usage:** abstract; uncountable
