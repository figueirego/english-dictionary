**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A unique complete translation-invariant measure for the σ-algebra which contains all k-cells in a given Euclidean space, and which assigns a measure to each k-cell which is equal to that k-cell's volume (as defined in Euclidean geometry&#58; i.e., the volume of the k-cell equals the product of the lengths of its sides).
