## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A nontheistic movement that views Judaism as the sum of Jewish cultural and historical practices, and that permits secular Jews to celebrate their Jewish identity by participating in Jewish holidays and rites of passage.

   **Usage:** Judaism

   **Source synonyms:** Secular Humanistic Judaism
