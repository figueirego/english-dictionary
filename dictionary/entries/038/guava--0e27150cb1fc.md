### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A tropical tree or shrub of the myrtle family, Psidium guajava.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Its yellowish tropical fruit, globular or pear-shaped with thin, yellow, green or brown skin, is often made into jams and jellies.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A medium reddish-pink color, like that of guava flesh (also called guava pink).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
