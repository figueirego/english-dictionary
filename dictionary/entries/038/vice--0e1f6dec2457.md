### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Bad or immoral behaviour. (Especially often, a habit that harms oneself or others)

   **Example:** Pride is a vice, not a virtue.

   **Example:** Smoking was a vice Sally picked up in high school.

   **Source synonyms:** profligacy; carrying-on; Corinthianism; knavery; profligacy; rakehood; vice

   **Source antonyms:** virtue

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of various crimes related (depending on jurisdiction) to weapons, prostitution, pornography, gambling, alcohol, tobacco, or drugs.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Clipping of vice squad.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; clipping; slang

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; law-enforcement

   **Alternative form of:** [vice squad](../2b1/vice-squad--ac4be25d180c.md)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A defect in the temper or behaviour of a horse, such as to make the animal dangerous, to injure its health, or to diminish its usefulness.
