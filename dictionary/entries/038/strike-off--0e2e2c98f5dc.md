### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To remove from a list or register.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** She struck off his name from the list.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To remove from a list or register.
   1. To be forbidden from practicing in a regulated profession (medicine, law etc.) by virtue of being removed (usually for malpractice) from a statutory register required to practice that profession.

      **Usage:** UK; transitive; usually; passive voice

      **Example:** The doctor was struck off the medical register for professional misconduct.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To void an obligation; to deduct.

   **Usage:** dated; transitive

   **Example:** He was kind enough to strike off the debt.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To print (a work) in a hurried manner.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I'll strike off a copy for the subeditor later.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To sever or separate by a blow.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Strike off his head, I command you!

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To start going in a new direction or course of endeavor.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** She decided to strike off on a new course.
