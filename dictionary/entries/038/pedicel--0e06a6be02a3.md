### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A stalk of an individual flower (or fruit, e.g., once fertilised) or spore-producing body within a cluster.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences

   **Source synonyms:** footstalk; strig

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A stalk of a fungus fruiting body.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; mycology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A stalk-shaped body part; an anatomical part that resembles a stem or stalk.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A narrow stalk-like body part connecting specific segments in certain insects and some other arthropods.
   1. A petiole; the connection between the thorax and abdomen of an insect of suborder Apocrita.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences; zoology

      **Source synonyms:** petiole

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A narrow stalk-like body part connecting specific segments in certain insects and some other arthropods.
   1. The connection between the cephalothorax and abdomen of a spider.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences; zoology

      **Source synonyms:** petiole

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A narrow stalk-like body part connecting specific segments in certain insects and some other arthropods.
   1. The second segment of the antenna of an insect, between the scape and the flagellum.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences; zoology

      **Source synonyms:** petiole

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The segment of an antler that attaches to the head of a cervid.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences; zoology
