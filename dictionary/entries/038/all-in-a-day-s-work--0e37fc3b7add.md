## Phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Coming with the territory.

   **Example:** Unfortunately, dealing with rude customers is all in a day's work around here.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A nonchalant dismissal of a significant accomplishment.

   **Example:** —Wow, it's amazing that you fixed it so well that no one would even know that it was ever broken! —All in a day's work!
