### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Without restrictions on holds or the actions permitted to opponents.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** a no holds barred match

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; hobbies; lifestyle; martial-arts; military; politics; sports; war; wrestling

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Having absolutely no limits or restrictions as far as the levels and nature of one's hostilities and offense; taking to an especially ruthless or vicious manner.

   **Usage:** figuratively; not-comparable

   **Example:** a no holds barred debating style that quickly transformed into cheap shots and bickering

   **Source synonyms:** unrestrained; unrestricted; unreserved; unconstrained; cutthroat
