### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The 1- to 3-letter international code for a chemical element. It is the same in all languages. Thus, gold always has the chemical symbol "Au", regardless of what people call it in their language.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. a similar symbol used to denote a different chemical entity, such as an isotope (e.g. D = deuterium, though "²H" is more correct), a radical (e.g. Ph \[or Φ\] = phenyl), an unknown (e.g. M = a metal, or X = a halogen), etc.

   **Usage:** broadly
