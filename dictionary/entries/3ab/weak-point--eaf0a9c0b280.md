## Noun

### Forms

- weak points — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Something (such as a skill, talent, characteristic, or property) at which someone, or something, is substandard.

   **Example:** Their soft skills are great, but their technical knowledge is a weak point that needs remedial help.

   **Source synonyms:** underbelly

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Synonym of weakest link.

   **Usage:** figuratively; literal

   **Example:** probing the perimeter for the weak point
