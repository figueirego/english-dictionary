### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To declare (a deceased person) as a saint, and enter them into the canon of saints.

   **Usage:** British; English; Oxford; US; transitive

   **Example:** Thomas Becket was canonized in 1173.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Catholicism; Christianity; Roman-Catholicism

   **Source synonyms:** saint

   **Source antonyms:** uncanonize

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To regard as a saint; to glorify, to exalt to the highest honour.

   **Usage:** British; English; Oxford; US; figuratively; transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To formally declare (a piece of religious writing) to be part of the biblical canon.

   **Usage:** British; English; Oxford; US; transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity

   **Source antonyms:** uncanonize

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To regard (an artistic or written work or its creator) as one of a group that are representative of a particular field.

   **Usage:** British; English; Oxford; US; broadly; transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Especially of a church&#58; to give official approval to; to authorize, to sanction.

   **Usage:** British; English; Oxford; US; transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity

   **Source antonyms:** uncanonize
