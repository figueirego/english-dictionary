### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To pull something sharply; to pull something out

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** She plucked the phone from her bag and dialled.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To take or remove (someone) quickly from a particular place or situation.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To play (a single string on a musical instrument) by pulling and then releasing it, such as on a guitar.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Whereas a piano strikes the string, a harpsichord plucks it.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To remove feathers from (a bird).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** defeather

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To rob, steal from; to cheat or swindle (someone).

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To play a string instrument pizzicato.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Plucking a bow instrument may cause a string to break.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To pull or twitch sharply.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** to pluck at somebody's sleeve

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To reject (a student) after they fail an examination for a degree.

   **Usage:** UK; transitive; university slang
