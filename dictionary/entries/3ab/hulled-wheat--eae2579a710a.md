**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of certain species of wheat that need to be processed after threshing to remove the inedible glumes or hulls from the heads&#58; einkorn wheat (Triticum monococcum), emmer (Triticum dicoccon), spelt (Triticum spelta), or farro (Triticum turgidum).
