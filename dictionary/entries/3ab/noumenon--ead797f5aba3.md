**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A thing as it is independent of any conceptualization or perception by the human mind, postulated by practical reason but existing in a condition which is in principle unknowable and unexperienceable.

   **Source context (verbatim):** from Kantian philosophy on

   **Source synonyms:** thing-in-itself
