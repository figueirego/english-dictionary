<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The previous experiences and life of a person, specifically (narratology, especially in film, television) a character in a dramatic work.

   **Example:** What’s the backstory of our new colleague?

   **Source synonyms:** background story

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A fictitious account of a person's history designed to conceal their actual history and motives.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** espionage; government; law-enforcement; military; politics; war
