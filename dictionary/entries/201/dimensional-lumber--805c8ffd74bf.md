### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Lumber that is finished (milled or planed) and cut to standardized width and depth; in some countries the nominal sizes differ from the actual sizes in well-established ways (for example, in the United States, a two by four is nominally 2 inches by 4 inches and is actually 1½ by 3½ inches).

   **Usage:** Canada; US; uncountable; usually

   **Source synonyms:** dimension lumber
