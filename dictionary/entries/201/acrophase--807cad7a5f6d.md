### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The time period in a cycle during which the cycle crests or peaks, especially the upper part of a sine wave fitted to a measurement of a circadian pattern or other biological rhythm.

   **Example:** The acrophase of the metabolic process ran from shortly mid-morning until sunset, with the peak amplitude recorded in mid-afternoon.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; physiology; sciences
