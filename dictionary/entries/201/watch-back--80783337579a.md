### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To watch (an audiovisual presentation) in its recorded form (as a recording), as contrasted with watching it live.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** We then instructed the trainees to watch back the key training videos for the task. We allotted for the several hours that it would take to watch them all back.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To watch (an audiovisual presentation) in its recorded form (as a recording), as contrasted with watching it live.
   1. To do so as part of the video-editing process in videography and cinematography.

      **Usage:** ambitransitive

      **Example:** When we watched back, we were relieved to find that an average viewer would hardly notice the wind noise.

      **Example:** When we watched it back, we were satisfied with its quality.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To watch (an audiovisual presentation) in its recorded form (as a recording), as contrasted with watching it live.
   1. To binge-watch (a TV show).

      **Usage:** ambitransitive

      **Example:** She can't wait to watch back the entire season — it's her Saturday night plans for this weekend!
