## Adjective · Record 2

### Forms

- more black and white — comparative
- most black and white — superlative
- black-and-white — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Of art, a photograph or photography, using dots or lines that are either black or white (with no other values involved).

   **Usage:** literal; strictly; literal; strictly

   **Example:** Using Adobe Photoshop, he opened the images that were grayscale and converted them to be black and white.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Of art, a photograph or photography, using shades of grey rather than shades of any other color.

   **Usage:** broadly; literal

   **Example:** Their photos from that era are all black and white.

   **Source synonyms:** grayscale; greyscale; monochrome

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. Of a television or monitor, displaying images in shades of grey rather than color.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. Classifying people, objects or concepts as two polar opposites, especially "right" and "wrong"; dichotomous and inflexible.

   **Example:** I wish she weren't always so black and white about every single thing.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. Easily divided into diametrically opposing categories.

   **Source synonyms:** night-and-day
