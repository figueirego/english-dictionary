## Interjection

### Pronunciation

- IPA: \[ɑ̈ɪ̯(d)oʊ̯̃(ʔ)(t̚)noʊ̯\] — source labels: US

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used in response to a question or command in which the target person does not know the answer to or how to respond.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used to indicate that one is speculating (often used sarcastically when stating the obvious).

   **Example:** Maybe you could, I don't know, directly talk to her about it instead of complaining to me for once?

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used to express exasperation or incredulity about a given situation or person.

   **Usage:** UK

   **Example:** The audacity of that lad... I don't know.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see I, don't, know.
