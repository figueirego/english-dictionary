### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Able and efficient; having the ability needed for a specific task; having the disposition to do something; permitting or being susceptible to something.

   **Example:** She is capable and efficient.

   **Example:** He does not need help; he is capable of eating on his own.

   **Example:** As everyone knew, he was capable of violence when roused.

   **Example:** That fact is not capable of proof.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of sufficient capacity or size for holding, containing, receiving or taking in; accessible to. Construed with of, for or an infinitive.

   **Usage:** obsolete
