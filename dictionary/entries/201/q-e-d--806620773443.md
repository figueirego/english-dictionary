### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Initialism of quod erat demonstrandum (“what was to be proved; what was to be demonstrated”)&#58; placed at the end of a mathematical proof to show that the theorem under discussion is proved.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; dated; initialism

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

   **Alternative form of:** [quod erat demonstrandum](../0b5/quod-erat-demonstrandum--2d61a70c33bb.md)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used to indicate that an argument or proposition is proved by the existence of some fact or scenario.

   **Usage:** broadly
