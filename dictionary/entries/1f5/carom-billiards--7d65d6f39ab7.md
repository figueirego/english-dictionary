## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A family of billiards games generally played on cloth-covered, pocketless tables, which often feature heated slate beds, typically having as the objective to score points or "counts" by caroming one's own cue ball off both the opponent's cue ball and the object ball(s) on a single shot.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** carambole billiards; carambole
