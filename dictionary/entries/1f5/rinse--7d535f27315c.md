### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To wash (something) quickly using water and no soap.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** You'd better rinse that stain before putting the shirt in the washing machine.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To remove soap from (something) using water.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Rinse the dishes after you wash them.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To drink and hangout with friends.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Let's Rinse later tonight at my house.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To swish (a liquid) around the inside of something.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To thoroughly defeat in an argument, fight or other competition.

   **Usage:** UK; slang

   **Example:** You got rinsed.
