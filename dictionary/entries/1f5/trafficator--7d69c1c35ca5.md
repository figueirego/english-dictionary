### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A blinking light on a motor vehicle that indicates the direction in which it is about to turn.

   **Usage:** Nigeria

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** automotive; transport; vehicles

   **Source synonyms:** blinker (informal); directional; directional signal; direction indicator; indicator (Australia; Britain; New-Zealand); turn indicator; turn signal (US)
