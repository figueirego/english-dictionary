### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To be suspended or expelled temporarily from the university, either compulsorily or voluntarily.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive; Durham University

   **Example:** The college rusticated him after he failed all his exams.

   **Example:** I was very unwell, so I had to rusticate for a year.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To construct so as to produce jagged or heavily textured surfaces.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To compel to live in or to send to the countryside; to cause to become rustic.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To go to reside in the country.

   **Usage:** intransitive
