### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A Hokkien and Teochew oyster dish similar to an omelette or pancake, where oysters are pan-fried or deep-fried and caked in beaten (chicken or duck) eggs mixed with tapioca starch or sweet potato starch. Other ingredients may include spring onion, garlic chives, salt, pepper, cilantro.

   **Usage:** Hong-Kong; Malaysia; Singapore; Taiwan

   **Source synonyms:** oyster cake; fried oyster

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see oyster, omelette.
