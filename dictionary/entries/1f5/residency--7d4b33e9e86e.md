### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The condition of being a resident of a particular place.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The home or residence of a person, especially in the colonies.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The position or term of a medical resident.

   **Usage:** US; countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; healthcare

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The position of a musical artist who commonly performs at a particular venue.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The trance DJ was keen to secure a residency in Ibiza.
