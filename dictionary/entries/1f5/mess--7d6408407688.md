**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A thing or group of things in a disagreeable, disorganised, or dirty state; hence a bad situation.

   **Source synonyms:** disorder; bedlam; car crash (figuratively); chaos; confusion; disarray; disorder; disorganization; dog's breakfast (Commonwealth; idiomatic); dog's dinner (British; idiomatic; slang); free-for-all (idiomatic); fuddle; hash; hot mess (US; idiomatic; slang); imbroglio; mash; maze (figuratively); mess; misorder; mommick (US; dialectal); muddle; mullock (British; dialectal); muss; shambles; shemozzle; snarl (figuratively); tangle (figuratively); topsy-turviness; upfuckery (rare; vulgar)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A large quantity or number.

   **Usage:** colloquial; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** My boss dumped a whole mess of projects on my desk today.

   **Example:** She brought back a mess of fish to fix for supper.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Excrement.

   **Usage:** countable; euphemistic; uncountable

**Source sense R1.S4**

   **Example:** Between the pain and the depression, I'm a mess.
