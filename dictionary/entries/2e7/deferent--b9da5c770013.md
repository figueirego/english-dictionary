## Noun · Etymology 2 · Record 2

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A duct in the body serving to carry away from, as opposed to an afferent one.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; medicine; sciences

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. An imaginary circle surrounding the Earth, in whose periphery either the heavenly body or the centre of the heavenly body's epicycle was supposed to be carried round.

   **Usage:** historical; Ptolemaic astronomy

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. That which carries or conveys.

   **Usage:** obsolete
