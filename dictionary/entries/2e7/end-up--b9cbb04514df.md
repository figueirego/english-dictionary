### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To bring to a conclusion.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The band usually ends up their concerts with their biggest hit song.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To come to a final place, condition, or situation, sometimes unplanned or unexpected; to turn out.

   **Usage:** copulative

   **Example:** They ended up angry at each other.

   **Example:** He ended up as an insurance agent.

   **Example:** I was going for a stroll, and ended up lost.

   **Example:** She gave her all, and ended up winning the contest.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To lift or tilt, so as to set on end.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to end up a hogshead
