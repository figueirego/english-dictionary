   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** grab; seize; snatch

   **Source synonyms:** clutch; grip; beclasp (dated); begrasp; begrip; belock (archaic); clasp; clench; clinch; cling; clutch; enclasp; fang (archaic; dialectal); fondle; glaum (dialectal); grab; grabble; grasp; grip; hang on; hold on; hold tight; hold; nab; nim (obsolete); seize; stranglehold; take
