### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To discard (trash, garbage, or the like), to toss out, to put in the trash.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He wanted to throw away the cup, but he couldn't find a trash can.

   **Example:** Don't throw the newspaper away, it goes in the recycle bin!

   **Source synonyms:** see at discard

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To waste, to squander.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** Voting for a third-party candidate sometimes feels like throwing your vote away.

   **Example:** The team threw away its chance at the semifinals.
