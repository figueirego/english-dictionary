### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A therapeutic device designed to apply pressure to the body of a hypersensitive person (usually someone on the autism spectrum) in order to calm them by providing grounding sensory stimulation.

   **Source synonyms:** hug machine; squeeze box; squeeze machine

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An environment that validates or reinforces a narrow set of beliefs by relying on false positivity and excluding any form of criticism.

   **Usage:** derogatory; offensive; slang

   **Source synonyms:** echo chamber; filter bubble; mutual admiration society; safe space; circle jerk
