## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A point in a convex set which does not lie in any open line segment joining two points in the set.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The maximum or minimum of a function.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A leaf vertex of a tree data structure.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A point that is physically located farthest in the direction of the edge of something.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A standpoint that goes farthest in some dimension or on some criterion.
