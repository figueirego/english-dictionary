### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To shout very loudly.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The teacher angrily bawled out my name.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To deliver a loud, hard scolding or lecture to; to reprimand in order to kill.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** After a mistake like that, there wasn't much to do besides bawl out the offender and clean up.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To have a serious argument accompanied by shouting.

   **Usage:** idiomatic
