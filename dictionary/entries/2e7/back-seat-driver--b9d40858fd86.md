### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An occupant of a large vehicle, such as a firetruck, who helps steer the vehicle from far in the back.

   **Usage:** dated

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person sitting (typically) in the back seat of a car issuing (unsolicited and usually unwanted) instructions to the driver.

   **Source synonyms:** nagavator

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An opinionated person who offers (unsolicited and often unwanted) advice on the best way to handle a situation.

   **Usage:** figuratively
