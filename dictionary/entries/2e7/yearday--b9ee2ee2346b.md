   **Usage:** historical; rare

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Synonym of anniversary, a day occurring in a yearly cycle.
   1. Synonym of birthday, the anniversary of one's birth.

      **Usage:** historical; nonstandard; rare

      **Source synonyms:** birthday (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Synonym of anniversary, a day occurring in a yearly cycle.
   1. An annual day; the anniversary of an event or occasion.

      **Usage:** historical; nonstandard; rare

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Synonym of anniversary, a day occurring in a yearly cycle.
   1. Synonym of deathday or (Christianity) year's mind, the anniversary of a person's death, particularly as observed with a requiem mass or other commemoration.

      **Usage:** historical; rare

      **Source synonyms:** deathday or (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A particular day numbered from the first day in the year, without regard to month divisions.
