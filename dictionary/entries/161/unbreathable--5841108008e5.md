### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Not suitable for breathing.

   **Example:** The atmosphere on Venus is an unbreathable mixture of carbon dioxide and sulphuric acid, amongst other things.

   **Source synonyms:** irrespirable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Not letting air seep through.

   **Example:** an unbreathable fabric
