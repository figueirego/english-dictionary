## Noun

### Forms

- black sheep — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A nonconformist; an unusual or unconventional person, who may be rejected or disdained by others for this reason.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** He was the black sheep in the family, as an artist among doctors and lawyers.

   **Source synonyms:** eccentric; free spirit; heteroclite; deviant; deviate; individualist; maverick; misfit; nonconformist; offbeat

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person who is not wholesome, honest, or trustworthy.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Source synonyms:** bad apple

   **Source antonyms:** golden child

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A worker who refuses to strike; a scab.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see black, sheep.

   **Example:** Baa baa black sheep have you any wool? Yes sir, yes sir, Three bags full.
