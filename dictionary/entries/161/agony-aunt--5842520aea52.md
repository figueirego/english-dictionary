### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Synonym of advice columnist, a writer of an advice column in a newspaper or other periodical, especially an actually or assumed female writer of such a column.

   **Usage:** Ireland; UK; informal

   **Example:** You should write in to the agony aunt; she'll sort it out for you.

   **Source synonyms:** advice columnist (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Someone commonly consulted for advice about personal problems.

   **Usage:** Ireland; UK; informal

   **Example:** I'm sick of everyone coming to me with their problems—I never wanted to be an agony aunt!
