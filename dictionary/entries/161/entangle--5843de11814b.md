### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To tangle up; to twist or interweave in such a manner as not to be easily separated.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The dolphins became entangled in a fishing net.

   **Source synonyms:** enmesh; ensnare; ensnarl; enmesh; ensnare (figuratively); ensnarl; entangle; foul; implicate (archaic); imply (archaic); involve (figuratively); mat; ravel; snarl; tangle

   **Source antonyms:** disentangle; unsnarl; disentangle; feeze (Scotland); tease out; [unravel](../169/unravel--5a6b64518c7e.md); unsnarl; untangle

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To involve in such complications as to render extrication difficult.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source antonyms:** disentangle

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To ensnare.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** perplex; bewilder; puzzle; addle; baffle; vex; lose; embroil; stick; stump; baffound; bamboozle; bedevil; befuddle; throw; bemud (figuratively); bemuse; bewilder; bumfuzzle; complicate; confound; confuddle (slang); confuse; confuzzle (childish; slang); dazzle; discombobulate; disconcert; blind; dumbfound; entangle (figuratively); ferhoodle; flabagast; flabbergast; flabbergaster; flurry; fluster; flummox; fuddle; jumble; knot (obsolete); mix up; muddle; muddy (figuratively); ravel; perplex; puzzle

   **Source antonyms:** disentangle; resolve; unpuzzle; absolve (obsolete); decipher; get to the bottom of (idiomatic); iron out; meliorate; cure; puzzle out; ravel (archaic); reason out; resolve; riddle; sleuth; solve; tease out (figuratively); think out; unpuzzle; [unravel](../169/unravel--5a6b64518c7e.md) (figuratively); unriddle; unsolve (obsolete); upsolve; work out; Any from Thesaurus&#58;untangle (figuratively)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To involve in difficulties or embarrassments; to embarrass, puzzle, or distract by adverse or perplexing circumstances, interests, demands, etc.; to hamper; to bewilder.

   **Usage:** transitive
