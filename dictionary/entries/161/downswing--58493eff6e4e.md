### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The portion of any movement along an arc or curve, heading in a lower direction.

   **Example:** The stock market is still reeling from the giant downswing on Tuesday.

   **Example:** Hold your head steady and keep your eye on the golf ball during your downswing.
