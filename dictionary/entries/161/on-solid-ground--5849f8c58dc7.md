## Prep\_phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Confident or justified regarding a subject or argument.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** He's on solid ground when he's analyzing the pros and cons of convertible debentures.

   **Source synonyms:** well-grounded; well-founded

   **Source antonyms:** ill-grounded; ill-founded; groundless; baseless; flimsy; unjustified

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In a safe and secure position.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** Our department is on solid ground if the company starts flirting with the idea of layoffs; some other departments are in shakier positions.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see on, solid, ground&#58; not on top of anything that is likely to give way or disintegrate (e.g., loose soil, shaky structures, water, an ice road, clouds).

   **Example:** Mr and Mrs Liu got caught in the flood that ripped through their low-lying neighborhood. "We're just thanking God that we're back on solid ground," Mr Liu told this reporter. "We were clinging to a log at one point."
