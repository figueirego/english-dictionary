### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The criminal offence of murder in a form considered by law to be the most serious; the precise distinction may vary by jurisdiction but typically involves premeditation, or the choice of victim or other circumstances being regarded as especially blameworthy.

   **Usage:** Canada; US; countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
