### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To give in; to give up; to leave in defeat.

   **Example:** Our last batter just struck out. Time to pack it up, boys.

   **Source context (verbatim):** with it

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To clear away and place into storage.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To put back together.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To move one’s residence.

   **Example:** After 5 months in Madrid, I've decided it is time to pack up.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To prepare for shipping, as a gift.

   **Example:** Pack up the crates.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To put into bags to prepare to move.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of things

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To break, to cease to function.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a machine

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To fill a pipe with cannabis for smoking.

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** Ralph, it's your turn to pack up the bowl.

   **Example:** The bong is already packed up.
