### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To be in a position in which the upper body is upright and supported by the buttocks.

   **Usage:** copulative; intransitive

   **Example:** After a long day of walking, it was good just to sit and relax.

   **Example:** You're finishing the chowder if you sit there (motionless) all evening!

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a person

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To move oneself into such a position.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** I asked him to sit.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a person

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To occupy a given position.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The dishes are still sitting on the table!

   **Example:** The temple has sat atop that hill for centuries.

   **Example:** Jim's pet parrot sat on his left shoulder.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of an object

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To remain in a state of repose; to rest; to abide; to rest in any position or condition.

   **Usage:** copulative; intransitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To be a member of a deliberative body.

   **Example:** I currently sit on a standards committee.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Of a legislative or, especially, a judicial body such as a court, to be in session.

   **Example:** In what city is the circuit court sitting for this session?

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; law

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To lie, rest, or bear; to press or weigh.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To be adjusted; to fit.

   **Example:** Your new coat sits well.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To be accepted or acceptable; to work.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** How will this new contract sit with the workers?

   **Example:** I don’t think it will sit well.

   **Example:** The violence in these video games sits awkwardly with their stated aim of educating children.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of an agreement or arrangement

**Source sense R1.S10**

   **Example:** The dining room table sits eight comfortably.

<a id="r1-s15"></a>
