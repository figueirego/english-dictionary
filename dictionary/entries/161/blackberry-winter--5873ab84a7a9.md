**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A period of cold weather that falls when blackberries are flowering, or more generally any time in spring or early summer (March, April or May).

   **Usage:** Midwestern-US; Southern-US; especially

   **Source synonyms:** blackthorn winter; dogwood winter
