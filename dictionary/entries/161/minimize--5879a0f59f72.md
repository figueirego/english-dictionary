**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make (something) smaller or as small as possible; shrink; reduce.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** We have to minimize the budget.

   **Example:** Try to minimize your biases.

   **Source synonyms:** besmall; smallify; abase (archaic); abate; belittle; besmall (rare); bring down; embase (obsolete); cut; decrease; decrement; diminish; foreshorten; lessen; lilliputianize; lower; mince; minimize; reduce; shrink; smallify

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To relegate or assign (something) to a less insignificant status; diminish.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The insurance adjuster tried to minimize the extent of the damage to lessen the company's exposure to liability.

   **Source synonyms:** lessen; lower; reduce; abase (archaic); abate; belittle; besmall (rare); bring down; embase (obsolete); cut; decrease; decrement; diminish; foreshorten; lessen; lilliputianize; lower; mince; minimize; reduce; shrink; smallify

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I didn't close anything, but I minimized all the windows so I could see the desktop.
