### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The transportation of goods (originally by water; now also (chiefly US) by land); also, the hiring of a vehicle or vessel for such transportation.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Goods or items in transport; cargo, luggage.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** The freight shifted and the trailer turned over on the highway.

   **Source synonyms:** lading

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Payment for transportation.

   **Usage:** countable; usually

   **Example:** The freight was more expensive for cars than for coal.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A burden, a load.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; usually

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Cultural or emotional associations.

   **Usage:** figuratively; specifically; uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Ellipsis of freight train.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; countable; ellipsis; usually

   **Example:** They shipped it ordinary freight to spare the expense.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** rail-transport; railways; transport

   **Alternative form of:** [freight train](../3d9/freight-train--f65f70a65aa7.md)
