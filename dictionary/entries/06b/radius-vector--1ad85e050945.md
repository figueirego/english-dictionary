### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of a point, a vector going from the origin to that point; equivalently, the transpose of the point.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** position vector

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An ideal straight line joining the center of an attracting body with that of a body describing an orbit around it, as a line joining the sun and a planet or comet, or a planet and its satellite.
