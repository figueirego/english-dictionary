### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. It may work differently in your situation, or be different in your experience.

   **Usage:** Canada; US; idiomatic; often

   **Example:** The battery lasts a day in my phone, but your mileage may vary.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used to express a possible difference in taste&#58; this is just my opinion, your opinion may be different.

   **Usage:** Canada; US; idiomatic

   **Example:** I think that red dress looks really good on you, but your mileage may vary, of course.
