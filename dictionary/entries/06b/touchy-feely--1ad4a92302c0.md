## Adjective · Record 1

### Forms

- more touchy-feely — comparative
- most touchy-feely — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having a fondness for physical contact with other people, especially to an excessive degree.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** My last boyfriend was a lovely guy, but I dumped him because he was too touchy-feely with me in public.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Driven by intuition or emotion, with a connotation of de-emphasis of rational thought or logic.

   **Usage:** derogatory; idiomatic; usually

   **Example:** He preferred the clarity of science and left the touchy-feely stuff to others.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Appealing to emotion, sympathy, or romance.

   **Usage:** derogatory; idiomatic; usually

   **Example:** I think the movie had too much touchy-feely nonsense and not enough action.

### Senses
