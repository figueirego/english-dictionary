### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To conceal or disguise.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** The politician tried to cover up his involvement in the scandal.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To clothe; to conceal one's private parts.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** Cover up, slut!

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To put covers over oneself in bed.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** Please cover up and go to sleep now.
