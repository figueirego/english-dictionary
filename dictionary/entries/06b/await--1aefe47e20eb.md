### Pronunciation

- IPA: /əˈweɪt/ — source labels: UK

### Forms

- awaited — participle; past

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To wait for.

   **Usage:** formal; transitive

   **Example:** I await your reply to my letter.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To expect.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To be in store for; to be ready or in waiting for.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Glorious rewards await the good in heaven; eternal suffering awaits mortal sinners in hell.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To serve or attend; to wait on, wait upon.

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To watch, observe.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To wait; to stay in waiting.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** These are exotic lands where great adventures await.
