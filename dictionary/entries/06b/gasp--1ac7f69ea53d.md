### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To draw in the breath suddenly, as if from a shock.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The audience gasped as the magician disappeared.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To breathe quickly or in a labored manner, as after exertion; to respire with heaving of the breast; to pant.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** We were all gasping when we reached the summit.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To speak in a breathless manner.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The old man gasped his last few words.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To pant with eagerness or excitement; to show vehement desire.

   **Example:** I'm gasping for a cup of tea.
