### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A small ticket or sign giving information about something to which it is attached or intended to be attached.

   **Example:** We laughed at her because the label was still on her new sweater.

   **Example:** The label says this silk scarf should not be washed in the washing machine.

   **Example:** Although the label priced this poster at three pounds, I got it for two.

   **Source synonyms:** sign; tag; ticket

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A name given to something or someone to categorise them as part of a particular social group.

   **Example:** Ever since he started going to the rock club, he's been given the label "waster".

   **Source synonyms:** category; pigeonhole

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A company that sells records.

   **Example:** The label signed the band after hearing a demo tape.

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To put a label (a ticket or sign) on (something).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The shop assistant labeled all the products in the shop.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To give (a label) to (someone or something) in order to categorise that person or thing.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive; ditransitive

   **Example:** He's been unfairly labeled as a cheat, although he's only ever cheated once.
