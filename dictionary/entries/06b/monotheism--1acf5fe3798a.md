### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Belief in the One True God, defined by More as personal, immaterial and trinitarian.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The belief in a single deity (one god or goddess); especially within an organized religion.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The belief that God is one person (Judaism, Unitarian Christianity, Islam), not three persons (Trinitarian Christianity, Hinduism)

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
