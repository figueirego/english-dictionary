### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A type of lettering form written lower than the things around it.

   **Example:** In chemical formulas the number of atoms in a molecule is written as a subscript, so we write H₂O for water which has two atoms of hydrogen for each one of oxygen.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** media; publishing; typography

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An index into an array or hash.
