### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any indigenous polytheistic religion.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Most people in that region practise their own form of paganism.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of a class of religions often associated with nature rituals.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Various neopagan movements have arisen, each advancing its own form of paganism. Some are monotheist.
