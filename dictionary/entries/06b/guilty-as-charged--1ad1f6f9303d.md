## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Guilty to the same extent as one is charged; guilty to the court's accusations exactly as they were presented.

   **Usage:** literally; not-comparable

   **Example:** My evidence will prove beyond a shadow of a doubt that the defendant is guilty as charged!

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Truly, indeed, verily responsible for having done something.

   **Usage:** broadly; not-comparable

   **Example:** My coworkers noticed that I was tired, and assumed that I had stayed up all night watching the game. And yes, I was guilty as charged.
