## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The gathering and submitting of completed absentee or mail-in voter ballots by third-party individuals, volunteers or workers, rather than submission by voters themselves directly to ballot collection sites. It is legal in some parts of the United States.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** ballot harvesting; ballot chasing
