   **Usage:** also; archaic; figuratively; transitive

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

   **Usage:** derogatory; intransitive

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A fatty layer of adipose tissue found immediately beneath the epidermis of whales and other cetaceans (infraorder Cetacea).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. A fatty layer of adipose tissue found immediately beneath the epidermis of whales and other cetaceans (infraorder Cetacea).
   1. A fatty layer of adipose tissue found in other animals which keeps them warm, especially Arctic animals such as sea lions and Antarctic animals such as penguins.

      **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. A fatty layer of adipose tissue found immediately beneath the epidermis of whales and other cetaceans (infraorder Cetacea).
   1. A person's fat tissue, usually when regarded as excessive and unsightly.

      **Usage:** derogatory; informal; offensive; uncountable

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. A fatty layer of adipose tissue found immediately beneath the epidermis of whales and other cetaceans (infraorder Cetacea).
   1. A jellyfish (subphylum Medusozoa).

      **Usage:** archaic; broadly; countable

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

   **Usage:** countable; derogatory
