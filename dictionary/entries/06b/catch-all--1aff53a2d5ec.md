### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any place or repository where things are placed indiscriminately or without careful thought.

   **Example:** If you're not careful, the entrance table will quickly become a catch-all for things that come in the door.

   **Example:** The category was a catch-all for items that were not filed elsewhere.
