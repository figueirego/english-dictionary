### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A pair of insulated electrical wires with alligator clips at each end (typically used to jump-start a car with a dead (flat) battery).

   **Usage:** often pluralized

   **Example:** He kept a jumper cable in an emergency box in the car, as he had once been caught with a dead battery.

   **Source synonyms:** booster cable; starter cable; jump lead (UK)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An electrical cable providing a connection between rail or road vehicles.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** rail-transport; railways; road; transport
