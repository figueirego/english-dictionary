### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Something that makes a clanking noise.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A robot or artificial intelligence.

   **Usage:** Internet; derogatory; ethnic; humorous; slur

   **Example:** I got laid off 'cause they got them goddamn clankers taking our jobs.

   **Source synonyms:** cogsucker; rust monkey; tinskin; wireback

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A person who uses automated technology or generative AI.

   **Usage:** Internet; broadly

   **Example:** Education is getting overtaken by clankers.
