### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A desire, hope, or longing for something or for something to happen.

   **Example:** It is my wish that the bequest (should) be given to an almshouse.

   **Example:** make someone's wish come true

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An expression of such a desire, often connected with ideas of magic and supernatural power.

   **Example:** make a wish

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The thing desired or longed for.

   **Example:** My dearest wish is to see them happily married.

   **Example:** You have my best wishes.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A water meadow.

   **Usage:** Sussex

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To desire; to want.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I'll come tomorrow, if you wish (it).

   **Example:** I wish it to be known that I wish them all to leave the company.

   **Example:** Anyone who wishes an aisle seat should see an attendant.

   **Example:** Oh, (I wish) that I'd never been born.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To hope (+ object clause with may or in present subjunctive).

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To hope (for a particular outcome), even if that outcome is unlikely to occur or cannot occur.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** I wish I could go back in time and teach myself what I know now.

   **Example:** I wished (that) it would rain.

   **Source context (verbatim):** followed by for

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To bestow (a thought or gesture) towards (someone or something).

   **Usage:** ditransitive

   **Example:** We wish you a merry Christmas.

   **Example:** I wouldn't wish that on my worst enemy.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To request or desire to do an activity.

   **Usage:** intransitive; followed by to and an infinitive

   **Example:** Anyone who wishes to may leave now.

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To recommend; to seek confidence or favour on behalf of.

   **Usage:** transitive
