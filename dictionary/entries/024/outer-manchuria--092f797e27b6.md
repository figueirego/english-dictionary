## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The part of Russia near northeastern China (including Primorsky Krai and other nearby areas) which was annexed by the Russian Empire in the mid 19th century understood as part of Manchuria; similar or equivalent to Russian Manchuria.

   **Usage:** neologism

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any proverbially distant or remote place.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Source synonyms:** Outer Mongolia; Timbuktu

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see outer, Manchuria (a region of northeast China and Manchuria)
