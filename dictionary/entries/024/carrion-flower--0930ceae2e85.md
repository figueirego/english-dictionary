## Noun

### Forms

- carrion flowers — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of a wide variety of plants that have a scent of carrion that attracts certain animals to the plants from which they disperse pollen, seeds. or spores.
   1. Amorphophallus spp., arums of the Old World tropics.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of a wide variety of plants that have a scent of carrion that attracts certain animals to the plants from which they disperse pollen, seeds. or spores.
   1. Rafflesia spp., of southeast Asia.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of a wide variety of plants that have a scent of carrion that attracts certain animals to the plants from which they disperse pollen, seeds. or spores.
   1. Stapelia spp., of Africa, especially South Africa.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any of a wide variety of plants that have a scent of carrion that attracts certain animals to the plants from which they disperse pollen, seeds. or spores.
   1. Smilax (syn. Nemexia), herabaceous vines of North America

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Any of a wide variety of plants that have a scent of carrion that attracts certain animals to the plants from which they disperse pollen, seeds. or spores.
   1. Bulbophyllum spp., certain orchids

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**
