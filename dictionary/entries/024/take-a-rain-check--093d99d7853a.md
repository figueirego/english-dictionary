### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To request or accept a rain check (an agreement from a merchant to honor a special offer, temporarily unavailable, after the expiration date).

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In social interactions, to request a deferred invitation, as a polite way to turn down an engagement, usually with the implication that one is simply postponing it and another time might be acceptable.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** I can't go with you to the museum this Saturday, but can I take a rain check?

   **Source synonyms:** raincheck
