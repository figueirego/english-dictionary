### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Either of two laws in formal logic which state that&#58;
   1. The negation of a conjunction is the disjunction of the negations; expressed in propositional logic as&#58; ¬ (𝑝 ∧ 𝑞) ⇔ (¬ 𝑝) ∨ (¬ 𝑞)

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Either of two laws in formal logic which state that&#58;
   1. The negation of a disjunction is the conjunction of the negations; expressed in propositional logic as&#58; ¬ (𝑝 ∨ 𝑞) ⇔ (¬ 𝑝) ∧ (¬ 𝑞)

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; logic; mathematics; philosophy; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Either of two laws in set theory which state that&#58;
   1. The complement of a union is the intersection of the complements; as expressed by&#58; (𝐴 ∪ 𝐵)′ = 𝐴′ ∩ 𝐵′

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences
