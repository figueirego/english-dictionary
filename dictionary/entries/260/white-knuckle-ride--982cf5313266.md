### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An extremely exciting and nerve-racking ride at an amusement park, etc., where one's hands grip on so tightly that the knuckles appear white.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A car ride or bike ride that is similarly nerve-racking, provoking fear of a crash.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any extremely exciting or nerve-racking experience.

   **Usage:** figuratively
