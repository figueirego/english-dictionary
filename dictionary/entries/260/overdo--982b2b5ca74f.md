### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To do too much; to exceed what is proper or true in doing; to carry too far.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I overdid the sweets during the holidays and put on some weight.

   **Source synonyms:** exaggerate; big up (slang); cut it fat (UK; obsolete; slang); exaggerate; hyperbolize (archaic); overcharge (dated); overdo; overemphasize; overstate; play up; stretch (figuratively); three-sheet

   **Source antonyms:** underdo; downplay

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To cook for too long.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to overdo the meat

   **Source synonyms:** overcook

   **Source antonyms:** underdo; undercook

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To give (someone or something) too much work; to require too much effort or strength of (someone); to use up too much of (something).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to overdo one’s strength

   **Source synonyms:** overtask; overtax; fatigue; exhaust; wear out

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To do more than (someone); to do (something) to a greater extent.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** excel; outdo; surpass
