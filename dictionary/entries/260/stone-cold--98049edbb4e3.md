### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Very cold (literally); lacking any semblance of warmth.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I forgot to turn on the burner under the soup and found it sitting there, still stone cold, twenty minutes later.

   **Source synonyms:** ice-cold; cold as ice; icy; freezing cold; frigid; cold as a well-digger's arse; algid; Arctic; Baltic; boreal; brass monkeys; brumal; chill; chilled; chilly; clay-cold; cold; cold as a mackerel; cold as charity; freezing; frosty; frore; frozen; gelid; glacial; hiemal; icy; inclement; nesh; nippy; parky (UK); polar; sleety; snowy; taters; wintry

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Lacking emotion or compassion.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** a stone-cold demeanor

   **Example:** a stone-cold killer

   **Source context (verbatim):** of persons or their behavior

   **Source synonyms:** stony; cold

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** a stone cold certainty
