## Prep\_phrase

### Forms

- off-camera — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In a position or situation where one is not being photographed or filmed.

   **Example:** He would only talk to us off camera.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In or related to a role behind the cameras, e.g. in production, lighting, editing, etc.

   **Example:** We have some of the best off-camera talent in the business.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** broadcasting; film; media; television
