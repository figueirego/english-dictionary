### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Increased awareness of the dangers of global warming, resulting from U.S. politician Al Gore's environmental activism and especially his 2006 documentary An Inconvenient Truth.

   **Source synonyms:** Al Gore effect

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The coincidental occurrence of unseasonably cold weather during some events associated with global warming activism, particularly those attended by Al Gore.

   **Usage:** humorous

   **Source synonyms:** Al Gore effect
