## Noun

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Logical consistency; lack of self-contradiction.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The staying together, or remaining in close relation, of non-physical things.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Her performance has lacked consistence over the last year.

   **Example:** This composer’s musical work is of extraordinary consistence.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The physical quality which is given by the degree of density, firmness, solidity, and viscosity; consistency.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The condition of adhering or standing together, or being fixed in union, as the parts of a body; coherence, existence, firmness, solidity.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. That which stands together as a united whole; a combination.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Standing still; quiescence, state of rest.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A substance which adheres together.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; obsolete except poetic
