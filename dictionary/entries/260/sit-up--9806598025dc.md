### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To assume a sitting position from a position lying down.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Despite being cancer-ridden, Lorin sat up to greet the visitors.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To sit erect.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Sit up straight, mister!

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To show sudden interest or surprise.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** He sat up when we mentioned the increased pay package.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To bounce, especially to a comfortable height.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports

   **Source context (verbatim):** of the ball

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To not go to bed (notionally remaining in a sitting position).

   **Example:** I sat up all night waiting for her to come home.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To stop pedalling hard.

   **Example:** Half way up the climb, Jones sat up and let Smith continue alone.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** cycling; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. For a young lady to open her house for several successive nights for any visitors who wish to pay their respects.

   **Usage:** India; historical
