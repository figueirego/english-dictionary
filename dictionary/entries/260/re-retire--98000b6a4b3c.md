### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To retire again.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** He re-retired after being unretired for a while. His unretirement was worth his while, but he plans to stay retired this time.

   **Source antonyms:** unretire

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To bring (something or someone) back from retirement.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** We re-retired this machine because our newest equipment truly obviates it, and there's no realistic prospect that it will ever save us any money in the future.

   **Source synonyms:** reinstate

   **Source antonyms:** unretire
