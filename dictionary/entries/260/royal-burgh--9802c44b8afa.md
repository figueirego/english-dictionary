### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A type of Scottish burgh founded by, or subsequently granted, a royal charter; these were legally abolished in 1975.

   **Usage:** Scotland; historical

   **Example:** Most royal burghs were either created by the Crown, or upgraded from another status, such as a burgh of barony.

   **Source synonyms:** burgh royal (obsolete); royal borough (obsolete)
