### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To force or pressure (someone) into accepting something.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He shoved a spicy dish on me.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To depart or move forward, especially when resuming wandering or continuing progress.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** You've been here awhile, but you better shove on from my town soon.
