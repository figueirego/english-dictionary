**Source sense R1.S3**

3. One of the major veins of the insect wing, between the subcosta and the media; the vein running along the costal edge of the discal cell.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; entomology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A line segment between any point of a circle or sphere and its center.

   **Example:** Fatima claims to have visited all the bars within a five-mile radius of her Manhattan apartment.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The length of this line segment.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Anything resembling a radius, such as the spoke of a wheel, the movable arm of a sextant, or one of the radiating lines of a spider's web.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**
