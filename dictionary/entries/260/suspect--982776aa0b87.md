### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To imagine or suppose (something) to be true, or to exist, without proof.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to suspect the presence of disease

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To distrust or have doubts about (something or someone).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to suspect the truth of a story

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To believe (someone) to be guilty.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I suspect him of being the thief.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To have suspicion.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To look up to; to respect.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive
