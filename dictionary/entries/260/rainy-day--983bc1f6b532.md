### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A difficult period of need, in reference to a time when money will be needed.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I've kept some money in the bank for a rainy day.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see rainy, day.

   **Example:** Rainy days and Mondays always get me down.
