### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To refuse to allow.

   **Example:** The prisoners were disallowed to contact with a lawyer.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To reject as invalid, untrue, or improper.

   **Example:** The goal was disallowed because the player was offside.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To overrule a colonial legislation by the sovereign-in-privy council.

   **Usage:** UK; historical

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law
