### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An illuminating realization or discovery, often resulting in a personal feeling of elation, awe, or wonder.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** It came to her in an epiphany what her life's work was to be.

   **Source synonyms:** aha moment; enlightenment; eureka moment; nirvana; satori

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A manifestation or appearance of a divine or superhuman being.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source synonyms:** theophany
