## Noun · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈskɹæmbl̩/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A rush or hurry, especially making use of the limbs against a surface.

   **Example:** a last-minute scramble to the finish line

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. An emergency defensive air force mission to intercept attacking enemy aircraft.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. A motocross race.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. Any frantic period of competitive activity.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. An impromptu maneuver or run by a quarterback, attempting to gain yardage or avoid being tackled behind the line of scrimmage.

   **Usage:** gridiron football

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. A statistic used in assessing a player's short game, consisting of a chip or putt from under 50 yards away that results in requiring one putt or less on the green.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** golf; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

**Source sense R2.S7**

7. A variant of golf in which each player in a team tees off on each hole, and the players decide which shot was best. Every player then plays their second shot from within a club length of where the best ball has come to rest, and the procedure is repeated until the hole is finished.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** golf; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r2-s8"></a>

**Source sense R2.S8**

8. A dish (meal) involving scrambled eggs and a hodgepodge of complementary ingredients, usually closer to a casserole than to an omelette.

   **Example:** The diner's menu features "the farmer's scramble" (under all-day breakfast) and "the fisherman's fricassee" (under dinner entrees).

   **Source synonyms:** hash

<a id="r2-s9"></a>

**Source sense R2.S9**
