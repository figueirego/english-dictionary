### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Being full; completeness.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** feel a sense of fullness

   **Example:** The actor enjoyed the fullness of his success.

   **Example:** She lived life in all its fullness.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The degree to which a space is full.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The degree to which fate has become known.

   **Usage:** figuratively; uncountable; usually

   **Example:** in the fullness of time

   **Example:** fullness of life

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A measure of the degree to which a muscle has increased in size parallel to the axis of its contraction. A full muscle fills more of the space along the part of the body where it is connected.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** bodybuilding; hobbies; lifestyle; sports
