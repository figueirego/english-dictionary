### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A clause or section, especially in a law, granting exceptions for people or organisations who were affected by previous conditions.

   **Example:** Many building codes include a grandfather clause exempting older buildings until some amount of remodeling occurs.
