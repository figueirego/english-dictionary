### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A thing (especially an aspect of something) which is beautiful.

   **Usage:** archaic

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A thing (especially an aspect of something) which is beautiful.
   1. A natural mark on a person's skin, especially a freckle or mole on a woman's face.

      **Usage:** archaic

      **Source synonyms:** beauty mark

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A thing (especially an aspect of something) which is beautiful.
   1. A patch or spot drawn or placed on the face in order to heighten beauty.

      **Usage:** archaic; historical

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** cosmetics; lifestyle

      **Source synonyms:** beauty mark; mouche

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A thing (especially an aspect of something) which is beautiful.
   1. Synonym of speculum (“a bright, lustrous patch of colour found on the wings of ducks and some other birds, usually situated on the distal portions of the secondary quills, and much more brilliant in the adult male than in the female”).

      **Usage:** archaic

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences; ornithology

      **Source synonyms:** speculum (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A place noted for its beauty, especially its natural scenery.

   **Usage:** UK
