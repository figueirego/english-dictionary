### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The favoring of relatives (most strictly) or also personal friends (more broadly) because of their relationship rather than because of their abilities.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Nepotism can get you very far in the world if you've got the right connections.

   **Source antonyms:** meritocracy
