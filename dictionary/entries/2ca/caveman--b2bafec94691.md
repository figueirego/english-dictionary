### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An early human or closely related species, popularly held to reside in caves.

   **Example:** The political cartoon showed the politician as a caveman, clubbing the budget depicted as a mammoth.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person with backward, primitive behavior, opinions, or interests.

   **Usage:** derogatory; figuratively; informal

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A person with backward, primitive behavior, opinions, or interests.
   1. A brutish person, one who behaves in a rough, uncivilized way.

      **Usage:** derogatory; figuratively; informal

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A person with backward, primitive behavior, opinions, or interests.
   1. Someone, especially a man, who has regressive, old-fashioned attitudes, particularly with regard to women; someone opposed to change or modernity.

      **Usage:** derogatory; figuratively; informal
