### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of a liquid; to move slowly down to lower substrate levels.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. (by extension) Of information, or resources; to move slowly down to lower levels of an organisation, or population.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** They might say that the economy is improving, but it is taking a long time for any money to filter down to the poorer classes.
