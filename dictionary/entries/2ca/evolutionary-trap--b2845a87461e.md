### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The situation of an evolved behaviour or trait of a species becoming detrimental to the survival or reproductive ability of individuals, possibly leading to extinction.

   **Usage:** evolutionary theory

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The situation of a rapid ecological change triggering decisions about choice of habitat that are detrimental for an individual; an ecological trap.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; ecology; natural-sciences
