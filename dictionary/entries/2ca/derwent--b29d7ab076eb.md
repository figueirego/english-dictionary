### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A river in Derbyshire, England, which flows into the River Trent.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A demolished village, now flooded by Ladybower Reservoir, with its site in High Peak borough, Derbyshire.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A civil parish (served by Derwent and Hope Woodlands Parish Council) in High Peak borough, Derbyshire, which formerly included the above village.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A river in North Yorkshire, England, which also forms the border with the East Riding of Yorkshire before joining the River Ouse.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A river in Cumbria, England, which flows into the Irish Sea at Workington.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A river forming the border between County Durham and Northumberland, before entering Tyneside and joining the River Tyne.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A river in Tasmania, Australia, which flows into the Tasman Sea near Hobart.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A hamlet and former village in Alberta, Canada.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A community in Thames Centre, Ontario, Canada.

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. An unincorporated community in Valley Township, Guernsey County, Ohio, United States.

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. A surname.
