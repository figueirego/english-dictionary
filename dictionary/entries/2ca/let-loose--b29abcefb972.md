<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To free; to release from restraint.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** loose; unleash; set loose; let rip

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To shout, make a loud sound, or perform a sudden, vehement action; to behave in a raucous, frenzied manner.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive; sometimes

   **Source context (verbatim):** followed by with or on

   **Source synonyms:** explode; go ape; holler; vociferate; let rip
