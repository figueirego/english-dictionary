### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To flow back again; to flow once more.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The sea flows and reflows.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To cause to flow again, to remelt.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Reflowing solder is a common form of rework in the manufacture of printed circuit boards&#58; you heat up the solder and it flows where it should have flowed the first time.
