### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One million million (10¹²) watts, an amount of power large enough to power an entire region with many cities. (Consuming 1 terawatt during a duration of 1 hour consumes 1 terawatt-hour of energy.).

   **Example:** As of 2025, it was estimated that all of the power stations on Earth were collectively generating about 15 terawatts at any given instant.
