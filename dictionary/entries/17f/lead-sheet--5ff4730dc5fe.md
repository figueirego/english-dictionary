**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A form of written music that specifies the melody, lyrics, and harmony.

   **Example:** A lead sheet is often the only form of written music used by a small jazz ensemble. One or more musicians will play the melody while the rest of the group improvises an appropriate accompaniment.
