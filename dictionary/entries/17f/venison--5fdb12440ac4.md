### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The meat of a deer, especially one that was hunted in its own natural habitat.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** After shooting a deer, field dressing is the next step necessary for high quality venison.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The meat of an antelope.

   **Usage:** South-Africa; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The meat of any wild animal that has been hunted rather than raised domestically.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** deer meat; deerflesh
