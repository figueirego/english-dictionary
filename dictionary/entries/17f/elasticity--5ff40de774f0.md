### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The property by virtue of which a material deformed under load can regain its original dimensions when unloaded

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The sensitivity of changes in a quantity with respect to changes in another quantity.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** If the sales of an item drop by 5% when the price increases by 10%, its price elasticity is −0.5.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** economics; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A measure of the flexibility of a data store's data model and clustering capabilities.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A system's ability to adapt to changes in workload by automatically provisioning and de-provisioning resources.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The ratio of the relative change in a function's output with respect to the relative change in its input, for infinitesimal changes at a certain point.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** point elasticity

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The quality of being elastic.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Adaptability.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Her elasticity allowed her to recover quickly.
