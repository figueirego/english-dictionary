### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who makes jokes.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A funny person.

   **Usage:** slang

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A jester.

   **Source synonyms:** court jester; fool; jester

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A playing card that features a picture of a joker (that is, a jester) and that may be used as a wild card in some card games.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Something kept in reserve that can be used to gain an advantage; a trump card.

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. An unspecified, vaguely disreputable person.

   **Example:** Some joker keeps throwing eggs at my windows.

   **Source synonyms:** clown

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. An unspecified, vaguely disreputable person.
   1. A loser.

      **Example:** Don't waste your breath on these jokers round here.

      **Source synonyms:** clown

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A man.

   **Usage:** New-Zealand; colloquial

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A clause in a contract that undermines its apparent provisions.

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. A friendly unit that acts as a suspected hostile unit in a military exercise.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. The option, in a pub quiz, of selecting one particular round in which one's team will score double points.

   **Example:** We used our joker as soon as the topic of sports was announced, since we are sport experts.

<a id="r1-s12"></a>

**Source sense R1.S12**

12. A fairy chess piece that moves like the last piece that was moved by the opponent.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** board-games; chess; games
