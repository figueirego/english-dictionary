### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who performs abortions, especially illegally or secretly.

   **Usage:** offensive; sometimes

   **Source synonyms:** aborter (rare); angel maker (euphemistic; historical); jack-knife barber (UK)

   **Usage:** derogatory

   **Source synonyms:** pro-abortionist; pro-choicer; pro-deather (derogatory)

   **Source antonyms:** anti-abortionist; anti-choicer (derogatory); fetus fetishist (derogatory); forced-birther (derogatory); pro-lifer; right-to-lifer
