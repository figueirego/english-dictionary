### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A hedgehog.

   **Usage:** archaic; dialectal

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A mischievous elf supposed sometimes to take the form of a hedgehog.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A mischievous child.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A street urchin, a child who lives, or spends most of their time, in the streets.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A sea urchin.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. One of a pair in a series of small card cylinders arranged around a carding drum; so called from its fancied resemblance to the hedgehog.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A neutron-generating device that triggered the nuclear detonation of the earliest plutonium atomic bombs.

   **Usage:** historical
