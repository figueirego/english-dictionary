### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A book in which visitors or guests (at, for example, a bed and breakfast) may leave comments about the establishment.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A feature of a website performing a similar function to a written guest book.
