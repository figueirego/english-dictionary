### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A point, near or within a body, through which its weight can be assumed to act when considering forces on the body and its motion under gravity. This coincides with the center of mass in a uniform gravitational field.

   **Usage:** US

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

   **Source synonyms:** COG

<a id="r1-s2"></a>
