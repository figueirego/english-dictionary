### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To form into a whole by uniting its parts; to construct; to build.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to fabricate a bridge or ship

   **Source synonyms:** manufacture

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To form by art and labor; to manufacture; to produce.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to fabricate computer chips

   **Source synonyms:** manufacture

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To invent and form; to forge; to devise falsely.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to fabricate a lie or story

   **Source synonyms:** confabulate; cook up; invent; make up; manufacture; trump up

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To cut up an animal as preparation for cooking, particularly used in reference to fowl.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** cooking; food; lifestyle
