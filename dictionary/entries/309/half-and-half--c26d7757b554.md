   **Usage:** not-comparable

## Noun · Record 3

### Forms

- half and halfs — plural
- half-and-half — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r3-s1"></a>

**Source sense R3.S1**

1. A mixture of two things, generally in about equal proportions.
   1. A mixture of cream and milk often used in coffee, defined by the US Food and Drug Administration to contain between 10.5% and 18% milk fat.

      **Usage:** US; countable; uncountable

<a id="r3-s2"></a>

**Source sense R3.S2**

2. A mixture of two things, generally in about equal proportions.
   1. A mixture of two alcoholic beverages, most frequently those brewed from malt, such as porter and ale or mild and bitter beers, in about equal parts; also used for equal parts of an alcoholic beverage and water.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r3-s3"></a>

**Source sense R3.S3**

3. A mixture of two things, generally in about equal proportions.
   1. A pizza with two different sets of toppings, one on each half.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r3-s4"></a>

**Source sense R3.S4**

4. A mixture of two things, generally in about equal proportions.
   1. A person of mixed race or with dual nationality.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r3-s5"></a>

**Source sense R3.S5**

5. A mixture of two things, generally in about equal proportions.
   1. A combination of both rice and chips as an accompaniment to a curry, as opposed to one or the other.

      **Usage:** Ireland; UK; countable; uncountable

<a id="r3-s6"></a>

**Source sense R3.S6**

6. A mixture of two things, generally in about equal proportions.
   1. A combination of beer and ale (or similar alcoholic drinks) in equal quantities.

      **Usage:** UK; countable; uncountable

<a id="r3-s7"></a>

**Source sense R3.S7**

7. A mixture of two things, generally in about equal proportions.
   1. Both oral sex and vaginal sex performed in sequence by a prostitute

      **Usage:** countable; slang; uncountable

<a id="r3-s8"></a>

**Source sense R3.S8**

8. A mixture of two things, generally in about equal proportions.
   1. A mixture of half lemonade and half iced tea.

      **Usage:** US; countable; uncountable

      **Source synonyms:** Arnold Palmer

<a id="r3-s9"></a>

**Source sense R3.S9**

9. A mixture of two things, generally in about equal proportions.
   1. A cup of coffee with the equivalent of half a packet of sugar and half a creamer of cream.

      **Usage:** Canada; Western; countable; uncountable
