### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A construction commonly found in Greek and Hebrew in which either a verb of motion is paired with a locative prepositional phrase or a static verb is paired with an allative preposition phrase.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** "I attacked the dog into the fire" employs a rough form of praegnans constructio.
