### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The chores of maintaining a house as a residence, especially cleaning.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The chores of maintaining a house as a residence, especially cleaning.
   1. Those workers of an establishment who maintain a house through cleaning, etc.

      **Usage:** countable; metonymically; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any general tasks that involve preparation.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The computer program does some general housekeeping involving initializing variables and opening files before beginning the main processing.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
