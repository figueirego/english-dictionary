### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A form of land tenure and small-scale food production, unique to the Highlands and islands of Scotland, in which individual crofts are established on the better land while a large area of poor-quality hill ground is shared by all the crofters of the township for grazing.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The process of exposing linen to the sun, on the grass, in the process of bleaching.
