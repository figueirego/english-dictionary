### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A place in England&#58;
   1. A village and civil parish in Derbyshire Dales district, Derbyshire (OS grid ref SK1632).

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A place in England&#58;
   1. A suburb and ward in the borough of Brent, Greater London (OS grid ref TQ1685).

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A place in England&#58;
   1. A market town and civil parish with a town council in Babergh district, Suffolk (OS grid ref TL8741).

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A place in Canada
   1. A city, a municipality in northern Ontario; in full, Greater Sudbury; established 2001.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A place in Canada
   1. A district in northern Ontario; in full, Sudbury District; Greater Sudbury exists as an enclave fully enclosed within the district, but administered separately.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A place in Canada
   1. A former regional municipality of the Sudbury District, Northern Ontario, Ontario; in full, Regional Municipality of Sudbury; created in 1973, carved out of the eponymous district; that was merged into Greater Sudbury in 2001.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A place in Canada
   1. A former city in Regional Municipality of Sudbury, Sudbury District, in Northern Ontario; established in 1883 as Sainte-Anne-des-Pins; that was merged into Greater Sudbury in 2001.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A place in the United States&#58;
   1. A town in Middlesex County, Massachusetts.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A place in the United States&#58;
   1. A town in Rutland County, Vermont.
