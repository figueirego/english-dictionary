### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Electrical wire that has the insulated stands enclosed in a further sheath.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Wire for the transmission of electricity or electronic signals, protected by an enclosing web of earthed wire mesh or metal foil to avoid electromagnetic interference from (or to) other signals.
