### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of comparing or the state or process of being compared.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** to bring a thing into comparison with another

   **Example:** there is no comparison between them

   **Source synonyms:** parallel

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An evaluation of the similarities and differences of one or more things relative to some other or each other.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** He made a careful comparison of the available products before buying anything.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. With a negation, the state of being similar or alike.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** There really is no comparison between the performance of today's computers and those of a decade ago.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A feature in the morphology or syntax of some languages whereby adjectives and adverbs are inflected to indicate the relative degree of the property they define exhibited by the word or phrase they modify or describe.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** In English, adjectives and adverbs have three forms when making a comparison&#58; the plain form "hot", the comparative form "hotter", and the superlative form "hottest".

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** grammar; human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. That to which, or with which, a thing is compared, as being equal or like; illustration; similitude.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A simile.

   **Usage:** countable; rhetoric; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The faculty of the reflective group which is supposed to perceive resemblances and contrasts.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; medicine; phrenology; psychology; sciences
