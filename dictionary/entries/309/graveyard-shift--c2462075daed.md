## Noun

### Forms

- graveyard shifts — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A work shift late at night, especially one from midnight to 8 AM on a three-shift schedule.

   **Example:** I had to work the graveyard shift for most of that year, and at first I hated it, but eventually I even kind of liked it, in some ways.

   **Source synonyms:** night shift

   **Source antonyms:** day shift

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A group of workers who work during this time slot.

   **Example:** You'll have to ask the graveyard shift — the equipment was here when we left.

   **Source synonyms:** night shift

   **Source antonyms:** day shift
