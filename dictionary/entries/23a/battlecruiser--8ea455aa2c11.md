### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A fast warship with thicker armor and heavier guns than a typical cruiser, but with armor, armament, or both still inferior to that of a dreadnought battleship; designed to act as an advanced scout of the battle fleet and to destroy enemy cruisers.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; nautical; politics; transport; war

   **Source synonyms:** supercruiser; cruiser-killer; large cruiser

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A warship of intermediate size and/or capability between a heavy cruiser and a (light) battleship.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** fiction; literature; media; publishing

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A boozer; a public house.

   **Usage:** Cockney; slang
