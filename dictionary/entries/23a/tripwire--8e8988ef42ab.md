### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A cord or wire arranged so that when snagged or pulled by an intruder, it will trigger a detector or trap or a device, such as a land mine.

   **Example:** Seeing the tripwire of a nearby bouncing Betty gave us cold chills, and we moved all the more slowly through the woods.

   **Source synonyms:** trip cord; tripline

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any means of detecting intruders or attackers.

   **Usage:** figuratively
