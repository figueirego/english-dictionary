### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. At a given point, the angle between magnetic north and true north.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** magnetic declination

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. At a given point, the angle between the line connecting this point with the geographical center of the earth and the equatorial plane.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astronomy; natural-sciences

   **Source synonyms:** δ

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A refusal.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** nonassent; denial; rejection; abnegation; declination; denial; turndown; disapproval; dissension; nonapproval; nonassent; nonconsent; prohibition; refusal; rejection; renunciation; repudiation; unapproval; unconsent

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The gradual decline in the overall fundamental frequency or pitch of speech over the course of an utterance, independent of local variations such as tones and pitch accents.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; phonology; prosody; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Declension.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; obsolete or non-native speakers' English

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** grammar; human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The act or state of bending downward; inclination.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** declination of the head

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The act or state of falling off or declining from excellence or perfection; deterioration; decay; decline.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s8"></a>
