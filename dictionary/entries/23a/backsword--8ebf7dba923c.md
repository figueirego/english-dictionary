### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A sword with one sharp edge, especially full length such, where shorter such are called broadsword and thereof.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A stick with a basket handle, used in rustic amusements.

   **Usage:** UK; dated

   **Source synonyms:** singlestick
