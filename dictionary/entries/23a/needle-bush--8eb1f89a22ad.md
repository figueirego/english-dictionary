## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of certain shrubs with needle-like leaves.
   1. Of species Vachellia farnesiana, sweet acacia, huisache, native to the tropical Americas, now widespread in warm climates on all continents.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of certain shrubs with needle-like leaves.
   1. Of genus Hakea, especially of species Hakea lissosperma and Hakea preissii, native to Australia.
