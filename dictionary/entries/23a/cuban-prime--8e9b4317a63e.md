**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A prime number that is a solution to one of two specific equations involving third powers of x and y. The two equations are p=(x³-y³)/(x-y),x=y+1,y&gt;0 and p=(x³-y³)/(x-y),x=y+2,y&gt;0.
