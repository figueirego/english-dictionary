## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The giving of encouragement to a person who exhibits a particular behaviour with the result that such behaviour is repeated.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** The mother did not realize that giving her son candy every time he cried was positive reinforcement.
