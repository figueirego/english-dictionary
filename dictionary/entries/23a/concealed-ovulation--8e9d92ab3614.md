## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A form of ovulation, in certain species, where the female shows no perceptible signals indicating that she is fertile.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Unlike chimpanzees, whose females have bright red swollen buttocks when ovulating, females in humans evolved to have concealed ovulation, and do not have any perceptible signs that indicate their fertility.
