### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The tail of a dove (family Columbidae); also, something having the shape of a dove's tail.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In full dovetail joint&#58; a type of joint where adjoining components are fastened by multiple tenons cut into wedge shapes resembling a dove's tail, which interlock with mortises having corresponding shapes.

   **Usage:** attributive; often

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** arts; business; carpentry; construction; crafts; hobbies; lifestyle; manufacturing; woodworking

   **Source synonyms:** culvertail; fantail; swallowtail

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A tenon cut into a wedge shape resembling a dove's tail so that it interlocks with a mortise having a corresponding shape in a dovetail joint.

   **Usage:** attributive; often

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** arts; business; carpentry; construction; crafts; hobbies; lifestyle; manufacturing; woodworking

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A line resembling a dovetail joint (sense 2.1).

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; heraldry; hobbies; lifestyle; monarchy; nobility; politics

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To unite (components) with a dovetail (noun sense 2.1) or similar joint.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** arts; business; carpentry; construction; crafts; hobbies; lifestyle; manufacturing; woodworking

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To combine or fit (things) together well.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** The executive board dovetailed its decision neatly with the prior projects the company had taken up.

   **Example:** Through my new project, I dovetail my interests in botany and programming.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To interweave (a number of algorithms or subprograms) so that they can be run more or less simultaneously.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To seamlessly move a melody from one instrument to another.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. Of several things&#58; to combine or fit together well.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

   **Example:** The parts of your essay should dovetail so that it is cohesive and coherent.
