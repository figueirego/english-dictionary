### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who speaks.

   **Example:** There were three different speakers, but I couldn't make out their accents.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Loudspeaker.

   **Example:** She lost her hearing after standing too close to the speaker at the festival.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Speakerphone.

   **Example:** you're on speaker

   **Example:** I'm gonna put you on speaker now

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The chair or presiding officer of certain legislative bodies, such as the U.K. House of Commons or the U.S. House of Representatives.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; politics

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. One who makes a speech to an audience.

   **Example:** hired speaker; motivational speaker

   **Example:** The company hired a motivational speaker to boost morale.

   **Source synonyms:** speechmaker; orator; presenter; announcer

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A book containing passages of text for use in speeches.

   **Usage:** US

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The producer of a given utterance, whether speech or text.

   **Usage:** especially

   **Source context (verbatim):** in linguistics

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. The producer of a given utterance, whether speech or text.
   1. The literary character uttering the lyrics of a poem or song, as opposed to the author writing the words of that character.

      **Usage:** especially

      **Example:** Popular culture often incorrectly attributes quotes from the speakers of poems or songs to the authors thereof, as when "I took the one less traveled by" is attributed to Robert Frost rather than to the speaker in Frost's "The Road Not Taken".

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** communications; journalism; literature; media; poetry; publishing; writing

      **Source context (verbatim):** in linguistics

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A key on a woodwind instrument of the clarinet family (compare octave key on other instruments) which induces the instrument to overblow.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music
