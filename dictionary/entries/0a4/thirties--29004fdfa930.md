### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. The fourth decade of a century&#58; the 1830s, 1930s, 2030s, etc.

   **Usage:** plural; plural-only

   **Example:** It says here on your resume that your last job was as a streetcorner apple vendor and that you haven't worked since the thirties.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. The decade of one's life from age 30 through age 39.

   **Usage:** plural; plural-only

   **Example:** He looked to be in his thirties.

   **Example:** I have a mid-thirties male found down, unresponsive.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

   **Usage:** plural; plural-only; rates

   **Example:** It's going to be in the low thirties tonight.
