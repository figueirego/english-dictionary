### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A self-administered procedure comprising an attempted exhalation against a closed airway, usually performed by closing one's mouth and pinching one's nose shut while attempting, with moderate force, to expel air.

   **Example:** Variations of the Valsalva manoeuvre can be used during medical examination.
