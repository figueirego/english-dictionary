### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A picture of a celebrity, an event etc., intended to be attached to a wall.

   **Example:** He has posters of his favorite band, sports teams and holiday resorts up.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An advertisement to be posted on a pole, wall etc. to advertise something.

   **Example:** I saw a poster for the film on the side of a bus.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. One who posts a message.

   **Usage:** Internet

   **Example:** Some posters left the online message board after the squabble.

4. A shot that hits a goalpost, scoring one point.

   **Usage:** informal; Australian rules football
