### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A punk-derived subculture of people who predominantly dress in black and usually old fashioned clothing, associated with mournful and macabre music and attitudes.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Philip has been into goth for many years, haven't you dear?

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A style of punk rock influenced by glam rock; gothic rock.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A person who is part of the goth subculture.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** We saw a solitary goth hanging out on a ledge by the train station.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A member of any alternative subculture based around rock music.

   **Usage:** UK; countable; slang

   **Example:** The park often sees fights between chavs and goths.

   **Source synonyms:** emo; grebo; greeber; mosher; rocker

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Rare form of Goth.

   **Usage:** form-of; rare

   **Form of:** [Goth](../170/goth--5c27a3a6564d.md)
