## Adjective · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of or relating to a measurement system that uses the foot as a measure of distance, the pound as a measure of force, and the second as a measure of time. Abbreviations&#58; ft-lb-s, fps, f.p.s.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** imperial — of measurement system

## Noun · Record 2

### Forms

- foot-pound-seconds — plural

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A unit of mechanical power, equal to that required to raise one pound through a distance of one foot in one second.
