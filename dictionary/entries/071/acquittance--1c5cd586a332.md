### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A writing which is evidence of a discharge; a receipt in full, which bars a further demand.

   **Usage:** countable; historical; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Payment of debt; settlement.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The release from a debt, or from some obligation or duty; exemption.

   **Usage:** countable; historical; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The dismissal of a legal charge against someone; acquittal.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** absolution; acquitment; assoilment; absolution (obsolete); acquittance (obsolete); acquitment (obsolete); acquittal; assoilment (archaic); compurgation (historical); disculpation; exculpation; exoneration; not guilty; quittance; vindication
