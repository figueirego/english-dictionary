## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A noun that modifies another noun (thus functioning adjectivally) and that is grammatically optional; it may be a single word, a compound noun, or a noun phrase.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** grammar; human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** noun adjunct; noun modifier; noun premodifier; qualifying noun; apposite noun

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A noun denoting bearer of a quality or an attribute of a subject, in reference to the lexical morpheme from which it is derived.

   **Usage:** when referring to a language other than English

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** grammar; human-sciences; linguistics; sciences
