## Adjective · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In the portion of the littoral zone (the shore) that is permanently but shallowly inundated (water-covered).

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source synonyms:** neritic; infralittoral; circalittoral; subtidal
