### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An image, often a photographic negative, produced by radiation other than visible light; especially an X-ray photograph.

   **Source synonyms:** radiogram; shadowgraph

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
