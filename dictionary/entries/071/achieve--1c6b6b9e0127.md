### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To carry out or conclude (something, as a task) successfully; to accomplish.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to achieve one’s goals

   **Example:** Hannah achieved her lifelong dream of winning a medal at the Olympics.

   **Example:** You can achieve anything if you put your mind to it.

   **Source synonyms:** compass; fulfil; realize; walk away with

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To gain or obtain (a desired objective, result, etc.) through effort or skill; to succeed in attaining (an objective, result, etc.); to win.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** attain; compass; get one's hands on; register

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To obtain (a physical thing); to get.

   **Usage:** transitive; archaic except literary

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To end or finish (something); to terminate; (specifically, military) to win (a battle or war).

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** complete; conclude; finalize

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To attain a goal; to succeed in something, now especially in academic performance; (generally) to make an effort; strive.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** He could really achieve in school if he worked hard.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Followed by to&#58; to attain a certain position, status, etc.

   **Usage:** archaic; intransitive; literary

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To come to an end; conclude, end; also, to result, to turn out; or (uncommon) to be affected by fate in a certain way.

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete
