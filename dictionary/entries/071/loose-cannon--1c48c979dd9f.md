## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

- loose cannons — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A cannon that breaks loose from its moorings on a ship during battle or storm, with the potential to cause serious damage to the ship and her crew.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An uncontrolled or unpredictable person, especially considered liable to cause unintentional or indiscriminate damage.

   **Usage:** broadly; idiomatic

   **Example:** Jack is considered a loose cannon due to his volatile personality and his track record of being unable to maintain his composure.
