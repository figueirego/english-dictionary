### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A typically legal or less harmful substance, such as marijuana, whose use is statistically associated with an increased likelihood of progressing to a more dangerous or illicit drug.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any thing or area of interest that tends to lead to a deeper involvement.

   **Usage:** broadly; figuratively
