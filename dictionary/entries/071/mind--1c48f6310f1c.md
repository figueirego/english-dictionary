### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To bring or recall to mind; to remember; bear or keep in mind.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To remember.

   **Usage:** regional

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To remind; put one's mind on.

   **Usage:** dialectal; obsolete

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To turn one's mind to; to observe; to notice.

   **Example:** Mind the new detour signs. If you don't mind, you'll end up running into bears in the forest.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To regard with attention; to treat as of consequence.

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To pay attention or heed to so as to obey; hence to obey; to make sure, to take care (that).

   **Usage:** imperative

   **Example:** Mind you don't knock that glass over.

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

**Source sense R2.S7**

7. To pay attention to, in the sense of occupying one's mind with, to heed.

   **Usage:** now rare except in phrases

   **Example:** You should mind your own business.

<a id="r2-s8"></a>

**Source sense R2.S8**

8. To look after, to take care of, especially for a short period of time.

   **Example:** Would you mind my bag for me?

<a id="r2-s9"></a>

**Source sense R2.S9**

9. To be careful about.

<a id="r2-s10"></a>

**Source sense R2.S10**

10. To purpose, intend, plan.

   **Usage:** now obsolete outside dialect

<a id="r2-s11"></a>

**Source sense R2.S11**

11. Take note; used to point out an exception or caveat.

   **Usage:** Ireland; UK

   **Example:** I'm not very healthy. I do eat fruit sometimes, mind.

<a id="r2-s12"></a>

**Source sense R2.S12**

12. To dislike, to object to; to be bothered by.

   **Example:** I wouldn't mind an ice cream right now.

   **Example:** Do you mind if I smoke?

   **Source context (verbatim):** in the negative
