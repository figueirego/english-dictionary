### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A payment in full; the state of having been paid in full.

   **Example:** At the current monthly payment level, it'll take 32 more months to reach pay-off.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A reward.

   **Example:** What's the pay-off for putting up with her nonsense for ages on end?

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A return on investment.

   **Example:** How soon could we realistically expect any pay-off from loading up on shares of that company?

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A bribe.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** The prison break never could have succeeded if it hadn't been for multiple pay-offs to various corrupt officers.

   **Source synonyms:** backhander; bribe; bung; payoff (informal); coffee money (Indonesia; Malaysia; Singapore); cold drink money (South-Africa; slang); dash (Nigeria); douceur; inducement; kopi money (Indonesia; Malaysia; Singapore); pay-off; payola; subornation; sweetener; tea money (Myanmar; Thailand; slang)

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A resolution or justification of an event that has already occurred.

   **Usage:** colloquial; especially

   **Example:** Where's the pay-off for that whole segment of the plot?

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** fiction; literature; media; publishing

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Ellipsis of payoff pitch.
