**Source sense R2.S1**

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The Bishop's family fellowshipped the new converts.

   **Example:** The Society of Religious Snobs refused to fellowship the poor family.

   **Usage:** US; especially; intransitive; now chiefly religious

   **Example:** The megachurch he attends is too big for making personal connections, so he also fellowships weekly in one of the church's small groups.

   **Example:** After she got married, she stopped fellowshipping with the singles in our church.

   **Source context (verbatim):** in Canada
