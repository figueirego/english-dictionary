**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One of the parts which by their repetition make up a flowering plant, each being a single joint of a stem with its leaf or leaves.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>
