### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Often with initial capital&#58; the first day of a month.

   **Usage:** plural; plural-only

   **Example:** The third day before the calends of February is 30 January, the third calends of March is 27 or 28 February, and the third of the calends of May is 29 April.

   **Source synonyms:** Kal.; first calends (rare)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Often with initial capital&#58; the first day of a month.
   1. The first day of a month of the Roman calendar.

      **Usage:** Ancient-Rome; historical; plural; plural-only

      **Source synonyms:** Kal.; first calends (rare)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A day for settling debts and other accounts.

   **Usage:** broadly; plural; plural-only

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Synonym of Rosh Hodesh (“the Jewish festival of the new moon, which begins the months of the Hebrew calendar”).

   **Usage:** Judaism; broadly; obsolete; plural; plural-only

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biblical; lifestyle; religion

   **Source synonyms:** Rosh Hodesh (synonym; synonym-of)
