### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A transferase that catalyzes the transfer of gamma-glutamyl functional groups from molecules such as glutathione to an acceptor that may be an amino acid, a peptide or water.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
