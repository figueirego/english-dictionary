### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Robbery at sea, a violation of international law; taking a ship away from the control of those who are legally entitled to it.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; crime

   **Example:** How should the international community respond to Somali piracy?

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

   **Source synonyms:** pirateship

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A similar violation of international law, such as hijacking of an aircraft.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The unauthorized duplication of goods protected by intellectual property law.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Some video game consoles use specially designed cartridges to make software piracy more difficult.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
