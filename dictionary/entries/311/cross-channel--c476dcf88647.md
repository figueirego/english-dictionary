### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. That travels across a channel of water; relating to somewhere on the opposite side of the channel
   1. Relating to the English Channel; to, from, or on the Continent; involving both Britain and the Continent, especially France

      **Usage:** UK; not-comparable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. That travels across a channel of water; relating to somewhere on the opposite side of the channel
   1. Relating to the body of water between the islands of Great Britain and Ireland (the Irish Sea, Saint George's Channel, and the North Channel); to, from, or in the other of the two islands

      **Usage:** Ireland; dated; not-comparable
