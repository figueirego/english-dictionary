### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who makes or constructs a tree, i.e., a branching diagram or a family tree.

   **Usage:** genealogy

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; genetics; medicine; natural-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One who makes or constructs saddle trees.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** equestrianism; hobbies; horses; lifestyle; pets; sports

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. One who combines the waxes (wax molds) for multiple rings onto a wax rod in order to cast a plaster mold for making multiple rings.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A software program that constructs family trees.
