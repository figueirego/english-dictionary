### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Chiefly followed by away&#58; to spend (time) without haste or purpose.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to dawdle away the whole morning

   **Source synonyms:** fiddle away

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To spend time idly and unfruitfully; to waste time.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source synonyms:** dally; dilly-dally; take one's time; arse about; arse around; bludge (Australia; New-Zealand; slang); bum around; cabbage (slang); clown around; dally; dawdle; dick about; dick around; diddle; dilly-dally; dretch (Scotland); drumble (obsolete); faff; faff about; faff around; fanny about; fanny around; fart about; fart around; fiddle about; fiddle around; fool about; fool around; footle; forslow; goof around; goof off; hang about; hang around; hang out; horse around; idle; jerk off (vulgar); lallygag; lamp (slang); laze; laze about; laze around; linger; loaf; loaf about; loaf around; loiter; lollop; lollygag; mess around; mill about; mill around; muck about; muck around; piddle; pingle (Britain; dialectal); stand by; tarry; tool around; waste time; while

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To move or walk lackadaisically.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** If you dawdle on your daily walk, you won’t get as much exercise.

   **Source synonyms:** loiter; idle
