<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To move around something.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source synonyms:** orbit

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

2. To cause to move around something; to cause to orbit.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To turn in a circle around an axis or fixed point.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source synonyms:** revolve; rotate; spin
