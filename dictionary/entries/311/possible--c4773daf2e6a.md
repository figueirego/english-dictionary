### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Able but not certain to happen; neither inevitable nor impossible.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; usually

   **Example:** Rain tomorrow is possible, but I wouldn't bet on it.

   **Example:** It's not just possible, it's probable that there will be rain tomorrow.

   **Source synonyms:** futurable

   **Source antonyms:** certain; inevitable; impossible

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Capable of being done or achieved; feasible.

   **Usage:** comparable

   **Example:** It's possible for anyone to learn to play the bagpipes.

   **Source synonyms:** achievable; doable; viable

   **Source antonyms:** impossible

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Being considered, e.g. for a position.

   **Example:** Jones and Smith are both possible for the opening in sales.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Apparently valid, likely, plausible.

   **Source synonyms:** likely; plausible

   **Source antonyms:** implausible
