### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Synonym of dandy or tonjon, a kind of open sedan chair, particularly one with its main pole suspended from smaller poles borne on the shoulders of two pairs of porters.

   **Usage:** India; historical

   **Source synonyms:** dandy or tonjon (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Synonym of mihaffa, a kind of enclosed litter, principally for women.

   **Usage:** Malaysia; historical

   **Source synonyms:** mihaffa (synonym; synonym-of)
