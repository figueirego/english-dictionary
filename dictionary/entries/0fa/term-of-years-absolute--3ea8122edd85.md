### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An estate in land held for a term of years of either a specified length or on a periodic basis, and which is not a term of years determinable (though may be determinable for other reasons); a leasehold estate in land.

   **Usage:** England; Wales; formal; misleading

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
