## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A problem in starting or running a locomotive or train, where excess power supplied to the driven wheels causes them to turn against the rail, especially when the rails are slippery, without moving the train forward.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** rail-transport; railways; transport

   **Source synonyms:** wheelspin
