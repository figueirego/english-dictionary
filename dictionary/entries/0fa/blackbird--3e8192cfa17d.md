### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A common true thrush, Turdus merula, found in woods and gardens over much of Eurasia, and introduced elsewhere.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
