### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. The act of storing sound, video, etc., in a permanent medium.

   **Example:** The one-party consent law permits the recording of telephone calls with the consent of at least one of the parties.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. A reproduction of sound, video, etc., stored in a permanent medium.

   **Example:** I made a recording of the TV show so that I could watch it later.

**Source sense R2.S3**
