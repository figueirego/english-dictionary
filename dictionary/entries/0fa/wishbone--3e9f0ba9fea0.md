## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈwɪʃˌboʊn/ — source labels: General-American

### Forms

- wishbones — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A forked bone between the neck and breast of a bird consisting chiefly of the two clavicles fused at their median or lower end, regarded as a lucky charm in some countries.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A spar in two parts, between which a sail is hoisted, the wishbone extending its clew.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any sailing vessel rigged with a wishbone.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A particular kind of offensive formation. See wishbone formation.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** American-football; ball-games; football; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

   **Source synonyms:** bone

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A suspension component similar in shape to a chicken wishbone.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** automotive; transport; vehicles
