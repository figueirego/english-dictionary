### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To exchange for cash.

   **Example:** The gamblers cashed in their remaining chips at the end of the night.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To settle one's debts, accounts, or bills, especially relating to gambling.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** He's just mad because I haven't cashed in yet.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To profit from something; to take advantage of an opportunity in order to profit, especially financially; to capitalize on.

   **Usage:** figuratively; often

   **Example:** Lots of people tried to cash in on that market, but few succeeded.

   **Source context (verbatim):** followed by on
