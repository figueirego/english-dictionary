### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Am not.

   **Usage:** dialectal; informal; nonstandard

   **Example:** I ain’t ready yet; gimme a sec.

   **Example:** Ain’t I a woman?

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Are not, aren’t; is not, isn’t.

   **Usage:** dialectal; informal; nonstandard

   **Example:** That ain't right.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Have not, haven’t; has not, hasn’t, when used as an auxiliary.

   **Usage:** dialectal; informal; nonstandard

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Don’t, doesn’t.

   **Usage:** dialectal; informal; nonstandard

   **Example:** Well, I ain’t wanna do that though.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Didn’t.

   **Usage:** dialectal; informal; nonstandard

   **Example:** I ain’t go into the bathroom last night that late.
