### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Possible or liable, but not certain, to occur.

   **Source synonyms:** incidental; casual

   **Source antonyms:** certain; inevitable; necessary; impossible

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Dependent on something that is undetermined or unknown, that may or may not occur.

   **Example:** The success of his undertaking is contingent upon events which he cannot control.
