### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To test or try out something, especially an automobile.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** I kicked the tires and then the salesperson invited me to take the car for a spin.

   **Source synonyms:** give something a whirl

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To take, as a companion, for a drive in a motor vehicle.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** On Sunday, I took my family for a spin through the surrounding countryside.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a person
