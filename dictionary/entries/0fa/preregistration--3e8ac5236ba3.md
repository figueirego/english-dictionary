## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

- preregistrations — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An early registration, before the normal or traditional time, as with early online check-in at hotels.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Registration (of a study design) before a clinical trial (or any other type of scientific experiment) begins, for methodologic and thus epistemologic rigor.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
