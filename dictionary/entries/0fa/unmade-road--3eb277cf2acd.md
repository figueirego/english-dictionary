### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A road that lacks a hard, smooth surface like asphalt or concrete, instead consisting of natural materials like gravel, dirt, or crushed stone.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A road that has not been improved or shaped in a significant way.
