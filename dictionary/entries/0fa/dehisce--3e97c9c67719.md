### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To burst or split open at definite places, discharging seeds, pollen or similar content.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Anthers dehisce when the flower opens.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To rupture or break open, as a surgical wound.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** A surgical wound may partially or completely dehisce after surgery, depending upon whether some or all of the layers of tissue come open.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences
