## Prep\_phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Further along, in terms of time or progress.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** They decided to save money by using the cheapest components available, but down the road they ran into problems with reliability.

   **Source synonyms:** down the line; down the track; later on

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Dismissed; fired from one's employment.

   **Usage:** slang

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see down, road.

   **Example:** There's a shop down the road that sells a few basic necessities.
