### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To squeeze (wet clothing or cloth), either by twisting with one's hands, or by passing it through a wringer, to remove the water.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To force someone to give (something), usually truth, or money.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I couldn't help it. I had to tell him. He wrung it out of me.

   **Example:** They wrung an extra $500 out of us for the transfer fees.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To push (an aircraft) to its performance limits; to push the envelope.

   **Usage:** transitive
