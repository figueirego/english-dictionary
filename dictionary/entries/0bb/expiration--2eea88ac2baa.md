### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An act, process, or instance of expiring.
   1. The act or process of breathing out, or forcing air from the lungs through the nose or mouth.

      **Usage:** uncountable

      **Example:** crackles heard on expiration

      **Source synonyms:** exhalation

      **Source antonyms:** inspiration; inhalation

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An act, process, or instance of expiring.
   1. A single outward breath.

      **Usage:** countable

      **Example:** labored expirations

      **Source synonyms:** exhalation

      **Source antonyms:** inspiration; inhalation

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An act, process, or instance of expiring.
   1. Other similar release of volatile matter.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source synonyms:** emission

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An act, process, or instance of expiring.
   1. The last emission of breath; thus, synonym of death.

      **Usage:** countable; euphemistic; uncountable

      **Source synonyms:** demise; quietus; death; end; tomb (literary); celestial transfer (slang); decease; decomposition; defunction (obsolete); dematerialization; demise; dirt nap (slang); doom; exit (euphemistic; often); expiration; fatality; liquidation; mortality (figuratively); passing; quietus; repose; sleep of the just (dated; euphemistic); sunset; the big sleep; crash; downfall; fall; (the) bane; (the) end

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An act, process, or instance of expiring.
   1. Any other (metaphorically comparable) cessation, extinction, or ending.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Example:** expiration date

      **Source synonyms:** termination; end

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. An act, process, or instance of expiring.
   1. That which is produced by breathing out, as a sound.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable
