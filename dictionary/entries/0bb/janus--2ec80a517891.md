### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The god of doorways, gates and transitions, and of beginnings and endings, having two faces looking in opposite directions.

   **Usage:** Roman

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; mysticism; mythology; philosophy; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used to indicate things with two faces (such as animals with diprosopus) or aspects; or made of two different materials; or having a two-way action.

   **Usage:** attributive

   **Example:** Janus cat, Janus particle

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used to indicate things with two faces (such as animals with diprosopus) or aspects; or made of two different materials; or having a two-way action.
   1. Used to indicate an azo dye with a quaternary ammonium group, frequently with the diazo component being safranine.

      **Usage:** attributive

      **Example:** Janus green B is a dye used widely in histology to stain cells for microscopic examination.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A two-faced person, a hypocrite.

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A moon of Saturn.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A male given name from Latin.

   **Usage:** rare
