### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. False, not authentic, not genuine.

   **Example:** His argument was spurious and had no validity.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Extraneous, stray; not relevant or wanted.

   **Example:** I tried to concentrate on the matter in hand, but spurious thoughts kept intruding.

   **Example:** Spurious emissions from the wireless mast were causing nearby electrical equipment to go haywire.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Bastardly, illegitimate.

   **Usage:** archaic
