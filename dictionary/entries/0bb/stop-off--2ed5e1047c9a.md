### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make a short visit somewhere, on the way to another place, either same-day or overnight.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** It's a long drive across Texas, so we're going to stop off in Austin for a night.

   **Example:** Let's stop off at the supermarket for a second, we have already run out of milk.

   **Example:** I stopped off at Welwyn North and took some photos.

   **Source synonyms:** stop; stop by; stop in; keep (obsolete); lodge; quarter; sleep over; sojourn; stay; stay over; stop; stop off; stop over; tarry

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** transitive
