## Adjective · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /pəˈɹɛmptəɹi/ — source labels: UK

### Forms

- more peremptory — comparative
- most peremptory — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Precluding debate or expostulation; not admitting of question or appeal.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

   **Source synonyms:** positive; absolute; decisive; conclusive; final

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of a date or deadline, absolutely requiring compliance or attendance; brooking no further delay; proceeding whether a party attends or not; final.

   **Example:** As this trial has now been postponed multiple times, there will be an adjournment to April 24, peremptory on the parties.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Positive in opinion or judgment; absolutely certain, overconfident, unwilling to hear any debate or argument (especially in a pejorative sense); dogmatic.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Firmly determined, resolute; obstinate, stubborn.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Accepting no refusal or disagreement; imperious, dictatorial.
