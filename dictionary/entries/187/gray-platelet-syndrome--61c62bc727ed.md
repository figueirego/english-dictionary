## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A rare congenital bleeding disorder caused by a reduction or absence of alpha-granules in blood platelets, and the release of proteins normally contained in these granules into the marrow, resulting in myelofibrosis.

   **Usage:** uncountable
