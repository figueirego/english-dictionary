### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The person who makes a telephone call.

   **Example:** ―I’ve got someone on the line. ―Who’s the caller?

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** communications; electrical-engineering; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; telecommunications; telephony

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A visitor.

   **Example:** a gentleman caller

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The person who stands at the front of the hall and announces the numbers.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** bingo; games

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A function that calls another (the callee).

   **Example:** If the called function throws an exception, the caller should be prepared to handle the error.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; programming; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A whistle or similar item used to call foxes.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The person who directs dancers in certain dances, such as American line dances and square dances.
