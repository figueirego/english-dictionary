## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. (of a metric in a domain) Measuring distance by the infimum, over joining paths, of path length weighted by the reciprocal of distance to the domain's boundary.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

   **Extractor diagnostics (not usage labels):** empty-gloss; no-gloss

**Supplemental definition (evidence reviewed; source gap preserved):** The original quotation concerns the quasihyperbolic metric of a domain. The specialist source defines the metric through weighted lengths of rectifiable curves.

**Definition sources:** [English Wiktionary&#58; quasihyperbolic](https://en.wiktionary.org/w/index.php?title=quasihyperbolic&oldid=81253634) — 81253634; CC-BY-SA-4.0; [Qingshan Zhou, Yaxiang Li and Yuehui He&#58; Quasihyperbolic mappings in length metric spaces, Comptes Rendus Mathématique 359, 237–247](https://comptes-rendus.academie-sciences.fr/mathematique/item/10.5802/crmath.154.pdf) — 2021-04-20; DOI 10.5802/crmath.154; CC-BY-4.0
