**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** castigation; chastisement; panning; admonishment; blame; brickbatting (figuratively); castigation; censure; onus; chastisement; criticism; critique; dispraise; discommendation (archaic); panning; reprehension; vituperation

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
