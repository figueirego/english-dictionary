### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To dry utensils, dishes etc. that have been washed.

   **Usage:** Australian; British; intransitive

   **Example:** You're not watching TV until you've wiped up!

   **Source synonyms:** dry up

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To completely remove spilled liquid or solids, typically by hand using a dishcloth.

   **Example:** I'll wipe up that spilled milk.

   **Example:** First let me wipe up all these bread crumbs.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To clean thoroughly, particularly with a dishcloth or rag.

   **Example:** I always wipe up the counter before making dinner.

   **Example:** Please wipe up the kitchen at the end of your shift.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To defeat (an opponent) thoroughly; trounce.

   **Usage:** US; figuratively; slang; transitive

   **Example:** We're going to wipe up your team just like we wiped up the Canaries.
