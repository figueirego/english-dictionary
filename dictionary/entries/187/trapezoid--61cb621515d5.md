**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A (convex) quadrilateral with two (non-adjacent) parallel sides.

   **Usage:** Canada; US

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** trapezium

   **Usage:** Australia; New-Zealand; UK

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** irregular quadrilateral; trapezium

<a id="r1-s3"></a>
