### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To carry forward the stake and winnings from a bet on to a subsequent wager or series of wagers.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** gambling; games

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To increase (an asset, money, etc.) by gambling or investing in a daring manner.

   **Usage:** broadly; transitive

   **Example:** Shall we parlay the value of our products?

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To convert (a situation, thing, etc.) into something better.

   **Usage:** broadly; transitive; generally

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

   **Usage:** alt-of; alternative; intransitive
