**Source sense R2.S1**

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** grammar; human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

2. A word in the form in which it potentially refers to something other than one person or thing; and other than two things if the language has a dual form.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** The plural of 'cat' is 'cats', but the plural of 'child' is 'children'.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** grammar; human-sciences; linguistics; sciences
