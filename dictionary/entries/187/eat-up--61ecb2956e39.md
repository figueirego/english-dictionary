## Verb

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To consume completely.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** He was so hungry that he ate up everything on his plate.

   **Example:** Dinner is served. Eat up!

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To cause (someone) to obsess; to figuratively consume (someone).

   **Usage:** figuratively; slang; transitive

   **Example:** His anger has been eating him up.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To subtract, use up.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** His behavior has eaten up all the goodwill we felt towards him.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To accept or believe entirely, immediately, and without questioning.

   **Usage:** figuratively; informal; transitive

   **Example:** She ate up everything that her image consultant said.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To acclaim or praise (someone or something); to consume (absorb information).

   **Usage:** slang; transitive

   **Example:** They eat up every book he puts out.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To find something to be very cute.

   **Usage:** US; informal; transitive

   **Example:** You're so cute, I could just eat you up!

   **Source context (verbatim):** of children or pets

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To go quickly on a route.

   **Usage:** figuratively; slang; transitive

   **Example:** We ate up the road to Pennsylvania.

   **Example:** You're gonna have to eat up the whole way there.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To be very good at; to succeed at; to smash. (Compare eat and leave no crumbs.)

   **Usage:** slang; transitive

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To completely dominate someone else, especially with a comeback or clapback.

   **Usage:** informal; slang

   **Example:** Girl, you ate him up!
