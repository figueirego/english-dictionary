### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. a type of bus stop, usually at the terminus of a route, where a bus can wait between journeys. Buses normally do not pick up passengers there, rather boarding takes place at a designated stop nearby.

   **Usage:** UK

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** transport

<a id="r1-s2"></a>
