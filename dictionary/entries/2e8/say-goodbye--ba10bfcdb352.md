### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To wish someone farewell upon their leaving.

   **Usage:** literal sense

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To separate from someone or something.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** If we can't agree on whether to have children, maybe it's time to say goodbye.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To cease to have, or have to do, something.

   **Usage:** figuratively; idiomatic

   **Example:** Say goodbye to long waits at the airport with our new preboarding service.

   **Example:** You can say goodbye to your inheritance if you annoy your parents too much!
