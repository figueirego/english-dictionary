### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person with old-fashioned views; hence, one who is very conservative or reactionary; a mossback.

   **Usage:** US; informal

   **Source synonyms:** conservative; reactionary; retrograde; traditionalist

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Synonym of mossback (“a person who stayed hidden to evade conscription (especially by the Confederate States Army) during the American Civil War (1861–1865)”).

   **Usage:** US; historical; informal; obsolete

   **Source synonyms:** mossback (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Senses relating to animals.
   1. A fish that is large and old, especially one that has algae growing on its back; a mossback.

      **Usage:** US; obsolete

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** fishing; hobbies; lifestyle
