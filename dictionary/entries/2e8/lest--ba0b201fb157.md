### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. For fear that; that not; in order to prevent something from happening; in case.

   **Usage:** formal; literary

   **Example:** He won't go outside, lest he be eaten by those ravenous eagles.

   **Example:** I brought my notes lest faulty memory lead me astray.

   **Source synonyms:** before (informal)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. that (without the negative particle; introduces the reason for an emotion.)

   **Usage:** formal; literary

   **Example:** There was danger/alarm lest the plan become known.

   **Example:** I am afraid lest I revealed too much.

   **Source context (verbatim):** after certain expressions denoting fear or apprehension
