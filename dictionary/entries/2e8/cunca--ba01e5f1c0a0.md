## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A traditional Galician unit of dry measure, equivalent to about 1–1.5 L depending on the substance measured.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A traditional Galician unit of land area reckoned as the amount needed to sow a cunca of seed, varying from 5–60 m² in different parts of Galicia.

   **Usage:** historical
