### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A straight line touching a curve at a single point without crossing it there.

   **Usage:** differential geometry

   **Source synonyms:** tangent line

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A function of an angle that gives the ratio of the sine to the cosine, in either the real or complex numbers. Symbols&#58; tan, tg.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A course suddenly and unexpectedly divergent from one which was previously being followed (especially one embarked on before reaching the end of the previous course).

   **Usage:** transferred sense

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A course suddenly and unexpectedly divergent from one which was previously being followed (especially one embarked on before reaching the end of the previous course).
   1. A new topic of discussion unrelated to the one which came before it, arrived at due to a single (peripheral or trivial) point in common with it.

      **Usage:** transferred sense

      **Example:** I believe we went off onto a tangent when we started talking about monkeys on unicycles at his retirement party.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A visual interaction between two or more lines or edges that creates a perceived relationship between them, often in a way that the artist did not intend.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** art; arts

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A small metal blade in a clavichord or other instrument that strikes the strings to produce sound.
