### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. With insufficient light for easy discernment or comprehension.

   **Example:** I could make out his image darkly.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. With a dark appearance.

   **Source synonyms:** blackly; sably

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. In a manner which is not readily visible or noticeable; inconspicuously.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Source synonyms:** anonymously; obscurely; unapparently; unnoticeably

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. In a manner which retards or prevents discernment; clandestinely.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Source synonyms:** covertly; furtively; inconspicuously; secretly; stealthily; surreptitiously

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. In a manner which is difficult to understand, or which retards or prevents understanding; incomprehensibly.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Source synonyms:** fathomlessly; inscrutably; mysteriously; obscurely; vaguely

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. In a manner which tends to produce uncertainty or confusion; bewilderingly.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Source synonyms:** bafflingly; perplexingly; puzzlingly

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. In a manner which produces an inward conviction of future misfortune; ominously.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Source synonyms:** forebodingly; threateningly

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. In a morbid manner; morbidly, sinisterly.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** a darkly comic dystopian drama
