### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Attestation of a fact or event; testimony.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** She can bear witness, since she was there at the time.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One who sees or has personal knowledge of something.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** As a witness to the event, I can confirm that he really said that.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Someone called to give evidence in a court.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** The witness for the prosecution did not seem very credible.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. One who is called upon to witness an event or action, such as a wedding or the signing of a document.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** The bridesmaid and best man at a wedding typically serve as the witnesses.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Something that serves as evidence; a sign or token.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A particular version of a text (seen as providing testimony of archetype or other earlier version)

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences; textual-criticism

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. An additional database server instance used in failover scenarios to decide whether the mirror should take over.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; databases; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

## Verb · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈwɪt.nɪs/ — source labels: General-American; Received-Pronunciation
- IPA: /ˈwɪt.nəs/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

- witnessing — participle; present
- witnessed — participle; past
- witnessed — past
- witnesse — alternative; obsolete
- witneße — alternative; obsolete

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To furnish proof of, to show.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** This certificate witnesses his presence on that day.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To take as evidence.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To see or gain knowledge of through experience.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He witnessed the accident.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To present personal religious testimony; to preach at (someone) or on behalf of.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source context (verbatim):** construed with to or for

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To see the execution of (a legal instrument), and subscribe it for the purpose of establishing its authenticity.

   **Example:** to witness a bond or a deed
