**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** As a child she had nothing to read but the legends of early Christian martyrs.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   1. A story of unknown origin describing plausible but extraordinary past events.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Example:** The legend of Troy was discovered to have a historical basis.

      **Source synonyms:** myth

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

   1. A story in which a kernel of truth is embellished to an unlikely degree.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source synonyms:** myth; tall tale
