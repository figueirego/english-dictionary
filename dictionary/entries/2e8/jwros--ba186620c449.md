### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Initialism of Joint with Rights of Survivorship Indicating that a property is owned by two or more individuals, usually in equal portions, and that, on the death of one individual, the property will continue to be owned by the surviving individual(s).

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; initialism

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

   **Alternative form of:** Joint with Rights of Survivorship Indicating that a property is owned by two or more individuals; usually in equal portions; [and that](../21d/and-that--877bd491f913.md); on the death of one individual
