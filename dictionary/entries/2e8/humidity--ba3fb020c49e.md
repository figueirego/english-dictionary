### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Dampness, especially that of the air.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** The high humidity made the air feel sticky and hot.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The amount of water vapour in the air.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** Plants in the greenhouse thrive in constant humidity.
