## Prep\_phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Situated in the upper half of the front page of a broadsheet newspaper, and thus more prominent, as the lower half is not usually visible when the folded newspaper is displayed for sale.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** journalism; media; newspapers

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. By extension, situated near the top of a web page or application screen; not requiring scrolling. See above the scroll.

   **Usage:** web and application design

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Occupying an exclusive position of relative prominence
