**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Representing a scream or shriek (especially in comic strips and books).

   **Usage:** onomatopoeic

   **Example:** Eek! There's a mouse in the bathtub!

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Expressing (sometimes mock) fear or surprise.

   **Usage:** onomatopoeic

   **Example:** I almost got fired from my job yesterday. Eek!

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Representing the shrill vocal sound of a mouse, rat, or monkey.

   **Usage:** onomatopoeic
