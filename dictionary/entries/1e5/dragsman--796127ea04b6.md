## Noun

### Forms

- dragsmen — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A driver of a carriage, coach, or drag, for public transport, private hire, or as a household servant; coachman.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One who races horses; an amateur jockey.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A thief who cuts the luggage from carriages.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. One who drags a body of water in search of something that is submerged.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. One who moves the carts or sledges at a mine; a putter.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. One who lays down the scent trail for a hunt.
