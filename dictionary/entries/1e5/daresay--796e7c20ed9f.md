### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Chiefly in the form I daresay&#58; to say something boldly; to affirm or assert.

   **Usage:** archaic; intransitive; originally

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Chiefly in the form I daresay&#58; to venture to say (as the speaker believes something is likely to be the case); to think something probable; to presume.

   **Usage:** broadly; intransitive

   **Example:** Are you going to stay up all night? I daresay that I am.
