### Senses

<a id="r3-s1"></a>

**Source sense R3.S1**

1. to flow back or recede

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The tides ebbed at noon.

   **Source synonyms:** go out; go down; ooze; reflux; wane

<a id="r3-s2"></a>

**Source sense R3.S2**

2. to fall away or decline

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The dying man's strength ebbed away.

<a id="r3-s3"></a>

**Source sense R3.S3**

3. to fish with stakes and nets that serve to prevent the fish from getting back into the sea with the ebb

   **Usage:** intransitive
