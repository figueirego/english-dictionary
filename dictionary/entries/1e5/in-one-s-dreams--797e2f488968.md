## Prep\_phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used to express the speaker's belief that a preceding statement expressed a desired rather than an actual state of affairs.

   **Usage:** colloquial; idiomatic

   **Example:** "Can't go? Why not? Got a hot date tonight?" / "In my dreams! No, I've got to visit a friend in the hospital."

   **Example:** "They said the economy will boost sales next quarter." / "In their dreams! Maybe next year the economy will recover."
