### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To heat from below.

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To intentionally operate a boiler, furnace, oven, etc., at a low level.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Usage:** intransitive
