### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any person or thing that brings stability.
   1. Any substance added to something in order to stabilize it, as for example a fuel additive that prolongs the shelf life of petrol.

      **Usage:** British; English; Oxford; US

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any person or thing that brings stability.
   1. An additional wheel on a bicycle to keep it upright to assist a learner.

      **Usage:** British; English; Oxford; US

      **Source synonyms:** training wheel

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any person or thing that brings stability.
   1. A gyroscopically controlled fin or similar device that prevents the excess rolling of a ship in rough seas.

      **Usage:** British; English; Oxford; US

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any person or thing that brings stability.
   1. An airfoil that stabilizes the flight of an aircraft or missile.

      **Usage:** British; English; Oxford; US

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Any person or thing that brings stability.
   1. A piece of backing fabric used in embroidery.

      **Usage:** British; English; Oxford; US

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; manufacturing; textiles

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. For a group operating on a set and an element x of the set, the set of all group elements fixing x.

   **Usage:** British; English; Oxford; US

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences
