## Preposition

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. \[Of one variable or unit of measurement which has a known relationship to another\] Using equivalent expressions that use one variable in place of expressions involving the other.

   **Example:** If x=2y then 5x can be expressed in terms of y as 10y.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In relation to a particular aspect; with respect to; as regards; concerning; regarding.

   **Example:** I switched to a different bank for better customer service, but there was little difference in terms of interest rates.

   **Example:** She has no idea how she wants the house to look in terms of décor.

   **Example:** First we consider things people naturally think in terms of, and then represent them computationally.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. As an approach or way of proceeding.
