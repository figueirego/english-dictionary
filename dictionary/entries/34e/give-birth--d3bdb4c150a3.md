### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To release live offspring from the body into the environment.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** It was clear that she was about to give birth.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of viviparous animals

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To become the parent of by birthing.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** She gave birth to a beautiful baby girl.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of viviparous animals; with to

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To become the source of.

   **Usage:** figuratively; idiomatic; transitive; usually

   **Example:** Einstein gave birth to a famous equation relating energy to mass.

   **Source context (verbatim):** with to
