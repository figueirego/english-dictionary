## Noun

### Pronunciation

### Forms

- place of birth — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The location where a person or an animal was born.

   **Example:** Portsmouth was Charles Dickens’ birthplace.

   **Source synonyms:** place of birth

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The location where something was created or devised.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** Coney Island was the birthplace of the hot dog.
