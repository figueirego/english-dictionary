### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To do as ordered by (a person, institution etc), to act according to the bidding of.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** obey the rules

   **Example:** obey your boss

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To do as one is told.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Soldiers are trained to obey.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To be obedient, compliant (to a given law, restriction etc.).

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete
