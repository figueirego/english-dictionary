### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Return to where one came from.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** When I get back from holiday, I expect the house to be tidy.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Reply (to someone); follow up (with someone).

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** I'll get back to you on that by Wednesday.

   **Example:** Please get back to me about his complaint. \["to" here connotes "reply".\]

   **Example:** Please get back with me about his complaint. \["with" here connotes "meet with", physically or virtually.\]

   **Source context (verbatim):** with with or to

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To retrieve (something); to have (something) returned.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I lent her my guitar over the Christmas holidays, and will get it back when the term starts.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To do something to hurt or harm (someone) who has hurt or harmed one; to take revenge.

   **Usage:** often; transitive

   **Example:** I'll get you back for this!

   **Example:** I'll get him back for this!

   **Example:** I'll get back at all of them for this!

   **Source context (verbatim):** with at or against

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To repay; to return the favor.

   **Usage:** Canada; US; colloquial; transitive
