### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An imaginary line around which an object spins (an axis of rotation) or is symmetrically arranged (an axis of symmetry).

   **Example:** The Earth rotates once a day on its axis

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A fixed one-dimensional figure, such as a line or arc, with an origin and orientation and such that its points are in one-to-one correspondence with a set of numbers; an axis forms part of the basis of a space or is used to position and locate data in a graph (a coordinate axis)

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The second cervical vertebra of the spine

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; medicine; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** epistropheus

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An imaginary, visualized plane separating two morphologically similar parts of an organism

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A form of classification and descriptions of mental disorders or disabilities used in manuals such as the DSM (Diagnostic and Statistical Manual of Mental Disorders)

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; medicine; psychiatry; psychology; sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The main stem or central part about which organs or plant parts such as branches are arranged

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. An alliance or coalition.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war

   **Source synonyms:** pact; compact; league

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. The centre of attention within a process (e.g. the axis of investigation)

   **Usage:** figuratively
