### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A coastal town in North Yorkshire, England (OS grid ref TA040880). It should now be a civil parish, with a town council elected on 1 May 2025.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The Borough of Scarborough, a former local government district in North Yorkshire, abolished without replacement in 2023.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A district and former municipality of Toronto, Canada.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A town in Cumberland County, Maine, United States.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

5. A neighbourhood in the village of Briarcliff Manor, Westchester County, New York, United States, named after the English town.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
