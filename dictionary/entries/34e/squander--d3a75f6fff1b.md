### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To waste, lavish, splurge; to spend lavishly or profusely; to dissipate.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Dost thou love life? Then do not squander time, for that's the stuff life is made of.

   **Source synonyms:** waste; lavish; splurge; slather; throw away; make ducks and drakes of; play ducks and drakes with; blow

   **Source antonyms:** conserve; hoard; save

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To scatter; to disperse.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive
