### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The action of reconstructing something, not necessarily to the earlier state.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The act of restoring something to an earlier state.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The reconstruction of the medieval bridge began last year.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The recreation or retelling of the (purported) events leading up to a certain outcome.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The detective's reconstruction of what happened that night is dubious.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A result of linguistic reconstruction; a model representing an unattested linguistic unit&#58; a phoneme, a morpheme or a word.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences
