### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An abstract mathematical object generalizing the relationship between spaces of functions and the germs of those functions at particular points. Formally, a topological space equipped with a sheaf of rings (called the structure sheaf).

   **Usage:** sheaf theory; scheme theory; sheaf theory; scheme theory
