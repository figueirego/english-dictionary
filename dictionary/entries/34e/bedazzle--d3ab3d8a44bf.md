### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To confuse or disarm by dazzling.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He was bedazzled by her charm and wit.

   **Source synonyms:** daze

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To decorate with sequins or other sparkly material; to bespangle.

   **Usage:** informal; transitive

   **Example:** She bedazzled her handbag.

   **Source synonyms:** embellish
