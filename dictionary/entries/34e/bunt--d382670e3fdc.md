### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The middle part, cavity, or belly of a sail; the part of a furled sail which is at the center of the yard.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The bunt of the sail was green.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A push or shove; a butt.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A ball that has been intentionally hit softly so as to be difficult to field, sometimes with a hands-spread batting stance or with a close-hand, choked-up hand position. No swinging action is involved.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The bunt was fielded cleanly.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; baseball; games; hobbies; lifestyle; softball; sports

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The act of bunting.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The manager will likely call for a bunt here.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; baseball; games; hobbies; lifestyle; softball; sports

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The second half of an outside loop, from level flight to inverted flight.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** aeronautics; aerospace; aviation; business; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences
