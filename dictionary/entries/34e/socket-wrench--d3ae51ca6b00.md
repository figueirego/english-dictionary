## Noun

### Forms

- socket wrenches — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A type of wrench (spanner) featuring multiple removable and interchangeable socket heads (sockets), each designed to fit a particular size of bolt head or other fastener, which can be attached by the drive to a ratcheting handle, breaker bar, or other driver.

   **Source synonyms:** ratchet wrench

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any wrench, including a nonratcheting one with an integral (nonremovable) head, that has an internal/concave feature (a socket) that engages the head of an external-wrenching fastener.

   **Usage:** archaic
