## Noun

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /əbˈsɛʃən/ — source labels: UK; US

### Forms

- obsessions — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The quality of being obsessed; an unshakeable feeling that one topic or activity must be pursued above other interests.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** My obsession with rugby resulted in me spending years following the sport.

   **Source synonyms:** obsessedness

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An idea that engenders a compulsive or irrational preoccupation, or the preoccupation thereby engendered.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** The downfall of the chairman is an obsession that consumes Laura's time.

   **Source synonyms:** idée fixe

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An activity or entity that inspires a compulsive and potentially unhealthy fixation, or the fixation thereby inspired.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** Editing Wikipedia started as a hobby, but it's turned into a complete obsession of mine.

   **Example:** His current obsession is getting that law passed.

   **Source synonyms:** kick (informal)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Influence or control by evil spirits without possession.

   **Usage:** uncountable
