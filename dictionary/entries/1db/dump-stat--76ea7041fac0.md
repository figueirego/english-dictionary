### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A statistic (of a player character skill or other property, etc.) where a poor value is accepted in order to allow for improved statistics in other areas.

   **Usage:** roleplaying games

   **Example:** Charisma is a common dump stat for players who want to give their PCs better strength or intelligence.
