### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Not broadcasting or transmitting on radio or television.

   **Example:** Channel 9 will be off air from 11 pm until 6 am.

   **Example:** The transmitter is off air after a fire.

   **Source synonyms:** off the air

   **Source antonyms:** on air

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Not being, or so as not to be, broadcast or transmitted by radio or television.

   **Example:** After ten years, the TV series was taken off air.

   **Example:** Fortunately his offensive remarks were off air.

   **Source synonyms:** dark; silent
