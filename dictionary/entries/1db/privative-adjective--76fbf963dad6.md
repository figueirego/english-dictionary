## Noun

### Forms

- privative adjectives — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An adjective used to show that a noun refers to something outside the category that the noun normally represents.

   **Example:** That's fake news.

   **Example:** Isn't that a toy sword?

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** grammar; human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An adjective indicating that a noun lacks some other noun, or a quality of that noun; usually formed in English by the affixation of the suffix -less or suffixoid -free to the noun that is absent.

   **Example:** finger + -less → fingerless

   **Example:** hope + -less → hopeless

   **Example:** heart + -less → heartless

   **Example:** sugar + -free → sugar-free

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** grammar; human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

   **Source antonyms:** possessional adjective
