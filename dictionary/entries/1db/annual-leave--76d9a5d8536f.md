## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Amount of time expressed as the number of days per year that an employee is entitled to be away from work.

   **Usage:** uncountable; workplace

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
