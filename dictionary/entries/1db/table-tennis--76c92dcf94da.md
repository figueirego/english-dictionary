## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A game or sport (similar to tennis) that involves the hitting of a light plastic ball with rackets (called paddles, or bats in Britain) across a table fashioned like a miniature tennis court.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** ping pong; flim-flam (archaic); whiff-whaff (archaic); gossima
