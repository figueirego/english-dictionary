### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To utter a high-pitched cry.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To make a sound resembling such a cry.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The jet engines whined at take off.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To complain or protest with a whine (compare whinge) or as if with a whine, i.e. in a peevish or self-pitying way.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To move with a whining sound.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The jet whined into the air.

   **Example:** The wind whined and moaned through the trees.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To utter with the sound of a whine.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The child whined all his complaints.

   **Example:** Kelly Queen was whining that the boss made him put on his tie.
