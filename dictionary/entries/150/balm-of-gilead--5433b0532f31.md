## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A rare perfume produced in Gilead and used medicinally, mentioned in the Bible, sometimes thought to have derived from the plant Commiphora gileadensis, and sometimes from a terebinth tree in the genus Pistacia.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** balm of Judea; balm of Mecca; balsam of Gilead; balsam of Judea; balsam of Mecca

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A remedy believed to cure all disease; a panacea.

   **Usage:** figuratively; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any plant of the species Cedronella canariensis, family Lamiaceae, native to Macaronesia.

   **Usage:** uncountable
