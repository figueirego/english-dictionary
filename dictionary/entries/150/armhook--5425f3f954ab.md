### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A hook-like projection on the arm of a cephalopod, such as on the armhook squid (Gonatus fabricii).

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A movement in which two people hook arms at the elbow and turn around each other.

   **Usage:** folk dancing

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A hook for maneuvering something using the arm.
