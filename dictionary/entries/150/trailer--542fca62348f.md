## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

### Forms

- trailers — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Someone who or something that trails (follows behind); something that is trailing.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Part of an object which extends some distance beyond the main body of the object.

   **Example:** the trailer of a plant

   **Source synonyms:** appendage; attachment; appendix; extension; extrusion

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An unpowered wheeled vehicle (not a caravan or camper) that is towed behind another, and used to carry equipment, etc., that cannot be carried in the leading vehicle.

   **Example:** At the end of the day, we put the snowmobiles back on the trailer.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A furnished vehicle towed behind another, and used as a dwelling when stationary.

   **Usage:** US

   **Example:** We drove our trailer to Yellowstone Park.

   **Source synonyms:** camper; caravan; travel trailer

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A prefabricated home that could be towed to a new destination but is typically permanently left in an area designated for such homes.

   **Usage:** US

   **Example:** The young couple′s first home was in a trailer.

   **Source synonyms:** mobile home

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A preview of a film, video game or TV show.

   **Usage:** US

   **Example:** The trailer for that movie makes it seem like it would be fun.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** media

   **Source synonyms:** preview; teaser

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A short blank segment of film at the end of a reel, for convenient insertion of the film in a projector.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. The final record of a list of data items, often identified by a key field with an otherwise invalid value that sorts last alphabetically (e.g., “ZZZZZ”) or numerically (“99999”); especially common in the context of punched cards, where the final card is called a trailer card.

   **Example:** The linked list terminates with a trailer record.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** sentinel

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. The last part of a packet, often containing a check sequence.

   **Example:** The encapsulation layer adds an eight-byte header and a two-byte trailer to each packet.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; networking; physical-sciences; sciences

   **Source antonyms:** header
