## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A box containing a telephone for use in emergency, usually mounted on a post in an area where normal access to telephones is inconvenient (such as on an interstate highway or a very long highway bridge)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The wall-mounted box allowing a person to speak into an intercom

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Synonym of telephone box (enclosure)

   **Usage:** Ireland; UK
