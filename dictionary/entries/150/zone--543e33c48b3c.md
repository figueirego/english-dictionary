### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To divide into or assign to sections or areas.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Please zone off our staging area, a section for each group.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To define the property use classification of (an area).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** This area was zoned for industrial use.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To enter a daydream state temporarily, for instance as a result of boredom, fatigue, or intoxication; to doze off.

   **Usage:** intransitive; slang

   **Example:** I must have zoned while he was giving us the directions.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To girdle or encircle.

   **Usage:** archaic; poetic; transitive

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To assign to a restricted category.

   **Example:** We've zoned each other as friends; we'll never be anything more.

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** zone out — enter a daydream state; doze off
