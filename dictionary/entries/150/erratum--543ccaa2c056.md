### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A published notice reporting an error belatedly discovered in a previous publication.

   **Example:** When the journal's editors found out about the misreported details, they issued an erratum.

   **Source synonyms:** corrigendum

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An error, especially one in a printed work.
