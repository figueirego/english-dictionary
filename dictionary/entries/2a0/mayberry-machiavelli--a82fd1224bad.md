### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who furthers a cause by "reducing every issue to its simplest, black-and-white terms for public consumption", i.e., denying nuance by presenting stark but fallacious alternatives.

   **Usage:** derogatory

   **Example:** That Mayberry Machiavelli thinks that anybody who didn't support the Iraq war is a Saddam Hussein supporter.
