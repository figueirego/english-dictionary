### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A narrow tube through which a jet of air is directed onto a flame; used in the analysis of minerals etc and in jewelry manufacture.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
