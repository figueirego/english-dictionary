### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A number that is the sum of all of its divisors except itself.

   **Example:** The factors of 6 are 1, 2, 3 and 6, and 1 + 2 + 3 = 6, so 6 is a perfect number.

   **Example:** The factors of 28 are 1, 2, 4, 7, 14 and 28, and 1 + 2 + 4 + 7 + 14 = 28, so 28 is a perfect number.
