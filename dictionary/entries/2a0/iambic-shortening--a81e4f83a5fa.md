### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In Latin, the shortening of an etymologically long final vowel, in a two-syllable word where the first vowel is also short. It tended to persist in uninflected words like bene, but was levelled out in favour of inflectional regularity in other words.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
