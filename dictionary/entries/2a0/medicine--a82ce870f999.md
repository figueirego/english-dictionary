### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A substance which specifically promotes healing when ingested or consumed in some way; a pharmaceutical drug.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** This medicine has fewer adverse effects than others in its drug class.

   **Example:** Using a weekly pill organizer is a good way to help remind yourself to take your medicine each day, and it also tells you whether you already took today's pills (it's not unusual to forget doing a habitual task)!

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any treatment or cure.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable

   **Example:** A legislative remedy might be some harsh medicine; is that cure worse than the ill?

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The study of the cause, diagnosis, prognosis and treatment of disease or illness.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** She's studying medicine at university because she wants to be a doctor in the future.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The profession and practice of physicians, including surgeons.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** The history of medicine can be discretized into eras with differing relationships between physicians and surgeons

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The profession and practice of physicians, including surgeons.
   1. Scientific medicine.

      **Usage:** countable; often; specifically; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The profession and practice of physicians, including surgeons.
   1. The profession and practice of nonsurgical physicians as sometimes distinguished from that of surgeons.

      **Usage:** historical; mainly; uncountable

      **Example:** the evolving relationship of medicine to surgery in the nineteenth century

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Ritual magic used, as by a medicine man, to promote a desired outcome in healing, hunting, or warfare; traditional medicine.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Among the Native Americans, a spiritual power, or any object or person through whom such power is channeled, believed to grant influence over natural or magical forces, provide protection, or effect healing.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. Black magic, superstition.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. A philter or love potion.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. A physician.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s12"></a>
