### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who snoozes; a sleeper.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Something that is boring, uninteresting or unremarkable; something that would tend to induce sleep.

   **Usage:** colloquial

   **Example:** The program is usually pretty good, but last Wednesday's episode was a real snoozer.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A thief who steals from hotel rooms while staying as a hotel guest.

   **Usage:** UK; obsolete; thieves' cant
