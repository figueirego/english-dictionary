### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To take back possession of (something)

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive

   **Example:** Ladies and gentlemen, please resume your seats.

   **Source synonyms:** regain

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To summarise.

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To start (something) again that has been stopped or paused from the point at which it was stopped or paused; continue, carry on.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** We will resume this discussion tomorrow at nine.

   **Source antonyms:** suspend

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To start again after an interruption or pause.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Normal service has resumed.
