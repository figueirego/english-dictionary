## Noun

### Forms

- deerskins — plural
- deer-skin — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Leather made from deer hide.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** The moccasin maker had a pile of deerskin on the table, waiting to be cut out.

   **Example:** Nichole wore a pair of deerskin gloves because she said she could feel more through them.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The hide, whether tanned or not, of one deer.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** The hunter exchanged twenty deerskins for a bottle of whiskey at the trading post.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An article of clothing manufactured from deerskin. Often constructed in the plural.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** The trapper wore deerskins because they were softer than cow leather garments and rustled less than canvas.
