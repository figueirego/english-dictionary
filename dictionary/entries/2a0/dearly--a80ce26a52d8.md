### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In a dear or precious manner.

   **Example:** the funeral of our dearly beloved sister

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In a dear or expensive manner.

   **Example:** a dearly priced item

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. At great expense; dear.

   **Example:** He paid dearly for his mistake.
