## Adverb

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In an amazing manner; in a way that causes amazement; wonderfully.

   **Example:** That violin solo was played amazingly.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Difficult to believe; strange but true.

   **Example:** Amazingly, no one was injured in the crash.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To a wonder-inspiring extent.

   **Example:** The car has amazingly low fuel consumption.
