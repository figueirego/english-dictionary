### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An ancient divination method of the Highland Scots involving animal sacrifice.
   1. A method of divination involving wrapping a person in the hide of a freshly-killed ox which was then placed beside a waterfall or other desolate place, to enable the person to foresee the outcome of an impending battle; the oracle of the hide.

      **Usage:** Scotland; historical; uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An ancient divination method of the Highland Scots involving animal sacrifice.
   1. A method of divination in which cats were roasted alive to call up the spirit of the demon cat who would grant the wishes of the torturers.

      **Usage:** Scotland; historical; uncountable; usually
