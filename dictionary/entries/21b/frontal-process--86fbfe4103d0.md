### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A long plate that is part of the maxilla and contributes to the formation of the lateral part of the nose and of the nasal cavity.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; medicine; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** nasal process
