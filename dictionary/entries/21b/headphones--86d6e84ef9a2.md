### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A pair of cushioned speakers worn over or in the ears so only the wearer can hear the sound.

   **Usage:** plural; plural-normally

   **Example:** Circumaural headphones enclose the ear, supra-aural headphones rest on the ear's surface, and earbuds are placed inside the ear.

   **Source synonyms:** cans; heads
