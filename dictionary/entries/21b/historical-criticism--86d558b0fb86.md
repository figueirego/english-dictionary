### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The systematic study of received texts, especially the Bible, as documents composed in a particular historical context with varying reliability as sources.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** higher criticism

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The systematic, reasoned study of history and critique of received historical traditions in general.

   **Usage:** archaic; uncountable
