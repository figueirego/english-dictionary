### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of several insects in the superfamily Cicadoidea, with small eyes wide apart on the head and transparent well-veined wings.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of several insects in the superfamily Cicadoidea, with small eyes wide apart on the head and transparent well-veined wings.
   1. The periodical cicada.
