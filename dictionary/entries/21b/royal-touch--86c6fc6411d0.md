## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The touching of a diseased person by the hand of a king, with the supposed purpose of restoring health. It was extensively practiced in England and France, particularly for scrofula.

   **Source synonyms:** king's touch
