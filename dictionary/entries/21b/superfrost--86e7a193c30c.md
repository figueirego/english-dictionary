### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Groundwater that is found immediately above the permafrost layer, which seasonally thaws, but is kept frozen more of the year due to the cold from the permafrost layer.

   **Usage:** countable; rare; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An exceptionally severe frost.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
