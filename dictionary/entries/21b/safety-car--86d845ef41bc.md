### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A vehicle employed to lead the competitors in an auto race for the duration of a few laps, with the intention of restricting their speed while a hazard exists on the circuit.

   **Example:** After a car crashed, the safety car was sent out to lead the remaining drivers safely past the site of the accident.
