### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Moving in the same direction as the overall state of an economy

   **Example:** The employment rate is procyclical, when the economy is doing well more people are working, when it's not doing well, fewer people are working.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** economics; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Magnifying the cyclical fluctuations of an economy.

   **Usage:** public policy
