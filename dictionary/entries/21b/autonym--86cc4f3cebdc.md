### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An infraspecific name in which the specific epithet is repeated.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences; taxonomy

   **Source synonyms:** type variety

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A name used by a group or category of people to refer to themselves or their language, as opposed to a name given to them by other groups.

   **Example:** Deutsche is the autonym of the people known in English as Germans.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anthropology; human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** endonym; selfname

   **Source antonyms:** exonym; xenonym

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The true name of a person or other entity, especially an author.

   **Source synonyms:** orthonym; alethonym

   **Source antonyms:** pseudonym; allonym; alias; pen name; nom de plume; nom de clavier; nom de Web; stage name; nom de guerre

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A work published under the author's true name.

   **Source antonyms:** allonym
