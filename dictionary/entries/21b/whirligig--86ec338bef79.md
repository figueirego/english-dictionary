### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Anything that whirls or spins around, such as a toy top or a merry-go-round.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A device incorporating spinning, wind-driven propellers or pinwheels, used as whimsical outdoor decoration in a garden or on a porch.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Ellipsis of whirligig beetle.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; ellipsis

   **Alternative form of:** [whirligig beetle](../352/whirligig-beetle--d4bfb38f747a.md)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A device for punishing prisoners, comprising a wooden cage that rapidly spins around to induce nausea.

   **Usage:** historical
