### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A shallow or more or less intermittent spring, or the stream of water (bourne) which feeds and springs forth from such a spring.

   **Usage:** UK; dialectal

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A violent flow or rush of water.

   **Usage:** UK; dialectal

   **Example:** How it did rain! It ran down the street in a lavant.
