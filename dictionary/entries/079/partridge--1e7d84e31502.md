### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any bird of a number of genera in the family Phasianidae, notably in the genera Perdix and Alectoris.

   **Example:** On the first day of Christmas, my true love sent to me a partridge in a pear tree.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences; ornithology

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The flesh or meat of this bird eaten as food.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A type of cannon charge composed of several missiles fired all together, similar to langrage or case-shot. Also a large cannon that shoots stones.

   **Usage:** obsolete
