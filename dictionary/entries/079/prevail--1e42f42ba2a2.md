### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To be superior in strength, dominance, influence, or frequency; to have or gain the advantage over others; to have the upper hand; to outnumber others.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Red colour prevails in the Canadian flag.

   **Example:** Sunny skies will prevail across the Northeast.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To triumph; to be victorious.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To be current, widespread, or predominant; to have currency or prevalence.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** In his day and age, such practices prevailed all over Europe.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To succeed in persuading or inducing.

   **Usage:** intransitive; often

   **Example:** I prevailed on him to wait.

   **Source context (verbatim):** with upon or on

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To avail.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive
