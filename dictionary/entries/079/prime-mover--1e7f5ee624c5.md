### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The initial agent that is the cause of all things.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; philosophy; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** first cause; first mover; primum mobile; unmoved mover

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A machine, such as a water wheel or steam engine, that receives and modifies energy as supplied by some natural source or fuel and transforms it into mechanical work

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Tractor unit. Or, more specifically, the front part of a semi-trailer type truck, i.e., the tractor to which the trailer part attaches.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** automotive; transport; vehicles

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A military or heavy construction vehicle.

   **Usage:** US

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**
