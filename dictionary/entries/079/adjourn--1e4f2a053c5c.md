### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To postpone.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The trial was adjourned for a week.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To defer; to put off temporarily or indefinitely.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To end or suspend an event.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The court will adjourn for lunch.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To move as a group from one place to another.

   **Usage:** formal; intransitive; uncommon

   **Example:** After the dinner, we will adjourn to the bar.
