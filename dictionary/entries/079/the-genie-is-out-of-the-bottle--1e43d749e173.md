### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Information has been released that will have ongoing consequences.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** It's been widely rumored the network was planning to cancel the show, but now the genie is out of the bottle and fans are furious.

   **Source synonyms:** the cat is out of the bag

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Something has been brought into reality that cannot be eliminated or undone.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I don't think there's any hope of repealing this city's silly parking bylaw. The genie is out of the bottle, sadly.
