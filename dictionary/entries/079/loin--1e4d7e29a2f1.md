### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The part of the body (of humans and quadrupeds) at each side of the backbone, between the ribs and hips.

   **Source synonyms:** lend; lumbus

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of several cuts of meat taken from this part of an animal.

   **Example:** beef loin; goat loin; lamb loin; pork loin; also chicken loin and turkey loin (but these are almost always referred to as tenderloins)

   **Example:** I cooked a loin for dinner tonight.
