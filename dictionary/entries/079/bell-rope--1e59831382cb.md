## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A short length of rope, spliced to the eye of the clapper of a ship's bell and by which it is struck.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A long length of rope attached by splicing to a church's bell, by which it is struck, and with a sally for holding it.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A rope used to ring a bell serving as an old-fashioned doorbell.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A lovelock.

   **Usage:** obsolete; slang
