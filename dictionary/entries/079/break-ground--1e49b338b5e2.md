### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To begin digging in the earth at the start of a new construction, or, originally, for cultivation.

   **Usage:** literally

   **Example:** They broke ground on the new library last month.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To initiate a new venture, or to advance beyond previous achievements.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** The invention breaks ground in its programming and its structure.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To lift off the sea bottom when being weighed.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

   **Source context (verbatim):** of an anchor

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To separate from the ground on takeoff; to become airborne.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of an aircraft
