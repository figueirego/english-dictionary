### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A subgenre of science fiction which focuses on computer or information technology and virtual reality juxtaposed with a degree of breakdown or radical change in the social order.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** literature; media; publishing; science-fiction

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A cyberpunk character, a hacker punk, a high-tech low life.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** The film The Matrix redefined what a cyberpunk looked like.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A writer of cyberpunk fiction.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

   **Usage:** uncountable
