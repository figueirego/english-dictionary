### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The formal performance of a solemn rite, such as Christian sacrament.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** We will attend the Christmas celebration in church.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The observance of a holiday or feast day, as by solemnities.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The celebrations around here for Easter are enjoyable.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The act or process of showing appreciation, joy, gratitude and/or remembrance, notably as a social event.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** She jumped in the air in celebration of her success.

   **Source synonyms:** jubilation; merriment

   **Source antonyms:** lamentation; mourning

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A social gathering for entertainment and fun; a party.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** We are having a celebration at McDonald's for my dad's new job.

   **Example:** Next week includes celebrations for both Martin's birthday and for his new job.

   **Source synonyms:** See Thesaurus&#58;celebration
