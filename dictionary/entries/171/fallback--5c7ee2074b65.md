### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An act of falling back.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** A failure of one of the rocket's first-stage engines just after liftoff would result in a destructive pad fallback.

   **Example:** The black hole formed as a result of fallback of ejecta onto the neutron star initially created by the supernova explosion.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A backup plan or contingency strategy; an alternative which can be used if something goes wrong with the main plan; a recourse.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** As a fallback, I suppose we can use typewriters if the word processing system fails.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A reduction in bitumen softening point, sometimes called refluxing or overheating, in a relatively closed container.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Pulverised material that falls back to earth after a nuclear explosion; fallout.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
