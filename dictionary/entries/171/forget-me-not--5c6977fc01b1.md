### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of the flowering plants of the genus Myosotis, mostly with a small five-petalled blue flower; a flower from such a plant.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of certain species of genus Cynoglossum

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A light tint of blue, the color of the flower.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any of various light-blue coloured lycaenid butterflies of the Asian and Australasian genus Catochrysops, especially Catochrysops strabo native to India and Southeast Asia.
