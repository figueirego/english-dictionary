### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Far off (physically, logically or mentally).

   **Example:** We heard a distant rumbling but didn't pay any more attention to it. She was surprised to find that her fiancé was a distant relative of hers. His distant look showed that he was not listening to me.

   **Source synonyms:** faraway

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Emotionally unresponsive or unwilling to express genuine feelings.

   **Example:** Ever since our argument, she has been totally distant toward me.

   **Source synonyms:** aloof; cold; aloof; cool; distant; frosty; hankty; hard-to-get (dated); offish; quiet; remote; standoffish; strange (obsolete); thorny; touch-me-not-ish; unapproachable; unbending; undemonstrative; unimpressionable; uninvolved

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Imported into a cable television system from a different market (and thus possibly incurring a copyright royalty).

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** broadcasting; media; television

   **Source antonyms:** local
