### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of a person&#58; overly assertive, bold, or domineering; pushy; also, ostentatious.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** The office manager is an unpleasantly obtrusive individual.

   **Source synonyms:** intrusive; overassertive; overbearing; sharp-elbowed; authoritarian (pejorative; usually); autocratic (pejorative); bossy (pejorative); coercive (pejorative); commanding; controlling (pejorative); demanding; despotic (pejorative); dictatorial (pejorative); dominant; domineering (pejorative); forceful (pejorative; usually); harsh (pejorative); heavy-handed (pejorative); iron-fisted (pejorative); iron-handed (pejorative); monocratic (pejorative); obtrusive; brassy; oppressive (pejorative); overbearing (pejorative); strict (pejorative; usually); tyrannical (pejorative)

   **Source antonyms:** inobtrusive; nonobtrusive; unobtrusive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of a thing&#58; noticeable or prominent, especially in a displeasing way.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** He has an obtrusive forehead.

   **Source synonyms:** in-your-face; ostentatious

   **Source antonyms:** discreet; inobtrusive; nonobtrusive; unobtrusive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Protruding or sticking out, especially in a way that obstructs.

   **Usage:** obsolete

   **Example:** The facade of the building was ornamented with obtrusive sculpted designs.

   **Source synonyms:** bulging; jutting; bulging; exserted; extuberant (obsolete); jutting; obtrusive (obsolete); outstanding; projecting; projectory; prominent; promontorial; protruding; protuberant; protuberous; salient (often)

   **Source antonyms:** inobtrusive; nonobtrusive; unobtrusive
