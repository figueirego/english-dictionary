### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A simple drawing of a man with lines to represent limbs and torso.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A dealer in gambling who gathers the dice with a stick.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A player in a game played with a stick (such as hockey or lacrosse).

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A person who makes confectionery by pulling candy onto a stick.

   **Usage:** US

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A pickpocket's accomplice who takes the stolen goods in case the pickpocket is searched.

   **Usage:** UK; archaic; slang

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A stick seller, especially of walking sticks.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A cocksman.

   **Usage:** slang

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A male drummer.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

   **Source synonyms:** sticksman
