## Suffix

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Attached to the name of a language (typically a constructed language) to denote a conlang derived from or based on that language.

   **Usage:** morpheme

   **Example:** Volapük + -ido → Volapükido (“conlang derived from Volapük”)

   **Example:** Toki Pona + -ido → tokiponido (“conlang derived from Toki Pona”)
