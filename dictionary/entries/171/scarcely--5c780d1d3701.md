### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Probably not.

   **Usage:** modal

   **Example:** One could scarcely find any trout in the stream without the stocking program.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Certainly not, hardly at all.

   **Usage:** modal

   **Example:** One could scarcely expect the man to know how to fly a helicopter.

   **Source synonyms:** scarce (literary)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Hardly&#58; only just; by a small margin.

   **Usage:** degree

   **Example:** Scarcely had she arrived when she was put to work.

   **Source synonyms:** scarce (literary)
