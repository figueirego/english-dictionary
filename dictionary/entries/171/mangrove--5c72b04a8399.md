### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of various tropical and subtropical evergreen shrubs or trees chiefly of the Rhizophoraceae family that have aerial roots and grow in clumps in brackish intertidal coastal areas; (specifically) any of various trees of the genus Rhizophora, especially the red mangrove (Rhizophora mangle).

   **Source synonyms:** mangle

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A forest of such shrubs or trees.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Source synonyms:** mangle

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Preceded by a descriptive word&#58; any of various shrubs or trees of genera other than Rhizophora which resemble plants of this genus in appearance and habitat.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Source synonyms:** mangle

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Synonym of mangal (“a tropical and subtropical coastal intertidal swampland ecosystem characterized by mangroves (sense 1) or similar shrubs and trees”).

   **Usage:** also; attributive; broadly

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; ecology; natural-sciences

   **Source synonyms:** mangal (synonym; synonym-of)
