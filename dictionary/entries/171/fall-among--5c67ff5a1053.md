### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To enter the company of a group (of people, animals etc.), especially by chance and unexpectedly, and suddenly.

   **Example:** to fall among thieves

   **Example:** They were faring in the jungle, when in a stound they fell among a band of thugs.

   **Example:** The fawn frolickingly fell among a pride of lions.
