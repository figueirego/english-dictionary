### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Synonym of booty, goods seized from an enemy by violence, particularly (historical) during the sacking of a town in war or (video games) after successful combat.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** The loot from the sack of Constantinople included the head of John the Baptist.

   **Source synonyms:** booty (synonym; synonym-of); goods seized from an enemy by violence (synonym; synonym-of); particularly (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Synonym of sack, the plundering of a city, particularly during war.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** He consented to the loot of the city by the men under his command.

   **Source synonyms:** sack (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any valuable thing received for free, especially Christmas presents.

   **Usage:** US; colloquial; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Synonym of money.

   **Usage:** slang; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** money (synonym; synonym-of)
