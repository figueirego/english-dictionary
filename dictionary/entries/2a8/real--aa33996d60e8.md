   **Example:** City hall has its place, but this pub is the real heart of the town.

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Example:** This is real leather.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

   **Example:** These are real tears!

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

   **Example:** No one has ever seen a real unicorn.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a number

**Source sense R1.S9**

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

**Source sense R1.S10**

   **Example:** This is a real problem.
