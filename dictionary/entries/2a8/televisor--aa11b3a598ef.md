### Senses

   **Usage:** dated; sometimes non-native speakers' English

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A television set.
   1. A (later partly) mechanical such apparatus, largely superseded during the 1930s by wholly electronic ones; originally and especially, one designed and patented by the Scottish inventor John Logie Baird (1888–1936).

      **Usage:** dated; historical; sometimes non-native speakers' English
