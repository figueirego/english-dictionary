### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A hard white translucent ceramic, originally made by firing kaolin, quartz, and feldspar at high temperatures but now also inclusive of similar artificial materials; also often (figurative) such a material as a symbol of the fragility, elegance, etc. traditionally associated with porcelain goods.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** Tableware and toilets are both made of porcelain.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Synonym of china&#58; porcelain tableware.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** He set the table with our porcelain and stemware.

   **Source synonyms:** china (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Synonym of kaolin&#58; the kind of clay traditionally used in China to manufacture porcelain.

   **Usage:** obsolete; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** kaolin (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An object made of porcelain, (particularly) art objects or items of tableware.

   **Usage:** countable; plural-normally

   **Example:** The museum has an extensive collection of rare Chinese porcelains.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Synonym of cowrie.

   **Usage:** countable; rare

   **Source synonyms:** cowrie (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Synonym of wampum&#58; strings of shells, beads, etc. used as ornamentation or currency; the composite shells, beads, etc.

   **Usage:** countable; historical; obsolete

   **Source synonyms:** wampum (synonym; synonym-of)

**Source sense R1.S7**

   **Usage:** capitalized; countable; often
