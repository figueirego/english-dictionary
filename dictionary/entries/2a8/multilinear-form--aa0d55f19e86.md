### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Given a vector space V over a field K of scalars, a mapping Vᵏ → K that is linear in each of its arguments;

   **Usage:** multilinear algebra

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** linear-algebra; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Given a vector space V over a field K of scalars, a mapping Vᵏ → K that is linear in each of its arguments; (more generally) a similarly multiply linear mapping Mʳ → R defined for a given module M over some commutative ring R.
