## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The dirt or other material on the edge of a baseball or softball field that warns a player that he or she is approaching the fence, especially the portion in the outfield.

   **Example:** Fly ball to deep center. Shelby goes back and makes the catch on the warning track.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; baseball; games; hobbies; lifestyle; softball; sports

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A non-racing surface at the margin of a racing course, representing the edge of the race track.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A non-driving surface at the edge of a road, with a different surfacing warning drivers of the approach to the edge of the road, as part of the shoulder of a road.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** automotive; transport; vehicles
