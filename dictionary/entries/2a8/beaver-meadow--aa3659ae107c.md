## Noun

### Forms

- beaver meadows — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A meadow formed from the former lakebed of a recently drained beaver pond or beaver marsh, the result of a failed beaver dam.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A meadow growing from the land created by detritus and debris filling up the beaver marsh or beaver lake behind a beaver dam.
