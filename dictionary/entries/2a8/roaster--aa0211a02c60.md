<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Someone who roasts coffee beans; a company or place of business that does so.

   **Example:** Several artisanal roasters are operating in the region now.

   **Source synonyms:** roastery

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A machine for roasting things; especially, one that roasts coffee beans.

   **Example:** They've invested in large roasters with impressive stainless steel parts.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A kitchen utensil used for roasting.

   **Example:** I found a really nice roaster in the kitchenware aisle, plus a bamboo cutting board.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** cooking; food; lifestyle

   **Source synonyms:** roaster oven; roasting tin; roasting pan
