### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An act or process of melting.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An act or process of melting.
   1. Severe uncontrolled overheating and melting of the fuel in the core of a nuclear reactor, with the potential for radiation to escape; (countable) an instance of this.

      **Usage:** uncountable; nuclear energy

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A mass of melted material.

   **Usage:** countable; rare

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Chiefly preceded by a descriptive word&#58; uncontrolled disorder which often has disastrous consequences; (countable) a situation involving this; a crisis.

   **Usage:** figuratively; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Chiefly preceded by a descriptive word&#58; uncontrolled disorder which often has disastrous consequences; (countable) a situation involving this; a crisis.
   1. Synonym of crash (“a sudden large decline in the value of money, stocks or other assets, etc., especially one that causes additional economic difficulties”).

      **Usage:** countable; figuratively

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance

      **Source synonyms:** crash (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. (Sudden) loss of control over one's behaviour, emotions, etc.; (countable) an instance of this.
   1. An emotional outburst; a fit, a tantrum.

      **Usage:** countable; figuratively; informal

      **Source synonyms:** melty; tantrum; catfit (US; dialectal); conniption; conniption fit; dummy spit; headfit (British; slang); hissy (US); hissy fit (US); meltdown (figuratively); melty; paddy; shit fit (US); snit fit (US); wobbler (British; slang); wobbly (British; slang)

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. (Sudden) loss of control over one's behaviour, emotions, etc.; (countable) an instance of this.
   1. A response to sensory overload or stress, in which an autistic person is overwhelmed by intense, seemingly disproportionate emotions, behaving erratically and becoming unable to mask (“act more neurotypically”).

      **Usage:** countable; figuratively; informal; autism

      **Source synonyms:** melty

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. (Sudden) loss of control over one's behaviour, emotions, etc.; (countable) an instance of this.
   1. Synonym of nervous breakdown (“an attack of a psychological disorder such as anxiety or depression which is so severe that it prevents a person from continuing to function normally”); also (loosely), a situation where one is unable to function mentally due to panic, stress, etc.

      **Usage:** countable; figuratively; informal

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; psychology; sciences

      **Source synonyms:** melty; mental breakdown; nervous breakdown (synonym; synonym-of)
