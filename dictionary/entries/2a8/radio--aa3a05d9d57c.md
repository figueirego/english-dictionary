### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The technology that allows for the transmission of sound or other signals by modulation of electromagnetic waves.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** send a message by radio

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A device that can capture (receive) the signal sent over radio waves and render the modulated signal as sound.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** (of a song, person, etc.) to be on the radio

   **Example:** (of a song, etc.) to play on/be played on/get played on/come on the radio

   **Example:** We heard the news over the radio.

   **Example:** We heard a lovely song on the radio.

   **Example:** She was listening to the radio when he knocked on the door.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A device that can capture (receive) the signal sent over radio waves and render the modulated signal as sound.
   1. Any audio playing device, such as the on-board entertainment system in a car, usually including a radio receiver as well as the capability to play audio from recorded media; see also car radio.

      **Usage:** broadly; countable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A device that can transmit radio signals.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The continuous broadcasting of sound via the Internet in the style of traditional radio.

   **Usage:** Internet; uncountable
