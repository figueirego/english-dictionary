### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The condition of being sweet (all senses).
   1. The condition of tasting sugary or tasting as if sugary, from either sugar or another sweet-tasting substance.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source synonyms:** saccharinity

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The condition of being sweet (all senses).
   1. A pleasant disposition; kindness.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Example:** Ruth's overwhelming sweetness made Robert forget about his hopelessly low school grades.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The condition of being sweet (all senses).
   1. The quality of giving pleasure to the mind or senses, pleasantness, agreeableness.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The condition of being sweet (all senses).
   1. A term of address for one's sweetheart.

      **Usage:** countable; informal; uncountable

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** dulceness (obsolete); dulciness (obsolete); dulcitude; saccharinity; sugariness; sweetness
