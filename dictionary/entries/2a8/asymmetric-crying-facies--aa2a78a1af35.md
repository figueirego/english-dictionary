### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A minor congenital anomaly caused by agenesis or hypoplasia of the depressor anguli oris muscle, causing weakness on one side of the face and in the corresponding corner of the mouth.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** ACF; Cayler syndrome; Cayler cardiofacial syndrome
