### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In basketball (and, less commonly, in other sports whose playing area is called a court as well), the portion of the court at either end; and, by extension, certain seating sections alongside these playing areas.

   **Example:** Tickets for seats in the endcourt are a lot more expensive than they were last time.
