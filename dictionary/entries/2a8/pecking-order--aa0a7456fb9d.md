### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The natural ranking of social status and dominance occurring in a group of birds.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences; zoology

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The usually informal hierarchy of authority, command, or social status, as determined by the especially natural propensity for domination of different members of a specific group over each other, such as older brothers and sisters over their younger siblings.

   **Usage:** broadly; figuratively

   **Source synonyms:** hierarchy
