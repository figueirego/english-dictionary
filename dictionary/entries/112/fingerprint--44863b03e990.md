## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈfɪŋɡɚˌpɹɪnt/ — source labels: General-American

### Forms

- fingerprints — plural
- finger-print — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The natural pattern of ridges on the tips of human fingers, unique to each individual.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The pattern left on a surface where an uncovered fingertip has touched, especially as used to identify the person who touched the surface.

   **Source synonyms:** finger impression

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A unique combination of features that serves as an identification of something.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** acoustic fingerprint

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A unique identification for a public key in asymmetric cryptography.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; cryptography; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A trace that gives evidence of someone's involvement.

   **Usage:** figuratively
