### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An athletics event where a competitor must hurl a heavy metal orb ("shot") as far as possible. Because of the weight of the ball this is done with a pushing ("putting") motion rather than a throwing motion.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** athletics; hobbies; lifestyle; sports
