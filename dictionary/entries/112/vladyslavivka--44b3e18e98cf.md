<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A village, the administrative centre of Vladyslavivka silrada, Theodosia Raion, the Crimea, Ukraine.

   **Source synonyms:** Oy Quyu

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A village, the administrative centre of Vladyslavivka silrada, Theodosia Raion, the Crimea, Ukraine.
   1. A silrada of Theodosia Raion, the Crimea, Ukraine, established a. 1923.

      **Source synonyms:** Oy Quyu

<a id="r1-s4"></a>
