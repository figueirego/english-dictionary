**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The metrical continuity between one colon and another.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; phonology; prosody; sciences

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; phonology; prosody; sciences
