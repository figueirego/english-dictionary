## Adverb · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈhɛns/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. From here, from this place, away.

   **Usage:** archaic; not-comparable

   **Example:** I'm going hence, because you have insulted me.

   **Example:** Get thee hence, Satan!

   **Source synonyms:** herefrom

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. From the living or from this world.

   **Usage:** archaic; figuratively; not-comparable

   **Example:** After a long battle, my poor daughter was taken hence.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. In the future from now.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** A year hence it will be forgotten.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a length of time

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. As a result; therefore, for this reason.

   **Usage:** conjunctive; not-comparable

   **Example:** I shall go to Japan and hence will not be here in time for the party.

   **Example:** The purse is handmade and hence very expensive.

   **Source synonyms:** consequently

### Related words

**Source antonyms:** hither
