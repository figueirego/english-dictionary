### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Sensation or perception of motion.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Sensation or perception of motion.
   1. The perception of the movement of one's own body, its limbs and muscles etc.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; physiology; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Sensation or perception of motion.
   1. A spectator's perception of the motion of a performer, or, the effect of the motion of a scene on the spectator.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable; performing arts

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Proprioception or static position sense; the perception of the position and posture of the body; also, more broadly, including the motion of the body as well. See usage notes below.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
