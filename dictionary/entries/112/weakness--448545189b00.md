### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The condition of being weak.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** In a small number of horses, muscle weakness may progress to paralysis.

   **Source synonyms:** debility; frailty; vulnerability; vincibility

   **Source antonyms:** strength; power; powerfulness; invulnerability; invincibility

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An inadequate quality; fault.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** His inability to speak in front of an audience was his weakness.

   **Example:** The only weakness in her plan was its reliance on the electricity being up and running.

   **Source synonyms:** defect; flaw; hole

   **Source antonyms:** strength; forte

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A special fondness or obsessing desire.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** She is an athlete who has a weakness for chocolate.

   **Source synonyms:** soft spot
