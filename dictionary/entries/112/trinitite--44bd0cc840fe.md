### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The glassy residue left on the desert floor after the Trinity nuclear bomb test of 1945 at Alamogordo, New Mexico, USA.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Synonym of atomsite&#58; any melt glasses left by atomic bombs
