## Adjective

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Spending part of the life cycle underwater. Also, spending a significant proportion of its time in both terrestrial and aquatic environments.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source context (verbatim):** of animals

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Having the roots partially or entirely underwater.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source context (verbatim):** of plants

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Comprising some aspects of aquatic environs and some aspects of terrestrial ones.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source context (verbatim):** of ecosystems as environs
