### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To have, show or express sympathy; to be affected by feelings similar to those of another, in consequence of knowing the person to be thus affected; to feel for.

   **Usage:** Canada; US; intransitive

   **Source synonyms:** commiserate; understand

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To support, favour, have sympathy (with a political cause or movement, a side in a conflict / in an action).

   **Usage:** Canada; US; intransitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To say in an expression of sympathy.

   **Usage:** Canada; US; transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To have a common feeling, as of bodily pleasure or pain.

   **Usage:** Canada; US; intransitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To share (a feeling or experience).

   **Usage:** Canada; US; obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To agree; to be in accord; to harmonize.

   **Usage:** Canada; US; intransitive
