### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A genetic disorder caused by the presence of all or part of a third copy of chromosome 21 (a chromosomal excess), whereby the patients typically have a delay in cognitive ability and physical growth, as well as a small head and tilted eyelids.

   **Usage:** Australia; Canada; Ireland; New-Zealand; US; uncountable; usually; medical genetics

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; neurology; neuroscience; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** Down's; mongolism; Mongolian idiocy; trisomy 21
