## Noun · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act or practice of inspecting the merchandise in a physical store, then purchasing the identical product from an online merchant.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The act or practice of using a physical store as a showroom for an online merchant.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Traditional retailers are devising strategies to counteract the threat from showrooming.
