### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To improve; to correct one's bad habits or behavior.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** He'd better shape up soon, or he'll fail the class.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To take shape; to transform into or become.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** The fog has vanished and it's shaping up to be a beautiful day.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To gesture or posture threateningly; to approach as if to fight; to pose to intimidate.

   **Usage:** intransitive; slang

   **Source synonyms:** bridge up
