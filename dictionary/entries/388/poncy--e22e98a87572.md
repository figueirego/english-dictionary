**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of, relating to, or characteristic of a ponce.
   1. Intended to impress others, particularly in an excessively refined or ostentatious manner; affected, pretentious.

      **Usage:** Commonwealth; Ireland; UK; derogatory; informal
