### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A slackening or arrest of the blood current, due not to a lessening of the heart’s beat, but to some abnormal resistance of the capillary walls.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; pathology; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Inactivity; a freezing, or state of motionlessness.

   **Usage:** figuratively; uncountable; usually

   **Example:** His company was sized for growth, not stasis.

   **Source synonyms:** stability; staticity

   **Source antonyms:** movement; flux

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A technology allowing something to be artificially frozen in time, so that it does not age or change.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** I was in stasis for forty years before I woke up orbiting this poxy planet!

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** literature; media; publishing; science-fiction

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. One of the sections of a cathisma or portion of the psalter.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually
