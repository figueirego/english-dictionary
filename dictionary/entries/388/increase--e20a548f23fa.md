### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. (of a quantity, etc.) To become larger or greater, to greaten.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** His rage only increased when I told him of the lost money.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To make (a quantity, etc.) larger.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To multiply by the production of young; to be fertile, fruitful, or prolific.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To become more nearly full; to show more of the surface; to wax.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The Moon increases.
