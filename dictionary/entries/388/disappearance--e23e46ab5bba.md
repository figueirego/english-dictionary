### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The action of disappearing or vanishing.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Wireless phone technology led to the disappearance of once-common coiled cords for telephone handsets.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Passing out of sight.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** From the shore, they watched the disappearance of the ships' masts into the haze.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Leaving secretly of without explanation

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The prosecuting attorney was concerned about the potential disappearance of witnesses.

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Cessation of existence.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
