### Senses

<a id="r3-s1"></a>

**Source sense R3.S1**

   **Example:** He took notes with a pen.

   **Example:** another novel from the pen of everybody's fave thriller writer.

   **Example:** Please use a pen, not a pencil, when filling out this form. Use black or blue ink only.

<a id="r3-s2"></a>

**Source sense R3.S2**

2. A writer, or their style.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** He has a sharp pen.

<a id="r3-s3"></a>

**Source sense R3.S3**

3. Marks of ink left by a pen.

   **Usage:** colloquial

   **Example:** He's unhappy because he got pen on his new shirt.

<a id="r3-s4"></a>

**Source sense R3.S4**

4. A light pen.

<a id="r3-s5"></a>

**Source sense R3.S5**

5. The internal cartilage skeleton of a squid, shaped like a pen.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences; zoology

<a id="r3-s6"></a>

**Source sense R3.S6**

6. A feather, especially one of the flight feathers of a bird, angel etc.

   **Usage:** archaic; dialectal; poetic

<a id="r3-s7"></a>

**Source sense R3.S7**

7. A wing.

   **Usage:** poetic

<a id="r3-s8"></a>

**Source sense R3.S8**

8. A syringe-like device for injecting a dose of medication such as insulin or epinephrine. (See Injector pen.)

<a id="r3-s9"></a>

**Source sense R3.S9**

9. Ellipsis of vapor pen (“electronic cigarette”).

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; ellipsis

   **Example:** a dab pen; a wax pen
