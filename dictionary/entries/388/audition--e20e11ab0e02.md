### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A performance, by an aspiring performer, to demonstrate suitability or talent.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** I've been to five auditions this week.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The sense of hearing.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** His audition was poor.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An act of hearing; being heard.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Something heard.

   **Usage:** countable; rare; uncountable
