### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The state or condition of being an idiot; the quality of having an intelligence level far below average.

   **Usage:** countable; dated; derogatory; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; psychology; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** idioticness; imbecility; moronicity; mental retardation; intellectual disability

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Lack of intelligence or sense; extremely foolish behaviour.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** The administrators, growing tired of such idiocy, put a new policy in place.

   **Source synonyms:** idioticness; imbecility; moronicity

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An idiotic act or utterance.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** The list of his idiocies is seemingly interminable.
