### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A predisposition or natural inclination, propensity, or a predilection; especially, a strong disposition or bent.

   **Example:** The child has a proclivity for exaggeration.

   **Source synonyms:** penchant; propensity; affinity; bag (idiomatic); bent; love; cup of coffee (idiomatic); cup of joe (idiomatic); cup of tea (idiomatic); dish (informal); fancy; jam; liking; partiality; penchant; tilt (US); predilection; predisposition; preference; proclivity; propension (rare); propensity; shine (slang); taste; thing (informal)
