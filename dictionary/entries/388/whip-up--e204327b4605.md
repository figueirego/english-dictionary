### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To produce (something) quickly, especially food.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I'll whip up a quick meal for our unexpected guests.

   **Source synonyms:** rustle up; whomp up; throw together; cook up

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To beat (stir) (a liquid) quickly so to introduce air and form foam.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** whip

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To arouse or excite.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** His speech whipped the crowd up into a frenzy.

   **Source synonyms:** stir up; urge; abet (obsolete); actuate; goose; fuel; light a fire under; send; sting; bring about; cheer (obsolete); draw; drive; encourage; egg on (idiomatic); engender; evoke; excite; foment; goad; grill (Scotland; US; obsolete); ignite (figuratively); impel; incite; induce; inflame (figuratively); instigate; invoke; lash; motive; motivate; move (obsolete); obligate; oblige; push; promote; prompt; provoke; rouse; set off; spark off; spur; spur on; stimulate; stir; stir up; stoke (figuratively); tempt; urge; whet (obsolete); whip up

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To lift or snatch up suddenly.

   **Usage:** transitive
