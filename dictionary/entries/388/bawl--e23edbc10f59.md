**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To shout or utter in a loud and intense manner.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** commanders bawling

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To wail; to give out a blaring cry.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** children bawling

   **Example:** cattle bawling
