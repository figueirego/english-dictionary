### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Rearing on both hind legs with the forelegs extended.

   **Usage:** originally

   **Example:** The Vienna riding school displays splendid rampant movement.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Rearing up, especially on its hind leg(s), with a foreleg raised and in profile.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; heraldry; hobbies; lifestyle; monarchy; nobility; politics

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Tilted, said of an arch with one side higher than the other, or a vault whose two abutments are located on an inclined plane.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** architecture

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Unrestrained or unchecked, usually in a negative manner.

   **Example:** Weeds are rampant in any neglected garden.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Rife, or occurring widely, frequently or menacingly.

   **Example:** There was rampant corruption in the city.
