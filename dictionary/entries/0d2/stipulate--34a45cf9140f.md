### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To require (something) as a condition of a contract or agreement.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** provide (formal)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To specify, promise or guarantee something in an agreement.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To acknowledge the truth of; not to challenge.

   **Usage:** US; formal; transitive

   **Example:** The defense stipulates that the witness has identified my client.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To ask for a contractual term.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source context (verbatim):** followed by for

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To mutually agree.

   **Usage:** formal; intransitive

   **Example:** We will stipulate to our receipt of all pertinent discovery documents.
