### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** a perennial stream

   **Source synonyms:** multiyear; yearslong; perennal (archaic; obsolete)

   **Usage:** figuratively; not-comparable

   **Example:** His artwork has a perennial beauty.

   **Source synonyms:** continual; enduring; everlasting; permanent; timeless; ageful; agelong; amaranthine; celestial; endless; enduring; eternal; eterne (obsolete); everduring (obsolete); evergreen (figuratively); everlasting; eviternal (obsolete); forever; heavenly; imperishable; indesinent (obsolete); lasting (obsolete); never-ending; perennial (figuratively); permanent; perpetual; perpetuate (adjective); persistent; sempiternal; timeless; unending; undeadly; undying

**Source sense R1.S3**

   **Usage:** figuratively; not-comparable

   **Example:** a perennial candidate in elections

   **Example:** Change is a perennial theme in politics.

   **Source synonyms:** repetitious; habitual; monotonous; perennial (figuratively); recurrent; recurring; repeating; repetitional; repetitionary; repetitious; repetitive
