### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Mildly ill.

   **Example:** He was indisposed with a cold.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Not disposed, predisposed, or inclined; unwilling.

   **Example:** I stayed indoors all day, feeling indisposed to finish mowing the lawn.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Not yet ready (especially with regard to receiving a visitor) because not yet arranged into a state of readiness (i.e., not disposed); (especially, more specifically)&#58;
   1. In the lavatory/WC.

      **Usage:** euphemistic
