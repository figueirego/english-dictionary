### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The legal authorization of one person to act as the agent of another.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** He confirmed that he had power of attorney.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law
