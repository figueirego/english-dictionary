### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of an animal (now chiefly of animals related to the ass or donkey, and the camel)&#58; to make its cry.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Whenever I walked by, that donkey brayed at me.

   **Source synonyms:** blore (archaic; dialectal); hee-haw

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To make a harsh, discordant sound like a donkey's bray.

   **Usage:** broadly; intransitive

   **Example:** He threw back his head and brayed with laughter.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To make or utter (a shout, sound, etc.) discordantly, loudly, or in a harsh and grating manner.

   **Usage:** transitive
