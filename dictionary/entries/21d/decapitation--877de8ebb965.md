### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Beheading; the act of beheading or decapitating.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** If the headsman's axe was sharp and his aim was true, decapitation was a quick and relatively painless form of death.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The ousting or destruction of the ruling body of a government or other organization.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** A decapitation strike carried out by drone killed many of the country's senior generals.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The unseating of a senior politician.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The decapitation of the Foreign Secretary was a significant publicity coup for the opposition.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; politics
