### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Accompanied by wind.

   **Example:** It was a long and windy night.

   **Source synonyms:** blowy; blustery; breezy

   **Source antonyms:** calm; windless

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Unsheltered and open to the wind.

   **Example:** They shagged in a windy bus shelter.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Empty and lacking substance.

   **Example:** They made windy promises they would not keep.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Long-winded; orally verbose.

   **Source synonyms:** circumlocutory; diffuse; periphrastic; palaverous; pleonastic; prolix; rigmarole; sesquipedalian; verbose; wordy

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Flatulent.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** The Tex-Mex meal had made them somewhat windy.

   **Source synonyms:** farty (informal); flatulent; windy; gassy; breezy; curmurring

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Nervous, frightened.

   **Usage:** slang
