### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The rear, back, or unseen portion (of something).

   **Example:** The back end of the operation is kept out of sight of the customers.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. That part of a hardware or software system that is farthest from the user; the internals rather than the user interface.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences
