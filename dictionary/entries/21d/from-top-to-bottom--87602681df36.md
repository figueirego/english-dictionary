### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In an exhaustive and pervasive manner.

   **Usage:** adjectival

   **Example:** She's a born leader from top to bottom.

   **Source synonyms:** from head to toe; from stem to stern

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In the manner of conducting a remedy, search, or investigation (etc.) that is thorough and complete.

   **Usage:** adverbial

   **Example:** They restored the car from top to bottom.

   **Source synonyms:** completely; thoroughly; through and through

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see from, top, to, bottom.
