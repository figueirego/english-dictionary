### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An exclamation of congratulations, usually followed by "hip hip hooray" three times.

   **Example:** "Three cheers for Jack! Hip hip!" "Hooray!" "Hip hip!" "Hooray!" "Hip hip!" "Hooray!"

   **Source synonyms:** three times three (archaic)
