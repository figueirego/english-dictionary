### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who gives birth to someone, usually a mother.

   **Usage:** dated; rare

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person who is someone's biological mother but who is not felt by their child(ren) as their mother in an emotional sense, typically due to long-term abuse
