### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A graph showing the curvilinear relationship between time and the concentration of a substance.

   **Example:** Blood estradiol levels after administration of a dose of estradiol can be visualized as a concentration–time curve.
