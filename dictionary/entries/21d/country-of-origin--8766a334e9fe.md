## Noun

### Forms

- countries of origin — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The country in which a person or thing is deemed to have originated for the purposes of laws and regulations.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The country where shipped goods are produced.

   **Usage:** international trade

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The country in which the last stage of substantial transformation of goods occurred.

   **Usage:** international trade

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The country in which the most important processing took place.

   **Usage:** international trade

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The country in which a substantial amount of processing took place.

   **Usage:** international trade

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The country indicated on a label as being the country in which the goods were made.

   **Usage:** consumer goods

   **Example:** It says "Made in Mexico". I wonder if that is the real country of origin.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Country of first publication.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** media; publishing
