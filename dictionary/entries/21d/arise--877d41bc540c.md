### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To come up from a lower to a higher position.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** to arise from a kneeling posture

   **Source synonyms:** rise; spring; stand up

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To come up from one's bed or place of repose; to get up.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** He arose early in the morning.

   **Source synonyms:** awaken; see Thesaurus&#58;wake

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To spring up; to come into action, being, or notice; to become operative, sensible, or visible; to begin to act a part; to present itself.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** A cloud arose and covered the sun.

   **Example:** A new challenge will arise every time you think you've solved the last one.

   **Example:** The issue began to arise when the team members couldn't agree on the plan.

   **Example:** Problems can arise unexpectedly, but we need to be prepared for them.
