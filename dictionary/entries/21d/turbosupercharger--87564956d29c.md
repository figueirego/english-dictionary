### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A turbocharger, an exhaust-powered turbine used to power increased air intake to an internal combustion engine; an exhaust-turbine powered (in place of engine-powered) supercharger.

   **Usage:** dated

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** aeronautics; aerospace; automotive; aviation; business; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; transport; vehicles

   **Source synonyms:** turbo

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A twincharger, a combination of a supercharger and a turbocharger.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** automotive; transport; vehicles
