### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make an obscene gesture (towards someone) by closing the fist and extending the middle finger upwards.

   **Usage:** transitive; usually

   **Example:** Zelda gave Travis the finger after he cut her off in traffic.

   **Source synonyms:** flick off; flip off; flip the bird

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To make any sort of gesture extending one of the fingers above the others.

   **Usage:** intransitive
