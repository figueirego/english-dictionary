## Noun

### Forms

- wings and prayers — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A precarious situation; a situation whose successful outcome is hoped for but uncertain.

   **Example:** His sudden career change was a bit of a wing and prayer.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An approach to addressing a problem whose effectiveness is doubtful.

   **Example:** The candidate’s speech had nothing to offer but wings and prayers.
