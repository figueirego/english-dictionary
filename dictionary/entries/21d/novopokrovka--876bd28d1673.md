## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Numerous settlements in Kazakhstan, Russia and Ukraine, including&#58;
   1. A rural settlement, the administrative centre of Novopokrovka settlement hromada, Dnipro Raion, Dnipropetrovsk Oblast, Ukraine, founded in 1783.
      1. A former selrada of Dnipropetrovsk Raion, Dnipropetrovsk Oblast, Ukraine, amalgamated into Novopokrovka settlement hromada in August 2015.

         **Source synonyms:** Shamshove; Pokrovka; Novo-Pokrovka

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   1. A rural settlement, the administrative centre of Novopokrovka settlement hromada, Dnipro Raion, Dnipropetrovsk Oblast, Ukraine, founded in 1783.
      1. A settlement hromada of Dnipro Raion, Dnipropetrovsk Oblast, Ukraine, established in August 2015.
