### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The part of anything that hangs loose, having no strain upon it.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** the slack of a rope or of a sail

   **Example:** take in the slack

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A dip in a surface.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. In particular, a shallow dell or hollow; a dip in the surface of terrain, such as between hills.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A low-lying marsh or a pool, especially a tidal or intermittent one which periodically fills and drains.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Forbearance; lenience; gracious allowance.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** cut \[someone\] some slack \[= cut them a break\]

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Unconditional listening attention given by client to patient.

   **Usage:** uncountable; psychotherapy

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Attributive form of slacks (“semi-formal trousers”).

   **Usage:** attributive; countable; form-of; uncountable

   **Form of:** [slacks](../37b/slacks--def963251058.md)
