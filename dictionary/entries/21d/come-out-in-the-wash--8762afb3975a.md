### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of problems or difficulties, to work out, resolve, or become understood eventually and naturally.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** It may look like a huge mess now, but I expect that it will all come out in the wash as time goes on.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of advantages and disadvantages, to cancel each other out.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** The number 12 bus is faster, but the number 17 stops nearer my house, so it all comes out in the wash.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of the truth&#58; to be inevitably revealed in the future.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** You can do everything you can to hide this, but everything comes out in the wash.
