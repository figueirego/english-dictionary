### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Enjoying good health; free from disease or disorder.

   **Example:** He was father to three healthy kids.

   **Example:** a healthy mind in a healthy body

   **Example:** Brush regularly to keep your teeth and gums strong and healthy.

   **Example:** My fruit trees are looking very healthy.

   **Source synonyms:** well; hale; fit

   **Source antonyms:** unhealthy

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Conducive to health.

   **Example:** A healthy diet can help to maintain proper weight.

   **Source synonyms:** healthful; wholesome; nourishing

   **Source antonyms:** unhealthy

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Evincing health.

   **Example:** Her face had a healthy glow.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Significant, hefty; beneficial.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** a healthy respect for authority

   **Example:** Sam unwrapped the sandwich and took a healthy bite out of the middle.

   **Example:** a healthy dislike, a healthy contempt
