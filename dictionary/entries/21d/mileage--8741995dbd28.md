### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The total distance travelled in miles or in air miles.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The number of miles travelled by a vehicle on a certain volume of fuel.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An allowance for travel expenses at a specified rate per mile.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A fee based on miles of use, as of an automobile or a railroad car.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The amount of service that something has yielded or may yield in future.

   **Usage:** countable; informal; uncountable

   **Example:** This old PC has still got plenty of mileage in it.

   **Example:** There’s quite a lot of mileage in language, speech and computing, particularly in research.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Something worth taking into consideration.

   **Usage:** countable; informal; uncountable

   **Example:** There’s some mileage in your argument.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The total number of a person's past sexual encounters.

   **Usage:** countable; slang; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** body count
