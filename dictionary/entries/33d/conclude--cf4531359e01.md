### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To end; to come to an end.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The story concluded with a moral.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To bring to an end; to close; to finish.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To bring about as a result; to effect; to make.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to conclude a bargain

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To come to a conclusion, to a final decision.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** From the evidence, I conclude that this man was murdered.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To make a final determination or judgment concerning; to judge; to decide.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To shut off; to restrain; to limit; to estop; to bar.

   **Usage:** often passive voice

   **Example:** The defendant is concluded by his own plea.

   **Example:** A judgment concludes the introduction of further evidence.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To shut up; to enclose.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To include; to comprehend; to shut up together; to embrace; to confine.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. to deduce, to infer (develop a causal relation)
