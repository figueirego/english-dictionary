### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any device used to jam radio reception.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A musician who jams.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A device (e.g. a jumar) which will slide along a rope in one direction but not the other, used in rock-climbing, caving etc.
