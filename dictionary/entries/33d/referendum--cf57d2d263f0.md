### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A direct popular vote on a proposed law or constitutional amendment.

   **Example:** a referendum on …

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; politics

   **Source synonyms:** plebiscite

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An action, choice, etc., which is perceived as passing judgment on another matter.

   **Example:** My father is taking my decision on whether to go to university as a referendum on his performance as a parent, and it's very stressful.
