## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The general well-being of someone, and the degree to which someone's daily experience is enjoyable or unenjoyable.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** health-related quality of life

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The general well-being of someone, and the degree to which someone's daily experience is enjoyable or unenjoyable.
   1. Concerning prevailing conditions in public, such as cleanliness, safety, and the pleasantness of the surroundings or lack thereof.

      **Usage:** attributive; countable; uncountable; usually

      **Example:** quality-of-life offenses such as graffiti writing, loitering, public urination, public drinking, aggressive panhandling, and turnstile jumping

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; law-enforcement

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences
