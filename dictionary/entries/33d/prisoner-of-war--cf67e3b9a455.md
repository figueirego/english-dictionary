### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A combatant or soldier who is captured by an enemy power during or immediately after an armed conflict.

   **Example:** As a prisoner of war, he was interrogated by his enemy captors.

   **Source synonyms:** POW (abbreviation); PW
