## Adjective · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Adhering closely; applied to certain creeping animals, such as caterpillars.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences; zoology

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Intermediate between two genera, groups, families, etc., and having some of the characteristics of each; interosculant.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The genera by which two families approximate are called osculant genera.

   **Example:** osculant groups

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Intermediate between multiple potentially reconstructible protoforms, but having a mismatch in semantics or morphology that cannot be explained through regular patterns of change.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; Bantu linguistics

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Tangent, touching at a single point.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r2-s2"></a>
