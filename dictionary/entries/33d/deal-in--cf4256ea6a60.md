### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To deal cards to someone entering a game; to enter someone into a game by dealing cards to that person.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** card-games; games

   **Source context (verbatim):** with in as adverbial particle

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To trade (buy and sell) a named commodity.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source context (verbatim):** with in as preposition

   **Source synonyms:** merchant

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To accept (something or a piece of information) as a basis for a decision.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To have an interest in something.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To give (someone) a share or portion of something; to include (someone) in a plan or scheme.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** We can deal you in on the robbery.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To discard another player's winning tile, causing them to declare a win.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** board-games; games; mahjong

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To be a winning tile that an opponent declares a win off of.

   **Usage:** broadly; intransitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** board-games; games; mahjong
