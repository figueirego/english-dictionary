### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To remove (something such as dirt) by wiping.

   **Usage:** literally; transitive

   **Example:** wipe a picture off the board

   **Example:** wipe water off the windscreen

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To clean (something), removing any dirt from it, by wiping it.

   **Usage:** literally; transitive

   **Example:** wipe off the countertop

   **Example:** The countertop is dirty. Please wipe it off.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To destroy completely, leaving no trace.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** Wipe that smile off your face!

   **Example:** Wipe his data off the computer.

   **Example:** Global warming could wipe some coastal areas off the map.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To remove semen off from the buttocks of a sexual partner after doing anal sex.

   **Usage:** slang
