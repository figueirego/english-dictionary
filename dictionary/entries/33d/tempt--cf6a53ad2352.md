### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To provoke someone to do wrong, especially by promising a reward; to entice.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** She tempted me to eat the apple.

   **Source synonyms:** entice; lure; pander; tease

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To attract; to allure.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Its glossy skin tempted me.

   **Source synonyms:** beguile; entrance; allure; arrest; attract; becharm (archaic); beguile; bewitch; beckon; captivate; charm; court; delight; draw; effascinate (obsolete); enchant; engage; enthrall; entrance; enrapture; fascinate; forspeak (obsolete); hypnotize (figuratively); interest; intrigue; mesmerize (figuratively); pull; pull in; seduce; sirenize; spellbind; tempt; tole

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To provoke something; to court.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** It would be tempting fate.

   **Source synonyms:** foment; urge; abet (obsolete); actuate; goose; fuel; light a fire under; send; sting; bring about; cheer (obsolete); draw; drive; encourage; egg on (idiomatic); engender; evoke; excite; foment; goad; grill (Scotland; US; obsolete); ignite (figuratively); impel; incite; induce; inflame (figuratively); instigate; invoke; lash; motive; motivate; move (obsolete); obligate; oblige; push; promote; prompt; provoke; rouse; set off; spark off; spur; spur on; stimulate; stir; stir up; stoke (figuratively); tempt; urge; whet (obsolete); whip up
