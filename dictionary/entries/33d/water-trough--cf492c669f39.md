**Source sense R1.S1**

1. a long trough placed between the rails in a railway track, which enabled a steam locomotive to replenish its water supply without stopping by lowering a scoop. They were removed at the end of steam train operation.

   **Usage:** UK

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** rail-transport; railways; transport
