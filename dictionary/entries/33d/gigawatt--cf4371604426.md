### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One thousand million (10⁹) watts, an amount of power large enough to power such things as a midsize town or several small ones. (Consuming 1 gigawatt during a duration of 1 hour consumes 1 gigawatt-hour of energy.)

   **Example:** In the 2020s, the race for AI data centers has Big Tech searching for ways to add multiple gigawatts of additional grid capacity to a regional power grid.

   **Example:** In a famous movie about time travel, the mad scientist tells the teenage hero that the time machine requires 1.21 gigawatts of power to operate.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The amount of data center capacity or amount of compute that this amount of power can provide under the current technological state of the art (PUE, clock speed, etc).

   **Usage:** metonymically

   **Example:** a gigawatt of compute
