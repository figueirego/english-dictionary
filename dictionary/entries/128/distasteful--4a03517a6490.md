## Adjective

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having a bad or foul taste.

   **Example:** The food had very distasteful flavour.

   **Source synonyms:** unpalatable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Unpleasant.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** Scrubbing the floors was a distasteful duty to perform.

   **Source synonyms:** unpalatable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Offensive.

   **Example:** distasteful language
