### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The position of being elder or senior; seniority, precedence of birth, primogeniture.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The personality of an elderly person.

   **Usage:** countable; nonce-word; uncountable

   **Source context (verbatim):** as a mock title of honour

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The office or position of elder in a church.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The collective body of (ecclesiastical) elders; a body or court of elders, a presbytery.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The smallest administrative division in Lithuania, equivalent to a ward.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** elderate
