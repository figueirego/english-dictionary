### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To come to have feelings of love, particularly romantic love.

   **Usage:** intransitive; animate object following “with”

   **Example:** I fell in love with him the moment I first saw him.

   **Example:** Have you ever fallen in love?

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a person

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To come to have feelings of love towards each other.

   **Usage:** intransitive; reciprocal

   **Example:** They met, fell in love, got married, and lived happily ever after.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To come to have feelings of intense liking directed at another person or a thing.

   **Usage:** intransitive; inanimate object following "with"

   **Example:** I have just fallen in love with Rome!

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a person

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** develop feelings; enamour

**Source antonyms:** fall out of love; disenamour
