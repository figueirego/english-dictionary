### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The first graded print of an edited film combining sound and picture, which is created for the client to view and approve; his answer decides when the rest of the copies of the film are printed or not.

   **Example:** The studio bosses were unwilling to approve the answer print of the novice director's debut.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** broadcasting; film; media; television
