### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An addition of extra charge on the agreed, stated, or baseline price. Sometimes portrayed by a buyer as excessive and rapacious; usually portrayed by a seller as justified and necessary.

   **Example:** Our airline tickets cost twenty dollars more than we expected because we had to pay a fuel surcharge.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The part of the price of a subsidized good or service that is not covered by the subsidy and so must be paid by the consumer.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An overprint on a stamp that alters (usually raises) the original nominal value of the stamp; used especially in times of hyperinflation.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; philately

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A painting in lighter enamel over a darker one that serves as the ground.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** art; arts

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A charge that has been omitted from an account as payment of a credit to the charged party

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A penalty for failure to exercise common prudence and skill in the performance of a fiduciary's duties.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. An excessive load or burden.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. The putting, by a commoner, of more animals on the common than he is entitled to.

   **Usage:** obsolete

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To apply a surcharge.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To overload; to overburden.

   **Example:** to surcharge an animal or a ship; to surcharge a cannon

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To overstock; especially, to put more cattle into (e.g. a common) than one has a right to do, or more than the herbage will sustain.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r2-s4"></a>
