### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A fictional creature, resembling a centaur, with the lower body of some four-or-more-legged animal and the upper body either of an anthropomorphic version of that same animal or (less commonly) of a human or another humanoid creature.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Example:** His character was a husky taur with dark red fur.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** lifestyle
