### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person paid to endorse a product while pretending to be impartial.

   **Example:** carnival barkers and their shills, fleecing the rubes

   **Source synonyms:** shiller

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any person enthusiastically endorsing a product; especially, one who is getting paid for the endorsement.

   **Usage:** derogatory

   **Example:** On the screen, there was that quack again, playing the shill for yet another drug company.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An accomplice at a confidence trick during an auction or gambling game, such as an accomplice of the seller who bids to drive up the price.

   **Example:** Sniping is necessary because if you bid before the last minute it just gives the shills more time to screw you over.

   **Source synonyms:** inside man; potted plant

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A house player in a casino.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** gambling; games

   **Source synonyms:** stick
