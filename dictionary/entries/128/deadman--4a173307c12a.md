### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A long object, often a timber or log, buried to serve as an anchor for a wall or for stays.

   **Example:** The building code requires deadmen for retaining walls.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; construction; manufacturing

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A cutout device that operates in the event that an operator releases the control handles, leaves the operator's seat, or otherwise seems to be no longer present and alert.

   **Usage:** safety equipment

   **Example:** a deadman device

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** agriculture; business; lifestyle; manufacturing; rail-transport; railways; transport
