### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of performing; carrying into execution or action; execution; achievement; accomplishment; representation by action.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** the performance of an undertaking or a duty

   **Example:** Though the result wasn't what we were hoping for, I have to commend the performance of the team, never giving up until the end.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. That which is performed or accomplished; a thing done or carried through; an achievement; a deed; an act; a feat; especially, an action of an elaborate or public character.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A live show or concert.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The band played a mix of old and new songs during their 90-minute performance.

   **Example:** We saw the whole ballet performance from the front row.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** art; arts

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The amount of useful work accomplished estimated in terms of time needed, resources used, etc.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Better performance means more work accomplished in shorter time and/or using fewer resources.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The actual use of language in concrete situations by native speakers of a language, as opposed to the system of linguistic knowledge they possess (competence), cf. linguistic performance.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences
