### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any member of the species Microgadus tomcod, of edible cod found in the Atlantic.

   **Source synonyms:** Atlantic tomcod; tommycod; frostfish; winter cod

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Source synonyms:** Pacific tomcod
