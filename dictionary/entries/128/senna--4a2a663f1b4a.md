### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of several plants of the tribe Cassieae, especially those of the genera Cassia and Senna, whose leaves and pods are used as a purgative and laxative.

   **Usage:** countable

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** uncountable
