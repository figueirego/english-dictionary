### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To fail to remember; to overlook or neglect (something).

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** After he graduated from university he lost sight of his career goals.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see lose, sight, of.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** The soldier lost sight of the enemy.
