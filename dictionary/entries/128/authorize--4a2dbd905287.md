### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To grant (someone) the permission or power necessary to do (something); to permit; to sanction or consent to.

   **Usage:** British; English; Oxford; US; ambitransitive

   **Example:** The General Assembly authorized the Council to take up the matter.

   **Example:** The judge authorized the wiretapping.

   **Source synonyms:** license; permit

   **Source antonyms:** ban; prohibit; deauthorize
