### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The unique temperature and pressure at which the solid, liquid and gas phases of a substance are all in equilibrium.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A point where the incident shock wave, the reflected shock wave, the Mach stem, and one slipstream meet.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A point where a curve or surface intersects itself along three arcs.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The intersection of an occluded front, cold front, and warm front.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** climatology; meteorology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The point where two boundaries, such as a dry line, outflow boundary, or cold front, intersect.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** climatology; meteorology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A point where three plates intersect.

   **Usage:** plate tectonics
