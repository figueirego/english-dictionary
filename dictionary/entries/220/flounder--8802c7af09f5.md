### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To act clumsily or confused; to struggle or be flustered.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** He gave a good speech, but floundered when audience members asked questions he could not answer well.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To flop around as a fish out of water.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To make clumsy attempts to move or regain one's balance.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Robert yanked Connie's leg vigorously, causing her to flounder and eventually fall.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To be in serious difficulty.
