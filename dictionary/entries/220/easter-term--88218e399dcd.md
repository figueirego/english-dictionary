### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Synonym of Paschal term (“the fourteenth day of the first lunar month of spring, formerly used in calculating dates”).

   **Usage:** historical

   **Source synonyms:** Paschal term (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The third term of the legal year, running from April to May, during which the upper courts of England and Wales, and Ireland, sit to hear cases.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The summer term of the University of Cambridge, and other educational institutions, running from April to June; equivalent to Trinity term at the universities of Oxford and Dublin. The term was modelled after the legal term, but does not begin and end on the same dates.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** education
