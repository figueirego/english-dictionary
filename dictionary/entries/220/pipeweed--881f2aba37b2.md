### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Tobacco prepared for smoking in a pipe; also, the leaves of herbs or other plants prepared for such use.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** lifestyle; smoking

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of a number of plants having thin and straight stems resembling pipes, often hollow or lacking branches.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of a number of plants having thin and straight stems resembling pipes, often hollow or lacking branches.
   1. The common horsetail or field horsetail (Equisetum arvense).

      **Usage:** specifically; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any of a number of plants having thin and straight stems resembling pipes, often hollow or lacking branches.
   1. The desert trumpet (Eriogonum inflatum) which has a straight stem with a swollen portion; formerly some Native American tribes in the Las Vegas Valley area turned such stems into pipes for smoking by removing the stem at the base and cutting the swollen portion in half to serve as a bowl.

      **Usage:** US; specifically; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The redrattle (Pedicularis flammea), a parasitic plant having hollow stems.

   **Usage:** obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A type of seaweed with tubelike fronds; especially the sea lettuce (Ulva intestinalis).

   **Usage:** obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. An unidentified sessile marine invertebrate, probably a soft coral or sponge.

   **Usage:** obsolete; uncountable
