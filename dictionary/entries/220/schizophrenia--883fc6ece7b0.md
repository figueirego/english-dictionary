### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A persistent, often chronic, mental illness characterised by abnormal perception, thinking, behavior and emotion, often marked by delusions.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; medicine; psychiatry; psychology; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A condition in which a person is supposed to have several distinct personalities; dissociative identity disorder.

   **Usage:** countable; proscribed; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any condition in which disparate or mutually exclusive activities coexist; a lack of decision between options.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; informal; uncountable
