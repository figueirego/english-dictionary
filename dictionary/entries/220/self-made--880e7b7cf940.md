## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having achieved success by one's own efforts, with little to no support from family members and others.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** My father was the quintessential self-made man.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a person

   **Source synonyms:** self-structured; self-formed

   **Source antonyms:** nepotistic; cronyish

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Made by oneself instead of bought or taken over.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a thing

   **Source synonyms:** homemade; homebrew; homespun; handmade; custom-made; made-to-measure; made-to-order; tailor-made
