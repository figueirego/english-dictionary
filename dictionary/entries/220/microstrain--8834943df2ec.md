### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A strain expressed in terms of parts per million.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The root mean square of the variations in the lattice parameters across the individual crystallites, usually across microscopic distances, &lt;ϵ²&gt;^(1/2). By definition, it cannot be negative. In contrast, macrostrain refers to the overall change in the lattice parameters caused by a composition change or temperature change or pressure change.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; materials science
