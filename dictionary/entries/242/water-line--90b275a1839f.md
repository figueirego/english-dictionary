### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The outline of a horizontal section of a vessel, as when floating in the water.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; manufacturing; shipbuilding

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any one of certain lines of a vessel, model, or plan, parallel with the surface of the water at various heights from the keel. In a half-breadth plan, the water lines are outward curves showing the horizontal form of the ship at their several heights; in a sheer plan, they are projected as straight horizontal lines.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; manufacturing; shipbuilding

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any one of several lines marked upon the outside of a vessel, corresponding with the surface of the water when she is afloat on an even keel. The lowest line indicates the vessel's proper submergence when not loaded, and is called the light water line; the highest, called the load water line, indicates her proper submergence when loaded.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The line corresponding to the surface of the water touching any submerged object or body.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The level at which water meets land along the shore of a body of water.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see water, line.
