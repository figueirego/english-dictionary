## Verb · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Pronunciation

### Forms

- spreads — present; singular; third-person
- spreading — participle; present
- spread — participle; past
- spread — past

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To stretch out, open out (a material etc.) so that it more fully covers a given area of space.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He spread his newspaper on the table.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To extend (individual rays, limbs etc.); to stretch out in varying or opposing directions. simple past and past participle of spread

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I spread my arms wide and welcomed him home.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To disperse, to scatter or distribute over a given area.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I spread the rice grains evenly over the floor.

   **Source synonyms:** sow; suffuse; pervade

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To proliferate; to become more widely present, to be disseminated.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To disseminate; to cause to proliferate, to make (something) widely known or present.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The missionaries quickly spread their new message across the country.

   **Source synonyms:** [sweep](../37f/sweep--dfd33796972c.md)

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To take up a larger area or space; to expand, be extended.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** I dropped my glass; the water spread quickly over the tiled floor.

   **Source synonyms:** [sweep](../37f/sweep--dfd33796972c.md)

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To smear, to distribute in a thin layer.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** She liked to spread butter on her toast while it was still hot.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To cover (something) with a thin layer of some substance, as of butter.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He always spreads his toast with peanut butter and strawberry jam.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To prepare; to set and furnish with provisions.

   **Example:** to spread a table

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. To open one’s legs, especially for sexual favours.

   **Usage:** intransitive; slang

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** disseminate; circulate; propagate; diffuse; put about
