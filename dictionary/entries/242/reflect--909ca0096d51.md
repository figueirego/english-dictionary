### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To bend back (light, etc.) from a surface.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** A mirror reflects the light that shines on it.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To be bent back (light, etc.) from a surface.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The moonlight reflected from the surface of water.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To mirror, or show the image of something.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The shop window reflected his image as he walked past.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To be mirrored.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** His image reflected from the shop window as he walked past.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To agree with; to closely follow.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Entries in English dictionaries aim to reflect common usage.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To give evidence of someone's or something's character etc.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The team's victory reflects the Captain's abilities.

   **Example:** The teacher's ability reflects well on the school.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To think seriously; to ponder or consider.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** People do that sort of thing every day, without ever stopping to reflect on the consequences.
