### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A flat stone used to pave a pathway, such as a walkway to one's home.

   **Example:** red brick pavers

   **Source synonyms:** paver block; paving block; paving stone; flagstone

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One who paves; one who lays pavement.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
