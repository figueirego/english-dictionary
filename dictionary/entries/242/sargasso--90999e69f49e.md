### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A brown alga, of the genus Sargassum, that forms large, floating masses.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** gulfweed; sargasso weed; sargassum

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A part of an ocean or sea characterized by floating masses of sargassos, like the Sargasso Sea.
