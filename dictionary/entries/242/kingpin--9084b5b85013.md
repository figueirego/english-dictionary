### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The axis around which steered wheels pivot; a bolt that holds the axis in place.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** automotive; transport; vehicles

   **Source synonyms:** kingbolt

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The pivot or connector between a tractor and trailer in the fifth-wheel coupling of semi-trailer truck.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** automotive; transport; vehicles

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The most important person in a usually evil undertaking or organization, often a druglord.

   **Usage:** figuratively; informal

   **Source synonyms:** big cheese; big enchilada; top boy; baron; big cheese; big enchilada; big gun; big kahuna; big man; big name; big noise; big shot; bigwig; big wheel; boss; grand poobah; head honcho; heavy hitter; heavyweight; high muckamuck; high muckety-muck; king of all one surveys; king of the castle; king of the hill; kingpin; macher (US); mayor; muckamuck; muckety muck; mugwump (masculine; usually); numero uno; panjandrum; poobah; rooster; somebody; top banana; top boy; top cat; top dog; top gun; very important person; VIP; wheel

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The pin at the centre of the triangle of bowling pins (originally the tallest pin in kayles). Sometimes also the headpin at the apex.

   **Usage:** ten-pin bowling

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The bolt holding together the truck of a roller skate or skateboard.
