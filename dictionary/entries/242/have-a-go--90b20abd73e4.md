### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make an attempt; to try.

   **Usage:** Australia; UK; informal; intransitive

   **Example:** I've never tried karate before, but I'm willing to have a go.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To attack someone physically; to have a fight.

   **Usage:** Australia; UK; idiomatic; informal; intransitive

   **Example:** I heard you had a go at Jack the other night.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To tell off (especially unnecessarily or excessively), to criticise.

   **Usage:** Australia; UK; idiomatic; informal; intransitive

   **Example:** My teacher had a go at me earlier, just for missing one sodding homework. I was fuming.
