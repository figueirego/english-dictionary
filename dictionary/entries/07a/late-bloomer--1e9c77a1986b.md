## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who reaches puberty or who matures comparatively later than is typical.

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person who enters a romantic relationship or engages in sexual intercourse significantly later in life.

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A person who achieves proficiency or success in a field significantly later in life.

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A flowering plant that blooms later in a growing season than other flowering plants.

   **Usage:** literally
