## Noun · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈfiː.lɪŋ/ — source labels: Australia; UK
- IPA: /ˈfi.lɪŋ/ — source labels: Canada; US

### Forms

- feelings — plural

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Sensation, particularly through the skin.

   **Example:** The wool on my arm produced a strange feeling.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Emotion; impression.

   **Example:** The house gave me a feeling of dread.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. Emotional state or well-being.

   **Usage:** always in the plural

   **Example:** You really hurt my feelings when you said that.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. Emotional attraction or desire.

   **Usage:** plural-normally

   **Example:** Many people still have feelings for their first love.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. Intuition.

   **Example:** He has no feeling for what he can say to somebody in such a fragile emotional condition.

   **Example:** I've got a funny feeling that this isn't going to work.

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**
