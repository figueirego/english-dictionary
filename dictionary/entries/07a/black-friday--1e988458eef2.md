## Proper noun

### Forms

- Black Fridays — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any Friday that is literally or figuratively darkened by catastrophe, or the anniversary thereof.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The conclusion of United States v. Scheinberg on April 15, 2011, after which major online poker sites stopped offering real money play to their United States customers.

   **Usage:** Internet; historical

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** card-games; poker

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The day after Thanksgiving Day, generally regarded as the first day of the Christmas season, and the busiest shopping day of the year.

   **Usage:** Canada; UK; US

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; commerce; retail

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The sales period involving heavy price reductions immediately following Thanksgiving Day, from Friday (the original Black Friday) through Monday (Cyber Monday).

   **Usage:** US; broadly

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Synonym of Friday the thirteenth (“a Friday falling on the 13th day of the month (and therefore doubly ill-omened)”).

   **Source synonyms:** Friday the thirteenth (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Good Friday, the Friday before Easter.

   **Usage:** obsolete; possibly
