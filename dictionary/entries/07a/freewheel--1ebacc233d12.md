## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A device in a transmission that disengages the driveshaft from the driven shaft when the driven shaft rotates faster than the driveshaft.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The mode or state of operation thus produced&#58; Synonym of neutral.
