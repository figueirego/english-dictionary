### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Synonym of Lake Tanganyika, a lake in Africa.

   **Source synonyms:** Lake Tanganyika (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A former British mandate and territory in East Africa, in existence from 1916 to 1961.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An independent country that existed between 1961 and 1964, coterminous with the former British territory, which became most of Tanzania.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A province (Tanganyika Province) in the east of the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Capital&#58; Kalemie, which borders the lake.
