### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To consume (a liquid) through the mouth.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** He drank the water I gave him.

   **Example:** You can lead a horse to water but you can't make him drink.

   **Source synonyms:** gulp; imbibe; quaff; sip; see Thesaurus&#58;drink

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To consume the liquid contained within (a bottle, glass, etc.).

   **Usage:** metonymically; transitive

   **Example:** Jack drank the whole bottle by himself.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To consume alcoholic beverages.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** You've been drinking, haven't you?

   **Example:** No thanks, I don't drink.

   **Example:** Everyone who is drinking is drinking, but not everyone who is drinking is drinking.

   **Source synonyms:** drink alcohol; booze; hit the bottle; hit the sauce

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To take in (a liquid), in any manner; to suck up; to absorb; to imbibe.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To take in; to receive within one, through the senses; to inhale; to hear; to see.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To toast (someone or something) with a drink, honour; to wish well (see drink to), especially

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To toast (someone or something) with a drink, honour; to wish well (see drink to), especially&#58;
   1. To express one's desire for the accomplishment of a toast, sentiment or event, to wish, hope (for), forward (especially as 'to drink the health (of someone)').

      **Usage:** archaic; transitive

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To toast (someone or something) with a drink, honour; to wish well (see drink to), especially&#58;
   1. \[with to ‘someone or something’\]

      **Usage:** archaic; obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive
