### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A function which maps any subset X of S onto another such subset (or possibly X itself) called the closure of X (thus providing a mathematical means to define closure).

   **Usage:** "on" a set S

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** algebra; mathematics; sciences; topology
