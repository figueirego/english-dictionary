### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A surrealist method in which one removes the staples from a staple-bound magazine, making into one "page" what had previously been more or less widely-separated pages; the resulting "page" can either be kept as is or used as the basis for a collage.

   **Usage:** uncountable
