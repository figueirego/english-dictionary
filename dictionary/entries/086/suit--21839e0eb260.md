### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To make proper or suitable; to adapt or fit.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To be suitable or apt for one's image.

   **Usage:** transitive; hairstyle or other fashion item

   **Example:** The ripped jeans didn't suit her elegant image.

   **Example:** That new top suits you. Where did you buy it?

   **Source context (verbatim):** said of clothes

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To be appropriate or apt for.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** The nickname "Bullet" suits her, since she is a fast runner.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To dress; to clothe.

   **Usage:** intransitive; most commonly used in the passive form

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To please; to make content; to fit someone's (or one's own) taste.

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** will build to suit \[on for-sale signs marking vacant lots\]

   **Example:** He is well suited with his place.

   **Example:** My new job suits me, as I work fewer hours and don't have to commute so much.

   **Example:** When's it suit you for me to call?

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To agree; to be fitted; to correspond (usually followed by to, archaically also followed by with).

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source synonyms:** agree; match; answer
