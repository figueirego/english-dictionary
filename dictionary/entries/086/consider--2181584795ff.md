### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To think about seriously.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Consider that we’ve had three major events and the year has hardly begun.

   **Example:** How can you know everything about everything, if you won't consider any dissent?

   **Source synonyms:** bethink; reflect; take; afterthink; agitate (rare); bethink; call to mind; chew; chew over; chew the cud; cogitate; consider; contemplate; deliberate; introspect; mull; mull over; muse; overthink (archaic); ponder; meditate; quander; reflect; rethink; reconsider; revolve (rare); ruminate; second-guess; think; think over

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To think about something seriously or carefully&#58; to deliberate.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source synonyms:** afterthink; agitate (rare); bethink; call to mind; chew; chew over; chew the cud; cogitate; consider; contemplate; deliberate; introspect; mull; mull over; muse; overthink (archaic); ponder; meditate; quander; reflect; rethink; reconsider; revolve (rare); ruminate; second-guess; think; think over

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To think about whether one will do (an action); to weigh as a possible course of action.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I’m considering going to the beach tomorrow.

   **Source synonyms:** think of; bethink

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To assign some quality to.

   **Usage:** ditransitive

   **Example:** Consider yourself lucky, but consider your opponent skillful.

   **Example:** I considered the pie undercooked.

   **Example:** I consider you (to be) an excellent friend.

   **Source synonyms:** deem; regard; think of; account (formal); appraise; count; esteem; see; censure (obsolete); consider; deem; hold; dub; find; interpret; judge; look upon; regard as; respect; think of; view as

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To look at attentively.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** She sat there for a moment, considering him.

   **Source synonyms:** regard; observe; attend (archaic); clock (slang); consider; give heed; heed; let in on; mark; mind (rare); notice; pass (obsolete); pay attention; pay heed; pick up what someone is putting down; privy; regard; take heed; take note; take notice

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To take up as an example.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Consider a triangle having three equal sides.

   **Source synonyms:** take

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To debate (or dispose of) a motion.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** This body will now consider the proposed amendments to Section 453 of the zoning code.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; parliamentary-procedure

   **Source synonyms:** deliberate; bethink

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To have regard to; to take into view or account; to pay due attention to; to respect.

   **Example:** He never seems to consider the feelings of others.

   **Source synonyms:** take into account; hear

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To believe or opine (that).
