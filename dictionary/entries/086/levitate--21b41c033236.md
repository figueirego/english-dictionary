### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To cause to rise in the air and float, as if in defiance of external forces such as gravity.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The magician levitated the woman.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To be suspended in the air, as if in defiance of external forces like gravity.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The guru claimed that he could levitate.
