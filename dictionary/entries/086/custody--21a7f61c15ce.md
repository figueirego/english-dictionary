## Noun

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The legal right to take care of something or somebody, especially children.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** The court awarded custody to the child's father.

   **Example:** The mother gained custody of the children.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Temporary possession or care of somebody else's property.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** I couldn't pay the bill and now my passport is in custody of the hotel management.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The state of being imprisoned or detained, usually pending a trial.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** He was mistreated while in police custody.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An area under the jurisdiction of a custos within the Order of Friars Minor.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** The Custody of the Holy Land includes the monasteries of Bethlehem, Nazareth, and Jerusalem.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Catholicism; Christianity; Roman-Catholicism
