### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To have a strong desire or ambition to achieve something.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** to aspire to / for / after / to do something; to aspire that something happens

   **Example:** He aspires to become a successful doctor.

   **Example:** We aspire that the world will be a better place.

   **Source synonyms:** crave; pursue; strive; yearn; dream

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To go as high as, to reach the top of (something).

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** ascend; mount

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To move upward; to be very tall.

   **Usage:** archaic; intransitive; literary; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** ascend; rise; soar; tower
