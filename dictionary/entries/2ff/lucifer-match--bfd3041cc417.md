### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A sulfur friction match whose head is treated with equal parts antimony trisulfide and potassium chlorate.

   **Usage:** UK; obsolete

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Synonym of phosphorus match, a strike-anywhere friction match coated with white phosphorus.

   **Usage:** UK; archaic

   **Source synonyms:** phosphorus match (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Synonym of friction match, inclusive of safety matches.
