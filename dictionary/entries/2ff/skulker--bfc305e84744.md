### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Agent noun of skulk; one who skulks.
   1. One who stays where they cannot be seen (often in a cowardly way or with the intent of doing harm).

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Agent noun of skulk; one who skulks.
   1. One who moves in a stealthy or furtive way; one who comes or goes while trying to avoid detection.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Agent noun of skulk; one who skulks.
   1. One who avoids an obligation or responsibility.

      **Source synonyms:** shirk; shirker; skulk
