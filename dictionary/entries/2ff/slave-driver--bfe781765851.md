## Noun

### Forms

- slave-drivers — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An overseer of slaves at work; a person who puts slaves to work.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An employer or supervisor who demands excessive amounts of work from an employee.

   **Usage:** colloquial; excessive; figuratively; usually

   **Example:** He's such a slave-driver! I can't believe he wants us to come in on Saturday.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Anyone who requests an amount of work from others that is deemed unreasonable.

   **Usage:** broadly; colloquial; excessive; figuratively; humorous; often; sarcastic; usually

   **Example:** Mom, I need a break after doing homework! You're nothing but a slave-driver!
