## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A broad term encompassing sexual assault, harassment, indecent assault, rape, and other sexual offences against a non-consenting victim.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** The use of sexual violence against civilian populations by invading armies.

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. (When contrasted with sexual assault.) A physical attack of a sexual nature involving penetration (as apposed to sexual assault which may not involve actual penetration).
