**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The period from year 1890 to (the end of) year 1899; almost the same period as the 10th (and last) decade of the 19th century, which, however, was the years 1891 to 1900.

   **Usage:** plural; plural-only

   **Source synonyms:** Gay Nineties; Naughty Nineties (British; English); Roaring Nineties
