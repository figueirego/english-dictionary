### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A rejection; a vote against admitting someone.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A black ball used to indicate such a negative vote.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** Regardless how many other people may have voted to approve a candidate for membership, a single blackball will reject the candidate.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A kind of large black sweet, a black-colored gobstopper.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source synonyms:** niggerball

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A substance for blacking shoes, boots, etc. or for taking impressions of engraved work.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A game, a standardized version of the English version of eight-ball.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** reds and yellows
