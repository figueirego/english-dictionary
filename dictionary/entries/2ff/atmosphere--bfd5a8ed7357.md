### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The gases surrounding the Earth or any astronomical body.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The air in a particular place.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The conditions (such as music, illumination, etc.) that can influence the mood felt in an environment.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** air; ambiance

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The apparent mood felt in an environment.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

   **Example:** The atmosphere for the football match was great.

   **Source synonyms:** feeling; mood

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A unit of measurement for pressure equal to 101325 Pa (symbol&#58; atm), approximately the atmospheric pressure at sea level.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
