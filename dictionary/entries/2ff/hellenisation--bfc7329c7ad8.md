### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The adoption of Greek manners and culture

   **Usage:** UK

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The act of causing a culture to become Greek

   **Usage:** UK

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The act of Hellenizing, making something Greek or Hellenistic in form or character, (cf. Latinize) such as by coining a word with Greek roots to express a concept that otherwise might be expressed in modern vernacular

   **Usage:** UK
