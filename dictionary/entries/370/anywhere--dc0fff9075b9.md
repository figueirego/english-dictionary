### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In or at any location.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** I don't know where I left my keys. They could be anywhere.

   **Example:** I'd rather be anywhere else.

   **Example:** For many 'home' is an affectation when used anywhere that 'house' would be appropriate, as in 'Home for Sale'.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To (in the direction of) any location.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Anywhere you go in France, there will be bread and wine with dinner.

   **Example:** I'm staying home today. I'm not going anywhere.

   **Example:** Are you going anywhere special this weekend?
