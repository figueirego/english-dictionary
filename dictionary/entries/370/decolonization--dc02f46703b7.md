### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The freeing of a colony etc from dependent status by granting it independence.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The reverse of colonization, i.e. granting back autonomy to a group.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; sciences; social-science; social-sciences

**Source sense R1.S3**

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences
