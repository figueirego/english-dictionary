## Proverb

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Although God allows challenges, adversities, and constraints to occur in one's life, He also provides other ways for one to survive and feel satisfied; (loosely, in either literal religious use or figurative nonreligious use) although one faces challenges, adversities, and constraints, there is always some other way to survive and feel satisfied; (moreover) something that seemed like a dispiriting setback may yet turn out to be a blessing in disguise.

   **Usage:** usually religious
