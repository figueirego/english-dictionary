## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A man who is accepted on the same terms, and as having the same worth, as other men in society.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A superior or remarkable man who stands out from other men; a leader or exemplar for other men.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Source synonyms:** man's man
