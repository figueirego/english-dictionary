## Adverb

### Pronunciation

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used immediately before or after a statement to indicate that there was no homosexual meaning intended, especially when such a meaning is a reasonable interpretation.

   **Usage:** informal; not-comparable; offensive; often; sarcastic

   **Example:** You look like you have been working out — no homo.

   **Example:** I love you bro, you're awesome; no homo.

   **Source synonyms:** pause; no Diddy
