### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The control mechanism by which a door is latched and unlatched.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of various devices designed to make opening a door easier.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
