### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To ponder, to go over in one's mind.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Idly, the detective thought what his next move should be.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To have (some statement) in one's mind; to say to oneself mentally.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** "I should phone my mother," I thought.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To communicate to oneself in one's mind, to try to find a solution to a problem.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** I thought for three hours about the problem and still couldn’t find the solution.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To conceive of something or someone

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** I tend to think of her as rather ugly.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To be of the opinion (that); to consider, judge, regard, or look upon (something) as.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I don't think it worth complaining about the leak in the roof, is it?

   **Example:** I hope you won’t think me stupid if I ask you what that means.

   **Example:** She thought it pointless starting before four o'clock.

   **Example:** I think she is pretty, contrary to most people.

   **Example:** Boxing is thought to be a dangerous sport.

   **Example:** It was thought that I was the mole in the company.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To guess; to reckon; to believe while admittedly being uncertain.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** We should/would have thought she could've washed her hands before, at least.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To plan; to be considering; to be of a mind (to do something).

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To presume; to venture.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. Ellipsis of think so.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; ellipsis; informal

   **Example:** These plants are dead. Uh, you think?

   **Source context (verbatim):** used to show obviousness or agreement

   **Alternative form of:** think so
