### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To reach or arrive at (a physical or abstract destination, or state of doing a certain activity).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I’ll call you when I get to the railway station.

   **Example:** Will you please stop waffling and get to the point!

   **Example:** Eventually we got to talking about my pay rise.

   **Example:** We will get to Toledo very soon.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To go to or be located at (a particular place).

   **Example:** Where has Jane got to? She was here just a moment ago.

   **Example:** So that's where my keys got to! I've been looking all over for them.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of someone or something that is or has been missing

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To be allowed to.

   **Usage:** informal; transitive

   **Example:** You won't get to have any dessert until you finish your vegetables.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To affect adversely; to upset or annoy.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** This job’s really getting to me. I don’t know how much longer I’ll last.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To attack, intimidate or kill; to corrupt or bribe.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He’s refusing to testify. I think the Mob got to him.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To begin (something); to get around to doing (something).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He got to his chores in the afternoon.
