### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who tattles (notifies authorities of illicit behavior) or is inclined to do so; a tattletale.

   **Example:** My sister is a big tattler; she always tells Mommy when I hit her!

   **Source synonyms:** informant; narc; stool pigeon; tattletale; abaddon (archaic); accuser; beefer; bewrayer; bigmouth; blabber; blabtale (obsolete); blabtongue (obsolete); canary; CI; finger (US; rare; slang); fink (slang); fizgig (Australia); gossip; grass (UK); informant; informer; jurat; leak; mole; narc; nark; nonce (UK; slang); outer; peacher; quisling; rat (slang); ratfink (slang); rooster (obsolete; slang); sneak; snitch (slang); snout (UK); squealer; state's evidence; stooge; stool pigeon; stoolie; supergrass (UK); sycophant (obsolete); tattler; tattletale; telltale tit; telltale; tout (Ireland); whistleblower

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Either of two similar bird species in the shorebird genus Heteroscelus (Tringa in some taxonomies).

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A device fitted to a vehicle to measure mileage etc.

   **Source synonyms:** tachograph
