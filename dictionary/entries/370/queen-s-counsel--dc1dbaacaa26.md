### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An honorific status officially conferred on senior or meritorious barristers (and occasionally other kinds of lawyer) during the reign of a queen.

   **Usage:** Canada; New-Zealand; UK; occasionally Australia

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A barrister or advocate appointed by the Crown during the reign of a queen.

   **Usage:** UK; historical
