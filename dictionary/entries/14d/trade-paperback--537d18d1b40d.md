### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A softcover edition of a previously hardcover book, usually produced with the exact same text as the hardcover.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** media; publishing

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A paperback book that is in one of the standard sizes for small paperbacks, generally for genre fiction.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** media; publishing
