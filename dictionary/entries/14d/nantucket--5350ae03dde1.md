### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A resort island south of Cape Cod, Massachusetts, United States, formerly a major whaling port.

   **Source synonyms:** Little Grey Lady of the Sea

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A census-designated place in the north-middle part of Nantucket in Nantucket County, Massachusetts, comprising about 12% of the land area of the island.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Ellipsis of Nantucket County, a combined town and county that includes the island of Nantucket, along with Tuckernuck and Muskeget.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; ellipsis
