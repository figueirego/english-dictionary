### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To vent; to talk or take action (especially in a ranting or unrestrained way) so as to relieve stress.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** Don't take it personally when he shouts like that. He's just blowing off steam.

   **Example:** I told her she could call me and talk any time she wanted to blow off steam.

   **Source synonyms:** blow off one's steam; let off steam
