### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make a mock-up or prototype version of something, without some or all off its intended functionality.

   **Example:** The carpenters dummied up a set of simple props for the rehearsals.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To make a fake version of something; to make a counterfeit.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To suddenly stop talking; shut up; to keep a secret; to refuse to talk.

   **Usage:** imperative; informal; sometimes

   **Example:** When he couldn't think of any more good lies, he just dummied up.

   **Example:** Everyone was discussing the new teacher, but when she entered the room they all dummied up.
