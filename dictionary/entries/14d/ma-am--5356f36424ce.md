### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A form of address for a woman&#58; contraction of madam.

   **Source synonyms:** 'm; marm

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A polite and formal way to address a woman.

   **Example:** Ma'am, will you be attending the dinner this evening?

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A respectful term of address to a woman of higher rank or position.

   **Example:** – Sergeant, have the platoon in formation and ready for inspection at 0800. – Aye aye, ma'am.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A title for a married woman.

   **Usage:** archaic

   **Example:** That is Ma'am Smith, the matriarch of the Smith families.

   **Source synonyms:** ball and chain (slang); better half (idiomatic); boss (with-definite-article); bride; dutch (UK; slang); 'er indoors (UK; colloquial); frow; geezer (UK; archaic; slang); her indoors (UK; colloquial); lady (historical); little woman (colloquial); little lady (colloquial); memsahib (historical); missus (colloquial); old lady (colloquial); old woman (colloquial); trouble; trouble and strife (Cockney; slang); wife; wifekin; wifelet; wifeling; wifey (informal)

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A female schoolteacher; a schoolmarm.

   **Usage:** US; archaic

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** education

   **Source synonyms:** schoolma'am
