### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A marine snail (Chrysomallon squamiferum) that lives near deep-sea hydrothermal vents in the Indian Ocean. It has a foot armored at the sides with iron-mineralised sclerites.

   **Source synonyms:** scaly-foot snail; sea pangolin; volcano snail
