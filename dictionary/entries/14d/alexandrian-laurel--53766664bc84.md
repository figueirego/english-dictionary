### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An ornamental shrub of species Danae racemosa, in the asparagus family, with glossy leaf-like stems.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An ornamental evergreen tree of species Calophyllum inophyllum, native from East Africa and India through the South Pacific which has strong wood and seeds containing a useful oil.
