### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. For a statistical sample a pair of values that delimit the interval for which there is a certain probability that the true value of some deduced variable lies between those values.

   **Usage:** plural; plural-normally

   **Example:** A sample of 1000 showed that the correlation between weight and height was 0.0167, with 95% confidence limits of 0.0142 and 0.0183.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences; statistics
