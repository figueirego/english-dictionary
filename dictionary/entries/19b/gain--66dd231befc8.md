### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To acquire possession of.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Looks like you’ve gained a new friend.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To have or receive advantage or profit; to acquire gain; to grow rich; to advance in interest, health, or happiness; to make progress.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The sick man gains daily.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To come off winner or victor in; to be successful in; to obtain by competition.

   **Usage:** dated; transitive

   **Example:** to gain a battle; to gain a case at law

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To increase.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To grow more likely to catch or overtake someone.

   **Usage:** intransitive; often; with-on

   **Example:** to gain ground

   **Example:** I’m gaining (on you).

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To reach.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to gain the top of a mountain

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To draw into any interest or party; to win to one’s side; to conciliate.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To put on weight.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** I’ve been gaining.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To run fast, often by a specified amount.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive; dated

   **Example:** Don't rely on that clock&#58; it gains.
