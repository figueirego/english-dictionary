   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

   **Source synonyms:** finale

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The optional final sound of a syllable or word, occurring after its nucleus and usually composed of one or more consonants.

   **Example:** The word “salts” has three consonants — /l/, /t/, and /s/ — in its coda, whereas the word “glee” has no coda at all.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; phonology; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** auslaut

   **Source antonyms:** anlaut; onset

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. In seismograms, the gradual return to baseline after a seismic event. The length of the coda can be used to estimate event magnitude, and the shape sometimes reveals details of subsurface structures.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; geology; natural-sciences
