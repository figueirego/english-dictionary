**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A modified dragon boat mounted on two bogies, one at the front and at the back, each with side-by-side pairs of ice skate blades as runners, and a tiller able to rotate the rear bogie. It runs on top of ice and is propelled by rowers with spiked sticks in place of oars.
