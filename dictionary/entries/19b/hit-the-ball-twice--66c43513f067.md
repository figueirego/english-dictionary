## Noun · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A rule ("law" in cricket) which when invoked results in the dismissal of a batsman (out), whereby the batsman has deliberately hit the ball with his bat a second time for a reason other than to protect his wicket.

   **Usage:** noun; phrasal; uncountable
