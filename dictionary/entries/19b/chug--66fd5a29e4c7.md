### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To make dull explosive sounds.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To move or travel whilst making such sounds.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** We were chugging along a back road when the engine cut out.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To move or travel at a steady, although not especially fast, pace.

   **Usage:** broadly; intransitive

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To drink a large amount (especially of beer) in a single action/without breathing; to chugalug. Usually chanted at the person who is drinking.

   **Usage:** colloquial; transitive

   **Example:** Chug! Chug! Chug!

   **Example:** I can't believe he chugged three beers.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To function or perform with difficulty, typically due to inadequate resources or performance limitations.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** My game chugs every time too many NPCs are onscreen.
