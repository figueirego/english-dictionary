### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Willful neglect of one's duty.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The new soldier did not clean his cabin and was scolded for dereliction and disobedience.

   **Example:** What he did was a terrible dereliction of duty.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The act of abandoning something, or the state of being abandoned.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Land gained from the water by a change of water-line.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
