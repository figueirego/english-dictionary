**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To deprive of vitality; to make lifeless; to weaken.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive; literal

   **Example:** Noncrushing surgical clamps avoid devitalizing the cells of arterial walls.

   **Example:** Economic stress has devitalized some shopping malls in the web era.

   **Source synonyms:** deanimate
