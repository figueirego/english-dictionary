### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Great respect.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The children treated their elders with deference.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The willingness to carry out the wishes of others.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** By tidying his room, he showed deference to his mother.
