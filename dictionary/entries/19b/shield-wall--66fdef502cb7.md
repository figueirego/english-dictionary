### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A defensive formation, used from antiquity through the early Middle Ages, in which soldiers stand shoulder to shoulder with their shields overlapping, creating a continuous barrier against enemy attack.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A barrier.

   **Usage:** otherwise archaic
