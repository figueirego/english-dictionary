## Noun

### Forms

- serologies — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The science that studies the blood serum, and especially the reaction between antigens and antibodies in serum.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The characteristics of the blood serum in a particular disease or organism.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A serologic test&#58; a blood test to detect the presence of, and often to measure the amount of, various components of the serum (usually antibodies, antigens, and immune complexes thereof).

   **Usage:** countable; informal; proscribed; sometimes

   **Example:** Diagnosis is presumptive by history and physical examination and is confirmed by serologies.
