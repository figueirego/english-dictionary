### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A kind of medieval Latin verse, generally alternative hexameter and pentameter, with rhyming at the middle and end of a line (that is, internal rhyme); also, the form of such verse.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** communications; journalism; literature; media; poetry; publishing; writing
