## Noun

### Forms

- first fiddles — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The fiddle part generally carrying the melody.

   **Example:** O'Neil played a lively first fiddle.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The player of the first fiddle part.

   **Example:** O'Neil had been first fiddle in the band for years.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. One who takes a leading part in anything.
