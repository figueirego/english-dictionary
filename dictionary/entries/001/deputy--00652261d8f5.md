### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One appointed as the substitute of others, and empowered to act for them, in their name or their behalf; a substitute in office.

   **Example:** the deputy of a prince

   **Example:** The deputy sheriff was promoted after his senior retired.

   **Example:** As the deputy store manager, he is able to fire staff.

   **Source synonyms:** lieutenant; representative; delegate; vice; vicegerent; number two

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person employed to install and remove props, brattices, etc. and to clear gas, for the safety of the miners.

   **Usage:** historical

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; mining

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The name for a member of legislature in some countries.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government
