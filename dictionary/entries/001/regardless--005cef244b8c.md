## Adverb · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ɹɪˈɡɑɹd.lɪs/ — source labels: General-American
- IPA: /ɹɪˈɡɑːd.lɪs/ — source labels: Received-Pronunciation

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Without attention to warnings or indications of bad consequences.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Tatiana knew the meat had passed its expiry date, but she ate it regardless.

   **Source synonyms:** anyway

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Anyway; irrespective of what has just been said.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The proposal may become law, or it may not. Regardless, we will adopt it as a club rule.

   **Source synonyms:** irregardless; irrespective; anyhow; anyway; at any rate; come what may; in any case

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** after all; algate (obsolete); always; any road up (British; dialectal); anyhoo; anyhow; anyroad (British; dialectal); anyway; anyways (informal); anywise; anywho; at all events; at any rate; be it what it would (archaic); come what may (idiomatic); hab or nab (obsolete); however (obsolete); in any case; in any event; regardless
