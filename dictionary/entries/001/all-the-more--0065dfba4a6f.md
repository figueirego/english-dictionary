## Adverb

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Even more; notably, but even more notably in light of additional information, either preceding or following the statement.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Lytle’s progress as a boxer is all the more remarkable when taking into account his unique circumstances.

   **Example:** I had to work all the harder with two people off sick.

   **Example:** If your crush likes football, that's all the more reason for coming with us to the game tomorrow night!
