### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To extinguish something, especially a flame, especially by means of a strong current of air or another gas.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He blows out the match.

   **Example:** The wind blew the candle out.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To destroy or degrade (something) quickly, especially inadvertently and prematurely; for example, to deflate (a tire) by puncturing it, to burn out a light bulb by overcurrent, or to injure a bodily joint.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I blew out a tire on some hard cornering.

   **Example:** I blew out an LED when I cranked up the power.

   **Example:** I blew out my shoulder when I grabbed a heavy box in a careless way.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To be destroyed or degraded quickly, especially inadvertently and prematurely.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The tire blew out on some hard cornering.

   **Example:** An LED blew out when I cranked up the power.

   **Example:** My shoulder blew out when I grabbed a heavy box in a careless way.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. In a sporting contest, to dominate and defeat an opposing team, especially by a large scoring margin.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The No. 1-rated football team proceeded to blow out its undermanned opponent.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To exhaust; to physically tire.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To force open or out by the expansive force of a gas or vapour.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To be driven out by the expansive force of a gas or vapour.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** A steam cock or valve sometimes blows out.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To talk violently or abusively.

   **Usage:** archaic; slang; vulgar

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To sing out, sing out loud.

   **Usage:** slang

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. To spend a lot of money; to splurge.

   **Usage:** slang
