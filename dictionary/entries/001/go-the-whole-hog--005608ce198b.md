**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To do something as completely or entirely as possible; to hold back or reserve nothing.

   **Usage:** US; idiomatic; informal; intransitive

   **Example:** If you can afford a new computer, you might as well go the whole hog and get it custom built.

   **Source synonyms:** go all out; go ham; go the entire animal; pull out all the stops
