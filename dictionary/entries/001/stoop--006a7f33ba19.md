## Verb · Etymology 1 · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /stuːp/ — source labels: Received-Pronunciation
- IPA: /stup/ — source labels: US
- IPA: /stʉp/ — source labels: Northern-Ireland; Scotland

### Forms

- stoops — present; singular; third-person
- stooping — participle; present
- stooped — participle; past
- stooped — past

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To bend the upper part of the body forward and downward to a half-squatting position; crouch.

   **Example:** He stooped to tie his shoe-laces.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To lower oneself; to demean oneself in doing something below one's status, standards, or morals.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** Can you believe that a salesman would stoop so low as to hide his customers' car keys until they agreed to the purchase?

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. Of a bird of prey&#58; to swoop down on its prey.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To cause to incline downward; to slant.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to stoop a cask of liquor

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To cause to submit; to prostrate.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To yield; to submit; to bend, as by compulsion; to assume a position of humility or subjection.

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

**Source sense R2.S7**

7. To descend from rank or dignity; to condescend.

**Source synonyms:** bend down — bend oneself forwards and downwards; crouch — bend oneself forwards and downwards; squat — bend oneself forwards and downwards
