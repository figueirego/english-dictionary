### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A serving of food or drink intended to remove food residue from the tongue, typically served between courses so that the diner can more accurately assess a new flavour.

   **Example:** Pickled ginger is used as a palate cleanser between sushi pieces.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Anything that serves as a temporary break or variation from the norm.

   **Usage:** broadly; figuratively
