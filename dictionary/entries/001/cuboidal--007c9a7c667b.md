### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Resembling a cube.

   **Example:** The alveolar cells are cuboidal in shape.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of, pertaining to, or having the shape of a cuboid.

   **Example:** A cuboidal block was formed.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of or pertaining to an epithelium with cells whose height and width are approximately the same (cube shaped).
