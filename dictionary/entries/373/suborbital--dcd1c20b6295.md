## Adjective · Record 1

### Forms

- sub-orbital — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Not reaching orbit; having a trajectory that does not reach orbital velocity and so must return to ground eventually.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The first rocket test was suborbital; it didn't even get off the ground but rather exploded on the launchpad.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astronomy; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Below the orbit of the eye.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; medicine; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** infraorbital
