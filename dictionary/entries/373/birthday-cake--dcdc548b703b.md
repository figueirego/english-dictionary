### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A cake, often iced and decorated, made to celebrate a person's birthday.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of several kinds of cakes commonly made to celebrate birthdays.

   **Usage:** US; attributive; countable; often; specifically; uncountable

   **Example:** Pat crushed up a package of birthday cake Oreos and sprinkled them over birthday cake ice cream.
