### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The ship occupied by the fleet's commander (usually an admiral); it denotes this by flying his flag.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; nautical; politics; transport; war

   **Source synonyms:** admiral; admiral ship

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The ship regarded as most important out of a group, e.g. a nation's navy or company's fleet.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The most important one out of a related group.

   **Usage:** attributive; broadly; often

   **Example:** The retail company's store in London is their flagship.

   **Source synonyms:** chief; primary; principal; showcase
