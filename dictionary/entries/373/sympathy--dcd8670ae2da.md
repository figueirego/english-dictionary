      **Source synonyms:** compassion; pity; bowels (archaic); commiseration; compassion; empathy; fellow feeling; mercy; pity; remorse (obsolete); rue (archaic; dialectal); ruth (archaic); sympathy; tenderheartedness; loving-kindness
