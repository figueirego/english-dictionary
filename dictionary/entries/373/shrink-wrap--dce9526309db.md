## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

- shrink-wraps — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A plastic film that is used to protect an item and which during application contracts so it tightly enclose it.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Please order another five rolls of shrink-wrap from the supply house.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The wrapping around an item that has been shrink-wrapped.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Please don't damage the shrink-wrap when you are boxing up the items for shipment.

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To apply shrink-wrap to (an item).

   **Example:** We spent all day shrink-wrapping boxes and now my feet hurt.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To reconstruct an extinct creature known from its fossils by depicting it with minimal soft tissue, yielding a thinner and less accurate representation of the creature.

   **Usage:** derogatory

   **Example:** Such shrink-wrapped models gave generations of schoolchildren mental images of dinosaurs that are probably much uglier and scrawnier than the creatures were in reality.
