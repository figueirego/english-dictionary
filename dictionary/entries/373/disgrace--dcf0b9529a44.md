### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The condition of being out of favor; loss of favor, regard, or respect.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The state of being dishonored, or covered with shame.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Now she lives in disgrace.

   **Source synonyms:** dishonor; ignominy

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Someone or something which brings dishonor; the cause of reproach or shame; great discredit.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** His behaviour at the party was a total disgrace! He was leeching on all the ladies, and insulting the men.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An act of unkindness; a disfavor.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** misgrace
