<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of, relating to, resembling, or containing brine (“water saturated or strongly impregnated with salt”), such as seawater or tears; salty.

   **Example:** on the briny deep

   **Source antonyms:** unbriny

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of a person&#58; difficult to get along with or to like; acerbic, prickly, unlikable.

   **Usage:** figuratively
