**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An identity stating that if A and B are matrices of size m × n and n × m respectively (either or both of which may be infinite) then, provided AB (and hence, also BA) is of trace class, det (I\_m+AB)= det (I\_n+BA), where I\_k is the k × k identity matrix.
