**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any feature likely to lead to the programmer or user shooting themselves in the foot.

   **Usage:** derogatory; humorous; slang

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; programming; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. by extension, generalised from (1.)&#58; An opportunity or object that allows the unfortunate to mostly figuratively but sometimes literally shoot themselves in the foot. A less obvious footgun may be a pitfall (1.).
