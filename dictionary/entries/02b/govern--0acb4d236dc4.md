### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make and administer the public policy and affairs of; to exercise sovereign authority in.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The old king governed the land wisely.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To exercise political authority; to run a government.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To control the actions or behavior of; to keep under control; to restrain.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Govern yourselves like civilized people.

   **Example:** a student who could not govern his impulses

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To exercise a deciding or determining influence on.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Chance governs the outcome of many card games.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To have or exercise a determining influence.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To handle, to manage, to oversee (a matter, an affair, a household, etc.).

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To control the speed, flow etc. of; to regulate.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** a valve that governs fuel intake

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To direct the course of, to guide in some direction, to steer.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive
