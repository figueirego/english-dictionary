### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A small ethnic group in Ethiopia related to the Agaw which historically practices a syncretic blend of Judaism and folk religion and speaks Qimant but has since been largely assimilated both linguistically and religiously into Amhara culture.
