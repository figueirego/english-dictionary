### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The outer pod of the tiny fruit of a number of species in the genus Zanthoxylum widely grown and consumed in Asia as a spice. Not closely related to black pepper or to chilli peppers.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Spice made from this pod.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A plant producing the pods, Zanthoxylum

   **Usage:** countable; usually
