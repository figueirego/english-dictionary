### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person or thing that bounces something, such as a ball.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A member of security personnel employed by bars, nightclubs, etc. to decide who can enter, maintain order, and deal with patrons who cause trouble.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Source synonyms:** doorman; chucker-out

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A short-pitched ball that bounces up towards, or above the height of the batsman’s head.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; cricket; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

   **Source synonyms:** bumper

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

   **Usage:** Internet

   **Source synonyms:** BNC
