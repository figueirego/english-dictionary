## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Commercials designed to evoke strong sensory responses from the audience as opposed to pitching a rational sales message.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Commercials that make you want to run to the fridge for a soda or drive to the restaurant for a burger are examples of one type of experiential advertising.
