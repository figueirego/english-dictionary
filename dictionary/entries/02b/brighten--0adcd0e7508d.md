### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make bright or brighter in color.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** We brightened the room with a new coat of paint.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To make illustrious, or more distinguished; to add luster or splendor to

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To make more cheerful and pleasant; to enliven

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** to brighten one’s prospects

   **Example:** Having Mark around the place really brightens things up.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To grow bright, or more bright in color; to clear up

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The sun starts to brighten around this time of the year.

   **Example:** The sky brightened as the storm moved on.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To become brighter or more cheerful in mood

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** She brightened when I changed the subject.
