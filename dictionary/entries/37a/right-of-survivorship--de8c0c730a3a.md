## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. (property law) The right of surviving co-owners to acquire a deceased co-owner’s interest in jointly held property.

   **Example:** joint tenants with right of survivorship

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

   **Extractor diagnostics (not usage labels):** empty-gloss; no-gloss

**Supplemental definition (evidence reviewed; source gap preserved):** The Concurrent estate section explicitly defines the term, matching the original collocation joint tenants with right of survivorship.
