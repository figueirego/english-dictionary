### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The scientific and cultural community engaged in higher education and research, taken as a whole.

   **Usage:** collective; uncountable

   **Example:** Academia continues to provide scientific education, despite attempts to turn it into a system of professional schooling.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Continuous study at higher education institutions; scholarship.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Not every university graduate wishes to pursue academia.
