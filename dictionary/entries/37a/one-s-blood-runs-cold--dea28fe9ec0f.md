## Phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One feels a physical shock upon realizing a direct threat to one's life, similar to the loss of blood in the brain. It affects the entire body for a few minutes, but does not cause the person to lose consciousness.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One experiences a visceral feeling of fear, horror, dread, foreboding, or dysphoria (sometimes with accompanying tingling paresthesias).
