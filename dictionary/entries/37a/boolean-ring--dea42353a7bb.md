### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A ring whose multiplicative operation is idempotent.

   **Example:** From the defining idempotency property of a Boolean ring it is possible to prove that such ring has to have the further properties that each element is its own inverse and that such ring must be commutative.
