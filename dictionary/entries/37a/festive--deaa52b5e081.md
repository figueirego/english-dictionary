### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having the atmosphere, decoration, or attitude of a festival, holiday, or celebration.

   **Example:** The room was decked out in festive streamers, with flowers everywhere.

**Source sense R1.S2**
