### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The planned movement of troops, vehicles etc.; a strategic repositioning; (later also) a large training field-exercise of fighting units.

   **Usage:** US

   **Example:** The army was on maneuvers.

   **Example:** Joint NATO maneuvers are as much an exercise in diplomacy as in tactics and logistics.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any strategic or cunning action; a stratagem.

   **Usage:** US

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A movement of the body, or with an implement, instrument etc., especially one performed with skill or dexterity.

   **Usage:** US

   **Source synonyms:** ease; wrestle

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A specific medical or surgical movement, often eponymous, done with the doctor's hands or surgical instruments.

   **Usage:** US

   **Example:** The otorhinolaryngologist performed an Epley maneuver and the patient was relieved of his vertigo.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A controlled (especially skillful) movement taken while steering a vehicle.

   **Usage:** US

   **Example:** Parallel parking can be a difficult maneuver.
