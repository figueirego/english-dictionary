<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The quality of being manly; the set of qualities, traits and abilities considered appropriate to men (as opposed to women or children); similarity to a man.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source synonyms:** virility; masculinity; manfulness; mannishness; maleness

   **Source antonyms:** unmanliness; womanliness
