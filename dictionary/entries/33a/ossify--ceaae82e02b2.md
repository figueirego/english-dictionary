### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To transform (or cause to transform) from a softer animal substance into bone; particularly the processes of growth in humans and animals.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Source antonyms:** deossify

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To become (or cause to become) inflexible and rigid in habits or opinions.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive; animate

   **Source synonyms:** harden

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To grow (or cause to grow) formulaic and permanent.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive; inanimate

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To calcify.

   **Usage:** rare
