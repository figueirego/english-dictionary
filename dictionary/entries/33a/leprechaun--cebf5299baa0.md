### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A type of diminutive, solitary fairy or sprite, traditionally depicted as a small, bearded man wearing a green coat and hat, often found repairing a single shoe; they typically possess hidden treasure, such as a pot of gold hidden at the end of a rainbow, and can be forced to reveal its location if captured.

   **Usage:** Ireland

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** arts; folklore; history; human-sciences; literature; media; publishing; sciences
