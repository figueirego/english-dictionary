### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To dilute with water; to add water to.

   **Example:** You need to water down the lemonade a bit more to make it less sweet, dear.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To make weaker, less effective.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To simplify or oversimplify; to make easier; to make less difficult.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** If you plan to teach this material to children, you may need to water it down.

   **Source synonyms:** dumb down

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To make less restrictive; to make more lenient.

   **Usage:** idiomatic
