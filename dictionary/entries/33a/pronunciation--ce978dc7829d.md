### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The way in which a word, phrase, letter, character etc. is made to sound when spoken.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** What is the pronunciation of "hiccough"?

   **Example:** In English, there are two pronunciations of the digraph 'th'&#58; voiced and unvoiced

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The characteristic sounds of a particular language, accent, or individual's speech.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** His Italian pronunciation is terrible.

   **Example:** Being a good newsreader isn't just about having clear pronunciation.
