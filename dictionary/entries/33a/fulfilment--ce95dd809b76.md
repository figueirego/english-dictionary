### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of fulfilling or the state or quality of being fulfilled; consummation; completion; realization.

   **Usage:** UK; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** To achieve fulfilment of one's hopes.

   **Example:** Fulfilment of contractual obligations.

   **Example:** We have yet to see the fulfilment of his potential.

   **Source synonyms:** dispatch

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A feeling of satisfaction obtained from one's aspirations or needs being fulfilled.

   **Usage:** UK; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** What brings you fulfilment in life?

   **Example:** Personal fulfilment comes from having a sense of purpose.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The activities performed once an order is received to fulfill the order; packaging, distributing and shipping goods.

   **Usage:** UK; countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business
