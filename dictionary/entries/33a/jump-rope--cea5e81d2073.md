### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The activity, game or exercise that involves a participant rhythmically jumping over a rope underfoot and overhead with both ends held in their hands, or alternately, held by two other participants.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** jump-roping; jumping rope; skipping; skip rope

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The length of rope, sometimes with handles, casing, or other additions, used in that activity.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** skipping rope

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A single jump in this activity, counted as a measure of achievement.

   **Usage:** colloquial; countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** rope-jump
