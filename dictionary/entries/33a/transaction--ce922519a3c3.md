### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of conducting or carrying out (business, negotiations, plans).

   **Example:** The transaction was made on Friday with the supplier.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A deal or business agreement.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An exchange or trade, as of ideas, money, goods, etc.; personal interaction.

   **Example:** I made the transaction with the vendor as soon as she showed me the pearls.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The transfer of funds into, out of, or from an account.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An atomic operation; a message, data modification, or other procedure that is guaranteed to perform completely or not at all (e.g. a database transaction).

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A record of the proceedings of a learned society.

   **Usage:** especially; plural

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A social interaction.

   **Usage:** transactional analysis
