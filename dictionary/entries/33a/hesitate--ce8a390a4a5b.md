### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To stop or pause respecting decision or action; to be in suspense or uncertainty as to a determination.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** He hesitated whether to accept the offer or not; men often hesitate in forming a judgment.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To stammer; to falter in speaking.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To utter with hesitation or to intimate by a reluctant manner.

   **Usage:** poetic; rare; transitive
