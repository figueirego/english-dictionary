### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who is hip, being keenly interested in the latest trends or fashions.

   **Source synonyms:** hepster; hippie; trendite; Corinthian; faddist (slang); fashionable; fashionist (archaic); fashionista; hepcat (dated); hepster (dated); hipster; sophisto (derogatory; slang); snappy dresser; trendite (slang); trendy; voguie; swell (dated)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person who is hip, being keenly interested in the latest trends or fashions.
   1. A person who rejects mainstream culture; an anti-conformist.

      **Usage:** broadly; chiefly music fandom

      **Source context (verbatim):** of the "Bohemian" sense

      **Source synonyms:** hepster; hippie; trendite; Corinthian; faddist (slang); fashionable; fashionist (archaic); fashionista; hepcat (dated); hepster (dated); hipster; sophisto (derogatory; slang); snappy dresser; trendite (slang); trendy; voguie; swell (dated)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A member of a Bohemian counterculture.

   **Source synonyms:** hippie

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An aficionado of jazz who considers themselves to be hip.

   **Source synonyms:** hepcat; hippie

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A follower of a fashion style prevalent in the 2000s and 2010s, typically associated with old-fashioned clothing, thick-rimmed glasses, and beards for men, and with alternative music and trendy food and drink.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A person who wears a hip flask (of alcohol).

   **Usage:** US; obsolete; Prohibition

   **Source synonyms:** vial villain; gentleman from Kentucky

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A dancer, particularly a female one during the 1930s.

   **Usage:** US; historical; obsolete

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Underwear with an elastic waistband at hip level.
