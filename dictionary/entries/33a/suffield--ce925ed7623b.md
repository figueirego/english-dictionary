### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A hamlet in Suffield-cum-Everley parish, North Yorkshire, England, previously in Scarborough district (OS grid ref SE9890).

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A hamlet in Cypress County, Alberta, Canada, named after Charles Harbord, 5th Baron Suffield.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>
