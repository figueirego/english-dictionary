### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Individually sustained verve or passion.

   **Usage:** UK; uncountable; usually

   **Example:** Valor wins the battle, not death!

   **Source synonyms:** prowess

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Value; worth.

   **Usage:** UK; uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Strength of mind in regard to danger; the quality that enables a person to encounter danger with firmness; a feeling of upstanding bravery against adversity.

   **Usage:** UK; uncountable; usually

   **Source synonyms:** bravery; courage; prowess; intrepidity
