## Adverb

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Even, furthermore, not to mention.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** He graduated this year — at the top of his class, no less!

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Just as, equally, as much/many as, not less.

   **Usage:** literally; not-comparable

   **Example:** Diet sodas are no less unhealthy than regular ones.

   **Example:** The driver had drunk no less than eight pints of beer.
