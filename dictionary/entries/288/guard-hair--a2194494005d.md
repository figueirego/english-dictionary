### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The top layer of fur on an animal, consisting of longer, generally coarser, nearly straight shafts of hair that protrude through the down hair layer.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
