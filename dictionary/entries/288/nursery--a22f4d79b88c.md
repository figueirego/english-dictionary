### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A place where nursing (“breastfeeding”) or the raising of children is carried on.
   1. Especially in European countries&#58; a room or area in a household set apart for the care of children.

      **Usage:** broadly; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A place where nursing (“breastfeeding”) or the raising of children is carried on.
   1. An institution for small children who not yet have begun primary school during daytime (when their parents work); a daycare centre.

      **Usage:** British; UK; countable; uncountable

      **Source synonyms:** crèche
