### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The actions or arguments of a sophist.

   **Usage:** historical; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>
