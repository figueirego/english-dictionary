### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The light reflected from the Moon, which seems to emanate from it.

   **Usage:** also; attributive; uncountable; usually

   **Source synonyms:** moonshine

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The silvery colour of the light reflected by the Moon.

   **Usage:** archaic; uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Synonym of moonshine (“illegally produced or smuggled spirits”).

   **Usage:** UK; archaic; dialectal; uncountable; usually

   **Source synonyms:** moonshine (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Chiefly in to do a moonlight&#58; short for moonlight flit (“an act of secretly leaving premises without paying the rent, supposedly at night by the light of the Moon; hence, any act of escaping at night”).

   **Usage:** countable; informal; usually

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A picture of a scene illuminated by light reflected by the Moon.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** art; arts

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A journey or event made at night when the Moon is shining.

   **Usage:** US; countable; obsolete; rare; uncountable; usually
