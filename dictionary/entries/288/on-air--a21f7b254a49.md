### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Broadcasting or transmitting through the medium of radio or television.

   **Example:** Julie is on air now, presenting the weather forecast.

   **Example:** Channel 9 will be on air in a few minutes, giving a live broadcast.

   **Example:** The transmitter remained on air all through the war.

   **Example:** The microphone was on air, and the whole country heard the outburst.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** broadcasting; media; radio; television

   **Source synonyms:** on the air

   **Source antonyms:** off air

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Being, or so as to be, broadcast or transmitted by radio or television.

   **Example:** Don't interrupt while the program is on air.

   **Example:** This show isn't on air at the moment — it's only on in the summer.

   **Example:** The popular television series was on air for ten years before it finally ended.

   **Example:** Was his interview on air?

   **Example:** We can't put this material on air.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** broadcasting; media; radio; television

   **Usage:** figuratively
