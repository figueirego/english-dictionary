## Noun

### Forms

- empty spaces — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An unoccupied space where something may be stored

   **Example:** You can park your car in that empty space.

   **Example:** You can put the key in that empty space.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An unoccupied area or volume.

   **Example:** The empty space at the sides of the page is called the "margin".

   **Example:** He spends all his time staring into empty space.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Vacuum; a space containing nothing at all

   **Example:** Atoms are mostly empty space.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A feeling of longing for someone or something that is gone; a sense of a thing's absence.

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Barren, desolate land, empty of life and people.

   **Usage:** figuratively
