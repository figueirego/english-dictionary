## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An amount of remuneration, reward, or other monetary recompense considered to be insultingly small.

   **Usage:** US; idiomatic; uncountable

   **Example:** If you sell one of those cars, your commission will be chump change.

   **Example:** I don't work for chump change. I quit!

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A sum of money considered to be insignificant.

   **Usage:** US; idiomatic; uncountable

   **Example:** He spent $300,000 for his new car, but that's chump change for a billionaire like him.

   **Source synonyms:** petty cash; pocket money; walk-around money; walking-around money; small change; pocket change; loose change; spare change; pittance
