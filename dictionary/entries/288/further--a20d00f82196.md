### Senses

<a id="r3-s1"></a>

**Source sense R3.S1**

1. To, at or over a greater distance in space, time or other extent.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; comparative form of far

   **Example:** I can run further than you.

   **Example:** I live a little further out of town.

   **Example:** How was your company doing ten years further back?

<a id="r3-s2"></a>

**Source sense R3.S2**

2. To a greater extent or degree.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; comparative form of far

   **Example:** Of the two civilisations, this one was further advanced.

   **Example:** I do not propose to discuss it any further. - Please, let me explain just a little further.

<a id="r3-s3"></a>

**Source sense R3.S3**

3. Beyond what is already stated or is already the case.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Chapter 10 further explains the ideas introduced in Chapter 9.

   **Example:** Don't confuse things further.

**Source sense R3.S4**

   **Usage:** conjunctive; not-comparable

   **Example:** It is overlong, and further, it makes no sense.

   **Usage:** not-comparable
