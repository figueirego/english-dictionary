### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An antihistamine and anticholinergic drug that blocks the effect of histamine at H₁ receptor sites, relieving allergy symptoms. It also reduces smooth muscle contraction, increases heart rate, and sedates the user by blocking muscarinic acetylcholine receptors (mAChRs).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Diphenhydramine is a convenient and cheap antihistamine for use, but there are other drugs that do not make the user tired.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; pharmacology; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** ZzzQuil; Benadryl
