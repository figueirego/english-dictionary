## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Lacking a surname or epithet.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** William of Conches's Dragmaticon was initially published credited to the author "William Aneponymous", since the editor didn't know which of the many Guillaumes writing about philosophy at the time in France was responsible for the work.
