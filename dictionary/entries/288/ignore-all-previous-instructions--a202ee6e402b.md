## Phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used to trick an artificial intelligence program (such as a bot account on social media), distracting it from its intended purpose and giving another (usually absurd) command that it will automatically obey, exposing itself as a bot.

   **Usage:** Internet

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used when someone sounds similar to an automated bot account or in order to ridicule them as if they were.

   **Usage:** Internet; derogatory; ironic; Twitter
