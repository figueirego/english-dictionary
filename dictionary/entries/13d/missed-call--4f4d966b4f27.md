## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

- missed calls — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A telephone call which the responder does not answer.

   **Usage:** telephoning

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A telephone call that is deliberately terminated by the caller before being answered by its intended recipient in order to communicate a pre-agreed message.

   **Usage:** telephoning
