### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Acting too quickly without considering the consequences and risks; not careful; hasty.

   **Example:** rash words spoken in the heat of debate

   **Source synonyms:** foolhardy; heady; impulsive; precipitate; daring; devil-may-care; headstrong; heedless; impetuous; imprudent; impulsive; precipitant; precipitate; precipitous; quick-and-dirty; rash; reckless; sudden; temeritous

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of corn or other grains&#58; so dry as to fall out of the ear with handling.

   **Usage:** Northern-England; archaic

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Requiring swift action; pressing; urgent.

   **Usage:** obsolete; rare

**Source sense R1.S4**

## Noun · Etymology 2 · Record 3

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ɹæʃ/ — source labels: General-American; Received-Pronunciation

### Forms

- rashes — plural

### Senses

<a id="r3-s1"></a>

**Source sense R3.S1**

1. An area of inflamed and irritated skin characterized by reddened spots that may be filled with fluid or pus. Also, preceded by a descriptive word (rare or obsolete), an illness characterized by a type of rash.

   **Example:** He came out in a rash because of an allergy.

   **Example:** She applied rash cream on herself to reduce the irritation.

   **Example:** A wet cloth should help with the rash on your arm.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** dermatology; medicine; sciences

<a id="r3-s2"></a>

**Source sense R3.S2**

2. An irregular distribution or sprinkling of objects resembling a rash (sense 1).

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r3-s3"></a>

**Source sense R3.S3**

3. An outbreak or surge in problems; a spate, string, or trend.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** There has been a rash of vandalism lately.

   **Source synonyms:** epidemic
