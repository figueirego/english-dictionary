### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who, in the Advaita philosophy of Hinduism, has attained nirvikalpa samadhi — the realization of the Self, Parasiva — and is liberated from rebirth while living in a human body.

   **Usage:** Hinduism

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A liberated being (not necessarily paramukta).
