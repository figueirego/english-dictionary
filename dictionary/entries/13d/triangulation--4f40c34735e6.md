### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A technique in which distances and directions are estimated from an accurately measured baseline and the principles of trigonometry; (countable) an instance of the use of this technique.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; natural-sciences; surveying

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The network of triangles so obtained, that are the basis of a chart or map.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; natural-sciences; surveying

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A delaying move in which the king moves in a triangular path to force the advance of a pawn.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** board-games; chess; games

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A subdivision of a planar object into triangles, and by extension the subdivision of a higher-dimension geometric object into simplices.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A process by which an unknown location is found using three known distances from known locations.

   **Usage:** uncountable; navigation

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; geology; natural-sciences; seismology

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The practice of repositioning one's group or oneself on the political spectrum in an attempt to capture the centre.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; politics

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The use of three (or more) researchers to interview the same people or to evaluate the same evidence to reduce the impact of individual bias.

   **Usage:** uncountable; qualitative research
