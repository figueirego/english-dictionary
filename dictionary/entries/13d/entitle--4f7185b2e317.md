### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To give a title to.

   **Example:** The deed entitled her to an undivided interest in the property.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To dignify by an honorary designation.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To give power or authority (to do something).

   **Example:** A passport entitles the bearer to travel to other countries.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To give rightful ownership.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To give a title to a book, film, play, etc.

   **Example:** His autobiography, entitled Life of a Driver, was a best-seller.
