## Adjective

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ɪnˈd͡ʒɛktɪv/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of, relating to, or being an injection&#58; such that each element of the image (or range) is associated with at most one element of the preimage (or domain); inverse-deterministic

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** one-to-one

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Loosely, having a certain generalizing property, abstracted from the study of ℚ as a ℤ-module. Formally, such that any short exact sequence of (left) R-modules beginning with M splits, or any of several equivalent statements&#58; See Injective module.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; module theory

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** algebra; mathematics; sciences

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a (left) module M over a ring R

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Loosely, having a property analogous to that which characterizes injective modules (see above). Formally, such that, given a monomorphism f&#58;X→Y in C, for every morphism g&#58;X→Q there exists a morphism h&#58;Y→Q such that h∘f=g; see Injective object.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; most generally

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** category-theory; computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

   **Source context (verbatim):** of an object Q in a category C

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Such that the objects (usually modules) involved in the resolution are injective (in the algebraic senses above).

   **Usage:** not-comparable; homological algebra

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a resolution
