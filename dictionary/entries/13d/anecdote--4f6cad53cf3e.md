**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A short account of a real incident or person, often humorous or interesting.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** tell an anecdote

   **Example:** relate a short anecdote

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

**Source sense R1.S3**
