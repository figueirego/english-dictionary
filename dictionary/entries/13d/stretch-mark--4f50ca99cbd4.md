### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of a series of red (or purple or faded), irregular stripes on the surface of the skin caused by rapid growth of the tissues lying just underneath. They may appear as a result of pregnancy, puberty, obesity, rapid muscle growth or family history.

   **Example:** Nobody in Cameron's family has stretch marks, and he thinks it's because their tissues grow slowly enough for the skin to catch up.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** dermatology; medicine; sciences
