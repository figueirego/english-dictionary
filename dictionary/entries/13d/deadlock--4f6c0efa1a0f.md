### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A standstill resulting from the opposition of two evenly matched forces; a stalemate or impasse.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The negotiation ended in deadlock, with neither party willing to compromise on the price.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An inability to continue due to two programs or devices each requiring a response from the other before completing an operation.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences
