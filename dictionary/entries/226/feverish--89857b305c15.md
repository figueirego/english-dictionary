### Pronunciation

### Forms

- more feverish — comparative
- most feverish — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having a fever, an elevated body temperature.

   **Example:** The illness made him feverish, so they applied cold compresses.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Filled with excess energy.

   **Example:** He worked with feverish excitement.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Morbidly eager.

   **Example:** a feverish desire to see her again
