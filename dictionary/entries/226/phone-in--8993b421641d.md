### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To deliver a message by telephone when etiquette demands the effort and respect conveyed by communication in person.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** Please give us the courtesy of showing up instead of phoning in.

   **Example:** Please give us the courtesy of delivering it in person instead of phoning it in.

   **Source synonyms:** call in

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To make a telephone call to a broadcasting station, especially to participate in a program being aired.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** We've got Brian phoning in from Phoenix. Brian, you're on the air.

   **Example:** I've been trying to phone in to your program for the last half hour.

   **Source synonyms:** call in

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To fulfill a responsibility with a minimum effort rather than the appropriate level of effort.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive; figuratively; idiomatic

   **Example:** phone it in

   **Example:** He thought he could phone in the scheduling process, but it takes sharp negotiations around here.

   **Example:** He thought he could phone it in. \[pronoun antecedent&#58; the scheduling process\]

   **Example:** We're sick of Alice's attitude. She's been phoning it in ever since Bob left. \[pronoun antecedent&#58; notional&#58; either "her job" or undefined\]

   **Source synonyms:** mail it in

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To make an unseemly, timid performance; to be restrained and timid when bold action is called for.

   **Usage:** transitive
