### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To paint or draw the likeness of.

   **Example:** I will portray a king on horseback.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To represent by an image or look.

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To describe in words; to convey.

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To play a role; to depict a character, person, situation, or event.

   **Example:** For my next movie, I will be portraying Shakespeare.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To adorn.

   **Usage:** obsolete
