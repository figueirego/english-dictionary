### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To wait in expectation of some event; to be ready.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** Please stand by for more instructions.

   **Source synonyms:** hang on; stay; abide (obsolete); back that train up; bide (archaic; dialectal); bide one's time (idiomatic); expect (obsolete); defer; hang on (idiomatic); hold on (idiomatic); hold one's breath (idiomatic); hold one's horses (idiomatic); hold one's water (idiomatic); hold out (idiomatic); mark time; stand by (idiomatic); stay; twiddle one's thumbs (idiomatic); wait

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To remain loyal or faithful to.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** Even though money is scarce sometimes, Ann stands by her decision to be a full-time mother.

   **Source synonyms:** (UK, idiomatic) go to the wall for someone; keep faith; abide by; hold on; keep faith; go to the wall for someone (UK; idiomatic); stand by (idiomatic); stick by; stick with

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To support; to continue to support despite things being bad.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** They stood by us all along and it's awesome to see them out here to support us today.

   **Source synonyms:** uphold
