### Pronunciation

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The theory/science of communication and control in living organisms or machines.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** control theory

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The art/study of governing, controlling automatic processes and communication.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Technology that is part of and controlled by a living organism (e.g. a cyborg); cybernetic or bionic technology such as prostheses.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>
