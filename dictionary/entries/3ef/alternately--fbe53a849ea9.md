### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In reciprocal succession; succeeding by turns; in alternate order.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Driving downtown is restricted to bikes with odd and even license plate numbers alternately by day of the month.

   **Source synonyms:** alternatingly

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. By alternation; when, in a proportion, the antecedent term is compared with antecedent, and consequent.
