### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A dangling modifier, especially a dangling participle.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** grammar; human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any body part that dangles, such as the penis, testicles, uvula, and sometimes tongue.

   **Usage:** slang

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A plotline that is metaphorically left to "dangle" or "hang"; in other words, the plotline is forgotten, phased out and eventually dropped, and thus a resolution is never achieved.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A large earring that hangs down far.

   **Usage:** informal

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. One who dangles about (loiters around) others, especially a man chasing after women.

   **Usage:** archaic
