### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of joining, or the state of being joined.

   **Example:** Their collaboration formed a fruitful junction of ideas.

   **Source synonyms:** juncture (formal)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A place where two things meet, especially where two roads meet.

   **Example:** Turn left at the next road junction.

   **Source synonyms:** juncture (formal)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A place where two or more railways or railroads meet.

   **Example:** The two rail lines meet at a major junction.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** rail-transport; railways; transport

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport
