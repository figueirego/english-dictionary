### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An enclosure usually made of thorn bushes, and latterly of steel fencing, for protection from marauders.

   **Usage:** Africa; East; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A stockade made of bushes and thorns.

   **Usage:** Africa; East; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A hide.

   **Usage:** Africa; East; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A hut.

   **Usage:** Africa; East; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A military or police post or magistracy.

   **Usage:** Africa; East; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A type of fertilizer rich in animal dung.

   **Usage:** Africa; East; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A method of composting in which animals are bedded on the material before it is used, allowing it to gather urine and dung.

   **Usage:** Africa; East; attributive; uncountable
