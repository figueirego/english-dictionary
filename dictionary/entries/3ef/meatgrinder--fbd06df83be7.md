### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A device for cutting meat into very small pieces.

   **Example:** Keep your fingers away from the throat of the meatgrinder.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An activity with a high casualty or attrition rate, such as a military battle or an occupational or athletic activity.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** The mountainous second day of the race was a real meatgrinder, with 20% of racers dropping out.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A process that is likely to be injurious in a non-physical way.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** Senate confirmation hearings are a real meatgrinder, discouraging people from government service.
