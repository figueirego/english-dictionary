### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An absurd, grotesque, misrepresentative or grossly inferior likeness or imitation.

   **Example:** A battlefield trial is a travesty of justice.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A pastiche, parody, or stylistic imitation; a burlesque literary or artistic imitation (typically of a more serious work).

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An appalling event, situation or outcome (especially in relation to another outcome to which it is grossly inferior).
