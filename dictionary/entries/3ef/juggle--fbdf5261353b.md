### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To manipulate objects, such as balls, clubs, beanbags, rings, etc. in an artful or artistic manner.

   **Example:** She can juggle flaming torches.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To perform various magic tricks or circus skills such as the diabolo, devil sticks, hat, and cigar box manipulation.

   **Usage:** dated; intransitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To handle or manage many tasks at once.

   **Example:** He juggled home, school, and work for two years.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To deceive by trick or artifice.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** I think they are juggling the company's books.

   **Source synonyms:** fudge; massage

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To joke or jest.

   **Usage:** archaic; intransitive
