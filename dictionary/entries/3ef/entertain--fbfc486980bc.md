### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To amuse (someone); to engage the attention of agreeably.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to entertain friends with lively conversation

   **Example:** The motivational speaker not only instructed but also entertained the audience.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To have someone over at one's home, or some other venue, for a party or visit.

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** They enjoy entertaining a lot.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To receive and take into consideration; to have a thought in mind.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The committee would like to entertain the idea of reducing the budget figures.

   **Example:** to entertain a proposal

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To take or keep in one's service; to maintain; to support; to harbour; to keep.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To meet or encounter, as an enemy.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To lead on; to bring along; to introduce.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s7"></a>
