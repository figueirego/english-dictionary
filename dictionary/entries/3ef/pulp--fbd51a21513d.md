### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A soft, moist, shapeless mass or matter.
   1. A mixture of wood, cellulose and/or rags and water ground up to make paper.

      **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A soft, moist, shapeless mass or matter.
   1. A mass of chemically processed wood fibres (cellulose).

      **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A soft, moist, shapeless mass or matter.
   1. A suspension of mineral particles, typically achieved by some form of agitation.

      **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A soft, moist, shapeless mass or matter.
   1. The soft center of a fruit.

      **Usage:** uncountable; usually

      **Source synonyms:** fruitflesh

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A soft, moist, shapeless mass or matter.
   1. The soft center of a tooth.

      **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A soft, moist, shapeless mass or matter.
   1. The underside of a human fingertip; a finger pad.

      **Usage:** uncountable; usually

      **Source synonyms:** palp

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A soft, moist, shapeless mass or matter.
   1. The very soft tissue in the spleen.

      **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

8. A magazine or book containing lurid subject matter and characteristically printed on rough, unfinished paper.

   **Usage:** figuratively; uncountable; usually
