## Phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A response used to indicate that speaker was in agreement with the preceding statement before it was made.

   **Source synonyms:** you're telling me; I know it; I know that's right; I know, right

   **Source antonyms:** I don't know

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An emphatic assertion that one has a solution, an answer, or an idea.

   **Example:** \- What are we going to do with this leaky hosepipe? - I know&#58; let's use it as a garden sprinkler!

   **Source synonyms:** eureka
