### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To pick out such parts (of a text) as may serve a purpose not intended by the original author; to mutilate; to pervert.

   **Example:** to garble a quotation

   **Example:** to garble an account

   **Example:** He's garbled the details, but the outline of the story is still pretty clear.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To make false by mutilation or addition.

   **Example:** The editor garbled the story.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To corrupt; to make unreadable, incomprehensible, or unintelligible.

   **Example:** The announcement was completely garbled.

   **Source synonyms:** scramble

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To sift or bolt; to separate the fine or valuable parts of (something) from the coarse and useless parts, or from dross or dirt.

   **Usage:** obsolete

   **Example:** to garble spices
