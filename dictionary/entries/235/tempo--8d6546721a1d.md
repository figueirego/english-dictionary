### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A frequency or rate.

   **Example:** 10 calls per hour isn't a bad start, but we'll need to up the tempo if we want to reach our target of selling insurance policies.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A move which is part of one's own plan or strategy and forces, e.g. by means of a check or attacking a piece, the opponent to make a move which is not bad but of no use (the player gains a tempo, the opponent loses a tempo), or equivalently a player achieves the same result in fewer moves by one approach rather than another.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** board-games; chess; games

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The timing advantage of being on lead, thus being first to initiate a strategy to develop tricks for one's side.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** bridge; games

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The timing of a particular event – earlier or later than in an alternative situation.

   **Example:** the tempo effect in fertility measurements

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The number of beats per minute in a piece of music; also, an indicative term denoting approximate rate of speed in written music (examples&#58; allegro, andante)

   **Example:** The conductor asked the orchestra to increase the tempo.

   **Example:** The song has a slow tempo that makes it relaxing.

   **Example:** The dancer kept pace with the shifting tempo of the music.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The steady pace set by the frontmost riders.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** cycling; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A small truck or cargo van with three or four wheels, commonly used for commercial transport and deliveries (particularly in Asian and African countries).

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A rapid rate of play by the offense resulting from reducing the amount of time which elapses after one play ends and the next starts.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** American-football; ball-games; football; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports
