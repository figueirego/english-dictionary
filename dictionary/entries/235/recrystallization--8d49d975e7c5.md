### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A technique for the purification of chemical compounds in which the compound is dissolved in a solvent and slowly cooled to form crystals.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The process of existing grains in metamorphic rocks changing size.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; geology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
