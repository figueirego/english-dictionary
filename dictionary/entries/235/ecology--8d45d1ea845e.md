### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The branch of biology dealing with the relationships of organisms with their environment and with each other.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any study of the relationships of components of a system with their environment and with each other.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** social ecology

   **Example:** linguistic ecology

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
