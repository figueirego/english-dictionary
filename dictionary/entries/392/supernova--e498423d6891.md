### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A bright and powerful explosion of a massive star which is sudden but brief, and causes the ejection of most or all of the star's matter and the emission of radiation; afterwards, the star becomes a black hole or a neutron star, or is destroyed.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astronomy; natural-sciences

   **Source synonyms:** SN

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
