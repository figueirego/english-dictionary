### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A village in Dolyna urban hromada, Kalush Raion, Ivano-Frankivsk Oblast, Ukraine.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A village in Dolyna urban hromada, Kalush Raion, Ivano-Frankivsk Oblast, Ukraine.
   1. A former silrada of Dolyna Raion, Ivano-Frankivsk Oblast, Ukraine, established in July 1993, merged into Dolyna urban hromada p. December 2018; administrative centre and sole constituent settlement&#58; Yavoriv.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A village in Kosiv urban hromada, Kosiv Raion, Ivano-Frankivsk Oblast, Ukraine, founded in 1694.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A village in Kosiv urban hromada, Kosiv Raion, Ivano-Frankivsk Oblast, Ukraine, founded in 1694.
   1. A former silrada of Kosiv Raion, Ivano-Frankivsk Oblast, Ukraine, merged into Kosiv urban hromada in June 2020; administrative centre and sole constituent settlement&#58; Yavoriv.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A village in Borynia settlement hromada, Sambir Raion, Lviv Oblast, Ukraine, founded in 1659.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A city, the administrative centre of Yavoriv urban hromada and Yavoriv Raion, Lviv Oblast, Ukraine, first mentioned in 1436.
   1. A raion of Lviv Oblast, Ukraine, established in January 1940, significantly expanded in July 2020 by the annexation to it of Mostyska Raion.

      **Source synonyms:** Yavorov
