### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used to express doubt or confusion.

   **Example:** Huh? Where did they go?

   **Source context (verbatim):** with rising pitch

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used to express amusement or subtle surprise.

   **Example:** Huh! I'm sure I locked it when I left.

   **Source context (verbatim):** with falling pitch

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used to reinforce a question.

   **Example:** Where were you last night? Huh?

   **Source context (verbatim):** with rising pitch

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Used either to belittle the issuer of a statement/question, or sarcastically to indicate utter agreement, and that the statement being responded to is an extreme understatement. The intonation is changed to distinguish between the two meanings - implied dullness for belittlement, and feigned surprise for utter agreement.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Example:** (belittlement) A&#58; "We should go to an amusement park; it would be fun." B&#58; "Huh."

   **Example:** (agreement) A&#58; "Murder is bad." B&#58; "Huh!"

   **Source context (verbatim):** with falling pitch

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Used to indicate that one did not hear what was said.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** Huh? Could you speak up?

   **Source context (verbatim):** with rising pitch

   **Source synonyms:** come again; pardon; what

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Used to create a tag question.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** It's getting kind of late, huh?

   **Source context (verbatim):** with falling pitch

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Representing the sound of heavy breathing.

   **Usage:** onomatopoeic

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Representing a chuckling sound.

   **Usage:** onomatopoeic

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A kiai, shouted as a limb is swung in attack.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; hobbies; lifestyle; martial-arts; military; politics; sports; war
