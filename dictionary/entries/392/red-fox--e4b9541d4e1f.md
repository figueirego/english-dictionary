### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A common species of fox, Vulpes vulpes native to North America, Asia, Europe and North Africa; small, with reddish fur, but larger than the arctic fox.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Certain varieties of species of the plant genus Celosia, such as of Celosia argentea.

   **Usage:** capitalized; uncountable; usually
