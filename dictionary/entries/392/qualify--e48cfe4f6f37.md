**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To describe or characterize something by listing its qualities.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To successfully fall under some category or description by meeting requisite conditions.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To make someone competent or eligible for some position or task.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

4. To become competent or eligible for some position or task.

   **Usage:** intransitive; reflexive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To certify or license someone for something.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To modify, limit, restrict or moderate something; especially to add conditions or requirements for an assertion to be true.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To mitigate, alleviate (something); to make less disagreeable.

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To compete successfully in some stage of a competition and become eligible for the next stage.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To give individual quality to; to modulate; to vary; to regulate.

   **Usage:** transitive
