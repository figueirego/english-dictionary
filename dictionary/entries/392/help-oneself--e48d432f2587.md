### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To serve oneself, especially to take food or other supplies, rather than wait to be served by another; used to indicate friendly permission or invitation.

   **Usage:** reflexive

   **Example:** If you want to take a shower, help yourself to towels; they're in the linen closet.

   **Example:** Is it all right if I get some water? —Oh, of course, help yourself!

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To take something freely; to serve oneself without restraint.

   **Usage:** reflexive

   **Example:** He said I could help myself to the chocolates.

   **Example:** He didn't even ask; he just helped himself to the special tea I was saving.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To exhibit self-control.

   **Example:** Sorry for eating all the chocolates - I couldn't help myself.

   **Source context (verbatim):** in the negative

   **Source synonyms:** contain oneself

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see help.

   **Example:** God helps those who help themselves.
