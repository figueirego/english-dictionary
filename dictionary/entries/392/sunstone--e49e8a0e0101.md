### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A translucent form of feldspar having flakes of hematite, used as an ornamental stone.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; geography; geology; mineralogy; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A stone, possibly Icelandic spar, said in late medieval sources to have been used by navigators in the Viking Age and later to find the location of the sun in an overcast sky, possibly via polarization of light.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
