## Noun

### Pronunciation

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A very short, unspecified length of time.

   **Usage:** colloquial

   **Example:** I’ll be back in a jiffy.

   **Source synonyms:** instant; jiff; mo; sec; tick; bat of an eye (idiomatic); bit; blink of an eye (idiomatic); crack (British; dated; slang); eyeblink (idiomatic); flash; glimpse (rare); half a mo; instant; jiff; jiffy; jot (obsolete); minute; mo; moment; New York minute; New York second; no time (idiomatic); sec; second; span; split-second; spurt; stound (archaic; dialectal); tick; trice; twink; twinkle; twinkling; while (Philippines)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A unit of time defined by the frequency of its basic timer – historically, and by convention, 0.01 of a second, but some computer operating systems use other values.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The length of an alternating current power cycle (1/60 or 1/50 of a second).

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; electrical-engineering; electricity; electromagnetism; electronics; energy; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The time taken for light to travel a specified distance in a vacuum, usually one centimetre (approximately 33.3564 picoseconds), but sometimes one foot or the width of a nucleon.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Ellipsis of jiffy bag, a padded envelope.

   **Usage:** UK; abbreviation; alt-of; ellipsis

   **Source synonyms:** bubble envelope; bubble mailer; Jiffy bag; padded envelope; padded mailer

   **Alternative form of:** [jiffy bag](../046/jiffy-bag--11a916b5cf3a.md)
