### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Incapable of being reversed or turned about or back; incapable of being made to run backwards.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** an irreversible engine

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Incapable of being reversed, recalled, repealed, or annulled.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** an irreversible sentence or decree
