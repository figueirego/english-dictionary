### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Showing or expressing empathy.

   **Example:** Be fair to people&#58; even those who manage to be empathic can't be telepathic. They're only human — they can't read your mind.

   **Source synonyms:** empathetic

   **Source antonyms:** apathetic; cold

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of, pertaining to, or being an empath, having an extraordinarily high capability of sensing the emotions of others.
   1. Doing so through psychological talent or training.

      **Example:** Be fair to people&#58; not everyone can be empathic, like Cathy is. Her gift is rare.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of, pertaining to, or being an empath, having an extraordinarily high capability of sensing the emotions of others.
   1. Doing so through psychicness, telepathy, etc&#58; that is, through either supernatural powers or neurotechnological powers.

      **Example:** I didn't know he was empathic until I realized that he had evidently read my mind with his cyborg implant.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** literature; media; parapsychology; pseudoscience; publishing; science-fiction

      **Source synonyms:** telempathic; telepathic
