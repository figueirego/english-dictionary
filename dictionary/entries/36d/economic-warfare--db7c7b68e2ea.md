## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The strategic use of economic measures (such as sanctions, embargoes, tariffs, or the freezing of assets) to weaken the economy of another state, often to achieve political or military objectives.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** The government shifted from kinetic operations to economic warfare to pressure the regime without deploying troops.
