## Verb · Record 1

### Forms

- wipes out — present; singular; third-person
- wiping out — participle; present
- wiped out — participle; past

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To destroy (especially, a large number or complete set of people or things); to obliterate.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** a tornado that nearly wiped out an entire town

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To physically erase (writing, computer data, etc.).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** nearly a year's worth of database records wiped out by disk failure coupled with lack of backups

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To do away with; to cause to disappear.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** trying to wipe out hunger and alleviate despair

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To exhaust (someone); to tire (them) out.

   **Usage:** informal; transitive

   **Example:** An hour of snow shoveling kind of wiped me out more than I expected.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To bankrupt (a person or company); to empty (a bank account); to erase (a bank balance).

   **Usage:** informal; transitive

   **Example:** They tried hard to hang on, but the Great Depression wiped them out.

   **Example:** Unexpected big-ticket expenses wiped out his bank balance.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To crash; to fall over or fall off (especially in board sports such as surfing, skateboarding, etc.).

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** shot into the half-pipe but then wiped out

   **Example:** A massive wave caused him to wipe out.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To knock (a surfer) off their board.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** A massive wave wiped him out.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports; surfing
