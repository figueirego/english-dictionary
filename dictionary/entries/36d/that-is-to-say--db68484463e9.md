## Adverb

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In other words. Used to introduce a clarification, simplification, or explanation

   **Usage:** conjunctive; not-comparable

   **Example:** One of the best things about works of music is that they are repeatable, that is to say that one can listen to the same work over and over without becoming tired of it.

   **Source synonyms:** that is; i.e.; better put; i.e.; in other words (idiomatic); put differently; that is; that is to say; to put it another way; what this means is; which is to say
