### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A northern horse mussel, any member of the species Modiolus modiolus of mussels of northern waters of Europe and the Atlantic and Pacific coasts of North America

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any in subfamily Modiolinae of mussels.

   **Usage:** broadly
