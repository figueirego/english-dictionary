## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of believing in something despite a lack of proof of its truth or existence, or the attempt of something without being sure of its possible outcome.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** He took a leap of faith by publishing his first book independently.

   **Source synonyms:** leap in the dark; act of faith
