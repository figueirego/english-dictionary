### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A large medal, usually decorative.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A cut of meat resembling a large medal.

   **Example:** pork medallion

   **Source synonyms:** medaillon

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A usually round or oval frame (often made of stucco) containing a decoration.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** art; arts

**Source sense R1.S4**
