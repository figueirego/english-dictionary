### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Layperson, someone who is not an ordained cleric or member of the clergy.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Someone who is not a professional in a given field.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** Carmen is not a professional anthropologist, but strictly a layman.

   **Example:** Let me explain it to you \[\[in layman's terms\|in layman's terms\]\].

   **Source antonyms:** professional

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A common person.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A person who is untrained or lacks knowledge of a subject.

   **Source antonyms:** expert; specialist

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Lay-sister or lay-brother, person received into a convent of monks, following the vows, but not being member of the order.
