### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Alert, awake; with one's mental faculties active.

   **Example:** The noise woke me, but it was another few minutes before I was fully conscious.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Aware of one's own existence; aware of one's own awareness.

   **Example:** Only highly intelligent beings can be fully conscious.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Aware of, sensitive to; observing and noticing, or being strongly interested in or concerned about.

   **Example:** I was conscious of a noise behind me. a very class-conscious analysis

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Deliberate, intentional, done with awareness of what one is doing.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Known or felt personally, internally by a person.

   **Example:** conscious guilt

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Self-conscious, or aware of wrongdoing, feeling guilty.

   **Usage:** rare
