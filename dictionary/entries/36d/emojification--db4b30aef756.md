### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The conversion into an emoji or emojis (e.g. the word smile into 🙂; the character ⚠ into the emoji presentation form ⚠️) or the creation of an emoji or emojis from an existing iconography.

   **Usage:** Internet; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A transformation in which an individual takes on features of an emoji, typically including yellow skin, a spherical head, and a personality dominated by a single emotion.

   **Usage:** uncountable
