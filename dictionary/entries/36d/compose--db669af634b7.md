### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make something by merging parts.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The editor composed a historical journal from many individual letters.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To make up the whole; to constitute.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** A church is composed of its members.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To comprise.

   **Usage:** nonstandard; transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To construct by mental labor; to think up; particularly, to produce or create a literary or musical work.

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** The orator composed his speech over the week prior.

   **Example:** Nine numbered symphonies, including the Fifth, were composed by Beethoven.

   **Example:** It's difficult to compose without absolute silence.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To calm; to free from agitation.

   **Usage:** reflexive; sometimes

   **Example:** Try to compose your thoughts.

   **Example:** The defendant couldn't compose herself and was found in contempt.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To arrange the elements of a photograph or other picture.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To settle (an argument, dispute etc.); to come to a settlement.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To arrange in proper form; to reduce to order; to put in proper state or condition.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To arrange (types) in a composing stick for printing; to typeset.

   **Usage:** dated

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** media; printing; publishing
