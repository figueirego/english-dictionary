### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. For what purpose.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** What is this tool for?

   **Example:** That's what friends are for.

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. For what reason; why.

   **Usage:** colloquial; not-comparable

   **Example:** He wants four copies of it? What for?

   **Example:** What do you want to know for?

### Senses

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. An unspecified punishment or rebuke.

   **Usage:** colloquial; idiomatic; usually

   **Example:** When your dad gets home, he'll give you (a) what for.

   **Source context (verbatim):** with "give someone"
