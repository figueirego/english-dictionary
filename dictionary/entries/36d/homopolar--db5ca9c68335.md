### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having symmetrically equal distribution of polarity; characterized by uniform electric or magnetic properties.

   **Example:** A homopolar bond involves equal sharing of electrons between atoms.

   **Example:** The homopolar generator produces direct current through electromagnetic induction.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of or relating to a covalent bond in which electrons are shared equally between atoms.

   **Example:** The bond between two hydrogen atoms is homopolar.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences
