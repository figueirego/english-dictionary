### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of investing, or state of being invested.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Giving your children a good education is a wise long-term investment.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A placement of capital in expectation of deriving income or profit from its use or appreciation.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance

   **Source antonyms:** divestment

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A vestment.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The act of surrounding, blocking up, or besieging by an armed force, or the state of being so surrounded.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
