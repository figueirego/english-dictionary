## Adjective

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. not caused by, or not causing, trauma or emotional distress

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** She was quite worried about her driving test, but overall it was a non-traumatic experience.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. not produced by wounds or mechanical stress

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The patient had a non-traumatic fracture of the femur.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences
