### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act or an instance of puncturing.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A hole, cut, or tear created by a sharp object.

   **Example:** There were two small punctures in his arm where the snake's fangs had pierced the skin.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A hole in a vehicle's tyre, causing the tyre to deflate.

   **Usage:** specifically

   **Example:** On the way back we got a puncture, and we were stuck at the roadside for three hours until help arrived.

   **Source synonyms:** flat tire (US); flat (US; informal); flat tyre (UK)
