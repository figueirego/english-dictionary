## Noun

### Forms

- thick ears — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A swollen ear resulting from a blow to the side of the head.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** In the football game he got caught by someone's elbow and came away with a thick ear.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A punishment or threatened punishment notionally taking the form of a blow around the head.

   **Usage:** broadly; informal; often in the phrase 'give someone a thick ear'

   **Example:** Say that again and I'll give you a thick ear.

   **Example:** I was cheeky to the Police Officer and got a thick ear for my trouble.
