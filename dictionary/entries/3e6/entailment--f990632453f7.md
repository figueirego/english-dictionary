### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of logically entailing, the state of being entailed, or something that is entailed.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Entailment does not imply causation&#58; if a set of premisses entail a conclusion, that does not mean (necessarily) that they cause that conclusion to be true.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The act of setting up an entail to restrict inheritance.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An entail.

   **Usage:** countable; nonstandard; uncountable
