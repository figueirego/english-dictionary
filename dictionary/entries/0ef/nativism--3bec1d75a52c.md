### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A policy of favoring native-born inhabitants over immigrants.

   **Usage:** US; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The policy of perpetuating the culture of the natives of a colonised country.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

   **Usage:** countable
