### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To relocate periodically from one region to another, usually according to the seasons.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Twice a year the geese migrate — from Florida to Canada and back again.

   **Example:** Twice a year the Minnesotans migrate from their state to the Gulf of Mexico.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To change one's geographic pattern of habitation.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Many groups had migrated to western Europe from the plains of eastern Europe.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To change habitations across a border; to move from one country or political region to another.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** To escape persecution, they migrated to a neutral country.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To move slowly towards, usually in groups.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Once the hosts started bickering in the kitchens, the guests began to migrate towards the living room.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To move gradually, especially from an intended to an unintended position.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Oil was able to migrate from the accessory gearbox into the magneto housing through the improperly installed gland seals.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To move computer code or files from one computer or network to another.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** They had finished migrating all of the affected code to the production server by 2&#58;00am, three hours later than expected.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To induce customers to shift purchases from one set of a company's related products to another.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** We were hoping to migrate the customers of the "C" series to the "E" series and the "E" customers to the "S" series.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; marketing
