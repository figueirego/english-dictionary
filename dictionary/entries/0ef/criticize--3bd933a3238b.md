### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To find fault (with something).

   **Usage:** British; English; Oxford; US; ambitransitive; transitive

   **Example:** They criticized him for endangering people's lives.

   **Source synonyms:** censure; pick at

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To evaluate (something), assessing its merits and faults.

   **Usage:** British; English; Oxford; US; ambitransitive; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** censure; appraise; judge
