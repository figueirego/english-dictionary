### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To seal with an airtight seal.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** They hermetically sealed the kettles to accelerate the cooking.

   **Example:** Some manufacturers isolated the electronic circuitry and hermetically sealed it in a metal capsule.
