### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A gun or cannon, especially a rifle, as seen from the imagined perspective of an uncivilized person or an animal.

   **Example:** The man pointed his thunderstick at the buffalo, and with a great noise and fire, the buffalo fell down dead.

   **Example:** We sneaked into the soldiers' camp, and stole five thundersticks.

   **Source synonyms:** fire-stick

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A narrow plastic balloon that is used as a promotional noise maker by hitting one against another.
