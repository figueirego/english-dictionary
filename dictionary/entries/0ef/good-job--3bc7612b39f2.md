## Noun · Record 2

### Forms

- good jobs — plural

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see good, job, for example, a task that has been done well, or an employment posting that is desirable.

   **Example:** He did a good job on repairing that roof. It wasn't an easy one, but he knew just what to do.

   **Example:** He has a good job&#58; lots of job security and an excellent benefits package.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. A good thing.

   **Usage:** UK; idiomatic

   **Example:** It's a good job that he was there to catch the vase when I dropped it.

   **Example:** Good job Sarah isn't scared of spiders&#58; I don't think I could cope without someone to remove them.
