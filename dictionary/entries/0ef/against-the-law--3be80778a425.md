## Prep\_phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Forbidden by law.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** It's against the law to knowingly allow an attractive nuisance to remain in place.

   **Example:** They knew darn well that it's against the law, and they did it anyway.

   **Source synonyms:** illegal; unlawful

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Being in principled opposition to a law's existence or wording, or to a school of thought about its application.

   **Example:** The mayor said that the law was passed last year, but also that he believes that most of the citizens are against the law and would support its repeal.
