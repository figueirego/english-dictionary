### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The conscious production or arrangement of sounds, colours, forms, movements, or other elements in a manner that affects the senses and emotions, usually specifically the production of the beautiful in a graphic or plastic medium.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** There is a debate as to whether graffiti is art or vandalism.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The creative and emotional expression of mental imagery, such as visual, auditory, social, etc.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Skillful creative activity, usually with an aesthetic focus.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** She's mastered the art of programming.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The study and the product of these processes.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** He's at university to study art.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Aesthetic value.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Her photographs are nice, but there's no art in them.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Artwork.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Sotheby's regularly auctions art for millions.

   **Example:** art collection

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A field or category of art, such as painting, sculpture, music, ballet, or literature.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** I'm a great supporter of the arts.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. (often in dichotomy with science) A subject understood best through intuition rather than methodology.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. Skill that is attained by study, practice, or observation.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. Contrivance, scheming, manipulation.

   **Usage:** dated; uncountable
