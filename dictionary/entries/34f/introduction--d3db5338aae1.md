## Noun

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˌɪntɹəˈdʌkʃn̩/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Forms

- introductions — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act or process of introducing.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** the introduction of a new product into the market

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A means, such as a personal letter, of presenting one person to another.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** David was feeling groggy at his introduction to Sophie, and didn't remember her name.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An initial section of a book or article, which introduces the subject material.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** This book features a preface by a well-known botanist, and an introduction by the author's mentor at university.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A written or oral explanation of what constitutes the basis of an issue.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** ECON 101&#58; introduction to economics

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** preface — initial section of a written work; isagoge; lead-in; lead; lede
