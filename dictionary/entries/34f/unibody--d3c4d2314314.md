## Noun

### Forms

- unibodies — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An automobile construction technique in which the body is integrated into a single unit with the chassis rather than having a separate body-on-frame.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** automotive; transport; vehicles

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A body of this type.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** automotive; transport; vehicles

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A shell that is the principal source of structural strength of an object; also, its design and construction.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A style of mobile phone, tablet, or laptop computer where the user cannot replace the battery because the device is manufactured as a single sealed unit.

   **Usage:** countable; euphemistic; uncountable
