### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A greasy or waxy substance that is used to style hair, making it look slick and shiny.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** brilliantine; hair gel

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Synonym of ointment, particularly thick waxy ointments scented with fruit used to beautify the skin or hair.

   **Usage:** countable; historical; obsolete; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** ointment (synonym; synonym-of); particularly thick waxy ointments scented with fruit used to beautify the skin or hair (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Synonym of cider, a soft or hard drink made from apples.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** cider (synonym; synonym-of)
