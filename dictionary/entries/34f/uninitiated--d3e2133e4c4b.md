## Noun · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ʌnɪnˈɪʃi.eɪtɪd/ — source labels: UK

### Forms

- the uninitiated — canonical; plural

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. People who have not been initiated and thus do not have the special knowledge of a particular group.

   **Usage:** plural; plural-only

   **Example:** His jargon-filled talk was gibberish to the uninitiated, but it was clear and concise to practitioners.

   **Source synonyms:** noninitiate; uninitiate; outsider; exoteric

   **Source antonyms:** esoteric
