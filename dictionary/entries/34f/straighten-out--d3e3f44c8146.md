### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make straight.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Straighten out your necktie and comb your hair.

   **Source synonyms:** [straighten](../365/straighten--d942f0a84262.md)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To correct or rectify.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** I hope they can straighten out the problem with my bill soon.

   **Source synonyms:** sort out

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To eliminate confusion from or concerning.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** As soon as I straighten out which of the twins is which, I'll start calling them by their names.

   **Source synonyms:** figure out; sort out; work out

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To correct; to stop doing something wrong.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** It is not enough to stand aside and hope problems straighten out on their own.

   **Source synonyms:** work out

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To tidy, neaten, or organize.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** sort out; tidy up

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To be made a made man in a Mafia organization.

   **Usage:** transitive; Mafia slang

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To make heterosexual.

   **Usage:** informal; transitive
