### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Mode of rule or management.

   **Example:** a prison regime

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A form of government, or the government in power, particularly an authoritarian or totalitarian one.

   **Example:** the dictator's regime

   **Example:** Heaven will eliminate the tyrannical regimes.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A period of rule.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A regulated system; a regimen.

   **Example:** a fitness regime

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A division of a Mafia crime family, led by a caporegime.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

   **Example:** A typical annual water level regime would include a gradual summer drawdown beginning in early May.
