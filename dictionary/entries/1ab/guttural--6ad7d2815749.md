**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of, relating to, or connected to the throat.

   **Example:** guttural duct of the ear; guttural pouch infection

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Having a place of articulation towards the back of the mouth; in modern use, uvular, pharyngeal, or glottal; in earlier or non-technical use, also including velar.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; phonetics; phonology; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Sounding harsh and throaty.

   **Example:** Arabic is considered a very guttural language, with many harsh consonants.
