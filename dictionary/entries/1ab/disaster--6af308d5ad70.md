### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An unexpected natural or man-made catastrophe of substantial extent causing significant physical damage or destruction, loss of life or sometimes permanent change to the natural environment.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** People would suffer disasters when society's morality degenerates.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An unfortunate or unpleasant occurrence, especially one resulting from an accident or something going wrong.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An wholly unsatisfactory result caused by something going wrong or being done poorly; a flop or failure.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** the cake was a disaster.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A skateboard trick involving a 180-degree ollie, landing on the center of the board with the front trucks facing towards the ramp and the back trucks over the lip. The skater then leans forwards to return in the ramp.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
