### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Wavering; lacking in commitment, certainty, support, and (often) clarity; namby-pamby.

   **Example:** Not wanting to be pressed for details, public relations gave a wishy-washy answer.

   **Source synonyms:** vacillating; vacillatory

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Thin or watery.

   **Example:** The wishy-washy orange juice served by the cafeteria not only failed to be sweet; it was barely orange.

   **Source synonyms:** watered-down; diluted; dilute
