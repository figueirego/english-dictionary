### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A mark of divine protection imposed on Cain, a biblical figure, for murdering his brother Abel.

   **Example:** The Bible vaguely describes the mark of Cain; it does not specify its exact nature.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A stigma of shame.

   **Usage:** rare

   **Example:** When the engineer's design failed, he received a mark of Cain causing others to choose not to work with him.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The darker skin found on people of non-European descent, believed to result from being a supposed descendant of Cain.

   **Usage:** derogatory; ethnic; obsolete; offensive; slur
