### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To change the culture of (a person) by the influence of another culture, especially a more dominant culture.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To cause (a person) to acquire the culture of a society (or of a region, industry, or company), as in the case of children growing up in that culture, immigrants learning that culture, or new hires learning the ropes of a job.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** socialize

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To be changed by acculturation.

   **Usage:** intransitive
