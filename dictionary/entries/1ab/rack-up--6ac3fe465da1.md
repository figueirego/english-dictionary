### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To arrange in a rack.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** At the start of the game, the referee racks up the red balls.

   **Example:** If you rack up, I'll break.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; billiards; games; hobbies; lifestyle; snooker; sports

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To gain (points etc.; in a game or sport), to accumulate.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** Chelsea racked up another 3 points at home to Bolton.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To acquire, to gather together.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** By the age of 18, he had already racked up thousands of dollars in debt.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

<a id="r1-s5"></a>
