## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A state or situation that appears to be completely disorganised, but where some things are under control.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** We arrived home to a scene of controlled chaos, with painters, electricians, plumbers, and workmen of all shapes and colours all over the house.
