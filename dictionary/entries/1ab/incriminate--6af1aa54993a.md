### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To accuse or bring criminal charges against.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The newspapers incriminated the innocent man unjustly.

   **Source synonyms:** criminate; inculpate; indict

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To indicate the guilt of.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** We have all sorts of evidence which incriminates you.
