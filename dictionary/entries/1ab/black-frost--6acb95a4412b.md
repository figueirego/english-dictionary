### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Cold so intense as to freeze vegetation and turn it black, without the formation of hoar frost.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; climatology; meteorology; natural-sciences

   **Source synonyms:** killing frost

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A mist of very cold droplets that freeze upon impact and coat objects (such as parts of a ship) with ice, potentially causing a ship to capsize.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport
