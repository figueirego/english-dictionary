### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. With respect to an integer base b, with b &gt; 1, a composite integer n such that bⁿ⁻¹ is congruent to one modulo n.

   **Example:** 341 is a Fermat pseudoprime to the base two.
