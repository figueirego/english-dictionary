### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A gathering to pick fruit.

   **Example:** We went to a strawberry picking last June.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Items remaining after others have selected the best; scraps, as of food.

   **Usage:** usually pluralized

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. Income or other gains, especially if obtained in an unscrupulous or objectionable manner.

   **Usage:** usually pluralized

   **Example:** easy pickings, rich pickings

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. Something picked or pulled out.

   **Example:** The schoolboy flicked his nose pickings across the classroom.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>
