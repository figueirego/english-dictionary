### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. After an ampulla, as for example (usually, more specifically) downstream of the ampulla of Vater in the lumen of the duodenum; structures and events that are postampullary thus exist in the midgut rather than in the foregut.

   **Usage:** not-comparable
