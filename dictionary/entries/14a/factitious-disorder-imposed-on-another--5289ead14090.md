### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A factitious (artificial) mental disorder in which someone feigns a health issue in another person, typically a caregiver onto their child, in order to garner attention or sympathy.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; medicine; psychiatry; psychology; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** FDIA; fabricated or induced illness by carers; factitious disorder by proxy; Meadow's syndrome; Münchausen syndrome by proxy
