### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To discharge through pores or incisions, as moisture or other liquid matter; to give out.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To flow out through the pores.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To give off or radiate a certain quality or emotion, often strongly.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Wearing that suit, Jasper just exudes class.
