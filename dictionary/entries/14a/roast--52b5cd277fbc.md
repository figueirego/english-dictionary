### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To cook food by heating in an oven or over a fire without covering, resulting in a crisp, possibly even slightly charred appearance.

   **Usage:** ergative; intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** to roast meat on a spit

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To cook by surrounding with hot embers, ashes, sand, etc.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to roast a potato in ashes

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To process by drying through exposure to sun or artificial heat.

   **Usage:** ergative; intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** Coffee beans need roasting before use.

   **Example:** to roast chestnuts or peanuts

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To heat to excess; to heat violently; to burn.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To admonish someone vigorously.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** I'm late home for the fourth time this week; my mate will really roast me this time.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To subject to bantering, severely criticize, sometimes as a comedy routine.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** He roasted a bully who then fought with him.

   **Example:** The class clown enjoys being roasted by mates as well as staff.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To dissipate the volatile parts of by heat, as ores.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** arts; crafts; engineering; hobbies; lifestyle; metallurgy; metalworking; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A piece of meat suited to roasting; meat that has been roasted.

   **Example:** Serve the roast with gravy and mashed potatoes.

   **Source synonyms:** joint

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. A meal consisting of roast foods.

   **Example:** Come over this weekend for Sunday roast.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. The degree to which something, especially coffee, is roasted.

   **Example:** Dark roast means that the coffee bean has been roasted to a higher temperature and for a longer period of time than in light roast.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. An instance of being severely admonished, criticized, roasted.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. A comical event, originally fraternal, where a person is subjected to verbal attack, yet may be praised by sarcasm and jokes.

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. A social event at which food is roasted and eaten.

   **Usage:** Canada; US

   **Example:** On Memorial Day we hosted a wiener roast in the backyard.

**Source sense R2.S7**
