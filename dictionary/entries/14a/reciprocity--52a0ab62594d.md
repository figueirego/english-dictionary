## Noun

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The characteristic of being reciprocal, e.g. of a relationship between people.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** In a friendship, reciprocity occurs where the contribution of each party meets the expectations of the other party.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A reciprocal relationship.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** reciprocation

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A relation of mutual dependence or action or influence.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A reciprocal construction involves two noun phrases where each of the participants occupies both the role of agent and patient with respect to the other. see&#58; Wikipedia&#58;Reciprocal pronoun.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** grammar; human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The mutual exchange of rights, privileges or obligations between nations. see&#58; Wikipedia&#58;Reciprocity (international relations).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; international law

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The responses of individuals to the actions of others.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; psychology; sciences
