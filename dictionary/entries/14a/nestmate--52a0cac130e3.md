### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One with which a nest is shared, such as a baby bird and its siblings or figuratively a child with children it is raised with.

   **Example:** The baby bird may snuggle with its nestmates for warmth but when food is offered they are strict competitors.
