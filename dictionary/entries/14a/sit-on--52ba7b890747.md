## Verb

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To block, suppress, restrain (something).

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** The chairman sat on the report until the end of the legislative session.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To restrain (a person).

   **Usage:** idiomatic; informal; transitive

   **Example:** He started to act up, but she sat on him.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To take no action on (something); to hold (something) in reserve without actually using.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** I sent the boss my proposal three weeks ago and he's just been sitting on it.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To be a member of (something).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He sits on the appropriations committee.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To hold an official inquiry regarding (something); to deliberate about (something).

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To insert (something) into one's vagina or anus from below.

   **Usage:** euphemistic; transitive

   **Example:** Let me sit on it.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see sit, on.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The cat sat on the mat.
