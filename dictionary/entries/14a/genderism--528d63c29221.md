## Noun

### Forms

- genderisms — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A belief that gender is rigid, usually a belief that it is binary, comprising male and female, and that the aspects of a person's gender are inherently linked to their sex at birth.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Belief that one gender is superior; discrimination on the basis of gender. (Compare sexism.)

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Opposition to traditional gender roles; belief that gender exists separate from sex; "gender ideology"; a variously-defined catch-all for things like transgender rights and feminism to which antigenderism is opposed.

   **Usage:** countable; derogatory; uncountable

   **Source context (verbatim):** in right-wing usage

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A gendered or gender-stereotyped behaviour, activity or statement.

   **Usage:** countable
