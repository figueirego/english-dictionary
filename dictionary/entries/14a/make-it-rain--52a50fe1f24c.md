### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To bring prosperity or work to an enterprise by selling, inventing or other productive or successful activity.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** It's time to make it rain, my friend!

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To throw a substantial amount of paper money so that it falls on a crowd, audience, performer, or group of performers, often as a way to show off one's wealth.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** John has a propensity to make it rain at parties when he is drunk.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see make, it, rain.

   **Example:** I asked higher powers to make it rain to save our crops.
