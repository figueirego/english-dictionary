<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To wound or cause physical harm to a living creature.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The rugby team's star player got injured in a violent collision.

   **Example:** I injured my ankle playing tennis.

   **Source synonyms:** harm; wound; traumatize; abuse; annoy (archaic); bewound; damage; damnify (obsolete); dere (UK; archaic; dialectal); do damage to; do for (UK); do ill; do violence to; forwork (archaic); grieve (archaic); harm; harrow; hurt; injure; maul; mayhem (archaic); misdo (archaic); put someone in hospital (UK); scaith (Scotland); scathe; vulnerate (archaic); wet (slang); wound
