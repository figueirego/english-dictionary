### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A non-autonomous entity, generally small and having few residents, which claims to be a sovereign state and mimics the actions of a state (with varying degrees of seriousness), but lacks any legal recognition and exists only in theory or in the mind of its creator; a micronational entity.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; politics

   **Source synonyms:** countercountry; ephemeral state; microcountry; micropatria; MN; mnation; mrnation; model country; nationette; project nation

   **Source antonyms:** country; macronation; nation; sovereign state

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Synonym of microstate (“a sovereign state which has a very small land area and population”).

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; politics

   **Source synonyms:** mini-country; minination; ministate; nationette; microstate (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A very small nation (“historically constituted, stable community of people, formed based on a common economic life, ethnicity, language, religion, and/or territory, and manifested in a common culture”) not amounting to a sovereign state.

   **Usage:** uncommon

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; politics

   **Source antonyms:** macronation
