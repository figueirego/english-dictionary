### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used while giving someone something. Here you are; there you go.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Synonym of there you have it.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Source synonyms:** there you have it (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Said to indicate that one has finished talking, or to express finality or fatalism, particularly regarding unpleasant news.

   **Usage:** UK; idiomatic

   **Example:** Right, the committee has considered your request and denied it, so there you are.
