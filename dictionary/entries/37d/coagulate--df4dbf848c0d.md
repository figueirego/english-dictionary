### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To become congealed; to convert from a liquid to a semisolid mass.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** In cheese making, milk coagulates into curds that become cheese.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To cause to congeal.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Rennet coagulates milk; heat coagulates the white of an egg.

### Related words

**Source antonyms:** dissolve; melt
