### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Happening or occurring during daylight, or primarily active during that time.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Most birds are diurnal.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Said of a flower that is open, or releasing its perfume during daylight hours, but not at night.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Having a daily cycle that is completed every 24 hours, usually referring to tasks, processes, tides, or sunrise to sunset; circadian.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Done once every day; daily, quotidian.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; uncommon

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Published daily.

   **Usage:** archaic; not-comparable
