   **Usage:** broadly

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A period of intense speculation in a market, causing prices to rise quickly to irrational levels as the metaphorical bubble expands, and then fall even more quickly as the bubble bursts.
