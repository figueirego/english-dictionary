### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To remove a sense of personal identity or individual character from something; to anonymize.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To present (something) as an impersonal object.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To suffer an episode of depersonalization.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** He's depersonalizing right now, so he's considering checking himself into the hospital.
