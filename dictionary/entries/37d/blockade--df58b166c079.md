### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The physical blocking or surrounding of a place, especially a port, in order to prevent commerce and traffic in or out.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any form of formal isolation or inhibition of something, especially with the force of law or arms.

   **Usage:** broadly

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The ships or other forces used to effect a naval blockade.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Inhibition of the activity (function) of chemical messengers or their receptors, such as (often) receptor antagonism.

   **Example:** beta-blockade

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; medicine; natural-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The act of preventing an opponent's pawn moving by placing a piece in front of it.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** board-games; chess; games

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Synonym of prime.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** backgammon; games

   **Source synonyms:** prime (synonym; synonym-of)
