## Noun

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. That which is affirmed; a declaration that something is true.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Her success was an affirmation of all her hard work.

   **Example:** The ceremony included an affirmation of loyalty.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A solemn pledge (to tell the truth, to bear allegiance, etc.), legally equivalent to an oath, taken by people who are forbidden to take a religious oath (such as Quakers) or otherwise prefer not to do so.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

   **Source synonyms:** solemn affirmation

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A form of self-forced meditation or repetition; autosuggestion.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Daily positive affirmations can improve self-confidence.
