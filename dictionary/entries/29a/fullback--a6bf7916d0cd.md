### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An offensive back whose primary jobs are to block in advance of the halfback on running plays and for the quarterback on passing plays.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** American-football; ball-games; football; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A position behind the main line of backs in both rugby union and rugby league. In rugby union, they wear number 15 jersey at the start of play and are the last line of defence responsible for catching punts.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; games; hobbies; lifestyle; rugby; sports

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A defensive player who assists the goalie in preventing the opposing team from scoring.

   **Usage:** field hockey
