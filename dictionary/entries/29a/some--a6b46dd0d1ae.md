### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A nonzero, unspecified proportion of (a bounded set of countable things)&#58; at least two.

   **Usage:** stressed form

   **Example:** Some people like camping.

   **Source synonyms:** any

   **Source antonyms:** zero; no; a lot of; many; numerous; countless

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. A nonzero, unspecified quantity or number of (an unbounded set of countable things).

   **Example:** Would you like some grapes?

   **Source synonyms:** any

   **Source antonyms:** zero; no; a lot of; many; numerous; countless

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. An unspecified amount of (something uncountable).

   **Example:** Would you like some water?

   **Example:** After some persuasion, he finally agreed.

   **Source synonyms:** any

   **Source antonyms:** zero; no

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. A certain, an unspecified or unknown.

   **Usage:** stressed form

   **Example:** I've just met some guy who said he knew you.

   **Example:** The sequence S converges to zero for some initial value v.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. A considerable quantity or number of.

   **Usage:** stressed form

   **Example:** He had edited the paper for some years.

   **Example:** He stopped working some time ago.

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. Approximately, about (with a number).

   **Usage:** stressed form

   **Example:** She has worked at the company for some thirty years now. (31 and two months, to be exact.)

   **Example:** There were only some three or four cars in the lot at the time.

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

**Source sense R2.S7**

7. Emphasizing a number.

   **Usage:** stressed form

   **Example:** She has worked at the company for some five years now! How remarkable!

<a id="r2-s8"></a>

**Source sense R2.S8**

8. A remarkable.

   **Usage:** informal; stressed form

   **Example:** He is some acrobat!

   **Example:** That was some speech you gave!
