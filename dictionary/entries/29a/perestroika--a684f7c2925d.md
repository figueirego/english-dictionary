### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A program of political and economic reform carried out in the Soviet Union in the 1980s and early 1990s under the leadership of Mikhail Gorbachev.

   **Usage:** historical; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** historical; uncountable
