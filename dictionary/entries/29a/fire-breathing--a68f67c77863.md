### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. That expels fire from the mouth and nostrils.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; such as a dragon

   **Example:** a fire-breathing dragon

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a fabled animal

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. That emits or belches forth fire.

   **Usage:** literary; not-comparable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. caustic, vitriolic or scathing.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; not-comparable

   **Example:** a fire-breathing rant
