### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To legally dissolve a marriage between two people.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** A ship captain can marry couples, but cannot divorce them.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To end one's own marriage to (a person) in this way.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Lucy divorced Steve when she discovered that he had been unfaithful.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To obtain a legal divorce.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Edna and Simon divorced last year; he got the house, and she retained the business.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To separate something that was connected.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The radical group voted to divorce itself from the main faction and start an independent movement.
