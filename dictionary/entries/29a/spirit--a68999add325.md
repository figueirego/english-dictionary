### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The soul of a person or other creature.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A supernatural being, often but not exclusively without physical form; ghost, fairy, angel.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** A wandering spirit haunts the island.

   **Example:** He has seen spirits roam the house.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A feeling of passion.
   1. Enthusiasm.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Example:** School spirit is at an all-time high.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A feeling of passion.
   1. Energy; ardour.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The manner or style of something.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** In the spirit of forgiveness, we didn't press charges.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Intent; real meaning; opposed to the letter, or formal statement.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** the spirit of an enterprise, or of a document

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A volatile liquid, such as alcohol. The plural form spirits is a generic term for distilled alcoholic beverages.

   **Usage:** countable; plural-normally; uncountable

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. One who is vivacious or lively; one who evinces great activity or peculiar characteristics of mind or temper.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** a ruling spirit; a schismatic spirit

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. Temper or disposition of mind; mental condition or disposition; intellectual or moral state.

   **Usage:** countable; in-plural; often; uncountable

   **Example:** to be cheerful, or in good spirits; to be down-hearted, or in bad spirits

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. Air set in motion by breathing; breath; hence, sometimes, life itself.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. A rough breathing; an aspirate, such as the letter h; also, a mark denoting aspiration.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s12"></a>

**Source sense R1.S12**

12. Any of the four substances&#58; sulphur, sal ammoniac, quicksilver, and arsenic (or, according to some, orpiment).

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** alchemy; pseudoscience

<a id="r1-s13"></a>

**Source sense R1.S13**

13. Stannic chloride.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; dyeing; manufacturing; textiles

<a id="r1-s14"></a>

**Source sense R1.S14**

14. The essence behind historical development of both individual and society evolving towards the Absolute.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; Hegelian

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; philosophy; sciences
