### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A long, narrow ditch or hole in the ground; either manmade, or formed naturally through erosion or tectonic plate movement.

   **Example:** The trenches for installing the underground cables were dug with a trencher.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A narrow excavation as used in warfare, as a cover for besieging or emplaced forces.

   **Example:** The soldiers in the trenches rely on logistical support.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A pit, usually rectangular with smooth walls and floor, excavated during an archaeological investigation.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** archaeology; history; human-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A trench coat.

   **Usage:** informal
