### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see settle, on.

   **Example:** The grounds settled on the bottom of the pot.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To make a decision or selection; to decide, arrange, or agree on.

   **Example:** He looked at all the colors for a long time before finally settling on a sage green.

   **Example:** settle on a plan

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To confer (an annuity, etc) upon by permanent grant; to assure to. (Compare settle (“formally, legally secure”).)
