### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To agree or assent \[with to ‘a proposal, a request’\]; to give way.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** to accede to a request

   **Source synonyms:** come around; concede; agree; acquiesce; assent

   **Source antonyms:** refuse

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To come to an office, state or dignity; to attain, assume (a position) \[with to\].

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** the house of Hanover acceded to the English throne

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To become a party to an agreement or a treaty \[with to\].

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** to accede to the European Union

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To approach; to arrive, to come forward.

   **Usage:** archaic; intransitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To give one's adhesion; to join up with (a group, etc.); to become part of.

   **Usage:** archaic; intransitive

   **Source synonyms:** band together; enroll

   **Source antonyms:** leave; secede; split off
