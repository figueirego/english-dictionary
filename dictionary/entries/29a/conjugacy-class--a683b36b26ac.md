### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A subset of a group which is an equivalence class in the quotient set of the group divided by conjugation as equivalence relation.

   **Example:** If a group acts on itself through conjugation, then its orbits are its conjugacy classes and its stabilizer subgroups are its centralizers.
