## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The apparent paradox that children learning English encounter many sentences amenable to dative shift (e.g. "give the book to me" → "give me the book") but apparently have no way to learn that this is not possible with certain verbs (e.g. "\*donate me the book" is unacceptable), and yet rarely make this kind of error.
