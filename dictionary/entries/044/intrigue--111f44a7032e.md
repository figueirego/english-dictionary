**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A complicated or clandestine plot or scheme intended to effect some purpose by secret artifice; conspiracy; stratagem; the practice of engaging in hidden schemes.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** His betrayal was the beginning of a complex intrigue intended to give him control of the company.

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A complicated or clandestine plot or scheme intended to effect some purpose by secret artifice; conspiracy; stratagem; the practice of engaging in hidden schemes.
   1. Clandestine intercourse between persons; illicit intimacy; a liaison or affair.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Example:** I suspect Suzanne and her mysterious friend of being involved in an intrigue.

3. A complicated or clandestine plot or scheme intended to effect some purpose by secret artifice; conspiracy; stratagem; the practice of engaging in hidden schemes.
   1. The plot of a play, poem or romance; the series of complications in which a writer involves their imaginary characters.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable
