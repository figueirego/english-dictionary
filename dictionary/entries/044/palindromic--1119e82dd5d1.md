## Adjective · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of, relating to, or being a palindrome.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** "Madam I'm Adam" is a palindromic phrase.

   **Example:** Palindromic prime numbers are those which read the same backwards as forwards.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Beginning and ending in a similar way.

   **Usage:** not-comparable
