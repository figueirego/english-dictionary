## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A (usually glossy and durable) transparent coat sprayed over the pigmented coats of a paint job or similar coating.

   **Example:** This car has an excellent clearcoat. It's very tough and scratch-resistant.

   **Example:** I sprayed a clearcoat over her pastel drawing, so it ought to last many years without smudging.
