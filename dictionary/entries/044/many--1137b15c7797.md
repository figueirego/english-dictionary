**Source sense R1.S2**

2. before a countable noun&#58; (in combinations such as 'as many', 'so many', 'this many') Used to indicate, demonstrate or compare the number of people or things.

   **Example:** I've never seen these many stars in the sky.

   **Example:** There may be as many as ten million species of insect.
