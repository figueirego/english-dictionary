**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An enclosure where pigs are kept, either a building or part of one.

   **Example:** The lower pasture also contains a pigsty.

   **Source synonyms:** hogpen; hogsty; pigpen; sty

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A dirty or very untidy place.

   **Usage:** colloquial

   **Example:** This room is a pigsty. When are you going to clean it?
