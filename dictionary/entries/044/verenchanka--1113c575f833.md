### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A village, the administrative centre of Verenchanka starostynskyi okruh and Verenchanka rural hromada, Chernivtsi Raion, Chernivtsi Oblast, Ukraine, first mentioned in 1589.
   1. A former silrada of Zastavna Raion, Chernivtsi Oblast, Ukraine, merged into Verenchanka rural hromada in June 2020.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A village, the administrative centre of Verenchanka starostynskyi okruh and Verenchanka rural hromada, Chernivtsi Raion, Chernivtsi Oblast, Ukraine, first mentioned in 1589.
   1. A rural hromada of Chernivtsi Raion, Chernivtsi Oblast, Ukraine, established in June 2020.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

   1. A starostynskyi okruh of Verenchanka rural hromada, Chernivtsi Raion, Chernivtsi Oblast, Ukraine.
