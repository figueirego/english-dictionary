### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Change between the states of seronegativity and seropositivity, in either direction&#58; either the process of this change or an instance of it.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; medicine; natural-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Change between the states of seronegativity and seropositivity, in either direction&#58; either the process of this change or an instance of it.
   1. Such change in the negative-to-positive direction, as a result of infection or immunization&#58; the development of specific antibodies in the blood serum.

      **Usage:** broadly; countable; uncountable; often strictly
