### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A disorder of the immune system causing adverse reactions to substances (allergens) not harmful to most and marked by the body's production of histamines and associated with atopy, anaphylaxis, and asthma; any condition of hypersensitivity to a substance.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** My dad can't live with pets because of an allergy.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** immunology; medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A disorder of the immune system causing adverse reactions to substances (allergens) not harmful to most and marked by the body's production of histamines and associated with atopy, anaphylaxis, and asthma; any condition of hypersensitivity to a substance.
   1. Specifically, hypersensitivity of class I in the modern classification thereof&#58; the immunoglobulin E–mediated type.

      **Usage:** broadly; strictly

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** immunology; medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An antipathy, as toward a person or activity.

   **Usage:** figuratively; informal

   **Example:** He has an allergy to reality TV.
