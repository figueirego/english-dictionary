### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Not active, temporarily or permanently.

   **Example:** The volcano is inactive, but is only dormant.

   **Example:** Inactive user accounts may be deleted after 90 days.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Not engaging in physical activity.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Not functioning or operating; broken down

   **Example:** The photocopier is inactive pending repair.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Retired from duty or service.

   **Example:** Admiral Jones is now on the inactive list.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Relatively inert.

   **Example:** Aluminium is inactive towards water.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Showing no optical activity in polarized light.

   **Example:** Synthetic glycine is optically inactive as it contains equal amounts of the d- and l- form.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics
