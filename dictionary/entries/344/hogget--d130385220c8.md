### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A young sheep of either gender from about 9 to 18 months of age (until it cuts 2 teeth).

   **Usage:** New-Zealand; UK; countable

   **Example:** They sent a lorryload of hoggets to the auction mart, and they will keep the rest for their own flock.

   **Source synonyms:** hogg

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The meat of a young sheep.

   **Usage:** New-Zealand; UK; uncountable

   **Example:** They make a delicious dinner special of hogget. It is made with rosemary and mint.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A young swine of the second year.

   **Usage:** UK; countable; obsolete

   **Example:** The monastery's records showed not only how many swine were raised each year but also their disposition, categorized by boars, sows, hoggets, and porklings.
