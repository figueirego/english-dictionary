### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A village, the administrative centre of Myhalky starostynskyi okruh, Piskivka settlement hromada, Bucha Raion, Kyiv Oblast, Ukraine, first mentioned in 1415.
   1. A former silrada of Borodianka Raion, Kyiv Oblast, Ukraine, amalgamated into Piskivka settlement hromada in January 2016; administrative centre and sole constituent settlement&#58; Myhalky.
