### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person with long experience of a particular activity.

   **Example:** The company's new hires were nervous, but the veterans were self-assured because, as one summarized it, "this isn't our first rodeo."

   **Source synonyms:** old hand; old-timer; warhorse

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person with long experience of a particular activity.
   1. A person who has served in the armed forces, especially an old soldier who has seen long service.

      **Example:** We honor veterans and senior citizens with a ten percent discount.

      **Source synonyms:** old hand; old-timer; warhorse

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A group, animal, etc. with long experience of a particular activity.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** Their mules were clearly veterans. They seemed to know what to do even better than the teenage farmhand who was driving the team.
