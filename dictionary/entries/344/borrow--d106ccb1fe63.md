### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To receive (something) from somebody temporarily, expecting to return it.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To receive money from a bank or other lender under the agreement that the lender will be paid back over time.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To adopt (an idea) as one's own.

   **Example:** to borrow the style, manner, or opinions of another

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To adopt a word from another language.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. In a subtraction, to deduct (one) from a digit of the minuend and add ten to the following digit, in order that the subtraction of a larger digit in the subtrahend from the digit in the minuend to which ten is added gives a positive result.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** arithmetic

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To lend.

   **Usage:** Malaysia; Singapore; West-Midlands; proscribed; Upper Midwestern US

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To temporarily obtain (something) for (someone).

   **Usage:** ditransitive

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To feign or counterfeit.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To secure the release of (someone) from prison.

   **Usage:** obsolete except in ballads

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. To receive (something, usually of trifling value) from somebody, with little possibility of returning it.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** Can I borrow a sheet of paper?

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. To interrupt the current activity of (a person) and lead them away in order to speak with them, get their help, etc.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** John, can I borrow you for a second? I need your help with the copier.

<a id="r1-s12"></a>

**Source sense R1.S12**

12. To adjust one's aim in order to compensate for the slope of the green.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** golf; hobbies; lifestyle; sports
