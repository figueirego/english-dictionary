### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A pronunciation that has been modified from its traditional form in order to reflect (one or another interpretation of) the spelling of the word in question.

   **Example:** The audio contained some spelling pronunciations, such as when "sieve" was mistakenly portrayed as rhyming with "leave".
