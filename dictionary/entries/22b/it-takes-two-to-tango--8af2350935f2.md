## Proverb

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Some things need the active cooperation of two parties; blame is to be laid on both parties in a conflict.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I can't do it by myself, and neither can you – it takes two to tango.

   **Example:** He started it! Well, it takes two to tango.
