### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To treat or react with nitrogen or a nitrogen-containing compound.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To convert ammonia or similar compound to a nitrate by oxidation, especially by the action of a microorganism.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To become nitre.

   **Usage:** intransitive
