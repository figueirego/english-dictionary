- IPA: /bɪˈloː(w)/ — source labels: India

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. In or to a lower place.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The town is situated on a hillside, with a river running below.

   **Example:** He was pulled below by a sea monster.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. In or to a lower place.
   1. On or to a lower storey.

      **Usage:** not-comparable

      **Example:** She lives below, on the ground floor.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. In or to a lower place.
   1. On or to a lower deck, especially as relative to the main deck.

      **Usage:** not-comparable

      **Example:** The captain went below to inspect the engine.

      **Example:** the landlubbers lying down below

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. Later in the same text.

   **Usage:** not-comparable
