**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To behave inconsistently; to vacillate or to waver, as between extremes of opinion or emotion.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** For a week, she couldn't get enough of me. The next week, her interest blew hot and cold, and a week later she dumped me.
