### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of realizing, as&#58;
   1. An act of figuring out or becoming aware.

      **Usage:** British; English; Oxford; US; countable; uncountable

      **Example:** He came to the startling realization that he had never really known the truth.

      **Example:** A bath is a lovely relaxing event, until the realization that you're soaking in your own crud.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The act of realizing, as&#58;
   1. The act of making real.

      **Usage:** British; English; Oxford; US; countable; uncountable

      **Example:** He did not stay around long enough to see the realization of the idea.

      **Example:** The realization of the plan took several years.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The act of realizing, as&#58;
   1. The result of an artistic effort.

      **Usage:** British; English; Oxford; US; countable; uncountable

      **Example:** The painter's realization of her concept was breathtaking.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The act of realizing, as&#58;
   1. The manner in which a phoneme is pronounced.

      **Usage:** British; English; Oxford; US; countable; uncountable
