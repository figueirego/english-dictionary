## Adjective

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈd͡ʒɛn(ə)ɹəs/ — source labels: UK; US

### Forms

- more generous — comparative
- most generous — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Noble in behaviour or actions; principled, not petty; kind, magnanimous.

   **Example:** Thank you for your generous words.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Willing to give and share unsparingly; showing a readiness to give more (especially money) than is expected or needed.

   **Example:** She's been extremely generous with her winnings.

   **Source synonyms:** free; bountiful; giving

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Large; more than ample; copious.

   **Example:** Add a generous helping of mayonnaise.

   **Example:** his generous buttocks

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Invigorating in its nature.

   **Example:** a generous wine

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Of noble birth.

   **Usage:** obsolete
