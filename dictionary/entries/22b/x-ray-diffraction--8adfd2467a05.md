### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The scattering of X-rays by the regular lattice of atoms or molecules in a crystal.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The diffraction pattern so obtained.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The technique of using such patterns to determine that geometry of a crystal, or the arrangements of atoms in a molecule; X-ray crystallography.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; analytical chemistry
