### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A polypeptide hormone that regulates carbohydrate metabolism (in various animals including humans).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Mommy said that Grandma's diabetes happened because her body stopped making its own insulin.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any exogenous preparation of this hormone for use as a medication.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The advent of insulin in the mid-20th century changed the lives of millions of people with diabetes.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any exogenous preparation of this hormone or its analogues for use as a medication.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** A&#58; What kind of insulin do you take? B&#58; I use basal-bolus therapy, which in my case involves glargine and lispro.
