### Senses

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

2. An unwelcome burden, presence, or obligation.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

3. That which is imposed, levied, or enjoined.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

4. A trick or deception put or laid on others.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
