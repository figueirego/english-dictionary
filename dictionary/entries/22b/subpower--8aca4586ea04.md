### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A minor power; Something that has control over a limited domain that falls within the larger domain of a greater power.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A type of function such that for every exponent a, there is a small integer ε such that for any positive value x less than ε, its image lies between xᵃ and x⁻ᵃ .
