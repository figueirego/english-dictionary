## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A surgical technique where the digestive tract is rearranged into a Y-shaped connection, usually by creating a small stomach pouch and rerouting the small intestine. It is most commonly used in weight-loss surgery (gastric bypass) to reduce food intake and calorie absorption.

   **Usage:** attributive; uncountable
