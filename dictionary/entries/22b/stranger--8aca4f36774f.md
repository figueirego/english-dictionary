## Noun · Etymology 2 · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈstɹeɪnd͡ʒə(ɹ)/ — source labels: Received-Pronunciation
- IPA: /ˈstɹeɪnd͡ʒɚ/ — source labels: General-American

### Forms

- strangers — plural

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A person whom one does not know; a person who is neither a friend nor an acquaintance.

   **Example:** That gentleman is a stranger to me.

   **Example:** Children are taught not to talk to strangers.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. An outsider or foreigner.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. One not admitted to communion or fellowship.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. A newcomer.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. Used ironically to refer to a person who the speaker knows.

   **Usage:** humorous

   **Example:** Hello, stranger!

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. One not belonging to the family or household; a guest; a visitor.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

**Source sense R2.S7**

7. One not privy or party to an act, contract, or title; a mere intruder or intermeddler; one who interferes without right.

   **Example:** Actual possession of land gives a good title against a stranger having no title.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r2-s8"></a>

**Source sense R2.S8**

8. A superstitious premonition of the coming of a visitor by a bit of stalk in a cup of tea, the guttering of a candle, etc.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r2-s9"></a>

**Source sense R2.S9**

9. A moth, Lacanobia blenna

   **Usage:** sometimes; with-definite-article
