### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The skin of an orange, including the pith.

   **Usage:** countable; literally; uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An uneven surface resembling orange peel, especially cellulite or an imperfect finish on a spray-painted object such as a car.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An uneven surface resembling orange peel, especially cellulite or an imperfect finish on a spray-painted object such as a car.
   1. A surface defect in which sheet metal has a rough texture, similar to an orange peel, due to the metal grains being relatively large.

      **Usage:** uncountable; usually

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; manufacturing

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually
