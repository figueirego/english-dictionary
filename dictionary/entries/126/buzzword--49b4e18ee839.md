### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A word drawn from, or imitative of, technical jargon, used more to impress others than to convey meaning.

   **Usage:** derogatory; sometimes

   **Example:** Their salespeople know all the right buzzwords, but they can't really help you solve your problems.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A little-known word used to sound scholarly or professional, or to flaunt one's vocabulary.

   **Example:** Why not just say "compare" instead of "juxtapose"? No one understands you when you use these buzzwords.
