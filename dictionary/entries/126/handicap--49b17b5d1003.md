### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Something that prevents, hampers, or hinders.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Age is often a handicap.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An allowance of a certain amount of time or distance in starting, granted in a race (or other contest of skill) to the competitor possessing disadvantages; or an additional weight or other hindrance imposed upon the one possessing advantages, in order to equalize, as much as possible, the chances of success.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The older boy won, even though his opponent had been granted a handicap of five meters.

   **Example:** A handicap in chess often involves removal of the queen's rook.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An individual's physical or mental disadvantage or disability.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; sometimes considered offensive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A race or similar contest in which there is an allowance of time, distance, weight, or other advantage, to equalize the chances of the competitors.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An old card game, similar to lanterloo.

   **Usage:** obsolete; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** card-games; games
