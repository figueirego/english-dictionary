## Interjection

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. I wasn't serious; it was only a joke.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** The winner of our trivia game here tonight will receive a prize of a million dollars. Just kidding! It's a fifty-dollar gift card.

   **Example:** She was just kidding when she teased him for being nerdy about zoology. In truth she likes his nerdiness.

   **Example:** Q&#58; Why would you say such a hurtful thing that you can’t take back? A&#58; I was just kidding.

   **Example:** The court is apparently not buying the defendants’ claim that they were just kidding about the death threats.

   **Source synonyms:** jk; JK; j/k; not; psych
