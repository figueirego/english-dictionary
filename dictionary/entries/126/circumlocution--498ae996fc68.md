### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A roundabout or indirect way of speaking; thus

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A roundabout or indirect way of speaking; thus&#58;
   1. Unnecessary use of extra words to express an idea, such as a pleonastic phrase (sometimes driven by an attempt at emphatic clarity) or a wordy substitution (the latter driven by euphemistic intent, pedagogic intent, or sometimes loquaciousness alone).

      **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A roundabout or indirect way of speaking; thus&#58;
   1. Necessary use of a phrase to circumvent either a vocabulary fault (of speaker or listener) or a lexical gap, either monolingually or in translation.

      **Usage:** uncountable

      **Example:** A technical word, such as hyperkalemia or hypoallergenic, can be glossed for general audiences with a circumlocution, such as "high potassium level" or "less likely to cause allergies" (respectively).

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An instance of such usage; a roundabout expression, whether an inadvisable one or a necessary one.

   **Usage:** countable
