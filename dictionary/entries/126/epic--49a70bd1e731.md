### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An extended narrative poem in elevated or dignified language, celebrating the feats of a deity, demigod (heroic epic), other legend or traditional hero.

   **Example:** The Icelandic epic took all night to recite.

   **Source synonyms:** epopee; epos

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Example:** The book was an epic in four volumes.
