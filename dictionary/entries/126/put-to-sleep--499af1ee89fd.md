### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To cause (someone) to sleep.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** His boring stories sometimes put me to sleep.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To help (someone) to bed.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** After they'd put the kids to sleep, they relaxed by the fire and chatted.

   **Source synonyms:** put to bed; put down

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To render dormant.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive; uncommon

   **Source synonyms:** put to rest; put to bed

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To kill an animal painlessly, often with an injection.

   **Usage:** euphemistic; transitive

   **Example:** It was sad to put our beloved spaniel to sleep, but it was better than letting him suffer.

   **Source synonyms:** put down; euthanize; put to bed with a shovel

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To give a general anesthetic prior to surgery.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** Mommy's surgery is not a big operation but it does require that they put her to sleep for a while.

   **Source synonyms:** put under; anesthetize; anaesthetise
