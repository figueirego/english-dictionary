### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of illustrating; the act of making clear and distinct.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The state of being illustrated, or of being made clear and distinct.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Something which illustrates; a comparison or example intended to make clear or apprehensible, or to remove obscurity.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** instance; example

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A picture designed to decorate a publication, or elucidate a literary work.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The illustration showing the water cycle made it much easier to understand for the children.

   **Example:** The sleeve of the band's new CD includes illustrations from deceased former members.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A calculated prevision of insurance premiums and returns (life insurance).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
