## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈɹʌndaʊn/ — source labels: General-American

### Forms

- rundowns — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A rough outline of a topic or situation.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; chiefly with definite article "the"

   **Example:** Could you give me the rundown on the new rules?

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A summary of the horses to be raced on a particular day, with their weights, jockeys, odds, etc.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** gambling; games

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A defensive play in which the runner is caught between two fielders, who steadily converge to tag the runner out.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Smith is caught in a rundown, but Jones will come around to score.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; baseball; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A Caribbean stew of meat or fish (typically mackerel) with reduced coconut milk, yam, tomato, onion and seasonings.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A reduction, e.g. of an activity, or in the size of something, such as a fleet.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
