### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To become more upright

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** His ears perked up when he heard there would be ice cream.

   **Source synonyms:** rise; stick up

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To become more lively or enthusiastic.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source synonyms:** cheer up; get it up

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To cause to be more upright, straighten up.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** right; straighten

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. to cause to be more lively or enthusiastic.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** elate; hearten; beatify; enrapture; begladden; blissen; bright; cheer; fetch; cheer up; cherish (obsolete); content; delight; elate; elevate (obsolete); enbliss; exhilarate; fain (obsolete); gay (dated; uncommon); gladden; gladden someone's heart (dated); gratify; happify; happy; hearten; jollify; laetificate (rare); light up; make someone's day; perk up; please; raise someone's spirits (idiomatic); rejoice; turn the frown upside down; upcheer (archaic); warm someone's heart (idiomatic); warm the cockles of someone's heart (idiomatic)

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To exalt oneself, take on a higher status or position.

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete

   **Source synonyms:** aggrandize; rise
