**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A festival, a fest; used in names of events.

   **Usage:** morpheme

   **Example:** summer + -fest → Summerfest

   **Example:** Yule + -fest → Yulefest

2. Appended to a noun to denote a thing, especially an event or artistic work, characterized by the noun's referent.
