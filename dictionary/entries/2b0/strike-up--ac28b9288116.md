### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To start something with somebody else, such as a conversation or relationship.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** To make him feel welcome, she struck up a conversation with the newly arrived guest.

   **Example:** He struck up a friendship with Redford that was to last for many years.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To start something, usually playing live music.

   **Usage:** ergative; idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** The band struck up and everyone started to dance.

   **Example:** The bride entered the church just as the "Wedding March" struck up.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To raise (as sheet metal), in making dishes, pans, etc., by blows or pressure in a die.
