### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A refrigeration system that drives its cooling effect using energy provided by a heat source, specifically by making a coolant (often water or ammonia) evaporate and be absorbed into another substance (often lithium bromide or water, respectively) before the two substances are again separated by heating.

   **Source synonyms:** absorption system
