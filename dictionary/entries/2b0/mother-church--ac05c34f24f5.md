## Noun

### Forms

- mother churches — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The Church regarded as nourishing and protecting its members.

   **Example:** Catholics regard Mary as Mother of the Church but an all-male hierarchy as the Mother Church.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A church with oversight over another or others, now especially a cathedral or metropolitan church.

   **Example:** Mothering Sunday arose from the custom of visiting mother churches at Mid-Lent, rather than the usual parish church.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The original church of a denomination, regarded as having birthed the others.

   **Example:** The mother church of Christian Scientists is in Boston, Massachusetts.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The original denomination or community of believers from which other denominations and communities of believers sprang.

   **Example:** The Church of England is the mother church of Anglicans around the world.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity
