## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The breeding of unrelated (or only distantly related) individuals.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Outbreeding is a positive thing, because of the wider gene pool.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The mating of people from different groups (especially as a result of social proscription).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Outbreeding hinders earnest studies of archaeogenetics, making it thorny to know precisely about ancient populations, migrations and linguistic groups.
