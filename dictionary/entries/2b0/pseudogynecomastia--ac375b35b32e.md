### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An enlargement of the breast(s) due to excessive deposition of fat or skin, as opposed to “true gynecomastia” which results from the development of glandular breast tissue.

   **Usage:** US; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences
