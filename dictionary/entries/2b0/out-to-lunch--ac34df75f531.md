## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Away eating lunch or for a midday break; especially, away from work or a job.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** She's out to lunch right now, but you may leave her a note.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Clueless, inattentive, or careless.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; informal; not-comparable

   **Example:** After he drove with his turn signal on for five miles, I was pretty sure he was out to lunch.
