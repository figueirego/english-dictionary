### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A tendency to be depressed, without hope.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source synonyms:** depression; despondence; dejectedness; gloom; lypemania

   **Source antonyms:** euthymia; euphoria

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A form of clinical depression, characterized by low-grade depression which lasts at least two years.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; medicine; psychiatry; psychology; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** persistent depressive disorder; PDD
