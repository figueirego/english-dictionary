## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The reference line at 180° longitude, on the opposite side of earth to the prime meridian.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; natural-sciences

   **Source synonyms:** Greenwich antimeridian

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A meridian of longitude opposite to any given meridian used as a point of reference.

   **Example:** 123° west is the antimeridian of the 57° east meridian.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; natural-sciences
