**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Affected by bloodlust; having a strong desire for bloodshed and carnage.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** Even the most bloodlusted fans were unsettled by the gratuitous bloodbath that was the final episode.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In the context of characters fighting&#58; being a version of the character who tries to kill their opponent in the fastest and most efficient manner, disregarding their typical morality and personality.

   **Usage:** slang; specifically

   **Example:** Who would win in a fight between Superman and bloodlusted Flash?
