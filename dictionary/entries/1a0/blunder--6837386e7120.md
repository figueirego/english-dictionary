### Senses

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To make a big mistake, especially when it is careless or stupid.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** to blunder in preparing a medical prescription

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To make a big mistake, especially when it is careless or stupid.
   1. To make a bad move, especially caused by tactical oversight.

      **Usage:** intransitive

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** backgammon; board-games; chess; games

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To make a big mistake, especially when it is careless or stupid.
   1. To lose a piece due to having made a bad move \[with the piece lost\].

      **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

      **Example:** blunder a rook

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** board-games; chess; games

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To make a big mistake, especially when it is careless or stupid.
   1. To overlook the possibility of, or end up in, a specified undesirable situation after making a bad move.

      **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

      **Example:** blunder a draw

      **Example:** blunder a mate in one

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** board-games; chess; games
