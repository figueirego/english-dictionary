## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Said of a group acting on a set, and with respect to an element of that set&#58; the subgroup of the given group whose action leaves that element fixed.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** algebra; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Said of a group acting on a set, and with respect to a subset of that set&#58; the subgroup of the given group for which the image of the set under the group action is a subset of the original set. Some authors instead define this to be the subgroup of the given group whose action leaves each element of the subset fixed.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** algebra; mathematics; sciences
