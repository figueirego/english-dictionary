### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To bring back into a particular place or condition; to restore.

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive; Physical movement.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To cause (something) to turn around a central point.

   **Usage:** transitive; Physical movement.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To orbit a central point (especially of a celestial body).

   **Usage:** intransitive; Physical movement.

   **Example:** The Earth revolves around the sun.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To rotate around an axis.

   **Usage:** intransitive; Physical movement.

   **Example:** The Earth revolves once every twenty-four hours.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To move in order or sequence.

   **Usage:** intransitive; Physical movement.

   **Example:** The program revolves through all the queues before returning to the start.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To ponder on; to reflect repeatedly upon; to consider all aspects of.

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive; Mental activity.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To read through, to study (a book, author etc.).

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive; Mental activity.
