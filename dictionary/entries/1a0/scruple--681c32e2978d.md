## Verb · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈskɹuːpəl/ — source labels: Received-Pronunciation
- IPA: /ˈskɹupəl/ — source labels: General-American

### Forms

- scruples — present; singular; third-person
- scrupling — participle; present
- scrupled — participle; past
- scrupled — past

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To hesitate or be reluctant to act due to considerations of conscience or expedience.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** They wouldn't scruple to cheat us if given the opportunity.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To excite scruples in; to cause to scruple.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To regard with suspicion; to question.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To question the truth of (a fact, etc.); to doubt; to hesitate to believe, to question.

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete

   **Example:** I do not scruple to admit that all the Earth seeth but only half of the Moon.
