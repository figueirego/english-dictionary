## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A method of applying a piece of embroidered or printed fabric (i.e. a patch) to another piece of fabric by using a heat activated adhesive.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The adhesive backing itself.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** It was easy add his the new merit badge to his uniform because the patch had a hot seal backing.
