   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

2. Fruit of the mango tree.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A green bell pepper suitable for pickling.

   **Usage:** US; countable; dated; uncountable; chiefly southern Midland US

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

5. A type of muskmelon, Cucumis melo.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Any of various hummingbirds of the genus Anthracothorax.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
