### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The suburbs as a whole and all that characterizes or pertains to them; (sometimes derogatory) the suburbs as encapsulated or represented by the typical characteristics or qualities of the people living there, especially complacency, conformity, conservativeness, dullness, etc.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** suburbandom; suburbanhood; suburbanism; suburbanity
