### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To roll oneself about in something dirty, for example in mud.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Pigs wallow in the mud.

   **Source synonyms:** welter

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To move lazily or heavily in any medium.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To immerse oneself in, to occupy oneself with, metaphorically.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

   **Example:** She wallowed in her misery.

   **Source synonyms:** grovel

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To live or exist in filth or in a sickening manner.

   **Usage:** intransitive

### Related words
