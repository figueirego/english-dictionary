   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** A ship moves rapidly.

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. The act of moving; a movement.

   **Example:** A slight move of the tiller, and the boat will go off course.

   **Example:** The drummer Cynthia praised her best friends' dance moves to the music.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. An act for the attainment of an object; a step in the execution of a plan or purpose.

   **Example:** He made another move towards becoming a naturalized citizen.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. A formalized or practiced action used in athletics, dance, physical exercise, self-defense, hand-to-hand combat, etc.

   **Example:** She always gets spontaneous applause for that one move.

   **Example:** He can win a match with that one move.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. The event of changing one's residence.

   **Example:** The move into my fiancé's house took two long days.

   **Example:** They were pleased about their move to the country.

   **Source synonyms:** removal; relocation

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. A change in strategy.

   **Example:** I am worried about our boss's move.

   **Example:** It was a smart move to bring on a tall striker to play against the smaller defenders.

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. A transfer, a change from one employer to another.

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

**Source sense R2.S7**

7. The act of moving a token on a gameboard from one position to another according to the rules of the game.

   **Usage:** board games

   **Example:** The best move of the game was when he sacrificed his rook in order to gain better possession.

   **Example:** It's your move! Roll the dice!

   **Example:** If you roll a six, you can make two moves.

   **Source synonyms:** play

<a id="r2-s8"></a>

**Source sense R2.S8**

8. A round, in which each player has a turn.

   **Usage:** plural-normally; board games

   **Example:** You can win in three moves if you do that.
