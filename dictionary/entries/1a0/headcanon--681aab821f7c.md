### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Elements and interpretations of a fictional universe accepted by an individual or small group of fans, but not necessarily found within or supported by the official canon.

   **Usage:** countable; slang; uncountable

   **Example:** In my own headcanon of Rocky IV, Ivan Drago is a Russian Mafia enforcer instead of a Red Army Captain.
