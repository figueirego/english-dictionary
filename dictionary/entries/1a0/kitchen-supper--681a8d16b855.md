## Noun

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈkɪtʃən ˈsʌpɚ/ — source labels: US

### Forms

- kitchen suppers — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The evening meal for servants, separate from the family's meal.

   **Usage:** dated

   **Example:** The cook prided herself on making the kitchen supper as delicious as the family's dinner.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An informal or semiformal meal served for guests, not necessarily one served in the kitchen.

   **Example:** The couple threw an impromptu kitchen supper when the dinner reservations fell through.
