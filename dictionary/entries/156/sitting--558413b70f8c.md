## Noun · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈsɪtɪŋ/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Forms

- sittings — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A period during which one is seated for a specific purpose.

   **Example:** Due to the sheer volume of guests, we had to have two sittings for the meal.

   **Example:** The Queen had three sittings for her portrait.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A seance or other session with a medium or fortuneteller.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A special seat allotted to a seat-holder, at church, etc.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The part of the year in which judicial business is transacted.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A legislative session (in the sense of meeting, and not period).

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The incubation of eggs by a bird.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A clutch of eggs laid by a brooding bird.

   **Usage:** collective

   **Example:** we have thirty-four chicks from eight sittings of eggs

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. An uninterrupted application to anything for a time; the period during which one continues at anything.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I read it in two sittings.
