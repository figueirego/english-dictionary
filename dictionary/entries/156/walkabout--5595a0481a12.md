## Noun

### Forms

- walkabouts — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A period, often extended, during which an Aboriginal person left a station or settlement to travel on country, typically seasonally or for traditional cultural reasons; a journey by foot taken by an Aboriginal as a temporary withdrawal from white society.

   **Usage:** Australia

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A walking trip.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A public stroll by some celebrity to meet a group of people informally.

   **Usage:** UK

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An absence, usually from a regular place with a possibility of a return.
