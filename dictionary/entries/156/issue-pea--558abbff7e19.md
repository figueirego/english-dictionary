**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A pea, or similar round body, used to provoke irritation in a wound, in order to promote secretion and the discharge of pus.

   **Usage:** archaic

   **Example:** Apart from peas, young unripe fruit of the orange tree was previously used as an issue pea
