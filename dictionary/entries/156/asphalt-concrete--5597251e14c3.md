### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A material often used for road surfacing, composed of a hard, granular material such as crushed stone combined with asphalt as a binder.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Before this street was paved with asphalt concrete, it was paved with cobbles.
