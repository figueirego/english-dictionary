### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To resolve (a contest, problem, dispute, etc.); to choose, determine, or settle.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** The election will be decided on foreign policies.

   **Example:** It was decided to meet here at midnight.

   **Example:** Her last-minute goal decided the game.

   **Source synonyms:** [choose](../1c9/choose--725fed9d1ae9.md); determine; pick; elect; accept; adopt; appoint; cast; [choose](../1c9/choose--725fed9d1ae9.md); crave; decide; designate; desire; determine; elect; embrace; espouse; excerpt; extract; favor; opt; pick; poll; predestine; preponderate; prefer; separate; select; settle; single out; vote; winnow

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To make a judgment, especially after deliberation.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** You must decide between good and evil.

   **Example:** I have decided that it is healthier to walk to work.

   **Source synonyms:** judge; make up one's mind

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To cause someone to come to a decision.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Your admonition decided me against my intended course of action.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Of a Turing machine&#58; to return a correct answer (for some yes-or-no problem) on every possible input.

   **Example:** No Turing Machine can decide the halting problem.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; computing-theory; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To cut off; to separate.

   **Usage:** obsolete

   **Source synonyms:** divorce; sever; sunder; becut; decide (obsolete); discerp; disintegrate; dispart (rare); dissever; divide; divorce; divvy up; part; partition; piecemeal; rescind; section; sever; split; split up; sunder
