### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A type of pastry that consists of an outer crust and a filling. (Savory pies are more popular in the UK and sweet pies are more popular in the US, so "pie" without qualification has different connotations in these dialects.)

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The family had steak and kidney pie for dinner and cherry pie for dessert.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of various other, non-pastry dishes that maintain the general concept of a shell with a filling.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Shepherd's pie is made of mince covered with mashed potato.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A pizza.

   **Usage:** Northeastern; US; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A paper plate covered in cream, shaving foam or custard that is thrown or rubbed in someone’s face for comical purposes, to raise money for charity, or as a form of political protest; a custard pie; a cream pie.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The whole of a wealth or resource, to be divided in parts.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** cake

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. An especially badly bowled ball.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; cricket; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

**Source sense R1.S7**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

   **Usage:** countable; informal; uncountable
