## Noun

### Forms

- white oaks — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An oak tree with rounded leaf lobes and relatively pale wood, native to eastern North America, Quercus alba.

   **Usage:** countable; usually

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A similar oak tree in a subgenus, Quercus subg. Quercus, especially section Quercus.

   **Usage:** countable; usually

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Lagunaria patersonia (Queensland white oak).

   **Usage:** countable; usually

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The wood of such trees.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually
