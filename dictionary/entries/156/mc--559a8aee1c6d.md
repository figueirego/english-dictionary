### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A patronymic used to form common Irish and Scottish names, similar to the English -son.

   **Usage:** morpheme

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used in combination with a non-name descriptive word to form mock names.

   **Usage:** derogatory; morpheme; often

   **Example:** Mc- + shit → McShit

   **Example:** Mc- + stupid → McStupid

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used to form the name of McDonald's products.

   **Usage:** morpheme

   **Example:** Mc- + griddle → McGriddle

   **Example:** Mc- + muffin → McMuffin

   **Example:** Mc- + rib → McRib

   **Example:** Mc- + café → McCafé

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Indicating a lack of depth or worth, by association with McDonald's.

   **Usage:** broadly; derogatory; morpheme

   **Example:** Mc- + car → McCar

   **Example:** Mc- + church → McChurch

   **Example:** Mc- + job → McJob

   **Example:** Mc- + mansion → McMansion

   **Example:** Mc- + dojo → McDojo

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Indicating an increase in availability or an increase in consumerism causing items to be more readily available.

   **Usage:** broadly; derogatory; humorous; morpheme

   **Example:** Mc- + nuke → McNuke
