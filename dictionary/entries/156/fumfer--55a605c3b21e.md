### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To speak awkwardly or evasively; to dither, waffle.

   **Usage:** US; ambitransitive; informal

   **Example:** She fumfered around and refused to give a straight answer.

   **Source synonyms:** fumfer; mutter; mouth; mumble; murmur

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To fiddle or fumble.

   **Usage:** US; ambitransitive; informal

   **Example:** I fumfered with the USB stick, trying and failing to plug it in.

   **Source synonyms:** feel around; fumble; scrabble; fish (informal); forage; scramble; glaum (dialectal); grabble; grope; grubble (obsolete); poke (figuratively)
