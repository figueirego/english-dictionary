### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One or more persons or things of relatively little consequence, importance, or value.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; plural; plural-only

   **Example:** My paycheck is small potatoes compared to hers.

   **Example:** He no longer works for individuals, since they are small potatoes compared to his corporate clients.
