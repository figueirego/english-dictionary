## Adjective

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈlaɪ.bəl.əs/ — source labels: UK

### Forms

- more libelous — comparative
- most libelous — superlative
- libellous — alternative; UK

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Defamatory, libeling, referring to something that causes harm to someone's reputation especially with malice or disregard.

   **Usage:** US

   **Example:** The accusation was libelous, full of falsehoods, spite and malice.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Meeting the legal standards for libel.

   **Usage:** US

   **Example:** The court found the statement was not libelous because it was true.
