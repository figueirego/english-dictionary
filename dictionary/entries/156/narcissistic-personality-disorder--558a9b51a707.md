### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A personality disorder characterized by an overinflated sense of self-importance (incl. subordination of other people's needs), sensitivity to criticism and difference in opinion and the denigration of others that are deemed lesser.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** People with narcissistic personality disorder usually have very low self-esteem and base all their self-worth on how much attention they receive.
