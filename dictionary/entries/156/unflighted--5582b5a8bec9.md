## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Lacking flight feathers, typically due to immaturity (compare flightless)

   **Usage:** not-comparable; rare

   **Example:** This breed of canary is unflighted for the first six months of its life.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of birds

   **Source antonyms:** flighted

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Lacking flights (arrows, a screw thread, etc.).

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Unflighted darts have a tendency to tumble.
