## Noun · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An angular mil, a unit of angular measurement equal to ¹⁄₆₄₀₀ of a complete circle. At 1000 metres one mil subtends about one metre (0.98 m). Also ¹⁄₆₀₀₀ and ¹⁄₆₃₀₀ are used in other countries.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A unit of measurement equal to ¹⁄₁₀₀₀ of an inch (25.4 µm), usually used for thin objects, such as sheets of plastic.

   **Example:** For this task, I prefer the plastic sheeting that is 10 mils thick.

   **Source synonyms:** thou

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A former subdivision (¹⁄₁₀₀₀) of the Maltese lira.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Clipping of milliliter.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; clipping; informal

   **Example:** We told her to give her daughter ten mils of cough syrup and to call us back if the cough gets worse.

   **Source synonyms:** mL; ml; cm³; cc

   **Alternative form of:** [milliliter](../2f0/milliliter--bc1ff1c283b6.md)

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Clipping of millimeter.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; clipping; informal

   **Example:** For this task, I prefer the plastic bars that are 10 mils in diameter.

   **Source synonyms:** mm; mike-mike

   **Alternative form of:** [millimeter](../1f8/millimeter--7e3f74fa1ea2.md)

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Clipping of milliradian.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; clipping; informal

   **Example:** A dot in a mil-dot reticle represents one mil, which corresponds to a few inches at 100 yards.

   **Alternative form of:** [milliradian](../3fa/milliradian--fe9d4ec99a39.md)
