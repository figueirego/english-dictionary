## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act or process of transferring blood products into a person's circulation intravenously; an instance of it.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** A blood transfusion stabilized her condition until surgery to stop her internal bleeding before it could begin.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** haemotransfusion; hemotransfusion

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A refreshing or reinvigoration of something.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncommon; uncountable

   **Example:** Our extramural sports program was on life support until a generous philanthropic donation gave it a much-needed blood transfusion.
