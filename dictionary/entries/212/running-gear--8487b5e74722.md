### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The wheels, suspension, steering, powertrain and usually the chassis of a motor vehicle.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** automotive; transport; vehicles

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Those components of a railway vehicle that run passively on the rails, unlike those of the driving gear. Traditionally these are the wheels, axles, axle boxes, springs and vehicle frame.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** railways; transport

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The wheels and axles of a carriage or other vehicle.
