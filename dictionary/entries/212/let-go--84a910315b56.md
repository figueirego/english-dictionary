### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To release from one's grasp; to go from a state of holding on to a state of no longer holding on.

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive; with-of

   **Example:** You're hurting him! Let him go!

   **Example:** Let go of the phone.

   **Source context (verbatim):** with object before go

   **Source synonyms:** release; unhand; leggo (imperative); relinquish

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To emotionally disengage or distract oneself from a situation.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To dismiss from employment.

   **Usage:** euphemistic

   **Example:** The secretary didn't work out, so her boss told her she was being let go.

   **Source synonyms:** decruit; dehire; unhire; lay off; fire; axe (figuratively); can (US; euphemistic); decruit (euphemistic); dehire; discard; remove; discharge; dismiss; downsize (euphemistic); fire; give the axe; give someone the boot (UK); give someone their cards; give the chop (idiomatic); give the elbow (UK); give someone the heave-ho (UK; idiomatic); give someone the old heave-ho (UK; idiomatic); lay off (US; idiomatic); let go (euphemistic); let someone go (euphemistic; idiomatic); make redundant; pink slip (US); promote to customer; retrench; sack; serve notice (idiomatic); shit-can (vulgar); term; terminate (euphemistic); throw out (idiomatic); unhire (euphemistic)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To ignore (a comment, etc.).

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To fail to maintain a standard of appearance, behavior, or performance.

   **Usage:** euphemistic; transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To fail to maintain a standard of appearance, behavior, or performance.
   1. To gain weight.

      **Usage:** euphemistic; transitive; usually reflexively

      **Example:** Wow, dude! You've really let yourself go this time!

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see let, go.

   **Example:** Please, Mom, can you let me go to her party?

   **Example:** We shan’t let our old car go to anyone for less than 15 grand.
