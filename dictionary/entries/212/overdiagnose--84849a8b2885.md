### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To diagnose something more often than it actually occurs.

   **Example:** Health experts deny claims that they overdiagnose mental health conditions.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

   **Source antonyms:** underdiagnose

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To diagnose as a clinical case what is truly a subclinical case.

   **Example:** Her GP incorrectly referred her to a pulmonologist, overdiagnosing what was clearly a case of subclinical, mild allergy.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences
