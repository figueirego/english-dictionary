### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To share or subscribe to (a belief, etc.).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I do not hold to their radical opinions.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To adhere strongly to (a conviction, story, etc.).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to hold to one's original story

   **Example:** Despite contrary admonitions, they held to their fundamentalist Christian views.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To follow, be consistent with (a rule, pattern, etc.)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To compel (someone) to remain faithful to (a commitment, moral standard, etc.).

   **Usage:** ditransitive

   **Example:** Jill holds her grandfather to her own standards and finds them wanting.
