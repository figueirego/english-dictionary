### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To enter without being noticed.

   **Example:** I'm going to try to sneak in by the back door.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To barely advance or be allowed entry in a competition or organization despite minimal credentials or competitors thought to be superior.

   **Example:** With Newport's team suffering from injuries, Springfield was able to sneak in to the quarter-finals.

   **Example:** Despite a mediocre grade in English, Pat just managed to sneak in to the university.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To take (something) in covertly; to smuggle.

   **Example:** He had sneaked the alcohol in and was now handing it out to his friends.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To insert (words, ideas, etc.) into something without drawing attention to it.

   **Example:** His ideas got snuck in to the final draft.

   **Source synonyms:** wind
