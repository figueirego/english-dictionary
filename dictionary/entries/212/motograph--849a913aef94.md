### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A device used in the making of a loud-speaking telephone, depending on the fact that the friction between a metallic point and a moving cylinder of moistened chalk, or a moving slip of paper, on which it rests is diminished by the passage of a current between the point and the moving surface.

   **Usage:** historical
