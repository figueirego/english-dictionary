### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Virtual stock having a calculated value, without real-world fluctuations, used to motivate and compensate employees.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance

   **Source synonyms:** phantom stock; unit stock
