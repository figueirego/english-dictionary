## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Synonym of Tower of the Winds, a monument in ancient Athens that included a clepsydra and multiple prominent sundials.

   **Usage:** historical

   **Source synonyms:** Tower of the Winds (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Synonym of Solarium, a monument in ancient Rome whose obelisk was once believed to have acted as a sundial gnomon.

   **Usage:** historical

   **Source synonyms:** Solarium (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A constellation of the southern sky, said to resemble a pendulum clock. It lies between the constellations Fornax and Dorado.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astronomy; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Alternative letter-case form of horologium, the book of hours in the Eastern Orthodox and Catholic churches.

   **Usage:** alt-of

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity

   **Alternative form of:** [horologium](../017/horologium--05f4489e5ead.md)
