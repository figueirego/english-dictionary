## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One of the twelve terms used in East Asian cultures to represent
   1. The (approximately) twelve years of Jupiter's orbital period, forming the Chinese zodiac and personified as twelve or sixty gods known as Tai Sui.

      **Usage:** historical

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astronomy; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One of the twelve terms used in East Asian cultures to represent
   1. The twelve animals representing the Chinese zodiac, (particularly) as a birthyear.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astrology; human-sciences; mysticism; philosophy; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. One of the twelve terms used in East Asian cultures to represent
   1. The twelve months of the Chinese lunar year.

      **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. One of the twelve terms used in East Asian cultures to represent
   1. The twelve bearings of traditional Chinese navigation, comprising 30° divisions of the compass beginning with due north.

      **Usage:** historical navigation

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. One of the twelve terms used in East Asian cultures to represent
   1. The duodecimal divisions of the Chinese seasons, beginning with the second of the three divisions of winter.

      **Usage:** historical

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astronomy; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. One of the twelve terms used in East Asian cultures to represent
   1. The duodecimal divisions of the day into "Chinese" or "double hours", beginning with 11 pm.

      **Usage:** historical chronometry

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. One of the twelve terms used in East Asian cultures to represent
   1. The exact time at the middle of a "Chinese" or "double hour", beginning with midnight.

      **Usage:** historical chronometry
