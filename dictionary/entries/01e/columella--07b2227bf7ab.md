### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of various small structures in plants or animals that are columnar in shape.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The skin at the end of the septum which separates the nostrils.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. In birds, reptiles, and amphibians, the small bone which carries vibration from the tympanum to the inner ear.

   **Usage:** comparative

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. In gastropods, the structure that extends down the middle of the whorls of the shell.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; malacology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The structure at the center of the calyx where the septa join together.

   **Usage:** coral science

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The central sterile portion of the sporangium in various fungi.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; mycology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A rod-shaped reinforcing element of the sexine layer of a pollen grain.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; chemistry; microbiology; natural-sciences; palynology; physical-sciences
