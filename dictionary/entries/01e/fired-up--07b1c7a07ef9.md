### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Very emotional or excited about something (whether positive or negative).

   **Usage:** colloquial

   **Example:** Watching the political commentators on cable news channels always gets him fired up.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a person

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Running or switched on; especially in the moments after just starting.

   **Usage:** colloquial

   **Example:** Now that all the ship's boilers were fired up, the voyage was well and truly underway.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of an engine or other machine
