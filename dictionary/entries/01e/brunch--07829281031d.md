### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A meal eaten later in the day than breakfast and earlier than lunch, often in a leisurely, festive, and/or social way, and often consisting of typical foods from both of those meals.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** I normally have brunch at the café next to work.

   **Example:** Let's do brunch sometime at the salad bar!

   **Example:** Feeling a bit peckish, Sandra made herself some brunch with the leftovers in her fridge.
