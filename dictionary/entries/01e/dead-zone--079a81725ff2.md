### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An area with no life.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An area of the ocean where oxygen levels are too low to support life, especially as a result of pollution.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; ecology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A region where mobile phones do not operate because there is no base station or repeater nearby.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** communications; electrical-engineering; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; telecommunications

   **Usage:** gridiron football

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports
