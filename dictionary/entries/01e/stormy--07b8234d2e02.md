### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of or pertaining to storms.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Characterized by, or proceeding from, a storm; subject to storms; agitated with strong winds and heavy rain.

   **Example:** a stormy season or a stormy day

   **Source synonyms:** angry
