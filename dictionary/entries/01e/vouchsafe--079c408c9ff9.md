### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To graciously give, to condescendingly grant a right, benefit, outcome, etc.; to deign to acknowledge.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** vouch (archaic)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To receive or accept in condescension.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To disclose or divulge.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** She vouchsafed to me that she regretted ever marrying him.

   **Example:** What have the gods told you? What have you foreseen? The gods have vouchsafed me nothing, told me nothing.
