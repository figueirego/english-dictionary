### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who makes a donation.

   **Example:** The charity raised $2,000 from various donors.

   **Example:** The hospital is seeking an organ donor.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An object, typically broken beyond repair, that is used for spare parts.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A group or molecule that donates either a radical, electrons or a moiety in a chemical reaction. Compare acceptor.

   **Example:** a carbonyl donor molecule
