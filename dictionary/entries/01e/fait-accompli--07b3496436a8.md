### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Something which has already happened, before those affected had the chance to raise objections or queries; especially, such a thing that cannot be undone.

   **Example:** Thanks to a bureaucratic fait accompli, the demolition was already approved and has already started.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An action or establishment that cannot be undone.

   **Example:** The introduction of white clover to the Americas is a fait accompli.

   **Source synonyms:** done deal; water under the bridge
