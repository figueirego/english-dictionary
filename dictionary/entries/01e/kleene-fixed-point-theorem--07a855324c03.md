**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Suppose (L,⊑) is a directed-complete partial order with a least element, and let f&#58;L→L be a Scott-continuous (and therefore monotone) function. Then f has a least fixed point, which is the supremum of the ascending Kleene chain of f.
