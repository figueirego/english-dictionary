### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Who ever&#58; an emphatic form of who.

   **Usage:** interrogative; nominative

   **Example:** Whoever thought up that stupid idea?

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any person or persons that.

   **Usage:** nominative; fused relative

   **Example:** Whoever breaks the law will be punished.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The person that (no matter who).

   **Usage:** nominative; fused relative

   **Example:** Whoever robbed that old lady ought to be locked up.

   **Example:** I don't know what it is. Ask whoever put it there.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Regardless of the person or persons that.

   **Usage:** nominative

   **Example:** Whoever stole the painting, the police will catch the thief in no time.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Any person or persons.

   **Usage:** informal; nominative

   **Example:** I don't care who gets it; give it to whoever.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Misspelling of who ever other than in interrogative use.

   **Usage:** alt-of; misspelling; nominative

   **Alternative form of:** who ever other than in interrogative use
