### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To replenish (a pasture, lawn, or field) by adding more seeds over top of the existing vegetation (as opposed to tilling and replanting the land).

   **Source synonyms:** oversow

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To sow excessively, at overdensity of seeds per unit area.
