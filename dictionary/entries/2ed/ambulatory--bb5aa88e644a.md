### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Example:** ambulatory exercise

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Able to walk about and not bedridden.

   **Usage:** comparable

   **Example:** an ambulatory patient

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** astir; up and about

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

   **Example:** ambulatory medical care

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

   **Example:** ambulatory fleet

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Accustomed to move from place to place; not stationary; movable.

   **Example:** an ambulatory court, which exercises its jurisdiction in different places

   **Source synonyms:** astir; mobile; ambulatory; animate; kinetic; mobile; motile; motive; movable; vagile
