### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make moist or moister.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** She put on some lip balm to moisten her lips.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To become moist or moister.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** His eyes began to moisten as he held back tears.
