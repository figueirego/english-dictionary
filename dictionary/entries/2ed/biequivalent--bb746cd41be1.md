### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Both left equivalent and right equivalent; Having the property that there exists a pair of mappings M₁ (A-&gt;B) and M₂ (B-&gt;A) such that M₁M₂(A) is equivalent to applying the unity operator to A and M₂ M₁(B) is equivalent to applying the unity operator to B.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences
