### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To accompany the driver of a vehicle on a journey as an armed escort (originally with a shotgun); (by extension) to accompany someone in order to assist and protect.

   **Usage:** US; idiomatic

   **Example:** He attended the meeting to ride shotgun for the sales team, in case anyone had a technical question.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To ride in the front passenger seat of a vehicle, next to the driver.

   **Usage:** US; broadly; idiomatic; slang

   **Example:** When both kids want to ride shotgun with Mom, they’ll just have to take turns.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To supervise a process and watch for any risks.

   **Usage:** US; broadly; idiomatic; slang
