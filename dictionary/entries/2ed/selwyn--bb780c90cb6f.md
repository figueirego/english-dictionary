## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A surname transferred from the given name.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A male given name from Old English transferred back from the surname, in quiet use since the 19th century.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of a number of places in Australia, New Zealand, Canada and US.
   1. A river in Canterbury, New Zealand, named after Bishop George Selwyn; in full, Selwyn River.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any of a number of places in Australia, New Zealand, Canada and US.
   1. A small settlement on the south side of the river in Canterbury, New Zealand.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Any of a number of places in Australia, New Zealand, Canada and US.
   1. A territorial authority of Canterbury, New Zealand, named after the river; in full, Selwyn District.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Any of a number of places in Australia, New Zealand, Canada and US.
   1. A locality, formerly a mining town in the Shire of Cloncurry, Queensland, Australia, named after the Selwyn Range.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Any of a number of places in Australia, New Zealand, Canada and US.
   1. A locality in Alpine Shire, north eastern Victoria, Australia.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Any of a number of places in Australia, New Zealand, Canada and US.
   1. A township in Peterborough County, Ontario, Canada, renamed from Smith-Ennismore-Lakefield in 2013.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. Any of a number of places in Australia, New Zealand, Canada and US.
   1. An unincorporated community in Mingo County, West Virginia, United States.

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. Ellipsis of Selwyn College, Cambridge.
