### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A menacing, ghost-like monster in children's stories.

   **Source synonyms:** bogey; bug-a-boo; bugaboo; bugbear; bogeyperson; bogeywoman

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any make-believe threat, especially one used to intimidate or distract, or a real threat greatly exaggerated for those purposes; often taking the form of a notorious historical personage suggested to return as a revenant to punish for wrongdoing.

   **Usage:** broadly; figuratively

   **Example:** The bogeyman of a Halloween-candy poisoner behind every door has animated occasional moral panics against trick-or-treating.

   **Example:** The specter of an election-vote recount is this year's political bogeyman.

   **Source synonyms:** bug-a-boo; bugaboo; chimera
