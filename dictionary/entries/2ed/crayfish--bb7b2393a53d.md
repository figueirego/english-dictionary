### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of numerous freshwater decapod crustaceans in superfamily Astacoidea or Parastacoidea, resembling the related lobster but usually much smaller.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of numerous freshwater decapod crustaceans in superfamily Astacoidea or Parastacoidea, resembling the related lobster but usually much smaller.
   1. A freshwater crustacean (family Cambaridae), sometimes used as an inexpensive seafood or as fish bait.

      **Usage:** New-England; Pennsylvania; Upper Midwestern US

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A rock lobster (family Palinuridae).

   **Usage:** Australia; New-Zealand; South-Africa

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A freshwater crayfish (family Parastacidae), such as the gilgie, marron, or yabby.

   **Usage:** Australia

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The species Thenus orientalis of the slipper lobster family (Scyllaridae).

   **Usage:** Singapore
