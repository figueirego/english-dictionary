### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any one of several temporary alliances between Christian countries of (especially mediaeval) Europe, formed for some specific objective of ostensible benefit to Christianity.

   **Usage:** historical

   **Example:** The initiative for a Holy League often came from a secular power, not the pope, but papal involvement was inevitable if it was to have the same spiritual benefits to participants as a crusade.
