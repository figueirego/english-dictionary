## Noun

### Forms

- micromanipulations — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The manipulation of objects too small to be seen with the unaided eye.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A very small adjustment; a tweak.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A subtly manipulative act; An interpersonal ploy to influence others without the direct use of power.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The statement "So-and-so is idiotic, and anyone who says otherwise is also idiotic." is a micromanipulation in at least the sense of the intent of isolating the dissidents and other people from each other.
