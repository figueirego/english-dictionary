### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A chivalric feat of arms involving the forcing of a passage protected by a knight or a group of knights.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any feat of arms, especially when used to display the prowess of the participants.
