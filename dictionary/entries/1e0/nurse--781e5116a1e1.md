### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person involved in providing direct care for the sick&#58;
   1. Anyone performing this role, regardless of training or profession.

      **Usage:** informal

      **Example:** My aunt was my nurse while I recuperated at home from surgery.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person involved in providing direct care for the sick&#58;
   1. A medical worker performing this role, typically someone trained to provide such care but having credentials and rank below a doctor or physician assistant.

      **Example:** The nurse made her rounds through the hospital ward.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A person involved in providing direct care for the sick&#58;
   1. A medical worker, such as a registered nurse, having training, credentials, and rank above a nurse assistant.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; healthcare

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A person (usually a woman) who takes care of other people’s children.

   **Usage:** archaic

   **Example:** They hired a nurse to care for their young boy.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. One who, or that which, brings up, rears, causes to grow, trains, or fosters.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** Eton College has been called "the chief nurse of England's statesmen".

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** agriculture; business; horticulture; lifestyle
