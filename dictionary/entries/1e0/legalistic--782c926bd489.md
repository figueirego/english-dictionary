## Adjective

### Forms

- more legalistic — comparative
- most legalistic — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of a person, following the letter of the law.

   **Example:** He's very legalistic, so let's hope the law is just and merciful as well as strict.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of a person, tending to resort to the law, as one who sues frequently.

   **Example:** His legalistic tendencies irritated his neighbors, especially since they had to defend themselves against his frivolous suits.

   **Source synonyms:** litigious; litigatious; litiginous

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Practicing or characterized by legalism.

   **Example:** She was legalistic in her observation of religious rules and traditions.
