### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To play (a game etc.) to its conclusion.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To play music to accompany the end of, or as a final segment in (a programme, broadcast etc.).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** And now, to play us out, please welcome Tom Waits.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To occur or develop in a certain manner.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Let's keep our heads down for a little while and see how things play out.

   **Example:** My date played out a little differently than I imagined.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To feed (rope, cord, etc.) so as to allow more length or slackness.

   **Usage:** transitive; perhaps influenced by or confused with 'pay out'

   **Example:** I sat at the top of the cliff and played out the rope as she descended.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To compete in a play-out; to compete against (an opponent) in a play-out.

   **Usage:** Europe; intransitive; transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To die.
