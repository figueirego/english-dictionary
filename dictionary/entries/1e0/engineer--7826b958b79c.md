**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Usage:** also; figuratively

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

   **Usage:** also; figuratively; obsolete

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A person professionally engaged in the technical design and construction of large-scale private and public works such as bridges, buildings, harbours, railways, roads, etc.; a civil engineer.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Source synonyms:** hostler

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Originally, a person engaged in designing, constructing, or maintaining engines or machinery; now (more generally), a person qualified or professionally engaged in any branch of engineering, or studying to do so.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Source synonyms:** hostler

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A person trained to operate an engine.
   1. A person who operates a steam engine; specifically (nautical), a person employed to operate the steam engine in the engine room of a ship.

      **Usage:** broadly; historical

      **Source synonyms:** hostler; engineman

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A person trained to operate an engine.
   1. A person who drives or operates a fire engine (firefighting apparatus).

      **Usage:** US; broadly

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** firefighting; government

      **Source synonyms:** hostler

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A person trained to operate an engine.
   1. A person who drives or operates a locomotive; a train driver.

      **Usage:** US; broadly

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** rail-transport; railways; transport

      **Source synonyms:** hostler

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Preceded by a qualifying word&#58; a person who uses abilities or knowledge to manipulate events or people.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** a political engineer

   **Source synonyms:** hostler

**Source sense R1.S9**

   **Usage:** broadly; derogatory; often

   **Source synonyms:** hostler
