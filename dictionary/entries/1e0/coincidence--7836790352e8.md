### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The property of being coincident, occurring at the same time or place.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The appearance of a meaningful connection between events when there is none.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** That the two writers were born and died on the same day is just a coincidence, although there are many conspiracy theories about it.

   **Example:** It is sheer coincidence that the cartoon resembles the local politician.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A coincidence point.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematical-analysis; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A fixed point of a correspondence; a point of a variety corresponding to itself under a correspondence.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences
