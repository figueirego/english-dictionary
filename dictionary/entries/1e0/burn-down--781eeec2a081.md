### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To cause (a structure) to burn to nothing.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The police are hoping to find the people who burned down the cottage.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To burn completely, so that nothing remains.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** A fire which started in the bedroom caused the cottage to burn down.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a structure

   **Source synonyms:** burn away; burn up; burn; burn away; burn down; burn up; burn to a crisp; char up; cinder; combure; combust (dated; transitive); cremate; forburn (obsolete); forsweal (obsolete); incinerate; scathe (poetic); torch

**Source sense R1.S3**
