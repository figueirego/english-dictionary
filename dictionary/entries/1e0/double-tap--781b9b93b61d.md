### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A tactical shooting technique of pulling the trigger of a weapon twice in quick succession to deliver two shots to the same target zone.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

2. Two shots or bullets fired in such a way.

   **Usage:** broadly
