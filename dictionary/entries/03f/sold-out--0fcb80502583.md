## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Out of stock.
   1. Unable to sell an item because no more are in inventory.

      **Example:** Sold-out stores can be a major problem around the time of a snowstorm.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Out of stock.
   1. Unavailable because all inventory has been sold.

      **Example:** The rock star played to dozens of sold-out arenas during his farewell tour.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Completely committed or dedicated to a cause, a team, a person, etc.; all in.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** After a close brush with death, she became a sold-out follower of her faith.
