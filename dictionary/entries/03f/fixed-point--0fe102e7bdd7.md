### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A fractional-number representation with a fixed number of digits after the decimal point. Compare floating point and integer.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences
