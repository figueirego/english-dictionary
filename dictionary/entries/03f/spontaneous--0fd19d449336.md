### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Self-directed; happening or acting without external cause or instigation; proceeding from natural feeling, temperament, or momentary impulse.

   **Example:** He made a spontaneous offer of help.

   **Source synonyms:** autonomous; automatic; involuntary; natural; unforced

   **Source antonyms:** induced; stimulated; voluntary

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Done by one's own free choice, or without planning.

   **Source synonyms:** impulsive; extemporaneous; unpremeditated; unprompted

   **Source antonyms:** calculated; deliberate; planned; premeditated

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Controlled and directed internally; self-active; spontaneous movement characteristic of living things.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Produced without being planted or without human cultivation or labor.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Sudden, without warning.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Said or done without prior planning or prior writing.

   **Example:** He made spontaneous remarks in the court, after being asked an unexpected question.

   **Source synonyms:** unwritten; abrupt; precipitous; subitaneous; abrupt; precipitant; precipitate; precipitous; spontaneous; subitaneous (obsolete); sudden; suddent
