### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An operator defined as a function of the differentiation operator (the operator which maps functions to their derivatives).

   **Example:** Define P(D) to be the differential operator given by P(D)\#61;D²\#43;5D\#43;6, where D is the differentiation operator. Then P(D)(\#92;sin(x))\#61;-\#92;sin(x)\#43;5\#92;cos(x)\#43;6.
