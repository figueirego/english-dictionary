### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having no head.

   **Source synonyms:** acephalic; headless

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Without a distinct head.

   **Usage:** applied to bivalve mollusks

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences; zoology

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Having the style spring from the base, instead of from the apex, as is the case in certain ovaries

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A system of society without centralised state authority, where power is welded amongst groups of community entities e.g. clans. Without a leader or chief.

   **Example:** an acephalous society / community

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; political-science; sciences; social-science; social-sciences; sociology

   **Source synonyms:** acephalic; leaderless

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Deficient in the beginning, as a line of poetry that is missing its expected opening syllable.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; phonology; prosody; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** acephalic

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Lacking the first portion of the text. (of a manuscript)

   **Source synonyms:** acephalic

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Without a beginning.

   **Usage:** rare
