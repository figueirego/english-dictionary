## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The waging of war in a manner which does not use nuclear, biological, or chemical weapons.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The waging of war in set military battles, as opposed to waging guerrilla warfare or fighting insurgencies.
