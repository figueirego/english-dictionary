## Noun · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Pronunciation

### Forms

- meetings — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of persons or things that meet.

   **Usage:** gerund; uncountable

   **Example:** Meeting him will be exciting. I enjoy meeting new people.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A gathering of persons for a purpose; an assembly.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** We need to have a meeting about that soon.

   **Source synonyms:** powwow

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The people at such a gathering.

   **Usage:** collective; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** What has the meeting decided.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An encounter between people, even accidental.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** They came together in a chance meeting on the way home from work.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A place or instance of junction or intersection; a confluence.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Earthquakes occur at the meeting of tectonic plates.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A religious service held by a charismatic preacher in small towns in the United States.

   **Usage:** countable; dated; uncountable; rustic US

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. An administrative unit in the Religious Society of Friends (Quakers).

   **Usage:** Quakerism; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Denver meeting is a part of Intermountain yearly meeting.

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** assembly; convocation; gathering; meet-up; rendezvous
