### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A traditional Japanese T-shaped, wrapped-front garment with square sleeves and a rectangular body, now generally worn only on formal occasions.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A long robe-like garment in Western fashion, which may be open at the front, loosely inspired by the Japanese garment.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A bathrobe or dressing gown.

   **Usage:** regional
