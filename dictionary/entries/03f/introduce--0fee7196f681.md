### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To cause (someone) to be acquainted (with someone else).

   **Usage:** ditransitive

   **Example:** Let me introduce you to my friends.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of people

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To make (something or someone) known, such as by formal announcement or recommendation.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The senator plans to introduce the bill in the next session.

   **Example:** Let me introduce our guest speaker.

   **Source synonyms:** give

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To add (something) to a system, a mixture, or a container.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Various pollutants were introduced into the atmosphere.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To bring (something) into practice.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Wheeled transport was introduced long ago.
