### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To cease an activity.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** We have been at this for hours; let’s call it a day and come back tomorrow when we are fresh.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To retire.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** After suffering massive losses for three years in a row, the boss decided to call it a day, and sold his company.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To end a period of activity, either because it is unsuccessful or because enough has been done.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** After he was reprimanded for slacking on the job, they decided to call it a day and fire Allen.
