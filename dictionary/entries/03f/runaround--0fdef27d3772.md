### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An evasive explanation in the form of multiple excuses.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I called them to find out what had happened to my parcel, but all I got was a runaround.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A detour or route that bypasses an obstacle.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A section of type that is narrower than that of the column it is part of; typically next to an illustration.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** media; printing; publishing

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A whitlow running around the fingernail but not affecting the bone.
