### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. The holder of a senior military title, originally designating the commander of an army and now a specific rank falling under field marshal (in the British army) and below general of the army or general of the air force in the US army and air forces.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** General Eisenhower was the first SACEUR.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. A great strategist or tactician.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Hannibal was one of the greatest generals of the ancient world.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. A general fact or proposition; a generality.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** We have dealt with the generals; now let us turn to the particulars.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. The head of certain religious orders, especially Dominicans or Jesuits.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. The equivalent of a king in Chinese chess, the xiangqi piece that is confined to the palace, moves orthogonally one point at a time, and whose loss decides the game.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** board-games; games; xiangqi

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. Synonym of admiral, a senior naval title, a great naval strategist or tactician.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

   **Source synonyms:** admiral (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

**Source sense R2.S7**

7. Short for general servant, a maid-of-all-work, a servant able to be assigned to any task in a household.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; colloquial; countable; historical; uncountable

   **Alternative form of:** general servant

<a id="r2-s8"></a>

**Source sense R2.S8**

8. Short for general election, a regular election for most offices in a region or country.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; countable

   **Example:** Sure, this candidate is the favorite in the primary among the party faithful, but how is he going to do in the general?

   **Alternative form of:** [general election](../277/general-election--9de30b37d75e.md)

<a id="r2-s9"></a>

**Source sense R2.S9**

9. Short for general anesthetic, a substance used to numb most feeling throughout the body.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; countable

   **Alternative form of:** [general anesthetic](../131/general-anesthetic--4c52bcf02fa8.md)

<a id="r2-s10"></a>

**Source sense R2.S10**

10. Short for general anesthesia, the category of substances used to numb most feeling throughout the body or an instance of such substances.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; uncountable

   **Alternative form of:** [general anesthesia](../25e/general-anesthesia--97a9f77d11e9.md)

<a id="r2-s11"></a>

**Source sense R2.S11**

11. Short for general insurance, the area of the insurance industry dealing with policies for the general public.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; uncountable
