### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A group of people organized for a common purpose.

   **Example:** a work brigade; a fire brigade

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A military unit composed of several regiments (or battalions) and including soldiers from different arms of service.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A group of people who share views or a specific characteristic.

   **Usage:** derogatory

   **Example:** More sympathy for career criminals from the bleeding-heart brigade!

   **Example:** I wouldn't even want to be seen dead with those nerds of the bowl-cut brigade.
