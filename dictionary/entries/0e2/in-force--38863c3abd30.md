## Prep\_phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. With a substantial number of men capable of exerting force or of making a show of force.

   **Example:** The police arrived in force at dawn to raid the drug lab.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. With a substantial display of resources.

   **Example:** They made the presentation in force to try to impress the client.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. In effect; applicable.

   **Example:** Some of the old laws against treason are no longer in force.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; insurance; law

   **Source synonyms:** effective
