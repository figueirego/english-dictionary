### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An adult male singer who uses head tone or falsetto to sing far higher than the typical male vocal range.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A male singing voice far higher than the typical male vocal range.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
