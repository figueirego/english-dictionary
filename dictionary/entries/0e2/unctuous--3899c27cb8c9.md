### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having the nature or properties of an unguent or ointment; greasy, oily.

   **Usage:** or other substance

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a liquid

   **Source synonyms:** mellowy; oleaginous; saponaceous; slaistery (Scotland); slimy; smarmy (dated; rare); unctious (obsolete); unctuose (obsolete; rare); unguinous

   **Source antonyms:** ununctuous

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Having fat or oil present; fatty, greasy, oily.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of an aroma or taste, or a beverage (such as coffee or wine) or food (such as gravy, meat, or sauce)&#58; having layers of concentrated, velvety flavour; lush, rich.

   **Source synonyms:** savorous

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Of soil&#58; soft and sticky.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Of a person&#58;
   1. Complacent, self-satisfied, smug.

      **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Of a person&#58;
   1. Profusely polite, especially in an insincere and unpleasant manner.

      **Usage:** figuratively

      **Source synonyms:** greasy; oily; oleaginous; slimy; smarmy; asslicky; fawning; greasy; groveling; ingratiating; obsequious; oily; oleaginous; schmoozy; self-abasing; slimy; soapy; sucky (slang); sycophantic; toadyish; toadying; truckling; unctuous
