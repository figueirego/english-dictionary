### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Bearing; manner of presenting oneself.

   **Example:** Her deportment impressed her interviewers.

   **Source synonyms:** bearing; comportment; posture

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Conduct; public behavior.

   **Example:** Their deportment changed visibly as the policeman approached.

   **Source synonyms:** behavior; conduct

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Apparent level of schooling or training.

   **Example:** His academic deportment did not match his degree record.

   **Example:** The nun's deportment reflected her vocation.
