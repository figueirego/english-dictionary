**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A junior minister in certain government systems, particularly in the United Kingdom, who assists a senior minister (the Secretary of State) and represents the department in parliament.

   **Example:** The newly appointed parliamentary under secretary answered questions regarding the new infrastructure policy in the House of Commons.
