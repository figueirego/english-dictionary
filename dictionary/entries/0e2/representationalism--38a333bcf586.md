### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The belief that the conscious perception of the world is actually an internal replica of the world in the mind of the beholder.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; philosophy; sciences
