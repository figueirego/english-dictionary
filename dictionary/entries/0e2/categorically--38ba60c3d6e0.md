## Adverb

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /kæt.əˈɡɒ.ɹɪk.(ə.)li/ — source labels: UK

### Forms

- more categorically — comparative
- most categorically — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In a categorical manner.
   1. By the use of categories; defining a category; defined by, or corollary to, a category.

      **Example:** a welfare scheme for the categorically needy

      **Source synonyms:** generically; classically; typically

      **Source antonyms:** instantially; on a case-by-case basis

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In a categorical manner.
   1. Absolutely; in all regards (leaving no doubt or ambiguity).

      **Usage:** modal

      **Example:** She categorically denied all allegations of misconduct.
