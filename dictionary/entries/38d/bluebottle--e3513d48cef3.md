### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A cornflower, a plant that grows in grain fields, Centaurea cyanus.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of various blowflies of the genus Calliphora that have an iridescent metallic-blue body and make a loud buzzing noise when flying.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A blue ant, Diamma bicolor, a parasitic wasp native to Australia.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any of various large papilionid butterflies of the genus Graphium, also called triangles, etc.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A police officer.

   **Usage:** Australia; Ireland; UK; derogatory; slang

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A bluestocking.

   **Usage:** derogatory; obsolete
