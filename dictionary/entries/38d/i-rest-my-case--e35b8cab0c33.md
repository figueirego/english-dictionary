## Phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. My argument is proven; I conclude my statement.

   **Example:** All the evidence points toward the defendant's guilt. I rest my case.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

   **Source synonyms:** the case rests

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The previous statement or proclamation encapsulates my view.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** The drive will be too long, the seats too few, and the people too many. I rest my case.

   **Source antonyms:** I stand corrected
