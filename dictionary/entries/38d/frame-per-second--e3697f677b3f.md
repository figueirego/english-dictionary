### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A measure of how many times an imaging or display device produces unique consecutive images (frames) in one second.

   **Usage:** plural-normally

   **Example:** The graphics are really choppy; I'm only getting 12 frames per second.
