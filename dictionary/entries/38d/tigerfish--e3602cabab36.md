### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of a number of species of fish with stripes on their bodies, otherwise fearsome appearance, or aggressive predatory behavior, including, among others&#58;
   1. Any fish in the percoid genus Datnioides.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of a number of species of fish with stripes on their bodies, otherwise fearsome appearance, or aggressive predatory behavior, including, among others&#58;
   1. Any barracuda.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of a number of species of fish with stripes on their bodies, otherwise fearsome appearance, or aggressive predatory behavior, including, among others&#58;
   1. Any of the species Hoplias malabaricus and related characins in the family Erythrinidae.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any of a number of species of fish with stripes on their bodies, otherwise fearsome appearance, or aggressive predatory behavior, including, among others&#58;
   1. Any of the genus Pterois of east Asian lionfishes.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Any of a number of species of fish with stripes on their bodies, otherwise fearsome appearance, or aggressive predatory behavior, including, among others&#58;
   1. Any of the family Pimelodidae of catfishes.

**Source sense R1.S6**

   1. Any fish in the genus Hydrocynus of characins.
