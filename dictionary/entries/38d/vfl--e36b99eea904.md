## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Initialism of Victorian Football League, an Australian rules football league formed in Victoria, Australia, in 1896; renamed the Australian Football League (AFL) in 1990 after expansion to other states starting in the 1980s.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; historical; initialism; Australian rules football

   **Alternative form of:** Victorian Football League

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Initialism of Victorian Football League; a second-tier regional (country area) Australian rules football league operating within Victoria; derived from the historic Victorian Football Association, from which the future AFL broke in 1896.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; initialism; Australian rules football

   **Alternative form of:** Victorian Football League
