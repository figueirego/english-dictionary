## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An impediment to walking due to the feet or legs.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** His lameness may have prevented him from walking but it didn't stop him from running for public office.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The quality of being lame, pathetic or uncool.

   **Usage:** countable; informal; uncountable

   **Example:** I can't believe the lameness of the special effects in that movie.
