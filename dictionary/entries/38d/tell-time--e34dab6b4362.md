### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To measure time, to determine the current time.

   **Example:** Ancient civilizations told time at night by the passage of stars.

   **Source synonyms:** keep time

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To read the time as displayed on an analog clock.

   **Usage:** specifically

   **Example:** He couldn't tell time until he was in his teens.
