## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

- Europoors — plural
- europoor — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person living in Eastern Europe, where the standard of living is lower than in the West.

   **Usage:** derogatory; offensive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any European person, the implication being that all of Europe has the living standards once associated with the poorest countries, or much lower than some non-European countries, especially the US.

   **Usage:** broadly; offensive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A European person, especially one without much money.
