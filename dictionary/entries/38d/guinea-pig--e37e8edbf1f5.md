<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any tailless rodent of the genus Cavia, which have short ears and superficially resemble large hamsters.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any tailless rodent of the genus Cavia, which have short ears and superficially resemble large hamsters.
   1. A domestic guinea pig, Cavia porcellus, often kept as a pet.

      **Usage:** especially

      **Source synonyms:** cavy (formal; name); domestic cavy; domestic guinea pig

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A living experimental subject.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** He became a human guinea pig and was paid by the company.

   **Source synonyms:** lab rat

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

   **Usage:** dated; slang
