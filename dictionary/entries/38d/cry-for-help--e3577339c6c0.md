## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see cry, for, help.

   **Example:** We heard the drowning woman's cries for help and ran to assist.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Acting out as a means of displaying a subconscious desire for attention or help.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** In her second year at the school Alexis stopped doing her homework and would often scribble on walls. Her teachers wondered whether this was a cry for help, or if she was simply misbehaving.
