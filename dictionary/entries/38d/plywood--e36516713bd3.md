### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Construction material supplied in sheets, and made of three or more layers of wood veneer glued together, laid up with alternating layers having their grain perpendicular to each other.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** After the hurricane there was a severe regional shortage of plywood, especially exterior plywood.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A specific grade or type of this construction material.

   **Usage:** countable; usually

   **Example:** We stock exterior plywoods, interior plywoods, and furniture plywoods.
