### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The world of the dead, located underneath the world of the living; the afterlife.

   **Example:** In Hindu scriptures, the four-eyed dogs Śārvara and Śyāma guard the underworld's entrance.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; lifestyle; mysticism; mythology; philosophy; religion; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** netherworld; Hades; hell

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. That part of society that is engaged in crime or vice, and particularly those involved in organized crime.

   **Example:** A disproportionate amount of local politicians have connections to the underworld.
