### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Lacking motivation, without impetus to strive or excel.

   **Example:** an unmotivated student

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a person or persons

   **Source synonyms:** demotivated

   **Source antonyms:** motivated; remotivated

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. For which there is no motive, or no apparent rational motive.

   **Example:** an unmotivated murder

   **Source context (verbatim):** of an act or behavior

   **Source synonyms:** unprovoked
