### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An inhabitant of a place; one who dwells in a certain place.

   **Example:** The giant squid is one of many denizens of the deep.

   **Source synonyms:** dweller; inhabitant; native; resident; inhabitant; dweller; resident; denizen

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One who frequents a place.

   **Example:** The denizens of that pub are of the roughest sort.

   **Source synonyms:** regular; habitué

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A person with rights between those of naturalized citizen and resident alien (roughly permanent resident), obtained through letters patent.

   **Usage:** UK; historical

   **Example:** Though born in Iceland, he became a denizen of Britain after leaving Oxford.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An animal or plant from a particular range or habitat.

   **Example:** The bald eagle is a denizen of the northern part of the state.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A foreign word that has become naturalised in another language in terms of use, but not in terms of form.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences
