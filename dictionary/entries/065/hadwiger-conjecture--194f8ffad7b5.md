### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A conjecture regarding the relationship between the number of colors needed by a given graph and the size of its largest clique minor.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** graph-theory; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The conjecture that at most 2n smaller homothetic bodies are necessary to contain any single given n-dimensional convex body.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The conjecture that every simplex can be dissected into orthoschemes, using a number of orthoschemes bounded by a function of the dimension of the simplex.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences
