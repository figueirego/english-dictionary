### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A system for controlling an aircraft's rotational or translational motion in one or more axes.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** The airplane's primary flight controls comprise elevators and ailerons driven by manually-controlled servo tabs, and a hydraulically-powered rudder and spoilerons.
