### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To fall back again; to slide or turn back into a former state or practice.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** He has improved recently but keeps relapsing into states of utter confusion.

   **Example:** to relapse into a stupor, into vice, or into barbarism

2. To recur; to worsen, be aggravated (after a period of improvement).

   **Usage:** informal; intransitive; specifically

   **Example:** relapsed after 5 months of being clean
