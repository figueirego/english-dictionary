### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To swallow with difficulty.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I was already stuffed, but I managed to choke down some more apple pie anyway. It was so good that I couldn't stop.

   **Example:** I didn't like her casserole because I don't care for cumin, but I managed to choke down a plateful anyway, because she meant well and I didn't have the heart to disappoint her.
