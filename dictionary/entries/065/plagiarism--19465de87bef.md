### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Copying of another person's ideas, text, or other creative work, and presenting it as one's own, especially without permission; plagiarizing.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Even if it's not illegal, plagiarism is usually frowned upon.

   **Example:** Copy from one, it's plagiarism. Copy from two, it's research.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Text or other work resulting from this act.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** The novel was awash in plagiarism, with entire passages lifted verbatim.

**Source sense R1.S3**

   **Usage:** countable
