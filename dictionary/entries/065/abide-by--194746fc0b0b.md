### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To accept (a decision or law) and act in accordance with it; to conform to (a decision or law); to acquiesce to (a decision or law).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I don't agree with it, but I'll abide by the decision to give the reward to her.

   **Example:** The defendent has abided by my ruling in good faith.

   **Source synonyms:** acquiesce; conform; abide by; accept; acquiesce; go along with; bite the bullet; face up to; face the music; grin and bear it; grit one's teeth; make a virtue of necessity; make the best of a bad job; pay the penalty; pay the price; suck it up; swallow a bitter pill; take sitting down

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To remain faithful to (something or someone); to adhere to (an idea or plan).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He may have shown his true colors, but he's supported me for years, so I'll abide by him.

   **Example:** She has served me loyally, abiding by my rule for almost my entire life.

   **Source synonyms:** stand by; go to the wall for; stick with; abide by; hold on; keep faith; go to the wall for someone (UK; idiomatic); stand by (idiomatic); stick by; stick with
