## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

- semiconvergents — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A kind of fraction. If (h\_n-1)/(k\_n-1), (h\_n)/(k\_n) are successive convergents, then any fraction of the form (h\_n-1+ah\_n)/(k\_n-1+ak\_n), where a is a nonnegative integer and the numerators and denominators are between the n and n+1 terms inclusive, is a semiconvergent.

   **Example:** The semiconvergents to the continued fraction expansion of a real number include all the rational approximations which are better than any approximation with a smaller denominator.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences
