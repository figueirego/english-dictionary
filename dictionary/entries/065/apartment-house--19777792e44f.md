### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Synonym of apartment building&#58; any building comprising apartments.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** Heritage House is a ten-story apartment house for seniors in the heart of downtown.

   **Source synonyms:** apartment building (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A house comparable in size to a single-family house but comprising several apartments; typically not more than two or three stories tall and containing a single-digit number of apartments (such as two to six).

   **Usage:** strictly

   **Example:** Armitage House is a stately old home on Main Street; it was built in the 1910s as the private home of a local factory owner, and years later it was converted into a trendy apartment house. \[tour bus banter\]
