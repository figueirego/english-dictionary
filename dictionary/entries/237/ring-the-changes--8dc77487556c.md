### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make patterned sound sequences on bells, starting and ending on the same tone.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To run through all possible variations.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To enliven by varying combinations.

   **Usage:** UK

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To pass counterfeit money.

   **Usage:** UK; slang; 19th century and earlier

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To trick a shopkeeper into giving too much change.

   **Usage:** dated; slang

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To take advantage of a change in order to make money.
