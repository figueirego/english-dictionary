**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A plant-derived polysaccharide found in cell walls (especially in fruits) that forms a gel under acidic conditions and is commonly used to thicken foods like jam and jellies.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Apple is rich in pectin and so is often added to other fruits when making jam so it will set.
