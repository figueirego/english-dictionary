### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of a group of gram-negative bacteria, of the genus Rickettsia, carried as parasites by ticks, fleas and lice, that cause typhus and other diseases.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
