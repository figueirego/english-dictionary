<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any interaction between two species that benefits both; typically involves the exchange of substances or services.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; ecology; natural-sciences

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** economics; sciences
