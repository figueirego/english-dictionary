### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who drives slowly or inexpertly, as for leisure or sightseeing.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I was eager to get there, but I got stuck behind one Sunday driver after another on that trip.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One who drives infrequently, and often poorly.

   **Usage:** dated
