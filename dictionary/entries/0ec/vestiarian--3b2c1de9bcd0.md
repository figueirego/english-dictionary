### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. During the vestiarian controversy, a supporter of the wearing of vestments by the clergy (as opposed to an anti-vestiarian who considered the wearing of vestments a form of idolatry).

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**
