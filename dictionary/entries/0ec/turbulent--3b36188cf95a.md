**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Violently disturbed or agitated; tempestuous, tumultuous.

   **Example:** It is dangerous to sail in turbulent seas.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Being in, or causing, disturbance or unrest.

   **Example:** The mid-19th century was a turbulent time in American history.
