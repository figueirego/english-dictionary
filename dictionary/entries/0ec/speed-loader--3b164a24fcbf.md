### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A pressed metal (or plastic) clip, holding a set number of cartridges for a firearm, and used for rapid reloading of the weapon or its magazine.

   **Example:** With a speed loader, a practiced user can dump and reload their revolver's cylinder surprisingly fast — even, in some cases, no slower than someone can swap magazines in a semiauto.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; firearms; government; military; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; politics; tools; war; weaponry

   **Source synonyms:** charger

**Source sense R1.S2**
