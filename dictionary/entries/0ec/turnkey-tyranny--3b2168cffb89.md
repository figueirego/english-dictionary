### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An institutional, legal, and technical infrastructure — particularly mass surveillance, data collection, and centralized state power — that is built up to a point where it is likely to be abused for authoritarian ends, or could be activated by any government so inclined with little additional effort.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Civil-liberties advocates warned that the expanding surveillance apparatus had created turnkey tyranny.
