### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. By habit; in a habitual manner.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** He is habitually inattentive of time.

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Occurring regularly or usually.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Professor Franklein is habitually pessimistic.
