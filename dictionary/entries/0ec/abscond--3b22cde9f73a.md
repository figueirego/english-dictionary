### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To flee, often secretly; to steal away.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The thieves absconded with our property.

   **Source synonyms:** flee; run away; steal away

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To flee, often secretly; to steal away.
   1. To hide, conceal, or absent oneself clandestinely, with the intent to avoid legal process

      **Usage:** intransitive

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

      **Source synonyms:** flee; run away; steal away; jump bail

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To flee, often secretly; to steal away.
   1. To abandon a hive.

      **Usage:** intransitive

      **Source context (verbatim):** of bee colonies

      **Source synonyms:** flee; run away; steal away

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To hide, to be in hiding or concealment.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To evade, to hide or flee from.

   **Usage:** transitive; uncommon

   **Example:** The captain absconded his responsibility.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To conceal; to take away.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** conceal
