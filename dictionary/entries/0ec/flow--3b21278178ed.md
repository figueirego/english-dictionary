### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Movement in people or things characterized with a continuous motion, involving either a non solid mass or a multitude.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The movement of a real or figurative fluid.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A formalization of the idea of the motion of particles in a fluid, as a group action of the real numbers on a set.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The notion of flow is basic to the study of ordinary differential equations.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Smoothness or continuity.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The room was small, but it had good symmetry and flow.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The amount of a fluid that moves or the rate of fluid movement.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Turn on the valve and make sure you have sufficient flow.

   **Example:** Other devices measure water flow in streams fed by melted ice.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A flow pipe, carrying liquid away from a boiler or other central plant (compare with return pipe which returns fluid to central plant).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A mental state characterized by concentration, focus, and enjoyment of a given task.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; psychology; sciences

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. The emission of blood during menstruation.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Tampons can be small or large, slender or thick. From “slender” to “super”, you can pick the size that matches your flow.

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. The ability to skillfully rap along to a beat.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; rap music jargon

   **Example:** The production on his new mixtape is mediocre but his flow is on point.

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To move as a fluid from one position to another.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Rivers flow from springs and lakes.

   **Example:** Tears flow from the eyes.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To proceed; to issue forth; to emanate.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Wealth flows from industry and economy.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To move or match smoothly, gracefully, or continuously.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The writing is grammatically correct, but it just doesn't flow.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To have or be in abundance; to abound, so as to run or flow over.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To hang loosely and wave.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To rise, as the tide; opposed to ebb.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The tide flows twice in twenty-four hours.

   **Source synonyms:** come in; come up

**Source sense R2.S7**

7. To arrange (text in a wordprocessor, etc.) so that it wraps neatly into a designated space; to reflow.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r2-s8"></a>

**Source sense R2.S8**

8. To allow (a liquid) to flow.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s9"></a>

**Source sense R2.S9**

9. To cover with water or other liquid; to overflow; to inundate; to flood.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s10"></a>

**Source sense R2.S10**

10. To cover with varnish.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s11"></a>
