### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A branch of number theory in which suitable ideas and techniques from analytic number theory are generalised and applied to a variety of mathematical fields.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** The prime number theorem serves as a prototypical example of an idea generalised via abstract analytic number theory and adapted to other fields; the emphasis is on results pertaining to asymptotic analysis.
