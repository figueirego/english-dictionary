### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An early telephone with a diaphragm of soft iron placed close to the pole of a magnet upon which is wound a coil of fine wire. Its vibrations produce corresponding currents in the wire by induction.

   **Usage:** dated
