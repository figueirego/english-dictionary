### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of cloth, clothing, furnishings, etc.&#58; frayed and worn to an extent that the nap is damaged and the warp and weft threads show; shabby, worn-out.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In poor condition; damaged, shabby; also, poorly equipped or provided for, inadequate, meagre, scanty.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Source synonyms:** awanting; deficient; dismal; exiguous; geason (UK; dialectal); inadequate; insufficient; lank (obsolete); lean; lousy; meager; measly; paltry; poor; raunchy; scant; scantling; scanty; scarce; skimpy; sleazy; slender (figuratively); spare; sparse; threadbare (figuratively); unsatisfactory; unsufficient; wanting; wanty (UK; dialectal)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of an argument, excuse, etc.&#58; used so often that it is no longer effective or interesting; banal, clichéd, trite.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Source synonyms:** banal; bathetic; bromidic; cheesy; stock; clichéd; commonplace; corny; dog-eared; hackneyed; hoary; overdone; overused; overworked; pat; played out; shopworn; stale; threadbare (figuratively); timeworn; tired; trite; warmed-over; well-worn

   **Source antonyms:** fresh; new; all-new; brand new; brand spanking new; cherry; fresh; freshly made; green; hot; mint; nascent; newish; new-laid; novel; pristine; red-hot; unused; verdurous; virgin; virginal; youthful

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An argument or assertion with little in the way of substance or supporting evidence.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Source synonyms:** awanting; deficient; dismal; exiguous; geason (UK; dialectal); inadequate; insufficient; lank (obsolete); lean; lousy; meager; measly; paltry; poor; raunchy; scant; scantling; scanty; scarce; skimpy; sleazy; slender (figuratively); spare; sparse; threadbare (figuratively); unsatisfactory; unsufficient; wanting; wanty (UK; dialectal)
