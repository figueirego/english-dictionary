### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used in hesitant speech, or to express uncertainty.

   **Usage:** UK; humorous

   **Example:** She was going to, erm... the salon, I think.

   **Source synonyms:** um; umm; em; eh; uh

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used to express embarrassment or subtle disagreement.

   **Usage:** UK; humorous

   **Example:** Erm, I don't think that was supposed to happen.

   **Example:** – Bob would never say something like that! – Erm, he just did.
