### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A serious form or stage of periodontal disease which may lead to loss of bone and falling out of teeth.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** An untreated gingivitis may lead to periodontitis, both forms of periodontal disease.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** dentistry; medicine; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** parodontitis
