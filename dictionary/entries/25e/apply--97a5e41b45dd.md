### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To lay or place; to put (one thing to another)

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to apply cream to a rash

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To put to use; to use or employ for a particular purpose, or in a particular case

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to apply funds to the repayment of a debt

   **Source synonyms:** appropriate; devote; use

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To make use of, declare, or pronounce, as suitable, fitting, or relevant.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** We need to apply the skills we’ve learned to solve this problem.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To put closely; to join; to engage and employ diligently or with attention.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** attach; incline

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To work diligently and attentively.

   **Usage:** reflexive

   **Example:** My parents say I could do well in school if I would apply myself.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To address oneself; to refer.

   **Usage:** reflexive; transitive; usually

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To submit oneself as a candidate (with the adposition "to" or "at" designating the recipient of the submission, and the adposition "for" designating the position).

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** I recently applied to the tavern for a job as a bartender.

   **Example:** Most of the colleges she applied to were ones she thought she had a good chance of getting into.

   **Example:** Many of them don't know it, but almost a third of the inmates are eligible to apply for parole or work-release programs.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To pertain or be relevant.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** That rule only applies to foreigners.

   **Source synonyms:** extend

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To busy; to keep at work; to ply.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. To visit.

   **Usage:** obsolete
