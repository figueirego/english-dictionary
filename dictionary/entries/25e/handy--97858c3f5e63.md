### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Easy to use, useful.

   **Example:** Some people regard duct tape as a handy fix-all.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Nearby, within reach.

   **Example:** You wouldn’t have a screwdriver handy, would you?

   **Example:** I keep a first-aid kit handy in case of emergency.

   **Source synonyms:** at hand; on hand; to hand

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Dexterous, skilful. (of a person)

   **Example:** She's very handy&#58; she made all her own kitchen cupboards.

   **Source synonyms:** crafty

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Physically violent; tending to use one's fists.

   **Usage:** slang

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Of a freight ship&#58; having a small cargo capacity (less than 40,000 DWT); belonging to the handysize class.
