### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who is highly intellectual or contemplative but detached from practical affairs, especially the need to earn a living.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Example:** My wife is such a luftmensch she missed our anniversary dinner because she was too busy reading her books!

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A beggar, a petty trader, a peddler, and various types of paupers.

   **Usage:** slang; Yiddish
