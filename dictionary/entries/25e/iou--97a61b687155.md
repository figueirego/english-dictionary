### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. I owe you.

   **Example:** Customarily, if you borrow $10 for lunch from a coworker, writing IOU $10 on a yellow sticky note acts as a legal promissory note that you will repay said amount on payday.

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. An informal promissory note that is not a negotiable instrument.

   **Example:** When David couldn’t afford to match the money in the poker pot, he added an IOU for the remaining amount instead.
