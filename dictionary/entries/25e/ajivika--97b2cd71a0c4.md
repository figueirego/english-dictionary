## Proper noun · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A heterodox school of Indian philosophy, believed to have been founded in the 5th century BCE by Makkhali Gosāla; a major rival of Vedic religion, early Buddhism, and Jainism.
