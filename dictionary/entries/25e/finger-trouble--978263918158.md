## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Fumbling use of the fingers that causes mistakes to occur, especially the pressing of wrong buttons on a machine or wrong keys on a keyboard.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** I had some finger trouble and began my letter 'Dead Sir or Madman'.
