### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The rule of an alien; rule from without; government by an extraterritorial power.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Despite installing puppet governments in the marches, the subjects of the empire’s conquered territories were still very conscious of living under heterarchy.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An example of this government.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** The proposed liberation of our largely undeveloped economy goes too far — the resulting foreign dominance of our markets would make us a heterarchy.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A system of organization where the elements of the organization are unranked (non-hierarchical) or where they possess the potential to be ranked a number of different ways.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
