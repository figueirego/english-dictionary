<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A serious and often fatal disease caused by the infection of an open wound with the anaerobic bacterium Clostridium tetani, found in soil and the intestines and faeces of animals.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; pathology; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** lockjaw

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
