### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. The members used to build a (structural) crib, usually of timbers or logs, but also of concrete, steel or even plastic; cribwork.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. As a whole, the heavy structure built to support an existing structure from underneath, as with a mineshaft or when raising a building off its foundation, as for moving to another location,

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** After the Loma Prieta earthquake, they had to put cribbing under portions of San Francisco's Embarcadero Freeway, for fear it would collapse.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. The cribbing used to support anything from below or on a side, as with a retaining wall, or to prop up a piece of heavy machinery.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. A self-injurious tendency of certain horses to swallow air while slobbering and biting onto objects in and about their enclosure; cribbing and windsucking are regarded as equine forms of obsessive-compulsive disorder.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; ethology

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** equestrianism; hobbies; horses; lifestyle; pets; sports
