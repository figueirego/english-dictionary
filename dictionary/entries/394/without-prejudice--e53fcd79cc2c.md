### Senses

   **Example:** without bias or prejudice

   **Usage:** formal

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Without affecting a legal interest.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Without binding future decisions or requiring a certain position to be taken in the future; (law) used to allow a statement or proposal in communications, while allowing the party to return to their original position without being impeded by the described statement or proposal.

   **Example:** The court granted temporary custody to the mother, without prejudice as to the father's right to seek permanent custody at a later hearing.

   **Example:** When attempting to settle a lawsuit, the parties generally communicate on a "without prejudice" basis.
