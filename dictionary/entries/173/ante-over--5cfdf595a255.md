**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A North American children's game of throwing a ball (or other small object) over a building (or other barrier) for the opposing team to catch. If they catch it, they run around the barrier and attempt to tag opposing players to bring them onto their own team; otherwise, they throw it back in the same way.
