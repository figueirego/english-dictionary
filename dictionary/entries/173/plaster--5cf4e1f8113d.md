### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To cover or coat something with plaster; to render.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to plaster a wall

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To apply a plaster to.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to plaster a wound

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To smear with some viscous or liquid substance.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Her face was plastered with mud.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To hide or cover up, as if with plaster; to cover thickly.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The radio station plastered the buses and trains with its advertisement.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To cover a surface extensively with something, especially posters, pictures, or notices.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The workmen plastered the wall with pictures.

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To bombard heavily or overwhelmingly; to overwhelm (with weapons fire).

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive
