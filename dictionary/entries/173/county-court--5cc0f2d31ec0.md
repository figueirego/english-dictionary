## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A civil court which deals with civil cases, such as divorce.

   **Usage:** UK; except Scotland

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A court which handles both civil and criminal cases.

   **Usage:** US

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An intermediate court; a district court.

   **Usage:** Australia; specifically; Victoria
