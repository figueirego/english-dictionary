**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To endure, tolerate, suffer through, or allow, especially something annoying.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; informal

   **Example:** I put up with a lot of nonsense, but this is too much.

   **Source synonyms:** abear; abide; aby (British; dialectal); accept; allow; bear; bide (dialectal); brook; dree (British; dialectal); endure; forbear; go along with; live with; pass; pocket; pouch; put up with; stand; stick; stomach; suffer; swallow; take; take sitting down (idiomatic); thole (British; dialectal); tolerate; undergang (British; dialectal); undergo
