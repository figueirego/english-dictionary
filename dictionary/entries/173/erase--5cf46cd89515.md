### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To remove (markings or information).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I erased that word from the page because it was wrong.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To obliterate information from (a storage medium), such as to clear or (with magnetic storage) to demagnetize.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I'm going to erase this tape.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To obliterate (information) from a storage medium, such as to clear or to overwrite.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I'm going to erase those files.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To remove a runner from the bases via a double play or pick off play

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Jones was erased by a 6-4-3 double play.

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To be erased (have markings removed, have information removed, or be cleared of information).

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The chalkboard erased easily.

   **Example:** The files will erase quickly.
