**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The son of Zeus and Leto (or Jupiter and Latona), and the twin brother of Artemis (or Diana). He was the god of light, music, medicine, and poetry; and prophecy, dance, manly beauty, and more.

   **Usage:** Greek; Roman

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; mysticism; mythology; philosophy; sciences

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astronomy; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Short for 1862 Apollo, an Apollo asteroid.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astronomy; natural-sciences

   **Alternative form of:** 1862 Apollo

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A United States space program, and the vehicles it created, used for human travel to the moon.

   **Usage:** US space program

   **Example:** Apollo 11 landed people on the moon for the first time.

**Source sense R1.S5**
