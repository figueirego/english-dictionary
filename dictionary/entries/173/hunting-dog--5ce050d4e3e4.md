### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any type of domestic dog that is used by humans as an aid in hunting; any individual of such a breed, especially one that does hunting work, but also any pet of such a breed.

   **Example:** Cookie lives in the suburbs and is seldom off her leash, but as a hunting dog, she's got that natural predisposition to get into the underbrush!

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Ellipsis of African hunting dog, a wild dog named for its hunting prowess; like all wild dogs, it hunts prey.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; ellipsis

   **Alternative form of:** [African hunting dog](../3d8/african-hunting-dog--f6228b444fa9.md)
