### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A weekday, often a Monday, on which most businesses are closed, and granted to workers as a national holiday.

   **Usage:** Ireland; UK

   **Example:** On a bank holiday weekend.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A closure of banks in a jurisdiction to curtail or prevent a bank run.

   **Usage:** euphemistic
