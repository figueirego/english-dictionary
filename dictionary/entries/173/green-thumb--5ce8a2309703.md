### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A natural skill for gardening.

   **Usage:** US; idiomatic

   **Example:** Wow, did you plant this entire garden on your own? You really must have a green thumb.

   **Source synonyms:** green fingers

   **Source antonyms:** black thumb; brown thumb

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person with this skill.

   **Usage:** US

   **Example:** I should ask the green thumb next door what she recommends for my droopy daffodils.
