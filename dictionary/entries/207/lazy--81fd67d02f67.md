### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Unwilling to do work or make an effort; disinclined to exertion.

   **Example:** Get out of bed, you lazy lout!

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Causing or characterised by idleness; relaxed or leisurely.

   **Example:** I love staying inside and reading on a lazy Sunday.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Showing a lack of effort or care.

   **Example:** lazy writing

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Sluggish; slow-moving.

   **Example:** We strolled along beside a lazy stream.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Lax&#58;
   1. Droopy.

      **Example:** a lazy-eared rabbit

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Lax&#58;
   1. Of an eye, squinting because of a weakness of the eye muscles.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Turned so that (the letter) is horizontal instead of vertical.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a cattle brand

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Employing lazy evaluation; not calculating results until they are immediately required.

   **Example:** a lazy algorithm

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; computing-theory; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

   **Usage:** UK; dialectal; obsolete
