### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A day on which a religious event or secular celebration is traditionally observed.

   **Example:** Today is a Wiccan holiday!

   **Source synonyms:** holy day; feast day; festival

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A day declared free from work by the state or government.

   **Source synonyms:** bank holiday (UK); national holiday; day off; off day

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A period of one or more days taken off work for leisure and often travel; often plural.

   **Usage:** Australia; UK

   **Example:** How much holiday are you allowed?

   **Source synonyms:** leave; time off; vacation (US); annual leave; break; day off; getaway; holiday (UK); leave; leave of absence; pause; recess; time away (idiomatic); time off (idiomatic); vac; vacation (Canada; US); vaca; vacay (US)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A period during which pupils do not attend their school; often plural; rarely used for students at university (usually&#58; vacation).

   **Usage:** Australia; UK

   **Example:** I want to take a French course this summer holiday.

   **Source synonyms:** vacation (US)

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A period during which, by agreement, the usual payments are not made.

   **Example:** a mortgage payment holiday

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A gap in coverage, e.g. of paint on a surface, or sonar imagery.

   **Source synonyms:** lacuna
