### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The situation where a minor league player comes up to the major league team for a short period of time.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Example:** I was up for a cup of coffee once; those were the best six days of my career.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; baseball; games; hobbies; ice-hockey; lifestyle; skating; sports

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One’s personal preference.

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An invitation to have sex.

   **Usage:** euphemistic

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. How cheap something is.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** This Wi-Fi jammer costs a cup of coffee.
