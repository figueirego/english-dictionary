### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To thrust into liquid, or into any penetrable substance; to immerse.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to plunge the body into water

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To cast, stab or throw deep and fast into some thing, state, condition or action.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** to plunge a dagger into the breast

   **Example:** He flicked a switch and the room was plunged into darkness.

   **Example:** to plunge a nation into war

   **Example:** the city was plunged into darkness

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To baptize by immersion.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To dive, leap or rush (into water or some liquid); to submerge oneself.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** he plunged into the river

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To fall or rush headlong into some thing, action, state or condition.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

   **Example:** to plunge into debt

   **Example:** to plunge into controversy

   **Example:** profits plunge 90%

   **Source synonyms:** slump

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To pitch or throw oneself headlong or violently forward, as a horse does.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To bet heavily and recklessly; to risk large sums in gambling.

   **Usage:** intransitive; slang

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To entangle or embarrass (mostly used in past participle).

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To overwhelm, overpower.

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete
