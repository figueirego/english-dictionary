### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To become very noisy and excited.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** Brazil scored, and the crowd went wild!

   **Source antonyms:** go mild

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To go ahead; do as one pleases (used to grant permission for or to give endorsement of a suggestion or proposal, especially when the speaker is not interested in the outcome of the proposal).

   **Usage:** idiomatic; imperative

   **Source synonyms:** go nuts; knock oneself out

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To land or pass wildly off-target.

   **Example:** The policeman's first two shots went wild before he took down the gunman with his third.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a ranged weapon shot
