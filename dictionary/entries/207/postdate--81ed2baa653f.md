### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To occur after an event or time; to exist later on in time.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To assign an effective date to a document or action later than the actual date.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** no postdated checks \[sign in a retail store\]

   **Example:** to postdate a contract, that is, to date it later than the time when it was in fact made

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To affix a date to after the event.

   **Usage:** transitive
