## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

- quiet coyotes — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A hand gesture used in classrooms to get students' attention, where a person forms a coyote with their hand by placing their thumb and two middle fingers together, while raising their pointer and little fingers like ears; a non-verbal signal for students to stop talking, close their mouths, and listen to the teacher.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Source synonyms:** silent fox

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To make or flash the quiet coyote hand gesture to request silence.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** The teacher quiet coyoted the class.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To fall silent, adopt the pose, or comply with the quiet coyote gesture.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** The kids quiet coyoted when the bell rang.
