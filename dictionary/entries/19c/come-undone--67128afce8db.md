### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see come, undone; to become undone or unfastened.

   **Example:** My shoelace came undone.

   **Example:** Your bandage is coming undone.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To become disintegrated, to break into parts or pieces.

   **Usage:** colloquial

   **Source synonyms:** come apart; fall apart

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To come to ruin, to fail; to lose one's composure or self-control.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** He came undone when his wife refused to support him.

   **Source synonyms:** come apart
