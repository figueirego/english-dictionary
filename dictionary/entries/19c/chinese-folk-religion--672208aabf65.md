### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The diverse array of indigenous religious practices among Han Chinese communities, encompassing the veneration of ancestors, spirits, deities, and immortals, often syncretized with elements of Buddhism, Confucianism, and Taoism.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** Shenism
