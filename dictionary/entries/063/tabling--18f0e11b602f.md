## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A forming into (graphic) tables; a display in tabular form.

   **Source synonyms:** tabulation

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The letting of one timber into another by alternate scores or projections, as in shipbuilding; a crude form of dovetailing.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; carpentry; construction; manufacturing

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A broad hem on the edge of a sail.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A setting-aside of a topic from discussion.

   **Usage:** US

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The introduction of a topic to a discussion.

   **Usage:** UK

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Representing a company or organization (at an exposition, fair, etc.), usually at a booth or display.

   **Usage:** metonymically

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

   **Usage:** obsolete
