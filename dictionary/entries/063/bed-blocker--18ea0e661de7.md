### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An elderly hospitalized person who is too infirm to return home but not sufficiently ill to necessitate continued hospitalization, creating a situation in which his or her hospital stay is prolonged while authorities or relatives search for a suitable placement amid the scarce resources of nursing homes or other long-term care facilities.

   **Usage:** UK; derogatory; idiomatic; public policy
