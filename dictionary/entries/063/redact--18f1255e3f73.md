### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To censor, to black out or remove parts of a document while leaving the remainder.

   **Usage:** transitive; usually

   **Example:** The military will redact the document before releasing it, blacking out sections that are classified.

   **Example:** The names and email addresses of the users were redacted from the public data.

   **Example:** By the time the Department of Defense is done redacting, there won't be much left to read.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To black out legally protected sections of text in a document provided to opposing counsel, typically as part of the discovery process.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To reduce to form, as literary matter; to digest and put in shape (matter for publication); to edit.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To draw up or frame a decree, statement, etc.

   **Usage:** rare

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To bring together in one unit; to combine or bring together into one.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To gather or organize works or ideas into a unified whole; to collect, order, or write in a written document or to put into a particular written form.

   **Usage:** obsolete

   **Source synonyms:** collate

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To insert or assimilate into a written system or scheme.

   **Usage:** obsolete; rare

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To bring an area of study within the comprehension capacity of a person.

   **Usage:** obsolete; rare

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To reduce to a particular condition or state, especially one that is undesirable.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

   **Usage:** obsolete
