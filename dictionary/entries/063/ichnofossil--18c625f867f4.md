### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A trace fossil; a fossil record that preserves some activity of an organism, but no part of the organism itself.

   **Example:** For example, an ichnofossil might be the preserved track an animal, or its burrow.
