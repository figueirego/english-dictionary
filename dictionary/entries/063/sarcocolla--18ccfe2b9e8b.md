### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A shrub of species Astragalus sarcocolla, native to Iran.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A shrub of species Penaea sarcocolla, native to South Africa.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Gum resin obtained from them, (historical) particularly A. sarcolla resin as used in traditional medicine and paint manufacture.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Similar resins produced from similar species of shrubs, particularly Penaea mucronata and Astragalus fasciculifolius.

   **Usage:** uncountable; inexact
