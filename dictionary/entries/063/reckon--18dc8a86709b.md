### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To count; to enumerate; to number; also, to compute; to calculate.

   **Usage:** Commonwealth

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To count as in a number, rank, or series; to estimate by rank or quality; to place by estimation; to account; to esteem; to repute.

   **Usage:** Commonwealth

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To charge, attribute, or adjudge to one, as having a certain quality or value.

   **Usage:** Commonwealth

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To conclude, as by an enumeration and balancing of chances; hence, to think; to suppose; -- followed by an objective clause

   **Usage:** Commonwealth; colloquial

   **Example:** I reckon he won't try that again.

   **Example:** I’ve just heard from the repairman&#58; it's going to cost £1000. I'd reckoned it’d be £500 at most.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To reckon with something or somebody or not, i.e. to reckon without something or somebody&#58; to take into account, deal with, consider or not, i.e. to misjudge, ignore, not take into account, not deal with, not consider or fail to consider; e.g. reckon without one's host

   **Usage:** Commonwealth

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To make an enumeration or computation; to engage in numbering or computing.

   **Usage:** Commonwealth; intransitive

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To come to an accounting; to draw up or settle accounts; to examine and strike the balance of debt and credit; to adjust relations of desert or penalty.

   **Usage:** Commonwealth
