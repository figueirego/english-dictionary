### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The atmospheric pressure denoted on a barometer.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The downward force of the atmosphere onto the Earth; at sea level a unit of air pressure is one atmosphere, air pressure decreases as the altitude increases and is measured in millibars or pascals.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** climatology; meteorology; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics
