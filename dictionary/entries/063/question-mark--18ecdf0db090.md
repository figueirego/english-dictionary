### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The punctuation mark "?", used at the end of a sentence to indicate a question.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** media; publishing; typography

   **Source synonyms:** interrogation mark; interrogation point; query; eroteme; interrogative; interrogative-point; note of interrogation; mark of interrogation

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A state of doubt or uncertainty.

   **Usage:** figuratively; informal

   **Example:** There’s a question mark over whether or not he’ll be fit for the next game.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An enigmatic, inscrutable, or mysterious person or thing; an enigma, a riddle.

   **Usage:** figuratively; informal

   **Source synonyms:** mystery; puzzle

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Polygonia interrogationis, a North American nymphalid butterfly with a silver mark on the underside of its hindwing resembling a question mark (sense 1).
