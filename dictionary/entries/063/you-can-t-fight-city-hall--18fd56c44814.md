### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Nothing can be done to change the situation, because it is a governmental decision or a similarly institutional decision (e.g., high-level corporate, senior military brass, dominant cultural norm).

   **Usage:** US; figuratively; literal

   **Example:** I see they're going to build the airport after all. I suppose you can't fight City Hall.
