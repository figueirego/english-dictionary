### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To find or search for an animal in the wild with the intention of killing the animal for its meat or for sport.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** State Wildlife Management areas often offer licensed hunters the opportunity to hunt on public lands.

   **Example:** Her uncle will go out and hunt for deer, now that it is open season.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To try to find something; search (for).

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** The little girl was hunting for shells on the beach.

   **Example:** The police are hunting for evidence.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To drive; to chase; with down, from, away, etc.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to hunt down a criminal

   **Example:** He was hunted from the parish.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To use or manage (dogs, horses, etc.) in hunting.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Did you hunt that pony last week?

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To use or traverse in pursuit of game.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He hunts the woods, or the country.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To move or shift the order of (a bell) in a regular course of changes.

   **Usage:** transitive; bell-ringing

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To shift up and down in order regularly.

   **Usage:** intransitive; bell-ringing

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To be in a state of instability of movement or forced oscillation, as a governor which has a large movement of the balls for small change of load, an arc-lamp clutch mechanism which moves rapidly up and down with variations of current, etc.; also, to seesaw, as a pair of alternators working in parallel.

   **Usage:** intransitive
