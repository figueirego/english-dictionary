### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Behaviour adopted to demonstrate one's superiority; pretentious or snobbish behaviour; airs and graces.

   **Usage:** archaic; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** affectations; airs; huffiness; pretensions

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Flighty, giddy, or silly behaviour; also, noisy merriment.

   **Usage:** obsolete; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** flightiness; giddiness; silliness; frolic; high jinks; romping

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A young woman regarded as flighty, giddy, or silly.

   **Usage:** UK; countable; dialectal
