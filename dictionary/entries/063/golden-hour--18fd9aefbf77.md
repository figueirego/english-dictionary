### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The first sixty minutes after sustaining major traumatic injury, during which there is the highest likelihood that prompt medical treatment will prevent death.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** golden time

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A period shortly after sunrise or before sunset, during which daylight is redder and softer than when the Sun is higher in the sky.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** arts; hobbies; lifestyle; photography

   **Source synonyms:** magic hour

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. In Japanese television, the period between 7 and 9 pm, the equivalent of the American prime time.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** broadcasting; media; television

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Hours worked outside of normal working hours, for which additional money is paid.

   **Usage:** in-plural
