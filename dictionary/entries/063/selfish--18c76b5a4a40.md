### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Holding one's own self-interest as the standard for decision making.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Having excessive or exclusive regard and concern for one's own well-being, profit or pleasure, with little or no consideration for others.

   **Source synonyms:** egoistic; egotistic; egotistical; self-centered; greedy

   **Source antonyms:** altruistic; otherish; philanthropic; selfless; unselfish; abstemious; otherish; selfless; self-abnegatory; self-denying; self-flagellatory; self-sacrificing; unselfish

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of video game characters&#58; relying on their own actions and capabilities to be effective in the game, rather than on other characters.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** video-games

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Related to or equivalent to a selfish gene; best understood as existing to preserve and replicate itself in competition with other entities at the same level of existence, rather than at the level of the individual or population containing it.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences
