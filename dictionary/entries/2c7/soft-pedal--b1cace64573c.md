### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To reduce the volume of (music, a sound, etc.).

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To reduce the force or impact of (something); to damp, to mute; especially, to minimize the less desirable aspects of (something); to play down, to tone down.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** de-emphasize; downplay

   **Source antonyms:** emphasize; highlight; spotlight

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To attempt to persuade someone about (something) through understatement, so that the listener accepts the good points as obvious.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Chiefly followed by on&#58; to act in a less assertive or forceful manner.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive
