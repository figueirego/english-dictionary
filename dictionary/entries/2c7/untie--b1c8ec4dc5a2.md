### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To loosen, as something interlaced or knotted; to disengage the parts of.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to untie a knot

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To free from fastening or from restraint; to let loose; to unbind.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To resolve; to unfold; to clear.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To become untied or loosed.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. In the Perl programming language, to undo the process of tying, so that a variable uses default instead of custom functionality.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; programming; sciences
