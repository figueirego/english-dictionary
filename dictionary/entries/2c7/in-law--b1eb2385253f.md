### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A relative by marriage (or through affinity).

   **Usage:** in-plural; often

   **Example:** The house got a complete going-over before the arrival of the in-laws.

   **Source synonyms:** affine

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Sometimes specifically a parent-in-law of one's child, for which the dedicated terms co-mother-in-law, co-father-in-law are rare.
