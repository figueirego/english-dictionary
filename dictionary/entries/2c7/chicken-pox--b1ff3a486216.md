### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The blisters formed by the varicella zoster virus (VZV).

   **Usage:** archaic; uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; pathology; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** chicken pock

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The highly contagious airborne disease caused by initial infection with varicella zoster virus, typically causing a skin rash that forms small itchy blisters.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; pathology; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** varicella

<a id="r1-s3"></a>
