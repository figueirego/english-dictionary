## Noun

### Forms

- blood brothers — plural
- blood brethren — obsolete; plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An unrelated male considered to share a brother-like relationship established by a ceremonial sharing of blood in some cultures.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An unrelated male considered to share such a strong relationship generally.

   **Usage:** broadly

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A brother by blood&#58; a brother-german or half-brother, as distinguished from step-brothers.
