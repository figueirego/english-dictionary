### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To trust; to have confidence in.

   **Usage:** intransitive; with on or upon; formerly also with in; with on or upon; formerly also with in

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To depend; to be dependent upon.

   **Usage:** intransitive; with on or upon

   **Example:** The city relies on the subway system.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To be intellectually contingent from; to stem from.

   **Usage:** intransitive; with on or upon

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To fasten, fix, attach

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

   **Example:** Therefore \[they\] must needs relye their faithe upon the sillie Ministers faithlesse fidelitie.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To bring together again; to rally.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

   **Example:** Petrius, that was a noble knyght, and bolde and hardy, relied his peple a-boute hym,
