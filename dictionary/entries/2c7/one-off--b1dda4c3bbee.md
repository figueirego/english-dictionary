## Noun · Record 2

### Forms

- one-offs — plural

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Something that is done, created, etc. only once, and often quickly, simply, or improvisationally.

   **Usage:** Commonwealth; idiomatic

   **Example:** I'll put together a quick one-off as a sample so we can taste the recipe.

   **Source synonyms:** stand-alone

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Something unique and remarkable.

   **Example:** It is a one-off; there is nothing else like it.
