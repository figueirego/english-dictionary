### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Clothes for wearing to bed and sleeping in, usually consisting of a loose-fitting shirt and pants/trousers.

   **Usage:** plural; plural-only

   **Example:** She put on a coat over her new pair of pajamas.
