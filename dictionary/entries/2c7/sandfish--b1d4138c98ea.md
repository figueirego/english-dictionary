### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any member of the genus Gonorynchus (also called beaked sandfishes), long, thin ray-finned fishes (family Gonorychidae)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of several marine fishes that burrow into sandy seabeds
   1. in Trichodontidae, a family of perch-like fish

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of several marine fishes that burrow into sandy seabeds
   1. of species Awaous tajasica, usually sand fish, a species of goby fish (family Gobiidae)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any of several marine fishes that burrow into sandy seabeds
   1. of species Malacanthus plumieri (sand tilefish, family Malacanthidae)

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Any of several marine fishes that burrow into sandy seabeds
   1. of species Diplectrum formosum (sand perch, family Serranidae)

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Other animals that notably burrow in sand&#58;
   1. sandfish skink (Scincus scincus), a land-dwelling lizard that swims in sand

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Other animals that notably burrow in sand&#58;
   1. a sea cucumber of species Holothuria scabra or Thelenota ananas.
