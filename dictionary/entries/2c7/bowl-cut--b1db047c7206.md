### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A haircut featuring a straight fringe across the front and the hair of even length around the rest of the head, resembling the shape created by trimming around an inverted bowl placed on the head.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** arts; crafts; hairdressing; hobbies; lifestyle
