### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A blood test that uses kaolin as an activator and cephalin to facilitate coagulation in a plasma sample, historically used to measure the amount of time it takes for a clot to form.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hematology; medicine; sciences
