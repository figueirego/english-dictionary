### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Fabric turned up at the bottom of trousers to make them shorter.

   **Example:** Why does all this fluff accumulate in the turn-ups?

   **Source synonyms:** cuff; trouser cuff

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A fight or disturbance.

   **Usage:** dated

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The next card taken from the top of a pack of cards and displayed.

   **Example:** I'll bet $10 that the next turn-up is an ace.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** card-games; games

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A stroke of good luck; something that appears unexpectedly; especially in turn up for the book.

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**
