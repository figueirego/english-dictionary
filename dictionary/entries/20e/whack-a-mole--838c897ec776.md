### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A situation where problems or tasks keep reappearing repeatedly.

   **Usage:** Canada; US; countable; idiomatic; uncountable

   **Example:** Trying to get rid of spam e-mails is like whack-a-mole&#58; as soon as you delete one, another appears.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A strategy of addressing recurrent, unpredictable problems in a piecemeal, temporary way at their point of emergence.

   **Usage:** Canada; US; countable; idiomatic; uncountable
