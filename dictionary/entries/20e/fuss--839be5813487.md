## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /fʌs/ — source labels: General-American; Received-Pronunciation
- IPA: /fʊs/ — source labels: Northern-England

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Excessive activity, worry, bother, or talk about something.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** They made a big fuss about the wedding plans.

   **Example:** What's all the fuss about?

   **Source synonyms:** hubbub; to-do; ado; argy-bargy (UK); bangarang; bobbery (dated); brouhaha; bunfight; bustle; bust-up; chaos; ferment; clamour; clatter; commotion; disruption; disturbance; flap; flutter; foofaraw; fracas; furor; fuss; hobbleshaw (obsolete); hoo-ha; hoo-hah; hubbub; hullabaloo; hurly-burly; kerfuffle; mayhem; merry hell; outcry; palaver; pandemonium; pother; quarrel; riot; rowdydow (archaic); ruckus; rumpus; shemozzle; shindy (slang); splatterdash; stir; stooshie (Scotland); three-ring circus; to-do; tomfoolery; trouble; tumult; turmoil; unrest; uproar; upstir

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A complaint or noise; a scene.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** If you make enough of a fuss about the problem, maybe they'll fix it for you.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An exhibition of affection or admiration.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** They made a great fuss over the new baby.

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To be very worried or excited about something, often too much.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** His grandmother will never quit fussing over his vegetarianism.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To fiddle; fidget; wiggle, or adjust

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Quit fussing with your hair. It looks fine.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To disturb (a person)

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To cry or be ill-humoured.

   **Usage:** US; especially; intransitive

   **Source context (verbatim):** of babies

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To show affection for, especially animals.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source context (verbatim):** with over

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

   **Usage:** transitive
