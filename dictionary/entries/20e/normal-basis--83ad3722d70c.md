### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. For a given Galois field 𝔽\_(qᵐ) and a suitable element β, a basis that has the form \{β, β^q, β^(q2), ... , β^(qm-1)\}.

   **Usage:** field theory

   **Example:** A normal basis is generated by the repeated action of the Frobenius endomorphism on a suitable element β; it is the orbit of β for that endomorphism.
