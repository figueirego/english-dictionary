### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To attack someone with great force.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** At dawn we really let 'em have it with a 30 minute artillery barrage.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To verbally assail someone.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** When I came home, he let me have it for wrecking the car.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see let, have, it.

   **Example:** When he showed me that he'd gotten the insurance certificate for our old car, I decided to let him have it.
