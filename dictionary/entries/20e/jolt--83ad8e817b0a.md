### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To push or shake abruptly and roughly.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The bus jolted its passengers at every turn.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To knock sharply

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To shock (someone) into taking action or being alert

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I jolted her out of complacency.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To shock emotionally.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Her untimely death jolted us all.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To shake; to move with a series of jerks.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The car jolted along the stony path.
