### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The end or final point of something.

   **Example:** The river reached its terminus at the wide delta near the sea.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The end point of a transportation system, or the town or city in which it is located.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A boundary or border, or a post or stone marking such a boundary.

   **Usage:** obsolete

   **Example:** A bronz statue marked the terminus of the king's dominion.
