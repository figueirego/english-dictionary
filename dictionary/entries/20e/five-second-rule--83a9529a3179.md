### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any rule that allows or prohibits certain behavior for five seconds after some event, especially&#58;
   1. The belief that food dropped on the ground is still hygienic to eat provided it is picked up within five seconds of touching the ground.

      **Usage:** informal

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any rule that allows or prohibits certain behavior for five seconds after some event, especially&#58;
   1. A rule that a closely-guarded player cannot hold the ball for more than five seconds without dribbling, passing, rolling, or shooting the ball.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; basketball; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any rule that allows or prohibits certain behavior for five seconds after some event, especially&#58;
   1. A rule that a player who throws in the ball from out of bounds must get rid of it in less than five seconds.
