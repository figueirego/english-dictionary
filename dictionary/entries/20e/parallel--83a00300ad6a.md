### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Equally distant from one another at all points.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The horizontal lines on my notebook paper are parallel.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Having the same overall direction; the comparison is indicated with "to".

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The two railway lines are parallel.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Either not intersecting, or coinciding.

   **Usage:** excessive; not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences

   **Source context (verbatim):** said of a pair of lines

   **Source antonyms:** perpendicular; skew

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Involving the processing of multiple tasks at the same time.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** a parallel algorithm

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

   **Source antonyms:** serial; sequential

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Analogous, similar, comparable.

   **Usage:** figuratively; not-comparable

   **Example:** the parallel lives of two citizens

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Coexisting but normally not interacting with the regular reality.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; usually; dimensions

   **Example:** parallel universe

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** literature; media; publishing; science-fiction

   **Source context (verbatim):** of realities

### Related words

**Source antonyms:** antiparallel; perpendicular; skew

**Source sense R3.S1**

<a id="r3-s2"></a>

**Source sense R3.S2**

2. Direction conformable to that of another line.

<a id="r3-s3"></a>

**Source sense R3.S3**

3. A line of latitude.

   **Example:** The 31st parallel passes through the center of my town.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; natural-sciences

<a id="r3-s4"></a>

**Source sense R3.S4**

<a id="r3-s5"></a>

**Source sense R3.S5**

5. Something identical or similar in essential respects.

<a id="r3-s6"></a>

**Source sense R3.S6**

6. A comparison made; elaborate tracing of similarity.

   **Example:** Johnson's parallel between Dryden and Pope

<a id="r3-s7"></a>

**Source sense R3.S7**

7. One of a series of long trenches constructed before a besieged fortress, by the besieging force, as a cover for troops supporting the attacking batteries. They are roughly parallel to the line of outer defenses of the fortress.

**Source sense R3.S8**
