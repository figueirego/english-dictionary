## Verb · Etymology 2 · Record 4

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈɔː.ɹɪˌɛnt/ — source labels: Received-Pronunciation
- IPA: /ˈɒɹ.ɪˌɛnt/ — source labels: Received-Pronunciation

### Forms

- orients — present; singular; third-person
- orienting — participle; present
- oriented — participle; past
- oriented — past

### Senses

<a id="r4-s1"></a>

**Source sense R4.S1**

1. To build or place (something) so as to face eastward.

   **Usage:** US; often; transitive

<a id="r4-s2"></a>

**Source sense R4.S2**

2. To align or place (a person or object) so that his, her, or its east side, north side, etc., is positioned toward the corresponding points of the compass; (specifically, surveying) to rotate (a map attached to a plane table) until the line of direction between any two of its points is parallel to the corresponding direction in nature.

   **Usage:** US; broadly; often; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** orientate

<a id="r4-s3"></a>

**Source sense R4.S3**

3. To direct towards or point at a particular direction.

   **Usage:** US; often; transitive

   **Example:** The workers oriented all the signs to face the road.

   **Source synonyms:** orientate

<a id="r4-s4"></a>

**Source sense R4.S4**

4. To determine which direction one is facing.

   **Usage:** US; often; reflexive; transitive

   **Example:** Let me just orient myself and we can be on our way.

<a id="r4-s5"></a>

**Source sense R4.S5**

5. To familiarize (oneself or someone) with a circumstance or situation.

   **Usage:** US; figuratively; often; reflexive; transitive

   **Example:** Give him time to orient himself within the new hierarchy.

   **Source synonyms:** orientate

   **Source antonyms:** disorient; disorientate

<a id="r4-s6"></a>

**Source sense R4.S6**

6. To set the focus of (something) so as to appeal or relate to a certain group.

   **Usage:** US; figuratively; often; transitive

   **Example:** We will orient our campaign to the youth who are often disinterested.

<a id="r4-s7"></a>

**Source sense R4.S7**

7. To change direction to face a certain way.

   **Usage:** US; intransitive; often
