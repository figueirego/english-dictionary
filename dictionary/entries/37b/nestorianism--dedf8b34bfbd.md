### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The Christological doctrine ascribed to Nestorius, condemned as heretical by the Council of Ephesus but later also ascribed to the Church of the East, holding that within Christ there are two distinct hypostases, divine and human.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The beliefs and practices of the Church of the East.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; derogatory; sometimes; uncountable
