### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having pronounced muscle development through weightlifting, bodybuilding, or other physical exercise.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Having excessive muscle (sometimes with implications of correspondingly inferior intellect).

   **Usage:** derogatory
