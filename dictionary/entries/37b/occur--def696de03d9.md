### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To happen or take place.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The liftoff will occur in exactly twelve seconds.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To present or offer itself.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** I will write if the opportunity occurs.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To come or be presented to the mind; to suggest itself.

   **Usage:** impersonal

   **Example:** One little incident of my school days occurs to me as amusing.

   **Example:** It occurred to him to tell me of the problem.

   **Source synonyms:** hit

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To be present or found.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The chemical monofluoroacetate occurs in all parts of Dichapetalum cymosum, and is responsible for its toxic effects.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** sciences
