### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An area that is for practical purposes an exclave, but does not meet the strict definition&#58; an area that is not completely separated from the main part, but can only in practice be reached by passing through another area. For instance, a region of a country that is not connected by road to the main part of the country without passing through another country.
