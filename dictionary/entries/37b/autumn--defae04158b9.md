### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Traditionally the third of the four seasons, when deciduous trees lose their leaves, and temperatures and daylight hours decrease; typically regarded as spanning the months of September, October, and November in the Northern Hemisphere, and the months of March, April and May in the Southern Hemisphere.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** autumn leaves

   **Source synonyms:** fall; harvest; back end

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The time period when someone or something is past its prime.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** decline

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A person with relatively dark hair and a warm skin tone, seen as best suited to certain colours in clothing.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
