### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any metal at the lower end of the electrochemical series that oxidizes readily.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; electrochemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences
