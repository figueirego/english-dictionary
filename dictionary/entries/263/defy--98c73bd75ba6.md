### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To challenge (someone) or brave (a hazard or opposition).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to defy an enemy; to defy the power of a magistrate; to defy the arguments of an opponent; to defy public opinion

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To refuse to obey.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** If you defy your teacher you may end up in detention.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To not conform to or follow a pattern, set of rules or expectations.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To renounce or dissolve all bonds of affiance, faith, or obligation with; to reject, refuse, or renounce.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive
