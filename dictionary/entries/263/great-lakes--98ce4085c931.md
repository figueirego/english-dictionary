### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A group of five lakes on the United States–Canada border, consisting of Lake Superior, Lake Michigan and Lake Huron (Lake Michigan–Huron), Lake Erie and Lake Ontario.

   **Usage:** plural-normally

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A settlement in the city of North Chicago, Lake County, Illinois, around Great Lakes station, named after the Naval Station Great Lakes.

   **Usage:** plural-normally

   **Source context (verbatim):** without "the"

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
