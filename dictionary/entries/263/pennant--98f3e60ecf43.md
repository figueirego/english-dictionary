### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A flag normally used by naval vessels to represent a special condition.
   1. The broad pennant flown by commodores.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A flag normally used by naval vessels to represent a special condition.
   1. The church pennant indicating religious services are taking place aboard ship.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A flag normally used by naval vessels to represent a special condition.
   1. The commissioning pennant flown on ceremonial occasions.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A small flag with pointed end, formerly carried by cavalry or other mounted troops to serve as a rallying point or ceremonial unit identification.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The winning of a competition, represented by a flag.

   **Example:** The New York Yankees have won the American League pennant far more often than any other team.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

<a id="r1-s8"></a>
