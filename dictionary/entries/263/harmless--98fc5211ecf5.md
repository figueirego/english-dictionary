### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Incapable of causing harm or danger; safe.

   **Example:** This snake looks nearly the same as its poisonous cousin but is harmless.

   **Source synonyms:** unharmful; nonharmful; benign; innocuous; innocent; undamaging; nonthreatening; unthreatening

   **Source antonyms:** harmful; deleterious; damaging; threatening

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Not intended to harm; inoffensive.

   **Example:** His humor can be annoying but is harmless.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Unharmed.

   **Usage:** obsolete

   **Source synonyms:** unscathed
