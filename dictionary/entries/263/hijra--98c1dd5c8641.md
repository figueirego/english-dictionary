**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The flight of Muhammad and his followers from Mecca to Yathrib (later Medina) in 622 ce, which forms the first year of the Islamic era (ah 1).

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. AH&#58; the Islamic era, dated from the Hijra.

   **Example:** Since the Muslim lunar year contains only 354 days, the year 2005 is approximately equivalent to Hijra 1426.
