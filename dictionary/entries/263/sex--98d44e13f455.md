### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A category into which sexually-reproducing organisms are divided on the basis of their reproductive roles in their species; the system of such categories, which can differ by organism or by taxonomic branch.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** The effect of the medication is dependent upon age, sex, and other factors.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Another category, especially of humans and especially based on sexuality or gender roles.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The members of such a category, taken collectively.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The distinction and relation between these categories, especially in humans; gender.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Women; the human female gender and those who belong to it.

   **Usage:** literary; obsolete; uncountable; with-definite-article

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Sexual activity, may be understood to be sexual intercourse unless preceded by a modifier.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Genitalia&#58; a penis or vagina/vulva.

   **Usage:** countable; euphemistic; slang

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Any kind of category, class, or community of people who possess shared characteristics, such as religion or race.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable
