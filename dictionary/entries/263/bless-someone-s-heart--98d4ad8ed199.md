## Phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used to express gratitude. (Compare bless you, God bless you.)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used to express affection.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used to soften criticism or express pity. (Compare the British usage of bless (expression of endearment or belittlement).)

   **Usage:** Southern-US; especially

   **Example:** My dog can't fit in her puppy bed anymore, but she still tries, bless her heart.

   **Example:** They're having a hard time getting used to the food down here. They don't like grits — bless their hearts, they don't even like sweet tea.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Used sarcastically to express contempt.

   **Usage:** Southern-US; especially

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** bless (interjection); bless you — expression of gratitude; bless someone's cotton socks (British) — criticism or pity; God love someone
