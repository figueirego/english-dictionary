## Noun · Record 2

### Forms

- jimps — plural

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A notch on the spine of a knife blade to increase grip, usually in sets of three or more (jimping).

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Such a notch elsewhere on the knife (handle scales, liner lock, choil) for the same purpose (grippiness).
