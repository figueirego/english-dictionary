### Senses

<a id="r3-s1"></a>

**Source sense R3.S1**

1. A marshy or muddy area.

<a id="r3-s2"></a>

**Source sense R3.S2**

2. A type of swamp or shallow lake system, typically formed as or by the backwater of a larger waterway, similar to a bayou with trees.

   **Usage:** Northern-US; Southern-US

   **Example:** We paddled under a canopy of trees through the slough.

<a id="r3-s3"></a>

**Source sense R3.S3**

3. A secondary channel of a river delta, usually flushed by the tide.

   **Usage:** US; Western

   **Example:** The Sacramento River Delta contains dozens of sloughs that are often used for water-skiing and fishing.

<a id="r3-s4"></a>

**Source sense R3.S4**

4. A state of depression.

   **Example:** John is in a slough.

<a id="r3-s5"></a>

**Source sense R3.S5**

5. A small pond, often alkaline, many but not all formed by glacial potholes.

   **Usage:** Canadian-Prairies

   **Example:** Potholes or sloughs formed by a glacier’s retreat from the central plains of North America, are now known to be some of the world’s most productive ecosystems.
