### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make a matron of; to make matron-like.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To act the part of a matron toward; to superintend or chaperone.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to matronize an assembly

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To criticize from a feminist perspective.

   **Usage:** derogatory; sometimes; transitive

   **Example:** to matronize female artists for sexploitation
