### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To calculate.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Can you work out 250 × 12 in your head for me?

   **Example:** Can you work out how to get to the university by car?

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To make sense of.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I can't work these instructions out.

   **Source synonyms:** figure out; sort out

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To decide.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I can't work out if I like him or not.

   **Source synonyms:** determine; judge; choose up (idiomatic); decide; determine; resolve; find; judge; posken (Judaism); make a decision; make up one's mind (idiomatic); take a decision; work out

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To bring about or cause to happen by work or effort.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I think we can work out a compromise.

   **Source synonyms:** hash out; hammer out

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To resolve; to find a solution for.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** They managed to work out their differences.

   **Source synonyms:** puzzle out; reason out; think out; absolve (obsolete); decipher; get to the bottom of (idiomatic); iron out; meliorate; cure; puzzle out; ravel (archaic); reason out; resolve; riddle; sleuth; solve; tease out (figuratively); think out; unpuzzle; unravel (figuratively); unriddle; unsolve (obsolete); upsolve; work out; Any from Thesaurus&#58;untangle (figuratively); straighten out; sort out

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To develop or devise in detail; to elaborate.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to work out a plan

   **Source synonyms:** plan out; lay out

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To smooth or perfect.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** This is a beta version; we're still working out the kinks.

   **Source synonyms:** iron out; smooth out

**Source sense R1.S8**

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** These figures just don't work out.

   **Source synonyms:** add up

9. To succeed; to result in a satisfactory situation.
