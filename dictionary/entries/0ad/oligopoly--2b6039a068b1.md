**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An economic condition in which a small number of sellers exert control over the market of a commodity.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Because of the ease of collusion, particularly price fixing, duopolies and oligopolies easily produce outcomes as bad for the general public as outright monopolies.
