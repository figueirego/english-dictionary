### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To begin eating.

   **Usage:** colloquial

   **Example:** I wanted to say grace before dinner, but the kids were already digging in.

   **Source synonyms:** chow down; fall to; tuck in

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To engage in a burst of hard work.

   **Usage:** colloquial

   **Example:** I've got to dig in for a couple of weeks to secure my promotion.

   **Source synonyms:** fall to; go for it

**Source sense R1.S3**

   **Usage:** also; figuratively

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war

**Source sense R1.S4**

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** to dig in one's feet, heels, etc.
