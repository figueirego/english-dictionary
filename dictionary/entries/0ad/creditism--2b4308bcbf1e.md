### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A socio-economic system associated with capitalism but distinguished from it due to wealth being loaned out rather than being invested.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** economics; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A theory that the quantity of credit is a more important financial indicator for central banks to use to govern the economy than the money supply or interest rates.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>
