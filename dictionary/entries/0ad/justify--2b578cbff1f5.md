### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To provide an acceptable explanation for.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** How can you justify spending so much money on clothes?

   **Example:** Paying too much for car insurance is not justified.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To be a good reason behind a normally-unacceptable action; to warrant.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Nothing can justify your rude behaviour last night.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To arrange (text) on a page or a computer screen such that the left and right ends of all lines within paragraphs are aligned.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The text will look better justified.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** media; publishing; typography

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To absolve, and declare to be free of blame or sin.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To give reasons for one’s actions; to make an argument to prove that one is in the right.

   **Usage:** reflexive

   **Example:** She felt no need to justify herself for deciding not to invite him.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To prove; to ratify; to confirm.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To show (a person) to have had a sufficient legal reason for an act that has been made the subject of a charge or accusation.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To qualify (oneself) as a surety by taking oath to the ownership of sufficient property.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law
