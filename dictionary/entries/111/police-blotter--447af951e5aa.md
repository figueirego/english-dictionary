### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A register, maintained by the desk sergeant, of people arrested or brought in for questioning to a police station; (UK) charge sheet.

   **Usage:** US

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A newspaper column publishing highlights therefrom.

   **Usage:** US

   **Example:** We read about his DUI in the police blotter.
