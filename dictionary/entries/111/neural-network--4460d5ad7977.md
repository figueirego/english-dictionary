### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A real or virtual computer system designed to emulate the human brain in its ability to "learn" to assess imprecise data. (Also called an artificial neural network when neural network is defined as including biological ones.)

   **Usage:** machine learning

   **Source synonyms:** neural net

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
