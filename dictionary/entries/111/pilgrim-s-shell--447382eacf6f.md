### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A scallop or cockleshell used as a sign that one had visited the Holy Land or representing the Camino de Santiago (Way of St. James) pilgrimage in Spain, especially of species Pecten jacobaeus or P. maximus.

   **Source synonyms:** pilgrim shell; pilgrim's scallop; Camino de Santiago shell

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A scallop or cockleshell used as a sign that one had visited the Holy Land or representing the Camino de Santiago (Way of St. James) pilgrimage in Spain, especially of species Pecten jacobaeus or P. maximus.
   1. Synonym of scallop.

      **Usage:** broadly

      **Source synonyms:** pilgrim shell; pilgrim's scallop; Camino de Santiago shell; scallop (synonym; synonym-of)
