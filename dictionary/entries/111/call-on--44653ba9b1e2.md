### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To visit (a person); to pay a call to.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** I really should call on my aunt more often.

   **Source synonyms:** pay a visit; visit; wait on

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To select (a student in a classroom, etc.) to provide an answer.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** He sat there, baffled, hoping nobody would call on him.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To request or ask something of (a person); to select for a task.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** The king called on his subjects to take up arms and defend the kingdom.

   **Source synonyms:** call upon

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To have recourse to.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** Exhausted, he called on his last ounce of strength.

   **Example:** The young adventurer set out on the new planet alone, thankful that she could still call on her captain for advice in case of emergency.

   **Source synonyms:** call upon; summon up

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To correct; to point out an error or untruth.

   **Usage:** ditransitive; idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** The salesman persisted in quoting a rate higher than was listed, until we called him on it.

   **Source synonyms:** correct; call out
