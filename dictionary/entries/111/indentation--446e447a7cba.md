### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of indenting or state of being indented.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A notch or recess, in the margin or border of anything.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** the indentations of a leaf

   **Example:** indentations of the coast

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A recess or sharp depression in any surface.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The act of beginning a line or series of lines at a little distance within the flush line of the column or page, as in the common way of beginning the first line of a paragraph.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** media; publishing; typography
