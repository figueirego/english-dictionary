### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act or process of transferring someone or something to another, of sending by reference, or referring.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The insurance company insists I get a referral from my regular doctor. I can't just go to the specialist; a GP has got to refer me.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A document used by schools detailing some form of a student's misbehavior and listing the actions taken before and after the student's receipt of the referral.

   **Usage:** countable; slang; uncountable

   **Example:** After misbehaving in class, George was given a referral for disrupting class and sent to the office.
