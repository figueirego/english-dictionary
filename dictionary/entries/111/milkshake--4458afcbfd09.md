### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A thick beverage consisting of milk and ice cream mixed together, often with fruit, chocolate, or other flavoring.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A thin beverage, similar to the above, but with no ice cream or significantly less of it.

   **Usage:** Australia; New-England; New-Zealand

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A beverage consisting of fruit juice, water, and some milk, as served in Southeast Asia.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Accidental emulsion of oil and water in an engine.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** This milkshake under the oil cap, or on the dipstick, indicates a blown head gasket.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; mechanical-engineering; mechanics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An alkaline supplement administered to a horse to improve its racing performance.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; horse-racing; horseracing; horses; lifestyle; pets; racing; sports
