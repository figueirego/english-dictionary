### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The direction and rate of change of the electric displacement field at a given point of space and time. It can be expressed as a vector.

   **Example:** When a capacitor is charging, a current flows into one of its two plates, a displacement current flows from that plate to its other plate, and a current flows out of that other plate.
