   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** People struggled to cope with the ever-increasing cost of living.

3. At any time.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** This is one of the best movies ever.

   **Example:** He's back and better than ever.

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. As intensifier following an interrogative word.

   **Usage:** informal; not-comparable

   **Example:** Was I ever glad to see you!

   **Example:** Did I ever!

5. Indicates experiential aspect, once; has or have (done something) before.
