### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To repeat (the exact words of a person).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The writer quoted the president's speech.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To prepare a summary of work to be done and set a price; to estimate.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Can you believe they quoted me $5,000?

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To name the current price, notably of a financial security.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; commerce

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To indicate verbally or by equivalent means the start of a quotation.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To observe, to take account of.

   **Usage:** archaic

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

   **Usage:** transitive
