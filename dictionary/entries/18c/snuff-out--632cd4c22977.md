### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To extinguish (literally, especially by physically covering or rubbing); to extinguish (figuratively)&#58; to stop (a process), to kill (a process).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The risk of fire was an ever-present danger, and every night at bedtime they performed the ritual of checking that every candle and lantern had been snuffed out.

   **Example:** He sighed, snuffed out his cigarette, and got back to work.

   **Example:** He sighed, snuffed out his wish to walk right out the door, and got back to work.

   **Source synonyms:** smother
