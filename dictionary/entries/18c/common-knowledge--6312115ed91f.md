## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. That which is known by most people, and thus cannot reasonably be contested.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A special kind of knowledge for a group of agents, such that when all the agents in a group G know p, they all know that they know p, they all know that they all know that they know p, and so on ad infinitum.

   **Usage:** uncountable
