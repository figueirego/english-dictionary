### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of several albatrosses, especially the black-footed albatross (Phoebastria nigripes) and the Laysan albatross, that dwell primarily on islands in the Pacific Ocean, often near naval bases.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. a foolish, silly, or awkward person or thing; a goon.

   **Usage:** slang

<a id="r1-s3"></a>
