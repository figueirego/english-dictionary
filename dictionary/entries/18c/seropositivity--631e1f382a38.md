### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The quality or state of being seropositive, of having blood serum that tests positive for a given pathogen, especially HIV.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Pretest probability inversely correlates with the probability that the reported seropositivity is a false-positive.

   **Source antonyms:** seronegativity

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Seropositivity prevalence rate&#58; synonym of seroprevalence.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; metonymically; uncountable

   **Example:** At first, the prophylaxis budgeting efforts were triaged to the areas with the highest seropositivity.
