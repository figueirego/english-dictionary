## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of a group of bushes in the genus Cyclopia whose flowers smell of honey and whose leaves are used to make an herbal tea.

   **Usage:** South-Africa

   **Source synonyms:** honeybush tea

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of several not closely related shrubs that have features reminiscent of honey, of species&#58;
   1. Cuttsia viburnea (native elderberry), of rainforests in New South Wales and Queensland in Australia.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of several not closely related shrubs that have features reminiscent of honey, of species&#58;
   1. Hakea lissocarpha (duck-and-drake-bush), of Western Australia

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any of several not closely related shrubs that have features reminiscent of honey, of species&#58;
   1. Melianthus major (Cape honeyflower), of South Africa and naturalised elsewhere

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Any of several not closely related shrubs that have features reminiscent of honey, of species&#58;
   1. Richea scoparia, of Tasmania
