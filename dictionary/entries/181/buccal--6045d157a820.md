**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of, relating to, near, involving, or supplying the cheek.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; relational

   **Example:** buccal surface of the molars

   **Example:** buccal branch of the facial nerve

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; dentistry; medicine; sciences

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** buccal cavity

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Administered in the mouth, not by swallowing but by absorption through the skin of the cheek; often by placing between the top gum and the inside of the lip.
