### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Characterized or marked by full, generous, pleasurable sensation.

   **Example:** The plentiful blankets and the voluptuous pillows of the bed called out to my tired body.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Curvaceous and sexually attractive.

   **Example:** The low neckline of her bodice emphasized her plump, voluptuous figure.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a person
