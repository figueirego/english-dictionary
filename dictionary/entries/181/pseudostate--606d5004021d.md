### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A geopolitical entity which has some of the trappings of an independently-governed country or territory, but which lacks real independence and is not recognized as a self-governing political entity by the international community of nations.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; politics

<a id="r1-s2"></a>
