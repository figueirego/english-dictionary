### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To express support or approval, especially officially or publicly.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The president endorsed John Smith as senator.

   **Source synonyms:** underwrite

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To write one's signature on the back of a cheque, or other negotiable instrument, when transferring it to a third party, or cashing it.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To provide an endorsement.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To give notice of something in a document.

   **Usage:** UK; transitive

   **Example:** The court endorsed his driving record with three penalty points for the drink-driving.

   **Example:** The writ of summons was endorsed with the nature of a claim, the cause thereof, and of the relief sought in the action.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To report (a symptom); to describe.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences
