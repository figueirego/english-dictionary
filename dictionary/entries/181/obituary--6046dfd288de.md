### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A brief notice of a person's death, especially one published in a newspaper or other publication; also (obsolete), the section of a newspaper where notices of deaths are published.

   **Source synonyms:** obit

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A brief biography of a person (especially one who is well-known) who has recently died, usually describing their life and achievements, particularly in the form of an article in a news publication or an item in a news broadcast.

   **Usage:** broadly

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An announcement or description of the end of something.

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A register of deaths, especially one maintained by a religious institution; a necrology.

   **Usage:** historical
