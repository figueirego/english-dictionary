### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make something dangerous or toxic by introducing impurities or foreign matter.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** This water is contaminated. It isn't safe to drink.

   **Source synonyms:** poison; pollute; [taint](../3ea/taint--fa98300cb837.md)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To soil, stain, corrupt, or infect by contact or association.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** defile; pollute; sully; assoil (obsolete); beclart (UK; dialectal); bedaub (obsolete); bedirty; imbrue (especially with blood) (archaic; literary); bedizen (UK; dialectal); befile (obsolete); befilth (rare); befoul; begrime; beray (archaic; obsolete); beshit (figuratively; vulgar); besmear; besmirch; besully; bewray (archaic; obsolete); blacken; blot; blur (obsolete); clat; contaminate; darken; defile; dirten (Uganda); dirty; elt (Northern-England; Scotland); file (archaic); foil (obsolete); foul; gunk; gunk up; inquinate; scunge; sling mud; slotter; slur; smear; smirch; smudge; smut; smutch; soil; soss (UK; dialectal); sowl (obsolete); spot; stain; sullow; sully; tarnish; uggle (UK; dialectal; obsolete); adulterate; corrupt; debauch; demoralize (dated); detort (dated); file; immoralize; lead astray; pervert; pollute; preposterate (obsolete); seduce; sophisticate (figuratively); suborn; subvert; tamper (dated); unmoralize; vitiate; warp

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To make unfit for use by the introduction of unwholesome or undesirable elements.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Do not contaminate the peanut butter with the jelly.

   **Source synonyms:** mar; mess up; ruin; ball up (dated; slang); blight; bumble; botch; bugger up (vulgar); bungle; contaminate; corrupt; crab (slang); deprave; disrupt; disturb; foil; fuck up (vulgar); jack up (vulgar); mar; mess up; mishandle; mommick (US; dialectal); pollute; put a crimp in; queer (dated; slang); ruin; [screw up](../08a/screw-up--229349272c27.md) (vulgar); soil; sour; spoil; stain; undo (literary); upset; turn upside down (idiomatic); upset the applecart (idiomatic); vitiate

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To infect, usually of a deadly virus.
