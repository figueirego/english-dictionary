### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To verify through brief investigation or revisit for assurance that there are no problems.

   **Usage:** most often in phrasal verb "check up on"

   **Example:** I dropped by to check up on my friend.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To verify something's accuracy.

   **Example:** He's checking up the figures.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To seem correct or plausible; to comport with known facts; to check out (with other facts).

   **Example:** The numbers she provided check up with my own data.
