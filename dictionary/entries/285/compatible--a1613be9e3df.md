### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Capable of working together without conflict.

   **Example:** This printer isn't compatible with my computer.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Able to get along well.

   **Example:** My neighbours and I are not very compatible&#58; they're loud and I'm an introvert.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Consistent; congruous.

   **Example:** His actions were compatible with his sermons.

   **Source synonyms:** compliant; congruent; in step; commensurate; compatible; compliant; conformable; conformant; congruent; congruous; consistent; in line (idiomatic); in step (figuratively); proportionate; uniform
