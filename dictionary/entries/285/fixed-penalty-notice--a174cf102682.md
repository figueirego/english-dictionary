### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A notice served on an offender by an authorised person, which is usually for committing a minor offence. It requires the offender to pay a fixed fine within a certain period of time, or appeal against it, possibly in court.

   **Usage:** Hong-Kong; UK

   **Source synonyms:** FPN (initialism)
