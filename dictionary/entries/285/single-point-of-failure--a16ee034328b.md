## Noun

### Forms

- single points of failure — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A component in a device, or a point in a network, that, if it were to fail, would cause the entire device or network to fail (normally eliminated by adding redundancy).

   **Example:** The Jesus nut got its name because it is a single point of failure — if it goes, there's nothing you can do but pray.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A minor mistake that creates a chain reaction.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** The intact twine in Dylan's beef Wellington was a single point of failure.
