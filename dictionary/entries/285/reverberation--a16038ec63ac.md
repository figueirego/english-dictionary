### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A violent oscillation or vibration.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The discomfort caused by the bat's reverberation surprised Tommy.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An echo, or a series of overlapping echoes.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The reverberation that followed Marilyn's shout filled the cavern.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The reflection of light or heat; a reflection in, or as though in, a mirror.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Like the several reverberations of the same image from two opposite looking glasses.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An evolving series of effects resulting from a particular event; a repercussion.

   **Usage:** countable; in-plural; uncountable

   **Example:** Reverberations from the Vietnam war affect our society to this day.
