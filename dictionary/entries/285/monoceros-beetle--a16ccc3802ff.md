### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any anthicid beetle of the species Notoxus monoceros, that have a prominent projection jutting out from their thoraxes.

   **Usage:** strictly

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any beetle of the subfamily Anthicinae, many of which have similar hornlike projections.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Source synonyms:** anthicine
