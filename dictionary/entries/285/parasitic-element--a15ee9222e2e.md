### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A circuit element or property that is present within an electrical component, and has a negative effect on the performance of the circuit.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; electrical-engineering; electricity; electromagnetism; electronics; energy; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An antenna element that receives and re-emits energy from the driven element, which has an effect on the performance of the antenna.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** broadcasting; media; radio
