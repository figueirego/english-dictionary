**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To decline to comment, especially on grounds that it might be incriminating.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** If you ask me who ate the last of the ice cream, I will simply take the fifth.

   **Source synonyms:** plead the Fifth
