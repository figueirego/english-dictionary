### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of a number of trees with milky sap&#58;
   1. Sideroxylon inermis, the white milkwood

      **Usage:** South-Africa

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of a number of trees with milky sap&#58;
   1. Mimusops zeyheri, the Transvaal red milkwood.

      **Usage:** South-Africa

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of a number of trees with milky sap&#58;
   1. Alstonia scholaris (scholar tree, milky pine), and other species in the genus Alstonia.

      **Usage:** Australia; South-Asia

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any of a number of trees with milky sap&#58;
   1. Trimeris scaevolifolia, a tree endemic to the Atlantic island of St. Helena.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Any of a number of trees with milky sap&#58;
   1. Many species of the genus Tabernaemontana, that are native to the Americas.
