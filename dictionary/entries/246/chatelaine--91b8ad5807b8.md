### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The mistress of a castle or large household.

   **Usage:** dated

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A chain or clasp worn at the waist by women with handkerchief, keys, etc., attached, supposed to resemble the chain of keys once worn by medieval chatelaines.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
