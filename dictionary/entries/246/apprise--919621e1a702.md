### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make (someone or oneself) aware of some information; to inform, to notify.

   **Usage:** reflexive; transitive

   **Example:** The ears apprise the brain of sound.

   **Source synonyms:** acquaint; keep (someone) abreast of; keep someone posted; acquaint; advise; apprise; discuss (obsolete); enlighten; impart; inform; keep informed; let know; notify; tell

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To formally impart (information) to someone; to advise, to notify.

   **Usage:** rare; reflexive; transitive
