### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An individual's level of wealth or income, as a measure of the tax rate they can afford to pay.

   **Usage:** attributive; often

   **Example:** ability-to-pay taxation

   **Example:** an ability-to-pay approach

   **Example:** Progressive taxes attempt to reduce the tax burden of people with a lower ability to pay.
