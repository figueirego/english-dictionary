### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A round sweet and juicy stone fruit, resembling peach or plum in taste, with a yellow-orange flesh, lightly fuzzy skin and a large seed inside.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** pickled apricots

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The apricot tree, Prunus armeniaca.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** apricot tree

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A pale yellow-orange colour, like that of an apricot fruit.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A dog with an orange-coloured coat.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The junction of the brain and brain stem on a target, used as an aiming point to ensure a one-shot kill.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; sniper slang

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A testicle.

   **Usage:** Australia; UK; countable; dated; plural-normally; slang; uncountable
