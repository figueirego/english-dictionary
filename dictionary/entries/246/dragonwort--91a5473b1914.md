### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Common bistort, a perennial herb of Europe and Asia, of species Bistorta officinalis (syns. Persicaria bistorta, Polygonum bistorta), formerly used as an astringent.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

<a id="r1-s3"></a>
