### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of or pertaining to a despot or tyrant.

   **Example:** This despotic age of the empire ended in a revolt.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Acting or ruling as a despot, tyrannical.

   **Example:** The despotic king dissolved the parliament, and took personal control of the country.
