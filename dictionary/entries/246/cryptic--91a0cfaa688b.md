**Source sense R1.S1**

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

2. Mystified or of an obscure nature; not easy to perceive.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Involving use of a code or cipher.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Of a crossword puzzle, or a clue in such a puzzle, using, in addition to definitions, wordplay such as anagrams, homophones and hidden words to indicate solutions.

   **Usage:** crosswording

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Well camouflaged; having good camouflage.
