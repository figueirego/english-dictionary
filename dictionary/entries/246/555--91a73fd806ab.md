## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Pertaining to a fake telephone number. The local exchange and area code number 555 was reserved in North American dialing plan for fictional numbers, prior to the 21st century.

   **Usage:** Canada; US; not-comparable

   **Example:** 555-2368, 1-555-321-2368, and (555)234-2368 are all 555 numbers; they aren’t real.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** fiction; literature; media; publishing
