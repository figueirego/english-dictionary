### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To keep up; to preserve; to uphold (a state, condition etc.).

   **Example:** maintain contact

   **Example:** maintain order

   **Example:** maintain a relationship

   **Example:** It’s important to maintain good posture while sitting.

   **Example:** She struggled to maintain her composure under pressure.

   **Source synonyms:** subsist

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To declare or affirm (a clause) to be true; to assert.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To keep in good condition and working order.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To support (someone), to back up or assist (someone) in an action.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To keep composed; to keep one's head up; to stay confident, strong, self-assured; to avoid being a victim; to keep oneself from falling apart.

   **Usage:** intransitive; slang; African-American Vernacular

   **Example:** No matter what, you gotta maintain.
