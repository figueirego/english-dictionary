### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A farmer; one who grows things.

   **Example:** He was an orange grower from Florida.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Something that grows.

   **Example:** These flowers are fast growers

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

   **Example:** I didn't like the song at first, but it is a real grower.
