## Noun · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of the four points that divide an ordered distribution into five parts, each containing a fifth (20%) of the population. Used commonly for income distributions.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences; statistics

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any one of the five groups.

   **Example:** Her income placed her in the lowest pentile of the income distribution.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences; statistics
