### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An ideology based on the belief that people, goods and information ought to be able to cross national borders unfettered.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A socio-economic system dedicated to free trade and free access to markets.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A supposed ideology or practice that (in conspiracy theories) aims to subordinate national sovereignty (legal systems, economies, etc) to centralized global authorities.

   **Usage:** countable; derogatory; uncountable; conspiracy theories

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** political-science; social-sciences

   **Source antonyms:** nationalism; populism
