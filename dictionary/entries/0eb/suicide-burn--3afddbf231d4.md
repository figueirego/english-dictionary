## Noun

### Forms

- suicide burns — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A method of soft-landing a rocket with a well-timed, high-thrust engine burn starting at the last possible second, such that it reaches a vertical speed of zero at precisely or almost precisely at the exact moment that it touches the landing pad and the engine shuts down.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** aerospace; business; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; rocketry

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An instance of such a landing.

   **Example:** A suicide burn is a more efficient method of landing than slowly decelerating over the length of a long descent, as a smaller proportion of the engine's thrust is spent fighting against gravity.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** aerospace; business; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; rocketry

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A rocket firing during such a landing.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** aerospace; business; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; rocketry
