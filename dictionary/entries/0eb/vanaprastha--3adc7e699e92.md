## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The third of four ashrama (stages) in a human life, characterised by a gradual transition of one's social and economic responsibilities to the next generation, and a move away from worldly and sexual pursuits to more spiritual concerns.

   **Usage:** Hinduism; uncountable
