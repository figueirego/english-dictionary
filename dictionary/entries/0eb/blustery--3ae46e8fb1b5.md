### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Blowing in loud and abrupt bursts.

   **Example:** Currently, there are blustery winds blowing in Patagonia.

   **Source synonyms:** blusterous; gusty

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Accompanied by strong wind.

   **Example:** Today is such a cold blustery day!

   **Source synonyms:** blowy; blusterous; breezy; squally; stormy; tempestuous; windy

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Pompous or arrogant, especially in one's speech; given to outbursts.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a person

   **Source synonyms:** blustering; blusterous; swaggering
