### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An (often walled) area of a city in which Jews are concentrated by force and law. (Used particularly of areas in medieval Italy and in Nazi-controlled Europe.)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An (often impoverished) area of a city inhabited predominantly by members of a specific nationality, ethnicity, or race.

   **Source synonyms:** barrio (Dominican-Republic; Venezuela); bad part of town; estate (UK); favela (Brazil); ghetto; Hell's Kitchen; jects (US); projects (US); scheme (UK); shantytown; sink estate (UK); slum

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An area in which people who are distinguished by sharing something other than ethnicity concentrate or are concentrated.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An isolated, self-contained, segregated subsection, area or field of interest; often of minority or specialist interest.

   **Usage:** derogatory; figuratively; sometimes

   **Source synonyms:** ivory tower

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Of or relating to a ghetto or to ghettos in general.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Unseemly and indecorous or of low quality; cheap; shabby, crude.

   **Usage:** informal; slang

   **Example:** My apartment's so ghetto, the rats and cockroaches filed a complaint with the city!

   **Example:** I like to drive ghetto cars; if they break down you can just abandon them and pick up a new one!

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. Characteristic of the style, speech, or behavior of residents of a predominantly black or other ghetto in the United States.

   **Usage:** US; derogatory; informal; offensive; often

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**
