### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A mixture consisting of egg white and a sweetener, variously mixed with (in western Europe) almonds or (in eastern Europe) hazelnuts or (in US) used without nuts as a filler in candy bars.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
