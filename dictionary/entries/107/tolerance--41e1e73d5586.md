### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The ability to endure pain or hardship; endurance.

   **Usage:** obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The ability or practice of tolerating; an acceptance of or patience with the beliefs, opinions or practices of others; a lack of bigotry.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** toleration; forbearance

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The ability of the body (or other organism) to resist the action of a poison, to cope with a dangerous drug or to survive infection by an organism.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The ability of the body (or other organism) to resist the action of a poison, to cope with a dangerous drug or to survive infection by an organism.
   1. Resistance to drunkenness.

      **Usage:** uncountable

      **Example:** Since he hadn't had any alcohol in years, his tolerance was very low, and it only took two drinks for him to get sloshed.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The variation or deviation from a standard, especially the maximum permitted variation in an engineering measurement.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** Our customers can generally accept ten times the tolerance which we can achieve in our machining operations.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The ability of the body to accept a tissue graft without rejection.

   **Usage:** uncountable
