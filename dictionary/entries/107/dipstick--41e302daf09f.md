### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A stick or rod used to measure the depth of a liquid. Often used to check the level at which a liquid in an opaque or inaccessible tank or reservoir stands; gauge.

   **Example:** I haven't checked the oil level of my car's motor since I lost the dipstick three months ago.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An exciseman or supervisor who measures with a dipstick.

   **Usage:** derogatory; obsolete

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The penis.

   **Usage:** slang

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A useless person of inferior intellect; a dipshit.

   **Usage:** derogatory; slang

   **Example:** That referee is a right dipstick.
