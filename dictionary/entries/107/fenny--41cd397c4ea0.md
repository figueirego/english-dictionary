### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Characteristic of or resembling a fen (“characteristically alkaline wetland containing peat below the waterline”); marshy, swampy; also, of land&#58; containing a fen or fens.

   **Source synonyms:** boggy; fenlike; fennish; quaggy; uliginous; boggish; boggy; fennish; fenny; marish; marshy; miry; moorish; moory; morassy; paludal; paludine; paludinous; palustral; palustrine; palustrous; poachy; quaggy; queachy; sloughy; slumpy (dialectal); spewy; swaly; swampish; swampy; syrtic; uliginous; wiery

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Now chiefly of plants&#58; growing or living in a fen.

**Source sense R1.S3**
