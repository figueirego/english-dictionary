### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To show flirtatious signs or sexual interest/attraction with one's eyes.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I saw him giving me the eye as soon as I entered the bar.

   **Source synonyms:** eye up; give someone the glad eye

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To give someone the stink eye.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I saw him giving me the eye as soon as I entered the office.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To give someone the evil eye.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I saw him giving me the eye as soon as I entered the town square.

4. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see give, someone, the, eye; for example, to hand someone the eye of a hook and eye.
