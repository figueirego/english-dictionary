## Noun

### Forms

- trick questions — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A question that attempts to elicit an answer in a certain way, often a misleading or incorrect one, or a question that lacks a clear answer, conceals its true intent, or obscures an underlying difficulty.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A complex question, whose wording hinders the ability to answer it correctly.

   **Usage:** rare
