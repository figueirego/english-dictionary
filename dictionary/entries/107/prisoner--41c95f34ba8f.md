### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person incarcerated in a prison, while on trial or serving a sentence.

   **Example:** Two other prisoners were staying in the same cell as him.

   **Source synonyms:** jailbird

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any person held against their will.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A person who is or feels confined or trapped by a situation or a set of circumstances.

   **Example:** I am no longer a prisoner to fear, for I am a child of God.

   **Example:** I'm a prisoner of your love.
