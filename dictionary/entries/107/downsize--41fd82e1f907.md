### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To reduce in size or number.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Joe and Edna moved to a bungalow after deciding to downsize when the children had left home.

   **Example:** The company chose to downsize by laying off half of its workers.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To reduce the workforce of.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** They downsized the division by offering attractive early-retirement packages and selling off an office building.

   **Source synonyms:** rightsize (euphemistic)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To terminate the employment of.

   **Usage:** euphemistic; transitive

   **Example:** Joe got downsized and became a plumber.
