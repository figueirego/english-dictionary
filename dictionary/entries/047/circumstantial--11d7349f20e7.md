## Adjective · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /səɹ.kəmˈstænʃəl/ — source labels: General-American
- IPA: /səːkəmˈstanʃəl/ — source labels: UK

### Forms

- more circumstantial — comparative
- most circumstantial — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Pertaining to or dependent on circumstances, especially as opposed to essentials; incidental, not essential.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Involving evidence that relies on inference to connect it to a conclusion of fact, thus, suggesting guilt but not directly proving it.

   **Example:** It is unlikely he will be convicted; the evidence against him is circumstantial at best.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Abounding with minor circumstances; in great detail; particular.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Full of circumstance or pomp; ceremonial.

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** accidental; circumstantial; coincidental; coinstantial; contingent; happenstantial; incidental; secondary

**Source antonyms:** certain; impossible; inevitable; necessary
