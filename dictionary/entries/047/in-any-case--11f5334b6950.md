## Prep\_phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Whatever be the case.
   1. Regardless (of an aforementioned point).

      **Usage:** idiomatic

      **Example:** Who knows how many more inches of snow will fall tonight, but in any case, we can expect to be doing a lot of shoveling tomorrow.

      **Example:** Who knows how many more inches of snow will fall tonight, but in any case, let's go to bed.

      **Source synonyms:** whatever the case may be; anyhow; anyway; at any rate; in any event; at all events; regardless

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Whatever be the case.
   1. Any way one slices it (emphasizing that a point is important no matter what).

      **Usage:** idiomatic

      **Example:** In any case, there are never fewer than two guards on duty here.

      **Source synonyms:** whatever the case may be; at any rate; by any measure; in any event; at all events

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see in, any, case.

   **Example:** Their most popular product, an assortment of ales, is packaged such that there are always at least two bottles of India pale ale in any case.

   **Example:** It is not clear that this particular legal question has ever been a determining factor in any case to date.
