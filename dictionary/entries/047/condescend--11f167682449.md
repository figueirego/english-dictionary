### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To come down or go down; to descend.

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To come down from a superior position and do something; to deign; (with a negative connotation) to stoop.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

   **Example:** The boss condescended not to sack him after much persuasion from his coworkers.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To treat someone as though inferior; to talk down to someone; to patronize.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Chiefly followed by on or upon&#58; to go into detail; to particularize, to specify.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To agree to something; to accede, to assent, to consent; also, to reach an agreement.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive; obsolete

   **Source antonyms:** discondescend

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To give way or yield in a deferential manner; to be amenable or compliant.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive; obsolete

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To graciously give; to vouchsafe.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive; obsolete

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To reach a certain point; to settle on.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive; obsolete

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To secretly make plans, usually to bring about a bad or illegal result; to conspire, to plot.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive; obsolete

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. To treat (someone) as though inferior; to talk down to (someone); to patronize.

   **Usage:** nonstandard; rare; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** belittle

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. Often preceded by the dummy pronoun it&#58; to agree to (something); to consent.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive; chiefly passive voice

   **Example:** It was condescended by the warring parties that they should lay down their arms and seek peace.
