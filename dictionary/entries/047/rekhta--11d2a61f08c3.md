## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A style of Hindi-Urdu, Hindavi, or sometimes Braj or Punjabi used in poetry that linguistically adapts Persian grammar and vocabulary (to a large extent); a mix of Persian and Khariboli.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The Hindustani language, the language of which Hindi and Urdu are literary standards.

   **Usage:** historical; obsolete
