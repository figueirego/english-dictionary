## Adverb · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈɛv.ɹi.(h)weə(ɹ)/ — source labels: UK
- IPA: /ˈɛv.ɹi.(h)wɛɹ/ — source labels: US

### Forms

- e'rywhere — alternative; US; dialectal
- e'erywhere — alternative; US; dialectal
- everwhere — alternative; US; dialectal
- every where — alternative; obsolete

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In or to all locations under discussion.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** He delivers the mail everywhere on this street.

   **Example:** We went everywhere at the school - we talked to all the teachers in their classrooms.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In or to a few or more locations.

   **Usage:** colloquial; excessive; not-comparable

   **Example:** We went to Europe last year and went everywhere&#58; Berlin, Paris, London, and Madrid.

   **Example:** When I shop for shoes, I like to look everywhere.

   **Example:** I've looked everywhere in the house and still can't find my glasses.

### Related words

**Source antonyms:** nowhere
