<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** All types of asbestos are potentially carcinogenic when inhaled.

   **Example:** Asbestos insulation was once ubiquitous.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; geography; geology; mineralogy; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; geography; geology; mineralogy; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The supposed material composing part of the body that is impervious to heat, or by extension other unpleasant sensations; especially hot or spicy food.

   **Usage:** attributive; colloquial; countable; uncountable
