### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Devilish action or conduct; wickedness; (more generally) troublemaking behaviour; mischief; (countable) an instance of this.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source synonyms:** devilry

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A devilish, mischievous, or reckless nature; mischievousness.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Something cruel or evil; also, something which causes distress or suffering.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Something cleverly constructed.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A dish which is devilled (“made piquant or spicy”); a devil; also, the elements of the dish which make it spicy, or the piquancy or spiciness of the dish.

   **Usage:** countable; humorous; often; uncountable; usually
