### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A decision on an issue of fact in a civil or criminal case or an inquest.

   **Example:** guilty verdict, not guilty verdict, final verdict

   **Example:** The jury returned a verdict of not guilty.

   **Example:** I hope the court overturns his verdict.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

   **Source synonyms:** finding

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An opinion or judgement.

   **Example:** a “not out” verdict from the umpire

   **Example:** We've reached a verdict on your proposal.
