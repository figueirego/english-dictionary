### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To obstruct.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The lorry is blocking off the road, so traffic has come to a standstill.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To book, set aside (an unbroken period of time to do an activity).

   **Example:** I'll need to block off a couple of weeks to refurnish my house.
