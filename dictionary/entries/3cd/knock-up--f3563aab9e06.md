### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To put together, fabricate, or assemble, particularly if done hastily or temporarily.

   **Usage:** colloquial

   **Example:** I'll just knock up a quick demo for the sales presentation.

   **Source synonyms:** knock together; run up

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To awaken (someone) as by knocking at the door; rouse; call; summon; also, to go door-to-door on election day to persuade a candidate's supporters to go to the polling station and vote. See also knocker up.

   **Usage:** UK

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To exhaust; wear out; tire out; to fatigue until unable to do more.

   **Usage:** dated

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To become exhausted or worn out; to fail of strength; to become wearied, as with labor; to give out.

   **Usage:** dated; intransitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To impregnate, especially out of wedlock or accidentally. See knocked up.

   **Usage:** US; slang

   **Example:** I guess his summer plans are shot now that he knocked his girlfriend up.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To gently hit the ball back and forth before a tennis match, as practice or warm-up, and to gauge the state of the playing surface, lighting, etc. See knock-up.

   **Usage:** intransitive; racket sports

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To make even at the edges, or to shape into book form.

   **Example:** to knock up printed sheets

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** arts; bookbinding; crafts; hobbies; lifestyle

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To straighten up a stack of paper.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** media; printing; publishing
