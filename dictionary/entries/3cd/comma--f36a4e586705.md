### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The punctuation mark ⟨,⟩ used to indicate a set of parts of a sentence or between elements of a list.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** media; publishing; typography

   **Source synonyms:** scratch comma; virgule; virgula; come; comma-point (obsolete)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A similar-looking subscript diacritical mark.

   **Usage:** Romanian

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** media; publishing; typography

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of various nymphalid butterflies of the genus Polygonia, having a comma-shaped white mark on the underwings, especially Polygonia c-album and Polygonia c-aureum of North Africa, Europe, and Asia.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; entomology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A difference in the calculation of nearly identical intervals by different ways.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A delimiting marker between items in a genetic sequence.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; genetics; medicine; natural-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. In Ancient Greek rhetoric, a short clause, something less than a colon, originally denoted by comma marks. In antiquity it was defined as a combination of words having no more than eight syllables in all. It was later applied to longer phrases, e.g. the Johannine comma.

   **Usage:** rhetoric

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A brief interval.

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A cloud shaped like a comma because of differential rotation of the cloud border and adjacent upward and downward vertical motions, often appearing in large and intense low-pressure systems.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** climatology; meteorology; natural-sciences
