### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A physical or photographic queue of people allegedly involved in a crime, allowing a witness to identify them.

   **Usage:** US

   **Example:** police lineup

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; law; law-enforcement

   **Source synonyms:** rogues' gallery

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A list of trains that are expected to cross over a specified section of track during a specified time period.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A line of people or vehicles, in which the individual at the front end is dealt with first, the one behind is dealt with next, and so on, and in which newcomers join at the end; a queue.

   **Usage:** Canada

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An ordered list.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Collectively, the members of a team.

   **Example:** The manager fielded his strongest lineup for the game against United.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The batting order.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; baseball; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The order in which race-cars are positioned at the start of a race, based on qualifying times.

   **Usage:** car racing

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. The set of product options available from a particular manufacturer or under a particular brand name.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; marketing

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. The members of a music group at any one time.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. The acts performing at a concert or festival.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** economics; entertainment; entertainment-industry; lifestyle; sciences

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. The list of programs scheduled to be broadcast by a television station, or a position in that list.
