## Noun

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈbɪtənəs/ — source labels: Received-Pronunciation

### Forms

- bitternesses — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The quality of having a bitter taste.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The quality of feeling bitter; acrimony, resentment; the quality of exhibiting such feelings.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** the bitterness of his words

   **Example:** She kept her bitterness about her mistreatment for the rest of her life.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The quality of eliciting a bitter, humiliating or harsh feeling.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Nothing could assuage the bitterness of their defeat.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Harsh cold.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The bitterness of the winter caught us all by surprise.
