### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A former small Turkish unit of weight, variously reckoned as 1.5–3.5 g (0.05–0.12 oz.).

   **Usage:** historical units of measurement

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Alternative form of dirham&#58; a former silver coin weighing one dirhem; modern currencies named for it.

   **Usage:** alt-of; alternative; chiefly historical Turkish contexts
