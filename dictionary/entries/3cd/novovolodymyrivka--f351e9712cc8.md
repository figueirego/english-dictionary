### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A village in Sakhnovshchyna settlement hromada, Berestyn Raion, Kharkiv Oblast, Ukraine, founded as a khutir in 1870.

   **Source synonyms:** Volodymyrivka

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A village in Prysyvashshia rural hromada, Kakhovka Raion, Kherson Oblast, Ukraine, founded in 1872.

   **Source synonyms:** Tuhrek; Novo-Volodymyrivka

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A village in Dolmativka rural hromada, Skadovsk Raion, Kherson Oblast, Ukraine, founded in 1805.
