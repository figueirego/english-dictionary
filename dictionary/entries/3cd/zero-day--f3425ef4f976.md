### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of warez, released less than a day after the official product release.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of a vulnerability, newly discovered, and therefore still not fixed and possibly exploited by hackers or other criminals.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; computer security

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of an exploit or its threat or an attack, benefiting from a newly found and yet unpatched or unmitigated flaw in software or hardware; using a zero-day vulnerability.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; computer security

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see zero, day.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Average residues of 1.44 and 2.18 mg/kg chlortetracycline were seen in liver and kidney, respectively, at zero-day withdrawal.

- zero day — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A vulnerability that has been discovered recently, and is yet unpatched or unmitigated; a zero-day vulnerability.

   **Usage:** computer security

   **Example:** New Internet Explorer zero-day exploited in Hong Kong attacks

**Source sense R2.S2**
