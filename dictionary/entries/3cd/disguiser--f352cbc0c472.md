### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person or thing that disguises.

   **Example:** A voice disguiser alters a person’s voice to protect their anonymity.

   **Example:** Incense can be used as an odour disguiser.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** archaic; historical
