### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A group sharing common characteristics, such as the same language, law, religion, or tradition.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A residential or religious collective; a commune.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A group of interdependent organisms inhabiting the same region and interacting with each other.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; ecology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A group of people interacting by electronic means for educational, professional, social, or other purposes; a virtual community.

   **Usage:** Internet; countable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The condition of having certain attitudes and interests in common.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A feeling of togetherness or connectedness, common identity or of possessing a similar outlook, perspective and purpose with some group of people.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Volunteering gave me a sense of community.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Common enjoyment or possession; participation.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete

   **Example:** a community of goods

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Common character; likeness.

   **Usage:** obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. Commonness; frequency.

   **Usage:** obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. A local area within a county or county borough which is the lowest tier of local government, usually represented by a community council or town council, which is generally equivalent to a civil parish in England.

   **Usage:** Wales; countable
