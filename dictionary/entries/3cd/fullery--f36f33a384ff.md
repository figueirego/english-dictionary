## Noun

### Forms

- fulleries — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A place built for the process of fulling wool in cloth-making. Usually refers to a period in history before the process was carried out at a mill.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A workshop for cleaning clothes, particularly in Ancient Roman times.
