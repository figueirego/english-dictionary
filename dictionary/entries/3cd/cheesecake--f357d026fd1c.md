### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A pie made of sweetened and flavoured cottage cheese or cream cheese, eggs and milk on a crunchy base.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Cheesecake is an especially delicious dessert.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A pie made of cream, eggs and milk (somewhat resembling the modern American chess cake).

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Imagery of scantily clad, sexually attractive young women; pin-ups.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Company policy forbids displaying cheesecake in the locker rooms.

   **Source synonyms:** leg art
