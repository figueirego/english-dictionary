### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The action of exposing something to something, such as skin to the sunlight.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The act or process of declaring or describing something through either speech or writing, in nonfiction or in fiction; the portions and aspects of a piece of writing that exist mainly to describe or explain a set of things (such as, in fiction, the setting, characters and other non-plot elements).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** This essay has too much exposition in it.

   **Example:** My reviewer said she couldn't picture the setting for my story, so I'm improving the exposition.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; narratology; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The act of expulsion, or being expelled, from a place.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An exhibition, especially of goods, artwork or cultural displays to the public.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The first prototype was unveiled at an exposition.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An essay or speech in which any topic is discussed in detail.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** I turned my research into an exposition on the traditional music of Borneo.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** authorship; broadcasting; communications; film; journalism; literature; media; publishing; television; writing

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. An opening section in fiction, in which background information about the characters, events or setting is conveyed.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; narratology; sciences

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music
