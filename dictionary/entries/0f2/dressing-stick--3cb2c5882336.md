### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A block of silicon carbide or aluminium oxide used to dress diamond tools by removing the build-up of metal etc.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of several sticks, often with a hook, used by the elderly or infirm to help put on clothes.
