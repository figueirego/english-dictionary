### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The goddess of youth, and a daughter of Jupiter and Juno. Juventas was the cupbearer of the gods until she married her paternal halfbrother, Hercules, with whom she had twin sons, Alexiares and Anicetus. She is the Roman counterpart of Hebe.

   **Usage:** Roman
