### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An older medical term most commonly used to refer to Hodgkin's lymphoma, a type of cancer of the lymphatic system characterized by malignant growth of lymphocytes and the presence of Reed-Sternberg cells.

   **Usage:** uncountable
