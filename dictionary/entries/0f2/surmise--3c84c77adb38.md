### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Thought, imagination, or conjecture, which may be based upon feeble or scanty evidence; suspicion; guess.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** surmises of jealousy or of envy

   **Example:** This opinion, however, is merely a surmise, which may or may not be the case.

   **Source synonyms:** assumption; conjecture; foreguess; given; guess; hunch; hypothesis; inference; posit; possibility; postulation; presumption; reckoning; speculation; supposition; surmise; suspicion; theory
