## Verb · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To mix or blend thoroughly and completely.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** She mixed up peat moss, humus, and compost to make potting soil.

   **Source synonyms:** scramble; jumble

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To combine thoroughly.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** All the smells of the food had mixed up together.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To prepare something from ingredients that are mixed.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** She mixed up a batch of her own potting soil.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To confuse or reverse.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** I always mix up Vermont and New Hampshire on a map.

   **Example:** I always mix up Jack with Jake.

   **Source synonyms:** jumble; addle; baffle; vex; lose; embroil; stick; stump; baffound; bamboozle; bedevil; befuddle; throw; bemud (figuratively); bemuse; bewilder; bumfuzzle; complicate; confound; confuddle (slang); confuse; confuzzle (childish; slang); dazzle; discombobulate; disconcert; blind; dumbfound; entangle (figuratively); ferhoodle; flabagast; flabbergast; flabbergaster; flurry; fluster; flummox; fuddle; jumble; knot (obsolete); mix up; muddle; muddy (figuratively); ravel; perplex; puzzle

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To confuse, befuddle (a person).

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To become involved with, especially socially or romantically.

   **Usage:** often; transitive; usually passive voice

   **Example:** He got mixed up with a bad crowd.

   **Example:** Then he got mixed up with an older woman.

   **Example:** Now, he's mixed up with some half-baked religion.

   **Source context (verbatim):** with be or get; with with

   **Source synonyms:** embroil

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To shuffle.

   **Example:** mix up this Rubik's cube
