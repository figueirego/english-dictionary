### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Not fit; not having the correct requirements.

   **Example:** Jack cannot run, making him unfit for the track team.

   **Source synonyms:** inapt; unsuitable; bad; discommensurate; improper; inappropriate; malapropos (formal); inapt; incommensurate; inept; inopportune; out of line (idiomatic); out of order (idiomatic); out of place; unapt; unfit; [unhappy](../0ba/unhappy--2e95d0e38d8d.md) (dated); unseemly; unsuitable

   **Source antonyms:** fit; suitable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Not fit, not having a good physical demeanor.

   **Example:** I've become so unfit after stopping cycling to town.

   **Source synonyms:** out of shape

   **Source antonyms:** fit; in shape

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Unsuitable for a particular purpose.

   **Usage:** often

   **Source context (verbatim):** with for
