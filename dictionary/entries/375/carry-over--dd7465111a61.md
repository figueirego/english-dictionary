### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see carry, over.

   **Example:** We carried her over the bridge.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To transfer (something) to a later point in time.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** The rent was carried over to December.

   **Example:** So many candidates there were that the ceremonies were carried over to the next morning.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To carry; to transfer an excess quantity to the next column of digits.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** arithmetic

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To continue cooking after being removed from a heat source.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The chicken will carry over before it is served.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** cooking; food; lifestyle

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive
