### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Made of, or relating to, gold.

   **Example:** She wore a golden crown.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Having a color or other richness suggestive of gold.

   **Example:** Under a golden sun.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of a beverage, flavoured or colored with turmeric.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Marked by prosperity, creativity etc.

   **Example:** The Renaissance was a golden era.

   **Example:** the Golden Horseshoe

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Advantageous or very favourable.

   **Example:** This is a golden opportunity

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Relating to a fiftieth anniversary.

   **Example:** It's not long until our golden wedding.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Relating to the elderly or retired.

   **Example:** After retiring, Bob and Judy moved to Arizona to live out their golden years.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Fine, without problems.

   **Usage:** UK; predicative; slang
