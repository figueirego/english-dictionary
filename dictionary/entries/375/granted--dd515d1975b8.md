## Adverb · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈɡɹæntɪd/ — source labels: General-American
- IPA: /ˈɡɹɑːntɪd/ — source labels: Received-Pronunciation

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Used to concede a point or to express acknowledgment or awareness, often before stating some contrasting or competing information; used as an expression of respect to the point in question.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** He's a good student and usually does well. Granted, he did fail that one test, but I think there were good reasons for that.

   **Example:** "You haven't been a very good father." "Granted."

   **Example:** Granted it's a long shot, but I might win at that.

   **Source synonyms:** admittedly; true
