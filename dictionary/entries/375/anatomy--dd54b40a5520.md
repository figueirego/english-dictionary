### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The science that deals with the form and structure of organic bodies; anatomical structure or organization.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Animal anatomy is also called zootomy; vegetable anatomy, phytotomy; and human anatomy, anthropotomy.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The study of the parts of any organized body, to discover their situation, structure, and economy.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The physical or functional organization of an organism, or part of it.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** I went to the Venice beach body-building competition and noticed the competitor from Athens, and let me tell you, that's what I call classic Greek anatomy.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A treatise or book on anatomy.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The form of an individual.

   **Usage:** colloquial; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The human body, especially in reference to the genitals.

   **Usage:** countable; euphemistic; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A skeleton or other dead body.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. The act of dividing anything, corporeal or intellectual, for the purpose of examining its parts.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** the anatomy of a discourse

   **Example:** the anatomy of love

   **Example:** Burton's famous treatise, "The Anatomy of Melancholy"

   **Source synonyms:** analysis
