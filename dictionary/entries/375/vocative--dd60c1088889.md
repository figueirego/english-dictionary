**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of or pertaining to calling; used in calling or vocation.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used in address; appellative; said of the case or form of the noun, pronoun, or adjective by which a person or thing is addressed.

   **Example:** In English, the vocative may be indicated by an addressee–address separation comma, or by the particle O, as in "What is the matter, sir?", "Mother, listen!", or "O Lord".
