### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The chief executive of the municipal government of a city, borough, etc., formerly (historical) usually appointed as a caretaker by European royal courts but now usually appointed or elected locally.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Ellipsis of mayor of the palace, the royal stewards of the Frankish Empire.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; ellipsis; historical

   **Alternative form of:** [mayor of the palace](../290/mayor-of-the-palace--a41581759544.md)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Synonym of mair, various former officials in the Kingdom of Scotland.

   **Usage:** historical

   **Source synonyms:** mair (synonym; synonym-of); various former officials in the Kingdom of Scotland (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A member of a city council.

   **Usage:** Ireland; obsolete; rare

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A high justice, an important judge.

   **Usage:** historical; obsolete

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A largely ceremonial position in some municipal governments that presides over the city council while a contracted city manager holds actual executive power.

   **Usage:** US

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A local VIP, a muckamuck or big shot reckoned to lead some local group.

   **Usage:** figuratively; humorous
