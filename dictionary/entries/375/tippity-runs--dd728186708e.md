**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A form of backyard cricket; the defining rule of the form, which requires the batsman to run if they hit the ball; the rule increases the pace of the game by creating more runout chances than in ordinary cricket.

   **Usage:** Australia; uncountable; Victoria

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; cricket; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports
