### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In a manner that one does not or cannot believe.

   **Example:** He gestured unbelievably. Everyone knew he was faking his illness.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** manner

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To an extent not to be believed.

   **Usage:** degree

   **Example:** His face turned unbelievably blue. We realized he was actually choking.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Contrary to expectations, amazingly.

   **Usage:** evaluative

   **Example:** Unbelievably, Rudy saved the man's life but got sued for breaking two of his ribs.
