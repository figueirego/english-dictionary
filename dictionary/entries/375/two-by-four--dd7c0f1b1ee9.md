### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A length of sawn wood of cross section nominally 2 inches by 4 inches (actual dimensions 1½ × 3½ in modern use), most often employed as structural framing lumber (dimensional lumber).

   **Usage:** Canada; US
