### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A laser light display that allows a performer to play an electronic instrument, similar to a harp, by selectively blocking the laser beams.

   **Example:** The laser harp was popularized by Jean Michel Jarre, and has featured in most of his concerts since 1981.
