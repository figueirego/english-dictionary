<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Located on the opposite side of, or referring to two distinct sides or ends of a region or object.

   **Usage:** morpheme

   **Example:** amphicontinental

   **Example:** amphitropical

   **Example:** amphidisc

   **Example:** amphicrania

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Either, both; having two distinct properties and able to function as either.

   **Usage:** morpheme

   **Example:** amphinucleolus

   **Example:** amphiphobic

   **Example:** amphithallic

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Merging two forms, and/or the fusion or inheritance that results from the combination.

   **Usage:** morpheme

   **Example:** amphimixis

   **Example:** amphidiploid

   **Example:** amphisome
