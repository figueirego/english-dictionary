## Noun

### Forms

- skeleton keys — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A very simple design of key that usually has a cylindrical shaft (sometimes called a "shank") and a single, minimal flat, rectangular tooth or "bit".

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A key that has parts filed away so that it will open a range of locks.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A master key.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A key (A crucial step or requirement) or (An idea, theory or observation that explains, justifies or renders (something) comprehensible).

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** The skeleton key to this plan is the marketing, which will make the plan make sense when you see it.
