### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of several quantities related to electricity.
   1. The static electric energy of a charged body.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

   1. The quantity of unbalanced positive or negative ions in or on an object; measured in coulombs.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics
