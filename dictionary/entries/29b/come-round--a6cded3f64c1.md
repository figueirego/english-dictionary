### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To change direction.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To change one's opinion to a prevailing one.

   **Example:** I'll explain it again, and maybe he'll come round to my way of thinking.

   **Example:** He's a reasonable man. I knew he'd come round eventually.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To return to a former condition.
   1. To recover consciousness, to come to

      **Example:** After the blow to my head, I took a while to come round.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To return to a former condition.
   1. To cease anger or hostility.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To visit someone's home or other regular place.

   **Example:** Would you like to come round for dinner tonight?

   **Example:** What day does the garbage man come round?
