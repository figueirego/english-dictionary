## Noun

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any country that is a subdivision of a larger sovereign state.

   **Example:** the constituent countries of the Kingdom of Denmark

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any country that is a subdivision of a larger sovereign state.
   1. An administrative division of the United Kingdom&#58; one of England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any country that is a subdivision of a larger sovereign state.
   1. A land of the Kingdom of the Netherlands, of which there are four&#58; the Netherlands proper (comprising its twelve European provinces and the three BES Islands of the Caribbean Netherlands&#58; Bonaire, Sint Eustatius, and Saba) and the three Caribbean island countries of Aruba, Curaçao, and Sint Maarten.

**Source sense R1.S4**
