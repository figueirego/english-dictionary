### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A variety of the Swedish language used by immigrants in the Swedish capital of Stockholm, especially in the suburb of Rinkeby.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. All immigrant created varieties of Swedish used by immigrants to Sweden.

   **Usage:** broadly; uncountable; usually
