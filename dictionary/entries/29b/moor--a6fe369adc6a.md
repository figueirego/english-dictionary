### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An extensive waste covered with patches of heath, and having a poor, light (and usually acidic) soil, but sometimes marshy, and abounding in peat; a heath. (Compare bog, peatland, marsh, swamp, fen.)

   **Example:** A cold, biting wind blew across the moor, and the travellers hastened their step.

   **Example:** In her girlish age, she kept sheep on the moor.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A game preserve consisting of moorland.
