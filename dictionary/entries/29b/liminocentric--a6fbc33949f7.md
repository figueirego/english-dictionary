### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having an edge or border which is simultaneously a centre, or internal areas that are indistinguishable from boundary areas.

   **Example:** The torus is a liminocentric composition.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Having an identity between the very small and very large such that both are equivalent or indistinguishable.

   **Example:** According to some versions of string theory, the universe displays a fractal-like identity between its scale extremities (the astronomically large and subatomically small), indicating a liminocentric structure.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Arranged such that the centre simultaneously encompasses external elements while remaining located within them.

   **Example:** Her analysis proposes a liminocentric chain of cause and effect, like a recursive set of Chinese boxes where the innermost box paradoxically contains the outermost.
