### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who does not contribute or pay appropriately; one who gets a free ride, etc. without paying a fair share.

   **Usage:** colloquial

   **Source synonyms:** free rider; moocher; bloodsucker; bludger (Australia; New-Zealand; slang); bummer (US; dated; slang); freeloader; free rider; gadfly (slang); leech; mooch; moocher; parasite; scrounge; scrounger; scunge (slang); smell-feast (archaic); sponge; sponger; sucker; trencher mate (archaic)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person who takes expired unsold merchandise from the back of supermarket premises.

   **Usage:** Canada; New-Zealand; UK
