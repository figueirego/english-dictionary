### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make worse; to impair.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Global warming has worsened the weather.

   **Example:** As for further retaliation&#58; adding such fuel to the fire, as it were, will only worsen the already unpleasant state of affairs.

   **Source synonyms:** aggravate; exacerbate; degrade; deteriorate; adulterate; aggravate; alloy; canker; contaminate; corrupt; debase; debauch; defile; deflower; degrade; deteriorate; downgrade; envenom; exasperate; exacerbate; infect; impair; leaven; pejorate; set back; stain; sophisticate; uplift; vitiate; weaken

   **Source antonyms:** ameliorate; improve; meliorate; mitigate

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To become worse; to get worse.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The weather has worsened.

   **Source synonyms:** badden; decline; deteriorate; go downhill; go south

   **Source antonyms:** improve
