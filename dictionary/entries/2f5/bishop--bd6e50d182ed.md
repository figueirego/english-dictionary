### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An overseer of congregations&#58; either any such overseer, generally speaking, or (in Eastern Orthodoxy, Catholicism, Anglicanism, etc.) an official in the church hierarchy (actively or nominally) governing a diocese, supervising the church's priests, deacons, and property in its territory.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An overseer of congregations&#58; either any such overseer, generally speaking, or (in Eastern Orthodoxy, Catholicism, Anglicanism, etc.) an official in the church hierarchy (actively or nominally) governing a diocese, supervising the church's priests, deacons, and property in its territory.
   1. A similar official or chief priest in another religion.

      **Usage:** nonstandard

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity; lifestyle; religion

**Source sense R1.S3**

   **Usage:** obsolete

   **Usage:** obsolete
