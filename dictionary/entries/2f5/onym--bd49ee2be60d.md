**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A combining form meaning “word” or “name”, used in terms that denote a particular kind of word or name.
   1. Word.

      **Usage:** morpheme

      **Example:** anti- (“opposite”) + -onym → antonym (“opposite word”)

      **Example:** epi- (“upon; after”) + -onym → eponym (“word (or name) derived from a proper noun”)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A combining form meaning “word” or “name”, used in terms that denote a particular kind of word or name.
   1. Name.

      **Usage:** morpheme

      **Example:** pseudo- (“false”) + -onym → pseudonym (“false name”)

      **Example:** topo- (“place”) + -onym → toponym (“name of a place”)

      **Example:** thalasso- (“sea”) + -onym → thalassonym (“name of a sea”)

      **Example:** anthropo- (“person”) + -onym → anthroponym (“name of a person”)

      **Example:** ornitho- (“bird”) + -onym → ornithonym (“name of a (species of) bird”)
