## Noun

### Forms

- kindlinesses — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The state of feeling kindly towards someone or something, or the actions inspired thereby.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Elmo looked upon his only granddaughter with kindliness, and often relented to her demands for chocolate.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Favourableness; mildness.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Naturalness.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable
