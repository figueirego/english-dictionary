### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Someone who believes that health and temperament are determined by bodily humours; a humoralist.

   **Usage:** archaic; historical

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Someone subject to whims or fancies; an eccentric.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A humorous or witty person, especially someone skilled in humorous writing or performance.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>
