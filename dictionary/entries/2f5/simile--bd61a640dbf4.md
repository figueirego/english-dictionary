### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A figure of speech in which one thing is explicitly compared to another, using e.g. like or as.

   **Usage:** countable; rhetoric; uncountable

   **Source antonyms:** dissimile

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Similarity or resemblance to something else; likeness, similitude.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
