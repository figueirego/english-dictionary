## Noun

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /dɪˈpɛndəns/ — source labels: UK

### Forms

- dependences — plural
- dependance — alternative; archaic

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The state of being dependent, of relying upon another.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** He had a deep dependence on her for guidance.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An irresistible physical or psychological need, especially for a chemical substance.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Her dependence on cocaine led to her ruin.
