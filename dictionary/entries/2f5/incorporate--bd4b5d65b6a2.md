### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To include (something) as a part.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The design of his house incorporates a spiral staircase.

   **Example:** to incorporate another's ideas into one's work

   **Source synonyms:** absorb; add on; assimilate; co-opt; corporate (obsolete); incorporate; incorpse (obsolete); integrate; subsume; swallow

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To mix (something in) as an ingredient; to blend

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Incorporate air into the mixture by whisking.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To admit as a member of a company

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To form into a legal company.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The company was incorporated in 1980.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To include (another clause or guarantee of the US constitution) as a part (of the Fourteenth Amendment, such that the clause binds not only the federal government but also state governments).

   **Usage:** US

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To form into a body; to combine, as different ingredients, into one consistent mass.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To unite with a material body; to give a material form to; to embody.
