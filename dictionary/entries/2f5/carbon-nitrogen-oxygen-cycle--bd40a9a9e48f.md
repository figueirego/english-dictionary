## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A group of nuclear reactions, involving the interaction of protons with carbon, nitrogen and oxygen nuclei, thought to be the main source of energy in main-sequence stars of greater than 1.4 solar mass.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astronomy; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

   **Source synonyms:** Bethe-Weizsäcker cycle; CNO cycle
