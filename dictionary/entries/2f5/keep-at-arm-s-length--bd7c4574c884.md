## Verb

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Keep at a distance, away from one's body.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Avoid direct confrontation or struggle.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Avoid a close relationship.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** She's been keeping me at arm's length all the time. She doesn't want to get involved.
