### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An easy or obvious conclusion, decision, solution; something requiring little or no thought.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** Choosing the older version which was as good as the newer version for half the cost was a no-brainer.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A conundrum requiring little or no thought to solve; A problem with an easy or obvious conclusion, decision, solution.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** This next exam question is a no-brainer.
