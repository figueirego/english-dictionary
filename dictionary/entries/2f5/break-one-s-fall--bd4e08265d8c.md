### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To provide alleviation to prevent someone from slamming into the ground at full speed; to prevent someone from hitting the ground too hard at the end of a fall.

   **Example:** A pile of leaves broke my fall when I slipped off the roof of the shed.
