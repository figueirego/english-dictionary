### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In a believable way.

   **Example:** He acted the part believably.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used to express the speaker's assessment of the credibility of a reported statement.

   **Example:** Believably, Mikey said John pushed him first.
