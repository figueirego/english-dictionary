### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In a direct manner; in a straight line or course.

   **Example:** He drove directly to the office, and didn't stop off at the petrol station.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In a straightforward way; without anything intervening; not by secondary but by direct means.

   **Example:** I'm sick of asking you to fire him; I'll just do it directly.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Plainly, without circumlocution or ambiguity; absolutely; in express terms.

   **Example:** I'm going to tell Natalie directly that I love her.

   **Example:** To put it more directly&#58; he's not 'made redundant' but sacked.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Exactly; just; at the shortest possible distance.

   **Example:** It's directly across the street.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Straightforwardly; honestly.

   **Example:** He told me directly that he'd cheated on me, and how sorry he was for it.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Immediately.

   **Usage:** dated

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Soon; next; in due time; as soon as it becomes convenient.

   **Usage:** Cornwall; Midlands; Southern-US; dated; dialectal; including

   **Example:** We'll go to the store directly, but first I need to finish sweeping.
