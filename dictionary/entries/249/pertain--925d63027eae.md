### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To belong to or be connected as a part, adjunct, attribute, or accessory.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** That spare wheel pertains to this car.

   **Source synonyms:** belong

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To relate to, concern, or be relevant to.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** That question doesn't pertain to the topic, so I'm not going to answer it.

   **Source synonyms:** relate; concern

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To apply; to be or remain in place; to continue to be applicable.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source synonyms:** apply

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To be appropriate or suitable.

   **Source synonyms:** suit; befit
