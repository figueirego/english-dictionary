### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A person's combined given name and surname.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** Hi, Yes, I'm here to register as a new primary care patient. OK, I'll have you fill out a form on this digital tablet, but first I need your first and last, date of birth, and the last four of your social \[the ending digits of an identification number\].
