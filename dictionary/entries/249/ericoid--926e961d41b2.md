## Adjective · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of or pertaining to plants of the genus Erica.
   1. Of plant leaves, small, often leathery, usually needle-like or scale-like, non-deciduous, and generally adapted to poor soils and arid conditions, such as in fynbos and maquis.

      **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of or pertaining to plants of the genus Erica.
   1. Of plant habit, having ericoid leaves, slender, scrubby and woody, like many Erica species.

      **Usage:** not-comparable
