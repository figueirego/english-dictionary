### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To regard with contempt or scorn.

   **Example:** Katie despised video games because she saw them as a trashy, boring waste of time.

   **Example:** James still despises his brother for the time he pushed him out of a tree during their childhood.

   **Source synonyms:** contemn; disdain; scorn; hate
