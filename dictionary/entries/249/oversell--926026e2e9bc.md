### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To agree to sell more of something than one can supply.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To be too eager in attempting to sell something.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source antonyms:** undersell

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To praise something to excess.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I don't want to oversell it, but \[second clause\]

   **Example:** I don't want to oversell it, but you've got to check out that new restaurant on Third Street.

   **Source antonyms:** undersell

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To sell for a higher price than; to exceed in sale price.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive
