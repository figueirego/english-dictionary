### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To cause someone to become accustomed to something that requires prolonged or repeated tolerance of one or more unpleasantries.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** habituate; harden; toughen

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To take effect, to be operative.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Jim buys a beach house that includes the right to travel across the neighbor's property to get to the water. That right of way is said "to inure to the benefit of Jim".

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

   **Source synonyms:** accrue

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To commit.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive
