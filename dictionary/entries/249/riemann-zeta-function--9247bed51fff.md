### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The function ζ defined by the Dirichlet series ζ(s)=∑ₙ₌₁ ᪲1/(nˢ)=1/(1ˢ)+1/(2ˢ)+1/(3ˢ)+1/(4ˢ)+⋯, which is summable for points s in the complex half-plane with real part &gt; 1; the analytic continuation of said function, being a holomorphic function defined on the complex numbers with pole at 1.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** analytic-number-theory; mathematics; number-theory; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A usage of (a specified value of) the Riemann zeta function, such as in an equation.

   **Usage:** countable; usually
