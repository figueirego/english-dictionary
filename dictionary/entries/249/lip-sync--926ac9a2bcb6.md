### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To move the lips in synchronization with sounds (generally recorded) so it appears they are the source of the sounds.

   **Usage:** transitive; singing

   **Example:** She lip-synced to her own recording during the show because of her laryngitis.

   **Example:** There was a great scandal when it was discovered the singer had been only lip-syncing.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music
