### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A connection or association; the condition of being related.

   **Source synonyms:** alliance

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The links between the x-values and y-values of ordered pairs of numbers especially coordinates.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Kinship; being related by blood or marriage.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A romantic or sexual involvement.

   **Example:** They have been in a relationship for ten years, but have never married.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A way in which two or more people behave and are involved with each other.

   **Example:** I have a good working relationship with my boss.

   **Source synonyms:** footing
