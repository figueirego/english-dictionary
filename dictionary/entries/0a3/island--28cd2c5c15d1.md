### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A contiguous area of land, smaller than a continent, totally surrounded by water.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A contiguous area of land, smaller than a continent, partially surrounded by water; a peninsula; a half-island.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** Despite its name, Barry Island is actually a peninsula

   **Source context (verbatim):** in place names

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An entity surrounded by other entities that are very different from itself.

   **Example:** The quiet library was an island of tranquility in the noisy, bustling city.

   **Example:** an island of colors on a butterfly's wing

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A superstructure on an aircraft carrier's deck.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A traffic island.

   **Example:** the island in the middle of a roundabout

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A roundabout; a traffic circle.

   **Usage:** West-Midlands; broadly

   **Example:** Dunton island, near Birmingham, is one of the most confusingly labelled islands in the U.K.

   **Example:** In Coventry, you will often hear people say&#58; “Turn right at the island”.

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A bench, counter, etc., that is not connected to a wall or other furniture and which can be used from any side.
