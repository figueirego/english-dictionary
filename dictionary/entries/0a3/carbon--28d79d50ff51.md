### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The chemical element (symbol C) with an atomic number of 6. It can be found in pure form for example as graphite, a black, shiny and very soft material, or diamond, a colourless, transparent, crystalline solid and the hardest known material.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** All life as we know it has carbon as the backbone of many of its molecules; carbon’s tetravalence gives it special importance in biochemical molecular bonds.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An atom of this element, in reference to a molecule containing it.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** A methane molecule is made up of a single carbon with four hydrogens.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A sheet of carbon paper.

   **Usage:** countable; informal

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A carbon copy.

   **Usage:** countable; informal

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
