## Noun · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈmɪnɪt/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A unit of time which is one sixtieth of an hour (sixty seconds).

   **Example:** You have twenty minutes to complete the test.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A short but unspecified time period.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** give me a minute

   **Example:** Wait a minute, I’m not ready yet!

   **Source synonyms:** instant; jiffy; mo; moment; sec; second; tic; bat of an eye (idiomatic); bit; blink of an eye (idiomatic); crack (British; dated; slang); eyeblink (idiomatic); flash; glimpse (rare); half a mo; instant; jiff; jiffy; jot (obsolete); minute; mo; moment; New York minute; New York second; no time (idiomatic); sec; second; span; split-second; [spurt](../0fa/spurt--3eadf09e0846.md); stound (archaic; dialectal); tick; trice; twink; twinkle; twinkling; while (Philippines)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A unit of angle equal to one-sixtieth of a degree.

   **Example:** We need to be sure these maps are accurate to within one minute of arc.

   **Source synonyms:** minute of arc; sexagesm; arcminute

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A (usually formal) written record of a meeting or a part of a meeting.

   **Usage:** in-plural; minutes

   **Example:** Let’s look at the minutes of last week’s meeting.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A unit of purchase on a telephone or other similar network, especially a cell phone network, roughly equivalent in gross form to sixty seconds' use of the network.

   **Example:** If you buy this model, you’ll get 100 free minutes.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A point in time; a moment.

   **Example:** I conked out the minute I got home.

   **Source synonyms:** instant; moment; instant; juncture; minute; moment; occasion; point in time; sith (obsolete); time

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A nautical or a geographic mile.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. An old coin, a half farthing.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A very small part of anything, or anything very small; a jot; a whit.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. A fixed part of a module.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** architecture

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. A while or a long unspecified period of time.

   **Usage:** Canada; US; dialectal; slang

   **Example:** Oh, I ain't heard that song in a minute!

   **Source synonyms:** age; spell; aeon; age; ages (excessive); blue moon; coon's age (US; idiomatic); crow's age (idiomatic); dog's age (US; idiomatic); donkey's years (Britain; idiomatic); eld (archaic; poetic); eon; eternity (excessive); forever (excessive); forever and a day; interim; interlude; interval; lifetime (excessive); long haul (idiomatic); long run (idiomatic); meantime; minute (slang); month of Sundays; spell; while (US); years (excessive); yonks (Australia; Britain; New-Zealand)
