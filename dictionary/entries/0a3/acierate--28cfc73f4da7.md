### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To convert (iron) into steel, or to coat with a layer of steel by converting only the surface of the iron.

   **Usage:** rare; transitive

   **Example:** The success of an attempt to acierate iron will depend on the amount of nitrogen contained by the iron during carburation.

   **Example:** As an economy measure, the manufacturer decided to use iron rather than steel, but to acierate the surface to limit oxidation.
