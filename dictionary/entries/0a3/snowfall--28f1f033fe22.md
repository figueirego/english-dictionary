### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An instance of falling of snow.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** There has been snowfall every day this week.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The amount of snow that falls on one occasion.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** London recorded a snowfall of 10 centimetres last night.
