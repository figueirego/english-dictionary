### Forms

- more professional — comparative

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Of, pertaining to, or in accordance with the (usually high) standards of a profession.

   **Example:** The doctor is very professional in his manner.

   **Example:** You should get professional advice about your tax liability.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Expert.

   **Usage:** broadly

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. Of an occupation, paid, especially so as to provide a livelihood.

   **Example:** a professional musician, a professional footballer

   **Source antonyms:** amateur

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. Engaging in persistent behaviour that is exaggeratedly implied to be a full-time, paid occupation.

   **Usage:** often; pejorative

   **Example:** Don't listen to him, he's a professional bullshitter.

   **Example:** As a professional optimist, I always look on the bright side.
