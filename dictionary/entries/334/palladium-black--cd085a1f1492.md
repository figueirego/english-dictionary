## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A coarse, sponge-like form of elemental palladium that offers a large surface area for hydrogen adsorption and can be used as a catalyst for hydrogenation reactions.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A black precipitate of elemental palladium, which forms on decomposition of palladium complexes.

   **Usage:** uncountable
