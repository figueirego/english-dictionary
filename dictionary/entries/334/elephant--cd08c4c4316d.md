### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A large mammal of the family Elephantidae in the order Proboscidea, having a trunk, and native to Africa and Asia.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** elephantid

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Anything huge and ponderous.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Synonym of elephant paper.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; paper

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** media; printing; publishing

   **Source synonyms:** elephant paper (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. used when counting to add length, so that each count takes about one second

   **Usage:** UK; childish; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Let's play hide and seek. I'll count. One elephant, two elephant, three elephant...

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Ivory.

   **Usage:** obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A xiangqi piece that is moved two points diagonally, may not jump over intervening pieces and may not cross the river.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
