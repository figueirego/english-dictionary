### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An L-shaped box-moving handcart with handles at one end, wheels at the base, with a small ledge to set objects on, flat against the floor when the hand-truck is upright. It makes otherwise bulky and heavy objects easier to move.

   **Usage:** Canada; US

   **Source synonyms:** bag barrow (New-Zealand); luggage barrow; sack barrow (UK); sack truck (UK); stack truck; trolley; hand trolley; dolly (proscribed; sometimes)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>
