### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. By a small margin.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; degree

   **Example:** I barely completed my homework. However, I got a score of 5 for it.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Almost not at all.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; degree

   **Example:** The plane is so far away now I can barely see it.

   **Example:** Yes, it is barely visible.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Recently, just now.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** I barely got to the store.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Merely.

   **Usage:** archaic; not-comparable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Openly, clearly, plainly.

   **Usage:** comparable; rare
