### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The smallest particle of a specific element or compound that retains the chemical properties of that element or compound; two or more atoms held together by chemical bonds.

   **Example:** Hydrogen chloride is a diatomic molecule, consisting of a hydrogen atom and a chlorine atom.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences
