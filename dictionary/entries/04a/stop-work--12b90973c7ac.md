### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A temporary cessation of work.
   1. Temporary cessation of labor as a form of protest.

      **Usage:** Australia; New-Zealand; attributive; usually

      **Example:** a stop-work meeting

      **Source synonyms:** walkout; strike

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A temporary cessation of work.
   1. Causing work to cease temporarily for administrative reasons such as safety concerns or legal action.

      **Usage:** Canada; US; attributive; usually

      **Example:** a stop-work order.
