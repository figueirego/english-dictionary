### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To press, force, or drive, particularly in filling, or in thrusting one thing into another; to stuff; to fill to superfluity.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to cram fruit into a basket; to cram a room with people

   **Example:** But you still need to check in with the Arch Linux community now and then to make sure that none of their crazy shit is going to be crammed down your throat the next update.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To fill with food to satiety; to stuff.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The boy crammed himself with cake

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To put hastily through an extensive course of memorizing or study, as in preparation for an examination.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** A pupil is crammed by his tutor.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To study hard; to swot.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To eat greedily, and to satiety; to stuff oneself.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To lie; to intentionally not tell the truth.

   **Usage:** British; dated; intransitive; slang

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To make (a person) believe false or exaggerated tales.

   **Usage:** British; dated; slang; transitive
