### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To total or count; to amount to.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** I don’t know how many books are in the library, but they must number in the thousands.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To limit to a certain number; to reckon (as by fate) to be few in number.

   **Usage:** transitive; passive voice

   **Example:** The old man knew that his days were numbered.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To count; to determine the quantity of.

   **Usage:** archaic; literary; transitive

   **Example:** The king ordered that all his subjects be numbered.

   **Example:** Who can number all the stars and who can count the desert sands?

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To label (items) with numbers; to assign numbers to (items).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Number the baskets so that we can find them easily.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To call out and assign a series of numbers (usually to people), either for the sake of dividing into groups or for counting.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Shelley numbered off the group into two teams for the baseball game.

   **Source context (verbatim):** with off

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To enumerate or list, especially while assigning numbers to.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source context (verbatim):** with off

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

**Source sense R2.S7**

7. To classify or include (in a group of things).

   **Usage:** transitive; usually

   **Example:** Alexander the Great's army numbered an elite cavalry among its ranks.

   **Source context (verbatim):** with among

<a id="r2-s8"></a>

**Source sense R2.S8**

8. To be classified or included (in a certain group or category of things).

   **Usage:** intransitive; usually

   **Example:** Her horses number among the fastest in her country.

   **Source context (verbatim):** with among
