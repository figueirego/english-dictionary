### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any animal whose teeth are continuously replaced.

   **Example:** Elephants and manatees are unusual among mammals because they are polyphyodonts. Most mammals are diphyodonts, replacing their teeth only once in their lives.
