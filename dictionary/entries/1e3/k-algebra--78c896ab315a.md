### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An algebra over a field; a ring with identity together with an injective ring homomorphism from a field, k, to the ring such that the image of the field is a subset of the center of the ring and such that the image of the field’s unity is the ring’s unity.
