### Pronunciation

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of two (or, rarely, more than two) things&#58; smaller in size (littler), in value, in importance etc.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Of the two issues, this one is of lesser importance.

   **Source antonyms:** greater

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of two (or, rarely, more than two) things&#58; smaller in size (littler), in value, in importance etc.
   1. In plant and animal names, denotes a smaller kind.

      **Usage:** not-comparable

      **Example:** lesser panda

      **Example:** lesser yellowlegs

      **Source antonyms:** greater

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of two (or, rarely, more than two) things&#58; smaller in size (littler), in value, in importance etc.
   1. Denotes the smaller geographic or administrative area that encompasses or belongs to a place.

      **Usage:** not-comparable

      **Example:** Lesser Antilles

      **Source antonyms:** greater
