## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The principle that the courts should not interpret any statute as curtailing or abrogating a common law right, freedom, or principle, unless the legislative body that made that statute clearly communicated the intention to impose such a restriction.
