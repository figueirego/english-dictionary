### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To abolish; to put an end to; to eliminate.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; informal; transitive

   **Example:** She tried to do away with the argument by changing the topic.

   **Example:** The criminals tried to do away with the evidence before the police arrived.

   **Example:** She’s working hard to do away with negative thinking.

   **Source synonyms:** expunge

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To have someone killed.

   **Usage:** euphemistic; idiomatic; informal; transitive

   **Example:** He plotted to do away with his rival.
