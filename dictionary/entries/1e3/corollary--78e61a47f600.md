### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A gift beyond what is actually due; an addition or superfluity.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An a fortiori occurrence, as a result of another effort without significant additional effort.

   **Example:** Finally getting that cracked window fixed was a nice corollary of redoing the whole storefront.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A proposition which follows easily from the statement or proof of another proposition.

   **Example:** We have proven that this set is finite and well ordered; as a corollary, we now know that there is an order-preserving map from it to the natural numbers.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; logic; mathematics; philosophy; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** concomitant

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A proposition which follows easily from the statement or proof of another proposition.
   1. A product or byproduct of an assertion following easily from another stated or proven assertion.

      **Usage:** metaphorical

      **Example:** One could say artificial intelligence is a corollary of humans; it was made by humans, it was trained on human products, and it was made for humans.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; logic; mathematics; philosophy; sciences

      **Source synonyms:** concomitant
