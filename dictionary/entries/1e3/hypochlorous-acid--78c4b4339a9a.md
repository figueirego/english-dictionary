### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A weak, unstable acid, HOCl, known only in solution; it is made by the action of chlorine on water and, along with its salts, the hypochlorites, occurs naturally in biology and has various practical uses in disinfection.

   **Usage:** uncountable
