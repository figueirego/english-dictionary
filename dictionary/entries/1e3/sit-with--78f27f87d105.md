### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To consider (something) slowly and carefully, to reflect on.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To accept, observe and experience a phenomenon, usually an emotion or feeling.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** My therapist said I should sit with these fears and see how it feels to let myself experience them.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To be harmonious with (something).

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I'm afraid his personality doesn't sit with the rest of the team.
