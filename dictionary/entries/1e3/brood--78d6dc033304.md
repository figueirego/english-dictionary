### Senses

<a id="r3-s1"></a>

**Source sense R3.S1**

1. To keep an egg warm to make it hatch.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** In some species of birds, both the mother and father brood the eggs.

<a id="r3-s2"></a>

**Source sense R3.S2**

2. To protect (something that is gradually maturing); to foster.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Under the rock was a midshipman fish, brooding a mass of eggs.

<a id="r3-s3"></a>

**Source sense R3.S3**

3. (typically with over, on or about) To dwell upon one's thoughts moodily and at length, mainly alone.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** He sat brooding over the upcoming battle, fearing the outcome.

<a id="r3-s4"></a>

**Source sense R3.S4**
