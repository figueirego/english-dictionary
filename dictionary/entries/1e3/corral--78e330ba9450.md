### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An enclosure for livestock, especially a circular one.

   **Example:** We had a small corral out back where we kept our pet llama.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An enclosure or area to concentrate a dispersed group.

   **Example:** Please return the shopping carts to the corral.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A circle of wagons, either for the purpose of trapping livestock, or for defense.

   **Example:** The wagon train formed a corral to protect against Comanche attacks.

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To capture or round up.

   **Example:** Between us, we managed to corral the puppies in the kitchen.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To place inside of a corral.

   **Example:** After we corralled the last steer, we headed off to the chuck wagon for dinner.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To make a circle of vehicles, as of wagons so as to form a corral.

   **Example:** The cattle drivers corralled their wagons for the night.
