## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Not affected by climatic conditions; capable of normal operation regardless of precipitation, wind, sun, etc.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** an all-weather tire

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Always there when needed, reliable, dependable, loyal, faithful.

   **Usage:** figuratively; not-comparable

   **Example:** an all-weather friend
