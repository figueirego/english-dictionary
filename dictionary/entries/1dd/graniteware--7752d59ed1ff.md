### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Ironware that has been coated with a layer of vitreous enamel with a swirled or speckled pattern resembling granite.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
