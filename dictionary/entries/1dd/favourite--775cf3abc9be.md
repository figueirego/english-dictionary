## Noun · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈfeɪv.ɹɪt/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Forms

- favorite — alternative; US

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A person or thing who enjoys special regard or favour.

   **Usage:** Commonwealth; Ireland; UK

   **Example:** The teacher's favourite always went first.

   **Source synonyms:** first love

   **Source antonyms:** unfavourite; unfavorite

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. A person who is preferred or trusted above all others.

   **Usage:** Commonwealth; Ireland; UK

   **Source synonyms:** top dog; darling

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. A contestant or competitor thought most likely to win.

   **Usage:** Commonwealth; Ireland; UK

   **Example:** You were my favourite to win the spelling competition.

   **Example:** She's the favourite, she'll probably be elected.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. A bookmark on a web browser or website.

   **Usage:** Commonwealth; Internet; Ireland; UK

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. A short curl dangling over the temples, fashionable in the reign of Charles II.

   **Usage:** Commonwealth; Ireland; UK; in-plural
