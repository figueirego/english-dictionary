### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A series of threads or other projections resembling a fringe.
   1. An individual thread in a fimbria, especially a fingerlike projection around the ovarian end of the Fallopian tube.

      **Usage:** plural-normally

      **Example:** When a follicle is mature, the egg within it bursts out of the ovary, and the Fallopian tube's fingerlike fimbria reach out and grab it.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; biology; medicine; natural-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A series of threads or other projections resembling a fringe.
   1. A hairlike appendage found on the cell surface of many bacteria; used by the bacteria to adhere to one another, to animal cells and to some inanimate objects.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** bacteriology; biology; microbiology; natural-sciences

      **Source synonyms:** pilus
