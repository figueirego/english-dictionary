### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The alimentary canal, especially the intestine.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The abdomen of a person, especially one that is enlarged.

   **Usage:** countable; informal; uncountable

   **Example:** You've developed quite a beer gut since I last met you.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The intestines of an animal used to make strings of a tennis racket or violin, etc.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A person's emotional, visceral self.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** I have a funny feeling in my gut.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A class that is not demanding or challenging.

   **Usage:** countable; informal; uncountable

   **Example:** You should take Intro Astronomy&#58; it's a gut.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A narrow channel, passage, or stream of water. (Compare gote.)

   **Usage:** countable; especially; uncountable

   **Example:** the Gut of Canso

   **Source context (verbatim):** in place names

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The sac of silk taken from a silkworm when ready to spin its cocoon, for the purpose of drawing it out into a thread. When dry, it is exceedingly strong, and is used as the snood of a fishing line.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
