### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To pass (strands of material) over and under one another to create a fabric; (by analogy) to weave long, narrow objects together.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** the interthreading of warp and weft

   **Source synonyms:** interlace; intertwine; interweave; betwine; braid; entwist; entwine; interlace; interthread; intertwine; intertwist; interweave; lace; lock; raddle; ruddle; twine; twist

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To alter a fabric by weaving additional strands into it; to bring (two or more things) together like the strands in fabric; to bring (one thing) together (with another thing).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** silk fabrics interthreaded with gold and silver

   **Source synonyms:** blend; combine; mix

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To be present in every part of (something) like strands running through it.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** crisscross; pervade; run through

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To integrate (strands of material into a fabric) by weaving.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To be or become woven or twisted together (with something); to be or become inextricably associated like strands woven or twisted together.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To move alternately on either side of people or objects; to weave in and out.

   **Usage:** intransitive
