### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A punch delivered in response to a previous punch by somebody else, such as an opponent in a boxing match&#58; a return blow, a retaliatory blow.

   **Source synonyms:** counterblow; counterattack; counterstrike

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A punch used in the cutting of other punches, often used to create the negative space in or around a glyph.

   **Usage:** historical

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** media; printing; publishing
