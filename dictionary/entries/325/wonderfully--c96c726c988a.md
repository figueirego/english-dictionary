### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In a wonderful manner.
   1. In an excellent manner.

      **Example:** He entertained us wonderfully.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In a wonderful manner.
   1. To an extent inspiring wonder.

      **Example:** On a clear night, the Montana sky is wonderfully vast and full of stars.

      **Example:** It was wonderfully stormy and dark on the night when the ship went down.

      **Source synonyms:** wondrously
