### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A device that vibrates or causes vibration.
   1. A vibrating device used for massage or sexual stimulation.

      **Example:** They recommend a vibrator because it's a safe alternative to sex.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A device that vibrates or causes vibration.
   1. A device designed to electromechanically interrupt current flowing to the step-up transformer which was used to generate the high tension positive supply in old battery-operated (automotive) valve radios.

      **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A device that vibrates or causes vibration.
   1. A trembler, as of an electric bell.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A device that vibrates or causes vibration.
   1. A vibrating reed for transmitting or receiving pulsating currents in a harmonic telegraph system.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A device that vibrates or causes vibration.
   1. A device for vibrating the pen of a siphon recorder to diminish frictional resistance on the paper.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A device that vibrates or causes vibration.
   1. An oscillator.

**Source sense R1.S7**

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A device that vibrates or causes vibration.
   1. A vibrating reed in a musical instrument, especially a reed organ.

9. A device that vibrates or causes vibration.
   1. Any of various vibrating devices, such as one for slackening the warp as a shed opens.
