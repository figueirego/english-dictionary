## Adjective · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /bæˈɹɒk/ — source labels: UK
- IPA: /bəˈɹoʊk/ — source labels: US

### Forms

- baroquer — comparative
- baroquest — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Ornate, intricate, decorated, laden with detail.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Complex and beautiful, despite an outward irregularity.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Chiseled from stone, or shaped from wood, in a garish, crooked, twisted, or slanted sort of way, grotesque.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Embellished with figures and forms such that every level of relief gives way to more details and contrasts.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Characteristic of Western art and music of the Early Modern period.

   **Usage:** capitalized; sometimes

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Overly and needlessly complicated.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** grotesquely baroque bureaucratic hassles

   **Example:** baroque bookkeeping in pursuit of tax dodges
