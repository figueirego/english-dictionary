### Forms

- hisses — present; singular; third-person
- hissing — participle; present
- hissed — participle; past
- hissed — past

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To make a hiss, a sibilant sound of air escaping.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** As I started to poke it, the snake hissed at me.

   **Example:** The poor cat hissed as it backed away from the approaching man.

   **Source synonyms:** fizzle; fizz

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To call someone by hissing.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To condemn or express contempt (for someone or something) by hissing.

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** The crowd booed and hissed her off the stage.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To utter (something) with a hissing sound.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To move with a hissing sound.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The arrow hissed through the air.

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To emit or eject (something) with a hissing sound.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

**Source sense R2.S7**

7. To whisper, especially angrily or urgently.

   **Usage:** transitive
