### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see squeeze, out.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Squeeze out some toothpaste and put it on your toothbrush.

   **Example:** She squeezed out another kid, apparently, since we last saw her. \[derogatory, informal\]

   **Source synonyms:** push out

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. (sports) To force (a competitor) out of one of a limited number of winning positions by taking over that position or a higher one.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive; usually

   **Example:** I auditioned and I would have had the role, but I got squeezed out by a more experienced actor.

   **Example:** They came from nowhere to take over second place, squeezing out the previously eighth-place team.

   **Source synonyms:** force out

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. (business) To oust (someone, especially shareholders).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** force out; push out

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To obtain (a difficult victory) in a competition.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The team managed to squeeze out a win in the final minutes.

   **Example:** The team managed to squeeze it out.

   **Source synonyms:** eke out
