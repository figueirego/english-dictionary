### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Unkempt, shabby, or in a state of disrepair.

   **Example:** He liked to wear an old ragtag coat that was so threadbare that he'd get sunburned through it.

   **Source synonyms:** bedraggled; decrepit; motheaten; tattered

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Very diverse; having irregular and dissimilar components.

   **Example:** The guerrillas were a ragtag band of local thugs, former soldiers, displaced farmers, and political idealists.

   **Source synonyms:** motley; jumbled; patchwork; uneven
