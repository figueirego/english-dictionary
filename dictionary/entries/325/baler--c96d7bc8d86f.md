## Noun

### Forms

- balers — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A machine for creating bales, e.g., of hay or cotton.

   **Example:** Farmer Jones is in straits because his baler is broken down and he's got three fields of hay ready to bale.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person who creates bales, either by operating or feeding such a machine, or by creating the bales by hand.

   **Example:** Hank worked as a baler for Farmer Jones part time, to make ends meet.
