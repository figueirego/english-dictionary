### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To narrow, especially by application of pressure.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** constrict the airway

   **Example:** constrict blood vessels

   **Example:** Clothing that is too tight can constrict blood flow.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To coil around (prey) in order to asphyxiate it. (of a snake)

   **Example:** The snake began to constrict its prey.
