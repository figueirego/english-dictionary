### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To hinder, impede, or prevent.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To bring a legal proceeding against a public official.

   **Example:** President Clinton was impeached by the House in November 1998, but since the Senate acquitted him, he was not removed from office.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To charge with impropriety; to discredit; to call into question.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To demonstrate in court that a testimony under oath contradicts another testimony from the same person, usually one taken during deposition.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law
