### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of plant parts&#58; extending downward, most often in the case of leaf blades that partly wrap or have wings around the stem or petiole and extend down along the stem.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of lamellae (the gills of a mushroom)&#58; broadly attached and extending down the stipe of the mushroom.

   **Example:** In this group of mushrooms, the attachment of the gills to the stipe is decurrent.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; mycology; natural-sciences
