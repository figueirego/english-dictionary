<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To engulf, surge over and submerge.

   **Example:** The dinghy was overwhelmed by the great wave.

   **Source synonyms:** swamp

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To overpower, crush.

   **Example:** In December 1939 the Soviet Union attacked Finland with overwhelming force.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To overpower emotionally.

   **Example:** He was overwhelmed with guilt.

   **Example:** Joy overwhelmed her when she realized that she had won a million dollars.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

4. To cause to surround, to cover.
