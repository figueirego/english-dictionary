### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who rubbernecks; someone who cranes their neck as though it were made of rubber to see something (such as a tourist attraction) or to watch an event (such as an accident); a rubberneck.

   **Example:** The minor accident caused a traffic jam as rubberneckers slowed down to get a good view of the battered cars.

   **Source synonyms:** gaper; gawker; gawper; onlooker; spectator; starer
