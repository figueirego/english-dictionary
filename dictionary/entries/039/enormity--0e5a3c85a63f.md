### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Deviation from what is normal or standard; irregularity, abnormality.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Deviation from moral normality; extreme wickedness, nefariousness, or cruelty.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Not until the war ended and journalists were able to enter Cambodia did the world really become aware of the enormity of Pol Pot’s oppression.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A breach of law or morality; a transgression, an act of evil or wickedness.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Great size; enormousness, hugeness, immenseness.

   **Usage:** proscribed; sometimes; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** ginormity; size; bigness; bulk; largeness; magnitude; scale
