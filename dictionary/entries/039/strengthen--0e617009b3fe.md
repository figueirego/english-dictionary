### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make strong or stronger; to add strength to; to increase the strength of; to fortify.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** strengthen a muscle

   **Example:** strengthen a wall

   **Example:** strengthen one's willpower

   **Example:** strengthen one's authority

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To empower; to give moral strength to; to encourage; to enhearten.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To augment; to improve; to intensify.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To reinforce, to add to, to support (someone or something)

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** strengthen an army

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To substantiate; to corroborate (a belief, argument, etc.)

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** strengthen the cause

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To grow strong or stronger.

   **Usage:** intransitive
