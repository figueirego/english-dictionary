### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Someone who watches or observes.
   1. Someone who observes something closely for professional reasons, such as an analyst or pundit.

      **Source context (verbatim):** as the final element in compounds

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Someone who watches or observes.
   1. A political representative aligned with a candidate sent to observe elections to report on irregularities. A scrutineer.

      **Usage:** US

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; politics

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Someone who keeps vigil.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

   **Source synonyms:** watchman; watchwoman

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**
