### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person or company that dismantles old or wrecked vehicles or other items, to reclaim useful parts.

   **Usage:** Australia

   **Source synonyms:** breaker; knacker; salvager; salvor; scrapper; scrap dealer; shipbreaker; car breaker

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One who breaks up situations or events.

   **Example:** home wrecker / marriage wrecker / party wrecker

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A tow truck.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A mooncusser (type of land-based pirate).

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Someone accused of the formal charge of wrecking; that is, of undermining the state in intangible ways.

   **Usage:** historical; Soviet Union; crime; Soviet Union; crime
