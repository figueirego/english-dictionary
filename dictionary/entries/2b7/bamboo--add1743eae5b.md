### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of the fast-growing grasses of the Bambusoideae subfamily, characterized by its woody, hollow, round, straight, jointed stem.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Wood of the bamboo plant as a material for building, furniture, etc.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A stick, rod, pole, or cane of bamboo, especially one used as a vessel for liquids, or as a tool for corporal punishment.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A didgeridoo.

   **Usage:** countable; slang; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A member of the British military or British East India Company who spent so much time in Indonesia, India, or Malaysia that they never returned home.

   **Usage:** countable; slang; uncountable
