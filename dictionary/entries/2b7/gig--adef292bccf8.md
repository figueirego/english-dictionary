### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Originally (music), a performing engagement by a musician or musical group; (by extension, film, television, theater) a job or role for a performer.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** I caught one of the Rolling Stones’ first gigs in Richmond.

   **Example:** Hey, when are we gonna get that hotel gig again?

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any job, especially one that is freelance or temporary, or done on an on-demand basis.

   **Usage:** broadly; informal

   **Example:** I had this gig as a file clerk but it wasn’t my style so I left.

   **Example:** That guy’s got a great gig over at the bike shop. He hardly works all day.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A demerit received for some infraction of a military deportment or dress code.

   **Usage:** US; informal

   **Example:** I received gigs for having buttons of my uniform undone.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war
