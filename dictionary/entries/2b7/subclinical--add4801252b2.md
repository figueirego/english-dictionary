## Adjective

### Forms

- sub-clinical — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Without signs and symptoms that are detectable by physical examination or laboratory test; not clinically manifest.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Some conditions may remain subclinical until they resolve without intervention, whereas others worsen from subclinical to clinical status.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a disease or injury

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Where some criteria are met but not enough to reach the status of a clinical diagnosis.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

   **Source context (verbatim):** in diagnosis

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Less than is needed for clinical reasons.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Off-label use of certain drugs at subclinical dosages has been a theme in realms of activity such as metabolic optimization, weight loss, body hacking, and neurohacking (e.g., nootropics); such drugs include metformin, beta-blockers, and others.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a dosage
