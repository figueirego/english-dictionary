### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To form a line; to fall into line.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The pedestrians aligned in such a way that from above they made a pyramidal pattern.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To adjust to a line; to range or form in line; to bring into line.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To organize in a consistent, defined way, perhaps in an abstract sense.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To identify (oneself) with, match, or support the behaviour, thoughts, etc. of another person, organization, or country.

   **Usage:** intransitive; reflexive

   **Source synonyms:** ally; support; side with

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To store (data) in a way that is consistent with the memory architecture, i.e. by beginning each item at an offset equal to some multiple of the word size.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To organize a linear arrangement of DNA, RNA, or protein sequences which have regions of similarity.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** bioinformatics; biology; computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To identify entities that refer to the same real-world object in different knowledge bases.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; human-sciences; information-science; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences
