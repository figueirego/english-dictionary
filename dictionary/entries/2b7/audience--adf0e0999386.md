### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A group of people within hearing; specifically, a large gathering of people listening to or watching a performance, speech, etc.

   **Example:** We joined the audience just as the lights went down.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Hearing; the condition or state of hearing or listening.

   **Usage:** archaic

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A widespread or nationwide viewing or listening public, as of a TV or radio network or program.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A formal meeting with a state or religious dignitary.

   **Example:** She managed to get an audience with the Pope.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The readership of a book or other written publication.

   **Example:** "Private Eye" has a small but faithful audience.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A following.

   **Example:** The opera singer expanded his audience by singing songs from the shows.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. An audiencia (judicial court of the Spanish empire), or the territory administered by it.

   **Usage:** historical
