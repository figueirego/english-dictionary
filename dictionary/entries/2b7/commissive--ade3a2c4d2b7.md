## Adjective · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. By commission; resulting from a positive act.

   **Example:** This error was commissive rather than omissive.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Making a commitment, such as a promise or threat, by illocutionary means.

   **Example:** "I will kill you if you do that" is a commissive statement.
