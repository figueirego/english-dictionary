### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The equilibrium between the input and output of water in a specific area or system over a given time period.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; geography; hydrology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An antiquated gravity-powered mechanism used to raise heavy loads (such as minerals in a mine shaft) by utilizing the weight of water.
