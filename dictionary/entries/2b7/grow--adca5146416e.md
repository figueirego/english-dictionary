### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To become larger, to increase in magnitude.

   **Usage:** ergative

   **Example:** Children grow quickly.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To undergo growth; to be present (somewhere).

   **Usage:** ergative

   **Example:** Apples now grow all over the world. The tallest trees grow to about 30 feet.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of plants

   **Source synonyms:** range

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To appear or sprout.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Leaf buds grew on the trees with the advance of spring.

   **Example:** A long tail began to grow from his backside.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To develop, to mature.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** As I grew throughout adolescence, I came to appreciate many things about human nature.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To cause or allow something to become bigger, especially to cultivate plants.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He grows peppers and squash each summer in his garden.

   **Example:** Have you ever grown your hair before?

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To assume a condition or quality over time.

   **Usage:** copulative

   **Example:** The boy grew wise as he matured.

   **Example:** My cares grew less (and less) until they evanesced completely.

   **Example:** You have grown strong.

   **Source synonyms:** become; get; go; turn; come; fall; wax

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To become attached or fixed; to adhere.

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete
