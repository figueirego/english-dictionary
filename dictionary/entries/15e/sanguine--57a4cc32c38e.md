### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having the colour of blood; blood red.

   **Usage:** literary

   **Source synonyms:** incarnadine

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Having a bodily constitution characterised by a preponderance of blood over the other bodily humours, thought to be marked by irresponsible mirth; indulgent in pleasure to the exclusion of important matters.

   **Usage:** obsolete

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; physiology; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** debaucherous; sybaritic

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Characterized by abundance and active circulation of blood.

   **Example:** a sanguine bodily temperament

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Warm; ardent.

   **Example:** a sanguine temper

   **Source synonyms:** animated; buoyant; spirited

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Anticipating the best; optimistic; confident; full of hope.

   **Example:** I'm sanguine about the eventual success of the project.

   **Source synonyms:** assured; bright; bullish; cheerful; cheery; confident; hopeful; optimistic; positive; ridibund; upbeat

   **Source antonyms:** blue; despondent; gloomy; pessimistic

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Full of blood; bloody.

   **Usage:** archaic

   **Source synonyms:** bloodied; gory; sanguinolent

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Bloodthirsty.

   **Usage:** archaic
