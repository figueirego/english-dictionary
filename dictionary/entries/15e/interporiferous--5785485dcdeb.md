**Source sense R1.S1**

1. lying between poriferous areas

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The poriferous zone at its broadest part is five eighths of the width of the interporiferous area. The interporiferous zone is widest at the margin, and is there a little more than twice the width of the broadest part of the poriferous zone.
