## Noun

### Forms

- special interests — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A particular political issue that is the focus of political advocacy.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A self-serving group with political power; especially, a shadowy one.

   **Source synonyms:** lobby

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A particular highly focused interest of an autistic person.

   **Example:** I'm dating a boy called Nick whose special interest is video games; he won't stop talking about Pokémon.

   **Source synonyms:** spin; spinterest
