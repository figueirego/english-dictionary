**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of or relating to Vigilantius (fl. c. 400), Christian presbyter who wrote a work, now lost, that opposed a number of common fifth-century practices, and which inspired one of the most violent of the polemical treatises of Jerome (died 420).

   **Usage:** historical
