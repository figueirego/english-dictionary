**Source sense R1.S1**

1. (in Lie superalgebra theory) A finite-dimensional Lie superalgebra equipped with a cobracket that is a graded one-cocycle for the adjoint action and whose dual defines a Lie superbracket.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

   **Extractor diagnostics (not usage labels):** empty-gloss; no-gloss

**Supplemental definition (evidence reviewed; source gap preserved):** The quoted work is specifically about Lie superbialgebras. The gloss explicitly confines itself to that Lie setting; it does not conflate the construction with an associative graded bialgebra.
