<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; slang; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The assimilation of borrowed lexes, either partial or whole, to word forms of the borrowing language.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>
