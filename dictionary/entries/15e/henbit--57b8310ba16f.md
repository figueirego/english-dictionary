### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An annual herb, of species Lamium amplexicaule, with pink or purple flowers and deeply crenate leaves.

   **Source synonyms:** henbit deadnettle; common henbit; greater henbit

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Other species of plant that resemble L. amplexicaule, especially related species of deadnettle in the genus Lamium.

   **Usage:** broadly
