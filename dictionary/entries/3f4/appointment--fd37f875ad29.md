### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of appointing a person to hold an office or to have a position of trust.

   **Example:** His appointment as treasurer was deemed suitable.

   **Source synonyms:** designation; tenure (formal)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The state of being appointed to a service or office; an office to which one is appointed.

   **Example:** the appointment of treasurer

   **Source synonyms:** tenure; formal

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A stipulation; an agreement; the act of fixing by mutual agreement.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An arrangement between people to meet; an engagement.

   **Example:** They made an appointment to meet at six.

   **Example:** I’m leaving work early because I have a doctor’s appointment.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A decree; a direction; an established order or constitution.

   **Example:** to submit to the divine appointments

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** lifestyle; religion

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The exercise of the power of designating (under a power of appointment) a person to enjoy an estate or other specific property; also, the instrument by which the designation is made.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The assignment of a person by an official to perform a duty, such as a presidential appointment of a judge to a court.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Equipment, furniture.

   **Usage:** in-plural

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. An honorary part or exercise, as an oration, etc., at a public exhibition of a college.

   **Usage:** US

   **Example:** to have an appointment

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. The allowance paid to a public officer.

   **Usage:** obsolete
