### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Makeup that makes the face appear white.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Whiteface is sometimes worn by clowns and mimes, or in the Gothic subculture.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any bird of the genus Aphelocephala.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
