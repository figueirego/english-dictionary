### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To moan or complain about (something).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He bemoaned the drought but went on watering his lawn.

   **Source synonyms:** bewail; lament; mourn; beef; bellyache; bemoan; bitch; bleat; whimper; carp; chunter; complain; crab; croak; dump on; frine; girn; give out; grumble; grotch; grouse; grouch; grump; gruntle; gripe; harp; inveigh; kvetch; latrate (obsolete); make a stink; murmur; mutter; moan; nag; nark; nudzh; pinch (obsolete); piss and moan; rail; rail out; repine (literary); ride; snivel; whine; whinge

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To be dismayed or worried about (someone), particularly because of their situation or what has happened to them.

   **Usage:** reflexive; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** grieve; lament; afterthink; becry; begrieve (archaic; rare); bemoan; bemourn (rare); bewail; beweep; condole (India); deplore; elegize; grieve; grieven; keen; lament; mean (Ireland; UK); mourn; pine; repent; sigh; sorrow; wail; wayment (obsolete)
