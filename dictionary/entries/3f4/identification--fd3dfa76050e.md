### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of identifying (i.e., which one, which thing).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; strictly

   **Example:** In many network protocols, identification is complemented by authentication.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The combination of identifying (which one, which thing) and validating the identity (proving or confirming it); identifying and authenticating viewed as a unitary concept in cognition.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; often; uncountable

   **Example:** Much education and experience is required for proper identification of bird species.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The state of being identified.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The problem's identification was the first step toward dealing with it.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A particular instance of identifying something.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A document or documents serving as evidence of a person's identity.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The authorities asked for his identification.

   **Source synonyms:** ID; documents; papers

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A feeling of support, sympathy, understanding, or belonging towards somebody or something.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** identification with the tribe
