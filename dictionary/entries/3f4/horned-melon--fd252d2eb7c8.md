### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A plant of the species Cucumis metulifer, bearing a cucumber-like fruit.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The fruit of the horned melon plant, having yellow-orange skin and lime green, jelly-like flesh with a tart taste, and texture similar to a cucumber.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
