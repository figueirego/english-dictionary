### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The husband of one's biological mother after her initial marriage to or relationship with one's biological father.

   **Source context (verbatim):** in the narrow sense

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The husband of one's parent distinct from one's biological father or person with a similar relationship.

   **Source context (verbatim):** in the broad sense
