### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of, pertaining to, or resembling the Moon (that is, Luna, the Earth's moon).

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** lunar observations a lunar eclipse

   **Source synonyms:** lunarlike; lunary (obsolete); moonish; moonlike; moonly; moony; selenian; selenic

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Shaped like a crescent moon; lunate.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Extremely high.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. (Believed to be) influenced by the Moon, as in character, growth, or properties.

   **Usage:** historical; not-comparable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Of or pertaining to silver (which was symbolically associated with the Moon by alchemists).

   **Usage:** not-comparable; obsolete

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** alchemy; chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; pseudoscience

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Of or pertaining to travel through space between the Earth and the Moon, or exploration and scientific investigation of the Moon.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astronomy; natural-sciences

**Source sense R2.S1**

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; medicine; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** intermedium; lunar bone; semilunar
