## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A boundary layer in a body of water between areas of different temperature or salinity.

   **Example:** The bay has fresher water at the surface and saltier water at the bottom, in between is the pycnocline.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A layer of water where the density changes rapidly with depth
