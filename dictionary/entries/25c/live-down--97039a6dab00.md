### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To live so as to subdue or refute.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** to live down slander

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To cause others to forget about something embarrassing that happened to oneself.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I'll never be able to live down that time my pants fell down at the school dance.

   **Source context (verbatim):** in the negative
