## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The belief of certain Catholics, primarily during the period immediately prior to Vatican I, that papal infallibility was not restricted to a small number of papal statements but applied ipso facto (by virtue of being said by the Pope) to all papal teachings and statements.

   **Usage:** uncountable
