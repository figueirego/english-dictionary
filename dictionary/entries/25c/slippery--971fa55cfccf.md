### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of a surface, having low friction, often due to being covered in a non-viscous liquid, and therefore hard to grip, hard to stand on without falling, etc.

   **Example:** Oily substances render things slippery.

   **Source synonyms:** greasy; slick; slimy; slippy; wet; slithery

   **Source antonyms:** sticky

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Evasive; difficult to pin down.

   **Usage:** broadly; figuratively

   **Example:** a slippery person

   **Example:** a slippery promise

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Liable to slip; not standing firm.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Unstable; changeable; inconstant.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**
