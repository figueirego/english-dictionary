### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A hard, calcareous structure present in the mouth of many vertebrate animals, generally used for biting and chewing food.

   **Example:** tooth decay

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A sharp projection on the blade of a saw or similar implement.

   **Source synonyms:** sawtooth

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A projection on the edge of a gear that meshes with similar projections on adjacent gears, or on the circumference of a cog that engages with a chain.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The property of resisting slippage when knotted.

   **Example:** Jute has more tooth than polypropylene.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A projection or point in other parts of the body resembling the tooth of a vertebrate animal.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences; zoology

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A pointed projection from the margin of a leaf.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The rough surface of some kinds of cel or other films that allows better adhesion of artwork.

   **Usage:** animation

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Liking, fondness (compare toothsome).

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** I have a sweet tooth&#58; I love sugary treats.

   **Source synonyms:** fondness; appetite; taste; palate

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. An irreducible component of a comb that intersects the handle in exactly one point, that point being distinct from the unique point of intersection for any other tooth of the comb.
