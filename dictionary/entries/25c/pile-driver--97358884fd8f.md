## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

- pile drivers — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A machine for forcing a pile, a long beam, into the ground as part of the construction of a foundation; usually by raising a weight and then dropping it on the beam.

   **Source synonyms:** monkey hammer

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person or thing who hits or attacks forcefully or powerfully.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** She scored a goal with a pile driver from the edge of the penalty box
