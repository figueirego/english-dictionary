1. A predicted (and disputed) implosion of established SaaS development companies as a result of emerging commercialized AI being fundamentally disruptive to the industry.

   **Example:** Some investors are quick to dump SaaS stocks because they predict a SaaSpocalypse, whereas others believe that AI is changing how SaaS companies do their work without making those companies unnecessary or unviable.
