### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Offering a conclusion with seriously deficient support in fact or logic; begging the question.

   **Example:** These claims are conclusory and unsupported by any specific allegations, let alone evidence.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Serving as or relating to a conclusion; concluding, postfatory.

   **Usage:** uncommon

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
