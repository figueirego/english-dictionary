### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A good thing; a thing to be thankful for or to appreciate duly.

   **Example:** Finding the dry cave was a boon to the weary travellers.

   **Example:** Anaesthetics are a great boon to modern surgery.

   **Source synonyms:** gift; blessing; benefit; benefaction; boon (archaic); concession; donative (archaic); fairing (rare); gift; grant; present; tithing (obsolete)

   **Source antonyms:** bane

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A prayer; petition.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

   **Usage:** UK; dialectal

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**
