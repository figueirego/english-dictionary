## Noun

### Forms

- bubble baths — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A bath in which an additive, usually soap, is poured directly into the water that creates bubbles; a foam bath.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The product poured in the bath to create those bubbles.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A laugh.

   **Usage:** Cockney; countable; slang; uncountable

   **Example:** Are you having a bubble bath?!

   **Source synonyms:** tin bath; giraffe; bubble
