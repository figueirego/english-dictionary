## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An extra full moon that appears in a subdivision of a year, namely&#58;
   1. The third full moon in a season that contains four rather than the usual three full moons.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An extra full moon that appears in a subdivision of a year, namely&#58;
   1. The second of two full moons that occur in the same calendar month (a definition created by mistake in the 20th century).

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The moon tinted towards blue as it appears in the sky, caused by dust or smoke in the atmosphere.

   **Usage:** countable; rare

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A long time.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; idiomatic

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A bright blue, sweet-flavored ice cream that is popular in the Midwestern U.S. and is a component of Superman ice cream.

   **Usage:** uncountable
