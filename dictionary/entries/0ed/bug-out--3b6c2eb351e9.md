## Verb · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To leave (a place) hastily.

   **Usage:** intransitive; slang

   **Example:** It's time I bugged out of this town&#58; it ain't safe no more.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To leave (a place) hastily.
   1. To abandon someone without warning.

      **Usage:** intransitive; slang

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To leave (a place) hastily.
   1. To miss school, play truant, play hooky.

      **Usage:** intransitive; slang

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To leave (a place) hastily.
   1. To leave civilization to live off the grid; to escape an apocalypse or emergency by leaving the area.

      **Usage:** intransitive; slang

      **Source antonyms:** bug in

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To cause to bulge.

   **Usage:** slang; transitive

   **Example:** Kim Goodman holds the world record for bugging out her eyes.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of one's eyes

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To bulge; to protrude.

   **Usage:** intransitive; slang

   **Example:** The 3D imagery will make your eyes bug out.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of eyes

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To freak out, to go crazy, e.g. from worry.

   **Usage:** intransitive; slang

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To crash or glitch.

   **Usage:** intransitive; slang

   **Example:** My PC keeps bugging out and rebooting for no reason.
