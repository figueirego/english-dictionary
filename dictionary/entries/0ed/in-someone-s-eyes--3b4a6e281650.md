## Prep\_phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Shining very brightly and directly at the face so that it is difficult to see because one is blinded by the light.

   **Example:** He couldn't see the traffic light because the sun was in his eyes.

   **Example:** I lost my night vision for ten minutes because Kevin stupidly shone his flashlight right in my eyes.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. According to someone's subjective point of view.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** In her eyes, there's nothing wrong with a cloud of cannabis smoke on a busy public beach.

   **Example:** That's rude and obnoxious in my eyes.

   **Source synonyms:** in someone's view; by one's lights; to one's mind; in one's opinion
