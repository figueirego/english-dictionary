### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To act as a patron of; to defend, protect, or support.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** enpatron (obsolete); patrocinate (obsolete)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To be a customer of (an establishment), especially a regular customer.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To treat condescendingly; to talk down to.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** condescend; infantilize; denigrate
