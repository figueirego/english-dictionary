### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Barring unforeseen circumstances, it is hoped that (while generally also indicating acceptance of God's will).

   **Usage:** not-comparable; often

   **Example:** God willing, we'll meet again same time next year.

   **Example:** See y'all again same time next year, God willing.

   **Source context (verbatim):** as disjunct

   **Source synonyms:** Lord willing; gods willing; more at Thesaurus&#58;God forbid § Antonyms
