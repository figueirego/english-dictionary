## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The concept of an armed attack on another state, when not at war, on the belief that the other state represents a real attack threat. It differs from a preemptive strike which applies to states which are already at war.

   **Usage:** uncountable
