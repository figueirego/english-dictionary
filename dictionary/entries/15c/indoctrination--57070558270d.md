### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of indoctrinating, or the condition of being indoctrinated.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** political indoctrination

   **Example:** The regime relied on political indoctrination in schools.

   **Example:** Critics accused the organization of religious indoctrination.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Instruction in the rudiments and principles of any science or belief system; information.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** ideological indoctrination

   **Example:** subject someone to indoctrination
