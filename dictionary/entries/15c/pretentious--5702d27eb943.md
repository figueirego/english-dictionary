### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Motivated by an inappropriate, excessive, or unjustified desire to impress others.

   **Example:** Her dress was obviously more pretentious than comfortable.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Marked by an unwarranted claim to importance or distinction.

   **Example:** Their song titles are pretentious in the context of their basic lyrics.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Marked by pretense (not necessarily false pretense); intent upon success; ambitious.

   **Usage:** obsolete; perhaps incorrect
