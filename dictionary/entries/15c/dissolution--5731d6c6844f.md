### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The termination of an organized body or legislative assembly, especially a formal dismissal.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** abolition; disestablishment

   **Source antonyms:** establishment; foundation

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Disintegration, or decomposition into fragments.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Dissolving, or going into solution.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The quality of being dissolute.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
