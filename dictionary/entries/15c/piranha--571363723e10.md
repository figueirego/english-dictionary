### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of certain freshwater fish of the subfamily Serrasalminae, native to South America; they are characterized by razor-sharp teeth and a reputation of being ferocious predators (despite most species being omnivorous).

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; ichthyology; natural-sciences; zoology

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Someone, especially a critic, who appears to be part of a swarm and attacks someone when they are vulnerable, as if in a feeding frenzy.

   **Usage:** derogatory; figuratively
