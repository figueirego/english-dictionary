### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of hybridizing, or the state of being hybridized.
   1. The linear combination of atomic orbitals in a molecule to form hybrid orbitals.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The act of hybridizing, or the state of being hybridized.

      **Usage:** countable; including; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences
