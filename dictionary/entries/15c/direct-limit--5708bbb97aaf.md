### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A set of equivalence classes which partition the disjoint union of the members of a direct system; each equivalence class being a sort of “drainage basin” of the mappings (of the morphisms) of the direct system, if these are analogically considered as “rivers”. (If i&lt;k,j&lt;k in the indexing poset, then there exist f\_ik&#58;A\_i→A\_k and f\_jk&#58;A\_j→A\_k. If a\_i∈A\_i,a\_j∈A\_j such that f\_ik(a\_i)=f\_jk(a\_j) then a\_i∼a\_j. If k = j then f\_jj(a\_j)=a\_j,f\_ij(a\_i)=a\_j.)

   **Example:** A direct limit has “canonical functions” which map each element of the disjoint union to its equivalence class.

   **Example:** Direct limits in the algebraic sense are models of category-theoretic colimits.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** algebra; mathematics; sciences
