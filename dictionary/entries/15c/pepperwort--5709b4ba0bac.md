### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of several species of perennial plant in the genus Lepidium (originally specifically dittander, species Lepidium latifolium), which is a member of the mustard and cabbage family.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of various species of plant in the genus Marsilea, especially Marsilea minuta.
