### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A deity or supreme being; a supernatural, typically immortal, being with superior powers, to which personhood is attributed.

   **Example:** The most frequently used name for the Islamic god is Allah.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An idol.
   1. A representation of a deity, especially a statue or statuette.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An idol.
   1. Something or someone particularly revered, worshipped, idealized, admired and/or followed.

      **Usage:** figuratively

      **Example:** Leo Messi is my god!

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A person in a very high position of authority, importance or influence; a powerful ruler or tyrant.

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A person who is exceptionally skilled in a particular activity.

   **Usage:** figuratively; informal

   **Example:** He is the god of soccer!

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. An exceedingly handsome man.

   **Usage:** figuratively; informal

   **Example:** Lounging on the beach were several Greek gods.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The person who owns and runs a multi-user dungeon.

   **Usage:** Internet; roleplaying games

   **Source synonyms:** arch; imp
