## Phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. That's life; expressing resignment to a situation or set of circumstances, usually without complaint

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** Unfortunately the insurance doesn't cover that. Sorry, but it is what it is.

   **Source synonyms:** c'est la vie; life's a bitch (vulgar); shit happens (vulgar); such is life; c'est la vie; it is as it is; it is how it is; it is what it is; life's a bitch (vulgar); shit happens (vulgar); so be it; so is life; so it goes; such is life; that's life; that's that and this is this; that's the way it goes; that’s the way it plays; that's the way life is; that's the way the ball bounces; that's the way the cookie crumbles; that's the way the mop flops; them's the breaks
