### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The female (typically) person or animal previously mentioned or implied.

   **Usage:** feminine; nominative; personal; singular; third-person

   **Example:** I asked Mary, but she said that she didn't know.

   **Example:** After the cat killed a mouse, she left it on our doorstep.

   **Example:** She seems a clever girl, your Isabel.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A ship or boat.

   **Usage:** endearing; feminine; nominative; personal; singular; sometimes; third-person

   **Example:** She could do forty knots in good weather.

   **Example:** She is a beautiful boat, isn’t she?

   **Example:** The tanker is 25 years old but she is still very seaworthy.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A country, or sometimes a city, province, planet, etc.

   **Usage:** dated; endearing; feminine; nominative; personal; singular; sometimes; third-person

   **Example:** She is a poor place, but has beautiful scenery and friendly people.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A thing, especially a machine or other object, such as a car, a computer, or (poetically) a season.

   **Usage:** dated; endearing; feminine; nominative; personal; poetic; singular; third-person

   **Example:** She only gets thirty miles to the gallon on the highway, but she’s durable.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A person whose gender is unknown or irrelevant (used in a work, along with or in place of he, as an indefinite pronoun).

   **Usage:** feminine; nominative; nonstandard; personal; singular; third-person

   **Source synonyms:** they

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The Catholic Church.

   **Usage:** feminine; nominative; personal; singular; third-person

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Catholicism; Christianity; Roman-Catholicism
