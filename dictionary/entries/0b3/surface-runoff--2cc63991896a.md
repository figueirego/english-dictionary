### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Overland flow of excess water (with or without accumulated contaminants) that cannot be absorbed by the ground as infiltration.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** When there is very heavy rainfall on California hillsides over a prolonged period, there is almost always ensuing surface runoff.

   **Source synonyms:** stormwater; runoff\[Appendix&#58;Glossary
