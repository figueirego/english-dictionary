## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈpɪt͡ʃˌfɔɹk/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Forms

- pitchforks — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An agricultural hand tool comprising a fork with sparse, light tines, attached to a long handle, used for pitching hay (especially loose hay) high up onto a stack (as on a wagon or haystack, or into a haymow).

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A similar fork with slightly more and heavier tines, used for mucking stalls and pitching soiled bedding into a wagon or manure spreader.

   **Source synonyms:** dungfork; manure fork; graip

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any fork used for farm labor, even a digging fork (but such usage is often considered ignorant by experienced farmers).

   **Usage:** broadly; informal

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An angry mob; the power or effects of such a mob.

   **Usage:** figuratively; in-plural; metonymically

   **Example:** The city council was facing the pitchforks once the public had realized what the new ordinance entailed.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A tuning fork.

   **Usage:** rare
