## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈmoʊkə/ — source labels: US
- IPA: /ˈmɒkə/ — source labels: Australia; UK

### Forms

- mochas — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A coffee drink with chocolate syrup added, or a serving thereof.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source synonyms:** café mocha; caffè mocha; coffolate; mochaccino

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A flavour that combines coffee and chocolate.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** mocha fudge

   **Example:** mocha brownies

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A dark brown colour, like that of mocha coffee.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A strong Arabian coffee.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Any of a number of species of moths in the geometrid subfamily Sterrhinae, many European species having dark brown coloration.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. An Abyssinian weight, equivalent to a Troy grain.

   **Usage:** countable; historical; uncountable
