### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A legal or property right to access to abundant, affordable and clean water.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

   **Source synonyms:** water right

   **Usage:** countable; historical; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law
