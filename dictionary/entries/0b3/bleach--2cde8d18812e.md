**Source sense R2.S1**

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** whitewash

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To lose color due to stress-induced expulsion of symbiotic unicellular algae.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Once coral bleaching begins, corals tend to continue to bleach even if the stressor is removed.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** semantically bleached words that have become illocutionary particles
