## Noun

### Forms

- superplants — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A plant growing on another, such as the mistletoe; an epiphyte.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A plant that is particularly robust and which thrives in poor growing conditions or dominates an ecosystem.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A plant that is unusual in its ability to perform an especially useful function

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A plant that can be eaten for particular health benefits or particularly high nutritional value; a superfood.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A plant that is created through unnatural means, such as genetic engineering, to be stronger than others.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A manufacturing plant that is particularly large and efficient, especially one that combines functions normally handled in separate plants.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; manufacturing

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A type of prosthesis composed of a fixed saddle rigidly connected to one or more abutments, especially one with subperiosteal implants.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** dentistry; medicine; sciences
