**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To display or show (something) for others to see, especially at an exhibition or contest.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He wanted to exhibit his baseball cards.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To demonstrate.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The players exhibited great skill.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To submit (a physical object) to a court as evidence.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I now exhibit this bloody hammer.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To put on a public display.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Will you be exhibiting this year?

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To administer as a remedy.

   **Example:** to exhibit calomel

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences
