### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A spherical mass of cheese or cream cheese, often including nuts or other additions and served as an hors d'oeuvre or finger food, usually with bread or crackers.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The tree Glochidion ferdinandi.
