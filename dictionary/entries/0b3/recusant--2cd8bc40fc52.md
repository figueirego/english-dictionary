### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Someone refusing to attend Church of England services, between the 16th and early 19th centuries, whether a Protestant dissident or a Roman Catholic.

   **Usage:** historical

   **Source synonyms:** Dissenter; Nonconformist; free churchman; autem cackler

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Anyone refusing to submit to authority or regulation.

   **Source synonyms:** refuser; defier; dissenter; objector; protester; iconoclast; maverick; nonconformist; rebel; refusenik; renegade
