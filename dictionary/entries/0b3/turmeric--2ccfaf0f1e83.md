### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An Indian plant (Curcuma longa), with aromatic rhizomes, part of the ginger family (Zingiberaceae).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The pulverized rhizome of the turmeric plant, used for flavoring and to add a bright yellow color to food.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** cooking; food; lifestyle

   **Source synonyms:** haldi

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A yellow to reddish-brown dye extracted from the turmeric plant.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** E100
