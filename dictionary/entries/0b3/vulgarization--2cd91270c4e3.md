### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The process of making something vulgar, especially by using the language of ordinary people.

   **Usage:** British; Canada; English; Oxford; US; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The process of making something accessible and attractive to the general public; the popular-science presentation of a topic, its popularization.

   **Usage:** British; Canada; English; Oxford; US; countable; uncommon; uncountable
