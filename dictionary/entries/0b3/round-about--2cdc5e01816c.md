## Adverb · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In an opposite direction.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The aircraft then came round about for another approach.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Round, around (i.e., approximately).

   **Usage:** emphatic; not-comparable; sometimes

   **Example:** I reckon there're round about a dozen cattle that escaped his drive and are now living down by the river until someone rounds them up.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Round, around (i.e., surrounding); closely surrounding.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** There's a scattering of small islands round about the main island; they're mostly uninhabited except for occasional camping by fishing crews.

   **Source synonyms:** near about

## Prep\_phrase · Record 2

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. At a point or time approximately equal to.

   **Example:** She expects to get home round about ten o'clock, if all goes well.
