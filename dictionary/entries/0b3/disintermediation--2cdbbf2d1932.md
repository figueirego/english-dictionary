### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The removal of funds from a financial institution such as a bank for direct purchase of financial instruments.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** banking; business; economics; finance; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The removal of an intermediary from a commercial transaction.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The news industry is undergoing a massive disintermediation due to the ubiquitous low-cost communication made possible by the internet.
