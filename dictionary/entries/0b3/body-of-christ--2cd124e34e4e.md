### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The corpse or resurrected body of Jesus Christ.

   **Usage:** literally

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The consecrated bread of the Eucharist, considered to be the flesh of Jesus Christ.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** Priest, presenting the host&#58; The Body of Christ. \[= This bread is the body of our saviour.\] Congregant, receiving the host&#58; Amen. \[= Yes, I agree that it is.\]

   **Source synonyms:** host

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. All believers in Jesus Christ as a group; the Church.

   **Usage:** figuratively
