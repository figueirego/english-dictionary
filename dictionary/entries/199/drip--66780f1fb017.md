## Noun · Etymology 2 · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈdɹɪp/ — variety unspecified in the source labels
- IPA: \[ˈd̠ɹ̠̈˔ɪp\] — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Forms

- drips — plural

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A drop of a liquid.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** I put a drip of vanilla extract in my hot cocoa.

   **Example:** I need a bucket to catch the drips.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. A falling or letting fall in drops; act of dripping.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. An apparatus that slowly releases a liquid, especially one that intravenously releases drugs into a patient's bloodstream.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** He's not doing so well. The doctors have put him on a drip.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. A limp, ineffectual, or uninteresting person.

   **Usage:** colloquial; countable; derogatory; uncountable

   **Example:** He couldn't even summon up the courage to ask her name... what a drip!

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. That part of a cornice, sill course, or other horizontal member that projects beyond the rest, and has a section designed to throw off rainwater.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** architecture

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. Style; swagger; fashionable and/or expensive clothing.

   **Usage:** slang; uncountable

   **Example:** His drip is looking fine, especially the Supreme t-shirt.
