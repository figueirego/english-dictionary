### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To compare two (sets of) things exhaustively.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive; slang

   **Example:** Me and bro went band for band with our garages, but I lost the moment he whipped out the whole ass 1930s tank.

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To compare who has more bands (stacks of $1000 bills).

   **Usage:** intransitive; literally; slang

   **Example:** Go band for band with me, bruh, we'll be counting like you count sheep falling asleep.
