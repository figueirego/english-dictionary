- IPA: /ʌnˈklɪɚ/ — source labels: US

### Forms

- unclearer — comparative
- more unclear — comparative
- unclearest — superlative
- most unclear — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Ambiguous; liable to more than one interpretation.

   **Example:** The remark she made comparing her life with that of a fish was unclear.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Not clearly or explicitly defined.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Not easy to see or read; indecipherable or unreadable.

   **Example:** From such a long distance away, the name on the signpost was unclear.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Not having a clear idea; uncertain.

   **Example:** I'm still unclear about what she meant by that remark.
