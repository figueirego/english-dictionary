### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The property or status (of a syllabic, generally a vowel) of being syllabic, i.e. able to be positioned in the syllable nuclei.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** The syllabicity of Proto-Indo-European syllabic sonorants \*y and \*w can easily be determined by a rule.
