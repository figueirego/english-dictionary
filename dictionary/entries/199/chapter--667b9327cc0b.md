**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One of the main sections into which a published work is divided, especially a book.

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One of the main sections into which a published work is divided, especially a book.
   1. A section of a work, a collection of works, or fragments of works, often manuscripts or transcriptions, created by scholars or advocates, not the original authors, to aid in finding portions of the texts.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Certain ecclesiastical bodies (under canon law)
   1. An assembly of monks, prebendaries and/or other clergymen connected with a cathedral, conventual or collegiate church, or of a diocese, usually presided over by the dean.

**Source sense R1.S4**

   1. A bishop's council.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

   1. An administrative division of an organization, usually local to a specific area.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A section of a social body.
   1. An organized branch of some society or fraternity, such as the Freemasons.
