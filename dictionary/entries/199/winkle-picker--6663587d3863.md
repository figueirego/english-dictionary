### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who harvests periwinkles (a kind of sea snail) to be eaten.

   **Usage:** Ireland; UK

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An implement used to extract the flesh of a periwinkle from its shell.

   **Source synonyms:** winkle pin

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A style of boot or shoe with a severely pointed toe, fashionable in the 1950s and 1960s.

   **Usage:** also; attributive

   **Example:** winkle-picker toes; winkle-picker stilettos

   **Source synonyms:** roach stomper
