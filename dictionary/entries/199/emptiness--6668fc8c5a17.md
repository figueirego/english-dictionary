### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The state or feeling of being empty; an emotion that combines hollowness, numbness, disconnection and a lack of meaning in life, resulting in existential boredom, ennui or depression.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
