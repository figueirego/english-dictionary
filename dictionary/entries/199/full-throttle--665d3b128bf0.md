## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A maximum setting on an engine or motor control&#58; especially, the totally open position of a valve for air intake, but also any corresponding maximum setting.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source antonyms:** idle; idling

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A maximum level of speed, effort, or risk.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** top gear; overdrive

   **Source antonyms:** low gear
