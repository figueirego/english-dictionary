## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The fibrous material covering the skin of a peach fruit, e.g. as opposed to the fuzzless skin of a nectarine.

   **Usage:** literally; uncountable

**Source sense R1.S2**
