## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A relay race in which members of a team take turns running progressively longer parts of a course. The first member runs 100 meters, the second 200 meters, the third 300 meters, and the fourth 400 meters, equalling one kilometer in total.

   **Usage:** uncountable
