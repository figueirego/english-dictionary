### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A folding magnifying glass which, when unfolded, forms a frame for the lens at a pre-focussed distance. Commonly used in stamp collecting.

2. A small magnifying glass, as might be used to check the thread count of linens.
