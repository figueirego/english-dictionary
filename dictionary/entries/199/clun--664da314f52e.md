### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A small town and civil parish with a town council in Shropshire, England (OS grid ref SO2980). The council has the curious title of "Clun Town Council with Chapel Lawn".

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A river in Shropshire and Herefordshire, England, which joins the River Teme.
