### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An audience or other group of people who are hyped over something; people who highly and sometimes excessively anticipates something that has been highly advertised and promoted.

   **Usage:** derogatory; sometimes

   **Example:** That new film is being advertised way too much, but it's working – everyone is joining the hype train.
