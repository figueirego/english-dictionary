### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Not accompanied by anything else; one in number.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Can you give me a single reason not to leave right now?

   **Example:** The vase contained a single long-stemmed rose.

   **Source synonyms:** lone; sole; solitary

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Not divided in parts.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The potatoes left the spoon and landed in a single big lump on the plate.

   **Source synonyms:** unbroken; undivided; uniform

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Designed for the use of only one.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** a single room

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Performed by one person, or one on each side.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** a single combat

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Not married, and (in modern times) not dating or without a significant other.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Forms often ask if a person is single, married, divorced, or widowed. In this context, a person who is dating someone but who has never married puts "single".

   **Example:** Josh put down that he was a single male on the dating website.

   **Source synonyms:** unmarried; unpartnered; available

**Source sense R1.S6**

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r1-s9"></a>
