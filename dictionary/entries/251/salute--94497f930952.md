## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An utterance or gesture expressing greeting or honor towards someone, (now especially) a formal, non-verbal gesture made with the arms or hands in any of various specific positions.

   **Example:** The soldiers greeted the dignitaries with a crisp salute.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A kiss, offered in salutation.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A discharge of cannon or similar arms, as a mark of honour or respect.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; nautical; politics; transport; war

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A pyrotechnic device primarily designed to produce a loud bang.

## Verb · Record 2

### Pronunciation

### Forms

- salutes — present; singular; third-person
- saluting — participle; present
- saluted — participle; past
- saluted — past

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To make a gesture in honor of (someone or something).

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** They saluted the flag as it passed in the parade.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To act in thanks, honor, or tribute; to thank or extend gratitude; to praise.

   **Example:** I would like to salute the many dedicated volunteers that make this project possible.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. to wave, to acknowledge an acquaintance.

   **Usage:** Ireland; informal

   **Example:** I saluted Bill at the concert, but he didn't see me through the crowd.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To address, as with expressions of kind wishes and courtesy; to greet; to hail.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To promote the welfare and safety of; to benefit; to gratify.

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To kiss.

   **Usage:** archaic
