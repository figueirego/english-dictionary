### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see pour, out.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To serve a drink into a cup or glass.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I poured out three beers for my guests.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To distribute or spread (something), as if it were a liquid.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To leave a place quickly, and in large numbers.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The rats came pouring out of every possible opening in the burning warehouse.

   **Source synonyms:** spill out

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To talk volubly and deeply. Usually implies telling the truth.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** She started crying, and she poured out the whole story, right from the beginning.
