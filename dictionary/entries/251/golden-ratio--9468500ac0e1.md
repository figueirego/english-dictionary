### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The irrational number (approximately 1.618), usually denoted by the Greek letter φ (phi), which is equal to the sum of its own reciprocal and 1, or, equivalently, is the square root of the sum of itself and 1.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** extreme ratio; golden cut; golden number; golden proportion; mean ratio; sectio divina; mul&#58;ϕ
