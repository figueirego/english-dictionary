### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To (cause to) gush; to flow or move in a rush.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive; informal

   **Example:** The tomato sauce was splurged all over the chips.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To spend lavishly or extravagantly, especially money.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive; informal

   **Example:** They decided to splurge on the biggest banana split for dessert.

   **Source synonyms:** splash out

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To produce an extravagant or ostentatious display.

   **Usage:** informal; intransitive
