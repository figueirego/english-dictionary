### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person in the relationship of a godfather to the other god-parents, and the legal parents, of a child.

   **Usage:** obsolete

   **Source synonyms:** gossip; compadre; compère

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In a male homosexual couple, the nonbiological father (partner of the biological father) of a child.

   **Source synonyms:** stepfather
