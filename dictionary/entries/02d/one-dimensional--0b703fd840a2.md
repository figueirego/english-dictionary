### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having length, but no width, height or depth.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; usually

   **Example:** A straight line is one-dimensional.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Lacking depth or believability; flat.

   **Usage:** figuratively; not-comparable; usually

   **Example:** one-dimensional characters

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Lacking personal depth and substance; conceited, vain, shallow, superficial.

   **Usage:** figuratively; not-comparable; usually
