## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An official edict that a particular substance or activity is dangerous, or an expression of such an edict.

   **Usage:** UK

   **Example:** The law requires a health warning on packets of cigarettes.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An indication or admission that an opinion, statistic, etc. is to be treated with doubt or scepticism.

   **Usage:** UK; colloquial

   **Example:** As I am a member of that political party, my views must come with a health warning.

   **Source synonyms:** disclaimer
