### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Son of the same parents as another person.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A male having at least one parent in common with another person (see half-brother, stepbrother).

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A male fellow member of a religious community, church, trades union etc.

   **Example:** Thank you, brother.

   **Example:** I would like to thank the brother who just spoke.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A form of address to a man.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** Brother, can you spare a dime?

   **Example:** Listen, brother, I don't know what you want, but I’m not interested.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A fellow black man.

   **Usage:** African-American Vernacular

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Somebody, usually male, connected by a common cause, situation, or affection.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Someone who is a peer, whether male or female.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Someone who is a kinsman or shares the same patriarch.

   **Usage:** poetic
