### Senses

<a id="r3-s1"></a>

**Source sense R3.S1**

<a id="r3-s2"></a>

**Source sense R3.S2**

2. An unincorporated community in Osage County, Missouri, United States, so named because of a local merchant's business practices (thus being derived from crook (thief)).
