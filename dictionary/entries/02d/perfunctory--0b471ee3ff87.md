### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Done only or merely to conform to a minimal standard or to fulfill a protocol or presumptive duty.

   **Example:** a perfunctory kiss

   **Source synonyms:** automatic; cursory; obligatory; pro forma; token; unthinking

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Performed in a careless or indifferent manner as a thing of rote.

   **Example:** He did a perfunctory job cleaning his dad's car, finishing quickly but leaving a few spots still dirty.

   **Source synonyms:** haphazard; mechanical; slipshod

   **Source antonyms:** careful; complete; thorough
