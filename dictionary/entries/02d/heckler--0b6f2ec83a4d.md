### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who heckles; somebody who insults, makes fun of, or teases.

   **Example:** The juggler always seemed to have a heckler or two in the crowd on Saturdays.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
