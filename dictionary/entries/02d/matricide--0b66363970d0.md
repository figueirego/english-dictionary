### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The killing of one's mother.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Gerald was imprisoned for matricide&#58; he strangled his mother.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person who kills his or her mother.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Nancy was a matricide; it happened four years ago.
