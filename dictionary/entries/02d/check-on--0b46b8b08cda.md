### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To personally verify that (someone or something) is safe, copacetic, appropriate or satisfactory.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I sent Tom to check on the northside's branch's progress.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To verify a specific fact; to quickly examine a situation, especially to look for updates or new information on a topic that has previously been discussed.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Have the price-tags been updated? I'll check on that right away, sir.
