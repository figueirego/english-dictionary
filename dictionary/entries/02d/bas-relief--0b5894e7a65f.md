### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A low or mostly-flat sculpture which is carved into a wall, or is in the form of a tile mounted flat to a wall, rather than a fully three-dimensional, free-standing figure.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; sculpture

   **Source synonyms:** basso-relievo; basso-rilievo; low relief; cavo-relievo; cavo-rilievo; intaglio-rilievo; sunken relief; cameo

   **Source antonyms:** alto-relievo; high relief
