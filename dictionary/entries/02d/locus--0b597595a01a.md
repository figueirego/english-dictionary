### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A place or locality, especially a centre of activity or the scene of a crime.

   **Example:** The cafeteria was the locus of activity.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The set of all points whose coordinates satisfy a given equation or condition.

   **Example:** A circle is the locus of points from which the distance to the center is a given value, the radius.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A fixed position on a string of DNA or RNA, especially a chromosome, that may be occupied by one or more genes.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; genetics; medicine; natural-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A passage in writing, especially in a collection of ancient sacred writings arranged according to a theme.

   **Usage:** in-plural
