### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To start criminal proceedings against.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to prosecute a man for trespass, or for a riot

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To charge, try.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To seek to obtain by legal process.

   **Example:** to prosecute a right or a claim in a court of law

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to prosecute a scheme, hope, or an investigation
