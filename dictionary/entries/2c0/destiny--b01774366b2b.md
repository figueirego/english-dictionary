## Noun

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. That to which any person or thing is destined; a predetermined or inevitable eventual state; a condition predestined by the Divine or by human will.

   **Example:** Death is the destiny of all mortal men.

   **Source synonyms:** fate; lot; orlay; chance (archaic); destin (obsolete); destiny; doom; fatality; fate; foredoom; foreordination; fortune; kismet; lot; orlay (Germanic); portion; predestination; predestiny (obsolete); preordination; qadar; weird (archaic); wyrd (Old-Norse)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One's eventual fate (not necessarily inevitable or predestined).

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The fixed order of things; invincible necessity; an irresistible power or agency conceived of as determining the future, whether in general or of an individual.

   **Example:** Dara doesn't believe in using dating apps - she insists that destiny will find her other half.

   **Source synonyms:** fate
