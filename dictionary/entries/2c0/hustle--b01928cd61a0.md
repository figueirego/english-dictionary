### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To push someone roughly; to crowd; to jostle.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To rush or hurry.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** I'll have to hustle to get there on time.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To bundle; to stow something quickly.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To con, swindle, or deceive, especially financially.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The guy tried to hustle me into buying into a bogus real estate deal.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To play deliberately badly at a game or sport in an attempt to encourage players to challenge one.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

   **Usage:** informal

   **Usage:** informal

   **Usage:** informal

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

   **Usage:** informal
