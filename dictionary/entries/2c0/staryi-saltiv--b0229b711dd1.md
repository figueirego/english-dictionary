### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A rural settlement, the administrative centre of Staryi Saltiv settlement hromada, Chuhuiv Raion, Kharkiv Oblast, Ukraine, first mentioned in the 1627 Book of the Great Drawing, in which it was already described as “an old settlement”.

   **Source synonyms:** Staro-Saltove; Staro-Saltiv

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A rural settlement, the administrative centre of Staryi Saltiv settlement hromada, Chuhuiv Raion, Kharkiv Oblast, Ukraine, first mentioned in the 1627 Book of the Great Drawing, in which it was already described as “an old settlement”.
   1. A former selrada of Chuhuiv Raion, Kharkiv Oblast, Ukraine, established somewhen 1917–1923, amalgamated into Staryi Saltiv settlement hromada in September 2015.

      **Source synonyms:** Staro-Saltove; Staro-Saltiv

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A rural settlement, the administrative centre of Staryi Saltiv settlement hromada, Chuhuiv Raion, Kharkiv Oblast, Ukraine, first mentioned in the 1627 Book of the Great Drawing, in which it was already described as “an old settlement”.
   1. A former raion of Kharkiv Oblast, Ukrainian Soviet Socialist Republic, established in 1923, disestablished in 1963.

      **Source synonyms:** Staro-Saltove; Staro-Saltiv
