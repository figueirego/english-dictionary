### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To feel comfortable or normal; to be in one's usual mood or state of health; to feel like oneself.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; informal

   **Example:** I hope you don't mind if I cancel our date this afternoon – I just don't feel myself today.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To feel particularly good (especially about oneself) or particularly into oneself.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; informal

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To touch oneself.
