## Noun · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- enPR: dĕnt — source labels: UK; US
- IPA: /dɛnt/ — source labels: UK; US

### Forms

- dents — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A shallow deformation in the surface of an object, produced by an impact.

   **Example:** The crash produced a dent in the left side of the car.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A minor effect made upon something.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** to make a dent

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A type of maize/corn with a relatively soft outer hull, and a soft type of starch that shrinks at maturity to leave an indentation in the surface of the kernel.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A sudden negative change, such as loss, damage, weakening, consumption or diminution, especially one produced by an external force, event or action

   **Usage:** broadly; informal

   **Example:** That purchase put a bit of a dent in my wallet.
