### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of the poisonous umbelliferous plants, of the genera
   1. Conium, either Conium maculatum or Conium chaerophylloides.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of the poisonous umbelliferous plants, of the genera
   1. Cicuta (water hemlock).

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Poison obtained from these Conium and Cicuta plants.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any of several coniferous trees, of the genus Tsuga, that grow in North America; the wood of such trees.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
