### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A mark consisting of multiple full stops (with or without spaces), used to indicate omitted, missing, or illegible words; or (in mathematics) that a pattern continues.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The ellipsis in 1, 2, 3, ..., 8, 9 means that the numbers 4, 5, 6, and 7 are not explicitly included, but are considered to be part of the pattern.

   **Example:** The ellipsis in 0.333... means that the number is a repeating decimal, having threes that go on forever.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; media; publishing; sciences; typography

   **Source synonyms:** dot dot dot (colloquial); suspension dots; suspension points

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The omission from a clause of a word or phrase that is nevertheless inferred using the context of the remaining element.

   **Usage:** countable; rhetoric; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** grammar; human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The omission of scenes in a film that do not advance the plot.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** broadcasting; film; media; television

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An ellipse.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences
