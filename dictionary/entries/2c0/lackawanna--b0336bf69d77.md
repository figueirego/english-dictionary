### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. the Lackawanna River in Pennsylvania, USA, a tributary of the Susquehanna River.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A city in Erie County, New York, United States, named after the Lackawanna Steel Company.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. a short name for the Delaware, Lackawanna and Western Railroad, which was merged into the Erie Lackawanna Railroad in 1960.
