### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A BMX trick where the rider puts their front foot from the pedal onto the front tire/fork area, leans forward, presses their shin against the handlebars to lock in, and slides or "jams" into a ramp (like a quarter pipe), often using the tire as a brake or pivot before popping back in; requires good balance, keeping weight forward, and finding the right pressure to avoid going over the bars.
