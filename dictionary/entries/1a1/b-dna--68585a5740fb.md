### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The default configuration of DNA in cells, right-hand twisting double helix with a significant size difference between major groove and minor groove, and approximately 10 base pairs per twist.

   **Usage:** uncountable
