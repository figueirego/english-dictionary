### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The feeling or experience of being astonished; great surprise.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The class looked on in astonishment as their teacher proceeded to tear the pages out of the textbook.

   **Source synonyms:** amazement; stupefaction; wonder; wonderment

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Something very surprising.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** marvel; stunner

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Loss of physical sensation; inability to move a part of the body.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** paralysis; numbness

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Loss of mental faculties, inability to think or use one's senses.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** stupor

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Loss of composure or presence of mind.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** consternation; dismay
