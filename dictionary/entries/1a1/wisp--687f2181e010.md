## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /wɪsp/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Forms

- wisps — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A small bundle, as of straw or other like substance; a twisted handful of something; any slender, flexible structure or group.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** A wisp of hair escaped her barrette and whipped wildly in the wind.

   **Example:** a wisp of a child

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A small, thin line of cloud, smoke, or steam.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** A wisp of smoke rose from the candle for a few moments after he blew it out.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A whisk, or small broom.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A will o' the wisp, or ignis fatuus.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An immeasurable, indefinable essence of life; soul.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A flock of snipe.

   **Usage:** archaic; collective; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A disease affecting the feet of cattle.

   **Usage:** uncountable
