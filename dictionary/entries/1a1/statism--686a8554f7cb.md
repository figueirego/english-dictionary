### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Synonym of secularism&#58; subservience of religious issues to political officials and expediency.

   **Usage:** derogatory; obsolete; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** secularism (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Synonym of statecraft or statesmanship.

   **Usage:** obsolete; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** statecraft or statesmanship (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Synonym of government or governance.

   **Usage:** archaic; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** government or governance (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The belief that most or all political power should be centralized in national governments.

   **Usage:** derogatory; often; uncountable; political philosophy

   **Source synonyms:** etatism; authoritarianism; totalitarianism; big government; dirigisme

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The belief that most or nearly all political power should be decentralized to provincial governments.

   **Usage:** derogatory; often; uncountable; political philosophy

   **Source synonyms:** regionalism; federalism; provincialism

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The belief that the state is a legitimate social institution.

   **Usage:** derogatory; often; uncountable; political philosophy

   **Source antonyms:** anarchism
