## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Current; of the present.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Modern-day Greek is a lot different from ancient Greek, or koine.

   **Example:** Of course, modern-day theories debunk that idea.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used to designate a present-day version of someone or something in history.

   **Usage:** informal; not-comparable

   **Example:** That couple led police on a wild goose chase like a modern-day Bonnie and Clyde!
