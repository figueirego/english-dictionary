### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A long-standing group of people sharing cultural aspects such as language, dress, norms of behavior and artistic forms.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** This society has been known for centuries for its colorful clothing and tight-knit family structure.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A group of people who meet from time to time to engage in a common interest; an association or organization.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** It was then that they decided to found a society of didgeridoo-playing unicyclists.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The sum total of all voluntary interrelations between individuals.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** The gap between Western and Eastern societies seems to be narrowing.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Companionship or association with one's fellows; friendly or intimate intercourse.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** The society of friends is one of life's great joys.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The people of one’s country or community taken as a whole.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Our global society develops in fits and starts.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. High society.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Smith was first introduced into society at the Duchess of Grand Fenwick's annual rose garden party.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A number of people joined by mutual consent to deliberate, determine and act toward a common goal.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law
