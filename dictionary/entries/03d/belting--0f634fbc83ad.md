### Senses

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A beating with a belt.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. A thorough defeat; a thrashing.

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. A system of beltwork, as in a conveyor or other mechanical device.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. The material from which belts for machinery are made.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. The act of singing in chest voice above one's vocal break in a higher range typically sung in head voice.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** I could hear you perfectly all the way in the back of the audience! I wish my belting were as good as yours.
