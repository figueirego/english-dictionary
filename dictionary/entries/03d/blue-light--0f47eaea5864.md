### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A mixture of chemicals (including nitre, sulfur and antimony) used in the eighteenth and nineteenth centuries for night-time signaling and general illumination.

   **Usage:** countable; historical; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A New England federalist, who opposed the Anglo-American War of 1812; (loosely), a federalist.

   **Usage:** US; colloquial; countable; historical; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A flashing light, usually fitted to an emergency vehicle.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see blue, light.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
