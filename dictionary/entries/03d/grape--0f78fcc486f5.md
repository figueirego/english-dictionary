### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A small, round, smooth-skinned edible fruit, usually purple, red, or green, that grows in bunches on female vines of most species of genus Vitis and the self-fertile plants of species Vitis vinifera.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** Grapes give us whole-fruit snacks, grape juice, raisins, wine, and more.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any woody vine of genus Vitis that bears clusters of grapes; a grapevine.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** wild grape covering the back slope

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of various fruits or plants with varying resemblances to those of genus Vitis but belonging to other genera, not necessarily edible.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** sea grape; tail grape

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A dark purplish-red colour, the colour of many grapes.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** For those seeking purply tones, the paint colors available include mauve, magenta, and grape.

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Clipping of grapeshot.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; clipping; uncountable

   **Example:** men mowed down by grape

   **Alternative form of:** [grapeshot](../208/grapeshot--822a13653f65.md)

**Source sense R1.S6**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
