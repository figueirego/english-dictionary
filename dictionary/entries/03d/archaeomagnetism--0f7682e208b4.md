### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The magnetism of the Earth in the geologic past; the study and measurement of this magnetism in order to date a specimen with iron.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; geology; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The magnetism induced by the Earth's magnetic field in magnetizable matter after heating to the Curie temperature; directional magnetism, thermoremanent magnetism.

   **Usage:** uncountable
