**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Be utterly unrestrained and uninhibited.
   1. Behave without shyness, timidity, or worry about the reactions of other people; being perfectly candid and without concealment of one's feelings or intentions in expressing oneself or revealing one's emotions.

      **Usage:** idiomatic

      **Source synonyms:** let one's hair down
