### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To follow urgently, originally with intent to capture or harm; to chase.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** pursue one's dreams

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To follow, travel down (a particular way, course of action etc.).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Her rival pursued a quite different course.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To aim for, go after (a specified objective, situation etc.).

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To participate in (an activity, business etc.); to practise, follow (a profession).

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To urge (someone or something) to act in a particular way.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The mayor promised to pursue the city to be an Olympic sports venue.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To act as a legal prosecutor.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To continue; to carry on.

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive
