### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To pay the penalty for (something); to atone for, to make amends.

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** make up

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To pay (something) as a penalty, to atone for; to suffer (something).

   **Usage:** archaic; figuratively; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** sustain

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To endure or tolerate (something); to experience.

   **Usage:** transitive; obsolete except Scotland

   **Source synonyms:** brook; go on; hold on; put up with; abide (archaic); aby (obsolete); astand (obsolete); endure; go on; lie; prevail; hold on; last; live; persist; remain; stick; stick it out; stick out; abear; abide; aby (British; dialectal); accept; allow; bear; bide (dialectal); brook; dree (British; dialectal); endure; forbear; go along with; live with; pass; pocket; pouch; put up with; stand; stick; stomach; suffer; swallow; take; take sitting down (idiomatic); thole (British; dialectal); tolerate; undergang (British; dialectal); undergo

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To pay for (something); to buy.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** procure; purchase; aby (obsolete); buy; cheap (obsolete); procure; purchase; snap up
