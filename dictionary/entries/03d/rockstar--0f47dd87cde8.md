## Noun

### Forms

- rockstars — plural
- rock star — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A member of a rock band, or a solo artist in the genre, especially one with celebrity status.

   **Example:** By 1967, Jimi Hendrix had become a rockstar.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person who is renowned or revered in his or her field of accomplishment.

   **Usage:** attributive; figuratively; sometimes

   **Example:** By 1985, Michael Jordan had become a rockstar of the NBA.

   **Source synonyms:** superstar; all-star; star

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Someone who works hard, achieves wins, or both; used in expressions of gratitude.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** Congratulations to Ashley, who once again proved that she is a rockstar by rescuing a key account from the threat of nonrenewal.

   **Source context (verbatim):** in corporate settings

   **Source synonyms:** superstar; all-star; star

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any animal, plant, or other thing that is renowned amongst others of its type.

   **Usage:** attributive; figuratively; sometimes

   **Example:** Many birds can sing beautifully, and various birds can mimic sounds that they hear, but lyrebirds are rockstars of mimickry in the bird world.

   **Example:** Owls are rockstars among raptors in the respect that they hunt amazingly well while also doing it under pitch-dark conditions and silently.

   **Source synonyms:** superstar; all-star; star
