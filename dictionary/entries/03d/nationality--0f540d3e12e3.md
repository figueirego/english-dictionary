### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Legal membership of a particular nation or state, by origin, birth, naturalization, ownership, allegiance or otherwise.

   **Example:** By living in the country for five years, you are entitled to get nationality.

   **Example:** Stefi was born in Spain to a Brazilian father and a Chilean mother, so is eligible for three nationalities.

   **Example:** Please include your nationality on the form.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A people sharing a common origin, culture and/or language, and possibly constituting a nation-state.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. National, i.e. ethnic and/or cultural, character or identity.

   **Usage:** archaic

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Nationalism or patriotism.

   **Usage:** archaic

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Political existence, independence or unity as a national entity.

   **Usage:** obsolete
