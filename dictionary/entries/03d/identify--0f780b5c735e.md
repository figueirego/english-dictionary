### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To establish the identity of someone or something.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** It was hard to identify the shoplifter because the CCTV records didn't have a clear image.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To disclose the identity of someone.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The Associated Press will not identify the suspect of the crime because he is a juvenile.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To establish the taxonomic classification of an organism.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To equate or make the same; to unite or combine into one.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To have a strong affinity with; to feel oneself to be modelled on or connected to.

   **Usage:** reflexive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To associate oneself with some group; to feel, or believe one feels, the same way.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To claim an identity; to describe oneself as a member of a group; to assert the use of a particular term to describe oneself.

   **Usage:** reflexive

   **Source context (verbatim):** with as
