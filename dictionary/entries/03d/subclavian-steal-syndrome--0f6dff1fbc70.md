### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A constellation of signs and symptoms that arise from retrograde (reversed) flow of blood in the vertebral artery or the internal thoracic artery, due to a proximal stenosis or occlusion of the subclavian artery.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
