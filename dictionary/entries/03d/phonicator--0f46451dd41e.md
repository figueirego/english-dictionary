### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An educator who champions the use of phonics instruction as a method of beginning or remedial reading instruction, often used as a self-referent.

   **Usage:** derogatory; humorous; slang; sometimes

   **Example:** I'm a phonicator, and proud of it!
