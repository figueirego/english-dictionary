## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The practice of using a broad range of pest-control techniques including both organic and nonorganic ones, with a goal of balancing pros and cons (such as toxicity versus efficacy, reducing chemical use but not allowing crop failure, etc).

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** IPM; IPC; integrated pest control
