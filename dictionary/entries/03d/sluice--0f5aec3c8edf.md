### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An artificial passage for water, fitted with a valve or gate, for example in a canal lock or a mill stream, for stopping or regulating the flow.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A water gate or floodgate.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Hence, an opening or channel through which anything flows; a source of supply.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The stream flowing through a floodgate.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A long box or trough through which water flows, used for washing auriferous earth.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; mining

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. An instance of wh-stranding ellipsis, or sluicing.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To emit by, or as by, flood gates.

   **Usage:** rare; transitive

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To wet copiously, as by opening a sluice

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To wash with, or in, a stream of water running through a sluice.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to sluice earth or gold dust in a sluice box in placer mining

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To wash (down or out).

   **Usage:** broadly; transitive

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To flow, pour.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To elide the complement in a coordinated wh-question. See sluicing.
