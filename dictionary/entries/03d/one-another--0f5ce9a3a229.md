### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used of a reciprocal relationship among a group of two or more people or things; compare each other.

   **Usage:** pronoun; reciprocal

   **Example:** The raw recruits helped one another get over the first few days.

   **Example:** Rainy days seemed to follow one another all summer.
