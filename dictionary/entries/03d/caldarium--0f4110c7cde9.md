### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In Roman baths, the hottest room, with a plunge-pool. It preceded the tepidarium and frigidarium.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
