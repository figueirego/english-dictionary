### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A group of people hired to attend a performance and to either applaud or boo.

   **Usage:** collective

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A group of fawning admirers.

   **Usage:** broadly

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
