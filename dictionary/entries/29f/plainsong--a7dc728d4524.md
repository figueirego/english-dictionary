**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A form of monophonic chant in unison using the Gregorian scale, sung in various Christian churches.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music
