### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Along a path which runs past someone or something.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** I watched the parade as it passed by.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. In the vicinity, near.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** There was a shepherd close by.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To or at a place, as a residence or place of business.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** I'll stop by on my way home from work.

   **Example:** We're right near the lifeguard station. Come by before you leave.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. Aside, away.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; uncommon outside the phrase 'put by'

   **Example:** The women spent much time after harvest putting jams by for winter and spring.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. Beyond or past a certain point.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; uncommon except in set phrases

   **Example:** I'll somehow manage to get by.
