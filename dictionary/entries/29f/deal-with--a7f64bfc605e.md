### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To handle verbally or in some form of artistic expression; to address or discuss as a subject.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** This book deals with incest.

   **Source synonyms:** address; cover; handle; plough; treat (especially); **Source context (verbatim):** in art or literature; regard

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To take action with respect to (someone or something).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The treasurer deals with financial matters.

   **Example:** The teacher knew how to deal with these lazy students.

   **Source synonyms:** engage; handle; take on; work

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To consider, as an example.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Let us deal with the case of China.

   **Source synonyms:** consider; look at; take

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To confront or overcome any difficulties presented by.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** We had to deal with having just a gallon of gas.

   **Example:** She's dealing with a lot of personal problems right now.

   **Example:** Thankfully he'd dealt with such issues before, so he had all the necessary experience.

   **Source synonyms:** come to terms with; contend with; cope with; get by with; grapple with; make do with; make out with; manage with

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To be in charge of, act on, or dispose of.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I can deal with this crew of workers.

   **Source synonyms:** care for; handle; look after; manage; take care of

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To behave in a certain way towards.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He deals fairly with his employees.

   **Source synonyms:** interact with
