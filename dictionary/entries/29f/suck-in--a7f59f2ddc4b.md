### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To draw inward using suction. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see suck, in.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** This is the component of the vacuum cleaner that sucks in dust.

   **Example:** I sucked in my belly, hoping to hide the extra weight I had put on over the holidays.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To cause (someone) to become slowly more and more involved in some business or situation that may not be to their advantage.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** I really didn't want to be on the committee, but somehow I got sucked in.
