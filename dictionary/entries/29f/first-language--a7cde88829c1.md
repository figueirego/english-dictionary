### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The first language one is taught to speak; one's native language.

   **Example:** She was born and raised in France, although her first language is Portuguese because her parents are from Brazil.

   **Source synonyms:** native language; mother tongue; mother language

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The language one feels most comfortable and capable with.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
