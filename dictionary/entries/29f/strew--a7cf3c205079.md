### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To distribute objects or pieces of something over an area, especially in a random manner.

   **Usage:** dated; except strewn

   **Example:** to strew sand over a floor

   **Example:** The files had been strewn all over the floor.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To cover, or lie upon, by having been scattered.

   **Usage:** archaic

   **Example:** Leaves strewed the ground.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To spread abroad; to disseminate.

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To populate with at random points; to cause to appear randomly distributed throughout.

   **Example:** error-strewn

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** scatter; sprinkle
