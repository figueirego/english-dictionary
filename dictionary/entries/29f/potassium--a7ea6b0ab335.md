### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A soft, waxy, silvery reactive metal that is never found unbound in nature; an element with atomic number 19 and atomic weight of 39.0983.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** We all need potassium, and green vegetables are a good source.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An atom of this element.

   **Usage:** countable; usually

   **Example:** This reaction substitutes a potassium for a sodium.
