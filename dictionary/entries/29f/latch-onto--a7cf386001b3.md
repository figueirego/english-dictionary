### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To obtain, acquire or get and keep hold of something.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** They latched onto the idea and gave it up only reluctantly.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To approach or stay close to someone, especially in a manner perceived as bothersome.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** He was looking for someone to talk to and latched onto me for some reason.
