### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An official summons or notice given to a person to appear.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** invocation; citation; invocation; subpoena; summons; writ of summons

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The paper containing such summons or notice.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The act of citing a passage from a text, or from another person, using the exact words of the original text or speech and giving credit to the original by referencing.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An entry in a list of sources from which information was taken, typically following a prescribed bibliographical style; a reference.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An instance of having had one's work cited.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** She has over 1,000 citations.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The passage or words quoted; a quotation.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** cite; quotation; quote

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A quotation with attached bibliographical details demonstrating the use of a particular lexical item in a dictionary, especially a dictionary on historical principles.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; lexicography; linguistics; sciences

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Enumeration; mention.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** It's a simple citation of facts.

   **Source synonyms:** listing; numbering; telling; citation; counting; enumeration; numbering; numeration; telling (archaic)

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A reference to decided cases, or books of authority, to prove a point in law.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. A commendation in recognition of some achievement, or a formal statement of an achievement.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
