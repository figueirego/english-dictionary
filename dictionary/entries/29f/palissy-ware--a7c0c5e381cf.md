## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Ceramics produced in the style of the famous French potter Bernard Palissy, characterized by three-dimensional modelled animals, often aquatic creatures such as snakes, fish, lizards, frogs and snails, arranged on large platters.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** rustic ware

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
