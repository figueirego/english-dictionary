### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Exactly as stated; read or understood without interpretation; according to the letter; not figurative or metaphorical; following the letter or exact words; not taking liberties; etymonic rather than idiomatic.

   **Example:** The literal translation of Spanish irse al otro barrio is "to go to the other neighbourhood" but it means "to die".

   **Example:** A literal reading of the law would prohibit it, but that is clearly not the intent.

   **Source synonyms:** verbatim; word-for-word

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Actual, real, physical.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. That which generally assumes that the plainest reading of a given text is correct but which allows for metaphor where context indicates it.

   **Usage:** broadly; hermeneutics

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Following the historical-grammatical method of biblical interpretation.

   **Usage:** specifically; hermeneutics

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** lifestyle; religion; theology

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Consisting of, or expressed by, letters (of an alphabet); using literation.

   **Usage:** uncommon

   **Example:** a literal equation

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Unimaginative; matter-of-fact; literal-minded.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a person

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Used nonliterally as an intensifier. See literally for usage notes.

   **Usage:** proscribed

   **Example:** Telemarketers are the literal worst.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Misspelling of littoral.

   **Usage:** alt-of; misspelling

   **Alternative form of:** [littoral](../381/littoral--e07d8df51c67.md)
