### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The price paid for obtaining, or price received for providing, money or goods in a credit transaction, calculated as a fraction of the amount or value of what was borrowed.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** Our bank offers borrowers an annual interest of 5%.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any excess over and above an exact equivalent

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A great attention and concern from someone or something; intellectual curiosity.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** He has a lot of interest in vintage cars.

   **Example:** I have a lot of interest in doing online coding.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Attention that is given to or received from someone or something.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Advantage, welfare, or something that serves it.

   **Usage:** in-plural; often; uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. An involvement, claim, right, share, stake in or link with a financial, business, or other undertaking or endeavor.

   **Usage:** countable; usually

   **Example:** When scientists and doctors write articles and when politicians run for office, they are required in many countries to declare any existing conflicts of interest (competing interests).

   **Example:** I have business interests in South Africa.

   **Example:** She has an interest in the proceedings, and all stakeholders' interests must be protected.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Something which, or someone whom, one is interested in.

   **Usage:** countable; usually

   **Example:** Lexicography is one of my interests.

   **Example:** Victorian furniture is an interest of mine.

   **Example:** The main character's romantic interest will be played by a non-professional actor.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Condition or quality of exciting concern or being of importance.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. Injury, or compensation for injury; damages.

   **Usage:** obsolete; rare; uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. The persons and companies interested in any particular business or measure, taken collectively.

   **Usage:** in-plural; often; uncountable; usually

   **Example:** the iron interest; the cotton interest

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

   **Usage:** attributive; historical; uncountable; usually
