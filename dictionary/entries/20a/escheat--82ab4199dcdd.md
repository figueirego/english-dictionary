## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The return, conversion, or vesting of property of a deceased person or closed business to the government (originally to a feudal lord) where there are no legal heirs or claimants. It is known as bona vacantia in the United Kingdom.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The property so reverted.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Plunder, booty.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. That which falls to one; a reversion or return.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To put (land, property) in escheat; to confiscate.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**
