### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Tending to create things, or having the ability to create; often, excellently, in a novel fashion, or any or all of these.

   **Example:** a creative dramatist who avoids cliche

   **Source synonyms:** pregnant; originative

   **Example:** a creative new solution to an old problem

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a created thing

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A type of set of natural numbers, related to mathematical logic.

   **Example:** a creative set

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences; set-theory

**Source sense R1.S4**

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** He is a visionary creative.
