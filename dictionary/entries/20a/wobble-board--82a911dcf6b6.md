### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An item of exercising equipment which challenges the sportsman to balance his weight on it as it could tilt in any direction.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A musical instrument consisting of a flat board which is held in the hands and shaken so that the board flexes in order to produce a rhythmic "whoop-whoop" noise.
