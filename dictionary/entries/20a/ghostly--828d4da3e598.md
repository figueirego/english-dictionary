### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of or pertaining to ghosts or spirits.

   **Example:** a ghostly figure with a hood

   **Example:** The graveyard was haunted by a ghostly figure of a young girl.

   **Example:** The ghostly moaning was heard from upstairs.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Spooky; frightening.

   **Example:** A ghostly hush fell.

   **Usage:** archaic
