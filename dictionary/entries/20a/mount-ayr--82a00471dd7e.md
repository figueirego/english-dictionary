### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A small town in Newton County, Indiana, United States. Derived from Mount Airy, North Carolina.

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A city, the county seat of Ringgold County, Iowa, United States. Named after Ayr, Scotland, Robert Burns' birthplace, "mount" signifying the highest point in the area.
