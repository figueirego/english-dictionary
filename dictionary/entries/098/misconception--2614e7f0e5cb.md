### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A mistaken belief, a wrong idea.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** There are several common misconceptions about the theory of relativity.

   **Example:** You're obviously under the misconception that I care about your problems.

   **Example:** He had the misconception that the word "misconception" meant becoming pregnant with a girl.
