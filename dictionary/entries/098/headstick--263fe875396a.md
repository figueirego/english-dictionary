### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A stick-like device strapped to a paralysed person's head to allow them to perform activities such as typing on a keyboard.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A straight piece of furniture placed at the head of a form, between the chase and the type.

   **Usage:** historical

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** media; printing; publishing
