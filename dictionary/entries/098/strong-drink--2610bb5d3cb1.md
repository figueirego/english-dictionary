## Noun

### Forms

- strong drinks — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An alcoholic beverage that has been distilled rather than only fermented and is thus stronger (having higher abv) than undistilled ones.

   **Usage:** countable; narrowly; uncountable

   **Example:** He had long been fond of drink, but now he was tending toward the strong drink especially.

   **Source synonyms:** spirits; hard liquor; hard drink; liquor; booze

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any alcoholic beverage, which is stronger (having abv) than nonalcoholic ones.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Would you like a soft drink or a strong drink tonight?

   **Source synonyms:** drink; hard drink; liquor; booze
