## Adjective · Record 1

### Forms

- more ironclad — comparative
- most ironclad — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Covered with iron, steel, or (loosely) any other tough metal; armor-plated.

   **Example:** Unlike the average vehicle, cash delivery vans are ironclad and almost unstoppable.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Solid or certain; not able to be disputed or questioned; irrefutable.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** The suspect had an ironclad alibi for his whereabouts on the night of the crime.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Rigorous; severe; exacting.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** an ironclad oath or pledge

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Stubborn; inflexible.

   **Usage:** figuratively

## Noun · Record 2

### Forms

- ironclads — plural

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A ship, vessel, or vehicle with a covering of iron, steel, or (loosely) any other tough metal.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. An armor-plated warship, (especially) one preceding the invention of harveyized steel.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. A wooden warehouse with an outer skin of corrugated metal.
