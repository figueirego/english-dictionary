### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A sudden outbreak, outburst or eruption (originally of flame, but now used more generally of any violent activity or emotion).

   **Source antonyms:** burnout; flameout

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A sudden intensification in activity.

   **Source antonyms:** burnout; flameout

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
