### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A formal notice to a user on a website (particularly YouTube or other video-sharing site) reprimanding the user for copyright infringement.

   **Usage:** Internet

   **Example:** I uploaded a copy of the film trailer to the site, but the distributor removed it and gave me a copystrike.
