### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. An (individual) apple tree raised from a pip (seed) (as opposed to one grafted); its fruit; a variety that came from such an individual (as its progenitor).

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. An (individual) apple tree raised from a pip (seed) (as opposed to one grafted); its fruit; a variety that came from such an individual (as its progenitor).
   1. Any of several such varieties that are good eating apples and tend to have a yellow or green skin with patches of red.

      **Example:** Cox's Orange Pippin

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. An excellent or admirable person or thing.
