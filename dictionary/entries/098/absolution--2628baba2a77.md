### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An absolving of sins from ecclesiastical penalties by an authority.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ecclesiastical; lifestyle; religion

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The forgiveness of sins, in a general sense.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The form of words by which a penitent is absolved.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An absolving, or setting free from guilt, sin, or penalty; forgiveness of an offense.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Governments granting absolution to the nation.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An acquittal, or sentence of a judge declaring an accused person innocent.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable; civil law

   **Source synonyms:** acquittance; assoilment; compurgation; absolution (obsolete); acquittance (obsolete); acquitment (obsolete); acquittal; assoilment (archaic); compurgation (historical); disculpation; exculpation; exoneration; not guilty; quittance; vindication
