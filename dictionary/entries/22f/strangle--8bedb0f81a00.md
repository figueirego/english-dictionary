### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To kill someone by squeezing the throat so as to cut off the oxygen supply.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** She strangled her husband and dissolved the body in acid.

   **Source synonyms:** scrag

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To choke, suffocate or throttle, whether the victim survives or not.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To stifle or suppress.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He strangled a scream.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To be killed by strangulation, or become strangled.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The cat slipped from the branch and strangled on its bell-collar.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To be stifled, choked, or suffocated in any manner.

   **Usage:** intransitive
