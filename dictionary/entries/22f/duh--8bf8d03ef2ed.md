### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A disdainful indication that something is obvious.

   **Example:** It's hot in the desert. - Well, duh!

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An indication of mock stupidity.

   **Example:** Duhhh, I'm Jasmine, I can't even tie my shoe laces right!

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A nonsensical utterance by an individual about to go unconscious, especially if they're dizzy.

   **Example:** Duuhh... I don't feel so good now...
