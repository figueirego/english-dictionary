### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To pull toward without touching.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** A magnet attracts iron filings.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To draw by moral, emotional or sexual influence; to engage or fix, as the mind, attention, etc.; to invite or allure.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to attract admirers

   **Example:** Advertising is designed to attract customers.

   **Example:** His big smile and brown eyes instantly attracted me.

   **Source synonyms:** beckon; appeal

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To incur.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Using the minibar in a hotel room attracts additional charges.
