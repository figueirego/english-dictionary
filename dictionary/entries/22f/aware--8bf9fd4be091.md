**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Vigilant or on one's guard against danger or difficulty.

   **Example:** Stay aware! Don't let your guard down.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Conscious or having knowledge of something; awake.

   **Example:** Are you aware of what is being said about you?

   **Example:** Gotta get going. I wasn’t aware that it was already so late.
