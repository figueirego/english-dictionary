### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A literary composition in the form of a letter or series of letters, especially one in verse.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A letter, especially one which is formal or issued publicly.

   **Usage:** humorous; literary

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A letter, especially one which is formal or issued publicly.
   1. Chiefly with a qualifying word, as in epistle dedicatory&#58; a letter of dedication addressed to a patron or reader published as a preface to a literary work.

      **Usage:** historical; humorous; literary; specifically

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. One of the books of the New Testament which was originally a letter issued by an apostle to an individual or a community.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An extract from a New Testament epistle (noun sense 3.1) or book other than a gospel which is read during a church service, chiefly the Eucharist.
