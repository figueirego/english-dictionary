### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A type of storage compartment with a lock, usually used to store personal possessions for public use, such as in schools, railway stations, place of work, gyms, sports centers.

   **Example:** The student placed her books in her locker when she arrived at school.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A lockable cubicle.

   **Example:** She was afraid to come out of the locker.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A storage compartment on a ship, not necessarily one that can be locked.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. One who locks something.

   **Usage:** rare

   **Example:** The locker of the trapped chest must be careful, so as not to spring the trap.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A locking differential.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** automotive; transport; vehicles

**Source sense R1.S6**

   **Usage:** historical

**Source sense R1.S7**
