### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Property to which individuals or corporations have certain exclusive property rights, but do not necessarily possess.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law; property
