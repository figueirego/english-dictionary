### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of travelling or sightseeing, particularly away from one's home.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** Mass tourism damaged the beach.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The industry in which such travels and sightseeing are organized.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** Tourism brings a lot of tax revenue to the area.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The act of visiting another region or jurisdiction for a particular purpose.

   **Usage:** figuratively; uncountable; usually

   **Example:** libel tourism; suicide tourism; sex tourism
