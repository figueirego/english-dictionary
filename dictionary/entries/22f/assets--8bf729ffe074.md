### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Any property or object of value that one possesses, usually considered as applicable to the payment of one's debts.

   **Usage:** plural; plural-normally

   **Example:** His assets are much greater than his liabilities.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. The left side of a balance sheet.

   **Usage:** plural; plural-normally

   **Example:** The assets must equal liabilities, otherwise there is an error in your balance sheet.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** accounting; business; finance

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. Sufficient estate; property sufficient in the hands of an executor or heir to pay the debts or legacies of the testator or ancestor to satisfy claims against it.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law
