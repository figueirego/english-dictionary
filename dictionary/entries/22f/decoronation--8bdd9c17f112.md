### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The removal of the crown of an ankylosed tooth while leaving the roots untouched.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** dentistry; medicine; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** coronectomy

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** dethroning; dethronement; accession
