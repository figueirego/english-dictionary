### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A return (e.g. to popularity, success, etc.) after an extended period of obscurity or underperformance.

   **Example:** That fad went out with the eighties, but I think it's making a comeback.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A retort or answer, particularly a quick or clever one.

   **Example:** I wish I had thought of a quick comeback for his comment.

   **Source synonyms:** rejoinder; retort

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An occurrence of an athlete or sports team in a competition overcoming a substantial disadvantage in points to win or draw.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports
