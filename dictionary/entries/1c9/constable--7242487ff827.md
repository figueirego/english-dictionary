<a id="r1-s2"></a>

2. A police officer or an officer with equivalent powers.

   **Usage:** UK

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The warden of a castle.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

   **Usage:** US

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

6. An elected head of a parish (also known as a connétable)

   **Usage:** Channel Islands
