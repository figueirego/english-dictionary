### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A woman, especially an attractive one, skilled at using magic; an alluring witch.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A beautiful, charming and irresistible woman.

   **Example:** She was the enchantress of men’s hearts.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A femme fatale.

   **Example:** His desire for that enchantress led him to financial ruin.
