### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Not yet hardened into bone or strengthened into sinew.

   **Usage:** literal

   **Example:** Most cartilage is not merely premature bone; but there is a grain of truth to the old notion that newborn creatures' bodies are yet in the gristle, as some bones are still developing.
