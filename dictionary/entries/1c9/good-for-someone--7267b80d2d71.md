## Interjection · Record 1

### Forms

- good on someone — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An exclamation of encouragement or congratulation to a person.

   **Example:** You got married? Good for you!

   **Example:** The president doesn’t dodge “what if” questions. Good for him!

   **Source synonyms:** well done; get you; bravissimo; bravo; bully; cock on (UK); congrats; congratulations; encore; fair fucks; full marks; get you (UK); good for someone; good for you; good going; good job; good on you (Australia; UK); good show; good work; hats off; hon (Ireland); hoorah; hooray; hurrah; hurray; huzza (rare); huzzah (rare); jolly good show (dated); keep it up; kudos; mazal tov; mazel tov; nice; nice one; proper job (UK); right on (US); shabash; there you go; way to go; well done; well said

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Sarcastically belittling a person for an unimportant achievement.

   **Example:** He has a diploma from Harvard? Good for him&#58; he's still not qualified for this job.

   **Source synonyms:** I don't care
