### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An amendment made in bad faith by a legislator who disagrees with the principles of a bill and seeks to make it useless rather than simply voting against it.

   **Example:** Senator Littlebrain's wrecking amendment made the bill nonsensical by deleting the clauses that said what the law would do.
