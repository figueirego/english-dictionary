### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To increase, to rise

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To reach or come to by way of increase; to arise or spring up because of growth or result, especially as the produce of money lent.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Environmental benefits that accrue to the area.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To be incurred as a result of the passage of time.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The monthly financial statements show all the actual but only some of the accrued expenses.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** accounting; business; finance

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To accumulate.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He has accrued nine sick days.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To become an enforceable and permanent right.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

   **Source synonyms:** inure
