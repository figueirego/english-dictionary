## Verb · Etymology 1 · Record 2

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To complete, to bring to perfection, to make whole.

   **Example:** We believe your addition will complement the team.

   **Source synonyms:** tie in

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To provide what the partner lacks and lack what the partner provides, thus forming part of a whole.

   **Example:** The flavors of the pepper and garlic complement each other, giving a very rich taste in combination.

   **Example:** I believe our talents really complement each other.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To change a voltage, number, color, etc. to its complement.
