### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The fine soft yellowish threads that are the stigmata of corn flowers, which are later enclosed inside the husk of corn ears next to the kernels on corn cobs.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Alternative form of cornsilk (a colour of yellow that is like that of these fibres)

   **Usage:** alt-of; alternative; uncountable
