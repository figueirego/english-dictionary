### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act or process of specializing.

   **Usage:** British; English; Oxford; US; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The area in which someone specializes.

   **Usage:** British; English; Oxford; US; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The adaptation of an organism to a specific environment, or adaptation of an organ to a particular function.

   **Usage:** British; English; Oxford; US; countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A proof, axiom, problem, or definition whose cases are completely covered by another, broader concept.

   **Usage:** British; English; Oxford; US; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** A triangle is a specialization of a polygon to one with exactly three sides.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences
