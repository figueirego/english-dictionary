### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The door at the main entrance to a building or house, normally fronts onto a street.

   **Usage:** literal

   **Example:** You'll find a light switch inside the front door on the left.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The entire main entrance to a building or house.

   **Usage:** broadly

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The habitual or designated main entrance to a room that has more than one door.

   **Example:** Entry is through the toilet's front door.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The normal portal page to a website.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The path of a pitch which starts inside and then slides over the plate.

   **Example:** His front door slider is hard to handle.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; baseball; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. vagina or penis, as a means of sexual intercourse, contrasted with the anus or "back door"

   **Usage:** slang
