### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To pick; to make the choice of; to select.

   **Example:** I chose a nice ripe apple from the fruit bowl.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To elect.

   **Example:** He was chosen as president in 1990.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To decide to act in a certain way.

   **Example:** I chose to walk to work today.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To prefer; to wish; to desire.
