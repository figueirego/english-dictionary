## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

- dozzles — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. the tobacco left at the bottom of a pipe and put on the top of the next fill&#58; dottle; in a general sense, a plug or a cap to top something off.

   **Usage:** chiefly North-country English dialectal

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. a paste flower on top of a pie cover.

   **Usage:** chiefly North-country English dialectal

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. the straw ornament on top of a haystack.

   **Usage:** chiefly North-country English dialectal

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A device, originally a heated sleave of fire clay, variously used to introduce molten metal to counter the formation of hollows in metal castings as they shrink while the mould cools. Now commonly called hot top or feeder head

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** hot top; dottle; feeder head
