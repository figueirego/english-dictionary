## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The method in which individuals are grouped together into families that live together.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A group of individuals who are related by blood and/or by marriage.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A group of family members who are posed together.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A taxonomic grouping at the family level.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A group of related items.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The strategy of putting related inventory items together.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The system of grouping children of differing ages together, used primarily at the preschool level.

   **Usage:** UK; uncountable
