### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To fall headlong forwards, notionally hitting one's face on the ground.

   **Usage:** literally

   **Example:** I tripped over my shoelace and fell flat on my face.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To fail miserably, especially in an obvious or embarrassing way.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** He tried to start his own business but fell flat on his face.
