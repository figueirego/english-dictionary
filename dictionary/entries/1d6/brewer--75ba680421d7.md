## Noun

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who brews (vocationally or avocationally); especially, one whose occupation is to prepare malt liquors, usually as part of an alemaking or beermaking process.

   **Example:** His great-grandfather was the brewer for the estate.

   **Source synonyms:** brewster

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A company that brews beer or ale.

   **Example:** By that time, Anheuser-Busch was the largest brewer in the region.

   **Source synonyms:** brewery
