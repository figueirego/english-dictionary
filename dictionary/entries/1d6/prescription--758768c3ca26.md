### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A written order from an authorized medical practitioner for provision of a medicine or other treatment, such as (ophthalmology) the specific lenses needed for a pair of glasses.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; pharmacy

   **Example:** The surgeon had written thousands of prescriptions for pain killers without proper examinations before the police raided the clinic.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; pharmacology; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** scrip; forescript; Rx; ℞

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The medicine or treatment provided by such an order.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** I need you to pick up gramma's prescriptions on your way home.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any plan of treatment or handling; the treatment or handling thus provided.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

   **Example:** Early to bed and early to rise is a prescription for a long, healthy, and terrible life.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Synonym of enactment, the act of establishing a law, regulation, etc., particularly in writing; an instance of this.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

   **Source synonyms:** enactment (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The act of establishing or formalizing ideal norms for language use, as opposed to describing the actual norms of such use; an instance of this.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. An established time period within which a right must be exercised and after which it is null and permanently unenforceable.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

   **Source synonyms:** extinctive prescription; liberative prescription

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. An established time period after which a person who has uninterruptedly, peacefully, and publicly used another's property acquires full ownership of it.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

   **Source synonyms:** acquisitive prescription; usucaption

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Synonym of self-restraint, limiting of one's actions especially according to a moral code or social conventions.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** self-restraint (synonym; synonym-of); limiting of one's actions especially according to a moral code or social conventions (synonym; synonym-of)
