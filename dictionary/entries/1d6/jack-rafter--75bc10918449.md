### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One of the shorter rafters used in constructing a hip roof.

   **Usage:** UK

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any secondary roof timber, such as the common rafters resting on purlins in a trussed roof; also, one of the pieces simulating extended rafters, used under the eaves in some styles of building.

   **Usage:** US
