### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The quality or state of being plural, or in the plural number.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The state of a pluralist; the holding of more than one ecclesiastical living at a time.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ecclesiastical; lifestyle; religion

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A social system that permits smaller groups within a society to maintain their individual cultural identities.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; sciences; social-science; sociology

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The belief that there should be diverse and competing centers of power in society.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; politics

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The acknowledgement of a diversity of political systems.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; politics

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The existence of differing legal systems in a population or area.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; philosophy; sciences
