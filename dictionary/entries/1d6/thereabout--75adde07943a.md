### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. About or near that place.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source synonyms:** thereabouts

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. About or around that date or time.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Dinner will be ready at six-thirty or thereabout.

   **Source synonyms:** thereabouts

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. About or near to that condition or quality.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source synonyms:** thereabouts

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Approximately that number or quantity.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The speed of light is 186,000 miles per second, right? Yes, thereabout.

   **Source synonyms:** thereabouts

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. About that or those&#58; concerning it or them.

   **Usage:** dated; not-comparable

   **Source synonyms:** thereon; thereupon; thereanent

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Near to that activity or situation.

   **Usage:** dated; figuratively; not-comparable

   **Source synonyms:** thereabouts
