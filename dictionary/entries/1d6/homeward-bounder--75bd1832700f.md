### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A vessel making a return trip to its home port or home country.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person making a return trip to their home country.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A roughly executed stitch used when repairing articles of clothing on a vessel nearing the end of a long voyage.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A goldmine which is highly productive, making the owners rich enough to abandon mining and return to their home country.

   **Usage:** Australia; obsolete
