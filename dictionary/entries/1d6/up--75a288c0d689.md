### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Indicating movement towards or location at a higher place or position.
   1. Away from the surface of the Earth or other planet; in opposite direction to the downward pull of gravity.

      **Usage:** not-comparable

      **Example:** I looked up and saw the airplane overhead.

      **Example:** Shoots grow up and roots grow down.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Indicating movement towards or location at a higher place or position.
   1. To or at a physically higher or more elevated position.

      **Usage:** not-comparable

      **Example:** All day we climbed up and up.

      **Example:** Lift the carpet up and look underneath.

      **Example:** He lives up in the hills.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Indicating movement towards or location at a higher place or position.
   1. To an upright or erect position.

      **Usage:** not-comparable

      **Example:** sit up; stand up; get up out of bed

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Indicating movement towards or location at a higher place or position.
   1. To a higher level of some quantity or notional quantity, such as price, volume, pitch, happiness, etc.

      **Usage:** figuratively; not-comparable

      **Example:** Gold has gone up with the uncertainty in the world markets.

      **Example:** Turn it up, I can barely hear it.

      **Example:** Listen to your voice go up at the end of a question.

      **Example:** Cheer up, the weekend's almost here.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Indicating movement in any other direction visualised as “up”.
   1. To or towards what is considered the top of something, irrespective of whether this is presently physically higher.

      **Usage:** not-comparable

      **Example:** Go back up to the top of the page.

      **Example:** As I lay on my back, a pain shot up from my toes to my chest.

      **Example:** Let’s move up to the front of the bus.

   1. To the north (as north is at the top of typical maps).

      **Usage:** not-comparable

      **Example:** I live in Florida, but I'm going up to New York to visit my family this weekend.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Indicating movement in any other direction visualised as “up”.
   1. Towards or at a central place, or any place that is visualised as ‘up’ by virtue of local features or local convention, or arbitrarily, irrespective of direction or elevation change.
