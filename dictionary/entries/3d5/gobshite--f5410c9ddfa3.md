### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who engages in nonsensical chatter or unwanted conversation.

   **Usage:** Ireland; UK; offensive; slang; vulgar

   **Example:** What’s that gobshite talking about now?

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** Ireland; UK; offensive; slang; vulgar

<a id="r1-s3"></a>
