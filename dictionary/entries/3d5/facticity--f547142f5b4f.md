### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The quality or state of being a fact.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source synonyms:** factuality; factualness

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In existentialism, the state of being in the world without any knowable reason for such existence, or of being in a particular state of affairs which one has no control over.

   **Usage:** specifically; uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; philosophy; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** dasein; thrownness

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A fact that is not changeable or that is assumed to be true without further evaluation.

   **Usage:** countable; usually

   **Source synonyms:** given; axiom; postulate
