### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To turn upside-down; to invert.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** The crisis turned the formulas that had seemed to work on their head.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To completely change.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** The global economic crisis has managed to turn stock exchanges worldwide on their head.
