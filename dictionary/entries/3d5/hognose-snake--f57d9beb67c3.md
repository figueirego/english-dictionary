### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A colubrid snake, from any of three genera, characterized by an upturned snout and notorious for playing dead when threatened.
   1. Heterodon (United States and northern Mexico)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A colubrid snake, from any of three genera, characterized by an upturned snout and notorious for playing dead when threatened.
   1. Leioheterodon (Madagascar)
