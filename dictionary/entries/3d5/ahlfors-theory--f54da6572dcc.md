### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A geometric counterpart to Nevanlinna theory that extends the applicability of the concept of covering surface (of a topological space) by defining a covering number (a generalised "degree of covering") applicable to any bordered Riemann surface equipped with a conformal Riemannian metric.

   **Usage:** uncountable; differential geometry

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** complex-analysis; mathematics; sciences
