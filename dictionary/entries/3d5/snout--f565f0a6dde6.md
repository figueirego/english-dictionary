### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The long, projecting nose, mouth, and jaw of a beast, as of pigs.

   **Example:** The pig rooted around in the dirt with its snout.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The front of the prow of a ship or boat.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A person's nose.

   **Usage:** derogatory

   **Example:** His glasses kept slipping further down onto his prominent snout.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The nozzle of a pipe, hose, etc.

   **Example:** If you place the snout right into the bucket, it won't spray as much.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The anterior prolongation of the head of a gastropod; a rostrum.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The anterior prolongation of the head of weevils and allied beetles; a rostrum.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Tobacco; cigarettes.

   **Usage:** UK; slang

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. The terminus of a glacier.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A police informer.

   **Usage:** slang

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. A butterfly in the nymphalid subfamily Libytheinae, notable for the snout-like elongation on their heads.
