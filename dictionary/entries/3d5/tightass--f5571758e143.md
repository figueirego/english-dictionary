### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Someone who does not know how to have fun, or who is so worried about insignificant things as to ruin any fun that anyone around them may be having.

   **Usage:** Canada; US; mildly; slang; vulgar

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Someone who is stingy, or who is tight with money.

   **Usage:** Canada; US; mildly; slang; vulgar
