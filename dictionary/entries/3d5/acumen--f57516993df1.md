**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Quickness of perception or discernment; penetration of mind; the faculty of nice discrimination; acuity of mind.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source synonyms:** sharpness; penetration; keenness; shrewdness; acuteness; acuity; wits; foxiness; intelligence; canniness

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences
