### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Powder obtained by grinding or milling cereal grains, especially wheat, or other foodstuffs such as soybeans and potatoes, and used to bake bread, cakes, and pastry.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The food made by grinding and bolting cleaned wheat (not durum or red durum) until it meets specified levels of fineness, dryness, and freedom from bran and germ, also containing any of certain enzymes, ascorbic acid, and certain bleaching agents.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually; US standards of identity

   **Source synonyms:** smeddum; plain flour; wheat flour; wheatmeal; white flour

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Powder of other material.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** almond flour

   **Example:** cassava flour

   **Example:** mustard flour

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Obsolete form of flower.

   **Usage:** alt-of; obsolete; uncountable; usually

   **Alternative form of:** [flower](../301/flower--c06b0cfe0cc5.md)
