**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An integer which is one more than two raised to a power which is itself a power of two (i.e., is expressible in the form 2^(2ⁿ)+1 for some n&gt;0); equivalently, a number that is one more than two raised to some power (is expressible as 2ⁿ+1) and is prime.
