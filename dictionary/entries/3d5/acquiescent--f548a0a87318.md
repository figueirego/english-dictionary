### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Willing to acquiesce, accept or agree to something without objection, protest or resistance.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Resting satisfied or submissive; disposed tacitly to submit.

   **Example:** an acquiescent policy
