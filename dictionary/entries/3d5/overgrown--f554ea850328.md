### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having large numbers of plants which have become too big, and are hence spoiling the picturesqueness of a garden.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Having grown bigger without developing maturity or changing character.

   **Usage:** attributive; derogatory; mildly

   **Example:** He spends so much money on new technology&#58; he's like an overgrown schoolboy buying toys.
