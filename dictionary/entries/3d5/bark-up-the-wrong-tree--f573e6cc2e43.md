**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To take the wrong approach to a situation; to follow a false lead; to attempt to solve a problem using mistaken assumptions about its true nature.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** The investigator spent days trying to prove the butler committed the murder, but it turns out he was barking up the wrong tree.
