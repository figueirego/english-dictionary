### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; mechanical; mechanical-engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** journalism; media

   **Source synonyms:** crossheading
