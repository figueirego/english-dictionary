### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A line or mark made by folding or doubling any pliable substance; hence, a similar mark, however produced.

   **Example:** His pants had a nice sharp crease.

   **Example:** His shirt was brand new with visible creases from its store fold.

   **Source synonyms:** rumple

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One of the white lines drawn on the pitch to show different areas of play; especially the popping crease, but also the bowling crease and the return crease.

   **Example:** DRS showed he was out of the crease in a controversial run out review.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; cricket; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The circle around the goal, which no offensive players may enter during play, unless after scoring.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; games; hobbies; lacrosse; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The area in front of each goal.

   **Source synonyms:** goal crease
