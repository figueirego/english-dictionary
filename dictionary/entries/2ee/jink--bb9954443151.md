### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To make a quick evasive turn or turns to confuse pursuers, incoming fire, etc.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To cause a vehicle to make a quick evasive turn.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. In the games of spoilfive and forty-five, to win the game by taking all five tricks; also, to attempt to win all five tricks, losing what has been already won if unsuccessful.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** card-games; games

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To elude; to cheat.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To clink or chink against.
