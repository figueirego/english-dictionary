### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Hatred or fear of, or prejudice against, transgender and transsexual people or transness in general, which may manifest in prejudice against people who do not conform to social gender roles (gender-nonconforming people) in general.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The preference of pairs of high-trans effect soft ligands to avoid being mutually trans by becoming cis and having other low-trans effect hard ligands trans to themselves.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually
