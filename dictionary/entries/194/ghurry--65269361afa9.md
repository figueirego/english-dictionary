## Noun · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Forms

- ghurries — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A clepsydra or water clock, consisting of a floating cup with a small hole in it, adjusted so that it fills and sinks in a fixed time.

   **Usage:** India; obsolete

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The gong by which the time so indicated is struck.

   **Usage:** India; obsolete

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A clock or watch.

   **Usage:** India; broadly; obsolete

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An hour, or some indefinite period of time.

   **Usage:** India; obsolete
