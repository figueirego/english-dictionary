### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Something suggested (with subsequent adposition being for)

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** make a suggestion

   **Example:** I have a small suggestion for fixing this&#58; try lifting the left side up a bit.

   **Example:** Traffic signs seem to be more of a suggestion than an order.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The act of suggesting.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Suggestion often works better than explicit demand.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Something implied, which the mind is liable to take as fact.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** He’s somehow picked up the suggestion that I like peanuts.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; psychology; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The act of exercising control over a hypnotised subject by communicating some belief or impulse by means of words or gestures; the idea so suggested.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Information, insinuation, speculation, as opposed to a sworn testimony and evidence.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law
