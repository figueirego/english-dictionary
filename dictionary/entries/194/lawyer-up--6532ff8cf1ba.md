### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To exercise one's right to legal representation; to engage the services of a lawyer.

   **Usage:** US; informal; intransitive

   **Example:** You'd better lawyer up!

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To conduct matters in accord with legal formalities or so as to avoid legal risk.

   **Usage:** US; informal; intransitive

   **Example:** Whenever we do business with those guys, we lawyer up to protect ourselves.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To arrange in a way reflecting legal advice.

   **Usage:** informal; transitive

   **Example:** By the time we finished lawyering up the agreement, we didn't want to sign it.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business
