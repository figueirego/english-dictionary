**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A journey undertaken to accomplish some task.

      **Usage:** archaic; literary

      **Source synonyms:** run

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A journey undertaken to accomplish some task.
   1. A mundane mission of no great consequence, concerning household or business affairs (dropping items by, doing paperwork, going to a friend's house, etc.)

      **Example:** The errands before he could start the project included getting material at the store and getting the tools he had lent his neighbors.

      **Example:** I'm going to town on some errands.

      **Source synonyms:** run

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The purpose of such a journey.

**Source sense R1.S4**
