### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To bathe in warmth; to be exposed to pleasant heat.

   **Usage:** intransitive; usually

   **Example:** to bask in the sun

   **Example:** She shivered until she got to the cozy fireplace, where she could bask in the heat coming off it.

   **Source context (verbatim):** followed by "in"

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To bathe in warmth; to be exposed to pleasant heat.
   1. To expose oneself to heat, especially sunlight, in order to raise body temperature, as a form of thermoregulation.

      **Usage:** intransitive; usually

      **Example:** My lizard likes to spend his days basking on the stone near the lamp.

      **Source context (verbatim):** of reptiles; followed by "in"

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To take great pleasure or satisfaction; to feel warmth or happiness.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive; usually

   **Example:** to bask in someone’s favour

   **Example:** I basked in her love.

   **Source context (verbatim):** followed by "in"
