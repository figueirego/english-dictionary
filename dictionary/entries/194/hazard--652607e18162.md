### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A game of chance played with dice, usually for monetary stakes, popular mainly from 14th c. to 19th c.

   **Usage:** countable; historical; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Chance; accident, unpredictability.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The risk of some loss or harm; danger, peril, the chance of an adverse outcome.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** He encountered the enemy at the hazard of his reputation and life.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A situation involving such a risk of harm or loss; (later also) a person or object which poses such a risk, something seen as a source of danger or difficulty.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** The video game involves guiding a character on a skateboard past all kinds of hazards.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Each of the various openings or galleries around the side of a court.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; real tennis

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Anything that is hazarded or risked, such as a stake in gambling.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A stroke which results in potting a ball, whether the object ball (winning hazard) or the player's ball (losing hazard).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; billiards; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** golf; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A cabstand, especially in Dublin.

   **Usage:** Ireland; countable; historical; uncountable

<a id="r1-s10"></a>
