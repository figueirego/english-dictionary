## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Not causative.
   1. Not a cause.

      **Usage:** not-comparable

      **Example:** The subsequent investigation found the earlier inadequate maintenance to be inappropriate but noncausative.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Not causative.
   1. Not involving, or affected by, causality.

      **Usage:** not-comparable

      **Example:** Such statistical analysis can establish correlation but cannot tell us whether the correlation is proximally causative, distally causative, or noncausative.
