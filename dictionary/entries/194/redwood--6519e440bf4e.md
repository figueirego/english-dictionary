### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A tree of the species Sequoia sempervirens.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; USDA-preferred term

   **Example:** This redwood deck will last forever.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of the evergreen conifers belonging to the genus Sequoia in the wide sense.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Wood of the species Sequoia sempervirens.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** This planter is made of redwood.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Wood of the species Pinus sylvestris.

   **Usage:** UK; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A tree of species Manilkara bidentata harvested for timber and latex.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Timber from the Manilkara bidentata tree.

   **Usage:** uncountable
