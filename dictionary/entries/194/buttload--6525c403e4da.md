### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A regional English measure of capacity of a heavy cart (a butt), containing 6 seams, or 48 bushels, equivalent to 384 gallons.

   **Usage:** UK; West-Country; obsolete

   **Source synonyms:** buttful

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A large amount carried in a butt.

   **Usage:** New-England; Southern-US; UK; dated

   **Example:** We spent all day Sunday and picked up a buttload of pecans.
