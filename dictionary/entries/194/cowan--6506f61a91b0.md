### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A Scottish surname from Scottish Gaelic; an anglicization of mac Eoghainn (“son of Ewen”)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A surname from Irish; an anglicization of mac Eógain (“son of Owen”)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A Jewish surname from Hebrew; a variant of Cohen.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An outer suburb of Sydney in Hornsby Shire, New South Wales, Australia. Apparently an anglicization of a Yuin-Kuric word meaning “big water”.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A town in western Manitoba, Canada.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A place in the United States&#58;
   1. A census-designated place in Stanislaus County, California.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A place in the United States&#58;
   1. An unincorporated community in Monroe Township, Delaware County, Indiana.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A place in the United States&#58;
   1. An unincorporated community in Buffalo Township, Union County, Pennsylvania.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A place in the United States&#58;
   1. A city in Franklin County, Tennessee; named after Dr. James Benjamin Cowan, a Civil War-era doctor whose family had lived in the area since the early 1800s.
