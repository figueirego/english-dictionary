### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who looks or stares; a gawker; a nosy or overly inquisitive person.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** After the accident, I had to shoo away a bunch of looky-loos.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A look at something; a glance; an opportunity to look around.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** I brought the inspector in for a looky-loo.

   **Example:** As I walked by, I couldn't help but take a looky-loo.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A person who comes into a store and looks around but doesn't buy anything.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Example:** I was busy all day, but barely made any commissions because I spent all my time with a couple looky-loos.
