### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The fundamental force of attraction which exists between all matter in the universe that tends to draw bodies towards each other, due to matter causing the curvature of spacetime. It is the weakest of the four fundamental interactions, and possesses a gauge boson known as the graviton.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

   **Source synonyms:** gravity
