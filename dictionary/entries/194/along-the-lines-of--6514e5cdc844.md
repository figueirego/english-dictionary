### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Similar to, but not exactly equal.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I was thinking along the lines of writing to the local paper, to see if we can get some reaction.

   **Source synonyms:** on the lines of

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see along, the, lines, of.

   **Example:** The shape is folded along the lines of symmetry.
