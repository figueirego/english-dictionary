### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The ratio between the amount of time in which the clock's signal is high and the clock's period.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; electrical-engineering; electricity; electromagnetism; electronics; energy; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The ratio between the percentage of time when a device is performing its work (duty) and the percentage of time when it is not working (e.g., idling, coasting, or turned off).

   **Usage:** generally

   **Example:** motors that are not designed for 80% duty cycles because that was never their appropriate use case

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences
