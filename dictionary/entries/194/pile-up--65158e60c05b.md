### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To form a pile, stack, or heap.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The kids piled up their boots and coats by the back door.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To collect or accumulate, as a backlog.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** The requests piled up while she was away.

   **Source synonyms:** head up

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To sustain a motor vehicle accident; to crash.

   **Usage:** intransitive
