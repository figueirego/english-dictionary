### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The filmmaking technique of shooting foreground action against an evenly-lit monochromatic background for the purpose of removing the background from the scene and replacing it with a different image or scene.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** broadcasting; film; media; television

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The visual effect resulting from this technique as well as the colored screen itself, especially blue.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** broadcasting; film; media; television

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
