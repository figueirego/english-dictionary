### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who does not respond.

   **Example:** The court sent "requests to show cause" to all questionnaire non-responders.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person who does not show an immune response to a virus after being vaccinated against it; someone who does not react normally to a drug.

   **Example:** A vaccine non-responder is a person who does not develop protective surface antibodies after completing two full series of injections.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences
