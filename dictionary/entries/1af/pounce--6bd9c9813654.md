### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r4-s1"></a>

**Source sense R4.S1**

1. To leap into the air intending to seize someone or something.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The kitten pounced at the ball I threw to it.

   **Example:** She pounced on the young man, because she loved him and wanted him for herself.

<a id="r4-s2"></a>

**Source sense R4.S2**

2. To attack suddenly by leaping.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** I was awakened from a dead sleep by my child pouncing on top of me from out of nowhere.

<a id="r4-s3"></a>

**Source sense R4.S3**

3. To eagerly seize an opportunity.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** I pounced on the chance to get promoted.

   **Example:** While he was out of town on vacation, I pounced, leaking the photos.

   **Example:** Why would I talk to the press and give them a chance to pounce on me?

<a id="r4-s4"></a>

**Source sense R4.S4**

4. To strike or seize with the talons; to pierce, as with the talons.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r4-s5"></a>

**Source sense R4.S5**

5. To stamp holes in; to perforate.

   **Usage:** transitive
