### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Large number, amount, or extent; profusion.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; singular; usually

   **Example:** We have a great deal of territory to cover.

   **Example:** Few expressed any great deal of confidence in the legislative branch.

   **Example:** The audience is generally unaware of the great deal of work that goes into its creation.

   **Example:** They didn't realize he had this great deal of intellectual depth.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see great, deal.

   **Example:** I got a great deal on a motorcycle.

   **Example:** There are plenty of great deals on flights.
