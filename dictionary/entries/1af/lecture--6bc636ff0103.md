### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A spoken lesson or exposition, usually delivered to a group.

   **Example:** During class today the professor delivered an interesting lecture.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A class that primarily consists of a (weekly or other regularly held) lecture (as in sense 1), usually at college or university.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** We will not have lecture tomorrow.

   **Example:** Lecture notes are online.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A berating or scolding, especially if lengthy, formal or given in a stern or angry manner.

   **Example:** I really don't want you to give me a lecture about my bad eating habits.

   **Source synonyms:** dressing down; going over; sermon; berating; browbeating; chiding; chockling (UK; dialectal); correption (obsolete); dirdum (Northern-England; Scotland); down the banks (Ireland; dated; slang); dressing down; going over; jawing; lecture; objurgation (uncommon); rixation (obsolete); sermon; scolding; throughgoing (Scotland)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The act of reading.

   **Usage:** obsolete

   **Example:** the lecture of Holy Scripture
