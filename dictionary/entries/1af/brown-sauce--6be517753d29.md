### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A sauce made by the addition of stock to a heated and browned mixture of butter and flour.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of many brands of bottled sauce based on malt vinegar, fruit and spices.

   **Usage:** Ireland; UK; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** HP Sauce is a popular variety of brown sauce.
