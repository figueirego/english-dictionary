### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Too implausible to be credible; beyond belief.

   **Usage:** literal

   **Source synonyms:** noncredible; unbelievable

   **Source antonyms:** believable; credible

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Amazing; astonishing; awe-inspiring.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** He was so wrapped up in watching the incredible special effects that he couldn't keep track of the story.

   **Source synonyms:** awesome; unbelievable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Marvellous; profoundly affecting; wonderful; excellent.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** I had such an incredible slice of pizza last night that I simply can't think about anything else.
