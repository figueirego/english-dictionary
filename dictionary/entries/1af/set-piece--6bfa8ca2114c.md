### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A piece of freestanding stage scenery.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any carefully planned sequence of operations, especially as part of a military operation.

   **Example:** set-piece battle

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An elaborate and interesting scene in a movie or video game, usually the most important and visually iconic scene in the work.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** broadcasting; film; media; television; video-games

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any planned strategy that a team uses after play is restarted with a free kick, penalty kick, corner kick, goal kick, throw-in or kickoff.

   **Example:** Roberto Carlos is deadly from set pieces.
