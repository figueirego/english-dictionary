### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who, or that which, consumes.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Someone who trades money for goods or services as an individual.

   **Example:** This new system favours the consumer over the producer.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** economics; sciences

   **Source antonyms:** producer

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The consumer base of a product, service or business.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** Our consumers are upwardly mobile and middle-class.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**
