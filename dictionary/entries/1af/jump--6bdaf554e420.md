### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To propel oneself rapidly upward, downward and/or in any horizontal direction such that momentum causes the body to become airborne.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The boy jumped over a fence.

   **Example:** Kangaroos are known for their ability to jump high.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To cause oneself to leave an elevated location and fall downward.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** She is going to jump from the diving board.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To pass by means of a spring or leap; to overleap.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to jump a stream

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

   **Usage:** intransitive

**Source sense R1.S5**

   **Usage:** intransitive
