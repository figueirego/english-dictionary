### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A man who is effeminate or who lacks some putative masculine virtue.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In computer programming circles, a person far removed from practice and concerned only with academic matters, unwilling to "get their hands dirty".
