### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A change whereby something goes from one place to another; a state of progression from one place to another; a change of position with respect to time.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

   **Source synonyms:** move; movement

   **Source antonyms:** rest; stasis

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A parliamentary action to propose something. A similar procedure in any official or business meeting.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** The motion to amend is now open for discussion.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An entertainment or show, especially a puppet show.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. from κίνησις (kinesis); any change. Traditionally of four types&#58; generation and corruption, alteration, augmentation and diminution, and change of place.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; philosophy; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Movement of the mind, desires, or passions; mental act, or impulse to any action; internal activity.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A formal request, oral or written, made to a judge or court of law to obtain an official court ruling or order for a legal action to be taken by, or on behalf of, the movant.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A movement of the bowels; the product of such movement.

   **Usage:** countable; euphemistic; uncountable

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Change of pitch in successive sounds, whether in the same part or in groups of parts. (Conjunct motion is that by single degrees of the scale. Contrary motion is when parts move in opposite directions. Disjunct motion is motion by skips. Oblique motion is when one part is stationary while another moves. Similar or direct motion is when parts move in the same direction.)

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A puppet, or puppet show.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. A piece of moving mechanism, such as on a steam locomotive.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; mechanical; mechanical-engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. Success; achievements, especially those that others cannot match.

   **Usage:** slang; uncountable
