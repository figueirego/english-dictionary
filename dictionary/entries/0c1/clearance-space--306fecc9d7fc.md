### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In a steam engine, the space enclosed in one end of the cylinder, between the valve and the piston, at the beginning of a stroke, including the space caused by the piston's clearance and the space in ports, passageways, etc.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
