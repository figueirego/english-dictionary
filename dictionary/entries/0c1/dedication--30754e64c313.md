## Noun

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˌdɛdɪˈkeɪʃən/ — source labels: UK; US

### Forms

- dedications — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of dedicating or the state of being dedicated.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** work with dedication

   **Example:** Her dedication is admirable.

   **Source synonyms:** dedicatedness

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A note addressed to a patron or friend, prefixed to a work of art as a token of respect, esteem, or affection.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** Her dedication for the book was only a cryptic "Once again, for T".

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The event, or the ceremony celebrating it, marking an official completion, opening, or beginning.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** The building's dedication is scheduled for next week.

   **Source synonyms:** inauguration

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The deliberate or negligent surrender of all rights to property.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** the dedication of this right-of-way

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law
