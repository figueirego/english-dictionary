### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Fairness, impartiality, or justice as determined in light of "natural law" or "natural right".

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source antonyms:** inequity; iniquity

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Various related senses originating with the Court of Chancery in late Medieval England
   1. The power of a court of law having extra-statutory discretion, to decide legal matters and to provide legal relief apart from, though not in violation of, the prevailing legal code; in some cases, a court "sitting in equity" may provide relief to a complainant should the code be found either inapplicable or insufficient to do so.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Various related senses originating with the Court of Chancery in late Medieval England
   1. A right which accrues to a party in a transaction because of the nature of the transaction itself, and which is exercisable upon a change of circumstances or conditions; in other words, an equitable claim.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Various related senses originating with the Court of Chancery in late Medieval England
   1. The body of law which was developed in the English Court of Chancery, which Court had extra-statutory discretion, and is now administered alongside the common law of Britain.

      **Usage:** England; countable; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law
