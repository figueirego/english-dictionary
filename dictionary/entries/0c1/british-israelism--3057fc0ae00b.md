## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A movement that holds the view that the people of England (or, more broadly, Great Britain) are genetically, racially, and linguistically the direct descendants of the Ten Lost Tribes of ancient Israel.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** Anglo-Israelism
