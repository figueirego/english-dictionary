## Adjective · Record 2

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Used to identify something as normally being in the area of the fireplace.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** A hearthside rug.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Pertaining to an activity normal to the household, usually associated with the living-room, whether or not it has a real fireplace.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Every Wednesday we have a hearthside meeting of the family.

   **Example:** This is a good hearthside book.
