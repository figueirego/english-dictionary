### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who plays a wind instrument, especially a bugler in the army.

   **Usage:** US; dated; slang

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A sailing ship; especially a large, iron-hulled, square-rigged ship with three or more masts.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

   **Usage:** US; slang
