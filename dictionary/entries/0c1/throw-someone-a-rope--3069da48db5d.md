### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To rescue someone by providing them with help, such as material aid, financial aid, a face-saving excuse, or a clue.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** He's out there making a fool of himself. Someone should throw him a rope.

   **Source synonyms:** save someone's life; save someone's skin; save someone's bacon; pull someone's bacon out of the fire; give someone a hand; lend a hand; bail out

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; to rescue someone from drowning by tossing a rope for them to grab onto and be dragged to safety.

   **Example:** He's out there fighting the riptide. We need to throw him a rope.
