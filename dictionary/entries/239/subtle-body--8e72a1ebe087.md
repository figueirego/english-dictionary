### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The second of three (or more) bodies that make up the fully embodied human in various Hindu philosophies.

   **Usage:** Hinduism

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; philosophy; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The embodiment of the soul or mind; part of the bodymind of the human being in syncretic, esoteric philosophy.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; mysticism; occult; parapsychology; philosophy; pseudoscience; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Those parts of the septenary, the 'seven principles of man' which constitute each human being, contrasted with the gross body, the physical human body.
