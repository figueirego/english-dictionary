### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To form one or more corners.

   **Usage:** dated; intransitive

   **Example:** Those two properties cornered up in the vicinity of Main Street.

   **Example:** The fenceposts will corner up the yard.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To sell drugs on a street corner; to go to such a spot to sell drugs.

   **Usage:** slang; African-American Vernacular

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To be in a corner (of a room); to trap or be trapped by assailants in the corner of a room.

   **Usage:** informal

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. to corner or be cornered (To trap in a position of great difficulty or hopeless embarrassment).

   **Usage:** broadly; informal
