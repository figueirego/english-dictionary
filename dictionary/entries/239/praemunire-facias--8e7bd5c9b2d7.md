**Source sense R1.S1**

1. More fully writ of praemunire facias&#58; a writ directing a sheriff to forewarn a person that they must appear before a court to answer a criminal charge of having brought a suit in or obeyed a foreign (especially papal) court or authority, thus challenging the supremacy of the Crown; a praemunire.

   **Usage:** England; historical
