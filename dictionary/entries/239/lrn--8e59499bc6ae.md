### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Initialism of luminous red nova&#58; an astronomical event, believed to be a type of stellar explosion, intermediate in power between a nova and a supernova, and such that as the explosive debris expands it reddens and cools, unlike a nova or supernova, in which thinning explosive debris exposes hotter interior layers.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; initialism

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astronomy; natural-sciences
