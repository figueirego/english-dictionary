## Adverb · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈʌð.əˌwaɪz/ — source labels: UK
- IPA: /ˈʌð.ɚˌwaɪz/ — source labels: General-American

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Differently, in another way.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** You may have a point, but I think otherwise.

   **Example:** Do you want the newest items put up top, or do you want it done otherwise?

   **Example:** Could I do otherwise than smile?

   **Example:** It is not permitted to sell or otherwise distribute any copies.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** manner

   **Source synonyms:** elsewise; contrarily; contrastingly

   **Source antonyms:** likewise; thuswise; nohow; in no way; not at all; by no means

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In different circumstances; or else.

   **Usage:** conjunctive; not-comparable

   **Example:** I’m not well today, otherwise I would have helped.

   **Example:** You have to open your umbrella, otherwise you'll get wet.

   **Source synonyms:** elsewise

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. In all other respects; apart from that; except for this.

   **Usage:** conjunctive; not-comparable

   **Example:** He lost his temper once in a while. Otherwise he behaved rationally.

   **Example:** There was one rotten apple in an otherwise perfect batch.

   **Source synonyms:** elsewise
