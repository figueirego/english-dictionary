### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. At a diagonal to another; of four corners, those diagonal to another.

   **Usage:** Canada; US; not-comparable

   **Example:** The Empire State Building and the old Altman's Department store are catercorner, at Fifth Avenue and East 34th Street, with the ESB at the southwest, and Altman's at the northeast.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Uneven, not square, as mislaid stones or people with a limping gait.

   **Usage:** UK; dialectal; not-comparable; obsolete
