### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having three axes.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** triaxial graph paper

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Having three planes of symmetry (e.g. having the shape of a scalene ellipsoid) and thus two axes of rotation

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astronomy; mathematics; natural-sciences; sciences
