### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An internal combustion engine, with six cylinders mounted in a straight line along the crankcase with all the pistons driving a common crankshaft.

   **Example:** He spent extra for a V8 because he doubted that a straight-six would be enough for towing his trailer.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** automotive; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; transport; vehicles

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A vehicle with such an engine.

   **Usage:** dated; metonymically

   **Example:** His grandpa was driving a straight-six at a time when few people had one.
