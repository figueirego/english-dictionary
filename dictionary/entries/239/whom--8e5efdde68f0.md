### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. What person or people; which person or people.
   1. As the object of a verb.

      **Usage:** formal; interrogative; often

      **Example:** Whom did you ask?

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. What person or people; which person or people.
   1. As the object of a preposition.

      **Usage:** formal; interrogative; often

      **Example:** To whom are you referring?

      **Example:** With whom were you talking?

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used to refer to a previously mentioned person or people.

   **Usage:** formal; often; relative

   **Example:** That is the woman whom I spoke to earlier. (defining)

   **Example:** Mr Smith, whom we all know well, will be giving the speech. (non-defining)

   **Example:** He's a person with whom I work (defining)

   **Example:** We have ten employees, half of whom are carpenters. (non-defining)

   **Example:** There is a new girl in the class whom I consider to be a wunderkind.

   **Example:** She felt miserable unless she had neighbors with whom to quarrel regularly.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The person(s) whom; whomever.

   **Usage:** formal; often; fused relative; archaic outside set patterns; fused relative; archaic outside set patterns

   **Example:** To whom it may concern, all business of John Smith Ltd. has now been transferred to Floggitt &amp; Runne.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Also used with names of collective nouns that are groups of people, especially singularly-named musical groups or sports teams.

   **Usage:** formal; informal; often; especially non-US

   **Example:** Whom was Lemmy bassist for? Motörhead!
