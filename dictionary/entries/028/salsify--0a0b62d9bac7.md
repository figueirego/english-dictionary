### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of several flowering plants, of the genus Tragopogon, most of which have purple flowers.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of several flowering plants, of the genus Tragopogon, most of which have purple flowers.
   1. Tragopogon porrifolius, a plant with linear leaves; cultivated for its light-skinned edible root and herbal properties. Known in particular as "white salsify" among other common names

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Pseudopodospermum hispanicum (syn. Scorzonera hispanica), a plant similar in flavor to T. porrifolius, with lanceolate leaves; cultivated for its dark-skinned edible root. Known in particular as "black salsify" among other common names

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The edible root of these plants.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually
