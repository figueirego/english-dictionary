### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To turn (a topic) into a museum subject; to create museums devoted to (a topic).

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To make (an object) museumworthy.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To turn (an object) into a museum piece.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To turn (a place or building) into an open-air museum, a house museum, a heritage site or a curated environment of some sort, to be preserved and presented to the public much like a museum piece.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To turn (a culture or community) into a living exhibit.

   **Usage:** derogatory; often; transitive
