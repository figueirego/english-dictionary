**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Indicates thinking or pondering.

   **Example:** Hmm... that's an interesting idea.

   **Example:** Hmm... I just can't decide between the two.

   **Example:** Hmm... creation or evolution? That’s the big question.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Indicates surprise or cluelessness; especially a response by a person who is questioned or addressed when not expecting it.

   **Example:** "You there! Where are you going?" "Hmm? Oh, I just need to get a drink."

   **Example:** "Could you pass me the paring knife?" "Hmm? What's a paring knife?"

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A demand for an answer to a question.

   **Example:** Just where were you until 2 a.m.? Hmm?
