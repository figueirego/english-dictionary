### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Initialism of personal communication; used to mark information obtained directly from communicating with a scholar instead of via analysis of one of their academic papers.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; initialism

   **Example:** John Smith (p.c.) informs me that money does grow on trees in Cockaigne.
