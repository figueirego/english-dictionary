### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any abrupt and inexplicable transition or occurrence.

   **Example:** Having a costumed superhero abduct the vicar was an utter non sequitur in the novel.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; narratology; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any invalid argument in which the conclusion cannot be logically deduced from the premises.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; logic; mathematics; philosophy; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** fallacy

   **Source antonyms:** sequitur

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A statement that does not logically follow a statement that preceded it.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A kind of pun that uses a change of word, subject, or meaning to make a joke of the listener’s expectation.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** comedy; entertainment; lifestyle
