### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The quality of being delicate.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Something appealing, usually a pleasing food, especially a choice dish of a certain culture suggesting rarity and refinement.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** a Chinese delicacy

   **Source synonyms:** ambrosia

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
