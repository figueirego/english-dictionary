### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A noun used as an adjective.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** attributive noun; noun adjunct\[Appendix&#58;Glossary

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A specific part of speech, categorized in Japanese as 形容動詞 (keiyō dōshi). Some of these words can be used as regular nouns, and all can be used as adjectives when followed by the postfix な (na), in contrast to Japanese common adjectives, 形容詞 (keiyōshi).

   **Source context (verbatim):** in Japanese grammar

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

   **Source context (verbatim):** in Czech and Slovak grammar
