### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Given a triangle ABC and a point P on its circumcircle, the three closest points to P on lines AB, AC, and BC are collinear. The line through these points is the Simson line of P.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences
