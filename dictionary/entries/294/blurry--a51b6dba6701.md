### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Not clear, crisp, or focused; having fuzzy edges.

   **Example:** If I take off my glasses, everything close up looks blurry.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of an image

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Not clear; lacking well-defined boundaries.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** It would seem that the line between flirting and sexual harassment has become quite blurry.
