## Adverb · Etymology 1 · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈsaɪdweɪz/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Forms

- more sideways — comparative
- most sideways — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. With a side to the front.

   **Example:** He builds houses sideways, with the front door on the side.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Towards one side.

   **Example:** A bishop moves only diagonally; a rook, only sideways, forward, and back.

   **Example:** He looked sideways at the new arrival, wondering who she was.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. Askance; sidelong.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. Neither upward nor downward.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** The economy has been moving sideways for several months now.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. Not as planned; towards a worse outcome.

   **Usage:** US; colloquial

   **Example:** We realized the project could go sideways very quickly if we didn't get the sales and marketing people on our side.

   **Source synonyms:** south
