**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A statistic used to gauge the similarity of two samples. It is equal to twice the number of elements common to both sets, divided by the sum of the number of elements in each set.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences; statistics
