### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An animal that attacks and kills humans for food, such as certain tigers or sharks; any animal that consumes human flesh.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A carnivorous plant large enough to consume a human; a man-eating plant.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A cannibal; a human that eats other humans.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A seductive dangerous woman, often readily taking and discarding male romantic partners.

   **Usage:** broadly; slang
