### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Marked by pessimism and little hopefulness; expecting the worst.

   **Example:** a pessimistic view of the future

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Pertaining to the worst-case scenario.

   **Example:** a pessimistic estimate

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Taking out exclusive locks on data to prevent conflicts with other processes that might modify it.

   **Example:** pessimistic concurrency; pessimistic locking
