### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Hoisted, lifted off the ground, or propped up using a jack.

   **Example:** Do you want to rotate the tires while we have the car jacked up?

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Describes a 4x4 automobile that has a "lift kit", raising the body and/or frame higher than stock.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Example:** He took us for a ride in his awesome new jacked up truck.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** automotive; transport; vehicles

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Significantly increased or expanded.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** It's hard to make ends meet with the jacked up price of gas.

   **Example:** The new jacked up triple-barrel cannons helped turn the tide of the war.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Under the influence of stimulants; high.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Example:** They were all jacked up on coke.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Stimulated, excited.

   **Usage:** slang

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Wrecked; damaged; ruined; injured.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Example:** That jacked up refrigerator hasn't moved from that curb in months.

   **Example:** My elbow is all jacked up from playing tennis.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Reprehensible; objectionable.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Example:** Dude, your girlfriend left you for your brother? That's jacked up.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Bankrupt; insolvent; ruined; done for.

   **Usage:** Australia; West-Country; dialectal; obsolete

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. Absent, having quit, given up, or having abandoned one's post.

   **Usage:** Australia; West-Country; dialectal; obsolete
