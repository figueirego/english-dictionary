## Noun · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /smʌd͡ʒ/ — source labels: UK; US

### Forms

- smudges — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A blemish or smear, especially a dark or sooty one.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** There was a smudge on the paper.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Dense smoke, such as that used for fumigation.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A heap of damp combustibles partially ignited and burning slowly, placed on the windward side of a house, tent, etc. to keep off mosquitoes or other insects.

   **Usage:** US; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A quantity of herbs used in suffumigation.

   **Usage:** countable; especially; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** lifestyle; paganism; religion

   **Source context (verbatim):** in the phrase "smudge stick" = "stick of incense"
