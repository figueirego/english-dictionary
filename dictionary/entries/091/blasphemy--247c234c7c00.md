### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An act of irreverence or contempt toward a god or toward something considered sacred; an impious act, utterance, view, etc.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** That imam said that drawing the prophet Muhammad was a form of blasphemy.

   **Source synonyms:** profanation; desecration; sacrilege; violation; blasphemy; desecration; profanation; sacrilege

   **Source antonyms:** reverence; veneration; worship; adoration

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An act of irreverence towards anything considered inviolable; the act of disregarding a convention.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** cursing; cussing; execration; imprecation; profanity; swearing
