### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A type of geometry (mathematical object representing a space and its spatial relationships); a homogeneous space X together with a symmetry group which represents the group action on X of some Lie group;

   **Usage:** differential geometry

   **Example:** Given a Klein geometry (G,H), the group G is called the principal group and G\#47;H is called the space of the geometry.

   **Example:** The space of a Klein geometry is a smooth manifold of dimension \#92;operatorname\#123;dim\#125;G-\#92;operatorname\#123;dim\#125;H.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
