### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To be alive; to have life.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** He's not expected to live for more than a few months.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To have permanent residence somewhere, to inhabit, to reside.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** I live at 2a Acacia Avenue. At that time I'd been living in a camper for about six months.

   **Source synonyms:** range

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To have permanent residence somewhere, to inhabit, to reside.
   1. (of an object) to have its proper place; to normally be stored.

      **Usage:** informal; intransitive

      **Example:** I washed your gravy boat. Where does it live?

      **Source synonyms:** range

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To survive; to persevere; to continue.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To endure in memory; to escape oblivion.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Her memory lives in that song.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To cope.

   **Usage:** excessive; intransitive

   **Example:** You'll just have to live with it! I can't live in a world without you.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To pass life in a specified manner.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** It is difficult to live in poverty. And they lived happily ever after.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To spend, as one's life; to pass; to maintain; to continue in, constantly or habitually.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** To live an idle or a useful life.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To act habitually in conformity with; to practice; to exemplify in one's way of life.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. To live as; to live being.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. To outlast danger; (of a ship or boat) to float.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** That rockslide trapped me in a cave, and I was trapped for three days, but I lived.

   **Example:** No ship could live in such a storm.

   **Example:** (in a video game) I don't know how I lived that, I should've died like 8 times.

<a id="r1-s12"></a>

**Source sense R1.S12**

12. To maintain or support one's existence; to provide for oneself; to feed; to subsist.

   **Usage:** intransitive; upon; or by; upon; or by

   **Example:** It is hard to live on the minimum wage. They lived on stale bread. Man shall not live by bread alone.

   **Source context (verbatim):** followed by on

<a id="r1-s13"></a>

**Source sense R1.S13**

13. To make the most of life; to experience a full, rich life.

   **Usage:** informal; intransitive

   **Example:** I'm sick of spending every day studying at home&#58; I want to go out there and live!

<a id="r1-s14"></a>

**Source sense R1.S14**

14. To spend a great deal of time in a particular place.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

   **Example:** She lives on the Internet.
