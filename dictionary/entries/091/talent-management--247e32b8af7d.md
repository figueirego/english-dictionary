## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Human capital management of the entire employee lifecycle. Companies that are engaged in talent management are strategic and deliberate in how they source, attract, select, train, develop, promote, and move employees through the organization. This term also incorporates how companies drive performance at the individual level (performance management).

   **Usage:** idiomatic; uncountable
