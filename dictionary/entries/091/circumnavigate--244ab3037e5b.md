### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To travel completely around somewhere or something (especially the Globe), especially by sail.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** We circumnavigated the Mediterranean.

   **Source synonyms:** compass; environ (obsolete)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To circumvent or bypass.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** go around

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To sail around the world.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; sailing; transport
