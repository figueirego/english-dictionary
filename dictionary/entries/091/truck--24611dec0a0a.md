## Noun · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A small wheel or roller, specifically the wheel of a gun carriage.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The ball on top of a flagpole.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. On a wooden mast, a circular disc (or sometimes a rectangle) of wood near or at the top of the mast, usually with holes or sheaves to reeve signal halyards; also a temporary or emergency place for a lookout. "Main" refers to the mainmast, whereas a truck on another mast may be called (on the mizzenmast, for example) "mizzen-truck".

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A heavier motor vehicle designed to carry goods or to pull a semi-trailer designed to carry goods; (in Malaysia/Singapore) a such vehicle with a closed or covered carriage.

   **Usage:** Australia; Canada; India; US; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** We rented a truck big enough to carry the whole load in one trip.

   **Source synonyms:** lorry

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A railroad car, chiefly one designed to carry goods.

   **Usage:** UK; countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** rail-transport; railways; transport

   **Source synonyms:** goods wagon; freight wagon; goods carriage; freight carriage; goods truck; freight truck; freight car (English; North-American)

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Any smaller wagon or cart or vehicle of various designs, pushed or pulled by hand or (obsolete) pulled by an animal, used to move and sometimes lift goods, like those in hotels for moving luggage or in libraries for moving books.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Abbreviation of railroad truck or wheel truck; a pivoting frame, one attached to the bottom of the bed of a railway car at each end, that rests on the axle and which swivels to allow the axle (at each end of which is a solid wheel) to turn with curves in the track.

   **Usage:** US; abbreviation; alt-of; countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** rail-transport; railways; transport

   **Source synonyms:** bogie (British; English)

   **Alternative form of:** railroad truck or wheel truck

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. The part of a skateboard or roller skate that joins the wheels to the deck, consisting of a hanger, baseplate, kingpin, and bushings, and sometimes mounted with a riser in between.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A platform with wheels or casters.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; theater

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. Dirt or other messiness.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
