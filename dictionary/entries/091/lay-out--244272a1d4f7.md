### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To expend or contribute money to an expense or purchase.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** She laid out hundreds of dollars to throw a great party.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To arrange (physically) in a certain way, so as to spread or space apart; to display (e.g. merchandise or a collection).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** She laid the blocks out in a circle on the floor.

   **Example:** She laid out a terrific spread for the party.

   **Source antonyms:** pick up; pack up

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To explain; to interpret.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Let me lay it out for you&#58; you're not going to get a better opportunity than this one.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To arrange; to design; to concoct; to think up.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He began to lay out the hull for an inexpensive but fast littoral drone.

   **Example:** She began to lay out how the circuits would be connected.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To prepare a body for burial.

   **Example:** They laid him out beautifully on a bier draped in satin.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To render (someone) unconscious; to knock out; to cause to fall to the floor; to kill.

   **Usage:** US; colloquial; transitive

   **Example:** I laid him out on the sidewalk after he tried to grab me.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To scold or berate.

   **Usage:** colloquial; transitive

   **Example:** She really laid him out for taking the money and then lying to try to cover it up.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To cease playing one's instrument.

   **Usage:** colloquial; intransitive; jazz

   **Example:** Hey man can you lay out on the bridge?

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To sunbathe.

   **Usage:** colloquial; intransitive

   **Example:** It's been raining so much all week that I haven't had a chance to lay out.
