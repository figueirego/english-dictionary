## Noun

### Forms

- frost lines — plural
- frostline — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The depth to which the water present in the soil is expected to freeze in winter.

   **Example:** The uninsulated water pipes must be installed below the frost line to prevent them from freezing.

   **Source synonyms:** frost depth; freezing depth

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The depth to which the water present in the soil is actually frozen in a given place at a given point in time.

   **Example:** Due to the thin layer of snow on the ground the frost line has penetrated unusually deep this year.

   **Source synonyms:** frost front

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. In a solar system, the distance from the protosun beyond which hydrogen-containing gas compounds freeze.

   **Example:** In our Solar System the frost line lies between Mars and Jupiter at about five astronomical units from the Sun.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astrophysics

   **Source synonyms:** snow line; ice line
