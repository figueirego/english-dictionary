### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who plans.

   **Example:** Johnny is a good planner. He starts his work in time to get it finished by the deadline.

   **Example:** Ms. Gunston is a planner for the county.

**Source sense R1.S2**
