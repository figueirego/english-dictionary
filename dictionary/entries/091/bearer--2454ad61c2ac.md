### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who, or that which, bears, sustains, or carries.

   **Source synonyms:** carrier; -phore

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Someone who helps carry the coffin or a dead body during a funeral procession.

   **Source synonyms:** pallbearer

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. One who possesses a cheque, bond, or other notes promising payment.

   **Example:** I promise to pay the bearer on demand.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A person employed or engaged to carry equipment on a safari, expedition, etc.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A person employed to carry a palanquin or litter.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A domestic servant in charge of household goods and clothing; a valet.

   **Usage:** India; dated

   **Source synonyms:** dressing-boy

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A waiter in a hotel or restaurant.

   **Usage:** India

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A tree or plant yielding fruit.

   **Example:** a good bearer

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. Someone who delivers a letter or message on behalf of another (especially as referred to in the letter or message).

   **Usage:** dated

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. A strip of reglet or other furniture to bear off the impression from a blank page.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** media; printing; publishing

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. A type or type-high piece of metal interspersed in blank parts to support the plate when it is shaved.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** media; printing; publishing
