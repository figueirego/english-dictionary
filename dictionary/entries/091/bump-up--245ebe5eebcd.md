### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To increase something suddenly.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To promote a person to a higher grade.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To give a more prominent place to; to advance position in queue.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To bump into something; to collide with something.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** It bumps up on the wall here.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To figuratively collide with something; to come into conflict over something.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** The legalization movement bumped up against political realities.

   **Example:** The program is bumping up on its fiscal cap.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To transfer (recorded footage) from a narrower to a wider tape format.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** broadcasting; media; television

   **Source antonyms:** bump down
