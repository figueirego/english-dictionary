### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make someone perform a task or duty, especially one they are not prepared or willing to do; to make something serve a function, especially one it was not designed or intended for.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** Anyone with basic first-aid training was pressed into service as a triage nurse after the earthquake.
