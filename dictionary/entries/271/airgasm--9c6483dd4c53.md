### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A relief or pleasure accompanying an increased quantity of air.
   1. An exhilaration felt during freefall, such as when skydiving or bungee jumping.

      **Usage:** countable; slang; uncountable
