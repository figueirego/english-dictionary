### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A game in which prizes are covered up and mixed together in a container, so that contestants can dip their hand into the container and randomly pull out a prize.

   **Usage:** UK

   **Source synonyms:** lucky draw

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A selection solely at random.

   **Usage:** broadly; figuratively; idiomatic

   **Source synonyms:** lucky draw
