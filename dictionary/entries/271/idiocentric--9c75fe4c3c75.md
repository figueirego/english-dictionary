### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Source synonyms:** egocentric; individualistic; self-interested; egocentric; egoistical; every man for himself; idiocentric; individualistic; interested (uncommon); porkish; reptilian (figuratively); selfish; selflike; selfsome; self-absorbed; self-centered; self-interested; self-minded; self-seeking; self-serving; solipsistic
