## Noun

### Forms

- sunbeams — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A ray of sunlight; especially, a demarcated, narrow, and intense (relative to ambient light) ray of sunlight (as revealed by, for example, its diffraction among dust particles).

   **Source synonyms:** sunshaft

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of various lycaenid butterflies of the genus Curetis.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any hummingbird of the genus Aglaeactis.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Synonym of sunshine (“ironic form of address to an inferior or troublemaker”).

   **Usage:** UK

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An item of cutlery or crockery laid out on a table, but not used, and which can be returned to the drawer without being washed.

   **Usage:** Australia; colloquial; dated
