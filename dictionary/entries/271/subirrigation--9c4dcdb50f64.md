### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A method of irrigation where water is delivered to the plant rootzone from below the soil surface and absorbed upwards. The excess water may be collected for reuse.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Subirrigation is used in growing field crops such as tomatoes, peppers, and sugar cane in areas with high water tables, such as Florida, and in commercial greenhouse operations.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** agriculture; business; lifestyle
