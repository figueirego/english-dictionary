### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A circular cage for a squirrel or other small animal, which rotates vertically as the animal runs at the bottom.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A tedious, repetitive, unfulfilling activity or situation, especially one in which no progress is achieved.

   **Usage:** broadly; figuratively

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any other device, such as a fan or rotor, that resembles a squirrel cage in form.
