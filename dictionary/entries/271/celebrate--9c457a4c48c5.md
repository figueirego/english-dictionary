### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To extol or honour in a solemn manner.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to celebrate the name of the Most High

   **Source synonyms:** fete

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To honour by rites, by ceremonies of joy and respect, or by refraining from ordinary business; to observe duly.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to celebrate a birthday

   **Source synonyms:** observe; keep

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To engage in joyful activity in appreciation of an event.

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** I was promoted today at work—let’s celebrate!

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To perform or participate in, as a sacrament or solemn rite; to perform with appropriate rites.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to celebrate a marriage

   **Source synonyms:** solemnize
