## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A type of differential geometry in which the differential invariants studied are invariant under volume-preserving affine transformations.

   **Usage:** uncountable; differential geometry

   **Example:** The basic difference between Riemannian and affine differential geometry is that in the affine case we introduce volume forms over a manifold instead of metrics.
