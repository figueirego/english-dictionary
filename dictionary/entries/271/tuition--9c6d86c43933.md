### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The training or instruction provided by a teacher or tutor.

   **Usage:** Ireland; UK; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Paid private classes taken outside of formal education; tutoring. (also used attributively)

   **Usage:** Australia; India; Malaysia; Singapore; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** tuition classes

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A sum of money paid for instruction (such as in a private school, boarding school, university, or college).

   **Usage:** Canada; Philippines; US; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The school's tuition will increase by five percent next year.

   **Source synonyms:** tuition fees

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Care, guardianship.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; uncountable
