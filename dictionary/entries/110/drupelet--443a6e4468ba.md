**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One of the small drupe-like subdivisions which compose the outer layer of certain fruit such as blackberries or raspberries.

   **Example:** It is best to pick the berries while all drupelets are of a consistent, dark red coloration.

   **Example:** The passengers on the chock-full boat were packed across the deck like drupelets.
