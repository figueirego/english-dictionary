### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Him; the male object of a verb or preposition that also appears as the subject

   **Usage:** masculine; personal; pronoun; reflexive; singular; third-person

   **Example:** He injured himself.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. He; used as an intensifier, often to emphasize that the referent is the exclusive participant in the predicate

   **Usage:** emphatic; masculine; personal; pronoun; singular; third-person

   **Example:** He was injured himself.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The subject or non-reflexive object of a predicate; he himself.

   **Usage:** Ireland; masculine; personal; pronoun; singular; third-person; otherwise archaic

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The subject or non-reflexive object of a predicate; he (used of upper-class gentlemen, or sarcastically, of men who imagine themselves to be more important than others)

   **Usage:** Ireland; masculine; personal; pronoun; singular; third-person

   **Example:** Has himself come down to breakfast yet?

   **Example:** Have you seen himself yet this morning?
