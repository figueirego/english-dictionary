### Pronunciation

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Denoting the effect that irradiation of a tissue has on remote nonirradiated tissue.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The regression of a liver tumor after irradiation of bone that the tumor metastasized to is an example of the abscopal effect.
