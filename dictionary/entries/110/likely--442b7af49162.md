## Adjective · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈlaɪkli/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Forms

- more likely — comparative
- likelier — comparative
- most likely — superlative
- likeliest — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Probable; having a greater-than-even chance of occurring.

   **Example:** Rain is likely later this afternoon.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Reasonably to be expected; apparently destined, probable.

   **Usage:** infinitive; with-to

   **Example:** Do you think there's likely to be snow?

   **Example:** He is likely to succeed at anything he tries.

   **Source context (verbatim):** as predicate

   **Source synonyms:** proximate

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Appropriate, suitable; believable; promising, having a good potential.

   **Example:** Jones is a likely candidate for management.

   **Example:** The relationship with social status is a likely topic for investigation.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Plausible; within the realm of credibility.

   **Example:** not a very likely excuse.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Leading with high probability to some specified outcome.

   **Example:** a likely recipe for disaster.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Attractive; pleasant.

   **Usage:** archaic

   **Example:** I found a likely spot under a shady tree for the picnic.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Similar; like; alike.

   **Usage:** obsolete

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** liable — as predicate, followed by "to" and infinitive
