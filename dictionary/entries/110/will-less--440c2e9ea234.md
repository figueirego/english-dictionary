### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of a person, lacking volition, not exercising one's will.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source synonyms:** irresolute

   **Source antonyms:** determined; resolute; willful

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of an action, involuntary.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Intestate; without a legal will governing one's property or estate.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** He died will-less.
