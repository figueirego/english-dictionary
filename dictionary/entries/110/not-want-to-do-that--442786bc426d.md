## Verb

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To be in a state of doing or being about to do something that one is likely to regret; usually unknowingly or forgetfully.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive; stative

   **Example:** You do not want to do that.
