### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A net unidirectional movement of electrons, or other charge carriers, caused by a potential difference.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** An electric current runs through this wire.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** electrical-engineering; electromagnetism; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The net charge that passes through some cross-section of a conducting material (in one direction), per unit time.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The electric current in this wire is 5 A.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** electrical-engineering; electromagnetism; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics
