### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To burn or shine unsteadily, or with a wavering light.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To keep going on and off; to appear and disappear for short moments; to flutter.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** A strong desire to smash up everything in the room kept flickering in his head.
