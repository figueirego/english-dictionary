### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To do random unplanned work or spend time idly

   **Usage:** informal; intransitive

   **Example:** Stop mucking about and get on with your work!

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To attempt to do something with a piece of equipment without understanding how it works.

   **Usage:** informal; intransitive

   **Example:** Don't muck about with the video machine, or you might break it.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To be playful; full of fun and high spirits; to treat a situation unseriously.

   **Usage:** Australia; UK; informal; intransitive

   **Example:** It was only a joke. We were only mucking about.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To play with; to toy with; to waste the time of (a person).

   **Usage:** transitive
