### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One or more small or immature fish.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One or more children.

   **Usage:** informal

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. One or more relatively small and insignificant individuals or things of relatively little consequence, importance, or value.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** The police did not arrest the drug dealer since he was a small fry compared to his boss.

   **Example:** These slot machines are just the small fry. The big games are in the back room.

   **Source synonyms:** small potatoes; minnow; nobody; no one

   **Source antonyms:** big fish
