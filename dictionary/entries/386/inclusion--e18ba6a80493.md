### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An addition or annex to a group, set, or total.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** The poem was a new inclusion in the textbook.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The act of including, i.e. adding or annexing, (something) to a group, set, or total.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** The inclusion of the poem added value to the course.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Anything foreign that is included in a material.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any material that is trapped inside a mineral during its formation, as a defect in a precious stone.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; geography; geology; mineralogy; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A nuclear or cytoplasmic aggregate of stainable substances.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; cytology; medicine; natural-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. An object completely inside a tissue, such as epidermal inclusion cyst, a cyst in the epidermis.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; histology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A mapping where the domain is a subset of the image.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Restriction; limitation.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable
