### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make quiet or put at rest; to pacify or appease; to quell; to calm.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to allay popular excitement

   **Example:** to allay the tumult of the passions

   **Source synonyms:** appease; assuage; compose; soothe; calm; quiet

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To alleviate; to abate; to mitigate.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to allay the severity of affliction or the bitterness of adversity

   **Source synonyms:** alleviate; abate

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To subside, abate, become peaceful.

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To mix (metals); to mix with a baser metal; to alloy; to deteriorate.

   **Usage:** archaic

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**
