## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A tense or tense-like verb form that is constructed analytically, i.e. with an auxiliary verb added (like the perfect tense in English or French), not synthetically (through simple inflection of a base form). Contrasted with a simple tense (like the English simple past or French passé simple.

   **Usage:** uncountable
