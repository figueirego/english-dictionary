### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The supercluster that includes the Milky Way galaxy.
   1. Synonym of Virgo Supercluster, a lobe of the Laniakea Supercluster; a supercluster representing all the nearby galaxies which have clumped together.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astronomy; natural-sciences

**Source sense R1.S2**

   1. Synonym of Laniakea, a supercluster representing all the galaxies drawn towards the local gravitational center.
