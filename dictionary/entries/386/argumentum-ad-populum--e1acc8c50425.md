### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A fallacious argument that concludes a proposition to be true because many or all people believe it; it alleges that “if many believe so, it is so”.

   **Usage:** rhetoric

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** argumentum ad numerum — “if many believe so, it is so”; bandwagon fallacy — “if many believe so, it is so”
