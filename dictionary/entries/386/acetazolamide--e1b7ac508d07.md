**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A drug that inhibits the enzyme carbonic anhydrase, used in the treatment of glaucoma and epilepsy and as a diuretic (now especially in the prevention of acute mountain sickness).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; pharmacology; sciences
