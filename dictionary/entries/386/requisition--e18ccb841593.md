## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A formal request for something.
   1. A formal demand made by one state or government upon another for the surrender or extradition of a fugitive from justice.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A formal request for something.
   1. A notarial demand for repayment of a debt.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A formal request for something.
   1. A formal request by a doctor or other clinician, usually on a standardized form, for specific medical tests or procedures to be performed on a patient by a third party, such as a laboratory or imaging center

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Example:** a lab requisition for blood tests

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A formal request for something.
   1. A demand by the invader upon the people of an invaded country for supplies, as of provision, forage, transportation, etc.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A formal request for something.
   1. A formal application by one officer to another for things needed in the public service.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Example:** a requisition for clothing, troops, or money

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. That which is required by authority; especially, a quota of supplies or necessaries.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A call; an invitation; a summons.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** a requisition for a public meeting
