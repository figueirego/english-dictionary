**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Of a horse, pack animal, etc.&#58; to move forward; go faster; or turn in a direction away from the driver, typically to the right.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** This horse won’t gee when I tell him to.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To cause an animal to move in this way.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** You may need to walk up to the front of the pack and physically gee the lead dog.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To agree; to harmonize.

   **Usage:** UK; dialectal; obsolete
