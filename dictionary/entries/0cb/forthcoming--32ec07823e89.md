## Adjective · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /fɔɹθˈkʌmɪŋ/ — source labels: General-American

### Forms

- more forthcoming — comparative
- most forthcoming — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Approaching or about to take place.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** I shall vote in the forthcoming election.

   **Source synonyms:** imminent; impending; around the corner (idiomatic); at hand; forthcoming; imminent; impendent; impending; in the offing (idiomatic); in the wind (idiomatic); instant (dated); on the boil; pending; upcoming; proximate; to come; toward (dated)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Available when needed; in place, ready.

   **Example:** The money was not forthcoming.

   **Source antonyms:** unforthcoming

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Willing to co-operate or provide information; candid, frank, responsive.

   **Example:** Once I explained why I needed to know, she was really forthcoming.

   **Source antonyms:** incommunicative
