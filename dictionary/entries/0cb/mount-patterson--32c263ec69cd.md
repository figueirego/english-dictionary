## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The highest peak in the Sweetwater Mountains, in Mono County, California, United States. Possibly named for James H. Patterson, a native of Ohio who settled at Big Meadows before 1867. Elevation&#58; 3,552 meters.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A mountain in the Waputik Range in Alberta. Named for John Duncan Patterson in 1917. Elevation&#58; 3,197 meters.
