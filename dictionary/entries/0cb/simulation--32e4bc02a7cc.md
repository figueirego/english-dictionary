### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Something that simulates a system or environment in order to predict actual behaviour.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** This exercise is a simulation of actual battle conditions.

   **Example:** The most reliable simulation predicts that the hurricane will turn north.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The process of simulating.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Despite extensive simulation in the design phase, the aircraft failed to behave as expected.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A video game designed to convey a more or less realistic experience, as of a sport or warfare.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Some kids in the classroom will be playing simulations after school.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** video-games

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Assuming an appearance which is feigned, or not true.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The act of falling over in order to be awarded a foul, when no foul has been committed.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; games; hobbies; lifestyle; soccer; sports
