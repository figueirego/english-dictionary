### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To exclude; to dismiss from participation or eligibility.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I may not be as young as I used to be, but I can still handle this job. Don't count me out.

   **Example:** They counted it out for these purposes because their accounting method handles it in another way.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a person or factor

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To enumerate items while organizing or transferring them.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The bank teller counted out five twenty-dollar bills and gave them to me.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To determine that a competitor has lost a match, by a referee's enumeration aloud of the increments of time for which the competitor has been incapacitated.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The champ was knocked unconscious and counted out in the third round.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** boxing; government; hobbies; lifestyle; martial-arts; military; politics; professional-wrestling; sports; war; wrestling

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To declare adjourned, as a sitting of the House, when it is ascertained that a quorum is not present.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To prevent the accession of (a legitimately elected person) to office, by a fraudulent return or count of the votes.

   **Usage:** transitive
