### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The reconstructed ancestor language or protolanguage of the Indo-European family of languages, which includes most European, Iranian, and Indian languages.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** The word mother comes from Proto-Indo-European \*méh₂tēr.

   **Example:** The plural English word brethren and the Farsi word that is pronounced as 'baradaran' both come from the same Proto-Indo-European root word.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences
