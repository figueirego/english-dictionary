### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A type of trailer hitch, which consists of a horseshoe-shaped plate on a multidirectional pivot, with a locking pin to couple with the kingpin of a truck trailer.

   **Usage:** US

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** road; transport

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A type of trailer hitch, which consists of a horseshoe-shaped plate on a multidirectional pivot, with a locking pin to couple with the kingpin of a truck trailer.
   1. In full, fifth-wheel trailer&#58; a large caravan or travel trailer that is connected to a pickup truck for towing by a hitch similar to the one described in sense 1 located in the center of the truck's bed.

      **Usage:** US

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** road; transport

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A horizontal wheel or segment of a wheel above the front axle and beneath the body of a carriage, forming an extended support to prevent it from overturning.

   **Usage:** US; historical

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** road; transport

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Anything superfluous or unnecessary.

   **Usage:** US; idiomatic; informal
