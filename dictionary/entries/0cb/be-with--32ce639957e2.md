### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To have sex with.

   **Usage:** euphemistic; transitive

   **Example:** We dated for almost a year, but I've never been with him.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To date or be boyfriend/girlfriend with.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I've been with her for almost a year now.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To agree with someone.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I came in here disagreeing, but I've changed my mind, and now I'm with you on this.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To understand someone's point or intention.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I'm sorry; I'm not with you on this point.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To be down with (something); believe in, like, or espouse.

   **Usage:** slang or African-American Vernacular

   **Example:** You know I ain't with using drugs.

   **Example:** You like robbing banks? Nah, I ain't with that.
