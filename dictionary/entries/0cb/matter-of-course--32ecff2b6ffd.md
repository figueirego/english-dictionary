## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An occurrence that is expected, usual, or taken for granted in a given situation (see Usage notes below)&#58;
   1. An outcome or course of events regarded as natural, logical, obvious, or inevitable.

      **Usage:** idiomatic

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An occurrence that is expected, usual, or taken for granted in a given situation (see Usage notes below)&#58;
   1. Proper or common practice.

      **Usage:** idiomatic

      **Example:** On some occasions standing ovations may be given to political leaders as a matter of course, rather than as a special honour.

      **Source synonyms:** custom; protocol; tradition; habit; routine

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see matter, of course.

   **Example:** but at this point it doesn't really matter, of course

   **Example:** that answer doesn't get to the heart of the matter, of course
