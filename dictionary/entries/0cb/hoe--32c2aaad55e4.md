### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of various tools for scraping, scratching, digging, or stirring soil or other materials.
   1. An agricultural and horticultural hand tool consisting of a long handle with a flat blade fixed perpendicular to it at the end, used for digging rows or removing weeds by hand.

      **Usage:** when not otherwise specified

      **Example:** For their organic row crops, they do the weeding with hoes. They get in there often, but it goes fast, and the weeds never get ahead.

      **Source synonyms:** hand hoe

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of various tools for scraping, scratching, digging, or stirring soil or other materials.
   1. Any of several implements or machines usually called by their more specific names, for example, backhoe.

      **Example:** The grading is on hold. In the meantime, get that hoe over here and work on this utility trench. \[Instructions issued to a worker who will operate a backhoe\]
