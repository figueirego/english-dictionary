### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who governs a country, province, or colony as the representative of a monarch.

   **Example:** Ireland was governed by a Viceroy representing the English King/Queen when it was part of the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Ireland.

   **Source synonyms:** vicar; provost

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A zongdu.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An orange and black North American butterfly (Limenitis archippus), so named because it is similar to, but smaller than, the monarch butterfly.
