## Noun

### Forms

- past lives — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A previously lived life in a different body, according to theories of reincarnation.

   **Example:** She claims to have been Cleopatra and Marie Antoinette in past lives.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A former period of one's life.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** I was a computer programmer in a past life, but now I raise pigs and chickens.
