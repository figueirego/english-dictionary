### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A three-dimensional measure of space that comprises a length, a width and a height. It is measured in units of cubic centimeters in metric, cubic inches or cubic feet in English measurement.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The room is 9×12×8, so its volume is 864 cubic feet.

   **Example:** The proper products can improve your hair's volume.

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Please turn down the volume on the stereo.

   **Example:** Volume can be measured in decibels.

   **Source synonyms:** loudness

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The issues of a periodical over a period of one year.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** I looked at this week's copy of the magazine. It was volume 23, issue 45.
