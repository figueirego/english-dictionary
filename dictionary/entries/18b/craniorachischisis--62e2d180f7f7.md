**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A lethal defect of the neural tube in which both the brain and spinal cord are left open.

   **Example:** Craniorachischisis is a variant of rachischisis that occurs when the entire spinal cord and brain are exposed – simultaneous complete rachischisis and anencephaly.^(\[Wikipedia\])
