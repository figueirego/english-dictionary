### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A covering flap in animals and insects, such as a gill cover or the lid of nits.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences; zoology

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The lidlike portion of a moss sporangium or of a fruit that detaches to allow the dispersal of spores or seeds.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A gum flap covering (part of) a partially erupted tooth, usually a wisdom tooth.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** dentistry; medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A structure which serves as a cover or lid.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. One of several flaps of cerebral cortex covering the insula.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; medicine; sciences
