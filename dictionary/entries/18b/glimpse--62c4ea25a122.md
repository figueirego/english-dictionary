### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To see or view (someone, or something tangible) briefly and incompletely.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To perceive (something intangible) briefly and incompletely.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** I have only begun to glimpse the magnitude of the problem.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Chiefly followed by at or upon&#58; to look at briefly and incompletely; to glance.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To shine with a faint, unsteady light; to glimmer, to shimmer.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source synonyms:** glitter; coruscate; flash; gleam; glitter; glisten; luster; sheen; shimmer; shine; sparkle

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To appear or start to appear, especially faintly or unclearly; to dawn.

   **Usage:** archaic; intransitive; poetic

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Sometimes followed by out&#58; to provide a brief and incomplete look.

   **Usage:** intransitive; rare
