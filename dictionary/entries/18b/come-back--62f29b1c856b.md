### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To return to a place.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To return to one's possession, especially of memories.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Suddenly it all came back to him, the tea, the petite madeleines, his mother.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To return to a relationship with a former romantic partner.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To return to a former state, usually a desirable one; to become fashionable once more.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Many people counted him out, but he came back better than ever.

   **Example:** Paisley is coming back this year.

   **Example:** I'll come back to the harder questions after finishing the rest of the test.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To retort.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** He came back with one of his snide remarks.
