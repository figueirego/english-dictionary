### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of using or adapting as an instrument; a series or combination of instruments.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The arrangement of a musical composition for performance by a number of different instruments; instrumental composition; composition for an orchestra or military band.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

   **Source synonyms:** orchestration

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The act or manner of playing upon musical instruments; performance.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** He had a wonderful instrumentation.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. On a vehicle, dashboard gauges monitoring engine functions and performance, along with other essential functions.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The car's instrumentation included fuel, temperature, voltimeter and oil pressure gauges, along with a speedometer and tachometer.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** automotive; transport; vehicles

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Monitoring gauges for any machine, such as a reactor.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The dynamic analysis of a program's performance and behaviour, usually by injecting profiling code into it.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** bytecode instrumentation

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
