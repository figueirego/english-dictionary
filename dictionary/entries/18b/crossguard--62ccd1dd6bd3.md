### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A metal bar fixed between, and at right angles to, the blade and the handle of a sword or other bladed weapon that stops an opponent's similar weapon from sliding along the blade and injuring the hand of the person wielding the sword.

   **Usage:** swords

   **Source synonyms:** guard; quillon
