### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of a family of curves defined as the locus of points p, such that each p is on a line that passes through a given fixed point P and intersects a given curve, C, and the distance from p to the point of intersection with C is a specified constant (note that for nontrivial cases two such points p satisfy the criteria, and the resultant curve has two parts).

   **Example:** The conchoid of a circle with respect to a point on the circle is a cardioid if the fixed distance is equal to the diameter of the circle.

   **Example:** The Conchoid of Nicomedes is the conchoid of a straight line with respect to a point not on the line.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A conchoidal fracture in rock.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; geology; natural-sciences
