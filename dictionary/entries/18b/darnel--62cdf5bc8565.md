### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of the genus Lolium of grasses, especially as a weed in wheat fields.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source synonyms:** ryegrass; bearded darnel; poison darnel; white darnel

2. Any of the genus Lolium of grasses, especially as a weed in wheat fields.
   1. Especially, the species Lolium temulentum, often host to a fungus toxic to humans and animals.
