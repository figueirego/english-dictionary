   1. Having little or no hair on the head, or having a large area of bare scalp on top of the head.

      **Source synonyms:** hairless

      **Source antonyms:** faxed; haired

**Source sense R1.S5**

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** The bald cypress is a tree that loses its leaves in winter.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Of a tyre, whose surface is worn away.

   **Usage:** figuratively
