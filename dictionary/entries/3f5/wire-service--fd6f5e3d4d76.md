### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A service provided by a news agency that transmits news reports to subscribers electronically, e.g. by telegraph or the Internet. The subscribers are usually media outlets such as newspapers. Examples include the Associated Press and United Press International.
