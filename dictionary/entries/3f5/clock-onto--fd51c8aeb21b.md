### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Synonym of clock on (but used transitively).

   **Example:** He clocked onto his shift late.

   **Source synonyms:** clock on (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

   **Example:** The company clocked onto her subterfuge and fired her for it.
