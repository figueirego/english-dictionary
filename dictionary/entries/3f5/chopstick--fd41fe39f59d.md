### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An East Asian eating utensil typically used as a pair held in one hand to grip food items, mix liquids, or scoop food out of a bowl. The utensil is a stick, usually made of wood, and measuring approximately 23 cm (10 inches) in length.

   **Usage:** plural-normally

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An Asian person.

   **Usage:** ethnic; slur

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A long straight stick used to keep baited hooks separated in deep-sea fishing tackle.

   **Usage:** obsolete
