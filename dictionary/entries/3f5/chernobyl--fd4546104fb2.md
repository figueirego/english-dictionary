### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A partially abandoned city and ghost town in northern Ukraine, known as the site of a nuclear disaster.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The 1986 nuclear accident which resulted in the abandonment of the aforementioned city.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** After Chernobyl, very few nuclear power plants were built for years.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
