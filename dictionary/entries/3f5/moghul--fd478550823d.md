### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A head of the Timurid dynasty founded by Zahīr-ud-Dīn Muhammad Bābur (1483-1530) which controlled large parts of southern Asia from the 16th to the 19th centuries.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A Mongol or Mongolian, especially a member or follower of the Moghul dynasty.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An important or successful person; a magnate. (Now usually as mogul.)
