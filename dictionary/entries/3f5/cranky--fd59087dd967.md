### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Not in good working condition; defective, faulty.

   **Usage:** usually

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a machine

   **Source synonyms:** shaky

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Grouchy, grumpy, irritable; easily upset.

   **Example:** He got home from a long day at work tired and cranky.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Not in perfect mental working order; eccentric, peculiar.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Synonym of crank (“of a ship&#58; liable to capsize because of poorly stowed cargo or insufficient ballast”).

   **Example:** a cranky vessel

   **Source synonyms:** crank (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Full of spirit; spirited.

   **Usage:** archaic
