### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To melt fully, especially metal or glass so that it can be remade into something else.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To lose structure by being heated to a molten state.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The reactor core melted down.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To have a breakdown; to collapse or fail utterly.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive
