### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In spite of, despite.

   **Example:** For all his protests, he was forced to have a bath.

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Applying to every element of a set; the universal quantifier ∀.

   **Usage:** literally

   **Example:** For all x in A, x² is even.
