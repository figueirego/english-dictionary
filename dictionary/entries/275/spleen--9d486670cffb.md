### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In vertebrates, including humans, a ductless vascular gland, located in the upper left abdomen near the stomach, which destroys old red blood cells, removes debris from the bloodstream, acts as a reservoir of blood, and produces lymphocytes.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; immunology; medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A bad mood; spitefulness. Compare gall.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; uncountable; except in the set phrase "to vent one's spleen"

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A sudden motion or action; a fit; a freak; a whim.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; rare; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Melancholy; hypochondriacal affections.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A fit of immoderate laughter or merriment.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
