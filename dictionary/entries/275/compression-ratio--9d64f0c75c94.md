### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The ratio of the volume between the cylinder head of an internal combustion engine and the piston before and after the compression stroke.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** automotive; transport; vehicles

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
