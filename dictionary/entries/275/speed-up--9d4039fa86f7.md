### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To accelerate; to increase speed.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** The car sped up as it went around the corner.

   **Source synonyms:** pick up speed

   **Source antonyms:** hold up; let up; slow; slow down; slow up

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To accelerate (something)&#58; to increase its speed, to make it go faster.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** You shouldn't speed up your car when you go around corners.

   **Source synonyms:** smarten

   **Source antonyms:** hold up; slow; slow down
