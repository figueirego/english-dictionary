### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The amount of time for a scene to fade to dark after lighting up.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** broadcasting; film; media; television

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The time during which a wireless signal is too faint to be accurately received.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences; signal-processing

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The amount of time for light or sound from a specified source to fade in from nothingness to the maximum that one is using, or to fade out from the maximum to nothingness.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; light engineering; sound engineering; light engineering; sound engineering
