## Adverb · Record 1

### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In a taboo or otherwise inappropriate manner due to timing; inappropriately related to a tragic or unpleasant recent event.

   **Example:** You're making jokes about yesterday's ferry accident? That's too soon, people died there!
