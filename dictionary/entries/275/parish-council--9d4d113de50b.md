## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The governing body of people elected to oversee management of a parish (i.e., a civil parish) and represent the interests of residents.

   **Source synonyms:** town council; city council; community council; parish assembly

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A body of parishioners who help administer an ecclesiastical parish.

   **Usage:** uncommon

   **Source synonyms:** vestry; parochial church council; PCC
