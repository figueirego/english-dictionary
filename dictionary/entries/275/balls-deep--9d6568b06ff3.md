### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. With the penis fully immersed in an orifice, more often the anus or vagina than the mouth.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; slang; vulgar

   **Example:** I like to fuck balls-deep. If they can't take it all it's not worth the time.
