## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈvɪd͡ʒəl/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Forms

- vigils — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An instance of keeping awake during normal sleeping hours, especially to keep watch or pray.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A period of observation or surveillance at any hour.

   **Example:** His dog kept vigil outside the hospital for eight days while he was recovering from an accident.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The eve of a religious festival in which staying awake is part of the ritual devotions.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A quiet demonstration in support of a cause.

   **Example:** The protesters kept vigil outside the conference centre in which the party congress was being held.
