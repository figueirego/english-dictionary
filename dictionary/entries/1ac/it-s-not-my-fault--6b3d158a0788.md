## Phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. I did not cause the problem.

   **Example:** It's not my fault that it's raining.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. I did not cause it (regardless of whether the subject matter is perceived as problematic).

   **Usage:** broadly; informal

   **Example:** It's not my fault that you're so smart!
