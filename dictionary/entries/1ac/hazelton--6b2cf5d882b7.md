### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An English habitational surname from Old English.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A male given name transferred from the surname.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A city in Idaho.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A village in British Columbia.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A city in North Dakota; named after early settler Hazel Roop.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A town in Kansas; named for founder Rev. J. Hazelton.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A neighborhood of Youngstown, Ohio; named for the landowning Haseltine family, respelled with the -ton placename suffix.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. An unincorporated community in California; named for Hazelton Blodget, son of a local oilman.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>
