### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A type of knife that with the press of a button, allows the blade to drop out of the handle, and then quickly opened up full, for quick use, also allowing it to be flicked open with a flick of the wrist.
   1. A folding knife of this type.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A type of knife that with the press of a button, allows the blade to drop out of the handle, and then quickly opened up full, for quick use, also allowing it to be flicked open with a flick of the wrist.
