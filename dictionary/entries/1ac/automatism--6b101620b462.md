## Noun

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ɔːˈtɒmətɪzəm/ — source labels: UK

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Automatic or involuntary action.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The power of initiating vital processes from within the cell, organ, or organism, independent of external stimulus.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The doctrine that all living beings are entirely controlled by physical or physiological causes, where consciousness is a noncontrolling accessory.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; philosophy; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** epiphenomenalism

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A surrealist painting technique whereby one attempts to move the brush, pen etc. without conscious control over it.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** art; arts

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An action performed subconsciously, without any apparent direction from the mind; a thought which appears spontaneously in one's consciousness.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; psychology; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** telergy
