## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An act or incident of pretending to forget one’s previous stances or corrupt activities in order to avoid accountability.

   **Usage:** derogatory; humorous; informal; neologism; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person’s tendency to forget or overlook details of past scams that they have encountered.

   **Usage:** derogatory; humorous; informal; neologism; uncountable
