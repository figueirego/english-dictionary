### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of an unpleasant event, to happen to someone who is thought to deserve it.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** What, now he wishes he hadn't voted for the Leopards Eating People's Faces Party? Serves him right!

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see serve, right.

   **Example:** If my memory serves me right, we had a similar discussion a few months ago.
