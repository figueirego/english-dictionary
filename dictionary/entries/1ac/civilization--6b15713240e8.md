### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An organized culture encompassing many communities, often on the scale of a nation or a people; a stage or system of social, political, or technical development.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** the Aztec civilization

   **Example:** Western civilization

   **Example:** Modern civilization is a product of industrialization and globalization.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Human society, particularly civil society.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** A hermit doesn't much care for civilization.

   **Example:** I'm glad to be back in civilization after a day with that rowdy family.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The act or process of civilizing or becoming civilized.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The teacher's civilization of the child was no easy task.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The state or quality of being civilized.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** He was a man of great civilization.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The act of rendering a criminal process civil.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Collectively, those people and places of the world considered to have a high standard of behavior and / or a high level of development. Commonly subjectively used by people of one society to exclusively refer to their society, or their elite sub-group, or a few associated societies, implying all others, in time or geography or status, as something less than civilised, as savages or barbarians. (Compare refinement, elitism, civilised society, the Civilised World.

   **Usage:** capitalized; sometimes; inherently emic

   **Example:** Some of the tourists in the upcountry might have embarrassed themselves if they'd been capable of having any shame, whining that they couldn't wait to get back to civilization.

   **Source synonyms:** ecumene

   **Source antonyms:** wilderness; wilds; anecumene
