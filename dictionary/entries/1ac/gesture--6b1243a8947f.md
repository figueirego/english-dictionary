### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A motion of the limbs or body, especially one made to emphasize speech.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The middle-finger gesture is really a nonverbal swear.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An act or a remark that serves as a formality or as a sign of attitude.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** We took flowers as a gesture of sympathy.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A motion made with a pointing device, or on a touchscreen, that is recognised by the system as a command.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** This Web browser can be controlled with mouse gestures.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; graphical-user-interface; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The manner of carrying the body; position of the body or limbs; posture.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable
