### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A set of labels applied to the various objects in a system, to packaged goods, and so on; the act or process of applying them.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The labeling is required to include the nutrition facts itemized in 21 CFR 101.9.

   **Source synonyms:** tagging; branding; trade dress

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The introduction of a traceable chemical group (e.g., containing an isotope or a fluorescent dye) into a protein or other biomolecule of interest so it can be tracked or quantified during experimental analysis.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biochemistry; biology; chemistry; microbiology; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

   **Source synonyms:** tagging
