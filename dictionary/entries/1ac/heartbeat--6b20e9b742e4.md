### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One pulsation of the heart; especially an irregular one, hence the emotion which causes it.

   **Example:** The policeman waited for a heartbeat in vain.

   **Example:** He alone gives me such heartbeats.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The rhythm at which a heart pulsates, a cardiac indicator.

   **Example:** If your heartbeat doesn't normalize soon, consult a doctor!

   **Source synonyms:** pulse

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A driving impulse or vital force; the vital center of something.

   **Example:** Music is the heartbeat of the people.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A very short space of time; an instant.

   **Example:** The ambulance arrived in a heartbeat.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A periodic signal generated by hardware or software to indicate normal operation or to synchronize other parts of a system.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences
