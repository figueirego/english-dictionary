### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any currency, money, or money-like asset that is primarily managed, stored or exchanged on computer systems, especially over the internet, and does not have a classical physical form of fiat currency that can be held in the hand. It may be either a cryptocurrency (involving blockchain and coin mining) or not.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** State-backed digital currencies, issued by central banks, are under development in various countries.

   **Source synonyms:** e-currency; electronic currency; virtual currency; cybercurrency; cybermoney; cybercash; e-cash; digital money; e-money; electronic money
