## Noun

### Forms

- misappreciations — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A failure to correctly and completely understand; an incorrect notion or belief that is a result of such a failure.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** It is the duty of the individual juror to strive to avoid any misappreciation of the evidence, no matter how it is represented by the barristers.

   **Example:** Although he claims to be a postmodernist, his misappreciation of the philosophy is such that he still clings to the idea of absolute truth.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An observed failure to appreciate the proper worth of a person, an act or a thing.

   **Usage:** countable; dated; uncountable
