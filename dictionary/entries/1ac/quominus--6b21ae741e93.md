## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A writ and legal fiction that (until the late 19th century) allowed the Court of Exchequer to obtain a jurisdiction over cases normally brought in the Court of Common Pleas, based on having the plaintiff in a debt case claim that he was a debtor to the king, and that the defendant's debt prevented him paying the king.

   **Usage:** attributive; historical; sometimes; uncountable

   **Example:** a quominus clause

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law
