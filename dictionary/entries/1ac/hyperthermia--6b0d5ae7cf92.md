### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The condition of having an abnormally high body temperature caused by a failure of the heat-regulating mechanisms of the body to deal with the heat coming from the environment.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; pathology; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** heatstroke

   **Source antonyms:** hypothermia

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences
