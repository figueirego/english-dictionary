### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To abide by the rules or conventions; (politics, in particular) to follow the party line.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** Television shows these days do not always toe the line of decency and common sense.

   **Source synonyms:** walk the line

   **Source antonyms:** cross the line

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To stand at one's mark before a footrace.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** Alberto Salazar is one of the most famous athletes to have toed the line at this great race.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To be on a boundary or in a grey area between two states.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** Her stories often toe the line between fact and fiction.

   **Source context (verbatim):** followed by between

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To push boundaries imposed by an authority; to push the envelope; to flirt with crossing the line.

   **Usage:** nonstandard

   **Example:** You were really toeing the line there with those comments.

   **Source synonyms:** sail close to the wind

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** conform; stay within the lines; toe the mark; toe the plank
