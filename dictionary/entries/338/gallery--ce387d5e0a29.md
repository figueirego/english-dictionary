### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An institution, building, or room for the exhibition and conservation of important objects, especially works of art.

   **Example:** In the gallery are displayed genuine artifacts dug up from the site, and in the gift shop you can buy a replica as a souvenir.

   **Example:** Some of her work is so impressive that it really ought to be in a gallery.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An establishment that buys, sells, and displays works of art.

   **Example:** Some of her best work can be yours&#58; it is available at a gallery, if you can afford it.

   **Source synonyms:** art dealer; dealer

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The uppermost seating area projecting from the rear or side walls of a theater, concert hall, or auditorium.

   **Example:** We could afford the less expensive seats in the gallery.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The spectators at an event, collectively.

   **Usage:** broadly; metonymically

   **Example:** The politician was clearly appealing to the gallery

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The part of a courtroom, often elevated and in the rear, where seating for the public audience is facilitated during trial.

   **Example:** appealing to the gallery

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

**Source sense R1.S6**

   **Example:** elegant galleries looking out onto manicured gardens
