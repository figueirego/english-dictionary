### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A census, an enumeration, a review, a survey.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A critical revision of a text.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A text established by critical revision.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A family of manuscripts which share similar traits; the variety of a language which is used in such manuscripts.

   **Example:** The existence of two major literary centres in the First Bulgarian Empire led to the emergence of two recensions of Old Church Slavonic&#58; the Bulgarian Recension and the Macedonian Recension.

   **Example:** The Russian recension of Old Church Slavonic emerged after the 10th century and was characterized by the substitution of /u/ for the nasal sound /õ/.
