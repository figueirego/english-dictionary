### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A commissioned officer in an armed military organization, typically the highest rank before flag officer ranks (generals). It is generally found in armies, air forces or naval infantry (marines).

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A military leader, distinct from the modern professional military rank.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An honorary civilian title bestowed by some southern US states, most commonly Kentucky; notably Colonel Sanders of KFC.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An informal title used to address an elderly man.

   **Usage:** Southern-US; dated

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A form of address for an auctioneer, from the American Civil War practice of commanding officers organizing the public sale of seized goods.

   **Usage:** US
