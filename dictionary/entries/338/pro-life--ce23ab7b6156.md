### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of, pertaining to, or supportive of the right to life; believing that life should be protected from its beginning to natural death in all or most circumstances.
   1. Opposed to the practice or legalization of abortion.

      **Usage:** US

      **Source synonyms:** anti-abortion

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of, pertaining to, or supportive of the right to life; believing that life should be protected from its beginning to natural death in all or most circumstances.
   1. Opposed to the legalization of euthanasia.

      **Usage:** specifically

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of, pertaining to, or supportive of the right to life; believing that life should be protected from its beginning to natural death in all or most circumstances.
   1. Opposed to the legalization of capital punishment.

      **Usage:** specifically

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Of, pertaining to, or supportive of the right to life; believing that life should be protected from its beginning to natural death in all or most circumstances.
   1. Opposed to suicide.

      **Usage:** specifically
