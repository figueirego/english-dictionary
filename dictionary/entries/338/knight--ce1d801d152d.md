<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A minor nobleman with an honourable military rank who had served as a page and squire.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An armored and mounted warrior of the Middle Ages.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** King Arthur and the Knights of the Round Table

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

   **Usage:** modern

   **Usage:** literary
