## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A condition where the body shows effects similar to excess aldosterone, such as high blood pressure, low potassium, and sodium retention. But aldosterone levels are actually normal or low.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Eating large amounts of liquorice can lead to pseudohyperaldosteronism.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; pathology; sciences
