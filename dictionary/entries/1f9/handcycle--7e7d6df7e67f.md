### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A land vehicle resembling a bicycle or tricycle but powered by the arms rather than the legs.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A mechanical exercise equipment similar to an exercise bicycle but with hand-operated pedals, used especially by seniors or people with disabilities for upper-body exercise.
