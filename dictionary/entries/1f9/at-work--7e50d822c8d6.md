### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see at, work. At one's workplace.

   **Example:** He's not home at the moment&#58; he's at work.

   **Example:** They've got a new photocopier at work.

**Source sense R1.S2**
