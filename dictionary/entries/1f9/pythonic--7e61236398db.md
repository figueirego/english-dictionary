### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of or pertaining to, or resembling, the Python in Ancient Greek mythology, a serpent which lived at Delphi (regarded as the centre of the Earth) until it was killed by Apollo.
