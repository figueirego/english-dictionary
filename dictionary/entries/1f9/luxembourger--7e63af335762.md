### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person from the independent Grand Duchy of Luxembourg or of Luxembourgish descent.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An inhabitant of the former duchy (originally county) of Luxembourg (originally Letzeburg)

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An inhabitant of the Belgian province of Luxembourg.

**Source sense R1.S4**
