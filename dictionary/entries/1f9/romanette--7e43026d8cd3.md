### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A Roman numeral in lower case, such as “ii”, as frequently introduces list items; or, a list item introduced by such.

   **Usage:** US; colloquial

   **Example:** The court held that romanette (ii) of the statute in issue did not limit eligibility for legal relief.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law; media; publishing; typography
