### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To destroy violently; to cause severe damage to something, to a point where it no longer works, or is useless.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He wrecked the car in a collision.

   **Example:** That adulterous hussy wrecked my marriage!

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To ruin or dilapidate.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To plunder goods from wrecked ships.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To dismantle wrecked vehicles or other objects, to reclaim any useful parts.

   **Usage:** Australia; transitive

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To involve in a wreck; hence, to cause to suffer ruin; to balk of success, and bring disaster on.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To be involved in a wreck; to be damaged or destroyed.

   **Usage:** intransitive
