### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Beaten or harassed by the weather; worn or damaged by exposure to the weather or the outdoors, especially to severe weather.

   **Example:** Farther down the beach they saw a weather-beaten shack and an upturned boat.

   **Example:** An old man with a weather-beaten face told them that the shack was used seasonally by fishermen.

   **Source synonyms:** weatherworn; weathered
