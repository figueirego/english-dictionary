## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A male given name from Latin Nicander \[in turn from Ancient Greek Νίκανδρος (Níkandros)\].

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. According to Herodotus, one of the three Peleiades.

   **Usage:** Greek

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; mysticism; mythology; philosophy; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A Spartan king who ruled from 750-720 BCE.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Nicander of Colophon, an ancient Greek doctor and poet from the second century CE.
