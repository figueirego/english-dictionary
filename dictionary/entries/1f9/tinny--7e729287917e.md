### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of or pertaining to or resembling tin.

   **Example:** The object had a tinny appearance.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Pertaining to the thinness and cheapness of tin or similar-looking metals, in contrast to a heavier, more valuable metal.

   **Example:** The shop was filled with tinny tourist geegaws.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Pertaining to a thin, unpleasant sound recalling that of tin being rapped.

   **Example:** The piano was not simply out of tune, but had become downright tinny.
