## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having no influence or significance on the issue at hand.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** The fact that pledges willingly submit themselves to hazing is neither here nor there, and any organization that engages in hazing will lose its charter.

   **Source synonyms:** beside the point; betwixt and between; [immaterial](../1b8/immaterial--6e2ed3762fbd.md); impertinent; irrelevant; unimportant; unrelated
