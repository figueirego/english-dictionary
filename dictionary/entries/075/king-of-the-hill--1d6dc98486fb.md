## Noun

### Forms

- kings of the hill — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A children's game in which one player stands on top of a hill or other location atop an incline, and attempts to repel other players whose goal is to capture his position.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; uncountable

   **Example:** He twisted his ankle while playing king of the hill and was not allowed to play again.

   **Source synonyms:** king of the castle

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A game mode with the goal of assuming control of an area for a specified duration.

   **Usage:** broadly; idiomatic

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** video-games

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The player in this game who stands atop the hill.

   **Usage:** countable; idiomatic

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A person who is meanwhile in leading position in an ongoing competition.

   **Usage:** broadly; idiomatic

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A person who has achieved a measure of success and is considered to be a leader in his field or otherwise dominant (for example, the dominating criminal in a region).

   **Usage:** broadly; idiomatic

   **Example:** He was considered a renegade in journalism until he won the Pulitzer Prize, but now he is the king of the hill.
