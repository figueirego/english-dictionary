### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Portable cases, large bags, and similar equipment for manually carrying, pushing, or pulling personal items while traveling

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** Please put your baggage in the trunk.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Factors, especially psychological ones, which interfere with a person's ability to function effectively.

   **Usage:** informal; uncountable; usually

   **Example:** This person has got a lot of emotional baggage.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A woman. A female, especially one who is saucy, impudent.

   **Usage:** countable; derogatory; obsolete; usually

   **Source synonyms:** disreputable; naughty

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An army's portable equipment; its baggage train.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war
