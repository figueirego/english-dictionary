### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The period of time immediately following a marriage.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A trip taken by a newly married couple during this period.

   **Example:** We went to Greece for our honeymoon.

   **Source synonyms:** wedding journey

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A period of goodwill at the beginning of a new term or relationship (e.g. towards a newly elected politician or in respect of a new business arrangement).

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** Now that the honeymoon is over, it’s time for us to get down to the business at hand.

   **Example:** The honeymoon period came to a swift end when the legislation was introduced.
