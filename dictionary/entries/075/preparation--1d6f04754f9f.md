### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of preparing or getting ready.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** I went over my notes in preparation for the exam.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The state of being prepared; readiness.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. That which is prepared.
   1. A substance, especially a remedy, that is prepared.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Example:** The traditional remedy is a bitter preparation made from steamed herbs.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. That which is prepared.
   1. A part of any animal body preserved as a specimen.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; medicine; sciences
