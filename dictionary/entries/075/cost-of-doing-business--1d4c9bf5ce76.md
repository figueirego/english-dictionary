## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The total ordinary expenses, such as materials, labor, and taxes, necessarily incurred in operating a commercial enterprise.

   **Usage:** often literal

   **Example:** Once you factor in rent, staff, and inventory, the cost of doing business around here eats up nearly half of gross revenue.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** accounting; business; finance

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An unwelcome or regrettable but predictable and unavoidable risk, loss, or inconvenience accepted as an inherent part of pursuing a particular goal or aim, especially when the risk may be impractical or counterproductive to try to reduce or eliminate.

   **Usage:** idiomatic
