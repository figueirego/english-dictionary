### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A hand printing tool, in the US often a roller, used to spread a thin even layer of ink. Early brayers, consisting of a vertical cylinder with a single handle, were designed to break up thick inks before spreading.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** media; printing; publishing
