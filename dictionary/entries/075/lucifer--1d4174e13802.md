### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The planet Venus as the daystar (the morning star).

   **Usage:** literary

   **Source synonyms:** Phosphorus; Eosphorus; Vesper; Hesperus; evening star; Venus

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The King of Babylon who was compared to the planet Venus in first the Wycliffe version then the King James Version of Isaiah 14&#58;12; it is unclear whether this verse refers to a specific king or to a representation of the entire line of kings of Babylon.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biblical; lifestyle; religion
