### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A seal which can magically transform into a human by shedding its skin.

   **Usage:** Celtic mythology

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Either of two similar-looking seals.
   1. The common seal (harbor seal) (Phoca vitulina).

      **Usage:** Scotland; Shetland

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
