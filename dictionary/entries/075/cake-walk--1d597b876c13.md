### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A type of dance originating in the United States in the 19th century.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. From the mid 1900s, a game at a fair or party in which people walk around a numbered circle along to music. When the music is stopped, the caller draws a number from a jar and whoever is standing on or closest to that number wins a cake.
