## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A small detail or distinction.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** We met the new captain while we were taking enemy fire and were unable to observe the niceties of formal introductions.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Subtlety or precision of use; exactness; preciseness.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** A rocket-propelled grenade doesn't have the nicety of a sniper round, but you must admit its effectiveness.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A delicacy of character or feeling usually from excessive refinement; fastidiousness.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Synonym of social nicety.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** social nicety (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. That which is delicate to the taste.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable
