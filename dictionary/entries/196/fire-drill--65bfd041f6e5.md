## Noun

### Forms

- fire drills — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An organized practice to prepare occupants of an office, school or other public building for evacuation in the event of a fire.

   **Example:** There was a fire drill this morning. We had to leave the building calmly and go to the birch tree, our meeting point.

   **Source synonyms:** fire practice

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any pointless, unproductive, useless, or chaotic activity.

   **Usage:** US; idiomatic

   **Example:** Since they've changed the standards again, our previous efforts now just amount to a fire drill.

   **Source synonyms:** Chinese fire drill; Polish fire drill

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A fire-starting tool consisting of a wooden rod and some primitive means of rapidly rotating the rod on a flat surface like a drill until tinder can be ignited.

   **Source synonyms:** bow drill; firestick; hand drill; pump drill; firestarter
