### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Lacking full energy, effort, commitment, or resolve.

   **Example:** He made a halfhearted attempt to read the chapter before giving up and dozing off.

   **Source synonyms:** lukewarm; tepid; adiaphroistic; apathetic; apathistical; blasé; blithe; deaf; cool; shiftless; dispassionate; dull; enervated; gallionic; halfhearted; heartless (rare); impassive; ineffervescible; insouciant; indifferent; insensible; jaded; languid; languorous; Laodicean (archaic); lethargic; listless; lackadaisical; lotophagous; lukewarm; meh; mopey; nonchalant; nonplussed (US); otiose; phlegmatic; pococurante; spiritless; supine; tepid; torpid; torpedinous (archaic); unaroused; uncaring; unconcerned; unenthusiastic; unimpressible; uninterested; unlively; unmoved; untouched; unzealous; world-weary; half-assed
