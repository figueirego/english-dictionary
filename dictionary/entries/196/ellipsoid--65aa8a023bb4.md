### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A surface, all of whose cross sections are elliptic or circular (including the sphere), that generalises the ellipse and in Cartesian coordinates (x, y, z) is a quadric with equation x²/a² + y²/b² + z²/c² = 1.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Such a surface used as a model of the shape of the earth.

   **Example:** Here the geoid is thirty meters below the ellipsoid.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; natural-sciences
