### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any tree (or the wood) of two species in the family Meliaceae&#58;
   1. Toona ciliata, native from southern Asia to Australia.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source synonyms:** toon; Australian redcedar; Indian cedar
