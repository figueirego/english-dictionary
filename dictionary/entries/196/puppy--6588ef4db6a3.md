### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A young dog, especially before sexual maturity (12–18 months).

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A young rat.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A young seal.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A dog with a youthful appearance, or any dog.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A woman’s breast.

   **Usage:** plural-normally; slang

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A (generic) thing; particularly something that is a nuisance; a sucker.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** I have another two dozen of these puppies to finish before I can go home.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A conceited and impertinent person, especially a young man.

   **Usage:** dated; derogatory
