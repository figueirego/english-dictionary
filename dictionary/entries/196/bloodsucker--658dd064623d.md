### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An animal or insect that drinks the blood of others, especially by sucking blood through a puncture wound; a leech, mosquito etc.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Someone who sheds blood; a bloodthirsty person; a violent criminal.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Someone who preys on others, or who attempts to take as much from others as possible; an extortioner; a leech.

   **Usage:** derogatory; figuratively

   **Source synonyms:** gadfly

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A supernatural being who feeds on (especially human) blood; a vampire.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Any of various lizards of the family Agamidae, especially the changeable lizard (Calotes versicolor) of Asia, and the jacky dragon of Australia, Amphibolurus muricatus).
