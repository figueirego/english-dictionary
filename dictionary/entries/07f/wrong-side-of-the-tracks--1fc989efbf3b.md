### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The part of town that is not inhabited by the wealthy. An area where the working class, poor or extremely poor live.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** He grew up on the wrong side of the tracks, but he made a success of himself.
