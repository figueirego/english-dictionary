### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The amount of time required for water to flow from the most remote point in a watershed to the watershed outlet, as a measure of the response of a watershed to a rain event.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; hydrology; natural-sciences
