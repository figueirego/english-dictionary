      **Usage:** cycle racing

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Someone or something that climbs (such as a mountain climber).
   1. A person who is constantly trying to get ahead socially.

      **Usage:** derogatory; figuratively

      **Source synonyms:** social climber

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Someone or something that climbs (such as a mountain climber).
   1. A bird that climbs, such as a parrot or woodpecker; specifically (archaic), a bird having two toes pointing forward, and two pointing backward, formerly regarded as being from the order Scansores or Zygodactyli (now obsolete as the birds formerly in this order have been reclassified into different orders); a zygodactyl.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences; ornithology

      **Usage:** Canada
