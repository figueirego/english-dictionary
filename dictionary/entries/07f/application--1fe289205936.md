### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of physically applying or laying on.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The application of this cream should reduce the swelling.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The substance applied.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The act of applying as a means; the employment of means to accomplish an end; specific use.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The act of directing or referring something to a particular case, to discover or illustrate agreement or disagreement, fitness, or correspondence.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** I make the remark, and leave you to make the application.

   **Example:** The application of a theory to a set of data can be challenging.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A computer program or the set of software that the end user perceives as a single entity as a tool for a well-defined purpose. (Also called&#58; application program; application software.)

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** This application can connect to most social networks.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences
