### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An artistic depiction, often of a person's profile, in the form of an outline without any other visual details, similar to a shadow, made by cutting a piece of black paper, filling in an outline with a solid colour (usually black), etc.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** art; arts

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A dark outline of someone or something seen against a brighter background.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** I could see a silhouette of a figure looking out from the window, but I couldn’t tell if it was a man or a woman.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An outline of a garment as it appears on a wearer.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** A-line is a classic silhouette for dresses and skirts.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** fashion; lifestyle

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A brief written description of someone or something; an outline, a sketch.

   **Usage:** figuratively
