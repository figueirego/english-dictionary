### Pronunciation

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A representative assembly in the Habsburg Netherlands (until 1581) and subsequently in the Dutch Republic (until 1795), representing the three estates of clergy, nobles and commons.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A similar assembly in prerevolutionary France, occasionally convened by the King as a consultive body; it was first convened in 1392 and last in 1789, when its dissolution led to the Revolution.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The bicameral parliament of the Kingdom of the Netherlands from 1815 onwards.
