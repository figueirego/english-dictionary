### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To relieve the pressure or compression on something.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source antonyms:** compress

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To bring someone (such as a diver) back to normal atmospheric pressure after being exposed to high pressure.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To restore (compressed data) to its original form.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences
