### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Packed into a box or boxes.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The books are boxed, ready for shipping.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Enclosed within a box.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** a boxed advertisement on the first page

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. In bridge and other card games if the cards in a pack are reversed face-up and face-down then the pack is said to be boxed.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. Of domesticated animals, discrete flocks or herds having become mixed, either accidentally or deliberately. (Aust. OED)

   **Usage:** not-comparable
