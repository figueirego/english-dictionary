## Noun · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˌpætɹəˈnɪmɪk/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Forms

- patronymics — plural
- patronymick — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A name acquired from one's father.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. A name acquired from the first name of one's father, grandfather or earlier (male) ancestor. Some cultures use a patronymic where other cultures use a surname or family name; other cultures (like Russia) use both a patronymic and a surname.

   **Usage:** broadly
