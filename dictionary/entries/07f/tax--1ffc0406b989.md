## Noun · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /tæks/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Money or goods collected by a government (or an entity to whom the government has delegated this power, e.g. in tax farming) to fund itself and its services, for example by levying a charge on income, purchases (sales), property or harvest, other than that money which is collected by the government in exchange for specific goods (e.g. the purchase of surplus vehicles).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** impost; tribute; contribution; duty; toll; rate; assessment; exaction; custom; demand; levy

   **Source antonyms:** subsidy

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A charge (of money, food, labor, etc) collected by a person, organization, etc; something required (exacted) from someone who is (really or notionally) under the control of the taxer, such as a contribution or service.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A burdensome demand

   **Usage:** figuratively; uncountable

   **Example:** a heavy tax on time or health
