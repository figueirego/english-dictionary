## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ɑdz/ — source labels: General-American
- IPA: /ɒdz/ — source labels: Received-Pronunciation

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The ratio of the probability of an event happening to that of it not happening.

   **Usage:** plural; plural-only

   **Example:** I'd say the odds are strongly in favor of the sun rising tomorrow morning.

   **Source synonyms:** chance

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The ratio of winnings to stake in betting situations.

   **Usage:** plural; plural-only

   **Example:** I looked at the odds given by all bookmakers.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An advantage given to a weaker opponent in order to equalize the game when playing casually, usually by removing one of the stronger player's pieces or by giving the weaker player more time.

   **Usage:** plural; plural-only

   **Example:** She beat me with knight odds but lost with rook odds.

   **Example:** The grandmaster gave his opponents significant time odds, of one minute versus ten minutes.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** board-games; chess; games
