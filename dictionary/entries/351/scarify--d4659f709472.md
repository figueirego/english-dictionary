### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To remove thatch (build-up of organic matter on the soil) from a lawn, to dethatch.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** agriculture; business; horticulture; lifestyle

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To make scratches or cuts on.

   **Example:** A combing tool is used to scarify, cross-scratch, or score the surface of a scratch coat or undercoat of plaster.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To make scratches or cuts on.
   1. To damage the testa (seed coat) of a seed by cutting, scraping, chemicals, hot water, or fire to allow permeation of water and faster germination.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** agriculture; business; horticulture; lifestyle

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To make scratches or cuts on.
   1. To break up, loosen, or roughen the surface of a field or road or a hard surface.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To make scratches or cuts on.
   1. To scratch, etch, burn, or cut designs into one's skin as a form of body modification.
