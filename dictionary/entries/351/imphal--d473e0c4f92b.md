### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A city, the state capital of Manipur, India.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A river that flows in the central plains of Manipur.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A valley that is flooded and drained by the Imphal river and its tributaries.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A former district, which consists of Imphal East and Imphal West.

   **Usage:** Imphal District

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A district that consists of areas that are in the eastern side of the Imphal River.

   **Usage:** Imphal East

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A district that consists of areas that are in the western side of the Imphal River.

   **Usage:** Imphal West

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A standard dialect of the official Meitei language, spoken in and around the state capital of Manipur.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A stealth guided missile destroyer warship of the Indian navy.
