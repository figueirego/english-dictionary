### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Relatively orange in colour.

   **Example:** That coat that you're wearing looks almost orangish in the evening sun.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Similar to an orange.

   **Example:** That ice cream tastes very orangish - are you sure it's just passion fruit?
