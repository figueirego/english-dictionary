### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To proceed with; to begin.

   **Example:** I went ahead with my plan.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used to grant permission for or to give endorsement of a suggestion or proposal.

   **Usage:** imperative

   **Example:** Go ahead and eat without me. I expect to be very late.

   **Source synonyms:** go for it; be my guest; feel free

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To send communication over the radio.

   **Usage:** procedure word

   **Example:** Bravo Six Actual, this is Bravo Six Four. Go ahead, over.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war
