### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of amassing.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** All her energy was devoted to the amassment of a vast fortune.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. That which is amassed; a large quantity (of something).

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** Through the Internet, we have access to an unprecedented amassment of information.
