### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A kind of projector in the 18th and 19th centuries.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A type of prison where all the cells are visible from the center, particularly if it is not possible for those in a cell to know if they are being watched.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A place in which people are subject to constant surveillance at totalitarian command.

   **Usage:** broadly; figuratively

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A room for the exhibition of novelties.
