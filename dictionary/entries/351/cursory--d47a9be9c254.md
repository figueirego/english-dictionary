### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Hasty or superficial.

   **Example:** cursory glance

   **Example:** A cursory view of the report revealed his egregious errors.

   **Example:** Most junk mail requires only a cursory glance.

   **Source synonyms:** fleeting; swift; cursory; express; fast; expeditious; feverish; fleet; fleeting; greased lightning; hasty; hectic; hectical; hyperactive; immediate; lightning fast; nimble; post haste; precipitant; quick; quick as a flash; quicksome; rapid; speedful; speedy; spit-spot (Britain); swift; tripsome (archaic); zippy (informal)
