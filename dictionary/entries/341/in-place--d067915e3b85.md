## Adverb · Record 2

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Into a proper or intended position; into place.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The girders were carefully set in place.

   **Source antonyms:** out of place

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Into a state of deployment or implementation.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** New procedures were put in place.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. Without changing position.

   **Usage:** Canada; US; not-comparable

   **Example:** They ran in place with full packs for an hour.

   **Example:** In the event of a radiation leak, shelter in place&#58; do not attempt to find a community shelter.
