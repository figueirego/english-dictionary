### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To emit (sweat or perspiration) through the skin's pores.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** I was perspiring freely after running the marathon.

   **Source synonyms:** sweat

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To be evacuated or excreted, or to exude, through the pores of the skin.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** A fluid perspires.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To cause (someone) to sweat.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive; rare

   **Source synonyms:** sweat
