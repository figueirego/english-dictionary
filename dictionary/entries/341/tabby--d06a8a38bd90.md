## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈtæb.i/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Forms

- tabbies — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A kind of waved silk, usually called watered silk, manufactured like taffeta, but thicker and stronger. The watering is given to it by calendering.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A concrete mixture of lime with shells, gravel, or stones, in equal proportions, with an equal proportion of water. When dry, this becomes as hard as rock.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A brindled cat.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A woman, irrespective of age, but often used for a young, attractive woman.

   **Usage:** Australia; countable; slang; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An old maid or gossip.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable
