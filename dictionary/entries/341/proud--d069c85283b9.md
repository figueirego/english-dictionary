### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Feeling honoured (by something); feeling happy or satisfied about an event or fact; gratified.

   **Example:** We're proud of having won / to have won.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Feeling honoured (by something); feeling happy or satisfied about an event or fact; gratified.
   1. That makes one feel proud (of something one did)

      **Example:** That was not the proudest thing I did but I can’t deny it.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Possessed of a due sense of what one deserves or is worth.

   **Example:** I was too proud to apologise.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Having too high an opinion of oneself; arrogant, supercilious, prideful.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biblical; lifestyle; religion

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Generating a sense of pride; being a cause for pride.

   **Example:** It was a proud day when we finally won the championship.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. standing upwards as in the manner of a proud person; stately or majestic.

   **Usage:** Of things

   **Example:** After it had healed, the scar tissue stood proud of his flesh.

   **Example:** The weld was still a bit proud of the panel, so she ground it down flush.
