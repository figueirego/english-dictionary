### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Expressed in terms of an exponent (power of a base), the base often being 10 or e.

   **Example:** In base-10 exponential form, 1,000,000 is written as 10⁶.

   **Example:** If not otherwise specified, "the" exponential function is normally understood to mean eˣ.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Characterised by a rate of change that is proportional to the value of the varying quantity, or, equivalently, by a doubling or halving over successive fixed intervals of time or other parameter, described as exponential growth or exponential decay.

   **Example:** There were two deaths on Monday, four on Tuesday, and eight on Wednesday. The rate of increase seems exponential.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Characterised by a very rapid rate of change, especially increase, or merely a very large amount or degree.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** There's been an exponential rise in the number of crimes reported this year, compared to last year.

   **Source synonyms:** extreme; huge

**Source antonyms:** nonexponential
