## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A low-growing plant native to the eastern US with inconspicuous green or brown flowers shaped like tiny jugs, Hexastylis arifolia.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of several other plants belonging to the genera Hexastylis or Asarum, all of which have similar flowers.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The jug-like flower of one of these plants.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually
