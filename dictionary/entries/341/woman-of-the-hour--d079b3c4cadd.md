## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A woman who has recently caught the attention of, or is being admired or honoured by, a large number of people.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; no-plural

   **Example:** After releasing her stunning exposé, she quickly became the woman of the hour in city politics.
