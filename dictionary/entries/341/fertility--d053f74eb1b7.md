### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The condition, or the degree, of being fertile.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Muckspreading increases the fertility of the soil.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The birthrate of a population; the number of live births per 1000 people per year.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The average number of births per woman within a population.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
