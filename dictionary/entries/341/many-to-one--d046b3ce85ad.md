## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having the property that many elements of one set may be assigned by the relationship to one element in the other set, and that a given element in the first set can be assigned by only one member of the second set.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; logic; mathematics; philosophy; sciences

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a relationship between two sets

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. From many sources to a single recipient.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Traditional management structure consisted of a hierarchical tree of many-to-one communications (from subordinate to manager), with each manager often significantly filtering what he passed on to his manager.
