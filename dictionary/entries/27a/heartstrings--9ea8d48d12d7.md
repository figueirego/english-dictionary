### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The tendons or other structures resembling cords once thought to be attached to, or to brace, the heart; especially the aorta, pulmonary artery, and other large arteries connected to them; also, the diaphragm.

   **Usage:** historical; plural; plural-normally

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Synonym of chordae tendineae (“the cord-like tendons that connect the papillary muscles to the tricuspid valve and the mitral valve in the heart”).

   **Usage:** broadly; plural; plural-normally

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; medicine; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** chordae tendineae (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The source of one's deepest emotions or inner feelings, especially compassion or love.

   **Usage:** figuratively; plural; plural-normally

   **Example:** The sad eyes of the orphan tugged at my heartstrings.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The essential or vital part of something; the core, the essence, the heart.

   **Usage:** archaic; figuratively; plural; plural-normally

   **Source synonyms:** bowels; sum; brass tacks; core; crux; essence; gist; drift; guts; gravamen; heart; heart and soul; heartstring (archaic); heartstrings (archaic); inwardness (obsolete); kernel; marrow; meat; moral of the story (idiomatic); nitty-gritty; nub; nuts and bolts (idiomatic); pith (figuratively); punctum saliens; quintessence; soul; spirit; stuff; substance; tenor
