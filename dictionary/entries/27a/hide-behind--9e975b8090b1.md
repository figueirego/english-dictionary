### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To use someone or something as a shield in order to avoid taking responsibility for something; to avoid attention by using someone or something as a shield.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Don't hide behind your spokesman, speak for yourself!

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see hide, behind.

   **Example:** Don't hide behind that rock, come out in the open!
