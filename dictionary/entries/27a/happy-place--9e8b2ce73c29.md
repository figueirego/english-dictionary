### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A mental visualization of a pleasant location, performed in order to avoid thinking or dealing with something unpleasant or painful.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any place (normally, one's home or some part thereof) regarded (by its owner or tenant) as a place of peace, love, fun, and enjoyment.

   **Usage:** idiomatic
