### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. begin to be; turn into (often with permanent states).

   **Usage:** copulative

   **Example:** She became a doctor when she was 25.

   **Example:** The weather will become cold after the sun goes down.

   **Example:** The full extent of my injuries did not become evident until they tried to move me.

   **Example:** The sense ‘state or process of bearing fruit’ has become imposed on fruition as the 20c. proceeded.

   **Source context (verbatim):** followed by an adjective or a noun

   **Source synonyms:** get; turn; go; come; fall; grow; wax

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To come about; happen; come into being; arise.

   **Usage:** intransitive; archaic other than in become of

   **Example:** What became of him after he was let go?

   **Example:** It hath becomen so that many a man had to sterve.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To be appropriate for.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** befit; suit; beseem

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Of an adornment, piece of clothing etc.&#58; to look attractive on (someone).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** That dress really becomes you.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To arrive, come (to a place).

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To get, receive.

   **Usage:** non-native speakers' English
