### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To hold back; to resist moving forward with something despite pressure or intention to do so; to delay, wait or stay behind.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** I think we should hang off on that section until we have more data.
