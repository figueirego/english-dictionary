### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To jump the queue, especially in a forceful manner.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** Don't push in! Go to the back of the queue!

   **Source synonyms:** cut in; push one's way in; jump the queue; jump queue

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To move a camera closer to its subject (resulting in a similar effect as zooming in, in which the camera is still but the focal length is adjusted).

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** broadcasting; cinematography; film; media; television

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see push, in.
