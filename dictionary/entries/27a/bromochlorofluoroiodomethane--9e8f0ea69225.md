## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A theoretical organic molecule made of one carbon atom bonded to four different halogens&#58; bromine, chlorine, fluorine, and iodine (CBrClFI₂). Because all four attached atoms are different, the molecule is chiral, meaning it has two mirror-image forms. It is mainly used as an example in stereochemistry and chirality studies.

   **Usage:** uncountable
