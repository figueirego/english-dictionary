## Proper noun · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Cape Horn, at the southern tip of South America.

   **Example:** Sailing around the Horn was an arduous journey for sailing ships.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The Horn of Africa, a peninsula of Africa which juts into the Arabian Sea.
