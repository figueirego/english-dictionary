### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Those qualities or features that are imperfect; the characteristic, state, or quality of being imperfect.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** You can accept your imperfection or try to improve.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Something that makes something else less than perfect; a blemish, impurity, error, etc.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** He loves me despite my imperfections.
