## Prep\_phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In a dramatically wrecked state.

   **Example:** is/are in flames \[hopes, goals, dreams, projects, etc\]

   **Example:** After the third major setback in as many days, their hopes were in flames.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see in, flames&#58; for example, on fire, aflame.

   **Example:** is/are in flames \[aircraft, ships, buildings\]

   **Example:** He was shocked to find that the whole building was in flames; how could the fire have spread so quickly?
