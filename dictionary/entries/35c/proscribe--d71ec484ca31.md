### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To forbid or prohibit.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The law proscribes driving a car while intoxicated.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To denounce.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The word ‘ain't’ is proscribed by many authorities.

   **Source antonyms:** recommend

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To banish or exclude.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Many Roman citizens were proscribed for taking part in rebellions.
