## Noun

### Forms

- chopping blocks — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A thick wooden slab used as a cutting surface for preparing food, splitting firewood or kindling, etc.

   **Source synonyms:** butcher block; butcher's block

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A block of wood against which a condemned person put their head prior to beheading.

   **Usage:** historical; informal

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A dangerous or precarious situation.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** Jim really put his head on the chopping block when he started flirting with the boss's daughter.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A dangerous or precarious situation.
   1. The threat of an impending budget cut.

      **Usage:** broadly

      **Example:** Our team might be on the chopping block — the company seems to be finding excuses to avoid funding these operating expenses.
