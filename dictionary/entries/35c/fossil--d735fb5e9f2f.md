### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The mineralized remains of an animal or plant.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any preserved evidence of ancient life, including shells, imprints, burrows, coprolites, and organically-produced chemicals.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; history; human-sciences; natural-sciences; paleontology; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

   **Usage:** figuratively
