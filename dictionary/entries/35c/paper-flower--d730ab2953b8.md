### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A plant, Bougainvillea glabra, and its flower head, so called for the papery bracts.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A plant, the peach-leaved bellflower, Campanula persicifolia, and its flower.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A plant, Psilostrophe cooperi, and its flower.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see paper, flower.

   **Example:** He was wearing a red paper flower in his lapel.
