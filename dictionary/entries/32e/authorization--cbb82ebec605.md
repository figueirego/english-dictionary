### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An act of authorizing.

   **Usage:** British; English; Oxford; US; countable

   **Source synonyms:** assent; consent; sanction; accedence; accession; allowance (obsolete); approbation; approval; assent; assentment (obsolete); authorization; OK; consent; sanction

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The state of being authorized to do something or to be somewhere; formally granted permission.

   **Usage:** British; English; Oxford; US; uncountable

   **Example:** I've got authorization. Call the office and you'll see.

   **Source synonyms:** authority; leave; sanction; allowance; authority; authorization; clearance; consent; leave (dated; formal); license; mandate; permission; permit (obsolete); warrant

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A document giving formal sanction, permission or warrant.

   **Usage:** British; English; Oxford; US; countable

   **Example:** Can I see your authorization?

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Permission, possibly limited, to spend funds for a specific budgetary purpose.

   **Usage:** British; English; Oxford; US; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** We've had the authorization for years, but we've never gotten an appropriation.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government
