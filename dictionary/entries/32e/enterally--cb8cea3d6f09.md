## Adverb

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. By means of the intestine.
   1. By means of the intestine by way of any gastrointestinal intake, whether orally or by tube feeding (nasogastric or nasoenteric).

      **Usage:** not-comparable

      **Example:** administered either parenterally or enterally

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. By means of the intestine.
   1. By means of the intestine by way of tube feeding (nasogastric or nasoenteric) as opposed to oral intake.

      **Usage:** not-comparable

      **Example:** fed either orally or enterally
