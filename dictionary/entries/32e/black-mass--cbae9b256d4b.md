## Noun

### Forms

- black masses — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A ritual, allegedly celebrated by witches, characterized by the inversion of the Traditional Latin Mass celebrated by the Roman Catholic Church.

   **Example:** Local lore held that the quarry had once been the site of black masses.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see black, mass; especially, a dark amorphous blob.

   **Example:** The paranormal investigators then aimed their cameras in the direction from which the black mass had been seen emerging.
