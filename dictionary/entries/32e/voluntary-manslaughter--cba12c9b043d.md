### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Manslaughter resulting from an intentional act done without intent to kill or premeditation and while in the heat of the moment.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Luis got arrested for voluntary manslaughter when he fatally struck Jimmy in a barfight.
