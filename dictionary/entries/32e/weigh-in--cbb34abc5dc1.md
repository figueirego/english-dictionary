### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To undergo a weigh-in.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Two days before the fight, the boxers weigh in with reporters watching.

   **Example:** His trailer weighed in lighter than it should have. He might have a leak.

   **Source context (verbatim):** with an indication of weight

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To subject to a weigh-in.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** They had to weigh him in at the loading dock.

   **Example:** They weighed every third truck in to check for overweight violations.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To weigh.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** He weighs in at upwards of 250 pounds.

   **Source context (verbatim):** with at

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To bring in one's weight, metaphorically speaking, to bear on an issue; frequently construed with on or with.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** Everyone wanted to weigh in on what kind of car he should buy.

   **Example:** Everyone spoke freely, until the boss weighed in.
