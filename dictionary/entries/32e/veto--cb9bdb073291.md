<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An invocation of that right.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

3. An authoritative prohibition or negative; a forbidding; an interdiction.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**
