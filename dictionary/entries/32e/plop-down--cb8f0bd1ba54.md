### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To sit or lie down heavily and inelegantly.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive; informal

   **Example:** They plopped themselves down on the bed.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To put down (something) in a heavy way.

   **Usage:** informal; transitive

   **Example:** He plopped his wallet down.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To pay.

   **Usage:** US; informal; transitive

   **Example:** I can't just plop down $50 for any old pair of jeans.
