### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One of the main contiguous landmasses, separated by water or geological features, on the surface of a planet, sometimes including its continental shelves and the islands on them.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; geology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Each of the traditional main regions into which the Earth’s land surface is divided, including both an extent of mainland and various conventionally associated islands, etc; namely, Africa, Europe, Asia, Australasia/Oceania, North America, South America, and Antarctica.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A large contiguous landmass as opposed to its islands, peninsulas, and so forth; mainland. Also specifically, the Old World continent of Europe–Asia–Africa&#58; see the Continent.

   **Usage:** broadly; obsolete

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A large connected expanse of something in general.

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Any continuous tract or area of land; country, connected region.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Land (as opposed to the water), dry land.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The body or mass of a celestial body such as the sun or moon.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Container, vessel, something that holds or contains something else.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s9"></a>
