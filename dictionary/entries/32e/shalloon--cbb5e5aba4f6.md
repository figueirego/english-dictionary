### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A fabric of tightly woven wool, mainly used for the linings of articles of clothing.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** countable; historical; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
