<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A thick, malleable substance made by mixing flour with other ingredients such as water, eggs, or butter, that is made into a particular form and then baked.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** Pizza dough is very stretchy.

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Money.

   **Usage:** slang; uncountable; usually

   **Example:** His mortgage payments left him short on dough.
