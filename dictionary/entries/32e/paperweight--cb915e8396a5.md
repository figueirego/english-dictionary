## Noun

### Forms

- paperweights — plural
- paper-weight — alternative; archaic

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A small, decorative, somewhat weighty object placed on one or more pieces of paper to keep them from fluttering away.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any object used for this purpose.

   **Example:** John used his coffee mug as a paperweight.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A useless piece of equipment.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Example:** On Windows, the card has a working driver. On Linux right now, it's a paperweight.
