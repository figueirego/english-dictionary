### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The women's quarters in a household; (hence, loosely) any building or area for women.

   **Usage:** Ancient-Rome; Ancient Greece

   **Source synonyms:** gynaeconitis

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An establishment in Rome where female workers made clothing and furniture for royalty.

   **Usage:** obsolete
