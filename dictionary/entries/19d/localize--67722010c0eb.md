### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make local; to fix in, or assign to, a definite place.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The intent of the aphorism "think globally, act locally" was to spur action by localizing the challenge.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To adapt (a product or service) for use in a particular country or region, typically by translating text into the language of that country and modifying currencies, date formats, etc.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** We need to localize our software for the Japanese market.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; computing; engineering; marketing; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences; software

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To adapt translated text to fit a local culture; to domesticate.

   **Example:** the ethical dilemma faced when to localize is to self-censor

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; computing; engineering; human-sciences; linguistics; marketing; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences; software; translation-studies

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To be located in or confined to; to fix upon or sequester in a locality.

   **Usage:** intransitive; often

   **Example:** After a brief systemic phase defeated by the immune system, the infection localized to certain areas where it went dormant.

   **Source context (verbatim):** with to

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To determine where something takes place or is to be found.

   **Usage:** proscribed; sometimes; transitive

   **Example:** After exploratory surgery, the extent of the problem was localized to the left lower quadrant.

   **Source synonyms:** locate

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To produce (from a ring and an ideal in that ring) the ring of fractions, where the set of allowed denominators is the compliment of the given ideal.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** algebra; mathematics; sciences
