## Noun

### Forms

- renewable energies — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Energy that is collected from renewable resources, such as wind, water, or the sun, usually and especially as applied to a category of electrical generation.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. All non-fossil fuel energy sources, including those that consume limited resources; especially, nuclear power.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; proscribed; sometimes; uncountable
