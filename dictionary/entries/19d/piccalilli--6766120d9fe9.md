### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A yellow pickle relish made from cauliflower, vegetable marrow, and other vegetables, pickled with vinegar, salt, sugar, and spiced with mustard, turmeric, and other spices.

   **Usage:** UK

   **Source synonyms:** chowchow; India relish

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A pickle, typically on a base of chopped green (unripe) tomatoes, but sometimes finely-chopped pickles, and possibly including other vegetables.

   **Usage:** US

   **Source synonyms:** relish
