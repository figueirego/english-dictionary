## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An unincorporated community in Van Buren Township, Brown County, Indiana, United States, possibly named after Col. William Christian, as in Virginia.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A village in Champaign County, Ohio, United States, named after Christiansburg, Virginia.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A town, the county seat of Montgomery County, Virginia, United States, named after Col. William Christian. Originally named Hans Meadows, then Christiansburgh, later revised.
