### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A filming style characterized by close-up camera work focusing on the reactions of a single character.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** broadcasting; cinematography; film; media; television

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A very short dramatic film clip or acted scene.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A short (typically minuteslong) serialized show made for the phone, characterized by rapid plot development, and originally developed in China.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** broadcasting; film; media; television

   **Source synonyms:** duanju; vertical drama; mobile drama
