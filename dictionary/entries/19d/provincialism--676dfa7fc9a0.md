### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The quality of being provincial; having provincial tastes, mentality, manners.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The blacksmith's provincialism showed in his speech and manner

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A word or locution characteristic of a region or district.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences
