### Pronunciation

### Forms

- most intensive — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Done with intensity or to a great degree; thorough.

   **Source antonyms:** unintensive; nonintensive; superficial

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Being made more intense.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Making something more intense; intensifying.
   1. Of agriculture&#58; increasing the productivity of an area of land.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** agriculture; business; economics; lifestyle; sciences

      **Source antonyms:** extensive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Making something more intense; intensifying.
   1. Of a word&#58; serving to give emphasis or force.

      **Example:** an intensive verb or preposition

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

      **Source synonyms:** intensative

      **Source antonyms:** downtoning

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Involving much activity in a short period of time; highly concentrated.

   **Example:** I took a three-day intensive course in finance.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Of or pertaining to innate or internal intensity or strength rather than outward extent.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Chiefly suffixed to a noun&#58; using something with intensity; requiring a great amount of something; demanding.

   **Example:** This job is difficult because it is so labour-intensive.
