**Source sense R1.S1**

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** slang

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An unlikely diagnosis, especially for symptoms probably caused by a common ailment.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Someone who has Ehlers-Danlos syndrome or hypermobility spectrum disorder

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A biracial person, specifically one born to a Sub-Saharan African person and a white person.

   **Usage:** derogatory; ethnic; slang; slur

   **Usage:** informal
