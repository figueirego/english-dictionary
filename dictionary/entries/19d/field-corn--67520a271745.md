### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any variety of corn (maize) grown primarily for fodder or use as grain; usually, one with lower sugar content than sweet corn and harvested either to be stored dry (on the cob or off, divorced of the stover) or ensiled along with the stover.

   **Usage:** US; uncountable; usually

   **Source synonyms:** dent corn; grain corn
