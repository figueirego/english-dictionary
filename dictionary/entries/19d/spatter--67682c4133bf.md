## Verb · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈspætɚ/ — source labels: General-American

### Forms

- spatters — present; singular; third-person
- spattering — participle; present
- spattered — participle; past
- spattered — past

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To splash (someone or something) with small droplets.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** When my wet chihuahua shook himself, I was spattered with smelly water.

   **Source synonyms:** spurt

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To cover, or lie upon (something) by having been scattered, as if by splashing.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** strew

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To distribute (a liquid) by sprinkling; to sprinkle around.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to spatter blood

   **Source synonyms:** asperge; asperse; besprinkle; sprinkle

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To send out or disperse (something) as if in droplets.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To send out small droplets; to splash in small droplets (on or against something).

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Make sure the pieces of fish are dry before you put them into the hot oil so that it doesn’t spatter.

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To injure by aspersion; to defame.

   **Usage:** figuratively; obsolete; transitive

## Noun · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈspætɚ/ — source labels: General-American

### Forms

- spatters — plural

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A spray or shower of droplets hitting a surface.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. A spot or spots of a substance spattered on a surface.

   **Example:** There was what looked like a spatter of blood on one wall.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. The sound of droplets hitting a surface.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. A burst or series of sounds resembling the sound of droplets hitting a surface.

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. A collection of objects scattered like droplets splashed onto a surface.

   **Usage:** figuratively
