### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An act of the Parliament of Great Britain passed in 1723 in response to a series of raids by two groups of poachers, known as the Blacks. It greatly strengthened the criminal code and specified over 200 capital crimes.

   **Usage:** UK; historical

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Act XI., 1836, of the Indian Legislature, which placed European subjects on a level with natives as to their subjection in civil causes to the British East India Company's courts, including those under native judges.

   **Usage:** India; derogatory; historical
