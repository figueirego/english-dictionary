### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To take a premeal (preprandial) bolus of insulin shortly before a meal (usually just a few minutes before).

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Should I prebolus or not before starchy dinners?

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To administer (one's insulin dose) in this way.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I'm going to prebolus my shot today.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To preempt bad effects of (one's meals) in this way.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I'm going to prebolus the hell out of that pizza.
