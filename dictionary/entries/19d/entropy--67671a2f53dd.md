### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A measure of the disorder present in a system.
   1. A measure of the disorder directly proportional to the natural logarithm of the number of microstates yielding an equivalent thermodynamic macrostate.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable; Boltzmann definition

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A measure of the disorder present in a system.
   1. Shannon entropy

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; information-theory; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A measure of the amount of energy in a physical system that cannot be used to do work.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics; thermodynamics

   **Source synonyms:** unavailable energy

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The capacity factor for thermal energy that is hidden with respect to temperature.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The dispersal of energy; how much energy is spread out in a process, or how widely spread out it becomes, at a specific temperature.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A measure of the amount of information and noise present in a signal.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; information-theory; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences; statistics

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The tendency of a system that is left to itself to descend into chaos.

   **Usage:** uncountable
