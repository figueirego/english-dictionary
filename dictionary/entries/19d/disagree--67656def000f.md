### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To fail to agree; to have a different opinion or belief.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** John disagreed with Mary frequently.

   **Example:** Bob says cats are friendlier than dogs, but I disagree.

   **Source synonyms:** beg to differ; quarrel; dispute

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To fail to conform or correspond with.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** My results in the laboratory consistently disagree with yours.
