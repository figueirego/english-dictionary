<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To subdue, put down, or silence (someone or something); to force (someone) to submit.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** crush

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To suppress, to put an end to (something); to extinguish.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to quell grief

   **Example:** to quell the tumult of the soul

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete

<a id="r1-s5"></a>
