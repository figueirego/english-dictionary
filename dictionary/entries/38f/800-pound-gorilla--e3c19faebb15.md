### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An entity that dominates its environment.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** Q&#58; Where does an 800-pound gorilla sleep? A&#58; Wherever it wants!

   **Example:** Back then, the mayor was an 800-pound gorilla in this town.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Something dangerous, menacing, or frightening that is obvious but not addressed.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** The mayor's abuse of his power was an 800-pound gorilla in polite conversation.

   **Source synonyms:** 800-pound gorilla in the room; gorilla in the room; elephant in the room; emperor's new clothes
