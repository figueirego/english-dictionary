### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To keep repeating a particular sound involuntarily during speech.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source synonyms:** hacker (Britain; archaic; dialectal); stutter

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To utter with a stammer, or with timid hesitancy.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He blushed, and stammered a few words of apology.

### Related words
