### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A stone that can be stepped on in crossing something, especially a marsh or creek.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Something used as a way to progress to something or somewhere else.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** He was full of ambition, and force, and life, intending all sorts of great things, and meaning to make his position a stepping stone to all that was excellent in public life.

   **Example:** Esperanto can make a good stepping stone language to Latin, which may be difficult for amateur linguists.
