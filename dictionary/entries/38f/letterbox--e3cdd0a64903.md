## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A box into which mail is put.
   1. A collection point for mail intended for onward delivery, a secure box or receptacle for this purpose.

      **Usage:** Commonwealth; Ireland; UK

      **Source synonyms:** mailbox; post box; pillar box (UK)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A box into which mail is put.
   1. A delivery point for mail, a box, compartment or slot for this purpose.

      **Usage:** Commonwealth; Ireland; UK

      **Example:** A wad of junk mail was pushed through the letterbox in the front door and dropped onto the mat.

      **Source synonyms:** mailbox; mail slot

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A hidden container that holds a logbook and rubber stamp, found by following clues as a form of recreation.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>
