<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To be or remain suspended.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The lights hung from the ceiling.

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The smoke hung in the room.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To veer in one direction.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

   **Usage:** intransitive; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports; tennis

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a ball in cricket

**Source sense R1.S5**

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He hung his head in shame.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To cause (something) to be suspended, as from a hook, hanger, hinges, or the like.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Hang those lights from the ceiling.

   **Example:** to hang a door

**Source sense R1.S7**

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The culprits were hanged from the nearest tree.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law
