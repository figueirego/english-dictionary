### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A rotund, overweight, or obese person.

   **Usage:** derogatory; slang; vulgar

   **Example:** He is such a fat-ass he can barely put his feet together.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A jibe used on someone disliked, frequently someone regarded as lazy.

   **Usage:** derogatory; vulgar

   **Example:** My boss is a fat-ass who doesn't know his job as well as I do.

   **Example:** Yes, your whole department is full of fat-asses.
