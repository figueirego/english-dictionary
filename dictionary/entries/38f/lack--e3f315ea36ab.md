### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** It is a question of money, or lack of same.

## Verb · Etymology 2 · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /læk/ — source labels: General-American; Received-Pronunciation

### Forms

- lacks — present; singular; third-person
- lacking — participle; present
- lacked — participle; past
- lacked — past

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To be without, not to have, to need, to require.

   **Usage:** stative; transitive

   **Example:** My life lacks excitement.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To be short (of or for something).

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** He'll never lack for company while he's got all that money.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To be in want.

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To see the deficiency in (someone or something); to find fault with, to malign, reproach.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To be off one's guard.

   **Usage:** African-American Vernacular

   **Example:** His opps caught him lacking.
