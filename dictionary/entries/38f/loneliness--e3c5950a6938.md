### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A feeling of depression resulting from being alone or from having no companions.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** The loneliness resulting from the loss of loved ones, lack of purpose in life, and social discrimination is a real inner hell.

   **Source synonyms:** ellingness; lonesomeness

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The condition or state of being alone or having no companions.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source synonyms:** aloneness; friendlessness; aloneness; isolation; loneliness; loneness (archaic); onehead; oneship; onlyhood; onliness; seclusion; solitariness; solitude

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The state of being unfrequented or devoid of human activity (of a place or time).

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source synonyms:** unfrequentedness

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A desire to be alone; disposition to solitude.

   **Usage:** obsolete; uncountable; usually
