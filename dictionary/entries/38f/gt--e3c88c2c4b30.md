## Symbol

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used to quote a person, especially to emphasize what was said and mock it.

   **Usage:** Internet; chiefly 4chan slang

   **Example:** “i mean ubuntu is better than debian anyways, it's even the most mainstream version of desktop linux” “&gt;ubuntu is better than debian”

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used to quote a person, especially to emphasize what was said and mock it.
   1. Used to emphasize and mock (what the speaker assumes is) someone's inner thoughts.

      **Usage:** Internet; chiefly 4chan slang

      **Example:** “and so i got a mac since they have less viruses\[…\]” “&gt;macs are virus-free”

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used to quote a person, especially to emphasize what was said and mock it.
   1. Used to emphasize and mock someone's actions.

      **Usage:** Internet; chiefly 4chan slang

      **Example:** “so i opened up internet explorer, went to google, and looked it up\[…\]” “&gt;using internet explorer in 2026”

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Used to quote a person, especially to emphasize what was said and mock it.
   1. Used for storytelling in short, fragmented statements.

      **Usage:** Internet; chiefly 4chan slang

      **Example:** &gt;be me &gt;decide to buy CS&#58;GO to see why it's so popular &gt;get instantly spawnkilled in my first game &gt;uninstall the game and demand a refund

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Used to signify enjoyment or appreciation of something (over everything else), often with many repetitions of the symbol.

   **Usage:** Internet

   **Example:** effort &gt;

   **Example:** random hugs &gt;&gt;&gt;&gt;&gt;
