### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who fills.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Something added to fill a space or add weight or size.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any semisolid substance used to fill gaps, cracks or pores.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A dermal filler, a substance injected beneath the skin to restore lost volume.

   **Usage:** cosmetic surgery

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A relatively inert ingredient added to modify physical characteristics; a bulking agent.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A short article in a newspaper or magazine.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A short piece of music or an announcement between radio or TV programmes.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Any spoken sound or word used to fill gaps in speech; filled pause.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. Cut tobacco used to make up the body of a cigar.

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. In COBOL, the description of an unnamed part of a record that contains no data relevant to a given context (normally capitalised when in a data division).

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; programming; sciences

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. A plant that lacks a distinctive shape and can fill inconvenient spaces around other plants in pots or gardens.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** agriculture; business; horticulture; lifestyle

<a id="r1-s12"></a>

**Source sense R1.S12**

   **Usage:** plural-normally

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; forestry
