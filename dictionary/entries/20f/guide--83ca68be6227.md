### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Someone who guides, especially someone hired to show people around a place or an institution and offer information and explanation, or to lead them through dangerous terrain.

   **Example:** The guide led us around the museum and explained the exhibits.

   **Source synonyms:** guider; xenagogue

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A document or book that offers information or instruction; guidebook.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A sign that guides people; guidepost.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any marking or object that catches the eye to provide quick reference.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Synonym of legend, a key to symbols, abbreviations, and terms on a map, chart, etc.

   **Source synonyms:** legend (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A device that guides part of a machine, or guides motion or action.
   1. A blade or channel for directing the flow of water to the buckets in a water wheel.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A device that guides part of a machine, or guides motion or action.
   1. A grooved director for a probe or knife in surgery.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A device that guides part of a machine, or guides motion or action.
   1. A strip or device to direct the compositor's eye to the line of copy being set.

      **Usage:** dated

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** media; printing; publishing

**Source sense R1.S9**

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; mysticism; occult; philosophy; sciences
