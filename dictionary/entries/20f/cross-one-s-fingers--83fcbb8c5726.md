### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To put the middle finger across the index finger, especially

   **Example:** She was seen crossing her fingers in the wedding photo.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To put the middle finger across the index finger, especially&#58;
   1. when wishing for luck, or

      **Example:** I hope everything turns out OK. I'll cross my fingers for you. / I'll cross my fingers that everything turns out OK.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To put the middle finger across the index finger, especially&#58;
   1. when telling a lie (and attempting to escape the moral and other consequence of that lie).
