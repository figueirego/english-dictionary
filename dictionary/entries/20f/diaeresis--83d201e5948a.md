### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A separation of one syllable (especially a vowel which is a diphthong, that is, beginning with one sound and ending with another) into two distinct syllables; distraction.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; phonology; prosody; sciences

   **Source antonyms:** synaeresis

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An occurrence of separate vowel sounds in adjacent syllables without an intervening consonant; a hiatus.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; phonology; prosody; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The diacritical mark consisting of two dots (¨) placed over a letter (especially the second of two consecutive vowels) to indicate that it is sounded separately, usually as a distinct syllable.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** communications; journalism; literature; media; orthography; publishing; writing

   **Source synonyms:** trema; umlaut

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A division, a separation.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; rare; rhetoric; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A natural break in rhythm when a word ends at the end of a metrical foot in a line of verse.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; phonology; prosody; sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. An act of separating body parts or tissues which are normally together.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences; surgery
