### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A street or path or tunnel that goes nowhere or is blocked on one end.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** That road comes to a dead end at the lake.

   **Source synonyms:** cul-de-sac; blind alley; impasse

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The inner end of a drift or tunnel.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; mining

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A position that offers no hope of change or progress.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** Her father suggested that she decline the job because it was a dead end.

   **Source synonyms:** cul-de-sac; impasse

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A split end in the hair.
