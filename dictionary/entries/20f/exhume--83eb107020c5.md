### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To dig out of the ground; to take out of a place of burial; to disinter.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The archeologist exhumed artifacts from the ground with a shovel.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To uncover; to bring to light.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** Long dead traditions are exhumed just for the tourists.
