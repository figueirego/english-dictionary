### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. That which sinks or descends.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One who sinks something.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A weight used in fishing to cause the line or net to sink.

   **Example:** Hook the sinker onto this loop.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** fishing; hobbies; lifestyle

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any of several high speed pitches that have a downward motion near the plate; a two-seam fastball, a split-finger fastball, or a forkball.

   **Example:** His sinkers drew one ground ball after another.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; baseball; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports
