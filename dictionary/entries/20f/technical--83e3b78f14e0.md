<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of or related to technology.

   **Example:** Since the 1940s there had been many technical advancements, and a Mach 3 interceptor was no longer a mere pipe dream.

   **Source synonyms:** technological; technologic

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a person

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Relating to, or requiring, technique.

   **Example:** The performance showed technical virtuosity, but lacked inspiration.

   **Source synonyms:** technic

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Relating to, or requiring, technique.
   1. Requiring advanced techniques for successful completion.

      **Example:** technical climbing

      **Source synonyms:** technic

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Relating to the internal mechanics of a market rather than more basic factors.

   **Usage:** securities and other markets

   **Example:** The market had a technical rally, due to an oversold condition.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. In the strictest sense, but not practically or meaningfully.

   **Example:** Crossing the front lawn of that house to get to the mailbox was a technical trespass.
