### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Incapable of being spoken or uttered.

   **Source synonyms:** unutterable; ineffable; inexpressible

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Impossible to speak about.

**Source sense R1.S3**

   **Source synonyms:** unutterable; undiscussable
