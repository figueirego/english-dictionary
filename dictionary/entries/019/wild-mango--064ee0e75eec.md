### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any wild-growing form of the mango (Mangifera indica).

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A bambangan, a plant of species Mangifera pajang, endemic to Borneo.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An African mango (Irvingia gabonensis), of tropical central Africa.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A white mango (Mangifera caesia), of Malesia, Indonesia, New Guinea, and the Philippines.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A green plum (Buchanania obovata), of Australia.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**
