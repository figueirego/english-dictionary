### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Unable to reproduce (or procreate).

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Terse; lacking sentiment or emotional stimulation, as in a manner of speaking.

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Fruitless, uninspiring, or unproductive.

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

   **Example:** a sterile kitchen table

<a id="r1-s5"></a>
