### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. (operator theory, of an approximate unit relative to a normed ideal) Asymptotically commuting with each specified operator, so that the norm of every corresponding commutator tends to zero.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

   **Extractor diagnostics (not usage labels):** empty-gloss; no-gloss

**Supplemental definition (evidence reviewed; source gap preserved):** The original quotation explicitly concerns approximate units relative to the Macaev norm. The primary normed-ideal definition states the commutator limit for each operator; the chosen norm is kept explicit, rather than replaced by ordinary operator norm.
