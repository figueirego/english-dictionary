### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of receiving.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** receipt

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The act or ability to receive radio or similar signals.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** We have poor TV reception in the valley.

   **Example:** The new system provides exceptional quality of the reception signal.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; electrical-engineering; electricity; electromagnetism; electronics; energy; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A social engagement, usually to formally welcome someone.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** After the wedding we proceeded to the reception.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A reaction; the treatment received on first talking to a person, arriving at a place, etc.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The ambassador's jokes met a cold reception.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The desk of a hotel or office where guests are received.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The reception of the hotel is on the second floor; exit the lift and turn right.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The school year, or part thereof, between preschool and Year 1, when children are introduced to formal education.

   **Usage:** UK; countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** education

   **Source synonyms:** kindergarten (US)

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The conscious adoption or transplantation of legal phenomena from a different culture.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

8. The act of catching a pass.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** American-football; ball-games; football; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports
