## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A back-bending asana (yoga pose), in which one lays face-down and then extends their arm to push only their upper body up, causing the back to arch.

   **Usage:** uncountable; yoga

   **Source synonyms:** cobra pose
