### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An argument or objection based on an ambiguity of wording or similar trivial circumstance; a minor complaint.

   **Example:** He harped on his quibble about how the dark red paint should be described as carmine rather than burgundy.

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To complain or argue in a trivial or petty manner.

   **Usage:** informal; intransitive

   **Example:** They are constantly quibbling over insignificant details.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To contest, especially some trivial issue in a petty manner.

   **Usage:** informal; rare; transitive

   **Example:** The customer quibbled the bill.
