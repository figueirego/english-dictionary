### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The state or quality of being active; activeness.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Pit row was abuzz with activity.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Something done as an action or a movement.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** The activity for the morning was a walk to the store.

   **Source synonyms:** pursuit; undertaking

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Something done for pleasure or entertainment, especially one involving movement or an excursion.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** An increasing number of sports activities are on offer at the university.

   **Example:** Quilting can be an enjoyable activity.
