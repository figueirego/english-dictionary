### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A symbolic and often arbitrary unit of measurement representing strength and fighting ability.

   **Usage:** Japanese; especially; slang

   **Example:** Miku's power level skyrockets up to 15,000 during the big battle in episode 31.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** fiction; lifestyle; literature; media; publishing

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An interest or specialized knowledge in a subject seen as uncool, especially related to video games and otaku culture.

   **Usage:** Internet; broadly

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Extreme political views, especially those on the far right.

   **Usage:** Internet; broadly

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see power, level.

   **Example:** This is a heavy-duty fridge that operates on a higher power level than most households can supply.
