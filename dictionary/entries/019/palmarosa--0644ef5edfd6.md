### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A plant related to lemongrass, but with a rose fragrance, of species Cymbopogon martini.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** ginger grass; motia grass; rosha grass; rusa grass; sofia grass

<a id="r1-s2"></a>
