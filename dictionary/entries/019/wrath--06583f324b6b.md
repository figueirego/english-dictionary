**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Great anger; (countable) an instance of this.

   **Usage:** formal; uncountable; usually

   **Example:** Homer relates an episode in the Trojan War that reveals the tragic consequences of the wrath of Achilles.

   **Source synonyms:** fury; ire; bile; choler; dander; displeasure; dudgeon; fury; gall; grudge; indignation; ire; rage; red mist (idiomatic); resentment; spleen; vexation; woodness; wrath

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Punishment, retribution, or vengeance resulting from anger; (countable) an instance of this.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** the wrath of God
