### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A diacritic, resembling a 180°-rotated ‘C’ (i.e., being similar in appearance to ⟨ᵓ⟩), written atop a consonant to mark gemination, superseded in Classical Latin by doubling the letter representing the geminated consonant.

   **Usage:** Old-Latin

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** media; publishing; typography

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A unit of weight equal to one quarter of an uncia.

   **Usage:** Roman measurements
