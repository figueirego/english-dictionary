### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To hold (something) in the memory; to remember; also, to be mindful of or pay attention to (something); to consider; to note.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** Bear in mind that I’m not as young as I was, so I can’t walk as fast as you.

   **Source synonyms:** keep in mind; bear in mind (idiomatic); bethink (obsolete); keep in mind; mind (dialectal); recall; recollect; remember; retrieve; think back; umbethink (British; dialectal)
