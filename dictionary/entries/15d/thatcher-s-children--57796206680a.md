## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. People who grew up or were born in the United Kingdom during the premiership of Margaret Thatcher (1979–1990) and who adopted the ideology of Thatcherism.

   **Usage:** plural; plural-only

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see Thatcher, children.

   **Usage:** plural; plural-only

   **Example:** Margaret Thatcher is a former Prime Minister of the United Kingdom. Denis Thatcher is her husband, and Mark Thatcher and Carol Thatcher are Thatcher's children.
