### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To achieve something.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to chalk up a victory

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To assign a debt to someone.

   **Usage:** Australia; archaic; slang

   **Example:** Give me another drink and chalk it up to John.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To attribute to something, often tangentially.

   **Usage:** US; slang

   **Example:** I know we lost, but can we chalk it up as a win?
