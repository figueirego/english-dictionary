### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To focus one's aim; to zoom in and center (on something).

   **Example:** The pilot zeroed in on the bunker and launched a guided missile.

   **Example:** Zero in and find a watermark in the image.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To successfully narrow down a search (for).

   **Example:** We have zeroed in on the source of the problem.

   **Example:** See if you can zero in on the caller.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To concentrate or focus one's attention (on a task).

   **Example:** One member of the check fraud team will zero in on the fingerprints.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To converge (on).

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** At the spring sale, everyone zeroed in on the bargain clothes.
