### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A genie who is imprisoned in a lamp, as in the tale of Aladdin.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** fiction; literature; media; publishing

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person who does, or is obliged to do, what they are bid; something that dutifully performs virtually any task.

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
