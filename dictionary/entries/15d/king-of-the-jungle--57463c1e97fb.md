### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The lion, Panthera leo.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Source synonyms:** king of beasts

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Someone who dominates some scene, being the leader in their field, the dominant criminal in a region, etc.

   **Usage:** figuratively; idiomatic

   **Example:** He thinks he's King of the Jungle around here? He's got another think coming.
