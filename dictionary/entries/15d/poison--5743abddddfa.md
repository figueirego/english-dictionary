### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A substance that is harmful or lethal to a living organism when ingested.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** We used a poison to kill the weeds.

   **Source synonyms:** atter; bane; contaminant; pollutant; toxin

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Anything harmful to a person or thing.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

   **Example:** Gossip is a malicious poison.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An alcoholic drink. (Mainly in the phrases "name your poison" and "what's your poison?")

   **Usage:** countable; idiomatic; informal; uncountable

   **Example:** — What's your poison? — I'll have a glass of whiskey.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any substance that inhibits catalytic activity.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

## Verb · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈpoj.zən/ — source labels: British; Southern; Standard

### Forms

- poisons — present; singular; third-person
- poisoning — participle; present
- poisoned — participle; past
- poisoned — past

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To use poison to kill or paralyse (somebody).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The assassin poisoned the king.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To pollute; to cause to become poisonous.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** That factory is poisoning the river.

   **Source synonyms:** contaminate; pollute; taint

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To cause to become much worse.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Suspicion will poison their relationship.

   **Example:** He poisoned the mood in the room with his non-stop criticism.

   **Source synonyms:** corrupt; taint

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To cause (someone) to hate or to have unfair negative opinions.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** She's poisoned him against all his old friends.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To inhibit the catalytic activity of.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To place false or malicious data into (a cache, etc.) as part of an exploit.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences
