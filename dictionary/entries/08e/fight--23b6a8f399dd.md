**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Senses relating to physical conflict&#58;
   1. To engage in combat with; to oppose physically, to contest with.

      **Usage:** transitive

      **Example:** My grandfather fought the Nazis in World War II.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Senses relating to physical conflict&#58;
   1. To conduct or engage in (battle, warfare, a cause, etc.).

      **Usage:** transitive

      **Example:** Our soldiers fought the battle just over that hill.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Senses relating to physical conflict&#58;
   1. To contend in physical conflict, either singly or in war, battle, etc.

      **Usage:** intransitive

      **Example:** A wounded animal will fight tooth and nail; relentless, savage and murderous.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Senses relating to physical conflict&#58;
   1. To contend in physical conflict with each other, either singly or in war, battle, etc.

      **Usage:** reciprocal

      **Example:** The two boxers have been fighting for more than half an hour.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Senses relating to physical conflict&#58;
   1. To cause to fight; to manage or manoeuvre in a fight.

      **Usage:** causative

      **Example:** to fight cocks; to fight one’s ship

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To strive for something; to campaign or contend for success.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** He fought for the Democrats in the last election.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To try to overpower; to fiercely counteract.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The government pledged to fight corruption.

## Noun · Etymology 2 · Record 2

### Forms

- fights — plural

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. An occasion of fighting.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** One of them got stuck in a chokehold and got stabbed to death during the fight.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. A battle between opposing armies.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; uncountable

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. A physical confrontation or combat between two or more people or groups.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Watch your language! Are you looking for a fight?

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. A boxing or martial arts match.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** I’m going to Nick’s to watch the big fight tomorrow night.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. A conflict, possibly nonphysical, with opposing ideas or forces; strife.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** I’ll put up a fight to save this company.

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. The will or ability to fight.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** That little guy has a bit of fight in him after all. As soon as he saw the size of his opponent, all the fight went out of him.

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

**Source sense R2.S7**

7. A screen for the combatants in ships; an arming.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable
