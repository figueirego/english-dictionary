## Noun · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈɹol.ɔf/ — source labels: General-American
- IPA: /ˈɹoʊ.lɔf/ — source labels: General-American

### Forms

- rolloffs — plural

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A sudden large uncommanded roll to the left or right experienced by an aircraft entering a stall, resulting from one wing stalling slightly before the other and exacerbated by the reduction in aileron control authority at high angles of attack.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** aeronautics; aerospace; aviation; business; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. A rolloff dumpster.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** There's so much junk to be removed that we ordered a rolloff.
