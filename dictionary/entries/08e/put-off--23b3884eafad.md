## Verb · Record 1

### Forms

- puts off — present; singular; third-person
- putting off — participle; present

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To postpone, especially through procrastination.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Don't put off your homework to the last minute.

   **Source synonyms:** shelve; mothball

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To delay (a task, event, etc.).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The storm put off the game by a week.

   **Example:** The storm put the game off by a week.

   **Example:** I'm too busy to see Mr Smith today. I'll have to put him off.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To distract; to disturb the concentration of.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Please be quiet. I'm trying to concentrate and you're putting me off.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To cause to dislike; to discourage (from doing).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Almost drowning put him off swimming.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To emit; to give off (an odor, smoke, etc.).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** This type of firewood puts off a strong smell.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To take off (something worn).

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive

   **Example:** to put off a mask
