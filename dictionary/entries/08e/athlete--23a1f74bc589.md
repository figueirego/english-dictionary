### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A participant in any of a group of sporting activities including track and field, road running, cross country running and racewalking.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person who actively participates in physical sports, especially with great skill; a sportsperson.

   **Usage:** Canada; US

   **Example:** She's the first athlete in her sport to obtain a corporate sponsor.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
