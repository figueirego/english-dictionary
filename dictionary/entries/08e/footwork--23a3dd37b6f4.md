### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any movement of the feet, especially intricate or complex movement, as in sports or dancing.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** The dancer impressed the crowd with his fancy footwork.

   **Example:** The horses impressed the riders with their unerring footwork, and the mountain goats simply astounded everyone with their surefootedness.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A subgenre of juke/ghetto house and style of street dance that originated in Chicago in the early 1990s.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Finesse.

   **Usage:** informal; uncountable; usually
