**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make a statement that is in error, inadvertently; to say (something) incorrectly, through a slip of the tongue.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The speaker misstated the year of his grandfather's birth by a hundred years, but it was an honest mistake&#58; how often does one speak of the 1800s?
