## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ɪkˈspɛns/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Forms

- expenses — plural
- expence — alternative; obsolete

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A spending or consuming, often a disbursement of funds.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** She went to great expense to ensure her children would get the best education.

   **Example:** Buying the car was a big expense, but will be worth it in the long run.

   **Example:** We had a training weekend in New York, at the expense of our company.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The elimination or consumption of something, sometimes with the notion of loss or damage to the thing eliminated.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Jones reached the final at the expense of Smith, who couldn't beat him.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Loss.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable
