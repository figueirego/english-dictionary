### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To end hostilities; to reach a peace agreement; to initiate or resume a cordial relationship after a period of animosity.

   **Example:** Both countries can rebuild now that they have made peace with each other.

   **Example:** He's finally made peace with his estranged father.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To settle a dispute or disagreement.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** The committee made peace and agreed to go with the new plan.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To accept something, especially if it is considered unfavourable.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I've finally made peace with the fact that having diabetes means no more sugar.

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** bury the hatchet — settle a dispute; come to terms with — accept something unfavourable
