### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of or relating to faculty, especially to mental faculty.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Not obligate; optional, discretionary or elective.

   **Source synonyms:** discretionary; elective; facultative; free; noncompulsory; nonmandatory; nonobligate; nonobligatory; nonrequired; nonrequisite; optional; optionary; unrequired; volitional; voluntary

   **Source antonyms:** basic; essential; indispensable; necessar; necessary; necessitous; needed; prerequisite; required; requisite

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. That grants permission or power to do something.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Able to perform a particular life function, or to live generally, in more than one way.

   **Example:** facultative feeder

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences

   **Source antonyms:** obligate

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. At which a given function is positive.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a point
