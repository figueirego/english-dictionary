### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A square matrix whose rows consist of nonnegative real numbers, with each row summing to 1. Used to describe the transitions of a Markov chain; its element in the i'th row and j'th column describes the probability of moving from state i to state j in one time step.

   **Usage:** stochastic processes

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences
