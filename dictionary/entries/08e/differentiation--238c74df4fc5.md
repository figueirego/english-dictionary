### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act or process of differentiating (generally, without a specialized sense).
   1. The act of treating one thing as distinct from another, or of creating such a distinction; of separating a class of things into categories; of describing a thing by illustrating how it is different from something else.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The act or process of differentiating (generally, without a specialized sense).
   1. The process of developing distinct components.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The process by which the components of multicellular life (cells, organs, etc.) are produced and acquire function, as when a seed develops the root and stem, and the initial stem develops the leaf, branches, and flower buds.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The evolutionary process by which one taxonomic group (species, genus, variety, etc.) becomes distinct from another, or acquires distinct features; the result of such a process&#58; distinctness.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; evolution

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The process of separation of cooling magma into various rock types.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; geology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The process of applying the derivative operator to a function; of calculating a function's derivative.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** calculus; mathematics; sciences
