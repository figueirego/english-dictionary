## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈpaɪpˌlaɪn/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Forms

- pipelines — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A conduit made of pipes used to convey water, gas or petroleum, etc.

   **Example:** An oil pipeline has been opened from the Caspian Sea.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A channel (either physical or logical) by which information is transmitted sequentially (that is, the first information in is the first information out).

   **Example:** 3D images are rendered using the graphics pipeline.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A system or process through which something is conducted.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** A new version of the software is in the pipeline, but has not been rolled out.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A widely observed pattern of development in personal interests, circumstances, or opinions.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** Many who grew up in foster homes in the county have fallen victim to the foster-care-to-homelessness pipeline.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A continuous, contributing source of benefits, talent, or innovation.

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The inside of a wave that a surfer is riding, when the wave has started closing over it.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports; surfing
