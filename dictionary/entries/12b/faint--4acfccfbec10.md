## Adjective · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /feɪnt/ — source labels: General-American; Received-Pronunciation

### Forms

- fainter — comparative
- faintest — superlative
- feint — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Lacking strength; weak; languid; inclined to lose consciousness

   **Example:** I felt faint after my fifth gin and tonic.

   **Example:** He almost fell faint due to the hot climate.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a being

   **Source synonyms:** dizzy

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Lacking courage, spirit, or energy; cowardly; dejected.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Barely perceptible; not bright, or loud, or sharp.

   **Example:** There was a faint red light in the distance.

   **Source synonyms:** dull; wan; bland; colourless; dim; dingy; dull; faint; lackluster; leaden; muddy; sad (figuratively); sober; uncolorful; unholiday; wan

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Performed, done, or acted, weakly; not exhibiting vigor, strength, or energy.

   **Example:** faint efforts

   **Example:** faint resistance

   **Example:** They damned the latest book with faint praise.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Slight; minimal.

   **Example:** a faint chance

   **Source synonyms:** remote

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Sickly, so as to make a person feel faint.

   **Usage:** archaic

## Verb · Etymology 2 · Record 3

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /feɪnt/ — source labels: General-American; Received-Pronunciation

### Forms

- faints — present; singular; third-person
- fainting — participle; present
- fainted — participle; past
- fainted — past

### Senses

<a id="r3-s1"></a>

**Source sense R3.S1**

1. To lose consciousness through a lack of oxygen or nutrients to the brain, usually as a result of suddenly reduced blood flow (may be caused by emotional trauma, loss of blood or various medical conditions).

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** A fainting fit.

<a id="r3-s2"></a>

**Source sense R3.S2**

2. To lose courage or spirit; to become depressed or despondent.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r3-s3"></a>

**Source sense R3.S3**

3. To decay; to disappear; to vanish.

   **Usage:** intransitive
