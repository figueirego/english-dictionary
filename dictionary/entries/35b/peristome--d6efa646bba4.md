### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One or two rings of tooth-like appendages surrounding the opening of the capsule of many mosses.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The parts of or surrounding the mouths of numerous invertebrates.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences; zoology
