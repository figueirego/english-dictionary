### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Several places in Gaza.
   1. A governorate of Southern Gaza, Gaza Strip, Palestine; in full, Rafah Governorate. Capital&#58; Rafah.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Several places in Gaza.
   1. A metropolitan region in Rafah governorate, Southern Gaza, Gaza Strip, Palestine, lying to the south of Khan Younus and to the north of Sinai, Egypt.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Several places in Gaza.
   1. A city, the capital of Rafah governorate, Southern Gaza, Gaza Strip, Palestine; lying next to the eponymous city in Egypt.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Several places in Gaza.
   1. A settlement, a refugee camp in the Rafah region, Rafah governorate, Southern Gaza, Gaza Strip, Palestine; in full, Rafah Refugee Camp, lying next to the eponymous city of Rafah.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A city in North Sinai governate, Sinai, Egypt, lying next to the eponymous city in Gaza.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Ellipsis of Rafah Crossing&#58; a border gate on the Egypt, — Gaza, Palestine, border in Rafah city.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; ellipsis

   **Alternative form of:** Rafah Crossing

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A city in Egypt and Gaza, Palestine, split by an international border, each half a city in their own right, as well.

   **Source synonyms:** Raphia
