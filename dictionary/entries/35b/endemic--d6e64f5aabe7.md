### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Native to a particular area or culture; originating where it occurs.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The endemic religion of Easter Island arrived with the Polynesian settlers.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Peculiar to a particular area or region; not found in other places.

   **Usage:** especially; not-comparable

   **Example:** Kangaroos are endemic to Australia.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of plants and animals

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Prevalent in a particular area or region, persistent within a population.

   **Usage:** especially; not-comparable

   **Example:** Malaria is endemic to the tropics.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of diseases
