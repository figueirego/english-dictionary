### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The science of the dynamics of bodies moving relative to gases, especially the interaction of moving objects with the atmosphere.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** He specializes in aerodynamics.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The aerodynamic properties of a particular object (typically a car or aircraft).

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** This type of airfoil doesn't have the right aerodynamics to achieve what they're trying to achieve.
