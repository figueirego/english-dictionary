### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A fabric, usually made of woven, knitted, or felted fibres or filaments, such as used in dressing, decorating, cleaning or other practical use. Sometimes, woven fabric specifically.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** fabric; material; textile; fabric; cloth; textile

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Specifically, a tablecloth, especially as spread before a meal or removed afterwards.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A piece of cloth used for a particular purpose.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Substance or essence; the whole of something complex.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Appearance; seeming.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A form of attire that represents a particular profession or status.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The priesthood.

   **Usage:** countable; idiomatic; uncountable; with-definite-article

   **Example:** He is a respected man of the cloth.
