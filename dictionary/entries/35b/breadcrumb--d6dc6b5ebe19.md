## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈbɹɛdˌkɹʌm/ — source labels: US

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A tiny piece of bread, either one that falls from bread as it is cut or eaten, or one made deliberately by crumbling bread.

   **Usage:** in-plural

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A single link in a chain indicating the hierarchical location of a directory, web page or similar, used as a navigation aid.

   **Usage:** in-plural

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; graphical-user-interface; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A fragment of data that serves to log the actions taken by a program, used in debugging.

   **Usage:** in-plural

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; programming; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. One in a series of clues leading to a person or place.

   **Usage:** figuratively; in-plural
