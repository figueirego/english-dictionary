### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any game played with a ball.

   **Example:** Basketball, cricket, and football are three kinds of ball game.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A specific contest or match between teams playing such a game, in particular a baseball game.

   **Example:** George and Harriet went to the stadium to see the ball game.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A sport played in the Aztec and Mayan civilizations; Mesoamerican ballgame.

   **Usage:** historical

   **Example:** The ball game was the main sport in Central America before the arrival of the Spanish.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Affair, issue, matter, subject, story, topic.

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A decisive outcome in regard to some ongoing situation; game over.

   **Usage:** figuratively; slang

   **Example:** If a U.S. presidential candidate receives 270 electoral votes, that's the ball game.
