### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To cause (a substance) to fall in fine drops (for a liquid substance) or small pieces (for a solid substance).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The confectioner sprinkled icing sugar over the cakes.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To cover (an object) by sprinkling a substance on to it.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The confectioner sprinkled the cakes with icing sugar.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To drip in fine drops, sometimes sporadically.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** It sprinkled outside all day long.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To rain very lightly outside.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** It sprinkled very early in the morning.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To baptize by the application of a few drops, or a small quantity, of water; hence, to cleanse; to purify.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To spread, as if by sprinkling.

   **Usage:** figuratively

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈspɹɪŋkəl/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

   **Example:** He decorated the Christmas card with a sprinkle of glitter.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

<a id="r2-s3"></a>
