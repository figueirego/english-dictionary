### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Maranta arundinacea from the Marantaceae family, a large perennial herb native to the Caribbean area with green leaves about 15 centimeters long.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Usually preceded by an attributive word&#58; some other plant whose rhizomes are used to prepare a substance similar to arrowroot (sense 3), such as Zamia integrifolia (Florida arrowroot) or Pueraria montana var. lobata (Japanese arrowroot or kudzu).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A starchy substance obtained from the rhizomes of an arrowroot plant used as a thickener.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Other plants with similar appearance or properties, such as common yarrow (Achillea millefolium)\|
