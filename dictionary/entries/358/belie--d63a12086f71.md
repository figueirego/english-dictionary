### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To tell lies about.

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** slander; calumniate

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To give a false representation of.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Her smooth complexion and dark hair belie her actual age.

   **Source synonyms:** misrepresent

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To contradict, to show (something) to be false.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Her obvious nervousness belied what she said.

   **Source synonyms:** contradict; give the lie to; put the lie to; give lie to; put lie to; put to lie

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To call a liar; to accuse of falsehood.

   **Usage:** rare; transitive

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To fill with lies; to lie to.

   **Usage:** rare; transitive

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To conceal the contradictory or ironic presence of (something).

   **Usage:** transitive; perhaps nonstandard

   **Example:** His calm demeanor belied his inner sense of guilt.

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

**Source sense R2.S7**

7. To show, evince or demonstrate (something) to be present, particularly something deemed contradictory or ironic.

   **Usage:** transitive; perhaps nonstandard
