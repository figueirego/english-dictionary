### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person of similar size and shape to an actor who substitutes for or replaces that actor during the lengthy process of setting up a shot, but who, unlike a double, does not appear in the film.

   **Example:** They used a stand-in to set up the lighting so that the actor did not have to be there during the lengthy process.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** broadcasting; film; media; television

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
