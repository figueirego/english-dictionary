## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A small addition to an already heavy burden which causes it to exceed the capacity.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; uncountable; almost always preceded by the

   **Example:** That's the last straw; it's a petty demand but I’m already under too much work. I quit!
