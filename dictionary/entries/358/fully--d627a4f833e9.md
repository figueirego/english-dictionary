### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In a full manner; without lack or defect; completely, entirely.

   **Example:** He is fully capable of meeting his responsibilities.

   **Source synonyms:** sufficiently; perfectly; completely; entirely

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used as an intensifier for a quantity.

   **Example:** it was fully four hours before we arrived home.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Exactly, equally.

   **Usage:** Followed by as

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. So as to be full (not hungry); to satiation.

   **Usage:** rare

   **Example:** to eat fully

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Used as a general intensifier; actually, really, literally.

   **Usage:** colloquial

   **Example:** I fully woke up at like 12 p.m. yesterday.
