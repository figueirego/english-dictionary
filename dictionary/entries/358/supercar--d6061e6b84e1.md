### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any high-performance sports car; especially, any superexpensive one of a socially exclusive marque designed to be among the fastest on Earth with no expense spared.

   **Example:** These billionaires' world is one of yachts, McLarens, and Lamborghinis — supercars out of reach of the 99%.
