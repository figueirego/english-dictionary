## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having two kinds of young, such as offspring from asexual reproduction versus those from sexual reproduction.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Certain annelids are ditokous.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences; zoology

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Producing only two offspring per reproductive cycle or gestation, such as two eggs in a clutch.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Some birds are ditokous.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences; zoology
