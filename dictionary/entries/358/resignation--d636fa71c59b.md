### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of resigning.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Jane tendered her resignation to the board of directors, but they refused.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A written or oral declaration that one resigns.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** hand in one's resignation

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An uncomplaining acceptance of something undesirable but unavoidable.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** With resignation I acknowledged that after the accident I would not be able to ski again.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The form by which a vassal returns the feu into the hands of a superior.

   **Usage:** countable; historical; uncountable; Scots law
