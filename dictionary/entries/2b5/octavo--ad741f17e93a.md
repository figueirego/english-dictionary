## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A sheet of paper 7 to 10 inches (= 17.78 to 25.4 cm) high and 4.5 to 6 inches (= 11.43 to 15.24 cm) wide, the size varying with the large original sheet used to create it. It is made by folding the original sheet three times to produce eight leaves.

   **Usage:** paper

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** media; printing; publishing

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
