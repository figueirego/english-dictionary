### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To continue or keep on.

   **Example:** This process has marched on no matter how we try to stop it.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To protest (a place or institution).

   **Example:** We're going to march on City Hall!

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To invade or attack (a place); to move towards a place in preparation for an attack.

   **Example:** At dawn, we march on the city.
