### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In an ironic manner; in a way displaying irony.

   **Example:** My colleague ironically avoids doctors like the plague!

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** manner

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used to draw attention to an ironic aspect of a situation being described.

   **Usage:** evaluative

   **Example:** Ironically, the automated checkout system ended up costing more in repairs than the workers it was meant to replace.
