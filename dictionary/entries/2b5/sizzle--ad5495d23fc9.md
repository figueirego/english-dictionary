### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make the sound of water boiling on a hot surface.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The sausages were sizzling on the barbecue.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To cook (something) in a manner which causes that sound.

   **Usage:** informal; transitive

   **Example:** I sizzled the bacon for a few minutes in the pan.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To be exciting or dazzling.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The song sizzled with energy.

### Senses

**Source sense R2.S1**

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** We heard the sizzle of the onions hitting the pan.
