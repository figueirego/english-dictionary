### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Pertaining to a pair (a, b) of set functions where a is supermodular, b is submodular, and they always satisfy the cross-inequality b(X) - a(Y) &gt; b(X-Y) -a(Y-X) for all X, Y.

   **Usage:** not-comparable
