### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To cover with tiles or stones, as a mosaic; to tile.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. to cover a two-dimensional shape, such that multiple copies of itself placed edge to edge cover an area leaving no space between the shapes.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Regular hexagons tessellate.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To completely fill (an area) when multiple copies of one or more two-dimensional shapes are placed edge to edge.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** It is possible to tessellate the plane with equilateral triangles and regular hexagons.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences
