### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To sell goods for a lower price than a competitor.

   **Example:** Our price-match guarantee&#58; we will not be undersold!

   **Source synonyms:** undercut

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To sell something for less than its value.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To put forward an idea, or to market a new product (or oneself), with insufficient enthusiasm or excessive understatement.

   **Example:** don't undersell it

   **Example:** don't undersell yourself

   **Source synonyms:** sell short

   **Source antonyms:** oversell
