   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A liability or contra account to recognise likely future adverse events associated with current transactions.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** We increased our provision for bad debts on credit sales going into the recession.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** accounting; business; finance

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** An arrest shall be made in accordance with the provisions of this Act.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

   **Source synonyms:** condition; disposition; stipulation

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Catholicism; Christianity; Roman-Catholicism
