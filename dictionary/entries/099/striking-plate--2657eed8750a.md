### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The plate against which the latch of a door lock strikes as the door is closed.

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Part of the centering of an arch, which is driven back to loosen the centering in striking it.
