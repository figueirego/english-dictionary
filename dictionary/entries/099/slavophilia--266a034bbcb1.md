## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A support or enthusiasm for Slavic culture and peoples.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source antonyms:** Slavophobia

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Source synonyms:** Slavophilism
