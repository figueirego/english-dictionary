### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The quality of being overly refined in an affected way (often used to describe speech or writing, but also visual art and dress).

   **Usage:** derogatory; uncountable; usually

   **Source synonyms:** affectation; fastidiousness

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An instance of preciosity; something that is overly refined in an affected way.

   **Usage:** countable; derogatory; usually

   **Source synonyms:** affectation

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The quality of being precious (of high value or worth).

   **Usage:** obsolete; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** preciousness; value; worth

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Something of high value or worth.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete

   **Source synonyms:** treasure
