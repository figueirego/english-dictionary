**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Usage:** Ireland; UK; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** Australia; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

3. The ball used in a game of footy.

   **Usage:** Australia; countable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

   **Usage:** New-Zealand; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

   **Usage:** countable; plural-normally; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

6. A football fan.

   **Usage:** countable; slang

   **Example:** Sam is a real footy, he's always watching it on TV and talking about it.
