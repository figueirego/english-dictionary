### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The method or power of gaining control over someone's personality or actions.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** hypnosis; hypnotism; power of suggestion

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The method or power of gaining control over someone's personality or actions.
   1. The theory and practice of Mesmer himself or his disciples, which involved hypnosis and a superstructure of theory that included animal magnetism.

      **Usage:** broadly; countable; uncountable; strictly

      **Source synonyms:** hypnosis; hypnotism; power of suggestion

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The state induced by hypnotic methods (especially those of Mesmer himself).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** hypnosis; trance
