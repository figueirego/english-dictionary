### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To pass above something, as when flying or moving on a higher road.

   **Usage:** Canada; Philippines; US

   **Example:** Gillian watched the overpassing shoppers on the second floor of the mall, as she relaxed in the bench on the ground floor.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To exceed, overstep, or transcend a limit, threshold, or goal.

   **Usage:** Canada; Philippines; US; transitive

   **Example:** Marshall was really overpassing his authority when he ordered the security guards to fire their tasers at the trespassers.

   **Example:** The precocious student had really overpassed her peers, and was reading books written for children several years older.
