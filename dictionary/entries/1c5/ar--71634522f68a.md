## Suffix · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of, near, or pertaining to; adjective suffix appended to various words, often nouns, to make an adjective form. Often added to words of Latin origin, but used with other words also.

   **Usage:** morpheme

   **Example:** alveolus + -ar → alveolar

   **Example:** lobe + -ar → lobar

   **Source synonyms:** -al; -an; -ary; -ese; -ic; -id; -ish; -like; -oid; -ory; -ous; -y
