### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A concise summary of a current situation

   **Example:** The president received a briefing on the situation before going to the press conference.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A meeting to share a concise summary of a current situation, especially in close preparation for an operation or undertaking.

   **Example:** Today's briefing of the press will take place at 10 o'clock.
