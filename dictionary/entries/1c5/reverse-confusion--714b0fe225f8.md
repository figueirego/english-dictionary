## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A situation where a competitor is responsible for a younger use of a mark and by exploiting his market dominance imparts his goodwill to the mistaking buyers of the senior user and erodes the latter’s goodwill and control of it.

   **Usage:** no-plural; uncountable; usually; trademark law

   **Source antonyms:** forward confusion
