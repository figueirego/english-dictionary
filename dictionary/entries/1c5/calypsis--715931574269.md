### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of covering, concealing, hiding, or veiling.
   1. The act of covering or concealing one’s face, or sexually attractive parts, especially in the presence of someone who is a sexual turn-off (extremely anerotic), or someone who is uncomfortable to be around.

      **Usage:** kinesics

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The act of covering, concealing, hiding, or veiling.
   1. The veiling of a bride during a wedding.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The act of covering, concealing, hiding, or veiling.
   1. The concealing of one's face while committing a robbery or other criminal act.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A type of closed or negative body language often expressing extremely anerotic feelings, sexual abhorrence, scorn and contempt toward someone viewed as a sexual turn-off in terms of physical appearance, personality, or social behavior.
