### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To delay movement or action until some event or time; to remain neglected or in readiness.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Wait here until your car arrives.

   **Example:** I’m still waiting for you to pay me back the money I lent you..

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To wait tables; to serve customers in a restaurant or other eating establishment.

   **Usage:** US; intransitive; stative

   **Example:** She used to wait in this joint.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To delay movement or action until the arrival or occurrence of; to await. (Now generally superseded by “wait for”.)

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive

   **Example:** to wait one’s turn

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To attend on; to accompany; especially, to attend with ceremony or respect.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To attend as a consequence; to follow upon; to accompany.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To defer or postpone (especially a meal).

   **Usage:** colloquial; obsolete

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To watch with malicious intent; to lie in wait

   **Usage:** obsolete; except in phrases

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To remain faithful to one’s partner or betrothed during a prolonged period of absence.

   **Usage:** intransitive
