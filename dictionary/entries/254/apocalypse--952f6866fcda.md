### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The written account of a revelation of hidden things given by God to a chosen prophet.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** Apocalypses of Adam and Abraham (Epiphanius) and of Elias (Jerome) are also mentioned.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biblical; lifestyle; religion

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Revelation (last book of the Bible, composed of twenty-two chapters, which narrates a vision of the end times).

   **Example:** He's been reading the Apocalypse again, and doomscrolling social media content that fixates on it.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Armageddon&#58; the destructive end of the world.

   **Example:** They keep predicting that the Apocalypse is nigh, but I notice that they have books and supplies that they're trying to sell.
