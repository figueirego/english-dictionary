### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To dull the senses or capacity to think thereby reducing responsiveness; to stun.

   **Usage:** especially; transitive

   **Example:** a stupefying drug; a stupefacient

   **Example:** He stupefied her by means of chloroform, a general anaesthetic.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To astonish or stun, especially as a result of some distressing action.

   **Usage:** broadly; figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** The police's negligence and callousness continued to stupefy her.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To deprive a material of the ability to undergo change or movement, especially deformation.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive
