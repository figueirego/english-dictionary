### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To adulterate (a drug) for the purpose of increasing its bulk, and therefore the profit when sold; to dilute, cut.

   **Usage:** transitive; recreational drugs

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To oppress or mistreat (a person).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** tread

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To interrupt, overpower, or override (another transmission on the same frequency) with one's own transmission.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** broadcasting; media; radio; radio-communications

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To approach as to confront or fight.

   **Usage:** dated; intransitive

   **Example:** He stepped on to the vicious man.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To intrude on someone else's turf or portfolio.

   **Usage:** slang; transitive

   **Example:** They're stepping on our operations.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see step, on.

   **Example:** He stepped on a rock.
