### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The day immediately before today; one day ago.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Today is the child of yesterday and the parent of tomorrow.

   **Example:** Yesterday was rainy, but by this morning it had begun to snow.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The past, often in terms of being outdated.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

   **Example:** yesterday's technology

   **Example:** The worker of today is different from that of yesterday.

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. On the day before today.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** I started to watch the video yesterday, but could only finish it this evening.

   **Source synonyms:** the last day (Ireland)

   **Source antonyms:** tomorrow

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. As soon as possible.

   **Usage:** informal; not-comparable

   **Example:** I want this done yesterday!
