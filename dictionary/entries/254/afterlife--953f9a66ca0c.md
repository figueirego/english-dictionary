## Noun

- afterlifes — plural; proscribed; rare

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A conscious existence after death; a supernatural life that follows one's natural life, in some worldviews.

   **Example:** Many religious people believe in an afterlife.

   **Source synonyms:** life after death; hereafter; eternal life

   **Source antonyms:** forelife; beforelife

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The place believed to be inhabited by people who have died.

   **Example:** You'll reunite later, somewhere in the afterlife.

   **Source synonyms:** afterworld

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The part of a person's life that follows a particular stage or event; later life.

   **Usage:** countable; informal; uncountable

   **Example:** They say that life begins at 40, but right now I'm more interested in the afterlife that begins at 65!

   **Source synonyms:** aftercareer

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The effects of a person's actions, or their reputation, after death.

   **Example:** The philanthropic endowment that she bequeathed gave her an ongoing afterlife.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The events or situations that result from a particular event; the later reception, consumption or reworking of something, especially a cultural production such as a film, book, etc.

   **Example:** The 1970s TV show M\*A\*S\*H had a long afterlife in syndication.

   **Source synonyms:** aftercareer; aftermath

   **Source antonyms:** history
