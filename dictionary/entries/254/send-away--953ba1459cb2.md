### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To dismiss from one's presence.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** The salesman was taking up too much time with his nonsense, so I sent him away.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To send (someone) to a particular place for a long time, as a family member, an employee, etc.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** We are going to send our son away to live with his uncle in America for a year.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To send (someone) to a particular place for a long time, as a family member, an employee, etc.
   1. To imprison (someone).

      **Usage:** euphemistic; idiomatic; transitive

      **Example:** the judge sent him away

      **Example:** They sent him away for that one.

      **Source synonyms:** lock up; shut away

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To dispatch orders to a place for something to be delivered.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** We sent away for some of the items in the gardening catalogue.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see send, away.
