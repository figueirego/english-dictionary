### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To survive in a storm (about a ship) and keep afloat.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To tackle a difficult situation and survive; to survive through; to weather.

   **Usage:** broadly; idiomatic; transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To go out in a vehicle with one's friends or crew.

   **Usage:** intransitive; slang; African-American Vernacular

   **Example:** Let's ride out and see what we can get into.
