## Verb · Record 1

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To run (towards someone or something); to hasten to a destination.

   **Example:** As I was walking along the road, a man suddenly ran up to me.

   **Example:** The dog ran up under the table to get his food.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To approach (an event or point in time).

   **Example:** We are putting on lots of special attractions as we run up to Christmas.

   **Source context (verbatim):** with to

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To take to a destination or before an authority.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To erect hastily, as a building.

   **Usage:** dated

   **Source synonyms:** throw up; knock up; knock together; slap together

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To make something, usually an item of clothing, very quickly.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** I'll run you up a skirt for tomorrow evening.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To bring (a flag) to the top of its flag pole.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** Stand quietly while the honor guard runs the flag up.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To string up; to hang.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Of a bowler, to run, or walk up to the bowling crease in order to bowl a ball.

   **Example:** He runs up... and bowls. Smashed away for four runs!

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; cricket; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To rise; to swell; to grow; to increase.

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** Accounts of goods credited run up very fast.

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. To accumulate (a debt).

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** He ran up over $5,000 in unpaid bills.

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. To thrust up, as anything long and slender.

   **Example:** The fence runs up along the edge of the pasture.

<a id="r1-s12"></a>

**Source sense R1.S12**

12. To warm up and test an airplane before a flight.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** aeronautics; aerospace; aviation; business; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s13"></a>

**Source sense R1.S13**

13. To accumulate money, drugs, etc.

   **Usage:** reflexive; slang; sometimes; African-American Vernacular

   **Example:** I run me up some big bills.

<a id="r1-s14"></a>

**Source sense R1.S14**

14. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see run, up.

   **Example:** The small boy ran up the hill.
