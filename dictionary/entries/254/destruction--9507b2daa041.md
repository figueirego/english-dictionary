### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of destroying.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The destruction of the condemned building will take place at noon.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The results of a destructive event.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Amid the seemingly endless destruction, a single flower bloomed.
