### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Inclined or having a propensity to love, or to sexual enjoyment.

   **Example:** an amorous disposition

   **Example:** We were both feeling amorous so the inevitable happened.

   **Source synonyms:** loving; fond; affectionate

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Indicating love or sexual desire.

   **Example:** She kept making these amorous suggestions.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of or relating to, or produced by, love.

   **Example:** She read me an amorous poem.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Affected with love; in love; enamored.

   **Usage:** dated

   **Example:** He had been amorous of her since schooldays.
