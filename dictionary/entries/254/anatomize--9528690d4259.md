### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To cut up or dissect (the body of a human being or an animal), specifically for the purpose of investigating its anatomy.

   **Usage:** British; English; Oxford; US; archaic; transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To cut up or dissect (the body of a human being or an animal), specifically for the purpose of investigating its anatomy.
   1. To punish (someone) by post mortem dissection following execution.

      **Usage:** British; English; Oxford; US; archaic; transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To cut up or dissect (a plant or one of its parts) to investigate its structure.

   **Usage:** British; English; Oxford; US; archaic; transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To scrutinize (something) down to the most minute detail.

   **Usage:** British; English; Oxford; US; archaic; figuratively; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** atomize; analyze

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To chemically analyse (a substance).

   **Usage:** British; English; Oxford; US; archaic; figuratively; obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To cut up or dissect the body of a human being or an animal.

   **Usage:** British; English; Oxford; US; archaic; intransitive
