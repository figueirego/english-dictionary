### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To hit (someone or something) all over repeatedly; hence, to behave violently towards or mistreat (someone or something).

   **Usage:** informal; transitive

   **Example:** It was known that he would knock his wife about when he had been drinking.

   **Source synonyms:** knock around; knock round

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To knock back a drink; to finish a drink.

   **Usage:** informal; transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To move or roam around aimlessly; to be in circulation.

   **Usage:** informal; intransitive

   **Source synonyms:** knock around; knock round

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To move or roam around aimlessly; to be in circulation.
   1. To live an unconventional life.

      **Usage:** broadly; informal; intransitive

      **Source synonyms:** knock around; knock round

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To be present at or inhabit a certain place.

   **Usage:** informal; intransitive

   **Source synonyms:** hang around

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To be present at or inhabit a certain place.
   1. To engage in a relaxing activity in a place; to hang around in.

      **Usage:** broadly; informal; intransitive

      **Example:** I like to knock about the garden on Saturdays.

      **Source synonyms:** hang around; potter about; potter around

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To be present at or inhabit a certain place.
   1. Often followed by with&#58; to spend time companionably; to hang around.

      **Usage:** broadly; informal; intransitive

      **Example:** I used to knock about with John when we were younger.

      **Example:** John and I used to knock about when we were younger.

      **Source synonyms:** hang around; hang; hang about; knock around; knock round

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To be present at or inhabit a certain place.
   1. To be mislaid in a place.

      **Usage:** broadly; informal; intransitive; usually in present participial form

      **Example:** I’ve got some scissors knocking about in the kitchen.

      **Source synonyms:** hang around; knock around; knock round; lie around
