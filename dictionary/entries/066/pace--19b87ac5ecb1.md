### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A step.
   1. A step taken with the foot.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A step.
   1. The distance covered in a step (or sometimes two), either vaguely or according to various specific set measurements.

      **Example:** Even at the duel, standing 10 paces apart, he could have satisfied Aaron’s honor.

      **Example:** I have perambulated your field, and estimate its perimeter to be 219 paces.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A way of stepping.
   1. A manner of walking, running or dancing; the rate or style of how someone moves with their feet.

      **Example:** We set off at a round pace.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A way of stepping.
   1. Any of various gaits of a horse, specifically a 2-beat, lateral gait.

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Speed or velocity in general.

   **Source synonyms:** tempo; rhythm

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A measure of the hardness of a pitch and of the tendency of a cricket ball to maintain its speed after bouncing.

   **Example:** He didn't bowl a lot of pace in the first T20I.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; cricket; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports
