### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A physical tilt or bend.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The inclination of his head increased and he awoke with a start.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A slant or slope.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The road up to the house had a steep inclination.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A tendency.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** His inclination to drink escalated to alcoholism.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The angle of intersection of a reference plane.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The astronomer calculated the inclination of the equator or ecliptic of Earth and the orbital planes of each visible heavenly body.

   **Example:** Artillery must take account of a weapon's precise inclination.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A person or thing loved or admired.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable
