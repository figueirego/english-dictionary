### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An animal from the subtribe Bubalina, also known as true buffalos, such as the Cape buffalo, Syncerus caffer, or the water buffalo, Bubalus bubalis.

   **Source synonyms:** buffle

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A related North American animal, the American bison, Bison bison.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Ellipsis of buffalo robe.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; ellipsis

   **Alternative form of:** [buffalo robe](../3aa/buffalo-robe--eaa93e9c1691.md)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The buffalo fish (Ictiobus spp.).

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A nickel.

   **Usage:** US; slang

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A looter during the Civil War.

   **Usage:** US; historical; slang

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A large or stupid person.

   **Usage:** derogatory; slang

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. An obese person, usually a woman.

   **Usage:** derogatory; slang

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A black male.

   **Usage:** derogatory; offensive; slang

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. Ellipsis of American buffalo (“gold bullion coin”).

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; ellipsis

   **Alternative form of:** [American buffalo](../195/american-buffalo--6552ef49fe77.md)
