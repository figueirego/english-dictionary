### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To stand or meet facing, especially in competition, hostility or defiance; to come face to face with.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** It is important that police officers learn to deescalate situations in which someone confronts them aggressively.

   **Source synonyms:** oppose; challenge

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To deal with.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** confront a problem

   **Source synonyms:** tackle

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To bring someone face to face with something.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** We should confront him about the missing money.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To come up against; to encounter.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Inter Milan are to confront Juventus in the final.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To engage in confrontation.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To set a thing side by side with; to compare.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To put a thing facing to; to set in contrast to.

   **Usage:** transitive
