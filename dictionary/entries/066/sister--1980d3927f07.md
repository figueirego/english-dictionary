### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A daughter of the same parents as another person; a female sibling.

   **Example:** My sister is always driving me crazy.

   **Source synonyms:** sis

   **Source antonyms:** brother

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A female member of a religious order; especially one devoted to more active service; (informal) a nun.

   **Example:** Michelle left behind her bank job and became a sister at the local convent.

   **Source synonyms:** nun

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any butterfly in the genus Adelpha, so named for the resemblance of the dark-colored wings to the black habit traditionally worn by nuns.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A senior or supervisory nurse, often in a hospital.

   **Usage:** UK

   **Source synonyms:** charge nurse

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Any woman or girl with whom a bond is felt through the same biological sex, gender or common membership in a community, race, profession, religion, organization, or ism.

   **Example:** Connie was very close to her friend Judy and considered her to be her sister.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A black woman.

   **Usage:** capitalized; slang; sometimes; African-American Vernacular

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A form of address to a woman.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** What’s up, sister?

   **Source synonyms:** darling; dear; love; lady (US); miss; pet (Northern; UK)

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A female fellow member of a religious community, church, trades union etc.

   **Example:** Thank you, sister. I would like to thank the sister who just spoke.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. An entity that has a special or affectionate, non-hierarchical relationship with another.

   **Usage:** attributive

   **Example:** sister publication

   **Example:** sister city

   **Example:** sister projects

   **Source synonyms:** affiliate; affiliated

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. A node in a data structure that shares its parent with another node.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; computing-theory; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. Something in the same class.

   **Usage:** attributive; usually

   **Example:** sister ships

   **Example:** sister facility
