## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈtɛmp(ə)ɹ(ə)n(t)s/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Forms

- temperances — plural
- temperaunce — alternative; obsolete

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Moderation or self-control in any action; control of the desire to act emotionally; calmness; (countable) an instance of this.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source synonyms:** patience; sedateness

   **Source antonyms:** intemperance

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Moderation or self-control in any action; control of the desire to act emotionally; calmness; (countable) an instance of this.
   1. Restraint or self-control when made angry or frustrated; forbearance.

      **Usage:** dated; uncountable; usually

      **Source synonyms:** patience; sedateness

      **Source antonyms:** intemperance

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. moderation in regard to the indulgence of natural appetites and passions; moderate or restrained indulgence.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually; Habitual

   **Example:** temperance in eating and drinking

   **Example:** temperance in the indulgence of joy

   **Source antonyms:** intemperance; gluttony; overindulgence

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. moderation in regard to the indulgence of natural appetites and passions; moderate or restrained indulgence.
   1. Moderation in the use of intoxicating substances; sobriety; specifically, complete abstinence from alcoholic beverages; teetotalism.

      **Usage:** uncountable; usually; Habitual

      **Source antonyms:** intemperance; gluttony; overindulgence

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. moderation in regard to the indulgence of natural appetites and passions; moderate or restrained indulgence.
   1. Synonym of chastity (“abstinence from any sexual activity considered immoral”).

      **Usage:** dated; uncountable; usually; Habitual

      **Source synonyms:** chastity (synonym; synonym-of)

      **Source antonyms:** intemperance; gluttony; overindulgence

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The action of bringing to a temperate or moderate state.

   **Usage:** obsolete; uncountable; usually

   **Source antonyms:** intemperance

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Proper moderation between extreme or opposing qualities; (countable) an instance of this.

   **Usage:** obsolete; uncountable; usually

   **Source antonyms:** intemperance

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Temperature which is neither too cold nor too hot.

   **Usage:** obsolete; uncountable; usually

   **Source antonyms:** intemperance
