### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The belief that phenomenalism and physicalism are alternative "world-versions", both useful in some circumstances, but neither capable of fully capturing the other.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; philosophy; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>
