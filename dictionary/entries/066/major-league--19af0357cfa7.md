## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

- major leagues — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An association of sports teams which plays at the highest skill level of its sport, especially Major League Baseball.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The highest level of competition in a field of endeavor.

   **Usage:** attributive; plural-normally

   **Example:** You can't be so casual. You're in the major leagues now.

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Of or pertaining to a major sports league; belonging to the professional level of a sport, such as US baseball.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Analogous in importance to a major league sports team.

   **Usage:** broadly
