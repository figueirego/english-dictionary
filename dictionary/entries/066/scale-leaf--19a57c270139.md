### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A small flat leaf resembling a scale, such as on many conifers.

   **Example:** An individual flower that is subtended by a scale leaf at its junction with the stem of a plant; a rudimentary leaf.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A small modified leaf, especially a colorless membranous one, such as on a rhizome or forming part of a bulb.
