### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A type of fastener made of plastic, comprising a screw and a captive nut, made for one-sided access in fastening body panels, fairings, splash shields, and so on; an instance thereof.

   **Example:** You can expect to wreck the existing scrivets during disassembly and to need new ones for installation during reassembly.
