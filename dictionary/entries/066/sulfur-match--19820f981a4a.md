### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A kind of taper or miniature torch produced by splintering tar barrels, immersing the slips into sulfur, and applying them to a flame.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A match activated by a sulfuric reaction, formerly by immersion of various chemicals in sulfuric acid and now more usually by friction and some amount of phosphorus.

   **Usage:** historical
