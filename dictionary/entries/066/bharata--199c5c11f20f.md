## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A legendary emperor of India, and is referred to in Hindu and Jain theology. He was son of King Dushyanta and Shakuntala in Mahabharata.

   **Usage:** Hinduism

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Younger brother of Rāma in Ramayana.

   **Usage:** Hinduism

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A muni of ancient India who authored Natya Shastra.

   **Usage:** Hinduism

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A male given name from Sanskrit used in India.
