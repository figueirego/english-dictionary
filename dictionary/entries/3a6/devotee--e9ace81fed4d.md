### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An ardent enthusiast or admirer.

   **Example:** He was a devotee of Arnold Schwarzenegger.

   **Example:** a devotee of classical music

   **Source synonyms:** addict; buff; aficionado; amateur; enthusiast; devotee; fancier; fan; fiend; freak; junkie; maniac; votary; zealot

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A believer in a particular religion or god.

   **Example:** devotees of Krishna

   **Example:** devotees thronged the temple

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** lifestyle; religion

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A person (almost exclusively a man) with a sexual attraction for people with physical disabilities, especially amputees.

   **Usage:** slang
