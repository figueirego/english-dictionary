### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A letter from a wife or girlfriend to her husband or boyfriend, informing that the relationship is over, often because the writer has initiated a relationship with someone else.

   **Example:** Thirty years later in Vietnam, he was still carrying the Dear John letter he had received from his high-school sweetheart.

   **Source synonyms:** Dear John
