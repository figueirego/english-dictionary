### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who grooves, or enjoys rhythmic music.

   **Usage:** colloquial

2. A groovy piece of music.

   **Usage:** colloquial

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

   **Usage:** Derbyshire; UK; archaic; dialectal
