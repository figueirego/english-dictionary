**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act or process of infantilizing, treating a non-infant as an infant, or more generally treating an adult like a young child.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Nursing homes lead to an infantilization of the elderly.

   **Source antonyms:** adultification
