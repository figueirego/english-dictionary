### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A nightjar, especially Caprimulgus europaeus (Eurasian nightjar).

   **Usage:** UK; regional

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A New World nightjar of the genus Chordeiles, especially Chordeiles minor.

   **Usage:** US

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A person whose preference or custom is to remain awake and active during the night and the early morning hours.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A nighthawker (metal detectorist who works illegally at night).

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An unlicensed prostitute who worked the roadsides at night.

   **Usage:** Japan; historical
