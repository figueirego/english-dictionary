### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To procrastinate, put off; to dawdle, avoid, or make progress slowly and reluctantly.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I have been dragging my feet about filing my taxes.

   **Source synonyms:** drag one's heels

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To intentionally stall, to delay, to obstruct.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Source synonyms:** slow-walk; dilly-dally
