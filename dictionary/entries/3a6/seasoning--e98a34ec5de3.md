### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Something used to add taste or flavour to food, such as salt and pepper or other condiment, herb or spice.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** cooking; food; lifestyle

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Anything added to increase enjoyment.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; figuratively; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A coat of polymerized oil inside a cooking vessel which renders the surface non-stick.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An alcoholic intoxication.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Some of our gentlemen officers, happening to stop at a tavern, or rather a sort of grogshop, took such a seasoning that two or three of them became “quite frisky.”

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
