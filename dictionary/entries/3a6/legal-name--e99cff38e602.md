## Noun

### Forms

- legal names — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The name of a natural person recognized on official records, especially as recorded on a birth certificate or as changed by a legal process.

   **Example:** He signs his name "Johnny Reb Smith", but his legal name is "Jean-Marie Smith".

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

   **Source synonyms:** government name; [wallet name](../36c/wallet-name--db012daed92d.md)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The name of an organization as indicated in its founding documents, or as subsequently changed by a legal process.

   **Example:** The legal name of "Jack's Mattresses" is "JM Enterprises \{2006\} (Illinois) Inc.".

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; law
