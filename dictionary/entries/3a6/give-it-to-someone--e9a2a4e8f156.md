### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To beat up, shoot, rob, murder, hit or provoke someone.

   **Usage:** colloquial

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To put up a good fight against someone.

   **Usage:** colloquial

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To punish, criticize or verbally abuse someone.

   **Usage:** colloquial

   **Source synonyms:** chastise; give what for; amerce; castigate (formal); chasten (archaic); chastise; come down on; correct; fix someone's wagon (idiomatic); have someone's guts for garters (Commonwealth; idiomatic); have someone's head (figuratively; idiomatic); have someone's hide (figuratively; idiomatic); give it to someone; give someone what for (idiomatic; slang); give what for (idiomatic); gruel; malavogue (Ireland; dated); nail someone to the wall; penalize; penance; punish; tan someone's hide (figuratively; idiomatic); tar out (dated; slang); teach someone a lesson (idiomatic)

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To have sexual relations with someone; to allow someone to have sex with oneself.

   **Usage:** colloquial

<a id="r1-s5"></a>
