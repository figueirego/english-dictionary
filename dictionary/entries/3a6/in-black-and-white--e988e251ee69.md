## Prep\_phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Explicitly, in writing, clearly and without doubt or misunderstanding, without any grey areas.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I'll believe that when I see it in black and white.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Using shades of gray rather than color.

   **Usage:** literally

   **Example:** This film was shot in black and white.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Having it displayed using shades of gray rather than color (as it was shot or recorded).
