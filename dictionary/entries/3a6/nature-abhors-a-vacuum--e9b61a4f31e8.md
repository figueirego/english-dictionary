## Phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Generating and maintaining a near-vacuum is challenging (requiring technical rigor) because ambient pressure tends to push matter forcefully into the evacuated chamber; and generating a true vacuum (devoid of any matter at all) is extremely challenging.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** The development of practical electric light bulbs was challenging not least because nature abhors a vacuum&#58; removing all air from the bulbs was not as easy as it at first seemed.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences; technology

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Generating a vacuum is impossible (a vacuum can never exist) owing to the inherent nature of the universe.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** The ancients believed that nature abhors a vacuum.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A power vacuum will usually be filled quickly.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; politics
