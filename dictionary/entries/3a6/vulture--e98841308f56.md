### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of several carrion-eating birds of the families Accipitridae and Cathartidae.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person who profits from the suffering of others.

   **Usage:** colloquial; figuratively

   **Example:** Within ten minutes of the accident, the vultures appeared and were organizing lawsuits.
