### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of various plants with red roots or leaves&#58;
   1. Species Sanguinaria canadensis (bloodroot).

      **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of various plants with red roots or leaves&#58;
   1. Species Rumex sanguineus (bloody dock, wood dock, bloody-veined dock).

      **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of various plants with red roots or leaves&#58;
   1. Family Haemodoraceae, flowering plants some of whose roots contain a red dye.

      **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any of various plants used or formerly used to staunch bleeding&#58;
   1. Sanguinary, common yarrow, Achillea millefolium.

      **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Any of various plants used or formerly used to staunch bleeding&#58;
   1. Various species in the genus Sanguisorba, burnet.

      **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Any of various plants used or formerly used to staunch bleeding&#58;
   1. Capsella bursa-pastoris, shepherd's purse.

      **Usage:** uncountable; usually
