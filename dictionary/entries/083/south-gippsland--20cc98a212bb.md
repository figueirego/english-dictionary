## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A local government area in the prefecture of Gippsland, the easternmost region of the state of Victoria, Australia; in full, South Gippsland Shire.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The southern region of Gippsland (a prefecture-level division of the state of Victoria, Australia); including the eponymous South Gippsland Shire, and the Latrobe Valley.
