### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To disturb or excite; to perturb or stir up (a person).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He was greatly agitated by the news.

   **Source synonyms:** disturb; unsettle; agitate; freak; sting; bother; bring down; discompose; unstring; disconcert; enturbulate (nonstandard); disturb; mortify (obsolete); perturb; put out; ruffle; throw off balance; unsettle; upset; flurry

   **Source antonyms:** appease; calm; quieten

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To cause to move with a violent, irregular action; to shake.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to agitate water in a vessel

   **Example:** The wind agitates the sea.

   **Source synonyms:** move; muddle; roil; shake

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To participate in political agitation (sense 3).

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To set in motion; to actuate.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** activate; animate; excite; trigger; rouse; set off

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To discuss or debate.

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** discuss; debate; canvass

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To mull over, or think deeply about; to consider, to devise.

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive

   **Example:** Politicians agitate desperate designs.

   **Source synonyms:** revolve; ruminate; afterthink; agitate (rare); bethink; call to mind; chew; chew over; chew the cud; cogitate; consider; contemplate; deliberate; introspect; mull; mull over; muse; overthink (archaic); ponder; meditate; quander; reflect; rethink; reconsider; revolve (rare); ruminate; second-guess; think; think over
