### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Tending to contribute to, encourage, or bring about some result.

   **Example:** A small, dark kitchen is not conducive to elaborate cooking.

   **Source synonyms:** instrumental; helpful; favorable; conducent
