### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To exchange favours, especially political favours.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To combine legislative items, either or both of which might fail on its own, into a single bill that is more likely to pass.

   **Usage:** US; figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** Republicans defeated Democratic efforts to logroll hate crimes legislation into the defense appropriations bill.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; politics

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To roll a log in a body of water, while balancing on it; to birl.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To move like rolling logs.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To safely move (a body) in an emergency (medical) situation, tilting them up, then laying them on a transport surface.

   **Usage:** transitive
