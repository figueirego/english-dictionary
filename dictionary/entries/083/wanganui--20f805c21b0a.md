### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A city and territorial authority in Manawatū-Whanganui region, on the west coast of the North Island of New Zealand.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A major river in the Manawatū-Whanganui region, North Island, New Zealand, which flows into the Tasman Sea at Wanganui. Official name&#58; Whanganui River.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

<a id="r1-s5"></a>
