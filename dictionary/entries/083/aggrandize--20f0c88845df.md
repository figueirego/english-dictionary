### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make great; to enlarge; to increase.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to aggrandize one's authority, distress

   **Source synonyms:** [embiggen](../0a4/embiggen--292362c48692.md); greaten; grow

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To make great or greater in power, rank, honor, or wealth (applied to persons, countries, etc.).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** elevate; empower; ennoble

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To make appear great or greater; to exalt.

   **Usage:** formal; transitive

   **Example:** aggrandize one's accomplishments and downplay others'

   **Source synonyms:** extol; laud; praise; aggrandize; applaud; enthrone (literary); appraise; belaud (rare); bepraise; commend; cry up; eulogize; exalt; extol; glorify; honor; idolize; laud; lionize; magnify; panegyrize; praise; puff; revere; speak well of; transfigure; venerate; worthy

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To increase or become great.

   **Usage:** intransitive; rare

   **Source synonyms:** enlarge; expand; swell
