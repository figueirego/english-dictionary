### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make widely known to the public.

   **Example:** The scandal was so publicised that he lost the next election.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To advertise, create publicity for.

   **Example:** They're already publicising next month's concert.
