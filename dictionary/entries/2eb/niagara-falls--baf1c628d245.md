### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A waterfall in Niagara river, Niagara region, on the international border between Canada and United States, in both Ontario and New York state; Massive waterfalls located on the Niagara River.

   **Source synonyms:** the Falls

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A city in Niagara county, New York, United States; near the Falls.

   **Source synonyms:** City of Niagara Falls

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A city in Niagara regional municipality, Ontario, Canada; near the Falls.

   **Source synonyms:** City of Niagara Falls
