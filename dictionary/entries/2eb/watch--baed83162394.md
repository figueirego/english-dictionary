### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A portable or wearable timepiece.

   **Example:** More people today carry a watch on their wrists than in their pockets; some people today don't use a watch at all, because their smartphone serves the purpose well enough.

   **Example:** He set the alarm on his watch to 8&#58;00 am.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The act of guarding and observing someone or something.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A particular time period when guarding is kept.

   **Example:** The second watch of the night began at midnight.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A period of wakefulness between the two sleeps of a biphasic sleep pattern (the dead sleep or first sleep and morning sleep or second sleep)&#58; the first waking.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A person or group of people who guard.

   **Example:** The watch stopped the travelers at the city gates.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The post or office of a watchman; also, the place where a watchman is posted, or where a guard is kept.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A group of sailors and officers aboard a ship or shore station with a common period of duty&#58; starboard watch, port watch.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A period of time on duty, usually four hours in length; the officers and crew who tend the working of a vessel during the same watch. (FM 55–501).

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. The act of seeing, or viewing, for a period of time.

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. A group of nightingales.

   **Usage:** collective; rare

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To look at, see, or view for a period of time.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** Watching the clock will not make time go faster.

   **Example:** I'm tired of watching TV.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To observe over a period of time; to notice or pay attention.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Watch this!

   **Example:** Put a little baking soda in some vinegar and watch what happens.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To mind, attend, or guard.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Please watch my suitcase for a minute.

   **Example:** He has to watch the kids that afternoon.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To be wary or cautious of.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** You should watch that guy. He has a reputation for lying.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To attend to dangers to or regarding.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** watch your head; watch your step

   **Example:** Watch yourself when you talk to him.

   **Example:** Watch what you say.

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To remain awake with a sick or dying person; to maintain a vigil.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

**Source sense R2.S7**

7. To be vigilant or on one's guard.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** For some must watch, while some must sleep&#58; So runs the world away.

<a id="r2-s8"></a>

**Source sense R2.S8**

8. To act as a lookout.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s9"></a>

**Source sense R2.S9**

9. To serve the purpose of a watchman by floating properly in its place.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a buoy

<a id="r2-s10"></a>

**Source sense R2.S10**

10. To be awake.

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete

<a id="r2-s11"></a>

**Source sense R2.S11**

11. To be on the lookout for; to wait for expectantly.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive
