### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To prepare a summary of (something).

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** Jim was asked to summarize the document by Wednesday.

   **Source synonyms:** brief (especially); **Source context (verbatim):** in writing

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To give a recapitulation of the salient facts; to recapitulate or review.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** After the meeting, Jim summarized the major decisions made.

   **Source synonyms:** sum up
