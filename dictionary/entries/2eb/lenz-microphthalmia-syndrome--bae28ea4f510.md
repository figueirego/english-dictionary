## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A very rare inherited disorder characterized by abnormal smallness of one or both eyes, sometimes with droopy eyelids, resulting in visual impairment or blindness.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** LMS; Lenz syndrome; Lenz dysplasia; Lenz dysmorphogenetic syndrome
