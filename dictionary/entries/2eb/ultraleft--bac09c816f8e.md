## Noun · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˌʌl.tɹəˈlɛft/ — source labels: General-American; Received-Pronunciation

### Forms

- ultralefts — plural
- ultra-left — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. The strain or strains of leftism often associated with left communism, which practice rejection of both social democracy and state socialism as well as opposition to Leninism; i.e, ultraleftism.

   **Usage:** collective; uncountable; usually

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. The left communist tendency characterized by a synthesis of Dutch-German council communism and Italian left communism.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually; strictly

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. Italian left communism; Bordigism.

   **Usage:** broadly; uncountable; usually

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. Leftism collectively considered uncompromising and sectarian, especially anti-Leninist Marxism.

   **Usage:** derogatory; uncountable; usually; far-left

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. An adherent of ultraleftism.

   **Usage:** countable; rare; usually

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. The most extreme ideologies of the political left.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually; generally
