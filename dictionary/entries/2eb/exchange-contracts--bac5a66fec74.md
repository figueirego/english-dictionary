### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To formally contract to purchase, sell or otherwise be party to a disposition of an estate in land; usually requiring the payment of a deposit by one of the parties.

   **Usage:** England; Northern-Ireland; Wales; idiomatic

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To create a contract for the disposition of an estate in land between negotiating parties, by a process which is immediately followed by the exchange of executed counterparts of the contract between each party's legal representative.

   **Usage:** England; Northern-Ireland; Wales; chiefly by legal practitioners
