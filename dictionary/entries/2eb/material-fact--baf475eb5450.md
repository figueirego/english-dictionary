### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A fact essential to make a case or a defense, or the absence of which negates a case or defense.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A fact that might alter a person's decision to buy a property or change the terms and price a buyer is willing to accept.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law; property

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A fact that might alter an insurer's decision to agree to provide insurance to a person or alter the terms and conditions or the rate of premium on a policy.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; insurance; law
