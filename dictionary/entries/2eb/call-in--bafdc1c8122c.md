### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To communicate with a base etc, by telephone.

   **Usage:** copulative; intransitive

   **Example:** I was too unwell to work yesterday so I called in sick.

   **Source synonyms:** phone in

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To report; communicate (a message) by telephone or similar.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The hoaxer called in a bomb threat.

   **Source synonyms:** phone in

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To pay a short visit.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** I'll call in this afternoon to pick up my prescription.

   **Source synonyms:** pop in; call on

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To summon someone, especially for help or advice.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The government called in the army to deal with the riots.

   **Source synonyms:** summon

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To withdraw something from sale or circulation.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** recall

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To request immediate repayment of (a debt).

   **Usage:** transitive
