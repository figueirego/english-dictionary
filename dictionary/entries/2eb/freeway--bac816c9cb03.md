### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A highway with grade-separated crossings (rather than level crossings) and designed (and only permitted) for high-speed motor-traffic running in two directions on one separate carriageway each

   **Usage:** US; Parts of Canada and Australia

   **Source synonyms:** motorway; expressway; autoroute; autobahn; interstate\[&#58;w&#58;Interstate Highway System\|Interstate Highway System\]\]&gt;; limited-access highway; controlled-access highway

<a id="r1-s2"></a>
