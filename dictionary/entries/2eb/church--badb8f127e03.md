**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** This building used to be a church before being converted into a library.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** These worshippers make up the Church of Christ.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A local group of people who follow the same Christian religious beliefs, local or general.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A particular denomination of Christianity.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** The Church of England separated from the Roman Catholic Church in 1534.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Christian worship held at a church; service.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source context (verbatim):** as bare noun

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Organized religion in general or a specific religion considered as a political institution.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Many constitutions enshrine the separation of church and state.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Any religious group or place of worship; a temple.

   **Usage:** countable; informal; uncountable

   **Example:** She goes to a Wiccan church down the road.

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Assembly.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable
