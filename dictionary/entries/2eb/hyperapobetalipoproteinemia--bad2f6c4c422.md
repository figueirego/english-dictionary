## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A condition characterized by abnormally elevated levels of apolipoprotein B (ApoB) in the blood, typically associated with an increased risk of atherosclerosis and cardiovascular disease. It often results from either genetic disorders or metabolic abnormalities affecting lipid metabolism.

   **Usage:** uncountable
