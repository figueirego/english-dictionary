### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A village and civil parish (served by Goldsborough and Flaxby Parish Council) in North Yorkshire, England, previously in Harrogate borough (OS grid ref SE3856).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
