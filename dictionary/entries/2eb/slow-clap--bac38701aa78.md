## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A form of clapping in which audience members clap slowly, with long intervals between claps, as a form of heckling and to demonstrate that they disapprove of a performer, speaker, etc.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A form of applause (especially used in films for dramatic effect) that slowly crescendoes from one person's clapping to a crowd's.
