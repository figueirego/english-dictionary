### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Consequently, by or in consequence of that or this cause; referring to something previously stated.

   **Usage:** conjunctive; not-comparable

   **Example:** Traditional values will always have a place—therefore, they will never lose relevance.

   **Example:** I've been explicitly forbidden to leave and therefore must stay longer.

   **Example:** I left the office at 5 o'clock. I was therefore not there when the fire started a quarter of an hour later.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. For that; for it (in reference to a previous statement).

   **Usage:** archaic; conjunctive; not-comparable
