## Noun · Record 2

### Forms

- rumblings — plural

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A muted sound of complaint or discontent (often used in the plural).

   **Example:** The rumblings of the masses precede the crumbling of the state.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. A deep low noise.

   **Example:** The rumbling of distant thunder echoed from the hilltop.
