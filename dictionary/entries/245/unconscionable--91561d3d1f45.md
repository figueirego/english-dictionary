### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Not conscionable; unscrupulous and lacking principles or conscience.

   **Source synonyms:** outrageous; immoral; unethical

   **Source antonyms:** conscionable; ethical

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Excessive, imprudent or unreasonable.

   **Example:** The effective rate of interest was unconscionable, but not legally usurious.

   **Source synonyms:** shocking; excessive; egregious

   **Source antonyms:** fair; reasonable
