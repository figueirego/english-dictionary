### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A large body of ice which flows under its own mass, usually downhill.

   **Example:** They warned that the effects of glacier melting on water resources are becoming “increasingly serious” for China.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; geology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An area of a mountain where snow is present year-round.

   **Example:** You can ski down the glacier in both the winter and the summer.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; mountaineering; sports

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Something that moves very slowly

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** The queue in the pharmacy was a glacier.
