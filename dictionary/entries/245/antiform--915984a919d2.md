### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A topographic feature which is composed of sedimentary layers in a convex formation, but may not actually form a real anticline (i.e., the oldest rocks may not be exposed in the middle).

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; geology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
