## Noun

### Forms

- elaborations — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act or process of producing or refining with labor; improvement by successive operations; refinement.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The natural process of formation or assimilation, performed by the living organs in animals and vegetables, by which a crude substance is changed into something of a higher order

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** the elaboration of food into chyme

   **Example:** the elaboration of chyle, or sap, or tissues

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Setting up a hierarchy of calculated constants in a language such as Ada so that the values of one or more of them determine others further down in the hierarchy.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The process of taking a parsed tree of an abstract integrated circuit definition in a language such as Verilog and creating a hierarchy of module instances that ends with primitive (atomic) gates and statements.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; electrical-engineering; electricity; electromagnetism; electronics; energy; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The level of processing of a message or argument.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; psychology; sciences
