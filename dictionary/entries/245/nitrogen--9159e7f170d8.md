### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The chemical element (symbol N) with an atomic number of 7 and atomic weight of 14.0067. It is a colorless and odorless gas.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** By molar fraction, nitric oxide contains equal parts nitrogen and oxygen.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Molecular nitrogen (diatomic nitrogen), N₂, a colorless, odorless gas at room temperature, which constitutes most of air (78% of it).

   **Usage:** uncountable; loose but very common; even in formal use; loose but very common; even in formal use

   **Example:** The air you breathe is mostly nitrogen.

   **Example:** The truck was carrying tanks of nitrogen, so there was no fire risk. Police stated that if the truck had been carrying tanks of oxygen, as it often does, there might have been an explosion.

   **Source synonyms:** dinitrogen

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A specific nitrogen atom within a chemical formula, or a specific isotope of nitrogen.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** The two nitrogens are located next to one another on the ring.
