## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A traditional food of China, used as a dish itself, or components of other dishes, and is also a folk remedy.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** jiuniang; Chinese fermented sweet rice; Chinese sweet fermented rice; Chinese fermented rice

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A traditional desert of Thailand, composed of a lightly fermented glutinous rice.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** pang khao mahk; khao mahk; Thai fermented sweet rice; Thai sweet fermented rice; Thai fermented rice

<a id="r1-s3"></a>
