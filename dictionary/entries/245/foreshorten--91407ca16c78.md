### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To render the image of an object such that it appears to be receding in space as it is perceived visually.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To abridge, reduce, contract.
