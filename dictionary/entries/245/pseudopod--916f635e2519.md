### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A temporary projection of the cytoplasm of certain cells, such as phagocytes, or of certain unicellular organisms, such as amoebas, that serves in locomotion.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; cytology; medicine; natural-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A projection acting as a foot in certain insect larvae.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences; zoology
