### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any body of publications purported to be scholarly or supported by critical scholarship but which fails to comply with scholarly standards.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An arrangement that in effect grants a scholarship to a student, without being considered on a par with a real or standard scholarship

   **Usage:** countable
