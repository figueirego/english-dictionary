   **Example:** Dear Sir/Madam/Miss, please notice our offices will be closed during the following bank holidays&#58;\[…\].

   **Example:** Dearest Joseph/son&#58;...

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A formal way to start (often after my) addressing somebody one likes or regards kindly.

   **Example:** My dear friend, I feel better as soon as you come sit beside my sickbed!

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. An ironic way to start (often after my) addressing an inferior or someone one dislikes.

   **Example:** My dear man, you ought to think twice about who you're trying to blackmail.
