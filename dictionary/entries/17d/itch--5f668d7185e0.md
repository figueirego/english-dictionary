### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To feel itchy; to feel a need to be scratched.

   **Usage:** intransitive; stative

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To have a constant, teasing urge; to feel strongly motivated; to want or desire something.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** He started learning to drive and he has been itching for opportunities to practice ever since.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To cause to feel an itch.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To scratch or rub so as to relieve an itch.

   **Usage:** colloquial; transitive
