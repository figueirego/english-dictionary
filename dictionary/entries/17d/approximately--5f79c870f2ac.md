### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Imprecise but close to in quantity or amount.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** approximately 60 beats per minute

   **Example:** In every box of matches there are approximately 40 matchsticks.

   **Example:** Per 100 g of chocolate there is approximately 11.6 g of saturated fat.

   **Source synonyms:** around; about; near; nearly

   **Source antonyms:** accurately; definitely; exactly; precisely
