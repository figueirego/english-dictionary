### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Someone who bids (all senses), but most commonly, one offers to pay a specified price at an auction.

   **Example:** The highest bidder shall get the house.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One who commands or orders.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>
