### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An action or stunt where a bicycle, motorcycle, or other vehicle is ridden for a short period while it is standing only on its rear wheel or wheels.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** Jim fell off his bike when he was trying to do a wheelie.

   **Source synonyms:** mono; wheelstand

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A wheelchair user.

   **Usage:** Australia; informal
