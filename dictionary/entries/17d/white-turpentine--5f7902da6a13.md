## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The resin obtained
   1. from the European silver fir (Abies alba) which was used for a brilliant varnish.

      **Usage:** historical; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   1. or from the longleaf pine (Pinus palustris).

      **Usage:** historical; uncountable

      **Usage:** historical; uncountable
