### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A procedural plan, usually but not necessarily tabular in nature, indicating a sequence of operations and the planned times at which those operations are to occur.

   **Example:** To complete on time, we must follow the schedule.

   **Source synonyms:** timeline; timetable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A serial record of items, systematically arranged.

   **Example:** The building code includes a schedule of acceptable configurations.

   **Source synonyms:** catalog; inventory; list; listing; register; registry; table

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A written or printed table of information, often forming an annex or appendix to a statute or other regulatory instrument, or to a legal contract.

   **Example:** schedule of tribes

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A written or printed table of information, often forming an annex or appendix to a statute or other regulatory instrument, or to a legal contract.
   1. One of the five divisions into which controlled substances are classified, or the restrictions denoted by such classification.

      **Usage:** US; capitalized; often

      **Example:** Heroin is a Schedule I drug with a high potential for abuse.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A written or printed table of information, often forming an annex or appendix to a statute or other regulatory instrument, or to a legal contract.
   1. One of the nine schedules of the Standard for the Uniform Scheduling of Medicines and Poisons. Identical to the American usage above.

      **Usage:** Australia

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law; medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. An allocation or ordering of a set of tasks on one or several resources.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computer; computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; science; sciences

**Source sense R1.S7**

   **Usage:** obsolete

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To create a time-schedule.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To plan (an activity or event) for a specific date or time.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The next elections are scheduled on the twentieth of November.

   **Source synonyms:** make; time

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To add (a name) to the list of those participating in an event; to reserve a place or time for.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I am scheduled for classes next month.

   **Example:** I'll schedule you for three-o'clock then.

   **Source synonyms:** sign up; register; enroll; book

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To admit (a person) to hospital as an involuntary patient under a schedule of the applicable mental health law.

   **Usage:** Australia; transitive

   **Example:** whether or not to schedule a patient

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** section (UK); commit (US)

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To classify as a controlled substance.

   **Usage:** US; transitive
