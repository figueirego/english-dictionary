### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Causing pain or distress, either physical or mental.

   **Source synonyms:** excruciating; torturous; agonizing; ailsome; excruciating; groanful (obsolete); hellish; hurtful; bruising; painable (obsolete); painful; terrible; tormenting; tormentous (dated; rare); torturous; unpleasant; wrenchful (rare)

   **Source antonyms:** painless; painfree

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Afflicted or suffering with pain (of a body part or, formerly, of a person).

   **Source synonyms:** doleful; sorrowful; smartful; irksome; annoying

   **Source antonyms:** painless; painfree

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Requiring effort or labor; difficult, laborious.

   **Source synonyms:** laborious; exerting

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Painstaking; careful; industrious.

   **Usage:** archaic

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Very bad, poor.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** His violin playing is painful.
