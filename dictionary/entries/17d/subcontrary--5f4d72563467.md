## Adjective · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Contrary in an inferior degree.
   1. Having, or being in, a contrary order; said of a section of an oblique cone having a circular base made by a plane not parallel to the base, but so inclined to the axis that the section is a circle; applied also to two similar triangles when so placed as to have a common angle at the vertex, the opposite sides not being parallel.

      **Usage:** not-comparable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Contrary in an inferior degree.
   1. Denoting the relation of opposition between the particular affirmative and particular negative. Of these both may be true and only one can be false.

      **Usage:** not-comparable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; logic; mathematics; philosophy; sciences
