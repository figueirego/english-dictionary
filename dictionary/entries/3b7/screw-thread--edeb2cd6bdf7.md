### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A helical ridge formed around a cylinder, or a helical groove formed around the inner wall of a bore, used mostly on fasteners and their connection points.

   **Example:** An internal screw thread is cut with a "tap", while external screw threads are cut with a "die".
