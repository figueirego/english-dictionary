### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The Japanese art of repairing broken pottery with lacquer dusted or mixed with powdered gold or similar material, highlighting the cracks instead of disguising them.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** kintsugi
