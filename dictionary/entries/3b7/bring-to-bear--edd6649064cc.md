### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To aim a weapon at a target.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The battleship brought her main guns to bear on the fort.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To apply; to employ something to achieve an intended effect.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** to bring pressure to bear on someone, to bring influence to bear on someone

   **Example:** Every possible pressure was brought to bear on the minister to ensure the unjust law was not passed.
