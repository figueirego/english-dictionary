## Noun · Record 2

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. The causal-final case. This case in the Hungarian language combines the causal case and the final case. It can express the cause of emotions (e.g. value someone for something) or the goal of actions (e.g. "kenyérért" for bread).

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** grammar; human-sciences; linguistics; sciences
