### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To determine the value of something or the solution to something by a mathematical process.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Calculate the square root of 3 to 10 decimal places.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To determine values or solutions by a mathematical process; reckon.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To plan; to expect; to think.

   **Usage:** US; dialectal; intransitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To ascertain or predict by mathematical or astrological computations the time, circumstances, or other conditions of; to forecast or compute the character or consequences of.

   **Example:** to calculate or cast one’s nativity

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To adjust for purpose; to adapt by forethought or calculation; to fit or prepare by the adaptation of means to an end.

   **Example:** to calculate a system of laws for the government and protection of a free people

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To imagine sequences of potential moves and responses without actually moving the pieces.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** board-games; chess; games
