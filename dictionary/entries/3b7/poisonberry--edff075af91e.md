### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Informal name of various plants with poisonous berries, most commonly nightshades&#58;
   1. Deadly nightshade.

      **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Informal name of various plants with poisonous berries, most commonly nightshades&#58;
   1. Bittersweet nightshade, Solanum dulcamara.

      **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Informal name of various plants with poisonous berries, most commonly nightshades&#58;
   1. Plants in the genus Cestrum&#58; Cestrum nocturnum, Cestrum parqui.

      **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Informal name of various plants with poisonous berries, most commonly nightshades&#58;
   1. Any other berry of a plant in the nightshade family, Solanaceae, most of which are poisonous at least when unripe.

      **Usage:** uncountable
