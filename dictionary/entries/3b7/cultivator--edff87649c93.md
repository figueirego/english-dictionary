## Noun

### Forms

- cultivators — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of several devices used to loosen or stir the soil, either to remove weeds or to provide aeration and drainage.

   **Example:** As the crop gets bigger, the shank spacing on the cultivator will need to be adjusted accordingly.

   **Source synonyms:** grubber; scarifier; scuffler

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person who cultivates.
   1. Someone who uses a cultivator (implement, device, or machine).

      **Example:** The cultivators were out among the row crops, keeping the weeds in check.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A person who cultivates.
   1. Someone who grows plants.

      **Example:** She was a devoted cultivator of roses and orchids.

      **Source synonyms:** grower; planter; farmer

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A person who cultivates.
   1. Someone who fosters something besides plants (such as human development or relationships).

      **Usage:** figuratively

      **Example:** She was a wonderful cultivator of potential in her direct reports.
