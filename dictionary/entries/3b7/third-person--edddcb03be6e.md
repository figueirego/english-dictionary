### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The words, word-forms, and grammatical structures, taken collectively, that are normally used of people or things other than the speaker or the audience.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; with-definite-article

   **Example:** In English, the third person consists of pronouns such as he, she, it, and they, verbs such as is and has, and most nouns.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. the form of a verb used when the subject of a sentence is not the audience or the one making the statement. In English, pronouns used with the third person include he, she, it, one, they, and who.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** “Is” is the third-person singular of “to be”.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** grammar; human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A form of narrative writing using verbs in the third person in order to give the impression that the action is happening to another person.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A form of video game in which the graphical view depicts the player character, rather than the view through the character's eyes.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** video-games

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Someone not associated with a particular matter; a third party.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law
