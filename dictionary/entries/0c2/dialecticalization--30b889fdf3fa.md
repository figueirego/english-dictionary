### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act or process of making dialectical.
   1. The process of (a language) separating into dialects.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The act or process of making dialectical.
   1. The introduction of a dialectic (exchange of arguments or contradiction of ideas) to explore an idea or topic.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The act or process of making dialectical.
   1. The initiation of dialectic (conflict), especially class conflict.

      **Usage:** Marxism; countable; uncountable
