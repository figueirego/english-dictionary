### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To strike out with the fist, usually without planning or accuracy.

   **Example:** Feeling somebody grab at his wallet in the darkness, he hit out at the assailant.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To react viciously (towards someone/something).

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To perform by good luck.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**
