### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make (something) larger.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** We'll need to enlarge our budget if we want to include the helicopter ride in our holiday.

   **Source synonyms:** embiggen; enlargen; largen; greaten; add; amplify; augment; dilate; eke / eke out; elevate; embiggen (humorous; nonstandard; usually); enhance; [enlarge](../0c2/enlarge--308eaa15a369.md); enlargen (nonstandard); enrich; greaten; grow; increase; increment; largen (UK; literary); magnify; propagate (obsolete); raise; strengthen; swell; up

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To grow larger.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source synonyms:** expand; grow; accelerate; accrue; aggrandize (rare); augment; embiggen (humorous; nonstandard; usually); enhance; [enlarge](../0c2/enlarge--308eaa15a369.md); greaten; grow; heighten; increase; swell; wax (literary); wind up

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To increase the capacity of; to expand; to give free scope or greater scope to; also, to dilate, as with joy, affection, etc.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Knowledge enlarges the mind.

   **Source synonyms:** expand

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To speak or write at length upon or on (some subject); expand; elaborate

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source synonyms:** dilate; expatiate; expound

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To release; to set at large.

   **Usage:** archaic

   **Source synonyms:** free; liberate; set free

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To get more astern or parallel with the vessel's course; to draw aft; said of the wind.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To extend the time allowed for compliance with (an order or rule).

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law
