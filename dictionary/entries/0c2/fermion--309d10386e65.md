### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any elementary or composite particle that has half-integer spin and thus obeys Fermi–Dirac statistics and the Pauli exclusion principle (equivalently, a particle for which the wavefunction of any system of identical such particles changes sign whenever two are swapped); a baryon, a lepton or a quark;

   **Usage:** particle; Standard Model

   **Example:** The fermions treated by the Standard Model are the (composite) baryons and the (elementary) leptons and quarks.

   **Example:** According to the spin–statistics theorem, the wavefunction of a system of identical fermions (particles of half-integer spin) is antisymmetric under the operation of swapping any two particles.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics
