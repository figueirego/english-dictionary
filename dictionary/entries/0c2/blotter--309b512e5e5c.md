### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A piece of blotting paper in a pad as a piece of desk furniture.

   **Source synonyms:** desk blotter; desk pad

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A piece of blotting paper in a pad as a piece of desk furniture.
   1. A strip of such paper saturated with perfume.

      **Usage:** perfumery

      **Source synonyms:** desk blotter; desk pad; mouillette

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A daily register of arrests and other events in a police station.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; law-enforcement

   **Source synonyms:** police blotter

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A register of the related events made in the form of the list of times and brief descriptions.

   **Example:** All transactions were entered in the cash blotter and agent's subsidiary ledger.

   **Example:** He maintains the political blotter blog.

**Source sense R1.S5**

   **Usage:** slang
