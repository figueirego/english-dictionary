### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To be formally referred for trial from a lower court to a higher one.

   **Example:** This matter has passed the bar.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To achieve a successful outcome on the bar examination&#58; to pass the test (make the grade) to become a licensed lawyer.

   **Example:** My cousin just passed the bar.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see pass, the, bar.

   **Example:** His truck can pass anything but a gas station, and he can barely pass the bar on his way to report to his probation officer.
