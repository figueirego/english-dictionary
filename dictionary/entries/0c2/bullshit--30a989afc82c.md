**Source sense R3.S1**

   **Usage:** ambitransitive; slang; vulgar

   **Example:** I think you’re bullshitting. Let’s just call your office and see if you even came in.

   **Example:** You’re bullshitting me. I called your office and you never even came in.

2. To have casual conversation with no real point; to shoot the breeze.

   **Usage:** intransitive; slang; vulgar

   **Example:** I will probably just go and bullshit with Joe for a while.

   **Usage:** intransitive; slang; vulgar
