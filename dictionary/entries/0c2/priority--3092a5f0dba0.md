### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An item's relative importance.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** He set his e-mail message's priority to high.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A goal of a person or an organisation.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** She needs to get her priorities straight and stop playing games.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The quality of being earlier or coming first compared to another thing; the state of being prior.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** In bankruptcy law, a business' debt to its employees has priority over its debt to a landlord, so the employees must be paid first.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A superior claim to use by virtue of being validly published at an earlier date.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
