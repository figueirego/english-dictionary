### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A current (constantly changing) trend, favored for frivolous rather than practical, logical, or intellectual reasons.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Popular trends, especially in clothing; the industry that designs clothing and sometimes other related items.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Check out the latest in fashion.

   **Example:** He had always been interested in fashion, so he decided to take a sewing class.

   **Source synonyms:** thing

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A style or manner in which something is done.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** the fashion of the ark, of a coat, of a house, of an altar, etc.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The make or form of anything; the style, shape, appearance, or mode of structure; pattern, model; workmanship; execution.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Polite, fashionable, or genteel life; social position; good breeding.

   **Usage:** countable; dated; uncountable

   **Example:** men of fashion
