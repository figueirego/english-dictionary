### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To create a rattling sound by shaking or striking.

   **Usage:** ergative; transitive

   **Example:** to rattle a chain

   **Example:** Rattle the can of cat treats if you need to find Fluffy.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To scare, startle, unsettle, or unnerve.

   **Usage:** figuratively; informal; transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To make a rattling noise; to make noise by or from shaking.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** I wish the dashboard in my car would quit rattling.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To assail, annoy, or stun with a rattling noise.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To scold; to rail at.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To drive or ride briskly, so as to make a clattering.

   **Example:** We rattled along for a couple of miles.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To make a clatter with one's voice; to talk rapidly and idly; often with on or away.

   **Example:** She rattled on for an hour.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To experience withdrawal from drugs.

   **Usage:** UK; slang
