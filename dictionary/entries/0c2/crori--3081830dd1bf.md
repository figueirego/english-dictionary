### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The possessor or collector of a crore, or ten million, of any given kind of money; especially an official collector of revenue to the extent of a crore of dāms, or 250,000 rupees, who was also at various times invested with the general superintendence of the lands in his district, and the charge of the police.

   **Usage:** India; obsolete
