### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Not awake; having no awareness (usually as the result of a head injury).

   **Example:** After the anesthetist administered the general anesthetic the patient was unconscious.

   **Source synonyms:** insentient; oblivious; out of it; unaware; down for the count; under; lost to the world; senseless; out; insensible

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Example:** My sudden fright was an unconscious response.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; psychology; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** subconscious
