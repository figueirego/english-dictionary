### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Able to be related to something else; connected with.

   **Example:** The damage was relatable to the recent earthquake.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Able to be related to on a personal level.

   **Example:** The characters in the play are very warm and relatable.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Able to be passed on verbally; capable of being narrated or suitable for relating.

   **Example:** His verbal abuse was not relatable to polite ears.
