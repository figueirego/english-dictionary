1. To work or act together, especially for a common purpose or benefit.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To allow for mutual unobstructed action.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Usage:** intransitive
