### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having leisure time, especially as a result of not having to work for a living.

   **Example:** The leisured class may produce great advances in the arts, or it may fritter away its time.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Leisurely, filled with leisure.
