## Proper noun

### Forms

- Fathers of the House — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A title bestowed on a legislative house member, particularly one where the legislature is called a "House".
   1. The member of a legislative house who has served the longest collective time in office.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A title bestowed on a legislative house member, particularly one where the legislature is called a "House".
   1. The member of a legislative house who has served the longest current continuous period.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A title bestowed on a legislative house member, particularly one where the legislature is called a "House".
   1. The member of a legislative house who is the oldest.
