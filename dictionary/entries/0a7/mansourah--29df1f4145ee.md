## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A town and commune in Mesra district, Mostaganem province, Algeria. See Mansourah, Mostaganem on Wikipedia.Wikipedia

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A district of Bordj Bou Arréridj province, Algeria; in full, Mansourah District. A municipality in this district is named Mansoura, but may have been spelt Mansourah previously.
