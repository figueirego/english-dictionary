## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A gift, purchase, etc. whose contents are concealed until after a selection is made; a bag as gift that comprises mystery prizes.

   **Example:** Winners can choose a gift from the grab bags on the table.

   **Source synonyms:** mystery bag

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A bag from which a gift or prize may be withdrawn (grabbed) unseen.

   **Source synonyms:** mystery bag

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any random assortment, selection or possibility.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** With that system, it's a grab bag which files we can find at any moment.

   **Source synonyms:** lucky dip; mixed bag; ragbag; hodgepodge; bouillabaisse; bric-a-brac; catch-all; clamjamfrey; cocktail; rummage; salad; anthology; gallery; gatherum; congeries; crazyquilt; disjecta membra; farrago; galimatias; gallimaufry; grab bag; hodgepodge; horse and rabbit stew; hotchpotch; hotchpot; job lot; jumble; macedoine; medley; melange; mingle-mangle; miscellanea; miscellany; mishmash; mixed bag; mixture; motley; odds and ends; oddments; olio; olla podrida; omnium-gatherum (humorous); pastiche; patchwork quilt; potpourri; ragbag; salmagundi; slurry; smorgasbord; welter; Whitman's sampler

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A moderately large bag of crisps or other snack, suitable for sharing.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A bag containing essential items, which can be easily picked up and taken in an emergency.

   **Source synonyms:** bug-out bag; go bag
