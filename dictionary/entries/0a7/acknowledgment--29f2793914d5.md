### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of acknowledging

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** admission

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The act of recognizing in a particular character or relationship; recognition of existence, authority, truth, or genuineness.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** He just grunted in acknowledgment of my greeting.

   **Source synonyms:** nod

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A reward or other expression or token of gratitude.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An expression of gratitude for a benefit or an obligation.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** the acknowledgment of a favor

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A message from the addressee informing the originator that the originator's communication has been received and understood.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** the acknowledgment of a wedding invitation

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A response (ACK) sent by a receiver to indicate successful receipt of a transmission (See Wikipedia article on transmission).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** communications; computing; electrical-engineering; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; networking; physical-sciences; sciences; telecommunications

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A recognition as genuine or valid; an avowing or admission in legal form.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** acknowledgment of a deed

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. The act of a person admitting a child as their own.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A formal statement or document recognizing the fulfillment or execution of a legal requirement or procedure.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law
