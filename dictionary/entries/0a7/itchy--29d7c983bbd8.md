### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Characterized by itching. (of a condition)

   **Example:** an itchy rash

   **Source synonyms:** pruritic

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Feeling an itching sensation; feeling a need to be scratched. (of a person, animal or body part)

   **Example:** My nose always gets itchy the moment I put on my face mask.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Causing an itching sensation.

   **Example:** He refuses to wear the new sweater; he says it’s itchy.

   **Source synonyms:** scratchy

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. In a state of agitation; easily alarmed.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Source synonyms:** anxious; jittery; jumpy; nervous; on edge

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Having a constant, teasing desire (for something, to do something); impatiently eager.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Source synonyms:** itching
