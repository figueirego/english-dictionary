## Phrase

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Introduces a situation in which the listener is forced to make an immediate choice or state a preference under pressure, especially where neither option is strongly preferred.

   **Usage:** excessive; idiomatic

   **Example:** Gun to your head, would you pick chocolate or vanilla?
