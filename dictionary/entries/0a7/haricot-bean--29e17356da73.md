### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A herbaceous plant from the Americas, Phaseolus vulgaris, the bean of which is eaten as both a green bean and dried, when it is cooked slowly as Boston baked beans.

   **Usage:** UK

   **Source synonyms:** haricot; common bean; pea bean
