## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /kənˈfɛkʃən/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Forms

- confections — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A food item prepared very sweet, frequently decorated in fine detail, and often preserved with sugar, such as a candy, sweetmeat, fruit preserve, pastry, or cake.

   **Example:** The table was covered with all sorts of tempting confections.

   **Source synonyms:** confect (formal)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The act or process of confecting; the process of making, compounding, or preparing something.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The result of such a process; something made up or confected; a concoction.

   **Example:** The defense attorney maintained that the charges were a confection of the local police.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An artistic, musical, or literary work taken as frivolous, amusing, or contrived; a composition of a light nature.

   **Usage:** dated

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Something, such as a garment or a decoration, that is very elaborate, delicate, or luxurious, usually also impractical or non-utilitarian.

   **Usage:** dated

   **Example:** Her gown was a marvelous confection of lace and tulle.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A preparation of medicine sweetened with sugar, honey, syrup, or the like; an electuary.
   1. A medicinal preparation of any kind, a compound of drugs.

      **Usage:** obsolete

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; pharmacology; sciences

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A preparation of medicine sweetened with sugar, honey, syrup, or the like; an electuary.
   1. A deadly poison.

      **Usage:** obsolete

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; pharmacology; sciences
