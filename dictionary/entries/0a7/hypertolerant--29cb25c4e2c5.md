## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Exhibiting hypertolerance.
   1. Unusually tolerant of environmental trace elements.

      **Usage:** not-comparable; usually

      **Example:** Brownfields may sometimes be inadvertent laboratories in which hypertolerant varieties can be studied.

      **Source context (verbatim):** of plants or microbes

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Exhibiting hypertolerance.
   1. Excessively tolerant of others' misbehavior.

      **Usage:** not-comparable; uncommonly

      **Example:** Patience is a virtue, but those who are overpatient or hypertolerant risk inviting abusers to treat them like a doormat.

      **Source context (verbatim):** of persons

      **Source synonyms:** hyperpatient; overpatient; lenient; permissive; lax
