### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Uncritically satisfied with oneself or one's achievements; smug.

   **Example:** He grew complacent as the years rolled on and the money rolled in.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Unduly unworried or apathetic with regard to a need or problem.

   **Example:** He tried to paint his audience as complacent, yelling that if they weren't mad as hell then they weren't paying enough attention.

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** smug; self-satisfied; placid
