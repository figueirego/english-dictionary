## Noun · Etymology 2 · Record 3

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈʍaɪtɪŋ/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

- whiting — plural

### Senses

<a id="r3-s1"></a>

**Source sense R3.S1**

1. A fish, Merlangius merlangus (family Gadidae), similar to cod, found in the North Atlantic; English whiting (US).

<a id="r3-s2"></a>

**Source sense R3.S2**

2. Any fish of many species that resemble Merlangius merlangus&#58;
   1. in family Gadidae&#58;
      1. Any of several marine fish found in North American coastal waters, including hakes (genus Merluccius), especially Merluccius bilinearis (silver hake).

         **Usage:** US

<a id="r3-s3"></a>

**Source sense R3.S3**

3. Any fish of many species that resemble Merlangius merlangus&#58;
   1. in family Gadidae&#58;
      1. Alaska pollock (Gadus chalcogrammus, syn. Theragra chalcogramma).

         **Usage:** Canada

<a id="r3-s4"></a>

**Source sense R3.S4**

4. Any fish of many species that resemble Merlangius merlangus&#58;
   1. in family Gadidae&#58;
      1. A blue whiting (Micromesistius poutassou), a marine fish of the Northern Hemisphere.

<a id="r3-s5"></a>

**Source sense R3.S5**

5. Any fish of many species that resemble Merlangius merlangus&#58;
   1. in family Gadidae&#58;
      1. A southern blue whiting (Micromesistius australis), a marine fish of the Southern Hemisphere.

<a id="r3-s6"></a>

**Source sense R3.S6**

6. Any fish of many species that resemble Merlangius merlangus&#58;
   1. in family Sillaginidae, smelt-whitings, inhabiting Indo-Pacific marine coasts, many species of which are commercially important whitefish.

<a id="r3-s7"></a>

**Source sense R3.S7**

7. Any fish of many species that resemble Merlangius merlangus&#58;
   1. in family Sciaenidae, Menticirrhus americanus (Carolina whiting, king whiting, southern kingcroaker, and southern kingfish) found along the Atlantic and Gulf Coasts of the United States.
