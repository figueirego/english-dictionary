### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A linguistic feature of Slovakian language, especially a Slovakian idiom or phrasing that appears in some other language.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An attitude, custom, or other feature that is characteristic for Slovaks.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s3"></a>
