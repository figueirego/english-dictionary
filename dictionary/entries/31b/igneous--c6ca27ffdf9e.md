### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Pertaining to or having the nature of fire; containing fire; resembling fire.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The stone had an igneous appearance.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Resulting from, or produced by, great heat. With rocks, it could also mean formed from lava or magma.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Granite and basalt are igneous rocks.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; geology; natural-sciences
