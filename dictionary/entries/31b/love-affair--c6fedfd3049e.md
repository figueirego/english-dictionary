## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An affair; a usually adulterous relationship between people who are not married to each other.

   **Example:** He was involved in a love affair with a married woman.

   **Source synonyms:** affaire; liaison

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A feeling of enthusiasm or strong liking for something; a fondness.

   **Example:** I've always had a love affair with stamps.
