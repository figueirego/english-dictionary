1. Denotes the one immediately following the current or most recent one.

   **Example:** Next week would be a good time to meet.

   **Example:** I'll know better next time.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Closest in the future, or closest but one if the closest is very soon; of days, sometimes thought to specifically refer to the instance closest to seven days (one week) in the future.

   **Example:** The party is next Tuesday; that is, not tomorrow, but eight days from now.

   **Example:** When you say next Thursday, do you mean Thursday this week or Thursday next week?
