### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The deflection of particles or radiation through angles greater than 90 degrees to the original direction of travel.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The particles or radiation deflected in this manner.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A portion of the energy of electromagnetic radiation such as a laser or radio waves that is scattered back in the direction of the source of radiation by an obscurant.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The sending of IP packets to essentially random addresses in response to incoming packets that have a spoofed origin.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** blowback; outscatter
