### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A coming to; the act of acceding and becoming joined.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** a king's accession to a confederacy

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Increase by something added; that which is added; augmentation from without.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Increase by something added; that which is added; augmentation from without.
   1. Such augmentation that adds to the collections of a museum or archive; a thing thus added.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source antonyms:** deaccession; deacquisition

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A mode of acquiring property, by which the owner of a corporeal substance which receives an addition by growth, or by labor, has a right to the part or thing added, or the improvement (provided the thing is not changed into a different species).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The act by which one power becomes party to engagements already in force between other powers.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** accession to the European Union

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The act of coming to or reaching a throne, an office, or dignity.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** her accession to the throne

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The invasion, approach, or commencement of a disease; a fit or paroxysm.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
