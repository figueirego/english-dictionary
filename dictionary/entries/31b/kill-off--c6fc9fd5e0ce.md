### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To eliminate, to make extinct.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** We killed off the Dodo by over-hunting.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of writers or producers, to permanently take a character out of a television series or other work by purposefully and deliberately having them killed within the plot.

   **Example:** The writers are killing off lots of people in the soap opera.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** broadcasting; film; literature; media; publishing; television
