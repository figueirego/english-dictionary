### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having a flat (planar) horizontal surface at top.

   **Example:** Mesas got their name from being tabular.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of an object or landform

   **Source synonyms:** tablelike

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Organized as a table or list.

   **Example:** In technical communication, don't use prose to present information that lends itself to a tabular presentation.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a document or information

   **Source synonyms:** tabulated; tabularized

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Calculated by means of a table.

   **Example:** tabular artillery direction

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Tending to split into thin flat pieces, as slate does.

   **Example:** The local bedrock is tabular.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; geology; natural-sciences
