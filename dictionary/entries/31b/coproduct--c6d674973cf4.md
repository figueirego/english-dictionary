### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of multiple products that are produced at the same time, or by the same process

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The colimit of two (or more) objects, without regard to any morphisms between them.

   **Example:** The coproduct is a structure constructed from several similar structures, equipped with appropriate inclusion maps, which generalises the disjoint union of sets, of topological spaces and the free product of groups.
