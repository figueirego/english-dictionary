### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A food consisting principally of raw vegetables (especially greens, e.g. lettuce), other savoury ingredients (typically cooked), fruit, or confectionary; often involving two or more such ingredients mixed together, with larger items cut or torn into small pieces, and usually with a dressing or binding; usually served cold or at room temperature, as a dish or savoury sandwich filling.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** We'd like a couple of cheese salads and two Pepsis, please.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One or more raw vegetables of the kind used in salads; typically greens (especially lettuce).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** sandwiches comprising a meat, a cheese, a salad, and a condiment

   **Example:** I prefer a bit of salad on my burger.

   **Example:** Buy a bag of salad at the supermarket.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any mixture of different things.

   **Usage:** countable; idiomatic; uncountable

   **Example:** word salad
