### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To enter a place by force or illicit means.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Someone broke in and stole his radio.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To smash (something) by forcing it inward.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To reach a state of functioning more smoothly through use or wear; to cause (something, or someone, new) to undergo this change.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive; ergative; idiomatic

   **Example:** These shoes will be more comfortable after they have broken in.

   **Example:** These shoes will be more comfortable after I have broken them in.

   **Source synonyms:** run in; wear in

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Starting something brand new or at a new level.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** He broke in with the New York Yankees.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To tame; make obedient; to train to follow orders of the owner.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a horse
