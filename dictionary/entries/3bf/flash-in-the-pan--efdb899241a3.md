### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A transient occurrence with no long-term impact.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** The concept turned out to be merely a flash in the pan and is no longer used.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A career notable for early success that is not followed by any significant accomplishment.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** He was named Best New Director of 1940, but his career was a flash in the pan.
