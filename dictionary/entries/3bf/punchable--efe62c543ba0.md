### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Suitable for being punched or perforated.

   **Example:** a material that is punchable at low temperatures

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Deserving to be punched or struck, or evoking the desire to do so.

   **Usage:** colloquial

   **Example:** He has an eminently punchable face!

   **Source synonyms:** punchworthy
