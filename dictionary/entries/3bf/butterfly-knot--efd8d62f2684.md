### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A type of knot used to provide an anchor point on a long line. Much used by mountaineers. Also known as the alpine butterfly knot.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A small decorative woven knot using one or two cords.
