### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Both detectable and at least modestly substantial&#58; large enough to be estimated and to be of practical relevance.

   **Usage:** usually

   **Example:** For many people, the benefits of exercise in improving mood and preventing achiness are appreciable.

   **Source synonyms:** considerable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Synonym of detectable.

   **Usage:** sometimes

   **Example:** A small hernia was just barely appreciable upon palpation.

   **Source synonyms:** perceptible; detectable (synonym; synonym-of)
