## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The use, by an organism, of the same colour as its background or other form of camouflage as a means of protection.

   **Usage:** rare; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences; zoology

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The anatomical state in which the same pigmentation or coloring occurs in the eyes, skin or hair.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; ophthalmology; sciences
