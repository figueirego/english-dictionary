### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The feeling of annoyance at impossibility from resistance or inability to achieve something.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The act of frustrating, or the state, or an instance of being frustrated.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The act of frustrating, or the state, or an instance of being frustrated.
   1. The state of contract that allows a party to back away from its contractual obligations due to (unforeseen) radical changes to the nature of the thing a party has been obligated to.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable
