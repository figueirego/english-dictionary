### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A water bottle, flask, or other vessel, typically used by a soldier or camper as a bottle for carrying water or liquor for drink.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A military mess kit.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A box with compartments for storing eating utensils, silverware etc.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A temporary or mobile café used in an emergency or on a film location etc.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A small cafeteria or snack bar, especially one in a military establishment, school, or place of work.

   **Example:** He ordered a salad at the canteen.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A small cafeteria or snack bar, especially one in a military establishment, school, or place of work.
   1. A cafeteria in a school or place of work.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A small cafeteria or snack bar, especially one in a military establishment, school, or place of work.
   1. Goods purchased from a prison canteen.
