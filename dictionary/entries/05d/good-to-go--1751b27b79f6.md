### Pronunciation

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Ready for use or normal operation, especially after repair or renewal.

   **Usage:** informal; not-comparable

   **Example:** I replaced the broken spring and now the toaster is good to go.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a thing

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Ready for some specific task or ready for normal activity, especially after preparation or recovery.

   **Usage:** informal; not-comparable

   **Example:** The doctor examined me and says I'm good to go.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a person
