### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To draw (an object, especially a sharp or angular one), along (something) while exerting pressure.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** She scraped the wooden plate with her fingernails.

   **Example:** That car often scrapes the shallowest of humps because of its low ground clearance.

   **Source synonyms:** scuff

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To remove (something) by drawing an object along in this manner.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Scrape the chewing gum off with a knife.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To injure or damage by rubbing across a surface.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** She tripped on a rock and scraped her knee.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To barely manage to achieve or attain.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I scraped a pass in the exam.

   **Source synonyms:** labor; squeeze; limp; squeak

   **Source antonyms:** stroll; breeze; ride; coast; romp

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To collect or gather, especially without regard to the quality of what is chosen.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Just use whatever you can scrape together.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To extract data by automated means from a format not intended to be machine-readable, such as a screenshot or a formatted web page.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** They didn't provide an API, so I simply scraped their website.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** He scraped and saved until he became rich.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To play awkwardly and inharmoniously on a violin or similar instrument.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** Some people covered their ears when he started to scrape his violin.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To draw back the right foot along the ground or floor when making a bow.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**
