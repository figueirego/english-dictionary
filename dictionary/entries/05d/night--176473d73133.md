<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The time when the Sun is below the horizon when the sky is dark.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** Most animals are awake at day and sleep at night.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The period of darkness beginning at the end of evening astronomical twilight when the sun is 18 degrees below the horizon, and ending at the beginning of morning astronomical twilight.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astronomy; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A period of time often defined in the legal system as beginning 30 minutes after sunset, and ending 30 minutes before sunrise.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An evening or night spent at a particular activity.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** a night on the town

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A day, or at least a night.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** I stayed my friend's house for three nights.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Nightfall.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** from noon till night

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Darkness (due to it being nighttime).

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** The cat disappeared into the night.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A dark blue colour, midnight blue.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s9"></a>
