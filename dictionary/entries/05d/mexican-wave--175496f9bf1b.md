### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A phenomenon that primarily occurs at sports events, where the members of the crowd stand up and/or raise their hands in turn, creating the illusion of a wave passing through the crowd.

   **Usage:** Commonwealth; Ireland; UK
