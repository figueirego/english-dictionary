### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Outside of regulatorily approved indications, but (unless otherwise specified) within the bounds of reasonable use as defined by a licensed prescriber.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The use of colchicine for gout is FDA approved, whereas the use of colchicine for pericarditis is off label (but is widely respected).

   **Example:** Off label use of colchicine for pericarditis is widely respected.
