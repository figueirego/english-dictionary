## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

- enates — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A relative whose relation is traced only through female members of the family.

   **Example:** A great-grandmother is an enate if she is your mother’s mother's mother.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any maternal female relative.
