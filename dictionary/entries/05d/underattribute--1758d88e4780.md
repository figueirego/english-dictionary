### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To attribute insufficiently or inadequately.
   1. To underestimate the role of some specified factors in causing (a particular outcome, action, or state of affairs).

      **Usage:** transitive

      **Source antonyms:** overattribute

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To attribute insufficiently or inadequately.
   1. To tend to mistakenly believe that (a certain quality) is not present when, in fact, it is.

      **Usage:** transitive

      **Source antonyms:** overattribute

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To attribute insufficiently or inadequately.
   1. To misattribute a work of art by asserting that the artist is a less renowned one than it actually is.

      **Usage:** transitive

      **Source antonyms:** overattribute
