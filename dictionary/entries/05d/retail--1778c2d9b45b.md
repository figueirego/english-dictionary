## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The sale of goods directly to the consumer, encompassing the storefronts, mail-order, websites, etc., and the corporate mechanisms, branding, advertising, etc. that support them.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** She works in retail.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The sale of securities or other financial instruments to private investors, as opposed to large institutions.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Retail price; full price; an abbreviated expression, meaning the full suggested price of a particular good or service, before any sale, discount, or other deal.

   **Usage:** colloquial; uncountable

   **Example:** I never pay retail for clothes.
