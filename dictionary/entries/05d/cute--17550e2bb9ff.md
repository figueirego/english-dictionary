### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Possessing physical features, behaviors, personality traits or other properties that are mainly attributed to infants and small or cuddly animals; e.g. fair, dainty, round, and soft physical features, disproportionately large eyes and head, playfulness, fragility, helplessness, curiosity or shyness, innocence, affectionate behavior.

   **Example:** Our reaction to cute attributes is understood as the way nature ensures mammals care for their young.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Lovable, charming, attractive or pleasing, especially in a youthful, dainty, quaint or fun-spirited way.

   **Example:** Let's go to the mall and look for cute girls.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Sexually attractive or pleasing; gorgeous.

   **Example:** He's got such cute buns.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Affected or contrived to charm; mincingly clever; precious; cutesy.

   **Example:** The actor's performance was too cute for me. All that mugging to the audience killed the humor.

   **Example:** Don't get cute with me, boy!

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Mentally keen or discerning (See also acute)

   **Example:** Cute trick, but can you do it consistently?

   **Source synonyms:** clever; shrewd

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Evincing cleverness; surprising in its elegance or unconventionality (but of limited importance).

   **Usage:** especially

   **Example:** There's a cute alternative proof of this using lambda calculus.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences
