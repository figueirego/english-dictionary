### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To grow or increase stature; to grow vigorously or luxuriantly; to enjoy a state of excellent health and well-being; to flourish.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Not all animals thrive well in captivity.

   **Example:** to thrive upon hard work

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To increase in wealth or success; to prosper, be profitable.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Since expanding in June, the business has really thrived.
