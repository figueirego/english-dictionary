### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act or process of capitalising.
   1. Choice of case (when writing a word); the act or process of writing a word in a particular case, such as ALL CAPS, Title Case, CamelCase, Sentence case, or all lowercase.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Example:** English and French have different rules for the capitalisation of the names of the days of the week.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The act or process of capitalising.
   1. Choice of case (when writing a word); the act or process of writing a word in a particular case, such as ALL CAPS, Title Case, CamelCase, Sentence case, or all lowercase.
      1. The act or process of writing (something&#58; either an entire word or text, or just the initial letter(s) thereof) in capital letters.

         **Usage:** countable; uncountable

         **Example:** The capitalisation of all nouns is a distinctive feature of German.

         **Source context (verbatim):** in particular

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The act or process of capitalising.
   1. The act or process of seizing (an opportunity) and profiting or obtaining an advantage (from it).

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable
