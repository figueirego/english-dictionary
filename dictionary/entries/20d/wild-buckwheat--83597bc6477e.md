### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any plant in the genus Eriogonum of wild buckwheats, in the knotweed or buckwheat family (Polygonaceae), as is true buckwheat (Fagopyrum esculentum).

   **Usage:** US; uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Other plants related to or resembling buckwheat&#58;
   1. Many other plants of the family Polygonaceae;

      **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Other plants related to or resembling buckwheat&#58;
   1. Tartary buckwheat (Fagopyrum tataricum), cultivated in the Himalayas;

      **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Other plants related to or resembling buckwheat&#58;
   1. tall buckwheat (Fagopyrum cymosum, syns. Fagopyrum acutatum, Fagopyrum dibotrys), cultivated for medicinal use and as animal fodder;

      **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Other plants related to or resembling buckwheat&#58;

      **Usage:** uncountable; usually
