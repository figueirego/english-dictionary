### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A sheet of plastic embedded with microscopic crystals of herapathite or similarly acting material, so that light passing through it is polarized.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Spectacles made with lenses of this material, once used to view certain 3-D movies, now used as sunglasses.

   **Usage:** in-plural

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A camera that develops its own film.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A print from such a camera.
