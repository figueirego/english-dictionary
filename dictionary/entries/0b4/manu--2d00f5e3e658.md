## Proper noun · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A title accorded to the progenitor of mankind, first king to rule this earth, who saved mankind from a great global flood.

   **Usage:** Hinduism

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The progenitor of the Aryan root-race, which is said to have originated in Atlantis.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A male given name from Sanskrit, commonly used in India.
