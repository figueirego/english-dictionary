### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To heap up in a mass; to pile up; to collect or bring together (either literally or figuratively), often gradually and without active intent.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He wishes to accumulate a sum of money.

   **Source synonyms:** amass; heap; hoard; store; accumulate; agglomerate; amass; build up; cumulate; heap; heap up; mound; pile; pile up; rack up; stack; stack up; upheap

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To gradually grow or increase in quantity or number.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** With her company going bankrupt, her divorce, and a gambling habit, debts started to accumulate so she had to sell her house.

   **Source synonyms:** aggregate; amound; collect; gather

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To take a higher degree at the same time with a lower degree, or at a shorter interval than usual.

   **Usage:** dated

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** education
