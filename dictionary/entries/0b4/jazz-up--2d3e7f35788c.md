### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make gaudy and bright.

   **Usage:** informal; transitive

   **Example:** I jazzed up my room with some new posters.

   **Source synonyms:** liven up; enliven; brighten up; brighten; gaudify

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To make more interesting by additions.

   **Usage:** informal; transitive

   **Example:** I jazzed up a supermarket pizza with olives and fried onions.

   **Source synonyms:** liven up; enliven; spice up; customize

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To give a rhythm or melody reminiscent of jazz.

   **Usage:** informal; transitive

   **Example:** They played a jazzed-up version of the original song.

   **Source synonyms:** jazzify
