### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A small passenger airplane, typically used for shorter connecting trips to smaller airports.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** The flight across country was not bad, but the ride in the puddle jumper between Boston and Connecticut was a little rough.

   **Source synonyms:** puddle hopper

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A wearable personal flotation device similar to a life vest especially worn by infants or small children in swimming pools.
