### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In pre-modern European folklore&#58; an infant of a magical creature that was secretly exchanged for a human infant. In British, Irish and Scandinavian mythology the exchanged infants were thought to be those of fairies, sprites or trolls; in other places, they were ascribed to demons, devils, or witches.

   **Usage:** European; also; figuratively

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** arts; folklore; history; human-sciences; literature; media; publishing; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** oaf; swapling

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person or object (especially when regarded as inferior) secretly exchanged for something else.

   **Usage:** broadly

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
