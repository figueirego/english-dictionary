### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To write.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** I set down this account so others may benefit from my experience.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To fix; to establish; to ordain.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To place, especially on the ground or a surface; to cease carrying; to deposit; to allow passengers to alight.

   **Usage:** UK; especially; transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To land.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** The bush pilot set down on a sandbar.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** aeronautics; aerospace; aviation; business; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To humiliate.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To regard (someone) in a particular way; to put down as.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I set him down as an idiot.
