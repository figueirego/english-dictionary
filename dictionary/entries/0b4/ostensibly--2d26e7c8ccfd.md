### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Seemingly, apparently, on the surface; supposedly, according to representations or implications made by someone (especially when their motives are suspected by others).

   **Usage:** modal; not-comparable

   **Example:** His interest in the railway was ostensibly a hobby, but people began wondering why he was taking photos of specific trains and specific equipment.

   **Example:** Her interest in his welfare was ostensibly charitable and magnanimous, but people began wondering who was going to inherit his assets when he died.

   **Source synonyms:** apparently; arguably; at first blush; ostensively; seemingly; reportedly; reputedly; purportedly; putatively; rumoredly; supposedly; allegedly; possibly; probably; likely; plausibly; presumably
