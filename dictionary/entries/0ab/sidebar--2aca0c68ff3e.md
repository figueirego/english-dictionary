## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

- sidebars — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A short news story printed alongside a larger one.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A block of information placed at the side of a printed page.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** media; publishing; typography

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A block of information placed at the side of a webpage.

   **Usage:** Internet

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A short conference, between a judge and the attorneys of a case, held outside the hearing of the jury and the spectators at the court.

   **Usage:** US

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The place in the courtroom where such a conference happens.

   **Usage:** US

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. An accessory side note or aside made during a conversation, without changing the scope of the audience.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A short conversation between a smaller portion of a group held outside the hearing of the rest of the group.
