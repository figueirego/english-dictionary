## Adverb

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. For the period of an entire day, or at least during all the daytime of a day.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** There hasn't been a breath of wind all day.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Easily; handily; in spades; by far.

   **Usage:** figuratively; not-comparable

   **Example:** Well, for twenty quid we're having it — it's worth that all day long.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. In total.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; slang

   **Example:** Table 2 needs three tacos, and Table 4 needs six, so that's nine tacos, all day.
