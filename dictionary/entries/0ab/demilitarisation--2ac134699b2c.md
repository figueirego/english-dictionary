## Noun

### Forms

- demilitarisations — plural
- demilitarization — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The reduction of the armed forces of a state or other political entity in its entire territory, usually at the end of hostilities or as part of a treaty.

   **Source antonyms:** militarization

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The elimination of the armed forces of a state or other political entity from a specific area in its territory, usually at the end of hostilities or as part of a treaty, such as in the case of demilitarized zones.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The conversion of a military or paramilitary force into a civilian one.
