### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The state or quality of being simple
   1. The quality or state of being unmixed or uncompounded

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Example:** the simplicity of metals or of earths

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The state or quality of being simple
   1. The quality or state of being not complex, or of consisting of few parts.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Example:** the simplicity of a machine

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The state or quality of being simple
   1. Lack of sharpness of mind; lack of ability to think using complex ideas; stupidity

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The state or quality of being simple
   1. Lack of artificial ornament, pretentious style, or luxury; plainness

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Example:** simplicity of dress, of style, or of language

      **Example:** simplicity of diet

      **Example:** simplicity of life

      **Source synonyms:** chastity

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The state or quality of being simple
   1. Lack of subtlety or abstruseness; clarity

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Example:** the simplicity of a doctrine

      **Example:** the simplicity of an explanation or a demonstration

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The state or quality of being simple
   1. Lack of complication; efficiency.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; rare; uncountable
