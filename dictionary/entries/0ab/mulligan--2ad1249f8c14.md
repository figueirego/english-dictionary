2. An unpenalized chance to re-take a stroke that went awry.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** If you lose your drive in the water, take a mulligan and try again.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** golf; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

**Source sense R1.S3**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; collectible card games

**Source sense R1.S4**

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; uncountable
