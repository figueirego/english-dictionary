## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Energetic but useless action, or a person engaged in such action.

   **Usage:** vulgar

   **Example:** The whole exercise was a monkey humping a football.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A manner of riding a horse, motorcycle or other vehicle, in which the rider crouches forward and may bounce in the saddle.

   **Usage:** vulgar

   **Example:** This poseur was riding like a monkey humping a football, but I blew past him.
