### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Something that happens by chance, especially an unfortunate event; an accident, a disaster.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person suffering from injuries or who has been killed due to an accident or through an act of violence.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Specifically, a person who has been killed (not only injured) due to an accident or through an act of violence; a fatality.

   **Usage:** countable; proscribed; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A person in military service who becomes unavailable for duty, for any reason (notably death, injury, illness, capture, or desertion).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Clipping of casualty department&#58; the accident and emergency department of a hospital providing immediate treatment.

   **Usage:** UK; abbreviation; alt-of; clipping; countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** A&amp;E; accident and emergency; emergency department; emergency room

   **Alternative form of:** [casualty department](../386/casualty-department--e1b495cae992.md)

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. An incidental charge or payment.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Someone or something adversely affected by a decision, event or situation.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s8"></a>
