### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The system that provides support to an organism, where in vertebrates it is internal and consists of bones and cartilage, and external in some other animals.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An anthropomorphic representation of a skeleton.

   **Example:** Her whimsical Halloween decorations include dancing skeletons who sing an eerie song.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A very thin person.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** She lost so much weight while she was ill that she became a skeleton.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The central core of something that gives shape to the entire structure; an outline of a longer project.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** The skeleton of the organisation is essentially the same as it was ten years ago, but many new faces have come and gone.

   **Example:** I've written three stories. Well, one is a skeleton, but the other two are mostly complete.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A frame that provides support to a building or other construction.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** architecture

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A client-helper procedure that communicates with a stub.

   **Example:** In remote method invocation, the client helper is a ‘stub’ and the service helper is a ‘skeleton’.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**
