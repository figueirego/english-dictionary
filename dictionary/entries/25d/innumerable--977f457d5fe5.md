### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Not capable of being counted, enumerated, or numbered.

   **Example:** The casualties of the Second World War were so great that they are innumerable.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of a very high number; extremely numerous.
