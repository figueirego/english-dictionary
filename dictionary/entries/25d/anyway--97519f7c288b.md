### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Regardless; anyhow.

   **Usage:** conjunctive; not-comparable

   **Example:** He didn't enjoy washing his car, but it was so dirty that he did it anyway.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used to indicate that a statement explains or supports a previous statement. See anyhow and at least.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** I don't think that's true. I haven't found any evidence, anyway.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used to indicate a change of subject.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Used at the end of a question for emphasis, or to direct the conversation to something of more broad importance (compare with more to the point).

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. In any way.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; obsolete
