### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A sign that serves as an abbreviation of the word number. It comprises a capital letter N with a lowercase letter o, the latter typically superscript and sometimes underlined. Examples of numero signs include No., No, №, and Nº.
