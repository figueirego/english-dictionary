### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A structure made by stacking playing cards in a pyramidal fashion.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any structure with alternating vertical and horizontal layers.

   **Usage:** attributive; broadly; often

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A structure, argument, or arrangement that is inherently flimsy or fragile and thus might collapse if any adversity were to come along and challenge it.
