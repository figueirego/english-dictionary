- hunched — past

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To bend the top of one's body forward while raising one's shoulders.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Don't hunch over your computer if you want to avoid neck problems.

   **Source synonyms:** slouch; stoop; lean

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To raise (one's shoulders) (while lowering one's head or bending the top of one's body forward); to curve (one's body) forward (sometimes followed by up).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** They stood outside the door hunching themselves against the rain and puffing on their cigarettes.

   **Example:** He hunched up his shoulders and stared down at the ground.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To walk (somewhere) while hunching one's shoulders.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source synonyms:** slouch

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To thrust a hump or protuberance out of (something); to crook, as the back.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To push or jostle with the elbow; to push or thrust against (someone).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** elbow; nudge

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To have a hunch, or make an intuitive guess.

   **Usage:** colloquial; intransitive
