### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Attractive; (by extension) awesome, cool, excellent.

   **Usage:** dated; slang

   **Example:** Wow, those shoes are the ginchiest!

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Example:** I got very ginchy about being left alone with Eileen, very hopeful and very anxious both at once.
