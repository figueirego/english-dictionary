## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A place or part of a place, especially one that is small, remote, or tedious to access.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** There was not one nook or cranny in the house to hide it.

   **Example:** There's not a nook or cranny that I haven't looked in.

   **Example:** Everyone went to sleep in some nook or cranny of the house.
