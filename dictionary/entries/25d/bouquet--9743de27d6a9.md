### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An arrangement or bunch of cut flowers; a nosegay.

   **Usage:** collective

   **Example:** For my birthday I received two bouquets.

   **Source synonyms:** arrangement

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A decoratively arranged bunch of something.

   **Example:** Each table was adorned with a bouquet of giant balloons.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The scent of a particular wine.

   **Example:** This Bordeaux has an interesting bouquet.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The middle note of a perfume.

   **Example:** The remarkable flower bouquet lasts for hours until it dissolves into a sweet vanilla smell.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A compliment or expression of praise.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A bouquet of circles.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences
