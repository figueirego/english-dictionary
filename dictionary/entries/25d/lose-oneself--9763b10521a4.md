### Senses

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To become deeply involved (in something) or overwhelmed by a (particular) sensation or emotion (in either a good way or a bad way).

   **Example:** Sometimes you just need to kick back, relax, and lose yourself in a good novel for a while.
