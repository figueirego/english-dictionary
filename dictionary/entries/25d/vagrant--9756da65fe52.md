## Noun · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈveɪɡɹənt/ — source labels: General-American; Received-Pronunciation

### Forms

- vagrants — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who wanders from place to place; a nomad, a wanderer.

   **Usage:** dated

   **Source synonyms:** itinerant; rover; ambler; flaneur; gadling; itinerant; nomad; peregrinator; rambler; rover; saunterer; straggler; stroller; vagrant (dated); walke-street (obsolete); wanderer

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person without settled employment or habitation who usually supports himself or herself by begging or some dishonest means; a tramp, a vagabond.

   **Usage:** specifically

   **Example:** Every morning before work, I see that poor vagrant around the neighbourhood begging for food.

   **Source synonyms:** drifter; hobo; bum; stiff; casual (Britain; dated); clochard (especially); **Source context (verbatim):** in France; derelict; dero; down-and-out; down-and-outer; drifter; epithite (obsolete); floater; gadling; hobo; Jack out of doors (obsolete); knight of the road (obsolete); landleaper; landloper; landlouper; nomad; paker (obsolete); palliard (archaic); pike; piker; ranger; rogue; rolling stone; rough sleeper (Britain); scatterling (obsolete); skelder (obsolete); skell; stroller; stumblebum; swagman; swaggie; toerag; tramp; truant (obsolete); vagabond; vagrant; wanderer; waif; yagger (Scotland; archaic)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any member of the species Vagrans egista of widely distributed Asian butterflies of the family Nymphalidae.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An animal, typically a bird, found outside its species' usual range.

   **Usage:** especially

## Adjective · Etymology 2 · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈveɪɡɹənt/ — source labels: General-American; Received-Pronunciation

### Forms

- more vagrant — comparative
- most vagrant — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Wandering from place to place, particularly when without any settled employment or habitation.

   **Example:** a vagrant beggar

   **Source synonyms:** itinerant; nomadic; peripatetic; vagabond; vagrom (obsolete); vague

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Of or pertaining to a vagabond or vagrant, or a person fond of wandering.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. Moving without a certain direction; roving, wandering; also, erratic, unsettled.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Source synonyms:** inconstant; straggling; straying; vagabond; vagrom (obsolete); vague; wayward
