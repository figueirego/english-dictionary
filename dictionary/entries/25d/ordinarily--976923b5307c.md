### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In accordance with normal custom or routine; as a matter of established occurrence.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; obsolete

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Usually or as a general rule; commonly.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Ordinarily I go to the match on a Saturday.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. In the usual manner; unexceptionally.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The man was ordinarily dressed.
