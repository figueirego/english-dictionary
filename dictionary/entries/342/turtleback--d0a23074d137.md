## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Anything having the shape of a turtle's back (that is, its shell).

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A convex deck at the bow or stern of a vessel, designed to shed seawater quickly.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An armor layout with an armored deck which slopes downwards towards the sides of the ship and connects to the lower edge of the main belt armor, designed to deflect shells striking the ship on trajectories close to horizontal.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; nautical; politics; transport; war

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A primitive stone celt of a form suggesting the back of a turtle.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A library binding of a mass market paperback with a generic hardcover.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Any plant of the genus Psathyrotes of annual and perennial forbs and low subshrubs native to dry areas of southwestern North America.
