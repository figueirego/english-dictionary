## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /tʌft/ — source labels: Canada; General-American; Received-Pronunciation
- IPA: /tɐft/ — source labels: Australia; New-Zealand

### Forms

- tufts — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A bunch of feathers, grass or hair, etc., held together at the base.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A cluster of threads drawn tightly through upholstery, a mattress or a quilt, etc., to secure and strengthen the padding.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A small clump of trees or bushes.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A gold tassel on the cap worn by titled undergraduates at English universities.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A person entitled to wear such a tassel.

   **Usage:** historical
