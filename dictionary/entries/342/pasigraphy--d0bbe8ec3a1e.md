### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A writing system where concepts are written instead of words. The system is not phonetic and uses ideograms, such as mathematical symbols. It is intended to be universally understood.

   **Example:** He spent his spare time on the weekend perfecting his very own pasigraphy.
