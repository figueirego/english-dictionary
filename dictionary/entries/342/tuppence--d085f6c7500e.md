### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Two pence (in pre- or post-decimalisation currency).

   **Usage:** Ireland; UK; countable; dated; informal; uncountable

   **Example:** Milk has gone up to tuppence ha’penny a pint.

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Ellipsis of tuppence worth (“one’s opinion”).

   **Usage:** Ireland; UK; abbreviation; alt-of; countable; ellipsis; idiomatic; uncountable
