## Verb · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To be divided into smaller groups or separate individuals, especially to go in different directions.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The soldiers split up into smaller squadrons to search the building.

   **Example:** For this activity, everyone needs to split up into pairs.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a group of people

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To cease to be in a relationship or marriage.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** After she left to go travelling, my girlfriend and I split up.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a romantic couple

   **Source synonyms:** separate; split; break up

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To separate two or more people.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The brothers never behaved in class when they were together, so we had to split them up for the exam.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To divide into smaller parts.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Let's split the project up into bite-sized tasks.

   **Source synonyms:** split; break up; break down
