### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To press lightly in a repetitive motion with a soft object without rubbing.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I dabbed my face with a towel.

   **Example:** Dab the tears from your eyes.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To apply a substance in this way.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The nurse will dab some ointment on that gash.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To strike by a thrust; to hit with a sudden blow or thrust.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To apply hash oil to a heated surface for the purpose of efficient combustion.

   **Usage:** slang

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To perform the dab dance move; to move both arms, parallel with one's head, to either side of the body.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** dance; dancing; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Synonym of daub (to mark a bingo card)
