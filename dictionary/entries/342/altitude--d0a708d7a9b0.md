### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The absolute height of a location, usually measured from sea level.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** As the altitude increases, the temperature gets lower, so remember to bring warm clothes to the mountains.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A vertical distance.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The line perpendicularly connecting a figure’s vertex, especially a triangle’s apex, to the side opposite to the vertex.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** All three altitudes of any triangle will always meet at point known as the orthocentre or orthocenter.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The length of such a line; the distance measured perpendicularly from a figure's vertex to the side opposite to the vertex.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The perpendicular height of a triangle is known as its altitude.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The angular distance of a heavenly body above our Earth's horizon.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astronomy; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
