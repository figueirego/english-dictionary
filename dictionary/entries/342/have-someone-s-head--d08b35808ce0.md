### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To punish someone severely, as by sacking (firing), imprisonment, or similarly harsh penalties.

   **Usage:** figuratively; idiomatic

   **Example:** The boss will have his head for losing the biggest customer over a mistake.

   **Source synonyms:** have someone's guts for garters; have someone's hide; tan someone's hide; amerce; castigate (formal); chasten (archaic); chastise; come down on; correct; fix someone's wagon (idiomatic); have someone's guts for garters (Commonwealth; idiomatic); have someone's head (figuratively; idiomatic); have someone's hide (figuratively; idiomatic); give it to someone; give someone what for (idiomatic; slang); give what for (idiomatic); gruel; malavogue (Ireland; dated); nail someone to the wall; penalize; penance; punish; tan someone's hide (figuratively; idiomatic); tar out (dated; slang); teach someone a lesson (idiomatic)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To kill somebody, especially with violence and especially by decapitation.

   **Usage:** literal

   **Example:** The tyrant would have their heads for their temerity.
