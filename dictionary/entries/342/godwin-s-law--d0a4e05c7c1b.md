### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An adage that asserts that any Usenet discussion will eventually mention the Nazis or Adolf Hitler.

   **Usage:** Internet; humorous; usually

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An adage that asserts that the first person in an argument (usually in an online comment thread or forum) to mention the Nazis or Adolf Hitler has lost.

   **Usage:** Internet; humorous; usually
