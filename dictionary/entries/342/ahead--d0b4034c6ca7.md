## Adverb

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /əˈhɛd/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. At or towards the front; in the direction one is facing or moving.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The island was directly ahead.

   **Example:** Just ahead you can see the cliffs.

   **Example:** Keep going straight ahead.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. So as to be further advanced, either spatially or in an abstract sense; to be superior.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** He finished two laps ahead of me.

   **Example:** In all of his classes Jack was ahead.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. In or for the future.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** There may be tough times ahead.

   **Example:** You've got to think ahead so as not to be unprepared.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To a later time.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Set the clock ahead an hour.

   **Example:** Push the deadline ahead a day, from the 20th to the 21st.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. At an earlier time; beforehand; in advance.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** He paid his rent ahead.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To an earlier time.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Push the deadline ahead a day, from the 21st to the 20th.
