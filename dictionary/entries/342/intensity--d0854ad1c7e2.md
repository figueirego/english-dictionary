### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The quality of being intense.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** intension (formal)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The degree of strength.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Time-averaged energy flux (the ratio of average power to the area through which the power "flows"); irradiance.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any of radiant intensity, luminous intensity or irradiance.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; natural-sciences; optics; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Synonym of radiance.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astronomy; natural-sciences

   **Source synonyms:** radiance (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The severity of an earthquake in terms of its effects on the earth's surface, and buildings. The value depends on the distance from the epicentre, and is not to be confused with the magnitude.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; geology; natural-sciences
