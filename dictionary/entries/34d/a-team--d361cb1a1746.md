### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The best of a set of teams in a single club.

   **Example:** The A team are playing away this Saturday.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A group or team which performs its work most effectively; a very good team.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** It looks like the A team are on duty tonight - I've never seen the hall cleared so quickly!

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A country's reserve team or development squad, comprising the best players not selected in the national team.

   **Example:** NSW all-rounder Moises Henriques has been named captain of the Australia A team to play England in Hobart next week.
