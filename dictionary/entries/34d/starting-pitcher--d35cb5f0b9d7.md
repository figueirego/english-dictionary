### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A pitcher who is usually used to start a game; a member of a team's rotation. Compare relief pitcher.

   **Example:** That team has the best starting pitchers in the league.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; baseball; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A pitcher who is recorded to begin the game.

   **Example:** The starting pitcher for today's game has not been announced yet.
