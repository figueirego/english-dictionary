### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A very rare, disabling genetic disorder of the connective tissue in which fibrous tissue (including ligaments, muscles, and tendons) ossifies (“transforms into bone”) when damaged.

   **Usage:** uncountable; medical genetics

   **Source synonyms:** Münchmeyer disease; myositis ossificans progressiva; stone man syndrome
