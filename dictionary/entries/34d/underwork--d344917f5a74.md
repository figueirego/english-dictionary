### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To require too little work from; to work insufficiently.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To work or operate in secret or clandestinely.

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To do less work than necessary (on).

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** to underwork a painting

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To do work for inadequate payment.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To injure by working secretly; to destroy or overthrow by clandestine measure; to undermine.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To do similar work for a lesser price than; to undercut.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** One mason may underwork another.
