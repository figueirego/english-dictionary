## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ɹɪˈmeɪnz/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The body or any of its matter that are left after a person (or any organism) dies; a corpse.

   **Usage:** plural; plural-only

   **Example:** They buried the remains of their longtime friend in the town cemetery.

   **Example:** The victim's remains were one small piece of bone.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Historical or archaeological relics.

   **Usage:** plural; plural-only

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The extant writings of a deceased person.

   **Usage:** plural; plural-only

   **Example:** To his great intellectual powers his published remains bear abundant witness.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. All that is left of the stock of some things; remnants.

   **Usage:** plural; plural-only

   **Example:** He couldn't bring himself to eat the remains of the chicken dinner.
