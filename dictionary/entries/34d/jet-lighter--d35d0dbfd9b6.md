### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A lighter that produces a powerful, wind-resistant, high-temperature blue flame by forcing butane gas through a narrow nozzle, mixing it with air for intense, concentrated combustion, unlike the softer, yellow flame of a regular lighter.

   **Source synonyms:** torch lighter; butane torch
