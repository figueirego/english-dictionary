### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To depose; to forcibly relieve a monarch of the monarchy.

   **Source synonyms:** depose; discrown; disenthrone; uncrown; unking; unmonarch; unqueen; unsceptre; unthrone

   **Source antonyms:** coronate; crown; enthrone; king; queen

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To remove any governing authority from power.

   **Source synonyms:** bring down; depose; divest; overthrow

   **Source antonyms:** empower; install; invest; take office

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To remove from any position of high status or power.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To remove (something) from a position of power or paramount importance.

   **Usage:** figuratively
