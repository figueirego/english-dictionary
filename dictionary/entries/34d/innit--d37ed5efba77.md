### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Contraction of isn't + it.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; colloquial; contraction; dialectal

   **Example:** Innit your birthday today?

   **Example:** It's your birthday today, innit?

   **Source synonyms:** ain't it (slang)

   **Alternative form of:** [isn't](../0b7/isn-t--2dd6dad08f7e.md)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used as a replacement for any other negative tag question, irrespective of person, number, or verb. May precede the statement.

   **Usage:** Multicultural-London-English; UK; colloquial; contraction; proscribed

   **Example:** Innit dolphins are a type of whale?

   **Example:** You were dissing my girl, innit?
