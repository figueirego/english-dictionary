### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To be accountable or responsible to; to account to; to report to.

   **Example:** I'm above you in the hierarchy, Jim, so you should be answering to me.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To justify oneself to (someone). (usually with have to)

   **Example:** You're going to have to answer to all of these charges to my satisfaction.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To respond to (a name); to treat as one's own name.

   **Example:** She's answered to Katie her entire life.
