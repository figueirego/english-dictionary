**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In legislative assemblies that permit unlimited debate (that is, a filibuster)&#58; a motion, procedure or rule by which debate is ended so that a vote may be taken on the matter. For example, in the United States Senate, a three-fifths majority vote of the body is required to invoke cloture and terminate debate.
