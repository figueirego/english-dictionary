### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A construction worker specializing in fabricating and erecting steel components

   **Example:** The steel workers and electricians went on strike, halting construction.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person involved in the fabrication of steel itself

   **Usage:** dated

   **Example:** The steel worker joined the United Steelworkers soon after starting work at the mill.
