## Noun

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The state or quality of being mortal.
   1. The state of being susceptible to death.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source antonyms:** immortality

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The state or quality of being mortal.
   1. The quality of being punishable by death.

      **Usage:** archaic; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The state or quality of being mortal.
   1. The quality of causing death.

      **Usage:** archaic; countable; uncountable

      **Source synonyms:** deadliness; lethality

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The number of deaths; and, usually and especially, the number of deaths per time unit (usually per year), expressed as a rate.
   1. Deaths resulting from an event (such as a war, epidemic or disaster).

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The number of deaths; and, usually and especially, the number of deaths per time unit (usually per year), expressed as a rate.
   1. The number of deaths per given unit of population over a given period of time.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; business; demographics; demography; ecology; insurance; natural-sciences
