### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A discipline of snowboarding and parasnowboarding, competing on a twisty curvy course with banked turns. The course is similar to that found in snowboardcross, except smoothed out and without the notch jumps or stepwise rhythm sections. Also unlike snowboardcross, competitors run separately instead of jointly in a heat.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** countable
