### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A hard variegated wood from a tree species of Brazil and the West Indies (Andira inermis), used in cabinet-work.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>
