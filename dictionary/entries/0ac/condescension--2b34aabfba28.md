### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of condescending; a manner of behaving in an outwardly polite way that nevertheless implies superiority to another; patronizing courtesy toward inferiors.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** Conscious condescension breeds panderers and enemies, not friends.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A patronizing attitude or behavior.

   **Usage:** derogatory; uncountable; usually

   **Source synonyms:** patronage

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Courtesy, affability of a superior towards an inferior.

   **Usage:** archaic; uncountable; usually; non-derogatory

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** condescendence — the act of condescending
