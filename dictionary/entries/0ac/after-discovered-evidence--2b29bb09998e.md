## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Evidence discovered after a legal decision has been reached. A party may attempt to have the decision reversed using this evidence. They must first prove that the evidence could not have been discovered until after the decision was reached.

   **Usage:** US; uncountable
