### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who participates in entertainment events in a very enthusiastic manner and who has a leading role in inspiring others to join in the spirit of festivity.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Source synonyms:** life and soul of the party; party animal
