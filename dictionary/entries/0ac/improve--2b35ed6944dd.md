**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make (something) better; to increase the value or productivity (of something).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Painting the woodwork will improve this house.

   **Example:** Buying more servers would improve performance.

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To become better.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** I have improved since taking the tablets.

   **Example:** The error messages have improved since the last version, when they were incomprehensible.

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To use or employ to good purpose; to turn to profitable account.

   **Usage:** dated

   **Example:** to improve one's time; to improve his means
