### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To resemble or have the appearance of being; to seem to be.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** It looks like rain later today.

   **Example:** It looks like it will rain later today.

   **Example:** It looks like I'm stuck with you.

   **Example:** Ostriches look like emus to some people, but they are only distantly related.

   **Example:** You wake up on what looks like Salisbury Plain, but in Africa.

   **Example:** The port does not look like much from the ground.

   **Example:** (UK) He always looks like scoring a goal if not two.
