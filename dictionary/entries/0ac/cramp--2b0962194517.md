## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /kɹæmp/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Forms

- cramps — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A painful contraction of a muscle which cannot be controlled; (sometimes) a similar pain even without noticeable contraction.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** He retired hurt at 31 due to a leg cramp.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. That which confines or contracts.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** restraint; shackle; hindrance

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A clamp for carpentry or masonry.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A piece of wood having a curve corresponding to that of the upper part of the instep, on which the upper leather of a boot is stretched to give it the requisite shape.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

### Forms

- cramped — participle; past
- cramped — past

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. (of a muscle) To contract painfully and uncontrollably.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To affect with cramps or spasms.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To prohibit movement or expression of.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** You're cramping my style.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To restrain to a specific physical position, as if with a cramp.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** You're going to need to cramp the wheels on this hill.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To fasten or hold with, or as if with, a cramp iron.

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To bind together; to unite.

   **Usage:** broadly

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

**Source sense R2.S7**

7. To form on a cramp.

   **Example:** to cramp boot legs
