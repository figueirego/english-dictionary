## Adjective · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Intended to be done, used, consumed etc. while sitting.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** It's nice to have a proper sit-down meal, rather than just a sandwich on the go.

## Noun · Record 2

### Forms

- sit-downs — plural

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. An act of sitting down, sometimes with other people in some form of social exchange.

   **Example:** I need a sit-down and a nice cup of tea.

   **Example:** Let's have a sit-down and talk about this properly.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. An act of sitting down, sometimes with other people in some form of social exchange.
   1. An interview.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. A sit-in, a protest of civil disobedience by people sitting and refusing to move.
