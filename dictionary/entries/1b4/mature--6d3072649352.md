**Source sense R2.S1**

   **Source synonyms:** develop; grow; [progress](../2d7/progress--b5f90a96e680.md); ripen

2. To attain maturity, to become mature or ripe.

   **Source synonyms:** ripen; ripen up

   **Usage:** transitive
