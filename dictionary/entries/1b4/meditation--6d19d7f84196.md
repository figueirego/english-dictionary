## Noun

### Pronunciation

### Forms

- meditations — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A devotional exercise of, or leading to, contemplation.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of various types of achieving more or less altered states of consciousness, such as vacancy of mind or prolonged contemplation on a single sensation or thought, through relaxed or focused mental and physical activity generally of a nonstrenuous and non–substance-induced nature.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A contemplative discourse, often on a religious or philosophical subject.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Her book is less a cookbook than a meditation on the craft of cookery.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A musical theme treated in a meditative manner.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Careful and thorough thought.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** deep meditation

   **Example:** in meditation

   **Example:** He was lost in careful meditation on how best to proceed when a sudden phone call forced him to decide.

   **Source synonyms:** cogitation; contemplation; deliberation; mulling; musing; pondering; meditation; reflection; rethinking; reconsideration; rumination; thought
