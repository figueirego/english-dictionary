## Phrase

### Forms

- what's up with ... — canonical

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. What's wrong with; what's the problem or matter with. Said to express that the behaviour of someone, or operation of something, is out of the ordinary or problematic.

   **Usage:** colloquial

   **Example:** What’s up with you?

   **Example:** What the hell's up with this stupid computer now?!

   **Source context (verbatim):** in rhetorical questions or salutations

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used to express disbelief or curiosity.

   **Usage:** colloquial

   **Example:** They raised my electricity rates again. What’s up with that?

   **Example:** What’s up with all this new music they’re playing?

   **Source context (verbatim):** in rhetorical questions

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. (The thing that is) the problem or issue with.

   **Usage:** colloquial

   **Example:** I'll tell you what's up with the Government!

   **Source context (verbatim):** in statements

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** what's with; what's the deal with; what's the matter with; what's the story with; what's eating
