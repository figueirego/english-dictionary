### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A black or brownish black rock formed from prehistoric plant remains, composed largely of carbon and burned as a fuel.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** The coal in this region was prized by ironmasters in centuries past, who mined it in the spots where the drainage methods of the day permitted.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A black or brownish black rock formed from prehistoric plant remains, composed largely of carbon and burned as a fuel.
   1. A type of coal, such as bituminous, anthracite, or lignite, and grades and varieties thereof, as a fuel commodity ready to buy and burn.

      **Usage:** countable

      **Example:** Put some coal on the fire.

      **Example:** Order some coal from the coalyard.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A piece of coal used for burning (this use is less common in American English)

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** Put some coals on the fire.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A glowing or charred piece of coal, wood, or other solid fuel.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** hot coals

   **Example:** glowing coals

   **Example:** Just as the campfire died down to just coals, with no flames to burn the marshmallows, someone dumped a whole load of wood on, so I gave up and went to bed.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Charcoal.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Content of low quality.

   **Usage:** Internet; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** I'm so sick of seeing this coal online.

   **Source antonyms:** gem

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Bombs emitting black smoke on impact.

   **Usage:** countable; slang; uncountable
