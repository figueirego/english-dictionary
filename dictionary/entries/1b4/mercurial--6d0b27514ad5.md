### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of the plants known as mercury, especially the annual mercury or French mercury (Mercurialis annua).

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person born under the influence of the planet Mercury; hence, a person having an eloquent, quickwitted, furtive, or volatile character.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astrology; human-sciences; mysticism; philosophy; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A chemical compound containing mercury.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A preparation of mercury, especially as a treatment for syphilis.

   **Usage:** historical

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; pharmacology; sciences

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Having a lively or volatile character; animated, changeable, quick-witted.

   **Usage:** comparable

   **Example:** his mercurial temperament

   **Source synonyms:** fickle; unpredictable

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Pertaining to the astrological influence of the planet Mercury; having the characteristics of a person under such influence (see adjective sense 1).

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astrology; human-sciences; mysticism; philosophy; sciences

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. Pertaining to the planet Mercury.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astronomy; natural-sciences

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. Of or pertaining to the element mercury or quicksilver; containing mercury.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. Caused by the action of mercury or a mercury compound.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. Pertaining to Mercury, the Roman god of, among other things, commerce, financial gain, communication, and thieves and trickery; hence (comparable), money-making; crafty.

   **Usage:** Roman; not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; mysticism; mythology; philosophy; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** Hermesian
