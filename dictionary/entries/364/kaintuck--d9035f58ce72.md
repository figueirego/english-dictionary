### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A native or resident of Kentucky, especially one who has a rustic character.

   **Usage:** US; dialectal

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

   **Usage:** US; dialectal; obsolete
