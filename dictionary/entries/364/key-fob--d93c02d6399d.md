### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An item carried on a key ring either as decoration or for security.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A passive wireless electronic device that usually uses RFID technology to control access to buildings, containers, computers, etc. by being placed near a detector.
