### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of amassing or gathering, as into a pile.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The process of growing into a heap or a large amount.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** an accumulation of earth, of sand, of evils, of wealth, or of honors

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A mass of something piled up or collected.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The concurrence of several titles to the same proof.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The continuous growth of capital by retention of interest or savings.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** accounting; business; finance

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The action of investors buying an asset from other investors when the price of the asset is low.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance
