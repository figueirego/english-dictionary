### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Acceptance of a belief or claim as true, especially on the basis of evidence.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Based on the scientific data, I give credence to this hypothesis.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Credential or supporting material for a person or claim.

   **Usage:** rare; uncountable

   **Example:** He presented us with a letter of credence.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A small table or credenza used in certain Christian religious services.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** lifestyle; religion

   **Source synonyms:** credence table

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A cupboard, sideboard, or cabinet, particularly one intended for the display of rich vessels or plate on open shelves.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A subjective probability estimate of a belief or claim.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** My credence in the proposition is around 90%.
