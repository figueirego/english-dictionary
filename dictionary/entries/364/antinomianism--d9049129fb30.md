### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The belief or teaching that because only the spiritual "law of faith" (Romans 3&#58;27) is essential for salvation, obedience to any practical or moral law has no role to play, even as a guide to conduct or as a test of the genuineness of faith.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity

   **Source synonyms:** antinomism; solifidianism; w&#58;sola fide

   **Source antonyms:** legalism; nullifidianism

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Opposition to the Torah.

   **Usage:** Judaism; countable; uncountable
