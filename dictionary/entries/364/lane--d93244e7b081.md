### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A road, street, or similar thoroughfare.

   **Example:** Penny Lane

   **Source context (verbatim):** used in street names

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A narrow passageway between fences, walls, hedges or trees.

   **Example:** There's a shortcut to the shops through this leafy lane.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A narrow road, as in the country.
