### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An amber-coloured traffic light indicating that vehicles should stop unless it is unsafe to do so, approximately equivalent to the yellow light in the US.

   **Usage:** UK

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Hesitance to proceed; limited approval or permission to proceed.

   **Usage:** UK; idiomatic

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A yellow light.

   **Usage:** US; dated
