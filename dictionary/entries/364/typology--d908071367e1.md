**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The systematic classification of the types of something according to their common characteristics.
   1. Classification of artefacts according to their characteristics.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** archaeology; history; human-sciences; sciences
