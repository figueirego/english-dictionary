### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A pair of games played one after another between the same teams.

   **Example:** This afternoon we will have a double-header between the Bears and the Wolves, games at 3pm and 7&#58;30pm.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A pair of sporting events, one after the other, at a single venue.

   **Example:** The NRL season opener at the Olympic stadium will be a double-header&#58; Dragons vs Eels and Souths vs Wests.

   **Example:** The next jazz double-header at The Sound Lounge will feature the Leonie Cohen Trio and the Sam Hawkins Quartet
