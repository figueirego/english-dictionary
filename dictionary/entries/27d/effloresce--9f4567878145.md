### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To burst into bloom; to flower.

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete except figuratively

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of something hidden&#58; to come forth, to emerge; also, to reach full glory or power.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Senses relating to chemistry.
   1. Of a substance&#58; to change from being crystalline to powdery by losing water of crystallization.

      **Usage:** intransitive

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Senses relating to chemistry.
   1. Of a salt&#58; to seep through some material (bricks, concrete, earth, rock, etc.) in a dissolved state, and then crystallize on a surface in a powdery form.

      **Usage:** intransitive

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Senses relating to chemistry.
   1. Of the surface of a material&#58; to become covered with a powdery salt (as described in sense 3.2).

      **Usage:** intransitive

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences
