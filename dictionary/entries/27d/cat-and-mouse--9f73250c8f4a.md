## Noun · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A relationship in which two parties closely monitor and challenge one another in a suspicious or self-protective manner, often because each party is attempting to gain an advantage over the other.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** The Allied and the enemy submarine played a game of cat and mouse, checking up on each other's whereabouts and strategies.

   **Example:** The hackers played a cat and mouse game with the system administrators&#58; the hackers kept trying new tricks, and the system administrators kept mounting electronic defenses.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A form of attack in which the attacker toys with the victim by inflicting minor damage or letting the victim have futile hopes of escape before finally finishing the victim off.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A children's game in which one player (the "cat") attempts to break through a ring of players to catch another player (the "mouse") in the centre.

   **Usage:** uncountable
