## Noun

### Forms

- navelworts — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Certain plants with features reminiscent of a human navel.
   1. Umbilicus spp., especially Umbilicus rupestris), a European perennial succulent herb with round, peltate leaves with a central depression.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Certain plants with features reminiscent of a human navel.
   1. Omphalodes spp., with seeds reminiscent of a navel.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Certain plants with features reminiscent of a human navel.
   1. Marsh pennywort, herbs of genera Hydrocotyle or Centella.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable
