### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To regard (someone or something) as horrifying or detestable; to feel great repugnance toward.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I absolutely abhor being stuck in traffic jams.

   **Source synonyms:** detest; disdain; loathe

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To fill with horror or disgust.

   **Usage:** impersonal; obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To turn aside or avoid; to keep away from; to reject.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To protest against; to reject solemnly.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive; canon law

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To feel horror, disgust, or dislike (towards); to be contrary or averse (to); construed with from.

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Differ entirely from.

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete
