### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To know how to.

   **Usage:** auxiliary; defective

   **Example:** She can speak English, French, and German.

   **Example:** I can play football.

   **Source antonyms:** cannot; can't

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To be able to.

   **Usage:** auxiliary; defective

   **Example:** Can you remember your fifth birthday?

   **Example:** Animals can experience emotions.

   **Example:** Can you hear that?

   **Example:** I can feel the baby moving inside me.

   **Source synonyms:** be able to

   **Source antonyms:** cannot; can't

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. May; to be permitted or enabled to.

   **Usage:** auxiliary; defective; informal; modal

   **Example:** You can go outside and play when you're finished with your homework.

   **Example:** Can I use your pen?

   **Source synonyms:** may

   **Source antonyms:** cannot; can't; may not; mayn't

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To have the potential to; to be possible for (someone or something) to.

   **Usage:** auxiliary; defective; modal

   **Example:** Can it be Friday already?

   **Example:** You can not only learn English here, you can also practice with native speakers.

   **Example:** Teenagers can really try their parents' patience.

   **Source antonyms:** cannot; can't

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Used to form requests, typically polite.

   **Usage:** auxiliary; defective; informal; modal

   **Example:** Can you please come over here?

   **Example:** Can we try that scene again?

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To know.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** cognize; grok; ken

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To be (followed by a word like able, possible, allowed). third-person singular simple present indicative of can

   **Usage:** India; nonstandard; proscribed

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To be able to or know how to (do something); an accompanying verb is not required if it is already inferable from context.

   **Usage:** Manglish; Singlish; auxiliary; intransitive

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To be fine or acceptable; to be possible; (with liao or already) to be enough. Often used in conjunction with a variety of clause-final particles, e.g., lah, meh or one, to express different attitudes towards the subject matter.

   **Usage:** Manglish; Singlish; intransitive
