### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A semiaquatic monotreme from eastern Australia with a bill resembling that of a duck, that has a mole-like body, a tail resembling that of a beaver, a waterproof pelt, and flat webbed feet (Ornithorhynchus anatinus).

   **Source synonyms:** duck-billed platypus; duckbill; duckmole; mallangong; tambreet; water mole
