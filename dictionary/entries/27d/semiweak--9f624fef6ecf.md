### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Usage:** not-comparable; Data Encryption Standard

   **Example:** There are 2⁵⁶ (7.21 × 10¹⁶, about 72 quadrillion) possible keys for DES, of which four are weak and twelve are semiweak.
