### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To become aware of, understand, or appreciate (a fact or situation, especially something which has been true for some time).

   **Usage:** US; transitive

   **Example:** He realized that he had left his umbrella on the train.

   **Example:** She desperately yelled at her young daughter, frantic to make her realize what she had done.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To sense (something) strongly or vividly as if real.

   **Usage:** US; transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To cause (something) to seem real; to make realistic; specifically, to present (something) clearly to the mind, a person, (archaic) oneself, etc., so that it seems real.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source antonyms:** derealize

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To convert (something imaginary or planned, as a goal or idea) into reality; to bring into real existence, to make real.

   **Usage:** transitive; chiefly passive voice; slightly formal; chiefly passive voice; slightly formal

   **Example:** The objectives of the project were never fully realized.

   **Source synonyms:** implement; execute; actualize; materialize; embody; accomplish

   **Source antonyms:** disrealize

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To achieve (one's) potential.

   **Usage:** reflexive; transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To convert (an asset or property, especially investments such as bonds, shares, etc.) into a more easily usable form such as money, especially by selling the asset or property.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** By realizing the company’s assets, the liquidator was able to return most of the shareholders’ investments.

   **Example:** Profits from the investment can be realized at any time by selling the shares.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To acquire (money, a profit, etc.) by selling an asset or property, through trade, etc.; also (followed by on), to make (money or a profit) on an investment, a venture, etc.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to realize large profits from a speculation

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Of an asset or property&#58; to generate (a specific amount of money or interest) when invested or sold.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To turn (an abstract linguistic object, especially a phoneme) into a speech sound actually used in a language.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The southern /v/ is realized as the voiced approximant \[ʋ\].

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. To obtain an entity from (an abstract group or structure).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. To arrange (a musical work written for a single performer) to be performed by an orchestra; to orchestrate.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

<a id="r1-s12"></a>

**Source sense R1.S12**

12. To complete (a musical work which is incomplete or not fully notated).

   **Usage:** transitive

**Source sense R1.S13**
