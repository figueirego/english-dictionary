### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To appropriate first.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He preempted that hill to be its king.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To displace or take the place of (by having higher precedence, etc).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Floppy disks were preempted by CDs.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To prevent or beat to the punch, to forestall an expected occurrence by acting first.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To secure (land, etc.) by the right of preemption (purchasing before others, e.g. land because one already occupies it).

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To make a preemptive bid at bridge.

   **Usage:** intransitive
