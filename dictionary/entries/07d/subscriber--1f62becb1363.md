### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who subscribes to a publication or a service.

   **Example:** Our magazine has over ten thousand subscribers.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A system or component that subscribes to something, such as an event, made available by a publisher.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; programming; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>
