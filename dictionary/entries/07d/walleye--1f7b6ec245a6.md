### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One or a pair of sideways-looking misaligned eyes.

   **Usage:** plural "walleyes"

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An unusually pale eye.

   **Usage:** plural "walleyes"

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A gamefish of species Sander vitreus, native to the Northern U.S. and Canada with pale, reflective eyes.
