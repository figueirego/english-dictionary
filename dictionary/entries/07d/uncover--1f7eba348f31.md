### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To remove a cover from.

   **Example:** The model railway was uncovered.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To reveal the identity of.

   **Example:** The murderer has finally been uncovered.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To show openly; to disclose; to reveal.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To remove one's hat or cap as a mark of respect.

   **Usage:** intransitive; reflexive
