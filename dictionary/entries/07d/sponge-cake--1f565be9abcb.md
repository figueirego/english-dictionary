### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A light, soft, baked dessert (commonly layered with cream and jam) that is typically made with flour, sugar, baking powder and eggs, and has a firm, yet well aerated structure, similar to that of a sea sponge.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** I made a sponge cake the other day. It was a disaster&#58; I forgot the baking powder! It didn't rise!

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Something that is simple or very easy.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; informal; uncommon; uncountable
