<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A system of government-imposed duties levied on imported or exported goods; a list of such duties, or the duties themselves.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A schedule of rates, fees or prices.
