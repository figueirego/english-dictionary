### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A worded or expressed sentence, phrase, or only a word on its own, which asks for information, a reply, or a response; an interrogative.

   **Example:** What is your question?

   **Source synonyms:** inquiry; enquiry; query; interrogation

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A subject or topic for consideration or investigation.

   **Example:** The question of seniority will be discussed at the meeting.

   **Example:** There was a question of which material to use.

   **Example:** I've ordered it all, but there’s still the question of who’s going to pay.

   **Source synonyms:** subject; topic; problem; consideration; proposition

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A doubt or challenge about the truth, accuracy, or validity of a matter.

   **Example:** His claim to the property has come under question.

   **Example:** The story is true beyond question.

   **Example:** He obeyed without question.

   **Example:** There was no question of his blaming me for his frustration.

   **Source synonyms:** issue; doubt

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A proposal to a meeting as a topic for deliberation.

   **Example:** I move that the question be put to a vote.

   **Source synonyms:** proposal

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Interrogation by torture.

   **Usage:** archaic; historical; with-definite-article

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Talk; conversation; speech.

   **Usage:** obsolete
