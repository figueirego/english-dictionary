### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A flexible strip attached to the bottom or side of a door to prevent draughts entering through the gap.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any other object used to prevent draughts, such as a chimney balloon.
