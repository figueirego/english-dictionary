### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Not cardiac.
   1. Not in or related to the heart.

      **Usage:** not-comparable; usually

      **Example:** There has been a decline in noncardiac deaths this year.

      **Source synonyms:** nonheart
