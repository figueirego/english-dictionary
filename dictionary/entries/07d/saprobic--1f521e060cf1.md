**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of, pertaining to, or of the nature of a saprobe or saprobes; that feeds on dead or decaying organic matter.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences
