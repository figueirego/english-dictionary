### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To begin working time, especially by punching in (entering a workplace by punching a time card with the time clock).

   **Usage:** US

   **Example:** We clocked in at 7&#58;30, but didn't actually do any work until at least 8.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To be measured at.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** Big Joe clocks in at 384 pounds, far outweighing his opponent.
