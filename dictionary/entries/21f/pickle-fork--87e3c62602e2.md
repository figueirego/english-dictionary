### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A fork with a long handle and usually two or three tines, used for extracting pickles from a tall jar.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A long-handled tool with two prongs used to unseat ball-and-socket joints.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>
