### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of or pertaining to, or having the characteristics of, a phantasm (“something seen but having no physical reality”); imaginary, unreal.

   **Source synonyms:** phantasmatic; phantasmatical; phantasmic; phantasmical; phantomatic

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of or pertaining to, or having the characteristics of, a phantasm (“something seen but having no physical reality”); imaginary, unreal.
   1. Of or pertaining to, or having the characteristics of, a phantom (“apparition or ghost”); ghostly, spectral.

      **Source synonyms:** phantasmatic; phantasmatical; phantasmic; phantasmical; phantomatic; apparitional; ghastly; ghostish; ghostlike; ghostly; phantomic; phantomlike; phantasmal; phantasmic; spectral; spooky; wraithlike; wraithy

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of or pertaining to, or having the characteristics of, a phantasm (“something seen but having no physical reality”); imaginary, unreal.
   1. Of or pertaining to, or having the characteristics of, a phantasm (“perception or vision of a living or dead person who is not physically present, often through telepathy”).
