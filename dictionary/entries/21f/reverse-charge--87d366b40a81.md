## Phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Added to an invoice to indicate that receiver of the goods or services in another member state of European Union is responsible for paying VAT.

   **Example:** This delivery is subject to reverse charge in the country of receipt.
