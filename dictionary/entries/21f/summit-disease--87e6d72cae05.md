### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A disease (usually fungal, caused by the species complex Entomophaga grylli) that affects various species of insect, especially grasshoppers, whereby the infected individual typically climbs to the upper parts of a plant, attaches itself there and dies, at which point the infecting agent disperses itself, usually in the form of spores.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; medicine; natural-sciences; pathology; phytopathology; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** treetop disease
