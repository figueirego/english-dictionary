**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To obtain somebody's permission to skip or cancel an event that one has previously arranged with them.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I wonder if I can beg off going to the meeting that day, since it will take me an extra two hours out of my way.
