### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To be in a relationship.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** Everybody knows now that Richard and Betsy are going together.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of two people

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To accompany each other (e.g. to an event).

   **Usage:** literally

   **Example:** Should we go separate, or should we go together?

   **Source context (verbatim):** of two people

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To correspond or fit well.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** Purple and blue are two colors that go together well.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of two or more things

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To be inseparable; to go hand in hand.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** Exercise and sweat often go together.
