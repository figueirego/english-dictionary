### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The physical means or contrivances to make something (especially a public service) possible; the required equipment, infrastructure, location etc.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Transport facilities in Bangkok are not sufficient to prevent frequent traffic collapses during rush hour.

   **Example:** A wide range of facilities is available at the sports center, including a weight room and saunas.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A building or campus specially designed for a specific purpose, such as incarceration, military use, or scientific experimentation.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The fact of being easy, or easily done; absence of difficulty; simplicity.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Dexterity of speech or action; skill, talent.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The facility she shows in playing the violin is unrivalled.

   **Source synonyms:** ease; knack; ability; technique; aptitude; capacity; craft; capability; competency; competence; cunning; dexterity; ease; facility; hand; knack; list; prowess; moxie; potential; proficiency; skill; talent; tekkers (Multicultural-London-English); touch

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Clipping of credit facility.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; clipping; countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** banking; business; finance

   **Alternative form of:** [credit facility](../360/credit-facility--d83fc4287ba7.md)

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A toilet.

   **Usage:** Canada; US; countable; in-plural; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A condition of mental weakness less than idiocy, but enough to make a person easily persuaded to do something against their better interest.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; Scots law

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

   **Usage:** countable; dated; uncountable
