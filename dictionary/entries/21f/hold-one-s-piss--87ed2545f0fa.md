### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To resist the urge to urinate.

   **Usage:** vulgar

   **Example:** They say I'm a camel because I can really hold my piss on long road trips.

   **Source synonyms:** hold it; hold one's pee; hold one's water

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To wait, particularly unwillingly.

   **Usage:** vulgar

   **Example:** Hey, hold your piss, okay? You'll get your turn when your number comes up.

   **Source synonyms:** abide (obsolete); back that train up; bide (archaic; dialectal); bide one's time (idiomatic); expect (obsolete); defer; hang on (idiomatic); hold on (idiomatic); hold one's breath (idiomatic); hold one's horses (idiomatic); hold one's water (idiomatic); hold out (idiomatic); mark time; stand by (idiomatic); stay; twiddle one's thumbs (idiomatic); wait

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To hide the effects of one's drunkenness; to convincingly behave as though sober while drunk.

   **Usage:** mildly; vulgar

   **Example:** I'd had a lot of beer, but I was able to hold my piss and get through the security checks and into the club.

   **Source synonyms:** hold one's liquor; hold one's alcohol; hold one's drink
