### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A point of a connected space whose removal causes the resulting space to be disconnected.

   **Example:** Every point of a line is a cutpoint, but no point of a circle is a cutpoint.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences; topology

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
