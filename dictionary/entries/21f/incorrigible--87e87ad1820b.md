### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Defective and impossible to materially correct or set aright.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The construction flaw is incorrigible; any attempt to amend it would cause a complete collapse.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Unmanageable; impervious to correction by punishment or pain.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** an incorrigible youth

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Incurably depraved; not reformable.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** His dark soul was too incorrigible to repent, even at his execution.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Unchangeably established in a belief or habit.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Intrinsically incapable of being corrected; impossible to disprove, by its very nature.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The statement "My knee hurts" is incorrigible.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; philosophy; sciences

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a proposition or belief

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Impossible to cure.

   **Usage:** archaic; not-comparable

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a disease
