### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The complete realisation and final form of some potential concept or function; the conditions under which a potential thing becomes actualized.

   **Usage:** Aristotelian metaphysics

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The complete realisation and final form of some potential concept or function; the conditions under which a potential thing becomes actualized.
   1. In the metaphysics of Aristotle (384–322 B.C.E.) and Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz (1646–1716)&#58; a soul; a monad (Leibniz).

      **Usage:** specifically; Aristotelian metaphysics

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A particular type of motivation, need for self-determination, and inner strength directing life and growth to become all one is capable of being; the need to actualize one's beliefs; having both a personal vision and the ability to actualize that vision from within.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; philosophy; sciences
