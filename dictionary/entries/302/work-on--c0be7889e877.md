### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To apply mental or physical effort to (something) in order to shape, form, improve, investigate, solve it, etc.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** John's in the studio working on the plans for the new house.

   **Example:** I've been working on this problem for several days, but I still haven't figured it out.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To exercise influence on someone.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Leave it to me. I'll try to work on the boss to give us the day off.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see work, on.
