## Interjection

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used to express annoyance or frustration or announce that one has reached the limit of one's patience or temper.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** That does it! I'm not waiting here any longer. I'll come back tomorrow instead.

   **Source synonyms:** that tears it; that's torn it; that's it

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used to announce or assert completion of a task.

   **Usage:** idiomatic
