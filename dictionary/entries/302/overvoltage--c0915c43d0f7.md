## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

- overvoltages — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The difference between the electric potential of an electrode or cell under the passage of a current and the thermodynamic value of the electrode or cell potential in the absence of electrolysis.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

   **Source synonyms:** overpotential

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The hazardous condition that occurs when the voltage in a circuit is raised above that for which it was designed.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
