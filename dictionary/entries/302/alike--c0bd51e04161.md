## Adverb · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /əˈlaɪk/ — source labels: UK; US

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. In the same manner, form, or degree; in common; equally.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** We are all alike concerned in religion.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. In the same manner, form, or degree; in common; equally.
   1. Used after a list of subjects to indicate that something applies equally to all of them.

      **Usage:** not-comparable

      **Example:** The new policy will benefit employees and customers alike.
