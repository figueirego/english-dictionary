### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of assuming, or taking to or upon oneself; the act of taking up or adopting.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** His assumption of secretarial duties was timely.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The act of taking for granted, or supposing a thing without proof; a supposition; an unwarrantable claim.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Their assumption of his guilt disqualified them from jury duty.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The thing supposed; a postulate, or proposition assumed; a supposition.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The minor or second proposition in a categorical syllogism.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; logic; mathematics; philosophy; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The taking of a person up into heaven.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A festival in honor of the ascent of the Virgin Mary into heaven, celebrated on 15 August.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Assumptio.

   **Usage:** countable; rhetoric; uncountable
