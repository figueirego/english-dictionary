## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A retailer that offers a wider selection of merchandise in a category than existing retailers, at lower prices, thereby gaining substantial share of the market and "killing" the existing retailers.

   **Usage:** US

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A speedrun so fast that beating it is considered unattainable, thereby "killing" the category it became the record for.
