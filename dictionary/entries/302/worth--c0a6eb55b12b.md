### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having a value of; proper to be exchanged for.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** How much / What is your house worth? - Now it's worth half what I paid for it. So it'd sure would be worthwhile to repair before putting it for sale.

   **Example:** Cleanliness is a virtue worth more than others.

   **Example:** A painting once thought to be worth thousands that is actually not worth much.

   **Example:** Is it well worth visiting your hometown? -Nah, Leicester is not worth it at all.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Deserving of.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** This rickety beater of a car isn’t worth repairing anymore.

   **Example:** He found going to the Edinburgh Castle was worth it.

   **Example:** I think you’ll find my proposal worth your attention.

   **Example:** I didn't think it worth complaining about his friendship not being worth having.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Valuable, worthwhile.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; obsolete; except in Scots

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Making a fair equivalent of, repaying or compensating.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** This job is hardly worth the effort.
