### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To take away a natural characteristic or inherent property of (a thing or a person).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** denaturalize

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To add something to (alcohol) that makes it unsuitable for consumption but leaves it suitable for most other purposes.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** While you cannot drink denatured alcohol, you can still use it to remove sticker glue from most surfaces.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To alter its original form or state, especially of a protein, by heat, acidity etc.

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biochemistry; biology; chemistry; microbiology; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

   **Source antonyms:** renature

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Of double-stranded DNA, specifically to split into two single strands.

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biochemistry; biology; chemistry; microbiology; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

   **Source antonyms:** renature

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To combine fissile material with nonfissile material in order to prevent its use in an atomic weapon.

   **Usage:** transitive; usually; nuclear physics
