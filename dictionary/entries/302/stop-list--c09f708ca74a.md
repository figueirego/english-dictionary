### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A list of words or other data items which, for some special reason, should be ignored or bypassed by a particular data processing operation.

   **Example:** If you equip your search engine with a stop list containing a few common words such as "a", "the", and "and", you can decrease the full-text index size by about 20%.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A list of people who subscribed to a publication (e.g. newspaper) but no longer wish to receive it.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** media

**Source sense R1.S3**
