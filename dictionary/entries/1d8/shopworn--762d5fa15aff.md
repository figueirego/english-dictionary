### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of a product in a retail store&#58; damaged from being on display and handled by customers, etc.; shop-soiled.

   **Example:** The handbag looks nice, but shopworn.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Not fresh or new.
   1. Of an idea, a piece or writing, etc.&#58; repeated so often as to have become uninteresting; clichéd, overused, tired.

      **Usage:** figuratively

      **Source synonyms:** shop-soiled; timeworn; banal; bathetic; bromidic; cheesy; stock; clichéd; commonplace; corny; dog-eared; hackneyed; hoary; overdone; overused; overworked; pat; played out; shopworn; stale; threadbare (figuratively); timeworn; tired; trite; warmed-over; well-worn
