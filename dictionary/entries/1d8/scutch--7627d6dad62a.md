**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A wooden implement shaped like a large knife used to separate the valuable fibres of flax or hemp by beating them and scraping from it the woody or coarse portions.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source synonyms:** scutcher; swingle

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**
