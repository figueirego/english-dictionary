### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An often-quoted saying that is supposed to be meaningful but has become unoriginal or hackneyed through overuse.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source synonyms:** cliché; byword; saying; adage; saw (archaic); proverb; maxim; apothegm; apophthegm; dictum; gnome; gnomic saying; mantra; paroemia

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A claim that is trivially true, to the point of being uninteresting.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source synonyms:** truism

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Flatness; lack of change, activity, or deviation.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Unoriginality; triteness.

   **Usage:** uncountable
