### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The latter part of life, when one is an older adult; especially, the years from age 65 onward.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** People often become hard of hearing in old age.

   **Example:** A ripe old age.

   **Source synonyms:** golden years; codgerhood; eld

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A cause of death representing natural death through the expected accumulation of health problems in advanced age.

   **Usage:** uncountable
