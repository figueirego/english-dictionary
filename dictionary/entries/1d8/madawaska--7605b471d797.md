## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A river that flows from Lake Témiscouata in Quebec to join the Saint John River at Edmundston, New Brunswick.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A river in the Saint Lawrence River drainage basin in Ontario, Canada.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. (full name&#58; Republic of Madawaska) An unrecognized state in what is now the northwestern corner of Madawaska County, New Brunswick, and adjacent areas of Aroostook County in the US state of Maine and of Quebec.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A community within the township of South Algonquin in Ontario, Canada.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A town in Aroostook County, Maine, United States, across the St. John River from Canada, opposite the Madawaska River at Edmundston.
