### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Pertaining to a trophy or to trophies.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; obsolete

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Adorned with trophies.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; obsolete

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Erected without Senatical grant by a prevailing general as a trophy (or tropæum) commemorating a battle in which he was victorious; compare triumphal.

   **Usage:** Roman; historical; not-comparable

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a monument or memorial

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Exhibited as a trophy of victory in war.

   **Usage:** not-comparable
