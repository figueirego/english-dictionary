## Adjective · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Pronunciation

### Forms

- more summary — comparative
- most summary — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Concise, brief, or presented in a condensed form; presenting information in such a form.

   **Example:** A summary review is in the appendix.

   **Source synonyms:** summative; summarizing\[participial adjective\|part. adj.\]\]&gt;; abstractive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Performed speedily, without formal ceremony, and (especially) without regard to legality.

   **Example:** They used summary executions to break the resistance of the people.

   **Source synonyms:** extrajudicial

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Performed by omitting the procedures of a full trial, but within a legally valid framework.

   **Example:** The matter was brought to a close by summary dismissal of the cases.

   **Example:** The summary executions caused outrage.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law
