## Prep\_phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In recent history, in recorded history amongst the lifespan of extant people; events or situations which can be remembered by people that are still alive.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** The Roberts Court is the most politically conservative in living memory.

   **Example:** Last summer, Georgia was hit by the mightiest heat wave in living memory.

   **Example:** Long believed to be extinct, the purple-bellied speckled turtle was sighted for the first time in living memory in a remote pasture near Chicago.
