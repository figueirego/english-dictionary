### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A telephone call to awaken someone at a certain time, especially one requested by the person while staying at a hotel.

   **Usage:** US; literally

   **Example:** She requested a five a.m. wake-up call from the front desk.

   **Source synonyms:** alarm call; morning call

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An alert, reminder, or call to action caused by a dramatic event.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** The recent deaths should serve as a wake-up call to others at risk.

   **Source synonyms:** reality check
