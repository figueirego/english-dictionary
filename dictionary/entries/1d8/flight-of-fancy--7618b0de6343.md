### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An idea, narrative, suggestion, etc., which is extremely imaginative and which appears to be entirely unrealistic, untrue, or impractical; thinking which is very speculative.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Source synonyms:** daydream; reverie
