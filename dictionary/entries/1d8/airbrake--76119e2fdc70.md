### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A brake (slowing device) that is operated pneumatically (by air pressure).

   **Example:** The powerful airbrake of the truck quickly brought it to a stop.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A movable control surface that extends to increase drag without affecting lift (unlike a spoiler); a device that provides aerodynamic braking.

   **Example:** The BAe 146 and Fokker 100's tailcone-mounted airbrakes allow them to slow down quickly for steep approaches without experiencing the lift degradation seen with spoiler-only aircraft.
