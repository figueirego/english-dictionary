## Noun

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈaʊtˌpoʊst/ — source labels: US

### Forms

- outposts — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A military post stationed at a distance from the main body of troops.

   **Example:** The outpost did not have enough ammunition to resist a determined assault.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The body of troops manning such a post.

   **Example:** Sgt. Smith fleeced most of the rest of the outpost of their earnings in their weekly game of craps.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A sentinel positioned outside.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An outlying settlement.

   **Example:** Beyond the border proper, there are three small outposts not officially under government protection.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A square protected by a pawn that is in or near the enemy's stronghold.
