## Noun · Record 2

### Forms

- contextualists — plural

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A proponent of contextualism, or the importance of context.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. One who believes that scalar implicatures arise from contextual inference, not from a default association with a word.

   **Example:** A contextualist believes that the phrase "some eels are fish" is primarily interpreted as "some, and perhaps all, eels are fish", and the pragmatic interpretation "some, but not all, eels are fish" only appears when demanded by the context.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; pragmatics; sciences

   **Source antonyms:** defaultist
