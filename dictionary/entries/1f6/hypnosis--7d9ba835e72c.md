### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A trancelike state, artificially induced, in which a person has a heightened suggestibility, and in which suppressed memories may be experienced.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** clinical hypnosis

   **Example:** under hypnosis

   **Example:** self-hypnosis

   **Example:** She entered a state of deep hypnosis.

   **Example:** The therapy used hypnosis to treat anxiety.

   **Usage:** uncountable
