### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A dry creek or streambed, a gulch which temporarily or seasonally fills and flows (after sufficient rain).

   **Source synonyms:** wadi; nahal; wash; dry wash\[regiolect
