### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A thick sauce made from the fat or juices that come out from meat or vegetables as they are being cooked.
   1. A dark savoury sauce prepared from stock and usually meat juices; brown gravy.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable; usually

      **Example:** A roast dinner isn't complete without gravy.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A thick sauce made from the fat or juices that come out from meat or vegetables as they are being cooked.
   1. A pale sauce prepared from a roux with meat fat; a type of béchamel sauce.

      **Usage:** Southern-US; countable; uncountable; usually

      **Example:** There are few foods more Southern than biscuits and gravy.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Sauce used for pasta.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually; chiefly Italian-American

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Curry sauce.

   **Usage:** India; Singapore; uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Unearned gain; extra benefit.

   **Usage:** informal; uncountable; usually

   **Example:** The first thousand tickets and the concessions cover the venue and the band. The rest is gravy.
