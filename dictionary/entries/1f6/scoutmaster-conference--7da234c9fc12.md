## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A usually informal conference between a youth Scout and his Scoutmaster, held as a requirement for rank advancement to a specific rank, that is intended to help prepare for the Scout's board of review by evaluating the Scout in certain ways.

   **Usage:** scouting

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An informal meeting between a youth Scout and a Scoutmaster about some specific occurrence or issue.

   **Usage:** scouting
