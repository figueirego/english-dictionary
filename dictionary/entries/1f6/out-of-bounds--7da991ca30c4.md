## Prep\_phrase · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Where one is prohibited to enter.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** You can play wherever you want, but remember that the cemetery is out of bounds.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Beyond the bounds of civility, morality, or normality.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** You were out of bounds to call him a criminal.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Outside the area of play; beyond the sideline of a playing field or court.

   **Example:** The ball went out of bounds.

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** beyond the pale; no-go; off-limits
