### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To obtain (something) in exchange for money or goods.

   **Usage:** ditransitive; transitive

   **Example:** Who'd Father buy the car for? He bought it for me, not for you.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To obtain, especially by some sacrifice.

   **Usage:** ditransitive; transitive

   **Example:** I've bought material comfort by foregoing my dreams.

   **Example:** You just bought yourself an assault charge!

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To suffer consequences for (something) through being deprived of something; to pay for (something one has done).

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To bribe.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He tried to buy me with gifts, but I wouldn't give up my beliefs.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To be equivalent to in value.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The dollar doesn't buy as much as it used to.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. to accept as true; to believe

   **Usage:** informal; transitive

   **Example:** I'm not going to buy your stupid excuses anymore!

   **Source synonyms:** swallow

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To make a purchase or purchases, to treat (for a drink, meal or gift)

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** She buys for Federated.

   **Example:** Let's go out for dinner. I'm buying.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To make a bluff, usually a large one.

   **Usage:** slang; transitive

   **Example:** Smith tried to buy the pot on the river with a huge bluff.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** card-games; poker
