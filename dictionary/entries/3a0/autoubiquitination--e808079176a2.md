## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Self-ubiquitination by a ubiquitinating enzyme, usually an E3 or E4 ubiquitin ligase. This activity can be direct, as in monoubiquitin of a lysine residue on the E3 or E4 itself, or indirect, through addition of ubiquitin to an existing polyubiquitin chain that is attached to the E3 or E4.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biochemistry; biology; chemistry; microbiology; natural-sciences; physical-sciences
