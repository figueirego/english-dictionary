## Noun

### Forms

- half titles — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A page before the title page in a book, bearing the title but no further details.

   **Usage:** book printing

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An abbreviated title of a book at the head of the first page of the text, or a title of any subdivision of a book when printed in a full page.

   **Usage:** book printing

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** bastard title; fly title
