### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The movement of water horizontally beneath the land surface, usually when the soil is completely saturated.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; hydrology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
