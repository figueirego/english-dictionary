## Adjective

### Pronunciation

### Forms

- most breathtaking — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. stunningly beautiful; amazing

   **Example:** He went to the Grand Canyon and spent a week taking in the breathtaking scenery all around him.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Very surprising or shocking; to such a degree as to cause astonishment.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** breathtaking stupidity or rudeness
