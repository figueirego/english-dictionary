### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To request the help of someone, often in the form of money.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** He begged on the street corner from passers-by.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To plead with someone for help, a favor, etc.; to entreat.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I beg your pardon. I didn’t mean to cause offence.

   **Example:** He begged her to go to the prom with him.

   **Source synonyms:** supplicate

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To unwillingly provoke a negative, often violent, reaction.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The way you keep eating raw meat, you're just begging to get tapeworms.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To obviously lack or be in need of something.

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** A captivating novel that just begs for a movie adaptation

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. In the phrase beg the question&#58; to assume.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. In the phrase beg the question&#58; to raise (a question).

   **Usage:** proscribed; transitive

   **Source antonyms:** set aside

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To ask to be appointed guardian for, or to ask to have a guardian appointed for.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law
