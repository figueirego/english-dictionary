## Noun · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈmæli/ — source labels: Australia; General-American; Received-Pronunciation

### Forms

- mallees — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A type of scrubland with low-growing thick eucalypts, characteristic of certain parts of Australia.

   **Usage:** Australia

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any semi-desert region of Australia where such scrub is the predominant vegetation.

   **Usage:** Australia

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of several low-growing eucalypts characteristic of such scrubland, especially Eucalyptus dumosa, Eucalyptus oleosa, and Eucalyptus socialis.

   **Usage:** Australia

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The growth habit of certain eucalypt species that grow with multiple stems springing from an underground lignotuber, shared by species of related genera.

   **Usage:** Australia

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences
