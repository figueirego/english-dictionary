### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A block of shares of stock that is not a multiple of some number, usually 100.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A set of leftover goods, of a quantity less than an evenly divisible shippable unit, often sold at reduced price.

   **Example:** Everard liked to shop at the stores specializing in odd lots down by the docks, as he was a cheapskate.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** economics; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** closeout; remainder

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An incomplete set or collection or assorted mix of items.

   **Usage:** broadly; figuratively
