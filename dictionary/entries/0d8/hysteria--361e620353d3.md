### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Behavior exhibiting excessive or uncontrollable emotions, in a wide range from joy to panic but usually including anxiety or fear.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A mental disorder characterized by emotional excitability etc. without an organic cause.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually; nosologically dated

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Synonym of conversion disorder.

   **Usage:** informal; uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; medicine; psychiatry; psychology; psychopathology; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** conversion disorder (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any disorder of women with some psychiatric symptoms without other diagnosis, ascribed to uterine influences on the female body, lack of pregnancy, or lack of sex.

   **Usage:** historical; obsolete; uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; medicine; psychiatry; psychology; sciences
