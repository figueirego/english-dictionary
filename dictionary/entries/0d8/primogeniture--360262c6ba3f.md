## Noun

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˌprʌɪmə(ʊ)ˈdʒɛnɪtʃə/ — source labels: Received-Pronunciation

### Forms

- primogenitures — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The state of being the firstborn of the children of the same parents.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The principle that the eldest child has an exclusive right of inheritance.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An instance of such a right of inheritance, established by custom or law.

   **Usage:** countable
