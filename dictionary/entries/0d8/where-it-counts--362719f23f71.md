## Adverb

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. in the most advantageous area or sphere

   **Example:** True, Madeline does not have a lot of book smarts, but she is plenty wise enough where it counts.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. in an effective manner

   **Example:** At first the boycott's impact was barely noticeable, but now the protesters are hurting the store where it counts.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. in the testicles

   **Usage:** euphemistic

   **Example:** A girl's best weapon — and one that should be used sparingly — is a swift kick where it counts.
