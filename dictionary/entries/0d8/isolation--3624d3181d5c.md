### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The state of being isolated, detached, or separated; the state of being away from other people.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** She lived her final year in complete isolation, not wanting to see anybody.

   **Source synonyms:** aloneness; seclusion; aloneness; isolation; loneliness; loneness (archaic); onehead; oneship; onlyhood; onliness; seclusion; solitariness; solitude

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The state of not having diplomatic relations with other countries (either with most or all other countries, or with specified other countries).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** diplomacy; government; politics

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences
