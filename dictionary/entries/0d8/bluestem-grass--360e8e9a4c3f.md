### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of several grasses that are adapted to high light levels.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of several grasses that are adapted to high light levels.
   1. Little bluestem (Schizachyrium scoparium) and others of the genus Schizachyrium.

      **Usage:** uncountable; usually
