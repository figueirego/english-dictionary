### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Absolutely not; under no circumstances.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** Touch that weird rock over there, I dare you! —No way!

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** You failed your exam again? No way!
