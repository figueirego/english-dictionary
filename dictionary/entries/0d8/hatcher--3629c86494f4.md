### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who hatches, such as a chicken farmer.

   **Example:** I bought some chicks from a commercial hatcher.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Something that hatches, such as a bird or the egg from which it hatches.

   **Example:** Incubators must be carefully monitored so that hatchers and nonhatchers may both be further handled promptly.

   **Source synonyms:** hatchling
