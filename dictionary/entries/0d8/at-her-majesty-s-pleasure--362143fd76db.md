### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Indefinitely; for an arbitrary period determined by the monarch or her government (especially for indefinite tenure or imprisonment).

   **Example:** The Governor serves at Her Majesty's pleasure.

   **Example:** The murderer was imprisoned at Her Majesty's pleasure.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a position notionally granted or imposed by a queen
