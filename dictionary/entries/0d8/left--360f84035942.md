### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Designating the side of the body toward the west when one is facing north; the side of the body on which the heart is located in most humans; the opposite of right. This arrow points to the reader's left&#58; ←

   **Example:** The left side.

   **Source synonyms:** sinister; sinistral; port

   **Source antonyms:** right; dexter; dextral

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Anticlockwise, particularly when describing a change in direction or orientation.

   **Example:** The road up ahead contains a left bend.

   **Example:** Rotate the bolt to the left to loosen it.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Designating the bank of a river (etc.) on one's left when facing downstream (i.e. facing forward while floating with the current); that is, the north bank of a river that flows eastward. If this arrow&#58; ⥲ shows the direction of the current, the tilde is on the left side of the river.

   **Example:** The Eiffel Tower is on the left bank of the Seine.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Left-wing; pertaining to the political left.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; politics

   **Source antonyms:** right

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Relating to the beginning or earlier part of a phrase.

   **Example:** left dislocation

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences; syntax
