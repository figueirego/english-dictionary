### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who intercedes, particularly
   1. A mediator, one who reconciles enemies or pleads for another.

      **Source synonyms:** agent; delegate; deputy; emissary; lieutenant; proxy; representative; secondary; solicitor (archaic); surrogate

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One who intercedes, particularly
   1. An intermediary, a middleman.

      **Source synonyms:** intermediary; middleman; go-between; solicitor

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. One who intercedes, particularly
   1. A saint believed to plead for a sinner to God.

      **Usage:** Greek Orthodoxy

      **Example:** St Mary the Intercessor

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Catholicism; Christianity; Roman-Catholicism
