**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Example:** Her years of experience enabled her to render a masterly performance.

   **Source synonyms:** maestrolike; masterlike; masterous (obsolete); able; artful; habile (formal); accomplished; adept; adroit; apt; capable; competent; consummate; conversant; crack; crafty; deft; dexterous; expert; facile; fluent; gain; handy; maestrolike; masterful; masterlike; masterly; masterous (obsolete); professional; proficient; qualified; quick; ready; skilled; skillful; thoroughbred; tight (obsolete); practiced; versed

   **Usage:** derogatory; obsolete; usually
