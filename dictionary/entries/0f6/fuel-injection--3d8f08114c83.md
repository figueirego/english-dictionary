## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The delivery, under pressure, of a metered amount of fuel, as a fine spray, into the intake airflow or combustion chambers of an internal combustion engine.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The equipment to achieve this delivery&#58; a fuel injection system.

   **Usage:** metonymically; uncountable

   **Example:** He replaced the carburetor with fuel injection.
