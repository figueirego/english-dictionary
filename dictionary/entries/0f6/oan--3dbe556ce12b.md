## Suffix

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used to convert the name of an atom or ion to an adjective that means containing cations or anions of that atom or ion.

   **Usage:** morpheme

   **Example:** bismuth + -oan → bismuthoan (“containing trivalent bismuth”)

   **Example:** cupro- (“copper”) + -oan → cuproan (“containing monovalent copper”)

   **Example:** plumbo- (“lead”) + -oan → plumboan (“containing divalent lead”)
