### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To set the boundaries or limits of.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To ascertain definitely; to figure out, find out, or conclude by analyzing, calculating, or investigating.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To fix the form or character of; to shape; to prescribe imperatively; to regulate; to settle.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To fix the course of; to impel and direct; with a remoter object preceded by to.

   **Example:** The news of his father's illness determined him to depart immediately.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To bring to a conclusion, as a question or controversy; to settle authoritative or judicial sentence; to decide.

   **Example:** The court has determined the cause.

   **Source synonyms:** find; judge; make a decision; choose up (idiomatic); decide; determine; resolve; find; judge; posken (Judaism); make a decision; make up one's mind (idiomatic); take a decision; work out

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To resolve (to do something); to establish a fixed intention; to cause (something) to come to a conclusion or decision; to lead.

   **Example:** I determined to go home at once.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To define or limit by adding a differentia.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; logic; mathematics; philosophy; sciences

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To bring to an end, finish; to come to an end, stop, end.

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive; otherwise obsolete

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law
