## Noun

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈfæk.əl.ti/ — source labels: General-American; Received-Pronunciation

### Forms

- faculties — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The academic staff at schools, colleges, universities or not-for-profit research institutes, as opposed to the students or support staff.

   **Usage:** Canada; Philippines; US

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A division of a university.

   **Example:** She transferred from the Faculty of Science to the Faculty of Medicine.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An ability, power, or skill.

   **Usage:** Often in the plural

   **Example:** He lived until he reached the age of 90 with most of his faculties intact.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An authority, power, or privilege conferred by a higher authority.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A licence to make alterations to a church.

   **Usage:** Church-of-England

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The members of a profession.
