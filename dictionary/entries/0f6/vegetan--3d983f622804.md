## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Vegetable-tanned leather, typically cow skin tanned using plant-derived tannins rather than chemicals.

   **Example:** Vegetan is a stiffer leather that takes tooling very well, but it is easily damaged by water without further treatment.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A synthetic microfibre, intended to be used as a vegan substitute for leather.
