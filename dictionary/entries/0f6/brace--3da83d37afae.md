## Verb · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /bɹeɪs/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Forms

- braces — present; singular; third-person
- bracing — participle; present
- braced — participle; past
- braced — past

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To prepare for something bad, such as an impact or blow.

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** All hands, brace for impact!

   **Example:** Brace yourself!

   **Example:** The boy has no idea about everything that's been going on. You need to brace him for what's about to happen.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To place in a position for resisting pressure; to hold firmly.

   **Example:** He braced himself against the crowd.

   **Example:** She braced her back against the wall.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To swing round the yards of a square rigged ship, using braces, to present a more efficient sail surface to the direction of the wind.

   **Example:** to brace the yards

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To stop someone for questioning, usually said of police.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To confront with questions, demands or requests.

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To furnish with braces; to support; to prop.

   **Example:** to brace a beam in a building

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

**Source sense R2.S7**

7. To draw tight; to tighten; to put in a state of tension; to strain; to strengthen.

   **Example:** to brace the nerves

<a id="r2-s8"></a>

**Source sense R2.S8**

8. To bind or tie closely; to fasten tightly.
