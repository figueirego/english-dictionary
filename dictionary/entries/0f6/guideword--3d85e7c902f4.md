### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A word or phrase that serves as one's guide.

   **Source synonyms:** watchword; catchword; byword; maxim; mantra; motto

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A word that is printed at the top or bottom of the page in a reference work, in order to help readers find a specific entry.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** media; publishing

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A word under the right-hand side of the last line on a book page that repeats the first word from the following page.

   **Usage:** historical

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** media; printing; publishing

   **Source synonyms:** catchword

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any of the words in a memo or email that define metadata regarding the message, such as sender, recipient, and subject.

   **Usage:** uncommon

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business
