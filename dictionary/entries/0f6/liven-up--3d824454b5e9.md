### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To improve a person's mood by making them more energetic.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Darren looks down at the moment; let's try to liven him up.

   **Source synonyms:** cheer up; liven; animate; arouse; awaken (figuratively); beghast; leaven; bright; brighten (figuratively); cheer; energize; enliven; enthuse (proscribed); exalt; excite; fire up; ginger; ginger up; inflame (figuratively); invigorate; inspire; inspirit; jazz; kindle (figuratively); liven; liven up; perk up; quicken; spice; spice up; stimulate; vitalise

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To become more happy, energetic or positive.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The party livened up after they brought out the cake.

   **Source synonyms:** blithen; cheer up; gladden
