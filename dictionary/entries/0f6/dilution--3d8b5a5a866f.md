### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The process of making something dilute.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A solution that has had additional solvent, such as water, added to it into order to make it less concentrated.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
