## Verb

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To ensure that something specific happens, is done etc., or of something happening.

   **Example:** I'm going to make sure that I get to the interview on time.

   **Example:** I expected to be able to get to the interview on time, but I left home earlier than usual just to make sure.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To make oneself certain (of something, or that something is the case); to verify, to ascertain.

   **Example:** When you leave, make sure you have locked the door behind you.

   **Example:** Make sure of your sources before you publish.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To feel certain of something; to be convinced.

   **Usage:** archaic

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To betroth.

   **Usage:** obsolete
