### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having the power to produce a required effect or effects.

   **Example:** The pill is an effective method of birth control.

   **Source synonyms:** efficacious

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Producing a decided or decisive effect.

   **Example:** The president delivered an effective speech!

   **Source synonyms:** telling

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Efficient, serviceable, or operative, available for useful work.

   **Example:** How long does it take to make a bunch of civilians an effective military force?

   **Example:** My effective income after taxes and child support is $500 a month.

   **Example:** The effective radiated power is determined by multiplying the transmitter power output with the antenna gain.

   **Example:** The effective voltage of an alternating current is 0.7 times its peak voltage.

   **Source antonyms:** nominal

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Actually in effect.

   **Example:** effective immediately

   **Example:** The curfew is effective at midnight.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Having no negative coefficients.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a cycle or divisor

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** algebra; mathematics; sciences

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a group action
