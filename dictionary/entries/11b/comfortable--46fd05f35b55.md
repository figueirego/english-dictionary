### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Providing physical comfort and ease; agreeable.

   **Example:** This is the most comfortable bed I’ve ever slept in.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In a state of comfort and content.

   **Example:** What a great guestroom! I'll be quite comfortable here.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Confident; relaxed; not worried about someone or something.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Amply sufficient, satisfactory.

   **Example:** A comfortable income should suffice to consider oneself rich.

   **Example:** The home team is ahead by a comfortable margin.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Comforting, providing comfort; consolatory.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Strong; vigorous; valiant.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Serviceable; helpful.

   **Usage:** obsolete
