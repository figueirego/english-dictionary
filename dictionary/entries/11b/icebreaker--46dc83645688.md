### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A ship designed to break through ice so that it, or other ships coming behind, can navigate on frozen seas.

   **Example:** The steel hulls of ice-breakers are much thicker than those of standard vessels.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A game, activity, humorous anecdote, etc., designed to ease tension and encourage interaction among individuals in a situation where they do not know each other well.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** The new college hallmates were awkward with each other at first, but after a game of charades as an icebreaker, they were laughing like old friends.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
