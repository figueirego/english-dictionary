### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of a topological space&#58; such that every open cover has a point finite open refinement. That is, given any open cover of the topological space, there is a refinement which is again an open cover with the property that every point is contained only in finitely many sets of the refining cover.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences; topology
