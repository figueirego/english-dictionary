### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A sweet, crystalline compound (not an alkaloid), trimethylammoniumacetate, found in sugar beet and similar plants, sometimes used to treat muscular degeneracy; the zwitterion (CH₃)₃N⁺CH₂COO⁻.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; organic-chemistry; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; organic-chemistry; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>
