### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The filing under state law of a claim naturally arising under federal law.

   **Usage:** US; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Suing his employer for negligence rather than for violation of the Federal Employers Liability Act was an artful pleading, and the defendant properly removed the proceedings to federal district court.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law
