**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An attempt to illegally access a computer network, website, etc., that uses a list of words (from a dictionary) to try to guess decryption keys or passwords.

   **Example:** Experts warn against using ordinary words as passwords as they are vulnerable to dictionary attacks.
