### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To make a squawking noise; to yell, scream, or call out shrilly.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To speak out; to protest.

   **Usage:** intransitive; slang

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To report an infraction; to rat on or tattle; to disclose a secret.

   **Usage:** intransitive; slang

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To produce a warning message, indicating a possible error.

   **Usage:** informal; intransitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; programming; sciences

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To set or transmit a four-digit transponder code. (Normally followed by the specific code in question.)

   **Example:** After discovering that the hydraulic system is faulty, the pilots squawked 7700 and declared mayday.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** aeronautics; aerospace; aviation; business; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

   **Usage:** US; dated; slang
