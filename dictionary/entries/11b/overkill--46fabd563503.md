### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A destructive capacity that exceeds that needed to destroy an enemy; especially with nuclear weapons.

   **Usage:** literally; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Destruction beyond what is necessary to kill, especially in murder.

   **Usage:** literally; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An unnecessary excess of whatever is needed to achieve a goal.

   **Usage:** broadly; uncountable

   **Example:** 24 hours of TV coverage of the US election verged on overkill.

   **Example:** Should I give you yet more homework, or would that be overkill?

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An unnecessary excess of disposal because of too high criteria of inspection.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; manufacturing

   **Source antonyms:** underkill
