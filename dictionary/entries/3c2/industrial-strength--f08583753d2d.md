## Adjective · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Made for use by businesses or manufacturers, as opposed to home consumers, and therefore typically larger, stronger, more difficult to operate, and less attractive in appearance.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The office was now large enough to need an industrial strength vacuum cleaner.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Unusually large or potent.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; often

   **Example:** That's one industrial strength cockroach! I'll need the industrial strength bug spray.

   **Source context (verbatim):** used whimsically
