## Noun

### Forms

- eponymies — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The status or condition of being an eponym (person lending their name to something); the distinction of having something named after oneself.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The time during which one serves as an eponym (person lending their name to something); the tenure of an eponym.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The practice of naming a tribe after its real or supposed progenitor (the eponym).

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anthropology; ethnology; human-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The semantic relation of eponyms (names or words derived from the names of persons or other entities).

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences; semantics
