### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A process whereby a vowel or a consonant is pronounced further to the front of the vocal tract than some reference point.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; phonetics; phonology; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A phonological relationship where a front vowel is found in place of a relative back vowel in an inflected form of a word.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; phonology; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An analogous relationship between the vowel sounds in a dialect of a language relative to the language standard or an earlier form of the language.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The movement of a word of phrase to nearer the beginning of a sentence or clause than it would usually appear, often for emphasis. May also occur as part of the standard syntax of particular constructions (e.g. wh-fronting).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences; syntax

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An act of putting on a false façade to impress or intimidate people.

   **Usage:** countable; slang; uncountable
