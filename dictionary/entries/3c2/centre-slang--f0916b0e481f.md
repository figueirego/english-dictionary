## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A form of obfuscatory slang in which the central vowel of a word is moved to the beginning, and other sounds optionally added, so for example mug might become ugmer.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** medial slang
