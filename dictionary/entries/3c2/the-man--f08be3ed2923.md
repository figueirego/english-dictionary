### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The oppressive powers that be, including the government and corporations, as coordinated outside of one's control.

   **Usage:** singular; singular-only

   **Example:** The man gets you down.

   **Example:** I'm sorry I couldn’t meet you earlier, but I spent all night working for the man.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An oppressive or domineering person of authority, usually male; especially, one's boss.

   **Usage:** singular; singular-only

   **Source synonyms:** old man

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The best man for a job; someone with exceptional skills.

   **Usage:** singular; singular-only

   **Example:** You’re the man!
