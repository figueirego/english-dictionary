### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A small round fruit of a dark blue or red color, of several plants in the related genera Vaccinium and Gaylussacia.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A shrub growing this fruit.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A small amount, a short distance, as in the phrase huckleberry above a persimmon.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A person of little consequence.

   **Usage:** slang

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The person one is looking for; the right person for the job.

   **Usage:** US; slang

   **Example:** I'm your huckleberry.
