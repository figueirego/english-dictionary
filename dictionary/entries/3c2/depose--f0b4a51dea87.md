### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To put down; to lay down; to deposit; to lay aside; to put away.

   **Usage:** literally; transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To remove (a leader) from (high) office without killing (them).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** A deposed monarch may go into exile as pretender to the lost throne, hoping to be restored in a subsequent revolution.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To give evidence or testimony, especially in response to interrogation during a deposition.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To interrogate and elicit testimony from during a deposition, typically by a lawyer.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** After we deposed the claimant we had enough evidence to avoid a trial.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To take or swear an oath.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To testify; to bear witness; to claim; to assert; to affirm.
