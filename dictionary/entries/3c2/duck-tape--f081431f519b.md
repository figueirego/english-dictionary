### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A kind of tape made from, or incorporating, cotton duck (type of fabric).

   **Usage:** countable; historical; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A type of multi-purpose heavy-duty adhesive tape.

   **Usage:** also; countable; uncountable; trademark

   **Example:** If you can't fix it with duck tape, it's not worth fixing.

   **Source context (verbatim):** used generically

   **Source synonyms:** duct tape; gaff tape; gaffer tape; gaffer's tape; gray tape
