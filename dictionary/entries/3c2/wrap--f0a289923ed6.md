### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To enclose (an object) completely in any flexible, thin material such as fabric or paper.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Christmas gifts are commonly known to be wrapped in paper.

   **Source synonyms:** bind; wind

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To enclose or coil around an object or organism, as a form of grasping.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** A snake wraps itself around its prey.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To conceal by enveloping or enfolding; to hide.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To finish shooting (filming) a video, television show, or movie.

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive; video production

   **Example:** To avoid going over budget, let's make sure we wrap by ten. (compare wrap up 2)

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To break a continuous line (of text) onto the next line

   **Usage:** transitive; usually; lines; words; text; lines; words; text

   **Example:** I wrapped the text so that I wouldn't need to scroll to the right to read it.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To make functionality available through a software wrapper.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To (cause to) reset to an original value after passing a maximum.

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** The row counter wraps back to zero when no more rows can be inserted.
