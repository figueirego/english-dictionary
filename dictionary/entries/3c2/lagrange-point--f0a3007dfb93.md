### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A point in an orbital configuration of a two-body system where a small object affected only by gravity can theoretically be stationary relative to the two larger objects.

   **Usage:** orbital mechanics

   **Example:** Mathematically, the Lagrange points represent solutions to a restricted form of the three body problem in which the mass of one object is assumed negligible compared to that of the two principal bodies—as, for example, an artificial satellite and the Earth–Moon system.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astrophysics
