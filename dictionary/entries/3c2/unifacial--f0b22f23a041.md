## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Usage:** not-comparable

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Having only one principal or specialized surface or edge.

   **Example:** Some foliaceous corals are unifacial, the polyp mouths being confined to one surface.
