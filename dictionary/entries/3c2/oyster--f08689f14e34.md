### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of certain marine bivalve mollusks, especially those of the family Ostreidae (the true oysters), usually found adhering to rocks or other fixed objects in shallow water along the seacoasts, or in brackish water in the mouth of rivers.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The delicate oyster-shaped morsel of dark meat contained in a small cavity of the bone on each side of the lower part of the back of a fowl.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of the fungi in the genera Pleurotus, Hohenbuehelia, or other related genera.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A pale beige color tinted with grey or pink, like that of an oyster.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A person who keeps secrets.

   **Usage:** colloquial; by analogy

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Something at one's disposal.

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A shoplifter.

   **Usage:** UK; slang

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Ellipsis of Oyster card.

   **Usage:** UK; abbreviation; alt-of; ellipsis; slang

   **Alternative form of:** [Oyster card](../210/oyster-card--84120a6bf6eb.md)
