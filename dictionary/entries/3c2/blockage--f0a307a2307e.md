### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The state or condition of being blocked.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The thing that is the cause of such a state, blocking a passage.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** There was a blockage in the sewer, so we called out the plumber.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Occlusion of a lumen (especially that of a blood vessel or intestine), or the thing that is causing it; as&#58;
   1. Synonym of thrombosis, thromboembolism, or embolism.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Example:** Blockage of circulation quickly leads to ischemia.

      **Example:** In selected cases, endovascular thrombectomy can quickly remove a blockage that pharmaceutical thrombolysis can't budge.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; medicine; natural-sciences; sciences

      **Source synonyms:** thrombosis (synonym; synonym-of); thromboembolism (synonym; synonym-of); or embolism (synonym; synonym-of)

**Source sense R1.S4**
