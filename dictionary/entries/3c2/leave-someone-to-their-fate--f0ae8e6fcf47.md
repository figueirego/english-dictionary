**Source sense R1.S1**

1. to abandon; to give up on; to leave on their own – chiefly in regard to avoid upcoming misfortune

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I thought I could rely on you, and yet you completely leave me to my fate during my illness!

   **Example:** When the brake pedal stopped working at that speed, I jumped out through the door and left my car to its fate.
