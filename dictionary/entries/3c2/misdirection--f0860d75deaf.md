## Noun

### Forms

- misdirections — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An act of misleading, of convincing someone to concentrate in an incorrect direction.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The magician used misdirection to get us to watch his left hand while he did something with his right hand.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Movement or tendency in the wrong direction.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An error of law within a judgement committed by a judge or judges of a lower court, particularly as found by an appeals court.

   **Usage:** UK; countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law
