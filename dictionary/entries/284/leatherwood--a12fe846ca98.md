## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of the genus Dirca of deciduous shrubs of North America, that have leathery bark.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A subalpine shrub or small tree found only in New Zealand, Olearia colensoi.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any member of the genus Cyrilla of trees and shrubs of the Americas.
