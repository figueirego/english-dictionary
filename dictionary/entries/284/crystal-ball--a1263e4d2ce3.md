## Noun

### Forms

- crystal balls — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A globe of glass or crystal that is used to foretell the future.

   **Example:** The psychic looked into the crystal ball and saw into the future.

   **Source synonyms:** crystal sphere; orbuculum; seeing stone; [shew stone](../03e/shew-stone--0f9696a82f16.md); showstone; scrying stone

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A manner or method of seeing into the future.

   **Usage:** broadly; figuratively

   **Example:** If her past is a crystal ball to her future, she will perhaps never be rich, even though she is a kind, hard-working person.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. One of the magickal tools of Wicca, used for divination and altar purposes.
