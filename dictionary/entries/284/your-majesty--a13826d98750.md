## Proper noun · Record 1

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A term of address used in direct address to a monarch of higher rank than a prince, such as a king, queen, emperor, or empress.

   **Usage:** formal

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A sarcastic term of address to anyone who is (or is acting) pompous or bossy.

   **Usage:** informal; sarcastic
