## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

- emulsions — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A stable suspension of small droplets of one liquid in another with which it is immiscible.

   **Example:** Mayonnaise is an emulsion where egg is used to keep oil and water mixed.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A stable suspension of small droplets of one liquid in another with which it is immiscible.
   1. A food preparation in which one or more ingredients are made into an emulsion.

      **Example:** a potato and cheese emulsion

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** cooking; food; lifestyle
