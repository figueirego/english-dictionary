## Noun

### Forms

- first strikes — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An attack (typically nuclear) launched without warning in the absence of a prior attack by one's opponent, with the intention of destroying one's opponent's ability to retaliate.

   **Example:** During the Cold War, both sides feared that the other was preparing to launch a first strike.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war

   **Source synonyms:** decapitation strike

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A first offense by a lawbreaker, as counted by a three-strikes law.

   **Usage:** criminal law

   **Example:** His first strike was when he stole a car to go for a joyride.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. One of the first few coins struck from a new set of dies.

   **Example:** Collectors pay a premium for first strikes in order to get their hands on coins with the crispest possible detailing.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; numismatics
