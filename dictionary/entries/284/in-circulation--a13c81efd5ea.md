### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Percolating between people or places.

   **Example:** The story has been in circulation for a while now.

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Freely available; not restricted in access; in general use.

   **Example:** Because of the Internet, there is a vast library of free video material in circulation.
