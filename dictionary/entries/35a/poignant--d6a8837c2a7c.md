### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Sharp-pointed; keen.

   **Usage:** obsolete; usually

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a weapon

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Neat; eloquent; applicable; relevant.

   **Example:** A poignant reply will garner more credence than hours of blown smoke.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Evoking strong mental sensation, to the point of distress; emotionally moving.

   **Example:** Flipping through his high school yearbook evoked many a poignant memory of yesteryear.

   **Source synonyms:** distressing; moving

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Piquant, pungent.

   **Usage:** figuratively; taste

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a smell

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Incisive; penetrating; piercing.

   **Usage:** figuratively; word

   **Example:** His comments were poignant and witty.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a look

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Inducing sharp physical pain.

   **Usage:** UK; dated
