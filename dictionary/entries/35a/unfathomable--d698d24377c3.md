### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Impossible to fathom.
   1. Especially of depth&#58; physically incapable of being measured; immeasurable.

      **Source synonyms:** abyssal; incalculable; inestimable; soundless; unplumbable; unsoundable

      **Source antonyms:** calculable; estimable; fathomable; measurable; plumbable; soundable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Impossible to fathom.
   1. Impossible to grasp the extent of, or to fully know or understand.

      **Usage:** figuratively

      **Example:** The sheer number of warriors the enemy attacked with was unfathomable.

      **Source synonyms:** bottomless; fathomless; impenetrable; unintelligible; abstruse; Chinese; dense; enigmatic; fathomless; bottomless; Greek; inapprehensible; incognizable; incomprehensible; ineffable; inenubilable (formal; literary); inexplicable; impenetrable; insensible; inscrutable; insoluble; insolvable; mysterious; nonunderstandable; obscure; retruse (obsolete); tenebrose (figuratively); thick; undecipherable; undiscoverable; unexplainable; unfathomable; ungraspable; unintelligible; unknowable; ununderstandable

      **Source antonyms:** explicable; fathomable; intelligible; penetrable; understandable; apprehensible; clear; limpid; comprehensible; explainable; explicable; explicatable; fathomable; graspable; illuminable; intelligible; [lucid](../252/lucid--948278737ada.md); pellucid; perspicuous; straightforward; tangible; understandable
