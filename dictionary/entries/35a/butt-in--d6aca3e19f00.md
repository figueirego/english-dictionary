### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To join a conversation or situation in which one is not welcome or invited; to interject (connotes a rude or discourteous interruption).

   **Usage:** idiomatic; informal

   **Example:** Hey, this is none of your business; don’t butt in!

   **Example:** Forgive me for butting in.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To join a queue not at the end, but in the middle, in violation of etiquette.

   **Usage:** informal; transitive
