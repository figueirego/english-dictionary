<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To even the slightest extent; at all.
   1. Used before different, before comparatives with more and less, and idiomatically before other comparatives, generally in interrogative, hypothetical, or negative contexts.

      **Usage:** not-comparable

      **Example:** If you get any taller, you'll start having to duck through doorways!

      **Example:** Is she any less enthused now that it's raining?

      **Example:** Is her enthusiasm any less now that it's raining?

      **Example:** You don't seem to be any the worse after such a tragic experience.

      **Example:** I will not remain here any longer.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To even the slightest extent; at all.
   1. Used to modify a verb, generally a finite verb or a non-finite verb in object position.

      **Usage:** US; not-comparable

      **Example:** That doesn't bother me any.

      **Example:** Unfortunately, I don't expect the documentation to help any.
