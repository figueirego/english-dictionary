### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To reject some act in a spiteful manner.

   **Example:** I tried to make up after the argument, but he just threw it in my face and stormed off.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To repay an act of kindness with disappointment or betrayal.

   **Example:** I supported you for months, and you've just thrown all that in my face by losing your job.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To lord over someone by exerting dominance, following a successful confrontation; sometimes in a subtle or snide manner.

   **Example:** Bringing the kids along was clearly just an excuse to throw it in my face after the divorce.

   **Source synonyms:** rub something in someone's face

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To raise a past transgression in order to admonish someone.

   **Example:** She always throws that time I cheated in my face when we argue. It was ten years ago!

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see throw, something, in, someone, face.
