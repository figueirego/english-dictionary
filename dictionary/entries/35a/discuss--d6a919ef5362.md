### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To converse or debate concerning a particular topic.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Let's sit down and discuss whether we should have a baby.

   **Example:** I don't wish to discuss this further.

   **Source synonyms:** bespeak; betalk; debate; talk about; treat

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To communicate, tell, or disclose (information, a message, etc.).

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** apprise; impart; acquaint; advise; apprise; discuss (obsolete); enlighten; impart; inform; keep informed; let know; notify; tell

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To break to pieces; to shatter.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** busticate; fragment; splinter; toshiver

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To deal with, in eating or drinking; consume.

   **Usage:** colloquial; obsolete; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** get outside; put away; consume; discuss (obsolete); get outside (informal); ingest; partake; put away; touch

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To examine or search thoroughly; to exhaust a remedy against, as against a principal debtor before proceeding against the surety.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law
