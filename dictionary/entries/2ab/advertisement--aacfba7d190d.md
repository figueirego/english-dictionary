### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A commercial solicitation designed to sell some commodity, service or similar.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Companies try to sell their products using advertisements in form of placards, television spots and print publications.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; marketing

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A public notice.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The city council placed an advertisement in the local newspaper to inform its residents of the forthcoming roadworks.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A recommendation of a particular product, service or person.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The good manners and intelligence of the students are an advertisement for the school.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Notoriety.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. In gin rummy, the discarding of a card of one's preferred suit so as to mislead the opponent into thinking you do not want it.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** card-games; games

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A preface.

   **Usage:** countable; literary; obsolete; uncountable
