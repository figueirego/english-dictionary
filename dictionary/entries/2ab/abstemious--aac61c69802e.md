### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Refraining from freely consuming food or strong drink; sparing in diet; abstinent, temperate.

   **Source synonyms:** abstinent; sober; temperate

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Sparing in the indulgence of the appetite or passions.

   **Source synonyms:** abstentious; continent; abstemious; otherish; selfless; self-abnegatory; self-denying; self-flagellatory; self-sacrificing; unselfish; abstemious; calm; collected; composed; continent; cool; dispassionate; inexcitable; imperturbable; regular (rare); sedate; sober; steady; staid; temperate; undisturbed; unexcitable; unexcited; unimpassioned; unpassionate; unperturbed; unruffled; unstirred; unsusceptible

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Sparingly used; used with temperance or moderation.

   **Source synonyms:** frugal; sparing; abstemious; canny; chary; moderate; cheap; close with a buck; economic; economical; frugal; provident (formal); husbandly (obsolete); huswifely; parsimonious; penny-pinching; prudent; spareful (obsolete); sparing; spary (obsolete); thrifty; tight; tightwad

   **Source antonyms:** extravagant; squandering; extravagant; improvident; imprudent; lavish; loose-handed; prodigal; profligate; reckless; spendthrift; squandering; uneconomical; unthrifty; wasteful

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Marked by, or spent in, abstinence.

   **Example:** an abstemious life

   **Source synonyms:** temperate

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Promotive of abstemiousness.

   **Usage:** rare
