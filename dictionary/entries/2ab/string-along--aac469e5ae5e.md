### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To keep somebody falsely believing that one has certain intentions.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** She's been telling me for ages that she wants to meet up, but won't give me a date. I'm convinced she's stringing me along.
