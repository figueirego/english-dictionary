## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The process or business of producing printed material by means of inked type and a printing press or similar technology.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** In those days, printing was big business in this city. Giant web-fed presses churned out countless tons of printed matter annually.

   **Source synonyms:** imprintery

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Synonym of printed matter.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** They delivered a huge batch of printing yesterday, so you can expect an invoice shortly.

   **Source synonyms:** printed matter (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Synonym of print run (“all the copies of a publication that have been printed in one batch”).

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** She owns a copy of the first printing, which is a favorite among book collectors because some of the typos in it seem like Freudian slips.

   **Source synonyms:** print run (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Handwritten characters that are not joined up.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** The instructions said, "Please sign your name and then print your name below your signature," so he wrote out his name underneath in the neatest printing that his hand could muster.

   **Source antonyms:** cursive
