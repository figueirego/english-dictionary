**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To follow (a leader or a de facto leader) without question or thought, leading to potentially disastrous consequences.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; informal

   **Example:** You can’t just follow someone off a cliff, especially when they lead you into bad decisions.

   **Example:** He had a habit of blindly following the leader, almost like he would follow them off a cliff.

   **Example:** Don’t be the kind of person who would follow someone off a cliff, always think for yourself.
