### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To reduce to a code, to arrange into a code.

   **Example:** The company president codified the goal as a one-line mission statement.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To collect and arrange in a systematic form.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To enact as an official rule or law.
