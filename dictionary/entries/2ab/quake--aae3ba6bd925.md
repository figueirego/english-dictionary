### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A trembling or shaking.

   **Example:** We felt a quake in the apartment every time the train went by.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An earthquake, a trembling of the ground with force.

   **Example:** California is plagued by quakes; there are a few minor ones almost every month.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Something devastating, like a strong earthquake.

   **Usage:** figuratively
