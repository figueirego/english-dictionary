## Adjective · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˌɔfˈhænd/ — source labels: US

### Forms

- more offhand — comparative
- most offhand — superlative
- off-hand — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Without planning or thinking ahead.

   **Example:** She gave an offhand speech.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Careless; without sufficient thought or consideration.

   **Example:** He doesn't realise how hurtful his offhand remarks can be.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Curt, abrupt, unfriendly.

   **Example:** She was quite offhand with me yesterday.

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** impromptu — without planning; extemporaneous; off-the-cuff
