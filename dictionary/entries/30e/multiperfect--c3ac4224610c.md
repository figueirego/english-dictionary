## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Being a generalization of a perfect number. For a given natural number k, a number n is called k-perfect (or k-fold perfect) iff the sum of all positive divisors of n (the divisor function, σ(n)) is equal to kn.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** pluperfect
