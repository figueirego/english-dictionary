## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A monument in County Meath, Ireland. A prehistoric neolithic monument, a circular ceremonial burial mound structure with astronomical alignments

   **Source synonyms:** Sí an Bhrú; Si an Bhru

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A monument complex in County Meath, Ireland; containing the mound, other burial structures, various henges, and additional neolithic structures in the area. It is a World Heritage Site.

   **Source synonyms:** Brú na Bóinne; Bru na Boinne
