## Noun

### Forms

- day shifts — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A regularly scheduled period of work during daylight hours, especially 8AM to 4PM; sometimes a 12-hour shift, such as 6AM to 6PM.

   **Example:** I prefer to work the day shift, but I take the night shift in stride.

   **Source synonyms:** first shift

   **Source antonyms:** night shift; graveyard shift

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A group of workers who work during this time slot.

   **Example:** You'll have to ask the day shift — the equipment was here when we left.

   **Source synonyms:** first shift

   **Source antonyms:** night shift; graveyard shift
