### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Collectively, personal ornamentation such as rings, necklaces, brooches and bracelets, made of precious metals and sometimes set with gemstones.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** The jewellery was very expensive in that shop.

   **Example:** She had more jewellery ornamented about her than any three ladies needed.

   **Source synonyms:** bling; bling-bling; tom; tomfoolery (Cockney; slang)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any tools, instruments, devices, etc., placed within a drill pipe.

   **Usage:** informal; uncountable; usually; oil industry

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The shop from which a jeweller trades or makes jewellery

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually
