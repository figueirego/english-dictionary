## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A series of drawings, in sequence, often but not necessarily depicting something funny or satirical in nature; more specifically, a single work consisting of a short row of such drawings.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** comics; literature; media; publishing

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An ongoing series of comics published in strip form, whether or not containing a continuing narrative.

   **Example:** Calvin and Hobbes was one of the most significant American comic strips of the 1980s and 1990s.
