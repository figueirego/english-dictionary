### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A city, a municipal and regional administrative centre, an as well as, a raion of Donetsk Oblast in the Donbas region, in eastern Ukraine, on the Kalmius river.

   **Usage:** city and raion

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An oblast in the Donbas region, in eastern Ukraine. Seat&#58; Kramatorsk; former seat&#58; Donetsk (prior to the Donbas War, where Russia occupied and set up puppet countries)

   **Usage:** province

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A self-declared country in Donetsk Oblast in the Donbas region, in eastern Ukraine. Seat&#58; Donetsk; occupying 1/3 of the province, but claiming all the province, before the Russian invasion of all of Ukraine in 2022.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** country; location; region

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A town in Rostov Oblast, Russia, on the Donets river.
