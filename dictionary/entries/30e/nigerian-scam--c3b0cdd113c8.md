## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An attempt to defraud by sending out large numbers of emails, each stating that the recipient is entitled to receive a large amount of money, but will need to supply banking account details, or to send payment for fees, before the payment can be passed on.

   **Usage:** Internet

   **Example:** Even savvy Internet users have fallen victim to the ubiquitous Nigerian scam.
