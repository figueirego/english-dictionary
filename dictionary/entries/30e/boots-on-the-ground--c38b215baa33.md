### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The ground forces actually fighting in a war or conflict, rather than troops not engaged or other military action such as air strikes.

   **Usage:** metonymically; plural; plural-only

   **Example:** The Pentagon may say we have enough, but that's not what I'm hearing from the boots on the ground.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war
