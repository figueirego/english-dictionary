## Verb

### Forms

- plays along — present; singular; third-person

- played along — past

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To take part in a charade, deception, or practical joke.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** James thought it was cruel for the fraternity members to make the pledge think his father had died suddenly and refused to play along.

   **Example:** James played along with their cruel trick.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To participate in a joke or trick while hiding one's knowledge of its true nature.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I knew that my blind date was an April Fools trick, but I played along anyway.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To play a musical instrument in synchrony with a playback of a recording.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I like to practise guitar by playing along with records.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To cooperate or go along with a plan.

   **Usage:** US; idiomatic

   **Example:** I have doubts about your plan, but I'll play along for now.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To pretend to cooperate or go along with a plan.

   **Usage:** US; idiomatic

   **Example:** I haven't told him yet, I'm still playing along with his scheme.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To manipulate (someone).

   **Usage:** UK; idiomatic

   **Example:** The con man plays his victims along until he senses the moment to ask for money.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see play, along.

   **Example:** The children spent the day playing along the river.
