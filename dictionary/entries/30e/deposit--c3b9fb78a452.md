## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /dɪˈpɑ.zɪt/ — source labels: General-American

### Forms

- deposite — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Sediment or rock that is not native to its present location or is different from the surrounding material. Sometimes refers to ore or gems.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; geology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Bailment of personal property to be kept gratuitously for the bailor (depositor) and without any benefit to the bailee (depositary), e.g. for storage, carriage, repair, etc.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Money placed in a bank account, as for safekeeping or to earn interest.

   **Example:** make a deposit (put money in an account)

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** banking; business

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Anything left behind on a surface.

   **Example:** a mineral deposit

   **Example:** a deposit of seaweed on the shore

   **Example:** a deposit of jam on my countertop

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A sum of money or other asset given as an initial payment, to show good faith, or to reserve something for purchase.

   **Example:** They put down a deposit on the apartment.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance

   **Source synonyms:** earnest money; down payment

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A sum of money given as a security for a borrowed item, which will be given back when the item is returned, e.g., a bottle deposit or can deposit

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A place of deposit; a depository.

## Verb · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /dɪˈpɒz.ɪt/ — source labels: Received-Pronunciation
- IPA: /dɪˈpɑ.zɪt/ — source labels: General-American

### Forms

- deposits — present; singular; third-person
- depositing — participle; present
- deposited — participle; past
- deposited — past
- deposite — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To lay down; to place; to put.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** A crocodile deposits her eggs in the sand.

   **Example:** The waters deposited a rich alluvium.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To lay up or away for safekeeping; to put up; to store.

   **Example:** to deposit goods in a warehouse

   **Source synonyms:** reposit

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To entrust one's assets to the care of another. Sometimes done as collateral.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To put money or funds into an account.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I had to deposit two months' rent into my landlord's account before he gave me the keys.

   **Source synonyms:** bank

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To lay aside; to rid oneself of.
