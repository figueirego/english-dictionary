### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To have as a necessary consequence; to lead to (something) as a consequence.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Correlation does not imply causation

   **Example:** The proposition that "all dogs are mammals" implies that my dog is a mammal.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a proposition

   **Source synonyms:** entail; implicate; import

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To suggest by logical inference.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** When I state that your dog is brown, I am not implying that all dogs are brown.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a person

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To hint; to insinuate; to suggest tacitly and avoid a direct statement.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** What do you mean "we need to be more careful with hygiene"? Are you implying that I don't wash my hands?

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a person or proposition

   **Source synonyms:** allude; hint; infer; insinuate; suggest; advert; allude; hint; imply; indicate; inkle; insinuate; intimate; moot (Northern-England; Scotland); refer; signify; suggest

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To enfold, entangle.

   **Usage:** archaic

   **Source synonyms:** envelop; wrap up; ensnare; implicate; enmesh; ensnare (figuratively); ensnarl; entangle; foul; implicate (archaic); imply (archaic); involve (figuratively); mat; ravel; snarl; tangle
