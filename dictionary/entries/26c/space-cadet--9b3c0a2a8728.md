### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person in training to be a (generally military) astronaut.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One who deals with reality in a way consistent with being under the influence of (or "spaced out on") drugs.

   **Usage:** derogatory; slang

   **Source synonyms:** space case

<a id="r1-s3"></a>
