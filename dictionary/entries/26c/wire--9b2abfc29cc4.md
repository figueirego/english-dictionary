### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To fasten with wire, especially with reference to wine bottles, corks, or fencing.

   **Example:** We need to wire that hole in the fence.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To string on a wire.

   **Example:** wire beads

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To equip with wires for use with electricity.

   **Example:** Do you know how to wire a plug?

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To connect, embed, incorporate, or include (something) into (something else) by or as if by wires&#58;
   1. To add (something) into a system (especially an electrical system) by means of wiring.

      **Example:** I'll just wire your camera to the computer screen.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To connect, embed, incorporate, or include (something) into (something else) by or as if by wires&#58;
   1. To add or connect (something) into a system as if with wires (for example, with nerves).

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To connect, embed, incorporate, or include (something) into (something else) by or as if by wires&#58;
   1. To connect, involve or embed (something) deeply or intimately into (something else, such as an organization or political scene), so that it is plugged in (to that thing) (“keeping up with current information about (the thing)”) or has insinuated itself into (the thing).

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

**Source sense R2.S7**

7. To set or predetermine (someone's personality or behaviour, or an organization's culture) in a particular way.

   **Usage:** figuratively; passive; usually

   **Example:** There's no use trying to get Sarah to be less excitable. That's just the way she's wired.

<a id="r2-s8"></a>

**Source sense R2.S8**

8. To send a message or monetary funds to another person through a telecommunications system, formerly predominantly by telegraph.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** Urgent&#58; please wire me another 100 pounds sterling.

   **Example:** The detective wired ahead, hoping that the fugitive would be caught at the railway station.

<a id="r2-s9"></a>

**Source sense R2.S9**

9. To make someone tense or psyched up. See also adjective wired.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Example:** Coffee late at night wires me good and proper.

<a id="r2-s10"></a>

**Source sense R2.S10**

10. To install eavesdropping equipment.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Example:** We wired the suspect's house.

<a id="r2-s11"></a>

**Source sense R2.S11**

11. To snare by means of a wire or wires.

<a id="r2-s12"></a>

**Source sense R2.S12**

12. To place (a ball) so that the wire of a wicket prevents a successful shot.

   **Usage:** transitive; croquet
