### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To close the spacing between lines (ranks) of troops, as they are deployed across a battlefield, thereby decreasing the depth of the formation as it faces the enemy side.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To regroup forces, especially when this involves overlooking differences in order to face a challenge or adverse situation. Often implies making a show of unity, especially to the public.

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To maintain silence and secrecy in the face of some external threat.

   **Usage:** figuratively
