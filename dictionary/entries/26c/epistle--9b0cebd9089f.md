### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Alternative letter-case form of epistle&#58;
   1. One of the books of the New Testament which was originally a letter issued by an apostle to an individual or a community.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Alternative letter-case form of epistle&#58;
   1. An extract from a New Testament epistle (sense 1.1) or book other than a gospel which is read during a church service, chiefly the Eucharist.
