### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To form curds so that it no longer flows smoothly; to cause to form such curds. (usually said of milk)

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** Too much lemon will curdle the milk in your tea.

   **Source synonyms:** curd

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To clot or coagulate; to cause to congeal, such as through cold. (metaphorically of blood)

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To cause a liquid to spoil and form clumps so that it no longer flows smoothly.

   **Usage:** transitive
