### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To give (especially public) notice of (something); to announce publicly.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To provide information about a person or goods and services to influence others.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** For personal needs, advertise on the internet or in a local newspaper.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To provide public information about (a product, service etc.) in order to attract public awareness and increase sales.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Over the air, they advertise their product on drive-time radio talk shows and TV news shows.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To notify (someone) of something; to call someone's attention to something.

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. In gin rummy, to discard a card of one's preferred suit so as to mislead the opponent into thinking you do not want it.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** card-games; games
