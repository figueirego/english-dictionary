### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A quilt or usually flat cloth bag with a filling (traditionally down) and usually an additional washable cover, used instead of blankets; often called a comforter or quilt, especially in US English.

   **Usage:** Canada; Ireland; New-Zealand; UK

   **Example:** He pulled the thick duvet over his head to block out the morning light.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** US; abbreviation; alt-of
