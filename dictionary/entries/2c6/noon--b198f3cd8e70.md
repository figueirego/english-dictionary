### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The time of day when the Sun seems to reach its highest point in the sky; solar noon.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** On Saturdays, I love to have a lie-in until noon.

   **Example:** The race is due to start at noon sharp.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The time of day when the Sun seems to reach its highest point in the sky; solar noon.
   1. The mean time of solar noon, marked as twelve o'clock on most clocks.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The corresponding time in the middle of the night; midnight.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The ninth hour of the day counted from sunrise; around three o'clock in the afternoon.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The highest point; culmination.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable
