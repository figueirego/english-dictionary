### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The prevention of, or the protective treatment for, disease.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The role of statins in prophylaxis for cardiovascular disease is well supported by the medical literature.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The prevention of, or the protective treatment for, disease.
   1. The cleaning of a patient's teeth.

      **Usage:** countable; sometimes; specifically; uncountable

      **Example:** We did his semiannual prophylaxis today, and his teeth and gums were found to be healthy.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

      **Source synonyms:** prophy; dental

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The prevention of, or the protective treatment for, disease.
   1. Protective measures against acquiring a sexually transmitted disease.

      **Usage:** countable; sometimes; specifically; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**
