### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A type of scam in which the scammer remotely tricks the mark into transferring funds to a "safe account" using some pretense, such as the funds being at risk of imminently being stolen. The "safe account" is in reality controlled by the scammer.
