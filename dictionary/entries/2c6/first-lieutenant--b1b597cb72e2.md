### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The second lowest rank of a commissioned officer in the United States Army, Air Force, or Marine Corps, ranking above a second lieutenant and below a captain. The rank of first lieutenant is equivalent to the naval rank of lieutenant junior grade.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war
