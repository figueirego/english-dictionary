### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To operate a vehicle that is low on fuel.

   **Example:** We were running on fumes by the time we arrived, but we made it to the next gas station.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** automotive; transport; vehicles

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To operate with few resources, or be physically or emotionally overworked.

   **Usage:** broadly; figuratively

   **Example:** The organization had been running on fumes for months, so it's not surprising that they closed their doors.

   **Source synonyms:** run on empty; [run out of steam](../134/run-out-of-steam--4d0b4a482027.md)
