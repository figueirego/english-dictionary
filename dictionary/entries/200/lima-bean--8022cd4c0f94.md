### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of species Phaseolus lunatus of bean-bearing plants.

   **Example:** We hoed the lima bean rows last night.

   **Source synonyms:** lima; butter bean; chad bean; double bean; Madagascar bean; sieva bean; wax bean

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Fruit of this plant.

   **Example:** We had lima beans with dinner last night.

   **Source synonyms:** lima; butter bean; chad bean; double bean; Madagascar bean; sieva bean; wax bean
