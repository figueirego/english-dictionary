### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A flatbread similar in style, composition, and texture to modern pizza doughs and topped with herbs, cheese and other products. Focaccia typically consists of high-gluten flour, oil, water, sugar, salt and yeast.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** focaccia bread

<a id="r1-s2"></a>
