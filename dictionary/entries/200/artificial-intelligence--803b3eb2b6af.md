### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Intelligence exhibited by an artificial (nonnatural, manmade, humanmade) entity.
   1. The branch of computer science dealing with the reproduction or mimicking of human-level intelligence, self-awareness, knowledge, conscience, and thought in computer programs.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computer; computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; science; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Intelligence exhibited by an artificial (nonnatural, manmade, humanmade) entity.
   1. The essential quality of a machine which thinks in a manner similar to or on the same general level as a real human being.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Example:** This computer possesses artificial intelligence.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

   1. A character or entity controlled by the game instead of a player; AI.
