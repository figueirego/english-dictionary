### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A means of acquiring title to another's real property without compensation by occupying the property in a manner that has under common law the requirements of being actual, open and notorious, exclusive, hostile, under cover of claim or right, and continuous for a certain number of years.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
