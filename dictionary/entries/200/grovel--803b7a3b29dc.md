### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To be prone on the ground.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To crawl.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To abase oneself before another person.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** She refused to grovel in front of the bully, standing her ground.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To be slavishly nice to someone or apologize in the hope of securing something.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** He had to grovel before his boss after missing the important meeting.

   **Example:** He hated to grovel, but he knew it was the only way to save his job.
