### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Capable of being flexed or bent without breaking; able to be turned or twisted without breaking.

   **Source synonyms:** pliable

   **Source antonyms:** stiff; brittle; inflexible; rigid

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Willing or prone to give way to the influence of others; not invincibly rigid or obstinate.

   **Source synonyms:** tractable; manageable; ductile

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Capable of adapting or changing to suit new or modified conditions or situations.

   **Example:** You can't always get what you want&#58; you need to learn to be flexible.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Capable or being adapted or molded in some way.

   **Example:** a flexible language

   **Source synonyms:** plastic; malleable
