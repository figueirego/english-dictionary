<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Completely clear (in various senses).
   1. Without ambiguity or doubt; completely understood.

      **Usage:** idiomatic; simile

      **Example:** Reread through the manual until the instructions are crystal clear in your head.

      **Source synonyms:** clear as crystal

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Completely clear (in various senses).
   1. Visually clear, a sharp image, of high acuity.

      **Usage:** idiomatic; simile

      **Example:** The picture on this TV is crystal clear – you can see every grain of sand!

      **Source synonyms:** clear as crystal
