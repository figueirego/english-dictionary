### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The origination of living organisms from lifeless matter; such genesis as does not involve the action of living parents.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; evolutionary theory

   **Source synonyms:** abiogeny; biopoiesis; origin of life

   **Source antonyms:** biogenesis; transformism
