### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Obliged, when called upon, to answer (for one’s deeds); answerable.

   **Example:** Everyone is accountable to God for their conduct.

   **Example:** You will be held accountable for your misdemeanors.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Obliged to keep accurate records (of property or funds).

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Liable to be called on to render an account.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Capable of being accounted for; explicable; explainable.

   **Usage:** formal; rare
