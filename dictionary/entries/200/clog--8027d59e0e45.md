## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /klɒɡ/ — source labels: UK
- IPA: /klɑɡ/ — source labels: US

### Forms

- clogs — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A type of shoe with an inflexible, often wooden sole sometimes with an open heel.

   **Example:** Dutch people rarely wear clogs these days.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A blockage.

   **Example:** The plumber cleared the clog from the drain.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A shoe of any type.

   **Usage:** UK; colloquial

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A weight, such as a log or block of wood, attached to a person or animal to hinder motion.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. That which hinders or impedes motion; an encumbrance, restraint, or impediment of any kind.

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To block or slow passage through (often with 'up').

   **Example:** Hair is clogging the drainpipe.

   **Example:** The roads are clogged up with traffic.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To encumber or load, especially with something that impedes motion; to hamper.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>
