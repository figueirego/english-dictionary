### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of or relating to argumentation; specifically, presenting a logical argument or line of reasoning; argumentive, discursive.

   **Source synonyms:** argumentary (archaic); discursory

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Prone to argue or dispute.

   **Source synonyms:** agonistic (rhetoric); argumentative; bangsome; bellicose; belligerent; contentious; debateful (obsolete); discordant; disputatious (formal); disputative (formal); factious; fractious; fratchety (Britain; dialectal); fratchy (Britain); litigious; logomachic; pettifogging; quarrelous (US); quarrelsome; rantankerous (US; dated; dialectal); squabbly; strifeful; unpacific

   **Source antonyms:** nonargumentative; unargumentative
