### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The highest and usually climactic note of a song or composition, especially one that is difficult to reach.

   **Example:** After weeks of practice Alyssa was finally able to hit the high note of her song perfectly.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

   **Source antonyms:** bass note

<a id="r1-s2"></a>
