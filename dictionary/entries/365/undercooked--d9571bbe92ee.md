### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Insufficiently cooked, so as to be unpalatable or inedible.

   **Example:** I can't eat this chicken – it's undercooked.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Very lightly cooked.

   **Example:** The broccoli was nicely undercooked.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. Done with insufficient energy, enthusiasm, etc.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** His stage portrayal of Hamlet was distinctly undercooked.
