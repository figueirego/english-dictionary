### Forms

- patinae — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A paten (a flat type of dish).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; originally

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The colour or incrustation which age and wear give to (chiefly metallic) objects; especially, the green rust which covers works of art such as ancient bronzes, coins and medals.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A green colour tinted with grey, like that of bronze patina.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A gloss or superficial layer.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

   **Example:** He enjoys a patina of respect by the police, despite being an ex-criminal.
