### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To enlarge an object by pushing air (or a gas) into it; to raise or expand abnormally

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** You inflate a balloon by blowing air into it.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To enlarge by filling with air (or a gas).

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The balloon will inflate if you blow into it.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To swell; to puff up.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** to inflate somebody with pride or vanity

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To decompress (data) that was previously deflated.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To represent something as being more important, better, or worse than it actually is; to exaggerate.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** Israel routinely claims that the Gaza Ministry of Health, which is run by Hamas, has a tendency to inflate the number of Palestinian casualties in the Gaza Strip.
