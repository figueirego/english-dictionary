### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To corroborate, prove, or confirm; to demonstrate; to provide evidence for.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** It was a promising idea, but the evidence did not bear out their theory.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To maintain and support to the end; to defend to the last.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To move quickly and sharply in an outward direction during a race; to veer out.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** That horse always bears out on the turns.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a horse
