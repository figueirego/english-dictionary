### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person from Nubia, variously intending
   1. A person from the region of Nubia in modern Egypt and Sudan or a descendant of such a person.

      **Usage:** informal

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person from Nubia, variously intending
   1. A person from any of the former states or provinces known as Nubia or a descendant of such a person.

      **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A person from Nubia, variously intending
   1. Synonym of person of color, any person with dark skin, particularly one from Africa.

      **Usage:** literary; inexact

      **Source synonyms:** person of color (synonym; synonym-of); any person with dark skin (synonym; synonym-of); particularly one from Africa (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A person from Nubia, variously intending
   1. A black slave, particularly one from East Africa.

      **Usage:** obsolete; inexact

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A person from Nubia, variously intending
   1. An Ethiopian Christian, particularly a Tewahedo Orthodox Christian.

      **Usage:** obsolete; inexact

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. An animal from Nubia, particularly
   1. Short for Nubian goat or Anglo-Nubian goat.

      **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of

      **Alternative form of:** Nubian goat or Anglo-Nubian goat

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

   1. A horse from the region of Nubia or a descendant of such a horse.

      **Usage:** obsolete; rare
