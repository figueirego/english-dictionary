### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A debt owed, usually to a business, from the perspective of that business.

   **Example:** One man's receivable is another man's payable.

   **Source antonyms:** payable

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. A debt arising from a sale on account or on credit.

   **Usage:** specifically
