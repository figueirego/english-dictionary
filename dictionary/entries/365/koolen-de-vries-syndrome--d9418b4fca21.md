## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A rare genetic disorder caused by a deletion of a segment of chromosome 17 containing six genes, commonly resulting in low birthweight and muscle tone at birth, poor feeding in infancy, and oromotor dyspraxia together with moderate developmental delays and learning disabilities.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** 17q21.31 microdeletion syndrome; KdVS
