### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Several similar marine flatfish of the righteye flounder family Pleuronectidae&#58;
   1. Pleuronectes platessa (European plaice), commonly found in the North Sea and Irish Sea, with smooth brown skin and red or orange spots.

      **Source synonyms:** plaice-fluke; Dutch plaice

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Several similar marine flatfish of the righteye flounder family Pleuronectidae&#58;
   1. Hippoglossoides platessoides (American plaice), of the North American Atlantic.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Several similar marine flatfish of the righteye flounder family Pleuronectidae&#58;
   1. Pleuronectes quadrituberculatus (Alaska plaice), of the eastern North Pacific.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Several similar marine flatfish of the righteye flounder family Pleuronectidae&#58;
   1. Acanthopsetta nadeshnyi (scale-eye plaice), of the western North Pacific.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**
