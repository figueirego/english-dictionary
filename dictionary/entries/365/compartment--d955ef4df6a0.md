### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A room, or section, or chamber, typically within a vehicle.

   **Example:** Two men were seated in a well-lit compartment of a third-class railway carriage.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One of the parts into which an area is subdivided.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

**Source sense R1.S4**
