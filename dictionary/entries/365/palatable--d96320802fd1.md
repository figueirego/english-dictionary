## Adjective

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Pleasing to the taste, tasty.

   **Example:** For some instant noodles make a palatable, if not especially nutritious, meal.

   **Source synonyms:** ambrosial; appetizing; dainty; delectable; delicious; delish (slang); exquisite; finger-lickin' good (US; colloquial); flavorful; flavorous; flavorsome; gustful (obsolete); lekker; lickerish (obsolete); lip-smacking; luscious; moreish (Britain; informal); mouthwatering; nummy (childish); ono; palatable; sapid; savory; scrummy (childish); tasteful; tasty; toothful (obsolete); toothsome; yummy (childish)

   **Source antonyms:** unpalatable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Tolerable, acceptable.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** The agreement was palatable to both of them.
