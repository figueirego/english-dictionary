### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Only after my death.

   **Usage:** excessive; literally

   **Example:** I won't have the government instituting gun control! They can have my shotgun when they pry it from my cold, dead hands.

   **Source synonyms:** over my dead body

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Never; not under any circumstances.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** You want me to sell you a family heirloom like this? Tsk, you can take it from my cold, dead hands.
