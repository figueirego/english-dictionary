## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

- Unitarians — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Alternative letter-case form of unitarian&#58; any Christian who refutes or disbelieves the doctrine of the Trinity.

   **Usage:** alt-of; now inexact

   **Source antonyms:** Trinitarian

   **Alternative form of:** [unitarian](../2f6/unitarian--bd82ae44a689.md)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A follower of Unitarian Universalism or a similar non-creedal (non-dogmatic) religion (either Christian or non-Christian) that originated historically from Christian Unitarianism.

   **Source synonyms:** Unitarian Universalist

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Alternative letter-case form of unitarian&#58; any monotheist, particularly non-Christian monotheists (Muslims, Jews, etc.) as discussed from a Christian perspective.

   **Usage:** alt-of; rare

   **Alternative form of:** [unitarian](../2f6/unitarian--bd82ae44a689.md)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A member of a political movement advocating a unitary state rather than a federal one, especially the Unitarios of nineteenth century Argentina (known as the Unitarian Party in English).
