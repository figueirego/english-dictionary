### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who employs and directs large groups of (usually agricultural) manual labourers, especially those who are poorly paid and illegally employed.

   **Usage:** UK

   **Example:** Many who immigrate illegally fall victim to gangmasters, who exploit them for low pay under extreme conditions.
