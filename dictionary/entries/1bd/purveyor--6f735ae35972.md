### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who purveys (“furnishes, provides; gets, procures”); a supplier; specifically, one in the business of supplying food or other necessary material goods; a provisioner.

   **Example:** The merchants are the purveyors of fine selections.

   **Source synonyms:** achatour; chandler; proveditor; provedore; provisor

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One who purveys (“furnishes, provides; gets, procures”); a supplier; specifically, one in the business of supplying food or other necessary material goods; a provisioner.
   1. An officer who obtained provisions such as accommodation and food for the household of a monarch or some other high-ranking person; also, an officer in charge of obtaining provisions for an army, a city, etc.

      **Usage:** UK; historical

      **Example:** groom purveyor yeoman purveyor

      **Source synonyms:** achatour; chandler; proveditor; provedore; provisor; foregoer; proveditor

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A person or group that promotes or spreads an idea, a viewpoint, etc.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** purveyors of false information

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. One who arranges or prepares something; an arranger, an orchestrator, a preparer.

   **Usage:** obsolete
