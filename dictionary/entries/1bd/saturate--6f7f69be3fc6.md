### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To cause to become completely permeated with, or soaked (especially with a liquid).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Rain saturated their clothes.

   **Example:** After walking home in the driving rain, his clothes were saturated.

   **Source synonyms:** drench; impregnate; soak

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To fill thoroughly or to excess.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** Modern television is saturated with violence.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To satisfy the affinity of; to cause a substance to become inert by chemical combination with all that it can hold.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** One can saturate phosphorus with chlorine.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To render pure, or of a colour free from white light.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; natural-sciences; optics; physical-sciences; physics
