### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Capable of, or (especially) optimized for, being skimmed (read through quickly).

   **Example:** Nearly all documentation of business processes should be skimmable, with the summaries clearly differentiated from the detailed expositions.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of writing

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Worthy only of being skimmed rather than closely read; that is, not worthy of close reading.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of writing
