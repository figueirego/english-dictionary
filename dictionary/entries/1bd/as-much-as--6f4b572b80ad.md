## Adverb · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Practically, just as, no less than; indicates an action tantamount to, but not identical to, the action described.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** She as much as admitted to lying.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. So much as, even.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

## Conjunction · Record 2

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see as, much.

   **Example:** Take as much as you like.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Used to say that two things are equal in amount or degree

   **Example:** He likes caramel as much as he likes fudge.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. Used to say that an amount is as large as another amount

   **Example:** He earns nearly as much as she does.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. Even though; despite the fact that; much as.

   **Example:** As much as I dislike him, I still voted for him.

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** although — even though; much as
