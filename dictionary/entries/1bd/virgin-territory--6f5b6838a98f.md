### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Land that has never been explored or developed.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source synonyms:** greenfield; virgin field

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Ideas or concepts or activities that have not yet been tried, explored or developed.

   **Usage:** broadly; figuratively; uncountable; usually

   **Example:** The company had engineered plenty of products, but manufacturing them was virgin territory.
