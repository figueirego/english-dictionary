### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A small or narrow opening, gap, slit, or hole.

   **Example:** an aperture in a wall

   **Source synonyms:** chink

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A hole which restricts the diameter of the lightpath through one plane in an optical system.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; natural-sciences; optics; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A hole which restricts the diameter of the lightpath through one plane in an optical system.
   1. The diameter of such a hole which restricts the width of the lightpath through the whole system. For a telescope, this is the diameter of the objective lens.

      **Example:** This telescope has a 100 cm aperture.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** arts; astronomy; engineering; hobbies; lifestyle; natural-sciences; optics; photography; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The (typically) large-diameter antenna used for receiving and transmitting radio frequency energy containing the data used in communication satellites, especially in the geostationary belt. For a comsat, this is typically a large reflective dish antenna; sometimes called an array.

   **Usage:** space flight

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** communication; communications

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The maximum angle between the two generatrices.

   **Usage:** rare

   **Example:** If the generatrix makes an angle θ to the axis, then the aperture is 2θ.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a right circular cone
