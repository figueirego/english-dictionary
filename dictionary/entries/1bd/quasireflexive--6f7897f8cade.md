## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of a relation ~ on a set S&#58; such that every element that is related to some element is also related to itself, i.e. ∀x,y∈S&#58; x~y ⇒ x~x ∧ y~y.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences; set-theory

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of a Banach space X&#58; such that the quotient X ′′ / J(X) has finite dimension d. (It is then said to be "quasireflexive of order d".)

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences; topology
