### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Lacking something essential; often construed with in.

   **Example:** They were deficient in social skills.

   **Example:** His voice is beautiful but deficient in power.

   **Example:** My diet is deficient in vitamin D.
