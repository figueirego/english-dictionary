**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To rub or polish with wax to create a slick, shiny and/or waterproof coating; to wax.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To stiffen by coating or permeating with wax.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

<a id="r1-s4"></a>
