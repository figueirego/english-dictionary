### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Sufficiently.

   **Example:** Those pears aren't ripe enough for the children to eat (them).

   **Example:** You've worked enough; rest for a bit.

   **Example:** We don't have long enough nails.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Fully; quite; used after adjectives to express slight augmentation of the positive degree, and sometimes equivalent to very.

   **Example:** He is ready enough to accept the offer.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. Used after certain adverbs to emphasise that a quality is notable, unexpected, etc.

   **Example:** Talking of Mr Smith, funnily enough, I saw him just the other day.

   **Example:** I left my camera on the train, but luckily enough someone handed it in to lost property.
