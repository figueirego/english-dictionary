### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To damage beyond use or repair; to damage (something) to the point that it effectively ceases to exist.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** The earthquake destroyed several apartment complexes.

   **Example:** Hooligans destroy unprovoked.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To neutralize, undo a property or condition.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Smoking destroys the natural subtlety of the palate.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To put down or euthanize.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Destroying a rabid dog is required by law.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To severely disrupt the well-being of (a person); ruin.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Her divorce destroyed her; she had a nervous breakdown and was severely depressed for more than a year.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To utterly defeat; to crush.

   **Usage:** informal; transitive

   **Example:** Remember when Germany destroyed Brazil 7–1 in the World Cup?

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To remove data.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The memory leak happened because we forgot to destroy the temporary lists.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To exhaust completely and thus recreate or build up.

   **Usage:** slang; transitive

   **Example:** This exercise is going to destroy all parts of your shoulders.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** bodybuilding; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To penetrate sexually in an aggressive way.

   **Usage:** slang; transitive; vulgar

   **Example:** She got destroyed by her lover on the carpet.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To sing a song extremely poorly.

   **Usage:** US; slang; transitive

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. To eat food quickly, hungrily or completely.

   **Usage:** slang; transitive

   **Example:** I could destroy some wings right now.
