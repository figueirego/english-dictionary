## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Something that is given away or handed out for free.

   **Example:** The T-shirt was a giveaway from the company that sells the software.

   **Source synonyms:** freebie

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An event at which things are given away for free.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The act of giving something away for free.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An indicator that makes something obvious or apparent.

   **Example:** The frosting in his beard was a giveaway that he had been munching the cake.

   **Source synonyms:** clue; sign; tell
