## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of a motion picture, having an "R rating" from one more motion picture rating authorities, typically meaning it is suitable only for adults.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** broadcasting; film; media; television

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of a motion picture, having an "R rating" from the Motion Picture Association (MPA), meaning it is suitable only for adults.

   **Usage:** US; not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** broadcasting; film; media; television
