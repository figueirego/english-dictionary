### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of the carnivorous mammals of the genera Mustela, Neogale, Poecilogale, and Lyncodon, having a slender body, a long tail and usually a light brown upper coat and light-coloured belly.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A least weasel (Mustela nivalis).

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of certain other species of the family Mustelidae.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A devious or sneaky person or animal, or a statement that is technically true but intentionally misleading.

   **Usage:** derogatory; informal

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A type of yarn winder used for counting the yardage of handspun yarn; spinner's weasel. It most commonly has a wooden peg or dowel that pops up from the gearing mechanism after a certain number of yards have been wound onto the winder.
