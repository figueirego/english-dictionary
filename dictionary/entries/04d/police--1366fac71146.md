**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Usage:** plural; plural-normally

   **Example:** Call the police!

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A constituted body of officers representing the civil authority of government, empowered to maintain public order and safety, enforce the law, and prevent, detect, and investigate crime.
   1. A department of local (usually municipal) government responsible for general law enforcement.

      **Usage:** Canada; US; historical; plural; plural-normally

      **Example:** The Cook County Sheriff's Department has jurisdiction across most of Chicago but focuses on the unincorporated area and tasks like prisoner transport, leaving the rest to the Chicago Police Department.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; law-enforcement

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A constituted body of officers representing the civil authority of government, empowered to maintain public order and safety, enforce the law, and prevent, detect, and investigate crime.
   1. A branch of the Home Office responsible for general law enforcement within a specific territory.

      **Usage:** UK; plural; plural-normally

      **Example:** Scotland Yard is, technically speaking, only the metropolitan police for Greater London but because of their importance they have special jurisdiction for some crimes across the United Kingdom.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; law-enforcement

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A constituted body of officers representing the civil authority of government, empowered to maintain public order and safety, enforce the law, and prevent, detect, and investigate crime.
   1. Any of the formally enacted law enforcement agencies at various levels of government.

      **Usage:** Australia; New-Zealand; plural; plural-normally
