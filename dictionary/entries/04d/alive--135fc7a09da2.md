### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having life; living; not dead.

   **Usage:** predicative

   **Example:** As long as the plant is alive, he will continue to water it.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In a state of action; in force or operation; existent.

   **Usage:** predicative

   **Example:** to keep the fire alive

   **Example:** to keep the affections alive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Busy with activity of many living beings; swarming; thronged; busy.

   **Usage:** predicative

   **Example:** Although quite dull during the day, the main street comes alive at night, with many bars and clubs opening.

   **Example:** First a solitary blackbird started up, and soon the whole forest was alive with birdsong.

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Carrying electrical current; energized.

   **Usage:** predicative

   **Example:** DANGER&#58; OVERHEAD CATENARY—WIRE IS ALIVE

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Aware of; sensitive to.

   **Usage:** predicative

   **Example:** We are alive to the ongoing potential for terrorist attacks.
