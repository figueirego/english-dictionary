### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of various climbing plants of the genus Muehlenbeckia that grow in New Zealand,
   1. especially, Muehlenbeckia australis

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of various climbing plants of the genus Muehlenbeckia that grow in New Zealand,
   1. scrambling pohuehue (Muehlenbeckia complexa)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of various climbing plants of the genus Muehlenbeckia that grow in New Zealand,
   1. leafless pohuehue (Muehlenbeckia ephedroides)

**Source sense R1.S4**
