### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Pronounced with an audible breath, a brief expulsion of air at the end of the sound.

   **Example:** In English, word-initial single voiceless consonants are aspirated, so that pit is pronounced \[pʰɪt\] but spit is pronounced \[spɪt\].

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; phonetics; phonology; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** [aspirate](../3e8/aspirate--fa1c27db9f2e.md)

   **Source antonyms:** unaspirated

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. In French, describing an initial letter h which, though silent, does not permit liaison or elision with the preceding word.

   **Usage:** French

   **Example:** The h in haine is aspirated, so one says la haine, not l'haine.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences
