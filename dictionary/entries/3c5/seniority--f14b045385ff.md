### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A measure of the amount of time a person has been a member of an organization, as compared to other members, and with an eye towards awarding privileges to those who have been members longer.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** It's an old-fashioned company, with parking spaces and other perks doled out on the basis of seniority.

   **Source synonyms:** anciennity; eldership
