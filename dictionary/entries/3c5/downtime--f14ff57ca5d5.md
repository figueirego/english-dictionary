### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Time lost due to the failure of some system or machinery, such as a computer crash or power outage.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source antonyms:** uptime

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A period of time when work or other activity is less intense or stops.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A period of time set aside for relaxation and rest; leisure time, free time.

   **Usage:** Canada; US; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** I’ve been working all weekend. I need some downtime.
