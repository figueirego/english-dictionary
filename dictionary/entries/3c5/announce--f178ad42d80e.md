### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To give public notice of, especially for the first time; to make known.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** proclaim; publish; make known; herald; declare; promulgate; sound; intimate

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To pronounce; to declare by judicial sentence.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** adjudicate; judge

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To act as announcer for (an event, usually sports).

   **Usage:** US; transitive

   **Example:** Our coach has retired, but occasionally he still announces the games.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To act or work as an announcer.

   **Usage:** US; intransitive

   **Example:** Our coach has retired, but occasionally he still announces.
