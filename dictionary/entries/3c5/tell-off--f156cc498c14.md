### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To number off (a group of soldiers); to divide up (soldiers) in this way.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To assign (someone) to a particular task, duty etc.; to depute, to allocate.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To count off (numbers, members of a sequence etc.); to enumerate.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To rebuke, to reprimand, or to admonish, often in a harsh, angry, direct way.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Joel's boss told him off for being late again.
