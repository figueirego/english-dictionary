### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To edit out (incorrect, offensive, or otherwise undesirable information) from a book or other publication; to cleanse; to purge.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The publisher decided to expurgate the love scene from the book, to make it more child-friendly.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To undertake editing out incorrect, offensive, or otherwise undesirable information from (a book or other publication); to cleanse; to purge.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The publisher decided to expurgate the book, which meant removing the love scene.

   **Source synonyms:** winnow
