### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Able to be separated.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Able to be brought to a form where all occurrences of the dependent and the independent variable are on opposite sides of the equal sign.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a differential equation

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Having a countable dense subset.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematical-analysis; mathematics; sciences

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a topological space

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any of several technical senses relating to the behavior of polynomials or objects over which polynomials can be defined&#58;
   1. Having no repeated roots (where roots are considered in an algebraic closure)

      **Example:** x²\#43;1 is separable, since its roots are i and -i.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** algebra; mathematics; sciences

      **Source context (verbatim):** of a polynomial

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Any of several technical senses relating to the behavior of polynomials or objects over which polynomials can be defined&#58;
   1. Having no repeated roots (where roots are considered in an algebraic closure)
      1. Such that none of the irreducible factors of P have a repeated root.

         **Usage:** obsolete; Galois theory

         **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** algebra; mathematics; sciences

         **Source context (verbatim):** of a polynomial P; of a polynomial

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Any of several technical senses relating to the behavior of polynomials or objects over which polynomials can be defined&#58;
   1. Such that the minimal polynomial of every element of E is a separable polynomial.

      **Usage:** Galois theory

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** algebra; mathematics; sciences

      **Source context (verbatim):** of an algebraic field extension E/F

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Any of several technical senses relating to the behavior of polynomials or objects over which polynomials can be defined&#58;
   1. Satisfying any of several technical conditions on the center of the algebra which generalize the situation of field extensions; see Separable algebra on Wikipedia.Wikipedia

      **Usage:** abstract

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** algebra; mathematics; sciences
