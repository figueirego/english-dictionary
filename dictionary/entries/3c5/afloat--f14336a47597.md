   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source antonyms:** ashore

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. Covered with water, bearing floating objects.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The decks are afloat.

   **Source synonyms:** awash; flooded

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. Covered, overspread, filled (with or in something).

   **Usage:** figuratively; not-comparable

   **Source synonyms:** alive; awash
