### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A jail cell; a period of incarceration in such a cell; a facility containing such cells.

   **Usage:** countable; slang; uncountable

   **Example:** He's in lockup for 30 days for drunk and disorderly.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A storage unit with a door secured by a padlock or deadbolt; a garage.

   **Usage:** UK; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Joe keeps his other car in a lockup downtown.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A condition in which one or more of a vehicle's wheels suddenly cease to rotate due to the application of excessive brake torque, causing the affected wheel(s) to skid.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; mechanical; mechanical-engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A condition where a system stops responding to inputs; a freeze.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Opcode $02 on the MOS 6502 causes a processor lockup if executed.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A device for locking type into position for printing.

   **Usage:** countable; historical; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** media; printing; publishing

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A fixed layout involving one or more logos and possibly related text.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; marketing
