### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The immature and green edible pod of a variety of the common bean, Phaseolus vulgaris, that has relatively flat pods.

   **Example:** Flat beans tend to take longer to cook than other varieties of green beans.

   **Source synonyms:** helda bean; Romano bean; runner bean

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A normal coffee bean (with a flat side), as opposed to a more oval peaberry.
