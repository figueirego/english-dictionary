1. A twist or fold.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

3. The shape of something rotating; a vortex.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

4. The state or condition of being convoluted.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; functional analysis
