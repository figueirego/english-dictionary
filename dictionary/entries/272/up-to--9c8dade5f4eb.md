### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see up, to.

   **Example:** She went up to the third floor.

   **Example:** Go up to the counter and ask.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Capable, ready or equipped, having sufficient material preconditions for, possibly willpower (at a particular moment).

   **Example:** to feel up to it

   **Example:** Are you up to lifting something that heavy?

   **Example:** Are you up to the challenge?

   **Source synonyms:** equal

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Capable, ready or equipped, having sufficient material preconditions for, possibly willpower (at a particular moment).
   1. Willing, interested in

      **Example:** Are you up to going to the beach?

      **Source synonyms:** equal

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. As much as; no more than (also with of).

   **Example:** You can make up to five copies.

   **Example:** Violators may face a fine of up to $300.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Until.

   **Example:** Up to that point, I liked her.

   **Example:** I've read up to chapter 5.

   **Source synonyms:** up until

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Within the responsibility of, to be attributed to the sphere of influence of, having someone or something as authoritative in.

   **Example:** It’s up to you whether to get the blue one or the red one.

   **Example:** It's up to the prosecution to prove that the defendant is guilty.

   **Source synonyms:** down to

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Doing, involved in.

   **Example:** to be up to

   **Example:** I have no idea what they've been getting up to while we've been away.

   **Example:** What are you up to today?

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Doing, involved in.
   1. Devising, scheming (planning something mischievous or inappropriate).

      **Example:** He looked like a man up to no good.

      **Example:** What are you kids up to with all that toilet paper?

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. Considering all members of an equivalence class the same.

   **Example:** There’s only one rooted tree with two leaves, up to ordering.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences
