## Noun

### Forms

- war graves — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A burial place, grave(s) for individual victim(s), soldiers and/or civilians, of warfare, including civil war.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A cemetery reserved for such victims.

   **Usage:** plural-normally

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A vessel that has sank or aircraft that has crashed during war and which serves as the resting place for one or more victims.
