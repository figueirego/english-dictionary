### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make something hard smaller and smoother by gradually rubbing off tiny bits; to wear down by grinding action.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The old dog's teeth had been ground down by constant use.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To weaken someone's morale over a long period.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive
