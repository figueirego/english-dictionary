### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Synonym of printer, printing house, &amp;c., a self-employed printer or printing establishment.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable

   **Example:** France's National Imprimery dates back to the Renaissance but now mostly prints ID cards.

   **Source synonyms:** printer (synonym; synonym-of); printing house (synonym; synonym-of); &amp;c (synonym; synonym-of)
