### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To move ahead.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** Schumacher is beginning to pull away from the rest of the racers.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To move oneself further from a target, either literally or figuratively

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** She pulled away from me when I approached her.

   **Example:** We used to be close, but Paul has been pulling away from me for years.
