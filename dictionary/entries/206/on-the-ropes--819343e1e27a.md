### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Leaning against the ropes of the boxing ring, as when exhausted and nearing defeat or collapse.

   **Example:** The champ had his opponent on the ropes at the end of the third round and knocked him out in the fourth.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** boxing; government; hobbies; lifestyle; martial-arts; military; politics; professional-wrestling; sports; war; wrestling

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Showing signs of imminent failure or collapse.

   **Usage:** broadly; figuratively

   **Example:** After a good start in the stock market, this company seems to be on the ropes.
