## Noun

### Forms

- burning bushes — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A biblical object described in Exodus 3&#58;1-22, used by Yahweh to communicate with Moses.

   **Usage:** Judaism

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any plant of several species of Euonymus&#58;
   1. A winged spindle or winged euonymus (Euonymus alatus), with vivid magenta Fall foliage, native to eastern Asia common as an ornamental and invasive in North America.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any plant of several species of Euonymus&#58;
   1. A western burning bush (Euonymus occidentalis), native to western North America

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any plant of several species of Euonymus&#58;
   1. An eastern burning bush (Euonymus atropurpureus), an ornamental shrub native to the midwestern US that bears a red berry.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A white dittany (Dictamnus albus), a perennial herb which gives off so much essential oil that it can sometimes be lit and will burn briefly without harming the plant.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A forest flame-creeper (Combretum paniculatum), a plant native to Africa.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A summer cypress (Bassia scoparia, syn. Kochia scoparia), a large annual herb in the family Chenopodiaceae, native to Eurasia, introduced to many parts of North America.
