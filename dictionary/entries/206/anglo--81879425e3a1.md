### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An English person or person of English ancestry.

   **Example:** Back when we went to the World Cup in South Africa, we saw many anglos waving English flags.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An English person or person of English ancestry.
   1. An Anglo-Australian (as opposed to Australians of Mediterranean or Middle Eastern background)

      **Usage:** Australia; informal

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A British person or person of British ancestry.

   **Usage:** nonstandard

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Clipping of anglophone.
   1. An English-speaking Quebecer.

      **Usage:** Canada; informal

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Clipping of anglophone.
   1. A North American, especially a white one (regardless of actual ethnicity), whose native language is English (as opposed to Americans who have another native language).

      **Usage:** Southwestern; US; informal; offensive; sometimes

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. An anglo concertina.
