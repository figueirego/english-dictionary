**Source sense R1.S4**

   **Usage:** figuratively; lowercase; often

   **Example:** a Byzantine system of regulations

   **Usage:** figuratively; lowercase; often

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

   **Usage:** figuratively; lowercase; often
