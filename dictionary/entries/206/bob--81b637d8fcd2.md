<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To move gently and vertically, in either a single motion or repeatedly up and down, at or near the surface of a body of water, or similar medium.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The cork bobbed gently in the calm water.

   **Example:** The ball, which we had thought lost, suddenly bobbed up out of the water.

   **Example:** The flowers were bobbing in the wind.

   **Source synonyms:** surge

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To move (something) as though it were bobbing in water.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I bobbed my head underwater and saw the goldfish.

   **Example:** bob one’s head

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To move (something) as though it were bobbing in water.
   1. To perform oral sex on someone.

      **Usage:** transitive; with-on

      **Example:** She bobbed on his knob.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To curtsy.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To strike with a quick, light blow; to tap.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Synonym of blob (“catch eels using worms strung on thread”).
