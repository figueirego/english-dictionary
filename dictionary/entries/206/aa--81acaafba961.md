### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Several waterways in Europe.
   1. A river in northern France, flowing 93 km from near Bourthes, Pas-de-Calais department into the North Sea near Gravelines, Nord department.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Several waterways in Europe.
   1. A small river in the Netherlands, flowing 72 km from near Nederweert, Limburg into the Dieze at 's-Hertogenbosch, North Brabant.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Several waterways in Europe.
