### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To move by dragging one's body along or close to a surface, like a worm or insect, or on one's hands and knees, like a human baby.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Clutching my wounded side, I crawled back to the trench.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To move or progress slowly or laboriously, or with great difficulty (for example due to feebleness) or frequent stops.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The rush-hour traffic crawled around the bypass.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To advance by guile and servility; to act in a servile manner, trying to please someone in order to gain favor.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Don’t come crawling to me with your useless apologies!

   **Source synonyms:** kowtow; grovel

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To spread by extending tendrils, stems, or branches; to creep or trail (as a vine).

   **Example:** The old house was covered in vines crawling over every available surface.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To swim using the crawl stroke.

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** I think I'll crawl instead of breaststroke.

   **Example:** I think I'll crawl the next hundred metres.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Followed by with&#58; see crawl with.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To feel a swarming sensation; to have a sensation like that produced by insects crawling over one's body.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The horrible sight made my skin crawl.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To spread unevenly; to fail to be or remain evenly spread.

   **Usage:** intransitive; ceramic glaze; etc; ceramic glaze; etc

   **Source context (verbatim):** of paint or varnish

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To move over (an area) on hands and knees, or by dragging the body along.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The baby crawled the entire second floor.

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. To move over (an area) slowly, with frequent stops.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** They crawled the downtown bars.

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. To visit (files or web sites) in order to index them for searching.

   **Usage:** Internet; transitive

   **Example:** Yahoo Search has updated its Slurp Crawler to crawl websites faster and more efficiently.
