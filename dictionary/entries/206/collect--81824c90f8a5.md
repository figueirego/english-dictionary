### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To gather together; amass.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Suzanne collected all the papers she had laid out.

   **Example:** The team uses special equipment to collect data on temperature, wind speed and rainfall.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To get; particularly, get from someone.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** A bank collects a monthly payment on a client's new car loan. A mortgage company collects a monthly payment on a house.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To accumulate (a number of similar or related objects), particularly for a hobby or recreation.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** John Henry collects stamps.

   **Example:** I don't think he collects as much as hoards.

   **Example:** My friend from school has started to collects mangas and novels recently

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To pick up or fetch

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Can you collect me from the airport?

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To form a conclusion; to deduce, infer. (Compare gather, get.)

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To collect payments.

   **Usage:** intransitive; often with on or against

   **Example:** He had a lot of trouble collecting on that bet he made.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To come together in a group or mass.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The rain collected in puddles.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To infer; to conclude.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To collide with or crash into (another vehicle or obstacle).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The truck veered across the central reservation and collected a car that was travelling in the opposite direction.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a vehicle or driver
