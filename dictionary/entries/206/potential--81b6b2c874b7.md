## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /pəˈtɛnʃəl/ — source labels: UK; US
- IPA: /po(ʊ)ˈtɛnʃəl/ — source labels: US; rare

### Forms

- potentials — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A currently unrealized ability (with the most common adposition being to).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Even from a young age it was clear that she had the potential to become a great musician.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The gravitational potential&#58; the radial (irrotational, static) component of a gravitational field, also known as the Newtonian potential or the gravitoelectric field.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The work (energy) required to move a reference particle from a reference location to a specified location in the presence of a force field, for example to bring a unit positive electric charge from an infinite distance to a specified point against an electric field.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A verbal construction or form stating something is possible or probable.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** grammar; human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** noumenon; spirit

**Source antonyms:** matter; phenomenon
