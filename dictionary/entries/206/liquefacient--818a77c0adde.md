## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

- liquefacients — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. That which serves to liquefy.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any agent, such as mercury, iodine, etc., that promotes the liquefying processes of the system, and increases the secretions.

   **Usage:** obsolete

   **Example:** potassium hydroxide is a liquefacient of tissues

   **Example:** sodium bicarbonate is a liquefacient to promote the expulsion of the false membrane.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences
