## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˌɹɛf.əˈɹiː/ — source labels: Received-Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An umpire or judge; an official who makes sure the rules are followed during a game.

   **Example:** The referee kicked Jim out of the game for fighting.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person who settles a dispute.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A person who writes a letter of reference or provides a reference by phone call for someone. (In the UK such a person is sometimes, and in the US the person is typically, referred to as a reference.)

   **Usage:** UK

   **Example:** Your application, along with letters from three referees, should be received by January 31.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An expert who judges the manuscript of an article or book to decide if it should be published.
