### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A rescue, especially a financial rescue.

   **Example:** The government bailout of that corporation is going to cost the taxpayers a hundred billion dollars.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The process of exiting an aircraft while in flight.
