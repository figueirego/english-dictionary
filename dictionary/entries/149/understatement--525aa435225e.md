### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A figure of speech whereby something is made to seem smaller or less important than it actually is, either through phrasing or lack of emphasis, often for ironic effect.

   **Usage:** rhetoric; uncountable; usually

   **Example:** A master of understatement

   **Source synonyms:** meiosis

   **Source antonyms:** auxesis; hyperbole

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An instance of such phrasing or lack of emphasis; an incomplete statement.

   **Usage:** countable; usually

   **Example:** Now that's an understatement.

   **Source synonyms:** trivialization

   **Source antonyms:** exaggeration; overstatement

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An incomplete disclosure that intentionally withholds relevant information.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually
