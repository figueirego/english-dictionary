### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Resembling or characteristic of an octopus, for example in having eight (or many) arms.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Widespread or able (from a central point) to control or manipulate many things.
