### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of a work of literature, a writer etc.&#58; being like or having the characteristics of a romance, or poetic tale of a mythic or quasi-historical time; fantastic.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Fictitious, imaginary.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Fantastic, unrealistic (of an idea etc.); fanciful, sentimental, impractical (of a person).

   **Example:** Mary sighed, knowing her ideals were far too romantic to work in reality.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Having the qualities of romance (in the sense of something appealing deeply to the imagination); invoking on a powerfully sentimental idea of life; evocative, atmospheric.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Pertaining to an idealised form of love (originally, as might be felt by the heroes of a romance); conducive to romance; loving, affectionate.

   **Example:** Their kiss started casually, but it slowly turned romantic.

   **Source antonyms:** platonic; non-sexual

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Alternative letter-case form of Romantic

   **Usage:** alt-of

   **Alternative form of:** [Romantic](../13b/romantic--4eca3d90f4c6.md)

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Experiencing romantic attraction.

   **Source synonyms:** allo; alloromantic

   **Source antonyms:** aromantic
