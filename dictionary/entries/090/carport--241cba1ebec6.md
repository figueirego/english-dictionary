### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A roofed structure for automobile storage, usually attached to a house and not fully enclosed like a garage but with at least one side open to the outdoors.

   **Example:** Franklin decided to improve his carport by walling in the sides and turning it into a garage.

   **Source synonyms:** porte cochère
