## Adjective · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Relieved of a duty, burden, responsibility, or pressure.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; not-comparable

   **Example:** off the hook for

   **Example:** Well, you're off the hook for next Tuesday's meeting, but you're still on the hook for this Friday's deadline.

   **Example:** When the boss assigned the project to Tom, the rest of us were relieved to be off the hook.

   **Example:** Without any evidence, the police had to let the suspect off the hook.

   **Source antonyms:** on the hook

## Adjective · Etymology 2 · Record 2

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Of a telephone, having an open connection; not hung up.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** I think he left the phone off the hook so that nobody would call him.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Of a telephone, ringing frequently.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The phone was ringing off the hook all night.
