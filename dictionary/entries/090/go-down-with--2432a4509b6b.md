### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see go, down, with.

   **Example:** I didn't go down there on my own, I went down with Sam and Betty.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To catch or contract (an illness).

   **Example:** Everyone's gone down with the flu, and I'm here in the office all by myself.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To die in the sinking of (a ship or boat).

   **Example:** Edward Smith went down with the Titanic.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport
