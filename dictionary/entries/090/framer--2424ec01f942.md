### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who makes frames for paintings.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person who assembles the frame of a ship.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A person who assembles the timbers of a wood-framed building.

   **Usage:** US

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A person who writes a new law.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A person who writes a new law.
   1. Any of the delegates to the Constitutional Convention that drafted the United States Constitution and the Bill of Rights.

      **Usage:** US; capitalized; historical; plural-normally; sometimes

      **Example:** the Framers of the Constitution

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; politics

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A person who frames another, attempting to have them convicted of a crime they did not commit.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A person who embeds another person's web pages in an HTML frame, so that they misleadingly appear to be part of the framing site.

   **Usage:** Internet
