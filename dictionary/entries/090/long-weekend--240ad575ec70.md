### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A weekend extended by one or more days off falling on a day or days immediately preceding or following it.

   **Example:** Labor Day weekend is the long weekend that defines the end of Summer in the US.

   **Example:** I took a long weekend with my wife when no one else had a holiday.
