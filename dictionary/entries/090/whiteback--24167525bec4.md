### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Various species that have a white back, including&#58;
   1. A wild duck, the canvasback (Aythya valisineria)

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Various species that have a white back, including&#58;
   1. The white-backed duck. (Thalassornis leuconotus)

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Various species that have a white back, including&#58;
   1. The white-backed griffon vulture (Gyps africanus)

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
