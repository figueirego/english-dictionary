### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A village providing a high standard of housing, built from the late 18th century onwards by landowners and business magnates to house workers.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A miniature model of a village, used as a toy or as a visitor attraction.
