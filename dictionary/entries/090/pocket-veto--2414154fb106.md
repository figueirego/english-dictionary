### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A (de facto) veto of a bill by the US President by restricting Congressional action on the bill, as, figuratively, by carrying the bill in his pocket until the deadline for signing it has passed, at a time when Congress has adjourned.

   **Usage:** US

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any similar process by which an executive prevents a bill from becoming law by failing to sign it, but without expressly vetoing it.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; politics
