### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A narrow, wooden or plastic platform mounted on pairs of wheels, on which one stands and propels oneself by pushing along the ground with one foot.

   **Usage:** recreation

   **Example:** She's been riding skateboards since she was eight years old.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports

   **Example:** A new crossover model will arrive next year and will be based on an existing skateboard.
