## Noun · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈæn.ɪ.meɪ/ — source labels: General-American; Received-Pronunciation

### Forms

- anime — plural
- animes — plural
- animé — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An animated work that originated in Japan, regardless of the artistic style.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An artistic style originating in, and associated with, Japanese animation, and that has also been adopted by a comparatively low number of animated works from other countries.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** I can draw an anime version of you, if you want.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An animated work, regardless of the country of origin.

   **Usage:** countable; proscribed; rare

**Source synonyms:** Japanimation (dated) — a Japanese animated work; Japanime (dated) — a Japanese animated work
