### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An individual's, ingroup's or society's view of the world and how it is to be interpreted.

   **Source synonyms:** cosmovision

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The totality of one's beliefs about reality.

   **Source synonyms:** cosmology

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A general philosophy or view of life.

   **Example:** The Elizabethan worldview differs from a modern worldview.
