### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A bearing on the drive shaft of a vessel, usually made of brass with an inner grooved rubber lining that is lubricated by the surrounding water entering the grooves as it rotates. It is a variant of the stave bearing.

   **Example:** When you replace a drive shaft on your boat, it is also a good idea to replace the cutlass bearing where the drive shaft exits the boat.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport
