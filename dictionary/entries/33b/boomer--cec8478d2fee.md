### Forms

- boomers — plural

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A baby boomer.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** Boomers came of age in the era of the Beatles and Led Zeppelin.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. An elderly person, regardless of generation.

   **Usage:** broadly; derogatory; slang; sometimes

   **Example:** I'm going to be a boomer by the time this thing is over.

   **Source synonyms:** antiquity; codger; gadgie; gaffer; geezer; gramps; greybeard; old codger; old fart; old man; unc; whitebeard

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. A person who is too old to be familiar with the use of recent technology.

   **Usage:** broadly; derogatory; slang; sometimes

   **Example:** I'm such a boomer; I have no idea how to use this phone.
