## Noun

### Forms

- rules of the road — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of the requirements for how drivers, bicyclists, etc, behave and operate while using public roadways.

   **Example:** To obtain a driving licence, you must pass a test to show that you understand the rules of the road.

   **Example:** It is a rule of the road in this country that a motorist must yield to a pedestrian who is in a pedestrian crossing.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The regulation requiring all traffic (travelling on a road or otherwise) to keep either to the left or the right.

   **Source context (verbatim):** with the

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. (nautical) A regulation concerning safe handling of vessels on roadsteads, seaways, sea lanes, etc.

   **Source context (verbatim):** with the

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A basic (fundamental) and general requirement or principle for how to operate or behave in a certain context or domain (for example, an industry, an occupation, a company, an association).

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively

   **Example:** You can't fill in fraudulent information on this form if you want to keep your job at this company. That's not a suggestion, that's just a rule of the road around here.
