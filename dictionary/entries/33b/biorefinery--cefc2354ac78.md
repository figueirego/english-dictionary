### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A facility that carries out coproduction of a range of biologically-based products (food, feed, materials, chemicals) and energy (fuels, power, heat) from biomass.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The coproduction process itself.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** biorefining
