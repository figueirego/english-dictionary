## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see sour, grapes.

   **Usage:** plural; plural-only

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Things that somebody pretends to despise because they cannot obtain or have them.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; plural; plural-only

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A putting down or expression of disdain about something that one desires but cannot have.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; plural; plural-only

   **Example:** I think his comments about that new car are just sour grapes because he can't afford it.
