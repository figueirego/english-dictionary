### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Her; the feminine object of a verb or preposition that also appears as the subject.

   **Usage:** feminine; personal; pronoun; reflexive; singular; third-person

   **Example:** Sandra injured herself.

   **Example:** The United States is causing problems for herself with this policy.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. She; an intensifier of the female subject or object, often used to indicate the directness or exclusiveness of that person .

   **Usage:** emphatic; feminine; personal; pronoun; singular; third-person

   **Example:** She was injured herself.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The subject or non-reflexive object of a predicate; she (used of upper-class ladies, or sarcastically, of women who imagine themselves to be more important than others)

   **Usage:** Ireland; feminine; personal; pronoun; singular; third-person

   **Example:** What's herself up to this time?

   **Example:** Have you seen herself yet this morning?
