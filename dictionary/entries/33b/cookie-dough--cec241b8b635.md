### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A paste usually formed of flour, sugar, water, eggs, and other ingredients which, when baked, hardens to form a cookie or a number of cookies.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A flavor designed to emulate the taste of the paste.

   **Usage:** uncountable
