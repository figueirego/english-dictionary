<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An area of wet (water-saturated), spongy (soft) land, often with trees, generally a rich ecosystem for certain plants and animals but ill-suited for many agricultural purposes. (A type of wetland. Compare marsh, bog, fen.)

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To drench or fill with water.

   **Example:** The boat was swamped in the storm.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To overwhelm; to make too busy, or overrun the capacity of.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** I have been swamped with paperwork ever since they started using the new system.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To plunge into difficulties and perils; to overwhelm; to ruin; to wreck.

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To clear (a road or an area) of brush, particularly so as to create a path for loggers to be able to access trees.

   **Usage:** Appalachia
