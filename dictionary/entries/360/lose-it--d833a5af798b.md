### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To be overcome with emotion.
   1. To become explosively angry; to lose one's temper.

      **Usage:** informal

      **Example:** When my dad found out I had failed the exams, he just lost it.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To be overcome with emotion.
   1. To feel devastated, or distraught, especially when one's sadness is overwhelming.

      **Usage:** informal

      **Example:** When she heard the news about her cousin's death, she lost it.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To be overcome with emotion.
   1. To begin to laugh uncontrollably.

      **Usage:** informal

      **Example:** When the teacher's chair broke, the class completely lost it.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To be overcome with emotion.
   1. To lose one's mind, go crazy.

      **Usage:** informal

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To cease to have a skill or ability, to lose one's touch, to be washed up.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** When you think about all she's done for the sport, it's kind of sad, but she's completely lost it over the past few years.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To lose control of a situation.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see lose, it.
