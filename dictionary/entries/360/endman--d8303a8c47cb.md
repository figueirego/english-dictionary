## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A man at one end of a row or line.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One who takes the most advanced or extreme view of anything; an extremist.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A type of stock character common in American minstrel shows, occurring in pairs, flanking a character in the middle, and distinguished by exchanging jokes in between skits and songs.

   **Usage:** obsolete

   **Source synonyms:** cornerman
