### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Characterized by camp or kitsch, especially when deliberate or intentional.

   **Example:** Stu liked to watch campy B-movie horror flicks from the 1950s, not because they scared him, but because he found them funny.

   **Source synonyms:** camp; corny; hammy; sill; tawdry; vulgar
