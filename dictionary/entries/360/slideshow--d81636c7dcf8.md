### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A presentation of a series of photographic slides, usually with a spoken or recorded explanation.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any sequence of images, shown one after the other (not simultaneously).

   **Source synonyms:** [slide deck](../21d/slide-deck--8775e9d57d7c.md)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any sequence of images, shown one after the other (not simultaneously).
   1. The situation where a game has a low frame rate due to insufficient computing power.

      **Usage:** informal

      **Example:** When there are too many enemies, the whole thing just turns into a slideshow.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** video-games
