## Noun

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈæŋ.ɡlɚ/ — source labels: US

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who fishes with a hook and line.

   **Example:** A throng of anglers lined the trout stream on opening day of trout season.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An anglerfish, Lophius piscatorius.

   **Example:** The angler lured a smaller fish into reach with the appendage on its head.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Someone who tries to work an angle; a person who schemes or has an ulterior motive.

   **Example:** Jonas was a consummate angler when it came the company's leave policy; he had it figured so he only needed to work six months out of the year.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A thief who uses a hooked stick to steal goods out of shop-windows, grates, etc.

   **Usage:** UK; archaic; thieves' cant
