### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of marking off a boundary or setting a limit, notably by belligerents signing a treaty or ceasefire.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A limit thus fixed, in full demarcation line.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any strictly defined separation.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** There is an alleged, in fact somewhat artificial demarcation in the type of work done by members of different trade unions.
