**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any device or mechanism that enables time to be measured or regulated.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** A sundial is a type of clock that measures time through the movement of the sun's shadow.

   1. In particular, a mechanical or electronic device that indicates the time of day through hands or a numeric display, and sometimes also through chimes.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Example:** The clock on the mantlepiece says midnight, but the church clock says quarter past.
