### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Not biological; not consisting of a biological substance or substances.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** nonbiological washing powder

   **Source synonyms:** abiotic

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. not according to parentage or ancestry; not based on biological kinship; by marriage, adoption, close friendship, etc.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; Of a relation

   **Example:** nonbiological father/mother
