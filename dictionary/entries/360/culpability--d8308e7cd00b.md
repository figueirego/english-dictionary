### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The degree of one's blameworthiness in the commission of a crime or offence.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** criminal culpability

   **Example:** The investigation established his culpability in the fraud.

   **Example:** Questions of legal culpability remain unresolved.

   **Source synonyms:** guiltiness; blameworthiness; culpability; guilt; guiltiness; noninnocence; uninnocence

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The amount of liability for a fault or wrongdoing.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** degree of culpability

   **Example:** establish culpability
