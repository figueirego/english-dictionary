### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A punk subculture and philosophy where individuals, often from marginalized groups such as the LGBTQIA+ community, neurodivergent, or disabled people, reclaim and reject the dehumanization they experience by society. Instead of trying to be seen as "human" according to a narrow, enforced definition, they embrace being perceived as non-human, aligning with abstract or void-like identities.
