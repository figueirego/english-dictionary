## Adjective · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Pronunciation

### Forms

- dizziest — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Experiencing a sensation of whirling and of being giddy, unbalanced, or lightheaded.

   **Example:** I stood up too fast and felt dizzy.

   **Source synonyms:** woozy

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Producing giddiness.

   **Example:** We climbed to a dizzy height.

   **Source synonyms:** dizzying

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Empty-headed, scatterbrained or frivolous; ditzy.

   **Example:** My new secretary is a dizzy blonde.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. simple, half-witted.

   **Usage:** UK; Yorkshire; dialectal
