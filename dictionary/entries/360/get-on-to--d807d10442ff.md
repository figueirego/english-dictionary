### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see get on, to.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To contact (someone) in order to raise or discuss a certain matter.

   **Usage:** UK

   **Example:** That pipe is leaking again. I'll get on to the plumber in the morning.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To progress to; to start working on.

   **Example:** I haven't got time to work on this report today; I'll get on to it tomorrow.
