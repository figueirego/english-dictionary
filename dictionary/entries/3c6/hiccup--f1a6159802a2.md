### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A spasm of the diaphragm, or the resulting sound.

   **Example:** There was a loud hiccup from the back of the room and the class erupted in laughter.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A spasm of the diaphragm, or the resulting sound.
   1. A case of the hiccups; the condition of having the spasms of hiccupping; singultus; diaphragmatic myoclonus.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any spasm or sudden change.

   **Usage:** broadly

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A minor obstacle or setback.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** There's been a slight hiccup in the processing of this quarter's results.

   **Source synonyms:** hang-up
