## Noun

### Forms

- halls of mirrors — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A carnival attraction with curved mirrors (funhouse mirrors) that distort the viewer's appearance.

   **Example:** The hall of mirrors was her favorite attraction at the fair.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A situation confusing for its false appearances, especially when they are compounded upon one another.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** The lies had become a hall of mirrors by the time the scam was exposed.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A glitch in some three-dimensional games where a missing texture prevents part of the scene from being rendered correctly, causing a trail of previously rendered frames to appear instead.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** video-games
