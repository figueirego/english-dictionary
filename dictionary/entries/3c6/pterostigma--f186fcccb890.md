### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A spot on the outer wing of insects which is often thickened or coloured and so stands out from the rest; it is a group of specialized cells and has an effect on flight dynamics.

   **Example:** The pterostigma is particularly noticeable in dragonflies.
