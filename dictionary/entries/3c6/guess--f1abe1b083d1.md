### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To reach a partly (or totally) unconfirmed conclusion; to engage in conjecture; to speculate.

   **Example:** We can only guess at what was going through her mind.

   **Example:** She guessed that the delivery driver must have got stuck in traffic.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To solve by a correct conjecture; to conjecture rightly.

   **Example:** He who guesses the riddle shall have the ring.

   **Example:** You guessed the right answer!

   **Example:** You will never guess what happened next.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To suppose, to imagine (introducing a proposition of uncertain plausibility).

   **Example:** That album is quite hard to find, but I guess you could try ordering it online.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To think, conclude, or decide (without a connotation of uncertainty). Usually in first person&#58; "I guess".

   **Usage:** colloquial

   **Example:** "I guess you were right." "What did he say?" "He guesses you were right."

   **Example:** "I guess I'll go to bed."

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To hit upon or reproduce by memory.

   **Usage:** obsolete

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** assume; conjecture; hypothesize; speculate; suppose; surmise; take a stab
