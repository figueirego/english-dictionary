**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A state of being pleased or contented; gratification.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** He remembered with pleasure his home and family.

   **Example:** I get a lot of pleasure from watching others work hard while I relax.

   **Source synonyms:** delight; gladness; gratification; happiness; indulgence; satisfaction

   **Source antonyms:** displeasure; pain

2. A person, thing or action that causes enjoyment.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** It was a pleasure to meet you.

   **Example:** Having a good night's sleep is one of life's little pleasures.

   **Source synonyms:** delight; joy; kick (informal)

3. Sexual enjoyment.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. One's preference.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** What is your pleasure&#58; coffee or tea?

   **Source synonyms:** desire; fancy; want; will; wish

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The will or desire of someone or some agency in power.

   **Usage:** formal; uncountable

   **Example:** to hold an office at pleasure&#58; to hold it indefinitely until it is revoked
