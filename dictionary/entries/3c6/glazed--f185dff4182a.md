**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Source synonyms:** glassy-eyed; glassy

   **Source antonyms:** unglazed; nonglazed; sharp-eyed; clear-eyed; lively; sharp

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Having a glaze (a coating).

   **Example:** a glazed ham

   **Example:** a glazed doughnut

   **Source synonyms:** glacé

   **Source antonyms:** unglazed; nonglazed; uncoated; noncoated
