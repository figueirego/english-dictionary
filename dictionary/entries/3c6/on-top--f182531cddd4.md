## Adverb

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. On the highest point or surface.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Stand on top and look down.

   **Example:** A cake with cream on top.

   **Example:** I can't take my undershirt off because I am wearing many more garments on top.

   **Source synonyms:** on top of

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In a dominant position.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; not-comparable

   **Example:** At the end of the season, Manchester United came out on top.
