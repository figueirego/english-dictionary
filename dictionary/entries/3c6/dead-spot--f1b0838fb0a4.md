### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An area that has no radio (or wi-fi) reception.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A part of a scene that has insufficient lighting.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** broadcasting; cinematography; film; media; television

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A location around an earthquake epicentre that is prone to earthquake.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; geology; natural-sciences; seismology

   **Source synonyms:** blind spot; blind zone

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**
