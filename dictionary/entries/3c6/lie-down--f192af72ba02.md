### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To assume a horizontal position.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Lie down on the bed until you feel better.

   **Source antonyms:** get up

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To assume a horizontal position.
   1. A standard command to a herding dog to assume this position, owing to its effect on the herd's movement.

      **Usage:** imperative; intransitive

      **Source antonyms:** get up

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To be lazy or remiss.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** He never got promoted because he was always lying down on the job.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To submit passively.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** I'm not going to lie down and take this!

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To have sexual intercourse (with).

   **Usage:** euphemistic

   **Source context (verbatim):** with 'with'
