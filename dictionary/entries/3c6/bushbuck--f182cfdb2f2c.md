### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Either of two species of antelope (Tragelaphus scriptus or Tragelaphus sylvaticus, considered by some sources to be the single species Tragelaphus scriptus) found in Sub-Saharan Africa.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any relatives of the above that share the same habitat.

   **Usage:** informal
