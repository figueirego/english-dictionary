### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The state representative of, or second-in-command of a governor, or Crown, who usually acts as the nominal chief executive officer of the realm.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The representative of the Crown in the provincial legislatures of Canada.

   **Usage:** Canada

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The next-in-line to the chief executive of a primary political division of the United States of America, such as a state, second to the governor.

   **Usage:** US
