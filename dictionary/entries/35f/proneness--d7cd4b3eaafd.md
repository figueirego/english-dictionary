## Noun

### Forms

- pronenesses — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The quality or state of being prone, or of bending downward.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** The proneness of animals is opposed to the erect posture of human beings.

   **Source antonyms:** supineness

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The state of lying with the face down.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Descent; declivity.

   **Usage:** dated; uncountable; usually

   **Example:** the proneness of a hill

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Inclination of mind, heart, or temper; propension; disposition.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** proneness to self-gratification
