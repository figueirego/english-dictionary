### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A swindler or grifter who sells worthless or fraudulent remedies, cures, or solutions such as snake oil, often an aggressive or showy manner.

   **Example:** She was dubbed a "modern day snake oil salesman" after she managed to swindle over thousands of internet customers.

   **Source synonyms:** bristler (obsolete); bunco artist; bunco-steerer (US; slang); chiseler; con artist; confidence artist; confidence trickster; conman; flimflammer; grifter; lugger (Australia; US); magsman; mountebank; saltimbanco; sharpster; sharker (archaic); shark-gull (obsolete); slicker; smooth operator; spiv; charlatan; con artist; conman; duper; fraud; fraudster; grifter; hustler; lugger (Australia; US); scammer; shyster; snake oil salesman; snake (figuratively); swindler; trickster
