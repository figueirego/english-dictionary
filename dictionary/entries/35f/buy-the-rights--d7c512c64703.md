**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To acquire the legal rights to a work, property, story, or intellectual creation, especially for purposes such as publication, adaptation, or distribution.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** The studio decided to buy the rights to the novel before any competitors could bid.
