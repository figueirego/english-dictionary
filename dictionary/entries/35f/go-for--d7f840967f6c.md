### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see go, for.

   **Example:** I'll go for some milk.

   **Example:** If John goes for three days without sleep, he will be very tired.

   **Example:** I need to go for a checkup at the clinic.

   **Example:** Tickets are going for upwards of $100.

   **Example:** I've gotta go for a job interview next Mon.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To try for, to attempt to reach.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I'll go for the world record.

   **Example:** Go for it!

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To undertake (an action); to choose an option.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** His phone was off and I couldn't ask his permission, so I decided to just go for it.

   **Example:** I'll go for a swim if it's warm enough.

   **Example:** I went for the pay-as-you-go plan.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To attack.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Careful, he'll go for your throat!

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To develop a strong interest in, especially in a sudden manner; to be infatuated with.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Clyde took one look at Bonnie and really went for her.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To favor, accept; to have a preference for.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Management won't go for such a risky project now.

   **Example:** Do you want to climb the mountain with me? Yeah, I could go for that.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To fall for (a trick)

   **Usage:** informal; transitive

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To apply equally to.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Stop taking my food from the fridge! That goes for you too, Nick!

   **Example:** What I'm about to say goes for all of you.

   **Example:** My wife hates football, and that goes for me as well.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To suffice to be used for; to serve as.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** It's a desk that goes for a dresser too.

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. To be accepted as.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. To cost.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** Here in Texas, gas can go for as little as two dollars a gallon.
