## Adverb

### Forms

- all-but — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Very nearly; everything short of. Most often precedes a verb or adjective.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The food is all but finished.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Everything except. Typically precedes a noun or noun phrase.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** All but three of the students skipped school the day of the parade.

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** almost; nearly; nigh on
