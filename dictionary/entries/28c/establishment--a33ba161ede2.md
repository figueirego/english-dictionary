### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act or process of establishing; a ratifying or ordaining; settlement; confirmation.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Since their establishment of the company in 1984, they have grown into a global business.

   **Source synonyms:** foundation

   **Source antonyms:** abolition

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The state of being established, founded, etc.; fixed state.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The firm celebrated twenty years since their establishment by updating their look.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. That which is established; as a form of government, a permanent organization, business or force, or the place where one is permanently fixed for residence.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Pedro's is a fine establishment serving a variety of delicious food.

   **Example:** exposing the shabby parts of the establishment

   **Source synonyms:** foundation

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The ruling class or authority group in a society; especially, an entrenched authority dedicated to preserving the status quo.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** It was clear that they viewed the British establishment as qualitatively different from the French establishment.

   **Example:** It's often necessary to question the establishment to get things done.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The number of staff required to run a department or organisation (often used in the context of healthcare and other public services).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The cancer department has an establishment of 10 doctors and 30 nurses.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The institution and official status of a church as a state church, especially that of the Church of England and historically of Ireland.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Not all Anglicans have supported establishment.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity

   **Source antonyms:** disestablishment
