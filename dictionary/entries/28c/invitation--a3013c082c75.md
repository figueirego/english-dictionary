<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of inviting; solicitation; the requesting of a person's company.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** an invitation to a party, to a dinner, or to visit a friend

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A document or verbal message conveying an invitation.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** We need to print off fifty invitations for the party.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
