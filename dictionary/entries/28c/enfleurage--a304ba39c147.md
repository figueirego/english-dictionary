### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The process of extracting fragrance (essential oils) from flowers by using unscented wax or fat, then extracting with alcohol.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** The perfumes of plants like jasmine could only be extracted by enfleurage, as other methods of the time would denature the scents.
