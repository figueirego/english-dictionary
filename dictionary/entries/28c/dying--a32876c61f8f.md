### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Approaching death; about to die; moribund.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The dying dog was put out of his misery with a single shot!

   **Example:** a dying fire

   **Example:** The battlefield was littered with the dead and dying.

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Declining, terminal, or drawing to an end.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** In the dying moments of daylight I glimpsed a sail on the horizon.

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Pertaining to death, or the moments before death.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** His dying words were of his mother.
