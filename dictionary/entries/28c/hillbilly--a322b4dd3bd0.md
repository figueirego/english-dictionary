## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Someone who is from the hills; especially from a rural area, with a connotation of a lack of refinement or sophistication.

   **Usage:** derogatory; often

   **Source synonyms:** hick; bogger (Ireland; UK); bogtrotter (Ireland); boor; bucolic; bydlo (Eastern; Europe; Russia); culchie (Ireland); carl; churl; corncob (US); cornhusker (US); country bumpkin; country cousin; clodhopper; dubbo (Australia); joskin; hayseed (US); hick; hob (obsolete); hobnail (obsolete); hog-rubber (obsolete); honyock (US); hunk (US); lob (obsolete); promdi (Philippines); provincial; rube (US); rustic; woolly back (UK); yokel

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A white person from the rural southern part of the United States, especially the Southeastern states.

   **Usage:** ethnic; slur
