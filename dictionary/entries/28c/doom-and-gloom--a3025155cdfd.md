### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Something considered indicative of feelings of pessimism and despair.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** The economic forecast contained the usual doom and gloom.

   **Example:** His sister was all doom and gloom after she lost the part.
