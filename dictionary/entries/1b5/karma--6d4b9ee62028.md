### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The sum total of a person's actions, which determine the person's next incarnation in samsara, the cycle of death and rebirth.

   **Usage:** Hinduism; Jainism; Sikhism; countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Buddhism; lifestyle; religion

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

   **Usage:** countable; uncommon; uncountable
