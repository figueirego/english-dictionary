### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To tilt one's face upwards slightly, thus raising one's nose, and often to look downwards at someone, as a sign of contempt, disgust, or scorn.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source synonyms:** hold one's nose up

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Followed by at.
   1. To refuse something offered, especially with contempt, disgust, or scorn, or with apparent disregard about offending the offerer.

      **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

      **Example:** I tried to help, but they turned up their noses at my advice.

      **Source synonyms:** hold one's nose up

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Followed by at.
   1. To regard or treat someone with contempt or scorn; to scorn; also, to disregard or ignore someone in a contemptuous or scornful way.

      **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

      **Source synonyms:** hold one's nose up
