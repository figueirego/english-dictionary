### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To give or bestow (e.g. a quality or property).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The sun imparts warmth.

   **Example:** to impart food to the poor

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To give a part or to share.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** bequeath; bestow; give; accord; add; assign; award; bestow; bequeath; confer; contribute; deliver; do; donate; furnish; give; give away; grant; hand; hand in; hand over; make over; minister (archaic); pass; present; provide; render; supply; turn in; turn over

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To make known; to show (by speech, writing etc.).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** disclose; tell; announce; forerun; blare; declare; disclose; herald; impart; make known; publish; proclaim; promulgate; promulge; acquaint; advise; apprise; discuss (obsolete); enlighten; impart; inform; keep informed; let know; notify; tell

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To hold a conference or consultation.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**
