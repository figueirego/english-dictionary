### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Capable of giving out a deep, resonant sound.

   **Example:** The highlight of the hike was the sonorous cave, which produced a ringing echo from the hiker’s shouts.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Full of sound and rich, as in language or verse.

   **Example:** He was selected to give the opening speech thanks to his imposing, sonorous voice.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Wordy or grandiloquent.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Produced with a relatively open vocal tract and relatively little obstruction of airflow.
