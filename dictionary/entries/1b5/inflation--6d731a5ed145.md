### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An act, instance of, or state of expansion or increase in size, especially by injection of a gas or liquid.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The inflation of the balloon took five hours.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An increase in the quantity of money, leading to a devaluation of existing money, adjusted for by way of higher nominal values.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Due to inflation, the monthly gym fee is rising by 10% from January.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** economics; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Undue expansion or increase, as of academic grades.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An extremely rapid expansion of the universe, theorized to have occurred very shortly after the Big Bang.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astronomy; cosmology; natural-sciences
