### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To embarrass oneself or others in public by behaving foolishly.

   **Usage:** UK; idiomatic

   **Example:** He tends to make an exhibition of himself at parties, trying to draw attention.

   **Example:** Stop making an exhibition of yourself and let others enjoy the moment.
