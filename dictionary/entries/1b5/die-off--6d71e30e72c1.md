### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To suffer a series of deaths; to die one by one; to progressively become extinct. (If a group of plants, animals, or people dies off, the entire group dies over a period of time.)

   **Source synonyms:** die out

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To become defunct or nonexistent.

   **Example:** The student guitar club died off after a few years.

   **Source synonyms:** die out

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of music, noise, etc., to reduce in volume until no longer audible.

   **Source synonyms:** die down
