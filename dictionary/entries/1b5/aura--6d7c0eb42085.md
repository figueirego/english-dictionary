### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A subtle emanation or exhalation of any substance, such as an aroma or odour.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A distinctive atmosphere or quality surrounding or associated with something or someone.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable; of

   **Example:** This place has an aura of 19th century Paris.

   **Example:** His latest successes have endued him with an aura of invincibility.

   **Source context (verbatim):** with about

   **Source synonyms:** air; feeling; mood; spirit; vibe

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A supposed emanation from and surrounding a living person or thing, regarded by mystics as consisting of the essence of the individual.

   **Usage:** also; countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** parapsychology; pseudoscience

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The charisma or suave excellence of a person, especially implying their vibe or energy.

   **Usage:** Internet; uncountable

   **Example:** He has insane aura.

   **Source synonyms:** rizz

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A subjective sensation experienced at the onset of a neurological condition, especially a migraine headache or epileptic seizure.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; pathology; sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A surrounding.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable
