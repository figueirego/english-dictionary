### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Strict adherence to a given form of conduct, practice etc.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One of several alternative computational paradigms for a given theory.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An approach to interpretation and/or evaluation focused on the (usually linguistic) structure of a literary work rather than on the contexts of its origin or reception.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** literature; media; publishing

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

**Source sense R1.S5**

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics; sciences
