### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A small, exclusive group of individuals, usually having some common characteristic such as lifestyle or social status, or common purpose; specifically one formed for disreputable purposes, or which purports to be an authority on a particular matter; a cabal.

   **Example:** This school used to be really friendly, but now everyone keeps to their own cliques.

   **Source synonyms:** camp; coterie; inner circle; circle; faction; gang

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A subgraph isomorphic to a complete graph.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** The problem of finding the largest clique in an arbitrary graph is NP-complete.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** graph-theory; mathematics; sciences

   **Source antonyms:** independent set

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A group of related websites that link to each other, like a webring but with exclusive membership determined by the clique owner.

   **Usage:** Internet; broadly

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An informal faction in the Republic of China (1912–1949) led by one or more warlords exercising regional control through local armed forces (particularly during the Warlord Era); a regional junta.

   **Usage:** China; broadly; historical
