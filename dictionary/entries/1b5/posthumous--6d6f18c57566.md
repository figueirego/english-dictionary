## Adjective

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈpɒs.t͡ʃʊ.məs/ — source labels: UK
- IPA: /ˈpɑs.t͡ʃə.məs/ — source labels: US

### Forms

- post-humous — alternative
- postumous — alternative; archaic

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. After the death of someone.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Taking place after one's own death.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Artists obscure during their life often receive posthumous recognition, too late for them to enjoy.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. In reference to a work, published after the author's death.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** His memoirs were his posthumous revenge on enemies he dared not take on alive.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. In reference to a musical opus, published or initially performed after the composer's death.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Born after the death of one's father.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; originally

   **Example:** Posthumous orphans never even knew their fathers.

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** post mortem; post obitum

**Source antonyms:** antemortem; anthumous; predeath; prehumous; premortem
