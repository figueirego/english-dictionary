### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An insoluble dark, bright blue pigment, ferric ferrocyanide (equivalent to ferrous ferricyanide), used in painting and dyeing, and as an antidote for certain kinds of heavy metal poisoning.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; inorganic-chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>
