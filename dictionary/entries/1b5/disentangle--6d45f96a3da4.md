### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To free something from entanglement; to extricate or unknot.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I had to disentangle him from his own shoelaces.

   **Source synonyms:** unsnarl; untangle; disentangle; feeze (Scotland); tease out; unravel; unsnarl; untangle

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To unravel; to separate into discrete components or units.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** unweave

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To become free or untangled.

   **Usage:** intransitive
