## Noun · Record 2

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. An inexact quantity or number, typically understood to be between 10 or 20 and 100.

   **Usage:** plural; plural-only

   **Example:** Our houses are tens of meters apart, so we don't have to worry about noise from our neighbours.

   **Example:** tens of thousands of voters

   **Source synonyms:** dozens

<a id="r2-s2"></a>
