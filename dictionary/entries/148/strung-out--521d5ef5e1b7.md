## Adjective · Record 1

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Experiencing withdrawal symptoms of an addiction.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Example:** While he was strung out, he ranted about conspiracies that he couldn't remember when he sobered.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Widely spaced.

   **Example:** After the storm the armada was strung out over the ocean, unable to cover each other in battle.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Prolonged in an unnecessary or time-filling manner.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; informal

   **Example:** Then I had to sit through a long strung-out discussion about nothing in particular.
