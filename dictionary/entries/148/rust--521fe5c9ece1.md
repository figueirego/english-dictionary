### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The deteriorated state of iron or steel as a result of moisture and oxidation; it consists mostly of iron(III) oxide (ferric oxide) and iron(II) oxide (ferrous oxide).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The rust on my bicycle chain made cycling to work very dangerous.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A similar substance based on another metal.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** copper rust

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A reddish-brown color.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A disease of plants caused by a reddish-brown fungus (Pucciniales).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Damage caused to stamps and album pages by a fungal infection.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

## Verb · Etymology 2 · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- enPR: rŭst — variety unspecified in the source labels
- IPA: /ɹʌst/ — source labels: Received-Pronunciation; US
- IPA: /ɹʊst/ — source labels: Northern-England
- IPA: /ɾʌst/ — source labels: Scotland; Wales

### Forms

- rusts — present; singular; third-person
- rusting — participle; present
- rusted — participle; past
- rusted — past

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To oxidize, especially of iron or steel.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The patio furniture had rusted in the wind-driven spray.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To cause to oxidize.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The wind-driven spray had thoroughly rusted the patio furniture.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To be affected with the parasitic fungus called rust.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To (cause to) degenerate in idleness; to make or become dull or impaired by inaction.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive; figuratively

   **Example:** My knowledge of Spanish has rusted over the years.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. Of a black cat or its fur, to turn rust-coloured following long periods of exposure to sunlight.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** It's very common for black cats to rust during the summer.

**Source synonyms:** oxidise; oxidize; corrode
