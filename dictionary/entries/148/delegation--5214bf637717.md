### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An act of delegating.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** turnover

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The condition of being delegated.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A group of delegates.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** the American delegation

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A method-dispatching technique describing the lookup and inheritance rules for self-referential calls.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The act whereby or constellation in which the performance of an obligation (owed to an obligee, presuming its validity; irrespective of the obligation as the target of the delegation, rarely called delegatary) is assigned by its debtor (delegator, obligor) to and towards another party (delegatee, delegate)

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law
