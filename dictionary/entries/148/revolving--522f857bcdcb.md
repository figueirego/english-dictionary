## Noun · Record 3

### Forms

- revolvings — plural

### Senses

<a id="r3-s1"></a>

**Source sense R3.S1**

1. The act of something that revolves or turns.

<a id="r3-s2"></a>

**Source sense R3.S2**

2. The process where you either take an non-closing linear shape or a 2D shape, take a 3D axis as the pivot, and basically revolve the linear shape around the axis to get a 3D object. (e.g. taking a 2D triangle and turning it into a cone, taking a circle and turning it into a sphere, taking a long U-shape and turning it into a test tube object)
