### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To pay money, to disburse; especially, to pay a great deal of money.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive; informal

   **Example:** Do you think we should shell out for the extra options package?

   **Source synonyms:** cough up; fork out; fork over
