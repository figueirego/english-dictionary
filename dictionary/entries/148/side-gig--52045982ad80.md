### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A gig (temporary or informal job) being done on the side; a person's non-primary job or other occupation, often distinguished by factors such as formality, legality, personal priority, and/or financial return.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Example:** I work in retail, but I also have a side gig as a content creator.

   **Example:** Sure, I do commercial and sponsored things, but those are just side gigs. Acting on the big screen will always be my passion.

   **Example:** That guy wants to appear as a serious and formal businessman and not talk about his side gig where he deals drugs on the street.

   **Source synonyms:** side hustle; side job
