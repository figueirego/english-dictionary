## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A time too soon to make a decision or come to a conclusion.

   **Usage:** plural; plural-only

   **Example:** It’s early days (yet) to know for certain.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The initial period of an innovation

   **Usage:** plural; plural-only

   **Example:** In the early days of television, the service was always breaking down.
