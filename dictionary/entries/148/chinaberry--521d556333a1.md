### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The bead tree or azedarach, Melia azedarach, a deciduous tree in the mahogany family Meliaceae, native to India, southern China and Australia, or its fruit.

   **Usage:** US

   **Source synonyms:** azedarach; bakain; bead tree; Cape lilac; Persian lilac; Pride of India tree; syringa; white cedar

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The soapberry (genus Sapindus, especially Sapindus saponaria), native to the Americas.

   **Usage:** US

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Actaea rubra, a poisonous herbaceous flowering plant in the family Ranunculaceae, native to North America.

   **Usage:** US

   **Source synonyms:** red baneberry; doll's eye
