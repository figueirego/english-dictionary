### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A set of choices from which the user or customer makes multiple selections, specifically one or more choices from each of two or more distinct categories.

   **Example:** Each camper picked from a Chinese menu of available activities&#58; one from the various field sports; one from the pool activities list; and two from a wide selection of arts and crafts classes.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see Chinese, menu.
