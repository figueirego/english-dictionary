### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A lawyer who represents defendants in legal proceedings.
   1. A lawyer who regularly represents defendants who carry insurance and is chosen by the insurance company.

      **Usage:** US

      **Source synonyms:** defence counsel; defender

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   1. A lawyer who regularly represents criminal defendants.
