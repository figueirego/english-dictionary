### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To join into a mass or whole.

   **Example:** The droplets coalesced into a puddle.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of separate elements

   **Source synonyms:** amalgamate; combine; join; merge; unite; fuse

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To form from different pieces or elements.

   **Example:** The puddle coalesced from the droplets as they ran together.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a whole or a unit

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To bond pieces of metal into a continuous whole by liquefying parts of each piece, bringing the liquids into contact, and allowing the combined liquid to solidify.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To merge, to intermingle freely.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of separate groups or persons

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To convert a null value to a defined value.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; databases; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences
