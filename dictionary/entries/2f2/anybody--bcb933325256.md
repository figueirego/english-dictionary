<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any one out of an indefinite number of persons; anyone; any person.

   **Example:** Anybody will do.

   **Example:** Is there anybody inside?

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person of some consideration or standing.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** Everybody who wants to be anybody will come to Jake's party.

   **Example:** Here one isn't anybody, if one doesn't dance like Travolta.
