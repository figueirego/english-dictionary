### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A fee paid for some liberty or privilege, particularly for the privilege of passing over a bridge or on a highway, or for that of vending goods in a fair, market, etc.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Loss or damage incurred through a disaster.

   **Example:** The war has taken its toll on the people.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A fee paid by the owner of materials or other goods for processing such goods, as under a tolling agreement.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A fee for using any kind of material processing service.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** We can handle on a toll basis your needs for spray drying, repackaging, crushing and grinding, and dry blending.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A tollbooth.

   **Usage:** US

   **Example:** We will be replacing some manned tolls with high-speed device readers.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A liberty to buy and sell within the bounds of a manor.

   **Usage:** UK; obsolete

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A portion of grain taken by a miller as a compensation for grinding.

   **Usage:** England; obsolete; regional
