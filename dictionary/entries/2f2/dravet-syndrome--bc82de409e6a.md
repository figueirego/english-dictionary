### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An autosomal-dominant genetic disorder that causes a catastrophic form of epilepsy, usually beginning in infancy, with prolonged seizures that are often triggered by high temperatures or fever.

   **Usage:** uncountable; medical genetics

   **Source synonyms:** SMEI; severe myoclonic epilepsy of infancy
