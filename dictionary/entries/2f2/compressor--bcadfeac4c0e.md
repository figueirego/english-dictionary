### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A device that produces pressure, such as a gas compressor that produces pressurized gas.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A device that squeezes (compresses).
   1. An instrument for compressing an artery (especially the femoral artery) or other part.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A device that squeezes (compresses).
   1. An apparatus for confining or flattening between glass plates an object to be examined with the microscope; a compressorium.

**Source sense R1.S4**
