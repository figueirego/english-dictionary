### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A species of tiny freshwater fish, Poecilia reticulata, popular in home aquariums, that usually has a plain body and black or dark blue tail for the females and a more colorful tail for the males. The guppies can vary in size, but males are 4 cm (1.6”) and females are 7 cm (2.8”).

   **Example:** Fred kept a tank of guppies by the windowsill.

   **Source synonyms:** millionfish

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any tiny fish.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** Throw that one back; it’s just a guppy!
