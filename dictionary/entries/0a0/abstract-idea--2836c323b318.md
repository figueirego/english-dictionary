### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An idea separated from a complex object, or from other ideas which naturally accompany it; as the solidity of marble when contemplated apart from its color or figure.

   **Usage:** metaphysics

<a id="r1-s2"></a>
