### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Expressing distance or motion.
   1. From (a place); off.

      **Usage:** dialectal; obsolete; except in phrases

      **Example:** Take the chicken out of the freezer.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Expressing distance or motion.
   1. Since, from (a given time, earlier state etc.).

      **Usage:** obsolete except in phrases

      **Example:** He hasn't been well of late.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Expressing distance or motion.
   1. From, away from (a position, number, distance etc.).

      **Example:** There are no shops within twenty miles of the cottage.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Expressing separation.
   1. Indicating removal, absence or separation, with the action indicated by a transitive verb and the quality or substance by a grammatical object.

      **Example:** Finally she was relieved of the burden of caring for her sick husband.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Expressing separation.
   1. Indicating removal, absence or separation, with resulting state indicated by an adjective.

      **Example:** He seemed devoid of human feelings.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Expressing separation.
   1. Indicating removal, absence or separation, construed with an intransitive verb.

      **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Expressing origin.
   1. Indicating an ancestral source or origin of descent.

      **Example:** The word is believed to be of Japanese origin.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Expressing origin.
   1. Introducing an epithet that indicates a birthplace, residence, dominion, or other place associated with the individual.

      **Example:** Jesus of Nazareth (after hometown)

      **Example:** Anselm of Canterbury (after diocese)

      **Example:** Anselm of Aosta (after birthplace)

      **Example:** Anselm of Bec (after monastery)

      **Example:** Pedro II of Brazil (after dominion)

      **Example:** Mrs Miggins of Gasworks Road, Mudchester (after place of residence)

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. Expressing origin.
   1. Indicating a (non-physical) source of action or emotion; introducing a cause, instigation; from, out of, as an expression of.

      **Example:** The invention was born of necessity.

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. Expressing origin.
   1. Indicates the source or cause of the verb.

      **Usage:** following an intransitive verb

      **Example:** It is said that she died of a broken heart.

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. Expressing origin.
   1. Indicates the subject or cause of the adjective.

      **Usage:** following an adjective

      **Example:** I am tired of all this nonsense.

<a id="r1-s12"></a>

**Source sense R1.S12**

12. Expressing agency.
   1. Indicates the agent (for most verbs, now usually expressed with by).

      **Usage:** following a passive verb

      **Example:** I am not particularly enamoured of this idea.

<a id="r1-s13"></a>

**Source sense R1.S13**

13. Expressing agency.
   1. Used to introduce the "subjective genitive"; following a noun to form the head of a postmodifying noun phrase (see also 'Possession' senses below).

      **Example:** The contract can be terminated at any time with the agreement of both parties.

<a id="r1-s14"></a>

**Source sense R1.S14**

14. Expressing agency.
   1. Used to indicate the agent of something described by the adjective.

      **Usage:** following an adjective

      **Example:** It was very brave of you to speak out like that.

<a id="r1-s15"></a>

**Source sense R1.S15**

15. Expressing composition, substance.
   1. Used to indicate the material or substance used.

      **Usage:** making etc.

      **Example:** Many 'corks' are now actually made of plastic.

      **Source context (verbatim):** after a verb expressing construction

<a id="r1-s16"></a>

**Source sense R1.S16**

16. Expressing composition, substance.
   1. Used to indicate the material of the just-mentioned object.

      **Usage:** directly following a noun

      **Example:** She wore a dress of silk.

<a id="r1-s17"></a>

**Source sense R1.S17**

17. Expressing composition, substance.
   1. Indicating the composition of a given collective or quantitative noun.

      **Example:** I'll have a dozen of those apples, please.

      **Example:** What a lot of nonsense!

<a id="r1-s18"></a>

**Source sense R1.S18**

18. Expressing composition, substance.
   1. Used to link a given class of things with a specific example of that class.

      **Example:** Germany's formal name is the Federal Republic of Germany.

      **Example:** Welcome to the historic town of Harwich.

<a id="r1-s19"></a>

**Source sense R1.S19**

19. Expressing composition, substance.
   1. Links two nouns in near-apposition, with the first qualifying the second; "which is also".

      **Example:** I'm not driving this wreck of a car.

<a id="r1-s20"></a>

**Source sense R1.S20**

20. Introducing subject matter.
   1. Links an intransitive verb, or a transitive verb and its subject (especially verbs to do with thinking, feeling, expressing etc.), with its subject-matter; concerning, with regard to.

      **Example:** I'm always thinking of you.

<a id="r1-s21"></a>

**Source sense R1.S21**

21. Introducing subject matter.
   1. Introduces its subject matter; about, concerning.

      **Usage:** following a noun (now chiefly nouns of knowledge, communication etc.)

      **Example:** He told us the story of his journey to India.

<a id="r1-s22"></a>

**Source sense R1.S22**

22. Introducing subject matter.
   1. Introduces its subject matter.

      **Usage:** following an adjective

      **Example:** This behaviour is typical of teenagers.

<a id="r1-s23"></a>

**Source sense R1.S23**

23. Having partitive effect.
   1. Introduces the whole for which is indicated only the specified part or segment; "from among".

      **Usage:** following a number or other quantitive word

      **Example:** Most of these apples are rotten.

<a id="r1-s24"></a>

**Source sense R1.S24**

24. Having partitive effect.
   1. Indicates a given part.

      **Usage:** following a noun

      **Example:** We want a larger slice of the cake.

<a id="r1-s25"></a>

**Source sense R1.S25**

25. Having partitive effect.
   1. Some, an amount of, one of.

      **Usage:** archaic; literary; or as a predicate after to be

      **Example:** On the whole, they seem to be of the decent sort.

      **Source context (verbatim):** with preceding partitive word assumed

<a id="r1-s26"></a>

**Source sense R1.S26**

26. Having partitive effect.
   1. Links to a genitive noun or possessive pronoun, with partitive effect (though now often merged with possessive senses, below; see also double possessive).

      **Example:** He is a friend of mine.

<a id="r1-s27"></a>

**Source sense R1.S27**

27. Expressing possession.
   1. Belonging to, existing in, or taking place in a given location, place or time. Compare "origin" senses, above.

      **Example:** He was perhaps the most famous scientist of the twentieth century.

<a id="r1-s28"></a>

**Source sense R1.S28**

28. Expressing possession.
   1. Belonging to (a place) through having title, ownership or control over it.

      **Example:** The owner of the nightclub was arrested.

<a id="r1-s29"></a>

**Source sense R1.S29**

29. Expressing possession.
   1. Belonging to (someone or something) as something they possess or have as a characteristic; the "possessive genitive". (With abstract nouns, this intersects with the subjective genitive, above under "agency" senses.)

      **Example:** Keep the handle of the saucepan away from the flames.

<a id="r1-s30"></a>

**Source sense R1.S30**

30. Forming the "objective genitive".

<a id="r1-s31"></a>

**Source sense R1.S31**

31. Forming the "objective genitive".
   1. Follows an agent noun, verbal noun or noun of action.

      **Example:** She had a profound distrust of the police.

<a id="r1-s32"></a>

**Source sense R1.S32**

32. Expressing qualities or characteristics.
   1. Links an adjective with a noun or noun phrase to form a quasi-adverbial qualifier; in respect to, as regards.

      **Usage:** archaic; literary

      **Example:** My companion seemed affable and easy of manner.

<a id="r1-s33"></a>

**Source sense R1.S33**

33. Expressing qualities or characteristics.
   1. Indicates a quality or characteristic; "characterized by".

      **Example:** Pooh was said to be a bear of very little brain.

<a id="r1-s34"></a>

**Source sense R1.S34**

34. Expressing qualities or characteristics.
   1. Indicates quantity, age, price, etc.

      **Example:** A young man of 20 has been paying interest at an exorbitant rate of 10%.

<a id="r1-s35"></a>

**Source sense R1.S35**

35. Expressing qualities or characteristics.
   1. Used to link singular indefinite nouns (preceded by the indefinite article) and attributive adjectives modified by certain common adverbs of degree.

      **Usage:** US; informal; considered incorrect by some

      **Example:** It's not that big of a deal. (Standard form&#58; It's not that big a deal.)

<a id="r1-s36"></a>

**Source sense R1.S36**

36. Expressing a point in time.
   1. During the course of (a set period of time, day of the week etc.), now specifically with implied repetition or regularity.

      **Usage:** regional

      **Example:** Of an evening, we would often go for a stroll along the river.

<a id="r1-s37"></a>

**Source sense R1.S37**

37. Expressing a point in time.
   1. For (a given length of time).

      **Usage:** UK; dialectal

      **Example:** I’ve not taken her out of a goodly long while.

      **Source context (verbatim):** in the negative

<a id="r1-s38"></a>

**Source sense R1.S38**

38. Expressing a point in time.
   1. Indicates duration of a state, activity etc.

      **Example:** After a delay of three hours, the plane finally took off.

      **Source context (verbatim):** after a noun

<a id="r1-s39"></a>

**Source sense R1.S39**

39. Expressing a point in time.
   1. Before (the hour); to (the hour).

      **Usage:** US

      **Example:** What's the time? / Nearly a quarter of three.

      **Example:** I'll be ready by five of noon.

<a id="r1-s40"></a>

**Source sense R1.S40**

40. Expressing a point in time.
   1. Before (the hour); to (the hour).
      1. Often used without the hour.

         **Usage:** US; informal
