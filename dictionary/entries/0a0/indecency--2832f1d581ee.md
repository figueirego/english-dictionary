### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Lack of decency; the property or degree of being indecent.

   **Example:** One sure sign of indecency is criticizing foreigners constantly, but remaining silent about the problems within your own land, or by your own people, or both.
