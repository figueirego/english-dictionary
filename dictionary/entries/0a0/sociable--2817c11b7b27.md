## Adjective · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈsoʊʃəbəl/ — source labels: General-American

### Forms

- more sociable — comparative
- most sociable — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Tending to socialize or be social.

   **Example:** He's normally pretty quiet, but he gets much more sociable around women.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a person

   **Source synonyms:** friendly; inviting; congenial; convivial; outgoing

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Offering opportunities for conversation; characterized by much conversation.

   **Example:** a sociable party

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Capable of being, or fit to be, united in one body or company; associable.

   **Usage:** archaic

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. No longer hostile; friendly.

   **Usage:** obsolete

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** clubbable; companionable; extroverted; gregarious; outgoing; sociable; social

**Source antonyms:** unsociable — antonym(s) of “tending to socialize”; introverted; shy; seclusive; solitary; unclubbable; unsocial
