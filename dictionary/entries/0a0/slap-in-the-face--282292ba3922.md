## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Something unexpectedly said or done which causes shock or offense; an insult, rebuke, or rebuff.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** That Joan's ex-boyfriend turned up to the school dance with Mary was a slap in the face for Joan, and the girls are no longer friends.

   **Source synonyms:** kick in the teeth; smack in the face
