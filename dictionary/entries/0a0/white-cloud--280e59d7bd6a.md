## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An unincorporated community in Harrison County, Indiana, United States. Named for frequent fog in the area.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A small city in Doniphan County, Kansas, United States. Named after James White Cloud of the Iowa people.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A city, the county seat of Newaygo County, Michigan, United States.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An unincorporated community in Hickory County, Missouri, United States. Probably named after Chief Mahaska (White Cloud).

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An unincorporated community in Nodaway County, Missouri, also spelt Whitecloud.
