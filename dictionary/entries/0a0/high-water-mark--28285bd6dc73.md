## Noun

### Forms

- high-water marks — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A mark, such as a line of seaweed, showing the highest level reached by a body of water.

   **Usage:** literal

   **Source antonyms:** low-water mark

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The peak, apex or acme of something; the maximum level; the furthest or highest point.

   **Usage:** broadly; figuratively

   **Example:** The Gettysburg campaign was the high-water mark of the Confederacy's invasions of the North.

   **Source antonyms:** low-water mark
