### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A ring- or donut-shaped area, object, or structure.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A ring of fibrous tissue; specifically (cardiology), such a ring around an opening of a heart valve, to which the valve leaflets and muscle fibres of the atria and ventricles are attached; an annulus fibrosus cordis.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A ring of light in a celestial body, especially when caused by an annular eclipse (for example, when the Sun and Moon are in line with the Earth, but the Moon does not completely cover the Sun's disc).

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astronomy; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A structure surrounding a sporangium (or part of it) which shrinks and causes it to rupture for spore dispersal; specifically, in a fern&#58; a structure around about two-thirds of the sporangium consisting of differentially thick-walled cells which dry and distort the sporangium; and in a moss&#58; a complete ring of cells around the tip of the sporangium which dissolves to cause the tip to detach.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The membranous remnants of a partial veil which leaves a ring on the stem of a mushroom.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; mycology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A dark ring on a fish's scale that is formed when a fish's growth rate slows down in the winter due to low food intake and the scale's circuli move closer to one another. The dark ring is used to estimate the fish's age, approximately one year per annulus.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; ichthyology; natural-sciences; zoology

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The region in a plane between two concentric circles of different radii.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Any topological space homeomorphic to the region in a plane between two concentric circles of different radii.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences; topology

   **Source synonyms:** cylinder

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. In a well such as an oil well or water well&#58; the space between a pipe or tube and any pipe, tube, casing, or sides of a hole surrounding it.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; technology
