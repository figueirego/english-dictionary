<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Away, having left.

   **Example:** Are they gone already?

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. No longer existing, having passed.

   **Example:** The days of my youth are gone.

   **Example:** All hopes for a truce are gone.

   **Example:** All the little shops that used to be here are now gone.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. Used up.

   **Example:** I'm afraid all the coffee is gone.

   **Example:** If the milk is all gone, we'll drink our coffee black.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. Broken, failed.

   **Example:** The bulb is gone. Can you put a new one in?

   **Example:** The car isn't driveable — the steering is gone.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. Dead.

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. Doomed, done for.

   **Example:** Have you seen the company's revenue? It's through the floor. They're gone.

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

**Source sense R2.S7**

7. Not fully aware of one's surroundings, often through intoxication or mental decline.

   **Usage:** colloquial

   **Example:** Don't bother trying to understand what Grandma says; she's gone.

<a id="r2-s8"></a>

**Source sense R2.S8**

8. Infatuated; in love (+ on, for, in).

   **Usage:** slang

<a id="r2-s9"></a>

**Source sense R2.S9**

9. Excellent, wonderful; crazy.

   **Usage:** US; dated; informal

   **Example:** It was a group of real gone cats.

<a id="r2-s10"></a>

**Source sense R2.S10**

10. Ago (used post-positionally).

   **Usage:** archaic

<a id="r2-s11"></a>

**Source sense R2.S11**

11. Weak; faint; feeling a sense of goneness.

   **Usage:** US

<a id="r2-s12"></a>

**Source sense R2.S12**

12. Of an arrow&#58; wide of the mark.

<a id="r2-s13"></a>

**Source sense R2.S13**
