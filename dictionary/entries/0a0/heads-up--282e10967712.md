### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A warning or call to pay attention; an advisory notice; a notice of what is to happen; a holler.

   **Usage:** US; idiomatic

   **Example:** Send everyone a heads-up about the inspection tomorrow.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Involving two players. (usually said when there are only two players in the table; or, when all players except two folded)

   **Example:** There were only two of us, so we started playing heads-up.

   **Example:** There are seven players on the table. Five folded, the remaining two played heads-up until the showdown.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** card-games; poker
