### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A comb with fine, closely spaced teeth, especially one used for removing head lice and their nits (eggs) from the hair; a nit comb.

   **Source synonyms:** toothcomb

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A means of making a thorough search.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** The police went through his possessions with a fine-tooth comb.

   **Source synonyms:** toothcomb
