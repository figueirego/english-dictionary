### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A marriage that is invalid because of an impediment or a lack of consent, but was entered into in good faith by at least one of the parties and follows the form established by canon law.

   **Source synonyms:** pseudomarriage
