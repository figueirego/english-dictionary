### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A physical feeling or perception from something that comes into contact with the body; something sensed.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Excitation of sensory organs.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; medicine; physiology; psychology; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A widespread reaction of interest or excitement.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A remarkable person.

   **Usage:** countable; dated; figuratively; uncommon; uncountable

   **Example:** You truly are a sensation.

   **Source synonyms:** event

**Source sense R1.S5**

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; slang; uncountable
