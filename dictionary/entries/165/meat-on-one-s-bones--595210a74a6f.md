## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An amount of flesh on one's body that prevents one from being very thin.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An amount of mass, substance, or strength on or within something's body.

   **Usage:** broadly; uncountable

   **Example:** You're dressing up as a canary? Can't you find something with a little more meat on its bones, like an eagle?

   **Example:** I'd rather buy a truck than a car; I need something with more meat on its bones.
