## Noun

### Forms

- bottlefeedings — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act or process of feeding a (human) baby with a bottle, as&#58;
   1. Implying (when not otherwise specified) feeding with commercial baby formula as contrasted with human breast milk.

      **Usage:** countable; especially; uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The act or process of feeding a (human) baby with a bottle, as&#58;
   1. Feeding with human breast milk expressed (pumped) earlier, as contrasted with feeding directly at the breast.

      **Usage:** also; countable; sometimes; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An instance (session) of such feeding activity.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Such feeding (or instance thereof) for any nonhuman animal, especially lambs (orphaned or rejected) or juvenile wildlife rescues.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
