### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In multiple directions or locations, often haphazardly; everywhere.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** They started firing all over the place.

   **Example:** In Genoa there are stray cats all over the place.

   **Example:** I've been looking for you all over the place.

   **Source synonyms:** scattershot

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Inconsistent; lacking a clear pattern; with a large amount of variation.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I asked each person in turn, and their answers were all over the place.

   **Source synonyms:** at random
