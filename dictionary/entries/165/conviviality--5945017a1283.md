### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The state of being convivial; a feeling of comfort and happiness, as from celebrating casually with friends.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** He's easy to talk to because of his conviviality.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A jovial activity or spirit, marked by easy and lively socializing; a hospitable and festive atmosphere.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** We're old friends, so once we started talking, the evening gained an air of conviviality.
