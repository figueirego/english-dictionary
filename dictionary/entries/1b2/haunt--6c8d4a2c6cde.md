### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To inhabit or to visit frequently (most often used in reference to ghosts).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** A couple of ghosts haunt the old, burnt-down house.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To make uneasy, restless.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The memory of his past failures haunted him.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To stalk; to follow.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The policeman haunted him, following him everywhere.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To live habitually; to stay, to remain.

   **Usage:** archaic; intransitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To accustom; habituate; make accustomed to.

   **Usage:** Northern-England; Scotland; UK; dialectal; transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To practise; to devote oneself to.

   **Usage:** Northern-England; Scotland; UK; dialectal; transitive

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To persist in staying or visiting.

   **Usage:** intransitive

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** nag — to make uneasy; live — to live habitually; dwell
