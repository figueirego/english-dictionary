### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of many species of sedge or grass that have leaves with sharp, serrated edges, including&#58;
   1. Carex geminata (in Carex subg. Carex);

      **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of many species of sedge or grass that have leaves with sharp, serrated edges, including&#58;

      **Usage:** New-Zealand; uncountable; usually
