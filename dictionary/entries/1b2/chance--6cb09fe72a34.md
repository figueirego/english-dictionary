### Senses

<a id="r4-s1"></a>

**Source sense R4.S1**

1. To happen by chance, to occur.

   **Usage:** archaic; intransitive

   **Example:** It chanced that I found a solution the very next day.

<a id="r4-s2"></a>

**Source sense R4.S2**

2. To befall; to happen to.

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive

<a id="r4-s3"></a>

**Source sense R4.S3**

3. To try or risk.

   **Example:** Shall we carry the umbrella, or chance a rainstorm?

<a id="r4-s4"></a>

**Source sense R4.S4**

4. To discover something by chance.

   **Example:** He chanced upon a kindly stranger who showed him the way.

<a id="r4-s5"></a>

**Source sense R4.S5**

5. To rob, cheat or swindle someone.

   **Usage:** Belize

   **Example:** The car broke down a week after I bought it. I was chanced by that fast-talking salesman.

<a id="r4-s6"></a>

**Source sense R4.S6**

6. To take an opportunity from someone; to cut a queue.

   **Usage:** Nigeria
