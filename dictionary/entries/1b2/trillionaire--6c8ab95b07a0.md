## Noun

### Pronunciation

### Forms

- trillionaires — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person whose wealth is at least one trillion (10¹²) currency units.
   1. One who is therefore very wealthy, because those currency units are not hyperinflated.

      **Usage:** especially

      **Example:** The CEO of our company is a multibillionaire and now hopes to become a trillionaire.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person whose wealth is at least one trillion (10¹²) currency units.
   1. One who is not wealthy (nor rare), because those currency units are hyperinflated.

      **Usage:** sometimes

      **Example:** Countless Zimbabweans had the dubious distinction of being trillionaires in 2008.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Synonym of gazillionaire (“an incredibly rich person”).

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** Who cares what that guy thinks? He may be a trillionaire, but he can’t buy good taste.

   **Source synonyms:** gazillionaire (synonym; synonym-of)
