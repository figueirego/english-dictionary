### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A heavy covered wagon with a curved boatlike body popular in the United States from about 1750–1850.

   **Example:** The heavy and boat-shaped Conestoga wagon should not be confused with the lighter and cheaper prairie schooners that largely replaced them.
