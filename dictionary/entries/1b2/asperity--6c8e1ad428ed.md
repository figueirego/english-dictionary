### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The quality of having a rough or uneven surface; roughness, unevenness; (countable, chiefly in the plural) a protruding or rough area or point on a surface; a protrusion.

   **Usage:** formal; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** bumpiness; ruggedness; bump; protuberance

   **Source antonyms:** smoothness

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The quality of having a rough or uneven surface; roughness, unevenness; (countable, chiefly in the plural) a protruding or rough area or point on a surface; a protrusion.
   1. A section of a fault line with high friction, such that there is no movement along this part of the fault except during an earthquake.

      **Usage:** countable; formal

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; geology; natural-sciences

      **Source synonyms:** bumpiness; ruggedness; bump; protuberance

      **Source antonyms:** smoothness

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The quality of being difficult or unpleasant to experience; (countable) a thing that is harsh and difficult to endure; a difficulty, a hardship.

   **Usage:** figuratively; formal; uncountable

   **Example:** the asperity of Maine’s winter

   **Source synonyms:** harshness; rigour; severity

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The quality of being harsh or severe in the way one behaves or speaks toward another person; (countable, chiefly in the plural) a deeply hostile or resentful feeling; an animosity, a hatred.

   **Usage:** figuratively; formal; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** acerbity; harshness; severity; sharpness

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Of sound&#58; gratingness, harshness.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; figuratively; formal; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Of taste&#58; harshness or sharpness; acridity, tartness.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; figuratively; formal; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** acrimony

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; figuratively; formal; uncountable
