## Noun

### Forms

- double aunts — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A woman who is one's aunt in two ways, e.g. being a sister of one parent and the wife of a sibling of the other.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A woman who is the aunt of exactly two people, especially when those two people are twins.

   **Usage:** rare
