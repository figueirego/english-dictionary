### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The first existence of anything; act or fact of commencing; the beginning.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The time of Henry VII nearly coincides with the commencement of what is termed modern history.

   **Source synonyms:** rise; origin; beginning; start; dawn
