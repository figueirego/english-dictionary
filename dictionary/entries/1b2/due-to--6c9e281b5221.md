## Preposition

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Caused by; resulting from; because of.

   **Example:** Rising unemployment due to the economic downturn is spreading.

   **Example:** The carrier will be held financially responsible for any damage due to reckless handling.

   **Example:** Due to the rising cost of ingredients, the company decided to change the recipe. \[disjunctive use, sometimes proscribed\]

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see due, to.

   **Example:** The steamship was due to arrive in the Port of New York tomorrow.

   **Example:** Our deep gratitude is due to all those who have helped.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. (of a proof) from, written by

   **Example:** The proof we provide is a nice indirect proof due to Euclid.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences
