### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A small portion remaining of a larger thing or group; part of a former whole.

   **Example:** The remnants of the Neanderthal race mixed with members of Homo sapiens.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The remaining fabric at the end of the bolt.

   **Example:** Usually not enough to make an entire project by itself, remnants of several fabrics can be used to make quilts.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An unsold end of piece goods, as cloth, ribbons, carpets, etc.

   **Example:** All the remnants were sold in the flea market.

   **Example:** A large number of remnants were taking up too much space, so we disposed of them.
