**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of a tense, not pertaining to the past; in particular, applicable to both the present and the future.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** In the phrases ‘I am running tomorrow’ and ‘I am running now,’ the main verb is in the non-past tense.
