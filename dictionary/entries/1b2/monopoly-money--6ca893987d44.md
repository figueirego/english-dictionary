## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Play money.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** toy currency; fun money

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Banknotes of foreign currency; especially those which are vibrant or flimsy.

   **Usage:** derogatory; often; slang; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** play money; funny money

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Money, especially paper money, that is perceived to have little or no value, or to be distributed freely.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Money that does not exist; referring to fraudulent record keeping.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; uncountable
