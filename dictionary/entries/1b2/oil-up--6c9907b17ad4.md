### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To lubricate with oil; to cover or smear with oil.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** They really oiled up that bike chain, didn't they?

   **Example:** They oiled it up with about five times more oil than was necessary.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To get (someone) drunk.

   **Example:** They really oiled him up last night, planning to get him to talk, but he'd been well warned about industrial espionage, and he kept his cool.
