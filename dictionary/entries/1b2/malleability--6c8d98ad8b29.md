## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The quality or state of being malleable.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The property by virtue of which a material can be extended in all directions without rupture by the application of load; a material's ability to be bent, formed, or shaped without cracking or breaking.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A property of a cryptographic algorithms in which an adversary can alter a ciphertext such that it decrypts to a related plaintext.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; cryptography; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences
