## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A mass burning of books, intended as a form of political and/or religious censorship.

   **Usage:** countable; literally; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any mass destruction, removal, or recalling of books, motion pictures, broadcasts, or electronic works as a form of censorship, especially motivated by religious or political objections to the material.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; idiomatic; uncountable
