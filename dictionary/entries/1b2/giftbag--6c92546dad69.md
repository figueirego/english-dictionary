### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A bag of gifts.

   **Example:** Every guest at the party was handed a giftbag on their way out.

   **Example:** Every speaker at the conference received a giftbag, which was placed in their hotel room in preparation for their arrival.

   **Source synonyms:** party bag; goody bag; swag bag

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A bag designed for use in wrapping gifts.

   **Example:** I put her birthday present in a giftbag, and I also threw some candy in there, just for some added fun.
