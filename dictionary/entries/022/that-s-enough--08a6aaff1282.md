## Interjection

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An admonition.

   **Example:** Amber&#58; Mom, Ashley punched me! Ashley&#58; No I did not! She's lying! Mom&#58; That's enough! I'll turn this car around and drive home if you two don't settle down right now!

   **Source synonyms:** enough already; enough; no more; please; cut it out; knock it off; shut up; STFU

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see that's, enough.

   **Example:** A&#58; Say when. B&#58; That's enough, thank you.
