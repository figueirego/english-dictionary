### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any organism that dislikes, or cannot thrive in, sunlight.

   **Source antonyms:** heliophile; heliotrope

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any organism that dislikes, or cannot thrive in, sunlight.
   1. A person who strenuously avoids direct sunlight (and, in some cases, all sunlight).
