### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A young dog, wolf, fox, seal, bat or shark, or the young of certain other animals.

   **Example:** The dog has had that bed since he was just a pup.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A young, inexperienced person.

   **Example:** The new teacher is a mere pup.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any cute dog, regardless of age.

   **Example:** My pup likes to run as fast as he can, yet cannot always stop in time!
