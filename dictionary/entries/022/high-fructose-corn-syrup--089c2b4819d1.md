### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A type of corn syrup that has undergone an enzymatic processing to convert some of its glucose into fructose in order to produce a desired sweetness.

   **Usage:** US; uncountable; usually

   **Source synonyms:** HFCS; isoglucose; glucose-fructose syrup; glucose/fructose; maize syrup; high-fructose maize syrup; corn sugar
