### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The pulp or pulplike matter remaining from a substance pressed to extract its juice or oil.
   1. Such residue from apples used in the process of cidermaking; also, a mass of crushed apples before the juice is pressed out.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source synonyms:** marc; stosh; pug

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The pulp or pulplike matter remaining from a substance pressed to extract its juice or oil.
   1. Such residue from grapes used in winemaking.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source synonyms:** marc; stosh

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The pulp or pulplike matter remaining from a substance pressed to extract its juice or oil.
   1. Such residue (especially castor bean cake or fish scrap) used as fertilizer.

      **Usage:** countable; historical; uncountable

      **Source synonyms:** marc; stosh

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Synonym of pulp (“a soft, moist mass formed by mashing something”).

   **Usage:** UK; archaic; countable; regional; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** pulp (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Sheep offal.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; obsolete; uncountable
