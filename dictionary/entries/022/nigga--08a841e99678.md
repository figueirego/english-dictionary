### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Pronunciation spelling of nigger.

   **Usage:** alt-of; dated; ethnic; offensive; pronunciation-spelling; slur; vulgar

   **Alternative form of:** [nigger](../048/nigger--120f6e5b4ea3.md)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A close and loyal friend.

   **Usage:** offensive; often; slang; vulgar; but also often used affectionately in African-American Vernacular

   **Example:** I'd never betray you, 'cause you my nigga, man.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A black person (usually a male friend), especially an African American.

   **Usage:** offensive; often; slang; vulgar; but tolerated in African-American Vernacular

   **Example:** As soon as I walked into the class and saw that sea of white faces, I felt alone, so I sat next to the only other nigga there.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any person, though chiefly male.

   **Usage:** offensive; often; slang; vulgar; chiefly African-American Vernacular

   **Example:** White niggas do be like, this is too spicy.

   **Example:** All these freshman niggas are so annoying.

   **Example:** These niggas out here bein' fake.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A male, especially an adult one, as opposed to a female.

   **Usage:** offensive; often; slang; vulgar; but tolerated in African-American Vernacular
