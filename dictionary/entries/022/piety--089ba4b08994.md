### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Reverence and devotion to God.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Colleen's piety led her to make sacrifices that most people would not have made.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** lifestyle; religion

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Similar reverence to one's parents and family or to one's country.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** patriotism as piety, when done right

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A devout or otherwise laudable act, thought, or statement.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A devout or otherwise laudable act, thought, or statement.
   1. A platitude that may be empty or at least facile and undercommitted.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Example:** He was quick with the pieties about hard work, honest communication, active listening, and respecting others' viewpoints, but walking the walk is different from talking the talk.
