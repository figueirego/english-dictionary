## Noun

### Forms

- big boys — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see big, boy.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A large object or person.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I've been dying to get behind the wheel of this big boy.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A male child who is considered mature enough for some purpose.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; informal

   **Example:** You're a big boy now, and big boys go to school.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An adult male, considered to be sufficiently mature and independent.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; informal

   **Example:** He is a big boy and can take care of himself.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. One who is in a dominant position, such as in business, elite sport, etc.

   **Usage:** plural-normally

   **Example:** He started a small manufacturing plant, but he couldn't compete with the big boys.
