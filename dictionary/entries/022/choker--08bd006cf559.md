### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A piece of jewelry or ornamental fabric, worn as a necklace or neckerchief, tight to the throat.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** fashion; lifestyle

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One who, or that which, chokes or strangles.

   **Source synonyms:** strangler

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. One who, or that which, chokes or strangles.
   1. A traffic calming device used to narrow a road and produce a chokepoint.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** road; transport

      **Source synonyms:** strangler

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. One who operates the choke of an engine during ignition.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Any disappointing or upsetting circumstance.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Example:** I lost £100 on the horses today — what a choker!

   **Source synonyms:** bummer; downer; pisser

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. One who performs badly at an important part of a competition because they are nervous, especially when winning.

   **Example:** The choker tag will always follow the Proteas until we win a trophy — Temba Bavuma

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A loop of cable fastened around a log to haul it.
