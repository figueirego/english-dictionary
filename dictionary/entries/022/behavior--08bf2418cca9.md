### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Human conduct relative to social norms.

   **Usage:** US; uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An inappropriate or forbidden action.

   **Usage:** US; countable; usually

   **Example:** When a patient has a behavior, document it in their records.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The way or manner a living creature behaves or acts generally.

   **Usage:** US; countable; uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A state of probation about one's conduct.

   **Usage:** US; informal; uncountable; usually

   **Example:** He was on his best behavior when her family visited.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An observable response produced by an organism.

   **Usage:** US; countable; uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; human-sciences; natural-sciences; psychology; sciences

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The way a device or system operates.

   **Usage:** US; uncountable; usually
