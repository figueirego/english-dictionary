### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. comparative form of great&#58; more great; of two (or, rarely or non-standardly, more than two) things, larger in size (bigger), in value, in importance etc.

   **Usage:** comparative; form-of

   **Example:** While the north of the country has been dry, there is a greater need for rain in the south.

   **Source antonyms:** lesser

   **Form of:** [great](../21d/great--87765da6ccf0.md)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. comparative form of great&#58; more great; of two (or, rarely or non-standardly, more than two) things, larger in size (bigger), in value, in importance etc.
   1. In plant and animal names, denotes a larger kind.

      **Example:** greater yellowlegs

      **Example:** greater celandine

      **Source antonyms:** lesser

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. comparative form of great&#58; more great; of two (or, rarely or non-standardly, more than two) things, larger in size (bigger), in value, in importance etc.
   1. Denotes the larger geographic or administrative area that encompasses or belongs to a place (especially if both share the same name).

      **Example:** Greater Antilles

      **Source antonyms:** lesser

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. comparative form of great&#58; more great; of two (or, rarely or non-standardly, more than two) things, larger in size (bigger), in value, in importance etc.
   1. Denotes inclusion of the surrounding urban areas and conurbation (of a city or other large settlement).

      **Usage:** capitalized; usually

      **Example:** Greater London

      **Example:** Greater New York includes adjacent parts of three states, as well as the City itself.

      **Source antonyms:** lesser

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. comparative form of great&#58; more great; of two (or, rarely or non-standardly, more than two) things, larger in size (bigger), in value, in importance etc.
   1. Denotes a state at its fullest historical (or desired future) extent including colonies, annexed land, etc.

      **Example:** Greater Armenia

      **Source antonyms:** lesser
