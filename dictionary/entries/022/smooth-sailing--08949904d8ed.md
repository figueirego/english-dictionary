### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used to describe an activity that is not encountering any problems.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; uncountable

   **Example:** It's all been smooth sailing; everything has gone exactly according to plan.

   **Example:** We had some problems at the very beginning, but it's been smooth sailing since the second week or so.
