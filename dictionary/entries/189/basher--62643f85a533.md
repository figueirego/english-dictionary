### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who bashes something, figuratively or literally.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One who bashes something, figuratively or literally.
   1. Before the days of pneumatic rivet guns, the member of a riveting team who hammered the rivet to upset the bucktail to form the field head (see rivet).

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** arts; business; construction; crafts; engineering; hobbies; lifestyle; manufacturing; metallurgy; metalworking; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. One who engages in gratuitous physical or verbal attacks on a group or type of people.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** a Paki-basher

   **Example:** He was beaten up by a queer-basher.

   **Usage:** UK; slang
