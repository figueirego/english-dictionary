### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To explain (something) to (someone), step by step.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** On my first day at work, the secretary walked me through the process of using the photocopier.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To rehearse (something) casually.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>
