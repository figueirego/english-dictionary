## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The northern part of the South (Southern United States), typically consisting of North Carolina, Tennessee, Arkansas, and eastern Oklahoma, sometimes also inclusive of southern Missouri, Virginia, West Virginia, Kentucky, eastern/southern/western Maryland, and southern Delaware.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In the American Civil War, those Confederate states which did not secede until after Fort Sumter&#58; Arkansas, North Carolina, Tennessee, and Virginia.
