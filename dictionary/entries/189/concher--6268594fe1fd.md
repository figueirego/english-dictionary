### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A machine (rather like a rotating pestle and mortar) used to refine the flavour and texture of chocolate by warming and grinding.

   **Source synonyms:** conch; conche

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person who operates such a machine.
