### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To wash the inside of something.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** We must wash out the bins; they are full of maggots.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To remove something by washing.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To be removed by washing.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To wear away by the flow of water; to erode.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** The sandcastle was washed out by the tide.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To cancel due to bad weather.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Source synonyms:** rain off

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To remove or obscure (something) by saturation (of light, noise, etc.)

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** The sun washes out the stars of the night sky.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To lose traction while going around a turn, especially in cycling, motorsports and skiing/snowboarding.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To fail utterly at something one has attempted.

   **Usage:** idiomatic
