### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To become quiet; to cease making sounds.

   **Usage:** informal; intransitive

   **Example:** Hush up now; you have talked too much.

   **Source synonyms:** hush; hush down; quiet down; quieten down; shut up

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To silence.

   **Usage:** informal; transitive

   **Example:** Some people say he knew too much and they killed him to hush him up.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To keep secret, to prevent from becoming known.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** They hushed up the suicide to prevent bad publicity.
