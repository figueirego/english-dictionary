### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A kind of funnel used in brewing fitting into the bung-hole of a tun or cask.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A funnel used in smelting, foundry work etc.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
