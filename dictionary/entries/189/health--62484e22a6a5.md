### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The state of being free from physical or psychological disease, illness, or malfunction; wellness.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** Her mental health is really affected by stressful environments.

   **Example:** My grampa still enjoys very good health.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A state of well-being or balance, often physical but sometimes also mental and social; the overall level of function of an organism from the cellular (micro) level to the social (macro) level.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** The directors are concerned about the financial health of the project.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Physical condition.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Cure, remedy.

   **Usage:** obsolete; uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A toast to prosperity.

   **Usage:** countable; usually

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The amount of damage an in-game entity can withstand before it is killed, destroyed or defeated.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** The enemies on this level have a lot of health.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** video-games
