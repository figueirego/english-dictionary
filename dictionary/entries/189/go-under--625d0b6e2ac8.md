## Verb

### Forms

- goes under — present; singular; third-person
- going under — participle; present
- went under — past

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To descend into a body of water; to founder.

   **Example:** The boat was too heavy and went under.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To collapse or fail, e.g. by going bankrupt.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** In the crisis, the company was forced to reduce its costs in order to avoid going under.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To die.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** If we don't get him to a doctor, he'll go under for sure.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To be named; to call oneself.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** He goes under the name of Mr X to remain anonymous.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To enter a trance, state of hypnosis, etc.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To be imprisoned.

   **Usage:** Australia; slang
