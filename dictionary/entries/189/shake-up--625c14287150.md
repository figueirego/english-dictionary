### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To agitate by shaking.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** If you shake up a can of cola and then open it, you get an explosion.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To restore to its original shape by shaking.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To upset or distress (a person).

   **Usage:** idiomatic; informal; transitive

   **Example:** Seeing the car accident really shook her up.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To reorganize, to make reforms in.

   **Usage:** informal; transitive

   **Example:** The new boss is going to shake things up, so be aware.
