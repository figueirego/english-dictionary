## Interjection

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used to indicate agreement.

   **Usage:** UK; West-Country

   **Example:** Oo arr, that's a good idea.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used to introduce a refutation, sometimes sarcastically.

   **Usage:** UK; West-Country

   **Example:** Oo arr, you and whose army?

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used to imply innuendo.

   **Usage:** UK; West-Country

   **Example:** You like peaches? Oo arr.
