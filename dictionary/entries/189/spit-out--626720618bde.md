### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see spit, out.

   **Example:** The cocktail tasted so disgusting that I had to spit it out immediately.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To say reluctantly (see also spit it out).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Well, you've obviously got something to say about their relationship, so just spit it out already!

   **Source synonyms:** come out with

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To rap; to repeat verses passionately or intensely.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Damn, look at him spitting out these bars!

   **Source synonyms:** spit

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To say scornfully.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** "You will die now!", he spat out.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To eject.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** My computer won't read this DVD. It just keeps spitting it out.
