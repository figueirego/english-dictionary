### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To move (something) from one place to another.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** We had to relocate the magazine rack because we bruised our shins on it too frequently when it was near the door.

   **Source synonyms:** remove

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To change one's domicile or place of business.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Alfred relocated to Colorado Springs to take advantage of the boom in the defense industry.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To lose something and find it again.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** I relocated the bird I spotted last week.
