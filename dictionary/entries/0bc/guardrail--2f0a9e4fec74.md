**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Source synonyms:** guard railing; guide board; guide rail; side rail

2. A rail set alongside a dangerous place as a barrier, to improve safety.
   1. A strengthened barrier installed on the sides of a (usually major) road to keep vehicles on the highway and reduce the risk and/or impact of crashes.
