### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To request or petition.
   1. To request (information, or an answer to a question).

      **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

      **Example:** I asked her age.

      **Example:** I didn't know the answer so I asked.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To request or petition.
   1. To request or enquire of (a person).

      **Usage:** ditransitive; transitive

      **Example:** I asked her (for) her age.

      **Example:** I’m going to ask this lady (for) directions.

      **Example:** If you want to know, ask her.

      **Example:** Ask him that he tell her —No need to, she'll already know by know.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To request or petition.
   1. To request (an item or service) (see also ask for).

      **Usage:** transitive; usually

      **Example:** to ask for a second helping at dinner

      **Example:** to ask for help with homework

      **Example:** to ask a favour

      **Example:** If you want help, you only have to ask.

      **Source context (verbatim):** with 'for' or intransitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To request or petition.
   1. To request (someone to do something).

      **Usage:** transitive

      **Example:** Emma asked Jim to close his eyes.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To request or petition.
   1. To request permission (to do something).

      **Example:** She asked to see the doctor.

      **Example:** Did you ask to use the car?

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To put forward (a question) to be answered.

   **Example:** to ask a question

   **Example:** to ask a riddle

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To require, demand, claim, or expect, whether by way of remuneration or return, or as a matter of necessity.

   **Example:** What price are you asking for the house?

   **Example:** It’s asking a lot of this old car to make it all the way up to Scotland.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To invite.

   **Example:** Don’t ask them to the wedding.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To indirectly encourage or make more likely (an undesirable outcome).

   **Usage:** figuratively; chiefly continuous aspect

   **Example:** Walking around with such an expensive watch is asking to be mugged.

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. To publish in church for marriage; said of both the banns and the persons.

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. To take (a person's situation) as an example.

   **Usage:** figuratively
