<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Running across a set direction of an object.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The motion about the longitudinal axis of an airplane is called roll.

   **Source synonyms:** lengthwise

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Running across a set direction of an object.
   1. Running in the direction of the long axis of a body.

      **Usage:** not-comparable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; medicine; sciences

      **Source synonyms:** lengthwise; craniocaudal; cephalocaudal; rostrocaudal

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Relating to the geographical longitude.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The longitudinal position of a ship refers to its angular distance east or west from the prime meridian.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Of a study, sampling data over time rather than merely once.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** longitudinal studies
