**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used to form female equivalents.

   **Usage:** morpheme

   **Example:** actor + -ess → actress

   **Example:** chanter + -ess → chantress

   **Example:** duke + -ess → duchess (“female ruler of a duchy”)

   **Example:** god + -ess → goddess

   **Example:** lion + -ess → lioness

   **Example:** prince + -ess → princess (“daughter of monarch (holding this title in her own right, who would become a queen regnant); the female equivalent of a prince”)

   **Source synonyms:** -a; -ette; -ine; -ress; she-; -trix

   **Source antonyms:** he-

   **Usage:** morpheme
