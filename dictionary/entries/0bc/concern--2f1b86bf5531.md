## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /kənˈsɝn/ — source labels: General-American
- IPA: /kənˈsɜːn/ — source labels: Received-Pronunciation

### Forms

- concerns — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. That which affects one’s welfare or happiness. A matter of interest to someone.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Mark’s health was of great concern to Connie.

   **Source synonyms:** interest

   **Source antonyms:** unconcern

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The placement of interest or worry on a subject.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Most people in Australia have no concern for the recent events in London.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A worry; a sense that something may be wrong; an identification of a possible problem.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Let me know if you have any questions or concerns about the document.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The expression of solicitude, anxiety, or compassion toward a thing or person.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Judy's eyes filled with concern as she listened to the news report.

   **Source synonyms:** care; trouble

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A business, firm or enterprise; a company.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The employees’ attitude is really hurting the concern.

   **Example:** a going concern

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Any set of information that affects the code of a computer program.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; programming; sciences

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To relate or belong to; to have reference to or connection with; to affect the interest of; to be of importance to.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To engage by feeling or sentiment; to interest.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** A good prince concerns himself in the happiness of his subjects.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I’m concerned that she’s becoming an alcoholic.
