### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An exclamation of astonishment, often after someone has said something shocking or unbelievable.

   **Usage:** UK; dated; informal

   **Example:** "Thomas just got arrested for shoplifting." "Well, I'll go to the foot of the stairs!"
