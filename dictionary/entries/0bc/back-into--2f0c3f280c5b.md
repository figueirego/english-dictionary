### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To advance to the post-season as a result of another team's loss, especially where one's own team has also lost.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** The 2006 St. Louis Cardinals backed into the playoffs.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To be forced into a situation by circumstances.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I didn't have any other options, I was backed into it.
