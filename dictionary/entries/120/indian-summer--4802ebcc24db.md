### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A stretch of sunny and warm, often hazy, days during late autumn.

   **Usage:** offensive; possibly

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The late autumn of life; a late flowering of activity before old age.

   **Usage:** figuratively

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see Indian, summer.
