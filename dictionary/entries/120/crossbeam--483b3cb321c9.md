### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A horizontal structural beam that runs perpendicular to the primary support beams; a girder.

   **Example:** The workman hung a light from a crossbeam between the main joists.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A beam laid across the bitts, to which the cable is fastened when riding at anchor.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport
