## Noun · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A method of deep diving which involves the diver remaining under high pressure for the entire period of a diving operation (as opposed to repeated pressurization and depressurization), resulting in their tissues being fully saturated with dissolved gas.

   **Usage:** uncountable
