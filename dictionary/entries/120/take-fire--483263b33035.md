### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To catch fire; to begin to burn.

   **Usage:** dated

   **Source synonyms:** go up in flames; catch fire; catch on fire; combust (intransitive); conflagrate; fire (dated); go up in flames; go up in smoke; ignite; light; spunk (obsolete); take fire (dated)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To become excited or enthused about something.

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To be subjected to enemy attack with firearms; to be under fire.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** The unit was taking fire from the guerrillas.

   **Example:** She took heavy fire from all quarters.
