### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. to pick up or clear up by scooping

   **Example:** Use this to scoop up the ice cream.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To take enthusiastically.

   **Example:** We scooped up every one of their albums as soon as they were released.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To grab or pick up something by accident.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** We must've scooped up a cold virus along with those kids.
