### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The highest honor or prize awarded in some competitions and contests.

   **Example:** Mom's strawberry jam won the blue ribbon at the Holland County Fair three years running.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The emblem of the Order of the Garter.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The badge adopted by a teetotal society.
