### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Speech or writing in which clauses or phrases are placed together without being separated by conjunctions.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** He said "I came; I saw; I conquered," is an instance of parataxis.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** grammar; human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

   **Source antonyms:** hypotaxis

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The juxtaposition of two images or fragments, usually starkly dissimilar, without a clear connection.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** literature; media; publishing

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A coalition or "partisan camp" in the Ancient Greek political system.

   **Usage:** historical; uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; politics
