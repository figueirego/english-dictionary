## Proper noun · Record 1

### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A fictional character, Uriah Heep, in the 1850 Charles Dickens novel David Copperfield, noted for his cloying humility, obsequiousness, and insincerity, the stereotypical yes-man.
