### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Making good, thorough, or careful use of resources; not consuming extra. Especially, making good use of time or energy.

   **Example:** An efficient process would automate all the routine work.

   **Example:** Our cleaners are almost too efficient&#58; they throw away anything left out on a desk.

   **Source synonyms:** lean; businesslike

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Expressing the proportion of consumed energy that was successfully used in a process; the ratio of useful output to total input.

   **Example:** The motor is only 20% efficient at that temperature.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Causing effects, producing results; bringing into being; initiating change (rare except in philosophical and legal expression efficient cause = causative factor or agent).

   **Example:** Ownership, maintenance, or use of the automobile need not be the direct and efficient cause of the injury sustained

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. effective, efficacious

   **Usage:** proscribed; old use

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** economical; efficacious; efficient; potent

**Source antonyms:** inefficient; prodigal; uneconomical
