### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A short, heavy club, often of wood, which is thicker or loaded at one end.

   **Example:** We smashed the radio with a steel bludgeon.

2. Something used to coerce someone.

## Verb · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈblʌd͡ʒ.ən/ — source labels: UK; US
- IPA: /ˈblʊd͡ʒ.ən/ — source labels: Northern-England

### Forms

- bludgeons — present; singular; third-person
- bludgeoning — participle; present
- bludgeoned — participle; past
- bludgeoned — past

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To strike or hit with something hard, usually on the head; to club.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The apprehended rioter was bludgeoned to death.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To coerce someone, as if with a bludgeon.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** Their favorite method was bludgeoning us with the same old arguments in favor of their opinions.
