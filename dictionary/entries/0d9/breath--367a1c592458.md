<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act or process of breathing.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** I could hear the breath of the runner behind me.

   **Example:** The child's breath came quickly and unevenly.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A single act of breathing in or out; a breathing of air.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** I took a deep breath and started the test.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Air expelled from the lungs.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** I could feel the runner's breath on my shoulder.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A rest or pause.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** Let's stop for a breath when we get to the top of the hill.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A small amount of something, such as wind, or common sense.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Even with all the windows open, there is hardly a breath of air in here.

   **Example:** If she had a breath of common sense, she would never have spoken to the man in the first place.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Fragrance; exhalation; odor; perfume.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Gentle exercise, causing a quicker respiration.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable
