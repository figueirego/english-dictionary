### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To be unable to do anything (about something) other than hope \[with that (+ clause); or (informal) with clause; or with so or (negative) not\]; especially with long odds.

   **Usage:** UK; dated; no-past-participle; no-present-participle

   **Example:** We can but hope that he returns alive.
