### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An interest rate that a central bank charges depository institutions that borrow reserves from it.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The interest rate used to discount future cashflows of a financial instrument; the annual interest rate used to decrease the amounts of future cashflows to yield their present value.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
