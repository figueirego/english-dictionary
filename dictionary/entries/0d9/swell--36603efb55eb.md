### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To become larger, fuller, or rounder, or expand in size or shape, usually as a result of pressure from within.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** My ankles had swelled in the heat.

   **Source synonyms:** balloon

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To cause to become bigger.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Rains and dissolving snow swell the rivers in spring.

   **Source synonyms:** distend

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To grow gradually in force or loudness.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The organ music swelled.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To cause to grow gradually in force or loudness.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To raise to arrogance; to puff up; to inflate.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to be swelled with pride or haughtiness

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To be raised to arrogance.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To be elated; to rise arrogantly.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To be turgid, bombastic, or extravagant.

   **Example:** swelling words a swelling style

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To protuberate; to bulge out.

   **Example:** A cask swells in the middle.
