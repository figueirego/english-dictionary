### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In a formal manner.

   **Example:** He was dressed too formally for the occasion.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In accordance with official procedure.

   **Example:** He formally filed a complaint, which involved much paperwork.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. In accordance with rigorous rules.

   **Example:** He proved it formally but gave his students no intuitive feel for the matter.
