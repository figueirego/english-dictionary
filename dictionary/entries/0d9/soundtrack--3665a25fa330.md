## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈsaʊndˌtɹæk/ — source labels: General-American

### Forms

- soundtracks — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A narrow strip running down a movie film that carries the recorded sound in synchronization with the pictures.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The sound (especially the music) component of a movie, video game, etc.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A recording or compilation of recordings of such music for sale.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A background sound that is part of a larger event.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Popular music associated with a particular historical era or time of one's life, especially nostalgically.

   **Example:** the soundtrack of our relationship
