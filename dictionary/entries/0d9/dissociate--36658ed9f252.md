### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To cease associating; To part.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** After the big fight, the gang totally dissociated from each other.

   **Source antonyms:** associate

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To cease associating; To part.
   1. To undergo dissociation.

      **Usage:** intransitive

      **Example:** Gerald checked himself into the hospital because he was dissociating.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; psychology; sciences

      **Source antonyms:** associate

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To cease associating with; to make unrelated; to sever a connection; to separate.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** A number of group members wish to dissociate themselves from the majority.

   **Source antonyms:** associate

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**
