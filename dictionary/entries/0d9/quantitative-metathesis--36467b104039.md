### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A form of metathesis or transposition involving quantity or vowel length, by which two neighboring vowels – one long, one short – switch their lengths, so that the long one becomes short, and the short one becomes long.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; phonology; prosody; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** transfer of quantity
