### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To cook or undergo heating slowly at or below the boiling point.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The soup simmered on the stove.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To cause to cook or to cause to undergo heating slowly at or below the boiling point.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Simmer the soup for five minutes, then serve.

   **Source synonyms:** coddle

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To be on the point of breaking out into anger; to be agitated.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To remain angry with someone or something past the point of exhaustion; to resign oneself to holding a grudge, especially after some failed attempts to resolve a situation.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

   **Example:** I tried to get through to him; all that's left for me to do is simmer.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To develop gradually, of an idea or plan.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

   **Source synonyms:** gestate; ferment
