### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To move busily and energetically with fussiness (often followed by about).

   **Example:** The commuters bustled about inside the train station.

   **Source synonyms:** flit; hustle; scamper; scurry

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To teem or abound (usually followed by with); to exhibit an energetic and active abundance (of a thing).

   **Example:** The train station was bustling with commuters.

   **Source synonyms:** abound; brim; bristle; burst; crawl; swell; teem

**Source sense R2.S3**
