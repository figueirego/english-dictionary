### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To cease giving breast milk to an offspring; to accustom and reconcile (a child or young animal) to a want or deprivation of mother's milk; to take from the breast or udder.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The cow has weaned her calf.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To cease giving breast milk to an offspring; to accustom and reconcile (a child or young animal) to a want or deprivation of mother's milk; to take from the breast or udder.
   1. To habituate (someone) to something, especially since childhood.

      **Usage:** broadly; often; transitive; chiefly passive voice

      **Example:** His generation was weaned on 1980's music.

      **Source context (verbatim):** followed by on

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To cease to depend on the mother's milk for nutrition.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The kittens are finally weaning.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To cause to quit something to which one is addicted, dependent, or habituated.

   **Usage:** broadly; transitive

   **Example:** He managed to wean himself off heroin.

   **Source context (verbatim):** normally "wean off"

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To cease to depend.

   **Usage:** broadly; intransitive

   **Example:** She is weaning from her addiction to tobacco.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To raise, to help grow toward maturity

   **Usage:** broadly; obsolete; transitive
