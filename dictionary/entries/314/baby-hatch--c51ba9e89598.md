### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A place where a person (typically the mother) can bring a baby to abandon it so that somebody else can find and care for it.
   1. A physical structure designated for this purpose, such as a foundling wheel.

      **Usage:** historical

      **Source synonyms:** baby box

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A place where a person (typically the mother) can bring a baby to abandon it so that somebody else can find and care for it.
   1. A modern healthcare facility for this purpose, incorporating an incubator.

      **Usage:** broadly

      **Source synonyms:** baby box
