### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An abrupt turn to face the opposite direction.

   **Example:** The soldier did an about-face and marched off.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A reversal in direction; a reversal of attitude or opinion.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** When Luke heard the news, he did an abrupt about-face on the policy.

### Forms

- about-facing — participle; present
- about-faced — participle; past
- about-faced — past
- about face — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To turn 180 degrees to face the opposite direction.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The soldiers would about-face immediately after the order was given.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To change opinion or attitude drastically.

   **Example:** A politician will about-face at the drop of a hat if he thinks there are votes in it.
