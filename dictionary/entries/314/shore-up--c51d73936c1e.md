### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To reinforce or strengthen (something at risk of failure).

   **Usage:** figuratively; often; transitive

   **Example:** They hastened outside between storms to shore up the damaged fence.

   **Example:** He needed something bold and dramatic to shore up his failing candidacy.

   **Example:** I shored up a geranium with earth after it had flopped over.

   **Source synonyms:** embolster (rare); prop up; underfoot; undergird; underpin; underprop; underset
