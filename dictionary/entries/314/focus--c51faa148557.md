### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A point at which reflected or refracted rays of light converge.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** The heat of sunlight at the focus of a magnifying glass can easily set dry leaves on fire.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; natural-sciences; optics; physical-sciences; physics

   **Source synonyms:** focal point

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A point of a conic at which rays reflected from a curve or surface converge.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The fact of the convergence of light on the photographic medium.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Unfortunately, the license plate is out of focus in this image.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** arts; broadcasting; cinematography; film; hobbies; lifestyle; media; photography; television

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The quality of the convergence of light on the photographic medium.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** During this scene, the boy’s face shifts subtly from soft focus into sharp focus.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** arts; broadcasting; cinematography; film; hobbies; lifestyle; media; photography; television

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Concentration of attention.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** I believe I can bring the high degree of focus required for this important job.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Something to which activity, attention or interest is primarily directed.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** My studies are my main focus right now.

   **Source synonyms:** focal point

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The exact point of where an earthquake occurs, in three dimensions (underneath the epicentre).

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** The earthquake's focus was at exactly 37 degrees north, 18 degrees south, seventy five meters below the ground.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; geology; natural-sciences; seismology

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. The status of being the currently active element in a user interface, often indicated by a visual highlight.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Text entered at the keyboard or pasted from a clipboard is sent to the component which currently has the focus.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; graphical-user-interface; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. The most important word or phrase in a sentence or passage, or the one that imparts information.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. An object used in casting a magic spell.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. The centre of an older fish's scale, which is the point where a younger fish's scale starts to grow from.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; ichthyology; natural-sciences; zoology

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To cause (rays of light, etc) to converge at a single point.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To adjust (a lens, an optical instrument) in order to position an image with respect to the focal plane.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** You'll need to focus the microscope carefully in order to capture the full detail of this surface.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; natural-sciences; optics; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To adjust itself or be adjusted such that light from a scene converges appropriately to create a clear image.

   **Usage:** intransitive; usually; optical instrument

   **Example:** I can't get the lens to focus.

   **Example:** The camera focuses automatically on the subject's eyes.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; natural-sciences; optics; physical-sciences; physics

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a lens

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To direct attention, effort, or energy to a particular audience or task.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The president focused her remarks on the newcomers.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To concentrate one’s attention.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** If you're going to beat your competitors, you need to focus.

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To concentrate one's attention on something; to have as one's central point of interest, concern, etc.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** I have to focus on my work.

   **Source context (verbatim):** followed by on or upon

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

**Source sense R2.S7**

7. To transfer the input focus to (a visual element), so that it receives subsequent input.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The text box won't receive the user's keystrokes unless you explicitly focus it.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; graphical-user-interface; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r2-s8"></a>

**Source sense R2.S8**

8. To aggregate figures of accounts.

   **Usage:** formerly

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** accounting; business; finance
