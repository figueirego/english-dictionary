### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who scores.

   **Example:** Alan Shearer finished among the top ten goal scorers in 10 out of his 14 seasons in the Premier League and won the top scorer title three times..

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One who keeps track of scores in a game; a scorekeeper.

   **Example:** The team was making goals so fast the scorer could barely keep up.
