### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To wind (something) again.

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To wind back, of something clocklike or recordlike, like a cassette, tape, film; especially to then replay or relive.

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** Let's rewind the clock to an earlier time.

   **Example:** Rewind the tape to the first chapter.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To regress the state of playback of a recording.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** I've rewound the audiobook to the start of the chapter.

   **Example:** This video player can rewind smoothly.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To go back or think back to a previous moment or place, or a previous point in a discourse.

   **Usage:** figuratively
