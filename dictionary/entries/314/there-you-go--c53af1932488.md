## Phrase

### Forms

- there ya go — alternative; colloquial

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. You have done it, or are doing it, correctly.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; informal

   **Example:** Attagirl! There you go! Keep it up! Now pass the ball back to me again!

   **Source synonyms:** well done; good job

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used to explain or justify something, such as one's course of action.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; informal

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Expressing that the conclusion is the same as it was suggested before; the other party's statement actually implies the same thing as one's own idea.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; informal

   **Example:** \- Do you want to punish crime more severely? - I'm not sure. I do want a lower crime rate... - Well, there you go.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Expressing exasperation.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; informal

   **Example:** There you go, yelling again. Will you shut up?

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Used while giving someone something.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; informal

   **Example:** He told me to bring it back to you when I was done. There you go! Happy Friday!

   **Source synonyms:** here you are; here you go; there you are
