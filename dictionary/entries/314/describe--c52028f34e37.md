### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To represent in words.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The feeling is difficult to describe, but not unpleasant.

   **Example:** The geographer describes countries and cities.

   **Example:** Several witnesses describe seeing lights in the sky that night.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To represent by drawing; to draw a plan of; to delineate; to trace or mark out.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to describe a circle by the compasses; a torch waved about the head in such a way as to describe a circle

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To give rise to a geometrical structure.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The function describes a very complex surface.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To introduce a new taxon to science by explaining its characteristics and particularly how it differs from other taxa.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The fungus was first described by a botanist.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences; taxonomy

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To distribute into parts, groups, or classes; to mark off; to class.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive
