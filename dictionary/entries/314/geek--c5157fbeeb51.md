### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A carnival performer specializing in bizarre and unappetizing behavior.

   **Usage:** dated

   **Example:** I once saw a geek bite the head off a live chicken.

   **Source synonyms:** freak

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person who is intensely interested in a particular field or hobby and often having limited or nonstandard social skills. Often used with an attributive noun.

   **Usage:** colloquial

   **Example:** I was a complete computer geek in high school, but I get out a lot more now.

   **Example:** Most famous actors are really theater geeks at heart.

   **Source synonyms:** anorak; buff; dork; freak; nerd; otaku; [propeller head](../385/propeller-head--e153bf36e98e.md)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An expert in a technical field, particularly one having to do with computers.

   **Usage:** broadly; colloquial

   **Example:** My laptop’s locked up again. I need a geek.

   **Source synonyms:** freak; hacker; tech guru

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The subculture of geeks; an esoteric subject of interest that is marginal to the social mainstream; the philosophy, events, and physical artifacts of geeks; geekness.

   **Usage:** colloquial; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An unfashionable or socially undesirable person.

   **Usage:** colloquial

   **Example:** Why do you hang around with them? They’re just geeks.
