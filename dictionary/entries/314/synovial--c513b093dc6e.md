### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Relating to or denoting a type of joint which is surrounded by a thick flexible membrane forming a sac into which is secreted a viscous fluid that lubricates the joint.
   1. Of, relating to, or secreting synovial fluid.

      **Usage:** not-comparable
