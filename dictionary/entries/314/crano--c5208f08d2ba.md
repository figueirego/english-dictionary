## Noun

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈkɹænoʊ/ — source labels: General-American

### Forms

- cranos — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who is hyper-fixated on the welfare of others and desperately wants to fix their problems, but feels completely helpless, physically exhausted, or mentally "braindead" due to their own personal limitations.

   **Usage:** Internet; derogatory; slang; sometimes

   **Example:** She's being a total crano again, staying up all night stressing over how to fix her friend's life while her own room is a mess.

### Related words
