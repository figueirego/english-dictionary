### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To run away; to escape.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The prisoner tried to flee, but was caught by the guards.

   **Source synonyms:** run for it

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To escape from.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Many people fled the country as war loomed.

   **Example:** Thousands of people moved northward trying to flee the drought.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To disappear quickly; to vanish; to fleet.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Ethereal products flee once freely exposed to air.
