### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A curry made from various spices especially coriander and cumin; and often with yoghurt sauce or nuts.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A curry made from various spices especially coriander and cumin; and often with yoghurt sauce or nuts.
   1. A curry of meat slowly braised with ghee.

      **Usage:** Bangladesh; India; Pakistan; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A curry made from various spices especially coriander and cumin; and often with yoghurt sauce or nuts.

      **Usage:** UK; countable; uncountable
