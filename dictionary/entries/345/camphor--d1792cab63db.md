### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A white transparent waxy crystalline isoprenoid ketone, 1,7,7-trimethylbicyclo\[2.2.1\]heptan-2-one, with a strong pungent odour, used in pharmacy, also known as laurel camphor or common/ordinary camphor.
