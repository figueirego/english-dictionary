## Noun

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈɡætlɪŋ ˌɡʌn/ — source labels: General-American

### Forms

- Gatling guns — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A type of repeating firearm with several barrels on a rotating frame, in use from the 1860s to the 1910s, providing fully automatic fire similar to that of modern machine guns and autocannons.

   **Example:** They say that the Gatling who invented Gatling guns imagined that they would reduce the scale of human warfare, poor old chap.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of various later weapons with a similar concept of operation (spinning a rotating set of barrels).

   **Usage:** broadly; slang

   **Example:** I stood in the door of an AC-130 and ran a Gatling gun. So it goes. What more is there to say about it?

   **Source synonyms:** minigun; autocannon
