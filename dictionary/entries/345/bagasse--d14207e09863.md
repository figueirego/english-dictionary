### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The residue from processing sugar cane or other plants such as sorghum or sugar beet after the juice is extracted.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source synonyms:** megass; megasse

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
