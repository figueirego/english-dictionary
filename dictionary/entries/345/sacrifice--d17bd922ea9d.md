### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Originally, the killing (and often burning) of a human being or an animal as an offering to a deity; later, also the offering of an object to a deity.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** lifestyle; religion

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A human being or an animal, or a physical object or immaterial thing (see etymology 1 sense 1.3), offered to a deity.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** lifestyle; religion

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The offering of devotion, penitence, prayer, thanksgiving, etc., to a deity.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** lifestyle; religion

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Jesus Christ's voluntary offering of himself to God the Father to be crucified as atonement for the sins of humankind.

   **Usage:** countable; specifically; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity; lifestyle; religion

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The rite of Holy Communion or the Mass, regarded as (Protestantism) an offering of thanksgiving to God for Christ's crucifixion, or (Roman Catholicism) a perpetual re-presentation of Christ's sacrificial offering.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; specifically; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity; lifestyle; religion

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The destruction or surrender of anything for the sake of something else regarded as more urgent or valuable; also, the thing destroyed or surrendered for this purpose.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

   **Example:** the sacrifice of one’s spare time in order to volunteer

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Ellipsis of sacrifice bunt or sacrifice hit (“a play in which the batter intentionally hits the ball softly with a hands-spread batting stance at the cost of an out to advance one or more runners”).

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; countable; ellipsis; figuratively; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; baseball; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

   **Alternative form of:** sacrifice bunt or sacrifice hit

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. In full sacrifice bid&#58; a bid of a contract which is unlikely to be fulfilled, that a player makes in the hope that they will incur fewer penalty points than the points likely to be gained by opponents in making their contract.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** bridge; games

   **Source synonyms:** save

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A monetary loss incurred by selling something at less than its value; also, the thing thus sold.

   **Usage:** countable; dated; figuratively; slang; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. An act of intentionally allowing one's piece to be captured by the opponent in order to improve one's position in the game.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** board-games; chess; games
