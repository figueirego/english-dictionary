## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Not clinical, as&#58;
   1. Medical but not clinical in the sense of clinical medicine, being instead, for example, radiological, histopathological, surgical, or laboratorial.

      **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Not clinical, as&#58;
   1. Not medical, being instead lay in nature, or at most paramedical.

      **Usage:** not-comparable

      **Example:** helping people to learn about first aid from a nonclinical viewpoint

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Not clinical, as&#58;
   1. Lacking clinical objectivity; subjective; emotional.

      **Usage:** not-comparable

      **Example:** Societal approaches to mental illness often clearly reflect a nonclinical bias, as for example with stigmatization or fear.
