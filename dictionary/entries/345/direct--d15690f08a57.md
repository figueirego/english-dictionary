### Senses

<a id="r3-s1"></a>

**Source sense R3.S1**

1. To manage, control, steer.

   **Example:** to direct the affairs of a nation or the movements of an army

<a id="r3-s2"></a>

**Source sense R3.S2**

2. To aim (something) at (something else).

   **Example:** They directed their fire towards the men on the wall.

   **Example:** He directed his question to the room in general.

<a id="r3-s3"></a>

**Source sense R3.S3**

3. To point out to or show (somebody) the right course or way; to guide, as by pointing out the way; to refer.

   **Example:** He directed me to the left-hand road.

<a id="r3-s4"></a>

**Source sense R3.S4**

4. To point out to with authority; to instruct as a superior; to order.

   **Example:** She directed them to leave immediately.

**Source sense R3.S5**
