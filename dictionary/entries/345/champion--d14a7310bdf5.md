### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An ongoing winner in a game or contest.

   **Example:** The defending champion is expected to defeat his challenger.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Someone who is chosen to represent a group of people in a contest.

   **Example:** Barcelona is eligible to play in FIFA Club World Cup as the champion of Europe.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Someone who fights for a cause or status.

   **Example:** Emmeline Pankhurst was a champion of women's suffrage.

   **Source synonyms:** paladin; knight

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Someone who fights on another's behalf.

   **Example:** champion of the poor

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A particularly notable member of a plant species, such as one of great size.
