### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To relax; to make quieter or less obtrusive; to make milder.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** Ask them to tone down that orange and pink color scheme a bit.

   **Example:** I hope they’ll tone it down and stop arguing so much.

   **Source antonyms:** tone up

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To moderate or relax; to diminish or weaken the striking characteristics of; to soften.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive; figuratively

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To make a television program, piece of writing, etc. less offensive and so more suitable for a family audience.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** broadcasting; literature; media; publishing; television
