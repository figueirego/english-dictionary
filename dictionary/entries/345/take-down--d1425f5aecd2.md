## Verb

### Forms

- takes down — present; singular; third-person
- taking down — participle; present
- took down — past
- taken down — participle; past

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To remove something from a wall or similar vertical surface to which it is fixed.

   **Example:** He took down the picture and replaced it with the framed photograph.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To remove something from a hanging position.

   **Example:** We need to take down the curtains to be cleaned.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To remove something from a website.

   **Example:** We must take this fake news item down today.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To write down as a note, especially to record something spoken.

   **Example:** If you have a pen, you can take down my phone number.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To remove a temporary structure such as scaffolding.

   **Example:** When everything else is packed, we can take down the tent.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To lower an item of clothing without removing it.

   **Example:** The doctor asked me to take down my trousers.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To arrest someone or to place them in detention.

   **Example:** You have been found guilty. Take the prisoner down.

   **Example:** We've got enough evidence now to take McFee down.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To swallow.

   **Example:** I took down the medicine and soon felt better.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To defeat; to destroy or kill (a person).

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. To force one’s opponent off their feet in order to transition from striking to grappling in jujitsu, mixed martial arts, etc.

   **Usage:** combat sports

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. To collapse or become incapacitated from illness or fatigue.

   **Usage:** colloquial; intransitive

<a id="r1-s12"></a>

**Source sense R1.S12**

12. To reduce.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to take the volume down a bit
