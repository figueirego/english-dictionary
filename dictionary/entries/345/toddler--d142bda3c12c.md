### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A young child (typically between one and three years old) who has started walking but not fully mastered it.

   **Example:** I can't believe Vanessa's baby is now a toddler already!

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One unsteady on their feet.

   **Usage:** broadly; figuratively

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any young child (typically before preadolescence) who is capable of walking.

   **Usage:** broadly; colloquial; rare

   **Example:** He gave up, said the sixes were being too rowdy... Can't believe he's scared of literal toddlers!
