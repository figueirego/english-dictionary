## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Without a cab and driver, and therefore a remotely controlled vehicle (driver's compartment in a truck or train).

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** a cabless booster locomotive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Without a roof over the driving position.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Without any taxicabs.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** a bleak, cabless city square
