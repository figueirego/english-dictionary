### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A larva of any chironomid midge in the (family Chironomidae).

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any polychete worm of genus Glycera.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>
