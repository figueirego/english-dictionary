### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Made physically hard.

   **Example:** hardened steel

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Having been accustomed or desensitized to something unpleasant through exposure or experience; inured.

   **Example:** hardened police officers

   **Source synonyms:** habituated; toughened; hard-bitten

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. Unfeeling; pitiless; callous.

   **Example:** The bloody scene could reduce even the most hardened soldier to tears.

   **Source synonyms:** harsh; severe; brutal; callous; cold; insensate (formal); hardhanded; cold-eyed; cruel; dour; thick-skinned; flint-hearted; forbidding; frosty; grim; hard; hard-boiled; hardened; hardhearted; heartless; harsh; humorless; icy; iron-hearted; insensitive; marble-hearted; rigorous; rockhearted; severe; stern; stonehearted; stony; stonyhearted; strict; tough; unsympathetic; unmoved

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. Firmly established in a behavior seen as bad; unlikely to change one's ways; inveterate.

   **Example:** hardened criminals

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. Having extra defences against attack; highly fortified.

   **Example:** a hardened air base
