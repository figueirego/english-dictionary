### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A pin in the form of a clasp, which has a guard to cover the sharp point; used to join two pieces of fabric, etc., together temporarily.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; manufacturing; sewing; textiles
