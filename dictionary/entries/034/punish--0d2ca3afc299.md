### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To cause (a child, student, or someone else being looked after, or a suspect or criminal) to suffer for crime or misconduct, to administer disciplinary action, typically by an authority or a person in authority (for example&#58; a parent, teacher, or police officer).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** If a prince violates the law, then he must be punished like an ordinary person.

   **Source synonyms:** castigate; penalize; pillory

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To treat harshly and unfairly.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** mistreat

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To handle or beat severely; to maul.

   **Usage:** colloquial; transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To consume a large quantity of.

   **Usage:** colloquial; transitive
