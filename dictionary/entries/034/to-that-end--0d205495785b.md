### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. For that reason, with that goal, intending to produce that result.

   **Usage:** conjunctive; formal; idiomatic

   **Example:** We think that the world would be a better place without advertisements. To that end, we are going to remove all of the banners from our website.

   **Example:** He was aiming to get into the school swimming team, and to that end he swam every evening.

   **Source synonyms:** for that reason; therefore; so; wherefore; to which end; to this end; towards that end; for which purpose; for that purpose; for this purpose; to accomplish which
