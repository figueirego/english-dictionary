### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To file a legal action against someone, generally a non-criminal (civil) action.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** sue someone for selling a faulty product

   **Example:** I plan to sue you for everything you have.

   **Source synonyms:** implead

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To seek by request; to make application; to petition; to entreat; to plead.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** The king of Bimsa sued for peace after Nynal's armies overran his territory; he knew any further resistance would only harm the common people.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To clean (the beak, etc.).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** falconry; hobbies; hunting; lifestyle

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a hawk

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To leave high and dry on shore.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to sue a ship

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive
