### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To speak with exaggerated or rapturous enthusiasm (about, (up)on or over something).

   **Usage:** British; English; Oxford; US; intransitive

   **Source synonyms:** rave

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To say (something) with exaggerated or rapturous enthusiasm.

   **Usage:** British; English; Oxford; US; transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To recount or describe (something) as a rhapsody, or in the manner of a rhapsody.

   **Usage:** British; English; Oxford; US; transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

   **Usage:** British; English; Oxford; US; intransitive
