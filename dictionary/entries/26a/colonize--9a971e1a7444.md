### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To settle (a place) with colonists, and hence make (a place) into a colony.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To settle among and establish control over (the indigenous people of an area).

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To begin a colony or colonies.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To intrude into and take over (the autonomy, experience, social movement, etc, of a less powerful person or group); to commandeer or appropriate.

   **Usage:** broadly; transitive
