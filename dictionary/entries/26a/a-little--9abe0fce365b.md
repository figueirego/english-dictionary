## Adverb · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To a small extent or degree.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The door was opened a little.

   **Example:** Helping not a little was a squad of volunteers.

   **Example:** How are you feeling? ~ A little better, thanks.

   **Source synonyms:** a bit; a little bit; a tad

   **Source antonyms:** a lot; a tad

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Slightly.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** She's a little strange, but nice enough.

   **Source synonyms:** a bit; a little; a little bit; a mite; a smidgen; a smidgeon; a tad; a touch (dialectal); a trifle; barely; closely; faintly; hardly; imperceptibly; just; kinda; kinda sorta; kind of (idiomatic); low-key; marginally; mildly; modestly; nearly; only just; scarcely; slightly; somewhat; sorta; sorta kinda; sort of

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Very.

   **Usage:** UK; not-comparable

   **Example:** It's a little big, don't you think?

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A lot.

   **Usage:** UK; not-comparable

**Source sense R1.S5**

## Determiner · Record 2

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A small amount of (an uncountable thing).

   **Example:** A little water has spilled.

   **Example:** All we need is a little time and money to carry this idea to fruition.

   **Example:** All that is left is a little bacon and a few eggs.

   **Example:** I've been starting to get a little pain in both knees.

   **Source antonyms:** zero; no; a lot of; much
