## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of a large number of parks, sports grounds, and some suburbs around the world, mainly in the British Commonwealth.
   1. A suburb of Perth, Western Australia, south-east of the city centre.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of a large number of parks, sports grounds, and some suburbs around the world, mainly in the British Commonwealth.
   1. A local government area in Western Australia, which includes the suburb; in full, the Town of Victoria Park.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of a large number of parks, sports grounds, and some suburbs around the world, mainly in the British Commonwealth.
   1. A southern suburb of Manchester, Greater Manchester, England (OS grid ref SJ8595).
