### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To flee by running.

   **Example:** The crowd had to run away from the burning structure with only the clothes on their backs.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To leave home, or other place of residence, usually unannounced, or to make good on a threat, with such action usually performed by a child or juvenile.

   **Example:** My parents want me to take a bath every day? Fine, I'm running away.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To become a runaway. (of a device or vehicle)

   **Example:** The train's brakes failed and it ran away.

   **Example:** An autotrim failure can cause stabiliser trim to rapidly run away in the nose-up or nose-down direction.
