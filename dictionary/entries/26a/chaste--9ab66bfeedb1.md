### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Synonym of pure and virtuous, particularly
   1. Sexually pure, abstaining from immoral or unlawful sexual intercourse.

      **Example:** Theirs was a chaste marriage, loving and content.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Synonym of pure and virtuous, particularly
   1. Synonym of celibate, abstaining from any sexual intercourse.

      **Example:** Theirs was a chaste marriage, cold and sexless.

      **Source synonyms:** celibate (synonym; synonym-of); abstaining from any sexual intercourse (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Synonym of pure and virtuous, particularly
   1. Synonym of virginal, innocent, having or providing no sexual knowledge or experience.

      **Example:** Her upbringing had been so chaste that her first experience French kissing ended in a panicked dash to the Piggly Wiggly to buy a home pregnancy test, just in case.

      **Source synonyms:** virginal (synonym; synonym-of); innocent (synonym; synonym-of); having or providing no sexual knowledge or experience (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Synonym of pure and virtuous, particularly
   1. Synonym of modest, particularly (sexism) in reference to behavior expected of sexually pure women.

      **Example:** I ask again what reason could a chaste woman possibly have for being out so late? and in that part of town? Your Honor, we repeat our motion that you recuse yourself from this case.

      **Source synonyms:** modest (synonym; synonym-of); particularly (synonym; synonym-of)
