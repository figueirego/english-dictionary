<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Loud; shrill, piercing, high-pitched; rough-sounding.

   **Example:** The trumpet sounded strident against the string orchestra.

   **Source synonyms:** stentorian (formal)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Grating or obnoxious.

   **Example:** The artist chose a strident mixture of colors.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Forceful (typically in a negative way) or obtrusive.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**
