## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈfɪlɪp/ — source labels: General-American; Received-Pronunciation

### Forms

- fillips — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The action of holding the tip of a finger against the thumb and then releasing it with a snap; a flick.

   **Usage:** archaic

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A sharp strike or tap made using this action, or (by extension) by other means.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Something unimportant, a trifle; also, the brief time it takes to flick one's finger (see noun sense 1); a jiffy.

   **Usage:** broadly

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Something that excites or stimulates.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** This measure gave a fillip to the housing market.

   **Example:** The athlete’s victory provided a much-needed fillip for national pride.

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To strike, project, or propel with a fillip (that is, a finger released quickly after being pressed against the thumb); to flick.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** flick; ping

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To project quickly; to snap.

   **Usage:** broadly; transitive

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To strike or tap smartly.

   **Usage:** broadly; transitive

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To drive as if by a fillip (noun sense 1); to excite, stimulate, whet.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** The spicy aroma filliped my appetite.

   **Source synonyms:** inflame; animate; awaken; electrify (figuratively); enflame (figuratively); enkindle (figuratively); enthrill; exagitate (obsolete); excite; exhilarate; exsuscitate (obsolete); fillip; fire (figuratively); fire the blood; galvanize (figuratively); gin up; ignite (figuratively); inflame (figuratively); intoxicate (figuratively); kindle (figuratively); quicken; rouse; send (slang); set fire to (figuratively); set on fire (figuratively); sharpen (figuratively); stir the blood; suscitate (obsolete); thrill; vitalise; wake; warm the blood; whet (figuratively); wind up; work up; yerk (Scotland; obsolete)

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To make a fillip (noun sense 1) (with the fingers).

   **Usage:** ambitransitive
