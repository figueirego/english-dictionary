### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Presence of a normal amount of protein in the blood.
   1. Total serum protein quantity within reference range.

      **Usage:** uncountable; usually

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; medicine; natural-sciences; sciences

      **Source antonyms:** dysproteinemia; hypoproteinemia; hyperproteinemia

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Presence of a normal amount of protein in the blood.
   1. Presence of normal types and amounts of proteins in the blood, as defined by panels of various proteins all having been tested and found to be of normal bioactivity and amount.

      **Usage:** uncommon; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; medicine; natural-sciences; sciences
