**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Such a piece that has been divided.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** Work on one subdivision at a time.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A parcel of land that has been divided into lots.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A group of houses created by the same builder or in the same general area.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** They're putting in a new subdivision out past Black Ranch Road.
