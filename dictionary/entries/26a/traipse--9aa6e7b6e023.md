### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To walk in a messy or unattractively casual way; to trail through dirt.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To walk about, especially when expending much effort, or unnecessary effort.

   **Usage:** colloquial; intransitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To travel with purpose; usually a significant or tedious amount.

   **Usage:** colloquial; intransitive

   **Example:** While you were traipsing round Africa, I had to take care of mum and dad!

   **Example:** So after all that work, I traipsed down to the shop to grab something to eat.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To walk (a distance or journey) wearily or with effort

   **Usage:** colloquial; transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. to walk about or over (a place) aimlessly or insouciantly.

   **Usage:** colloquial; transitive
