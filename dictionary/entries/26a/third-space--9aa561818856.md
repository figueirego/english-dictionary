### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A state or situation in which cultural distinctions do not apply, allowing one to transcend cultural assumptions.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A place that is neither home nor work, providing a venue for social interaction in a way that those places do not provide.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** third place

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An extracellular and extravascular space where abnormal fluid buildup can occur when fluid balance goes wrong.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
