**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of various mesembryanthemums of the genus Carpobrotus, native to coastal areas of Australia, and producing edible fruits.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Term of abuse for an ugly person.
