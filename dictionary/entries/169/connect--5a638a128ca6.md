### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To join (to another object)&#58; to attach, or to be intended to attach or capable of attaching, to another object.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** I think this piece connects to that piece over there.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of an object

   **Source synonyms:** affix; join; put together; unite; accouple (obsolete); affix; assemble; associate; attach; bewed; bind; clasp; clinch; combine; conglomerate; conglutinate; conjoin; connect; construct; couple; entwine (figuratively); fay; fix; graft; hitch; hook; inosculate; interlink; join; joint; knit (figuratively); knot; link; marry (figuratively); piece together; piecen; pinion; put together; splice; unite; wed (figuratively); yoke

   **Source antonyms:** disconnect

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To join&#58; to attach, or to be intended to attach or capable of attaching, to each other.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Both roads have the same name, but they don't connect&#58; they're on opposite sides of the river, and there's no bridge there.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of two objects

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To arrive at an intended target; to land.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** When that roundhouse kick connected with his temple it sent him flying across the room.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a blow

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To join (two other objects), or to join (one object) to (another object)&#58; to be a link between two objects, thereby attaching them to each other.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The new railroad will connect the northern part of the state to the southern part.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of an object

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To join (two other objects), or to join (one object) to (another object)&#58; to take one object and attach it to another.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I connected the printer to the computer, but I couldn't get it work.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a person

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To join an electrical or telephone line to a circuit or network.

   **Example:** When the technician connects my house, I'll be able to access the internet.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To associate; to establish a relation between.

   **Example:** I didn't connect my lost jewelry with the news of an area cat burglar until the police contacted me.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To make a travel connection; to switch from one means of transport to another as part of the same trip.

   **Example:** I'm flying to London where I connect with a flight heading to Hungary.
