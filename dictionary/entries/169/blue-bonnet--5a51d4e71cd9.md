### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A traditional flat Scottish hat made of blue wool; a blue tam-o'-shanter.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Someone who wears such a hat; a Scotsman, especially a Scottish soldier.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of several blue flowering plants, especially cornflower, Centaurea cyanus, and plants of the genus Scabiosa.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A blue Australian parrot, especially Northiella haematogaster.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Any of several North American species of Lupinus common in the southwestern US, especially Lupinus texensis and Lupinus subcarnosus.

   **Usage:** US; especially
