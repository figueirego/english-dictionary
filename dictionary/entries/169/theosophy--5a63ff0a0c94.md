### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any doctrine of religious philosophy and mysticism claiming that knowledge of God can be attained through mystical insight and spiritual ecstasy, and that direct communication with the transcendent world is possible.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; lifestyle; philosophy; religion; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** lifestyle; religion
