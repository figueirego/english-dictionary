### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. it; A thing as the object of a verb or preposition that also appears as the subject

   **Usage:** neuter; personal; pronoun; reflexive; singular; third-person

   **Example:** When the opportunity presented itself, no feasible course of action suggested itself (to us).

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. it; used to intensify the subject, especially to emphasize that it is the only participant in the predicate

   **Usage:** emphatic; neuter; personal; pronoun; singular; third-person

   **Example:** The door itself is quite heavy.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. it; used to refer back to an earlier subject

   **Usage:** archaic; emphatic; neuter; personal; pronoun; singular; third-person
