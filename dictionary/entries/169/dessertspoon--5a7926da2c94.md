### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A unit of measure, being equivalent to two teaspoons or two-thirds of a tablespoon, or approximately ten millilitres; a dessertspoonful.

   **Source synonyms:** dsp.; dstspn.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. More generally, that volume of a substance which is contained within a dessert spoon.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An item of cutlery; a spoon, larger than a teaspoon and smaller than a tablespoon, used for eating dessert.

   **Usage:** rare
