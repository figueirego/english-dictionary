### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An industrial heating device, such as for smelting metal or firing ceramics.

   **Example:** Plans for the next phase include furni capable of inert atmospheres and partial vacuums.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A device that provides heat for a building.

   **Usage:** Canada; US

   **Example:** HVAC services include furnace maintenance.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any area that is excessively hot.

   **Usage:** colloquial; figuratively

   **Example:** The busy kitchen became a sweltering furnace.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A place or time of punishment, affliction, or great trial; severe experience or discipline.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** forged in the furnace of fierce competition

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A smithy or whole ironworks.

   **Usage:** historical; metonymically

   **Example:** The furnace at Durham helped keep Washington's army at arms.

   **Source synonyms:** forge
