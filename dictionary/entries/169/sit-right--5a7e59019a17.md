### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To suit one's constitution, so as not to upset one's stomach or make one sick when consumed.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** She's in the bathroom ― I guess the food was not sitting right.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of food or drink; in the negative

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To make one feel comfortable, to seem appropriate or acceptable to.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** The way you talk to your wife doesn't sit right with me.

   **Source context (verbatim):** in the negative
