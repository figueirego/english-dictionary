### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Unsuitable for some particular purpose.

   **Example:** That was a most inopportune spot for a picnic.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Happening/occurring at an inconvenient or inappropriate time.

   **Example:** The inopportune arrival of the bus cut short the interesting conversation.
