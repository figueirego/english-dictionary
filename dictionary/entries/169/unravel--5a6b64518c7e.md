### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To cause (something) to no longer be ravelled or tangled; to disentangle, to untangle.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Mother couldn’t unravel the ball of wool after the cat had played with it.

   **Source synonyms:** unsnarl; disentangle; feeze (Scotland); tease out; unravel; unsnarl; untangle

   **Source antonyms:** ravel

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To separate the threads of (something knitted or woven, such as clothing or fabric).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Stop playing with the seam of the tablecloth! You’ll unravel it.

   **Source synonyms:** ravel

   **Source antonyms:** ravel

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To separate the connected or united parts of (something); to throw (something) into disorder; to confound, to confuse, to disintegrate.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** to unravel the broad consensus which was created

   **Example:** to unravel the compromise achieved in the treaty

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To clear (something) from complication or difficulty; to investigate and solve (a mystery, a problem, etc.); to disentangle, to unfold, to work out.

   **Usage:** also; figuratively; reflexive; transitive

   **Example:** to unravel the confusion

   **Example:** to unravel a plot

   **Source synonyms:** ravel; unpuzzle; unriddle; straighten out; absolve (obsolete); decipher; get to the bottom of (idiomatic); iron out; meliorate; cure; puzzle out; ravel (archaic); reason out; resolve; riddle; sleuth; solve; tease out (figuratively); think out; unpuzzle; unravel (figuratively); unriddle; unsolve (obsolete); upsolve; work out; Any from Thesaurus&#58;untangle (figuratively)

   **Source antonyms:** ravel

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To reverse or undo (something); to annul, to negate.

   **Usage:** figuratively; obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To become no longer ravelled or tangled.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source synonyms:** ravel

   **Source antonyms:** ravel

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Of threads&#58; to become separated from something knitted or woven, such as clothing or fabric; also, of something knitted or woven&#58; to separate into threads; to come apart.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source synonyms:** come undone; ravel

   **Source antonyms:** ravel

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Of a thing&#58; to have its connected or united parts separated; to be thrown into disorder; to become confused or undone; to collapse.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive
