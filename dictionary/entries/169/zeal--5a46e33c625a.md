### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The fervour or tireless devotion for a person, cause, or ideal and determination in its furtherance; diligent enthusiasm; powerful interest.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** She extols the virtues of veganism with missionary zeal.

   **Source synonyms:** ardour; eagerness; enthusiasm; intensity; passion

   **Source antonyms:** apathy

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** zealot
