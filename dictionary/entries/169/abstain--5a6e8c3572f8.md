### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Keep or withhold oneself.

   **Usage:** obsolete; reflexive; transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Refrain from (something or doing something); keep from doing, especially an indulgence.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** In order to improve his health, Rob decided to abstain from smoking.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Refrain from (something or doing something); keep from doing, especially an indulgence.
   1. To refrain from drinking alcohol.

      **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Fast (not eat for a period).

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Deliberately refrain from casting one's vote at a meeting where one is present.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** I abstain from this vote, as I have no particular preference.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Hinder; keep back; withhold.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive
