## Adjective

### Forms

- life-and-death — alternative
- life or death — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of critical importance to the survival of a living organism.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Good camoflouge is life or death for many animals in the wild.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Having death as a possible or even likely outcome; perilous.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Ascending Mount Everest can be life or death for an inexperienced climber.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of critical importance to the success of a particular endeavor.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; not-comparable

   **Example:** Obtaining financing was a life-or-death matter for the research.
