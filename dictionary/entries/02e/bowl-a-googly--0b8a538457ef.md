<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. to surprise (someone); to introduce (to someone) something unexpected, underhand or requiring a quick reaction or correction.

   **Usage:** UK; figuratively; idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** Fred bowled me a googly when he asked me to explain those statistics in the meeting.
