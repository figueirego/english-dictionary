### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To separate or set apart (someone or something from other persons or things).

   **Usage:** archaic; literary; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** allocate; appropriate; shelve; assign; dedicate; detach; designate; earmark; preselect; put aside; secern (archaic); secernate (archaic); separate (obsolete); sepose (obsolete); sequester; set apart; set aside

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To separate (something from other things) in the mind; to discriminate, to distinguish.

   **Usage:** broadly; literary; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** differentiate; discern

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Synonym of secrete (“to extract or separate (a substance) from the blood, etc., for excretion or for the fulfilling of a physiological function”).

   **Usage:** archaic; literary; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** secrete (synonym; synonym-of)
