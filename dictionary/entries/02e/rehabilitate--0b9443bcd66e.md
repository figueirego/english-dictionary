### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To restore (someone) to their former state, reputation, possessions, status etc.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To vindicate; to restore the reputation or image of (a person, concept etc.).

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To return (something) to its original condition.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** restore; roll back; deconvert; reconvert; rehabilitate; restitute; restore; revert; roll back

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To restore or repair (a vehicle, building); to make habitable or usable again.

   **Usage:** Canada; US; transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; construction; manufacturing

   **Source synonyms:** fix; renovate; amend; correct; fix; fix up; furbish; retrieve; make whole; mend; patch; put to rights; rectify; recondition; reform; refurbish; remediate; remedy; renew; renovate; restore; restrive; revamp; right

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To restore to (a criminal etc.) the necessary training and education to allow for a successful reintegration into society; to retrain.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** criminology; human-sciences; law; sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To return (someone) to good health after illness, addiction, etc.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**
