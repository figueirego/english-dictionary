### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Not likely to be true.

   **Example:** It's highly improbable that aliens abducted you.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Not likely to happen.

   **Example:** Due to the loss of power, it is improbable that we will begin on time.
