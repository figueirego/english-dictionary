### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To submit a suffering person or an animal to euthanasia.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; informal; transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To end or destroy something for the good of the individuals involved in it.

   **Usage:** figuratively; idiomatic; informal; transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To relieve somebody's anxiety by supplying information that they wanted to know.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; informal; transitive

   **Example:** Well, I'll put you out of your misery&#58; the amount we won on the lottery was only ten dollars.
