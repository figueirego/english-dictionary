<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A formal meal served in the middle of the day.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Most people thought the food at the luncheon was mediocre.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A large, cheap, processed sausage served in thin slices.

   **Usage:** New-Zealand; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Ian bought the luncheon since it was on special.

   **Source synonyms:** Belgium; devon; fritz; polony

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

3. Any midday meal; lunch.

   **Usage:** countable; dated; uncountable

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>
