### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of or relating to George Kingsley Zipf (1902–1950), American linguist who studied statistical occurrences in different languages and proposed the Zipfian distribution, codified as Zipf's Law, one of a family of discrete power law probability distributions.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of or pertaining to Zipf's law or its Zipfian distribution.
