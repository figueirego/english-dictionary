### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An informal greeting, not requiring a literal response. In form a question, and thus followed by a question mark. Typical responses include

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I'm very well, thank you. How are you? (formal) I'm fine, thank you. / Fine, thanks. (informal) / Fine, and you? (informal)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. (Following a noun phrase; stress on are) An expression of derision, that the preceding referent is unworthy of the name. In form of an exclamation, and thus often followed by an exclamation mark.

   **Usage:** Ireland; idiomatic
