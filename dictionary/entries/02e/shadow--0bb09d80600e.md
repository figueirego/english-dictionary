### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A dark image projected onto a surface where light (or other radiation) is blocked by the shade of an object.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** My shadow lengthened as the sun began to set.

   **Example:** The X-ray showed a shadow on his lung.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Relative darkness, especially as caused by the interruption of light; gloom; obscurity.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** I immediately jumped into shadow as I saw them approach.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An area protected by an obstacle (likened to an object blocking out sunlight).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The mountains block the passage of rain-producing weather systems and cast a "shadow" of dryness behind them.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A reflected image, as in a mirror or in water.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; poetic; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. That which looms as though a shadow.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

   **Example:** I lived in her shadow my whole life.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A small degree; a shade.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** He did not give even a shadow of respect to the professor.

   **Example:** I don't have a shadow of doubt in my mind that my plan will succeed.

   **Source context (verbatim):** in the negative

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. An imperfect and faint representation.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** He came back from war the shadow of a man.

   **Example:** The neopagan ritual was only a pale shadow of the ones the Greeks held thousands of years ago.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A trainee, assigned to work with an experienced officer.

   **Usage:** UK; countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; law-enforcement

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. One who secretly or furtively follows another.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The constable was promoted to working as a shadow for the Royals.

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. An inseparable companion.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. A drop shadow effect applied to lettering in word processors etc.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** media; publishing; typography

<a id="r1-s12"></a>

**Source sense R1.S12**

12. An influence, especially a pervasive or a negative one.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s13"></a>

**Source sense R1.S13**

13. A spirit; a ghost; a shade.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s14"></a>

**Source sense R1.S14**

14. An uninvited guest accompanying one who was invited.

   **Usage:** Latinism; countable; obsolete; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** umbra

<a id="r1-s15"></a>

**Source sense R1.S15**

15. An unconscious aspect of the personality.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; Jungian psychology
