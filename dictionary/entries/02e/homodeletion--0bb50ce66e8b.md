### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A type of chromosomal deletion where both copies (alleles) of a particular DNA sequence are lost from an individual's genome.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The study identified a homodeletion in the CDKN2A gene, which is associated with an increased risk of certain cancers.
