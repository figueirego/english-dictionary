### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of, relating to, or being the person or entity after which someone or something is named; serving as an eponym.

   **Example:** Prince Hamlet is the eponymous protagonist of the Shakespearian tragedy Hamlet.

   **Example:** Robinson Crusoe is the eponymous hero of the book.

   **Example:** The language Limburgish is named after the eponymous provinces in Belgium and the Netherlands.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of a thing&#58; named after a person or entity.

   **Example:** The American singer-songwriter Madonna released her eponymous album in 1983.
