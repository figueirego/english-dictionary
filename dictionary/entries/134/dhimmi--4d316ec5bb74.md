<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A protected and specially taxed non-Muslim subject of a state governed under dhimma, a form of social contract, in accordance with sharia law.

   **Usage:** historical

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Islam; lifestyle; religion

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A non-Muslim characterized by a conciliatory stance towards Islam, abstaining from articulating opposing beliefs and refraining from criticizing the religion.

   **Usage:** derogatory; offensive

   **Source synonyms:** dhimwit
