### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An area of land set aside for environment preservation or recreation.
   1. A tract of ground kept in its natural state, about or adjacent to a residence, such as for the preservation of game, for walking, riding, or the like.

      **Example:** She went to the park for a jog with him.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An area of land set aside for environment preservation or recreation.
   1. A piece of ground in or near a city or town, enclosed and kept for ornament and recreation.

      **Example:** Hyde Park in London; Central Park in New York

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An area of land set aside for environment preservation or recreation.
   1. An enclosed parcel of land stocked with animals for hunting, which one may have by prescription or royal grant.

   **Usage:** US

   1. An open space occupied by or reserved for vehicles, matériel or stores.

      **Example:** a wagon park; an artillery park

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. An area used for specific purposes.
   1. A partially enclosed basin in which oysters are grown.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

   **Usage:** UK

   **Usage:** Australia; New-Zealand; colloquial

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To bring (something such as a vehicle) to a halt or store in a specified place.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** You can park the car in front of the house.

   **Example:** I parked the drive heads of my hard disk before travelling with my laptop.

   **Source antonyms:** unpark

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To defer (a matter) until a later date.

   **Usage:** figuratively; informal; transitive

   **Example:** Let's park that until next week's meeting.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To bring together in a park, or compact body.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to park artillery, wagons, automobiles, etc.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To enclose in a park, or as in a park.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To hit a home run; to hit the ball out of the park.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He really parked that one.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; baseball; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To engage in romantic or sexual activities inside a nonmoving vehicle that was driven to a suitable spot for that purpose.

   **Usage:** US; intransitive; slang

   **Example:** They stopped at a romantic overlook, shut off the engine, and parked.

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

**Source sense R2.S7**

7. To sit, recline, or put, especially in a manner suggesting an intent to remain for some time.

   **Usage:** informal; reflexive; sometimes; transitive

   **Example:** He came in and parked himself in our living room.

   **Example:** Park your bags in the hall.

<a id="r2-s8"></a>

**Source sense R2.S8**

8. To invest money temporarily in an investment instrument considered to relatively free of risk, especially while awaiting other opportunities.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** We decided to park our money in a safe, stable, low-yield bond fund until market conditions improve.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance

<a id="r2-s9"></a>

**Source sense R2.S9**

   **Usage:** Internet

<a id="r2-s10"></a>

**Source sense R2.S10**

10. To enclose in a park, or partially enclosed basin.

   **Usage:** transitive; oyster culture

<a id="r2-s11"></a>

**Source sense R2.S11**

11. To promenade or drive in a park.

   **Usage:** dated; intransitive

<a id="r2-s12"></a>

   **Usage:** dated; intransitive
