### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To the fullest extent or degree.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** They've totally demolished the bank on Main Street.

   **Source synonyms:** completely; entirely; fully; wholly

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Very; extremely.

   **Usage:** colloquial; not-comparable; degree

   **Example:** That was totally wicked!

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Definitely; for sure.

   **Usage:** colloquial; modal; not-comparable

   **Example:** That was totally not what happened.
