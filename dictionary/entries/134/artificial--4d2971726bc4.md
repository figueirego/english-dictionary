### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Man-made; made by humans; of artifice.

   **Example:** The flowers were artificial, and he thought them rather tacky.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Insincere; fake, forced, or feigned.

   **Example:** Her manner was somewhat artificial.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Not natural or normal&#58; imposed arbitrarily or without regard to the specifics or normal circumstances of a person, a situation, etc.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Based on characteristics useful for identification, without regard for the formal differences used in classification.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences; taxonomy

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Conveying some meaning other than the actual contents of one's hand.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** bridge; games

   **Source synonyms:** conventional
