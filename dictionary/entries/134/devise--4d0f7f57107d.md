### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To use one’s intellect to plan or design (something).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to devise an argument; to devise a machine, or a new system of writing

   **Source synonyms:** lay; set; design; plan; create

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To leave (property) in a will.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To form a scheme; to lay a plan; to contrive; to consider.

   **Usage:** archaic; intransitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To plan or scheme for; to plot to obtain.

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To imagine; to guess.
