## Verb · Record 1

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To mature and become an adult.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** What do you want to be when you grow up?

   **Example:** Attila grew up a nomad on the plains of Hungary

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To start to develop; to flourish.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Socialism grew up in the industrial cities.

   **Example:** A town grew up at the junction of the two rivers.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To stop acting as or like a child (often used as an imperative interjection).

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** Will you grow up please, and stop making silly faces?

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** age — to mature; upgrow; blossom — to start to develop; prosper — to start to develop

## Interjection · Record 2

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Used to criticize childish behavior, especially that considered unreasonable for one's age.

   **Usage:** imperative

   **Example:** You're giggling because I said "butt"? Grow up, seriously.

   **Example:** Grow up! The Bible is nothing more than a astrotheological allegory for things like the Sun!
