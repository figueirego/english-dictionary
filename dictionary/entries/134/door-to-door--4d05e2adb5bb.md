<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. From one household or business to the next.

   **Usage:** metonymically; not-comparable

   **Example:** He carried the petition door to door, collecting signatures.

   **Example:** He sold magazine subscriptions at inflated prices door to door.
