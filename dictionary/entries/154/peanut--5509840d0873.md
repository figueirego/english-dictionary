**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A legume resembling a nut, the fruit of the plant Arachis hypogaea, native to South America.

   **Example:** He always liked eating peanuts along with tea before biscuits came into his life.

   **Source synonyms:** goober; goober bean; goober pea; monkey nut; pinder
