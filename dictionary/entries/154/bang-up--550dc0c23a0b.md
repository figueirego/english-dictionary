### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To damage.

   **Usage:** informal; transitive

   **Example:** He banged up his new car last night.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To put someone in prison, or into their individual cell.

   **Usage:** informal; transitive

   **Example:** He was so drunk that the police banged him up for the night.

   **Source synonyms:** dub up

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To inject a recreational drug.

   **Usage:** intransitive; slang
