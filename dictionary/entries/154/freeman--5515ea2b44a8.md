### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A free person
   1. A person who is not a serf or slave.

      **Usage:** historical; usually

      **Source antonyms:** nonfreeman; slave; bondsman; serf

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A free person
   1. A burgher with full freedom of a city, as opposed to nobles, outsiders, bondsmen, and others.

      **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A free person
   1. A person who has received an honorary freedom of a city.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A free person
   1. A person who is a citizen of a free country, as opposed to a subject of a tyranny or totalitarian dictatorship.

      **Usage:** usually

      **Source context (verbatim):** as two words

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A free person
   1. A person who immigrated to Australia freely, as opposed to those transported as convicts, or such a transported convict who has regained his freedom.

      **Usage:** Australia; historical

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A free person
   1. An independent furtaker.

      **Usage:** Canada; US; historical
