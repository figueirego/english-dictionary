### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A diminutive of the female given names Adelaide, Adeline, Adrienne, and Addison, from the Germanic languages or French or transferred from the surname.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A diminutive of the male given name Adam, from Hebrew.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A surname originating as a patronymic, derived from the given name Adam.
