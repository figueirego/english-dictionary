## Adverb

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈhɛvɪli/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Forms

- more heavily — comparative
- most heavily — superlative
- heauily — alternative; obsolete

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. With a great weight.

   **Example:** heavily burdened

   **Example:** She fell heavily into bed.

   **Example:** He clomped heavily up the stairs in his boots.

   **Example:** The great clod trod heavily on my toes!

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To a considerable degree, to a great extent.

   **Example:** He relied heavily on the data collected by the others.

   **Example:** Your advice still weighs heavily with me.

   **Example:** This problem weighs heavily on my mind.

   **Example:** He drank heavily.

   **Example:** heavily in debt; heavily tattooed

   **Example:** heavily pregnant

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. In a manner designed for heavy duty.

   **Example:** heavily armed soldiers; heavily armoured tanks; heavily reinforced walls

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. So as to be thick or heavy.

   **Example:** heavily built young men; his heavily muscled arms

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. In a laboured manner.

   **Example:** he breathed heavily

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Sadly.

   **Example:** "It was all my fault," he replied heavily.
