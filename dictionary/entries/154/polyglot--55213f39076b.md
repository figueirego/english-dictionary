### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Of a person&#58; speaking, or versed in, many languages; multilingual.

   **Source synonyms:** polyglotted (archaic); polyglottic; polylingual

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Containing, or made up of, several languages; specifically, of a book (especially a bible)&#58; having text translated into several languages.

   **Example:** a polyglot bible a polyglot lexicon

   **Source synonyms:** polyglossic; polyglottal; polyglottic; polyglottish (rare)

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. Comprising various (native) linguistic groups; multilingual.

   **Example:** A polyglot region without a clearly dominant culture may develop an artificial lingua franca, such as Pidgin English in the South Sea.
