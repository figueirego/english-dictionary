### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A package wrapped, as for shipment or transport.

   **Example:** I saw a brown paper parcel on my doorstep.

   **Source synonyms:** package

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An individual consignment of cargo for shipment, regardless of size and form.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An individual item appearing on an invoice or receipt (only in the phrase bill of parcels).

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A division of land bought and sold as a unit.

   **Example:** I own a small parcel of land between the refinery and the fish cannery.

   **Source synonyms:** plot

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A group of birds.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. An indiscriminate or indefinite number, measure, or quantity; a collection; a group.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A small amount of food that has been wrapped up, for example a pastry.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A portion of anything taken separately; a fragment of a whole; a part.

   **Example:** A certain piece of land is part and parcel of another piece.
