## Noun

### Forms

- moments of silence — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A period of silence for quiet meditation, prayer or reflection.

   **Example:** The pastor always opened his Sunday service with a moment of silence.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A period of silence conducted to honor or provide a gesture of respect to a recently deceased person, as part of the mourning process. May also be conducted following a tragic event or on the anniversary thereof.

   **Example:** The principal requested a moment of silence in memory of the two girls who died in a car accident over the weekend.
