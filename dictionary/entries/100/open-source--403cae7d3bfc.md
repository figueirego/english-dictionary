### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of or relating to software where the source code is freely available and licensed in a manner that permits modification and redistribution.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Linux is an open-source operating system.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences; software

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of or relating to digital resources made publicly available alongside their source files to enable their modification and redistribution.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** open-source hardware (with editable CAD files)

   **Example:** open-source fonts

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Describing any product whose composition or method of manufacturing is public knowledge and not proprietary.

   **Usage:** broadly; not-comparable

   **Example:** open-source hardware (schematics provided for free)

   **Example:** open-source cola

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Relying on publicly available information (open sources).

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** open-source intelligence

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** espionage; government; military; politics; war
