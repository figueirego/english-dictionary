### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The quality of agreeing or corresponding; being suitable and appropriate.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A relation between two numbers indicating they give the same remainder when divided by some given number.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; number-theory; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The quality of being isometric — roughly, the same measure and shape.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Matrix similarity by an orthogonal matrix.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** linear-algebra; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; universal

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** algebra; mathematics; sciences
