## Noun · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈstɜːlɪŋ/ — source labels: Received-Pronunciation
- IPA: /ˈstɜɹlɪŋ/ — source labels: General-American
- IPA: /ˈstɛɾlɪŋ/ — source labels: Scotland

### Forms

- sterlings — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An English silver penny first introduced by the Normans.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete except historical

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A penny issued in other countries, such as Scotland.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The currency of the United Kingdom, also called the pound sterling

   **Usage:** broadly; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Short for sterling silver (“an alloy containing not less than 92.5 percent silver, the remainder usually being copper; articles made from this alloy collectively”).

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; broadly; uncountable

   **Alternative form of:** [sterling silver](../07a/sterling-silver--1ebcadb8b5e9.md)

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Former British gold or silver coinage of a standard fineness (0.91666 for gold and 0.925 for silver).

   **Usage:** broadly; historical; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Synonym of pennyweight (“a unit of mass equal to 24 grains, or ¹⁄₂₀ of a troy ounce”)

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; obsolete

   **Source synonyms:** pennyweight (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Money generally.

   **Usage:** broadly; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. The standard degree of fineness.

   **Usage:** broadly; obsolete; uncountable
