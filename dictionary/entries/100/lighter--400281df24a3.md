### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who lights (“sets fire to or ignites”) a thing.

   **Example:** a lighter of lamps

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An electrical or mechanical device, usually handheld and reusable, used to light things; especially, a device which creates a small flame to light cigarettes, stoves, etc.

   **Example:** Cigarette in mouth, he clutched his pockets in search of a lighter.

   **Source synonyms:** briquet (dated)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A piece of flammable material which is set on fire and used to light something else; especially, a piece of folded or twisted paper used in this way to light a pipe.

   **Usage:** archaic
