### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A grouping of genetically related organisms that may or may not be morphologically similar and are recognized by systematists as being in different species or subspecies and may even belong to different genera.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; genetics; medicine; natural-sciences; sciences
