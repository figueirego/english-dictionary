### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of, pertaining to, or resembling the material coral.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
