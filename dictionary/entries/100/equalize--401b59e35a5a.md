**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make equal; to cause to correspond in amount or degree.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to equalize accounts, burdens, or taxes

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To be equal to; to equal, to rival.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

3. To make the scoreline equal by scoring points.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports
