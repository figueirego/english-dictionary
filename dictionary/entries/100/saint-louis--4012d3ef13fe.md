### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A city in eastern Missouri, United States, near the confluence of the Mississippi and Missouri rivers and formerly important in the westward expansion of the United States.

   **Source synonyms:** Gateway to the West; Mound City; Rome of the West

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A river in northeastern Minnesota, United States.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Alternative form of Saint-Louis (“any of various places in French-speaking areas including France, Canada, Guadeloupe, New Caledonia, Réunion, and Senegal”).

   **Usage:** alt-of; alternative
