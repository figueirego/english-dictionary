### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Willing to respond to persuasion or suggestions.

   **Source synonyms:** persuadable; agreeable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Willing to comply; easily led.

   **Source synonyms:** compliant; complaisant; amenable; biddable; complaisant; compliant; controllable; docile; governable; indulgent; manageable; maniable (obsolete); meek; passive; pliant; rulable; tractable; yielding

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Liable to be brought to account, to a charge or claim; responsible; accountable; answerable.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Liable to the legal authority of (something).

   **Example:** Decisions of the Boards of Appeal are amenable to actions before the Court of Justice of the European Communities.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Being a locally compact topological group carrying a kind of averaging operation on bounded functions that is invariant under translation by group elements.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a group
