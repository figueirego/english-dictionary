### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Not intended seriously; jocular or humorous.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** He gave a tongue-in-cheek explanation of why the sky was blue, offering a theory about some primordial discount on light blue paint.

   **Source synonyms:** witty; amusing; bright; diverting; droll; entertaining; humorous; jocose; jocular; piquant; quick; quick on the draw; quick-witted; sharp; tongue-in-cheek
