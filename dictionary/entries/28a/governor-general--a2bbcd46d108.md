### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An official appointed by the reigning British monarch to govern a Commonwealth realm as the monarch's representative.

   **Usage:** UK

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An official in a similar position in other countries.

   **Example:** Nikolai Bobrikov served as governor-general of the Grand Duchy of Finland from 1898 until his untimely death in 1904.
