### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A dogmatic decree or command, especially issued by one who rules without popular consent.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** ukase

<a id="r1-s2"></a>
