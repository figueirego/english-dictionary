### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A major subprovincial city, the provincial capital of Jiangsu, China, on the lower Yangtze; a former capital of China.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Synonym of Jiangnan, Nanzhili, Liangjiang, etc. as imperial Chinese provinces or viceroyalties administered from Nanjing.

   **Usage:** historical; informal

   **Source synonyms:** Jiangnan (synonym; synonym-of); Nanzhili (synonym; synonym-of); Liangjiang (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Various other Chinese cities during periods when they acted as a southern capital of a kingdom or imperial dynasty.

   **Usage:** historical

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** Jiangning (historical) — capital of Jiangsu; Tianjing — capital of Taiping Heavenly Kingdom; T'ien-ching (historical) — capital of Taiping Heavenly Kingdom
