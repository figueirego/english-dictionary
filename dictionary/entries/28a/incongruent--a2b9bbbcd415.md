**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Out of place, incompatible, inharmonious, not congruent.

   **Usage:** figuratively; higher register

   **Example:** The yellow blazer was incongruent for the funeral.

   **Example:** Her knowledge of world politics was incongruent in someone so young.

   **Source synonyms:** incongruous; absurd; inappropriate
