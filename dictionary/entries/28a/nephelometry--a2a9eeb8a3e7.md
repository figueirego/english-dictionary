## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The measurement of the concentration of a solution, suspension or dispersion based upon its light-scattering properties.

   **Usage:** uncountable; analytical chemistry

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A technique used to determine the levels of antibodies or antigens in a suspension, based on its light-scattering properties.

   **Usage:** uncountable
