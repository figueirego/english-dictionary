**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any unit of (originally cultural) information, such as a practice or idea, that is transmitted verbally or by repeated action from one mind to another in a comparable way to the transmission of genes.

   **Usage:** originally

   **Source synonyms:** culturgen

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Media, usually humorous, which is copied and circulated online with slight adaptations, such as basic pictures, video templates, etc.

   **Usage:** Internet

   **Example:** He posted a meme that went viral within a few hours on social media.

   **Example:** He stuck memes on the wall to campaign for the position of President.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A specific instance of a meme, such as an image macro or a video, often with humorous superimposed text.

   **Usage:** Internet

   **Example:** This meme generator lets you make your own memes by adding a caption to existing images, or by uploading your own image.

   **Example:** I'm always posting memes on the groupchat.

**Source sense R1.S4**

   **Usage:** Internet; derogatory
