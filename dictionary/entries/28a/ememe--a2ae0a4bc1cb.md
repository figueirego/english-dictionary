### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any emotional message implied or inferred (metamessage) contained within communication, which is commonly recognised within a specific group of people or culture.

   **Example:** An ememe can be any language which can be culturally used to convey an emotion. Even 'Ok'or 'How are you' can be examples of ememes.
