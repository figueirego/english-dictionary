### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A UK army rank with NATO code OR-6, senior to corporal and junior to warrant officer ranks.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war

   **Source synonyms:** sarge; sarnt

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The highest rank of noncommissioned officer in some non-naval military forces and police.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A lawyer of the highest rank, equivalent to the doctor of civil law.

   **Usage:** historical

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A title sometimes given to the servants of the sovereign.

   **Usage:** UK; historical

   **Example:** sergeant surgeon, i.e. a servant, or attendant, surgeon

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A bailiff.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**
