### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of various types of footwear with thick soles, often used to elevate the foot, especially wooden clogs.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

   **Usage:** obsolete

   **Usage:** UK; dialectal
