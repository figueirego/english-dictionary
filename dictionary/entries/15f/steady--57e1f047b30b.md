### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Firm in standing or position; not tottering or shaking; fixed; firm.

   **Example:** Hold the ladder steady while I go up.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Constant in feeling, purpose, or pursuit; not fickle, changeable, or wavering; not easily moved or persuaded to alter a purpose; resolute.

   **Example:** a man steady in his principles, in his purpose, or in the pursuit of an object

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Smooth and not bumpy or with obstructions.

   **Example:** a steady ride

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Phlegmatic, calm; not easily upset, excited, or disturbed.

   **Example:** a steady tone of voice

   **Source synonyms:** even-tempered

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Regular and even.

   **Example:** the steady course of the Sun; a steady breeze of wind

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Slow.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. With whom one is going steady.

   **Usage:** dated; informal

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Confident and cool-headed under pressure; competent; reliable, adept.

   **Usage:** Singapore; Singlish; predicative
