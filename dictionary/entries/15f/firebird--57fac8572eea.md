### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A scarlet tanager (Piranga olivacea). (This species is now classified as belonging to the cardinal family&#58; Cardinalidae)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A Baltimore oriole (Icterus galbula).

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A vermilion flycatcher (Pyrocephalus rubinus).

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A magical and prophetic glowing or burning bird from a faraway land, which is both a blessing and a harbinger of doom to its captor
