## Adjective · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of a body of water, completely still with no waves and no wind.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** We were drifting on a dead calm sea for a third week in a row.

## Noun · Record 2

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Dead calm prevails over the Atlantic.
