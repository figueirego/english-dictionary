### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Opposition or repugnance of mind; fixed dislike often without any conscious reasoning.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Due to her aversion to the outdoors she complained throughout the entire camping trip.

   **Example:** Live with aversion to classic men's wear, likely die with aversion to classic men's wear.

   **Source synonyms:** antipathy; disinclination; reluctance

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An object of dislike or repugnance.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Pushy salespeople are a major aversion of mine.

   **Source synonyms:** abomination

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The act of turning away from an object.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable
