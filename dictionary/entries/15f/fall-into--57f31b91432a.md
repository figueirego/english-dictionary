### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To go into something by falling.

   **Usage:** literally; transitive

   **Example:** The cat might fall into the pool if you're not careful.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To visit (a place or a person).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I hope to see her whenever I next fall into the area.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To enter a condition or state, especially a negative one.

   **Example:** After it closed, the old mall fell into disrepair.

   **Example:** Our senator fell into disrepute because of the banking scandal.

   **Example:** Since her husband died, the patient has fallen into a deep depression.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To enter something (especially a profession) without having planned it.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** Most of us didn't plan to be designers; we just fell into the job.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To be classified as; to fall under.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** Trees fall into two main categories&#58; deciduous and evergreen.
