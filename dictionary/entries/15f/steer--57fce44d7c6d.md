### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To guide the course of a vessel, vehicle, aircraft etc. (by means of a device such as a rudder, paddle, or steering wheel).

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The boat steered towards the iceberg.

   **Example:** I steered homeward.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To guide the course of a vessel, vehicle, aircraft etc. (by means of a device such as a rudder, paddle, or steering wheel).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I find it very difficult to steer a skateboard.

   **Example:** When planning the boat trip, we had completely forgotten that we needed somebody to steer.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To be directed and governed; to take a direction, or course; to obey the helm.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The boat steers easily.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To direct a group of animals.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To maneuver or manipulate a person or group into a place or course of action.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Hume believes that principles of association steer the imagination of artists.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To conduct oneself; to take or pursue a course of action.

   **Usage:** reflexive

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To direct a conversation.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To direct or send an object into a specific place

   **Usage:** transitive
