### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To break from internal pressure.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** I blew the balloon up too much, and it burst.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To cause to break from internal pressure.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I burst the balloon when I blew it up too much.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To cause to break by any means.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To separate (printer paper) at perforation lines.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I printed the report on form-feed paper, then burst the sheets.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To enter or exit hurriedly and unexpectedly.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To erupt; to change state suddenly as if bursting.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The flowers burst into bloom on the first day of spring.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To produce as an effect of bursting.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to burst a hole through the wall

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To interrupt suddenly in a violent or explosive manner; to shatter.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. Of neurons&#58; to exhibit an activation pattern where periods of rapid action potential spiking are followed by quiescent periods much longer than typical interspike intervals.

   **Usage:** intransitive
