### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Not connected, coherent, or continuous.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** The hours of his illness were disjointed and confusing as he drifted in and out of consciousness.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Having the paws and head (but not the tail) detached from the body.

   **Example:** a lion disjointed
