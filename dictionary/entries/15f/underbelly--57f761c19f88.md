### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The underside of an animal.

   **Example:** We identified the species of the snake by its blue-grey underbelly.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The underside of any thing.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The side which is not normally seen, normally a dark, immoral place.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** The area by the canal is the underbelly of town – not something you’d see in the guidebooks.
