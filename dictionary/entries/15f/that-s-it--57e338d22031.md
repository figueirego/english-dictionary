### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. That's all (there is nothing more to the issue).

   **Example:** So, that's it? I thought there would be some more.

   **Example:** I'm not going, and that's it.

   **Source synonyms:** is all; that's all; that's your lot

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. I have had it (an exasperated refusal to tolerate the situation further).

   **Source synonyms:** that does it; enough is enough

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Yes!, exactly!, bingo!
