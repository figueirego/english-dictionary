### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To engage in sodomy with (someone); to engage in anal (or, rarely, oral) sex as the penetrator (especially without consent).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** bugger

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To engage in sexual intercourse with (an animal), to engage in bestiality.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To commit sodomy; to engage in anal sex.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To cause great humiliation or harm to (someone or something); to cause great damage to (something, especially from behind).

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To cause (a community) to resemble the proverbially sinful biblical city of Sodom.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To cause to be swallowed up or buried (like the biblical city of Sodom, as a punishment).

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive
