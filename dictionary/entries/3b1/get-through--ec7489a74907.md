### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To overcome; to endure.

   **Usage:** informal; transitive

   **Example:** Military training was hard, but I got through it.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To complete; to finish.

   **Usage:** informal; transitive

   **Example:** She got through her book this morning!

   **Example:** I was so thirsty that I got through three glasses of orange juice.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To use up; to expend.

   **Usage:** informal; transitive

   **Example:** With near-daily trips to Houston and back, he really gets through a lot of fuel in one month.

   **Source synonyms:** burn through; run through

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To reach its destination.

   **Usage:** informal; intransitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To communicate successfully across a barrier; to make contact, or get one's point across; to reach the addressee, possibly with difficulty.

   **Usage:** informal; intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** He was such a dyed-in-the-wool partisan that I just couldn't get through to him and change his mind.

   **Example:** He was such a dyed-in-the-wool partisan that I just couldn't get it through his skull.

   **Source synonyms:** patch; [get across](../2ac/get-across--ab2f4c6b74c1.md)
