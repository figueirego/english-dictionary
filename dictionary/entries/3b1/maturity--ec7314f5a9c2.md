### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The state of being mature, ready or ripe; the prime state of productibility and self expression.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Some foods and drinks, like wine, only reach their full taste at maturity, which literally comes at a price.

   **Example:** The ability to take responsibility is a sign of maturity.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. When bodily growth has completed and/or reproduction can begin.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The entire tank of guppies was in their maturity and ready to mate.

   **Example:** Some insect species reach sexual maturity well before their own bodily maturity

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The state of a debt obligation at the end of the term of maturation thereof, once all interest and any applicable fees have accrued to the principal.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance; insurance

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Date when payment is due.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** The note was cashed at maturity.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance; insurance
