### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Well-grounded or justifiable, pertinent.

   **Example:** I will believe him as soon as he offers a valid answer.

   **Source synonyms:** arguable; groundly; proper; warrantable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Acceptable, proper or correct; in accordance with the rules.

   **Example:** A valid format for the date is DD/MM/YY.

   **Example:** Do not drive without a valid license.

   **Source synonyms:** legit; legitimate

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Related to the current topic, or presented within context, relevant.

   **Source synonyms:** applicable; germane; applicable; apposite; apt; ad rem; well-placed; felicitous; germane; in point; material; on point; on-topic; pertinent; relevant; salient; to the point (idiomatic); valid

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Of a formula or system&#58; such that it evaluates to true regardless of the input values.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; logic; mathematics; philosophy; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Of an argument&#58; whose conclusion is always true whenever its premises are true.

   **Example:** An argument is valid if and only if the set consisting of both (1) all of its premises and (2) the contradictory of its conclusion is inconsistent.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; logic; mathematics; philosophy; sciences
