2. Abstract algebra&#58; A broad field of study in modern mathematics (often mentioned alongside analysis) loosely characterized by its concern for abstraction and symmetry, dealing with the behavior, classification, and application of a large class of objects (called algebraic structures) and the maps between them (called, most generally, morphisms).

   **Usage:** capitalized; sometimes; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

   1. A universal algebra.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** algebra; mathematics; sciences

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any of several objects of study in Algebra
   1. An algebraic structure consisting of a module over a commutative ring (or a vector space over a field) along with an additional binary operation that is bilinear over module (or vector) addition and scalar multiplication.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** algebra; mathematics; sciences

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematical-analysis; mathematics; sciences; set-theory

   **Source synonyms:** field of sets; algebra of sets
