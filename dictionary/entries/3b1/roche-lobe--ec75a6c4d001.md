### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The teardrop-shaped region around a star in a binary system within which orbiting material is gravitationally bound to that star.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astronomy; natural-sciences
