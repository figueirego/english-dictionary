### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of a diet, involving eating a lower number of calories than would normally eaten, usually with the intention of losing weight.

   **Source synonyms:** calorie-restricted; restricted

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of a food or drink, having fewer calories than its standard form.
