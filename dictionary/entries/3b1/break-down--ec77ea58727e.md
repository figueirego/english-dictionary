### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To stop functioning.

   **Usage:** intransitive; usually; vehicle

   **Example:** I am afraid my computer will break down if I try to run it at too high a speed.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computer; computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a machine

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To unexpectedly collapse, physically or in structure.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To intentionally demolish; to pull down.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To fail, especially socially or for political reasons.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Talks broke down between Prime Minister John Doe and the opposition party.

   **Example:** Relations broke down between Greece and Turkey.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To give in or give up&#58; relent, concede, surrender.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** Is it worth taking it to a repair shop, or should I just break down and buy a new one?

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To render or to become unstable due to stress, to collapse physically or mentally.

   **Usage:** ergative; figuratively

   **Example:** She is back to work now, after she broke down the other day.

   **Source synonyms:** come undone

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To render or to become weak and ineffective.

   **Usage:** ergative; figuratively

   **Example:** His authority and influence over his coordinates broke down gradually.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To (cause to) decay, to decompose.

   **Usage:** ergative

   **Example:** Leaves and grass will break down into compost faster if you keep them moist.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To separate into a number of parts.

   **Source synonyms:** break apart; [break up](../045/break-up--115648e47de1.md); take apart; tear down; tear up

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. To divide into parts to give more details, to provide a more indepth analysis of.

   **Usage:** ergative; figuratively

   **Example:** If you don't understand, ask him to break down the numbers for you.

   **Source synonyms:** analyze

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. To digest.

   **Usage:** ergative

   **Example:** His stomach took a while to break down his food.

   **Example:** His food took a while to break down in his stomach.

   **Source synonyms:** catabolize

<a id="r1-s12"></a>

**Source sense R1.S12**

12. To bust down or bust a move; to perform energetic, often freestyle or hip-hop moves, frequently during a song’s instrumental break where only drums or bass are playing; see breakdown.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music
