## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The time, place and circumstance in which something (such as a story or picture) is set; context; scenario.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The act of setting (various senses).

   **Example:** the setting of the sun

   **Example:** the setting, or hardening, of moist plaster of Paris

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A piece of metal in which a precious stone or gem is fixed to form a piece of jewelry.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A level or placement to which a knob or control is set.

   **Example:** the volume setting on a television

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The act of marking the position of game, as a setter does.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Hunting with a setter.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Something set in, or inserted.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A piece of vocal or choral music composed for particular words (set to music).

   **Example:** Schubert's setting of Goethe's poem

   **Example:** Bach's setting of the Magnificat

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. The mounting of a play, etc., for the stage.

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. The direction of a current of wind.
