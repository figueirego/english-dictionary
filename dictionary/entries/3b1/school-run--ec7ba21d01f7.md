### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A period of increased traffic where parents or carers take or collect children to and from school by car; a journey for this purpose during this period.

   **Usage:** UK

   **Example:** The roads are crammed with parents on the school run.

   **Example:** Would you mind doing the school run for James this morning?
