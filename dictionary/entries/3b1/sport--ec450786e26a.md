### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any activity that uses physical exertion or skills competitively under a set of rules that is not based on aesthetics.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Basketball is her favorite sport, and she also enjoys various other sports as well.

   **Example:** Sport can be an excellent form of exercise because the competition and sense of achievement can help with both motivation and pleasure.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person who exhibits either good or bad sportsmanship.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** Jen may have won, but she was sure a poor sport; she laughed at the loser.

   **Example:** The loser was a good sport, and congratulated Jen on her performance.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Somebody who behaves or reacts in an admirably good-natured manner, e.g. to being teased or to losing a game; a good sport.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** You're such a sport! You never get upset when we tease you.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Something fun, pastime; amusement.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; uncountable

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A toy; a plaything; an object of mockery.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Gaming for money as in racing, hunting, or fishing.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To amuse oneself, to play.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** children sporting on the green

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To mock or tease, treat lightly, toy with.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Jen sports with Bill's emotions.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To display; to have as a notable feature.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Jen's sporting a new pair of shoes; he was sporting a new wound from the combat

   **Source synonyms:** rock; wear

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To divert; to amuse; to make merry.

   **Usage:** reflexive

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To represent by any kind of play.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To practise the diversions of the field or the turf; to be given to betting, as upon races.

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

**Source sense R2.S7**

7. To assume suddenly a new and different character from the rest of the plant or from the type of the species; said of a bud, shoot, plant, or animal.

<a id="r2-s8"></a>

**Source sense R2.S8**

8. To close (a door).

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive
