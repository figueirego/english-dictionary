<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To talk nonsense or to lie.

   **Usage:** Commonwealth; Ireland; UK; slang; vulgar

   **Example:** I shagged David Beckham last week. – Stop chatting shit; that's a load of bollocks.

   **Source synonyms:** chat shite; talk shit; talk shite; talk trash

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To have a casual or meaningless conversation.

   **Usage:** Commonwealth; Ireland; UK; slang; vulgar

   **Example:** My brother came round and we were chatting shit for hours.

   **Source synonyms:** shoot the breeze; shoot the shit
