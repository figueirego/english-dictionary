### Senses

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Said of an air-breathing aquatic animal such as a porpoise or penguin&#58; To repeatedly jump out of the water to take a breath and dive back in a continuous motion.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**
