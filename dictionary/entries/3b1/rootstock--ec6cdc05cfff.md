### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A healthy and vigorous-rooted plant that is used in grafting, most commonly as a sound base to support a scion that bears desirable fruit in orchard culture.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** agriculture; business; lifestyle

   **Source synonyms:** rootstalk
