### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To creep out; to give a creepy or sketchy feeling to someone.

   **Usage:** Canada; US; informal; transitive

   **Example:** The awkward and agitated customer began to sketch them out a bit.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see sketch, out.

   **Example:** I sketched out the trees and the lake before adding texture and colour to the scene.
