## Verb

### Forms

- clears out — present; singular; third-person
- clearing out — participle; present
- cleared out — participle; past
- cleared out — past

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To completely empty.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** We had to clear out the attic so the guest could sleep there.

   **Example:** Clear out your e-mail inbox to make more space.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To rob or steal from (someone).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Kelly disappeared last night, and she cleared me out.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To be victorious in gambling against (someone); to financially ruin (someone).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I don't have any money, Betty cleared me out.

   **Source synonyms:** clean out

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. to remove or eject (from), especially forcibly.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The police cleared the crowd out of the street to investigate the accident.

   **Example:** We need to get the trees cleared out the way before anything can drive down this path.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To leave quickly.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** They cleared out as soon as the cops arrived.

   **Source synonyms:** clear away; clear off; cheese it

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To become empty.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The town is packed in the summer, but clears out in the winter.

   **Source synonyms:** empty out
