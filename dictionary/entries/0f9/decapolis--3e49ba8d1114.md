### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A group of ten cities on the eastern frontier of the Roman Empire located in modern-day Israel, Jordan and Syria.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An alliance of ten free imperial cities in Alsace within the Holy Roman Empire (now in Grand Est, France) from 1354 to 1678.
