### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Applied to various creatures characterized by large teeth.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** the bigtooth cardinalfish

   **Example:** the bigtooth river stingray

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Applied to certain plants whose leaves have large points.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** the bigtooth aspen
