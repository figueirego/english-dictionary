### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Obstinate; strong-minded; unyielding to the desires, concern or opinions of others.

   **Example:** He was so self-willed that he refused to do anything that did not grant instant satisfaction or direction.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Possessing self-will; determined to pursue one's own desires or adhere to one's own opinion..

   **Example:** The idea of self-willed machines, which could decide to take over the world, scares all the people smart enough to create such devices.
