**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To emerge again; to come into view after having hidden.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To come out of a situation, object or a liquid after having entered it.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Soon after it submerged, the submarine reemerged from the ocean.
