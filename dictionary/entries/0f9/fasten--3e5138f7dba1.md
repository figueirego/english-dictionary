### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To attach or connect in a secure manner.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** The sailor fastened the boat to the dock with a half-hitch.

   **Example:** Fasten your seat belts!

   **Example:** Can you fasten these boards together with some nails?

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To cause to take close effect; to make to tell; to land.

   **Example:** to fasten a blow
