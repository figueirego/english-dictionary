### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To cause (liquid, or liquid-like substance) to flow in a stream, either out of a container or into it.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** pour water from a jug

   **Example:** pour wine into a decanter

   **Example:** to pour oil onto chips

   **Example:** to pour out sand or dust.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To send out as in a stream or a flood; to cause (an emotion) to come out; to cause to escape.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** My teacher poured scorn on my attempts at writing.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To send forth from, as in a stream; to discharge uninterruptedly.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To flow, pass, or issue in a stream; to fall continuously and abundantly.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** the rain poured down.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To rain hard.

   **Usage:** impersonal

   **Example:** It's pouring outside.

   **Source synonyms:** spate

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Of a beverage, to be on tap or otherwise available for serving to customers.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To move in a throng, as a crowd.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The people poured out of the theater.

   **Source synonyms:** sweep; crowd; throng

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To move (a drunk or unsteady person) into or out of a place or vehicle.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** She poured him into a taxi and handed the driver the fare.
