### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A warm cloth cap worn while sleeping, often with pajamas, being common attire in northern Europe before effective home heating became widespread.

   **Example:** Winston wore a nightcap to stave off the cold.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; clothing; fashion; lifestyle; manufacturing; textiles

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A beverage drunk before bed that is usually alcoholic.

   **Example:** I'll make myself a nightcap of whisky and lemon before heading to bed.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Something that a person reads or listens to before bed.

   **Usage:** broadly; figuratively

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The final match of a sporting contest, especially the second game of a baseball doubleheader.

   **Usage:** US

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; baseball; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A cap drawn over the face of the condemned person before they are hanged.
