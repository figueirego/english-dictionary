### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The sound of metal or glass clattering against itself.

   **Example:** He heard the jingle of her keys in the door and turned off the screen.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A small piece of metal attached to a musical instrument, such as a tambourine, so as to make a jangling sound when the instrument is played.

   **Example:** Her tambourine didn't come with any jingles attached.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A memorable short song or verse, or in some cases a snippet of a popular song with its lyrics modified, often used for the purposes of advertising a product or service in a TV or radio commercial.

   **Example:** That used-car dealership's jingle has been stuck in my head since we heard that song.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** advertising; broadcasting; business; entertainment; lifestyle; marketing; media; music

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A carriage drawn by horses.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A brief telephone call.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Example:** Give me a jingle when you find out something.

   **Source synonyms:** bell; ring; tinkle

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A jingle shell.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Coin money.

   **Usage:** slang; uncountable

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

### Senses

**Source sense R2.S1**

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The beads jingled as she walked.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

2. To cause to make a noise of metal or glass clattering against itself.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** She jingled the beads as she walked.

   **Usage:** dated; intransitive
