### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. That which stops up or fills a gap or hole.

   **Usage:** rare

   **Source synonyms:** fillgap

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Something spoken to fill up an uncomfortable pause in speech; a filled pause or filler.

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A short-term fix or temporary measure used until something better can be obtained; that which serves as an expedient in an emergency; a band-aid solution.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** The small company used their old product with a few kludged enhancements as a stopgap until they could develop a new product.
