### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To vanish.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source synonyms:** dematerialize; vanish; perish; dissolve; perish; walk (informal)

   **Source antonyms:** appear

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To go missing; to become a missing person.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Eighteen years after Jaycee Dugard disappeared in 1991, she was found alive in the summer of 2009.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To go away; to become lost.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To make vanish; especially, to abduct or murder for political reasons.

   **Usage:** euphemistic; often; transitive

   **Example:** The Chinese government is well-known for disappearing particularly vocal political dissidents.
