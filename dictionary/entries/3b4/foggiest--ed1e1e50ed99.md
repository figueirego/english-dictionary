### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Usage:** form-of; superlative

   **Example:** Moscow is the foggiest city I've ever been to.

2. Slightest, faintest, least.

   **Example:** I don't have the foggiest idea how to get to Piccadilly Circus on the Tube.

   **Example:** She wasn't going to get the foggiest notion about modern writing from those old books.
