### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To interrupt or curtail before the planned end time.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The party was cut short because everything was getting broken.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To stop someone from finishing what they are saying.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He cut her short because he knew what she was going to say.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see cut, short.
