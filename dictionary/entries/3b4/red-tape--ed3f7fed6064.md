## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The binding tape once used for holding important documents together.

   **Usage:** uncountable

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Time-consuming regulations or bureaucratic procedures.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; metonymically; uncountable
