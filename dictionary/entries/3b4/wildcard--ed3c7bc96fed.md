### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A character that takes the place of any other character or string that is not known or specified.

   **Example:** If the character \* is acting as a wildcard, then the pattern a\*m matches each of the words amalgam, atom and alum.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences
