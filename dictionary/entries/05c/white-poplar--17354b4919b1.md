### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any tree of several species of genus Populus of poplars&#58;
   1. any tree of species Populus alba, a poplar found in Central Europe, Morocco and central Asia.

      **Source synonyms:** abele; silver poplar

**Source sense R1.S2**
