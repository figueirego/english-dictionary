### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To bring upon oneself or expose oneself to, especially something inconvenient, harmful, or onerous; to become liable or subject to.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Cruelty incurs calamity.

   **Example:** I've incurred scorn and odium for my actions.

   **Source synonyms:** contract
