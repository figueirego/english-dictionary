### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A distortion of sensory perception where real stimuli lead to a false or misleading impression of reality.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** We saw what looked like a tiger among the trees, but it was an illusion caused by the shadows of the branches.

   **Example:** Using artificial additives, scientists can create the illusion of fruit flavours in food.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; psychology; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A misapprehension; a belief in something that is in fact not true.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** Jane has this illusion that John is in love with her.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A magician’s trick.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The state of being deceived or misled.

   **Usage:** uncountable
