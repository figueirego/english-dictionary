## Verb

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To cause to stick to a surface.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I can't stick this picture down. I need better glue.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. to stick to a surface.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Eventually the plaster stuck down on my arm.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To write something casually.

   **Example:** If you don't know the answer, just stick down any old thing&#58; you might get a mark for it.
