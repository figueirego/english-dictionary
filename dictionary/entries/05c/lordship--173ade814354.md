### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The state or condition of being a lord.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Title applied to a lord, bishop, judge, or another man with a title.

   **Usage:** broadly; capitalized; countable; often; uncountable

   **Example:** May I ask that the order be granted, if your lordship so pleases?

   **Source context (verbatim):** with "his" or "your"

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A man (sometimes a boy) who is behaving in a seigneurial manner or acting like a lord, behaving in a bossy manner or lording it up

   **Usage:** countable; humorous; uncountable

   **Source context (verbatim):** with "his" or "your"

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Seigniory; domain; the territory over which a lord holds jurisdiction; a manor.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Dominion; power; authority.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
