## Noun

### Forms

- hatchet jobs — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A journalistic or other treatment which portrays its subject in a very unfavorable manner; a work of criticism which aims to destroy a reputation.

   **Example:** That article about the mayor is nothing more than a hatchet job.

   **Source synonyms:** slam piece; hit piece; traducement; calumny; calumniation; obloquy; defamation

   **Source antonyms:** puff piece

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An action or a program which imposes deep reductions in expenditure.

   **Example:** This so-called reform of our wage agreement was really just one big hatchet job!
