### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To collapse inward; to crumble.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The plant shrivelled from lack of water.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To become wrinkled.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** His fingers were shriveled from being in the bath for too long.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To draw into wrinkles.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The hot sun shrivelled the leaves.
