### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A gentile who performs certain acts which are forbidden to observant Jews on Shabbat, such as kindling fire.

   **Example:** Sometimes the family was helped on the Sabbath by a shabbos goy who would light some candles in their house.

   **Source synonyms:** Sabbath Gentile; Sabbath goy

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A gentile who holds philo-Semitic views, and is perceived as being subservient to Jewish people.

   **Usage:** derogatory; offensive; white supremacy
