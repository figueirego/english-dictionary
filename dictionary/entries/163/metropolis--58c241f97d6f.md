### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The mother (founding) polis (city state) of a colony.

   **Usage:** especially Ancient Greece

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** history; human-sciences; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** mother city; metropole

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A large, busy city, especially as the main city in an area or country or as distinguished from surrounding rural areas.

   **Source synonyms:** metropolitan city

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The see of a metropolitan bishop, ranking above its suffragan diocesan bishops.

   **Usage:** Orthodox Christianity

   **Source synonyms:** archbishopric

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A generic focus in the distribution of plants or animals.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; ecology; natural-sciences
