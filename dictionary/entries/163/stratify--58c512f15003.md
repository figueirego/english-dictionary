### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To become separated out into distinct layers or strata.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Even without a pronounced social class system, people in a large society tend to stratify.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To separate out into distinct layers or strata.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** In this cut you can see how the sedimentary rock layers have been clearly stratified.
