### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A strong, unpleasant emotion or feeling caused by actual or perceived danger or threat.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** He was struck by fear on seeing the snake.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A phobia, a sense of fear induced by something or someone in particular.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** Not everybody has the same fears.

   **Example:** I have a fear of ants.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Something one is afraid of; the object of one’s fear.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** Spiders are my greatest fear.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Terrified veneration or reverence, particularly towards God, gods, or sovereigns.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** dread

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A feeling of dread and anxiety when waking after drinking a lot of alcohol, wondering what one did while drunk.

   **Usage:** UK; countable; uncountable; with-definite-article; "the fear"

   **Source synonyms:** hangxiety

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** fear; afraidness

**Source antonyms:** courage

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To be afraid of (something or someone); to consider or expect (something or someone) with alarm.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I fear the worst will happen.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To feel fear.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Never fear; help is always near.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To worry about, to feel concern for, to be afraid for \[with for\].

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** She fears for her son’s safety.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To venerate; to feel awe towards.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** People who fear God can be found in Christian churches.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To regret.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I fear I have bad news for you&#58; your husband has died.

6. To cause fear to; to frighten.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive
