### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Free from color; transmitting light without color-related distortion.

   **Example:** achromatic color scheme

   **Example:** achromatic image

   **Example:** The designer chose an achromatic color scheme of black, white, and gray.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; natural-sciences; optics; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Containing components such as achromatic lenses and prisms, designed to prevent color-related distortion.

   **Example:** An achromatic lens reduces color distortion in optical instruments.
