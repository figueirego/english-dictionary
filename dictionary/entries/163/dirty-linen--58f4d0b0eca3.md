## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Matters of an embarrassing or unsavoury nature that one would normally wish to keep private.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** A lot of dirty linen was exposed during the inquiry.

**Source sense R1.S2**
