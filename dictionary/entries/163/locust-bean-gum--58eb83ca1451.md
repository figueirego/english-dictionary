### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A vegetable gum extracted from the seeds of the carob (locust beans) and used as a food additive; chemically, it is a galactomannan.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** carob gum; carubin; tragasol (dated)
