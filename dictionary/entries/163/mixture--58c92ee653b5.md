### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of mixing.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The mixture of sulphuric acid and water produces heat.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Something produced by mixing.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** An alloy is a mixture of two metals.

   **Source synonyms:** amalgam

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Something that consists of diverse elements.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The day was a mixture of sunshine and showers.

   **Source synonyms:** stew

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A medicinal compound, typically a suspension of a solid in a solution

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** A teaspoonful of the mixture to be taken three times daily after meals

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A compound organ stop.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A cloth of variegated colouring.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

   **Usage:** India; Sri-Lanka; countable; uncountable
