## Noun · Record 2

### Forms

- elementoids — plural

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. (historical chemistry) A supposed constituent of a chemical element, differing slightly from related constituents, in William Crookes's theory of meta-elements.

   **Extractor diagnostics (not usage labels):** no-gloss

**Supplemental definition (evidence reviewed; source gap preserved):** Crookes explicitly names his proposed intermediate constituents meta-elements or elementoids in his original 1888 Chemical Society address. This is an exact nominal attestation with its concept explained, rather than a noun inferred from the related adjective. The historical and hypothetical labels distinguish the abandoned theory from present-day chemical classification.

**Definition sources:** [English Wiktionary&#58; elementoid](https://en.wiktionary.org/w/index.php?title=elementoid&oldid=89028285) — 89028285; CC-BY-SA-4.0; [William Crookes, Address of the President, Chemical Society, The Chemical News 57 (1 June 1888), pp.216–217](https://archive.org/details/chemicalnewsjour5758londuoft) — 1888 primary text; OCR captured 2026-10-23; Public-domain-USA historical text (1888)

**Editorial transformation:** Independently worded, narrowly historical English supplement from the original public-domain scientific address. This does not assert that the proposed elemental constituents exist, nor equate the term without qualification with modern isotopes.
