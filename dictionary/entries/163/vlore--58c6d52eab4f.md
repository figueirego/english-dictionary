## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The third largest city in Albania, located on the Bay of Vlorë and the foothills of the Ceraunian Mountains at the Strait of Otranto; it is the seat of its eponymous county, municipality and municipal unit.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A county of Albania.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A municipality in the county of Vlorë, Albania.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A municipal unit of the municipality of Vlorë in the county of Vlorë, Albania.
