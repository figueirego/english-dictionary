### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A mature or elderly woman, especially one of a higher social rank.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A mature or elderly woman, especially one of a higher social rank.
   1. A woman with the character of a mother or matriarch.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A mature or elderly woman, especially one of a higher social rank.
   1. A woman in charge of the domestic arrangements of an establishment or institution, especially, the nursing officer or chief nurse of a hospital.

      **Example:** the matron of a school or hospital

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A wife or a widow, especially, one who has borne children.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A housekeeper, especially, a woman who manages the domestic economy of a public institution.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A female prison officer.

   **Usage:** US
