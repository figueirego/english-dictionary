## Adverb · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To an excessive degree.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; proscribed; sometimes

   **Example:** Parents can be overly protective of their children.

   **Source synonyms:** too; excessively; superfluously

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To a high degree; very.

   **Usage:** negative; not-comparable; usually

   **Example:** I'm not overly enthusiastic about meeting him.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Superficially.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; obsolete

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Carelessly, without due attention.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; obsolete

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. With a sense of superiority, haughtily.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; obsolete
