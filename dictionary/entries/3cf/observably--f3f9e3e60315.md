## Adverb

### Forms

- more observably — comparative
- most observably — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To a detectable degree, sufficient to be observed.

   **Usage:** degree

   **Example:** As the planet orbits the star, it causes a wobble in the star's motion, which can be detected as a small but observably large Doppler effect.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In an observable manner.

   **Example:** From the point of view of earth-based observatories, the effect of the planet's orbit around the star is that the star observably wobbles.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** manner

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Indicates that the proposition can be verified by observation.

   **Usage:** modal

   **Example:** Observably, the star wobbles.
