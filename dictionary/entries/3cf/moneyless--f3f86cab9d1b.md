<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. That does not use money as a means of exchange.

   **Example:** In eras when trade was largely by barter and taxes were usually paid in kind, moneyless transactions could constitute a large percentage of a national economy.

   **Source synonyms:** nonmoney
