### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of several varieties of thin cotton cloth.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually; textile

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Fabric made of cotton, flax (linen), hemp, or silk, finely or coarsely woven.

   **Usage:** US; uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of a wide variety of tightly-woven thin fabrics, especially those used for bedlinen.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Woven cotton or linen fabrics, especially when used for items other than garments.

   **Usage:** US; uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A mock-up of a garment pattern made of inexpensive fabric for the purpose of optimizing its fit on the wearer, before the garment is produced in more expensive fabric.

   **Usage:** countable; usually

   **Example:** Fitting this pattern is tricky, so we recommend you make a muslin first.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Any of several different moths, especially the muslin moth, Diaphora mendica.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Woman as sex object; prostitute, as in a bit of muslin.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually
