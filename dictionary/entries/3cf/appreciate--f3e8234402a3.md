### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To be grateful or thankful for.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I appreciate your efforts.

   **Example:** We sincerely appreciate your help.

   **Example:** Any aid will be warmly appreciated.

   **Example:** I'd appreciate it so much if you'd keep me abreast of developments.

   **Source synonyms:** esteem; acknowledge

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To view as valuable.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** You must learn to appreciate time.

   **Source synonyms:** cherish; esteem; value; appreciate; bedear; cherish; esteem; hold dear; prize; think much of; treasure; value

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To be fully conscious of; understand; be aware of; detect.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** It is essential for the reader to appreciate how important this argument is.

   **Example:** I appreciate that what I'm asking you to do is very difficult.

   **Source synonyms:** realize; grasp; acknowledge

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To increase in value.

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** The value of his portfolio appreciated by 80% over eight years.

   **Source synonyms:** bedear; endear

   **Source antonyms:** depreciate
