### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A large open space for children to play in, usually having dedicated play equipment (such as swings and slides).

   **Usage:** outdoors

   **Example:** The kids have to go to the playground during recess, no matter the weather.

   **Example:** The local council have set up a new playground, equipped with slides, swings, and a climbing wall.

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** The exclusive tropical island was a millionaire's playground.
