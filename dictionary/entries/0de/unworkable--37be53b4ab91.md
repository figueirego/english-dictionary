### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Not workable.
   1. Not operable; not practical; unmanageable.

      **Example:** These plans seem unworkable; moreover, a budget for their implementation remains a fantasy.

      **Source synonyms:** inoperable; nonworking; infeasible; untenable; unusable

      **Source antonyms:** workable; practicable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Not workable.
   1. Not suitable or amenable for being manipulated or transformed.

      **Example:** unworkable soils; unworkable clays; unworkable metals

      **Source context (verbatim):** of raw materials

      **Source synonyms:** unprocessable; unusable
