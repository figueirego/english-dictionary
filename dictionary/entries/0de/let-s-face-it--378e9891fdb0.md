## Phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Said about something said to be true (esp. an unpleasant truth); to admit something; often without "let's".

   **Usage:** colloquial

   **Example:** Face it. "It's raining cats and dogs" is an absurd yet evocative expression.

   **Example:** Let's face it. Climbing this mountain isn't gonna be easy, but the challenge itself is part of the point, isn't it?
