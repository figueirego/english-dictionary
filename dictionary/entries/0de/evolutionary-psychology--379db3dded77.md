## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A theoretical approach to psychology that attempts to explain mental and psychological traits—such as memory, perception, or language—as adaptations, that is, as the functional products of natural selection.

   **Usage:** uncountable; evolutionary theory
