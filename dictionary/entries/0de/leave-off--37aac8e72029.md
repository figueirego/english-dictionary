### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To omit.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To desist; to cease.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** Leave off hitting him!

   **Source synonyms:** let off; let up

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To stop with a view to resuming at a later point.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** We will now continue where the other team left off.
