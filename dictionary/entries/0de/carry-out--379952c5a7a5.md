### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To hold while moving it out.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** We’ll have to carry the piano out of the shop.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To fulfill.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** She finally carried out her lifelong ambition when she appeared in a Hollywood blockbuster.

   **Source synonyms:** discharge

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To execute or perform; to put into operation; to do.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** For the entire last summer they were carrying out their plan to renovate the living room.

   **Source synonyms:** run
