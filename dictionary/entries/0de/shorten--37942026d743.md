**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make shorter; to abbreviate.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To become shorter.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To make deficient (as to); to deprive (of).

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To make short or friable, as pastry, with butter, lard, etc.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To reduce or diminish in amount, quantity, or extent; to lessen.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to shorten an allowance of food
