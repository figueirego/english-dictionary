- IPA: /ˌɪn(t)ɚˈmidiˌɛɹi/ — source labels: US

### Forms

- intermediaries — plural

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. An agent acting as a mediator between sides to facilitate agreement or cooperation.

   **Source synonyms:** mediator; middleman; go-between; intercessor

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. A person or organisation in an intermediate position (in a transaction, agreement, supply chain, etc.)

   **Example:** The intermediary between the manufacturer and retailer is the wholesaler

   **Source synonyms:** middleman; broker

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. One or several stages of an event which occurs after the start and before the end.
