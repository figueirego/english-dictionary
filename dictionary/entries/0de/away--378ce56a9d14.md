### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. From a place, hence.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** He went away on vacation.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** I tried to approach him, but he turned away.

3. Aside, so as to discard something.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** throw away, chuck away, toss away

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Christmas is only two weeks away.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. In or to something's usual or proper storage place.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** I'll dry the dishes and you put them away.

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. In or to a secure or out-of-the-way place.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The jewels were locked away in the safe.

   **Example:** He was shut away in the castle tower for six months.

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. From a state or condition of being; out of existence.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** fade away, die away

   **Example:** I'll sleep the rest of the day away/out and, hopefully, I'll manage to sleep this hangover off/away.

**Source sense R1.S8**

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. Come away; go away; take away.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; by ellipsis

   **Example:** Away! Be gone! And don't let me see you round here again!

   **Source context (verbatim):** as imperative

**Source sense R1.S10**
