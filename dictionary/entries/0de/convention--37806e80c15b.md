### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A meeting or gathering.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The convention was held in Geneva.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A formal deliberative assembly of mandated delegates.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The EU installed an inter-institutional Convention to draft a European constitution.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The convening of a formal meeting.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A formal agreement, contract, rule, or pact.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A treaty or supplement to such.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; international law

   **Example:** The Vienna convention at the Vienna Congress (1814-15) standardized most of diplomatic conduct for generations.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A practice or procedure widely observed in a group, especially to facilitate social interaction; a custom.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Table seatings are generally determined by tacit convention, not binding formal protocol.

   **Example:** The convention of driving on the right is reinforced by law.
