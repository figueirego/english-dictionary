### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** broadly

- spanned — participle; past

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To extend across (a gap or space between two sides).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The suspension bridge spanned the canyon.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To cover or extend over (an area or distance).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The parking lot spans three acres.

   **Example:** The forest spans the entire valley.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To extend through (a period of time).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The novel spans three centuries.

   **Example:** His career spanned five decades.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To measure by the span of the hand with the fingers extended, or with the fingers encompassing the object.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**
