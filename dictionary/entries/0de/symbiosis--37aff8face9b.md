### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A relationship of mutual benefit, especially among different species.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The close and long-term relationship or interaction between two or more different species.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; ecology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The close and long-term relationship or interaction between two or more different species.
   1. The close and long-term relationship or interaction between two or more different species in which all organisms benefit; mutualism.

      **Usage:** countable; especially; uncountable

      **Example:** Bees and flowers display symbiosis. The bees get nectar and the flowers get pollinated.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; ecology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

**Source sense R1.S5**
