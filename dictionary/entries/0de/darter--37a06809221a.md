## Noun · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Forms

- darters — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who darts, or who throws darts; that which darts.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any member of the family Anhingidae, waterbirds with long necks.

   **Source synonyms:** snakebird

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of various darting freshwater fish of the family Percidae, that are usually small and brightly coloured and are native to North America.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any of the similar South American (and Panamanian) freshwater fish in the characin family Crenuchidae

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Any of the dragonflies in the genus Sympetrum

   **Usage:** UK

   **Source synonyms:** meadowhawk

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Any of various hesperiid butterflies of the genera Arrhenes and Telicota.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A game of darts that is won with the specified number of darts thrown.

   **Usage:** in-compounds

   **Example:** With a scoring target of 501, a nine-darter is a perfect game.
