### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of two objects, at right angles; perpendicular to each other.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** A chord and the radius that bisects it are orthogonal.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of a pair of vectors&#58; having a zero inner product; perpendicular.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The normal vector and tangent vector at a given point are orthogonal.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of a square matrix&#58; such that its transpose is equal to its inverse.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Of a linear transformation&#58; preserving its angles.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Of grid graphs, board games and polyominoes&#58; vertical or horizontal but not diagonal.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Of a pair of elements in an ortholattice&#58; each less than or equal to the orthocomplement of the other.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

**Source sense R1.S7**

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences; statistics
