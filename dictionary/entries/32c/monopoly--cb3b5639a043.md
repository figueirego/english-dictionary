### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A situation, by legal privilege or other agreement, in which solely one party (company, cartel etc.) exclusively provides a particular product or service, dominating that market and generally exerting powerful control over it.

   **Source antonyms:** monopsony; polypoly

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An exclusive control over the trade or production of a commodity or service through exclusive possession.

   **Example:** A land monopoly renders its holder(s) nearly almighty in an agricultural society.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The privilege granting the exclusive right to exert such control.

   **Example:** Granting monopolies in concession constitutes a market-conform alternative to taxation for the state, while the crown sometimes bestowed a monopoly as an outrageous gift.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The market thus controlled.

   **Usage:** metonymically

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The holder (person, company or other) of such market domination in one of the above manners.

   **Usage:** metonymically

   **Source synonyms:** monopolist
