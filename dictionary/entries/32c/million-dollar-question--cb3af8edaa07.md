### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A question that is very important, difficult to answer, or (especially) both.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Source synonyms:** sixty-four dollar question; sixty-four thousand dollar question; billion-dollar question

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; a question to be decided, on whose answer depends a million-dollar expenditure.
