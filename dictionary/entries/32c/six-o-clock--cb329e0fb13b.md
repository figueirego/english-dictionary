## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

- six o’clock — canonical

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The start of the seventh hour of the day; 6&#58;00 a.m. (06&#58;00).

   **Usage:** In both the 12-hour clock and the 24-hour clock

   **Example:** The breakfast buffet opens at six o'clock (= "The breakfast buffet opens at 6&#58;00 a.m.").

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The start of the nineteenth hour of the day; 6&#58;00 p.m. (18&#58;00).

   **Usage:** Only in the 12-hour clock

   **Example:** The next train comes at six o'clock (= "The next train comes at 18&#58;00").

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A position behind (horizontal clock orientation) or below (vertical clock orientation) (from the location of the 6 mark on a clock face)

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** We've got some bogeys at six o'clock.

   **Example:** You've got a bogey on your six o'clock.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A pose in which the lower leg and raised upper leg form a vertical line.

   **Usage:** ballet
