### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used as an instruction for people to remove their hats out of respect, generally towards someone of importance.

   **Usage:** dated

   **Source context (verbatim):** as an imperative

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used to express approval or praise&#58; congratulations, kudos, well done!

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** Hats off to the Jamaican bobsled team for their able representation of their country in the Olympics against serious obstacles.

   **Source context (verbatim):** as an imperative
