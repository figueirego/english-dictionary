### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An artery supplying the deep structures of the face (as the nasal cavities, palate, tonsils, and pharynx) and sending a branch to the meninges of the brain.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; medicine; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** internal maxillary artery

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Either of the two arteries supplying external and internal structures of the maxillary region.

   **Usage:** dated
