## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The process of finding a solution to a problem by trying many possible solutions and learning from mistakes until success is attained.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** Navigating by trial and error may get you there eventually, but using a map is faster.
