### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Composed of clay or containing (much) clay; clayish.

   **Source synonyms:** argillaceous; argillous

   **Source antonyms:** nonclayey; unclayed

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Covered or dirtied with clay.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Resembling clay; claylike, clayish.

   **Source synonyms:** bolar

   **Source antonyms:** nonclayey

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Of the human body, as contrasted with the soul; bodily, human, mortal.

   **Usage:** figuratively
