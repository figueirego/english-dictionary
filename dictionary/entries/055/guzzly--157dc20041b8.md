### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Relating to guzzling (drinking greedily or with gusto).
   1. Tending to guzzle; drinking greedily; consuming a large amount of liquid.

      **Source synonyms:** eager; greedy

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Relating to guzzling (drinking greedily or with gusto).
   1. Involving guzzling.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Relating to guzzling (drinking greedily or with gusto).
   1. Intended to be guzzled or inviting guzzling; (of a wine) easy to guzzle.

      **Source synonyms:** gluggy; quaffable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Relating to guzzling (drinking greedily or with gusto).
   1. Resembling the sound of guzzling.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Relating to guzzling (drinking greedily or with gusto).
   1. Full of liquid that can be guzzled.
