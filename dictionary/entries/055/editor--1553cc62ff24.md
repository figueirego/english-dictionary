### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who edits or makes changes to documents.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A copy editor.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A person who edited a specific document.

   **Example:** John Johnson wrote this term paper and the editor was Joan Johnson.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A person at a newspaper, publisher or similar institution who edits stories and/or decides which ones to publish.

   **Example:** John is the city editor at the Daily Times.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A machine used for editing (cutting and splicing) movie film or audio tape etc.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A program for creating and making changes to files, especially text files.

   **Example:** This editor won't open JPG files. I need a graphics editor.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Someone who manipulates video footage and assembles it into the correct order etc for broadcast; a picture editor.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** broadcasting; cinematography; film; media; television
