### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To pick up the receiver of a telephone, generally with the intention of making a phone call.

   **Example:** These deals won't last, so don't wait! Pick up the phone and dial Big Harry today! He'll find the perfect car for you!

   **Example:** If you ever need help, all you've gotta do is pick up the phone and give me a call.

   **Source antonyms:** hang up

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To answer an incoming telephone call.

   **Example:** She dialed nervously, praying that Grandma would pick up the phone and everything would turn out OK.

   **Source antonyms:** hang up
