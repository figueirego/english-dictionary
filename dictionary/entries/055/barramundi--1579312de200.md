### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A diadromous fish of species Lates calcarifer, of the Centropomidae family, order Perciformes, widely distributed in the Indo-West Pacific; a popular food fish.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Either of two fish, Scleropages leichardti and S. jardinii, of the family Osteoglossidae.

   **Usage:** Australia

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The fish Neoceratodus forsteri of the family Neoceratodontidae; the Australian lungfish.

   **Usage:** Australia; obsolete
