### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A paradox involving statements such as "this sentence is false", or "the following statement is true&#58; the previous statement is false", which cannot be meaningfully regarded as either true or false.

   **Example:** Graham Priest has argued the liar paradox is a true dialetheia.
