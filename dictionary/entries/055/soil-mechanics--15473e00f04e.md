## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The application of mechanics (especially fluid mechanics) to soils, especially as a basis for the design and construction of building foundations and earthworks.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Consulting with the Foo firm brought soil mechanics into the plant engineer's toolchest.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The mechanical behavior and performance of a soil.

   **Usage:** metonymically; uncountable

   **Example:** The plan to build a dam just downriver from Fooville was foiled by unsatisfactory soil mechanics.
