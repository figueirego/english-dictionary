### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To perform on behalf of someone or with someone in mind; to behave in a manner befitting someone.

   **Usage:** informal; usually

   **Example:** I've tried hard to do right by him. Even so, he continues to do badly by me.

   **Example:** Do as you would be done by.

   **Source context (verbatim):** used with right/well or wrong/ill
