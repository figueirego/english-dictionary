### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To utter a loud, protracted, mournful sound or cry, as dogs and wolves often do.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To utter a sound expressive of pain or distress; to cry aloud and mournfully; to lament; to wail.

   **Source synonyms:** ululate

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To make a noise resembling the cry of a wild beast.

   **Example:** They howled with laughter at the prank.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To utter with outcry.

   **Example:** to howl derision
