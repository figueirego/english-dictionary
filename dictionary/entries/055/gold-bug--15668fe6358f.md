## Noun

### Forms

- gold bugs — plural
- goldbug — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person with a particularly great enthusiasm for investing in gold (the metal).

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** The swing in equities scared him so much that he became a gold bug for a while.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person who is opposed to fiat currency and supports a return to the gold standard.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** The swing in inflation scared him so much that he became a gold bug for a while.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** economics; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A person who collects gold items.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** If you see the decorating style in his living room, you'll know that he's a gold bug.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A particularly great enthusiasm for gold — whether as an investment, a hoard, a collectible, or otherwise — portrayed figuratively as an arthropod that bites people and infects them, causing a mental illness.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** bitten by the gold bug

   **Example:** He was bitten by the gold bug a long time ago, and some of the venom is still in his veins.
