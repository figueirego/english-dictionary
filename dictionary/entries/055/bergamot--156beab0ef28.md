### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A tree of the orange family (Citrus × limon, syn. Citrus bergamia), having a roundish or pear-shaped fruit, from the rind of which an essential oil of delicious odor is extracted, much prized as a perfume.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The fruit from the bergamot tree.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The essence or perfume made from the fruit.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A variety of snuff perfumed with bergamot.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Either of two plants of the mint family noted for their bergamot-like scent&#58;
   1. Mentha × piperita, nothosubspecies citrata, more commonly known as bergamot mint

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Either of two plants of the mint family noted for their bergamot-like scent&#58;
   1. Monarda didyma, also known as American bergamot or bee balm.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A variety of pear.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
