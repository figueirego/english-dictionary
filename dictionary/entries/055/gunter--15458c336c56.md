### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A device used in sailing, for two main configurations of rig&#58;
   1. A wire that leads from a point near the end of a gaff to a point near the other end. A block travels along this wire, and a halyard is attached to the block. This allows the gaff to be raised to the vertical by a single halyard.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; sailing; transport

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A device used in sailing, for two main configurations of rig&#58;
