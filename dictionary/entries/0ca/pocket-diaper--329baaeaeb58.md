### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A type of cloth diaper, secured with snaps or velcro, that consists of a waterproof inner and outer layer sewn together, creating a pocket, which is stuffed with absorbent insert material based on a baby's absorbency needs.

   **Usage:** Canada; US
