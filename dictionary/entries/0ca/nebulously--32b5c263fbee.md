## Adverb

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈnɛb.jʊ.ləs.li/ — source labels: Received-Pronunciation; US

### Forms

- more nebulously — comparative
- most nebulously — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In a manner like that of a cloud or haze.

   **Example:** The vapour drifted nebulously into the hall.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. As if viewed through a cloud or haze.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Vaguely, without clear purpose or specific intention.

   **Example:** He waved his hand nebulously in the direction he intended to go.
