### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A device used to stir. (Cardinal types include wood or plastic strips (for beverages) and magnetic objects that react to a spinning magnet nearby (for chemistry beakers).

   **Example:** We're out of coffee stirrers again and I'm not using my finger!

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person who stirs something.

   **Example:** The stirrers in the chocolate factory often get chocolate all over their uniforms.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A person who spreads rumours or causes agitation.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Example:** Why would you say something so hurtful? God, you are such a stirrer!
