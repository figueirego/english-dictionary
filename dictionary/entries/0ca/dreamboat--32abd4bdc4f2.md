<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An exceptionally good-looking and sexually attractive person, particularly a man.

   **Usage:** dated; humorous; informal

   **Example:** My new fella is such a dreamboat. He can dance, can sing, and is the captain of the school hockey team.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** archaic; slang
