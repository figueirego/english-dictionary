### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A day in honor of one's mother on the second Sunday in May in the United States and Canada.

   **Usage:** Canada; Philippines; US

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Synonym of Mothering Sunday, the fourth Sunday of Lent, a moveable feast three weeks before Easter, used as a day in honor of mothers in some Commonwealth countries.

   **Usage:** Ireland; UK; informal

   **Source synonyms:** Mothering Sunday (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Other similar holidays honoring mothers or one's mother in various countries.
