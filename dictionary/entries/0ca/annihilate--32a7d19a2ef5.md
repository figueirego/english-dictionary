### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To reduce to nothing, to destroy, to eradicate.

   **Example:** An atom bomb can annihilate a whole city.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To react with antimatter, producing gamma radiation and (for higher-mass reactants, especially composite particles such as protons) lighter particles (such as pions, muons, and neutrinos).

   **Usage:** particle

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To treat as worthless, to vilify.

   **Usage:** archaic

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To render null and void; to abrogate.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To cause to become zero by means of an annihilator operator.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences
