### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A decision that is not unanimous made by a panel of decision-makers, such as an appellate court.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. an outcome in several full-contact combat sports where two of the three judges score for the same fighter as the winner, while the third judge scores for the other fighter.
