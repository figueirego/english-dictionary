## Noun · Record 2

### Forms

- sentencings — plural

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. The act of pronouncing a judicial sentence on someone convicted of a crime.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** After the verdict, the sentencing was not delayed.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. That which has been pronounced as a judgement or sentence for a crime.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. The act of creating one or more complete sentences from fragmented thoughts and phrases.

   **Usage:** colloquial; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** He struggled with sentencing his frayed and angry verses from poem to prose.
