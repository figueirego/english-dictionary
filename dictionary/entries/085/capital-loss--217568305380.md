### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A decrease in the value of a capital asset; an amount by which the proceeds of the sale of a capital asset by its owner are less than its cost to the owner.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; economics; finance; sciences
