## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Nitrogen gas that has been frozen, by cooling it to extremely low temperatures, resulting in a crystalline structure similar to regular ice but consisting of nitrogen molecules instead of water molecules.
