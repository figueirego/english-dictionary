### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A kind of block with an opening in one side to receive the bight of a rope.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A block equipped with a hook that hooks a cable so that it can be doubled back on itself in order to double the load carrying ability of the cable.
