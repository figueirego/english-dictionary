### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To do without (something enjoyable); to relinquish.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I've had to forgo my student discount in exchange for taking the job.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To refrain from, to abstain from.

   **Usage:** literary; transitive

   **Example:** I shall forgo actually stepping inside, thank you.
