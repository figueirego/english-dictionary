### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An area on both sides of the river Rhine, in the west of Germany. Roughly equivalent to the former Prussian Rhine Province, now the greater part of Rhineland-Palatinate plus the south-western half of North Rhine-Westphalia.

   **Usage:** usually

   **Source context (verbatim):** with the definite article

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
