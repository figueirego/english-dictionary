## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: \[ˈdaɪ.əɫ\] — source labels: UK; US

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A graduated, circular scale over which a needle moves to show a measurement (such as speed).

   **Example:** The dial on the dashboard showed the car was nearly out of gas.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A graduated, circular scale over which a needle moves to show a measurement (such as speed).
   1. Such a field as part of a clock face; (metonymic) the entire clock face.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A sundial.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A panel on a radio etc showing wavelengths or channels; a knob that is turned to change the wavelength etc.

   **Example:** Turn the dial to Radio 4&#58; my favourite show is on!

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A disk with finger holes on a telephone; used to select the number to be called.

   **Example:** His hands were too fat to operate the dial on the telephone.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A person's face.

   **Usage:** Australia; UK; slang

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A miner's compass.

## Verb · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: \[ˈdaɪ.əɫ\] — source labels: UK; US

- dialed — US; past

- dialled — UK; past

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To control or select something with a dial, or (figuratively) as if with a dial.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The lead guitarist for the rock band Spinal Tap dialed his amplifier to 11.

   **Example:** The president has recently dialled down the rhetoric.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To select a number on a telephone, regardless of a physical dial.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** In an emergency dial 999.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To call (a person or organization) using a telephone.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To use a dial or a telephone.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Please be careful when dialling.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To initiate a connection to a remote computer service such as a database.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The application failed when attempting to dial the Postgres server, because there were too many open connections.

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To use a dial-up modem to connect a personal computer to the Internet.

   **Usage:** dated; intransitive

   **Example:** I always check my guestbook when I dial, just in case anyone saw my Web site.
