### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To form stitches in; especially, to sew in such a manner as to show on the surface a continuous line of stitches.

   **Example:** to stitch a shirt bosom.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To sew, or unite or attach by stitches.

   **Example:** to stitch printed sheets in making a book or a pamphlet.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To practice/practise stitching or needlework.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** agriculture; business; lifestyle

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**
