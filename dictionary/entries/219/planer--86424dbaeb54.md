### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A tool which smooths a surface or makes one surface of a workpiece parallel to the tool's bed.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** arts; business; carpentry; construction; crafts; hobbies; lifestyle; manufacturing; woodworking

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person employed to use this kind of woodworking tool.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A large machine tool in which the workpiece is traversed linearly (by means of a reciprocating bed) beneath a single-point cutting tool. (Analogous to a shaper but larger and with the workpiece moving instead of the tool.) Planers can generate various shapes, but were most especially used to generate large, accurate flat surfaces. The planer is nowadays obsolescent, having been mostly superseded by large milling machines.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; mechanical; mechanical-engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A wooden block used for forcing down the type in a form, and making the surface even.

   **Usage:** archaic

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** media; printing; publishing
