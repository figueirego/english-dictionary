## Noun

### Forms

- polynomial bases — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A basis of a polynomial ring (said ring being viewed either as a vector space over the field of coefficients or as a free module over the ring of coefficients).

   **Usage:** ring theory

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** algebra; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Specifically, a basis, of the form \{1, α, ..., αⁿ⁻¹\}, of a finite extension F\_(qⁿ) of a Galois field F\_q, where α is a primitive element of F\_(qⁿ) (i.e., a root of a degree-n primitive polynomial over F\_q).

   **Usage:** field theory

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** algebra; computing; cryptography; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a finite field
