### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A deadly game in which a person loads a single bullet in the cylinder of a revolver, spins the cylinder so that the bullet's location is unknown, points the muzzle at their head, and pulls the trigger. In its most lethal form, played by multiple participants each of whom takes a turn until the weapon discharges.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** games

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any activity, especially a needless one, that carries a high risk of death or disaster.

   **Usage:** figuratively; uncountable
