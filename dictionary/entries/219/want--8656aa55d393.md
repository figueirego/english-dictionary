### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To wish for or desire (something); to feel a need or desire for; to crave, hanker, or demand.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I want you as a friend, not a foe.

   **Example:** What do you want to eat? I want you to leave. I never wanted to go back to live with my mother.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To wish for or desire (something); to feel a need or desire for; to crave, hanker, or demand.
   1. To make it easy or tempting to do something undesirable, or to make it hard or challenging to refrain from doing it.

      **Usage:** broadly; transitive

      **Example:** The game developers of Candy Crush want you to waste large, copious amounts of your money on in-game purchases to buy boosters and lives.

      **Example:** Depression wants you to feel like the world is dark and that you are not worthy of happiness. The first step to making your life better from this day forward is to stop believing these lies.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To wish, desire, or demand to see, have the presence of or do business with.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Ma’am, you are exactly the professional we want for this job.

   **Example:** Danish police want him for embezzlement.

   **Source context (verbatim):** in particular

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To desire (to experience desire); to wish.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** You can leave if you want.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To be advised to do or aim for something (compare should, ought).

   **Usage:** colloquial; interrogative; second-person; usually; often future tense

   **Example:** You’ll want to repeat this three or four times to get the best result.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To lack and be in need of or require (something, such as a noun or verbal noun).

   **Usage:** colloquial; transitive

   **Example:** That chair wants fixing and a clean.

   **Example:** What you really want is a good smack!

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To have occasion for (something requisite or useful); to require or need.

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To be lacking or deficient or absent.

   **Usage:** dated; intransitive

   **Example:** There was something wanting in the play.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To be in a state of destitution; to be needy; to lack.

   **Usage:** dated; intransitive

   **Example:** The paupers desperately want.

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. To lack and be without, to not have (something).

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive

   **Example:** She wanted anything she needed.

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. To lack and perhaps be able or willing to do without.

   **Usage:** broadly; obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s12"></a>

**Source sense R1.S12**

12. To desire a romantic or (especially) sexual relationship with someone; to lust for.

   **Example:** Dang, girl! Your brother is gorgeous! I want him so bad!
