### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Pleasurably entertained.

   **Example:** The children chased one another in a circle in front of their amused parents.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Displaying amusement.

   **Example:** There was an amused grin on his face.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. Enjoying humorous aspects of something.

   **Usage:** usually

   **Example:** He was very amused to note the disarray of his opponents.

   **Example:** He was very much amused by the lyrics.
