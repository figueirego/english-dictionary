## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The eastern region of Gippsland (a prefecture-level division of the state of Victoria, Australia); including the eponymous Shire of East Gippsland, and the Shire of Wellington.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A local government area in Victoria, Australia; in full, the Shire of East Gippsland.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Ellipsis of Shire of East Gippsland and Far East Gippsland. A shire in the prefecture of Gippsland, the easternmost region of the state of Victoria, Australia.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; ellipsis

   **Source synonyms:** Far East Gippsland; East Gippsland Shire; Shire of East Gippsland

   **Alternative form of:** Shire of East Gippsland and Far East Gippsland
