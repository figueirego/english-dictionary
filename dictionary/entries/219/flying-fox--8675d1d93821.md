## Noun

### Forms

- flying foxes — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A large Australasian bat, of the genera Pteropus or Acerodon in the family Pteropodidae, having a fox-like face.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any bat of the family Pteropodidae (see Usage notes).

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A cyprinid fish of species Epalzeorhynchos kalopterus.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A device consisting of a chair suspended on a pulley, and running along a length of cable, used for transporting across ravines etc.

   **Source synonyms:** zipline

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A cable-operated gyroscopic camera that is suspended on a ropeway, used in filming motion pictures and sporting events.
