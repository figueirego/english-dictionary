### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The pipes, together with the joints, tanks, stopcocks, taps, and other fixtures of a water, gas, or sewage system in a house or other building.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Water was dripping from the ceiling, so we figured there was a problem with the plumbing.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The trade or occupation of a plumber.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A system of vessels or ducts in the human body, especially the genitourinary system.

   **Usage:** countable; informal; uncountable

   **Example:** My plumbing was playing up, so I had to see the doctor.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Practical, concrete work serving to support other work.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; informal; uncountable

   **Example:** This is not scientific research as such&#58; it's just plumbing.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

   **Usage:** countable
