### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To take into account, consider.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The problem is that they didn't allow for the extra centimetre of overlap, so it didn't fit correctly.

   **Example:** You must allow for the fact that his hearing isn't very good.

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To make possible; to allow.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The gap between my classes allowed for me and my friends to go eat lunch.
