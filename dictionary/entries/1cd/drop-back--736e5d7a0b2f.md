### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of a quarterback or other player in the backfield, to take a number of steps back from the line of scrimmage immediately after the snap or hike of the ball, to avoid defenders.

   **Example:** The Tiger quarterback is dropping back to pass.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** American-football; ball-games; football; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To move to a lower (or slower) position in a race or competition; to slow down so as to meet up with someone behind oneself

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see drop, back.

**Source sense R2.S1**

   **Example:** With his ankle injury his drop back is not pretty.
