### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. For reasons that are easy to understand or sympathise with.

   **Example:** Understandably, he was too embarrassed to speak.

   **Example:** Embarrassed, he understandably refused to speak.

   **Example:** He was understandably embarrassed, and could not speak.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In an understandable manner.

   **Example:** When presenting in front of the class, take care to speak clearly and understandably.
