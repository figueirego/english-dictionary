### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any mathematical rule expressed symbolically.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** mathematical formula

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A symbolic expression of the structure of a compound.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** H₂O is the formula for water.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

   **Source synonyms:** chemical formula

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A plan or method for dealing with a problem or for achieving a result.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The company's winning formula includes excellent service and quality products.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A formulation; a prescription; a mixture or solution made in a prescribed manner; the identity and quantities of ingredients of such a mixture.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The formula of the rocket fuel has not been revealed.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A fixed phrase or set of words intended to be interpreted non-literally, typically used attitudinally or as part of convention; a formulation.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Avoid the typical epistolic formalae, such as "Kind regards".

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A formal statement of doctrine.

   **Usage:** countable; especially; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** lifestyle; religion

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Ellipsis of infant formula, drink given to babies to substitute for mother's milk.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; countable; ellipsis; uncountable

   **Example:** They tried switching formulas, and it seemed to work&#58; the baby seemed more content while feeding.

   **Alternative form of:** [infant formula](../107/infant-formula--41de37fef0fd.md); drink given to babies to substitute for mother's milk

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A syntactic expression of a proposition, built up from quantifiers, logical connectives, variables, relation and operation symbols, and, depending on the type of logic, possibly other operators such as modal, temporal, deontic or epistemic ones.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; logic; mathematics; philosophy; sciences
