### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Either of two Californian boring weevils (Rhinostomus frontalis and Scyphophorus yuccae).

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A large moth-like butterfly (skipper), Megathymus yuccae of the family Hesperiidae, whose larva bores in yucca roots.
