### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Open to debate; not fully proved or confirmed.

   **Example:** Those data are debatable&#58; no one has been able to replicate them.

   **Example:** It's debatable whether he is the best candidate for the position.

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Example:** The lack of bonuses this year is not debatable.
