### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An incarnate being or form.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A version or iteration (of something).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A living being embodying a deity or spirit.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** avatar

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An assumption of human form or nature.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A person or thing regarded as embodying or exhibiting some quality, idea, or the like.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The leading dancer is the incarnation of grace.

   **Source synonyms:** embodiment; instantiation; realization
