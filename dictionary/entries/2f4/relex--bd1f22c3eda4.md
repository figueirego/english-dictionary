### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A conlang created by substituting new vocabulary into the grammar of an existing language.

   **Example:** Lojban started as a relex of Loglan, but the languages' grammars have since diverged.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A programming language that differs from another only by the names of keywords, functions, etc.
