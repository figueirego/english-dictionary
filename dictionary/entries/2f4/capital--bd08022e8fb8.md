### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Money and wealth&#58; the means to acquire or produce goods and services, especially in a non-barter system.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** He does not have enough capital to start a business.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; economics; finance; insurance; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** asset

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Already-produced durable goods available for use as a factor of production, such as tools and bulldozers (equipment) and office buildings (structures).

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** He pointed out that it takes both labour and capital to produce goods.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** economics; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** asset

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The capitalist class; capital owners considered collectively as a politically influential group — commonly represented by wealthy individuals and private institutional investors.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** The markets crashing symbolized capital’s discontent with the tax reforms passed.

   **Example:** He warned us darkly that capital will never be sated.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; politics

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A city designated as a legislative seat by the government or some other authority, often the city in which the government is located; otherwise the most important city within a country or a subdivision of it.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** Washington D.C. is the capital of the United States of America.

   **Example:** The Welsh government claims that Cardiff is Europe’s youngest capital.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The most important city in the field specified.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. An uppercase letter.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source synonyms:** caps; majuscule

   **Source antonyms:** minuscule

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Knowledge; awareness; proficiency.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Interpreters need a good amount of cultural capital in order to function efficiently in the profession.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. The chief or most important thing.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable
