## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. When the eyes of a potato tuber have germinated before the potato was got up, and have formed a number of small unripe tubers attached to the old, supertuberation.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; obsolete; Chester

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. When the axillary buds on the stem of a potato grow into small green tubers, as is often the case in wet seasons.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; obsolete; Chester
