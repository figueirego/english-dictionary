## Noun

### Forms

- numbers games — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A lottery in which bettors attempt to guess which numbers will later be drawn randomly; especially, an illicit one.

   **Example:** Back in that day, mafias ran the numbers games and there was no such thing as a state lottery.

   **Source synonyms:** numbers\[Appendix&#58;Glossary

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The incorrect use of statistics (numbers) to reflect a desired result (usually misleading).

   **Example:** They're playing a numbers game to make it look like seat belts and vaccines are dangerous.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An activity that benefits from having many things to work with or on; a focus on quantity.

   **Example:** In this business, breakeven is a numbers game&#58; if you can achieve enough volume, you'll be all right.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A game or sport that is dominated by analytics.

   **Usage:** uncommon

   **Example:** Somehow, to me, it's not quite as fun now that they've turned it into a numbers game.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports
