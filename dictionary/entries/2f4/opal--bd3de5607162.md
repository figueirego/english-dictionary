### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A mineral consisting, like quartz, of silica, but inferior to quartz in hardness and specific gravity, of the chemical formula SiO₂·nH₂O.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; geography; geology; mineralogy; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A mineral consisting, like quartz, of silica, but inferior to quartz in hardness and specific gravity, of the chemical formula SiO₂·nH₂O.
   1. A precious gem, an iridescent gemstone found in the opal-silica mineral substrate (potch)

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; gemology; geography; geology; jewelry; lifestyle; mineralogy; natural-sciences; physical-sciences
