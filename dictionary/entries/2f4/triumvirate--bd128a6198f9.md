### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A council of three magistrates ruling jointly; specifically, the First Triumvirate (60 or 59 – 53 B.C.E.) or the Second Triumvirate (43 – 33 or 27 B.C.E.); the office of a triumvir (“one of such magistrates”), or of the three triumviri.

   **Usage:** Ancient-Rome; historical

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any group of three joint rulers.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any group of three people regarded as significant in some way; also (rare), a group of three things; a trio.

   **Usage:** broadly; figuratively

   **Source synonyms:** leash; tern; ternary; ternion; threeness; threesome; triad; trinary; trine; trinity; trio; triplet; troika
