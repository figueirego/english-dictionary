### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person, thing, or name typifying a certain quality or idea; an embodiment or exemplification.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** Adolf Hitler was the personification of anti-Semitism.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An artistic representation of an abstract quality as a human.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** The Grim Reaper is a personification of death.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The process of creating such a representation&#58; a literary device or other artistic method in which an inanimate object or an idea is given human qualities.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** You might search in vain if you go looking for personification in his works&#58; his style was very concrete.

   **Example:** The writer used personification to convey her ideas.
