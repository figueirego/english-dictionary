## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A rhetorical device in which a series is arranged in "proper order", particularly
   1. A descending order from best, greatest, etc. to worst, lowest, etc.

      **Usage:** rhetoric; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A rhetorical device in which a series is arranged in "proper order", particularly
   1. A chronological order from first to last.

      **Usage:** rhetoric; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A rhetorical device in which a series is arranged in "proper order", particularly
   1. An ascending order from worst, lowest, etc. to best, greatest, etc; a climax.

      **Usage:** obsolete; rhetoric; uncountable; imprecise

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** anticlimax — descending series; chronological order — temporal series; arrangement — temporal series; climax — ascending series
