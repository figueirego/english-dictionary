### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In the final stages of conversion from pupa to adult, e.g. waiting to emerge from a cocoon, or escaping from an aquatic pupal stage to terrestrial adulthood.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of an adult insect

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** especially an insect

   **Source context (verbatim):** of an animal
