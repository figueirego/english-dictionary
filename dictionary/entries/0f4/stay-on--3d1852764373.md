**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see stay, on.

   **Example:** How does your hat manage to stay on in such strong wind?

   **Example:** The light stays on even if there's a power outage.

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. to continue in a place or situation, while others leave.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** Some of her friends decided it was time to go home, but she wanted to stay on in there until sunrise.
