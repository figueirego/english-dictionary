### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who reads.

   **Example:** an early reader, a talented reader

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person who reads a publication.

   **Example:** 10,000 weekly readers

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A person who recites literary works, usually to an audience.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A proofreader.

   **Source synonyms:** proofreader; printer's reader

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A person employed by a publisher to read works submitted for publication and determine their merits.

   **Source synonyms:** publisher's reader; first reader

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A position attached to aristocracy, or to the wealthy, with the task of reading aloud, often in a foreign language.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A university lecturer ranking below a professor.

   **Usage:** UK

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Any device that reads something.

   **Example:** a card reader, a microfilm reader

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A book of exercises to accompany a textbook.

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. An elementary textbook for those learning to read, in a native language or a foreign language.

   **Example:** Appletons’ School Readers

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. A literary anthology.

<a id="r1-s12"></a>

**Source sense R1.S12**

12. A lay or minor cleric who reads lessons in a church service.

<a id="r1-s13"></a>

**Source sense R1.S13**
