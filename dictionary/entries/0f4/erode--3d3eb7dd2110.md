### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To wear away by abrasion, corrosion, or chemical reaction.

   **Example:** The cliff is gradually being eroded by the waves.

   **Source synonyms:** corrode; eat away; gnaw; undermine; denude

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To destroy gradually by an ongoing process.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** to erode a person’s trust

   **Example:** to erode society’s morals

   **Source synonyms:** corrode; eat away
