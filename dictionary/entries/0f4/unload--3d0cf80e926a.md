### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To remove the load or cargo from (a vehicle, etc.).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to unload a ship

   **Example:** to unload a camel

   **Source synonyms:** offload; download

   **Source antonyms:** load up

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To remove (the load or cargo) from a vehicle, etc.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to unload bales of hay from a truck

   **Source synonyms:** offload; download

   **Source antonyms:** load up

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To deposit one's load or cargo.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To give vent to or express; to unburden oneself of.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** to unload on someone

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To remove (something previously loaded) from memory.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To discharge, pour, or expel.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To get rid of or dispose of.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To sell or dispose of (something) with the intent to deceive; to attempt to pass off a counterfeit or inferior product as genuine.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive; idiomatic
