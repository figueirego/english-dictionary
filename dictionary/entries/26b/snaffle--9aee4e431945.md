### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A broad-mouthed, loose-ringed bit (metal in a horse's mouth). It brings pressure to bear on the tongue and the corners of the mouth, and is often used as a training bit.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
