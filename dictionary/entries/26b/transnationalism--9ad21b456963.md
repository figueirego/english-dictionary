## Noun

### Forms

- transnationalisms — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The phenomenon, or the analytical view, that people's activities and cultures transcend national boundaries and, in at least some ways albeit not others, national sovereignty.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source antonyms:** nationalism; nativism; regionalism; tribalism

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Support for culture or business that crosses national boundaries.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source antonyms:** nationalism; nativism; regionalism; tribalism
