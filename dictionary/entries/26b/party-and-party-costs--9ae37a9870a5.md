## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The reasonable and necessary costs incurred by a party to a lawsuit, often in accordance with a scale of costs published by the court and excluding any work not necessary to resolve the case.

   **Usage:** Commonwealth; plural; plural-only

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An award granted by a court requiring one party (normally the unsuccessful party in litigation) to pay another party the amount of party and party costs the latter incurred.

   **Usage:** plural; plural-only
