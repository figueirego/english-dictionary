### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Under Roman law, the act of giving false testimony.

   **Usage:** countable; historical; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The act of obtaining a favour or grant by unfair representation through suppression or fraudulent concealment of facts.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Catholicism; Christianity

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; Scots law

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**
