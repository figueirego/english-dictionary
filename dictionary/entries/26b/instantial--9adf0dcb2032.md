### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Relating to, being, or constituting an instance.
   1. Being instantiated.

      **Usage:** not-comparable

      **Example:** when hypotheticals become instantial

      **Source synonyms:** instated; realize

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Relating to, being, or constituting an instance.
   1. Relating to a particular instance of something, not the general case.

      **Usage:** not-comparable

      **Example:** Some of those attributes are merely instantial, whereas others are truly generic.

      **Source synonyms:** incidental; coincidental; random; sporadic

      **Source antonyms:** generic; systemic; categorical; typical; classical; classic
