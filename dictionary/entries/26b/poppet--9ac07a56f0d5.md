### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An endearingly sweet or beautiful child.

   **Usage:** informal; term of endearment

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A young woman or girl.

   **Usage:** informal; term of endearment

   **Example:** Come 'ere, poppet!

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The stem and valve head in a poppet valve.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A doll (such as a voodoo doll) made in witchcraft to represent a person, used in casting spells on that person.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**
