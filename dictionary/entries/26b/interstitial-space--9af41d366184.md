## Noun

### Forms

- interstitial spaces — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An in-between space.
   1. (architecture) The gaps between buildings/floors in architecture, used for systems and movement; the neglected or overlooked spaces between built structures, like alleys, courtyards, or empty lots.

      **Usage:** broadly

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An in-between space.
   1. (anatomy) The fluid-filled area between cells in tissues (interstitium).

      **Usage:** broadly

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An in-between space.
   1. (sociology) The informal, transitional zones where people from different groups or fields (e.g., hobbyists, professionals) interact.

      **Usage:** broadly
