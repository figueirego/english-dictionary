### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A unit of time of one twenty-fourth of a day (sixty minutes).

   **Example:** I spent an hour at lunch.

   **Example:** There's a bus at 20 past the hour.

   **Example:** We still have a couple hours left to do in the yard.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** time

   **Source synonyms:** stound; microcentury

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A season, moment, or time.

   **Example:** Don't come home ever again at this unearthly hour.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The time.

   **Usage:** poetic

   **Example:** The hour grows late and I must go home.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Used after a two-digit hour and a two-digit minute to indicate time.

   **Usage:** in-plural

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The amount of labor demanded by an employer in terms of time.

   **Usage:** in-plural

   **Example:** I asked my manager for more hours.

   **Example:** The shop wasn't giving me enough hours so I started searching for a second job.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The set times of prayer, the canonical hours, the offices or services prescribed for these, or a book containing them.

   **Usage:** in-plural

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A distance that can be traveled in one hour.

   **Usage:** US

   **Example:** This place is an hour away from where I live.
