### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A trailer without front axle and with wheels only at the trailing end, designed to be pulled via a pivoting arrangement which also partially supports its weight.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Ellipsis of semi-trailer truck (“a tractor-trailer or big rig, consisting of a semi-trailer and a semitruck pulling it”).

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; ellipsis

   **Source synonyms:** articulated lorry (British); artic; 18-wheeler; juggernaut (British; slang); truck and trailer

   **Alternative form of:** [semi-trailer truck](../2fb/semi-trailer-truck--befb9c8475ce.md)
