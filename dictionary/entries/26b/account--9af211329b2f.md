## Noun · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /əˈkaʊnt/ — source labels: US

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A registry of pecuniary transactions; a written or printed statement of business dealings or debts and credits, and also of other things subjected to a reckoning or review.

   **Example:** The firm failed to file its accounts on time.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** accounting; business; finance

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A bank account.

   **Example:** The money was refunded to her account.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** banking; business

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A statement in general of reasons, causes, grounds, etc., explanatory of some event; a reason of an action to be done.

   **Example:** No satisfactory account has been given of these phenomena.

   **Source synonyms:** accounting; explanation

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A reason, grounds, consideration, motive; a person's sake.

   **Example:** Don't trouble yourself on my account.

   **Example:** on no account

   **Example:** on every account

   **Example:** on all accounts

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A record of events; a relation or narrative.

   **Example:** An account of a battle.

   **Source synonyms:** narrative; narration; relation; recital; report; description; explanation

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. An estimate or estimation; valuation; judgment.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Importance; worth; value; esteem; judgement.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Authorization as a specific registered user in accessing a system.

   **Example:** Since the system outage, I've been unable to log in to my account.

   **Source synonyms:** membership; registration

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A reckoning; computation; calculation; enumeration; a record of some reckoning.

   **Usage:** archaic

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. Profit; advantage.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** The young man soon turned his woodworking skills to some account.
