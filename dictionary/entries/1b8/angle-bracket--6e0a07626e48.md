### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of a number of angle-shaped brackets.
   1. Either of the inequality signs &lt; and &gt;, when used as brackets in programming languages and markup languages.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of a number of angle-shaped brackets.
   1. Either of a pair of symbols, ⟨ ⟩, used to enclose text in various technical contexts, and in mathematical formulas to denote an interval, a continuous parameter or an inner product.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; media; publishing; sciences; typography

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of a number of angle-shaped brackets.
   1. Either of a pair of symbols, 〈 〉, used in Chinese, Japanese, and Korean punctuation.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** media; publishing; typography

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any of a number of angle-shaped brackets.
   1. Either of a pair of symbols, 〈 〉, used to enclose the name of the domain in which a concept and a term is used.

      **Usage:** terminology
