### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A right or estate inherited from one's father; or, in a larger sense, from any male ancestor.

   **Example:** The museum holds an important part of the country's cultural patrimony.

   **Example:** The cathedral is regarded as part of the region's historical patrimony.

   **Example:** cultural patrimony

   **Example:** national patrimony

   **Example:** historical patrimony

   **Source synonyms:** heirloom

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Formerly, a church estate or endowment.
