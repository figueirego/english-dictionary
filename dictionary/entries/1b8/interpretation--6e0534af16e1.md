### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An act of interpreting or explaining something unclear; a translation; a version.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** The interpretation of a dream, or of an enigma

   **Example:** The interpretation of the rules

   **Source synonyms:** gloss

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A sense given by an interpreter; an exposition or explanation given; meaning.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** Commentators give various interpretations of the same passage of Scripture.

   **Source synonyms:** reading

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The discipline or study of translating one spoken or signed language into another (as opposed to translation, which concerns itself with written language).

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences; translation-studies

   **Source synonyms:** interpreting

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The power of explaining.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An artist's way of expressing thought or embodying a conception of nature through art.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. An act or process of applying general principles or formulae to the explanation of the results obtained in special cases.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. An approximation that allows aspects of a mathematical theory to be discussed in ordinary language.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. An assignment of a truth value to each propositional symbol of a propositional calculus.

   **Usage:** countable; model theory

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; logic; mathematics; philosophy; sciences
