### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Laughing (or laugh) out loud.

   **Usage:** Internet; literal

   **Example:** I just saw the video where he opens the door and gets the surprise! LOL!

   **Example:** Bro got milk spilt over his head! LOL!

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Lots of laughs. (occasionally used)

   **Usage:** Internet

   **Example:** I love the one where he orders a cup of tea but then chaos ensues! LOL!

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Indicates light-heartedness or amusement, or that the accompanying statement is not intended as serious; sometimes extends to irony or sardonicism, in the same way that spoken humor sometimes does. Often now a discourse marker independent of laughter.

   **Usage:** Internet; figuratively; by dilution

   **Example:** I hope you weren't expecting a welcome wagon, LOL.

   **Example:** In the course of managing your expectations, you'll need to account for the usual amount of pettiness and dishonesty among your fellow humans lol.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Lots of love.

   **Usage:** dated; letter-writing

   **Example:** P.S.&#58; Give my regards to Elizabeth and to the children! LOL!
