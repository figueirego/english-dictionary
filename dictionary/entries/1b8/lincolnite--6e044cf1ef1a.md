## Noun

### Forms

- Lincolnites — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A supporter of Abraham Lincoln (1809–1865), his policies, or Northern interests during the American Civil War.

   **Usage:** US; historical

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A member or alumnus of Lincoln College, Oxford.

   **Usage:** informal; Oxford University

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A resident of the English city Lincoln.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A resident of Lincolnshire.

   **Usage:** broadly

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A resident of the Nebraskan city Lincoln.
