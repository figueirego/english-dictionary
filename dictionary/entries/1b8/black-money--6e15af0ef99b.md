## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Income that is not reported to a government in order to evade taxes.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** dirty money

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Money that is obtained illegally, especially that which circulates outside the formal financial system in the underworld.

   **Usage:** India; uncountable

   **Example:** In India, the government faces the issue of combating black money stashed overseas.

   **Source synonyms:** dirty money; boodle (US)

   **Source antonyms:** white money
