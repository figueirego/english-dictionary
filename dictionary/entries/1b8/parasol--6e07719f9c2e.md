### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A small light umbrella used as protection from the sun.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A miniature paper umbrella used as a decoration in tropical-themed cocktails.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**
