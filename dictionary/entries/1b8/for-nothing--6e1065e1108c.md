## Adverb

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. For free.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** I got it for nothing. They are giving them away as publicity.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. For no reason.
   1. In vain, uselessly, wastefully, or pointlessly.

      **Usage:** not-comparable

      **Example:** I wrote ten pages of information for nothing. He just chucked it in a drawer without reading it!

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. For no reason.
   1. Without cause or without due cause.

      **Usage:** not-comparable

      **Example:** A&#58; Then he walked up to me and shoved me. B&#58; What the hell? What for? A&#58; For nothing!

      **Example:** A&#58; Then she filed suit against the restaurant. B&#58; What the hell? For what? A&#58; For nothing! Not so much for allegedly being negligent as for having some money that she might shake down!

      **Source synonyms:** for no good reason

      **Source antonyms:** not for nothing; with good reason
