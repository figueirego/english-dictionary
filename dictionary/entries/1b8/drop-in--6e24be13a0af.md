## Noun · Record 2

### Forms

- drop-ins — plural

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. One who casually drops in (visits unannounced or without appointment).

   **Example:** The office was efficient, but not well equipped to handle drop-ins.

   **Source synonyms:** walk-in

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. An informal event that does not require booking in advance.

   **Source synonyms:** walk-in

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. A place that can be visited casually, without an appointment.

   **Source synonyms:** walk-in

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. The act of dropping in; that is, taking a wave that another surfer is already riding.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports; surfing

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. A pitch that is prepared away from the ground or venue, and dropped into place only when a match is to be played.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; cricket; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports
