### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To give tone or variety of tone to; to vocalize.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To utter with a musical or prolonged note or tone; to speak or recite with singing voice; to chant.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to intone the church service

   **Source synonyms:** sing

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To utter a tone; utter a protracted sound.

   **Usage:** intransitive
