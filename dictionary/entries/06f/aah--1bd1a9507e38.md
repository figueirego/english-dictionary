## Interjection · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Indication of amazement or surprise or enthusiasm.

   **Example:** Aah! That's amazing!

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Indication of joyful pleasure.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Indication of sympathy.

   **Example:** Aah, I feel so bad for you...

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Indication of mouth being opened wide.

   **Example:** Dentists would always instruct, say aah!

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To express understanding.

   **Example:** Aah. Now I understand.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The sound of one screaming (with as many a's or h's as needed for emphasis).

   **Example:** AAAHHH! A bug! A bug! Get it off me! Get it off me!

   **Example:** Aah! A rat!
