   **Usage:** collective; often

   **Example:** Language is what separates man and beast.

   **Usage:** UK

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

   **Source synonyms:** monster

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

   **Usage:** slang

   **Example:** The subwoofer that comes with this set of speakers is a beast.
