## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The transference of authority, control, power or knowledge from one agency to another, or from one state to another.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The information passed on in such a case.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. the process of transferring an ongoing call or data session from one channel connected to the core network to another channel.

   **Usage:** cellular telecommunications

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. the process of transferring satellite control responsibility from one earth station to another without loss or interruption of service.

   **Usage:** satellite telecommunications

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The transfer of goods from the dealer to the purchaser, often of illegal goods.
