## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A typically two-handed gesture resembling quotation marks, indicating that the gesturer is speaking ironically or using unusual terminology.

   **Usage:** plural-normally

   **Source synonyms:** finger quote

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An emphatic tone of voice used to indicate a euphemism, or displeasure with a false or inaccurate phrase being quoted.

   **Usage:** broadly; plural-normally

   **Example:** "So you were out with 'the boys'," she said vexedly, putting air quotes around the last two words.
