## Noun

### Forms

- golden girls — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A girl or young woman who has become unusually successful at an early age.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A favored girl, held in high esteem by others, and for whom there are high hopes.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A young woman who is beautiful in a wholesome and healthy way.

   **Example:** In her day, Betty Grable was the golden girl of America.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An older woman, a female senior citizen.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** Ha ha, we were teasing our mom that she's a golden girl now.
