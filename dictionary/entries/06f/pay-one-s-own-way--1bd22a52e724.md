### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To pay for oneself, not relying on the generosity of others.

   **Example:** He paid his own way through community college and then transferred to a state university.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of persons and their activities

   **Source synonyms:** pay one's own freight

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Synonym of pay for itself.

   **Example:** Any in-house product development must pay its own way.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of inanimate things or processes
