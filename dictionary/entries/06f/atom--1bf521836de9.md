### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The smallest possible amount of matter which still retains its identity as a chemical element, now known to consist of a nucleus surrounded by electrons.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A hypothetical particle posited by Greek philosophers as an ultimate and indivisible component of matter.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** history; human-sciences; sciences

   **Source context (verbatim):** of science

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The smallest, indivisible constituent part or unit of something.

   **Usage:** now generally regarded figuratively

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. In logical atomism, a fundamental fact that cannot be further broken down.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; philosophy; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

   **Usage:** historical
