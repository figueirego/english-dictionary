### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A group of people who sing together; a company of people who are trained to sing together.

   **Usage:** collective

   **Example:** The church choir practices Thursday nights.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** architecture

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. One of the nine ranks or orders of angels.

   **Usage:** Christian

   **Example:** Seraphim, Cherubim, and Thrones are three of the choirs of angels.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** angelology; lifestyle; religion; theology

   **Usage:** collective

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** art; arts
