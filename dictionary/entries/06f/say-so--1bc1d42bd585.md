### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Authority, as backing some statement, injunction, or command.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** I wouldn't buy anything that expensive just on the say-so of a saleman.

   **Example:** You are not to go into that room without my say-so.

   **Example:** On whose say-so?
