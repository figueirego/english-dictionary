## Adjective · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /əˈd͡ʒeɪ.sənt/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Lying next to, close, or contiguous; neighboring; bordering on.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Because the conference room is filled, we will have our meeting in the adjacent room.

   **Source synonyms:** abutting; adjoining; contiguous; juxtaposed; near; conterminous

   **Source antonyms:** apart; distant; nonadjacent

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Just before, after, or facing.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The picture is on the adjacent page.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Related to; suggestive of; bordering on.

   **Usage:** figuratively; not-comparable; postpositional
