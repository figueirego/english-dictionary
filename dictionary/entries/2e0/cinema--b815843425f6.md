### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A movie theatre, a movie house.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** The cinema is right across the street from the restaurant.

   **Source synonyms:** pictures (UK); moviehouse (US); movies; movie theater (US); movie theatre (rare)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Films collectively.

   **Usage:** uncountable
