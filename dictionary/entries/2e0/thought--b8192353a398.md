### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A representation created in the mind without the use of one's faculties of vision, sound, smell, touch, or taste; an instance of thinking.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** The greatest weapon against stress is our ability to choose one thought over another.

   **Example:** I hate the thought of going back to work Monday morning.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The operation by which mental activity arise or are manipulated; the process of thinking; the agency by which thinking is accomplished.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Without freedom of thought there can be no such thing as wisdom, and no such thing as public liberty without freedom of speech.

   **Source synonyms:** thinking

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A way of thinking (associated with a group, nation, region or idea).

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** Traditional eastern thought differs markedly from that of the west.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Anxiety, distress.

   **Usage:** dialectal; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The careful consideration of multiple factors; deliberation.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** After much thought, I have decided to stay.

   **Source synonyms:** cogitation; contemplation; deliberation; mulling; musing; pondering; meditation; reflection; rethinking; reconsideration; rumination; thought

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A very small amount, distance, etc.; a whit or jot.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
