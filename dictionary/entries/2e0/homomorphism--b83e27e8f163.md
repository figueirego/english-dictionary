### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A structure-preserving map between two algebraic structures of the same type, such as groups, rings, or vector spaces.

   **Example:** A field homomorphism is a map from one field to another one which is additive, multiplicative, zero-preserving, and unit-preserving.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** algebra; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A similar appearance of two unrelated organisms or structures, as for example with fish and whales.
