### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A lesser criminal act in most common law legal systems, generally punished less severely than a felony.

   **Usage:** US; historical; criminal law

   **Example:** He was charged with a misdemeanor rather than a felony.

   **Example:** The offense is treated as a minor misdemeanor.

   **Example:** criminal misdemeanor

   **Example:** charged with a misdemeanor

   **Source context (verbatim):** in UK

   **Source synonyms:** petty crime

<a id="r1-s2"></a>
