## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A note-taking method where a sheet of paper is divided into three sections&#58; one for questions (taking the left half of the sheet), one for notes related to the question (taking the right half), and one for a summary of the page (taking up the bottom section).

   **Usage:** plural; plural-only

   **Example:** Your notes must follow the Cornell notes format.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Notes taken using this method.

   **Usage:** plural; plural-only

   **Example:** Taking Cornell notes is a real pain because I have to draw the lines on the paper every time.
