## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A form of prevention, usually medication, that is taken before exposure to a pathogen, preventing development of disease after such exposure.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** PrEP

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A form of prevention, usually medication, that is taken before exposure to a pathogen, preventing development of disease after such exposure.
   1. A preventative medical regimen of HIV medicines used to protect against HIV seroconversion after an exposure.

      **Usage:** especially; uncountable

      **Example:** Some doctors will prescribe pre-exposure prophylaxis to sexually active people to prevent them from getting HIV/AIDS.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences
