### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To give permission, power, or the legal right to do something.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** allow; let; permit

   **Source antonyms:** ban; bar; forbid; prohibit; prohibit; ban; banish; bar; criminalize; debar; disallow; enjoin; estop; forbid; forspeak (obsolete); foresnaffle; interdict; outlaw; proscribe; taboo; veto; forfend

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To give someone more confidence and/or strength to do something, often by enabling them to increase their control over their own life or situation.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** John found that starting up his own business empowered him greatly in social situations.

   **Source synonyms:** inspire

   **Source antonyms:** disempower; dishearten; disspirit
