<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An amount charged in return for permission to do something&#58;
   1. An amount charged ancillary to the (occasionally null) base cost of a product or service; an additional charge, nominally for some additional privilege, ability or permission.

      **Example:** booking fee, late fee

      **Example:** no hidden fees; &amp;nbsp the sticker price is $1 but all the extra fees push the price into triple digits; it costs just 4.99 and there are no fees

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An amount charged in return for permission to do something&#58;
   1. An amount charged for something&#58; the base cost of purchasing some thing, service, etc.

      **Example:** admission fee, membership fee, drivers' license fee

      **Example:** the fee for entry to the zoo is increasing from 50 to 60 dollars

      **Source synonyms:** tax

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A fixed rate or price charged for (chiefly white-collar) professional services.

   **Example:** lawyers' fees, tuition fee, bank fees, legal fees, consulting fee

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**
