### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A satellite navigation system; the navigation that is done via the use of such a system.

   **Usage:** countable; informal; uncountable

   **Example:** Back then there was no satnav — you needed to keep a paper atlas in the delivery van and to consult it daily.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A satellite navigation system receiver.

   **Usage:** Commonwealth; countable; informal; uncountable

   **Example:** Good thing we have a satnav to help us find our way.

   **Source synonyms:** GPS receiver
