### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A clue.

   **Example:** I needed a hint to complete the crossword.

   **Source synonyms:** indication; tip

## Verb · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /hɪnt/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Forms

- hints — present; singular; third-person
- hinting — participle; present
- hinted — participle; past
- hinted — past

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To imply without a direct statement; to provide a clue.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** She hinted at the possibility of a recount of the votes.

   **Source synonyms:** intimate

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To bring to mind by a slight mention or remote allusion; to suggest in an indirect manner.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to hint a suspicion

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To develop and add hints to a font.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The typographer worked all day on hinting her new font so it would look good on computer screens.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To give (a person) a hint; to tip off.

   **Usage:** nonstandard; transitive

   **Example:** If you find the treasure in the video game, please do not hint other players about it.
