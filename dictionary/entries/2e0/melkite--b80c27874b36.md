### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In 5th-century Syria and Egypt, a sobriquet applied to Chalcedonians by their opponents, denoting the Chalcedonians' fidelity to the Byzantine emperor.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A member of the Greek Orthodox Patriarchate of Alexandria, the Greek Orthodox Patriarchate of Antioch or the Greek Orthodox Patriarchate of Jerusalem; ellipsis of Orthodox Melkite.

   **Source context (verbatim):** since the Great Schism

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A member of the Melkite Greek Catholic Church; ellipsis of Catholic Melkite.

   **Source context (verbatim):** since 1724
