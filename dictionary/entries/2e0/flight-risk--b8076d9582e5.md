## Noun

### Forms

- flight risks — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The possibility that a person under the custody of law enforcement will abscond if granted bail.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** The court took flight risk into consideration when deciding bail for Mr Smith.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person who is considered likely to abscond.

   **Usage:** countable; usually

   **Example:** The court refused to grant Mr Smith bail as he was considered a flight risk.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law
