### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used to indicate that a specified or implied action cannot succeed or offers no advantage if successful

   **Usage:** colloquial; often

   **Example:** What's the use trying to tell him? He won't listen.

   **Example:** What's the use of worrying? It never was worthwhile.
