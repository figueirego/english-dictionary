**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A widely occurring mineral comprising calcium fluoride plus or minus impurities, of various colours, used as a flux in steelmaking, and in the manufacture of glass, enamels and hydrofluoric acid.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Fluorite is an important natural resource of Thailand.
