### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A particular approximate common multiple of the tropical year and the synodic month; in other words, the 19-year period over which the lunar phases occur on the same dates.

   **Usage:** calendar studies

   **Example:** According to the Metonic cycle, a lunar calendar begins on the same solar date every 19 years.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astronomy; natural-sciences
