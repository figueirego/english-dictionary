### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of several species of finches or finch-like birds that typically forage for food on the ground.
   1. Certain of Darwin's finches or Galapagos finches (Geospiza spp.)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of several species of finches or finch-like birds that typically forage for food on the ground.
   1. Towhees (Pipilo and sometimes Melozone), of North America.
