### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A kingfisher whose nesting by the sea was said, in classical mythology, to cause the Gods to restrain the wind and waves.

   **Usage:** poetic

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A kingfisher whose nesting by the sea was said, in classical mythology, to cause the Gods to restrain the wind and waves.
   1. The dead body of such a bird, said in Tudor times to act as a weather vane when hung from a beam.

      **Usage:** poetic

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A tropical kingfisher of the genus Halcyon, such as the sacred kingfisher (Halcyon sancta) of Australia.
