<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A feeling of sympathy at the misfortune or suffering of someone or something.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** I can't feel any pity towards the gang, who got injured while attempting to break into a flat.

   **Example:** take pity on someone

   **Source synonyms:** mercy; ruth; bowels (archaic); commiseration; compassion; empathy; fellow feeling; mercy; pity; remorse (obsolete); rue (archaic; dialectal); ruth (archaic); sympathy; tenderheartedness; loving-kindness

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Feeling of contempt one has for someone who is hopelessly inferior.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Something regrettable.

   **Usage:** countable; singular; singular-only

   **Example:** It's a pity you're feeling unwell because there's a party on tonight.

   **Example:** What a pity about the band breaking up. I loved them!

   **Source synonyms:** shame
