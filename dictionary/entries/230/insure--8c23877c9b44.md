### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To provide for compensation if some specified risk occurs. Often agreed by policy (contract) to offer financial compensation in case of an accident, theft or other undesirable event.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I'm not insured against burglary.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To deal in such contracts; subscribe to a policy of insurance

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Alternative spelling of ensure; to make sure or certain of; guarantee.

   **Usage:** US; alt-of; alternative; dated; transitive

   **Alternative form of:** [ensure](../09f/ensure--27ce25259481.md)
