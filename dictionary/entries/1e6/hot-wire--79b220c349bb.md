### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To wire, especially to a power supply, using a makeshift electrical connection.

   **Usage:** informal; transitive

   **Example:** I bypassed the switch and hot-wired the circuit board to the power supply.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In particular, to start (an automobile engine) by bypassing the ignition key wiring, especially in order to steal the vehicle.

   **Usage:** informal; transitive
