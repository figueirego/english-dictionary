### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Towards a (higher) position closer to the sky than the ground.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** look upwards

   **Example:** push upwards

   **Example:** soar upwards

   **Example:** The balloon drifted upwards into the sky.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To a higher figure or amount.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Prices are moving steadily upwards.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Towards something which is higher in order, larger, superior etc.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

   **Usage:** not-comparable
