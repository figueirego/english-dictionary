### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The part of the face below the mouth; the jaw.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Specifically, the bone therein; the mandible of humans and other vertebrates that, with the cranium, composes the skull.
