### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The side of something that is in darkness or unlit, or has less illumination.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The side of something that is metaphorically dark, i.e. evil, distressing or otherwise undesirable; the negative aspect of something.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively

   **Source synonyms:** downside

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Synonym of farside (“the side of a moon that faces away from the planet that it orbits”).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astronomy; natural-sciences

   **Source synonyms:** farside (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Synonym of nightside (“the side of a planet that faces away from the sun around which it orbits”).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astronomy; natural-sciences

   **Source synonyms:** nightside (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music
