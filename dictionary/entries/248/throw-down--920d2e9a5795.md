### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To cause something one is holding to drop, often forcefully.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The soldiers threw down their weapons and surrendered.

   **Source synonyms:** cast down; cast aside; throw aside

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To destroy or demolish.

   **Usage:** dated; transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To produce or perform (something) admirably or forcefully.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; slang; transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To accomplish or produce something in a grand, respectable, or successful manner; to "represent".

   **Usage:** broadly; idiomatic; intransitive; slang

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To drink a large amount quickly.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive; slang

   **Example:** "We need to finish these five pitchers in half an hour, so throw down as fast as you can!"

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To fight; to make a stand.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive; slang

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To make an individual contribution to a group effort (e.g. money pool, collaborative record album).

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive; slang

   **Example:** "We're goin' in on a pizza; you in?" "Yeah, I'll throw down."
