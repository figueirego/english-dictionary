### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Someone who handles and keeps bears, especially for use in public entertainments such as bearbaiting or dancing displays; a bearward.

   **Usage:** historical

   **Source synonyms:** bearherd; bear warden

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Someone acting as a chaperone and tutor to a wealthy young man travelling abroad, especially in Europe.

   **Usage:** broadly; historical

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A captor; also, a guard, a jailer.

   **Usage:** broadly; historical; humorous; obsolete; rare
