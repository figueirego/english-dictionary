### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The process by which something or someone (usually a word) is made more English. Usually applied to language or culture.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Dublin, the name of the capital of Ireland, is an anglicisation of the Gaelic name Dubh Linn.

   **Source synonyms:** Anglification
