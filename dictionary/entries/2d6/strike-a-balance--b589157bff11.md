### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To find a compromise between two conflicting things; to choose a moderate option between two extremes.

   **Example:** You should try to strike a balance between your social life and work life.

   **Example:** I like to think I strike a balance between being too strict and too lenient with my children.

   **Source synonyms:** strike a medium

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To bring out the relative state of a debtor and creditor account.
