## Noun

### Forms

- fire boxes — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The hearth of a metal-forging shop; the forge of a smithy.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An enclosed hearth with hatch for refueling that is attached to or built into a boiler that supplies steam for mechanical power.

   **Example:** The brakeman had to help the fireman shovel coal into the firebox as the engineer coaxed his locomotive up grade.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The internal hearth of a furnace, stove, or heater.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The hearth of an outdoor grill.

   **Example:** When the bottom of the grill firebox burned through, everybody ran over and stomped embers too keep the picnic shelter from catching fire.
