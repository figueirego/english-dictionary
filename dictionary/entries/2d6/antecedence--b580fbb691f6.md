### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The relationship of preceding something in time or order.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** precedence; priority; antecedence; antecedency; anteriority; precedence; priority

   **Source antonyms:** subsequence; posteriority; posteriorness; subsequence; succession

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. That which precedes something or someone (e.g. prior events, origin, ancestry).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The length of time by which one event or time period precedes another.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The relationship between a pronoun and its antecedent.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** grammar; human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A geologic process that explains how and why antecedent rivers can cut through mountain systems instead of going around them.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; geology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. An apparent motion of a planet toward the west.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astronomy; natural-sciences

   **Source synonyms:** retrogradation
