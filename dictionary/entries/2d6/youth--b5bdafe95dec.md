### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The quality or state of being young.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Her youth and beauty attracted him to her.

   **Source synonyms:** juvenility; youngness; youngth (archaic); youthfulness

   **Source antonyms:** age; dotage; old age; senility

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The part of life following childhood; the period of existence preceding maturity or age; the whole early part of life, from childhood, or, sometimes, from infancy, to adulthood.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Make the most of your youth, it will not last forever.

   **Example:** I made many mistakes in my youth, but learned from them all.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A young person.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** There was a group of youths hanging around the parking lot, reading fashion magazines and listening to music.

   **Source synonyms:** adolescent; child; kid; lad; teen; teenager; youngster

   **Source antonyms:** adult; grown-up

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A young man; a male adolescent or young adult.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source synonyms:** boy; young man

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Young persons, collectively.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source context (verbatim):** used as plural or singular

   **Source synonyms:** adolescents; kids; teenagers; teens; young people; youngsters
