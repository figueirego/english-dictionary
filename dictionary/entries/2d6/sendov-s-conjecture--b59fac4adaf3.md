## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A conjecture concerning the relationship between the locations of roots and critical points of a polynomial function of a complex variable. It states that for a polynomial f(z)=(z-r\_1)⋯(z-r\_n), qquad (n&gt;2) with all roots r₁, ..., rₙ inside the closed unit disk \|z\| ≤ 1, each of the n roots is at a distance no more than 1 from at least one critical point.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** Ilieff's conjecture
