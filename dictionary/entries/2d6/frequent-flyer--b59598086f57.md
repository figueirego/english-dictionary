## Noun

### Forms

- frequent flyers — plural
- frequent flier — alternative; US

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who flies frequently on commercial airliners.

   **Example:** As he flew home every weekend, he considered himself a frequent flyer.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Someone who is habitually a nuisance.

   **Usage:** derogatory; idiomatic; informal

   **Example:** Drunks tend to be the most common frequent flyers.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A patient who frequents a health facility with minor complaints or malingering.

   **Usage:** derogatory; idiomatic; slang

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences
