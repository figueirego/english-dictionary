### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Resembling, having some of the characteristics of (used to form adjectives from nouns).

   **Usage:** morpheme

   **Example:** Even at 13 years old, she still had a childlike voice.

   **Example:** I saw the snake-like coils of the garden hose peeking out from under the deck.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used to form adverbs from adjectives or nouns; alternative of -ly.

   **Usage:** dialectal; morpheme

   **Example:** The Spanish speak machine-gun-like.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used to form genres out of existing games through the process of analogy.

   **Usage:** morpheme

   **Example:** The Soulslike genre of games are very difficult and thought to play.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** games; gaming
