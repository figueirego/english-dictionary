### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A state of greatly dulled or completely suspended consciousness or sensibility; (particularly medicine) a chiefly mental condition marked by absence of spontaneous movement, greatly diminished responsiveness to stimulation, and usually impaired consciousness.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** He fell into a drunken stupor.

   **Example:** She woke from a deep stupor after the medication.

   **Example:** The shock left him in a stupor of disbelief.

   **Example:** The heat drove the workers into a midday stupor.

   **Example:** He sat in a dazed stupor on the couch.

   **Example:** The team stumbled off the field in a stupor of exhaustion.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A state of extreme apathy or torpor resulting often from stress or shock.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** daze
