### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A form of scientific medicine whose present-day form scientifically and legally overlaps with non-osteopathic medical science and practice. It developed from origins in osteopathy but diverged therefrom during the 20th century; today, an MD degree and a DO degree have professional parity.

   **Usage:** Canada; US; especially; uncountable
