### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The action of forbearing (“abstaining or refraining from doing something”); (countable) an instance of this.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source antonyms:** unforbearance

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The action of forbearing (“abstaining or refraining from doing something”); (countable) an instance of this.
   1. The action of refraining from enforcing something which is due (such as a debt or obligation) or a right which one has; (countable) an instance of this.

      **Usage:** uncountable

      **Source antonyms:** unforbearance

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The action of forbearing (“abstaining or refraining from doing something”); (countable) an instance of this.
   1. Restraint and tolerance under provocation; patient self-control; indulgence, lenity.

      **Usage:** uncountable

      **Source synonyms:** patience

      **Source antonyms:** unforbearance
