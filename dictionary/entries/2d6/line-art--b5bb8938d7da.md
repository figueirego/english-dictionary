## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Images that consist of distinct straight and curved lines placed against a (usually plain) background, without gradations in shade (darkness) or hue (color).

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** line drawing

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Artwork to be reproduced with one solid color, contrasted with artwork having tone or shading (halftones), or full color (color separations).

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** arts; graphic-design; media; printing; publishing

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The outlines of a drawing as opposed to color, shading, and texture.

   **Usage:** uncountable
