### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Issuing, emerging.

   **Usage:** archaic; not-comparable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Issuing or emerging from something, especially from the bottom (or a division) of the field or chief, or from an ordinary.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; heraldry; hobbies; lifestyle; monarchy; nobility; politics
