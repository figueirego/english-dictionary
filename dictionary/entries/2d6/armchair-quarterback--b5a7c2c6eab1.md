## Noun

### Forms

- armchair quarterbacks — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who watches sports on television, typically American football, who often verbally suggests plays or critiques the players and coaches.

   **Usage:** US; colloquial; idiomatic

   **Example:** My dad is such an armchair quarterback; he recognizes every bad decision the coach makes as soon as the play is over and he knows whether or not it worked.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person who criticizes something in which they are not involved and lack expertise.

   **Usage:** US; colloquial; figuratively; idiomatic
