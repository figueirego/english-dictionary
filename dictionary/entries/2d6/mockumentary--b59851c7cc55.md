### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A film or television programme presented as if it were a documentary but that is not factual and often a parody or satire.

   **Example:** “This Is Spinal Tap” is one of the most famous mockumentaries.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** broadcasting; film; media; television
