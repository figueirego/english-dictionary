### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A coefficient quantifying how the reactivity of a nuclear reactor changes due to the formation of bubbles in the reactor's coolant.

   **Usage:** nuclear physics

   **Example:** Because the Chernobyl reactor had a large positive void coefficient, the reaction accelerated rapidly once the coolant water started to boil.
