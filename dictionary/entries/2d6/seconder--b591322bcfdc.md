### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Something that lasts or takes a specified number of seconds.

   **Usage:** in-compounds

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The person who makes a second to a motion in any formal procedure, such as a parliamentary procedure.

   **Example:** He's been the seconder on many motions, but I don't think he's ever made a motion himself.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A Cub Scout serving as a second-in-command to a sixer.

   **Usage:** scouting

   **Source synonyms:** second
