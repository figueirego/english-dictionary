### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An institution which holds books and/or other forms of media for use by the public or qualified people often lending them out, as well as providing various other services for its users.

   **Example:** She went to exchange her books at her local library.

   **Source synonyms:** bookhouse (nonstandard); bibliotheca (obsolete); bibliotheque (obsolete)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any institution that lends out its goods for use by the public or a community.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** You can check out a bandsaw from the tool library.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A collection of books or other forms of stored information.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A collection of information or reference materials not in book form.

   **Example:** She has the most impressive record library I've ever seen.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A room dedicated to storing books.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A collection of software routines that provide functionality to be incorporated into or used by a computer program.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; programming; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** library file

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A collection of DNA material from a single organism or relative to a single disease.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; genetics; medicine; natural-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. The deck or draw pile.

   **Usage:** Magic&#58; The Gathering

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** card-games; games
