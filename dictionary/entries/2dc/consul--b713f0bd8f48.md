### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Either of the two heads of government and state of the Roman Republic or the equivalent nominal post under the Roman and Byzantine Empires.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of the three heads of government and state of France between 1799 and 1804.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A count or earl.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A councillor
   1. A member of early modern city councils in southern France and Catalonia.

      **Usage:** historical; obsolete

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A councillor
   1. An officer of the trading and merchant companies of early modern England.

      **Usage:** historical; obsolete

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A councillor
   1. An official in various early modern port and trading towns, elected by resident foreign merchants to settle disputes among themselves and to represent them to the local authorities.

      **Usage:** historical; obsolete

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. An official residing in major foreign towns to represent and protect the interests of the merchants and citizens of their country.

   **Usage:** broadly

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A high government official, generally either a coruler himself or a counsellor directly under the ruler.

   **Usage:** obsolete
