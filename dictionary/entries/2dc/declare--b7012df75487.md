1. To make clear, explain, interpret.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To assert or announce formally, officially, explicitly, or emphatically.

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** He declared him innocent.

   **Example:** declare bankruptcy

   **Example:** declare victory

   **Example:** declare war

   **Example:** declare independence

   **Example:** declare an innings closed

   **Example:** I'll declare for the presidency.

   **Source synonyms:** disclose; make known; announce; forerun; blare; declare; disclose; herald; impart; make known; publish; proclaim; promulgate; promulge

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To inform government customs or taxation officials of goods one is importing or of income, expenses, or other circumstances affecting one's taxes.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** disclose; make known; divulge

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To show one's cards in order to score.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** card-games; games

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. For a constituency in an election to officially announce the result

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Houghton and Sunderland South was the first constituency to declare in the 2015 general election.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; politics

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The decision of the captain to let the bowling side bat in test cricket to save time without being all out.

   **Example:** He declared at 689-7.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; cricket; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To explicitly establish the existence of (a variable, function, etc.) without necessarily describing its content.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The counter "i" was declared as an integer.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; programming; sciences

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To declare war.

   **Usage:** slang; grand strategy games

   **Example:** France declared on me, I'm finished!

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To state that a thing shall happen or affirm a condition in the hopes of seeing it happen spiritually, in contrast to prayer which takes the form of a request.

   **Usage:** particularly Evangelicalism

   **Example:** He prayed to God "please heal my sister", while she declared "I am healed in Jesus' name".

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity
