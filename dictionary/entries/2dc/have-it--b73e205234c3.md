### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To state or hold as true; to insist.

   **Example:** Conventional wisdom has it that heat rises, but in fact heat diffuses&#58; hot air rises.

   **Example:** He'll have it that I am maliciously misleading him.

   **Source synonyms:** hold

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To buy&#58; to purchase.

   **Example:** Twenty quid, you say? Yes, we'll have it, thank you.

   **Example:** (Well, for only twenty quid, we're having it all day long, aren't we?)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To accept (an excuse, a behavior, etc); to believe.

   **Example:** He tried to make excuses but she wouldn't have it / was not having it / was having none of it.

   **Source context (verbatim):** in the negative

   **Source synonyms:** buy it

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To be the object of ridicule, rebuke, critique, etc.

   **Source synonyms:** get it

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To possess some desirable quality or ability; to be all that.

   **Example:** I was nervous about her performing on stage, but after all these years, she still has it.

   **Example:** I was nervous about her performing on stage, but after all these years, she’s still got it. \[alloform\]
