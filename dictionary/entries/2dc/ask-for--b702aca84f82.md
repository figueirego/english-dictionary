### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To request; to order.

   **Example:** I asked for a glass of water.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To increase the likelihood of something undesirable by persisting in some action; to invite.

   **Example:** If you keep on speeding, you are asking for trouble.
