**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To appear or emerge unexpectedly or inexplicably, frequently in large numbers or quantity.

   **Usage:** derogatory; idiomatic

   **Example:** He won the lottery last year and he has had old "friends" and distant relatives coming out of the woodwork ever since.
