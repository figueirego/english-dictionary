## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any one of the types of atom distinguished by having a certain number of protons in its nucleus.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

   **Source synonyms:** element

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A chemical substance made entirely of one such type of atom; any one of the simplest chemical substances that cannot be decomposed in a chemical reaction or by any chemical means and made up of atoms all having the same number of protons.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

   **Source synonyms:** elementary substance; element
