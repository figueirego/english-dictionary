### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who wanders aimlessly, who roams, who travels at a lounging pace. One who walks to observe and enjoy rather than to get somewhere.

   **Source synonyms:** ambler; saunterer; stroller; wanderer
