## Verb

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To fasten (a piece of clothing, etc.); to tighten (a nut etc.)

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** I can't do up my shirt. The button is missing.

   **Example:** Help me do up this zipper.

   **Example:** You hold it in place while I do up the nut.

   **Source antonyms:** undo

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To redecorate (a room, etc.); to make improvements to (a home or domestic property).

   **Usage:** colloquial; idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** I'm going to do up the living room next.

   **Example:** They've done up the house so that they can sell it more easily.

   **Source synonyms:** fix up; spruce up; vamp up

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To execute a task or performance; to execute it well.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; informal; transitive

   **Example:** This time I'm going to do it up right.

   **Example:** Previously they had cut corners on the dinner menu, but this time they really did it up.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To pack together and envelop; to pack up, tie up, or both.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** I did up the parcel with string and took it to the post office.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To accomplish thoroughly.

   **Usage:** dated; transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To starch and iron.

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To beat up; to physically assault.

   **Usage:** slang

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To style (someone's hair); (especially) to give someone (i.e., oneself or another) an updo.

   **Usage:** transitive
