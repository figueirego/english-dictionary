### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The ancient capital of the 13th nome of Lower Egypt, located in the northern suburbs of modern Cairo, Egypt.

   **Usage:** historical

   **Source synonyms:** Iunu; Iwnw; On; Awen; Heliopolis in Augustamnica
