### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An opportunity or benefit which has no cost, especially one enjoyed or undertaken at the expense of others.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The practice of buying and selling shares or other securities without actually having the capital to cover the trade.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance
