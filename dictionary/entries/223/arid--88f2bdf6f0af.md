### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Very dry.

   **Example:** The cake was arid.

   **Source antonyms:** wet; drenched; damp; moist

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Describing a very dry climate. Typically defined as less than 25 cm or 10 inches of rainfall annually.

   **Example:** Deserts are known for being arid.

   **Source synonyms:** xeric

   **Source antonyms:** rainforest; humid

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Devoid of value.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** The millionaire viewed his gift as arid.

   **Source antonyms:** valuable; costly; precious
