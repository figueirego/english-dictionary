### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The vote (tally) in an election; a single vote (ballot) as a part of that tally.

   **Example:** The constitution prescribes how to resolve a tie in the electoral vote.

   **Example:** In the United States, a populous state such as California or Texas has a lot of electoral votes.
