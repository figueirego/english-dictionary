### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who, or that which, propels.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A mechanical device with evenly-shaped blades that turn on a shaft to push against air or water, especially one used to propel an aircraft or boat.

   **Example:** The plane’s propeller spun rapidly before takeoff.

   **Example:** The boat is powered by a single propeller.

   **Example:** He repaired the damaged propeller after the storm.
