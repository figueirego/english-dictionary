### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Something fit to be imitated; an ideal, a worthy model or role model&#58; a desirable example.

   **Example:** Michelangelo's "David" is an exemplar of Renaissance sculpture.

   **Source synonyms:** archetype; epitome; example; exemplar; icon; model; paradigm; paragon; pattern; quintessence; template

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Something typical or representative; an example that typifies its class.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A pattern after which others should be made; an archetype.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A well-known use of a scientific theory.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A manuscript used by a scribe to make a handwritten copy; the original document to be reproduced in a copy machine.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A copy of a book or piece of writing.
