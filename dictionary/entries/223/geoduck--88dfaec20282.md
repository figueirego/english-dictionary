### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The species of large saltwater clam Panopea generosa, native to the northeast Pacific coasts from Alaska to Washington State, distinguished by its deep burrowing and long unprotected siphon.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The species of large saltwater clam Panopea generosa, native to the northeast Pacific coasts from Alaska to Washington State, distinguished by its deep burrowing and long unprotected siphon.
   1. its flesh as a seafood.

      **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Other species of Panopea, especially Panopea zelandica, native to the coasts of New Zealand.

   **Usage:** New-Zealand; countable; uncountable
