**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To train before testing or further training.

   **Example:** In stage one, rats were pretrained to drink from a rotary device.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To train (a neural network) on a large corpus or other dataset to acquire a base of general-purpose knowledge, representations, or capabilities that can subsequently be adapted, specialized, or aligned through further training.
