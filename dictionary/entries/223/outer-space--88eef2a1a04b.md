**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A region outside of explored space.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any region of space beyond limits determined with reference to boundaries of a celestial system or body, especially the region of space immediately beyond Earth's atmosphere; sometimes, space beyond Earth's solar system.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Astronauts train for years before traveling to outer space.

   **Example:** The telescope captured incredible images from outer space.

   **Example:** Science fiction often imagines alien civilizations in the depths of outer space.
