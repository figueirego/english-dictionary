## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An excellent performance.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Someone or something whose performance is excellent.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Someone or something who is generally well-mannered, conscientious, etc.

   **Usage:** sarcastic; sometimes

   **Example:** You can count on Sam to help out afterwards, he's a real class act.

   **Example:** Oh yeah, they're a real class act; they left their garbage all over the grass.
