## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A man who donates sperm, usually anonymously, to a sperm bank or fertility clinic.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A man who is the father of a woman's child or children, but no longer has a relationship, or has a hostile relationship, with the woman, and is typically uninvolved with the children as well.

   **Usage:** US; derogatory; idiomatic; slang
