### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The fleshy portion, opposite the nail, of the first segment (first phalange) of the human thumb.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; medicine; sciences
