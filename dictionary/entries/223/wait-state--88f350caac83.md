### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of several instances in which a computer's processor cannot execute instructions (either for the entire computer, or just for a specific task) until an I/O operation completes, or until an interrupt is resolved.

   **Example:** With memory that slow, the processor is going to spend most of its time on wait states.
