## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A small town and civil parish in Teignbridge district, on the edge of Dartmoor, Devon, England (OS grid ref SX7569).

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A suburb of Melbourne, Victoria.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A river in Western Australia, which flows into the Indian Ocean.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A large town in Canterbury, New Zealand.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A river (with two branches) in Canterbury, New Zealand which flows past the town to the Pacific Ocean.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A territorial authority that includes the town, in Canterbury, New Zealand; in full, Ashburton District.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A town in KwaZulu-Natal, South Africa.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A neighbourhood of Baltimore, Maryland, United States.
