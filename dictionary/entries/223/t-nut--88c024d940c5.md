### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of two or more types of nut with a shape, in profile, suggesting the capital letter T.
   1. Such a nut die-formed from sheet metal and used in woodworking.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of two or more types of nut with a shape, in profile, suggesting the capital letter T.
   1. Such a nut milled from thick metal stock and used in machinery.
