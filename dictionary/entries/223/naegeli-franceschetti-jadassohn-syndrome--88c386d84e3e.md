## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A rare autosomal-dominant form of ectodermal dysplasia, characterized by reticular skin pigmentation, diminished function of the sweat glands, the absence of teeth, and hyperkeratosis of the palms and soles.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** NFJS; chromatophore nevus of Naegeli; Naegeli syndrome
