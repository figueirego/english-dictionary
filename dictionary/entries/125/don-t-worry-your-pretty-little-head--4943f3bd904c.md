## Phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used to tell someone, usually a girl or woman, not to be concerned about a matter, implying that they do not have the mental capacity to understand it.

   **Usage:** patronising or humorous

   **Example:** You've put the plug in the wrong way round again, but don't worry your pretty little head&#58; I'll fix it.
