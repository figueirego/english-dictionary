## Noun

### Forms

- my Ladies — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used in direct address to female peers of the realm, usually now excluding duchesses and, potentially, the Lords Spiritual.

   **Usage:** UK

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used in direct address to female bishops.

   **Usage:** UK

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used in direct address to the female Lord Mayors of certain important cities, including London and Canterbury.

   **Usage:** UK

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Used in direct address to certain female judges, particularly High Court Judges and above.

   **Usage:** UK
