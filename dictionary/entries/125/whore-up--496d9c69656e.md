### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To force or influence (someone) to be sexually promiscuous or to look like a prostitute.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To make oneself look sexually promiscuous or similar to a prostitute.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To pimp (someone).

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To offer or be willing to change (something) to make it popular in a way that constitutes selling out.

   **Usage:** broadly; figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** He's whoring up the movie in response to notes from the studio.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To work as a prostitute; to hire a prostitute.

   **Usage:** intransitive
