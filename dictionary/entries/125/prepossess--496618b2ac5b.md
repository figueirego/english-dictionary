### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Chiefly followed by by or with&#58; to preoccupy (someone) in an emotional or mental way, so as to preclude other things.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To cause (someone) to have a previous inclination against, for, or to something; to bias or prejudice; specifically, to induce in (someone) a favourable opinion beforehand, or at the outset.

   **Usage:** broadly; transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To cause (someone) to think a certain way.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To occupy or possess (something) beforehand.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Chiefly followed by of or with&#58; to cause (oneself) to obtain possession of something beforehand, or ahead of someone else.

   **Usage:** obsolete; reflexive; transitive; chiefly passive voice

   **Example:** to prepossess oneself of land
