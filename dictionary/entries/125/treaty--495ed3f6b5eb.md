### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A formal binding agreement concluded by subjects of international law, namely, states and international organizations; a convention, a pact.

   **Usage:** countable; international law

   **Example:** to sign a peace treaty

   **Example:** to write up a treaty touching climate change

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Chiefly in in treaty&#58; discussions or negotiations in order to reach an agreement.

   **Usage:** archaic; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Chiefly in private treaty&#58; an agreement or settlement reached following negotiations; a compact, a contract, a covenant.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The manner or process of treating someone or something; treatment; also, the manner in which someone or something acts or behaves; behaviour.

   **Usage:** obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The addressing or consideration of a subject; discussion, treatment.

   **Usage:** obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A formal, systematic discourse on some subject; a treatise.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. An act of beseeching or entreating; an entreaty, a plea, a request.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete
