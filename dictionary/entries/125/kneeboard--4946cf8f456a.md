### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A short (often less than 6'), broad (sometimes extremely broad) surfboard designed to be ridden in the surf in a kneeling position, and often having traction pads in the central area as an alternative to wax, to help the knees grip the deck.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports; surfing

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A clipboard carried in the lap of an airplane pilot, on which basic flight data is recorded.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** aeronautics; aerospace; aviation; business; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
