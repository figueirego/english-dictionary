## Noun

### Forms

- surrogate fathers — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A man who takes care of a child when one or more of the parents are unavailable (see also surrogate parent, foster parents).

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A man who acts as a father figure to a child usually when a father is not in the picture.
