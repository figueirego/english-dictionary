**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Usage:** Northern-England; Scotland; also; figuratively

   **Source synonyms:** fare; journey; sith (obsolete); trip

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

   **Usage:** Northern-England; Scotland

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

   **Usage:** Northern-England; Scotland

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

4. A journey to transport something between two places; a run; also, the quantity of items so transported.
