### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of exalting or raising high; also, the state of being exalted; elevation.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A feeling of happiness so intense as to be carried away by it; joyful pride bordering on ecstasy, often resulting from acclaim or triumph.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The refinement or subtilization of a body, or the increasing of its virtue or principal property.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. That placement of a planet in the zodiac in which it is deemed to exert its strongest influence.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astrology; human-sciences; mysticism; philosophy; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Apotheosis; becoming a god in the highest degree of glory after death.

   **Usage:** Mormonism; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A flock of doves, larks or skylarks.

   **Usage:** collective; countable; uncommon; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** exultation

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. An abnormal sense of personal well-being, power, or importance, observed as a symptom in various forms of insanity.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences
