### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The wing of a bat, or its shape.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Several South or Southeast Asian species of tailless dark swallowtail butterflies in the genus Atrophaneura.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An area of flabby fat under a person's arms.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Source synonyms:** bingo wings

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A long sleeve with a deep armhole, tapering toward the wrist.

   **Source synonyms:** magyar sleeve

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. One of a pair of swinging doors which typically do not lock nor cover the full vertical range of the doorway (leaving a large gap at the top and bottom), common especially in saloons.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A heart-shaped rollercoaster feature with two inversions.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. An exercise or posture on the stomach wherein a dumbbell row or lateral raise is performed.

   **Usage:** attributive; often
