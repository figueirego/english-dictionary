### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In the United States during 1961, a civil rights activist who participated in one or more trips by bus or other forms of transport, known as freedom rides, through parts of the southern U.S. to demonstrate opposition to racial prejudice and segregation.

   **Usage:** US; historical

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; politics
