### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The lowermost part of a doorway that one crosses to enter; a sill.

   **Source synonyms:** doorsill; doorstep

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An entrance; the door or gate of a house.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Source synonyms:** doorway; portal

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any end or boundary.

   **Usage:** broadly

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The outset of something; the point of entry, or the beginning of an action.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** on the threshold of maturity

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The start of the landing area of a runway.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** aeronautics; aerospace; aviation; business; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The quantitative point at which an action is triggered, especially a lower limit.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The wage or salary at which income tax becomes due.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. The point where one is mentally or physically vulnerable in response to a provocation or to other nuisances.
