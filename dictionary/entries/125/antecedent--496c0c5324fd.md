### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Any thing that precedes another thing, especially the cause of the second thing.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. An ancestor.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. A word, phrase or clause referred to by a pronoun or other pro-form.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** grammar; human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. The conditional part of a hypothetical proposition, i.e. p→q, where p is the antecedent, and q is the consequent.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; logic; mathematics; philosophy; sciences

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. The first of two subsets of a sequent, consisting of all the sequent's formulae which are valuated as true.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; logic; mathematics; philosophy; sciences

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. The first term of a ratio, i.e. the term a in the ratio a&#58;b, the other being the consequent.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

**Source sense R2.S7**

7. Previous principles, conduct, history, etc.

   **Usage:** in-plural
