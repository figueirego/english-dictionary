### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of many fish of the order Saccopharyngiformes that resemble eels, and which are sometimes treated as true eels, members of Anguilliformes.

   **Source synonyms:** saccopharyngiform

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of many fish of the order Saccopharyngiformes that resemble eels, and which are sometimes treated as true eels, members of Anguilliformes.
