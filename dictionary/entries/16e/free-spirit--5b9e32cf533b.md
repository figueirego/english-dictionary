### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who is a nonconformist or who does not recognize the authority of others.

   **Source synonyms:** eccentric; heteroclite; individualist; eccentric; free spirit; heteroclite; deviant; deviate; individualist; maverick; misfit; nonconformist; offbeat

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** historical
