### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A soft drink consisting of slightly carbonated apple juice to distinguish it from cider or hard cider, which is an alcoholic drink.

   **Usage:** US; countable; uncountable; trade term

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A carbonated alcoholic beverage based on apples, a champagne-style apple cider/hard cider/hard apple cider/cider

   **Usage:** Canada; countable; uncountable
