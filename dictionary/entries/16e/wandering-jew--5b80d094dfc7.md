### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A Jewish shoemaker who, in the Christian tradition, taunted Jesus Christ on the way to his crucifixion and for that was condemned to wander the Earth until the return of Jesus (ie. the second coming).
