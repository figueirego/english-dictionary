### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To find or learn something for the first time.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Turning the corner, I discovered a lovely little shop. I discovered that they sold widgets.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To remove the cover from; to uncover (a head, building etc.).

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To expose, uncover.

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive

   **Example:** The gust of wind discovered a bone in the sand.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To create by moving a piece out of another piece's line of attack.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** This move discovers an attack on a vital pawn.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** board-games; chess; games

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To question (a person) as part of discovery in a lawsuit.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To reveal (information); to divulge, make known.

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive

   **Example:** I discovered my plans to the rest of the team.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To reconnoitre, explore (an area).

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To manifest without design; to show; to exhibit.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive
