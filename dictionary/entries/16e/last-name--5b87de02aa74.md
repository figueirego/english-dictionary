### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Synonym of surname, a person's family name.

   **Source synonyms:** surname (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The last element of a full name, particularly when it could also be given name in East Asian and other cultural traditions.

   **Example:** In a structure typical for East Asia cultures, last names are usually used as given names. It usually differentiates that person from the other members of a group (typically a family or clan) who have a common family name
