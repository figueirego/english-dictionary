### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Resembling or characteristic of water.

   **Source synonyms:** aqueous; aquose; waterish; flowy; fluent; fluid; fluxive; free-flowing; liquid; mobile; runny; thin; watery

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Wet, soggy or soaked with water.

   **Source synonyms:** drenched; moist; waterlogged; bewatered; damp; drenched; dripping; drunk; humid; irriguous (archaic; poetic); madid (dated); moist; moisty; overwatered; plashy; saturated; soaked; sodden; soggy; soppy; spewy; spongy; squidgy (Britain); thoan (Britain; dialectal); thone (Britain; dialectal); undried; watered; waterlogged; watery; wet; wetter than an otter's pocket (informal); wiery (obsolete)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Diluted or having too much water.

   **Example:** watery coffee

   **Source synonyms:** wishy-washy

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Thin and pale therefore suggestive of water.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of light

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Weak and insipid.

   **Source synonyms:** bland; flavorless; bland; cardboard (figuratively); flaggy (obsolete); flavorless; ingustible; intastable; savorless; insipid; tame; gustless (obsolete); sapidless (archaic); mawkish (archaic); smackless; tasteless; unsavory; untasteable; vapid; wallowish (archaic); watery; waugh (British; dialectal); wearish (obsolete); wersh (British; dialectal)

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Discharging water or similar substance as a result of disease etc.

   **Example:** I took my cat to the vet because I was worried about his watery eyes.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Tearful.

   **Source synonyms:** lachrymose; moist; teary; cryey (rare); crysome; lachrymate (literary; rare); lachrymose; larmoyant (rare); maudlin (obsolete); moist; sobful (archaic); tearful; teary; watery; weepy

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Containing many bodies of water.
