### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A dot placed above a letter to indicate that it is a copying error and should be ignored.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** communications; journalism; literature; media; orthography; publishing; writing

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A dot placed above a consonant in Irish, when it is written in Gaelic script, to indicate that the consonant is lenited.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** communications; journalism; literature; media; orthography; publishing; writing
