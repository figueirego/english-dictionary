### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A triumphant shouting of great happiness; rejoicing, especially as celebratory outbursts; a feeling of joyous exultation.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** I knew it had been a victory when I heard the jubilations coming from that neighborhood.

   **Example:** The jubilation she felt when she received the award kept her grinning all week.

   **Source synonyms:** jubilance; jubilancy
