### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A style of humor marked by broad improbabilities with little regard to regularity or method.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A motion picture or play featuring this style of humor.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** The farce that we saw last night had us laughing and shaking our heads at the same time.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A situation abounding with ludicrous incidents.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** The first month of labor negotiations was a farce.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A ridiculous or empty show.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An elaborate lie.

   **Usage:** countable

**Source sense R1.S6**
