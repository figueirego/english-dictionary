### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To skim through a series of television stations, each for a brief moment, by repeated use of a remote control.

   **Example:** I channel-surfed for half an hour before deciding there was nothing worth watching.

   **Example:** To change the channel is one thing, but he was channel-surfing so much that I didn't even enjoy watching TV with him.

   **Source synonyms:** channel hop; zap
