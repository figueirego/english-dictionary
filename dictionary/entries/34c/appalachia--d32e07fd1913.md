### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A region in the eastern United States, covering a part of the Appalachian Mountains, that stretches from southwestern Pennsylvania to northern Alabama, usually excluding New York State and New England.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A town in Wise County, Virginia.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An island landmass occurring during most of the Late Cretaceous (100.5 to 66 million years ago) that now makes up the eastern half of North America.
