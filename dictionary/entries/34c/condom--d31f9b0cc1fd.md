### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A flexible sleeve made of latex or other impermeable material such as polyurethane or sheepskin, worn over an erect penis during sexual intercourse as a contraceptive or as a way to prevent the spread of STIs during PIV, anal, or oral.

   **Example:** buy a pack of condoms

   **Example:** flavoured condom

   **Example:** ribbed condoms

   **Source synonyms:** external condom; male condom

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of various forms of flexible sleeve or barrier, variously shaped for either penetrative or receptive body parts, made of latex or other impermeable material such as polyurethane or sheepskin, worn (by anyone with) during sexual activity to prevent pregnancy or the spread of STIs.

   **Usage:** broadly

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** condom; cum catcher (slang); rubber (Canada; US; slang)
