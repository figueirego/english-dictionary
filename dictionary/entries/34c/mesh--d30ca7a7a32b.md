## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /mɛʃ/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Forms

- meshes — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A structure made of connected strands of metal, fiber, or other flexible/ductile material, with evenly spaced openings between them.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The opening or space enclosed by the threads of a net between knot and knot, or the threads enclosing such a space.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The engagement of the teeth of wheels, or of a wheel and rack.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A measure of fineness (particle size) of ground material. A powder that passes through a sieve having 300 openings per linear inch but does not pass 400 openings per linear inch is said to be -300 +400 mesh.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A polygon mesh.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computer-graphics; computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. In mesh analysis&#58; a loop in an electric circuit (to which Kirchhoff's voltage law can be applied).

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; electrical-engineering; electricity; electromagnetism; electronics; energy; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A network topology with each device connected to multiple other devices in lieu of a central switch. Redundancy on a mesh network prevents single points of failure.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; networking; physical-sciences; sciences
