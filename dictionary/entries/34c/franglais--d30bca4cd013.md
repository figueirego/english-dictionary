### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. French terms or expressions recently borrowed from the English language.

   **Usage:** derogatory; often

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The poor French spoken by anglophones.

   **Usage:** derogatory; often

<a id="r1-s3"></a>
