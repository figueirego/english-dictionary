## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A child’s toy, consisting of a (usually plastic) bucket and spade, used for building simple sandcastles at the beach.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Denoting traditional seaside leisure activities.

   **Usage:** attributive

   **Example:** the bucket and spade brigade
