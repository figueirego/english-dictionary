### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To recognize someone or something as different from others based on its characteristics.

   **Source synonyms:** differentiate; discriminate; decern; discern; differentiate; differentialize (rare); discriminate; distinguish; set apart; tell; tell apart (idiomatic)

   **Source antonyms:** confuse

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To see someone or something clearly or distinctly.

   **Source synonyms:** discern; make out

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To make noticeably different or better from others through accomplishments.

   **Usage:** reflexive; transitive

   **Example:** The soldier distinguished himself in combat and received a medal.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To confer an honor upon.

   **Example:** The soldier was distinguished with a medal for his bravery.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To make to differ.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive
