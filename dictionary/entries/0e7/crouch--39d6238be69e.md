**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To bend down; to stoop low; to stand close to the ground with legs bent, like an animal when waiting for prey, or someone in fear.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** We crouched behind the low wall until the squad of soldiers had passed by.

   **Source synonyms:** huddle

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** intransitive
