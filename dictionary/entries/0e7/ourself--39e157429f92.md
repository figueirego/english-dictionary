### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The reflexive of the royal or editorial we&#58; myself (as used by one who is a monarch, writer or speaker when referring to oneself as we).

   **Usage:** first-person; pronoun; proscribed; reflexive; sometimes

   **Example:** In the present study, we will limit ourself to the simplest of cases.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The reflexive of the generic we&#58; oneself.

   **Usage:** first-person; pronoun; proscribed; reflexive; sometimes

   **Example:** We should love our neighbor as ourself.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Reflexive of "we" referring to an individual person being addressed, especially a person in the speaker's care.

   **Usage:** colloquial; first-person; form-of; proscribed; reflexive; sometimes

   **Example:** Hello Mrs Miggins. Did we manage to wash ourself this morning?

   **Example:** Well done Timmy! Did we make that model ourself?

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The reflexive of we&#58; ourselves.

   **Usage:** first-person; proscribed; sometimes; African-American Vernacular
