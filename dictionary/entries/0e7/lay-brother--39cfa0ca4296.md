**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A layman who is a member of a religious congregation or monastic order without the intention to become a priest.

   **Example:** In feudal days lay brothers were usually uneducated commoners, who did most menial, manual labor while the learned priestly monks held the more spiritual jobs.
