## Noun

### Forms

- chief petty officers — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A warrant officer belonging to one of the two classes of chief petty officer&#58; chief petty officer first class and chief petty officer second class.

   **Usage:** Canada; Navy

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A non-commissioned officer in the Royal Navy, ranking above petty officer and below warrant officer class 2.

   **Usage:** Navy; UK

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A non-commissioned officer in the United States Navy and United States Coast Guard, ranking above petty officer first class and below senior chief petty officer.

   **Usage:** Navy; US
