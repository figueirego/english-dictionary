### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A coupling that allows different parts of a machine not in line with each other some freedom of movement at the same time as transmitting rotary motion. Typical applications include driveshafts and socket wrenches.

   **Source synonyms:** U-joint; universal coupling; Cardan joint; Hardy-Spicer joint; Hooke's joint
