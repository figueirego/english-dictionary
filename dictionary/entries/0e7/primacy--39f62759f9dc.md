<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The state or condition of being prime or first, as in time, place, rank, preference etc.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** establish the primacy of something

   **Example:** acknowledge the primacy of something

   **Example:** assert primacy
