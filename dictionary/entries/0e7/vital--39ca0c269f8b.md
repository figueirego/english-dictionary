### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Relating to or characteristic of life.

   **Example:** vital energies; vital functions; vital actions

   **Source synonyms:** lifely

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Necessary to the continuation of life; being the seat of life; being that on which life depends.

   **Example:** The brain is a vital organ.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Invigorating or life-giving.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Necessary to continued existence.

   **Example:** The transition to farming was vital for the creation of civilisation.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Relating to the recording of life events.

   **Example:** Birth, marriage and death certificates are vital records.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Very important.

   **Example:** It is vital that you don't forget to do your homework.

   **Source synonyms:** crucial; necessary; significant; big; central; consequential; critical; crucial; epochful; essential; extraordinary; fateful; fatefraught (archaic; rare); fatesome (rare); heavy; pivotal; high on the totem pole; historic; innegligible; key; life-or-death; meaningful; necessary; noted; noticeable; observable; outstanding; paramount; pertinent; relevant; remarkable; salient; significant; unignorable; valuable; vital; [weighty](../01d/weighty--0775d5adecb9.md)

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Containing life; living.

   **Source synonyms:** extant; live; kicking; above ground (figuratively); alive; alive and kicking (idiomatic); alive and well; animate; animated; extant; kicking (slang); live; lively (archaic); livesome; living; quick (archaic); still with us; surviving; undeceased; vital; vitalic

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Lively, having vitality

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. Capable of living; in a state to live; viable.
