### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The distance from the base to the top of something.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** stature

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The distance from the base to the top of something.
   1. The vertical distance from the ground to the highest part of a standing person or animal (withers in the case of a horse).

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Example:** — What's your height? — 180 centimetres.

      **Source synonyms:** stature

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The distance from the base to the top of something.
   1. The minimum distance from a vertex of a triangle to (the extension of) the edge opposite, namely along a line perpendicular to the edge.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Example:** The area of a triangle is "a half base times height".

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences

      **Source synonyms:** stature; altitude

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The distance from the base to the top of something.
   1. The amplitude of a sinusoid.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

      **Source synonyms:** stature

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The distance of something above the ground or some other chosen level.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** We flew at a height of 15 000 meters.

   **Example:** I'm afraid of heights.

   **Source synonyms:** altitude

   **Source antonyms:** depth
