### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A common design for doors constructed from five separate components&#58; two vertical pieces (stiles), two horizontal pieces (rails) forming a frame, and a distinct center panel, creating a traditional look that allows for wood movement and different materials like MDF or wood veneer for the panel; often seen in Shaker styles.
