### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An instance of moving to live in another place for a while.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Seasonal moving of animals, as mammals, birds or fish, especially between breeding and non-breeding areas.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Movement in general.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The migration of lead from a can to the food inside it can cause lead poisoning.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Instance of changing a platform from an environment to another one.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The movement of cells in particular directions to specific locations.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biochemistry; biology; chemistry; microbiology; natural-sciences; physical-sciences
