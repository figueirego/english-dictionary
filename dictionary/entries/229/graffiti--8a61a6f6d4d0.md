### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Drawings or words drawn on a surface in a public place, usually made without authorization.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** The underpass is a popular place for graffiti artists.

   **Example:** The city council spends thousands of pounds removing graffiti from public buildings.
