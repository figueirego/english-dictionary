### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used to allude to something unsaid or hinted at.

   **Usage:** disjunctive

   **Example:** He seemed a little too interested in the details, if you know what I mean \[implying that perhaps he had ulterior motives\].

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used as a rhetorical question to confirm attention, understanding, or agreement at the end of an utterance or each segment thereof; synonym of do you know what I'm saying.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** Then you've got to jiggle it just right, if you know what I mean, before you can get it to come out of there.
