### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To remove the ions from; to deprive of ions.
   1. To cause (an ionized substance) to return to a neutral state; to convert (an ion) into a neutral atom or molecule.

      **Usage:** physical; transitive

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

      **Source synonyms:** unionize

      **Source antonyms:** ionize

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To remove the ions from; to deprive of ions.
   1. To remove the ionic constituents from (a liquid, e.g. water).

      **Usage:** physical; transitive

      **Example:** Water can be deionized using ion exchange resins.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To be deprived of ions.

   **Usage:** intransitive; physical

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To be deprived of ions.
