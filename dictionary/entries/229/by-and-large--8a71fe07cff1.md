## Adverb

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Mostly; generally; all things considered.

   **Usage:** focus; idiomatic; not-comparable

   **Example:** It was, by and large, an unexceptional presentation.

   **Source synonyms:** for the most part; generally; mostly; on the whole; usually; all in all; all things considered (idiomatic); altogether; broadly; as a rule (idiomatic); basically; by and large; chiefly; feckly (Scottish); for the most part (idiomatic); generally; in the large; in the main; largely; mainly; mostly; on the whole; overall; predominantly; pretty well; primarily; principally

   **Usage:** idiomatic; not-comparable; obsolete
