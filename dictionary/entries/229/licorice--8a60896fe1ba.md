### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A plant of species Glycyrrhiza glabra, or sometimes in North America, the related American licorice plant Glycyrrhiza lepidota.

   **Usage:** countable; usually

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A type of candy made from that plant's dried root or its extract.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source synonyms:** sugarallie (Scotland; informal)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A black color, named after the licorice.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A flavoring agent made from dried root portions of the aforementioned plant.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A supposed aphrodisiac made in the past from dried roots of Glycyrrhiza glabra and Glycyrrhiza echinata.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. An attractive woman.

   **Usage:** endearing; uncountable; usually
