### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A Y-shaped stick with an elastic sling between the arms used for shooting small projectiles.

   **Usage:** Australia; Philippines; US

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A stationary, often triangular object that launches any ball that hits its longest side back at a high force, now usually located above the flipper and between it and the inlane, with one each for both lower flippers.

   **Usage:** pinball

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A sling bikini.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An implement of a broad fabric worn around and connecting both bicipites brachii to support benching the most heavy weights.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The act of passing cars queuing in the left-hand lane and using the right-hand lane to go around a roundabout, exiting at the desired junction having avoided the queue.

   **Usage:** UK; slang

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** automotive; transport; vehicles

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Synonym of slingshot effect.

   **Source synonyms:** slingshot effect (synonym; synonym-of)
