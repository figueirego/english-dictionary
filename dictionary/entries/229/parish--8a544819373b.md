### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An administrative part of a diocese that has its own church.

   **Usage:** Anglicanism; Eastern Orthodoxy; Anglicanism; Eastern Orthodoxy

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Catholicism; Christianity; Roman-Catholicism

   **Source synonyms:** vicarate

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The community attending that church; the members of the parish.

   **Source synonyms:** parishioner

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An ecclesiastical society, usually not bounded by territorial limits, but composed of those persons who choose to unite under the charge of a particular priest, clergyman, or minister; also, loosely, the territory in which the members of a congregation live.

   **Usage:** US

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. In any of various countries, an administrative subdivision of an area, often of a county.
   1. A civil subdivision of a British county, often corresponding to an earlier ecclesiastical parish; a similar subdivision in Ireland.

      **Source synonyms:** civil parish; civil parish

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. In any of various countries, an administrative subdivision of an area, often of a county.
   1. An administrative subdivision in the U.S. state of Louisiana that is equivalent to a county in other U.S. states.

      **Source synonyms:** civil parish
