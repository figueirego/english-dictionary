### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Vocalisations made by birds, considered collectively.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** First a solitary blackbird started up, and soon the whole forest was alive with birdsong.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A vocalisation made by a bird, more complex than a call, for purposes associated with courtship and territory.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences; ornithology
