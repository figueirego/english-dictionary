### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A recorded sequence of images displayed on a screen at a rate sufficiently fast to create the appearance of motion; a film; a video.

   **Usage:** Australia; Canada; New-Zealand; US

   **Source synonyms:** flick; motion picture; moving picture; moving-picture show; photoplay; picture show; picture

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A professionally made motion picture of a substantive narrative work, typically around 1 to 3 hours long, typically intended for cinema, art, entertainment, and a widespread audience; a feature film, theatrical film.

   **Example:** My favorite actor is starring in the new movie.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A cinema; a movie theatre.

   **Usage:** Australia; Canada; New-Zealand; US; plural-normally

   **Example:** Let's go to the movies.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any event, especially one that is unpleasant or tiresome.

   **Usage:** figuratively; informal

   **Example:** Don't worry; this isn't the first time I've been through this movie.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An extremely fun and exciting experience.

   **Usage:** figuratively; slang

   **Example:** Every night with you is a movie!
