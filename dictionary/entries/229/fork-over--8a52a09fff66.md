### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To turn over (soil, hay, etc.) with a gardening fork.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I forked over half the allotment this morning.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To pay, give up or hand over a large amount of money.

   **Usage:** US; idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** Hundreds of spectators forked over the 70 bucks for tickets.
