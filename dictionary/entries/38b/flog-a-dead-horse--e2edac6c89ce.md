### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To attempt to get more out of something that cannot give more; to attempt to arouse fresh interest in something that is either hopeless or already settled.

   **Usage:** UK; idiomatic

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To attempt to get extra work out of a ship's crew during the dead horse period.

   **Usage:** dated

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport
