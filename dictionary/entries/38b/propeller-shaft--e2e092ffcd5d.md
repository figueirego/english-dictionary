## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The shaft which transmits the rotary motion from an engine to a propeller, as on a ship or aircraft.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. the shaft which connects the gearbox to the differential on the axle of a rear-wheel-drive or four-wheel-drive vehicle.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** automotive; transport; vehicles
