### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To lose strength and become weak; to be in a state of weakness or sickness.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To pine away in longing for something; to have low spirits, especially from lovesickness.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** He languished without his girlfriend.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To live in miserable or disheartening conditions.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** He languished in prison for years.

   **Source synonyms:** rot

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To be neglected; to make little progress, be unsuccessful.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The case languished for years before coming to trial.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To make weak; to weaken, devastate.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To affect a languid air, especially disingenuously.

   **Usage:** archaic; intransitive
