### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To restore a friendly relationship; to bring back or return to harmony.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** to reconcile people who have quarrelled

   **Example:** to wait until others have reconciled

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To make things compatible or consistent.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to reconcile differences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To make the net difference in credits and debits of a financial account agree with the balance.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** accounting; business; finance
