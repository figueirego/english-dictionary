## Noun · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person employed to gloss over a poor public image (or present it in a better light) in business and politics, especially after unfavourable results; a lobbyist; a PR person.

   **Example:** Many believed that the reduction in public spending was a disaster but the spin doctors presented it as a triumph for lower taxation.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; government; politics
