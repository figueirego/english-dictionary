### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In an equal manner; in equal shares or proportion; with equal and impartial justice; evenly.

   **Example:** All citizens are equally taxed.

   **Example:** The pie was divided equally among the guests.

   **Example:** They shared equally in the spoils.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** manner

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In equal degree or extent; just as.

   **Usage:** degree

   **Example:** The gas stations are equally far from the highway.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used to link two or more coordinate elements

   **Usage:** conjunctive

   **Example:** John suffered setbacks at his job. Equally, Frank's business slowed.
