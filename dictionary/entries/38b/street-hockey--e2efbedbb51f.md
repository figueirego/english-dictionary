## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An informal kind of hockey (field hockey) played on a quiet street.

   **Usage:** UK; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An informal version of hockey (ice hockey) played on a quiet street, with a ball instead of a puck.

   **Usage:** Canada; US; uncountable
