### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Something which portends or is perceived to portend either a good or evil event or circumstance in the future, or which causes a foreboding; a portent or augury.

   **Example:** The ghost's appearance was an ill omen.

   **Example:** A rise in imports might be an omen of economic recovery.

   **Example:** The egg has, during the span of history, represented mystery, magic, medicine, food and omen.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A thing of prophetic significance.

   **Example:** A sign of ill omen.
