### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The phase of the moon when it is in conjunction with the sun and is therefore obscured.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The moon when it is in conjunction with the sun.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

   **Source synonyms:** waxing moon
