### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A sudden thought, particularly one that solves a long-standing problem.

   **Usage:** US

   **Example:** I had been working on the problem for weeks, and then I had a brainstorm and saw that the solution was easy.

   **Source synonyms:** brainwave

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. A session of brainstorming, investigating a problem to try to find solutions.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. An unexpected mental error.

   **Usage:** UK
