### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A negative drug trip.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Example:** Normally those pills give me a boost, but last night they gave me a downer.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any drug that has depressant qualities.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Source antonyms:** upper

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Something or someone disagreeable, dispiriting or depressing; a killjoy.

   **Usage:** slang

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A livestock animal that has collapsed.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A form of industrial action in which workers down tools and refuse to work.

### Related words
