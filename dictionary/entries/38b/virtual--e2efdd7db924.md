### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In effect or essence, rather than in fact or reality; also, imitated, simulated.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** In fact a defeat on the battlefield, Tet was a virtual victory for the North, owing to its effect on public opinion.

   **Example:** Virtual addressing allows computer applications to believe that there is much more physical memory than actually exists.

   **Source synonyms:** de facto

   **Source antonyms:** actual; real; [true](../2d6/true--b5bea41b6c62.md); veritable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. For practical purposes, though not technically; almost complete, very near.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The angry peasants were a virtual army as they attacked the castle.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Operating using a computer and/or online rather than physically present.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** a virtual assistant; a virtual personal trainer

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

**Source sense R1.S4**

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences
