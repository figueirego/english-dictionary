### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To learn, or attempt to learn, the essence or nature of one's character and the aims or desires one pursues in life.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** When he was in his early twenties, he backpacked around Europe to find himself.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To unexpectedly or unintentionally begin to do or experience something.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** As you enter the cafe, you find yourself wondering why they decided to paint the entire room blue.

   **Example:** When news of his wife’s murder spread around the media, he found himself in front of a press conference explaining his actions.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To be in a particular state of mind.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** How do you find yourself this morning?

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To find (something) for oneself.

   **Usage:** literally

   **Example:** I need to find myself a boat.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To discover oneself to be in a particular place.

   **Usage:** literally

   **Example:** I got drunk and woke to find myself in the neighbour’s garden the next morning.
