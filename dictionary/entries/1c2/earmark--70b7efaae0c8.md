## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈɪ(ə)ɹˌmɑɹk/ — source labels: General-American

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A mark made or tag attached to the ear of an animal (chiefly a livestock animal), generally to identify or indicate something about the animal, such as ownership, health or gestation status, etc.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** agriculture; business; lifestyle

   **Source synonyms:** lugmark

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A distinguishing or identifying mark or sign; specifically (archaic), a mark of ownership.

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An act of designating certain funds to be used for a specific purpose.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Specifically, a designation by the Congress that certain federal funds be appropriated for a specific project.

   **Usage:** US; figuratively

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; politics

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To make a mark or attach a tag to the ear of (an animal, chiefly a livestock animal), generally to identify or indicate something about the animal, such as ownership.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** agriculture; business; lifestyle

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To put a distinguishing or identifying mark or sign on (something); to make (something) distinctive or identifiable.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To designate or set aside (someone or something) for a particular purpose; to allocate.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** allot; appropriate; allocate; appropriate; shelve; assign; dedicate; detach; designate; earmark; preselect; put aside; secern (archaic); secernate (archaic); separate (obsolete); sepose (obsolete); sequester; set apart; set aside

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. Chiefly followed by for&#58; to designate (certain funds) to be used for a specific purpose.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive
