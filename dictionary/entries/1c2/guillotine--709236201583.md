### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A machine used for the application of capital punishment by decapitation, consisting of a tall upright frame from which is suspended a heavy diagonal-edged blade which is dropped onto the neck of the person to be executed; also, execution using this machine.

   **Usage:** also; figuratively; historical

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A device or machine with a cutting blade.
   1. A device used for cutting the pages of books, stacks of paper, etc., to straight edges, usually by means of a hinged or sliding blade attached to a flat platform.

      **Usage:** broadly

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A device or machine with a cutting blade.
   1. An instrument with a sliding blade for cutting the tonsils, uvula, or other body parts.

      **Usage:** broadly

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences; surgery

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A parliamentary procedure for fixing the dates when various stages of discussion of a bill must end, to ensure that the enactment of the bill proceeds expeditiously.

   **Usage:** UK; broadly; informal

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; law; politics

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A legislative motion that debate be ended and a vote taken; a cloture.

   **Usage:** US; broadly; informal

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; law; politics

   1. To execute (someone) with a guillotine.
