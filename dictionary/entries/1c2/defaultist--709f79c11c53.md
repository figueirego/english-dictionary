## Noun · Record 2

### Forms

- defaultists — plural

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. One who believes that scalar implicatures arise from a default association with a word, rather than arising from contextual inference.

   **Example:** A defaultist believes that the default interpretation of the phrase "some eels are fish" is the pragmatic interpretation "some, but not all, eels are fish".

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; pragmatics; sciences

   **Source antonyms:** contextualist
