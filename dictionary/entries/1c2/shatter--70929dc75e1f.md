### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of brittle solid materials (like glass or ice), to violently break into pieces.

   **Usage:** ergative

   **Example:** The miners used dynamite to shatter rocks.

   **Example:** a high-pitched voice that could shatter glass

   **Example:** The old oak tree has been shattered by lightning.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To destroy, disable.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To dispirit or emotionally defeat.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to be shattered in intellect

   **Example:** to have shattered hopes

   **Example:** to have a shattered constitution

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Of seeds&#58; to disperse (become dispersed) upon ripening.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** agriculture; business; lifestyle

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To scatter about.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To fall sometimes connoting hard, as if to smash something, other times light and dispersed.

   **Usage:** intransitive
