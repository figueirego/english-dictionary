## Adjective · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Making no significant difference.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** It's all the same to me if you want to leave early.

   **Example:** Certainly, many women have found me interesting. All the same, the women closest to me are invariably deeply dissatisfied.

   **Source synonyms:** all one

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see all, same.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** His speeches are all the same.
