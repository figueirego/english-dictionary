### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A sensation of dryness in the throat associated with a craving for liquids, produced by deprivation of drink, or by some other cause (such as fear, excitement, etc.) which stops the secretion of the pharyngeal mucous membrane.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** We developed a great thirst during our hike.

   **Example:** He had quite a thirst on and so headed to his favourite Dublin pub.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The condition producing the sensation of thirst.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** If you go out in the desert for long without water, the thirst can drive you mad.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A want and eager desire (for something); a craving or longing.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

   **Example:** She had a real thirst for news and that greatly helped her career.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Sexual lust.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

   **Example:** He had a thirst for her and she had the same for him.

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** craving (figuratively); longing (figuratively)
