### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who, or that which, scorches.

   **Usage:** literally

   **Source synonyms:** sizzler

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A very hot day.

   **Usage:** colloquial

   **Example:** Tomorrow will be a scorcher, so carry water and use sunscreen if you're going out.

   **Source synonyms:** sizzler; stinker

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A very good goal, notably made with a very hard shot.

   **Example:** What a scorcher! See the net reverberate!

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; games; hobbies; lifestyle; soccer; sports

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

   **Usage:** colloquial

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

   **Usage:** colloquial; dated

<a id="r1-s6"></a>
