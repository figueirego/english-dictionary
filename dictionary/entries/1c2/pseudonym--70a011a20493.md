### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A fictitious name (more literally, a false name), as those used by writers and movie stars.

   **Example:** The Reverend Charles Lutwidge Dodgson wrote "Alice's Adventures in Wonderland" under the pseudonym Lewis Carroll.

   **Source synonyms:** anonym; byname; to-name
