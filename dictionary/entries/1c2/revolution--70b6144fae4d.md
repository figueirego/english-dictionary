### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A political upheaval in a government or state characterized by great change.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** The revolution had completely transformed the monarchy into a republic.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; politics

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The popular removal and replacement of a government, especially by sudden violent action.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; politics

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Rotation&#58; the turning of an object around an axis, one complete turn of an object during rotation.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** rev; wheel

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. In the case of celestial bodies, the traversal of one body along an orbit around another body.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** It takes 365 days for the Earth to complete one revolution around the sun.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astronomy; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A sudden, vast change in a situation, a discipline, or the way of thinking and behaving.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** the AI revolution

   **Source antonyms:** evolution

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A round of periodic changes, such as between the seasons of the year.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Consideration of an idea; the act of revolving something in the mind.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. a rule in Tycoon where if four cards are placed down at once, the card hierarchy is reversed.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** card-games; games

   **Source antonyms:** counterrevolution
