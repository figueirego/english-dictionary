### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A rabbit, especially the European rabbit, Oryctolagus cuniculus (formerly known as Lepus cuniculus).

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Rabbit fur.

   **Usage:** UK; dialectal

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Locally for other rabbit-like or hyrax-like animals, such as the Cape hyrax (das, dassie) or the pika (Ochotona princeps, formerly Lagomys princeps).

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Locally for other rabbit-like or hyrax-like animals, such as the Cape hyrax (das, dassie) or the pika (Ochotona princeps, formerly Lagomys princeps).
   1. Used in the Old Testament as a translation of Hebrew שָׁפָן (shafán), thought to be the rock hyrax (Procavia capensis, syn. Hyrax syriacus).

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A simpleton; one who may be taken in by a cony-catcher.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. An edible West Indian fish, a grouper given in different sources as&#58; Epinephelus apua, the hind of Bermuda; nigger-fish, Epinephelus punctatus; Cephalopholis fulva.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Any of certain members of family Epinephelidae of Atlantic groupers, such as mutton hamlets, graysby, Cuban coneys, and rooster hinds.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A burbot.

   **Usage:** UK; dialectal

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A woman; a sweetheart.

   **Usage:** obsolete
