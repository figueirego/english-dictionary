**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A country located primarily in North America, stretching from the Atlantic to the Pacific Ocean and consisting of fifty states (including Alaska and Hawaii) and the District of Columbia, while asserting sovereignty over several territories; short form United States; abbreviation USA. Capital&#58; Washington, D.C.. Largest city&#58; New York City.
