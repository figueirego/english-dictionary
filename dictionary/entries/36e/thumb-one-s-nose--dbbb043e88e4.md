### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To place a thumb upon the tip of the nose, typically with the fingers spread and while simultaneously wiggling one's fingers, in a gesture of disrespect.

   **Example:** My brother thumbed his nose in reply to my snide remarks.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To act disrespectfully, especially by flouting the object of disrespect.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** Richard Feynman delighted in thumbing his nose at the dissidents and workers.
