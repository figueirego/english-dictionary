### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A pair of yellow lines painted at the side of a road to show that parking is not permitted at any time.

   **Usage:** UK

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
