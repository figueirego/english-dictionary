   **Usage:** informal

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Indicating surprise at, or requesting confirmation of, some new information; to express skepticism.
   1. A phatic confirmation by a listener, to signal active listening; mostly devoid of literal meaning, with the phatically contrived incredulity being merely formulaic.

      **Usage:** broadly; informal

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. Indicating that what was just said was obvious and unnecessary; contrived incredulity.

   **Usage:** informal; sarcastic; typically exaggerated question

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. Indicating affirmation, agreement.

   **Usage:** US; informal

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. Indicating displeasure at another person's behaviour or statement.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** Well, really! How rude.
