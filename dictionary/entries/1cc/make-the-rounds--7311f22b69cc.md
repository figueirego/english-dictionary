### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To circulate from one place to another.

   **Example:** The naked photos of that celebrity have been making the rounds in the tabloids.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To go from one place to another for a particular reason.

   **Example:** The doctors at the hospital check on the patients when they make the rounds at nine o'clock.
