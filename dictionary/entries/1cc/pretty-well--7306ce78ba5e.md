## Adverb

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Synonym of pretty much (“almost completely; very nearly; nearly completely”).

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** That scene earlier pretty well destroyed my chances of getting the job.

   **Source synonyms:** pretty much (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see pretty, well.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** She plays the piano pretty well, having studied for six years.
