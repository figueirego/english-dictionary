## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A hoof, claw, or talon.

   **Usage:** dated

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A section of a cylinder, cone, or other solid of revolution, cut off by a plane oblique to the base; so called from its resemblance to the hoof of a horse.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Synonym of claw (“slender appendage or process at the base of a petal”).

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences

   **Source synonyms:** unguis; claw (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A surgical instrument for use in removing a dead fetus.
