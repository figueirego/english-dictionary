**Source sense R2.S1**

   **Source synonyms:** aficionado; enthusiast; connoisseur

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. A player on a team who primarily employs one particular skill or takes one particular role.

   **Source antonyms:** all-rounder

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. Any of several non-commissioned ranks corresponding to that of corporal.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war
