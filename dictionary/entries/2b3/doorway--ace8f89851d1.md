### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The passage of a door; a door-shaped entrance into a house or a room.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** I stood by the doorway to watch her go.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An opening or passage in general.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively

   **Example:** Soon after dawn, he came to the doorway of my tent.
