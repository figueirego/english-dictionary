### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of grain, fruit, flowers, etc., to grow ripe; to become mature.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Grapes ripen in the sun.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To approach or come to perfection.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To cause (something) to mature; to make ripe.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The warm sun ripened the corn.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To mature; to fit or prepare; to bring to perfection.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** ripen the judgment
