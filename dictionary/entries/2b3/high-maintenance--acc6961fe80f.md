### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Requiring a high degree of maintenance to ensure proper functioning, and without which it is likely to break down.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a system

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Requiring a lot of attention or reassurance; emotionally needy

   **Usage:** derogatory; figuratively

   **Example:** He has this incredibly high-maintenance girlfriend; if he doesn't tell her that he loves her every five minutes, she tends to break down into hysterical depressive weepy fits.
