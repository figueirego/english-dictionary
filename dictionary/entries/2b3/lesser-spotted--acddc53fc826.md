### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Part of the name of some (often fictional) species of animal, used as a placeholder for some obscure or rare species that is of little interest or importance.

   **Usage:** humorous; not-comparable

   **Example:** She's traipsed off to Borneo to see the lesser spotted pygmy owl, or something like that.
