## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The quality of being subtle.
   1. The quality of being scarcely noticeable or difficult to discern. (of things)

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Example:** the subtlety of the Mona Lisa’s smile

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The quality of being subtle.
   1. The quality of being done in a clever way that is not obvious or not direct; the quality of being carefully thought out. (of things)

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Example:** the subtlety of a writer’s analysis / of a singer’s phrasing

      **Source synonyms:** refinement

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The quality of being subtle.
   1. The quality of being able to achieve one's aims through clever, delicate or indirect methods. (of people)

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Example:** With all his usual subtlety, he quietly fixed the problem before anyone else noticed it.

      **Source synonyms:** discretion; finesse; savoir-faire

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The quality of being subtle.
   1. The quality of being able to notice or understand things that are not obvious. (of people)

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source synonyms:** acumen; perceptiveness; perspicacity

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An instance of being subtle, a subtle thing, especially a subtle argument or distinction.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** The subtleties of this overture are often overlooked.

   **Source synonyms:** nicety; nuance

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. An ornate medieval illusion dish or table decoration, especially when made from one thing but crafted to look like another.

   **Usage:** countable; historical

   **Example:** At the king's coronation feast, several subtleties were served between main courses.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The quality of being clever in surreptitious or deceitful behaviour; an act or argument that shows this quality.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** artifice; craftiness; cunning; deceitfulness; slyness; trickery

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A trick that creates a false appearance.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete

   **Source synonyms:** deception; illusion

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. The property of having a low density or thin consistency.

   **Usage:** obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. The property of being able to penetrate materials easily.

   **Usage:** obsolete; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** penetrancy; piercingness
