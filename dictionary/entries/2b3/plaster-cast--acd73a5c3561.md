## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A cast made of a stiff material (often plaster of Paris and gauze) in order to immobilize a broken bone for the period of healing.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences; surgery

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A copy of a piece of art or other object cast in plaster.

   **Example:** The police got a plaster cast of the suspect's footprint.
