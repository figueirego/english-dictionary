### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An amount that can be picked up by one foot.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A quantity (of something) that covers the foot.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The amount of pressure that can be exerted by pressing with one foot.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A complete set of toes for one foot.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A small number, about five.

   **Usage:** humorous

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. As much as one can handle, especially of something handled with one's feet.

   **Usage:** humorous
