### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The kind, condition, and/or the set of characteristics of something, as opposed to the quantity of something.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** The lattice structure of a steel profoundly changes its qualities.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A single trait.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** While being impulsive can be great for artists, it is not a desirable quality for engineers.

   **Example:** Security, stability, and efficiency are good qualities of an operating system.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Good characteristics in particular; level of excellence; freedom of faults and flaws.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** This school is well-known for having teachers of high quality.

   **Example:** Quality of life is usually determined by health, education, and income.

   **Source synonyms:** duty (usually); **Source context (verbatim):** used in combination

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Good worth, good make.

   **Usage:** attributive; countable; uncountable; occasionally proscribed

   **Example:** For this building project we need to use quality materials that will last for many decades.

   **Example:** That was a quality game by Jim Smith.

   **Example:** We had some quality time yesterday!

   **Example:** A quality system ensures products meet customer requirements.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Position; status; rank.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. High social position. (See also the quality.)

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** A peasant is not allowed to fall in love with a lady of quality.

   **Example:** Membership of this golf club is limited to those of quality and wealth.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. In a two-phase liquid–vapor mixture, the ratio of the mass of vapor present to the total mass of the mixture.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics; thermodynamics

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. The third step in OPQRST where the responder investigates what the NOI/MOI feels like.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** To identify quality try asking, "what does it feel like?".

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** emergency-medicine; medicine; sciences

**Source sense R1.S9**
