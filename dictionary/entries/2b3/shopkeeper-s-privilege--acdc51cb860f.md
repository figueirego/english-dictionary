## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A law in the United States, according to which a shopkeeper can detain a suspected shoplifter on store property for a reasonable amount of time, as long as the shopkeeper has cause to believe that the detainee actually committed, or attempted to commit, theft of store property.

   **Usage:** uncountable
