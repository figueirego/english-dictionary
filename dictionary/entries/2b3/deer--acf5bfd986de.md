### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A ruminant mammal with hooves and often antlers, of the family Cervidae, or one of several similar animals from related families of the order Artiodactyla, such as the musk deer or mouse deer.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   1. Such a mammal that is in the family Cervidae.

      **Usage:** countable

      **Source context (verbatim):** in particular

      **Source synonyms:** true deer; cervid; cervine

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A ruminant mammal with hooves and often antlers, of the family Cervidae, or one of several similar animals from related families of the order Artiodactyla, such as the musk deer or mouse deer.
   1. Such a mammal that is in the family Cervidae.
