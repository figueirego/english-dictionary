### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make a direct impact, especially if violent.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** When a body collides with another, then momentum is conserved.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To come into conflict, or be incompatible.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** China collided with the modern world.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To meet; to come into contact.

   **Usage:** intransitive; poetic

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To cause to collide.

   **Usage:** transitive
