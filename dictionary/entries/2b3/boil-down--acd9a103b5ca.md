### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To reduce in volume by boiling.

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** He boiled the soup down so it wouldn't be so weak.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To become reduced (to the most central elements or ingredients&#58; to the essence, core, or implication for action).

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** So what this boils down to is that you still owe me that fifty bucks.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** My dissertation is 342 pages long, and I'm required to boil it down to a one-page abstract?!
