### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To discharge; to end the employment or service of.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The company dismissed me after less than a year.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To order to leave.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The soldiers were dismissed after the parade.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To dispel; to rid one’s mind of.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He dismissed all thoughts of acting again.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To reject; to refuse to accept.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The court dismissed the case.

   **Example:** She dismissed the idea as ridiculous.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To invalidate; to treat as unworthy of serious consideration.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** By telling the victim to "get over it", the listener dismissed the victim's feelings.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To send or put away, to discard with disregard, contempt or disdain. (sometimes followed by as).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** She dismissed him with a wave of the hand.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To get a batsman out.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He was dismissed for 99 runs.
