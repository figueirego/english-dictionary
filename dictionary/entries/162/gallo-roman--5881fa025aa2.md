## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

- Gallo-Romans — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A Romanized Gaul, i.e. a Gaulish person who adopted or adapted the culture, language etc. of the Roman Empire following the establishment of Roman rule in the region of Gaul.

   **Usage:** historical
