### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To look fixedly (at something).

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** She found him staring with raised eyebrows at her.

   **Source context (verbatim):** followed by "at"

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To influence in some way by looking fixedly.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to stare a timid person into submission

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To be very conspicuous on account of size, prominence, colour, or brilliancy.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** staring windows or colours

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To stand out; to project; to bristle.

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete
