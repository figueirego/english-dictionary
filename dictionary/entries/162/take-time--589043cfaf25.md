### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To require a comparatively long period of time.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** Learning a foreign language takes time.

   **Example:** It takes time to get used to having a step-parent.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To volunteer to spend one's time (for a purpose or beneficiary).

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** He took time to help us with his son with his homework.

   **Example:** He took time for his son.
