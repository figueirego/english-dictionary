### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Official designation for currency in some parts of the world, including Canada, the United States, Australia, New Zealand, Singapore, Hong Kong, and elsewhere. Its symbol is $.

   **Source synonyms:** buck; smackeroo

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Money generally.

   **Usage:** broadly

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A ringgit, a unit of currency in Malaysia.

   **Usage:** Malaysia; broadly; colloquial

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A quarter of a pound or one crown, historically minted as a coin of approximately the same size and composition as a then-contemporary dollar coin of the United States, and worth slightly more.

   **Usage:** UK; colloquial; historical

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Imported from the United States, and paid for in U.S. dollars. (Note&#58; distinguish "dollar wheat", North American farmers' slogan, meaning a market price of one dollar per bushel.)

   **Usage:** attributive; historical

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A unit of reactivity equal to the interval between delayed criticality and prompt criticality.

   **Usage:** nuclear physics
