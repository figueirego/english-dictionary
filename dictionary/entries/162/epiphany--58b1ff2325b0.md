### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A manifestation of the divinity of Jesus Christ, especially to the Magi (Matthew 2&#58;1–12), but also at his baptism and the Wedding at Cana.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An annual Christian feast celebrating these events, usually on January 6, the twelfth day after Christmas.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity

   **Source synonyms:** Three Kings' Day; Twelfth Day; Twelfthtide; Twelvetide; Theophany; **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Eastern-Christianity; Christianity

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The day of the celebration, January 6, or sometimes (in Western Christianity), the Sunday between January 2 and 8.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The season or time of the Christian church year, either from the Epiphany feast day to Shrove Tuesday (the day before Ash Wednesday, the start of Lent), or from the Epiphany feast day to the feast of Candlemas (marking the presentation of Jesus Christ in the Temple in Jerusalem).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. an experience of sudden and striking insight

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity; human-sciences; psychology; sciences
