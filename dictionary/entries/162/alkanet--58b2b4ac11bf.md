### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any member of the species Alkanna tinctoria, a plant whose root is used as a red dye.
   1. The dyeing matter extracted from the roots of the plant with water or oil, used for cotton especially but also silk and wool, giving a deep red colour.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source synonyms:** orchanet; orcanette; dyer's alkanet; bugloss; dyers' bugloss; Spanish bugloss; Languedoc bugloss

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any member of the species Alkanna tinctoria, a plant whose root is used as a red dye.
   1. Other plants of the genus Alkanna.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source synonyms:** orchanet; orcanette; dyer's alkanet; bugloss; dyers' bugloss; Spanish bugloss; Languedoc bugloss
