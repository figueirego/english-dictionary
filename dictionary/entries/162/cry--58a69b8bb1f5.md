### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To shed tears; to weep, especially in anger or sadness.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** That sad movie always makes me cry.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To utter loudly; to call out; to declare publicly.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To shout, scream, yell.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To forcefully attract attention or proclaim one’s presence.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To utter inarticulate sounds, as animals do.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source synonyms:** call

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To cause to do something, or bring to some state, by crying or weeping.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Tonight I’ll cry myself to sleep.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To make oral and public proclamation of; to notify or advertise by outcry, especially things lost or found, goods to be sold, auctioned, etc.

   **Example:** to cry goods

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To make oral and public proclamation of; to notify or advertise by outcry, especially things lost or found, goods to be sold, auctioned, etc.
   1. Hence, to publish the banns of, as for marriage.
