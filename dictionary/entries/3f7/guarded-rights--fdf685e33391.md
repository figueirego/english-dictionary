### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Those rights laid down by a constitution as being guaranteed to all individuals in the society; human rights.

   **Usage:** plural; plural-only

   **Example:** At the time, persons thought that if the fledgling government designed an exclusive list of guarded rights, then whatever rights were not on the list would be compromised by the authorities.
