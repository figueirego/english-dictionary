### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An inland county of England bordered by Gloucestershire, Somerset, Dorset, Oxfordshire, Hampshire and Berkshire.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A unitary authority of the county of Wiltshire, England which includes all of the traditional county except Swindon, a separate unitary authority.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually
