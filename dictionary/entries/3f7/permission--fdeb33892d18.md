**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Sire, do I have your permission to execute this traitor?

   **Source synonyms:** allowance; license; permit

   **Source antonyms:** prohibition

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The act of permitting.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Flags or access control lists pertaining to a file that dictate who can access it, and how.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** I used the "chmod" command to change the file's permission.
