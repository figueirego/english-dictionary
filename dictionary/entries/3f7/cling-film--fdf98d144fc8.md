### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A plastic film (originally polyvinylidene chloride but now polyethylene) that is so light and thin that it clings to any object about which it is wrapped; used to cover and preserve foodstuffs etc.

   **Usage:** Philippines; UK; countable; rare; uncountable
