## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A skin condition in which accumulation of sebum, keratin, sweat, dirt and debris leads to a localized patch of skin discoloration or a wart-like plaque, caused by inadequate cleansing and/or exfoliation of a body part.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** dermatitis neglecta
