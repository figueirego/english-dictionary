### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A stalemate, or a confrontation among two or more sides that no side can win.

   **Usage:** slang

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A stalemate, or a confrontation among two or more sides that no side can win.
   1. An inconclusive standoff, ending in mutual retreat.

      **Usage:** slang

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A stalemate, or a confrontation among two or more sides that no side can win.
   1. A confrontation among two or more armed parties, none of which wants to attack first (fearing that the other could retaliate), but neither of which will disarm (for fear the other will attack).

      **Usage:** slang

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A stalemate, or a confrontation among two or more sides that no side can win.
   1. A three-way or more standoff.

      **Usage:** slang

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A stalemate, or a confrontation among two or more sides that no side can win.
   1. A pot that is split among the players because of a tie.

      **Usage:** slang
