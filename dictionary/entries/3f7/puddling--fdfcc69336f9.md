## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The action of forming a puddle.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The process of working clay, loam, pulverized ore, etc., with water, to render it compact, or impervious to liquids.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The act of lining a canal with puddle to make it watertight.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; canal engineering

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The processing of pig iron (with high carbon content) by placing it into a reverberatory furnace, stirring to expose it to oxygen and create puddled iron and then (with further stirring to fully decarbonize it) wrought iron.

   **Usage:** countable; historical; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; metallurgy; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A group of mallards (ducks).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A behaviour in which animals like butterflies seek out moist substances to obtain nutrients.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
