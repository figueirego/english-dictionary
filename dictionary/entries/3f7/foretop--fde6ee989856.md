### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The top of the head; the top of the forehead.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The lock of hair which grows on top of the forehead; the corresponding part of a wig.

   **Usage:** obsolete

**Source sense R1.S3**
