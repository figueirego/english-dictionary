### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The tendency to reduce a religion to its most fundamental tenets, based on strict interpretation of core texts.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** lifestyle; religion

   **Source synonyms:** bibliolatry

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The belief that fundamental financial quantities are the best predictor of the price of a financial instrument.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance
