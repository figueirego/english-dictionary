### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Secret; covert; undisclosed.

   **Usage:** adjectival

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Secretly; covertly; undisclosedly.

   **Usage:** adverbial

   **Example:** I was paid under the table, so I didn't have to pay taxes on the income but I couldn't complain when I was cheated.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. In or into a state of stupification or boredom.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. In or into a state of drunkenness.

   **Source synonyms:** under the mahogany

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see under, table.
