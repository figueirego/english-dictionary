<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who advocates military force to an ardent degree and (usually also) advocates the militarization of society.

   **Example:** It is widely agreed that rule by intransigent militarists led the Empire of Japan to ruin.

   **Source synonyms:** bellicist; hawk; warmonger; warnik; jingoist

   **Source antonyms:** pacifist; conscientious objector

<a id="r1-s2"></a>
