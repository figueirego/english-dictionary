<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of a person, look, or behavior&#58; without emotion; distant; (sometimes) silently disapproving.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** I don't know why she gave me an ice-cold look — I had thought that the topic of conversation was totally innocuous.

   **Source synonyms:** cold as ice; icy; frigid; stone cold; frosty

**Source sense R1.S3**
