### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used as a polite formula to ask for a person's name, or to request that they join one in a dance, etc.

   **Example:** May I have the pleasure? \[ = an offer to dance\]

   **Example:** May I have the pleasure of this dance?

   **Example:** May I have the pleasure of your company on the dance floor?

   **Example:** I'm sorry, but I don't believe I've had the pleasure. \[ = I don't recall having met you or having been introduced to you before\]

   **Example:** I'm sorry, but I don't believe I've had the pleasure of meeting you yet.
