### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A fundamental, generic term used when referring to the measurement (count, amount) of a scalar, vector, number of items or to some other way of denominating the value of a collection or group of items.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** You have to choose between quantity and quality.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An indefinite amount of something.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Some soap making oils are best as base oils, used in a larger quantity in the soap, while other oils are best added in a small quantity.

   **Example:** Olive oil can be used practically in any quantity.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A specific measured amount.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** This bag would normally cost $497.50 for a quantity of 250, at a price of $1.99 per piece.

   **Example:** Generally it should not be used in a quantity larger than 15 percent.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A considerable measure or amount.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The Boeing P-26A was the first all-metal monoplane fighter produced in quantity for the U.S. Army Air Corps.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Property of a phenomenon, body, or substance, where the property has a magnitude that can be expressed as number and a reference.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** metrology

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Indicates that the entire preceding expression is henceforth considered a single object.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** x plus y quantity squared equals x squared plus 2xy plus y squared.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Length of sounds.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; phonology; sciences
