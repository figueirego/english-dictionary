### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having a secondary, supplementary or subordinate function by accompanying as a subordinate; aiding in a secondary way; being additional; contributing or being contributory.

   **Example:** accessory garments

   **Example:** Surprise parties succeed with the help of accessory neighbors or colleagues.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Assisting a crime without actually participating in committing the crime itself; being connected as an incident or subordinate to a principal.

   **Example:** Her actions were clearly accessory because they helped him commit and probably also conceal the crime.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Present in a minor amount, and not essential.

   **Example:** accessory mineral

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Supernumerary and generally nonfunctional.

   **Example:** She had an accessory finger on one hand.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; medicine; natural-sciences; sciences

## Noun · Etymology 2 · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /əkˈsɛs.(ə.)ɹi/ — source labels: General-American; Received-Pronunciation
- IPA: /ækˈsɛs.(ə.)ɹi/ — source labels: General-American; Received-Pronunciation
- IPA: /əˈsɛs.(ə.)ɹi/ — source labels: proscribed

### Forms

- accessories — plural
- accessary — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Something that belongs to part of another main thing; something additional and subordinate, an attachment.

   **Example:** the accessories of a mobile phone

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. An article that completes one's basic outfit, such as a scarf or gloves.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** fashion; lifestyle

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. A person who is not present at a crime, but contributes to it as an assistant or instigator.

   **Example:** She was an accessory to the murder because she helped him commit and probably also conceal the crime.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. Something in a work of art without being indispensably necessary, for example solely ornamental parts.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** art; arts
