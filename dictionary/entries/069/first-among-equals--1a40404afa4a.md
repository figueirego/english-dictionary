### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person or position that is formally of equal rank to others in a group, but nonetheless takes a leading role within that group.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** The Orthodox consider the bishop of Rome to have always been merely a first among equals.
