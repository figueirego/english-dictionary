### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A stripe on each side of the head of an animal that includes the eye, and is of a different colour from the rest of the head.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences; zoology

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
