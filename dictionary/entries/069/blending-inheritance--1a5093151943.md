## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The obsolete theory that progeny inherits any characteristic as the average of the parents' values, so for example crossing a red and a white variety of flower would yield pink-flowered offspring.

   **Usage:** historical; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences
