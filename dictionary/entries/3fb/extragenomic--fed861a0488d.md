### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Describing any inherited genetic information that is not part of an organism's genome

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Plasmids are small circles of extragenomic DNA that exists and replicates independently from the bacterial chromosome.
