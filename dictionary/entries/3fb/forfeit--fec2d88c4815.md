<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To suffer the loss of something by wrongdoing or non-compliance

   **Example:** He forfeited his last chance of an early release from jail by repeatedly attacking another inmate.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To lose a contest, game, match, or other form of competition by voluntary withdrawal, by failing to attend or participate, or by violation of the rules

   **Example:** Because only nine players were present, the football team was forced to forfeit the game.

   **Source synonyms:** capitulate; surrender; disqualify; forgo; withgo
