## Phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see guess, what.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used to introduce a surprising outcome or one that the hearer is not expected to try to guess.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; by ellipsis; rhetoric question; by ellipsis; rhetoric question

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used to dramatize the introduction of an unsurprising outcome.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I've only had two job interviews and – guess what – no job offers.
