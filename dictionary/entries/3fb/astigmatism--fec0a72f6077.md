### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A defect of a lens such that light rays coming from a point do not meet at a focal point so that the image is blurred.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; natural-sciences; optics; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A disorder of the vision, usually due to a misshapen cornea, such that light does not focus correctly on the retina causing a blurred image.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; pathology; sciences
