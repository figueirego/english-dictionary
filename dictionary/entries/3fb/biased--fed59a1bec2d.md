### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Exhibiting bias; prejudiced.

   **Example:** The newspaper gave a biased account of the incident.

   **Example:** Obviously I'm biased, but…

   **Source synonyms:** partial; prejudiced; tendentious

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Example:** a biased coin

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Angled at a slant.

   **Example:** The table had a biased edge.
