### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Excessively eager and attentive to please or to obey instructions; fawning, subservient, servile.

   **Source synonyms:** fawning; ingratiating; servile; slavish; sycophantic; truckling; smarmy; asskissing; asslicky; fawning; greasy; groveling; ingratiating; obsequious; oily; oleaginous; schmoozy; self-abasing; slimy; soapy; sucky (slang); sycophantic; toadyish; toadying; truckling; unctuous

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Obedient; compliant with someone else's orders or wishes.

   **Source synonyms:** bowsome (archaic); buxom (obsolete); compliant; hearsome (rare); morigerous (obsolete); obedible (obsolete); obedient; obsequent (obsolete); obsequious (archaic); tall (obsolete); toward
