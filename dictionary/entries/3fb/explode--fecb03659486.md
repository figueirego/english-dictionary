### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To fly apart with sudden violent force; to blow up, to burst, to detonate, to go off.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The bomb explodes.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To destroy with an explosion.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The assassin exploded the car by means of a car bomb.

   **Source synonyms:** blow up; blow; blast; burst

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To make a violent or emotional outburst; to suddenly give expression to powerful and often negative or unpleasant emotion, especially anger.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

   **Example:** She exploded when I criticised her hat.

   **Source synonyms:** blow up

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To increase suddenly.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

   **Source synonyms:** blow up

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To increase arbitrarily or boundlessly.

   **Example:** The function f(x) = 1/x explodes around x = 0.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To destroy violently or abruptly.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** They sought to explode the myth.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To create an exploded view of.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Explode the assembly drawing so that all the fasteners are visible.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To disprove or debunk.

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To emerge suddenly.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

   **Example:** to explode into the mainstream; to explode onto the scene

   **Source synonyms:** burst

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. To ejaculate.

   **Usage:** slang; vulgar

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. To break (a delimited string of text) into several smaller strings by removing the separators.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; programming; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** split; unstring

<a id="r1-s12"></a>

**Source sense R1.S12**

12. To decompress (data) that was previously imploded.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** unstring

<a id="r1-s13"></a>

**Source sense R1.S13**

13. To open all doors and hatches on an automobile.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s14"></a>

**Source sense R1.S14**

14. Of a die, to produce the highest face result and consequently reroll.

   **Usage:** intransitive; board gaming
