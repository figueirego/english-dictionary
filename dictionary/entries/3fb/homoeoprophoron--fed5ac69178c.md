### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The repetition of the same consonant sound, especially at the beginning of neighboring words; essentially, extreme or faulted alliteration.

   **Usage:** countable; rhetoric; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; phonology; prosody; sciences
