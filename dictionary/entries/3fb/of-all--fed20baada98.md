## Adverb

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used as an intensifier with superlative forms of adjectives.

   **Example:** Best of all was the bicycle I got. First of all, I'd like to thank my agent. He was the greatest playwright of all.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used as an intensifier with nouns to denote being ultimate.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Marks a thing or person as a particularly unexpected or unwanted example of its category.

   **Example:** While on holiday in Spain, we ran into Mark, of all people.
