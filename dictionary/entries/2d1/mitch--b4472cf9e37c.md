### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To pilfer; filch; steal.

   **Usage:** dialectal; transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To shrink or retire from view; lurk out of sight; skulk.

   **Usage:** dialectal; intransitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To be absent from (school) without a valid excuse; to play truant, to skive off.

   **Usage:** Ireland; Wales; ambitransitive

   **Example:** John said he was going to mitch the last lesson today.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

   **Usage:** dialectal; intransitive
