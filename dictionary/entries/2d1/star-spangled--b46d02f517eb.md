### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. adorned with starshaped emblems, especially as spangles woven into fabric or other material; identifying the United States flag or another star-based design that resembles the United States flag.

   **Usage:** US

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. quintessentially American; displaying feelings of patriotic pride in American identity.

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. representing, upholding, or believing in traditional American values and the moral purpose of American society and culture.
