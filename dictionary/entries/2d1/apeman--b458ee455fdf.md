## Noun

### Forms

- apemen — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A prehistoric man resembling an ape, especially one held to be a precursor to modern humans; a non-human australopithecine.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A human raised from childhood by apes (generally in reference to, or in imitation of, the fictional character Tarzan).

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A reputed or legendary, large, bipedal, ape-like creature, as Bigfoot or the Yeti, or a primitive australopithecine still extant today.
