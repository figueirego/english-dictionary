### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An English habitational surname from Old English from either of two places in Norfolk and Suffolk.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The name of several places, including&#58;
   1. A civil parish in King's Lynn and West Norfolk district, Norfolk, England, that includes Walpole St Andrew and Walpole St Peter.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The name of several places, including&#58;
   1. A village and civil parish (served by Cookley and Walpole Parish Council) in East Suffolk district, Suffolk, England (OS grid ref TM3674).

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The name of several places, including&#58;
   1. A town in Norfolk County, Massachusetts, United States, named after Robert Walpole.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The name of several places, including&#58;
   1. A town and census-designated place therein, in Cheshire County, New Hampshire, United States.
