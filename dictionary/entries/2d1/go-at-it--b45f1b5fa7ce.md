### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. As go at + it; spoken emphasis typically on 'go'.

   **Example:** I can't figure out this problem. I've been going at it \[going at the problem\] for hours now.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. As go + at it; spoken emphasis typically on 'at'.
   1. To engage in some activity, especially vigorously or enthusiastically.

      **Usage:** informal

      **Example:** We were all going at it like crazy, trying to get the job finished before the deadline.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. As go + at it; spoken emphasis typically on 'at'.
   1. To fight or argue.

      **Usage:** intransitive; slang

      **Example:** Joe accidentally spilled his drink on Eric, Eric got up and insulted his mother, and before you know it, they were going at it.

      **Source synonyms:** get into it; mix it up; throw down; let someone have it
