### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Before now, until now, up to the present time; from the beginning to this point.

   **Usage:** formal; not-comparable; temporal

   **Example:** We now make available these works which were heretofore unpublished.

   **Example:** He had more liberty now than he'd known heretofore.

   **Source synonyms:** hitherto; as yet (idiomatic); as of yet (idiomatic); erenow; herebefore (archaic); hereto (archaic); heretofore; heretoforetime (obsolete); hereunto (archaic); hereuntofore (rare); hitherto; hithertofore (rare); so far; thus far; to date (idiomatic); up to now; yet

   **Source antonyms:** hereafter; henceforth

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Previously within the present text; earlier herein.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** heretofore referred to as

   **Example:** heretofore mentioned

   **Source synonyms:** hereinabove; hereabove; hereinbefore; herebefore

   **Source antonyms:** hereinafter; hereinunder; hereafter; hereunder; hereforth; henceforth
