## Noun

### Forms

- black men — plural
- blackman — alternative; nonstandard

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A male adult member of an ethnic group having dark pigmentation of the skin, typically of sub-Saharan African descent; or the appearance thereof.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** I saw a black man with a dog and a white man on a bicycle.

   **Source synonyms:** BIPOC; BME (Britain); colored; colored person (dated); minority; **Source context (verbatim):** in white majority countries; nonwhite (uncommon); person of color

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Black men or black people collectively; black culture.

   **Usage:** countable; dated; uncountable; usually; with-definite-article

   **Example:** The state's policy of "separate but equal" was really about keeping the black man down.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An evil spirit, the bogeyman, a demon.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A German-derived game of tag, in which the tagging player is called the "black man".

   **Usage:** archaic; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see black, man.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
