### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The events, collectively, which follow a previously mentioned event; the aftermath.

   **Usage:** dated

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A narrative that is written after another narrative set in the same universe, especially a narrative that is chronologically set after its predecessors, or (perhaps improper usage) any narrative that has a preceding narrative of its own.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; narratology; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A narrative that is written after another narrative set in the same universe, especially a narrative that is chronologically set after its predecessors, or (perhaps improper usage) any narrative that has a preceding narrative of its own.
   1. Synonym of postquel; a narrative work that follows-on and is set after an prior existing work.

      **Usage:** by restriction

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; narratology; sciences

      **Source synonyms:** postquel (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any text that continues on from another text.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The remainder of the text; what follows. Used exclusively in the set phrase "in the sequel".

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

   **Usage:** Scotland; historical
