### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To give up, hand over, surrender (something).

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To entrust (something) to another.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** She gave the deeds over to the solicitor.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To devote or resign to a particular purpose or activity; to yield completely.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The factory has been entirely given over to aircraft manufacture.

   **Example:** He gave himself over to a monastic life.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To quit, to abandon.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To give up; abandon; stop.

   **Usage:** Ireland; Northern-England; Scotland; UK; dialectal; intransitive

   **Example:** Give over with your nonsense, will you!
