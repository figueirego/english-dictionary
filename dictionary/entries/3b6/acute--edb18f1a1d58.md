### Pronunciation

### Forms

- acuter — comparative
- more acute — comparative
- acutest — superlative
- most acute — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Brief, quick, short.

   **Example:** It was an acute event.

   **Source synonyms:** fast; rapid

   **Source antonyms:** leisurely; slow

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. High or shrill.

   **Example:** an acute accent or tone

   **Source antonyms:** grave

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Intense; sensitive; sharp.

   **Example:** She had an acute sense of honour

   **Example:** Eagles have very acute vision.

   **Source synonyms:** keen; powerful; strong; peracute

   **Source antonyms:** dull; obtuse; slow; witless

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Urgent.

   **Example:** His need for medical attention was acute.

   **Source synonyms:** emergent; pressing; sudden

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. With the sides meeting directly to form an acute angle (at an apex or base).

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences

   **Source antonyms:** obtuse; subacute

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Less than 90 degrees.

   **Example:** The teacher pointed out the acute angle.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences

   **Source context (verbatim):** of an angle

   **Source antonyms:** obtuse

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Having all three interior angles measuring less than 90 degrees.

   **Example:** an acute triangle

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a triangle

   **Source synonyms:** acute-angled

   **Source antonyms:** obtuse; obtuse-angled

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Of an accent or tone&#58; generally higher than others.

   **Usage:** historical

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. Sharp, produced in the front of the mouth. (See Grave and acute on Wikipedia.Wikipedia )

   **Usage:** dated

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; phonology; sciences

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a sound

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. Of an abnormal condition of recent or sudden onset, in contrast to delayed onset; this sense does not imply severity, unlike the common usage.

   **Example:** He dropped dead of an acute illness.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. Of a short-lived condition, in contrast to a chronic condition; this sense also does not imply severity.

   **Example:** The acute symptoms resolved promptly.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

   **Source antonyms:** chronic

<a id="r1-s12"></a>

**Source sense R1.S12**

12. Of a letter of the alphabet, having an acute accent.

   **Usage:** postpositional

   **Example:** The last letter of ‘café’ is ‘e’ acute.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** communications; journalism; literature; media; orthography; publishing; writing
