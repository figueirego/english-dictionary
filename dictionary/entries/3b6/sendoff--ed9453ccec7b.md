### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A demonstration of affection and well-wishing to someone leaving a place or group to begin a new undertaking; a farewell.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A party for an colleague who is leaving; a farewell party.

   **Example:** We had a sendoff for our departing colleague as he left for a new job.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A memorial service or funeral.
