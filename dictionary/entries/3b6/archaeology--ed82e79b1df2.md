### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The study of the past by excavation and analysis of its material remains.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The study of the past by excavation and analysis of its material remains.
   1. The actual excavation, examination, analysis, and interpretation.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Example:** The building's developers have asked for some archaeology to be undertaken.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The study of the past by excavation and analysis of its material remains.
   1. The actual remains together with their location in the stratigraphy.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Example:** The archaeology will tell us which methods of burial were used by the Ancient Greeks.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The study of the past by excavation and analysis of its material remains.
   1. The academic subject; in the USA&#58; one of the four sub-disciplines of anthropology.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Example:** She studied archaeology at Edinburgh University.
