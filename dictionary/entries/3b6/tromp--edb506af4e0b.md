### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To tread heavily, especially to crush underfoot.

   **Usage:** US; ambitransitive

   **Example:** Mother yelled at my brothers for tromping through her flowerbed.

   **Example:** The hoodlums were tromping pumpkins they had stolen from their neighbors' Halloween displays.

   **Source synonyms:** march; stamp; stomp; tramp; trample

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To utterly defeat an opponent.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** The team had been tromped by their cross-town rivals, and the players were embarrassed to show their faces in school the next day.

   **Source synonyms:** clobber; decimate; rout; trounce; whip
