### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of the genus Euterpe of palm trees native to Central America, South America, and the West Indies; especially of species Euterpe oleracea (acai palm).

   **Usage:** Guyana

   **Source synonyms:** manicole palm

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The Guyana manicole palm or manacachilla (Prestoea tenuiramosa), a species of palm tree native to South America; also, the wood of this tree.

   **Usage:** Guyana; attributive

   **Source synonyms:** manicole palm
