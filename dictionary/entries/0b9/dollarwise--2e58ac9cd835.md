## Adverb

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In terms of dollars (as opposed to percentage, or to another currency); moneywise; monetarily; financially.

   **Usage:** US; not-comparable

   **Example:** Dollarwise it was the biggest change in years, but percentagewise it was not a record-breaking change.
