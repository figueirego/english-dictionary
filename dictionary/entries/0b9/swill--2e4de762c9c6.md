## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A mixture of solid and liquid food scraps fed to pigs etc; especially kitchen waste for this purpose.

   **Usage:** collective; countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** hogwash; slops; pigswill

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any disgusting or distasteful liquid.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** I cannot believe anyone could drink this swill.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Anything disgusting or worthless.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; figuratively; uncountable

   **Example:** This new TV show is a worthless load of swill.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A large quantity of liquid drunk at one swallow.

   **Usage:** countable; informal; uncountable

   **Example:** He took a swill of his drink and tried to think of words.

   **Source synonyms:** swig

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Inexpensive beer or alcohol.

   **Usage:** countable; informal; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A badly-thrown pass.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; ultimate frisbee
