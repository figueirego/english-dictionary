### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To ask (about something).

   **Usage:** Australia; Canada; US; ambitransitive; intransitive

   **Example:** May I inquire to whom I have the honor of speaking?

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To make an inquiry or an investigation.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive; intransitive

   **Example:** Police are already inquiring into the incident.

**Source sense R1.S3**

   **Usage:** ambitransitive; obsolete; transitive
