## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A 1994 controversy around allegations by two Arkansas state troopers that they arranged sexual liaisons for then-governor Bill Clinton.

   **Usage:** US

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A 2007 controversy involving New York governor Eliot Spitzer, who allegedly ordered the state police to create special records of senate majority leader Joseph L. Bruno's whereabouts when he traveled with police escorts in New York City.

   **Usage:** US

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
