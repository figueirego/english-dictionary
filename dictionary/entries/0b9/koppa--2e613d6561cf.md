### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An Ancient Greek letter representing /k/, Ϙ / ϙ, replaced by kappa by the Classical Greek period.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The Greek numeral Ϟ (lowercase ϟ), descended from the ancient letter and having the numerical value of 90.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>
