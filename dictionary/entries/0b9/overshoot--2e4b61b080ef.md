### Senses

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To go past something; to go too far.

   **Example:** When you drive, you must remember to not overshoot the parking space and end up with two wheels over the line.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To shoot beyond; to shoot too far to hit something.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To pass swiftly over; to fly beyond.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

   **Example:** to overshoot the truth
