### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Relating to, or based on, practice or action rather than theory or hypothesis.

   **Example:** Jack didn't get an engineering degree, but has practical knowledge of metalworking.

   **Example:** Modern engineering applies science to practical problems.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Being likely to be effective and applicable to a real situation; able to be put to use.

   **Example:** Jack's knowledge has the practical benefit of giving us useful prototype parts.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of a person, having skills or knowledge that are practical.

   **Example:** All in all, Jack's a very practical chap.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Of a prop&#58; having some degree of functionality, rather than being a mere imitation.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; theater

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Light fixtures used for set lighting and seen in the frame of a shot as part of the scenery.

   **Example:** Practical lighting can help sell the illusion that the film is a real situation.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** broadcasting; film; media; television
