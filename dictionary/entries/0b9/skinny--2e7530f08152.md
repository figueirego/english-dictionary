### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Thin, generally in a negative sense (as opposed to slim, which is thin in a positive sense).

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** Her recent weight loss has made her look rather skinny than slender

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of food or a beverage, having reduced fat or calories.

   **Usage:** informal

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Naked; nude (chiefly used in the phrase skinny dipping).

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Tight-fitting.

   **Example:** skinny jeans

   **Source context (verbatim):** of clothing

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Synonym of thin (“type of shot where the ball is struck by the bottom part of the club head”).
