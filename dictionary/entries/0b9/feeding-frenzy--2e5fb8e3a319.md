## Noun

### Forms

- feeding frenzies — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A wild, turbulent situation in which multiple sharks or other predatory fish attack one or more edible creatures simultaneously, in competition with each other.

   **Example:** When the terrified, bleeding cow was washed overboard into the river, the hungry piranhas entered a feeding frenzy, turning the water red with blood.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A voracious competition among would-be purchasers, etc. for limited resources.

   **Usage:** broadly; idiomatic

   **Example:** The manager hesitated to open the doors to the thronging holiday crowds, knowing that a feeding frenzy would soon ensue near the display of coveted toys.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An event in which participants erratically grab and eat food.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** Karen's new students thought treat day was a feeding frenzy and horded the snack table.
