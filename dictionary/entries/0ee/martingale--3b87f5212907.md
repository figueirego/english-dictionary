### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A piece of harness used on a horse to keep it from raising its head above a desired point.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** equestrianism; hobbies; horses; lifestyle; pets; sports

   **Source synonyms:** tie-down

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A spar, or piece of rigging that strengthens the bowsprit.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A stochastic process for which the conditional expectation of future values given the sequence of all prior values is equal to the current value.

   **Example:** If a gambler plays a fair game repeatedly, his payoff over time is a martingale.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A strategy in which one doubles the stake after each loss.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** gambling; games

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A strap attached to the sword handle, preventing a sword being dropped if disarmed.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** fencing; government; hobbies; lifestyle; martial-arts; military; politics; sports; war
