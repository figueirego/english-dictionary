## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One of various graphical representations of a cognitive or psychological profile.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A photographic image having a supposed supernatural or spiritual origin.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** parapsychology; pseudoscience

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of various devices used for automatic writing.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** parapsychology; pseudoscience

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any of various devices that purportedly read a person's thoughts.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. psychobiography

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A piece of prose that describes hallucinations or phantoms.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A photograph, film, or other recorded image that evokes the feeling of a person, time, or place.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A device that measures a person's head according to phrenological principles, invented by Henry C. Lavery in the first half of the 20th century.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; medicine; phrenology; psychology; sciences
