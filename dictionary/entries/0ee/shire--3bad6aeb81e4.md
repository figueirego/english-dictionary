### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An administrative area or district between about the 5th to the 11th century, subdivided into hundreds or wapentakes and jointly governed by an ealdorman and a sheriff; also, a present-day area corresponding to such a historical district; a county; especially (England), a county having a name ending in -shire.

   **Usage:** UK; historical

   **Example:** Yorkshire is the largest shire in England.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The people living in a shire (noun sense 1.1) considered collectively.

   **Usage:** UK; broadly

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The general area in which a person comes from or lives.

   **Usage:** UK; broadly; informal

   **Example:** When are you coming back to the shire?

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An administrative area or district in other countries.

   **Usage:** broadly

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An administrative area or district in other countries.
   1. An outer suburban or rural local government area which elects its own council.

      **Usage:** Australia; attributive; broadly; often

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Ellipsis of shire horse (“a draught horse of a tall British breed, usually bay, black, or grey”).

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; ellipsis

   **Alternative form of:** [shire horse](../161/shire-horse--58755e254f44.md)

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A district or province governed by a person; specifically (Christianity), the province of an archbishop, the see of a bishop, etc.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A region; also, a country.

   **Usage:** broadly; obsolete; generally
