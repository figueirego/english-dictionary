### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A practice such as witchcraft, sorcery, necromancy, or black magic.

   **Usage:** in-plural; often

   **Example:** dabbling in the black arts

   **Example:** In 1690s Salem, anyone was at mortal risk of being accused of practicing the black arts.

   **Source synonyms:** black magic; dark art; diablerie; bewitchery; black arts; charmwork; conjuration; conjuring; craft (obsolete); dark arts; diablerie; druidcraft; dweomercraft (rare); dwimmer (neologism; obsolete); dwimmercraft; enchantment; goety (obsolete); gypsycraft; hexcraft; hexerei; jugglery (archaic); magic; mojo; sorcery; sortilege; spellcasting; spellcraft; spellwork; thaumaturgy; wandwork; warlockry; whistness (UK; dialectal); witchdom; witchery; witchcraft; witchwork; wizardry; wizardcraft (rare); wonderworking

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A process that is esoteric or difficult to master.

   **Usage:** broadly; figuratively

   **Example:** Operating a hand card punch was something of a black art.

   **Source synonyms:** dark art
