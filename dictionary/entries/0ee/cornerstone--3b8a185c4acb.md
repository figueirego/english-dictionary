### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A stone forming the base at the corner of a building.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Such a stone used ceremonially, often inscribed with the architect's and owner's names, dates and other details.

   **Example:** The cornerstone on the Flatiron Building is set on the Fifth Avenue facade.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. That which is prominent, fundamental, noteworthy, or central.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** Exceptional service is the cornerstone of the hospitality industry.

   **Example:** That is the cornerstone of any meaningful debate about budgets and projects, regulations and policies.
