### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An elevation of land of considerable dimensions rising more or less abruptly, forming a conspicuous figure in the landscape, usually having a small extent of surface at its summit.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** Everest is the highest mountain in the world.

   **Example:** We spent the weekend hiking in the mountains.

   **Example:** We collected plant specimens from mountain meadows above 2000 meters.

   **Source synonyms:** mount

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Something very large in size or quantity; a huge amount; a great heap.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** He was a real mountain of a man, standing seven feet tall.

   **Example:** There's still a mountain of work to do.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A difficult task or challenge.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable
