## Noun

### Forms

- theilerioses — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any disease caused by Theileria parasites, transmitted by species of ticks.
   1. East Coast fever, a disease of cattle, sheep, and goats caused by the parasite Theileria parva, transmitted by Rhipicephalus appendiculatus

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any disease caused by Theileria parasites, transmitted by species of ticks.
   1. Human theileriosis, caused by Theileria microti, transmitted by deer ticks.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any disease caused by Theileria parasites, transmitted by species of ticks.
   1. Tropical theileriosis, a theileriosis of cattle from the Mediterranean and Middle East area, from Morocco to Western parts of India and China, caused by Theileria annulata, transmitted by ticks of Hyalomma.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable
