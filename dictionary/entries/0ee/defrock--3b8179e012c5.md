### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To divest of a frock.

   **Usage:** literally

   **Source synonyms:** disfrock; unfrock

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To formally remove the rights and authority of a member of the clergy.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** The defrocked priest may no longer perform rites.

   **Source synonyms:** disfrock; unfrock; unordain

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To formally remove the rights and authority of someone, e.g. a government official or a medical practitioner.

   **Usage:** broadly
