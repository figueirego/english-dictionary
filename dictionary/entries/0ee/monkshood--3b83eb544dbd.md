### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of various poisonous plants, of the genus Aconitum, with blue or white flowers in the shape of a hood.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The dried leaves or flowers of these plants formerly used as a source of medicinal alkaloids.

   **Usage:** uncountable
