### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Distended beyond the natural state by some internal agent, especially fluid, or expansive force.

   **Example:** I have a turgid limb.

   **Source synonyms:** bloated; distended; inflated; swelled; swollen; tumescent; tumid; turgescent; boln (obsolete); bulging; bulled; distended; extuberant (obsolete); farced; nodose; puffed; protuberant; swollen; tumid; turgid

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of a river, inundated with excess water as from a flood; swollen.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Overly complex and difficult to understand; grandiloquent; bombastic.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of language or style

   **Source synonyms:** bombastic; grandiose; pompous
