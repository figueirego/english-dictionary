<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An annual plant (Cicer arietinum) in the pea family, widely cultivated for the edible seeds in its short inflated pods.

   **Source synonyms:** Bengal gram; garbanzo

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A seed of this plant, often used as a food.

   **Example:** Chickpea is valued for its nutritive seeds with high protein content.
