## Noun

### Forms

- food pyramids — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A pyramid-shaped diagram in layers, where each layer represents a group of animals that feeds on the layer below.

   **Example:** The teacher reminded us to always start the food pyramid with the plants on the bottom.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any pyramidal diagram giving advice on how to eat healthily, particularly those widely publicized by the United States government.

   **Usage:** US; nutrition

   **Example:** The food pyramid surprised me by showing that we should eat lots of cheese.
