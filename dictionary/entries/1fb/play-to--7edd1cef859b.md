### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see play, to.

   **Example:** At the festival, the band played to 20,000 people.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To act in a way most suitable to; to act in a way that best brings out (a quality, condition, etc.).

   **Example:** He played to his strengths tonight.

   **Example:** The advertisement plays to people's fears about cancer.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To act in order to gain the attention or admiration of.

   **Example:** to play to the crowd

   **Example:** to play to the cameras
