## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

- knuckleballs — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A pitch thrown with the ball gripped on the fingertips and released with no rotation, which travels over an unpredictable path to the plate due to microturbulence in the air.

   **Example:** Even the pitcher doesn't know where his knuckleball is going.

   **Example:** Boston Red Sox pitcher Tim Wakefield is best known for his use of the knuckleball.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; baseball; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A type of delivery where a fast bowler holds the ball on the knuckles of the index and middle fingers.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; cricket; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An event that is hard to predict.

   **Usage:** figuratively; informal

   **Example:** Our vendor keeps throwing us knuckleballs.
