### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To suffer decomposition due to biological action, especially by fungi or bacteria.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The apple left in the cupboard all that time had started to rot.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To decline in function or utility.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Your brain will rot if you spend so much time on the computer, Tony!

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To (cause to) deteriorate in any way, as in morals; to corrupt.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To make putrid; to cause to be wholly or partially decomposed by natural processes.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to rot vegetable fiber

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To spend a long period of time (in an unpleasant place or state).

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

   **Example:** to rot in prison

   **Example:** to rot in Hell

   **Example:** If I hadn't rotted in bed all day I would've come...

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To expose, as flax, to a process of maceration, etc., for the purpose of separating the fiber; to ret.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To talk nonsense.

   **Usage:** dated; slang

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Damn; blast.

   **Usage:** imperative; slang; transitive
