### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Roughly or hastily laid out; intended for later refinement.

   **Example:** The first draft included a sketchy design.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Not thorough or detailed.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Resembling a comedy sketch, of sketch quality.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Of questionable or doubtful quality.

   **Usage:** Canada; US; slang

   **Example:** The sketchy repair job did not look like it would hold.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Suspected of taking part in illicit or dishonorable dealings.

   **Usage:** Canada; US; slang

   **Example:** Because he is so sketchy, I always think that he is up to something.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a person

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Disturbing or unnerving, often in such a way that others may suspect them of intending physical or sexual harm or harassment.

   **Usage:** Canada; US; slang

   **Example:** Jack is so sketchy. I think he's stalking me.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a person

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Disturbing, or indicating possible danger.

   **Usage:** Australia; slang

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a situation
