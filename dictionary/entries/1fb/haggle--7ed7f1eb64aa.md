### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To argue for a better deal, especially over prices between a buyer and seller.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** I haggled for a better price because the original price was too high.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To hack (cut crudely)

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To stick at small matters; to chaffer; to higgle.
