### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The oral opening on the pseudembryo of an echinoderm.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A false osculum or secondary opening which replaces the original osculum of a sponge.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A false opening that resembles a stoma but without any true aperture.

   **Usage:** broadly

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A North American rodent, Geomys bursarius.
