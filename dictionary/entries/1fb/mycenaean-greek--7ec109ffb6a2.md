### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The most ancient attested form of the Greek language, spoken on the Greek mainland and on Crete in the 16th to 11th centuries BC, written in Linear B script.

   **Usage:** uncountable
