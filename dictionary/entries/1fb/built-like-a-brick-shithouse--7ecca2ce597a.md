## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of a man, having a muscular body, particularly with well-developed muscles of the chest, arms and abdominals.

   **Usage:** colloquial; idiomatic; not-comparable; vulgar; simile

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of a woman, having an athletic or muscular body; often implying also having large breasts or an attractive body.

   **Usage:** Canada; US; colloquial; idiomatic; not-comparable; vulgar; simile
