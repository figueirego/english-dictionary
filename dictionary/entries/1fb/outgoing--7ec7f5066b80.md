## Adjective · Record 1

### Pronunciation

### Forms

- more outgoing — comparative
- most outgoing — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Extroverted&#58; talkative, friendly, and social, especially with respect to meeting new people easily and comfortably; outgiving.

   **Example:** Tom is very outgoing and enjoys meeting people; his brother, on the other hand, is painfully shy.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Going out, on its way out.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Is there any outgoing post?

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Being replaced in office (while still in office but after election has determined that he/she will be replaced).

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The outgoing politician wasn't too disappointed he'd lost the election; he was tired of political infighting.
