**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The most extreme or furthest point of something.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** tip

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A hand or foot.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Guillain–Barré syndrome causes one to not be able to move one’s extremities.
