### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Unpleasantly sticky; yucky; disgusting.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** I stepped in something icky and it smells terrible.

   **Source synonyms:** gross; ishy

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Excessively sentimental.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Source synonyms:** schmaltzy; syrupy; treacly; bathetic; cheesy; squishy; corny; drippy; gooey (figuratively); hokey (US; informal); icky; kitsch; kitschy; maudlin; mawkish; mushy; overemotional; oversentimental; overwrought; sappy (US; figuratively); schmaltzy; sickly sweet; slushy; soppy (UK; figuratively); soupy; syrupy (figuratively); treacly (figuratively)
