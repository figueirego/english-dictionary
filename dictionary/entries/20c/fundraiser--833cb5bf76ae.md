### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An event undertaken to get money by voluntary contributions for a particular activity or cause.

   **Example:** This year’s fundraiser will be a walkathon.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person who collects money from the public for some cause.

   **Example:** She works as fundraiser for the Democratic Party.
