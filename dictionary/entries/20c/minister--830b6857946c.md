### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who is trained to preach, to perform religious ceremonies, and to afford pastoral care at a Protestant church.

   **Example:** The minister said a prayer on behalf of the entire congregation.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity; Protestantism

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person (either a layperson or an ordained clergy member) who is commissioned to perform some act on behalf of the Catholic Church.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Catholicism; Christianity; Roman-Catholicism

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A politician who heads a ministry

   **Example:** He was newly appointed to be Minister of the Interior.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government

   **Source synonyms:** minister; government minister

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. In diplomacy, the rank of diplomat directly below ambassador.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A servant; a subordinate; an officer or assistant of inferior rank; hence, an agent, an instrument.
