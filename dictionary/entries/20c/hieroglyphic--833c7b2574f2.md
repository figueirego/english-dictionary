### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A writing system of ancient Egypt, Minoans, Maya and other civilizations, using pictorial symbols to represent individual sounds, often as a rebus.

   **Usage:** in-plural

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any symbol used in this system; a hieroglyph.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
