## Noun

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act, process, method, or an instance of representing by a system or set of marks, signs, figures, or characters.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A system of characters, symbols, or abbreviated expressions used in an art or science or in mathematics or logic to express technical facts or quantities.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** This section lists all algebraic notation used in this book.

   **Example:** The notations used in the two books differed.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A specific note or piece of information written in such a notation.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** She made a notation in the margin of the book.
