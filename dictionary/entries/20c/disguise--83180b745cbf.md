### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Material (such as clothing, makeup, a wig) used to alter one’s visual appearance in order to hide one's identity or assume another.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** A cape and moustache completed his disguise.

   **Example:** hiding one's treacherous intentions under the guise of friendship

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The appearance of something on the outside which masks what’s beneath.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

   **Example:** old ideas in a new guise

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The act or state of disguising, notably as a ploy.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Any disguise may expose soldiers to be deemed enemy spies.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A change of behaviour resulting from intoxication, drunkenness.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; uncountable

### Forms

- disguising — participle; present
- disguised — participle; past

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To change the appearance of (a person or thing) so as to hide, or to assume an identity.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Spies often disguise themselves.

   **Source synonyms:** masquerade

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To transform or disfigure, to change the appearance of in general.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To avoid giving away or revealing (something secret); to hide by a false appearance.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He disguised his true intentions.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To dress in newfangled or showy clothing, to deck out in new fashions.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To dissemble, to talk or act falsely while concealing one’s thoughts.

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To affect or change by liquor; to intoxicate.

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive
