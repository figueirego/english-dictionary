### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The state of a network in which the number of nodes is far in excess of the number of users.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The state of the brain, in schizophrenia or epileptic seizure, in which an excessive number of connections are made between neurons.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; pathology; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Increased connectedness of human beings through social media, etc.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
