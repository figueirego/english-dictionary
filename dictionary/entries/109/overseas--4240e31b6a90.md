## Adjective · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˌəʊvəˈsiːz/ — source labels: Received-Pronunciation
- IPA: /ˌoʊvɚˈsiːz/ — source labels: US

### Forms

- oversea — alternative; UK

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Abroad, especially across the sea.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Overseas branches of the company are doing well.

   **Source synonyms:** transmarine; ultramarine (archaic)

   **Source antonyms:** domestic

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used with ethnicities, nationalities, or religious affiliations&#58; living (being resident) in a foreign country.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Overseas Chinese communities exist in North and South America.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Across a sea; to or in an area across a sea.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Her overseas trip went well.
