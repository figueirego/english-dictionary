## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An unfavorable thing that must be done or accepted, especially because the available alternative courses of action or inaction would be worse.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** Another punishing round of chemo is a necessary evil if Lisa is to have a fighting chance against the cancer.

   **Example:** My boss is a real piece of work, but my kids make keeping this job a necessary evil.
