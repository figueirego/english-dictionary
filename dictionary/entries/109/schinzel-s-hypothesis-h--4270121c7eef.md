### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A famous open problem in mathematics, the hypothesis stating that, for every finite collection f\_1,f\_2,…,f\_k of non-constant irreducible polynomials over the integers with positive leading coefficients, one of the following conditions holds&#58; (i) there are infinitely many positive integers n such that all of f\_1(n),f\_2(n),…,f\_k(n) are simultaneously prime numbers, or (ii) there is an integer m&gt;1 (called a fixed divisor) which always divides the product f\_1(n)f\_2(n)⋯f\_k(n).
