### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Pertaining to or based on experience, as opposed to theory.

   **Example:** The lengths were calculated according to the empirical rules of the trade.

   **Example:** For some presumptive diagnoses, empirical antibiotic therapy begins immediately, whereas specific antibiotic therapy must await the results of the culture and sensitivity test.

   **Source antonyms:** theoretical

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Pertaining to, derived from, or testable by observations made using the physical senses or using instruments which extend the senses.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Verifiable by means of scientific experimentation.

   **Example:** demonstrable with empirical evidence

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; philosophy; sciences

   **Source context (verbatim):** of science

   **Source antonyms:** anecdotal; theoretical
