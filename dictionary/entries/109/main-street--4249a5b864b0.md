## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The proper name of the main street of any of many villages, towns, or small cities, especially in the United States, Canada, Ireland, and some parts of Scotland.

   **Source synonyms:** High Street

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Everyday working-class people and small business owners, especially with regard to their concerns, social views, etc.

   **Usage:** Canada; US; collective; idiomatic

   **Example:** Main Street will never accept such fashions.

   **Source synonyms:** common man; everyman; High Street; precariat; proletariat; working class; middle class
