## Noun

### Forms

- occluders — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An object that partially or completely impedes the amount of light reaching the eye.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An implement designed to temporarily block light to one eye.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A catheter-delivered device that blocks a hole in the wall of a heart, often having an umbrella-type design and folded up until the catheter arrives at the area of the defect in the heart. Once in place the occluder is unfolded on both sides of the defect so that pressure from both sides keeps it in place.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** cardiology; medicine; sciences
