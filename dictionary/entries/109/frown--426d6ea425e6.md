## Verb · Etymology 2 · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈfɹæʊ̯n/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Forms

- frowns — present; singular; third-person
- frowning — participle; present
- frowned — participle; past
- frowned — past

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To have a frown on one's face.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** She frowned when I told her the news.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To manifest displeasure or disapprobation; to look with disfavour or threateningly.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

   **Example:** Noisy gossip in the library is frowned upon.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To repress or repel by expressing displeasure or disapproval; to rebuke with a look.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Let us frown the impudent fellow into silence.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To communicate by frowning.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Frank frowned his displeasure with my proposal.
