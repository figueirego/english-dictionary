### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To drain (a living or dead body, or (medicine, surgery) a body part) of blood.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** bleed white

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To kill (a person or animal) by means of blood loss.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** bleed out

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To bleed profusely; also, to die by means of blood loss.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source synonyms:** bleed out

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To cause blood to drain from a body or body part, or profuse bleeding.

   **Usage:** intransitive
