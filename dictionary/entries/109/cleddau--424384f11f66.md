## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A river in Pembrokeshire, Wales, with two branches, Eastern Cleddau and Western Cleddau; the combined river flows into Milford Haven.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A short river in Fiordland, Southland, New Zealand, which flows into Milford Sound. The association with the Welsh river and Milford Haven may be intentional.
