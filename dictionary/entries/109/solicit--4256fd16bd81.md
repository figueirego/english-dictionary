### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To persistently endeavor to obtain an object, or bring about an event.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to solicit alms, or a favour

   **Source synonyms:** supplicate; thig

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To woo; to court.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** address; romance

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To persuade or incite one to commit some act, especially illegal or sexual behavior.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** If you want to lose your virginity, you should try to solicit some fine looking women.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To offer to perform sexual activity, especially when for a payment.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** My girlfriend tried to solicit me for sex, but I was tired.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To make a petition.

   **Source synonyms:** appeal; petition; request

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To disturb or trouble; to harass.

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To urge the claims of; to plead; to act as solicitor for or with reference to.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** plead

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To disturb; to disquiet.

   **Usage:** obsolete; rare; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** worry
