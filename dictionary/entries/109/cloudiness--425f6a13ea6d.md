## Noun

### Forms

- cloudinesses — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of the sky, weather, etc.&#58; the state of being cloudy.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Expect considerable cloudiness with occasional rain showers.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of a liquid&#58; the property of being murky.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Cloudiness in the water is most often caused in new aquariums by white bacteria.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of information, a writing style, etc.&#58; the property of being obscure.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

   **Example:** Avoid cloudiness in your essay-writing.
