### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Something that actuates something else.

   **Example:** The mind is the actuator of the body.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A usually electric device that causes a mechanical device (i.e., a mechanism) to be switched on or off. For example; an electric motor that opens and closes a valve.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The mechanism that moves the head assembly on a disk drive.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computer-hardware; computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**
