### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To step or walk (on or across something); to trample.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** He trod back and forth wearily.

   **Example:** Don't tread on the lawn.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To step or walk upon.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Actors tread the boards.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To proceed, to behave (in a certain manner).

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** to tread lightly, to tread gently

   **Example:** to tread carefully, to tread cautiously, to tread warily

   **Source context (verbatim):** with certain adverbs of manner

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To beat or press with the feet.

   **Example:** to tread a path; to tread land when too light; a well-trodden path

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To work a lever, treadle, etc., with the foot or the feet.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To go through or accomplish by walking, dancing, etc.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To crush under the foot; to trample in contempt or hatred; to subdue; to repress.

   **Source synonyms:** step on

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To copulate; said of (especially male) birds.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To copulate with (a hen).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a male bird

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. To crush grapes with one's feet to make wine

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** stomp
