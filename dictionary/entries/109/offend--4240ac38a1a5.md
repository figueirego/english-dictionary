### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To hurt the feelings of; to displease; to make angry; to insult.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Your accusations offend me deeply.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To feel or become offended; to take insult.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Don't worry. I don't offend easily.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To physically harm, pain.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Strong light offends the eye.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To annoy, cause discomfort or resent.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Physically enjoyable frivolity can still offend the conscience

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To sin, transgress divine law or moral rules.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To transgress or violate a law or moral requirement.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Wearing faded baggy offends against the club's rules of proper dress.

   **Source context (verbatim):** with "against"

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To cause to stumble; to cause to sin or to fall.

   **Usage:** archaic; obsolete; transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biblical; lifestyle; religion
