### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The optical analogue of radar, using intense pulses of laser light to measure the composition and structure of the atmosphere.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** Tesla bet on the idea that in the design of self-driving cars, lidar doesn't add enough capability, beyond what the cameras already provide, to be worth the additional cost.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A device that provides such imaging.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** There's a lidar mounted on the roof of that Waymo.
