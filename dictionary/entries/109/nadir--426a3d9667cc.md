### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The point of the celestial sphere, directly opposite the zenith; inferior pole of the horizon; point of the celestial sphere directly under the place of observation.

   **Source synonyms:** antizenith

   **Source antonyms:** zenith

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The lowest point; time of greatest depression.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Source synonyms:** slough of despond; trough; bathos; low point; low-water mark; dead

   **Source antonyms:** height; peak

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The axis of a projected conical shadow; the direction of the force of gravity at a location; down.

   **Example:** The nadir of the sun is the axis of the shadow projected by the Earth.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astronomy; natural-sciences

   **Source synonyms:** down

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An empty box added beneath a full one in a beehive to give the colony more room to expand or store honey.

   **Usage:** archaic

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** agriculture; beekeeping; business; lifestyle

   **Source antonyms:** duplet; super
