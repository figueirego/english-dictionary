### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A severe tropical cyclone; an intense storm rotating around a central eye.

   **Example:** Whole villages have been leveled by the hurricane coastal residents braced for.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A severe tropical cyclone in the North Atlantic Ocean, Caribbean Sea, Gulf of Mexico, or in the eastern North Pacific off the west coast of Mexico, with winds of 119 km/h (74 miles per hour) or greater accompanied by rain, lightning, and thunder that sometimes moves into temperate latitudes.

   **Example:** Janice was a tropical depression 10 days ago, a tropical storm 5 days ago, and a hurricane yesterday and today.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** climatology; meteorology; natural-sciences

   **Source context (verbatim):** in particular

   **Source synonyms:** typhoon; cyclone

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A great forceful onrush.

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A sweet alcoholic drink made with rum, lemon juice (or sometimes other fruit juice), and either passion fruit syrup or fassionola.

   **Usage:** cocktails
