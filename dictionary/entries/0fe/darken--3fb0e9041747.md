**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make dark or darker by reducing light.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** bedark; blacken; endark; swarten

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source synonyms:** blacken; mirken; swarten

   **Usage:** impersonal

   **Source synonyms:** dusk
