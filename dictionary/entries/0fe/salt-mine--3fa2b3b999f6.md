### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any mine used for the extraction of salt.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any laborious work situation, especially in a confined space.

   **Usage:** broadly

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An abundance of indignation felt by one person or a group of people due to over-sensitivity, humourlessness, disappointment, or defeat; a gold mine (sense 3) of salt (noun sense 11).

   **Usage:** Internet; derogatory

   **Example:** The fan forum was an absolute salt mine after the football team's loss to the last-place team in its division.
