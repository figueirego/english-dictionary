## Noun

### Forms

- indeterminisms — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The doctrine that all human actions are not so much determined by the preceding events, conditions, causes or karma as by deliberate choice or free will.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ethics; human-sciences; lifestyle; philosophy; religion; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A case in which the uncertainty principle applies; a case in which certain pairs of physical properties such as the position and momentum of a particle cannot be known simultaneously.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any situation in which the outcome cannot be completely predicted in advance.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A situation in which there are multiple valid options for next step in a process.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences
