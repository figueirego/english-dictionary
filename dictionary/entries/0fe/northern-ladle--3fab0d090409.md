### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A bright circumpolar asterism of the northern sky, said to resemble a ladle. It is part of the constellation Ursa Major and includes the stars Alkaid, Mizar, Alioth, Megrez, Dubhe, Merak, and Phecda.

   **Usage:** especially Asia

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astronomy; natural-sciences
