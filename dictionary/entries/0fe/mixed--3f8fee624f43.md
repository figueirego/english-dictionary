### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Having two or more separate aspects.

   **Example:** I get a very mixed feeling from this puzzling painting.

   **Example:** The various studies produced mixed results.

   **Source synonyms:** heterogeneous; ambivalent; conflicted; equivocal; [catholic](../213/catholic--84ef9e571add.md); dishomogeneous; diverse; diversified; eclectic; heterogeneous; liberal; manifold; miscellaneous; mixed; motley; multifarious; multifold; sundry; variable; varied; various

   **Source antonyms:** homogeneous; unmixed; homogeneous; invariable; of a piece (idiomatic); onefold; samely (dialectal); uniform; uniformal (obsolete); unmixed; unvaried; unvarying

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Not completely pure, tainted or adulterated.

   **Example:** My joy was somewhat mixed when my partner said she was pregnant&#58; it's a lot of responsibility.

   **Source synonyms:** impure; polluted; adulterated; common (obsolete); contaminated; corrupted; mixed; polluted; sophisticated; tainted

   **Source antonyms:** pure

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. Including both male(s) and female(s).

   **Example:** The tennis match was mixed, with a boy and a girl on each side.

   **Example:** My son attends a mixed school, my daughter an all-girl grammar school.

   **Source synonyms:** co-ed; unsegregated

   **Source antonyms:** single-sex

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. Stemming from two or more races or breeds.

   **Example:** The benefit dog show has both mixed and single-breed competitions.

   **Example:** Mixed blood can surprisingly produce inherited properties which neither parent showed

   **Source synonyms:** hybrid; mongrel

   **Source antonyms:** pedigree; pure; pureblooded; purebred

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. Polarizing; including both positive and negative feedback.

   **Example:** The movie has received mixed reviews from movie critics.

   **Source synonyms:** disunifying; divisive

   **Source antonyms:** unanimous; unifying
