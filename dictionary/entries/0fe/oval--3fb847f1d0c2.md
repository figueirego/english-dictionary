### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

   1. A two-dimensional one.

      **Source synonyms:** ellipse

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An elongated round shape resembling an ellipse or an egg.
   1. A three-dimensional one.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A thing having such a shape, such as an arena.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

   **Usage:** Australia

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**
