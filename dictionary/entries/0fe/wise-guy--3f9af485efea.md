### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who is insolent or flippant; one who makes jokes or perpetrates pranks.

   **Usage:** derogatory; humorous; informal; mildly; sarcastic

   **Example:** Her reputation for being a wise guy made her new teachers hesitant about having her in their classes.
