### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An electromechanical or electronic bell, klaxon, chime, horn, speaker, strobe light or other device which warns people in a building etc. of a possible fire or other condition requiring evacuation.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The sound or other warning made by a fire alarm.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An emergency response to a fire by the fire department with manpower and equipment.
