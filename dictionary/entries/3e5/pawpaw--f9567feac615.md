### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of several types of trees having edible fruit
   1. Any member of the genus Asimina of trees and shrubs native to eastern North America, especially common pawpaw (Asimina triloba).

      **Source synonyms:** custard apple; prairie banana; hillbilly mango

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of several types of trees having edible fruit
   1. Papaya (Carica papaya), a widely cultivated tropical fruit tree.

      **Usage:** Australia

      **Source synonyms:** papaya

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of several types of trees having edible fruit
   1. Mountain pawpaw (Vasconcellea pubescens), a fruit tree native to South America.

      **Source synonyms:** mountain pawpaw; mountain papaya

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The fruit itself of such trees.
