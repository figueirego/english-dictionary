### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To increase (something) in extent or intensity; to intensify or step up.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** Violence escalated during the election.

   **Example:** The shooting escalated the existing hostility.

   **Example:** A small fight escalated into a big fight.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In technical support, to transfer a customer, a problem, etc. to the next higher level of authority

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The tech 1 escalated the caller to a tech 2.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To climb.

   **Usage:** uncommon

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To move by escalator.

   **Usage:** rare

   **Source synonyms:** escalator
