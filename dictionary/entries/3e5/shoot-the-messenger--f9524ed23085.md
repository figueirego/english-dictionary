**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To blame a problem on whoever reported it; to hold somebody accountable for a problem because they brought attention to it.

   **Usage:** humorous; idiomatic; sarcastic

   **Example:** I know you won't like this news, but please don't shoot the messenger.
