### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used to form the cohortative of verbs, equivalent of the first-person plural imperative in some other languages.

   **Example:** Let’s eat lunch sometime, shall we?

   **Example:** I say let’s dance.

   **Example:** Let's buy a bottle of champagne if Mom comes, we’ll wait and see.

   **Example:** Let's go for a walk, shall we? -Sure, let’s.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used to form the hortative of verbs, equivalent of the second-person plural imperative in some other languages, chiefly instructional

   **Example:** Let’s make sure we don’t forget proper punctuation, let's not let those mistakes happen again.

   **Example:** Hey guys, let’s check to make sure that we proofread.

   **Example:** Y’all, let’s stop talking please, y’all are driving me up the wall!
