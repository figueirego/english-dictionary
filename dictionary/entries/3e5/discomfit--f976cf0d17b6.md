### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To embarrass (someone) greatly; to confuse; to perplex; to disconcert.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Don't worry. Your joke did not really discomfit me.

   **Source synonyms:** abash; disconcert; abase; abash; bring low; confuse; confound; debase; degrade; demean; depress; disconcert; discomfit; dishonour; embarrass; humble; humiliate; lower; mortify; put to shame; shame

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To defeat the plans or hopes of; to frustrate; disconcert.

   **Usage:** rare; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** foil; thwart

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To defeat completely; to rout.

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** overthrow; vanquish
