<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Similarity of form
   1. the similarity in form of organisms, which may be due to convergent evolution or shared genetic background, e.g. an algae species in which the haploid and diploid life stages are indistinguishable based on morphology.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Similarity of form
   1. the similarity in the crystal structures of similar chemical compounds.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Similarity of form
   1. the similarity in the structure or processes of different organizations.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; sciences; social-science; sociology

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A one-to-one correspondence
   1. A bijection f such that both f and its inverse f⁻¹ are homomorphisms, that is, structure-preserving mappings.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable; group algebra

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A one-to-one correspondence
   1. a one-to-one correspondence between all the elements of two sets, e.g. the instances of two classes, or the records in two datasets.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computer; computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; science; sciences

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A one-to-one correspondence
   1. A morphism which has a two-sided inverse; the composition of the morphism and such an inverse yields either one of two identity morphisms (depending on the order of composition).

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** category-theory; computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences
