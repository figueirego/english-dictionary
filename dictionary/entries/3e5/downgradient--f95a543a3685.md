### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A position down along a gradient from a starting position.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. a location that receives groundwater from another location; similar to downstream.

   **Example:** Monitoring wells are installed downgradient of a contamination source to detect a contaminant plume.
