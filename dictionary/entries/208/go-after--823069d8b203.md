### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To pursue in attempt to catch another.

   **Example:** The downed officer couldn't go after the criminal.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To pursue an object or a goal.

   **Example:** In Little League, usually everyone goes after the ball.

   **Example:** Inspired, the scientist went right after the new idea.
