### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A long-established organization or type of organization, particularly one involved with education, public service, or charity work.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The University of the South Pacific is the only internationally accredited institution of higher education in Oceania.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A custom or practice of a society or community.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The institution of marriage is present in many cultures but its details vary widely across them.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The building or buildings which house such an organization.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** He's been in an institution since the crash.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A mental institution.

   **Usage:** countable; informal; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Any facility where people (especially those who are mentally or physically disabled or sick, or who are prisoners) are committed (confined), where their freedom to leave is restricted.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Any long established and respected place or business.

   **Usage:** countable; informal; uncountable

   **Example:** Over time, the local pub has become something of an institution.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A person long established in a place, position, or field.

   **Usage:** countable; informal; uncountable

   **Example:** She's not just any old scholar; she is an institution.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. The act of instituting something.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The institution of higher speed limits was a popular move but increased the severity of crashes.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. The act by which a bishop commits a cure of souls to a priest.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. That which institutes or instructs, particularly a textbook or system of elements or rules.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. A correctional institution.

   **Usage:** countable; informal; uncountable
