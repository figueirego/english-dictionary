**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Unable (or partially able) to hear.

   **Example:** My brother has been deaf since sustaining injuries in the war.

   **Example:** It's important for TV shows to provide closed captioning for the deaf.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Example:** Those people are deaf to reason.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

3. Of or relating to the community of deaf people.

   **Usage:** obsolete

   **Example:** a deaf nut; deaf corn
