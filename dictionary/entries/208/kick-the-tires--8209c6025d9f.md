<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To inspect something to ensure it meets expected standards or has favored characteristics, typically before committing to purchasing or otherwise selecting it.

   **Usage:** colloquial; idiomatic

   **Source synonyms:** kick the wheels; take for a spin; put someone through their paces

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To inspect something to ensure it meets expected standards or has favored characteristics, typically before committing to purchasing or otherwise selecting it.
   1. To make a superficial assessment of, or a show of interaction with, an item for sale, without any real intent to purchase.

      **Usage:** colloquial; idiomatic

      **Example:** Are you planning on buying anything, or are you just here to kick the tires?

      **Source synonyms:** kick the wheels; take for a spin; put someone through their paces

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
