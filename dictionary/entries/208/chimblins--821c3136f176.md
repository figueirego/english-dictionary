### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Small fragments or crumbs produced by nibbling or gnawing; tiny pieces left after something has been chewed, crumbled, or worn away, especially as by mice or vermin.

   **Usage:** Midlands; archaic; dialectal; plural; plural-only; chiefly Buckinghamshire

   **Example:** The mice have chimbled the cheese all to pieces, and left nothing but the chimblings

   **Source synonyms:** chumblings; nibblings; crumbs

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. (by extension, rare, family usage) Small scraps or morsels of food; leftover bits of meat or other fragments remaining after a meal.

   **Usage:** plural; plural-only; regional
