### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To alter the terms of an agreement or an agreed target, or the rules of a negotiation while it is ongoing, especially in an unfair way.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** This is a Government that meets its economic targets and does not move the goalposts.

   **Example:** We were given a moving date, and an exchange date, but the buyers kept moving the goalposts and giving us a later date.
