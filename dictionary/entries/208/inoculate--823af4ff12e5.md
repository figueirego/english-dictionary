### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To introduce an antigenic substance or vaccine into something (e.g. the body) or someone, such as to produce immunity to a specific disease.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** immunology; medicine; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** immunize; vaccinate; vaccine (archaic)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To safeguard or protect something as if by inoculation.

   **Usage:** broadly; transitive

   **Example:** Her upbringing overseas inoculated her against prejudice.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To add one substance to another.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The culture medium was inoculated with selenium to investigate the rate of uptake.

   **Source synonyms:** spike

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To graft by inserting buds.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to inoculate the bud of one tree or plant into another

   **Example:** to inoculate a tree

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To introduce into the mind (used especially of harmful ideas or principles).

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** to inoculate someone with treason or infidelity

   **Source synonyms:** imbue; implant
