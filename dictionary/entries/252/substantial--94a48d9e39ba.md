### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having a substance; actually existing.

   **Example:** substantial life

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Not imaginary; real; actual; true; veritable.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Corporeal; material; firm.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Having good substance; strong; stout; solid; firm.

   **Example:** substantial cloth

   **Example:** a substantial fence or wall

   **Source synonyms:** sturdy

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Addressing the substance of an injury

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Possessed of goods or an estate; moderately wealthy.

   **Example:** a substantial freeholder

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Large in size, quantity, or value; ample; significant.

   **Example:** He inherited a substantial amount of money from his uncle.

   **Example:** A substantial number of people went to the event.

   **Source synonyms:** healthy; serious

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Most important; essential.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. Satisfying; having sufficient substance to be nourishing or filling.

   **Example:** I don't just want a snack; I need something substantial.

   **Example:** Teddy had a few crackers in his backpack, but he needed something more substantial if he was to survive a three-day trek.
