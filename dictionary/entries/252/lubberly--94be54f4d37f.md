**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Clumsy and stupid; resembling a lubber (an inexperienced person).

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Lacking in seamanship; of or suitable to a landlubber who is new to being at sea and unfamiliar with the ways of a sailor.
