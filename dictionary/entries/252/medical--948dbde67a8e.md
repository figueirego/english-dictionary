### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of or pertaining to the practice of medicine.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; usually

   **Example:** Do you have any medical experience?

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Intended to have a therapeutic effect; medicinal.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; usually

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Requiring medical treatment.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; usually

   **Example:** A costly medical condition can bankrupt you if it doesn't kill you first.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Pertaining to the state of one's health.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; usually

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Pertaining to or requiring treatment by other than surgical means.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; usually

   **Example:** medical ward

   **Source synonyms:** nonsurgical; nonoperative

   **Source antonyms:** surgical; operative

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Pertaining to medication specifically (that is, pharmacotherapy), rather than to other aspects of medicine and surgery.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; usually

   **Example:** medical oncology, radiation oncology, and surgical oncology

   **Example:** surgical therapy only when medical therapy fails
