### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Resembling or worthy of a picture or painting; having the qualities of a picture or painting; pleasingly beautiful.

   **Example:** We looked down onto a beautiful, picturesque sunset over the ocean.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Strikingly graphic or vivid; having striking and vivid imagery.

   **Usage:** broadly; figuratively

   **Example:** picturesque language
