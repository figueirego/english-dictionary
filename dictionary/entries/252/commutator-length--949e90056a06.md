## Noun

### Forms

- commutator lengths — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The number of multiplicands needed, at a minimum, to express a given group element as a product of commutators.

   **Example:** In \#92;langlea,b\#124;b²\#61;1\#92;rangle, the commutator length of aba\#123;-2\#125;ba is two, since it equals (aba\#123;-1\#125;b\#123;-1\#125;)(b\#123;-1\#125;a\#123;-1\#125;ba) but isn't a commutator.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The supremum, over all elements of a given group's derived subgroup, of their commutator lengths.

   **Example:** The commutator length of \#92;langlea,b\#124;b²\#61;1\#92;rangle is at least two, since there's an element of commutator length two in it.
