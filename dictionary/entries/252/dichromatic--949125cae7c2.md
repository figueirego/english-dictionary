### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. having two colors.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. having a form of colorblindness in which only two of the three primary colors can be distinguished

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; pathology; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. having two independent channels for conveying color information in the eye.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences; zoology

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. occurring or existing in two different ornamentations or colors, typically as a form of sexual dimorphism.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. having two hues, either of which may be visible depending on both the concentration of the absorbing substance and the depth or thickness of the medium traversed, such as in pumpkin seed oil. A form of polychromatism.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; natural-sciences; optics; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. exhibiting dichroism; dichroic.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; natural-sciences; optics; physical-sciences; physics
