### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The sound of a quiet cough or of clearing one's throat.

   **Usage:** onomatopoeic

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Demanding attention.

   **Example:** Ahem! Could we please get started?

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Expresses disapproval or annoyance.

   **Example:** Ahem! In case you didn't notice, I did my share of the work, too.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Indicates the speaker does not wish to be perceived as rude, often intimating an impolite meaning to a third party that the speaker is not addressing.
   1. Indicates subtle sarcasm, polite lying, or euphemism.

      **Example:** I really (ahem!) liked the chocolate broccoli surprise.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**
