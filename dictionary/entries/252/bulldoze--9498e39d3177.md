### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To destroy with a bulldozer.

   **Example:** He's certainly very chirpy for a man whose house has just been bulldozed down.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To push someone over by heading straight over them. Often used in conjunction with "over".

   **Example:** He just ran across the field bulldozing everyone over.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To push through forcefully.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To push into a heap, as a bulldozer does.

   **Example:** Again the animal had bulldozed all of its bedding into a heap at one end of its cage.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To shoot down an idea immediately and forcefully.

   **Example:** That was a good suggestion, but you just bulldozed it.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To intimidate; to restrain or coerce by intimidation or violence; used originally of the intimidation of black voters in Louisiana.

   **Usage:** US; dated; slang
