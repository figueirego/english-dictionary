### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Capitalist ideology which stresses the importance of deregulation and private ownership over the means of production.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** capitalism; government; human-sciences; ideology; philosophy; politics; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Capitalist ideology that focuses on the compatibility of support for civil liberties and free markets on the one hand and a concern for social justice or the worst-off’s well-being on the other.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** capitalism; government; human-sciences; ideology; philosophy; politics; sciences
