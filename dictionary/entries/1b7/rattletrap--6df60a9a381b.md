## Noun · Record 2

### Forms

- rattletraps — plural

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A mechanical device, particularly an automobile, that is worn out, run down, or mechanically unreliable as indicated by noises it makes in operation.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** Mom always worried about our safety in my friend's rattletrap. I told her not to worry, as it can't go fast enough to be dangerous.

   **Source synonyms:** banger; bucket of bolts; banger (UK); beater; bucket of bolts; bucket of rust; clunker; crate; crock (UK); flivver (Canada; US; dated); gas guzzler; hooptie; jalopy (US; dated); junker (Canada; US); lemon; old banger (UK); rattletrap; rustbucket; shed (UK); shitbox (vulgar); shitpot (vulgar); shitcan (vulgar); shitbucket (vulgar); tin Lizzie; wreck

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Any piece of miscellaneous equipment or junk.

   **Usage:** dated

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. A type of noisemaker in which the teeth of a gear repeatedly bend and release a stiff tongue.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. A person's mouth.

   **Usage:** dated; derogatory; slang

   **Example:** Shut your rattletrap!

   **Source synonyms:** trap; yap
