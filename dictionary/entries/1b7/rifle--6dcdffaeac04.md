### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To quickly search through many items (such as papers, the contents of a drawer, a pile of clothing).

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** She made a mess when she rifled through the stack of papers, looking for the title document.

   **Source synonyms:** riffle

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To commit robbery or theft.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To search with intent to steal.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** ransack; pillage; plunder; loot

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To strip of goods; to rob; to pillage.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To seize and bear away by force; to snatch away; to carry off.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

   **Usage:** transitive
