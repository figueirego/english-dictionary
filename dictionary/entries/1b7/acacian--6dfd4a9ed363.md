### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A follower of Acacius of Caesarea, a 4th-century moderate Arianist bishop whose followers signed the Nicene Creed but adopted a different theological understanding of it based on the Father merely resembling the Son.

   **Usage:** historical
