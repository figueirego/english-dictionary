### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To be troubled; to give way to mental anxiety or doubt.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Disturb the peace of mind of; afflict with mental agitation or distress; to exercise.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Your tone of voice worries me.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To harass; to irritate or distress.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The President was worried into military action by persistent advisors.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To seize or shake by the throat, especially of a dog or wolf.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Your dog’s been worrying sheep again.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To touch repeatedly; to fiddle with.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To strangle.

   **Usage:** Scotland; obsolete; transitive
