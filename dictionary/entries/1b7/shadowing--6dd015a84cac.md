<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** espionage; government; military; politics; war

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**
