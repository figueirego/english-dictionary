## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A province in the region of Emilia-Romagna, in northern Italy.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A coastal city, the present-day capital of the province of Ravenna.

   **Example:** Ravenna served as the capital city of the Western Roman Empire from 402 until the latter's collapse in 476; thereafter it was the capital of the Ostrogothic Kingdom and then of the Byzantine Exarchate of Ravenna until the last exarch was executed by the Lombards in 751.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A city, the county seat of Portage County, Ohio, United States, named after the Italian city.
