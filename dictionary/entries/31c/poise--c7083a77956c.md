   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To be of a given weight; to weigh.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To add weight to, to weigh down.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To hold (something) with or against something else in equilibrium; to balance, counterpose.

   **Usage:** archaic

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To hold (something) in equilibrium, to hold balanced and ready; to carry (something) ready to be used.

   **Example:** I poised the crowbar in my hand, and waited.

   **Example:** to poise the scales of a balance

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

**Source sense R2.S7**
