## Noun

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An external body part that projects from the body.

   **Example:** bodily appendage

   **Example:** The insect has a pair of sensory appendages.

   **Source synonyms:** extremity; member

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A natural prolongation or projection from a part of any organism.

   **Example:** The small building was added as an appendage to the main structure.

   **Source synonyms:** outgrowth; appendix; process

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A part that is joined to something larger.

   **Example:** He disliked being treated as a mere appendage to the committee.

   **Source synonyms:** appendix; annex
