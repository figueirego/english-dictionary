### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To give or bestow, as a gift from God.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** The Lord has blessed me with a beautiful family!

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To give something favourable (without the implication of religious faith).

   **Usage:** broadly; slang

   **Example:** Hey, could you bless me with some cash?

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see bless, someone, with.

   **Example:** The priest blessed the worshippers with some holy water.
