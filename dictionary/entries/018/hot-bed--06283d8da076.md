### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A mass of organic matter decomposing and giving off heat from microbial metabolic action.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A garden bed using such a heat-generating mass; a hotbox.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** agriculture; business; horticulture; lifestyle

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A central point of activity, relating to a specific task.

   **Usage:** broadly; figuratively

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A bed shared by workers, sailors, or soldiers operating in shifts or watches (named for the idea that it is still warm when the next occupant gets into it).

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A heated print bed, so as to help the lower levels of extruded filament stick together.

   **Usage:** 3D printing
