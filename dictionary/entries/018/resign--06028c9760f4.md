### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To give up; to relinquish ownership of.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To hand over (something to someone), place into the care or control of another.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To voluntarily leave (a job or position), in particular a hard-fought position of high status.

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** I am resigning in protest of the unfair treatment of our employees.

   **Example:** He resigned the crown to follow his heart.

   **Source synonyms:** vacate

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To submit passively; to give up as hopeless or inevitable.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He had no choice but to resign the game and let his opponent become the champion.
