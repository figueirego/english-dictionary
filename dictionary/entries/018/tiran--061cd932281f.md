### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Ellipsis of Tiran Island&#58; an island formerly of Egypt, since 2017 of Saudi Arabia, located between the southern end of the Sinai Peninsula and Arabian Peninsula.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; ellipsis

   **Alternative form of:** Tiran Island

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Ellipsis of Tiran Strait&#58; a strait in the Red Sea, Asia, between this island and the Sinai.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; ellipsis

   **Alternative form of:** [Tiran Strait](../272/tiran-strait--9c9971eaef7e.md)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Ellipsis of Straits of Tiran&#58; a straits in the Red Sea, Asia, between the Sinai Peninsula and Arabian Peninsula, containing this island, which separates the Gulf of Aqaba and the main body of the Red Sea.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; ellipsis

   **Alternative form of:** [Straits of Tiran](../01a/straits-of-tiran--06abd059598e.md)
