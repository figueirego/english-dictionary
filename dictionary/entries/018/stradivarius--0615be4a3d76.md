### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A stringed instrument built by members of the Stradivari family, particularly Antonio Stradivari.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A violin made by another manufacturer, named to capitalise on the fame of the instruments made by Antonio Stradivari.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

   **Source synonyms:** Strad

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A name invoked as a standard of excellence in unrelated fields.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** A Bath boat is the Stradivarius of destroyers!
