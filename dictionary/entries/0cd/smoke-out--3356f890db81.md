### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To drive out (something or somebody) using smoke.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** We smoked the critters out of their hole.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To expose (something or somebody).

   **Usage:** broadly; figuratively; idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** The culprit will be smoked out by the end of the day.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To offer to smoke cannabis with someone; to smoke cannabis with someone.

   **Usage:** slang; transitive

   **Example:** I didn't have any weed, so Joe smoked me out with his stash.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To smoke too much (usually cannabis); to become too stoned.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Example:** He was smoked out and really needed to take a break.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To run out of tobacco or cannabis; to be emptied of tobacco or cannabis.

   **Usage:** passive; slang; usually

   **Example:** We passed the bong around until it was smoked out.
