### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To move without purpose or specified destination; often in search of livelihood.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** to wander over the fields

   **Source synonyms:** err; roam

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To stray; stray from one's course; err.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** A writer wanders from his subject.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To commit adultery.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source synonyms:** cheat

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To go somewhere indirectly or at varying speeds; to move in a curved path.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source synonyms:** drift

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Of the mind, to lose focus or clarity of argument or attention.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source synonyms:** distract; scatter
