### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A river in Kent, England, running from the confluence of the Great Stour and Little Stour to the English Channel at Pegwell Bay.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>
