### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A circus clown routine in which an implausibly large number of clowns climb into or out of a small car.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** comedy; entertainment; lifestyle

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The undersized car used in a circus clown car routine.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A very small car.

   **Usage:** broadly; derogatory; often

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A car or small enclosed space with too many people or things in it.

   **Usage:** broadly

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Any process with a comically large number of participants, especially participants who themselves are comical or ridiculous.

   **Usage:** derogatory; figuratively; often

   **Example:** clown-car primaries

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; politics

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A car for clowns, especially the car of a circus train that carries the clowns.

   **Usage:** literally

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A car that looks gaudy or otherwise ridiculous.

   **Usage:** derogatory
