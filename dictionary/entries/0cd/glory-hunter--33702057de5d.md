**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A fan of a sports club who supports the club only due to its current success or popularity.

   **Example:** I heard he started supporting Man City right around 2019! What a glory hunter!

   **Example:** He really only likes the club because they've won the past few years. He's just a glory hunter and has no interest in the club's history at all.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports
