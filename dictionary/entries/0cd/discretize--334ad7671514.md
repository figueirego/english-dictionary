### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To convert (a continuous space) into an equivalent discrete space, often for the purposes of easier calculation.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To represent (any spectrum of reality) as a set of discrete categories or classes; to categorize (a spectral aspect of reality) by lumping together some of its finer distinctions.

   **Usage:** broadly; transitive

   **Example:** A rainbow truly contains thousands of color values but can usually be discretized by human sentience into bands of primary and secondary colors.
