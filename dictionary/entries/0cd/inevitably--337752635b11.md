## Adverb

### Pronunciation

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In a manner that is impossible to avoid or prevent.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Inevitably, all creatures eventually die.

   **Example:** The sun inevitably rises.

   **Example:** Despite the town's best efforts, the dam inevitably gave way.

   **Example:** The inevitably cold winter approaches.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. As usual; predictably; as expected.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Inevitably, the next-door neighbour began to mow his lawn just as she lays down her head after a long night shift.

   **Example:** The child inevitably began to cry when his mother went to work.

   **Example:** The inevitably long line of customers queued for the latest 'Harry Potter'.
