## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The medical theory and practices of Chinese culture, especially herbal medicine, acupuncture, tui na, Daoyin and osteopathy, for preventing or treating illness, or promoting health and well-being; in China (and to an extent elsewhere) still often used in concert with scientific medicine (as complementary medicine).

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** Chinese medicine; Chinese traditional medicine; TCM
