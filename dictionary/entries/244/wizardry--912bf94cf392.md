   **Source synonyms:** black art; witchery; wizardcraft; bewitchery; black arts; charmwork; conjuration; conjuring; craft (obsolete); dark arts; diablerie; druidcraft; dweomercraft (rare); dwimmer (neologism; obsolete); dwimmercraft; enchantment; goety (obsolete); gypsycraft; hexcraft; hexerei; jugglery (archaic); magic; mojo; sorcery; sortilege; spellcasting; spellcraft; spellwork; thaumaturgy; wandwork; warlockry; whistness (UK; dialectal); witchdom; witchery; witchcraft; witchwork; wizardry; wizardcraft (rare); wonderworking

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
