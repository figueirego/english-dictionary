## Prep\_phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. From the beginning; starting with no advantage or prior preparation.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** There were so many errors in the program that the programmer decided to rewrite it from scratch.

   **Example:** When the writer finished writing his book, it was stolen and now he has to rewrite it from scratch.

   **Example:** He had no money and no rich friends, so he had to build his business from scratch.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. From basic materials or raw ingredients.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** She said she wanted to build a new house from scratch.

   **Example:** He was out of pancake mix so he had to make the batter from scratch.
