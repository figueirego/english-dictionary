### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The profession or art of devising, granting and blazoning coats of arms, tracing genealogies and ruling on questions of protocol or rank.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Rouge Dragon Pursuivant is a specialist in heraldry at the College of Arms.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An armorial ensign along with its history and description.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Pageantry.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Onlookers were impressed by the rich and colorful heraldry.
