### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To join something, after signing.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To commit oneself, as to a project, a goal, an organization, a cause.

   **Example:** I never signed on for this.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To begin broadcasting a radio or television signal, usually at the beginning of a broadcasting day and after being off the air for several hours.

   **Example:** Years ago, the TV station would sign on at 5 a.m., but now it broadcasts 24 hours a day.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** broadcasting; media

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To log on; to start using a computer, radio, or other multicast device; to start talking.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Source synonyms:** log in; log on; sign in

   **Source antonyms:** log off; log out; sign off; sign out

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To apply to receive unemployment benefits.

   **Usage:** UK; intransitive
