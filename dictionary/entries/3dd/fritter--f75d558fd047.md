### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To squander or waste time, money, or other resources; e.g. occupy oneself idly or without clear purpose, to tinker with an unimportant part of a project, to dally, sometimes as a form of procrastination.

   **Usage:** intransitive; often; around; or away; around; or away

   **Example:** I was supposed to do work, but I frittered around all afternoon.

   **Example:** He can’t figure out how to finish the paper he’s writing, so he’s resorted to frittering with the fonts.

   **Example:** It is quite possible to fritter one's life away in answer to the endless calls of others.

   **Source context (verbatim):** with about

   **Usage:** transitive
