## Adjective · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˌɡʊd ˈfeɪθ/ — source labels: General-American; Received-Pronunciation

### Forms

- more good faith — comparative
- most good faith — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Having or done with good, honest intentions; well-intentioned.

   **Example:** A good faith buyer.

   **Example:** A good faith attempt.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Presuming that all parties to a discussion are honest and intend to act in a fair and appropriate manner.

   **Example:** Good faith bargaining.
