### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A sheep of a Spanish breed with long, fine hair.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The wool of this sheep.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The fabric made from this wool (or from any similar yarn).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A dress made out of merino fabric.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**
