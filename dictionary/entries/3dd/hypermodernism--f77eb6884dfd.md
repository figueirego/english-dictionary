## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The state or condition of being hypermodern.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An art movement distinguished from modernism and postmodernism chiefly by its extreme and antithetical (inverted) approach.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** art; arts
