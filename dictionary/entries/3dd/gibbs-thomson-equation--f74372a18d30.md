### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An equation for the structural depression in the freezing point or melting point in a confined geometry, in its compact form&#58; Δ,T\_m(x)=(k\_GT)/x, where the Gibbs–Thomson coefficient k\_GT assumes different values for different liquids and different interfacial geometries (e.g. spherical, cylindrical or planar).
