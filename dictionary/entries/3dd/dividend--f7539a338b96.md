### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A cash payment of money by a company to its shareholders, usually made periodically (e.g., quarterly or annually).

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A number or expression that is to be divided by another.

   **Example:** In "42 ÷ 3" the dividend is the 42.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** arithmetic

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Beneficial results from a metaphorical investment (of time, effort, etc.)

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** His 10,000 hours of practice and recitals eventually paid dividends when he become first-chair violinist.
