### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. (historical medicine) A substance regarded as having a weak narcotic effect.

   **Extractor diagnostics (not usage labels):** no-gloss

**Supplemental definition (evidence reviewed; source gap preserved):** Rafinesque’s original Medical Flora volumeII, printed p29, uses subnarcotic in a noun series introduced by an indefinite article and applies it to Lycopus virginicus. The same paragraph expressly describes that plant as a particularly mild narcotic. Together these establish the nominal class and its intended weak effect without relying solely on the prefix or the existing adjective. The definition reports the historical categorization and does not validate Rafinesque’s therapeutic or safety assertions.
