### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A geographical area under the remote control of a country; especially to extract resources or exploit labor from that area.

   **Example:** Much of the eastern United States was formerly a British colony; other areas were French, Spanish, Dutch, or Swedish colonies.

   **Example:** Bermuda is a crown colony of Great Britain.

   **Example:** Spain still has colonies in North Africa.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A group of people who settle an area and maintain ties to their country of origin.

   **Example:** a colony of British expats in Spain

   **Example:** The Amana Colonies in Iowa were settled by people from Germany.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A group of people with similar interests, occupations, or characteristics, living in a particular area; the area such people occupy.

   **Example:** a nudist colony; the statue was put up right in the middle of the artist colony

   **Example:** a leper colony on the outskirts of town; most buildings in the penal colony were made of concrete

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A group of organisms of same or different species living together in close association.

   **Example:** ant colony; coral colony

   **Example:** a colony of specialized polyps and medusoids

   **Example:** colonies of stem cells

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An apartment complex or neighborhood.

   **Usage:** India

   **Example:** Our colony is quite small, but each apartment is large.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A local group of Beaver Scouts.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A potential new chapter of a fraternity or sorority awaiting official recognition from their headquarters.
