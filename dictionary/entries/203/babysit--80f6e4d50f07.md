### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To watch or tend someone else's child for a period of time, often for money.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** My daughter is babysitting for the Morgans at number ten, who are going out on a date night.

   **Example:** We need someone to babysit our children while we go to the theater.

   **Source synonyms:** childsit

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To watch or tend a thing or process without normally intervening in it, e.g. as a precaution should an emergency happen.

   **Usage:** informal; transitive

   **Example:** The reaction takes several hours, so we leave a graduate student to babysit it.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To watch or attend anything or anyone more closely than ought to be needed; to have to help or coax too much.

   **Usage:** informal; transitive

   **Example:** He left me to babysit the new guy while he got some work done.

   **Source synonyms:** coddle
