### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To surrender on stipulated terms, end all resistance, give up, go along with or comply.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** He argued and hollered for so long that I finally capitulated just to make him stop.

   **Source synonyms:** wave the white flag; see Thesaurus&#58;surrender

   **Source antonyms:** beat; bring down; convince (obsolete); chew up; eat someone for breakfast (slang); eat someone's lunch (US; slang); flog (Australia; New-Zealand); humble; kick ass; malavogue (Ireland; dated); murdelize; outfox; outguess; outrun; outsmart; outwit; overcome; overpower; overwin; own; pulverise; pwn; rape (offensive; slang); reduce; spank; steal the show; subdue; subvert; surmount; thrash; trounce; vanquish

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To draw up in chapters, heads or articles; to enumerate, specify.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive; obsolete

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To draw up articles of agreement with; to propose terms, treat, bargain, parley.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To make conditions, stipulate, agree, formulate, conclude (upon something).

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive
