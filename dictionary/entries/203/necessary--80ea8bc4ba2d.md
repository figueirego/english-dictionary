<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Required, essential, whether logically inescapable or needed in order to achieve a desired result or avoid some penalty.

   **Example:** Although I wished to think that all was false, it was yet necessary that I, who thus thought, must in some sense exist.

   **Example:** It is absolutely necessary that you call and confirm your appointment.

   **Source synonyms:** basic; essential; indispensable; necessar; necessary; necessitous; needed; prerequisite; required; requisite

   **Source antonyms:** unnecessary

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Unavoidable, inevitable.

   **Example:** If it is absolutely necessary to use public computers, you should plan ahead and forward your e-mail to a temporary, disposable account.

   **Source synonyms:** inevitable; natural

   **Source antonyms:** evitable; incidental; impossible

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

   **Usage:** obsolete
