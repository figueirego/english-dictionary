## Adjective · Record 1

### Forms

- more unsettled — comparative
- most unsettled — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Disturbed, upset.

   **Example:** I was unsettled by the sudden outburst, and since I didn't know what to do I just stood there, confused.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Not in a steady condition, uncertain, subject to change.

   **Source synonyms:** problematic

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Not populated, having no settlers or other inhabitants.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Unpaid.

   **Example:** We need to settle this bill; even if you think the charge is too high we can't just leave it unsettled.
