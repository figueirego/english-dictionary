## Proper noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A period, sometimes confounded with the Age of Revolution in Europe and/or the pre-March era (Vormärz) specifically in Germany; variously beginning in 1789 (the French Revolution) or (in Germany) either in 1815 (German Confederation) or 1830 (French July Revolution) and leading up to the Revolutions of 1848.

   **Usage:** European

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** history; human-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A period from 1848-49 (Revolutions of 1848) characterised by political upheavals throughout Europe which aimed to replace monarchies with independent nation-states.

   **Usage:** European

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** history; human-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A general term for similar revolutions in recent times; cf. Arab Spring

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** history; human-sciences; sciences
