### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of various species of flowering plants in certain genera in family Asteraceae, that typically repel insects&#58;
   1. mostly in two tribes of subfamily Asteroideae

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of various species of flowering plants in certain genera in family Asteraceae, that typically repel insects&#58;
   1. mostly in two tribes of subfamily Asteroideae
      1. in tribe Astereae
         1. Conyza spp. (butterweeds or horseweed)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of various species of flowering plants in certain genera in family Asteraceae, that typically repel insects&#58;
   1. mostly in two tribes of subfamily Asteroideae
      1. in tribe Astereae
         1. Erigeron spp., especially Erigeron acre (blue fleabane)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any of various species of flowering plants in certain genera in family Asteraceae, that typically repel insects&#58;
   1. mostly in two tribes of subfamily Asteroideae
      1. in tribe Inuleae
         1. Inula spp. (yellowheads)

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Any of various species of flowering plants in certain genera in family Asteraceae, that typically repel insects&#58;
   1. mostly in two tribes of subfamily Asteroideae
      1. in tribe Inuleae
         1. Pluchea spp. (camphorweeds)

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Any of various species of flowering plants in certain genera in family Asteraceae, that typically repel insects&#58;
   1. mostly in two tribes of subfamily Asteroideae
      1. in tribe Inuleae
         1. Pulicaria spp. (false fleabane), especially common fleabane (Pulicaria dysenterica subsp. dysenterica, formerly Inula dysenterica)

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Any of various species of flowering plants in certain genera in family Asteraceae, that typically repel insects&#58;
   1. Also, in subfamily Cichorioideae, Vernonia spp. (ironweeds).
