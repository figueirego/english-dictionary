### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A checkmate pattern in which a queen, supported by a piece of its same color, is placed diagonally adjacent to the enemy king. The two flight squares that the queen does not control are occupied by pieces of the king's own color.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** board-games; chess; games
