**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** headroom; ullage

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** countable; informal; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** brainspace

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. One's mental state or state of mind.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

4. A time and opportunity for reflection and thinking.

   **Usage:** countable; informal; uncountable
