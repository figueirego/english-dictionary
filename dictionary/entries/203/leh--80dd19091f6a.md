**Source sense R1.S1**

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

   **Usage:** interrogative

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used to highlight or relay noteworthy information.

   **Usage:** declarative

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

   **Usage:** declarative

   **Example:** Many houses were destroyed leh.

   **Usage:** declarative

   **Example:** You all damn noisy leh how to sleep?

   **Usage:** declarative
