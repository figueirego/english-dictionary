## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The misrepresentation of one's own true motive.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** bad-faith interpretation

   **Source synonyms:** dishonesty; insincerity; bloody-mindedness

   **Source antonyms:** good faith

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An intent to deceive or mislead another to gain some advantage; dishonesty or fraud in a transaction (such as knowingly misrepresenting the quality of something that is being bought or sold).

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A malicious motive by a party in a lawsuit, which has an effect on the ability to maintain causes of action and obtain legal remedies.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The existentialist concept of denying one's total freedom of will.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; philosophy; sciences
