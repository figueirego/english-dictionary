### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The most widespread dialect of Serbo-Croatian, which is used as the dialectal basis for the standard Bosnian, Croatian, Montenegrin, and Serbian versions of the language. The word what is usually rendered as što, whence the name.
