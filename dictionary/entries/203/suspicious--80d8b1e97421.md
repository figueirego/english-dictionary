### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Arousing suspicion.

   **Example:** His suspicious behaviour brought him to the attention of the police.

   **Source synonyms:** questionable; queer

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
