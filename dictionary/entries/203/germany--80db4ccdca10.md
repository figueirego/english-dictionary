### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A nation or civilization occupying the country around the Rhine, Elbe, and upper Danube Rivers in Central Europe, taken as a whole under its various governments.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A nation or civilization occupying the country around the Rhine, Elbe, and upper Danube Rivers in Central Europe, taken as a whole under its various governments.
   1. The German Sprachraum; the countries and territories within Europe where German is or was the primary language.

      **Usage:** countable; historical; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The principal state in this country, including
   1. A nominal medieval kingdom forming part of the Carolingian and Holy Roman Empires; (metonymic, now uncommon) the Holy Roman Empire in its entirety; (metonymic, obsolete) the Austrian Habsburg empire in its entirety.

      **Usage:** countable; historical; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The principal state in this country, including
   1. An empire formed by Prussia in 1871 with its capital at Berlin.

      **Usage:** countable; historical; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The principal state in this country, including
   1. A republic formed in 1918 with its capital at Berlin, inclusive of the Nazi regime who controlled it after 1933.

      **Usage:** countable; historical; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The principal state in this country, including
   1. The socialist republic formed in 1949 with its capital at Berlin, more often known in English as East Germany.

      **Usage:** countable; historical; uncommon; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The principal state in this country, including
   1. A country in Central Europe, formed in 1949 as West Germany, with its provisional capital Bonn until 1990, when it incorporated East Germany. Official name&#58; Federal Republic of Germany. Capital and largest city&#58; Berlin.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. The various states in this country either over time or during periods of disunity and division, sometimes (inexact) inclusive of the Holy Roman Empire and Austria-Hungary's other holdings.
