### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A photograph of a person with an inscription (often on the body or a piece of paper) used on the Internet as an autograph of a celebrity or as a sign of love for a celebrity from a fan.

   **Usage:** Internet

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A meet-and-greet with a K-pop artist where fans can ask questions, receive autographs, take photos or receive hugs.

   **Usage:** South Korean idol fandom

   **Source synonyms:** fansigning

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A photograph or video of a minor or target containing an inscription (often their username, a group name, or a slur) coerced by predatory online networks such as 764 or the com as a form of humiliation, extortion, or proof of grooming.

   **Usage:** Internet; criminal law
