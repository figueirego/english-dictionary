## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The situation where a project (such as a movie, musical record, or piece of software) is mired in problems that delay its production and release.

   **Usage:** colloquial; uncountable

   **Example:** The third movie in the trilogy has been in development hell for ten years already! I want to see how the story ends!

   **Source synonyms:** development limbo; production hell
