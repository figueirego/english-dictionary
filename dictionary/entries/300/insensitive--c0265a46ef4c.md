### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Expressing or feeling little or no concern, care, compassion, or consideration for the feelings, emotions, sentiments, or concerns of other people; inconsiderate or incompassionate.

   **Source synonyms:** ruthless; uncompassionate; unsensitive

   **Source antonyms:** compassionate; ruthful; sensitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Not expressing normal physical feeling.

   **Source synonyms:** anesthetic; insensate; insensible; unsensitive; dead

   **Source antonyms:** sensate; sensitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Not expressing normal emotional feelings; cold; tactless; undiplomatic.

   **Source synonyms:** harsh; rude; unsensitive; aweless; improper; bad-mannered; barbaric; bold (Ireland); bumptious; crude; churlish; impudent; discourteous; gauche; ill-bred; ill-mannered; impolite; incivil (rare); insolent; lack-grace; mismannered; rantipole (obsolete); rough; rude; uncivil; uncivilized; uncourteous; uncouth; uncultured; underbred; unmannerly; unpolished; unpolite; unrefined; brutal; callous; cold; insensate (formal); hardhanded; cold-eyed; cruel; dour; thick-skinned; flint-hearted; forbidding; frosty; grim; hard; hard-boiled; hardened; hardhearted; heartless; harsh; humorless; icy; iron-hearted; insensitive; marble-hearted; rigorous; rockhearted; severe; stern; stonehearted; stony; stonyhearted; strict; tough; unsympathetic; unmoved

   **Source antonyms:** sensitive; tactful; warm

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**
