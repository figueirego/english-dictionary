### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make similar or common.

   **Example:** He attempted to commonize the various standards by ensuring a similar format and implementation.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To deprecate the importance of, or make ordinary.

   **Example:** She gave each participant a trophy, regardless of win or loss, to commonize the award for winning.
