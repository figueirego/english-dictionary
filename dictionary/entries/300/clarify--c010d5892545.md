### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make or become clear or bright by freeing from impurities or turbidity.

   **Usage:** ergative; such as wine or syrup

   **Example:** What's the best way to clarify cooking oil?

   **Example:** Leave the wine for 24 hours and it will clarify.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of liquids

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To make or become clear or easily understood; to explain or resolve in order to remove doubt or obscurity.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** Please clarify what you mean by this.

   **Example:** Over time, the situation gradually clarified.

   **Source synonyms:** explicitize; get straight; bring home

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To glorify.

   **Usage:** obsolete
