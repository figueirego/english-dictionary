### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of an allocation, in proportion to some factor that can be exactly calculated.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Tenants who have given 30 days' notice who do not leave on the last day of the month will be charged on a pro rata basis.

   **Example:** The workers' overtime is calculated on pro rata basis.
