### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of creating something, or (more broadly) any process involving the specified thing.

   **Usage:** morpheme

   **Example:** lith- (“stone”) + -i- + -faction → lithifaction (“the compaction and cementation of sediment into rock”)

   **Example:** aer- (“air”) + -i- + -faction → aerifaction (“aeration, hyperinflation of the lungs”)
