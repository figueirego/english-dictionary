### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A vehicle, usually a car, that may be hired for single journeys by members of the public, particularly one with an automated meter to calculate the fare.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** I cabbed a package downtown in my father's taxi.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An aircraft used for practicing ground manoeuvres.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; slang; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** aeronautics; aerospace; aviation; business; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The movement of an aircraft across an airport's surface under its own power; a phase of aircraft operation involving this movement.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** During taxi, the flaps and slats are extended and the second engine (if not already running) is started.

   **Example:** Seat belts must be kept fastened during taxi, takeoff, turbulence, and landing.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** aeronautics; aerospace; aviation; business; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Synonym of shared taxi.

   **Usage:** Iran; South-Africa; countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** shared taxi (synonym; synonym-of)
