### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An insular state of the United States, formerly a territory. Capital&#58; Honolulu.

   **Source synonyms:** Aloha State; Hawaii State; State of Hawaii

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An archipelago of the Pacific Ocean, between North America and Oceania.

   **Source synonyms:** Hawaiian Islands; Islands of Hawaii; Sandwich Islands (obsolete)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An island of Hawaii archipelago, Pacific Ocean, of Hawaii County, State of Hawaii, United States. The largest of the Hawaiian Islands.

   **Usage:** specifically

   **Source synonyms:** Big Island (informal); Hawaii Island; Island of Hawaii
