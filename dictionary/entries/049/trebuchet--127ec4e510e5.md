### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A medieval siege engine consisting of a large pivoting arm heavily weighted on one end.

   **Example:** Medieval trebuchets are said to have been capable of launching 90-kg projectiles over distances of more than 300 meters.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A torture device for dunking suspected witches by means of a chair attached to the end of a long pole.
