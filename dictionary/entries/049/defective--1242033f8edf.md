**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Source antonyms:** complete; perfect; undefective

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a lexeme

**Source sense R1.S3**

   **Usage:** Arabic

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** grammar; human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a verb

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Not capable of representing all the phonemic distinctions of a language it is used to write.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** communications; journalism; literature; media; orthography; publishing; writing

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a script
