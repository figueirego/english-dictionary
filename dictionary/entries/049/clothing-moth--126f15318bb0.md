## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any moth that feeds on natural fiber, especially the following from subfamily Tineinae&#58;
   1. Niditinea fuscella (brown-dotted clothes moth)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any moth that feeds on natural fiber, especially the following from subfamily Tineinae&#58;
   1. Tinea pellionella (case-bearing clothes moth)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any moth that feeds on natural fiber, especially the following from subfamily Tineinae&#58;
   1. Tineola bisselliella (common clothes moth, webbing clothes moth)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any moth that feeds on natural fiber, especially the following from subfamily Tineinae&#58;
   1. Trichophaga tapetzella (carpet moth, tapestry moth)

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Any moth that feeds on natural fiber, especially the following from subfamily Tineinae&#58;
