### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To assess the similarities and differences between two or more things \["to compare X with Y"\]. Having made the comparison of X with Y, one might have found it similar to Y or different from Y.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Compare the tiger's coloration with that of the zebra.

   **Example:** You can't compare my problems and yours.

   **Source synonyms:** set against; weigh against; collate

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To declare two things to be similar in some respect \["to compare X to Y"\].

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Astronomers have compared comets to dirty snowballs.

   **Source synonyms:** liken

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To form the three degrees of comparison of (an adjective).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** We compare "good" as "good", "better", "best".

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** grammar; human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To be similar (often used in the negative).

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** A sapling and a fully-grown oak tree do not compare.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To get; to obtain.

   **Usage:** obsolete
