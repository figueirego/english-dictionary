## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A group of individuals who didn't adhear to the condemnation of Nestorius at the Council of Ephesus (431 CE).

   **Usage:** historical; plural; plural-only

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A faction comprised of people who initially refused to acknowledge the authority of the Council of Chalcedon.

   **Usage:** historical; plural; plural-only

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A radical Monophysite sect in the era after the Council of Chalcedon (451 CE). This sect eventually split into three groups, the Anthropomorphites, Barsanuphists, and Esianists, before being absorbed by the Jacobites in the 9th century.

   **Usage:** historical; plural; plural-only

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A 5th century faction of Eutychians who had no leader.

   **Usage:** historical; plural; plural-only

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The followers of Severus of Antioch.

   **Usage:** historical; plural; plural-only

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The followers of Patriarch Paul of Alexandria after his deposition, and who later aligned themselves with the Miaphysites.

   **Usage:** historical; plural; plural-only

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A 6th century sect with no sucession of priests, who professed to uphold a valid Eucharist by baking bread with the crumbs of blessed bread baked inside it.

   **Usage:** historical; plural; plural-only

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A congregation whose priest refuses alleigance to their bishop.

   **Usage:** archaic; plural; plural-only; theological

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A sect of the Levellers and free socagers who claimed no feudal superior but the King.

   **Usage:** archaic; historical; plural; plural-only
