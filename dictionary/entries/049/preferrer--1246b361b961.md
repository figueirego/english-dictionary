### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who prefers (a stated person or thing).
   1. A person who likes one thing more than another; a person who favours something.

      **Example:** a preferrer of red wine

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person who prefers (a stated person or thing).
   1. A person who advances or promotes someone or something.

      **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A person who prefers (a stated person or thing).
   1. A person who presents or submits something to an authority.
