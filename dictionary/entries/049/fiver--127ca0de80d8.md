### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A banknote with a value of five units of currency.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Example:** Do you have a fiver I could borrow? I can pay you back tomorrow.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The value in money that this represents.

   **Usage:** broadly; slang

   **Example:** I bought the chocolates; they were only a fiver.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A clenched fist.

   **Usage:** colloquial

   **Source synonyms:** bunch of fives

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A mathematical puzzle played on a 5 × 5 grid.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A Zaydi Shiite Muslim, who disagrees with the majority of Shiites on the identity of the Fifth Imam.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Islam; lifestyle; religion

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A person who gives five percent of their income or five hours a week of their time to charity (a reduction of ten percent tithing).

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** lifestyle; religion
