## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Being at the scale of, or having the scope of, local and regional.
   1. Restricted to a certain limited area of the body (of a human, animal, or plant).

      **Usage:** especially

      **Example:** locoregional anesthesia; locoregional therapy; locoregional nerve block

      **Example:** chemotherapy for breast cancer by locoregional irradiation

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

      **Source synonyms:** regional

      **Source antonyms:** global; universal; systemic; systemic; bodywide
