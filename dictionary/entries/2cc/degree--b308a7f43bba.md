### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A completed curriculum of study in tertiary education.

   **Example:** She has two bachelor's degrees and is studying towards a master's degree.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** education

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A unit of measurement of angle equal to ¹⁄₃₆₀ of a circle's circumference.

   **Example:** A right angle is a ninety-degree angle.

   **Example:** Most humans have a field of vision of almost 180 degrees.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A unit of measurement of temperature on any of several scales, such as Celsius or Fahrenheit.

   **Example:** 212 degrees Fahrenheit is equal to 100 degrees Celsius.

   **Example:** Water boils at 100 degrees Celsius.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The sum of the exponents of a term; the order of a polynomial.

   **Example:** A quadratic polynomial is a polynomial of degree 2.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** algebra; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The dimensionality of a field extension.

   **Usage:** field theory

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The number of edges that a vertex takes part in; a valency.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** graph-theory; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; logic; mathematics; philosophy; sciences
