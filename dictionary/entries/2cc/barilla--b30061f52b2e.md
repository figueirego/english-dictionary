### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of several not closely related saltmarsh plants that were once burnt to obtain soda ash, such as Soda inermis.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The alkali produced from the plant, an impure carbonate of soda, used for making soap, glass, etc., and for bleaching.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Impure soda obtained from the ashes of any seashore plant, or kelp.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
