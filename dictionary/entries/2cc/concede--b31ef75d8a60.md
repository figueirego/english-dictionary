### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To yield or suffer; to surrender; to grant

   **Example:** I have to concede the argument.

   **Example:** He conceded the race once it was clear he could not win.

   **Example:** Kendall conceded defeat once she realized she could not win in a battle of wits.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To grant, as a right or privilege; to make concession of.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To admit or agree to be true; to acknowledge

   **Source synonyms:** give; grant

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To yield or make concession.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To have a goal or point scored against

   **Example:** I don't know how they conceded that goal; their defense was so solid.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. (of a bowler) to have runs scored off of one's bowling.
