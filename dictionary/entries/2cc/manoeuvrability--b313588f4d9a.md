## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The quality of being manoeuvrable; ability to be manoeuvred.

   **Usage:** UK; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Articulated buses were invented to improve the manoeuvrability of long buses capable of carrying a large number of passengers.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Ability to manoeuvre; ability to carry out a manoeuvre or strategic plan.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** My manoeuvrability is limited. I don't have many options left.
