### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who admires another romantically but purposely remains anonymous to the extent that the subject of the admiration is unaware of the admirer's identity.

   **Example:** I received a Valentine's card from my secret admirer.
