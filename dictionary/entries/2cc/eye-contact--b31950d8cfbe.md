### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The condition or action of looking at another human or animal in the eye.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** to make eye contact with someone

   **Example:** If you make the animal angry, walk slowly backwards and avoid making eye contact.
