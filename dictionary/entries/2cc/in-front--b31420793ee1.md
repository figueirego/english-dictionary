## Prep\_phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In the position ahead. Often with of.

   **Example:** The leader, Jones, is well in front.

   **Example:** Hughes is in front of Smith, but behind Jackson.

   **Example:** I wasn't concentrating, and I crashed into the car in front.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In the position where it can be seen.

   **Example:** Put your hands in front of you, on the desk.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. In the position opposite.

   **Example:** Sit in front of the computer.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. In the position nearest.

   **Example:** Put your book in front where it will be easier to reach.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. In the most forward position.

   **Example:** This car will seat two people in front and three in the back.
