## Noun · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A sudden and complete withdrawal from a dependent substance, especially a drug.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; informal; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The physiological effects of such a withdrawal.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

   **Usage:** US; slang; uncountable

## Adverb · Record 2

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

   **Usage:** idiomatic; not-comparable

   **Example:** It is difficult, but possible to quit smoking cold turkey.
