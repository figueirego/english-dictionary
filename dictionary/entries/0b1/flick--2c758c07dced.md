### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A short, quick movement, especially a brush, sweep, or flip.

   **Example:** He removed the speck of dust with a flick of his finger.

   **Example:** She gave a disdainful flick of her hair and marched out of the room.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A motion picture, movie, film; (in plural, usually preceded by "the") movie theater, cinema.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** My all-time favorite flick is "Gone with the Wind."

   **Example:** Want to go to the flicks tonight?

**Source sense R1.S3**
