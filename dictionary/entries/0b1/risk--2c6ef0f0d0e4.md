### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The probability of a negative outcome to a decision or event.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** There is risk of being brutalized, arrested, imprisoned and tortured, all because I want you to know the truth about this matter.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The magnitude of possible loss consequent to a decision or event.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The potential negative effect of an event, determined by multiplying the likelihood of the event occurring with its magnitude should it occur.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; economics; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A possible adverse event or outcome.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source synonyms:** danger; peril; hazard

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A possible adverse event or outcome.
   1. A type of adverse event covered under an insurance policy.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; insurance

      **Source synonyms:** danger; peril; hazard

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A thing (from the perspective of how likely or unlikely it is to cause an adverse effect).

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** That man is going to be a big risk once he's out of prison.

   **Example:** Those stairs are a major risk.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

   1. A borrower (such as a mortgage-holder or person with a credit card).
