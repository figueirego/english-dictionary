### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To rotate so as not to face someone or something.

   **Usage:** literally; transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To rotate oneself so as not to face someone or something.

   **Usage:** intransitive; literally

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To bend or turn from a fixed course.

   **Usage:** literally; transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To bend or turn from a fixed course.

   **Usage:** intransitive; literally

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To refuse to admit someone or accept something.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** He was turned away at the border because he didn't have a valid visa.

   **Example:** The referee turned away the players' protests.

   **Source synonyms:** turn back

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To avert or ward off the occurrence or effects of.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** to turn away disaster

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To dismiss from service.

   **Usage:** dated; transitive

   **Example:** to turn away a servant

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To forsake or refuse an association or commitment.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

   **Example:** He turned away from his old comrades.

   **Example:** She increasingly turned away from politics.

   **Example:** Some people donate what they can, while others simply turn away.

   **Example:** Turn away from a life of sin.

   **Source synonyms:** turn one's back
