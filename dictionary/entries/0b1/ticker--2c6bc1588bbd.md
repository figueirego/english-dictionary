## Noun

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who makes a tick mark.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A measuring or reporting device, particularly one which makes a ticking sound as the measured events occur.

   **Example:** The ticker was showing an increased rate of flow.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A ticker tape, either the traditional paper kind or a scrolling message on a screen.

   **Example:** I checked the prices on the ticker one last time before placing the trade.

   **Example:** To my surprise, the ticker showed that the deal had already gone through.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A heart, especially a human one.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Example:** My ticker gave out and I had to go to the hospital for surgery.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A watch (timepiece).

   **Usage:** slang

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A birdwatcher who aims to see (and tick off on a list) as many bird species as possible.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; birdwatching; natural-sciences; ornithology

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A beer drinker who aims to try as many different beers as possible and keeps a record of all the beers they have drunk.
