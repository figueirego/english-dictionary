**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The width, usually measured in hertz, of a frequency band.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The width of the smallest frequency band within which the signal can fit.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a signal

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

   **Usage:** countable; informal; uncountable
