### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Having a single plane of symmetry; e.g., a monoaxial ellipsoid is an ellipsoid produced by rotating an ellipse about one of its axes

   **Usage:** not-comparable
