## Noun

### Forms

- concentration camps — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A camp where troops are assembled, prior to combat or transport.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A camp where large numbers of people, especially political prisoners, prisoners of war, refugees etc., are detained for the purpose of confining them in one place, typically with inadequate or inhumane facilities.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A situation of overcrowding and extremely harsh conditions.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** The UN inspector stated that the Australian government's migrant detention facilities were in effect concentration camps.
