### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Those countries not aligned with the west or the east during the Cold War.

   **Usage:** historical

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; politics

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Developing countries, especially those of Africa, Asia and Latin America.

   **Usage:** derogatory; sometimes

   **Example:** Our district has many immigrants from Third World countries

   **Source synonyms:** Global South

   **Source antonyms:** First World

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any developing country, or a country lacking the perceived internationally respected ideal standards of a society (e.g. sufficient rule of law and internationally respected level of prosperity).

   **Usage:** attributive; derogatory; informal

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. People of marginalized or exploited races, collectively.
