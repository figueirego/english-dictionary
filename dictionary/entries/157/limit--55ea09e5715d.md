### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A restriction; a bound beyond which one may not go or proceed.

   **Example:** There are several existing limits to executive power.

   **Example:** Two drinks is my limit tonight.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A value to which a sequence converges. Equivalently, the common value of the upper limit and the lower limit of a sequence&#58; if the upper and lower limits are different, then the sequence has no limit (i.e., does not converge).

   **Example:** The sequence of reciprocals has zero as its limit.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of several abstractions of this concept of limit.

   **Example:** Category theory defines a very general concept of limit.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The cone of a diagram through which any other cone of that same diagram can factor uniquely.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** category-theory; computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** inverse limit; projective limit

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Fixed limit.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** card-games; poker

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The final, utmost, or furthest point; the border or edge.

   **Example:** the limit of a walk, of a town, or of a country
