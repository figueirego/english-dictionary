## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having recently left one stage of life to begin another.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** You can hardly expect someone fresh out of basic training to be a superhero who can see around every corner.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Having completely exhausted one's supply of (a commodity); especially, having just run out recently.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** Customer&#58; I'd like a sesame bagel, please. Waiter&#58; Sorry, hon, we're fresh out of sesame. How about sourdough?

   **Example:** They've tried nothing and they're fresh out of ideas. \[sarcastic comment about someone's lack of problem-solving skills\]
