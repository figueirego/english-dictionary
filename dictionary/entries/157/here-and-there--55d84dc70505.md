## Adverb

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In, at or to various places; in one place and another.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. From time to time; intermittently, occasionally.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; uncommon

   **Example:** I get the odd day off here and there.
