### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Cold, chilly; icy.

   **Example:** The air was frosty; I could see my breath and walked quickly with my hands in my pockets.

   **Example:** I'd like a frosty milkshake.

   **Source synonyms:** frigid; wintry; algid; Arctic; Baltic; boreal; brass monkeys; brumal; chill; chilled; chilly; clay-cold; cold; cold as a mackerel; cold as charity; freezing; frosty; frore; frozen; gelid; glacial; hiemal; icy; inclement; nesh; nippy; parky (UK); polar; sleety; snowy; taters; wintry

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Having frost on it or in it.

   **Example:** The frosty pumpkin is the sign of the end of the growing season, soon the greenery will wither and harvest end for the year.

   **Example:** The frosty beverage gave him a brain freeze.

   **Source synonyms:** frostbound; frosted; rimed

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Having an aloof or inhospitable manner.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** After the divorce, she was civil but frosty to her ex.

   **Source synonyms:** cool; harsh; severe; aloof; cool; distant; frosty; hankty; hard-to-get (dated); offish; quiet; remote; standoffish; strange (obsolete); thorny; touch-me-not-ish; unapproachable; unbending; undemonstrative; unimpressionable; uninvolved; brutal; callous; cold; insensate (formal); hardhanded; cold-eyed; cruel; dour; thick-skinned; flint-hearted; forbidding; frosty; grim; hard; hard-boiled; hardened; hardhearted; heartless; harsh; humorless; icy; iron-hearted; insensitive; marble-hearted; rigorous; rockhearted; severe; stern; stonehearted; stony; stonyhearted; strict; tough; unsympathetic; unmoved
