## Noun

### Forms

- cover charges — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An amount of money to be paid for entering a bar or restaurant where entertainment is provided.

   **Example:** The cover charge was pretty high, but the band was really good.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An amount of money added to a restaurant bill in lieu of tips, and to pay for uncharged items such as bread and water.
