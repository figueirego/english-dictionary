**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A characteristic, possession, associate, or previous deed that hinders one's ability to succeed.

   **Usage:** figuratively; idiomatic

   **Example:** He bought the properties last year hoping to make a profit by reselling them; however, with the economic downturn, they have become an albatross around his neck.
