### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person from Ethiopia or of Ethiopian descent.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A black-skinned person, especially one from Africa.

   **Usage:** dated

   **Example:** The Ethiopian eunuch.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A very skinny person, referencing the historic malnutrition and poverty in Ethiopia.

   **Usage:** derogatory; slang
