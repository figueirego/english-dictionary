### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Quality, state or condition of being tender.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** He picked her up in his arms with great tenderness.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A tendency to express warm, compassionate feelings.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** When the lovers were together, their cold indifference gave way to love and tenderness.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A concern for the feelings or welfare of others.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** When they saw the poor orphans, they were overwhelmed with tenderness for them.

   **Example:** Everybody needs a little tenderness sometimes.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A pain or discomfort when an affected area is touched.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** He noted her extreme tenderness when he touched the bruise on her thigh.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; pathology; sciences
