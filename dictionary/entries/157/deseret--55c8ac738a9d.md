### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A state, proposed in 1849 and never recognized, which would have included most of Utah and Nevada and parts of other states.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The state of Utah itself, especially the Salt Lake Valley and environs.

   **Usage:** poetic

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A script, invented in Utah, conceived of as a radical reform and replacement of the Latin script, intended for writing English phonetically. Example&#58; the word seven in Deseret is 𐑅𐐯𐑂𐐮𐑌.
