### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The required manual dexterity used to perform magic tricks and illusions.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A performance of such a skill.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** legerdemain; prestidigitation; conjuration; conjuring; dweomercraft (rare); hocus-pocus (US); illusionism; jugglery (archaic); legerdemain; magic; palmistry; prestidigitation; prestigiation (obsolete); sleight of hand
