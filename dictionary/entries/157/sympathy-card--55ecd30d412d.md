### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A greeting card sent to family members of one who has died to express regret.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An attempt to gain sympathy or an advantage in a discussion by portraying oneself as a victim in need of sympathy (used with the verb "play").
