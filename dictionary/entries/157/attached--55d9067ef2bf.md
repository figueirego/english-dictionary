## Adjective · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /əˈtæt͡ʃt/ — source labels: General-American; Received-Pronunciation

### Forms

- more attached — comparative
- most attached — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Connected; joined.

   **Example:** The car has something of a history attached to it.

   **Source synonyms:** appendant (formal)

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Fond of (used with to).

   **Example:** I'm very attached to my pets.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. Included as an attachment with a communication (especially an email or other electronic communication).

   **Example:** Please see the attached file in my previous email.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. In a romantic or sexual relationship.

   **Example:** As far as I know, he isn't attached, so I'm going to invite him out on a date.

   **Example:** I'm not ready to get attached, as I want to continue sleeping around.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. Broadly joined to a stem or stipe, but not decurrent.

   **Example:** In this group of mushrooms, the attachment of the gills to the stipe ranges from attached to almost decurrent.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; mycology; natural-sciences

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. Of a residential building, sharing walls with similar buildings on two, usually opposite, sides.
