## Determiner · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /huːz/ — source labels: UK; US

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of whom, belonging to whom; which person's or people's.

   **Usage:** interrogative

   **Example:** Whose (wallet) is this?

   **Example:** We should buy a house. ~ With whose money?

   **Example:** For whose benefit are you acting.?

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of whom, belonging to whom.

   **Usage:** relative

   **Example:** This is the man whose dog caused the accident.

   **Example:** Venus, whose sister is Serena, won the latest championship.

   **Example:** I dedicate this award to my parents, without whose help I wouldn't have made it this far.

   **Example:** The victim was a youngster, both of whose eyes were missing.

   **Example:** It is Marconi whose name is associated with the development of radio.

   **Example:** Ammon is an Egyptian god near whose temple ammonia and ammoniac were said to come.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of which, belonging to which.

   **Usage:** relative

   **Example:** We saw several houses whose roofs were falling off.

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. That or those of whom or belonging to whom.

   **Usage:** interrogative

   **Example:** Several people have lost their suitcases. Whose have you found?

   **Example:** He asked whose the umbrella was.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. That or those of whom or belonging to whom.

   **Usage:** relative

   **Example:** This car is blocking the way, but Mr Smith, whose it is, will be here shortly.
