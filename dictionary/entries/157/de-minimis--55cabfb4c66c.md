## Adjective · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /də ˈmɪnəmɪs/ — source labels: General-American

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Concerning things that are so minor as to be negligible, trivial, or trifling. Often used to describe exemptions in government rules and regulations.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The tax is not payable on amounts below the de minimis limit of £100 per year.

   **Example:** The de minimis rules on import duty mean that alcohol for personal consumption is exempt from any charge.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Beneath the notice of the law; so minor as not to warrant a penalty or remedy.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The behaviour of the accused was technically assault, but the judge found it de minimis and entered an acquittal.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law
