## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An alternative universe within a fictional work, separate from the universe normally depicted, where familiar characters and settings are present but in a systematically "reversed" manner, often by being evil or otherwise hostile.

   **Example:** Star Trek&#58; The Original Series introduced a mirror universe in which humans had aggressively conquered parts of the galaxy rather than forming the peaceful Federation, and the setting was revisited in several of the franchise's spinoff shows.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** literature; media; publishing; science-fiction

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A hypothetical universe, a counterpart to this universe but with certain elementary particles reversed in a specific way.

   **Usage:** rare
