## Adverb

### Forms

- everytime — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. At each occasion that.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Every time I see you, it makes me happy.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used to express a strong preference for one thing over another.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; informal; not-comparable

   **Example:** You can keep your wine — give me beer every time!

   **Example:** If I had the choice between going to work and staying at home, I'd pick staying at home every time.
