### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To move in circles or through a circuit.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To cause (a person or thing) to move in circles or through a circuit.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To move from person to person, as at a party.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To spread or disseminate.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to circulate money or gossip

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To become widely known.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Of decimals&#58; to repeat.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** a circulating decimal

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences
