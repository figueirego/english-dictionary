### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To cry out or shout in contempt.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To make a hoo, the cry of an owl.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

   **Example:** Mary felt extremely offended when the workers hooted at her.

4. To sound the horn of a vehicle.

   **Example:** When you arrive to pick me up, hoot, and I'll come outside.
