### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An action or statement in response to a stimulus or other event.

   **Example:** The announcement of the verdict brought a violent reaction.

   **Example:** You were in the courtroom. What is your reaction?

   **Example:** When I last tried to eat strawberries I had a terrible allergic reaction.

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A transformation in which one or more substances is converted into another by combination or decomposition.

   **Example:** In this reaction, the acid and base will neutralize each other, producing a salt.
