## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The language of one's ancestors, especially a language one would like to learn.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A minority language that one acquires as a first language but uses and/or is exposed to less later in life in favor of a more dominant majority language.

   **Example:** Spanish is considered a heritage language in the United States.
