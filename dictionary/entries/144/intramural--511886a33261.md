## Adjective · Record 1

### Forms

- more intramural — comparative
- most intramural — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Within the walls; within one institution, particularly a school.

   **Example:** Intramural sports involve teams that are all within the same school.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Within the substance of the walls of an organ.

   **Example:** intramural pregnancy

### Related words

**Source antonyms:** extramural — antonym(s) of “inside the walls”
