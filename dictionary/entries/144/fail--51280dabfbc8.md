### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To be unsuccessful.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Throughout my life, I have always failed.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Not to achieve a particular stated goal. (Usage note&#58; The direct object of this word is usually an infinitive.)

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The truck failed to start.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To neglect.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The report fails to take into account all the mitigating factors.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Of a machine, etc.&#58; to cease to operate correctly.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** After running five minutes, the engine failed.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To be wanting to, to be insufficient for, to disappoint, to desert; to disappoint one's expectations.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I've failed my parents many times growing up.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To receive one or more non-passing grades in academic pursuits.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** I failed English last year.

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To give a student a non-passing grade in an academic endeavour.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The professor failed me because I did not complete any of the course assignments.

**Source sense R1.S8**

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

   **Example:** The crops failed last year.

   **Usage:** archaic

<a id="r1-s12"></a>
