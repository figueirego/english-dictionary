### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any soup made with either pieces of chicken or chicken broth as its main ingredient.

   **Usage:** countable; literally; uncountable

   **Example:** He should have guessed that her great-great-grandmother's secret chicken soup recipe was a can from the store.

   **Example:** Chicken soup is proverbial as a wholesome food whose nutrients will help your body recover from illness.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A cure for a wide variety of illnesses or other problems.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

   **Example:** Duct tape is the chicken soup of plumbing.

   **Source synonyms:** chicken noodle soup; panacea

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A mixed-up mush.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

   **Example:** His hand got caught in a meat grinder and turned into chicken soup.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The aqueous solution of nutrients used in the Viking lander biological experiments to detect microbial Martian life.

   **Usage:** countable; historical; slang; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biochemistry; biology; chemistry; microbiology; natural-sciences; physical-sciences
