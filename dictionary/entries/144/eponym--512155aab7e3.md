### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who gave or supposedly gave their name to a people, place, institution, etc.

   **Example:** The ancient Greeks and Romans tended to credit nearly every location and ethnicity to a legendary eponym&#58; Hellas to Hellen, Rome to Romulus, Egypt to Aegyptus, etc.

   **Example:** In ancient Greece, the eponymous archon gave his name to the year in which he held office; he was accordingly known as an eponym.

   **Example:** John Montagu, 4th Earl of Sandwich, is the eponym of the sandwich.

   **Source synonyms:** eponymist; namesake

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Something that is named after a person.

   **Source synonyms:** namesake

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A name or term taken or derived from the name of a person.

   **Usage:** proscribed; sometimes

   **Example:** Alexandria (the name of a city in Egypt) is an eponym derived from the name of Alexander the Great.

   **Example:** To galvanize and galvanization are eponyms derived from the name of Luigi Galvani.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A name or term taken or derived from any proper noun, inclusive of places, brands, etc.

   **Usage:** broadly; proscribed; sometimes

   **Example:** The term tangerine is an eponym in reference to Tangier.

   **Example:** The unflattering eponym shanghai derived from the behavior of American shippers, not the Chinese themselves.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** deonym

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Synonym of epitome, a person taken as a symbol or quintessential representative of some trait, school, etc.

   **Usage:** archaic

   **Example:** Rockefeller became the very eponym of wealth.

   **Source synonyms:** epitome (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Synonym of epithet, a distinguishing title.

   **Usage:** archaic

   **Example:** It was only posthumously that Julian was distinguished with the eponym "Apostate".

   **Source synonyms:** epithet (synonym; synonym-of)
