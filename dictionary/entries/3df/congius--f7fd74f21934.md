### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A Roman unit of liquid measure reckoned as the volume of 10 Roman pounds of wine and equivalent to about 3.3 L although differing slightly over time.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A Roman unit of mass instituted by Vespasian equivalent to about 3.3 kg, the weight of a congius of water.

   **Usage:** historical
