### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Half of the way between two points.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** I was already halfway home when I missed my keyring.

   **Example:** One day has gone by and she's already halfway through the book.

   **Example:** It's July 1st, so this year is halfway over.
