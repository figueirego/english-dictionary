### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Full-scale, as a model that is the same size as what it is a model of.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The life-sized statue was the same size as the subject, and since he had never fit in that chair, neither did the statue.
