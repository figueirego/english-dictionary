**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To destroy (buildings, etc.), especially in a planned or intentional fashion.

   **Usage:** literally; transitive

   **Example:** They demolished the old mill and put up four townhouses.

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive
