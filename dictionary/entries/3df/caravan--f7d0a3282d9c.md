### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A convoy or procession of travellers, their cargo and vehicles, and any pack animals, especially camels crossing a desert.

   **Source synonyms:** camel train; caravanserai; convoy; pack train; wagon train

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A furnished vehicle towed behind a car, etc., and used as a dwelling when stationary.

   **Usage:** Australia; New-Zealand; South-Africa; UK

   **Source synonyms:** camper; recreational vehicle; RV; trailer; travel trailer

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
