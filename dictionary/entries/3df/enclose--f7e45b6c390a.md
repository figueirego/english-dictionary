### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To surround with a wall, fence, etc.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to enclose lands

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To insert into a container, usually an envelope or package.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Please enclose a stamped self-addressed envelope if you require a reply.
