### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To press down.

   **Example:** Depress the upper lever to start the machine.

   **Source synonyms:** downbear; squash

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To make depressed, sad or bored.

   **Example:** Winter depresses me.

   **Source synonyms:** deject; get down; aggrieve; attrist (archaic); begloom (archaic); begrieve (archaic); besorrow; grieve; pain; bring down; come down on; contristate (obsolete); darken; dash; deject; depress; desolate; dispirit; engrieve (archaic); forset (UK; dialectal; figuratively); get down; grieven; moan (obsolete); oppress; repent (archaic); rue (obsolete); sadden; sadden up; tristitiate (obsolete); ungladden; unhappy; upset

   **Source antonyms:** cheer up

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To cause a depression or a decrease in parts of the economy.

   **Example:** Lower productivity will eventually depress wages.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** economics; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To bring down or humble; to abase (pride, etc.).
