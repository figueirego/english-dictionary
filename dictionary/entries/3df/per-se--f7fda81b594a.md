## Adverb · Record 1

### Pronunciation

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Without determination by or involvement of extraneous factors; by its very nature.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Some people say that a hangover is caused by impurities in the drink, not by the alcohol per se.

   **Source synonyms:** by itself; in itself; in and of itself; as such; sui generis; by definition; by nature; essentially; in essence; ipso facto; intrinsically; essentially; in and of itself; inherently; innately; intrinsically; naturally; per se

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In a true or literal sense; as one would expect from the name or description.

   **Usage:** nonstandard; not-comparable; more loosely

   **Example:** It's not a museum per se, but they do have some interesting artefacts.

   **Source context (verbatim):** in the negative

   **Source synonyms:** exactly; strictly speaking; sensu stricto; stricto sensu; truly

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. As the principle of its own determination and positing itself.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; philosophy; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Not leaving discretion to the judge to take into account additional factors that could rebut the judgment, deriving the qualification from the statute.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The law makes drunk driving illegal per se.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law
