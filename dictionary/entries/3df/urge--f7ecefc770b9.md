### Senses

**Source sense R2.S1**

   **Source synonyms:** animate; encourage; impel; stimulate

   **Source synonyms:** instigate; abet (obsolete); actuate; goose; fuel; light a fire under; send; sting; bring about; cheer (obsolete); [draw](../143/draw--50f7becde477.md); drive; encourage; egg on (idiomatic); engender; evoke; excite; foment; goad; grill (Scotland; US; obsolete); ignite (figuratively); impel; incite; induce; inflame (figuratively); instigate; invoke; lash; motive; motivate; move (obsolete); obligate; oblige; push; promote; prompt; provoke; rouse; set off; spark off; spur; spur on; stimulate; stir; stir up; stoke (figuratively); tempt; urge; whet (obsolete); whip up

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** agitate; strain; test; vex
