### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To mix two things irregularly, placing things of one kind among things of other.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To scatter or insert something into or among other things.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** When writing, I intersperse details.

   **Source synonyms:** thread

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To diversify by placing or inserting other things among something.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Mother Nature interspersed the petunias with a few dandelions, but it was a pretty garden, anyway.
