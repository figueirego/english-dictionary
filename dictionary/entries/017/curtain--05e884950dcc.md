### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A piece of cloth covering a window, bed, etc. to offer privacy and keep out light.

   **Usage:** household

   **Example:** He drew the curtains at 11&#58;00pm before falling asleep.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; fabrics; furniture; lifestyle; manufacturing; textiles

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A similar piece of cloth that separates the audience and the stage in a theater.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; entertainment; fabrics; furniture; lifestyle; manufacturing; textiles; theater

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The beginning of a show; the moment the curtain rises.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** He took so long to shave his head that we arrived 45 minutes after curtain and were denied late entry.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; theater

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The flat area of wall which connects two bastions or towers; the main area of a fortified wall.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** fortifications; government; military; politics; war

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Death, final curtain.

   **Usage:** euphemistic; in-plural; sometimes

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. That part of a wall of a building which is between two pavilions, towers, etc.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** architecture

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A flag; an ensign.

   **Usage:** derogatory; obsolete

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. The uninterrupted stream of fluid that falls onto a moving substrate in the process of curtain coating.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>
