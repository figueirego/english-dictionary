### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Similar to sauce; having the consistency or texture of sauce.

   **Example:** Bring the tomatoes to a boil and then simmer until they reach a saucy consistency.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Impertinent or disrespectful, often in a manner that is regarded as entertaining or amusing; smart.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** She is a loud, saucy child who doesn't show a lot of respect to her elders.

   **Source synonyms:** audacious; bold; get wise; bold-faced; bold as brass; brassy; malapert (literary); brazen; brazen-faced; cheeky; chutzpadik; cocket (obsolete); cute; dicacious (rare); facey; familiar; flippant; flip; forward; fresh; impious; impudent; impertinent; insolent; irreverent; pert (rare); petulant; sassy (US); saucy

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Impudently bold; pert.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Sharp; pungent; piquant.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Mildly erotic.

   **Example:** I enjoyed the dancing, but my husband found it a little too saucy.
