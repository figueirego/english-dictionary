### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To pull something out with a sudden jerk.

   **Usage:** informal; transitive

   **Example:** He suddenly whipped out his gun and pointed it at his doctor.

   **Example:** I whipped out my wallet and laid a five-pound note on the bar.

   **Source synonyms:** pull out

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To complete or produce rapidly.

   **Usage:** informal; transitive

   **Example:** You need the report by this afternoon? No problem! I will whip one out for you in 10 minutes.
