### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A card displayed by the referee when a player is sent off for a serious infringement of the rules.

   **Example:** In the 1994 FIFA World Cup, Argentine player Diego Maradona was given a red card for striking US player Tab Ramos.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any official instructions to vacate an area.

   **Usage:** colloquial

   **Example:** I hoped to park in the members' area but the attendant showed me the red card.
