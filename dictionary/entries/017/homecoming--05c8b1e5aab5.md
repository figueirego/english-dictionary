### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act or event of returning home.

   **Example:** His homecoming was saddened by the news of his mother's death the day before.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In high schools and colleges, a tradition centered around a football game, a parade and the coronation of a Homecoming Queen.

   **Usage:** Canada; US; also; attributive

   **Example:** Many alumni come back for homecoming, and many freshmen are advised to flee.

   **Example:** homecoming ball

   **Example:** homecoming game

   **Example:** homecoming party

   **Example:** homecoming rave

   **Source synonyms:** hoco

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. In high schools and colleges, a tradition centered around a football game, a parade and the coronation of a Homecoming Queen.
   1. Ellipsis of harvest homecoming, a traditional (street) party and/or dance near the start of the school year, at around fall mid-terms. It traditionally marks the start of harvest break, where students go home in autumn to harvest the crops on the family farms.

      **Usage:** Canada; US; abbreviation; also; alt-of; attributive; ellipsis

      **Source synonyms:** hoco

      **Alternative form of:** [harvest homecoming](../237/harvest-homecoming--8dec05088985.md)
