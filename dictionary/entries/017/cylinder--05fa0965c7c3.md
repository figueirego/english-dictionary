## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A surface created by projecting a closed two-dimensional curve along an axis intersecting the plane of the curve.

   **Example:** When the two-dimensional curve is a circle, the cylinder is called a circular cylinder. When the axis is perpendicular to the plane of the curve, the cylinder is called a right cylinder. In non-mathematical usage, both right and circular are usually implied.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A solid figure bounded by a cylinder and two parallel planes intersecting the cylinder.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any object in the form of a circular cylinder.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A cylindrical cavity or chamber in a mechanism, such as the counterpart to a piston found in a piston-driven engine.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The space in which a piston travels inside a reciprocating engine or pump.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** automotive; transport; vehicles

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A container in the form of a cylinder with rounded ends for storing pressurized gas; a gas cylinder.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. An early form of phonograph recording, made on a wax cylinder.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. The part of a revolver that contains chambers for the cartridges.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. The corresponding tracks on a vertical arrangement of disks in a disk drive considered as a unit of data capacity.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences
