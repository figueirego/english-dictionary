### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make tighter.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Please tighten that screw a quarter-turn.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To become tighter.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** That joint is tightening as the wood dries.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To make money harder to borrow or obtain.

   **Example:** If the government doesn't tighten the money supply, inflation is certain to be harsh.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** economics; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To raise short-term interest rates.

   **Example:** The Fed is expected to tighten by a quarter-point.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** economics; sciences
