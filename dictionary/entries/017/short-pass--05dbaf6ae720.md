### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A pass of the ball between teammates too close together to allow an opponent to get between them. There is no particular distance the ball must travel to qualify as a short pass. For instance, if the passing player drops the ball and retreats before her teammate takes possession, it is not a short pass. A short pass is against the rules and results in a free pass to the opposition.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; games; hobbies; lifestyle; netball; sports

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
