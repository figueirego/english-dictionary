### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The ability to speak; the faculty of uttering words or articulate sounds and vocalizations to communicate.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** He had a bad speech impediment.

   **Example:** After the accident she lost her speech.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The act of speaking, a certain style of it.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** It was hard to hear his speech over the noise.

   **Example:** Her speech was soft and lilting.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A formal session of speaking, especially a long oral message given publicly by one person.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** The candidate made some ambitious promises in his campaign speech.

   **Source synonyms:** address; allocution; monologue; oration; soliloquy

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A dialect, vernacular, or (dated) a language.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source synonyms:** language; leid (Scottish; poetic); lingo (colloquial); speech; tongue (metonymically)

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Language used orally, rather than in writing.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** This word is mostly used in speech.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. An utterance that is quoted; see direct speech, reported speech

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** grammar; human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Public talk, news, gossip, rumour.

   **Usage:** uncountable
