### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The shortest and thickest digit of the hand that for humans has the most mobility and can be made to oppose (moved to touch) all of the other fingers.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The part of a slider that may be moved linearly along the slider.

   **Example:** a scroll-bar thumb

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; graphical-user-interface; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A thumbnail picture.

   **Usage:** Internet; colloquial

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A top hatch plate for covering an excavator bucket, used to keep material in the digger bucket after scooping it up, and not letting it spill out.

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To touch or cover with the thumb.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to thumb the touch-hole of a cannon

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To turn the pages of (a book) in order to read it cursorily.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** thumb through

   **Example:** I thumbed through the book and decided not to bother reading it all.

   **Source context (verbatim):** with through

   **Source synonyms:** peruse; flip through

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To gesture with the thumb, for example when flagging a ride.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To hitchhike.

   **Example:** So I started thumbin' back east, toward my hometown.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** lifestyle; tourism; transport; travel

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To soil or wear with the thumb or the fingers; to soil, or wear out, by frequent handling.

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To manipulate (an object) with the thumb; especially, to pull back the hammer or open the cylinder of a revolver.

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

**Source sense R2.S7**

7. To fire (a single action revolver) quickly by pulling the hammer while keeping the trigger depressed.
