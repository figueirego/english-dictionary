### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of the day; prepared for the day in question

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The soup du jour is French onion.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Currently stylish; en vogue; trendy; favoured now but likely temporary; latest.

   **Usage:** broadly; not-comparable

   **Example:** Beth came to the party with her boyfriend du jour.

   **Example:** The high unemployment numbers are the government's excuse du jour for not doing anything about the environment.
