### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Auspicious.

   **Example:** It is a fortunate sign if the sun shines on a newly wedded couple.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Happening by good luck or favorable chance.

   **Example:** Patrick was the unlikely match-winner as Berkeley earned a fortunate victory over Chisolm.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Favored by fortune.

   **Example:** We were fortunate not to be fined for speeding.

   **Example:** This is a time when we think of those less fortunate than ourselves.
