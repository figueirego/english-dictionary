## Noun · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- enPR: skī — source labels: General-American; Received-Pronunciation
- IPA: /skaɪ/ — source labels: General-American; Received-Pronunciation

### Forms

- skies — plural
- skie — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The atmosphere above a given point, especially as visible from the surface of the Earth as the place where the sun, moon, stars, and clouds are seen.

   **Example:** That year, a meteor fell from the sky.

   **Source synonyms:** blue; firmament; heaven; lift (Scotland); welkin (archaic; literary; poetic)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. With a descriptive word&#58; the part of the sky which can be seen from a specific place or at a specific time; its climate, condition, etc.

   **Example:** I lay back under a warm Texas sky.

   **Example:** We’re not sure how long the cloudy skies will last.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Usually preceded by the&#58; the abode of God or the gods, angels, the souls of deceased people, etc.; heaven; also, powers emanating from heaven.

   **Usage:** archaic; literary; poetic

   **Example:** This mortal has incurred the wrath of the skies.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Ellipsis of sky blue.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; ellipsis

   **Alternative form of:** [sky blue](../320/sky-blue--c801969a1a2f.md)

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The set of all lightlike lines (or directions) passing through a given point in space-time.

   **Usage:** theoretical physics

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** celestial sphere

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. In an art gallery&#58; the upper rows of pictures that cannot easily be seen; also, the place where such pictures are hung.

   **Usage:** informal; obsolete; rare

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A cloud.

   **Usage:** obsolete
