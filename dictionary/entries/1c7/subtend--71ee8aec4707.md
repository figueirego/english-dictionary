### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To use an angle to delimit (mark off, enclose) part of a straight or curved line, for example an arc or the opposite side of a triangle.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** A 43° angle subtends an arc of about 0.75 meters on a circle with a radius of 1 meter.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To extend or stretch opposite something; to be part of a straight or curved line that is opposite to and delimits an angle.

   **Usage:** also; transitive

   **Example:** A hypotenuse subtends the right angle of a right triangle.

   **Example:** An arc measuring about 0.75 meters on a circle with a radius of 1 meter subtends an angle of about 43°.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To form the central angle of a circle underneath an arc.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The points A and B on the circumference form the arc AB, which subtends the central angle θ.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To stand beneath or close to, as a bract at the base of a flower.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences
