### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To empty, hollow or remove substance from (something) using a chisel.

   **Usage:** literally; transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To make (something) into an exception; to place (a topic) out of consideration.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** We have to chisel out school funding from the debate.
