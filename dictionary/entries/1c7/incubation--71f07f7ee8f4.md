### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Sitting on eggs for the purpose of hatching young; a brooding on, or keeping warm, to develop the life within, by any process.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The development of a disease from its causes, or the period of such development.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; pathology; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A period of little reaction which is followed by more rapid reaction.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. One of the four proposed stages of creativity (preparation, incubation, illumination, and verification)&#58; the unconscious recombination of thought elements that were stimulated through conscious work at one point in time, resulting in novel ideas at a later point.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; psychology; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Sleeping in a temple or other holy place in order to have oracular dreams or to receive healing.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
