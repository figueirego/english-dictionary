### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To become cooler; to be reduced in temperature.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** You can drink the coffee when it's cooled down.

   **Source synonyms:** cool off

   **Source antonyms:** warm up; hot up

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To become less agitated or excited.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** I'd love to go to Israel, but I'll have to wait until the violence cools down.

   **Source synonyms:** calm down

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To decrease in degree or intensity.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To cause (something) to become cooler; to reduce the temperature of.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** We had to cool down the equipment with water before using it.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To cause (someone) to become less agitated or excited.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Dave was kicking and screaming, so I had to cool him down with some mellow music.

   **Source synonyms:** calm down
