### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of several glandular villi of the uterus of viviparous sharks which supply nutrient to the embryos

   **Example:** The trophonema consists of 50–60 trophocytes that maintain contact with &lt;1% of the oocyte surface and forms a circular, bowel-shaped depression on the luminal surface of the gastrodermis as they sink into the mesoglea with the oocyte.
