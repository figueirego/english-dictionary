### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. On the topic that was just previously mentioned.

   **Example:** I heard there's a lemon shortage. On that note, do we have any lemonade stocked up?

   **Example:** You've all done a very good job. I'd like to end the meeting on that note. See you Monday.
