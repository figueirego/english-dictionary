**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To ridicule or mock someone; to make fun of someone.

   **Usage:** Commonwealth; Ireland; UK; idiomatic; intransitive; slang

   **Example:** Are you takin’ the mickey? You’ll get yer ’ead bashed in.
