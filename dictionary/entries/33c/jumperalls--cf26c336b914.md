## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A one-piece garment combining trousers with an upper-body covering, worn as workwear or children’s clothing.

   **Usage:** plural; plural-only

   **Extractor diagnostics (not usage labels):** no-gloss

**Supplemental definition (evidence reviewed; source gap preserved):** A 1928 manufacturer advertisement explicitly describes Jumperalls as a one-piece work suit; the 1980 manufacturer catalogue applies the term to children’s corduroy garments with shoulder straps, legs and a snap crotch. The broader definition accommodates both constructions without incorrectly requiring sleeves or a bib.
