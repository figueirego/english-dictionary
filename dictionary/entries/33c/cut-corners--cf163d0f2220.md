### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To bypass a prescribed route so as to gain competitive advantage or to circumvent traffic signals or other rules of the road.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To do a less-than-thorough or incomplete job; to do something poorly; to take inappropriate shortcuts.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** The guy who built the fence cut corners when sinking the posts, and the fence fell over in the last storm.

   **Example:** Do you know why Wendy's has square burgers? Because they don't cut corners.

   **Source synonyms:** skimp; take short cuts; follow the path of least resistance; take the easy way out; half-ass
