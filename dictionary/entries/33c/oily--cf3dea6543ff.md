### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Covered with or containing oil.

   **Example:** oily machinery; oily rags; oily fish; oily cooking

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Resembling oil.

   **Example:** oily swells; oily seas

   **Source synonyms:** oilish; oillike

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

   **Source synonyms:** slick; buttery; oleaginous; unctuous; smarmy
