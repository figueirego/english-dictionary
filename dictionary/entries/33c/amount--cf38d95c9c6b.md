### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The total, aggregate or sum of material (not applicable to discrete numbers or units or items in standard English).

   **Example:** The amount of atmospheric pollution threatens a health crisis.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

2. A quantity or volume.

   **Example:** Pour a small amount of water into the dish.

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To total or evaluate.

   **Usage:** intransitive; with-to

   **Example:** The money in my pocket amounts to three dollars and change.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To be the tantamount to; to reach up to the level of.

   **Usage:** intransitive; with-to

   **Example:** He was a pretty good student, but never amounted to much professionally.

   **Example:** His response amounted to gross insubordination.
