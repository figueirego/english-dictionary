### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To gain (success, reward, recognition) through applied effort or work.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** You can have the s'mores&#58; you earned them, clearing the walkway of snow so well.

   **Example:** With their hard work and dedication, they earned respect and a seat at the table.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To receive payment for work or for a role or position held (regardless of whether effort was applied or whether the remuneration is deserved or commensurate).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He earns seven million dollars a year as CEO. My bank account is only earning one percent interest.

   **Example:** She earns more than forty thousand dollars a year in passive income from her parents' investments — that's what she gets before she even gets out of bed or lifts a finger.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To receive payment for work.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Now that you are earning, you can start paying me rent.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To cause (someone) to receive payment or reward.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** My CD earns me six percent!

   **Example:** In that era, all their long, hard, dangerous labor in the mines barely earned them even enough to eat!

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To achieve by being worthy of.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to earn a spot in the top 20
