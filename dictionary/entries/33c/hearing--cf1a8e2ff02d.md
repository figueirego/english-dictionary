## Noun · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈhɪː.ɹɪŋ/ — source labels: Received-Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈhiː.ɹɪŋ/ — source labels: Scotland

### Forms

- hearings — plural

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. The sense used to perceive sound.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** My hearing isn't what it used to be, but I still heard that noise.

   **Source synonyms:** audition

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. The distance or physical region within which something may be heard; earshot.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** She moved out of hearing and I lost the end of the sentence.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. Something heard; a report or piece of news.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. The act by which something is heard; the act of perceiving by sound or the auditory sense.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. A proceeding at which discussions are heard.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** There will be a public hearing to discuss the new traffic light.

   **Example:** All I want is for my views to get a fair hearing.

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. A legal procedure done before a judge, without a jury, as with an evidentiary hearing.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

**Source sense R2.S7**

7. A scolding.

   **Usage:** countable; dated; informal; uncountable
