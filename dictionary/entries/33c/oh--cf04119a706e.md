## Interjection · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /əʊ/ — source labels: UK
- IPA: \[ö̞\] — source labels: Yorkshire
- IPA: /oʊ/ — source labels: US

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Expression of surprise.

   **Example:** Oh! I didn't see you there.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Expression of wonder, amazement, or awe.

   **Example:** Oh, wow! That's amazing.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Expression of understanding, affirmation, recognition, or realization.

   **Example:** Oh, so that's how it works.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A word to precede an annoyed remark.

   **Example:** Oh, leave me alone.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A word to precede an added or offhand comment or afterthought.

   **Example:** Oh, and don't forget your coat.

   **Example:** What if he says, "Oh, I need to see your ID"?

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. An invocation or address (similar to the vocative in languages with noun declension), often with a term of endearment.

   **Example:** Oh, gosh.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Exclamation for drama or emphasis.

   **Usage:** often; poetic

   **Example:** Oh, when will it end?

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Expression of pain. See ouch.

   **Example:** Oh! That hurt.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. Space filler or extra syllable, especially in (popular) music.

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. Expression of mild scepticism.

   **Usage:** interrogative

   **Example:** "You should watch where you're going!" "Oh?"
