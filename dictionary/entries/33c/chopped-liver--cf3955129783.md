### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A Jewish pâté-like food, usually spread on bread, made by mincing beef or chicken liver and onions which have been broiled or fried in schmaltz (“chicken fat”) together with hard-boiled eggs.

   **Usage:** uncountable
