### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who writes, or produces literary work; an author can refer to themselves as "the writer".

   **Example:** Has your girlfriend written you another letter already? She’s quite a writer!

   **Example:** I met some of my favourite authors at the writers' convention.

   **Example:** the present writer (referring to oneself as the writer)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Anything that writes or produces output.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A machine or tool used for writing, often by chording keys on a specialized keyboard (e.g. braillewriter, microwriter, stenograph).

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The seller of an option.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A clerk of a certain rank in the service of the East India Company, who, after serving a certain number of years, became a factor.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. An ordinary legal practitioner in Scottish country towns.

   **Usage:** Scotland

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A petty officer in the United States navy who keeps the watch-muster and other books of the ship.

   **Usage:** US

   **Source synonyms:** ship writer; ship's writer

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A graffiti artist.

   **Usage:** slang
