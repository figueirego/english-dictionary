### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A communication from a bank to a person holding an account in that bank, usually issued monthly, detailing the value of the holdings in that account and the effects of all transactions occurring with respect to that account.

   **Source synonyms:** account statement
