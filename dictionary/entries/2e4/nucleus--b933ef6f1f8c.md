### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The core, central part of something, around which other elements are assembled.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An initial part or version that will receive additions.

   **Example:** This collection will form the nucleus of a new library.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The massive, positively charged central part of an atom, made up of protons and neutrons.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A large membrane-enclosed organelle found in eukaryotic cells which contains genetic material.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; cytology; medicine; natural-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A ganglion, cluster of many neuronal bodies where synapsing occurs.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; medicine; neuroanatomy; neurology; neuroscience; sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The central part of a syllable, most commonly a vowel.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; phonetics; phonology; sciences

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A small bee-hive used to create a colony from a larger existing one.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** agriculture; beekeeping; business; lifestyle
