## Noun

### Forms

- free riders — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who gets a free ride on a ship, or similar; one who rides on a means of transportation gratis.

   **Source synonyms:** stowaway

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Someone who obtains goods or services legally without paying.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** The store failed because all of the manager's friends were free riders who drove paying customers away.

   **Source synonyms:** freeloader; bloodsucker; bludger (Australia; New-Zealand; slang); bummer (US; dated; slang); freeloader; free rider; gadfly (slang); leech; mooch; moocher; parasite; scrounge; scrounger; scunge (slang); smell-feast (archaic); sponge; sponger; sucker; trencher mate (archaic)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. One who obtains benefit from a public good without paying for it directly.

   **Example:** A lighthouse is the classic example of a public good because it is difficult to prevent a ship (a free rider) from using it.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** economics; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A worker at an open shop who is able to enjoy the benefits negotiated by a union without being a member of the union.

   **Usage:** industrial relations
