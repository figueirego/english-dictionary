### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To move upwards.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source synonyms:** ascend; rise; descend; go down

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To be built or erected.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** There are new offices going up in town.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To rise or increase in price, cost, or value.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Bananas have gone up because of a shortage.

   **Source synonyms:** rise; increase

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To be consumed by fire.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The building went up in smoke. Once the fire got out of the basement, the building went up in minutes.

   **Source synonyms:** burn up; burn; combust (intransitive); forburn (obsolete); go up; outburn; torch

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To forget lines or blocks during public performance.

   **Usage:** intransitive; performing arts
