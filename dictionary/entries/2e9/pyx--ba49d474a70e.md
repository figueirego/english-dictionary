## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A small, usually round container used to hold the host (“consecrated bread or wafer of the Eucharist”), especially when bringing communion to the sick or others unable to attend Mass.

   **Usage:** also; figuratively

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Catholicism; Christianity; Roman-Catholicism

   **Source synonyms:** pyxis (rare); ciborium

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A (small) box; a casket, a coffret.

   **Usage:** broadly; rare

   **Source synonyms:** pyxis

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A box used in a mint as a place to deposit sample coins intended to have the fineness of their metal and their weight tested before the coins are issued to the public.

   **Usage:** UK

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A compass used by sailors.

   **Usage:** obsolete; rare

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To place (the host) in a pyx.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To enclose (something) in a box or other container; specifically, to place (a deceased person's body) in a coffin; to coffin, to encoffin.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**
