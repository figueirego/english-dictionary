### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A loaf bread which contains chunks of prosciutto and provolone, using lard.

   **Usage:** New-York-City; countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** food; lifestyle

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Similar loaf breads which contains other sorts of deli meats, other cheeses, and using other oils and fats.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A loaf bread filled with leftover bits of meat and cheese, and other remains.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; uncountable

**Source sense R1.S4**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
