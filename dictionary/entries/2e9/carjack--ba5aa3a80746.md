### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To steal an automobile forcibly from (someone).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Someone should carjack that pompous jerk and teach him a lesson!

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To forcibly steal (a vehicle).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** These twerps have carjacked their last Mercedes.
