### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To provide a service to (someone); to act as a servant to (someone); to serve (someone) as a waiter or waitress in a restaurant.

   **Example:** Is someone waiting on you yet?

   **Source synonyms:** attend to; aid; attend; attend to; await; bestand; bestead; dance attendance (British); do (British; dated); do for (British; dated); serve; service; stay (obsolete); tend; wait; wait on; wait upon

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To wait for (a person).

   **Usage:** colloquial

   **Example:** I'm waiting on you before we can leave.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To wait for (a thing, or an event to take place).

   **Usage:** colloquial

   **Example:** I'm waiting on the light to change.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To be in store for (someone).

   **Source synonyms:** await

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To visit (someone) as a courtesy, on business or for ceremony.

   **Usage:** archaic

   **Source synonyms:** attend; call on

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To occur as a consequence of (something), to result from (something).

   **Usage:** archaic

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

7. To carry out or perform (a duty, function, etc.).

   **Usage:** archaic

   **Source synonyms:** attend to
