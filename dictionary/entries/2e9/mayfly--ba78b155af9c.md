### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of the many fragile insects of the order Ephemeroptera that develop in fresh water and live very briefly as winged adults.

   **Example:** While some animals take months to reproduce, mayfly need just one perfect day.

   **Source synonyms:** dayfly; drake; drakefly; ephemera; ephemerid; fishfly; shadfly; upwing fly; upwinged fly

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** attributive; figuratively
