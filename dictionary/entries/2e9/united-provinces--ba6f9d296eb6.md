### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A historic region of India, now the state of Uttar Pradesh.
   1. A province of British India from 1937 to 1947.

      **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A historic region of India, now the state of Uttar Pradesh.
   1. A province of the Dominion of India from 1947 to 1950.

      **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A republic that existed from the formal creation of a confederacy in 1581 by several Dutch provinces (which earlier seceded from Spanish rule) until the Batavian Revolution in 1795; the predecessor state of the modern Netherlands.

   **Usage:** historical

   **Source synonyms:** Dutch Republic; Republic of the Seven United Netherlands
