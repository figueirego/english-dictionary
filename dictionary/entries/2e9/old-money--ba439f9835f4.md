## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Families that have been wealthy for generations or members of such families.

   **Usage:** derogatory; idiomatic; often; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The monetary system used in the United Kingdom and Ireland before decimalisation in 1971 and consisting of pounds, shillings, pence and farthings.

   **Usage:** Ireland; UK; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The imperial system of measurement or Fahrenheit, as opposed to the metric system or Celsius.

   **Usage:** Ireland; UK; humorous; uncountable

   **Example:** These scales say I weigh 72 kilograms; what's that in old money?
