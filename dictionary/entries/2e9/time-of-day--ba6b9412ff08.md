## Noun

### Forms

- times of day — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The time according to the clock.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A loosely specified period of time, minutes or hours in duration, especially daytime, or point in time.

   **Example:** "At what time of day and year are the winds strongest?" / "Winter mornings, I think, or maybe at day’s end."

   **Example:** She would fold laundry at this time of day.

   **Example:** I love that time of day. The light is magical.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The greeting appropriate to the time of day.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. era&#58; time; day.

   **Usage:** dated; dialectal; informal

   **Example:** that's how it was in that time of day \[ = at that time, back in that day\]

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The right thing; the ticket; that which is needed.

   **Usage:** archaic
