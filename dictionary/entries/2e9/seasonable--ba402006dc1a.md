### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Opportune; occurring at an appropriate or suitable time.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Appropriate to the current season of the year.

   **Example:** The temperature outside was quite seasonable, neither warmer nor colder than I had expected.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Ephemeral; lasting for just one season.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. In season (said of game when it is legal to be hunted and killed).

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Well-seasoned; matured (e.g. timber).

   **Usage:** obsolete
