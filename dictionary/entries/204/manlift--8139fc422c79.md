### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A permanently installed device used in place of an elevator, consisting of a series of handle-and-step groups attached to a vertical conveyor, and used to transport people, typically in an industrial environment.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A piece of portable equipment consisting of a large cage, bucket, or basket at the end of an extensible boom, and used by workers to reach inaccessible places.
