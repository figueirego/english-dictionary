### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Naively or unreasonably optimistic.

   **Usage:** derogatory

   **Example:** Though he took a Panglossian view of the world in his youth, he became jaded as he grew older.

   **Source synonyms:** Panglossic; Pollyannaish; artless; callow; childlike; verdant; clueless; credulous; dewy-eyed (idiomatic); cute; **Source context (verbatim):** used ironically; fleeceable; foppotee; green (idiomatic); guileless; ingenuous; innocent; jejune; Panglossian; Panglossic; quaint; **Source context (verbatim):** used ironically; simple; simple-hearted; simple-minded; starry-eyed; unaffected; undesigning; unpretentious; unsophisticated; untested; unworldly; wide-eyed; immature; born yesterday; wet behind the ears; virgin

**Source sense R1.S2**
