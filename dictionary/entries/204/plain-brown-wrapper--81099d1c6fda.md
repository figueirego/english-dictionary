### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An obscuring cover or wrapper, originally to allow postal delivery of sexually explicit periodicals.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any veil, disguise, or mask.

   **Usage:** broadly

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

   **Usage:** broadly

<a id="r1-s4"></a>
