### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The maximum amount of chips in one's stack that is able to be wagered, after all the possible bets, raises and reraises.

   **Example:** Consider a heads-up no-limit game&#58; if Player A has 40k chips and Player B has 70k chips, the effective stack size is 40k chips.
