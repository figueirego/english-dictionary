## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The amount a species yields, when grown with other species compared to yield in a monoculture.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The amount of biomass an ecological community (e.g. a grassland patch) yields, compared to any of its member species alone.

   **Usage:** uncountable
