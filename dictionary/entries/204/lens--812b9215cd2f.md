<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An object, usually made of glass, that focuses or defocuses the light that passes through it.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; natural-sciences; optics; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A device which focuses or defocuses other waves or radiation, such as microwave radiation, electron beams, sound waves (acoustic lenses), or explosions (explosive lenses).

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A convex shape bounded by two circular arcs, joined at their endpoints, the corresponding concave shape being a lune.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The transparent crystalline structure in the eye.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A body of rock, ice, or water shaped like a convex lens.

   **Usage:** earth science

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A convex layer of fresh groundwater that floats above the denser saltwater, usually found on small coral or limestone islands and atolls.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; hydrology; natural-sciences

   **Source synonyms:** freshwater lens; Ghyben-Herzberg lens

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; programming; sciences
