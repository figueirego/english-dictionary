### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A formal investigation, often held before a jury, especially one into the cause of a death

   **Source synonyms:** assize

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An inquiry, typically into an undesired outcome

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The jury hearing such an inquiry, and the result of the inquiry

   **Usage:** obsolete; rare

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

   **Usage:** Philippines; broadly; criminal law
