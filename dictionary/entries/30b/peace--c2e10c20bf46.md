### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A state of tranquility, quiet, and harmony. For instance, a state free from civil disturbance.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** Our lounge strives to maintain an environment of peace for the comfort of our customers.

   **Source synonyms:** frith (poetic)

   **Source antonyms:** disruption; noise; irritation

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A feeling of tranquility, free from oppressive and unpleasant thoughts and emotions.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** The safety equipment will give me some peace of mind.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Death.

   **Usage:** euphemistic; figuratively; uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Harmony in personal relations.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A state free of war, in particular war between different countries.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** May there be peace in our time.

   **Source antonyms:** war; violence
