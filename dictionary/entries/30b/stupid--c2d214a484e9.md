### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Without intelligence.
   1. Lacking in intelligence.

      **Example:** It got trapped in the fishing net because it's a big stupid jellyfish!

      **Source context (verbatim):** of animates

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Without intelligence.
   1. Exhibiting the quality of having been done by someone lacking in intelligence.

      **Example:** So I have to pay first, and then wait in line? That's a stupid rule!

      **Example:** He made the stupid decision of standing in a half-hour queue for a hamburger, instead of calling ahead.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Without intelligence.
   1. Unpleasant; annoying to the speaker. This sense can be used alongside obscene words.

      **Example:** The stupid rusty wire cut my thumb when I grabbed it.

      **Example:** Stupid sexy Flanders!

4. Without intelligence.
