### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To place into isolation to prevent the spread of any contagious disease.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Venice began quarantining incoming ships for 40 days in 1448 to prevent further outbreaks of bubonic plague.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To enter or stay in quarantine, particularly to self-quarantine to avoid an epidemic disease.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** International travelers must quarantine themselves at their own expense in a designated hotel for 14 days upon arrival.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To impose a quarantine, to establish quarantine regulations.

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. Synonym of isolate more generally.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** J.F.K. "quarantined" Cuba rather than blockading it to avoid needless escalation of the conflict.

   **Source synonyms:** isolate more generally (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. Synonym of restrict.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** restrict (synonym; synonym-of)
