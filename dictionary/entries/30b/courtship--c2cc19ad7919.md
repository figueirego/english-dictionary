### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of paying court, that is, demonstrating such politeness and respect as is traditionally given at a court (“a formal assembly of a sovereign's retinue”).
   1. The ceremonial performance of acts of courtesy to a dignitary, etc.

      **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The act of paying court, that is, demonstrating such politeness and respect as is traditionally given at a court (“a formal assembly of a sovereign's retinue”).
   1. The act of wooing a person to enter into a romantic relationship or marriage; hence, the period during which a couple fall in love before their marriage.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The act of paying court, that is, demonstrating such politeness and respect as is traditionally given at a court (“a formal assembly of a sovereign's retinue”).
   1. The behaviour exhibited by an animal to attract a mate.

      **Usage:** broadly; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The act of paying court, that is, demonstrating such politeness and respect as is traditionally given at a court (“a formal assembly of a sovereign's retinue”).
   1. The act of trying to solicit a favour or support from someone.

      **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Elegance or propriety of manners fitting for a court; courtliness; (by extension) courteous or polite behaviour; courtesy.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The pursuit of being a courtier, such as exercising diplomacy, finesse, etc.; also, the artifices and intrigues of a court; courtcraft.

   **Usage:** obsolete; uncountable
