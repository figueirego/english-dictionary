### Senses

<a id="r3-s1"></a>

**Source sense R3.S1**

1. Forming derivative nouns (originally masculine), with the sense ‘son of, belonging to’, as in placenames, patronymics or diminutives; -ite.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; morpheme

   **Example:** Ealing, Dorking, Reading, Worthing

   **Example:** Browning, Channing, Ewing

   **Example:** Middle English \*bunt + -ing → bunting

   **Example:** skill + -ing → shilling

   **Example:** fourth + -ing → farthing

<a id="r3-s2"></a>

**Source sense R3.S2**

2. Forming nouns having a specified quality, characteristic, or nature; of the kind of

   **Usage:** morpheme

   **Example:** sweet + -ing → sweeting

   **Example:** white + -ing → whiting

   **Example:** geld + -ing → gelding
