## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈkɑpiˌɹaɪt/ — source labels: General-American
- IPA: /ˈkɒpiˌɹaɪt/ — source labels: Received-Pronunciation

### Forms

- copyrights — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The right by law to be the entity which determines who may publish, copy and distribute a piece of writing, music, picture or other work of authorship.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** Copyright is a separate legal area from trademarks.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Such an exclusive right as it pertains to one or more specific works.

   **Usage:** countable; usually

   **Example:** The artist lost the copyrights to her songs when she signed the contract.

   **Example:** The images are still copyright of the original artist.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A violation of copyright law; copyright infringement.

   **Usage:** informal; nonstandard; uncountable; usually

   **Example:** no copyright intended
