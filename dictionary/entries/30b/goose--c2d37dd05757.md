### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of various grazing waterfowl of the family Anatidae, which have feathers and webbed feet and are capable of flying, swimming, and walking on land, and which are generally bigger than ducks.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** There is a flock of geese on the pond.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A female goose.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; strictly

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The flesh of the goose used as food.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A silly person.

   **Usage:** countable; slang; uncountable; plural geese or gooses

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A tailor's iron, heated in live coals or embers, used to press fabrics.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** goose iron

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A young woman or girlfriend.

   **Usage:** South-Africa; countable; dated; slang; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. An old English board game in which players moved counters along a board, earning a double move when they reached the picture of a goose.

   **Usage:** historical; uncountable
