### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A kind of package in which pepper and other dry commodities used to be exported from Southeast Asia.

   **Usage:** archaic; historical

   **Example:** The robbin of rice in Malabar weighed about 84 pounds.
