### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An adamant belief in a falsehood despite incontestable evidence.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The state of being deluded or misled, or process of deluding somebody.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** It is far better to grasp the universe as it really is than to persist in delusion, however satisfying and reassuring.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. That which is falsely or delusively believed or propagated; false belief; error in belief.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A strong belief contrary to evidence.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
