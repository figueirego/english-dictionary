### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who marks with points, especially in writing Hebrew.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An official charged with keeping track of attendance at religious services.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
