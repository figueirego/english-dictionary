### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To divide into portions and dispense.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He distributed the bread amongst his followers.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To supply to retail outlets.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The agency distributes newspapers to local shops.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To deliver or pass out.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** A network of children distributes flyers to every house.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To scatter or spread.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I raked the soil then distributed grass seed.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To apportion (more or less evenly).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The robot's six legs distributed its weight over a wide area.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To classify or separate into categories.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The database distributed verbs into transitive and intransitive segments.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To be distributive.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Multiplication distributes over addition.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To separate (type which has been used) and return it to the proper boxes in the cases.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** media; printing; publishing

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To spread (ink) evenly, as upon a roller or a table.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** media; printing; publishing

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. To employ (a term) in its whole extent; to take as universal in one premise.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; logic; mathematics; philosophy; sciences

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. To have employees working remotely from multiple locations.

   **Example:** distributed business; distributed employee

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business
