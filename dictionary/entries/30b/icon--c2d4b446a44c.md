### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An image, symbol, picture, portrait, or other representation, usually as an object of religious devotion.

   **Source synonyms:** idol (sometimes); graven image (pejorative)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A type of religious painting portraying a saint or scene from Scripture, often done on wooden panels.

   **Usage:** especially

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity; Eastern-Christianity; lifestyle; religion

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A person or thing that is the best example of a certain profession or some doing.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** That man is an icon in the business; he personifies loyalty and good business sense.

   **Source synonyms:** archetype; byspel (rare); case; beau ideal; case in point; embodiment; epitome; eponym (archaic); exemplar; exemplification; example; exemplificator; Exhibit A; illustration; incarnation; instance; paradigm; prototype; quintessence; representative; sample (obsolete); specimen; symbol; ur-type; archetype; epitome; example; exemplar; icon; model; paradigm; paragon; pattern; quintessence; template

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A small picture that represents something.

   **Example:** Click the loudspeaker icon to configure audio settings.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; graphical-user-interface; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A small picture that represents something.
   1. An image used to represent a user; an avatar or profile picture.

      **Usage:** Internet; informal

      **Example:** Is it okay if I use your drawing as my icon?

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; graphical-user-interface; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A word, character, or sign whose form reflects and is determined by the referent; onomatopoeic words are necessarily all icons.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences; semiotics
