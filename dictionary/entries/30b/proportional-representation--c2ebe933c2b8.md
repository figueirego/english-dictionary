## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A voting principle aimed at securing a close match between the percentage of votes that groups of candidates (usually political parties) obtain in elections, and the percentage of seats they receive in the elected body.

   **Usage:** uncountable
