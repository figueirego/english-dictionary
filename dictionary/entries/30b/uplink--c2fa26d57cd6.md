## Noun · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The portion of a communications link used for the transmission of signals from a ground station to a satellite or to an airborne platform.

   **Source antonyms:** downlink

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The communication path from a mobile device to a base station, a consumer to the network backbone, a client device to a server, etc.

   **Usage:** by analogy; less formally; by analogy; less formally

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** communications; electrical-engineering; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; telecommunications
