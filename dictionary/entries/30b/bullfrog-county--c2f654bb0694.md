## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A former county of Nye, Nevada, United States, containing Yucca Mountain; but having no residents, no buildings, no roads; except for the infrastructure of the U.S. national nuclear waste storage site at Yucca. Bullfrog county, a 12mix12mi square centered around Yucca, was split from Nye County in 1987 and merged back into it in 1989. The county of Bullfrog was completely enclaved inside Nye. The county seat was Carson City, the capital of Nevada, and not located inside Bullfrog.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A proposed county in Nye, Nevada, United States. Seat&#58; Bullfrog. A failed proposal from 1909 to split off southern Nye County into a new separate county. This proposed county would also contain the Bullfrog Mining District, and the future failed county of Bullfrog within its territory, and the now ghosttown of Bullfrog.
