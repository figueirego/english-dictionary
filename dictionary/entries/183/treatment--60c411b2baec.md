### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The process or manner of treating someone or something.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** He still has nightmares resulting from the abusive treatment he received from his captors.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Medical care for an illness or injury.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** A treatment or cure is applied after a medical problem has already started.

   **Example:** Cancer survivors who got radiation treatments as children have nearly twice the risk of developing diabetes as adults.

   **Example:** The change is due largely to the increased availability of antiretroviral treatment.

   **Source synonyms:** course

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The use of a substance or process to preserve or give particular properties to something.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A treatise; a formal written description or characterization of a subject.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A brief, third-person, present-tense summary of a proposed film.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** broadcasting; film; media; television

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. entertainment; treat

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. harsh punishment; retribution

   **Usage:** countable; euphemistic; uncountable

   **Example:** He’s been a naughty boy, so give him a good treatment.
