<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

   1. Neither straight up nor on the rocks

      **Example:** I like my whisky neat, so you have no need for ice at all.

      **Source context (verbatim):** of liquors or cocktails; of alcoholic beverages; of liquids

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Conditions with a liquid reagent or gas performed with no standard solvent or cosolvent.

   **Example:** The Arbuzov reaction is performed by adding the bromide to the phosphite, neat. The molecular beam was neat acetylene.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

   **Usage:** archaic

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Having a simple elegance or style; clean, trim, tidy, tasteful.

   **Example:** The front room was neat and carefully arranged for the guests.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Well-executed or delivered; clever, skillful, precise.

   **Example:** Having the two protagonists meet in the last act was a particularly neat touch.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. Facile; missing complexity or details in the favor of convenience or simplicity.

   **Example:** Courts should not reduce this case to a neat set of legal rules.

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. Good, excellent, desirable; interesting; cool.

   **Usage:** Canada; US; colloquial

   **Example:** Hey, neat convertible, man.

**Source sense R1.S11**
