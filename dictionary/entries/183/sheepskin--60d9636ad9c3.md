### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The skin of a sheep, especially an adult sheep, and especially when used to make parchment or in bookbinding.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** lambskin

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The skin of a sheep, especially an adult sheep, and especially when used to make parchment or in bookbinding.
   1. The tanned skin of a sheep with the fleece left on, especially when used for clothing, rugs, etc.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source synonyms:** lambskin

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
