### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To assign a quantity to.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To determine the value of (a variable or expression).

**Source sense R1.S3**
