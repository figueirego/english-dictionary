<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Capable of being eaten without harm; suitable for consumption; innocuous to humans.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** edible fruit

   **Source synonyms:** esculent (formal)

**Source sense R1.S2**
