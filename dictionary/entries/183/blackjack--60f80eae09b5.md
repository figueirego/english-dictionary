**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** card-games; games

   **Source synonyms:** pontoon; twenty-one; vingt-et-un

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A hand in the game of blackjack consisting of a face card and an ace.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** card-games; games

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A variant of switch where each player is initially dealt the same number of cards, usually seven, and when one player plays a black jack the player whose turn comes next has to pick up that many cards, unless they play a red jack (as this normally cancels a black jack).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** card-games; games

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A variant of hearts where the Jack of Spades is the penalty card.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** card-games; games

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The flag (i.e., a jack) traditionally flown by pirate ships; popularly thought to be a white skull and crossed bones on a black field (the Jolly Roger).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A small, flat, blunt, usually leather-covered weapon loaded with heavy material such as lead or ball bearings, intended to inflict a blow to the head that renders the victim unconscious with diminished risk of lasting cranial trauma.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
