- hob-nob — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To drink together.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To associate with in a friendly manner, often with those of a higher class or status; to fraternize.

   **Usage:** derogatory; intransitive; often

   **Example:** The ambitious young student hobnobbed with the faculty at the prestigious college he hoped to attend.

   **Example:** His favorite spot in the club was the bar, where he could hobnob with the big-wigs.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To have or have not; to give or take.

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete; rare

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To toast one another by touching glasses.

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete
