### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The great circle on the celestial sphere that is the mean apparent path of the sun as viewed from the earth.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astronomy; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The plane in which this great circle lies.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astronomy; natural-sciences

   **Source synonyms:** ecliptic plane; plane of the ecliptic

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
