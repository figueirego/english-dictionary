### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The point in the sky vertically above a given position or observer; the point in the celestial sphere opposite the nadir.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astronomy; natural-sciences

   **Source antonyms:** nadir; perigee

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The highest point in the sky reached by a celestial body.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astronomy; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Highest point or state; peak.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** Winning the continental championship was the zenith of my career.

   **Source synonyms:** acme; apogee; culmination; pinnacle; apex
