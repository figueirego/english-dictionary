### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. (Of a resource) To support or sustain someone for a limited period.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; sometimes; transitive

   **Example:** These crisps should hold me over until supper.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To save, delay.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** We will have to hold over these files until tomorrow.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To remain in office, possession, residency etc., beyond a certain date.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** We should hold over this report until the new department lead comes in.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To be in a position to harm (someone); to have damaging information on (someone).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He's holding those allegations over my head.
