**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Collaboration can be a useful part of the creative process.

   **Example:** During the interwar period, fascists proposed class collaboration as a response to communist class struggle.

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A production or creation made by collaborating.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** The husband-and-wife artists will release their new collaboration in June this year.

**Source sense R1.S3**

   **Usage:** uncountable
