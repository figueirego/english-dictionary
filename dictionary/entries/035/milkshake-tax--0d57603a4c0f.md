### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A tax on sugary prepackaged milk-based drinks, which were formerly excluded from the tax on sugary drinks.

   **Usage:** UK; informal

   **Example:** The milkshake tax will increase the cost of a latte as well as a milkshake.
