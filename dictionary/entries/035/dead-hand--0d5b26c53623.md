## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A weapons system, implemented by the Soviet Union and by the Russian Federation as the successor state thereof, capable of closing a strategic nuclear strike control loop in the absence of normal command and control channels, analogous in concept to a dead man's switch, in anticipation of a decapitation strike.

   **Example:** Sometimes people glibly speak as if the Dead Hand were a thing of the past, but it is foolhardy to assume so.

   **Source synonyms:** doomsday weapon; doomsday device; doomsday bomb

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any such system, whether the cardinal instance (the Soviet/Russian one) or another.

   **Example:** The talk then turned darkly to the prospects for building a Dead Hand of their own.
