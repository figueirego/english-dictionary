## Verb · Etymology 1 · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /steɪd͡ʒ/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Forms

- stages — present; singular; third-person
- staging — participle; present
- staged — participle; past
- staged — past

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To produce on a stage, to perform a play.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The local theater group will stage "Pride and Prejudice".

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To arrange in a manner intended to deceive.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The salesman's demonstration of the new cleanser was staged to make it appear highly effective.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To furnish and decorate (a property) to make it more appealing to potential buyers.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to stage a house or flat

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; real-estate

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To orchestrate; to carry out.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The workers staged a strike.

   **Example:** A protest will be staged in the public square on Monday.

   **Example:** They wanted to put in a new government, so they staged a coup.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To place in position to prepare for use.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** We staged the cars to be ready for the start, then waited for the starter to drop the flag.

   **Example:** to stage data to be written at a later time

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To determine what stage (a disease, etc.) has progressed to.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

**Source sense R2.S7**

7. To jettison a spent stage of a multistage rocket or other launch vehicle and light the engine(s) of the stage above it.

   **Usage:** space flight

   **Example:** In Kerbal Space Program, you stage away used-up parts of your rocket by hitting the spacebar.

<a id="r2-s8"></a>

**Source sense R2.S8**

8. To work an internship, usually as a chef or waiter.

   **Usage:** Canada; US; intransitive
