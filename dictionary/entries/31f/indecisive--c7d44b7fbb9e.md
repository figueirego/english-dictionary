### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of a person, not decisive, not marked by promptness or decision.

   **Example:** They were all being very indecisive about what to wear to the party.

   **Source synonyms:** ambivalent

   **Source antonyms:** decisive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of a contest, etc., inconclusive or uncertain.

   **Example:** The outcome of the race was so indecisive.
