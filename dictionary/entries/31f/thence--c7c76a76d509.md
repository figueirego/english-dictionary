## Adverb

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ðɛns/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. From there, from that place or from that time.

   **Usage:** formal; not-comparable

   **Example:** I came thence.

   **Example:** Cross fix at 6000 feet, thence descend to 3000 feet and fly direct to MAP (missed approach point).

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Deriving from this fact or circumstance; therefore, therefrom.

   **Usage:** literary; not-comparable

   **Example:** I had a really bad car accident, and thence came all my backpains.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. From that time; thenceforth; thereafter.

   **Usage:** archaic; not-comparable
