<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A restatement of a text in different words, often to clarify meaning or from memory rather than verbatim.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** close paraphrase

   **Example:** The article included a brief paraphrase of the speech.
