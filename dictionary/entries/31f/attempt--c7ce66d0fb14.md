### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. The action of trying at something.

   **Example:** We made an attempt to cross the stream, but didn’t manage.

   **Example:** This poem is much better than the feeble attempt of mine.

   **Example:** It was worth the attempt.

   **Example:** No matter how many failed attempts we made, we maintained a positive attitude and tried again and again until we succeeded.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. An assault or attack, especially an assassination attempt.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. A substantial but unsuccessful effort (to commit a crime).

   **Usage:** criminal law

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. An act of trying to commit suicide

   **Example:** Her attempt was not successful, and resulted in her being hospitalized.
