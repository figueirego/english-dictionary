### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To fly over something.

   **Example:** My family planned to take pictures from the ground as I overflew them in the hot air balloon.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To fly too far past something.

   **Example:** The small size of the airstrip meant that pilots would frequently overfly and have to circle back.
