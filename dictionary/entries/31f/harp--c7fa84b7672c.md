### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To repeatedly mention a subject, especially so as to nag or complain.

   **Usage:** derogatory; usually; with-on

   **Example:** Why do you harp on a single small mistake?

   **Example:** Why do you harp on about a single small mistake?

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To play on (a harp or similar instrument).

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**
