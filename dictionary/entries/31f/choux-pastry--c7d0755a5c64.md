**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A type of light pastry (made just from butter, water, flour and eggs) that is used to make profiteroles, éclairs, chouquettes, etc.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** If you don't get your choux pastry just right, the profiteroles will taste foul.
