### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To send or convey from one person, place or thing to another.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To spread or pass on (something such as a disease or a signal).

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To impart, convey or hand down by inheritance or heredity.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To communicate (news or information, especially electronically).

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** broadcast

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To convey or conduct (energy or force) through a mechanism or medium.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To send out (a signal, as opposed to receive).

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** A Mayday call was transmitted by the stricken vessel.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To go out; to be sent, conveyed, spread, passed on, or communicated.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The Mayday call transmitted successfully.
