### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who exerts power, or has the power to act.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One who acts for, or in the place of, another (the principal), by that person's authority; someone entrusted to act on behalf of or in behalf of another, such as to transact business for them.

   **Example:** He worked as an agent for the government.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A person who looks for work for another person and brokers a deal between the hiree and hirer.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Someone who works for an intelligence agency&#58; whether an officer or employee thereof or anyone else who agrees to help their efforts (for ideology, for money, as blackmailee, or otherwise).

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An active power or cause or substance; something (e.g. biological, chemical, thermal, etc.) that has the power to produce an effect.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. In the client-server model, the part of the system that performs information preparation and exchange on behalf of a client or server. Especially in the phrase “intelligent agent” it implies some kind of autonomous process which can communicate with other agents to perform some collective task on behalf of one or more humans.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The participant of a situation that carries out the action in this situation, e.g. "the boy" in the sentences "The boy kicked the ball" and "The ball was kicked by the boy".

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** grammar; human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** doer

   **Source antonyms:** patient

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A cheat who is assisted by dishonest casino staff.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** gambling; games

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A law enforcement officer tasked with enforcing a specific field of law.

   **Usage:** US

   **Example:** The Bureau of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms and Explosives officers are just simply referred to as "ATF agents".

   **Example:** All federal agents are federal police officers but not all federal police officers are agents. For example, Drug Enforcement Administration agents are both agents and federal police officers while US Marshals are just federal police officers.

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. A respectful term of address for an agent, especially a law enforcement agent.

   **Usage:** US; capitalized; usually
