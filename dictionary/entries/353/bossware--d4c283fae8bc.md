## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Software installed on employees' computers for the purpose of allowing a manager to track their working time and activity.

   **Usage:** informal; uncountable

   **Example:** Our organisation sent us laptops to work from home, but it has some very intrusive bossware installed on it.

   **Source synonyms:** tattleware
