### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Harsh and rough-sounding.

   **Example:** At night, raucous ruckus took place in the swamp.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Disorderly and boisterous.

   **Example:** Acts of vandalism were committed by a raucous gang of drunkards.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Loud and annoying.

   **Example:** The new neighbors had a raucous ruction in the small hours last night.
