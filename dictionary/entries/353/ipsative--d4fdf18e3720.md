### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Denoting a measure that forces a person to choose among multiple desirable options.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Myers-Briggs uses ipsative measures, whereas the Five-Factor Model uses normative measures.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; psychology; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Comparing a measure of a person against that person’s previous assessments, rather than against the performance of others.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** education
