### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The state of feeling confused or mystified because one cannot understand a complicated matter, a problem, etc.; bewilderment, confusion; (countable) often in in a puzzle&#58; an instance of this.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** bafflement; perplexity

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A thing such as a complicated matter or a problem which is difficult to make sense of or understand; also, a person who is difficult to make sense of or understand; an enigma.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** Where he went after he left the house is a puzzle.

   **Source synonyms:** anybody's guess; anyone's guess; conundrum; mystery; crux

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Often preceded by a descriptive word&#58; a game or toy, or a problem, requiring some effort to complete or work out, which is intended as a pastime and/or to test one's mental ability.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** crossword puzzle jigsaw puzzle

   **Source synonyms:** brainteaser; poser

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Short for puzzle-peg (“a piece of wood secured under a dog's jaw to keep the dog's nose away from the ground so that it uses the scent in the air to track its quarry, and to prevent the dog from tearing the quarry once found”).

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; countable; obsolete

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; hunting; lifestyle

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To cause (oneself or someone, or their mind, etc.) to feel confused or mystified because they cannot understand a complicated matter, a problem, etc.; to confuse, to mystify, to perplex.

   **Usage:** also; reflexive; transitive; often passive voice

   **Source synonyms:** empuzzle; addle; baffle; vex; lose; embroil; stick; stump; baffound; bamboozle; bedevil; befuddle; throw; bemud (figuratively); bemuse; bewilder; bumfuzzle; complicate; confound; confuddle (slang); confuse; confuzzle (childish; slang); dazzle; discombobulate; disconcert; blind; dumbfound; entangle (figuratively); ferhoodle; flabagast; flabbergast; flabbergaster; flurry; fluster; flummox; fuddle; jumble; knot (obsolete); mix up; muddle; muddy (figuratively); [ravel](../3bd/ravel--ef6ef65be57b.md); perplex; puzzle

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To use (one's brain or mind) to try to work out a complicated matter, a problem, etc.; also, to try to work out (a complicated matter, a problem, etc.).

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To (intentionally) make (something) complicated or confused, and so difficult to resolve or understand; to confuse, to complicate.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** confound; entangle; muddle; obfuscate

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To cause (someone) to not know what to do due to some problem, situation, etc.; to bewilder, to confound, to perplex.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive; chiefly passive voice

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. Often followed by about, over, or upon&#58; to feel confused or mystified because one cannot understand a complicated matter, a problem, etc.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. Often followed by about, over, or or upon&#58; to think deeply in bewilderment to try to work out a complicated matter, a problem, etc.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** We puzzled over the curious-shaped lock, but were unable to discover how the key should be inserted.

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

**Source sense R2.S7**

7. To search in a confused or mystified manner.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s8"></a>

**Source sense R2.S8**

8. Followed by through&#58; to solve a complicated matter, a problem, etc., by working through confusing or difficult matters.

   **Usage:** intransitive
