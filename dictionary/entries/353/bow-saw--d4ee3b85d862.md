### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A lightweight metal-framed saw in the shape of a bow with a removable coarse wide blade, used for cutting tree trunks.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A woodworking saw used for straight or curved cuts.
