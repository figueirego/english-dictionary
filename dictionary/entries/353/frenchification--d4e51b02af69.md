## Noun

### Forms

- Frenchifications — plural
- frenchification — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act or process of making French or more French-like, especially in informal contexts.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The Frenchification of the shopping district is complete&#58; every shop has been redone in a Haussmannian style and baguettes, wine, and accordion music are inescapable.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Something that has been made more French or French-like.

   **Usage:** countable; slang; uncountable

   **Example:** The ubiquitous café is just a Frenchification of the old English coffeehouse, providing the ambiance of peaceful al fresco dining without actually moving any chairs outdoors.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Synonym of Gallicization, translation into French or a French translation.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** Gallicization (synonym; synonym-of); translation into French or a French translation (synonym; synonym-of)

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** Gallicization; francization (Quebec); frenchifying (active)
