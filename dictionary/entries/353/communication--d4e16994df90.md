### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act or fact of communicating anything; transmission.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** communication of smallpox

   **Example:** communication of a secret

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The concept or state of exchanging data or information between entities.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Some say that communication is a necessary prerequisite for sentience; others say that it is a result thereof.

   **Example:** The node had established communication with the network, but had as yet sent no data.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A message; the essential data transferred in an act of communication.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Surveillance was accomplished by means of intercepting the spies' communications.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The body of all data transferred to one or both parties during an act of communication.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The subpoena required that the company document their communication with the plaintiff.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An instance of information transfer; a conversation or discourse.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The professors' communications consisted of lively discussions via email.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A passageway or opening between two locations; connection.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** A round archway at the far end of the hallway provided communication to the main chamber.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A connection between two tissues, organs, or cavities.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Association; company.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. Participation in Holy Communion.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. A trope by which a speaker assumes that his hearer is a partner in his sentiments, and says "we" instead of "I" or "you".

   **Usage:** countable; rhetoric; uncountable
