### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A formal exposition of a subject, especially a research paper that students write in order to complete the requirements for a doctoral degree in the US and a non-doctoral degree in the UK; a thesis.

   **Example:** write a dissertation

   **Example:** write up a dissertation

   **Example:** hand in a dissertation

   **Example:** complete a dissertation

   **Example:** She spent three years writing her doctoral dissertation.

   **Example:** The student defended his dissertation successfully.

   **Example:** Her dissertation focused on medieval literature.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A lengthy lecture on a subject; a treatise; a discourse; a sermon.
