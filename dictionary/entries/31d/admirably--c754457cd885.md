## Adverb

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈæd.mə.ɹə.bli/ — source labels: Australia; UK
- IPA: /ˈæd.mɪɹ.ə.bli/ — source labels: Canada; US

### Forms

- more admirably — comparative
- most admirably — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In a way worthy of admiration.

   **Example:** He succeeded admirably in drawing fire away from the troop transports.

   **Example:** Admirably, he went down with his ship after the surviving crew got away in lifeboats.

   **Source synonyms:** laudably

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To an admirable degree.

   **Example:** They have made admirably great improvements in quality.
