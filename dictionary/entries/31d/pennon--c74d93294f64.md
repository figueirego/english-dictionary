### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A long, narrow flag or streamer, often swallowtailed or triangular, usually hung at the top of a helmet or lance, originally the ensign of a knight under the rank of knight banneret, and later of a lancer regiment.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any banner or flag.

   **Usage:** broadly

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Synonym of pinion (“a wing”).

   **Usage:** archaic; broadly; poetic

   **Source synonyms:** pinion (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A heraldic charge in the form of a pennon (sense 1).

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; heraldry; hobbies; lifestyle; monarchy; nobility; politics

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Synonym of pennant (“a flag or streamer used on a ship”).

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

   **Source synonyms:** pennant (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A Knight Bachelor; also, a soldier who carries an ensign.

   **Usage:** broadly; obsolete

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. An ornament that dangles or hangs down.

   **Usage:** broadly; obsolete; rare

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Something resembling a pennon (sense 1).

   **Usage:** figuratively
