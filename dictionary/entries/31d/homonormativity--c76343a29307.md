### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The assumption that sexual and romantic attraction and activity between people of the same sex is normal, either as the only normal, or in a culture which also treats opposite-sex activity as normal (and typically assumes all people are either male or female).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The adoption of heterosexist values, beliefs and norms into the gay or queer community.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
