## Adverb

### Forms

- most arithmetically — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In a way characterized by addition (of increments) rather than multiplication (by factors) or exponentiation (by exponents)&#58; by an arithmetic progression.

   **Example:** Over the past decade the business has grown arithmetically, which shows that it is serving a demand successfully; but because it has not grown exponentially, its growth is viewed by some investors as "not good enough."

   **Source context (verbatim):** of the nature of increases or decreases

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In terms of arithmetic.

   **Example:** The two terms are arithmetically equivalent, but they are not equally intuitive regarding what phenomenon they represent in the real world.
