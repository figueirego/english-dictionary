<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The part of a country which borders or faces another country or unsettled region.

   **Example:** the frontier of civilization

   **Source synonyms:** march; marches; border; marchland; borderland

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The most advanced or recent version of something; the leading edge.

   **Example:** the frontier of solid state electronics

   **Source synonyms:** vanguard; state of the art
