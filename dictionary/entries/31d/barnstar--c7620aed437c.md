### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A decorative painted object or image, often in the shape of a five-pointed star but occasionally in a circular wagon-wheel style, used to adorn a barn.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A lucky charm resembling a star plate, similar to a horseshoe.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An image posted on a wiki user's personal page by another user, as an informal award or form of recognition.
