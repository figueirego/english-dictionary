### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who constructs a deck of playing cards in certain games where players can make decisions about which cards to include.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A card game, board game or video game where construction of a deck of cards is a main element of gameplay.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. One who constructs a deck (raised flat surface that can be walked on).
