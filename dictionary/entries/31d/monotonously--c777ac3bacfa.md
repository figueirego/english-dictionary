## Adverb

### Forms

- more monotonously — comparative
- most monotonously — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In a manner that is tedious, repetitious or lacking in variety.

   **Example:** She worked monotonously at the assembly line.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In a droning manner, that does not change pitch.

   **Example:** The minister spoke monotonously and his congregation began to doze.
