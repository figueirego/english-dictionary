### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To be like or similar to (something); to represent as similar.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The twins resemble each other.

   **Source synonyms:** recall; favor

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To compare; to regard as similar, to liken.

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To counterfeit; to imitate.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To cause to imitate or be like; to make similar.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive
