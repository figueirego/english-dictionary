### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The fermionic supersymmetric partner of a boson (a bosino), symbolized by a tilde over the nonsupersymmetric particle symbol.

   **Usage:** morpheme

   **Example:** The supersymmetric partner of the photon is a photino.

   **Example:** In supersymmetry theory, all bosons have fermionic counterparts, known as bosinos.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

   **Usage:** morpheme; chiefly DoggoLingo

   **Example:** pupper + -ino → pupperino
