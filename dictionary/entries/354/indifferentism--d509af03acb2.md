### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The doctrine that all religions are equally valid.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** lifestyle; religion

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually; more broadly

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

   **Usage:** countable; usually
