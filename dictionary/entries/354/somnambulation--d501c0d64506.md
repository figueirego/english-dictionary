## Noun

### Forms

- somnambulations — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The phenomenon of sleepwalking.

   **Usage:** countable; rare; uncountable

   **Example:** He is known for his somnambulation, as well as his vivid sleep-talking.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An instance of sleepwalking.

   **Usage:** countable; rare; uncountable

   **Example:** He broke his arm on one of his many somnambulations.
