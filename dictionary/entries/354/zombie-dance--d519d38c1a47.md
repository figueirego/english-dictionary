### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A type of dance in which participants use dance moves that mimic the undead, often combined with a masquerade and costumes.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** dance; dancing; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** dance; dancing; hobbies; lifestyle; sports
