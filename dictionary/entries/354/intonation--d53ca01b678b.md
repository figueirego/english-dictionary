**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The rise and fall of the voice in speaking.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
