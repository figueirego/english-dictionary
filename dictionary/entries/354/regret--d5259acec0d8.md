### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To feel sorry about (a thing that has or has not happened), afterthink&#58; to wish that a thing had not happened, that something else had happened instead.

   **Example:** He regretted his words.

   **Example:** I don't regret enrolling in law, but I regret not studying some Chinese.

   **Source synonyms:** rue

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To feel sorry about (any thing).

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** I regret that I have to do this, but I don't have a choice.

   **Example:** They said they regretted to inform us that the train would be late.

   **Source synonyms:** grieve; lament; aggrieve (obsolete); besorrow; condole (obsolete); darken; engrieve (archaic); erme (obsolete); gloom; grieve; grieven; bleed; lament; moan (poetic); mourn; remorse; repent; regret; rue (archaic); sigh; sorrow; wayment (obsolete); yearn (obsolete)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To miss; to feel the loss or absence of; to mourn.

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Emotional pain on account of something done or experienced in the past, with a wish that it had been different; a looking back with dissatisfaction or with longing.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** nostalgia; remorse; repentance; unsatisfaction; agenbite; remorse; rue (archaic; dialectal); ruth (archaic); compunction; contrition; guilt; penitence; repentance; self-reproach; sorriness

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Dislike; aversion.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. The amount of avoidable loss that results from choosing the wrong action.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; decision theory

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. One who was invited to an event who was unable to attend, but notified the organizer of this beforehand; a nonattendee.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
