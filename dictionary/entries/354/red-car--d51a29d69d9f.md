### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A streetcar of the former Pacific Electric Railway mass transit system that went all across Greater Los Angeles.

   **Usage:** historical; informal

   **Example:** My father used to talk about how you could take a red car just about anywhere in the Los Angeles area.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The entire now-defunct corporation and system of the Pacific Electric Railway.

   **Usage:** historical

   **Example:** The red car operated from 1901–1961.

**Source sense R1.S3**
