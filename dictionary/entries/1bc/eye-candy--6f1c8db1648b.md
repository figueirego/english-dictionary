## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A very attractive person or persons, or the salient visible physical attributes thereof.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** I'm going to the beach to check out some eye candy.

   **Source synonyms:** beaut; beauty; bobby-dazzler (Australia; Britain); crumpet (Britain); dish; vision; dreamboat; eye candy; good looker; looker; lulu (slang); sex on a stick; sex on legs; smoke show; stunna (Britain); stunner
