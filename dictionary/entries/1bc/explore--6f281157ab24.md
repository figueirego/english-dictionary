### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To seek for something or after someone.

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To examine or investigate something systematically.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The committee has been exploring alternative solutions to the problem at hand.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To travel somewhere in search of discovery.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** It was around that time that the expedition began exploring the Arctic Circle.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To examine diagnostically.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To (seek) experience first hand.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** It is normal for a boy of this age to be exploring his sexuality.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To be engaged exploring in any of the above senses.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** He was too busy exploring to notice his son needed his guidance.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To wander without any particular aim or purpose.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The boys explored all around till cold and hunger drove them back to the campfire one by one.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To seek sexual variety, to sow one's wild oats.

   **Usage:** transitive
