### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The delay that occurs when a call arrives at an automatic switching device and no channel or facility is immediately available to process the call.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** communications; electrical-engineering; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; telecommunications

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The time between the instant a system receives a call attempt and the instant of initiation of ringing at the target device.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
