## Noun

### Forms

- chains of thought — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A connected sequence of ideas, reasoning, or mental associations that someone experiences in thinking; the logical or associative progression of someone’s reasoning.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Source synonyms:** train of thought; line of thought; thought path; thread of thought; thought process

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A sequence of intermediate reasoning steps generated by a model to break down and solve a problem before producing its final answer or response.

   **Usage:** artificial intelligence
