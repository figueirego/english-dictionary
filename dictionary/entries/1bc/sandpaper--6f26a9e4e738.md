### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Strong paper coated with (traditionally) sand or (today) manufactured aluminum oxide, silicon carbide, or other abrasive material, used for smoothing and polishing.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** They like to finish it off with a piece of 600-grit sandpaper before cleaning and painting.

   **Source synonyms:** sanding paper; emery paper

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A sheet of such paper.

   **Usage:** countable; usually

   **Example:** I need a scraper and a sandpaper over here.
