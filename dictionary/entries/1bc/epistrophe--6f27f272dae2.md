### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The repetition of the same word or words at the end of successive phrases, clauses or sentences.

   **Usage:** countable; rhetoric; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** epiphora; antistrophe

   **Source antonyms:** anaphora

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An arrangement of chlorophyll grains on the outer surface of plant cells, as opposed to apostrophe (an arrangement at right angles to the surface).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences
