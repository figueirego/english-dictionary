### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In fortification&#58; an indented parapet, formed by a series of rising members called cops or merlons, separated by openings called crenelles or embrasures, the soldier sheltering himself behind the merlon while he fires through the embrasure or through a loophole in the battlement.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any high wall for defense.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The towering roof of heaven.
