## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A belief in unrealizable or impractical policies.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A socialist doctrine that stresses the limited value of political, economic, and social reforms within a capitalist economy, arguing that the pursuit of such reforms strengthens support for the existing system.

   **Usage:** Marxism; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; politics
