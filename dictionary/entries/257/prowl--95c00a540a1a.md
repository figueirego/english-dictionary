### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To rove over, through, or about in a stealthy manner; especially, to search in, as for prey or booty.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** Watch the lioness prowling in the shrubbery for zebras.

   **Example:** It's easy to sneak vandalism into Wiktionary as there are few other users prowling the Recent Changes page.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To idle; to go about aimlessly.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** That dandy has nothing better to do than prowl around town all day in his pinstripe suit.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To collect by plunder.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

   **Example:** to prowl money
