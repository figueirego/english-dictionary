<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A small, showy ornament, especially a piece of jewellery.

   **Example:** That little trinket around her neck must have cost a bundle.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** It’s only a little trinket, but it reminds her of him.

   **Usage:** obsolete

   **Usage:** in-plural; obsolete
