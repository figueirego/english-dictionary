### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. a blessing said in vain

   **Usage:** Judaism

   **Example:** You don't say hamotzi on brisket! That's totally a bracha levatala!

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. a waste, a pointless action

   **Usage:** Judaism; figuratively

   **Example:** I spent the whole day trying to get to the market, but by they were closed by the time I got there! The whole thing was a bracha levatala.
