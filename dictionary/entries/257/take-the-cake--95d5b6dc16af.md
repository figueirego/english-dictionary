**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used as an affirmation or accolade of someone occupying the top rank in a comparison or contest; to win or be the best (at).

   **Usage:** US; colloquial; idiomatic

   **Example:** Thanks! You guys take the cake.

   **Example:** Once the party was over, everyone agreed that Elizabeth and her Harriet Tubman getup had taken the cake.
