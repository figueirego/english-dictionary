## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An issue to be decided by the judge, concerning the application or interpretation of the law.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A question that the law itself has authoritatively answered, so that the court may not answer it as a matter of discretion.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An issue about what the law is on a particular point; an issue in which the parties argue about, and the court must decide, what the true rule of law is.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An issue that, although it may turn on a factual point, is reserved for the court and excluded from the jury; an issue that is exclusively within the province of the judge and not the jury.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law
