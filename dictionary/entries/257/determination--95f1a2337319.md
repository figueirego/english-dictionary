## Noun

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of determining, or the state of being determined.
   1. The act, process, or result of any accurate measurement, as of length, volume, weight, intensity, etc.

      **Usage:** countable

      **Example:** the determination of the wavelength of light

      **Example:** the determination of the level of salt in sea water

      **Example:** the determination of the oxygen in the air

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The act of determining, or the state of being determined.
   1. The act of defining a concept or notion by giving its essential constituents.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The act of determining, or the state of being determined.
   1. The addition of a distinguishing feature to a concept or notion, thus limiting its extent.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source antonyms:** generalization

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The act of determining, or the state of being determined.
   1. The act of determining the relations of an object, such as genus and species; the referring of minerals, plants, or animals, to the species to which they belong; classification

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Example:** I am indebted to a friend for the determination of most of these shells.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Direction or tendency to a certain end; impulsion.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** purpose

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The quality of mind which reaches definite conclusions; decisive character; resoluteness; fortitude.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Through sheer determination, we managed to escape from the tornado.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The state of decision; a judicial decision, or ending of controversy.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. That which is determined upon; result of deliberation; purpose; conclusion formed; fixed resolution.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** The tribunal has reached a determination in respect of this matter.

   **Source synonyms:** decision; conclusion; verdict; award; censure (obsolete); choice; opinion; conclusion; decision; deduction; determination; ergotism; illation; inference; judgement/judgment; mind; result; sight; take; verdict; view

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A flow, rush, or tendency to a particular part

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** a determination of blood to the head

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. Bringing to an end; termination; limit.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; otherwise obsolete

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law
