### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A man who rides a horse.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A soldier on horseback, especially a cavalryman.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A man skilled in horsemanship, especially an equestrian.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A man in charge of work horses; a teamster.

   **Usage:** UK; archaic; especially

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** agriculture; business; lifestyle

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A man in charge of transport horses; a hostler (ostler).

   **Usage:** UK; archaic; especially

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Any of the swift-running land crabs of the genus Ocypode.

   **Usage:** archaic

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Any fish of the genus Equetus.

   **Usage:** archaic

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A fictional beast that is part horse and part man.
