### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To put one's body into a posture or series of postures, especially hoping that one will be noticed and admired.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** If you're finished posturing in front of the mirror, can I use the bathroom now?

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To pretend to have an opinion or a conviction.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The politicians couldn't really care less about the issue&#58; they're just posturing for the media.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To place in a particular position or attitude; to pose.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to posture oneself; to posture a model
