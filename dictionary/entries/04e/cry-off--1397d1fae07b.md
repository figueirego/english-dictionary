### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To cancel something previously arranged with someone.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** Sorry, but I have to cry off the game on Saturday, as my mother-in-law is coming to visit.

   **Usage:** intransitive
