## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A runtime environment where little to no abstraction is available&#58; usually employing a low-level programming language, without access to any operating system facilities, and interfacing hardware directly.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; programming; sciences
