### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A route used in mountaineering, specifically rock climbing, in which the descent occurs by a different route than the ascent.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** climbing; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A series of points, with angles and distances measured between, traveled around a subject, usually for use as "control" i.e. angular reference system for later surveying work.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; natural-sciences; surveying

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A screen or partition.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Something that thwarts or obstructs.

   **Example:** He will succeed, as long as there are no unlucky traverses not under his control.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A gallery or loft of communication from side to side of a church or other large building.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** architecture

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A formal denial of some matter of fact alleged by the opposite party in any stage of the pleadings. The technical words introducing a traverse are absque hoc ("without this", i.e. without what follows).

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The zigzag course or courses made by a ship in passing from one place to another; a compound course.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A line lying across a figure or other lines; a transversal.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. In trench warfare, a defensive trench built to prevent enfilade.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. A traverse board.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To travel across, to go through, to pass through, particularly under difficult conditions.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He will have to traverse the mountain to get to the other side.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To visit all parts of; to explore thoroughly.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to traverse all nodes in a network

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To lay in a cross direction; to cross.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To rotate a gun around a vertical axis to bear upon a military target.

   **Example:** to traverse a cannon

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; government; military; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; politics; tools; war; weaponry

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To climb or descend a steep hill at a wide angle (relative to the slope).

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** climbing; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To (make a cutting, an incline) across the gradients of a sloped face at safe rate.

   **Example:** the road traversed the face of the ridge as the right-of-way climbed the mountain

   **Example:** The last run, weary, I traversed the descents in no hurry to reach the lodge.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; hobbies; lifestyle; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; skiing; sports

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

**Source sense R2.S7**

7. To act against; to thwart or obstruct.

<a id="r2-s8"></a>

**Source sense R2.S8**

8. To pass over and view; to survey carefully.

<a id="r2-s9"></a>

**Source sense R2.S9**

9. To plane in a direction across the grain of the wood.

   **Example:** to traverse a board

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; carpentry; construction; manufacturing

<a id="r2-s10"></a>

**Source sense R2.S10**

10. To deny formally.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r2-s11"></a>

**Source sense R2.S11**
