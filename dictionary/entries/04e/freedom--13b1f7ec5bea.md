### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The state of being free.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Having recently been released from prison, he didn't know what to do with his newfound freedom.

   **Source synonyms:** freehood

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The lack of a specific constraint, or of constraints in general; a state of being free, unconstrained.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** Freedom of speech is a basic democratic value.

   **Example:** People in our city enjoy many freedoms.

   **Example:** Every child has a right to freedom from fear and freedom from want.

   **Source synonyms:** freeness

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The right or privilege of unrestricted use or access

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Freedom of a city

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Frankness; openness; unreservedness.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Improper familiarity; violation of the rules of decorum.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
