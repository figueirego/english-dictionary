### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act or process of making a building into a castle.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The addition of battlements to a building, as for example when a feudal manor house needed fortification against border-raider attacks.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of a set of slots or grooves in an object, often machined into a metal part, as for example on a castellated nut.

   **Usage:** countable
