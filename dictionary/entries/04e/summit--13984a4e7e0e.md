**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The topmost point or surface of a thing; the apex, the peak.
   1. The highest point of a hill, mountain, or similar geographical feature.

      **Example:** In summer, it is possible to hike to the summit of Mount Shasta.

      **Source synonyms:** acme; summity; zenith

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The topmost point or surface of a thing; the apex, the peak.
   1. Synonym of anther (“the pollen-bearing part of the stamen of a flower”) or (rare) stigma (“the sticky part of a flower that receives pollen during pollination”).

      **Usage:** obsolete

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The topmost point or surface of a thing; the apex, the peak.
   1. One of the two vertices of a crystal with a rhombohedral shape where the angles of each face are equal; also, the highest point of a crystal with a pyramidal or tetrahedral shape.

      **Usage:** obsolete; rare

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; crystallography; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

      **Source synonyms:** acme; summity; zenith

   **Usage:** figuratively

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The highest level of political leadership.

   **Usage:** archaic; figuratively

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; politics

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. An assembly or gathering of the leaders of countries to discuss issues of international significance; also (loosely), an important or high-level gathering or meeting.

   **Usage:** broadly; figuratively
