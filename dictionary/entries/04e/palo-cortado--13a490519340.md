### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A rare variety of sherry that is initially aged under flor to become a fino or amontillado, but inexplicably loses its veil of flor and begins aging oxidatively as an oloroso, resulting in a wine with some of the richness of oloroso and some of the crispness of amontillado.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
