### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of conveying; carrying.

   **Usage:** countable; uncommon; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A means of conveyance.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A (mostly four-wheeled) lighter vehicle chiefly designed to transport people, generally drawn by horse power.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The carriage ride was very romantic.

   **Source antonyms:** wagon

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Ellipsis of railway carriage (“a passenger railroad car, or any railroad car”).

   **Usage:** UK; abbreviation; alt-of; countable; ellipsis; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** rail-transport; railways; transport

   **Alternative form of:** [railway carriage](../2a1/railway-carriage--a84e2a64ddf2.md)

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The manner or posture in which one holds or positions a body part, such as one's arm or head.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The runner has a very low arm carriage.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A manner of walking and moving in general; how one carries oneself, bearing, gait.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** a tall woman with a beautiful upright carriage.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. One's behavior, or way of conducting oneself towards others.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. The part of a typewriter supporting the paper, the part of a machine tool such as a lathe that guides the bit as it cuts or otherwise manipulates the workpiece.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A shopping cart.

   **Usage:** New-England; US; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. A stroller; a baby carriage.

   **Usage:** UK; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. A gun carriage.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s12"></a>

**Source sense R1.S12**

12. The charge made for conveying (especially in the phrases carriage forward, when the charge is to be paid by the receiver, and Carriage Paid To).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** freight; freightage; cartage; charge; rate

<a id="r1-s13"></a>

**Source sense R1.S13**

13. That which is carried, baggage.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; uncountable
