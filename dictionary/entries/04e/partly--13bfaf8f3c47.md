### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In part, or to some degree, but not completely.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** They had partly finished the house, so they could move in before winter.

   **Example:** The car was partly visible in the garage.
