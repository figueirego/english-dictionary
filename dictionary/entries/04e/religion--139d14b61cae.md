## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ɹɪˈlɪd͡ʒ.ən/ — source labels: Australia; Canada; General-American; Received-Pronunciation
- IPA: /ɹəˈləd͡ʒ.ən/ — source labels: New-Zealand

### Forms

- religions — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Belief in a spiritual or metaphysical reality (often including at least one deity), accompanied by practices or rituals pertaining to the belief.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** My brother tends to value religion, but my sister not as much.

   **Source synonyms:** theism; faith; belief

   **Source antonyms:** nonreligion; nonreligiousness; nonbelief; unbelief; irreligion; irreligiousness; atheism; antitheism; antireligion; antireligiousness

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A particular system of such belief, and the rituals and practices proper to it.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** Islam is a major religion, particularly in North Africa and Southwest Asia.

   **Example:** Mormonism is a new religion, while Zoroastrianism is an old one.

   **Source synonyms:** faith; credo; creed

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The way of life committed to by monks and nuns.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** The monk entered religion when he was 20 years of age.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Rituals and actions associated with religious beliefs, but considered apart from them.

   **Usage:** informal; uncountable

   **Example:** I think some Christians would love Jesus more if they weren't so stuck in religion.

   **Example:** Jack's spiritual, but he's not really into religion.

   **Source synonyms:** superstition (pejorative)

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Any practice to which someone or some group is seriously devoted.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** At this point, Star Trek has really become a religion.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Faithfulness to a given principle; conscientiousness.

   **Usage:** obsolete; uncountable

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** faith; religion
