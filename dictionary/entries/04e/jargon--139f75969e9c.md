### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A technical terminology unique to a particular subject.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** He used technical jargon but I managed to catch the drift of his argument.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A language characteristic of a particular group.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Speech or language that is incomprehensible or unintelligible; gibberish.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Cut the jargon and get to your point.
