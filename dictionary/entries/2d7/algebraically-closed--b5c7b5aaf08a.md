## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Which contains as an element every root of every nonconstant univariate polynomial definable over it (i.e., over said field).

   **Usage:** not-comparable; field theory

   **Example:** The fundamental theorem of algebra states that the field of complex numbers, \#92;mathbb\#123;C\#125;, is algebraically closed.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** algebra; mathematics; sciences
