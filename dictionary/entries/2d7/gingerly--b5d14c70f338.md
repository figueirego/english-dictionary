### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In a cautious and delicate manner; (very) carefully or cautiously.

   **Example:** He placed the glass jar gingerly on the concrete step.

   **Source synonyms:** ginger (Britain; Canada; US; regional); tentatively; warily
