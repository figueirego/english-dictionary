## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A large island (sometimes also including some of the surrounding smaller islands) off the north-west coast of Western Europe, made up of England, Scotland, and Wales.

   **Source synonyms:** GB; Gramarye

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The United Kingdom, a kingdom and country in Northern Europe including the island of Great Britain as well as Northern Ireland on the northeastern portion of the island of Ireland.

   **Usage:** broadly

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A former kingdom existing on the island of Great Britain from 1707 to 1801, consisting of England, Scotland and Wales; it was in personal union with the Kingdom of Ireland and later merged with it. Official name&#58; Kingdom of Great Britain.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. England, Scotland and Wales in combination, to the exclusion of Northern Ireland.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; law; politics

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The United Kingdom national team (often inclusive of Northern Ireland).

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports
