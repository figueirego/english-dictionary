### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The quality produced by the sensation of taste or, especially, of taste and smell in combined effect.

   **Usage:** US; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The flavor of this apple pie is delicious.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Flavoring, a substance used to produce a taste.

   **Usage:** US; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Flavor was added to the pudding.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A variety (of taste) attributed to an object (food, candy, chewing gum, medicine, etc).

   **Usage:** US; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** What flavor of bubble gum do you most enjoy?

   **Example:** My favorite flavor of lip balm is called mystical minty camomile.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The characteristic quality of something.

   **Usage:** US; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** the flavor of an experience

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A kind or type.

   **Usage:** US; countable; figuratively; informal; uncountable

   **Example:** Debian is one flavor of the Linux operating system.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Style.

   **Usage:** US; countable; slang; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** dancing; hip-hop; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. One of the six types of quarks (top, bottom, strange, charmed, up, and down) or three types of leptons (electron, muon, and tauon).

   **Usage:** US; countable; particle; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. The quality produced by the sensation of smell; odour; fragrance.

   **Usage:** US; archaic; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** the flavor of a rose
