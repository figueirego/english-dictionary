### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Movement or advancement through a series of events, or points in time; development through time.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** Testing for the new antidote is currently in progress.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Specifically, advancement to a higher or more developed state; development, growth.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** Science has made extraordinary progress in the last fifty years.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An official journey made by a monarch or other high personage; a state journey, a circuit.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A journey forward; travel.

   **Usage:** archaic; uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Movement onwards, forwards, or towards a specific objective or direction; advance.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** The thick branches overhanging the path made progress difficult.
