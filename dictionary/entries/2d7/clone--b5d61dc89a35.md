### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A living organism (originally a plant) produced asexually from a single ancestor, to which it is genetically identical.

   **Example:** This new species is a clone of the mimosa plant.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A group of identical cells derived from a single cell.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; cytology; medicine; natural-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A copy or imitation of something already existing, especially when designed to simulate it.

   **Example:** The computer manufacturer produced IBM PC clones in the 1990s.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A person who is exactly like or very similar to another person, in terms of looks or behavior.

   **Usage:** informal

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A Castro clone.

   **Usage:** slang
