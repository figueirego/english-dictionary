### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The reverse, in contrast to the obverse.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The B-side of a phonograph record.

   **Example:** Nice tune. What's on the flipside?

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

   **Source synonyms:** B-side

   **Source antonyms:** A-side

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A necessary consequence or corollary of something that acts as a negation or counterbalance; especially one seen as opposite, or as pro versus con.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** Walking to work is definitely healthier – on the flipside, though, it takes twice as long.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The occasion when we meet again (e.g. when I return from work, etc.; said on parting); later or tomorrow.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** See you on the flipside!
