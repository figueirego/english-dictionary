## Adjective

### Pronunciation

### Forms

- nonrhotic — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of an accent, most often one of English&#58; not pronouncing the written letter "r" after a vowel, as in Received Pronunciation, most other accents of England, the Boston accent, traditional NYC English, many accents of southern Louisiana, Australian English, etc.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** In a non-rhotic accent, the word 'sort' can be pronounced like the word 'sought'.
