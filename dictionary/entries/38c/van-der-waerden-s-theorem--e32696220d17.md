## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In Ramsey theory, a theorem stating that, for any given positive integers r and k, there is some number N such that if the integers \{1, 2, ..., N\} are colored, each with one of r different colors, then there are at least k integers in arithmetic progression whose elements are of the same color.
