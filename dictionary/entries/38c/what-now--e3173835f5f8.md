## Phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A request for advice or instruction on what should be done.

   **Source synonyms:** now what

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A request, usually rhetorical, to know what, in a series of negative events, is the next negative event occurring.

   **Source synonyms:** now what
