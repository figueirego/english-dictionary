## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act or process of protecting data from unauthorized use by ensuring security and privacy through consciously chosen and enforced rules and methods.

   **Usage:** attributive; often; uncountable; usually

   **Source synonyms:** data privacy

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The business function (team, department, squad, etc) that leads others in their performance of this act or process.

   **Usage:** capitalized; often; uncountable; usually

   **Example:** This proposal has to be cleared by Legal, Human Resources, and Data Protection before it can proceed further.
