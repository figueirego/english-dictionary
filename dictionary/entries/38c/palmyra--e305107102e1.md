### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An ancient city, an oasis in the Syrian Desert, in present-day central Syria, recorded historically from around 2000 BCE, subsequently subject to various empires and destroyed in 273 CE and again in 1400, when it was reduced to a village.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A city, the county seat of Marion County, Missouri, United States.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A town and village therein, in Wayne County, New York, United States.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A census-designated place, the county seat of Fluvanna County, Virginia, United States.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A number of townships in the United States, listed under Palmyra Township.
