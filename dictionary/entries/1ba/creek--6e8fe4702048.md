### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A small inlet, often saltwater, leading to the sea or to the main channel of a river, especially a river estuary.

   **Usage:** UK

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The inner part of a port that is used as a dock for small boats.

   **Usage:** UK

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A stream of water, typically a stream of freshwater smaller than a river; in Australia, also used of river-sized bodies of water.

   **Usage:** Australia; Canada; New-Zealand; US

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any turn or winding.
