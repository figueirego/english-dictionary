### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A byproduct from brewing that is comparable to the distillers' grains from distilling and can be put to some of the same uses, such as animal feed supplementation; it comprises what is left of the malted grains after the fermentation, which includes much cellulose, lignin, other fiber, and proteins.

   **Usage:** plural; plural-only

   **Source synonyms:** draff
