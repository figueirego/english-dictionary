### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The idea that in a literary work such as a poem, virtue should be rewarded and vice punished.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; narratology; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** poetic justice

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Synonym of poetic justice (“the fact of someone experiencing what they deserve for their actions, especially when this happens in an ironic manner”).

   **Usage:** broadly; uncountable; generally

   **Source synonyms:** poetic justice (synonym; synonym-of)
