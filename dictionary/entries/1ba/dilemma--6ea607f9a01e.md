### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A circumstance in which a choice must be made between two or more alternatives that seem equally undesirable.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any difficult circumstance or problem.

   **Source synonyms:** bind; fix; pickle; problem; quandary; ass in a sling (vulgar); bind; bucket of syrup (idiomatic); calvary; cauldron; cross (figuratively); cross to bear (idiomatic); difficulty; dilemma; dire straits; doghouse; fix; hole; jam; kettle (idiomatic); kettle of fish (idiomatic); ordeal; pickle; pinch; plight; predicament; quandary; quagmire; situation; spot; spot of bother; sticky situation; sticky wicket; tight spot; trouble

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A type of syllogism of the form "if A is true then B is true; if C is true then D is true; either A or C is true; therefore either B or D is true".

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; logic; mathematics; philosophy; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Offering to an opponent a choice between two (equally unfavorable) alternatives.

   **Usage:** rhetoric
