### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The source of, or reason for, an event or action; that which produces or effects a result.

   **Usage:** countable; often; with-of; typically of adverse results

   **Example:** They identified a burst pipe as the cause of the flooding.

   **Source synonyms:** basis; genesis

   **Source antonyms:** effect; result

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Sufficient reason.

   **Usage:** especially; uncountable

   **Example:** There is no cause for alarm.

   **Example:** The end of the war was a cause for celebration.

   **Example:** He has no cause to do that.

   **Source context (verbatim):** with for and a bare noun

   **Source synonyms:** grounds; justification; occasion

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A goal, aim or principle, especially one which transcends purely selfish ends.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source synonyms:** objective; purpose; aim; ambition; aspiration; end; terminus ad quem (formal); goal; intention; object; objective; purpose; success; target; telos

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Sake; interest; advantage.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Any subject of discussion or debate; a matter; an affair.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A suit or action in court; any legal process by which a party endeavors to obtain his claim, or what he regards as his right; case; ground of action.

   **Usage:** countable
