### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A polite expression of either agreement or disagreement (clarified by following speech)&#58; can be used to denote conclusion of cursory difference(s) of opinion between speakers ("yeah-nah, he's a top bloke at the end of the day"), and/or perceived escalations of discord ("yeah-nah, I can, however, see where you're coming from").

   **Usage:** Australia; New-Zealand
