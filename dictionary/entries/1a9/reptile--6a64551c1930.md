### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A (typically scaly and egg-laying) cold-blooded vertebrate tetrapod animal, of the class Reptilia (excluding birds), such as a lizard, turtle, snake, crocodile, alligator, etc.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any member of the class Reptilia, including birds.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences; zoology

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any creeping or crawling animal, one that moves along close to the ground on its belly or on short legs, such as a lizard, snake, salamander, frog, toad, or scorpion.

   **Usage:** dated

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An animal belonging to either the class Reptilia or the class Amphibia.

   **Usage:** dated

   **Source synonyms:** herptile; herp

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An ectothermic, scaly animal, whether sauropsid or synapsid, of the clade Amniota.

   **Usage:** dated; proscribed

   **Example:** Prior to the 1990s, the non-mammalian synapsids were considered true reptiles.

   **Example:** mammal-like reptile

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. An abject, grovelling, loathsome or repulsive person.

   **Usage:** dated; figuratively

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** creeper; reptile
