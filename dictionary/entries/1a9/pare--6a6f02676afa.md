### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To remove the outer covering or skin of something with a cutting device, typically a knife.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Victor pared some apples in preparation to make a tart.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To reduce, diminish or trim gradually something as if by cutting off.

   **Usage:** transitive; often with down or back

   **Example:** Albert had to pare his options down by disregarding anything beyond his meager budget.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To trim the hoof of a horse.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To sharpen a pencil.

   **Usage:** Ireland; slang
