## Adjective · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /dɪˈmɪdi.ət/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Divided into two (equal) halves.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Consisting of only one half of what the normal condition requires; having the appearance of lacking one half.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** a dimidiate leaf, which has only one side developed

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Having the organs of one side, or half, different in function from the corresponding organs on the other side.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** dimidiate hermaphroditism

### Related words
