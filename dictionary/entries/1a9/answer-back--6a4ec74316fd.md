### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To reply impertinently to (someone); to talk back.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** I'll thank you not to answer back like that! Where are your manners?

   **Example:** She answered him back rudely.

2. To reply to a question from (someone) at a later time.

   **Example:** Please answer me back before the end of the week.
