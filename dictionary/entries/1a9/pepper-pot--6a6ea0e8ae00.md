### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A small container with a perforated top used for sprinkling ground pepper.

   **Source synonyms:** pepper shaker; peppershaker; pepperette

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A mushroom, Myriostoma coliforme.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A soup made of tripe, vegetables and dumplings, seasoned with pepper.

   **Source synonyms:** Philadelphia pepper pot

<a id="r1-s4"></a>
