### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The state of being entrapped.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The entrapment of the victims in the wreckage made rescue difficult.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Action by law enforcement personnel to lead an otherwise innocent person to commit a crime, in order to arrest and prosecute that person for the crime.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** A detective asking you to buy illegal marijuana for a dying man would be police entrapment.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A method of isolating specific cells or molecules from a mixture, especially by immobilization on a gel.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences
