**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To smoke cigarettes by continuously lighting one from the glowing end of the previous one.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To smoke tobacco cigarettes frequently.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To smoke by continuously lighting one from another.

   **Usage:** transitive
