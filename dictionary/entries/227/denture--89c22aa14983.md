### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A set of teeth, the teeth viewed as a unit.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An artificial replacement of one or more teeth.

   **Example:** After the mouth surgery, she was fitted with a denture.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A complete replacement of all teeth in a mouth.

   **Usage:** in-plural; often
