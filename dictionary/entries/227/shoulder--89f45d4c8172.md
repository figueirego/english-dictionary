### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To push (a person or thing) using one's shoulder.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To bully, manipulate or pull rank on (somebody).

   **Usage:** broadly; transitive

   **Example:** He shouldered me out of my own band.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To put (something) on one's shoulders.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To place (something) against one's shoulders.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To bear a burden, as a financial obligation.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To accept responsibility for.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** shoulder the blame

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

**Source sense R2.S7**

7. To form a shape resembling a shoulder.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Usage:** intransitive
