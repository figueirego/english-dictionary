## Noun

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ɾoˈtonda/ — source labels: Philippines

### Forms

- rotondas — plural
- rotunda — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A roundabout; traffic circle.

   **Usage:** Philippines

   **Example:** Welcome Rotonda is a roundabout located in Quezon City.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A road intersection, not necessarily circular in shape.

   **Usage:** Philippines; broadly

   **Example:** Pasay Rotonda is the intersection of EDSA and Taft Avenue, located in Pasay City.

   **Example:** Pasig Rotonda is the intersection of Pasig Boulevard and Dr. Sixto Antonio Avenue, located in Pasig City.
