**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To touch with the lips or press the lips against, usually to show love or affection or passion, or as part of a greeting.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To (cause to) touch lightly or slightly; to come into contact.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** The nearside of the car just kissed a parked truck as he took the corner at high speed.

   **Example:** His ball kissed the black into the corner pocket.

   **Usage:** reciprocal

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive
