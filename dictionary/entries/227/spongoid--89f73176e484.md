### Senses

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. (historical pathology) Newly formed spongy tissue described in bones affected by rickets.

   **Extractor diagnostics (not usage labels):** no-gloss

**Supplemental definition (evidence reviewed; source gap preserved):** The original noun request is unqualified and has no quotation identifying a competing referent. In the English translation of Guérin’s own memoir, he expressly gives the new tissue the name spongoid and subsequently uses the term substantivally. This establishes a narrow historical pathological sense independently of the adjective sibling. It does not equate spongoid with normal cancellous bone or assert that this nineteenth-century account is a current clinical explanation. The source does not corroborate the original generated plural form, which is preserved as source data rather than newly endorsed.
