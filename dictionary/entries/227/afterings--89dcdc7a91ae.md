### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The last milk that is obtained when milking a cow. Often considered the poorest quality of milk.

   **Usage:** plural; plural-only

   **Example:** All that was left in the cow were the afterings.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The last of any substance that is of a poorer quality than the earlier substance, e.g. the dregs of coffee.

   **Usage:** broadly; plural; plural-only
