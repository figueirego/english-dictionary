### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The date on which something runs out or expires and becomes out of date, in many cases requiring renewal.

   **Usage:** UK

   **Example:** The expiry date of my debit card is the 30th of April.

   **Example:** You'd better throw that milk out&#58; it's passed the expiry date.

   **Source synonyms:** expiry; expiration date; EXP
