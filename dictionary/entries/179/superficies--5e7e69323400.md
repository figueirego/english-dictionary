### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A two-dimensional magnitude that has length and breadth; especially such a surface that forms the boundary of a solid.
   1. The area of a two-dimensional surface.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A two-dimensional magnitude that has length and breadth; especially such a surface that forms the boundary of a solid.
   1. The visible, external surface of a body.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>
