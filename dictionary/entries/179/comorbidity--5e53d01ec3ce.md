### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The presence of one or more disorders (or diseases) in addition to a primary disease or disorder.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Extensive comorbidity is the hallmark of immunodeficiencies.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A disease thus coexisting.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** Coronary artery disease is often a comorbidity of diabetes mellitus.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The effect of such additional disorders or diseases.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences
