### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To dedicate time to an activity.

   **Example:** These days, many people spend time surfing social networking sites.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To accompany someone for leisure; to hang out.

   **Example:** It's my day off today - let's spend some time together. Parents should spend more time with their children.
