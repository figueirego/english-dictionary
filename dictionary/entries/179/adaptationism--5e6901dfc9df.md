## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The view that many traits of organisms are evolved adaptations.

   **Usage:** uncountable; evolutionary theory

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A set of methods for distinguishing traits resulting from adaptation from those arising through other processes.

   **Usage:** uncountable; evolutionary theory

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The belief that all traits are adaptations brought about by natural selection, when some are only functionless by-products (also called "spandrels").

   **Usage:** derogatory; uncountable; evolutionary theory
