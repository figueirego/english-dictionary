### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of a student, to fail a class; to not pass.

   **Usage:** US; ambitransitive

   **Example:** He flunked math, again.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of a teacher, to deny a student a passing grade.

   **Usage:** US; transitive

   **Example:** Unsatisfied with Fred's progress, the teacher flunked him.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To fail any challenge

   **Usage:** US; broadly

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To shirk (a task or duty).

   **Usage:** US; dated; informal

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To back out through fear. (Commonly in the phrase 'flunk it', the 'it' referring to a specific task avoided; sometimes without specific reference, describing a person's attitude to life in general.)

   **Usage:** US
