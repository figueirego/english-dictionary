### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To spill or pour out, because of a leak.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Water leaked out onto the floor.

   **Source synonyms:** [escape](../2cc/escape--b3140286ac71.md)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To be revealed (especially, of information intended to be kept secret).

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

   **Example:** The news of her pregnancy eventually leaked out to the press.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To disclose secret information surreptitiously or anonymously.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive
