**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A placename&#58;
   1. A city in Stirling council area, Scotland, originally in Stirlingshire.
      1. A council area of Scotland, one of 32 created in 1996, with its administrative centre in the city.

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A placename&#58;
   1. A city in Stirling council area, Scotland, originally in Stirlingshire.
      1. A former local government district in Central Region, Scotland from 1975 to 1996, which became the council area.
