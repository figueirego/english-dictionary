### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To unplug or cut power to a machine.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Shut off the machine and pull the plug.

   **Example:** He put an end to the loud music by pulling the plug.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To turn off life support.

   **Usage:** informal; intransitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To commit suicide.

   **Usage:** informal; intransitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To cease to support; to halt.

   **Usage:** informal; intransitive

   **Example:** It's a wonder the director didn't pull the plug on that project months ago.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To cease production or publication.

   **Usage:** informal; intransitive
