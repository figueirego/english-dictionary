### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The bodily and mental deterioration associated with old age&#58; Synonym of senescence.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** second childhood

   **Source antonyms:** juvenescence; juvenility

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The losing of memory and reason due to senescence.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** He was entering his years of senility and not liking it a bit.

   **Source antonyms:** juvenility

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
