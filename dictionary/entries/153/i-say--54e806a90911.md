## Interjection

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An exclamation of surprise or protest.

   **Usage:** UK; dated

   **Example:** I say! That's impressive!

   **Example:** I say, what is the meaning of this?

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An exclamation used to call someone or attract someone's attention.

   **Usage:** UK; dated

   **Example:** I say! ... John ... Are you still there? If you're going into town could you get some eggs?

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used for emphasis, or when resuming after diversion or interruption.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A traditional introduction to a joke, usually thrice repeated.

   **Usage:** dated

   **Example:** I say, I say, I say! Did you hear the one about the nun in a maternity ward?
