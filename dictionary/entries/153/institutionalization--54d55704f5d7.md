### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The process of establishing a practice as a norm.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The government began the institutionalization of background checks for internet users.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The process of committing a person to a facility where their freedom to leave will be restrained, usually a mental hospital.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The institutionalization of Syd Barrett was a major event in the history of the rock group Pink Floyd.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The presence of social deficits or disabilities as a result of a long period spent in a prison, mental hospitals, etc.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** institutional syndrome
