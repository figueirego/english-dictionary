**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A reserve or substitute.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** If the goalkeeper is injured, we have a backup.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A copy of a file or record, stored separately from the original, that can be used to recover the original if it is damaged or destroyed.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** After the power failure, we had to restore the database from backup.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An accumulation of material caused by a (partial) obstruction or (complete) blockage of the flow or movement of the material, or an accumulation of material that causes an overflow due to the flow being greater than the maximum possible flow.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The accident caused a mile-long backup on the highway.

   **Example:** The blockage caused a backup in the plumbing.
