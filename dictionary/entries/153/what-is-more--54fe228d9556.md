## Adverb

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used to introduce a statement which builds on the preceding statement, but expresses a stronger sentiment.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; not-comparable

   **Example:** I've done it wrong, and what's more, I don't care.

   **Source synonyms:** furthermore; moreover; in addition; more to the point
