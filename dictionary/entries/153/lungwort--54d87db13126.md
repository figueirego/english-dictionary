### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of various European plants, of the genus Pulmonaria (family Boraginaceae), that were once used to treat respiratory disorders.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of several other, not closely related plants, used to treat respiratory disorders of species&#58;
   1. Hieracium murorum (in family Asteraceae, French lungwort or golden lungwort)

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of several other, not closely related plants, used to treat respiratory disorders of species&#58;
   1. Helleborus niger (family Ranunculaceae, black lungwort)

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any of several other, not closely related plants, used to treat respiratory disorders of species&#58;
   1. Mertensia (family Boraginaceae)
      1. Mertensia virginica (American lungwort, lungwort oysterleaf, smooth lungwort, tree lungwort)

         **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Any of several other, not closely related plants, used to treat respiratory disorders of species&#58;
   1. Mertensia (family Boraginaceae)
      1. Mertensia maritima (sea lungwort)

         **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Any of several other, not closely related plants, used to treat respiratory disorders of species&#58;
   1. Verbascum thapsus (in family Scrophulariaceae, bullock's lungwort, cow's lungwort or clown's lungwort)

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable
