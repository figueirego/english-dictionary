### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A short period of rest while lying.

   **Usage:** Commonwealth; English; especially

   **Example:** have a lie-down; have a little lie-down; have a wee lie-down

   **Example:** After a long morning working, he decided to have a little lie-down before cooking lunch.
