### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Capable of being perceived very clearly.

   **Example:** Her voice was distinct despite the heavy traffic.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Different from one another (with the preferable adposition being "from").

   **Example:** Horses are distinct from zebras.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Noticeably different from others; distinctive.

   **Example:** Olga's voice is quite distinct because of her accent.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Separate in place; not conjunct or united; with from.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Distinguished; having the difference marked; separated by a visible sign; marked out; specified.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Marked; variegated.

   **Usage:** obsolete
