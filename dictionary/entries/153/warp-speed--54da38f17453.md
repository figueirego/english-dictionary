### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A hypothetical, extremely rapid, speed, resulting from entering a separate dimension, termed hyperspace; much faster than the speed of light.

   **Example:** The ship was accelerating to warp speed, when one of the engines failed.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** literature; media; publishing; science-fiction

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any very fast speed.

   **Usage:** broadly; informal

   **Example:** Wow, you can cycle at warp speed!
