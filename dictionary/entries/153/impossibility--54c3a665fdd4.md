### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Something that is impossible.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Meeting the deadline is an impossibility; there is no way we can be ready in time.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The quality of being impossible.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The state of being unable to do something.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** inability; incapability; helplessness
