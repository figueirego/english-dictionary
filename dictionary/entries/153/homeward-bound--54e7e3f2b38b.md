**Supplemental definition (evidence reviewed; source gap preserved):** The guarded adverb slot is based on a grammatical misclassification. Collins explicitly classifies the whole phrase as an adjective, and Oxford treats bound in this directional use as an adjective modified by homeward. This supports a conservative reading-layer correction of R2 from adverb to adjective.
