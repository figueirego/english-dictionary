### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A mixture consisting of beaten egg whites and either sugar or other flavorings; it is often baked to brown the surface and may be used as a topping or in other ways, most often as a dessert.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The key to a good baked Alaska is the meringue topping.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A shell made of this mixture which serves as the receptacle for fruit, ice cream or sherbet.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Shirley likes to have strawberry with her meringue.
