### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any object like a beaver's tail, oval or tear-drop in plan, usually broad and flat; usually beaver-tail.

   **Usage:** Canada; US

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A fried-dough pastry of such shape; usually beaver tail.

   **Usage:** Canada; US

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A species of prickly pear cactus, Opuntia basilaris.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A gun grip of such shape.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A kind of wetsuit having a flap that passes over the crotch and is fastened at the front.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The tail of a beaver.
