### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To enclose around; to tighten or shrink; to collapse.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** Sometimes it feels like the walls are closing in on me.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To catch up with in a chase; to near the end of a pursuit.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** The police closed in on the suspect.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To near a goal or completion.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I think we are closing in on the end of this project.
