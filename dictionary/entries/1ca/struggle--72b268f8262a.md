### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To strive, to labour in difficulty, to fight (for or against), to contend.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** During the centuries, the people of Ireland struggled constantly to assert their right to govern themselves.

   **Source synonyms:** agonize (literary)

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To have difficulty with something.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** One of the doctor’s patients struggled with depression.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To strive, or to make efforts, with a twisting, or with contortions of the body.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** She struggled to escape from her assailant's grasp.
