### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Either of two people (or, less commonly, animals) who shared the same uterus at the same time; one who was born at the same birth as a sibling.

   **Example:** twin girls, twin daughters, twin sisters, twin boys, twin sons, twin brothers, twin babies, twin children

   **Example:** Katie is Jessica's twin.

   **Example:** The twins are coming over this afternoon.

   **Source synonyms:** twindle; twinling; doublet

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Either of two similar or closely related objects, plans, institutions, people, etc.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** Your idea is the twin of mine.

3. Either of two similar or closely related objects, plans, institutions, people, etc.
   1. Either of two or more things, people, etc. that share a single feature.

## Verb · Etymology 1 · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- enPR: twĭn — variety unspecified in the source labels
- IPA: /twɪn/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Forms

- twins — present; singular; third-person
- twinning — participle; present
- twinned — participle; past
- twinned — past
- twynne — alternative; obsolete

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To separate, divide.

   **Usage:** transitive; obsolete outside Scotland

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To split, part; to go away, depart.

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete outside Scotland

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To join, unite; to form links between (now especially of two places in different countries); to pair with.

   **Usage:** passive; usually

   **Example:** Reading, the English town, is twinned with Clonmel in Ireland.

   **Example:** Coventry twinned with Dresden as an act of peace and reconciliation, both cities having been heavily bombed during the war.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To give birth to twins.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To be, or be like, a twin to (someone else); to match in some way.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To be, or be like, a pair of twins (for example, to dress identically); to be paired or suited.

   **Usage:** intransitive
