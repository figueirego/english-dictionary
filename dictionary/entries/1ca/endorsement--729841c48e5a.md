## Noun

### Pronunciation

### Forms

- endorsements — plural
- indorsement — alternative; archaic

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act or quality of endorsing

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The association announced its endorsement of the policy.

   **Example:** The bank required that cheque endorsement be witnessed by a cashier.

   **Example:** Companies sometimes pay millions for product endorsement by celebrities.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An amendment or annotation to an insurance contract or other official document (such as a driving licence).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Mr. Jones paid extra for the flood damage endorsement on his house insurance.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An instructor's signed acknowledgement of time practising specific flying skills.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Once she obtained the endorsement of her night flying hours, Joanna was approved to take the pilot's examination.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** aeronautics; aerospace; aviation; business; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Permission to carry out a specific skill or application in a field in which the practitioner already has a general licence.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; certification

   **Example:** Wanted&#58; Accredited teacher with Grade 12 mathematics endorsement.

   **Example:** To transport gasoline, truckers must have a valid licence and the hazardous materials endorsement.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** education

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Sponsorship, in means of money, by a company, business or enterprise.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** After the Olympics, he was hoping to get an endorsement deal.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Support from an important, renowned figure of a media (celebrity, politics, sports, etc.), to get back up.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** I'm not sure whether an endorsement from Donald Trump will help or hurt.
