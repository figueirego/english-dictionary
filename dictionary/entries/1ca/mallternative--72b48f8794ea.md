### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any style or genre that is ostensibly counterculture or alternative, but actually mainstream enough to be encountered at a mall, or among the type of people who frequent a mall.

   **Usage:** attributive; derogatory; often; sometimes; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An alternative to a mall, particularly an open-air urban shopping district.

   **Usage:** countable
