### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A marriage of convenience conducted by two persons who do not intend to live as a couple, generally for the purpose of gaining some advantage arising from married status.

   **Source synonyms:** fake marriage; false marriage; fictitious marriage; mock marriage; paper marriage
