## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The active, hands-on process of exploration and discovery through which individuals (especially children) construct their own understanding of the natural world.

   **Usage:** uncountable; early childhood education

   **Example:** In a constructivist classroom, sciencing is valued over the mere memorization of facts.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The act of applying the scientific method or technical skills to solve a problem, often in a creative or improvisational manner.

   **Usage:** informal; uncountable

   **Example:** We're going to have to do some serious sciencing to get this rover moving again.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The systematic classification or study of a particular subject.

   **Usage:** rare; uncountable
