### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A trademark, or a distinctive name for a brand, or for a range of products by a single manufacturer.

   **Example:** The company that invented the category remains a name brand to this day.

   **Source antonyms:** off brand; store brand

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A product having such a mark or name.

   **Example:** When it comes to laundry detergent, she always buys a name brand, just to be safe.

   **Source antonyms:** off brand; store brand
