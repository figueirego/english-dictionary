### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person with a reputation of importance or power.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; informal

   **Example:** They stopped traffic so some big shot and his entourage could have the whole road to themselves.

   **Source synonyms:** bigwig; baron; big cheese; big enchilada; big gun; big kahuna; big man; big name; big noise; big shot; bigwig; big wheel; boss; grand poobah; head honcho; heavy hitter; heavyweight; high muckamuck; high muckety-muck; king of all one surveys; king of the castle; king of the hill; kingpin; macher (US); mayor; muckamuck; muckety muck; mugwump (masculine; usually); numero uno; panjandrum; poobah; rooster; somebody; top banana; top boy; top cat; top dog; top gun; very important person; VIP; wheel

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A rare and significant chance to achieve fame or success.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; informal

   **Example:** her one big shot at the brass ring

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see big, shot.
