### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A written work edited and published, as by a certain editor or in a certain manner, or at a certain time.

   **Example:** She wanted a copy of the Clericotes edition, but had to settle for the 1921 edition. She had never liked abridged editions.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** media; publishing

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The whole number of copies of a work printed and published at one time.

   **Example:** The first edition was soon sold.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An instance of the first or second sense.

   **Example:** What he had found was a particularly valuable first edition.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A particular instance of an event.

   **Example:** The 2014 edition of the Tour de France started in Leeds, Yorkshire.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports
