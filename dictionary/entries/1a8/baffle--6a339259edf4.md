## Noun · Etymology 1 · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈbæf(ə)l/ — source labels: General-American

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A device used to dampen, muffle, restrain, or otherwise control the movement of a fluid, or waves travelling through a fluid such as light or sound; specifically, a surface positioned inside an open area to inhibit direct motion from one place to another without preventing motion altogether.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Tanker trucks use baffles to keep the liquids inside from sloshing around.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; technology

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. A lever for operating the throttle valve of a winding engine.

   **Usage:** US; countable; dialectal; uncountable; coal mining

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; technology

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. A device to prevent the spreading of sound or light.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** sound baffle

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; technology

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. A barrier designed to obstruct and confuse enemies, rendering them vulnerable.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. An argument or objection based on an ambiguity of wording or similar trivial circumstance; a minor complaint; a quibble.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete
