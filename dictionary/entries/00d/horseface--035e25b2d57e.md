## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The face of a horse.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A face (of a human or other non-horse animal) that is long and ugly, with coarse features, thus having a resemblance or fancied resemblance to the face of a horse.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Someone who is unattractive because they have a horseface.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Source synonyms:** dogface
