### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The outside boundary, parts or surface of something.

   **Example:** The suburbs are a city's periphery.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A first-rank administrative division of Greece, subdivided into provinces.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
