### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To receive (property, a title, etc.), by legal succession or bequest, usually after the previous owner's death.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** After Grandad died, I inherited the house.

   **Source antonyms:** bequeath; leave

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To receive (property, a title, etc.), by legal succession or bequest, usually after the previous owner's death.
   1. To come into an inheritance.

      **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

      **Example:** Lucky old Daniel – his grandfather died rich, and he's inherited.

      **Source antonyms:** bequeath; leave

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To take possession of as a right (especially in Biblical translations).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Your descendants will inherit the earth.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To receive a characteristic from one's ancestors by genetic transmission.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Let's hope the baby inherits his mother's looks and his father's intelligence.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To derive from people or conditions previously in force.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** This country has inherited an invidious class culture.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To derive (existing functionality) from a superclass.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** ModalWindow inherits all the properties and methods of Window.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; programming; sciences

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To derive a new class from (a superclass).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; programming; sciences

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To put in possession of.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive
