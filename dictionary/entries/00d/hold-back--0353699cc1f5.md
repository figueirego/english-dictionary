### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To act with reserve; to contain one's full measure or power.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** Don't hold back. Hit it as hard as you can.

   **Source synonyms:** pull punches

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To contain; stop.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** The dam can't hold back that much water.

   **Example:** I was swallowing hard to hold back the tears.

   **Example:** His shyness holds him back from making friends.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To delay the progress of, especially in school.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** He's a year older than his classmates, because he was held back in second grade.
