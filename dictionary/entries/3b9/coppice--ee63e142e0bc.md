### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To manage (a wooded area) sustainably, as a coppice, by periodically cutting back woody plants to promote new growth.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Her plan to coppice the woods should keep her self-sufficient in fuel indefinitely.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To sprout from the stump.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Few conifer species can coppice.
