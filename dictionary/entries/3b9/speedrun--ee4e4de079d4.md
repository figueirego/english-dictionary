**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A playthrough of a computer or video game in which the whole game or a select part of it, such as a single level, is played with the intent of completing it as quickly as possible, optionally with certain prerequisites.

   **Example:** In the speedrun of Super Mario Bros. 3, Anonymous Jones finishes the whole game in just 11 minutes.
