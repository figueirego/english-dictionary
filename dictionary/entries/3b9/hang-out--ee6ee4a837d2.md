### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To spend time with somebody; to regularly meet with somebody.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive; often; slang

   **Example:** After the film, do you want to go hang out?

   **Example:** He hung out with his friends all day yesterday.

   **Source context (verbatim):** with with

   **Source synonyms:** socialize; hang with; roll with

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To lodge or reside; to exist.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive; slang

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To be unyielding; to hold out.

   **Usage:** dated; informal

   **Example:** The juryman hangs out against an agreement.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To crave or desperately want (something).

   **Usage:** informal

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To endure or delay.

   **Usage:** informal

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see hang, out.

   **Example:** When a dog pants, its tongue often hangs out of its mouth.
