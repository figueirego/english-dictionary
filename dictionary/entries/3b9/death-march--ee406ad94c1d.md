## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A forced movement of people, on foot, in such circumstances that many die during the journey.

   **Example:** the Bataan Death March

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A project that requires a stretch of unsustainable overwork, or whose participants feel it is destined to fail.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** management

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
