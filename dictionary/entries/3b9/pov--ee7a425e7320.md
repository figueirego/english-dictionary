1. Initialism of point of view.
   1. The (cognitive, mental, or emotional) viewpoint of a certain person; (attributively) from that viewpoint.

      **Source synonyms:** viewpoint

      **Source antonyms:** NPOV; point-of-viewlessness; eticness

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Initialism of point of view.
   1. The (cognitive, mental, or emotional) viewpoint of a certain person; (attributively) from that viewpoint.
      1. A discourse marker signalling "imagine, if you will, being in the situation that this text, photo, or video depicts", even in cases where third-person viewpoint exists; compare also fourth wall joke.

         **Usage:** broadly

         **Source synonyms:** viewpoint

         **Source antonyms:** NPOV; point-of-viewlessness; eticness
