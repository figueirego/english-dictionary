### Senses

<a id="r3-s1"></a>

**Source sense R3.S1**

1. A mechanical device that supports another part or reduces friction.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; mechanical; mechanical-engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r3-s2"></a>

**Source sense R3.S2**

2. The horizontal angle between the direction of an object and another object, or between it and that of true north; a heading or direction.

   **Usage:** navigation

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r3-s3"></a>

**Source sense R3.S3**

3. One's understanding of one's orientation or relative position, literally or figuratively.

   **Usage:** especially; in-plural

   **Example:** Do we go left here or straight on? Hold on, let me just get my bearings.

   **Example:** I started a new job last week, and I still haven't quite found my bearings.

   **Source context (verbatim):** in phrases such as 'get one's bearings'

<a id="r3-s4"></a>

**Source sense R3.S4**

4. Relevance; a relationship or connection.

   **Example:** That has no bearing on this issue.

<a id="r3-s5"></a>

**Source sense R3.S5**

5. One's posture, demeanor, or manner.

   **Example:** She walks with a confident, self-assured bearing.

   **Source synonyms:** presence

**Source sense R3.S6**

6. That part of any member of a building which rests upon its supports.
