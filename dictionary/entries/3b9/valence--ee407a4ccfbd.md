### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The combining capacity of an atom, functional group, or radical determined by the number of atoms of hydrogen with which it will unite, or the number of electrons that it will gain, lose, or share when it combines with other atoms, etc.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

   **Source synonyms:** atomicity (historical); quantivalence (historical); quantivalency (historical); valency

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The number of binding sites of a molecule, such as an antibody or antigen.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The number of arguments that a verb can have, including its subject, ranging from zero to three or, less commonly, four.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** In this assignment you will analyze each of the following sentences and determine the valence of the highlighted verb.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** valency

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A one-dimensional value assigned by a person to an object, situation, or state, that can usually be positive (causing a feeling of attraction) or negative (repulsion).

   **Usage:** especially; uncountable

   **Example:** anger and fear have negative valence

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; psychology; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The value which a person places on something.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; sciences; social-science; sociology

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. For a correspondence T on a curve&#58; a number k such that the divisors T(P)+kP are all linearly equivalent.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** algebraic-geometry; geometry; mathematics; sciences
