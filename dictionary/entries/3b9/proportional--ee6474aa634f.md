**Source sense R1.S1**

1. At a constant ratio (to). Two magnitudes (numbers) are said to be proportional if the second varies in a direct relation arithmetically to the first. Symbol&#58; ∝.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** commensurate
