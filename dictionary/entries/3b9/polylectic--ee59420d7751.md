### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of or relating to an insect (especially a bee) which collects pollen from the flowers of a variety of unrelated plants.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; entomology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of or relating to a term consisting of more than one word.

   **Example:** A polylectic term is a multi-word term, as opposed to a monolectic term which is a one-word term.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** grammar; human-sciences; linguistics; sciences
