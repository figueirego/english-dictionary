**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Example:** “Happy” is a synonym of “glad”.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences; semantics

   **Source synonyms:** poecilonym; near-synonym; parasynonym; plesionym

2. Any of the various names that could in theory be used for a taxon in accordance with the relevant rules.
   1. The subset of those names other than the one to be used according to the rules.

      **Usage:** informal

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences; taxonomy

**Source sense R1.S3**

      **Usage:** broadly
