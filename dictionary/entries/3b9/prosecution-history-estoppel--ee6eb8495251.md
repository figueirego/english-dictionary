## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In United States patent law, the circumstance under which a person who has filed a patent application, and then made amendments to the application to accommodate the patent law and secure issuance of the patent, is prevented from bringing a cause of action for infringement of the patent where the allegedly infringing acts falling within the matter disclaimed by the amendment.

   **Usage:** uncountable
