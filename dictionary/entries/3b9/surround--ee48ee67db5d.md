### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To encircle something or simultaneously extend in all directions.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** bound

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To enclose or confine something on all sides so as to prevent escape.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The lions surrounded the deer herd so they had no way to escape.

   **Example:** They surrounded each other in the classroom and started trading hits.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To pass around; to travel about; to circumnavigate.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive
