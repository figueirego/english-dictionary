### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The practice of maintaining, in a society, a single culture, suppressing the (sub)cultures of e.g. minority groups and excluding external influences.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The creation of a monoculture in agriculture.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
