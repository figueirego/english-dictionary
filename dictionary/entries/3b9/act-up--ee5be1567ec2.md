### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To misbehave; to cause trouble.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** My allergies act up during hay fever season.

   **Example:** Children may act up in class in an effort to get attention.

   **Example:** My car started acting up after I drove through that huge puddle.

   **Source synonyms:** carry on; act out; play up

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To perform the duties of an appointment senior to that actually held; to deputise for a superior.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** Acting up is always recommended when you find yourself under a new boss.
