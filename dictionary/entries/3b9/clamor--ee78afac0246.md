### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To cry out or demand.

   **Usage:** US; intransitive

   **Example:** Anyone who tastes our food seems to clamor for more.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To demand by outcry.

   **Usage:** US; transitive

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To become noisy insistently.

   **Usage:** US; intransitive

   **Example:** After a confused murmur the audience soon clamored

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To influence by outcry.

   **Usage:** US; transitive

   **Example:** His many supporters successfully clamor his election without a formal vote

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To silence.

   **Usage:** US; obsolete; transitive
