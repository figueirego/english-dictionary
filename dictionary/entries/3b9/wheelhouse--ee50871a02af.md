### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A building or other structure containing a (large) wheel, such as the water wheel of a mill.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A building or other structure containing a (large) wheel, such as the water wheel of a mill.
   1. The partially enclosed structure above and around a wheel of an automobile, typically partly formed by a portion of a fender panel that has been extended outward beyond the plane of the rest of the panel.

      **Source synonyms:** wheel arch; wheel well

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A building or other structure containing a (large) wheel, such as the water wheel of a mill.
   1. An enclosed compartment on the deck of a vessel such as a fishing boat, originally housing its helm or steering wheel, from which it may be navigated; on a larger vessel it is the bridge.

      **Source synonyms:** pilothouse

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A building or other structure containing a (large) wheel, such as the water wheel of a mill.
   1. The enclosed structure around the paddlewheel of a steamboat.

      **Source synonyms:** paddle box

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A prehistoric structure from the Iron Age found in Scotland, characteristically including an outer wall within which a circle of stone piers (resembling the spokes of a wheel) form the basis for lintel arches supporting corbelled roofing with a hearth at the hub.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** archaeology; history; human-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A pitch location which is favourable to the hitter.

   **Usage:** Canada; US; broadly

   **Example:** The pitch was right in his wheelhouse, and he hit a grand slam.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; baseball; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

   **Source context (verbatim):** from sense 1.2

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A person's area of authority or expertise.

   **Usage:** Canada; US; figuratively

   **Example:** Horse viruses are in Pat’s wheelhouse.

   **Source synonyms:** bailiwick; domain

   **Usage:** Canada; US; figuratively
