## Adjective

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˌʌnˈsʌŋ/ — source labels: UK; US

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Which has not been lauded or appreciated.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The backstage crew of the movie were the unsung heroes.

   **Source synonyms:** unhonored; unglorified; unreputed; fameless (obsolete); gloriless; inglorious (archaic); nameless; nonfamous; obscure; renownless (archaic); unfamous; unhonored; unglorified; ungloriful; unglorious; unnoticed; unnoted; unreputed; unrenowned; unsung

   **Source antonyms:** sung; bait (Multicultural-London-English); fabled; famed; famous; famoused (obsolete); iconic; irrecondite; known; legendary; mere (obsolete); notable; noted; noteworthy; proverbial; recognizable; renowned; storied; well-known

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Not sung.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The third hymn remained unsung.

   **Source synonyms:** omitted

   **Source antonyms:** sung
