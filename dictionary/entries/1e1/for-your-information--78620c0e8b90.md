## Prep\_phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used to introduce a rebuttal to the interlocutor's previous statement, especially when the statement angered or offended the speaker.

   **Usage:** colloquial

   **Example:** For your information, my son was a straight A student in college, so he's not just some schmo!

   **Source synonyms:** I'll have you know that…

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used as a satiric preface to highlight information that an interlocutor should already know or should be able to surmise.

   **Usage:** colloquial

   **Example:** For your information, you're reading English in Wiktionary.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see for, your, information.

   **Example:** I kept the dates and times in the logbook for your information.
