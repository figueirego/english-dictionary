**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see look, down.

   **Example:** look down someone's shirt

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
