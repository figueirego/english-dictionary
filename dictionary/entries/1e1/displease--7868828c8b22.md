### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make not pleased; to cause a feeling of disapprobation or dislike in; to be disagreeable to; to vex slightly.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The boy's rudeness displeased me.

   **Example:** I felt displeased with the boy.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To give displeasure or offense.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To fail to satisfy; to miss of.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive
