### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A women's hairstyle, associated with lower social classes, in which the hair is pulled tightly back from the face and fastened behind the head, thus pulling the features up and back and giving an effect similar to a facelift.

   **Usage:** British; derogatory; slang
