### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To create the rough shape of (something, such as a machined part or a sculpture); to do the first-fix setup of (something, such as plumbing or wiring).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The first step in creating the statue was roughing it out from a large block of stone.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To produce (a plan, diagram etc.) in rough.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive
