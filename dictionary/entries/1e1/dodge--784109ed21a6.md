### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To avoid (something) by moving suddenly out of the way.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** He dodged traffic crossing the street.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To avoid; to sidestep.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** The politician dodged the question with a meaningless reply.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To elude.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To go, or cause to go, hither and thither.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive; archaic

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To make an area of an image lighter (when processing photographs in a darkroom, this is accomplished by decreasing the exposure of that area to light).

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** arts; broadcasting; film; hobbies; lifestyle; media; photography; television; videography

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To follow by dodging, or suddenly shifting from place to place.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To trick somebody.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive; dated
