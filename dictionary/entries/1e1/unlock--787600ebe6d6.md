### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To undo or open a lock or something locked by, for example, turning a key, or selecting a combination.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I unlocked the door and walked in.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To obtain access to something; to meet the requirements of a security or protection system.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I unlocked the dictionary article so I could edit it.

   **Example:** This computer game is shareware, but you can pay for a code to unlock the full version.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To configure (a mobile phone) so that it is not bound to any particular carrier.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** communications; electrical-engineering; engineering; mobile-telephony; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; telecommunications; telephony

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To disclose or reveal previously unknown knowledge or potential.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The discovery of a clue unlocked the mystery.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To be or become unfastened or unrestrained.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To make available.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To undermine something that has control over a situation; to find a way to counter or oppose.

   **Usage:** transitive
