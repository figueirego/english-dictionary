### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A geographic region in Southeastern Europe in the Balkans which includes the Republic of North Macedonia, the region of Macedonia in Greece, the Pirin region of Bulgaria, and small parts of Albania and Serbia.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An ancient Greek kingdom in Southeastern Europe in the Balkans, located to the north of Thessaly, comprising the Greek city of Thessaloniki and its surroundings.

   **Usage:** historical

   **Source synonyms:** Macedon

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Former name of North Macedonia&#58; a country in Southeastern Europe in the Balkans.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A geographic region and former administrative region of Greece, comprising the regions of West Macedonia, Central Macedonia and the East Macedonia part of the region of East Macedonia and Thrace.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A geographic region in southwestern Bulgaria; in full, Pirin Macedonia or Bulgarian Macedonia.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A former constituent republic of Yugoslavia.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A former province of the Roman Empire.

   **Usage:** historical
