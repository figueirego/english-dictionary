### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To form a circle around; to encircle.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To include within its scope; to circumscribe or go round so as to surround; to enclose; to contain.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** embrace

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To include completely; to describe fully or comprehensively.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** This book on English grammar encompasses all irregular verbs.

   **Source synonyms:** comprehend (archaic)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To go around, especially, to circumnavigate.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Drake encompassed the globe.
