<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A policy of abstaining or of the desirability of withholding one's vote or other participation with regard to some aspect of the political process.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Abstentionism is a manifestation of political independence.
