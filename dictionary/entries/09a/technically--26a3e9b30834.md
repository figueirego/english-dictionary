### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Based on precise facts, which, however, may be contrary to common belief or casual terminology.

   **Example:** Technically, he was Canadian, but everyone assumed he was American.

   **Example:** Technically, a guinea pig is not a pig, nor is it from Guinea.

   **Source synonyms:** stricto sensu

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Based on formal (de jure) arguments, which, however, may be misleading.

   **Example:** technically correct

   **Example:** Due to an omission in the peace treaty of the Russo-Japanese War, Montenegro was technically at war with Japan for 102 years.

   **Example:** Stalin was not technically the Head of State.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. In terms of technique.

   **Example:** Although she is technically gifted, her piano playing lacks passion.

   **Source synonyms:** techniquewise

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. In a technical (involving specialized knowledge) way.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. According to the current state of technology.

   **Example:** For now, it is technically impossible to have a manned flight to Mercury.

   **Source synonyms:** technologically
