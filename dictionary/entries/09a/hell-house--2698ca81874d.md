### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A haunted house attraction, typically run by a religious group, depicting the torments of the damned in hell.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An entertainment, at a fair etc, in which visitors pay to be frightened by gruesome exhibits etc.

   **Source synonyms:** haunted house; house of horrors
