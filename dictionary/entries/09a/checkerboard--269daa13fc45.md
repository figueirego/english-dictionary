**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A square board divided into sixty-four equal squares arranged in eight rows and eight columns, with adjacent squares alternating in color, typically black and white, used to play chess, checkers or draughts.

   **Usage:** board games

   **Source synonyms:** draughtboard
