### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To restrain by force, especially by law or authority; to repress; to curb.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To use force, threat, fraud, or intimidation in an attempt to compel one to act against their will.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** They coerced their children into going to the country park.

   **Source synonyms:** hustle

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To force an attribute, normally of a data type, to take on the attribute of another data type.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences
