## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An unintended interruption in a broadcast during which nothing is transmitted&#58; a silent period on radio or a silent period with no picture on television.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; uncountable

   **Example:** They fired the production engineer because too many moments of dead air were interrupting the broadcast.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** broadcasting; media

   **Source synonyms:** tumbleweed moment

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A quiet or awkward moment in a conversation where neither individual is speaking.

   **Usage:** broadly; idiomatic; uncountable

   **Example:** Today we're going to try sitting with silence a bit. Don't worry about any moments of dead air in the conversation.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Air that is subject to little or no ventilation, thus, little to no air exchange.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Sealing up the house so perfectly, without planning for any active ventilation to replace the passive ventilation that you have eliminated, is not a good idea, because it encourages pockets of dead air. If you're going to seal it up so tight, then you need to plan for active ventilation through a filter, rather than no ventilation at all.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; mechanical; mechanical-engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences
