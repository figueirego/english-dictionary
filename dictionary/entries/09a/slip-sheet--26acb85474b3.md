### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Sheet material, such as reinforced kraft paper, rosin-sized paper, polyester scrim or polyethylene sheeting, placed between two components of a roof assembly to ensure that no adhesion occurs between them and to prevent possible damage from chemical incompatibility, wearing or abrasion of the membrane.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; construction; manufacturing; roofing

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A strong, thin pallet-sized sheet of plastic or fiberboard used to transport heavy items.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** economics; sciences; shipping; transport

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
