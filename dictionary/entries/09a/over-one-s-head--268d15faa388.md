## Adjective

### Forms

- over one’s head — canonical

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. More complex or confusing than one can understand; beyond one’s comprehension.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** This is way over my head. Can you explain it more simply?

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. More than one can handle; too much (especially in over one's head).

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I’m in over my head on this project. Can you help?

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Performing at a level greatly superior to one's usual level of performance.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** He just shot a spectacularly over his head round of golf and beat all of us.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Directed to someone with authority over the person, so as to avoid requiring the approval of the person himself or herself.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see over, head.
