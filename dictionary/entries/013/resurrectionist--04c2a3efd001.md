## Noun

### Forms

- resurrectionists — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who sells cadavers to anatomists, surgeons, etc., especially by exhuming corpses from graves; a graverobber.

   **Usage:** euphemistic; historical

   **Source synonyms:** bodysnatcher; resurrectioner; resurrection man

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A believer in a future bodily resurrection.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** lifestyle; religion

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. One who resurrects an abandoned idea, practice, etc.; a revivalist.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. One who sells repaired or reconditioned goods; a refurbisher.

   **Usage:** humorous; obsolete

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A racehorse that (once or numerously) suddenly recovers its stamina midrace.

   **Usage:** humorous; obsolete

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** equestrianism; hobbies; horses; lifestyle; pets; sports
