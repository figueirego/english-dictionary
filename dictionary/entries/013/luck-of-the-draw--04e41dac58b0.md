## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The random production or occurrence of favorable or unfavorable results.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; uncountable

   **Example:** She got a big winner \[ticket for a large prize\]. It was the luck of the draw!

   **Example:** She got a real dud. Well, that's the luck of the draw for you.

   **Source synonyms:** chance; happenstance; fortuity
