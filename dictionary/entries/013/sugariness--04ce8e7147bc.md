## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The property of being sugary or sweet (either literally or figuratively).
   1. The amount or degree of containing sugar (saccharides).

      **Usage:** uncountable

      **Example:** The sugariness of the sauce went well with the tartness of the citrus fruit.

      **Source antonyms:** tartness; sourness; bitterness

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The property of being sugary or sweet (either literally or figuratively).
   1. The amount or degree of some emotional trait such as complaisance, politeness, or kindness.

      **Usage:** figuratively; uncountable

      **Example:** The sugariness of her reply was ambiguous&#58; was it placation, or a veiled dismissal, or merely warm hospitality?

      **Source synonyms:** sweetness; saccharinity

      **Source antonyms:** tartness; sourness; bitterness; frostiness; dourness
