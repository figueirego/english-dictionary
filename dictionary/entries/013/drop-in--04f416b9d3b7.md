### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see drop, in.

   **Example:** The keys were dropped in the fishbowl.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To arrive casually and unannounced, with little or no warning; also, to visit without an appointment.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I was in the garden covered with mud when my grandmother dropped in for a visit.

   **Source synonyms:** drop by; pop in; stop by; stop in

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To paddle into and take off on a wave another surfer is already riding.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports; surfing

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To fall in; to cave in.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To install components, particularly car engines.

   **Usage:** US; transitive

   **Example:** We dropped in all-new parts in the sports car.
