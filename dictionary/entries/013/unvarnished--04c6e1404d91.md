## Adjective

### Forms

- unvarnisht — alternative; archaic

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Not having been coated with varnish.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** unvarnished furniture

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Natural, unmodified, unembellished, not exaggerated.

   **Usage:** broadly; figuratively; not-comparable

   **Example:** She told the unvarnished truth.

   **Source synonyms:** plain; simple; austere; bare; chaste; inornate; modest; plain; quiet; severe; simple; spartan; unadorned; unembellished; unextravagant; unornamented; undecked; ungarnished; untrimmed; unvarnished
