### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To take up an issue with another person's boss or other superior rather than beginning or continuing to deal with the original person.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** She went over his head and took her complaint directly to the president of the company.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To escape someone's comprehension.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I listened carefully, but the technical jargon went over my head.
