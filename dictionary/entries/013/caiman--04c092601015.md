### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of the relatively small crocodilians of the genus Caiman, within the family Alligatoridae.

   **Usage:** strictly

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A crocodilian of the subfamily Caimaninae, which includes the very large black caiman.

   **Usage:** broadly

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A semi-aquatic lizard, of the genus Dracaena, found in South America. To differentiate from crocodilian caimans, they are referred to as caiman lizards.
