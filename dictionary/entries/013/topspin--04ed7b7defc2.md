### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A rotational motion, especially that given to a ball, in which the upper surface spins in the direction of motion.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The tennis star was famed for her topspin.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An edgy, quirky or otherwise engaging quality that holds the interest of the audience.

   **Usage:** uncountable
