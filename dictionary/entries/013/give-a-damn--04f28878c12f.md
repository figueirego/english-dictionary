### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To be concerned about, have an interest in, to care (about something).

   **Usage:** idiomatic; informal; sometimes; vulgar

   **Example:** He doesn't give a damn about your child's painting, he's just interested in the gold frame.

   **Example:** If she actually gave a damn what the law said, she wouldn't have stolen the car in the first place, now would she?

   **Source context (verbatim):** in the negative

   **Source synonyms:** becare; bother; care; concern oneself; heed (archaic); mind; reck (archaic); trouble oneself; worry
