### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The aesthetic and culture of the early World Wide Web (roughly 1990s-early 2000s); used to describe an early era of the Internet, often used in relation with Web 1.0.

   **Usage:** Internet; uncountable; aesthetic

   **Example:** In contrast with modern sleek, corporate websites, the Neocities platform allows users to create personal webpages reminiscent of the Old Web featuring tiled backgrounds, animated GIFs, guestbooks, and simple HTML.

   **Source synonyms:** webcore
