### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make (an animal or person) unconscious or cause (an animal or person) to die by preventing breathing; to smother, to suffocate.

   **Usage:** also; figuratively; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** asphyxiate; burke; scomfish; strangle; suffocate

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To cause (someone) difficulty in breathing, or a choking or gagging feeling.

   **Usage:** excessive; transitive

   **Example:** The heat was stifling the children.

   **Source synonyms:** choke; gag; strangle; throttle

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To prevent (a breath, cough, or cry, or the voice, etc.) from being released from the throat.

   **Usage:** also; figuratively; transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To make (something) unable to be heard by blocking it with some medium.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** gag; muffle; bequiet; bestill; damp; dampen; dull; gag (figuratively); hush; hush down (informal); hush up (informal); muffle; mute; muzzle (figuratively); quiet; quiet down; quieten; quieten down; silence; stifle; still

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To keep in, hold back, or repress (something).

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** The army stifled the rebellion.

   **Source synonyms:** hinder; restrain; smother; suppress; throttle; impede; behedge; guard; govern (formal); smother; bind (figuratively); chasten; choke back; check; inhibit; catch (reflexive); contain; vanquish; cork (figuratively); curb; quench; quell; bury; curtail; dampen; hamshackle (figuratively); hold back (idiomatic); hold down (idiomatic); limit; repress; restrain; restrict; shackle (figuratively); stay; stifle; stop; straiten; subdue; suppress; throttle (figuratively); underbring; withhold; withstrain (archaic)

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To prevent (something) from being revealed; to conceal, to hide, to suppress.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** cover up; hide away; occult; secrete; veil

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To treat (a silkworm cocoon) with steam as part of the process of silk production.

   **Usage:** transitive; agriculture (sericulture)

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To die of suffocation.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Two firemen tragically stifled in yesterday’s fire when trying to rescue an old lady from her bedroom.

   **Source synonyms:** asphyxiate; choke; scomfish; suffocate
