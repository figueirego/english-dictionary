### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An organism that feeds chiefly on animals; an animal that feeds on meat as the main part of its diet.

   **Example:** As juveniles the crocodiles are frequently predated by larger carnivores.

   **Source synonyms:** meateater; zoophage

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A mammal belonging to the order Carnivora.

   **Example:** The panda and the panther are both carnivores.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences; zoology

   **Source synonyms:** carnivoran

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A person who is not a vegetarian.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Source synonyms:** meateater; meatarian; meatatarian (humorous; often); kreophagist (archaic); nonvegetarian; bloodmouth (derogatory)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A follower of the carnivore diet.

   **Usage:** informal
