   1. Anything V-shaped similarly to the classic chevron shape.

      **Usage:** broadly

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A group of birds in flight, when flying in a V formation, as in cranes, ducks, geese, gulls, pelicans and swans.

   **Usage:** collective

   **Source synonyms:** wedge

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. One of the V-shaped markings on the surface of roads used to indicate minimum distances between vehicles.

   **Usage:** UK

5. A wedge-shaped sediment deposit observed on coastlines and continental interiors around the world.
