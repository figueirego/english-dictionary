### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To apportion out, distribute.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To manage or supervise the conduct, performance or execution of; to govern or regulate the parameters for the conduct, performance or execution of; to work in an administrative capacity.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To minister (to).

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** administering to the sick

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To settle, as the estate of one who dies without a will, or whose will fails of an executor.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To give, as an oath.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To give (a drug, to a patient), be it orally or by any other means.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** We administered the medicine to our dog by mixing it in his food.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To cause (a patient, human or animal) to ingest (a drug), either by openly offering or through deceit.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences
