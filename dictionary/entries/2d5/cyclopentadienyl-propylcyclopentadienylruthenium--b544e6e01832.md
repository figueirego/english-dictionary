## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A ruthenium-based organometallic compound used in chemical vapor deposition to form thin ruthenium films. It has a Ru center bound to two cyclopentadienyl ligands, one carrying a propyl group, and is valued for efficient deposition rates but can struggle to form uniform films on oxide surfaces.

   **Usage:** uncountable
