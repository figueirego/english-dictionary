### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A curtain, especially one used in some Hindu or Muslim traditions to conceal women from the gaze of people, particularly men and strangers.

   **Usage:** Bangladesh; India; Myanmar; Nepal; Pakistan; South-Asia; also; countable; figuratively

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A long veil or other attire covering most of the body, worn by women in some Muslim societies.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The situation or system of secluding women from the gaze of people, particularly men and strangers, in some Muslim and Hindu traditions, by using a curtain or screen, and/or wearing a face veil or attire covering most of the body.

   **Usage:** broadly; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Keeping apart; isolation, seclusion; also, concealment, secrecy.

   **Usage:** broadly; figuratively; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The period between the announcement of an election or referendum and its conclusion, during which civil servants refrain from making policy announcements or taking actions that could be seen as advantageous to certain candidates in the election.

   **Usage:** UK; broadly; countable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; politics

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A striped cotton cloth which is used to make curtains.

   **Usage:** broadly; obsolete; rare; uncountable
