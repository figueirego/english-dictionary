## Prep\_phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In a state of misunderstanding or inadvertent poor relationship with someone.

   **Usage:** especially

   **Example:** My relationship with my neighbours started off on the wrong foot, but we're very close now.

   **Source context (verbatim):** in expressions such as 'start off on the wrong foot'

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In a disadvantaged situation, or following a mistaken course of action.

   **Example:** In this legal battle we were on the wrong foot from the start.

**Source antonyms:** on the right foot
