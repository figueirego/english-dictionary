### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To formally announce one's arrival at a location or event (usually a hotel) to a proprietor or employee of that location or event in order to secure admission, accommodations, or other services.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To seek to verify (a claim or some data).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Can you check into his story? See if you can disprove it.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To announce on social media that one has come to a location.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** She was downtown earlier. I saw that she checked into the Starbucks on Main Street.
