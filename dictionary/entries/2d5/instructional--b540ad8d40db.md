### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Intended for purposes of instruction, for teaching.

   **Example:** The manual might have been instructional had anybody actually taken the time to read it.

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Example:** There are 33 instructional resignations tonight, double the amount at this time last year.
