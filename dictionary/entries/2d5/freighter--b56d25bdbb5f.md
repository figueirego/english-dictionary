### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who loads a ship.

   **Source synonyms:** lader; docker; dockworker; stevedore; longshoreman; loader

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One who charters and loads a ship.

   **Source synonyms:** charterer

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. One employed in receiving and forwarding freight.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. One for whom freight is transported.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A ship used mainly to carry freight.

   **Example:** oil freighter

   **Example:** bulk freighter
