### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A bullet that is shot by an unseen assailant or one that cannot be seen or heard when it is shot.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; government; military; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; politics; tools; war; weaponry

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A bullet that cannot be found or that is untraceable through ballistic testing.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ballistics; engineering; government; military; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics; politics; tools; war; weaponry

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An imaginary bullet.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A supernatural bullet that wounds or kills a person, especially one that is shot by a supernatural assailant.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** arts; fantasy; folklore; history; human-sciences; literature; media; publishing; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A bullet that can hurt a ghost or undead creature.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** fantasy

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Something nonphysical (such as words, actions, or memory) that causes physical or emotional damage.
