**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A synthetic, non-approved research compound belonging to the substituted phenethylamine/amphetamine-related class. It has been investigated in preclinical studies as a monoaminergic activity enhancer affecting neurotransmitter systems such as dopamine and norepinephrine. It has no approved medical use and very limited or no human clinical research data.
