## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An official scoring judgement in which a runner is not credited with a stolen base because the team playing the field did not try to stop the steal. This typically occurs late in a game when the fielding team is winning by at least two runs.

   **Example:** The play was scored as defensive indifference.
