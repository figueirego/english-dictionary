### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of various instruments designed to determine the color of something, by comparison with standard colors or by spectroscopy.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An analytic instrument that estimates the concentration of a substance in a sample by measuring its color against the solution's complementary color.

   **Usage:** analytical chemistry
