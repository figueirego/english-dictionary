**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A department, service or location where items that are found can be claimed by their owners.

   **Usage:** Australia; Canada; New-Zealand; Philippines; US; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Well, if you can't find your glasses, then go to lost and found and ask if anyone turned them in.
