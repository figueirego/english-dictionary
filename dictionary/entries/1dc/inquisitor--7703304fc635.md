### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who inquires, especially searchingly or ruthlessly.

   **Example:** During the meeting, Jake turned into an inquisitor, firing questions at the presenter non-stop.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An official of the ecclesiastical court of the Inquisition.

   **Usage:** historical
