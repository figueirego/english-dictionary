### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of observing, and the fact of being observed (see observance)

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The act of noting and recording some event; or the record of such noting.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A remark or comment.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A judgement based on observing.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An evaluation conducted through observing.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** I need to do your observation before the end of the week.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
