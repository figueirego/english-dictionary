## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A pipe to conduct something downwards.
   1. A large external pipe in a water-tube boiler, carrying unheated water from the steam drum down to the water drum as part of the circulation path.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A pipe to conduct something downwards.
   1. A pipe for leading the hot gases from the top of a blast furnace downward to the regenerators, boilers, etc.
