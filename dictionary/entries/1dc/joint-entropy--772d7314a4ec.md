### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The Shannon entropy of a "script" whose "characters" are elements of the Cartesian product of the sets of characters of the component scripts.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** If random variables X and Y are mutually independent, then their joint entropy H(X,Y) is just the sum H(X)\#43;H(Y) of its component entropies. If they are not mutually independent, then their joint entropy will be H(X)\#43;H(Y)-I(X\#59;Y) where I(X\#59;Y) is the mutual information of X and Y.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; information-theory; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences
