### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Someone who scavenges, especially one who searches through rubbish for food or useful things.

   **Example:** garbage scavenger

   **Example:** A scavenger picked through the rubbish heap.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An animal that feeds on decaying matter such as carrion.

   **Example:** Vultures are natural scavengers.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A street sweeper.

   **Usage:** Ireland; UK; obsolete

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A child employed to pick up loose cotton from the floor in a cotton mill.

   **Usage:** Ireland; UK; historical

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A substance used to remove impurities from the air or from a solution.
