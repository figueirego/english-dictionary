### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A girl or an unmarried young woman; maiden.

   **Usage:** dated; poetic

   **Source synonyms:** damsel; maiden

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An adult or adolescent female servant or cleaner (short for maidservant). (In feudal times this could be anyone from a high-ranking assistant to a low-ranking cleaner.)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A virgin, now female but originally one of either gender.

   **Usage:** archaic
