## Noun · Etymology 1 · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /meɪnz/ — source labels: UK

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. The domestic electrical power supply, especially as connected to a network or grid.

   **Usage:** UK; uncountable

   **Example:** I plugged it into the mains and it blew up!

   **Example:** We don't have mains power out here, only a generator.

   **Example:** mains electricity, mains voltage

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. A large-scale network or grid supplying any of various other services, such as water, gas or sewerage, to properties.

   **Usage:** UK; uncountable

   **Example:** We have gas heating, but it's bottled gas, not mains gas.

   **Example:** The properties formerly relied on a well, but are now connected to the mains water supply.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. The main course of a meal.

   **Usage:** uncountable
