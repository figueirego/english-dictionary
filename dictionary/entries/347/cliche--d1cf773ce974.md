## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Something, most often a phrase or expression, that is overused or used outside its original context, so that its original impact and meaning are lost. A trite saying; a platitude.

   **Usage:** rhetoric

   **Example:** The villain kidnapping the love interest is a bit of a cliché.

   **Example:** I know it's a bit of a cliché, but love really does conquer all.

   **Source synonyms:** convention; platitude; stereotype; trope; byword; saying; adage; saw (archaic); proverb; maxim; apothegm; apophthegm; dictum; gnome; gnomic saying; mantra; paroemia

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A stereotype (printing plate).

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** media; printing; publishing
