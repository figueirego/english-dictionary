**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A diminutive of the female given name Christine and of its variant forms; fairly common as a formal female given name in the 20th century.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A mostly Scottish and Irish diminutive of the male given name Christopher.

**Source sense R1.S3**
