### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** video-games

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** broadly

3. Death.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** When you have cancer, you pretty much have two options&#58; chemotherapy, or game over.
