### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any member of the species Scophthalmus maximus (syn. Psetta maxima) of flatfish native to Europe.

   **Source synonyms:** sea pheasant; turbit

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of various other flatfishes of family Scophthalmidae that are found in marine or brackish waters.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>
