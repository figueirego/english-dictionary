### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A type of formal attire for men, normally consisting of a jacket (traditionally with tails), trousers, waistcoat, shirt and tie (traditionally a bow tie).

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A type of formal attire for women, typically a jacket over a dress, or a jacket and blouse with trousers or a skirt.
