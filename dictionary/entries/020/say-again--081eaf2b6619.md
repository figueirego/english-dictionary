## Phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. "What did you say?" or "Repeat what you have said." A polite formula used when one has not heard or understood what has been said.

   **Usage:** colloquial; idiomatic

   **Example:** I'm sorry, I didn't hear you. Please say again?

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To repeat part of or entire message transmitted over the radio, used instead of "repeat" which is an artillery procedure word.

   **Usage:** procedure word

   **Example:** Bravo Six, say again, over.

   **Example:** Bravo Actual, I say again&#58; Eight foot mobiles moving east, over.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war
