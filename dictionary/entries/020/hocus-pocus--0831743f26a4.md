### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Some ineffectual action or speech, especially if intended to divert attention; nonsense.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Religious or supernatural phenomena one holds to be nonsense or mere trickery; superstitious mumbo-jumbo.

   **Usage:** derogatory; uncountable; usually

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. Some action carried out to bring about change as if by magic; a trick; sleight of hand, trickery.

   **Usage:** US; also; attributive; countable; uncountable; usually

   **Source synonyms:** legerdemain; prestigiation; sleight of hand; conjuration; conjuring; dweomercraft (rare); hocus-pocus (US); illusionism; jugglery (archaic); legerdemain; magic; palmistry; prestidigitation; prestigiation (obsolete); sleight of hand

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. A conjurer.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; usually

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**
