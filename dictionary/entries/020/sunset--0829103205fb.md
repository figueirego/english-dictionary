### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The moment each evening when the sun disappears below the western horizon.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** at sunset

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The changes in color of the sky before and after sunset.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The final period of the life of a person or thing.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

   **Example:** one's sunset years

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A set termination date.

   **Usage:** attributive; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The tax increase legislation included a sunset clause requiring renewal to prevent the tax increase from expiring.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The region where the sun sets; the west.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
