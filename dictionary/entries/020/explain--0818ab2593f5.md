### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make plain, manifest, or intelligible; to clear of obscurity; to illustrate the meaning of.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The issue was explained to the governor in detail.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To give the reason for, justification for, or cause of.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To make flat, smooth out.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To unfold or make visible.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To make something plain or intelligible.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** She tried to explain but he wouldn’t listen.
