### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A radical change in thinking from an accepted point of view to a new one, necessitated when new scientific discoveries produce anomalies in the current paradigm.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** sciences

   **Source synonyms:** reconceptualization; rethinking; reframing; remodeling

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
