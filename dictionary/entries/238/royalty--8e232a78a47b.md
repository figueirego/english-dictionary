## Noun

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈɹɔɪəlti/ — source labels: UK

### Forms

- royalties — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The rank, status, power or authority of a monarch.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. People of royal rank, plus their families, treated as a group.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A royal right or prerogative, such as the exploitation of a natural resource; the granting of such a right; payment received for such a right.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The payment received by an owner of real property for exploitation of mineral rights in the property.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Payment made to a writer, composer, inventor etc for the sale or use of intellectual property, invention etc.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To make more money from a book than it cost to run an advertising campaign for it; to make enough in royalties to cover the advance a book received.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** authorship; broadcasting; communications; film; journalism; literature; media; publishing; television; writing

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Someone in a privileged position.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A king and a queen as a starting hand in Texas hold 'em.

   **Usage:** countable; slang; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** card-games; poker

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. The bounds of a royal burgh.

   **Usage:** Scotland; countable; historical; uncountable
