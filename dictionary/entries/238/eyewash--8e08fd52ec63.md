## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The washing out of the eyes with copious solution (usually water alone) to quickly remove an irritant, as for example in laboratories and industrial plants; the washing solution itself.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; usually

   **Example:** an eyewash station on every floor of the building

   **Example:** a bottle of eyewash

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A soothing medicated lotion for the eyes.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Something deceptive and superficially pleasing but without accurate or genuine substance&#58; camouflage; disguise; false front; pretense; nonsense.

   **Usage:** figuratively; slang; uncountable; usually

   **Source synonyms:** hogwash; whitewash; window dressing
