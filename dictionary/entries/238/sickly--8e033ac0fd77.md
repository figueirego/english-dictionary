### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Frequently ill or in poor health; weakly.

   **Example:** a sickly child

   **Source synonyms:** valetudinarian (formal)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Not in good health; (somewhat) sick.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Characterized by poor or unhealthy growth. (of a plant)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Appearing ill, infirm or unhealthy; giving the appearance of illness.

   **Example:** a sickly pallor

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Shedding a relatively small amount of light; (of light) not very bright.

   **Source synonyms:** faint; pale; wan

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Lacking intensity or vigour.

   **Example:** a sickly smile

   **Example:** a sickly winter sun

   **Source synonyms:** faint; feeble; insipid; weak

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Associated with poor moral or mental well-being.

   **Source synonyms:** unhealthy

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Tending to produce nausea.

   **Example:** a sickly smell; sickly sentimentality

   **Source synonyms:** nauseating; sickening

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. Overly sweet.

   **Source synonyms:** cloying; saccharine

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. Marked by the occurrence of illness or disease (of a period of time).

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s11"></a>
