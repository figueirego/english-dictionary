### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To eat quickly, greedily, hungrily, or ravenously.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** gobble; gorge; wolf; engorge

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To rapidly destroy, engulf, or lay waste.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** The fire was devouring the building.

   **Source synonyms:** consume; devastate; overwhelm

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To take in avidly with the intellect or with one's gaze.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** She intended to devour the book.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To absorb or engross the mind fully, especially in a destructive manner.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** After the death of his wife, he was devoured by grief.

   **Source synonyms:** devastate; overwhelm

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Synonym of eat&#58; to be very good at something; to slay.

   **Usage:** Internet; intransitive

   **Example:** She devoured! She left no crumbs!

   **Source synonyms:** go hard; kick ass; bang (slang; stative); devour (slang); eat (slang; stative); eat and leave no crumbs (idiomatic; slang); [excel](../3ea/excel--faba1e00af8e.md); go hard (slang; stative); kick (slang); kick ass (idiomatic; stative; vulgar); rock (slang); rule (slang; stative); shine (copulative); slap (slang; stative); slay (African-American-Vernacular-English); eat (synonym; synonym-of)
