### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A consequence or ensuing result of some action.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** You realize this little stunt of yours is going to have some pretty serious repercussions.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The act of driving back, or the state of being driven back; reflection; reverberation.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** the repercussion of sound

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Rapid reiteration of the same sound.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences
