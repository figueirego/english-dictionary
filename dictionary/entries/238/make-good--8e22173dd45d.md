### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To achieve substantial success in life, often in business.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Source synonyms:** come good

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To complete successfully; to fulfil (a promise).

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive; with-on

   **Example:** He made good his threat and made good his escape by jumping from a second-story window.

   **Example:** He made good on his threat to jump from a second-story window.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To match the first player's bet with one's own, rather than dropping out.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** gambling; games

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To remedy or compensate for (a defect or deficiency).

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive; with-on

   **Example:** The company made good the damage by paying my repair costs.

   **Example:** The company made good on the damage by paying my repair costs.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To make (a surface) level or even.

   **Usage:** idiomatic
