### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who deals in goods, especially automobiles; a middleman.

   **Example:** That used car dealer gave me a great deal on my 1962 rusted-out Volkswagen bug!

   **Source synonyms:** salesman; peddler

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A drug dealer, one who peddles illicit drugs.

   **Source synonyms:** peddler

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A particular type of stock broker or trader.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The person who deals the cards in a card game.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. One who deals or metes out anything.
