### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of a system, currently not connected (generally electrically) to a larger network. For example, a power plant which is not connected to the grid, or a computer which is not connected to the Internet or to any other communications service.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** I saved the web pages so I could read them later offline.

   **Example:** He only had the option to play offline games because there was no Wi-Fi.

   **Source antonyms:** online

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Happening in the physical world (the real world) as opposed to on the internet.

   **Usage:** broadly; not-comparable

   **Example:** offline meetings

   **Example:** offline dating

   **Source synonyms:** IRL; meatspace

   **Source antonyms:** online
