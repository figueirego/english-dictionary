### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having a tendency or desire to shoot a firearm irresponsibly before adequately identifying the target.

   **Example:** The bystanders were indiscriminately gunned down by trigger-happy gang members.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Inclined to behave recklessly or violently at the slightest provocation.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** Be careful when you work near him; he's trigger-happy with that chain-saw.

   **Example:** He was so trigger-happy that he wheeled and punched me when I brushed by him in the hall.
