### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To measure time.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To indicate the time correctly (of a timepiece).

   **Example:** How well does this old watch keep time?

   **Source antonyms:** lose time

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To make any regular rhythmical movements at the same time with others.

   **Example:** He couldn't carry a tune in a wheelbarrow, but he keeps time like a damn machine.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To control the beat or rhythm of a song played by a music group.

   **Example:** Isn't one of the conductor's main jobs to keep time?

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To maintain a schedule set out in a timetable. (e.g. of a train)

   **Source antonyms:** lose time
