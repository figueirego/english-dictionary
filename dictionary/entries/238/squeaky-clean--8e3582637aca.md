### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Very clean, like a hard surface that squeaks when rubbed.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source synonyms:** spotless; immaculate; spick-and-span; clean; clean as a bean; clean as a hound's tooth; clean as a new penny; clean as a new pin; clean as a whistle; immaculate; pristine; spick-and-span; spotless; squeaky clean; stainless; unsoiled; unsullied

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** broadly; not-comparable

   **Example:** Mr. Smith's reputation was that of a squeaky clean polititian.
