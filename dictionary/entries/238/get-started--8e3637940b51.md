### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To begin an activity.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Come on guys, settle down, it's time to get started

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To begin (an activity or event); to get something to start.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Get the car started and then we can set off.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To cause (someone) to start talking about something in length.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive
