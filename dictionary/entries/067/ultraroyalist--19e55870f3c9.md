## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A member of a reactionary faction which sat in the French parliament from 1815 to 1830 under the Bourbon Restoration, favouring the monarchy and opposing liberal, republican, and democrat ideas.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person or group that favours monarchy and opposes liberal, republican, and democrat ideas; a person or group that has extremely royalist views.
