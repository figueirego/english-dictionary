### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An increase in size, number, value, or strength.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Nanoelectronics is one of the area's few growth industries .

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Ellipsis of economic growth.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; countable; ellipsis; uncountable

   **Example:** Growth was dampened by a softening of the global economy in 2001, but picked up in the subsequent years due to strong growth in China.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** economics; sciences

   **Alternative form of:** [economic growth](../317/economic-growth--c5cf49a58771.md)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An increase in psychological strength or resilience; an increased ability to overcome adversity.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Struggle, disappointment, and criticism all contribute to a person's growth.

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The act of growing, getting bigger or higher.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences
