### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used to mark a vowel letter as having its 'long' sound&#58; ⟨ā⟩ /eɪ/, ⟨ē⟩ /iː/, ⟨ī⟩ /aɪ/, ⟨ō⟩ /oʊ/, ⟨ū⟩ /juː/.
