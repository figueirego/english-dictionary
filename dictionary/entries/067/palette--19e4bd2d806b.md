### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A thin board on which a painter lays and mixes colours.

   **Usage:** painting

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A set of colours available to be used (or mixed).

   **Example:** Use your mouse to select the colour you want to use from the circular colour palette.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The range of colors in a given work or item or body of work.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The range of tones or tone colors in a given musical composition or performance.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A set of makeup including a variety of colors, often packaged together in a tray.

   **Example:** She removed an eyeshadow palette from her bag.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A visual selection of colours, tools, commands, etc.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; graphical-user-interface; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A plate of armour covering the points of junction at the bend of the shoulders and elbows.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A plate against which a person presses their chest to give force to a hand-operated drill.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. Any set or group of limited choices, options, or tasks from all ones that are currently available.

   **Usage:** figuratively; uncommon
