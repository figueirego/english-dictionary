### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. (zoology, traditional classification) A salamander of the group Cryptobranchiata, comprising Amphiumidae and Cryptobranchidae, whose adults lack external gills.

   **Extractor diagnostics (not usage labels):** no-gloss

**Supplemental definition (evidence reviewed; source gap preserved):** The original noun section has no quotation that conflicts with this restricted zoological use. Iordansky’s own comparative-anatomy paper repeatedly uses the exact plural for salamanders, explicitly identifies the two families, and explains the loss of external gills in adults. The paper also warns that the grouping no longer has formal taxonomic value. The definition preserves that traditional scope and does not import the different axolotl example from the existing adjective.
