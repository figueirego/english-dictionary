## Noun

### Forms

- barrios — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A municipality or subdivision of a municipality in Spanish America, and in Spain itself.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A slum on the periphery of a major city, or a low to middle-class neighborhood in a lesser city, in Venezuela or the Dominican Republic.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A rural barangay or neighborhood.

   **Usage:** Philippines

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An area or neighborhood in a US city inhabited predominantly by Spanish-speakers or people of Hispanic origin.

   **Usage:** US; informal
