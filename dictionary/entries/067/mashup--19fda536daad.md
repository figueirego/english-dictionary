### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Something consisting of two or more elements combined together.
   1. An artistic work that consists primarily of parts borrowed from other works, or features a mixture of genres.

      **Usage:** informal; slang

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** art; arts

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Something consisting of two or more elements combined together.
   1. A remix created by combining two or more songs from different artists into one piece of music.

      **Usage:** informal; slang

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Something consisting of two or more elements combined together.
   1. A derivative work consisting of two or more pieces of (generally digital) media joined together, such as a video clip with a different soundtrack applied for humorous effect, or a map overlaid with user-supplied data.

      **Usage:** informal; slang

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Something consisting of two or more elements combined together.
   1. A Web application that combines data or functionality from more than one source.

      **Usage:** Internet; informal

      **Example:** This app is a mashup mixing news from the BBC and maps from Google.
