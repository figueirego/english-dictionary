### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An ethnographic study drawing on one's own anecdotal or personal experience, or an approach to conducting research in which anecdotal or personal experience is foregrounded.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The aborigine's autobiography was also an autoethnography. It told much about the culture he grew up in.
