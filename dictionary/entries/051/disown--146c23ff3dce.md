### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To refuse to own, or to refuse to acknowledge one’s own.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Lord Capulet and his wife threatened to disown their daughter Juliet if she didn’t go through with marrying Count Paris.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To repudiate any connection to; to renounce.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** disavow; disclaim; abjure; deny; disavow; disclaim; disown; forsay (archaic); forswear; renounce; repudiate; wash one's hands of

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To detach (a job or process) so that it can continue to run even when the user who launched it ends his/her login session.

   **Usage:** Unix; transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences
