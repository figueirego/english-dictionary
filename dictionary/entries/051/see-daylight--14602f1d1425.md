### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To reach an understanding of something previously obscure; to arrive at a solution to a problem.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To near completion of a lengthy task.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I've been cataloguing my stamp album all weekend; now finally I'm beginning to see daylight.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To become exposed to view; to be seen.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I have a lot of old junk in the attic that hasn't seen daylight for years.

   **Example:** The report was suppressed and never saw daylight.

   **Source context (verbatim):** in the negative

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see see, daylight.

   **Usage:** idiomatic
