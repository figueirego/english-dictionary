### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To have a sudden, pinching or sharp pain in a specific part of the body, like a twitch.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** My side twinges if I sit too long.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To pull and twist.

   **Usage:** dialectal; intransitive; obsolete except UK

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To pull and twist (someone or something); to pinch, to tweak, to twitch, to wring.

   **Usage:** dialectal; obsolete; transitive; except UK

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To affect or torment (someone, their mind, or part of their body) with one or more sudden, pinching or sharp pains; to irritate.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To prick or stimulate (one's conscience).

   **Usage:** figuratively; obsolete; transitive

## Noun · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /twɪnd͡ʒ/ — source labels: General-American; Received-Pronunciation

### Forms

- twinges — plural

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A sudden, pinching or sharp pain in a specific part of the body, especially one lasting for a short time.

   **Example:** I got a twinge in my arm.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. A turn, a twist.

   **Usage:** also; figuratively; rare

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. A sudden, sharp feeling of an emotional or mental nature, as of guilt or sadness; a pang, a paroxysm, a throe; also, a prick of the conscience.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** a twinge of embarrassment

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. A sudden, sharp occurrence of something; a nip.

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. Synonym of earwig (“insect of the order Dermaptera”).

   **Usage:** UK; dialectal

   **Source synonyms:** earwig (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. An act of pulling and twisting; a pinch, a tweak, a twitch.

   **Usage:** obsolete
