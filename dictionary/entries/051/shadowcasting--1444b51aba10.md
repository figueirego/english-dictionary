### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The generation of shadows from a three-dimensional model.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computer-graphics; computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** uncountable; microscopy
