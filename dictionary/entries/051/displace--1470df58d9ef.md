### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To put out of place; to disarrange.

   **Source synonyms:** outplace

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To move something, or someone, especially to forcibly move people from their homeland.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To supplant, or take the place of something or someone; to substitute.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To replace, on account of being superior to or more suitable than that which is being replaced.

   **Example:** Electronic calculators soon displaced the older mechanical kind.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To have a weight equal to that of the water displaced.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a floating ship

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To repress.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; psychology; sciences
