### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Those states of the Southern United States that were among the original Thirteen Colonies.

   **Usage:** US; historical; usually

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The states which created the Confederacy during the American Civil War, as contrasted with those that joined afterward.
