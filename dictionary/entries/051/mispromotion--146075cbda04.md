### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of mispromoting; the advertisement of something as having properties it does not have.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The act of promoting someone to a position for which they are not qualified.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
