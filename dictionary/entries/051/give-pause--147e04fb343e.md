### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To cause (someone) to hesitate and think carefully about something, giving (the person) cause to be unsure or concerned about something.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I was going to put up the new logo, since a majority of people like it a little better than the old one, but the ferocity of those who think it's terrible gives me pause.

   **Example:** Here is a fact that will give you pause&#58; some states do not publish the voting records of their legislators.
