### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The condition of a solution being more highly concentrated than is normally possible.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. the condition of a vapour having a higher saturation vapour pressure than is normally possible.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
