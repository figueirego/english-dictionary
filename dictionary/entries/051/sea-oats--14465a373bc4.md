### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any species of oat-like grasses that can tolerate salty and alkaline conditions.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** seaside oats; araña; arroz de costa

<a id="r1-s2"></a>
