**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** the Russian Partition

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The division of a territory into two or more autonomous ones.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Monarchies where partition isn't prohibited risk weakening through parcellation and civil wars between the heirs.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A vertical structure that divides a room.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** a brick partition; lath and plaster partitions

6. That which divides or separates; that by which different things, or distinct parts of the same thing, are separated; boundary; dividing line or space.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
