### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A craft for transportation on or in water, air, or space.
   1. Any craft designed for transportation on or in water, such as a ship, boat, or submarine.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

      **Source synonyms:** watercraft

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A craft for transportation on or in water, air, or space.
   1. A craft designed for transportation through air or space.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Dishes and cutlery collectively, especially if made of precious metals.

   **Usage:** dialectal; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A container of liquid or other substance, such as a glass, goblet, cup, bottle, bowl, or pitcher.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A person as a container of qualities or feelings.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** A teacher should be a vessel of knowledge.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A tube or canal that carries fluid in an animal or plant.

   **Example:** Blood and lymph vessels are found in humans; xylem and phloem vessels are found in plants.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences
