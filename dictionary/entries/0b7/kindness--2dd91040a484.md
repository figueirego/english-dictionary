**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The state of being kind.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** Jamie's kindness is legendary, so much that people have taken advantage of her.

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An instance of kind or charitable behaviour.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** How can I thank you for your many kindnesses?
