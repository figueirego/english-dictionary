### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A covering or integument, usually referring to a thin layer or membrane in an organism.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An integument such as the inner membrane of the coat of a seed.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A covering such as the thin layer of bone in the roof of the middle ear of mammals.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. In insects such as winged cockroaches and locusts, the stiff, membranous fore wings; in many species they are not primarily used for flight, but serve as protective covering for the delicate hind wings, which are the main organs of flight. The more heavily armoured fore-wings of most beetles are called elytra, not tegmina.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; entomology; natural-sciences
