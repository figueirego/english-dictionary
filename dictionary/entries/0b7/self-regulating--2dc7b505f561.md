### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Able to control itself.

   **Example:** The motor was self-regulating, until the governor broke and it suddenly raced out of control.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of an organization, establishing standards for itself rather than having them legislated for it by government.

   **Example:** Lawyers are self-regulating through the bar association, which sets standards and imposes penalties.
