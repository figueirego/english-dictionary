### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A second-year undergraduate student in a college or university, or a second-year student in a four-year secondary school or high school.

   **Usage:** Philippines; US

   **Example:** She was very mature for a sophomore and had several friends who were juniors or even seniors.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. A three-year-old horse.

   **Usage:** US

   **Example:** The filly had looked promising as a sophomore, but concerns over her health had prompted the owner to pull her from the season’s early races.
