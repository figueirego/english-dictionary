## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A coded warning used by men to alert each other that female guests ("ducks") are present ("on the pond"), so that for politeness they should moderate their language.

   **Usage:** Australia; plural; plural-only

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Members of a batting order who are on base; baserunners.

   **Usage:** plural; plural-only

   **Example:** There are two ducks on the pond for the cleanup hitter.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; baseball; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports
