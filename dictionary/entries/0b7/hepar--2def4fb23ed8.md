### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. liver of sulphur; a substance of a liver-brown colour, sometimes used in medicine, formed by fusing sulphur with carbonates of the alkalis (especially potassium).

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>
