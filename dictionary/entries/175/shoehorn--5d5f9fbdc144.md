### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To use a shoehorn.

   **Usage:** literally

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To force (something) into (a tight space); to squeeze (something) into (a schedule, etc); to exert great effort to insert or include (something); to include (something) despite potent reasons not to.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** I shoehorned his dozen burgeoning bags into the backseat of my tiny car, and off we went.

   **Example:** His staff want to shoehorn an extra stop into his already packed campaigning schedule.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To force some current event into alignment with some (usually unconnected) agenda, especially when it is fallacious.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** People claiming to be psychic may shoehorn an event into fulfillment of some vague past prediction.
