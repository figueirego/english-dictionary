### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The unordered state of matter in classical accounts of cosmogony.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any state of disorder; a confused or amorphous mixture or conglomeration.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** to descend into chaos

   **Example:** After the earthquake, the local hospital was in chaos

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A behavior of iterative nonlinear systems in which arbitrarily small variations in initial conditions become magnified over time.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. One of the two metaphysical forces of the world in some fantasy settings, as opposed to law.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** fantasy

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A vast chasm or abyss.

   **Usage:** obsolete; uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A given medium; a space in which something exists or lives; an environment.

   **Usage:** obsolete; rare; uncountable; usually
