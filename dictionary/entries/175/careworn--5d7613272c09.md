**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Worn down by cares&#58; showing the signs of long-term stress, tired and haggard due to prolonged worry.

   **Example:** The weeks of working hard to look after his sick family left him looking careworn.

   **Source synonyms:** drawn; woeworn; careworn; haggard; drawn; weary; woeworn (archaic)
