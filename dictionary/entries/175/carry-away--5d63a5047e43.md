### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To steal; to take away.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to carry away a child

   **Source context (verbatim):** as of a thing or person

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To be so excited that one is no longer in control of one's actions or behavior.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; informal

   **Source synonyms:** catch

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To move away, as physically or emotionally.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To take possession of the mind of; to charm, transport, or delude.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to be carried away by music, or by temptation

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To break under sudden pressure of violent wind.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a mast or rigging
