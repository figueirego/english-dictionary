### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To allow to be used by someone temporarily, on condition that it or its equivalent will be returned.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I will only lend you my car if you fill up the tank.

   **Example:** Where is that hundred euros I lent you months ago?

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To make a loan.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To be suitable or applicable, to fit.

   **Usage:** reflexive

   **Example:** Poems do not lend themselves to translation easily.

   **Example:** The long history of the past does not lend itself to a simple black and white interpretation.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To afford; to grant or furnish in general.

   **Example:** Can you lend me some assistance?

   **Example:** The famous director lent his name to the new film.

   **Example:** The old silver lends an air of elegance to an otherwise drab room.

   **Example:** The evidence lends credence to the witness's testimony.

   **Example:** The subtle use of strings lends fluidity to the composition.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To borrow.

   **Usage:** proscribed
