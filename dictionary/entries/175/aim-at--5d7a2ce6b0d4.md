### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To create or design for a particular audience.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I’ve created a program aimed at the educated over 40’s.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To intend to do or achieve.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I’m aiming at the Governorship of Kansas.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see aim, at.

   **Example:** This gun is aimed at your head.
