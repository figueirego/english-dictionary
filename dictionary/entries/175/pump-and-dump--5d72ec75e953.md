## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

- pump and dumps — plural
- pump-and-dump — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A form of financial fraud where the fraudster purchases stocks at a very low price, generates excitement about those stocks in order to create a temporary price increase, then sells the stocks before the price depreciates.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance

   **Source synonyms:** buy, lie and sell high

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A situation where a woman uses a breast pump and then discards the milk, typically because she has had an infection, or has been drinking alcohol.

   **Usage:** countable; slang; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A situation where a person has sex with a partner on a single occasion, without immediate intention of further contact or pursuing a serious relationship.

   **Usage:** countable; slang; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** fuck and chuck; hit it and quit it; smash and dash; one-night stand
