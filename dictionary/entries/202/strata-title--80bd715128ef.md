### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. (law) A form of title over parts of real property, originally designed to facilitate ownership and mortgage of separate parts of a building, often on multiple levels. Usually some common property is the responsibility of all strata title holders via an owner's corporation.

   **Usage:** Australia; British-Columbia; Singapore

   **Source synonyms:** condominium; commonhold; sectional title
