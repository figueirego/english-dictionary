## Noun

### Forms

- done deals — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An agreement that has been finally resolved or decided.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** It's a done deal — he already sold me the prize wine bottle from his collection, and I already popped the cork.

   **Source synonyms:** done dotta

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An established fact that cannot be undone.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** They're telling us that climate change is a done deal — that now we're into mitigation and adaptation instead of prevention.

   **Source synonyms:** fait accompli; water under the bridge
