### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act, process, or result of cancelling; as, the cancellation of certain words in a contract, or of the contract itself.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** annulment; revocation; stoppage; termination; abolition; abrogation; annulment; cancellation; deauthentication; devalidation; invalidation; nullification; renunciation; revocation; termination; vitiation

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The operation of striking out common factors, e.g. in both the dividend and divisor.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A postmark that marks a postage stamp so as to prevent its reuse.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; philately

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. In United States intellectual property law, a proceeding in which an interested party seeks to cancel the registration of a trademark or patent.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law
