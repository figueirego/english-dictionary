### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A service for sending, receiving, and/or storing email, usually on a remote web server, in such a way that it is accessible through a web browser.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** Hotmail, Yahoo! Mail and Gmail are well-known webmail services.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The email thus stored.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** I'm going to check my webmail.
