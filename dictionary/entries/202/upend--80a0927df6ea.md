### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To end up; to set on end.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To tip or turn over.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** When he upended the bottle of water over his sleeping sister, the lid popped off and surprised them both.

   **Example:** upend the box and empty the contents

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To destroy, invalidate, overthrow, or defeat.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** The scientific evidence upended the popular myth.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To affect or upset drastically.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** By the middle of March 2020, the COVID-19 pandemic upended normal life for virtually all Americans.
