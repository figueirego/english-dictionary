## Noun

### Forms

- prehistories — plural
- pre-history — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The time before written records in any area of the world; the events and conditions of those times.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** prehistoric age

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The study of those times.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any past time (even recent) treated as such a distant, unknowable era.

   **Usage:** countable; excessive; humorous; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The history leading up to some event, condition, etc.

   **Usage:** countable; often; uncountable

   **Source context (verbatim):** as pre-history
