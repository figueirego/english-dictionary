### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Exempt; not subject to.

   **Usage:** usually

   **Example:** As a diplomat, you are immune from prosecution.

   **Source context (verbatim):** with "from"

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Protected by inoculation, or due to innate resistance to pathogens.

   **Usage:** usually

   **Example:** I am immune to chicken pox.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Not vulnerable.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** Alas, he was immune to my charms.

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Of or pertaining to immunity.

   **Example:** immune system

   **Example:** We examined the patient's immune response.
