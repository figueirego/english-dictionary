### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who sails; one whose occupation is sailing or navigating ships or other waterborne craft.

   **Example:** He's a talented sailor and has spent many years at sea.

   **Source synonyms:** seafarer; seaman; mariner

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person who sails; one whose occupation is sailing or navigating ships or other waterborne craft.
   1. In particular, a member of the crew of a (civilian or military) vessel, as opposed to a captain, admiral, etc.; a mariner; a common seaman.

      **Source synonyms:** seafarer; seaman; mariner; jack; tar; jacktar

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A person who sails; one whose occupation is sailing or navigating ships or other waterborne craft.
   1. A person who sails sailing boats (as opposed to boats powered by other means) as a sport or recreation.

      **Source synonyms:** seafarer; seaman; mariner

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A person who sails; one whose occupation is sailing or navigating ships or other waterborne craft.
   1. A term of address supposedly used by prostitutes and flirtatious women towards men.

      **Usage:** humorous

      **Source synonyms:** seafarer; seaman; mariner

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Any of various nymphalid butterflies of the genera Neptis, Pseudoneptis and Phaedyma, having white markings on a dark base and commonly flying by gliding.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A stiff straw hat with a flat, circular brim and a low, flat crown.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A brick, for example in a course of brickwork, that is laid vertically on its shortest end (smallest face), with its widest face facing the outside of the wall.
