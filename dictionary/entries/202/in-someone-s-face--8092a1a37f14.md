## Prep\_phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Onto or into someone's face.

   **Usage:** literally

   **Example:** Who would want to sit next to Larry, the guy who's too dumb not to blow his cigar smoke in your face?

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In front of someone's face; before someone's eyes.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** The people on the beach blanket near ours were fine until they started changing into their swimsuits right in our faces.
