### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In a simplistic way.

   **Example:** He explained relativity, but very simplistically.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used to announce or excuse an oversimplified explanation or description. (Compare with in a nutshell.)

   **Example:** Simplistically, he was fired with pay.

### Related words
