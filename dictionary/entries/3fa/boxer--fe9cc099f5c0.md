<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A participant in a boxing match&#58; a fighter who boxes.

   **Example:** You can tell she's a boxer by looking at her nose.

   **Source synonyms:** pugilist; prizefighter

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A type of internal combustion engine in which cylinders are arranged in two banks on either side of a single crankshaft.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>
