### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A noun that denotes an agent (human or nonhuman) that performs the action denoted by the verb from which the noun is derived.

   **Example:** The agent noun “cutter” means a person who cuts, or a thing that is made to or is able to cut something.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** grammar; human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** nomen agentis
