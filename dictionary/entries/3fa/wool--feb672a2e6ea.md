### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The hair of the sheep, llama and some other ruminants.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A cloth or yarn made from such hair.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Anything with a fibrous texture like that of sheep's wool.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A dingy white color inspired by the natural shade of wool.

   **Usage:** broadly; uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A fine fiber obtained from the leaves of certain trees, such as firs and pines.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Short, thick hair, especially when crisped or curled.

   **Usage:** obsolete; uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Yarn, including that made from synthetic fibers.

   **Usage:** New-Zealand; UK; uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A woolly back; a resident of a satellite town outside Liverpool, such as St Helens or Warrington. See also Yonner.

   **Usage:** derogatory; uncountable; usually; Liverpool

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A marijuana cigarette or cigar laced with crack cocaine.

   **Usage:** slang; uncountable; usually

   **Source synonyms:** wooler; woolie
