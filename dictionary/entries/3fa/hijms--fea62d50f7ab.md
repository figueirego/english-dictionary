### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Initialism of His Imperial Japanese Majesty's Ship, an Imperial Japanese Navy ship; an informal ship prefix used for a warship of the Imperial Japanese Navy, usually in reference to the period from the Meiji Revolution to the Washington Naval Treaty.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; initialism; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; nautical; politics; transport; war

   **Source synonyms:** IJN; HMS; HIMS
