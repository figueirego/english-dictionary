## Noun

### Forms

- composite flowers — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A flower that is made up of multiple simpler flowers; an inflorescence of the pseudanthium type.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An entire plant that has such flowers.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A particular family of such plants, the Asteraceae or Compositae (commonly referred to as the aster, daisy, composite, or sunflower family).
