### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of several drums.
   1. A small two-headed drum, used in Dominican merengue music, usually played by striking one side with a mallet and the other with the hand.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of several drums.
   1. A large two-headed bass drum, used in Mexican banda and regional genres like tamborazo, usually played by striking both sides with a mallet (bolillo).

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of several drums.
   1. A wide two-headed drum used mainly in Colombian cumbia, played by striking both sides with heavy sticks (baquetas).
