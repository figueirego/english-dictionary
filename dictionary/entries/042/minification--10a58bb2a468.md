### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The reduction in apparent size of objects viewed through a lens or mirror shaped so as to increase the field of view, such as a convex or aspheric mirror or a Fresnel lens.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The removal of white space and unnecessary characters from a web page's source code in order to reduce its size and improve download time.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
