**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The weight of the half hydrogen molecule, or of the hydrogen atom, taken as the standard in comparing the atomic weights of the elements.

   **Usage:** obsolete

   **Example:** An atom of oxygen weighs sixteen microcriths.
