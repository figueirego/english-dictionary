### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Superficial gentleness and courtesy masking a strong and unyielding will or determination.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** If you will stab your opponent in the back, it must be with a velvet glove on your hand and the sweetest smile on your face.
