**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To draw conclusions from examining; to assess; to appraise.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** assess; esteem; rate; appraise; apprise (archaic); apprize (archaic); assess; esteem (dated); evaluate; judge; price; prize (obsolete); rate; valuate; value; weigh (figuratively); worthen
