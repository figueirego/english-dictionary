### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A tail of a horse.

   **Usage:** literal

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of various simple vascular plants, of the order Equisetales, that have hollow stems and produce spores.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A Turkish standard denoting rank.

   **Example:** Commanders were distinguished by the number of horsetails carried before them. The sultan had seven, the grand vizier five, and the pashas three, two, or one.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A mare's tail, a water plant in genus Hippuris.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Any waterfall, the descending water of which maintains contact with bedrock most of the time.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. cauda equina, a bundle of nerve fibers.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A ponytail hairstyle.

   **Usage:** rare
