### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An area mainly in the borough of Bromley, south-east Greater London, England, named after the "palace" that was moved there after being erected for The Great Exhibition of 1851 (OS grid ref TQ3471).
