### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A place or institution where people are held against their will, in the US especially for long-term confinement, as of those convicted of serious crimes or otherwise considered undesirable by the government.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The cold stone walls of the prison had stood for over a century.

   **Source synonyms:** bridewell; big house

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Confinement in prison (sense 1).

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Prison was a harrowing experience for him.

   **Source synonyms:** imprisonment

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any restrictive environment, such as a harsh academy or home.

   **Usage:** colloquial; countable; figuratively; uncountable

   **Example:** The academy was a prison for many of its students because of its strict teachers.
