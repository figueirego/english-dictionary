## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The apparent angular elevation of the heavenly bodies above their true places, caused by the refraction of the rays of light in their passage through the Earth's atmosphere.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** astronomical refraction; astronomic refraction
