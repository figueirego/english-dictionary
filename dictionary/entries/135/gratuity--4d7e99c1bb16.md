### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An additional payment given freely as thanks for service.

   **Example:** service gratuity

   **Example:** cash gratuity

   **Example:** leave with a gratuity

   **Example:** The waiter received a generous gratuity.

   **Example:** The company gave each employee a retirement gratuity.

   **Source synonyms:** douceur; perquisite; grace; pourboire; tip; baksheesh; cumshaw; benefaction; boon (archaic); concession; donative (archaic); fairing (rare); gift; grant; present; tithing (obsolete)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Euphemistic form of fee, in contexts where such additional payments have been made obligatory.

   **Usage:** euphemistic; form-of

   **Source synonyms:** service charge; douceur; tip

   **Form of:** [fee](../2e0/fee--b80cdaae9b32.md); in contexts where such additional payments have been made obligatory

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Euphemistic form of bribe.

   **Usage:** euphemistic; form-of

   **Source synonyms:** backhander; [bribe](../3f3/bribe--fce07324e0e7.md); bung; payoff (informal); coffee money (Indonesia; Malaysia; Singapore); cold drink money (South-Africa; slang); dash (Nigeria); douceur; inducement; kopi money (Indonesia; Malaysia; Singapore); pay-off; payola; subornation; sweetener; tea money (Myanmar; Thailand; slang)
