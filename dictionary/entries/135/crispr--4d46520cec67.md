**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Segments of bacteria and archaea DNA containing short repetitions of base sequences.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; microbiology; natural-sciences

   **Source synonyms:** SRSR

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The CRISPR/Cas9 system, which functions to provide immunity to bacteria and archaea by finding a section of DNA and replacing it with an alternative.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; genetics; medicine; natural-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>
