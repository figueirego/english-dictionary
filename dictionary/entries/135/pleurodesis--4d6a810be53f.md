### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The production of adhesions in the pleural cavity, typically by the use of sclerosing agents or surgical abrasion, used especially in the treatment of recurrent pneumothorax, pleural effusion and chylothorax.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences; surgery
