### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Constantly complaining, especially in a childish way due to insignificant matters; fretful, whiny.

   **Example:** Due to the long wait, there were several peevish patients in the doctor’s waiting room.

   **Source antonyms:** unpeevish

**Source sense R1.S2**
