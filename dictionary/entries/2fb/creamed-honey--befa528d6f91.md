### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Honey which has been crystallized in a way that produces many small crystals rather than the normal larger crystals, giving it a lighter colour and smoother, spreadable consistency.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** churned honey; honey fondant; spun honey; whipped honey
