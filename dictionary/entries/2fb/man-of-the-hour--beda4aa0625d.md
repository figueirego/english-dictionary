## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A man who has recently caught the attention of, or is being admired or honoured by, a large number of people.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** Everyone will want to talk to you on your wedding day. You'll be the man of the hour.

   **Source synonyms:** man of the moment; toast of the town
