### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To rotate, revolve, spin or turn rapidly.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The dancer whirled across the stage, stopped, and whirled around to face the audience.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To have a sensation of spinning or reeling.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** My head is whirling after all that drink.

   **Source synonyms:** swim; reel

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To make something or someone whirl.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The dancer whirled his partner round on her toes.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To remove or carry quickly with, or as with, a revolving motion; to snatch.

   **Usage:** transitive
