### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A fuse for cannons, rockets, or other artillery, consisting of strong paper filled with a nitre, sulphur, and mealed powder, used principally to ignite the priming in proving guns, and as an incendiary material in shells.

   **Usage:** historical

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war
