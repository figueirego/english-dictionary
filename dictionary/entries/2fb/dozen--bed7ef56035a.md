### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A set of twelve.

   **Example:** Can I have a dozen eggs, please?

   **Example:** I ordered two dozen doughnuts.

   **Example:** There are hundreds of people from each continent in the convention but only a few dozen came from Africa.

   **Example:** Pack the shirts in dozens, please.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A large, unspecified number of, comfortably estimated in small multiples of twelve, thus generally implied to be significantly more than ten or twelve, but less than perhaps one or two hundred; many.

   **Usage:** always followed by of

   **Example:** There must have been dozens of examples just on the first page.

   **Example:** There were dozens and dozens of applicants before the job was posted.

   **Source context (verbatim):** as plural only

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An old English measure of ore containing 12 hundredweight.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; metallurgy; natural-sciences; physical-sciences
