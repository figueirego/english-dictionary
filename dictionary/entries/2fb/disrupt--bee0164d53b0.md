### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To throw into confusion or disorder.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Hecklers disrupted the man's speech.

   **Source synonyms:** dislocate

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To interrupt or impede.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Work on the tunnel was disrupted by a strike.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To improve a product or service in ways that displace an established one and surprise the market.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The internet makes it easier for leaner businesses to disrupt the larger and more unwieldy ones.
