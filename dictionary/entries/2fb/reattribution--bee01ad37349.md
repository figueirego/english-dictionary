## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of attributing again.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** the reattribution of a text of disputed authorship

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The process of determining whether artwork is real or fake, and, if fake, determining when and by whom it was created.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; crime

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** art; arts

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A therapeutic technique in which the client is encouraged to consider possible alternate causes for events.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; psychotherapy

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
