## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The quality or property of corroding or being corrosive, of eating away or disintegrating; acrimony.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** figuratively; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>
