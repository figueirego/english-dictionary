### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An instance of descending; act of coming down.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** We climbed the mountain with difficulty, but the descent was easier.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A way down.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** We had difficulty in finding the correct descent.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A sloping passage or incline.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The descent into the cavern was wet and slippery.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Lineage or hereditary derivation.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Our guide was of Welsh descent.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A drop to a lower status or condition; decline.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** After that, the holiday went into a steep descent.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A falling upon or invasion.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
