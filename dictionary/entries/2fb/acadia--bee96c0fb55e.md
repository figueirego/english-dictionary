### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A former French colony in North America in the 17th and early 18th centuries, spanning over what are now the Maritime provinces of eastern Canada (Nova Scotia, New Brunswick and Prince Edward Island) and part of the state of Maine in the United States.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Acadia National Park, a national park in Maine.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A parish in southern Louisiana, first settled by some Acadian exiles then by mostly Franco-Americans&#58; see Acadia Parish.
