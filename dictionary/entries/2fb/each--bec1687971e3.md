   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The apples cost 50 cents each.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Individually; separately; used in a sentence with a plural subject to indicate that the action or state described by the verb applies to all members of the described group individually, rather than collectively to the entire group.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** We ordered half a chicken each, but we each got a whole one.

   **Example:** You are each right in a different way.

   **Example:** There are three of us and we have five dollars each, so that means we've got 15 dollars.
