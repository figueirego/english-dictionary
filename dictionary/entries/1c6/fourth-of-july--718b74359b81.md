**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The national holiday of Independence Day in the United States, celebrated (commonly with fireworks) on the fourth day in July to mark the anniversary of the signing of the Declaration of Independence from Great Britain in 1776.

   **Source synonyms:** Fourth; Independence Day
