### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of recollecting, or recalling to the memory; the act of recalling to memory.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source synonyms:** reminiscence; remembrance

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The power of recalling ideas to the mind, or the period within which things can be recollected; remembrance.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** Alas that distant event isn't within my recollection anymore.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. That which is recollected; something called to mind; a reminiscence.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** One of his earliest recollections.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The act or practice of collecting or concentrating the mind; concentration; self-control.

   **Usage:** archaic; uncountable; usually

   **Example:** From such an education Charles contracted habits of gravity and recollection.

   **Example:** In a world filled with media and electronic devices, recollection can be difficult.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Catholicism; Christianity

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A spiritual retreat, especially one that is short.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Catholicism; Christianity
