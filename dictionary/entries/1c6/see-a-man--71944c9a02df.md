**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used in place of a real explanation when excusing oneself for a short period of time, particularly (euphemistic) to use the toilet or (historical) drink alcohol.

   **Usage:** dated; idiomatic

   **Example:** I'll be right back. I've just got to go to the ladies' room to see a man.
