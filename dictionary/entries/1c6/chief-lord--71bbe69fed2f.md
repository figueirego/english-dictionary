### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Synonym of overlord&#58; the lord of a fee immediately owed personal service or rent by a tenant, a tenant's liege lord.

   **Usage:** historical

   **Example:** Most feudal obligations were owed to the chief lord of the estate, but others were owed only and directly to the king of the realm.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

   **Source synonyms:** overlord (synonym; synonym-of)

**Source sense R1.S2**
