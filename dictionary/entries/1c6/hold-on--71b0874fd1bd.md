**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To grasp or grip firmly.

   **Example:** Hold on tightly to the railing.

   **Source synonyms:** belock; hold tight; beclasp (dated); begrasp; begrip; belock (archaic); clasp; clench; clinch; cling; clutch; enclasp; fang (archaic; dialectal); fondle; glaum (dialectal); grab; grabble; grasp; grip; hang on; hold on; hold tight; hold; nab; nim (obsolete); seize; stranglehold; take

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To keep; to store something for someone.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** Hold on to my umbrella while I ride the roller coaster.

   **Source synonyms:** keep; store

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To retain an advantage.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I'm holding on to my trump cards until I really need them.

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Wait a short while.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Source synonyms:** cool one's heels; hang on; abide (obsolete); back that train up; bide (archaic; dialectal); bide one's time (idiomatic); expect (obsolete); defer; hang on (idiomatic); hold on (idiomatic); hold one's breath (idiomatic); hold one's horses (idiomatic); hold one's water (idiomatic); hold out (idiomatic); mark time; stand by (idiomatic); stay; twiddle one's thumbs (idiomatic); wait
