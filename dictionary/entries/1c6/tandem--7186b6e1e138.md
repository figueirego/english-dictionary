### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A carriage pulled by two or more draught animals (generally draught horses) harnessed one behind the other, both providing pulling power but only the animal in front being able to steer.

   **Usage:** also; attributive; countable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Two draught animals (generally draught horses) harnessed one behind the other.

   **Usage:** also; attributive; broadly; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A thing with two components arranged one behind the other.

   **Usage:** also; attributive; broadly; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** If you want a canoe that can seat both of you comfortably, you’ll need a tandem.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A thing with two components arranged one behind the other.
   1. Ellipsis of tandem bicycle (“a bicycle or tricycle in which two people sit one behind the other, both able to pedal but only the person in front being able to steer”).

      **Usage:** abbreviation; also; alt-of; attributive; broadly; countable; ellipsis; specifically; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** cycling; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

**Source sense R1.S5**

   **Usage:** also; attributive; broadly; countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A group of two or more machines, people, etc., working together; hence (uncountable), close collaboration.

   **Usage:** also; attributive; countable; figuratively

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**
