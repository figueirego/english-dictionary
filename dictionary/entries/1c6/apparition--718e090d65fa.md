### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An act of becoming visible; appearance; visibility.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The thing appearing; a visible object; a form.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An unexpected, wonderful, or preternatural appearance; especially, one by a ghost, phantom, or suchlike; the entity itself.

   **Example:** The region's economy is buoyed by tourism driven by past apparitions of the Virgin Mary.

   **Example:** The attic is said to be haunted by the ghostly apparition of a young girl who died there.

   **Example:** He spoke to the apparition, and although its lips did not move, he somehow heard its reply.

   **Source synonyms:** vision

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The first appearance of a star or other luminary after having been invisible or obscured; opposed to occultation.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astronomy; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A period of consecutive days or nights when a particular celestial body may be observed, beginning with the heliacal rising of the body and ending with its heliacal setting.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astronomy; natural-sciences
