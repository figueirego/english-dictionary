### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To remove, whether bit by bit or suddenly.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** When we stripped away the old wallpaper, we discovered an interesting mural painted on the wall.

   **Example:** The government has stripped away some of our basic rights by passing a series of controversial laws.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To discard or disregard; to ignore (a factor that obscures the reality).

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** If you strip away the hype, you find that he is just an ordinary person trying to make a living, like everyone else.
