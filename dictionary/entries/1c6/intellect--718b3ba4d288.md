### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The faculty of thinking, judging, abstract reasoning, and conceptual understanding; the cognitive faculty.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Intellect is one of man's greatest powers.

   **Source synonyms:** brain; brains (colloquial); cleverness; head; brightness; esprit; intellect; intelligence; mind; nous; psyche; reason; smartness; smarts (colloquial); wit; judgment

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The capacity of that faculty (in a particular person).

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** They were chosen because of their outstanding intellect.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A person who has that faculty to a great degree.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Some of the world's leading intellects were meeting there.

   **Source synonyms:** intellectual
