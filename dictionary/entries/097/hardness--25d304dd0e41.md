### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The quality of being hard.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An instance of this quality; hardship.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The quantity of calcium carbonate dissolved in water, usually expressed in parts per million (ppm).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; inorganic-chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The resistance to scratching, cutting, indentation or abrasion of a metal or other solid material.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The penetrating ability of electromagnetic radiation, such as x-rays; generally, the shorter the wavelength, the harder and more penetrating the radiation.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The measure of resistance to damage of a facility, equipment, installation, or telecommunications infrastructure when subjected to attack.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A measure of how hard a material is.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** The hardness of the material was high.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences
