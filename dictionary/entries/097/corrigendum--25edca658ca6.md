### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An error that is to be corrected in a printed work after publication.

   **Source synonyms:** erratum

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A list of errors in a printed work as a separate page of corrections. (The items thus listed will sometimes be corrected in subsequent print runs, if any occur; they are then called reprint corrections.)

   **Usage:** plural-normally

   **Example:** errata and corrigenda

   **Source synonyms:** errata
