## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A commissioned officer of the navy whose rank is immediately below that of a lieutenant.
   1. A commissioned officer of the Royal Navy ranking above a midshipman and below a lieutenant.

      **Usage:** UK

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; navy; politics; war

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   1. A commissioned officer of the navy ranking above an acting sub-lieutenant and below a lieutenant.
