### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A period of three months or about three months; (financial)&#58; quarter.

   **Example:** She is in the second trimester of her first pregnancy.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One of the terms of an academic year in those learning institutions that divide their teaching in three roughly equal terms, each about three months long. Compare semester.

   **Example:** The school operates on a trimester schedule.

   **Source synonyms:** trisem
