### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Behavior that is intended to seem humble but comes across as fake and unflattering.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Although having a large ego is considered undesirable, at times it is proper to take credit where it is due rather than display false modesty.
