### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A practice of communicating with one's God, or with some spiritual entity.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Through prayer I ask for God's guidance.

   **Example:** In many cultures, prayer involves singing.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An act of praying.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The specific words or methods used for praying.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Christians recite the Lord's Prayer.

   **Example:** For Baha'is, there's a difference between obligatory prayers and devotional prayers.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A meeting held for the express purpose of praying.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Grandpa never misses a chance to go to prayer.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A request; a petition.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** This, your honor, is my prayer; that all here be set free.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The remotest hope or chance.

   **Usage:** countable; singular; uncountable

   **Example:** That team doesn't have a prayer of winning the championship.

   **Source context (verbatim):** in negative constructions
