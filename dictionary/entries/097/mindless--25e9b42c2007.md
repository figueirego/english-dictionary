### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Showing a lack of forethought or sense.

   **Source synonyms:** brainless; unmindful; senseless; thoughtless

   **Source antonyms:** mindful; thoughtful

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Having no sensible meaning or purpose.

   **Example:** An act of mindless violence.

   **Source synonyms:** senseless

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Heedless.

   **Source synonyms:** unmindful; absent-minded; thoughtless

   **Source antonyms:** mindful

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Overly repetitive and unchallenging, not requiring any careful attention or providing any significant stimulation.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a thing done

   **Source synonyms:** automatic; rote; dull

   **Source antonyms:** engrossing

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Lacking a mind.

   **Example:** Atheism alleges that mindless scientific forces control the universe.

   **Source synonyms:** brainless; unconscious; unsentient

   **Source antonyms:** conscious; sentient
