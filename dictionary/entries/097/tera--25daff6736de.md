### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In the International System of Units and other metric systems of units, multiplying the unit to which it is attached by 10¹² (short scale trillion or long scale billion).

   **Usage:** morpheme

   **Example:** a 2-terawatt increase

   **Source synonyms:** mul&#58;T

   **Source antonyms:** pico-

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. tebi- (2⁴⁰ = 1024⁴).

   **Usage:** morpheme; proscribed; but still in common use

   **Example:** a 2-terabyte hard drive

   **Source synonyms:** mul&#58;Ti-
