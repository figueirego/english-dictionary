### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To insert something (or oneself) between other things.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to interpose a screen between the eye and the light

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To interrupt a conversation by introducing a different subject or making a comment.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To offer (one's help or services).

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To be inserted between parts or things; to come between.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To intervene in a dispute, or in a conversation.

   **Usage:** intransitive
