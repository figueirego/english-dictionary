### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To open the mouth widely and take a long, rather deep breath, often because one is tired or bored, and sometimes accompanied by pandiculation.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** I could see my students yawning, so I knew the lesson was boring them.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To say while yawning.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To present a wide opening; gape.

   **Example:** The canyon yawns as it has done for millions of years, and we stand looking, dumbstruck.

   **Example:** Death yawned before us, and I hit the brakes.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To open the mouth, or to gape, through surprise or bewilderment.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To be eager; to desire to swallow anything; to express desire by yawning.

   **Usage:** obsolete

   **Example:** to yawn for fat livings
