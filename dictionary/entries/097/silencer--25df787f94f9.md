### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Something that silences or (notionally) nearly silences.
   1. An attachment to a gun that reduces the sound it emits.

      **Source synonyms:** suppressor

      **Source antonyms:** loudener

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Something that silences or (notionally) nearly silences.
   1. The muffler of an internal combustion engine.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Something that silences or (notionally) nearly silences.
   1. Any of various devices to silence the humming noise of telegraph wires.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Something that silences or (notionally) nearly silences.
   1. Something that prevents or greatly reduces gene expression.
