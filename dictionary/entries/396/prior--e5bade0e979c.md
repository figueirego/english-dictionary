**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Usage:** not-comparable

2. More important or significant.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Chiefly in prior probability&#58; of the probability of an event&#58; determined without knowledge of the occurrence of other events that bear on it, before additional data is collected.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; Bayesian statistics
