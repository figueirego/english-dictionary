### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To take away by force; to carry away (a human being) wrongfully and usually with violence or deception; to kidnap.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to abduct children

   **Example:** I was abducted by aliens.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
