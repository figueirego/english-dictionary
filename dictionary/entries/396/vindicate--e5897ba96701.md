### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To clear of an accusation, suspicion or criticism.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to vindicate someone's honor

   **Source synonyms:** absolve; clear; exonerate; acquittance (obsolete); assoil (archaic); assoilzie (Scotland); acquit; absolve; clear; disculp (rare); disculpate; exculpate; exonerate; vindicate

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To justify by providing evidence.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to vindicate a right, claim or title

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To maintain or defend (a cause) against opposition.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to vindicate the rights of labor movement in developing countries

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To be proven reasonable, correct, or justified.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To provide justification for.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The violent history of the suspect vindicated the use of force by the police.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To lay claim to; to assert a right to; to claim.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To liberate; to set free; to deliver.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To avenge; to punish.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

   **Example:** a war to vindicate infidelity
