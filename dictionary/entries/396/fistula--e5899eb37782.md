### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An abnormal connection or passageway between organs or vessels that normally do not connect.

   **Example:** communicating fistula

   **Example:** blind fistula

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

   **Usage:** rare

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
