### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The degree of control that the elevators can exert over the pitching motion of an aircraft; related to elevator design and airspeed.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** The airspeed was so low that there was insufficient elevator authority to recover from the stall.
