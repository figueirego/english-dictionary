### Senses

<a id="r4-s1"></a>

**Source sense R4.S1**

1. To employ delaying tactics against.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He stalled the creditors as long as he could.

<a id="r4-s2"></a>

**Source sense R4.S2**

2. To employ delaying tactics; to stall for time.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Soon it became clear that she was stalling to give him time to get away.

<a id="r4-s3"></a>

**Source sense R4.S3**

3. To come to a standstill.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r4-s4"></a>

**Source sense R4.S4**

4. To cause to stop making progress; to hinder; to slow down; to delay or forestall.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r4-s5"></a>

**Source sense R4.S5**

5. To exceed the critical angle of attack, resulting in loss of lift.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** aeronautics; aerospace; business; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r4-s6"></a>

**Source sense R4.S6**

6. To cause to exceed the critical angle of attack, resulting in loss of lift.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The pilot stalled the plane by pulling the nose up too high at a slow airspeed.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** aeronautics; aerospace; business; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

   **Example:** to stall a cart
