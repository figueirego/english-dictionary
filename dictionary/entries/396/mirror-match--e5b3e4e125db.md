### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A match in a 1v1 fighting game in which the two opposing players play as the same character.

   **Example:** Alex won yesterday's mirror match where he and his friend played as Scorpion.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** video-games

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A match in any game where two opposing players play as the same faction.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** In this mirror match, both players have chosen to play as the Defenders faction.
