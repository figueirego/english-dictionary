### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A natural disaster that involves the breakup and downhill flow of rock, mud, water and anything caught in the path.

   **Source synonyms:** earthfall; landslip; mudflow

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A vote won by a wide or overwhelming majority.

   **Example:** The candidate won at 61% to 39%, which most people are calling a landslide.

   **Source synonyms:** landslide victory
