### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The loss or separation of a body part, either by surgery or due to trauma.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Immediately observable were avulsion of several teeth and extensive lacerations and bruising to the face.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; pathology; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An abrupt change in the course of a river, typically from one channel to another.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Movement of soil during a flood, or during a change in the course of a river, especially when a resulting change of land ownership is involved.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
