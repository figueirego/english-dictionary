### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The omission of a vowel or syllable from a word, usually indicated by the apostrophe symbol ( ' ). examples&#58; 'til, can't

   **Usage:** archaic

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The apostrophe symbol used to mark this elision.

   **Usage:** archaic

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An open notehead ( ~ ) or an apostrophe ( ' ) used as a neume to mark a low, unaccented syllable at the beginning of a phrase or a short quick syllable

   **Usage:** historical

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**
