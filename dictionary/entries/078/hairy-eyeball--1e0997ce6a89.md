### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A look askance at someone; a look of disdain or skepticism.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Example:** He was giving me the hairy eyeball.

   **Source synonyms:** dirty look; stink eye; see more there

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A fond look at someone while batting one's eyelashes.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Example:** He was telling me about a girl looking at him and he said, "She gave me the hairy eyeball." That meant she liked him.
