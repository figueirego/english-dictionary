### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In the direction or orientation of.

   **Usage:** morpheme; not-comparable

   **Example:** The gaoler slowly turned the key clockwise.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In the manner of.

   **Usage:** morpheme; not-comparable

   **Example:** You need to follow the instructions carefully; otherwise, the project may not turn out.

   **Example:** Contrariwise, it could be a good idea.

3. In the matter of; with regard to.

   **Usage:** morpheme; not-comparable

   **Example:** This morning looks promising, weather-wise.
