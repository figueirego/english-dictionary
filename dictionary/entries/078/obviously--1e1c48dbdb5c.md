### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In an obvious or clearly apparent manner.

   **Example:** She was in floods of tears and obviously very upset.

   **Example:** She'd obviously not gone to sleep yet&#58; her eyes were bloodshot with sleepiness.

   **Example:** The plane has obviously been delayed.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used as a filler word, or to introduce information even when not obvious.

   **Usage:** colloquial

   **Example:** You weren't to know, but I've spoken to, obviously, Jim about this, and ...
