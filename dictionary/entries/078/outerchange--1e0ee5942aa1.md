### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A connection between two stations within walking distance of each other but which are not physically connected; used especially where a journey involving such a connection can be done on a single ticket.

   **Usage:** London; UK

   **Example:** The five minute walk between Northwick Park and Kenton tube stations is a valid outerchange.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** rail-transport; railways; transport
