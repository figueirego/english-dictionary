### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To design a new process or mechanism.

   **Example:** After weeks of hard work, I invented a new way to alphabetize matchbooks.

   **Source synonyms:** conceive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To create something fictional for a particular purpose.

   **Example:** I knew I had to invent an excuse, and quickly.

   **Example:** We need a name to put in this form, so let's just invent one.

   **Source synonyms:** make up; [manufacture](../3b4/manufacture--ed0a3b494b3c.md); spin

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To come upon; to find; to discover.

   **Usage:** obsolete
