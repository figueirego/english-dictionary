### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A member of a race of folkloric beings from Orkney and Shetland; cognate to the Scandinavian troll.
   1. “hidden people” (Faroese huldufólk, Norwegian huldrefolk), subterraneans (Norwegian underjordiske, Swedish underjordiska, Gutnish di sma undar jordi), fairies, “troll-folk”, and thereof (Swedish oknytt)

      **Usage:** Orkney; Shetland; countable; dated; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** arts; folklore; history; human-sciences; literature; media; mysticism; mythology; philosophy; publishing; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A member of a race of folkloric beings from Orkney and Shetland; cognate to the Scandinavian troll.
   1. ghost; cognate to Scandinavian folklore of gnomes, wights, trolls, etc, being supernatural ghosts

      **Usage:** Orkney; Shetland; countable; dated; uncountable
