### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The nearly flat part of the shell structure of a tortoise or other animal, similar in composition to the carapace.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences; zoology

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A half-jacket worn under the jacket for padding or for safety.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** fencing; government; hobbies; lifestyle; martial-arts; military; politics; sports; war

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A man's shirt-bosom.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An ornamental front panel on a woman's bodice.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A breastplate.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A film of air trapped by specialized hairs against the body of an aquatic insect, and which acts as an external gill.

   **Example:** The plastron of a diving beetle is not directly a source of oxygen, but acts as a gill, acquiring oxygen from the surrounding water.
