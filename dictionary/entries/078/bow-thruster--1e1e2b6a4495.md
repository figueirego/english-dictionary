### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A propulsion unit mounted in or on the bow of a vehicle.
   1. A sideways-pointing thruster mounted in a tunnel in the bow or under the bow in a pod, for increased maneuverability.
