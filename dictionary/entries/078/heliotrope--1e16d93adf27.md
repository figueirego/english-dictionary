### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A plant with flowers which turn to face and follow the sun, such as (archaic) marigolds and sunflowers.

   **Usage:** also; countable; figuratively

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences

   **Source synonyms:** heliotropian (obsolete); turnsole

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A plant with flowers which turn to face and follow the sun, such as (archaic) marigolds and sunflowers.
   1. A plant of the genus Heliotropium, especially a common heliotrope (Heliotropium arborescens), which has clusters of purple flowers with a strong fragrance.

      **Usage:** also; countable; figuratively; specifically; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences

      **Source synonyms:** heliotropian (obsolete); turnsole; cherry pie; garden heliotrope

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A plant with flowers which turn to face and follow the sun, such as (archaic) marigolds and sunflowers.
   1. With a qualifying word&#58; any of various plants resembling those of the genus Heliotropium.

      **Usage:** also; countable; figuratively; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences

      **Source synonyms:** heliotropian (obsolete); turnsole

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The fragrance of Heliotropium arborescens flowers, or a scent resembling this fragrance.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A light purple or violet colour like that of Heliotropium arborescens flowers.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. An instrument that uses a mirror to reflect sunlight for purposes such as signalling, or (surveying) triangulation (where the reflected light is detected by another surveyor positioned some distance away).

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source synonyms:** sematrope (dated); helio

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. An ancient type of sundial consisting of a bowl with a perpendicular gnomon mounted in the centre.

   **Usage:** countable; historical

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Synonym of bloodstone (“a green chalcedony that is sprinkled with red spots or veins of hematite”).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; geography; geology; mineralogy; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

   **Source synonyms:** bloodstone (synonym; synonym-of)
