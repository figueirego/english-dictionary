### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To acquire, or attempt to acquire knowledge or an ability to do something.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To attend a course or other educational activity.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To gain knowledge from a bad experience so as to improve.

   **Example:** learn from one's mistakes

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To study.

   **Example:** I learn medicine.

   **Example:** They learn psychology.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To come to know; to become informed of; to find out.

   **Example:** He just learned that he will be sacked.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To teach.

   **Usage:** now only in non-standard speech and dialects

   **Example:** Give him a clip round the ear. That'll learn him!

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** study
