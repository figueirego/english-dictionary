### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make (something) perpetual; to make (something) continue for an indefinite time; also, to preserve (something) from extinction or oblivion.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** eternalize; eternize; immortalize; perpetuate

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To make (something) perpetual; to make (something) continue for an indefinite time; also, to preserve (something) from extinction or oblivion.
   1. To record (the testimony of a witness) which may be lost before a matter comes to trial.

      **Usage:** transitive

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

      **Source synonyms:** eternalize; eternize; immortalize; perpetuate

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To prolong the existence of (something) by repetition; to reinforce.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** continue
