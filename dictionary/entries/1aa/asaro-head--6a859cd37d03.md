**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A three-dimensional simplified, geometric sculpture of the human head used by artists to understand how light and shadow fall on facial planes; a method of revealing volume and form for more realistic drawing and painting, rather than just copying features; breaks the complex head shape into flat, angular surfaces.

   **Example:** The most basic 3D model of a human head is the Asaro head.
