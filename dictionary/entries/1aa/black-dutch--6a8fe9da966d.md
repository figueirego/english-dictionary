### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A European with dark hair, eyes, and skin, especially one from or claiming to be from Germany or the Netherlands, sometimes (derogatory, ethnic slur) with disparaging racial overtones.

   **Usage:** US; historical; obsolete

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person passing for such a European, especially Native Americans or people from multiracial families.

   **Usage:** US; historical; obsolete

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A Dutch citizen with sub-Saharan African ancestry.

   **Source synonyms:** Afro-Dutch

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A member of the Sinti ethnic group in North America, especially those of German-Sinti ancestry; an American-Sinti person.

   **Usage:** US
