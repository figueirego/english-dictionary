### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The part of a church in front of the choir, often reserved for the clergy and choristers.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The division of a choir, that is divided as in some Spanish churches, that is further away from the sanctuary.
