### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To communicate, usually by means of speech.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Let's sit down and talk.

   **Example:** Although I don't speak Chinese, I managed to talk with the villagers using signs and gestures.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To discuss; to talk about.

   **Usage:** informal; transitive

   **Example:** They sat down to talk business.

   **Example:** That's enough about work, let's talk holidays!

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To speak (a certain language).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** We talk French sometimes.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Used to emphasise the importance, size, complexity etc. of the thing mentioned.

   **Usage:** informal; transitive

   **Example:** Are you interested in the job? They're talking big money.

   **Example:** We're not talking rocket science here&#58; it should be easy.

   **Source context (verbatim):** used in progressive tenses

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To confess, especially implicating others.

   **Usage:** intransitive; slang

   **Example:** Suppose he talks?

   **Example:** She can be relied upon not to talk.

   **Example:** They tried to make me talk.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To criticize someone for something of which one is guilty oneself.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** I am not the one to talk.

   **Example:** She is a fine one to talk.

   **Example:** You should talk.

   **Example:** Look who's talking.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To gossip; to create scandal.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** People will talk.

   **Example:** Aren't you afraid the neighbours will talk?

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To manifest outwardly in speech, as opposed to reality or action.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To influence someone to express something, especially a particular stance or viewpoint or in a particular manner.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** That's not like you at all, Jared. The drugs are talking. Snap out of it!

   **Source context (verbatim):** used in progressive tenses

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A conversation or discussion; usually serious, but informal.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** We need to have a talk about your homework.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. A lecture.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** There is a talk on Shakespeare tonight.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. Gossip; rumour.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** There's been talk lately about the two of them.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. A major topic of social discussion.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; preceded by the; often qualified by a following of; preceded by the; often qualified by a following of

   **Example:** She is the talk of the day.

   **Example:** The musical is the talk of the town.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. A customary conversation by parent(s) or guardian(s) with their (often teenage) child about a reality of life; in particular&#58;
   1. A customary conversation in which parent(s) explain sexual intercourse to their child.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable; preceded by the

      **Example:** Have you had the talk with Jay yet? I found a condom in his room.

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. A customary conversation by parent(s) or guardian(s) with their (often teenage) child about a reality of life; in particular&#58;
   1. A customary conversation in which the parent(s) of a black child explain the racism and violence they may face, especially when interacting with police, and strategies to manage it.

      **Usage:** US; countable; uncountable; preceded by the

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

**Source sense R2.S7**

7. Empty boasting, promises or claims.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** The party leader's speech was all talk.

   **Source context (verbatim):** not preceded by an article

<a id="r2-s8"></a>

**Source sense R2.S8**

8. Meeting to discuss a particular matter.

   **Usage:** countable; plural-normally; uncountable

   **Example:** The leaders of the G8 nations are currently in talks over nuclear weapons.

   **Source synonyms:** conference; debate; discussion; meeting
