### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An opponent or enemy.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One who antagonizes or stirs.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A chemical that binds to a receptor but does not produce a physiological response, blocking the action of other agonist chemicals, such as endogenous chemical messengers.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biochemistry; biology; chemistry; microbiology; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

   **Source synonyms:** blocker

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The main character or force opposing the protagonist in a literary work or drama.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** authorship; broadcasting; communications; film; journalism; literature; media; publishing; television; writing

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A muscle that acts in opposition to another.

   **Example:** A flexor, which bends a part, is the antagonist of an extensor, which extends it.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; medicine; sciences
