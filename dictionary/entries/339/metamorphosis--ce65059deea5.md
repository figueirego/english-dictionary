### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A transformation, such as one performed by magic.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** development; transfiguration; transmutation; conversion; development; metamorphosis; transfiguration; transfigurement; transformation; transmogrification; transmutation; sea change

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A noticeable change in character, appearance, function, or condition.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A change in the form and often habits of an animal after the embryonic stage during normal development (e.g. the transformation of a caterpillar into a butterfly or a tadpole into a frog).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A change, usually degenerative, in the structure of a specific body tissue.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; pathology; sciences
