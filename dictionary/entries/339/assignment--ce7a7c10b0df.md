**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of assigning; the allocation of a job or a set of tasks.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** This flow chart represents the assignment of tasks in our committee.

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The categorization of something as belonging to a specific category.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** We should not condone the assignment of asylum seekers to that of people smugglers.

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The categorization of something as belonging to a specific category.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An assigned task.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The assignment the department gave him proved to be quite challenging.

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A position to which one is assigned.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Unbeknownst to Mr Smith, his new assignment was in fact a demotion.
