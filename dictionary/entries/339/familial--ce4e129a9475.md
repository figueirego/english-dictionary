## Adjective

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /fəˈmɪljəl/ — source labels: General-American; Received-Pronunciation

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of or pertaining to a human family.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Mark had to leave work due to familial obligations.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Pertaining to a taxon at the rank of family.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Having milky latex is a familial characteristic of Apocynaceae.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences; taxonomy

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of or pertaining to any grouping of things referred to as a family.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** "Grandfather", "mother", and "brother" are some English familial terms.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Inherited.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** fatal familial insomnia

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; pathology; sciences
