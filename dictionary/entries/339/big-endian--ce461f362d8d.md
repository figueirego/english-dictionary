## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Storing the most significant byte of a multibyte number at a lower address than the least significant byte; that is, "big end" first.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Transmitting the most significant byte of a multibyte number before transmitting the least significant byte; that is, "big end" first.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; networking; physical-sciences; sciences
