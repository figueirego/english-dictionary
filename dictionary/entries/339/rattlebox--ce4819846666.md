## Noun

### Forms

- rattleboxes — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A toy or instrument that makes a rattle sound; a rattle.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An American herb (Crotalaria sagittalis), whose ripe seeds rattle in the inflated pod.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any species of Crotalaria, a genus of yellow-flowered herbs, with inflated, many-seeded pods.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An ornamental shrub (Sesbania punicea) native to Brazil, Argentina, Paraguay and Uruguay.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Any species of Rhinanthus, a genus of annual hemiparasitic herbaceous plants.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Senna covesii, a perennial subshrub native to the United States and Mexico.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A species of moth, (Utetheisa ornatrix), that feeds on Crotalaria.
