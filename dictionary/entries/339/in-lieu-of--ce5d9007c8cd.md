### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Instead of; in place of.

   **Example:** They gave him a cash award, in lieu of the promised prize package.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In light of.

   **Usage:** proscribed

   **Example:** In lieu of recent events, more caution is needed.
