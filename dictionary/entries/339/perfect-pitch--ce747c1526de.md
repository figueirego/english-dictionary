## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The ability to identify a note by name on hearing it, or to re-create a specified note from memory, without the benefit of an externally provided reference pitch.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

   **Source synonyms:** absolute pitch

   **Source antonyms:** tone-deafness

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. By extension, a reliable innate sense of exactly what is true, appropriate, or needed in a given particular situation.

   **Usage:** figuratively; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The exact pitch of a note, described by its frequency in vibrations per second.

   **Usage:** countable; uncommon; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music
