### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To rob at gunpoint.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** I think they intend to stick up the bank.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To be prominent; to point upwards.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** No matter how much I brush it, my hair always sticks up.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To speak or act in defence (of).

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Don't let them push you around, stick up for yourself.

   **Source context (verbatim):** with for

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To defy, to confront, to stand up to.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source context (verbatim):** with to

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To maintain a commitment (as, to a proposition or role)

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source context (verbatim):** with to

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see stick, up. To put or post up by sticking.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Stick up the postcard with a bit of tape.
