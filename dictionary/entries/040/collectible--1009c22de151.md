**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Worthy or suitable for collecting on historical/financial grounds, or for meeting a personal aesthetic.

   **Example:** collectible stamps, collectible china, collectible Beatles memorabilia

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Rightfully subject to payment.

   **Example:** a collectible bill

<a id="r1-s3"></a>
