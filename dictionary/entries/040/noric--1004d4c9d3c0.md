### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of or relating to the ancient region of Noricum.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of or relating to a racial category proposed by the anthropologist Victor Lebzelter, supposed to be a lighter subtype of the Dinaric, and characterized by tall stature, brachycephaly, nasal convexity, a long face and a broad forehead.
