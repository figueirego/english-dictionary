## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈɹəʊdi/ — source labels: UK
- IPA: /ˈɹoʊdi/ — source labels: General-American

### Forms

- roadies — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One of the crew for a musical group or other travelling stage production, especially a stagehand or technician.

   **Example:** I was a roadie for a heavy metal band for four years.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A cyclist who rides on the roads rather than off-road.

   **Usage:** colloquial

   **Example:** I don't know about any trails for cycling because I'm mainly a roadie.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An alcoholic beverage for the ride, for consumption while one is driving.

   **Usage:** colloquial

   **Example:** The bartender said he's not allowed to let me leave with a roadie.
