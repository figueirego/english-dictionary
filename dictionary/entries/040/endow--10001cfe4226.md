### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To give property to (someone) as a gift; specifically, to provide (a person or institution) with support in the form of a permanent fund of money or other benefits.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To enrich or furnish with some faculty or quality.

   **Usage:** transitive; or rarely by of

   **Source context (verbatim):** followed by with

   **Source synonyms:** begift; endue; clothe

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To naturally furnish (with something).

   **Usage:** transitive; usually in the passive voice

   **Example:** She was endowed with a beautiful voice.

   **Source synonyms:** bless; gift

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To provide with a dower (“the portion that a widow receives from her deceased husband's property”) or a dowry (“property given to a bride”).

   **Usage:** archaic; obsolete; transitive
