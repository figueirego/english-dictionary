## Proverb

### Forms

- the Lord works in mysterious ways — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Expressing confidence that a conundrum has a solution despite it not being apparent.

   **Source synonyms:** everything happens for a reason

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Commenting on a situation that has led to some unexpected outcome in a surprising or convoluted way.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Commenting that a seemingly unfortunate or unfavourable situation or change may be beneficial later or in the long run.

   **Example:** Person A&#58; It seems that I'm about to be fired from my job. Person B&#58; Well, God works in mysterious ways — maybe it'll be the kick you need to apply to university...

   **Source synonyms:** every cloud has a silver lining; everything happens for a reason
