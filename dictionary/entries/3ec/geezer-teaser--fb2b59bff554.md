### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A film, most often an action film released direct to video and primarily distributed via video on demand (VOD), for which older, well-known male actors ("geezers") are billed in lead or co-lead roles and prominently featured (teased) in promotional material, but only appear sporadically during the film itself.

   **Usage:** derogatory; slang

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** broadcasting; film; media; television
