## Adverb

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used to make a statement less offensive, by way of euphemism.

   **Example:** Your son's behaviour at school has been, shall we say, "difficult".

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used as a warning that the description may be imprecise.

   **Source synonyms:** shall I say

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see shall, we, say.
