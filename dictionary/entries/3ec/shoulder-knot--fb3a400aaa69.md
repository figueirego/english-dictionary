### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An ornamental knot of ribbon or lace worn on the shoulders of fashionable men's jackets, especially in the 17th and 18th centuries.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A similar ornament of other materials worn on a military dress uniform.
