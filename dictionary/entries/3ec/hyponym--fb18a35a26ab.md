### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A more specific term; a subordinate grouping word or phrase; a term designating a subclass of another more general class described by the given word.

   **Example:** The words “dog”, “cat”, and “horse” are hyponyms of “animal” because dogs, cats, and horses are types of animal.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences; semantics

   **Source synonyms:** subordinate
