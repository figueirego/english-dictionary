### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The condition or state of being unleavened.
   1. Of bread, etc.&#58; the state of not being raised with a leaven or raising agent such as yeast.

      **Usage:** uncountable

      **Source antonyms:** leavenedness

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The condition or state of being unleavened.
   1. The condition or quality of being unaffected by something.
      1. The condition or quality of not being controlled or moderated by some characteristic, such as courtesy, tact, etc.; immoderation, unrestraint.

         **Usage:** figuratively; uncountable

         **Source synonyms:** uncontrolledness

         **Source antonyms:** leavenedness

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The condition or state of being unleavened.
   1. The condition or quality of being unaffected by something.
      1. The condition or quality or being unaffected by sin; purity, sinlessness.

         **Usage:** figuratively; uncountable

         **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity
