### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. And... not (introducing a negative statement, without necessarily following one).

   **Usage:** literary

   **Example:** Nor did I stop to think, but ran.

   **Example:** They are happy, nor need we worry.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A function word introducing each except the first term of a series, indicating none of them is true.

   **Example:** I am neither hungry nor thirsty.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Neither.

   **Usage:** archaic

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Used to introduce a further negative statement.

   **Example:** The struggle didn't end, nor was it diminished.

   **Example:** I hardly spoke with nor listened to anybody else.

   **Example:** She had no way of knowing, nor did we, that he’d done it.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Than.

   **Usage:** UK; dialectal

   **Example:** He's no better nor you.
