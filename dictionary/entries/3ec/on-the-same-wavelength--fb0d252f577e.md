### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Thinking in the same way; having similar thought processes (in general).

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I don't really understand him; we're just not on the same wavelength.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In complete agreement; having the same perspective (on a particular issue).

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** Luckily, the members of the committee were on the same wavelength, so we didn't have endless discussions about what words meant.
