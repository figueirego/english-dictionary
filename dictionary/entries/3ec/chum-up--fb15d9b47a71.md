### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To be friendly toward (with or to) someone, especially in an ingratiating way; to form a friendship (with).

   **Usage:** idiomatic; informal

   **Example:** I chummed up with a few of my new work colleagues.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To initiate (a new prisoner) through a ritual involving beating him with sticks and swords, accompanied by music, to extort money from him.

   **Usage:** UK; obsolete; slang; transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To apply chum to water as bait.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To render a situation frenzied and confusing.

   **Usage:** broadly
