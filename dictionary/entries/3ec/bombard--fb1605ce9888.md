### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To continuously attack something with bombs, artillery shells or other missiles or projectiles.

   **Example:** The enemy's stronghold was bombarded for 3 hours straight.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To attack something or someone by directing objects at them.

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To continuously send or direct (at someone)

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** I was bombarded with WhatsApp messages after appearing on the news.

   **Example:** Please don't bombard me with questions right now, I'll answer them at the end of the statement.
