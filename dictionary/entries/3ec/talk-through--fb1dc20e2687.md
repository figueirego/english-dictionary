### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To tell (someone) step by step how to do something.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The dispatcher will have to talk him through landing the plane in the middle of a hurricane!

   **Source synonyms:** walk through

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To comfort someone as they endure trauma; to help someone consider an issue or see certain aspects of it.

   **Example:** I'll talk him through the problem.

   **Example:** She's utterly grief-stricken, so I'm going over to talk her through it.
