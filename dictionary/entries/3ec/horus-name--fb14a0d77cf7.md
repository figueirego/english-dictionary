### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Synonym of serekh name&#58; the first and oldest of the five names of the pharaonic titulary, traditionally enclosed in a serekh surmounted by the figure of a god

   **Usage:** historical

   **Source synonyms:** serekh name (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. specifically, a serekh name featuring Horus atop the serekh, as opposed to those featuring a different god such as Set

   **Usage:** historical
