## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A bayberry plant or fruit of family Myricaceae, especially species of Myrica, particularly
   1. of species Myrica cerifera (wax myrtle).

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A bayberry plant or fruit of family Myricaceae, especially species of Myrica, particularly
   1. of species Myrica rubra (yangmei)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Certain snowberry plants and their fruit
   1. of the genus Symphoricarpos (ghost berry)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Certain snowberry plants and their fruit
   1. of the genus Gaultheria, especially Gaultheria oppositifolia
      1. of species Gaultheria appressa (white waxberry)

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Certain snowberry plants and their fruit
   1. of the genus Gaultheria, especially Gaultheria oppositifolia
      1. of species Gaultheria depressa (alpine waxberry)

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A barley sugar bush (Pollichia campestris)
