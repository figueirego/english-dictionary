## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The quality or degree of having a nutty flavor or texture.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The nuttiness of the Belgian chocolates was remarkable.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The quality or degree of having a nutty aspect; craziness or zaniness.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** He watched a little of the sitcom, but the nuttiness was off-putting, so he decided to read a book instead.

   **Source synonyms:** nuttery
