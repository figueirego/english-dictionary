1. To be preoccupied with a single topic or emotion.

   **Usage:** passive voice; constructed with "with"; passive voice; constructed with "with"

   **Example:** Some people are obsessed with sports.

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Thoughts of her obsess my every waking moment.
