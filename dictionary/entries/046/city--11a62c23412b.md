### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A large settlement, bigger than a town; sometimes with a specific legal definition, depending on the place.

   **Example:** São Paulo is the largest city in South America.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A settlement granted special status by royal charter or letters patent; traditionally, a settlement with a cathedral regardless of size.

   **Usage:** UK; historical

   **Source context (verbatim):** in Ireland and Commonwealth

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The central business district; downtown.

   **Usage:** Australia

   **Example:** I'm going into the city today to do some shopping.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A large amount of something (used after the noun).

   **Usage:** slang

   **Example:** It’s video game city in here!
