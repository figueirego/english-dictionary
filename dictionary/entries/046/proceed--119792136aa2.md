### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To move, pass, or go forward or onward; to advance; to carry on.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** to proceed on a journey

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To pass from one point, topic, or stage, to another.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** to proceed with a story or argument

   **Example:** Work is proceeding at a steady gait.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To come from; to have as its source or origin.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Light proceeds from the sun.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To go on in an orderly or regulated manner; to begin and carry on a series of acts or measures; to act methodically.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To be transacted; to take place; to occur.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To be applicable or effective; to be valid.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a rule

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To begin and carry on a legal process.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To take an academic degree.

   **Usage:** intransitive
