## Noun

### Forms

- passbooks — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A customer's record of deposits and withdrawals from a savings account or current account at a bank, typically recorded in a small booklet. The bank keeps its own record, which is final in any dispute.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** banking; business

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A book that passes between a trader and a customer, used to record credit purchases.

   **Usage:** dated

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A kind of internal passport used during apartheid to limit the movements of citizens outside of their homelands or designated areas.

   **Usage:** South-Africa; historical
