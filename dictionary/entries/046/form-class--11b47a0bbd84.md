### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A collection of organisms that is given formal recognition at the rank of class with a taxonomic name, but which is known to be an artificial group rather than a natural one.

   **Example:** All fungi for which sexual reproduction is unknown are assigned to the form class Deuteromycota until such reproduction is discovered in that species.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences; taxonomy

<a id="r1-s2"></a>
