### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used to indicate agreement, often sarcastically.

   **Usage:** colloquial

   **Example:** Are you going to the party? — Oh, yeah!

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used to introduce a refutation, sometimes sarcastically.

   **Usage:** colloquial

   **Example:** (In a poker game)&#58; I raise you twenty. — Oh, yeah? I think you're bluffing. I call.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An exclamation of excitement or joy.

   **Usage:** colloquial

   **Example:** Oh, yeah, I got a new video game!

   **Source antonyms:** oh no

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Used when suddenly remembering something.

   **Usage:** colloquial

   **Example:** Oh, yeah, there was a police car outside the building for some reason.

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** yes — agreement; yeah — agreement; yeah right — refutation; oh really — refutation; aww yeah — exclamation of excitement; all right — exclamation of excitement
