### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The amount of space between adjacent text characters.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** media; publishing; typography

   **Source synonyms:** tracking

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The practice of typing a letterspace in between the letters of a word (for emphasis) or an abbreviation (for delimitation), which in current orthographies and styles tends to be limited (to only certain uses).

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** The typewritten identification papers from 1942 recorded the surname as A C K E R M A N N, with capitals and letterspacing.

   **Example:** The typewritten memo from 1952 referred to the CIA as the C. I. A., with periods and letterspacing.
