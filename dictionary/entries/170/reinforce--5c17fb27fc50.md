### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To strengthen, especially by addition or augmentation.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He reinforced the handle with a metal rod and a bit of tape.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To emphasize or review.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The right homework will reinforce and complement the lesson!

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To encourage (a behavior or idea) through repeated stimulus.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Advertising for fast food can reinforce unhealthy dietary tendencies.
