## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A measure of how likely it is to draw a false conclusion in a statistical test, when the results are really just random variations.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences; statistics

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The probability, usually expressed as a percentage, of making a decision to reject the null hypothesis when the null hypothesis is actually true; the probability of making a type I error.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences; statistics
