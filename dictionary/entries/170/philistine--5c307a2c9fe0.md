### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A non-Semitic person from ancient Philistia, a region in the southwest Levant in the Middle East.

   **Usage:** historical

   **Source synonyms:** Philistee (obsolete); Philistian (archaic; rare); Palestine

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An opponent (of the speaker, writer, etc); an enemy, a foe.

   **Usage:** figuratively; plural-normally; frequently humorous

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. In German universities&#58; a person not associated with the university; a non-academic or non-student; a townsperson.

   **Usage:** historical; university slang

   **Source synonyms:** philister (archaic); Philister

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Alternative letter-case form of philistine (“a person who is ignorant or uneducated; specifically, a person who lacks appreciation of or is antagonistic towards art or culture, and who has pedestrian tastes”).

   **Usage:** alt-of

   **Example:** It is Shakespearean, you Philistine!

   **Alternative form of:** [philistine](../014/philistine--052622c63025.md)
