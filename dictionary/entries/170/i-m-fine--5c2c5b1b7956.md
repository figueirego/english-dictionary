## Phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Conventional response to "how are you?", indicating that one is well.

   **Source synonyms:** I'm fine, thank you

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Indicating that one's state is no cause for concern, e.g. in response to someone checking on or expressing concern for one's well-being, such as "are you all right?"

   **Example:** I've just been in an accident. I'm fine, but the car is totaled.
