<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A broad sweep or expanse, such as of land or of people.

   **Usage:** figuratively; often

   **Example:** A large swath of the population is opposed to this government policy.

   **Example:** Five days after Hurricane Katrina, large swaths of New Orleans are still submerged in water.
