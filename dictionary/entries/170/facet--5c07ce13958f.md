### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any one of the flat surfaces cut into a gem.

   **Example:** This facet of the diamond was masterfully cut to enhance its value.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One among many similar or related, yet still distinct things.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** The child's learning disability was only one facet of the problems contributing to his delinquency.

   **Source synonyms:** aspect

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. One of a series of things, such as steps in a project.

   **Example:** We had just about completed the research facet of the project when the order came to cancel it.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. One member of a compound eye, as found in insects and crustaceans.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A smooth circumscribed surface.

   **Example:** the articular facet of a bone

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Any of the small joints at each segment of the spine that provide stability and help guide motion

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The narrow plane surface between flutings of a column; a fillet.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** architecture

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A face of codimension 1 of a polytope.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

**Source sense R1.S9**
