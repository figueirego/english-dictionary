## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A term whose meaning is the complement of another term, in an either-or fashion, such as mortal in relation to immortal.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences; semantics

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One of a pair of words wherein affirmative use of one entails the negative of the other with no gradability.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; philosophy; sciences; semantics
