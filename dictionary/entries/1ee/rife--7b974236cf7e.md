## Adjective · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ɹaɪf/ — source labels: UK

### Forms

- rifer — comparative
- rifest — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Widespread, common, prevalent, current (mainly of unpleasant or harmful things).

   **Example:** Smallpox was rife after the siege had been lifted.

   **Example:** Rumors were rife that the factory was about to shut down.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Abounding; present in large numbers, plentiful.

   **Example:** Red deer are rife in these woodlands.

   **Example:** Seeds are rife in a watermelon.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Full of (mostly unpleasant or harmful things).

   **Example:** Many post-colonial governments were rife with lawlessness and corruption.

   **Source synonyms:** fraught

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Having power; active; nimble.

   **Usage:** obsolete

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** pandemic — widespread; ubiquitous; filled — abounding, plentiful
