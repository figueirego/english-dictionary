### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To consider it not possible for (someone) (to do something).

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I wouldn't put it past him to spread malicious gossip about you.

   **Example:** I wouldn't put it past her to steal from shops, but I would put it past her to steal from her own mother.

   **Source context (verbatim):** in the negative

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see put, it, past.

   **Example:** He smashed the ball with his left foot and put it past the goalkeeper.
