### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To purify; reduce to a fine, unmixed, or pure state; to free from impurities.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to refine gold

   **Example:** to refine iron

   **Example:** to refine wine

   **Example:** to refine sugar

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To become pure; to be cleared of impure matter.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To purify of coarseness, vulgarity, inelegance, etc.; to polish.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to refine someone's manners

   **Example:** to refine a language

   **Example:** a refined style

   **Example:** to refine one's tastes

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To improve in accuracy, delicacy, or excellence.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To make nice or subtle.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to refine thought

   **Example:** to refine someone's language
