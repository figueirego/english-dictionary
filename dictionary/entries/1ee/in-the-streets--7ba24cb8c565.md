## Prep\_phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Aroused, agitated, or incited to widespread protest, to civil disorder, or to revolution.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** The people will be in the streets if the policymakers allow hyperinflation to happen.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of persons

   **Source synonyms:** up in arms

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In the daily life of the general public.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** blood in the streets

   **Example:** A standard pearl of investment advice says that the bargains begin while there's still blood in the streets. \[ = widespread economic pain and disruption\]

   **Source synonyms:** on the street; on the ground; in the field

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see in, the, streets.

   **Example:** There was flooding in the streets between the riverfront and the viaduct.
