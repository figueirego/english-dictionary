## Noun

### Forms

- old boys — plural
- OB — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An alumnus, especially of a public school.

   **Usage:** UK

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A former member of a group.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A man with an established network of friends who assist one another in social and business situations; a good old boy.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An elderly man.

   **Usage:** UK; informal

   **Example:** I got talking to an old boy in the pub, and he told me what the village used to be like in the old days.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A familiar term of address for a man of any age.

   **Usage:** UK; dated

   **Example:** Listen, old boy, I've got some news.
