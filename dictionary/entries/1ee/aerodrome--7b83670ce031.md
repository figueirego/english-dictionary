### Senses

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An airfield.
   1. Any area of land or water used for aircraft operation, regardless of facilities.

      **Usage:** Australia; Canada; term of art

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

3. An airfield.
   1. An airfield used for managed aircraft operation, either military or civilian, having such facilities as are necessary for operation.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

      **Usage:** UK
