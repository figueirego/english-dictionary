### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** congenital deformity

   **Example:** severe deformity

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An ugly or misshapen feature or characteristic.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** physical deformity
