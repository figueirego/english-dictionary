### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The cooling effect of wind, especially on the human body, which causes the "feels like" temperature to be lower than the thermometer temperature.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The thermometer says zero, but with the wind chill it's more like minus five.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** climatology; meteorology; natural-sciences

   **Source synonyms:** wind chill factor

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The still-air temperature equivalent to a given combination of temperature and wind speed, as far as its cooling effect on exposed flesh is concerned.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; uncountable
