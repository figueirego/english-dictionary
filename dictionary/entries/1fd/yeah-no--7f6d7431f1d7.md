## Interjection

### Forms

- yeah no — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used sarcastically to answer "no" to a question where the negative answer should have been obvious.

   **Usage:** Australia; California; New-Zealand; colloquial; emphatic; especially

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A softened negative response to a question or statement that seems plausible but is not factual.

   **Usage:** US; colloquial

   **Example:** "Look at those clouds; it might rain." "Yeah, no, the weatherman says it won't rain until at least next week."

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An agreement that the answer is negative.

   **Usage:** colloquial

   **Example:** "I hope you weren't expecting us to finish it this week." "Yeah, no, that's fine — I figured it might not be that quick."

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An agreement; the “yeah” functions as affirmation, while the “no” could be replaced by the phrase “don’t worry.”
