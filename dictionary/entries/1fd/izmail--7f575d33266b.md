### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A city, the administrative centre of Izmail urban hromada and Izmail Raion, Odesa Oblast, Ukraine, founded in 1589.
   1. A former oblast of the Ukrainian Soviet Socialist Republic, established in August 1940, annexed to Odesa Oblast in February 1954.

      **Source synonyms:** Tuchkov; Akkerman Oblast

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A city, the administrative centre of Izmail urban hromada and Izmail Raion, Odesa Oblast, Ukraine, founded in 1589.
   1. A former miskrada of Odesa Oblast, Ukraine, superseded by Izmail urban hromada in June 2020; administrative centre and sole constituent settlement&#58; Izmail.

      **Source synonyms:** Tuchkov

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A city, the administrative centre of Izmail urban hromada and Izmail Raion, Odesa Oblast, Ukraine, founded in 1589.
   1. A raion of Odesa Oblast, Ukraine, established in November 1940, considerably enlarged in July 2020.

      **Source synonyms:** Tuchkov; Suvorove Raion

<a id="r1-s4"></a>
