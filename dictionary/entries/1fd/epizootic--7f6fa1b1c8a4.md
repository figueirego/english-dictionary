### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An occurrence of a disease or disorder in a population of non-human animals at a frequency higher than that expected in a given time period.

   **Example:** At the same time as an epidemic of the flu broke out among the people, an epizootic of the swine flu broke out among their pigs.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** epidemiology; medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A particular epizootic disease.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A disease or ailment (of humans).

   **Usage:** dialectal; humorous; in-plural; often

   **Example:** Johnny's not doing so well today, I think he caught the epizootic.
