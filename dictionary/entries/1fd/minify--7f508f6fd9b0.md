### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make smaller.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To reduce in apparent size, as for example objects viewed through a lens or mirror shaped so as to increase the field of view, such as a convex or aspheric mirror or a Fresnel lens.
