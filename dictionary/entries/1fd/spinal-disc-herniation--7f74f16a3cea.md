### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A medical condition affecting the spine, in which a tear in the outer, fibrous ring of an intervertebral disc allows the soft, central portion to bulge out beyond the damaged outer rings.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; pathology; sciences

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** slipped disc. This term is misleading and is not medically accurate as the spinal discs are firmly attached between the vertebrae and cannot out of place.
