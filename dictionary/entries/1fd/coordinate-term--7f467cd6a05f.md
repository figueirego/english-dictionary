### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A term (word or noun phrase) that shares a hypernym (or hypernymic noun phrase) with another&#58; that is, one that names another type (kind) within the same class.

   **Example:** The words oak, maple, spruce, and linden are coordinate terms beneath the hypernym of tree.

   **Example:** The terms HDA, LAMP, MDA, NASBA, RPA, SDA, and TMA are coordinate terms beneath the hypernymic term isothermal nucleic acid amplification test.
