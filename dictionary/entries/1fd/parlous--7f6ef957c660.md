### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Attended with peril; dangerous, risky.

   **Example:** The situation became parlous when the weather made resupply impossible.

   **Source synonyms:** dangerous; dangersome; hazardous; hot (informal); jeopardous; wicked; forbidding; parlous; periculous; perilous; precarious; risky; unsafe

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Appalling, dire, terrible.

   **Example:** in a parlous state

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Dangerously clever or cunning; also, remarkably good or unusual.

   **Usage:** England; also; dialectal; obsolete; rare
