## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

- lappets — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A small decorative fold or flap, especially of lace or muslin, in a garment or headdress.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A wattle or flap-like structure on the face.

   **Example:** Some vultures have lappets of bare flesh on the sides of the head.

   **Example:** Tompot blennies are distinguished by a pair of lappets above the eyes and dark stripes along the body.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences; zoology
