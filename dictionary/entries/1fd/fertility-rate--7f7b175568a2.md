### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The average number of children that would be born to a woman over her lifetime if she followed the current average pattern of fertility among a given group of women and survived through her reproductive years; used as an indicator of strength of population growth.

   **Example:** In America the fertility rate of recent immigrants tends to be higher than that of native population.

   **Example:** In most industrialized countries the fertility rate has dropped well below replacement rate.
