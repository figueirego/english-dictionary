### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In a state of heightened mental preparedness for a difficult or hazardous enterprise, or one where success is particularly sought.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; informal

   **Example:** I'm getting myself psyched up to cross the rope bridge.

   **Source synonyms:** psyched; girded; steeled

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Enthusiastic.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; informal

   **Example:** Our investors are psyched up about the profitability potential of our new product.

   **Source synonyms:** psyched; pumped up; pumped
