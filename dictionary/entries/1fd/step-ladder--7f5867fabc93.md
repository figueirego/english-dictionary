### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A ladder with steps or treads instead of rungs that is hinged in the middle to form an inverted V, with stays to keep the two halves at a fixed angle.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
