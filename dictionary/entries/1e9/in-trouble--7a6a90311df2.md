**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Example:** He had a knack for getting in trouble.

   **Source synonyms:** in a world of pain; in a world of trouble

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Liable to be punished for misbehavior; in disgrace.

   **Usage:** also

   **Example:** You broke the binoculars?? You're in deep trouble, mister!

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a child or someone being looked after; used in law and crime-related contexts

**Source sense R1.S3**
