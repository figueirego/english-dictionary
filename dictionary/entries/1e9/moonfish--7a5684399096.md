### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of various species of fish of a round appearance in several families&#58;
   1. Any member of the species Mene maculata (razor moonfish), sole accepted, extant species of family Menidae, of tropical coastal waters of the Indo-Pacific area;

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of various species of fish of a round appearance in several families&#58;
   1. A platyfish, of either of Xiphophorus maculatus or X. variatus (family Poeciliidae).

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of various species of fish of a round appearance in several families&#58;
   1. Any member of most species of the genera Selene and Paraselene (family Carangidae).

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any of various species of fish of a round appearance in several families&#58;
   1. Any member of the genus Lampris of opahs (family Lamprididae);

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Any of various species of fish of a round appearance in several families&#58;
   1. Any member of the species Mola mola (common mola or ocean sunfish; family Molidae).
