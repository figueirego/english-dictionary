### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make (something) completely full.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** jam

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To fill the tank of a vehicle with fuel.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** It costs a fortune these days to fill up.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To become completely full.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To become tearful as a result of strong emotion.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To annoy, or displease, by taunting, or by excessive nagging.

   **Usage:** colloquial; idiomatic

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To satisfy the hunger of (someone).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Thanks for the chocolate cake – it really filled me up!

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To satisfy one's hunger; to stop being hungry.

   **Usage:** intransitive; reflexive

<a id="r1-s8"></a>
