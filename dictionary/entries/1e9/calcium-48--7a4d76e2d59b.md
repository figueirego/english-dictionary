## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A naturally-occurring isotope of calcium, 4820Ca, with 20 protons and 28 neutrons. It decays very slowly, with a half-life of 4.3x10¹⁹ years. Because it is unusually neutron-rich, it is popular for the manufacture of superheavy elements.

   **Usage:** uncountable
