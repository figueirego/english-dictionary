### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. too fat, as opposed to meaty

   **Usage:** or a meat

   **Example:** Protein brought out more of the muscling and red meat before the cattle got too wastey with too much fat.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of an animal

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. soft as a result of getting overripe

   **Example:** Frosted or wastey fruit will be unsalable.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a fruit or a vegetable
