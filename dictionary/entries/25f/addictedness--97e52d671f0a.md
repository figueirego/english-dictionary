## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The quality or state of being addicted; attachment.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** She refused to live in a non-urban environment because of her addictedness to used bookstores and coffee shops.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The degree of addiction.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** His addictedness was such that unless physically restrained, he would continuously self-administer the drug until he lapsed into unconsciousness.
