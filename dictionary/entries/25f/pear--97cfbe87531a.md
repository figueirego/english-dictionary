### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An edible fruit produced by the pear tree, similar to an apple but typically elongated towards the stem.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A type of fruit tree (Pyrus communis).

   **Source synonyms:** pear tree

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The wood of the pear tree (pearwood, pear wood).

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Choke pear (a torture device).

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. avocado, alligator pear

   **Usage:** Jamaica

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**
