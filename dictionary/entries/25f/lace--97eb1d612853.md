## Noun · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /leɪs/ — source labels: UK; US

### Forms

- laces — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A light fabric containing patterns of holes, usually built up from a single thread.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A cord or ribbon passed through eyelets in a shoe or garment, pulled tight and tied to fasten the shoe or garment firmly.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** your laces are untied, do them up!

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A snare or gin, especially one made of interwoven cords; a net.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Spirits added to coffee or another beverage.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; slang; uncountable

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** shoelace — cord; for a shoe; tie — cord; for a garment

   **Usage:** ergative; transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** cycling; hobbies; lifestyle; sports
