## Noun

### Forms

- dry powders — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A powder found in a fire extinguisher that is expelled to smother the fire (in contrast with liquid types of extinguisher).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A powder found in an inhaler that delivers a bronchodilator for antiasthmatic or antiemphysemic purposes (indications).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Cash (or cash-like securities) kept in reserve in case of need.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Private equity firms were sitting on more than $1 trillion in dry powder at the end of 2011.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see dry, powder; especially, gunpowder kept dry to keep it from spoiling before use.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
