### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A traditional Australian Aboriginal string bag, made from twisted bark fibres and used for gathering food.

   **Usage:** Australia

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any similar loose bag with a large strap, normally made of soft cloth.

   **Usage:** Australia; broadly

<a id="r1-s3"></a>
