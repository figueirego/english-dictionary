### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Rigid adherence to a particular sect, denomination, ideology, or party.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** They were discussing sectarianism between Shias and Sunnis.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A political system in which power is shared among religious sects based on a constitutional formula distributing public offices among these sects.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** In Lebanese sectarianism, one half of the members of parliament are Christians and the other half are Muslims.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; politics
