**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Referring to various aspects of asexual reproduction&#58;
   1. (An instance of) reproduction by the development of a single gamete (an ovum or ovule) without fertilisation by a gamete of the opposite sex; compare monogenesis, metagenesis, and heterogamy.
