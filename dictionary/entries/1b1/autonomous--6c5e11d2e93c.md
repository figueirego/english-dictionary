### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Governing itself independently.

   **Example:** an autonomous territory

   **Source synonyms:** sovereign; self-governing

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of or relating to a territory that governs itself independently.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Sentient; self-aware.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Acting on one's own or independently; of a child, acting without being governed by parental or guardian rules.

   **Source synonyms:** self-standing; self-directed

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Used with no subject, indicating an unknown or unspecified agent; used in similar situations as the passive in English (the difference being that the theme in the English passive construction is the subject, while in the Celtic autonomous construction the theme is the object and there is no subject).

   **Usage:** Celtic linguistics

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a verb form
