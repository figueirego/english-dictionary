### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To ruin unintentionally.

   **Usage:** informal; intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** The terrorists have really mucked up our holiday plans.

   **Source synonyms:** mess up; blow it; bobble; boob; crap up; miscue (informal); drop a bollock; drop the ball; err; flub; flub up; foul up; fuck up; goof; goof up; make a mistake; mess up; misdo; misstep; put a foot wrong; screw up; slip up; stuff up; stumble; trip over the wire; trip up

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To clown around; to have fun, often at the expense of others.

   **Usage:** Australia; informal; intransitive

   **Example:** It's the last day of school and everyone is mucking up.

   **Source synonyms:** muck about; muck around; fuck about; fuck around
