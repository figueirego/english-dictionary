### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Hard to ignite; not supporting or conveying flame; tending to curtail flames' spread.

   **Source synonyms:** fire-retardant; fire-resistant

   **Source antonyms:** flammable

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A substance used to make an object flameproof.

   **Example:** The flame-retardant used on the child's pajamas would keep them from bursting into flame, but it caused a rash.
