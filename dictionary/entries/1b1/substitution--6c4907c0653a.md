### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of substituting or the state of being substituted.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** In this football tournament, three substitutions can be made during the match.

   **Source synonyms:** commutation (formal)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A substitute or replacement.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Gary is off this weekend, so Jeff will be his substitution.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The replacement of an atom, or group of atoms, in a compound, with another.

   **Usage:** countable; especially; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; organic-chemistry; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The expansion of the lexicon of a language by native means in correspondence to a foreign term.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences
