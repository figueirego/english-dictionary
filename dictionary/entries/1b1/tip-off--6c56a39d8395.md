### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To alert or inform (someone), especially confidentially.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** An anonymous caller tipped off the police that the suspect would be in the area.

   **Example:** Someone must have tipped him off. \[A likely explanation for why he has disappeared.\]
