### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The Pahlavi script, a writing system derived from the Aramaic script and used to write several Iranian languages, the most common form of which was the Book Pahlavi.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A surname from Persian
   1. A transliteration of the Persian surname پهلوی (pahlavi), borne by members of the Pahlavi dynasty which ruled Iran in the 20th century.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable
