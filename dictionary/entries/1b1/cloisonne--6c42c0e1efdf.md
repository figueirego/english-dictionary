## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A decorative technique for metalwork, especially brass, whereby colored enamel is baked in partitions (cloisons) between raised ridges of the metal.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Cloisonne is sometimes seen as a cheaper alternative to jewelled encrustation or filigree.

   **Example:** Zoë wore a pair of cloisonne earrings she had bought in France.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Objects decorated by this technique collectively.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The museum had a fine collection of medieval Italian cloisonne.
