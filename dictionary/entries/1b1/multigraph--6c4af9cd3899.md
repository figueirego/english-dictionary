## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A set V (whose elements are called vertices or nodes), taken together with a multiset E, each of whose elements (called an edge or line) is a cardinality-two multisubset of V.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** graph-theory; mathematics; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** pseudograph

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A set V (as before), taken together with a multiset E, each of whose elements is a cardinality-two subset of V.

   **Usage:** uncommon

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** graph-theory; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A combined rotary typesetting and printing machine for office use. The type is transferred semi-automatically by means of keys from a type-supply drum to a printing drum. The printing may be done by means of an inked ribbon to print "typewritten" letters, or directly from inked type or a stereotype plate, as in a printing press.

   **Usage:** historical

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** media; printing; publishing

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A specific sequence of letters treated as a (phonemic) unit.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** polygraph
