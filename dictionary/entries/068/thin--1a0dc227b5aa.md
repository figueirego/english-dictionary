### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having little thickness or extent from one surface to its opposite.

   **Example:** thin plate of metal; thin paper; thin board; thin covering

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Very narrow in all diameters; having a cross section that is small in all directions.

   **Example:** thin wire; thin string

   **Source synonyms:** twiggy

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Having little body fat or flesh; slim; slender; lean; gaunt.

   **Example:** thin person

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Of low viscosity or low specific gravity.

   **Example:** Water is thinner than honey.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Scarce; not close, crowded, or numerous; not filling the space.

   **Example:** The trees of a forest are thin; the corn or grass is thin.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Describing a poorly played golf shot where the ball is struck by the bottom part of the club head. See fat, shank, toe.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** golf; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Lacking body or volume; small; feeble; not full.

   **Example:** a thin, tight-lipped smile

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Slight; small; slender; flimsy; superficial; inadequate; not sufficient for a covering.

   **Example:** a thin disguise

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. Of a route&#58; relatively little used.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** aeronautics; aerospace; aviation; business; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. Poor; scanty; without money or success.
