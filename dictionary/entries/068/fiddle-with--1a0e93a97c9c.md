### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To manipulate or toy with (an object), especially in a nervous, restless or aimless manner.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Stop fiddling with your food!

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To tinker with&#58; to make small adjustments or improvements to (something), especially in an experimental or unskilled manner.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** If you fiddle with the controls, you might be able to get a better TV picture.

   **Example:** The deadline for my report is two o'clock, but I'm still fiddling with it.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To manipulate in order to gain something for oneself.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive
