### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A methodology and activities associated with the interpersonal aspects of running a government, an organization, or a movement.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** local politics

   **Example:** national politics

   **Example:** office politics

   **Example:** Most broadly, politics is everything that humans do, short of warfare, to influence or control each other; more narrowly, it is everything they do in a civilian context to determine the definitions of their polity, state, and government.

   **Example:** War is the continuation of politics by other means; war is politics with the addition of hyperviolence and extra-⁠civilian scope.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The profession of conducting political affairs.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** He made a career out of politics.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. One's political stands and opinions.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; treated as a plural noun

   **Example:** Their politics are clear from the bumper stickers on their cars.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Political maneuvers or diplomacy between people, groups, or organizations, especially involving power, standing, influence or conflict.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Real-world beliefs and social issues irrelevant to the topic at hand.

   **Usage:** countable; informal; singular; uncountable

   **Example:** We're trying to talk about comic books, don't mention politics.
