## Noun

### Forms

- life histories — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An investigation into the details and activities of a person over the course of their lifetime; one’s background.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** His life history shows that he frequently moves from town to town, moving from one job to the next.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Information concerning a person’s diseases and medical disorders suffered during his or her lifetime; especially useful if the disorders are genetic.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The set of stages that an organism, or a species, experiences over its lifetime, from conception to death.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences
