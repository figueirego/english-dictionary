## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A mere semblance of legal right; a means to do something with the apparent authority of law but actually in contravention of law.

   **Usage:** US; uncountable

   **Example:** The plaintiff claimed that under color of law the officer had deprived him of his civil rights.
