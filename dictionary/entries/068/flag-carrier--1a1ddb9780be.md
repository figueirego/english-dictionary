### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person carrying a standard or flag, usually at sporting events and parades, previously on the battlefield.

   **Source synonyms:** flag-bearer; standard-bearer

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A strong supporter of a cause, or perhaps the strongest and most visible supporter of said cause.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; politics

   **Source synonyms:** flag-bearer; standard-bearer

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A transportation company, such as a shipping or airline company, that is registered in a given state.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business

**Source sense R1.S4**
