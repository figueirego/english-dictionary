### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A style of heavy overcoat, originally one with a short cloak on the shoulder; also, a style of frock coat with slanted pockets and velvet trimmings.

   **Usage:** countable; historical; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A type of corded tape used in dressmaking; a Petersham ribbon.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A thick woollen fabric used to make coats; Petersham cloth.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
