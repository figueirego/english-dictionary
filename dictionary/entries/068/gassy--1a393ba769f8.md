### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having the nature of, or containing, gas.

   **Source synonyms:** gaseous; gasiferous; gaslike

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Having the nature of, or containing, gas.
   1. Containing dissolved gas (usually carbon dioxide); fizzy.

      **Usage:** specifically

      **Source context (verbatim):** of a beverage

      **Source synonyms:** gaseous; gasiferous; gaslike; bubbly; carbonated; effervescent; sparkling; abubble; bubblesome; bubbly; ebullient (archaic); effervescent; fizzy; foamy; frothy; gassy; sparkling; sparkly; spumescent (rare)

      **Source antonyms:** flat; noncarbonated; uncarbonated; ungassy; ineffervescent; noneffervescent; nonsparkling; still; uneffervescent; ungassy; unfizzy

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Having the nature of, or containing, gas.
   1. Tending to burp; burpy.

      **Usage:** specifically

      **Source context (verbatim):** of a person

      **Source synonyms:** gaseous; gasiferous; gaslike

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Having the nature of, or containing, gas.
   1. Tending to release flatus; flatulent.

      **Usage:** specifically

      **Source context (verbatim):** of a person

      **Source synonyms:** gaseous; gasiferous; gaslike; farty; flatulent

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Tending to cause flatulence.

   **Example:** Beans are a gassy food.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of food or drink

   **Source synonyms:** farty
