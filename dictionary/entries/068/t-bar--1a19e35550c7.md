### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A type of ski lift where skiers/snowboarders are dragged up the ski run while they slide along on their skis/snowboards on the slope. They hang onto a T-shaped bar, a crossbar handle and a lead shaft linking to the drive cable.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; skiing; sports
