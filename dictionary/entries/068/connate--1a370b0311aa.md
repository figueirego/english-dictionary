### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of the same or a similar nature; proceeding from the same stock or root.

   **Source synonyms:** akin; cognate; akin; cognate; connate; kindred; agnate (formal)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Inborn.

   **Source synonyms:** connate; connatural; congenital; congenite (obsolete); genetous (archaic); inborn; inbred; indigenous; ingenite; innate; native; natural; to the manner born; unlearned; unlearnt

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. United with other organs of the same kind (for example sepals connate with sepals, petals connate with petals, or stamens with stamens).

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences

   **Source antonyms:** adnate

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Trapped within a rock at the time of its formation (especially of water or petroleum).

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; geology; natural-sciences
