### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The quality or state of being spiritual.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Concern for that which is unseen and intangible, as opposed to physical or mundane.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** African Indigenous Spirituality

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Appreciation for religious values.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. That which belongs to the church, or to a person as an ecclesiastic, or to religion, as distinct from temporalities.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An ecclesiastical body; the whole body of the clergy, as distinct from, or opposed to, the temporality.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** first estate; spiritualty; third sex; clergy; first estate; spirituality (obsolete); spiritualty; third sex (dated)
