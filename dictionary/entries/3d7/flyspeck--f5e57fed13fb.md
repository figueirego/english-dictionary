### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Housefly excrement, visible as a minuscule black dot.

   **Example:** The wall of the kitchen above the fruitbowl was dotted with flyspeck.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Anything tiny or insignificant.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** From the foothills below, Jean, Jan, and James were mere flyspecks, easily lost among the straggly pines that formed the treeline.

   **Example:** We passed through some flyspeck town, barely more than a gas station and a stoplight, but I never saw the name.
