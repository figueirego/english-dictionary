### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. An MOT test (an annual test of roadworthiness for most UK motor vehicles over three years old, or four years old in Northern Ireland).

   **Usage:** UK; colloquial; countable

   **Example:** I can’t drive over this week, the car is in for its MOT.

   **Source synonyms:** vehicle inspection (US)

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Any regular checkup.

   **Usage:** UK; broadly; countable

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. An aggregate which meets one of the three Ministry of Transport standards for use in road, path and sports court subbase layers.

   **Usage:** UK; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** MOT Type 1 gives the strongest base, MOT Type 3 the best drainage, and MOT Type 2 is intermediate for both
