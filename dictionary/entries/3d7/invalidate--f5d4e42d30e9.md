### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make invalid (especially in to contract law)

   **Example:** The circuit court judge's ruling was invalidated by a superior judge.

   **Source synonyms:** extinguish; nullify

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To make or declare (an argument, statement, or theory) unsound or erroneous; disprove.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To render invalid; discredit.

   **Example:** Telling an angry person to "calm down" can invalidate their feelings.
