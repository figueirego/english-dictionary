### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A follower of the Italian noblewoman Guglielma of Bohemia (1210–1281), who practiced and preached an alternative, feminized version of Christianity in which she predicted the end of time and her own resurrection as the Holy Spirit incarnate.

   **Source synonyms:** Guillelmite
