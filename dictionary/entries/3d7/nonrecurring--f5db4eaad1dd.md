**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Not recurring; occurring only once.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** In accounting, nonrecurring items are exceptional transactions that are not related to normal business operations. The most common nonrecurring items are capital gains, additional write-downs or reversals of write-downs, and provisions for planned restructuring and penalties.
