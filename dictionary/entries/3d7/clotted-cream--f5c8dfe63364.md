### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A thick, sweet dairy product, made by skimming the thickened layer off the top of unhomogenized cream that has been heated and allowed to stand. Fat content is at least 55%.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** I like to have scones with jam and clotted cream for afternoon tea.
