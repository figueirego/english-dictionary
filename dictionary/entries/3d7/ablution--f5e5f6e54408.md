### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of washing something.
   1. Originally, the purifying of oils and other substances by emulsification with hot water; now more generally, a thorough cleansing of a precipitate or other non-dissolved substance.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The act of washing something.
   1. The act of washing or cleansing the body, or some part of it, as a religious rite.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The act of washing something.
   1. Washing oneself; bathing, cleaning oneself up.

      **Usage:** countable; humorous; literary; plural-normally; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The act of washing something.
   1. The rinsing of the priest's hand and the sacred vessel following the Communion with, depending on rite, water or a mix of it and wine, which may then be drunk by the priest.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity; Western-Christianity

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The act of washing something.
   1. wudu, ritual washing of the limbs and head performed immediately before prayer or holding the Quran

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Islam; lifestyle; religion

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The liquid used in the cleansing or ablution.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The ritual consumption by the deacon or priest of leftover sacred wine of host after the Communion.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; Eastern Orthodoxy

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. The location or building where the showers and basins are located.

   **Usage:** UK; countable; plural; plural-only; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war
