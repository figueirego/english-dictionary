**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Said in reply to something that one believes will never happen.

   **Usage:** New-Zealand; idiomatic; informal

   **Example:** I’m going to get into work on time today. — That’ll be the day!

   **Source synonyms:** that'll be the frosty Friday
