## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A city in northwestern Albania (the country's fifth largest), located on a plain between the marshlands of Lake Shkodër and the foothills of the Albanian Alps, surrounded on three sides by the rivers Kir, Drin and Buna; it is the seat of its eponymous county, municipality and administrative unit.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A county of Albania.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A municipality of the county of Shkodër, Albania.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An administrative unit of the municipality of Shkodër in the county of Shkodër, Albania.
