### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An adjective that points out which person, object or concept is being referred to; whether it is singular or plural; and whether it is near or far from the speaker or writer.

   **Example:** In the sentence I like this music, the word this is a demonstrative adjective.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** grammar; human-sciences; linguistics; sciences
