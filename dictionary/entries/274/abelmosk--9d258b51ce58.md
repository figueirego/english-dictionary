<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The edible and aromatic seed pods (properly, capsules) of the Abelmoschus moschatus.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The tropical evergreen shrub Abelmoschus moschatus itself.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

   **Usage:** countable; plural-normally; uncommon; uncountable
