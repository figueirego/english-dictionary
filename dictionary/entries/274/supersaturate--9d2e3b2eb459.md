**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To cause a solution to have more solute dissolved in it than it can stably contain at current conditions.

   **Example:** In science class the teacher prepared a supersaturated solution by cooling a saturated solution. He then added a small seed crystal and a large precipitate formed in just a second.
