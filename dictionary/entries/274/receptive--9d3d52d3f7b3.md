### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Capable of receiving something.

   **Source antonyms:** irreceptive; nonreceptive; unreceptive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Ready to receive something, especially new concepts or ideas.

   **Example:** receptive to the idea

   **Example:** The patient was receptive to her treatment.

   **Source synonyms:** acceptive; susceptive

   **Source antonyms:** unreceptive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Ready for reproduction; fertile.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences
