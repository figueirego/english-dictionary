### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A young sheep; especially, one younger than nine months.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Mary had a little lamb, its fleece as white as snow.

   **Example:** Mark and Mark have raised some lambs for market, and they won a blue ribbon for quality at the fair.

   **Source synonyms:** sheepling

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A young goat; a kid.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The flesh of a lamb used as food; (sometimes loosely) the flesh of a sheep of any age used as food.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A person who is meek, docile, and easily led.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** sheep

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Lambskin.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A simple, unsophisticated person.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

   **Usage:** countable; slang; uncountable
