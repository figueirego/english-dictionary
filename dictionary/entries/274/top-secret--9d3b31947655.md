## Adjective · Record 1

### Forms

- top-secret — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Information classified at one of the highest levels of sensitivity by a government, based on an assessment that it would cause exceptionally grave damage to national security if disclosed.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** NSA's report on the Liberty incident was originally classified top secret.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A level of security clearance needed to access such information.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Pertaining to any especially sensitive closed-room activities.

   **Usage:** figuratively; not-comparable

   **Example:** Steve Jobs set up a top secret team to design the Macintosh.
