### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A place regarded as fit only for dogs&#58; a horrid, mean habitation.

   **Usage:** derogatory; idiomatic

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A small, shallow bay or inlet, usually surrounded by high cliffs, that is accessible only by smaller boats.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A type of small schooner designed in the 19th century to navigate in shallow waters and to conduct coastal shipping in and out of doghole ports.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A mine worked by fewer than fifteen miners, which is small enough that some safety laws do not apply.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; mining

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A mine worked by fewer than fifteen miners, which is small enough that some safety laws do not apply.
   1. Such a small mine that is dug independently by one or a few miners, often clandestinely and illegally&#58; a bootleg mine.

      **Usage:** slang

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; mining

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. An excavated area that acts as an access hole or that connects different parts of a mine.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; mining

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A tiny, uncomfortable hole or cell, usually too small to stand in, in which prisoners are confined as punishment.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. An underground bolthole dug to hide from enemy soldiers.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. One of the entrances to a system of prairie dog tunnels.

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. A hole that was dug by a dog.

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**
