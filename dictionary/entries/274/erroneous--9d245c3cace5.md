### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Containing an error; inaccurate.

   **Example:** His answer to the sum was erroneous.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Derived from an error.

   **Example:** His conclusion was erroneous, since it was based on a false assumption.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Mistaken.

   **Example:** Her choice at the line-up proved to be erroneous, as she had only seen the mugger for an instant.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Wandering; erratic.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Deviating from the requirements of the law, but without a lack of legal authority, thus not illegal.

   **Example:** If, while having the power to act, one commits error in the exercise of that power, he acts erroneously.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law
