### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Expressed or represented as being accurate, practicable, or not idealistic.

   **Example:** A realistic appraisal of the situation.

   **Source synonyms:** raw

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Relating to the representation of objects, actions or conditions as they actually are or were.

   **Example:** A realistic novel about the Victorian poor.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Possible or practical to achieve; based on what is real or practical.

   **Example:** a realistic goal
