### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Excessively or falsely sentimental; showing a sickly excess of sentiment.

   **Source synonyms:** cutesy; maudlin; schmaltzy; sickly-sweet; slushy; mushy; soppy; treacly

   **Source antonyms:** rational

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Feeling sick, queasy.

   **Usage:** archaic; dialectal

   **Source synonyms:** nauseated; vomity; barfy; mawkish (archaic; dialectal); nauseated; nauseatic; pukey; pukish; queasy; sick; sick to one's stomach (US); vomity
