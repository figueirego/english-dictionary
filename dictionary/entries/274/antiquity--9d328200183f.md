### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Ancient times; faraway history; former ages.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Cicero was an eloquent orator of antiquity.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The people of ancient times.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An old gentleman.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The historical period preceding the Middle Ages (c. 500-1500), primarily relating to European history.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** history; human-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A relic or monument of ancient times, such as a coin, a statue, etc.; an ancient institution.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; often constructed as an uncountable plural

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The state of being ancient or of ancient lineage.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
