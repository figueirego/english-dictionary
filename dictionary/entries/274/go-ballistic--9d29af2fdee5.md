### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To become very angry and irrational.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** The guy went ballistic when I tried to tell him he couldn't return the socks if the package had been opened.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To travel to its target partly via unpowered ballistic or quasi-ballistic flight, usually following burnout of its rocket motor (of a usually rocket-powered, predominantly non-glide projectile or aerial vehicle, such as a ballistic missile).

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** aerospace; business; engineering; government; military; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; politics; rocketry; war

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To travel on an unguided or ballistic trajectory, having lost power, control or guidance (of a powered, usually guided missile or other aerial vehicle).

   **Usage:** slang
