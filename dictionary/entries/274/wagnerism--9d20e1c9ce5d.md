## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The philosophical and artistic ideals put forward by the composer and theatre director Richard Wagner.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** Wagnerianism

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An attachment, sometimes fanatical, to the music of Wagner.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The use of the Wagner Act, a form of labour union legislation.
