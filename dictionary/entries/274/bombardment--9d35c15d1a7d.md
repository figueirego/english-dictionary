### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of bombing, especially towns or cities.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The city suffered heavy bombardment during the conflict.

   **Example:** The building was damaged by weeks of aerial bombardment.

   **Example:** heavy bombardment

   **Example:** aerial bombardment

   **Example:** artillery bombardment

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Heavy artillery fire.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** cannonade

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The incidence of an intense stream of high-energy particles directed at a substance.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An onslaught (of criticism etc.)

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

   **Example:** The company faced a bombardment of complaints after the announcement.

   **Example:** a bombardment of questions
