## Phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A method of drawing attention to some changed aspect of a person or thing.

   **Example:** What have you done to the bathroom? It’s spotless. Thanks!

   **Example:** What has he done to his hair? I’ve seen bomb sites tidier than that.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An expression of frustration, irritation or displeasure in the face of careless, negligent or reckless behaviour.

   **Example:** What have you done? You’ve spilt it all over my new shirt!

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An expression of cynicism or scepticism; frequently when faced with a recurring pattern of mistakes by another.

   **Example:** What has she done this time, officer?

   **Example:** “Please, sir—” / “What have you done now, Perkins?”

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An expression of fear that a person has made a terrible mistake, the true extent of which is beyond comprehension.

   **Example:** Oh god! What have I done? I’ve doomed us all!
