## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A period of time during which a person is obliged to be a member of a panel of people who may be called as members of one or more juries.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** I can't come to work next week as I am on jury duty and I can't get out of it.
