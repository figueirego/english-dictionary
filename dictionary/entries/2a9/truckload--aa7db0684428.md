## Noun

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The contents of a full truck or lorry.

   **Source synonyms:** truckful

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A large number.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Example:** We were expecting just a handful of people to come to the festival, but then a truckload of revellers started arriving at the festival gates.
