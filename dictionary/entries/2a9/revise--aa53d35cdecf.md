<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To review, alter and amend, especially of written material.

   **Example:** This statute should be revised.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To look over again (something previously written or learned), especially in preparation for an examination.

   **Usage:** Australia; New-Zealand; UK

   **Example:** I've only just begun to revise for my exams.
