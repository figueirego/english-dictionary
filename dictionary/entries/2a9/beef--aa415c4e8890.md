### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The meat from cattle or other bovines; especially, that from adults.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** I love eating beef.

   **Example:** He was cooking beef for lunch.

   **Source synonyms:** cowflesh; oxflesh

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The meat from cattle or other bovines; especially, that from adults.
   1. The edible portions of a cow (including those which are not meat).

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable; on product packaging

      **Example:** lean finely textured beef

      **Example:** boneless lean beef trimmings

      **Source context (verbatim):** in the meat industry

      **Source synonyms:** cowflesh; oxflesh

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The meat from cattle or other bovines; especially, that from adults.
   1. Muscle or musculature; size, strength or potency.

      **Usage:** broadly; slang; uncountable

      **Example:** Put some beef into it! We've got to get the car over the bump.

      **Example:** We've got to get some beef into the enforcement provisions of that law.

      **Source synonyms:** cowflesh; oxflesh

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The meat from cattle or other bovines; especially, that from adults.
   1. Essence, content; the important part of a document or project.

      **Usage:** figuratively; slang; uncountable

      **Example:** The beef of his paper was a long rant about government.

      **Source synonyms:** cowflesh; oxflesh; meat

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Bovine animals.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A bovine (cow or bull) being raised for its meat.

   **Usage:** Canada; US; countable; uncommon; plural beeves

   **Example:** Do you want to raise beeves?

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A grudge; dislike (of something or someone); lack of faith or trust (in something or someone); a reason for a dislike or grudge. (often + with)

   **Usage:** countable; slang; uncountable; plural beefs

   **Example:** He's got beef over what you said.

   **Example:** He's got a beef with everyone in the room.

   **Example:** Remember what happened last fall? That's his beef with me.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A criminal charge.

   **Usage:** countable; slang; uncountable

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. Fibrous calcite or limestone, especially when occurring in a jagged layer between shales in Dorset.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; Dorset
