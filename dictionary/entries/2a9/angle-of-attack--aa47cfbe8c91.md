## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The angle between the chord line of an airfoil and the airflow over it; one of the determiners of the amount of lift produced by an airfoil.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** aeronautics; aerospace; business; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The angle between a mid-sail and the direction of the wind.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The angle between the plane of rotation of a wheel and the outer rail in the track curve.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** rail-transport; railways; transport
