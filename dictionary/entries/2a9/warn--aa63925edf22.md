### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make (someone) aware of (something impending); especially&#58;
   1. To make (someone) aware of impending danger, evil, etc.

      **Usage:** transitive

      **Example:** We waved a flag to warn the oncoming traffic about the accident.

      **Example:** I phoned to warn him of the road closure.

      **Source synonyms:** foretoken (literary)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To make (someone) aware of (something impending); especially&#58;
   1. To notify or inform (someone, about something).

      **Usage:** transitive

      **Example:** I warned him he'd be getting a huge box of birthday presents from me.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To make (someone) aware of (something impending); especially&#58;
   1. To summon (someone) to or inform of a formal meeting or duty.

      **Usage:** transitive

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To make (someone) aware of (something impending); especially&#58;
   1. To make a sound (e.g. clicking or whirring) indicating that it is about to strike or chime (an hour).

      **Usage:** ambitransitive; obsolete; possibly; transitive

      **Source context (verbatim):** of a clock

5. To caution or admonish (someone) against unwise or unacceptable behaviour.

   **Usage:** transitive
