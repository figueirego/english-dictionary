## Adverb

### Forms

- more urgently — comparative
- most urgently — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. With great haste, with a sense of urgency, because it is very important.

   **Example:** We all urgently need to apply solutions to the problem of pollution.

   **Example:** Funds are urgently required if this work is to be completed as planned.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Continuously. With insistence.

   **Example:** He pleaded urgently for release from what he considered to be an unjust imprisonment.
