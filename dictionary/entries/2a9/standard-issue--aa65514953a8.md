## Adjective · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Regularly and conventionally distributed by an organization (for instance, to its personnel); for regular usage; not specialized.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Most employees of this company get a standard issue laptop, but those in the art department get a more powerful model.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a piece of equipment

   **Source synonyms:** standard; stock; regular; baseline

   **Source antonyms:** special; specialized; special issue; custom; customized; bespoke
