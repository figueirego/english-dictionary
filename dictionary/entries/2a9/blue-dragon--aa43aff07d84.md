## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A species of nudibranch sea slug, Glaucus atlanticus, blue in colour and living in the pelagic zone of the ocean.

   **Source synonyms:** blue sea dragon; sea swallow; blue angel; blue glaucus; dragon slug; blue sea slug; blue ocean slug

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A species of small, floating, blue nudibranch sea slug, Glaucus marginatus, looking very similar to Glaucus atlanticus.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A species of nudibranch sea slug, Pteraeolidia ianthina, ranging in colours from blue, yellow, and green.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see blue, dragon.
