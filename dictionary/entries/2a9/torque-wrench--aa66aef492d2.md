### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A workshop tool used to precisely apply a specific torque to a fastener such as a nut or bolt; it is a wrench equipped with a torque indicator.

   **Source synonyms:** torque spanner

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A torsion wrench, an L-shaped lockpicking tool.
