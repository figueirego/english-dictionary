<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To hinder the development of something.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To revert to an earlier stage of development.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To retort.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To drink quickly, especially alcoholic beverages.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** We threw back drinks until the next morning.

   **Source synonyms:** toss off

5. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see throw, back.

   **Example:** I threw back my head and laughed uproariously.
