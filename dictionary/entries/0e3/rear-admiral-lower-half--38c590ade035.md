### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A commissioned officer in the United States Navy, Coast Guard, NOAA Corps, or PHS Corps of a grade superior to a captain and junior to a rear admiral (upper half). A rear admiral (lower half) is equal in grade or rank to an Army, Marine Corps, or Air Force brigadier general.

   **Usage:** US

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; nautical; politics; transport; war

<a id="r1-s2"></a>
