### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The lending of securities by one brokerage to another, typically for a secondary purpose such as to cover a stock short position. Not to be confused with collateral-type stock loans.

   **Usage:** uncountable; securities industry
