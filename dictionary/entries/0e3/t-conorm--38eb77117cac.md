### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A binary function from \[0,1\] × \[0,1\] to \[0,1\], which, when given (a,b) as input, returns one minus a t-norm of (1 − a, 1 − b).

   **Usage:** fuzzy logic

   **Example:** The t-conorm \#92;sqrt\#123;x²\#43;y²-x²y²\#125; is dominated by the t-conorm x\#43;y-xy, which is in turn dominated by the t-conorm \#123;x\#43;y\#92;over 1\#43;xy\#125;.

   **Example:** A t-conorm acts as a disjunction in fuzzy logic or as a union in fuzzy set theory. When one of its arguments is 0, it returns its other argument; when one of its arguments is 1, it returns 1. It is both associative and commutative, and its partial derivatives with respect to its parameters are non-negative.
