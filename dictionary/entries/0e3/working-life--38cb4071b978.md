## Noun

### Forms

- working lives — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of a person, the period of their life spent in employment, between leaving school and retirement.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of an object, its lifespan, especially a durable good; its expected duration or its actual duration.

   **Example:** Cheaper tires typically have a shorter working life than the pricier alternatives, which means that the total cost of ownership (per mile) is closer to equal although the upfront cost is more affordable.

   **Source synonyms:** service life; useful life
