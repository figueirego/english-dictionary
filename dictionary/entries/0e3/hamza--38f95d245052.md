### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A sign used in the written Arabic language representing a glottal stop. Hamza may appear as a stand-alone letter (ء (ʔ)) or most commonly diacritically over or under other letters, e.g. أ (ʔ) (over an alif \[ا\]), إ (ʔ) (under an alif), ؤ (ʔ) (over a wāw \[و\]) or ئ (ʔ) (over a dotless yāʾ \[ى\]). The exact seat of hamza is governed by an orthographic rule – the "seat of hamza rule".
