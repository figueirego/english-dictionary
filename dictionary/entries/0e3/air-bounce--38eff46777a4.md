**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A throw at a downward angle with an upward attitude causing the disc to descend during the beginning of the flight and then rise.

   **Usage:** ultimate frisbee

   **Example:** He threw a textbook air bounce, under the marker's arms, over the cup's heads.
