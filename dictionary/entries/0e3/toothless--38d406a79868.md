### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having no teeth.

   **Example:** a toothless old man

   **Source synonyms:** edentate; edentulous; teethless

   **Source antonyms:** dentate; dentiferous; dentigerous; dentulous; toothed; toothy

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Weak; having no ability to enforce something.
