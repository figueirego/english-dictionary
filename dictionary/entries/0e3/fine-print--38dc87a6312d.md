**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The details, restrictions, terms, or conditions, especially of a contract, often printed in very small type.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; uncountable

   **Example:** Before signing up for such an offer, be sure to read the fine print carefully.

   **Source synonyms:** mouseprint; small print
