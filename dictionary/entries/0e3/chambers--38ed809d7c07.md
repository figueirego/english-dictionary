## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈt͡ʃeɪmbəz/ — source labels: Received-Pronunciation
- IPA: /ˈt͡ʃeɪmbɚz/ — source labels: General-American

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A set of rooms in a building used as an office or a residential apartment.

   **Usage:** archaic; plural; plural-only

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Chiefly in in chambers&#58; a judge's private office which is used for hearings that do not need to be held in open court.

   **Usage:** broadly; plural; plural-only

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Originally, a set of rooms at an Inn of Court used by one or more barristers as an office and residence; now, the office of one or more barristers in any building.

   **Usage:** UK; broadly; plural; plural-only

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Euphemistic form of chamber pot (“a container used for defecation and urination”); also, synonym of potty (“a small (chiefly plastic) pot used by children for defecation and urination when toilet-training”).

   **Usage:** euphemistic; form-of; plural; plural-only

   **Form of:** [chamber pot](../1a3/chamber-pot--68d86595475e.md)

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. In full king's chambers&#58; parts of the sea next to the coast of England and Wales delimited by imaginary lines connecting headlands, over which the Crown asserted exclusive jurisdiction; these have now been superseded by the concept of the territorial sea.

   **Usage:** UK; historical; plural; plural-only

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A midmorning break at Eton College.

   **Usage:** plural; plural-only
