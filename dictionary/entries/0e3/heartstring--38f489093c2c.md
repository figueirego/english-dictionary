### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. singular of heartstrings
   1. A tendon or other structure resembling a cord once thought to be attached to, or to brace, the heart.

      **Usage:** historical; rare

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; medicine; sciences
