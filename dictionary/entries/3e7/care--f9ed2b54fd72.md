### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To be concerned (about), to have an interest (in); to feel concern (about).

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** She doesn’t care what you think.

   **Example:** I don’t care, I’m still going.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To want, to desire; to like; to be inclined towards or interested in.

   **Usage:** formal; intransitive; polite

   **Example:** Would you care for another slice of cake?

   **Example:** Would you care to dance?

   **Example:** I don’t care to hear your opinion.

   **Source context (verbatim):** in the negative

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To be affected by, to treat as relevant for a subsequent course of action.

   **Usage:** broadly; informal; intransitive

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. (with for) To look after or look out for.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Young children can learn to care for a pet.

   **Example:** He cared for his mother while she was sick.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To mind; to object.

   **Usage:** Appalachia; intransitive
