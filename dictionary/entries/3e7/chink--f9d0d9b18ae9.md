**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Source synonyms:** aperture

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A narrow opening such as a fissure or crack.
   1. A narrow beam or patch of light admitted by such an opening.

      **Example:** I noticed a chink of light under the door.

      **Source synonyms:** aperture

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A chip or dent in something metallic.

   **Example:** The warrior saw a chink in her enemy's armor, and aimed her spear accordingly.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A vulnerability or flaw in a protection system or in any otherwise formidable system.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** The chink in the theory is that the invaders have superior muskets.
