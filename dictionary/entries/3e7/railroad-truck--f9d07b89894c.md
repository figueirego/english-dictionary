### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A chassis or framework-like structure underneath a railroad car to which wheel axles (and, hence, wheels) are attached through bearings. (Not to be confused with railway truck or railtruck, which are British English synonyms of railroad car)

   **Usage:** US

   **Source synonyms:** wheel truck; truck; bogie (British; English)
