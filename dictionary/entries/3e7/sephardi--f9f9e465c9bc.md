### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of or relating to Jews of Iberia and their traditions, customs, and rituals. Their traditional vernacular is Ladino.

   **Usage:** strictly

   **Source synonyms:** Sephardic

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of or relating to the Jews following the Sephardic law and customs; essentially, all non-Ashkenazi Jews in Israel, including those not of Iberian origin.

   **Usage:** Israel
