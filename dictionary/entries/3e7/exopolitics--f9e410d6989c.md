## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The art or science of government as concerned with creating or influencing policy toward extraterrestrial phenomena and extraterrestrial beings.

   **Usage:** neologism; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The art or science of government as concerned with creating or influencing policy towards beings and phenomena from other universes and dimensions.

   **Usage:** neologism; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The relationship of terrestrial politicians to any future human colonies off the Earth, e.g. on the Moon, other planets, deep space and other star systems.

   **Usage:** neologism; uncountable
