## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A former continent on Earth. A proposed ancient continent that would later merge together with the continents of Vaalbara and Superia to form the supercontinent Kenorland. It is observable as a former supercraton composed of the cratons of Slave, Wyoming, Dharwar, Zimbabwe

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; geology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Synonym of Slave Craton, a sub-craton forming part of the Canadian Shield, the continental core of North America

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; geology; natural-sciences
