### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A small cavity or pit.
   1. Any of the many tiny air sacs in the lungs in which the bronchioles terminate and where oxygen and carbon dioxide are rapidly exchanged with the pulmonary capillaries.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; medicine; sciences

      **Source synonyms:** pulmonary alveolus

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A small cavity or pit.
   1. The bony socket in which the root of a tooth resides.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; dentistry; medicine; sciences

      **Source synonyms:** dental alveolus; tooth socket; alveolar socket

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A small cavity or pit.
   1. An acinus (saclike cavity) in a gland.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A small cavity or pit.
   1. The socket of a macrotrichium (seta).

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; entomology; natural-sciences
