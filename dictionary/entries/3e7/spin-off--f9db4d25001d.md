### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An offshoot.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An incidental benefit or unexpected pay-off.

   **Example:** Space research often provides a spin-off for everyday technology.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A by-product.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A fictional work where the protagonist was introduced in a preceding work or at least shares the same setting, often in a different aspect.

   **Example:** "Frasier" was a spin-off from the sitcom "Cheers".

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** fiction; literature; media; publishing

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The formation of a subsidiary company that continues the operations of part of the parent company; the company so formed.

   **Source synonyms:** hive-off
