### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A river, a right tributary of the Teteriv, having its course entirely within Borodianka and Piskivka settlement hromadas, Bucha Raion, Kyiv Oblast, Ukraine.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A rural settlement, the administrative centre of Piskivka settlement hromada, Bucha Raion, Kyiv Oblast, Ukraine, first mentioned in 1600.
   1. A former selrada of Borodianka Raion, Kyiv Oblast, Ukraine, amalgamated into Piskivka settlement hromada in January 2016.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A rural settlement, the administrative centre of Piskivka settlement hromada, Bucha Raion, Kyiv Oblast, Ukraine, first mentioned in 1600.
   1. A settlement hromada of Bucha Raion, Kyiv Oblast, Ukraine, established in January 2016.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A village in Lebedyn urban hromada, Sumy Raion, Sumy Oblast, Ukraine.
