   **Usage:** transitive; usually; task; profession; task; profession

   **Example:** The students understood the assignment.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a skill

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To comprehend a fact or principle; to regard or come to regard a belief as such.

   **Usage:** transitive; usually

   **Example:** I understand that company policy says I can't get a refund, but can you make an exception?

   **Example:** There's been no formal declaration, but it's understood that a state of war exists between the two countries

   **Source context (verbatim):** with clause as object

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To know the intent, motives or character of; (of events) to know the causes of or reasons for.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** One day you say you love me, the next you ignore me—I don't understand you!

   **Source context (verbatim):** of people

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To believe or infer, to think one grasps sufficiently despite potentially incomplete knowledge.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I've been given to understand that you have a package for me?

   **Example:** At least four people are understood to have been injured.
