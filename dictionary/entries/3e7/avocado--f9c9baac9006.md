<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The large, usually yellowish-green or black, savory fruit of the avocado tree.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** alligator pear; avocado pear; butter pear; butter fruit; abacate

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The avocado tree, Persea americana, of the laurel family.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A dark chartreuse color, like the color of the skin of an avocado.

   **Usage:** uncountable
