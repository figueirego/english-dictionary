### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A eukaryotic organism of the kingdom Fungi typically having chitin cell walls but no chlorophyll or plastids. Fungi may be unicellular or multicellular.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; mycology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The flesh of such an organism.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A spongy, abnormal excrescence, such as excessive granulation tissue formed in a wound.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; uncountable
