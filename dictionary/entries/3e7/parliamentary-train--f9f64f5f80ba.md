## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A cheap, slow public passenger train as provided for under the Railway Regulation Act 1844 (which ordered railways to run at least one train a day each way, at a cost to passengers of no more than one penny a mile, on every railway line in the country).

   **Usage:** UK; historical

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** rail-transport; railways; transport

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A passenger train that serves a line or station very infrequently (often only once a day or once a week), allowing the train operator to effectively end the service without getting official permission to close the line.

   **Usage:** UK

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** rail-transport; railways; transport

   **Source synonyms:** parliamentary service; parliamentary
