## Adjective · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Packed heavily, especially beyond normal capacity; overloaded.

   **Example:** The tray was overladen with food.

   **Example:** The trunk was overladen with clothes.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Burdened excessively.

   **Example:** The student is overladen with homework.

   **Example:** The first-time parents are overladen with new responsibility.
