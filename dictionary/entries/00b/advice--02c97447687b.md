### Forms

- advices — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An opinion offered to guide behavior in an effort to be helpful.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** She was offered various pieces of advice on what to do.

   **Example:** My kids never listen to advice.

   **Example:** On the advice of her doctor, Mary cut down on her carbohydrates.

   **Example:** I have some useful advice to give you.

   **Example:** Sushil never took the advice of his parents, but still became rich.

   **Source synonyms:** hint; pointer

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Deliberate consideration; knowledge.

   **Usage:** obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Information or news given; intelligence.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; uncountable; commonly in plural

   **Example:** late advices from France

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. In language about financial transactions executed by formal documents, an advisory document.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** An advice of an incoming settlement payment order may be given to an off-line receiving bank.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. In commercial language, information communicated by letter; used chiefly in reference to drafts or bills of exchange.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** a letter of advice

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A communication providing information, such as how an uncertain area of law might apply to possible future actions.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** An advice issued by a Monitoring Committee could be applicable in a Dutch court

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Counseling to perform a specific legal act.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Counseling to perform a specific illegal act.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. In aspect-oriented programming, the code whose execution is triggered when a join point is reached.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; programming; sciences
