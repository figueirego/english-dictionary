### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To remove a veil from; to uncover; to reveal something hidden.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** expose; uncloak; bare; bewray; betray; display; expose; find out; lay bare; luster (obsolete); nake (Scotland); open (obsolete); reveal; show; uncloak; uncover; unfold; unveil

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To show, especially for the first time.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The car company are going to unveil the new sports car model next month.
