### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any member of the genus Spilomyia of hoverflies (Syrphidae), many with coloring resembling that of hornets, some of which are predators of insect pests, others nectar-feeding pollinators.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of the genus Asilus and allied genera of large, fierce dipterous robber flies (Asilidae) that often prey on insects larger than themselves to suck their blood, the males of which resemble hornets in coloring.

   **Usage:** archaic
