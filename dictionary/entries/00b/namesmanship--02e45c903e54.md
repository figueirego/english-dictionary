## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The way names are assigned.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The art of naming things in order to influence the reactions of others.

   **Usage:** uncountable; more specifically

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The pursuit of status by having things named after oneself.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The art of name-dropping or giving the names of people who are involved with a project, in order to confer status or legitimacy.
