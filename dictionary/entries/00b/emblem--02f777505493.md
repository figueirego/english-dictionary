### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A representative symbol, such as a trademark, a badge or logo.

   **Example:** The medical trucks were emblazoned with the emblem of the Red Cross.

   **Source synonyms:** symbol; token; ensign

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Something that represents a larger whole.

   **Example:** The rampant poverty in the ethnic slums was just an emblem of the group's disenfranchisement by the society as a whole.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Inlay; inlaid or mosaic work; something ornamental inserted in a surface.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A picture accompanied with a motto, a set of verses, etc. intended as a moral lesson or meditation.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A picture placed on the field of the escutcheon.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; heraldry; hobbies; lifestyle; monarchy; nobility; politics

   **Source synonyms:** charge
