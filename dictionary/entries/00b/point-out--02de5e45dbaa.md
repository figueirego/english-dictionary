### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To identify among a group of similar subjects, or in a scene where the subject might not be readily seen or noticed, with a gesture of the body.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** He pointed out the little brown bird in the tree.

   **Example:** She pointed out the two drummers in the class.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To tell, remind, indicate.

   **Usage:** figuratively; idiomatic

   **Example:** I would just like to point out that we need to finish our meeting by 9 o'clock.
