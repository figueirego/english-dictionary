**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To provide (food or drinks) for free.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** At the conference, they laid on a wonderful buffet.

2. To provide.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To apply or implement (something).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He laid on the solicitude pretty thickly.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To repeatedly say (particular types of thing).

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** He laid on compliments.

5. To do something excessively.

   **Example:** He's going to lay on a coat of primer before painting the wall.
