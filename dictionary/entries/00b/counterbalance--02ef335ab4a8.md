   **Usage:** literally

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

- counterbalancing — participle; present
- counterbalanced — participle; past
- counterbalanced — past

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To apply weight in order to balance an opposing weight.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Big brother counterbalances his two siblings to the pound.

   **Source synonyms:** counterpoise; equiponderate; counterweigh

   **Source antonyms:** outweigh

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To match or equal in effect when applying opposing force.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** Arm wrestling is undecided as long as the opponents counterbalance each other.

   **Example:** The defenders' knowledge of the terrain roughly counterbalances the attackers' superior equipment.

   **Source synonyms:** counterpoise; counteract

   **Source antonyms:** [overcome](../0b9/overcome--2e4d65166ca1.md); overpower
