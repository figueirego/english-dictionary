### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A name used by a group or category of people (an ingroup) to refer to themselves, their nation or their language, as opposed to a name given to them by other groups (outgroups).

   **Source synonyms:** autonym; selfname

   **Source antonyms:** exonym; xenonym
