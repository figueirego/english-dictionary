### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An appearance of light, especially one which is indistinct or small, or short-lived.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source synonyms:** leam (Britain; dialectal)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An indistinct sign of something; a glimpse or hint.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

   **Example:** The rescue workers preserved a gleam of optimism that the trapped miners might still survive.

   **Source synonyms:** flicker; glimmer; trace

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A bright, but intermittent or short-lived, appearance of something.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A look of joy or liveliness on one's face.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Sometimes as hot gleam&#58; a warm ray of sunlight; also, a period of warm weather, for instance, between showers of rain.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Brightness or shininess; radiance, splendour.

   **Usage:** obsolete; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** dazzle; lambency; shine
