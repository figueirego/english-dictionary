### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Cautious, suspicious, wary, hesitant, or nervous about something; having reservations or concerns.

   **Example:** Since he was bitten by a dog when he was young, he has always been leery of animals.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Sly; artful; knowing.

   **Usage:** UK; obsolete; slang

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Leering; lecherous or unpleasant.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a look or smile
