**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any testicular lump, mass, swelling, or tumor; the term predates modern histopathology and thus (a) is vague and (b) does not correspond specifically to any current neoplastic disease entity as defined by the World Health Organization's WHO Classification of Tumours series or other major reference works on that topic.

   **Usage:** dated
