### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To give up, abandon or retire from something; to trade away.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to relinquish a title

   **Example:** to relinquish property

   **Example:** to relinquish rights

   **Example:** to relinquish citizenship or nationality

   **Example:** relinquish power

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To let go (free, away), physically release.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To metaphorically surrender, yield control or possession.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To accept to give up, withdraw etc.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The delegations saved the negotiations by relinquishing their incompatible claims to sole jurisdiction.
