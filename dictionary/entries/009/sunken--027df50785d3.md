**Source sense R2.S1**

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The sunken ship lay at a depth of 100 feet

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Seeming to have fallen deeper back into the face due to tiredness, illness, or old age.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** She looked old and thin with sunken cheeks and hollow eyes.
