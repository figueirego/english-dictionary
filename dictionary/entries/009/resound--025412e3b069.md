### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make (sounds), or to speak (words), loudly or reverberatingly.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of a place&#58; to cause (a sound) to reverberate; to echo.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To praise or spread the fame of (someone or something) with the voice or the sound of musical instruments; to celebrate, to extol; also, to declare (someone) to be a certain thing.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To repeat (another's words, opinions, etc.).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** echo

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Of a place&#58; to reverberate with sound or noise.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The street resounded with the noise of the children’s game.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Of a sound, a voice, etc.&#58; to reverberate; to ring.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source synonyms:** echo

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Especially of a musical instrument&#58; to make a (deep or reverberating) sound; also, to make sounds continuously.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The sound of the brass band resounded through the town.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Of an event&#58; to have a major effect in a certain place or time.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. Of a person, their reputation, etc.&#58; to be much lauded or mentioned.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive
