### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A certificate issued by a neutral country, usually at a custom house, to a merchant ship and its crew, cargo etc. during wartime, allowing them to pass safely through waters of a belligerent. Often synonymous with the term "passport".

   **Usage:** historical

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport
