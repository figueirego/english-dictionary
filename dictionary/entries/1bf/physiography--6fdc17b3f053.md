### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The subfield of geography that studies physical patterns and processes of the Earth. It aims to understand the forces that produce and change rocks, oceans, weather, and global flora and fauna patterns.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The descriptive part of a natural science as distinguished from the explanatory or theoretical part.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** mineral physiography
