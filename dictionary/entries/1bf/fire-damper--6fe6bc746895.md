## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A passive fire protection product used in heating, ventilation, and air conditioning ducts.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A damper which closes off an air duct automatically in the event of fire so as to restrict the passage of fire and smoke.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A mine worker who detects and clears a mineshaft of firedamp by intentionally burning or blowing it up.

   **Usage:** archaic; historical

   **Example:** Jack is a former fire damper who was injured in an explosion.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; mining
