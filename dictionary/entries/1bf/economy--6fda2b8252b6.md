### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Effective management of a community or system, or especially its resources.
   1. The regular operation of nature in the generation, nutrition and preservation of animals or plants.

      **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

      **Example:** animal economy, vegetable economy

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Effective management of a community or system, or especially its resources.
   1. System of management; general regulation and disposition of the affairs of a state or nation, or of any department of government.

      **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Effective management of a community or system, or especially its resources.
   1. A system of rules, regulations, rites and ceremonies.

      **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

      **Example:** the Jewish economy

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Effective management of a community or system, or especially its resources.
   1. The disposition or arrangement of any work.

      **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

      **Example:** the economy of a poem

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The study of money, currency and trade, and the efficient use of resources.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Frugal use of resources.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** economy of word

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The system of production and distribution and consumption. The overall measure of a currency system; as the national economy.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. The method of divine government of the world. (See Economy (religion) on Wikipedia.Wikipedia )

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The economy of God is in Jesus Christ alone.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** lifestyle; religion; theology

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. The part of a commercial passenger airplane or train reserved for those paying the lower standard fares; economy class.

   **Usage:** US; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. Management of one’s residency.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; uncountable
