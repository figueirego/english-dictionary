### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Part of the diencephalon that receives input from the thalamus and projects to the association areas of the neocortex.

   **Example:** The neothalamus contains the largest division of nuclei in the brain and is larger in animals with a well-developed cortex.
