### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To carry one's packed lunch from home, either literally or figuratively in a brown paper bag or a lunchbox, rather than purchase it from a cafeteria or restaurant.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive; informal

   **Example:** I'm brown bagging it again this month because the combination of healthy plus inexpensive is hard to beat otherwise.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To consume alcohol in public from the bottle or can, while it is held concealed inside a brown paper bag, so as to avoid attention e.g. from the police.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive; informal

   **Example:** I'm brown bagging it again today because I haven't managed to kick this habit yet.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To plant seed saved from one's own harvest, as opposed to buying new seed from a seed company.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive; informal

   **Example:** I'm brown bagging it again this year because I don't need to be spending money on expensive hybrids.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** agriculture; business; lifestyle
