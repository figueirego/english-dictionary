## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A coach-less (no in-game coaching) form of basketball, with 2 opposing sides of 3 on-court players at a time on a side, on a court with a single basket, half the size of a regular basketball court, where defenders and attackers switch during play.

   **Usage:** uncountable
