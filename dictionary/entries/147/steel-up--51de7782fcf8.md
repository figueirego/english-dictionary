### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To steel oneself (to harden or strengthen).

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** He steeled up before leaving the house.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To make steely.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** She steeled up her voice.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To gather the mental composure for (something).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He's steeling up his courage to call her.
