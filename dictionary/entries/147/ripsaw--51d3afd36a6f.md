### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A saw that is designed to cut wood along its grain, i.e. to rip, to execute a rip cut.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** arts; business; carpentry; construction; crafts; hobbies; lifestyle; manufacturing; woodworking

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A genre of music played with a ripsaw and other instruments, originally associated mainly with Turks and Caicos Islands.

   **Usage:** Caribbean; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music
