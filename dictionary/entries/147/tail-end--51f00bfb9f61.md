### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The hindmost part of anything (a person, animal, or object), the rear end; the butt, buttocks; hindquarters, rump.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The last part of a period of time, event, or situation; the concluding or final part.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** At the tail end of summer.

   **Example:** The tail end of a lecture.

   **Example:** The tail end of a conversation.
