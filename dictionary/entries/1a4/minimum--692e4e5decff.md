### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The lowest limit.

   **Example:** We prefer candidates with a minimum of 4 years experience in the field.

   **Example:** We need a minimum of three staff members on duty at all times.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The smallest amount.

   **Example:** He always tries to get away with doing the minimum.

   **Example:** Please keep noise to a minimum after 11 o'clock

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A period of minimum brightness or energy intensity (of a star).

   **Example:** The Maunder minimum of the Sun reportedly corresponded to a period of great cold on Earth.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astronomy; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A lower bound of a set which is also an element of that set.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematical-analysis; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The smallest member of a batch or sample or the lower bound of a probability distribution.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences; statistics
