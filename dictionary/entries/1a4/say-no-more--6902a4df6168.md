## Phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. What has already been said conveys all the meaning and information needed to draw a conclusion concerning a matter which it would be imprudent to discuss further.

   **Usage:** humorous; idiomatic; often

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A standard formula to end a conversation, i.e. bye, see you soon.

   **Usage:** Commonwealth; Multicultural-London-English

   **Source synonyms:** say nothing; say none

   **Source antonyms:** what you saying
