## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An honour whereby one is made into a knight, and whereafter one is entitled to be called Sir.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** the knighthood of Sir \[So-and-So\]

   **Example:** He's got an OBE and MBE, and his recent work should entitle him to a knighthood.

   **Example:** They rushed to congratulate Sir John Smith on his knighthood.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The quality of being a knight.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The knights collectively, the body of knights.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
