### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The state of being quiescent; dormancy.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The state of being at rest, quiet, still, inactive, or motionless.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The action of bringing something to rest or making it quiescent; the action of coming to rest or to a quiescent state.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The period when a cell is in a term of no growth and no division.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; microbiology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. In insects, a temporary slowing down of metabolism and development in response to adverse environmental conditions, which, unlike diapause, does not involve physiological changes.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; entomology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. In computer chess algorithms, the property of a position that is relatively stable and not liable to be disrupted by a winning tactical move.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** board-games; chess; games
