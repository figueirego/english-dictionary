## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A ball that has been hit with a bat which moves forward by a batter who is in an arms spread stance, usually in an attempt to bunt the ball past a charging infielder

   **Example:** The push bunt went between the pitcher and the first baseman who was charging too hard.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; baseball; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The act of bunting with a bat that moves toward the ball

   **Example:** The push bunt is only used in certain situations.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; baseball; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports
