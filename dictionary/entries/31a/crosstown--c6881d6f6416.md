### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Extending across a city or town.

   **Usage:** US; not-comparable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Connecting different areas of a city or town without passing through downtown.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; public transportation

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

   **Usage:** not-comparable
