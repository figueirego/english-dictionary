### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To attempt to get work or money from someone who is too weak or poor to provide it.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To practice extreme parsimony.

   **Usage:** Australia; UK; idiomatic; obsolete

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To regret; to feel self-pity

   **Usage:** Australia; New-Zealand; idiomatic

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To blame someone for something that is not their fault.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To get drunk.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

   **Usage:** US; dated; dialectal; idiomatic
