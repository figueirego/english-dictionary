### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Possessing beauty, impressing the eye; attractive.

   **Example:** Anyone who has ever met her thought she was absolutely beautiful.

   **Example:** There's a beautiful lake by the town.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Possessing beauty, impressing the eye; attractive.
   1. Used emphatically or ironically, after the noun it qualifies.

      **Example:** the house beautiful

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. That one likes very much.

   **Example:** He was a beautiful person; he would drop everything to help you.

   **Example:** You've done a beautiful thing today.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Affording pleasure to any of the senses, especially that of hearing; delightful.

   **Example:** Beethoven's most beautiful sonata

   **Example:** It's beautiful outside, let's go for a walk.

   **Example:** These carnations smell beautiful but those daffodils smell funny.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Impressing with charm in an intellectual or moral way, through inherent suitability or elegance.

   **Example:** The skater performed a beautiful axel.
