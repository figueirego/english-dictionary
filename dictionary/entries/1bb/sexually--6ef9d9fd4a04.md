### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In a sexual manner; erotically.

   **Example:** She kissed him sexually.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. With respect to sex (either of the two divisions of organisms).

   **Example:** The firm discriminated sexually in its hiring and retention practices.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. With respect to sexual intercourse.

   **Example:** He was sexually active by the age of fifteen.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. By means of sexual intercourse.

   **Example:** AIDS and gonorrhea are sexually transmitted diseases.

   **Example:** A parrot is a sexually reproducing organism.
