### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To get rid of; to dispense with.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** They disposed of the stolen property.

   **Example:** The national power committee has trouble disposing of nuclear waste.

   **Source synonyms:** consign; lose

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To transfer to another's control.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To deal with conclusively, e.g. a threat, a difficult situation.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To arrange in an orderly way.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To settle or conclusively deal with, e.g. a dispute; to dispose.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To have available, or at one's disposal.

   **Usage:** European-Union; transitive

   **Example:** The Commission may not be able to assess the reliability of the data provided by Member States and may not dispose of independent information sources.
