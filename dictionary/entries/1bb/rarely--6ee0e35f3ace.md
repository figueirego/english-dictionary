### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Not occurring at a regular interval; seldom; not often.

   **Example:** We rarely go to the theatre.

   **Example:** Rarely do you ever find an eagle this far up the river.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Unusually well; excellently.

   **Example:** After all the schooling, I can finally write rarely now.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To a rare degree; very.

   **Example:** I became a rarely good judge of the best in modern painting.
