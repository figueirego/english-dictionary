### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To rule over; to govern or determine by superior authority.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To rule or determine in a contrary way; to decide against; to abrogate or alter.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To nullify a previous ruling by a higher power.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The line judge signalled the ball was in, but this was overruled by the umpire.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To dismiss or throw out (a protest or objection) at a court.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law
