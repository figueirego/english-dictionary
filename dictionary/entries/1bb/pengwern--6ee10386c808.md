## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A former city, the former capital of Powys, Wales region, Britannia, Great Britain island. Predecessor of Shrewsbury, Shropshire, England, UK. The city was burned to the ground by the Lloegrin. The city was capital of Powys during the Roman Era.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Synonym of Powys&#58; A former kingdom in the Wales region, Britannia, Great Britain island; a kingdom during the Roman Era; which would split into medieval Powys and Pengwern.

   **Source synonyms:** Powys (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A former kingdom in the Wales region, Britannia, Great Britain island. Capital&#58; Pengwern; a medieval kingdom, successor kingdom to the Roman Era's Powys; along with medieval Powys. The kingdom was extinguished by the Lloegrin.
