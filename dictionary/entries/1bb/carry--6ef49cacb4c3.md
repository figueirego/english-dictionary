### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To lift (something) and take it to another place; to transport (something) by lifting.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To notionally transfer from one place (such as a country, book, or column) to another.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to carry the war from Greece into Asia

   **Example:** to carry an account to the ledger

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To convey by extension or continuance; to extend.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The builders are going to carry the chimney through the roof.

   **Example:** They would have carried the road ten miles further, but ran out of materials.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To move; to convey using force

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** impel; conduct

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To lead or guide.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To stock or supply (something); to have in store.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The corner drugstore doesn’t carry his favorite brand of aspirin.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To adopt (something); take (something) over.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I think I can carry Smith’s work while she is out.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To adopt or resolve on, especially in a deliberative assembly

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The court carries that motion.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. In an addition, to transfer the quantity in excess of what is countable in the units in a column to the column immediately to the left in order to be added there.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Five and nine are fourteen; carry the one to the tens place.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** arithmetic

**Source sense R1.S10**

   **Example:** Always carry sufficient insurance to protect against a loss.

**Source sense R1.S11**
