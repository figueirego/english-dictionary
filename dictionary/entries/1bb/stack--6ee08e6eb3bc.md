### Pronunciation

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To arrange in a stack, or to add to an existing stack.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Please stack those chairs in the corner.

   **Source synonyms:** build up; stack up; accumulate; agglomerate; amass; build up; cumulate; heap; heap up; mound; pile; pile up; rack up; stack; stack up; upheap

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To arrange the cards in a deck in a particular manner, especially for cheating.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** This is the third hand in a row where you've drawn four of a kind. Someone is stacking the deck!

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** card-games; games

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To arrange or fix to obtain an advantage; to deliberately distort the composition of (an assembly, committee, etc.).

   **Usage:** broadly; transitive

   **Example:** to be stacked against (someone)

   **Example:** The Government was accused of stacking the parliamentary committee.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To take all the money another player currently has on the table.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I won Jill's last $100 this hand; I stacked her!

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** card-games; poker

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To crash; to fall.

   **Usage:** Australia; US; slang; transitive

   **Example:** Jim couldn't make it today as he stacked his car on the weekend.

   **Source synonyms:** smash; wreck

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To operate cumulatively.

   **Example:** A magical widget will double your mojo. And yes, they do stack&#58; if you manage to get two magical widgets, your mojo will be quadrupled. With three, it will be octupled, and so forth.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** games; gaming
