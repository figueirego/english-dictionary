**Source sense R3.S1**

1. A high point or position, literally (as, an elevated place; a superior region; a height; the sky; heaven) or figuratively (as, a point of success or achievement; a time when things are at their best, greatest, most numerous, maximum, etc).

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** It was one of the highs of his career.

   **Example:** Inflation reached a ten-year high.

<a id="r3-s2"></a>

**Source sense R3.S2**

2. A high point or position, literally (as, an elevated place; a superior region; a height; the sky; heaven) or figuratively (as, a point of success or achievement; a time when things are at their best, greatest, most numerous, maximum, etc).
   1. The maximum atmospheric temperature recorded at a particular location, especially during one 24-hour period.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Example:** Today's high was 32 °C.

**Source sense R3.S3**

3. A period of euphoria, from excitement or from an intake of drugs.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** That pill gave me a high for a few hours, before I had a comedown.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r3-s5"></a>

   **Usage:** countable; informal
