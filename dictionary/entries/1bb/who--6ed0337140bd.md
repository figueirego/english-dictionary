### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. What person or people; which person or people; asks for the identity of someone; used in a direct or indirect question.

   **Usage:** interrogative; nominative; plural; singular

   **Example:** Who says they care? —They themselves do.

   **Example:** Who is coming to your party? Who are your favourite singers?

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Introduces a relative clause having a human antecedent.
   1. With antecedent as subject.

      **Usage:** nominative; plural; relative; singular

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Introduces a relative clause having a human antecedent.
   1. With antecedent as object&#58; whom.

      **Usage:** nominative; plural; relative; singular; non-formal

      **Example:** That's the man who I saw earlier.

      **Example:** My brother, who you met the other day, is coming to stay for the weekend.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Whoever, he who, they who.

   **Usage:** nominative; plural; singular; fused relative; archaic or marginal; fused relative; archaic or marginal

   **Example:** Who dares wins.

   **Example:** Who insults my mother insults me.

   **Example:** Give it to who deserves it.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Also used with names of collective nouns that are groups of people, especially singularly-named musical groups or sports teams.

   **Usage:** informal; nominative; plural; singular; especially non-US

   **Example:** Who are the Miami Heat?
