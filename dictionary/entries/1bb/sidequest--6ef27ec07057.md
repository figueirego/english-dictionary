### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In fictional or historical writings about chivalric romances&#58; a quest embarked upon by a knight or other person which is not the main quest.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** fiction; history; human-sciences; literature; media; publishing; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A quest given to a player which has no direct bearing on the outcome of the main campaign or story of a video game, but often provides additional rewards.

   **Example:** Completion of a sidequest often results in the acquisition of money or items that are otherwise unavailable, or unlocks another sidequest.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** video-games

   **Source synonyms:** side mission
