### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any member of either of two species of venomous pit viper found on the Ryukyu Islands of Japan.
   1. Protobothrops flavoviridis (syn. Trimeresurus flavoviridis).

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
