### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To remove air or some other gas from within an elastic container, e.g. a balloon or tyre.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To cause an object to decrease or become smaller in some parameter, e.g. to shrink

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** collapse

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To reduce the amount of available currency or credit and thus lower prices.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** economics; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To become deflated.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To let (someone) down, disappoint them, or put them in their place.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** deflate someone's ego
