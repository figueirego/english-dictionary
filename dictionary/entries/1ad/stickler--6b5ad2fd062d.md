## Noun

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A referee or adjudicator at a fight, wrestling match, duel, etc. who ensures fair play.

   **Usage:** Cornwall

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Someone who insistently advocates for something, especially for strict observance of a formal rule.

   **Example:** Lexicographers are sticklers for correct language.

   **Source synonyms:** dogmatist; formalist; pedant; traditionalist
