### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To fall apart; to disintegrate.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive; often

   **Example:** The bread roll crumbled when I tried to slice it; it was too stale.

   **Example:** The empire crumbled when the ruler's indiscretions came to light.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To break into crumbs.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** We crumbled some bread into the water.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To mix (ingredients such as flour and butter) in such a way as to form crumbs.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Using your fingers, crumble the ingredients with the fingertips, lifting in an upward motion, until the mixture is sandy and resembles large breadcrumbs.
