## Noun · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ɪnˈtɪə.ɹɪ.ə/ — source labels: Received-Pronunciation
- IPA: /ɪnˈtɪ.ɹi.ɚ/ — source labels: General-American

### Forms

- interiors — plural

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. The inside of a building, container, cavern, or other enclosed structure.

   **Example:** The gardens are just divine, but the interior of the house are even more splendid.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. The inside regions of a country, continent, etc., distanced from the borders or coasts.

   **Example:** Sir Richard Burton explored far into the African interior.

   **Source synonyms:** backcountry; upcountry; hinterland

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. The set of all interior points of a set.
