### Forms

- sence — alternative; archaic

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. From&#58; referring to a period of time ending in the present and defining it by the point in time at which it started, or the period in which its starting point occurred.
   1. Continuously during that period of time.

      **Example:** I'd known her only since the previous year, so from/since the moment we got married we’ve quarrelled.

      **Example:** I'd been working since six o’clock, and I was getting tired.

## Conjunction · Record 3

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /sɪn(t)s/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Forms

- sence — alternative; archaic

### Senses

<a id="r3-s1"></a>

**Source sense R3.S1**

1. From the time that.

   **Example:** I have loved you since I first met you.

   **Example:** I've known her since I've lived in her street, but I've not seen her out of the house since her husband died.

   **Example:** Since leaving school, I've completely changed.

<a id="r3-s2"></a>

**Source sense R3.S2**

2. Because.

   **Example:** Since you didn't call, we left without you.

<a id="r3-s3"></a>

**Source sense R3.S3**

3. When or that.

   **Usage:** obsolete
