## Noun · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The practice of jumping into the sea or similar body of water from a cliff or other high point such that the jumper enters the water vertically straight, like a tombstone.

   **Usage:** UK; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The process of (automatically) initiating software sleep mode on an app.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An unwanted effect in the manufacture of electronic circuit boards, in which a component stands up on end instead of lying flat.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; electrical-engineering; electricity; electromagnetism; electronics; energy; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. In page layout, putting articles side by side so that the headlines are adjacent (also referred to as bumping heads), or so that headline from one article is adjacent to the photo from another.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** journalism; media

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. In highway driving, a blockage in traffic caused by a semi-trailer truck attempting to pass another with insufficient acceleration.

   **Usage:** Southern-US; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The practice of leaving a marker in a location where a digital record has been withdrawn, in order to signify that the record had previously existed.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source context (verbatim):** in digital libraries

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A tombstone pattern on an electrocardiogram.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences
