## Verb

### Forms

- has on — present; singular; third-person
- having on — participle; present
- had on — participle; past
- had on — past

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To be wearing.

   **Example:** She has on a nice red shirt and skinny jeans.

   **Example:** I was wearing a bathrobe and had nothing on underneath.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To have turned on (an electronic device).

   **Example:** I didn’t hear you because I had the radio on.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To trick or deceive deliberately; to play a prank on.

   **Usage:** Australia; New-Zealand; UK; colloquial

   **Example:** Are you having me on?

   **Example:** Fifty quid for that piece of junk? He's having me on he is!

   **Source synonyms:** have a laugh

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To have (something) scheduled.

   **Usage:** UK; colloquial

   **Example:** So what do you have on this weekend? I have nothing on myself.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To possess at the point of arrest.

   **Usage:** colloquial; African-American Vernacular

   **Example:** I had some pills on me when that cop picked me up.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To possess evidence of wrongdoing regarding (someone); to press charges on (someone).

   **Usage:** colloquial; African-American Vernacular

   **Example:** They have two bodies on me.
