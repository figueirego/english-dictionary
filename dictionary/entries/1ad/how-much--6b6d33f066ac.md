### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. What amount or quantity of (something stated or implied).

   **Usage:** interrogative

   **Example:** How much (cheese) is left in the fridge? - This is how much we've got left.

   **Example:** How much of a problem will that cause the government?

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. What amount of money.

   **Usage:** specifically; when referring to the cost of an item

   **Example:** How much do you want for it?

   **Example:** How much is it?

   **Example:** How much is it worth?
