### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. At all. (Used with "can't", "couldn't" etc. to emphasize a lack of skill.)

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** I can't dance to save my life.

   **Source synonyms:** for the life of one; even if one's life depended on it; for toffee

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** excessive; informal

   **Example:** She wouldn't eat baked beans to save her life.
