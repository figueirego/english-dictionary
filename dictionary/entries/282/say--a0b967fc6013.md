### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To pronounce.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Please say your name slowly and clearly.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To recite.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Martha, will you say the Pledge of Allegiance?

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To tell, either verbally or in writing.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He said he would be here tomorrow.

   **Example:** The note I think he said she wrote is nowhere to be found.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To indicate in a written form.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The sign says it’s 50 kilometres to Paris.

   **Example:** What time does it say on the clock?

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To have a common expression; used in singular passive voice or plural active voice to indicate a rumor or well-known fact.

   **Usage:** impersonal; transitive

   **Example:** They say "when in Rome, do as the Romans do", which means "behave as those around you do."

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Suppose, assume; used to mark an example, supposition or hypothesis.

   **Usage:** imperative; informal; transitive

   **Example:** A holiday somewhere warm – Florida, say – would be nice.

   **Example:** Say he refuses. What do we do then?

   **Example:** Say your family is starving and you don't have any money, is it okay to steal some food?

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To speak; to express an opinion; to make answer; to reply.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To bet as a wager on an outcome; by extension, used to express belief in an outcome by the speaker.

   **Usage:** especially; informal; transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance; money

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a possession
