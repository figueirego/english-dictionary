## Noun

### Forms

- brake blocks — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One of two small blocks fitted to a brake caliper, which rub on the wheel rim when the brake is applied.

   **Usage:** on a bicycle

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A block fitted to locomotives or rolling stock, which rubs on the steel tyre of the wheel when the brake is applied.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** rail-transport; railways; transport

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A block (perhaps wooden) used for braking a horse-drawn vehicle.
