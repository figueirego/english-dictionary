**Source sense R1.S1**

1. All the things under discussion.

   **Usage:** literally

   **Example:** I checked the list again and everything is done.

   **Example:** Thank you for everything you've done for us.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Many or most things.

   **Usage:** colloquial; excessive

   **Example:** A&#58; What do you want to do at the amusement park? B&#58; Everything!

   **Example:** I did everything today – washed the dishes, cut the lawn, did the laundry.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A state of well-being (from all parts of the whole).

   **Usage:** colloquial

   **Example:** Everything failed, didn't it?

   **Example:** She wasn't feeling well this morning but now everything is fine.

   **Example:** Since the company lost its best customer everything has gotten worse.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Considerable effort.

   **Usage:** colloquial

   **Example:** It took everything in me to resist the temptation to skip work on my birthday.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The most important thing.

   **Usage:** colloquial

   **Example:** I can't believe I made it in time – timing is everything!
