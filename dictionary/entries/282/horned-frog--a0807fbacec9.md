### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of several large carnivorous frogs, of the genus Megophrys, Pelobatrachus, and Ceratophrys, that have elongated "eyebrows".

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Synonym of horned lizard; genus Phrynosoma, especially the Texas horned lizard.
