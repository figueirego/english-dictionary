### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Someone skilled in the writing of shorthand or the use of a stenotype machine, especially for the purpose of rapid transcription of speech. One employed in such a role&#58; a professional transcriptionist who employs shorthand.

   **Source synonyms:** stenographist; shorthand typist (UK)
