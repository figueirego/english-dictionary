## Noun

### Forms

- monkeys in the middle — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A children's game in which two players keep an object away from a third player (who is in between them) by throwing it past that player.

   **Usage:** uncountable; children's games

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The third player in that game.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A middleman, particularly in a difficult spot or situation.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; figuratively

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** pig in the middle; piggy in the middle (British); keep-away (US)
