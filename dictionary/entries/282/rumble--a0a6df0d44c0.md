**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Example:** The rumble from passing trucks made it hard to sleep at night.

   **Usage:** slang

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

   **Usage:** dated

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To make a low, heavy, continuous sound.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** If I don't eat, my stomach will rumble.

   **Example:** I could hear the thunder rumbling in the distance.

   **Source synonyms:** grumble

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To discover deceitful or underhanded behaviour.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The police is going to rumble your hideout.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The truck rumbled over the rough road.
