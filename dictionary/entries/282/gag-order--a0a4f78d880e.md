### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An order issued by a court prohibiting specified persons from discussing a case outside limitations set by the court.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s2"></a>
