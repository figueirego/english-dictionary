**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Former name of Starosillia&#58; a village, the administrative centre of Chobruchi starostynskyi okruh, Rozdilna urban hromada, Rozdilna Raion, Odesa Oblast, Ukraine; known by this name from February 1945 until September 2024.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Former name of Starosillia&#58; a village, the administrative centre of Chobruchi starostynskyi okruh, Rozdilna urban hromada, Rozdilna Raion, Odesa Oblast, Ukraine; known by this name from February 1945 until September 2024.
   1. A former silrada of Rozdilna Raion, Odesa Oblast, Ukraine, established somewhen 1956–1965, merged into Rozdilna urban hromada in May 2020.
