### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A festival or holiday commemorating the birth of Jesus Christ and incorporating various Christian, pre-Christian, pagan, and secular customs including gift-giving, which in Western Christianity is celebrated on December 25 (Christmas Day) in most places.

   **Usage:** also; attributive; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Do you celebrate Christmas?

   **Example:** This Christmas we’ll open presents, then go to grandma’s for dinner.

   **Source synonyms:** Chrimble; Chrimbo; Chrissy; Crimble; Crimbo; Noel; Yule

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Ellipsis of Christmas season (“the period of time before and after Christmas Day, during which people prepare for and celebrate Christmas”); Christmastime.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; also; alt-of; attributive; countable; ellipsis; often; uncountable

   **Example:** The last three Christmases have been good for retailers.

   **Example:** Christmas shoppers spent less this December than last year, but our store will probably see just as many returned items during the twelve days of Christmas.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; marketing

   **Alternative form of:** [Christmas season](../105/christmas-season--4152540b7091.md)
