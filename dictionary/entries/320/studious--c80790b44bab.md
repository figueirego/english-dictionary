### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Given to thought, or to the examination of subjects by contemplation; contemplative.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Dedicated to study; devoted to the acquisition of knowledge from books

   **Example:** a studious scholar

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Earnest in endeavors; attentive; diligent

   **Usage:** usually followed by an infinitive or by "of"

   **Example:** be studious to please

   **Example:** She is studious to find new friends and allies

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Planned with study; deliberate; studied.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Favorable to study; suitable for thought and contemplation

   **Example:** the studious shade
