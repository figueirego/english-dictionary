### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A key postulate of quantum mechanics, giving the probability that a measurement of a quantum system will yield a given result. In its simplest form, it states that the probability density of finding a particle at a given point, when measured, is proportional to the square of the magnitude of the particle's wavefunction at that point.

   **Usage:** quantum mechanics

   **Source synonyms:** Born's rule
