### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. (in Jane F. Brown's nomenclature for PolyHIPE supports) The polymer-bound tris-OH linker functionality, whose three hydroxymethyl groups provide sites for attaching molecules in solid-phase synthesis.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Extractor diagnostics (not usage labels):** no-gloss
