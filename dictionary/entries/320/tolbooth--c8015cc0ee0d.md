### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The traditional municipal building of a Scottish town or burgh, usually including a meeting-hall, court, prison and (eponymically) a place for the receipt of taxes, duties and fines.

   **Usage:** Scotland
