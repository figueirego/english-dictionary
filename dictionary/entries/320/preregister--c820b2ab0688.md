### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To register for something (especially for a course of education) prior to its start.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To register or enroll (a person, especially a student) prior to the start of something.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To register (a study design and its planned statistical analysis) before the study by reporting (them) in the scientific literature via a registered report.

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive; methodology
