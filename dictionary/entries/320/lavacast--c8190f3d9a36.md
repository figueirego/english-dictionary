### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A large structure created by building a framework (which can itself be a lavacast) and pouring lava over it, and then water, which transforms the lava into cobblestone blocks. Commonly used to quickly cover a large area without manually placing blocks.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The act of creating lavacasts.
