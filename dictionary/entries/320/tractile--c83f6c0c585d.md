### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Capable of being drawn or stretched out in length.

   **Source synonyms:** ductile; tensile

   **Source antonyms:** intractile; irretractile

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Pertaining to traction or pulling.

   **Source synonyms:** tractional; tractive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Capable of being guided, influenced, or led.

   **Usage:** dated

   **Source synonyms:** tractable; ductile; fictile; formable; moldable; plastic; sequacious; shapeable; bowsome (archaic); buxom (obsolete); compliant; hearsome (rare); morigerous (obsolete); obedible (obsolete); obedient; obsequent (obsolete); obsequious (archaic); tall (obsolete); toward

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Of financial assets&#58; able to be drawn or procured from a place of deposit; liquid.

   **Usage:** obsolete; rare
