### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To check quickly; to make a brief or cursory visual inspection of.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To move (one's eyes) so as to inspect or take in (someone or something).

   **Usage:** ditransitive

   **Example:** He looked his eyes over the shabbily-dressed man.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To quickly read or (especially) proofread; to quickly scan text, especially while checking for errors.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Synonym of overlook, to survey, to look at as if from on high.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** overlook (synonym; synonym-of); to survey (synonym; synonym-of); to look at as if from on high (synonym; synonym-of)
