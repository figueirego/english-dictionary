### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A word that expresses one or more of a specific number of parts of a whole.

   **Example:** In English, most ordinal numbers double as fractional numbers — "third", "fourth", and so on — with the exception of "second", whose corresponding fractional number is "half".

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** grammar; human-sciences; linguistics; sciences
