### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A long, broad and thick piece of timber, as opposed to a board which is less thick.
   1. A flat piece of wood on which to cook fish, so that the wood provides flavor and helps to produce a pleasing texture.

      **Example:** A dish called "plank salmon" likely includes a salmon filet cooked on a plank.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A long, broad and thick piece of timber, as opposed to a board which is less thick.
   1. An object made of planks; planking.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A long, broad and thick piece of timber, as opposed to a board which is less thick.
   1. A solid and substantial portion of an ingredient with a flat side, cooked and served whole.

      **Example:** That dish is served with a plank of cauliflower.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to plank a floor or a ship

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

   **Usage:** colloquial; transitive

   **Usage:** transitive
