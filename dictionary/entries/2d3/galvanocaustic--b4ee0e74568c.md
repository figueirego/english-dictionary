## Noun · Record 2

### Forms

- galvanocaustics — plural

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. (historical surgery) Cauterization or cutting of tissue with an instrument heated by an electric current.

   **Extractor diagnostics (not usage labels):** no-gloss

**Supplemental definition (evidence reviewed; source gap preserved):** Le Monnier’s original 1873 article is titled Galvanocaustic and expressly defines the exact uninflected term as a class of surgical operations before describing the electrically heated instrument. This directly establishes the historical noun, rather than deriving it from the adjective or from the separate word galvanocaustics. The label limits the lexical sense to the historical usage; procedural instructions and the author’s safety claims are excluded.
