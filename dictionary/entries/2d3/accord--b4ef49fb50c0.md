1. Agreement of opinion, will, or action.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Agreement in pitch and tone; harmony, musical concord.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Agreement or harmony of things in general.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** the accord of light and shade in painting

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A distinctive mixture of fragrances or the odor thereof.

   **Usage:** countable; perfumery

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An agreement between parties in controversy, by which satisfaction for an injury is stipulated, and which, when executed, prevents a lawsuit.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. An international agreement.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; international law

   **Example:** The Geneva Accord of 1954 ended the French-Indochinese War.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Voluntary or spontaneous impulse to act.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Nobody told me to do it. I did it of my own accord.
