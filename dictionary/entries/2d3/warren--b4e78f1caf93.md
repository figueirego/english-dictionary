### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A system of burrows in which rabbits or other animals live.

   **Source synonyms:** burrow; earth

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A system of burrows in which rabbits or other animals live.
   1. A structure where rabbits are kept as pets or for food, etc; the rabbits living in such a structure.

      **Source synonyms:** burrow; earth

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A mazelike place of passages and/or rooms in which it's easy to lose oneself; especially one that may be overcrowded.

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The class of small game such as hare, pheasants, stoats, etc., as opposed to beasts of chase such as deer, bear, and foxes.

   **Usage:** archaic

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A place legally authorized for the keeping, breeding and hunting of beasts of warren, especially rabbits.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The right to maintain and hunt an area of small beasts, similar to a free warren, but with certain limitations, such as restricting the right to hunt on parts of the land held by freeholders.

   **Usage:** historical
