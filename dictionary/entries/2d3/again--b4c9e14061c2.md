**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Another time&#58; indicating a repeat of an action.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** I enjoyed it so much I went again the next day.

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Back (to a former place or state).

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** We need to bring the old customs to life again.

   **Example:** The South will rise again.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Over and above a factor of one.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** This stick is quite long, but that other one is half as long again.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Used metalinguistically, with the repetition being in the discussion, or in the linguistic or pragmatic context of the discussion, rather than in the subject of discussion.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Great, thanks again!

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Used metalinguistically, with the repetition being in the discussion, or in the linguistic or pragmatic context of the discussion, rather than in the subject of discussion.
   1. Tell me again, say again; used in asking a question to which one may have already received an answer that one cannot remember.

      **Usage:** not-comparable

      **Example:** What's that called again?

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Used metalinguistically, with the repetition being in the discussion, or in the linguistic or pragmatic context of the discussion, rather than in the subject of discussion.
   1. I ask again, I say again; used in repeating a question or statement.

      **Usage:** not-comparable

      **Example:** Again, I'm not criticizing, I just want to understand.

      **Usage:** not-comparable
