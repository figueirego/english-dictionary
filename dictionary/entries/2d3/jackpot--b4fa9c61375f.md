**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An unexpected windfall or reward.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

4. A concentration of surface wood or fuel.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; forestry

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A large accumulated point bonus, originally awarded after a long set of actions but now often easily available in multiball modes.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; pinball

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A ball game in which a thrower calls out a point value and whether the ball must be caught "alive" (in the air) or "dead" (having touched the ground), and a group of catchers compete to catch the ball.
