### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any member of the genus Castilleja of herbaceous perennial plants.

   **Source synonyms:** paintbrush

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Gaillardia pulchella, a flowering plant of southern US, from Arizona to Florida, and northern Mexico.

   **Source synonyms:** firewheel; Indian blanket; sundance
