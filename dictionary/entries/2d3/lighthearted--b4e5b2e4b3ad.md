### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Joyful, glad, taking pleasure in being alive; not depressed or sad.

   **Example:** The lighthearted young lovers ran over the hills.

   **Source synonyms:** gladsome; joyous; air-headed; blissed out; blissful; enrapt; rapt; cock-a-hoop; delighted; ecstatic; elated; euphoric; exultant; filled with happiness; giddy; gladsome; gleeful; greatly pleased; joyful; joyous; lighthearted; orgasmic; on cloud nine; over the moon; overjoyed; thrilled; walking on air

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Enjoyably lacking of seriousness, not grave.

   **Example:** His lighthearted banjo performance was appreciated because it relieved the tension in tight situations.

   **Example:** I continued my speech in a more light-hearted vein.

   **Source synonyms:** easygoing; laid-back; airy; at ease; at peace; carefree; careless (archaic); casual; chill; cool; devil-may-care; easy-breezy; easygoing; happy-go-lucky; indifferent; insouciant; laid-back; leisurely; lighthearted; loose; mellow; offhand; nonchalant; pococurante; relaxed; serene; solute; tranquil; unanxious; unbothered; unconcerned; undisturbed; unruffled; untroubled; unworried; wanton (obsolete)
