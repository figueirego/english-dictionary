## Verb

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To experience; to suffer; to fall upon.

   **Example:** With the rise of the Internet, some media fell on hard times.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To be assigned to; to acquire a new responsibility, duty or burden; to fall to.

   **Example:** This decision is important, and it falls on you to make it.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To occur on a particular day.

   **Example:** The first day of spring this year will fall on a Tuesday.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a holiday or event

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Of the eyes or gaze&#58; to alight upon.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To attack (someone); to join an attack on (someone), especially a surprise attack.

   **Usage:** archaic
