## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A technique used to track faults in networks, such as cable breaks or loose connections, in which a pulse of a known shape is transmitted over the network and an echo is created when the pulse strikes an obstacle or cable end.

   **Usage:** uncountable
