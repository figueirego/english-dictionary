### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To talk a great deal.

   **Example:** I can speak volumes about this school.

   **Source synonyms:** tell volumes

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To convey significant information beyond what is explicit, especially unintentionally.

   **Example:** In our day-to-day experiences, it is often such nonverbal emotional information carried by the human voice that “speaks volumes.”

   **Source synonyms:** say it all; tell volumes

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To convey significant information.

   **Usage:** situation; or object; situation; or object

   **Example:** The way you listen speaks volumes.

   **Example:** The lighted decorated Christmas tree, a treat for all, speaks volumes of meaning.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of an action

   **Source synonyms:** tell volumes
