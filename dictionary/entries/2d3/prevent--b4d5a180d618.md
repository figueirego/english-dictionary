### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To stop (an outcome); to keep from (doing something).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I brush my teeth regularly to prevent tooth decay.

   **Example:** Barriers have been put up to prevent more people (from) entering the stadium.

   **Source synonyms:** throttle; anticipate; avert

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To take preventative measures.

   **Usage:** archaic; intransitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>
