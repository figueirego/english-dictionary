### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A room or enclosure (such as a private cubicle) that contains a baby's changing table; (loosely) any baby-changing area.

   **Usage:** UK; informal

   **Example:** We walked into the baby change.

   **Source synonyms:** baby-changing station

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Synonym of baby-changing station (“a baby's changing table”).

   **Usage:** UK; informal; rare

   **Example:** We walked into the baby change. Ouch!
