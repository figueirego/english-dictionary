## Adverb

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A comparison of fighters' (usually boxers) skill levels, regardless of what weight class they are in.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Sugar Ray Robinson is pound for pound the best boxer ever.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** boxing; government; hobbies; lifestyle; martial-arts; military; politics; sports; war

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Pertaining to how any two things compare when bearing in mind their real or relative, figurative weight.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** A tin of tomatoes weighing 250 g and costing $2.50 is more expensive, pound-for-pound, than one weighing 500 g and costing $4 (as the former costs $1.00 per 100 g and the latter $0.80 per 100 g).

   **Example:** My gal is pound for pound sweeter than chocolate.
