## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A flowering plant, species Daucus carota, especially the flowering part of the plant.

   **Usage:** Canada; US; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Ammi majus (false Queen Anne's lace), native to the Nile River Valley, with white lace-like flower clusters.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Anthriscus sylvestris (cow parsley), a herbaceous biennial or short-lived perennial plant in the family Apiaceae.

   **Usage:** uncountable
