### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To apply a bail to (a cow); to trap or corner.

   **Usage:** Australia; transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To stop (someone) to talk to them; to buttonhole.

   **Usage:** Australia; New-Zealand; transitive

   **Example:** I'm late because Debbie bailed me up on the phone.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To rob (someone).

   **Usage:** Australia; transitive

   **Example:** They bailed me up for fifty bucks.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To corner (someone); to hold (someone) under threat of violence; for dogs to hold people or animals at bay.

   **Usage:** Australia; transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. For rocks, clay, etc. to build up around a drill.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Misspelling of bale up.

   **Usage:** alt-of; misspelling
