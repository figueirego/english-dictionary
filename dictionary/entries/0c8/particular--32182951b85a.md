<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Pertaining only to a part of something; partial.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Specific; discrete; concrete.

   **Example:** I couldn't find the particular model you asked for, but I hope this one will do.

   **Example:** We knew it was named after John Smith, but nobody knows which particular John Smith.

   **Source synonyms:** concrete; discrete; individual; particular; peculiar (dated); proper; singular; specific

   **Source antonyms:** general

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Specialised; characteristic of a specific person or thing.

   **Example:** I don't appreciate your particular brand of cynicism.

   **Source synonyms:** optimized; specialistic

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Known only to an individual person or group; confidential.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Distinguished in some way; special (often in negative constructions).

   **Example:** My five favorite places are, in no particular order, New York, Chicago, Paris, San Francisco and London.

   **Example:** I didn't have any particular interest in the book.

   **Example:** He brought no particular news.

   **Example:** She was the particular belle of the party.

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Of a person, concerned with, or attentive to, details; fastidious.

   **Usage:** comparable

   **Example:** He is very particular about his food and if it isn't cooked to perfection he will send it back.

   **Example:** a full and particular account of an accident

   **Source synonyms:** accurate; careful; surgical; comprehensive; solicitous; conscientious; exhaustive; fastidious; groundly (archaic); in-depth; intensive; letter-perfect; mindful; minute; particular; polished; precise; painstaking; punctilious; rigorous; root and branch; scrupulous; scrutinous; sedulous; strict; thorough; tight

**Source sense R1.S8**
