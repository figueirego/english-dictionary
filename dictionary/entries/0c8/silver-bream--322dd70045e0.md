### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A fish from any of several species&#58;
   1. Blicca bjoerkna (white bream), a freshwater species of cyprinids from Europe and Western Asia.

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A fish from any of several species&#58;
   1. Pseudocaranx dentex (silver trevally, white trevally), marine species of jack widespread in tropical and warm temperate waters of the Atlantic, Indian, and Pacific Oceans, as well as the Mediterranean Sea.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A fish from any of several species&#58;
   1. Acanthopagrus australis (yellowfin bream, surf bream), eastern Australian sea breams.

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A fish from any of several species&#58;
