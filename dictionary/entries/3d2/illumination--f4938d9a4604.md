### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of illuminating, or supplying with light; the state of being illuminated.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The room was filled with soft illumination from the candles.

   **Source synonyms:** lightness

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Festive decoration of houses or buildings with lights.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Adornment of books and manuscripts with colored illustrations. See illuminate (transitive verb).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Splendour; brightness.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Enlightening influence; inspiration.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

   **Example:** The philosopher’s work gave intellectual illumination to many.
