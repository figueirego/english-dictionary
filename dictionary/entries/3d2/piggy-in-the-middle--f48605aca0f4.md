### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A game where one group has to keep an object, normally a ball, within their group and away from another group or individual.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The player in that game from whom the object is kept away.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Keeping something between one's own group, away from others.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A person caught in an awkward situation between two other people.

   **Usage:** countable
