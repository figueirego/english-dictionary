### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A substance used to treat an illness, relieve a symptom, or modify a chemical process in the body for a specific purpose.

   **Example:** Aspirin is a drug that reduces pain, acts against inflammation and lowers body temperature.

   **Example:** The revenues from both brand-name drugs and generic drugs have increased.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; pharmacology; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** conservatory (obsolete); drug; medication; medicine; medicament; pharmaceutical; pharmaceutical drug

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A psychoactive substance, especially one which is illegal and addictive, ingested for recreational use, such as cocaine.

   **Example:** We took drugs and partied all night.

   **Example:** They're on drugs.

   **Example:** She used to be a drug addict.

   **Source synonyms:** recreational drug; drug; dope; gear; horse; stuff; substance

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Anything, such as a substance, emotion, or action, to which one is addicted.

   **Usage:** broadly

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any commodity that lies on hand, or is not salable; an article of slow sale, or in no demand.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Ellipsis of drugstore.

   **Usage:** Canada; US; abbreviation; alt-of; ellipsis; informal

   **Alternative form of:** [drugstore](../3b4/drugstore--ed015c0da936.md)

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To administer intoxicating drugs to, generally without the recipient's knowledge or consent.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** She suddenly felt strange, and only then realized she'd been drugged.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To add intoxicating drugs to with the intention of drugging someone.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** She suddenly felt strange. She realized her drink must have been drugged.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To prescribe or administer drugs or medicines.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

   **Usage:** intransitive; rare
