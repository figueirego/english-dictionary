### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Following a noun or noun phrase&#58; by, for, or to each one; each by itself.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** These melons cost a dollar apiece.

   **Source synonyms:** all; individually; singly; piecemeal; piecewise

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. For an undetermined distance.

   **Usage:** New-England; especially; not-comparable

   **Example:** down the road apiece
