## Verb · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ɹʌb/ — source labels: Received-Pronunciation; US
- enPR: rŭb — source labels: Received-Pronunciation; US

### Forms

- rubs — present; singular; third-person
- rubbing — participle; present
- rubbed — participle; past
- rubbed — past

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To move (one object) while maintaining contact with another object over some area, with pressure and friction.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I rubbed the cloth over the glass.

   **Example:** The cat rubbed itself against my leg.

   **Example:** I rubbed my hands together for warmth.

   **Example:** I couldn't rub the stain from the tablecloth.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To be rubbed against something.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** My shoes are beginning to rub.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To spread a substance thinly over; to smear.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** meat rubbed with spices before barbecuing

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To move or pass with difficulty.

   **Usage:** dated

   **Example:** to rub through woods, as huntsmen

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To scour; to burnish; to polish; to brighten; to cleanse; often with up or over.

   **Example:** to rub up silver

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To hinder; to cross; to thwart.

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

**Source sense R2.S7**

7. To touch the jack with the bowl.

   **Usage:** transitive; bowls
