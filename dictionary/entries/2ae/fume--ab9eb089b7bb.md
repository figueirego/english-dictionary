## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /fjuːm/ — source labels: UK

### Forms

- fumes — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A gas or vapour/vapor that is strong-smelling or dangerous to inhale.

   **Example:** Don't stand around in there breathing the fumes while the adhesive cures.

   **Source synonyms:** effluvium

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A material that has been vaporized from the solid or liquid state to the gas state and re-coalesced to the solid state.

   **Example:** Lead fume is a greyish powder, mainly comprising lead sulfate.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Rage or excitement which deprives the mind of self-control.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Anything unsubstantial or airy; idle conceit; vain imagination.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The incense of praise; inordinate flattery.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A passionate person.

   **Usage:** obsolete

## Verb · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /fjuːm/ — source labels: UK

### Forms

- fumes — present; singular; third-person
- fuming — participle; present
- fumed — participle; past
- fumed — past

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To expose (something) to fumes; specifically, to expose wood, etc., to ammonia in order to produce dark tints.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To apply or offer incense to.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To emit fumes.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To pass off in fumes or vapours.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To express or feel great anger.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive or reporting verb

   **Example:** He’s still fuming about the argument they had yesterday.

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To be as in a mist; to be dulled and stupefied.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive
