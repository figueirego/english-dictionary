**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The president condemned the terrorists.

   **Source synonyms:** admonish; objurgate; upbraid; admonish; animadvert; bash; bawl out; tilt; clobber (informal); cannonade; slash; lash; blame; scorch (informal); blast; berate; berisp (obsolete); castigate; censure; charge; chastise; chew out; chide; condemn; criticize; denounce; denunciate; deprecate; derogate; dispraise; drag (slang); dress down; eat someone alive; excoriate; find fault; fulminate; get at; get down; get up (Australia; Britain); give somebody a piece of one's mind; give somebody what-for; inveigh; lace into; lambaste; lay into; lecture; light into; objurgate; pooh-pooh; put someone on blast (slang); rate; read somebody the riot act; rebuke; reprehend; reprimand; reproach; reprove; rip into; rubbish (Australia; Britain); savage; scold; skewer (figuratively); slam; slate; tear into; [tell off](../3c5/tell-off--f156cc498c14.md); threap; tick off (Britain); tosh (Britain); keelhaul; upbraid; vilify; vituperate

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To judicially pronounce (someone) guilty.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** convict; damn; sentence; adjudge; convict; condemn; damn; deem (obsolete); doom; judge (obsolete); sentence

   **Source antonyms:** acquit; clear; vindicate; acquittance (obsolete); assoil (archaic); assoilzie (Scotland); acquit; absolve; clear; disculp (rare); disculpate; exculpate; exonerate; vindicate

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To judicially announce a verdict upon a finding of guilt; To sentence

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The judge condemned him to death.

   **Example:** She was condemned to life in prison.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To confer eternal divine punishment upon.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** damn

   **Source antonyms:** save

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To destine to experience bad circumstances; to doom.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** Too many people are condemned to a life of poverty.

   **Source synonyms:** curse; fate; foreordain; preordain
