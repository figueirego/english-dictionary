### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A male domestic chicken (Gallus gallus domesticus) or other gallinaceous bird.

   **Usage:** Australia; Canada; Kent; New-Zealand; US; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A bird or bat which roosts or is roosting.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An informer.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; obsolete; slang; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A violent or disorderly person.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; obsolete; slang; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A powerful, prideful, or pompous person.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A man.

   **Usage:** New-Zealand; countable; figuratively; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A wild violet, when used in a children's game based on cockfighting.

   **Usage:** US; countable; historical; regional; uncountable

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Legislation solely devised to benefit the legislators proposing it.

   **Usage:** US; obsolete; slang; uncountable
