### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Feeling great anger; raging; violent.

   **Example:** a furious animal; parent furious at their child's behaviour

   **Source synonyms:** fierce; infuriate; rampant; ferocious; fierce; furious; rampant; grim (obsolete); incessive; infuriate (rare); rabid (figuratively); raging; savage; volatile; [wild](../1f9/wild--7e73b026cfd1.md)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Rushing with impetuosity; moving with violence.

   **Example:** a furious stream; a furious wind or storm
