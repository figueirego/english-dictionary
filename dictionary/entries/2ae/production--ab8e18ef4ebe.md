   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

3. The act of being produced.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The widgets are coming out of production now.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The total amount produced.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** They hope to increase spaghetti production next year.

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The presentation of a theatrical work.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** If you get the chance, try and catch the new production of "Hamlet."

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. An occasion or activity made more complicated than necessary.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** He made a simple meal into a huge production.
