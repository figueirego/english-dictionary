**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The Irish Hobby, an extinct breed of horse.

   **Usage:** originally

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A child's toy consisting of a (usually wooden or cloth) horse's head mounted on a stick.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A topic that elicits a prolonged and enthusiastic discussion from an individual.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** Don't ask him about global warming&#58; it's his favourite hobby horse, you'll never get away.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>
