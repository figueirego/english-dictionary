### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A multicellular organism of the kingdom Animalia that is usually mobile, with cells that are not encased in a rigid cell wall (distinguishing them from plants and fungi) and derives energy solely from the consumption of other organisms.

   **Example:** Humans, like other animals, need air to breathe and food to eat.

   **Source synonyms:** critter

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any member of the kingdom Animalia other than a human.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Source synonyms:** beast

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A higher animal; an animal related to humans.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** When he's hungry my toddler opens his mouth like an animal instead of asking us to feed him.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A higher animal; an animal related to humans.
   1. A tetrapod; a land-dwelling nonhuman vertebrate.

      **Usage:** broadly; colloquial

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A higher animal; an animal related to humans.
   1. A warm-blooded animal; a mammal or bird.

      **Usage:** broadly

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A higher animal; an animal related to humans.
   1. A non-human mammal.

      **Usage:** broadly

      **Example:** I spent my summer studying the animals and birds of the two islands.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A person who behaves wildly; a bestial, brutal, brutish, cruel, or inhuman person.

   **Usage:** derogatory; figuratively

   **Example:** My students are animals.

   **Source synonyms:** brute; monster; savage

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A person who overeats or eats rapidly and noisily.

   **Usage:** derogatory; figuratively

   **Source synonyms:** animal; belly-god (obsolete); big back; buzgut (UK; archaic); cormorant; dog; epicure; gannet; glutton; gorger; gourmand; gormandizer; greedyguts; guttle (Britain; archaic; dialectal); hog; Homer Simpson; helluo; pig; slow belly; trencherman

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** He's a political animal.
