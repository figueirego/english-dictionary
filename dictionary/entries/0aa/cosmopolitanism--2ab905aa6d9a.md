### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The quality of being cosmopolitan.
   1. The idea that all of humanity belongs to a single moral community.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source synonyms:** cosmopolitanness

      **Source antonyms:** tribalism; nativism
