### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Widely cultivated cereal grass, typically Avena sativa.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** The oat stalks made good straw.

   **Example:** The main forms of oat are meal and bran.

   **Example:** World trade in oat is increasing.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of the numerous species, varieties, or cultivars of any of several similar grain plants in genus Avena.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** The wild red oat is thought to be the ancestor of modern food oats.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The seeds of the oat, a grain, harvested as a food crop and for animal feed.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; usually

   **Source context (verbatim):** as plural

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A simple musical pipe made of oat-straw.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The tiniest amount; a whit or jot.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
