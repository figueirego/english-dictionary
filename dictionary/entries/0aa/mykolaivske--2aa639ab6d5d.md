### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A former village in Mykhalchyna-Sloboda silrada, Novhorod-Siverskyi Raion, Chernihiv Oblast, Ukraine, founded in the 1920s–30s, disincorporated in July 2016.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A village in Shevchenkove rural hromada, Mykolaiv Raion, Mykolaiv Oblast, Ukraine, founded in 1957.
   1. A former silrada of Vitovka Raion, Mykolaiv Oblast, Ukraine, amalgamated into Shevchenkove rural hromada in September 2016; administrative centre and sole constituent settlement&#58; Mykolaivske.
