## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A norm for the rational numbers, with some prime number p as parameter, such that any rational number of the form pᵏ(a/b) — where a, b and k are integers and a, b and p are coprime — is mapped to the rational number p⁻ᵏ and 0 is mapped to 0. (Note&#58; any nonzero rational number can be reduced to such a form.)

   **Usage:** field theory

   **Example:** According to Ostrowski's theorem, only three kinds of norms are possible for the set of real numbers&#58; the trivial absolute value, the real absolute value, and the p-adic absolute value.ᵂᴾ

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; number-theory; sciences
