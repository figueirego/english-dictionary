## Noun · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /tɹɛnd/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An inclination in a particular direction.

   **Example:** Her trend is to dump boyfriends when it gets serious.

   **Example:** the upward trend of stock-market prices

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A tendency.

   **Example:** There is a trend, these days, for people in films not to smoke.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A fad or fashion style.

   **Example:** Miniskirts were one of the biggest trends of the 1960s.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An innovation, or a development of innovations.

   **Example:** New trends in AI research are resulting in new opportunities.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A general development over distance.

   **Example:** The trend of the boundary goes around the mountains.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A line drawn on a graph that approximates the trend of a number of disparate points; the shift over time represented by such a curve on a graph.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** graph-theory; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The lower end of the shank of an anchor, being the same distance on the shank from the throat that the arm measures from the throat to the bill.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. The angle made by the line of a vessel's keel and the direction of the anchor cable, when she is swinging at anchor.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To have a particular direction; to run; to stretch; to tend.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The shore of the sea trends to the southwest.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To cause to turn; to bend.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To be the subject of a trend; to be currently popular, relevant or interesting.

   **Usage:** informal; intransitive; social media

   **Example:** What topics have been trending on social networks this week?
