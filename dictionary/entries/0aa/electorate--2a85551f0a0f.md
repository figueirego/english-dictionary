### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The collective people of a country, state, or electoral district who are entitled to vote.

   **Usage:** collective

   **Example:** The votes have been counted and the electorate has spoken.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; politics

   **Source synonyms:** constituency

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The office, or area of dominion, of an Elector (“a German prince entitled to elect the emperor of the Holy Roman Empire”); an electorship.

   **Usage:** historical

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; politics

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A geographical area represented by one or more elected officials; a constituency, an electoral district.

   **Usage:** Australia; New-Zealand

   **Example:** The electorate of Finchley borders on the electorate of Much-Binding-in-the-Marsh, splitting the new housing estate of Royal Cupolas.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; politics

   **Source synonyms:** division (Australia); riding (Canada); seat (Australia); ward
