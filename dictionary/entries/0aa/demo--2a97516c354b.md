### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A demonstration or visual explanation.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** The professor prepared a demo to help her class understand the topic.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A recording of a song meant to demonstrate its overall sound for the purpose of getting it published or recorded more fully.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** After hearing the demo, the record label approved funding to record the song with a full band.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An example of a product used for demonstration and then sold at a discount.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Source synonyms:** floor model

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A march or gathering to make a political protest.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Source synonyms:** march; demonstration

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An edition of limited functionality to give the user an example of how the program works.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** The game's developers released a demo version to the public 3 months before the full release.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A non-interactive audiovisual computer program developed by enthusiasts to demonstrate the capabilities of the machine. See demoscene.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; demoscene; engineering; lifestyle; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A democrat.

   **Usage:** informal

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A demographic group.

   **Usage:** collective; informal

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. Demolition.

   **Usage:** informal; especially construction and DIY

   **Example:** The demo portion of the reno project is the relatively fast and inexpensive part. The rebuilding takes more time and money.
