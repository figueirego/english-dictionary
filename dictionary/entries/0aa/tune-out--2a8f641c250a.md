### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To stop paying attention to; to ignore; to zone out.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** I have a hard time tuning out a television that is on.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To change the channel or frequency away from.

   **Example:** I was tired of talk radio so I tuned out of that station and tuned into one playing all music.
