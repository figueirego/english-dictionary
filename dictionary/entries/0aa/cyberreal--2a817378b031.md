## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Synonym of virtual, existing on the internet (in cyberspace) but not in real life.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** My online beachfront home is cyberreal estate.

   **Source synonyms:** virtual (synonym; synonym-of); existing on the internet (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Real within the context of the internet, although limited or attenuated in real life.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Our relationship is cyberreal, which is fine for now.
