### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Happening at an opportune or advantageous time.

   **Example:** Their most recent release to the market was well-timed.

   **Source synonyms:** timely; seasonable; in good season; opportune; well-timed; tempestive (obsolete); opportune; seasonable; tidy (obsolete); timeful (rare); timely; timeous; heaven-sent; timesome

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Example:** A well-timed snare drum enlivened the music.
