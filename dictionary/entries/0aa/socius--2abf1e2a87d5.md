### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of the autonomous tribes and city states of the Italian Peninsula in permanent military alliance with the Roman Republic until the Social War of 91–88 BC.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An associate; a fellow of an academy, etc.

**Source sense R1.S3**
