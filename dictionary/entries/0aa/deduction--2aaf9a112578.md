<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. That which is deducted; that which is subtracted or removed.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** extract; reduction; cut; decrement; deduction; dip; drop; extract; extraction; fall; lowering; reduction; shrinkage

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. That which is deducted; that which is subtracted or removed.
   1. A sum that can be removed in tax calculations, usually from the taxable amount; something that is written off.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Example:** standard deduction

      **Example:** You might want to donate the old junk and just take the deduction.

      **Source synonyms:** extract; reduction; cut; decrement; deduction; dip; drop; extract; extraction; fall; lowering; reduction; shrinkage

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. That which is deducted; that which is subtracted or removed.
   1. A sum withheld from an employee's pay for the purpose of paying tax.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance; law

      **Source synonyms:** extract; reduction; cut; decrement; deduction; dip; drop; extract; extraction; fall; lowering; reduction; shrinkage

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A process of reasoning that moves from the general to the specific, in which a conclusion follows necessarily from the premises presented, so that the conclusion cannot be false if the premises are true.
   1. A conclusion; that which is deduced, concluded or figured out.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Example:** He arrived at the deduction that the butler didn't do it.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; logic; mathematics; philosophy; sciences

      **Source synonyms:** ergotism; illation; verdict; award; censure (obsolete); choice; opinion; conclusion; decision; deduction; determination; ergotism; illation; inference; judgement/judgment; mind; result; sight; take; verdict; view

      **Source antonyms:** induction

**Source sense R1.S5**
