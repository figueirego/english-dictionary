## Adjective

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Not of or relating to a corridor.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of a train or railway coach, not having a corridor running the length of the coach providing access to the passenger compartments; each compartment would have an outside door on each side.

   **Usage:** dated; not-comparable

   **Example:** No non-corridor coaching stock remains in normal service.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** rail-transport; railways; transport
