## Verb

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To administer the vows of a religious order to (someone); to admit to a religious order.

   **Usage:** transitive; chiefly passive voice

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To declare oneself (to be something).

   **Usage:** reflexive

   **Example:** They've professed themselves delighted with the results.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To declare; to assert, affirm.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** Having professed her belief in the remedy, she had little choice but to try it.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To make a claim (to be something); to lay claim to (a given quality, feeling etc.), often with connotations of insincerity.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Many profess to despise what secretly they hunger after.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To declare one's adherence to (a religion, deity, principle etc.).

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To work as a professor of; to teach.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To claim to have knowledge or understanding of (a given area of interest, subject matter).

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive
