### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A single elongated piece of a given material, roughly round in cross-section, often twisted with other fibers to form thread.

   **Usage:** US; countable

   **Example:** The microscope showed a single blue fiber stuck to the sole of the shoe.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A material in the form of fibers.

   **Usage:** US; uncountable

   **Example:** The cloth is made from strange, somewhat rough fiber.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A material whose length is at least 1000 times its width.

   **Usage:** US; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Please use polyester fiber for this shirt.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; manufacturing; textiles

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Dietary fiber.

   **Usage:** US; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Fresh vegetables are a good source of fiber.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Moral strength and resolve.

   **Usage:** US; countable; figuratively; uncountable

   **Example:** The ordeal was a test of everyone's fiber.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The preimage of a given point in the range of a map.

   **Usage:** US; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Under this map, any two values in the fiber of a given point on the circle differ by 2π.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The pullback of a morphism along a global element (called the fiber of the morphism over the global element).

   **Usage:** US; countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** category-theory; computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A kind of lightweight thread of execution.

   **Usage:** US; countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A long tubular cell found in bodily tissue.

   **Usage:** US; countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; cytology; medicine; natural-sciences; sciences
