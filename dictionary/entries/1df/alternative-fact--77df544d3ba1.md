### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Something presented as a fact for reasons of political expediency; a fiction, a falsehood, a lie.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see alternative, fact.

   **Example:** That Newton was an empirical scientist is a fact. An alternative fact is that he was an alchemist.
