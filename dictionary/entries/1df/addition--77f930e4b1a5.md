### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of adding anything.

   **Example:** The addition of five more items to the agenda will make the meeting unbearably long.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

3. The arithmetic operation of adding.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** arithmetic

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A dot at the right side of a note as an indication that its sound is to be lengthened one half.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law
