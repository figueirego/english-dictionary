### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Synonym of arrowslit.

   **Example:** There were arrow-holes in the battlements, from which archers were shooting.

   **Source synonyms:** arrowslit (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A hole of puncture in a body, the result of being shot with an arrow.

   **Example:** There was an arrow-hole in his abdomen, from which blood was seeping.
