### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To dictate policy.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To control or direct according to rule, principle, or law.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To adjust (a mechanism) for accurate and proper functioning.

   **Example:** to regulate a watch, i.e. adjust its rate of running so that it will keep approximately standard time

   **Example:** to regulate the temperature of a room, the pressure of steam, the speed of a machine, etc.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To put or maintain in order.

   **Example:** to regulate the disordered state of a nation or its finances

   **Example:** to regulate one's eating habits
