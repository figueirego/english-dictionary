### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of clothing, a place, etc.&#58; unkempt and worn or otherwise in poor condition due to age or neglect; scruffy.

   **Example:** They lived in a tiny apartment, with some old, shabby furniture.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of a person&#58; wearing ragged or very worn, and often dirty, clothing.

   **Example:** The fellow arrived looking rather shabby after journeying so far.

4. Poor in quality; also, showing little effort or talent.
