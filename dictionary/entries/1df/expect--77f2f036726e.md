### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To predict or believe that something will happen

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** when you least expect it

   **Example:** I expect to be able to walk again after getting over my broken leg.

   **Example:** The hostage is expected to be released later today.

   **Example:** I expect it'll rain this weekend, but I hope it won't.

   **Example:** I did not expect you'd come before I asked you to, but do expect you to apologize for it.

   **Source synonyms:** anticipate; hope; look for; bargain for

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To consider obligatory or required.

   **Source synonyms:** call for; demand

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To consider reasonably due.

   **Example:** My parents expected too much of me when I was in college.

   **Example:** You are expected to get the task done by the end of next week.

   **Example:** How am I expected to reach the next level?

   **Source synonyms:** hope; [want](../219/want--8656aa55d393.md); wish

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To be pregnant, to consider a baby due.

   **Usage:** continuous aspect only

   **Example:** We are expecting our third in June.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a woman or couple

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To wait for; to await.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** await; abide (archaic); attend; await; bide (archaic); expect (obsolete); hold up (idiomatic); keep (obsolete); remain; stay; tarry (dated); tend (obsolete); wait (rare); wait on; wait for

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To wait; to stay.

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete

   **Source synonyms:** wait
