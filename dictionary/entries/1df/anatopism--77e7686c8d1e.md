### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A thing that is out of its proper place; the geographic counterpart to anachronism.

   **Usage:** rare

   **Example:** A war elephant described rampaging through Tenochtitlan in a novel about the Aztec Empire would be an anatopism.
