### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Sunny (exposed to a high amount of sunlight).

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Rendered warm or brown due to exposure from the sun.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Having an attractive appearance from recent sun exposure, i.e. a slight tan, often a slight sunburn, and a radiant glow.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>
