### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Something that distracts.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Poking one's eye is a good distraction from a hurting toe.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The process of being distracted.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** We have to reduce distraction in class if we want students to achieve good results.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Perturbation; disorder; disturbance; confusion.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Mental disarray; a deranged state of mind; insanity.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The incessant nightmares drove him to distraction.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Traction so exerted as to separate surfaces normally opposed.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences
