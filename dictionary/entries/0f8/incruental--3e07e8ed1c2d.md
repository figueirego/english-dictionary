### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Bloodless; characterized by no blood loss or very little bleeding.

   **Usage:** obsolete

   **Example:** The herbal treatment served as an incruental alternative to surgery.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Characterized by very little or no bloodshed.

   **Usage:** obsolete

   **Example:** The battle was surprisingly incruental.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Characteristic of a small, living sacrifice.

   **Usage:** obsolete

   **Example:** How incruental an act for him, to simply drop a quarter in the poor woman's cup each morning.
