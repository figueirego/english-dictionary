### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An ancient Roman unit of liquid measure reckoned as the volume of 1+²⁄₃ Roman ounce of wine and equivalent to about 45 mL although differing slightly over time.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A ladle, especially (historical) a small one used for serving wine in ancient Greece and Rome.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Synonym of kyathos (“an ancient ladle and serving beaker”).

   **Usage:** historical

   **Source synonyms:** kyathos (synonym; synonym-of)
