### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make something up or invent it as one goes on; to proceed guided only by imagination, intuition, and guesswork rather than by a careful plan.

   **Example:** He had no speech prepared, so he improvised.

   **Example:** They improvised a simple shelter with branches and the rope they were carrying.

   **Example:** She improvised a lovely solo.
