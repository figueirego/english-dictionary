### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To unleash, let loose, uncork; to allow (something) to happen or proceed without restraint.

   **Usage:** informal; transitive

   **Example:** When she heard the news, she let rip a tirade heard throughout the office.

   **Example:** Ok, it’s all clear. Let ’er rip.

   **Example:** He let rip a fart that emptied the elevator at the next floor.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To utter or release without restraint.

   **Usage:** intransitive; usually

   **Example:** She let rip with a tirade everyone heard.

   **Example:** She let rip about Mary’s flirting with Lizzie’s boyfriend.

   **Source context (verbatim):** with a prepositional phrase

   **Source synonyms:** let loose
