### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To determine the weight of an object.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Often with "out", to measure a certain amount of something by its weight, e.g. for sale.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He weighed out two kilos of oranges for a client.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To determine the intrinsic value or merit of an object, to evaluate.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** You have been weighed in the balance and found wanting.

   **Example:** He took a long time weighing his options.

   **Source synonyms:** assess; price; value; appraise; apprise (archaic); apprize (archaic); assess; esteem (dated); evaluate; judge; price; prize (obsolete); rate; valuate; value; weigh (figuratively); worthen

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To judge; to estimate.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive; obsolete

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To consider a subject.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To have a certain weight.

   **Usage:** copulative; intransitive; stative

   **Example:** I weigh ten and a half stone.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To have weight; to be heavy; to press down.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To be considered as important; to have weight in the intellectual balance.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s9"></a>
