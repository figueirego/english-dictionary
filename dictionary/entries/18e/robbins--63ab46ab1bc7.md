### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An English surname originating as a patronymic, from Robin + -s.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A surname from Irish \[in turn originating as a patronymic\], an adopted anglicization of Mac Roibín (“son of Robin”) (Cribbin).

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A locale in the United States&#58;
   1. A census-designated place in Sutter County, California.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A locale in the United States&#58;
   1. A village and suburb of Chicago in Cook County, Illinois; named for real estate developer Eugene S. Robbins.

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A locale in the United States&#58;
   1. An unincorporated community in Haight Township, Ontonagon County, Michigan; named for local sawmill owner F. S. Robbins.
