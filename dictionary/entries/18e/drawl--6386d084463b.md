### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To drag on slowly and heavily; to dawdle or while away time indolently.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To utter or pronounce in a dull, spiritless tone, as if by dragging out the utterance.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To move slowly and heavily; to move in a dull, slow, lazy manner.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To speak with a slow, spiritless utterance, as from affectation, laziness, or lack of interest.

   **Usage:** intransitive
