## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A student organization present in many colleges, universities, and high schools. In higher education, the students' union is often accorded its own building on the campus, dedicated to social, organizational activities, and representation.

   **Usage:** UK

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The building where the union provides such services.

   **Usage:** UK

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A student organization to provide services to students, especially of higher education (in this sense only US English, and then almost always "student union")

   **Usage:** UK
