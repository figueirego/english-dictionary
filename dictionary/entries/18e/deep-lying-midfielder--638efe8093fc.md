**Source sense R1.S1**

1. (soccer) A midfielder who plays close to the defensive line, supporting the defence and passing the ball from deep positions.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; games; hobbies; lifestyle; soccer; sports
