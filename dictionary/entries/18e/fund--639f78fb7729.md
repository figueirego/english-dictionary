### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A sum or source of money.

   **Example:** the fund of a bank, commercial house, manufacturing corporation, etc.

   **Example:** a fund for the maintenance of underprivileged students

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An organization managing such money.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A money-management operation, such as a mutual fund.

   **Example:** Several major funds were declared insolvent recently.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A large supply of something to be drawn upon.

   **Example:** He drew on his immense fund of knowledge.
