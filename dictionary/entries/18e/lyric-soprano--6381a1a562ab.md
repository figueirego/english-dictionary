### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A common operatic voice type characterized by a warm, full, and bright sound; considered ideal for singing smooth (legato) lines and portraying sympathetic heroines; not as agile as a coloratura or as powerful as a dramatic soprano.
