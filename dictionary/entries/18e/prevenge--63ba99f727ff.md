### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of taking, or an action taken against someone or something, ostensibly in retaliation for another act that has not yet been committed; pre-emptive action characterized as revenge.

   **Usage:** humorous; uncountable

   **Example:** Anticipating Wally's inevitable sarcasm, Boris let fly with a few cutting remarks in a stunning act of prevenge.
