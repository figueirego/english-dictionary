## Noun · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The latest developments in a particular situation or a person's life.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; uncountable

   **Example:** We send out a newsletter to our clients every week to tell them what's new in the company.

<a id="r2"></a>

## Phrase · Record 2

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. An informal greeting asking the other person what has recently happened in their life. A typical response might be "Not much; you?". The greeting is not always literal and may just be used to say hello.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Source synonyms:** hello; hi; what's up

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. A rhetorical request for some real news, on hearing a report that was not news because it represents a continuing predictable and unsatisfactory situation.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; rhetoric

   **Example:** You're still having problems with that idiot you employed last year? So, what's new?

   **Source synonyms:** what else is new
