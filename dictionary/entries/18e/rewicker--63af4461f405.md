### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To redo the wickerwork, as the wicker seat of a piece of furniture

   **Example:** So as to be able to use them for years to come, Susan decided to rewicker Grandma Rose's old dining room table chairs.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To rework, update, redo, reformulate; especially in the case of a multi themed, or woven, product.

   **Example:** After learning more details of the customer's needs, the team felt it was important to rewicker the proposal.
