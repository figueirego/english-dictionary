### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Very hot; burning, scorching; also, glowing with heat.

   **Source synonyms:** fervid

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of cold&#58; intense, severe.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of a drug&#58; having an abundance of heat as one of the four qualities believed to be characteristic of all things; hot rather than cold, dry, or moist.

   **Usage:** historical; obsolete; pharmacy
