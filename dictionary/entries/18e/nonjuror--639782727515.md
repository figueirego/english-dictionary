### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Someone who refuses to swear a particular oath, specifically a clergyman who refused to take the oath of allegiance to William and Mary in 1689.

   **Usage:** historical; Anglicanism

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One who is not a juror.
