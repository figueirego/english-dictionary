### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Originally, a list of articles of clothing that had been sent to be laundered.

   **Usage:** archaic

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A long and often tedious list of items.

   **Usage:** broadly; idiomatic

   **Source synonyms:** litany

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An unrealistic list of skills, qualifications and experiences, as demanded by employers when hiring staff, or claimed by a candidate on a résumé.

   **Usage:** colloquial
