### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of or pertaining to the manner of a master of an art, technique, profession, or craft; masterly.

   **Example:** The brushwork on the skin tones was masterful.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In the manner of a master; very skillful.

   **Example:** The masterful conducting ensured the orchestra gave of their best.

   **Source synonyms:** magistral (formal)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Having or showing the qualities of a master vis-à-vis a dependent or subordinate; authoritative; domineering.
