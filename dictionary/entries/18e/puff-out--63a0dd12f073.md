### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To inflate.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** He puffed out his cheeks.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To blow briefly and lightly.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He puffed out a perfect smoke ring.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To emit puffs of smoke or exhaust

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** The engine puffed out white smoke.
