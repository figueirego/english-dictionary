### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In a fundamental, essential or basic manner.

   **Example:** They've added some fancy features, but it's basically still a crappy car.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Essentially; mostly; pretty much.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used to refer to the most important or fundamental point that the speaker is making or to indicate a generalization or simplification; sometimes tending towards a meaningless filler word.

   **Usage:** filler

   **Example:** I shouldn't have done it, basically.

   **Example:** To get your eyes tested, you should basically go to the optician's.
