## Noun

### Forms

- milkweeds — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of several plants that have a milky sap and have pods that split to release seeds with silky tufts.
   1. Principally of the genus Asclepias in order Gentianales, family Apocynaceae

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of several plants that have a milky sap and have pods that split to release seeds with silky tufts.
   1. Also of the genus Calotropis in order Gentianales, family Apocynaceae

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of several plants that have a milky sap and have pods that split to release seeds with silky tufts.
   1. Euphorbia peplus and Euphorbia heterophylla, in order Malpighiales.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A monarch butterfly (Danaus spp).
