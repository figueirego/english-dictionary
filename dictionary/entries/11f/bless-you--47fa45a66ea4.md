### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Short for (may) God bless you&#58; said as a short prayer for the recipient.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of

   **Alternative form of:** (may) God bless you

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Said to someone who has just sneezed, as a polite remark.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Source synonyms:** gesundheit

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used to express non-understanding of someone who has just used an unusual or foreign word or phrase (as if to imply that it sounds like a sneeze).

   **Usage:** humorous; idiomatic
