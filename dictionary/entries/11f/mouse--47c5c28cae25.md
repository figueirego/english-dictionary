### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To move cautiously or furtively, in the manner of a mouse (the rodent) (frequently used in the phrasal verb to mouse around).

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To hunt or catch mice (the rodents), usually of cats.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To close the mouth of a hook by a careful binding of marline or wire.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Captain Higgins moused the hook with a bit of marline to prevent the block beckets from falling out under slack.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To navigate by means of a computer mouse.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

   **Usage:** nonce-word; obsolete; transitive
