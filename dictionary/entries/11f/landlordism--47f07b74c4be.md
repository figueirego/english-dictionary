### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An economic system under which a few private individuals (landlords) own property, and rent it to tenants.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A specific variation or implementation of such a system.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually
