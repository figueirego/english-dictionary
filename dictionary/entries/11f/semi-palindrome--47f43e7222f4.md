## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A word or phrase that forms a different word or phrase when its letters are reversed.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A word or phrase in which a small portion is palindromic, while the rest is not.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A sequence of DNA that can be superimposed with its reverse complement.
