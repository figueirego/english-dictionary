### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Drinking water served at the dining table, traditionally chilled, usually without charge.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually; food service

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually
