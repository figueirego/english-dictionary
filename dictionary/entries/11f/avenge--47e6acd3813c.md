### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To take vengeance (for); to exact satisfaction for by punishing the injuring party; to vindicate by inflicting pain or evil on a wrongdoer.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to avenge the murder of his brother

   **Example:** They avenged the wrong done to them.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To revenge oneself (on or upon someone).

   **Usage:** reflexive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To take vengeance.

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To treat revengefully; to wreak vengeance on.

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive
