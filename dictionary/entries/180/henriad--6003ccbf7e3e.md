## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A group of four of Shakespeare’s plays&#58; Richard II; Henry IV, Part 1; Henry IV, Part 2; and Henry V, with the implication that these four plays are Shakespeare’s epic poem.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Either of two groups of four each of Shakespeare’s plays&#58; The "first Henriad" (Henry VI, part 1, Henry VI, part 2, Henry VI, part 3, and Richard III), and the "second Henriad" (Richard II; Henry IV, Part 1; Henry IV, Part 2; and Henry V).https&#58;//web.archive.org/web/20210709182046/https&#58;//www.thegreatcoursesdaily.com/richard-ii-shakespeare-questions-divine-right-of-kings/

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The title translated into English of an epic poem, Le Henriade, by Voltaire.
