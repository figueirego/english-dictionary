### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A paved part of road, usually in a village or a town.

   **Example:** Walk down the street until you see a hotel on the right.

   **Example:** The man wearing a red coat is standing on the street.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A road as above, but including the sidewalks (pavements) and buildings.

   **Example:** I live on the street down from Joyce Avenue.

   **Example:** I've been shopping in Oxford Street.

   **Source synonyms:** way

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The roads that run perpendicular to avenues in a grid layout.

   **Usage:** Canada; US; specifically

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Metonymic senses&#58;
   1. The people who live in such a road, as a neighborhood.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Metonymic senses&#58;
   1. The people who spend a great deal of time on the street in urban areas, especially, the young, the poor, the unemployed, and those engaged in illegal activities.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Metonymic senses&#58;
   1. An illicit or contraband source, especially of drugs.

      **Example:** I got some pot cheap on the street.

      **Example:** The seized drugs had a street value of $5 million.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Metonymic senses&#58;
   1. Ellipsis of Wall Street.

      **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; ellipsis

      **Example:** Orders were reported to have increased 2% monthly, ahead of the 1.2% expected by the street.

      **Example:** Professional services and other revenue made up $577 million, edging out street estimates for $541.4 million.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance

      **Alternative form of:** [Wall Street](../345/wall-street--d148f25bd6d6.md)

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Living in the streets.

   **Usage:** attributive

   **Example:** a street cat

   **Example:** a street urchin

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. Streetwise slang.

   **Usage:** slang; uncountable

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. People in general, as a source of information.

   **Usage:** in-plural; slang

   **Example:** Streets say something's happening tomorrow.

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. A great distance.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** He's streets ahead of his sister in all the subjects in school.

<a id="r1-s12"></a>

**Source sense R1.S12**

12. Each of the three opportunities that players have to bet, after the flop, turn and river.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** card-games; poker
