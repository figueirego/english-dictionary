### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Bound or obliged in law or equity; responsible; answerable.

   **Example:** The surety is liable for the debt of his principal.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Subject; susceptible; prone.

   **Example:** This crime is liable to imprisonment for life.

   **Example:** A man liable to heart disease.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Exposed to a certain contingency or causality, more or less probable.

   **Example:** Electronic devices are liable to malfunction when exposed to extreme heat.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Likely.

   **Usage:** predicative

   **Example:** Someone is liable to slip on your icy sidewalk.

   **Source context (verbatim):** with to-infinitive
