### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Immoderate, excessive.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Not temperate, of no good influence for one's confort or soundness; (figuratively) unwholesome, stormy.

   **Usage:** obsolete; air or elements

   **Source context (verbatim):** of the weather

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Not properly tempered; disordered through excess or deficiency of some constituent; (by extensions, of bodily or mental condition) disordered, out of order; diseased, out of health; ill-conditioned.

   **Usage:** obsolete

   **Source context (verbatim):** in the obsoleted theory of humours
