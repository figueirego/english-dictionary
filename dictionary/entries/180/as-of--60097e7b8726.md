## Preposition

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. At or by a specified time, with the implication that the said condition will be true from that time onward.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** All leave is cancelled as of now.

   **Example:** The server will be down for maintenance as of tomorrow afternoon.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. At a specified time, with the implication that the said condition was or will be true at that time.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** The US has a population of 300 million, as of 2006.
