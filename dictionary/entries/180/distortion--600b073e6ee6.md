### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An act of distorting.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

2. A result of distorting.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A misrepresentation of the truth.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The story he told was a bit of a distortion.

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Noise or other artifacts caused in the electronic reproduction of sound or music.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** This recording sounds awful due to the distortion.
