### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any member of the genus Nelumbo of aquatic plants in the family Nelumbonaceae.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A water lily (Nymphaea), especially those of Egypt or India.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A legendary plant eaten by the Lotophagi of the Odyssey that caused drowsiness and euphoria.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A number of other plants bearing lotus in their scientific or common names (see Derived terms below).
   1. A date plum or Caucasian persimmon (Diospyros lotus).

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A number of other plants bearing lotus in their scientific or common names (see Derived terms below).
   1. Any member of the genus Lotus, a terrestrial genus with small flowers that includes bird's-foot trefoils and deervetches.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A number of other plants bearing lotus in their scientific or common names (see Derived terms below).
   1. A lotustree or lotus jujube (Ziziphus lotus), a shrub with edible fruit.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. An architectural motif of ancient Egyptian temples.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Ellipsis of lotus position.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; ellipsis
