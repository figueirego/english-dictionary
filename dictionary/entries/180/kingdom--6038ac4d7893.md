### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A realm having a king or queen as its actual or nominal sovereign.

   **Source synonyms:** kingship

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A realm, region, or conceptual space where something is dominant.

   **Example:** the kingdom of thought

   **Example:** the kingdom of the dead

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
