## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One of the two isometric forms of propanol; a primary alcohol with the formula CH₃CH₂CH₂OH, formed naturally in small amounts during many fermentation processes and used as a solvent in the pharmaceutical industry mainly for resins and cellulose esters.

   **Usage:** uncountable
