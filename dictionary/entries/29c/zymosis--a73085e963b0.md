### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A fermentation

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A process by which an infectious disease is believed to be developed, seen as similar to the process of fermentation, and caused by so-called microzymes, also known as zymes.

   **Usage:** obsolete; uncountable; usually

   **Example:** zymosis of a healthy organism
