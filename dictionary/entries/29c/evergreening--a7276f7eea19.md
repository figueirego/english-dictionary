## Noun · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of various actions that extend the lifespan of some object, idea, practice, etc., making it evergreen.
   1. The practice of slightly altering a drug to extend patent protections.

      **Usage:** uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** intellectual-property; law; medicine; patent-law; pharmaceuticals; pharmacology; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of various actions that extend the lifespan of some object, idea, practice, etc., making it evergreen.
   1. The practice of repeatedly refinancing the debt of an unprofitable company so as to postpone the day of its default (to some indefinite future time), which in many cases may render it a zombie company.

      **Usage:** uncountable
