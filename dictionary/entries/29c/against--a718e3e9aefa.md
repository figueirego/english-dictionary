### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In a contrary direction to.

   **Example:** It is hard work to swim against the current.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In physical opposition to; in collision with.

   **Example:** The rain pounds against the window.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. In physical contact with, so as to abut or be supported by.

   **Example:** The ladder was leaning against the wall.

   **Example:** The puppy rested its head against a paw.

   **Example:** The kennel was put against the back wall.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Close to, alongside.

   **Example:** A row of trees stood against a fence.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. In front of; before (a background).

   **Example:** The giant was silhouetted against the door.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. In contrast or comparison with.

   **Example:** He stands out against his classmates.

   **Example:** This report sets out the risks against the benefits.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. In competition with, versus.

   **Example:** The Tigers will play against the Bears this weekend.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Contrary to; in conflict with.

   **Example:** Doing this is against my principles.

   **Example:** It is against the law to smoke on these premises.

   **Example:** There was no car in sight so we crossed against the red light.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. In opposition to.

   **Example:** His mind is closed against all arguments.

   **Example:** Are you against freedom of choice?

   **Example:** He waged a ten-year campaign against the company that was polluting the river.

   **Example:** I'd bet against his succeeding.

   **Source antonyms:** for

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. Of betting odds, denoting a worse-than-even chance.

   **Example:** That horse is fifty-to-one against, so it has virtually no chance of winning.

   **Source antonyms:** on

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. In exchange for.

   **Example:** The vouchers are redeemable against West End shows and theatre breaks.

<a id="r1-s12"></a>

**Source sense R1.S12**

12. As counterbalance to.

<a id="r1-s13"></a>

**Source sense R1.S13**

13. As a charge on.

   **Example:** Tax is levied against income from sales.

<a id="r1-s14"></a>

**Source sense R1.S14**

14. As protection from.

   **Example:** He turned the umbrella against the wind.

<a id="r1-s15"></a>

**Source sense R1.S15**

15. In anticipation of; in preparation for (a particular time, event etc.).

   **Example:** The stores are kept well stocked against a time of need.

<a id="r1-s16"></a>

**Source sense R1.S16**

16. To be paid now in contrast to the following amount to be paid later under specified circumstances, usually that a movie is made or has started filming.

   **Usage:** Hollywood

   **Example:** The studio weren't sure the movie would ever get made, so they only paid $50,000 against $200,000. That way they wouldn't be out very much if filming never began.

<a id="r1-s17"></a>

**Source sense R1.S17**

17. Exposed to.

   **Usage:** obsolete
