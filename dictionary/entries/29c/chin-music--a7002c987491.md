## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A fastball thrown near the batter's chin, often to set up a subsequent pitch thrown low and outside.

   **Usage:** informal; uncountable

   **Example:** Jones backed Smith off the plate with a little chin music.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; baseball; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A bouncer aimed near the batsman's chin.

   **Usage:** informal; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; cricket; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

   **Usage:** US; dated; slang; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

   **Usage:** informal; uncountable; folk music
