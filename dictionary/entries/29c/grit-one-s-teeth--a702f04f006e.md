### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To clench one's teeth together tightly because of pain, anger, frustration, or self-assurance.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To face up to a difficult or disagreeable situation and deal with it.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** There's a storm coming, so I grit my teeth and start rowing with all my might.

   **Source synonyms:** face up to; bite the bullet; grin and bear it; suck it up
