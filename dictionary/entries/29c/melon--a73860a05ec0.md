### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Some of the plants of the family Cucurbitaceae grown for food, generally not including the cucumber.
   1. Genus Cucumis, the true melon (including various cultigens like honeydew and cantaloupes), the horned melon, and others.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Some of the plants of the family Cucurbitaceae grown for food, generally not including the cucumber.
   1. Genus Citrullus, the watermelon and others.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Some of the plants of the family Cucurbitaceae grown for food, generally not including the cucumber.
   1. Benincasa hispida, the winter melon.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Some of the plants of the family Cucurbitaceae grown for food, generally not including the cucumber.
   1. Momordica charantia, the bitter melon.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

**Source sense R1.S6**

   **Usage:** uncountable
