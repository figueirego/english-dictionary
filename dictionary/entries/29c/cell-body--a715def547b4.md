## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A cell of a unicellular organism, constituting its entire body.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The main body of a cell, containing its nucleus and organelles (as opposed to various projections or elements of external structure such as cell wall, flagella, dendrites, etc.).

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; cytology; medicine; natural-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The main body of a cell, containing its nucleus and organelles (as opposed to various projections or elements of external structure such as cell wall, flagella, dendrites, etc.).
   1. The nucleus-containing central part of a neuron exclusive of its axons and dendrites that is the major structural element of the gray matter of the brain and spinal cord, the ganglia, and the retina.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; cytology; medicine; natural-sciences; neurology; neuroscience; sciences

      **Source synonyms:** neurocyton; perikaryon; soma
