## Noun · Record 2

### Forms

- cease-and-desist — canonical

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A written demand that a party stops and does not resume a certain behavior, such as copyright infringement, slander, or libel, threatening legal action if the recipient fails to do so.

   **Usage:** attributive

   **Example:** She received a cease-and-desist from his solicitors soon after the podcast.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. An order by a government agency, sanctioned by a court, that one must stop and not resume a certain (usually illegal) behavior.

   **Usage:** US; attributive

   **Example:** His remarks did not stop the courts from issuing the cease-and-desist order.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law
