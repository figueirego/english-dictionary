## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A set of measurements of a patient's condition taken regularly to assess the state of bodily functions&#58; CSM, skin signs, lung sounds, temperature, pulse, blood pressure, and respiration.

   **Usage:** plural; plural-only

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** vitals

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** broadly; figuratively; plural; plural-only

   **Example:** the vital signs of the economy
