### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A hanging flap of skin or other tissue, especially one covering the cornea (in trachoma) or cartilage (in rheumatoid arthritis).

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A type of accessory cloud, looking like shreds either attached to, or separated from, the main cloud formation; mainly associated with nimbostratus, cumulus, and cumulonimbus.
