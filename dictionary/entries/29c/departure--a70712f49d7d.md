### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of departing or something that has departed.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The departure was scheduled for noon.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A deviation from a plan or procedure.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A death.

   **Usage:** countable; euphemistic; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The distance due east or west made by a ship in its course reckoned in plane sailing as the product of the distance sailed and the sine of the angle made by the course with the meridian.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; navigation

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The difference in easting between the two ends of a line or curve.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The area is computed by latitudes and departures.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; natural-sciences; surveying

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The desertion by a party to any pleading of the ground taken by him in his last antecedent pleading, and the adoption of another

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable
