### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Pockets long enough inside to accommodate more than the average number or size of contents.

   **Usage:** literally; plural; plural-normally

   **Example:** I like the deep pockets on these pants because my cellphone doesn't fall out when I run with my dog.

   **Example:** It was a good thing his coat had deep pockets because he has big hands and it was quite cold that day.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An ample supply of money, especially money which one is willing to spend.

   **Usage:** figuratively; plural; plural-normally

   **Example:** We need an investor with deep pockets to help us get our invention to market.
