### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of various procedures of enlarging a topological space to make it compact.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences; topology

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The space resulting from any such procedure.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences; topology

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any modification of a theory such that an infinite parameter becomes finite.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics
