### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make reparation, compensation, amends or satisfaction for an offence, crime, mistake or deficiency.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** He tried to atone for his mistakes by working harder.

   **Example:** She gave a donation to atone for her past actions.

   **Example:** The ritual was performed to atone for sins.

   **Source synonyms:** expiate; propitiate; repair

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To bring at one or at concordance; to reconcile; to suffer appeasement.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To agree or accord; to be in accordance or harmony.

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To unite in making.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To absolve (someone else) of wrongdoing, especially by standing as an equivalent.

   **Usage:** proscribed
