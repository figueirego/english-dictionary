### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. That causes the mind to boggle; that is beyond one's ability to understand or figure out.

   **Example:** Faced with a mind-boggling selection of special-purpose shampoos, he gave up and simply purchased something inexpensive with a pleasant fragrance.

   **Source synonyms:** astounding; bewildering; bogglesome; inexcogitable (obsolete); mind-blowing; mystifying; overwhelming; stupefying; unimaginable
