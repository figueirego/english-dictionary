## Proper noun · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The system of remuneration in which fishermen or merchant mariners are paid a lay (a share of the vessel's profit) plus the value of room and board derived from being shipboard and eating in the mess.

   **Example:** He preferred the lay system because there was a chance that he could really clean up if the luck held out.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** maritime; nautical; transport

### Senses

**Source sense R2.S1**

   **Usage:** UK; archaic; especially

   **Example:** They farmed this whole valley on the lay system back then, and the lord kept the horses and ploughmen busy.
