## Noun

### Forms

- window dressings — plural
- window-dressing — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The decorative display of retail merchandise in store windows; the goods and trimmings used in such a display.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** shop window

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The process, skill or task of creating such a display.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A means of creating a deceptively favourable impression of something or someone; something for appearance only.

   **Usage:** figuratively; uncountable

   **Example:** These latest modifications are mere window dressing; the same problems remain.

   **Source synonyms:** eyewash
