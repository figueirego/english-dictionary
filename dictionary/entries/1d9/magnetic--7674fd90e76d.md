### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of, relating to, operating by, or caused by magnetism.

   **Example:** a magnetic recorder

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Having the properties of a magnet, especially the ability to draw or pull.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Determined by earth's magnetic fields.

   **Example:** magnetic north

   **Example:** the magnetic meridian

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Having an extraordinary ability to attract&#58; attractive, alluring, persuasive

   **Example:** He has a magnetic personality.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Having, susceptible to, or induced by, animal magnetism.

   **Usage:** archaic

   **Example:** a magnetic sleep
