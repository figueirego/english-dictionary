### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To disallow; to proscribe.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Smoking in the restaurant is forbidden.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To deny, exclude from, or warn off, by express command.

   **Usage:** ditransitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To oppose, hinder, or prevent, as if by an effectual command.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** An impassable river forbids the approach of the army.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To accurse; to blast.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To defy; to challenge.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

   **Example:** What part of "no" do you forbid to understand?

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** prohibit; disallow; ban; veto
