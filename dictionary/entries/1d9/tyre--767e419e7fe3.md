## Noun · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /taɪə(ɹ)/ — source labels: UK

### Forms

- tyres — plural
- tire — alternative; US

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The ring-shaped protective covering around a wheel which is usually made of rubber or plastic composite and is either pneumatic or solid.

   **Example:** pneumatic tyres

   **Example:** runflat tyres

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The metal rim, or metal covering on a rim, of a (wooden or metal) wheel, usually of steel or formerly wrought iron, as found on (horse-drawn or railway) carriages and wagons and on locomotives.

   **Example:** iron tyres for the coach and iron shoes for the horse

   **Example:** tyres and rails of steel, and every axle with roller bearings
