## Conjunction

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used to indicate that the following clause is not true and that this negatively impacts the importance of the preceding clause.

   **Example:** I didn't finish my dinner today. Not that my sister is bad at cooking, I just wasn't that hungry.
