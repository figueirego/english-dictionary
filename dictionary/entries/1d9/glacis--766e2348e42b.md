### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A gentle incline.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A gentle incline.
   1. A gentle sloping landform created by the deposition or erosion of material.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; geology; geomorphology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A gentle incline.
   1. A gentle incline in front of a fortification which protects it from cannon fire and exposes attackers to more effective return fire from defenders.

      **Usage:** also; figuratively

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** architecture; government; military; politics; war

      **Source synonyms:** talus

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A gentle incline.
   1. In full glacis plate&#58; the angled armour plate on the front of a tank which protects it from projectiles; also (often nautical), such a plate protecting an opening (for example, on a ship).

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A gentle incline.
   1. A device for sorting mail which slides parcels across a sloped surface.

      **Usage:** postal
