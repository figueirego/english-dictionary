### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A polite form of address for a woman or lady.

   **Example:** Mrs Grey wondered if the outfit she was trying on made her look fat. The sales assistant just said, “It suits you, madam”.

   **Example:** Later, Mrs Grey was sitting in her favourite tea shop. “Would madam like the usual cream cakes and patisserie with her tea?” the waitress asked.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The mistress of a household.

   **Source synonyms:** dame; woman; lady; matron; mistress

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A conceited or quarrelsome girl.

   **Usage:** colloquial

   **Example:** Selina kept pushing and shoving during musical chairs. The nursery school teacher said she was a bad-tempered little madam.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A woman who runs a brothel, particularly one that specializes in finding prostitutes for rich and important clients.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Example:** After she grew too old to work as a prostitute, she became a madam.

   **Source synonyms:** abbess; bawd; lady abbess; nookie-bookie; whoremistress; brothelkeeper

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A hated or contemptuous woman; used as a general term of abuse

   **Usage:** India; derogatory; slang
