## Noun · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈdiː.vi.ənt/ — source labels: UK

### Forms

- deviants — plural

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A person who deviates, especially from norms of social behavior.

   **Example:** He was branded as a deviant and ostracized.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. A thing, phenomenon, or trend that deviates from an expectation or pattern.

   **Example:** As the graph shows, the March sales trend is the deviant.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. A member of the online art community DeviantArt.

   **Usage:** Internet
