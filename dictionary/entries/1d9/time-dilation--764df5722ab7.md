### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The slowing of the passage of time experienced by objects in motion relative to an observer (measurable only at relativistic speeds), or due to being in a gravitational well.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; relativity

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The perceived change in the speed of time experienced due to psychological stress, hormones, or drugs; the rate of time perception, chronoception.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
