## Noun · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /dɹoʊv/ — source labels: General-American

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A cattle drive, or a drive of another livestock animal (e.g. sheep).
   1. The herd being driven in a cattle (or other animal) drive; thus, a number of animals driven to market or new pastures; a group of livestock animals more generally.

      **Usage:** collective

      **Source synonyms:** drift

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A cattle drive, or a drive of another livestock animal (e.g. sheep).
   1. A large number of people on the move.

      **Usage:** broadly; figuratively; plural-normally

      **Example:** in droves

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A cattle drive, or a drive of another livestock animal (e.g. sheep).
   1. A group of other animals, especially hares when seen in coursing or hunting.

      **Usage:** collective

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A road or track along which cattle are habitually, used to be or could be driven; a droveway.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A narrow drain or channel used in the irrigation of land.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A broad chisel used to bring stone to a nearly smooth surface.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The grooved surface of stone finished by the drove chisel.
