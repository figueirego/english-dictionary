## Noun

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ɛnˈæktmənt/ — source labels: Canada; General-American

### Forms

- enactments — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of enacting, or the state of being enacted.
   1. The act of playing the part of.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Example:** The actors' powerful enactment of the play was breathtaking.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The act of enacting, or the state of being enacted.
   1. The act of making (a bill) into law.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Example:** The enactment of this law will be a great step backward for our country.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A piece of legislation that has been properly authorized by a legislative body.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The enactments passed by the council that year included sweeping reforms.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law
