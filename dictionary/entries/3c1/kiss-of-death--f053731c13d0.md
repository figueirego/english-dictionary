## Noun

### Forms

- kisses of death — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A kiss on the cheek that signifies the death of the receiver, as delivered by a mob boss or one with such influence.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Something that may seem good and favorable but that actually brings ruin to hopes, plans, etc.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; informal

   **Example:** The role in the soap opera was the kiss of death for Ann's career as a theatrical actress.
