### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Someone advertising for customers in an aggressive way.

   **Example:** Be careful of the ticket touts outside the arena; they are famed for selling counterfeits.

   **Source synonyms:** barker; pitchman; spruiker (Australia)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person at a racecourse who offers supposedly inside information on which horse is likely to win.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An informer in the Irish Republican Army.

   **Usage:** colloquial

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A spy for a smuggler, thief, or the like.

   **Usage:** archaic; colloquial
