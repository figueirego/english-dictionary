### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A line formed by the surface of the water on the hull of a ship when she is afloat; any of a series of short lines marked on the hull to show where the waterline would be under different loadings

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A horizontal line indicating the shape of an airfoil.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** aeronautics; aerospace; aviation; business; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A line showing where the water has been.

   **Source synonyms:** watermark

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The line formed by where the water level of a body of water currently is&#58; where the water's surface currently meets the bank; it may move higher or lower depending on the weather, tide, or season.

   **Source synonyms:** water's edge

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The inner rim of the eyelid, just behind the lash line; primarily used in reference to the application of eyeliner.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** cosmetics; lifestyle

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A water pipeline.
