## Noun

### Forms

- cover stories — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A story included in a publication that is highlighted on the front cover.

   **Example:** We didn't even think it was that important, at first, and yet by the end of the week it had become the cover story of the next issue.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** media

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A fictitious account that is intended to hide one's real motive.

   **Example:** The cover story of an intelligence agent posing as a foreign service officer is called a diplomatic cover in the trade.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** espionage; government; military; politics; war

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any fictitious account used to hide, distract from, or falsely explain the truth which one intends to conceal.

   **Usage:** broadly
