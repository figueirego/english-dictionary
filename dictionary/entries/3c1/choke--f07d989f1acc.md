### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To be unable to breathe because of obstruction of the windpipe (for instance food or other objects that go down the wrong way, or fumes or particles in the air that cause the throat to constrict).

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Ever since he choked on a bone, he has refused to eat fish.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To prevent (someone) from breathing or talking by strangling or filling the windpipe.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The collar of this shirt is too tight; it’s choking me.

   **Source synonyms:** asphyxiate; strangle; suffocate; throttle

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To obstruct (a passage, etc.) by filling it up or clogging it.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to choke a cave passage with boulders and mud

   **Source synonyms:** block up; bung up; clog; congest; jam; obstruct; stop up

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To hinder or check, as growth, expansion, progress, etc.; to kill (a plant by robbing it of nutrients); to extinguish (fire by robbing it of oxygen).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** choke out; stifle

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To perform badly at a crucial stage of a competition, especially when one appears to be clearly winning.

   **Usage:** colloquial; intransitive

   **Example:** He has a lot of talent, but he tends to choke under pressure.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To move one's fingers very close to the tip of a pencil, brush or other art tool.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To hold the club or bat lower on the shaft in order to shorten one's swing.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; baseball; games; golf; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To be checked or stopped, as if by choking

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source synonyms:** stick

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To check or stop (an utterance or voice) as if by choking.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. To have a feeling of strangulation in one's throat as a result of passion or strong emotion.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. To give (someone) a feeling of strangulation as a result of passion or strong emotion.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s12"></a>

**Source sense R1.S12**

12. To say (something) with one’s throat constricted (due to emotion, for example).

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s13"></a>

   **Usage:** transitive
