### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A bell used in fowling at night, to frighten birds, and, with a sudden light, to make them fly into a net.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A bell to be hung on the neck of a sheep or cow.
