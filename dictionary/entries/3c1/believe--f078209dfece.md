### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To accept as true, particularly without absolute certainty (i.e., as opposed to knowing).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** If you believe the numbers, you'll agree we need change.

   **Example:** I believe there are faeries.

   **Source synonyms:** understand; feel; see; buy (informal); swallow (informal); credit

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To accept that someone is telling the truth.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Why did I ever believe you?

   **Source synonyms:** trust; Adam and Eve (Cockney; slang)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To have been persuaded to accept the factuality of something despite a lack of sufficient evidence therefore.
   1. To have been convinced by rational means to accept the truth of a proposition.

      **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To have been persuaded to accept the factuality of something despite a lack of sufficient evidence therefore.
   1. To have been influenced by arational means (such as emotional experience, the imagination of need, etc.) to accept the truth of a proposition.

      **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To have religious faith; to believe in a greater truth.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** After that night in the church, I believed.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To opine, think, reckon.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Do you think this is good? —Hmm, I believe it's okay.

   **Source synonyms:** hold

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. \[with in\]
   1. To ascribe existence to.

      **Example:** Do you believe in God / the Easter Bunny / ghosts?

      **Example:** Since I don't believe in reincarnation, I believe that the only way to eliminate suffering is to die.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. \[with in\]
   1. To believe that (something) is right or desirable.

      **Example:** I don't believe in sex before marriage.

      **Example:** I don't believe in making my bed.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. \[with in\]
   1. To have confidence in the ability or power of.

      **Example:** I believe in you, man! You can do it!
