### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A cultivar of pea, Pisum sativum, having an edible pod like the snow pea, but differing from the latter in that its pod is more rounded.

   **Source synonyms:** sugar snap pea
