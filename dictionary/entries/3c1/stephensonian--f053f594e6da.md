### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of or relating to George Stephenson (1781–1848), English engineer who built the first public intercity railway line to use steam locomotives.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of or relating to Neal Stephenson (born 1959), American writer of speculative fiction and cyberpunk novels.
