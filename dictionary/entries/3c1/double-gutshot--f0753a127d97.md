### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A drawing hand in which there are two gutshot possibilities, i.e., two opportunities at making a straight, either of which needs only one card.

   **Example:** Example of double gutshot&#58; My hole cards are 87. The board is A64T. With the right card on the river, I can make 8-high straight or T-high straight.
