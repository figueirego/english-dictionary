## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An eruption; the sudden appearance of a rash, disease, etc.

   **Example:** Any epidemic outbreak causes understandable panic.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A sudden increase.

   **Example:** There has been an outbreak of vandalism at the school.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An outburst or sudden eruption, especially of violence and mischief.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** There has been an outbreak of broken windows in the street.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Synonym of breakout (“escape from prison”).

   **Source synonyms:** breakout (synonym; synonym-of)
