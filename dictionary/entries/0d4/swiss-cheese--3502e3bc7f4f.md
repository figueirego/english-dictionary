**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Synonym of Emmentaler, a Swiss cheese with many holes

   **Usage:** informal; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** Emmentaler (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** colloquial; uncountable
