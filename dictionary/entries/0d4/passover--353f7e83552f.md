### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The one-day Biblical feast or festival (not a holy day) that begins at twilight at the beginning of the fourteenth day of the first month (Abib or Nisan 14), during which the firstborn sons of the Israelites were passed over by God while those of the Egyptians were killed; this feast day is then immediately followed by the seven-day Feast of Unleavened Bread (Nisan 15 to 21; the first and seventh days are holy days or yearly Sabbaths).

   **Usage:** Judaism; also; attributive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biblical; lifestyle; religion

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Synonym of Last Supper (“the meal that Jesus Christ ate with his disciples on the night before his death”).

   **Usage:** also; attributive; broadly

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity

   **Source synonyms:** Last Supper (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Synonym of Paschal Lamb (“Jesus Christ symbolized as a sacrifice for the sins of humankind”).

   **Usage:** also; attributive; broadly; figuratively

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity

   **Source synonyms:** Paschal Lamb (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The seven-day (in Reform Judaism) or eight-day (Orthodox and Conservative Judaism) Jewish festival of Pesach (also called the Feast of Unleavened Bread (מַצּוֹת (matsót)), commemorating the Biblical story of the Exodus.

   **Usage:** Judaism; also; attributive; broadly

   **Source synonyms:** Feast of Matsot; Feast of Matzot; Festival of Matsot; Festival of Matzot; Festival of Unleavened Bread

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Synonym of Paschal Lamb (“the lamb traditionally eaten during Pesach”); also, the meal at which this lamb is eaten.

   **Usage:** Judaism; also; attributive; broadly

   **Source synonyms:** Paschal Lamb (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A lamb which was sacrificed during the original Passover (sense 1).

   **Usage:** Judaism; also; attributive; broadly; obsolete

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A major festival or observance.

   **Usage:** also; attributive; figuratively
