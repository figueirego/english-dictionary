### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A pleasing sight; something that is beautiful to look at, particularly after a long period of deprivation.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** After seventeen days of rain and floods, the 4 am sunrise was a sight for sore eyes.

   **Source antonyms:** eyesore
