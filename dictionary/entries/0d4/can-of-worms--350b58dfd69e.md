## Noun

### Forms

- cans of worms — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A complex, troublesome situation, which produces (or whose resolution will produce) many problems.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** If someone gets a promotion that might not be deserved, it could open up a whole can of worms with the other employees.

   **Example:** Questioning the decision would definitely open a can of worms.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see can, worm.
