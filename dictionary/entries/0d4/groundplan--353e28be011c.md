### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A diagram showing the physical layout of a set, especially the placement of structures on the stage, as opposed to items on the walls or suspended from the ceiling.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; theater

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A diagram, usually to scale, showing the layout of the ground level of a building or other physical structure; floorplan.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A framework or pattern showing the form of something, without all the details.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A hypothetical anatomical form from which members of a clade are adaptations.

   **Usage:** evolutionary theory
