### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A plant of certain species of the genus Brassica, or of related genera (especially Sinapis alba, in the family Brassicaceae, with yellow flowers, and linear seed pods).

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source synonyms:** senvy

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Powder or paste made from seeds of the mustard plant, and used as a condiment or a spice.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** When the waitress brought the food, I asked whether she had any Dijon mustard.

   **Example:** He loved having mustard and toast for breakfast.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The leaves of the mustard plant, used as a salad.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** Mustard and cress sandwiches.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Dark yellow colour, the colour of mustard.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. One of a family of vesicants containing one or more 2-chloroethyl (C₂H₄Cl) groups, commonly used in chemical warfare and cancer chemotherapy.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The tomalley of a crab, which resembles the condiment.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Ellipsis of mustard gas.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; ellipsis; uncountable; usually

   **Alternative form of:** [mustard gas](../168/mustard-gas--5a02a45569ef.md)

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Energy, power (when throwing a baseball).

   **Usage:** colloquial; uncountable; usually
