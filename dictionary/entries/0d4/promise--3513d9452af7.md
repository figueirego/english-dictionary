### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An oath or affirmation; a vow.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** When I make a promise, I always stick to it.

   **Example:** I asked him not to, and we all heard his promise that he wouldn't.

   **Example:** He broke his promise&#58; he said he'd return my jewellery, but never did.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A transaction between two persons whereby the first person undertakes in the future to render some service or gift to the second person or devotes something valuable now and here to their use.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Something promised.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** That jewelry was a promise to me.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Reason to expect improvement or success; potential.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** His grade are poor, but I see promise in him.

   **Example:** Your claim has little promise in court.

   **Example:** She shows great promise as an actress.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A placeholder object representing the eventual result of an asynchronous operation.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; programming; sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Bestowal or fulfillment of what is promised.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete

   **Example:** It is time they receive their promise.

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To commit to (some action or outcome), or to assure (a person) of such commitment; to make an oath or vow.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive; ditransitive

   **Example:** If you promise not to tell anyone, I will let you have this cake for free.

   **Example:** She promised to never return to this town again.

   **Example:** He promised me a big kiss if I would drive him to the airport.

   **Example:** I can't promise success, but I'll do the best I can.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To give grounds for expectation, especially of something good.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** The clouds promise rain.
