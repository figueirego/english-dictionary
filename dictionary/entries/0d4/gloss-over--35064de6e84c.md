### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To cover up a mistake or a crime; to hush up or whitewash.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** They glossed over the problem, hoping that the customers wouldn't notice.

   **Source synonyms:** paper over

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To treat something with less care than it in some ways deserves; to ignore, or at least to discount or dismiss for the current purposes.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** This book only glosses over quantum mechanics; it doesn't go into detail.
