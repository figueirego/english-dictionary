### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To live off the earnings of a prostitute.

   **Usage:** Australia; obsolete; slang

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To not earn one's keep, to live off someone else or off welfare when one could be working.

   **Usage:** Australia; New-Zealand; slang

   **Source synonyms:** freeload; sponge

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To avoid one's responsibilities; to leave it to others to perform duties that one is expected to perform.

   **Usage:** Australia; New-Zealand; slang

   **Source synonyms:** shirk

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To do nothing, to be idle, especially when there is work to be done.

   **Usage:** Australia; New-Zealand; slang

   **Source synonyms:** idle; laze; lounge

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To take some benefit and give nothing in return.

   **Usage:** Australia; New-Zealand; slang

   **Example:** Can I bludge a cigarette off you?

   **Source synonyms:** cadge; scrounge
