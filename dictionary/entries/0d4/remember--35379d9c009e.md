### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To recall from one's memory; to have an image in one's memory.

   **Source synonyms:** think; place

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To memorize; to put something into memory.

   **Example:** Please remember this formula!

   **Source synonyms:** retain

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To keep in mind; to be mindful of.

   **Example:** Remember what I've said.

   **Source synonyms:** mind

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To not forget (to do something required)

   **Example:** Remember to lock the door when you go out.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To convey greetings from.

   **Example:** Please remember me to your brother.

   **Example:** She asks to be remembered to you all.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To put in mind; to remind (also used reflexively).

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To engage in the process of recalling memories.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** You don't have to remind him; he remembers very well.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To give (a person) money as a token of appreciation of past service or friendship.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** My aunt remembered me in her will, leaving me several thousand pounds.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To commemorate, to have a remembrance ceremony.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Today we remember and honour those who have served.
