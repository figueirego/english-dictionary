### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In accordance with reason.

   **Example:** They were able handle their disagreements reasonably.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Fairly; satisfactorily; not extremely.

   **Example:** The shoes were reasonably priced.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Quite; to some extent.

   **Example:** That car goes reasonably fast.
