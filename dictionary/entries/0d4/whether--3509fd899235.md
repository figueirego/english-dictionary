### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Introduces a simple indirect question (with no correlative).

   **Example:** Whether we'll be on time I'm unsure.

   **Example:** Do you know whether he's coming?

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Indicates doubt between possibilities (usually with correlative or).

   **Example:** He chose the correct answer, but whether by luck or by skill I don't know.

   **Example:** I can't decide whether to get divorced now or wait.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Introduces a disjunctive adverbial clause qualifying the main clause (with correlative or).

   **Example:** He's coming, whether you like it or not.

   **Example:** I'll enjoy playing with my friends again, whether we win or lose.

   **Example:** Whether or not you're successful, you can be sure you did your best.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Introduces a direct question between alternatives (often with correlative or).
