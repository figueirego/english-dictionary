## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The convention by which one bit in the representation of a number indicates the numeric sign, while the rest indicate the magnitude.

   **Example:** The IEEE standard for floating point uses signed magnitude for the mantissa.
