### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A long, slender, chiefly terrestrial amphibian of the order Caudata, superficially resembling a lizard.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A creature much like a lizard that is resistant to and lives in fire (in which it is often depicted in heraldry), hence the elemental being of fire.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; mysticism; mythology; philosophy; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A metal utensil with a flat head which is heated and put over a dish to brown the top.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** cooking; food; lifestyle

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A small broiler (North America) or grill (Britain) that heats the food from above, used in professional cookery primarily for browning.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The chef first put the steak under the salamander to sear the outside.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** cooking; food; lifestyle

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A pouched gopher (Geomys pinetis etc.)

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A large poker.

   **Usage:** UK; countable; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Solidified material in a furnace hearth.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; metallurgy; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

   **Source synonyms:** Shadrach

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; construction; manufacturing
