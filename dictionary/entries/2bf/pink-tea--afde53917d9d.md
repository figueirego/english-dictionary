### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A fashionable formal tea party or other social gathering; specifically, one organized and attended chiefly by women to discuss matters of suffrage, raise funds for charity, etc.

   **Usage:** US; countable; historical; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any exclusive social gathering.

   **Usage:** US; broadly; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Something (as an event or policy) excessively polite and refined; specifically, one regarded as ineffective and weak.

   **Usage:** US; also; attributive; countable; figuratively; uncountable

   **Source context (verbatim):** in the negative

   **Source synonyms:** tea party

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A hot drink from the Indian subcontinent with a pink colour, made with gunpowder tea, baking soda, and milk.

   **Usage:** India; Pakistan; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** Kashmiri tea; noon chai
