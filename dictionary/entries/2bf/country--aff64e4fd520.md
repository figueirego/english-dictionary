### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The territory of a nation; a sovereign state or a region once independent and still distinct in institutions, language, etc.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A large area regarded as distinct from other areas, e.g. because it is inhabited by a specific group of people

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Basque Country

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An area of land of undefined extent; a region, a district.

   **Usage:** UK; countable; especially; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An area of land of undefined extent; a region, a district.
   1. An area of land of indefinite extent or of more or less definite extent in relation to human occupation, especially characterized by its particular physical features, or its suitability for a particular activity or connected with its population (by race, dialect, culture, etc.) or a person, especially a writer, or their works.

      **Usage:** UK; countable; especially; uncountable

      **Example:** Thomas Hardy country

      **Example:** Hikers love the wild country that lies northwest of this river confluence.

      **Example:** Since this was rebel country, checkpoints were set up along the road.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A rural area, as opposed to a town or city; the countryside.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually preceded by “the”

   **Example:** I grew up in the country with a 12-gauge shotgun.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The inhabitants or people of a district, region, or nation; the populace, the public.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** a scandal that rocked the country

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Traditional lands of Indigenous people with embedded cultural, spiritual, cosmological, ecological, and physical attributes and values.

   **Usage:** Australia; capitalized; countable; uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Ellipsis of country music.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; countable; ellipsis; uncountable

   **Example:** a country song

   **Example:** a country singer

   **Example:** a country festival

   **Alternative form of:** [country music](../07a/country-music--1eaed6b566ef.md)

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. The spirit of the country (rural places)&#58; the spirit of country folkways; those folkways.

   **Usage:** countable; informal; uncountable

   **Example:** You can take the boy out of the country, but you can't take the country out of the boy.
