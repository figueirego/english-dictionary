## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A principle used in physiology and medicine, originally applied to measure cardiac output, stating that the total uptake (or release) of a substance by an organ is equal to the product of the blood flow through the organ and the arteriovenous concentration difference of the substance.
