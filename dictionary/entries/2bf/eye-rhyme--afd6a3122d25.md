### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A pair of words (or syllables) that, because of their spelling, look as if they rhyme but, because of different pronunciation, do not. Many eye rhymes are historic rhymes.

   **Source synonyms:** sight rhyme
