### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who, or that which, roots; one that tears up by the roots.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A type of heavy machinery similar to a plow for breaking up soil, concrete, asphalt, etc.

   **Usage:** broadly

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A blade for producing a narrow groove in a piece of wood.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** arts; business; carpentry; construction; crafts; hobbies; lifestyle; manufacturing; woodworking

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A device for boring a pathway through a blocked drain or sewer.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. One who roots or rummages through something.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**
