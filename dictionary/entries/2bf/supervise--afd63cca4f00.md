### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To oversee or direct (a task or organization).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Without someone to supervise them, the group will lack direction.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To oversee a task, organization, etc.; to provide supervision; to act as a supervisor.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To look over so as to read; to peruse.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive
