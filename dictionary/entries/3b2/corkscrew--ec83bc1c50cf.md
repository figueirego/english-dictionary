### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An implement for opening bottles that are sealed by a cork. Sometimes specifically such an implement that includes a screw-shaped part, or worm.

   **Example:** I opened the wine with a corkscrew.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The screw-shaped worm of a typical corkscrew.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A type of sharp, twisting punch, often one thrown close and from the side.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** boxing; government; hobbies; lifestyle; martial-arts; military; politics; sports; war

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

   **Usage:** amusement rides
