### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Stock (material) suitable for making shims; often in the form of sheets of metal, plastic, or wood of various thicknesses, ready to have pieces cut out of them as needed.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; trades

   **Example:** They keep 1-thou and 2-thou shimstock handy when they're building these assemblies.
