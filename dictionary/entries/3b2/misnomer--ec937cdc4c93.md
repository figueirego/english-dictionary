### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A mistake in the naming of a person or place; a misidentification.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An incorrect use of a term, especially one which is misleading; a misname.

   **Usage:** broadly

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A term which is misleading, even if firmly established, technically correct, or both.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** The name Chinese checkers is a misnomer since the game has nothing to do with China.

   **Example:** The word blackboard as applied to green or brown chalkboards is a misnomer but is not incorrect, as the broad sense of the word is idiomatic.

   **Source antonyms:** aptronym

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Something which is asserted not to be true; a mistaken belief, a falsehood, a myth.

   **Usage:** broadly; nonstandard; proscribed

   **Example:** It’s a misnomer that all doctors have bad handwriting.
