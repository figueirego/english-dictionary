## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

- pseudoprimes — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An integer that possesses at least one characteristic of a prime number without actually being prime. The characteristic is typically chosen to make such "false primes" very rare.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A Fermat pseudoprime; a composite integer n satisfying bⁿ⁻¹≡1(mod n) for some integer b such that b &gt; 1.

   **Usage:** specifically
