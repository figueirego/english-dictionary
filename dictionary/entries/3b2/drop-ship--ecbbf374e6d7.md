### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To deliver goods for a business directly to its customers, as though the business owned a relevant inventory, but with the manufacturer as the real source of the delivery.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** If you drop ship the orders, then you don't need to own the products; thus, fewer of your customers suspect you're acting as a middle man.
