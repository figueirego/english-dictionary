### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A rough and rapid method of writing by substituting symbols for letters, words, etc.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** brachygraphy (obsolete); stenography; phonography

   **Source antonyms:** longhand

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The jargon becomes a shorthand for these advanced concepts.
