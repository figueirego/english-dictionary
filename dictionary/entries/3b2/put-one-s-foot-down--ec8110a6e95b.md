### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To insist, demand, or refuse with finality.

   **Example:** I finally put my foot down and demanded to talk to someone in charge.

   **Source synonyms:** dig in one's heels; pound the table

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To make a vehicle go faster; accelerate.

   **Usage:** informal; literally

   **Source synonyms:** step on it; floor it; put the pedal to the metal
