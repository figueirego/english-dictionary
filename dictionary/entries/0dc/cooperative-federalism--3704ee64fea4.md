## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A form of federalism in which national, state, and local governments interact cooperatively and collectively to solve common problems, rather than making separate but similar policies.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** marble cake federalism
