### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To undo a folding.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to unfold a map; to unfold a tablecloth; She unpacks the new dress and unfolds it carefully.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To become unfolded.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The curtains unfolded to reveal the stage.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To turn out; to happen; to develop.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Events unfolded in an unexpected way.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To lay open to view or contemplation; to bring out in all the details, or by successive development; to reveal.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to unfold one's designs; to unfold the principles of a science

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To reassemble a line of text that was split across multiple lines.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

   **Source antonyms:** fold
