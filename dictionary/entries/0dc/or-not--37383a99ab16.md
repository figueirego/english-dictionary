### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Emphasizes that a particular claim or idea makes no difference regarding what can or has happened.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. A sardonic response to a new event that contradicts what the speaker has just stated.

   **Example:** "I bet the green car will make it unscathed!" said Mildred. Suddenly, there came a loud crunch from the racetrack. "Or not," she sighed.
