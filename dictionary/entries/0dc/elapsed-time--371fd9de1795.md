**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The duration of time that has passed between the commencement of the event and its completion, irrespective of any time-outs, half-time breaks, or other interruptions.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; especially drag racing

   **Example:** With a bit of tuning, these cars can run 12-second ETs (elapsed times).
