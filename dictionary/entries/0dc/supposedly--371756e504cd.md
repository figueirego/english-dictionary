## Adverb

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /səˈpəʊ̯.zɪd.li/ — source labels: UK
- IPA: /səˈpoʊ̯.zɪd.li/ — source labels: US

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. As a matter of supposition; in the beliefs or according to the claims of some people.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** People from other planets have supposedly visited Earth in flying saucers.

   **Example:** According to your testimony, you were supposedly at home watching TV when the murder occurred.

   **Source synonyms:** supposably; reportedly; reputedly; purportedly; putatively; rumoredly; allegedly; ostensibly; possibly; probably; likely; plausibly

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used in place of "supposed to be," often in informal or colloquial speech, as a mispronunciation.

   **Usage:** Philippines; informal; not-comparable

   **Example:** My parents told me I am supposedly in bed by 10 P.M.
