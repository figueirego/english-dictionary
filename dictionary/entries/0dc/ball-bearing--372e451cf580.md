### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A bearing assembly which uses spherical bearing balls as the rolling elements, usually consisting of two ballraces (rings).

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; mechanical; mechanical-engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An individual spherical ball, usually made of steel or other hard material, suitable for use in a ball-based rolling element bearing.

   **Usage:** broadly; often

   **Source synonyms:** ball; bearing ball
