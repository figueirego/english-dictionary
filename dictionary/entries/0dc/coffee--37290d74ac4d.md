### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A beverage made by infusing the beans of the coffee plant in hot water.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** He and his friends used to order coffee whenever they went out.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A serving of this beverage.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The seeds of the plant used to make coffee, called ‘beans’ due to their shape.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The powder made by roasting and grinding the seeds.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A tropical plant of the genus Coffea.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A pale brown color, like that of milky coffee.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The end of a meal, when coffee is served.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** He did not stay for coffee.
