## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A soft, dull black powder consisting of finely divided metallic platinum obtained by reduction and precipitation from its solutions. It absorbs oxygen to a high degree, and is employed as an oxidizer.

   **Usage:** uncountable
