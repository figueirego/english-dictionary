### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To give a sentimental feel to.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Let us not sentimentalize things just because they are old. Getting all dewy-eyed about it because it was started in the 17th century doesn't make sense when we're talking about the town dump.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>
