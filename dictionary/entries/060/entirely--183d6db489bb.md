## Adverb

### Pronunciation

### Forms

- entierly — alternative; archaic
- intirely — alternative; archaic; nonstandard

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To the full or entire extent.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** It is entirely up to you where we go, as I’ll be happy with anywhere.

   **Example:** The cake hadn’t entirely been eaten, so some was saved for later.

   **Example:** manuscript pages entirely without ornament.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To the exclusion of others; solely.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** I'm entirely to blame.

   **Example:** This part of the park is used entirely by the workers; everyone else will have to go to the other side.

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** completely — to the full extent; wholly; solely — to the exclusion of others
