### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The process where a donor is immunised by the injection of an antigen prepared from microorganisms isolated from the recipient; wherein the blood is collected and defibrinated after being immunised, and the latter(i.e patient) is presumably passively immunised by means of antibody formed in the donor.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
