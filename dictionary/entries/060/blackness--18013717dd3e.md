### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The state or quality of being black in colour.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** The blackness of outer space comes from the lack of anything to reflect light rather than the absence of light.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any space that such colour pervades.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** Out of the blackness came some flickers of light.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Darkness, gloominess; depression.

   **Usage:** figuratively; uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The quality of being evil or dismal.

   **Usage:** figuratively; uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The state of being of African descent.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The experiences and culture of Black people.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually
