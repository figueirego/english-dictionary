## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A Meitei ethnic male given name.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A Meitei emperor of the Ningthouja dynasty, who ruled Kangleipak, in the 14th century CE.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A king who ruled Moirang from 1083 CE to 1138 CE, and a contemporary of Khamba and Thoibi.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A king who ruled Moirang from 1764 CE to 1768 CE, and a maternal uncle of Bhagyachandra of Ningthouja dynasty.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A deified chief of Phunal region of Kangleipak.
