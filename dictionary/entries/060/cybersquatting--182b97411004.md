## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The registration of a well-known brand or company name as an Internet domain name in the hope of selling it at a later date.

   **Usage:** Internet; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A form of passing off, often with the intent referred to in the first sense above.

   **Usage:** Internet; uncountable
