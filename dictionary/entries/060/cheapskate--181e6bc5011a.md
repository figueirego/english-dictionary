### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Someone who stingily avoids spending money.

   **Example:** He is such a cheapskate, he found that jumper in a dustbin.

   **Source synonyms:** miser; carl (Scotland; obsolete); cheapass (slang); cheapie (informal); cheapskate; cheapo (slang); churl; clutchfist (obsolete); el cheapo (slang); last of the big spenders; magpie; miser; moneygrubber; muckworm; nickel nurser (idiomatic); nickel-shaver (idiomatic); nig (archaic); niggard; nigon (obsolete); nipcheese; nipfarthing (obsolete); penny-father (obsolete); penny pincher; pickpenny; piker; pinch-commons (obsolete); pinch-gut (obsolete); pincher; pinchfist; pinchpenny; scrimp; scrooge; snudge (derogatory; dialectal); squirrel; skinflint; tight-arse (slang); tightwad

   **Source antonyms:** spendthrift

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Someone who does not give freely.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** My mum is such a cheapskate that she didn't lend me money to go shopping.
