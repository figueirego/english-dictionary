### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act or state of continuing or being continued; uninterrupted extension or succession

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** prolongation; propagation

   **Source antonyms:** discontinuation; termination

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. That which extends, increases, supplements, or carries on.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** the continuation of a story

   **Example:** The series' continuation was commercially if not artistically successful.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A representation of an execution state of a program at a certain point in time, which may be used at a later time to resume the execution of the program from that point.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; programming; sciences
