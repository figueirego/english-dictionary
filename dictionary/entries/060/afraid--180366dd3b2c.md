### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Impressed with fear or apprehension; in fear.

   **Usage:** predicative

   **Example:** He is afraid of death.

   **Example:** He is afraid to ask her out.

   **Example:** He is afraid of the future.

   **Example:** I’m not afraid of telling / to tell you the truth&#58; I'm reluctant to go in you car because I’m too afraid of crashing.

   **Source synonyms:** afeared; alarmed; anxious; apprehensive; fearful; timid; timorous

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Regretful, sorry; expressing a reluctance to face an unpleasant situation.

   **Usage:** predicative

   **Example:** I am afraid I cannot help you in this matter.

   **Source synonyms:** sorry

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Worried about, feeling concern for, fearing for (someone or something).

   **Usage:** predicative
