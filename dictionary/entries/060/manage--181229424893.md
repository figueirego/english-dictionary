### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To direct or be in charge of.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Even though Jack is a novice, he manages his team with great success.

   **Source synonyms:** handle

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To handle or control (a situation, job).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The government managed the inflation very poorly.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To handle with skill, wield (a tool, weapon etc.).

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To succeed at an attempt in spite of difficulty.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** He managed to climb the tower.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To succeed at an attempt in spite of difficulty.
   1. To end up doing something that could or should have been avoided.

      **Usage:** intransitive; ironic

      **Example:** She somehow managed to sit on the wet paint.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To achieve (something) without fuss, or without outside help.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** It's a tough job, but I'll manage.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To manage to say; to say while fighting back embarrassment, laughter, etc.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** "That's nice, dear!", she managed.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To train (a horse) in the manège; to exercise in graceful or artful action.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To treat with care; to husband.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. To bring about; to contrive.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive
