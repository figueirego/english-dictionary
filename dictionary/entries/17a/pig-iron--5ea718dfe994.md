### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A brittle, impure form of iron produced by smelting iron ore which is often shaped into blocks; it is unsuitable for working but suitable for casting, and so is commonly used as an industrial raw material; (countable) a sample of such iron.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; metallurgy; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

   **Source synonyms:** crude iron

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An iron plate which is hung between a piece of meat and the fire used to cook it to prevent the meat from burning if the fire is too hot.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** cooking; food; lifestyle

   **Source synonyms:** pig plate
