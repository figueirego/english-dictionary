### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To secretly cooperate with other people in order to commit a crime or other wrongdoing; to collude, to conspire.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of parts of a plant&#58; to be converging or in close contact; to be connivent.

   **Usage:** intransitive; rare

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Often followed by at&#58; to pretend to be ignorant of something in order to escape blame; to ignore or overlook a fault deliberately.

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete

   **Source synonyms:** dissimulate (rare); look the other way; shut one's eyes; turn a blind eye; wink

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To open and close the eyes rapidly; to wink.

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete
