### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. That which overflows; the excess or side effect.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The spillover from the dam due to the heavy rains will run down this channel and harmlessly dump into that river, we hope.

   **Example:** The spillover from this war will be many little massacres of civilians by civilians.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The spread of infectious disease between different species of animal and particularly to humans.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** epidemiology; medicine; sciences
