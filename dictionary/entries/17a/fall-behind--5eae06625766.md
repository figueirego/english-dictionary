### Senses

1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see fall, behind.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To be late (for a regular event)

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** You're falling behind with the rent.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To be progressively below average in performance.

   **Usage:** intransitive
