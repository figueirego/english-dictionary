### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To try or experiment with (something or someone) in order to see if it works, is true, or is successful.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To explore (something one has little experience with), especially to do so in a safe environment.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

3. To explore how (someone) responds to something; To feel someone out.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To pass a placement test that enables one to avoid taking a course.

   **Usage:** intransitive
