## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A part of the theory of meromorphic functions that describes the asymptotic distribution of solutions to the equation ƒ(z) = a, as a varies.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** A key tool in Nevanlinna theory is the Nevanlinna characteristic, T(r,f), which measures the rate of growth of a meromorphic function.
