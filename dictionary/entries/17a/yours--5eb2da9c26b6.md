### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. That or those belonging to you; the possessive second-person singular pronoun used without a following noun.

   **Example:** If this edit is mine, the other must be yours.

   **Example:** Their encyclopedia is good, but yours is even better.

   **Example:** Are all these socks yours?

   **Example:** The socks of yours are a bit niffy.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Your house or home.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** Let's go over to yours.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Written at the end of a letter, before the signature.

   **Example:** Yours sincerely, Yours faithfully, Yours, Sincerely yours,
