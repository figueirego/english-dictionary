<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A software facility that saves an open document (or progress in a game, etc.) periodically to avoid the risk of data loss.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Thanks to autosave, I was able to recover most of my unsaved thesis after a power cut.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

   **Usage:** countable
