### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A word relayed to a person to gain admittance to a place or to gain access to information.

   **Usage:** security

   **Example:** Only if a would-be visitor knew the password du jour could he pass; the guards allowed no exceptions.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A string of characters used to log in to a computer or network, to access a level in a video game, and so on; archetypally a word but nowadays often an alphanumeric string or a phrase.

   **Example:** A strong password has a mixture of lowercase and uppercase letters, numbers, and punctuation marks.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; cryptography; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences
