### Forms

- more exquisite — comparative
- most exquisite — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Especially or extraordinarily fine or pleasing; exceptional.

   **Example:** They sell good coffee and pastries, but their chocolate is exquisite.

   **Example:** Sourav Ganguly scored an exquisite century in his debut Test match.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Carefully adjusted; precise; accurate; exact.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Recherché; far-fetched; abstruse.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Of special beauty or rare excellence.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Exceeding; extreme; keen, in a bad or a good sense.

   **Example:** exquisite pain or pleasure

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Of delicate perception or close and accurate discrimination; not easy to satisfy; exact; fastidious.

   **Example:** exquisite judgment, taste, or discernment

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** beautiful; delicate; discriminating; perfect
