## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A feeling of anxiety or regret after making, or after promising to make, a purchase, especially a purchase encouraged by a high-pressure sales pitch.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A feeling of regret after any kind of decision, for example voting for a certain party or consenting to sexual intercourse with someone.

   **Usage:** uncountable; transferred sense

   **Source synonyms:** postdecisional regret
