### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The process of making something external, or visible from the outside.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
