### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To solemnly reject (someone or something); to abandon (someone or something) forever; to disavow, to disclaim, to repudiate.

   **Usage:** formal; transitive

   **Example:** to abjure errors

   **Source synonyms:** renounce; abjure; deny; disavow; disclaim; disown; forsay (archaic); forswear; renounce; repudiate; wash one's hands of

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To renounce (something) upon oath; to forswear; specifically, to recant or retract (a heresy or some other opinion); to withdraw.

   **Usage:** formal; historical; transitive

   **Example:** to abjure allegiance to a prince

   **Source synonyms:** disavow; renounce; abjure; backpedal; disavow; disown; eat one's words (figuratively); recall; recant; retract; revoke; swallow; take back; unsay; unspeak (rare); walk back; withcall (rare); withdraw

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To cause (someone) to recant or retract (a heresy or some other opinion).

   **Usage:** formal; historical; transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Especially in abjure the realm&#58; to swear an oath to leave (a place) forever.

   **Usage:** formal; historical; transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To cause or compel (someone) to leave a place forever; to banish.

   **Usage:** formal; historical; obsolete; rare; transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To solemnly reject; to abandon forever.

   **Usage:** formal; intransitive

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To recant or retract a heresy on oath.

   **Usage:** formal; historical; intransitive
