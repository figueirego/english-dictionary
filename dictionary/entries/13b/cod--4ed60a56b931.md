### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of various types of sea fish or the meat from said fish.
   1. A sea fish of the family Gadidae which are sold as "cod", as haddock (Melanogrammus aeglefinus), and whiting (usually Merlangius merlangus).

      **Source synonyms:** codfish

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of various types of sea fish or the meat from said fish.
   1. A sea fish of the family Gadidae which are sold as "cod", as haddock (Melanogrammus aeglefinus), and whiting (usually Merlangius merlangus).
      1. A sea fish of the genus Gadus, inclusive of Atlantic cod (Gadus morhua), Pacific cod (Gadus macrocephalus), and Greenland cod (Gadus ogac or Gadus macrocephalus ogac).

         **Source synonyms:** codfish

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of various types of sea fish or the meat from said fish.
   1. A sea fish of the family Gadidae which are sold as "cod", as haddock (Melanogrammus aeglefinus), and whiting (usually Merlangius merlangus).
      1. A sea fish of the genus Gadus, inclusive of Atlantic cod (Gadus morhua), Pacific cod (Gadus macrocephalus), and Greenland cod (Gadus ogac or Gadus macrocephalus ogac).
         1. An Atlantic cod (Gadus morhua).

            **Source synonyms:** codfish; common cod; milwell; Scotch cod

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any of various types of sea fish or the meat from said fish.
