### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A dish made of grain or legumes, milk or water, heated and stirred until thick and typically eaten for breakfast.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** Eat your porridge while it's hot!

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A dish made of grain or legumes, milk or water, heated and stirred until thick and typically eaten for breakfast.
   1. Oat porridge; oatmeal.

      **Usage:** UK; uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A dish made of grain or legumes, milk or water, heated and stirred until thick and typically eaten for breakfast.
   1. Rice porridge; congee.

      **Usage:** Malaysia; Singapore; uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A prison sentence.

   **Usage:** UK; slang; uncountable; usually

   **Example:** Just do your porridge and keep your head down.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A type of thick soup or stew, especially thickened with barley.

   **Usage:** rare; uncountable; usually
