### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Performing a headcount; the counting of people.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A head tax.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** poll tax

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A system of remuneration for providers of health care, in which providers enroll patients as permanent clients and receive a fixed (and capped) periodic payment for each enrollee (i.e., per capita).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
