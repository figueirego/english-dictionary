### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A marine crustacean of the subclass Cirripedia that attaches itself to submerged surfaces such as tidal rocks or the bottoms of ships.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The barnacle goose.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. In electrical engineering, a change made to a product on the manufacturing floor that was not part of the original product design.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. On printed circuit boards, a change such as soldering a wire in order to connect two points, or addition such as an added resistor or capacitor, subassembly or daughterboard.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; electrical-engineering; electricity; electromagnetism; electronics; energy; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A deprecated or obsolete file, image or other artifact that remains with a project even though it is no longer needed.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences; software

   **Source synonyms:** cruft (collective)

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. An instrument like a pair of pincers, to fix on the nose of a vicious horse while shoeing so as to make it more tractable.

   **Usage:** in-plural; obsolete

   **Source synonyms:** twitch

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

   **Usage:** UK; archaic; in-plural; slang
