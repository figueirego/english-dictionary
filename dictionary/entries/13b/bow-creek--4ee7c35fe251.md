## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A river in Greater London, England, which is the tidal part of the River Lea before it reaches the Thames.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A river in Devon, England, the tidal part of the Harbourne River before it reaches the tidal River Dart; the hamlet of Bow in Ashprington parish is located where the Harbourne becomes Bow Creek.
