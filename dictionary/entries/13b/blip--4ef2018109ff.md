### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To make a short beep sound.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The door blipped as I showed my electronic identity card and passed through.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To change state abruptly, such as between off and on or dark and light, sometimes implying motion.

   **Usage:** informal; intransitive

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. Synonym of bleep (“to replace offending words in a broadcast recording with a tone”).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** bleep (synonym; synonym-of)
