### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To leave (school, a race, etc.) prematurely and voluntarily.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** Nothing went well in high school, so he dropped out.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To opt out of conventional society.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To be lost or momentarily interrupted.

   **Usage:** usually; electronic signal

   **Example:** I can't make phone calls because the line keeps dropping out.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of sound

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see drop, out.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To give way under someone.

   **Usage:** intransitive; Of a trapdoor or similar

   **Example:** The board he was walking on dropped out.

   **Source synonyms:** fall out

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To cause someone to slip and/or fall down.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive; usually; Of the ground; floor; snow; a worldview or foundation; Of the ground; floor; snow; a worldview or foundation

   **Example:** The ground dropped out from under him.

   **Example:** When she left him, his world dropped out beneath him.

   **Source synonyms:** fall out

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To dismiss, break up with or abandon (someone).

   **Usage:** slang; transitive
