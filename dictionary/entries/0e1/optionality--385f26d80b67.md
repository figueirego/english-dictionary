### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The value of additional optional investment opportunities available only after having made an initial investment.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The short-term payoff for this is modest, but the optionality value is enormous.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
