### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To knock over with an air current, most often wind.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I'll huff and I'll puff and I'll blow your house down.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To clean off from top to bottom with an air current from a compressed air hose.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Blow down this enclosure and do some chip handling before you run this machine any further.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; manufacturing

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Synonym of dirty down.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** broadcasting; media; television

   **Source synonyms:** dirty down (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To kill (someone) with a gun.

   **Usage:** US; dated; slang; transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To signal to someone by blowing into a whistle.

   **Usage:** US; dated; slang; transitive
