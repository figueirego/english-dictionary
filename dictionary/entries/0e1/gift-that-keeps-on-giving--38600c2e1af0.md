### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Something with ongoing consequences; usually, one with positive ongoing consequences.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** Those flower bulbs that our neighbor gave us are a gift that keeps on giving — they keep blooming year after year.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Something with ongoing consequences; usually, one with positive ongoing consequences.
   1. A sexually transmitted disease.

      **Usage:** idiomatic; slang

      **Example:** That one-night stand that some tramp gave him is a gift that keeps on giving — he's had syphilis for years now.
