### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A specialized encyclopedia about the prefixed subject.

   **Usage:** morpheme

   **Example:** unicorn + -o- + -pedia → Unicornopedia (“an encyclopedia about unicorns”)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A general encyclopedia in the structure described by the prefix.

   **Usage:** morpheme

   **Example:** kid + -pedia → Kidpedia (“an encyclopedia for young children”)
