### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of providing approval and support; backing; championship.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** His vigorous patronage of the conservatives got him in trouble with progressives.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Customers collectively; clientele; business.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The restaurant had an upper-class patronage.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The act or state of being a customer of some business.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The restaurant had "Thank you for your patronage!" printed on its take-out bags.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A communication that indicates lack of respect by patronizing the recipient; condescension; disdain.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Granting favours or giving contracts or making appointments to office in return for political support.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; politics

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Guardianship, as of a saint; tutelary care.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The right of nomination to political office.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. The right of presentation to church or ecclesiastical benefice; advowson.

   **Usage:** UK; countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law
