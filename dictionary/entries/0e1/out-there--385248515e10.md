### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Extreme; radical; far removed from the ordinary; crazy, nutty, loony.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** I like the ideas Melissa came up with, but Brad's ideas were just out there.

   **Example:** The president failing to acknowledge the numerous side-effects linked to covid restrictions just shows how out there he is.

## Prep\_phrase · Record 2

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. In the public eye.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** If you want to improve your public speaking, you should put yourself out there more.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. In the world at large.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** There are a lot of crazy people out there.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. In a position that makes one vulnerable to rejection or scrutiny from another person, especially in a romantic context.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** If you want to find love, you'll have to put yourself out there.
