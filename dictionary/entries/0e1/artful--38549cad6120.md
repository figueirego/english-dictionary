### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Characterized by, or performed with, cleverness or contrivance; clever, ingenious.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Not naturally produced; artificial; imitative.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Exhibiting or using much art or skill; dexterous; skilful.

   **Source synonyms:** adroit

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Skilful at using dishonest or unfair means to achieve a purpose; crafty, cunning.

   **Usage:** especially

   **Source synonyms:** deceitful; underhand; astute; artful; cautelous (obsolete); crafty; cunning; devious; diabolical (slang); foxlike; foxy; frood (Northern-England; Scotland); guileful; shrewd; slim (South-Africa); sly; subtle (archaic); tricksy; tricky; vulpine; wily

**Source sense R1.S5**
