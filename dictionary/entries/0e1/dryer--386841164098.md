### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who, or that which, dries; any device or facility employed to remove water or humidity, e.g. a desiccative

   **Example:** The sun and a northwesterly wind are great driers of the earth.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Short for clothes dryer, tumble dryer

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; most often

   **Alternative form of:** [clothes dryer](../3eb/clothes-dryer--fae116d06ea0.md); [tumble dryer](../396/tumble-dryer--e59ecfa9ea5d.md)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Short for hair dryer

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; rare

   **Alternative form of:** [hair dryer](../010/hair-dryer--042107b15c1a.md)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A catalyst used to promote the drying of paints and varnishes by oxidative crosslinking.
