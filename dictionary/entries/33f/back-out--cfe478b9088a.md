### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To reverse (a vehicle) from a confined space.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He backed out of the garage.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To withdraw from something one has agreed to do.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** She backed out of organizing the fund-raising.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To convince (someone) to withdraw from a challenge.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To bet on someone losing.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** gambling; games

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To undo (a change).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I had to back out the changes made to the computer when it became apparent that they had stopped it working properly.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To exit a mode or function.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** I chose that menu option by accident, so I pressed Escape to back out.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To draw from behind the back (a knife etc.) (as also bare back).

   **Usage:** Multicultural-London-English; transitive
