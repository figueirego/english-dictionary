### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The portion of the United States which is located on the North American continent; the United States exclusive of Hawaii and other overseas territories.

   **Usage:** US

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; natural-sciences

   **Source synonyms:** continental US
