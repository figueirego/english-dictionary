### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The fundamental physical value α, presumed to be constant, that characterizes the strength of the electromagnetic force. Equal to about 1/137 = 0.0072973525.

   **Usage:** uncountable; electrodynamics

   **Source synonyms:** Sommerfeld's constant; electromagnetic coupling constant
