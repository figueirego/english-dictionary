## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Situated above a spine or spines; especially, situated above, or on the dorsal side of, the neural spines of the vertebral column, or above, or in front of, the spine of the scapula.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** supraspinate fossa

   **Example:** supraspinate tendon
