### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A small carnivorous mammal of the Old World that resembles a weasel, Martes zibellina, from cold regions in Eurasia and the North Pacific islands, valued for its dark brown fur.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any other marten, especially Martes americana (syn. Mustela americana).

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A pelt of fur of a sable or of one of another species of martens; a coat made from this fur.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An artist's brush made from the fur of the sable, the kolinsky sable-hair brush.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A black colour on a coat of arms.

   **Usage:** uncountable
