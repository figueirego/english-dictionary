### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The Republic of China in Taiwan as opposed the People's Republic of China; primarily used during the Cold War prior to 1979.

   **Usage:** dated

   **Source antonyms:** Red China

<a id="r1-s2"></a>
