## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An ideology that emphasizes racial or ethnic identity, especially when combined with nationalism.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An ideology that emphasizes racial or ethnic identity, especially when combined with nationalism.
   1. An ideology, articulated by historian Simon Dubnow, that declared Jewish people to be a diaspora nation without a territory.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An ideology that emphasizes racial or ethnic identity, especially when combined with nationalism.
   1. Völkisch nationalism; the desire for a homogeneous population that excludes foreigners and the belief that different racial or ethnic groups have different rights.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An ideology that emphasizes racial or ethnic identity, especially when combined with nationalism.
   1. A Japanese ideology that emphasized a common identify among East Asian peoples, associated with the fascist movement during the WWII era.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An ideology that emphasizes racial or ethnic identity, especially when combined with nationalism.
   1. A movement within the Heathen or neo-pagan community that states one cannot convert to Heathenry unless one has the appropriate ancestry.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Synonym of populism.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** populism (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. An artistic aesthetic that is based on traditional practices that are passed on from individual to individual, such as folk tales, folk music, common cultural practices, etc.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A movement within Nigerian theater started by Sam Ukala that focuses on folk tales as a reaction against colonialism.

   **Usage:** uncountable; more specifically

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A folksy saying, expression, or practice.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. A philosophical stance that meaning is based on common usage by ordinary people rather than an abstract principle.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; philosophy; sciences
