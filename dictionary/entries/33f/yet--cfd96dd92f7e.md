## Adverb · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /jɛt/ — source labels: Canada; General-American; Received-Pronunciation
- IPA: /jet/ — source labels: Australia; New-Zealand
- IPA: /jɪt/ — source labels: Northumbria; unstressed

### Forms

- yit — alternative; Ireland; US; colloquial
- yitt — alternative; obsolete
- ytt — alternative; obsolete

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Thus far; up to the present; up to some unspecified time.
   1. In negative or interrogative use, often with an expectation or potential of something happening in the future.

      **Usage:** not-comparable

      **Example:** I haven't finished yet.

      **Example:** Have you finished yet?

      **Example:** We do not yet know what happened.

      **Example:** He has never yet been late for an appointment.

      **Example:** I don't know if I can tell you yet.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Thus far; up to the present; up to some unspecified time.
   1. In negative imperative use, asking for an action to be delayed.

      **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Thus far; up to the present; up to some unspecified time.
   1. In affirmative use&#58; still.

      **Usage:** archaic; not-comparable; poetic

      **Example:** He is yet breathing. (He is still breathing.)

      **Example:** O, say, does that Star-Spangled Banner yet wave \[…\]? (US National Anthem)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. At some future time; eventually.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The riddle will be solved yet.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Not as of the time referenced.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; with-infinitive

   **Example:** I’ve yet to see him. — I have not yet seen him.

   **Example:** I had yet to go to a convention. — I had not yet gone to a convention.

   **Example:** They are yet to win a single match. — They have not yet won a single match.

   **Example:** He seemed yet to be convinced. — He seemed not yet to have been convinced.

   **Source context (verbatim):** after 'have' and certain copulative verbs

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. In addition.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** There are two hours yet to go until our destination.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Even.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; degree

   **Example:** K-2 is yet higher than this.

   **Example:** Oh no! Yet more problems!
