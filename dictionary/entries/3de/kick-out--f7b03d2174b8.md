### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To eject, dismiss, expel, or forcefully remove (someone or something).

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** They will kick out a disruptive patron.

   **Example:** I got kicked out for eating inside.
