## Noun

### Forms

- job shops — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A business or facility specializing in the manufacture or fabrication of parts in relatively small quantities, produced to the specifications or requirements supplied by the customer.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A production unit that processes jobs each of which requires processing at multiple workstations, in any order, which processing may vary in time taken at each workstation.

   **Usage:** production management

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An agency that supplies technical personnel usually on short-term temporary contracts.

### Related words
