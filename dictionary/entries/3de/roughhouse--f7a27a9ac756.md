### Senses

<a id="r3-s1"></a>

**Source sense R3.S1**

1. To behave rowdily or violently.
   1. To play roughly (with physical roughness).

      **Usage:** intransitive

      **Example:** Out in the street, some delinquents were roughhousing, until the police drove by.

      **Source synonyms:** horseplay

<a id="r3-s2"></a>

**Source sense R3.S2**

2. To behave rowdily or violently.
   1. To commit physical assault.

      **Usage:** intransitive

      **Example:** Out in the street, some delinquents were roughhousing, until the police drove by.

<a id="r3-s3"></a>

**Source sense R3.S3**

3. To treat (someone or something) roughly or violently.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Out in the street, some delinquents were roughhousing a victim, until the police drove by.

   **Source synonyms:** rough up; work over
