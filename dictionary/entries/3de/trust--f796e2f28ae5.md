   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Usage:** countable; rare; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. The confidence vested in a person who has legal ownership of a property to manage for the benefit of another.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** I put the house into my sister's trust.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; trust law
