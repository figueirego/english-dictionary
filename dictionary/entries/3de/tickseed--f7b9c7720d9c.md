### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A seed or fruit resembling a tick in shape, or in clinging to the skin or hair/fur.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A plant producing such seed or fruit, such as those in the genera&#58;
