**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** the birth of an empire

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The circumstances of one's background, ancestry, or upbringing.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** He was of noble birth, but fortune had not favored him.

**Source sense R1.S5**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
