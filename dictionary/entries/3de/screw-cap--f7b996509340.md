### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The cap of a container, especially a bottle or jar, which screws onto the threaded lip of the container.

   **Example:** Four-year-old Anna surprised her parents when she unscrewed the screw cap and poured herself a glass of milk, like an old pro.

   **Source synonyms:** screw top

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The style of closure that has such a cap; a container that has such a cap.

   **Usage:** metonymically

   **Example:** Corks remain prevalent, but nowadays the wine industry also offers plenty of screw caps, too, for occasions that warrant them or customers who prefer them.

   **Example:** When you buy the wine tonight, pick up some screw caps in addition to whichever corked bottles you buy.

   **Source synonyms:** screw top
