### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of disqualifying, or the state of being disqualified

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** the disqualification of certain people for holding certain positions

   **Example:** Henry won the match due to the disqualification of his opponent.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. That which disqualifies; that which causes someone to be unfit for a certain purpose or role

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Conviction of crime is a disqualification of a person for office.

   **Example:** Sickness is a disqualification for labor.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The act of being forbidden from further play in a basketball game due to the accumulation of too many personal fouls.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; basketball; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

   **Source synonyms:** fouling out
