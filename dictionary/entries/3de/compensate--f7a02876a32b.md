### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To do (something good) after (something bad) happens.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To pay or reward someone in exchange for work done or some other consideration.

   **Example:** It is hard work, but they will compensate you well for it.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To make up for; to do something in place of something else; to correct, satisfy; to reach an agreement such that the scales are literally or (metaphorically) balanced; to equalize or make even.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** His loud voice cannot compensate for a lack of personality.

   **Example:** To compensate me for his tree landing on my shed, my neighbor paved my driveway.

   **Source synonyms:** make up

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To adjust or adapt to a change, often a harm or deprivation.

   **Example:** I hate that old car because it steers a little to the left and I'm always compensating for that when I drive it.

   **Example:** To compensate for his broken leg, Gary uses crutches.
