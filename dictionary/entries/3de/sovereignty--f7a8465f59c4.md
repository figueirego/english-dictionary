### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The quality or state of being sovereign.
   1. Of a ruler (especially a monarch)&#58; supreme authority or dominion over something.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source synonyms:** diadem (literary)

      **Source antonyms:** nonsovereignty

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The quality or state of being sovereign.
   1. Of a nation or other polity&#58; the state of being able to control resources, make laws independently, and otherwise govern itself without the coercion or concurrence of other polities.

      **Usage:** broadly; countable; uncountable

      **Source synonyms:** autarchy; independence; nationality; nationhood

      **Source antonyms:** nonsovereignty

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The quality or state of being sovereign.
   1. Of a person&#58; the liberty to decide one's actions and thoughts.

      **Usage:** broadly; countable; uncountable

      **Source antonyms:** nonsovereignty

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The quality or state of being sovereign.
   1. Pre-eminent or superior excellence; also, superior ability to achieve something; mastery.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source antonyms:** nonsovereignty

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A territory under the rule of a sovereign; an independent or self-governing nation or other polity.

   **Usage:** countable
