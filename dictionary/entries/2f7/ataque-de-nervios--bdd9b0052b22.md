## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A psychological syndrome among Iberian-descended cultures, involving uncontrollable screaming or shouting, crying, trembling, sensations of heat rising in the chest and head, dissociative experiences, and verbal or physical aggression.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** Puerto Rican syndrome
