## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Not requiring the use of a brush.
   1. Of an electric motor&#58; that does not use brushes, physical contacts for the commutator.

      **Usage:** not-comparable

      **Example:** a brushless motor

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Not requiring the use of a brush.
   1. Not requiring the use of a hairbrush, scrubbing brush, or other brush.

      **Usage:** not-comparable

      **Example:** a brushless shaving cream

      **Example:** a brushless finish on the concrete
