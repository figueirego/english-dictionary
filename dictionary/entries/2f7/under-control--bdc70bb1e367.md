## Prep\_phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Being taken care of or being addressed.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** Don't worry; the police have been called and the situation is under control.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Being actively controlled, or able to be actively controlled in its current state.

   **Usage:** literal

   **Example:** Throughout the turbulence encounter, the pilots kept the aircraft under control.

   **Example:** The control rods absorb neutrons in the reactor core, keeping the fission reaction under control and preventing it from going prompt critical.
