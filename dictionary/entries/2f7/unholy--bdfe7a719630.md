### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Not holy; (by extension) evil, impure, or otherwise perverted.

   **Example:** The priest's unholy behaviour brought the church into disrepute.

   **Source synonyms:** mishallowed; profane

   **Source antonyms:** holy; blessed; celestial; devoutful (obsolete); divine; hallowed; heavenly; holy; godly; sacred; sanctimonious (archaic)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Dreadful, terrible, excessive, or otherwise atrocious.

   **Example:** What an unholy mess your room is in!

   **Example:** I've been spending an unholy amount of time trying to write a novel!
