## Verb

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To notice in advance, especially to recognise as a threat or problem; to foresee.

   **Usage:** informal; person; or occurrence

   **Example:** You're a perfect hitman; you look like a regular guy. Nobody sees you coming.

   **Example:** My pension plan went broke. I didn't see that development coming.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a thing

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To recognise (someone) as a potential victim to swindle or otherwise deceive.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** You were charged $1,000 for a car wash? They must have seen you coming!

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a person

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see coming.

   **Example:** The foul ball hit me on the head. I didn't see it coming.
