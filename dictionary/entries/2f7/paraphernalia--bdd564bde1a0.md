### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Miscellaneous items, especially the set of equipment required for a particular activity.

   **Usage:** plural; plural-only

   **Example:** drug paraphernalia

   **Example:** He has an impressive collection of bicycling paraphernalia, but he doesn't ride very often anymore.

   **Source synonyms:** stuff; utensil

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Objects that a married woman owns, such as clothing and jewellery, apart from her dowry.

   **Usage:** archaic; plural; plural-only; family law

   **Source synonyms:** parapherna; paraphernals
