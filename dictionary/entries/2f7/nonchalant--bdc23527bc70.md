### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Casually calm and relaxed.

   **Example:** We handled the whole frenetic situation with a nonchalant attitude.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Indifferent; unconcerned; behaving as if detached.

   **Example:** He is far too nonchalant about such a serious matter.
