### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A card that can have holes or notches cut in it, especially one for storing data, that can be sorted according to combinations of holes present or absent.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A card that can have holes or notches cut in it, especially one for storing data, that can be sorted according to combinations of holes present or absent.
   1. Such a card, the size of a US dollar bill and having 80 columns of 12 rows, used in early mainframe computers.

      **Usage:** historical

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A card given to a customer and punched with a hole at each purchase, allowing a reward to be claimed when fully punched.
