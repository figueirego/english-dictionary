<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act or art of composing and making music, poetry, and the like, extemporaneously

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** He played a quick improvisation on the keyboard.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>
