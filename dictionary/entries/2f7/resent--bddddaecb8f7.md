### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To feel resentment over; to consider as an affront.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The bride greatly resented being left at the church.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To express displeasure or indignation at.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Do you resent us for offering you that choice?

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To be sensible of; to feel.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. In a positive sense, to take well; to receive with satisfaction.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To recognize; to perceive, especially as if by smelling; -- associated in meaning with sent, the older spelling of scent, to smell. See resent (intransitive verb).

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To give forth an odor; to smell; to savor.

   **Usage:** obsolete
