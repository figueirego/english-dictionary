## Noun

### Forms

- blackout bags — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A bag made of dark material that prevents the entrance of light, for example to immerse a human subject's head in darkness, or to protect photographic film.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A portable kit containing items needed for power blackout survival.
