### Forms

- beneficiaries — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who benefits or receives an advantage.

   **Example:** You are the lucky beneficiary of this special offer.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One who benefits from the distribution, especially out of a trust or estate.

   **Usage:** especially trust law

   **Example:** If any beneficiary does not survive the settlor for a period of 30 days then the trustee shall distribute that beneficiary’s share to the surviving beneficiaries by right of representation.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. One who benefits from the payout of an insurance policy.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; insurance
