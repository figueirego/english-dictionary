## Adverb

### Pronunciation

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. For a special purpose, person, or occasion.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** We had this cake made specially for your birthday.

   **Example:** The train is waiting specially for a high-ranking diplomat.

   **Example:** The school is specially for any child whose schooling was disrupted by illness.

   **Example:** I had my insoles specially made.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Extremely.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; proscribed

   **Example:** She's specially good at running.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. In particular

   **Usage:** not-comparable; proscribed

   **Example:** I love all animals, specially cute furry ones.
