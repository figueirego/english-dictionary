## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A set of precepts that one individual tries to observe in daily life. For example, precepts of preserving human life, speaking truthfully, or being nice to people.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** His personal conduct is above reproach.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The way a person behaves.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** His personal conduct needs to be evaluated.
