## Noun

### Forms

- gasmen — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A worker for a company that supplies gas (in the sense of the gaseous fuel), especially one who visits premises to read the meter, test appliances etc.

   **Usage:** colloquial

   **Example:** I spent all morning waiting for the gasman to call.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A man employed in the manufacture of gas.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The man who controls the lights of the stage.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A worthless person (male)

   **Usage:** slang
