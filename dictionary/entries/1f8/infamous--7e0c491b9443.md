### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having a bad reputation; disreputable; notorious; unpleasant or evil; widely known, especially for something scornful.

   **Example:** He was an infamous traitor.

   **Example:** She is infamous for perjury.

   **Source synonyms:** ill-famed; nefarious; nithing; disreputable; ill-famed; infamous; nefarious; niddering (archaic); nidering (archaic); nithing (archaic); notorious

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Causing infamy; disgraceful.

   **Example:** This infamous deed tarnishes all involved.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Subject to a judicial punishment depriving (the person) of certain rights (e.g. the rights to hold public office, exercise the franchise, receive a public pension, serve on a jury, or give testimony in a court of law).

   **Usage:** UK; historical

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a person

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Punishable by death or imprisonment.

   **Usage:** archaic

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a crime
