### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A bond of unity or agreement between individuals, united around a common goal or against a common enemy, such as the unifying principle that defines the labor movement; mutual support within a group.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** A long time union member himself, Phil showed solidarity with the picketing grocery store workers by shopping at a competing, unionized store.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Willingness to support another person in a difficult position or needs affection.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Only the solidarity provided by her siblings allowed Margaret to cope with her mother's harrowing death.
