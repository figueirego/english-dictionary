## Preposition

### Forms

- in name of — alternative; dated

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. With appeal to; by; often used to introduce oaths

   **Example:** In the name of all things sacred, please stop that screaming!

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. For the sake of.

   **Source synonyms:** on behalf of

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. By the authority of.

   **Example:** In the name of the law, come out with your hands up.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Under the name or possession of.

   **Example:** He put the house in the name of his children.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Under the designation or excuse of.

   **Example:** These additional checks at airports are justified in the name of security.
