### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To confuse; especially, to lose a pursuer.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I never saw her without glasses before, so it threw me off when she got contact lenses.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To introduce errors or inaccuracies; to skew.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** The dirt in the apparatus threw off the results.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of a horse, to eject its rider.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To fling away.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To expel, reject, or renounce.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To split off.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To give forth in an unpremeditated manner.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To deduct from a price in order to compensate for problems.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To remove (clothing) haphazardly and tossing it on the floor.

   **Usage:** informal; transitive

   **Example:** She threw off her coat.

**Source sense R1.S10**
