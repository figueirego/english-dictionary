### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The chemistry of those compounds that occur in living organisms, and the processes that occur in their metabolism and catabolism; the study of such chemistry.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** My aunt has a degree in biochemistry.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The chemical characteristics of a particular living organism.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** The biochemistries of fungal and bacterial cells are quite distinct.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The biochemical activity associated with a particular chemical or condition.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** Our study compared the biochemistries of epilepsy and Parkinson's.

   **Example:** The biochemistry of NO differs from that of NO₂.
