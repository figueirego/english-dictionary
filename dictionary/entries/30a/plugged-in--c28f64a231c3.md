## Adjective · Record 1

### Forms

- more plugged in — comparative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. With its plug properly connected to the electric plug socket.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Check if the television is plugged in.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of an electric device

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Fully aware; in a state of keeping up with current information about a topic; engaged wholeheartedly in a task or cause

   **Usage:** broadly; colloquial

   **Example:** By subscribing to this magazine, you can stay plugged in to current issues.

   **Example:** My new boss is much more plugged in to the needs of his employees than my last boss.

   **Example:** Even the most plugged-in politics enthusiasts had a hard time understanding his oblique references.

   **Source synonyms:** connected; informed; well-informed; involved
