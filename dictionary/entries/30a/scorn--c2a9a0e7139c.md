### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To feel or display contempt or disdain for something or somebody; to despise.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** contemn; disdain; contemn; despect (archaic); disrespect; despise; disdain; disesteem; look down on (idiomatic); misrespect; scorn; spurn; fleer (archaic); flout; gird; jeer (archaic); jibe; scoff; scorn

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To reject, turn down with disdain.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He scorned her romantic advances.

   **Source synonyms:** spurn

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To refuse to do something, as beneath oneself.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** She scorned to show weakness.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To scoff, to express contempt.

   **Usage:** intransitive
