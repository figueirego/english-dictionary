### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To experience; to pass through a phase.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The project is undergoing great changes.

   **Source synonyms:** go through; [take](../346/take--d1905cf90af2.md); undercome; run; see; stand

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To suffer or endure; bear with.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The victim underwent great trauma.

   **Example:** She had to undergo surgery because of her broken leg.

   **Source synonyms:** brook; put up with; abear; abide; aby (British; dialectal); accept; allow; bear; bide (dialectal); brook; dree (British; dialectal); endure; forbear; go along with; live with; pass; pocket; pouch; put up with; stand; stick; stomach; suffer; swallow; [take](../346/take--d1905cf90af2.md); take sitting down (idiomatic); thole (British; dialectal); tolerate; undergang (British; dialectal); undergo

**Source sense R1.S3**

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive
