### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make a loud, deep cry, especially from pain, anger, or other strong emotion.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To laugh in a particularly loud manner.

   **Example:** The audience roared at his jokes.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of animals (especially a lion), to make a loud deep noise.

   **Example:** The lioness roared to scare off the hyenas.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Generally, of inanimate objects etc., to make a loud resounding noise.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To proceed vigorously.

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To cry aloud; to proclaim loudly.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To be boisterous; to be disorderly.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To make a loud noise in breathing, as horses do when they have a certain disease.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To cry.

   **Usage:** Midlands; North; UK; Yorkshire; informal
