### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To want (something) to happen, with a sense of expectation that it might \[with that (+ clause); or (informal) with clause; or with so or (negative) not\].

   **Example:** They are hoping it does not rain, but I expect it will.

   **Example:** He's still hoping that everything will turn out fine.

   **Example:** — Is he going to shut up soon? — I hope so.

   **Example:** I'm going to get a new car. I hope it will be better than the last one.

   **Example:** I'd hoped I'd find a job, but I never did, so I was hoping you could lend me some cash.

   **Example:** I hope \{to - (that) I'll\} have finished by next Sat at the latest.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To intend to do something and look forward to the prospect of having done it \[with to (+ infinitive)\].

   **Usage:** catenative

   **Example:** I hope to succeed.

   **Example:** I hope to be going back again soon.

   **Example:** I was hoping to find a pair of jeans, but I couldn't.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To expect optimistically that one might get something (either a change in circumstance or an object) \[with for\].

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** They're hoping for the best, but I don't think it's looking very good.

   **Example:** I'm hoping for my boss to offer me a pay raise.

   **Example:** He's past hoping for any improvement.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To place confidence; to trust with confident expectation of good \[with in\].

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To wish.

   **Usage:** dialectal; nonstandard; transitive

   **Example:** I hope you all the best.

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. The feeling of trust, confidence, belief or expectation that something wished for can or will happen.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** All hopes for a truce are gone after the latest attack.

   **Example:** After losing my job, there's frail hope of affording my world cruise.

   **Example:** There is still hope that we can find our missing cat.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. The actual thing wished for.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. A person or thing that is a source of hope.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** We still have one hope left&#58; my roommate might see the note I left on the table.

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. The virtuous desire for future good.
