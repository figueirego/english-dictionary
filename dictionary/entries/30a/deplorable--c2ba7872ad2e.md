### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To be deplored.
   1. To be felt sorrow for; worthy of compassion; lamentable.

      **Example:** We were all saddened by the deplorable death of his son.

      **Source synonyms:** deplorate (obsolete); pathetic; atrabiliary; atrabilious; deplorable; depressing; distressing; grievous; heartbreaking; heartrending; miserable (obsolete); lamentable; lachrymable (archaic); mournful; regrettable; rueful; sad; saddening; sorrowful; tragic; unhappy; upsetting; woebegone; woeful

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To be deplored.
   1. Deserving strong condemnation; shockingly bad, wretched.

      **Example:** Poor children suffer permanent damage due to deplorable living conditions and deplorable treatment by law enforcement.

      **Example:** Poor children are often accused of having deplorable manners, when they are, in fact, simply responding to society in ways that mirror how society treats them.

      **Source synonyms:** damnable; scornworthy
