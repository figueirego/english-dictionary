### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To stick out from a surface without breaking it.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** He stood six feet tall, with muscular arms bulging out of his black T-shirt.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. (of a container, etc.) To have the surface stretched by something pushing out; to swell; to belly.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The submarine bulged because of the enormous air pressure inside.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To bilge, as a ship; to founder.
