## Interjection · Record 2

### Forms

- whatthefuck — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Used to express astonishment, shock, incredulity, or disbelief (as an ellipsis of longer expressions).

   **Usage:** vulgar

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Used to express nonchalance or the dismissal of any consequences of something one is about to do.

   **Usage:** vulgar

   **Example:** I finally said “eh, what the fuck” and quit my job.
