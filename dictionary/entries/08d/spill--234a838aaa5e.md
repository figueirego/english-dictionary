### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To drop something so that it spreads out or makes a mess; to accidentally pour.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I spilled some sticky juice on the kitchen floor.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To spread out or fall out, as above.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Some sticky juice spilled onto the kitchen floor.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To overflow out of a designated area.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The crowd spilled onto Maple Avenue.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a crowd or people within a crowd

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To drop something that was intended to be caught.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To mar; to damage; to destroy by misuse; to waste.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To be destroyed, ruined, or wasted; to come to ruin; to perish; to waste.

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To overflow or flow out, over or off something.

   **Usage:** also; figuratively; intransitive

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To cause or flow out and be lost or wasted; to shed.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To cause to be thrown from a mount, a carriage, etc.

   **Usage:** obsolete; slang; transitive

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**
