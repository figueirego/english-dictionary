### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To grip suddenly; to seize; to clutch.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I grabbed her hand to pull her back from the cliff edge.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To make a sudden grasping or clutching motion (at something).

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The suspect suddenly broke free and grabbed at the policeman's gun.

   **Example:** Stop grabbing or I won't give you any cookies.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To restrain someone; to arrest.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To grip the attention of; to enthrall or interest.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** How does that idea grab you?

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To quickly collect, retrieve, or take.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** Come in and grab a seat \[i.e. sit down\].

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To consume something quickly.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** We'll just grab a sandwich and then we'll be on our way.

   **Example:** Is there time to grab a coffee?

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To take the opportunity of.
