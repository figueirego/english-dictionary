## Adverb

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. With reference to artifacts.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Apparently but not truly, with the appearance being due to artifacts; spuriously.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; medical imaging or laboratory testing

   **Example:** The weight measurements in the first run were artifactually high because of a tare weight error.

   **Example:** Some histopathologic details were artifactually obscured by the staining.
