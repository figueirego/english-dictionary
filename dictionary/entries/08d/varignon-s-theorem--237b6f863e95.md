## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A statement in Euclidean geometry that deals with the construction of a particular parallelogram (the Varignon parallelogram) from an arbitrary quadrilateral.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A theorem stating that the torque of a resultant of two concurrent forces about any point is equal to the algebraic sum of the torques of its components about the same point.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; mechanical-engineering; mechanics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences
