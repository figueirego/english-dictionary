### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To chew cud. (Said of ruminants.) Involves regurgitating partially digested food from the rumen.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** A camel will ruminate just as a cow will.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To meditate or reflect.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** I didn't answer right away because I needed to ruminate first.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To meditate or ponder over; to muse on.

   **Usage:** transitive
