**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Tumbling upside down; somersaulting.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** She tripped and rolled head over heels down the hill.

   **Source synonyms:** arse over tit; ass over teakettle; base over apex

**Source sense R1.S2**
