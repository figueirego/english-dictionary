### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A small, relatively inexpensive Christmas present suitable for placing in a child's stocking, which was traditionally hung by a fireplace on Christmas Eve in anticipation that Santa Claus would fill it with gifts.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Source synonyms:** stocking filler
