## Noun

### Forms

- highpriest — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A clergyman with a higher function than a normal priest.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** lifestyle; religion

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In the Bible, the male individual who was responsible for making the annual sacrifice on the Day of Atonement (Yom Kippur); always a descendant of Aaron, the older brother of Moses. See Kohen Gadol.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Jesus Christ.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The second-lowest office in the Melchizedek priesthood.

   **Usage:** Mormonism

   **Example:** Jake was ordained a high priest when he was called to be a counselor in a bishopric.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A person holding a position of power or influence; an authority in a field of study, doctrine, art or a movement.

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A second or third degree male witch in Wicca.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Wicca; lifestyle; religion
