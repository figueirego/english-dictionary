### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To sing (a song) loudly; to emit (music) loudly.

   **Usage:** informal; transitive

   **Example:** He belted out Sweet Child of Mine at the karaoke.

   **Example:** Lively show tunes being belted out by the pianist on the grand piano.

   **Example:** The jukebox belted out tunes from the fifties, and waiters shouted food orders to the cooks behind the enormous steel counter.
