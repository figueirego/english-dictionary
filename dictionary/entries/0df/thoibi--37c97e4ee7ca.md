## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A female given name from Meitei; A transliteration of the Manipuri female given name ꯊꯣꯏꯕꯤ (thoʼibi).

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A princess of Moirang kingdom; daughter of Crown Prince Chingkhu Akhuba, the younger brother of King Chingkhu Telheiba (1083-1138 CE). She was the heroine and the female protagonist of the Khamba-Thoibi epic.
