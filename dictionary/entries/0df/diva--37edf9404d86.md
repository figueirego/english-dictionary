### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any female celebrity, usually a well known singer or actress.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person with an inflated sense of self, who has high expectations of others, and who is extremely demanding and fussy when it comes to personal privileges.

   **Usage:** derogatory; informal; slang

   **Source synonyms:** prima donna; Miss Piggy

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. One who amazes or stuns, especially in a confident and feminine manner; (by extension) a term of endearment.

   **Usage:** Internet

   **Example:** Who is this DIVA 💜?
