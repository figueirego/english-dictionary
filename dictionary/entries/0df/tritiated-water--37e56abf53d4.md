## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Water in which one or both protium (normal hydrogen) atoms have been replaced by atoms of tritium hydrogen.
   1. Fully tritiated water&#58; super-heavy water (³H₂O).

      **Usage:** uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; inorganic-chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences
