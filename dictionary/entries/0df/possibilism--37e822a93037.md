### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; human-sciences; philosophy; politics; sciences

   **Source antonyms:** actualism

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; natural-sciences
