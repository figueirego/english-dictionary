## Noun · Record 2

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A step in which one foot meets the other foot without passing it and the weight shifts to the foot that just moved.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**
