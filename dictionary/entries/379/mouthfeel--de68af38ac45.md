## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A feeling (other than taste) perceived inside the mouth.
   1. The texture of food, drink, etc. as perceived by the mouth.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Example:** Dark ale tends to have a creamy mouthfeel.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A feeling (other than taste) perceived inside the mouth.
   1. The sensations of the tongue when it moves around in the mouth.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Example:** I have a much better mouthfeel since my broken tooth has been repaired.
