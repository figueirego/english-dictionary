### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having four forward speeds.

   **Usage:** attributive; uncountable

   **Example:** My car has a four-speed transmission. It is not as efficient as my daughter's five-speed.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a transmission

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A transmission that has four forward speeds (it might or might not be a manual transmission); (metonymic) a vehicle with such a transmission.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** four-speed manual

   **Example:** four-speed automatic
