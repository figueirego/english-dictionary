### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of the several cereal grains, of the genus Triticum, that yields flour as used in bakery.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** They bought a sack of wheat and a sack of rye with the intent to try their hand at milling and baking.

   **Source synonyms:** triticum

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A light brown color, like that of wheat.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** They debated the paint color options for the bathroom walls and settled on wheat.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Ellipsis of wheat bread

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; ellipsis; uncountable
