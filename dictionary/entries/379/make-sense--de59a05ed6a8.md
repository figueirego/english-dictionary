### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To be sensible, coherent, reasonable.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** The thing doesn’t make sense to me.

   **Example:** Somehow the combination didn’t make sense, but Cranston took it at face value, whatever that was worth.

   **Source synonyms:** add up; figure; track; check out

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To decipher or understand.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive; with-of

   **Example:** Can you make sense of her handwriting?

   **Source synonyms:** make head or tail of

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used to express interest or desire in something; to be pleasing or beneficial; to work, be operative, or be advantageous to.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** Maybe we should take a break. I mean, our relationship just isn't making much sense anymore.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To be in the realm of the ordinary, to be not particularly developed.

   **Usage:** slang; generally negated

   **Example:** If you want to grow spinal erectors that don’t make sense, you have to do a lot of bent-over compound lifts.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** bodybuilding; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

   **Source antonyms:** ridiculous; absurd
