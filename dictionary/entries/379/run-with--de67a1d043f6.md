### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To be streaming with a fluid.

   **Usage:** literally

   **Example:** After a long run, his face was running with sweat.

   **Example:** The streets were running with rain water.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To follow something through to completion or realization.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; informal

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To take an incomplete or inadequate (plan, text, etc.) and develop it further, often with the implication of carelessness.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; informal

   **Example:** They took this three-second sound bite and ran with it to try to smear me.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To be a member of (a gang, hooligan firm, etc.); to associate with a, typically disreputable, individual or group.

   **Usage:** US; idiomatic; informal

   **Source synonyms:** consort (formal); keep company

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see run, with.

   **Example:** The thief was running with the purse in his hands.
