### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A list of dramas, operas, pieces, parts, etc., which a company or a person has rehearsed and is prepared to perform or display.

   **Example:** The conjurer expanded his repertoire with some new tricks.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The set of skills, abilities, experiences, etc., possessed by a person.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An amount, body, or collection of something.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**
