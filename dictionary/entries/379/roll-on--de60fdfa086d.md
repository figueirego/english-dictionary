## Verb · Record 1

### Forms

- rolls on — present; singular; third-person

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To pass; to go on; to elapse.

   **Usage:** especially; intransitive

   **Source context (verbatim):** of time

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To continue to move forwards.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To load or apply something with a rolling motion.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He rolled on some deodorant, threw on a shirt, and ran out the door.

   **Example:** He rolled on some paint, slapped up a "wet paint" sign, and called it a day.

   **Example:** You don't have to lift this equipment onto the truck, because it rolls right on if you align its casters with those little grooves.

   **Source antonyms:** roll off

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To snitch or tattle (on a person or group), to reveal a secret under pressure, usually regarding criminal matters.

   **Usage:** slang; transitive

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** go by — to elapse; keep on — to move forwards; go on; inform on; grass up
