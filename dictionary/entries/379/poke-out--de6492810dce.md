### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To be partly visible past an obstruction or obstructions.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source synonyms:** stick out; protrude

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To emerge from behind, in, or under something.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source synonyms:** pop out

   **Usage:** transitive
