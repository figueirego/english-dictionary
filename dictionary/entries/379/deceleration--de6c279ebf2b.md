### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act or process of decelerating.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** The rocket is now in deceleration.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The amount by which a speed or velocity decreases (and so a scalar quantity or a vector quantity), an acceleration having a negative numerical value.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** The brakes produce a deceleration of 10 metres per second.
