### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The flexible structure connecting a foetus with the placenta; it transports nourishment to the foetus and removes waste.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; medicine; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** navel-string; umbilicus

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The line that supplies an astronaut with oxygen and communications when outside a spacecraft.

   **Usage:** spacecraft

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of the various lines connecting a rocket to its launch pad before liftoff.

   **Usage:** space flight

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Synonym of funiculus.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences

   **Source synonyms:** funiculus (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An overdependence on or overattachment to support, especially from one's parents or guardians.

   **Usage:** figuratively
