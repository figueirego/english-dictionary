### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Almost but not quite.

   **Example:** With our Medicare supplemental insurance plan, there are virtually no claim forms to fill out. (Advertisement)

   **Source synonyms:** as good as; more or less; nearly; practically

   **Source antonyms:** actually

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In essence or functionally, but not in fact, formally, or technically.

   **Example:** virtually a member of the family

   **Source synonyms:** effectively; practically

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of a subgroup of finite index.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** algebra; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences; topology
