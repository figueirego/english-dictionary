**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Source synonyms:** in-basket; in-tray

   **Source antonyms:** outbox

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An electronic folder serving the same purpose, but for electronic files, especially email.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** The kids, my ex, my parents, the job, bills — my inbox is full.
