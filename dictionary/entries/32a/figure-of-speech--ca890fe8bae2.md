## Noun

### Forms

- figures of speech — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A word or phrase that intentionally deviates from ordinary language use to produce a rhetorical effect.

   **Usage:** rhetoric; stylistic

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A turn of phrase that is not intended to be interpreted literally, but is used solely as a rhetorical device for the purpose of facilitating effective and nuanced communication.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** I hope you didn't take it the wrong way, that was a figure of speech.

**Source synonyms:** figure of speech; turn of phrase
