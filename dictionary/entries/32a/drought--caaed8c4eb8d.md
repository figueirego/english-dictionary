### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A period of unusually low rainfall, longer and more severe than a dry spell.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** His village had faced a drought twice.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A longer than expected term without success, particularly in sport.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; informal; uncountable

   **Example:** Sri Lanka ended their ten-game drought with a consolation win over Bangladesh.

   **Source synonyms:** losing streak

   **Source antonyms:** winning streak

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. dryness, aridness, dry heat

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; uncountable
