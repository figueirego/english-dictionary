### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having a color.

   **Usage:** US

   **Example:** Wash colored items separately from whites and darks to prevent the colors from bleeding.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Having a particular color or kind of color.

   **Usage:** US; in-compounds

   **Example:** The room was red, with a dark-colored rug.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Having prominent colors; colorful.

   **Usage:** US

   **Example:** The singer wore a colored shirt.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Biased; pervasively (but potentially subtly) influenced in a particular way.

   **Usage:** US

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Of skin color other than white; in particular, black.

   **Usage:** US; dated; offensive; usually

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Belonging to a multiracial ethnic group or category, having ancestry from more than one of the racial groups of Southern Africa (black, white, and Asian). (Under apartheid, used as a metadescription for mixed-race people and peoples such as the Cape Coloureds.)

   **Usage:** Botswana; Namibia; South-Africa; US; Zimbabwe; capitalized; sometimes

   **Example:** Most of the colored community speaks Afrikaans, whereas languages like Xhosa or Venda are typically spoken by blacks and English is spoken mostly by whites.

   **Source synonyms:** Eurafrican

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Designated for use by colored people (in either the US or South African sense).

   **Usage:** US; historical

   **Example:** a colored drinking fountain

   **Example:** a colored hospital
