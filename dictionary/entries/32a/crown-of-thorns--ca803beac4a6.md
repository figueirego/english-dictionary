### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The crown made of a thorny plant that Christ was forced to wear before his Crucifixion, in mocking reference to his alleged status as ‘king of the Jews’, or a representation of this.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A spiny Mediterranean tree, Ziziphus spina-christi.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A spiny spurge, Euphorbia milii, native to Madagascar.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The plant Koeberlinia spinosa.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Ellipsis of crown-of-thorns starfish

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; ellipsis

   **Alternative form of:** [crown-of-thorns starfish](../2dd/crown-of-thorns-starfish--b756a82102b0.md)

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A woodworking technique, used in tramp art, in which interlocking pieces are notched to intersect at right angles and form joints and self-supporting objects.
