### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A type of fallacious argument in which the attempt is made to refute a theory or belief by discrediting the person(s) who advocate that theory or belief; an ad hominem argument.

   **Usage:** rhetoric

**Source synonyms:** ad hominem; ad hominem argument
