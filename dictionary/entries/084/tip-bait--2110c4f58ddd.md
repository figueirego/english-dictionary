### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The promise of a large tip (usually on a delivery app) so that the deliverer feels incentivised to swiftly reach their destination, only to deliberately decrease or cancel the tip once the delivery is completed.

   **Usage:** Internet
