### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To be discernible despite obstruction; to be apparent or evidenced.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive; usually

   **Example:** Though she makes lots of spelling and grammar mistakes in her article, her passion for the subject shines through.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of positive things

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To be visible across a barrier.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The clouds are heavy, but the sunlight is still managing to shine through.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of light
