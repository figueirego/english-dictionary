## Proverb

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The month of April, traditionally a rainy period, gives way to May, when flowers will bloom because of the water provided to them by the April rains.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A period of discomfort or hardship can provide the basis for a period of happiness and joy.

   **Usage:** broadly; figuratively
