## Verb

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To remain beneath a level.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The results bubbled under the limit.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To be in the background or margins of a phenomenon.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The Republicans were bubbling under in the area, waiting for an electoral break.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To be successful on a modest scale, without yet being fully established.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The band was still bubbling under then, but their fame was about to explode.
