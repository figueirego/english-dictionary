### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To be larger, greater than (something).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The company's 2005 revenue exceeds that of 2004.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To be better than (something).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The quality of her essay has exceeded my expectations.

   **Example:** Descriptions of nature that far exceed anything else we've heard.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To go beyond (some limit); to surpass; to be longer than.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Your password cannot exceed eight characters.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To predominate.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To go too far; to be excessive.

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete
