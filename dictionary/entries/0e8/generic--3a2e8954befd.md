### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Very broad; pertaining or appropriate to large classes or groups as opposed to specific instances.

   **Example:** Capri pants can be a generic term for any cropped slim pants.

   **Source antonyms:** specific; instantial

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Lacking in precision, often in an evasive fashion; vague; imprecise.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of a product or drug, not having a brand name; nonproprietary in design or contents; fungible with the rest of its class.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; pharmacology; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Pertaining to genera of life instead of particular species thereof.

   **Example:** There are scores of generic names within the order Decapoda, which includes many sea creatures that are called shrimp.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences; taxonomy

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Specifying neither masculine nor feminine; epicene; unisex.

   **Usage:** nonstandard

   **Example:** Words like salesperson and firefighter are generic.

   **Example:** This included criticism of the generic use of man to include men and women.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** grammar; human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Of a procedure, written so as to operate on any data type, the type required being passed as a parameter.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Of a point, having coordinates that are algebraically independent over the base field.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Relating to genre.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. Having no distinguishing characteristics; unoriginal.

   **Example:** That movie was so generic; it was such a bore!
