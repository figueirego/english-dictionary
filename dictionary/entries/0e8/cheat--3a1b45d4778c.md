   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** My husband cheated on me with his secretary.

   **Example:** After he found out his wife cheated, he left her.

   **Source synonyms:** step out on

**Source sense R1.S3**

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He cheated death when his car collided with a moving train.

   **Example:** I feel as if I've cheated fate.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To deceive; to fool; to trick.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** My ex-wife cheated me out of $40,000.

   **Example:** He cheated his way into office.

   **Source synonyms:** rook; blench; lirt; burn (informal; passive; usually); beguile (literary)
