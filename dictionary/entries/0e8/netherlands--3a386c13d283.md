<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The Kingdom of the Netherlands. A country in Western Europe, consisting of four constituent countries&#58; the Netherlands per se, Aruba, Curaçao and Sint Maarten.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The main constituent country of the Kingdom of the Netherlands, located primarily in Western Europe bordering Germany and Belgium. Capital and largest city&#58; Amsterdam.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. the Low Countries, a region of Western Europe located around the low-lying areas in and around the Rhine–Meuse–Scheldt delta, including the countries of the Netherlands, Belgium and Luxembourg.

   **Usage:** historical; rare; uncountable; usually
