## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The state of having invested heavily in just one area.

   **Usage:** plural; plural-only

   **Example:** The stock market decline wouldn’t have hurt him so badly if he hadn’t had all his eggs in one basket.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The state of having devoted all of one’s resources to one thing.

   **Usage:** plural; plural-only

   **Example:** At his age, he won't get another shot; he really has all his eggs in this one basket.
