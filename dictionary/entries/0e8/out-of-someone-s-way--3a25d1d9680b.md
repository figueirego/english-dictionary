## Prep\_phrase · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Not in the direct route that one would normally use.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I gave him a lift, even though his house was a little out of my way.
