## Adjective

### Forms

- most senseless — superlative
- senselesse — alternative; obsolete

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Without feeling or desire to work; deprived of sensation.

   **Example:** The blow to his head rendered him senseless; he didn’t awaken until he was in the ambulance.

   **Source synonyms:** unconscious; insensible

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Lacking meaning or purpose; without common sense.

   **Example:** What a senseless waste of money.

   **Source synonyms:** pointless; meaningless

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Without a discernible meaning or purpose, especially of a violent or evil act.

   **Example:** The school shooting was a senseless act of violence.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Without consideration, awareness or sound judgement

   **Example:** He took senseless risks, not even aware of the danger he was in.

   **Source synonyms:** unreasonable; unwise; stupid
