### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any property that is movable, that is, not real estate.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The lawyers were on about the common law around personal property.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

   **Source synonyms:** personalty

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any property that belongs to one person.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; non-legal

   **Example:** That pasture is the boss's own personal property. No company cattle and no one else's animals are allowed down there without his written permission.
