### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A read operation that does not erase the data being read in the process of reading it.

   **Usage:** attributive; often

   **Example:** Although non-destructive reads are theoretically possible with FeRAM, the process - requiring illumination of the memory cells with UV light - is completely impractical for most purposes, favoring the more-practical (but destructive) method of forcing every cell to 0, seeing which cells produced a tiny current pulse, and writing back 1s to those cells.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences
