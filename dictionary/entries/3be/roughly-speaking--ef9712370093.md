## Adverb

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Not being totally accurate (used as a phrase to specify).

   **Example:** For our prosperity is inherited from our ancestors, and is of an earlier date than the prosperity not only of Philip, but, roughly speaking, of all the kings that have ever reigned in Macedonia.

   **Source synonyms:** broadly speaking

   **Source antonyms:** strictly speaking
