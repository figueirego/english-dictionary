### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To take for granted; to conclude, with less than absolute supporting data; to believe.

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** I suppose we all agree that this is the best solution.

   **Example:** I won't be back before midday, I don't suppose.

   **Example:** I don't suppose you could lend me a euro, could you?

   **Example:** We're a place setting short, there'll be four of us - always supposing your sis really comes.

   **Example:** Mum, can go to the park yet? ~ Suppose you did your homework first.

   **Source synonyms:** assume

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To theorize or hypothesize.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Suppose that A implies B and B implies C. Then A implies C.

   **Source synonyms:** assume

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To imagine; to believe; to receive as true.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To reckon to be, to account or esteem as.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To require to exist or to be true; to imply by the laws of thought or of nature.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Purpose supposes foresight.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To put by fraud in the place of another; to substitute fraudulently.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive
