### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To put up to hang.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** I hung up my wash on the line.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To terminate a telephone call, originally by hanging the receiver on its hook or cradle thereby automatically disconnecting the line.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive; idiomatic

   **Example:** When my mother started telling me to be careful over the phone, I threatened to hang up on her.

   **Source synonyms:** ring off

   **Source antonyms:** pick up the phone

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To stop talking.

   **Usage:** figuratively; idiomatic; intransitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To keep delayed, suspended, held up, or stuck.

   **Usage:** colloquial; idiomatic; transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To rob or mug (someone).

   **Usage:** idiomatic

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To arrest (someone); to send (someone) to prison.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To quit (something); to give up or stop doing (something); to abandon (something).

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** He hung up the flute after college.

   **Example:** I think it's time to hang it up.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To distress, disadvantage or harm (someone).

   **Usage:** idiomatic
