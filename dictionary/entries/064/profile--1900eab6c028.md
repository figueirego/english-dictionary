### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The outermost shape, view, or edge of an object.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** His fingers traced the profile of the handle.

   **Source synonyms:** contour

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The shape, view, or shadow of a person's head from the side; a side view.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** The brooch showed the profile of a Victorian woman.

   **Example:** Driver's licenses have a photograph of the person on them, which is in full face if the person is above legal drinking age, or in profile if not.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A summary or collection of information, especially about a person.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** Law enforcement assembled a profile of the suspect.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A specific page or field in which users can provide various types of personal information in software or Internet systems.

   **Usage:** Internet; countable

   **Example:** I just updated my Facebook profile to show I got engaged.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Reputation, prominence; noticeability.

   **Usage:** figuratively; uncountable

   **Example:** Acting is, by nature, profession in which one must keep a high profile.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The amount by which something protrudes.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Choose a handle with a low profile so it does not catch on things.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A smoothed (e.g., troweled or brushed) vertical surface of an excavation showing evidence of at least one feature or diagnostic specimen; the graphic recording of such as by sketching, photographing, etc.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A section of any member, made at right angles with its main lines, showing the exact shape of mouldings etc.
