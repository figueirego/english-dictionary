### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A quick, light sweeping motion.

   **Example:** With a quick whisk, she swept the cat from the pantry with her broom.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A kitchen utensil, now usually made from stiff wire loops fixed to a handle (and formerly of twigs), used for whipping (or a mechanical device with the same function).

   **Example:** He used a whisk to whip up a light and airy souffle.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A bunch of twigs, hair, etc., used as a brush.

   **Example:** Peter dipped the whisk in lather and applied it to his face, so he could start shaving.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A small handheld broom with a small (or no) handle.

   **Example:** I used a whisk to sweep the counter, then a push-broom for the floor.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A plane used by coopers for evening chines.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A kind of cape, forming part of a woman's dress.

## Verb · Etymology 1 · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈwɪsk/ — variety unspecified in the source labels
- IPA: /ˈʍɪsk/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Forms

- whisks — present; singular; third-person
- whisking — participle; present
- whisked — participle; past
- whisked — past

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To move something with quick light sweeping motions.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Vernon whisked the sawdust from his workbench.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. In cooking, to whip e.g. eggs or cream.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The chef prepared to whisk the egg whites for the angel's food cake.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To move something rapidly and with no warning.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The governess whisked the children from the room before they could see their presents.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To move lightly and nimbly.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The children whisked down the road to the fair, laughing and chattering as they went.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To move whiskers.

   **Usage:** transitive
