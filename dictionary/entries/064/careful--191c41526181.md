### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Taking care; attentive to potential danger, error or harm; cautious.

   **Example:** He was a slow and careful driver.

   **Example:** Be very careful (while) trekking through the jungle.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Conscientious and painstaking; meticulous.

   **Example:** They made a careful search of the crime scene.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Full of care or grief; sorrowful, sad.

   **Usage:** obsolete
