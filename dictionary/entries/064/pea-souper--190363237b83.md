### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A dense, yellowish fog, often mixed with smoke; a pea-soup fog, a smog.

   **Usage:** Canada; UK; informal

   **Example:** A man bumped into a lamp post during a pea-souper; he immediately apologised to it, not realising what it was.

   **Example:** I can hear the bell on the buoy, but I can’t see anything in this pea-souper.

   **Source synonyms:** pea soup fog; pea-soup fog; peasoup fog

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A French-Canadian person, especially a Francophone from the province of Québec.

   **Usage:** Canada; derogatory; slang

   **Example:** Those pea-soupers are the worst drivers on the road!
