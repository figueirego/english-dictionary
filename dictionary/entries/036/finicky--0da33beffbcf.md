### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Fastidious and fussy; difficult to please; exacting, especially about details.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** My editor is very finicky about punctuation. Every dot and comma has to be just right.

   **Example:** She has a finicky baby that's hard to feed.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a person

   **Source synonyms:** fastidious; fussy; anal-retentive; choosy; conscientious; dainty; exacting; fastidious; finical; finicking; finickity; finicky; finikin; fussy; hypercritical; lily-handed; meticulous; niggly; nitpickish; nit-picky; nitty; overcritical; overscrupulous; particular; pedantic; pernickety; persnickety; picky

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Demanding; requiring above-normal care.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** The lawnmower is a bit finicky in cold weather.
