**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Alternative form of cycle of fifths.

   **Usage:** alt-of; alternative

   **Example:** The sharp side of the circle of fifths can be read off directly from the white keys on a piano's octave (by making the appropriate jumps)&#58; Do–So–Re–La–Mi–Ti–Fi. Also, the flat side of the circle of fifths can almost be read off entirely from the black keys on a piano's octave (with the exception of Fa)&#58; Do–Fa–Te–Me–Le–Ra–Se.

   **Alternative form of:** [cycle of fifths](../0e5/cycle-of-fifths--394d005c10da.md)
