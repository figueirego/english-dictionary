### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. It is not important; do not fret; used to reassure or comfort the person to whom it is said.

   **Usage:** hortative

   **Example:** I'm afraid I’ve broken your mug. — Never mind, it was old and I was going to throw it away.

   **Example:** Did you fall over and hurt your knee? Never mind, I’ll put a bandage on it.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Do not be concerned (about someone or something, or about doing something).

   **Usage:** hortative

   **Example:** Never mind about me — you go and I’ll join you later.

   **Example:** Here’s some money for you. Never mind about paying me back; you can keep it.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Indicates a withdrawal or retractment of a previous statement.

   **Usage:** hortative

   **Example:** You're a fool. — What did you call me? — Never mind.

   **Example:** Can you hand me that screwdriver? Wait, never mind, give me the fire extinguisher instead.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Let alone; much less.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** They wouldn't go near that place, never mind spending an entire day there.
