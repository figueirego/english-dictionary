<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To make a reply or response to.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** He answered the question.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To speak in defence against; to reply to in defence.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to answer a charge or accusation

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To respond to a call by someone at a door or telephone, or other similar piece of equipment.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** She answered the door.

   **Example:** Nobody answered when I knocked on the door.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To suit a need or purpose satisfactorily.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** It answers the need.

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To be accountable or responsible; to make amends.

   **Example:** The man must answer to his employer for the money entrusted to his care.
