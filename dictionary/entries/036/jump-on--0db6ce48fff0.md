### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To board a vehicle.

   **Example:** Jump on a nº9 bus, and it will take you more or less to the door.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To attack verbally, to criticise excessively.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I hate the way she's always jumping on me for the slightest little mistake.
