## Noun

### Forms

- cassises — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A blackcurrant plant (Ribes nigrum); the flavor of its berries.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A liqueur made from these berries, especially crème de cassis.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** Cassis and soda is a popular drink.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A wine flavor note, suggesting the fruity and full-bodied characteristics of the fruit; mostly referred to as simply blackcurrant in the UK, where the fruit is common.

   **Usage:** US; uncountable; usually
