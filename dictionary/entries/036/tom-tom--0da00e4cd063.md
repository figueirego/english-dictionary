## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

- tom-toms — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A small drum, often one of a joined pair, beaten with the hands, frequently associated with tribal cultures.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any cylindrical drum, with no snare; part of a drum kit.

   **Usage:** usually

   **Source context (verbatim):** as a pair

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Alternative form of tam-tam (“a kind of flat gong”).

   **Usage:** alt-of; alternative
