**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To hastily put on (clothes).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He threw on a T-shirt and raced outside.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To activate, to turn on (a device etc.).
