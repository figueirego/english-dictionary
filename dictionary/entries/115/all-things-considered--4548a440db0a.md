## Adverb

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Generally speaking; in terms of the big picture.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; modal; not-comparable

   **Example:** Of course some areas are more dangerous than others, but all things considered Glasgow is quite a safe place to live.

   **Source synonyms:** all in all; by and large; for the most part; in the main; on balance; on the whole; all in all; all things considered (idiomatic); altogether; broadly; as a rule (idiomatic); basically; by and large; chiefly; feckly (Scottish); for the most part (idiomatic); generally; in the large; in the main; largely; mainly; mostly; on the whole; overall; predominantly; pretty well; primarily; principally; all things considered; altogether; at large; as a whole; broadly; catholicly; in general; generally; generically; overall; universally

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Despite possible indications to the contrary.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; modal; not-comparable

   **Example:** Though only a few people attended the premiere, all things considered the play was rather a success.

   **Source synonyms:** actually; as a matter of fact; in fact; overall; above a bit; actually; all things considered (idiomatic); as a matter of fact; by right (obsolete); certainly; come to think of it (idiomatic); de facto; definitely; de jure; fact is (idiomatic); forsooth (archaic; poetic); frankly; honestly; in actuality; in all honesty; indubitably; in fact; in faith (archaic); in point of fact; in reality; in truth; indeed; legitimately; literally; not gonna lie; properly; really; right; simply; soothfast (obsolete); soothly (archaic); surely; to be frank; to be honest; TBH; tbh; to tell the truth (idiomatic); truly; truthfully; undoubtedly; verily (archaic); very much; wis (dialectal)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Given the constraints of the situation; relative to what would have been understandable.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; modal; not-comparable

   **Example:** Although rationing was strict, they had a couple of chickens and a vegetable patch and they ate well, all things considered.

   **Source synonyms:** nevertheless; otherwise; considering
