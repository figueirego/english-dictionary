### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To enclose or surround (something) with a hedge, trees or other plant life.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I've been working on hedging in the yard with trees.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To limit or constrain (someone).

   **Usage:** broadly; figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** She was hedged in by her husband's rules.

   **Example:** It was a promise, but hedged in with so many ifs and buts that I wouldn't rely on it.
