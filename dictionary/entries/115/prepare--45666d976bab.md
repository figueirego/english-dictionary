### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make ready for a specific future purpose; to set up; to assemble or equip; to forearm.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** We prepared the spacecraft for takeoff.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To make ready for eating or drinking; to cook.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** We prepared a fish for dinner.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To make oneself ready; to get ready, make preparation.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** We prepared for a bumpy ride.

   **Source synonyms:** get

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To produce or make by combining elements; to synthesize, compound.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** She prepared a meal from what was left in the cupboards.
