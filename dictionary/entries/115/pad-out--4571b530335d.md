### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To add something extra to something to make it appear more substantial.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** Some students pad out their essays by adding a whole lot of quotes from random sources.

   **Example:** Have you heard about girls padding out their bras to make their breasts look bigger?

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To sleep or go to bed.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; slang

   **Example:** Because of my job I usually pad out just before nine o'clock.
