### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of contradicting.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** His contradiction of the proposal was very interesting.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A statement that contradicts itself, i.e., a statement that claims that the same thing is true and that it is false at the same time and in the same senses of the terms.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** There is a contradiction in Clarence Page's statement that a woman should have the right to choose and decide for herself whether to have an abortion and at the same time she should not have that right.

   **Example:** There is a contradiction in what you say&#58; she can't be both married and single.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A logical inconsistency among two or more elements or propositions.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** Marx believed that the contradictions of capitalism would lead to socialism.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A proposition that is false for all values of its propositional variables or Boolean atoms.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; logic; mathematics; philosophy; sciences
