### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Small enough to be carried in a vest pocket or other small container.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** She carried a vest-pocket copy of the New Testament in her purse at all times.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Smaller than would ordinarily be expected.

   **Usage:** broadly; not-comparable

   **Example:** Levi's apartment was a vest-pocket Graceland; every inch of wall and shelf space was covered by Elvis memorabilia.
