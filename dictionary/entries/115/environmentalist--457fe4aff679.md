## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

- environmentalists — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who advocates for the protection of the environment and biosphere from misuse from human activity through such measures as ecosystem protection, waste reduction and pollution prevention.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One who holds the view that environment, rather than heredity or culture, is the primary factor in the development of an individual or group.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anthropology; human-sciences; sciences; social-science; social-sciences
