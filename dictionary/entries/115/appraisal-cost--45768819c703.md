**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The costs that are incurred in assessing that products/services conform to the requirements. It relates to testing, executing, and examination to assess whether specified quality is being maintained. Some example component items of the total appraisal costs are&#58; product quality audits, process control monitoring, inspection and testing of goods, and maintenance of test equipment.
