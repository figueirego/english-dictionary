### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The amount that will fit in a mouth.

   **Example:** He swallowed a mouthful of sea water when he fell in.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Quite a bit.

   **Usage:** slang

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Something difficult to pronounce or say.

   **Example:** “She sells sea shells” is a bit of a mouthful to say.

   **Source synonyms:** jawbreaker

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A tirade of abusive language.

   **Example:** to give someone a mouthful
