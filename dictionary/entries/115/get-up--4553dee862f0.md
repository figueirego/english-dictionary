**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To move in an upward direction; to ascend or climb.

   **Usage:** literally

   **Example:** The tired horse eventually got up and over the hurdle.

   **Example:** I'm having difficulty getting up the stairs.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To rise from one's bed, usually upon waking up in order to begin one's day.

   **Example:** I didn't get up until midday.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To move from a sitting or lying position to a standing position; to stand up.

   **Example:** Get up off the couch and clean this mess!
