### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A meeting or get-together; a party or social function.

   **Example:** The dinner was a star-spangled gathering unequaled in history.

   **Example:** I met her at a gathering of engineers and scientists.

   **Source synonyms:** assemblage; affair

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A group of people or things.

   **Example:** a gathering of Catholics

   **Source synonyms:** assemblage; concourse; congregation

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A section, a group of bifolios, or sheets of paper, stacked together and folded in half.

   **Example:** This gathering machine forms the backbone of a bookbinding operation.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** arts; bookbinding; crafts; hobbies; lifestyle

**Source sense R1.S4**
