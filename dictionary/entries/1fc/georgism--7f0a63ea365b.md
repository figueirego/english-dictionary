## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An economic philosophy stating that things found in nature, most importantly land, belong equally to all. It is usually associated with the idea of a single tax on the value of land.

   **Usage:** uncountable
