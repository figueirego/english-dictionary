## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A style of language, used by bureaucrats, that involves jargon or euphemism to the detriment of broader understanding.

   **Usage:** derogatory; informal; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Wordy, ostentatious talk or writing that resembles bureaucratic writing.

   **Usage:** derogatory; informal; uncountable

   **Example:** The company president's annual statement was complete bureaucratese&#58; it was utterly content-free.
