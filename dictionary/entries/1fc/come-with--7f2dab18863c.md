### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see come, with.

   **Example:** Come with us and see the film!

   **Example:** My new computer came with a keyboard and mouse, but no speakers.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To join and come along.

   **Usage:** South-Africa; informal; intransitive; Upper Midwestern US

   **Example:** We’re going out to lunch. Do you want to come with?

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Synonym of come up with (“to manage to produce something by inventing, creating, thinking of, or obtaining it”).

   **Example:** He had to come with an idea to end bullying.

   **Source synonyms:** come up with (synonym; synonym-of)
