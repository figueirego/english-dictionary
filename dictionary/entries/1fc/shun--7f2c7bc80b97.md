### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To avoid, especially persistently; ostracize.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Acrophobes shun mountaineering.

   **Source synonyms:** blacklist

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To escape (a threatening evil, an unwelcome task etc).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I'll shun meeting them for as long as possible.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

   **Usage:** transitive
