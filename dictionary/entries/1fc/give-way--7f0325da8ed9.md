## Verb

### Forms

- gives way — present; singular; third-person
- giving way — participle; present
- gave way — past
- given way — participle; past

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To yield to persistent persuasion.

   **Example:** The mother gave way to her crying child.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To collapse or break under physical stresses.

   **Example:** After years of neglect, the rusty old bridge could give way at any time.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To be followed, succeeded, or replaced by.

   **Example:** Winter gave way to spring.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To give precedence to other road users.

   **Example:** At the crossing, cars must give way to pedestrians.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To allow another person to intervene to make a point or ask a question whilst one is delivering a speech.

   **Example:** "Yes, I will give way."

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To allow the expression of (a pent-up emotion, grief, etc.).

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To begin rowing.

   **Usage:** imperative

   **Source context (verbatim):** as command to the crew

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** accede — yield to persistent persuasion; come around; concede

**Source antonyms:** way enough — begin rowing
