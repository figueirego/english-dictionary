### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A jacketlike garment with very long sleeves which can be secured in place, thus preventing the wearer from moving their arms; formerly often used in psychiatric hospitals to prevent patients from injuring themselves or others.

   **Source synonyms:** straitwaistcoat (dated)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any situation seen as confining or restricting.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** our ever-increasing bureaucratic straitjacket of regulations
