## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A tall weed, resembling milkweed, with a ball-shaped seed pod, Gomphocarpus fruticosus (syn. Asclepias fruticosa) and Gomphocarpus physocarpus (syn. Asclepias physocarpa).

   **Usage:** Australia; informal; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** balloon cotton, swan plant; (in USA) milkweed

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Cotton grass (Eriophorum spp.).

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A shrubby herb (Gossypium thurberi) of southern Arizona and Mexico.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any of the cultivated cottons that have escaped and established themselves in the wild.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Any of several plants of the genera Abutilon and Hibiscus, especially Hibiscus moscheutos.

   **Usage:** uncountable
