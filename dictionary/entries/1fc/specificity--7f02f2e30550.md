## Noun

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The state of being specific rather than general.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The extent to which a characteristic is specific to a given person, place, or thing; thus&#58;
   1. The probability, in a binary classification test, of a true negative being correctly identified.

      **Usage:** uncountable; usually

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences; statistics

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The extent to which a characteristic is specific to a given person, place, or thing; thus&#58;
   1. The extent to which a particular diagnostic test is specific for, or a symptom or sign is specific to, a given condition.

      **Usage:** uncountable; usually

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences
