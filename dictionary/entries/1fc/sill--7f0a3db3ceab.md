**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A breast wall; window breast; horizontal brink which forms the base of a window.

   **Usage:** also "window sill"

   **Example:** She looked out the window resting her elbows on the window sill.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** architecture

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A threshold; horizontal structural member of a building near ground level on a foundation or pilings, or lying on the ground, and bearing the upright portion of a frame; a sill plate.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; construction; manufacturing

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A stratum of rock, especially an intrusive layer of igneous rock lying parallel to surrounding strata.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; geology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A threshold or brink across the bottom of a canal lock for the gates to shut against.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A raised area at the base of the nasal aperture in the skull.

   **Example:** the nasal sill

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; medicine; sciences

**Source sense R1.S6**
