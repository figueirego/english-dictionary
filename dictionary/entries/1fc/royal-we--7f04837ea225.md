### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The first-person plural pronoun as traditionally used in formal speech by any royal personage to refer to themselves, most especially a sovereign in their role as the monarch.

   **Example:** Queen Victoria is the monarch most commonly associated with the use of the royal we, as in "We are not amused."

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Synonym of editorial we.

   **Usage:** humorous
