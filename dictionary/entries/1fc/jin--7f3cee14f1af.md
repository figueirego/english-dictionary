### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A river (晉水) in Shanxi Province, China, emptying into the Fen River.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A Chinese surname.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A titular principality of the Wei Empire (est. AD 264) and other later Chinese states.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An empire in China (AD 265–420) established by Sima Yan's overthrow of the Wei and replaced by barbarian conquerors in the north and Liu Yu's Song Empire in the south.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Synonym of Sima&#58; the dynasty which ruled this empire.

   **Usage:** historical

   **Source synonyms:** Sima (synonym; synonym-of)

**Source sense R1.S7**

   **Usage:** historical; chiefly Chinese contexts

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A river (Jin River, 晉江／晋江 (Jìnjiāng)) in Quanzhou Municipality, Fujian, China, emptying into the Taiwan Strait.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A prefecture of imperial China centered on Linfen, Shanxi.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. Former name of Linfen as the seat of the prefecture.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. An realm in early-10th century Shaanxi loyal to the Tang after their overthrow by the Later Liang.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s12"></a>

**Source sense R1.S12**

12. The Later Jin, a short-lived empire in 10th-century Shaanxi; the 930s and '40s, the era of this empire.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s13"></a>

**Source sense R1.S13**

13. Synonym of Shi&#58; the dynasty which ruled this empire.

   **Usage:** historical

   **Source synonyms:** Shi (synonym; synonym-of)
