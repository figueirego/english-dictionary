### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To feed or supply (cattle, sheep, etc.) with grass; to furnish pasture for.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To feed on; to eat (growing herbage); to eat grass from (a pasture)

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** Cattle graze in the meadows.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To tend (cattle, etc.) while grazing.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To eat small amounts of food periodically throughout the day, rather than at fixed mealtimes, often not in response to hunger.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To shoplift by consuming food or drink items before reaching the checkout.

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To rub or touch lightly the surface of (a thing) in passing.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** the bullet grazed the wall

   **Source synonyms:** glance

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

**Source sense R2.S7**

7. To cause a slight wound to; to scratch.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to graze one's knee

<a id="r2-s8"></a>

**Source sense R2.S8**

8. To yield grass for grazing.

   **Usage:** intransitive
