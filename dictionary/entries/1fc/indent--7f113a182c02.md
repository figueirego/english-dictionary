## Verb · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ɪnˈdɛnt/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Forms

- indents — present; singular; third-person
- indenting — participle; present
- indented — participle; past
- indented — past

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To notch; to jag; to cut into points like a row of teeth

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to indent the edge of paper

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To be cut, notched, or dented.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To dent; to stamp or to press in; to impress

   **Example:** indent a smooth surface with a hammer

   **Example:** to indent wax with a stamp

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To cut the two halves of a document in duplicate, using a jagged or wavy line so that each party could demonstrate that their copy was part of the original whole.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To enter into a binding agreement by means of such documents; to formally commit (to doing something); to contract.

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete; reflexive

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To engage (someone), originally by means of indented contracts.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

   **Example:** to indent a young man to a shoemaker; to indent a servant

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

**Source sense R2.S7**

7. To begin (a line or lines) at a greater or lesser distance from the margin. See indentation, and indention. Normal indent pushes in a line or paragraph. "Hanging indent" pulls the line out into the margin.

   **Example:** to indent the first line of a paragraph one em

   **Example:** to indent the second paragraph two ems more than the first

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** media; publishing; typography

<a id="r2-s8"></a>

**Source sense R2.S8**

8. To crook or turn; to wind in and out; to zigzag.

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete

<a id="r2-s9"></a>

**Source sense R2.S9**

9. To make an order upon; to draw upon, as for military stores.

   **Usage:** India; Singapore; dated elsewhere

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war
