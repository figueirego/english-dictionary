### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A word in a language that bears a deceptive resemblance to a word in another language but in fact has a different meaning.

   **Example:** A word and its false friend may well be etymologically related&#58; in such cases semantic shifts have made them drift apart.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; lexicography; linguistics; sciences; translation-studies

   **Source synonyms:** faux ami; false cousin

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
