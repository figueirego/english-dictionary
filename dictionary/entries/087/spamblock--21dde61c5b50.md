**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Text inserted into an email address in order to make it invalid, though still readable to a human being. For example, in "jsmith-\[AT\]-example-\[DOT\]-com", the "@" and "." symbols have been replaced with literal text to prevent automated spambots from finding the email address while still leaving it readable.
