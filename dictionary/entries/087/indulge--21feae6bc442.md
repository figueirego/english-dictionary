### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To yield to a temptation or desire.

   **Usage:** intransitive; often

   **Example:** He looked at the chocolate but didn't indulge.

   **Example:** I indulged in drinking on the weekend.

   **Source context (verbatim):** followed by "in"

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To satisfy the wishes or whims of.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Grandma indulges the kids with sweets.

   **Example:** I love to indulge myself with beautiful clothes.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To give way to (a habit or temptation); to not oppose or restrain.

   **Example:** to indulge sloth, pride, selfishness, or inclinations

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To grant an extension to the deadline of a payment.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To grant as by favour; to bestow in concession, or in compliance with a wish or request.
