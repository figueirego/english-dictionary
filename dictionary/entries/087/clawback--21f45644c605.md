## Noun

### Forms

- clawbacks — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A rule that permits a party to take back evidentiary materials that were mistakenly turned over to the other party but to which the other party would not have been entitled.

   **Usage:** US; countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

   **Source context (verbatim):** of evidence

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Money that a party is entitled to keep under one tax provision but is taken by another tax provision.

   **Usage:** US; countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** economics; government; law; sciences; taxation

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any recovery of a performance-related payment based on discovery that the performance was not genuine.

   **Usage:** US; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The airline got a clawback provision in the event of failure of the engines to meet fuel-consumption targets.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A flatterer or sycophant.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable
