### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To communicate with one's voice, to say words out loud.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** I was so surprised I couldn't speak.

   **Example:** You're speaking too fast.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To have a conversation.

   **Usage:** intransitive; reciprocal

   **Example:** It's been ages since we've spoken.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To communicate or converse by some means other than orally, such as writing or facial expressions.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** He spoke of it in his diary.

   **Example:** Speak to me only with your eyes.

   **Example:** Actions speak louder than words.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To deliver a message to a group; to deliver a speech.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** This evening I shall speak on the topic of correct English usage.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To be able to communicate in a language.

   **Usage:** stative; transitive

   **Example:** He speaks Mandarin fluently.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To be able to communicate in a language.
   1. To be able to communicate in the manner of specialists in a field.

      **Usage:** broadly; stative; transitive

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To utter.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I was so surprised that I couldn't speak a word.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To communicate (some fact or feeling); to bespeak, to indicate.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To understand (as though it were a language).

   **Usage:** humorous; informal; sometimes; transitive

   **Example:** Sorry, I don't speak idiot.

   **Example:** So you can program in C. But do you speak C++?

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. To produce a sound; to sound.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. Of a bird, to be able to vocally reproduce words or phrases from a human language.

<a id="r1-s12"></a>

**Source sense R1.S12**

12. To address; to accost; to speak to.

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive
