### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Able to be walked; suitable for pedestrians.

   **Example:** This route is no longer walkable since the bush and the vines grew over it.

   **Example:** Cities were generally much more walkable before the mid-20th century.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Short enough or close enough to be accessible by walking.

   **Example:** Is the train station walkable from here?
