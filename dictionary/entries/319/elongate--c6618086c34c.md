### Senses

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To make (something) long or longer, for example, by pulling or stretching; to make (something) elongated; to extend, to lengthen.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** draw out; prolong; stretch; draw out; elongate; enlengthen; extend; lengthen; produce; prolong; protract; stretch

   **Source antonyms:** abbreviate; abridge; chop down; chop off; cut short; compress; condense; curt; curtail; lop off; make shorter; pare; prune; reduce in length; shorten; trim; truncate; saw off

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Followed by from&#58; to move to or place (something) at a distance from another thing; to remove.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To become long or longer, for example, by being pulled or stretched; to become elongated.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. Of a plant part&#58; to grow long; also, to have a long and slender or tapering form.

   **Usage:** intransitive
