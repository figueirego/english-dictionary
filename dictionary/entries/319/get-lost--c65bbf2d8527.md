### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To lose one's way.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Don't you have a map? How did we get lost?

   **Example:** I got lost in his reasoning.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To go missing; to be in a location unknown to someone who is looking for it.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** My keys got lost while I was out today.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of an object

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To be absent, to seem to be absent.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The violins get lost with the rest of the music.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To exit from the scene.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive; usually in imperative and reported speech

   **Example:** I don't want to have to tell you again&#58; Get lost!
