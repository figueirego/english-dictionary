## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A ghost town in the Cassiar Mountains, Cassiar Land District, Northern Interior, Interior, Mainland, British Columbia, Canada.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A former provincial electoral district in British Columbia, Canada.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A historical region in northwest British Columbia, Canada, site of an 1870's gold rush; in full, Cassiar Country.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Ellipsis of the Cassiar Mountains&#58; a mountain group in the Cassiar Land District, in northwest British Columbia, Canada, named after the district.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; ellipsis

   **Source synonyms:** Cassiars; Cassiar Ranges

   **Alternative form of:** the Cassiar Mountains

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Ellipsis of the Cassiar Land District&#58; a cadastral division in northwest British Columbia, Canada, named after the historic Cassiar Country region.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; ellipsis

   **Source synonyms:** Cassiar County; Cassiar District; Cassiar Country

   **Alternative form of:** the Cassiar Land District

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A river in the Cassiar Land District, in northwest British Columbia, Canada; in full, Cassiar River.
