**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To work together with others to achieve a common goal.

   **Example:** Wikipedia is a website where anyone can collaborate.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To voluntarily cooperate treasonably, as with an enemy occupation force in one's country.

   **Example:** If you collaborate with the occupying forces, you will be shot.
