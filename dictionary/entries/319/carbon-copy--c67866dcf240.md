## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

- carbon-copy — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A copy produced in an alternated stack of ordinary sheets of paper and carbon papers. The pressure applied on the top sheet (by a pen or typewriter) causes every carbon paper to release its carbon cover, thus reproducing the writing on the subjacent layers of paper.

   **Example:** In preparing the contract, we'll make a carbon copy for our records.

   **Source synonyms:** cc (abbreviation)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A duplicate or lookalike.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** This new home is simply a carbon copy of the one down the street.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A duplicate copy of an email.

   **Usage:** Internet
