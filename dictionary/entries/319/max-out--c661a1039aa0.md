### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To reach the limit of one's capabilities.

   **Usage:** informal; intransitive

   **Example:** In the bench press, he has maxed out at 110 kg.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To reach the limit of one's capabilities.
   1. To reach a maximum or a point at which no more growth or improvement is possible.

      **Usage:** informal; intransitive

      **Example:** This machine maxes out at a 110 kW output.

      **Source synonyms:** top out

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To reach the limit of one's capabilities.
   1. To use (something) to the limit of its capabilities.

      **Usage:** informal; intransitive; transitive

      **Example:** to max out one's credit card(s)

      **Example:** I've spent all afternoon shopping and maxed out my credit card.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To reach the limit of one's capabilities.
   1. To reach the end of a complete sentence with no early parole, time off for good behavior, etc.

      **Usage:** US; informal; intransitive; slang

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To relax.

   **Usage:** US; slang
