### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To artificially maintain an outward sign of normality; to maintain an appearance of wealth, comfort, or propriety.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** For my father's sake, I thought I must keep up appearances, but the food stuck in my throat, and I could not swallow another mouthful.

   **Example:** With tact and management it would be possible to partially satisfy creditors, and keep up appearances for six months more.

   **Source synonyms:** maintain appearances; save appearances
