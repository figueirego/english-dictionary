### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Something that is false; an untrue assertion.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** The belief that the world is flat is a falsity.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The characteristic of being untrue.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** The falsity of that statement is easily proven.
