### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To consider questionable or unlikely; to hesitate to believe; to lack confidence in; to question.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** He doubted that was really what you meant.

   **Example:** I had no wish to go, though I doubt if they would have noticed me even if I had.

   **Source synonyms:** distrust; mistrust

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

2. To harbour suspicion about; suspect.

   **Usage:** transitive; archaic outside Scotland

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

3. To anticipate with dread or fear; to apprehend.

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

## Noun · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- enPR: dout — source labels: General-American; Received-Pronunciation
- IPA: /daʊt/ — source labels: General-American; Received-Pronunciation
- IPA: /dʌʊt/ — source labels: Canada

### Forms

- doubts — plural
- dout — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Disbelief or uncertainty (about something); (countable) a particular instance of such disbelief or uncertainty.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** There was some doubt as to who the child's real father was.

   **Example:** I have doubts about how to convert this code to JavaScript.

   **Source synonyms:** incertitude (formal)

   **Source antonyms:** belief; confidence; faith

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. A point of uncertainty, especially a yes/no or a multiple-choice question

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete outside of India

   **Example:** Ma'am, I have a doubt about today's lecture

   **Source synonyms:** disbelief

   **Source antonyms:** belief; tenet

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. a question (that one has)

   **Usage:** countable; non-native speakers' English
