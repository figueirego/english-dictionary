### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any member of the species Linnaea borealis of woodland subshrubs with opposite evergreen rounded oval leaves and pendulous pink flowers that occur in pairs.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any member of the species Dyschoriste oblongifolia of dicotyledonous flowering plants of the family Acanthaceae, native to coastal plains regions of the United States.
