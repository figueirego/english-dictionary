### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Jumping, or passing, from one thing or subject to another, without order, planning, or rational connection; lacking logical sequence.

   **Example:** He wandered round, cleaning up in a desultory way.

   **Example:** I teach a class of desultory minds.

   **Example:** subject to desultory fire from the enemy

   **Source synonyms:** aimless; chaotic; disconnected; erratic; haphazard; meandering; quodlibetic; quodlibetical; random; rambling; scattered; unmethodical; unsystematic; wandering

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Out of course; by the way; not connected with the subject.

   **Example:** I made a desultory remark while I was talking to my friend.

   **Example:** She made a desultory attempt at conversation.

   **Source synonyms:** extraneous; incidental; irrelevant; nongermane; spurious; stray; unconnected; ungermane; unrelated

   **Source antonyms:** apropos; germane; relevant

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Disappointing in performance or progress.

   **Source synonyms:** half-assed; halfhearted

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Leaping, skipping or flitting about, generally in a random or unsteady manner.

   **Usage:** obsolete
