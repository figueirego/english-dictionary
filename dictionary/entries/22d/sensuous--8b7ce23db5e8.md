### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Appealing to the senses, or to sensual gratification.

   **Example:** Although we rarely see Casanova himself on our tour of his sensuous world, we feel his presence as we look at paintings, sculpture, snuff boxes, embroidered vests, silk dresses, silver candy dishes, etc.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of or relating to the senses; sensory.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

   **Example:** I consider myself a sensuous lover.
