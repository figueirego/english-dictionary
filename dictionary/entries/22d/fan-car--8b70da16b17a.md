### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A car with a fan to create a vacuum underneath the car, increasing downforce and better sticking the car to the driving surface, increasing traction.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** automotive; hobbies; lifestyle; motor-racing; racing; sports; transport; vehicles
