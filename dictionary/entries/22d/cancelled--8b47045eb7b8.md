### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. No longer planned or scheduled.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The cancelled show would have drawn some 5,000 fans.

   **Source synonyms:** off

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Marked over the stamp, to show that the stamp has been used.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Most of the cancelled specimens were somewhat less valuable philatelically.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a mail item

**Source sense R2.S3**
