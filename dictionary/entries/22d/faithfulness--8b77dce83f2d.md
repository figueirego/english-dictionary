## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The state of being faithful.
   1. The state or act of keeping faith.
      1. Constancy of support, alliance, and respect.

         **Usage:** countable; uncountable

         **Example:** His faithfulness to his wife and family was admired.

         **Source synonyms:** allegiance; loyalty; fidelity; devotion

         **Source antonyms:** faithlessness; disloyalty; infidelity

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The state of being faithful.
   1. The state or act of keeping faith.
      1. Constancy of belief.

         **Usage:** countable; uncountable

         **Example:** His faithfulness kept him going through the darkest times.

         **Source synonyms:** fidelity

         **Source antonyms:** faithlessness; infidelity

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The state of being faithful.
   1. Consistency; exactness in copying or following.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Example:** faithfulness to the original
