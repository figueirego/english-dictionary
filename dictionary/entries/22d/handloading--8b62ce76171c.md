## Noun · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The loading of cartridges for firearms when it is done by hand and (especially) at home (in semi-homemade manner)&#58; assembling them from the individual components (e.g., casing, primer, gunpowder or other charge, and shot or bullet).

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** With handloading of their practice rounds, they can save money, especially in light of how expensive the store-bought cartridges can be these days.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The loading of a firearm, usually a pistol or rifle, by hand.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source antonyms:** autoloading
