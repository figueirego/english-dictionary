## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A speculation as to what might have happened if something else had happened earlier.

   **Usage:** informal

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A hypothetical case or situation, usually evoked by a sense of anxiety or uncertainty.

   **Usage:** informal
