### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The process of becoming generic; an instance of it.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** genericized trademark; abstraction; despecification; generalization; genericization; universalization

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. With respect to a trademark, the act or process of becoming so widely identified as a reference for a type of product as to cease representing a particular brand or manufacturer of that type of product.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

   **Source synonyms:** genericide; trademark erosion

<a id="r1-s3"></a>
