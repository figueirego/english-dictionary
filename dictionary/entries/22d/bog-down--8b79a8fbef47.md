### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To become stuck (as if in a bog) and unable to progress.

   **Usage:** intransitive; "get"-passive

   **Example:** We've gotten bogged down in trifling details.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** transitive
