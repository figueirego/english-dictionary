### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An occasional tendinous connection between the flexor pollicis longus and the flexor digitorum profundus of the index finger and/or middle finger, found in around 21% of the population.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** Linburg-Comstock syndrome
