### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A regularly-shaped slab of clay or other material, affixed to cover or decorate a surface, as in a roof-tile, glazed tile, stove tile, carpet tile, etc.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A rectangular graphic.

   **Example:** Each tile within the map consists of 256 × 256 pixels.

   **Example:** Sprites and tiles that are hidden in the prototype ROM file can be recovered.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of various flat cuboid playing pieces used in certain games, such as dominoes, Scrabble, or mahjong.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A stiff hat.

   **Usage:** dated; informal

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A Lego piece that is 1/3 the height of a brick, and is smooth without studs on top.

   **Usage:** Lego building
