## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The principle of succession in royal families, especially among the Eastern Roman emperors, by which a younger son, if born after the accession of his father to the throne, was preferred to an elder son who was born prior to it.

   **Usage:** rare; uncountable
