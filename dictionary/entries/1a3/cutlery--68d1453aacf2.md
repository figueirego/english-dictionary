<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Sharp cutting utensils.

   **Usage:** US; uncountable; usually

   **Example:** Proper maintenance of your cutlery will keep the blades from dulling.

2. Eating or serving utensils such as knives, forks, and spoons.

   **Usage:** UK; uncountable; usually
