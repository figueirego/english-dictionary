### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A stop made during an automobile race at the pit to refuel and perform other periodic maintenance on the vehicle.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; motor-racing; racing; sports

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A brief stop for fuel, rest or refreshment during an automobile journey.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** I had to make a couple of pit stops along the way from Cadiz to Santiago de Compostela to give myself a break.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A stop during any pedestrian or automobile travel for any utilitarian personal purpose, especially use of a bathroom.

   **Usage:** informal

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A place that offers food, beverages, and other goods and services for those stopping on an automobile journey.

   **Usage:** informal

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A utilitarian stop on a figurative journey.

   **Usage:** figuratively; informal
