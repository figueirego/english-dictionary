### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A surgical procedure in which the gallbladder is connected to the stomach to allow bile to drain into the stomach, typically used as a bypass option in cases of biliary obstruction when standard drainage routes are not feasible.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences; surgery
