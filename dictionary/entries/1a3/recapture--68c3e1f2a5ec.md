### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of capturing again.

   **Example:** The recapture of the escaped prisoner made the news.

   **Source synonyms:** recatch

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To capture something for a second or subsequent time, especially after a loss.

   **Example:** The warden hoped to recapture the escaped prisoners before they reached the town.

   **Example:** New engine designs permit the vehicle to recapture the kinetic energy lost through braking.
