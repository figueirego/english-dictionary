### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make someone believe, or feel sure about something, especially by using logic, argument or evidence.

   **Example:** I wouldn't have or do something, unless I'm convinced that it's good.

   **Example:** He convinced me I had talent, and persuaded me to study music.

   **Source synonyms:** assure; convert; [convict](../0a8/convict--2a15ed74fb2c.md); satisfy

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To persuade.

   **Source synonyms:** win over; bring round; [convict](../0a8/convict--2a15ed74fb2c.md); convince; draw; gain over (US); persuade; pimp (US; slang); prevail upon (idiomatic); procure; push; suade (obsolete); talk around; talk over; wash with; win over (idiomatic)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To overcome, conquer, vanquish.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** beat; bring down; convince (obsolete); chew up; eat someone for breakfast (slang); eat someone's lunch (US; slang); flog (Australia; New-Zealand); humble; kick ass; malavogue (Ireland; dated); murdelize; outfox; outguess; outrun; outsmart; outwit; overcome; overpower; overwin; own; pulverise; pwn; rape (offensive; slang); reduce; spank; steal the show; subdue; subvert; surmount; thrash; trounce; vanquish

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To behave believably in a role; to make someone perceive oneself as the character being portrayed.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** acting; broadcasting; entertainment; film; lifestyle; media; television; theater

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To confute; to prove wrong.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** debunk; disprove; rebut; refute

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To prove guilty; to convict.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive
