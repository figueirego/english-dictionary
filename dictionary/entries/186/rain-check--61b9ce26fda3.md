### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A reissue, at no extra charge, of a ticket for a baseball game or other outdoor event postponed or interrupted because of rain.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An agreement to honor a current offer after its normal expiration.
   1. A promise or voucher from a merchant to a customer&#58;
      1. A (special) price guarantee for a sold-out item or temporarily unavailable service.

         **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An agreement to honor a current offer after its normal expiration.
   1. A promise or voucher from a merchant to a customer&#58;
      1. The promise to make good on any experience that the customer deems unsatisfactory.

         **Usage:** figuratively; uncommon

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An agreement to honor a current offer after its normal expiration.
   1. In social interactions, a deferred invitation, or deferred acceptance of an invitation.

      **Usage:** broadly; figuratively

      **Example:** I can't go with you to the museum this Saturday, but can I take a rain check and go some other day?
