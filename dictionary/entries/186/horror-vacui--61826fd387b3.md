### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The Aristotelian principle that there are no vacuums in nature.

   **Usage:** historical; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The abhorrence of a vacuum; the general tendency to dislike empty space.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. In particular, the tendency of an artist (or of art) to fill an entire surface with detail, leaving no blank space.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** The Where's Wally? series exhibits horror vacui.
