## Noun

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The thought process of considering, of taking multiple or specified factors into account (with of being the main corresponding adposition).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** After much consideration, I have decided to stay.

   **Example:** Consideration of environmental effects is needed when choosing material.

   **Source synonyms:** deliberation; thought

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Something considered as a reason or ground for a (possible) decision.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** factor; motive; reason

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The tendency to consider others and make allowances for their needs or desires.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** You showed remarkable consideration in giving up your place for your friend.

   **Example:** Will you noisy children show some consideration and stop your infernal screaming? I'm trying to study!

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A payment or other recompense for something done.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Sure I'll move my car, but only for a consideration.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A matter of inducement for something promised; something valuable given as recompense for a promise, which causes the promise to become binding as a contract.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** In consideration of the performance of B's obligations hereunder, A hereby grants to B's personal license.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Importance or regard; a claim to notice or attention.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Money was of no consideration there.
