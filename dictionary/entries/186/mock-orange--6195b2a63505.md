### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of many shrubs of the genus Philadelphus having white, fragrant flowers, for which they are cultivated outside their natural range
   1. especially, Philadelphus coronarius (sweet mock orange), widely cultivated

      **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of many shrubs of the genus Philadelphus having white, fragrant flowers, for which they are cultivated outside their natural range
   1. Philadelphus lewisii (Lewis' mock-orange), of western North America

      **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of several similar but not closely related plants.
   1. Bursaria spinosa (Australian blackthorn)

      **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

   1. Maclura pomifera (Osage orange), of North America

      **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

      **Usage:** uncountable
