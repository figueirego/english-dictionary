### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Not flattering, attractive or appropriate.

   **Example:** She wore a rather unbecoming hairstyle.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Not in keeping with the expected standards of one's position.

   **Example:** He was accused of conduct unbecoming an officer and a gentleman.
