### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A very short period of time; a moment.

   **Example:** She paused for only an instant, which was just enough time for John to change the subject.

   **Source synonyms:** eyeblink; flash; jiffy; bat of an eye (idiomatic); bit; blink of an eye (idiomatic); crack (British; dated; slang); eyeblink (idiomatic); flash; [glimpse](../18b/glimpse--62c4ea25a122.md) (rare); half a mo; instant; jiff; jiffy; jot (obsolete); minute; mo; moment; New York minute; New York second; no time (idiomatic); sec; second; span; split-second; spurt; stound (archaic; dialectal); tick; trice; twink; twinkle; twinkling; while (Philippines)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A single, usually precise, point in time.

   **Example:** The instant the alarm went off, he fled the building.

   **Example:** Liftoff occurs the instant the bolts are released.

   **Source synonyms:** minute; moment

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A beverage or food which has been pre-processed to reduce preparation time, especially instant coffee.
