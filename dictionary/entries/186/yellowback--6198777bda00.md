### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any member of species Sciades parkeri of sea catfish, having yellow coloration on the upper part of its body.

   **Usage:** obsolete; rare

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A cheap novel with a yellow cover; any cheaply reproduced novel, typically with sensational content.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
