### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A pipe that carries fluid which is being drained.
   1. A vertical pipe carrying water from the roof gutter down the side of a building; downspout.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A pipe that carries fluid which is being drained.
   1. A conduit for carrying rainwater or flood water.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A pipe that carries fluid which is being drained.
   1. A pipe that carries wastewater from a bathtub, shower, sink, etc.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A pipe that carries fluid which is being drained.
   1. A pipe that is part of a device or appliance for carrying away waste fluid.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The type of pipe that is used to construct a drainpipe.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A type of form-fitting trousers with highly tapered legs.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
