### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To continue (at a task) briskly or promptly.

   **Usage:** informal; intransitive

   **Example:** Naked Twister will have to wait. I've got to crack on with my essay.

   **Source synonyms:** get on

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To continue apace.

   **Usage:** informal; intransitive

   **Example:** The project is really cracking on.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To put on.

   **Usage:** dated; transitive

   **Example:** to crack on more sail, or more steam

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To crack down on or arrest (someone).

   **Usage:** slang; transitive

   **Example:** They need to crack on those drug dealers.
