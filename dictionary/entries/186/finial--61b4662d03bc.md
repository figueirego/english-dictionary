### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Especially in Gothic architecture&#58; an ornament, often in the form of a bunch or knot of foliage, on the peak of the gable of a roof, a pediment, a pinnacle, etc.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** architecture

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any decorative fitting on the corner, end, or top of an object such as a canopy, a fencepost, a flagpole, a curtain rod, or the newel post of a staircase.

   **Usage:** broadly
