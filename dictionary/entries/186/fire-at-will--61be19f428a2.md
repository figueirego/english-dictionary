## Phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Fire when ready. As a command, it orders troops to use weapons at their discretion and choose their own targets, allowing the individual soldier a greater freedom of timing the shot with target movement and similar.

   **Example:** Fire at will! \[imperative\]

   **Example:** Once we're in the woods, we plan to fire at will. \[nonimperative\]

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war
