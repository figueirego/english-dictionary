### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The state of being dark; lack of light; the absolute or comparative absence of light.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** The darkness of the room made it difficult to see.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The state or quality of reflecting little light, of tending to a blackish or brownish color.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** The darkness of her skin betrayed her Mediterranean heritage.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any space that such colour pervades.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Out of the darkness came some flickers of light.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Gloom; gloominess; depression.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The product of being dark.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Lack of understanding or compassion; spiritual or mental blindness.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Secrecy; concealment.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Lack of knowledge; obscurity or meaning or intelligibility; the unknown.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. Nothingness, vanity, emptiness.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. Hell.

   **Usage:** uncountable
