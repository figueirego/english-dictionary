### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A region directly above a VOR navigation station where the aircraft's navigation receiver experiences signal ambiguity.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** aeronautics; aerospace; aviation; business; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences
