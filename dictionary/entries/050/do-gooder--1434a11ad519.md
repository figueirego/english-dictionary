### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who advocates a certain course of action, often of political or social concern, and often with the naive conviction of their own moral superiority.

   **Usage:** derogatory

   **Example:** Prohibition in the United States was an unsuccessful attempt by do-gooders to save people from the dangers of alcohol, whether they wanted to be saved or not.
