### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A heavy, waterproof sheet of material, often cloth or plastic sheet, used as a cover or blanket (often as weatherproofing, or to keep loose cargo from blowing off a lorry).

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** Throw a tarpaulin over that woodpile before it gets wet.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Canvas waterproofed with tar, used as a cover.

   **Usage:** archaic; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A hat made of, or covered with, painted or tarred cloth, worn by sailors and others.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** rain hat

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A sailor.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; slang

   **Source synonyms:** tar
