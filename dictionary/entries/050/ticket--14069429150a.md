## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈtɪk.ɪt/ — source labels: General-American; Received-Pronunciation
- IPA: /ˈtɪk.ət/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Forms

- tickets — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A small document that acts as proof of something, often thereby granting the holder some ability.
   1. A pass entitling the holder to admission to a show, concert, sporting event, etc.

      **Example:** I've got two tickets for the match on Saturday; want to come?

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A small document that acts as proof of something, often thereby granting the holder some ability.
   1. A pass entitling the holder to board a train, a bus, a plane, or other means of transportation.

      **Example:** train ticket bus ticket plane ticket

      **Example:** You must show your ticket to the conductor.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A small document that acts as proof of something, often thereby granting the holder some ability.
   1. A permit to operate a machine on a construction site.

      **Source synonyms:** license

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A small document that acts as proof of something, often thereby granting the holder some ability.
   1. A certificate or token of a share in a lottery or other scheme for distributing money, goods, etc.

      **Example:** lottery ticket raffle ticket

      **Example:** If I'd used my usual numbers, it would've been a winning ticket! Unlucky!

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A small document that acts as proof of something, often thereby granting the holder some ability.
   1. A certificate of qualification as a ship's master, pilot, or other crew member.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A small document that acts as proof of something, often thereby granting the holder some ability.
   1. A solution to a problem; something that is needed in order to do something.

      **Usage:** figuratively

      **Example:** That's the ticket.

      **Example:** I saw my first bike as my ticket to freedom.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A citation for a traffic violation.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A service request, used to track complaints or requests that an issue be handled.

   **Usage:** usually technical support

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A list of candidates for an election, or a particular theme to a candidate's manifesto.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** Joe has joined the party's ticket for the county elections.

   **Example:** Joe will be running on an anti-crime ticket.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; politics

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. A small note or notice.

   **Usage:** dated

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. A tradesman's bill or account (hence the phrase on ticket and eventually on tick).

   **Usage:** dated

<a id="r1-s12"></a>

**Source sense R1.S12**

12. A label affixed to goods to show their price or description.

<a id="r1-s13"></a>

**Source sense R1.S13**

13. A visiting card.

   **Usage:** dated

<a id="r1-s14"></a>

**Source sense R1.S14**
