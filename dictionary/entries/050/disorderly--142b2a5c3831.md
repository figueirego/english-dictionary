## Adjective · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /dɪsˈɔː.də.li/ — source labels: UK

### Forms

- more disorderly — comparative
- most disorderly — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Not in order; marked by disorder or disarray.

   **Example:** The books and papers are in a disorderly state.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Not acting in an orderly way, as the functions of the body or mind.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Not complying with the restraints of order and law; unruly; lawless.

   **Example:** disorderly people; disorderly assemblies

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Offensive to good morals and public decency.

   **Example:** a disorderly house

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law
