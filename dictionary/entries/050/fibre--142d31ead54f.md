### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A single piece of a given material, elongated and roughly round in cross-section, often twisted with other fibres to form thread.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** The microscope showed several different fibres stuck to the sole of the shoe.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Material in the form of fibres.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** The cloth was made from strange, somewhat rough fibre.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Dietary fibre.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Fresh vegetables are a good source of fibre.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Moral strength and resolve.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The ordeal was a test of everyone’s fibre.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The preimage of a given point in the range of a map.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Under this map, any two values in the fibre of a given point on the circle differ by 2π.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences
