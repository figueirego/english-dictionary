### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see go, in.

   **Example:** It's getting cold. Let's go in.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To become obscured by clouds.

   **Usage:** moon or stars

   **Example:** It's chilly now the sun's gone in.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of the sun

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To begin or become involved in some activity.

   **Example:** I wish I'd known that going in.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To share in part of a project's or plan's duties or costs.

   **Example:** If you guys are fixing up that boat together to share it, I'll go in too.

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To become understood or accepted.

   **Example:** You have to tell him a hundred times if you want it to go in.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a fact or concept

   **Source synonyms:** sink in

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To perform invasive surgery.
