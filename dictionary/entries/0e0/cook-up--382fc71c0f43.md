### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To prepare (food or chemical substances) by cooking or heating.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Let me cook up some eggs and bacon before you go.

   **Example:** To cook up a batch of biodiesel, scientists stir together methanol and vegetable fat.

   **Example:** Owsley Stanley started cooking up those famous purple LSD tabs.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To manufacture; to invent (something, often a deceit or falsehood); to counterfeit (something).

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** The financiers cooked up an instrument called a collateralized debt obligation.

   **Example:** He really cooked up a good one this time, something about an airline disaster.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To prepare (heroin, opium, crack, or meth) by heating or otherwise manufacturing.

   **Usage:** slang

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To manufacture a significant amount of illegal drugs (LSD, methamphetamine, etc.).

   **Usage:** slang

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see cook, up.
