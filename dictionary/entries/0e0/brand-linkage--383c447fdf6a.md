### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The degree to which the consumer understands a commercial's message is for a specific brand of product rather than the product category.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** If viewers remember a funny commercial was for tissues instead of the brand Puffs, then the ad has poor brand linkage.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** advertising; business; marketing
