### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The section between the throat and shank of the mouthpiece of a brass instrument.

   **Example:** More conical backbores give a richer tone, while more cylindrical ones give a brighter, more projected tone.
