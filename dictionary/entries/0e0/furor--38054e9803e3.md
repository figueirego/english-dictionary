### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A general uproar or commotion.

   **Usage:** US; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Violent anger or frenzy.

   **Usage:** US; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The verdict of not guilty created a true furor in the courtroom.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A state of intense excitement.

   **Usage:** US; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The story of the princess's affair caused a furor among journalists.
