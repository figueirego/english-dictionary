## Noun · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of several children's games played with small balls (made of marble or more commonly glass).

   **Usage:** plural; plural-only

   **Example:** Egyptian boys played marbles before the days of Moses.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Sanity.

   **Usage:** figuratively; plural; plural-only; usually in a limited number of expressions

   **Example:** Grandpa's lost his marbles, but at least he still recognizes us.

   **Example:** Granny's still got all her marbles.

   **Example:** His lack of marbles is becoming ever more apparent.

   **Source synonyms:** buttons; buttons (colloquial); lucidity; lucidness; marbles (colloquial); mind; saneness; sanity
