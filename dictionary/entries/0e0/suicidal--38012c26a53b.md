### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Pertaining to suicide.

   **Example:** He is an authority on the precursors of suicidal behavior.

   **Source antonyms:** nonsuicidal

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of a person, likely to commit, or to attempt to commit, suicide.

   **Example:** After losing his job, his wife, and his leg in a single week, he became suicidal.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of a voluntarily-chosen course of action, highly likely to certain to result in the deaths of the participants; dangerous or reckless to such a degree as to be tantamount to suicide for those taking part.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Extremely reckless.

   **Usage:** excessive; informal

   **Example:** His driving habits are utterly suicidal.
