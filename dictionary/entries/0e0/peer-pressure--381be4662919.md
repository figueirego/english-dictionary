### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Inducement or influence by one's peers.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** She vowed to give up drinking during the exam period, but eventually succumbed to peer pressure and was out drinking with her friends within a week of the resolution.
