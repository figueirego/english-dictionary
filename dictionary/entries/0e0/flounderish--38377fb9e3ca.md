### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. that flounders; that moves with awkward struggles

   **Example:** The readers were expecting things to be a little more direct, and so they're probably feeling a bit flounderish.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. (of a speech) moving from topic to topic, not focused

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** I had a big flounderish diatribe written up, but this isn't the place for it.
