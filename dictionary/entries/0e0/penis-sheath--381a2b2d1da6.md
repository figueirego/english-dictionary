### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A chiefly ceremonial item of clothing that covers the penis; sometimes only such items that do not cover the scrotum (as distinct from a phallocrypt); usually form-fitting or otherwise shaped to resemble the penis.

   **Source synonyms:** phallocrypt
