### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A dog which serves people by helping them with activities of daily living, with search and rescue, or with other (non-draft and nonfarming) tasks.
   1. A dog which assists the work of military or law enforcement officers.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; law-enforcement; military; politics; war

      **Source synonyms:** assistance dog; aid dog

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A dog which serves people by helping them with activities of daily living, with search and rescue, or with other (non-draft and nonfarming) tasks.
   1. A dog which assists a person who has a physical disability or a mental disability such as post-traumatic stress disorder.

      **Usage:** Canada; US

      **Source synonyms:** assistance dog; aid dog; aid dog; assistance dog; helper dog
