## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˌɹɛɡjʊˈleɪʃən/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Forms

- regulations — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of regulating or the condition of being regulated.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A law or administrative rule, issued by an organization, used to guide or prescribe the conduct of members of that organization.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** Army regulations state a soldier AWOL over 30 days is a deserter.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A type of law made by the executive branch of a government, usually as authorized by a statute made by the legislative branch giving the executive the authority to do so.

   **Usage:** countable; in-plural; often; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A type of law made by the executive branch of a government, usually as authorized by a statute made by the legislative branch giving the executive the authority to do so.
   1. A numbered provision within such kind of legislation.

      **Usage:** countable; in-plural; often; singular

      **Example:** Regulation 18B of the Defence (General) Regulations 1939 (S.R. &amp; O. 1939/927) authorized the British government to intern suspected spies and collaborators during World War II.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A form of legislative act which is self-effecting, and requires no further intervention by the Member States to become law.

   **Usage:** European-Union; countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Mechanism controlling DNA transcription.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; genetics; medicine; natural-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences
