### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To write at full length or in expanded form.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Don't use abbreviations; write out words!

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To write or fill out (a ticket or citation).

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To exclude from a narrative or history.

   **Usage:** broadly; transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To remove (a character in a fictional series such as a TV show) by writing the script so as to explain their disappearance (through death, moving away, etc.).

   **Usage:** broadly; transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** broadcasting; media; radio; television

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To exhaust one's mental capacity by too much writing.

   **Usage:** intransitive
