## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A level of analysis that concerns large-scale and highly multivariable phenomena or factors.

   **Example:** at the macrolevel

   **Example:** on the macrolevel

   **Example:** This study attempts to describe what is happening at the macrolevel and how it affects individual cases.

   **Source antonyms:** microlevel
