### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A device that charges or recharges.

   **Example:** Put the batteries in the charger overnight so we can use them tomorrow.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One who charges.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A large horse trained for battle and used by the cavalry.

   **Usage:** historical

   **Example:** The knight rode a white charger.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A large platter.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A large decorative plate, sometimes used under dinner plates or other savoury-dish vessels in a multi-course meal.

   **Example:** The fancy restaurant used a white porcelain charger when serving.

   **Source synonyms:** service plate; underplate

<a id="r1-s6"></a>
