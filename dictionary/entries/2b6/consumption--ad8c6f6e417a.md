### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of eating, drinking or using.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** The consumption of snails as food is more common in France than in England.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The amount consumed.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** gross national consumption

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The act of consuming or destroying.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** The fire's consumption of the forest caused ecological changes.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The wasting away of the human body through disease.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; pathology; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Pulmonary tuberculosis and other diseases that cause wasting away, lung infection, etc.

   **Usage:** dated; uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; pathology; sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

   **Usage:** dated; euphemistic; uncountable; usually
