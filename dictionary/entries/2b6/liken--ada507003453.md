### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Followed by to or (archaic) unto&#58; to regard or state that (someone or something) is like another person or thing; to compare.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The physics teacher likened the effect of mass on space to an indentation in a sheet of rubber.

   **Source antonyms:** contrast; unliken

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Chiefly followed by to&#58; to make (oneself, someone, or something) resemble another person or thing.

   **Usage:** also; rare; reflexive; transitive

   **Source antonyms:** unliken

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To represent or symbolize (something).

   **Usage:** rare; transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Followed by to&#58; to be like or resemble; also, to become like.

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete
