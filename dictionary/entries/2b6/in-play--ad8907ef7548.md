## Prep\_phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. As a joke.

   **Example:** You mustn't take what I said so seriously, it was only said in play.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In operation, active, currently valid, at play, at work.

   **Example:** It's hard to tell which physical forces are in play at this altitude.

   **Example:** This deadly scenario with this hurricane is still in play, so please take precaution.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Available to be played according to the rules of the game in question; within the boundaries of the pitch, within the realm of the possible, etc.

   **Usage:** also; figuratively

   **Example:** now everything is in play

   **Example:** The ball is in play for an average of just seven minutes out of a sixty-minute game.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Capable of being won by more than one party or candidate.

   **Example:** Though the state has not changed hands in 20 years, the latest polls suggest it may now be in play.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; politics

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

   **Example:** Despite the best efforts of the executive committee, it seems the company is now in play.
