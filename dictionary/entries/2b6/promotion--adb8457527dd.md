### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An advancement in rank or position.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** I'll have to give myself a promotion!

   **Source antonyms:** demotion

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Dissemination of information in order to increase its popularity.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** the promotion of the idea of global warming in schools

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An event intended to increase the reach or image of a product or brand.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The price cut is serving as a promotion of the manufacturer's new beverage varieties.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; marketing

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Transformation of a pawn into a piece (by reaching the opponent's back rank).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** board-games; chess; games

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Forward motion. (Contrast remotion.)

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; entomology; natural-sciences; zoology
