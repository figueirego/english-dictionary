### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To include as a constituent part or functionality.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** They were keen to integrate their new skills into the performance.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To join a group or an environment harmoniously; to make oneself fit in.

   **Usage:** intransitive; reflexive; sometimes; usually

   **Example:** The refugees integrated well into the community.

   **Example:** The eco-friendly building integrates with the forest around it.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To form into one whole; to make entire; to complete; to renew; to restore; to perfect.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To give the sum or total of a varying quantity over an interval such as a period of time or an area.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To subject to the operation of integration; to find the integral of an equation.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To desegregate, as a school or neighborhood.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** President Eisenhower had to call out the National Guard to integrate Little Rock Central High School.

   **Source antonyms:** segregate

**Source sense R1.S7**

   **Usage:** transitive
