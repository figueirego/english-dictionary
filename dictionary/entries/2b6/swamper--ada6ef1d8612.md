### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who lives in a swampy area.

   **Usage:** US

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person who clears a road for lumberers in a forest or swamp.

   **Usage:** US

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

3. Someone or something that swamps or overwhelms.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

   **Usage:** Canada; US; slang

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

   **Usage:** Australia; slang
