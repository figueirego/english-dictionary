### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Intended for or likely to be understood by only a small number of people with a specialized knowledge or interest, or an enlightened inner circle.

   **Example:** The writing in this manual is very esoteric; I’d need a degree in engineering just to understand it!

   **Source synonyms:** arcane; recondite; acroamatic

   **Source antonyms:** exoteric

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Having to do with concepts that are highly theoretical and without obvious practical application; often with mystical or religious connotations.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Confidential; private.

   **Example:** an esoteric purpose

   **Example:** an esoteric meeting

   **Source synonyms:** secretive
