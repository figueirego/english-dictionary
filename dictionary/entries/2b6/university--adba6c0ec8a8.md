### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An institution of higher education that provides facilities for teaching, research, and the conferral of academic degrees across undergraduate, graduate, and often professional levels.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** She's studying mathematics at university.

   **Example:** The only reason why I haven't gone to university is because I can't afford it.
