### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To measure by steps or paces; hence, to divide (a space), or to form a series of marks, by successive measurements, as with dividers.

   **Usage:** separable

   **Example:** He measured the garden by stepping it off.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To avoid a conflict; to back down.

   **Usage:** African-American Vernacular; inseparable; African-American Vernacular; inseparable

   **Example:** You better step off, man...

   **Source antonyms:** step

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see step, off.

   **Usage:** inseparable

   **Example:** He stepped off the train.
