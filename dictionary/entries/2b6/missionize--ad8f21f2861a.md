### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To work as a missionary; to do missionary work.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To do missionary work among (a people) or in (a particular place).

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To teach and to bring under the control of European religion and culture through the mission system, as the Spanish did with Native Americans in their colonies.

   **Usage:** historical; transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To make suitable for a military (or intelligence gathering, etc) mission.

   **Usage:** transitive
