### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To fall asleep from exhaustion or intoxication; to lie down.

   **Usage:** informal; intransitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To flake or be flaky&#58; to prove unreliable; to abandon or desert someone.

   **Usage:** informal; intransitive

   **Example:** John said he could drive me to the airport, but then he flaked out on me, and I had to call a cab at the last minute.
