### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A rare disease caused by the interruption of peripheral or central sensory pathways of the trigeminal nerve, often involving a slowly enlarging uninflamed ulcer and feelings of picking, rubbing, or scratching in the afflicted areas.
