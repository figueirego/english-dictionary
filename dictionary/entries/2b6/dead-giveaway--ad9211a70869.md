### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A giveaway that is especially obvious, especially ruinous, or both, disclosing, usually unintentionally, a fact or an intention.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** That disguise is a dead giveaway! Choose something less obvious.
