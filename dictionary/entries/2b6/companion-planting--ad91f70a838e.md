## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

- companion plantings — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A type of interplanting in which two or more crops are grown in an interspersed or juxtaposed manner so as to enjoy mutual benefit from one another's presence (for example, attraction of pollinators or repulsion of pests).

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** cocropping; coplanting

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An instance of such planting; a set of plants thus created; a single plant so planted.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source synonyms:** companion plants; [companion plant](../0d1/companion-plant--3450a848b497.md)
