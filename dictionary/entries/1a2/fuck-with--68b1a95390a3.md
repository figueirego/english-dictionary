<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To interact with in a careless or inappropriate way.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; slang; vulgar

   **Example:** Don't fuck with that gang! They all carry guns!

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To play with in a lighthearted or joking manner; kid with; play tricks on.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; slang; vulgar

   **Example:** Don't take it serious, man, I'm just fucking with you.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To affect seriously or disturbingly; give someone a hard time; to trouble, harass.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; slang; vulgar

   **Example:** The thought that she might be cheating on me is really fucking with me.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To work or associate with, roll with, have dealings with; (by extension) to like, approve of, enjoy.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; slang; vulgar; especially African-American Vernacular

   **Example:** I fuck with you heavy.
