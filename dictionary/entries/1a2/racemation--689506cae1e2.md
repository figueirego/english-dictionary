## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A cluster or bunch, as of grapes. Also used figuratively.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The cultivation or gathering of clusters of grapes.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The formation of a racemate from a pure enantiomer; racemization.

   **Usage:** archaic; uncountable; usually

   **Example:** racemation of amino acids
