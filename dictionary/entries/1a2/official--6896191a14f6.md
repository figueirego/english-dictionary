### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of or about an office or public trust.

   **Example:** official duties

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Derived from the proper office or officer, or the appropriate authority; made or communicated by authority

   **Example:** an official statement or report

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Approved by authority; authorized.

   **Example:** The Official Strategy Guide

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Approved by authority; authorized.
   1. Dubious but recognized by authorities as the truth or canon.

      **Usage:** Of a statement

      **Example:** Despite these testimonies, "accidental asphyxiation" remains his official cause of death.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Sanctioned by the pharmacopoeia; appointed to be used in medicine; officinal.

   **Example:** an official drug or preparation

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; pharmacology; sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Discharging an office or function.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Relating to an office, especially a subordinate executive officer or attendant.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Relating to an ecclesiastical judge appointed by a bishop, chapter, archdeacon, etc., with charge of the spiritual jurisdiction.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. True, real, beyond doubt.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** Well, it's official&#58; you lost your mind!

<a id="r1-s10"></a>
