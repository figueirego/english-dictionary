### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In a sincere or earnest manner; honestly.

   **Example:** I sincerely hope they make it home safely.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A conventional formula for ending a letter, used when the salutation addresses the person for whom the letter is intended by his or her name.

   **Usage:** US

   **Example:** Please consider the proposal carefully and let me know what you decide. Sincerely, Fred
