### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who confesses faith in Christianity in the face of persecution, but who is not martyred.

   **Example:** Long before Edward I, the English had a King Edward who they considered a martyr and a King Edward who they considered a confessor.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One who confesses to having done something wrong.

   **Source synonyms:** confessant

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A priest who hears confession and then gives absolution.

   **Usage:** Eastern Orthodoxy

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Catholicism; Christianity; Roman-Catholicism

   **Source synonyms:** father confessor

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Someone who acts as listener and helper.

   **Usage:** broadly; figuratively
