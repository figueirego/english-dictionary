## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who confabulates; a companion with whom one chats or confers.

   **Source synonyms:** confabulator

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One who invents elaborate stories, especially false ones misrepresented as if they were true.
