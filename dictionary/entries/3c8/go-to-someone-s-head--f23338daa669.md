### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To strongly affect a person, especially to the detriment of his or her senses or mental faculties.
   1. To cloud one's thinking, as do alcohol and many drugs.

      **Usage:** idiomatic

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To strongly affect a person, especially to the detriment of his or her senses or mental faculties.
   1. To greatly raise one's conception of their social status or accomplishments.

      **Usage:** idiomatic

      **Example:** He caught the biggest fish on record and it really went to his head.
