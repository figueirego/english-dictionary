### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Relating to time, or units of time.

   **Example:** He is 67 in chronological age, but has the mind and body of someone 55.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In order of time from the earliest to the latest.

   **Example:** The live report lists all the events in chronological order.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. In order of time from the earliest to the latest.
   1. Relating to the arrangement of events in the order in which they occur within a story, rather than the order in which they are released.
