### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To manage an area of responsibility in a way which is inept, incompetent, or dishonest.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** She mismanaged my finances and left me bankrupt.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To behave, in a management capacity, in a manner which is inept, incompetent, or dishonest.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The fundamental reason why the company failed is that the CEO and other top managers simply mismanaged.
