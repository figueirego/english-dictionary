### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A windstorm of limited extent, such as a tornado, dust devil, or waterspout, characterized by an inward spiral motion of the air with an upward current in the center; a vortex of air.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person or body of objects or events sweeping violently onward.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** Once he got that new scooter he turned into a whirlwind and damaged all the flowers.

   **Example:** The weeks leading up to the convention were a whirlwind of preparation and hurried activity.

   **Example:** swept up in the whirlwind of war

   **Source synonyms:** vortex; maelstrom
