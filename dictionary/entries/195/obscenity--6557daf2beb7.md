## Noun

### Forms

- obscenities — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Something that is obscene.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** Martha wouldn't go into the art museum because, as she put it, "They have obscenities just sitting out, on display!"

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An act of obscene behaviour.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** Bestiality was outlawed as an obscenity in the strongly conservative community.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Specifically, an offensive word; a profanity; a dirty word.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** Eliza couldn't stand her daughter's music; as she saw it, it was just shouted obscenities and a heavy drum beat.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Lewdness, indecency, or offensive behaviour or material.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** The coalition of religious conservatives was campaigning against, in their view, rampant obscenity in the entertainment industry.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The fact of being obscene.

   **Usage:** uncountable
