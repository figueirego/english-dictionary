## Prep\_phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Middle-aged or older.

   **Usage:** euphemistic

   **Example:** a lady of a certain age

   **Example:** Now that I'm of a certain age, I need reading glasses to see up close.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a person

   **Source synonyms:** getting on in years (indeterminate); on in years; long in the tooth; older; senior; elderly; geriatric

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see of, a, certain, age&#58; belonging to some particular specified age range.

   **Example:** All able-bodied men of a certain age were subject to conscription by royal decree.
