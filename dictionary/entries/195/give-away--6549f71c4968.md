### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make a gift of (something).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I didn't like that book, so I gave it away.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To sell very cheaply.

   **Usage:** broadly; informal; transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To relinquish control over.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To formally hand over a bride to the bridegroom; often by her father.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Who giveth away this woman to this man in Holy matrimony?

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To unintentionally reveal a secret or divulge undisclosed information; to betray or expose someone.

   **Usage:** reflexive; sometimes; transitive

   **Example:** He gave himself away with a stupid lie.

   **Example:** He gave away his hiding place when he accidentally sneezed.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To concede an advantage in weight, time, height etc.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Despite giving away twenty pounds in weight, the challenger found a knock-out blow in the second round.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To concede.
