### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Allowing selection from a fixed list of individually priced options shown on the menu.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; food and drink

   **Example:** The restaurant had a huge à la carte menu and four specials that day.

   **Source antonyms:** table d'hôte

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Selective; only choosing what one likes or desires.

   **Usage:** broadly; figuratively; not-comparable

   **Source synonyms:** cherry-picking

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A meal but only the viand (without rice).

   **Usage:** Philippines; not-comparable; particularly in restaurants
