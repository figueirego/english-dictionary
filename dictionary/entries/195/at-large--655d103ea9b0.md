### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. On the loose; roaming freely; not confined.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** For a nervous twenty-four hours, three wanted criminals were at large in the city.

   **Example:** The ambassador-at-large was designated to the Middle East as a region, rather than to a specific country.

   **Source synonyms:** in the wild; on the loose; on the run; on the lam

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In full, fully.

   **Usage:** obsolete

   **Source synonyms:** entirely; in full; wholly

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. In general; as a whole.

   **Example:** Some people support the measure, but the community at large will probably be against it.

   **Source synonyms:** generally; in general

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Having an electorate across multiple districts.

   **Usage:** US

   **Example:** The city has five city council districts; however, the mayor is elected at large.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; politics

   **Source context (verbatim):** of an election or elected position
