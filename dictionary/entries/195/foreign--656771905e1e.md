### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Located outside a country or place, especially one's own.

   **Example:** foreign markets; foreign soil

   **Example:** He liked visiting foreign cities.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Originating from, characteristic of, belonging to, or being a citizen of a country or place other than the one under discussion.

   **Example:** foreign car; foreign word; foreign citizen; foreign trade

   **Example:** There are many more foreign students in Europe since the Erasmus scheme started.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Relating to a different nation.

   **Example:** foreign policy; foreign navies

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Not characteristic of or naturally taken in by an organism or system.

   **Example:** foreign body; foreign substance; foreign gene; foreign species

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Alien; strange; uncharacteristic.

   **Usage:** formerly with from

   **Example:** It was completely foreign to their way of thinking.

   **Source context (verbatim):** with to

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Held at a distance; excluded; exiled.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. From a different legal jurisdiction (state, province), even if within the same country.

   **Usage:** Canada; US

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Belonging to a different organization, company etc.

   **Example:** My bank charges me $2.50 every time I use a foreign ATM.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. Outside, outdoors, outdoor.

   **Usage:** obsolete
