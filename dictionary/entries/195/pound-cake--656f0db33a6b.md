### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A dense yellow cake, the traditional recipe for which consists of equal quantities (nominally one pound) of butter, eggs, flour, and sugar.

   **Usage:** US; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** countable; slang; uncountable
