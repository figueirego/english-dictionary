**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One of the Greek numerals. A symbol formed from one or more of the letters of the Greek alphabet which has been modified by the addition of a mark, thus&#58; Δ' θ'. These are used to numerate monarchs, chapters, etc as Roman numerals are used in English.
