## Noun · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /slɒt/ — source labels: UK
- IPA: /slɑt/ — source labels: US

### Forms

- slots — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A narrow depression, perforation, or aperture; especially, one for the reception of a piece fitting or sliding into it.

   **Example:** insert tab A into slot B

   **Source antonyms:** tab

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A period of time or position within a schedule or sequence.

   **Example:** I've booked your haircut for the 2 p.m. slot.

   **Example:** With a strong finish he ended up in the third-ranked slot overall.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Clipping of slot machine (“a game of chance played for money using a coin slot”).

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; clipping; especially; in-plural; informal

   **Example:** I walked past the poker tables and went straight to the slots.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** gambling; games

   **Alternative form of:** [slot machine](../086/slot-machine--219035c2a453.md)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The track of an animal, especially a deer; spoor.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A crack or fissure in a glacier or snowfield; a chasm; a crevasse.

   **Usage:** Antarctica

**Source sense R1.S6**

   **Usage:** slang

**Source sense R1.S7**

- IPA: /slɑt/ — source labels: US

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To put something (such as a coin) into a slot (narrow aperture).

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To assign something or someone into a slot (gap in a schedule or sequence).

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To create a slot (narrow aperture or groove), as for example by cutting or machining.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To put something where it belongs.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To kill.

   **Usage:** Rhodesia; UK; slang; sometimes elsewhere in the Commonwealth

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To fall, or cause to fall, into a crevasse.

   **Usage:** Antarctica

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

**Source sense R2.S7**

7. To kick the ball between the posts for a goal; to score a goal by doing this.

   **Usage:** informal; Australian rules football
