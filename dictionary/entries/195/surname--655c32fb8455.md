### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The portion of a person's name that is generally hereditary or treated as an indicator of a person's family, which may be shared with other members of the family, or otherwise derived from their names in some fashion; distinguished from that person's given name(s).

   **Example:** James is my first name, and Smith is my surname.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Synonym of epithet, an additional name, particularly those derived from a birthplace, quality, or achievement.

   **Usage:** obsolete

   **Source synonyms:** epithet (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Synonym of nickname, an additional name given to a person, place, or thing, a byname.

   **Usage:** obsolete

   **Source synonyms:** nickname (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The cognomen of Roman names.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** classical-studies; history; human-sciences; sciences
