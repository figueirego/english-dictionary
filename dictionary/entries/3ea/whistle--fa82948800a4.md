## Verb · Etymology 2 · Record 2

### Pronunciation

### Forms

- whistles — present; singular; third-person
- whistling — participle; present
- whistled — participle; past

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To make a shrill, high-pitched sound by forcing air through the mouth. To produce a whistling sound, restrictions to the flow of air are created using the teeth, tongue and lips.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** Never whistle at a funeral.

   **Example:** He was whistling a happy tune.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To make a similar sound by forcing air through a musical instrument or a pipe etc.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** The steam train whistled as it passed by.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To move in such a way as to create a whistling sound.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** A bullet whistled past.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To send, signal, or call by a whistle.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To request admission to Opus Dei, a Roman Catholic organization.

   **Usage:** intransitive; slang; Opus Dei
