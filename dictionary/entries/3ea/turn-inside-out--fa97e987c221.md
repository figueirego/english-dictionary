### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To invert; to turn so that the outside surface is inside and the inside surface is outside.

   **Usage:** ergative

   **Example:** The wind was blowing so hard that my umbrella turned inside-out.

   **Source synonyms:** evert

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To disarrange (a space) by searching through it exhaustively.

   **Usage:** figuratively; informal; transitive

   **Example:** The thieves turned the house inside-out but never found the hidden jewelry.

   **Source synonyms:** ransack

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To alter or change radically.

   **Usage:** figuratively; informal

   **Example:** to turn a person's life inside-out

   **Source synonyms:** upend

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To make (a player, especially a defender) turn the wrong way and hence get past him.

   **Usage:** broadly; transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports
