### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To bring into existence; (sometimes in particular&#58;)

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** You can create the color orange by mixing yellow and red.

   **Source synonyms:** generate; make

   **Source antonyms:** annihilate; extinguish

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To bring into existence; (sometimes in particular&#58;)
   1. To bring into existence out of nothing, without the prior existence of the materials or elements used.

      **Usage:** especially; transitive

      **Source context (verbatim):** of a god

      **Source synonyms:** generate; make

      **Source antonyms:** annihilate; extinguish

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To bring into existence; (sometimes in particular&#58;)
   1. To make or produce from other (e.g. raw, unrefined or scattered) materials or combinable elements or ideas; to design or invest with a new form, shape, function, etc.

      **Usage:** transitive

      **Example:** Couturiers create exclusive garments for an affluent clientele.

      **Source synonyms:** generate; make; invent

      **Source antonyms:** annihilate; extinguish; imitate

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To cause, to bring (a non-object) about by an action, behavior, or event, to occasion.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** A sudden chemical spill on the highway created a chain‐collision which created a record traffic jam.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To confer or invest with a rank or title of nobility, to appoint, ordain or constitute.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Henry VIII created him a Duke. Last month, the queen created two barons.

   **Example:** Under the concordate with Belgium, at least one Belgian clergyman must be created cardinal; by tradition, every archbishop of Mechelen is thus created a cardinal.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To be or do something creative, imaginative, originative.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Children usually enjoy creating, never mind if it is of any use!

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. In theatre, to be the first performer of a role; to originate a character.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To make a fuss, complain; to shout.

   **Usage:** UK; colloquial; intransitive
