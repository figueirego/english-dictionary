**Source sense R1.S1**

1. At the third level of some system
   1. The least severe level of felony, typically involving a lack of planning and/or the presence of various mitigating factors.

      **Usage:** not-comparable

      **Example:** The jury found him guilty of third-degree murder since he had only meant to beat the clown, not to kill him.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law
