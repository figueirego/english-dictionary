### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Agent noun of rope; one who uses a rope, especially one who throws a lariat or lasso.

   **Usage:** agent; form-of

   **Form of:** [rope](../3f9/rope--fe4d55b9ffa5.md)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A ropemaker (a maker of ropes).

   **Usage:** dated

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. One who ropes goods; a packer.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Synonym of outside man (“accomplice who locates a mark to be swindled by a confidence trickster”).

   **Usage:** slang

   **Source synonyms:** outside man (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A person hired by a gambling establishment to locate potential customers and bring them in.

   **Source synonyms:** lugger; picker-up; roper-in; runner; steerer

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. An undercover informer.

   **Usage:** slang

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**
