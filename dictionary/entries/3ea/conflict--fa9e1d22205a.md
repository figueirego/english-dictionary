### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A clash or disagreement, often violent, between two or more opposing groups or individuals.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The conflict between the government and the rebels began three years ago.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An incompatibility, as of two things that cannot be simultaneously fulfilled.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** I wanted to attend the meeting but there's a conflict in my schedule that day.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. in fiction/literature, opposing forces that create uncertainty as to whether the character(s) will achieve their goal(s).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
