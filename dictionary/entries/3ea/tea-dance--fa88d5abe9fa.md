### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A late afternoon or early evening dance, typically with a live orchestra (often referred to as a palm court orchestra) playing light classical music.

   **Usage:** dated

   **Example:** Since the 1970s, gay-oriented tea dances have been popular with the gay community.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>
