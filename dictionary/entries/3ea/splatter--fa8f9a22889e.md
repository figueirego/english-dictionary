## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈsplætɚ/ — source labels: General-American

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An uneven shape or mess created by something dispersing on impact.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** He had a hard time cleaning up the paint splatters on the carpet.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A genre of gory horror.

   **Usage:** attributive; uncountable

   **Example:** splatter film; splatter movie

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** broadcasting; film; media; television

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Spurious emissions resulting from an abrupt change in a transmitted radio signal.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The drink splattered all over me, the table, and the floor when I knocked it over.
