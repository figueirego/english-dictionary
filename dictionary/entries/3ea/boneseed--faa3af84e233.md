**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An upright shrub in the sunflower family (Osteospermum moniliferum, syn. Chrysanthemoides monilifera), native to South Africa but is an invasive pest in Australia and New Zealand.

   **Usage:** New-Zealand; countable; uncountable
