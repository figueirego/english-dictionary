### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The state of being befuddled; a feeling of being dazed and perplexed due to confusion.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** His words struck me with a sense of befuddlement that lasted until I got home.

   **Source synonyms:** bafflement; bewilderment; confusion; perplexity; puzzlement
