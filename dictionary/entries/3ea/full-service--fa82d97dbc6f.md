### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A radio format consisting of a mix of music with locally produced programming and segments focusing on local issues.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** WXYZ used to offer full service to listeners in the region, until it was bought out by a conglomerate and converted to become all corporate country and commercials, all the time.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** broadcasting; media; radio

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The ability to offer many types of related service rather than a limited set of specialisms.

   **Usage:** attributive; often; uncountable

   **Example:** By offering everything that a publisher needs — including editing, proofreading, printing, binding, and distribution — Acme Corporation provides full service to the trade.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Service that includes waiting on the customer.

   **Usage:** attributive; often; uncountable

   **Example:** By pumping the gas, washing the windshield, checking the oil dipstick, and checking the tire pressure, old-fashioned service stations offered full service to the motorist.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business

   **Source antonyms:** self-service

   **Usage:** euphemistic; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** lifestyle; sex; sexuality
