### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An indefinite, but usually small, number of.

   **Usage:** preceded by another determiner

   **Example:** There are a few cars (=some, but a relatively small number) in the street.

   **Example:** Quite a few people (=a significant number) were pleasantly surprised.

   **Example:** I think he's had a few drinks.

   **Example:** The few cheapest hotels I could find are the farthest too.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Not many; a small (in comparison with another number stated or implied) but somewhat indefinite number of.

   **Example:** Very few did she have not to go there, did she?

   **Example:** I was expecting a big crowd at the party, but very few people (=almost none) turned up.

   **Source context (verbatim):** used alone

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Obscuring one to two oktas (eighths) of the sky.

   **Example:** Tonight&#58; A few clouds. Increasing cloudiness overnight.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** climatology; meteorology; natural-sciences

   **Source context (verbatim):** of clouds

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. (US?) Having a 10 percent chance of measurable precipitation (0.01 inch); used interchangeably with isolated.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** climatology; meteorology; natural-sciences

   **Source context (verbatim):** of rainfall with regard to a location
