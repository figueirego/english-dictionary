### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To persevere with an activity of consuming something, both literally and figuratively.

   **Example:** I ploughed through two helpings, but then I didn't have room for any more.

   **Example:** If you can plough through the first three chapters, then the plot starts to get interesting.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To forcefully make a passage to move through.

   **Example:** I managed to plough through the crowds and get to the information desk.
