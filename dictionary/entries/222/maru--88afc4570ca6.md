### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A Japanese ship, especially a merchant ship.

   **Example:** the Nippon Maru

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A semi-voiced diacritic (゜) used with Japanese kana to change a /h/ to a /p/.

   **Usage:** colloquial

   **Source synonyms:** handakuten

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A large, circular punctuation mark (。) used as a full stop in Japanese text.
