## Noun · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Initialism of red, green, blue; an additive color model in which colors are represented by adding varying amounts of red, green, and blue light.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; initialism; uncountable

   **Source antonyms:** CMY

   **Alternative form of:** [red](../2c7/red--b1f51a511f1d.md); [green](../2e9/green--ba4788b226aa.md); [blue](../059/blue--16477688c0e0.md)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Something extending across the RGB color space; (especially) something having a wide variety of vivid colors.

   **Usage:** attributive; broadly; uncountable

   **Example:** RGB wallpaper

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. RGB lighting; lights (especially LEDs) able to display a wide range of colors by being able to individually adjust the strength of the red, green, and blue channels.

   **Usage:** broadly; slang; uncountable

   **Example:** An RGB keyboard

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Initialism of red-giant branch. A classification of stars.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; initialism; uncountable
