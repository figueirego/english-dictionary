### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Usage:** Marxism; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** academia; scholarly; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

   **Usage:** Marxism; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** academia; scholarly; sciences
