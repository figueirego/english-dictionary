### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In a metaphorical manner; not literally; by means of metaphor.

   **Example:** He conveyed his teachings metaphorically.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** manner

   **Source synonyms:** nonliterally

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used to draw attention that what follows is a metaphor, not to be taken literally

   **Example:** Metaphorically, he was crushed.

   **Source context (verbatim):** as a sentence adverb
