### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To agitate rapidly and repetitively, or to stir with a rowing or rocking motion; generally applies to liquids, notably cream.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Now the cream is churned to make butter.

   **Example:** no-churn ice cream

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To produce excessive and sometimes undesirable or unproductive activity or motion.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To move rapidly and repetitively with a rocking motion; to tumble, mix or shake.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** I was so nervous that my stomach was churning.

   **Source synonyms:** heave

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To stop using a company's product or service.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a customer

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To repeatedly cancel and rebook a reservation in order to refresh ticket time limits or other fare rule restrictions.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** aeronautics; aerospace; aviation; business; engineering; lifestyle; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; tourism; transport; travel

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To continually sign up for new credit cards in order to earn signup bonuses, airline miles, and other benefits.

   **Usage:** US; informal

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance; lifestyle; tourism; transport; travel

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance
