## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A carboxylic acid derived from phenanthrene extracted from plants of the family Aristolochiaceae; though sometimes used as a herbal remedy, it is a rodent carcinogen and can cause endemic nephropathy in humans.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; organic-chemistry; physical-sciences
