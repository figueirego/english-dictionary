### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any species discovered first as a fossil and believed extinct, but which is later found living; an organism that has remained unchanged over geological periods.

   **Usage:** evolutionary theory

   **Example:** The coelacanth and the dawn redwood are living fossils.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any living species which very closely resembles fossil relatives in most anatomical details.

   **Usage:** evolutionary theory

   **Example:** Crocodiles are living fossils that haven’t changed their appearance much in millions of years.
