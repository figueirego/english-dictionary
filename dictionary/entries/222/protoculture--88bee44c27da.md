### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A base or root culture that serves as the basis for later distinct recognised cultural traditions.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The passing of behaviours from one generation to another among non-human primates. These cultures are very rudimentary, and do not exhibit complex cultural technology.
