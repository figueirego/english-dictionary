### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A male parent, especially of a human; a male who parents a child (which he has sired, adopted, fostered, etc.).

   **Example:** My father was a strong influence on me.

   **Example:** My friend Tony just became a father.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A male ancestor more remote than a parent; a progenitor; especially, a first ancestor.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A term of respectful address for an elderly man.

   **Example:** Come, father; you can sit here.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A term of respectful address for a priest.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A person who plays the role of a father in some way.

   **Example:** My brother was a father to me after my parents got divorced.

   **Example:** The child is father to the man.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A pioneering figure in a particular field.

   **Example:** Albert Einstein is the father of modern physics.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Something that is the greatest or most significant of its kind.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Something inanimate that begets.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A member of a church council.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. The archived older version of a file that immediately precedes the current version, and was itself derived from the grandfather.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences
