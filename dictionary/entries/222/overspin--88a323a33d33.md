## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈoʊvɚˌspɪn/ — source labels: General-American

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An excessive amount of spin applied to a projectile such that its nose does not turn down at the summit of the trajectory.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A rotational motion, especially that given to a ball, in which the upper surface spins in the direction of motion; topspin;top.

   **Usage:** uncountable
