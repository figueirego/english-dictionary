### Senses

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. At or close to the state of the art.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Involving greater complexity; more difficult, elaborate or specialized.

   **Example:** The option can be found under advanced settings.

   **Example:** I'm taking a course in advanced knitting techniques.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. Having moved forward in time or space (e.g. advanced ignition timing).

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. In a late stage of development or education; having greatly progressed beyond an initial stage.

   **Example:** This is an advanced prototype, it already has most of the features the final product will have.

   **Example:** This book is suitable for advanced learners of English.
