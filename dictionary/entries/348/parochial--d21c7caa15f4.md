### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Pertaining to a parish.

   **Example:** The parish council handles parochial affairs. \[civil context\]

   **Example:** The rector and vestry handle parochial affairs. \[church context\]

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Characterized by an unsophisticated focus on local concerns to the exclusion of wider contexts; elementary in scope or outlook.

   **Usage:** derogatory

   **Example:** The use of simple, primary colors in the painting gave it a parochial feel.

   **Example:** Some people in the United States have been accused of taking a parochial view, of not being interested in international matters.
