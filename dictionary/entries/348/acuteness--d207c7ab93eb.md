## Noun

### Forms

- acutenesses — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The quality of being acute or pointed.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** the acuteness of an angle

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of the senses or feelings&#58; the faculty of precise discernment or perception; sensitiveness.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** By acuteness of feeling, we perceive small objects or slight impressions.

   **Example:** By acuteness of intellect, we discern nice distinctions.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of sounds&#58; shrillness; high pitch.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Violence of a disease, which brings it speedily to a crisis.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Shrewdness, quickness of mind.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually
