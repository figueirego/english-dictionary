### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A fuel-based auxiliary power unit that extends the range of a battery electric vehicle by driving an electric generator that charges the vehicle's battery.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** automotive; transport; vehicles

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A device which extends a signal beyond its normal limit.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** communications; electrical-engineering; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; telecommunications

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
