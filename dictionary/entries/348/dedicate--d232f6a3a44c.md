## Verb · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈdɛdɪˌkeɪt/ — source labels: UK; US

### Forms

- dedicates — present; singular; third-person
- dedicating — participle; present
- dedicated — participle; past
- dedicated — past

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To set apart for a deity or for religious purposes; consecrate.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To set apart for a special use.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** dedicated their money to scientific research.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To commit (oneself) to a particular course of thought or action.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** dedicated ourselves to starting our own business.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To address or inscribe (a literary work, for example) to another as a mark of respect or affection.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To open (a building, for example) to public use.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To show to the public for the first time.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** dedicate a monument.
