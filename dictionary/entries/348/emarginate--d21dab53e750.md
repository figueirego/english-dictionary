### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. With the outline of the margin more or less concave in places, usually at the apex.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences

   **Source context (verbatim):** of leaves

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Having roughly the same height or width for most of its length, becoming much shallower or narrower before reaching the attachment point.

   **Example:** In this group of mushrooms, the attachment of the gills to the stipe is emarginate.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; mycology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Having a margin that has concave edges as though with parts removed or notched.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; biology; medicine; natural-sciences; sciences; zoology

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Of a crystal&#58; having edges or corners of the primitive form beveled, crossed by a face.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; geography; geology; mineralogy; natural-sciences; physical-sciences
