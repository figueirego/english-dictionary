### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any plant of the species Kalanchoe pinnata (cathedral bells, life plant, miracle leaf, Goethe plant, mother of thousands, never dead, parvu, wonder-of-the-world (Trinidad)).

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Synonym of epiphyte (a plant living on another plant nonparasitically).
