### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To change the shape of (an object) to make it smoother and especially more circular or ovoid.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source antonyms:** square off

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To change (a number) to an approximation having fewer significant digits.

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** The instructions say that we are not to round off until we reach the final figure. \[in a calculation with interim steps\]

   **Example:** Round off 15.4 to 15, round off 15.51 to 15.5 or to 16, round off 0.499 to 0, and round off 970,000 to 1 million.

   **Example:** "This product contains no PCBs" is a typical commercial distortion if it actually contains 0.498 of the measurement unit, rounded off to "0"

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To complete or finish something.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source antonyms:** finish up; polish off
