## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An ethnic group larger than a band or clan (and which may contain clans) but smaller than a nation (and which in turn may constitute a nation with other tribes). The tribe is often the basis of ethnic identity.

   **Example:** the Twelve Tribes of Israel; Germanic tribes; Celtic tribes

   **Example:** The Formation of Kazakh Identity&#58; From Tribe to Nation-state

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anthropology; history; human-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A tribal nation or people.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A nation or people considered culturally primitive, as may be the case in Africa, Australia or Native America.

   **Usage:** derogatory; informal

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A socially cohesive group of people within a society.

   **Example:** She found her tribe among fellow cross-stitchers.

   **Source synonyms:** nation; ingroup

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A class or group of things.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A hierarchical rank between family and genus.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences; taxonomy

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A species or group of similar species of animal.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A group of affiliated Mardi Gras Indians.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A collective noun for various animals.

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. A family of animals descended from some particular female progenitor, through the female line.

   **Usage:** stock breeding

   **Example:** the Duchess tribe of shorthorns
