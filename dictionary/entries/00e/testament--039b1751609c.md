## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈtɛs.tə.mənt/ — source labels: UK; US
- IPA: /ˈtes.tə.mənt/ — source labels: Australia

### Forms

- testaments — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A solemn, authentic instrument in writing, by which a person declares his or her will as to disposal of his or her inheritance (estate and effects) after his or her death, benefiting specified heir(s).

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

   **Source synonyms:** will; last will and testament; last will

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One of the two parts to the scriptures of the Christian religion&#58; the New Testament, considered by Christians to be a continuation of the Hebrew scriptures, and the Hebrew scriptures themselves, which they refer to as the Old Testament.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A tangible proof or tribute.

   **Example:** The ancient aqueducts are a testament to the great engineering skill of the Roman Empire.

   **Example:** His remarkable recovery is a testament to the doctor's skill.

   **Source synonyms:** testimony

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A credo, expression of conviction.

   **Example:** The prime minister's speech was a glowing testament to the cabinet's undying commitment to the royal cause.
