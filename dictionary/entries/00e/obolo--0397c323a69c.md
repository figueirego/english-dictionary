### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A member of an ethnic group or tribe living mainly in the Niger Delta region of southern Nigeria. Obolo people can be found mainly in Rivers State and Akwa Ibom State, Nigeria.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The language spoken by the Obolo people.
