### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A mythological bird, said to be the only one of its kind, which lives for 500 years and then dies by burning to ashes on a pyre of its own making, ignited by the sun. It then arises anew from the ashes.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; mysticism; mythology; philosophy; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Anything that is reborn after apparently being destroyed.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** Astronomers believe planets might form in this dead star's disk, like the mythical Phoenix rising up out of the ashes.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A mythological Chinese chimerical bird whose physical body symbolizes the six celestial bodies; a fenghuang.

   **Usage:** Chinese

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; mysticism; mythology; philosophy; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A Greek silver coin used briefly from 1828 to 1832, divided into 100 lepta.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A marvelous person or thing.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A geometer moth of species Eulithis prunata.
