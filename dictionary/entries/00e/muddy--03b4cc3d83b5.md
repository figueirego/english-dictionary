### Pronunciation

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Covered or splashed with, or full of, mud (“wet soil”).

   **Example:** He slogged across the muddy field.

   **Example:** Take off your muddy boots before you come inside.

   **Source synonyms:** oozy (Scotland); clatchy

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of water or some other liquid&#58; containing mud or (by extension) other sediment in suspension; cloudy, turbid.

   **Example:** The previously limpid water was now muddy as a result of the struggle between the alligator and the wild boar.

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of or relating to mud; also, having the characteristics of mud, especially in colour or taste.
