### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The state of being accountable; liability to be called on to render an account or give an explanation; liability to be held responsible or answerable for something.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An open determination of one's responsibility for something and imposition of consequences.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An acceptance of good faith of one's responsibility for something and of its consequences.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The obligation imposed by law or regulation on an officer or other person for keeping an accurate record of property, documents, or funds.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war
