### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A colonial insect of the infraorder Isoptera, in the order Blattodea; typically known for certain white-bodied species that devour and infest wood, though other species eat different plant matter, such as grasses.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
