### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In an expression involving division, the number by which another number is being divided.

   **Example:** In "42 ÷ 3" the divisor is the 3.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** arithmetic

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An integer that divides another integer an integral number of times, the former being called a divisor of the latter.

   **Example:** 10 is a divisor of 100, but not of 101.

   **Example:** The positive divisors of 6 are 1, 2, 3 and 6.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An integer that divides another integer an integral number of times, the former being called a divisor of the latter.
   1. A ring element which can be multiplied (by some other ring element) to yield a third ring element, the first being called a divisor of the third. If the ring is noncommutative, then one specifies whether a divisor is left, right, or two-sided.

      **Usage:** broadly

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** algebra; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any of several kinds of formal object associated to an algebraic variety, scheme, etc., which play a role similar to that of homology or cohomology groups in the study of topological spaces.
   1. An element of the free abelian group on the points of the space.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** algebraic-geometry; geometry; mathematics; sciences

      **Source context (verbatim):** in the study of Riemann surfaces

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Any of several kinds of formal object associated to an algebraic variety, scheme, etc., which play a role similar to that of homology or cohomology groups in the study of topological spaces.
   1. A Weil divisor&#58; an element of the free abelian group on the codimension-1 subvarieties (or subschemes).

      **Usage:** on a variety (or integral locally Noetherian scheme)

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** algebraic-geometry; geometry; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Any of several kinds of formal object associated to an algebraic variety, scheme, etc., which play a role similar to that of homology or cohomology groups in the study of topological spaces.
   1. A Cartier divisor; see Cartier divisors on Wikipedia.Wikipedia

      **Usage:** on a variety (or integral Noetherian scheme)

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** algebraic-geometry; geometry; mathematics; sciences
