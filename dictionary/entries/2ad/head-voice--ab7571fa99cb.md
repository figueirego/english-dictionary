### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A kind of voice of high pitch and of a thin quality ascribed to resonance in the head; voice of the thin register. In producing it, the vibration of the cords is limited to their thin edges in the upper part, which are then presented to each other.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; human-sciences; lifestyle; linguistics; music; phonology; sciences
