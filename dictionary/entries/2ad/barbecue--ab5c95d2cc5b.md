### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A fireplace or pit for grilling food, typically used outdoors and traditionally employing hot charcoal as the heating medium.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** We cooked our food on the barbecue.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A meal or event highlighted by food cooked in such an apparatus.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** We're having a barbecue on Saturday, and you're invited.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Meat, especially pork or beef, which has been cooked in such an apparatus (i.e. smoked over indirect heat from high-smoke fuels) and then chopped up or shredded.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** She ordered a plate of barbecue with a side of slaw.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A hog, ox, or other large animal roasted or broiled whole for a feast.

   **Usage:** countable; dated; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A floor on which coffee beans are sun-dried.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A framework of sticks.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable
