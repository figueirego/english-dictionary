### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Edible fruit of the mamoncillo (Melicoccus bijugatus), a soapberry whose tastes resembles a mix of lychee and lime.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

<a id="r1-s3"></a>
