## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of the days occupied on a voyage, etc., under a charter, including Sundays.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A day on which vehicles, locomotives, etc., are run or driven, especially when these are otherwise kept or exhibited statically, such as at a museum.

   **Example:** The bus museum's next running day is August 12. Come along and enjoy a ride on one of our vintage buses!
