## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The afterlife, as a supernatural realm inhabited by spirits of deceased people.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; usually preceded by the and sometimes capitalized

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The time after the solution or completion of a problem, adversity, or challenge.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; usually preceded by the

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The other side of the Atlantic Ocean (usually between the United States and the United Kingdom).

   **Usage:** idiomatic; preceded by the

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The other television channel (when only two were available).

   **Usage:** UK; dated; idiomatic; slang

   **Example:** This is boring. What's on the other side?

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see other, side.

   **Example:** We crossed to the other side of the road.

   **Example:** Give the other side two options to choose from so they will not feel stuck with an ultimatum.
