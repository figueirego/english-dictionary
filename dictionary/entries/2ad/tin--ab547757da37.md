   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Usage:** countable; metonymically; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An airtight container, made of tin-coated steel (called tinplate or tin), (formerly) tin, aluminium, or another metal, used to preserve food, or hold a liquid or some other product.

   **Usage:** Commonwealth; UK; countable

   **Example:** a tin of baked beans; a tobacco tin; a tin of shoe polish

   **Example:** Several tins of paint were needed to paint the house.

   **Example:** empty tins, cans, and plastic containers are recycled in the blue bins.

   **Source synonyms:** can

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** baking tin
