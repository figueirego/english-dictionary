### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of softening a mass by malaxating.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A kneading or softening, especially the chewing and squeezing by which certain species of hunting wasps prepare captured prey as food for their larvae.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; entomology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The process of slowly churning milled oil crops such as olives, allowing droplets of oil to aggregate for more effective separation.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** agriculture; business; lifestyle

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
