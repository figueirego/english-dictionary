## Noun

### Forms

- holiday seasons — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The period of the late fall and early winter when several holidays occur, such as Halloween, American Thanksgiving, Christmas, New Year's Day, Hanukkah, and Kwanzaa.

   **Usage:** Canada; US

   **Example:** What are your plans for the holiday season?

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A period in the summer when people go on holiday (vacation).

   **Usage:** UK
