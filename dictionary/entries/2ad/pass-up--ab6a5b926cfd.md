### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To refuse (not accept); forgo.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** He passed up my invitation for dinner, saying he was too busy.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To submit (hand in) items to a person, usually one's teacher.

   **Usage:** Malaysia; Singapore; transitive

   **Example:** The teacher told us to pass up our English homework.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see pass, up.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Can you pass up this box to the guy on the ladder?
