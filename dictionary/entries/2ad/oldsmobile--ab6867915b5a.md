### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An American car brand belonging to GM. Founded in 1897; with GM since 1908; retired from new cars since 2004; still active IP of GM.

   **Example:** A TV commercial blared in the background, repeating the brand's catchphrase&#58; "This is not your father's Oldsmobile — this is the new generation of Olds!"
