### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The body with the power to make and/or enforce laws to control a country, land area, people or organization.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** British government has historically centred exclusively on London.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The relationship between a word and its dependents.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** grammar; human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** regimen; rection

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The state and its administration viewed as the ruling political power.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** If the citizens must follow the law, then the government must follow the constitution.

   **Source synonyms:** state; administration

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The management or control of a system.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The tenure of a head of government; the ministry or administration led by a specified individual.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The Sunak government announced plans to stem the flow of migrants coming into Great Britain.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. In a parliamentary system, the political party or coalition in power; its condition of being in power.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The team tasked with presenting and speaking in favour of a resolution, as opposed to the opposition.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; debating

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A guberniya.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**
