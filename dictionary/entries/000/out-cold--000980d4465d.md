## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Soundly unconscious, either because asleep or because otherwise knocked out.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** He was growing unsteady as the blows landed, and that final right hook finished him — he was out cold.

   **Source synonyms:** down for the count
