<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The right-hand side of, originally, a ship or other marine vessel, and now also an aircraft or spacecraft, when one is facing the front, or the bow or fore.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; space flight

**Source sense R1.S2**
