### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To be expensive.

   **Example:** Those extra nights tacked onto his hotel stay will cost him dear.

   **Example:** It will cost him dear to tack additional nights onto his hotel stay.

   **Source synonyms:** cost an arm and a leg; cost a pretty penny

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To cause a lot of pain or suffering.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** His intransigence will cost him dear in the end.

   **Example:** It will cost him dear to be so intransigent for so long.
