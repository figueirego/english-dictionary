### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The art or act of cultivating (improvement of land for or by agriculture), as&#58;
   1. Tillage&#58; plowing, sowing and raising crops.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Example:** The cultivation of these valleys dates back thousands of years.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The art or act of cultivating (improvement of land for or by agriculture), as&#58;
   1. Light tillage&#58; turning or stirring the soil, especially for weed control.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Example:** The cultivation of the hillside led to soil erosion.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The state of being cultivated or used for agriculture

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** These fields are in cultivation.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Devotion of time or attention to the improvement of (something)

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** His steadfast cultivation of their relationship finally bore fruit.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Advancement or refinement in physical, intellectual, or moral condition

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** She is a woman of great cultivation.
