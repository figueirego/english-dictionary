### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A smooth surface, usually made of glass with reflective material painted on the underside, that reflects light so as to give an image of what is in front of it.

   **Example:** I had a look in the mirror to see if the blood had come off my face.

   **Example:** He broke the mirror with the pickaxe.

   **Example:** We could see the lorry in the mirror, so decided to change lanes.

   **Source synonyms:** glass; looking-glass

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An object, person, or event that reflects or gives a picture of another.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** His story is a mirror into the life of orphans growing up.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A website or server that contains replicated data from another site.

   **Usage:** Internet

   **Example:** Although the content had been deleted from his blog, it was still found on some mirrors.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A mirror carp.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An ethical-didactic treatise that details exemplary conduct through models, advising kings, princes, clergy, etc. on how to behave.

**Source sense R2.S1**

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To act as a reflection of, either by being identical to, or by being identical but reversed.

   **Usage:** transitive
