### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To become unfocused, as if through boredom.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Someone's eyes may glaze over before or during a seizure.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of eyes

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To form a glazed coating&#58; to ice over or otherwise to become covered in a glossy sheen.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To gloss over.

   **Usage:** proscribed; transitive; viewed as catachrestic

   **Example:** An important issue that is often glazed over.
