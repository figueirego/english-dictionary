### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A relatively wide magnetic tape used for recording visual images and sounds (as of a television production) for subsequent playback or broadcasting.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Synonym of videocassette (“a cassette containing videotape”).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
