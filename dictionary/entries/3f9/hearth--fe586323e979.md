### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The place in a home where a fire is or was traditionally kept for home heating and for cooking, usually constituted by at least a hearthstone and often enclosed to varying degrees by any combination of reredos, fireplace, oven, smoke hood, or chimney.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A hearthstone, either as standalone or as the floor of an enclosed fireplace or oven.

   **Example:** cooking on an open hearth

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A fireplace&#58; an open recess in a wall at the base of a chimney where a fire may be built.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The lowest part of a metallurgical furnace.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A brazier, chafing dish, or firebox.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Home or family life.

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A household or group in some forms of the modern pagan faith Heathenry.

   **Usage:** Germanic

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** lifestyle; paganism; religion
