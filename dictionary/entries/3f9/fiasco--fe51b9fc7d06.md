**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A sudden or unexpected failure.

   **Example:** The event turned into a complete fiasco when the power went out.

   **Example:** His speech was a fiasco that left the audience confused.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Source synonyms:** debacle
