### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An SI unit of volume equal to that of a cube having sides each one millimetre in length, equal to 10⁻⁹ cubic metres.

   **Usage:** UK

   **Source synonyms:** mm³; cmm; cumm (abbreviation)
