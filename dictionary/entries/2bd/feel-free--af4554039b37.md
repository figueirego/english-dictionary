### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To feel able without giving offense.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** The co-pilot didn't feel free to speak up to the pilot in the cockpit.

   **Source context (verbatim):** with to-infinitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. You have my permission.

   **Example:** While you're babysitting, feel free to open anything in the spirit cupboard.

   **Source context (verbatim):** as imperative; with to-infinitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
