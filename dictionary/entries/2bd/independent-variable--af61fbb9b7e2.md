## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In an equation, any variable whose value is not dependent on any other in the equation.

   **Example:** In the equation z = x² + y, x and y are the independent variables.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** algebra; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The variable that is changed or manipulated in a series of experiments.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** sciences
