### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An animal cross between a swan and a goose, especially one produced from a male swan and a female goose.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Source synonyms:** gwan

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person or thing sharing the characteristics of two otherwise separate groups; a hybrid (also see Swoose)

   **Usage:** informal

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
