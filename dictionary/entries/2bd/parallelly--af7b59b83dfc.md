## Adverb

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. At the same time.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** We planned parallelly for the three most probable scenarios.

   **Source synonyms:** in parallel

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. In a parallel position or direction, even equidistant.

   **Example:** This section of the power line runs parallelly to the railway.
