### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To change (or to be changed) from a solid state to a liquid state, usually by a gradual heat.

   **Usage:** ergative

   **Example:** I melted butter to make a cake.

   **Example:** When the weather is warm, the snowman will disappear; he will melt.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To dissolve, disperse, vanish.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

   **Example:** His troubles melted away.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To soften, as by a warming or kindly influence; to relax; to render gentle or susceptible to mild influences; sometimes, in a bad sense, to take away the firmness of; to weaken.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive; figuratively

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To be discouraged.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To be emotionally softened or touched.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

   **Example:** She melted when she saw the romantic message in the Valentine's Day card.

   **Example:** My heart melted when I first heard the song.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To be very hot and sweat profusely.

   **Usage:** colloquial; intransitive

   **Example:** I need shade! I'm melting!

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** to found — change from solid to liquid; to thaw — change from solid to liquid
