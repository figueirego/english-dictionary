### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A marker on a frame of film, originally a hole literally punched through the film, signalling to the projectionist that the reel is due to be changed.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** broadcasting; film; media; television

   **Source synonyms:** cigarette burn (slang)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A small, round hole created in the shoe, typically on the vamp or quarter, used for decorative purposes or to enhance ventilation.

   **Usage:** footwear

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A small, round section placed near the top of the screen of some smartphones, housing the front-facing camera while maximizing screen space.
