### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A player who waits around a marking contest aiming to get the ball if it falls down to the ground (because the opposing players leaping for it have spoiled each other's efforts).

   **Usage:** Australian rules football

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A small, usually metal, tool designed to remove crumbs from a tablecloth.
