### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To occur or take place.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Let me tell you how it happened.

   **Example:** I hear there're fireworks happening tonight.

   **Source synonyms:** come to pass

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To happen to; to befall.

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To do or occur by chance or unexpectedly.

   **Usage:** impersonal; intransitive; with-infinitive

   **Example:** Take an umbrella in case it happens to rain.

   **Example:** Do you happen to have an umbrella?

   **Example:** If you should (happen to) need any help, I'll be there.

   **Example:** It happened that a police car was passing my house just at that moment.

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To encounter by chance.

   **Source context (verbatim):** followed by on or upon

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To become popular or trendy.

   **Usage:** informal
