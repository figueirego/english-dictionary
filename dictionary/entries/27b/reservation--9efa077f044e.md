## Noun

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˌɹɛz.ɚˈveɪ.ʃən/ — source labels: General-American

### Forms

- reservations — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of reserving, withholding or keeping back.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The committee authorised the reservation of funds.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The act of reserving, withholding or keeping back.
   1. The practice of reserving part of the consecrated bread of the Eucharist for the communion of the sick.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The act of reserving, withholding or keeping back.
   1. The act of the pope to reserve to himself the right to nominate to certain benefices.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Something that is withheld or kept back.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A limiting qualification regarding certainty or risk; a doubt or concern.

   **Usage:** countable; in-plural; often; uncountable

   **Example:** I have reservations about your intentions.

   **Example:** I have some reservations about cosigning a loan for him.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A tract of land set apart by the US government for the use of a Native American people, such as the Hopi Reservation or the Navajo Nation's territory.

   **Usage:** US; countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** reserve; res; rez; rezzy

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. An arrangement by which accommodation or transport arrangements are secured in advance.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** I have a hotel reservation in the name of Mr Smith.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. The area which separates opposing lanes of traffic on a divided motorway or dual carriageway.

   **Usage:** UK; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** A vehicle crashed through the central reservation into the path of oncoming traffic.

   **Source synonyms:** central reservation; median; median strip

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. The setting aside of a certain percentage of vacancies in government institutions for members of backward and underrepresented communities (defined primarily by caste and tribe).

   **Usage:** India; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. A power of an overseeing authority to suspend legislation in the jurisdiction being overseen pending the approval of a higher authority. Such as a provincial/state governor reserving a bill passed by the state/provincial legislature from assent, pending approval of the federal government; or a federal bill passed by federal parliament being reserved by the viceroy pending approval by the crown.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; law; politics
