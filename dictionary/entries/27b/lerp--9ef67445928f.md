### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A sweet secretion, produced by the larvae of the family Psyllidae, that forms scales on eucalyptus leaves.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One of the lice whose larvae produce this secretion.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
