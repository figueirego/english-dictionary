### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The property of a watercraft that makes it fit to go to sea.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** The ship leaked like a sieve and rode like a barrel; in other words it was completely lacking in seaworthiness.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport
