## Noun

### Forms

- G.I. Joes — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An American soldier.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Example:** Most of the Dutch locals were glad to see G.I. Joe coming to town in 1944.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any American servicemember.

   **Usage:** broadly

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A fictional character based on an archetype of an American soldier fighting for righteous causes (e.g., freedom, liberty, rule of law), embodied in various comic books, toys, TV shows, and movies.

   **Example:** A small boy was in the yard playing with a weather-beaten G.I. Joe doll.
