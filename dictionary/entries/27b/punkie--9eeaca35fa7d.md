**Source sense R2.S1**

1. In full punkie lantern&#58; a lantern similar to a jack-o'-lantern consisting of a gourd such as a pumpkin or a root vegetable such as a mangelwurzel or swede which has been hollowed out, in which a candle has been placed; these are chiefly displayed during Punkie Night in late October.

   **Usage:** England; South; West; chiefly Somerset
