### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A complex of buildings in New York City, three of which were destroyed in the 9/11 attacks, including the Twin Towers. New buildings were built to replace the destroyed buildings, one of the new buildings is the Freedom Tower.

   **Source synonyms:** World Trade

<a id="r1-s2"></a>
