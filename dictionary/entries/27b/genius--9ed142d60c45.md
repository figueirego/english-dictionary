### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Someone possessing extraordinary intelligence or skill; especially somebody who has demonstrated this by a creative or original work in science, music, art, etc.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** She's a genius; she won a Nobel Prize at fifteen!

   **Source antonyms:** idiot

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Extraordinary mental capacity.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Inspiration, a mental leap, an extraordinary creative process.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** a work of genius

   **Example:** to add a dash of cinnamon amid such umami was pure genius

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The tutelary deity or spirit of a place or person.

   **Usage:** Roman; also; countable; figuratively

   **Example:** and the genius of the place&#58; the growing enthusiasm for codified standards in the Army and Navy
