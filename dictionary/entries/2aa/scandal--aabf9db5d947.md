### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An incident or event that disgraces or damages the reputation of the persons or organization involved.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Their affair was reported as a scandal by most tabloids.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Damage to one's reputation.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The incident brought considerable scandal to his family.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Widespread moral outrage, indignation, as over an offence to decency.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** When their behaviour was made public it caused a great scandal.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A word or deed, lacking in rectitude in some manner, which is an occasion of the spiritual ruin of another.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** lifestyle; religion; theology

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Defamatory talk; gossip, slander.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** According to village scandal, they weren't even married.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. amateur or homemade pornography

   **Usage:** Philippines; colloquial; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. commotion.

   **Usage:** Philippines; colloquial; countable; informal; uncountable
