### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Written by hand in normal characters, as opposed to shorthand.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source antonyms:** shorthand

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Written by hand (with pen or pencil), rather than printed out; handwritten.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** I had to write the essay out twice longhand – they wouldn’t let me print it out. It took forever!
