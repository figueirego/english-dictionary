### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Someone who terminates or ends something, especially (in later use) an assassin or exterminator.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A text character or string that serves to mark the end of a document or transmission.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astronomy; natural-sciences

   **Source synonyms:** twilight zone; grey line; separatrix

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**
