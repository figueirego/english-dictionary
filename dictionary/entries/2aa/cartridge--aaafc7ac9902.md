<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The package consisting of the bullet, primer, and casing containing gunpowder; a round of ammunition.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; firearms; government; military; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; politics; tools; war; weaponry

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A prefabricated subassembly that can be easily installed in or removed from a larger mechanism or replaced with another interchangeable subassembly.

   **Usage:** broadly

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A replaceable valve assembly within a faucet or shower fixture that regulates the flow and temperature of the water.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; construction; manufacturing; plumbing

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences
