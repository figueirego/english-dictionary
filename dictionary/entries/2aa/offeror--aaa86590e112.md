### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who makes an offer to another; someone who presents something to another for acceptance or rejection.

   **Example:** A unilateral contract consists of a promise on the part of the offeror and performance of the requisite terms by the offeree.

   **Example:** Acceptance of the offer terminates the power of revocation that the offeror ordinarily has.
