## Verb

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To physically arrange a shop or workplace.

   **Example:** He set up shop in his garage every Saturday.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To establish a business.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** They set up shop as venture capitalists on Sand Hill Road.

   **Source antonyms:** close up shop

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To position oneself in a particular spot for some period of time.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** She set up shop on the stoop while she ate her croissant.

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** hang out one's shingle
