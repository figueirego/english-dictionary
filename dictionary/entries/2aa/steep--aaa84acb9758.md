### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of a near-vertical gradient; of a slope, surface, curve, etc. that proceeds upward or downward at an angle approaching vertical.

   **Example:** a steep hill or mountain; a steep roof; a steep ascent; a steep barometric gradient

   **Source synonyms:** brant; hilly

   **Source antonyms:** shallow; gentle; easy

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Expensive.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** Twenty quid for a shave? That's a bit steep.

   **Source synonyms:** costly; pricey; spendy; 5-star; big-ticket; chargeful (obsolete); cheap at half the price (humorous); costful (archaic); costly; cost-prohibitive (euphemistic); fancy; dear (Ireland; UK); dearthful (Scotland; obsolete); dispendious (archaic); ex (Singapore); expenny (slang); expensive; exy (Australia; slang); high-cost; lickpenny (obsolete); million-dollar (figuratively); high-priced; princely; out of sight; overdear; pricey; ruinous; spendy (US; regional); spenno; spenny (Australia; UK; informal); splurgy (US; slang); steep

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Difficult to access; not easy reached; lofty; elevated; high.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. resulting in a mast or windshield angle that strongly diverges from the perpendicular.

   **Usage:** or a car's windshield

   **Example:** The steep rake of the windshield enhances the fast lines of the exterior.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of the rake of a ship's mast
