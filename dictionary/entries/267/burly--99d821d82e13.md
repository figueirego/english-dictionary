### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Large, well-built, and muscular.

   **Usage:** usually

   **Example:** He’s a big, burly rugby player who works as a landscape gardener.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a man

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Great, amazing, unbelievable.

   **Usage:** UK; slang; East End of London

   **Example:** That goal was burly.

   **Example:** Kimi Räikkönen is a burly Formula 1 driver.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of large magnitude, either good or bad, and sometimes both.

   **Usage:** US; slang; surf culture and/or Southern California

   **Example:** That wave was burly! (i.e. large, dangerous and difficult to ride)

   **Example:** This hike is going to be burly, but worth it because there is good body surfing at that beach.
