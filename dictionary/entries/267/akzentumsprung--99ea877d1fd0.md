## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A type of development of certain diphthongs, especially in Anglo-Frisian, that was traditionally explained as the shifting of prominence from the first component of the diphthong to the second, that is, a change from a falling diphthong to a rising one.

   **Usage:** uncountable; Germanic studies
