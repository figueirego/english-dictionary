### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The male ruler of a barony.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A male member of the lowest rank of English nobility (the equivalent rank in Scotland is lord).

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A person of great power in society, especially in business and politics.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** railroad baron

   **Example:** tech baron

   **Source synonyms:** magnate; tycoon; baron; big cheese; big enchilada; big gun; big kahuna; big man; big name; big noise; big shot; bigwig; big wheel; boss; grand poobah; head honcho; heavy hitter; heavyweight; high muckamuck; high muckety-muck; king of all one surveys; king of the castle; king of the hill; kingpin; macher (US); mayor; muckamuck; muckety muck; [mugwump](../21e/mugwump--87abf9b5329f.md) (masculine; usually); numero uno; panjandrum; poobah; rooster; somebody; top banana; top boy; top cat; top dog; top gun; very important person; VIP; wheel

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A prisoner who gains power and influence by lending or selling goods such as tobacco.

   **Usage:** UK; slang

<a id="r1-s5"></a>
