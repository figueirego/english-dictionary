### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To invoke the Fifth Amendment to the United States Constitution, which protects witnesses from being forced to incriminate themselves.

   **Usage:** US

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To refuse to answer a question, or refuse to speak especially when the response would be embarrassing or uncomfortable.

   **Usage:** broadly; humorous; informal

   **Example:** Yo ain't no city in the world like this // An' if you ask how I know, I gots ta plead the fifth (1998, Miami (Will Smith song))

   **Source synonyms:** take the fifth
