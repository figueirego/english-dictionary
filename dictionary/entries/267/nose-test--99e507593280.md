### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An inspection of the nasal passages or a trial of their function, as for breathing difficulties or olfactory ability.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; physiology; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** smell test

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Synonym of sniff test.
   1. An inspection of an object by smelling it, as for cleanliness of clothing or freshness of food.

      **Source synonyms:** smell test

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Synonym of sniff test.

      **Usage:** figuratively

      **Source synonyms:** reality check; sanity check; sanity test
