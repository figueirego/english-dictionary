### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A shallow basin, sometimes ornamental, filled with water for wild birds to drink from or in which to bathe.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Random, inconsequential amounts of residual water on a roof membrane.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A light or superficial wash of the body, using a sink or similar.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Source synonyms:** bitch bath (vulgar); whore's bath (vulgar)
