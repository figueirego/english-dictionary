## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A bone that forms a knuckle in the human hand, in an animal's paw or any bone that forms a similar bump.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Such a bone once used in knucklebones and other children's games of chance.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A die.

   **Usage:** slang
