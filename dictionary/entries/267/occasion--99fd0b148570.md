### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A favorable opportunity; a convenient or timely chance.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** At this point, she seized the occasion to make her own observation.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The time when something happens.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** On this occasion, I'm going to decline your offer, but next time I might agree.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An occurrence or state of affairs which causes some event or reaction; a motive or reason.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** I had no occasion to feel offended, however.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Something which causes something else; a cause.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An occurrence or incident.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** event; happening; affair; befalling; episode; instance; event; goings-on (plural; plural-only); happening; hap (archaic; slang); incident; occasion (obsolete); occurrence; proceeding

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A particular happening; an instance or time when something occurred.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** I could think of two separate occasions when she had deliberately lied to me.

   **Example:** a momentous occasion in the history of South Africa

   **Source synonyms:** instant; point in time; instant; juncture; minute; moment; occasion; point in time; sith (obsolete); time

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A need; requirement, necessity.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** I have no occasion for firearms.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A special event or function.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Having people round for dinner was always quite an occasion at our house.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A reason or excuse; a motive; a persuasion.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
