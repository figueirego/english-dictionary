## Noun

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A craftsperson or company who makes and/or maintains watches.

   **Example:** The watchmakers of Switzerland were pioneers of screw machine technology.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. God in His role as the creator according to the watchmaker analogy.

   **Usage:** capitalized; often

   **Example:** We have the great watchmaker to thank for these glorious sunrises and sunsets.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; lifestyle; philosophy; religion; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** clockmaker
