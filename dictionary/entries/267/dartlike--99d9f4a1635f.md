### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Resembling a dart.
   1. Resembling the movement of a thrown dart&#58; swift and abrupt.

      **Example:** dartlike movements

      **Source synonyms:** darting

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Resembling a dart.
   1. Resembling a dart otherwise (for example, in shape and use).

      **Example:** dartlike projectiles
