### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The contents of an automobile (passengers, supplies, etc.) for one trip.

   **Example:** I brought a carload of flowers to her.

   **Example:** We brought a carload of kids and camping gear on our road trip out west.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The quantity of goods that can be carried in a freight car.

   **Usage:** Canada; US

   **Example:** less-than-carload freight

   **Example:** By 1887 the company had grown so large that it was buying its flour and sugar by the carload.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** rail-transport; railways; transport
