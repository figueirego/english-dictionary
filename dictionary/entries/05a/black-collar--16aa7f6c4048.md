## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of or pertaining to employment in the black market; that is, to engagement in illicit trade or distribution of untaxed goods and services.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; rare

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
