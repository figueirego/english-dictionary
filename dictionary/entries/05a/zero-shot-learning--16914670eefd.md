### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A problem setup in machine learning, where, at test time, a learner observes samples from classes that were not observed during training and attempts to predict the class that they belong to, typically based on auxiliary information.

   **Usage:** uncountable; machine learning
