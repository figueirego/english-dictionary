### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A small fold or ridge of tissue that supports or restrains the motion of the part to which it is attached, in particular a fold of skin beneath the tongue, or between the lip and the gum.

   **Example:** Humans have three oral frenulums&#58; beneath the tongue, between the upper lip and gum, and between the lower lip and gum.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; medicine; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** frenum

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A small fold or ridge of tissue that supports or restrains the motion of the part to which it is attached, in particular a fold of skin beneath the tongue, or between the lip and the gum.
   1. Such a ridge of tissue connecting the foreskin to the glans on the underside of the penis; the penile frenulum.

      **Usage:** specifically

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; medicine; sciences

      **Source synonyms:** frenum; fiddlestring

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A bristle or row of bristles on the edge of the hindwings of some lepidoptera which unites the wings by interlocking with a catch on the posterior part of the forewings.
