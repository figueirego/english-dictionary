## Noun

### Forms

- petrolheads — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who is overly reliant on the use of their car, resisting any suggestion to use other means of transport.

   **Usage:** colloquial

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A bogan who is overly fond of their car or motorbike and enjoys showing it off and making noise with it.

   **Usage:** Commonwealth; colloquial; derogatory

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A person involved in motor racing as either participant or dedicated spectator.

   **Usage:** Commonwealth; colloquial

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A car enthusiast.

   **Usage:** Ireland; UK; colloquial
