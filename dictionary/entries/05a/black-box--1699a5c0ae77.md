### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A flight recorder; the brightly colored cockpit voice recorders and flight data recorders in an aircraft designed to aid in determining the cause of an accident.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** scrutinizing all utterances recorded by the black box

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** aeronautics; aerospace; aviation; business; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

   **Source synonyms:** event recorder; opaque box; trip recorder

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any similar device on motor vehicles, such as rail event recorders and ship trip recorders.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** the pilothouse's analogue of an airliner's black box

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** transport

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A theoretical construct or device with known input and output characteristics but unknown method of operation.

   **Example:** The authentication method and its details can be treated as a black box for purposes of this security architecture overview.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A device used in phreaking that prevents the calling party from being billed for the call placed.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A type of theater characterized by a lack of decoration or complex mechanisms.

   **Usage:** attributive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** broadcasting; drama; dramaturgy; entertainment; film; lifestyle; media; television; theater

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Synonym of football (“the leather briefcase containing classified nuclear war plans which is always near the US President”)

   **Source synonyms:** football (synonym; synonym-of)
