### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Expresses the prospective aspect relative to a given time frame&#58; something that will happen, or is intended, at the time, to happen.

   **Example:** I’m going to throw out the milk if nobody’s going to drink it.

   **Example:** I was going to cut the grass, but it started raining.

   **Example:** Mary is going to have another baby. It’ll most probably have blue eyes, because both parents do.

   **Example:** I’ve already agreed on a price, so I’m going to buy it regardless.

   **Example:** I’ve been going to stop by for ages, but I’ve only just found time.

   **Example:** I have been told about a row house going to be available soon.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see be, going, to.

   **Example:** It's getting late; I must be going to my grandma's.
