### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A photograph, especially one taken quickly or in a sudden moment of opportunity.

   **Example:** He carried a snapshot of his daughter.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A glimpse of something; a portrayal of something at a moment in time.

   **Example:** The article offered a snapshot of life in that region.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A file or set of files captured at a particular time, often capable of being reloaded to restore the earlier state.

   **Example:** This game is so hard that I find myself taking a snapshot every few seconds in case I get killed.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences
