## Prep\_phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Working at one's job; occupied in some manner during one's hours of remunerated employment.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Source synonyms:** on the job

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. During one's official working hours; in or into a position of remunerated employment.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of a taxicab, engaged for hire; displayed numerically as time or fare on the meter of a taxicab.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. In the official time remaining in a game or other sporting event.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. In the official time expired in a game or other sporting event.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Under scrutiny due to having to make a decision or produce results within a set period of time.

   **Usage:** chiefly sports drafts

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Displayed numerically on the mileage or kilometric gauge.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a motor vehicle

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Remunerated per unit of time.

   **Example:** Alas, the delivery boy is on the clock; he could and would work much faster on commission!

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see on, the, clock (displayed on the dial of a clock).
