**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To repeat aloud (some passage, poem or other text previously memorized, or in front of one's eyes), often before an audience.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Janice is able to recite pi to 100 decimals.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

   **Usage:** transitive
