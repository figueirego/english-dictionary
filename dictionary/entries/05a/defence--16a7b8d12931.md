## Noun · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /dɪˈfɛns/ — source labels: Received-Pronunciation

### Forms

- defense — alternative; US

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The action of defending, of protecting from attack, danger or injury.

   **Usage:** UK; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Something used to oppose attacks.

   **Usage:** UK; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An argument in support or justification of something.

   **Usage:** UK; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A strategy and tactics employed to prevent the other team from scoring; contrasted with offence.

   **Usage:** UK; countable; uncountable; team sports

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The portion of a team dedicated to preventing the other team from scoring; contrasted with offence.

   **Usage:** UK; countable; uncountable; team sports

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Government policy or (infra)structure related to the military.

   **Usage:** UK; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Department of Defence

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Prohibition; a prohibitory ordinance.

   **Usage:** UK; countable; obsolete; uncountable
