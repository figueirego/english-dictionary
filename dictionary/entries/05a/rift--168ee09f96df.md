**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A chasm or fissure.

   **Example:** The Grand Canyon is a rift in the Earth's surface, but is smaller than some of the undersea ones.

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A lack of cohesion; a state of conflict, incompatibility, or emotional distance.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** My marriage is in trouble&#58; the fight created a rift between us and we can't reconnect.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A break in the clouds, fog, mist etc., which allows light through.
