### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person, especially a man or a woman, from an urban neighborhood who overtly exhibits attributes associated with inner city life, especially in both behavior and appearance.

   **Usage:** derogatory; slang; African-American Vernacular

   **Example:** Let's get a couple of hood rats over for the party tonight.
