## Adjective · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈtɛkst.bʊk/ — source labels: Canada; Received-Pronunciation; US
- IPA: /ˈtekst.bʊk/ — source labels: Australia; New-Zealand

### Forms

- more textbook — comparative
- most textbook — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Of or pertaining to textbooks or their style, especially in being dry and pedagogical; textbooky, textbooklike.

   **Usage:** literally

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Having the typical characteristics of some class of phenomenon, so that it might be included as an example in a textbook.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** textbook case

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. Done exactly correctly, in an exemplary way that might be described in a textbook.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** Well done everyone, the tree fell exactly where we planned. That was textbook.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. Learned from, or as if learned from, a textbook, as opposed to personal discovery or experience.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** He has a textbook understanding of company law but no practical experience of litigation.
