**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To cut short the tail of (an animal).

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

   **Example:** Curtailing horses procured long horse-hair.

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To shorten or abridge the duration of; to bring an end to; to truncate.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** When the audience grew restless, the speaker curtailed her speech.
