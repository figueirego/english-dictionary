## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A drink combining coconut water (or coconut milk), condensed milk (or evaporated milk), and alcohol, especially gin.

   **Usage:** Bahamas; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** gully wash

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** Jamaica; uncountable
