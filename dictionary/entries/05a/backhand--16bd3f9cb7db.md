## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈbækhænd/ — source labels: UK; US

### Forms

- backhands — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A stroke made across the chest from the off-hand side to the racquet hand side; a stroke during which the back of the hand faces the shot.

   **Usage:** tennis and other

   **Example:** She has a mean backhand.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; games; hobbies; lifestyle; racquet-sports; sports

   **Source antonyms:** forehand

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Handwriting that leans to the left.

   **Example:** It was written in a shaky backhand.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The standard throw; a throw during which the disc begins on the off-hand side and travels across the chest to be released from the opposite side.

   **Usage:** Ultimate Frisbee

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The back of the hand&#58; the reverse side (dorsal side) of the human hand or other primate hand.

   **Source antonyms:** palm

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The hand towards the back of the board.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports; surfing

   **Source antonyms:** forehand
