### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Without use or the possibility to be used.

   **Example:** This fork's prongs are bent. It's useless now.

   **Source synonyms:** unusable; unutilizable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Unhelpful, not useful; pointless (of an action).

   **Example:** I think it's useless to keep this discussion going. It's like talking to a brick wall.

   **Example:** I tried my best to make him quit smoking, but my efforts were useless. He now smokes six packs a day.

   **Source synonyms:** [void](../111/void--445be54d48a2.md)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Good-for-nothing; not dependable.

   **Usage:** derogatory

   **Example:** Bill never mows the lawn, takes out the trash or anything. He's useless, but I love him anyways.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a person

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Of a person, Unable to do well at a particular task or endeavor.

   **Usage:** colloquial; derogatory

   **Example:** My brother is useless at most computer games, but he is an awesome PS2 player.

   **Example:** Why do you keep trying? You're obviously useless at it.

### Related words
