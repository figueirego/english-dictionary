## Noun

### Forms

- big words — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An erudite word, especially a Latinate or multisyllabic one; a word used by scholars, intellectuals, etc., but that is not commonly known outside of academia.

   **Example:** The customer complained that the instructions were incomprehensible, and our office tried to provide remedial help, but when they complained that “supplementary” and “atmospheric” were big words, we knew that they were beyond help.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Boastful or exaggerated statements.

   **Usage:** in-plural

   **Example:** big words from a little man

   **Source synonyms:** big talk
