### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of or relating to sight; visual.

   **Example:** Strabismus is an optical defect.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Designed to assist or enhance sight

   **Example:** A microscope is an optical instrument.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

3. Of or relating to optics.

   **Example:** Refraction is an optical effect.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Of or relating to visible light.

   **Example:** Optical telescopes don't work when it is cloudy.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Incorporating light-sensitive devices.

   **Example:** An optical switch opens the door automatically.
