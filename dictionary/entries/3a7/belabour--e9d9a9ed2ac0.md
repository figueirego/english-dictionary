### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To labour about; labour over; to work hard upon; to ply diligently.

   **Usage:** UK; obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To beat or thump (someone) soundly.

   **Usage:** UK; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** buffet; thrash

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To attack (someone) verbally.

   **Usage:** UK; transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To discuss or explain (something) excessively or repeatedly; to harp on or overelaborate.

   **Usage:** UK; transitive
