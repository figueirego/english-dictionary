### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Acronym of Fas-associated protein with death domain, an adaptor protein that bridges members of the tumor necrosis factor receptor superfamily, such as the Fas-receptor, to procaspases 8 and 10, to form the death-inducing signaling complex (DISC) during apoptosis.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; acronym; alt-of; uncountable
