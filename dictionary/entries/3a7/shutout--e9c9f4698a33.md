### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Closing and forbidding entry, as a lockout in which management prevents works from working.

   **Example:** A shutout is a reverse strike&#58; the union complained and the workers wanted to work, but management was opposed.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A game that ends with the losing side being held scoreless.

   **Example:** The score wasn't just lopsided&#58; it was a shutout.
