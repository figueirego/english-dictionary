## Phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Indicating that one doubts that a question is being asked in seriousness or good faith, but is willing to respond in earnest.

   **Usage:** colloquial

   **Example:** —Why did the chicken cross the road? —OK, I'll bite. Why did it cross the road?

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Indicating that one is willing to engage with a potentially difficult or inconvenient situation.

   **Usage:** colloquial

   **Example:** This isn't usually my type of movie, but I'll bite.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see I'll, bite.
