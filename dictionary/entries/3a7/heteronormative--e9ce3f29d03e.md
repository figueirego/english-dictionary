### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of or relating to the institutions and practices that privilege or value heterosexuality, heterosexual relationships, and traditional gender roles as fundamental and "natural" within society.

   **Example:** Much of the language used when discussing wedding planning is heteronormative, which can alienate homosexual couples.

   **Source antonyms:** homonormative; nonheteronormative
