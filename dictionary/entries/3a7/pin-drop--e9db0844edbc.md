## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

- pin-drops — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The sound of a small metal pin dropping onto the ground or a floor; notable because a falling pin makes almost no sound when it hits the ground except for when a setting is so quiet that even the smallest, negligible noise would be noticeable.

   **Usage:** figuratively; idiomatic

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A small increment or amount.
