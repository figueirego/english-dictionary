## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The time of day when the sun appears above the eastern horizon.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** I'll meet you at the docks at sunrise.

   **Source synonyms:** sunup; daybreak; sparrow-fart; ass crack of dawn (Canada; US; vulgar); break; break of dawn; break of day; butt crack of dawn (Canada; US); cockcrow; crack of dawn (idiomatic); dawn; daybreak; daylight; dayspring (archaic); first light; lauds (historical); solrise (rare); sparrow-fart (Australia; New-Zealand; UK; slang); sun (literary); sunlight; sunrise; sunrising (obsolete); sunup (US)

   **Source antonyms:** sunset; sundown; cockshut (obsolete); crepusculum; dark; dimmity; duckish (Canada); dusk; evenfall; mirkning (Scotland); night; nightfall; sun (literary); sundown (US); sunset; vespers (historical)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The change in color of the sky at dawn.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Did you see the beautiful sunrise this morning?

   **Source antonyms:** sunset

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any great awakening.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

   **Example:** It was the sunrise of her spirit.

   **Source antonyms:** sunset
