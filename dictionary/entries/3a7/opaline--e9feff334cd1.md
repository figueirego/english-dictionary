## Noun · Record 2

### Forms

- opalines — plural

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A clear to white liquid secreted by sea hares (genus Aplysia) that becomes viscous upon contact with water.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The attack on a sea hare by a spiny lobster causes release of the whitish opaline, followed the co-release of both opaline and ink.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Any of several minerals resembling opal, including a variety of yellow chalcedony.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; geography; geology; mineralogy; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. Synonym of opal glass.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
