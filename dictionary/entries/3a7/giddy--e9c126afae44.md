### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Feeling a sense of spinning in the head, causing a perception of unsteadiness and being about to fall down; dizzy.

   **Usage:** predicative

   **Example:** The man became giddy upon standing up so fast.

   **Source synonyms:** fuzzy; fuzzy-headed; fuzzy-minded; light-headed; rattleheaded; shoogly (Scotland); vertiginate; westy (Britain; dialectal); woozy

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Causing or likely to cause dizziness or a feeling of unsteadiness.

   **Usage:** attributive

   **Example:** They climbed to a giddy height.

   **Source synonyms:** vertiginous

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Moving around something or spinning rapidly.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Unable to concentrate or think seriously; easily excited; impulsive; also, lightheartedly silly; frivolous.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Source synonyms:** brainsick (obsolete); changeable; feather-headed (informal); flighty; giglot (obsolete); inconsistent; light-headed; overlight (dated)

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Unable to concentrate or think seriously; easily excited; impulsive; also, lightheartedly silly; frivolous.
   1. Used as an intensifier.

      **Usage:** broadly; dated

      **Source synonyms:** brainsick (obsolete); changeable; feather-headed (informal); flighty; giglot (obsolete); inconsistent; light-headed; overlight (dated)

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Joyfully elated; overcome with excitement or happiness.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** The boy was giddy when he opened his birthday presents.

   **Source synonyms:** ecstatic; joyous; air-headed; blissed out; blissful; enrapt; rapt; cock-a-hoop; delighted; ecstatic; elated; euphoric; exultant; filled with happiness; giddy; gladsome; gleeful; greatly pleased; joyful; joyous; lighthearted; orgasmic; on cloud nine; over the moon; overjoyed; thrilled; walking on air

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Feeling great anger; furious, raging.

   **Usage:** UK; broadly; dialectal

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Of an animal, chiefly a sheep&#58; affected by gid (“a disease caused by parasitic infestation of the brain by tapeworm larvae”), which may result in the animal turning around aimlessly.

   **Usage:** UK; dialectal

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** agriculture; biology; business; lifestyle; medicine; natural-sciences; pathology; sciences; veterinary; zoology

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

   **Usage:** figuratively; obsolete
