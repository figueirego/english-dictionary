### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To repress.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The dictatorial state keeps down its dissidents very efficiently.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To restrain or control (a sound).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** We must keep the noise down, or the neighbours will complain.

   **Example:** Keep it down in there! Some of us are trying to sleep!

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To cause not to increase or rise.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** It is essential to keep the numbers down to avoid overcrowding.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To retain in the stomach without vomiting.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** It is difficult to keep anything down when you have the flu.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To lie low; to stay concealed by not standing up.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** You had better keep down or they will see you.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To refrain from using capital letters more than absolutely necessary.

   **Usage:** obsolete

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** media; printing; publishing

   **Source antonyms:** keep up
