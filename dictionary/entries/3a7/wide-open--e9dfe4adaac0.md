### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Completely open.

   **Example:** The front door was wide open.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Having no laws or law enforcement.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** It was a wide open frontier town before the railroad came.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Unsettled or unresolved.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** The fate of the loan sailor is still wide open.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Vulnerable or unprotected.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** He left himself wide open to criticism.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Without the presence of opponents nearby.

   **Example:** He was wide open and scored easily after receiving the pass.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Of the situation at the beginning of trading when there is a large spread between bids and asking prices.
