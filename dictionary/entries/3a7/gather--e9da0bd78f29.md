### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To collect normally separate things.

   **Example:** I've been gathering ideas from the people I work with.

   **Example:** She bent down to gather the reluctant cat from beneath the chair.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To collect normally separate things.
   1. Especially, to harvest food.

      **Example:** We went to gather some blackberries from the nearby lane.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To collect normally separate things.
   1. To accumulate over time, to amass little by little.

      **Example:** Over the years he'd gathered a considerable collection of mugs.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To collect normally separate things.
   1. To congregate, or assemble.

      **Usage:** intransitive

      **Example:** People gathered round as he began to tell his story.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To collect normally separate things.
   1. To grow gradually larger by accretion.

      **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To bring parts of a whole closer.

   **Example:** She gathered the shawl about her as she stepped into the cold.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To bring parts of a whole closer.
   1. To add pleats or folds to a piece of cloth, normally to reduce its width.

      **Example:** A gown should be gathered around the top so that it will remain shaped.

**Source sense R1.S8**

   1. To bring stitches closer together.

      **Example:** Be careful not to stretch or gather your knitting.

      **Example:** If you want to emphasise the shape, it is possible to gather the waistline.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; knitting; manufacturing; textiles

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To bring parts of a whole closer.
   1. To bring together, or nearer together, in masonry, as for example where the width of a fireplace is rapidly diminished to the width of the flue.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** architecture

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

   1. To haul in; to take up.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport
