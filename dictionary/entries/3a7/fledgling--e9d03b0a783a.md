### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A young bird which has just developed its flight feathers (notably wings).

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. An insect that has just fledged, i.e. undergone its final moult to become an adult or imago.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**
