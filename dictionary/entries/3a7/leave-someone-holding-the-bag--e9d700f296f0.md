### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To abandon somebody, leaving him or her holding the responsibility or blame.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** After the accident, he just vanished and left me holding the bag.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To remove the value from an article or arrangement and leave somebody holding the empty (or valueless) container.

   **Usage:** figuratively
