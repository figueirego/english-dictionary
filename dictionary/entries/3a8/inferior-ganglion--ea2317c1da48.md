### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The lower and larger of the two sensory ganglia of the glossopharyngeal nerve.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; medicine; neuroanatomy; neurology; neuroscience; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** petrosal ganglion; petrous ganglion

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The lower of the two ganglia of the vagus nerve that forms a swelling just beyond the exit of the nerve from the jugular foramen.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; medicine; neuroanatomy; neurology; neuroscience; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** inferior vagal ganglion; nodose ganglion
