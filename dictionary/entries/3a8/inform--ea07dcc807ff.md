### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To instruct, train (usually in matters of knowledge).

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To communicate knowledge to.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To impart information or knowledge.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To act as an informer; denounce.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To give form or character to; to inspire (with a given quality); to affect, influence (with a pervading principle, idea etc.).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** His sense of religion informs everything he writes.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To make known, wisely and/or knowledgeably.

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To direct, guide.

   **Usage:** formal; transitive

   **Example:** Don’t forget the code of ethics that informs this profession.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To take form; to become visible or manifest; to appear.

   **Usage:** archaic; intransitive
