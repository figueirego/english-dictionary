### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A duty which one owes and ought to perform on the basis of considerations of right and wrong, but which he is not legally bound to fulfill, as a duty to be charitable, a duty to pay a debt barred by the act of limitations, etc.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law
