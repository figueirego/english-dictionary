## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

- whole cloths — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A newly made textile which has not yet been cut.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; manufacturing; textiles

   **Source synonyms:** broadcloth

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The fictitious material from which complete fabrications, lies with no basis in truth, are made.

   **Usage:** figuratively; uncountable; used attributively or preceded by various prepositions

   **Example:** Mr. Doe's account of the accident was made from whole cloth.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Something made completely new, with no history, and not based on anything else.

   **Usage:** figuratively; uncountable

   **Example:** The plans for the widget were drawn from whole cloth.
