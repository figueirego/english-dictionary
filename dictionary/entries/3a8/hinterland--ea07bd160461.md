### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The land immediately next to, and inland from, a coast.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The rural territory surrounding an urban area, especially a port.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A remote or undeveloped area.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** shadow

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** interior; backcountry
