### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A futile search, a fruitless errand; a useless and often lengthy task whose execution is inordinately complex relative to the value of the outcome.

   **Usage:** figuratively; idiomatic

   **Example:** I went on a wild-goose chase all over the town looking for that adapter until I discovered they no longer make them.

   **Example:** Diagnosing this software application's problems is a wild-goose chase because it is built in an environment that has poor debugging tools.

   **Source synonyms:** merry dance; exercise in futility; fool's errand; snipe hunt; snark hunt
