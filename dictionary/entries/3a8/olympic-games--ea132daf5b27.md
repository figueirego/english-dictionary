## Proper noun

### Forms

- the Olympic Games — canonical; plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An international multi-sport event (inspired by the ancient festival) taking place every fourth year.

   **Usage:** plural; plural-only

   **Example:** Every athlete's dream is to compete at the Olympic Games.

   **Source synonyms:** Olympics; Games\[Appendix&#58;Glossary

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A sporting festival held every four or five years on the Plain of Olympia in southern Greece, in honour of Zeus; these games were the inspiration for the modern version, which began in the 1890s as a revival of the Ancient Greek ones.

   **Usage:** historical; plural; plural-only; Ancient Greece

   **Example:** The first Olympic Games are traditionally dated to 776 BC.

   **Source synonyms:** Olympics
