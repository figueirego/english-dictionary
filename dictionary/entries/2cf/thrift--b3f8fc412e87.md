## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The characteristic of using a minimum of something (especially money).

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** His thrift can be seen in how little the trashman takes from his house.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A savings bank.

   **Usage:** US; countable

   **Example:** Usually, home mortgages are obtained from thrifts.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of various plants of the genus Armeria, particularly Armeria maritima.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Success and advance in the acquisition of property; increase of worldly goods; gain; prosperity; profit.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Vigorous growth, as of a plant.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** frugality — characteristic of using a minimum of something
