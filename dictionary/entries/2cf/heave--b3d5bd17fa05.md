### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. An effort to raise something, such as a weight or one's own body, or to move something heavy.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** We gave one more heave and the wall toppled over at last.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. An upward motion; a rising; a swell or distention, as of the breast in difficult breathing, of the waves, of the earth in an earthquake, etc.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. A horizontal dislocation in a metallic lode, taking place at an intersection with another lode.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. The measure of extent to which a nautical vessel goes up and down in a short period of time.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport
