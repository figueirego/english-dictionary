### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r2-s3"></a>
