### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Calling attention; flashy; standing out to the eye.

   **Usage:** derogatory; sometimes

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Making a striking or aesthetically pleasing display.

   **Example:** showy flowers

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Inclined to showing (by conveying an impression so that events are described in the narrative), instead of telling (by explicitly stating facts).

   **Usage:** informal

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a literary work
