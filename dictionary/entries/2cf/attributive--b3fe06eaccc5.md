**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Modifying another word, typically a noun, while in the same phrase.

   **Example:** In "this big house", big is attributive, whereas in "this house is big", it is predicative.

   **Example:** In "this tiger is a man-eater," man is an attributive noun.
