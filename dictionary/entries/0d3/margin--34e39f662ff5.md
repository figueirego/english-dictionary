   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A difference or ratio between results, characteristics, scores.

   **Example:** margin of victory

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A permissible difference; allowing some freedom to move within limits.

   **Example:** margin of error

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance

**Source sense R1.S8**
