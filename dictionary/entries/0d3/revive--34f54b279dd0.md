### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To cause (a person or animal) to recover from a faint; to cause (a person or animal) to return to a state of consciousness.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Her grandmother said that if she lost consciousness, she would not want to be revived.

   **Example:** The dying puppy was revived by a soft hand.

   **Source synonyms:** rescue

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To bring (a person or animal which is dead) back to life.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** reanimate; resurrect

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To cause (something) to recover from a state of decline, neglect, oblivion, or obscurity; to make (something) active or lively again; to reanimate, to revitalize.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** The Manx language has been revived after dying out, and is now taught in some schools on the Isle of Man.

   **Example:** This new paint job should revive the surgery waiting room.

   **Source synonyms:** reestablish; refresh; reinvigorate; renew; revivify; rally
