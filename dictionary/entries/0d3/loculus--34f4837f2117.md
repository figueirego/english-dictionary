## Noun

### Forms

- loculi — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A little place or space; a cell; a chamberlet.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In ancient catacombs and tombs of some types, a small separate chamber or recess cut into the rock, for the reception of a body or urn.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. One of the spaces between the septa in the Anthozoa.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences; zoology

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. One of the compartments of a several-celled ovary.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences
