### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The monumental portal at the Ottoman palace where the supreme tribunals were held; became the surname of the first palace in Bursa, transferred to the even grander Topkapı palace after the capital's transfer to Istanbul (the former Byzantine imperial capital Constantinople)

   **Usage:** literally

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The Ottoman sultanic court, hence the imperial government, notably the vizierial divan and the entire empire, or a personage with a similar standing.

   **Usage:** figuratively
