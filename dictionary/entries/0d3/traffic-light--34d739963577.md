### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A signalling device positioned at a road intersection or pedestrian crossing indicating the moment it is safe to drive, ride or walk, using a universal color code.

   **Source synonyms:** stop light; traffic signal; robot; robot policeman

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A signalling device positioned at a road intersection or pedestrian crossing indicating the moment it is safe to drive, ride or walk, using a universal color code.
   1. A road intersection controlled by one or more such devices.

      **Usage:** plural-normally

      **Example:** How do I get to the hospital? ~ You go straight on to the traffic lights, then turn left at the traffic lights and the hospital is on your right.

      **Source synonyms:** stop light; traffic signal; robot; robot policeman

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A colour code using the three signals of a traffic light (green, yellow or amber, and red) as a metaphor.

   **Example:** The Department of Health recommends that food packaging display the recommended daily intake with a traffic light system, for ease of understanding.

   **Source synonyms:** lights; signals
