## Adjective

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of a word's sound or of a language, without any stress (accent).

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of a person's speech, not pronounced with a distinctive accent.

   **Example:** The Frenchman spoke clear, unaccented English.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of a written word, having no accent marks.

   **Source synonyms:** accentless

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Of a written word, having no diacritical marks of any kind.

   **Source synonyms:** accentless; diacriticless; undiacritized
