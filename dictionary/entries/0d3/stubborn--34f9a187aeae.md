### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Refusing to move or to change one's opinion; obstinate; firmly resisting; persistent in doing something.

   **Example:** People are pretty stubborn about their political beliefs, so why bother arguing?

   **Example:** Blood can make a very stubborn stain on fabrics if not washed properly.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Physically stiff and inflexible; not easily melted or worked.
