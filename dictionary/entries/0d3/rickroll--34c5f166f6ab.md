### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To mislead (someone) into following a seemingly innocuous hyperlink, or sometimes a QR code, that leads to a YouTube video of Rick Astley's song "Never Gonna Give You Up".

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I try not to get rickrolled by sketchy YouTube links, but sometimes you let your guard down.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To surprise or trick (someone) into hearing this song.

   **Usage:** broadly; transitive

   **Example:** The DJ rickrolled the whole audience by suddenly playing Rick Astley’s hit song.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To cause (someone) to unexpectedly engage with this song through an indirect method, such as through steganography, sheet music, or by inserting the lyrics into an unrelated context.

   **Usage:** broadly; transitive

   **Example:** She inserted the lyrics of “Never Gonna Give You Up” into her speech so casually that most listeners didn’t even realize they’d been rickrolled.
