### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Pertaining to trees with relatively broad, flat leaves as opposed to needles, or woodlands made up of such trees.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used in the name of a plant or tree&#58; having especially broad leaves.

   **Usage:** not-comparable
