### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of or pertaining to. Adjectival suffix appended to various words, often nouns, to produce an adjective form. Often added to words of Latin origin, but used with other words also.

   **Usage:** morpheme

   **Example:** devolution + -ary → devolutionary

   **Source synonyms:** -al; -ar; -ese; -ic; -id; -ish; -like; -oid; -ory; -ous; -y

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Ending of some substantives borrowed or inherited from Latin and French.

   **Usage:** morpheme

   **Example:** dictionary, plenipotentiary

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Having the specified arity.

   **Usage:** morpheme

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** -adic
