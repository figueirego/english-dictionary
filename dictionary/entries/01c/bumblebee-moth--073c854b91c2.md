### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any member of any of several species of moth that somewhat resemble bumblebees in coloration.
   1. Abraxas grossulariata (magpie moth).

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any member of any of several species of moth that somewhat resemble bumblebees in coloration.
   1. Aphomia sociella (bee moth).

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any member of any of several species of moth that somewhat resemble bumblebees in coloration.
   1. Hemaris diffinis (syn. Haemorrhagia diffinis, snowberry clearwing).

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any member of any of several species of moth that somewhat resemble bumblebees in coloration.
   1. Hemaris thysbe (hummingbird clearwing).
