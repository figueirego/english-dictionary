### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To sunbathe.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Source synonyms:** catch some rays; catch some sun

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To become sunburned.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To reflect light from the sun.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** A coin on the ground caught the sun and I stooped to pick it up.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** The sunflowers will catch the sun if they are planted there.
