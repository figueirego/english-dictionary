### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who or thing that collects, or which creates or manages a collection.

   **Example:** She is an avid collector of nineteenth-century postage stamps.

   **Example:** That old piano is just a big dust collector.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person who is employed to collect payments.

   **Example:** She works for the government as a tax collector.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A person who is employed to collect payments.
   1. A mafioso whose task is to collect protection money from small businesses

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The amplified terminal on a bipolar junction transistor.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; electrical-engineering; electricity; electromagnetism; electronics; energy; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A compiler of books; one who collects scattered passages and puts them together in one book.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. One holding a Bachelor of Arts in Oxford, formerly appointed to superintend some scholastic proceedings in Lent.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A major sewer which collects sewerage from a number of smaller branch sewers.
