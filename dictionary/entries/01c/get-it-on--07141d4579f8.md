### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see get, it, on.

   **Example:** Where's your coat? Get it on and let's get out of here.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To have sex.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Example:** I can see the sparks between us. Let's get it on, baby!

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To engage in a fight.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Example:** You want a piece of me? OK, let's get it on!.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To hurry up; to get a move on; to start.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I need to get it on&#58; there's not much time left.
