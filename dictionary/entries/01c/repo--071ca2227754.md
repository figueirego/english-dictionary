   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; clipping; countable; informal; uncountable

   **Example:** Can you do a repo tomorrow? It's an SUV in Springfield.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; clipping; countable; informal; uncountable

   **Example:** That cruise ship starts its repo tomorrow; it's going to the Caribbean for the winter.

   **Alternative form of:** [repositioning](../339/repositioning--ce48322ecfd8.md)
