### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Referring to various species of flowers.
   1. Tragopogon, a type of aster that closes at midday.

      **Source synonyms:** goat's-beard; Jack-go-to-bed-at-noon; salsify

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Referring to various species of flowers.
   1. Flowers of the family Aizoaceae, especially those of the genus Carpobrotus.

      **Source synonyms:** ice plant; pigface
