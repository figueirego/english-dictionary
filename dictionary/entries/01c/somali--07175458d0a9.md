## Noun · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /səˈmɑːli/ — source labels: UK
- IPA: /soʊˈmɑːli/ — source labels: US
- IPA: /səˈmɑːli/ — source labels: US

### Forms

- Somali — plural
- Somalis — plural

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. An ethnic group native to the Somali Peninsula; a Somali, a group of Somalis, the Somalis.

   **Usage:** collective; countable

   **Example:** Historically, Somalis were a nomadic people herding camels, goats, and sheep. Nowadays, many if not most Somalis are urbanized people.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. The Somali ethnic group as a whole; the Somali.

   **Usage:** collective; in-plural; uncountable

   **Example:** The Somali are an ethnic group of Cushitic origin indigenous to the Somali Peninsula. Their language is called Somali.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. A citizen or resident from the country of Somalia.

   **Usage:** collective; countable

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. A person from a Somali-majority republic, state, or region, including but not limited to&#58; the Republic of Somaliland, the Somali region of Ethiopia (often called the Ogaden, but more neutrally the Somali region), or autonomous states within Somalia's federal system such as Puntland.

   **Usage:** collective; countable

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. A domestic cat breed derived from the Abyssinian cat.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** fox cat; long-haired Abyssinian
