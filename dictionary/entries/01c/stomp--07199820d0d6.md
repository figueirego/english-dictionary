### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A deliberate heavy footfall; a stamp.

   **Usage:** countable; informal

   **Example:** She obliterated the cockroach with one stomp.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Any of various dances incorporating repeated heavy, rhythmic steps.

   **Usage:** countable; informal; uncountable

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. A style of jazz music for stomp dances of the early twentieth century.

   **Usage:** informal; uncountable

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. A piece of music in this style.

   **Usage:** countable; informal

   **Example:** King Porter Stomp

   **Usage:** countable; dated; informal; slang
