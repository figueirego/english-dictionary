### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act or condition of prostrating oneself (lying face-down), as a sign of humility.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A part of the ordination of Catholic and Orthodox priests, in which the ordinand lies prostrate in a gesture of submission to the will of God.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The ordination ceremony includes a variety of rituals, rich in meaning and history, e.g., the prostration, laying on of hands, anointing of hands, giving of the chalice and paten, and sign of peace. — Diocese of Rochester, NY

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The state of lying face down (prone).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The condition of being prostrated, as from heat; complete loss of strength.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A reverential bow performed in Middle Eastern cultures.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** sujud
