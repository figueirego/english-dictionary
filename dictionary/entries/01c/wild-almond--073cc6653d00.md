### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see wild, almond.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A wild-growing almond tree (Prunus amygdalus, syn. Prunus dulcis), native to the Middle East and South Asia.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A tree of any of several species, similar in fruit to P. amygdalus
   1. Irvingia malayana, native to southeast Asia

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A tree of any of several species, similar in fruit to P. amygdalus
   1. Brabejum stellatifolium, native to South Africa

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A tree of any of several species, similar in fruit to P. amygdalus
   1. Prunus fasciculata, native to the southwestern United States

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A tree of any of several species, similar in fruit to P. amygdalus
   1. Prunus turneriana, native to Papua New Guinea and Australia
