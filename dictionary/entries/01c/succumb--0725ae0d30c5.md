### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To yield to an overpowering force or overwhelming desire.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** succumb to temptation

   **Example:** succumb under misfortunes

   **Example:** Thai culture as in many other Asian cultures, is succumbing to the influence of westernization.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To give up, or give in.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To die.

   **Usage:** euphemistic; intransitive

   **Example:** succumb to pneumonia
