<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Synonym of lusitanize, to make Portuguese or more Portuguese-like.

   **Usage:** rare; transitive

   **Example:** Traders and governors attempted to Portuguesify Goa and Macao for centuries... You can Portuguesify Castilian Spanish easily enough...

   **Source synonyms:** lusitanize (synonym; synonym-of); to make Portuguese or more Portuguese-like (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Synonym of lusitanize, to become Portuguese or more Portuguese-like.

   **Usage:** intransitive; rare

   **Example:** Their sandwiches are portuguesified by frying the meat with copious garlic and covering everything with thick slabs of red pepper paste.

   **Source synonyms:** lusitanize (synonym; synonym-of); to become Portuguese or more Portuguese-like (synonym; synonym-of)
