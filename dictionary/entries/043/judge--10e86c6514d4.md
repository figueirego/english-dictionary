### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A public official whose duty it is to administer the law, especially by presiding over trials and rendering judgments; a justice.

   **Source synonyms:** magistrate (usually); **Source context (verbatim):** of low rank; justice (usually); **Source context (verbatim):** of high rank; justiciar; justiciary (historic); **Source context (verbatim):** of high rank; chief justice; Chief Justiciar; Capital Justiciary; Chief Justiciary; justiciar; justiciary (obsolete); justicer; sheriff (historic; obsolete); bailiff (obsolete); reeve; doomsman

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person who decides the fate of someone or something that has been called into question.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A person officiating at a sports event, a contest, or similar; referee.

   **Example:** At a boxing match, the decision of the judges is final.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A person who evaluates something or forms an opinion.

   **Example:** She is a good judge of wine.

   **Example:** They say he is a poor judge of character considering all the unreliable friends he has made.

   **Source synonyms:** deemer; deemster

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A shophet, a temporary leader appointed in times of crisis in ancient Israel.

   **Usage:** historical

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biblical; lifestyle; religion

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** doomsman (archaic); judg (obsolete); judge; justice; justicer (archaic); justiciar (historical); justiciary (historical)

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To sit in judgment on; to pass sentence on (a person or matter).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** A higher power will judge you after you are dead.

   **Source synonyms:** rule on; try; adjudge; adjudicate; arbitrate; deem (obsolete); doom (obsolete); hear; judge; rule on; settle; try

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To sit in judgment, to act as judge.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Justices in this country judge without appeal.

   **Source synonyms:** adjudicate; make a decision; choose up (idiomatic); decide; determine; resolve; find; judge; posken (Judaism); make a decision; make up one's mind (idiomatic); take a decision; work out; adjudicate; judge; sit in judgement

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To judicially rule or determine.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To sentence to punishment, to judicially condemn.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

   **Example:** He was judged to die for his crimes.

   **Source synonyms:** deem; sentence; adjudge; convict; condemn; damn; deem (obsolete); doom; judge (obsolete); sentence

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To award judicially; to adjudge.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To form an opinion on; to appraise.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I judge a man’s character by the cut of his suit.

   **Source synonyms:** evaluate; rate; appraise; apprise (archaic); apprize (archaic); assess; esteem (dated); evaluate; judge; price; prize (obsolete); rate; valuate; value; weigh (figuratively); worthen

**Source sense R2.S7**

7. To constitute a fitting appraisal or criterion of; to provide a basis for forming an opinion on.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r2-s8"></a>

**Source sense R2.S8**

8. To arbitrate; to pass opinion on something, especially to settle a dispute etc.

   **Usage:** intransitive
