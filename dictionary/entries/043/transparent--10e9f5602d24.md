### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. See-through, clear; having the property that light passes through it almost undisturbed, such that one can see through it clearly.

   **Example:** The waters of the lake were transparent until the factory dumped waste there.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a material or object

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. See-through, clear; having the property that light passes through it almost undisturbed, such that one can see through it clearly.
   1. Of a graphical image or animated GIF, having parts that allow the background to show through.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** arts; graphic-design

      **Source context (verbatim):** of a material or object

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Open, public; having the property that theories and practices are publicly visible, thereby reducing the chance of corruption.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a system or organization

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Obvious; readily apparent; easy to see or understand.

   **Example:** His reasons for the decision were transparent.

   **Example:** I love playing poker with Steve, because he's so transparent.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Having the property of transparency, i.e. sufficiently accurate that the compressed result is perceptually indistinguishable from the uncompressed input.
