### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. a person (of either sex) entering the first year of an institution, especially a high school (ninth grade for US, grade 9 for Canada, grade 7 for Philippines), a university, or legislative body

   **Usage:** Canada; Philippines; US

   **Example:** At the time I was a wide-eyed freshman, but I was soon to grow jaded and cynical.
