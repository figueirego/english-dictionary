### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To maintain; to preserve; to prevent from deteriorating or falling.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To continue with (work, etc).

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** Keep up the good work, team!

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To maintain position; to prevent oneself from falling behind; to stay even or ahead of another while walking, driving, etc.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** They ran so fast I could hardly keep up.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To ensure that one remains well-informed about something.

   **Example:** I always try to keep up with (or "keep up on") current affairs.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To prevent someone from going to bed or to sleep

   **Example:** The crying baby kept me up all night

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To use capital letters as much as reasonable.

   **Usage:** obsolete

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** media; printing; publishing

   **Source antonyms:** keep down
