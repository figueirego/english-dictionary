### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any herb with aroma or taste reminiscent of apple and mint, used for flavoring, herbal teas, and jellies.
   1. Of species Mentha suaveolens

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source synonyms:** apple-scented mint; pineapple mint; round-leaved mint; wooly mint

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any herb with aroma or taste reminiscent of apple and mint, used for flavoring, herbal teas, and jellies.
   1. Hybrid mints involving M. suaveolens as a parent
      1. Mentha × rotundifolia (M. longifolia × M. suaveolens)

         **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any herb with aroma or taste reminiscent of apple and mint, used for flavoring, herbal teas, and jellies.
   1. Hybrid mints involving M. suaveolens as a parent
      1. Mentha × villosa (M. spicata × M. suaveolens)

         **Usage:** countable; uncountable
