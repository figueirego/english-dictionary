### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In an incidental manner.
   1. By happenstance; in a particular case which has little to no bearing on, and little to no causal relation to, any other case.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** manner

      **Source synonyms:** coincidentally; happenstantially; circumstantially; by chance; accidentally; semirandomly; randomly

      **Source antonyms:** inevitably; certainly; systemically; systematically; essentially

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In an incidental manner.
   1. Not of central or critical importance.

      **Example:** The book discussed the subject, but only incidentally.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** manner

      **Source synonyms:** in passing

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. In an incidental manner.
   1. Parenthetically, by the way.

      **Usage:** conjunctive; speech act

      **Example:** Incidentally, did you hear anything new from your brother yesterday?

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** manner
