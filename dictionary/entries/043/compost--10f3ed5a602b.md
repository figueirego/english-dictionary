### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The decayed remains of organic matter that has rotted into a natural fertilizer.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Dig plenty of compost into clay or sandy soil to improve its structure.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A medium in which one can cultivate plants.

   **Usage:** UK; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Once the seed tray is filled with compost, insert the seeds spaced 3 cm apart from one another.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A mixture; a compound.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable
