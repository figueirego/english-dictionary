## Proper noun

### Forms

- the City of London — canonical

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The area in central London, England containing the financial district, usually abbreviated to the City.

   **Source synonyms:** the City; Square Mile

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The local authority and city which is part of Greater London.

   **Source synonyms:** City of London Corporation; Mayor and Commonalty and Citizens of the City of London

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The site of the original Roman Londinium.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. British financial institutions or financial markets as a whole; (by extension) British big-business interests.

   **Usage:** metonymically
