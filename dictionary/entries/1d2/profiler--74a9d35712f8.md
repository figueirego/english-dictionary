### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A mechanical device that cuts complex, irregular shapes by tracing a pattern.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person who creates a profile for something or someone, especially a psychological profile for an unknown criminal.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A device that analyzes something and builds up a profile.

   **Example:** a sediment profiler

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**
