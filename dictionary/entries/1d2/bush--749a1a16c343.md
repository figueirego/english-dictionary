   **Usage:** archaic; dialectal

   **Source context (verbatim):** in placenames

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A shrub or branch, properly, a branch of ivy (sacred to Bacchus), hung out at vintners' doors, or as a tavern sign; hence, a tavern sign, and symbolically, the tavern itself.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A person's pubic hair, especially a woman's.

   **Usage:** slang; vulgar

   **Source synonyms:** bush; carpet; floss; fluff; forest; fur; fuzz; grass; growth; grove; [lettuce](../121/lettuce--48695422fbc2.md); maidenhair; mound; muffin; nest; netherhair; pubes; rug; short and curlies; short hairs; shrubs; snatch; stuff; thatch
