## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Consisting of two hexagonal parts united.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** A dihexagonal pyramid is composed of two hexagonal pyramids placed base to base.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Having twelve similar faces.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** a dihexagonal prism
