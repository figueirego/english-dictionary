### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Heated to the point that it glows with a visible red color.

   **Example:** The smith's apprentice was still wary of manipulating the red-hot metal.

   **Source synonyms:** glowing hot; fervent; fiery

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Very hot.

   **Usage:** excessive

   **Example:** That curry was red-hot.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Emotionally charged, especially with anger or enthusiasm.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** He really delivered a red-hot speech today.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Having very strong sex appeal.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** Did you see that red-hot bikini model in today's paper?

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Very fresh, exciting, and up-to-date.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** Tune in at ten to catch this red-hot story!
