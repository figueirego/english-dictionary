### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The healthy fusion of adjacent bones by the growth of a bony material.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** normal human cranial synostosis

   **Example:** Examples of normal human developmental synostosis occur in the mandible and in the sacrum.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; physiology; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The abnormal bony fusion of a joint.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Various synostoses are typical of Antley-Bixler syndrome.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; pathology; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** synosteosis
