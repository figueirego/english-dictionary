### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The octave above another.

   **Example:** Succeeding superoctaves are pitches found at frequencies four, eight, sixteen times, and so on, of the fundamental frequency.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music
