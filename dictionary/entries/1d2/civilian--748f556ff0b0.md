### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person following the pursuits of civil life, especially one who is not an active member of the armed forces.

   **Example:** Three civilians were apprehended by the soldiers and taken away in a military vehicle.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person who does not belong to a particular group or engage in a particular activity, an outsider.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** The bathroom was for employees only, so civilians weren't allowed to use it.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. One skilled in civil law.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A student of civil law at a university or college.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A person not affiliated with a gang.

   **Usage:** slang

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. An employee of the civil service.

   **Usage:** Indian-English
