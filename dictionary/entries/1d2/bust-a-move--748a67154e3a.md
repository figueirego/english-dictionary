**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To dance.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive; African-American Vernacular

   **Example:** Damn, see that guy on the dance floor? He can bust a move!

   **Source synonyms:** cut a rug

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To initiate an action, such as a departure, attack, etc.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive; African-American Vernacular

   **Example:** It's time to go. Let's bust a move.
