## Prep\_phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Not for sale at this time. (Implies that being for sale is possible in the future or has been true in the past.)

   **Example:** That property is off the market again. They delisted it last week.

   **Source synonyms:** NFS; unavailable

   **Source antonyms:** on the market; for sale; available

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In a committed relationship (romantic, spousal, significant other, etc) and thus not available for dating.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** Sorry, ladies, he's off the market.

   **Source antonyms:** on the market; available
