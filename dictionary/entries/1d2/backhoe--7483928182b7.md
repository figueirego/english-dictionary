### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A piece of excavating equipment, either an integral subassembly or an attachment, consisting of a digging bucket or scoop on the end of an articulated arm, drawn backwards to move earth; used in excavator/digger and backhoe tractors.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Ellipsis of backhoe tractor&#58; A specialized tractor with the backhoe subassembly. This type of tractor has been superceded by the backhoe loader and trackhoe in most roles.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; ellipsis

   **Alternative form of:** backhoe tractor

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Ellipsis of backhoe loader&#58; A multi-purpose tractor with a front-mounted loading bucket and a rear-mounted digging bucket. The tractor combines a front-end loader/loader (component) and an excavator/digger (component).

   **Usage:** Australia; Canada; US; abbreviation; alt-of; ellipsis

   **Source synonyms:** digger

   **Alternative form of:** [backhoe loader](../270/backhoe-loader--9c353ae5bc9a.md)
