### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A device to assist in motion as a cane, especially one that provides support under the arm to reduce weight on a leg.

   **Example:** He walked on crutches for a month until the cast was removed from his leg.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Something or someone that supports, often used negatively to indicate that it is not needed and causes an unhealthy dependency; a prop.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** Alcohol became a crutch to help him through the long nights; eventually it killed him.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A form of pommel for a woman's saddle, consisting of a forked rest to hold the leg of the rider.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A knee, or piece of knee timber.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A forked stanchion or post.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. An improvised device, usually made from a piece of cardboard, to hold the last end of a joint.

   **Usage:** slang
