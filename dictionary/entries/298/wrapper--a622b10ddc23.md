### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Something that is wrapped around something else as a cover or protection&#58; a wrapping.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An outer garment; a loose robe or dressing gown.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. One who, or that which, wraps.

   **Example:** He proved to be a remarkably efficient wrapper of parcels.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A construct, such as a class or module, that serves to mediate access to another.

   **Usage:** object-oriented programming

   **Example:** We need a Perl wrapper for this C++ library.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A startup company that sells technology that it largely does not make.

   **Usage:** derogatory
