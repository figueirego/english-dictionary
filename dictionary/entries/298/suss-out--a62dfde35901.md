### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To come to understand (a person).

   **Usage:** colloquial; transitive

   **Example:** We've sussed him out—he only drinks on Fridays and only in that bar in town.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To manage to work (something) out, to discover or determine (something).

   **Usage:** colloquial; transitive

   **Example:** We've sussed out how to open the lock.
