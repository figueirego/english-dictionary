### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Senses relating to Charles and Erasmus Darwin.
   1. Of or pertaining to the scientific views advanced by the English biologist, geologist, and naturalist Charles Darwin, especially his theory that living organisms evolve through the natural selection of inherited variations that increase organisms' ability to compete, survive, and reproduce.

      **Source synonyms:** Darwinic; Darwinical (archaic; humorous; rare); Darwinist; Darwinistic; Darwinite (derogatory)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Senses relating to Charles and Erasmus Darwin.
   1. Of or pertaining to Darwinism, which includes the theories of Charles Darwin and other scientists.

      **Usage:** broadly

      **Source synonyms:** Darwinist; Darwinistic

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Senses relating to Charles and Erasmus Darwin.
   1. Competitive, especially in a ruthless manner.

      **Usage:** broadly

      **Source synonyms:** dog eat dog

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Senses relating to Charles and Erasmus Darwin.
   1. Exhibiting an ability to adapt or develop in order to survive; adaptable.

      **Usage:** broadly

      **Source synonyms:** agile; flexible

      **Source antonyms:** inadaptable; inflexible; rigid; unadaptable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Senses relating to Charles and Erasmus Darwin.
   1. Of or pertaining to the philosophical and scientific views, or poetic style, of the natural philosopher, physiologist, and poet Erasmus Darwin.

      **Usage:** historical

      **Source synonyms:** Darwinic (obsolete; rare)

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Of or pertaining to Darwin, the capital city of the Northern Territory, Australia.
