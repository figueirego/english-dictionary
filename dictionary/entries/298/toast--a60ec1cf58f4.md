### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Bread that has been toasted (cooked lightly by browning).

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** I ate a piece of toast for breakfast.

   **Example:** This toast and marmalade tastes heavenly.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A proposed salutation (e.g. saying "cheers") while drinking alcohol.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** At the reception, there were many toasts from the well-wishers.

   **Source synonyms:** pledge

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A person, group, or notable object to which a salutation with alcohol is made; a person or group held in similar esteem.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** He was the toast of high society.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Something that is irreparably damaged or used up, especially when destroyed by heat or fire; something which has been burnt to a crisp or incinerated.

   **Usage:** US; slang; uncountable

   **Example:** The metal frame survived the fire, but the plastic and rubber bits are toast.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Something that will be no more; something subject to impending destruction, harm or injury.

   **Usage:** US; slang; uncountable

   **Example:** to become/be toast

   **Example:** If I ever get my hands on the guy that stole my wallet, he’s toast!

   **Example:** You are going to be toast if you don't submit your school project on time.

   **Source synonyms:** dead duck; dead meat

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A type of extemporaneous narrative poem or rap.

   **Usage:** Jamaica; countable; slang

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. An old toast ("a lively fellow who drinks excessively").

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; slang

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A transient, informational pop-up overlay, less interactive than a snackbar.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; graphical-user-interface; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A piece of toast.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete outside India

## Verb · Etymology 2 · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /təʊst/ — source labels: Received-Pronunciation
- IPA: /toʊst/ — source labels: General-American
- IPA: /təʉst/ — source labels: Australia

### Forms

- toasts — present; singular; third-person
- toasting — participle; present
- toasted — participle; past
- toasted — past
- tost — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To cook lightly by browning by means of direct exposure to a fire or other heat source.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** We used to enjoy toasting marshmallows around the campfire.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To cook lightly by browning under a grill or in a toaster; to grill.

   **Usage:** intransitive; specifically

   **Example:** Top with cheese and toast under the grill for a few minutes.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To engage in a salutation, often with raising of the drinking vessel, while drinking an alcoholic beverage in honor of someone or something.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** We toasted the happy couple many times over the course of the evening.

   **Example:** You all have achieved so much together thus far, so I think that deserves a toast! Let's toast!

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To warm thoroughly.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I toasted my feet by the fire.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To perform an extemporaneous narrative poem or rap.

   **Usage:** Jamaica; slang

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music
