### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To collide with (an object), usually with the intention of damaging it or disabling its function.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** The man, driving an SUV, then rammed the gate, according to police.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To strike (something) hard, especially with an implement.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** To build a sturdy fence, you have to ram the posts deep into the ground.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To seat a cartridge, projectile, or propellant charge in the breech of a firearm by pushing or striking.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** After placing the cartridge in the musket, ram it down securely with the ramrod.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To force, cram or thrust (someone or something) into or through something.

   **Usage:** also; figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** I quickly rammed another charge down the barrel and took aim.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To fill or compact by pounding or driving.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** They rammed the earth walls to make them more compact

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To thrust during sexual intercourse.

   **Usage:** slang
