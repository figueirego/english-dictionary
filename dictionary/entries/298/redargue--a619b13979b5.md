### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To disprove or refute (someone) in an argument.

   **Usage:** transitive; obsolete except in Scots law

   **Source synonyms:** counterargue

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To rebut or refute (an argument, a proposition, etc.).

   **Usage:** transitive; obsolete except in Scots law

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Often followed by for or of&#58; to censure, to rebuke, to reprove (someone or something).

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To argue (a case, proposition, etc.) against someone.

   **Usage:** obsolete; rare; transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To present a disproof or refutation of an argument, a person, etc.

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete
