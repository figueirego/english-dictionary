### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Less dense than usual; thin.

   **Example:** The air at high altitudes at the top of mountains is rarefied.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a gas etc.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Distant from the lives and everyday concerns of ordinary people; esoteric, exclusive, select.

   **Example:** This is one of the most sought-after models in the rarefied world of high-performance sports car collecting.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Elevated in style or nature, sublime; of high intellectual or moral value.
