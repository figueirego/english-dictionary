### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To use up one's resources, especially a singular one or one not readily restored.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** We were playing checkers and, although I'd started off well, I seemed to have shot my bolt too soon.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of a male, to ejaculate.

   **Usage:** slang
