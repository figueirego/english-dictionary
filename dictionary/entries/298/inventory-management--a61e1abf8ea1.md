## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The formal management of the timing and quantities of goods to be ordered and stocked by an organization in order that demand can always be satisfied without excess expenditure.

   **Usage:** uncountable; supply chain

   **Source synonyms:** stock control
