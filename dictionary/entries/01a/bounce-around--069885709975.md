### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To discuss or brainstorm (ideas) on a topic.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** We bounced around some suggestions in-house and then workshopped the best ones afterward.

   **Example:** We bounced some ideas around but didn't take it any further.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Synonym of jump around.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source synonyms:** jump around (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To switch between jobs, locations and other situations often.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

   **Example:** He was a grinder operator for years. He bounced around for a while because the small shops kept going under.
