## Phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The harm has occurred, and nothing can be done to prevent it now; it might have been preventable, but cannot be prevented retroactively.

   **Example:** By the time Jack can get there it will be too late. The damage will have been done.
