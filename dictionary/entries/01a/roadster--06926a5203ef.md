### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A horse for riding or driving on the road.

   **Usage:** archaic

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A bicycle, or tricycle, adapted for common roads, rather than for the racing track, usually of classic style and steel-framed construction.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** cycling; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

   **Source synonyms:** high nelly; sit-up-and-beg

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. One who drives much; a coach driver.

   **Usage:** UK; dated

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A hunter who keeps to the roads instead of following the hounds across country.

   **Usage:** UK; dated; slang

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An open automobile having a front seat and a rumble seat.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** automotive; transport; vehicles

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A person who lives along the road.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. a sea-going vessel riding at anchor in a road or bay.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A clumsy vessel that works its way from one anchorage to another by means of the tides.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport
