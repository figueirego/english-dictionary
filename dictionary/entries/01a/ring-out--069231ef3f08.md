### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To sound clearly and loudly.

   **Example:** Suddenly, a shot rang out and someone screamed.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To make a phone call from an internal phone system to a general telephone network number.

   **Example:** You can ring out if you dial 9 first and wait for the tone before dialling the number.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** communications; electrical-engineering; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; telecommunications; telephony
