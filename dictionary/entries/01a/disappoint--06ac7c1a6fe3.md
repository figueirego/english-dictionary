### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To sadden or displease (someone) by underperforming, or by not delivering something promised or hoped for.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** His lack of respect disappointed her.

   **Example:** I was disappointed by last year’s revenue.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To deprive (someone of something expected or hoped for).

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To fail to meet (an expectation); to fail to fulfil (a hope).

   **Usage:** dated; transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To show (an opinion, belief, etc.) to be mistaken.

   **Usage:** dated; transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To prevent (something planned or attempted).

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** frustrate; thwart

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To dispossess (someone) of an office.

   **Usage:** nonstandard; obsolete; rare; transitive
