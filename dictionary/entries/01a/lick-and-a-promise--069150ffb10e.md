## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A quick, not-very-thorough wash.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** My grandparents called me only an hour before they decided to come visit, so I had to give the dirty living room a lick and a promise.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The hasty or incomplete performance of a task, done with the intention ("a promise") of thorough completion later.

   **Usage:** broadly; idiomatic
