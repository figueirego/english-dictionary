### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A support structure comprising joined parts or conglomerated particles and intervening open spaces of similar or larger size.

   **Usage:** literally

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The arrangement of support beams that represent a building's general shape and size.

   **Usage:** literally

   **Source synonyms:** ossature

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The larger branches of a tree that determine its shape.

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A basic conceptual structure.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** These ‘three principles of connexion’ compose the framework of principles in Hume's account of the association of ideas.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A reusable piece of code (and, sometimes, other utilities) providing a standard environment within which an application can be implemented.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences; software

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. An established and structured system of rules and principles used for analyzing and describing the structure of a language.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** grammar; human-sciences; linguistics; sciences
