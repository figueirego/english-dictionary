### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To delay taking action; to wait until later.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** He procrastinated until the last minute and had to stay up all night to finish.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To put off; to delay (something).

   **Usage:** transitive
