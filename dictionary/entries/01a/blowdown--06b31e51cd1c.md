## Noun

### Forms

- blowdowns — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The removal of liquid and solid hydrocarbons from a refinery vessel by the use of pressure.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; chemical engineering

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Cooling fluid discharged from a plant at the end of its cycle.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; industrial engineering

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Uprooting, overtopping, or bole breakage of trees by the wind; windthrow and windsnap.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; forestry

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The action of aerodynamic forces to tend to cause a deflected control surface to return to its neutral position, or prevent the surface from deflecting past a certain point.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** At higher airspeeds, the 737's maximum rudder deflection is limited by blowdown, helping to prevent flight-control inputs from overstressing the aircraft's vertical stabilizer.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** aeronautics; aerospace; aviation; business; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An instance of blowing down with compressed air.

   **Usage:** countable; informal; uncountable

   **Example:** Give these parts a blowdown with your air hose before you put them in the ultrasonic cleaning tank.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; manufacturing
