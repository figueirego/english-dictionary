<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Laziness; slowness of mind; disinclination to action or labour; a feeling combining indifference and lethargy; a dragging idleness.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Hot summer days inspire a sense of sloth in me.

   **Source synonyms:** acedia; slothfulness

   **Source antonyms:** diligence; liveliness

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any animal in the suborder Folivora.
   1. Any member of species of the families Choloepodidae and Bradypodidae of herbivorous, arboreal South American mammals, noted for its slowness and inactivity.

      **Usage:** countable; especially; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
