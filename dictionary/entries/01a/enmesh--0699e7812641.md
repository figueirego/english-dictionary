**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To mesh; to tangle or interweave in such a manner as not to be easily separated, particularly in a mesh- or net-like manner.

   **Source synonyms:** ensnare; ensnarl; entangle; enmesh; ensnare (figuratively); ensnarl; entangle; foul; implicate (archaic); imply (archaic); involve (figuratively); mat; ravel; snarl; tangle
