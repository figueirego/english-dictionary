**Source sense R1.S1**

1. (zoology, of a bryozoan colony or its base) Having rootlike structures for attachment to a substrate.

   **Extractor diagnostics (not usage labels):** no-gloss

**Supplemental definition (evidence reviewed; source gap preserved):** The guarded adjective has no restrictive quotation; its derived biradiculate does not constrain the domain. Hara’s original bryozoan study links the exact adjective to a specific attachment system&#58; printed p42 describes articulated zooecia connected by joints with rhizoids or stolons used for attachment, and p44 describes the same kind of small articulated colonies with a radiculate or stolonate base. The paired descriptions, together with the discussion of basal rootlets and rooting attachment on p42, support a narrowly scoped contextual interpretation of radiculate as possessing rootlike attachment structures. This is an original supplemental zoological definition, not a claim to recover the source contributor’s intended sense or to interpret the separate moss, coral or soil usages. It is not inferred from word formation alone.
