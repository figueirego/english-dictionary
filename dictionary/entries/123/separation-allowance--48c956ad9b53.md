## Noun

### Forms

- separation allowances — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A sum of money paid regularly to support the dependents of a member of the military while he or she is serving away from home.

   **Source synonyms:** family separation allowance

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Money paid as compensation to someone whose employment has ended.

   **Example:** The Mayor and Council will be entitled to a separation allowance upon completion of their term in office.

   **Example:** Workers who resign within thirty days after receiving a layoff notice are eligible for a separation allowance of two weeks’ salary for each year of service.

   **Source synonyms:** separation pay; severance pay
