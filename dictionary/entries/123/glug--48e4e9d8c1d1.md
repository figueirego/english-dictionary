**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The sound made when a significant amount of liquid is poured suddenly out of something, such as a jug or bottle.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The amount of liquid issued when the "glug" sound is heard.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** Pour a glug of bleach into the toilet.

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To flow in noisy bursts.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The water glugged down the sink.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To quickly swallow liquid.

   **Usage:** informal; transitive

   **Example:** He had glugged that glass of wine before she got a chance to introduce herself.
