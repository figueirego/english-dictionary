### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A single aspect of a given thing.

   **Example:** This film can be enjoyed on many dimensions - the script is great, the acting is realistic, and the special effects will simply take you aback.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A measure of spatial extent in a particular direction, such as height, width or breadth, or depth.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A construct whereby objects or individuals can be distinguished.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The number of independent coordinates needed to specify uniquely the location of a point in a space; also, any of such independent coordinates.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The number of elements of any basis of a vector space.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** linear-algebra; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. One of the physical properties that are regarded as fundamental measures of a physical quantity, such as mass, length and time.

   **Example:** The dimension of velocity is length divided by time.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Any of the independent ranges of indices in a multidimensional array.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A universe or plane of existence.

   **Example:** a machine that lets you travel to a parallel dimension.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** fantasy; literature; media; publishing; science-fiction
