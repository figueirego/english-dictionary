### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An Afrocentric subgenre of science fiction that foregrounds familial, spiritual and historical connections among Black people, paying particular attention to African culture, history and mythology, and African perspectives, rather than those of the African diaspora in the western world.

   **Usage:** Africa; neologism; uncountable; usually
