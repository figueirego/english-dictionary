## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The observation that, when three or more parameters are estimated simultaneously, there exist combined estimators more accurate on average (that is, having lower expected mean squared error) than any method that handles the parameters separately.

   **Usage:** decision theory; estimation theory; decision theory; estimation theory

   **Source synonyms:** Stein's paradox; Stein's phenomenon
