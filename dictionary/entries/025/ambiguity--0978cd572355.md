### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The state of being ambiguous; the state of leaving room for more than one interpretation.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** His speech was made with such great ambiguity that neither supporter nor opponent could be certain of his true position.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An instance of this state&#58; words or statements that are open to more than one interpretation, explanation or meaning, especially if that meaning cannot be determined from the context.

   **Usage:** countable
