### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A miserable, dysfunctional state or society that has a very poor standard of living or severe censorship, oppression, etc.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** cacotopia; anti-utopia

   **Source antonyms:** utopia

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Anatomical tissue that is not found in its usual place.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The patient suffers from adrenal dystopia.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; pathology; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** ectopia; ectopy; heterotopia; malposition
