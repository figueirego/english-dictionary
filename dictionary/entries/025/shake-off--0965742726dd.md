### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To remove (something attached to, on or clinging to an object) by shaking.

   **Example:** The archaeologists shook off the dust that had fallen from the roof, and promptly continued their work.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To dissociate oneself from (an allegation or rumour).

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To lose someone who is tracking you.

   **Example:** The police are chasing us! Quick, turn into that side street! We've got to shake them off.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To rid oneself of a malady or its symptoms

   **Example:** I just can't shake off this cold.
