### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To blame oneself for something; to acknowledge a fault.

   **Example:** I take responsibility for my poor choice; it was my fault.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To accept or take on an obligation or liability.

   **Example:** The new captain will take responsibility for the ship at the beginning of January.
