### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having the shape of an apple, or in cross section, like the cross section of an apple, or somewhat suggestive of it; especially with regard to top-heavy apple varieties rather than thoroughly round ones.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Having the shape of an apple, or in cross section, like the cross section of an apple, or somewhat suggestive of it; especially with regard to top-heavy apple varieties rather than thoroughly round ones.
   1. Having android fat distribution, not gynoid fat distribution.

      **Example:** apple-shaped obesity

      **Source synonyms:** android
