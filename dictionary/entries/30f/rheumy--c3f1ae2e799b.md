### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of, relating to, or made of rheum (“thin or watery discharge of mucus or serum”); watery.

   **Source synonyms:** rheumatic; rheumic

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Producing rheum from the mucous membranes; (also figuratively) especially of the eyes&#58; filled with rheum; watery.

   **Source synonyms:** rheumed

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Especially of the air&#58; damp, moist.

   **Usage:** literary; obsolete; poetic
