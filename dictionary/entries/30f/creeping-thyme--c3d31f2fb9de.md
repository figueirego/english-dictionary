### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A species of thyme native to Europe and northern Africa, Thymus praecox, grown as a fragrant groundcover due to its tendency to branch and grow horizontally.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually
