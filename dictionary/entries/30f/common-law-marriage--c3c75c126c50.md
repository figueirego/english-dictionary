### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A state of marriage, or legal recognition similar to that of marriage, enjoyed by a couple who have not undergone an official marriage ceremony, but instead based on the duration of cohabitation or other practical characteristics of the relationship.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** informal marriage; marriage by habit and repute; mop marriage
