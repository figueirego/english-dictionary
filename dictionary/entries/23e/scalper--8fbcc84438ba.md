### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who scalps, or removes the scalp of another.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One who scalps tickets to popular entertainment events&#58; buying them in advance and then selling them (e.g. online or just outside the venue of the event), often at inflated prices.

   **Usage:** Canada; US

   **Example:** We could see three different scalpers moving through the crowd outside the arena, each muttering the characteristic refrain&#58; “Need any tickets?”

   **Source synonyms:** ticket tout (UK)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. One who scalps any other item with limited availability.

   **Usage:** broadly

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A gambler who scalps.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** gambling; games

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A person on an open outcry exchange trading floor who buys and sells rapidly for his or her own account, aiming to buy from a seller and a little later sell to a buyer, making a small profit from the difference (roughly the amount of the bid/offer spread, or less).

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A machine for removing the ends of grain, such as wheat or rye, or for separating the different grades of broken wheat, semolina, etc.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A surgical instrument for scraping carious bones.

   **Source synonyms:** scalping-iron
