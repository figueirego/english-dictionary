### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Each of the divisions of a year based on the prevailing weather&#58; spring, summer, autumn (fall) and winter; or the rainy (monsoon) and dry season, depending on the climate.

   **Source synonyms:** yeartide; yeartime

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A period of the year when something particular happens.

   **Example:** mating season

   **Example:** the football season

   **Example:** the holiday season

   **Source synonyms:** time

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A period of the year in which a place is most busy or frequented for business, amusement, etc.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A year, but with emphasis on the most important part of the year in context, such as the months when plants or animals grow and reproduce.

   **Usage:** metonymically; pars pro toto

   **Example:** This invasive species reproduces so rapidly that it can completely take over an area in just a couple seasons.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The period over which a series of Test matches are played.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; cricket; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. That which gives relish; seasoning.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A group of episodes of a television or radio program broadcast in regular intervals with a long break between each group, usually with one year between the beginning of each.

   **Usage:** Australia; Canada; US

   **Example:** The third season of Robot Chicken aired from 2007 to 2008.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** broadcasting; media

   **Source synonyms:** series

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. An extended, undefined period of time.

   **Usage:** archaic

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A period of time in one’s life characterized by a particular emotion of situation.

   **Usage:** Evangelical Christianity

   **Example:** a season of faithlessness

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. The full set of downloadable content for a game, which can be purchased with a season pass.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** video-games

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To habituate, accustom, or inure (someone or something) to a particular use, purpose, or circumstance.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to season oneself to a climate

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To prepare by drying or hardening, or removal of natural juices.

   **Usage:** broadly; transitive

   **Example:** The timber needs to be seasoned.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To become mature; to grow fit for use; to become adapted to a climate.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To become dry and hard, by the escape of the natural juices, or by being penetrated with other substance.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The wood has seasoned in the sun.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To mingle&#58; to moderate, temper, or qualify by admixture.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To impregnate (literally or figuratively).

   **Usage:** obsolete
