### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A member of a taxonomic group of plants or algae, chiefly one whose taxonomic name ends (or ended) in -phyta.

   **Usage:** morpheme

   **Example:** arthrophyte, cyanophyte

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A plant that grows in a specified habitat.

   **Usage:** morpheme

   **Example:** acrophyte

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A plant that grows in a specified form or habit.

   **Usage:** morpheme

   **Example:** anthophyte, macrophyte

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A pathological growth.

   **Usage:** morpheme

   **Example:** osteophyte
