### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Equipment designed for a particular purpose; an apparatus.

   **Example:** The laboratory included an elaborate setup for measuring the energy.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The fashion in which something is organized or arranged.

   **Example:** The classroom setup was simple and efficient.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A situation orchestrated to frame someone; a covert effort to place the blame on somebody.

   **Example:** Trust me, that was a setup!

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An installer.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The process or instance of arranging resources for performing a specific operation, as a run of a particular product.

   **Usage:** operations

   **Example:** A simple setup on the bottling line involves reloading bottles and labels; emptying, cleaning, and reloading the tanks; and a test run.

   **Source synonyms:** make-ready; makeready

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The tendency of persistent wind to produce higher water levels at the downwind shore of a body of water and lower at the upwind shore.

   **Example:** There was a strong wind setup at the south end of the lake after a day of northerly gales.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; hydrology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A move or set of moves which are meant to draw out a reaction which leaves an exploitable opening in defense.

   **Example:** The jab is most commonly used as a setup for other strikes.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** boxing; government; hobbies; lifestyle; martial-arts; military; politics; sports; war
