### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To smile, parting the lips so as to show the teeth.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Why do you grin? Did I say something funny?

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To express by grinning.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** She grinned pleasure at his embarrassment.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To show the teeth, like a snarling dog.

   **Usage:** dated; intransitive

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To grin as part of producing a particular facial expression, such as a smile or sneer.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He grinned a broad smile when I told him the result.

   **Example:** He grinned a cruel sneer when I begged him to stop.
