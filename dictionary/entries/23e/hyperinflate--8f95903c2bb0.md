### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To inflate excessively.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Wishing for unlimited money would hyperinflate the market and make your money useless.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To be inflated excessively.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** As the chest wall and lungs hyperinflate, they progressively resist further inflation by virtue of their elastic recoil characteristics.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To subject to hyperinflation.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Both economies recklessly decide to hyperinflate their currencies.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** economics; sciences
