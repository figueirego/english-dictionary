### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A printed card allowing spectators of a game to identify players and record progress.

   **Example:** A scorecard and a short pencil were tucked into his breast pocket.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A tabular representation of the most important statistics of an innings or match.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; cricket; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports
