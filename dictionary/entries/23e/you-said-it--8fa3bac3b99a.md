## Phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used to express complete agreement with a previous statement by the interlocutor.

   **Source synonyms:** tell me about it; amen

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used to emphasize that a wording or a turn of phrase was chosen by the interlocutor, not by the speaker.

   **Example:** A&#58; I kept giving him the benefit of the doubt, but now where have we arrived at? Call a spade a spade&#58; he's a liar! B&#58; You said it — I didn't.
