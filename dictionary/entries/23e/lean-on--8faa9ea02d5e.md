### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see lean, on.

   **Example:** They sat there leaning on their elbows.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To depend upon for support; to require assistance from.

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To put pressure on; to attempt to compel a person to do something; to exert influence on.

   **Example:** He didn't make a donation until some of his customers leaned on him.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To criticize or reprimand for the purpose of changing behavior.
