## Phrase · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used to dismiss another person's argument by indicating that one is not prepared to hear or read anything further from that person. It is often used while simultaneously holding up the hand with the palm facing the speaker.

   **Usage:** US; colloquial; dated; sarcastic; African-American Vernacular
