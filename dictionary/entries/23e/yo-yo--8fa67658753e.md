### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A toy consisting of a spheroidal or cylindrical spindle having a circular groove in which string is wound; it is used by holding the string in the fingers and reeling the spindle up and down by movements of the wrist.

   **Example:** I bought a yo-yo from the toy store yesterday.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A volatile market that moves up and down.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Someone who vacillates.

   **Usage:** informal

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A dogfighting maneuver involving the attacker temporarily exchanging altitude for airspeed, or vice versa, in order to rapidly catch up with the defender or to prevent an overshoot.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** aeronautics; aerospace; aviation; business; engineering; government; military; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; politics; war

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A cloth rosette formed by gathering the outside edge of a circle of fabric in toward the centre using a running stitch.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; manufacturing; sewing; textiles

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A foolish, annoying or incompetent person.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** It is hard to watch the management for very long and not conclude that the place is run by a bunch of yo-yos.
