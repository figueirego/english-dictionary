### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To ease a burden, particularly to ease a worry; make less painful; to comfort.

   **Example:** mollify someone’s anger

   **Example:** attempt to mollify

   **Example:** mollify criticism

   **Example:** Her calm explanation helped mollify their concerns.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To appease anger, pacify, gain the good will of.

   **Example:** He tried to mollify the angry customer.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To soften; to make tender.
