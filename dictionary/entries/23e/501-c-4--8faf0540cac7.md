**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An organization that complies with section 501(c)(4) of the U.S. federal tax code. A non-profit organization exempt from some federal income taxes, due to being dedicated social welfare purposes.

   **Usage:** US; organizations

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** economics; government; sciences; taxation
