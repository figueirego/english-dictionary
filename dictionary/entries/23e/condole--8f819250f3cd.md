### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Followed by with&#58; to express condolence to, or sympathetic sorrow with, someone; to lament in sympathy with someone.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** She condoled with her father over the death of his mother.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To express deep sorrow; to grieve, to lament.

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete

   **Source synonyms:** besorrow; mourn; rue; aggrieve (obsolete); besorrow; condole (obsolete); darken; engrieve (archaic); erme (obsolete); gloom; grieve; grieven; bleed; lament; moan (poetic); mourn; remorse; repent; regret; rue (archaic); sigh; sorrow; wayment (obsolete); yearn (obsolete)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To express regret or sorrow over (an undesirable event or other misfortune); to bemoan, to grieve, to lament.

   **Usage:** formal; often; transitive; archaic or obsolete except India

   **Source synonyms:** bewail; mourn; afterthink; becry; begrieve (archaic; rare); bemoan; bemourn (rare); bewail; beweep; condole (India); deplore; elegize; grieve; grieven; keen; lament; mean (Ireland; UK); mourn; pine; repent; sigh; sorrow; wail; wayment (obsolete)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To express condolence to, or sympathetic sorrow with (someone); to lament in sympathy with (someone).

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** commiserate; feel sorry for; commiserate; compassion (obsolete); compassionate (archaic); condole (obsolete); feel someone's pain (figuratively); feel sorry for; mercify (obsolete; rare); pity; rue (archaic); take pity

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To express or feel sorrow for (oneself); to bewail, to mourn.

   **Usage:** obsolete; reflexive; transitive
