### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Not, the reverse of. (Especially forming words with a negative connotation)

   **Usage:** morpheme

   **Example:** dis- + agree → disagree

   **Example:** dis- + connect → disconnect

   **Example:** dis- + satisfied → dissatisfied

   **Example:** dis- + interested → disinterested

   **Example:** dis- + honour → dishonour

   **Source synonyms:** un-; non-; de-; in-; an-; a-

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Expressing separation or removal.

   **Usage:** morpheme

   **Example:** dis- + card → discard (“to throw out a card”)

   **Example:** dis- + bar → disbar (“to expel from the bar”)

   **Example:** dis- + franchise → disfranchise (“to remove one's franchise, to prevent from voting”)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used as an intensifier.

   **Usage:** morpheme

   **Example:** dis- + embowel → disembowel

   **Example:** dis- + annul → disannul (“to annul”)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Alternative form of dys- (“incorrect”).

   **Usage:** alt-of; alternative; morpheme

   **Example:** dis- + function → disfunction
