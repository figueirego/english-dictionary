### Pronunciation

- enPR: sīʹ-əns — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A particular discipline or branch of knowledge that is natural, measurable or consisting of systematic principles rather than intuition or technical skill.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** Of course in my opinion Social Studies is more of a science than an art.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Specifically the natural sciences.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** My favorite subjects at school are science, mathematics, and history.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Knowledge gained through study or practice; mastery of a particular discipline or area.

   **Usage:** archaic; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The fact of knowing something; knowledge or understanding of a truth.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** lifestyle; religion; theology

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The collective discipline of study or learning acquired through the scientific method; the sum of knowledge gained from such methods and discipline.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Knowledge derived from scientific disciplines, scientific method, or any systematic effort.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The scientific community.

   **Usage:** collective; uncountable

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Synonym of sweet science (“the sport of boxing”).

   **Usage:** countable; euphemistic; uncountable; with-definite-article

   **Source synonyms:** sweet science (synonym; synonym-of)
