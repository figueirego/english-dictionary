## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A département (equivalent of a county) in France’s eastern region Bourgogne (Burgundy)

   **Example:** Dijon is the capital of the Côte d’Or

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A chain of hills West of the Saône valley, which the above département was named after

   **Example:** The Côte d’Or includes many fine Burgundy wine appellations
