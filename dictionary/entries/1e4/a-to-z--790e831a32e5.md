### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of a set of street maps of most British towns and cities; properly the Geographer's A–Z Street Atlas.

   **Usage:** British; English; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** I always keep a small set of A to Zs in the car.

   **Usage:** uncountable
