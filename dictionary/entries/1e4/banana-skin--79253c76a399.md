## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The outer skin of a banana, especially once removed from the rest of the banana, which may be slippery if stepped on.

   **Usage:** UK

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A cause of difficulty, or potential embarrassment.

   **Usage:** UK

   **Example:** The subject is becoming a political banana skin.
