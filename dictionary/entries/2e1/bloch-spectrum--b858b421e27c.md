## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A concept relating to energy spectra. Let H be the one-dimensional Schrödinger equation operator H=-(d²)/(dx²)+U\_α, where U\_α is a periodic function of period α. The Bloch spectrum of H is the set of values E for which all the solutions of (H − E)φ = 0 are bounded on the whole real axis.

   **Usage:** quantum mechanics
