## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In Proto-Indo-European linguistics, an ablaut form of a root characterised by the presence of the \*/e/ phoneme.

   **Usage:** uncountable; Indo-European linguistics

   **Example:** \*bʰer- is the e-grade of the root meaning “to carry, bear”.

   **Source synonyms:** full-grade
