**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In civil law, a case where two or more people disagree and one or more of the parties take the case to a court for resolution.

   **Example:** The lawyer advised his client against filing a lawsuit as it would take a lot of time and money to resolve.

   **Example:** My ex-fiancé filed a lawsuit against me for a false restraining order and defamation.
