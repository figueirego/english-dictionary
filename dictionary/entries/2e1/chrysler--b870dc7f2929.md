## Proper noun · Record 1

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A motor vehicle brand (a make) owned by the Chrysler Corporation (1925–1998) and its successor corporations, most recently Stellantis.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** a Chrysler dealership

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A former motor vehicle manufacturer, the Chrysler Corporation (1925–1998); its successor corporations still informally called by that name, most recently Stellantis's North American subsidiary.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** From the 1920s through the 1960s, Chrysler was known within the automotive industry for impressive engineering.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A surname.

   **Usage:** countable
