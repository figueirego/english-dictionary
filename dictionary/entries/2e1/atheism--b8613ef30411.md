### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A non-belief in deities.
   1. Belief that no deities exist (sometimes including rejection of other religious beliefs).

      **Usage:** uncountable; usually; strictly

      **Source synonyms:** ;; Epicureanism; antireligiousness; irreligion; irreligiousness

      **Source antonyms:** theism; religion

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A non-belief in deities.
   1. A lack of belief in deities, or a belief that there is insufficient evidence to believe in a god.

      **Usage:** broadly; uncountable; usually

      **Source synonyms:** ;; Epicureanism; irreligion; irreligiousness; nonreligion; nonreligiousness

      **Source antonyms:** theism; religion

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A non-belief in deities.
   1. Absence of belief that any deities exist (including absence of the concept of deities).

      **Usage:** uncountable; usually; very loosely

      **Source synonyms:** ;; Epicureanism

      **Source antonyms:** theism; religion

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Neglect of one's obligations to God; behavior incongruent with belief in God (regardless of belief in the strict sense).

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source context (verbatim):** from a religious perspective

   **Source synonyms:** godlessness

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Absence of belief in a particular deity, pantheon, or religious doctrine (notwithstanding belief in other deities).

   **Usage:** historical; rare; uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A rejection of all religions, even non-theistic ones.

   **Usage:** proscribed; sometimes; uncountable; usually
