### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To escort or carry an item or person to a destination.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** You should bring a bottle of wine along.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To be responsible for convincing or causing a group to join alongside oneself.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** If we get Jerry, he'll bring along most of the executive committee for the vote.

   **Example:** She can bring along the voters they need.
