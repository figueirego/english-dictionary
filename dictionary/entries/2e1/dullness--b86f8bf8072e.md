### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The quality of being slow of understanding things.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source synonyms:** dullwittedness; dimness; dimwittedness

   **Source antonyms:** sharpness; acuity; brightness; brain; brains (colloquial); cleverness; head; brightness; esprit; intellect; intelligence; mind; nous; psyche; reason; smartness; smarts (colloquial); wit; judgment

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The quality of being uninteresting; boring; humorless or irksome.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Lack of interest or excitement.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The lack of visual brilliance; want of sheen.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** dullness of autumn

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. bluntness.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source context (verbatim):** of an edge

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The quality of not perceiving or kenning things distinctly.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** dullness of sight, or of hearing

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Drowsiness.

   **Usage:** archaic; uncountable; usually
