### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To secrete saliva, especially in anticipation of food.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To secrete any substance in a similar way.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** The alien beast drooled slime.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To react to something with uncontrollable desire.

   **Usage:** figuratively; informal; intransitive

   **Example:** That boy is so attractive I drool whenever I see him!

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To talk nonsense; drivel.
