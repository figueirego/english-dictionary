### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To form an opinion and take action on an issue which carries the risk of professional or legal liability; particularly where there is no obvious course of action.

   **Usage:** UK; intransitive

   **Example:** Collate together all the information you can, but do remember that Michael's the one who'll take a view, not you.

   **Example:** If the insurer refuses to cover the defect in the policy, the client is just going to have to take a view.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business
