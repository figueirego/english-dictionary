### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To express contempt and disrespect for someone or something, or to act accordingly.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; mildly; slang; vulgar

   **Example:** You just completely pissed on my job performance here.

   **Example:** If you cancel this contract, you're basically pissing on me and all the work I've done for you.

   **Source synonyms:** shit on; dump on; cack on; kak on

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To reject (a person, an idea, etc.)

   **Usage:** idiomatic; imperative; mildly; slang; vulgar

   **Example:** She wants us to work outside in the dark? Piss on that. I'm going home early.

   **Example:** You've done nothing but complain the whole time we've been here. Piss on you. I'm done listening to you.
