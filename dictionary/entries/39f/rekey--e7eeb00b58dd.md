### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To enter information into a device, such as a keyboard or keypad, after it has been done at least once before.

   **Example:** She had to rekey the security code.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences
