### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A traditional Spanish and Portuguese unit of dry measure or volume, highly variable by region and substance but often equivalent to about 4.6 liters (one celemin).

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A traditional unit of land area, nominally the amount of land that could be sown with one almud of seed.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A low, wide box used as a physical measure for this unit.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Alternative form of almude (“a Portuguese unit of measure”).

   **Usage:** alt-of; alternative; historical

   **Alternative form of:** [almude](../305/almude--c178327ddaff.md)
