## Noun

### Forms

- services of process — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The delivery of information to a person or entity providing notice that they are being sued with sufficient detail for them to respond to the suit.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The process server could not effect service of process because the defendant was nowhere to be found.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The effective notification of a lawsuit such that a court may proceed with the case.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The court found plaintiff's service of process effective, although the defendant refused to receive the complaint.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law
