### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To not reside on the premises of one's employer (used especially of domestic staff such as nannies, cooks, maids, etc.)

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Our nanny used to live out, but now she lives with us.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To fulfill or act out a dream or fantasy or aspiration.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Jackie is going to fashion school to live out her dream of becoming a fashion designer.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To pass time or to pass the remainder of one's life, especially in a particular place or situation.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to live out one's days

   **Example:** He is paying $200,000 a year to live out his days on a cruise liner.
