### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A throw that begins flying through the air, but because of the angle it is thrown at, begins to turn 45° in order for it to descend and begin to roll, like a roller (a type of throw).

   **Usage:** disc golf

   **Example:** He threw a throller over the wall and rolled all the way to circle's edge.
