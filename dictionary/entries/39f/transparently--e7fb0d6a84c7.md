### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In a transparent manner; with nothing hidden.

   **Example:** They conducted their business transparently, solely in public meetings.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. So as to admit light without distortion.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** manner

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. So as to be readily perceived and understood.

   **Usage:** modal

   **Example:** Transparently to all, he had nothing of substance to say.
