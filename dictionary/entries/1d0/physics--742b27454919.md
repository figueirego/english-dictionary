### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The branch of science concerned with the study of the properties and interactions of space, time, matter and energy.

   **Usage:** plural; plural-only; uncountable

   **Example:** Newtonian physics was extended by Einstein to explain the effects of travelling near the speed of light; quantum physics extends it to account for the behaviour of atoms.

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The physical aspects of a phenomenon or a system, especially those examined or studied scientifically.

   **Usage:** plural; plural-only; uncountable

   **Example:** The physics of car crashes would not let Tom Cruise walk away like that.
