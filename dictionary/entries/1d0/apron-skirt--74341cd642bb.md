### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The lower, skirt-like part of an apron of the type that covers the front of both the torso and the thighs.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A skirt with an apron-like panel incorporated into the front.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An item of clothing worn by women in some North American indigenous communities, consisting of an apron-like flap hanging over the front of the thighs, sometimes with an additional flap hanging in the back.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A long skirt with a slit up the back as far as the buttocks, designed for women to wear over their breeches while riding a horse.

   **Usage:** historical
