## Noun · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A historically constituted, stable community of people, formed based on a common economic life, ethnicity, language, religion, and/or territory, and manifested in a common culture.

   **Usage:** collective

   **Example:** The Roma are a nation without a country.

   **Example:** pre-Columbian nations

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A historically constituted, stable community of people, formed based on a common economic life, ethnicity, language, religion, and/or territory, and manifested in a common culture.
   1. A community united by some trait (especially an interest) but not historically constituted.

      **Usage:** broadly; collective; humorous; informal; often

      **Example:** the Dallas Cowboys nation

      **Example:** the hip-hop nation

      **Source synonyms:** tribe

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A sovereign state; (loosely, metonymic, proscribed) a country.

   **Usage:** metonymically; international law

   **Example:** Though legally single nations, many states comprise several distinct cultural or ethnic groups.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An association of students based on the birthplace or ethnicity of its members.

   **Usage:** historical

   **Example:** Once widespread across Europe in medieval times, nations are now largely restricted to the ancient universities of Sweden and Finland.

   **Source synonyms:** student nation

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A great number; a great deal.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. In North America, an Indigenous people with federal and/or state recognition as such and (often also) their federally recognized territory.

   **Example:** The Choctaw Nation is the third-largest federally recognized tribe in the United States and the second-largest Indian reservation in area.

   **Example:** The term Navajo Nation refers both to the people and to their territory.
