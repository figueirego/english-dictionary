### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A ticket book, a collection of tickets in the form of a booklet often sold at a discount to single tickets.

   **Example:** Rather than buy each ticket individually, you can buy a carnet of ten tickets for a lower price.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A customs document that allows the temporary duty-free importation of a particular article

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law
