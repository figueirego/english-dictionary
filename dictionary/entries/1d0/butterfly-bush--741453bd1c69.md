## Noun

### Forms

- butterfly bushes — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of several plants that conspicuously attract butterflies.
   1. Any buddleia, of genus Buddleja.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of several plants that conspicuously attract butterflies.
   1. Any buddleia, of genus Buddleja.
      1. Especially, one of the species Buddleja davidii or Buddleja globosa.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of several plants that conspicuously attract butterflies.
   1. Blue butterfly bush (Rotheca myricoides, syn. Clerodendrum ugandense).

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any of several plants that conspicuously attract butterflies.
   1. The butterfly weed (Asclepias tuberosa).
