### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To inhale a larger quantity of air than usual, and immediately expel it; to make a deep single audible respiration, especially as the result or involuntary expression of fatigue, exhaustion, grief, sorrow, frustration, or the like.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** When she saw it wasn't damaged, she sighed with relief.

   **Example:** He sighed. It was going to be a long night.

   **Example:** He sighed over the lost opportunity.

   **Source synonyms:** suspire (obsolete); sithe

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To lament; to grieve.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source synonyms:** sithe (obsolete); yearn; aggrieve (obsolete); besorrow; condole (obsolete); darken; engrieve (archaic); erme (obsolete); gloom; grieve; grieven; bleed; lament; moan (poetic); mourn; remorse; repent; regret; rue (archaic); sigh; sorrow; [wayment](../34c/wayment--d33880685e68.md) (obsolete); yearn (obsolete)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To utter sighs over; to lament or mourn over.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** grieve; lament; sithe (obsolete); afterthink; becry; begrieve (archaic; rare); bemoan; bemourn (rare); bewail; beweep; condole (India); deplore; elegize; grieve; grieven; keen; lament; mean (Ireland; UK); mourn; pine; repent; sigh; sorrow; wail; [wayment](../34c/wayment--d33880685e68.md) (obsolete)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To make a sound like sighing.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The wind sighed in the trees.

   **Source synonyms:** sithe (obsolete)

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To exhale (the breath) in sighs.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** She sighed a sigh that was nearly a groan.

   **Example:** sigh a note and sing a note

   **Example:** She sighed her relief when she found her missing toddler.

   **Source synonyms:** sithe (obsolete)

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To express by sighs; to utter in or with sighs.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** "I guess I have no choice," she sighed.

   **Example:** She sighed her frustrations.

   **Source synonyms:** sithe (obsolete)
