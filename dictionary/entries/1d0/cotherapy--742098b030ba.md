### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Two or more simultaneous therapies.
   1. All the components together.

      **Usage:** uncountable

      **Example:** In severe cases, cotherapy with corticosteroids and monoclonal antibodies is indicated.

      **Source synonyms:** polytherapy

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Two or more simultaneous therapies.
   1. Any component among the group, considered individually.

      **Usage:** countable

      **Example:** In severe cases, monoclonal antibodies are added as a cotherapy.
