### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Capable of being navigated; deep enough and wide enough to afford passage to vessels.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a body of water

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Seaworthy; in a navigable state; steerable.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a boat

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Steerable, dirigible.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a balloon

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Easy to navigate.

   **Example:** This Web site isn't very navigable. I can't tell which image links to which page.
