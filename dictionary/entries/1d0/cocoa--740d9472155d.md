### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The dried and partially fermented fatty seeds of the cacao tree from which chocolate is made.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An unsweetened brown powder made from roasted, ground cocoa beans, used in making chocolate, and in cooking.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A hot drink made with milk, cocoa powder, and sugar.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Do you like cocoa?

   **Source synonyms:** chocolate; drinking chocolate; hot chocolate

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A serving of this drink.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** I like to watch TV with a cocoa.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A light to medium brown colour.

   **Usage:** countable
