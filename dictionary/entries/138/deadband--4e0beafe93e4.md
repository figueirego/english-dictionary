### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The range through which an input can be varied without initiating an observable response. Deadband is usually expressed in percent of span.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The thermostat was errantly set with a deadband from 18°C to 27°C, causing the air conditioner to not turn on below 27°C, and the heater to not come on above 18°C.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In the domain of a transfer function, the band of input values where the output is zero. "(the output is 'dead' - no action occurs)"

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The game will not respond to small movements of the joystick, until the stick is pushed out of its dead zone.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences; systems-theory

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Backlash in a mechanical system.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The steering system has so much backlash that it takes a full rotation of the wheel to exit its deadband and affect the steering.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; mechanical; mechanical-engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences
