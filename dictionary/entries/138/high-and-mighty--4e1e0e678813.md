## Adjective · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Overbearingly arrogant; ostentatiously self-important or self-aggrandizing.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** The high and mighty are to be found at the Fairlawn Country Club on pleasant afternoons.

   **Example:** Ever since she was placed in the gifted program, she's become so high and mighty that no one wants to be near her.
