### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A hill in West Jerusalem, in Israel, on which ancient Jerusalem was partly built; a centrepiece to Biblical accounts of old days and future eschatological events.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biblical; lifestyle; religion

   **Source synonyms:** Mount Zion

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Jerusalem city proper; or more strictly speaking, the Temple Mount.

   **Usage:** poetic

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The whole nation of Israel.

   **Usage:** broadly

<a id="r1-s4"></a>
