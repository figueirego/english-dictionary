### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of various wading birds of the genera Egretta or Ardea that includes herons, many of which are white or buff, and several of which develop fine plumes during the breeding season.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A plume or tuft of feathers worn as a part of a headdress, or anything imitating such an ornament.

   **Source synonyms:** aigrette

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The flying feathery or hairy crown of seeds or achenes, such as the down of the thistle.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The crab-eating macaque (Macaca fascicularis)

   **Usage:** obsolete
