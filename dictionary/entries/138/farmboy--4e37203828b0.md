## Noun

### Forms

- farmboys — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A boy or young man who works on a farm, especially one who is growing up there as a child of the farmer.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A man who grew up on a farm and prides himself on a farm-based identity.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** He likes to say that he's still just a farmboy at heart.
