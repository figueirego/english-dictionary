### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To enter on a list; to enroll; to register.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To voluntarily join a cause or organization, especially military service.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The army wants potential soldiers to enlist.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To recruit the aid or membership of others.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** We enlisted fifty new members.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To secure; to obtain.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** They enlisted the government's support.
