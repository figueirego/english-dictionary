## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The whole territory of China, including China proper, Tibet, Manchuria, Xinjiang and Inner Mongolia.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The historic territories of China or its tributary states, or the imagined future territories of China, including e.g. Taiwan, Outer Mongolia, Indochina, and Outer Manchuria.
