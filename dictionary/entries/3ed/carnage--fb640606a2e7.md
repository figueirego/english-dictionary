### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Death and destruction.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** There was carnage after the school play ended with 96 deaths.

   **Source synonyms:** massacre; bloodbath

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The corpses, gore, etc. that remain after a massacre.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any great loss by a team; a game in which one team wins overwhelmingly.

   **Usage:** figuratively; uncountable; usually

   **Example:** The game against Sri Lanka and Australia was carnage with Australia winning by 287 runs.
