- IPA: /ˈveɪpɚ/ — source labels: General-American

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Cloudy diffused matter such as mist, steam or fumes suspended in the air.

   **Usage:** US; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The gaseous state of a substance that is normally a solid or liquid.

   **Usage:** US; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Something insubstantial, fleeting, or transitory; unreal fancy; vain imagination; idle talk; boasting.

   **Usage:** US; countable; idiomatic; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any medicinal agent designed for administration in the form of inhaled vapour.

   **Usage:** US; countable; dated; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Hypochondria; melancholy; the blues; hysteria, or other nervous disorder.

   **Usage:** US; archaic; countable; in-plural; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Wind; flatulence.

   **Usage:** US; countable; obsolete; uncountable
