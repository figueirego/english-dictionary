## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

- self-reports — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A kind of survey or questionnaire in which respondents read the questions and select responses by themselves, without researcher interference.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Something said by someone with the purpose of conveying a neutral stance on a subject but unintentionally leading to that person's bias being revealed.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** His constantly talking about rape laws was a bit of a self-report.
