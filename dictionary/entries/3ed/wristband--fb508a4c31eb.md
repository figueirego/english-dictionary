### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The cuff of a sleeve that wraps around the wrist.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A strip of material worn around the wrist, e.g. to absorb perspiration, especially in sports.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A decorative band or bracelet that encircles the wearer's wrist.

   **Source synonyms:** wristlet

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A band that supports a wristwatch.

   **Source synonyms:** watchband; watchstrap

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A cord worn around the wrist, used to hold a small object such as a knife&#58; a lanyard

   **Source synonyms:** lanyard
