### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An object or person who spreads.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A spacer or device for keeping two objects apart.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A device used to spread bulk material.

   **Example:** Ellen put grass seed on the lawn with a seed spreader.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A knife or spatula used to distribute a substance such as butter or jelly.

   **Example:** James used a spreader to butter her bagel.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A horizontal athwartships spar attached to the mast of a sailboat in order to extend the shrouds away from the mast.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A machine for combining and drawing fibers of flax to form a sliver preparatory to spinning.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The moving platform of a container crane.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A horizontal bar or frame attached to two or more points on a load, enabling the load to be lifted, especially by crane, with greater stability and more even spread of forces.
