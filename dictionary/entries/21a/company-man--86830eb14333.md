### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A male employee who has a great—and often, in the view of others, an excessive—commitment to serving the interests of the organization which employs him.

   **Source synonyms:** organization man; suit; yes-man

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A spy or other operative of an intelligence service, especially the U. S. Central Intelligence Agency.
