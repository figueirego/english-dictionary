## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see steaming, hot&#58; very hot in a way that involves giving off steam or extremely hot water vapour.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Don't touch that radiator! It's steaming hot!

   **Source synonyms:** steaming; boiling hot; scalding hot; piping hot

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Very hot in a way that involves visible water vapour.

   **Usage:** broadly; idiomatic; not-comparable

   **Example:** Those horses are steaming hot. He's got to give that team a rest in the shade soon or there'll be trouble.

   **Source synonyms:** steaming; boiling hot; baking hot

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Very hot.

   **Usage:** broadly; idiomatic; not-comparable

   **Example:** Watch out — the chrome parts of the car are steaming hot from the sun.

   **Source synonyms:** steaming; boiling hot; scalding hot; piping hot; baking hot; smoking hot
