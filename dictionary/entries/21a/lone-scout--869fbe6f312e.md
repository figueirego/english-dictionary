### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A member of the Scouting movement who is in an isolated area or otherwise does not participate in a regular Scouting unit or organization.

   **Usage:** scouting

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A member of the Lone Scouts of America, a Scouting organization for American boys that operated from 1915 to 1924, after which it was merged with the Boy Scouts of America.

   **Usage:** US; historical; scouting
