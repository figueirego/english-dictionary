**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The entropy of a quantum state. If the state is expressed as a quantum density matrix ρ, then this entropy can be expressed mathematically as S=-tr(ρ log ρ) where tr is the trace operator and the logarithm is natural.

   **Usage:** quantum mechanics
