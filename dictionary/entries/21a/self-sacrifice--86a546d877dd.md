## Noun

### Forms

- self-sacrifices — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The giving up of one's own benefit for the good of others.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Caring for a loved one with a chronic illness inevitably involves self-sacrifice.

   **Source synonyms:** self-abnegation; self-denial

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The act of making the ultimate sacrifice of giving up one's life for a cause.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** His brave self-sacrifice won him a posthumous medal, but I think he'd have preferred to receive it in person.
