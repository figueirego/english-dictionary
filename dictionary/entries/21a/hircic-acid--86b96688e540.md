## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A type of oily acid (a mixture rich in capric- and caproic acids) made from mutton suet, having an odor similar to that of a goat, used in the early days of the production of soap, and in soup-making.

   **Usage:** historical; uncountable
