## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An intimate physical embrace, typically while lying (or sitting)&#58; a snuggle, an instance of lying together snugly, as one might with a partner (more intimate than a hug, and typically of longer duration).

   **Usage:** US

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An affectionate embrace, a hug, such as is given to family members and close friends (less intimate than a snuggle, and typically of shorter duration).

   **Usage:** UK

   **Example:** Give me a cuddle, Paul; it'll cheer you up.

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To lie together snugly (with someone), in an intimate physical embrace; to snuggle.

   **Usage:** US; intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** The young lovers cuddled on the couch.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To embrace (someone) affectionately; to hug (someone) closely.

   **Usage:** UK; intransitive; transitive

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To cradle (e.g. a baby) in one's arms so as to give comfort, warmth.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** She cuddled the infant before bedtime.

   **Example:** I'm cold; can you roll over here and cuddle me, honey?

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To lie close or snug; to crouch; to nestle.
