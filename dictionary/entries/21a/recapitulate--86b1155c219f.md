### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To summarize or repeat in concise form.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** The entire symphony was recapitulated in the last four bars.

   **Example:** We still have five minutes left, so let's recapitulate the lecture.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To reproduce or closely resemble (as in structure or function).

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To mirror or repeat in analogous form, especially in reference to an individual's development passing through stages corresponding to the species' stages of evolutionary development.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences

   **Source context (verbatim):** of an organism
