## Noun

### Forms

- hairy armadillos — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Certain species of armadillo found in South America.
   1. An armadillo of any species in the Chaetophractus (family Chlamyphoridae).
      1. An Andean hairy armadillo (Chaetophractus nationi), present in Bolivia.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Certain species of armadillo found in South America.
   1. An armadillo of any species in the Chaetophractus (family Chlamyphoridae).
      1. A big hairy armadillo (Chaetophractus villosus), one of the largest and most numerous armadillos in southern South America; peludo.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Certain species of armadillo found in South America.
   1. An armadillo of any species in the Chaetophractus (family Chlamyphoridae).
      1. A screaming hairy armadillo (Chaetophractus vellerosus), a burrowing armadillo found in the central and southern parts of South America.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Certain species of armadillo found in South America.
   1. A hairy long-nosed armadillo (Dasypus pilosus) (family Dasypodidae), endemic to Peru.
