### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The final person remaining in a battle or competition after everyone else has been eliminated.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** One contestant will be eliminated at the end of every round. The last man standing wins $1,000,000.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A multiplayer deathmatch gameplay mode in which the last surviving player is the winner.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** video-games
