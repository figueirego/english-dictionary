## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Eaten on the premises where one purchased it.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** a dine-in meal

   **Source synonyms:** eat-in

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Being or relating to premises where one can eat one's purchases.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** a dine-in restaurant

   **Source synonyms:** eat-in
