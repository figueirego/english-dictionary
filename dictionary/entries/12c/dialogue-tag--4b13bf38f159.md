### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see dialogue, tag.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A sentence part that attributes a piece of written dialogue to its speaker. (e.g. In "Get out of here," he said, "he said" is a dialogue tag.)

   **Example:** Not every paragraph of dialogue needs to have a dialogue tag attached to it.
