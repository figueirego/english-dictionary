### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A character or part played by a performer or actor.

   **Example:** My neighbor was the lead role in last year's village play.

   **Example:** Her dream was to get a role in a Hollywood movie, no matter how small.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The expected behaviour of an individual in a society.

   **Example:** The role of women has changed significantly in the last century.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The function or position of something.

   **Example:** Local volunteers played an important role in cleaning the beach after the oil spill.

   **Example:** What role does the wax in your earhole fulfill?

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Designation that denotes an associated set of responsibilities, knowledge, skills, or privileges

   **Example:** The project manager role is responsible for ensuring that everyone on the team knows and executes his or her assigned tasks.

   **Source synonyms:** capacity

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The function of a word in a phrase.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** grammar; human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. In the Raku programming language, a code element akin to an interface, used for composition of classes without adding to their inheritance chain.

   **Usage:** object-oriented programming
