**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A person who has severely impaired physical abilities because of deformation, injury, or amputation of parts of the body.

   **Usage:** countable; offensive; uncountable

   **Example:** He returned from war a cripple.

   **Source synonyms:** disabled

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; figuratively; uncountable

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. A shortened wooden stud or brace used to construct the portion of a wall above a door or above and below a window.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

**Source sense R2.S4**

   **Usage:** dialectal; uncountable; Southern US except Louisiana
