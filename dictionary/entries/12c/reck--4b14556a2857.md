### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To take account of (someone or something); to care for; to consider, to heed, to regard.

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive; intransitive (usually with of or for)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To want (to do something); to desire to, to be inclined to, to care to.

   **Usage:** archaic; catenative

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To know about, to know of, to be aware of.

   **Usage:** archaic; intransitive; with-of

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To reckon, to consider, to regard (someone or something) as.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To concern (someone); to be important or of interest to; to matter.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive; archaic; dialectal

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To concern oneself, to trouble oneself.

   **Usage:** dialectal; obsolete; reflexive
