### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To estimate too low; to perceive (someone or something) as having a lower value, quantity, worth, etc., than what he/she/it actually has.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** misjudge; misunderestimate; sell short; underapproximate

   **Source antonyms:** overapproximate; overestimate; overapproximate; overestimate; overhold; overrate; overreckon; overstate; overthink (rare)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To perceive or expect (someone or something) to be less significant or difficult than it actually is.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I totally underestimated the task.

   **Source synonyms:** belittle; make light of; bagatellize; banalize; belittle; cut down (idiomatic); de-emphasize; denigrate; degrade; deprecate; derogate; disparage; dispraise; downplay; lilliputianize; make little of (idiomatic); make nothing of (idiomatic); minimize; play down (idiomatic); set at nought (archaic); superficialize; trivialize; underestimate; underplay; understate; vilipend (dated; formal)

   **Source antonyms:** overdo; big up (slang); cut it fat (UK; obsolete; slang); exaggerate; hyperbolize (archaic); overcharge (dated); overdo; overemphasize; overstate; play up; stretch (figuratively); three-sheet
