## Verb

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To compose (a document), especially following a standard form; prepare a plan.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I asked my lawyer to draw up a new will.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To arrange in order or formation.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Sergeant, please draw the men up in ranks of three.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To cause to come to a halt.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Draw up the carriage just around the corner!

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To come to a halt.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The tractor drew up alongside the haystack.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To withdraw upwards.
