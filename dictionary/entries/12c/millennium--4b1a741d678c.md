## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /mɪˈlɛnɪəm/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Forms

- millennia — plural
- millenniums — plural
- millenium — alternative; archaic

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A period of time consisting of one thousand years.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source synonyms:** chiliad; kiloannum; mul&#58;ka; kiloyear; kyr; yearthousand

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The year in which one period of one thousand years ends and another begins, especially the year 2000.

   **Usage:** with-definite-article

   **Example:** A huge fireworks display was put on in Sydney to celebrate the millennium.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any long (and possibly endless) era of universal happiness, peace, and prosperity, predicted by various people to come about someday.

   **Usage:** archaic

   **Example:** expecting a millennium

   **Source synonyms:** utopia
