### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The feathers covering the body of a bird collectively.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences; ornithology

   **Source synonyms:** pennage (obsolete)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Feathers used ornamentally; feathering; (countable) a bunch or tuft of such feathers; a plume.

   **Usage:** archaic; broadly; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Feathers which are fed to a hawk to cause it to regurgitate, for the purpose of cleaning its crop; a type of casting.

   **Usage:** broadly; obsolete; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** falconry; hobbies; hunting; lifestyle

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A person's dress, especially when elaborate or fine.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A person's external appearance, especially when showy.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable
