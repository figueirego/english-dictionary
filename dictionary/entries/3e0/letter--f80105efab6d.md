### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A symbol in an alphabet.

   **Example:** There are twenty-six letters in the English alphabet.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A written or printed communication, usually defined as longer and more formal than a note. (Sometimes specifically one that is on paper.)

   **Example:** I wrote a letter to my sister about my life.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The literal meaning of something, as distinguished from its intended and remoter meaning (the spirit).

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Literature.

   **Usage:** in-plural

   **Example:** Benjamin Franklin was multiskilled – a scientist, politician and a man of letters.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A division unit of a piece of law marked by a letter of the alphabet.

   **Example:** Letter (b) constitutes an exception to this provision.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A size of paper, 8½ in × 11 in (215.9 mm × 279.4 mm).

   **Usage:** US; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** short bond paper

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A size of paper, 215 mm × 280 mm.

   **Usage:** Canada; uncountable

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Clipping of varsity letter.

   **Usage:** US; abbreviation; alt-of; clipping; scholastic

   **Alternative form of:** [varsity letter](../055/varsity-letter--156cc988201f.md)

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A single type; type, collectively; a style of type.

   **Usage:** dated

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** media; printing; publishing
