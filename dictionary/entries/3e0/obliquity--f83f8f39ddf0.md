### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The quality of being oblique in direction, deviating from the horizontal or vertical; or the angle created by such a deviation.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** obliqueness

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Axial tilt.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astronomy; natural-sciences

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a planet

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Mental or moral deviation or perversity; immorality.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** degeneracy; depravity; perversion; baseness; corruption; defilement; degeneracy; degradation; depravation; depravedness; depravity; impurity; iniquity; lewdity; malignity; misdoing; misguilt; obliquity; peccability; perversion; pollution; pravity (archaic); sin; sinfulness; turpitude; vitiation; wickedness; wrongdoing

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The quality of being obscure, oftentimes willfully, sometimes as an exercise in euphemism.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** obscurity
