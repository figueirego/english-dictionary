### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To become wide or wider.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** His eyes widened as her negligee fell to the floor.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To make wide or wider.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To let out clothes to a larger size.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** She widened his trousers for him.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To broaden or extend in scope or range.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The police widened their enquiries.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To convert to a data type that can hold a larger number of distinct values.

   **Usage:** transitive
