### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Places in Ukraine&#58;
   1. A former fortress-city on the Crimea, founded in antiquity as Taphros, believed to have first been fortified by the Crimean Scythians, rendered uninhabitable in November 1920 by the Perekop–Chonhar Operation of the Russian Civil War; the fortress ruins are known by their Crimean Tatar name, Or Qapı.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Places in Ukraine&#58;
   1. The isthmus in Ukraine that connects the Crimea with the Ukrainian mainland, separating Karkinit Bay to its west from the Syvash to its east.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Places in Ukraine&#58;
   1. A village in Suvorove silrada, Perekop Raion, the Crimea, Ukraine, founded in the wake of the fortress-city’s destruction and named for it.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Places in Ukraine&#58;
   1. A village in Henichesk urban hromada, Henichesk Raion, Kherson Oblast, Ukraine, founded in 1926.

5. Places in Ukraine&#58;
