## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The start of the fourth hour of the day; 3&#58;00 a.m. (03&#58;00).

   **Usage:** uncountable; In both the 12-hour clock and the 24-hour clock

   **Example:** The front desk closes at three o'clock (= "The front desk closes at 3&#58;00 a.m.").

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The start of the sixteenth hour of the day; 3&#58;00 p.m. (15&#58;00).

   **Usage:** uncountable; Only in the 12-hour clock

   **Example:** The next train comes at three o'clock (= "The next train comes at 15&#58;00").

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To the right (horizontal clock orientation) (from the location of the 3 mark on a clock face)

   **Usage:** informal; uncountable

   **Example:** We've got a bogey at three o'clock.
