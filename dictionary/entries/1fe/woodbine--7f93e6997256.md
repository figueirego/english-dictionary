### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of several climbing vines, especially the honeysuckle (Lonicera spp.) and the creepers of genus Parthenocissus.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of several climbing vines, especially the honeysuckle (Lonicera spp.) and the creepers of genus Parthenocissus.
   1. in genus Lonicera&#58;
      1. European honeysuckle or common honeysuckle (Lonicera periclymenum).

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of several climbing vines, especially the honeysuckle (Lonicera spp.) and the creepers of genus Parthenocissus.
   1. in genus Lonicera&#58;
      1. European fly honeysuckle, dwarf honeysuckle, or fly woodbine (Lonicera xylosteum).

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any of several climbing vines, especially the honeysuckle (Lonicera spp.) and the creepers of genus Parthenocissus.
   1. in genus Parthenocissus&#58;
      1. Virginia creeper Parthenocissus quinquefolia.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Any of several climbing vines, especially the honeysuckle (Lonicera spp.) and the creepers of genus Parthenocissus.
   1. in genus Parthenocissus&#58;
      1. Japanese creeper or Boston ivy (Parthenocissus tricuspidata).

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Any of several climbing vines, especially the honeysuckle (Lonicera spp.) and the creepers of genus Parthenocissus.
   1. in genus Parthenocissus&#58;
      1. Thicket creeper, false Virginia creeper, of grape woodbine (Parthenocissus inserta, syn. Parthenocissus vitacea).

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Any of several climbing vines, especially the honeysuckle (Lonicera spp.) and the creepers of genus Parthenocissus.
   1. Devil's darning needle (Clematis virginiana).

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Any of several climbing vines, especially the honeysuckle (Lonicera spp.) and the creepers of genus Parthenocissus.
   1. Yellow jessamine (Gelsemium sempervirens).
