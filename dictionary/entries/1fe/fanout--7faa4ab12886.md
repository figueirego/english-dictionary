### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The degree to which something fans out, or splits into separate sections.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** B+ trees have very high fanout, which reduces the number of I/O operations required to find an element in the tree.

   **Example:** The fanout of a logic gate output is the number of gate inputs to which it is connected.
