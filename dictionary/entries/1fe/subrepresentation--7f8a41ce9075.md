### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Given a representation (W,ψ) of (say) a group G, a linear subspace of W which is preserved by the action of G in the sense that g · v ∈ V for all v ∈ V.

   **Usage:** representation theory

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences
