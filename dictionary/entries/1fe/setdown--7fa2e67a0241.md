## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of setting down something or someone.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** setdown areas in a factory for materials unloaded from incoming vehicles

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The act of descending onto a surface (of an aircraft or spacecraft).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** landing; touchdown

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The humbling of a person by act or words.

   **Usage:** countable; dated; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A retort or a reproof that has a humbling effect.

   **Usage:** countable; dated; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** put-down

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A sit-down meal eaten by a tramp; a charitable meal provided to a tramp in the giver's home.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; slang; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A person’s buttocks.

   **Usage:** US; countable; obsolete; slang; uncountable
