<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To rub a surface with a sharp object, especially by a living creature to remove itching with nails, claws, etc.

   **Example:** Could you please scratch my back?

   **Source synonyms:** scrabble

2. To rub the skin with rough material causing a sensation of irritation; to cause itching.

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To rub the skin with rough material causing a sensation of irritation; to cause itching.
   1. To irritate someone's skin with one's unshaven beard when kissing.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To mark a surface with a sharp object, thereby leaving a scratch (noun).

   **Example:** A real diamond can easily scratch a pane of glass.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To get such scratches.

   **Example:** This platter scratches easily.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a surface

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To cross out, strike out, strike through some text on a page.

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To cross out, strike out, strike through some text on a page.
   1. Hence, to remove, ignore, or delete.
