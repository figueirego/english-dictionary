## Noun

### Forms

- penalty loops — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A branch of a racecourse that adds a loop that starts and ends at the branching point, to extend the course for use in penalizing competitors with extra distance to travel.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A penalty assessed to competitors in a race, where they must race through a penalty loop branch of a racecourse.
