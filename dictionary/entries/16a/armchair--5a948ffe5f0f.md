## Adjective · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈɑː(ɹ)mtʃɛə(ɹ)/ — source labels: UK
- IPA: /ˈɑɹmt͡ʃɛɚ/ — source labels: US

### Forms

- arm-chair — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Remote from actual involvement (including cases where a person has retired from previously active involvement).

   **Usage:** figuratively; not-comparable

   **Example:** armchair travels

   **Example:** These days I'm an armchair detective.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Unqualified or uninformed but yet giving advice, especially on technical issues, such as law, architecture, medicine, military theory, or sports; relating to such advice.

   **Usage:** derogatory; figuratively; not-comparable

   **Example:** He's just an armchair lawyer who thinks he knows a lot about the law because he reads a legal blog.

   **Example:** After the game, the armchair quarterbacks talked about what they would have done differently to win.
