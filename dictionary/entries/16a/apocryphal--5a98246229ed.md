### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of, or pertaining to, the Apocrypha.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of doubtful authenticity, or lacking authority; not regarded as canonical.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** Many scholars consider the stories of the monk Teilo to be apocryphal.

   **Source synonyms:** allonymous; spurious

   **Source antonyms:** canonical

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of dubious veracity; of questionable accuracy or truthfulness; anecdotal or in the nature of an urban legend.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** There is an apocryphal tale of a little boy plugging the dike with his finger.

   **Source synonyms:** anecdotal
