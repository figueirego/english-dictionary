## Noun

### Forms

- revaluations — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The process of altering the relative value of a currency or other standard of exchange.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** After the new party took power, the government declared a revaluation of the currency in an attempt to limit runaway inflation.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A reassessment of the value or worth of something; a reappraisal or reevaluation.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** After the soldiers raided her farm for supplies, she was forced to a revaluation of their benefit as protectors.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The application of compound growth to the value of a pension benefit, specifically from the date of the member leaving the scheme (for example, moving to a different employer) to the date that the member starts receiving the benefit (typically retirement).

   **Usage:** UK; countable; uncountable; pensions
