### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Either of the two openings in the abdominal muscle wall allowing the inguinal canal to pass through.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A form of body art wherein jewelry is placed in a cauterized puncture of the umbilicus.

   **Example:** I think 10 years old is too young for an abdominal ring.
