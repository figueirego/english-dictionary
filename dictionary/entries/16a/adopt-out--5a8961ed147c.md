### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To expel a child from a family by placing them for adoption; to put a child up for adoption privately, without going through an adoption agency.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To send a son or daughter away to live in another country. (Used with to).

   **Example:** The Chinese girl was adopted out to the United States.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To send a child to be adopted by family of a different culture and ethnicity.
