## Verb · Record 2

### Pronunciation

### Forms

- overflows — present; singular; third-person
- overflowing — participle; present
- overflowed — past
- overflowed — participle; past
- overflown — participle; past; proscribed; sometimes

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To flow over the brim of (a container).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The river overflowed the levee into the road.

   **Source synonyms:** overrun

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To cover with a liquid, literally or figuratively.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The flash flood overflowed most of the parkland and some homes.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To cause an overflow.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To flow over the brim of a container.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The waters overflowed into the Ninth Ward.

   **Source synonyms:** brim over; overbrim; overflood

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To be subject to a load that exceeds limits or capacity.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The hospital ER was overflowing with flu cases.

   **Source synonyms:** brim over; overbrim

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To be subject to a load that exceeds limits or capacity.
   1. To have something in superabundance; to abound in something.

      **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

      **Source synonyms:** brim over; overbrim

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

**Source sense R2.S7**

7. To (cause to) exceed the available numeric range.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** Calculating 255+1 will overflow an eight-bit byte.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences
