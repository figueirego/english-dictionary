### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A mythological creature (usually humanoid and undead) said to feed on the blood or life energy of the living.

   **Example:** Bram Stoker's novel Dracula built on centuries-old stories of vampires and also encouraged new growth of the mythology.

   **Source synonyms:** nosferatu; lamia

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person with habits traditionally ascribed to (literal) vampires, such as heliophobia, being a night owl, having pale skin, and so on.

   **Usage:** colloquial; humorous; usually

   **Example:** Somehow I doubt that Kelly will want to go surfing with us — she's such a vampire that she might recoil from the sun, lol!

   **Source synonyms:** heliophobe; goth

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A person with the medical condition porphyria cutanea tarda, colloquially known as vampirism, with effects such as photosensitivity and brownish-red stained teeth.

   **Usage:** colloquial; offensive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A blood-sucking bat; vampire bat (Desmodus rotundus)

   **Source synonyms:** vampire bat

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A person who drains one's time, energy, money, etc.

   **Usage:** derogatory; figuratively

   **Example:** emotional vampire

   **Example:** You have to be careful what you tell her, because she's such a vampire for drama.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A vamp&#58; a seductive woman who exploits men.

   **Usage:** dated

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A medical technician who works with patients' blood; especially, a phlebotomist.

   **Usage:** US; slang

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Synonym of anti-ship missile (ASM), particularly an incoming hostile one.

   **Usage:** US; slang

   **Example:** Vampire. Vampire. Vampire. Battle stations.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war

   **Source synonyms:** anti-ship missile (synonym; synonym-of)
