## Verb

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make a mess of; to untidy, disorder, soil, or muss.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The afternoon breeze messed up my hair.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To cause a problem with; to introduce an error or mistake in; to make muddled or confused; spoil; ruin.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The change messed something up, and it's not working anymore.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To botch, bungle; to perform poorly on.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Well, I messed up my solo, but otherwise it was a good concert.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To make a mistake; to do something incorrectly; to perform poorly.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** He has a hard time getting started because he's afraid he'll mess up.

   **Example:** She messed up on her final exam.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To cause (another person) to make unwanted mistakes in a given task, usually through distraction or obnoxious behavior.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Stop bumping me! You keep messing me up!

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To damage; injure.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He messed up his elbow at the track meet.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To manhandle; beat up; rough up.

   **Usage:** slang; transitive

   **Example:** Her brother's friends messed him up a little after he cheated on her.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To discombobulate, utterly confuse, or confound psychologically; to throw into a state of mental disarray.

   **Usage:** slang; transitive

   **Example:** That girl totally messed me up, man. I'm not sure who I am anymore.
