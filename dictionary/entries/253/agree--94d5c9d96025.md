### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To be in harmony about an opinion, statement, or action; to have a consistent idea between two or more people.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** All parties agree in the expediency of the law.

   **Example:** I mostly agree with what you said, but I consider your last point to be unfair.

   **Example:** I couldn’t agree more with what you say, but my brother still doesn't quite agree.

   **Source synonyms:** concur; stipulate; **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To give assent; to accede.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** It was agreed to meet here at midday.

   **Example:** The workers did not agree to the new terms offered by the trade union.

   **Source context (verbatim):** followed by "to"

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To yield assent to; to approve.

   **Usage:** Ireland; UK; transitive

   **Example:** We agreed a bargain yesterday.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To make a stipulation by way of settling differences or determining a price; to exchange promises; to come to terms or to a common resolve; to promise.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To resemble; to coincide; to correspond; to tally.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The picture does not agree with the original.

   **Example:** The two scales agree exactly.

   **Example:** The witnesses' stories agree in most details with the accused's.

   **Source synonyms:** check

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To suit or be adapted in its effects; to do well.

   **Usage:** intransitive; now always with with

   **Example:** The same food does not agree with every constitution.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To correspond to (another word) in a grammatical category, such as gender, number, case, or person.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** In Romanian, all articles, adjectives, and pronouns agree in gender, number and case with the noun they refer to.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** grammar; human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To consent to a contract or to an element of a contract.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law
