### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of things or settings, having been organized; in good order.

   **Example:** Your desk is so organized!

   **Source antonyms:** disorganized; cluttered; disarrayed; messy; chaotic

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of a person, characterized by efficient organization.

   **Example:** Your desk is so neat and tidy — I've never met someone so organized before!

   **Source antonyms:** disorganized; messy

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Unionized.

   **Usage:** labour

   **Source synonyms:** unionized

   **Source antonyms:** unorganized; ununionized
