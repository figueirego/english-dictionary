### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any room where visitors may be entertained; now, the living room.

   **Usage:** UK; somewhat dated outside of India

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A multifunctional room that can be used for any purpose in a palace or castle.

   **Usage:** UK

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A levée where ladies are presented at court or to society.

   **Usage:** UK

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A private room on a railroad sleeping car.

   **Usage:** US
