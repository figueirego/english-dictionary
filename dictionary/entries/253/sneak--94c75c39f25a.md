### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To creep or go stealthily; to come or go while trying to avoid detection, as a person who does not wish to be seen.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** He decided to sneak into the kitchen for a second cookie while his mom was on the phone.

   **Source synonyms:** skulk; steal; slink

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To take (something) in a surreptitious manner, so as to avoid being noticed.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I went to sneak a chocolate but my dad caught me.

   **Source synonyms:** pilfer; filch

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To stealthily bring someone something.

   **Usage:** ditransitive

   **Example:** She asked me to sneak her a phone next month.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To hide, especially in a mean or cowardly manner.

   **Usage:** dated; transitive

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To inform an authority of another's misdemeanours.

   **Usage:** informal; intransitive; with-on

   **Example:** If you sneak on me I'll bash you!

   **Source synonyms:** grass; snitch; tell tales
