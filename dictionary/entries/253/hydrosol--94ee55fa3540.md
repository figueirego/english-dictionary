### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A sol (solid-in-liquid colloid) in which the continuous phase (dispersing medium) is water.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An herbal distillate&#58; an aqueous product, made from plants (especially herbs) via steam distillation (usually specifically hydrodistillation); such a product is a colloidal suspension of essential oils that also has some water-soluble components dissolved in the water.
