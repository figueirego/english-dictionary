## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A rare congenital brain malformation in which the cerebellar vermis does not fully form, and the fourth ventricle and posterior fossa are enlarged with cerebrospinal fluid.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** Dandy-Walker syndrome; DWM; DWS
