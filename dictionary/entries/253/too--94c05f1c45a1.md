### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Likewise.

   **Usage:** focus; not-comparable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Also, in addition marks a statement as equally valid as the preceding one.

   **Usage:** conjunctive; not-comparable

   **Example:** There has been a cutback in federal subsidies. Rates have been increasing too.

   **Example:** He's always willing to help. He, too, is generous with his time.

   **Example:** It is important to remember, too, that she is generous with his time. Sometimes, too, she does ask for the odd favor.

   **Source synonyms:** as well; additionally; eke

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To an excessive degree, more than enough indicates that the degree of a quality is more than what is needed or wanted.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; degree

   **Example:** I'm still much too young to drive, and my grandpa is already far too old.

   **Example:** This water is too polluted for us to drink (it).

   **Example:** Don't be too quick to blame others.

   **Source synonyms:** unduly; over; over-; overly; excessively; extremely; unnecessarily; all too

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To a high degree, very.

   **Usage:** colloquial; not-comparable; degree

   **Example:** She doesn't talk too much. I'm not too sure about this.

   **Source synonyms:** so; all that

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Used to contradict a negative assertion with present and simple past forms of be, do, and auxiliary verbs

   **Usage:** childish; colloquial; emphatic; not-comparable

   **Example:** You're not old enough yet. ― I am too!

   **Example:** You can't jump that fence. ― Can too jump it!

   **Example:** We haven't been mean to you. ― Have too, plenty of times

   **Source synonyms:** so

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Used for emphasis, without reference to any previous statement.

   **Usage:** archaic; colloquial; not-comparable
