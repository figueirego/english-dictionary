### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To represent in a physical or concrete form; to incarnate or personify.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** As the car salesman approached, wearing a plaid suit and slicked-back hair, he seemed to embody sleaze.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To represent in some other form, such as a code of laws.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The US Constitution aimed to embody the ideals of diverse groups of people, from Puritans to Deists.

   **Example:** The principle was recognized by some of the early Greek philosophers who embodied it in their systems.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To comprise or include as part of a cohesive whole; to be made up of.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To unite in a body or mass.

   **Usage:** intransitive
