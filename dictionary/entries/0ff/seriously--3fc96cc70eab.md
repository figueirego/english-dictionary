### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In a serious or literal manner.

   **Example:** He was hoping that we would take him seriously.

   **Example:** Jimmy jokingly called Bob a doofus. Bob took the insult seriously.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** manner

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Gravely; deeply; very much.

   **Example:** That was a seriously unpleasant thing to say.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used to attempt to introduce a serious point in a less serious conversation.

   **Example:** Now, seriously, why did you forget to feed the cat today?

   **Source synonyms:** no really; no kidding; real talk

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Used to call back to a previous point, in disbelief or for emphasis.

   **Example:** You baked ten (10) cakes. Seriously, why did you do that?

   **Source synonyms:** no really; no kidding

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. In an extreme or major way; majorly.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** Unless you're seriously strapped (armed), you're about to be not okay too.
