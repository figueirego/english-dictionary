### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A tutelary angel watching over a particular person (and sometimes rescuing them) in monotheistic contexts.

   **Example:** I think your guardian angel was working overtime this week. You're lucky to be unscathed.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** lifestyle; religion

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person who benevolently watches over another or rescues them.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** The guy who came upon the accident scene was my guardian angel. He doused the flames and called the paramedics.
