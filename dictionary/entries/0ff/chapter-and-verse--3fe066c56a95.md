## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The chapter number and verse number (within a particular book) that locate a quotation from the Bible.

   **Usage:** literally; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Specific references from an authoritative book or document, cited to support a statement or position.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Very full and detailed information.

   **Usage:** informal; uncountable

   **Example:** The main suspect refused to answer any questions, but the police got chapter and verse from his accomplice.
