### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To deliver into the hands of an enemy by treachery or fraud, in violation of trust; to give up treacherously or faithlessly.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** An officer betrayed the city.

   **Example:** He betrayed his own sister to the secret police.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To prove faithless or treacherous to, as to a trust or one who trusts; to be false to; to deceive.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to betray a person or a cause

   **Example:** Quresh betrayed Sunil to marry Nuzhat.

   **Example:** My eyes have been betraying me since I turned sixty.

   **Source synonyms:** sell; quisle; traitorize

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To violate the confidence of, by disclosing a secret, or that which one is bound in honor not to make known.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To disclose (a secret, etc.) in deliberate violation of someone’s confidence.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** give away

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To disclose or indicate, for example something which prudence would conceal; to reveal unintentionally.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Though he had lived in England for many years, a faint accent betrayed his Swedish origin.

   **Source synonyms:** give away

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To mislead; to expose to inconvenience not foreseen; to lead into error or sin.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To lead astray; to seduce (as under promise of marriage) and then abandon.

   **Usage:** transitive
