### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The meat of sheep used as food; especially, that of adults.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** sheepflesh; sheepmeat

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The meat of a goat, urial, or other caprine.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; often qualified with the type

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A sheep.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Em, a unit of measurement equal to the height of the type in use.

   **Usage:** countable; slang; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** media; publishing; typography

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A prostitute.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; slang; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** laced mutton

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. An old Anglo-French gold coin impressed with the image of a lamb.

   **Usage:** countable; historical; uncountable
