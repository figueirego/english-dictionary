## Noun · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The interval between the two halves of a sports match.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Let's get some pizza at half-time.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Half or approximately half the number of hours usually worked in a specified period.

   **Usage:** attributive; countable; sometimes; uncountable

   **Example:** I'm following a half-time schedule this month.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A tempo in which the rhythm is emphasized so that it seems to have half as many metric divisions (e.g. emphasizing the start of every other bar rather than every bar).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The time taken for a physical quantity to halve the difference between its present value and its final value.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics
