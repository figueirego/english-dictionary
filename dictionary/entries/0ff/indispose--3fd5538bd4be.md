### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To render unfit or unsuited; to disqualify.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To make indisposed, or slightly unwell.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
