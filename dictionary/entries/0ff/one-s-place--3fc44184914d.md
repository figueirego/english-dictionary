## Noun

### Forms

- one's places — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One's position (location, post, rank stance, status, situation) in life.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** One's future place in the company is a hard question.

   **Example:** Hey, you're in my place! You know I always sit here!

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One's position (location, post, rank stance, status, situation) in life.
   1. One's position in a team, company, etc.

      **Usage:** idiomatic

      **Example:** Steve left if you want his place on the team.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. One's position (location, post, rank stance, status, situation) in life.
   1. One's social position in a hierarchy.

      **Usage:** idiomatic

      **Example:** Your place is in the kitchen washing dishes.

      **Example:** You have your place in the coal mines!

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. One's position (location, post, rank stance, status, situation) in life.
   1. Instead of someone or something else; as a substitute for someone or something (compare in place of).

      **Usage:** idiomatic

      **Example:** In the new game, in the Deagle's place, we now got the Colt 45.
