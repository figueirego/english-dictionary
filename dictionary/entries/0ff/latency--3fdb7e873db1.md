### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Concealment; the state of being latent; the state of being hidden.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Dormancy; the state of being inactive.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A delay, an interval between the initiation of something and the occurrence.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** network latency

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; electrical-engineering; electricity; electromagnetism; electronics; energy; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The time delay between a request for data or action and the beginning of the response or execution, often measured to assess system performance.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; artificial intelligence

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The delay between a stimulus and the response it triggers in an organism.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** period of latency

   **Example:** The disease has a long period of latency.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A stage in Sigmund Freud's psychoanalytic theory of the psychosexual development of children where children become asexual until their sexual desires come back at puberty.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
