### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who or that which operates a switch.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One who or that which switches, that is, changes in some way.

   **Example:** career switcher

   **Example:** Switch to Acme brand razors for a closer shave! For a limited time, we'll pay you to be a switcher!
