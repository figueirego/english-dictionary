### Forms

- floods — present; singular; third-person
- flooding — participle; present
- flooded — participle; past
- flooded — past
- floud — alternative; obsolete

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To overflow, as by water from excessive rainfall.

   **Example:** People flooded into the streets in protest.

   **Example:** The basement flooded with rainwater.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To cover or partly fill as if by a flood.

   **Example:** The floor was flooded with beer.

   **Example:** They flooded the room with sewage.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To provide (someone or something) with a larger number or quantity of something than can easily be dealt with.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** The station's switchboard was flooded with listeners making complaints.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To paste numerous lines of text to (a chat system) in order to disrupt the conversation.

   **Usage:** Internet; ambitransitive

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To bleed profusely, as after childbirth.
