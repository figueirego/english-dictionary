### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of alienating.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** The alienation of that viewing demographic is a poor business decision.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The state of being alienated.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** His alienation from his family means he has nowhere to go at Christmas.

   **Source synonyms:** estrangement

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Emotional isolation or dissociation, as from being separated from social connections or estranged from one's family or community; a feeling of being an outsider.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** I didn't care about anything back then, not holidays or family or my career, I felt utter alienation from everything around me.

   **Example:** When parents get divorced, their child sometimes experiences parental alienation, in which they are estranged from the noncustodial parent.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Verfremdungseffekt.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; theater

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The transfer of property to another person.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law; property

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The estrangement of people from aspects of their human nature as a consequence of the division of labour and living in a society of stratified social classes (e.g., under capitalism or feudalism).

   **Usage:** Marxism; uncountable; usually
