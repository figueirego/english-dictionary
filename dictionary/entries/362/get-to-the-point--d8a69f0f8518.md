### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To state (something) directly; as opposed to in a long-winded way.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I don't have much time, so could you please get to the point.

   **Source synonyms:** cut to the chase; get on with; come to the point

   **Source antonyms:** beat around the bush

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see get, to, the, point.

   **Example:** We got to the point where we don't know how to continue.
