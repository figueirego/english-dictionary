## Adverb · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ɪnˈdiːd/ — source labels: Received-Pronunciation
- IPA: /ɪnˈdid/ — source labels: General-American

### Forms

- endeed — alternative; obsolete

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Synonym of actually or truly.

   **Usage:** modal; not-comparable

   **Example:** Indeed, he made several misplays.

   **Example:** Yes, I do indeed look very similar to you.

   **Source synonyms:** yea (archaic); actually or truly (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In fact.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; degree

   **Example:** As a soccer player, he is terrible indeed.

   **Source context (verbatim):** after the adjective modified

## Interjection · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ɪnˈdiːd/ — source labels: Received-Pronunciation
- IPA: /ɪnˈdid/ — source labels: General-American

### Forms

- endeed — alternative; obsolete
- indeedy — alternative; Canada; US; regional

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Indicates agreement with another speaker's previous statement.

   **Example:** "I am a great runner." "Indeed!"

   **Source synonyms:** absolutely; for real; forsooth; indubitably; sure thing; true that; facts; fax; trufax; that's right

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Indicates doubt or disagreement with another speaker's previous statement.

   **Example:** "I am a great runner." "Indeed?"

   **Source context (verbatim):** with interrogative intonation
