### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A master of arts or other subjects whose regency has ceased, and therefore is no longer obliged to lecture.

   **Usage:** UK; historical; universities

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Someone who is not a member of the Board of Regents, especially in the Smithsonian Institution

   **Usage:** US
