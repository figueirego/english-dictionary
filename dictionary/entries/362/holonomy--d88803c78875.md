### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Given a smooth closed curve C on a surface M, and picking any point P on that curve, the holonomy of C in M is the angle by which some vector turns as it is parallel transported along the curve C from point P all the way around and back to point P.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; differential geometry
