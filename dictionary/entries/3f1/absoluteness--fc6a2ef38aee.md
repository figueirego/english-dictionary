### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The fact of being finished or perfected; completeness.

   **Usage:** obsolete; uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The characteristic of being absolute in nature or scope.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** the absoluteness of his sovereignty, the absoluteness of his convictions

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Absolute authority, unlimited power; absolutism, despotism.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The fact of being without qualifications or conditions; certainty, unconditionality.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Independent autonomy.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually
