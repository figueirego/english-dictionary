### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A fruit-bearing shrub of the aggregate species Rubus fruticosus and some hybrids.

   **Source synonyms:** bramble; brambleberry

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The soft fruit borne by this shrub, formed of a black (when ripe) cluster of drupelets.

   **Source synonyms:** bramble; brambleberry

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any Rubus berry that is black or blackish; the plant that produces it.

   **Usage:** broadly; informal
