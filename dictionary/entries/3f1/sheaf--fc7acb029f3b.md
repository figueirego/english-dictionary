<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. An abstract construct in topology that associates data to the open sets of a topological space (i.e. a presheaf) in such a way so as to make the local and global data compatible, generalizing the situation of functions, fiber bundles, manifold structure, etc. on a topological space. Formally, a presheaf ℱ whose sections are, in a technical sense, uniquely determined by their restrictions onto smaller sets&#58; that is, given an open cover U\_i of U&#58;
   1. If two sections over U agree under restriction to every U\_i, then the sections are the same.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. An abstract construct in topology that associates data to the open sets of a topological space (i.e. a presheaf) in such a way so as to make the local and global data compatible, generalizing the situation of functions, fiber bundles, manifold structure, etc. on a topological space. Formally, a presheaf ℱ whose sections are, in a technical sense, uniquely determined by their restrictions onto smaller sets&#58; that is, given an open cover U\_i of U&#58;
   1. Given a family of sections s\_i∈ℱ(U\_i) such that all pairs (s\_i,s\_j) agree under restriction to U\_i∩U\_j, there is a (unique) section s over U whose restriction to U\_i is s\_i.
