### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A cross marked on a building occupied by victims of plague, as a warning of contagion.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A stone structure set up outside the established market areas in time of plague, allowing town and country dwellers to trade with one another while supposedly minimising the risk of contagion.

   **Usage:** historical
