### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. With, or as if with, a roaring sound.

   **Example:** roaringly angry; roaringly drunk

   **Example:** The train came roaringly out of the tunnel.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Example:** a roaringly popular marketplace
