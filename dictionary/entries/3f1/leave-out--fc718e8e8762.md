### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To omit, to not include, to neglect to mention.

   **Example:** The journalist decided to leave out certain details from her story.

   **Example:** The journalist decided to leave the sleaze out of her story.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To cease or desist from (a behaviour or activity); see also leave it out.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** Hey! Leave out the complaining now, will you?

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see leave, out.

   **Example:** After breakfast, there were still some ingredients left out that needed to be put away.
