### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A sex chromosome that differs from an ordinary autosome in form, size, or behaviour.

   **Example:** The human sex chromosomes are a typical pair of allosomes. The X chromosome is present in the ovum, while either X or Y chromosomes can be present in sperm.
