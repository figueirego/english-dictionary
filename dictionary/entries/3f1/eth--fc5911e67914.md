**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used to form the third-person singular present indicative of verbs.

   **Usage:** archaic; morpheme

   **Example:** Pride goeth before a fall... The good Lord giveth and the good Lord taketh away...

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used broadly with various verb forms for ironic pseudoarchaic or pseudoecclesiastical effect.

   **Usage:** humorous; morpheme

   **Example:** Hark, the assistant manager doth emaileth ye... But thou saideth!... Rolling a natural 20, he killedeth the kobolds... Verily, I am shooketh...
