**Source sense R1.S1**

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A device, electronic or mechanical, that helps a person tune a musical instrument by showing the deviation of the played pitch from the desired pitch.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>
