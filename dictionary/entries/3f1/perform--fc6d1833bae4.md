### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To do (something); to execute.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The scientists performed several experiments.

   **Example:** It took him only twenty minutes to perform the task.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To exhibit an expected pattern of behavior; to function; to work.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The new employee performs well.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To exhibit an expected pattern of behavior; to function; to work.
   1. Of a man, to be able to initiate or fulfill an act of sexual intercourse.

      **Usage:** intransitive; uncommon

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** lifestyle; sexology; sexuality

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To act in a way set forth in a contract.
   1. To act in accordance with (a contract); to fulfill one’s terms of (a contract).

      **Usage:** transitive

      **Example:** Failure to perform a contract on time may constitute a breach of contract.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To act in a way set forth in a contract.
   1. To fulfill contractually agreed-to terms.

      **Usage:** intransitive

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law
