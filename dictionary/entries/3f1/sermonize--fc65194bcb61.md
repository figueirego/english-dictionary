### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To speak in the manner of a sermon; to preach; to propagate one's morality or opinions with speech.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To preach a sermon to (somebody); to give (somebody) instruction or admonishment on the basis of one's morality or opinions.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To say in the manner of a sermon or lecture.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To inculcate rigid rules.

   **Usage:** intransitive
