### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To spend time outside, off of the Internet. Used in the imperative to suggest that someone is out of touch with reality, and should gain some real-world perspective.

   **Usage:** Internet; derogatory; often dismissal

   **Example:** Maybe their idle and petty chatter wouldn't devastate you if you would touch grass a bit more often.

   **Source synonyms:** go outside

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see touch, grass.

   **Example:** Q&#58; Can't you read?! My sign says to stay off the lawn! A&#58; I didn't touch your grass, pal.
