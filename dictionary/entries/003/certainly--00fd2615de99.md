### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In a way which is certain; with certainty.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Without doubt, surely.

   **Example:** The accident was certainly caused by human error.

   **Example:** That was certainly sweet of him.

   **Example:** 'I'm all grown up now' -'You certainly have (grown)!'

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An emphatic affirmative answer; of course.

   **Example:** Would you like it with ice? Certainly, and with lemon please.
