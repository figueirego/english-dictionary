### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A weasel found in northern latitudes (Mustela erminea in Eurasia, Alaska, and the Arctic, Mustela haidarum in Haida Gwaii, Mustela richardsonii in the rest of North America) which's dark brown fur turns white in winter, apart from the black tip of the tail.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The white fur of this animal, traditionally seen as a symbol of purity and used for judges' robes.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The office of a judge.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; figuratively; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The fur of this animal, used as a heraldic tincture&#58; a white field with a repeating pattern of stylized black spots.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; heraldry; hobbies; lifestyle; monarchy; nobility; politics

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Any of various moths, especially in the family Yponomeutidae

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Plural of ermine.

   **Usage:** countable; form-of; plural; uncountable

   **Form of:** [ermine](../003/ermine--00f696866aa9.md)
