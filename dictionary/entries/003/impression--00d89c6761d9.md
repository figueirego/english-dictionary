### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The indentation or depression made by the pressure of one object on or into another.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** His head made an impression on the pillow.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The overall effect of something, e.g., on a person.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** He tried to make a good impression on his parents.

   **Source synonyms:** take

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A vague recalling of an event, a belief.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** I have the impression that he's already left for Paris.

   **Source synonyms:** perception

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An impersonation, an imitation of the mannerisms of another person.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An outward appearance.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A performance metric representing an instance where a post or ad is shown once.

   **Usage:** Internet; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The first coat of colour, such as the priming in house-painting etc.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; painting

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A print on paper from a wood block, metal plate, etc.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
