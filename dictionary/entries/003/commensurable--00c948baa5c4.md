## Adjective

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Able to be measured using a common standard.

   **Example:** A yard and a foot are commensurable, as both may be measured by inches.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Related in size or scale; commensurate or proportionate.

   **Example:** His salary is commensurable to his ability.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. (of two or more numbers) Divisible by the same number ᵂᴾ

   **Example:** The numbers 12 and 18 are commensurable, as both are divisible by 6, while 12 and 19 are incommensurable.
