### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A small unfretted stringed instrument with four strings tuned (lowest to highest) G-D-A-E, usually held against the chin and played with a bow.

   **Example:** When I play it like this, it's a fiddle; when I play it like this, it's a violin.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any instrument of the violin family, always inclusive of violins, violas and cellos, and sometimes further including the double bass.

   **Usage:** proscribed; sometimes; inexact

   **Example:** The string quartet, one of the most popular groupings in chamber music, is composed entirely of violins&#58; two violins proper, one viola, and one cello.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A violinist in an orchestra or group.

   **Usage:** metonymically

   **Example:** The violins are seated with sufficient elbow room.

   **Example:** She is first violin in the London Symphony Orchestra.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music
