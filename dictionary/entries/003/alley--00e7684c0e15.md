### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A narrow street or passageway, especially one through the middle of a block giving access to the rear of lots of buildings.

   **Example:** The parking lot to my friend's apartment building is in the alley.

   **Source synonyms:** alleyway

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The area between the outfielders.

   **Example:** He hit one deep into the alley.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; baseball; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

   **Source synonyms:** gap

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An establishment where bowling is played.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** bowling; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

   **Source synonyms:** bowling alley

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An elongated wooden strip of floor along which a bowling ball is rolled.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** bowling; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

   **Source synonyms:** lane

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The extra area between the sidelines or tramlines on a tennis court that is used for doubles matches.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports; tennis

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A walk or passage in a garden or park, bordered by rows of trees or bushes.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A passageway between rows of pews in a church.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Any passage having the entrance represented as wider than the exit, so as to give the appearance of length.

   **Usage:** perspective drawing

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. The space between two rows of compositors' stands in a printing office.

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. A group of clowns; the collective noun for clowns.

   **Usage:** informal; rare
