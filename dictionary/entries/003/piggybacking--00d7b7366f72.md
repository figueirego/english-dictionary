## Noun · Record 2

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. The act or process by which someone piggybacks.
   1. Riding on someone else's back.

      **Usage:** uncountable

      **Example:** No piggybacking or other horseplay is allowed in the hallways.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. The act or process by which someone piggybacks.
   1. Transport involving vehicles arranged in an analogous way.

      **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. The act or process by which someone piggybacks.
   1. Inappropriately gaining entry into a restricted area, past a checkpoint, by tagging along behind another person who is authorized.

      **Usage:** uncountable

      **Example:** The security team watches who comes through the door, and they will stop anyone who is seen piggybacking behind a badgeholder.

      **Source synonyms:** tailgating

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. The act or process by which someone piggybacks.
   1. Joining in an activity that is initiated or led by someone else.

      **Usage:** uncountable

      **Example:** Piggybacking on others' innovations seems to be his only plan for success.
