### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To cook (something) by baking.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** When you finish baking off the first batch, start the second.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** cooking; food; lifestyle

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To be cooked by baking.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The cake will finish baking off in an hour.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To cook off or reduce by baking.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Six pounds of batter will bake off to three pounds of prepared product.
