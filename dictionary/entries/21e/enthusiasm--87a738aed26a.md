### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An intensity of feeling; an excited interest or eagerness.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Try to curb your enthusiasm.

   **Example:** They have a great enthusiasm for country music.

   **Source synonyms:** spirit

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Something in which one is keenly interested.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Possession by a god; divine inspiration or frenzy.

   **Usage:** countable; historical; obsolete; uncountable
