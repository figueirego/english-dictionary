<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A task, especially a regularly needed task for the upkeep of a home or similar, such as housecleaning or preparing meals.

   **Example:** Before we moved in together, my partner and I divided up the chores&#58; he cooks and vacuums, and I do the dishes and laundry.

   **Example:** The children were made to do their daily chores before being allowed to play games.

   **Source synonyms:** domesticity (formal)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A task that is difficult, unpleasant, or tediously routine.

   **Example:** I used to enjoy being self-employed, but it's become a bit of a chore recently.
