### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Mental senses related to determining, reckoning, or perceiving
   1. To determine the number, amount, or value of \[something\].

      **Usage:** transitive; archaic outside of idioms

      **Example:** All told, there were over a dozen. Can you tell time on a clock? He had untold wealth.

      **Source synonyms:** count; reckon; enumerate

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Mental senses related to determining, reckoning, or perceiving
   1. To notice, discern. (Roughly, "can tell" means "know" but with a sense of direct perception.)

      **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

      **Example:** I can tell that you're thrilled.

      **Example:** You can tell you've struck a nerve when even the crowd stops laughing.

      **Example:** She couldn't tell the words on the chalkboard without her glasses.

3. Mental senses related to determining, reckoning, or perceiving
