### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To render fit, suitable, or correspondent; to adapt.

   **Usage:** often; reflexive; transitive

   **Example:** to accommodate ourselves to circumstances

   **Source synonyms:** adapt; conform; adjust; arrange; suit

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To cause to come to agreement; to bring about harmony; to reconcile.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to accommodate differences

   **Source synonyms:** reconcile

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To provide housing for.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to accommodate an old friend for a week

   **Source synonyms:** quarter

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To provide sufficient space for.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To contain comfortably; to have space for.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** This venue accommodates three hundred people.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To provide with something desired, needed, or convenient.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to accommodate a friend with a loan

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To do a favor or service for; to oblige.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** oblige

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To show the correspondence of; to apply or make suit by analogy; to adapt or fit, as teachings to accidental circumstances, statements to facts, etc.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to accommodate prophecy to events

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To give consideration to; to allow for.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. To adapt oneself; to be conformable or adapted; become adjusted.

   **Usage:** intransitive; rare

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. Of an eye, to change focal length in order to focus at a different distance.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; ophthalmology; sciences
