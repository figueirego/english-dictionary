**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A village, the administrative centre of Kutsurub rural hromada, Mykolaiv Raion, Mykolaiv Oblast, Ukraine, first mentioned in 1767.
   1. A former silrada of Ochakiv Raion, Mykolaiv Oblast, Ukraine, amalgamated into Kutsurub rural hromada in October 2015; administrative centre and sole constituent settlement&#58; Kutsurub.
