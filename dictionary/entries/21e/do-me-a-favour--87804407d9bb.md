## Phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used to ask someone for a favour (may be polite or sarcastic).

   **Usage:** literally

   **Example:** Could you possibly do me a favour and pass me that newspaper?

   **Example:** Do me a favour and shut up.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Expressing incredulity.

   **Usage:** UK; idiomatic

   **Example:** You expect me to clean up all this mess for you? Do me a favour.
