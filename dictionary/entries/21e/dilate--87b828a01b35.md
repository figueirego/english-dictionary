### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To enlarge; to make bigger.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The eye doctor put drops in my eye to dilate the pupil so he could see the nerve better.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To become wider or larger; to expand.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** His heart dilates and glories in his strength.

   **Source antonyms:** contract

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To speak largely and copiously; to dwell in narration; to enlarge; with "on" or "upon".

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To use a dilator to widen (something, such as a vagina).

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences
