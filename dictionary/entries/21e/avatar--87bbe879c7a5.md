### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An incarnation of a deity, particularly Vishnu.

   **Usage:** Hinduism

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A representation within a dimension of an entity that exists outside of that dimension.
   1. One which serves to act in place of or on behalf of the entity; a simulacrum.

      **Usage:** broadly

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A representation within a dimension of an entity that exists outside of that dimension.
   1. One which serves to act in place of or on behalf of the entity; a simulacrum.
      1. The embodiment of an idea or concept; an instantiation, especially an incarnation or personification.

         **Usage:** broadly

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A representation within a dimension of an entity that exists outside of that dimension.
   1. One which serves as the means through which the entity interacts with its environment; a player character or host.

      **Usage:** broadly

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A representation within a dimension of an entity that exists outside of that dimension.
   1. One which serves as the identifying token for the entity within the dimension.
      1. A graphical representation of a user or persona; used in reference to any digital environment, but especially nonmetaversal ones.

         **Usage:** broadly

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A representation within a dimension of an entity that exists outside of that dimension.
   1. One which serves as the identifying token for the entity within the dimension.
      1. A character model, used virtually as an emulation of a user or their persona.

         **Usage:** broadly

         **Source synonyms:** avi; skin
