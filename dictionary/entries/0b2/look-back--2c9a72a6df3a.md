### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To think of (past events) in retrospect (in hindsight).

   **Example:** When I look back at my summer holidays as a child, I smile.

   **Source synonyms:** think back; hark back; review; reminisce; recollect; recall; remember

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To reconsider or regret.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** You do what you have to do, and don't look back.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see look, back.
