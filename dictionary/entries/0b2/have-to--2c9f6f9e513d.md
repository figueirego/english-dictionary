### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Must; need to; to be urged to; to be required to; indicates obligation.

   **Example:** You have to wear a seat belt.

   **Example:** I have to go to the bathroom.

   **Example:** I just have to have that shirt.

   **Example:** Wise men speak because they have something to say; fools because they have to say something.

   **Example:** She said you had not to talk to me, but you don't have to obey her anymore.

   **Source synonyms:** need to; have got to; got to; gotta

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Must; expresses a logical conclusion.

   **Example:** that has to be the postman; it has to be an electrical fault

   **Source context (verbatim):** with be

   **Source synonyms:** be bound to; have got to; got to; gotta; must
