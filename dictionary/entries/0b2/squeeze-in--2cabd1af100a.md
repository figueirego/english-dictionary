### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To find time or other resources for.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** My appointment book is pretty full, but I can just squeeze you in.

   **Example:** I found a time to squeeze in a meeting with you.

   **Example:** I squeezed the meeting in.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To pack tightly together.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** We don't have much room in this car, so everyone will have to squeeze in.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To force in, stretch something to make something larger fit.

   **Example:** My son was able to squeeze in the tight crawl space to retrieve the cat.
