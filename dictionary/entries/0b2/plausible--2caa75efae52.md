### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Seemingly or apparently valid, likely, or acceptable; conceivably true or likely.

   **Example:** a plausible excuse

   **Source synonyms:** credible; believable; reasonable; tenable; specious

   **Source antonyms:** implausible; incredible; unbelievable; far-fetched

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Obtaining approbation; specifically pleasing; apparently right; specious.

   **Example:** a plausible pretext; plausible manners; a plausible delusion

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Worthy of being applauded; praiseworthy; commendable; ready.

   **Usage:** obsolete
