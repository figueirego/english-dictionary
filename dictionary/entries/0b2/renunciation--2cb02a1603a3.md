## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of rejecting or renouncing something as invalid.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The President's renunciation of the treaty has upset Congress.

   **Source synonyms:** nonapproval; rejection; annulment; invalidation; abnegation; declination; denial; turndown; disapproval; dissension; nonapproval; nonassent; nonconsent; prohibition; refusal; rejection; renunciation; repudiation; unapproval; unconsent; abolition; abrogation; annulment; cancellation; deauthentication; devalidation; invalidation; nullification; renunciation; revocation; termination; vitiation

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The resignation of an ecclesiastical office.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The bishop's renunciation was on account of his ill health.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The act by which a person abandons a right acquired, but without transferring it to another.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. In the Anglican baptismal service, the part in which the candidate in person or by his sureties renounces the Devil and all his works.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity
