### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The systematic and deliberate destruction of a group of people; typically by killing substantial numbers of them, on the basis of their ethnicity, religion, nationality, or similar grounds.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** A genocide will always be followed by the denial that it ever happened.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** linguistic genocide
