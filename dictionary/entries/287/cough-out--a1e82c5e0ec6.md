### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To remove by coughing, especially figuratively, to say through coughs.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He coughed out blood.

   **Example:** "I'm okay," he coughed out.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To give (money) to someone.

   **Usage:** especially; transitive

   **Example:** Eventually the machine coughed out the money.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of an ATM
