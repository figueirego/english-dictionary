**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Criminal neglect or wrongful execution of duty, especially by a public official; (countable) a specific instance of this.

   **Usage:** historical; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

2. In full misprision of felony or misprision of treason&#58; originally, a less serious form of felony or treason; later, the crime of (intentionally) failing to give information about a felony or treason that one knows about; (countable) an instance of this.

   **Usage:** historical; uncountable

   **Example:** misprision of heresy

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law
