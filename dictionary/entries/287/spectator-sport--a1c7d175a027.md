## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A sporting activity which has a relatively high ratio of watchers to direct participants.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Something, especially a process or activity, which is a popular object of observation; an activity which a person prefers to watch rather than to participate in.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An activity which consists of watching or observing.
