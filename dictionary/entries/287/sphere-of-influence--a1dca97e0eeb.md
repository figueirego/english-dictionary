## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A spatial region over which a state has a level of cultural, economic, military or political exclusivity.

   **Example:** Under the Monroe Doctrine, the United States considered Central and South America within its sphere of influence, therefore justifying American interference with these countries' domestic politics.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; politics

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A concept division over which a person, organization, etc. exerts control.

   **Usage:** broadly; figuratively
