### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Behaving in a manner that shows honor; decent, having integrity.

   **Usage:** US

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Worthy of respect; respectable.

   **Usage:** US

   **Source synonyms:** estimable; noble; venerable; admirable; allowable (obsolete); commendable; creditable; estimable; glorious; honorable; laudable; praiseworthy; respectable; reverable; reverend; valuable; venerable; worthy

   **Source antonyms:** contemptible; despicable; mean

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Complying with cultural rules regarding honor; not provoking shame or disgrace.

   **Usage:** US

   **Example:** That culture considered it no disgrace to be defeated in honorable combat.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A courtesy title, given in Britain and the Commonwealth to a cabinet minister, minister of state, or senator, and in the United States to the president, vice president, congresspeople, state governors and legislators, and mayors.

   **Usage:** US

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; politics

   **Source synonyms:** Hon'ble (abbreviation); Hon. (abbreviation)

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A courtesy title, given in the United Kingdom to all sons and daughters of viscounts and barons and to the younger sons of earls.

   **Usage:** US
