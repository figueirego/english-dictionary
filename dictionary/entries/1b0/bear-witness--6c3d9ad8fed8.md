## Verb

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To deliver a testimony, especially as witness

   **Example:** He hesitated to bear witness against them in court for fear of reprisals by the felons' accomplices

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To prove, demonstrate

   **Example:** The dubious state of the whole society bears witness of years of misrule
