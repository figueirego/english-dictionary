### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To repossess a mortgaged property whose owner has failed to make the necessary payments; used with on.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** They have to move out of their house because the bank foreclosed on their mortgage.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To cut off (a mortgager) by a judgment of court from the power of redeeming the mortgaged premises.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To shut up or out; to prevent from doing something.

   **Usage:** transitive; originally

   **Source synonyms:** stop; prevent; bar; exclude
