## Noun · Record 2

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. (medicine) An anterior shoulder dislocation in which the head of the humerus lies below the coracoid process.

   **Extractor diagnostics (not usage labels):** no-gloss

**Supplemental definition (evidence reviewed; source gap preserved):** The original separate noun request has no conflicting quotation. Gnawali and colleagues use the actual plural subcoracoids in their original study; because that sentence alone could be editorially irregular, Daya and Nakamura’s independently authored teaching review is also checked&#58; it uses the singular substantively and immediately explains the position of the displaced humeral head. The proposal identifies this dislocation subtype, not a bursa and not an acromioclavicular dislocation involving the clavicle.
