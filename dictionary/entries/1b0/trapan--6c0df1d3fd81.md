### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An act of entrapping or tricking; an entrapment; also, a thing which entraps or tricks; a snare or trap; a stratagem or trick.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person (or occasionally an animal) that traps or tricks another into doing something that benefits them but harms the victim; a fraudster, a swindler, a trickster.

   **Usage:** archaic; obsolete; probably originally thieves' cant

   **Source synonyms:** bunco-steerer (US; slang); charlatan; cheat; jackal; cheater; chiseler; cozener; defrauder; finagler; fleecer; fraud; fraudmeister; fraudster; humbug (slang); goldbrick (US; dated; slang); grifter; gull (British; obsolete; slang); gyp; landshark (obsolete; slang); pickpenny; rook; scammer; scamp; Scandaroon (obsolete); schemester; sharper; slicker; spieler; swindler; trapan (archaic; obsolete); [trepan](../0cb/trepan--32e40da58784.md) (archaic; obsolete)
