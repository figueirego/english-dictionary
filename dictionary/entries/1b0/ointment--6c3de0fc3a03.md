**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A viscous preparation of oils and/or fats, usually containing medication, used as a treatment or as an emollient.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The doctor prescribed an antibiotic ointment for the wound.

   **Example:** She applied a soothing herbal ointment to her dry skin.
