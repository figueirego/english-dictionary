### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A concrete margin along the edge of a road; a kerb (UK, Australia, New Zealand).

   **Usage:** US

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A raised margin along the edge of something, such as a well or the eye of a dome, as a strengthening.

   **Usage:** US

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Something that checks or restrains; a restraint.

   **Usage:** US; figuratively

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A riding or driving bit for a horse that has rein action which amplifies the pressure in the mouth by leverage advantage placing pressure on the poll by means of the crown piece of the bridle and chin groove by means of a curb chain.

   **Usage:** US

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** equestrianism; hobbies; horses; lifestyle; pets; sports

   **Source synonyms:** curb bit

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A sidewalk, covered or partially enclosed, bordering the airport terminal road system with adjacent paved areas to permit vehicles to off-load or load passengers.

   **Usage:** Canada; US

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A swelling on the back part of the hind leg of a horse, just behind the lowest part of the hock joint, generally causing lameness.

   **Usage:** US
