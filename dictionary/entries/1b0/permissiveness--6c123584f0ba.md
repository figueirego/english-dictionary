## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The relative likelihood of something or someone to grant permission or allow something to happen.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** The permissiveness of campus life leads many college freshmen to try things they would never have experienced at home.
