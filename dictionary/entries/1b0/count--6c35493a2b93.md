### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To recite numbers in sequence.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Can you count to a hundred?

   **Example:** The psychiatrist asked her to count down from a hundred by sevens.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To determine the number of (objects in a group).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Count the number of apples in the bag and write down the number on the spreadsheet.

   **Source synonyms:** tell

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To amount to, to number in total.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To be of significance; to matter; to be considered (as something); to be included (of something).

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive; passive-like

   **Example:** Your views don’t count here.

   **Example:** It does count if you cheat with someone when you’re drunk.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To be an example of something&#58; often followed by as and an indefinite noun.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive; passive-like

   **Example:** Apples count as a type of fruit.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To consider something as an example of something or as having some quality; to account, to regard as.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He counts himself a hero after saving the cat from the river.

   **Example:** I count you as more than a friend.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To reckon in, to include in consideration.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** They walked for three days, not counting the time spent resting.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To take account or note (of), to care (for).

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To recount, to tell.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. To plead orally; to argue a matter in court; to recite a count.

   **Usage:** UK; intransitive; obsolete

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law
