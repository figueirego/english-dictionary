## Adjective · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /juˈtɛk.tɪk/ — source labels: US

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Describing the chemical composition or temperature of a mixture of substances that gives the lowest temperature at which the mixture becomes fully molten. A further requirement is that that temperature is lower than the melting point of any of the pure component substances.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Describing the thermodynamic equilibrium conditions where a liquid coexists with two solid phases.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** For a mixture with two components at a fixed pressure, the eutectic reaction can only happen at a fixed chemical composition and temperature, called the eutectic point.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences
