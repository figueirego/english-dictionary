<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having a pleasing appearance, good-looking, attractive, particularly

   **Example:** That is one handsome tree you've got there.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Having a pleasing appearance, good-looking, attractive
   1. Of a man or boy&#58; attractively manly, having a pleasing face and overall effect.

      **Example:** I was struck dumb. Here was the most handsome man I’d ever seen in my life coming out of the surf.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Having a pleasing appearance, good-looking, attractive
   1. Of a woman&#58; statuesque, beautiful in a masculine or otherwise imposing way.

      **Example:** She was either handsome or her uniform created a flattering effect but—being very nearsighted—he couldn't tell from this distance.

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Good, appealing, appropriate.

   1. Suitable or fit in action; marked with propriety and ease; appropriate.
