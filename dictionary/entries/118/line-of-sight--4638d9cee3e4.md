## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A straight line along which an observer has a clear view.

   **Example:** in my line of sight

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The line which passes through the front and rear sight, at any elevation, when they are sighted at an object.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; government; military; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; politics; tools; war; weaponry
