### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To obtain or receive (something) from something else.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Reading books is the best way to derive knowledge.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To deduce (a conclusion) by reasoning.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; logic; mathematics; philosophy; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To find the derivation of (a word or phrase).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To create (a compound) from another by means of a reaction.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To originate or stem (from).

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Her excellent organisation skills derive from her time as a secretary in the army.

**Source sense R1.S6**
