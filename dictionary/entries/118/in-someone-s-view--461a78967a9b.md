## Prep\_phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The way or manner in which someone sees or perceives something&#58; the way or manner in which someone feels about something.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** In her view, there's only one thing to be done about it&#58; read them the riot act.

   **Example:** In my view, there's nothing wrong with a little give-and-take about such issues.

   **Source synonyms:** in someone's eyes; by one's lights; to one's mind; in one's opinion

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see in, someone's, view.

   **Example:** If they build that monstrosity then it will be right in our view every time we look out our windows.
