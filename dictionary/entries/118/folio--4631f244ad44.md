### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A leaf of a book or manuscript.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A page of a book, that is, one side of a leaf of a book.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A page number. The even folios are on the left-hand pages and the odd folios on the right-hand pages.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** media; printing; publishing

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A sheet of paper folded in half.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** media; printing; publishing

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A book made of sheets of paper each folded in half (two leaves or four pages to the sheet); hence, a book of the largest kind, exceeding 30 centimetres in height.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** A rare copy of Shakespeare’s First Folio.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** media; printing; publishing

   **Source synonyms:** F (abbreviation); f; fo; 2º

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A wrapper for loose papers.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A page in an account book; sometimes, two opposite pages bearing the same serial number.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** accounting; business; finance

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A protective case with a flap that folds to cover the screen of a mobile device.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A leaf containing a certain number of words; hence, a certain number of words in a piece of writing, as in England, in law proceedings 72, and in chancery, 90; in New York, 100 words.

   **Usage:** dated

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law
