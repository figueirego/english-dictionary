### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A device that captures sound waves onto an engraved archive; a lathe.

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A device that records or plays sound from cylinder records.

   **Usage:** UK; historical

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

   **Usage:** Canada; US; historical

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

   **Usage:** dated
