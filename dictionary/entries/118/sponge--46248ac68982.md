### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To take advantage of the kindness of others.

   **Usage:** intransitive; slang

   **Example:** He has been sponging off his friends for a month now.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To get by imposition; to scrounge.

   **Usage:** transitive; intransitive with on or upon

   **Example:** to sponge a breakfast

   **Source synonyms:** blag

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To deprive (somebody) of something by imposition.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To clean, soak up, or dab with a sponge.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To suck in, or imbibe, like a sponge.

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To wipe out with a sponge, as letters or writing; to efface; to destroy all trace of.

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

**Source sense R2.S7**

7. To be converted, as dough, into a light, spongy mass by the agency of yeast or leaven.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** baking; cooking; food; lifestyle
