## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A postal service allowing a person to send mail without affixing postage, the recipient paying when collecting the mail.

   **Usage:** UK; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A postal service allowing an electoral candidate, political party or government department to send mail wholly paid for by Royal Mail (as the statutory universal postal service provider).

   **Usage:** UK; uncountable
