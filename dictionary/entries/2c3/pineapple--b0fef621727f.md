### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A tropical plant, Ananas comosus, native to South America, having thirty or more long, spined and pointed leaves surrounding a thick stem.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The ovoid fruit of the pineapple plant, which has very sweet white or yellow flesh, a tough, spiky shell and a tough, fibrous core.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The flesh of a pineapple fruit used as a food item.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An Australian fifty dollar note.

   **Usage:** countable; slang; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A web burrfish (Chilomycterus antillarum, syn. Chilomycterus geometricus)

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A light yellow colour, like that of pineapple flesh (also called pineapple yellow).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A pinecone; the cone of the fir.

   **Usage:** countable; dialectal; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A hand grenade. (From the similarity to the shape of a pineapple fruit.)

   **Usage:** countable; slang; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** grenade; hand grenade

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A hairstyle consisting of a ponytail worn on top of the head, imitating the leaves of a pineapple.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
