### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Continental land, as distinguished from islands.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** mainland

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Land, as opposed to water or air.

   **Usage:** colloquial; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** dry land; on solid ground

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Familiar territory (in a figurative sense); real-world concerns.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; uncountable

   **Example:** It's nice to be back on terra firma after a long session of analyzing the philosopher's abstruse theories.

   **Source synonyms:** on solid ground

   **Source antonyms:** castle in the air; air castle; pie in the sky; pipe dream
