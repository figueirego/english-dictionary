### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The rubbing of one object or surface against another.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A force that resists the relative motion or tendency to such motion of two bodies in contact.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A massage of the body to restore circulation.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

   **Usage:** figuratively; uncountable; usually
