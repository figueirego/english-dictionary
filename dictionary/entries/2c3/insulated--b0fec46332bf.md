## Adjective · Record 1

### Forms

- more insulated — comparative
- most insulated — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Protected from heat, cold, noise etc, by being surrounded with an insulating material.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Placed or set apart.

   **Example:** an insulated house or column

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Isolated or separated from other conducting materials, or sources of electricity.

   **Example:** Early insulated wires were covered in silk rather than plastic.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of an electrically conducting material

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Situated at so great a distance as to be beyond the effect of gravitation; said of stars supposed to be so far apart that the effect of their mutual attraction is undetectable.

   **Usage:** dated

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astronomy; natural-sciences
