**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A hairpiece or half wig that covers the crown or thinning areas of the head without covering the full scalp to add volume, fullness, and coverage for hair loss, blending with the hair for a natural look unlike full wigs; made using synthetic hair on a base (like mesh or lace).
