### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A milk-based product stiffened by a bacterium-aided curdling process, and sometimes mixed with fruit or other flavoring.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** milk food (obsolete)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any similar product based on other substances (e.g. soy yogurt).

   **Usage:** countable; especially; in-compounds; uncountable
