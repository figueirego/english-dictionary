### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The shedding or spilling of blood.

   **Usage:** literally; uncountable; usually

   **Example:** avoid bloodshed

   **Example:** The revolution resulted in heavy bloodshed.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A slaughter; destruction of life, notably on a large scale.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** political bloodshed

   **Example:** widespread bloodshed

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The shedding of one's own blood; specifically, the death of Christ.

   **Usage:** obsolete; uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A bloodshot condition or appearance; an effusion of blood in the eye.

   **Usage:** obsolete; uncountable; usually
