**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Usage:** not-comparable; typically weapons

   **Example:** An area-denial mine might not kill a soldier, but will severely restrict, slow down, or endanger a tank. Many area-denial weapons continue to pose risks to civilians, years after they were first set up.
