## Adjective · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. That does not use or require contact in order to happen or operate.
   1. Conducted without human-to-human contact to improve ease of use or reduce the potential spread of a contagious disease; contact-free.

      **Usage:** not-comparable; usually; exchange

      **Source context (verbatim):** of a delivery; of physical goods
