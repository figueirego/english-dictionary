### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A transliteration of the Russian surname Никола́ев (Nikolájev)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A male surname from Russian, feminine equivalent Nikolayeva.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A khutor in Znamenka rural settlement, Morozovsk Raion, Rostov Oblast, Southern Federal District, Russia.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Russian name of Mykolaiv (four villages and two cities in the Ukraine)

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Russian name of Mykolaiv (four villages and two cities in the Ukraine)
   1. The former Russian name, from the 8th (O.S.) or 20th (N.S.) of October 1802 until the 15th (O.S.) or 27th (N.S.) of May 1803, of the Kherson Governorate of the Russian Empire.
