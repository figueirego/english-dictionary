### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Half of the celestial sphere, as divided by either the ecliptic or the celestial equator .

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astrology; astronomy; human-sciences; mysticism; natural-sciences; philosophy; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A realm or domain of activity .

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Half of the Earth, such as the Northern Hemisphere, Southern Hemisphere, Western Hemisphere or Eastern Hemisphere, Land Hemisphere, Water Hemisphere etc. .

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any half-sphere, formed by a plane intersecting the center of a sphere. .

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A map or projection of a celestial or terrestrial hemisphere .

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** cartography; geography; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; medicine; sciences
