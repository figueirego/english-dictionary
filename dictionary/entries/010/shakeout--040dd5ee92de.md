## Noun

### Forms

- shakeouts — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An event that causes marginal constituents to be eliminated.

   **Example:** The dot-com shakeout of the 1990s left only the most durable, most profitable, and most well backed companies surviving.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The separation of molds from their flask, the castings from the molding sand, and potentially the cores from the castings.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The shaking of an object to spread it wide and eject any debris.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The shaking of one's arms or legs, in order to resist muscle fatigue.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports
