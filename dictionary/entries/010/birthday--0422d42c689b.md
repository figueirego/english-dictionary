### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The anniversary of the day on which someone is born.

   **Example:** When's your birthday? Mine's on April 1.

   **Example:** I'll be thirty-seven (years old) next birthday.

   **Example:** My fortieth birthday is only two years off!

   **Source synonyms:** natal day

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The anniversary of the day on which something is created.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The date on which someone is born or something is created, more commonly called birthdate or date of birth.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A birthday party.

   **Example:** I'd like to invite you all to my birthday.
