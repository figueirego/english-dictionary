## Noun · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ʌndəˈstandɪŋ/ — source labels: Received-Pronunciation
- IPA: /ˌʌndɚˈstændɪŋ/ — source labels: General-American

### Forms

- understandings — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of one that understands or comprehends; the mental process of discernment of meaning.

   **Usage:** uncountable; with-of

   **Example:** There are certain things that defy human understanding.

   **Source synonyms:** comprehension

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Reason or intelligence; ability to grasp the full meaning of knowledge; ability to infer.

   **Usage:** countable; with-of

   **Example:** She has a solid understanding of particle physics.

   **Source synonyms:** comprehension; grasp

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Opinion, judgement, or outlook.

   **Usage:** countable; with-of

   **Example:** According to my understanding, the situation is quite perilous. I wonder if you see it this way, too.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An agreement of minds; harmony; something mutually understood or agreed upon.
   1. An informal contract; a mutual agreement.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable; with-of

      **Example:** I thought we had an understanding - you do the dishes, and I throw the trash.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An agreement of minds; harmony; something mutually understood or agreed upon.
   1. A reconciliation of differences.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable; with-of

      **Example:** The parties of the negotiation have managed to come to an understanding.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Sympathy.

   **Usage:** uncountable; with-of

   **Example:** He showed much understanding for my problems when he heard about my past.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Legs or feet.

   **Usage:** colloquial; countable; in-plural; uncountable; with-of
