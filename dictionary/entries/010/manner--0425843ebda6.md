### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Mode of action; way of performing or doing anything.

   **Source synonyms:** method; style; form; fashion; way

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Characteristic mode of acting or behaving; bearing.

   **Example:** His natural manner makes him seem like the boss.

   **Example:** The captain's imperturbable manner gave the crew confidence.

   **Source synonyms:** air; comportment; mien; abear; abearance; bearing; behavior; carriage (archaic); comportment; conduct; deportment; demeanor; guise; haviour (obsolete); manner; mien; air; appearance; aspect; bearing; demeanor; deportment; garb (figuratively); guise; hue (obsolete); likeness; look; manner; mien

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. One's customary method of acting; habit.

   **Example:** These people have strange manners.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Good, polite behaviour.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The style of writing or thought of an author; the characteristic peculiarity of an artist.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A certain degree or measure.

   **Example:** It is in a manner done already.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Sort; kind; style.

   **Example:** All manner of persons participate.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Standards of conduct cultured and product of mind.
