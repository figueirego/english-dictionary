### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Physically located at a deep level.

   **Example:** a deep-seated tumour

   **Example:** Weaknesses in the earth’s crust allow deep-seated heat to rise nearer to the surface.

   **Source antonyms:** superficial; surface-level

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Firmly implanted or established in thought or behavior and difficult to change; deeply ingrained.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** She has a deep-seated belief in the essential goodness of human nature.

   **Source synonyms:** bred-in-the-bone; deep-rooted; deep-lying
