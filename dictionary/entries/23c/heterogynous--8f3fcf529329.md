## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having females very unlike the males in form and structure.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Some heterogynous insects have winged males and wingless females.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences; zoology

   **Source synonyms:** sexodimorphic

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Having females of two different kinds, one sexual and the other abortive or neuter, as ants or bees.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences; zoology
