### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To change with time or a similar parameter.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He varies his magic tricks so as to minimize the possibility that any given audience member will see the same trick twice.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To institute a change in, from a current state; to modify.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** You should vary your diet. Eating just bread will do you harm in the end.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Not to remain constant&#58; to change with time or a similar parameter.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** His mood varies by the hour.

   **Example:** The sine function varies between −1 and 1.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To display differences.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The sprouting tendency of potatoes varies between cultivars, years and places of growing.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of the members of a group

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To be or act different from the usual.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** I'm not comfortable with 3.Nc3 in the Caro-Kann, so I decided to vary and play exd5.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To make of different kinds; to make different from one another; to diversify; to variegate.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To embellish; to change fancifully; to present under new aspects, as of form, key, measure, etc. See variation.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To disagree; to be at variance or in dissension.

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete
