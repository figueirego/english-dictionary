### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The disturbed mass of air or water pushed aft (or forward when in reverse) by the propeller of an aircraft or propeller-driven watercraft.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** aeronautics; aerospace; aviation; business; engineering; natural-sciences; nautical; physical-sciences; transport

   **Source synonyms:** prop blast

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A byproduct of thrust produced by a propeller.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A mythical substance supposedly used to wash propellers.

   **Usage:** countable; humorous; uncountable

   **Example:** We sent the new recruit to obtain a bucket of prop wash.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** aeronautics; aerospace; aviation; business; engineering; natural-sciences; nautical; physical-sciences; transport
