## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of mixing cards or mah-jong tiles so as to randomize them.

   **Example:** He made a real mess of the last shuffle.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The act of reordering anything, such as music tracks in a media player.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An instance of walking without lifting one's feet.

   **Example:** The sad young girl left with a tired shuffle.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A rhythm commonly used in blues music, consisting of a series of triplet notes with the middle note missing, so that it sounds like a long note followed by a short note, and suggests a walker dragging one foot.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A dance move in which the foot is scuffed back and forth across the floor.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** dance; dancing; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A trick; an artifice; an evasion.

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To put in a random order.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** Don't forget to shuffle the cards.

   **Example:** You shuffle, and I'll deal.

   **Example:** The data packets are shuffled before transmission.

   **Example:** I'm going to shuffle all the songs in my playlist.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To change; modify the order of something.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To move in a slovenly, dragging manner; to drag or scrape the feet in walking or dancing.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** He shuffled out of the room.

   **Example:** I shuffled my feet in embarrassment.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To change one's position; to shift ground; to evade questions; to resort to equivocation; to prevaricate.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To use arts or expedients; to make shift.

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To shove one way and the other; to push from one to another.

   **Example:** to shuffle money from hand to hand

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

**Source sense R2.S7**

7. To remove or introduce by artificial confusion.
