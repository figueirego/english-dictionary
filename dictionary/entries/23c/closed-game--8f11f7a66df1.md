### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A chess opening characterized by the moves 1.d4 d5, in which both White and Black move their queen's pawn to the fourth rank with their first move.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** board-games; chess; games
