### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The body of binding rules and regulations, customs, and standards established in a community by its legislative and judicial authorities.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; usually; with-definite-article

   **Example:** The courts interpret the law but should not make it.

   **Example:** In theory, entrapment is against the law.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The body of binding rules and regulations, customs, and standards established in a community by its legislative and judicial authorities.
   1. The body of such rules that pertain to a particular topic.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable; usually; with-definite-article

      **Example:** property law

      **Example:** commercial hunting and fishing law

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The body of binding rules and regulations, customs, and standards established in a community by its legislative and judicial authorities.
   1. Common law, as contrasted with equity.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable; usually; with-definite-article

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A binding regulation or custom established in a community in this way.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** There is a law against importing wallabies.

   **Example:** A new law forbids driving on that road.

   **Example:** The court ruled that the executive order was not law and nullified it.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A rule, such as&#58;
   1. Any rule that must or should be obeyed, concerning behaviours and their consequences. (Compare mores.)

      **Usage:** broadly; countable; uncountable

      **Example:** "Do unto others as you wish them to do unto you" is a good law to follow.

      **Example:** the law of self-preservation

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A rule, such as&#58;
   1. A rule or principle regarding the construction of language or art.

      **Usage:** broadly; countable; uncountable

      **Example:** the laws of playwriting and poetry

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A rule, such as&#58;
   1. A statement (in physics, etc) of an (observed, established) order or sequence or relationship of phenomena which is invariable under certain conditions. (Compare theory.)

      **Usage:** broadly; countable; uncountable

      **Example:** the laws of thermodynamics

      **Example:** Newton's third law of motion states that to every action there is always an equal and opposite reaction.

      **Example:** This is one of several laws derived from his general theory expounded in the Philosophiæ Naturalis Principia Mathematica.

      **Source synonyms:** law of nature; law

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
