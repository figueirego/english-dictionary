## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The regions in which a sheriff has authority.

   **Example:** Crossing the river got him beyond his old nemesis' sheriffdom and into the territory of a more liberal authority.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The office, or period of office, of a sheriff.

   **Example:** His sheriffdom began with his election and ended with his death.
