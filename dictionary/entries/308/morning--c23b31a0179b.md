### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The early part of the day, especially from midnight to noon.

   **Example:** I'll see you tomorrow morning.

   **Example:** I'm working in the morning, so let's meet in the afternoon.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The early part of anything.

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. That part of the day from dawn until the main meal (typically in late afternoon).

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The first alcoholic drink of the day; a morning draught.

   **Usage:** Scotland
