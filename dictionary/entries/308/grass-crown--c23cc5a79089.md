### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The highest and rarest of all military decorations in the Roman Republic and early Roman Empire, presented to a general, commander or officer whose actions saved a legion or the entire army, and taking the form of a chaplet made from plant materials taken from the battlefield.

   **Usage:** historical

   **Source synonyms:** blockade crown; obsidional crown
