## Noun

### Forms

- twelve o'clocks — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The start of the thirteenth hour of the day; noon (noontime); 12&#58;00 p.m. (12&#58;00).

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually; In both the 12-hour clock and the 24-hour clock

   **Example:** The next train comes at twelve o'clock (= "The next train comes at noon").

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The start of the first hour of the day; midnight; 12&#58;00 a.m. (00&#58;00).

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually; Only in the 12-hour clock

   **Example:** The bar closes at twelve o'clock (= "The bar closes at midnight").

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A position straight ahead (horizontal clock orientation) or above (vertical clock orientation) (from the location of the 12 mark on a clock face)

   **Usage:** informal; uncountable; usually

   **Example:** We've got a bogey at twelve o'clock (= "We've got a bogey straight in front of us").
