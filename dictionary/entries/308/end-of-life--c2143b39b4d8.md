### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The termination of the sale of or support for goods and services.

   **Usage:** attributive; often; uncountable

   **Example:** That operating system version is approaching end-of-life.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; commerce

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The time near death; (attributively) concerning medical care options for patients who are considered critically ill with prognosis of approaching death.

   **Usage:** attributive; often; uncountable

   **Example:** end-of-life palliative care

   **Example:** This is but one example of the hard decisions that must be made near end of life.

   **Example:** Hospice provides end-of-life care for a period usually lasting a few days or a few weeks; occasionally patients recover unexpectedly.
