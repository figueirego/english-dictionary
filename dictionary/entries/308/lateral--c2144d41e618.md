### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To the side; of or pertaining to the side.
   1. Situated on one side or other of the body or of an organ, especially in the region furthest from the median plane.

      **Example:** The medial side of the knee faces the other knee, while the outer side of the knee is lateral.

      **Example:** A fish senses changes in hydrodynamic pressure with its lateral line.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; biology; medicine; natural-sciences; sciences; zoology; zootomy

      **Source antonyms:** medial

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To the side; of or pertaining to the side.
   1. Affecting the side or sides of the body, or confined to one side of the body.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; pathology; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To the side; of or pertaining to the side.
   1. Acting or placed at right angles to a line of motion or strain.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To the side; of or pertaining to the side.
   1. At the same level in hierarchy or rank, especially pertaining to a movement that does not involve a change in rank, status, or level.

      **Example:** Instead of a promotion, I opted for a lateral move to a similar position in the marketing department.

      **Example:** lateral hiring

      **Example:** a lateral transfer

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Non-linear or unconventional, as in, lateral thinking; outside the box.

   **Usage:** UK

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. (of a consonant, especially the English clear l) Pertaining to speech sounds generated by partially blocking the egress of the airstream with the tongue, leaving space on one or both sides of the occlusion for air passage.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; phonetics; phonology; sciences
