## Adverb

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To a low degree, not particularly, not especially.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** I’m not really hungry now, so let’s have dinner later.

   **Example:** A&#58; Are you hungry yet? B&#58; Not really.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Not actually (a hypothetical will not, in fact, come to pass), often rhetorical.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** You’re not really going to marry him, are you?
