### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A baked good such as a cake or biscuit intended to be eaten with tea or at an afternoon tea.
   1. A flat, round bread bun, usually containing currants, sultanas or peel and often served toasted and buttered with tea.

      **Usage:** Ireland; Southern-England; UK

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A baked good such as a cake or biscuit intended to be eaten with tea or at an afternoon tea.
   1. A bread roll without fruit; a barm.

      **Usage:** Cumbria; Northern-England; Lancashire and West Yorkshire

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A baked good such as a cake or biscuit intended to be eaten with tea or at an afternoon tea.
   1. A snack consisting of shortbread or wafer topped with meringue or marshmallow, coated in chocolate.

      **Usage:** Ireland; Scotland; UK

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A baked good such as a cake or biscuit intended to be eaten with tea or at an afternoon tea.
   1. A traditional type of dense, large cookie, typically hard-baked, not particularly sweet, and with few extra fillings.

      **Usage:** Southern-US; US; especially

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A baked good such as a cake or biscuit intended to be eaten with tea or at an afternoon tea.
   1. A small delicate cake or pastry; a petit four.

      **Usage:** US

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A baked good such as a cake or biscuit intended to be eaten with tea or at an afternoon tea.
   1. A sweet cake similar to pound cake, sometimes sprinkled with cinnamon and caster sugar, often served warm.

      **Usage:** Australia

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A cake flavoured with tea.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A cake flavoured with tea.
   1. A fruit cake similar to a barmbrack (but often without yeast) flavoured with tea and whiskey, especially associated with Halloween.

      **Usage:** Ireland

      **Source synonyms:** tea brack

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A brick of dried tea.
