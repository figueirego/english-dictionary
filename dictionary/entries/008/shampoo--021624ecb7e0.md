### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A traditional Indian and Persian body massage given after pouring warm water over the body and rubbing it with extracts from herbs.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; originally

   **Source synonyms:** massage

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A commercial liquid soaplike product for washing hair or other fibers, such as carpets.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; hygiene

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An instance of washing the hair or other fibres with shampoo.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** I’m going to give the carpet a shampoo.

   **Source synonyms:** shampooing

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

   **Usage:** countable; humorous; slang; uncountable
