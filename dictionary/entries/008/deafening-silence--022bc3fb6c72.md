**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A noteworthy silence, or absence of response, especially one signifying disapproval or lack of enthusiasm.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; uncountable; usually

   **Example:** The suggestion that they work through the holidays met with deafening silence.
