### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A single-storey house, typically with rooms all on one level, or sometimes also with upper rooms set into the roof space.

   **Example:** My aunt can't manage the stairs any more, so she's moving to a bungalow.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A thatched or tiled one-story house in India surrounded by a wide veranda; a similar house in this style.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A chalet or lodge.

   **Usage:** Atlantic-Canada

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A detached, freestanding house or mansion.

   **Usage:** Malaysia; Singapore
