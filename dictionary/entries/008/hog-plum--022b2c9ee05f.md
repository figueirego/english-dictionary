### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of several species of plants that produce plum-like fruit.
   1. Spondias spp., especially Spondias mombin.

      **Source synonyms:** yellow mombin; yellow Spanish plum

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of several species of plants that produce plum-like fruit.
   1. Colubrina spp.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of several species of plants that produce plum-like fruit.
   1. Certain species of Prunus, particularly Prunus angustifolia (Chickasaw plum), Prunus rivularis (wild-goose plum), and Prunus umbellata (Allegheny plum).

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any of several species of plants that produce plum-like fruit.
   1. Ximenia americana (false sandalwood, yellow plum).

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Any of several species of plants that produce plum-like fruit.
   1. Metopium toxiferum (poisonwood).
