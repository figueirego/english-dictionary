### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Offensive to good taste, tactless, overzealous, excessive.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Excessively flattering (connoting insincerity).

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Characterised or marked by fullness; abundant, copious.

   **Example:** The fulsome thanks of the war-torn nation lifted our weary spirits.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Fully developed; mature.

   **Example:** Her fulsome timbre resonated throughout the hall.
