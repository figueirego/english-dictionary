### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A fish bowl or barrel-shaped container where balls used in a raffle or a lottery are placed. In the case of a raffle, tickets may also be used. Can be made out of wire, glass, clear plastic, etc. Often has a handle, which, when rotated, mixes up the balls or tickets.

   **Usage:** Philippines

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** gambling; games

   **Source synonyms:** raffle drum; bingo drum; lottery drum

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A system by which the government gives out assets, such as land, supposedly at random, but which some feel is actually rigged.

   **Usage:** Philippines; chiefly political
