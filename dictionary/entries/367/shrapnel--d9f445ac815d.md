### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An anti-personnel artillery shell used in World War I which carries a large number of individual bullets or balls close to the target and then ejects them to allow them to continue along the shell's trajectory and strike the target individually.

   **Usage:** historical; uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The bullets or balls from the aforementioned type of artillery shell.

   **Usage:** historical; uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any shot, fragments, or debris thrown out by an exploding shell, bomb, or landmine.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Debris.

   **Usage:** broadly; figuratively; uncountable; usually

   **Example:** The dog ate my sandwich, and there was shrapnel all over the place from him tearing open the bag.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Loose change.

   **Usage:** figuratively; slang; uncountable; usually
