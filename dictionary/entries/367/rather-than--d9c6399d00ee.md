## Conjunction · Record 1

### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. And not.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used to indicate that the following alternative is less preferred.

   **Example:** I'd like to go home early rather than risk(ing) the roads later.

   **Example:** I prefer making my kids' toys myself rather than buying them.

   **Example:** It was my wife, rather than I, who decided to leave.

   **Example:** It is never better to let kids have their way whenever they wish rather than standing up for one's parental authority.
