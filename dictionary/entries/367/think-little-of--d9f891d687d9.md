### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To dislike; to hold in low esteem; to look upon without favour or admiration.

   **Source antonyms:** think much of

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To be willing to do without compunction or deep consideration.
