### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Love of one's country; devotion to the welfare of one's compatriots; passion which inspires one to serve one's country.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The actions of a patriot.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** chauvinism; superpatriotism
