## Adjective · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈboɹ.dɚˌlaɪn/ — source labels: US

### Forms

- more borderline — comparative
- most borderline — superlative
- border-line — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Nearly; not clearly on one side or the other of a border or boundary, ambiguous.

   **Example:** I would rather hire a talented layman than a university graduate with borderline qualifications.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of questionable taste or acceptability; approaching bad taste.

   **Example:** Your borderline remarks about my aunt’s dress destroyed my evening.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Exhibiting or characteristic of borderline personality disorder.
