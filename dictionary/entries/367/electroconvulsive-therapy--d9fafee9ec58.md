### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A biomedical therapy for severely depressed patients in which a brief electric current is sent through the brain of an anesthetized patient, with the noted side effect of memory loss.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** The therapeutic effects of low and high energy electroconvulsive therapies are quite different.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences
