## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The effect on the appearance of an object by the atmosphere between it and a viewer, such as blurring caused by fog.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A technique in which an illusion of depth is created by painting more distant objects with less clarity, and with a lighter tone.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** art; arts
