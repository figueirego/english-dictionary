### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To go aboard a craft or vessel for transportation.

   **Example:** All passengers please embark now.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To start, begin.

   **Example:** Phil embarked on his journey yesterday.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To cause to go on board a vessel or boat; to put on shipboard.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To engage, enlist, or invest (as persons, money, etc.) in any affair.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He embarked his fortune in trade.
