### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To be an example of; to have all the attributes of.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Mozart could be said to personify musical genius.

   **Source antonyms:** unpersonify

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To create a representation of (an abstract quality) in the form of a character or persona.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The writer personified death in the form of the Grim Reaper.

   **Source antonyms:** unpersonify
