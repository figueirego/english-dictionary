### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see throw, out.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To discard; to dispense with something; to throw away.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** Just throw that pen out if it doesn't write anymore.

   **Example:** They decided to throw out the idea because it would have been too expensive.

   **Source synonyms:** toss out; toss; junk; 86; bin; can; cast aside; cast off; cast away; chuck; chuck away; chuck out; discard; dispose of; ditch; dump; get rid of; jettison; pitch; scrap; shift; throw aside; throw away; throw out; toss; trash

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To dismiss a legal case.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To dismiss or expel someone from any longer performing duty or attending somewhere.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** The board threw the man out, because he wouldn't cooperate and agree with their plans to remodernize the facility.

   **Example:** The ushers threw the woman out of the auditorium, because she kept shouting out insults to the guest of honor when he made his speech.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To offer an idea for consideration.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** Let me throw this out there – how about if we make the igloo out of butter? Would that work?

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To produce in a haphazard fashion.

   **Example:** This program keeps throwing out errors.

7. To emit.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Usage:** transitive
