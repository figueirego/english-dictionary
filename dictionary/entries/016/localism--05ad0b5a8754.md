### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Attachment to or preference for a particular local place, especially the place where one lives; hence, feelings, opinions, or policies which emphasize or focus on local phenomena; (countable) an instance of this.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Attachment to or preference for a particular local place, especially the place where one lives; hence, feelings, opinions, or policies which emphasize or focus on local phenomena; (countable) an instance of this.
   1. The policy or practice of giving more control and decision-making power to local authorities or residents, rather than to the central government; also, support for this.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; politics

      **Source synonyms:** localization

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A thing which is characteristic of a particular local place.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A linguistic feature such as a word or phrase that is unique to a locality.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An approach to understanding linguistic expressions in terms of movement and position (sometimes abstract; for example, one who is hungry is said to be in a notional "space" of hunger).

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The belief that language functions are localized to various parts of the brain.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The belief that fevers or diseases causing them originate from particular organs or parts of the body, or are due to conditions in a particular local place where patients live.

   **Usage:** historical; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences
