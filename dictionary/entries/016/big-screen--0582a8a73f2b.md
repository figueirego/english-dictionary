## Noun

### Forms

- big screens — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The large viewing surface upon which films are projected in a cinema.

   **Example:** You should watch Star Wars on the big screen for the full effect.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Movies, or that which is related to movies or cinema.

   **Usage:** broadly; idiomatic

   **Example:** Stars of the stage and the big screen gathered for the awards ceremony.

   **Example:** Jane Smith made her big-screen debut in 2005, appearing as a librarian in a Hollywood crime thriller.
