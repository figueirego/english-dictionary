### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Synonym of Dinas, in Rhondda Cynon Taf, south Wales, used to distinguish it from other places named Dinas. This name is used by Dinas Rhondda railway station, which is actually on the other (northern) side of the River Rhondda, in Trealaw.

   **Source synonyms:** Dinas (synonym; synonym-of); in Rhondda Cynon Taf (synonym; synonym-of); south Wales (synonym; synonym-of)
