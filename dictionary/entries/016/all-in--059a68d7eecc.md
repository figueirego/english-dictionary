### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Very tired.

   **Usage:** dated; not-comparable

   **Example:** \[Yawn\] Well, I'm all in! Time to turn in! Good night all!

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. With everything included.

   **Usage:** UK; not-comparable

   **Source synonyms:** all-inclusive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Having no further stake to wager, but remaining active in a hand.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** By betting his last $100, John was all in.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** card-games; poker

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Totally committed.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** John is now all in on getting this product to market by year end, come hell or high water.
