### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Term of respect for a woman of the peerage without using her title.

   **Example:** "Her ladyship will be unable to attend tonight," he said, with a wink because he hadn't said why.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Formal form of address for a lady judge (as opposed to the informal "judge").

   **Usage:** Commonwealth; England
