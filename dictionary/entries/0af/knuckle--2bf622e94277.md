## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- enPR: nŭkˈ-əl — variety unspecified in the source labels
- IPA: /ˈnʌkəl/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Forms

- knuckles — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of the joints between the bones of the fingers.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A mechanical joint.

   **Usage:** broadly

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The curved part of the cushion at the entrance to the pockets on a cue sports table.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; billiards; games; hobbies; lifestyle; pool; snooker; sports

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The kneejoint of a quadruped, especially of a calf; formerly, the kneejoint of a human being.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A cut of meat of various kinds.

   **Example:** Beef knuckle is from the knee joint. Pork knuckle, or ham hock, is from the joint between the tibia/fibula and the metatarsals of the foot of a pig, where the foot was attached to the leg.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The joint of a plant.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A convex portion of a vessel's figure where a sudden change of shape occurs, as in a canal boat, where a nearly vertical side joins a nearly flat bottom.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; manufacturing; shipbuilding

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A contrivance, usually of brass or iron, and furnished with points, worn to protect the hand, to add force to a blow, and to disfigure the person struck; a knuckle duster.

   **Example:** brass knuckles

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. The rounded point where a flat changes to a slope on a piste.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; skiing; snowboarding; sports

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To apply pressure, or rub or massage with one's knuckles (noun sense 1).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He knuckled the sleep from his eyes.

   **Example:** She knuckled her eyes in disbelief.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To strike or punch.

   **Usage:** slang; transitive

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

3. To bend the fingers.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive
