### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. singular of shenanigans&#58; a deceitful confidence trick; also, an act of mischief; a prank, a trick; an act of mischievous play, especially by children.

   **Usage:** countable; form-of; in-plural; informal; singular

   **Example:** I spotted his next shenanigan—saw it coming—and so avoided being fooled.

   **Source synonyms:** caper; capriccio; curvet; gag; gambade (Scotland); high jinks; [lark](../322/lark--c8916e65e41f.md); marlock; monkeyshine; practical joke; prank; prat; reak (obsolete); rag (dated); shenanigan; trick; windup (UK)

   **Form of:** [shenanigans](../16e/shenanigans--5bac9ccaac05.md)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Dishonest, underhanded, or unscrupulous activities or behaviour; skulduggery, trickery; also, mischievous behaviour or play; high jinks.

   **Usage:** dated; informal; rare; uncountable
