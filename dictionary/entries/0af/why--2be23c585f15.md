**Source sense R1.S1**

1. For what cause, reason, or purpose.
   1. Introducing a complete question.

      **Usage:** interrogative; not-comparable

      **Example:** Why is the sky blue?

      **Example:** Why did you do that?

      **Example:** I don’t know why he did that.

      **Example:** Tell me why the moon changes phase.

   1. Introducing a complete question.
      1. With a negative, used rhetorically to make a suggestion.

         **Usage:** interrogative; not-comparable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. For what cause, reason, or purpose.
   1. Introducing a verb phrase (bare infinitive clause).

      **Usage:** interrogative; not-comparable

      **Example:** Why spend money on something you already get for free?

      **Example:** Why not tell him how you feel?

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. For what cause, reason, or purpose.
   1. Introducing a noun or other phrase.

      **Usage:** interrogative; not-comparable

      **Example:** Why him? Why not someone taller?

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. For which cause, reason, or purpose.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; relative

   **Example:** That's the reason why I did that.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The cause, reason, or purpose for which.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; fused relative

   **Example:** That is why the sky is blue.

   **Example:** Why most of us volunteer is that we need some fresh air.
