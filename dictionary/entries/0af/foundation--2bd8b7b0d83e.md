### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of founding, fixing, establishing, or beginning to erect.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The foundation of his institute has been wrought with difficulty.

   **Source synonyms:** establishment

   **Source antonyms:** abolition; dissolution; ruination

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. That upon which anything is founded; that on which anything stands, and by which it is supported; the lowest and supporting layer of a superstructure; underbuilding.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** basis; groundwork; underbuilding; underframework; warp and weft; underpinning

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The result of the work to begin something; that which stabilizes and allows an enterprise or system to develop.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** groundwork; platform; stage

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. In solitaire or patience games, one of the piles of cards that the player attempts to build, usually holding all cards of a suit in ascending order.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** card-games; games

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The lowest and supporting part or member of a wall, including the base course and footing courses; in a frame house, the whole substructure of masonry.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The foundations of this construction have been laid out.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** architecture

   **Source synonyms:** base; groundwall

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A donation or legacy appropriated to support a charitable institution, and constituting a permanent fund; endowment.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. That which is founded, or established by endowment; an endowed institution or charity.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The Wikimedia Foundation, Inc. is the parent organization of the Wiktionary collaborative project.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Cosmetic cream roughly skin-colored, designed to make the face appear uniform in color and texture.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** cosmetics; lifestyle

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A basis for social bodies or intellectual disciplines.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
