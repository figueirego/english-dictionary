### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To bring into commerce from an earlier condition (such as idea alone, experimental prototypes alone, or one-off custom builds only).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** the work required not only to invent a device but also to commercialize it

   **Source synonyms:** productize

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To apply business methodology to something in order to profit (such as introducing salability to a resource that comes from, or rightfully belongs to, the commons).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** finding the right balance in paying for water infrastructure (treatment plants and pipe networks) but not commercializing water to the point of excluding low-income people from adequate access to a basic necessity

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To exploit something for maximum financial gain, sometimes by sacrificing quality.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** a market district formerly served mainly by artisans but increasingly commercialized for mass tourism
