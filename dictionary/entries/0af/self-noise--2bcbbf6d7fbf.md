## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The input sound level that creates the same output voltage as the receiving microphone in the absence of any sound, and which represents the lowest point of the microphone's dynamic range.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>
