### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act or process of becoming manifest.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The last known manifestation of the ghost was over ten years ago.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The embodiment of an intangible, or variable thing.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** This particular manifestation resembled a young girl crying.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The symptoms or observable conditions which are seen as a result of some disease.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A pattern or logo on a sheet of glass, as decoration or to prevent people from accidentally walking into it.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A political demonstration or protest.

   **Usage:** countable; dated; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Willing something into existence (See manifest (verb)).

   **Usage:** countable; informal; uncountable

   **Example:** I don't believe in manifestation.
