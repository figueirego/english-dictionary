### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A picture formed by a word or words.

   **Example:** An iconograph can take the form of irregularly shaped letters or (especially in the case of poems) irregularly aligned text.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An engraving or other picture or illustration for a book.
