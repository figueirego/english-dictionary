### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make statements that profess commitment, knowledgeability, or intended action (especially when the speaker's actual commitment, knowledge, or further action are in doubt).

   **Usage:** idiomatic; informal; intransitive

   **Example:** When it comes to reducing greenhouse gas emissions, he talks the talk, but it remains to be seen if he walks the walk.

   **Example:** Some leaders only talk the talk but aren't prepared to take any risks.
