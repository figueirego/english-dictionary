## Noun

### Forms

- dress rehearsals — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A rehearsal prior to a public spectacle, where all the performers are in costume, in order to test all aspects of the production by closely reproducing the actual conditions of a performance.

   **Example:** We've got three dress rehearsals before the opening night to prepare ourselves for any potential technical issues.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; theater

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The final rehearsal before a performance, whether or not costumes or performance dress are worn.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any complex activity that serves to prepare for a more important iteration of it later.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** Although the train derailment ended up not being very serious, the emergency services' response served as a dress rehearsal in case of a much worse disaster.
