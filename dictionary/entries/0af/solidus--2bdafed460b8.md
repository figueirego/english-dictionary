### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Various medieval and early modern coins or units of account
   1. A Roman ~23k gold coin introduced by Diocletian in AD 301 and called by that name, but reissued at a slightly lower weight by Constantine I.

      **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Various medieval and early modern coins or units of account
   1. Its successor Byzantine coins, from the eleventh century onward of progressively debased weight and purity.

      **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Various medieval and early modern coins or units of account
   1. Synonym of sol or sou&#58; a Carolingian unit of account equivalent to a solidus of silver.

      **Usage:** historical; obsolete

      **Source synonyms:** sol (synonym; synonym-of); sou (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Various medieval and early modern coins or units of account
   1. Synonym of soldo&#58; the silver coins of various Italian states.

      **Usage:** historical; obsolete

      **Source synonyms:** soldo (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Various medieval and early modern coins or units of account
   1. Synonym of shilling&#58; an English unit of account and, following the Tudor dynasty, silver coin.

      **Usage:** historical; obsolete

      **Source synonyms:** shilling (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The weight of the Roman gold coin, 1/60 of a Roman pound under Diocletian or 1/72 lb. (about 4.5 grams) after Constantine.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A medieval French weight, 1/20 of the Carolingian pound.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Synonym of slash ⟨/⟩, originally (UK) in its use as the shilling mark and now its formal designation by the ISO and Unicode.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** media; publishing; typography

   **Source synonyms:** slash (synonym; synonym-of); originally (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. The formal name of the oblique strikethrough overlay (as in A̷ and B̸) in Unicode.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** media; publishing; typography

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. The division line between the numerator and the denominator of a fraction, whether horizontal or oblique.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** media; publishing; typography

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. The line in a phase diagram marking the temperatures and pressures below which a given substance is a stable solid.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics
