### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To dress or ornament (someone or something), especially in a gaudy, showy, or tasteless manner.

   **Usage:** also; figuratively; literary; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** bedaub; embellish; gaud; overdecorate

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To make (someone or something) dirty; to bedaub, to besmear, to dirty.

   **Usage:** Northern-England; literary; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** bedirty; befoul; sully; assoil (obsolete); beclart (UK; dialectal); bedaub (obsolete); bedirty; imbrue (especially with blood) (archaic; literary); bedizen (UK; dialectal); befile (obsolete); befilth (rare); befoul; begrime; beray (archaic; obsolete); beshit (figuratively; vulgar); besmear; besmirch; besully; bewray (archaic; obsolete); blacken; blot; blur (obsolete); clat; contaminate; darken; defile; dirten (Uganda); dirty; elt (Northern-England; Scotland); file (archaic); foil (obsolete); foul; gunk; gunk up; inquinate; scunge; sling mud; slotter; slur; smear; smirch; smudge; smut; smutch; soil; soss (UK; dialectal); sowl (obsolete); spot; stain; sullow; sully; tarnish; uggle (UK; dialectal; obsolete)
