### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of the Papally-endorsed military expeditions undertaken by the Christians of Latin Europe in the 11th to 13th centuries to reconquer the Levant from the Muslims, as well as expeditions along the Baltic Sea and against the Cathars.

   **Usage:** historical

   **Example:** During the crusades, many Muslims and Christians and Jews were slaughtered.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any war instigated and blessed by the Church for alleged religious ends, (especially) papal-sanctioned military campaigns against infidels or heretics.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A grand, concerted effort towards some purportedly worthy cause.

   **Usage:** figuratively; sometimes ironically

   **Example:** a crusade against drug abuse

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A mass gathering in a political campaign or during a religious revival effort.

   **Usage:** dated

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity; Protestantism; government; politics

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A Portuguese coin; a crusado.

   **Usage:** archaic
