### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To turn to the advantage of.

   **Usage:** often; reflexive; transitive

   **Example:** I availed myself of the opportunity.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To be of service to.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Artifices will not avail the sinner in the day of judgment.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To promote; to assist.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To be of use or advantage; to answer or serve the purpose; to have strength, force, or efficacy sufficient to accomplish the object.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The plea in court must avail.

   **Example:** This scheme will not avail.

   **Example:** Medicines will not avail to halt the disease.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To provide; to make available.

   **Usage:** Africa; India; elsewhere proscribed

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To use or take advantage of (an opportunity or resource).

   **Usage:** Africa; India; elsewhere proscribed

   **Example:** You can avail discounts on food.
