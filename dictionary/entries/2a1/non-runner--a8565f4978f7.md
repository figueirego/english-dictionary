## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who does not take part in the physical activity of running.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A racehorse that is withdrawn before the start of a race.

   **Example:** There were two non-runners in the three o'clock at Puddletown.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A vehicle that cannot move under its own power, owing to mechanical defect or failure.

   **Example:** The Merc on the forecourt is a non-runner.
