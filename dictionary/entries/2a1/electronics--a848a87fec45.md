### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The study and use of electrical devices that operate by controlling the flow of electrons or other electrically charged particles or by converting the flow of charged particles to or from other forms of energy.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Electronics is a popular subject.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Devices which require the flow of electrons through conductors and semiconductors in order to perform their function; devices that operate on electrical power (battery or outlet)

   **Usage:** countable; in-plural; uncountable

   **Example:** Look at all the electronics in this room!

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Electronic circuitry.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The electronics is the difference between a modern and an old-fashioned thermostat.

   **Example:** The electronics are completely updated.
