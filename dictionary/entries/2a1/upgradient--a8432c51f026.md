### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A position up along a gradient from a starting position.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. a location that is the source groundwater for another location; similar to upstream.

   **Example:** Uncontaminated groundwater is found upgradient of a contamination source.
