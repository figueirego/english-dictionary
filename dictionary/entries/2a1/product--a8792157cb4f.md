### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Anything that is produced; a result.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The product of last month's quality standards committee is quite good, even though the process was flawed.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Anything that is produced; a result.
   1. The amount of an artifact that has been created by someone or some process.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Example:** They improve their product every year; they export most of their agricultural production.

      **Source synonyms:** endwork; production; output; creation; yield

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Anything that is produced; a result.
   1. A consequence of someone's efforts or of a particular set of circumstances.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Example:** Skill is the product of hours of practice. His reaction was the product of hunger and fatigue.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Anything that is produced; a result.
   1. A chemical substance formed as a result of a chemical reaction.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Example:** This is a product of lime and nitric acid.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Anything that is produced; a result.
   1. A quantity obtained by multiplication of two or more numbers.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Example:** The product of 2 and 3 is 6. The product of 2, 3, and 4 is 24.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** arithmetic

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Anything that is produced; a result.
   1. Any operation or a result thereof which generalises multiplication of numbers, like the multiplicative operation in a ring, product of types or a categorical product.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Anything that is produced; a result.
   1. Any tangible or intangible good or service that is a result of a process and that is intended for delivery to a customer or end user.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A commodity offered for sale.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** That store offers a variety of products. We've got to sell a lot of product by the end of the month.

   **Source synonyms:** merchandise; wares; goods

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A commodity offered for sale.
   1. Illegal drugs, especially cocaine, when viewed as a commodity.

      **Usage:** US; countable; slang; uncountable

      **Example:** I got some product here — you buying?

      **Source synonyms:** merchandise; wares; goods

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. Ellipsis of beauty product&#58; any preparation to be applied to the hair, skin, nails, etc. Often specifically a preparation used to hold one's hair in a desired arrangement.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; ellipsis; uncountable

   **Example:** Wash excess product out of your hair.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** cosmetics; lifestyle

   **Alternative form of:** [beauty product](../21f/beauty-product--87fffcccfddd.md)
