### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Based on experience as opposed to theoretical knowledge.

   **Example:** I have no idea why this works, but empirically, it works.

   **Source antonyms:** nonempirically; unempirically

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Based on data and evidence gathered in the real world.

   **Example:** We need to test this idea empirically instead of just speculating about it.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** sciences

   **Source antonyms:** nonempirically; unempirically
