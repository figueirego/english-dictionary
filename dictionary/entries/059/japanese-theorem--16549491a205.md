### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A theorem stating that, no matter how one triangulates a cyclic polygon, the sum of inradii of triangles is constant. Conversely, if the sum of inradii is independent of the triangulation, then the polygon is cyclic.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A theorem stating that the centers of the incircles of certain triangles inside a cyclic quadrilateral are vertices of a rectangle.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences
