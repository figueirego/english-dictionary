### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. of a woman, an aunt of one's niece or nephew who is not one's sister; a co-sister-in-law as common aunt to the children of two women's married siblings.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
