### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who breeds plants or animals (professionally).

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Ellipsis of breeder reactor, a type of nuclear reactor that creates more fissile material than it consumes, often used for the production of atomic weapons.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; ellipsis

   **Alternative form of:** [breeder reactor](../291/breeder-reactor--a4775d19f2a1.md)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A pattern that exhibits quadratic growth by generating multiple copies of a secondary pattern, each of which then generates multiple copies of a tertiary pattern.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** cellular-automata; computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A person who has had or who is capable of having children; a person who is focused on the rearing of their own children.

   **Usage:** derogatory; slang

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A heterosexual person; one whose sexual intercourse can lead to breeding; straggot.

   **Usage:** derogatory; humorous; LGBTQ slang

   **Example:** Since the breeders started coming here, you can never tell who likes cock.
