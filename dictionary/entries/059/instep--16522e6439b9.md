### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The arched part of the top of the foot between the toes and the ankle.

   **Example:** high instep

   **Example:** arched instep

   **Example:** injured instep

   **Example:** The boot pressed uncomfortably against his instep.

   **Example:** She kicked the ball with the inside of her instep.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A section of any footwear covering that part of the foot.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
