### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Acting with or showing good sense; able to make good judgements based on reason or wisdom, or reflecting such ability.

   **Source synonyms:** reasonable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Characterized more by usefulness, practicality, or comfort than by attractiveness, formality, or fashionableness, especially of clothing.

   **Example:** I only wear high heels on formal occasions; otherwise, I prefer sensible shoes.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Able to be sensed by the senses or the psyche; able to be perceived.

   **Usage:** especially; formal

   **Example:** For Plato the belief in sensible objects is fallible.

   **Source synonyms:** perceptible; appreciable; detectable

   **Source antonyms:** insensible; imperceptible; unappreciable; undetectable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Able to feel or perceive.

   **Usage:** archaic

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Liable to external impression; easily affected; sensitive.

   **Usage:** archaic

   **Example:** a sensible thermometer

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Of or pertaining to the senses; sensory.

   **Usage:** archaic

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Cognizant; having the perception of something; aware of something.

   **Usage:** archaic
