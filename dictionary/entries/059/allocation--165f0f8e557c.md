### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The process or procedure for allocating things, especially money or other resources.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The allocation of new permits is on a first-come, first-served basis.

   **Source synonyms:** apportionment

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. That which is allocated; allowance, entitlement.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The farmer received his full allocation of water from the government.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Restriction of an embryonic cell and its clonal descendants to a particular cell type or body region.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** embryology; medicine; sciences
