### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A village, the administrative centre of the selsoviet of the same name of Yermakovsky Raion, Krasnoyarsk Krai, Siberian Federal District, Russia.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A village, the administrative centre of the selsoviet of the same name of Yermakovsky Raion, Krasnoyarsk Krai, Siberian Federal District, Russia.
   1. A selsoviet in Yermakovsky Raion, Krasnoyarsk Krai, Siberian Federal District, Russia.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A former village in Mironovka selsoviet, Bagansky Raion, Novosibirsk Oblast, Siberian Federal District, Russia, founded in 1908 and disestablished in 1978.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A settlement in Mikheyevo selsoviet, Severny Raion, Orenburg Oblast, Volga Federal District, Russia.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A village in Oleksandrivka settlement hromada, Kramatorsk Raion, Donetsk Oblast, Ukraine.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A village in Kalynivske settlement hromada, Beryslav Raion, Kherson Oblast, Ukraine, founded in 1917.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>
