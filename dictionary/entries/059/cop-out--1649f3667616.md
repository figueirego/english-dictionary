## Noun

### Forms

- cop-outs — plural
- copout — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Avoidance or inadequate performance of a task or duty; the action of copping out.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Example:** His disappearance on the day of the audition was just a cop-out.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An excuse made in order to avoid performing a task or duty; a reason offered when someone cops out.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Example:** It was a cop-out to say he couldn't sign the petition because he sprained his wrist.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A person who cops out.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Example:** He's always a cop-out when there's hard work to be done.
