**Source sense R1.S1**

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To take revenge for (a particular harmful action) or on behalf of (its victim); to avenge.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Arsenal revenged their loss to Manchester United last time with a 5–0 drubbing this time.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

   **Usage:** reflexive; transitive

   **Example:** We've finally revenged ourselves on our enemies.

**Source sense R2.S3**
