### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To reduce the amount of (something).

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive; with-on

   **Example:** The foliage is out of control and needs to be cut back.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To reduce spending.

   **Usage:** intransitive; with-on

   **Example:** We need to cut back heavily on office supplies. Is there another vendor we can use?

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To reduce consumption.

   **Usage:** intransitive; often; with-on

   **Example:** He needs to cut back on doughnuts. He weighs 289 pounds!

   **Example:** I've cut back to one pack of cigarettes a week.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To perform the cutback maneuver.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports; surfing

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To use a short cut; to move to a location to block someone; to cut someone off

   **Usage:** intransitive; often

   **Example:** He almost scored but the defender cut back to him.

   **Source context (verbatim):** with to
