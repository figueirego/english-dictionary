### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To distract; to draw attention elsewhere.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The father tried to preoccupy the child with his keys.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To worry or concern (someone) so as to distract them.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** It always preoccupies me when he acts like this.

   **Source synonyms:** exercise

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To occupy or take possession of beforehand.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive
