**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; mysticism; mythology; philosophy; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Source synonyms:** enigma

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

   **Usage:** dated
