## Adverb · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In a manner such that the face, front, or surface which is normally directed forward for viewing is positioned downward.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** She was sleeping face-down on the sofa.

   **Example:** He placed the page face-down on the table so that we could not see its contents.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a person or object

   **Source antonyms:** face-up
