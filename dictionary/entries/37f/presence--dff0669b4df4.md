### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The fact or condition of being present, or of being within sight or call, or at hand.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Any painter can benefit from the presence of a live model from which to draw.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The part of space within one's immediate vicinity.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Bob never said anything about it in my presence.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A quality of poise and effectiveness that enables a performer to achieve a close relationship with their audience.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A quality that sets an individual out from others; a quality that makes them noticed and/or admired even if they are not speaking or performing.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Despite being less than five foot, she filled up the theatre with her stage presence.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Something (as a spirit) felt or believed to be present.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** I'm convinced that there was a presence in that building that I can't explain, which led to my heroic actions.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A company's business activity in a particular market.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. An assembly of great persons.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. The state of being closely focused on the here and now, not distracted by irrelevant thoughts.
