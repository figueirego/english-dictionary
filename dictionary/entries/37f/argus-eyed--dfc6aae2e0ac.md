### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Ever vigilant; watchful to the point that nothing escapes notice.

   **Example:** Nothing got past the Argus-eyed proctor at the back of the hall.

   **Source synonyms:** eagle-eyed; hawk-eyed; lynx-eyed; open-eyed; wakeful; watchful; alert; aware; heedful; hip; observant; on guard; on the lookout; on the watch; on watch; vigilant; wakeful; wakesome; wary; watchful; woke (African-American-Vernacular-English)

   **Source antonyms:** heedless; inattentive; oblivious; unobservant

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Possessing exceptionally keen eyesight; sharp-eyed.

   **Example:** An Argus-eyed hawk can pick out a mouse from half a mile up.

   **Source synonyms:** keen-sighted; quick-sighted; sharp-sighted
