### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An open area in a town housing a public market.

   **Source synonyms:** emporium

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The space, actual or metaphorical, in which a market operates.

   **Example:** Some high-street retailers were slow to enter the new digital marketplace of the Internet.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The world of commerce and trade.

   **Usage:** broadly

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A place or sphere for the exchange of anything, such as ideas or fashions.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** marketplace of ideas
