## Verb · Etymology 3 · Record 3

### Pronunciation

### Forms

- moderates — present; singular; third-person
- moderating — participle; present
- moderated — participle; past
- moderated — past

### Senses

<a id="r3-s1"></a>

**Source sense R3.S1**

1. To reduce the excessiveness of (something).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to moderate rage, action, desires, etc.

   **Source synonyms:** accoy; mitigate; soften; temper; temperate; qualify

<a id="r3-s2"></a>

**Source sense R3.S2**

2. To become less excessive.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** He used to be an extremist but moderated later in life.

   **Source synonyms:** cool; settle down; soften; ease off

<a id="r3-s3"></a>

**Source sense R3.S3**

3. To preside over (something) as a moderator.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to moderate a synod

   **Source synonyms:** arbitrate; chair

<a id="r3-s4"></a>

**Source sense R3.S4**

4. To act as a moderator; to assist in bringing to compromise.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** We need more users to volunteer to moderate the comment section of our forum.

<a id="r3-s5"></a>

**Source sense R3.S5**

5. To supply with a moderator (substance that decreases the speed of neutrons in a nuclear reactor and hence increases likelihood of fission).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** a graphite-moderated reactor

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics
