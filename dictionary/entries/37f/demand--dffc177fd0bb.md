### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The desire to purchase goods and services.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Prices usually go up when demand exceeds supply.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The market force that causes buyers to be both willing and able to buy a good or service, as measured by the amount of that good or service that is currently salable at any given price point; the amount itself.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Supply and demand ebb and flow in a complex interplay.

   **Example:** Demand for the kitchenware with the pastel colorway has been running low lately, at an average of only 37 units per week.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** economics; sciences

   **Source antonyms:** supply

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A forceful claim for something.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Modern society is responding to women's demands for equality.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A requirement.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** His job makes many demands on his time.

   **Example:** There is a demand for voluntary health workers in the poorer parts of Africa and Asia.

   **Source synonyms:** call

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An urgent request.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** She couldn't ignore the newborn baby's demands for attention.

   **Source synonyms:** call

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. An order.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. More precisely peak demand or peak load, a measure of the maximum power load of a utility's customer over a short period of time; the power load integrated over a specified time interval.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; electricity supply

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** imposition — a requirement
