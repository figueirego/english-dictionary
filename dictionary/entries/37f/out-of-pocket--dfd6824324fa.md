### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Lacking funds, or suffering a financial loss; broke.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; not-comparable

   **Example:** After three races he was £10 out of pocket.

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Not available; unable to be reached.

## Adjective · Record 2

### Forms

- out-of-pocket — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Of or pertaining to the spending of cash rather than using credit.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** I had lots of out-of-pocket expenses.

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Shocking and unexpected or inappropriate.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; slang
