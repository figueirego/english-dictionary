## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A gear (setting) in a vehicle's transmission which, when selected, makes the vehicle move backward. (Denoted with symbol R on a shifter's labeling.)

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The reverse gear of a Ford Model T was engaged with a foot pedal.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** automotive; transport; vehicles

   **Source synonyms:** reverse; reversing gear

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A gear fitted to a machine which allows it to run in the direction opposite to normal operation.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** This old lathe has a reverse gear, but it wouldn't be used under most conditions, and in fact its use is unsafe under most conditions because the chuck is attached with nothing but a right-hand screw thread!

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; mechanical; mechanical-engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

   **Source synonyms:** reversing gear

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A backward movement; a retreating from a goal; an undoing progress instead of moving forward.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable
