2. The narrow portion that forms the pouring spout of a bottle; the neck of a bottle.
   1. A portion of a bottleneck placed on the finger and used as a guitar slide.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. In traffic, any narrowing of the road, especially resulting in a delay.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Source synonyms:** chokepoint

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The part of a process that is too slow or cumbersome due to the lessening of available throughput.

   **Usage:** broadly; figuratively

   **Example:** It is easy to create entries; processing the paperwork is the bottleneck.

   **Example:** The bottleneck in this computer program is the inefficient sorting process; we should replace it with a faster one.
