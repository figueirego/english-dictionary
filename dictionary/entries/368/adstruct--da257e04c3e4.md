### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To improve something by augmentation or supplementation&#58;
   1. To build on to an existing structure.

      **Example:** Since my little house will no longer accomodate my growing family, I plan to adstruct it by means of an addition.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To improve something by augmentation or supplementation&#58;
   1. To augment a grouping of things, to add material to a heap.

      **Example:** I haven't as much dirt in this pile as I would like; I think that I shall adstruct it by shovelling more dirt on top.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To provide supportive evidence to the extent that the validity of a proposition is reinforced.

   **Usage:** argumentation or debate

   **Source context (verbatim):** in academic theses
