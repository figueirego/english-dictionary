### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. From the time when a law, legal right or decree, contract, ownership interest, partnership (etc.) comes into force.

   **Example:** As the Act was passed without the required quorum, the constitutional court struck down the Act and declared it to be void ab initio

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law
