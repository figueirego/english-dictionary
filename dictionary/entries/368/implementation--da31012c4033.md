### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The process of moving an idea from concept to reality. In business, engineering and other fields, implementation refers to the building process rather than the design process.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Now that the requirements are complete we can move on to implementation.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A result of implementing something; a finished product, system or device.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** His implementation works, but it needs some fine-tuning.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; electrical-engineering; electricity; electromagnetism; electronics; energy; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics
