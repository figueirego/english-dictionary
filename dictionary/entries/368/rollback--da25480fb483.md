## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

- rollbacks — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A return to a prior state by undoing some operation, especially of policy or price changes.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A return to a prior state by undoing some operation, especially of policy or price changes.
   1. A withdrawal of military forces.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A return to a prior state by undoing some operation, especially of policy or price changes.
   1. An operation which returns a database, or group of records in a database, to a previous state (normally to the previous commit point).

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; databases; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

      **Source synonyms:** reversion; revert

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The situation where a rollercoaster fails to reach the top of a hill and instead rolls backward.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A form of flatbed truck adapted or designed specifically to carry other vehicles, for use in recovery (wrecking, salvage) or for transporting.

   **Usage:** countable; informal; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** automotive; transport; vehicles

   **Source synonyms:** slide truck; tow truck\[Appendix&#58;Glossary

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The strategy of forcing a change in the major policies of a state, usually by replacing its ruling regime, or by totally annihilating an enemy's armed forces and occupying the country, as was done in World War II to Italy, Germany, and Japan.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** political-science; social-sciences

   **Source synonyms:** regime change

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. An uncommanded reduction in the thrust of a jet engine.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** aeronautics; aerospace; aviation; business; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences
