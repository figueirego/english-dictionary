### Forms

- what say — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Used to introduce a proposal

   **Usage:** not-comparable; slang

   **Example:** Whatsay to splitting the cost with me?

   **Example:** Whatsay we split the cost?

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. What say

   **Usage:** archaic; not-comparable

   **Example:** Whatsay you, my Lord?
