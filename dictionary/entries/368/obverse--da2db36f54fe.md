## Noun · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈɑb.vɚs/ — source labels: General-American
- IPA: /ɑbˈvɝs/ — source labels: General-American

### Forms

- obverses — plural

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. The heads side of a coin, or the side of a medal or badge that has the principal design.

   **Example:** The medal had a cross on the obverse and had a name inscribed on the reverse.

   **Source antonyms:** reverse

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**
