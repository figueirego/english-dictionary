### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To cross an item out by drawing a straight line through it, as with a ruler.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To reject an option from a list of possibilities.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** As Jane Doe had an alibi, the police were able to rule her out as a suspect.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To make something impossible.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** The constant rain ruled out any chance of a game of tennis.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To disallow.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To make unavailable.

   **Usage:** transitive
