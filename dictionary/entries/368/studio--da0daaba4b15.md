### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An artist’s or photographer’s workshop or the room in which an artist works.

   **Example:** His studio was cramped when he began as an artist.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An establishment where an art is taught.

   **Example:** As he gained a reputation, he took larger space and took students into his studio,

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A place where radio or television programs, records or films are made.

   **Example:** The recording studio had some slight echo, but was good enough to make a demo.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A company or organization that makes films, records or other artistic works.

   **Example:** The studios still make films, but they rely on the strength of their distribution.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A studio apartment.

   **Example:** It’s a lovely little studio with almost a river view.
