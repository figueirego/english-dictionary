### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Waders; rubber pants (including oversized boots) worn when wading in shallow water, especially when digging for clams with a clam rake.

   **Usage:** plural; plural-only

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** plural; plural-only

   **Source synonyms:** capri pants
