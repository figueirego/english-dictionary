### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In an accidental manner; by chance, unexpectedly.

   **Example:** He discovered penicillin largely accidentally.

   **Source synonyms:** casually; fortuitously

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Unintentionally.

   **Example:** He accidentally exposed the bacteria to mold spores.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Unintentionally.
   1. Used with the verb omitted but implied, the verb itself having the meaning 'omit', 'delete' or similar.

      **Usage:** humorous; informal

      **Example:** I accidentally the word 'not'. (meaning "I accidentally omitted the word 'not'")

      **Example:** Oops, I accidentally the entire database. (meaning "I accidentally deleted the entire database")
