### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Obvious or easy to notice.

   **Example:** He was conspicuous by his absence.

   **Source synonyms:** apparent; evident; pointed; observable; perceivable; apparent; arrant; undoubted; glaring; bait (Multicultural-London-English); blatant; clear; clear as a bell; clear as day; clear cut (idiomatic); conspicuous; crystal clear; cut and dried (idiomatic); decided; elucidate (obsolete); evident; eyely (archaic); intuitive; manifest; notable (dated); noticeable; obvious; open-and-shut; ostensible; palpable; patent; plain; plain as a haystack; plain as a pikestaff; plain as day; plain as Dunstable highway; plain as porridge; plain as print; plain as Salisbury; plain as the nose on one's face; prima facie; res ipsa loquitur; splendent; straightforward; undissembled; conspicuous; detectable; discernable; noticeable; observable; perceivable; perceptible; sensable; sensible

   **Source antonyms:** inconspicuous

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Noticeable or attracting attention, especially if unattractive.

   **Example:** He had a conspicuous lump on his forehead.

   **Source synonyms:** attention-grabbing; flashy; prominent

   **Source antonyms:** inconspicuous
