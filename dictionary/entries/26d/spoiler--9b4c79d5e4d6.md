### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who spoils; a plunderer; a pillager; a robber; a despoiler.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One who corrupts, mars, or renders useless.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

   **Example:** Good netiquette dictates that one warn of spoilers before discussing them, so that readers who wish to do so may experience the surprises for themselves.
