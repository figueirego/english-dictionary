### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To deny the truth or validity of (a statement or statements).

   **Example:** His testimony contradicts hers.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To oppose (a person) by denying the truth or pertinence of a given statement.

   **Example:** Everything he says contradicts me.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To be contrary to (something).

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To say things that conflict with each other.

   **Usage:** reflexive

   **Example:** The prime minister contradicted herself during her speech.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To give an order contrary to (another order or wish), oppose (something).

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To give an order contrary to one given by (another person), oppose or resist (someone).

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To speak against; to forbid.

   **Usage:** obsolete
