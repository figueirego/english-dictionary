### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of a page in a book or other publication&#58; having its corner folded down, either due to having been read many times, or intentionally as a sort of bookmark.

   **Example:** By thumbing to the dog-eared pages, she quickly found the items in the catalog she wanted to order.

   **Example:** The pages in his favourite book were dog-eared from years of reading it at bedtime.
