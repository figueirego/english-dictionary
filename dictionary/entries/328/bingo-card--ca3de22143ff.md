### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A card used in playing the game of bingo.

   **Usage:** literally

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A list of expected or stereotypical events, outcomes, or clichés considered likely to occur in a specific situation, especially in reference to an event considered unlikely.

   **Usage:** broadly; figuratively

   **Example:** Boland, Cummins, and Lyon all with centuries in the same innings. I wonder how many people had that on their bingo card.
