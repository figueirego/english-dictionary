### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To sort out, to fix, to mend, to repair.

   **Usage:** Northern-England; especially

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To make preparations; to put things in order; to do trifling business.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To line the hearth of a furnace with sand prior to pouring molten metal.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To be upset or in a bad mood.

   **Usage:** Geordie; reflexive

   **Example:** Divint fettle yersel ower that!

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To remove (as by sanding) the seam lines left by the meeting of two molds.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ceramics; chemistry; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To machine away seam lines or more generally to make small adjustments to a component or machine to improve its fit or operation.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He wants to fettle his gearchange mechanism before the next competition.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences
