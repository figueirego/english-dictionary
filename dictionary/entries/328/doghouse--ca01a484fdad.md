## Noun

### Forms

- doghouses — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any small house or structure or enclosure used to house a dog.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A structure of small size, similar to a doghouse, but offering useful shelter for a human.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Mechanically, an equipment cover with an opening, with a shape resembling a doghouse.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A difficult or demoralizing situation.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A traffic signal with five sections&#58; two on the bottom, two in the middle, and one on top.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Any shabby or disreputable establishment.

   **Usage:** informal

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The condition of being in the doghouse (subject to someone's anger or disapproval).

   **Example:** He's out of the doghouse now that he's bought his wife some flowers.
