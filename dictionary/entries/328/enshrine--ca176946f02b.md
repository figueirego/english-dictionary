### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To enclose (a sacred relic etc.) in a shrine or chest.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To preserve or cherish (something) as though in a shrine; to preserve or contain, especially with some reverence.

   **Usage:** broadly; transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To protect (an idea, ideal, or philosophy) within an official law or treaty.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** Other measures, such as compensation for victims, will be enshrined in the proposed new law.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law
