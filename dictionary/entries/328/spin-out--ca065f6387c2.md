### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To prolong; to make longer (in time or extent).

   **Usage:** ambitransitive; idiomatic

   **Example:** The film was spun out to three hours, despite the plot running out after two.

   **Example:** Somehow I need to spin out this essay to 2,000 words.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To rotate about the yaw axis while skidding, such that the vehicle's longitudinal axis is no longer aligned with its direction of linear motion.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Two cars spun out on the icy road and wound up in a ditch.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** automotive; transport; vehicles

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a vehicle

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To cause one's vehicle to rotate about the yaw axis while skidding, such that the vehicle's longitudinal axis is no longer aligned with its direction of linear motion; or to experience such behavior from one's vehicle.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** The Formula One driver spun out on a tight curve.

   **Example:** Bob accidentally spun out his Mercedes when he hit a patch of black ice.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** automotive; transport; vehicles

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a driver

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To form a subsidiary company which continues part of the prior operations of the parent company.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To freak out; to experience a mental health crisis, especially one that is public or damaging; to be drawn into and derailed by a psychotic break, burst of anger, etc; to fail catastrophically, especially if caused by a freak out.

   **Usage:** informal

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To be at the upper limit of one's cadence, and therefore unable to pedal faster, especially when there is no higher gear available, such as when descending very rapidly.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** cycling; hobbies; lifestyle; sports
