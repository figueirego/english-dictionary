## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A seat among a group of seats in an enclosure, as at a theater or stadium.

   **Example:** He's got a box seat. We're in the bleachers.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A favorable vantage point.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** His desk afforded him a box seat to see who spoke to whom at the watercooler.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A coachbox.
