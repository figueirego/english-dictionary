### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To exude (a liquid) in small drops; also, to give off (a vapour) which condenses in small drops.

   **Usage:** UK; transitive

   **Example:** Firs distil resin.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To impart (information, etc.) in small quantities; to infuse.

   **Usage:** UK; broadly; figuratively; transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To heat (a substance, usually a liquid) so that a vapour is produced, and then to cool the vapour so that it condenses back into a liquid, either to purify the original substance or to obtain one of its components; to subject to distillation.

   **Usage:** UK; transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Followed by off or out&#58; to expel (a volatile substance) from something by distillation.

   **Usage:** UK; transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To extract the essence of (something) by, or as if by, distillation; to concentrate, to purify.

   **Usage:** UK; also; figuratively; transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To transform a thing (into something else) by distillation.

   **Usage:** UK; also; figuratively; transitive

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To make (something, especially spirits such as gin and whisky) by distillation.

   **Usage:** UK; also; figuratively; transitive

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To train a neural network on the outputs or behaviour of a more complex model in order to transfer selected knowledge and capabilities and create a smaller, more focused, or more efficient model.

   **Usage:** UK; also; figuratively; transitive; machine learning

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To dissolve or melt (something).

   **Usage:** UK; obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. To fall or trickle down in small drops; to exude, to ooze out; also, to come out as a vapour which condenses in small drops.

   **Usage:** UK; intransitive

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. To flow or pass gently or slowly; hence (figuratively) to be manifested gently or gradually.

   **Usage:** UK; intransitive

<a id="r1-s12"></a>

**Source sense R1.S12**

12. To drip or be wet with some liquid.

   **Usage:** UK; intransitive

<a id="r1-s13"></a>

**Source sense R1.S13**

13. To turn into a vapour and then condense back into a liquid; to undergo or be produced by distillation.

   **Usage:** UK; intransitive
