## Noun · Record 2

### Forms

- royalists — plural
- Royalist — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A monarchist (supporter of monarchy) or supporter of a particular royal régime.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. A legitimist, a supporter of a particular royal line, especially one in danger of being dispossessed of a throne or actually dispossessed of such, and claiming to have the better claim to the throne on the basis of line of descent; especially&#58;
   1. a Cavalier, a supporter of King Charles I of England during the English Civil War.

      **Usage:** historical

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. A legitimist, a supporter of a particular royal line, especially one in danger of being dispossessed of a throne or actually dispossessed of such, and claiming to have the better claim to the throne on the basis of line of descent; especially&#58;
   1. a supporter of the House of Bourbon, in France since the 18th century and especially during the French Revolution.

      **Usage:** historical

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. A legitimist, a supporter of a particular royal line, especially one in danger of being dispossessed of a throne or actually dispossessed of such, and claiming to have the better claim to the throne on the basis of line of descent; especially&#58;
   1. a supporter of Ferdinand VII of Spain in Spanish South America during the South American Wars of Independence of the 1810s and 1820s.

      **Usage:** historical
