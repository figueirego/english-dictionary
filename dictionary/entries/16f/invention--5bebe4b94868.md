## Noun

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ɪnˈvɛnʃən/ — source labels: UK

### Forms

- inventions — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Something invented.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** My new invention will let you alphabetize your matchbook collection in half the usual time.

   **Example:** I’m afraid there was no burglar. It was all the housekeeper’s invention.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The act of inventing.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The invention of the printing press was probably the most significant innovation of the medieval ages.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The capacity to invent.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** It took quite a bit of invention to come up with a plan, but we did it.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A small, self-contained composition, particularly those in J.S. Bach’s Two- and Three-part Inventions.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** I particularly like the inventions in C-minor.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The act of discovering or finding; the act of finding out; discovery.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** That judicial method which serveth best for the invention of truth.

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** discovery
