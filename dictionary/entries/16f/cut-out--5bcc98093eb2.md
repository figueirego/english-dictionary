## Verb · Record 1

### Forms

- cuts out — present; singular; third-person
- cutting out — participle; present
- cut out — participle; past
- cut out — past

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see cut, out. To sever from something larger, with or as if with a sharp-edged instrument.

   **Example:** Cut out the letters and paste them on the poster.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To refrain from (doing something, using something etc.), to stop or cease (doing something).

   **Usage:** informal; transitive

   **Example:** He had to cut out smoking in order to be prepared for the marathon.

   **Example:** She kept clicking her heels. He told her to cut it out.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To remove; to omit.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** If we cut out the middle-man, we will both have better profits.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To oust; to replace.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To separate (an animal) from the herd.

   **Usage:** Australia; New-Zealand; US

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To stop working, to switch off; (of a person on the telephone etc.) to be inaudible, be disconnected.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** It was around then that the engine suddenly cut out.

   **Example:** Can you say that again? You keep cutting out.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To leave suddenly.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** He must have cut out of the party.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To arrange or prepare.

   **Usage:** usually passive voice

   **Example:** He has his work cut out for him.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To intercept.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. To take a ship out of a harbor etc. by getting between her and the shore.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. To serve time in prison as an alternative to paying fines.

   **Usage:** Australia; intransitive; slang

<a id="r1-s12"></a>

**Source sense R1.S12**

12. To spend (money).

   **Usage:** Australia; New-Zealand
