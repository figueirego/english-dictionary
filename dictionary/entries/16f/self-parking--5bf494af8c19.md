## Noun · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A sensor-based system that allows a vehicle to park automatically.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** This car comes with self-parking and automatic lanekeeping.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** automotive; transport; vehicles

   **Source synonyms:** autoparking

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The action performed by a vehicle that has such capability.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** They were amazed the first time they witnessed self-parking. It was as if a ghost were turning the steering wheel!

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Parking of one's own car, as contrasted with valet parking.

   **Usage:** dated; uncountable

   **Example:** A&#58; Did the wedding have valet parking? B&#58; No, it was self-parking, but the parking garage did have a section marked "reserved for wedding guests today".
