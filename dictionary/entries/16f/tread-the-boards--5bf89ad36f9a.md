### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To work as a theatre actor.

   **Usage:** dated; humorous; idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** He seems to think that he's the greatest actor who's ever trod the boards.

   **Source synonyms:** tread the stage; walk the boards

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To write plays for the theatre.

   **Usage:** dated; figuratively; humorous; idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** After years of acting, she decided to tread the boards as a playwright instead, penning three successful comedies for the local theater company.
