**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who prefers to remain at home, rather than participate in social events elsewhere.

   **Usage:** Canada; US; informal

   **Example:** Although my uncle likes to travel, my aunt is a homebody, so he usually comes with us while she stays home with her cats.
