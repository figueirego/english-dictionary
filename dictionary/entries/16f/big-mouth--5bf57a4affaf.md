### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The mouth of someone who talks too much, especially by making exaggerated claims or by inappropriately revealing information.

   **Usage:** derogatory; idiomatic; often

   **Example:** Shut your big mouth or I'll shut it for you.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person who talks too much, especially by making exaggerated claims or by inappropriately revealing information.

   **Usage:** derogatory; idiomatic; often

   **Example:** Shut up, big mouth!
