<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The symbol &amp;, used to represent the conjunction and or (dated) the Latin conjunction et (and).

   **Example:** The ampersand character in many logics acts as an operator connecting two propositions.

   **Source synonyms:** ampassy; epershand

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To add an ampersand (“the symbol &amp;”) to (a text).

   **Usage:** rare; transitive

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To associate (someone or something) with another person or thing; also, to associate (two or more persons or things) together; to add, to combine, to join.

   **Usage:** figuratively; rare; transitive
