## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A process by which the exons of the RNA produced by transcription of a gene (a primary gene transcript or pre-mRNA) are reconnected in multiple ways during RNA splicing, so as to allow production of multiple forms of protein from one gene.

   **Usage:** uncountable
