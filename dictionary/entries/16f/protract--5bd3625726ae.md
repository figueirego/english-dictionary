### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To draw out; to extend, especially in duration.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To use a protractor.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To draw to a scale; to lay down the lines and angles of, with scale and protractor; to plot.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; natural-sciences; surveying

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To put off to a distant time; to delay; to defer.

   **Example:** to protract a decision or duty

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To extend; to protrude.

   **Example:** A cat can protract and retract its claws.
