### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Negates the meaning of the modified verb.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Did you take out the trash? — No, I did not. I do not think it was my turn, was it?

   **Example:** Not knowing any better, I went ahead.

   **Example:** It was felt desirable that she not leave school before fourteen.

   **Example:** I'd rather Mom (did) not stay, cuz the best thing about a holiday is not working.

   **Example:** Not a day passes when I don't regret not having enrolled in medicine.

   **Example:** A callback pay for the workers called in to work when not scheduled to do so.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To no degree.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** That is not red; it's green.

   **Example:** It's not you, it's me.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used to indicate the opposite or near opposite, often in a form of understatement.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** After not a few threats, they got what they’d been after for so long.

   **Example:** In the not too distant future my view on the matter might be not a million miles away from yours.

   **Example:** I wonder if I shouldn't see a doctor - I've been feeling funny the last few days.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Used before a determiner phrase, a pronominal phrase etc. to convey a negative attitude (e.g. denial, sadness, anger) towards something.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Oh god, not that! Anything but that!

   **Example:** Not another rainy day!

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Used before a non-finite clause (especially a gerund-participial clause) or less commonly a determiner phrase to ironically convey some attitude (e.g. surprise, incredulity, amusement, or embarrassment) towards something.

   **Usage:** informal; ironic; not-comparable
