### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used to dismiss a correction to one's adherence to an alternative standard.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** A&#58; Then you hook the hose up to the bibcock, and you're done. B&#58; You mean the sillcock. A&#58; Tomayto, tomahto.

   **Source synonyms:** potayto, potahto

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used to dismissively suggest that a particular distinction is a distinction without a difference; used to express the assertion that two similar things are effectively the same, despite any trivial difference.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** A&#58; Then you revise the database record for that transaction, and you're done. B&#58; You mean you replace the old record with a new one. A&#58; Tomayto, tomahto.
