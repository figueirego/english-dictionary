### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To enclose in a bottle sealed with a cork.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Here's a bottle to cork that up in.

   **Source synonyms:** cork

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To block with a cork or something similar.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Cork up that pipe.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Shut up; be quiet.

   **Usage:** imperative; intransitive; slang; usually

   **Example:** Hey! Cork up in there!

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To make (someone) be quiet.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** You gotta cork him up.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To suppress an emotion or impulse.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** He's been corking that anger up for years.

   **Source synonyms:** bottle up

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To put cork in a baseball bat; to use a baseball bat with cork in it.

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** I'm sure's been corking up.

   **Example:** He must have corked up that bat.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; baseball; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports
