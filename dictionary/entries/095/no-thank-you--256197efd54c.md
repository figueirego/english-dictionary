### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A polite way of saying no in response to an offer.

   **Example:** “Do you want all the strawberries we didn't eat?” “No thank you, I'm full.”

   **Source synonyms:** no thanks; thanks but no thanks

   **Source antonyms:** yes please
