### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To be available, to have the time, to be without commitments over a certain period of time (thus being able to choose what to do with it, instead of following a schedule).

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To have enough time, e.g. for doing something.

   **Example:** She had time to think about it.
