### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A night several days before Bonfire Night in which members of a bonfire society congregate at their headquarters (usually a pub) to be initiated with the current year's badges and receive information on the upcoming event.

   **Usage:** Sussex
