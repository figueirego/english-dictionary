### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To halt, stop, eliminate, stamp out, or put down, often suddenly or by force.

   **Usage:** US; transitive

   **Example:** Even the king's announcement could not squelch the rumors.

   **Source synonyms:** quash

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To suppress the unwanted hiss or static between received transmissions by adjusting a threshold level for signal strength.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** broadcasting; electrical-engineering; engineering; media; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; radio; radio-technology

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To make a sucking, splashing noise as when walking on muddy ground.

   **Usage:** UK; intransitive

   **Example:** The mud squelched underfoot; it had been raining all night.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To walk or step through a substance such as mud.

   **Usage:** UK; intransitive

   **Example:** The mud was thick and sticky underfoot, but we squelched through it nonetheless.
