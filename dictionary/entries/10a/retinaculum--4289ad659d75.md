### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A connecting band.

   **Example:** the retinacula of the ileocaecal and ileocolic valves

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One of the annular ligaments which hold the tendons close to the bones at the larger joints, as at the wrist and ankle.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. One of the retractor muscles of the proboscis of certain worms.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences; zoology

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A loop on the underside of the forewing of some moths.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences; zoology

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A small gland or process to which bodies are attached; as, the glandular retinacula to which the pollinia of orchids are attached, or the hooks which support the seeds in many acanthaceous plants.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences

   **Source synonyms:** jaculator
