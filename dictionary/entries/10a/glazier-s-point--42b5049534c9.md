### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A small spike or clip (such as a triangular bit stamped from sheet metal) used in glazing (window installation and repair) to affix the panes to the muntins, mullions, and frames, before glazier's putty is applied over it; traditionally or archetypically used by glaziers (although today likely to be used by renovation contractors or DIY renovators).
