## Adjective · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having a specified disposition or temper.

   **Usage:** in-compounds; not-comparable

   **Example:** even-tempered

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Pertaining to the metallurgical process for finishing metals.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; metallurgy; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Pertaining to the industrial process for toughening glass, or to such toughened glass.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Moderated or balanced by other considerations.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source synonyms:** mellow; sober; measured; mellow; middle of the road (idiomatic); mild; moderate; nonexcessive; nonextreme; nonfanatic; proper; reasonable; reasonous; sober; temperate; tempered; unexcessive; unextreme

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Pertaining to the well-tempered scale, where the twelve notes per octave of the standard keyboard are tuned in such a way that it is possible to play music in any major or minor key and it will not sound perceptibly out of tune.
