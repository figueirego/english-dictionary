### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used with other words to show that someone lacks an explanation for what he or she is doing or has done.

   **Example:** Your teacher told me that you vandalized a library book today. I am very disappointed in you. What do you have to say for yourself? Now I don't know if I can trust you around library books.
