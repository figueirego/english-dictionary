### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A roadside inn, usually having a central courtyard where caravans (see sense 3) can rest, providing accommodation for travellers along trade routes in Asia and North Africa.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A place resembling a caravanserai (sense 1) as being a place for resting temporarily, or a meeting place (especially one that is busy, or where people of different cultures encounter each other).

   **Usage:** broadly

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Synonym of caravan (“a convoy of travellers, their cargo and vehicles, and pack animals”).

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Source synonyms:** caravan (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A hostelry, an inn; also (humorous), an (upscale) hotel.

   **Usage:** archaic; broadly
