## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A artificial blue pigment made from barium copper tetrasilicate (“BaO·CuO·(SiO₂)₄”), used in ancient China from the Western Zhou period (1045 BCE) until the end of the Han dynasty (circa 220 CE).

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** Han blue
