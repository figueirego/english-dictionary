**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of telephone numbers dialed in North America, the beginning sequence of a premium-rate telephone number, which involves an additional charge assessed to the caller, part of which is returned to the owner of the number.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** If you're looking for a pretty lady who will whisper sweet nothings into your ear, call 1-900-TRIPLE-X tonight. Additional charges may apply. Not available in all areas. You must be 18 or older to call.
