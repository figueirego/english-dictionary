### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To notice or view, especially carefully or with attention to detail.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** From this vantage point we can observe the behavior of the animals in their natural habitat.

   **Example:** She got up before dawn to observe the lunar eclipse.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To follow or obey the custom, practice, or rules (especially of a religion).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Please observe all posted speed limits.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To take note of and celebrate (a holiday or similar occurrence), to keep; to follow (a type of time or calendar reckoning).

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To comment on something; to make an observation.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The senator observed that the bill would be detrimental to his constituents.
