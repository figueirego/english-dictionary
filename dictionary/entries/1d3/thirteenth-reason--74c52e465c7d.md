### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A relatively minor annoyance or inconvenience, jokingly implied to be the "last straw" that will motivate someone to commit suicide.

   **Usage:** Internet; excessive; singular; singular-only

   **Example:** I swear, if you don't cut that out, you'll be my thirteenth reason why.
