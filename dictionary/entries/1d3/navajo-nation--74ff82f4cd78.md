### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A nation in the United States, a tribal sovereign domestic dependent nation established in 1868 by the Treaty of Bosque Redondo, nominally located in the states of Arizona and New Mexico. Capital&#58; Window Rock.
   1. The people of this nation, or their government.

      **Example:** Legally speaking, such a question could be decided only by the Navajo Nation.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A nation in the United States, a tribal sovereign domestic dependent nation established in 1868 by the Treaty of Bosque Redondo, nominally located in the states of Arizona and New Mexico. Capital&#58; Window Rock.
   1. The territory of this nation.

      **Example:** My parents were on their way to Santa Fe but made a wrong turn somewhere and ended up in the Navajo Nation.

      **Source synonyms:** Navajo Reservation
