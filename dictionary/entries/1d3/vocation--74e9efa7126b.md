### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A divine calling to establish one's lifestyle.

   **Usage:** Ecclesiastical; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The Catholic Church supports and teaches us that there are three vocations&#58; the single life, married life, and the religious life or priesthood.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An occupation for which a person is suited, trained or qualified.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Nursing is a vocation that many people find horrendous.
