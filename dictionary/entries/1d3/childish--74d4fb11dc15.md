### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Immature in thought or behaviour.

   **Example:** Your childish temper tantrums are not going to change my decision on this matter.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Suitable for or expected of a child.

   **Example:** This game is very childish.
