**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Source synonyms:** cataract

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An old English coin from the reign of Elizabeth I, minted for the use of the East India Company, and bearing the picture of a portcullis on the reverse.

   **Usage:** historical
