### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An area intermediate between two mutually exclusive states or categories, where the border between the two is fuzzy or ill-defined.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** It exists in a grey area between legal and illegal.

   **Source antonyms:** bright line; black and white; night and day

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A topic that is not clearly one thing or the other, that is open to interpretation.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Source synonyms:** penumbra
