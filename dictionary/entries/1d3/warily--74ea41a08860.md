## Adverb

### Forms

- more warily — comparative
- most warily — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In a careful and guarded manner, especially to avoid potential danger or harm.

   **Example:** She walked through the dark alley, warily glancing around to make sure she wasn't being followed.

   **Source synonyms:** cautiously; vigilantly; carefully; prudently

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Without trust; in a manner showing a lack of trust.

   **Example:** He listened to the salesman's pitch warily, sensing a potential scam.

   **Source synonyms:** suspiciously; distrustfully; skeptically
