### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A state of mind producing serious, long-term lowering of enjoyment of life or inability to visualize a happy future; any of several mental disorders with this state of mind as a central feature.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** I used to suffer from depression, but now I'm mostly content with my life.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; psychology; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** despondency; hopelessness; misery; blueness; tristesse (literary); mirthlessness; blues; crestfallenness; dejection; depression; despondency; distress; dolefulness; downheartedness; drearihead (obsolete); forlornness; grief; heartsore (rare); ill-being; infelicity; melancholia; melancholy; misery; mishappiness (obsolete); mourn (literary); prosternation (obsolete); regret (obsolete); rue (archaic; dialectal); ruth (obsolete); [sadness](../185/sadness--614bb7aa0bf3.md); sorrow; sorrowfulness; tristfulness; undelight; unhappiness; woe; woebegoneness; woefare; wretchedness

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A period of low morale or unhappiness (a period of experiencing the above-mentioned state of mind) which lasts longer than several weeks and may include ideation of self-inflicted injury or suicide.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; psychology; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An area that is lower in topography than its surroundings.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An area of lowered air pressure that generally brings moist weather, sometimes promoting hurricanes and tornadoes.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** climatology; meteorology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A period of major economic contraction.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** economics; sciences

   **Source antonyms:** boom

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A period of major economic contraction.
   1. Four consecutive quarters of negative, real GDP growth (for example, this operational definition is used by the US NBER and many other writers).

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Example:** The Great Depression was the worst financial event in US history.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** economics; sciences

      **Source antonyms:** boom

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The act of lowering or pressing something down.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Depression of the lever starts the machine.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A lowering, in particular a reduction in a particular biological variable or the function of an organ, in contrast to elevation.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; medicine; natural-sciences; physiology; sciences
