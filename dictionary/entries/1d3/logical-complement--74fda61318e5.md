### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The number, obtained by complementing every bit of a given number, which produces all ones when exclusive ored with the given number.

   **Example:** The logical complement of 0xAAAA is 0x5555 on a 16-bit machine, and 0xFFFF5555 on a 32-bit machine.
