## Noun · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈɹuː.tə(ɹ)/ — source labels: Received-Pronunciation
- IPA: /ˈɹaʊ.tɚ/ — source labels: General-American

### Forms

- routers — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Someone who routes or directs items from one location to another.

   **Example:** The router directed the movement of the company's trucks.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any device that directs packets of information using the equivalent of Open Systems Interconnection layer 3 (network layer) information. Most commonly used in reference to Internet Protocol routers.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** communications; electrical-engineering; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; telecommunications

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A device that connects local area networks to form a larger internet by, at minimum, selectively passing those datagrams having a destination IP address to the network which is able to deliver them to their destination; a network gateway.

   **Usage:** Internet

   **Example:** The router was configured to forward packets outside of a certain range of IP addresses to its internet uplink port.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. In integrated circuit or printed circuit board design, an algorithm for adding all wires needed to properly connect all of the placed components while obeying all design rules.

   **Usage:** electronic design automation

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; electrical-engineering; electricity; electromagnetism; electronics; energy; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A list and record of instructions that follows a part in a manufacturing process.

   **Source synonyms:** traveller
