### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of a canonical set of elliptic integrals to which all others may be reduced. They are a modern alternative to the Legendre forms, and unlike the Legendre forms they are unchanged by the exchange of certain subsets of their arguments.
