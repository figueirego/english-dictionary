### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To officially sanction; to ratify; to confirm; to set as satisfactory.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Although we may disagree with it, we must nevertheless approve the sentence handed down by the court-martial.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To regard as good; to commend; to be pleased with; to think well of.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** We approve the measure of the administration, for it is an excellent decision.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To make proof of; to demonstrate; to prove or show practically.

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To consider worthy (to); to be pleased (with); to accept.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Her mother never approves of any of her boyfriends. She thinks nobody is good enough for her little girl.

   **Source context (verbatim):** followed by "of"

   **Source synonyms:** cheer

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To show to be worthy; to demonstrate the merits of.

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive; usually

   **Source context (verbatim):** with a reflexive pronoun
