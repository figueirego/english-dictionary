### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of various edible fish, formerly species of the genus Tilapia, but now placed in other cichlid genera (such as Oreochromis), native to Africa and the Middle East but cultivated worldwide.

   **Source synonyms:** St. Peter's fish

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A fishy (resembling a cisgender woman) drag queen; a drag queen who could pass for a woman, as opposed to one who is exaggeratedly feminine.
