## Adjective

### Forms

- prochoice — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Supportive of a person's right to choose whether or not to have an abortion.

   **Usage:** public policy

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ethics; human-sciences; law; philosophy; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Supportive of a person's right to choose whether or not to end their life by euthanasia.

   **Usage:** public policy

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ethics; human-sciences; law; philosophy; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Supportive in general of a person's right to choose; supportive of self-determination, bodily integrity, and individual sovereignty.

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** proabortion (derogatory; sometimes); antilife (derogatory); prodeath (derogatory)
