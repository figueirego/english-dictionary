### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of a man&#58; to promise to take (a woman) as a future spouse; to plight one's troth to.

   **Usage:** archaic; formal; transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Often of a parent or guardian&#58; to promise that (two people) be married to each other; specifically and chiefly, to promise that (a woman) be given in marriage to a man; to affiance.

   **Usage:** archaic; formal; transitive

   **Example:** He betrothed his daughter to a distant relative.

   **Example:** The couple were betrothed not long after they met each other.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of God&#58; to enter into a relationship with (believers, or the church as a whole); also, of a priest&#58; to pledge (himself) to the church prior to being consecrated as a bishop.

   **Usage:** archaic; figuratively; formal; transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To pledge or promise oneself to (a cause); to espouse; also (sometimes reflexive), to pledge or promise (oneself or one's efforts) to a cause or to do something.

   **Usage:** figuratively; formal; obsolete; transitive
