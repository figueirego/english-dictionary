### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Every person is entitled to their personal preferences and tastes.

   **Example:** I would never want my bathroom decorated in chartreuse and turquoise, but to each his own, I suppose.

   **Source synonyms:** chacun à son goût; there's no accounting for taste; different strokes for different folks; to each their own

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Everyone should receive what they deserve.
