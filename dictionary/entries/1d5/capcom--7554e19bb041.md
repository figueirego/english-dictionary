### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Syllabic abbreviation of capsule communicator; a NASA position, the liaison between an in-space crew and mission control.

   **Usage:** US space program

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A company or work whose quality, style, aesthetic, gameplay, etc. are similar to those associated with the Japanese video game company CAPCOM (usually positive connotations).

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A company with dubious business strategies and sales practices similar to those associated with the Japanese video game company CAPCOM (e.g. repeatedly recycling old material, marketing old content as new, releasing incomplete "early-access" games, requiring further payments to unlock already paid-for content, sacrificing quality for quantity).
