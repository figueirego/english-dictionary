**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The disposal of consumer waste in a recycling bin in the hope of it being recycled, when it cannot or is unlikely to be recycled.

   **Usage:** neologism; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** aspirational recycling; wishful recycling
