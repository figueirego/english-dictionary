## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A quantity introduced to compensate for uncertainty.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** We don't know exactly how much the load will weigh, so we'll add in a fudge factor to make sure we don't underestimate.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any padding or compensation built into a guess or estimate.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. variant of "pucker factor", meaning something is/was frightening.

   **Usage:** alt-of; alternative; idiomatic
