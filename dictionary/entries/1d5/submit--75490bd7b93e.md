### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To yield or give way to another.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** They will not submit to the destruction of their rights.

   **Example:** We submit to their superior judgment.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To yield (something) to another, as when defeated.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To enter or put forward for approval, consideration, marking etc.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** I submit these plans for your approval.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To subject; to put through a process.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I was submitted to a binding pledge.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To win a fight against (an opponent) by submission.

   **Usage:** mixed; transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; hobbies; lifestyle; martial-arts; military; politics; professional-wrestling; sports; war; wrestling

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To let down; to lower.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To put or place under.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive
