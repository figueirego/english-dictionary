### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having the fibres dyed before they are formed into cloth.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of textiles

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Firmly established in one's beliefs or habits; having a specified characteristic, identity, etc. deeply ingrained in one's nature.

   **Usage:** figuratively; idiomatic

   **Example:** Smith was a dyed-in-the-wool typist and never really got used to writing on computers.

   **Example:** John Major was described by his opponents as a dyed-in-the-wool Conservative.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a person

   **Source synonyms:** inveterate
