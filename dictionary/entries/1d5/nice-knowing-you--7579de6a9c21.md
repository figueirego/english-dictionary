## Phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A farewell for when the speaker thinks or knows that they will never see the recipient again; goodbye forever.

   **Example:** Well, nice knowing you, but I gotta head home. Take care of yourself out there.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A farewell for when the speaker thinks or knows that they will never see the recipient again; goodbye forever.
   1. A farewell to someone the speaker is about to kill or to/from someone who will soon die.

      **Usage:** euphemistic
