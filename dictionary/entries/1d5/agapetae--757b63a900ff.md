## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Women of the early Christian church who cohabited in a state of "spiritual love" with clergy or laymen who had vowed chastity.

   **Usage:** historical; plural; plural-only

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Monks or clergy who cohabited with virgins and widows of the church.

   **Usage:** historical; plural; plural-only

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A 4th century Spanish sect founded by Agape and her husband Elpidius that rejected the institution of marriage and was deemed heretical for its perceived hedonism. Considered also to be a forerunner of Priscillianism.

   **Usage:** historical; plural; plural-only
