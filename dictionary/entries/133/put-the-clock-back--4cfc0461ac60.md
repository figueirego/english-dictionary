### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To change the time in a time zone to an earlier time, e.g. for the end of daylight saving time.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** Don't forget that this Sunday we put the clocks back an hour.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To put something back to the state it was in a previous era.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** He wants to turn the clock back to the 1950s.

   **Source synonyms:** turn back the clock; turn the clocks back
