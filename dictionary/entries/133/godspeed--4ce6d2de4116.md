### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. The wishing of someone a smooth journey, or success, especially at a parting; (countable) a statement expressing this.

   **Usage:** literary; uncountable

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Chiefly in in the Godspeed of&#58; the most important part of something, or point at which something happens; also, the last minute or last moment.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; figuratively; literary; uncountable

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. In back of Godspeed&#58; a distant, remote, or unknown place.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; literary; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** back of beyond
