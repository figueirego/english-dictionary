### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To undergo hardship.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Many artists suffer before becoming famous.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To feel pain.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** At least he didn't suffer when he died in the car crash.

   **Source synonyms:** agonize; anguish; thole

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To become worse.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** If you keep partying like this, your school-work will suffer.

   **Source synonyms:** deteriorate; worsen; atrophy; break down; crack (archaic); decline; deteriorate; degenerate; devolve; confound; fade; fall off; go bad; go downhill; go left; go off; go pear-shaped; go sideways; go south; go to hell; go to pot; go to seed; go to the dogs; go wrong; lapse; molder; pejorate; retrograde; suffer; spend; sweal; shrivel; tabefy; wither; wither away; wane; wanse; worsen

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To endure, undergo.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I've been suffering your insults for years.

   **Example:** We hope you never have to suffer the same pain.

   **Source synonyms:** bear; dree; thole; abear; abide; aby (British; dialectal); accept; allow; bear; bide (dialectal); brook; dree (British; dialectal); endure; forbear; go along with; live with; pass; pocket; pouch; put up with; stand; stick; stomach; suffer; swallow; take; take sitting down (idiomatic); thole (British; dialectal); tolerate; undergang (British; dialectal); undergo
