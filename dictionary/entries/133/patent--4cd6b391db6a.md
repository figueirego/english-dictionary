**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** countable; specifically; uncountable; originally

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

   **Usage:** countable; specifically; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A specific grant of ownership of a piece of real property; a land patent.

   **Usage:** US; countable; historical; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A product in respect of which a patent (sense 1.2.2) has been obtained.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Ellipsis of patent leather (“a varnished, high-gloss leather typically used for accessories and shoes”).

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; ellipsis; uncountable

   **Alternative form of:** [patent leather](../05a/patent-leather--16abd8b49b32.md)

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A licence or (formal) permission to do something.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

<a id="r1-s9"></a>
