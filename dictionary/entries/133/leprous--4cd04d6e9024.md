### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of or relating to one of the diseases known as leprosy.

   **Source synonyms:** lepric (rare); leproid; lazar; lazarlike

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Infected with one of the diseases known as leprosy.

   **Source synonyms:** leprosed; leprosied

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Similar to leprosy or its symptoms.

   **Source synonyms:** lazarlike

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Having the appearance of the skin of one infected with leprosy; flaking, peeling, scabby, scurfy.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Immoral, or corrupted or tainted in some manner; also, ostracized, shunned.

   **Usage:** archaic; figuratively

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Of gold or other metals&#58; contaminated with other substances; impure.

   **Usage:** historical

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** alchemy; pseudoscience

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Synonym of leprose (“covered with thin scurfy scales, scaly-looking”).

   **Usage:** archaic

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences

   **Source synonyms:** leprose (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Causing leprosy or a disease resembling it.

   **Usage:** obsolete
