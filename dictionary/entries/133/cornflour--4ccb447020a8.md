### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A very fine starch powder derived from corn (maize) used in cooking as a thickener, to keep things from sticking, or as an anti-caking agent.

   **Usage:** UK; uncountable; usually

   **Source synonyms:** cornstarch; maizeflour; maizestarch

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A flour for baking and cooking that is ground from corn (maize).

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source synonyms:** maizeflour; maizena (South-Africa)
