## Noun

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /dɪsbɪˈliːf/ — source labels: UK

### Forms

- disbeliefs — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An unpreparedness, unwillingness, or an inability to believe that something is the case.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** She cried out in disbelief on hearing that terrorists had crashed an airplane into the World Trade Center in New York City.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Astonishment.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** I stared in disbelief at the Grand Canyon.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The loss or abandonment of a belief; the cessation of belief.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually
