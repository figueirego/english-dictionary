### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To succeed in (something); especially, to succeed in making a living.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** He quit his regular job and tried to make a go of it as an artist.

   **Example:** They made a go of the new business.

   **Example:** They had to sell farm; after three consecutive crop failures, they just couldn’t make a go of it.
