### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To speak; to utter.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He mouthed his opinions on the subject at the meeting.

   **Example:** How can you get up there and mouth such clichés?

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To represent (words or sounds) by making the actions of speech, but silently, without producing sound; to frame.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The prompter mouthed the words to the actor, who had forgotten them.

   **Example:** She mouthed a warning to me as the teacher entered the room.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To move the mouth, with or without sound; to form (air or words) with the mouth, with or without sound.

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. Ellipsis of mouth the words; to speak insincerely.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; ellipsis; figuratively

   **Alternative form of:** [mouth the words](../170/mouth-the-words--5c36ce069cac.md)

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To utter with a voice that is overly loud or swelling.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To exit at a mouth (such as a river mouth)

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

**Source sense R2.S7**

7. To pick up or handle with the lips or mouth, but not chew or swallow.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The fish mouthed the lure, but didn't bite.

<a id="r2-s8"></a>

**Source sense R2.S8**

8. To take into the mouth; to seize or grind with the mouth or teeth; to chew; to devour.

<a id="r2-s9"></a>

9. To form or cleanse with the mouth; to lick, as a bear licks her cub.
