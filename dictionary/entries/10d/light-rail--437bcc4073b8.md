### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A kind of passenger railway in urban and suburban settings typically characterized by a mixture of tram (or in North America streetcar) and metro features, crossing streets at grade crossings and normally powered by electricity.

   **Usage:** Canada; US; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The new Denver light rail runs along the Interstate highway.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** rail-transport; railways; transport

   **Source antonyms:** heavy rail
