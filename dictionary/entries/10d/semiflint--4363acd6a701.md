### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Maize or corn that is a hybrid where one parent species is flint and the other has a soft outer hull.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Clay that is hard rather than plastic but that lacks the conchoidal fracture pattern of flint.
