### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To cancel an order for food.

   **Usage:** US; informal; transitive; chiefly restaurants

   **Example:** 86 the ham and eggs for table two!

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To temporarily remove an item from the menu.

   **Usage:** US; informal; transitive; chiefly restaurants

   **Example:** 86 the lobster bisque – we won’t have the lobster delivery until tomorrow.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To throw out; to discard.

   **Usage:** US; informal; transitive

   **Example:** We finally had to 86 that old printer after it jammed one too many times.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To deny service to.

   **Usage:** US; informal; transitive

   **Example:** The restaurant 86ed us because we didn't fit the dress code.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To kill.

   **Usage:** US; informal; transitive

   **Example:** He stole from me and snitched on me, so I 86ed him.
