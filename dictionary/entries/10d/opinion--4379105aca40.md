## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A belief, judgment or perspective that a person has formed, either through objective or subjective reasoning, about a topic, issue, person or thing.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** I would like to know your opinions on the new filing system.

   **Example:** In my opinion, white chocolate is better than milk chocolate.

   **Example:** Every man is a fool in some man's opinion.

   **Example:** We invite you to state your opinions about the suggestions.

   **Source synonyms:** conclusion; piece; sentiment; sight; take; verdict; view; award; censure (obsolete); choice; opinion; conclusion; decision; deduction; determination; ergotism; illation; inference; judgement/judgment; mind; result; sight; take; verdict; view

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The judgment or sentiment which the mind forms of persons or things; estimation.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** assessment; esteem; regard

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Favorable estimation; hence, consideration; reputation; fame; public sentiment or esteem.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Obstinacy in holding to one's belief or impression; opiniativeness; conceitedness.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** dogmatism

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The formal decision, or expression of views, of a judge, an umpire, a doctor, or other party officially called upon to consider and decide upon a matter or point submitted.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** decision; judgment; order; ruling

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A judicial opinion delivered by an Advocate General to the European Court of Justice where he or she proposes a legal solution to the cases for which the court is responsible.

   **Usage:** European-Union; countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law
