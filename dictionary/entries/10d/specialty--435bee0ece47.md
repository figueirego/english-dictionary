### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. That in which one specializes; a chosen expertise or talent; bailiwick.

   **Usage:** Australia; Canada; US

   **Example:** medical specialty

   **Example:** academic specialty

   **Example:** They cook well overall, but their true specialty is pasta.

   **Example:** The doctor ordered a consultation with another doctor in a different specialty.

   **Source synonyms:** specialism; department (informal)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A product that originates in and is characteristic of a place.

   **Usage:** Australia; Canada; US

   **Example:** regional specialty

   **Example:** house specialty

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Particularity.

   **Usage:** Australia; Canada; US; obsolete

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A particular or peculiar case.

   **Usage:** Australia; Canada; US

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An attribute or quality peculiar to a species.

   **Usage:** Australia; Canada; US

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A contract or obligation under seal; a contract by deed; a writing, under seal, given as security for a debt particularly specified.

   **Usage:** Australia; Canada; US

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law
