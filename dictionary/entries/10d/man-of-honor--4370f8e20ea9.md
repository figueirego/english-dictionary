### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A man who serves in the role of maid of honor at a wedding, assisting the bride in the ceremony.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A made man, a full member of a criminal syndicate.
   1. A mafioso. A member of the traditional Italian crime syndicates. A member under omerta.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A made man, a full member of a criminal syndicate.
   1. A mobster, a member of organized crime.

      **Usage:** broadly

**Source sense R1.S4**
