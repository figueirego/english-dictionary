## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The practice of bettering oneself without relying on the assistance of others.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** a self-help book

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An act of redressing or preventing a wrong by one's own actions rather than through legal proceedings.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** self-help repossession

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law
