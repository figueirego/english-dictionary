### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A cord of great toughness made from the intestines of animals, especially of sheep, used for strings of musical instruments, racquets, sutures etc.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

   **Usage:** uncountable
