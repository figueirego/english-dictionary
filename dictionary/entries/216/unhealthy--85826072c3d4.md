### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Characterized by, or conducive to poor health.

   **Example:** The villagers lived in unhealthy surroundings.

   **Source synonyms:** unwholesome

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Sick or ill.

   **Example:** He was an unhealthy child.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Tending to corrupt.

   **Example:** He liked unhealthy reading material.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Characterized by disturbed mental health.

   **Example:** He had an unhealthy interest in fire.
