### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Mutually destructive; most often applied to warfare.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Internecine strife in Gaza claimed its most senior victim yesterday when militants assassinated one of the most hated security chiefs there.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Characterized by struggle within a group, usually applied to an ethnic or familial relationship.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The Mongol people were plagued by internecine conflict until Genghis Khan unified them by focusing their aggression outwards on other peoples.
