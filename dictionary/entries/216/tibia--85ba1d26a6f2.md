### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The inner and usually the larger of the two bones of the leg or hind limb below the knee, the shinbone.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The second segment from the end of an insect's leg, between the femur and tarsus.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; entomology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The third segment from the end of an arachnid's leg, between the patella and metatarsus.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** arachnology; biology; natural-sciences; zoology

<a id="r1-s4"></a>
