### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A rite of initiation in which the baptized person is placed into water, often wholly submerged, and then raised from it as the ceremonial act of baptism.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity

   **Source synonyms:** baptism by immersion; baptism by submersion; submersion baptism
