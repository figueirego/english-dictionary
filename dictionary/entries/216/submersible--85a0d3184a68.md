## Noun · Record 2

### Forms

- submersibles — plural

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A small nonmilitary, nonnuclear submarine for exploration.

   **Usage:** UK

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. A retroactive term used for nonnuclear submarines; nuclear submarines are termed true submarines.

   **Usage:** UK

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. A term used primarily by some navies for nuclear submarines, termed true submersibles, because they cannot retroactively declare that their nonnuclear submarines should be called by a different name.

   **Usage:** UK

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. A very small baby submarine designed for specific localized missions, usually while tethered to a submarine or ship for life support and communications.

   **Usage:** US

   **Source synonyms:** anchor; midget-submarine

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. An underwater vehicle with limited mobility, similar to a submarine, but less mobile.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport
