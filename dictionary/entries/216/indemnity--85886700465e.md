### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Security from damage, loss, or penalty.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** indemnification; insurance; assurance

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An obligation or duty upon an individual to incur the losses of another.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

   **Source synonyms:** indemnification; liability; responsibility

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Repayment; compensation for loss or injury.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** indemnification; reparations; recompense; recompensation; amends; acquittal; assyth (obsolete); bote (historical); compensation; indemnification; indemnity; mends (Scotland); quittal (obsolete); recompensation (proscribed; sometimes); recompense; redress; relief; remuneration; reparation; requital; requite; restitution; satisfaction

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The right of an injured party to shift the loss onto the party responsible for the loss.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

   **Source synonyms:** indemnification

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A principle of insurance which provides that when a loss occurs, the insured should be restored to the approximate financial condition occupied before the loss occurred, no better, no worse.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; insurance

   **Source synonyms:** indemnification
