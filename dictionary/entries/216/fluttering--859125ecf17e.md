## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

- flutterings — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Rapid back-and-forth or up-and-down waving or oscillation of, e.g. wings or a vibrating engine; the condition of engaging in such an action.

   **Example:** I was entranced by the colorful fluttering of the butterfly's wings.

   **Example:** The valve's fluttering is a problem, you have to find where it comes from.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The action of atrial flutter, in which the upper chambers of the heart beat too rapidly.

   **Example:** Treatment will reduce the fluttering in your heart.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A feeling of internal shifting, as with indigestion, nervous flutters, etc.

   **Example:** The fluttering in her belly reminded her that her child was coming one day soon.

   **Example:** He felt a fluttering of discontent, but he said nothing.
