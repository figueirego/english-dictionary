### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The identification of geographic location, as of an electronic device or an animal being tracked.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The latitude and longitude coordinates of a particular location. Term and definition standardized by ISO/IEC 19762-5&#58;2008 (this standard has since been revised by ISO/IEC 19762&#58;2016).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
