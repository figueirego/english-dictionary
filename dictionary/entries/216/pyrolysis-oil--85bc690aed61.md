### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of various oils produced by pyrolysis.
   1. Biocrude as an alternative to fossil crude (fossil crude oil).

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of various oils produced by pyrolysis.
   1. An oil similar to biocrude made from plastic waste cleaned up from the environment (i.e., from remediation of pollution).

      **Example:** The researchers hope that the energy needed to make the pyrolysis oil from the garbage patch can be supplied by solar-plus-battery installations and new types of geothermal systems.
