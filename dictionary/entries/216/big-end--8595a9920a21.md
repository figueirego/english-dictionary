### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In a piston engine, the larger end (crankshaft end) of the connecting rod that connects a piston to the crankshaft.

   **Example:** From the sound of that motor, I'd say yer big end's gone, mate.

   **Source antonyms:** small end
