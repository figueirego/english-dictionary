### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act or process of removing water from something.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** drying; desiccation

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The condition in which water in the body drops below normal levels, usually caused by illness, sweating or by not drinking enough.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The runner was taken to hospital suffering from severe dehydration.

   **Example:** Drinking plenty of water can help prevent dehydration.
