### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A part or parts remaining after some has/have been removed or already occurred.

   **Example:** My son ate part of his cake and I ate the remainder.

   **Example:** You can have the remainder of my clothes.

   **Example:** He spent the remainder of the war in a prisoner-of-war camp.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The amount left over after subtracting the divisor as many times as possible from the dividend without producing a negative result. If n (dividend) and d (divisor) are integers, then n can always be expressed in the form n = dq + r, where q (quotient) and r (remainder) are also integers and 0 ≤ r &lt; d.

   **Example:** 17 leaves a remainder of 2 when divided by 3.

   **Example:** 11 divided by 2 is 5 remainder 1.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The number left over after a simple subtraction

   **Example:** 10 minus 4 leaves a remainder of 6

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Excess stock items left unsold and subject to reduction in price.

   **Example:** I got a really good price on this shirt because it was a remainder.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; commerce

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An estate in expectancy which only comes in its heir's possession after an estate created by the same instrument has been determined

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law
