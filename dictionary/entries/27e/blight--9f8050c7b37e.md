### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A diseased condition suffered by a plant; specifically, a complete and rapid chlorosis, browning, then death of plant tissues such as floral organs, leaves, branches, or twigs, especially one caused by a fungus; a mildew, a rust, a smut.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; medicine; natural-sciences; pathology; phytopathology; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The cause of such a condition, often unseen but believed to be airborne; specifically, a bacterium, a virus, or (especially) a fungus; also, an aphid which attacks fruit trees.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; medicine; natural-sciences; pathology; phytopathology; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A state of cloudy, humid weather.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A diseased condition of the face or skin; specifically, bleeding under the conjunctiva of the eye, a form of skin rash, or a palsy of the face due to cold.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; dated; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; pathology; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Something that impedes development or growth, or spoils any other aspect of life.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** bane

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A rundown and unsightly condition of an urban area; also, such an area.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; specifically; uncountable

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To affect the fertility or growth of (a plant) with a blight (noun sense 1.1), especially one caused by a fungus; to blast, to mildew, to smut.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; medicine; natural-sciences; pathology; phytopathology; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** blighten

   **Usage:** broadly; dated; transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; pathology; sciences
