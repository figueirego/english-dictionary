## Noun

### Forms

- fly-by-nights — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A creature which flies at night; a nocturnal flier or traveler.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One who departs or flees at night in order to avoid creditors, law enforcement etc.

   **Usage:** attributive; often

   **Example:** Do not give your credit card number to that fly-by-night operation.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A person or business that appears and/or disappears rapidly, appearing untrustworthy, or giving an impression of transience.

   **Usage:** derogatory; idiomatic

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A traveling businessman or tradesman.

   **Usage:** derogatory; idiomatic
