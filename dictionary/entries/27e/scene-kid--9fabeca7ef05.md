### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A young person belonging to a current popular fad or fashion subgenre, often relating to a musical genre.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Source synonyms:** trendy; scenester

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. (by extension) A member of the scene subculture which was popular amongst young people in Canada and the United States in the 2000s and early 2010s; typically to describe an adolescent.
