## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

- graphotypes — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A process for producing a design upon a printing block. Prepared chalk or zinc oxide is pressed upon a smooth plate by a hydraulic press, and the design is drawn upon this in a peculiar ink which hardens the surface wherever it is applied. The surface is then carefully rubbed or brushed, leaving the lines in relief.

   **Usage:** historical
