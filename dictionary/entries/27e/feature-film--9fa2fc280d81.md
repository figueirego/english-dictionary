## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The main, full-length film in a cinema program that also included a short film and often a newsreel. As of now, it plays after the trailers.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any film produced to be shown in cinemas before being released for home viewing.
