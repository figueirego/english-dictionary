1. Irregular in inflection.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Signifying a nominal stem which alternates between more than one form when declined for grammatical case, particularly in the suffix (such as between \*-r̥- in the strong stem and \*-n̥- in the weak stem).

   **Usage:** Indo-European-studies

   **Example:** The Proto-Indo-European term \*wódr̥ (“water”) is typical of nouns with heteroclitic stems&#58; the strong stem \*wódr̥- occurs in the direct cases while the weak stem \*wédn̥- is found instead in the oblique cases, hence the contrast between nominative \*wódr̥ and genitive \*wédn̥s.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>
