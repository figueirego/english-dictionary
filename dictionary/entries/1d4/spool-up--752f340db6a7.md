### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To wind up onto a spool.

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To increase in rotational speed, producing an increase in thrust.

   **Example:** The large rotational inertia of the heavy compressor and turbine rotors makes the engine slow to spool up from idle.
