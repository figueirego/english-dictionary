### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To persist or continue.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** Keep on trucking!

   **Example:** Mum, Jimmy keeps on poking me!

   **Source synonyms:** continue; go on

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To persist in talking about a subject, perhaps to the annoyance of the listener.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** For goodness sake, will you stop keeping on about it!

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To cause or allow to remain in an existing position.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** The new boss would like to keep the present secretary on.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To cause or allow to remain in an existing position.
   1. To cause or allow to remain in the on or open position.

      **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

      **Example:** Should I turn off this light or keep it on?

      **Example:** Should I shut off this water or keep it on?

      **Source synonyms:** leave on

      **Source antonyms:** turn off; shut off
