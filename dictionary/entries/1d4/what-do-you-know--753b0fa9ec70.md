## Phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. What a surprise; guess what.

   **Usage:** colloquial; often; rhetoric; sarcastic; set phrase

   **Example:** Well, what do you know, it's raining again.

   **Source synonyms:** who would have thunk it

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see what, do, you, know.; especially, synonym of you don't know what you're talking about.

   **Example:** You think so, eh? Well what do you know?
