## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of several plants with a velvety leaf as a most notable feature.
   1. A tall Asian herb, of species Abutilon theophrasti, that has velvety heart-shaped leaves and yellow flowers.

      **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of several plants with a velvety leaf as a most notable feature.
   1. Tree mallow (Malva eriocalyx), native to Europe.

      **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of several plants with a velvety leaf as a most notable feature.
   1. Yellow velvetleaf (Limnocharis flava), an aquatic flowering plant native to southeast Asia.

      **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any of several plants with a velvety leaf as a most notable feature.
   1. A succulent of species Kalanchoe beharensis, native to Madagascar, often kept as a houseplant.

      **Usage:** uncountable; usually
