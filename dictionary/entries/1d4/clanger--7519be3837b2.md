## Noun · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Something that clangs; an alarm bell.

   **Usage:** also; figuratively

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The clapper of a bell, anything that strikes a bell or other metal object to make a ringing sound.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A very noticeable mistake; an attention-getting faux pas.

   **Usage:** Commonwealth; Ireland; UK; informal; often in the phrase drop a clanger

   **Example:** In his speech, the best man got the bride's name wrong. What a clanger!

   **Example:** He dropped a real clanger when he criticized the paraplegic for not standing.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A mistake made by a player; counted in the game statistics in the category "errors including frees against".

   **Usage:** Australia; Australian rules football

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An early hi-hat consisting of cymbals mounted on the rim of a bass drum and struck with an arm on the drum's pedal.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A cicada, Psaltoda claripennis, of New South Wales and Queensland, having an upper body of green and brown and clear wings with green veins.

   **Usage:** Australia

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; entomology; natural-sciences
