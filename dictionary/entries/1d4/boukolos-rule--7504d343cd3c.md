**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A rule of the Proto-Indo-European reconstructed language that states that a labiovelar stop (\*kʷ, \*gʷ, \*gʷʰ) dissimilates to an ordinary velar stop (\*k, \*g, \*gʰ) next to the vowel \*u or its corresponding glide \*w.
