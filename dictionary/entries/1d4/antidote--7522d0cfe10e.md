### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A remedy to counteract the effects of poison.

   **Example:** She reached the hospital in time to receive the antidote for the snake venom.

   **Source synonyms:** mithridatic; alexipharmic; alexipharmac; alexipharmacum; alexiteric; alexitery; treacle; theriac

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Something that counteracts or prevents something harmful.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** We need an antidote for this misinformation.

   **Source synonyms:** remedy
