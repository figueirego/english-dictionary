### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The sideways or downward movement of dissolved or suspended material within soil caused by rainfall

   **Usage:** countable; soil science

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Creation of geological deposits (eluvial deposits) by in situ weathering or weathering plus gravitational movement or accumulation.

   **Usage:** uncountable
