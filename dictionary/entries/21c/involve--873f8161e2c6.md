   **Example:** My job involves forecasting economic trends.

   **Source synonyms:** comprehend

   1. To include (something) as a logical or natural, or necessary component, or consequence or effect of something else; to entail, to imply.

      **Usage:** specifically; transitive

      **Source synonyms:** comprehend

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To cause or engage (someone or something) to become connected or implicated, or to participate, in some activity or situation.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** By involving herself in her local community, Mary met lots of people and also helped make it a nicer place to live.

   **Example:** How can we involve the audience more during the show?

   **Example:** I don’t want to involve him in my personal affairs.

   **Source synonyms:** include
