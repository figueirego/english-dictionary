### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The empty space in the center of a donut.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A small, ball-shaped pastry, made in the same manner as a donut, roughly the size of the hole in a donut.

   **Usage:** Canada; US

   **Example:** These donut holes are delicious!

   **Source synonyms:** munchkin; timbit

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An income bracket in which an individual is ineligible for benefits, despite such benefits being available to those in both the immediately lower and higher income brackets.

   **Usage:** US; slang; welfare

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; insurance
