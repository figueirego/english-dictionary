### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A wax secreted by bees from which they make honeycomb; or, the processed form of this wax used in the manufacture of various goods.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** The candle was made entirely of natural beeswax.

   **Example:** She polished the furniture with beeswax to give it a shine.

   **Example:** The beekeeper collected honey and beeswax from the hives.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** Canada; US; humorous; informal; uncountable; usually

   **Example:** mind your own beeswax
