### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any moisture from the atmosphere condensed by cool bodies upon their surfaces.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Moisture in the air that settles on plants, etc in the morning or evening, resulting in drops.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** rore (obsolete)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An instance of such moisture settling on plants, etc.

   **Usage:** countable; but see usage notes

   **Example:** There was a heavy dew this morning.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Anything that falls lightly and in a refreshing manner.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An emblem of morning, or fresh vigour.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable
