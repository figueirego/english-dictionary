**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Source synonyms:** First Day; Lord's Day; Sun//Sun.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

   **Usage:** informal

   **Usage:** informal

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

   **Usage:** attributive; informal

<a id="r1-s5"></a>
