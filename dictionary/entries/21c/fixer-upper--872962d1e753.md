## Noun

### Forms

- fixer-uppers — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A house, property, car, or other object that needs to be fixed up or repaired, often purchased as an investment.

   **Usage:** US; informal

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A potential romantic partner who may need some motivation to improve their personality or habits.

   **Usage:** broadly; informal

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A person who tinkers or fixes things up; in particular, someone who fixes up houses, properties, etc. as an investment.

   **Usage:** informal

**Source synonyms:** doer-upperer (Australia; colloquial); fix-it-upper
