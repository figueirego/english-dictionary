### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A vector change in velocity, especially a small change as a result of a corresponding change in some other parameter.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The maximum possible change in the scalar speed of a rocket or other vehicle if operated in a vacuum away from external forces.

   **Usage:** space flight

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The minimum scalar change in speed required to perform a particular manoeuvre.
