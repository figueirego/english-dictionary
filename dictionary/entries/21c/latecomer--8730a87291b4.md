### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who has arrived comparatively recently.

   **Example:** The old families in the neighborhood look down on him as a latecomer, his family only arrived in the 18th century, too nouveau for them.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One who arrived late.

   **Example:** At the party the host made a point of greeting all the latecomers he missed in the first pass.
