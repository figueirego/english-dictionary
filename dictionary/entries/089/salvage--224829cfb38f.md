   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

4. The money from the sale of rescued goods.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The similar rescue of property liable to loss; the property so rescued.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The process of acquiring, dismantling, and stocking the pieces of old property such as ships, houses, and vehicles, so that they can be sold on to be reused or recycled.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Anything put to good use that would otherwise have been wasted, such as damaged goods.

   **Usage:** attributive; countable; sometimes; uncountable

   **Example:** salvage cars auction
