### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of or resembling a monolith.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Consisting of a single piece of homogeneous material as opposed to a composite material or an assembly of multiple parts.

   **Example:** A monolithic chunk of titanium with facets for cutting, wrenching, and prying.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Having a massive, unchanging structure that does not permit individual variation.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** Mainstream culture is hardly the monolithic block that its caricaturization often implies.

   **Source synonyms:** homogeneous

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Of a single structure, a singular component; instead of an assembly.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** Monolithic space stations only have a single station module.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Consisting of a single program or codebase.

   **Example:** Monolithic kernels perform all operating system duties in kernel space.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences; software

   **Source antonyms:** modular
