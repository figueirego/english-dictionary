### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To castigate someone; to dress them down.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** His boss really gave him hell when she found out that he'd been lying about filing the reports.

   **Source synonyms:** raise hell

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To torment; To make miserable.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To put up a fight against; to battle strongly and effectively.

   **Example:** A nickname of U.S. President Harry Truman was "Give-'em-Hell Harry" because he could really give them hell when he wanted to.
