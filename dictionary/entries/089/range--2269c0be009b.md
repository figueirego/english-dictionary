### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To travel over (an area, etc); to roam, wander.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To rove over or through.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to range the fields

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To exercise the power of something over something else; to cause to submit to, over.

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To bring (something) into a specified position or relationship (especially, of opposition) with something else.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. Of a variable, to be able to take any of the values in a specified range.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The variable x ranges over all real values from 0 to 10.

   **Source synonyms:** run

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To classify.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to range plants and animals in genera and species

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

**Source sense R2.S7**

7. To form a line or a row.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The front of a house ranges with the street.

<a id="r2-s8"></a>

**Source sense R2.S8**

8. To be placed in order; to be ranked; to admit of arrangement or classification; to rank.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s9"></a>

**Source sense R2.S9**

9. To set in a row, or in rows; to place in a regular line or lines, or in ranks; to dispose in the proper order.

   **Usage:** transitive

**Source sense R2.S10**
