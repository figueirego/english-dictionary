### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A traditional children's toy consisting of a wooden cup with a handle, and a ball attached to the cup by a string.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source synonyms:** cup-and-ball

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The game in which the player attempts to catch the ball in the cup of such a toy.
