## Noun · Record 3

### Pronunciation

### Forms

- post mortems — plural
- postmortem — alternative
- post-mortem — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r3-s1"></a>

**Source sense R3.S1**

1. An investigation of a corpse to determine the cause of death.

   **Source synonyms:** autopsy; PM

<a id="r3-s2"></a>

**Source sense R3.S2**

2. Any investigation after the conclusion of an activity, particularly when said activity produces an unwanted outcome.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** management

   **Source synonyms:** debriefing; AAR

<a id="r3-s3"></a>

**Source sense R3.S3**

   **Usage:** UK; obsolete; university slang
