### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A photograph, printed in a magazine or other publication, of a sexually attractive person (often nude or provocatively dressed), and intended to be removed and pinned up on a wall.

   **Example:** pin-ups on every wall of their dorm room

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The person so depicted.

   **Usage:** attributive; often

   **Example:** pin-up girl

   **Example:** Damn, she could be a pin-up, for real.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A figurehead, a person who represents an idea, cause, etc.

   **Usage:** figuratively
