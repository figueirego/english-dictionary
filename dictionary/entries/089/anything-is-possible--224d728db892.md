### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used to express hope or encouragement that even an unlikely goal can be achieved.

   **Extractor diagnostics (not usage labels):** no-gloss

**Supplemental definition (evidence reviewed; source gap preserved):** The primary Paralympic brand document explicitly connects this exact phrase with inspiration, determination and expanding what people can achieve. This supports the encouragement reading only; it does not assert as a fact that literally every event is possible or import the video as a quotation.
