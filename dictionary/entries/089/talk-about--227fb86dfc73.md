### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used to draw attention to the speaker's characterization of someone or something.

   **Usage:** colloquial; idiomatic; imperative

   **Example:** Talk about a loose cannon! Did you hear his TV speech?

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used to draw attention to the speaker's approval or affirmation of someone or something.

   **Usage:** colloquial; idiomatic; present; progressive; usually

   **Example:** \- I'm gonna wine you and dine you and give you everything you need. - Yes! Now that's what I'm talking about!

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To say; used before a direct quote or paraphrase.

   **Usage:** African-American Vernacular

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see talk, about.

   **Source synonyms:** discuss; talk over; bechat; confer; bespeak (archaic; formal); betalk (dialectal); chat; discuss; moot; talk about; talk over; treat
