### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The loss of water by evaporation in terrestrial plants, especially through the stomata; accompanied by a corresponding uptake from the roots.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; physiology; sciences
