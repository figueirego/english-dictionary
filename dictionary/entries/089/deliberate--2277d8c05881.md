<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Done on purpose; intentional.

   **Example:** a deliberate attempt to cover up his crime

   **Source synonyms:** purposeful; volitional; advertent; conscious; witting; deliberate; intentional; on purpose; purposeful; purposive; volitational; volitional; voluntary; wilful

   **Source antonyms:** unintentional; unwitting

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Formed with deliberation; carefully considered; not sudden or rash.

   **Example:** a deliberate opinion; a deliberate measure or result

   **Source synonyms:** careful; cautious; well-advised; alert; attentive; careful; cautelous (obsolete); cautious; canny; chary; circumspect; conservative; deliberate; discreet; guarded; [heedful](../253/heedful--94eeff05c43a.md); heedy; hesitant; meticulous (archaic); mindful; prudent; reckful; safe; sensible; shy; slow; tentative; timid; timorous; well-advised; wary

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of a person, weighing facts and arguments with a view to a choice or decision; carefully considering the probable consequences of a step; slow in determining.

   **Example:** The jury took eight hours to come to its deliberate verdict.

   **Source synonyms:** circumspect; thoughtful

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Not hasty or sudden; slow.
