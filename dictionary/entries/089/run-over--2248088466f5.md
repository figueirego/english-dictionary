### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To exceed the allotted time.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** The previous presentation ran over and ours had to start late.

   **Source synonyms:** overrun

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To cross by running.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The athletes must run over the bridge to reach the finish line.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To drive over, causing injury or death.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** Can you believe somebody would just run over a cat like that?

   **Source synonyms:** overrun

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To mistreat out of disregard.

   **Usage:** broadly; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** overrun

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To describe briefly; to summarize or recapitulate; to go through or consider quickly.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** Before we start the project, let's just run over who is doing what.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To rehearse quickly.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** You'd better run over your statement before going on the platform.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To overflow.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** The bath water nearly ran over.

   **Source synonyms:** overrun
