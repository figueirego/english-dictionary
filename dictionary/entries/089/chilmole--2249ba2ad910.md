### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A Yucatecan black mole (a rich, dark sauce) made with dried chilies, spices like cumin and oregano, and often includes ingredients like garlic, vinegar, and annatto seeds.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A Belizean sauce or stew made with tomatoes, onions, chilies, and often includes ingredients like spices, vinegar, and sometimes even radishes.
