### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To get involved in; to investigate or explore.

   **Example:** I don't want to go into the details now.

   **Example:** We need to go into the background of the case before jumping to conclusions.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To embark upon as an occupation or profession.

   **Example:** I worked for a while as a PA before I went into teaching.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To divide, to be a factor of.

   **Example:** 11 goes into 88 and 99 but not 100.

   **Example:** 7 goes into 46 six times, with remainder 4.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To attack; to assault physically.

   **Usage:** colloquial

   **Source synonyms:** walk into

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see go, into.
