### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act or process of freeing from zinc.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The undesirable removal of zinc from zinc-containing alloys by a chemical reaction, as when red rot occurs in brass due to presence of acids.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually
