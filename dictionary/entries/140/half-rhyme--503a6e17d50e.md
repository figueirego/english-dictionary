## Noun

### Forms

- half rhymes — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A form of imperfect rhyme in which the final (coda) consonants of stressed syllables (and, in modern English poetry, any following syllables to the end of the words) are identical in sound, but the vowels of the stressed syllables are not.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** near rhyme; quasi-rhyme; slant rhyme

   **Source antonyms:** exact rhyme; full rhyme; perfect rhyme

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An instance of this form of rhyme.

   **Usage:** countable
