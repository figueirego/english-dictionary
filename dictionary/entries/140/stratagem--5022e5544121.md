### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A tactic or artifice designed to gain the upper hand, especially one involving underhanded dealings or deception.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** ploy; ruse

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Specifically, such a tactic or artifice in military operation.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Military deception or artifice.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Cunning and artifice in general.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable
