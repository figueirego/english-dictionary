## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

- big deals — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Something very important or difficult; a matter of great concern; a considerable feat or achievement.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; informal; often; sarcastic

   **Example:** It's a big deal to him to get this promotion.

   **Example:** It's no big deal if you don't finish it today.

   **Example:** Why do you always have to make such a big deal of tiny punctuation errors?

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Someone very important; a VIP.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; informal

   **Example:** She's a big deal in music in Spain.

   **Source synonyms:** big, fat, hairy deal

## Interjection · Record 2

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Indicates that something is not important or impressive; so what.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; informal; ironic

   **Example:** He can run a mile in six minutes? Big deal! Some people can do it in four.

   **Example:** So you beat the worst team in the league. Big deal, call the TV crews.
