**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To ask for the presence or participation of someone or something.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** We invited our friends round for dinner.

   **Source synonyms:** ask out; ask

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To request formally.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I invite you all to be seated.

   **Example:** I always invite criticism of my essays.

   **Source synonyms:** ask; beseech; entreat; request

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To encourage.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Wearing that skimpy dress, you are bound to invite attention.

   **Source synonyms:** ask for; encourage; provoke
