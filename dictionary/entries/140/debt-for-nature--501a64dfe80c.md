### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Being or relating to a financial transaction that writes off a portion of a developing nation's foreign debt in exchange for local investments in environmental conservation.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** a debt-for-nature swap, exchange, or scheme
