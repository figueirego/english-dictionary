   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; phonetics; phonology; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The bus was stuck at the stop as its wheelchair ramp wouldn’t retract after use.
