### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A fade in which the volume of one audio track is lowered as that of another is raised.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A fade in which the picture of one video track is diminished as that of another is brightened.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** broadcasting; film; media; television

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
