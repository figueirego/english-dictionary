### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The right to have something.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person's perceived but unjustified right to something; a tendency to claim what one does not deserve.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Power, an authority to do something.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Something to which one is entitled.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A legal obligation on a government to make payments to a person, business, or unit of government that meets the criteria set in law, such as social security in the US.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
