### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Try the same thing (or similar things) often enough, and, even if the general standard is poor, sometimes one will be successful.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. If enough (perhaps false or reckless) accusations are made against someone, their reputation will suffer, whether or not this is deserved
