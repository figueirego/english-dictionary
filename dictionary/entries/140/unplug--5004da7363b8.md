### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To disconnect from a supply, especially an electrical socket.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** To save electricity, I unplug my computer when I am not at home.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To stop using electronic devices, especially for relaxation or to reduce stress.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To remove a blockage from (especially a water pipe or drain).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I had to unplug the sink the other day&#58; there was far too much hair and gunk down there.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To temporarily disconnect from worldly experiences.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** I need to unplug and spend some time with my family.
