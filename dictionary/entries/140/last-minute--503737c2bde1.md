## Noun · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An arbitrary (non-specific) point in time, too close to a deadline to reasonably begin a critical task.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I like to do everything at the last minute. It does tend to make me late for things, though.
