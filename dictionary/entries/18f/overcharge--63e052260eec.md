## Verb · Record 1

### Forms

- overcharges — present; singular; third-person
- overcharging — participle; present
- overcharged — participle; past

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To charge (somebody) more money than the correct amount or to surpass a certain limit while charging a bill.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Source synonyms:** gouge

   **Source antonyms:** undercharge

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To continue to charge (an electrical device) beyond its capacity.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To charge (someone) with an inflated number or degree of legal charges (for example, charging them with a more serious crime than was committed); to upcharge.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To charge or load too heavily; to burden; to oppress.

   **Usage:** dated; transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To fill too full; to crowd.

   **Usage:** dated; transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To exaggerate.

   **Usage:** dated; transitive

   **Example:** to overcharge a description

   **Source synonyms:** hyperbolize; overstate; stretch; big up (slang); cut it fat (UK; obsolete; slang); exaggerate; hyperbolize (archaic); overcharge (dated); overdo; overemphasize; overstate; play up; stretch (figuratively); three-sheet

## Noun · Record 2

### Forms

- overcharges — plural

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. An excessive load or burden.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. An excessive charge in an account.

   **Example:** This was a blatant overcharge. They tripled the price because they thought that the customer wouldn't know any better.

   **Source synonyms:** price gouging

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. Any amount erroneously charged to a customer above the correct price for a product or service.

   **Example:** The software glitch caused an overcharge.

   **Source antonyms:** undercharge

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. An excessive charge of a rechargeable battery.

   **Example:** It is impossible to end up with an overcharge of your smartphone under any normal circumstances because its hardware and firmware stop the charging when the battery is full.
