## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who believes in enjoying sensuality and the experience of pleasant sensations.

   **Source synonyms:** pleasure seeker; voluptuary

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One who holds to the doctrine of sensualism.

   **Source synonyms:** Aristippian; Cyrenaic; Epicurean
