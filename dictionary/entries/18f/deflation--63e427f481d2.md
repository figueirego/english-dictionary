## Noun

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An act or instance of deflating.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The loss caused utter deflation and disappointment among the fans.

   **Source antonyms:** inflation

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A decrease in the general price level, that is, in the nominal cost of goods and services as well as wages.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** economics; sciences

   **Source antonyms:** inflation

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An economic contraction.

   **Usage:** countable; euphemistic; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** economics; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The removal of soil and other loose material from the ground (or another surface) by wind, leaving it exposed to erosion.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; geology; natural-sciences
