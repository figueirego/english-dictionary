### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A musical composition with lyrics for voice or voices, performed by singing.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Thomas listened to his favorite song on the radio yesterday.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any musical composition.

   **Usage:** broadly; colloquial; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Poetical composition; poetry; verse.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The act or art of singing.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A melodious sound made by a bird, insect, whale or other animal.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** I love hearing the song of canary birds.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The distinctive sound that a male bird utters to attract a mate or to protect his territory; contrasts with call; also, similar vocalisations made by female birds.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences; ornithology

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A low price, especially one under the expected value; chiefly in for a song.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** He bought that car for a song.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. An object of derision; a laughing stock.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable
