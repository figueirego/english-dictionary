### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Demonstrating thought or careful consideration.

   **Example:** Most thoughtful people eventually solve the puzzle.

   **Source synonyms:** careful; contemplative; pensive; thoughty; ruminant; introspective

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Demonstrating kindness or consideration for others.

   **Example:** Preparing dinner is a thoughtful gesture for a new mother.

   **Source synonyms:** thoughty; considerate; attentive; caring

### Related words
