### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A part or structure that serves as a guide&#58;
   1. Either of a pair of folds of peritoneum which connects the caudal end of the fetal testis with the bottom of the scrotum in a male fetus, and by failing to elongate in proportion to the rest of the fetus causes the descent of the testis into the scrotum; or an analogous cord of connective tissue attached to the ovary in a female fetus, which is the precursor of the round and ovarian ligaments.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** embryology; medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A part or structure that serves as a guide&#58;
   1. (more fully gubernaculum dentis) A cord or sheath of connective tissue that connects the sac of an unerupted permanent tooth with the surface of the gum.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** dentistry; medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A part or structure that serves as a guide&#58;
   1. The reproductive tract of an invertebrate, or part of it; especially the section containing the gonophores (in a hydrozoan); a sclerotized structure in the cloaca (in a nematode).

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences; zoology

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A part or structure that serves as a guide&#58;
   1. (rare) A trailing flagellum in certain protists used for steering.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences
