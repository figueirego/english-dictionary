**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To overstep a boundary, rule, or limit; to go too far or do something unacceptable.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I can tolerate a lot, but they really crossed the line when they broke the equipment.

   **Source synonyms:** go too far; overstep the mark
