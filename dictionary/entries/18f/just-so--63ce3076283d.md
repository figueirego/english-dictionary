### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In the precise manner indicated.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** If you don’t turn the knob just so, the door won’t open.

   **Source synonyms:** even so

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. very neatly and tidily.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; Arranged

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To the same extent.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see just, so

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Your dog is just so friendly.

   **Example:** I like any flower, just so as it’s real.
