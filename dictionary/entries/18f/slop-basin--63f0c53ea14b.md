### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A container used for urinating or defecating when it is impossible or inconvenient to go to a bathroom or toilet; a bedpan, a chamber pot. Commonly used in hospitals, where it is normally called bedpan. Formerly used in private residences, particularly those without an indoor toilet or bathroom.

   **Usage:** dated

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A bowl for receiving the leavings of tea or coffee cups at table

   **Usage:** UK
