## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A man who does or can play the lead role in a movie, play or other production; a person who frequently plays such roles.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The type of conventionally handsome and approachable man who is perceived as being capable of playing male lead roles in movies or other productions.
