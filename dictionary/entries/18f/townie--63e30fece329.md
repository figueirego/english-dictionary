### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person living in a university area who is not associated with the university.

   **Usage:** UK; US

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person who has moved from a town or city to a rural area. Especially, one who is perceived not to have adopted rural ways.

   **Usage:** UK

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A person familiar with the town (urbanised centre of a city) and with going out on the town; a street-wise person.

   **Usage:** UK

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A chav.

   **Usage:** UK; derogatory

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A working-class citizen in a metropolitan area.

   **Usage:** US

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A person who lives in a city or town, or has an urban outlook.

   **Usage:** Australia; New-Zealand; UK; informal
