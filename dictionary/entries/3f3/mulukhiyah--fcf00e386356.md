**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Corchorus species, particularly jute mallow (Corchorus olitorius), used as a vegetable in Middle Eastern and North African cuisine, which can be boiled into a mucilaginous broth.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** Egyptian spinach; nalita
