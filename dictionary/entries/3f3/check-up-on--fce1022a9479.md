### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To examine or inspect something in order to determine its condition; to check out.

   **Example:** I will check up on the car tomorrow.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To look after or inquire about someone's well-being.

   **Example:** Katie's sick, so I need to check up on her and see if she needs anything.
