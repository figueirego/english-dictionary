### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An automatic response to a simple stimulus which does not require mental processing.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The descendant of an earlier language element, such as a word or phoneme, in a daughter language.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

   **Source antonyms:** etymon

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

   **Usage:** rare

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The descendant of anything from an earlier time, such as a cultural myth.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A reflection or an image produced by a reflection; the light reflected from an illuminated surface to one in shade.

   **Example:** A reflex camera uses a mirror to reflect the image onto a ground-glass viewfinder.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** arts; hobbies; lifestyle; photography
