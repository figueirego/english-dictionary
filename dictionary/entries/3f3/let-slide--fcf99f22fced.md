### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To let go; to allow; to release; to pass over without action.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The police officer let the ticket slide when she found her brother-in-law's car illegally parked.

   **Example:** The administrator let the minor infraction slide with only a disapproving look.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of intangibles

   **Source synonyms:** let something ride

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To pass out of one's thought as not being of any consequence.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To tolerate a violation of a norm from.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The judge let me slide on the speeding, but not on a $200 seat-belt violation.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of persons

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To allow the condition of something to deteriorate due to negligence or apathy.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** He let the farm slide after inheriting it from his father.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see let, slide.
