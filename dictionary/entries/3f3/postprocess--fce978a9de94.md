### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To process after other processing has been completed.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** He's been postprocessing all morning. He hopes to finish today.

   **Source antonyms:** preprocess

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To subject to postprocessing.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He's been postprocessing the 3D-printed housing by doing certain grinding and polishing steps.

   **Example:** She has to postprocess the CAD/CAM file, to prepare it for the relevant machine, before she can post it.

   **Source antonyms:** preprocess
