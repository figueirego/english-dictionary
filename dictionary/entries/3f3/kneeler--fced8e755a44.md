## Noun

### Forms

- kneelers — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who kneels.

   **Example:** All the kneelers sighed with relief when they were told it was over and they could finally stand up.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A hassock.

   **Example:** The parish deacon was always pushing to get new kneelers for the church, probably because he was old and his knees were sensitive.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An apparatus that permits the loading door of a bus to decrease in height in order to facilitate boarding of passengers that are seniors and physically disadvantaged

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. On a gable end, the stone (often decorated) at the base of the slope of the gable on either side.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** architecture

   **Source synonyms:** kneestone
