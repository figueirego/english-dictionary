## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The tendency of a design project or product cycle to accumulate more and more features or details, rather than to be completed and released at a more basic level.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Our sales division promised our client a feature in our software that is currently not feasible for us to implement, which will certainly lead to feature creep.

   **Source synonyms:** creeping featurism; featuritis
