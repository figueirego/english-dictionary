### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A tumultuous disturbance of the public peace by a large group of people, often involving violence or damage to property.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The protests began peacefully but turned into riots after several days.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A wide and unconstrained variety.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

   **Example:** In summer this flower garden is a riot of colour.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A humorous or entertaining event or person.

   **Usage:** colloquial; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
