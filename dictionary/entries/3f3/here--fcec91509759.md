### Senses

<a id="r4-s1"></a>

**Source sense R4.S1**

1. Used semi-assertively to offer something to the listener.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Example:** Here, now I'm giving it to you.

<a id="r4-s2"></a>

**Source sense R4.S2**

2. Used for emphasis at the beginning of a sentence when expressing an opinion or want.

   **Usage:** Ireland; UK; slang

   **Example:** Here, I'm tired and I want a drink.

<a id="r4-s3"></a>

**Source sense R4.S3**

3. Used to attract someone's attention, often in a hostile way.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** Here! You! Stop doing that!

<a id="r4-s4"></a>

**Source sense R4.S4**

4. Said in response to one's name being called during a roll call, indicating that one is present.

   **Example:** Smith ... Here! ... Jones ... Here! ... Walters ... ... Anyone seen Walters?

<a id="r4-s5"></a>

**Source sense R4.S5**

5. Said to command a person or higher animal to come to the speaker.

   **Example:** "Fido! Here!" — "Woof! Woof!"
