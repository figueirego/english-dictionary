### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An itinerant tinsmith and mender of household utensils made of metal.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A member of the Irish Traveller community or of other itinerant groups; a gypsy.

   **Usage:** Ireland; UK; dated; offensive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A mischievous person, especially a playful, impish youngster.

   **Usage:** usually

   **Source context (verbatim):** with "little"

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Someone who repairs, or attempts repair, on anything mechanical, or who invents such devices; one who tinkers; a tinkerer.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An act of repair or invention.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** I'll have a tinker and see what I can do.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A hand mortar.

   **Usage:** obsolete

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Any of various fish&#58; chub mackerel, silverside, skate, or young mackerel about two years old.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A razor-billed auk, a bird of species (Alca torda).
