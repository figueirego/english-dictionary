### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A planned economy in which the government (or some other central authority) is solely responsible for resource allocation, distribution and the setting of prices, with little to no market influence.

   **Example:** Some scholars continue to blame the USSR's command economy for its 1991 collapse.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** economics; government; politics; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** centrally planned economy; directive planned economy
