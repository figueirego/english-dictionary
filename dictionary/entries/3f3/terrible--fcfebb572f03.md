### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Causing terror, alarm or fear; dreadful

   **Example:** The witch laid a terrible curse on him.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Formidable, powerful.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Intense; extreme in degree or extent.

   **Example:** He paid a terrible price for his life of drinking.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Very unpleasant; disagreeable.

   **Example:** The food was terrible, but it was free.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Having extremely bad qualities; lousy.

   **Example:** Whatever he thinks, he is a terrible driver.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Prone to a particular temptation.

   **Usage:** Ireland; especially

   **Example:** He's a terrible man for beer.

   **Source context (verbatim):** with "for"

   **Source synonyms:** awful
