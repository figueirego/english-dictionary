### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An elected official in Ancient Rome, a tribune of the plebs.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A military officer in Ancient Rome ranking below a legate and above a centurion, a military tribune.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A protector of the people; a public figure who appeals to and on behalf of the people through oratory.

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The domed or vaulted apse in a cathedral housing the bishop's throne (see).
