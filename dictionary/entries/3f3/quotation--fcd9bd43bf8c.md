## Noun

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /kwoʊˈteɪʃn̩/ — source labels: General-American

### Forms

- quotations — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A fragment of a human expression that is repeated by somebody else, for example from literature or a famous speech.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** "Where they burn books, they will also burn people" is a famous quotation from Heinrich Heine.

   **Source synonyms:** citation; cite; quote

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The act of quoting someone or something.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** citation

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A price that has been quoted for buying or selling.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** Let's get a quotation for repairing the roof before we decide whether it's worth doing.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The act of setting a price.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A quota, a share.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete
