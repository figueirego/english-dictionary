## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈteɪlˌɡeɪt/ — source labels: UK; US

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A hinged board or hatch at the rear of a vehicle that can be lowered for loading and unloading.

   **Example:** Drop the tailgate, please, and I'll load these pallets.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** automotive; transport; vehicles

   **Source synonyms:** tailboard

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The hinged rear door of a hatchback.

   **Usage:** UK; especially

   **Example:** Open up the tailgate, please, and retrieve her suitcases.

   **Source synonyms:** hatch

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The downstream gate in the lock on a canal or river, or in an irrigation system.

   **Example:** The locktender closed the tailgate and the chamber started to fill.

   **Source antonyms:** headgate

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Ellipsis of tailgate party.

   **Usage:** US; abbreviation; alt-of; ellipsis

   **Example:** Are you coming to the tailgate? We'll be grilling brisket.

   **Alternative form of:** [tailgate party](../16c/tailgate-party--5b19f72f5ca4.md)

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A tunnel for drawing spent air away from the working face of a mine.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; mining

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To drive dangerously close behind another vehicle.

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** If that idiot keeps tailgating our car they'll end up rear-endeding us.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** automotive; transport; vehicles

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To follow another person through access control on their access, rather than on one’s own credentials, especially when entering a door controlled by a card reader.

   **Source synonyms:** piggyback; draft

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To privately purchase or sell a security immediately after trading in the same security for a client.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a broker
