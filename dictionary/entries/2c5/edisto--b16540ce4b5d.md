## Proper noun · Record 1

### Forms

- Edistow — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A subtribe of the now-extinct Cusabo, a Native American tribal group who lived in what is now South Carolina.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One of South Carolina's Sea Islands, the larger part of which lies in Charleston County, with its southern tip in Colleton County.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A free-flowing blackwater river in South Carolina, flowing from its sources in Saluda and Edgefield counties, to its Atlantic Ocean mouth at Edisto Beach.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An unincorporated community and census-designated place in Orangeburg County, South Carolina, United States.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Ellipsis of Edisto Beach.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; ellipsis
