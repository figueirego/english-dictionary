## Verb

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To be unhelpful or inappropriate&#58; usually used in the negative, as "not go amiss", to express that something would be welcome or useful.

   **Example:** A nice cup of tea wouldn't go amiss.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see go, amiss.

   **Example:** We must find out what went amiss in order to avoid such mishaps in the future.
