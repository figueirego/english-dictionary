### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To cause moisture to be completely removed from; to make dry.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Placing socks on the radiator will dry them out more quickly.

   **Source synonyms:** dessicate; dehydrate

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To have excess water evaporate or be otherwise removed; to become dry.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Your trousers need to dry out before you put them on.

   **Source synonyms:** dessicate; dehydrate

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To (cause to) sober up; to cease to be drunk; to quit using drugs or alcohol.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive; colloquial; idiomatic

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To become less oily, affecting play, after being oiled up.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** bowling; hobbies; lifestyle; sports
