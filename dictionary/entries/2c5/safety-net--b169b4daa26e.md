## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A large net placed horizontally beneath performing aerialists such as trapeze artists or tightrope walkers, intended to catch a performer who falls and to protect him or her from harm.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Anything, such as a governmental program, that provides security against extreme disadvantage or misfortune.

   **Usage:** broadly; figuratively
