### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A traditional Irish dish of back bacon simmered with cabbage and other vegetables such as onions, turnips and carrots, served with the bacon sliced and much of the boiled liquid retained with the vegetables.

   **Usage:** Ireland; uncountable
