### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A long narrow smooth piece of wood designed to depress the tongue, to allow for examination of the rear of the oral cavity.

   **Source synonyms:** spatula

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any device used to depress the tongue.
