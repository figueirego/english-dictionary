### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Ambergris, the waxy product of the sperm whale.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Ambergris, the waxy product of the sperm whale.
   1. Formerly thought to be the product of a plant.

      **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A hard, generally yellow to brown translucent or transparent fossil resin from extinct coniferous trees of the pine genus, used for jewellery, decoration and later dissolved as a binder in varnishes. One variety, blue amber, appears blue rather than yellow under direct sunlight.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A yellow-orange colour.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The intermediate light in a set of three traffic lights, which when illuminated indicates that drivers should stop when safe to do so. See also yellow light.

   **Usage:** Australia; Commonwealth; UK; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The stop codon (nucleotide triplet) "UAG", or a mutant which has this stop codon at a premature place in its DNA sequence.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** an amber codon, an amber mutation, an amber suppressor

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biochemistry; biology; chemistry; genetics; medicine; microbiology; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Hesitance to proceed, or limited approval to proceed; an amber light.

   **Usage:** uncountable
