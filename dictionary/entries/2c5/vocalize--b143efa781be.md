### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To express with the voice, to utter.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To produce noises or calls from the throat.

   **Example:** We could hear the monkeys vocalizing, though we could not see them.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of animals

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To sing without using words.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To turn a consonant into a vowel.

   **Example:** In Hong Kong English, /l/ may be vocalized at the end of a syllable.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences
