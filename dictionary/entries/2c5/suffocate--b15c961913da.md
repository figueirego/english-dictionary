### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To suffer, or cause someone to suffer, from severely reduced oxygen intake to the body.

   **Usage:** ergative

   **Example:** Open the hatch, he is suffocating in the airlock!

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To die due to, or kill someone by means of, insufficient oxygen supply to the body.

   **Usage:** ergative

   **Example:** He suffocated his wife by holding a pillow over her head.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To overwhelm, or be overwhelmed (by a person or issue), as though with oxygen deprivation.

   **Usage:** ergative; figuratively

   **Example:** I'm suffocating under this huge workload.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To destroy; to extinguish.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to suffocate fire
