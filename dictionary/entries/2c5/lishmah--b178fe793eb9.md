**Source sense R1.S1**

1. for its own sake, purely for the sake of something, without outside influence such as possible rewards

   **Usage:** English; Jewish; Judaism

   **Example:** A true tzadik doesn't do a mitzvah for skhar, rather he does a mitzvah lishmah and b'simcha!
