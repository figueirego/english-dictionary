2. A day following a great feast in the Eastern Orthodox and Eastern Catholic churches, on which a person related to the events is remembered.

   **Example:** The Synaxis of John the Baptist follows the Theophany (Baptism of Jesus), the Synaxis of Gabriel follows the Annunciation, and the Synaxis of the Theotokos (i.e. Mary) follows Christmas (Birth of Jesus).
