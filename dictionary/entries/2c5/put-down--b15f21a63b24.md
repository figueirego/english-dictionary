**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To insult, belittle, or demean.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** They frequently put down their little sister for walking slowly.

   **Source antonyms:** compliment; talk up

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of money as deposit, to pay.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** We put down a $1,000 deposit.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** banking; business; commerce

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To halt, eliminate, stop, or squelch, often by force.

   **Example:** The government quickly put down the insurrection.

   **Source synonyms:** end; stop; eliminate; extinguish

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To euthanize (an animal).

   **Usage:** euphemistic

   **Example:** Rex was in so much pain, they had to put him down.

   **Source synonyms:** put to sleep; euthanize

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To execute (a person), especially extrajudicially.

   **Usage:** euphemistic

   **Source synonyms:** execute; down; eliminate

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To write (something).

   **Example:** Put down the first thing you think of on this piece of paper.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To terminate a call on (a telephone); to hang up.

   **Example:** Don't put the phone down. I want a quick word with him, too.

   **Source synonyms:** hang up

   **Source antonyms:** pick up

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To add a name to a list.

   **Example:** I've put myself down for the new Spanish conversation course.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To make prices, or taxes, lower.

   **Usage:** Ireland; UK

   **Example:** BP are putting petrol and diesel down in what could be the start of a price war.

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. To place a baby somewhere to sleep.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I had just put Mary down when you rang. So now she's crying again.

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. To give something as a reason for something else.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** She put her long life down to daily meditation.
