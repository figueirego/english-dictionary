### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of making a list of items; an itemizing.

   **Example:** His itemization of the inventory did seem accurate, however it omitted several crucial entries.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The system of items available to players and their design and qualities.

   **Example:** The itemization in this game is terrible. All the good items are so rare, it gives players no desire to grind at all.
