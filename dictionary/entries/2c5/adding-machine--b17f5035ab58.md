### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A simple mechanical or electromechanical calculator that performs simple calculations, such as addition or subtraction. It aids repetitive arithmetic, as compared with unaided human effort. It cannot do advanced functions like a scientific calculator can.

   **Source synonyms:** totaliser; totalizer; accounting machine; bookkeeping machine; tabulator
