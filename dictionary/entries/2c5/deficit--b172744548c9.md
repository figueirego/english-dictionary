### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Deficiency in amount or quality; a falling short; lack.

   **Example:** The crop output this year has been comparatively small, owing to the deficit in rainfall.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
