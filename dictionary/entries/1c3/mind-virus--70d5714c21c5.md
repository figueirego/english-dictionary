## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A concept, belief, or message that propagates through a population by, analogized to the spread of a biological virus; a self-replicating idea or notion that spreads between individuals, typically by exploiting psychological incentives such as fear, hope, or social pressure.

   **Source synonyms:** brain virus

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A type of virus, or virus-like entity, that targets the brain, disrupting it or causing it to think in a specific way.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** literature; media; publishing; science-fiction
