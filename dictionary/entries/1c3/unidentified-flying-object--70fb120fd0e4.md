## Noun

### Forms

- unidentified flying objects — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Anything not readily explainable appearing to move through or be suspended in the air.

   **Example:** The lights seen over the city last night, originally termed unidentified flying objects, turned out to be spotlights from a car dealership reflecting on low clouds.

   **Source synonyms:** UFO; UAP; unidentified aerial phenomenon; unexplained aerial phenomenon

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Anything not readily explainable appearing to move through or be suspended in the air.
   1. Such things as seem to be discrete objects and comparable to aircraft.

      **Usage:** especially and originally

      **Example:** She said that the unidentified flying object then turned around, zoomed to a higher altitude, and loitered briefly before speeding off.

      **Source synonyms:** UFO; UAP; unidentified aerial phenomenon; unexplained aerial phenomenon

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An alien spacecraft.

   **Usage:** colloquial

   **Example:** He said that the strange beings then took him aboard their unidentified flying object and examined his body.
