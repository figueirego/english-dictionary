### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Sure in one's mind, positive; absolutely confident in the truth of something.

   **Example:** I was certain of my decision.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Not to be doubted or denied; established as a fact.

   **Example:** Now that more experiments have been run, the theory is certain and the argument is settled.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Sure to happen, inevitable; assured.

   **Example:** It is certain that Spain will reach the finals. / Spain is now certain to reach the finals. / Spain is now certain of a place in the finals.

   **Example:** Bankruptcy is the certain outcome of your constant gambling and lending.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Unfailing; infallible.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Fixed; regular; determinate.

   **Example:** at certain intervals

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Particular and definite, but unspecified or unnamed; used to introduce someone or something without going into further detail.

   **Example:** Every wine has a certain distinctive character which sets it apart from all others.

   **Example:** Each morning, she would see a certain man rush past her window on his way to work.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Used to denote that the speaker is referring to a specific person or thing that they do not want to name directly, implying that the listener should infer the identity of the referent.

   **Usage:** euphemistic; preceded by "a"

   **Example:** I would have been here on time, but a certain someone lost the car keys!

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Named but not previously mentioned.

   **Usage:** preceded by "a"

   **Example:** Looking inside the cover, they learned that the book had once belonged to a certain R. Jones.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a person

   **Source synonyms:** one

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. Used before the name of someone famous that people are expected to know.

   **Usage:** preceded by "a"

   **Example:** Since the last British government to make such a proposal was that of a certain Margaret Thatcher, it might not seem unreasonable.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a person

   **Source synonyms:** one

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. Determined; resolved.

   **Usage:** obsolete
