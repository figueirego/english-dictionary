### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To treat with affection, care, and tenderness; to nurture or protect with care.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To have a deep appreciation of; to hold dear.

   **Example:** I cherish your friendship.

   **Example:** his long-cherished dream

   **Source synonyms:** prize; treasure; appreciate; bedear; cherish; esteem; hold dear; prize; think much of; treasure; value

   **Source antonyms:** despise
