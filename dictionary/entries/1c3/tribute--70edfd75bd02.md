### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An acknowledgment of gratitude, respect or admiration; an accompanying gift.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Please accept this as a tribute of our thanks.

   **Source synonyms:** testimonial

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An acknowledgment of gratitude, respect or admiration; an accompanying gift.
   1. An homage made in a body of work to another work or creator.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Example:** Did you catch the Up in Smoke tribute in that episode of That '70s Show?

      **Source synonyms:** testimonial

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A payment made by one nation to another in submission.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The Ancient Romans made their conquered countries pay tribute.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Extortion; protection money.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A payment made by a feudal vassal to his lord.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A certain proportion of the mined ore, or of its value, given to the miner as payment.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; mining

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Ellipsis of cum tribute.

   **Usage:** Internet; abbreviation; alt-of; countable; ellipsis; uncountable; vulgar

   **Alternative form of:** [cum tribute](../095/cum-tribute--254f4a5f0da7.md)
