### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of assessing and prescribing a payment, penalty, price, valuation, etc.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable; Scots law

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The form of existence belonging to a particular object, entity etc.; a mode of being.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; philosophy; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The change undergone by a word when used in a construction.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The result of modifying something; a new or changed form.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The act of making a change to something while keeping its essential character intact; an alteration or adjustment.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** behavior modification officer

   **Example:** Jim's modification to the radio's tuning resulted in clearer sound.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A change to an organism as a result of its environment that is not transmissible to offspring.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Due to his sunbathing, Jim's body experienced modifications&#58; he got a tan.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A change to a word when it is borrowed by another language.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The Chinese word kòutóu had a modification made to become the English kowtow.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences
