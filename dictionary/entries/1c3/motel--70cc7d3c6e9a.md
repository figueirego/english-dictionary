### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A type of hotel or lodging establishment, often located near a major highway, which typically features a series of rooms whose entrances are immediately adjacent to a parking lot to facilitate convenient access to parked automobiles.

   **Usage:** also; attributive

   **Source synonyms:** motor hotel

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A low-cost short-stay hotel, often with hourly rates rather than daily rates, and notorious for permitting illicit sexual activities; a love hotel.
