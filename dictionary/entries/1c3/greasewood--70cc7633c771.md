### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Spiny shrubs containing oil, of the genus Sarcobatus, native to the United States, especially Sarcobatus vermiculatus.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** Fremontia

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of several other North American desert shrubs with glossy or resinous leaves in several species. including&#58;
   1. Adenostoma fasciculatum (chamise).

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of several other North American desert shrubs with glossy or resinous leaves in several species. including&#58;
   1. Baccharis sarothroides (desert broom)

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable
