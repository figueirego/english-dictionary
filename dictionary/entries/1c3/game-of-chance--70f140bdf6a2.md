### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A game in which the outcome is at least partly determined by random variables (chance and luck) rather than strictly by strategy.

   **Example:** The state fair offers many family-friendly amusements, including tasty foods, games of chance, a petting zoo, and more.

   **Example:** Some religious sects frown upon any games of chance, even if no gambling is involved.
