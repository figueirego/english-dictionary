### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To swing back and forth, especially if with a regular rhythm.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** A pendulum oscillates slower as it gets longer.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To vacillate between conflicting opinions, etc.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The mood for change oscillated from day to day.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To vary above and below a mean value.

   **Usage:** intransitive
