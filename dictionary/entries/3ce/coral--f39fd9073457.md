### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of many species of marine invertebrates in the class Anthozoa, most of which build hard calcium carbonate skeletons and form colonies, or a colony belonging to one of those species.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A hard substance made of the skeletons of these organisms.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A somewhat yellowish orange-pink color; the color of red coral (Corallium rubrum) of the Mediterranean Sea, commonly used as an ornament or gem.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The ovaries of a cooked lobster; so called from their color.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A piece of coral, usually fitted with small bells and other appurtenances, used by children as a plaything.

   **Usage:** countable; historical; uncountable
