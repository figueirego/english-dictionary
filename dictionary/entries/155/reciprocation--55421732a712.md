### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of reciprocating; interchange of acts; a mutual giving and returning.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** the reciprocation of kindness

   **Source synonyms:** reciprocity

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Alternate recurrence or action; as, the reciprocation of the sea in the flow and ebb of tides, or the back and forth motion in a piston engine.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** oscillation
