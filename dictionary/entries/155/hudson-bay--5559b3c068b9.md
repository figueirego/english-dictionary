### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A gulf officially in Nunavut, but also surrounded by Manitoba, Ontario and Quebec, Canada, a southern extension of the Arctic Ocean. A large body of saltwater in northeastern Canada.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A town in east-central Saskatchewan, Canada, previously named Hudson Bay Junction, a railway junction for a line to Hudson Bay.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A rural municipality, the Rural Municipality of Hudson Bay No. 394, which surrounds the Saskatchewan town.
