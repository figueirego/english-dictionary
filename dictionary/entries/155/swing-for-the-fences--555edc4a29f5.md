### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To swing at the ball as hard as possible, with the aim of getting a home run.

   **Usage:** US

   **Example:** Don't swing for the fences unless it's an easy ball; be content with running part way.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; baseball; games; hobbies; lifestyle; softball; sports

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To do something extremely ambitious, especially rashly.

   **Usage:** US; idiomatic

   **Example:** I'm not going to swing for the fences tomorrow, but I'm hoping my speech will be warmly received.
