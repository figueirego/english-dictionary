**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any language deliberately constructed to disguise or distort its actual meaning, often by employing euphemism or ambiguity.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** The report was riddled with so much corporate doublespeak that it was impossible to interpret.
