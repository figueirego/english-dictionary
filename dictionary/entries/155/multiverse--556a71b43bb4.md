### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The world, considered as lacking in purpose, design, or predictability.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; philosophy; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The hypothetical group of all the possible universes in existence.

   **Example:** Our universe is a very small part of the multiverse.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astronomy; cosmology; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

   **Source synonyms:** maniverse; meta-universe; metauniverse; metaverse; pluriverse; superverse

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The different canons, continuities or timelines of a fictional property, considered as a whole.

   **Example:** In the DC multiverse, our reality is called "Earth-33". But in the Marvel multiverse, it's "Earth-1218".

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** fiction; literature; media; publishing
