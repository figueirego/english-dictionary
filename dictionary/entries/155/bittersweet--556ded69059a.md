### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Both bitter and sweet.

   **Example:** bittersweet chocolate

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Expressing contrasting emotions of pain and pleasure.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** The break-up was very bittersweet; they both hurt to end it, but were glad it was over.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of bittersweet color (see the noun section, below).
