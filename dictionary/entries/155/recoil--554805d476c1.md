### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A starting or falling back; a rebound; a shrinking.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** the recoil of nature, or of the blood

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The state or condition of having recoiled.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The energy transmitted back to the shooter from a firearm which has fired. Recoil is a function of the weight of the weapon, the weight of the projectile, and the speed at which it leaves the muzzle.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; firearms; government; military; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; politics; tools; war; weaponry

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An escapement in which, after each beat, the scape-wheel recoils slightly.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
