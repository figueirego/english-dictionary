### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A lawyer; one who advises or represents others in legal matters as a profession.

   **Usage:** US

   **Source synonyms:** lawyer; barrister; solicitor; counsel; counselor; advocate\[Appendix&#58;Glossary; abogado; advocate; attorney; avocat; counsel; counselor; latitat (obsolete; slang); lawman (rare); lawyer; legal representative; limb of the law (archaic; slang); man of law (archaic)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An agent or representative authorized to act on someone else's behalf in accordance with that person's instructions.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

   **Source synonyms:** agent; proxy; representative

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. One such who practised in the courts of the common law.

   **Usage:** UK; dated; 19th century and earlier

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A solicitor.

   **Usage:** UK; derogatory; rare; usually; 20th century and later

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An honorific given to lawyers and notaries public, or those holders by profession who also do other jobs. Usually capitalized or abbreviated as Atty.

   **Usage:** Philippines; US; sometimes

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. An attorney tree (Clusia spp.)

**Source sense R1.S7**
