### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of disjoining; disunion, separation.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The state of being disjoined, contrasting, or opposing.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** the disjunction expressed by disjunctive conjunctions, such as but or or

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The proposition resulting from the combination of two or more propositions using the or operator.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; logic; mathematics; philosophy; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A logical operator that results in “true” when any of its operands are true.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. During meiosis, the separation of chromosomes (homologous in meiosis I, and sister chromatids in meiosis II).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences
