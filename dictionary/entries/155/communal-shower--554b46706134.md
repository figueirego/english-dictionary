## Noun

### Forms

- communal showers — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A place where multiple showers are in the same room, for multiple people to use at the same time, wherein there may or may not be a partition between the showers.

   **Example:** The new fitness center has communal showers in the locker rooms.

   **Source synonyms:** gang shower

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An instance of using such a place to take a shower.

   **Example:** The basketball team took a communal shower after the game.
