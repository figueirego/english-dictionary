## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The process of making anything Russian.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** the Russification of Western rock music

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The process of making anything Russian.
   1. A policy that enforces traditional Russian values either domestically or internationally.

      **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The process of making anything Russian.
   1. Localization of a software or hardware interface (e.g. text on buttons of a washing machine) into the Russian language, whether using Cyrillic or transliteration. See Computer Russification.

      **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The process of making anything Russian.
   1. Enabling a computer system to work with the Russian Cyrillic alphabet.

      **Usage:** rare; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences
