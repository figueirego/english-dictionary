   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To persuade someone not to do something; to discourage.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Their boss deterred them from both taking holidays at the same time, claiming he couldn't manage it all on his own.
