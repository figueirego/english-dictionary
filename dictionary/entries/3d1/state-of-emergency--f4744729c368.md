### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An unusually difficult or dangerous situation in which a government gives itself special powers or suspends normal legal procedures so as to regain control.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A governmental decree declaring such situation.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The status quo following such declaration.
