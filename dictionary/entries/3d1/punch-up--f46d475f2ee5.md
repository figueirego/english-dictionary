### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To beat (someone) up by punching, to fist-fight.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He came back all punched up. He got a few stitches — they really punched him up good.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To attack, counterattack, or target a group of greater power or status than oneself.

   **Usage:** intransitive; slang

   **Example:** Her standup act involves a lot of punching up. It's possible to punch down in standup, a little bit here and there, but it's tricky to get right.

   **Source synonyms:** punch upward; punch upwards

   **Source antonyms:** punch down

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To make bolder, zestier, or more exciting.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He punched up his sauce with some habaneros. He figured he needed something to punch it up a little.

   **Source synonyms:** kick up; spice up

**Source sense R1.S4**
