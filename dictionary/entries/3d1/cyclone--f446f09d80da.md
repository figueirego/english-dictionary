## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈsaɪ.kloʊn/ — source labels: US
- IPA: /ˈsaɪ.kləʊn/ — source labels: Received-Pronunciation

### Forms

- cyclones — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any weather phenomenon consisting of a system of winds rotating around a centre of low atmospheric pressure; a low pressure system.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Source synonyms:** storm

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A tropical cyclone occurring in the South Pacific or Indian Ocean.

   **Usage:** specifically

   **Source synonyms:** typhoon; hurricane

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The more or less violent, small-scale circulations such as tornadoes, waterspouts, and dust devils.

   **Usage:** informal

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A strong wind.

   **Usage:** informal

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A cyclone separator; the cylindrical vortex tube within such a separator
