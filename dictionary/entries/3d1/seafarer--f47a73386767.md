### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Someone whose occupation is operating a ship or boat at sea, for either civilian or military purposes.

   **Example:** An intergovernmental panel will draw up plans to relieve seafarers who have been stuck at sea during a crisis.

   **Source synonyms:** mariner; seaman; sailor

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Anyone who travels by sea.

   **Example:** Clearly the earliest Australians were seafarers who landed there tens of thousands of years ago; our quest to know more about them — did they come from Melanesia? what kind of boats did they have? were they blown out to sea by a storm? — is ongoing.
