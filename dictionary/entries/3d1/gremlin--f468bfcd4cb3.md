<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Usage:** obsolete; Royal Air Force slang

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** aeronautics; aerospace; aviation; business; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

   **Source synonyms:** absolute zero; also-ran; chopped liver (humorous; informal); cipher; crumb (slang); Joe Nobody; little guy; minnow; nebbish; nobody; no one; nothing; nothingburger; nonentity; picayune (US; informal); pip-squeak (US); pleb; peon; prole; puny (archaic); quidam (obsolete); rinky-dink (US; informal); shrimp; wankstain (UK); zero (US); zilch (US)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An imaginary creature reputed to be mischievously inclined, for example, to damage or dismantle machinery.

   **Usage:** Royal Air Force slang

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** aeronautics; aerospace; aviation; business; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any mysterious, unknown source of mischief or trouble, or the problem created thereby.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** We rechecked everything, and we suspect gremlins in the database.

   **Source synonyms:** bug; glitch; hitch

   **Usage:** broadly; slang

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; skateboarding; sports; surfing

   **Source synonyms:** grem; gremmie; gremmy; grommet
