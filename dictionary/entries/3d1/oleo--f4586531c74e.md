### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A type of energy-absorbing landing gear strut in which sudden compression or extension of the strut causes a gas-filled chamber to push or pull a piston which forces oil through a small orifice, resulting in absorption of energy through viscous effects.

   **Example:** If the oil leaks out of the oleos, they won't be able to cushion the aircraft against a hard landing.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** aeronautics; aerospace; aviation; business; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences
