**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A conserved property of the light in an optical system which characterizes how "spread out" the light is in terms of angle and area&#58; it is the product of its cross-sectional area (normal to the direction of propagation) and the solid angle it subtends.

   **Usage:** uncountable
