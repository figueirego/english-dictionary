## Adjective · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Lacking a definition or value.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. That does not have a logically sound meaning within a specific formal system and is thus not assigned an interpretation.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The result of division by zero is undefined.

   **Example:** Within the set of real numbers, the square root of a negative number is undefined.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. That behaves unreliably due to dependence on an uninitialized value or inconsistently ported code.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** In C, relying on a variable's value right after a malloc call is undefined behvaior.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences
