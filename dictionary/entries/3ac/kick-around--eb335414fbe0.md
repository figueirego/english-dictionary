### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To abuse or mistreat; to bully; to be rough with.

   **Example:** Don't be afraid to kick it around a little. It is sturdy.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To wander loose; to float around; to hang around; to exist; to remain unexploited or unused.

   **Usage:** continuative; present; usually

   **Example:** Is this your pen I found kicking around in my drawer?

   **Example:** to kick around ideas
