## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A region of Southeast Asia&#58; generally used to refer to Maritime Southeast Asia, which includes Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, Brunei, and the Philippines.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An archipelago of Southeast Asia&#58; Malay Archipelago or the Malay world.

   **Usage:** Brunei; Malaysia; Singapore

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Indonesian Archipelago, the islands comprising the country of Indonesia or to the geographical groups which include its islands.

   **Usage:** Indonesia

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A country in Southeast Asia&#58; Endonym for the Republic of Indonesia, including everything contained within it (i.e., covering the regions, people, cultures, languages, etc.).

   **Usage:** Indonesia

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The planned capital city of Indonesia in East Kalimantan, on Borneo.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A region of Southeast Asia. The conquered territories of the Majapahit empire, corresponding to present-day Indonesia (main) and several Southeast Asian countries.

   **Usage:** archaic; historical
