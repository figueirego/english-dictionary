### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To pass on knowledge to.

   **Usage:** ditransitive

   **Example:** Can you teach me to sew? Can you teach sewing to me?

   **Source synonyms:** educate; instruct

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To pass on knowledge generally, especially as one's profession; to act as a teacher.

   **Usage:** intransitive; stative

   **Example:** She used to teach at university.

   **Source antonyms:** learn

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To cause (someone) to learn or understand (something).

   **Usage:** ditransitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To cause to know the disagreeable consequences of some action.

   **Usage:** ditransitive

   **Example:** I'll teach you to make fun of me!

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To show (someone) the way; to guide, conduct; to point, indicate.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

   **Example:** ‘The bliss is there’, mumbled the old man and taught to Heaven.
