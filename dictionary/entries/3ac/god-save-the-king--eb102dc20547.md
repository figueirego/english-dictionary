## Interjection · Record 2

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. An expression of one’s patriotism and hope for the long life of the monarch, especially in his presence.

   **Usage:** Australia; Canada; New-Zealand; UK

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. A formal statement at the end of many proclamations issued by the King or in his name.

   **Usage:** UK

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. A formal declaration following the death of a reigning monarch when the next monarch is male.

   **Usage:** UK

   **Example:** The Queen is dead! God save the King!
