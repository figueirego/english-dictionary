## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The injection of an otherwise non-pharmacological and non-active irritant solution into the body, generally in the region of tendons or ligaments for the purpose of strengthening weakened connective tissue and alleviating musculoskeletal pain.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** proliferation therapy
