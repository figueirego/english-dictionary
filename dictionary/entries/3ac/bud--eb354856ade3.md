### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A newly sprouted leaf or blossom that has not yet unfolded.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** After a long, cold winter, the trees finally began to produce buds.

   **Source synonyms:** budset

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

   **Example:** breast buds

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A small rounded body in the process of splitting from an organism, which may grow into a genetically identical new organism.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** In this slide, you can see a yeast cell forming buds.

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To form buds.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The trees are finally starting to bud.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To reproduce by splitting off buds.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Yeast reproduces by budding.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To begin to grow, or to issue from a stock in the manner of a bud, as a horn.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Seeds of dissent were budding among the recruits.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To put forth as a bud.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**
