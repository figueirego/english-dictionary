### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A fuss made over something.

   **Example:** I was embarrassed that they made such a big to-do out of my birthday.

   **Source synonyms:** ado; bustle; fuss; ado; argy-bargy (UK); bangarang; bobbery (dated); brouhaha; bunfight; bustle; bust-up; chaos; ferment; clamour; clatter; commotion; disruption; disturbance; flap; flutter; foofaraw; fracas; furor; fuss; hobbleshaw (obsolete); hoo-ha; hoo-hah; hubbub; hullabaloo; hurly-burly; kerfuffle; mayhem; merry hell; outcry; palaver; pandemonium; pother; quarrel; riot; rowdydow (archaic); ruckus; rumpus; shemozzle; shindy (slang); splatterdash; stir; stooshie (Scotland); three-ring circus; to-do; tomfoolery; trouble; tumult; turmoil; unrest; uproar; upstir

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A task that has been noted as one that must be completed, especially on a list.

   **Example:** My to-do list has been growing longer every day.

   **Example:** I have so many to-dos, I don't know where to start.
