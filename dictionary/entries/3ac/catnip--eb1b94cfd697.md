### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of the about 250 species of flowering plant of the genus Nepeta, family Lamiaceae, certain of which are said to have medicinal qualities.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** catmint; catwort

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of the about 250 species of flowering plant of the genus Nepeta, family Lamiaceae, certain of which are said to have medicinal qualities.
   1. Nepeta cataria and Nepeta grandiflora (and perhaps other species), which are well-known for causing an apparently harmless pheromone-based intoxication among certain cats.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Example:** Some cats go bonkers over catnip; others ignore it.

      **Source synonyms:** catmint; catwort

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Something that causes excitement or interest.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

   **Example:** Their outrage was catnip to the trolls who had deepfaked the video.

   **Source synonyms:** crack
