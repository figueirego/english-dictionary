### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To blow on something causing it to topple.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The wind blew over the pole.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To be knocked down by wind.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The tree blew over in the storm.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To pass naturally; to go away; to calm down or subside.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** They huddled, waiting for the storm to blow over.

   **Example:** You cannot simply wait for a problem like that to blow over.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To overwhelm.

   **Usage:** transitive
