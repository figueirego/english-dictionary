**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An organization, the objective of which is to carry out a charitable purpose.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** The event raised money for local charities.

   **Example:** He donated half his earnings to charity.

   **Example:** give to charity

   **Example:** registered charity

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The goods or money given to those in need, or the act of doing it.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source synonyms:** dole

**Source sense R1.S3**

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** act of charity
