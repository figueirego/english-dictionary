### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A heavy object used for battering down walls and gates or a siege engine that includes such an object.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A modern device, usually in the form of a metal bar with handles, chiefly used by police and firefighters to force open locked doors.

   **Source synonyms:** enforcer

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
