### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A hand tool for adjusting nuts and bolts.

   **Usage:** Commonwealth; Ireland; UK

   **Example:** Pass me that spanner, Jake; there's just one more bolt to screw in.

   **Source synonyms:** wrench

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A hand tool for adjusting nuts and bolts.
   1. Any of certain types of such a tool that have a pin or hook to engage the driven object.

      **Usage:** Canada; Commonwealth; Ireland; UK; US

      **Example:** The groove in this toolholder is what the spanner grabs onto when you are changing the tool.

      **Source synonyms:** wrench

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. One who, or that which, spans.

   **Usage:** rare

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A (usually sparse) graph whose shortest path distances approximate those in a dense graph or other metric space.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** graph-theory; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A hand tool shaped like a small crank handle, for winding the spring of a wheel lock on a musket.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; government; military; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; politics; tools; war; weaponry

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A device in early steam engines for moving the valves for the alternate admission and shutting off of the steam.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A problem, dilemma or obstacle; something unexpected or troublesome (in the phrase spanner in the works)

   **Usage:** Ireland; UK

   **Example:** Halfway through the production of Macbeth, the director found that the stage was smaller than he expected. This really threw a spanner in the works.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A stupid or unintelligent person; one prone to making mistakes, especially in language.

   **Usage:** Ireland; UK; derogatory; mildly

   **Example:** You spanner, Rodney! I wanted some time, not a bunch of thyme!
