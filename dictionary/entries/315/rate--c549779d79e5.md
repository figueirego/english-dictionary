<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The worth of something; value.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The proportional relationship between one amount, value etc. and another.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** At the height of his powers, he was producing pictures at the rate of four a year.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

3. Speed.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The car was speeding down here at a hell of a rate.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The relative speed of change or progress.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The rate of production at the factory is skyrocketing.

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The price of (an individual) thing; cost.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** He asked quite a rate to take me to the airport.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A set price or charge for all examples of a given case, commodity, service etc.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Postal rates here are low.

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A wage calculated in relation to a unit of time.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** We pay an hourly rate of between $10 – $15 per hour depending on qualifications and experience.

**Source sense R1.S8**
