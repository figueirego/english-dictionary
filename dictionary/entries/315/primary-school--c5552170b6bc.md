**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A formal, obligatory kind of school that typically begins with nursery school or first grade and ends at fifth or sixth grade.

   **Usage:** Australia; India; New-Zealand; Philippines; UK; countable; sometimes; uncountable

   **Example:** The child's mother was tearful as he boarded the bus for his first day of primary school.
