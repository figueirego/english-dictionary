### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A round, colourful sea cucumber of the genera Paracucumaria and Paracolochirus, found primarily in Indo-Pacific waters.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A sea urchin.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The fruit of a West Indian palm (Manicaria plukenetii ⇒ ) Manicaria saccifera, often found floating in the sea.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A troolie palm; any member of the species Syzygium grande of trees, that can reach 30 m, found along the coastal mountains of Southeast Asia or its fruit.
