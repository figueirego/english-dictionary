## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A method of grafting by cutting the ends of the scion and stock completely across and obliquely, in such a manner that the sections are of the same shape, then lapping the ends so that the one cut surface exactly fits the other, and securing them by tying or otherwise.

   **Usage:** uncountable
