<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of, characterized by, or pertaining to murder or murderers.

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of a person&#58; intending or likely to commit murder; bloodthirsty, homicidal.

   **Example:** murderous behaviour

   **Example:** She gave me a murderous look.

   **Source synonyms:** assassinous (obsolete)

   **Source antonyms:** nonmurderous; unmurderous
