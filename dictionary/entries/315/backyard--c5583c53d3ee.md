### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A yard to the rear of a house or similar residence.

   **Usage:** Australia; Canada; New-Zealand; US

   **Source antonyms:** front yard; forecourt

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person's neighborhood, or an area nearby to a person's usual residence or place of work and where the person is likely to go.

   **Usage:** Australia; Canada; New-Zealand; US; colloquial

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An area nearby to a country or other jurisdiction's legal boundaries, particularly an area in which the country feels it has an interest.

   **Usage:** Australia; Canada; New-Zealand; US; colloquial
