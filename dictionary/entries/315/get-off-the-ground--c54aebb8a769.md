### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To succeed or begin to succeed.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** His big plans to get rich never quite seemed to get off the ground.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To make (something) succeed.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** He couldn't get George's career off the ground.
