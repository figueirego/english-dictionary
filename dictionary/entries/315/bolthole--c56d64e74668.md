### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A hole in an animal’s den, or through a wall or fence, used for escape or emergency exit, i.e., a hole the animal may bolt through.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A hidden place of emergency refuge.

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A second home, etc., where one can seek an escape or refuge from the stresses of everyday life.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** a bolthole in the Dordogne
