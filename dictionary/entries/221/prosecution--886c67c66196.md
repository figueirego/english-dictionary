### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of prosecuting a scheme or endeavor.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The prosecution of the war fell to Winston Churchill.

   **Example:** Many apartheid perpetrators escaped prosecution for their persecution of black Africans and political dissidents.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The institution of legal proceedings (particularly criminal) against a person.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The prosecuting party.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. In many countries, a legal body and institution, usually part of the state apparatus, empowered to perform prosecution. Prosecutor's Office. See Prosecutor.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Backed by public outrage, the prosecution requested the death penalty to be imposed on the murderer.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law
