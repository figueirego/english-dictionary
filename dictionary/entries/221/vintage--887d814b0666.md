### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Of or relating to a vintage, or to wine identified by a specific vintage.

   **Usage:** attributive

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Having an enduring appeal; high-quality.

   **Usage:** attributive

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. Classic, or old enough to be recognizably outdated but not old enough to be antique (such as watches, video or computer games from the 1980s or 1990s, old magazines, etc.).
   1. built between the years 1919 and (usually) 1930 (or sometimes 1919 to 1925 in the USA).

      **Usage:** attributive; Of a motor car
