### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A morpheme or element of a morpheme that contains no consonants or vowels, but only tone. It cannot be pronounced by itself, but affects the tones of neighbouring morphemes.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; phonology; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A phonemic tone which is not associated to a syllable and is only pronounced in its effect on neighboring tones, such as by causing downstep.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; phonology; sciences
