### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To take upon oneself; to start, to embark on.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** They undertook the new venture, not sure where it would lead.

   **Example:** They undertook cleaning the house room by room.

   **Source synonyms:** tackle; take on; set about

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To commit oneself (to an obligation, activity etc.).

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** He undertook to exercise more in future.

   **Example:** They undertook to clean the house room by room.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To pass a slower moving vehicle on the curbside rather than on the side closest to oncoming traffic.

   **Usage:** UK; informal

   **Source antonyms:** overtake

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To pledge; to assert, assure; to dare say.

   **Usage:** archaic; intransitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To take by trickery; to trap, to seize upon.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To assume, as a character; to take on.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To engage with; to attack, take on in a fight.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To have knowledge of; to hear.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To have or take charge of.

   **Usage:** obsolete
