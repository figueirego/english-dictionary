### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A member of the family Coronaviridae, comprising viruses which infect animals and human beings, and the genome of which consists of a single strand of RNA.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; microbiology; natural-sciences; virology

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A member of the family Coronaviridae, comprising viruses which infect animals and human beings, and the genome of which consists of a single strand of RNA.
   1. SARS-CoV-2, the specific coronavirus that causes the infectious disease COVID-19.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable; often the intended sense

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; microbiology; natural-sciences; virology

      **Source context (verbatim):** since 2020
