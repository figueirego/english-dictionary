## Noun

### Forms

- Brothers — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Title of respect for an adult male member of a religious or fraternal order.

   **Example:** At the monastery, Brother Stephen supervises the kitchen.

   **Example:** Please welcome Brother Smith as he moves from his former congregation to his new congregation.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Title of respect for an adult male member of a fraternal/sororal organization, or comrade in a movement, or even a stranger using fictive kin.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A title used to personify or respectfully refer to concepts or animals.

   **Example:** The Native American had a kinship with nature, even referring to Mother Earth and Brother Bear.
