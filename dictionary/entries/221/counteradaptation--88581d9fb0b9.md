### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. adaptation that counters a change to an organism's environment
   1. The development of tolerance; the adaptation of the body's homeostatic mechanisms to counteract a drug effect.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; pharmacology; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. adaptation that counters a change to an organism's environment
   1. The evolutionary adaptation of one species to adaptive changes by another.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. adaptation that counters a change to an organism's environment
   1. The distortion of one sensory modality that accompanies adaptation to alterations in a perceptual cues from a related modality.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable
