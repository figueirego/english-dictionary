### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Synonym of arthrodesis, the artificial fusion of a joint between two or more bones.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually; orthopedics

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences; surgery

   **Source synonyms:** arthrodesis (synonym; synonym-of)

   **Usage:** rhetoric; uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** grammar; human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** syndeton (synonym; synonym-of)
