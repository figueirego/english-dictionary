## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A coastal town in northwestern Albania that is a growing tourist destination due to its beaches and resort accommodations, located 5-6 km northwest of the city of Lezhë by the Adriatic Sea below a mountain side; the town was the site of the ancient harbour of Nymphaeum and is now home to one of Albania's ports of entry; it is the seat of its eponymous municipal unit.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A municipal unit of the municipality of Lezhë in the county of Lezhë, Albania.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A former municipality of the county of Lezhë, Albania.
