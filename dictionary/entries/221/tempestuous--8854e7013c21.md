### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of, pertaining to, or resembling, a tempest; also, of a place&#58; frequently experiencing tempests; (very) stormy.

   **Source synonyms:** breme; nimbose; procellous

   **Source antonyms:** untempestuous

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Characterized by disorderly, frenetic, or violent activity; stormy, tumultuous, turbulent; also, of a person, their behaviour or nature, etc.&#58; characterized by bouts of bad temper or sudden changes of mood; impetuous, stormy, temperamental.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** After their tempestuous argument, they did not speak to each other for weeks.

   **Source antonyms:** untempestuous
