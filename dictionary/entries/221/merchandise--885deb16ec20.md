### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Goods which are or were offered or intended for sale.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** Good business depends on having good merchandise.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Commercial goods connected (branded) with an entity such as a team, band, company, charity, work of fiction, festival, or meme. (Commonly shortened to merch.)

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A commodity offered for sale; an article of commerce; a kind of merchandise.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; usually

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The act or business of trading; trade; traffic.

   **Usage:** archaic; uncountable; usually

- merchandised — participle; past
- merchandised — past

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To engage in trade; to carry on commerce.

   **Usage:** archaic; intransitive

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To engage in in-store promotion of the sale of goods, as by display and arrangement of goods.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** He started his career merchandising in a small clothing store chain.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To engage in the trade of.

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To engage in in-store promotion of the sale of.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He got hired to merchandise some new sporting goods lines.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To promote as if for sale.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The record companies don't get as good a return on merchandising artists under contract.
