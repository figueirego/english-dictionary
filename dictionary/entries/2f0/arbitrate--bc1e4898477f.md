### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make a judgment (on a dispute) as an arbitrator or arbiter

   **Example:** to arbitrate a disputed case

   **Source synonyms:** adjudge; deem; rule on; adjudge; adjudicate; arbitrate; deem (obsolete); doom (obsolete); hear; judge; rule on; settle; try

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To assign an arbitrary value to, or otherwise determine arbitrarily.

   **Example:** We wish to show f is continuous. Arbitrate epsilon greater than zero...
