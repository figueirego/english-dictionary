### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To supply with oxygen or air.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Blood is aerated in the lungs.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To bubble or sparge with a gas, especially oxygen or air.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** Carbon dioxide aerated the drink and made it fizzy.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To incorporate a gas, especially oxygen or air, into a solid or semisolid material.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The mousse was aerated by beating rapidly.
