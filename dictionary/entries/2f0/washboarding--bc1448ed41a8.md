## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The appearance of ripples or bumps on a dirt or gravel road, caused by wear from traffic, erosion, or poor grading.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source synonyms:** corrugation

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A streaky appearance on solid patches of color printed on corrugated materials, resulting from the uneven pressure exerted due to corrugations.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An unexplained behavior in which bees move across the surface of their hive with a rocking motion.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually; bees
