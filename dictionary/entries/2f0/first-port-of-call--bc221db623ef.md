### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The first port that a vessel calls in at after the start of a voyage

   **Example:** After leaving from Southampton, our first port of call will be Gibraltar.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The first place to go to start a process.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** When you move to a new country, your first port of call is often the local police.

   **Example:** To find the meaning of a word, your first port of call should be a decent dictionary.
