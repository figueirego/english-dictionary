## Noun

### Forms

- midspheres — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A middle sphere or region.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The sphere which, if it exists, is tangent to every edge of a given polyhedron and with respect to which the polyhedron's vertices are the inversion poles of the planes of the faces of its dual polyhedron (and vice versa).

   **Usage:** solid geometry

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The sphere, between two given spheres, by which each of the latter may be inverted into the other.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A supposed realm between earth and heaven.

   **Usage:** Vedic; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; lifestyle; mysticism; mythology; philosophy; religion; sciences
