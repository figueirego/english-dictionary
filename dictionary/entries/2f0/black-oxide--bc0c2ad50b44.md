### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of several coatings for steels or copper; some are oxides (mainly iron(II,III) oxide), some are nitrites or nitrates, and some are selenides; all are black; the main purposes are the antireflective and cosmetic effects; the anticorrosion effects are small.

   **Usage:** countable; idiomatic; uncountable

   **Example:** hot black oxide

   **Example:** cold black oxide

   **Source synonyms:** blackening

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; any oxide that is black; often, iron(II,III) oxide.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
