### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A railway track on a steep slope in a zigzag formation, in which a train travels in a reverse direction at each switch.

   **Usage:** attributive; figuratively; often

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** rail-transport; railways; transport

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A railway track on which there are steep ascents and descents, a train moving partially or fully by the force of gravity using the momentum generated when descending to travel up an ascending part of the track; especially (UK, dated), such a track built for fun rides at an amusement park; a type of rollercoaster.

   **Usage:** attributive; figuratively; often

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** rail-transport; railways; transport

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A flight path consisting of a series of steep ascents and descents, generally flown as a stunt.

   **Usage:** attributive; broadly; figuratively; often

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** aeronautics; aerospace; aviation; business; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A path or road having a series of steep ascents and descents.

   **Usage:** UK; attributive; broadly; figuratively; often

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A sharp bend in a path or road which causes a traveller to almost reverse their direction of travel, especially one of a series of such bends on an incline; a hairpin bend; also a path or road having such a series of bends.

   **Usage:** Canada; US; attributive; broadly; figuratively; often

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** road; transport
