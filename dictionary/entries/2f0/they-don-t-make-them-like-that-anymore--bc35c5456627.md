### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Expressing such notions as that the person referred to is excellent and that owing to the decline of society, such people have become rarer; the old school was better; the youth today lack something that previous generations had more of; all the good ones are taken; and so on.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** A&#58; What a guardian angel that guy was to so many people. B&#58; Yup. They don't make them like that anymore.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a person

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see they, don't, make, them, like, that, anymore&#58; the model of vehicle, machine, or device exemplified by the instance under discussion is no longer manufactured.
