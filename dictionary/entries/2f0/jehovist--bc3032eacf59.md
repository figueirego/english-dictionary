## Noun · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /d͡ʒəˈhəʊvɪst/ — source labels: UK
- IPA: /d͡ʒəˈhoʊvɪst/ — source labels: US

### Forms

- Jehovists — plural
- Yahwist — alternative
- Yahwehist — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. One who maintains that the vowel points of the word Jehovah, in Hebrew, are the proper vowels of that word; opposed to adonist.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. A member of the Jehovah's Witnesses.

   **Usage:** derogatory

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. Anyone who uses the word "Jehovah" as the name of God in worship.
