### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A device that directs and controls energy, often in the form of movement or electricity, to produce a certain effect.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A vehicle operated mechanically, such as an automobile or an airplane.

   **Usage:** dated

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An answering machine or, by extension, voice mail.

   **Usage:** abbreviation

   **Example:** I called you earlier, but all I got was the machine.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** communications; electrical-engineering; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; telecommunications; telephony

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A computer.

   **Example:** Game developers assume they're pushing the limits of the machine.

   **Example:** He refuses to turn off his Linux machine.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A person or organisation that seemingly acts like a machine, being particularly efficient, single-minded, or unemotional.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** Bruce Campbell was a "demon-killing machine" because he made quick work of killing demons.

   **Example:** The government has become a money-making machine.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Especially, the group that controls a political or similar organization; a combination of persons acting together for a common purpose, with the agencies which they use.

**Source sense R1.S7**

   **Usage:** euphemistic; obsolete
