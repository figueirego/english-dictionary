### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who will exchange currency of one type for another for a fee or percentage.

   **Example:** the money changers in the temple

   **Example:** The money changer gave me rupees for my dollars.

   **Source synonyms:** bureau de change; currency exchange

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

2. A portable device for dispensing change.
