**Source sense R2.S1**

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** arpeggiate

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**
