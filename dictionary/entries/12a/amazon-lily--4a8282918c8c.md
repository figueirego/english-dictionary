### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A bulbous plant, Eucharis amazonica, of the family Amaryllidaceae, having tight, compact rosettes of glossy green leaves and tall spikes of white, fragrant flowers.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A giant water lily, Victoria amazonica, with circular leaves of up to 8 feet in diameter having large white female flowers that bloom at night, and change to pink male ones the next night.
