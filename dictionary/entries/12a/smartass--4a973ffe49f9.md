### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An insolent person, especially one who makes snide remarks.

   **Usage:** derogatory; mildly; slang; vulgar

   **Example:** Lots of luck, smartass.

   **Source synonyms:** smart aleck

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Synonym of know-it-all, an obnoxious clever person or a person making obnoxious efforts to seem clever by acting or behaving as if they know everything.

   **Usage:** mildly; vulgar

   **Source synonyms:** know-it-all (synonym; synonym-of)
