### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To be extremely obvious to (someone), to be impossible for (someone) to miss and ignore.

   **Usage:** colloquial; idiomatic

   **Example:** These new recruits are terrible soldiers. They wouldn't know what to do with an enemy if one came up and bit them.

   **Source context (verbatim):** in the negative

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** colloquial; idiomatic

   **Example:** You really think you can convince the committee? Come on! You wouldn't know charisma if it came up and bit you.
