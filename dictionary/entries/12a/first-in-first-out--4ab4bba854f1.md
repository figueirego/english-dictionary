**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A method of inventory accounting that values items withdrawn from inventory at the cost of the oldest item assumed to remain in inventory.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** accounting; business; finance

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** management
