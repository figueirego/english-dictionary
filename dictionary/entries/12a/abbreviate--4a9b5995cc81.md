### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To shorten by omitting parts or details.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To speak or write in a brief manner.

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To make shorter; to shorten (in time); to abridge; to shorten by ending sooner than planned.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To reduce a word or phrase by means of contraction or omission to a shorter recognizable form.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Bovine spongiform encephalopathy is more commonly known by its abbreviated form BSE.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To reduce to lower terms, as a fraction.

   **Usage:** transitive
