### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Difficult or impossible to break and therefore able to withstand rough usage.

   **Example:** unbreakable comb

   **Source synonyms:** infrangible

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Resilient.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** the unbreakable spirit of the rebels

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Not able to be broken in.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a horse

### Related words

**Source antonyms:** breakable; fragile
