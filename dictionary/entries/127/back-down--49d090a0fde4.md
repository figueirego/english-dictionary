### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To take a less aggressive position in a conflict than one previously had, or has planned to have.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** I was about to sue them, but I had to back down.

   **Example:** The boys had planned to fight during recess, but backed down after the principal found out.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To make (someone) take a less aggressive position.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To withdraw from a commitment or position; back out.

   **Usage:** idiomatic
