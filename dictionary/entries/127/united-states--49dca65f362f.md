### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A country in North America, also including Hawaii in Oceania; in full, United States of America.

   **Usage:** singular; formerly also plural

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A country in North America, also including Hawaii in Oceania; in full, United States of America.
   1. The American federal government.

      **Usage:** broadly; singular; formerly also plural

      **Example:** The United States effectively nationalized Fannie and Freddie during the 2008 financial crisis to prevent their collapse.

      **Example:** The United States is causing problems for herself with this policy.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The collection of individual states of the United States of America.

   **Usage:** in-plural

   **Example:** life in these United States

   **Example:** The Judicial power of the United States shall not be construed to extend to any suit in law or equity, commenced or prosecuted against one of the United States by Citizens of another State, or by Citizens or Subjects of any Foreign State. (Eleventh Amendment to the United States Constitution, 1794)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A federal nation consisting of several states, whether actual, historical or hypothetical.

   **Usage:** name

   **Example:** the Republic of the United States of Brazil (the First Brazilian Republic)

   **Example:** the United States of Mexico (the United Mexican States)

   **Example:** the United States of Europe

   **Example:** the United States of the Ionian Islands
