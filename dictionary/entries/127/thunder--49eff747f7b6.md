### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The loud rumbling, cracking, or crashing sound caused by expansion of rapidly heated air around a lightning bolt.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Thunder is preceded by lightning.

   **Example:** Thunder cracked overhead.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A deep, rumbling noise resembling thunder.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Off in the distance, he heard the thunder of hoofbeats, signalling a stampede.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An alarming or startling threat or denunciation.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

4. The discharge of electricity; a thunderbolt.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To produce thunder; to sound, rattle, or roar, as a discharge of atmospheric electricity.

   **Usage:** impersonal

   **Example:** It thundered continuously.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To make a noise like thunder.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The train thundered along the tracks.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To (make something) move very fast (with loud noise).

   **Usage:** ergative

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To say (something) with a loud, threatening voice.

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** "Get back to work at once!", he thundered.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To produce something with incredible power.
