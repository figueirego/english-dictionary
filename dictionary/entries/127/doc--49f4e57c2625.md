### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A nickname for a medical doctor, a person with an advanced educational degree, or a learned person.

   **Example:** I'm not feeling too well, Doc.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A common form of address for combat medics, especially naval hospital corpsmen.

   **Usage:** US; slang

   **Example:** Doc was awarded a Navy Cross after she ran through a minefield to get to an injured marine.
