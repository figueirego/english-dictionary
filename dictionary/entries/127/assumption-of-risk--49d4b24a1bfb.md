## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An affirmative defense in tort law which bars a plaintiff's right to recovery against a tortfeasor if the defendant can demonstrate that the plaintiff assumed risks inherent to the dangerous activity in which the plaintiff was participating.

   **Usage:** uncountable
