### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any member of any species of genus Acer, characterized by (usually) palmate, five-lobed leaves and winged seeds, and often grown for syrup or wood

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Russian maple

   **Example:** They have a large maple in their yard.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any member of any species of genus Acer, characterized by (usually) palmate, five-lobed leaves and winged seeds, and often grown for syrup or wood
   1. A small tree (Acer campestre), known as the common maple, field maple, or hedge maple, which has palmate leaves with five lobes and winged seeds, and is chiefly native to East Asia, Europe, North Africa, and North America.

      **Usage:** UK; countable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any member of any species of genus Acer, characterized by (usually) palmate, five-lobed leaves and winged seeds, and often grown for syrup or wood
   1. Often preceded by a descriptive word&#58; a tree of the genus Acer; especially the sugar maple (Acer saccharum) native to North America which has brightly colored autumn foliage and is the primary source of maple syrup.

      **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Chiefly preceded by a descriptive word&#58; a tree of a genus other than Acer which has an appearance or wood similar to an Acer tree.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** Queensland maple

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Wood of an Acer tree, valued for its attractive appearance and hardness; also, similar wood from a tree of a genus other than Acer; (countable) a particular type of such wood.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** This table is made of maple and also has some fruitwood inlays.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The light-brown color of the wood of a maple tree (etymology 1, noun sense 3).

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The flavor of maple syrup made from the sap of a maple tree, or an imitation of that flavor.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** What flavour is your ice-cream? Mine’s maple—it’s delicious.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Short for maple pea (“the field pea (Pisum sativum subsp. arvense) which has brown, speckled seeds used dry rather than fresh; a seed of this plant”).

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; countable

   **Alternative form of:** maple pea
