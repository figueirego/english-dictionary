### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To deceive or delude (using guile).

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To charm, delight or captivate.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I will never touch The Orb, even though its mysterious glow seduces and beguiles.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To cause (time) to seem to pass quickly, by way of pleasant diversion.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** We beguiled the hours away.
