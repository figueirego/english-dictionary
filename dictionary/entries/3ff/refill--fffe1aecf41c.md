### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. That which serves to refill or replace something.
   1. An additional helping of food or drink at reduced cost.

      **Example:** Today it is $2 for a coffee, with free refills throughout the day.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. That which serves to refill or replace something.
   1. Refuelling, fuel to refill an empty fuel tank (of a vehicle or machine) or other fuel container.

      **Example:** Tank's empty. We need to go for a refill.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. That which serves to refill or replace something.
   1. A product intended to replace the used contents of a container or the exhausted supply of a device.

      **Example:** We're cutting back. No new printers or pens, just refills.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. That which serves to refill or replace something.
   1. A repeat of a prescription.

      **Usage:** pharmacy

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An act or process of refilling.

   **Example:** capillary refill
