### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A rural settlement in Novotroitske settlement hromada, Henichesk Raion, Kherson Oblast, Ukraine, founded in 1816.
   1. A former raion of Kherson Oblast, Ukrainian Soviet Socialist Republic, established in February 1935, disestablished in December 1962.
