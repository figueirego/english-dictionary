### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To stunt, inhibit (progress, ideas, etc.) or make dull and uninteresting, especially through routine that is overly restrictive or limiting.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Bureaucracy and over-regulation have stultified the economy.

   **Source synonyms:** inhibit; impair; dull

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To make useless or worthless.

   **Usage:** dated; transitive

   **Example:** His business plan was stultified by new technologies.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To cause to appear foolish; to deprive of strength; to stupefy.

   **Usage:** dated; transitive

   **Example:** The politicians continued to stultify themselves.

   **Source synonyms:** humiliate

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To prove to be of unsound mind or demonstrate someone's incompetence.

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive
