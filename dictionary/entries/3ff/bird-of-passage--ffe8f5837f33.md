### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A migratory bird, especially a passage migrant (“a bird that stops somewhere for a short time during a seasonal migration”).

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences; ornithology

   **Source synonyms:** migrant

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Source synonyms:** bird of passage; gadabout; itinerant; peregrinator; rambler; rover; traveller; voyager; wanderer; wayfarer
