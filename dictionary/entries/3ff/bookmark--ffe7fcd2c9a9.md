### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A strip of material used to mark a place in a book.

   **Example:** He placed a bookmark on page 234, which had an interesting footnote from the author.

   **Source synonyms:** bookmarker

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
