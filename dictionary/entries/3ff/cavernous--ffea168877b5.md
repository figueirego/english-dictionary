**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Source synonyms:** cavelike

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Resembling a cavern in size, shape, or atmosphere.
   1. Giving the impression of vast, dark depths.

      **Source synonyms:** cavelike
