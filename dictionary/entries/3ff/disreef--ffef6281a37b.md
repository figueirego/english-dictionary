**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To allow (a reefed parachute) to fully open.

   **Usage:** space flight

   **Example:** Once the SRBs decelerate following initial main-parachute deployment, the parachutes disreef in stages to gradually slow the boosters to a safe splashdown speed.
