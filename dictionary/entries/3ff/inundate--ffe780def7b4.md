### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To cover with large amounts of water; to flood.

   **Example:** The Dutch would sometimes inundate the land to hinder the Spanish army.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To overwhelm.

   **Example:** The agency was inundated with phone calls.
