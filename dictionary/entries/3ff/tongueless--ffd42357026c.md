## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having no tongue.
   1. Having no tongue whether through its normal nature, because of birth defect, or because of amputation.

      **Usage:** not-comparable

      **Example:** tongueless amphibians

      **Source context (verbatim):** of an animal or person

      **Source antonyms:** tongued; aglossal

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Having no tongue.
   1. Having no tongue because hitched in another way.

      **Usage:** not-comparable

      **Example:** tongueless plow

      **Source context (verbatim):** of wagons and farm implements

      **Source antonyms:** tongued

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Lacking speech; mute.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Making no sound; silent, speechless.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Expressed without speech; wordless, unspoken.

   **Usage:** not-comparable
