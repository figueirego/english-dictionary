## Adverb

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /pɹɪˈsɪ.pɪ.təs.li/ — source labels: US

### Forms

- more precipitously — comparative
- most precipitously — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In a precipitous manner.
   1. Abruptly, perilously, sharply, steeply, severely.

      **Example:** In the west of Ireland, the Cliffs of Moher drop precipitously into the North Atlantic.

      **Source context (verbatim):** of changes within physical environments

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In a precipitous manner.
   1. Abruptly; with no notice or ceremony&#58; in a sudden or hasty manner, instantly, quickly.

      **Example:** Shortly after the conversation turned to the results of the recent election, John silently stood from the table, and without explanation, precipitously walked out on the dinner party.

      **Source context (verbatim):** of events in general

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. In a precipitous manner.
   1. Done with a lack of proper decorum, or without adequate thought, care or concern for consequences.

      **Example:** In signing that contract without reading all of the provisions, I have performed what was a legal requirement most precipitously, leading me to wish that I had taken more care.

      **Example:** Look here son, in stealing that candy bar from Belsito's market, you have acted precipitously in the extreme; now you will have the unenviable responsibility of telling Mr. Belsito what you have done, and of expressing your heartfelt regret.

      **Source context (verbatim):** of actions involving intentionality

      **Source synonyms:** carelessly; impulsively; rashly; rash
