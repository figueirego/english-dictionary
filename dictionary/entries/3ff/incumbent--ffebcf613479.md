<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Chiefly followed by on or upon&#58; leaning, or lying, reclining, or resting, on something else.

   **Source synonyms:** recumbent

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Chiefly followed by on or upon&#58; leaning, or lying, reclining, or resting, on something else.
   1. Of an anther&#58; lying on the inner side of the filament; also, of a cotyledon&#58; having its back lying against the radicle.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences

      **Source synonyms:** recumbent; notorhizal

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Chiefly followed by on or upon&#58; leaning, or lying, reclining, or resting, on something else.
   1. Of a body part such as a hair, spine, or wing&#58; bent downwards or otherwise positioned so that it, or part of it, rests on or touches something else; specifically (ornithology), of the hind toe of a bird&#58; fully resting on a support.

      **Example:** the incumbent toe of a bird

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences; zoology

      **Source synonyms:** recumbent

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Being the current holder of an office or a title; specifically (Christianity, obsolete), of an ecclesiastical benefice.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** If the incumbent senator dies, he is replaced by a person appointed by the governor.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** Proper behaviour is incumbent on all holders of positions of trust.
