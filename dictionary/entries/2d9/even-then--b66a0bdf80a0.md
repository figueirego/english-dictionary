## Adverb

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Still, even at that time; then as well as beforehand.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Regardless of the circumstances (often ones that have just been stated).

   **Usage:** contrastive; idiomatic; not-comparable

   **Example:** I'm used to being disappointed, and even then, this is underwhelming.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. At that very moment.

   **Usage:** formal; not-comparable

   **Source synonyms:** just then
