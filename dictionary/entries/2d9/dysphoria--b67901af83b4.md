### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A state of feeling unwell or unhappy; a feeling of emotional and mental discomfort and suffering from restlessness, malaise, depression, or anxiety.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** body dysphoria

   **Example:** premenstrual dysphoria

   **Example:** weight dysphoria

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Ellipsis of gender dysphoria.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; countable; ellipsis; uncountable

   **Example:** height dysphoria

   **Example:** bottom dysphoria

   **Example:** voice dysphoria

   **Alternative form of:** [gender dysphoria](../1ed/gender-dysphoria--7b515b28b1ce.md)
