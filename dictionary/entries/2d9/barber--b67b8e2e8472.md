**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person whose profession is cutting the hair and beards of usually male customers.

   **Source synonyms:** haircutter; hairdresser; hairstylist; tonsor (obsolete); cutbeard (obsolete)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

<a id="r1-s3"></a>
