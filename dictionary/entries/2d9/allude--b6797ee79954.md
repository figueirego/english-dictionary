### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To refer to something indirectly or by suggestion; to invoke it by implication rather than mention.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** First, she alluded to her husband as "the man at home looking after the children". Then she referred to him as "my George".
