## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Mud or silt that becomes heavily saturated with water, causing it to behave like a liquid; similar to quicksand, it often appears solid when undisturbed but liquefies under weight, causing anything stepping on it to sink.

   **Usage:** uncountable
