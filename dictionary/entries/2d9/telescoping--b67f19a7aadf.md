### Senses

**Source sense R3.S1**

1. The act of extending or contracting in the manner of a telescope.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r3-s2"></a>

**Source sense R3.S2**

2. Extending limited data to make up for gaps

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r3-s3"></a>

3. A telescopic action.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r3-s4"></a>

**Source sense R3.S4**

4. The temporal displacement of an event in which people perceive recent events as being more remote than they are and distant events as being more recent than they are.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
