## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The point at which gravitational force (or other universally and uniformly acting force) may be supposed to act on a given rigid, uniformly dense body; the centre of gravity or centre of mass.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; geometry; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics; sciences

   **Source context (verbatim):** of an object or a geometrical figure

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The point of intersection of the three medians of a given triangle; the point whose (Cartesian) coordinates are the arithmetic mean of the coordinates of the three vertices.

   **Usage:** specifically

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a triangle

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. the point whose (Cartesian) coordinates are the arithmetic mean of the coordinates of a given finite set of points.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a finite set of points

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An analogue of the centre of gravity of a nonuniform body in which local density is replaced by a specified function (which can take negative values) and the place of the body's shape is taken by the function's domain.

   **Example:** The centroid of an arbitrary function f is given by \#92;frac\#123;\#92;intxf(x)dx\#125;\#123;\#92;intf(x)dx\#125;, where the integrals are calculated over the domain of f.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematical-analysis; mathematics; sciences

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a function

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. the arithmetic mean (alternatively, median) position of a cluster of points in a coordinate system based on some application-dependent measure of distance.

   **Usage:** cluster analysis

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences; statistics

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a cluster of points

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Given a tree of n nodes, either (1) a unique node whose removal would split the tree into subtrees of fewer than n/2 nodes, or (2) either of a pair of adjacent nodes such that removal of the edge connecting them would split the tree into two subtrees of exactly n/2 nodes.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** graph-theory; mathematics; sciences

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a tree
