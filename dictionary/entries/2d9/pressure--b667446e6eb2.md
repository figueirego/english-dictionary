### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A pressing; a force applied to a surface.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Apply pressure to the wound to stop the bleeding.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The amount of force that is applied over a given area divided by the size of this area; force per unit area.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An amount of pressure.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A contrasting force or impulse of any kind.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** the pressure of poverty; the pressure of taxes; the pressure of motives on the mind; the pressure of civilization.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Distress.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** She has felt pressure lately because her boss expects her to get the job done by the first.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Urgency.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** the pressure of business

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Ellipsis of blood pressure.

   **Usage:** India; abbreviation; alt-of; countable; ellipsis; uncountable
