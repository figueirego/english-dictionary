## Noun

### Forms

- warm-ups — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of exercising or stretching in preparation for strenuous activity.

   **Example:** If you don't do your warm-ups properly you have a greater chance of injury.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any act of preparation for a performance.

   **Example:** Drivers are allowed 5 minutes for warm-up before the race starts.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A period of time allocated for performing warm-ups.

   **Example:** Keep a sharp eye on the referee during warm-up to check out his mood.
