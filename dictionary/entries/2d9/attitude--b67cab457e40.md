<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The position of the body or way of carrying oneself.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The ballet dancer walked with a graceful attitude.

   **Source synonyms:** posture; stance

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A disposition or state of mind.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

   **Example:** Don't give me your negative attitude.

   **Example:** You've got a nice attitude today.

   **Source synonyms:** posture; spirit; outlook

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Unpleasant behavior.

   **Usage:** Canada; Philippines; UK; US; countable; informal; uncountable

   **Example:** He doesn't take attitude from anybody.

   **Example:** I asked the waiter for a clean fork and all I got was attitude.

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The orientation of a vehicle or other object relative to the horizon, direction of motion, other objects, etc.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The airliner had to land with a nose-up attitude after the incident.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** aeronautics; aerospace; business; engineering; natural-sciences; nautical; physical-sciences; transport

   **Source synonyms:** trim; orientation
