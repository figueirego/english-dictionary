<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A system of ethics based on the premise that something's value may be measured by its usefulness.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; philosophy; sciences
