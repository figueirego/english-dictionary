## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The time when a tournament is paused due to time constraints, when a player's next move has been written down held until the tournament resumes, during which the player can examine possible play outcomes.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** board-games; chess; games

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The time when a deferred operation eventually takes place.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computer; computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The time when someone or something is not actively making progress but will resume again later.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
