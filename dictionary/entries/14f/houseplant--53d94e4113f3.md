### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A plant that is grown indoors in places such as a house or office for decorative purposes.

   **Example:** Herbert thought that adding some houseplants would brighten up his desk.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A variety of plant that is especially suited to such cultivation, or that is frequently grown in such settings.

   **Example:** "I always thought a begonia was a houseplant!" exclaimed Sue, staring at the towering tropical specimen.
