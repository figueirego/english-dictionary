## Prep\_phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In the process of doing something considered criminal or otherwise wrong; used to emphasize the eye-witness evidence.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** She denied it but she had been spotted in the act.

   **Example:** He was caught in the act in the store after hours.

   **Example:** He was caught in the act of climbing out the window.
