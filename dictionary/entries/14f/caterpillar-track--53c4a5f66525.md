### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A generic trademark name for a continuous track in the form of a steel or rubber belt fitted around the wheels of crawlers, bulldozers, excavators, loaders, backhoes, tanks and similar off-road vehicles in such a manner as to spread the load on the ground surface and thereby improve traction and reduce sinking into soft surfaces.

   **Source synonyms:** continuous track; track; crawler
