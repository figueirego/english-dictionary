### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In due season; at an opportune or fitting time.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Varying with the season.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. In a manner appropriate to the season.

   **Example:** The weather has become seasonably cooler lately.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**
