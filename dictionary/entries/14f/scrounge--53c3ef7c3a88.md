### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To hunt about, especially for something of nominal value; to scavenge or glean.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** scrounge for food

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To obtain something of moderate or inconsequential value from another.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** As long as he's got someone who'll let him scrounge off them, he'll never settle down and get a full-time job.
