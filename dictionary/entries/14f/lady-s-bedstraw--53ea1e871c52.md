### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A herbaceous perennial flowering plant, Galium verum, used in the Middle Ages for bedding and for making red dye.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source synonyms:** yellow bedstraw; Our Lady's bedstraw

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
