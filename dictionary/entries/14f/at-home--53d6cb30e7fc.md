## Prep\_phrase · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see at, home. In one's place of residence.

   **Example:** Sorry sir, I left my homework at home.

   **Example:** "Where's David?" "He stayed at home to fix the washing machine."

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. At ease; comfortable.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I feel at home around my girlfriend's family.

   **Example:** I'm right at home in my new university.

   **Example:** He's quite at home discussing French literature.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. In the home of one's parents.

   **Example:** I can't believe it&#58; she's 28 and still lives at home.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. In a private residence as opposed to a nursing home.

   **Example:** He is really elderly and can still live at home.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Playing at its usual venue, playing as the home team.

   **Example:** The team has won three-quarters of its games at home, but less than half of away games.

   **Example:** The team has a 6–2 record at home.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a team

   **Source antonyms:** away; away from home; on the road

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Available or willing to receive visitors.

   **Usage:** dated
