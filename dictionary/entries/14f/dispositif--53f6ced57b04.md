### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A part of a document that communicates the decisions taken by some organization or nation on a particular issue.

   **Usage:** international law

   **Example:** Thereafter the court ruled unanimously in paragraph 2c of its dispositif that threat or use of force by means of nuclear weapons is contrary to the UN charter and is unlawful.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. According to Michel Foucault, the aggregate of institutional, physical and administrative mechanisms and knowledge structures that enhance and maintain the exercise of power within society (see dispositif).

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; philosophy; sciences
