### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The use of a biological structure or function for a purpose other than that for which it initially evolved.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; evolutionary theory

   **Example:** Birds initially developed wings and feathers as a means of heat regulation. The use of wings for flight is an example of exaptation.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The promotion of meaningless or redundant material so that it does new grammatical (morphosyntactic or phonological) or semantic work.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; uncountable
