### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. At the site of a job, or at the location of the customer of a product or service, as opposed to at the vendor's factory, office, or studio.

   **Example:** If we can't figure out how to diagnose this problem remotely, we're going to have to go on site again.

   **Example:** The final paint touch-ups will be performed on site, after installation.

   **Source synonyms:** on the premises; on location
