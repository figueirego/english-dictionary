### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An arrangement of two or more weapons so each can fire diagonally at something in front of a line between them.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The danger to a third party passing between two belligerents firing at one another.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The two factions were either side of the road. A civilian lorry was caught in the crossfire and became collateral damage

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The danger to a third party passing between a gunman and his target.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A heated confrontation between opposing factions.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; uncountable
