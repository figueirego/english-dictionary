### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A day marked in red on calendars; a church feast day.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A particularly significant day; a day of personal or sectarian celebration.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** Monday was a red letter day for her. She accomplished a lot and had fun doing it.

   **Example:** We saw losses for days in a row, but Black Tuesday was the worst red letter day of them all.

   **Source synonyms:** day of days; red letter date
