## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A natural or artificial egg placed in a bird's nest, to encourage the bird to lay its own eggs there.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A saving; a reserve of money.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** Over time, they accumulated a tidy nest egg and retired comfortably.
