### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The engineering science that deals with practical applications where liquid is in motion and transmits energy.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** applied hydraulics

   **Example:** The engineer specialized in fluid mechanics and hydraulics.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The hydraulic system of a vehicle or other machine.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** hydraulics system

   **Example:** The brakes work on the principle of hydraulics.
