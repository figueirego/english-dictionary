### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To cause (something) to move rapidly in opposite directions alternatingly.

   **Usage:** ergative; transitive

   **Example:** The earthquake shook the building.

   **Example:** He shook the can of soda for thirty seconds before delivering it to me, so that, when I popped it open, soda went everywhere.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To move (one's head) from side to side, especially to indicate refusal, reluctance, or disapproval.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Shaking his head, he kept repeating “No, no, no”.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To move or remove by agitating; to throw off by a jolting or vibrating motion.

   **Example:** to shake fruit down from a tree

   **Example:** The door finally shook free of its hinges.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To disturb emotionally; to shock.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Her father’s death shook her terribly.

   **Example:** He was shaken by what had happened.

   **Source synonyms:** traumatize

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To lose, evade, or get rid of (something).

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** I can’t shake the feeling that I forgot something.

   **Example:** Nothing could shake his faith.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To move from side to side.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** She shook with grief.

   **Source synonyms:** shiver; tremble

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To shake hands.

   **Usage:** intransitive; usually

   **Example:** OK, let’s shake on it.

   **Source context (verbatim):** as "shake on"

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To dance.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** She was shaking it on the dance floor.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To give a tremulous tone to; to trill.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to shake a note in music

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. To threaten to overthrow.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** The experience shook my religious belief.

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. To be agitated; to lose firmness.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive
