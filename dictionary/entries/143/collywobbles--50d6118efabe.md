### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A stomach ache or an upset stomach.

   **Usage:** UK; humorous; informal; plural; plural-only

   **Example:** Petunia’s off school today with a touch of the collywobbles.

   **Source synonyms:** bellyache; mulligrubs; tummyache

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Anxiety, fear, uneasiness.

   **Usage:** UK; broadly; humorous; informal; plural; plural-only

   **Example:** I don’t like walking through the forest at night. It gives me the collywobbles.

   **Source synonyms:** butterflies; butterflies in one’s stomach; creeps; heebie-jeebies; willies
