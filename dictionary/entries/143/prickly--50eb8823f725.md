## Adjective · Record 1

### Forms

- pricklier — comparative
- prickliest — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Covered with sharp points.

   **Example:** The prickly pear is a cactus; you have to peel it before eating it to remove the spines and the tough skin.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Easily irritated; thorny.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** He has a prickly personality. He doesn't get along with people because he is easily set off.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Difficult; complicated; hairy or thorny.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** It was a prickly situation.

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** thorny — covered with sharp points; spiny — covered with sharp points
