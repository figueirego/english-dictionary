### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A covered walkway, with open sides, that connects two buildings.

   **Example:** One of his favorite pastimes was strolling through the breezeway on warm spring afternoons.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The space between two groups of rowhouses in the middle of a city block.
