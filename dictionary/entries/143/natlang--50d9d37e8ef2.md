### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A natural language, one that arose without intentional design.

   **Usage:** informal; natural language processing

   **Example:** Proto-Indo-European was a natlang that can only be reconstructed with the help of its attested daughter languages.

   **Source antonyms:** conlang; constructed language
