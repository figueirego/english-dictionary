## Suffix

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Alternative form of -ed now only standard with words which end in -e, but historically permissible in most of the same places as -ed.

   **Usage:** alt-of; alternative; morpheme

   **Alternative form of:** -ed now only standard with words which end in -e; but historically permissible in most of the same places as -ed

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An empty suffix, perhaps derived from the past-tense suffix above, added in some dialects to the present tense forms of some words which then add an additional -ed in the past tense.

   **Usage:** morpheme

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Marks ordinals written in digits when the final term of the spelled number is "second" or "third"

   **Usage:** morpheme

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** -nd — marking ordinals ending with "second"; -rd — marking ordinals ending with "third"
