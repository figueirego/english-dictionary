### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An event during which hardware devices are tested for interoperability with emerging standards by physically connecting them together.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** In the New Serial Bus (NSB) plugfest, the FooMonkey device was unable to connect to a NSB controller and therefore did not receive compliance certification.
