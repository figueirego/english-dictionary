### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Usually capitalized as Dame&#58; a title equivalent to Sir for a female knight.

   **Usage:** UK

   **Example:** Dame Edith Sitwell

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

2. A matron at a school, especially Eton College.

   **Usage:** UK

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. In traditional pantomime&#58; a melodramatic female often played by a man in drag.

   **Usage:** UK
