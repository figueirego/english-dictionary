### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A marked natural ability or skill.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** He has a real talent for drawing.

   **Source synonyms:** endowment; gift; ability; technique; aptitude; capacity; craft; capability; competency; competence; cunning; dexterity; ease; facility; hand; knack; list; prowess; moxie; potential; proficiency; skill; talent; tekkers (Multicultural-London-English); touch

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. People of talent, viewed collectively; a talented person.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The director searched their talent pool to fill the new opening.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; hobbies; lifestyle; media; sports

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. One, especially a woman, from a particular place or area, judged by their attractiveness.

   **Usage:** countable; slang; uncountable

   **Example:** Not much talent in this bar tonight—let's hit the clubs.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A historical unit of weight and money used in ancient Greece, the Roman Empire, and the Middle East, equal to about 30 to 60 kg in various times and places.

   **Usage:** countable; historical; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A desire or inclination for something.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable
