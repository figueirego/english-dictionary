### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Synonym of porcelain, a hard white translucent ceramic made from kaolin, now (chiefly US) sometimes distinguished in reference to tableware as fine or good china.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** It's a china doll.

   **Source synonyms:** porcelain (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Chinaware&#58; porcelain tableware.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** He set the table with china, cloth napkins, and crystal stemware.

   **Example:** The traditional 20th anniversary gift is china.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Cheaper and lower-quality ceramic and ceramic tableware, distinguished from porcelain.

   **Usage:** US; dated; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Synonym of China root, the root of Smilax china (particularly) as a medicine.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** China root (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Synonym of cheyney&#58; worsted or woolen stuff.

   **Usage:** obsolete; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** cheyney (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Synonym of China rose, in its various senses.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source synonyms:** China rose (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Synonym of mate (rhyme of china-plate).

   **Usage:** Australia; Cockney; South-Africa; countable; slang

   **Example:** All right, me old china?

   **Source synonyms:** mate (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Tea from China, (particularly) varieties cured by smoking or opposed to Indian cultivars.

   **Usage:** dated; uncountable

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

   **Usage:** US; countable; obsolete
