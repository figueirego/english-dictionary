### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A woman who is a subsequent wife or partner of a person's parent, usually following the divorce or separation of their parents, or the death of one's biological mother.
   1. A person or thing likened to a stereotypical stepmother, having cold, cruel, or ungenerous qualities.

      **Source synonyms:** bonus mother (euphemistic)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A woman who is a subsequent wife or partner of a person's parent, usually following the divorce or separation of their parents, or the death of one's biological mother.
   1. An animal, typically a bird, who raises the offspring of another animal.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences

      **Source synonyms:** bonus mother (euphemistic)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Synonym of co-mother (“the non-birth mother of a child in a lesbian couple”).

   **Source synonyms:** co-mother (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Clipping of stepmother's blessing.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; clipping

   **Alternative form of:** [stepmother's blessing](../0be/stepmother-s-blessing--2fb0fedc8c5c.md)

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A viola, especially Viola tricolor, heartsease.

   **Usage:** rare
