### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make distinctions.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Since he was color blind he was unable to discriminate between the blue and green bottles.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To treat or affect differently, depending on differences in traits.

   **Example:** Low self-esteem can affect both rich and poor people&#58; it doesn't discriminate.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To treat or affect differently, depending on differences in traits.
   1. To make decisions harmful to (a person or group) based on prejudice.

      **Usage:** intransitive

      **Example:** The law prohibits discriminating against people based on their skin color.

      **Source context (verbatim):** construed with against

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To treat or affect differently, depending on differences in traits.

      **Usage:** intransitive; uncommon

      **Source context (verbatim):** construed with against

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To set apart as being different; to mark as different; to separate from another by discerning differences; to distinguish.

   **Usage:** transitive
