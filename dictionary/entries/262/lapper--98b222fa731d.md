## Noun · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Forms

- lappers — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who laps liquid, who takes liquid in with the tongue.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Something (especially a race) that has a stated number of laps.

   **Usage:** in-compounds

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A competitor who is one lap behind another, in the same race, and hence physically in front.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. One who wraps or folds.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A mechanism that overlaps material to make it thicker; a lapping cylinder or lapping machine.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A headsail that overlaps the mast.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; sailing; transport
