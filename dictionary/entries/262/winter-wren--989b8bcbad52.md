### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A bird of the species Troglodytes hiemalis, of the coniferous forests of the cooler parts of North America, from British Columbia to the Atlantic coast.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of what are now consider separate species, the Eurasian wren (Troglodytes troglodytes) and the Pacific wren (Troglodytes pacificus)
