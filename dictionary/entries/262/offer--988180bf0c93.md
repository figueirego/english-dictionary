### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To propose or express one's willingness (to do something).

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** She offered to help with her homework.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To present in words; to proffer; to make a proposal of; to suggest.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Everybody offered an opinion.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To place at someone’s disposal; to present (something) to be either accepted or turned down.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He offered use of his car for the week.

   **Example:** He offered his good will for the Councilman's vote.

   **Source synonyms:** extend

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To place at someone’s disposal; to present (something) to be either accepted or turned down.
   1. To present (something) for sale.

      **Usage:** transitive

      **Source synonyms:** extend

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To present (something) to God or gods, as a gesture of worship or as a sacrifice.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To present (something) to the sight etc.; to provide for use, consideration etc.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The city offers beautiful architecture.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a thing

   **Source synonyms:** offer up; showcase

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

**Source sense R2.S7**

7. To place (something) in a position where it can be added to an existing mechanical assembly.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r2-s8"></a>

**Source sense R2.S8**

8. To bid, as a price, reward, or wages.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I offered twenty dollars for it. The company is offering a salary of £30,000 a year.

<a id="r2-s9"></a>

**Source sense R2.S9**

9. To happen, to present itself.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s10"></a>

**Source sense R2.S10**

10. To make an attempt; typically used with at.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r2-s11"></a>

**Source sense R2.S11**

11. To put in opposition to; to manifest in an offensive or defensive way; to threaten.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to offer violence to somebody

   **Example:** The peasants offered no resistance as they were rounded up.
