### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The replacement of stale or noxious air with fresh.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The mechanical system used to circulate and replace air.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An exchange of views during a discussion.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The public exposure of an issue or topic.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The bodily process of breathing; the inhalation of air to provide oxygen, and the exhalation of spent air to remove carbon dioxide.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** This toxin impairs ventilation by causing inflammation in the bronchioles.

   **Source synonyms:** respiration

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Ellipsis of mechanical ventilation.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; countable; ellipsis; uncountable

   **Example:** It is time to try to wean this patient from ventilation.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences
