### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An Old Irish pronominal clitic added to the end of an accentual unit containing a pronoun, now more commonly called emphatic suffixes.

   **Usage:** Celtic linguistics

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A Welsh reduplicated or conjunctive pronoun (appearing in modern Welsh as minnau, tithau, yntau, hithau, ninnau, chwithau, and hwythau).

   **Usage:** Celtic linguistics
