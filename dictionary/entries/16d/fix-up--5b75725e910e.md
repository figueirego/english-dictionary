### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To provide (someone) with something; to furnish.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive; informal

   **Example:** I need someone to fix me up with a tent and some camping gear.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To set up a date or to introduce people, especially with the intention of a possible romantic or sexual coupling.

   **Usage:** transitive; usually

   **Example:** Can you fix me up with a date for Saturday night?

   **Source context (verbatim):** with with

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To repair or refurbish.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I've been fixing up the boat for the holidays.

   **Source synonyms:** amend; correct; fix; fix up; furbish; retrieve; make whole; mend; patch; put to rights; rectify; recondition; reform; refurbish; remediate; remedy; renew; renovate; restore; restrive; revamp; right

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To prepare or provide (something).

   **Usage:** informal; transitive

   **Example:** His mother will fix up a snack for the sleepover tonight.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To get one's act together; to sort oneself out.

   **Usage:** Multicultural-London-English; slang

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To settle up with; reimburse.

   **Usage:** dated; slang

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To get an abortion; to procure an abortion for someone.

   **Usage:** slang; transitive

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To do a dose of a drug, especially heroin; to provide a drug (often alcoholic drink) to someone addicted to it.

   **Usage:** intransitive; slang
