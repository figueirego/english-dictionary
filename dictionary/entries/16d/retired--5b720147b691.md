### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ɹɪˈtaɪəɹd/ — source labels: US

### Forms

- more retired — comparative
- most retired — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Secluded from society (of a lifestyle, activity etc.); private, quiet.

   **Example:** I lead a retired lifestyle.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of a place, far from civilization, not able to be easily seen or accessed; secluded.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Having left employment, especially on reaching pensionable age. (of people)

   **Example:** The retired workers are a major expense due to their pensions.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. No longer in use or production.

   **Example:** Following Jackie Robinson's success, his uniform number, 42, became a retired number across all major league teams.
