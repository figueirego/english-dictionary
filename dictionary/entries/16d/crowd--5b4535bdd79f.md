### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To press forward; to advance by pushing.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The man crowded into the packed room.

   **Source synonyms:** barge

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To press together or collect in numbers.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** They crowded through the archway and into the park.

   **Source synonyms:** swarm; throng; crowd in; crush; pile; horde

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To press or drive together, especially into a small space; to cram.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He tried to crowd too many cows into the cow-pen.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To fill by pressing or thronging together

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To push, to press, to shove.

   **Usage:** often; transitive

   **Example:** They tried to crowd her off the sidewalk.

   **Source context (verbatim):** used with "out of" or "off"

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To approach another ship too closely when it has right of way.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To carry excessive sail in the hope of moving faster.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a square-rigged ship

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To press by solicitation; to urge; to dun; hence, to treat discourteously or unreasonably.

   **Usage:** transitive

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A group of people congregated or collected into a close body without order.

   **Example:** After the movie let out, a crowd of people pushed through the exit doors.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Several things collected or closely pressed together; also, some things adjacent to each other.

   **Example:** There was a crowd of toys pushed beneath the couch where the children were playing.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. The so-called lower orders of people; the populace; the vulgar.

   **Usage:** with-definite-article

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. A group of people united or at least characterised by a common interest.

   **Example:** That obscure author's fans were a nerdy crowd which hardly ever interacted before the Internet age.

   **Example:** We're concerned that our daughter has fallen in with a bad crowd.
