## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The practice of sequentially raising and harvesting a second (or third, or sometimes more) crop (usually vegetables) in the same field in a single growing season, especially in warmer climates such as California's Imperial Valley or the Rio Grande valley of Texas and Mexico.

   **Usage:** uncountable
