### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Reaction wood, in gymnosperms and amborella, that forms on the side of the part which is under compression and straightens its bend, and which has a lower percentage of cellulose and a higher percentage of lignin than normal wood.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
