### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A stake driven into the ground.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** a picket fence

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A type of punishment by which an offender had to rest his or her entire body weight on the top of a small stake.

   **Usage:** countable; historical; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A tool in mountaineering that is driven into the snow and used as an anchor or to arrest falls.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. One of the soldiers or troops placed on a line forward of a position to warn against an enemy advance; or any unit (for example, an aircraft or ship) performing a similar function.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A sentry.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; sometimes; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A protester positioned outside an office, workplace etc. during a strike (usually in plural); also the protest itself.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Pickets normally endeavor to be non-violent.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The card game piquet.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** card-games; games

   **Usage:** transitive
