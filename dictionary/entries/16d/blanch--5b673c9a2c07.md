### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To grow or become white.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** His cheek blanched with fear.

   **Example:** The rose blanches in the sun.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To take the color out of, and make white; to bleach.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to blanch linen

   **Example:** Age has blanched his hair.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To cook by dipping briefly into boiling water, then directly into cold water.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** cooking; food; lifestyle

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To whiten, for example the surface of meat, by plunging into boiling water and afterwards into cold, so as to harden the surface and retain the juices.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To bleach by excluding light, for example the stalks or leaves of plants by earthing them up or tying them together.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To make white by removing the skin of, for example by scalding.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to blanch almonds

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To give a white lustre to (silver, before stamping, in the process of coining)

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To cover (sheet iron) with a coating of tin.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To give a favorable appearance to; to whitewash; to whiten;

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** palliate
