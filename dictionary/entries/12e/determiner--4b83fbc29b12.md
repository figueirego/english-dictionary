### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Someone or something that determines, or helps to determine, something else.
   1. A factor that defines something, or one among several that define it.

      **Example:** In quality management, the determiners of quality sometimes differ across use cases.

      **Source synonyms:** definer

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Someone or something that determines, or helps to determine, something else.
   1. Someone or something (especially a sentient agent) that determines; a decider of a disposition.

      **Example:** He is the determiner \[usually referring to God (in theological contexts) and job roles (in administrative contexts)\]

      **Example:** Who is the determiner? \[administratively or theologically\]

      **Example:** The software is the determiner if no input is given before the prompt times out.

      **Source synonyms:** decider; decisor; disposer; definer; arbiter; arbitrator; decisionmaker

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A member of a class of words functioning in a noun phrase to identify or distinguish a referent without describing or modifying it.

   **Example:** Definite articles, indefinite articles, and cardinal numbers acting as quantifiers are types of determiners.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** grammar; human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** determinative\[Appendix&#58;Glossary

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A dependent function in a noun phrase that marks it as definite or indefinite. This function is usually filled by words in the determinative class but may be filled by other elements such as a genitive pronoun.

   **Example:** In the sentence "Many people voted for him," the word many is a determiner.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** grammar; human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** determinative\[Appendix&#58;Glossary
