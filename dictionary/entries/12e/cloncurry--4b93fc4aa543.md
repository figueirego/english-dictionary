## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A river in north-west Queensland, Australia, which joins the Flinders River named after Lady Cloncurry in Ireland; in full, the Cloncurry River.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A town in the Shire of Cloncurry, Queensland, named after the river, on which it is situated.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A local government area in Queensland, Australia, with its administrative centre in the town; in full, the Shire of Cloncurry.
