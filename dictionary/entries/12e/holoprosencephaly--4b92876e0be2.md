### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A congenital disorder involving insufficient division of the lobes of the brain during fetal development, resulting in skull and facial deformities ranging from minor cleft lip problems to cyclopia, in which a single eye develops where the nose would normally be.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences; teratology
