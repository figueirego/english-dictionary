## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The side of a fabric, often with a less visible color or pattern, that is not intended to be seen on a finished project.

   **Example:** Trace the pattern on the wrong side so that any mistakes won't show when you're done.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; manufacturing; sewing; textiles

   **Source antonyms:** right side

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see wrong, side.

   **Example:** Don't drive on the wrong side of the road.
