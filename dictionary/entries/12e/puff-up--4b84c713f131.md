## Verb

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To inflate with air.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** We puffed up the balloons.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To fluff up, such as an animal raising its fur or feathers to appear larger or conserve body heat.

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** Small birds puff up while sleeping to keep warm.

   **Example:** The angry cat hissed and puffed up its fur.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To swell due to injury or illness.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** My hand puffed up where the cat had scratched me.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To become proud. (Often written as puffed up with pride.)

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To trump up.
