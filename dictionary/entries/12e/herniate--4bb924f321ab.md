**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of a tissue, structure, or part of an organ&#58; to protrude through the muscular tissue or the membrane by which it is normally contained, causing a hernia.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** When the small intestine herniates into the groin, one has an inguinal hernia.
