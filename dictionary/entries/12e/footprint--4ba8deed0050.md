## Noun

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈfʊtpɹɪnt/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Forms

- footprints — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The impression of the foot in a soft substance such as sand or snow.

   **Source synonyms:** footmark; ichnogram

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Space required by a piece of equipment.

   **Example:** This computer has a smaller footprint.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The amount of hard drive space required for a program.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The audit trail left by a crashed program.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A trace left behind that gives evidence of some action having occurred.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. What a person has done before, as a profession or lifestyle, serving as a model for others.

   **Usage:** figuratively; in-plural

   **Example:** He is following in his father's footprints.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The surface space occupied by a structure.

   **Example:** the footprint of a building

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A company's geographic market presence.

   **Example:** The store, which is slated to open next month, increases the company's footprint in the market to 14 locations.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. The ecological impact of a human activity, machine, etc.

   **Example:** My carbon footprint is very high.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; ecology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. Availability of a satellite from the ground.

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. A polygon representing the shape of an item.

   **Example:** building footprint

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** cartography; geography; natural-sciences
