## Noun

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /dɪˈnaɪ.əl/ — source labels: General-American; Received-Pronunciation

### Forms

- denials — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An assertion of untruth.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The singer has issued a sweeping denial of all the rumors.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The negation in logic.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The denial of "There might be X" is the null, "False, there is no X."

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; logic; mathematics; philosophy; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A refusal or failure to provide or grant something that is requested or desired.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The denial of medical treatment to those who cannot afford to pay is scandalous.

   **Example:** I cannot understand the bank's denial of my loan application.

   **Example:** Every time we asked for an interview we got a denial.

   **Source synonyms:** nonassent; refusal; rejection; abnegation; declination; denial; turndown; disapproval; dissension; nonapproval; nonassent; nonconsent; prohibition; refusal; rejection; renunciation; repudiation; unapproval; unconsent

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Refusal to believe that a problem exists.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** We couldn't break through his denial about being alcoholic.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Refusal to admit responsibility for wrongdoing.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Negationism, denialism of historical facts or accepted interpretation.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A defense mechanism involving a refusal to accept the truth of a phenomenon or prospect.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** He is in denial that he has a drinking problem.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; psychology; sciences

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A disownment or disavowal

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The denial of Jesus by Peter.
