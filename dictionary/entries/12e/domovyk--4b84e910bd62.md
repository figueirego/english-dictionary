### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In Ukrainian folklore, a spirit of a house who protects the inhabitants.

   **Usage:** Slavic mythology

   **Example:** Domovyk is a kind house spirit. He lives in rural houses, under a stove or in the roof space. He protects the family inhabiting the house from bad people, unkind spirits and evil creatures.
