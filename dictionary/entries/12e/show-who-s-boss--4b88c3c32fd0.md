### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To demonstrate oneself to be dominant; to show that one has the upper hand.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To demonstrate that one is dominant over or superior to someone; to establish that one has control of some device or intractable object.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive
