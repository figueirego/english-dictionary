### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Sexual urges or drives.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; common usage

   **Example:** Good grief man, control your libido!

   **Source synonyms:** horniness (vulgar)

   **Source antonyms:** frigidity

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Drives or mental energies related to or based on sexual instincts but not necessarily sexual in and of themselves.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** For Freudians, libido means the desire to "unite and bind" with objects in the world.

   **Example:** The ego as an organ which seeks to synthesize thoughts in the psyche is said to be driven by libido or eros.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; psychology; sciences

   **Source antonyms:** destrudo; mortido
