### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To mind one's own business.
   1. In a way whereby one avoids dilettantism&#58; avoiding giving an opinion in domains where one's skills and knowledge are not at an expert level.

      **Usage:** idiomatic; informal

      **Source synonyms:** stick to one's knitting; know one's place

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To mind one's own business.
   1. In a way whereby one avoids interfering in others' interests, regardless of skill level.

      **Usage:** idiomatic; informal

      **Source synonyms:** stick to one's knitting; know one's place

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To keep to one's own kind; to avoid intermixing or associating with other types of people.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; informal

   **Source synonyms:** know one's place

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see stay, in, one's, lane; for example, either manually or via lanekeeping by software.
