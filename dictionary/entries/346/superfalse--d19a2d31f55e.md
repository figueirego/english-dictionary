### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In supervaluationism, necessarily false regardless of the values of variables.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** "I am asleep and I am not asleep" is superfalse, because I can never be both.
