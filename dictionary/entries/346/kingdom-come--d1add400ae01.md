### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The place that one will go to after one's death; the afterlife.
   1. Death; also, a state of complete annihilation.

      **Usage:** colloquial; countable; figuratively; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The place that one will go to after one's death; the afterlife.
   1. Heaven or paradise.

      **Usage:** colloquial; countable; specifically; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The rule of God over the world in the future; especially, according to those believing in millenarianism, during a period of peace beginning with the second coming of Jesus Christ and lasting a millennium.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity

<a id="r1-s4"></a>
