### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A runner at the front or ahead.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. By extension, a non-competitor who leads out the competitors on to the circuit, or who runs/rides the course prior to competitor trials, usually testing or checking the way.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A precursor or harbinger, a warning ahead.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A forebear, an ancestor, a predecessor.

   **Example:** Bakelite is a forerunner of today's plastics.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A postage stamp used in the time before a region or area issues stamps of its own.
