## Noun

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈsnaɪ.pɚ/ — source labels: General-American

### Forms

- snipers — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person using long-range small arms for precise attacks from a concealed position.

   **Example:** The sniper shot him in the head.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A sniper rifle.

   **Usage:** broadly

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any attacker using a non-contact weapon against a specific target from a concealed position.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. One who shoots from a concealed position.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. One who criticizes; a person who frequently snipes at others.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A hunter of snipe (the bird).

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A player who specializes in scoring goals.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Example:** Alexander Ovechkin is often regarded as the greatest sniper of his generation.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; ice-hockey; lifestyle; skating; sports

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A person or automated process set up by a person who or which attempts to win an online auction by placing a bid only seconds before the auction ends, leaving no time for other bidders to respond
