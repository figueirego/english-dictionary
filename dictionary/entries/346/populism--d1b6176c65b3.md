### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A political doctrine or philosophy that proposes that the rights and powers of ordinary people are exploited by a privileged elite, and supports their struggle to overcome this.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; human-sciences; philosophy; politics; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
