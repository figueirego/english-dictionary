### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any parasitical worm of the class or infraclass Cestoda, which infests the intestines of animals, including humans, often infecting different host species during their life cycle.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any parasitical worm of the class or infraclass Cestoda, which infests the intestines of animals, including humans, often infecting different host species during their life cycle.
   1. A broad fish tapeworm, Diphyllobothrium latum.

      **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Infection by tapeworms.

   **Usage:** uncountable
