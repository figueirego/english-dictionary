## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A form of navigation used by homing missiles, based on the fact that two vehicles are on a collision course when their direct line of sight does not change direction as the range closes.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** pro nav; PN (initialism)
