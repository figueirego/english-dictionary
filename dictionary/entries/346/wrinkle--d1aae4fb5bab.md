## Noun · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Pronunciation

### Forms

- wrincle — alternative; obsolete

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A small furrow, ridge or crease in an otherwise smooth surface.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A line or crease in the skin, especially when caused by age or fatigue.

   **Example:** Spending time out in the sun may cause you to develop wrinkles sooner.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A fault, imperfection or bug especially in a new system or product; typically, they will need to be ironed out.

   **Example:** We need to iron out the wrinkles in the plan before implementing it.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A twist on something existing; a novel difference.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** We've added a couple of new wrinkles to the policy.
