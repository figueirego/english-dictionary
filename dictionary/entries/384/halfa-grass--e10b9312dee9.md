### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A north African and south Asian grass of species Desmostachya bipinnata, with tough stems woven into rope, sandals, mats, etc., and sacred in Hinduism, Buddhism and other religions.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** kusha; kusha grass

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Other north African grasses with similar uses, such as sparto (Stipa tenacissima and Lygeum sparticum) and cogongrass (Imperata cylindrica)

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
