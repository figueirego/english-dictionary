## Verb

### Forms

- leaves behind — present; singular; third-person
- leaving behind — participle; present

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To abandon.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** We left behind our luggage at the hotel.

   **Example:** How could you leave me behind like that?

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To forget about.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** We (accidentally) left behind our bags at the airport.

   **Example:** Just leave your cares behind you and enjoy the party.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To not live longer than; to be survived by.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** Mrs Johnston died at thirty, and left behind three young daughters.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To leave (a trace of something).

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** The wound I got in my car accident left behind a massive scar.

   **Example:** Unfortunately, this cleaning product leaves behind a noticeable residue.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To outdo; to progress faster than (someone or something else).

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** This product leaves behind all its competitors in the market.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To pass.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** As soon as we made our way onto the highway, we left the skyscrapers behind us.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see leave, behind.

   **Usage:** idiomatic
