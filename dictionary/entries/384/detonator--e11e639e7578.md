### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A device used to detonate an explosive device etc.

   **Source synonyms:** fuze; fuse

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A small explosive device attached to the railhead to provide an audible warning when a train passes over it.

   **Usage:** UK

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** rail-transport; railways; transport

   **Source synonyms:** torpedo

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

   **Usage:** archaic
