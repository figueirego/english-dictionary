### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Interference with the administration of law and justice, as by ignorance of a justification, fraud of evidential validity, or not disclosing discovery of facts, or by doing damage to a witness, officer, juror.

   **Usage:** US; countable; uncountable
