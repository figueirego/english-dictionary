### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A highly interesting or suspenseful written work that one is compelled to read very rapidly.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** Her debut novel is such a page-turner that it's one of those books that you can't put down.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person who is responsible for turning the pages of another musician’s (especially a pianist or organist’s) sheet music during a performance.

   **Example:** She's a famous soloist today, but she did her time as a page-turner, just as many others have done.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see page, turner.
