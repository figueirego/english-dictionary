**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The quality of a statement that, when taken in context, may actually mean something different from, or the opposite of, what is written literally; the use of words expressing something other than their literal intention, often in a humorous context.

   **Usage:** countable; rhetoric; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   1. An ironic statement.

      **Usage:** countable; rhetoric

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Dramatic irony&#58; a theatrical effect in which the meaning of a situation, or some incongruity in the plot, is understood by the audience, but not by the characters in the play.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Socratic irony&#58; ignorance feigned for the purpose of confounding or provoking an antagonist.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
