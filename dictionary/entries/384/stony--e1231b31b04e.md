### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. As hard as stone.

   **Example:** a stony polymer

   **Source synonyms:** rock hard; stonelike

   **Source antonyms:** soft

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Containing or made up of stones.

   **Example:** a stony path

   **Source synonyms:** pebbly; rocky; shingly

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of a person, lacking warmth and emotion.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** a stony grandmother

   **Source synonyms:** stone cold; cold; cool; hardhearted; heartless; impassive; unemotional; unfeeling; brutal; callous; cold; insensate (formal); hardhanded; cold-eyed; cruel; dour; thick-skinned; flint-hearted; forbidding; frosty; grim; hard; hard-boiled; hardened; hardhearted; heartless; harsh; humorless; icy; iron-hearted; insensitive; marble-hearted; rigorous; rockhearted; severe; stern; stonehearted; stony; stonyhearted; strict; tough; unsympathetic; unmoved

   **Source antonyms:** passionate; warm

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Of an action or expression, such as a look, showing no warmth of emotion; (usually also) emotionally cold, silent, and motionless or nearly so.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** a stony look

   **Example:** a stony silence
