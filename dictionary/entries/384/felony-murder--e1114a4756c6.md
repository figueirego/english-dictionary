### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of committing or abetting a crime that results in the killing of a person, regardless of intent, and even if the committer or abettor is not the one who killed the person.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** In many states, an act of arson that accidentally kills someone automatically makes the arsonist liable for felony murder.
