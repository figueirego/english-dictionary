### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To watch; to observe without participating.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** My performance seems somehow always to get worse when there are other people looking on.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To think of something in a particular way.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I look on him not as a boss, but as a friend.

   **Example:** We don't look kindly on people like you.

   **Example:** I look poorly on any suggestion of overspending.
