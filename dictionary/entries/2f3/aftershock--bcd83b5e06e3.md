## Noun

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An earthquake that follows in the same vicinity as another, usually larger, earthquake (the "mainshock").

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; geology; natural-sciences; seismology

   **Source synonyms:** afterquake

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. By extension, any result or consequence following a major event.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Emotional and physical distress following a traumatic event.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
