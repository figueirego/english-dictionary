**Source sense R1.S2**

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A person, usually one who is retired, who travels from a cold climate to a warmer one in the winter; one who overwinters elsewhere.

   **Source synonyms:** snowbirder
