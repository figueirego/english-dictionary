### Forms

- tardier — comparative
- tardiest — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Late; overdue or delayed.

   **Example:** He yawned, then raised a tardy hand over his mouth.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Moving with a slow pace or motion; not swift.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Ineffectual; slow-witted, slow to act, or dull.

   **Example:** His tardy performance bordered on incompetence.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Unwary; unready (especially in the phrase take (someone) tardy).

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Criminal; guilty.

   **Usage:** obsolete

- IPA: /ˈtɑɹdi/ — source labels: General-American

### Forms

- tardies — plural

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A piece of paper given to students who are late to class.

   **Usage:** US

   **Example:** The teacher gave her a tardy because she did not come into the classroom until after the bell.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. An instance of a student's being marked as tardy by a teacher on the teacher's attendance sheet.
