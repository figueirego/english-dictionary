### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The power, right, or authority to interpret and apply the law.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** legal jurisdiction

   **Example:** The case falls outside the court’s jurisdiction.

   **Source synonyms:** power; authority; disposition; craft (obsolete); force; might; potence; potency; power; strength; virtue; Note&#58; All terms are usually figurative.; area; arena; bailiwick; domain; department; discipline; field; jurisdiction; lookout; orbit; province; realm; section; sector; sphere; territory; turf; pale; wheelhouse (US; slang); world

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The power or right to exercise authority.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** outside the jurisdiction of

   **Source synonyms:** oyer and terminer (UK; historical); soc and sac

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The power or right to perform some action as part of applying the law.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Police officers have no jurisdiction in that district.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The authority of a sovereign power to govern or legislate.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The limits or territory within which authority may be exercised.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The United Kingdom consists of three jurisdictions&#58; England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.
