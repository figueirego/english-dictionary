### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To affirm to be correct, true, or genuine.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** When will the appraiser attest the date of the painting?

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To certify by signature or oath.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** You must attest your will in order for it to be valid.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To certify in an official capacity.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To supply or be evidence of.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** Her fine work attested her ability.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To put under oath.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To call to witness; to invoke.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive
