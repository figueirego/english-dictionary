## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The southern islands of the Lesser Antilles, composed of the island nations of Dominica, Martinique, Saint Lucia, Saint Vincent and the Grenadines, Barbados, Grenada and Trinidad and Tobago.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
