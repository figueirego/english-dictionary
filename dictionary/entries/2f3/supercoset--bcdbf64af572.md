**Source sense R1.S1**

1. (supergeometry and string theory) A coset space formed from a Lie supergroup by quotienting by a subgroup, such as the diagonal even subgroup in a permutation supercoset.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

   **Extractor diagnostics (not usage labels):** empty-gloss; no-gloss

**Supplemental definition (evidence reviewed; source gap preserved):** The original quotation concerns supercoset string sigma models. The cited paper gives explicit quotient constructions and distinguishes their Lie supergroup numerator from the even subgroup denominator.
