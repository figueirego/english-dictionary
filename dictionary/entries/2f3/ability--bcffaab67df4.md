### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Suitableness.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The quality or state of being able; capacity to do or of doing something; having the necessary power.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** This phone has the ability to have its software upgraded wirelessly.

   **Example:** This wood has the ability to fight off insects, fungus, and mold for a considerable time.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The legal wherewithal to act.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Physical power.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; now limited to Scotland dialects

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Financial ability.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A unique power of the mind; a faculty.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A skill or competence in doing; mental power; talent; aptitude.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** a mixed-ability class

   **Example:** They are persons of ability, who will go far in life.

   **Example:** She has an uncanny ability to defuse conflict.
