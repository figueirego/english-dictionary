### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A scalar λ, such that there exists a non-zero vector x (a corresponding eigenvector) for which the image of x under a given linear transformation A is equal to the image of x under multiplication by λ; i.e. Ax=λx.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** linear-algebra; mathematics; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** characteristic root; characteristic value; eigenroot; latent value; proper value
