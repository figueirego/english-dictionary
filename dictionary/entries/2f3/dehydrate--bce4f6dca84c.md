### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To remove water from; to dry up.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** dehydrate food

   **Example:** Running in the heat can quickly dehydrate you.

   **Example:** The fruit was dehydrated and packed for storage.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To lose water from one's body; to become dehydrated.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** dehydrate rapidly
