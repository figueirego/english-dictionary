### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To surrender (someone or something); to inform on (someone).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He was surrounded, so gave himself up.

   **Example:** They gave him up to the police.

   **Source synonyms:** surrender; yield

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To relinquish (something)

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He gave up his seat to an old man.

   **Example:** The king gave up land in exchange for peace.

   **Source synonyms:** forlend (archaic); surrender; yield

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Devote or dedicate (oneself, one's life, etc.) to something.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I gave myself up to working for this cause.

   **Example:** She persuaded us to give up our time to the project.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To have someone obtain (something) to one's disadvantage; to concede.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** A good pitcher will give up very few home runs.

   **Example:** Don't tell them where you are, and try not to give up any clues either.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To stop or quit (an activity, etc.).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** They gave up the search when it got dark.

   **Source synonyms:** cease; discontinue; blin; turn away from

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To abandon (someone or something)

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I gave up my faith years ago.

   **Source synonyms:** desert; forlet (dialectal; obsolete); forsake

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To lose hope concerning (someone or something)

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** They gave him up for dead.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To admit defeat, capitulate; to desist for that reason.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** OK, I give up, you win.

   **Source synonyms:** [capitulate](../203/capitulate--80edff99a8e5.md); give in; surrender; throw in the towel; wave the white flag

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To execute a trade on behalf of another broker.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. To acknowledge, recognize as.

   **Usage:** US; dialectal; intransitive

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. To reveal (something).

   **Usage:** US; intransitive; slang

   **Example:** He gave up that he was the one who opened the door.
