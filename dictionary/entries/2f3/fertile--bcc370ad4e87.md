### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of land, etc.&#58; capable of growing abundant crops; productive.

   **Source synonyms:** fat

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of one's imagination, etc.&#58; active, productive, prolific.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Source synonyms:** productive; prolific; pregnant; batful; luxuriant; fertile; fruitful; thriving; yielding; yieldly; voluminous

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Capable of reproducing; fecund, fruitful.

   **Example:** Most women at the age of fifty are not fertile.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences

   **Source synonyms:** childing (archaic)

   **Source antonyms:** barren; infertile; sterile

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Capable of developing past the egg stage.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Not itself fissile, but able to be converted into a fissile material by irradiation in a reactor.

   **Example:** There are two basic fertile materials&#58; uranium-238 and thorium-232.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics
