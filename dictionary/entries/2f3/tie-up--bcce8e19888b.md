### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To secure with rope, string, etc.

   **Example:** Don't forget to tie up your hair before you bake.

   **Example:** The robbers tied up the bank employees before forcing a way into the vault.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To occupy, detain, keep busy, or delay.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** He has been tying up the phone lines for hours now.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To complete, finish, or resolve.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I'd like to tie up the project before I leave.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To immobilize a capital&#58; make a capital investment that makes that capital unavailable.

   **Example:** Don't tie up your capital in aging accounts.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance
