### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make sweet to the taste.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to sweeten tea

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To make (more) pleasant or to the mind or feelings.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to sweeten life

   **Example:** to sweeten friendship

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To make mild or kind; to soften.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to sweeten the temper

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To make less painful or laborious; to relieve.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to sweeten the cares of life

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To soften to the eye; to make delicate.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To make pure and healthful by destroying noxious matter.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to sweeten the air

   **Example:** to sweeten rooms or apartments that have been infected

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To make warm and fertile.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to dry and sweeten soils

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To raise the pH of (a soil) by adding alkali.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** agriculture; business; lifestyle

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To restore to purity; to free from taint.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to sweeten water, butter, or meat

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. To make more attractive; said of offers in negotiations.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to sweeten the deal by increasing the price offered

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

   **Usage:** intransitive
