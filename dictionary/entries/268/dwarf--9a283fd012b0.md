### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any member of a race of beings from (especially Scandinavian and other Germanic) folklore, usually depicted as having some sort of supernatural powers and being skilled in crafting and metalworking, often as short with long beards, and sometimes as clashing with elves.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; mysticism; mythology; philosophy; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** dwarfin; dwarrow

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person of short stature, often one whose limbs are disproportionately small in relation to the body as compared with typical adults, usually as the result of a genetic condition.

   **Usage:** offensive; sometimes

   **Source synonyms:** elf; midget; pygmy

   **Source antonyms:** ettin; giant

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An animal, plant or other thing much smaller than the usual of its sort.

   **Example:** dwarf tree

   **Example:** dwarf honeysuckle

   **Source synonyms:** runt

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A dwarf star.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astronomy; natural-sciences

### Senses

<a id="r3-s1"></a>

**Source sense R3.S1**

1. To render (much) smaller, turn into a dwarf (version).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** miniaturize; shrink

<a id="r3-s2"></a>

**Source sense R3.S2**

2. To make appear (much) smaller, puny, tiny; to be much larger than.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The newly-built skyscraper dwarfs all older buildings in the downtown skyline.

<a id="r3-s3"></a>

**Source sense R3.S3**

3. To make appear insignificant.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Bach dwarfs all other composers.

   **Source synonyms:** eclipse; overshadow; outshadow; outshine; outdo; put to shame; upstage; surpass; outmatch; outstrip

<a id="r3-s4"></a>

**Source sense R3.S4**

4. To become (much) smaller.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source synonyms:** shrink

<a id="r3-s5"></a>

**Source sense R3.S5**

5. To hinder from growing to the natural size; to make or keep small; to stunt.
