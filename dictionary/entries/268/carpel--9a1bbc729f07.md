### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A constituent part of a flower pistil&#58; the female reproductive organs in a flower. A carpel is composed of an ovary, a style, and a stigma, although some flowers have carpels without a distinct style. In origin, carpels are leaves (megasporophylls) that have evolved to enclose the ovules. A pistil may be composed of a single carpel or of several carpels fused together.
