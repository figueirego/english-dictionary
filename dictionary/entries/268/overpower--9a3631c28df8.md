### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To subdue someone by superior force.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** We overpowered the opposing army within a couple of hours.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To excel or exceed in power; to cause to yield; to subdue.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Bright light overpowers the eyes.

   **Source synonyms:** overbear

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To render imperceptible by means of greater strength, intensity, etc.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The dish was OK, but the garlic slightly overpowered the herbs.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To make excessively powerful.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** video-games

   **Source antonyms:** nerf
