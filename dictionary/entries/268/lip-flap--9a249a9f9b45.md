### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A failure in audio-video synchronization, so that an actor's lips move but no sound is heard.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** broadcasting; film; media; television

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A movement of an actor's lips, which is to be matched as closely as possible in a dubbed version of the film.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** broadcasting; film; media; television
