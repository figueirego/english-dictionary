### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Someone, usually male, who uses (or has skill with) magic, mystic items, and magical and mystical practices.

   **Example:** The wizard conjured up a large fireball.

   **Source synonyms:** conjurer; mage; magician; magic user; mystic; sorcerer; warlock; witch

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One who is especially skilled or unusually talented in a particular field.

   **Example:** He was a financial wizard, capable of predicting the movements of the stock markets.

   **Source synonyms:** expert; genius; prodigy

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A computer program or script used to simplify complex operations, often for an inexperienced user.

   **Example:** Use the "Add Network Connection" wizard to connect to a network in a series of simple steps.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** assistant

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An administrator of a multi-user dungeon, or someone who has earned similar rights through gameplay; a user in wizard mode.

   **Usage:** Internet

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A wise man; a sage.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A virgin over the age of 30 who does not socialize and thus cannot find a sexual partner.

   **Usage:** humorous; sometimes; 4chan slang and incel slang

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Someone who deepfakes pornographic material using content from social media (usually selfies of people) at the request of others.

   **Usage:** 4chan slang
