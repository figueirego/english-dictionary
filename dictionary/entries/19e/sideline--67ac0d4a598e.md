### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A line at the side of something.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Something that is additional or extra or that exists around the edges or margins of a main item.

   **Example:** She started the business as a sideline to her regular work and it ended up becoming the greater source of income.

   **Example:** Soup need not be just a sideline to a meal; if you like, it can be the main course.

   **Example:** I often do electronic repairs as a sideline.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A line for hobbling an animal by connecting the fore and the hind feet of the same side.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A line defining the side boundary of a playing field. Used in Canadian football, field lacrosse and basketball.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports

   **Source synonyms:** touchline

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The area outside the playing field beyond each sideline.

   **Usage:** plural-normally

   **Example:** The coach stood on the sidelines and bellowed commands at the team.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The outside or perimeter of any activity.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** to relegate to the sidelines

   **Example:** She installed the whole fixture while he simply watched from the sidelines.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A secondary road, especially a byroad at right angles to a main road.

   **Usage:** Canada

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A side hustle.
