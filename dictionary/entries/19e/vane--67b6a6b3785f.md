### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A weather vane.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of several usually relatively thin, rigid, flat, or sometimes curved surfaces radially mounted along an axis, as a blade in a turbine or a sail on a windmill, that is turned by or used to turn a fluid.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The flattened, web-like part of a feather, consisting of a series of barbs on either side of the shaft.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences; ornithology

   **Source synonyms:** vexillum

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A sight on a sextant or compass.

   **Usage:** navigation

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. One of the metal guidance or stabilizing fins attached to the tail of a bomb or other missile.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; government; military; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; politics; tools; war; weaponry
