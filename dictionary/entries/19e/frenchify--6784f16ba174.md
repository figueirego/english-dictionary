### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make French or more French-like in any way.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** This café likes to frenchify the names of its dishes to make them sound better than they actually are — they call their fish and chips "poisson-frites".

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To become French or more French-like.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** This restaurant has frenchified.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Synonym of gallicize, to translate into the French language.

   **Usage:** transitive; uncommon

   **Source synonyms:** gallicize (synonym; synonym-of); to translate into the French language (synonym; synonym-of)
