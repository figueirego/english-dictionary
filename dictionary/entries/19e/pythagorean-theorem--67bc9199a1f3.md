### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A mathematical theorem which states that the square of the length of the hypotenuse of a right triangle is equal to the sum of the squares of those of the two other sides.

   **Example:** The Pythagorean theorem only applies to right triangles.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** Baudhayana theorem; Gougu theorem

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A generalization of the Pythagorean theorem for Euclidean triangles to Hilbert spaces.

   **Usage:** functional analysis
