## Noun

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The state or character trait of being cheap (stingy).

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** His cheapness was legendary; he refused to use candles or lamps, not to reduce the risk of fire but rather to save a penny.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a person

   **Source synonyms:** frugality; meanness; miserliness; niggardliness; parsimony; stinginess; thriftiness; tightness

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The state or characteristic of being cheap (whether inexpensive, poorly made, or both).

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a product

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The state or characteristic of being cheap (whether inexpensive, poorly made, or both).
   1. Synonym of shoddiness.

      **Usage:** uncountable; usually

      **Example:** The cheapness of the manufacturing is insulting. \[the product is poorly made\]

      **Source context (verbatim):** of a product

      **Source synonyms:** shoddiness (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The state or characteristic of being cheap (whether inexpensive, poorly made, or both).
   1. Synonym of inexpensiveness; affordability.

      **Usage:** uncountable; usually

      **Example:** The cheapness of the offerings is amazing. \[the prices are surprisingly good\]

      **Source context (verbatim):** of a product

      **Source synonyms:** inexpensiveness (synonym; synonym-of)
