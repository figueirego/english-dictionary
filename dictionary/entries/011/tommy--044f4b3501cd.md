### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A British infantryman, especially one from World War I. Ellipsis of Tommy Atkins.

   **Usage:** UK; countable; slang; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** tommy boy

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Bread or breadlike foodstuff, generally a penny roll.

   **Usage:** UK; countable; obsolete; slang; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The supply of food carried by workmen as their daily allowance.

   **Usage:** UK; countable; obsolete; slang; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A truck, or barter; the exchange of labour for goods instead of money; the scrip by which such exchange occurs.

   **Usage:** UK; countable; obsolete; slang; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A tommy bar.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Short for Tommy gun

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; countable; slang; uncountable

   **Alternative form of:** [Tommy gun](../122/tommy-gun--488bd252b00b.md)

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Tommyrot; nonsense.

   **Usage:** countable; dated; slang; uncountable

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Synonym of dicky (“detachable shirt front”).

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; slang; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** dicky (synonym; synonym-of)
