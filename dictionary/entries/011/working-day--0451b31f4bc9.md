## Noun

### Forms

- working days — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of those days of a week on which work is done, officially Monday to Friday in many countries (even though many people work on weekends).

   **Usage:** UK; mainly

   **Example:** It will take five working days to process your application.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The part of a day in which work is done; the number of hours one must work per day for a specified wage.

   **Usage:** UK; mainly

   **Example:** Your working day is 8 hours.

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** workday (US; mainly); 9 to 5 — part of the day; nine to five — part of the day; weekday — day on which work is done; business day — day on which work is done in legal and official usage
