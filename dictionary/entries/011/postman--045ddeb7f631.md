### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Someone who delivers the post (mail) to, and/or collects the post from, residential or commercial addresses, or from public mailboxes.

   **Usage:** Commonwealth; Ireland; UK

   **Source synonyms:** mailman; mail carrier; letter carrier; postal carrier; mailperson; postperson

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One of the two most experienced barristers in the Court of Exchequer, who have precedence in motions, so called from the place where he sat, the other of the two being the tubman.

   **Usage:** England; historical
