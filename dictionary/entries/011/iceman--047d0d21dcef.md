## Noun

### Forms

- icemen — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who trades in ice; a person employed to deliver block ice to those lacking electric refrigeration.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A man who is skilled in travelling upon ice, as among glaciers.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A human figure carved out of ice.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An ancient human male mummified in ice.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An assassin.

   **Usage:** slang

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. One who is cool under pressure.

   **Usage:** slang

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A man in attendance at a frozen pond where skating etc. is going on.

   **Usage:** dated
