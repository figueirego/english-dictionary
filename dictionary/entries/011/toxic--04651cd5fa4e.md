### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having a chemical nature that is harmful to health or lethal if consumed or otherwise entering into the body in sufficient quantities.

   **Example:** Tobacco smoke contains many toxic substances.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; pharmacology; sciences; toxicology

   **Source synonyms:** poisonous; venomous

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Appearing grossly unwell; characterised by serious, potentially life-threatening compromise in the respiratory, circulatory or other body systems.

   **Example:** The child appeared toxic on arrival at the hospital.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Severely negative or harmful.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** a toxic environment that promoted bullying

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Of a person, hateful or strongly antipathetic.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** It is not good to be around toxic people.
