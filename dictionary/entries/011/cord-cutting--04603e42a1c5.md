## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The cancellation of traditional subscription television services such as cable or satellite, in favor of Internet services.

   **Usage:** informal; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A ritual in which a cord (real or envisioned) is severed to symbolize the conclusion of something unwanted, such as a bad habit or relationship.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; mysticism; occult; philosophy; sciences
