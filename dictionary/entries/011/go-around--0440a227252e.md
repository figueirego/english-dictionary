### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To move or spread from person to person.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The rumor is going around that Mr. X and Ms. Y are having an affair. There's a cough going around.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To visit (a place) or with (somebody).

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** I'm going around to John's house later.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To fight or argue; to obsess over something.

   **Usage:** intransitive; slang

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To be shared with everyone.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** There's plenty of fish to go around.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To perform a go-around maneuver.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** ATC told the flight to go around because another plane was still on the runway.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** aeronautics; aerospace; aviation; business; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see go, around.

   **Example:** Let's make the wheels go around.
