**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Ill will or hatred toward another, accompanied with the desire to unjustifiably irritate, annoy, or thwart; a want to disturb or put out another; mild malice.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** He was so filled with spite for his ex-wife, his brother was afraid of what he might do.

   **Source synonyms:** grudge; rancor
