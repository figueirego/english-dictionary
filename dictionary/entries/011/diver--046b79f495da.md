### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Source synonyms:** urinator (obsolete)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

   **Usage:** Ireland; UK

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

   **Usage:** London; dated
