### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To purchase (something already sold, misplaced, destroyed, or given away).

   **Example:** Investors have already started buying back shares they had sold last year in an effort to increase their profits.

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To be used to purchase (something already sold, misplaced, destroyed, or given away).
