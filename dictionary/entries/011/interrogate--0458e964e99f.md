### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To question or quiz, especially in a thorough or aggressive manner.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The police interrogated the suspect at some length before they let him go.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To query (something); to request information from (something).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to interrogate a database

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To examine (something) critically.

   **Usage:** literary; transitive
