### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A cake whose recipe references lines from the King James Bible as ingredients in encoded form, where the ingredient is a noun found in that verse. The recipe is used to induce biblical learning and rote memorization of this particular edition of the Bible.

   **Usage:** US

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity; cooking; food; lifestyle
