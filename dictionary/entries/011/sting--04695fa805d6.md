### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A bump left on the skin after having been stung.

   **Example:** Look at this nasty hornet sting&#58; it's turned blue!

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A puncture made by an insect or arachnid in an attack, usually including the injection of venom.

   **Example:** She died from a bee sting.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A pointed portion of an insect or arachnid used for attack.

   **Source synonyms:** stinger

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A sharp, localized pain primarily on the epidermis.

   **Example:** That plant will give a little sting if you touch it.

   **Source synonyms:** burn

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A sharp-pointed hollow hair seated on a gland which secretes an acrid fluid, as in nettles.

## Verb · Etymology 2 · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈstɪŋ/ — variety unspecified in the source labels
- IPA: /ˈstɪŋɡ/ — source labels: Midlands; Northern-England

### Forms

- stings — present; singular; third-person
- stinging — participle; present
- stung — past
- stang — dialectal; past; rare
- stung — participle; past

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To hurt, usually by introducing poison or a sharp point, or both.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To puncture with the stinger.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** A mosquito stung me on the arm.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of an insect or arachnid

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To hurt, to be in pain (physically or emotionally).

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive; sometimes

   **Example:** My eyes are stinging from the chopped onions.

   **Example:** My hand stings after knocking on the door so long.

   **Example:** Still, it stung when a slightly older acquaintance asked me why I couldn't do any better.

   **Source synonyms:** smart

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To cause harm or pain to.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** I thought I could park in front of the hotel, but they stung me for five pounds!
