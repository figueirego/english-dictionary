### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To be alive, existent, or present.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive; stative

   **Example:** I'll be around for another hour or so.

   **Example:** This restaurant has been around since 1938.

   **Example:** The article said that dinos were around for about 250 million years.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To be near; to socialize with.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** You're fun to be around.
