## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Education beyond a level viewed as basic, such as any beyond universal government-funded secondary education.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** He pursued various higher education via trade school and community college.

   **Source synonyms:** further education; higher learning; tertiary education; tertiary-level education

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Education beyond a level viewed as basic, such as any beyond universal government-funded secondary education.
   1. Education at a university level or beyond. (Often with more or less connotation of education that leads to higher rank in a hierarchy of social class.)

      **Usage:** uncountable

      **Example:** For some, the goals of their higher education involved deep meaningfulness or the frontiers of knowledge; others aspired merely to elitism. Some were honest enough to admit to themselves that meaning, service, money, and class rank were all among their motives.

      **Source synonyms:** further education; higher learning; tertiary education; tertiary-level education
