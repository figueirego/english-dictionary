### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A place or building, consisting of a stage and seating, in which an audience gathers to watch plays, musical performances, public ceremonies, and so on.

   **Usage:** US; countable

   **Source synonyms:** playhouse

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A region where a particular action takes place; a specific field of action, usually with reference to war.

   **Usage:** US; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** His grandfather was in the Pacific theater during the war.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A lecture theatre.

   **Usage:** US; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An operating theatre or locale for human experimentation.

   **Usage:** US; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** This man is about to die, get him into theater at once!

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A cinema.

   **Usage:** US; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** We sat in the back row of the theater and threw popcorn at the screen.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Drama or performance as a profession or art form.

   **Usage:** US; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** I worked in theater for twenty-five years.

   **Source synonyms:** fabulous invalid

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Any place rising by steps like the seats of a theater.

   **Usage:** US; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A conspicuous but unproductive display of action.

   **Usage:** US; countable; derogatory; figuratively; uncountable; often following a noun used attributively

   **Example:** The Senate confirmation hearings were just theater.

   **Example:** security theater; hygiene theater
