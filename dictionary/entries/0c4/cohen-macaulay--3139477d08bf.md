## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Such that its depth is equal to its Krull dimension.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; commutative algebra

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a finite module over a noetherian local ring

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Cohen-Macaulay as a module over itself.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; commutative algebra

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a noetherian local ring

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Such that all localizations of M at maximal ideals contained in the support of M are either Cohen-Macaulay or trivial.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; commutative algebra

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a module M over a noetherian ring

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Cohen-Macaulay as a module over itself.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; commutative algebra

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a noetherian ring
