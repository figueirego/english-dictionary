## Adverb

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used to make a suggestion.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** What about going to a restaurant tonight?

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used to ask someone to consider something or someone that they have apparently not considered.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** You're obviously having a good time, but what about me?

   **Example:** Move to another country? What about where we’re going to live?

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used to repeat a question but referring to a different subject or object.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** “Is John coming tomorrow?” “Yes.” “What about David?”

   **Example:** “Do you like football?” “No.” “What about basketball?”

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Used to inquire about the purpose of someone introducing a subject to the speaker.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** “Do you remember John Smith from work?” “Yeah, what about him?” “Oh, nothing. I just ran into him today.”

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Used to express approval of an occurrence or a result.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** What about the second goal!

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** how about — making a suggestion; how’s about — making a suggestion; what of — asking for consideration; how about — asking for consideration
