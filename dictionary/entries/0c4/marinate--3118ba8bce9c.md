### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To allow a sauce or flavoring mixture to absorb into something; to steep or soak something in a marinade to flavor or prepare it for cooking.

   **Usage:** ergative; transitive

   **Example:** You'll get a better flavour from the chicken if you marinate it first.

   **Example:** After the chicken has marinated for two hours, discard the remaining marinade.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To allow a substance to be absorbed into the hair or scalp.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of a substance, to absorb into the hair or scalp.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Of ideas or feelings, to mentally develop over time.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Especially of a haircut, to settle in and for one to get used to it.

   **Usage:** figuratively; informal

   **Example:** It's not a bad haircut! You just got to let it marinate!
