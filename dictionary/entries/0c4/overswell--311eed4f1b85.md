**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To swell or rise above (something, especially the rim of a container, the sides of something hollow, etc.).

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** In some years the river overswells its banks, causing widespread flooding.

   **Source synonyms:** overflow; spill over
