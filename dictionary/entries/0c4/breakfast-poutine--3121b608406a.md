## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A hot breakfast dish that is a variation of poutine. It is composed of pan-fried cubed potatoes and cheese curds, smothered in hollandaise sauce and topped with a fried egg, sometimes with breakfast sausage medallions, diced ham, and/or bacon bits.

   **Usage:** uncountable
