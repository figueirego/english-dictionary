### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person presiding over a meeting, sometimes especially a man. (Compare chairwoman, chairperson, chair.)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The head of a corporate or governmental board of directors, a committee, or other formal entity.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
