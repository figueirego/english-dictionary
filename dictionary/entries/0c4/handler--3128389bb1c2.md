### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who handles something (especially manually) or someone.

   **Usage:** literally

   **Example:** The handler of a weapon gets a symbolic sensation of deadly power.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A controller, trainer, someone who handles a specified thing, animal or person (especially a prizefighter).

   **Example:** The spy's handler told him to approach the subject by posing as a dog handler.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An advisor or manager providing guidance to a person occupying public office or a prominent position.

   **Example:** The governor received guidance on how to best disseminate key information to the public from her handler.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A subroutine that handles a particular situation such as an event or exception.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; programming; sciences
