## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of several diseases notably found among miners, either formerly or still so.
   1. Nystagmus. (Caused among miners by prolonged eye strain in low-light conditions; still prevalent but no longer especially among miners.)

      **Usage:** dated; historical; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of several diseases notably found among miners, either formerly or still so.
   1. Miner's worm&#58; hookworm. (Still prevalent but no longer especially among miners.)

      **Usage:** dated; historical; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of several diseases notably found among miners, either formerly or still so.
   1. Miner's lung&#58; black lung. (Still prevalent but usually not called by this name anymore.)

      **Usage:** dated; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any of several diseases notably found among miners, either formerly or still so.
   1. Miner's phthisis&#58; silicosis. (Still prevalent but usually not called by this name anymore.)

      **Usage:** dated; uncountable
