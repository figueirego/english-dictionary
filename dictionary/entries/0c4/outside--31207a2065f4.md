### Forms

- owtside — alternative; obsolete

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The part of something that faces out; the outer surface.

   **Example:** He's repainting the outside of his house.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The external appearance of someone or something.

   **Example:** Her outside was stern, but inside was a heart of gold.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The space beyond some limit or boundary.

   **Example:** Viewed from the outside, the building seemed unremarkable.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The furthest limit, as to number, quantity, extent, etc.

   **Example:** It may last a week at the outside.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The part of a road towards the central division&#58; towards the right if one drives on the left, or towards the left if one drives on the right.

   **Example:** On a motorway, you should always overtake other vehicles on the outside.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The side of a curved road, racetrack etc. that has the longer arc length; the side of a racetrack furthest from the interior of the course or some other point of reference.

   **Example:** On the final bend, the second-place car tried to go around the outside of the leader but spun off into the barrier.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The outer part of the sea, away from the peak of a wave.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports; surfing

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A passenger riding on the outside of a coach or carriage.

   **Usage:** UK; colloquial; dated
