### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The brother or brother-in-law of one’s parent.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The male cousin of one’s parent.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used as a fictive kinship title for a close male friend of one's parent or parents.

   **Usage:** endearing

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Used as a title for the male companion to one's (usually unmarried) parent.

   **Usage:** euphemistic

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A source of advice, encouragement, or help.

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A pawnbroker.

   **Usage:** UK; dated; informal

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. An affectionate term for a man of an older generation than oneself, especially a friend of one's parents, by means of fictive kin.

   **Usage:** especially; parts of UK and South Asia

   **Source context (verbatim):** in the Southern US

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. An older African-American male.

   **Usage:** Southern-US; archaic; slang

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. Any middle-aged or elderly man older than the speaker and/or listener.

   **Usage:** Brunei; Malaysia; Myanmar; Singapore; informal
