### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To become misty.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To become coated with condensation; to fog up.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The bathroom mirror misted up when he opened the shower door.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To become teary.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

   **Example:** Years later, the song we played at our wedding still made me mist up.
