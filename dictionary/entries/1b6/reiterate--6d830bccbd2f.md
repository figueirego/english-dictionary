**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Let me reiterate my opinion.

   **Source synonyms:** repeat

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To say (something) for a second time, but word it differently.

   **Usage:** rare; transitive

   **Example:** Was I hard to understand? Sorry, I'll try to reiterate.

   **Source synonyms:** rephrase
