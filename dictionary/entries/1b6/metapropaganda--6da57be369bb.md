## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A type of propaganda that seeks to influence an audience by either persuading or dissuading them about other given propaganda.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Counterpropaganda that seeks to characterize an opposing propaganda as a false message is a form of metapropaganda.
