### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An inspection of an object by smelling it, as for cleanliness of clothing or freshness of food.

   **Source synonyms:** nose test; smell test

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An inspection of an object by smelling it, as for cleanliness of clothing or freshness of food.
   1. An informal check of whether an idea or proposal is credible, ethical, etc., using one's common sense or sense of propriety.

      **Usage:** figuratively

      **Example:** But does it pass the sniff test?

      **Source synonyms:** nose test; smell test; nose test; smell test; reality check; sanity check; sanity test

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A medical procedure involving a person taking quick, short sniffs which cause contraction of the diaphragm during a fluoroscopy, enabling abnormalities of the diaphragm to be detected; diaphragm fluoroscopy.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences
