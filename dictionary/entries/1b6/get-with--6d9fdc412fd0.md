### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To become intimate with; to kiss and cuddle with; to have sex with.

   **Usage:** informal; transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To be in the company of; to accompany.

   **Usage:** informal; obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To align oneself with.

   **Example:** We have to get with the times or we'll be left behind.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. to contact; make contact with

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** I'll get with you tomorrow on what we're going to do about the party.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To enjoy oneself or be satisfied regarding (something); to understand or accept (something).

   **Example:** At first I was awkward, but eventually I got with the music.

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To be impregnated.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The stallion got with foal three out of four mares.
