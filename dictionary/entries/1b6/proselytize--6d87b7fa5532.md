### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To advertise one’s religious beliefs; to convert (someone) to one’s own faith or religious movement or encourage them to do so.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive; broadly

   **Example:** It is illegal to proselytize \[children\] in some countries

   **Source synonyms:** evangelize; convert

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To advertise one’s religious beliefs; to convert (someone) to one’s own faith or religious movement or encourage them to do so.
   1. To coerce into religious conversion.

      **Usage:** ambitransitive; broadly; strictly

      **Source synonyms:** evangelize; convert; convert

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To advertise a non-religious belief, way of living, cause, point of view, (scientific) hypothesis, social or other position, political party, or other organization; to convince someone to join such a cause or organization or support such a position; to recruit someone.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive; broadly

   **Example:** He has strong political views, and an annoying habit of proselytizing at parties.

   **Source synonyms:** advocate; back; endorse; peddle; recruit
