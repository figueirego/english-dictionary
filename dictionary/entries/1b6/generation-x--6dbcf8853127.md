**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Usage:** Australia; Ireland; New-Zealand; UK; US; originally

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The generation of people born after the baby boom that followed World War II, especially those born from 1965 to 1980, sometimes characterized as cynical, disaffected, lacking direction in life, and unwilling to take part fully in society.

   **Usage:** Australia; Ireland; New-Zealand; UK; US; specifically
