### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To alter so as to make false; especially when done with intent to deceive.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to falsify a record or document

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To misrepresent.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To counterfeit; to forge.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to falsify money

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To prove to be false.

   **Usage:** transitive; otherwise archaic

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To show (an item of charge inserted in an account) to be wrong.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** accounting; business; finance

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To baffle or escape.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To violate; to break by falsehood.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

   **Example:** to falsify one's faith or word
