**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To bend over deeply at the waist.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** doubled over in pain

   **Source synonyms:** double up; buckle over

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** transitive; usually; fabric

   **Example:** He doubled over the sheet of paper and wrote something on the back.
