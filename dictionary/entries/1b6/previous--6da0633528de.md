### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

   **Usage:** UK; countable; informal; uncountable

   **Example:** It turned out the shoplifter had a lot of previous.

   **Source synonyms:** form

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

   **Usage:** UK; countable; informal; uncountable

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

   **Usage:** UK; countable; informal; uncountable

   **Example:** The two players refused to shake hands — there's a bit of previous there.
