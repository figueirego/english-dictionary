## Suffix

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used to form an adjective meaning "possession of or relation to" from nouns ending in -oma or -omata.

   **Usage:** morpheme

   **Example:** carcinoma + -omatous → carcinomatous (“in relation to carcinomas”)

   **Example:** glaucoma + -omatous → glaucomatous (“in relation to glaucoma”)

   **Example:** scotoma + -omatous → scotomatous (“in relation to scotomata”)
