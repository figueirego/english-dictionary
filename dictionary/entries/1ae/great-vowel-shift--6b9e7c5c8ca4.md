### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A major change in the pronunciation of the English language, affecting the sounds of long vowels, that took place in England between 1350 and 1700.

   **Example:** Because English spelling was becoming standardized in the 15th and 16th centuries, the Great Vowel Shift is responsible for many of the peculiarities of English spelling.
