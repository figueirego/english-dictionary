### Senses

   **Example:** Mary danced into the house.

   **Example:** Pour the wine into the decanter.

   **Example:** The nomads shave intricate designs into their camels' fur.

   **Example:** She eased her enormous frame into the chair.

2. To or towards the region of.

   **Example:** We left the house and walked into the street.

   **Example:** The eagle flew off into the wide blue sky.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To the condition or state.

   **Example:** They lulled us into thinking we still had time.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Against, especially with force or violence.

   **Example:** The car crashed into the tree.

   **Example:** I wasn't careful, and walked into a wall.

   **Example:** My boss surprised me into admitting I'd been leaving work early lately.

   **Example:** Hundreds of readers were tricked into sending them money.

5. Indicates transition into another form or substance.

   **Example:** I carved the piece of driftwood into a sculpture of a whale.

   **Example:** Right before our eyes, Jake turned into a wolf!

   **Example:** My friend left a jack-o-lantern-themed eraser on the rear deck of her car since last October, and it calcified into a sorry, pallid, sun-bleached stone.
