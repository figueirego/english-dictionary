### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A small comma-type interval between two musical notes, equal to the frequency ratio 81&#58;80 (= 1.0125) or around 21.51 cents. It often manifests itself as the difference between the Pythagorean major third of 81&#58;64 and the classic major third of 5&#58;4, between the Pythagorean minor third of 32&#58;27 and the classic minor third of 6&#58;5, and between the "major whole tone" of 9&#58;8 and "minor whole tone" of 10&#58;9.
