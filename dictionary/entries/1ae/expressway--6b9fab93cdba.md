### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A divided highway, especially one whose intersections and direct access to adjacent properties have been eliminated.

   **Usage:** US; regional

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Synonym of freeway.

   **Usage:** Canada; Malaysia; Philippines; Singapore; South-Korea; parts of the US

   **Source synonyms:** freeway (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A road built for high speed traffic, but not up to motorway standards or designated a motorway.

   **Usage:** New-Zealand; UK
