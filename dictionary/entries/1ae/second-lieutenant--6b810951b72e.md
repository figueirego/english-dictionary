### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The lowest rank of a commissioned army officer, below a lieutenant.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The lowest rank of a commissioned officer in the United States Army, United States Air Force, or United States Marine Corps, below a first lieutenant. The rank of second lieutenant is equivalent to the naval rank of ensign.
