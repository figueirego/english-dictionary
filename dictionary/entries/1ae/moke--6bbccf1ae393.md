### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A donkey.

   **Usage:** colloquial; dialectal

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The mesh of a net, or of anything resembling a net.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A black person.

   **Usage:** US; archaic; derogatory; ethnic; slang; slur

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A performer, such as a minstrel, who plays on several musical instruments.

   **Usage:** dated; theatrical slang

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A stupid person; a dolt.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. British small utility vehicle (styled "MOKE").

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A mixture of cannabis and tobacco, especially smoked from a bong or water pipe.

   **Usage:** US; slang
