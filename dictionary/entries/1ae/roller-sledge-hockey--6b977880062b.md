## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A parasport, an adapted form of roller hockey, and a floor version of (ice) sledge hockey, where disabled athletes rid atop roller sledges (roller sleds), and propel themselves with a pair of roller sledge hockey sticks, that function as canes/"walking sticks" and hockey sticks. Various variants of roller sledge hockey use different configurations of wheels.

   **Usage:** uncountable; field hockey or ice hockey
