- IPA: /list/ — source labels: General-American

### Senses

<a id="r3-s1"></a>

**Source sense R3.S1**

1. In a degree below all others; in the lowest or smallest degree.

   **Usage:** negative; superlative

   **Example:** My best routine was the one I had practised the least.

   **Example:** I don’t much like housework, and doing the dishes least of all.

   **Example:** That decision rewards those who least deserve it.
