### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A place or part of a place, especially one that is small, remote, or tedious to access.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** Everyone helped out to clean every nook and cranny of the house.

   **Example:** Cleaning this equipment really isn't hard except that getting into all the nooks and crannies is time-consuming.

   **Source context (verbatim):** with "any" or "every"
