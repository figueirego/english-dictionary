<a id="r1-s2"></a>

2. Having a visible or identifying mark.
   1. Having a secret mark on the back for cheating.

      **Source context (verbatim):** of a playing card

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Clearly evident; noticeable; conspicuous.

   **Example:** The eighth century BC saw a marked increase in the general wealth of Cyprus.

   **Source synonyms:** manifest; noticeable; obtrusive; palpable; patent; noted

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Distinguished by a positive feature.

   **Usage:** phoneme; form

   **Example:** "Young" is the marked element of the old/young pair, since the usual way of asking someone's age is "How old are you?".

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a word

   **Source synonyms:** singled out; targeted; positive

   **Source antonyms:** unmarked

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Singled out; suspicious; treated with hostility; the object of vengeance.

   **Example:** Former police officer Johnnie suddenly found himself a marked man when his fiancée fell for a ruthless gangster.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. In police livery, as opposed to unmarked. (of a police vehicle)
