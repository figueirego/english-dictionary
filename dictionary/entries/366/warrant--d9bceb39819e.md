### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To protect, keep safe (from danger).

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To give (someone) an assurance or guarantee (of something); also, with a double object&#58; to guarantee (someone something).

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To guarantee (something) to be (of a specified quality, value, etc.).

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To guarantee as being true; (colloquial) to believe strongly.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** That tree is going to fall, I’ll warrant.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To authorize; to give (someone) sanction or warrant (to do something).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I am warranted to search these premises fully.

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To justify; to give grounds for.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Circumstances arose that warranted the use of lethal force.
