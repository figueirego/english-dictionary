## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who waters plants.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One who waters down, or dilutes, something.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A device used for watering.

   **Example:** She installed an automatic waterer for her garden.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A device from which livestock, poultry, or pets may drink.

   **Example:** Clean the chicks' waterer each time you refill it.
