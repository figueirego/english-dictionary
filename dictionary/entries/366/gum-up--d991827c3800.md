## Verb

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To become gooey or gummy.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Some types of lubricating grease gum up as they age.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To cause to be gooey or gummy, especially with the effect of obstructing the operation of some mechanism or process.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Aging grease had gummed up the record changer.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To make non-functional; to interfere with or put into a state of disorder; to ruin.

   **Usage:** broadly; idiomatic; transitive
