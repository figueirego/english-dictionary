### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A Greek fast food consisting of pieces of meat and sometimes vegetables grilled on a skewer, variously also called kalamaki or kebab or, in sandwich form, gyro or shawarma.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** kalamaki

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A wrapped pancake dish filled with meat, salad and some kind of sauce or dressing, commonly called a kebab.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
