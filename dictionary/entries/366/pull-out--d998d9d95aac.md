### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see pull, out.

   **Example:** I need to pull the splinter out of my hand.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To withdraw; especially of military forces; to retreat.

   **Usage:** also; figuratively; intransitive

   **Example:** The troops pulled out of the conflict.

   **Example:** The mayor pulled out of the race for Senate after numerous opinion polls had him polling at less than 10 percent.

   **Example:** The racehorse pulled out of the Stakes with a hurt foot.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To transition from a dive to level or climbing flight.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** After releasing its bomb, the plane pulled out of its dive.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** aeronautics; aerospace; aviation; business; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

   **Source context (verbatim):** of an aircraft

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To use coitus interruptus as a method of birth control.

   **Usage:** intransitive; literally

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To remove something from a container.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** He pulled his gun out before she had a chance to scream.

   **Source synonyms:** whip out; draw

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To maneuver a vehicle from the side of a road onto the lane.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** When joining a road, you should check for traffic before pulling out.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To draw out or lengthen.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To draw out by dragging or tugging.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To succeed despite long odds.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** Nobody thought they'd win, but the football team pulled out a victory.

   **Example:** They were behind by 29 points in the third quarter, but they pulled it out at the buzzer.
