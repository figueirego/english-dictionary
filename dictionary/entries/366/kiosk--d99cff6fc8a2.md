### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A small enclosed structure, often freestanding, open on one side or with a window, used as a booth to sell newspapers, cigarettes, etc.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A similar but unattended stand for the automatic dispensing of tickets, etc; an e-kiosk.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A public telephone booth.
