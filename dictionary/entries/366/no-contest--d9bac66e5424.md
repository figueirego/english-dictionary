## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

- no contests — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A type of plea in a criminal proceeding where the defendant neither admits nor denies guilt but consents to the imposition of punishment as if guilty.

   **Usage:** US; countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

   **Source synonyms:** nolo contendere

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A clause in a will that imposes a penalty of forfeiture against any beneficiary who challenges the will.

   **Usage:** US; countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The result of a boxing bout, promoted as an exhibition when the sport of boxing was illegal, when a knockout did not occur.

   **Usage:** countable; historical; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** boxing; government; hobbies; lifestyle; martial-arts; military; politics; sports; war

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A bout that the referee cancels early, either due to a serious injury early in the fight or due to a lack of action

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** boxing; government; hobbies; lifestyle; martial-arts; military; politics; sports; war

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

## Interjection · Record 2

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Used to indicate that a competition or comparison is one-sided and so its outcome is too obvious to be interesting.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Source synonyms:** no comparison

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Expresses certainty in a choice between options or preferences.

   **Usage:** emphatic; informal

   **Example:** I'll take a Porsche, no contest!
