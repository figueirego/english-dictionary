## Noun

### Forms

- walkaways — plural
- walk-away — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A case of a person walking away (e.g. an inmate from a facility, a cult member from a community); a person who walks away.

   **Example:** There had been several walkaways from the juvenile center.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An easy victory; a very unequal contest (also used attributively).

   **Usage:** US

   **Example:** The upcoming election is unlikely to be a walkaway for the encumbent.

   **Example:** a walkaway victory; a walkaway winner

   **Source synonyms:** walkover

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A business termination in which the entrepreneur winds up the business with its obligations met.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A property that has been abandoned when the mortgager failed to make their payments, while the mortgagee has abandoned its foreclosure action to avoid the costs associated with ownership.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; real-estate

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. In a negotiation, the terms representing the least a party is willing to accept (also used attributively).

   **Example:** walkaway price; walkaway point

   **Source synonyms:** bottom line

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A vehicle lease under which the lessee is not responsible for the value of the vehicle at the end of the lease period.

   **Usage:** US

   **Source synonyms:** closed-end lease

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Intended to be consumed while walking.

   **Usage:** US; attributive

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a food or drink

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A kind of sweet pastry containing cream cheese.

   **Usage:** US

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A meal break during which food is not provided, and workers are expected to leave the location to eat.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** broadcasting; film; media; television

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. The theft of a suitcase at a hotel through distraction, deception, etc.; a person who engages in this type of theft.

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. An elevated enclosed passage providing access fore and aft from the bridge of a merchant vessel; any similarly elevated walkway.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** literature; media; nautical; publishing; science-fiction; transport

   **Source synonyms:** catwalk

<a id="r1-s12"></a>

**Source sense R1.S12**

12. A type of sea shanty.

   **Usage:** historical; obsolete

   **Source synonyms:** hand-over-hand; stamp-and-go

<a id="r1-s13"></a>

**Source sense R1.S13**

13. A person who forgets to take their change after purchasing a ticket at the circus; the money abandoned in this way.

   **Usage:** US; obsolete
