### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Working time outside of one's regular hours.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Workers are usually paid extra for working overtime.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The rate of pay, usually higher, for work done outside of or in addition to regular hours.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An extra period of play when a contest has a tie score at the end of regulation.

   **Usage:** US; countable

   **Example:** That last-second shot ties the game 99-99 and sends it to overtime!

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports

   **Source synonyms:** extra time (UK); OT

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Extra laps after the set race length of laps or time, to allow for a green-white-checkered finish.

   **Usage:** US; countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; motor-racing; racing; sports

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

6. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see over, time.
