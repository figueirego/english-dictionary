### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An approving description or a cry of encouragement to someone whose behavior is titillating, such as someone aiming to dress sexily or someone performing in erotic dancing or stripping.

   **Usage:** colloquial; imperative; often

   **Example:** I like the way you work it / no diggity / I got to bag it up
