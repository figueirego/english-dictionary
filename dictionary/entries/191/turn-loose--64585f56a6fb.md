### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To release or let go; to permit (someone or something) to roam freely or to act freely.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** Don't turn the dog loose in the yard before you check whether the gate is closed.

   **Example:** He's so creative, and I'd love to turn him loose in my garden sometime and see what he dreams up.

   **Example:** Phil - we're turning you loose on this hospital project. Please start right away.
