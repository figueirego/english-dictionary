### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To avoid or shirk, either by failing to perform, or by performing in a grossly insufficient, negligent, or superficial manner.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** Faced with the prospect of cooking for himself, his first thought was to cop out and order a pizza.

   **Source synonyms:** goof off; weasel out; blow off (US; idiomatic); cop out (idiomatic); dog; doss; duck; flinch; goldbrick (dated); goof off (US); lollygag (US); malinger; procrastinate; shirk; shrink; slack off; soldier; swing the lead (idiomatic); weasel out (idiomatic)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To plead guilty and ask for mercy.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To blame; to assign blame to; to get into trouble.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; slang

   **Example:** His lawyer copped him out on just one felony charge.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To remove from a situation or crowd, such as for arrest or punishment.

   **Usage:** US; dated; idiomatic

   **Example:** Somebody should have copped him out last night.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To leave a gang.

   **Example:** I can't believe you copped out on us.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To abandon, let down or betray (someone).

   **Usage:** idiomatic; slang

   **Example:** She thought I'd copped out on her.

   **Source synonyms:** double-cross; sell out; stab in the back; assfuck (Canada; US; vulgar); betray; burn (slang); cop out (idiomatic; slang); cross out (slang); cross up (Canada; US); double-cross; do someone dirty; do the dirty on (Australia; UK; idiomatic); false (obsolete); go back on; knife; let down (idiomatic); play someone false; quisle (rare); renegade (dated); sell; sell the pass (Ireland; idiomatic); sell down the river (idiomatic); sell down (idiomatic); sell out; stab in the back; swike (dialectal; obsolete); traitorize; turncoat

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To win (someone) over; to get on (someone's) good side.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; slang; transitive
