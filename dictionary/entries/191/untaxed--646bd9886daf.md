## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Not subject to being taxed.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** During August, clothes costing less than $75 are untaxed, to try to help the poor to buy clothes and the merchants to make money.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Not having had the required taxes paid on it.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Bootleggers make good money selling untaxed cigarettes, until they are caught.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Not tired or strained, working well within capacity.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The motor was untaxed by the steep hill because the vehicle was so lightly loaded.
