### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The final stage of a game of chess, when there are few pieces left.

   **Example:** Rooks become much more important in the endgame.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** board-games; chess; games

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The final stage of a game of bridge, when there are few cards left.

   **Example:** You can't really use squeeze plays until the endgame.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** bridge; games

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The final stage of any game.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** Being stuck with two V tiles can make for an awkward Scrabble endgame.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The final stage of an extended process or course of events, especially with the implication of the imminent realization of a masterful strategy or plan.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** What's his endgame, do you think?

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The gameplay available in a massively multiplayer online role-playing game for players who have completed all of the preset challenges.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** video-games

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The thematic element of an advanced cryptic crossword puzzle, typically solved after completing the grid.

   **Usage:** crosswording
