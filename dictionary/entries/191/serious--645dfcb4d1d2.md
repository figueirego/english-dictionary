### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Without humor or expression of happiness; grave in manner or disposition.

   **Example:** deadly serious

   **Example:** It was a surprise to see the captain, who had always seemed so serious, laugh so heartily.

   **Source synonyms:** earnest; solemn

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Important; weighty; not insignificant.

   **Example:** This is a serious problem. We'll need our best experts.

   **Source synonyms:** desperate

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Really intending what is said (or planned, etc); in earnest; not jocular or deceiving.

   **Example:** After all these years, we're finally getting serious attention.

   **Example:** He says he wants to buy the team, but is he serious?

   **Example:** I was being deadly serious when I made that suggestion.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Committed.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a relationship
