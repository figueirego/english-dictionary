### Senses

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; uncountable

2. The act of constructing, construction, fabrication.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The structure of anything, the manner in which the parts of a thing are united; workmanship, texture, make.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; uncountable

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The physical material of a building.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** This church dates back to the 11th century, though the great majority of its fabric is fifteenth century or later.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The framework underlying a structure.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

   **Example:** the fabric of our lives

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A material made of fibers; especially, a woven one.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** cotton fabric

   **Source synonyms:** material; textile; cloth

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The texture of a cloth.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
