## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The difference (sometimes defined to be the absolute difference) between the measured or inferred value of a quantity and its true value.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Shortest distance between the center of impact or the center of burst of a group of shots, and the point of impact or burst of a single shot within the group.

   **Usage:** military gunnery

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Error of a sight consisting of its error in relation to a master service sight with which it is tested, including the known error of the master service sight.

   **Usage:** military gunnery
