### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A sound in speech which has some qualities of a consonant and some qualities of a vowel.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; phonetics; phonology; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** glide; semiconsonant

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A letter which represents a semivowel sound, such as w or y in English.
