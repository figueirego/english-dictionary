### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Collectively, peoples living nomadic, pastoral, hunter-gatherer or other ways of life outside the modern industrial norm.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Synonym of Túwaquachi.

   **Usage:** Hopi religion

   **Source synonyms:** Túwaquachi (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A notional standard of living or level of development lower than that in the stereotyped Third World.

   **Usage:** attributive; derogatory; informal
