### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of or relating to a continent or continents.

   **Example:** continental drift

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of the mainland, as opposed to an island offshore.

   **Example:** continental Europe

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Relating to, or characteristic of, continental Europe.

   **Example:** continental breakfast

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Of or relating to the confederated colonies collectively, in the time of the Revolutionary War.

   **Usage:** US; historical

   **Example:** continental money
