### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any injury to the body of a living thing.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** mild trauma; major trauma

   **Source synonyms:** insult; injury; wound; scath; scathe; lesion; dere

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any injury to the body of a living thing.
   1. Serious injury, resulting from predation, violence, an accident such as a fall, a vehicle crash, etc.

      **Usage:** countable; specifically; uncountable; usually

      **Example:** We're not talking about paper cuts or sprains here — we're talking about trauma.

      **Source synonyms:** insult; injury; wound; scath; scathe; lesion; dere

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An emotional wound leading to psychological injury.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** What these patients eventually realized was that they all had trauma that had fed into their addictions.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An event that causes great distress.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The earthquake here 14 years ago was a national trauma that still affects the economy today.
