### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A system of government that substitutes for the reign of a king or queen when that king or queen becomes unable to rule.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The country was governed by a regency while the king was still a minor.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The time during which a regent is in power.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The prince served as regent during the regency.

   **Example:** during the regency

   **Example:** period of regency

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An administrative division ranking below a province in Indonesia.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
