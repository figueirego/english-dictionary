### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To scrape completely; cover all over with scrapes.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He should not have used that old dirty rag. Look how it scraped up his paint job.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. to collect together by scraping

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. to gather together something needed (especially money)

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** If I empty all my pockets, search around the house, and ask a few friends for a loan, I should be able to scrape up enough money to buy that new suit.
