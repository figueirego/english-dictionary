### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To purposely avoid interaction with others; to be introverted.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** She preferred to keep to herself after her husband died.

   **Source synonyms:** keep oneself to oneself

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To refrain from telling or giving; to keep secret, or refuse to share.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** keep oneself to oneself
