### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The outward appearance or form of a person or thing.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source synonyms:** veneer

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The outward appearance or form of a person or thing.
   1. Followed by of&#58; the outward appearance of a person or thing when regarded as similar to that of another person or thing.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source synonyms:** veneer

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The outward appearance or form of a person or thing.
   1. Followed by of&#58; the outward appearance of a person or thing which is different from what the person or thing actually is; also, an outward appearance of a thing which does not actually exist.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source synonyms:** veneer

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Followed by of&#58; a person or thing that is seen; an apparition, a vision.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Followed by of&#58; a person or thing that looks similar to another person or thing; a likeness.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source synonyms:** portrait

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Followed by of&#58; a bare or mere appearance of something.

   **Usage:** countable; always in the negative

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A person's non-verbal behaviour or demeanour which shows their feelings, thoughts, etc., or which is faked to hide such true feelings, thoughts, etc.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. In the form make semblance&#58; an act of appearing; an appearance, a manifestation; also, a false appearance, a pretence.

   **Usage:** countable; dated

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. The quality or state of being similar; likeness, resemblance, similarity.

   **Usage:** archaic; uncountable

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. The chance of something happening; likelihood, probability.

   **Usage:** obsolete; uncountable
