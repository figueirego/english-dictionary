### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A load of goods that is transported by any method (not just by ship).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** We're expecting another shipment of oranges tomorrow.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The act of transporting goods.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Shipment of hazardous waste on this route is strictly prohibited.
