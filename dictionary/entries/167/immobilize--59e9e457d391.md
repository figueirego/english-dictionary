### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To render motionless; to stop moving or stop from moving.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** It is best to immobilize the injury until a doctor can examine it.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To render incapable of action.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To modify a surface such that things will not stick to it

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To tie up a capital&#58; make a capital investment that makes that capital unavailable.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Don't immobilize your capital in aging accounts.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance
