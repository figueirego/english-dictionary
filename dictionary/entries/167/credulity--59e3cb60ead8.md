### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A willingness to believe in someone or something in the absence of reasonable proof; credulousness.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** a tall story that strains credulity

   **Source synonyms:** credulosity; credulousness; gullibility; naivety

   **Source antonyms:** skepticism; doubt; wariness; [suspicion](../15d/suspicion--5779368d6e88.md)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Faith, credence; acceptance or maintenance of a belief.

   **Usage:** countable; nonstandard; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** belief; faith; credence

   **Source antonyms:** incredulity
