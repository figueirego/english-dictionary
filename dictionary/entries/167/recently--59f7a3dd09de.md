### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In the recent past.
   1. a short while ago.

      **Example:** My recently published book about the life stories of recently-arrived immigrants.

      **Source context (verbatim):** referring to a point of time in the recent past

      **Source synonyms:** newly; lately; freshly

      **Source antonyms:** long ago; long since

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In the recent past.
   1. in recent times.

      **Example:** I haven't been sleeping well recently.

      **Source context (verbatim):** referring to a time period starting in the recent past and including the present

      **Source synonyms:** newly; lately; freshly

      **Source antonyms:** long ago; long since
