### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To grant as a possession; to bestow.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The college has conferred an honorary degree upon the visiting Prime Minister.

   **Source synonyms:** afford

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To talk together, to consult, discuss; to collogue.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** They were in a huddle, conferring about something.

   **Source synonyms:** palaver

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To compare.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To bring together; to collect, gather.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To contribute; to conduce.

   **Usage:** obsolete
