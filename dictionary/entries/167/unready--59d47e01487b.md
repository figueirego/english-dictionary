### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Not ready.
   1. Not yet mature; not yet ripe.

      **Example:** As for our apple supply, this year's crop is unready, and last year's stored crop is now exhausted, so we're temporarily embarrassed, I'm afraid.

      **Source synonyms:** immature; unripe

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Not ready.
   1. Not yet prepared.

      **Example:** The junior varsity team is unready for a serious competition; a lot of training remains to be done.

      **Source synonyms:** unprepared
