### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A (Japanese) hot spring.

   **Example:** The hottest springs in Japan aren't in the north but at the Obama Onsen outside Nagasaki.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A Japanese-style resort hotel at a natural hot spring, offering on-site bathing.

   **Example:** We stayed at a traditional onsen with tatami, yukata, and kaiseki at Onyang outside Seoul.
