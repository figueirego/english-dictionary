### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see hit, too, close, to, home.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To occur too close for comfort; to happen dangerously or uncomfortably near.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I just barely managed to keep my job during the layoff – it really hit too close to home.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To be relatable in a negative way; to cause negative emotions by being similar or reminding; to be uncomfortably alike one's personal negative experiences.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** As a veteran, the war film I watched yesterday hit too close to home, making me remember many awful things.
