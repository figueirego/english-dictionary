## Adjective

### Forms

- postwar — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Pertaining to a period of time immediately following the end of a war; where there is a cessation of conflict.
   1. After the most recent or significant war in a culture's history.

      **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Pertaining to a period of time immediately following the end of a war; where there is a cessation of conflict.
   1. After the end of World War II in 1945.

      **Usage:** not-comparable
