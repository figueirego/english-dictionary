### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To give life or spirit to; to revive or animate.

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** inspirit; invigorate; quicken; vitalise; vivify

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To make more lively, cheerful or interesting.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The game was much enlivened when both teams scored within five minutes of each other.

   **Source synonyms:** cheer up; liven; liven up; quicken
