## Verb · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈdɒk.ɪt/ — source labels: UK
- IPA: /ˈdɑ.kɪt/ — source labels: Canada; General-American
- IPA: /ˈdɔk.ɪt/ — source labels: Australia

### Forms

- dockets — present; singular; third-person
- docketing — participle; present
- docketed — participle; past
- docketed — past
- docquet — alternative; archaic

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To enter or inscribe in a docket, or list of causes for trial.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To label a parcel, etc.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to docket goods

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To make a brief abstract of (a writing) and endorse it on the back of the paper, or to endorse the title or contents on the back of; to summarize.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to docket letters and papers

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To make a brief abstract of and inscribe in a book.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** judgments regularly docketed
