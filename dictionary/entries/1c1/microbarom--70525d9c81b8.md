### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A kind of atmospheric infrasonic wave generated in marine storms by a non-linear interaction of ocean surface waves with the atmosphere.

   **Usage:** acoustics

   **Example:** Microbaroms are a significant noise source that can potentially interfere with the detection of infrasound from nuclear explosions.
