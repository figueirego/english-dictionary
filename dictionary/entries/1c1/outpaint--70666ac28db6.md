**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To paint more or better than; to surpass in painting.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to outpaint Picasso

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To extend an image by guessing how it might look beyond its borders; to perform the inverse of a crop (chiefly in the context of AI image models).

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive
