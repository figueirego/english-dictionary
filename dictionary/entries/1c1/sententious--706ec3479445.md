## Adjective

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Using as few words as possible; pithy and concise.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Tending to the frequent use aphorisms or maxims; especially, in a manner that is affected, trite or self-righteous.

   **Example:** His sententious remarks quickly bored the audience.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Full of meaning.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Relating to a sentence; sentential.

   **Usage:** obsolete
