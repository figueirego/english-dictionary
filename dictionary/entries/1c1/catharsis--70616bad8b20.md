### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A release of emotional tension after an overwhelming vicarious experience, resulting in the purging or purification of the emotions, as through watching a dramatic production (especially a tragedy).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** broadcasting; drama; dramaturgy; entertainment; film; lifestyle; media; television; theater

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any release of emotional tension to the same effect, more widely.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A purification or cleansing, especially emotional.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A therapeutic technique to relieve tension by reestablishing the association of an emotion with the memory or idea of the event that first caused it, and then eliminating it by complete expression (called the abreaction).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; psychology; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A purging of the digestive system.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences
