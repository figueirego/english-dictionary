## Noun

### Forms

- honey buzzards — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of certain birds of prey in family Accipitridae
   1. All species of genus Pernis
      1. European honey buzzard (Pernis apivorus), a summer migrant to most of Europe and western Asia, wintering in tropical Africa

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of certain birds of prey in family Accipitridae
   1. All species of genus Pernis
      1. crested honey buzzard (Pernis ptilorhynchus), wintering in tropical south east Asia, a summer migrant to Siberia

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of certain birds of prey in family Accipitridae
   1. All species of genus Pernis
      1. barred honey buzzard (Pernis celebensis), found in Indonesia and the Philippines

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any of certain birds of prey in family Accipitridae
   1. All species of genus Henicopernis
      1. black honey buzzard (Henicopernis infuscatus), endemic to Papua New Guinea

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Any of certain birds of prey in family Accipitridae
   1. All species of genus Henicopernis
      1. long-tailed honey buzzard (Henicopernis longicauda), found in Indonesia and Papua New Guinea
