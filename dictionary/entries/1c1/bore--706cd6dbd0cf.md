### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To inspire boredom in somebody.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to bore someone to death

   **Example:** Reading books really bores me; films are much more exciting.

   **Source antonyms:** interest

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To make a hole through something.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To make a hole with, or as if with, a boring instrument; to cut a circular hole by the rotary motion of a tool.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** to bore for water or oil

   **Example:** An insect bores into a tree.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To form or enlarge (something) by means of a boring instrument or apparatus.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to bore a steam cylinder or a gun barrel; to bore a hole

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To make (a passage) by laborious effort, as in boring; to force a narrow and difficult passage through.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to bore one’s way through a crowd

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To be pierced or penetrated by an instrument that cuts as it turns.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** This timber does not bore well.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To glare (as if to drill a hole with the eyes).

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Their eyes bore into my back.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To push or drive (a boxer into the ropes, a boat out of its course, etc.).

   **Usage:** slang; transitive

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To push forward in a certain direction with laborious effort.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. To fool; to trick.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

   **Usage:** Australia

   **Source synonyms:** artesian bore; artesian well

   1. The diameter of a cylinder in a piston engine or compressor.

      **Usage:** especially

      **Example:** The bore times the stroke times the number of cylinders equals displacement.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. The tunnel inside of a gun's barrel through which the bullet travels when fired, or (by extension) its diameter.

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. A tool, such as an auger, for making a hole by boring.

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

**Source sense R2.S7**

7. A capped well drilled to tap artesian water.

<a id="r2-s8"></a>

**Source sense R2.S8**

8. A capped well drilled to tap artesian water.
   1. The place where such a well exists.

<a id="r2-s9"></a>

**Source sense R2.S9**

9. One who inspires boredom or lack of interest; an uninteresting person.

   **Example:** My neighbour is such a bore when he talks about his coin collection.

<a id="r2-s10"></a>

**Source sense R2.S10**

10. Something dull or uninteresting.

   **Example:** What a bore that movie was! There was no action, and the dialogue was totally uncreative.
