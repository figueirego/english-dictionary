### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To cause to move faster; to quicken the motion of; to add to the speed of.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To quicken the natural or ordinary progression or process of.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to accelerate the growth of a plant, the increase of wealth, etc.

   **Example:** The company plans to accelerate its growth with new marketing strategies.

   **Example:** She had to accelerate her work pace to meet the deadline.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To cause a change of velocity.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To hasten, as the occurrence of an event.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to accelerate our departure

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To enable a student to finish a course of study in less than normal time.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** education

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To become faster; to begin to move more quickly.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The car began to accelerate as soon as the light turned green.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Grow; increase.

   **Usage:** intransitive
