## Phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used to ask someone if they are willing to do something.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** Let's go to the movies tonight, what do you say?

   **Source synonyms:** what say you

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used to remind a child to say a polite expression.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** That man just gave you a lovely birthday gift, young lady; what do you say? ― Thank you!

   **Example:** I want cake! ― What do you say? ― Please!

   **Example:** When Aunt Fiona from Florida gave Jordan some candy, Mom said, "What do you say?" and Jordan replied, "Thank you!"
