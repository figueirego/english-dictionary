### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An appliance designed to protect electrical devices from voltage spikes by limiting the voltage supplied to an electric device by either blocking or by shorting to ground any unwanted voltages above a safe threshold.

   **Source synonyms:** surge suppressor
