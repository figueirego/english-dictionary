### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person unwilling to participate in activities; a curmudgeon or party pooper.

   **Usage:** derogatory; idiomatic

   **Example:** Have a little fun sometimes and don't be such a stick-in-the-mud.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. More generally, one who is slow, old-fashioned, or unprogressive; an old fogey.

   **Usage:** derogatory; idiomatic
