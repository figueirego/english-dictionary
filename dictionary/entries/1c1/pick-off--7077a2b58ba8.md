### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To remove by picking.

   **Usage:** literally

   **Example:** Before you recycle the bottle you need to pick off the label.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To dispose of tasks, obstacles, opponents etc. precisely, one by one.

   **Example:** Let's pick off these issues starting from the top.

   **Example:** England's batsmen were nothing more than sitting ducks waiting to be picked off by these unerringly accurate marksmen.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To dispose of tasks, obstacles, opponents etc. precisely, one by one.
   1. To shoot one by one.

      **Example:** The sniper picked off the incoming police one at a time.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To throw out a runner by tagging them whilst they are not in contact with any of the three bases or home plate.

   **Example:** The pitcher attempted to pick off the runner at first, but he was safe.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; baseball; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

**Source sense R1.S5**
