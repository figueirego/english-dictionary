### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A survey of people, usually statistically analyzed to gauge wider public opinion.

   **Source synonyms:** survey

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A formal vote held in order to ascertain the most popular choice.

   **Example:** The student council had a poll to see what people want served in the cafeteria.

   **Source synonyms:** vote; election

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A polling place (usually as plural, polling places).

   **Example:** The polls close at 8 p.m.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The result of the voting, the total number of votes recorded.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The head, particularly the scalp or pate upon which hair (normally) grows.

   **Usage:** now rare outside veterinary medicine contexts

   **Source synonyms:** scalp

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A mass of people, a mob or muster, considered as a head count.

   **Source context (verbatim):** in extended senses of the above

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The broad or butt end of an axe or a hammer.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. The pollard or European chub, a kind of fish.

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To solicit mock votes from (a person or group).

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To vote at an election.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To register or deposit, as a vote; to elicit or call forth, as votes or voters.

   **Example:** He polled a hundred votes more than his opponent.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To cut off; to remove by clipping, shearing, etc.; to mow or crop.

   **Example:** to poll the hair; to poll wool; to poll grass

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To cut the hair of (a creature).

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

**Source sense R2.S7**

7. To remove the horns of (an animal).

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s8"></a>

**Source sense R2.S8**

8. To remove the top or end of; to clip; to lop.

   **Example:** to poll a tree

<a id="r2-s9"></a>

**Source sense R2.S9**

9. To (repeatedly) request the status of something (such as a computer or printer on a network).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The network hub polled the department’s computers to determine which ones could still respond.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** communication; communications; computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

**Source sense R2.S10**

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source context (verbatim):** with adverb

<a id="r2-s11"></a>

**Source sense R2.S11**

11. To extort from; to plunder; to strip. Especially in conjunction with pill for emphasis.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r2-s12"></a>

**Source sense R2.S12**

12. To impose a tax upon.

<a id="r2-s13"></a>

**Source sense R2.S13**

13. To pay as one's personal tax.

<a id="r2-s14"></a>

**Source sense R2.S14**

14. To enter, as polls or persons, in a list or register; to enroll, especially for purposes of taxation; to enumerate one by one.

<a id="r2-s15"></a>

**Source sense R2.S15**
