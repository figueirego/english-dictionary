### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. That which happens after, that which follows, usually of strongly negative connotation in most contexts, implying a preceding catastrophe.

   **Example:** In contrast to most projections of the aftermath of nuclear war, in this there is no rioting or looting.

   **Source synonyms:** lattermath

   **Source antonyms:** foremath; beforemath

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A second mowing; the grass which grows after the first crop of hay in the same season.

   **Usage:** obsolete

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** agriculture; business; lifestyle

   **Source synonyms:** lattermath; rowen
