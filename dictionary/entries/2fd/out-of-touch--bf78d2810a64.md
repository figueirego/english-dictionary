## Adjective

### Forms

- more out of touch — comparative
- most out of touch — superlative
- outta touch — alternative; US; colloquial

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. No longer maintaining contact or communications.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I had been out of touch with my old friend for a long time when she called.

   **Source synonyms:** incommunicado; in the dark; out of the loop

   **Source antonyms:** in touch

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Disconnected or insulated from the everyday experiences and concerns of ordinary people.

   **Usage:** derogatory; idiomatic

   **Example:** The billionaire, who was once a man of the people, grew increasingly out of touch.

   **Example:** They're so out of touch that all they can do all day is compare yacht sizes.

   **Source synonyms:** out of the loop; out of one's mind

   **Source antonyms:** in touch; well-grounded; grounded; well-rounded

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Old-fashioned, no longer aware of popular culture.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. No longer conversant with crucial basics such as facts, reality, the real world, and sanity; not aware or realistic.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** Did his answer strike you as out of touch with reality?

   **Source synonyms:** out of one's mind

   **Source antonyms:** in touch
