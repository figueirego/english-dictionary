### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The ability or condition of shining with reflected light; sheen, gleam, gloss, sparkle, shine, etc.

   **Usage:** US; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** metallic luster

   **Example:** pearly luster

   **Example:** the diamond’s luster

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The ability or condition of shining with reflected light; sheen, gleam, gloss, sparkle, shine, etc.
   1. The way in which the surface of any particular type of mineral reflects light differently from other minerals, which is helpful in telling minerals apart.

      **Usage:** US; countable; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; geology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Shining light from within, luminosity, brightness, shine.

   **Usage:** US; countable; figuratively; uncountable

   **Example:** the sun’s luster

   **Example:** the luster of the minor stars

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Shining beauty, splendor, attractiveness or attraction.

   **Usage:** US; countable; figuratively; uncountable

   **Example:** After so many years in the same field, the job had lost its luster.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Shining fame, renown, glory.

   **Usage:** US; countable; figuratively; uncountable

   **Example:** After the scandal, the idol lost his luster and could only get work in Vegas.

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Polish, social refinement.

   **Usage:** US; countable; figuratively; uncountable
