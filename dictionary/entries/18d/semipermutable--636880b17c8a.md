## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. That commutes with every subgroup whose order is relatively prime to its own.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Every permutable subgroup of a finite group is semipermutable. The converse, however, is not necessarily true.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** group-theory; mathematics; sciences

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a subgroup
