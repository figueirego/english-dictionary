### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To hold in the hand.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** You can hand-hold many professional cameras, but using a tripod may be a better idea.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To watch or attend unnecessarily closely (as if holding a child's hand to lead it along).

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** I'm sick of having to hand-hold every new person we hire. Why can't we train them first?
