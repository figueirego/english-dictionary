## Adjective · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈklʌmzi/ — source labels: UK; US

### Forms

- clumsier — comparative
- clumsiest — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Awkward, lacking coordination.

   **Example:** He's very clumsy. I wouldn't trust him with carrying the dishes.

   **Source synonyms:** bumbling; bearish; ungainly

   **Source antonyms:** graceful; dexterous

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Not elegant or well-planned, lacking tact or subtlety.

   **Example:** It is a clumsy solution, but it might work for now.

   **Example:** What a clumsy joke

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Awkward or inefficient in use or construction, difficult to handle or manage especially because of shape.

   **Source synonyms:** unwieldy; ungainly
