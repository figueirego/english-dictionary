### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used to seize on a point and highlight its comparative importance, especially as a refutation or negation to a statement made by another speaker.

   **Usage:** colloquial; idiomatic

   **Example:** But that's the thing, see? Like you said, you can't just go spending your money like that.
