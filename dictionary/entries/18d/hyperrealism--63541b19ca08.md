### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A style in art that attempts to reproduce highly realistic graphic representations

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A compositional style defined by Noah Creshevsky as "an electroacoustic musical language constructed from sounds that are found in our shared environment (realism), handled in ways that are somehow exaggerated or excessive (hyper)."

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music
