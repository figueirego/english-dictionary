### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The originator or creator of a work, especially of a literary composition; or, one of the creators of a collaborative work.

   **Example:** The copyright of any original writing belongs initially and properly to its author.

   **Example:** Have you read any Corinthian authors?

   **Example:** This paper has three authors.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The initial owner of the copyright to a work, especially a work made for hire or a work of corporate authorship.

   **Usage:** US

   **Example:** The author named on the copyright registration for the Android robot is Google Inc.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** copyright; intellectual-property; law

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Someone who writes books for a living.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Principal; the primary participant in a crime.

   **Usage:** obsolete; criminal law

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. One's authority for something&#58; an informant.

   **Usage:** archaic

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The creator or cause of anything.

   **Usage:** figuratively
