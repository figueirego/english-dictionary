### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To be in a casual or illicit romantic relationship.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** Brad and Mary have a thing.

   **Example:** They’ve got a thing.

   **Example:** They had a thing, but it's over now.

   **Source synonyms:** have a thing going on

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To have a crush on, or a soft spot for, someone or something.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** Brad has a thing for Mary.

   **Example:** I have a thing for ghost stories.

   **Source synonyms:** have the hots for; fancy

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To have an issue, such as a hang-up.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** Don't ask Mary to drive you there, because she has a thing about highway driving. It sometimes gives her panic attacks.

   **Example:** He has a thing about not stepping on sidewalk cracks.
