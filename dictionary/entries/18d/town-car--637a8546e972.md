## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A car having a kind of bodystyle produced from 1908 to 1939 with an external or open-topped driver's position and an enclosed compartment for passengers.

   **Example:** Her great-uncle was a wealthy man. The type who had a town car and who had a right-hand man to drive it.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** automotive; transport; vehicles

   **Source synonyms:** coupé de ville

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A hired non-stretch limousine, traditionally but not necessarily a standard-wheelbase Lincoln Town Car or Cadillac sedan.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** They called to get a town car sent out to the airport to pick up the visiting executives for the sales conference.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** automotive; transport; vehicles

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Synonym of city car.

   **Usage:** nonstandard

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** automotive; transport; vehicles
