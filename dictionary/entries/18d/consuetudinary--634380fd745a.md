### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A ritual book containing the forms and ceremonies used in the services of a particular monastery, cathedral or religious order.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
