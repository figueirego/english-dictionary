### Forms

- muttering — participle; present
- muttered — participle; past

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To utter words, especially complaints or angry expressions, indistinctly or with a low voice and lips partly closed; to say under one's breath.

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** You could hear the students mutter as they were served sodden spaghetti, yet again, in the cafeteria.

   **Example:** The beggar muttered words of thanks, as passersby dropped coins in his cup.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To speak softly and incoherently, or with imperfect articulations.

   **Example:** The asylum inmate muttered some doggerel about chains and pains to himself, over and over.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To make a sound with a low, rumbling noise.

   **Example:** April could hear the delivery van's engine muttering in the driveway.
