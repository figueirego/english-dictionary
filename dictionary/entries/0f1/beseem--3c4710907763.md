### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Generally with a qualifying word such as ill or well&#58; to appear, look, or seem (a certain way for someone or something).

   **Usage:** ambitransitive; archaic

   **Example:** This inn beseems well for a weary wayfarer.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Without any qualifying word&#58; to be appropriate or creditable (for someone or something).

   **Usage:** ambitransitive; archaic

   **Example:** It beseems you not to grumble thus.

   **Source synonyms:** become; befit; suit

   **Source antonyms:** misbeseem (archaic); unbeseem (obsolete)
