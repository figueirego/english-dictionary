### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To fall heavily due to lack of energy.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** He flopped down in front of the television, exhausted from work.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To cause to drop heavily.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The tired mule flopped its ears forward and trudged on.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To fail completely; not to be successful at all (of a movie, play, book, song etc.).

   **Usage:** informal; intransitive

   **Example:** The latest album flopped and so the studio canceled her contract.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To pretend to be fouled in sports, such as basketball, hockey (the same as to dive in soccer)

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** It starts with Chris Paul, because Blake didn't really used to flop like that, you know, last year.

   **Example:** While Stern chastised Vogel for on Thursday calling the Heat "the biggest flopping team in the NBA," he did intimate that he sees merit in the sentiment.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To strike about with something broad and flat, as a fish with its tail, or a bird with its wings; to rise and fall; to flap.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The brim of a hat flops.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To have (a hand) using the community cards dealt on the flop.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Both players flopped sets! Cards dealt on the flop&#58; Q95. Player A's hole cards&#58; 55 (making three of a kind&#58; 555). Player B's hole cards&#58; QQ (making three of a kind&#58; QQQ).

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** card-games; poker

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To stay, sleep or live in a place.

   **Usage:** intransitive; slang

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To flip; to reverse (an image).

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To deny someone parole.

   **Usage:** slang; transitive
