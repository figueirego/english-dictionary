## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Knowledge of a set of circumstances sufficient to make actions based on those circumstances wrongful.

   **Usage:** uncountable; criminal law

   **Example:** The defendant was convicted because the prosecutor proved the defendant's prior knowledge of the victim's particular vulnerability.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Prior to the inception of an insurance policy, knowledge of specific extant circumstances that could reasonably give rise to a claim under that policy.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** All our policies require a declaration of no prior knowledge as a matter of routine.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; insurance; law

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see prior, knowledge.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** I had no prior knowledge of linguistics, but that didn't stop me from writing a dictionary.
