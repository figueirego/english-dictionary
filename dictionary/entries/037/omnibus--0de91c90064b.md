### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A bus (vehicle for transporting large numbers of people along roads).

   **Usage:** dated

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An anthology of previously released material linked together by theme or author, especially in book form.

   **Example:** This work is an omnibus containing all books in the Remembrance of Earth’s Past series.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A broadcast programme consisting of all of the episodes of a serial that have been shown in the previous week.

   **Example:** The omnibus edition of The Archers is broadcast every Sunday morning at 11&#58;00.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A stamp issue, usually commemorative, that appears simultaneously in several countries as a joint issue.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An assistant waiter.

   **Usage:** US; obsolete; slang

   **Source synonyms:** busboy; busser

**Source sense R1.S6**
