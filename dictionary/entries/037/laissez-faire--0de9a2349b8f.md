### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Practicing or representing governmental noninterference, or minimal interference, especially in economic affairs; pertaining to free-market capitalism.

   **Example:** I think the city should take a laissez faire approach to this; getting involved would only make things worse.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** economics; government; politics; sciences

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Advocating such noninterference.

   **Example:** The Senator claims to be laissez faire, but he voted in favor of the subsidies.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** economics; government; politics; sciences

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. Resulting from such noninterference.

   **Example:** The price ceiling was well below the laissez faire price that demand would have supported, so there were always shortages.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** economics; sciences

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. Avoiding interference in other people's affairs; choosing to live and let live.
