### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Prone to sneeze with little (if any) nasal agitation.

   **Example:** I feel sneezy.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Characterised by sneezes.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** I had a very sneezy cold.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Resembling a sneeze.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** There was a sneezy sound from behind the hedge.
