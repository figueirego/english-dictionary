### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To chew so as to make something pulpy or tangled.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** She had to chew up the food for him because his teeth are too small.

   **Example:** The tape got chewed up in the cassette player.

   **Example:** My dog always chews up my daughter's teddy bears.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To ruin (especially land), by digging.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To defeat utterly.

   **Usage:** US; slang; transitive

   **Example:** The Yankees chewed up the Dodgers.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To upset or bother (someone).

   **Usage:** slang; transitive
