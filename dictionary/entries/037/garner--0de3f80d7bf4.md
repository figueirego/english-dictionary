### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To reap grain, gather it up, and store it in a granary.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To gather, amass, hoard, as if harvesting grain.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To earn; to get; to accumulate or acquire (attitudes, reputations, or actions from others) by some effort or due to some fact

   **Usage:** figuratively; often; transitive

   **Example:** To garner support

   **Example:** He garnered a reputation as a language expert.

   **Example:** Her new book garnered high praise from the critics.

   **Example:** His poor choices garnered him a steady stream of welfare checks.

   **Source synonyms:** reap; gain

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To gather or become gathered; to accumulate or become accumulated; to become stored.

   **Usage:** rare; transitive
