## Noun

### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A sometimes harmless, sometimes painful skin condition that can affect the focal surface of the fingers and/or the palm or soles of the feet, characterized by dry skin and superficial, air-filled blisters.

   **Usage:** uncountable
