### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. The action of calculating or estimating something.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** By that reckoning, it would take six weeks to go five miles.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. An opinion or judgement.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** day of reckoning

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. A summing up or appraisal.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
