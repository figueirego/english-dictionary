### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To log off; to stop using a computer, radio, etc.; especially, to stop talking.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** He finished the conversation and signed off.

   **Source synonyms:** log off; log out; sign out

   **Source antonyms:** log in; log on; sign in; sign on

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To cease broadcasting a radio or television signal, usually at the end of a broadcasting day.

   **Example:** Before he signed off the radio station for the night, the disc jockey played the National Anthem.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** broadcasting; media

   **Source synonyms:** closedown

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. to give one's official approval to something for which it is needed, especially by a signature.

   **Example:** Once the vice-president signs off on the project, we can start construction.

   **Source context (verbatim):** followed by “on”

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To use a valediction, such as the end of a letter or broadcast.
