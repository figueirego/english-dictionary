### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Poured over ice, usually in reference to alcoholic drinks.

   **Example:** I'll have a whisky on the rocks.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In a bad state; experiencing difficulties or at risk of failing.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** I knew my business was on the rocks, so I started considering going into voluntary bankruptcy.

   **Example:** Joe and Tammy's relationship is on the rocks again.
