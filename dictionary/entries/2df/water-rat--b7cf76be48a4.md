### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of various aquatic or semi-aquatic rodents from Florida and southern Georgia, especially Neofiber alleni; the muskrat.

   **Usage:** US

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A water mouse, especially, the rakali, Hydromys chrysogaster.

   **Usage:** Australia

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A European water vole (Arvicola amphibius, formerly Arvicola terrestris).

   **Usage:** informal

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A petty thief or waterfront ruffian.

   **Usage:** US; slang

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A person fond of water sports.

   **Usage:** slang

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A member of the water police.

   **Usage:** Australia; slang
