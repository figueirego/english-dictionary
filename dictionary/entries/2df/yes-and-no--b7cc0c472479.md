## Phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An answer in reply to a yes-no question, indicating there is no simple "yes" or "no" answer

   **Example:** Is she happy? — Yes and no, hard to say.

   **Example:** Have you been to Paris? — Yes and no. I've been to the airport, but didn't get to see the city.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see yes, and, no.

   **Example:** You can only use yes and no answers.
