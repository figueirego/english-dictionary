## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈtɹʌmpɪt/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Forms

- trumpets — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A musical instrument of the brass family, generally tuned to the key of B-flat; by extension, any type of lip-vibrated aerophone, most often valveless and not chromatic.

   **Example:** The royal herald sounded a trumpet to announce their arrival.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Someone who plays the trumpet; a trumpeter.

   **Example:** The trumpets were assigned to stand at the rear of the orchestra pit.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The cry of an elephant, or any similar loud cry.

   **Example:** The large bull gave a basso trumpet as he charged the hunters.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. One who praises, or propagates praise, or is the instrument of propagating it.

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A funnel, or short flaring pipe, used as a guide or conductor, as for yarn in a knitting machine.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A kind of traffic interchange involving at least one loop ramp connecting traffic either entering or leaving the terminating expressway with the far lanes of the continuous highway.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A powerful reed stop in organs, having a trumpet-like sound.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Any of various flowering plants with trumpet-shaped flowers, for example, of the genus Collomia.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A supporter of Donald Trump, especially a fervent one.

   **Usage:** US; capitalized; often; slang

## Verb · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈtɹʌmpɪt/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Forms

- trumpets — present; singular; third-person
- trumpeting — participle; present
- trumpeted — participle; past
- trumpeted — past

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To sound loudly, be amplified

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The music trumpeted from the speakers, hurting my ears.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To play the trumpet.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Cedric made a living trumpeting for the change of passersby in the subway.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. Of an elephant, to make its cry.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** The circus trainer cracked the whip, signaling the elephant to trumpet.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To give a loud cry like that of an elephant.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To proclaim loudly; to promote enthusiastically

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Andy trumpeted Jane's secret across the school, much to her embarrassment.
