## Adverb

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used to show that the truth or existence of one thing does not logically justify the truth or existence of another thing.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Just because he didn't accept your friend request (it) doesn't mean he has forgotten you nor does it imply that he no longer wants to be friends with you.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used to say that something has insufficient grounds for being done.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. For no particular reason.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source synonyms:** for the hell of it
