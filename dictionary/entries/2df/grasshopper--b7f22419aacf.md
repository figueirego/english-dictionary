### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A mostly herbivorous insect of the order Orthoptera, noted for its ability to jump long distances and for the habit of some species communicating by stridulation; they are related to but distinct from crickets.

   **Source synonyms:** grasshop

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A mostly herbivorous insect of the order Orthoptera, noted for its ability to jump long distances and for the habit of some species communicating by stridulation; they are related to but distinct from crickets.
   1. In the strict sense, refers to insects in the suborder Caelifera, particularly those in the family Acrididae.

      **Source synonyms:** grasshop

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A mostly herbivorous insect of the order Orthoptera, noted for its ability to jump long distances and for the habit of some species communicating by stridulation; they are related to but distinct from crickets.
   1. In a looser sense, also includes the katydids (also known as longhorned grasshoppers or bush crickets), which are members of the family Tettigoniidae, of the suborder Ensifera.

      **Source synonyms:** grasshop

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

5. A young student in the initial stages of training who has been chosen on account of their obvious talent.

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

6. A frequent user of marijuana.
