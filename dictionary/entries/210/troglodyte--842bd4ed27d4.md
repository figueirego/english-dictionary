### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A member of a supposed prehistoric race that lived in caves or holes, a caveman.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Anything that lives underground.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** The cave was populated by albino scorpions, blind salamanders, and other troglodytes.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The Eurasian wren, Troglodytes troglodytes.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A reclusive, reactionary or out-of-date person, especially if brutish.

   **Usage:** derogatory

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A person who chooses not to keep up to date with the latest software and hardware.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences
