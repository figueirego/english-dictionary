### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A geographic region in southwest Asia, spanning from the rivers Euphrates and Tigris, located in modern Iraq, eastern Syria, and southeast Turkey; the site of one of the most ancient civilizations in the history of man.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** history; human-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A former province of the Roman Empire, existing from 116 to 117 AD and again from 198 to 637 AD.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** history; human-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The Mandate for Mesopotamia, a League of Nations mandate from 1920 to 1932, entrusted to the United Kingdom, that was the precursor to the independent state of Iraq.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** history; human-sciences; sciences
