### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To cut into something by clamping the teeth.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** As soon as you bite that sandwich, you'll know how good it is.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To hold something by clamping one's teeth.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To attack with the teeth.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** That dog is about to bite!

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To behave aggressively; to reject advances.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** If you see me, come and say hello. I don't bite.

   **Source context (verbatim):** in the negative

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To take hold; to establish firm contact with.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** I needed snow chains to make the tires bite.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To have significant effect, often negative.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** For homeowners with adjustable rate mortgages, rising interest will really bite.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To bite a baited hook or other lure and thus be caught.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Are the fish biting today?

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a fish

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To accept something offered, often secretly or deceptively, to cause some action by the acceptor.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To sting.

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** These mosquitoes are really biting today!

   **Source context (verbatim):** of an insect

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. To cause a smarting sensation; to have a property which causes such a sensation; to be pungent.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** It bites like pepper or mustard.

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. To cause sharp pain or damage to; to hurt or injure.

   **Usage:** figuratively; sometimes; transitive

   **Example:** Pepper bites the mouth.

<a id="r1-s12"></a>

**Source sense R1.S12**

12. To cause sharp pain; to produce anguish; to hurt or injure; to have the property of so doing.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s13"></a>

**Source sense R1.S13**

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The anchor bites.

   **Usage:** transitive
