<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Located towards or at the bottom of a slope or hill.

   **Example:** Moraines are found at the downhill end of a glacier.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Going down a slope or a hill.

   **Example:** This year's race is on a downhill course.
