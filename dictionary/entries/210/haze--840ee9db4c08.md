### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Very fine solid particles (smoke, dust) or liquid droplets (moisture) suspended in the air, slightly limiting visibility. (Compare fog, mist.)

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A reduction of transparency of a clear gas or liquid.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An analogous dullness on a surface that is ideally highly reflective or transparent.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** The soap left a persistent haze on the drinking glasses.

   **Example:** The furniture has a haze, possibly from some kind of wax.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any state suggestive of haze in the atmosphere, such as mental confusion or vagueness of memory.

   **Usage:** figuratively; uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The degree of cloudiness or turbidity in a clear glass or plastic, measured in percent.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually; packaging

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Any substance causing turbidity in beer or wine.

   **Usage:** countable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** beverages; brewing; business; food; lifestyle; manufacturing
