### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To install signposts on.

   **Usage:** transitive; chiefly passive voice

   **Example:** The route wasn't signposted, and we got lost on the way.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To direct (somebody) to services, resources, etc.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive
