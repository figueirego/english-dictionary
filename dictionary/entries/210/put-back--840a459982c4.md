### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To return something to its original place.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He carefully put the vase back on the shelf.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To turn back; to return.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To postpone an arranged event or appointment.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The meeting has been put back to 5.00 pm.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To drink fast; to knock down alcohol.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** You'll need to put that drink back quickly; it's very nearly closing time.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To change the time in a time zone to an earlier time.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** Don't forget that this Sunday we put the clocks back an hour.
