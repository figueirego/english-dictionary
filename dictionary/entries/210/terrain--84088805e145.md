### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A single, distinctive rock formation; an area having a preponderance of a particular rock or group of rocks.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; geology; natural-sciences

   **Source synonyms:** terrane

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An area of land or its particular features.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The race will be run over a variety of terrain, including grass and sand.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The surface of the earth; the ground.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** This approach requires the aircraft to stay at an altitude of at least 3000 feet MSL until crossing the VOR in order to maintain terrain clearance.

   **Example:** controlled flight into terrain
