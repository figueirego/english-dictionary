### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A boiling natural spring which throws forth jets of water, mud, etc., at frequent intervals, driven upwards by the expansive power of steam.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astronomy; geography; geology; natural-sciences; planetology; volcanology

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A momentary vertical jet or fountain of fluid driven upwards by a violent force.

   **Usage:** broadly

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An instantaneous, and often dangerous, hot water heater.

   **Usage:** UK; archaic

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A domestic water boiler.

   **Usage:** South-Africa; South-Asia
