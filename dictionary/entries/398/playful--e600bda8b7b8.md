### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Enjoying play; sportive; prone to playing frequently, in the manner of a child or young pet (viz. puppy or kitten).

   **Example:** Actually, we are pretty playful in our romantic life.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Funny; humorous; jesty; frolicsome; frisky.

   **Example:** John is a playful fellow.

   **Example:** A party hat is a playful conical hat people wear at parties.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Fun; recreational; unserious.

   **Example:** A brainteaser is a playful puzzle posed as a test of intelligence.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Experimental.

   **Example:** He was a rather playful artist.
