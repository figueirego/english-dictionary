### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who is the latest, the last, or the slowest in a group to arrive somewhere or to perform a certain task.

   **Usage:** childish; humorous; idiomatic; slang

   **Example:** Last one there is a rotten egg!

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An egg (“closeted transgender person”) who overcompensates by being actively transphobic.

   **Usage:** chiefly transgender slang

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
