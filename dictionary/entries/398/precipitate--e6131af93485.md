### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make something happen suddenly and quickly.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to precipitate a journey, or a conflict

   **Example:** it precipitated their success

   **Example:** Back to his sight precipitates her steps.

   **Source synonyms:** advance; accelerate; hasten; speed up

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To throw an object or person from a great height.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** throw; fling; cast; bung (Australia; UK); cast; chuck; chunk (Southern-US; slang); cook (UK; dialectal; obsolete); dash; dump; feck; fling; jerk (obsolete); heave; hield; hoy (Australia; Northumbria); huck; hurl; hurtle (archaic; literary); launch; lob; peck (regional); peg (slang); pick (obsolete); pitch; precipitate; project; quoit; shy; slight; skew (Yorkshire); sling; thrill (obsolete); throw; toss; traject; warp (obsolete; rare); whang (Australia; UK); whip; whop; wing; yeet (slang)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To send violently into a certain state or condition.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** we were precipitated into a conflict

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. (chemistry) To come out of a liquid solution into solid form.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Adding the acid will cause the salt to precipitate.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. (chemistry) To separate a substance out of a liquid solution into solid form.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To have water in the air fall to the ground, for example as rain, snow, sleet, or hail; be deposited as condensed droplets.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** It will precipitate tomorrow, but we don't know whether as rain or snow.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** climatology; meteorology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To cause (water in the air) to condense or fall to the ground.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To fall headlong.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To act too hastily; to be precipitous.

   **Usage:** intransitive
