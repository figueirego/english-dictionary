### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Being of secondary consequence to a causal chain of processes, but playing no causal role in the process of interest.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of or pertaining to a mental process that occurs only as an incidental effect of electrical or chemical activity in the brain or nervous system.
