### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of or pertaining to a volcano or volcanoes.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** volcanic heat

   **Example:** volcanic activity

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Produced by a volcano, or, more generally, by igneous agencies.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** volcanic tufa

   **Example:** volcanic ash

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Changed or affected by the heat of a volcano.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Reminiscent of a volcano; explosive, violent, full of suppressed anger, energy etc.
