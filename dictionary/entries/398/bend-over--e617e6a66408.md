### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To bend one's upper body forward and down while standing or kneeling.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** I had to bend over to see all the way down there.

   **Source synonyms:** stoop

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To act submissively.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** You really bend over when the boss comes calling, don't you?

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see stay, over.

   **Example:** Sandrita was bent over the sink doing the dishes.
