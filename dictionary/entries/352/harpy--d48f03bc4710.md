### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A mythological creature generally depicted as a bird-of-prey with the head of a maiden, a face pale with hunger and long claws on her hands personifying the destructive power of storm winds.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; mysticism; mythology; philosophy; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A shrewish woman.

   **Usage:** derogatory

   **Source synonyms:** battle axe; bitch; brim (UK; slang); brimstone; cat; cockatrice (archaic); crow; dragon; faggot (Ireland; UK; obsolete); fishwife; harpy; harridan; hellcat; jade; nag; nudnik; scold; she-devil; shrew; termagant; virago; vixen; witch

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. One who is rapacious or ravenous; an extortioner.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any of a number of eagle-like birds of prey of the subfamily Harpiinae, especially the species Harpia harpyja.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The European moor buzzard or marsh harrier (Circus aeruginosus).
