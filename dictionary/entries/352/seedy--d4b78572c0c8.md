### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Literal senses&#58;
   1. Containing or full of seeds.

      **Example:** Pomegranates are as seedy as any fruit you are likely to see.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Literal senses&#58;
   1. Seedlike; having the flavour of seeds.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Literal senses&#58;
   1. Having a peculiar flavour supposed to be derived from the weeds growing among the vines; said of certain kinds of French brandy.

      **Usage:** colloquial

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Inferior in condition or quality.
   1. Shabby, run-down, possibly connected with bad, dishonest or illegal activities, somewhat disreputable.

      **Usage:** figuratively

      **Source synonyms:** sleazy

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Inferior in condition or quality.
   1. Untidy, unkempt.

      **Usage:** figuratively

      **Example:** His seedy, dirt-smudged visage caused her to look at him askance.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Inferior in condition or quality.
   1. Infirm, unwell, gone to seed.

      **Usage:** figuratively

      **Example:** With her aching back and pronounced limp, she was feeling particularly seedy today.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Inferior in condition or quality.
   1. Suffering the effects of a hangover.

      **Usage:** figuratively

      **Example:** After last night’s party we were all feeling pretty seedy.
