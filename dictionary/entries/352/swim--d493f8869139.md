### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To move through the water, without touching the bottom; to propel oneself in water by natural means.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To become immersed in, or as if in, or flooded with, or as if with, a liquid.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** swimming in self-pity

   **Example:** a bare few bits of meat swimming in watery sauce

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To move around freely because of excess space.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To traverse (a specific body of water, or a specific distance) by swimming; or, to use a specific swimming stroke; or, to compete in a specific swimming event.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** For exercise, we like to swim laps around the pool.

   **Example:** I want to swim the 200-yard breaststroke in the finals.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To cause to swim.

   **Usage:** transitive; uncommon

   **Example:** to swim a horse across a river

   **Example:** Half of the guinea pigs were swum daily.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To float.

   **Usage:** archaic; intransitive

   **Example:** sink or swim

   **Example:** There's oil swimming on the water.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To be overflowed or drenched.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To immerse in water to make the lighter parts float.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to swim wheat in order to select seed

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To test (a suspected witch) by throwing into a river; those who floated rather than sinking were deemed to be witches.

   **Usage:** historical; transitive

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. To glide along with a waving motion.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. To have a great quantity of something.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** You should be swimming in money after this job.
