### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act or practice of traveling into the future or past.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The act of modifying the internal clock of a video game or the platform running said video game, usually in order to trigger time-based events earlier than they're meant to.

   **Example:** I often time-travel in Animal Crossing so that I don't have to wait for my trees to grow.
