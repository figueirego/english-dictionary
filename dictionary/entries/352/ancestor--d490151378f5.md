### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One from whom a person is descended, whether on the father's or mother's side, at any distance of time; a progenitor; a forefather; a forebear.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An earlier type; a progenitor.

   **Example:** This fossil animal is regarded as the ancestor of the horse.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. One from whom an estate has descended;—the correlative of heir.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. One who had the same role or function in former times.

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A word or phrase which serves as the origin of a term in another language.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences
