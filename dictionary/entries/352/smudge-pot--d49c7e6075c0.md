## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A set of kerosene flares with round bottoms and wicks in a protected cage, once used to mark highway construction sites and as utility flares.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An oil-burning device used to prevent frost on fruit trees.

   **Source synonyms:** orchard heater; choofa

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A pot of tar and oil burned to produce thick noxious smoke to discourage pests.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A bowl used to hold herbs that are to be burnt and to catch the resulting ashes.

   **Source synonyms:** smudge bowl
