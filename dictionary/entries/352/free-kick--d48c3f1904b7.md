### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. a kick in which a player may kick the ball without interference from the opposition. Such a kick may be awarded for a foul by the opposition, or earned by a player such as by taking a mark.

   **Usage:** Australian rules football; other ballgames; Australian rules football; other ballgames

   **Example:** a free-kick specialist

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** American-football; ball-games; football; games; hobbies; lifestyle; rugby; soccer; sports

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The usual means of restarting play after a foul is committed, where the non-offending team restarts from where the foul was committed.

   **Usage:** Gaelic football
