### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A citizen from the country of Somalia.

   **Usage:** collective; countable

   **Example:** A Han Chinese man who is a citizen of Somalia is a Somalian but not an ethnic Somali. One can be a Somalian and not a Somali at the same time.
