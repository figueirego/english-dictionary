**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A vertical (or near vertical) opening (shaft) running from a courtyard to the sky, thus allowing air to circulate to high-rise apartments or offices.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** architecture

   **Source synonyms:** air well; ventilation shaft; vent shaft; lightwell

<a id="r1-s2"></a>
