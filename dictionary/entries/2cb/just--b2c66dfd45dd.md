### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Only, simply, merely.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Just plant a few tomatoes, unless you can freeze or dry them.

   **Example:** He calls it vermilion, but it's just red to me.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Only, simply, merely.
   1. Introduces a disappointing or surprising outcome that renders futile something previously mentioned.

      **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. Used to reduce the force of an imperative; simply.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; sentence adverb

   **Example:** Just follow the directions on the box.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. Used to increase the force of an imperative; simply, without questioning.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; sentence adverb

   **Example:** I'm tired of playing games – just get it done.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. Used to convey a less serious or formal tone.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** I just called to say "hi".

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. Absolutely, positively.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; degree

   **Example:** It is just splendid!

   **Example:** just fine

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

**Source sense R2.S7**

7. Barely, hardly, scarcely.
   1. Moments ago, only very recently.

      **Usage:** not-comparable

      **Example:** They just left, but you may leave a message at the desk.

<a id="r2-s8"></a>

**Source sense R2.S8**

8. Barely, hardly, scarcely.
   1. By a narrow margin; closely; narrowly; almost not.

      **Usage:** not-comparable

      **Example:** The fastball just missed my head!

      **Example:** The fire department arrived just in time.

      **Example:** The piece just might fit.

<a id="r2-s9"></a>

**Source sense R2.S9**

9. Exactly, precisely, perfectly.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** He wants everything just right for the big day.

### Related words
