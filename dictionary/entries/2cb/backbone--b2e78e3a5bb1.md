### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The series of vertebrae, separated by disks, that encloses and protects the spinal cord, and runs down the middle of the back in vertebrate animals.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any fundamental support, structure, or infrastructure.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively

   **Example:** Before automobiles, railroads were a backbone of commerce.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Courage, fortitude, or strength.

   **Usage:** figuratively; uncountable

   **Example:** He would make a good manager, if he had a little more backbone.
