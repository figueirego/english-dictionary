<a id="r1-s2"></a>

2. A horse-drawn cab with four wheels.

   **Usage:** historical; slang

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

   **Usage:** Australia; Canada; US; informal

   **Usage:** UK; Yorkshire; dialectal
