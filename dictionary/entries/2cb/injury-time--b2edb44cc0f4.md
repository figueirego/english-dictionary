## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Additional time played after each period of a game in order to compensate for stoppages due to player injuries; loosely extended to include time added on to compensate for all types of stoppages, i.e. synonymous with stoppage time.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports
