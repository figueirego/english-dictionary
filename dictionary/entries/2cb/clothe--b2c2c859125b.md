### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To adorn or cover with clothing; to dress; to supply clothes or clothing.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to feed and clothe a family; to clothe oneself extravagantly

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To cover or invest, as if with a garment.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** to clothe somebody with authority or power
