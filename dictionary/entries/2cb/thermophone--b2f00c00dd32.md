### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A form of transducer that generates sound waves of precise power.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A portable telethermometer, using a telephone in connection with a differential thermometer.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A telephone involving heat effects, as changes in temperature (hence in length) due to pulsations of the line current in a fine wire connected with the receiver diaphragm.

   **Usage:** historical
