### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A nurse who takes care of a baby, but does not breastfeed.

   **Usage:** historical

   **Example:** The family found a dry nurse to take care of the infant.

**Source sense R1.S2**
