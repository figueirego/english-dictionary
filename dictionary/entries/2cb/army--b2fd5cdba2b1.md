### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A large, highly organized military force, concerned mainly with ground (rather than air or naval) operations.

   **Example:** The army was sent in to quell the uprising.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A large, highly organized military force, concerned mainly with ground (rather than air or naval) operations.
   1. Used absolutely for that entire branch of the armed forces.

      **Example:** The army received a bigger share of this year's budget increase than the navy or air force.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A large, highly organized military force, concerned mainly with ground (rather than air or naval) operations.
   1. Within a vast military, a very large tactical contingent (e.g. a number of divisions).

      **Usage:** capitalized; often

      **Example:** The Fourth Army suffered such losses that its remainders were merged into the Second Army, also deployed on the Western front.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The governmental agency in charge of a state's army.

   **Example:** The army opposed the legislature's involvement.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A large group of people working toward the same purpose.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** It took an army of accountants to uncover the fraud.

   **Example:** On sunny days the beaches draw armies of tourists of all kinds.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A large group of animals, especially caterpillars or social insects working toward the same purpose.

   **Usage:** collective; figuratively

   **Example:** Our house is being attacked by an army of ants.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Any multitude.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** There was an army of construction cranes working on building the skyscraper.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. The military as a whole.

   **Example:** The People's Liberation Army Navy of China.

   **Example:** The Yugoslav Army consisted of the War Navy, Ground Forces, and Air Force.

   **Example:** Iran's army consists of the Navy, Ground Forces, and Air Force.
