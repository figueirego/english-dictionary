### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An English surname originating as an occupation from Middle English connere, cunnere "inspector (of weighs and measures)".

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An Irish and Scottish surname from Irish, a variant of Connor.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A male given name transferred from the surname.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A municipality in Apayao province, Philippines named after American Norman Conner.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>
