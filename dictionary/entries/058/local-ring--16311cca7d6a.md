### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A commutative ring with a unique maximal ideal, or a noncommutative ring with a unique maximal left ideal or (equivalently) a unique maximal right ideal.

   **Example:** The unique maximal ideal of a (commutative) local ring contains all of the zero divisors of such ring, and all elements of the ring outside of it are units. Then in a local ring, the sum of any two zero divisors is also a zero divisor. Contrapositively, if two ring elements add up to a unit then one of them must be a unit as well. A simple example of a local ring is \#92;mathbb\#123;Z\#125;\#95;8.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** algebra; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
