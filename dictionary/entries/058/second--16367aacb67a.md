### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Number-two; following after the first one with nothing between them. The ordinal number corresponding to the cardinal number two.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** He lives on Second Street.

   **Example:** The second volume in "The Lord of the Rings" series is called "The Two Towers".

   **Example:** He became the second player to hit 50000 runs for his county.

   **Example:** You take the first one, and I'll have the second.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Next to the first in value, power, excellence, dignity, or rank; secondary; subordinate; inferior.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** His job is second priority, because his family is the most important thing in his life.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Being of the same kind as one that has preceded; another.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Residents of Texas prepared for Hurricane Harvey, which would in some ways turn out to become the second Hurricane Katrina.
