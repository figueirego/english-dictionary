### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Avoidance of saying or reluctance to say too much; discretion, tight-lippedness; (countable) an instance of acting in this manner.

   **Usage:** also; figuratively; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** reserve; taciturnity

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A silent and reserved nature.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** introversion; reservation; bashfulness; demureness; diffidence; reluctance; introversion; quietness; reservation; reticence; shyness; sheepishness; timidity

   **Source antonyms:** ostentation; chattiness; diarrhea of the mouth; garrulity; garrulousness; logorrhea; logorrhoea; loquaciousness; loquacity; multiloquence; talkativeness; talkativity; verbal diarrhea; verbal diarrhoea

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Followed by of&#58; discretion or restraint in the use of something.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Often followed by to&#58; hesitancy or reluctance (to do something).

   **Usage:** proscribed; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** disinclination; hesitation

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Synonym of aposiopesis (“an abrupt breaking-off in speech”).

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; rhetoric; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** aposiopesis (synonym; synonym-of)
