### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A scale insect of the species Dactylopius coccus, native to the tropical and subtropical Americas, which lives on prickly pear cacti (genus Opuntia).

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A vivid red dye made from the dried bodies of cochineal insects.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** E120

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The vivid red colour of this dye.
