## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A self-contained page or group of pages meant to supplement a larger website.

   **Usage:** Internet

   **Example:** The movie site has a microsite where fans can speculate about the ending.

   **Source synonyms:** minisite; weblet

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A small area with distinctive ecological characteristics.

   **Example:** This species was found only in the microsite at the foot of the bluff.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; ecology; natural-sciences

   **Source synonyms:** microhabitat; microenvironment
