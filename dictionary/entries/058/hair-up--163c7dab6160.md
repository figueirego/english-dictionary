### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To grow (more) hair.
   1. To grow its longer, thicker winter coat.

      **Usage:** US; intransitive

      **Source context (verbatim):** of an animal whose body hair changes seasonally

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To grow (more) hair.
   1. To grow facial hair, especially after shaving; (rarely) to grow body hair.

      **Usage:** US; intransitive

      **Source context (verbatim):** of a person

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

   **Usage:** US; transitive
