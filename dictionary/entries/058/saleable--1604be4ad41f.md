### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Suitable for sale; marketable; worth enough to try to sell; that can be sold (i.e., for which it is possible to find a willing and able buyer).

   **Example:** They held an estate sale last Saturday, which cleared out everything that was saleable; what's left will require a skip for disposal.

   **Source synonyms:** sellable; vendable; vendible

   **Source antonyms:** unsaleable; unsalable; unsellable; unmerchantable; unmarketable
