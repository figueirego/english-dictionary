### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An incompetent person, a blockhead; a loser.

   **Usage:** colloquial; derogatory

   **Example:** That chump wouldn't know his ass from a hole in the ground.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A gullible person; a sucker; someone easily taken advantage of; someone lacking common sense.

   **Usage:** colloquial; derogatory

   **Example:** It shouldn't be hard to put one over on that chump.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The thick end, especially of a piece of wood or of a joint of meat.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A person's head or face.

   **Usage:** UK; obsolete; slang
