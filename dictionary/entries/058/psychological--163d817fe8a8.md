### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of or pertaining to psychology.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** An inkblot test is a method of psychological evaluation.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Relating to the mind and behavior or to the mental, emotional, and behavioral characteristics pertaining to a specified person, group, or activity.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** I'm concerned about their psychological well-being.

   **Example:** The film was a dark psychological thriller.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Without an objective, or reasonably logical foundation.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Prices seem to have stopped rising, having hit the psychological ceiling of just under 100 dollars.
