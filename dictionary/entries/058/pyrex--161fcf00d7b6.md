## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A kind of heat-resistant borosilicate glass, made by mixing a high concentration of boron oxides into the molten glass to reduce its thermal expansion coefficient.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

   **Usage:** broadly; uncountable
