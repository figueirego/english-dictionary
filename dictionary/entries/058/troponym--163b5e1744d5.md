### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A verb that indicates more precisely the manner of doing something by replacing a verb of a more generalized meaning.

   **Example:** ‘Stroll’ is a troponym of ‘walk’, since it involves a slow and relaxed sort of walking.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** grammar; human-sciences; linguistics; sciences
