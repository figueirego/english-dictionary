### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Drugged.

   **Example:** He was so doped after the surgery that it took him 2 hours to remember his name.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Describing a semiconductor that has had small amounts of elements added to create charge carriers.

   **Example:** The silicon was doped with boron to make a p-type semiconductor.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; electrical-engineering; electricity; electromagnetism; electronics; energy; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Covered with dope, a glue- or paint-like pore filler.

   **Example:** The doped fabric covering the Hindenburg was very flammable.
