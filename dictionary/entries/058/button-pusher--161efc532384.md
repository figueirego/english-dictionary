## Noun

### Forms

- button pushers — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Someone who pushes someone else's buttons&#58; someone who goads, annoys, provokes, etc.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** When you're dealing with Tom, you need to keep in mind that he can be a button pusher sometimes. Stay cool, and don't reinforce that behavior.

   **Source synonyms:** gadfly; goader; provocateur; provoker

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A low-skilled or unskilled worker&#58; someone from whom one can expect little or nothing more than rote pushing of buttons (not showing, for example, critical thinking, insight, or leadership).

   **Usage:** derogatory; mildly; often

   **Example:** Bill has been working here for 4 years and is looking for ways to upskill. He says he doesn't want to be a button pusher forever.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; manufacturing
