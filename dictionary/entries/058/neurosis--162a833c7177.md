### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A mental disorder, less severe than psychosis, marked by anxiety or fear differing from normal measures by its intensity, and resulting from a failure to compromise or properly adjust during the developmental stages of life, between normal human instinctual impulses and the demands of human society.

   **Usage:** countable; dated; uncountable; current in psychology and philosophy

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; pathology; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** psychoneurosis\[Appendix&#58;Glossary

   **Source antonyms:** physioneurosis
