- jarring — participle; present

### Senses

<a id="r4-s1"></a>

**Source sense R4.S1**

1. To knock, shake, or strike sharply, especially causing a quivering or vibrating movement.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He hit it with a hammer, hoping he could jar it loose.

<a id="r4-s2"></a>

**Source sense R4.S2**

2. To harm or injure by such action.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r4-s3"></a>

**Source sense R4.S3**

3. To shock or surprise.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** I think the accident jarred him, as he hasn’t got back in a car since.

<a id="r4-s4"></a>

**Source sense R4.S4**

4. To act in disagreement or opposition, to clash, to be at odds with; to interfere; to dispute, to quarrel.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

<a id="r4-s5"></a>

**Source sense R4.S5**

5. To (cause something to) give forth a rudely tremulous or quivering sound; to (cause something to) sound discordantly or harshly.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** The clashing notes jarred on my ears.

<a id="r4-s6"></a>

**Source sense R4.S6**

6. To quiver or vibrate due to being shaken or struck.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r4-s7"></a>

**Source sense R4.S7**

7. Of the appearance, form, style, etc., of people and things&#58; to look strangely different; to stand out awkwardly from its surroundings; to be incongruent.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive
