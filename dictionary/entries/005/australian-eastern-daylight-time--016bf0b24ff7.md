**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The time zone that encompasses the Australian states of New South Wales (excluding Broken Hill and area), Victoria and Tasmania from the first Sunday of October to the first Sunday of April; it is eleven hours ahead of Coordinated Universal Time (UTC+11).
