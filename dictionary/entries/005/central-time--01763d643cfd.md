## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The time zone in central North America corresponding to UTC minus six hours during standard time, and UTC minus five hours during daylight saving time.

   **Source synonyms:** Central Standard Time Zone; Central Time Zone; CT

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The time zone in central Europe corresponding to UTC plus one hour.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
