### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To decrease the focal length of a zoom lens in order to obtain a reduced magnification of the image, or a wider-angle view.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The director asked to zoom out for a wider shot of the landscape.

   **Example:** The drone's camera began to zoom out as it ascended higher into the sky.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To manipulate a display (such as a computer screen) so as to make the image smaller and possibly less detailed.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** You can zoom out on the screen to see the entire document.
