### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The manner in which two things may be associated.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The relation between diet and health is complex.

   **Example:** There's no linear relation between mortality and size.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A member of one's extended family; a relative.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Yes, he's a relation of mine, but only a distant one.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A relationship; the manner in which and tone with which people or states, etc. interact.

   **Usage:** countable; in-plural; uncountable

   **Example:** the foreign relations of the United States

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The act of relating a story.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Your relation of the events is different from mine.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A set of ordered tuples.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences; set-theory
