### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A people; a tribe or nation; the inhabitants of a region, especially the native inhabitants.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. People, persons.

   **Usage:** collective; countable; plural; uncountable

   **Example:** There were a lot of folk in the streets.

   **Example:** Young folk, old folk, everybody come / To our little Sunday School, and have a lot of fun.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. One’s relatives, especially one’s parents.

   **Usage:** collective; countable; plural; uncountable; usually

   **Example:** I need to call my folks back home.

   **Source context (verbatim):** as folks

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music
