## Noun · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Forms

- red gums — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of several species of trees found in Australia&#58;
   1. Corymbia calophylla (marri), native to Western Australia, with red gum effusions.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of several species of trees found in Australia&#58;
   1. Eucalyptus camaldulensis (Murray red gum, river red gum), a eucalyptus tree with red wood.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of several species of trees found in Australia&#58;
   1. Eucalyptus macrorhyncha (red stringybark, Cannon's stringybark, capertee stringybark)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any of several species of trees found in Australia&#58;
   1. Eucalyptus tereticornis (forest red gum), with red wood.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A tree of the hazel family, of species Liquidambar styraciflua, found in North America.
