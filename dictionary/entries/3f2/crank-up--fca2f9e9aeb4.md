### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To start something mechanical, an act that often used to involve cranking.

   **Example:** Let's crank up the old motorcycle and take it for a spin.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To muster up the mental energy to do something.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; reflexive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To prepare (something).

   **Usage:** idiomatic; slang

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To increase, as the volume, power or energy of something.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** He cranked up the volume to 11.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To describe in praiseworthy terms; to promote.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To inject heroin.

   **Usage:** intransitive; slang
