### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To give birth to a calf.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The farmer could tell Bessie was about to calve.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To assist in a cow’s giving birth to a calf.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The farmer calved Bessie for almost two hours.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To give birth to (a calf).

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To shed a large piece.

   **Usage:** especially; figuratively; intransitive; a glacier; an ice sheet; a glacier; an ice sheet

   **Example:** The glacier was starting to calve even as we watched.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of an ice shelf

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To break off.

   **Usage:** especially; figuratively; intransitive

   **Example:** The sea was dangerous because of icebergs calving off the nearby glacier.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of an iceberg

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To shed (a large piece); to set loose (a mass of ice).

   **Usage:** especially; figuratively; transitive; a glacier; an ice sheet; a glacier; an ice sheet

   **Example:** The glacier was starting to calve an iceberg even as we watched.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of an ice shelf
