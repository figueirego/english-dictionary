## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The group Cl2C=CCl–, formed by replacing all three hydrogen atoms of a vinyl group with chlorine.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

   **Extractor diagnostics (not usage labels):** empty-gloss; no-gloss

**Supplemental definition (evidence reviewed; source gap preserved):** Two independent named conjugates and explicit substitutive rules identify the C2Cl3 substituent. It is not defined as the whole metrifonate drug cited among original derivatives.
