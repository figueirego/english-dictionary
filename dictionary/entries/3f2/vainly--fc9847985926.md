### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In a vain pursuit; to no avail.

   **Example:** She searched vainly for her reflection in the shattered mirror.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. With a vain attitude; in a self-approving manner.

   **Example:** She stared vainly at her reflection in the mirror.
