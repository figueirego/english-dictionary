### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Something that is owned.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Leave those books alone! They are my property.

   **Example:** Important types of property include real property (land), personal property (other physical possessions), and intellectual property (rights over artistic creations, inventions, etc.).

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A piece of real estate, such as a parcel of land.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** There is a large house on the property.

   **Example:** He owns over ten properties in the country.

   **Source synonyms:** land; parcel

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Real estate; the business of selling houses.

   **Usage:** UK; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** He works in property as a housing consultant.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The exclusive right of possessing, enjoying and disposing of a thing.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An attribute or abstract quality associated with an individual, object or concept.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Charm is his most endearing property.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. An attribute or abstract quality which is characteristic of a class of objects.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Matter can have many properties, including color, mass and density.

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. An editable or read-only parameter associated with an application, component or class; especially (object-oriented programming) one that encapsulates an underlying variable.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
