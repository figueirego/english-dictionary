### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person or firm that owes money; one in debt; one who owes a debt.

   **Example:** The debtor agreed to repay the loan within six months.

   **Example:** The law protects both the debtor and the creditor.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** economics; sciences

   **Source antonyms:** creditor

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One who owes another anything, or is under obligation, arising from express agreement, implication of law, or principles of natural justice, to pay money or to fulfill some other obligation; in bankruptcy or similar proceedings, the person who is the subject of the proceeding.

   **Example:** As a debtor, he faced constant letters from the bank.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law
