### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To completely fail; to have no result.

   **Usage:** slang; vulgar

   **Example:** All his hard work went to shit when the company filed for bankruptcy.

   **Source synonyms:** go to hell (vulgar); go to hell in a handbasket; go to ratshit; die in the ass; come to nought; come to nothing; take a shit; take a crap; crap out; shit the bed

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To become much worse.

   **Usage:** slang; vulgar

   **Example:** This city has gone to shit ever since he became mayor.

   **Source synonyms:** go to hell (vulgar); go to hell in a handbasket; go to ratshit; go downhill; go to pot; go to the dogs
