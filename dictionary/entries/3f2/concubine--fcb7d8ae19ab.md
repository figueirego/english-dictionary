### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A sexual partner, especially a woman, to whom one is not or cannot be married.

   **Source synonyms:** mistress; sprunk; bedfellow; bedmate; fuck (slang; vulgar); hookup; lover; paramour (obsolete); mate; root (slang; vulgar); sex partner; sexual partner; bit on the side (Britain); concubine; doxy (archaic); dulcinea (obsolete); goomah (US; slang); leman (archaic); loon (Scotland); minx (dated; derogatory); mistress; number two (Philippines); side bitch (vulgar); side chick (slang); side piece (slang); side ting (Britain; Multicultural-London-English); sprunk (obsolete); strumpet; wench (archaic); wifelet (informal)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A woman who lives with a man, but who is not a wife.

   **Source synonyms:** cohabitor; cohabitant; domestic partner

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A slave-girl or woman, kept for instance in a harem, who is held for sexual service.
