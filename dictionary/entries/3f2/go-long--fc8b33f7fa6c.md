### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To buy a financial product, such as a share, so as to profit from a rise in its value; compare go short.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** I would recommend going long on tech stocks this year.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To run far from the person throwing a ball in order to receive a long pass; (especially American football, Canadian football) to run down the field away from the quarterback to receive a long or Hail Mary pass.

   **Example:** He yelled to Steve "Go long!" just as the ball was snapped.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports
