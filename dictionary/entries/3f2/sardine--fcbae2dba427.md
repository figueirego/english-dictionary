### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any one of several species of small herring which are commonly preserved in olive oil or in tins for food, especially the European sardine (Sardina pilchardus, syn. Clupea pilchardus). The American sardines of the Atlantic coast are mostly the young of the Atlantic herring and of the menhaden, and many canned sardines in America are sprats.

   **Example:** They feasted on premium sardines packed in olive oil, as did their hunting dogs, who insisted on joining the meal.

   **Source synonyms:** pilchard

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Someone packed or crammed into a small space.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** They watched bemused as countless sardines disgorged from a railway car that was never designed to hold so many passengers.
