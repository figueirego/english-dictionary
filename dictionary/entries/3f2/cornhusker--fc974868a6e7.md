### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person or device that removes husks from corn.

   **Source synonyms:** cornshucker

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person who is a native or resident of the U.S. state of Nebraska.

   **Usage:** capitalized; usually
