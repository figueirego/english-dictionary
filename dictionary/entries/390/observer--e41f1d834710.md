### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who makes observations, monitors or takes notice.

   **Example:** Most impartial observers agreed that Sampras had not served well.

   **Source synonyms:** beholder; observator (obsolete); onlooker

   **Source antonyms:** observee

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One who adheres or follows laws, guidelines, etc.

   **Example:** I shall be an observer of the local customs.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A person sent as a representative, to a meeting or other function to monitor but not to participate.

   **Example:** The UN sent many observers to the country's first elections.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A country or other entity which has limited participation rights within an organization.

   **Example:** The Vatican and Palestine have observer status at the UN.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A crew member on an aircraft who makes observations of enemy positions or aircraft.

   **Example:** The only crew-member to survive the crash was the Canadian observer.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A sentry etc. manning an observation post.

   **Example:** We waited till dusk when the observers' vision was poorest.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Synonym of spotter.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; motor-racing; racing; sports

   **Source synonyms:** spotter (synonym; synonym-of)
