### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of, or pertaining to, the U.S. state of Washington or its culture.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** His concern for the cause of environmental protection was very Washingtonian of him.

## Noun · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˌwɒʃɪŋˈtəʊnɪ.ən/ — source labels: UK
- IPA: /ˌwɑʃɪŋˈtoʊ.ni.ən/ — source labels: General-American

### Forms

- Washingtonians — plural

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A native or resident of the state of Washington in the United States of America.

   **Example:** Any real Washingtonian will be quick to tell you that "Washington state" is a university.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. An inhabitant or a resident of Washington, D.C.

   **Example:** Many a hardworking Washingtonian have expressed their frustrations at the influx of rich Marylanders moving into their city.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. A member of the Washingtonian movement, a 19th-century sobriety support group.

   **Usage:** historical
