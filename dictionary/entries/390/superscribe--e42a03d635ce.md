### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. to write on the exterior of, the surface of, or above.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He superscribed each character with its Latin-alphabet equivalent.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. to write (something) on the exterior of an object, such as a document or an envelope.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** His wife superscribed her own notes on each of his letters before sending them in packets to the editor.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To address (an envelope etc.).

   **Usage:** transitive
