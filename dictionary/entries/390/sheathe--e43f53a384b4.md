### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To put (something such as a knife or sword) into a sheath.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source antonyms:** unsheathe

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To encase (something) with a protective covering.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source antonyms:** unsheathe

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of an animal&#58; to draw back or retract (a body part) into the body, such as claws into a paw.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source antonyms:** unsheathe

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To thrust (a sharp object like a sword, a claw, or a tusk) into something.

   **Usage:** dated; figuratively; literary; poetic; transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To abandon or cease (animosity, etc.)

   **Usage:** figuratively; obsolete; rare; transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To provide (a sword, etc.) with a sheath.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To relieve the harsh or painful effect of (a drug, a poison, etc.).

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive
