### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The ability, or the due use of one's ability, to foresee or prepare wisely for the future.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Having the foresight to prepare an evacuation plan may have saved their lives.

   **Source synonyms:** prescience; forethought

   **Source antonyms:** hindsight

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The ability to foresee future events in a supernatural or paranormal way, such as psychically.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** foreknowledge; clairvoyance; precognition; prescience; divination; prophecy; psychicness

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The front sight on a firearm (e.g., rifle, handgun).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source antonyms:** backsight; hindsight

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A bearing taken forwards towards a new object.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; natural-sciences; surveying
