### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Whether desired or not; without regard for consequences or wishes of those affected; whether willingly or unwillingly.

   **Source synonyms:** nilly-willy; nolens volens

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Seemingly at random; haphazardly.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** The novel Alice in Wonderland describes a place where things happen willy-nilly.

   **Example:** Some writers chasing money churn out novels willy-nilly.
