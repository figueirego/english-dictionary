### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A body style for cars in which the roof is extended rearward to produce an enclosed area in the position and serving the function of the boot (trunk) of a sedan or saloon.

   **Usage:** Australia; Canada; New-Zealand; US

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** automotive; transport; vehicles

   **Source synonyms:** estate car; estate\[Appendix&#58;Glossary; station sedan

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A vehicle (either horse-drawn or motorized) providing transport to and from a railway station for passengers and their luggage.

   **Usage:** obsolete
