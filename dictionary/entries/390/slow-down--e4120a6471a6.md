## Verb

### Forms

- slows down — present; singular; third-person
- slowing down — participle; present
- slowed down — participle; past
- slowed down — past

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To decelerate.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** When approaching a bend in the road, slow down, and speed up after leaving it.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To reduce the velocity, speed, or tempo of something.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** With a comfortable lead, the football team slowed down the tempo of the game.

   **Source synonyms:** retard

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To become less intense, enthusiastic, etc., usually with a positive connotation, implying that one is stripped of exaggerated or unnecessary eagerness.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** Whoa, slow down there, bucko. Everyone will get a chance. Be patient.

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** slow up; decelerate; cumber (dated); foreslow (obsolete); put the brakes on (idiomatic); rein in (idiomatic); retard; slow; slowen (nonstandard); slow down

**Source antonyms:** speed up; accelerate
