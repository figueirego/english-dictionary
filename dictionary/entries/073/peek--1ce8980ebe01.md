<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To look slyly, or with the eyes half closed, or through a crevice; to peep.

   **Usage:** informal; intransitive

   **Example:** Close your eyes, and no peeking!

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To be only slightly, partially visible, as if peering out from a hiding place.

   **Usage:** informal; intransitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To briefly move out of cover (to gather visual information).

   **Usage:** informal; intransitive; especially first-person shooters

   **Example:** Sniper watching the site; don't peek.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** video-games

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To take a look at; to check out.

   **Usage:** informal; transitive

   **Example:** Peek my new shoes.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To retrieve (a value) from a memory address.

   **Usage:** dated; informal; transitive
