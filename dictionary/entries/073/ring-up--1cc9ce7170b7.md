### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To telephone; to call someone on the telephone.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To enter (a payment) into a cash register, or till in a shop, or record a credit- or debit-card payment.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Just ring up these items first. You can ring up those separately.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To record the payment of.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I'll take these. You can ring me up.

   **Example:** The cashier took a long time to ring him up.

   **Usage:** transitive
