## Interjection

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Expressing pity, commiseration, or benediction (in a literally or figuratively religious way).

   **Usage:** second or third person

   **Example:** God help him, he's just trying to stay afloat.

   **Example:** Well, you certainly tried your best, God help you.

   **Source synonyms:** heaven help someone; God love someone

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Expressing a threat or a warning to the person who violates some rule or condition. (The implication is that something so bad will happen, such as the speaker punishing the other person or a disaster ensuing, that the other person will need God's protection from that wrath or calamity.)

   **Usage:** second or third person

   **Example:** God help you

   **Example:** God help them

   **Example:** God help you if you break my equipment because you were acting like a bull in a china shop. \[ = I'll beat your ass if you do that.\]

   **Example:** God help you if you set sail in this weather. \[ = You'll be lucky to survive if you do that.\]

   **Source synonyms:** heaven help someone

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An expression of astonishment, dismay, bemusement, or a jocular hint of any of those.

   **Usage:** first-person

   **Example:** God help me

   **Example:** God help us

   **Example:** God help us! They're actually going to run an AI arms race without bothering to agree a framework for AI safety first.

   **Example:** They might just have the tiger by the tail, God help us.

   **Example:** I'm going to have a go, God help me. Wish me luck.

   **Source synonyms:** heaven help someone; lawdamercy; Jesus Christ; Jesus, Mary and Joseph; Lord love a duck
