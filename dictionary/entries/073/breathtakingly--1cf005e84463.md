## Adverb

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈbɹɛθˌteɪ.kɪŋ.li/ — source labels: US

### Forms

- more breathtakingly — comparative
- most breathtakingly — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In a breathtaking manner.

   **Example:** The view was breathtakingly beautiful.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To a degree that causes shock or awe; shockingly.

   **Example:** It was a breathtakingly foolish thing to say in public.
