### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Nearer the forward end, especially in the front of the body; nearer the head or forepart of an animal.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Nearer the forward end, especially in the front of the body; nearer the head or forepart of an animal.
   1. (of a part of a flower or leaf) Situated further away from the mainstem.

      **Usage:** not-comparable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; biology; botany; medicine; natural-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Nearer the forward end, especially in the front of the body; nearer the head or forepart of an animal.
   1. Pronounced with an obstruction located in front of the palato-alveolar region of the mouth, e.g. b, p, d, t.

      **Usage:** not-comparable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; human-sciences; linguistics; medicine; phonetics; phonology; sciences

   **Usage:** formal; not-comparable
