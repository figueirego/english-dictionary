## Adverb

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Too many to be counted (either by reason of being infinite or for practical constraints).

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The stars in the sky are uncountably many. Even a lifetime would not suffice to number them all.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In an uncountable fashion.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Some nouns can be used both countably and uncountably.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** grammar; human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. In a way that is incapable of being put into one-to-one correspondence with the natural numbers or any subset thereof.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences
