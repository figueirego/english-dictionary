### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A strike or blow that leads to death, especially a coup de grâce.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Something that prevents the completion, or ends the existence, of a project etc.; a fatal setback.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** The opening of the bridge dealt a deathblow to the ferry.
