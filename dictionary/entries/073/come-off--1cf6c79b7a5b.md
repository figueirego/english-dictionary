### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To become detached.

   **Example:** One of the wagon wheels came off.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To have some success; to succeed.

   **Example:** He tried his Chaplin impression, but it didn't really come off.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To have an orgasm.

   **Usage:** dated; intransitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To appear; to seem; to project a certain quality.

   **Example:** I'm sorry if I came off as condescending; that wasn't my intention.

   **Example:** You should be careful about how you come off during interviews.

   **Source synonyms:** come across

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To escape or get off (lightly, etc.); to come out of a situation without significant harm.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To occur; to take place; to turn out; to end up.

   **Example:** It came off as we expected.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To come away (from a place); to leave.

   **Usage:** obsolete?

   **Example:** The 290 bus route comes off the A316 at Hanworth.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To finish being printed.

   **Usage:** obsolete

   **Example:** This one came off easily.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** media; printing; publishing

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a printed sheet

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To quit (a drug or habit); to stop doing (something).

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. To stop playing (music).

   **Usage:** intransitive
