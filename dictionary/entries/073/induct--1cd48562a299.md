### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To bring in as a member; to make a part of.

   **Example:** Franklin was inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in 1987, the first female inductee \[…\]

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To formally or ceremoniously install in an office, position, etc.

   **Example:** It is my pleasure to induct the new Officers for this coming term.

   **Source synonyms:** seat (formal)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To introduce into (particularly if certain knowledge or experience is required, such as ritual adulthood or cults).

   **Example:** She was inducted into the ways of the legal profession.

   **Source synonyms:** initiate

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To draft into military service.

   **Example:** At the time of war the President is authorized by law to induct persons into the armed forces involuntarily.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To introduce; to bring in.

   **Usage:** obsolete

   **Example:** The ceremonies in the gathering were first inducted by the Venetians.
