### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. to put into a stack.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Stack up the boxes.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. to pile up; to accumulate.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To put a group of abstract things together.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** to stack up memories

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Often followed by against or among&#58; to compare with (something); to measure up.
