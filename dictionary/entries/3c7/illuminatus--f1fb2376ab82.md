## Noun

### Forms

- Illuminati — plural
- Illuminatuses — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A member of any of various societies, sects or other people claiming religious or intellectual enlightenment.
   1. A member of the early church who had received baptism and been given a lighted taper as a symbol of the spiritual illumination they had received.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A member of any of various societies, sects or other people claiming religious or intellectual enlightenment.
   1. A member of a Spanish sect of the sixteenth century, who believed that, by means of prayer, they had attained so perfect a state as to have no need of ordinances, sacraments, good works, etc.; the Alumbrados or Perfectibilists.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A member of any of various societies, sects or other people claiming religious or intellectual enlightenment.
   1. A member of certain associations in Europe who combined to promote social reform in pursuit of perfection; especially one originated in 1776 by Adam Weishaupt.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A member of any of various societies, sects or other people claiming religious or intellectual enlightenment.
   1. A member of an obscure sect of French familists.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A member of any of various societies, sects or other people claiming religious or intellectual enlightenment.
   1. A member of the Hesychasts, Mystics, and Quietists.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A member of any of various societies, sects or other people claiming religious or intellectual enlightenment.
   1. A member of the Rosicrucians.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A member of an alleged global elite secret society which has as its ultimate objective the subjugation of humanity (world domination or New World Order).
