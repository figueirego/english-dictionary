   **Example:** extract of dandelion

**Source sense R1.S4**

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A solid preparation obtained by evaporating a solution of a drug, etc., or the fresh juice of a plant (distinguished from an abstract).

**Source sense R1.S6**

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To draw out; to pull out; to remove forcibly from a fixed position, as by traction or suction, etc.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to extract a tooth from its socket, a stump from the earth, or a splinter from the finger

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To withdraw by squeezing, distillation, or other mechanical or chemical process. Compare abstract (transitive verb).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to extract an essential oil from a plant

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To pick out; to cite or reproduce a snippet of

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** extract a passage from a text.

   **Example:** extract a line from a song.

   **Example:** extract a clip from a video

   **Example:** extract the drum parts from an mp3.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To select parts of a whole

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** We need to try to extract the positives from the defeat.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To determine (a root of a number).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Please extract the cube root of 27.
