### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make suitable; to make to correspond; to fit or suit.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** proportion

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To fit by alteration; to modify or remodel for a different purpose; to adjust.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to adapt a story for the stage

   **Example:** to adapt an old machine to a new manufacture

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To make by altering or fitting something else; to produce by change of form or character.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to bring out a play adapted from the French

   **Example:** a word of an adapted form

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To make oneself comfortable to a new thing.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** They could not adapt to the new climate and so perished.
