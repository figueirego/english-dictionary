**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A rock one keeps and cares for as if it were a pet, often purchased as a novelty item and jokingly treated as having needs like those of a living animal.

   **Example:** I made a cute family of pet rocks by putting eye, nose, and mouth stickers on big stones.
