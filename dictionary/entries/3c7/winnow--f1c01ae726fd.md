### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To subject (granular material, especially food grain) to a current of air separating heavier and lighter components, as grain from chaff.

   **Usage:** transitive; literal

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** agriculture; business; lifestyle

   **Source synonyms:** wind

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To separate, sift, analyse, or test by separating items having different values.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** They winnowed the field to twelve.

   **Example:** They winnowed the winners from the losers.

   **Example:** They winnowed the losers from the winners.

   **Source synonyms:** comb out

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To blow upon or toss about by blowing; to set in motion as with a fan or wings.

   **Usage:** literary; transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To move about with a flapping motion, as of wings; to flutter.

   **Usage:** dated; intransitive; literary
