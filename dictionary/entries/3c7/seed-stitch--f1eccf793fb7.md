### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A basic basketweave in which knit and purl stitches alternate in every column ("wale") and every row ("course"), i.e. every knit stitch is flanked on all four sides by purl stitches, and vice versa.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; knitting; manufacturing; textiles

   **Source synonyms:** moss stitch

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One in a series of unconnected straight stitches facing random directions and used to fill empty space.

   **Usage:** embroidery
