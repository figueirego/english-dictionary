### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To remove (a crease or creases) with an iron.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** That shirt still has a few more wrinkles to iron out.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To make (something) flat or smooth as if with an iron.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To resolve (a dispute); to solve (a problem).

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** Let's just sit down and iron out an agreement on this issue.

   **Example:** We need to iron out the wrinkles in the plan before implementing it.
