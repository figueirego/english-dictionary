## Noun

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ɹɪˈbɛl.i.ən/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Forms

- rebellions — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Armed resistance to an established government or ruler.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** The government is doing its best to stop rebellion in the country.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Defiance of authority or control; the act of rebelling.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** Having a tattoo was Mathilda's personal rebellion against her parents.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An organized, forceful subversion of the law of the land in an attempt to replace it with another form of government.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** The army general led a successful rebellion and became president of the country.
