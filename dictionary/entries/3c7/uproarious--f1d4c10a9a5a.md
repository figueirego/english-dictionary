### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Causing, or likely to cause, an uproar.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Characterized by uproar, that is, loud, confused noise, or by noisy and uncontrollable laughter.

   **Source synonyms:** uproarish; blaring; blasting; booming; cacophonous; clangorous; clamorous; clattering; deafening; dinsome (Scotland); earsplitting; loud; noisy; obstreperous; piercing; rackety; raucous; resounding; roisterous; rowdy; strepitous; strident; thundering; tumultuous; uproarious; vociferous

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Extremely funny; hilarious.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Source synonyms:** amusing; chucklesome; comedic; comic; comical; droll; filarious (slang); frolicsome (rare); funnisome; funny; gut-busting; hilarious; howlarious (slang); humourful; humorous; hysterical; jocular; joculatory (obsolete); jokes (Multicultural-London-English); jokesome; joky; laughable; laugh-out-loud; laughworthy (rare); playful; priceless (figuratively); rib-tickling; risible; screaming; sidesplitting; sniggersome; tattyfilarious; thalian; thigh-slapping; uproarious; wacky; zany

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. In a mess; dishevelled, untidy.

   **Usage:** figuratively
