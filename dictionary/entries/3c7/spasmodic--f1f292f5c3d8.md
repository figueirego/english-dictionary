### Forms

- most spasmodic — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of or relating to a spasm; resembling a sudden contraction of the muscles.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Convulsive; consisting of spasms.

   **Example:** spasmodic asthma

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Intermittent or fitful; occurring in abrupt bursts.

   **Example:** spasmodic zeal or industry

   **Source synonyms:** patchy; stop-start; broken; fitful; ragged; catchy; discontinuous; intermissive; intermittent; patchy; spasmodic; stop-start

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Erratic or unsustained.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Of or relating to the spasmodic poets, a group of British Victorian poets who wrote introspective drama in verse.
