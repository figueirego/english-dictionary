### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Capable of being dug.
   1. Possible and (especially) feasible to dig physically.

      **Example:** The rocky soil was diggable, but only with difficulty.

      **Source synonyms:** excavatable

      **Source antonyms:** undiggable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Capable of being dug.
   1. Worthy of digging (appreciating and enjoying).

      **Usage:** uncommon

      **Example:** Yeah, their jazz is diggable, but they leave something on the table by not getting funkier with the horns.

      **Source synonyms:** appreciable; enjoyable
