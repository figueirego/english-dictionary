### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The inability to feel pain.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** epidural analgesia

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; pathology; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** analgia

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A process or the act of temporarily reducing the ability to feel pain.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** This office procedure is quick and straightforward, but it does require some analgesia.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; pathology; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** pain relief

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A medication that performs this action&#58; one that relieves pain.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; metonymically; often; proscribed; uncountable

   **Example:** apply an analgesia

   **Example:** She was able to take analgesia orally.

   **Source synonyms:** analgesic
