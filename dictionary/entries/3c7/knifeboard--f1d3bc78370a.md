### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A board on which knives are cleaned or polished.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A way of arranging benches back to back, resembling a knifeboard; common on the open upper deck of early double-deck omnibuses and trams.

   **Usage:** attributive; historical

<a id="r1-s3"></a>
