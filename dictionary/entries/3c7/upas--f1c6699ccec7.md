## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A tree, Antiaris toxicaria, of the mulberry family, common in the forests of Java and the neighboring islands, with poisonous secretions.

   **Usage:** countable; usually

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A virulent poison used in Java and the adjacent islands for poisoning arrows and derived from the tree.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A poison prepared from the climbing plant Strychnos tieute.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually
