### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Deserving of ridicule; foolish, absurd.

   **Example:** That hairstyle looks ridiculous.

   **Example:** It's ridiculous to charge so much for a little souvenir.

   **Example:** You make ridiculous statements a lot, like saying that UFOs are real.

   **Source synonyms:** silly; willy nilly; frivolous; goofy; funny; humorous; absurd; odd; surreal; unreasonable; absurd; Laputan; cockamamie (slang); cockeyed; farcical; foolish; full of it; irrational; inconsistent; incongruous; jabberwocky; laughable; ludicrous; nonsensical; preposterous; priceless (figuratively); rich; ridiculous; skimble-skamble; unreasonable

   **Source antonyms:** straightforward; serious; somber; solemn

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Astonishing, extreme, unbelievable.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** In a ridiculous feat of engineering, the team can calculate the difference in arrival time to within one-tenth of a nanosecond.

   **Example:** Gaines is a classic motor player with a low center of gravity and ridiculous strength.
