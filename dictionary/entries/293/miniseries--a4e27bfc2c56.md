### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A radio or television series with a small number of episodes not intended to last a complete season.

   **Example:** My favorite miniseries of all time is the old BBC adaptation of "I, Claudius".

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A relatively short comic book series with a predetermined number of instalments.
