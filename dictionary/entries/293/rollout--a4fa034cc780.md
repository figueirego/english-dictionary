### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An act of rolling out; gradual deployment.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The phase of a landing after touchdown but before the aircraft slows to taxi speed.

   **Example:** The pilot kept a vigilant eye out for runway obstructions throughout the rollout.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** aeronautics; aerospace; aviation; business; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** American-football; ball-games; football; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A form of analysis in which the same position is played many times (with different dice rolls) and the various outcomes are recorded.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** backgammon; games

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A game variant in which players progressively reveal their cards.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** card-games; poker
