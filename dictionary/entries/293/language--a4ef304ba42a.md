### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A body of words, and set of methods of combining them (called a grammar), understood by a community and used as a form of communication.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** The English and German languages are both members of the West Germanic language family.

   **Example:** Deaf and mute people communicate using sign language.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The ability to communicate using words.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** the gift of language

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A sublanguage&#58; the slang of a particular community or jargon of a particular specialist field.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** legal language; the language of chemistry

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The specific wording or style of a text, such as a law or a contract.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The expression of thought (the communication of meaning) in a specified way; that which communicates something, as language does.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

   **Example:** body language; the language of the eyes

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A body of sounds, signs or signals by which animals communicate, and by which plants are sometimes also thought to communicate.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A computer language; a machine language.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A manner of expression.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. The particular words used in a speech or a passage of text.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** The language used in the law does not permit any other interpretation.

   **Example:** The language he used to talk to me was obscene.

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. Profanity.

   **Usage:** euphemistic; uncountable
