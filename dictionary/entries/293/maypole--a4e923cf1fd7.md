### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A pole, garlanded with streamers held by people who dance around it to celebrate May Day.

   **Example:** How would you like the maypole decorated?

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A very tall girl or young lady.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A maypole-like structure of sticks placed about a sapling in the bowers of certain species of bowerbird.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences; ornithology
