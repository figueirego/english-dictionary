## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

- race realists — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A believer in scientific racism (“racism presented as science or as supported by science”); a person who believes empirical evidence exists to support the notion of innate racial differences (that some races are superior and others inferior).

   **Usage:** euphemistic

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One who believes that the human species is divided into observable races.

   **Source antonyms:** race detractor

<a id="r2"></a>

## Adjective · Record 2

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Pertaining to the belief that scientific evidence exists for inborn racial differences ("race realism"&#58; scientific racism), generally used to support racial discrimination or the idea that some racial groups are inferior to others.

   **Usage:** not-comparable
