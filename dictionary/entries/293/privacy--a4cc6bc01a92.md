### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The state of being secluded from the presence, sight, or knowledge of others.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** I need my privacy, so please stay out of my room.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Freedom from unwanted or undue disturbance of one's private life.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Those prying neighbours have robbed me of my sense of privacy.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Freedom from damaging publicity, public scrutiny, surveillance, and disclosure of personal information, usually by a government or a private organization.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Privacy is assumed by many to be among common-law rights.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A place of seclusion.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A relationship between parties seen as being a result of their mutual interest or participation in a given transaction, contract etc.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

   **Source synonyms:** privity

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Secrecy.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A private matter.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete

   **Source synonyms:** secret
