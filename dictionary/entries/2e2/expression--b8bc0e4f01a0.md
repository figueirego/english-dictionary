### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The action of expressing thoughts, ideas, feelings, etc.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A particular way of phrasing an idea.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A colloquialism or idiom.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The expression "break a leg!" should not be taken literally.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A facial appearance usually associated with an emotion.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** They stared at the newcomer with a puzzled expression.

   **Example:** The best poker players can tell if the opponents have a good hand by looking at their expression.

   **Example:** Her expression changed from joy to misery after realising her winning lottery ticket had expired.
