### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A meeting or date.

   **Example:** I have a rendezvous with a friend in three hours.

   **Source synonyms:** assignation

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An agreement to meet at a certain place and time.

   **Example:** Get the party started at the rendezvous at oh six hours.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A place appointed for a meeting, or at which persons customarily meet.

   **Source synonyms:** meeting place

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The appointed place for troops, or for the ships of a fleet, to assemble; also, a place for enlistment.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A set of orbital maneuvers during which two spacecraft arrive at the same orbit and approach to a very close distance.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** aerospace; business; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; rocketry

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A retreat or refuge.
