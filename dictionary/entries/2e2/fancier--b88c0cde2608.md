### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who fancies; a person with a special interest, attraction or liking for something.

   **Example:** She's definitely a cat fancier&#58; she has nine of them in her home and the walls are covered with pictures of more.

   **Source synonyms:** aficionado

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person who breeds or grows a particular animal or plant for points of excellence.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
