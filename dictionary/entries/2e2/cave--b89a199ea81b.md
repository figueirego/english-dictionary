**Source sense R1.S1**

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A storage cellar, especially for wine or cheese.

   **Example:** This wine has been aged in our cave for thirty years.

   **Example:** My room was a cozy cave where I could escape from my family.

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To surrender.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** He caved under pressure.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To collapse.

   **Example:** First the braces buckled, then the roof began to cave, then we ran.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To hollow out or undermine.

   **Example:** The levee has been severely caved by the river current.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To engage in the recreational exploration of caves.

   **Source synonyms:** spelunk

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. In room-and-pillar mining, to extract a deposit of rock by breaking down a pillar which had been holding it in place.

   **Example:** The deposit is caved by knocking out the posts.
