**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To opt to have winnings staked as another wager, typically on the same outcome, rather than paid out.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** gambling; games

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

2. To decline to exit an investment position in expectation of continued (as opposed to new) growth.

   **Usage:** informal; transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance

**Source sense R1.S3**
