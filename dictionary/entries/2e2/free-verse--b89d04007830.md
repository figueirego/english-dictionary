### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A poetic form divided into lines of no particular length or meter, without a rhyme scheme.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Whitman uses free verse to achieve effects impossible under even the broad restrictions of blank verse.
