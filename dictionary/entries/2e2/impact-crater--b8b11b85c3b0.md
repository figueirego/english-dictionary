### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A crater formed from a hypervelocity impact, typically of a meteorite, as opposed to one formed by other means such as vulcanism.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astronomy; geography; geology; natural-sciences

   **Source synonyms:** astrobleme; meteor crater

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A crater formed by a process involving impact (not necessarily a hypervelocity one).
