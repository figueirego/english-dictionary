### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Either of two major versions of the main religious text in the Judeo-Christian group of traditions.
   1. The main religious text in Christianity.

      **Example:** In my religion class we learn about the Bible, as well as religious texts of other religions.

      **Source synonyms:** Christian Bible; Holy Bible; Good Book; Book; word of God; word of life

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Either of two major versions of the main religious text in the Judeo-Christian group of traditions.
   1. The Jewish holy book that was largely incorporated into the Christian Bible.

      **Example:** She's Jewish, but she doesn't read the Bible because she's not religious.

      **Source synonyms:** Tanakh; Tanach; Jewish Bible; Hebrew Bible; Hebrew Scriptures; Hebrew-Aramaic Scriptures; Old Testament\[Appendix&#58;Glossary

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The analogous holy book of another religion or belief system.

   **Example:** He conveys to his foreign students the importance of the Qur'an by explaining that it is the Bible of Islam.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The principal important document of some area of skill or expertise.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** This technical handbook is the Bible of our trade.
