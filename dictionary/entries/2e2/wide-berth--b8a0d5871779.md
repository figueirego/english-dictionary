## Noun

### Forms

- wide berths — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Adequate distance from sea vessels or other objects to ensure safety and maneuverability.

   **Example:** Steer to port and give the rocks a wide berth.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A considerable or comfortable distance from a person or object, especially for safety or deliberate avoidance.

   **Usage:** broadly; idiomatic

   **Example:** Our cat always gives the vacuum cleaner a wide berth.

   **Example:** He's so obnoxious that everyone gives him a wide berth.
