### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To be full to overflowing; to bristle.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To be wealthy.

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To be highly productive.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To be present or available in large numbers or quantities; to be plentiful.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Wild animals abound wherever man does not stake his claim.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To revel in.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To be copiously supplied.

   **Example:** The wilderness abounds in traps.

   **Example:** This pond abounds with fish.
