### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A word or idiom of the Hungarian language (that has been borrowed by another language).

   **Usage:** countable; rhetoric

   **Source synonyms:** Magyarism

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A custom or typical practice (e.g. in music) of the Hungarian people.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Synonym of Hungarism, a Hungarian fascist movement.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** Hungarism (synonym; synonym-of)
