### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To add a name to the list of people who are participating in something.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** We signed Bob up for kitchen cleanup duty.

   **Source synonyms:** register; enroll

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To add one's own name to the list of people who are participating in something (including amateur, but not professional, teams).

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Terry signed up for the soccer team.

   **Source synonyms:** register; reserve; book; [schedule](../17d/schedule--5f72005e81c4.md); enroll

   **Source antonyms:** cancel; resign

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To agree to purchase some good or service.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** After much deliberation we decided to sign up for satellite TV.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To enlist in the armed forces.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Didn't you hear? He's just signed up!

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war

   **Source synonyms:** enlist
