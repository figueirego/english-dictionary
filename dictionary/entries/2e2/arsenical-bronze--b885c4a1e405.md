## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A natural and artificial alloy of copper containing a small amount of arsenic, that oxidates less than pure copper and was widely used during the chalcolithic period.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Oetzi the Iceman's axe was of arsenical bronze.
