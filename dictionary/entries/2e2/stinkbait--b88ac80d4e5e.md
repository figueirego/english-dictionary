### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A form of bait composed of malodorous ingredients, designed to attract fish (primarily catfish) that hunt by scent.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Deke mashed a wad of stinkbait onto the treble hook, then rinsed his fingers in the water to reduce the foul stench before lifting his beer can.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** fishing; hobbies; lifestyle
