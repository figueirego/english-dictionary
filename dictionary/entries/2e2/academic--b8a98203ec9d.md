### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Belonging to the school or philosophy of Plato.

   **Example:** the academic sect or philosophy

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Belonging to an academy or other higher institution of learning, or a scholarly society or organization.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. In particular&#58; relating to literary, classical, or artistic studies like the humanities, rather than to technical or vocational studies like engineering or welding.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Having little practical use or value, as by being overly detailed and unengaging, or by being theoretical and speculative with no practical importance.

   **Example:** I have always had an academic interest in hacking.

   **Example:** the distinction is academic

   **Example:** an academic question

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Having a love of or aptitude for learning.

   **Example:** I’m more academic than athletic — I get lower marks in phys. ed. than in anything else.
