## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An interpretation of science intended for a general audience, rather than for other scientists or students.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of, or having to do with science, but aimed at ordinary people as opposed to scientists; intended for general consumption.

   **Usage:** attributive; uncountable

   **Example:** It was published in a popular science magazine.
