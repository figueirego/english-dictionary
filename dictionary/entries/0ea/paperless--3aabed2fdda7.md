## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Without paper.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The paperless walls of the bedroom looked grey and cheerless.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Relating to or involving the communication or storage of information electronically, rather than with paper.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Many banks are now offering paperless billing.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Lacking official documentation or evidence of identity.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The government will aim to identify paperless refugees.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a person
