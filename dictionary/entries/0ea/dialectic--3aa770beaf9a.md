## Noun · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any formal system of reasoning that arrives at a truth by the exchange of logical arguments.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A contradiction of ideas that serves as the determining factor in their interaction.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** This situation created the inner dialectic of American history.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. development by way of overcoming internal contradictions

   **Usage:** Marxism; countable; uncountable
