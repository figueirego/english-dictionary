### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see put, past.

   **Example:** He put the ball past the goalkeeper.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To consider (something) to be not possible for (someone) (to do); (see also put it past).

   **Usage:** ditransitive; idiomatic

   **Example:** Would he go so far as to call the police? I wouldn't put it past him.

   **Example:** I wouldn't put even that past him. In fact, I'd put nothing past him.

   **Source context (verbatim):** in the negative

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To conceal or disguise (something) so that it is undetected by (someone) (see also put one past).

   **Example:** You'll never put that wheeze past our eagle-eyed boss.
