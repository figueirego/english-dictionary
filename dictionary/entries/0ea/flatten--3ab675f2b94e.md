**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make something flat or flatter.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** As there was a lot of damage, we chose the heavy roller to flatten the pitch.

   **Example:** Mary would flatten the dough before rolling it into pretzels.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To press one's body tightly against a surface, such as a wall or floor, especially in order to avoid being seen or harmed.

   **Usage:** reflexive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To knock down or lay low.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The prize fighter quickly flattened his challenger.

   **Usage:** intransitive
