**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A louse (Pediculus humanus).

   **Usage:** British-Army; Canada; US; colloquial; dated; slang

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war

   **Usage:** Australia; Canada; US; childish; colloquial; plural-normally

   **Example:** I’m not drinking from his glass until I wash the cooties off it.
