**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of the ancient festivals held in honour of the Olympian god Dionysus, and corresponding to the Roman Bacchanalia.

   **Usage:** historical; plural; plural-only

   **Example:** The greater Dionysia were held at Athens in March or April, and were celebrated with elaborate performances of tragedies and comedies.
