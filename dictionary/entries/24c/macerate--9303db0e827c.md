### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To soften (something) or separate it into pieces by (or as if by) soaking it in a heated or unheated liquid.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To reduce solids to small pieces (in a macerator).

   **Source synonyms:** comminute; crush; pulverize

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To make lean; to cause to waste away.

   **Usage:** archaic

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

   **Usage:** obsolete
