**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The middle part of a season, such as a sporting, television, or growing season.

   **Example:** Tourism has a high season (summer), a midseason (spring and fall), and a low season (winter).

   **Example:** The amount of water needed is about 50% of the water used in the midseason.

   **Example:** Underperforming TV programs are often replaced in the midseason of the schedule.
