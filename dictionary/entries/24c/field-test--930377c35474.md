## Noun

### Forms

- field tests — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A test of something conducted in conditions under which it was designed to actually operate, especially out in the real world instead of in a laboratory or workshop.

   **Example:** When it comes to combat equipment and vehicle design, computer simulations and field tests are both crucial, and neither one can be a total substitute for the other.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A test of something conducted in conditions under which it was designed to actually operate, especially out in the real world instead of in a laboratory or workshop.
   1. A test of something that can be performed in the field rather than solely in a laboratory or workshop.

      **Example:** Not until the 1990s did mycologists develop a field test that could differentiate between these types of fungus.
