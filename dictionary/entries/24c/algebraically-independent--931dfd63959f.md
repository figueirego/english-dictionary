## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. (Of a subset S of the extension field L of a given field extension L / K) whose elements do not satisfy any non-trivial polynomial equation with coefficients in K.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; field theory

   **Example:** The singleton set \#92;\#123;\#92;alpha\#92;\#125; is algebraically independent over K if and only if the element \#92;alpha is transcendental over K.

   **Example:** A subset S\#92;subsetL is algebraically independent over K if every element of S is transcendental over K and over each of the extension fields over K generated by the remaining elements of S.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** algebra; mathematics; sciences
