## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

- centerpunches — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A hand tool used to prepare a workpiece prior to drilling a hole. It typically compresses and then suddenly releases a spring, creating a small indentation that, in turn, guides and centers the drill.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The indentation left by such a tool.
