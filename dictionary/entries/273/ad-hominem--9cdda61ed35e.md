## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

- ad hominems — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Ellipsis of argumentum ad hominem&#58; A fallacious objection to an argument or factual claim by appealing to a characteristic or belief of the person making the argument or claim, rather than by addressing the substance of the argument or producing evidence against the claim; an attempt to argue against an opponent's idea by discrediting the opponent themselves.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; ellipsis

   **Alternative form of:** [argumentum ad hominem](../32a/argumentum-ad-hominem--ca87052c34fe.md)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A personal attack.

   **Usage:** informal; proscribed
