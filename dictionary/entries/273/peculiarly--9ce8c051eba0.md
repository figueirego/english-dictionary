### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Such as to be greater than usual; particularly; exceptionally.
   1. To greater degree than is usual.

      **Usage:** degree

      **Example:** He has a peculiarly strong accent.

      **Example:** This racehorse is peculiarly fast.

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Such as to be greater than usual; particularly; exceptionally.
   1. In a manner that is greater than usual.

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Such as to be strange or odd.
   1. Strangely, oddly.

      **Usage:** degree

      **Example:** His nose is peculiarly bent.
