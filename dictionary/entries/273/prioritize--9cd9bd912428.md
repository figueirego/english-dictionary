### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To value, do, or choose something first, or before other things.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** When I don't have time to buy everything at the store, I prioritize fresh fruit and vegetables over foods like rice or noodles.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To arrange or list a group of things in order of priority or importance.

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To rank something as having high priority.

   **Usage:** transitive
