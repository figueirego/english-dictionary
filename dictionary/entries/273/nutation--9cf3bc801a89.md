### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A bobbing motion that accompanies the precession of a spinning rigid body.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>
