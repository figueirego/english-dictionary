### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The quality or state of being prudent&#58; circumspection and good judgment in knowing how best to act; (countable, archaic) an instance of this.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** cautel; discretion; farsightedness; foresightedness; judiciousness; providence; prudentness; prudency

   **Source antonyms:** imprudence; rashness; recklessness; unprudence

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Synonym of frugality (“the quality of avoiding unnecessary expenditure; economy, parsimony, thrift, thriftiness”).

   **Usage:** specifically; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** frugality (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Synonym of providence (“preparation for the future; foresight”).

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** providence (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Synonym of wisdom (“an element of personal character that enables one to distinguish the wise from the unwise; wise advice”).

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** sagacity; acuity; astuteness; clairvoyance; discernment; discrimination; gumption; insight; nous; penetration; perspicacity; prudence; sagaciousness; sagacity; sageliness; sageness; sapience; savvy; sharpness; sophy (obsolete); understanding; wisdom; wiseness; wit; wisdom (synonym; synonym-of)
