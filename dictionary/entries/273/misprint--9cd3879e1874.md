**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An accidental mistake in print.

   **Example:** There is a misprint in the first print run&#58; On page 42 where the character yells, "You're going to catch a cod if you spend all night fishing", the dialogue was meant to say, "You're going to catch a cold if you spend all night fishing."
