### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The process or result of rationalizing, or expressing in rational terms.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Some rationalization of the language of his lectures is, of course, necessary.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A statement of one's motives, or of the causes of some event.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Her self-flagellating rationalization for the theft was mostly ignored by the jury.

   **Source synonyms:** rationale; reasoning

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The reorganization of a company or organization in order to improve its efficiency through the reallocation of resources and changes in its workforce.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** It began with the familiar political motives of civil service rationalization and downsizing.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** economics; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** consolidation

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The concealment of true motivation in some non-threatening way.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; medicine; psychiatry; psychology; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The simplification of an expression without changing its value.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Here, the rationalization of the denominator was unnecessary.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences
