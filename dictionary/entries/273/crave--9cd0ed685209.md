### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To desire strongly, so as to satisfy an appetite; to long or yearn for.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** to crave for peace

   **Example:** to crave after wealth

   **Example:** to crave drugs

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To ask for earnestly; to beg or demand, as from a figure of authority.

   **Usage:** archaic; literary; transitive

   **Example:** I humbly crave your indulgence to read this letter until the end.
