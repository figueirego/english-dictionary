### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A number of things that follow on one after the other or are connected one after the other.

   **Example:** A series of seemingly inconsequential events led cumulatively to the fall of the company.

   **Source synonyms:** chain; line; sequence; stream; succession; array; chain; procession; consecution (archaic); sequence; series; string; succession; train

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A television or radio program consisting of several episodes that are broadcast at regular intervals.

   **Example:** Friends was one of the most successful television series in recent years.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** broadcasting; media

   **Source synonyms:** show; program

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Synonym of season (“one of the groups of episodes that together make up a whole series”).

   **Usage:** UK

   **Example:** I enjoyed the third series of Friends.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** broadcasting; media

   **Source synonyms:** season (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The sequence of partial sums ∑ᵢ₌₁ⁿa\_i of a given sequence aᵢ.

   **Example:** The harmonic series has been much studied.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A group of matches between two sides, with the aim being to win more matches than the opposition.

   **Example:** The Blue Jays are playing the Yankees in a four-game series.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; baseball; cricket; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The optional taxonomic rank above order/subseries, but below superorder.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; ichthyology; natural-sciences; taxonomy; zoology

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The optional taxonomic rank above group, but below epifamily.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; lepidopterology; natural-sciences; taxonomy; zoology

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

8. A subdivision of a genus, a taxonomic rank below that of section (and subsection) but above that of species.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences; taxonomy

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; commerce
