### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Possible to excuse; worthy of being excused.

   **Example:** excusable reason

   **Source synonyms:** forgivable; pardonable; venial

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Describing an illegal act that was committed involuntarily, for example under false information or because of a faulty device. Distinguished from justifiable, describing an illegal act that was intentional but justified for other reasons.
