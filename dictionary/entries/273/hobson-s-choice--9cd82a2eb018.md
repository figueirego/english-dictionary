### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A situation where a person is presented with the illusion of a free choice, but in reality there is only one option available&#58; accepting the offer or having nothing at all.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Source synonyms:** nonchoice

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A choice between several undesirable options; Sophie's choice.

   **Usage:** broadly; idiomatic

   **Source synonyms:** Catch-22; dilemma; Hobson's choice; Morton's fork; no-win situation; quandary; Sophie's choice

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The voice.

   **Usage:** Cockney; slang

   **Source synonyms:** Hobson's
