## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The frontmost edge of a wing or other airfoil of an aircraft; sometimes contains slots or slats.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** aeronautics; aerospace; business; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The foremost part of any physical object.

   **Example:** the leading edge of a cricket bat

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The foremost part of a trend etc; a vanguard.

   **Usage:** broadly

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The forefront of practice or technology.

   **Source synonyms:** bleeding edge
