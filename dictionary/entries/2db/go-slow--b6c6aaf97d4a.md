### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A period of time spent in an unusually slow fashion.

   **Usage:** Ireland; UK

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Deliberate slow working as a protest during an industrial dispute.

   **Usage:** Ireland; Nigeria; UK

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
