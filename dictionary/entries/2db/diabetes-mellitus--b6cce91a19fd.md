**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A medical disorder commonly called diabetes, characterized by varying or persistent hyperglycemia (high blood sugar levels), especially after eating. It is classified in two types, which are different etiologically but similar in clinical effects&#58; type 1 and type 2.

   **Usage:** uncountable
