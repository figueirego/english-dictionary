### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A habitual patron, regular purchaser, returning client; a person or company who has a custom of buying from a particular business.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person or company who purchases or receives a product or service from a business or merchant or intends to do so.

   **Example:** Every person who passes by is a potential customer.

   **Example:** Airlines are the chief customers of the commercial divisions of large-aircraft manufacturers such as Airbus, Boeing, and Embraer.

   **Source synonyms:** patron; client; solicitor

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A person, especially one engaging in some sort of interaction with others.

   **Usage:** informal

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A native official who exacted customs duties.

   **Usage:** India; historical
