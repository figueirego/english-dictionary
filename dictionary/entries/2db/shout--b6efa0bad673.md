### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A loud burst of voice or voices; a violent and sudden outcry, especially that of a multitude expressing joy, triumph, exultation, anger, or great effort.

   **Example:** give out a shout

   **Example:** hear a desperate shout

   **Example:** an ear-piercing shout

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A round of drinks in a pub; the turn to pay the shot or scot; an act of paying for a round of drinks.

   **Usage:** Australia; New-Zealand; UK; slang

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A call-out for an emergency services team.

   **Usage:** Australia; UK; slang

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A greeting, name-check or other mention, for example on a radio or TV program.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** Next up the new single from Beyoncé, but first a shout to Barry Bloggins and his wife Belinda...

   **Source synonyms:** shout out

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A suggestion; an idea.

   **Usage:** informal

## Verb · Etymology 1 · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ʃaʊt/ — variety unspecified in the source labels
- IPA: /ʃʌʊt/ — source labels: Canada
- IPA: /ʃuːt/ — source labels: Northumbria

### Forms

- shouts — present; singular; third-person
- shouting — participle; present
- shouted — participle; past
- shouted — past

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To utter a sudden and loud cry, as in joy, triumph, exultation or anger, or to attract attention, to animate others, etc.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Mom shouted to us to come sit at the table.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To utter with a shout; to cry; to shout out

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** They shouted his name to get his attention.

   **Source synonyms:** halloo

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To forcefully attract attention or proclaim one’s presence.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To pay for food, drink or entertainment for others.

   **Usage:** Australia; New-Zealand; slang

   **Source synonyms:** treat

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To post a text message (for example, email) in upper case, regarded as the electronic messaging equivalent of oral shouting.

   **Usage:** Internet

   **Example:** Please don't shout in the chat room.

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To treat with shouts or clamor.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive
