### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To convert from church-controlled to independent of the church; to secularize.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** They will laicise each of the schools in the district.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To reduce from clergy to layman.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Due to his controversial views the Vatican decided to laicise the priest.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To convert to lay status.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The soup kitchen laicised, it moved out of the church and registered as an independent non-profit.
