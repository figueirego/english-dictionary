## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any nonzero ring R such that for any two (two-sided) ideals P and Q in R, the product PQ = 0 (the zero ideal) if and only if P = 0 or Q = 0.

   **Usage:** ring theory

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** algebra; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Synonym of prime subring.
