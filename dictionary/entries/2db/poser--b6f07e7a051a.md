### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A particularly difficult question or puzzle.

   **Usage:** UK

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Someone who asks a question or sets a problem.

   **Example:** Even as a child, she was a habitual poser of difficult questions.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Someone who, or something which, poses; a person who sets their body in a fixed position, such as for photography or painting.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A poseur (one who affects some behaviour, style, attitude or other condition, often to impress or influence others); a fake fan.

   **Usage:** derogatory; slang; chiefly music fandom
