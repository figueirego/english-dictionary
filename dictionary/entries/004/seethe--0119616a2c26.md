### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of a liquid or other substance, or a container holding it&#58; to be boiled (vigorously); to become boiling hot.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of a liquid, vapor, etc., or a container holding it&#58; to foam or froth in an agitated manner, as if boiling.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

   **Source synonyms:** bubble

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of a person&#58; to be in an agitated or angry mental state, often in a way that is not obvious to others.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

   **Source synonyms:** broil

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Of a place&#58; to be filled with many people or things moving about actively; to buzz with activity; also, of people or things&#58; to move about actively in a crowd or group.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Of a place&#58; to have inhabitants in an angry or disaffected mood; to be in a state of unrest.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To overboil (something) so that it loses its flavour or texture; hence (figurative), to cause (the body, the mind, the spirit, etc.) to become dull through too much alcoholic drink or heat.

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive; chiefly passive voice

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To soak (something) in a liquid; to drench, to steep.

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive; chiefly passive voice

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To boil (something); especially, to cook (food) by boiling or stewing; also, to keep (something) boiling.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. Of the stomach&#58; to digest (food).

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; physiology; sciences
