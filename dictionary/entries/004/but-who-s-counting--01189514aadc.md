### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used as a retort or comeback, often to deprecate oneself or another for excessive concern or attention to detail.

   **Usage:** colloquial; humorous; rhetoric; sarcastic

   **Example:** There are only 258 more shopping days until Christmas, but who's counting?

   **Example:** You've made that mistake eight times now. But who's counting?

   **Example:** I've been looking for a job for six months now, but who's counting?
