### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A rare neurovisual perceptual disorder characterized by distorted perception of human faces, in which facial features may appear altered in size, shape, position, or configuration despite intact visual input. It is typically associated with dysfunction in cortical regions involved in face processing, particularly in the occipitotemporal visual pathway.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; neurology; neuroscience; ophthalmology; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** demon face syndrome
