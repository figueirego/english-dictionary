**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A marking (often consisting of an image of a bicycle with two inverted V shapes either above or below it) on the surface of a paved road shared by both bicycles and other vehicles indicating a portion of the road that cyclists may use.

   **Usage:** Canada; US

   **Source synonyms:** shared lane marking
