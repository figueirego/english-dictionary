### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To leave secretly with another person, often with the intention of getting married or of living together against the wishes of the family.

   **Example:** She ran away with my best friend.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To steal something and physically escape with it.

   **Example:** The robber ran away with some valuable paintings.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To be misled (by).

   **Example:** Don't run away with the idea that this money will solve all your problems.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To overwhelm, get the better of.

   **Example:** He allowed his emotions to run away with him.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To be superior or outstanding in something.

   **Example:** Team USA ran away with the medal count.
