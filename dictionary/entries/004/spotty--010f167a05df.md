## Adjective · Record 1

### Forms

- spottier — comparative
- spottiest — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having spots; spotted.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Occurring in non-contiguous positions or locations.

   **Example:** Mobile phone reception in this area tends to be rather spotty.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of inconsistent quality.

   **Example:** Many of the buyers had a spotty credit history.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of goods or conditions

   **Source synonyms:** shoddy; iffy

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Inconsistent&#58; not reliably good, sometimes bad.

   **Example:** Many of the low-cost accessories (cords, chargers, etc) sold in the dollar store had spotty quality.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of quality

   **Source synonyms:** shoddy; iffy
