### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see go, to, work. (This sense is a translation hub.)

   **Example:** I used to go to school in Bristol in the morning, and then I'd go to work in my hometown in the afternoon.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To begin performing some task or work.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To go to one's job, as by commuting.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To beat up, to batter.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I saw Bruno go to work on Big Timmy; when he was through he didn't have much of a face left.
