### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A difficult situation from which there is no escape because it involves mutually conflicting or dependent conditions.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** For us it’s been a real Catch-22&#58; when we have the time to take a vacation, we don’t have enough money, and when we have enough money, we don’t have the time.

   **Source synonyms:** Catch-22; dilemma; Hobson's choice; Morton's fork; no-win situation; quandary; Sophie's choice
