### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Your own self (singular).

   **Usage:** pronoun; reflexive

   **Example:** Be careful with that fire or you'll burn yourself.

   **Example:** After a good night's sleep you'll feel like yourself again.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. You (singular); used emphatically, especially to indicate exclusiveness of the referent's participation in the predicate, i.e., that no one else is involved.

   **Usage:** emphatic; pronoun

   **Example:** You yourself know that what you wrote was wrong.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. You (singular); even when "you" has not been used previously; see usage notes.

   **Usage:** Ireland; especially; proscribed

   **Example:** I spoke with yourself last in February.

   **Example:** No one understands me like yourself.

   **Example:** My colleague and yourself are invited to the conference.

   **Example:** I only spoke to my lawyer and yourself about the issue.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Oneself.

   **Example:** These days you have to watch yourself in the center of the city.
