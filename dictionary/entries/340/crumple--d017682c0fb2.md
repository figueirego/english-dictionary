### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To rumple; to press into wrinkles by crushing together.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He crumpled the note and threw it away.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To cause to collapse.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He crumpled the car's body panels when he backed into a post.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To become wrinkled.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The car's body panels crumpled when they hit the post.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To collapse; to surrender.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

   **Example:** The team's defensive strategy crumpled.

   **Example:** The defenders crumpled owing to exhaustion and dehydration.

   **Source synonyms:** buckle; cave; cave in; fold
