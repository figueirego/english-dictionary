## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The highest gear that is available in a motor vehicle or car.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** His Packard rarely saw top gear, because in the 1930s this county barely had any roads smooth and straight enough to use it on.

   **Source synonyms:** fifth gear; overdrive

   **Source antonyms:** granny gear; low gear; first gear

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The highest level of performance or activity.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

   **Example:** Things really shifted into top gear once they found a new angel investor.

   **Source synonyms:** overdrive; full throttle

   **Source antonyms:** low gear
