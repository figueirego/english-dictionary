### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A bright circumpolar asterism of the northern sky, said to resemble a ladle or cart, and a part of the constellation Ursa Major which includes the stars Mizar, Dubhe, and Alkaid. The other stars comprising it are Alioth, Megrez, Merak, and Phecda.

   **Usage:** US; especially
