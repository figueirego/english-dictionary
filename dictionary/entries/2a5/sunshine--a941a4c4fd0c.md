## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈsʌnʃaɪn/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Forms

- sunshines — plural
- sun-shine — alternative; archaic

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The direct rays, light or warmth of the sun.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** We were warmed by the bright sunshine.

   **Source synonyms:** sun; sunlight

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A location on which the sun's rays fall.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** We moved out of the shade and into the sunshine.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Geniality or cheerfulness.

   **Usage:** figuratively; uncountable; usually

   **Example:** I enjoyed the sunshine of her smile.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A source of cheerfulness or joy.

   **Usage:** figuratively; uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The effect which the sun has when it lights and warms some place.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A friendly form of address often reserved for juniors.

   **Usage:** Ireland; UK; uncountable; usually

   **Example:** Alright, sunshine, safe to cross now.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. An ironic form of address directed toward someone perceived as inferior or troublesome.

   **Usage:** Ireland; UK; uncountable; usually

   **Example:** OK, sunshine, listen up and listen good. There's five vandalised telephone boxes out there and I know you're responsible.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Used to address someone who has just woken up or is very sleepy.

   **Usage:** humorous; uncountable; usually

   **Example:** Good morning, sunshine!

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. Ellipsis of orange sunshine.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; ellipsis; slang; uncommon; uncountable; usually

   **Alternative form of:** [orange sunshine](../142/orange-sunshine--5097588709fa.md)
