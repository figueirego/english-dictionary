<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of shape&#58;
   1. Circular or cylindrical; having a circular cross-section in one direction.

      **Usage:** physical

      **Example:** We sat at a round table to make conversation easier.

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of shape&#58;
   1. Spherical; shaped like a ball; having a circular cross-section in more than one direction.

      **Usage:** physical

      **Example:** The ancient Egyptian demonstrated that the Earth is round, not flat.

**Source sense R1.S3**

   1. Loosely or approximately circular.

      **Usage:** physical

   1. Plump.
