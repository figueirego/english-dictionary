### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see look, up.

   **Example:** We stayed up late to look up at the stars.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To have better prospects.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** Things started looking up after Jim moved back in with his parents.

   **Source synonyms:** get better; improve; ameliorate; aprimorate (non-native speakers’ English (chiefly Portuguese)); amend; batten; better (formal); enhance; hone; improve; meliorate; refine; get better; look up

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To obtain, or seek to obtain, information about something.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To obtain, or seek to obtain, information about something.
   1. To obtain information about something from a reference book.

      **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

      **Example:** I didn't know what a mitochondrion was until I looked it up in a dictionary.

      **Source synonyms:** consult

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To obtain, or seek to obtain, information about something.
   1. To enter a query into a database or search engine.

      **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

      **Example:** If you look up a bunch of random characters, there will probably be no results.

      **Source synonyms:** search

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To reconnect or meet with someone that one used to know.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** track down
