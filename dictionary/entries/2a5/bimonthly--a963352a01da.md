## Adjective · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Occurring once every two months.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The organization's bimonthly meetings allow for two discussions each quarter.

   **Example:** His bimonthly appointment was almost due.

   **Source synonyms:** bimensal; bimestrial

**Source sense R1.S2**
