### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any disease of the peripheral nervous system; peripheral neuropathy.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Long-standing diabetes often causes neuropathy in the retinas, fingertips, and toes.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any disease of the nerves and nervous system, usually and more specifically the peripheral nervous system, thus including both non-neuraxial and neuraxial instances of neural damage or dysfunction but excluding the neuraxis's psychiatric aspects (mental illnesses).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** the contribution of central neuropathy on postural impairment observed in diabetic patients with peripheral neuropathy

   **Example:** insights into the underlying molecular pathogenesis of diabetes-induced CNS neuropathy

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences
