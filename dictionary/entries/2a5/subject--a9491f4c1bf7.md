## Noun · Etymology 2 · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- enPR: sŭbʹjĕkt — variety unspecified in the source labels
- IPA: /ˈsʌb.d͡ʒɛkt/ — variety unspecified in the source labels
- IPA: /ˈsʌb.d͡ʒɪkt/ — source labels: Received-Pronunciation

### Forms

- subjects — plural

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. The noun, pronoun or noun phrase about whom the statement is made. In active clauses with verbs denoting an action, the subject is the actor. In clauses in the passive voice the subject is the target of the action.

   **Example:** In the active sentence ‘The cat ate the mouse’, ‘the cat’ is the subject and ‘the mouse’ is the object.

   **Example:** In the passive sentence ‘the mouse was eaten by the cat’, ‘the mouse’ is the subject even though it is the target (patient) of the action, and ‘the cat’ is the agent.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** grammar; human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

   **Source antonyms:** object; predicate

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. By extension&#58; an actor or agent; one who takes action.

   **Example:** the subjects and objects of power

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. The main topic of a paper, work of art, discussion, field of study, etc.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. A particular area of study.

   **Example:** Her favorite subject is physics.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. A citizen in a monarchy.

   **Example:** I am a British subject.

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. A person ruled over by another, especially a monarch or state authority.

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

**Source sense R2.S7**

7. The main theme or melody, especially in a fugue.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

<a id="r2-s8"></a>

**Source sense R2.S8**

8. A human, animal, or an inanimate object that is being examined, treated, analysed, etc; especially, one being studied in a scientific experiment, such as a clinical trial.

   **Example:** human subject research

   **Example:** animal subjects

<a id="r2-s9"></a>

**Source sense R2.S9**

9. A being that has subjective experiences, subjective consciousness, or a relationship with another entity.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; philosophy; sciences

<a id="r2-s10"></a>

**Source sense R2.S10**

10. That of which something is stated.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; logic; mathematics; philosophy; sciences

<a id="r2-s11"></a>

**Source sense R2.S11**

11. A variable isolated on one side of an equality, expressed in terms of other variables.
