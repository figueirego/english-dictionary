### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The power that enables something to move.

   **Usage:** figuratively; sometimes; uncountable; usually

   **Example:** Using electricity as motive power for railroads will do away with fuel trains, tenders, coal handling, water, and all that.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The locomotives or other powered units that operate on a railway.

   **Usage:** collective; uncountable; usually
