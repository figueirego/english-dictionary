### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A type of reflecting telescope having two concave mirrors&#58; the primary mirror (a paraboloid) collects the light and brings it to a focus before the secondary mirror (a ellipsoid), where it is reflected back through a hole in the centre of the primary, and thence out the bottom end of the instrument, where it can be viewed with the aid of the eyepiece.
