## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈkɔɹnˌflaʊ.ɚ/ — source labels: US

### Forms

- cornflowers — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A small annual plant in the family Asteraceae, Centaurea cyanus, usually with bushy blue flowers which grows natively in European cornfields (i.e. wheatfields).

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A plant of the species Cichorium intybus.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A color, cornflower blue.
