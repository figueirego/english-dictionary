<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Haughty, pompous, snobbish; inclined to turn up one's nose.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** Sir Sydney was an arrogant, snooty, bigoted prig, so it was with some satisfaction that I wrote out his parking ticket.

   **Source synonyms:** airy; arrogant; chesty (informal); authoritarian; biggity; big-headed (slang); blustery; turgescent (formal); boastful; lordly; bombastic; buggish (dialectal; obsolete); cocky; conceited; disdainful; domineering; egotistical; entitled; flatulent (obsolete); flaunting; full of oneself; glorious (obsolete); grandiose; hankty; haught (obsolete); haughty; haut (obsolete); high-handed; high-hat (colloquial); high and mighty; highfalutin (colloquial); hoity-toity (colloquial); holier-than-thou; hubristic; hubristical; imperious; inflated; insolent; la-di-da; orgulous (archaic; literary); ostentatious; overbearing; overweening; pompous; presumptuous; pretentious; priggish; puffed up; sanctimonious; self-important; snobbish (slang); snooty (slang); snotty (slang); stout; stuck up (slang); supercilious; thrasonic; thrasonical; toploftical; toplofty; tumid; turgid; uppish; uppity; uppy; upstage; vain

   **Source antonyms:** abased; demure; diffident; humble; humble-hearted; low; lowly; meek; mild; modest; poor; resigned; self-effacing; unpretentious; verecund (rare)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of an event, a thing, etc.&#58; elite, exclusive.

   **Usage:** broadly; informal

   **Example:** Her parties were snooty affairs; custom-designed cocktail dresses that cost more than a decent luxury car were de rigueur.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Easily angered or irritated; irritable, short-tempered.

   **Usage:** broadly; informal; uncommon
