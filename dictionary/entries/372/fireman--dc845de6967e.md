### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Someone (especially male) who is skilled in the work of fighting fire.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** firefighting; government

   **Source synonyms:** firefighter; smoke eater

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person (originally a man) who keeps the fire going underneath a steam boiler (originally, shoveling coal by hand), particularly on a railroad locomotive or steamship.

   **Example:** The fireman's job was about to get very interesting; they had just run out of coal!

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; rail-transport; railways; transport

   **Source synonyms:** stoker; bakehead

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An assistant on any locomotive, whether steam-powered or not.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** rail-transport; railways; transport

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A relief pitcher (reflecting the figurative analogy of rescuing the situation).

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; baseball; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A safety inspector in coal mines.

   **Usage:** historical

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; mining
