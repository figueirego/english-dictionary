### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of several species of trees of the genus Aesculus.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

   1. Aesculus hippocastanum (horse chestnut).

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of several species of the related Mexican buckeye (genus Ungnadia).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Seed or fruit of these plants.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A native or resident of the American state of Ohio.

   **Usage:** US; countable; slang; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** Ohioan

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Any member of the species Junonia coenia of butterflies; a common buckeye.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A confection made from peanut butter fudge partially dipped in chocolate to leave a circle of peanut butter visible, supposed to resemble the nut of the Ohio buckeye tree.

   **Usage:** US; countable; uncountable
