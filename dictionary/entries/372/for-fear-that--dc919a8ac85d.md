### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In order to attempt to prevent the risk that (a particular incident or event might occur).

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** He never comes for fear that it'll happen again, and I didn't tell you about it before for fear that he would find out.
