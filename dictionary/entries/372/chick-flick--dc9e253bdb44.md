### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A film, typically about romance, which is generally more popular with women than men.

   **Usage:** colloquial; derogatory; informal

   **Example:** She went with a couple friends to see a chick flick about some girl dating an astronaut.

   **Source synonyms:** chick film

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A sexually exploitative or pornographic film depicting women, intended for straight men.

   **Usage:** dated; slang

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any film depicting young women or teenage girls as the main characters.

   **Usage:** derogatory; slang
