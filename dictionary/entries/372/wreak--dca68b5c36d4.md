### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To cause harm; to afflict; to inflict; to harm or injure; to let out harm.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The earthquake wreaked havoc in the city.

   **Example:** She wreaked her anger on his car.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To chasten, or chastise/chastize, or castigate, or punish, or smite.

   **Usage:** archaic

   **Example:** The police abused their authority to wreak an innocent.

   **Example:** The criminal has been wreaked by the Judge to spend a year in prison.
