### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any form of guitar-driven alternative rock that is particularly or notably emotional; a broadly encompassing genre of rock music characterized by expressive, often confessional lyrics.
   1. A subgenre of late 1980s hardcore punk originating in Washington D.C., characterized by intense, highly emotional performances, dynamic shifts, and a departure from traditional punk song structures (also emocore).

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable; originally

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any form of guitar-driven alternative rock that is particularly or notably emotional; a broadly encompassing genre of rock music characterized by expressive, often confessional lyrics.
   1. A 1990s indie rock movement characterized by complex guitar work (often borrowing from math rock), dynamic shifts, arpeggiated melodies, and deeply introspective lyrics; commonly referred to as Midwest emo.

      **Usage:** broadly; countable; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

      **Source synonyms:** Midwest emo

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any form of guitar-driven alternative rock that is particularly or notably emotional; a broadly encompassing genre of rock music characterized by expressive, often confessional lyrics.
   1. A commercially successful 2000s alternative rock and pop-punk movement characterized by theatricality, melancholic, or romantically frustrated themes, and highly polished production.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

      **Source synonyms:** emo pop

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any form of guitar-driven alternative rock that is particularly or notably emotional; a broadly encompassing genre of rock music characterized by expressive, often confessional lyrics.
   1. Electronic-infused subgenres incorporating aggressive, satirical, or hedonistic themes layered over post-hardcore or pop-punk foundations (e.g., crunkcore, neon pop-punk).

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable; derivative

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. An individual presenting as such, intentionally or otherwise.
   1. A person associated with that subculture and musical style.
      1. A participant in the heavily aestheticized 2000s and 2010s subculture.

         **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   1. (by extension) A typically young individual whose outward persona is characterized by introspection, sensitivity, melancholy, or angst; considered to be overly emotional or who is associated with the emo subculture.
