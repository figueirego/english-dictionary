### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who enters into, or offers themselves for, any service of their own free will, especially when done without pay.

   **Example:** The volunteers at the nature reserve meet up ever other Sunday to help its upkeep.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One who enters into military service voluntarily (but who, when in service, is subject to discipline and regulations like other soldiers), as opposed to a conscript.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A voluntary member of the organized militia of a country, as distinguished from a regular or member of the standing army.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A person who acts out of their own will without a legal obligation, such as a donor.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A plant that grows spontaneously, without being cultivated on purpose; see volunteer plant in Wikipedia.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** agriculture; biology; botany; business; lifestyle; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To enlist oneself as a volunteer.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To do or offer to do something voluntarily.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** to volunteer for doing the dishes

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To offer, usually unprompted.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to volunteer an explanation

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To offer the services of (someone else) to do something.

   **Usage:** informal; transitive

   **Example:** My sister volunteered me to do the dishes.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To grow without human sowing or intentional cultivation.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences
