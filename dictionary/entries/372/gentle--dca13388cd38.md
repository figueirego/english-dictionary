### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Tender and amiable; of a considerate or kindly disposition.

   **Example:** Stuart is a gentle man; he would never hurt you.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Soft and mild rather than hard or severe.

   **Example:** I felt something touch my shoulder; it was gentle and a little slimy.

   **Source synonyms:** silken

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Docile and easily managed.

   **Example:** We had a gentle swim in the lake.

   **Example:** a gentle horse

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Gradual rather than steep or sudden.

   **Example:** The walks in this area have a gentle incline.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Polite and respectful rather than rude.

   **Example:** He gave me a gentle reminder that we had to hurry up.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Well-born; of a good family or respectable birth, though not noble.

   **Usage:** archaic
