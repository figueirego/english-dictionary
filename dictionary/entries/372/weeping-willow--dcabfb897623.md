### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A willow of species Salix babylonica, with pendulous branches, native to China.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of the numerous decorative cultivars and hybrids of Salix babylonica with white willow (Salix alba) or crack willow (Salix fragilis).
