## Prep\_phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Holding the last qualifying position with qualification still in progress, and thus liable to lose that position.

   **Example:** Smith was on the bubble but lost out when Jones was faster by a tenth of a second.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; motor-racing; racing; sports

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Having qualification for an event depend on the upcoming performances of other competitors; hence, at risk of being eliminated, and reversely, being close to qualification; on the verge.

   **Example:** The Knights had been on the bubble for the tournament, but played their way in by winning their last five games.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Uncertain of success.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** The new bond issue now appears to be on the bubble, with support eroding.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see on, bubble.
