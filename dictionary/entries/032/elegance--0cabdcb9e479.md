### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Grace, refinement, and beauty in movement, appearance, or manners.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** The bride was elegance personified.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Restraint and grace of style.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** The simple dress had a quiet elegance.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The beauty of an idea characterized by minimalism and intuitiveness while preserving exactness and precision.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** The proof of the theorem had a pleasing elegance.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A refinement or luxury.

   **Usage:** countable; dated; usually
