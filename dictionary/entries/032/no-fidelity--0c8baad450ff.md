## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Extremely low fidelity.

   **Usage:** excessive; humorous; not-comparable; sound

   **Example:** He busted out an ancient no-fidelity tabletop radio and proceeded to play some tinny music from the AM dial.

   **Source synonyms:** low fidelity; lo-fi; LoFi; lofi

   **Source antonyms:** high fidelity; hi-fi; HiFi; hifi

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Involving no electronics at all, being an original (unreproduced) noise.

   **Usage:** humorous; not-comparable; sound

   **Example:** Get a load of my no-fidelity radio&#58; my neighbor is singing off-tune while he weeds his garden.

   **Source antonyms:** high fidelity; hi-fi; HiFi; hifi
