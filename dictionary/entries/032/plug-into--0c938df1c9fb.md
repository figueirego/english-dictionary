### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To connect an electric device into something using a plug.

   **Usage:** ditransitive

   **Example:** I spent ages trying to fix my computer, before realizing that it wasn't plugged into the wall.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To join something in the manner of a plug.

   **Usage:** ditransitive; figuratively

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To become aware of or knowledgeable about.

   **Usage:** ditransitive; idiomatic

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To replace a variable with a number in order to solve an equation.

   **Usage:** ditransitive; idiomatic

   **Example:** If you know two sides of a right triangle, you can find the third by plugging them into the Pythagorean Theorem.
