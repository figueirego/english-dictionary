### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To review materials already learned in order to make sure one does not forget them, usually in preparation for an examination.

   **Usage:** intransitive; literary; transitive; usually

   **Example:** Students are expected to start studying for final exams in March.

   **Example:** I need to study my biology notes.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To take a course or courses on a subject.

   **Usage:** literary; transitive

   **Example:** I study medicine at the university.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To acquire knowledge on a subject with the intention of applying it in practice.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Biologists study living things.

   **Example:** In 2015, scientists found that 82 percent of glaciers studied in China had decreased in size.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To look at carefully and minutely.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He studied the map in preparation for the hike.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To fix the mind closely upon a subject; to dwell upon anything in thought; to muse; to ponder.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To endeavor diligently; to be zealous.

   **Usage:** archaic; intransitive
