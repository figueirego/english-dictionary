## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of various high-ranking officials, often diplomats.

   **Example:** The United Nations has a number of high commissioners.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An ambassador of one Commonwealth country to another.

   **Usage:** Commonwealth

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An indirect ruler of a British protectorate or territory, equivalent to a governor in a crown colony.

   **Usage:** historical
