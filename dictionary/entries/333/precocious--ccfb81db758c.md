### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Characterized by exceptionally early development or maturity.

   **Example:** The precocious plant was already blooming flowers by day 4.

   **Source antonyms:** serotinous

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Exhibiting advanced skills and aptitudes at an abnormally early age.

   **Example:** The precocious child began reading the newspaper at age four.

   **Source synonyms:** prodigious; tranty

   **Source antonyms:** altricial
