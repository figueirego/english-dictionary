### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Impossible to calculate.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** incomputable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Too much, too vast, or too numerous to enable computation.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The loss the National Library suffered from the fire is incalculable.

   **Source synonyms:** innumerable; unimaginable; huge

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of a person's mood or character, etc.&#58; impossible to predict.

   **Usage:** comparable

   **Example:** He is incalculable when he's drunk. He can turn from an amiable person into a violent horror in a moment.

   **Source synonyms:** capricious; fickle
