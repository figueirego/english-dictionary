## Noun · Record 2

### Forms

- centigrades — plural

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A centigrade scale for temperature having the freezing point of water defined as 0° and its boiling point defined as 100° at standard atmospheric pressure.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** All temperatures are expressed in centigrade unless otherwise noted.

   **Example:** a temperature of 34 centigrade

   **Source synonyms:** Celsius scale

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. A single degree on this scale.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; usually

   **Example:** a temperature of 34 centigrades

   **Source synonyms:** degree Celsius; degree centigrade; mul&#58;°C; mul&#58;℃

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. The scale for angle measurement on which a gradian (a grad or gon) is the hundredth part of a quadrant.

   **Usage:** countable; usually
