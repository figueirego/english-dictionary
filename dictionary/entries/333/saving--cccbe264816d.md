## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈseɪ.vɪŋ/ — source labels: Canada; UK; US
- IPA: /ˈsæɪ.vɪŋ/ — source labels: Australia

### Forms

- savings — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A reduction in cost or expenditure.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The shift of the supplier gave us a saving of 10 percent.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Something (usually money) that is saved, particularly money that has been set aside for the future.

   **Usage:** countable; plural-normally

   **Example:** I invested all my savings in gold.

   **Example:** The collapse of Enron wiped out the life savings of many people, leaving them poor in their retirement.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The action of the verb to save.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Exception; reservation.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

   **Example:** saving and transitional provisions

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law
