### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A part, usually performed by instruments, that gives support or adds to the background in music, or adds for ornamentation; also, the harmony of a figured bass.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Brooks performed a saxophone solo on stage, with Robert as accompaniment on the bass.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

   **Source synonyms:** comping

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. That which accompanies; something that attends as a circumstance, or which is added to give greater completeness to the principal thing, or by way of ornament, or for the sake of symmetry.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** A side salad is a common accompaniment to a main dish.

   **Example:** forecasting torrential rain, with an accompaniment of crashing thunder.

   **Example:** Green sauce is the perfect accompaniment to baked fish.

   **Source synonyms:** attachment; supplement; accessory; accompaniment; addition; add-on; adjunct; affix; annex; infusion; apanage; appendage; appendix (obsolete); extra; increase; increment; supplement
