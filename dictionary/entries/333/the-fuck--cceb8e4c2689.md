### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used with an interrogative pronoun as an intensifier to express anger, frustration, incredulity, or other strong emotion.

   **Usage:** slang; vulgar

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used after verbs forming a part of a phrasal verb as an intensifier to imply aggressive emphasis.

   **Usage:** slang; vulgar

   **Example:** Shut the fuck up!

   **Example:** Get the fuck out!

   **Example:** Calm the fuck down!

   **Example:** Back the fuck off!

   **Example:** Stay the fuck away from me!

   **Example:** Hurry the fuck up.

   **Example:** Let's get the fuck to work.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used as a shortened form of the common interrogative phrases.

   **Usage:** slang; vulgar; by ellipsis

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Used to emphatically express that something isn't true.

   **Usage:** slang; vulgar

   **Example:** —Mom, I'm going out with my friends tonight. —The fuck you are!

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Used to emphasize an adjective; fucking.

   **Usage:** slang; vulgar; African-American Vernacular
