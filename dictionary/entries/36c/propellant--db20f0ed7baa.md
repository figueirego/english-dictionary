## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. anything that propels
   1. fuel, oxidizer, reaction mass or mixture for one or more engines (especially internal combustion engines or jet engines) that is carried within a vehicle prior to use

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. anything that propels
   1. the compressed gas in a pressurised container (especially an aerosol can) that is used to expel its content

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. anything that propels
   1. the explosive (cordite, gunpowder, etc) found in ammunition cartridges

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable
