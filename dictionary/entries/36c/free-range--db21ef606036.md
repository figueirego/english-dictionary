## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of, pertaining to, or produced by animals that are allowed to roam freely, rather than being confined indoors.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Allowed to move about outdoors without supervision.

   **Usage:** broadly; not-comparable

   **Example:** free-range children

   **Source context (verbatim):** of children

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Relating to a hands-off parenting style in which children are allowed to go out and explore alone.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** free-range parents
