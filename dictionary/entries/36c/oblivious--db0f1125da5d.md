### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Lacking awareness; unmindful; unaware, unconscious of.

   **Usage:** usually

   **Example:** Your grandmother is completely oblivious to her surroundings.

   **Source context (verbatim):** with to or of

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** dated; usually; with-of; never with to
