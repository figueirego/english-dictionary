## Noun

### Forms

- putty knives — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A hand tool with a flexible blade used by glaziers to apply putty to seal window panes to the frame and mullions.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A similar tool with a wide, flat blade used by painters, plasterers, and carpenters to spread putty to fill holes and cracks.
