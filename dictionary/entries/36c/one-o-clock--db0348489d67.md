## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The start of the second hour of the day; 1&#58;00 a.m. (01&#58;00).

   **Usage:** In both the 12-hour clock and the 24-hour clock

   **Example:** The bar closes at one o'clock (= "The bar closes at 1&#58;00 a.m.").

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The start of the fourteenth hour of the day; 1&#58;00 p.m. (13&#58;00).

   **Usage:** Only in the 12-hour clock

   **Example:** The next train comes at one o'clock (= "The next train comes at 13&#58;00").

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A position ahead and slightly rightward (horizontal clock orientation) or almost directly above (vertical clock orientation) (from the location of the 1 mark on a clock face)

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** We've got a bogey at one o'clock.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A dandelion.

   **Usage:** informal
