## Noun

### Forms

- custard pies — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A pie made from an uncooked custard mixture added to an uncooked or partially cooked crust and then baked as a whole.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A prop used in slapstick, consisting of an open pie filled with custard or cream (or, more often nowadays, simply a paper plate covered with shaving foam or a similar substance) that is pushed into another person's face.
