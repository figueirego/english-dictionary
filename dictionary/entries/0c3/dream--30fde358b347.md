### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Imaginary events seen in the mind while sleeping.

   **Example:** Last night I had a vividly scary dream about an immortal snail that chased me around and could kill me if it touched me.

   **Example:** Crazy things like that only happen in dreams.

   **Source synonyms:** sweven (archaic)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A hope or wish.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** This year I fulfilled a lifelong dream of visiting the South Pacific.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A visionary scheme; a wild conceit; an idle fancy.

   **Example:** live in a dream

   **Example:** wake up from a dream

   **Example:** impossible dream

   **Example:** a dream of bliss

   **Example:** the dream of his youth

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To see imaginary events in one's mind while sleeping.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Although people primarily dream during the REM phase of sleeping, they can dream during non-REM sleep as well.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To hope, to wish.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Lucy dreams of becoming a scientist when she grows up.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To daydream.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Stop dreaming and get back to work.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To envision as an imaginary experience (usually when asleep).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I dreamed a vivid dream last night.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To consider the possibility (of).

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** I wouldn't dream of snubbing you in public.
