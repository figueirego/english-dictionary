**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Oriented backwards, particularly after a mishap.

   **Usage:** colloquial

   **Example:** The car spun out of control and I ended up ass-backwards in a ditch.

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Hopelessly misguided; contrary to what is normal, expected, or logical; having taken the utterly wrong choice.

   **Usage:** colloquial; idiomatic
