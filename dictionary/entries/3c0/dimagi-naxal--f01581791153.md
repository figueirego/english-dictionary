### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A self-applied, often defiant or ironic label adopted by a person (typically a government critic, activist, or intellectual) who embraces the accusation as a badge of pride rather than an insult.

   **Usage:** informal; Indian politics; often satirical; Indian politics; often satirical

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person accused of intellectually supporting or sympathizing with Naxalism or Maoism, without necessarily taking part in armed activity; used to characterize government critics, activists, or intellectuals as ideologically aligned with Naxalites.

   **Usage:** derogatory; Indian politics
