### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An expression used to concede a point, but suggesting that the broader argument is incorrect.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** You can play the exercises pretty well on piano, fair enough, but can you play the movement expressively?

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used to acknowledge a person's point or argument; denotes that, upon consideration, something is correct or reasonable; an expression of acknowledgment or understanding.

   **Source synonyms:** very well; I see
