### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A rapid illegal U-turn performed by putting the car in neutral and applying the hand brake, thereby producing a controlled skid, with the car still moving backwards when forward power is again applied.

   **Usage:** US; colloquial

   **Example:** Did you see the moonshiner’s turn he pulled in that chase scene?
