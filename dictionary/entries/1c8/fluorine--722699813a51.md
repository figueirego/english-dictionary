**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The chemical element (symbol F) with an atomic number of 9. It is the lightest of the halogens, a pale yellow-green, highly reactive gas that attacks all metals.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** an octahedron of fluorines
