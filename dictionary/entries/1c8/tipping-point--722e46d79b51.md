### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The point at which a slow, reversible change becomes irreversible, often with dramatic consequences.

   **Usage:** broadly; figuratively; catastrophe theory

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences
