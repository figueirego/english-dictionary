### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A crate for packing soap.

   **Usage:** literally

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A small platform, being or resembling a literal soapbox, on which a person stands in order to give an impromptu speech or espouse their views.

   **Example:** The madman obtained a soapbox which he stood on at the corner of Broadway and Wall street, to shout out his prophecy of the end of the world.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A platform or outlet, such as a media platform, that a person uses to express their opinions.

   **Usage:** figuratively; often; pejorative

   **Example:** The politician used Twitter as his soapbox.

   **Example:** Please stop using council meetings as your own personal soapbox.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A figurative means of talking repeatedly or at length about one's opinions on one's pet topic.

   **Usage:** derogatory; especially

   **Example:** He's been on his soapbox all day about the new football coach.

   **Source context (verbatim):** in phrases such as 'on/off one's soapbox'

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A figurative means of talking repeatedly or at length about one's opinions on one's pet topic.
   1. The topic itself.

      **Usage:** broadly; derogatory; especially

      **Example:** His soapbox is the lack of public toilets in the city.

      **Source context (verbatim):** in phrases such as 'on/off one's soapbox'

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A soapbox car.
