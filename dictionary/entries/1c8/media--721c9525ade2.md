## Noun · Etymology 2 · Record 3

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈmiːdiə/ — source labels: Received-Pronunciation
- IPA: /ˈmidi.ə/ — source labels: General-American

### Forms

- media — plural
- medias — plural

### Senses

<a id="r3-s1"></a>

**Source sense R3.S1**

1. The means and institutions for publishing and broadcasting information.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; often treated as uncountable

   **Example:** As a result of the rise of, first, television news and entertainment media and, second, web-based media, traditional print-based media has declined in popularity.

<a id="r3-s2"></a>

**Source sense R3.S2**

2. The totality of content items (television shows, films, books, photographs, etc.) which are broadcast or published.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; often treated as uncountable

   **Example:** Fighter pilots are depicted as cool in popular media like Top Gun.

<a id="r3-s3"></a>

**Source sense R3.S3**

3. The journalists and other professionals who comprise the mass communication industry.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; usually; often treated as uncountable

   **Example:** Some celebrities dislike press conferences, where the media bombards them with questions.

   **Source context (verbatim):** with a definite article

<a id="r3-s4"></a>

**Source sense R3.S4**

4. Files and data comprising material viewable by humans, but usually not plain text; audiovisual material.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences
