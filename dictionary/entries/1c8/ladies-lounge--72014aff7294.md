**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see lady, lounge.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A room in a pub or hotel, separate from the main drinking area, in which drinks are served; originally a place for women to drink in when not welcome or not comfortable in the traditionally male-oriented public bar, and latterly a more genteel area than the public bar.
