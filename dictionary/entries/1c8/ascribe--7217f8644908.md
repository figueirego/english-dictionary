### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To regard as arising from a specified cause or source.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** One may ascribe these problems to the federal government; however, at this stage it is unclear what caused them.

   **Source synonyms:** accredit; attribute; impute

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To attribute (a book, painting, or any other work of art or literature) to a writer or creator.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** It is arguable as to whether we can truly ascribe this play to Shakespeare.

   **Source synonyms:** attribute

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To believe in or agree with; to subscribe.

   **Usage:** nonstandard

   **Source context (verbatim):** with to
