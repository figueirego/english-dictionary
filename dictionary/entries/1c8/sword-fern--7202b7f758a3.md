### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any fern in the genus Nephrolepis, typically with long, straight-sided fronds shaped like a two-edged sword, and widely grown as houseplants.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Some ferns in the genus Polystichum which look like the typical Nephrolepis sword ferns.
