### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To be very noticeably (and undesirably or humorously) different; to be prominently out of place; to be particularly obtrusive or conspicuous (usually unintentionally); to attract unwanted attention or notice.

   **Usage:** simile

   **Example:** Don't wear a business suit to that party; you'll stick out like a sore thumb.

   **Source synonyms:** stick out a mile

   **Source antonyms:** belong; blend in; fit; fit in
