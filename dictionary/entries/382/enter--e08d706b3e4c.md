**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To go or come into an enclosed or partially enclosed space.

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** transitive

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To go or come into (a state or profession).

   **Usage:** figuratively

**Source sense R1.S4**

   **Usage:** transitive
