### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A traditional walking stick used by travelers on religious journeys, especially the Camino de Santiago, symbolizing faith, support, and defense; often featuring a hook for carrying items and a cross-piece for resting.

   **Source synonyms:** palmer's staff; bourdon
