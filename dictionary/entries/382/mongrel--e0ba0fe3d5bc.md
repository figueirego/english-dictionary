### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Anything of mixed kind.
   1. A dog with a lineage of two or more breeds.

      **Usage:** derogatory; often

      **Example:** That dog is a mongrel; who knows what breed it could be!

      **Source synonyms:** bitsa; bitser; bitzer (Australia; Ireland; New-Zealand; UK); cur; mutt; tyke (dialectal); Heinz 57

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Anything of mixed kind.
   1. A person of mixed race.

      **Usage:** derogatory; ethnic; offensive; often; slur

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A thuggish, obnoxious, or contemptible person; (often preceded by "poor") a pitiable person.

   **Usage:** Commonwealth; Ireland; UK; slang

   **Source synonyms:** bastard

**Source sense R1.S4**

   **Usage:** Australia; slang
