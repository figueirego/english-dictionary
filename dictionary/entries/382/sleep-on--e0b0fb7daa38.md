### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To postpone (a decision) at least overnight, to avoid making a hasty choice.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I like the idea, but let me sleep on it before we go ahead.

   **Example:** There's no chance to sleep on the matter. You have to decide now.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To miss or ignore; to underappreciate; to underestimate the quality of something.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; slang

   **Example:** People are really sleeping on that restaurant down the street.

   **Example:** I can't believe I slept on that movie for so long.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see sleep, on.
