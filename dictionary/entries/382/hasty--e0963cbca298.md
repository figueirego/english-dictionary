### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Acting or done in haste; hurried or too quick; speedy due to having little time.

   **Example:** Without much thinking about it they made a hasty decision to buy it.

   **Source synonyms:** brash

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Acting or done in haste; hurried or too quick; speedy due to having little time.
   1. Made in haste.

      **Source synonyms:** brash

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Acting or done in haste; hurried or too quick; speedy due to having little time.
   1. Ripening or coming to maturity early.

      **Source synonyms:** brash

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Acting or done in haste; hurried or too quick; speedy due to having little time.
   1. Eager or impatient to act or get something done.

      **Source synonyms:** brash

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Acting or done in haste; hurried or too quick; speedy due to having little time.
   1. Characterized by undue quickness of action, and thus lacking careful thought or consideration; rash, precipitate.

      **Source synonyms:** brash

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

   **Usage:** archaic
