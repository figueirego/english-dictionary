### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A tapering, rapidly rotating cloud descending from a larger storm cloud, indicating the presence of an intense vortex, which potentially becomes a tornado if it reaches the ground.

   **Example:** When we saw the funnel cloud, we headed for the basement.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** climatology; meteorology; natural-sciences
