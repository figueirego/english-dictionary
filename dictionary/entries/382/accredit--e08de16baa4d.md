### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To ascribe; attribute; credit with.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Nationalisms often accredit all the unemployment issues to immigration.

   **Source synonyms:** chalk up to; impute; put down to; accredit; ascribe; attach (figuratively); attribute; chalk up to (idiomatic); charge; draw (figuratively; obsolete); impute; lay; put down to (idiomatic); repose (obsolete)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To put or bring into credit; to invest with credit or authority; to sanction.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To send with letters credential, as an ambassador, envoy, or diplomatic agent; to authorize, as a messenger or delegate.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To believe; to put trust in.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To enter on the credit side of an account book.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To certify as meeting a predetermined standard; to certify an educational institution as upholding the specified standards necessary for the students to advance.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The school was an accredited college.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To recognize as outstanding.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To credit.

   **Usage:** literally; transitive
