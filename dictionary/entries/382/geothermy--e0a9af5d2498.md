### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The heat produced by the natural processes of the Earth, including heat from the undercrust, plate tectonics, volcanism, and other sources.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; geology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The process and practice of harvesting heat produced by the Earth to be used as an energy source.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; electrical; electrical-engineering; electricity; electromagnetism; energy; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The study of heat produced naturally by the Earth, usually considered a subdiscipline of geophysics.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; geology; natural-sciences

   **Source synonyms:** geothermics
