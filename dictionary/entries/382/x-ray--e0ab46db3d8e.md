### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A ray of short-wavelength electromagnetic radiation usually produced by bombarding a metal target in a vacuum. Used to create images of the internal structure of objects; this is possible because X-rays pass through most objects and can expose photographic film.

   **Example:** X-rays are light with a wavelength between 0.1 and 10 nm.

   **Source synonyms:** Röntgen radiation; Roentgen-ray

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A radiograph&#58; an image made with X-rays.

   **Example:** The doctor ordered some X-rays of my injured wrist.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An X-ray machine, a machine for performing radiography.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A tactical motif where a piece exerts influence or control over a square, piece, or line indirectly through another intervening piece.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** board-games; chess; games

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A cheat that allows a user to see other players, enemies and important objects through walls and other barriers.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** video-games

   **Source synonyms:** wallhack; ESP

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To take a radiograph of; to obtain an image of using X-ray radiation, especially for the purpose of medical diagnostic evaluation.

   **Example:** Of course there was nothing wrong with my left wrist. They X-rayed the wrong arm!
