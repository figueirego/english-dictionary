## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An act of stamping the foot, paw or hoof.

   **Example:** The horse gave two quick stamps and rose up on its hind legs.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An indentation, imprint, or mark made by stamping.

   **Example:** My passport has quite a collection of stamps.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A device for imprinting designs.

   **Example:** She loved to make designs with her collection of stamps.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A small piece of paper, with a design and a face value, used to prepay postage or other dues such as tax or licence fees.

   **Example:** I need one first-class stamp to send this letter.

   **Example:** Now that commerce is done electronically, tax stamps are no longer issued here.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A small piece of paper bearing a design on one side and adhesive on the other, used to decorate letters or craft work.

   **Example:** These stamps have a Christmas theme.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A tattoo.

   **Usage:** figuratively; slang

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A single dose of lysergic acid diethylamide.

   **Usage:** slang

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A kind of heavy pestle, raised by water or steam power, for crushing ores.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. Cast; form; character; distinguishing mark or sign; evidence.

   **Example:** the stamp of criminality

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To step quickly and heavily, once or repeatedly.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The toddler screamed and stamped, but still got no candy.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To move (the foot or feet) quickly and heavily, once or repeatedly.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The crowd cheered and stamped their feet in appreciation.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To strike, beat, or press forcibly with the bottom of the foot, or by thrusting the foot downward.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To mark by pressing quickly and heavily.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** This machine stamps the metal cover with a design.

   **Example:** This machine stamps the design into the metal cover.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To give an official marking to, generally by impressing or imprinting a design or symbol.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The immigration officer stamped my passport.

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To apply postage stamps to.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I forgot to stamp this letter.

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

**Source sense R2.S7**

7. To mark; to impress.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive
