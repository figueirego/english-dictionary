## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

- matters of fact — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An issue concerning the factual circumstances of a cause of action that is to be tried or proved; an allegation forming the basis of a claim or defense, as opposed to a matter of law.

   **Example:** Whether he left his house on the night of the murder is a matter of fact.

   **Example:** Matters of fact are issues for a jury, while matters of law are for the court.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A point of fact; a claim or statement about (empirical) facts, as opposed to conjecture or opinion.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** It’s a matter of fact that I came into work yesterday.
