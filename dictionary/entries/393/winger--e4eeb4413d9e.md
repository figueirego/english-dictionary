### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One of the casks stowed in the wings of a vessel's hold, being smaller than such as are stowed more amidships.

   **Example:** We hauled out a winger of grog.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An offensive player who plays on either side of the center.

   **Example:** The center passed to the left winger, who shot and scored.
