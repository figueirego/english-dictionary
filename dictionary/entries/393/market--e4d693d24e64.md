## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈmɑːkɪt/ — source labels: UK
- IPA: /ˈmɑɹkɪt/ — source labels: US
- IPA: /ˈmɑɹkət/ — source labels: US

### Forms

- markets — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A gathering of people for the purchase and sale of merchandise, often periodic at a set time.

   **Example:** The right to hold a weekly market was an invaluable privilege not given to all towns in the Middle Ages.

   **Example:** There's a market every Thursday and Saturday in the town square.

   **Source synonyms:** mart; fair

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A relatively spacious outdoor or covered site where traders set up stalls, either temporarily or permanently or semi-permanently, and buyers browse the merchandise.

   **Example:** This site on North Street used to be the market, until it was redeveloped.

   **Source synonyms:** marketplace; bazaar; arcade

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any physical store selling groceries, such as a grocery store or convenience store.

   **Example:** Stop by the market on your way home and pick up some milk.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A group of potential or current customers for one's product.

   **Example:** We believe that the market for the new widget will be the older homeowner.

   **Example:** Senior citizens are our core market at present, and we can't afford to alienate them as we explore other markets.

   **Source synonyms:** sale

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A geographical area or region where a certain commercial demand exists.

   **Example:** Foreign markets were lost as our currency rose versus their valuta.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A formally organized, sometimes monopolistic, system of trading in specified goods or effects.

   **Example:** The stock market ceased to be monopolized by the paper-shuffling national stock exchanges with the advent of Internet markets.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The sum total traded in a process of individuals trading for certain commodities.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. The price for which a thing is sold in a market; hence, value or worth; market value.

   **Example:** Q&#58; What's the market on such a thing, nowadays? A&#58; Oh, no less than forty, I should reckon.

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To make (products or services) available for sale and promote them.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** We plan to market an ecology model by next quarter.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To promote for or as if for sale.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To sell.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** We marketed more this quarter already than all last year!

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To deal in a market; to buy or sell; to make bargains for provisions or goods.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To shop in a market; to attend a market.

   **Usage:** intransitive
