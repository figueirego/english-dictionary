## Adjective · Record 1

### Forms

- more performative — comparative
- most performative — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Being enacted as it is said.

   **Example:** Saying "I do" as part of a wedding ceremony is performative, enacting a marriage.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; philosophy; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Insincere, inauthentic or disingenuous; done solely or largely as a performance, to produce an impression or enhance one's reputation.

   **Example:** We were beginning to suspect that her ethical qualms were sometimes performative, as the urgent need for fairness and justice seemed to apply to some people but not to others.

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** performatory; workly; illocutionary
