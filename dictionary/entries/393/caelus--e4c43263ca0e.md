## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The personification of the sky and heavens. Either the brother-husband or son-and-husband of Terra, with whom he fathered the Titans and the Cyclops. He is the Roman counterpart of Uranus.

   **Usage:** Roman

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; mysticism; mythology; philosophy; sciences
