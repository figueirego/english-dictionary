## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

- slipforms — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A type of process for setting concrete which uses moveable forms that are moved and reused once the concrete is stiff enough to retain its shape under its own weight.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A moveable form used when setting concrete using the slipform technique.

   **Usage:** countable
