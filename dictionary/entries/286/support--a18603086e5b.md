   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Don’t move that beam! It supports the whole platform.

   **Example:** He staggered away from the accident site, supported by one friend on each side.

   **Source synonyms:** bear; bestand; hold up; stut; undergird; underprop; upbear; upgird; uphold; take; bolster

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I support France in the World Cup.

   **Source synonyms:** adhere

   **Source antonyms:** oppose

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To provide sustenance or maintenance for; to sustain in integrity or livelihood.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Mars does not currently support human habitation.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To help, particularly financially; to subsidize.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The government supports the arts in several ways.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To provide evidence for; to lend credibility to.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The testimony is not sufficient to support the charges.

   **Source synonyms:** attest; corroborate; establish; substantiate; sustain; verify; undergird

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To serve, as in a customer-oriented position; to give support to.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The IT Department supports the research organization, but not the sales force.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

   **Usage:** transitive
