<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A garden tool with a row of pointed teeth fixed to a long handle, used for collecting debris, grass, etc., for flattening the ground, or for loosening soil; also, a similar wheel-mounted tool drawn by a horse or a tractor.

   **Source synonyms:** horserake

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   1. A tool with a straight edge at the end used by a croupier to move chips or money across a gaming table.

      **Usage:** broadly

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** gambling; games

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A similarly shaped tool used for other purposes.
   1. A type of lockpick that has a ridged or notched blade that moves across the pins in a pin tumbler lock, causing them to settle into a shear line.

      **Usage:** broadly

**Source sense R1.S4**

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** cellular-automata; computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences
