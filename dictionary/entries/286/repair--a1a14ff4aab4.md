### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To restore to good working order, fix, or improve damaged condition; to mend; to remedy.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** to repair a house, a road, a shoe, a ship

   **Example:** to repair a shattered fortune

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To make amends for, as for an injury, by an equivalent; to indemnify for.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** to repair a loss or damage

   **Example:** How can I repair this wrong?
