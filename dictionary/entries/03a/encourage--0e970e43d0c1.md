### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To mentally support; to motivate, give courage, hope or spirit.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I encouraged him during his race.

   **Example:** Delia's coach encouraged her to focus on the positives.

   **Source synonyms:** bield; embolden; hearten; abet (obsolete); actuate; goose; fuel; light a fire under; send; sting; bring about; cheer (obsolete); draw; drive; encourage; egg on (idiomatic); engender; evoke; excite; foment; goad; grill (Scotland; US; obsolete); ignite (figuratively); impel; incite; induce; inflame (figuratively); instigate; invoke; lash; motive; motivate; move (obsolete); obligate; oblige; push; promote; prompt; provoke; rouse; set off; spark off; spur; spur on; stimulate; stir; stir up; stoke (figuratively); tempt; urge; whet (obsolete); whip up

   **Source antonyms:** discourage; dishearten

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To spur on, strongly recommend.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** We encourage the use of bicycles in the town centre.

   **Example:** We encourage you to cycle instead of taking the car.

   **Source synonyms:** exhort; admonish; adjure; advise; advocate; counsel; encourage; urge; exhort; prompt; recommend; suggest; tip; warn

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To foster, give help or patronage.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The royal family has always encouraged the arts in word and deed.

   **Source synonyms:** promote; encourage; foster; feed; grow; culture; cultivate; promote; raise
