### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A non-Unicode pictogram, similar to an emoji, automatically appended to a trending and often branded hashtag in a tweet.

   **Usage:** Internet

   **Example:** After posting a tweet about Wombat Strike Six, an upcoming videogame she was excited about, Monique noticed that the hashtag \#wombatrevenge had an associated hashflag depicting a small, furious wombat.
