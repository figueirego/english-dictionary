## Noun

### Forms

- cutters and pasters — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An editor who cuts and pastes or copies and pastes text, stories, pictures, etc. to be published.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** She's a great cutter and paster; she can get half the magazine done in a week.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** journalism; media

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Anyone who copies text from one source and pastes it to another, using a computer.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** Zack is a keen cutter and paster; he likes grabbing headlines from political and entertainment sites and posting them on his page.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences
