### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A district of a city or town (in a country other than China) in which there is a large concentration of Chinese residents and businesses.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A similar district filled with East Asians and Southeast Asians.

   **Usage:** broadly; proscribed
