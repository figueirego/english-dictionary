**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Great Britain, a large island (sometimes also including some of the surrounding smaller islands) off the north-west coast of Western Europe, made up of England, Scotland, and Wales; especially (but not exclusively) during antiquity.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Usage:** countable; in-plural; uncountable

   **Usage:** countable; historical; uncountable
