### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Nimble with hands or body; dexterous; skillful; adept.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Quick to understand, learn, and devise or apply ideas; intelligent.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Mentally quick and resourceful; skilled at achieving what one wants in a mentally agile and inventive way.

   **Example:** clever like a fox

   **Example:** With a clever lawyer, she could easily be acquitted.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Smart, intelligent, or witty; mentally quick or sharp.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Sane; in one's right mind.

   **Usage:** archaic

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Showing mental quickness and resourcefulness.

   **Example:** This is a simple but clever trick to solve the problem.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of objects or actions

   **Source context (verbatim):** of objects or actions
