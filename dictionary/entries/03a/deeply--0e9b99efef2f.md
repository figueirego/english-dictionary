### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To a deep extent or degree; very greatly.

   **Example:** I am deeply concerned about this matter.

   **Example:** I deeply regret my behaviour.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. So as to extend far down or far into something.

   **Example:** The surface of the planet was deeply cratered.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. At depth.

   **Example:** In this part of the river salmon swim deeply.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. In a profound, not superficial, manner.

   **Example:** I thought deeply about the problem.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. In large volume.

   **Example:** breathe deeply, drink deeply
