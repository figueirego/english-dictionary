## Verb

### Forms

- comes across — present; singular; third-person
- coming across — participle; present
- came across — past
- come across — participle; past

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see come, across.

   **Example:** He came across the street towards me.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To change sides; to cross over to work for the opposition.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** You argued well in court but your firm doesn't pay its lawyers well, so why don't you come across to ours?

   **Source synonyms:** come over

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To give an appearance or impression; to project a certain image; to seem or appear (to be some way).

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** How did she come across when you met with her?

   **Example:** How did she come across to you?

   **Example:** She came across as sharp and well-grounded.

   **Example:** A business suit and adequate elocution help her to come across as the competent professional she is.

   **Source synonyms:** come off; come over

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To find, usually by accident.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** In the dark he came across an old box by accident.

   **Source synonyms:** come upon; run across; encounter; happen across

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To produce what was desired; to come up with the goods.

   **Example:** He came across with the rest of the money when we mentioned our lawyer.

   **Source synonyms:** come through

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To give in and do what is wanted or expected; to acquiesce to something.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Source synonyms:** come around

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To give in and do what is wanted or expected; to acquiesce to something.
   1. To have sex; to give in to seduction.

      **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

      **Source synonyms:** come around

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To give in and do what is wanted or expected; to acquiesce to something.
   1. To confess to something.

      **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

      **Source synonyms:** come around
