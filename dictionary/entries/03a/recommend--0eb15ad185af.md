### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To bestow commendation on; to represent favorably; to suggest, endorse or encourage as an appropriate choice.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The board recommends Philips, given his ample experience in similar positions.

   **Source synonyms:** commend

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To make acceptable; to attract favor to.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** A city that has much to recommend it.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To advise, propose, counsel favorably.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The therapist recommends resting the mind and exercising the body.

   **Example:** My therapist recommended that I rest the mind and exercise the body.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To commit, to confide to another's care, confidence or acceptance, with favoring representations.

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive

   **Example:** A medieval oblate's parents recommended the boy for life to God and the monastery.

### Related words

**Source antonyms:** deprecate; disapprove; discourage; disrecommend; oppose
