**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The loading of hazardous cargoes is not permitted.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A load, especially in the engineering and electrical engineering senses of force exerted, or electrical current or power supplied.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** I need to recheck the loadings on the external walls.

   **Example:** The loading on the generators peaks during the early evening.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An increased rate of pay given to an employee in order to compensate for specified non-standard working conditions, or a lack of specific other benefits. For example, an employee paid a casual loading is paid more than an equivalent full-time or part-time employee's rate, to compensate for a lack of benefits such as sick leave or annual leave.

   **Usage:** Australia; countable; industrial relations

   **Example:** The position advertised a $40 per hour rate, inclusive of the casual loading.

   **Example:** The salary package also included 30% shift loading to compensate for the long hours.
