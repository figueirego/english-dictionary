### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The elongated curved tropical fruit of a banana plant, which grows in bunches and has a creamy flesh and a smooth skin.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Jimmy had a banana for breakfast.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The elongated curved tropical fruit of a banana plant, which grows in bunches and has a creamy flesh and a smooth skin.
   1. In particular, the sweet, yellow fruit of the Cavendish banana cultivar, which may be eaten raw, as distinct from e.g. a plantain for cooking.

      **Usage:** Canada; Ireland; UK; US; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The tropical tree-like plant which bears clusters of bananas, a plant of the genus Musa (but sometimes also including plants from Ensete), which has large, elongated leaves.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A yellow color, like that of a banana's skin.

   **Usage:** uncountable
