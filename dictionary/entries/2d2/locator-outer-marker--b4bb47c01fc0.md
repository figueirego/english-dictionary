**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A ground-based radio navigation aid consisting of a pair of colocated radio beacons at the final approach fix of an ILS or IGS approach course&#58; an NDB to guide aircraft to the approach fix, and an outer marker to alert aircraft as they pass over the station.
