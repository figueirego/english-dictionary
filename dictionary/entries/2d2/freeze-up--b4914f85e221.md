## Verb

### Forms

- freezes up — present; singular; third-person
- freezing up — participle; present

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To be adversely affected by ice, and not function.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. (of machines and software) To come or bring to a sudden halt, stop working (functioning).

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** Since the last update, the program freezes up after a few minutes of use.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. (of people and other animals) To stop or be stopped due to attentiveness, fear, surprise, etc.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** Despite all of the rehearsals, as soon as I got on stage I froze up.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To become cold and formal in demeanour.

   **Usage:** intransitive
