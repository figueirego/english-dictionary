### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The study of the design or final purpose of natural occurrences, that is, of such occurrences being the result of intention instead of prior causes.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; philosophy; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The study of the design or final purpose of natural occurrences, that is, of such occurrences being the result of intention instead of prior causes.
   1. Design, final purpose, or intention in a natural occurrence; (countable) an assertion or instance of this.

      **Usage:** broadly; uncountable

      **Example:** Their proposed explanation at the time was riddled with teleology.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; philosophy; sciences

      **Source antonyms:** dysteleology

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The belief or theory that a natural occurrence is the result of divine design or intention rather than the laws of nature or science; theoteleology; (countable) a particular belief or theory of this sort.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** lifestyle; religion
