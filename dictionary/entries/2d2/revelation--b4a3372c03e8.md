### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of revealing or disclosing.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The revelation of the culprits' identities was cathartic for the populace.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Something that is revealed.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Something dramatically disclosed.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** This invention was a revelation for farmers throughout the region, who immediately adopted it.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A manifestation of divine truth.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** lifestyle; religion; theology

   **Source synonyms:** apocalypse

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Something that turns out to be a great success.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** This invention was a revelation for farming throughout the region, where it doubled crop yields.
