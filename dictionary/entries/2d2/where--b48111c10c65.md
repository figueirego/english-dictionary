### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In, at or to what place.

   **Usage:** interrogative; not-comparable

   **Example:** Where are you?

   **Example:** He asked where I grew up.

   **Example:** Where are you going?

   **Example:** Where you going?

   **Example:** I've forgotten where I was in this book, but it was probably around chapter four.

   **Example:** I hardly knew where I was going.

   **Source synonyms:** whither

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In what situation.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Where would we be without our parents?

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. In, at or to the place (that) or a place (that).

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Stay where you are.

   **Example:** Go back where you came from.

   **Example:** Let's go where it's warmer.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. In, at or to any place (that); wherever; anywhere.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Please sit where you like.

   **Example:** Their job is to go where they are called.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. In, at or to which.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; relative

   **Example:** This is the place where we first met.

   **Example:** He is looking for a house where he can have a complete office.

   **Example:** That's the place where we went on holiday.

   **Example:** Here's a picture of York, where I was born.

   **Example:** Show me an example where it happened that way.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The place in, at or to which.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; fused relative

   **Example:** He lives within five miles of where he was born.

   **Example:** This is a photo of where I went on holiday.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A situation or case in which.

   **Usage:** informal; not-comparable; fused relative

   **Example:** A function is where two variables are related.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. In a/the situation, position, case, etc. in which.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** You cannot be too careful where explosives are involved.

   **Example:** Where no provision under this Act is applicable, the case shall be decided in accordance with the customary practices.
