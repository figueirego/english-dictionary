### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To bribe, especially to deter oversight.

   **Usage:** informal; transitive

   **Example:** I thought the guards would give us trouble, but apparently he had paid them off.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To become worthwhile; to produce a net benefit.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Her years of Spanish classes finally paid off when she found herself in Mexico and realized she could communicate with people.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To pay back; to repay.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He paid off the loan three months early.

   **Source synonyms:** extinguish

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To pay back (repay) the entirety of a loan, thereby effecting the release of a lien on (the thing that was financed).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** This contract requires you to pay off the car by 2025.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To pay and discharge (an employee).

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To fall to leeward, as the head of a vessel under sail.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport
