### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The state of being brothers or a brother (also figuratively).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** brotherdom

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An association of people for any purpose, such as a society of monks; a fraternity.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** James formed a kind of brotherhood for ex-pats who were working in Valencia.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. All the people engaged in the same business, especially those of the same profession

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** the legal brotherhood

   **Example:** the medical brotherhood

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. People, or (poetically) things, of the same kind.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
