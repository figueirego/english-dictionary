### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The time zone that encompasses South Australia and Broken Hill in New South Wales from the first Sunday of April to the first Sunday of November and the Northern Territory year-round; it is nine hours and thirty minutes ahead of Coordinated Universal Time (UTC+09&#58;30).
