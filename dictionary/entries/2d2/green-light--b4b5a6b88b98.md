## Noun

### Forms

- green lights — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A traffic light in its green state (indicating that vehicles may proceed).

   **Example:** The drive home was much faster than usual; somehow we managed to hit every green light.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Approval, or permission to proceed.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** We have a green light on the project. Work starts today.

   **Source synonyms:** go

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A go-ahead to commit a crime, specifically to carry out a hit on someone (i.e. to kill).

   **Usage:** slang

   **Example:** Yo man, I heard someone had put a green light out on you, so watch your back.
