### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The female offspring of a horse's sire/dam that is by/out of a sibling of its other parent.

   **Usage:** horse breeding

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The female offspring of a horse's dam and a son of its sire.

   **Usage:** horse breeding

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The female offspring of a horse's sire and a daughter of its dam.
