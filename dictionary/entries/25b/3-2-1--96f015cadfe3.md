### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Following the mnemonic "3-2-1 rule" for data archiving and protection&#58; three copies of a piece of data are created, using two different types of media, and one is stored offsite.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; relational

   **Example:** 3-2-1 backup
