### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An unemployed person who has no intention of seeking a job, and survives on government-funded unemployment benefits.

   **Usage:** Ireland; slang

   **Example:** Paddy was a dolehead who was too lazy to ever work a day in his life.
