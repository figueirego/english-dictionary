### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Place or source of origin.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Many supermarkets display the provenance of their food products.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The place and time of origin of some artifact or other object. See Usage notes below.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** This spear is of Viking provenance.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** archaeology; history; human-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The history of ownership of a work of art.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The picture is of royal provenance.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** art; arts

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The copy history of a piece of data, or the intermediate pieces of data used to compute a final data element, as in a database record or web site (data provenance).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The execution history of computer processes which were used to compute a final piece of data (process provenance).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Background; history; place of origin.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a person
