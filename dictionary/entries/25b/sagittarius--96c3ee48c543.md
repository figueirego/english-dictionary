<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A constellation of the zodiac traditionally figured as a centaur drawing a bow. It contains the stars Kaus Australis, Kaus Borealis and Nunki.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astronomy; natural-sciences

   **Source synonyms:** Archer; Centaur; Sagittar (obsolete); Sagittary (obsolete)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The zodiac sign for the archer, ruled by Jupiter and covering November 23 - December 21 (tropical astrology) or December 16 - January 14 (sidereal astrology).

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astrology; human-sciences; mysticism; philosophy; sciences
