### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To be very loud; to produce a very loud or disruptive noise.

   **Usage:** figuratively; idiomatic; informal

   **Example:** For God's sake, kids, pipe down — you're going to wake the dead with that racket!

   **Source synonyms:** raise the dead; raise hell

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see wake, the, dead&#58; to hold a wake over a deceased person.

   **Usage:** uncommon
