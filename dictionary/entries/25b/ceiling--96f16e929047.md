### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The overhead closure of a room.

   **Example:** The dining room had an ornate ceiling.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The upper limit of an object or action.

   **Source synonyms:** cap

   **Source antonyms:** floor

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The highest altitude at which an aircraft can safely maintain flight.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** aeronautics; aerospace; aviation; business; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The measurement of visible distance from ground or sea level to an overcast cloud cover; under a clear sky, the ceiling measurement is identified as "unlimited."

   **Example:** Even though it was cloudy, there was still enough ceiling for the Blue Angels to perform a great show.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** climatology; meteorology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The smallest integer greater than or equal to a given number.

   **Example:** The ceiling of 4.5 is 5; the ceiling of −4.5 is −4.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The inner planking of a vessel.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The maximum permitted level in a financial transaction.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. The overhead interior surface that covers the upper limits of a room.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** architecture
