### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To use up.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The power plant consumes 30 tons of coal per hour.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To eat.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Baby birds consume their own weight in food each day.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To completely occupy the thoughts or attention of.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Desire consumed him.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To destroy completely.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The building was consumed by fire.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To waste away slowly.

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To obtain and use goods or services as an individual.

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** In a materialistic society, individuals are taught to consume, consume, consume.

   **Example:** If you consume this product while in Japan, you may be subject to consumption tax.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** economics; sciences

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To absorb information, especially through the mass media.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The Internet has changed the way we consume news.
