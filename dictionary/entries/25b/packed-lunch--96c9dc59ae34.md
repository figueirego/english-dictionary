### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A meal, sandwiches or anything packed and intended to be eaten away from home; especially, one packed at home as opposed to a portable packaged lunch from a vendor.

   **Example:** I forgot my packed lunch when I left for work this morning.

   **Source synonyms:** lunchpack
