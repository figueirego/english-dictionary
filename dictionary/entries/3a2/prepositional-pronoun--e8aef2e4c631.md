## Noun

### Forms

- prepositional pronouns — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A type of pronoun used in Celtic and Semitic languages, where a preposition followed by a personal pronoun creates a new word.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In certain Romance languages, the pronoun form which must be used following a preposition.
