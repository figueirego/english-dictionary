### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Arranged like rays that radiate from, or converge to, a common centre.

   **Example:** The spokes on most modern bicycle wheels are tangential to the hub rather than radial.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of, or relating to a radius (line joining to a geometric centre).

   **Example:** What is the relationship between arc length and radial length?

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of, or relating to a radius (line joining to a geometric centre).
   1. Moving or acting along a radius.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Of, or relating to the radius bone.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Of, or relating to the radius (vein), and/or the wing areas next to it.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; entomology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Developing uniformly on all sides.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Having reinforcing cords placed in parallel across the tyre at 90 degrees to the direction of travel, especially as contrasted with cross-ply; see also radial tyre.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a vehicle tyre
