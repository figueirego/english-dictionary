### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A round (cylindrical) vessel, such as a cask, of greater length than breadth, and bulging in the middle, made of staves bound with hoops, and having flat ends (heads). The word is sometimes applied to a similar cylindrical container made of metal, usually called a drum.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** a cracker barrel

   **Source synonyms:** bbl; cask

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   1. Such a cask of a certain size, holding one-eighth of what a tun holds. (See a diagram comparing cask sizes.)

      **Usage:** countable

      **Source synonyms:** bbl; cask; bbl
