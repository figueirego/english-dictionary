### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To leave one's present location; to leave somewhere for another place.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** Alright, troops, line up and move out!

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To vacate one's place of residence or employment.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** We must move out before the end of the month or we'll be paying extra rent.

   **Source synonyms:** quit; vacate

   **Source antonyms:** move in
