### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To confuse or overpower the sight of (someone or something, such as a sensor) by means of excessive brightness.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Dazzled by the headlights of the lorry, the deer stopped in the middle of the street.

   **Example:** Antidrone lasers can burn or dazzle a drone's sensors.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To render incapable of thinking clearly; to overwhelm with showiness or brilliance.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** The delegates were dazzled by the originality of his arguments.

   **Source synonyms:** impress; overpower

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To be overpowered by light; to be confused by excess of brightness.

   **Usage:** intransitive
