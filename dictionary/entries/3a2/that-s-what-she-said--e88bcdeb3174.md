### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A joking retort that draws attention to the possibility of sexual innuendo in what was just said.

   **Usage:** humorous; idiomatic

   **Example:** Arthur&#58; "That assignment took me all week to finish. It was so long and hard." Bob&#58; "That's what she said."
