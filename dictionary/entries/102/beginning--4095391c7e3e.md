## Noun · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /bɪˈɡɪn.ɪŋ/ — source labels: General-American; Received-Pronunciation
- IPA: /ˈbɪ.ɡɪ.nɪŋɡ/ — source labels: India

### Forms

- beginnings — plural
- begynnynge — alternative; obsolete

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of doing that which begins anything; commencement of an action, state, or space of time; entrance into being or upon a course; the first act, effort, or state of a succession of acts or states.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. That which is begun; a rudiment or element.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. That which begins or originates something; the source or first cause.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** What was the beginning of the dispute?

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The initial portion of some extended thing.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The author describes the main character’s youth at the beginning of the story.

   **Example:** That house is at the beginning of the street.
