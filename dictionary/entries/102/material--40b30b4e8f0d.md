### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Any matter (as metal, wood, plastic, fiber, etc.) from which the whole or the greater part of something physical (as a machine, tool, building, fabric, etc.) is made.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Asphalt, composed of oil and sand, is a widely used material for roads.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. A person, or people collectively, who are qualified or suited for a certain position or activity.

   **Usage:** collective; countable; sometimes; uncountable; preceded by a qualifying word

   **Example:** boy/girlfriend material

   **Example:** marriage material

   **Example:** We have lots of presidential material in various public offices.

   **Source synonyms:** appoint

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. Apparatus for doing or making something.

   **Usage:** countable; plural; uncountable; usually

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. Something (as data, observations, perceptions or ideas) that may be incorporated, elaborated or otherwise reworked into a finished form or new form, or may serve as the basis for arriving at interpretations, judgments or conclusions.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** teaching materials

   **Example:** We were a warm-up act at the time; we didn't have enough original material to headline.

**Source sense R2.S5**

**Source sense R2.S6**

   **Example:** The material of his character was basically good.
