## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A long-term strategy or endeavor.

   **Example:** We were surprised that he didn't object immediately, but then we realized that he's playing the long game.

   **Example:** I'm in it for the long game.

   **Source synonyms:** long haul

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The version of the game played until one team makes ten points, as opposed to the short game, which is played to five points.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The aspect of the game in which the strategy is to advance downfield by throwing the ball to a receiving player; the passing game.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** American-football; ball-games; football; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The portion of the game, played with driver clubs, in which the ball is advanced down the fairway to the putting green.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** golf; hobbies; lifestyle; sports
