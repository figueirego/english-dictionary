### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who has never had sexual intercourse, or (uncommonly) an animal that has never mated.

   **Source synonyms:** maiden; unicorn bait; virgo intacta; vestal

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person who has never engaged in any sexual activity at all.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Someone vowed to virginity (usually a woman and often a consecrated virgin), or someone who died in defense of their virginity; (especially) one venerated as a saint.

   **Usage:** Orthodoxy or historical

   **Example:** In its list of saints, the Catholic Church classifies St. Agnes, St. Maria Goretti, and St. Thérèse of Lisieux as virgins.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Catholicism; Christianity

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. One who has never used or experienced a specified thing.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** I've never eaten tofu before—you could say I'm a tofu virgin.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Any of several species of gossamer-winged butterflies of the family Lycaenidae.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A female insect producing eggs from which young are hatched, though there has been no fecundation by a male; a parthenogenetic insect.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; entomology; natural-sciences

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** maid (archaic); unicorn bait (humorous; slang); virgin
