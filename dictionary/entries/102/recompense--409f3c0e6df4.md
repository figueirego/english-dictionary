## Noun · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈɹɛkəmˌpɛns/ — source labels: Canada; General-American; Received-Pronunciation

### Forms

- recompenses — plural
- recompence — alternative; obsolete

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An equivalent returned for anything given, done, or suffered; compensation; reward; amends; requital.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** meed; payback; recompence

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. That which compensates for an injury, or other type of harm or damage.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** He offered money as recompense for the damage, but what the injured party wanted was an apology.

   **Source synonyms:** amends; recompence; remuneration; restitution; satisfaction; amends; acquittal; assyth (obsolete); bote (historical); compensation; indemnification; indemnity; mends (Scotland); quittal (obsolete); recompensation (proscribed; sometimes); recompense; redress; relief; remuneration; reparation; requital; requite; restitution; satisfaction

### Pronunciation

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To reward or repay (someone) for something done, given etc.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To give compensation for an injury, or other type of harm or damage.

   **Example:** The judge ordered the defendant to recompense the plaintiff by paying $100.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

   **Usage:** transitive
