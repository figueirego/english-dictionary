## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The process where a nation or entity allows the value of its currency to fluctuate freely in the foreign exchange market, removing the fixed exchange rate or peg to another currency.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** banking; business; finance

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A situation where a stablecoin loses its fixed value and trades at a price that is significantly different from its intended peg.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; cryptocurrencies; cryptocurrency; finance
