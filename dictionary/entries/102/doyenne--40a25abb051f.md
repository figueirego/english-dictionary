### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A female doyen.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The senior or eldest female member of a group, especially one who is most or highly respected.

   **Example:** Helen Thomas is frequently referred to as "the doyenne of the White House press corps".

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A woman who is highly experienced and knowledgeable in a particular field, subject, or line of work; expert.
