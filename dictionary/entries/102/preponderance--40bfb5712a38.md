### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Superiority in amount or number; the bulk or majority; also, a large amount or number; an abundance, a profusion.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** weight

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Superiority of influence, power, a quality, etc.; an outweighing, predominance, pre-eminence.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** preponderancy (obsolete); preponderation; prevalence

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Greater physical weight.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** preponderancy (obsolete)
