### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person or party that is consulted.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person whose occupation is to be consulted for their expertise, advice, service or help in an area or specialty; a party whose business is to be similarly consulted.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Synonym of informant.

   **Usage:** Canada; US

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** informant (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A senior hospital-based physician or surgeon who has completed all specialist training and has been placed on the specialist register in their chosen speciality, roughly equivalent to an attending physician in North America.

   **Usage:** Ireland; UK

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences
