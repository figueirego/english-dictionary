### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An inhabitant of Great Britain, particularly (historical) a Celt from the area of Roman Britain or (obsolete) a Welshman; a British person.

   **Example:** The ancient Britons were particularly fond of Anglesey, which the Romans knew as Mona.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A citizen of the United Kingdom or (historical, obsolete) its overseas empire.

   **Example:** The victims included 3 Canadians, 2 Irishmen, and 1 Briton.

   **Example:** The hiker was a Briton from New Zealand.
