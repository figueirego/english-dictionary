### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To regulate, adjust or adapt.
   1. To change the pitch, intensity or tone of one's voice or of a musical instrument.

      **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To regulate, adjust or adapt.
   1. To vary the amplitude, frequency or phase of a carrier wave in proportion to the amplitude etc of a source wave (such as speech or music).

      **Usage:** transitive
