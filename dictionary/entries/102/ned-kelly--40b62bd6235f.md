## Noun

### Forms

- Ned Kellys — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A figure or person akin to the iconic Australian bushranger Ned Kelly, typically denoting someone with audacious recklessness or morally questionable business dealings, or who opposes or defies authority.

   **Usage:** Australia; slang

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An instance of behaviour that exemplifies such characteristics.

   **Usage:** Australia; slang

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A figure or person who, like Ned Kelly, shows great bravery, daring, tenacity, or ferocity.

   **Usage:** Australia; slang

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The belly. Often shortened to Ned.

   **Usage:** slang
