### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To act before (someone), especially to prevent an action.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** preclude; provide; preempt; discount

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To take up or introduce (something) prematurely.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The advocate plans to anticipate a part of her argument.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To know of (something) before it happens; to expect.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to anticipate the pleasures of a visit

   **Example:** to anticipate the evils of life

   **Example:** Please anticipate a journey of an hour from your house to the airport.

   **Source synonyms:** expect; foretaste; foresee

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To eagerly wait for (something)

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Little Johnny started to anticipate the arrival of Santa Claus a week before Christmas.

   **Source synonyms:** look forward to
