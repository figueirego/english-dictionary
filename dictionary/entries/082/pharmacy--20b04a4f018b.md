### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A place where prescription drugs are sold or dispensed.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The science of medicinal substances, inclusive of pharmaceutics, pharmaceutical chemistry, pharmacology, phytochemistry, and forensics.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The role or occupation of a pharmacist.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A collection, group or set of drugs or similar products; a set of drugs taken.

   **Usage:** collective; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** In that neighborhood, you could find a dealer for any drug, a whole pharmacy of chemical distractions.

   **Example:** He hoped to get off the pharmacy of medications that quack put him on.

   **Example:** Her garden was a pharmacy of herbs.
