### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Twenty four hours per day, seven days per week, designating a round-the-clock service, typically including holidays, as might be offered by a supermarket, ATM, gas station, concierge service or manned data center.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** They're open twenty-four seven; you can go there any time you want.

   **Source synonyms:** all the time; constantly; continuously; non-stop; nonstop; unceasingly; unstoppingly; twenty-four hours a day; round the clock

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Constantly, with little or no interruption; continually; very often.

   **Usage:** colloquial; figuratively; not-comparable; often

   **Example:** I'm ready to skydive twenty-four seven. \[⁠nonliteral, hyperbolic⁠\]
