### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A statement or an idea which is unproven, but is thought to be true; a guess.

   **Usage:** countable; formal; uncountable

   **Example:** I explained it, but it is pure conjecture whether he understood, or not.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A supposition based upon incomplete evidence; a hypothesis.

   **Usage:** countable; formal; uncountable

   **Example:** The physicist used his conjecture about subatomic particles to design an experiment.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A statement likely to be true based on available evidence, but which has not been formally proven.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Interpretation of signs and omens.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable
