<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A nonacademic, periodical publication which consists of articles by multiple writers on some broad topic or theme.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A nonacademic, periodical publication which consists of articles by multiple writers on some broad topic or theme.
   1. A radio or television show where each episode comprises a variety of features or segments, akin to the format of a print magazine.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** broadcasting; media
