### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To hum or sing (a song or tune), or to speak (words), softly in a low pitch or in a sentimental manner; specifically, to sing (a popular song) in a low, mellow voice.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He was crooning a song.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To soothe (a person or an animal) by singing softly.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To hum or sing, or to speak, softly in a low pitch or in a sentimental manner; specifically, to sing a popular song in a low, mellow voice.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** She was crooning, but I couldn’t make out what the song was.

   **Source synonyms:** coo

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To lament, to moan.

   **Usage:** Northern-England; Scotland; intransitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To make a continuous hollow low-pitched moan, as of cattle; to bellow, to low.

   **Usage:** Northern-England; Scotland; intransitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Of a large bell&#58; to make a low-pitched sound.

   **Usage:** Northern-England; Scotland; intransitive
