### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A small beanbag or sandbag used as a ball in a number of sports and games. It is typically controlled by the feet, but in some sports every part of the body except the hands and arms may be used.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
