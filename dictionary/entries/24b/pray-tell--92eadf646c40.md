## Interjection

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Please explain (something the requester does not yet understand).

   **Usage:** archaic

   **Example:** Pray tell us, how will they fare while you are away?

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Expressing incredulity, please explain (some claim or position the speaker doubts).

   **Usage:** idiomatic; sarcastic; usually

   **Example:** But how, pray tell, do you "notice" something that happens when you're asleep?
