### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An act or instance of making something larger.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Rick was ashamed about the size of his penis, so he had a penis enlargement.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A making more obvious or serious; exacerbation.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An image, particularly a photograph, that has been enlarged.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Freedom from confinement; liberty.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
