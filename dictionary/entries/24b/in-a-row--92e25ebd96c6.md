### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Successively, one after the other, in succession; together.

   **Example:** After making losses four years in a row, the manager knew she had to sack someone.

   **Source synonyms:** together; on the trot; on the spin; on the bounce

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Placed in a straight line.

   **Example:** Bottles of every type of alcohol they had were lined up in a row behind the bar.
