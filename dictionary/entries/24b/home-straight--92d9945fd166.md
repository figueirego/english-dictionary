### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The straight part of a running track (or similar) leading to the finishing line.

   **Example:** He was only able to get out into the clear coming into the home straight.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** athletics; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

   **Source synonyms:** home stretch
