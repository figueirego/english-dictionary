### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To look after, to care for.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** My elderly mother needs to be taken care of.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To deal with, handle.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Can somebody take care of the customers while I clean this mess?

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To kill.

   **Usage:** euphemistic; slang; transitive

   **Example:** In the motion picture ‘The Godfather’, gangster Virgil Sollozzo took care of Luca Brasi by having him strangled.
