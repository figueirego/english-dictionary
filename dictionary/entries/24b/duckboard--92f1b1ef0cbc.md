## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

- duckboards — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of a long series of boards laid from side to side as a path across wet or muddy ground.

   **Usage:** in-plural

   **Example:** In an attempt to alleviate the problem, wooden planking, known as duckboards, was placed at the bottom of trenches and across other areas of muddy or waterlogged ground.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Wooden, low walkway or short part of a path with one or more planks, logs, or boards laid after each other lengthwise, often two planks wide.

   **Source synonyms:** bog board; bog bridge; puncheon

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A panel of wooden slats typically laid on a concrete floor in a workshop, to reduce fatigue for a person operating a machine tool or working at a bench.
