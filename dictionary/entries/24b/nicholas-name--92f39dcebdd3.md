### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A longer name corresponding to a shortened version common as a nickname, either of which may be the person or thing's actual name.

   **Usage:** humorous

   **Example:** He always calls me my nicholas-name Josephine, even though I keep telling him JJ is fine.

   **Example:** My legal name is Tim, but my nicholas-name is Timothy.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A familiar name for a person or thing that is used in place of its actual name, usually longer or less wieldy.

   **Usage:** humorous

   **Example:** I'm Kai, but my friends call me Kylesternus. It's a nicholas-name.
