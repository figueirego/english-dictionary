### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A name in a vernacular language, distinct from a taxonomic name, that is used to refer to an organism and may, though not always, correspond to a taxonomic name.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences; taxonomy

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A name that does not unambiguously define a substance.
