### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To exaggerate or overstate a feeling or emotion.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** She really laid it on thick when her pet died.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To flatter.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** He knew he needed to lay it on thick in his job interview.

   **Source synonyms:** jolly; make fair weather; tickle the ear; brush up to (archaic; slang); collogue; [flatter](../364/flatter--d92d2c2d4bb1.md); flatterize (nonstandard); glaver (obsolete); gloze; jolly (archaic); lay it on thick (idiomatic); make fair weather (archaic); palaver; preen; puff; soothe; speak well of; stroke (figuratively; obsolete); tickle the ear (idiomatic)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To guilt, guilt-trip.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** Her mother laid it on thick, telling her to call home more often.
