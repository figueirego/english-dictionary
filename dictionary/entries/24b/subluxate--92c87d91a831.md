<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To cause the subluxation of something&#58; to partially dislocate or displace.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He subluxated the joint.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To undergo subluxation&#58; to become partially dislocated or displaced.

   **Usage:** intransitive
