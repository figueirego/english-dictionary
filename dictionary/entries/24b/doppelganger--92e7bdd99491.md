### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A ghostly double of a living person, especially one that haunts such a person.

   **Source synonyms:** double; doubleganger

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An evil twin.

   **Source synonyms:** alter ego

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A remarkably similar double.

   **Source synonyms:** lookalike; alter ego

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A monster that takes the forms of people, usually after killing them.
