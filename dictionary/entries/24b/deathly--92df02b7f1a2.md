### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Appearing as though dead, or on the verge of death.

   **Example:** He has a deathly pallor.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Deadly, fatal, causing death.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

3. Extreme.

   **Example:** He has a deathly fear of crocodiles.

- IPA: /ˈdɛθli/ — source labels: UK

### Forms

- more deathly — comparative
- most deathly — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. In a way that resembles death.

   **Example:** He was deathly pale.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Extremely, dreadfully.

   **Example:** The water was deathly cold.
