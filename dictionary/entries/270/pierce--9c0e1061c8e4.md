### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To puncture; to break through.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The diver pierced the surface of the water with scarcely a splash.

   **Example:** to pierce the enemy's line

   **Example:** A shot pierced the ship.

   **Source synonyms:** transfix

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To create a hole in the skin for the purpose of inserting jewelry.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Can you believe he pierced his tongue?

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. to break or interrupt abruptly

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** A dreadful scream pierced the silence.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To get to the heart or crux of (a matter).

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** to pierce a mystery

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To penetrate; to affect deeply.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** A stab of fear pierced my heart.
