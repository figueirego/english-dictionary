### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To feel pain, sorrow, or regret for what one has done or omitted to do; the cause for repenting may be indicated with "of".

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Marry in haste, repent at leisure.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To be sorry for sin as morally evil, and to seek forgiveness; to cease to practice sin and to love.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** lifestyle; religion; theology

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To feel pain on account of; to remember with sorrow.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To be sorry for, to regret.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I repent my sins.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To cause to have sorrow or regret.

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To cause (oneself) to feel pain or regret.

   **Usage:** obsolete; reflexive

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To change one's mind.

   **Usage:** archaic; intransitive
