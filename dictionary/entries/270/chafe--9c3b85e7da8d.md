### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To excite heat in by friction; to rub in order to stimulate and make warm.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To excite passion or anger in; to fret; to irritate.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To fret and wear by rubbing.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to chafe a cable

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To rub; to come together so as to wear by rubbing; to wear by friction.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To be worn by rubbing.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** A cable chafes.

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To have a feeling of vexation; to be vexed; to fret; to be irritated.
