### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Earth, soil, land, or ground as a physical surface.

   **Usage:** countable; literary; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The material world as opposed to the heavens.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A continent or large landmass, especially on another planet or moon; e.g. Arabia Terra or Aphrodite Terra.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A lunar highland or mountainous region with relatively high albedo; e.g. Terra Nivium.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
