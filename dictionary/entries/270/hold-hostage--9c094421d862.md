### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To hold as hostage.
   1. To have possession or custody of (a person) as security for performance against a treaty, a pledge, or a demand, especially now an extra-legal demand.

      **Usage:** idiomatic

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To hold as hostage.
   1. To seek advantage in a situation by threatening the well-being or success of (a person or thing not directly involved in the situation).

      **Usage:** idiomatic

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To hold as hostage.
   1. To sabotage (a computer) with intent to blackmail the owner.

      **Usage:** idiomatic

      **Example:** Before Frank could turn in his final exam, Jigsaw held his computer hostage.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computer; computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; science; sciences

      **Source context (verbatim):** of a hacker or malware

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To hold in custody or use for any advantage.

   **Usage:** idiomatic
