### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To ban a person from a city for five or ten years through the procedure of ostracism.

   **Usage:** British; English; Oxford; US; historical; transitive; Ancient Greece

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To exclude a person from a community or from society by not communicating with them or by refusing to acknowledge their presence; to refuse to associate with or talk to; to shun.

   **Usage:** British; English; Oxford; US; broadly

   **Source synonyms:** blackball; cut someone dead; give someone the cold shoulder; send to Coventry; leper; brush off (idiomatic); discount; disregard; dissemble; despise; ignore; misheed; misregard; neglect; pass; pass by (idiomatic); pay no heed (idiomatic); put aside (idiomatic); set at nought (archaic); shrug off (idiomatic); slough; take no notice of (idiomatic); tune out (idiomatic); unheed; unmind
