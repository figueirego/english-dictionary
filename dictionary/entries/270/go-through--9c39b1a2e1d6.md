## Verb

### Forms

- goes through — present; singular; third-person
- going through — participle; present
- went through — past
- gone through — participle; past

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To travel from one end of something to the other.

   **Usage:** literally

   **Example:** The train went through the tunnel.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To execute or carry out.

   **Example:** They went through the process step by step and ended up with Beef Wellingtom.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To examine or scrutinize (a number or series of things).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Every morning, she went through her mail over a cup of coffee.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To rob (someone), especially after searching through their clothes.

   **Usage:** dated; slang

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To enact or recite the entire length of (something).

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To undergo, suffer, experience.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I went through a lengthy immigration process before I was allowed across the border.

   **Example:** She's yet to go through puberty, although she's already 17.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To use up or wear out (clothing etc.).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Her family drinks so much milk that they go through two gallons a week.

   **Example:** I've gone through two pairs of shoes already this holiday.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To progress to the next stage of something.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** If United don't lose by more than 2 goals, they should go through to the next round.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To reach an intended destination after passing through some process.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** My payment hasn't gone through yet.

   **Example:** I don't think my instant messages are going through.

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. To beat, fight or attack (someone).

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. To be present and intense.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The cardamom goes through the cake.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a flavor or ingredient
