### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In the International System of Units, the base unit of thermodynamic temperature; ¹⁄\_(273.16) of the thermodynamic temperature of the triple point of water.

   **Example:** Ice melts above 273.15 kelvins.

   **Example:** Water boils above 373.15 kelvins.

   **Source synonyms:** degree Kelvin; degree absolute

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One degree of difference on this scale, as a unit interval.

   **Example:** The dew point here today is only 1 kelvin above what it was yesterday.

   **Example:** The interval between the freezing and boiling points of water is 100 kelvins.

   **Source synonyms:** degree Kelvin; degree absolute

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A measurement of color warmth defined by the temperature kelvin at which an incandescent blackbody would emit a given color.
