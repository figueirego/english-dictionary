### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Rebirth; regeneration; (countable) an instance of this.

   **Usage:** also; figuratively; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** palingenesia; palingenesy (archaic; rare); renaissance; resuscitation; revival

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Rebirth; regeneration; (countable) an instance of this.
   1. The recurrence of historical events in the same order in an infinite series of cycles.

      **Usage:** also; countable; figuratively; uncountable

      **Source synonyms:** palingenesia; palingenesy (archaic; rare); renaissance; resuscitation; revival

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Rebirth; regeneration; (countable) an instance of this.
   1. Spiritual rebirth through the transmigration of the soul.

      **Usage:** also; countable; figuratively; historical; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; lifestyle; philosophy; religion; sciences; theology

      **Source synonyms:** palingenesia; palingenesy (archaic; rare); renaissance; resuscitation; revival; metempsychosis

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The apparent repetition, during the development of a single embryo, of changes that occurred previously in the evolution of its species.

   **Usage:** historical; obsolete; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences

   **Source synonyms:** palingeny (obsolete; rare); recapitulation

   **Source antonyms:** caenogenesis

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The regeneration of magma by the melting of metamorphic rocks.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; geology; natural-sciences
