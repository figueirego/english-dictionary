### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Liquid material, generally saliva, that dribbles or drools outward and downward from the mouth.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** There was dried slobber on his coat lapel.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A dribbly shower (of rain).

   **Usage:** countable; uncommon; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Mud, muck; a miry, slushy or muddy mixture.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A jellyfish.

   **Usage:** countable; dated; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Attributive form of slobbers; causing or relating to the veterinary medical condition slobbers.

   **Usage:** attributive; countable; form-of; uncountable
