### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A legal principle in the law of equity that prevents a party from asserting otherwise valid legal rights against another party because of conduct by the first party, or circumstances to which the first party has knowingly contributed, making it unjust for those rights to be asserted.

   **Usage:** common; countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law
