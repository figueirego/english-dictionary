### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Extremely disheartening&#58; that causes erosion of moral fortitude or that eliminates hope or motivation.

   **Example:** This litany of travails is soul-crushing.

   **Source synonyms:** soul-destroying; demotivating; demoralizing; despiriting; disappointing; discouraging; disheartening; dispiriting

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Extremely boring.

   **Example:** soul-crushing boredom

   **Example:** I can't sit through another lecture from this professor — his delivery is soul-crushing.
