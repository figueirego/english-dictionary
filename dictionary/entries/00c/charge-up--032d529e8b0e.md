### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To recharge, to give electrical power to something.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** You should charge up your phone before the camping trip, as there won't be any power supplies.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To motivate, to instill someone with determination.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** The coach had to charge up his players with a powerful speech before the final.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To deliberately infect (a person) with HIV as part of bug-chasing.
