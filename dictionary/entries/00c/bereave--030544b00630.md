### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To deprive by or as if by violence; to rob; to strip.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To take away by destroying, impairing, or spoiling; take away by violence.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To deprive of power; prevent.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To take away someone or something that is important or close; deprive.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Death bereaved him of his wife.

   **Example:** The family has been recently bereaved.

   **Example:** The castaways were bereft of hope.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To destroy life; cut off.

   **Usage:** intransitive; rare
