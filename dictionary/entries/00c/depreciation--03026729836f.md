## Noun

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /dɪˌpɹiːʃɪˈeɪʃən/ — source labels: UK

### Forms

- depreciations — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The state of being depreciated; disparagement.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The decline in value of assets.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** asset depreciation

   **Example:** currency depreciation

   **Example:** The accountant calculated the annual depreciation of the machinery.

   **Example:** There was a sharp depreciation in the value of the currency.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The measurement of the decline in value of assets. Not to be confused with impairment, which is the measurement of the unplanned, extraordinary decline in value of assets.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** annual depreciation

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** accounting; business; finance
