### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A mixture of fruit, spices and suet used as a filling for mince pies.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Minced meat, mince.

   **Usage:** countable; rare; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; by analogy

**Source sense R1.S4**
