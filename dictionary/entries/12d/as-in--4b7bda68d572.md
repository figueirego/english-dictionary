## Adverb

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In the sense of.

   **Usage:** conjunctive; idiomatic; not-comparable

   **Example:** "bow" as in the weapon, not the front of a ship

   **Source synonyms:** namely; i.e.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In the sense of.
   1. As pronounced in.

      **Usage:** conjunctive; idiomatic; not-comparable

      **Example:** That's B as in boy.

      **Source synonyms:** namely; i.e.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. By which I mean; that is to say.

   **Usage:** informal; not-comparable

   **Example:** The TV is completely broken, as in I can't get any picture at all.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Used to emphasize a point.
   1. added after a point has been made

      **Usage:** Philippines; not-comparable

      **Example:** He's so tall, as in!

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Used to emphasize a point.
   1. asked if the prior statement is really true; really

      **Usage:** Philippines; not-comparable

      **Example:** A&#58; It's so traffic a while ago. B&#58; As in? A&#58; Yes, it took me an hour to pass through!

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see as, in.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** In Sweden, as in most countries, ...
