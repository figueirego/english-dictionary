### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A region producing milk that may be supplied to the area of demand with practical (competitively advantageous) speed and cost of transport; that is, the region that produces the milk that is consumed by a particular population or subpopulation.

   **Example:** Counties adjoining a large city tended to supply most of its milk before highway and trucking improvements broadened the milkshed.
