### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Living or occurring only in the presence of oxygen e.g. aerobic bacteria.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The bacteria require an aerobic environment to grow.

   **Source antonyms:** anaerobic

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of exercise, performed while maintaining a sufficient supply of oxygen to meet bodily energy needs.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of or relating to aerobics.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** She does aerobic exercise three times a week.

   **Example:** He joined an aerobic fitness class at the gym.
