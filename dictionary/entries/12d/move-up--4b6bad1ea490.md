<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To put (something) higher or further.
   1. To promote, put onto a higher level.

      **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To put (something) higher or further.
   1. To raise, put onto a higher place.

      **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To put (something) higher or further.
   1. To put higher on a list.

      **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To reschedule (something) to an earlier date or time.

   **Usage:** US; transitive

   **Example:** The meeting was moved up to Tuesday.

   **Source synonyms:** move forward; prepone

   **Source antonyms:** move back

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To move higher.
   1. To be promoted.

      **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To move higher.
   1. To pass to a higher level.

      **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To move one's position to allow others to occupy a place.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Can you move up to the next seat so we can sit together, please?

   **Source synonyms:** move over

**Source sense R1.S8**
