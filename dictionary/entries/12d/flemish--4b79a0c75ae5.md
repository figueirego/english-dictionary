## Adjective · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈflɛmɪʃ/ — source labels: General-American; Received-Pronunciation

### Forms

- more Flemish — comparative
- most Flemish — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of or relating to Flanders, either as the historical county of Flanders (the current provinces of West Flanders and East Flanders in Belgium, Zeelandic Flanders in the Netherlands and French Flanders); or as the Dutch-speaking region of Belgium.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of or relating to the Belgian standard variety of the Dutch language.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of or relating to West Flemish, East Flemish and/or French Flemish dialects of Dutch.

## Proper noun · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈflɛmɪʃ/ — source labels: General-American; Received-Pronunciation

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Standard Dutch as it is spoken in Flanders.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. West Flemish (including French Flemish and Zeelandic Flemish) and East Flemish dialects of Dutch.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. Any of the Low Franconian dialects or languages spoken in northern Belgium, mainly West Flemish, East Flemish, Brabantian dialects of Dutch and Limburgish.
