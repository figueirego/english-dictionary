## Noun

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈs(j)ʉːpəˌɹænjʉːˈæɪʃn̩/ — source labels: Australia

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A retirement benefit fund, an accumulation of regular deductions from one′s wage or salary while employed and similar regular contributions from the employer, usually administered by an independent entity; a pension.

   **Usage:** Australia; New-Zealand; UK; uncountable; usually

   **Source synonyms:** pension; super

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The condition or of being superannuated; old age or obsolescence.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source synonyms:** retirement; elderliness; disuse; age; chair days (obsolete); codgerhood; eld (archaic; poetic); elderliness; fogeydom; geezerdom (US; slang); golden years (euphemistic; idiomatic); old age; oldhood; senescence; senectitude; superannuation; twilight years (idiomatic); sunset years (idiomatic); vetustity (rare); desuetude; disusage; disuse; inusitation (archaic); obsolescence; obsoleteness; obsoletion; superannuation; unuse
