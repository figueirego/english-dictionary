### Pronunciation

### Forms

- proceding — alternative; obsolete

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. The act of one who proceeds, or who prosecutes a design or transaction.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. An event or happening; something that happens.

   **Example:** I had a grandstand view of the proceedings.

   **Source synonyms:** befalling; incident; affair; befalling; episode; instance; event; goings-on (plural; plural-only); happening; hap (archaic; slang); incident; occasion (obsolete); occurrence; proceeding

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. A published collection of papers presented at an academic conference, or representing the acts of a learned society.

   **Usage:** always in plural

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. Progress or movement from one thing to another.

   **Source synonyms:** procedure

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. A measure or step taken in a course of business; a transaction.

   **Example:** an illegal proceeding

   **Example:** a cautious or a violent proceeding

   **Source synonyms:** measure; step; transaction

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**
