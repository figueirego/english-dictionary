### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A word that negates or removes the meaning of the word it qualifies.

   **Usage:** dated; derogatory

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A word used to hedge a statement, for example to make it vague, equivocal, or misleading.

   **Usage:** derogatory

   **Source synonyms:** hedge
