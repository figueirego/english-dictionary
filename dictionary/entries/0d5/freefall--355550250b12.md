### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A state of motion affected by no acceleration or force other than that of gravity.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A state of motion but allowing for the presence of incidental air resistance not caused intentionally by devices such parachutes or wings.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A rapid, uncontrolled decline or worsening.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; figuratively; uncountable

   **Example:** As civil war continues in Iraq, the U.S. president's approval ratings are in freefall.
