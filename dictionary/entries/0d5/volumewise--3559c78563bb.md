## Adverb

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Regarding volume or volumes (in any sense of those words); in terms of volume or volumes.
   1. Regarding the amount of change measured absolutely rather than relatively (i.e., as a fraction of an earlier baseline value).

      **Usage:** not-comparable

      **Example:** They keep reporting their sales increases volumewise — because big numbers sound impressive — but what the investors care about is the percentagewise figure, which is anemic at only 0.4%.

      **Example:** Our sales are doing fine volumewise, but our margins are too low. We need to increase efficiency somehow.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Regarding volume or volumes (in any sense of those words); in terms of volume or volumes.
   1. Regarding volume or volumes (amount of space occupied); in terms of volume or volumes.

      **Usage:** not-comparable

      **Example:** Volumewise, Lake Baikal is the largest freshwater lake on Earth, but areawise, Lake Superior is the largest.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Regarding volume or volumes (in any sense of those words); in terms of volume or volumes.
   1. Regarding volume (loudness of sound).

      **Usage:** not-comparable

      **Example:** There's nothing wrong with the audio volumewise — I can hear it loud and clear. But the tone color is weirdly distorted.
