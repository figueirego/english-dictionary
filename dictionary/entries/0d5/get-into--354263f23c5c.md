### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To move into (an object), such that one ends up inside it.

   **Example:** She got into the car.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To put on (an item of clothing).

   **Example:** I'm soaking wet. I need to get into some dry clothes.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To reach into or interact with (an object).

   **Example:** The small child got into everything.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To become involved or interested in (a discussion, issue, or activity); to come to enjoy (something).

   **Example:** He got into politics.

   **Example:** My sister has got into flower arranging recently.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To enter (an unfavourable state).

   **Example:** How did we get into such a mess?

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To cause to behave uncharacteristically; to possess.

   **Example:** I don't know what's gotten into that child.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To interrogate in order to arrive at a deeper understanding.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To penetrate (someone) sexually.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To owe money to (someone); to become involved in something unsavory.

   **Example:** He's gotten into the gang to the tune of five grand.

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. To argue about or discuss (something).

   **Example:** Oh let's not get into that again.
