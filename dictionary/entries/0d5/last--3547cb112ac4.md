### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Final, ultimate, coming after all others of its kind.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Will try to fix it by myself for now&#58; as a last resort, we can always take it to a grease monkey.

   **Example:** Eyes Wide Shut was the last film to be directed by Stanley Kubrick.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Most recent, latest, last so far.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The last time I saw him, he was married.

   **Example:** I have received your note dated the 17th last, and am responding to say that\[…\]

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Farthest of all from a given quality, character, or condition; most unlikely, or least preferable.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** He is the last person to be accused of theft.

   **Example:** The last person I want to meet is Helen.

   **Example:** More rain is the last thing we need right now.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Being the only one remaining of its class.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Japan is the last empire.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Supreme; highest in degree; utmost.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Lowest in rank or degree.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Three contestants will win awards, but the last prize is just a book voucher.

**Source sense R2.S1**

   **Example:** We went there last year.

   **Example:** I was last to go; you're next.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Closest in the past, or closest but one if the closest was very recent; of days, sometimes thought to specifically refer to the instance closest to seven days (one week) ago, or the most recent instance before seven days (one week) ago.

   **Example:** It's Wednesday, and the party was last Tuesday; that is, not yesterday, but eight days ago.

   **Example:** When you say last Monday, do you mean the Monday just gone, or the one before that?

   **Example:** Where my brother had stood last night, I now stood.
