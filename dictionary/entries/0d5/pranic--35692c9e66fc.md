**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of or relating to prana.

   **Usage:** yoga

   **Example:** In yoga, we do not look at foods in terms of vitamins, minerals or proteins. We categorize food in the three following ways – positive pranic food, negative pranic food, and zero pranic food. Positive substances are those which, when consumed, add prana to the system. The pranic energy, the vital energy in the body, will increase.
