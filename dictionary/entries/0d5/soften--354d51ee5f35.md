### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make something soft or softer.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Soften the butter before beating in the sugar.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To undermine the morale of someone (often soften up).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Before the invasion, we softened up the enemy with the artillery.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To make less harsh.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Having second thoughts, I softened my criticism.

   **Source synonyms:** subdue; temper

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To become soft or softer.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The butter softened as it warmed up.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To become or make (a consonant) more lenis, to lenite.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; phonology; sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To palatalize.

   **Usage:** Slavic phonology
