### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To raise to the same level or status; to improve so as to make as good as others.

   **Usage:** transitive; literal and figurative

   **Example:** The government promises to level up the department.

   **Source synonyms:** step up

   **Source antonyms:** level down

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To reduce economic and developmental inequalities across the United Kingdom.

   **Usage:** UK; specific

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To progress to the next level of player character stats and abilities, often by acquiring experience points in role-playing games.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive; roleplaying games

   **Example:** I leveled up after defeating the dragon.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** video-games
