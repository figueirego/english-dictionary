## Prep\_phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Not in the correct musical pitch.

   **Example:** The violins go out of tune in damp weather.

   **Example:** By the end of the song, I was completely out of tune with the guitar.

   **Source synonyms:** off-pitch; pitchy

   **Source antonyms:** in tune

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Not in agreement or in harmony (with something).

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** The party’s social policy is out of tune with the values of most citizens.

   **Source synonyms:** out of step
