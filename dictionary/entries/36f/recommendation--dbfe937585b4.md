### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An act of recommending.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. That which is recommended.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A commendation or endorsement.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Your next employer may require a recommendation.

   **Source synonyms:** testimonial

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A suggestion or proposal about the best course of action (with adpositions including "about" for the context and "to" for the course of action)

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** He made a recommendation about what food to order.

   **Example:** We followed the recommendation to order sushi.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Content which is automatically recommended to a user by the suggestion algorithm of a website or app.

   **Usage:** Internet; countable; plural; uncountable; usually; especially YouTube

   **Source synonyms:** recommended; suggested; suggestion
