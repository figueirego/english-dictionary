### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The state of being stagnant; (countable) an instance of this.
   1. The state of lacking flow or motion, usually causing a lack of freshness or health; (countable) an instance of this.

      **Usage:** uncountable

      **Example:** Factors known to encourage the growth of harmful bacteria inside cooling systems include the stagnation of the water.

      **Source synonyms:** restagnation; stagnance; stagnancy

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The state of being stagnant; (countable) an instance of this.
   1. The state of lacking activity, change or progress, or excitement in an unhealthy manner; inactivity, staleness; (countable) an instance of this.

      **Usage:** figuratively; uncountable

      **Example:** The general concern about industrial stagnation inspired an overhaul of the patent system.

      **Source synonyms:** restagnation; stagnance; stagnancy

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The state of being stagnant; (countable) an instance of this.
   1. The state of lacking activity, change or progress, or excitement in an unhealthy manner; inactivity, staleness; (countable) an instance of this.
      1. The state of low or no growth in an economy; (countable) an instance of this.

         **Usage:** figuratively; uncountable

         **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** economics; sciences
