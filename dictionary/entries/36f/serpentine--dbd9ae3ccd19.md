### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of, pertaining to, or characteristic of snakes.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of, or having attributes associated with, the serpent referred to in the book of Genesis in the Bible, such as craftiness or deceitfulness.

   **Example:** The wily criminal was known for his serpentine behavior.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Having the form or shape of a snake.

   **Example:** There are serpentine species of lizards which do not have legs.

   **Source synonyms:** ophidian

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Curving in alternate directions; sinuous.

   **Example:** The serpentine path through the mountains was narrow and dangerous.

   **Source synonyms:** sinuous; tortuous; winding

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Pertaining to the serpentine subgroup of minerals.

   **Example:** serpentine soils
