### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An omission of words needed to fully express the sense of a phrase.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A line or dash used to show that text has been omitted.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A mutation of the initial sound of a word by which voiceless sounds become voiced, voiced stops become nasal consonants, and vowels acquire a prothetic nasal consonant&#58; see Appendix&#58;Irish mutations\#Eclipsis.

   **Usage:** Irish; countable; uncountable; Manx grammar

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** grammar; human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** nasalization
