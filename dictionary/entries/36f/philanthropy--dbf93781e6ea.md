### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Benevolent altruism with the intention of increasing the well-being of humankind.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Charitable giving, charity.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** As public funding is reduced, we depend increasingly on private philanthropy.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A philanthropic act.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** His tombstone lists his various philanthropies.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A charitable foundation.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** the Rockefeller philanthropies
