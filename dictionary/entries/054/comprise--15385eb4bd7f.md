### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To be made up of; to consist of (especially a comprehensive list of parts).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The whole comprises the parts.

   **Example:** The parts are comprised by the whole.

   **Example:** Our committee comprises a president, secretary, treasurer and five other members.

   **Source synonyms:** subsume; contain; include; embrace; comprehend

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To compose; to constitute.

   **Usage:** passive; proscribed; sometimes; usually

   **Example:** The whole is comprised of the parts.

   **Example:** The parts comprise the whole.

   **Source synonyms:** make up

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
