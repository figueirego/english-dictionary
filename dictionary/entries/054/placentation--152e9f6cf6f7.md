### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The local fusion of the embryonic stage of an animal to its parent for physiological exchange to promote the growth and development of the young; involves a placenta in non-egglaying mammals.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The manner in which the seeds are attached to the placenta within the ovary locules.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Parietal placentation occurs in the Brassicaceae family.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences
