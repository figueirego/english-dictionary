## Noun

### Forms

- principal parts — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of the forms of a word which contain its stem(s) in the simplest form, or such a form that, when taken with all the other principal parts (showing various inflections), allows the entire paradigm to be derived.

   **Usage:** plural-normally

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** grammar; human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any one of however many parts of speech regarded as the most fundamental or indispensable.

   **Usage:** obsolete; plural-normally

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** grammar; human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any one of however many parts of speech regarded as the most fundamental or indispensable.
   1. The noun, verb, or adverb.

      **Usage:** obsolete; plural-normally; rare

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** grammar; human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

   1. The noun or the verb.

      **Usage:** obsolete; plural-normally

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** grammar; human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

**Source sense R1.S5**
