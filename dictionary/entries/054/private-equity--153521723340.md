## Noun

### Forms

- private equities — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Capital stock in a private company that does not offer stock to the general public.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** That company is closely held; its equity is all private equity.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An investment fund that specializes in buying companies in order to restructure and then sell them with a profit; the industry comprising such firms.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Although private equity is not inherently villainous, the horror stories from past takeovers give the industry a bad name, especially in cases where it assumes the mantle of landlord or hospital owner.
