**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A group of entities previously mentioned.

   **Usage:** nominative; plural; singular; sometimes; third-person; usually; the third-person plural nominative

   **Example:** Fred and Jane? They just arrived.

   **Example:** Dogs may bark if they want to be fed.

   **Example:** Plants wilt if they are not watered.

   **Example:** I have a car and a truck, but they are both broken.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A single person, previously mentioned, whose gender is unknown, irrelevant, or non-binary.
   1. One whose gender is unknown, irrelevant, or both.

      **Usage:** nominative; plural; singular; sometimes; third-person; usually; the third-person singular nominative; occasionally proscribed

      **Example:** Somebody requested a seat at Friday's performance but didn't say if they preferred the balcony or the floor. \[The gender is unknown and irrelevant.\]

      **Example:** I asked my friend if they wanted to come, but they said no. \[The gender is known but irrelevant.\]

      **Example:** One of the boys tripped over, and they hit their head on the door. \[The gender is known but irrelevant; the particular subclass of usage in this example was seldom accepted before the 21st century, but its acceptance is increasing.\]

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A single person, previously mentioned, whose gender is unknown, irrelevant, or non-binary.
   1. One who is nonbinary.

      **Usage:** nominative; plural; singular; sometimes; third-person; usually; the third-person singular nominative; occasionally proscribed

      **Example:** There is no need to panic. Robin should be here soon and they will know what to do.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. People; some people; people in general; someone, excluding the speaker.

   **Usage:** indefinite; nominative; plural; pronoun; singular; sometimes; third-person; usually; vague meaning

   **Example:** They say it’s a good place to live.

   **Example:** They didn’t have computers in the old days.

   **Example:** They built this city on rock and roll.

   **Example:** They don’t make them like they used to.

5. The authorities, the (power) elites, the powers that be, the establishment, the man, the system&#58; government, police, employers, etc.

   **Example:** They'll tax us for the air we breathe next.

   **Example:** They should increase our wages.

   **Example:** Ha, you believe the moon is real? That's just what they want you to think.
