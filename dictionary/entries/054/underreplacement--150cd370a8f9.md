## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The replacement of a substance at a level less than what makes up for whichever specific deficiency or loss is present.

   **Usage:** no-plural; nutrition; nursing; nutrition; nursing

   **Example:** They need to watch out for underreplacement of magnesium because an adverse effect of the medication is impaired magnesium metabolism.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** undersupplementation

   **Source antonyms:** overreplacement; oversupplementation

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The replacement of equipment, supplies, or staffing at a level less than what makes up for baseline attrition, either appropriately (e.g., because of reduced demand) or inappropriately (e.g., because of budget constraints or underinvestment).

   **Usage:** no-plural

   **Example:** The company has pledged that its AI use will not produce any layoffs; one way that headcount reduction may nonetheless occur will be if underreplacement of workers lost to normal attrition is pursued.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

   **Source antonyms:** overreplacement
