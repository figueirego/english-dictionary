### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To ascertain the quantity of a unit of material via calculated comparison with respect to a standard.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** We measured the temperature with a thermometer.

   **Example:** You should measure the angle with a spirit level.

   **Source synonyms:** take; dimension

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To be of (a certain size), to have (a certain measurement).

   **Usage:** stative

   **Example:** The window measured two square feet.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To estimate the unit size of something.

   **Example:** I measure that at 10 centimetres.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To judge, value, or appraise.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To obtain or set apart; to mark in even increments.

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

   **Usage:** rare

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

**Source sense R2.S7**

7. To adjust by a rule or standard.

<a id="r2-s8"></a>

**Source sense R2.S8**

   **Source context (verbatim):** with out or off
