### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A former unit of electric conductance, equivalent to and superseded by the siemens but still sometimes used in some contexts.

   **Example:** If resistance is measured in ohms, conductance is measured in mhos.

   **Source synonyms:** siemens; mul&#58;S
