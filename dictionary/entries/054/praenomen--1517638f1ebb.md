### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A first name or given name; particularly the first of the tria nomina (three nouns) borne by free male Roman citizens.

   **Usage:** Ancient-Rome

   **Example:** Julius Caesar's praenomen was Gaius.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The throne name of a pharaoh, the fourth of the five names of the royal titulary, traditionally encircled by a cartouche and preceded by the title nswt-bjtj.

   **Usage:** historical

   **Example:** Tutankhamun's praenomen or throne name was Nebkheperrure, "Lord of the forms of Ra".

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The genus name in a binomial scientific name.

   **Example:** In the name Tyrannosaurus rex, "Tyrannosaurus" is the praenomen.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences; zoology
