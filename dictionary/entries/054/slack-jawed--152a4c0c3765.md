### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. With the mouth in an open position and the jaw hanging loosely, especially as indicating bewilderment or astonishment.

   **Source synonyms:** dazzled; dumbfounded; open-mouthed; stunned

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Unsophisticated or unthinking; dimwitted in appearance.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Source synonyms:** dopey; stupid; stupefied

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Overly talkative; indiscreet.

   **Usage:** dated

   **Source synonyms:** big-mouthed
