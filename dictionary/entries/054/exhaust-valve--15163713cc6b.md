## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A valve through which the products of combustion escape from the cylinder to the manifold.

   **Source context (verbatim):** in an internal combustion engine

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A valve that allows steam to escape from a cylinder after use.

   **Source context (verbatim):** in a steam engine
