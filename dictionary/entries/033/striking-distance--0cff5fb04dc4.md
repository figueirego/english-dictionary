## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A distance at which a soldier, military force, vessel, etc. is sufficiently near to be able to attack an opponent.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A distance at which one is sufficiently near to an objective to have an opportunity to achieve it.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable
