### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An imitation of something, particularly a well-known product, usually of lower quality and price than the original.

   **Usage:** attributive; often

   **Example:** It's not a name-brand bag; it's just a cheap knockoff.

   **Example:** He tried to sell us some knockoff watches.

   **Source synonyms:** rip-off; fake; phony
