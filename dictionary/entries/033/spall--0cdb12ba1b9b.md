### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To break into fragments or small pieces.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To undergo weathering, aging, or other wear that involves the crumbling of the substrate.

   **Usage:** especially

   **Example:** When the bearing race spalled under high load, it led to the failure of the bearing.

   **Example:** Once the concrete had spalled extensively, the bridge could no longer pass an honest inspection.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of ferroconcrete structures or metal objects
