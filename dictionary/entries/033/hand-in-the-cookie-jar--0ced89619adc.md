### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used to indicate an instance of being caught doing something wrong, dishonest, or unauthorized, especially stealing or abusing a position of trust.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** She had her hand in the cookie jar throughout her term of office.

   **Example:** He was caught with his hand in the cookie jar and eventually found guilty of embezzlement.

   **Source synonyms:** caught raiding the cookie jar; fingers in the cookie jar
