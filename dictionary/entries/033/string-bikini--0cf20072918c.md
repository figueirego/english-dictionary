**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A woman's two-piece swimsuit that is more revealing than a regular bikini, consisting of two triangular pieces connected at the groin but not at the sides, where a thin "string" wraps around the waist, connecting the two parts with a top that is similar and is tied in place by the attached "string" pieces.
