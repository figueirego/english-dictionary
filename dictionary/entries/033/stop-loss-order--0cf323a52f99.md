### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A conditional order placed with a stockbroker (or similar) to close one's position if the market drops (or rises in some cases) to a specified price level.

   **Example:** The shares are currently trading at $4.40 and he has issued a stop loss order to sell if they fall to $4.20.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance; stock-market
