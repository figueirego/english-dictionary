### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A household or commercial appliance used for keeping food fresh using refrigeration.

   **Source synonyms:** fridge; icebox

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A similar device used to keep non-food items cold, such as blood, photographic film, drugs, or pharmaceuticals like insulin.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
