## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of several not closely related black pepper-like spices from West Africa.
   1. Aframomum melegueta (grains of paradise) from the ginger family (Zingiberaceae).

      **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of several not closely related black pepper-like spices from West Africa.
   1. Piper guineense (West African pepper) from the pepper family (Piperaceae).

      **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
