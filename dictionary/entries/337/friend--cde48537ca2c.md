### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person, typically someone other than a family member, spouse or lover, whose company one enjoys and towards whom one feels affection.

   **Example:** John and I have been friends ever since we were roommates at college.

   **Example:** Trust is important between friends.

   **Example:** I used to find it hard to make friends when I was shy.

   **Example:** We became friends in the war and remain friends to this day.

   **Example:** We were friends with some girls from the other school and stayed friends with them.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An associate who provides assistance; patron, mentor.

   **Example:** The Automobile Association is every motorist's friend. The police is every law-abiding citizen's friend.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A person with whom one is vaguely or indirectly acquainted.

   **Example:** a friend of a friend

   **Example:** I added him as a friend on Facebook, but I hardly know him.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A person who backs or supports something.

   **Example:** I’m not a friend of cheap wine.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An object or idea that can be used for good.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** Fruit is your friend.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Used as a form of address when warning someone.

   **Usage:** colloquial; ironic

   **Example:** You’d better watch it, friend.
