### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used as a greeting when meeting someone for the first time in the morning.

   **Example:** The teacher welcomed the kids to school with "Good morning, boys and girls."

   **Source synonyms:** good morrow

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A parting in the morning.

   **Usage:** uncommon; more formal

   **Example:** Thank you for coming everyone and I hope to see you again next year. Good morning.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used to greet someone who has just awakened (irrespective of the time of day).

   **Usage:** broadly; humorous

   **Example:** Up at the crack of dusk! Good morning!

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Said to someone who has come to a belated realization.

   **Usage:** broadly; informal

   **Example:** You're just realizing that now? Good morning!
