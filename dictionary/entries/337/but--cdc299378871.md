### Senses

<a id="r3-s1"></a>

**Source sense R3.S1**

1. However, although, nevertheless, on the other hand (introducing a clause contrary to prior belief or in contrast with the preceding clause or sentence).

   **Example:** She is very old but still attractive.

   **Example:** She’s welcomed them but been rude to us.

   **Example:** You told me I could do that, but she said that I could not.

<a id="r3-s2"></a>

**Source sense R3.S2**

2. On the contrary, rather (as a regular adversative conjunction, introducing a word or clause in contrast or contradiction with the preceding negative clause or sentence).

   **Example:** I am not rich but \[I am\] poor. Not John but Peter went there.

   **Example:** It’s not so much that we don’t wanna go, but I just haven’t time.

<a id="r3-s3"></a>

**Source sense R3.S3**

3. Used at the beginning of a sentence to express opposition to a remark.

   **Usage:** colloquial

   **Example:** But I never said you could do that!

<a id="r3-s4"></a>

**Source sense R3.S4**

4. Except that (introducing a subordinate clause which qualifies a negative statement); also, (archaic) with omission of the subject of the subordinate clause, acting as a negative relative, "except one that", "except such that".

   **Example:** I cannot but feel offended.

   **Example:** My kid does nothing but watch TV all day.

<a id="r3-s5"></a>

**Source sense R3.S5**

5. Used to link an interjection to the following remark as an intensifier.

   **Usage:** colloquial

   **Example:** Wow! But that's amazing!

<a id="r3-s6"></a>

**Source sense R3.S6**

6. Without it also being the case that; unless that (introducing a necessary concomitant).

   **Usage:** archaic

   **Example:** It never rains but it pours

   **Example:** I never hear this song but I think of you.

<a id="r3-s7"></a>

**Source sense R3.S7**

7. Except with; unless with; without.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r3-s8"></a>

**Source sense R3.S8**

8. Only; solely; merely.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r3-s9"></a>

**Source sense R3.S9**

9. Until.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r3-s10"></a>

**Source sense R3.S10**
