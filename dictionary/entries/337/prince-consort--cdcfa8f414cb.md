### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The husband of a queen or an empress, who is not a king or an emperor in his own right, or is accorded this title in a monarchy in which only male monarchs may bear the title "king."

   **Source synonyms:** king consort; emperor consort

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The husband of a princess, who is not a prince in his own right.

   **Usage:** gender equality
