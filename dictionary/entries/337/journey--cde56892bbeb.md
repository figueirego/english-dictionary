**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A set amount of travelling, seen as a single unit; a discrete trip, a voyage.

   **Example:** The journey to London takes two hours by train.

   **Example:** “Don't set out on a journey using someone else's donkey.” ~ African proverb

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any process or progression likened to a journey, especially one that involves difficulties or personal development.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** the journey to political freedom

   **Example:** my journey of dealing with grief
