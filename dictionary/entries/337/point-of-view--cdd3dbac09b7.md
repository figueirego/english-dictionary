## Noun

### Forms

- points of view — plural
- point-of-view — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A position from which something is seen.

   **Example:** From the operator's point of view, there is a large blind spot on one side of the machine.

   **Example:** From an economist's point of view, business is all about money.

   **Source synonyms:** outlook; perspective; POV; standpoint; viewpoint

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An attitude, opinion, or set of beliefs.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** His point of view is that there is only one true religion.

   **Source synonyms:** outlook; perspective; POV; standpoint; viewpoint; slant; bias

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The perspective from which a narrative is related.

   **Usage:** literary theory

   **Example:** The storyline in the film “The Usual Suspects” is presented from the point of view of an unreliable narrator.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** literature; media; publishing

   **Source synonyms:** viewpoint; perspective
