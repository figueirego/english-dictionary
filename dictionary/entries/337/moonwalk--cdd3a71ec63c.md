### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An exploration of the Moon's surface on foot by an astronaut.

   **Usage:** space flight

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** broadly; space flight

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
