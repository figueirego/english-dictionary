### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who multiplies or causes something to increase.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A number by which another (the multiplicand) is to be multiplied.

   **Example:** In the expression 5 × 7, the "5" is a multiplier.

   **Example:** The score multiplier increases each time you complete a level of the video game.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** arithmetic

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An adjective indicating the number of times something is to be multiplied.

   **Example:** English multipliers include "double" and "triple".

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** grammar; human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A ratio used to estimate total economic effect for a variety of economic activities.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** economics; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Any of several devices used to enhance a signal

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Any of several devices used to enhance a signal
   1. a coil; when Johann Schweigger in 1820 invented the electric coil, increasing the electro-magnetic field from a single wire, this invention was called a multiplier.

      **Usage:** obsolete

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics
