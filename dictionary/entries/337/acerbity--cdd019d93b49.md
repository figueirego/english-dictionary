### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Sourness of taste, with bitterness and astringency, like that of unripe fruit.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Harshness, bitterness, or severity.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

   **Example:** acerbity of temper, of language, of pain

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
