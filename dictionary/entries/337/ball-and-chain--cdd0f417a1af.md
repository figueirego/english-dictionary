## Noun

### Forms

- balls and chains — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A heavy iron ball attached to a prisoner's leg by a chain as a means of restraint.

   **Example:** In those days a prisoner here had to carry his ball and chain when he was walking to the chow hall.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One's spouse, especially a wife, as a symbol of restraint.

   **Usage:** derogatory; slang

   **Example:** I'll have to ask the old ball and chain and get back to you.

   **Source synonyms:** other half; better half

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Anything that restricts one's freedom or opportunities.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** His boss turned his conscientiousness into a ball and chain by giving him all the hard jobs.
