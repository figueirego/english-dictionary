## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

- bottom lines — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The final balance; the amount of money or profit left after everything has been tallied.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The summary or result; the most important information.

   **Usage:** countable; idiomatic; uncountable

   **Example:** The bottom line is that there simply are not enough hours in the day to finish all there is to do.

   **Source synonyms:** upshot; net-net

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A minimum acceptable result from a negotiation.

   **Usage:** countable; idiomatic; uncountable

   **Example:** My bottom line for this job is $125,000; any less and I'll walk away.

   **Source synonyms:** BATNA; reservation price
