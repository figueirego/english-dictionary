### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A Hindu temple.

   **Usage:** Hinduism; obsolete

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A tall building in an Indian, Burmese, or Thai style erected at Hindu or Buddhist temples in South and Southeast Asia, a stupa surmounted with a high point.

   **Usage:** Hinduism; uncommon

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Buddhism; lifestyle; religion

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A multi-storey tower in a Chinese style used in various contexts across East Asia and Nepal, usually with upturned curving roofs above each storey.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Alternative form of pagod, an idol, chiefly in South and Southeast Asia.

   **Usage:** alt-of; alternative; archaic

   **Alternative form of:** [pagod](../2d6/pagod--b5a5e1dc26aa.md)

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A unit of currency, a coin made of gold or half gold, issued by various dynasties and colonial powers in southern India.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Short for pagoda sleeve.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; rare

   **Alternative form of:** [pagoda sleeve](../100/pagoda-sleeve--40299e6acea2.md)
