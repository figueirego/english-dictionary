### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A situation where a game remains apparently playable, but further progress is impossible, typically due to a design flaw or glitch.

   **Example:** I defeated the boss, but got hit at the exact same time; now I'm stuck in a softlock and can't exit the level.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** video-games
