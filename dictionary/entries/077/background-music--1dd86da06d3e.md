### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any music played in a public space whose main function is to create an atmosphere suitable to a specific occasion, rather than to be listened to.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any music in a film, video game, or other medium that establishes mood and is not meant to be the focus of attention.

   **Usage:** uncountable
