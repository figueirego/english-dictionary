**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Broken; not properly functioning.

   **Usage:** Commonwealth; slang

   **Example:** Your telly is buggered, best get it fixed.

   **Example:** It's well and truly buggered now; you may as well throw it out.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In trouble; in a bad situation.

   **Usage:** Commonwealth; slang

   **Example:** The police caught you on CCTV, now you're really buggered.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Tired, worn-out, exhausted.

   **Usage:** Commonwealth; Ireland; UK; slang

   **Example:** You'll have to take over from here, mate, I'm completely buggered.
