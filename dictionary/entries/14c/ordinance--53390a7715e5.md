### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A local law, passed by e.g. a city.

   **Usage:** US; countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** bylaw (Commonwealth)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An edict or decree, authoritative order.
   1. Prior to the Third English Civil War, a decree of Parliament.

      **Usage:** England; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An edict or decree, authoritative order.
   1. Detailed legislation that translates the broad principles of the university's charter and statutes into practical effect.

      **Usage:** Commonwealth; UK; countable; uncountable; pre-1992 universities

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An edict or decree, authoritative order.
   1. A law enacted by the Hong Kong Legislative Council.

      **Usage:** Hong-Kong; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An edict or decree, authoritative order.
   1. A temporary legislation promulgated by the president on the recommendation of the cabinet.

      **Usage:** India; Pakistan; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A religious practice or ritual prescribed by a church.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Alternative form of ordnance (“military equipment, especially artillery”).

   **Usage:** alt-of; alternative; countable; proscribed; uncountable

   **Alternative form of:** [ordnance](../315/ordnance--c54c98847679.md)
