<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To praise, glorify (someone or something, especially God).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** exalt; extol; laud; aggrandize; applaud; enthrone (literary); appraise; belaud (rare); bepraise; commend; cry up; eulogize; exalt; extol; glorify; honor; idolize; laud; lionize; magnify; panegyrize; praise; puff; revere; speak well of; transfigure; venerate; worthy

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To make (something) larger or more important.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To make (someone or something) appear greater or more important than it is; to intensify, exaggerate.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To make (something) appear larger by means of a lens, magnifying glass, telescope etc.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To have effect; to be of importance or significance.

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete; slang
