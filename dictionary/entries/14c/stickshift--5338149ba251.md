## Noun

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈstɪkˌʃɪft/ — source labels: General-American

### Forms

- stickshifts — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A manual transmission controlled by a gear lever.

   **Usage:** US

   **Example:** In Europe in the era before modern electric vehicles, most cars had a stickshift.

   **Source synonyms:** manual

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A motor vehicle with a manual transmission controlled by a gear lever.

   **Usage:** US

   **Example:** My old car was a stickshift, but my new one is an automatic.

   **Source synonyms:** manual

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Cars, collectively, with such a transmission.

   **Usage:** US; informal; uncountable

   **Example:** Do you know how to drive stickshift? \[=drive stick\]

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A gear lever; especially, one that rises from the floor.

   **Usage:** US

   **Example:** It is uncomfortable to be a middle passenger in this truck because your legs tend to be in the way of the stickshift.
