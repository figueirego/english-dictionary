### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A Japanese dish made of small portions of sticky white rice flavored with vinegar, usually wrapped in seaweed and filled or topped with fish, vegetables or meat.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** For the vegetarians, she served cucumber sushi.

   **Example:** He went to a Japanese restaurant and ordered sushi and noodles.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One of the portions from this dish.

   **Usage:** countable; rare; usually

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Raw fish, especially as a Japanese dish.

   **Usage:** proscribed; uncountable; usually
