## Noun · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /bɑː(ɹ)ˈbɛə.ɹi.ən/ — source labels: UK
- IPA: /bɑɹˈbɛəɹ.i.ən/ — source labels: US

### Forms

- barbarians — plural

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A non-Greek or a non-Roman citizen.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. An uncivilized or uncultured person, originally compared to the hellenistic Greco-Roman civilisation; usually associated with senseless violence and self-harm or other such shows of brute force and lack of mental faculty.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. A person destitute of culture; a Philistine.

   **Usage:** derogatory

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. Someone from a developing country or backward culture.

   **Usage:** derogatory

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. A brutish warrior depicted in sword and sorcery and other fantasy works; typically clad in primitive furs or leather and usually favoring physical strength over intelligence while often possessing a bellicose temperament and disdain for laws.

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. A cruel, savage, inhumane, brutal, violently aggressive person, particularly one who is unintelligent or dim-witted; one without pity or empathy.

   **Usage:** derogatory

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

**Source sense R2.S7**

7. A foreigner, especially with barbaric qualities as in the above definitions.

   **Usage:** derogatory

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** alien — foreigner; outlander; peregrine
