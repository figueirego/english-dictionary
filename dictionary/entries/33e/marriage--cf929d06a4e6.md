<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The state of being married.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** You should enter marriage for love.

   **Source synonyms:** wedlock

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A union of two or more people that creates a family tie and carries legal, social, or religious rights and responsibilities.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** alliance

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A union of two or more people that creates a family tie and carries legal, social, or religious rights and responsibilities.
   1. The union of only two people, to the exclusion of all others.

      **Usage:** countable; sometimes; specifically; uncountable

      **Example:** My grandparents' marriage lasted for forty years.

      **Example:** Pat and Leslie's marriage to each other lasted forty years.

      **Source synonyms:** alliance

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A union of two or more people that creates a family tie and carries legal, social, or religious rights and responsibilities.
   1. The union of two people of opposite sex, to the exclusion of all others.

      **Source synonyms:** alliance
