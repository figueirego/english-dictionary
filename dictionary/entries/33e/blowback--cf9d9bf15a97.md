### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A type of action where the pressure from the fired cartridge blows a sliding mechanism backward to extract the fired cartridge, chamber another cartridge, and cock the hammer.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; firearms; government; military; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; politics; tools; war; weaponry

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An unintended adverse result, especially of covert political action.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** fallout

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The act of shotgunning (inhaling from a pipe etc. and exhaling into another smoker's mouth).

   **Usage:** countable; slang; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. On a steam locomotive, the reversal of exhaust gases when the regulator is closed without using the blower.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** rail-transport; railways; transport
