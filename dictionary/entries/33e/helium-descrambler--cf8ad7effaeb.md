### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An electronic device that lowers the pitch of speech, to compensate for the high-pitched speech of someone breathing in a helium atmosphere mixture (e.g. heliox or heliair), so as to be understandable to other people.

   **Usage:** saturation diving

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** diving; engineering; hobbies; lifestyle; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sports; technical; underwater-diving
