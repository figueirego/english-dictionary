### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The same but with the two items mentioned reversed.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; generally after and or or

   **Example:** Since there are two contestants left in the race, either Bob will come first place and Alice second place, or vice versa.

   **Example:** The most loved scholar of the university wrote a memorable diary to his students, and vice versa, the students wrote to him.

   **Example:** I’m going to give her a diamond necklace for her birthday, and vice versa, she’s going to get me a Premium Pass for FIFA 24 for mine.
