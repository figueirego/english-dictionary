## Verb

### Forms

- goes for it — present; singular; third-person
- going for it — participle; present

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To put maximum effort into achieving something.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** To win the competition he has to beat his personal best, and he's going for it.

   **Example:** He really went for it.

   **Example:** Go for it! You can win this!

   **Source synonyms:** go all out; give 110%; dig in; fall to

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To decide to do something; especially after a period of hesitation.

   **Example:** His phone was off so I couldn't ask his permission, so I decided to just go for it.

   **Source synonyms:** make a move

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used to grant permission for or to give endorsement of a suggestion or proposal.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; imperative

   **Example:** May I use your bathroom? ― Go for it!

   **Source synonyms:** go ahead
