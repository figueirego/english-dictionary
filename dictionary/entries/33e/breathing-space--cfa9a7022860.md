### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Sufficient space or a span of time in which to recover one's breath.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** breathing room; breathing spell; breathing while

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

**Source sense R1.S3**
