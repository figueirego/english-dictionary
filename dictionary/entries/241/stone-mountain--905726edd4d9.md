## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A mountain in Appalachians, DeKalb County, Georgia, United States, a quartz monzonite dome monadnock former pluton igneous intrusion, containing the world's largest bas relief, of three Confederate leaders on horseback.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Said carving.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A city in DeKalb County, Georgia, United States. Ellipsis of Stone Mountain Village. Named after the mountain.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An unincorporated region of DeKalb and Gwinnett, Georgia, United States, surrounding the mountain.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A mountain in Descartes Highlands, Moon.
