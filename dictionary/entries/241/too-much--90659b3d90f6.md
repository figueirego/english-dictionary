## Adverb · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /tu ˈmʌtʃ/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To a greater extent than is wanted or required; excessively.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** You talk (much/far/rather) too much.

   **Example:** I was too much affected by their criticisms.

   **Example:** Nothing too much out of the ordinary.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To a sufficiently strong degree to prevent some other action from happening.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** I'm not leaving now ... I'm enjoying myself too much!

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. More (of something) than is desirable or required.

   **Example:** You ate too much cake at the party, and that's why you feel sick.

   **Example:** There's far too much attention being paid to it.

   **Example:** She's got too much nose but not enough chin.

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Much; even a moderate amount of.

   **Example:** They call it a beach, but there isn't too much sand there.

   **Source context (verbatim):** in negative constructions
