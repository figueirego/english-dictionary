### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Water or other liquid finely suspended in air. (Compare fog, haze.)

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** It was difficult to see through the morning mist.

   **Source synonyms:** brume

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A layer of fine droplets or particles.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** There was an oily mist on the lens.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Anything that dims, darkens, or hinders vision.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To form mist.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** It's misting this morning.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To spray fine droplets on, particularly of water.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I mist my tropical plants every morning.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To rain in very fine droplets.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To cover with a mist.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The lens was misted.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** My eyes misted when I remembered what had happened.
