## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Effective immediately after a traumatic event such as an external shock.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Anterograde amnesia is a loss of the ability to create new memories after the event that caused the amnesia.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Moving or occurring in the normal forward or downstream direction of.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Movement of mitochondria and other cell parts from a neuron's cell body toward the synapse is called anterograde transport.

   **Example:** Anterograde tracing is a research technique which is used to trace neural connections from their source to their point of termination. The opposite technique is retrograde tracing.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** antegrade
