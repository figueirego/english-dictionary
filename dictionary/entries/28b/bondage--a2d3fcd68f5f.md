### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The state of being enslaved or the practice of slavery.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** debt bondage

   **Example:** In Judeo-Christian tradition, the Israelites fled bondage at the hands of the Egyptians, only to wander in the wilderness for the next four decades.

   **Source antonyms:** freedom

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The state of lacking freedom; constraint.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** He lived in financial bondage to his cocaine habit; no matter how much he earned, it all seemed to disappear up his nose.

   **Source antonyms:** freedom

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The practice of physically restraining people for sexual pleasure, such as by tying up or shackling.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Their marriage broke up when she discovered he had been engaging in bondage games with a local dominatrix while he was supposedly working out at the gym.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** lifestyle; sex; sexuality

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Applied to clothing with many buckles, zips, etc., associated with punk and goth subcultures.

   **Usage:** attributive; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** bondage trousers; bondage jeans; bondage pants
