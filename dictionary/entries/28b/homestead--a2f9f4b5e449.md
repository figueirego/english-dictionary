## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A house together with surrounding land and buildings, especially on a farm; the property comprising these.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A house together with surrounding land and buildings, especially on a farm; the property comprising these.
   1. A parcel of land in the interior of North America, usually 160 acres, that was distributed to settlers from Europe or eastern North America under the Dominion Lands Act of 1870 in Canada or the Homestead Act of 1862 in the United States.

      **Usage:** Canada; US

      **Source synonyms:** farmstead; quarter section

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The place that is one's home.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A cluster of several houses occupied by an extended family.

   **Usage:** South-Africa

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The home or seat of a family; place of origin.

   **Usage:** obsolete
