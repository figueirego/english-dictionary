### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A crustacean of the Nephropidae family, dark green or blue-black in colour turning bright red when cooked, with a hard shell and claws, which is used as a seafood.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Various other crustaceans that resemble true lobsters&#58;
   1. A spiny lobster, also called the rock lobster, a crustacean of the Palinuridae family, pinkish red in colour, with a hard, spiny shell but no claws, which is used as a seafood.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Various other crustaceans that resemble true lobsters&#58;
   1. A slipper lobster (a crustacean of the family Scyllaridae).

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Various other crustaceans that resemble true lobsters&#58;
   1. A squat lobster.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A soldier or officer of the imperial British Army (due to their red or scarlet uniform).

   **Usage:** countable; historical; slang; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. An Australian twenty-dollar note, due to its reddish-orange colour.

   **Usage:** countable; slang; uncountable
