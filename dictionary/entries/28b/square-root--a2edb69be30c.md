## Noun

### Forms

- square roots — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The number which, when squared, yields the original number.

   **Example:** The square roots of 4 are 2 and −2, the square root of 0 is 0, and the square roots of −4 are 2i and −2i.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** sqr; sqrt; mul&#58;√

   **Source antonyms:** square

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The positive number which, when squared, yields another number&#58; the principal square root.

   **Example:** The square root of 4 is 2. No negative real square roots exist. Negative numbers have no real square roots.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences
