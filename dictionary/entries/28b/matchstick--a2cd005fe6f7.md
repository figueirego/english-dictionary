### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A small, slender piece of wood or cardboard serving as a component of a match.

   **Example:** A piece of wood or cardboard becomes a matchstick once the end has been dipped in a sulfurous compound to turn it into a complete match.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any similarly small and thin piece.

   **Usage:** in-plural

   **Example:** The explosion blew the powder shed to matchsticks.

   **Source synonyms:** shred; sliver; splinter
