## Proper noun · Record 2

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. An endangered native Romance linguistic variety spoken in the Benasque Valley in Huesca province in the Aragon region of Spain, transitional between Aragonese, Catalan, and Gascon but usually regarded as a dialect of Aragonese.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. The people of Benasque city or of the Benasque Valley.

   **Usage:** plural; plural-only
