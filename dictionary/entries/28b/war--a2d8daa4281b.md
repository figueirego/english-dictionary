### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Organized, large-scale, armed conflict between countries or between national, ethnic, or other sizeable groups, usually but not always involving active engagement of military forces.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** War is merely the continuation of policy by other means; War is hell

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A particular conflict of this kind.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** a war of succession... a war of attrition... the Cold War... World War III...

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Protracted armed conflict against irregular forces, sometimes those considered terrorists.

   **Usage:** countable; proscribed; sometimes

   **Example:** the Great Emu War... the Global War on Terrorism...

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Protracted state or state-sponsored political violence against dissidents.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; proscribed; sometimes

   **Example:** the Dirty War...

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Any protracted conflict, particularly
   1. A sustained campaign against a social problem, idea, set of values, etc.

      **Usage:** US; broadly; countable; uncountable

      **Example:** the War on Poverty... the War on Drugs... the War on Christmas...

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Any protracted conflict, particularly
   1. A protracted instance of fierce competition in trade.

      **Usage:** broadly; countable; uncountable

      **Example:** price wars... Cola Wars... format wars...

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Any protracted conflict, particularly
   1. A protracted struggle between rival organizations for control of a particular institution.

      **Usage:** broadly; countable; uncountable

      **Example:** FISA–FOCA war...

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Any protracted conflict, particularly
   1. A prolonged conflict between two groups of organized criminals, usually over organizational or territorial control.

      **Usage:** broadly; countable; uncountable; crime

      **Example:** turf war... gang war... Castellammarese War...

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. Any protracted conflict, particularly
   1. An argument between two or more people with opposing opinions on a topic or issue.

      **Usage:** Internet; broadly; countable; uncountable

      **Example:** flame war... edit war...

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. An assembly of weapons; instruments of war.

   **Usage:** obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. Armed forces.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s12"></a>

**Source sense R1.S12**
