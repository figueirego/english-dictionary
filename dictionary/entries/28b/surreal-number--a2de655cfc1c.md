### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any element of a certain field equivalent to the real numbers augmented with infinite and infinitesimal numbers (respectively larger and smaller (in absolute value) than any positive real number).

   **Example:** Conway's construction of surreal numbers relies on the use of transfinite induction.

   **Example:** Conway's approach was to build numbers from scratch using a construction inspired by his game theory research; the resulting class of surreal numbers proved much larger than the class of real numbers.
