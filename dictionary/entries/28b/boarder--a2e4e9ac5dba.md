### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A pupil who lives at school during term time.

   **Example:** The student body consisted primarily of boarders, except for a few children belonging to the school staff.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Someone who pays for meals and lodging in a house rather than a hotel.

   **Example:** When I left for college, my parents took on a boarder in my old room to help defray expenses.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. One who boards a vehicle.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A sailor attacking an enemy ship by boarding her, or one repelling such attempts by an enemy.

   **Example:** The captain shouted at the crew to grab arms and repel boarders.
