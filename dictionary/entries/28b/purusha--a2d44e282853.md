### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In early Vedas, a cosmic man whose sacrifice by the gods created all life.

   **Usage:** Hinduism

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In the Upanishad, an abstract essence of self, spirit, and the Universal Principle that is eternal, without form and all-pervasive, distinguished from the mutable material world.

   **Usage:** Hinduism
