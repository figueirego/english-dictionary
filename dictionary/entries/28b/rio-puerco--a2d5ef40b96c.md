## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A river in New Mexico, United States, a tributary of the Rio Grande. Rather than meaning "pig river", in this case it is translated as "dirty" or "muddy river".

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A river in New Mexico, a tributary of the Rio Chama.
