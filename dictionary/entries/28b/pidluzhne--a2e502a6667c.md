### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A former village in Kapytolivka silrada, Izium Raion, Kharkiv Oblast, Ukrainian Soviet Socialist Republic, annexed to the silrada’s administrative centre, Kapytolivka, somewhen 1969–1971.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A village, the administrative centre of Pidluzhne starostynskyi okruh, Kostopil urban hromada, Rivne Raion, Rivne Oblast, Ukraine, first mentioned in 1577.
   1. A former silrada of Kostopil Raion, Rivne Oblast, Ukraine, established in 1939, merged into Kostopil urban hromada in June 2020.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A village, the administrative centre of Pidluzhne starostynskyi okruh, Kostopil urban hromada, Rivne Raion, Rivne Oblast, Ukraine, first mentioned in 1577.
   1. A starostynskyi okruh of Kostopil urban hromada, Rivne Raion, Rivne Oblast, Ukraine, established in April 2021.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A former village in Hirnyky silrada, Ratne Raion, Volyn Oblast, Ukrainian Soviet Socialist Republic, annexed to the silrada’s administrative centre, Hirnyky, somewhen 1979–1987.
