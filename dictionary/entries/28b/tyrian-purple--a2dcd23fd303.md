## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

- Tyrian purples — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A deep purple dyestuff obtained from the bodies of mollusks of certain species of the family Muricidae, originally certain of those of the eastern Mediterranean, now also other species from elsewhere.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A deep purple colour, tinted with red, like that of the dye.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
