## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of flowing; a continuous moving on or passing by, as of a flowing stream.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A state of ongoing change.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The schedule is in flux at the moment.

   **Example:** Languages, like our bodies, are in a continual flux.

   **Source antonyms:** stasis

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A chemical agent for cleaning metal prior to soldering or welding.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** It is important to use flux when soldering or oxides on the metal will prevent a good bond.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The rate of transfer of energy (or another physical quantity), especially an electric or magnetic field, through a given surface.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** That high a neutron flux would be lethal in seconds.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A disease which causes diarrhea, especially dysentery.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Diarrhea or other fluid discharge from the body.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The state of being liquid through heat; fusion.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
