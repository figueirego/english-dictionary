### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Habitual laziness or sloth.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source synonyms:** accidie; idleness; inapplication; indolency (obsolete); inexertion; noynoying (Philippines); oisivity (obsolete; rare); slothfulness; sluggishness; unservice (obsolete)

   **Source antonyms:** application; diligence; hardworkingness (rare); industriousness; industry

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Lack of pain in a tumour.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; pathology; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A state in which one feels no pain or is indifferent to it; a lack of any feeling.

   **Usage:** obsolete; uncountable; usually

   **Source synonyms:** unsensibleness (obsolete); indolency (obsolete)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A state of repose in which neither pain nor pleasure is experienced.

   **Usage:** obsolete; uncountable; usually

   **Source synonyms:** indolency (obsolete)
