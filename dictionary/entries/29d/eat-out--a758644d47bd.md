**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To dine at a restaurant or such public place.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Jacky wanted Korean barbecue, but his mom didn't want to eat out.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To erode or encroach upon by gradually consuming.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To perform cunnilingus or anilingus.

   **Usage:** slang; transitive; vulgar

   **Example:** The lesbian couple ate each other out.

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To reprimand severely.

   **Usage:** slang; transitive

   **Example:** He will eat me out if he hears I ate his girlfriend out.
