### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A white person, typically young and male, considered to be overly infatuated with African-American or (UK) Afro-Caribbean culture, a cultural appropriator.

   **Usage:** derogatory; ethnic; slur

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. A nonblack person of any race considered to be inauthentically appropriating black culture and behavior.

   **Usage:** broadly; imprecise

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. A white person considered to be sincerely appreciative of black culture, as opposed to poseurs and wannabes.

   **Usage:** dated; uncommon; African-American Vernacular
