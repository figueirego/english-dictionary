**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A tradition within the Anglican church more heavily influenced by Protestant and Reformed belief and practice, characterised by an informal style of worship, a less elevated view of the priesthood and sacraments, and a focus on evangelical principles.

   **Usage:** uncountable; Anglicanism
