### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A specification of an object or process, referring to requirements to be satisfied and thus conditions to be met for them to solve a problem.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A plan (with more or less detail) for the structure and functions of an artifact, building or system.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The initial design of the park was rejected for being too expensive.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A pattern, as an element of a work of art or architecture.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** We're working on some new designs for our range of summer shirts

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The composition of a work of art.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Intention or plot.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** We have designs on winning the league next season.

   **Example:** To be hateful of the truth by design.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The shape or appearance given to an object, especially one that is intended to make it more attractive.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The art of designing

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Danish furniture design is world-famous.
