### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Pruriently detailed and sensationalistic about something shocking or horrifying, especially with regard to violence or sex.

   **Example:** The accident was described with lurid detail.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Melodramatic.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Ghastly, pale, wan in appearance.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Being of a light yellow hue.

   **Example:** The lurid lighting of the bar made for a very hazy atmosphere.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Having a brown colour tinged with red.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences
