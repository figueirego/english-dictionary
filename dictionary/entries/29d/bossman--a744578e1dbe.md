   **Usage:** derogatory; informal; mildly; often

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. (MLE, informal)
   1. Used as a term of address for an unfamiliar, possibly distrusted man&#58; bud, buddy, friend.

      **Usage:** informal; vocative
