### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A relationship that considers two elements equivalent if one can be obtained from the other by a sequence of transformations of the form yzx -&gt; yxz whenever x &lt; y ≤ z or xzy -&gt; zxy whenever x ≤ y &lt; z.

   **Usage:** uncountable
