### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To become organized, current, or aware; to adopt the prevailing viewpoint.

   **Usage:** US; idiomatic; informal

   **Example:** When the student was behind on his vocabulary, the teacher said, "get with the program!"

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To work productively toward the objective of a shared enterprise, especially after the objective or the environment has changed.

   **Usage:** US; idiomatic; informal

   **Example:** Though natural gas prices dropped, the folks doing solar didn't get with the program.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To comply with the norms of a social group, especially a shared enterprise; to comply with the accepted or prevailing way of doing things.

   **Usage:** US; idiomatic; informal

   **Example:** Dude, get with the program! We don't do casual Fridays any more.
