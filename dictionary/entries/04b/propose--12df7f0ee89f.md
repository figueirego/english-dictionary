### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To suggest a plan, course of action, etc.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I propose going to see a film.

   **Example:** to propose an alliance

   **Example:** to propose a question for discussion

   **Source synonyms:** put forth; suggest; project (rare); forthput

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To ask for a person's hand in marriage.

   **Usage:** intransitive; sometimes; with-to

   **Example:** He proposed to her last night and she accepted him.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To intend.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He proposes to set up his own business.
