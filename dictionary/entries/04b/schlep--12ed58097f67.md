### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To carry, drag, or lug.

   **Usage:** Jewish; informal; slang; transitive

   **Example:** I'm exhausted after schlepping those packages around all day.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To go, as on an errand; to carry out a task.

   **Usage:** Jewish; informal; intransitive; slang

   **Example:** I schlepped down to the store for some milk.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To act in a slovenly, lazy, or sloppy manner.

   **Usage:** Jewish; informal; intransitive; slang

   **Example:** I just schlepped around the house on Sunday.

   **Source synonyms:** schlump

## Noun · Record 2

### Pronunciation

   **Usage:** Jewish; New-York-City; informal; slang

   **Usage:** Jewish; New-York-City; informal; slang

   **Usage:** Jewish; New-York-City; informal; slang
