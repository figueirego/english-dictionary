## Adverb

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Fundamentally; in essence; in reality; as part of an honest appraisal.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; not-comparable

   **Example:** He seems like an average businessman, but deep down he's an overgrown kid with a necktie.

   **Example:** She said she was confident, but deep down she knew she had messed up.
