### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Any of a wide variety of articles, usually made of fabrics, animal hair, animal skin, or some combination thereof, used to cover the human body for warmth, to preserve modesty, or for fashion.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Marco runs a clothing brand.

   **Example:** You should wear warm clothing when it is cold outside.

   **Example:** All but two pieces of clothing came out of the washing machine stained.

   **Example:** A poncho is an item of clothing used by many people in Latin America.

   **Example:** A shirt is an article of clothing that covers the torso and arms.

   **Source synonyms:** attire; apparel; clothes; duds; garments; habiliment; item of clothing; kit (UK); piece of clothing; raiment; threads; wardrobe

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. An act or instance of putting clothes on.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The clothing and unclothing of the idols was of special significance.

   **Source synonyms:** dressing

   **Source antonyms:** undressing

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. The art or process of making cloth.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**
