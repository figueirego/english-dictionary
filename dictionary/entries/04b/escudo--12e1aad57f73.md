### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The state currency formerly used in Portugal and its colonies, divided into 100 centavos. The symbol $ is positioned between the escudos and centavos, i.e. 2$50.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The currency formerly used in Chile and replaced by the Peso.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The currency of Cape Verde.
