## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A sociopolitical and economic movement of the 19th century that originated in Manchester, England, and argued that free trade would lead to a more equitable society, making essential products available to all.

   **Usage:** historical; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** Manchesterdom

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A form of municipal socialism particularly associated with Greater Manchester under mayor Andy Burnham, focused on devolving public services to local public control, and attracting investment through partnerships.

   **Usage:** UK; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; politics
