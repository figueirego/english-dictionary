## Noun

### Forms

- terms of address — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A word or phrase used to address or refer to someone or something without using his, her, their or its name (for example, His Excellency or Your Honor).

   **Source synonyms:** form of address; style

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A title used before someone's name when addressing them or referring to them (for example, Mister Jones or Lord Mountbatten).
