### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Chiefly preceded by the.
   1. Idleness or laziness regarded as a medical condition; also, depression, especially resulting from a hangover.

      **Usage:** Ireland; archaic; countable; humorous; slang; uncountable; chiefly England (Cornwall, Northern England)

      **Source synonyms:** fever lurden; fever-lurgy; lurgy-fever

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Chiefly preceded by the.
   1. A non-specific (often infectious) disease, especially one with symptoms similar to those of a cold or flu, which renders one unfit to attend social events, go to work, etc.

      **Usage:** Australia; Ireland; New-Zealand; UK; broadly; countable; humorous; possibly; slang; uncountable

      **Example:** the dreaded lurgy
