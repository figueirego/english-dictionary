### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Especially of a liquid, to become solid due to low temperature.

   **Usage:** copulative; intransitive

   **Example:** The lake froze solid.

   **Source synonyms:** solidify

   **Source antonyms:** defrost; liquify; unfreeze

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To lower something's temperature to the point that it freezes or becomes hard.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Don't freeze meat twice.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To drop to a temperature below zero degrees celsius, where water turns to ice.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** It didn't freeze this winter, but last winter was very harsh.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To be affected by extreme cold.

   **Usage:** informal; intransitive

   **Example:** It's freezing in here!

   **Example:** Don't go outside wearing just a t-shirt; you'll freeze!

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Of a machine or system, to come to a sudden halt, to stop working (functioning).

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** Since the last update, the program freezes after a few minutes of use.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences; software

   **Source synonyms:** freeze up; grind to a halt; hang; lock up; seize; seize up

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Of a person or other animal, to stop (become motionless) or be stopped due to attentiveness, fear, surprise, etc.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** Despite all of the rehearsals, I froze as soon as I got on stage.

   **Source synonyms:** freeze up

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To cause someone to become motionless.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** halt; immobilize

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To lose or cause to lose warmth of feeling; to shut out; to ostracize.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** Over time, he froze towards her, and ceased to react to her friendly advances.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To cause loss of animation or life in, from lack of heat; to give the sensation of cold to; to chill.

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. To prevent the movement or liquidation of a person's financial assets

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The court froze the criminal's bank account.

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. Of prices, spending etc., to keep at the same level, without any increase.

<a id="r1-s12"></a>

**Source sense R1.S12**

12. To prevent from showing any visible change.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** Pressing the pause button will freeze the video playback.

   **Example:** Playback will freeze if you pause the video.

<a id="r1-s13"></a>

**Source sense R1.S13**

13. To trap (the puck) so that it cannot be played.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; ice-hockey; lifestyle; skating; sports
