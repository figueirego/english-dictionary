**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In the neo-Gricean semantics and pragmatics of Laurence R. Horn&#58; a reformulation of Paul Grice's maxim of relation combined with the second submaxim of quantity and the third and fourth submaxims of manner, stating&#58; "Say no more than you must (given Q)", and implying that there is no reason to make a stronger statement if the extra information can be contributed by implicature.
