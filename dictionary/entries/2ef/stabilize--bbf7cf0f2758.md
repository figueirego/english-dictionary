### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make stable.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Jody stabilized the table by putting a book under the short leg.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To become stable.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The country will stabilize after the next election ends.
