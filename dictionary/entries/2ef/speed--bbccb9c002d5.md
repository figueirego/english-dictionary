### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The state of moving quickly or the capacity for rapid motion.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** How does Usain Bolt run at that speed?

   **Source synonyms:** celerity; rapidity; velocity

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The rate of motion or action, specifically the magnitude of the velocity; the rate distance is traversed in a given time.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Speed limits provide information to the drivers about the safe speed to travel in average conditions.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics; sciences

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** arts; hobbies; lifestyle; photography

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The duration of exposure, the time during which a camera shutter is open (shutter speed).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** arts; hobbies; lifestyle; photography
