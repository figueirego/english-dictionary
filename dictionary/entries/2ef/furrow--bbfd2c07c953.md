### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any trench, channel, or groove; often found on wood or metal.
   1. A trench cut in the soil, as when plowed in order to plant a crop.

      **Example:** Don't walk across that deep furrow in the field.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any trench, channel, or groove; often found on wood or metal.
   1. A deep wrinkle in the skin of the face, especially on the forehead.

      **Example:** When she was tired, a deep furrow appeared on her forehead.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Cart wheels can furrow roads.

**Source sense R2.S2**

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** As she pored over the company's bewildering tax documents, she furrowed her brows, wrinkled her nose, and began to frown her befuddlement.
