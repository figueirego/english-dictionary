### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A golden-haired young girl, featuring as the protagonist of a traditional fairy tale, who inquisitively enters the house of three bears and tries their three bowls of porridge, three chairs, and three beds, in each case finding only one that suits her.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A happy medium or optimal point between two extremes.

   **Usage:** attributive; figuratively
