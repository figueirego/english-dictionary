### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To cease paying attention.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** As soon as I turned my back, he started writing on the wall.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To forsake, to abandon; to ignore.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

   **Example:** He got off to a strong start, only to turn his back on the project two months later.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To turn away, as when fleeing or in contempt.

   **Usage:** intransitive
