### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The British state during part or all of the Interregnum.
   1. The state during 1649–1653, before the Protectorate in 1653–1659.

      **Usage:** historical; precisely

      **Source synonyms:** Commonwealth

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The British state during part or all of the Interregnum.
   1. The state at any time during the Interregnum (1649–1660), including the Protectorate.

      **Usage:** broadly; historical

      **Source synonyms:** Commonwealth
