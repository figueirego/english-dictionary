### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To decline to participate; particularly, to decline to dance.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** I don't like this tune, and I'm rather tired, so I think I'll just sit out.

   **Example:** I don't like this tune, and I'm rather tired, so I think I'll just sit it out.

   **Source synonyms:** opt out

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To await the cessation of, without taking part.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To be away from the table in the midst of an ongoing game, thus automatically placing one's forced bets and otherwise folding every hand when it is one's time to act.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** card-games; poker

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To lean out to the windward side of a sailboat in order to counterbalance the effects of the wind on the sails.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

   **Source synonyms:** hike; lean out

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To escape a hold while face down by swinging one's legs around into the sitting position.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; hobbies; lifestyle; martial-arts; military; politics; sports; war; wrestling
