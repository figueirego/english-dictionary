### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Fragments or splintered pieces; numerous tiny disconnected items.

   **Usage:** informal; plural; plural-only

   **Example:** The urn shattered into smithereens the moment it hit the ground.

   **Example:** When the waiter dropped the platter, one smithereen struck him above the knee, and the four remaining smithereens scattered across the floor.

   **Source synonyms:** shards; shivereens; smithers
