### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of various common types of small insects or other animals which cause harm and annoyance.

   **Usage:** plural; singular

   **Example:** The area was crawling with all sorts of vermin&#58; fleas, lice, mice, and rats to name a few. Thus, we felt as if vermin were crawling over us.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An animal that preys on game, such as a fox or a weasel.

   **Usage:** plural; singular

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An obnoxious or mean and offensive person.

   **Usage:** plural; singular

   **Example:** Bring these vermin to the Palace of Justice.
