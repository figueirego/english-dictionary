### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make complex; to modify so as to make something intricate or difficult.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To involve in a convoluted matter.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Don't complicate yourself in issues that are beyond the scope of your understanding.

   **Example:** John has been complicated in the affair by new tapes that surfaced.

   **Example:** The DA has made every effort to complicate me in the scandal.

   **Source synonyms:** intricate; entangle; embroil; [involve](../21c/involve--873f8161e2c6.md); mix up; mire

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To coexist with (another disease) creating a complication.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences
