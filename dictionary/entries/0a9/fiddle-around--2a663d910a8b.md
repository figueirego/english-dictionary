### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To toy or play (with something) in an idle, fidgety, aimless or unstructured way; to mess around (with); to muck around (with).

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** Stop fiddling around with those matches before you set the place on fire.

   **Source context (verbatim):** with "with"

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To tinker (with)&#58; to make small adjustments or improvements (to something), especially in an experimental or unskilled manner.

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** I've been fiddling around all morning, trying to get this old lawnmower to work.
