### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A funeral pile; a combustible heap on which corpses are burned.

   **Example:** The villagers built a funeral pyre to honor their dead.

   **Example:** The effigy was set ablaze on the pyre.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any heap or pile of combustibles.

   **Example:** Flames rose high from the pyre in the night sky.
