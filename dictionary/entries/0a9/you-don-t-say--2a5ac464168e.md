### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Really?; no kidding!; is that so? (sometimes used sarcastically in response to the obvious)

   **Example:** They're going to have a baby? You don't say!

   **Example:** Hey, guess what? There's a country called Brazil! –You don't say.
