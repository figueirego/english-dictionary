## Adjective · Record 1

### Forms

- more underweight — comparative
- most underweight — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of an inappropriately or unusually low weight.

   **Example:** He's so underweight he's had to buy smaller clothes.

   **Example:** He's thirty pounds underweight.

   **Example:** The market trader was fined for selling underweight bags of fruit.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Not too heavy for an intended purpose.

   **Example:** The suitcase is just slightly underweight; I'll let it on the plane.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Being less invested in a particular area than market wisdom suggests.

   **Example:** The fund is underweight in mining.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance

### Related words

**Source antonyms:** overweight — antonym(s) of “of low weight”; overweight — antonym(s) of “not too heavy”
