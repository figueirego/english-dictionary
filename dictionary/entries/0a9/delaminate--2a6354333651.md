### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To cause (something assembled by lamination) to come apart into the layers that make it up.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To come apart into its component layers.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Water damage caused the plywood of the floor to delaminate. The layers came apart and the whole piece had to be replaced.
