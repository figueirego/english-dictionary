### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A sketch done on the ground of a work of art before paint, pastel, ink, or other more permanent medium is applied.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** art; arts

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An image of a figure that is slid under the page on which a designer draws a garment, so that the result conforms to the figure's proportions.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** arts; design; fashion; lifestyle
