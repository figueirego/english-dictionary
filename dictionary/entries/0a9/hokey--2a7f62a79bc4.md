### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Phony, as if a hoax; noticeably contrived; of obviously flimsy credibility or quality.

   **Usage:** US; colloquial

   **Example:** When asked for his book report, Chad came up with a series of hokier and hokier excuses.

   **Example:** I thought the windshield wiper blades were a little hokey when I saw their cheap packaging.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Corny; overly or unbelievably sentimental.

   **Usage:** US; colloquial

   **Example:** Terry hated going to the cinema with Pat; she always chose hokey romantic comedies that made him want to gag.

   **Source synonyms:** cheesy; kitschy; schmaltzy; bathetic; cheesy; squishy; corny; drippy; gooey (figuratively); hokey (US; informal); icky; kitsch; kitschy; maudlin; mawkish; mushy; overemotional; oversentimental; overwrought; sappy (US; figuratively); schmaltzy; sickly sweet; slushy; soppy (UK; figuratively); soupy; syrupy (figuratively); treacly (figuratively)
