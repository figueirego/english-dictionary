## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of several not closely related plants around the world, generally with round leaves of about the shape and size of a penny.
   1. Any member of the species Umbilicus rupestris of succulent, perennial flowering plants (penny-pies, wall pennywort, kidney wort, navelwort) in the stonecrop family (Crassulaceae).

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source context (verbatim):** in Europe

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of several not closely related plants around the world, generally with round leaves of about the shape and size of a penny.
   1. An edible Asiatic pennywort (Centella asiatica)

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source context (verbatim):** in Asia

      **Source synonyms:** Asiatic pennywort; gotu kola; Indian pennywort; spade leaf

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of several not closely related plants around the world, generally with round leaves of about the shape and size of a penny.
   1. Any aquatic or semi-aquatic plant in the genus Hydrocotyle (water pennywort, Indian pennywort, marsh penny, thick-leaved pennywort, and white rot).

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any of several not closely related plants around the world, generally with round leaves of about the shape and size of a penny.
   1. Any aquatic or semi-aquatic plant in the genus Hydrocotyle (water pennywort, Indian pennywort, marsh penny, thick-leaved pennywort, and white rot).
      1. such as edible dollarweed (Hydrocotyle umbellata).

         **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Any of several not closely related plants around the world, generally with round leaves of about the shape and size of a penny.
   1. Anemone hepatica.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source synonyms:** liverwort; more there

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Any of several not closely related plants around the world, generally with round leaves of about the shape and size of a penny.
   1. Virginia pennywort (Obolaria virginica)

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Any of several not closely related plants around the world, generally with round leaves of about the shape and size of a penny.
   1. Ivy-leaved toadflax (Cymbalaria muralis)

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Any of several not closely related plants around the world, generally with round leaves of about the shape and size of a penny.
   1. Any member of the species Cymbalaria aequitriloba, a plant with tiny ivy-like leaves and copious purple-pink flowers with yellow throats.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable
