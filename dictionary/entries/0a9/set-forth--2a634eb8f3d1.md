### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To state; describe; give an account of.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Where any judge falls under any of the challengeable grounds set forth in section 13, the judge may state the grounds to the Court and remove himself from the case concerned.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To present for consideration; to propose.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To begin a journey or expedition.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Columbus set forth with three small ships.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To start.

   **Usage:** intransitive
