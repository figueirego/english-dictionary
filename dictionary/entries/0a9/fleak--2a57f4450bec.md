## Noun · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Synonym of flake.
   1. A small, light piece that is only loosely joined to something else, and which has a tendency to detach.

      **Usage:** dialectal; obsolete except UK

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Synonym of flake.
   1. A thin piece that is chipped or peeled off from the surface of something else.

      **Usage:** dialectal; obsolete except UK

      **Source synonyms:** chip; splinter; bit; chip; chunk; tranche; clip; crumb; crumbling; cutting; flake; fleak (dialectal; obsolete); fragment; hunk; lump; mammock (obsolete); ort; piece; piecemeal; portion; scrap; scraplet; slice; snead; snippet; steck (dialectal; obsolete)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Synonym of flake.
   1. A thin piece that the flesh of some animals (such as fish) tends to break into.

      **Usage:** dialectal; obsolete except UK
