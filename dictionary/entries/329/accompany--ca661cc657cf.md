### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To go with or attend as a companion or associate; to keep company with; to go along with.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Geoffrey accompanied the group on their pilgrimage.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To supplement with; add to.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To perform an accompanying part or parts in a composition.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To perform an accompanying part next to (another instrument or musician).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The strings were accompanied by two woodwinds.

   **Example:** I will accompany her on the oboe.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To associate in a company; to keep company.

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To cohabit (with).

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To cohabit with; to coexist with; occur with.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To be found at the same time.

   **Example:** Thunder almost always accompanies lightning during a rain storm.
