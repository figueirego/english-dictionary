### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To come physically close to, using body language in an attempt to persuade another (often hesitant) party to snuggle or embrace.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He forgot the whole argument when she cosied up to him on the couch.

   **Example:** The kitten cosied up to the gruff old hound, and all resistance disappeared.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To form a relationship with some one or some thing for the purpose of obtaining some benefit.

   **Usage:** broadly; transitive

   **Example:** It is often suggested that politicians are too inclined to cosy up to big business in order to receive funds for election expenses.

   **Example:** The sales guys might be able to cosy up to the customers, but they are inexperienced in technical support.
