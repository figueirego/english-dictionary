   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** We batted a few ideas around.

**Source sense R3.S2**

   **Usage:** intransitive

3. To strike or swipe as though with a bat.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The cat batted at the toy.

**Source sense R3.S4**
