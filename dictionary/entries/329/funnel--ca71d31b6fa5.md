### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To use a funnel.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To proceed through a narrow gap or passageway akin to a funnel; to condense or narrow.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Expect delays where the traffic funnels down to one lane.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To channel, direct, or focus (emotions, money, resources, etc.).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Our taxes are being funnelled into pointless government initiatives.
