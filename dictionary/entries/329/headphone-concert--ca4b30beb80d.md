### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A live music concert or performance where the audience, in the same venue as the performing artist, listens to the live music or sounds by means of headphones.

   **Example:** The Flaming Lips put on the first headphone concert on March 20 1999 at the South By Southwest festival in Dallas.
