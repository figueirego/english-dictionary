### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In a remarkable manner.

   **Example:** He performed the piece remarkably, offering novel interpretations to its nearly cliched passages.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** manner

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To a noteworthy extent.

   **Usage:** degree

   **Example:** That dog is remarkably fierce.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used to draw special attention to a proposition.

   **Usage:** evaluative

   **Example:** Remarkably, three State assembly elections were decided by a total of fewer than one hundred votes.
