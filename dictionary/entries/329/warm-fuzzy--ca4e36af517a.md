## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A good impression; a feeling of comfort, happiness or trust.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** I suppose they are a reputable business, but I didn't get a warm fuzzy from their salesman.

   **Source antonyms:** cold prickly

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A sense of accomplishment after performing an act.

   **Usage:** derogatory; in-plural; informal; often

   **Example:** John picks up litter in our neighborhood because it gives him warm fuzzies.
