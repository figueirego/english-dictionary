### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The number (either a fraction or an integer) resulting from the division of one number by another.

   **Example:** Dividing 12 by 4 yields a quotient of 3.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** arithmetic

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. By analogy, the result of any process that is the inverse of multiplication as defined for any mathematical entities other than numbers.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A quotum or quota.

   **Usage:** obsolete; rare

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The number of runs scored per wicket lost when batting, divided by the number of runs conceded per wicket taken when bowling — used as a league table tiebreaker in some first-class tournaments.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; cricket; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A mathematical structure formed by identifying elements within a larger one.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** group-theory; mathematics; sciences
