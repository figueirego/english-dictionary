### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of, or relating to mathematics.

   **Example:** a mathematical problem

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Extremely precise and accurate, as though having the exactness of a mathematical equation.

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Possible but highly improbable.

   **Example:** The team has a mathematical chance of being promoted, but they need to win the next seven matches.
