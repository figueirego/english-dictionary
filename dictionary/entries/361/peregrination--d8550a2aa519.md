### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person's life regarded as a temporary stay on earth and a journey to the afterlife.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** lifestyle; religion; theology

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A journey made by a pilgrim; a pilgrimage; also (uncountable) the making of pilgrimages.

   **Usage:** archaic; broadly; countable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A journey or trip, especially by foot; also (uncountable) journeying, travelling.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Broad or systematic discussion of a subject; (countable) an instance of this; a discourse.

   **Usage:** broadly; figuratively; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** perambulation

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Straying from the main subject in speech or writing; digression; (countable) an instance of this.

   **Usage:** broadly; figuratively; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** perambulation

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The state of living abroad temporarily; sojourning; (countable) an act of doing this; a sojourn.

   **Usage:** broadly; obsolete; uncountable
