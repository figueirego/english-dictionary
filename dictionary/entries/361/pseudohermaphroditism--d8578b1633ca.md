### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The condition of having internal reproductive organs / gonads that are typical of one sex, but external genitalia that is typical of the other, or that is ambiguous, which occurs for example in some people with androgen insensitivity syndrome.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
