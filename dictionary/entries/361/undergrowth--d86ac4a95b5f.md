### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The plants in a forest which only reach a relatively low height (such as shrubs and bushes).

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** tangled undergrowth

   **Example:** clear the undergrowth

   **Example:** The hikers pushed through thick undergrowth.

   **Example:** The forest floor was covered with dense undergrowth.
