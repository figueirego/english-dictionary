### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The quality of being contingent, of happening by chance.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** possibility; unpredictability; option; choice; alternative; possibility; contingency

   **Source antonyms:** inevitability; impossibility

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A possibility; something which may or may not happen. A chance occurrence, especially in finance, unexpected expenses.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source synonyms:** chance; contingency; contingent; eventuality; possibility; potentiality

   **Source antonyms:** castle in the air (idiomatic); impossibility; pie in the sky (idiomatic); pipe dream (idiomatic)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An amount of money which a party to a contract has to pay to the other party (usually the supplier of a major project to the client) if they do not fulfill the contract according to the specification.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A statement which is neither a tautology nor a contradiction.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; logic; mathematics; philosophy; sciences
