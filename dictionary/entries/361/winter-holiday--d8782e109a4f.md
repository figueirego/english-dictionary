## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A multiday time of rest from work or studies during the winter months.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** This year we'll spend our winter holiday in Spain.

   **Source synonyms:** holidays

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see winter, holiday&#58; a holiday (holy day or comparable) that falls during the winter; especially, one that celebrates winter or, most specifically, the winter solstice.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Most religions have at least one winter holiday.

   **Source synonyms:** winter festival
