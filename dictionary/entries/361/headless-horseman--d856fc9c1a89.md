## Proper noun · Record 1

### Forms

- the Headless Horseman — canonical

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A mythical figure who has appeared in folklore around the world since the Middle Ages, who is traditionally depicted as a man upon horseback who is missing his head.

## Noun · Record 2

### Forms

- Headless Horsemen — plural
- headless horseman — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A mythical man (perhaps of several) upon horseback who is missing his head, inspired by the Headless Horseman mythical figure.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. A corpse of a man that is missing the head due to decapitation.
