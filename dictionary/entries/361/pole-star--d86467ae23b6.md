### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The star visible to the naked eye which was in the past, is now, or will be in the future nearest a celestial pole of a planet.

   **Example:** Polaris is currently the pole star of planet Earth.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astronomy; natural-sciences

   **Source synonyms:** polar star; pole arctic (obsolete)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person, principle, or thing which acts as a guide; a guiding light; also, something which attracts much attention; a centre of attention.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Source synonyms:** cynosure; guide star; guiding star; lodestar
