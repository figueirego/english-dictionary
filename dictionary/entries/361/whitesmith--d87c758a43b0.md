### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who forges things out of tin or pewter; a tinsmith.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A worker in iron who finishes or polishes the work, in distinction from one who forges it.
