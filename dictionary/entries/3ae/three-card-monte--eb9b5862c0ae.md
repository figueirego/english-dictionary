### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A confidence game in which the victim, or mark, is tricked into betting a sum of money that they can find the money card, for example the queen of hearts, among three face-down playing cards.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source synonyms:** find the lady; follow the bee; follow the lady; menage-a-card; three-card marney; three-card trick; three-way; three-card shuffle; triplets
