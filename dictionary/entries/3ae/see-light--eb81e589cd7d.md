**Source sense R1.S1**

1. (figuratively, in a financial context) To begin to discern a prospect of covering one’s expenses.

   **Extractor diagnostics (not usage labels):** no-gloss

**Supplemental definition (evidence reviewed; source gap preserved):** The complete original1978 fund-raising letter explains production costs exceeding the record company’s payment, the need for enough album sales to break even, and little prospect of remuneration. Within that immediate sequence the author uses seeing light of the musicians’ financial prospects. The narrow financial reading is a contextual interpretation of that verified primary use; it does not claim that the expression universally means to earn a profit, that the venture has already broken even, or that this is the only idiomatic sense.
