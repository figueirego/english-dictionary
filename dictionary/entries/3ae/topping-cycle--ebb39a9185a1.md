### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The high-temperature power cycle of a combined cycle power plant.

   **Example:** The very-high-temperature helium turbine, driven directly by the superheated coolant from the fission reactor, serves as the topping cycle for a mercury-vapor turbine powered by the heat given up by the helium; the mercury turbine itself serves as the topping cycle for the steam turbine that takes up most of the space of the turbine hall.
