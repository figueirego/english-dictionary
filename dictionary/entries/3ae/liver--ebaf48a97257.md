### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A large organ in the body that stores and metabolizes nutrients, destroys toxins and produces bile. It is responsible for thousands of biochemical reactions.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Steve Jobs is a famous liver transplant recipient.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. This organ, as taken from animals used as food.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** I'd like some goose liver pate.

   **Example:** You could fry up some chicken livers for a tasty treat. — Nah, I don't like chicken liver.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A dark brown colour, tinted with red and gray, like the colour of liver.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any of various chemical compounds—particularly sulfides—thought to resemble livers in color.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

   **Example:** He gave his horse some liver of antimony.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences
