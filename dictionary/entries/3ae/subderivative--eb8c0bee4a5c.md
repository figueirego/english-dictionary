### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A word derived indirectly, from a derivative of another.

   **Example:** "Friendliness" is a subderivative&#58; it is derived from "friendly", which comes from "friend".

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A generalization of the notion of a derivative, defined as the slope of a line that either touches, or is everywhere below the line of a convex function.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences
