### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. When giving someone a present or doing a favour for someone, the kindness behind the act is more important than any other consideration.

   **Example:** I know the gift he gave you for your wedding is not worth much, but I hope you'll understand it's the thought that counts.
