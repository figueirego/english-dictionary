### Forms

- fragilest — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Easily broken, not sturdy; of delicate material.

   **Example:** She caught the fragile vase before it could shatter on the floor.

   **Example:** The chemist synthesizes a fragile molecule.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Readily disrupted or destroyed.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** The UN tries to maintain the fragile peace process in the region.

   **Source synonyms:** uneasy

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Feeling weak or easily disturbed as a result of illness.

   **Usage:** UK

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Thin-skinned or oversensitive.

   **Usage:** UK

   **Example:** He is a very fragile person and gets easily depressed.

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** breakable; delicate; destructible; flimsy; tender; fragile; frail; frailsome; frangible; slim (Scotland); tenuous

**Source antonyms:** antifragile; durable; indestructible; unbreakable; unfragile
