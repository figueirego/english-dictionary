## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈkæləmni/ — source labels: General-American; Received-Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A false accusation or charge brought to tarnish another's reputation or standing.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Falsifications or misrepresentations intended to disparage or discredit another.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Accusations of abuse were pure extortive calumny in a malicious bid to make money.
