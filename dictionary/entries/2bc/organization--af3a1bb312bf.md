### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The quality of being organized.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** This painting shows little organization at first glance, but little by little the structure becomes clear.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The way in which something is organized, such as a book or an article.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** The organization of the book is as follows.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A group of people or other legal entities with an explicit purpose and written rules.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** In response to the crisis, the nations in the region formed an organization. If you want to be part of this organization, you have to follow its rules.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A group of people consciously cooperating.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** Over time, the spontaneous movement had become an organization.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A major league club and all its farm teams.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** He's been in the Dodgers' organization since 2003.
