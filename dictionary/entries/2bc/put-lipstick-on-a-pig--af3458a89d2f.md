### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To superficially alter something in the hope of making it seem more appealing than it is in actuality.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; slang

   **Example:** That new box is like putting lipstick on a pig. It's still the same buggy program inside.

   **Source synonyms:** polish a turd; sugarcoat the pill
