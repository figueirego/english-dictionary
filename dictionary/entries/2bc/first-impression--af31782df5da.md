## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The event when one person first encounters another person and forms a mental image of them. ᵂᵖ

   **Example:** I obviously didn't make much of a first impression with him, because he didn't recognize me from the party two weeks ago.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. (of a case, issue, etc.) The state of not having been previously raised within a particular jurisdiction. ᵂᵖ

   **Example:** Because the law was just recently passed, this is a case of first impression.
