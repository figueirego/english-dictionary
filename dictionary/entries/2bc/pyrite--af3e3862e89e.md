### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The common mineral iron disulfide (FeS₂), of a pale brass-yellow color and brilliant metallic luster, crystallizing in the isometric system.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; geography; geology; mineralogy; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any metallic-looking sulphide, such as the above, which is the most common.

   **Usage:** usually

   **Source context (verbatim):** as a plural

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. (usually as a plural&#58; pyrites) Any metal dichalcogenide that is isostructural to the common mineral.

   **Usage:** solid state chemistry

   **Example:** Copper diselenide can occur both as a marcasite and a pyrite.
