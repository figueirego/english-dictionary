## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The standard training regimen for introducing new recruits to a military organization's discipline and basic operational skills.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Next thing I know, they were drafting me and sending me off to Fort Sill for basic training. Talk about whiplash.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war

   **Source synonyms:** boot camp

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A more or less analogous (albeit nonmartial) experience in the civilian world.

   **Usage:** broadly; figuratively; often; uncountable

   **Example:** There's no such thing as formal apprenticeship in this industry. The only preparation you get is some basic training where you sink or swim while trying to look alive.
