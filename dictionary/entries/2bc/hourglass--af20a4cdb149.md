### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A clock made of two glass vessels connected by a narrow passage through which sand flows.

   **Example:** After each game turn, invert the hourglass to reset the time limit for the next player.

   **Source synonyms:** sandglass; sand timer; watch glass

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
