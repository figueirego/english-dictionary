### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Physical injury; hurt; damage.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** No harm came to my possessions.

   **Example:** You can do a lot of harm to someone if you kick them in the teeth.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Emotional or figurative hurt.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Although not physically injured in the car accident, she received some psychological harm.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Detriment; misfortune.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** I wish him no harm.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. That which causes injury, damage, or loss.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
