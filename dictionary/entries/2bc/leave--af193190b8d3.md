### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To have a consequence or remnant.
   1. To cause or allow (something) to remain as available; to refrain from taking (something) away; to stop short of consuming or otherwise depleting (something) entirely.

      **Usage:** transitive

      **Example:** I left my car at home and took a bus to work.

      **Example:** The ants did not leave so much as a crumb of bread.

      **Example:** There's not much food left. We'd better go to the shops.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To have a consequence or remnant.
   1. To cause, to result in.

      **Usage:** copulative; intransitive; transitive

      **Example:** The lightning left her dazzled for several minutes.

      **Example:** Infantile paralysis left him lame for the rest of his life.

      **Example:** She left disappointed.

      **Example:** The accident left the car a heap of twisted metal.

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To have a consequence or remnant.
   1. To put; to place; to deposit; to deliver, with a sense of withdrawing oneself.

      **Example:** Leave your hat in the hall.

      **Example:** We should leave the legal matters to lawyers.

      **Example:** I left my sewing and went to the window to watch the falling snow.
