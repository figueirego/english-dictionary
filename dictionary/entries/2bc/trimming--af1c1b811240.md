## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act or process of someone who trims.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An instance of that act or process.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** I gave a good trimming to my elderly neighbour's hedges.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Material that is removed by someone trimming something, as a piece of steak.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** He gave the trimmings to the dog under the kitchen table.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An ornamental accessory to a dress or other piece of clothing.

   **Usage:** countable; historical; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An accessory or accompaniment.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Christmas dinner with all the trimmings

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A reprimand or chastising.

   **Usage:** colloquial; countable; dated; uncountable

   **Example:** to give a boy a trimming
