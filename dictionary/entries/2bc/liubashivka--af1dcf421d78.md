### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A former village in Yurkivka silrada, Uman Raion, Cherkasy Oblast, Ukrainian Soviet Socialist Republic, first mentioned in 1775, annexed to the silrada’s administrative centre, Yurkivka, in July 1958.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A rural settlement, the administrative centre of Liubashivka settlement hromada, Podilsk Raion, Odesa Oblast, Ukraine, founded in the eighteenth century.
   1. A former volost of Ananiv povit, Kherson governorate, Russian Empire, merged into Troitske Raion in March 1923.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A rural settlement, the administrative centre of Liubashivka settlement hromada, Podilsk Raion, Odesa Oblast, Ukraine, founded in the eighteenth century.
   1. A former raion of Odesa Oblast, Ukraine, established in March 1923, disestablished in July 2020.

      **Source synonyms:** Yasenove Raion

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A rural settlement, the administrative centre of Liubashivka settlement hromada, Podilsk Raion, Odesa Oblast, Ukraine, founded in the eighteenth century.
   1. A former selrada of Liubashivka Raion, Odesa Oblast, Ukraine, established in 1957, amalgamated into Liubashivka settlement hromada in February 2018.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A rural settlement, the administrative centre of Liubashivka settlement hromada, Podilsk Raion, Odesa Oblast, Ukraine, founded in the eighteenth century.
   1. A settlement hromada of Podilsk Raion, Odesa Oblast, Ukraine, established in February 2018.
