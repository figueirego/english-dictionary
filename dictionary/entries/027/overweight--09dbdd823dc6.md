### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having a higher weight, especially body fat, than what is generally considered healthy for a given body type and height.

   **Example:** Before her diet, Jane was noticeably overweight, but she shed five kilos in the next two months.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a person

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Weighing more than what is allowed for safety or legal commerce.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law; transport

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a vehicle

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Having a portfolio relatively heavily invested in.

   **Example:** Our portfolio is very overweight (in) Asian technology stocks.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance; investment

   **Source context (verbatim):** followed by a noun or prepositional phrase indicating a security or type of security
