### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A bivalve mollusk of many kinds, especially those that are edible; for example soft-shell clams (Mya arenaria), hard clams (Mercenaria mercenaria), sea clams or hen clams (Spisula solidissima), and other species, possibly originally applied to clams of species Tridacna gigas, a huge East Indian bivalve.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A type of strong pincers or forceps.

   **Usage:** historical; in-plural

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A kind of vise, usually of wood.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A dollar.

   **Usage:** US; in-plural; slang

   **Example:** Those sneakers cost me fifty clams!

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A Scientologist.

   **Usage:** derogatory; slang

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A vagina or vulva.

   **Usage:** slang; vulgar

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A wrong or misplaced note.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. One who clams up; a taciturn person, one who refuses to speak.

   **Usage:** informal

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. mouth (Now found mostly in the expression shut one's clam)

   **Usage:** US; dated; slang
