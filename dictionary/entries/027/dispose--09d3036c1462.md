### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To eliminate or to get rid of something.

   **Usage:** intransitive; with-of

   **Example:** I dispose of my trash in the garbage can.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To distribute or arrange; to put in place.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To deal out; to assign to a use.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To incline.

   **Example:** In these uncertain times, I am disposed towards caution.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To bargain; to make terms.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To regulate; to adjust; to settle; to determine.

   **Usage:** obsolete
