### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To collect something such as leaves into a pile, often with a rake.

   **Usage:** literally

   **Source synonyms:** rake up

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To receive or to collect a large quantity of (something, especially money).

   **Usage:** colloquial; figuratively

   **Example:** The company barely lifted a finger, and yet it still raked in more than two million euros in consulting fees.
