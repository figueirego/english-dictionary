### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any fish of genus Lumpenus, resembling an eel, found in subarctic waters, especially Lumpenus sagitta and Lumpenus fabricii (slender eelblenny).

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A carpet eel-blenny (Congrogadus subducens).

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Certain fish of genus Notograptus.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Eel snake blenny (Peronedys anguillaris), of Australia
