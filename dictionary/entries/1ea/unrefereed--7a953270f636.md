## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Not judged by a formal referee.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Our friendly games of ball are unrefereed, which is fine until we have to settle a dispute by screaming at each other.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of a journal article&#58; not reviewed before being printed.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Being printed in an unrefereed journal isn't as prestigious as being published in a refereed academic journal.
