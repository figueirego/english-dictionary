### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The state of being external or externalized.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A thing that is external relative to something else.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An impact, positive or negative, on any party not involved in a given economic transaction or act.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** Waste is a negative externality arising from consumption.
