### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To land atop of; to fall on the surface of.

   **Usage:** literally

   **Example:** In 1969, Apollo 11 was the first manned spacecraft to land on the moon.

   **Example:** I was shoved down and my backpack landed on the pavement.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To reach a common conclusion or agreement, usually following a small period of disagreement.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I wanted pepperoni pizza, but my friend was a vegetarian, so we landed on having cheese pizza.
