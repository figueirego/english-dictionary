### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To negotiate a lower price.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** If he offers a very high price, see if you can talk him down before you agree to anything.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To verbally assist someone to land an aircraft.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** aeronautics; aerospace; aviation; business; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

   **Source context (verbatim):** of ground control

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To convince a person to cease a drastic action or response, such as a suicide attempt or act of rage.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To overcome by spoken argument.

   **Usage:** transitive

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To comfort someone experiencing a bad trip, to help them come down.

   **Usage:** slang; transitive
