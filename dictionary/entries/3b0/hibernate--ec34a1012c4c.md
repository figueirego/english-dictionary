### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To spend the winter in a dormant or inactive state of minimal activity, low body temperature, slow breathing and heart rate, and low metabolic rate; to go through a winter sleep.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Hedgehogs and bears are two of the many mammals that hibernate in winter.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To live in seclusion.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To enter a standby state which conserves power without losing the contents of memory.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences
