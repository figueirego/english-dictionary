### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To divide or separate the parts of (especially something flimsy, such as paper or fabric), by cutting or tearing; to tear off or out by violence.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to rip a garment; to rip up a floor

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To tear apart; to rapidly become two parts.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** My shirt ripped when it was caught on a bramble.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To remove violently or wrongly.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** A child untimely ripped from its parents' arms.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To get by, or as if by, cutting or tearing.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To move quickly and destructively.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

   **Example:** The tornado ripped through northern Nebraska.

**Source sense R1.S6**
