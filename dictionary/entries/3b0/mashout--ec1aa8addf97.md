## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The step or process, in brewing beer, of raising the mash (e.g. by adding hot water to it) to a temperature around 75 °C or 170 °F, so as to stop the enzymatic conversion of starch to fermentable sugar and make the mash and wort more fluid.

   **Usage:** uncountable
