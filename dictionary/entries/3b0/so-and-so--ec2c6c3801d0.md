## Noun

### Forms

- so and so — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A placeholder name for a person or thing, used when a name is not known; a generic person or thing.

   **Example:** She told me to ask so-and-so, but he didn't know, either.

   **Example:** Business letters normally begin "Dear Mr So-and-So".

   **Example:** He will tell you to do so-and-so, but read the instructions first, anyway.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used in place of epithets such as SOB, bastard, etc.

   **Usage:** derogatory; euphemistic

   **Example:** That miserable so-and-so messed up my work!
