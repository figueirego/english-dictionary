### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The tenderest part of a loin of meat, especially of pork or beef.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A district of a city where corruption is common, often because the district is devoted to questionable businesses (peep shows, etc) which are easy for police to blackmail and extort.

   **Usage:** US; countable; dated; slang; uncountable
