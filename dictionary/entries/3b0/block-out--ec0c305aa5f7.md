### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To prevent from entering or penetrating.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** High-factor suncream blocks out the sun's rays.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To prevent (a thought) from entering one's mind.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** After finding out she had terminal cancer, she tried to block out any thoughts of her own mortality.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To cover something, so as to make it impossible to see.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To begin to reduce to shape; to mark out roughly; to lay out.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to block out a plan
