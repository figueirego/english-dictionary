### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A book or an encyclopedia of recipes and cookery tips.

   **Example:** They inherited an interesting nineteenth-century cookbook from their grandparents. It tells how to render lard.

   **Source synonyms:** cookery book; recipe book

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any book or set of strategies and tips.

   **Usage:** broadly; figuratively

   **Example:** The Anarchist Cookbook
