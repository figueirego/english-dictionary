### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The image-making power of the mind; the act of mentally creating or reproducing an object not previously perceived; the ability to create such images.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Imagination is one of the most advanced human faculties.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Particularly, construction of false images; fantasizing.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** You think someone's been following you? That's just your imagination.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Creativity; resourcefulness.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** His imagination makes him a valuable team member.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A mental image formed by the action of the imagination as a faculty; something imagined.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** conception; notion; imagining
