### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To steal; to escape with ill-gotten gains.

   **Example:** Thieves made away with £50,000 of jewellery in last night's heist.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To transfer or alienate; hence, to spend; to dissipate.

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To do away with; to destroy.

   **Usage:** obsolete
