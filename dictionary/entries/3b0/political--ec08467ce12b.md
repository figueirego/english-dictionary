### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Concerning or relating to politics, the art and process of governing.

   **Example:** Political principles are rarely absolute, as political logic holds an imperfect result by compromise is better than a theoretically perfect abstention from the political process in the opposition.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Concerning a polity or its administrative components.

   **Example:** Good political staff is hard to find, they may neither be ambitious and corrupted by power nor tempted by private sector careers.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Inappropriately motivated by political calculation, as it pertains to elections, power dynamics, ideologies, etc.

   **Usage:** derogatory

   **Example:** The media alleged that the Federal Reserve chairman’s decision to cut rates before the election was political.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Of or relating to views about social relationships that involve power, standing, influence or conflict.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Interested in politics.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a person
