### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Example:** Clear out those rocks. The surface must be even.

   **Example:** Despite her fear, she spoke in an even voice.

3. Equal in proportion, quantity, size, etc.

   **Example:** The distribution of food must be even.

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Of an integer, divisible by two.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Four, fourteen and forty are even numbers.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Of a number, convenient for rounding other numbers to; for example, ending in a zero.

   **Usage:** informal

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. On equal monetary terms; neither owing nor being owed.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. On equal terms of a moral sort; quits.

   **Usage:** colloquial

   **Example:** You biffed me back at the barn, and I biffed you here—so now we're even.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Parallel; on a level; reaching the same limit.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

<a id="r1-s10"></a>
