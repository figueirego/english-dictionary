## Phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used to remind players not to dwell on minor mistakes, issues or conflicts that happened during the course of play.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; usually

   **Example:** Don't worry about it, just leave it all on the field.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used to encourage players or anyone to give their best performance, not sparing any effort, not going away with any unspent energy.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; often

   **Example:** Go and give it your all! Leave it all on the field!

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports

   **Source synonyms:** give one's all; go all out; give 110%; pull out all the stops; do one's utmost; empty the tank; give it one's best shot
