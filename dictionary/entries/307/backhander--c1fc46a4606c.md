### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A glass of wine given out of turn, the bottle having been handed backwards.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A blow with the back of the hand; a blow delivered with a swing of the arm across the chest.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A bribe, a secret payment.

   **Usage:** informal

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A shot played backhand, a backhand stroke.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; games; hobbies; lifestyle; racquet-sports; sports

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A player who plays a backhand shot.

   **Example:** She's a terrific backhander.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; games; hobbies; lifestyle; racquet-sports; sports

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A surfer who approaches a swell with the wave behind them ("on their backhand") rather than facing the wave ("on their forehand").
