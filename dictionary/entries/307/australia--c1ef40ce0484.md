### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An island of Oceania.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A country consisting of a main island, the island of Tasmania and other smaller islands, located in Oceania; historically, a collection of former colonies of the British Empire. Official name&#58; Commonwealth of Australia. Capital&#58; Canberra.

   **Source synonyms:** Aussie; Aussieland; land down under; New Holland; Oz; Terra Australis; Upsidedownland; Stralia; Straya

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A continent consisting of the land on the Australian tectonic plate, i.e. Australia, New Guinea and intervening islands.

   **Example:** He is going to Australia for his honeymoon for 10 days.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; geology; natural-sciences

   **Source synonyms:** Meganesia; Sahul; Oceania
