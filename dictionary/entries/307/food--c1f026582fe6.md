### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any solid substance that can be consumed by living organisms, especially by eating, in order to sustain life.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** The innkeeper brought them food and drink.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A foodstuff, a type of food.

   **Usage:** countable; usually

   **Source synonyms:** belly-timber (archaic; humorous; regional); foodstuff; provender

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Anything that nourishes or sustains.

   **Usage:** figuratively; uncountable; usually

   **Example:** The man's inspiring speech gave us food for thought.

   **Example:** Mozart and Bach are food for my soul.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any illegal substance or illegal substances, drugs.

   **Usage:** Multicultural-London-English; uncountable; usually
