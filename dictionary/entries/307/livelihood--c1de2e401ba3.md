### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A means of providing the necessities of life for oneself (for example, a job or income).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** an independent livelihood; to make / earn / gain / win a good livelihood by the sweat of one's brow

   **Source synonyms:** living; subsistence; sustenance

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Property which brings in an income; an estate.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Liveliness; appearance of life.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The course of someone's life; a person's lifetime, or their manner of living; conduct, behaviour.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable
