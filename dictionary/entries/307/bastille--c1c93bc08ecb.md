### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Chiefly in French contexts&#58; a bastion (“projecting part of a rampart or other fortification”) or tower of a castle; also, a fortified tower or other building; or a small citadel or fortress.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A jail or prison, especially one regarded as mistreating its prisoners.

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Synonym of workhouse (“an institution for homeless poor people funded by the local parish, where the able-bodied were required to work”).

   **Usage:** UK; derogatory; figuratively

   **Source synonyms:** workhouse (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The fortified encampment of an army besieging a place; also, any of the buildings in such an encampment.

   **Usage:** historical

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war
