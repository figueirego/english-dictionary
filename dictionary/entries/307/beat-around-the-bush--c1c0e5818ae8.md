### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To treat a topic, but omit its main points, often intentionally.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To delay or avoid talking about something difficult or unpleasant.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** Just stop beating around the bush and tell me what the problem is!

   **Source synonyms:** go around the houses; prevaricate; pussyfoot; ramble; tergiversate; waffle

   **Source antonyms:** cut to the chase; get on with; get to the point
