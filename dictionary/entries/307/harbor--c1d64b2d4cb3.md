### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To provide a harbor or safe place for.

   **Usage:** US; transitive

   **Example:** The docks, which once harbored tall ships, now harbor only petty thieves.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To take refuge or shelter in a protected expanse of water.

   **Usage:** US; intransitive

   **Example:** The fleet harbored in the south.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To drive (a hunted stag) to covert.

   **Usage:** US; transitive

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To hold or persistently entertain in one's thoughts or mind.

   **Usage:** US; transitive

   **Example:** She harbors a conviction that her husband has a secret, criminal past.
