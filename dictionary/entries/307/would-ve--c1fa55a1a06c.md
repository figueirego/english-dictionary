### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Contraction of would + have, indicating a non-occurring action or state that was conditional on another non-occurring event in the past.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; contraction

   **Example:** We would've been warmer if you had closed the window.

   **Example:** I would've gone if it hadn't rained.
