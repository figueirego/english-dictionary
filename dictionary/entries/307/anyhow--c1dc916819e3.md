### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In any way or manner whatever.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** manner

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In any case. Used to indicate that a statement explains or supports a previous statement.

   **Usage:** conjunctive; not-comparable

   **Example:** Anyhow, he is dead now.

   **Example:** I think they’re asleep; anyhow, they’re quiet.

   **Usage:** Singapore; Singlish; broadly; not-comparable
