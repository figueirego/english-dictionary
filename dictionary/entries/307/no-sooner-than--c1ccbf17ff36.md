## Conjunction · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. At the moment when; instantaneously after; immediately after.

   **Example:** No sooner had he arrived than the others grew talkative.

   **Example:** She'd no sooner passed her driving test than/when her family started asking for lifts.

   **Source synonyms:** as soon as; once; immediately; instantly; hardly

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used to indicate that the second action mentioned is as likely as, or is preferred to, the first action mentioned.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I'd no sooner shop at their store than jump in a lake.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see no, sooner, than.

   **Example:** He will arrive no sooner than I will.

   **Source antonyms:** no later than
