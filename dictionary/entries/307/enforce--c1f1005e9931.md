### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To keep up, impose or bring into effect something, not necessarily by force.

   **Example:** The police are there to enforce the law.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To apply a rule or enforcement action to a person or user account.

   **Example:** This will give us the tools we need to enforce accounts like these.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To give strength or force to; to affirm, to emphasize.

   **Example:** The victim was able to enforce his evidence against the alleged perpetrator.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To strengthen (a castle, town etc.) with extra troops, fortifications etc.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To intensify, make stronger, add force to.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To exert oneself, to try hard.

   **Usage:** obsolete; reflexive

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To compel, oblige (someone or something); to force.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To make or gain by force; to force.

   **Usage:** obsolete

   **Example:** to enforce a passage

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To put in motion or action by violence; to drive.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. To give force to; to strengthen; to invigorate; to energize.

   **Usage:** obsolete

   **Example:** to enforce arguments or requests

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. To urge; to ply hard; to lay much stress upon.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s12"></a>

**Source sense R1.S12**

12. To prove; to evince.

   **Usage:** obsolete
