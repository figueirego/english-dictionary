## Suffix

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Agent suffix, equivalent to -er, appended to some nouns (especially those ending in the letter "w" or "v").

   **Usage:** idiomatic; morpheme; rare; usually

   **Example:** law + -yer → lawyer (AmE /ˈlɔɪər/)

   **Example:** saw + -yer → sawyer /ˈsɔːjər/
