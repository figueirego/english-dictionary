## Noun

### Forms

- backwoodsmen — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who is acclimated to living in a forest area that is far removed from civilization or modern conveniences.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An uncivilized or uncultured person (according to the value judgments of the speaker).

   **Source synonyms:** woodman

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A Peer who is seldom present in the House of Lords of the United Kingdom Parliament, who may be encouraged to attend when a very important vote is expected.

   **Usage:** UK; informal

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; politics
