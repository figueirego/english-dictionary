### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To kill (a person) by hanging, especially to lynch.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** hang; scrag; forhang (obsolete); Guy Fawkes; hang; ketch (rare); send to the scaffold; scrag; stretch (archaic; slang); string up; truss (archaic; slang); tuck up (obsolete; slang)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To die by hanging.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive; obsolete

   **Source synonyms:** hang; swing; dance on a rope (dated); dance on nothing (archaic; idiomatic); dance the hempen jig (colloquial); dance the Tyburn jig (archaic); dance upon nothing (archaic); dangle; hang; ride a horse foaled by an acorn (obsolete); stretch hemp; string up (obsolete); swing; take a ride to Tyburn (archaic); trine; truss (archaic; slang); wag hemp

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To suspend by means of rope, cord or similar material.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To concatenate; to link in a line.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** to string up a sentence
