### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of disowning or disinheriting a child.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The act of abdicating; the renunciation of a high office, dignity, or trust, by its holder.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The voluntary renunciation of sovereign power.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** abdication of the throne, government, power, authority

   **Example:** the king’s abdication

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The renunciation of interest in a property or a legal claim; abandonment.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The action of being deposed from the seat of power.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable
