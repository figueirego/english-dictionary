### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A place of safety, refuge, or protection.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** My car is a sanctuary, where none can disturb me except for people who cut me off.

   **Source synonyms:** haven; refuge; shelter; zoar

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An area set aside for protection.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The bird sanctuary has strict restrictions on visitors so the birds aren't disturbed.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A state of being protected, asylum.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The government granted sanctuary to the defector, protecting him from his former government.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The consecrated (or sacred) area of a church or temple around its tabernacle or altar.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
