## Adjective · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /sɛnˈtɛnɪəl/ — source labels: UK; US

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Relating to, or associated with, the commemoration of an event that happened a hundred years before.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** a centennial ode

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Happening once in a hundred years.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** a centennial jubilee a centennial celebration

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Lasting or aged a hundred years.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

### Related words
