## Proper noun · Record 1

### Forms

- the Northeast — canonical
- North East — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of various particular regions named for the cardinal direction in which they lie.
   1. The northeastern region of the contiguous United States including New England and the Mid-Atlantic states, sometimes inclusive of Maryland, Delaware, Washington, DC, Virginia and West Virginia.

      **Usage:** US

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of various particular regions named for the cardinal direction in which they lie.
   1. Alternative form of North East (“a region of England”).

      **Usage:** UK; alt-of; alternative

      **Alternative form of:** [North East](../1c7/north-east--71ee2f35538c.md)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of various particular regions named for the cardinal direction in which they lie.
   1. The northeastern provinces of Heilongjiang, Jilin, and Liaoning in China.

      **Usage:** China

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any of various particular regions named for the cardinal direction in which they lie.
   1. The northeastern region of India including Assam and its surrounding states.

      **Usage:** India
