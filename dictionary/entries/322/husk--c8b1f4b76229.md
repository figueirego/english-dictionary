## Noun · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /hʌsk/ — source labels: General-American; Received-Pronunciation
- IPA: /hʊsk/ — source labels: Northern-England

### Forms

- husks — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The dry, leafy or stringy exterior of certain vegetables or fruits, which must be removed before eating the meat inside.

   **Example:** A coconut has a very thick husk.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any form of useless, dried up, and subsequently worthless exterior of something.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** His attorney was a dried-up husk of a man.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The supporting frame of a run of millstones.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A group of hares.

   **Usage:** collective; rare
