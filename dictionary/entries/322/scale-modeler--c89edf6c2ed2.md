## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any implement or application that creates a miniature model of a real object by assembling and customizing a scaled down model.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One who builds scale models.

   **Example:** Hang onto your paint brushes, scale modelers! A new kit is coming soon to pay homage to the legendary Boeing 747!

   **Source synonyms:** modelmaker; modeler
