**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Example:** Storing extra food for the winter was a wise decision.

   **Example:** They were considered the wise old men of the administration.

   **Source synonyms:** hot (informal)

   **Source antonyms:** improvident

2. Disrespectful.

   **Usage:** colloquial; ironic; sarcastic

   **Example:** Don't get wise with me!

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Aware, informed (to something).

   **Usage:** colloquial

   **Example:** Be careful&#58; the boss is wise to your plan to call out sick.
