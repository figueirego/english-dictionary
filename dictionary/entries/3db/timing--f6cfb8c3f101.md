### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An occurrence or event.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The regulation of the pace of e.g. an athletic race, the speed of an engine, the delivery of a joke, or the occurrence of a series of events.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The key to telling a good joke is timing.

   **Example:** The officials used the latest technology to ensure timing down to the nearest millisecond.

   **Example:** perfect timing

   **Example:** The timing of the announcement is related to this months's board meeting.

   **Usage:** uncountable
