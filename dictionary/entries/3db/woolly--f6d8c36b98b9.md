### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Made of wool.

   **Example:** Put on a woolly jumper and turn down the thermostat.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Having a thick, soft texture, as if made of wool.

   **Example:** woolly hair

   **Example:** There was nothing left in the fruit bowl but a brown banana and a couple of woolly pears.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Unclear, fuzzy, hazy, cloudy.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** That's the sort of woolly thinking that causes wars to start.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Clothed in wool.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Cross; irritable.

   **Usage:** archaic; slang

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Uncivilized and unruly.

   **Usage:** US; informal

   **Example:** wild and woolly
