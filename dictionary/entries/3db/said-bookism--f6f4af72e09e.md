### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A verb (such as "explained", "shouted", or "uttered") used to indicate dialogue when writing fiction, chosen so as to avoid using the word "said".

   **Usage:** rare

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** authorship; broadcasting; communications; film; journalism; literature; media; publishing; television; writing

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** uncountable
