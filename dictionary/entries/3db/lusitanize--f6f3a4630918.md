### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make Portuguese or more Portuguese-like.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Traders and governors attempted to lusitanize Goa and Macao for centuries.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To become Portuguese or more Portuguese-like.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Their sandwiches are lusitanized by frying the meat with copious garlic and covering everything with thick slabs of red pepper paste.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To translate or adapt into the Portuguese language.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The poet Camões used the lusitanized plural form cafres in the fifth canto of his 1572 poem Os Lusíadas.
