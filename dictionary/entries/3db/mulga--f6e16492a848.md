### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of a number of small acacia trees, especially Acacia aneura, forming dense scrub in dry inland areas of Australia.

   **Usage:** Australia

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any region where mulga is the predominant vegetation.

   **Usage:** Australia; preceded by definite article

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The outback.

   **Usage:** Australia; colloquial; in-compounds

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Something made from the wood of a mulga tree.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Ellipsis of mulga wire (“a message or story transmitted through an informal gossip network, especially one containing false information”).

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; ellipsis

   **Alternative form of:** [mulga wire](../31e/mulga-wire--c7806199f6ea.md)
