### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of a system&#58; connected (generally electrically) to a larger network.
   1. Of a generator or power plant&#58; connected to the grid.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of a system&#58; connected (generally electrically) to a larger network.
   1. Of a computer&#58; actively connected to the Internet or to some other communications service.

      **Example:** Is this modem online?

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Available over, or delivered from, the Internet.

   **Example:** I prefer to read online newspapers.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Connected to the Internet.

   **Example:** I'll be online tonight, so I'll be able to reply to your email.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Available on a computer system, even if not networked.

   **Example:** Press the F1 key to access the online help.

   **Example:** The program comes with an online manual, supplied on compact disc.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Of a system&#58; active, particularly building facilities (such as power) or a factory or power plant.

   **Example:** The power is online.

   **Example:** Once this factory comes online, it will double car production in our country!

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Immersed in Internet culture. (Usually modified by an intensifier such as extremely or terminally)

   **Usage:** slang

   **Example:** terminally online

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Able to process an input piece-by-piece without needing to have the entire input available to it from the start.
