### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To have each of a team's batting line-up positions complete an at-bat in the same half-inning.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; baseball; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To have each of a team's batting line-up positions complete an at-bat in the same half-inning.
   1. To have a team's first batter of the inning come to bat for a second time (after all of the others in the batting line-up have batted). (See the usage note below.)

      **Usage:** intransitive

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; baseball; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To discuss.

   **Usage:** informal; transitive

   **Example:** I think we've batted this idea around enough to take a decision.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To flit quickly from place to place.

   **Usage:** informal; intransitive

   **Example:** You must be tired after batting around all day.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To waste time; to idle or loaf.

   **Usage:** informal; intransitive
