## Phrase

### Forms

- I know you are, but what am I — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used to assert that an insult made by the party to whom the phrase is directed is actually true of that party, and not of the person using the phrase. Usually considered to be a playground taunt.

   **Usage:** childish; humorous; rhetoric

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used to mock a retort in a dispute that tries to reverse an insult on its original speaker.

   **Usage:** Internet; rhetoric

   **Source synonyms:** IKYABWAI

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used to direct back a statement as a compliment.

   **Usage:** humorous; rare
