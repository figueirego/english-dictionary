### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A tree, genus Cola, bearing large brown seeds ("nuts") that are the source of cola extract.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
