### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Tending to stick; able to adhere via the drying of a viscous substance.

   **Example:** Is this tape sticky enough to stay on that surface?

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Difficult, awkward.

   **Example:** This is a sticky situation. We could be in this for weeks if we're not careful.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of a death&#58; unpleasant, grisly.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Of weather&#58; hot and windless and with high humidity, so that people feel sticky from sweating.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Mawkish, sentimental.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Tending to stay the same; resistant to change.

   **Example:** a sticky price; sticky wages

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a window
