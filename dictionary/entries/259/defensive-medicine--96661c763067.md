## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The practice of recommending a diagnostic test or medical treatment that is not necessarily the best option for the patient, but mainly serves to protect the physician against a potential malpractice lawsuit from the patient.

   **Usage:** uncountable
