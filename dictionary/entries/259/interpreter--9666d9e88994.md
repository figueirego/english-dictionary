<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person or thing that interprets the meaning of something for another, particularly

   **Example:** ... an interpreter of dreams ...

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person or thing that interprets the meaning of something for another
   1. A person or thing that translates speech or signing, as opposed to texts.

      **Example:** A Japanese man who is tried before a German court is assisted by an interpreter in making oral statements.
