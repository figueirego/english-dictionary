### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Separate; distinct; individual; non-continuous.

   **Example:** a government with three discrete divisions

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. That can be perceived individually, not as connected to, or part of, something else.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Consisting of or permitting only distinct values drawn from a finite, countable set.

   **Example:** a discrete sum

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Having separate electronic components, such as individual diodes, transistors and resistors, as opposed to integrated circuitry.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; electrical; electrical-engineering; electricity; electromagnetism; energy; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Having separate and independent channels of audio, as opposed to multiplexed stereo or quadraphonic, or other multi-channel sound.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** audio; electrical-engineering; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Having each singleton subset open&#58; said of a topological space or a topology.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences; topology

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Disjunctive; containing a disjunctive or discretive clause.

   **Example:** "I resign my life, but not my honour" is a discrete proposition.
