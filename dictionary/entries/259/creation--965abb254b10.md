<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Something created such as an invention or artwork.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** I think the manufacturer was so ashamed of its creation that it didn't put its name on it!

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The act of creating something.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** The restructure resulted in the creation of a number of shared services.
