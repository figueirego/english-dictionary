### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Applications or websites allowing users to share content and communicate with one another through the Internet.

   **Usage:** plural; singular; uncountable

   **Example:** The early 21st century saw a huge increase in social media thanks to the widespread availability of the Internet.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A particular social media site or account.

   **Usage:** countable; informal; plural; singular

   **Example:** I decided to delete all my social medias that I rarely use.
