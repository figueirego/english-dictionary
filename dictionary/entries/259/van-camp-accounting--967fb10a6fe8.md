## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In California community property law, a mechanism to deal with community funds and/or labor used to enhance the value of separate property. The manager's services are valued at a market rate, and community expenses are subtracted from that amount. The result is considered community property.

   **Usage:** uncountable
