## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A large floating mass of ice; pack ice.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A pack of crushed ice applied to the body in order to reduce pain or inflammation.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A plastic sac of silicone that can be cooled in a freezer and then used in a cool bag, cool box or cooler; a freezer block.
