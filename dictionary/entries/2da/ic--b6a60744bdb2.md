**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used to form adjectives from nouns with the meaning “of or pertaining to”.

   **Usage:** morpheme

   **Example:** Cyril + -ic → Cyrillic

   **Example:** acid + -ic → acidic

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used to denote certain chemical compounds in which a specified chemical element has a greater oxidation number than in the equivalent compound whose name ends in the suffix -ous. For example sulphuric acid (H₂SO₄) has more oxygen atoms per molecule than sulphurous acid (H₂SO₃).

   **Usage:** morpheme

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences
