### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. to take a break, or rest while doing a strenuous activity, so that one's breathing becomes easier

   **Example:** They stood for a moment at the end of the set to catch their breath before resuming play.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. to take in a breath sharply and hold it, usually in reaction to a sudden shock, or surprise

   **Example:** John caught his breath when he saw the bottle rolling unstoppably towards the opposite edge of the table.
