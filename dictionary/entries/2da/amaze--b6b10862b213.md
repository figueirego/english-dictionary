### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To fill (someone) with surprise and wonder; to astonish, to astound, to surprise.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He was amazed when he found that the girl was a robot.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To stun or stupefy (someone).

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To bewilder or perplex (someone or oneself).

   **Usage:** also; obsolete; reflexive; transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To fill (someone) with panic; to panic, to terrify.

   **Usage:** obsolete; poetic; transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To experience amazement; to be astounded.

   **Usage:** archaic; intransitive

**Source synonyms:** amaze; astonish; astound; bewitch (rare); surprise; wow
