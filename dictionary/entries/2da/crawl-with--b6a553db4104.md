### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To include or be covered with swarms or large numbers of (something, especially insects or people); to have in great numbers or multitudes.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** The kitchen was crawling with cockroaches.

   **Example:** The scene of the incident was crawling with reporters for days afterwards.
