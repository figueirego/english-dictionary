### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of or relating to two clauses.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** "It is surprising that Mary likes apples" is a biclausal sentence consisting of an independent clause and a dependent clause.
