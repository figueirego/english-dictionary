### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Expressing disagreement with an opinion expressed by another.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; imperative

   **Example:** That idea makes no sense at all. – Speak for yourself! I think it's a great idea.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To have obvious implications; to require no explanation.

   **Example:** His fantastic record as a manager speaks for itself.

   **Example:** His actions speak for themselves.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see speak, for. To provide an opinion only on one's own behalf.

   **Example:** I'm here speaking for myself, not for my company.
