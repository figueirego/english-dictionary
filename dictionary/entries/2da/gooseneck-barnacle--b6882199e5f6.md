### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of the species Pollicipes pollicipes or P. polymerus, filter-feeding crustaceans that live attached to hard surfaces of rocks and flotsam in the ocean intertidal zone, with a long and muscular stalk, which is edible and considered a delicacy in several Mediterranean countries.

   **Source synonyms:** goose barnacle

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any barnacle in the superseded order Pedunculata, now all or most of infraclass Thoracica, most of which are filter-feeders that attach to surfaces by a long stalk, though some are parasites.
