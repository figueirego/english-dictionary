### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To supply with light.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The sun enlightens the Earth.

   **Source synonyms:** illumine; illuminate; alight (archaic); belight; beshine (dialectal); bright; enlighten; fire; illume (archaic); illuminate; illumine; illustrate (obsolete); irradiate; light; lighten; light up; luster (obsolete); lustrate (obsolete); shed light on

   **Source antonyms:** endarken

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To make something clear to (someone); to give knowledge or understanding to.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** She tried to enlighten them about the rules of the program, but they remained belligerently and self-righteously ignorant.

   **Example:** Care to enlighten me, if you think I'm so benighted?

   **Source synonyms:** apprise; clarify; explain; notify; inform; edify
