### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In a mighty manner.
   1. With much physical force, power, or strength; also, with divine or superhuman power or strength.

      **Usage:** literary

      **Example:** The chivalrous knights entered the lists and fought mightily.

      **Source synonyms:** mightly; mighty; strongly; powerfully; vigorously

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In a mighty manner.
   1. With much emotional, intellectual, or mental force or power; vehemently.

      **Source synonyms:** mightly

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. In a mighty manner.
   1. Of a characteristic&#58; provided or present to a powerful or strong extent.

      **Source context (verbatim):** before an adjective or participial adjective

      **Source synonyms:** mightly
