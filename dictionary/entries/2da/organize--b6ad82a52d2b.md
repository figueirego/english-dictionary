### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To arrange in working order.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** marshal

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To constitute in parts, each having a special function, act, office, or relation; to systematize.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To furnish with organs; to give an organic structure to; to endow with capacity for the functions of life

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** an organized being

   **Example:** organized matter

   **Source context (verbatim):** used in the past participle

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To sing in parts.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to organize an anthem

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To band together into a group or union that can bargain and act collectively; to unionize.

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** the workers decided to organize; their next task was to organize the workers at the steel mill
