## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Extremely angry.

   **Usage:** usually in the phrase 'go off the deep end'

   **Example:** I told him that I'd broken the vase, and he went off the deep end.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Crazy, erratic, or irrational.

   **Usage:** US; idiomatic

   **Example:** It used to be a funny comic, but lately it has gone off the deep end.
