### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A small ramp cut into a curb to allow wheelchairs, baby carriages, bicycles, etc. to more easily enter or leave a sidewalk.

   **Usage:** US

   **Source synonyms:** curb ramp; dropped kerb; kerb ramp; pram ramp

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A lowered area of curb to allow vehicles to access driveways or approaches, a drop kerb.
