### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The food and beverage a person or animal consumes.

   **Example:** The diet of the giant panda consists mainly of bamboo.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A controlled regimen of food and drink choices, as to gain or lose weight or otherwise influence health.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** He's been on a diet since he found out that he has coronary atherosclerosis.

   **Example:** The doctor put me on a low-salt diet.

   **Example:** My jeans are too tight, so I'm going on a diet.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any habitual intake or consumption.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** He's been reading a steady diet of nonfiction for the last several years.
