### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A city, the administrative centre of Bilopillia urban hromada, Sumy Raion, Sumy Oblast, Ukraine, founded in 1672.
   1. A miskrada of Bilopillia Raion, Sumy Oblast, Ukraine, amalgamated into Bilopillia urban hromada in September 2018.

      **Usage:** historical

      **Source synonyms:** Kryha

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A city, the administrative centre of Bilopillia urban hromada, Sumy Raion, Sumy Oblast, Ukraine, founded in 1672.
   1. A former raion of Sumy Oblast, Ukraine, established in 1923, disestablished in July 2020.

      **Usage:** historical

      **Source synonyms:** Kryha
