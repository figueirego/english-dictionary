## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

- caprioles — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A leap that a horse makes with all fours, upwards only, without advancing, but with a kick or jerk of the hind legs when at the height of the leap.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A leap or caper, as in dancing.
