### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Rent paid under a ground lease, usually long-term or in perpetuity, for a surface right or estate in land where the landowner (surface owner) and the owner of improvements (ground lessor) are separate; the improvements are effectively security for the payment of the rent.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
