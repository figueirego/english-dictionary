### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make (something) new again; to restore to freshness or original condition.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To replace (something which has broken etc.); to replenish (something which has been exhausted), to keep up a required supply of.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To make new spiritually; to regenerate.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** lifestyle; religion; theology

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To become new, or as new; to revive.

   **Usage:** archaic; intransitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To begin again; to recommence.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** This murder has been a cold case for decades until last year when the new sheriff renewed the investigation.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To repeat.

   **Usage:** rare

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To extend a period of loan, especially a library book that is due to be returned.

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** I'd like to renew these three books. Did you know that you can renew online?
