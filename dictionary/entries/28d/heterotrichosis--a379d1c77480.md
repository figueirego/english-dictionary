### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. (historical ophthalmology) Variation in hair colour within or between the eyebrows or eyelashes.

   **Extractor diagnostics (not usage labels):** no-gloss

**Supplemental definition (evidence reviewed; source gap preserved):** Cornaz’s 1848 medical monograph explicitly explains heterotrichosis superciliorum as two-coloured hairs in one eyebrow or different colours between the eyebrows, then uses the bare term for the same two patterns in eyelashes.
