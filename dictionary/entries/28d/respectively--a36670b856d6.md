### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In a relative manner; often used when comparing array lists, where the term denotes that the items in the lists correspond to each other in the order they are given.

   **Example:** Serena Williams and Roger Federer won the women's and men's singles titles, respectively, at the 2010 Australian Open.

   **Example:** The 49th and 50th states of the United States of America are Alaska and Hawaii, respectively.
