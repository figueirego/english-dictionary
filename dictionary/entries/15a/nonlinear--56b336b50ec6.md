## Adjective

### Forms

- non-linear — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of a set of points, not lying on a straight line.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source antonyms:** linear

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of a molecule, whose atoms do not lie in a straight line.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of a function, having a product of independent variables, or a variable with an exponent not equal to one.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Of a system, whose output is not directly proportional to its input.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Erratic and unpredictable; tending to jump back and forth.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** a film with a nonlinear plot
