## Noun

### Forms

- sign languages — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of several natural languages, typically used by the deaf, in which words consist of hand shapes, motions, positions, and facial expressions.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The sign language (sense 1) that is used locally or that is mistakenly believed to be the only one.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** I'm taking night classes to learn sign language.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Sign languages (sense 1) considered collectively.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** There are some unique properties found in sign language compared to spoken language.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Communication through gestures as a substitute for language, for example, between monks under a vow of silence or during trade amongst different linguistic groups.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
