### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A stringed musical instrument (chordophone), usually with a round body, a membrane-like soundboard and a fretted neck, played by plucking or strumming the strings.

   **Example:** I come from Alabama with my banjo on my knee...

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of various similar musical instruments, such as the Tuvan doshpuluur, with a membrane-like soundboard.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An object shaped like a banjo, especially a frying pan or a shovel.

   **Usage:** slang

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A cul-de-sac with a round end.

   **Usage:** UK

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A miner's round-nosed shovel.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; mining

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. An egg sandwich fried on a flattop and served in a bun like a burger, ellipsis of egg banjo.

   **Usage:** Malaysia

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The frenulum of the penis.

   **Usage:** slang; vulgar
