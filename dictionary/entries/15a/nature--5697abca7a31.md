### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The way things are, the totality of all things in the physical universe and their order, especially the physical world in contrast to spiritual realms and flora and fauna as distinct from human conventions, art, and technology.

   **Usage:** capitalized; often; uncountable

   **Example:** Nature doesn't lie.

   **Example:** The laws of nature are written in the language of mathematics.

   **Example:** Tectonic activity is part of nature, so there's no way to stop earthquakes.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The particular way someone or something is, especially
   1. The essential or innate characteristics of a person or thing which will always tend to manifest, especially in contrast to specific contexts, reason, religious duty, upbringing, and personal pretense or effort.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Example:** It's not in my nature to steal.

      **Example:** You can't help feeling that way. It's human nature.

      **Example:** Power corrupts. That's just the nature of the beast.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The particular way someone or something is, especially
   1. The distinguishing characteristic of a person or thing, understood as its general class, sort, type, etc.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Example:** What was the nature of your relationship with the deceased?

      **Example:** The best medium might be petroleum, liquified gas, or something of that nature.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The particular way someone or something is, especially
   1. Synonym of caliber&#58; the class of a gun.

      **Usage:** UK; countable; obsolete; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war

      **Source synonyms:** caliber (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The vital functions or strength of someone or something, especially (now dialect) as requiring nourishment or careful maintenance or (medicine) as a force of regeneration without special treatment.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A requirement or powerful impulse of the body's physical form, especially
   1. The need to urinate and defecate.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Example:** I hear the call of nature.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A requirement or powerful impulse of the body's physical form, especially
   1. Sexual desire.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable; now chiefly African-American Vernacular

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A requirement or powerful impulse of the body's physical form, especially
   1. Spontaneous love, affection, or reverence, especially between parent and child.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable; now chiefly UK regional and African-American Vernacular

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A product of the body's physical form, especially semen and vaginal fluids, menstrual fluid, and (obsolete) feces.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. A part of the body's physical form, especially (obsolete) the female genitalia.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; uncountable
