### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Sexual intercourse by a married person with someone other than their spouse. See more synonyms at Thesaurus&#58;cuckoldry

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; strictly

   **Example:** She engaged in adultery because her spouse has a low libido, while hers is very high.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Lewdness or unchastity of thought as well as act, as forbidden by the sixth/seventh commandment; sinful sexual behavior as a category.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Thou shalt not commit adultery.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biblical; lifestyle; religion
