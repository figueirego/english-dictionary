**Source sense R1.S1**

1. (rare, management) The role or office of directing an organization or one of its divisions.

   **Extractor diagnostics (not usage labels):** no-gloss

**Supplemental definition (evidence reviewed; source gap preserved):** The English attestation in this primary management paper is confined to its bilingual abstract, where gerency explicitly corresponds to Spanish gerencia. Its body explains that term as a managerial role or office and describes coordination within organizations and functional divisions. This supplies a scoped management sense for the unqualified noun; it is not inferred only from vicegerency, and it does not assert current general English usage or that the English form has all the meanings of Spanish gerencia.
