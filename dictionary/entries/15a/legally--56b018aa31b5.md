### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. As permitted by law; not contrary to law.

   **Example:** You can legally park in the lot on weekends without paying the fee, they won't ticket you.

   **Source synonyms:** lawfully; lawfully; legally; legitimately; licitly; illegally; criminally; forbiddenly; illegally; illegitimately; illicitly; unlawfully

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. From a legal perspective.

   **Example:** Legally, I think you are covered, but there are angry guys with baseball bats outside.

   **Source synonyms:** legalistically
