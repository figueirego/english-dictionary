### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An act of investing with a crown; a crowning.

   **Usage:** also; attributive; literally

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An act of investing with a crown; a crowning.
   1. An act or the ceremony of formally investing a sovereign or the sovereign's consort with a crown and other insignia of royalty, on or shortly after their accession to the sovereignty.

      **Usage:** also; attributive; literally; specifically

      **Example:** King Charles III’s coronation is to be much less elaborate compared to his mother’s.

      **Source synonyms:** crowning; crownment (obsolete); sacration (obsolete; rare)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A completion or culmination of something.

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A success in the face of little or no opposition.

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. In the game of checkers or draughts&#58; the act of turning a checker into a king when it has reached the farthest row forward.

   **Usage:** rare; board games
