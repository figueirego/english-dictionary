### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act or process of making safe an explosive device (for example, a bomb, mine, or improvised explosive device), as by removing, disabling, or neutralizing its fuse, detonator, etc.

   **Usage:** uncountable; explosives

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The act or process of calming, de-escalating, or otherwise making a potentially dangerous, volatile, or tense situation less dangerous or tense.

   **Usage:** figuratively; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A particular instance or operation of defusing, especially an explosive device.

   **Usage:** countable
