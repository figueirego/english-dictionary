### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A low or downcast condition; meanness of spirit; abasement; degradation.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** an abjection from the beatific regions where God, and his angels and saints, dwell forever

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Something cast off; garbage.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The act of bringing down or humbling; casting down.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

   **Example:** The abjection of the king and his realm.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The act of casting off; rejection.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The fact of being marginalized as deviant.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
