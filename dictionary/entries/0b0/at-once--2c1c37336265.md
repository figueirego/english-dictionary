### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. At the same time; simultaneously; together.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Can you pat your head and rub your belly at once? He tried to eat four cookies at once.

   **Source synonyms:** at one time; at once

   **Usage:** not-comparable
