### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An acute myocardial infarction, sometimes fatal, caused by the sudden occurrence of coronary thrombosis, which obstructs the blood supply to the heart and leads to necrosis of heart muscle tissue.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** cardiology; medicine; pathology; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** myocardial infarction

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A major fright.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** She'll have a heart attack when she sees that!

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Death or failure.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** My computer had a heart attack when I tried to get it to run that program.
