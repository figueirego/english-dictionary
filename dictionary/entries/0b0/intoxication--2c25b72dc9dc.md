### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A poisoning, as by a spirituous or a narcotic substance.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** He suffered acute intoxication from the combined effects of several drugs.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The state of being intoxicated or drunk.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** inebriation; ebriety; drunkenness

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The act of intoxicating or making drunk.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A high excitement of mind; an elation which rises to enthusiasm, frenzy, or madness.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable
