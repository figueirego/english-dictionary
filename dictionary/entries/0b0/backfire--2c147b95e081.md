### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Of a gun, cannon, Bunsen burner, etc., to fire in the opposite direction, for example due to an obstruction in the barrel.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Of a reciprocating engine, to experience a premature ignition of fuel or an ignition of exhaust gases, making a popping sound.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To fail in a manner that brings down further misfortune.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** His attempt to make money by importing luxury cars backfired horribly when fuel prices tripled.

   **Example:** Her plan backfired on her.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To set a back fire (small, controlled fire to limit spread of a larger fire).
