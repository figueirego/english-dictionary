### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. For; in support of; approving; supporting.

   **Usage:** US

   **Example:** Many people are in favor of capital punishment.

   **Source synonyms:** all for; pro

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. For the benefit of; to the order of.

   **Usage:** US

   **Example:** He endorsed the note in favor of his uncle who had lent them the money.

   **Source synonyms:** in behalf of

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Out of preference for.

   **Usage:** US

   **Example:** He left school after just three years in favor of a professional sports career.

   **Source synonyms:** in something's favor
