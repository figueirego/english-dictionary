### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of several species resembling a typical olive tree from plant families some of which are only very distantly related.
   1. Bontia daphnoides (family Scrophulariaceae; bastard olive)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of several species resembling a typical olive tree from plant families some of which are only very distantly related.
   1. Several species in the genus Elaeagnus (family Elaeagnaceae)
      1. Elaeagnus angustifolia (silver berry, oleaster, Persian olive)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of several species resembling a typical olive tree from plant families some of which are only very distantly related.
   1. Several species in the genus Elaeagnus (family Elaeagnaceae)
      1. Elaeagnus latifolia (oleaster)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any of several species resembling a typical olive tree from plant families some of which are only very distantly related.
   1. Halesia carolina (family Styracaceae; Carolina silverbell, little silverbell)

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Any of several species resembling a typical olive tree from plant families some of which are only very distantly related.
   1. Nyssa aquatica, an American swamp-growing tree (family Cornaceae; water tupelo)

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Any of several species resembling a typical olive tree from plant families some of which are only very distantly related.
   1. Subspecies of Olea europea, which includes the cultivated olive (family Oleaceae)
      1. Olea europaea subsp. cuspidata

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Any of several species resembling a typical olive tree from plant families some of which are only very distantly related.
   1. Subspecies of Olea europea, which includes the cultivated olive (family Oleaceae)
      1. Olea europaea subsp. europaea (syn. Olea oleaster)

**Source sense R1.S8**

   1. Cartrema americana (syn. Osmanthus americanus; devilwood, American olive; family Elaeagnaceae)
