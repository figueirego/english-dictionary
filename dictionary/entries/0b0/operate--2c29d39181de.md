### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To perform a work or labour; to exert power or strength, physical or mechanical; to act.

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** Could someone explain how this meeting operates?

   **Example:** In this town, the garbage removal staff operate between six o'clock at midnight.

   **Example:** The police had inside knowledge of how the gang operated.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To produce an effect.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To produce an effect.
   1. To produce an appropriate physical effect; to issue in the result designed by nature; especially (medicine) to take appropriate effect on the human system.

      **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To produce an effect.
   1. To act or produce an effect on the mind; to exert moral power or influence.

      **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To bring about as an effect; to cause.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To perform some manual act upon a human body in a methodical manner, and usually with instruments, with a view to restore soundness or health, as in amputation, lithotomy, etc.

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** The surgeon had to operate on her heart.

   **Example:** I'm being operated tomorrow.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To deal in stocks or any commodity with a view to speculative profits.

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To put into, or to continue in, operation or activity; to work.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to operate a machine

   **Example:** to operate a system

   **Example:** to operate a casino
