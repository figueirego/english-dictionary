## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

- fiddlesticks — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One of a pair of sticks, similar to drumsticks, used by a second player to percuss the strings of a fiddle while the main player plays via bow.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A bow used to play the fiddle.

   **Source synonyms:** fiddlebow

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A whit; a jot.

   **Example:** I don't care a fiddlestick!
