### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of several horse-shaped chalk figures in England, such as the Uffington White Horse.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A wave that is blown by the wind such that its crest is broken up and appears white and foamy; a whitecap.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A recreational drug in the form of white powder&#58; heroin or cocaine.

   **Usage:** countable; slang; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A large mass of tough sinewy substance in the head of sperm whales, just above the upper jaw and extending in streaks into the junk above it. It resembles blubber, but contains no oil.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A soldier who is the child of a minister, high-ranking civil servant or officer, or comes from a highly influential family, who may receive preferential treatment from superiors.

   **Usage:** Singapore; countable; derogatory; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**
