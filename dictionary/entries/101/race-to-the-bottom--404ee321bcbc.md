## Noun

### Forms

- races to the bottom — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A situation in which competition between governments leads to very excessive (harmful) deregulation.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** economics; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A situation in which competition between entities leads to elimination of perks or amenities, often in the interest of cutting costs.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any situation in which multiple parties are seemingly competing to achieve the greatest level of mediocrity.
