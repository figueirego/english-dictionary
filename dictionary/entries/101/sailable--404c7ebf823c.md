### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. That can be sailed.

   **Example:** The canoes of that tribe were not sailable; they were paddled on rivers and lakes, and they did not go to sea, as the sea coast was controlled by other tribes.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

   **Source context (verbatim):** of vessels

   **Source antonyms:** unsailable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Navigable.

   **Example:** The upper reaches of this bay are not sailable; there are too many shoals and no dredged channel.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

   **Source context (verbatim):** of waters

   **Source antonyms:** unsailable; unnavigable
