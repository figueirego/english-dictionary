### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make a living; earn money for necessities.

   **Example:** It is difficult to provide for my family working on minimum wage.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To act to prepare for something.

   **Example:** provide against disaster.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To establish as a previous condition; to stipulate.

   **Example:** The contract provides that the work be well done.

   **Example:** I'll lend you the money, provided that you pay it back by Monday.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To give what is needed or desired, especially basic needs.

   **Example:** Don't bother bringing equipment, as we will provide it.

   **Example:** We aim to provide the local community with more green spaces.

   **Example:** This resort provides an evening meal for its guests.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To furnish (with), cause to be present, supply.

   **Source synonyms:** afford; treat

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To make possible or attainable.

   **Example:** He provides us with an alternative option.

   **Example:** Plants provide (us) with the oxygen we breathe.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To foresee, to consider in advance.

   **Usage:** Latinism; obsolete

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To appoint to an ecclesiastical benefice before it is vacant. See provisor.
