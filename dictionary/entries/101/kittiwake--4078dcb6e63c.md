### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Either of two small gulls in the genus Rissa of the family Laridae that nest in colonies on sea cliffs and spend the winter on the open ocean.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. the black-legged kittiwake (Rissa tridactyla), no other kittiwake being found in Europe.

   **Usage:** Ireland; UK
