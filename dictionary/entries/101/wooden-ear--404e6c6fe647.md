## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Tone-deafness of any kind.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** have a wooden ear

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Tone-deafness of any kind.
   1. Tone-deafness for musical tone.

      **Usage:** idiomatic

      **Example:** A music teacher is challenged when a student seems to have a wooden ear.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Tone-deafness of any kind.
   1. Tone-deafness for usage tone.

      **Usage:** idiomatic

      **Example:** She fancies herself a writer, but she'll steamroll idiom in a way that reveals a wooden ear.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see wooden, ear.

   **Example:** These dolls were traditionally made of fabric stuffed with straw, and they often had carved wooden ears and polished coals for eyes.
