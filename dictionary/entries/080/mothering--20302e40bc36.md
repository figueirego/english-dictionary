## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The nurturing of a child by its mother.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The protective behavior of a mother towards her child.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Nurturing or protective behavior reminiscent of that performed by a literal mother.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
