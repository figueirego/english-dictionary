### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A drink of coffee made from espresso and steamed milk, generally topped with foam.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** caffè latte; cafe latté; coffee latté

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A similar drink, where the espresso is replaced with some other flavoring ingredient such as chai, maté or matcha (green tea).

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** matcha latte

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Affluent leftism.

   **Usage:** attributive; countable; figuratively; informal; uncountable

   **Example:** latte liberal
