### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The trait of oversimplifying things by ignoring complexity and complications.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An instance of a brief or reductive depiction that neglects the full nuance and complexity of a concept or issue, and that omits or overlooks important details.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
