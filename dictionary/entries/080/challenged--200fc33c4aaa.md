### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Disabled; lacking some physical or mental attribute or skill; used after adverbs to make politically correct adjectives.

   **Usage:** euphemistic

   **Example:** mentally challenged

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used following adverbs to make adjectives in imitation of and mocking adjectives of this kind.

   **Usage:** humorous

   **Example:** He's not short or bald; he's vertically and follicularly challenged.
