### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A former country in Southeastern Europe in the Balkans, now split into 6 countries&#58; Bosnia and Herzegovina, Croatia, North Macedonia, Montenegro, Serbia, and Slovenia.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A former country in Southeastern Europe in the Balkans, now split into 6 countries&#58; Bosnia and Herzegovina, Croatia, North Macedonia, Montenegro, Serbia, and Slovenia.
   1. The Kingdom of Yugoslavia, a kingdom ruled by the House of Karađorđević which existed from 1918 to 1941.

      **Usage:** historical; specific

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A former country in Southeastern Europe in the Balkans, now split into 6 countries&#58; Bosnia and Herzegovina, Croatia, North Macedonia, Montenegro, Serbia, and Slovenia.
   1. The Federal People's Republic of Yugoslavia and the Socialist Federal Republic of Yugoslavia, a communist state which existed from 1945 to 1992.

      **Usage:** historical; specific

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A former country in Southeastern Europe, a rump state formed by Serbia and Montenegro after 1992; in full, Federal Republic of Yugoslavia.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A former country in Southeastern Europe, a rump state formed by Serbia and Montenegro after 1992; in full, Federal Republic of Yugoslavia.
   1. Serbia and Montenegro, after 2003 until 2006, when it disintegrated.

      **Usage:** broadly; historical; nonstandard
