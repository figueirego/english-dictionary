### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To sleep for longer than intended.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** I overslept and was late for school.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To sleep for longer than one intended.

   **Usage:** archaic; reflexive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To sleep beyond (a given time), to sleep through (an event etc.).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to oversleep one's usual hour of rising
