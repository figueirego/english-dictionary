### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Full of liveliness and activity; characterized by quickness of motion or action.

   **Example:** We took a brisk walk yesterday.

   **Source synonyms:** lively; spirited; quick; round

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Full of spirit of life; effervescing.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Sparkling; fizzy.

   **Usage:** archaic

   **Example:** brisk cider

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Stimulating or invigorating.

   **Example:** This morning was a brisk fall day. It wasn't cold enough for frost, but you wanted to keep moving.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Abrupt, curt in one's manner or in relation to others.

   **Example:** The service in that restaurant was brisk and impersonal.
