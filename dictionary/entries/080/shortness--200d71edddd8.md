### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The property of being short, of being small of stature or brief.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** the shortness of a stubby pencil

   **Example:** the patient's shortness of breath

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The result or product of being short.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The property of being short or terse.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** I was chastened by the shortness of her reply.
