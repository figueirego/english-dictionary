### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A result of a test that shows as absent something that is present.

   **Example:** While a false positive from a cancer screening test will unnecessarily frighten a healthy person, a false negative will deprive them of timely treatment.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A type II error (“accepting the null hypothesis when it is false”).

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences; statistics
