### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A fanciful life form that is part human and part beast; a creature recognizable as human but possessing physical or primitive behavioral characteristics that are exclusive to animals.

   **Usage:** informal

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person compared to such a creature, especially an unkempt and uncivilized, or strong and sexually aggressive man.

   **Usage:** slang; by analogy
