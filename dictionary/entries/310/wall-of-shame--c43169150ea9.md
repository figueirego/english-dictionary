### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A collection of the worst or most hated entries in a particular subject, medium, field, or for people who have failed to complete a challenge, etc.

   **Usage:** capitalized; usually

   **Example:** They deserve to be inducted into a Congressional Wall of Shame.

   **Source antonyms:** hall of fame
