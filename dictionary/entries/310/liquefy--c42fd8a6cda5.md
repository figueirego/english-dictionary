### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make (something) into a liquid.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** We’ll liquefy this rock by heating it in a furnace until it melts and flows out.

   **Source synonyms:** liquidate; liquidize; melt

   **Source antonyms:** condense; freeze; solidify

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To distort and warp (an image).

   **Usage:** broadly; transitive; image manipulation; especially Adobe Photoshop; image manipulation; especially Adobe Photoshop

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To become liquid.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The substance liquefied upon heating.
