### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In writing, particularly handwritten or printed as opposed to digital.

   **Example:** During the final exam, students are allowed to bring their computer science notes that are on paper.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Based on debatable inference; in theory; by appearances only.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** On paper they're perfect for each other, but it's been six months and their relationship is already on the rocks.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

   **Usage:** figuratively
