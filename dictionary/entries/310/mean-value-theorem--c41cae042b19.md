## Proper noun · Record 1

### Forms

- the mean value theorem — canonical

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The theorem that for any real-valued function that is differentiable on an interval, there is a point in that interval where the derivative of the curve equals the slope of the straight line between the graphed function values at the interval's end points.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** calculus; mathematics; sciences

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** Lagrange's mean value theorem; mean value theorem for derivatives
