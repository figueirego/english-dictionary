## Adjective · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of or pertaining to Christopher Columbus, an explorer who introduced greatly widened contacts between Eurasia and the Americas, or his life or time.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Columbian contact \[the events, from 1492 to 1504, that ended the precolumbian era\]

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of or from any of the places called Columbia.
   1. American, from the United States of America.

      **Usage:** dated; not-comparable; poetic

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of or from any of the places called Columbia.
   1. Misspelling of Colombian.

      **Usage:** alt-of; misspelling; not-comparable

## Noun · Record 2

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. An inhabitant of any place called Columbia.
   1. An American, a person from the United States of America.

      **Usage:** countable; dated; poetic; uncountable

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. An inhabitant of any place called Columbia.
   1. Misspelling of Colombian.

      **Usage:** alt-of; countable; misspelling; uncountable

      **Alternative form of:** [Colombian](../0f0/colombian--3c3837ad9659.md)

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
