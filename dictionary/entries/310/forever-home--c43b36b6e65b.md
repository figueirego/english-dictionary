## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A permanent home found for a child or animal who is to be adopted.

   **Example:** We were so glad to rescue Jezebel from the shelter and bring her to her forever home.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A home in which a resident settles with the expectation of living there for the rest of their life.

   **Example:** We put in the strongest bid that we could, because we wanted the home so bad — it felt like our forever home from the first moment we stepped inside.

   **Source synonyms:** forever house
