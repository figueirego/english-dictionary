### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of various villages in England.
   1. A village and civil parish in Huntingdonshire district, Cambridgeshire (OS grid ref TL1987).

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of various villages in England.
   1. A village and civil parish in Westmorland and Furness, Cumbria, previously in South Lakeland district (OS grid ref SD5278).

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
