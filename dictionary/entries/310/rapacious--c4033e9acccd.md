### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Voracious; avaricious.

   **Usage:** also; figuratively

   **Source synonyms:** acquisitive; avaricious; cormorant (attributive; obsolete); covetous; edacious; esurient; grasping; greedy; miserable (obsolete); piggish; miserly; rapacious; selfish; sordid; sharp-set; voracious

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Given to taking by force or plundering; aggressively greedy.

   **Example:** The natives were driven out by rapacious colonists.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Subsisting off live prey.

   **Usage:** usually a bird

   **Source context (verbatim):** of an animal

   **Source synonyms:** predacious; predatory
