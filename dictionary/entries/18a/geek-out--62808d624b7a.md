### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To enthuse about a specific topic; to be engrossed in an interest, especially in discussing it with others.

   **Usage:** intransitive; slang

   **Example:** Sorry, I didn't mean to spend fifteen minutes discussing the definition of "decimate". I guess I geeked out for a bit.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To do geeky things; to act geeky.

   **Usage:** intransitive; slang

   **Example:** Hey guys, let's geek out tonight and have a code-a-thon!

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To be scared, freak out, panic; to hesitate in fear.

   **Usage:** intransitive; slang

   **Example:** I geeked out there for a minute and broke the lockpick.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To scare or freak out (somebody).

   **Usage:** slang; transitive
