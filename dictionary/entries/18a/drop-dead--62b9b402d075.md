## Adverb · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Very impressively or spectacularly.

   **Usage:** informal; not-comparable

   **Example:** My date was drop-dead gorgeous!

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Absolutely final; being the deadline, especially in relation to a date set out in a contract upon which adverse consequences will flow if the contract has not been fulfilled.

   **Usage:** informal; not-comparable

   **Example:** drop-dead date

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; law
