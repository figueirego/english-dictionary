### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. With to as particle

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. With to as particle&#58;
   1. To apply oneself; to focus; to get one's head down.

      **Usage:** intransitive

      **Example:** All hands, turn to!

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. With to as preposition&#58;
   1. To become.

      **Usage:** intransitive

      **Example:** all things turn to dust

      **Example:** Iron turns to rust in the presence of air and water.

      **Source synonyms:** turn into; convert; change; transform; get (intransitive); turn; go; come; fall; grow; wax; make; make into

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. With to as preposition&#58;
   1. To cause (something or someone) to become.

      **Usage:** transitive

      **Example:** He turned water to wine.

      **Source synonyms:** turn into; convert; change; transform; make (transitive); make into

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. With to as preposition&#58;
   1. To consult for advice or help.

      **Example:** When I'm in trouble, I turn to my pastor to help me.

      **Source synonyms:** run to; look to

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. With to as preposition&#58;
   1. To start to do or to use something in an attempt to deal with a difficult and unpleasant situation.

      **Example:** Breakups can be incredibly difficult, but one may wish to seek therapy rather than turn to drugs.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. With to as preposition&#58;
   1. To direct one's attention or efforts toward something.

      **Example:** In 1939 he turned to films in earnest.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. With to as preposition&#58;
   1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see turn, to.

      **Example:** Turn to the left up here.

      **Example:** To start my homework, I had to turn to page twenty in my textbook.
