### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The property of a word that sounds like what it represents.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A word that sounds like what it represents, such as gurgle, stutter, or hiss.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A word that sounds like what it represents, such as gurgle, stutter, or hiss.
   1. A word that appropriates a sound for another sensation or a perceived nature, such as thud, beep, or meow; an ideophone, phenomime.

      **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The use of language whose sound imitates that which it names.

   **Usage:** rhetoric; uncountable
