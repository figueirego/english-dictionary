### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The branch of geometry dealing with what can be deduced in Euclidean geometry when the notions of line length and angle size are ignored.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** As an alternative to the axiomatic approach, affine geometry can be studied via the properties of affine transformations, which do not, in general, preserve distances or angles, but do preserve alignment of points and parallelism of lines.

   **Example:** The notion of parallelism remains central to affine geometry, in which the parallel postulate is replaced by Playfair's axiom, a version of the postulate that relies on neither distance nor angle size.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A geometry that is otherwise Euclidean but disregards lengths and angle sizes.

   **Usage:** countable
