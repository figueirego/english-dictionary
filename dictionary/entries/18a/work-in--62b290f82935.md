### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To find time or space for, amid other things.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** It'll be a tight schedule, but we can work you in.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To work out using a machine in between the sets of someone who is already using that machine.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Is it okay if I work in while you rest?

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see work, in.

   **Example:** Next, you have to work the eggs in the batter.

   **Example:** He works in cybersecurity.
