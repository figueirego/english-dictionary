2. An obligation, debt or responsibility owed to someone.
   1. Any kind of debt recorded on the right-hand side of a balance sheet.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** accounting; business; finance

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
