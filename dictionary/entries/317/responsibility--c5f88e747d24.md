### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The state of being responsible, accountable, or answerable.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Responsibility is a heavy burden.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The state of being liable, culpable, or responsible for something in particular.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** guilt

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A duty, obligation or liability for which someone is held accountable.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Why didn't you clean the house? That was your responsibility!

   **Source synonyms:** place; charge

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A duty, obligation or liability for which someone is held accountable.
   1. The obligation to carry forward an assigned task to a successful conclusion.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source synonyms:** place; charge

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A duty, obligation or liability for which someone is held accountable.
   1. The obligation for the proper custody, care, and safekeeping of property or funds entrusted to someone’s possession or supervision.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source synonyms:** place; charge
