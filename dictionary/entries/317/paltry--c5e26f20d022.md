### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Trashy, trivial, of little value.

   **Example:** This is indeed a paltry flyer about a silly product.

   **Example:** She made some paltry excuse and left.

   **Source synonyms:** insignificant; unimportant; petty; trivial; beggarly

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of little monetary worth.

   **Example:** Could someone hope to survive on such a paltry income?

   **Example:** Student grants these days are paltry, and many students have to take out loans.

   **Source synonyms:** meager; worthless; pitiful; trifling

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Despicable; contemptibly unimportant.

   **Example:** a paltry coward
