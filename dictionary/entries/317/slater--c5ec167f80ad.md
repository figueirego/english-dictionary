### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who lays slates, or whose occupation is to slate buildings.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any terrestrial isopod crustacean of the genus Porcellio and allied genera; a woodlouse.

   **Usage:** Australia; New-Zealand; Scotland
