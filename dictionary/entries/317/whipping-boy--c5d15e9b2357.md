### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A boy who was whipped in the stead of a misbehaving prince in early modern Europe.

   **Usage:** historical

   **Example:** As the whipping boy, he must take the whippings for the royal heir, Prince Brat.
