### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The recording of speed or changes in speed, including the rate of movement or flow.

   **Extractor diagnostics (not usage labels):** no-gloss

**Supplemental definition (evidence reviewed; source gap preserved):** Independent primary research and historical medical terminology both explicitly attest the noun as speed or flow-rate recording.
