### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. That has been translated into the Latin language.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of a word, made similar in appearance or form to a Latin word.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. That has been transliterated into the characters of the Latin script; Romanized.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>
