## Noun

### Forms

- second fiddles — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A fiddle part in harmony to the first fiddle.

   **Example:** The second fiddle on that tune is almost as hard as the first fiddle.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The person playing second fiddle.

   **Example:** I've been second fiddle in that orchestra for ten years now.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A sidekick or subordinate, or the role of such a person.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I'm tired of being your second fiddle!
