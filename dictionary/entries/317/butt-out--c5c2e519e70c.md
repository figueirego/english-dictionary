### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To stop interfering; mind one's own business.

   **Usage:** Canada; US; derogatory; intransitive; slang

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To tell someone rudely to leave them alone or not get involved in what they're doing.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; imperative

   **Example:** The government should butt out of people's private lives.
