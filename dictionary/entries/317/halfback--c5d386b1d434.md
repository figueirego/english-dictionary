### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of various positions on the field of play between the forwards and the fullbacks.

   **Usage:** field sports

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A player who occupies one of these positions.
   1. An offensive back whose primary jobs are to carry the ball on running plays and to serve as a receiver or blocker on passing plays.

      **Usage:** field sports

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** American-football; ball-games; football; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A player who occupies one of these positions.
   1. A defensive back whose primary jobs are to cover the slotback on passing plays and prevent running plays from going to the outside.

      **Usage:** Canadian; field sports

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; football; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A player who occupies one of these positions.
   1. A player in the halfback position, with both offensive and defensive roles.

      **Usage:** field sports; field hockey; field hockey

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A player who occupies one of these positions.
   1. One of three players (the centre halfback and two halfback flankers) on the line between the centre line and the fullback line.

      **Usage:** field sports; Australian rules football; Australian rules football
