### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To yield assent; to accord; to acquiesce, agree, consent; to adapt oneself, to conform.

   **Example:** comply with rules

   **Example:** comply fully

   **Example:** failure to comply

   **Example:** All workers must comply with safety regulations.

   **Example:** He refused to comply with their demands.

   **Source synonyms:** give way; humor; accede; agree; allow; cave in; come around (idiomatic); come round (idiomatic); comply; concede; give way; grant; soothe (obsolete); yield (rare)

   **Source antonyms:** [violate](../203/violate--80c3675033fd.md)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To accomplish, to fulfil.

   **Usage:** archaic

   **Source synonyms:** carry out; consummate

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To be ceremoniously courteous; to make one's compliments.

   **Usage:** archaic

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To enfold; to embrace.

   **Usage:** archaic
