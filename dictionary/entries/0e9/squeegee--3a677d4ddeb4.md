2. A tool for scraping consisting of a blade of rubber or some other material attached at a right angle to a handle.
   1. A long-handled tool with a blade used for cleaning and/or drying surfaces, or for levelling paths, roadways, etc.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A tool for scraping consisting of a blade of rubber or some other material attached at a right angle to a handle.
   1. A short-handled tool with a blade for drying car windshields, windows, etc.

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A tool for scraping consisting of a blade of rubber or some other material attached at a right angle to a handle.
   1. A long-handled tool with a blade used on ships for swabbing decks and spreading protective coatings.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

      **Source synonyms:** squilgee

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A tool consisting of a roller attached to a handle, used for applying pressure, removing liquid, etc.
   1. A street-cleaning machine consisting of a roller with blades pulled by a horse.

      **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A tool consisting of a roller attached to a handle, used for applying pressure, removing liquid, etc.
   1. A tool used to press film into a mount, remove excess moisture from a print, etc.; a squeezer.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** arts; hobbies; lifestyle; photography

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A tool consisting of a roller attached to a handle, used for applying pressure, removing liquid, etc.
   1. A tool with a roller or blade used to force ink through a stencil in silk-screen printing.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** media; printing; publishing

**Source sense R1.S8**
