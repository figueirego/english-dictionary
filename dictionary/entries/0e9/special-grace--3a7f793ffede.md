### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In Reformed theology&#58; the grace by which God redeems, sanctifies, and glorifies His people. Unlike common grace, which is universally given, special grace is bestowed only on those whom God elects to eternal life through faith in Jesus Christ.
