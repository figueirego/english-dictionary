### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An ancient torture device, supposed to have been devised by Perillos of Athens, consisting of a hollow bull fashioned from brass in which victims were imprisoned and burned to death.

   **Source synonyms:** bronze bull; Sicilian bull
