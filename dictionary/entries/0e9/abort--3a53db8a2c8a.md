<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Example:** We've had aborts on three of our last seven launches.

**Source sense R1.S3**

   **Example:** We've had three aborts over the last two days.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /əˈbɔːt/ — source labels: Received-Pronunciation
- IPA: /əˈbɔɹt/ — source labels: US

### Forms

- aborting — participle; present
- aborted — participle; past

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To miscarry; to bring forth (non-living) offspring prematurely.

   **Usage:** intransitive; regional

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To cause a premature termination of (a fetus); to end a pregnancy before term.

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To end prematurely; to stop in the preliminary stages; to turn back.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To stop or fail at something in the preliminary stages.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To become checked in normal development, so as either to remain rudimentary or shrink away wholly; to cease organic growth before maturation; to become sterile.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To cause an organism to develop minimally; to cause rudimentary development to happen; to prevent maturation.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

**Source sense R2.S7**

7. To abandon a mission at any point after the beginning of the mission and prior to its completion.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war

<a id="r2-s8"></a>

**Source sense R2.S8**

8. To terminate a mission involving a missile or rocket; to destroy a missile or rocket prematurely.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** aeronautics; aerospace; business; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r2-s9"></a>

**Source sense R2.S9**

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences
