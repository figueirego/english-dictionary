### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To remove from on board a vessel; to put on shore

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The general disembarked the troops.

   **Source synonyms:** land; debark

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To go ashore out of a ship or boat; to leave a train or aircraft.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source synonyms:** debark; alight; get off; get out

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To go ashore from (a boat); to leave (a train or aircraft)

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** We disembarked the ferry.
