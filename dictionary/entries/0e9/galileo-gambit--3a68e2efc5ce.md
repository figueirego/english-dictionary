### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Purposeful attraction of criticism or ridicule for the purpose of portraying one's argument as oppressed and therefore correct. Also, the argument that one is correct due to such opposition.

   **Example:** That old liar pulled a Galileo gambit and made sure everyone would hate him so he could claim he was just a persecuted free thinker.

   **Source synonyms:** Galileo fallacy
