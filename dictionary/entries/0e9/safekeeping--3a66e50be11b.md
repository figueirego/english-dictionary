## Noun

### Forms

- safekeepings — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. the act of keeping something safe; protection from harm, damage, loss, or theft

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. the storage of assets in a protected area

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** I locked the documents in my bureau for safekeeping.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. the responsibility of a guardian

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. not returning checks; a system wherein banks keep checks (cheques) that people write, rather than returning them to the account holder with their monthly statement.

   **Usage:** US; uncountable; usually
