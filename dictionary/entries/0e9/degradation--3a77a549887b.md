## Noun

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˌdɛɡɹəˈdeɪʃən/ — source labels: Received-Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of reducing in rank, character, or reputation, or of abasing; a lowering from one's standing or rank in office or society

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** abasement

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The state of being reduced in rank, character, or reputation; baseness; moral, physical, or intellectual degeneracy; disgrace; abasement; debasement.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** debauchment; depravement; perversion; abasement; adulteration; corruption; debasement; debauchment; defilement; depravation; depravement (archaic); impurification; perversion; pollution (rare); profanation

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Diminution or reduction of strength, efficacy, or value; degeneration; deterioration.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** debasement; worsening; aggravation; corruption; debasement; degeneration; degradation; deterioration; devolution; embasement (obsolete); exacerbation; ruining; withering; worsening; wrecking

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A gradual wearing down or wasting, as of rocks and banks, by the action of water, frost etc.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; geology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A deleterious change in the chemical structure, physical properties or appearance of a material from natural or artificial exposure.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The state or condition of a species or group which exhibits degraded forms; degeneration.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Arrest of development, or degeneration of any organ, or of the body as a whole.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** muscle degradation

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. The gradual breakdown of components of a material, as a result of a natural element, i.e.&#58; heat, cold and wind.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
