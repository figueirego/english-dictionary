### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Charles Darwin's theory regarding the evolution of living organisms through natural selection (set out chiefly in his works On the Origin of Species, 1859; and The Descent of Man, and Selection in Relation to Sex, 1871); also, belief in this theory.

   **Usage:** uncountable; evolutionary theory

   **Source synonyms:** Darwinianism

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Charles Darwin's theory regarding the evolution of living organisms through natural selection (set out chiefly in his works On the Origin of Species, 1859; and The Descent of Man, and Selection in Relation to Sex, 1871); also, belief in this theory.
   1. Short for neo-Darwinism (“the synthesis of Charles Darwin's theory of evolution through natural selection with the modern genetic understanding of heredity”).

      **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; uncountable; evolutionary theory

      **Source synonyms:** Darwinianism

      **Alternative form of:** [neo-Darwinism](../0a6/neo-darwinism--29af6f8fac4d.md)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Charles Darwin's theory regarding the evolution of living organisms through natural selection (set out chiefly in his works On the Origin of Species, 1859; and The Descent of Man, and Selection in Relation to Sex, 1871); also, belief in this theory.
   1. Chiefly preceded by a descriptive word.
      1. Any of various theories in biology which apply aspects of Darwin's theory (noun sense 1) such as adaptation, competition, or gradual evolution; also, belief in such a theory.

         **Usage:** broadly; uncountable; evolutionary theory

         **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences

         **Source synonyms:** Darwinianism

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Charles Darwin's theory regarding the evolution of living organisms through natural selection (set out chiefly in his works On the Origin of Species, 1859; and The Descent of Man, and Selection in Relation to Sex, 1871); also, belief in this theory.
   1. Chiefly preceded by a descriptive word.
      1. Any of various theories, now generally discredited, which apply aspects of Darwin's theory (noun sense 1) to other situations such as the development of ideas, organizations, or social groups.

         **Usage:** broadly; uncountable; evolutionary theory

         **Example:** social Darwinism

         **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** pseudoscience

         **Source synonyms:** Darwinianism

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Charles Darwin's theory regarding the evolution of living organisms through natural selection (set out chiefly in his works On the Origin of Species, 1859; and The Descent of Man, and Selection in Relation to Sex, 1871); also, belief in this theory.
   1. Chiefly preceded by a descriptive word.
      1. A process of gradual evolution; also, ruthless competition for achievement or survival.

         **Usage:** broadly; countable; uncountable; evolutionary theory; generally; generally

         **Source synonyms:** Darwinianism

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Erasmus Darwin's poetic style, or theory of natural philosophy suggesting that living organisms developed from simpler lifeforms (set out in his work Zoonomia, 1794–1796).

   **Usage:** countable; historical; poetic; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences

   **Source synonyms:** Darwinianism
