### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To remove a stoppage; to clear a blockage.

   **Example:** I was able to unstop the clogged toilet by using a plunger.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To unplug or uncork a container.

   **Example:** I unstopped a bottle of wine to celebrate.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To draw out the stops of (an organ).
