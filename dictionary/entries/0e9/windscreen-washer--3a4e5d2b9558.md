### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A device that pumps water mixed with detergent from a tank through small nozzles onto the windscreen (the windshield) of a vehicle (e.g. car, van, lorry, train, aircraft, boat) so as to clean it.

   **Usage:** UK

   **Source synonyms:** wet arm system; windshield washer; spritzer; schpritzer
