### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Synonym of that's that; there is nothing more to say or to do concerning the matter and is not up for discussion or debate.

   **Usage:** humorous; idiomatic

   **Example:** In the middle of our argument, my cousin left without saying another word, so I guess that's that on that.
