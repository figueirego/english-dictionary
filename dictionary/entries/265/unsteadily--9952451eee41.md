### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In an unsteady manner, so as to shake or falter.

   **Example:** He climbed the stairs unsteadily, grasping the banister and gasping for breath.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In an uncertain manner; with hesitation or timidity.
