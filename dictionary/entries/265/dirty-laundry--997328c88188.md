## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Unflattering or embarrassing facts, or questionable activities that one wants to remain secret, but which someone else may use as blackmail.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; uncountable

   **Example:** If you don't tell me why you did it, I'll air all your dirty laundry to your boss.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A clothes hamper or other container in which soiled laundry is placed.

   **Usage:** metonymically; uncountable

   **Example:** Place your dirty clothes in the dirty laundry.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see dirty, laundry.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** If you bring your dirty laundry round on Saturday, I'll wash it for you.
