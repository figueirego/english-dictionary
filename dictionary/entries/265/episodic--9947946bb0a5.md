### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Relating to an episode.

   **Source synonyms:** episodical

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Sporadic, happening infrequently and irregularly.

   **Example:** Fortunately, my episodic bouts of dizziness didn't prevent me from climbing Chichen Itzá.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Consisting of a sequence of seemingly unconnected episodes.

   **Example:** I just read five attempts at episodic novels — has nobody heard of a plot anymore?
