### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Unable to clearly distinguish the difference in pitch between different notes.

   **Example:** Although he was tone-deaf, he was a great lyricist.

   **Example:** This is a distinction unavailable to the tone-deaf.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Having little or a shallow or banal appreciation of music, whether or not as a result of tone deafness.

   **Usage:** colloquial

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Out of touch with the experience of ordinary people.

   **Usage:** colloquial; figuratively

   **Example:** a politician's tone-deaf remarks about the price of bread

   **Example:** The elected official's tone-deaf remarks about the operations of the clerk’s office were disappointing.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Insensitive and offensive as a result of not noticing the current social context.

   **Usage:** colloquial; figuratively
