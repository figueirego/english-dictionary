### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The innermost line near the edge of a printed map which separates it from the margin.

   **Example:** The horizontal neatlines were formed by lines of latitude.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** cartography; geography; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The line on plan to which a structure is to be built, usually ignoring any small irregularities of the building material.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; construction; manufacturing

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
