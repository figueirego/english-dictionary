### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To entertain or occupy (someone or something) in a pleasant manner; to stir (someone) with pleasing emotions.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I watch these movies because they amuse me.

   **Example:** It always amuses me to hear the funny stories why people haven't got a ticket, but I never let them get in without paying.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To cause laughter or amusement; to be funny.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** His jokes rarely fail to amuse me.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To keep in expectation; to beguile; to delude.

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To occupy or engage the attention of; to lose in deep thought; to absorb; also, to distract; to bewilder.

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** entertain; gratify; please; divert; beguile; exhilarate; slay
