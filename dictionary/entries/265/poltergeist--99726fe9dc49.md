**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An unseen ghost which makes noises and causes disruption, especially by causing physical objects to move or fly about.

   **Example:** A poltergeist haunts the house by moving objects around, making chain-rattling noises and throwing things.

   **Example:** The chapel is haunted by a demonic poltergeist.
