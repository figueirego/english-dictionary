### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To express sadness or sorrow for; to grieve over (especially a death).

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** For two months, she mourned her father's passing.

   **Example:** She mourned the loss of her independence.

   **Source synonyms:** grieve; lament; weep; aggrieve (obsolete); besorrow; condole (obsolete); darken; engrieve (archaic); [erme](../195/erme--657a1ca5b9f1.md) (obsolete); gloom; grieve; grieven; bleed; lament; moan (poetic); mourn; remorse; repent; regret; rue (archaic); sigh; sorrow; wayment (obsolete); yearn (obsolete); afterthink; becry; begrieve (archaic; rare); bemoan; bemourn (rare); bewail; beweep; condole (India); deplore; elegize; grieve; grieven; keen; lament; mean (Ireland; UK); mourn; pine; repent; sigh; sorrow; wail; wayment (obsolete)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To engage in the social customs of mourning; to commemorate a death and/or honour the deceased socially.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** In that society, a spouse is mourned for one year, a parent for six months.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To utter in a sorrowful manner.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To wear mourning.

   **Usage:** intransitive
