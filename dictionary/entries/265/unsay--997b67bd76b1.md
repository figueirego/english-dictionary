### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To withdraw, retract (something said).

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To cause something not to have been said; to make it so that one never said something (since this is physically impossible, usually in the subjunctive).

   **Example:** I wish I could unsay that.

   **Example:** There are some things I'd like to unsay... to my boss... right before he decided to fire me.
