### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To leave a place quickly or briefly.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** I have to dash off to the store, but I'll be back soon.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To write quickly or informally.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** Let me dash off a quick note.

   **Source synonyms:** scribble off
