### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The study of the characteristics of human populations, especially with regards to their makeup and fluctuations and the social causes behind these.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually
