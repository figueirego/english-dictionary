### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To endure (a hardship or challenge) by being tough&#58; relying on one's toughness.

   **Usage:** informal; transitive

   **Example:** When their main crop failed because of drought, they had no choice but to tough it out&#58; they lived in poverty that winter.

   **Example:** He toughed out his parents' divorce.

   **Source synonyms:** tough it out; face out; stick it out
