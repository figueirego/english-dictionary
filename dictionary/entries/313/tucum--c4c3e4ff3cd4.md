### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A fine, strong fibre obtained from the young leaves of a Brazilian palm (Astrocaryum vulgare), used for cordage, bowstrings, etc.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
