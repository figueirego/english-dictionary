### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To demand, to insist upon (having); to call for authoritatively.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Naturally to demand (something) as indispensable; to need, to call for as necessary.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To demand (of someone) to do something.

   **Example:** It is required (of me) that I give evidence in court.

   **Source synonyms:** provide

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**
