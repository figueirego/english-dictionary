### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any turbulent, frothy water as found in river rapids or surf.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** The boat's wake left two strands of white water that glistened in the moonlight.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The turbid, sediment-rich water of certain rivers.

   **Usage:** uncountable
