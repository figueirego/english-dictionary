### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A measured portion of medicine taken at any one time.

   **Example:** Why did he give you only a single 500 mg dose? The correct dosage of this antibiotic is one 500 mg tablet twice a day for 10 days.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The quantity of an agent (not always active), substance, or radiation administered or experienced at any one time.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Anything disagreeable that must be taken.

   **Usage:** dated; figuratively

   **Source synonyms:** fill

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A good measure or lengthy experience of something.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** a healthy dose of skepticism.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A venereal infection.

   **Usage:** dated

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A cold; a common, viral illness of the nasal passage, sometimes with fever.

   **Usage:** Ireland; colloquial

   **Example:** There's a dose going round.
