### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A type of domestic pea grown for use as a dry seed rather than as a vegetable.
   1. Any of certain varieties of Pisum sativum (formerly Lathyrus oleraceus).

      **Source synonyms:** maple; maple pea

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A type of domestic pea grown for use as a dry seed rather than as a vegetable.
   1. Any of certain varieties of Vigna unguiculata.

      **Source synonyms:** cowpea
