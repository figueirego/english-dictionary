## Noun

### Forms

- field days — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A day for maneuvers and tactical exercises in the field (across the landscape).

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A school day for athletic events.

   **Usage:** US

   **Source synonyms:** sports day

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A day of class taken away from school for a field trip.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A great time or a great deal to do; a period of bustling activity.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** They went to the park and had a field day playing on the swings.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A great time or a great deal to do, at somebody else's expense.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** The reporters will have a field day with a comment like that.

   **Example:** The scandal was a field day for the press.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A day on which there is top-to-bottom all-hands cleaning.

   **Usage:** Navy; US; specifically; US Coast Guard and US Marine Corps

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war
