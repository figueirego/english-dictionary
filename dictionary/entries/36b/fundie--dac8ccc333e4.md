### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A fundamentalist Christian.

   **Usage:** derogatory; slang

   **Example:** Some fundies are picketing at the capitol.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A religious fundamentalist of any faith.

   **Usage:** broadly; derogatory; slang

   **Example:** He's a Jewish fundie, keeps everything really kosher.
