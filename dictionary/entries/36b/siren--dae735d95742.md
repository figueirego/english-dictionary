### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One of a group of nymphs who lured mariners to their death on the rocks.

   **Usage:** Greek

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; mysticism; mythology; philosophy; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One who sings sweetly and charms.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A dangerously seductive woman.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A member of Sirenia, an order of mammals.

   **Source synonyms:** sirenian

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A member of a genus of aquatic salamanders of the family Sirenidae, commonly used for all species in the family Sirenidae.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Any of various nymphalid butterflies of the genus Hestina.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A device, either mechanical or electronic, that makes a piercingly loud sound as an alarm or signal, or the sound from such a device.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A musical instrument, one of the few aerophones in the percussion section of the symphony orchestra (patented as Acme Siren in 1895).

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. An instrument for demonstrating the laws of beats and combination tones.

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. An astrophysical event that can be used for calculating cosmic distances.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astronomy; astrophysics; natural-sciences
