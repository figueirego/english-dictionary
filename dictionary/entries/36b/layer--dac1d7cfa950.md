**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A single thickness of some material covering a surface.

   **Example:** Wrap the loaf in two layers of aluminum foil before putting it in the oven.

   **Example:** After the first coat of paint dried, he applied another layer.

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A single thickness of some material covering a surface.
   1. An item of clothing worn under or over another.

      **Example:** It's cold now but it will warm up this afternoon. Make sure you wear layers.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A (usually) horizontal deposit; a stratum.

   **Example:** I find seven-layer cake a bit too rich.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. One of the items in a hierarchy.

   **Example:** mired in layers of deceit

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. One in a stack of (initially transparent) drawing surfaces that comprise an image; used to keep elements of an image separate so that they can be modified independently from one another.

## Noun · Etymology 2 · Record 3

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈleɪ.ə/ — source labels: Received-Pronunciation
- IPA: /ˈleɪ.ɚ/ — source labels: General-American

### Forms

- layers — plural

### Senses

<a id="r3-s1"></a>

**Source sense R3.S1**

1. A person who lays anything, such as tiles or a wager.

<a id="r3-s2"></a>

**Source sense R3.S2**

2. A mature female bird, insect, etc. that is able to lay eggs.

   **Example:** As for which hen we'll cull for next Sunday's dinner, we certainly won't eat Henrietta yet — she's still a prime layer.

   **Example:** When dealing with an infestation of headlice, the first step is to eliminate the layers.

<a id="r3-s3"></a>

**Source sense R3.S3**

3. A mature female bird, insect, etc. that is able to lay eggs.
   1. A hen kept to lay eggs; a breed of chicken bred to maximize laying output.

      **Example:** We keep a flock of layers and flock of broilers.

<a id="r3-s4"></a>

**Source sense R3.S4**

4. A shoot of a plant, laid underground for growth.
