## Noun

### Forms

- traditional counties — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A county without direct administrative functions. e.g. Yorkshire, Perthshire, Dublin or Suffolk County, Massachusetts.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. any of the historic divisions of Great Britain no longer used for administrative purposes in favour of ceremonial counties and administrative counties

   **Usage:** UK

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. any of the historical sub-national divisions used for the purposes of geographic demarcation, local government, and sport.

   **Usage:** Ireland
