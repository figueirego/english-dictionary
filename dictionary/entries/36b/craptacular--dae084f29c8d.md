**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Spectacularly awful; so poor in quality as to become a spectacle.

   **Usage:** US; slang; vulgar

   **Example:** The movie was so craptacular that it generated legions of ironic fans who watched it over and over solely to mock it mercilessly.
