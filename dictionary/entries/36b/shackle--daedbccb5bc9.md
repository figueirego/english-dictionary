## Noun · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A restraint fitted over a human or animal appendage, such as an ankle, finger, or wrist, normally used in a pair joined by a chain.

   **Usage:** plural-normally

   **Example:** The prisoner lay in shackles in his gloomy cell.

   **Source synonyms:** fetter

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A restraint fitted over a human or animal appendage, such as an ankle, finger, or wrist, normally used in a pair joined by a chain.
   1. A U-shaped piece of metal secured with a bolt or pin across the ends, or a hinged metal loop secured with a quick-release locking pin mechanism, used for attaching things together while allowing for some degree of movement; a clevis.

      **Usage:** broadly; plural-normally

      **Source synonyms:** fetter

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A restraint fitted over a human or animal appendage, such as an ankle, finger, or wrist, normally used in a pair joined by a chain.
   1. Part of a padlock that consists of a loop of metal (round or square in cross section) that encompasses what is being secured by the lock.

      **Usage:** broadly; plural-normally

      **Source synonyms:** fetter

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A restraint fitted over a human or animal appendage, such as an ankle, finger, or wrist, normally used in a pair joined by a chain.
   1. A hook, ring, or other device for connecting, holding, lifting, etc.; specifically (nautical), a small incomplete ring secured with a bolt across the ends, used to connect lengths of cable or chain together, or to keep a porthole closed.

      **Usage:** broadly; plural-normally

      **Source synonyms:** fetter

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A restraint fitted over a human or animal appendage, such as an ankle, finger, or wrist, normally used in a pair joined by a chain.
   1. Synonym of hobble or hopple (“a short strap tied between the legs of a horse, allowing it to wander a short distance but not to run off”).

      **Usage:** broadly; plural-normally

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** agriculture; business; lifestyle

      **Source synonyms:** fetter; hobble (synonym; synonym-of); hopple (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A restraint fitted over a human or animal appendage, such as an ankle, finger, or wrist, normally used in a pair joined by a chain.
   1. A link for connecting railroad cars; a draglink, drawbar, or drawlink.

      **Usage:** broadly; plural-normally

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** rail-transport; railways; transport

      **Source synonyms:** fetter; dragbar

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A restraint fitted over a human or animal appendage, such as an ankle, finger, or wrist, normally used in a pair joined by a chain.
   1. Ellipsis of shackle-bone (“wristbone, carpal; wrist”).

      **Usage:** Scotland; abbreviation; alt-of; archaic; broadly; ellipsis; plural-normally

      **Source synonyms:** fetter

      **Alternative form of:** [shackle-bone](../2db/shackle-bone--b6c51d4a2f81.md)

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A restraint fitted over a human or animal appendage, such as an ankle, finger, or wrist, normally used in a pair joined by a chain.
   1. A fetter-like band worn on an appendage as an ornament; an anklet, an armlet, a bracelet, a wristlet.

      **Usage:** broadly; obsolete; plural-normally; rare

      **Source synonyms:** fetter

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A restraint on one's action, activity, or progress.

   **Usage:** figuratively; plural-normally

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. A length of cable or chain equal to 12½ fathoms (75 feet or about 22.9 metres), or later to 15 fathoms (90 feet or about 27.4 metres).

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport
