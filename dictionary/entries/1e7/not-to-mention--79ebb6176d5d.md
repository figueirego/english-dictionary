## Conjunction

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An apophasis used by a speaker to introduce, despite claiming not to address, a given point of discussion.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** The city was already having staffing difficulties, not to mention having a three million dollar budget shortfall.
