## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /deɪn/ — source labels: UK

### Forms

- Danes — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person from Denmark or of Danish descent.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In Anglo-Saxon England, any of the seafaring raiders and settlers who attacked and colonized parts of England from the late 8th century onward; a Viking.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A member of a Germanic tribe inhabiting the Danish islands and parts of southern Sweden.

   **Usage:** historical

## Proper noun · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /deɪn/ — source labels: UK

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A surname transferred from the nickname for someone who came from Denmark, also a variant of Dean.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. A male given name transferred from the surname, or from the ethnic term Dane (like Scott or Norman).

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. A river, the River Dane, in Cheshire, England, which joins the River Weaver at Northwich.
