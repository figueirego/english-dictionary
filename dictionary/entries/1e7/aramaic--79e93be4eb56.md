### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A subfamily of languages in the Northwest Semitic language group, including, but not limited to&#58;
   1. The language of the Arameans from the tenth century BC&#58; often called Old Aramaic.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A subfamily of languages in the Northwest Semitic language group, including, but not limited to&#58;
   1. The language of the administration in the Assyrian, Babylonian and Persian empires from the seventh to fourth centuries BC&#58; often called Imperial Aramaic or Official Aramaic.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A subfamily of languages in the Northwest Semitic language group, including, but not limited to&#58;
   1. The language of portions of the Hebrew Bible, mainly the books of Ezra and Daniel&#58; often called Biblical Aramaic.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A subfamily of languages in the Northwest Semitic language group, including, but not limited to&#58;
   1. The language of Jesus of Nazareth&#58; a form of Jewish Palestinian Aramaic or Galilean Aramaic.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A subfamily of languages in the Northwest Semitic language group, including, but not limited to&#58;
   1. The language of Jewish targums, Midrash and the Talmuds, Jewish Babylonian Aramaic.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A subfamily of languages in the Northwest Semitic language group, including, but not limited to&#58;
   1. The liturgical language of various Christian churches&#58; often called Syriac.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A subfamily of languages in the Northwest Semitic language group, including, but not limited to&#58;
   1. The liturgical language of the Mandaeans&#58; usually called Mandaic.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A subfamily of languages in the Northwest Semitic language group, including, but not limited to&#58;
   1. Any language of this family today called Neo-Aramaic, and separated by religion also Judeo-Aramaic and Syriac
