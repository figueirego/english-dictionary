### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To become lost; to lose one's bearings, or idea of where one is.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To cease to succeed or to follow the right path; to fail.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** The band lost their way after their second album, and split up after a string of failures.
