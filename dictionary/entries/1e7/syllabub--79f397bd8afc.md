### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A drink dating back to the 16th century consisting primarily of milk curdled with an alcoholic beverage or some acid such as lemon juice, which is usually then sweetened and spiced.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A dessert pudding derived from the drink.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** everlasting syllabub; solid syllabub

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Something lacking substance; something frothy, insubstantial, or lightweight.

   **Usage:** countable; dated; figuratively; uncountable
