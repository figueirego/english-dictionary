### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To announce one's intent to leave a job; to inform an employer that one is leaving.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** He gave notice yesterday that he'll leave in two weeks.

   **Example:** Did you hear that Jack gave notice today?
