### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The scientific study of the origin and evolution of a word's semantic meaning across time, including its constituent morphemes and phonemes.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The entire catalogue of meanings that a word, morpheme, or sign has carried throughout its history.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** Although written the same, the words lead (the metal) and lead (the verb) have totally different etymologies.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An account of the origin and historical development of a word as presented in a dictionary or the like.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source synonyms:** derivation

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The direct origin of a name, as in who someone was named after.

   **Usage:** countable
