### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A work shift between a day shift and a night shift, such as from 4PM to midnight, and the group of workers scheduled to work such a shift at a facility.

   **Usage:** US

   **Source synonyms:** second shift

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A system of shifts where each worker alternates between day shifts and night shifts.

   **Usage:** US
