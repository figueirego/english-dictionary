## Noun

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /dɪsˌpɛnˈseɪʃən/ — source labels: UK

### Forms

- dispensations — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of dispensing or dealing out; distribution

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** a fair dispensation of money

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The distribution of good and evil by God to man.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. That which is dispensed, dealt out, or given; that which is bestowed on someone

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A system of principles, promises, and rules ordained and administered; scheme; economy

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** the Patriarchal, Mosaic, and Christian dispensations

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The relaxation of a law in a particular case; permission to do something forbidden, or to omit doing something enjoined; exemption.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The relaxation of a law in a particular case; permission to do something forbidden, or to omit doing something enjoined; exemption.
   1. In the Roman Catholic Church, an exemption from some ecclesiastical law, or from an obligation to God which a person has incurred of his own free will (oaths, vows, etc.).

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Catholicism; Christianity
