### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A painting, drawing, sculpture or other piece of creative, visual art.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** A myriad of Hellenic artworks reflects the perceptions of the citizens of Ancient Greece.

   **Source synonyms:** work of art; art piece; piece of art; art object

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** The text has been reviewed, but the photographer hasn't delivered some of the artwork.
