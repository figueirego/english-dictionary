### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Serving the public image which a company seeks to associate with its product.

   **Example:** The use of red and yellow is very on-brand for McDonald's.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; marketing

   **Source antonyms:** off-brand

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Consistent with one's reputation, perhaps to a stereotypical degree.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** A Canadian who loves hockey? I guess that's pretty on-brand.

   **Source antonyms:** off-brand
