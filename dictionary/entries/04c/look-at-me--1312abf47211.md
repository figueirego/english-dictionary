## Interjection

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used to call others' attention to a prideworthy achievement, or (in humor) sarcastically to an embarrassing mishap.

   **Example:** I made it all the way to level 7 without any of your help! Look at me!

   **Example:** Wow, look at me. I can't even boil water without burning my fingers.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used to focus a listener's attention, often followed by a threat of discipline.

   **Example:** Okay, look at me. If you don't have every one of those projects done by the end of the week, you're fired.
