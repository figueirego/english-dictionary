### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A worthless or superficially meaningless post on a message board, newsgroup, or other online discussion platform, often intended to derail or distract from relevant conversation.

   **Usage:** Internet; derogatory; vulgar

   **Source synonyms:** funpost; poast; trashpost

   **Source antonyms:** effortpost

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A seemingly worthless or superficially meaningless image, video or piece of text posted to a social media platform that is intended to be humorously or ironically enjoyed.

   **Usage:** Internet; humorous; vulgar
