### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A name for an inhabitant or native of a specific place, usually derived from the name of the place.

   **Example:** Why is it that people from the United States use American as their demonym?

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A pseudonym formed of an adjective.

   **Usage:** obsolete

   **Example:** The Logophile has my favourite demonym; I would write under it if he didn't.
