## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. That does not work (operate or function).

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source synonyms:** inoperative; nonfunctioning; nonfunctional; out-of-order; out-of-service

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Not employed; employed in unpaid labor, such as homemaking and childcare.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** She works more than 12 hours a day at homemaking and childcare, so it is ironic that she is classified as a nonworking spouse.
