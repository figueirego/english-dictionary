## Verb · Record 2

### Forms

- bleeds out — present; singular; third-person
- bleeding out — participle; present
- bled out — participle; past
- bled out — past
- bleed-out — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To die due to excess blood loss; to bleed to death.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To kill by causing such bloodloss.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To leak out; to spread.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

   **Example:** Most slang begins in small communities, but some words bleed out into wider society over time.
