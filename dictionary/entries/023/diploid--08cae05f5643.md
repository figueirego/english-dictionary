## Adjective · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈdɪploiːʔ/ — source labels: General-American

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of a cell, having a pair of each type of chromosome, one of the pair being derived from the ovum and the other from the spermatozoon. Most somatic cells of higher organisms are diploid.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; genetics; medicine; natural-sciences; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
