### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of or pertaining to clamor.
   1. Of great intensity. (of sounds)

      **Example:** a clamorous fire alarm

      **Source synonyms:** loud

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of or pertaining to clamor.
   1. Creating a loud noise. (of people, animals or things)

      **Example:** clamorous trumpets

      **Source synonyms:** noisy

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of or pertaining to clamor.
   1. Expressed loudly. (of emotions or feelings)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Of or pertaining to clamor.
   1. Filled with or accompanied by a great deal of noise. (of times, places, events or activities)

      **Example:** a clamorous market

      **Source synonyms:** noisy

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Of or pertaining to clamor.
   1. Insistently expressing a desire for something.

      **Source context (verbatim):** of people or speech

      **Source synonyms:** vociferous

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Having especially (and often unpleasantly) bright or contrasting colours or patterns.

   **Source synonyms:** garish; gaudy; loud
