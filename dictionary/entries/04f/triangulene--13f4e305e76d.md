### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A flat hydrocarbon graphene fragment, C₂₂H₁₂, composed of six fused benzene rings in a triangular configuration; being a radical having two unpaired electrons.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; organic-chemistry; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
