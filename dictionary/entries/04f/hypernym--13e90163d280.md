### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A superordinate word or phrase; a term whose referents form a set which includes as a subset the referents of a subordinate term—as, for example, insect (being a hypernym) for ant.

   **Example:** “Musical instrument” is a hypernym of “guitar” because a guitar is a musical instrument&#58; a guitar belongs to a class or type of musical instruments.

   **Example:** Hypernyms of “dog” and “cat” include “mammal”, “homeotherm”, “carnivore”, and “quadruped”.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences; semantics

   **Source synonyms:** hyperonym; blanket term; genus; superordinate; umbrella term

   **Source antonyms:** hyponym; species; subordinate
