### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To become red or pink in the face (and sometimes experience an associated feeling of warmth), especially due to shyness, love, shame, excitement, or embarrassment.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The love scene made him blush to the roots of his hair / to the tips of his ears.

   **Example:** He wasn't used to this much attention, so he blushed as he saw dozens of pairs of eyes watching him.

   **Source synonyms:** color; flush; crimson

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To be shy, ashamed, or embarrassed (to do something).

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To become red or pink.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To suffuse with a blush; to redden; to pinken; to make rosy.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To change skin color in the face (to a particular shade).

   **Usage:** copulative

   **Example:** When he saw it, he blushed a beet red.

   **Example:** I wasn't surprised, but it was embarrassing enough that I blushed a little pink.

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To express or make known by blushing.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Looking at me with a knowing glare, she blushed her discomfort with the situation.

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

**Source sense R2.S7**

7. To have a warm and delicate colour, like some roses and other flowers.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The garden was full of blossoms that blushed in myriad shades to form a beautiful carpet of color.

<a id="r2-s8"></a>

**Source sense R2.S8**

8. To glance with the eye, cast a glance.

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete

<a id="r2-s9"></a>

**Source sense R2.S9**

9. Of dope or varnish&#58; to develop an undesirable white precipitate on the surface, due to being applied in humid conditions.

   **Usage:** intransitive
