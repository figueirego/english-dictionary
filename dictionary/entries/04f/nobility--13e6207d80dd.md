### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A noble or privileged social class, historically accompanied by a hereditary title; aristocracy.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The castle was once home to the Scottish nobility.

   **Example:** Titles of nobility were passed down through generations.

   **Source synonyms:** aristocracy; noblesse; upper class

   **Source antonyms:** plebeian

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The quality of being noble.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** He displayed nobility of character when helping his rivals.

   **Source synonyms:** aristocracy; nobleness; noblesse

   **Source antonyms:** meanness; ignobility
