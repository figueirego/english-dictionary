**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Usage:** US

   **Example:** The woman colorized her hair.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To convert black and white media to color by digital post production (as is often done in digital photography and in video special effects).

   **Usage:** US

   **Example:** There is a colorized version of Casablanca.
