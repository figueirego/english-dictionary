### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An attempt to come to conclusions despite incomplete information and possibly due to a preexisting bias.

   **Example:** Rather than collecting the facts, it was a rush to judgement on the part of the department's leaders.

   **Source synonyms:** jump to conclusions
