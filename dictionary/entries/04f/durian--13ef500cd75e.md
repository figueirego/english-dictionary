<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The spiky edible fruit of this tree, known for its strong taste and very strong, unpleasant odor.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
