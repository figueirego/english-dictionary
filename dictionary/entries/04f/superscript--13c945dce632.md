## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

- superscripts — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A type of lettering form that appears as a number, figure, or symbol above the normal line of type, located at the right or left of another symbol or text.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** In x², the 2 written in superscript means “to the power of two”.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** media; publishing; typography
