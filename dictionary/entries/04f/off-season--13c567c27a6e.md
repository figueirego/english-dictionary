### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The part of the year when business is most slack and there are fewest clients or visitors.

   **Example:** Seaside resorts in the off-season are really depressing.

   **Source synonyms:** low season; white week

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A period of time when regular competitions are not being held, or when an athlete is not participating in competitions.
