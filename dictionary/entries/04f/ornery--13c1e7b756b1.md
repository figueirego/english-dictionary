### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Disagreeable, stubborn, and troublesome to deal with; cantankerous.

   **Usage:** Appalachia; especially; informal

   **Source synonyms:** contrary; crabby; cross-grained; crotchety; honery; ill-tempered; intractable; obnoxious; obstreperous; peevish; stroppy; unsociable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Troublesome to deal with in a good way; mischievous, prankish, teasing.

   **Usage:** US; general; humorous; informal

   **Source synonyms:** full of the devil

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Ordinary, commonplace; hence, inferior, plain-looking, unpleasant.

   **Usage:** obsolete
