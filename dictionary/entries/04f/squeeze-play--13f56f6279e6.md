### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The tactic of bunting the ball to help a runner at third base score.

   **Example:** The squeeze play is on! Jones bunts, and Smith scores from third.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; baseball; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An instance of 'squeezing' someone&#58; a use of pressure or force to achieve one's goal.

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The tactic of running a suit to compel another player to waste potentially winning cards.

   **Example:** Squeeze play is an important part of the endgame.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** bridge; games

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The tactic of reraising a raiser and a caller to compel one or both to fold, often as a bluff.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** card-games; poker
