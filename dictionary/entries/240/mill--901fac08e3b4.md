### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A grinding apparatus for substances such as grains, seeds, etc. (Some are small and simple, and some are large and complex.)

   **Example:** Pepper has a stronger flavor when it is ground straight from a mill.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The building housing such a grinding apparatus; also, any similar building that houses a similarly material activity (such as weaving, fulling, dying, etc.); the place of business comprising such a building and its outbuildings and grounds.

   **Example:** My great-grandfather worked in a mill. He filled the flour sacks and then carried and loaded them.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A machine used for expelling the juice, sap, etc., from vegetable tissues by pressure, or by pressure in combination with a grinding, or cutting process; any similar apparatus that otherwise processes.

   **Example:** a cider mill; a cane mill

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A machine for grinding and polishing.

   **Example:** a lapidary mill

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A milling machine for machining of solid metal, wood, or plastic.

   **Example:** Several machinists were busy operating the lathes, mills, and drill presses on the shop floor.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A milling cutter used on such a machine.

   **Example:** After the drilling and tapping operations, we come in with a mill and do some contouring.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A manufacturing plant for paper, steel, textiles, flooring, and some other kinds of materials.

   **Example:** Once the billet gets through the 56-inch mill, it is ready for reheating.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. The building complex housing such a plant; the place of business comprising such buildings and their grounds.

   **Example:** My grandfather worked in a mill. He operated machinery, did maintenance, and drove forklifts.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. An establishment that handles a certain type of situation or procedure routinely, or produces large quantities of an item without much regard to quality. (The notion of churning out massive amounts indiscriminately underlies the figurative metaphor.)

   **Usage:** derogatory; figuratively; usually

   **Example:** Those shysters selling the purebreds are running quite a mill over there, eh?

   **Example:** Getting the book through the editorial mill could take months.

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. An institution or pseudo-institutional business awarding credentials (such as diplomas, degrees, certificates, or certifications) of either dubious value or fraudulent nature; one selling essays or other documents for the buyers (usually students) to fraudulently pass off as their own.

   **Usage:** derogatory; figuratively

   **Example:** Those shysters cranking out the "IT training certificates" are running quite a mill over there, eh?

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. An engine.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** Under the hood is a mill that you and I can't afford — a nitrous-blown hemi with custom heads.

<a id="r1-s12"></a>

**Source sense R1.S12**

12. A boxing match or fistfight.

   **Usage:** archaic; informal

   **Example:** They were betting big on a great mill, but they didn't realize that the fix was in.

<a id="r1-s13"></a>

**Source sense R1.S13**

13. A hardened steel roller with a design in relief, used for imprinting a reversed copy of the design in a softer metal, such as copper.

   **Usage:** die sinking

<a id="r1-s14"></a>

**Source sense R1.S14**

14. An excavation in rock, transverse to the workings, from which material for filling is obtained.

   **Example:** They pulled me off the main crew and had me digging in the mill.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; mining

<a id="r1-s15"></a>

**Source sense R1.S15**

15. A passage underground through which ore is shot.

   **Example:** The geologists were responsible for keeping the work within the mill.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; mining

   **Source synonyms:** country

<a id="r1-s16"></a>

**Source sense R1.S16**

16. The raised or ridged edge or surface made in milling anything, such as a coin or screw.

   **Source synonyms:** milling

<a id="r1-s17"></a>

**Source sense R1.S17**

17. A prison treadmill.

   **Usage:** historical

   **Example:** They kept us in line by threatening to put us on the mill twice as long if we complained.

<a id="r1-s18"></a>

**Source sense R1.S18**

   **Usage:** US; World-War-I; slang; World War II

   **Example:** He spent some time in the mill after that escapade.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war
