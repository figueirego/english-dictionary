## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who disposes of scraps, such as one who collects scrap metal to sell to a scrap dealer.

   **Example:** After the uncle died they sold off most of his rustbuckets to a scrapper and kept one to fix up.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person who fights doggedly, who exhibits indomitable will.

   **Example:** He's a real scrapper, even against impossible odds, he always keeps fighting.
