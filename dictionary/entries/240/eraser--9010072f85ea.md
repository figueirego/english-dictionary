### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One who erases.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An object used to erase or remove something written or drawn by a pen or a pencil.

   **Usage:** Canada; Philippines; US

   **Example:** I have worn out the eraser on this pencil.

   **Source synonyms:** rubber (Australia; Brunei; India; New-Zealand; UK); [india rubber](../10d/india-rubber--43533f96cbec.md) (UK); bungee (UK)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An object used to erase something written either by chalk on a chalkboard, by a marker on a whiteboard, or by some other erasable implement; a chalkboard eraser, whiteboard eraser, etc.

   **Usage:** Canada; Philippines; US

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An overwriter program used to prevent data recovery.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences
