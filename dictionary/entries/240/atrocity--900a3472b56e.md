### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An extremely cruel act; a horrid act of injustice.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** to carry out / commit / perpetrate an atrocity

   **Example:** The regime is guilty of mass atrocities including forced displacement and the use of chemical weapons.

   **Source synonyms:** depravity; outrage; turpitude

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The quality or state of being atrocious; enormous wickedness; extreme criminality or cruelty.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** atrociousness; brutality; heinousness; enormity

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An object considered to be extremely unattractive or undesirable.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source synonyms:** abomination
