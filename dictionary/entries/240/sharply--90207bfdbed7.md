### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In a sharp manner, in various senses.
   1. So as to terminate in a sharp point or edge.

      **Example:** The stick was sharply tapered at one end.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In a sharp manner, in various senses.
   1. Rapidly, abruptly.

      **Example:** When I touched the snake it recoiled sharply.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. In a sharp manner, in various senses.
   1. Suddenly and intensely like a gasp, but typically as the result of an emotional reaction.

      **Usage:** to describe breathing

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. In a sharp manner, in various senses.
   1. Precisely, accurately.

      **Example:** a sharply focused essay

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. In a sharp manner, in various senses.
   1. Quickly and alertly.

      **Example:** Jones, the centre forward, started the game sharply.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. In a sharp manner, in various senses.
   1. Of speech, sternly, harshly or critically.

      **Example:** When I asked her about her husband, she replied sharply that she had not seen him for several months.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. In a sharp manner, in various senses.
   1. Stylishly, smartly.

      **Example:** He was sharply dressed in an expensive suit.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. In a sharp manner, in various senses.
   1. So as to form a sharp, or tight, angle.

      **Example:** Turn sharply to the right.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. In a sharp manner, in various senses.
   1. Steeply; precipitously.

      **Example:** The road descends sharply.

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. In a sharp manner, in various senses.
   1. Piercingly; keenly; severely; painfully.

      **Example:** His old war wound twinged sharply.

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. In a sharp manner, in various senses.
   1. In an intellectually alert and penetrating manner.

<a id="r1-s12"></a>

**Source sense R1.S12**

12. In a sharp manner, in various senses.
   1. In a strongly distinguishing or differentiating manner; acutely.

      **Example:** The wealth of this small country contrasts sharply with that of its neighbours.
