### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A furrow or groove in an organ or a tissue, especially that marking the convolutions of the surface of the brain.

   **Example:** The sulci and gyri are the grooves and ridges, respectively.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** anatomy; medicine; sciences
