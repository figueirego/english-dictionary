### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To boil insufficiently.

   **Example:** If you underboil an egg, you'll end up with a softboiled egg instead of the hardboiled egg that you wanted; and if you overboil an egg, you'll end up with a hardboiled egg instead of the softboiled egg that you wanted.
