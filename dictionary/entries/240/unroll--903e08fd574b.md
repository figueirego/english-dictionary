### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To straighten something that has been rolled, twisted or curled.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Unroll your sleeping bag and spread it on the floor of the tent.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To emerge, be revealed or become apparent; to unfold.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** We will have to see how the events unroll.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To replace (a loop in a program) with a repetitive sequence of the individual instructions that the loop would carry out, sometimes used as an optimization.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; programming; sciences; software-compilation

   **Source synonyms:** unwind
