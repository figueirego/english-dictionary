### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To enter illegally or by force, especially in order to commit a crime.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** Somebody broke into his car and stole his tools and CDs.

   **Example:** Hackers broke into the bank's computer system and stole customer data.

   **Example:** His house was broken into only last week.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To open or begin to use.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** I finally broke into the second package of cookies.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To successfully enter a profession or business.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** He hopes to break into show business.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To begin suddenly.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The horse broke into a gallop as they neared the barn.

   **Example:** I just broke into tears while talking to my mom.

   **Example:** She broke into song.

   **Example:** She broke into a smile, wrenching herself away from our head physician.
