### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A container for ink, designed and usually positioned so that a person may conveniently dip a pen (such as a dip pen or quill pen) into it whenever a refill is needed.

   **Source synonyms:** inkpot; inkstand

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A store of ink built into a pen, comprising a tube attached to the tip of the pen.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Euphemistic form of incel (“a member of an online subculture who define themselves as unable to find a partner”).

   **Usage:** Internet; euphemistic; form-of; Reddit slang
