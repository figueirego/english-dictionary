**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In the works of philosopher Gilles Deleuze, a lectosign is an image that must be read as much as it is seen or heard. Deleuze introduced this concept in his book Cinema 2&#58; The Time-Image to describe cinematic images that demand interpretation beyond their immediate visual or auditory presentation.
