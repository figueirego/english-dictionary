### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To become happier or less downcast.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** I cheered up after seeing the results.

   **Source synonyms:** blithen; liven up; perk up

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To make someone happier or less downcast.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** The arrival of the unexpected letter cheered him up almost immediately.

   **Source synonyms:** cheer; perk up; beatify; enrapture; begladden; blissen; bright; cheer; fetch; cheer up; cherish (obsolete); content; delight; elate; elevate (obsolete); enbliss; exhilarate; fain (obsolete); gay (dated; uncommon); gladden; gladden someone's heart (dated); gratify; happify; happy; hearten; jollify; laetificate (rare); light up; make someone's day; perk up; please; raise someone's spirits (idiomatic); rejoice; turn the frown upside down; upcheer (archaic); warm someone's heart (idiomatic); warm the cockles of someone's heart (idiomatic)

   **Source antonyms:** depress
