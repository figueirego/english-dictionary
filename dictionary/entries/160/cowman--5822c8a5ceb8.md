### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A man involved in a free-range cattle business, in either ownership or hired hand roles.

   **Source synonyms:** cattleman; rancher; cowhand; cowherd; [cowperson](../008/cowperson--021ee542201c.md); cowpoke; cowboy

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A man involved in a dairy cattle or feedlot cattle business.

   **Source synonyms:** herdsman; cowhand; cowherd; cowkeeper
