### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An asteracean plant with a fragrance reminiscent of apples&#58;
   1. Matricaria chamomilla, German chamomile or Hungarian chamomile, with fragrant flowers used for tea, and as an herbal remedy.

      **Usage:** UK; countable; uncountable

      **Source synonyms:** German camomile; Hungarian camomile

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An asteracean plant with a fragrance reminiscent of apples&#58;
   1. Chamaemelum nobile (formerly Anthemis nobilis), English chamomile or Roman chamomile, a ground cover with fragrant foliage.

      **Usage:** UK; countable; uncountable

      **Source synonyms:** Roman camomile; English camomile

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of several other similar plants. (See below)

   **Usage:** UK; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Ellipsis of camomile tea.

   **Usage:** UK; abbreviation; alt-of; countable; ellipsis; uncountable

   **Alternative form of:** [camomile tea](../16f/camomile-tea--5beedb383a7d.md)
