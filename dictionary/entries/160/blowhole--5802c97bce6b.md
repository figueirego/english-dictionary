### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The spiracle, on the top of the head, through which cetaceans breathe.

   **Usage:** marine biology

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A hole in sea ice where cetaceans and pinnipeds come to breathe.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A top-facing opening to a cavity in the ground very near an ocean's shore, leading to a marine cave from which wave water or bursts of air are expelled.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; geology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A vent for the escape of steam or other gas.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; geology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An unintended cavity filled with air in a casting product.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; metallurgy; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A vertical opening in the top of a computer case that lets hot air (primarily from the CPU heat sink) escape quickly.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computer-hardware; computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences
