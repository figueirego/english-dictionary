## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Plants in the mint family, Lamiaceae), native to North America and having a pennyroyal-like scent, including&#58;
   1. Bluecurls (Trichostema dichotomum, native to the eastern US.

      **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Plants in the mint family, Lamiaceae), native to North America and having a pennyroyal-like scent, including&#58;
   1. A plant of species Trichostema setaceum (Trichostema lineare in older references), also native to the eastern US, which is usually qualified as linear-leaved or narrow bastard pennyroyal.

      **Usage:** uncountable
