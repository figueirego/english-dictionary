### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see stop, by.

   **Example:** The car stopped by the side of the road.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To pay a visit briefly and informally.

   **Example:** I'll stop by this afternoon to collect it.
