## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. All possible knowledge of a subject matter.

   **Example:** He was always one step ahead of me, as he seems to know every trick in the book.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. All possible means, both honest and dishonest.

   **Example:** The solicitor tried every trick in the book to get me to sign the contract, but I wasn't having any of it!
