### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act or process of acquiring.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The acquisition of sports equipment can be fun in itself.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The thing acquired or gained; a gain.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** That graphite tennis racquet is quite an acquisition.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The process of sampling signals that measure real world physical conditions and converting these signals into digital numeric values that can be manipulated by a computer.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
