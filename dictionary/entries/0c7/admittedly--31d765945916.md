## Adverb

### Forms

- more admittedly — comparative
- most admittedly — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. As is acknowledged to be true; by general admission; confessedly.

   **Example:** Admittedly, vandalism is a bit of a problem.

   **Example:** She was admittedly the one who killed the baron.

   **Source synonyms:** admittably; admissibly; granted; concedably

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Signals a concession followed by a counterpoint usually headed by but.

   **Usage:** conjunctive

   **Example:** It's only a theory, admittedly, but it might work.

   **Example:** Admittedly he can't swim, but he still loves going to the seaside anyway.

   **Source synonyms:** admittably; admissibly; granted; concedably
