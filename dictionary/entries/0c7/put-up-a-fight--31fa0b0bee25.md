### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To offer some form of resistance to an attack.

   **Example:** She put up a fight when the mugger tried to steal her purse.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To protest or make a fuss, especially over a proposed course of action.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** Their youngest son always put up a fight when it was bedtime.
