## Prep\_phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Below the cost or price of purchase.

   **Example:** We sold those old computers at a loss.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance; trading

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Not sure; uncertain; lacking further ideas, direction, or ability.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I have fixed the parts I understand, and the rest leaves me at a loss.

   **Example:** His policies may be ineffective, but he is never at a loss for words.

   **Example:** I'm at a loss how to proceed.
