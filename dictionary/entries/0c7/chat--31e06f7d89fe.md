### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To be engaged in informal conversation.

   **Example:** She chatted with her friend in the cafe.

   **Example:** I like to chat over a coffee with a friend.

   **Source synonyms:** confab; discourse; parley; chat; common (obsolete); confab (informal); confabulate; conversate (nonstandard); converse (formal); convo (informal); discourse (formal); jaw (informal); parley; treat; word (archaic)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To talk a while in a friendly manner.

   **Example:** I met my old friend in the street, so we chatted for a while.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To talk of; to discuss.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** They chatted politics for a while.

   **Source synonyms:** bespeak; moot; talk about; bechat; confer; bespeak (archaic; formal); betalk (dialectal); chat; discuss; moot; talk about; talk over; treat

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To chat shit (to speak nonsense, to lie).

   **Usage:** informal; often; slang

   **Example:** Don't listen to me, I'm chatting.

   **Source context (verbatim):** as chatting

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To exchange text or voice messages in real time through a computer network such as a social media chat room or messaging application (as if having a face-to-face conversation instead of SMS or writing emails or letters).

   **Example:** Do you want to chat online later?
