## Adjective · Record 1

### Pronunciation

### Forms

- more subjective — comparative
- most subjective — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Formed, as in opinions, based upon a person's feelings or intuition, rather than upon observation or purely logical reasoning; coming more from within the observer than from observations of the external environment.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Pertaining to subjects as opposed to objects (A subject is one who perceives or is aware; an object is the thing perceived or the thing that the subject is aware of.)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Resulting from or pertaining to personal mindsets or experience, arising from perceptive mental conditions within the brain and not necessarily or directly from external stimuli.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Lacking in reality or substance.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. As used by Carl Jung, the innate worldview orientation of the introverted personality types.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Mind-dependent.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; philosophy; sciences

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Experienced by a person mentally and not directly verifiable by others.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; philosophy; psychology; sciences

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Describing conjugation of a verb that indicates only the subject (agent), not indicating the object (patient) of the action. (In linguistic descriptions of Tundra Nenets, among others.)

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** grammar; human-sciences; linguistics; sciences
