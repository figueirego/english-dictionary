### Forms

- left over — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. Something left behind; an excess or remainder.

   **Example:** It's a leftover from yesterday, but it's still perfectly good.

   **Source synonyms:** oddment

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. Remaining after a meal is complete or eaten for a later meal or snack.

   **Usage:** in-plural; usually

   **Example:** Not leftovers again!

   **Example:** The entire wheel of cheese is a leftover from the party.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of food

   **Source synonyms:** scraps
