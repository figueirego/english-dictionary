### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An Atlantic salmon, Salmo salar, especially a female after spawning.

   **Usage:** Scotland

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of various dark-colored fish of the Old World, especially the rudderfish (Centrolophus niger).

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of various dark-colored toothed whales.
   1. The pilot whale (Globicephalus spp.).

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any of various dark-colored toothed whales.
   1. The killer whale (Orcinus orca).

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Either of two dark-colored fish of the west Atlantic, the tautog of New England (Tautoga onitis) and the black sea bass (Centropristis striata).

   **Usage:** Canada; US

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Any of various dark-colored fishes of Australasia, especially the luderick (Girella tricuspidata), and a freshwater fish, Gadopsis marmoratus.

   **Usage:** Australia; New-Zealand
