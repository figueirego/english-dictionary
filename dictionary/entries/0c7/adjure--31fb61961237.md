### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To formally command or request (someone) to do something, either in the name of a deity, or to suffer a curse or punishment if they do not comply; to bind (someone) under oath; to charge.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To earnestly advise or urge (someone); to exhort.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Party members are adjured to promote awareness of this problem.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To earnestly appeal to (someone, or a deity); to entreat, to implore.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** beg; beseech; plead

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To summon (the Devil or an evil spirit); to invoke; also, to exorcize (the Devil or an evil spirit).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity
