<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Synonym of pharmacist&#58; a person who sells medicine, especially (historical) one who made and sold their own medicines in the medieval or early modern eras.

   **Usage:** archaic; dated

   **Source context (verbatim):** in UK; in US

   **Source synonyms:** pharmacist (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Synonym of pharmacy&#58; an apothecary's shop, a drugstore.

   **Usage:** archaic; historical

   **Source synonyms:** pharmacy (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
