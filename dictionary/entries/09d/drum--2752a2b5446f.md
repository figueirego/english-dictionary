### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To beat a drum.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To beat with a rapid succession of strokes.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** The ruffed grouse drums with his wings.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To drill or review in an attempt to establish memorization.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He’s still trying to drum Spanish verb conjugations into my head.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To throb, as the heart.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To go about, as a drummer does, to gather recruits, to draw or secure partisans, customers, etc.; used with for.

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. Of various animals, to make a vocalisation or mechanical sound that resembles drumming.
