## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The first of ten successive avatars of the god Vishnu, who saved the first man Manu from a great flood in the form of a fish.

   **Usage:** Hinduism

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An ancient kingdom mentioned in the Mahabharata, ruled by King Virāta.

   **Usage:** Hinduism

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
