## Noun

### Forms

- green papers — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A tentative government report of a proposal without any commitment to action, being the first step in changing the law; a discussion document.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An official document sponsored by the Crown of propositions put before the nation for discussion.

   **Usage:** Canada

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see green, paper.
