### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of or pertaining to multiple distinct academic disciplines or fields of study.

   **Example:** This journal is interdisciplinary&#58; it has articles on everything from biology to electrical engineering.

   **Source synonyms:** crossdisciplinary; multidisciplinary; pluridisciplinary; transdisciplinary

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of or pertaining to a linkage between multiple distinct academic disciplines or fields of study.

   **Example:** The interdisciplinary co-operation at this university is very impressive.
