### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To act in response.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** How did she react to the news?

   **Source synonyms:** take

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To act or perform a second time; to do over again; to reenact.

   **Usage:** archaic; transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To return an impulse or impression; to resist the action of another body by an opposite force

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Every body reacts on the body that impels it from its natural state.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To act upon each other; to exercise a reciprocal or a reverse effect, as two or more chemical agents; to act in opposition.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To cause chemical agents to react; to cause one chemical agent to react with another.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** chemistry; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To post a reaction (icon or emoji indicating how one feels about a posted message).

   **Usage:** Internet; intransitive
