### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see piping, hot&#58; very hot in a way that involves sizzling, crackling, or similar noises.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Don't touch the pie! It's piping hot, straight out of the oven.

   **Source synonyms:** hissing hot; boiling hot; scalding hot; steaming hot; steaming; baking hot; hot; calid (rare); choleric (obsolete); heated
