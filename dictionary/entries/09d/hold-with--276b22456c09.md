### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To approve of, to condone.

   **Usage:** always in the negative

   **Example:** I don't hold with that sort of thing!

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To agree with someone (to be in harmony about an opinion or perspective).

   **Usage:** US

   **Example:** I hold with Aristotle on ethics.
