### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To defend oneself by fighting.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The bully hadn't been expecting his victim to fight back, and now the bully has quadriplegia.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To defend oneself by fighting.
   1. To counterattack (counterstrike); to resist an attack or respond after one.

      **Usage:** intransitive

      **Example:** During the Pearl Harbor attack, the U.S. Navy began fighting back, and it wouldn't stop until August 1945.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. to repress; to struggle to repress.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** fight back tears

   **Example:** She tried to fight back her laughter.

   **Example:** It was especially after the Battle of Midway that the U.S. was able to fight the Japanese back toward their home islands.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To overturn a losing deficit.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source context (verbatim):** in sports
