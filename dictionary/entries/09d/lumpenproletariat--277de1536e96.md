### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The lowest stratum of the proletariat, whose mental and physical health, mores and morale have deteriorated, through exploitation by the bourgeoisie, to a degree where they cannot build class consciousness and hence cannot be mobilized for the revolution.

   **Usage:** Marxism

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A social underclass; the riffraff.
