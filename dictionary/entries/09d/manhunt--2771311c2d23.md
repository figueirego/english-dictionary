### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An organized search for a criminal or enemy.

   **Example:** After the murderer escaped, there was a full-scale manhunt to catch him.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A variant of hide and seek in which a player who is caught by the seeker themselves becomes a seeker, and the final player remaining is declared the winner.

   **Usage:** playground games
