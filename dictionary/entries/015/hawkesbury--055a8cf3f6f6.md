## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A hamlet and civil parish in South Gloucestershire district, Gloucestershire, England (OS grid ref ST7686).

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A village in Nuneaton and Bedworth borough, Warwickshire, England (OS grid ref SP3684).

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A town in the United Counties of Prescott and Russell, Ontario, Canada, named after Lord Hawkesbury.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A river, the Hawkesbury River, in New South Wales, Australia, named after Lord Hawkesbury (as in Ontario).

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A local government area, the City of Hawkesbury, located near Sydney in New South Wales, Australia, named after the river.
