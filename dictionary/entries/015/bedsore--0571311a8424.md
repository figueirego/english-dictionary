### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A lesion caused by unrelieved pressure to any part of the body, especially portions over bony or cartilaginous areas, such as frequently develops on a person confined to a bed by infirmity.

   **Usage:** dated; plural-normally

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences
