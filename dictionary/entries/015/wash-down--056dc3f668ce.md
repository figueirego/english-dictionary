### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To help to swallow (something, especially food or a pill) by drinking a liquid.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Now we just need a drink to wash it down.

   **Example:** I had a nice beef sandwich washed down with a pint of bitter.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To wash (something) completely from top to bottom.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** You must wash the wall down well before painting.

   **Example:** We'll wash it down with a garden hose and just a touch of soap.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To send (something) down the drain by washing action.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** There was some food residue in the sink, so she washed it down.
