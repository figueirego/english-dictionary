## Suffix

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used to form an adjective meaning "of or pertaining to" from a noun ending in -aneus or -aneum.

   **Usage:** morpheme

   **Example:** calcaneus + -aneal → calcaneal (“of or pertaining to the calcaneus”)

   **Example:** calcaneum + -aneal → calcaneal (“of or pertaining to the calcaneum”)

   **Example:** cutaneus + -aneal → cutaneal (“of or pertaining to the cutaneus”)
