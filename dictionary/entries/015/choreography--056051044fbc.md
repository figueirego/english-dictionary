### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The art of creating, arranging and recording the dance movements of a work, such as a ballet.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** She has staged many successful ballets, so her choreography skills must be excellent.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The art of creating, arranging and recording the dance movements of a work, such as a ballet.
   1. The art of creating and arranging sequences of movement for performances of any kind, such as in fight choreography.

      **Usage:** broadly; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The art of creating, arranging and recording the dance movements of a work, such as a ballet.
   1. The art of creating and arranging any activities that involve social coordination or orchestration.

      **Usage:** broadly; countable; figuratively; uncountable

      **Example:** the delicate choreography of a marathon transplant operation involving several surgical suites

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The dance steps, sequences or styles peculiar to a work, group, performance or institution.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** The show's singing and acting was excellent, but the choreography was dull and poorly-done.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The representation of these movements by a series of symbols.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** I've written down the choreography for y'all to take a look at.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The notation used to construct this record.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Take a look at this, it's the choreography for our next show.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. An organised display by fans in the stadium, involving props such as flags, banners, musical instruments and pyrotechnics.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; games; hobbies; lifestyle; soccer; sports

   **Source synonyms:** tifo
