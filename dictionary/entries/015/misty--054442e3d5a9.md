### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Covered in mist; foggy.

   **Example:** It's very misty this morning; I can't see a thing!

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Dim; vague; obscure.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** a misty memory of his childhood

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. With tears in the eyes; dewy-eyed.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** Her eyes grew misty the night her long-time friend passed away.
