## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Extremely thick and heavy, so as to make it impossible to move.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; simile

   **Example:** We're not going to be able to break down this door&#58; it's solid as a rock.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Very reliable and dependable

   **Usage:** not-comparable; simile

   **Example:** Stewart has been solid as a rock ever since becoming the new vice-chairman.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a person
