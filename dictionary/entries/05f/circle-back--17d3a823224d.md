### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To return to a previous location or state; to come back to.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive

   **Example:** I have to circle back to the office to pick up a few things.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To return to a previous topic of discussion; to bring up again.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive; corporate jargon

   **Example:** We're out of time right now, but we can circle back on this tomorrow.

**Source sense R1.S3**
