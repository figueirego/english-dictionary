### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A measure of weight containing a hundred pounds, variously understood as
   1. A unit of measurement containing 100 avoirdupois pounds (about 45.4 kg).

      **Usage:** Canada; US

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A measure of weight containing a hundred pounds, variously understood as
   1. A unit of measurement containing 112 avoirdupois pounds (eight stone or about 50.8 kg).

      **Usage:** UK; historical

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A measure of weight containing a hundred pounds, variously understood as
   1. Synonym of quintal, a unit of measurement containing various amounts depending on the objects weighed.

      **Usage:** historical

      **Source synonyms:** quintal (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A measure of weight containing a hundred pounds, variously understood as
   1. Various units of ancient measurement systems consisting of 100 units usually translated as "pounds".

      **Usage:** historical
