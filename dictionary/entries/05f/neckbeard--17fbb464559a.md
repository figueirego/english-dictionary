### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A style of facial hair wherein the beard is allowed to grow down the chin and is trimmed to an even length over the entirety of the face.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
