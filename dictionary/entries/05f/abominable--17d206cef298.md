### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Worthy of, or causing, abhorrence, as a thing of evil omen; odious in the utmost degree; very hateful; detestable; loathsome; execrable.

   **Example:** abominable crime

   **Example:** utterly abominable

   **Example:** He committed an abominable act of cruelty.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Excessive, large (used as an intensifier).

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Very bad or inferior.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Disagreeable or unpleasant.

   **Example:** abominable weather

   **Example:** The prisoners were kept in abominable conditions.
