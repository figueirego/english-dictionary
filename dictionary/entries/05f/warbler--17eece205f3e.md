### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of various small passerine songbirds, especially of the family Sylviidae (Old World warblers) and Parulidae (New World warblers).

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Applied formerly to various other passerine bird families, such as the Australian Acanthizidae.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. One who warbles.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A device that makes a warbling sound.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**
