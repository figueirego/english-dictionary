### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An official approval; the mark denoting it (in a document); a sanction; (figuratively) any semiofficial, authoritative, or humorous approval.

   **Example:** These watermarked documents are marked with the building inspector's seal of approval.

   **Example:** Our cat has clearly given the new catfood his seal of approval.

   **Source synonyms:** stamp of approval; certification; endorsement
