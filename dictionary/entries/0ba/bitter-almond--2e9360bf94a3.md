### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A tree of the species Prunus amygdalus (order Rosales).

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The seed of this tree, which is unpalatably bitter and bears many times the cyanide of the sweet almond.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A tree of the species Brabejum stellatifolium (order Proteales).
