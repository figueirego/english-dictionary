### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The rate of occurrence of anything; the relationship between incidence and time period.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** With growing confidence, the Viking’s raids increased in frequency.

   **Example:** The frequency of bus service has been improved from one every 15 to one every 12 minutes.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The property of occurring often rather than infrequently.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** The FAQ addresses questions that come up with some frequency.

   **Example:** The frequency of the visits was what annoyed him.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The quotient of the number of times n a periodic phenomenon occurs over the time t in which it occurs&#58; f=n/t.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** The frequency of the musical note A above middle C is 440 oscillations per second.

   **Example:** The frequency of a wave is its velocity v divided by its wavelength \#92;lambda&#58; f\#61;v\#47;\#92;lambda.

   **Example:** Broadcasting live at a frequency of 98.3 megahertz, we’re your rock alternative!

   **Example:** The frequency for electric power in the Americas is generally 60 Hz rather than 50.

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A tone, character, mood, or vibe

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
