### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A small receptacle with a perforated top used for dispensing pepper or similar spices.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** pepper shaker; peppershaker; pepper pot
