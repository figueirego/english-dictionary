### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A mass of things heaped together; a heap.

   **Usage:** collective

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A group or list of related items up for consideration, especially in some kind of selection process.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** When we were looking for a new housemate, we put the nice woman on the "maybe" pile, and the annoying guy on the "no" pile.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A mass formed in layers.

   **Example:** a pile of shot

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A funeral pile; a pyre.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A large amount of money.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Example:** He made a pile from that invention of his.

   **Source synonyms:** bundle; mint (informal); small fortune (colloquial)

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A large building, or mass of buildings.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A bundle of pieces of wrought iron to be worked over into bars or other shapes by rolling or hammering at a welding heat; a fagot.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A vertical series of alternate disks of two dissimilar metals (especially copper and zinc), laid up with disks of cloth or paper moistened with acid water between them, for producing a current of electricity; a voltaic pile, or galvanic pile.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. A beam, pole, or pillar, driven completely into the ground, usually as one of a group that constitutes a foundation.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** architecture; civil-engineering; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

**Source sense R1.S11**

   **Usage:** often; transitive

   **Example:** They were piling up wood on the wheelbarrow.

   **Source context (verbatim):** used with the preposition "up"

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** We piled the camel with our loads.

**Source sense R2.S3**

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To place (guns, muskets, etc.) together in threes so that they can stand upright, supporting each other.

   **Usage:** transitive
