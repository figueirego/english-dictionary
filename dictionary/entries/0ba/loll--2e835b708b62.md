### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To act lazily or indolently while reclining; to lean; to lie at ease.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To hang extended from the mouth, like the tongue of an animal heated from exertion.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To let (the tongue) hang from the mouth.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** The ox stood lolling in the furrow.
