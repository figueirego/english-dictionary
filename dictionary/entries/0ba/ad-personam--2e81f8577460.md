## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Given exclusively to one specific person, and not transferrable to or heritable by others. (of a role, title, privilege, etc.)

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** bishop ad personam

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Targeted at one person or group of people in particular, but not generally applicable to the entire community.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** The decriminalization of accounting fraud was a law ad personam intended save the President from legal trouble.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Synonym of ad hominem.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source synonyms:** ad hominem (synonym; synonym-of)
