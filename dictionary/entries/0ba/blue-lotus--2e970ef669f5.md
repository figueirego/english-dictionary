## Noun

### Forms

- blue lotuses — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A lily-like aquatic flower growing in Asia and North Africa, Nymphaea caerulea, revered by the Ancient Egyptians.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An aquatic flower of species Nymphaea nouchali, native to the Indian subcontinent.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A perennial aquatic flower native to tropical Asia and Queensland, Australia, of species Nelumbo nucifera, having attractive flowers and sometimes used in food.
