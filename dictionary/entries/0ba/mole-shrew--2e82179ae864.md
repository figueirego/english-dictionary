### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of various shrews that resemble moles, of the genera Surdisorex and Anourosorex.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Synonym of short-tailed shrew, any of the genus Blarina of relatively large shrews with relatively short tails, found in North America.
