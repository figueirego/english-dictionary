### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A polyhedral approximation to a cylinder (formed by stacked rings of isosceles triangles, arranged within each ring in the same pattern as an antiprism), used as a pathological example of the difficulty of defining the area of a smooth (curved) surface as the limit of the areas of polyhedra.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** Chinese lantern; Schwarz's polyhedron
