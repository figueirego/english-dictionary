**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A general election for all the representatives and one-third of the senators in Congress in a year without a presidential election as well.

   **Usage:** US

   **Example:** In the United States, general elections occur in even-numbered years, with the quadrennial presidential elections distinguished from the midterm elections between them.
