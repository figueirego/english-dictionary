### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The result of no longer possessing an object, a function, or a characteristic due to external causes or misplacement.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** loss of limb; weight loss; loss of cognitive functions; loss of appetite.

   **Example:** People are less willing to take a risk to make a gain than to avoid a loss.

   **Example:** In other areas, glacier loss creates serious risk of a dry period across the Third Pole, Wang said.

   **Source synonyms:** expense

   **Source antonyms:** gain

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The destruction or ruin of an object.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Something that has been destroyed or ruined.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** It was a terrible crash; both cars were total losses.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Defeat; an instance of being defeated.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** The match ended in their first loss of the season.

   **Source antonyms:** win; victory

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The death of a person or animal.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** We mourn his loss.

   **Example:** The battle was won, but losses were great.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. The condition of grief caused by losing someone or something, especially someone who has died.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Her daughter's sense of loss eventually led to depression.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The sum an entity loses on balance.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** The sum of expenditures and taxes minus total income is a loss, when this difference is positive.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; finance; financial

   **Source antonyms:** profit

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Electricity of kinetic power expended without doing useful work.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The inefficiency of many old-fashioned power plants exceeds 60% loss before the subsequent losses during transport over the grid.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences
