## Adjective

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having two sides, bilateral.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Reversible.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** A reversible coat is two-sided and would allow you to change your appearance.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Whose salient properties apply when (the specified object) appears on either side of a given binary operator.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** two-sided inverse — two-sided identity

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** algebra; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Having two aspects or viewpoints.

   **Usage:** figuratively; not-comparable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. That is necessarily or absolutely different than the respective coordinate alethic or temporal modality as well as its opposite.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; logic; mathematics; philosophy; sciences

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a modality
