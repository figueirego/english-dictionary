## Verb · Record 1

### Forms

- flares up — present; singular; third-person
- flaring up — participle; present
- flared up — participle; past

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To burn brightly again.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The fire flared up after we added more wood to it.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To become more intense suddenly.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Reports indicate that tensions have flared up in the Middle East again.

   **Example:** The pain in my shoulder flares up when I turn my head.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To burst out suddenly, as in anger.

   **Usage:** intransitive; sometimes

   **Example:** The insult made him flare up.

   **Source context (verbatim):** with “out” instead of “up”
