### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A table leg, set into a frame in the form of a gate, that may be swung back to allow a leaf of the tabletop to hang down; having such a leg or legs.

   **Usage:** also; attributive

   **Example:** a gateleg desk; a gateleg shelf

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** furniture; lifestyle
