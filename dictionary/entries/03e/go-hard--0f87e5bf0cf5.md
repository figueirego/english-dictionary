### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To strive to one's utmost; to give one's all in an endeavour, at sport, etc.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source synonyms:** go strong

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To be excellent.

   **Usage:** intransitive; slang; stative

   **Example:** Did you hear this band's new album? Yeah, it went hard af!

   **Example:** This pic goes hard. Feel free to screenshot.

   **Source synonyms:** slap; slay; bang (slang; stative); devour (slang); eat (slang; stative); eat and leave no crumbs (idiomatic; slang); excel; go hard (slang; stative); kick (slang); kick ass (idiomatic; stative; vulgar); rock (slang); rule (slang; stative); shine (copulative); slap (slang; stative); slay (African-American-Vernacular-English)

   **Source antonyms:** go soft

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To have sex vigorously.

   **Usage:** intransitive; slang

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see go, hard.

   **Example:** I wanted to eat some bread but it had gone hard.
