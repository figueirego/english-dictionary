### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A loud cry or uproar.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** His appearance was greeted with an outcry of jeering.

   **Source synonyms:** rumpus; noise

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A strong protest.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

   **Example:** The proposal was met with a public outcry.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An auction.

   **Usage:** India; archaic; countable; uncountable

   **Example:** to send goods to an outcry
