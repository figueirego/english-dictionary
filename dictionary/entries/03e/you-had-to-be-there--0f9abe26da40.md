## Phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used to indicate to the interlocutor that the situation being talked about could only be properly understood if that person had been present.

   **Example:** What he did was so hilarious, the way he was dancing... well, you had to be there.
