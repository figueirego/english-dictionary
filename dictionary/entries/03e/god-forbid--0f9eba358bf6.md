## Interjection · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. indicating that the occurrence of something is very undesirable

   **Usage:** idiomatic; subjunctive

   **Example:** If, God forbid, there's a fire, head for the nearest exit.

   **Example:** God forbid anybody get any real work done around here.

   **Example:** A&#58; Do you think he'll win the election? B&#58; God forbid!

   **Source synonyms:** heaven forbid; heaven forfend
