### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Bad or wrong; badly or wrongly.

   **Usage:** morpheme

   **Example:** misbehaviour; misspelling; to misbehave

   **Source synonyms:** mal-

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Bad or wrong; badly or wrongly.
   1. Incorrect; incorrectly.

      **Usage:** morpheme

      **Example:** misidentification; to misidentify

      **Source synonyms:** mal-; para-; dis-; dys-; mal-

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Failed; in a manner resulting in failure (to do, complete or achieve something).

   **Usage:** morpheme

   **Example:** miscarriage (failed carriage of a pregnancy to term), to miscarry, to misacknowledge

   **Source synonyms:** dis-; dys-

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Unintentional, accidental, mistaken; unintentionally, accidentally, mistakenly.

   **Usage:** morpheme

   **Example:** Yes, I did "Buy now", but it was a misclick.

   **Example:** I misclicked something on my computer, and now everything is in Hungarian!

   **Source synonyms:** mal-

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. False, falsely.

   **Usage:** morpheme

   **Example:** misaccusation, to misaccuse

   **Source synonyms:** pseudo-
