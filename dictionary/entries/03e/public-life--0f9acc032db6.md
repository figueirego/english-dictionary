## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The aspects of social life which occur in public, in the open, as opposed to more private social interaction within families, private clubs, etc.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source antonyms:** personal life; private life; dream life; nightlife; love life; sex life
