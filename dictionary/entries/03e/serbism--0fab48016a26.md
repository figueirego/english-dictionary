## Noun

### Forms

- Serbisms — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A linguistic feature of Serbian language, especially a Serbian idiom or phrasing that appears in some other language.

   **Usage:** countable; rhetoric

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An attitude, custom, or other feature that is characteristic for Serbs.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Esteem for and emulation of cultural and ethnic heritage of Serbs.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source synonyms:** Serbophilia

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Nationalistic ideology declaring that being a Serb should be the highest cultural or political priority.

   **Usage:** uncountable
