### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To continue to live; to remain alive.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Whether by a miracle or by good luck, all twenty passengers survived.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a person

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To continue to exist.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** This town has been hit by two hurricanes, but it survives.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of an object or concept

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To live past (a life-threatening event)

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Whether by a miracle or by good luck, all twenty passengers survived the crash.

   **Example:** He did not survive the accident.

   **Example:** This house has survived two hurricanes.

   **Source synonyms:** overlive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To live longer than (someone); to outlive (someone or something); to outlast (something).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** His children survived him.

   **Example:** He was survived by his children.

   **Source synonyms:** overlive

   **Source antonyms:** predecease

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To be a victim of nonfatal but substantial harm and nonetheless to display the strength to heal; especially, after a crime or an illness, especially sexual or physical abuse or assault, cancer, or a natural disaster.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** I just know you'll get through this, because that's what survivors like you do&#58; they not only survive but thrive.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To avoid relegation or demotion to a lower division or league.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** They narrowly survived, but the heat is on.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a team
