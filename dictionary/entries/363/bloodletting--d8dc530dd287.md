### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The archaic practice of treating illness by removing some blood, believed to be tainted, from the stricken person.

   **Usage:** historical

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** bleeding

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The diminishment of any resource with the hope that this will lead to a positive effect.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** Yet another round of corporate bloodletting.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A circumstance, such as a battle, where a large amount of blood is likely to be spilled through violence.

   **Source synonyms:** bloodshed
