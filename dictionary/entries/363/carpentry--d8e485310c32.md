### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The trade of manipulating materials in order to construct, install, and/or repair buildings or other structures.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The trade of manipulating materials in order to construct, install, and/or repair buildings or other structures.
   1. The trade of cutting and joining wood, timber, or lumber; woodworking.

      **Usage:** uncountable
