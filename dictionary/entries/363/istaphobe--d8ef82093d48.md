### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A catch-all combination of pejorative terms ending in ist or -phobe (such as racist or homophobe), perceived as being used to silence and discredit opposing viewpoints in discussions of social justice issues.

   **Usage:** Internet; sarcastic

   **Example:** Nowadays saying anything online will get you called some sort of istaphobe.
