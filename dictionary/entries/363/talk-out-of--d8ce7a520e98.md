### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To talk to someone in order to dissuade them from doing something.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** My brother wanted a pet squirrel, but I talked him out of it.

   **Example:** She talked the officer out of giving her a ticket.

   **Example:** The con artist talked his victims out of their life savings.
