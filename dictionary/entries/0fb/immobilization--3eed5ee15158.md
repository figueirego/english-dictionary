### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act or process of preventing a thing from moving.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The broken bones were able to heal, thanks to the immobilization of her leg in a cast.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The process in which organic soil matter is decomposed and absorbed by microorganisms, and thus not available to plants.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; soil science
