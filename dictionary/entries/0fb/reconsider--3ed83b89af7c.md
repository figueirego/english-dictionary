### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To consider a matter again.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** Is there any way I can get you to reconsider selling your car?

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To change one's mind, with or without actually considering the matter again.

   **Usage:** informal
