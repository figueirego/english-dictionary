## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. a method of grafting in which the scion is placed in a cleft or slit in the stock or stump made by sawing off a branch, usually in such a manner that its bark evenly joins that of the stock.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** agriculture; business; horticulture; lifestyle
