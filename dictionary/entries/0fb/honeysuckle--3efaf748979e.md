### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of the many species of arching shrubs and climbing vines of the genus Lonicera in the Caprifoliaceae family, many with sweet-smelling, bell-shaped flowers.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of the many species of arching shrubs and climbing vines of the genus Lonicera in the Caprifoliaceae family, many with sweet-smelling, bell-shaped flowers.
   1. A light yellow color inspired by the color of honeysuckle blossoms.

      **Usage:** broadly; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of several species of superficially similar plants from Australia.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable
