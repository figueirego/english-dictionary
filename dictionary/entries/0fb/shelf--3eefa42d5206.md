### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A flat, rigid structure, fixed at right angles to a wall or forming a part of a cabinet, desk, etc., and used to display, store, or support objects.

   **Example:** We keep the old newspapers on the bottom shelf of the cupboard, and our photos on the top shelf.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The capacity of such an object

   **Example:** a shelf of videos

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A projecting ledge that resembles such an object.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The part of a repository where shelvesets are stored.
