### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any dipterous (“two-winged”) insect or fly of the family Oestridae (commonly known as a bot fly) or Tabanidae (horse fly), noted for irritating animals by buzzing about them, and biting them to suck their blood; a gadbee.

   **Source synonyms:** warble fly; stoat-fly; stout

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person or thing that irritates or instigates.

   **Usage:** also; attributive; figuratively

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A person or thing that irritates or instigates.
   1. A person who upsets the status quo by posing novel or upsetting questions, or attempts to stimulate innovation by being an irritant.

      **Usage:** also; attributive; figuratively; specifically

      **Source synonyms:** eccentric; free spirit; heteroclite; deviant; deviate; individualist; maverick; misfit; nonconformist; offbeat

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Synonym of gadabout (“a person who restlessly moves from place to place, seeking amusement or the companionship of others”).

   **Usage:** also; attributive; figuratively

   **Source synonyms:** social butterfly; gadabout (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A person who takes without giving back; a bloodsucker.

   **Usage:** also; attributive; derogatory; figuratively; slang

   **Example:** He’s a regular gadfly and takes advantage of his friend’s generosity.

   **Source synonyms:** bloodsucker; bludger (Australia; New-Zealand; slang); bummer (US; dated; slang); freeloader; free rider; gadfly (slang); leech; mooch; moocher; parasite; scrounge; scrounger; scunge (slang); smell-feast (archaic); sponge; sponger; sucker; trencher mate (archaic)
