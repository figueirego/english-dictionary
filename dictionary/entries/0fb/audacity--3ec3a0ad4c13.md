### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Insolent boldness, especially when imprudent or unconventional.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The brash private had the audacity to criticize the general.

   **Example:** Somebody never pays his loans, yet he has the audacity to ask the bank for money.

   **Source synonyms:** audaciousness; chutzpah; outdaciousness; temerity; gall

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Fearlessness, intrepidity or daring, especially with confident disregard for personal safety, conventional thought, or other restrictions.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
