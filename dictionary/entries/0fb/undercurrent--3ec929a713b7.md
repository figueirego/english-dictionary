### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A current of water which flows under the surface, and often in a different direction from surface currents.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A tendency of feeling or opinion that is concealed rather than exposed.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** The meeting was pervaded with an undercurrent of dread, as the managers tried not to admit that firings were looming.

   **Source synonyms:** subcurrent; subtext
