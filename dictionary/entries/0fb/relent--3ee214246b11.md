### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To give in or be swayed; to become less hard, harsh, or cruel; to show clemency.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** He had planned to ground his son for a month, but relented and decided to give him a stern lecture instead.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To slacken; to abate.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** We waited for the storm to relent before we ventured outside.

   **Example:** He will not relent in his effort to reclaim his victory.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To lessen, make less severe or intense.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To become less rigid or hard; to soften; to yield, for example by dissolving or melting

   **Usage:** dated; intransitive

   **Source context (verbatim):** of substance
