### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A trap which can easily be avoided by a competent person, and thus proves the incompetence of anybody trapped by it.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** To show he doesn’t do his fair share of work, I’ve set him an elephant trap. If he falls in he’ll look like a clown.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Often in the form Elephant Trap&#58; a faulty attempt by White to win a pawn in a popular variation of the Queen's Gambit Declined, which is generally only effective against inexperienced players.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** board-games; chess; games
