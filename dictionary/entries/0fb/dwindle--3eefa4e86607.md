### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To decrease, shrink, diminish, reduce in size or intensity.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Their supplies began to dwindle after a week.

   **Example:** Interest in the project slowly dwindled.

   **Example:** dwindle away

   **Example:** dwindle to nothing

   **Example:** dwindle steadily

   **Source synonyms:** fade away; peter out; spin down (figuratively); trail off; wane; wanse

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To fall away in quality; degenerate, sink.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To lessen; to bring low.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>
