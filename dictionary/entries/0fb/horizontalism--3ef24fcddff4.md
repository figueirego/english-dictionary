### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A political or social theory or system that advocates the equitable distribution of power in a society.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** horizontalidad; horizontality

<a id="r1-s2"></a>
