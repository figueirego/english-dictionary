**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A child born to Jewish parents whose relationship is incestuous or otherwise forbidden by Jewish law (e.g. an unmarried man and a married woman), or the descendant of such a person.

   **Usage:** Judaism

   **Usage:** derogatory
