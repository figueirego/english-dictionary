### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To turn aside from a course.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The workers diverted the stream away from the road.

   **Source synonyms:** deflect

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To distract.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Don't let him divert your attention; keep your eye on the ball.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To entertain or amuse (by diverting the attention)

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To turn aside; to digress.

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete
