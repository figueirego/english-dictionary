### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A chance for advancement, progress or profit.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** seize the opportunity

   **Example:** take an opportunity

   **Example:** missed opportunity

   **Example:** The world is full of opportunities and it's up to me to see them and pursue them.

   **Example:** the opportunity came and went

   **Example:** You'll get a second opportunity if you miss this one.

   **Source synonyms:** opening

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A favorable circumstance or occasion.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Having a holiday is a great opportunity to relax.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Opportuneness.

   **Usage:** countable; nonstandard; uncountable

   **Example:** The Court questioned the opportunity of introducing these measures in such an uncertain economic climate.
