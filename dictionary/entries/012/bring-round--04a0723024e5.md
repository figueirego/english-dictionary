### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To bring something when coming.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Can you bring round some beer when you come tonight?

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To resuscitate; to cause to regain consciousness.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** We thought he was going to die, but the doctors managed to bring him round.

   **Source synonyms:** revive; wake up; arouse; awake; awaken; bring round; cry; knock up (British); rouse; roust; uprouse; wake; wake up; waken

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To change someone's opinion or point of view.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** She was opposed to the new housing development, but we eventually brought her round.

   **Source synonyms:** influence; persuade; bring round; convict; convince; draw; gain over (US); persuade; pimp (US; slang); prevail upon (idiomatic); procure; push; suade (obsolete); talk around; talk over; wash with; win over (idiomatic)
