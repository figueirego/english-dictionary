### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To evaluate; to estimate or anticipate the magnitude, difficulty, or strength of something.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** It's a good idea for boxers to size up their opponents before their matches.

   **Example:** Before we can begin to size up the problem, we'll need more information.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To wear a larger size of clothing.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** This shirt runs tight, so you should size up.
