### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The wheels, tyres, brakes, shock absorbers etc of an aircraft.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Modern landing gear usually has a tricycle configuration.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** aeronautics; aerospace; aviation; business; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

   **Source synonyms:** undercarriage

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of the assemblies which support an aircraft when it is on the ground.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** Large modern commercial aircraft have a nose landing gear and two main landing gears.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** aeronautics; aerospace; aviation; business; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences
