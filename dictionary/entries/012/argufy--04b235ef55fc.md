### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To argue without any aim; to dispute; to disagree.

   **Usage:** colloquial; intransitive

   **Source synonyms:** wrangle

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To dispute (a point, fact, etc.), to argue about (something).

   **Usage:** colloquial; transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To reason (something) out, to fully consider, think through.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive; archaic; colloquial

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To persuade (someone) through argument.

   **Usage:** archaic; colloquial; transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To weary (someone) with arguing.

   **Usage:** archaic; colloquial; transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To be evidence of (something).

   **Usage:** archaic; colloquial; transitive

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To be of importance or relevance; to make a difference, to be of use.

   **Usage:** archaic; colloquial; intransitive
