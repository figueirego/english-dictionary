### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A tale or story which is fantastic and greatly exaggerated; also, an account of questionable veracity; a lie, an untruth.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** He returned on Monday with a tall tale about a 100-pound fish he had caught.

   **Source synonyms:** Banbury story of a cock and a bull; cock-and-bull story; fish story; tall story; traveller's tale
