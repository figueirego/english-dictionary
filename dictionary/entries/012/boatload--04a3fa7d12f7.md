### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Cargo or passengers that fill a boat.

   **Example:** a boatload of ferry passengers

   **Source synonyms:** boatful

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A large quantity.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Example:** He showed up an hour later with a whole boatload of hamburgers, chips, cookies, and assorted other munchies, not to mention sodas and beer, and we all fell in and stuffed ourselves silly.
