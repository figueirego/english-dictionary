### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A σ-algebra which is obtained as a "completion" of a given σ-algebra, which includes all subsets of the given measure space which simultaneously contain a member of the given σ-algebra and are contained by a member of the given σ-algebra, as long as the contained and containing measurable sets have the same measure, in which case the subset in question is assigned a measure equal to the common measure of its contained and containing measurable sets (so the measure is also being completed, in parallel with the σ-algebra).

   **Example:** Every σ-algebra has a μ-completion&#58; if a σ-algebra is complete, then it is equal to its μ-completion, otherwise it is contained by its μ-completion.
