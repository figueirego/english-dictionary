### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To burn the flesh with a hot iron, either as a marker (for criminals, slaves etc.) or to cauterise a wound.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** When they caught him, he was branded and then locked up.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To burn the flesh with a hot iron, either as a marker (for criminals, slaves etc.) or to cauterise a wound.
   1. To mark (especially cattle) with a brand as proof of ownership.

      **Usage:** transitive

      **Example:** The ranch hands had to brand every new calf by lunchtime.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To make an indelible impression on the memory or senses.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Her face is branded upon my memory.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To stigmatize, label (someone).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He was branded a fool by everyone that heard his story.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To associate a product or service with a trademark or other name and related images.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** They branded the new detergent "Suds-O", with a nature scene inside a green O on the muted-colored recycled-cardboard box.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; marketing

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To be very hot, to burn.

   **Usage:** intransitive
