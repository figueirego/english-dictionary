<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A tile divided into two squares, each having 0 to 6 (or sometimes more) dots or pips (as in dice), used in the game of dominoes.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** dominoes; games

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A country that is expected to react to events in a neighboring country, according to the domino effect.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; politics

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A masquerade costume consisting of a hooded robe and a mask covering the upper part of the face.

   **Source synonyms:** domino costume

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The mask itself.

   **Source synonyms:** domino mask; half mask; eyemask

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The person wearing the costume.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A polyomino made up of two squares.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geometry; mathematics; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** 2-omino
