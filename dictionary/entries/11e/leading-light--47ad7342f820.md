### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An acclaimed expert, one of the foremost experts, a luminary.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** Stephen Hawking is a leading light in physics, people say he's the greatest physicist since Einstein.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A light that helps to guide vessels into a port.

   **Usage:** navigation
