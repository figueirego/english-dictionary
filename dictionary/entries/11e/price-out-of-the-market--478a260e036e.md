### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To charge an exorbitant price for a service or product so that no one will purchase it.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** He's pricing out of the market, asking for $100,000 for that shack!

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To force competition out of business by offering lower or discount prices.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Supermarkets are trying to price farmers' markets out of the market by offering lower prices.
