## Adverb

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Regarding or relating to orthography (the way in which languages, usually natural languages, are written).

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Although it is possible to represent this phonetic distinction orthographically, such representation is not emically realized.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. According to standardized or formally accepted orthography&#58; using proper spelling, and (more strictly) proper spelling, punctuation, capitalization, and italics.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** stylings that are orthographically valid (acceptable), albeit not preferable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Using an orthographic projection (a way of showing three-dimensional objects in two-dimensional engineering drawings).

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** To represent an internal feature orthographically, dashed lines are used instead of solid ones.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; drafting; engineering; manufacturing; natural-sciences; physical-sciences
