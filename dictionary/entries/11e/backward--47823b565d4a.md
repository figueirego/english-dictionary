## Adverb · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- enPR: băk'wərd — source labels: US
- IPA: /ˈbækwɚd/ — source labels: US

### Forms

- more backward — comparative
- most backward — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. At, near or towards the rear of something.

   **Example:** The passenger turned around and walked backward, towards the tail of the aircraft.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. In a direction opposite to that in which someone or something is facing or normally pointing.

   **Example:** He tilted his head backward.

   **Example:** The grandfather clock toppled backward and crashed to the ground.

   **Example:** Her arm was bent backward at an odd angle.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. In a direction opposite to the usual direction of movement.

   **Example:** In reverse gear the wheel turns backward.

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. Oppositely to the desired direction of progress, or from a better to a worse state.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** This project seems to be going backward.

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. In a reversed orientation; back to front.

   **Example:** He had his cap on backward.

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. In a reversed order or sequence.

   **Example:** A palindrome reads the same backward as forward.

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

**Source sense R2.S7**

7. Toward or into the past.

   **Example:** As we begin the new millennium, it behoves us to look backward as well as forward.

<a id="r2-s8"></a>

**Source sense R2.S8**

8. In the past.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r2-s9"></a>

**Source sense R2.S9**

9. By way of reflection; reflexively.
