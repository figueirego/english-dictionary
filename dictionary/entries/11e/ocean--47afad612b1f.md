### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. One of the large bodies of water separating the continents.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The continuous body of salt water covering a majority of the Earth's surface.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** sea

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Water belonging to an ocean.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** The island is surrounded by ocean

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An immense expanse; any vast space or quantity without apparent limits.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

   **Example:** the boundless ocean of eternity

   **Example:** an ocean of difference

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A blue colour, like that of the ocean (also called ocean blue).

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
