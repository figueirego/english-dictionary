### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To cause the playout of (a video, audio, or other digital media file) to move very fast backwards in time, so that when the device is played, it will start at an earlier point.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of a digital media file, to be so moved.

   **Usage:** intransitive
