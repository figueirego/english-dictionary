### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A brief examination, a peek or glance.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** I’ve had a look-see at your work, and I think you’ve done a pretty good job of things.

   **Example:** I'll just take a look-see at the problem and come right back, then we can go to lunch.

   **Example:** He gave me a quick look-see at what they're working on.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person who serves as lookout or scout.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An audition attended by an actor or model so that they can be visually appraised.

   **Source synonyms:** go-see
