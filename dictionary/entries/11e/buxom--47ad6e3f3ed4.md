**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having a full, voluptuous figure, especially possessing large breasts.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a woman

   **Source synonyms:** bosomy; big-breasted; busty; chesty; plump; round; shapely; full-throated; built; buxom; chubby; curvaceous; curvesome; curvy; embonpoint; full-figured; juicy; sensual; luscious; pneumatic; red-hot; Rubenesque; sonsy (British; dialectal); thicc (Internet); thick (slang); toothsome; voluptuous; well-endowed; well-proportioned; zaftig (US)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Full of health, vigour, and good temper.

   **Usage:** dated

   **Source synonyms:** gay; vigorous

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

   **Usage:** obsolete

   **Source synonyms:** pliant; pliable; flexible; malleable; plastic
