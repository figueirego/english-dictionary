**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Nevertheless; yet, still; in spite of that.

   **Usage:** conjunctive; not-comparable

   **Example:** He told me not to do it. However, I did it anyway. / I did it anyway, however. / I, however, did it anyway.

   **Example:** She wanted to go; however, she decided against it.

   **Example:** I didn't argue with him; I still think, however, that he is wrong.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. In contrast.

   **Usage:** conjunctive; not-comparable

   **Example:** The conference itself went very well. The party afterwards, however, was a disaster.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Regardless of how; no matter how

   **Usage:** not-comparable; degree; fused relative; degree; fused relative

   **Example:** However clear you think you've been, and however many questions you may've answered, there'll always remain some doubts.

   **Example:** Any rule of thumb, however true (it is), is useless unless you understand its underlying logic.

   **Example:** However much I study, I never get good grades.

   **Source synonyms:** howsoever; howso; howsoevermuch

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Regardless of how; regardless of the way in which.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; fused relative

   **Example:** Let me know when you've had your interview, however it goes.

   **Example:** However we do this, it isn't going to work.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** manner

   **Source synonyms:** howsoever; howso; anyhow

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. In any way in which; how.

   **Usage:** not-comparable; fused relative

   **Example:** She offered to help however she could.

   **Example:** Wear your hair however you want.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** manner

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. In any way that one likes or chooses; in a haphazard or spontaneous way.

   **Usage:** informal; not-comparable

   **Example:** I don't care; just do it however.

   **Example:** Nothing was really planned; things just happened however.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** manner
