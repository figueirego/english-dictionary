## Noun · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈsɪndɪkət/ — source labels: General-American; Received-Pronunciation

### Forms

- syndicates — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A group of individuals or companies formed to transact some specific business, or to promote a common interest; a self-coordinating group.

   **Example:** a gambling syndicate

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A group of individuals or companies formed to transact some specific business, or to promote a common interest; a self-coordinating group.
   1. A group of gangsters engaged in organized crime.

      **Usage:** crime

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A group of individuals or companies formed to transact some specific business, or to promote a common interest; a self-coordinating group.
   1. A group of media companies, or an agency, formed to acquire content such as articles, cartoons, etc., and to publish it in multiple outlets; a chain of newspapers or other media outlets managed by such an organization.

      **Usage:** mass media

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The office or jurisdiction of a syndic; a body or council of syndics.
