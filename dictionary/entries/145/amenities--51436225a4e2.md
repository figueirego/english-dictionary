### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Synonym of pleasantries.

   **Usage:** plural; plural-only

   **Example:** The prime ministers engaged in diplomatic amenities before signing the treaty.

   **Source synonyms:** pleasantries (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Things or circumstances that are welcome and make life a little easier or more pleasant; a public facility.

   **Usage:** plural; plural-only

   **Example:** All the little amenities the hotel provided made our stay very enjoyable.

   **Example:** The complex includes the usual public utilities, such as gas and electricity, and amenities like Wi-Fi and a gym (its toilets with toiletries included too).

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A restroom or a public toilet (that is to say, one in a public facility).

   **Usage:** US; broadly; euphemistic; plural; plural-only

   **Example:** Where can we find the amenities here, please?
