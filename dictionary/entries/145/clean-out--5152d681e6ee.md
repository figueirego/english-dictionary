### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To clean, especially to tidy by removing the contents.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Clean out your purse and at least get rid of all the trash you're hauling around.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To take all money or possessions from.

   **Usage:** slang; transitive

   **Example:** The divorce cleaned him out.

   **Source synonyms:** clear out; take to the cleaners

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To hit or strike (someone); to beat up.

   **Usage:** dated; slang; transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To defeat, to cause to wash out.
