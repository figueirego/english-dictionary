### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Astonishingly great or large; huge; enormous.

   **Example:** One cannot appreciate how stupendous the Matterhorn is without seeing it.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of stunning excellence or degree; marvelous.

   **Example:** The renovator created a stupendous new look for our house.
