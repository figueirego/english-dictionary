## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Someone who is recognized by a religious body as having some authority within that body.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The leader of a religious order.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. In Catholicism, any of a number of individuals, including priests, cardinals, bishops, and the supreme pontiff.
