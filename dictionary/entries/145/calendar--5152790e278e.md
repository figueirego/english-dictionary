### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any system by which time is divided into days, weeks, months, and years.

   **Example:** The three principal calendars are the Gregorian, Jewish, and Islamic calendars.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A means to determine the date consisting of a document containing dates and other temporal information.

   **Example:** Write his birthday on the calendar hanging on the wall.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A list of planned events.

   **Example:** The club has a busy calendar this year.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An orderly list or enumeration of persons, things, or events; a schedule.

   **Example:** a calendar of bills presented in a legislative assembly; a calendar of causes arranged for trial in court

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An appointment book (US), appointment diary (UK)

   **Usage:** US
