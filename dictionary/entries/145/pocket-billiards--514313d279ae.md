### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of a family of billiards games played on a specific class of billiards table, having six receptacles called pockets along the rails, into which balls are deposited as the main goal of play.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** pool; pocket pool
