### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Not perceptible, not detectable, too small in magnitude to be observed.

   **Example:** We all missed the imperceptible shake of his head as he tried to warn us without being seen.

   **Source synonyms:** imperceivable; undistinguishable; unperceivable

   **Source antonyms:** detectable; perceptible

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Beyond the purview of man; too great and all-encompassing to be perceived.

   **Source synonyms:** cosmic

   **Source antonyms:** perceptible
