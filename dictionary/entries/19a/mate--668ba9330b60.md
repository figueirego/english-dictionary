### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An evergreen tree, Ilex paraguariensis, native to South America, cultivated for its leaves.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source synonyms:** yerba mate; yerba

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A beverage, resembling tea, made from the dried leaves of this plant.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** We spent a month in Buenos Aires drinking nothing but maté and Fernet with soda.

   **Source synonyms:** maté tea

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A cup of this drink.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The container used to prepare this drink, traditionally a small calabash.

   **Usage:** countable; uncommon

   **Source synonyms:** gourd
