## Adverb

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. recently; just now

   **Example:** I've only just cleaned the floor, and you've made muddy tracks!

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. barely; by only a small margin

   **Example:** I got 51%, so I only just passed the exam.

   **Source synonyms:** scarcely
