### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To cause to become smaller.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The dryer shrank my sweater.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To become smaller; to contract.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** This garment will shrink when wet.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To cower or flinch.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Molly shrank away from the blows of the whip.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To draw back; to withdraw.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To withdraw or retire, as from danger.

   **Usage:** figuratively; intransitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To move back or away, especially because of fear or disgust.

   **Usage:** intransitive
