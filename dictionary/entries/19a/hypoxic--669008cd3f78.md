**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of, pertaining to, or suffering from hypoxia.

   **Example:** Chronic obstructive pulmonary disease had left her hypoxic and wheezing.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Describing a breathing gas mixture that contains less than 21% oxygen.

   **Example:** Rarely, nitrox mixtures are intentionally hypoxic, under special conditions.
