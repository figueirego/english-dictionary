### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To boil to such an extent as to overflow its container.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** I forgot about the rice and it boiled over.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To reach the point where aggressive action is taken.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; intransitive; usually

   **Source context (verbatim):** of anger

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To act aggressively angry.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** He boiled over all night.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To buck aggressively.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The stallion boiled over, throwing Robert from her back.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of horse(s)
