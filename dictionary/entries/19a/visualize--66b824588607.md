### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To perceive (something) visually; to see.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To depict (something) in a way which can be seen.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to visualize data using a chart

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To form a mental picture of (something); to picture (something) in the mind; to envisage.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To make (a hidden or unclear body part, process, or object) visible by optical methods (such as endoscopy, magnetic resonance imaging, ultrasound, or X-rays), or other techniques.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

   **Source antonyms:** hide

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>
