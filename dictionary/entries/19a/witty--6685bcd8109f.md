### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Clever; amusingly ingenious.

   **Example:** His speech was both witty and informative.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Full of wit.

   **Example:** His frequent quips mark him as particularly witty.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Quick of mind; insightful; in possession of wits.

   **Example:** She may have grown older, but she has grown no less witty.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Wise, having good judgement.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Possessing a strong intellect or intellectual capacity; intelligent, skilful, ingenious.

   **Usage:** archaic
