### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** detachment; disjunction; division; rupture; severance

   **Source antonyms:** annexation; combination; unification; combination; composite; conjunction; fusion; junction; juncture; melding; merger; unification; union; unity

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The act or condition of two or more people being separated from one another.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The act or condition of a married couple living in separate homes while remaining legally married.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
