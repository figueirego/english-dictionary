### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To carry or bear from one place to another; to remove; to convey.

   **Example:** to transport goods; to transport troops

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To deport to a penal colony.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To move (someone) to strong emotion; to carry away.

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** Music transports the soul.
