### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. All of a countable group (considered individually), without exception.

   **Example:** Every person in the room stood and cheered.

   **Example:** She watches my every move.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Denotes equal spacing at a stated interval, or a proportion corresponding to such a spacing.

   **Example:** We stopped for refreshments every ten miles.

   **Example:** The alarm is going off every few minutes.

   **Example:** Every third bead was red, and the rest were blue. The sequence was thus red, blue, blue, red, blue, blue etc.

   **Example:** Decimation originally meant the execution of every tenth soldier in a unit; that is, ten per cent of soldiers were killed.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Denotes an abundance of something.

   **Example:** We wish you every happiness in the future.

   **Example:** He shows every sign of becoming an excellent player. I have every confidence in him.

   **Example:** There is every reason why we shouldn't.

   **Example:** The police will make every effort to trace the missing girl.

   **Source context (verbatim):** with certain nouns
