### Forms

- repellant — alternative

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A substance or solution used to repel insects, dangerous animals, or other pests.

   **Example:** None of the mosquito repellents we've tried work.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. A substance or treatment for a fabric etc to make it impervious to something.

   **Example:** They applied dirt repellent to the sports car.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. Someone or something that repels.

   **Usage:** broadly; obsolete
