### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A small shoot or twig of a tree or other plant; a spray.

   **Example:** a sprig of laurel or of parsley

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An ornament resembling a small shoot or twig.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. One of the separate pieces of lace fastened on a ground in applique lace.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A youth; a lad.

   **Usage:** derogatory; humorous; mildly; sometimes

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A brad, or nail without a head.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A small eyebolt ragged or barbed at the point.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**
