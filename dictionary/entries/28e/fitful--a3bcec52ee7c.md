### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Characterized by fits (convulsions or seizures).

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Characterized by sudden bursts of activity with periods of inactivity in between; intermittent, irregular, unsteady.

   **Usage:** broadly
