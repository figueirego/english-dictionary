### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An area in which land mines or naval mines have been laid.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A matter or situation presenting multiple risks, dangers, complications or difficulties.

   **Usage:** broadly; figuratively

   **Example:** Be careful. Foreign property investments are a minefield, and some people have lost a lot of money.
