   **Source synonyms:** employment; usage; note; nait

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

3. Usefulness, utility, benefit; purpose, point.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** What's the use of a law that nobody follows?

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** This tool has many uses.

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Occasion or need to employ; necessity.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** I have no further use for these textbooks, but we may have use for all these other things later.

**Source sense R1.S6**

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; rare; uncountable

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
