### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A village in Valky urban hromada, Bohodukhiv Raion, Kharkiv Oblast, Ukraine, founded in 1643.
   1. A former sotnia of Kharkiv Regiment, Sloboda Ukraine, extant at least as early as 1732, abolished in 1765.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A village in Valky urban hromada, Bohodukhiv Raion, Kharkiv Oblast, Ukraine, founded in 1643.
   1. A former volost of Valky povit, Kharkiv Governorate, the Russian Empire, established no later than 1779, abolished no earlier than 1914.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A village in Valky urban hromada, Bohodukhiv Raion, Kharkiv Oblast, Ukraine, founded in 1643.
   1. A former silrada of Valky Raion, Kharkiv Oblast, Ukraine, established in 1921, merged into Valky urban hromada in June 2020; administrative centre and sole constituent settlement&#58; Perekip.
