<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Source synonyms:** sweatshirt

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A similar garment worn for warmth.

   **Usage:** US

   **Source synonyms:** jumper; pullover; jersey; cardigan; wooly

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. One who sweats (produces sweat).

   **Source synonyms:** perspirer

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. One who or that which causes to sweat.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A diaphoretic remedy.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. An exploitative middleman who subcontracted piece work in the tailoring trade.

   **Usage:** historical

   **Source synonyms:** exploiter

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. One who sweats coins, i.e. removes small portions by shaking them.

   **Usage:** archaic

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A London street ruffian in Queen Anne's time who prodded passers-by with his sword-point.
