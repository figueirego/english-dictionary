## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A swear word or other offensive word.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A word or remark that is forcefully spoken, particularly during an argument, sharply expressing anger or criticism.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>
