### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Fully sufficient; found in copious supply; in great quantity; overflowing.

   **Example:** Blackberries are abundant in this part of the country in October, so we always make lots of jam.

   **Example:** an abundant selection of carpets to choose from

   **Example:** a delicate face framed by abundant black hair

   **Source antonyms:** rare; scarce

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Richly supplied; wealthy; possessing in great quantity.

   **Source synonyms:** flush; rife

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Being an abundant number, i.e. less than the sum of all of its divisors except itself.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

   **Source antonyms:** deficient
