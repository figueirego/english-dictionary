### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A nonvenomous snake of species Natrix natrix, native to Europe, with a yellow collar.

   **Source synonyms:** European grass snake

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**
