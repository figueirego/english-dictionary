**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To play about roughly, energetically or boisterously.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** sport (formal)

   **Example:** When the kids're allowed to romp in the bedroom, they break something.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. (Often used with down) To press forcefully, to encourage vehemently, to oppress.

   **Usage:** US; transitive

   **Example:** If I romp down on the gas, it'll do sixty in six seconds.

   **Example:** Coach Smith had to romp on 'em to get 'em out of a losing streak.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To make progress or win easily.

   **Example:** I romped through my finals.

   **Example:** England romped to an easy win over Australia.

   **Source antonyms:** squeak

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To move with little effort relatively quickly.

   **Example:** We romped along with the wind astern.

   **Source context (verbatim):** with adverb

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To engage in playful or illicit sex.
