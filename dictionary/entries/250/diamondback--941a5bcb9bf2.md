### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Either of two species of rattlesnake having a diamond pattern on the back&#58; Crotalus adamanteus, found in the southeastern United States, and Crotalus atrox, found in the southwestern United States and northwestern and central Mexico.

   **Usage:** common; name
