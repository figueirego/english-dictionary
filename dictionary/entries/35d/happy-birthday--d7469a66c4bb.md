### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Said or written to a person who is celebrating their birthday in order to convey the good wishes of the speaker or writer (literally, "may you have a happy birthday")

   **Example:** Happy birthday, Joe! Have a fantastic day!

   **Source synonyms:** many happy returns; many happy returns of the day
