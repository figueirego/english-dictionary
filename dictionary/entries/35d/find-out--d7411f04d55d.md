### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To discover, as by asking or investigating.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive

   **Example:** I don't know who the twenty-first president of the United States was, but it should be very easy to find out.

   **Source synonyms:** get at

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To discover or expose (someone) as disobedient, dishonest, etc.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He had been fiddling the books for years, but finally he was found out.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To uncover a weakness in (someone).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He may cope with the multiple choice questions, but the written exam will find him out.

   **Example:** If you don't try, you do not find it out.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To discover (something), as by perceiving or coming upon it.

   **Usage:** archaic

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To receive the consequences of one's actions.

   **Usage:** intransitive; slang

   **Example:** fuck around and find out

   **Example:** With the way he behaved, it's no wonder he found out.
