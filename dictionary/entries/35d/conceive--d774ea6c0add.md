**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To have a child; to become pregnant (with).

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** Assisted procreation can help those trying to conceive.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To develop; to form in the mind; to imagine.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To imagine (as); to have a conception of; to form a representation of.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive; ditransitive; with-of

   **Example:** Can you conceive of him as a leader?
