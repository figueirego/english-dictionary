## Verb · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /snɔɹt/ — source labels: General-American

### Forms

- snorts — present; singular; third-person
- snorting — participle; present
- snorted — participle; past
- snorted — past

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To make a snort; to exhale roughly through the nose.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** She snorted with laughter.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To express or force out by snorting.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He snorted a derisory reply and turned on his heel.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To express contempt or disgust by (or as if by) a snorting sound.

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To inhale (usually a drug) through the nose.

   **Usage:** slang; transitive

   **Example:** to snort cocaine

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To snore.

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To sail at periscope depth through the use of a snort or snorkel.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

   **Source context (verbatim):** of submarines
