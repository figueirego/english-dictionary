### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A sitting beside or near; especially as a participant in an official proceeding such as a meeting of an organization or a court case; an official membership or seat.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An official review and recognition of the feudal distribution of land, including the tenants living on that land and the terms of their holdings, which was held every seven years (the term of each tenant's lease).

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s3"></a>
