### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To evade or escape from (someone or something), especially by using cunning or skill.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To shake off (a pursuer); to give someone the slip.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To escape being understandable to; to be incomprehensible to.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I get algebra, but calculus eludes me.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To escape someone's memory, to slip someone's mind.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The solution of that brainteaser eludes me and the name of the author eludes my memory too.
