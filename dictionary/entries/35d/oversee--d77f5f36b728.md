### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To survey, look at something in a wide angle.

   **Usage:** literally; transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To supervise, guide, review or direct the actions of a person or group.

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** It is congress's duty to oversee the spending of federal funds.

   **Source synonyms:** superintend

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To inspect, examine.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Gamekeepers oversee a hunting ground to see to the wildlife's welfare and look for poachers.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To fail to see; to overlook, ignore.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To observe secretly or unintentionally.

   **Usage:** transitive
