### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A protuberance on the face housing the nostrils, which are used to breathe or smell.

   **Example:** She had a small nose between two sparkling blue eyes.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A snout, the nose of an animal.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The tip of an object.

   **Example:** the nose of a tea-kettle, a bellows, or a fighter plane

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The bulge on the side of a piece of a jigsaw puzzle, that fits into the hole of its adjacent piece.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The length of a horse’s nose, used to indicate the distance between horses at the finish of a race, or any very close race.

   **Example:** Red Rum only won by a nose.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; horse-racing; horseracing; horses; lifestyle; pets; racing; sports

   **Usage:** perfumery

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The sense of smell.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Bouquet, the smell of something, especially wine.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. The skill in recognising bouquet.

   **Example:** It is essential that a winetaster develops a good nose.

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. Skill at finding information.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** A successful tabloid reporter has a nose for gossip.
