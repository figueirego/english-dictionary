### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The lightest and most common isotope of hydrogen, having a single proton and no neutrons&#58; 11H. It is the only stable nuclide with no neutrons.

   **Example:** So far there are no differences among the protiums extracted at various labs, except for concentration levels.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics

   **Source synonyms:** mul&#58;H-1; mul&#58;¹H; hydrogen; hydrogen-1; hydrogen 1; light hydrogen; normal hydrogen

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An atom of this isotope.

   **Example:** This radioactive decay leaves behind a protium as well.
