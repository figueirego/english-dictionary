### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The crime of unlawfully breaking into a vehicle, house, store, or other enclosure with the intent to steal.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The crime of unlawfully breaking into a vehicle, house, store, or other enclosure with the intent to steal.
   1. Under the common law, breaking and entering of the dwelling of another at night with the intent to commit a felony.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The crime of unlawfully breaking into a vehicle, house, store, or other enclosure with the intent to steal.
   1. Under the Model Penal Code, entering a building or occupied structure with purpose to commit a crime therein, unless the premises are at the time open to the public or the actor is licensed or privileged to enter.

      **Usage:** US; countable; uncountable
