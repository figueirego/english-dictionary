### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Literary type using invented or imaginative writing, instead of real facts, usually written as prose.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** I am a great reader of fiction.

   **Example:** the fiction section of the library

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** literature; media; publishing

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A verbal or written account that is not based on actual events (often intended to mislead); an untrue belief.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The company’s accounts contained a number of blatant fictions.

   **Example:** The butler’s account of the crime was pure fiction.

   **Example:** separate the fact from the fiction
