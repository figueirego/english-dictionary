### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Eternity, the duration of the universe.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An immeasurably or indefinitely long period of time.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A long period of time.

   **Usage:** US; excessive; informal

   **Example:** It’s been eons since we last saw each other.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A period of one billion (short scale, i.e. 1,000,000,000) years; a gigayear.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astronomy; geography; geology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. The longest geochronologic unit, being a period of hundreds of millions of years; subdivided into eras.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; geology; natural-sciences

**Source sense R1.S6**
