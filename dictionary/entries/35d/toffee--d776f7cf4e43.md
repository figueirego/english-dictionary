### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A type of chewy confectionery made by boiling sugar (or treacle, etc) with butter or milk, then cooling the mixture so that it becomes hard.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A small, individual piece of toffee.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any kind of sweets; candy.

   **Usage:** Northern-England; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Nonsense; rubbish.

   **Usage:** UK; slang; uncountable

   **Example:** What a load of old toffee!
