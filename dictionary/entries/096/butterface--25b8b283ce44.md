### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who has an attractive body but an unattractive face.

   **Usage:** derogatory; slang

   **Example:** I can’t even look at her, she’s such a butterface.

   **Source synonyms:** biffer; boner-shrinker; bonerkiller; buttface; choad; frump; minger; monkey; monkeyface; munter; pukeface; schlub; stinka; troll; trollface; uggo; ugly duckling; toad; battle-ax; beldame (archaic); boot; brown-bagger; cunt; dog; gorgon; hag; harridan; moose; munter (UK; derogatory; slang); skag; skank; Sybil (archaic); two-bagger; whore

   **Source antonyms:** butterbody

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An unattractive face.

   **Usage:** derogatory; slang

   **Example:** I can’t even look at her, she’s got such a butterface.
