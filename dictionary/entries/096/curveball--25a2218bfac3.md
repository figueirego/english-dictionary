### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A forespin pitch thrown by rotating the index and middle fingers down and resulting in motion down "curve"

   **Example:** He bit on a curveball in the dirt.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; baseball; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

   **Source synonyms:** curve; deuce; yakker

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

   **Usage:** US; broadly

   **Example:** Life has thrown him a few curveballs.
