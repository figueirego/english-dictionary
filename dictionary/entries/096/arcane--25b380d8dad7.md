### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Understood by only a few.

   **Example:** arcane rituals

   **Example:** arcane knowledge

   **Example:** The professor’s lecture was full of arcane references.

   **Example:** The manuscript contained arcane symbols no one could decipher.

   **Example:** He had an arcane knowledge of ancient rituals.

   **Source synonyms:** esoteric

   **Source antonyms:** mundane

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Obscure, mysterious.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** arcane origins

   **Example:** arcane details

   **Source synonyms:** enigmatic; esoteric; recondite; clandestine

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Requiring secret or mysterious knowledge to understand.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Extremely old (e.g. interpretation or knowledge), and possibly irrelevant.

   **Example:** an arcane law
