### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any member of the genus Pterophyllum of freshwater, tropical cichlids.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any marine fish of the family Pomacanthidae, common on shallow tropical reefs.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>
