### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A room in a church where the clergy put on their vestments and where these are stored; also used for meetings and classes.

   **Example:** The choirboys change into their cassocks in the vestry.

   **Source synonyms:** vestiary; sacristy

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An assembly of persons (ad hoc or recurrent) who manage parochial affairs; so called because usually held in a vestry.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A committee of parishioners elected to administer the temporal affairs of a parish.

   **Example:** The vestry meets on the first Tuesday of every month.

   **Source synonyms:** parochial church council; PCC; parish council
