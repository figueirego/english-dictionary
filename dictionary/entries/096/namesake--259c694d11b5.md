### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An entity that lends its name to another entity.

   **Source synonyms:** eponym

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An entity that lends its name to another entity.
   1. A person with the same name as another.

      **Example:** The statesman Winston Churchill has a namesake, the American novelist Winston Churchill.

      **Source synonyms:** eponym; homonym (formal)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An entity that lends its name to another entity.
   1. A person with the same name as another.
      1. One who is named after another, often a child named after the parent or an ancestor.

         **Example:** Bill Sr's namesake, Bill Jr, is 30 years younger.

         **Source synonyms:** eponym; homonym (formal)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An entity that lends its name to another entity.
   1. A person with the same name as another.
      1. One for whom another is named, often the parent or ancestor who gave the name to a child.

         **Example:** Bill Jr's namesake, Bill Sr, is 30 years older.

         **Source synonyms:** eponym; homonym (formal); eponym

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Something (especially a ship, a building, or a medical condition, symptom, or sign) that is named after someone or something.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Source synonyms:** eponym
