## Interjection · Etymology 1 · Record 1

### Pronunciation

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An expression of one's own physical pain.

   **Example:** Ouch! You stepped on my toe! That hurt!

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An expression in sympathy at another's pain.

   **Example:** Ouch! Her sunburn looks awful.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A reply to an insult seen as savage (frequently one that is tongue-in-cheek or joking).

   **Example:** Ouch. How could you say that?

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An expression of disappointment.

   **Example:** Ouch, I really wanted to do that.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Expressing surprise at the high price of something.

   **Usage:** slang

   **Example:** Ouch, one hundred thousand dollars for a car! I could never afford that!
