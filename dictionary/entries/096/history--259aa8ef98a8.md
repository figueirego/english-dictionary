### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The aggregate of past events, both unrecorded and recorded.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The history of mammals stretches back many millions of years.

   **Example:** History repeats itself if we don’t learn from its mistakes.

   **Example:** He dreams of an invention that will make history.

   **Source synonyms:** past; ages; background

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The empirical study of past events, as distinct from literature, myth, or scripture; the assessment of notable events.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** He teaches Latin American history at the university and publishes books about the Cold War.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The portion of the past that is known and recorded by this field of study, as opposed to all earlier and unknown times that preceded it.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** in all of human history and prehistory

   **Example:** since the dawn of history

   **Source synonyms:** ages

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A set of events involving an entity.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** a long and sordid history

   **Example:** What is your medical history?

   **Example:** The family's history includes events best forgotten.

   **Example:** This car has something of a history attached to it.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A record or narrative description of past events.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** I really enjoyed Shakespeare's tragedies more than his histories.

   **Example:** a short history of post-Columbian colonization

   **Example:** Thucydides wrote a history of the Peleponnesian War that uses different sources than Herodotus' work.

   **Source synonyms:** account; chronicle; story; tale

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A list of past and continuing medical conditions of an individual or family.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** A personal medical history is required for the insurance policy.

   **Example:** He has a history of cancer in his family.

   **Example:** This diagnosis is usually based solely on the history and physical examination, although laboratory tests are occasionally also obtained.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** medical history

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A record of previous user events, especially of visited web pages in a browser.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** I visited a great site yesterday but forgot the URL. Luckily, I didn't clear my history.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** log

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Something that no longer exists or is no longer relevant.

   **Usage:** countable; informal; uncountable

   **Example:** I told him that if he doesn't get his act together, he's history.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. Shared experience or interaction.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** There is too much history between them for them to split up now.

   **Example:** He has had a lot of history with the police.

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. A historically significant event.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** You are witnessing history!
