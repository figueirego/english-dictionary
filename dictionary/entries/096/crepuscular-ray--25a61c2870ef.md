### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A sunbeam seen just after sunset or just before sunrise, caused by a cloud below the horizon and dust particles in the air above the horizon.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A cloud shadow cast on the bright sky.
