### Forms

- sturdier — comparative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of firm build; stiff; stout; strong.

   **Example:** a sturdy oak tree

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Solid in structure or person.

   **Example:** It was a sturdy building, able to withstand strong winds and cold weather.

   **Example:** The dog was sturdy and could work all day without getting tired.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Foolishly obstinate or resolute; stubborn.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Resolute, in a good sense; or firm, unyielding quality.

   **Example:** a man of sturdy piety or patriotism

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** hardy; staunch; stalwart; roboreous (rare); roborean (obsolete)
