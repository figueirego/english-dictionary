### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A plant whose stem is not woody and does not persist beyond each growing season.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Grass; herbage.

   **Usage:** archaic; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any green, leafy plant, or parts thereof, used to flavour or season food.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A plant whose roots, leaves or seeds, etc. are used in medicine.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** If any medicinal herbs used by witches were supposedly evil, then how come people from at least the past benefited from the healing properties of such herbs?
