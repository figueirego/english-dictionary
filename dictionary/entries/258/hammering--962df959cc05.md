## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

- hammerings — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A period of being beaten or hammered.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Repeated hammerings by the smith thin the metal and make it tough.

   **Example:** Hearing a hammering on the door, I ran downstairs to open it.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A dented appearance on silverware, produced by successive blows of a hammer.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A heavy defeat.

   **Usage:** countable; slang; uncountable
