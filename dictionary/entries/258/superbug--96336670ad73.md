## Noun

### Forms

- superbugs — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A strain of bacteria that is resistant to many antibiotics (e.g., MDR, XDR).

   **Usage:** informal

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any pathogen resistant to many, even all, of the biocides or other agents used to treat similar pathogens.

   **Usage:** informal

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An invasive insect, Bemisia tabaci, that is a major pest of vegetable crops, both directly and as a virus vector.

   **Usage:** US
