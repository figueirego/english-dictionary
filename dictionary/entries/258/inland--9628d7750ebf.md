### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Within the land; relatively remote from the ocean or from open water; interior.

   **Example:** an inland town

   **Source antonyms:** coastal; seaside; inlying

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Limited to the land, or to inland routes; not passing on, or over, the sea.

   **Example:** inland commerce

   **Example:** inland navigation

   **Example:** inland transportation

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Confined to one country or state; domestic; not foreign.

   **Example:** an inland bill of exchange

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Of a sophisticated background, especially as relates to a royal court or national capital.

   **Usage:** archaic

   **Example:** inland bred
