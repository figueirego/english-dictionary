### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The total amount of time required for the net magnetization vector to recover to its ground state in the direction of the main static magnetic field.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; nuclear physics

   **Source synonyms:** longitudinal relaxation time; spin-lattice relaxation time
