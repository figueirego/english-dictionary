## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A profound disruption in the shared frameworks for knowledge production, validation, and dissemination, where established epistemic regimes, methodologies, and concensus mechanisms fail, leading to widespread uncertainty regarding truth or reality.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any other crisis of epistemics or epistemology, such as a replication crisis.
