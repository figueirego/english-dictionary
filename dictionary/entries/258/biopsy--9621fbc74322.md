### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The removal and examination of a sample of tissue, cells, or bodily fluid from a living body for diagnostic purposes.

   **Example:** We will need to perform a biopsy to determine whether the tumour is malignant or benign.
