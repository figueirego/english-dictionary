### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any plant from which bees usually collect nectar, pollen, or both for making honey.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** agriculture; beekeeping; business; lifestyle

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The name of a number of not closely related plants.
   1. A plant of the genus Hoya, especially the porcelainflower or waxplant (Hoya carnosa) from the tendency for excess nectar to drip from its flowers.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; botany; natural-sciences

      **Source synonyms:** hoya
