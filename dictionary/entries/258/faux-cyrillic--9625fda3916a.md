## Noun · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The usage of Cyrillic letters in place of visually similar (though not necessarily phonetically similar) Roman letters in languages which use the Roman alphabet, to create a more 'Russian' feel for a text.

   **Example:** The line 'IБ ТНIБ ТЯIP ЯEДГГУ ИEСEББДЯУ?' (for 'Is this trip really necessary') is a piece of faux Cyrillic found in Frank Zappa's song 'I don't wanna get drafted'.

   **Source synonyms:** pseudo-Russian; fake Cyrillic
