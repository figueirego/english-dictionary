### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Below the level of the human.
   1. In a spiritual or metaphysical way.

      **Source antonyms:** suprahuman; superhuman

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Below the level of the human.
   1. In a taxonomic way that views humans as innately superior to other primates and other mammals.

      **Usage:** archaic

      **Source synonyms:** nonhuman; subhuman
