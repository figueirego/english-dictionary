### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Displaced from the centre.

   **Usage:** Australian; Canada; Irish; New-Zealand; South-African; UK

   **Example:** Place the decorations off-centre on the table.

   **Source synonyms:** askew

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Eccentric, unbalanced, slightly wrong.

   **Usage:** Australian; Canada; Irish; New-Zealand; South-African; UK; idiomatic

   **Example:** Every time I see him, he acts a bit off-centre.
