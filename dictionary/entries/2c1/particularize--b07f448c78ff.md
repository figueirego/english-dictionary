### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make particular, as opposed to general; to restrict to a specific or individual case, class etc.; to single out.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To be specific about (individual instances); to go into detail (about), to specify.

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To differentiate, make distinct from others.

   **Usage:** intransitive
