### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To retain (someone) as an employee.

   **Example:** Our company employs hundreds of people.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To provide (someone) with a new job; to hire.

   **Example:** Yesterday our local garage employed a new mechanic.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To use (someone or something) for a job or task.

   **Example:** The burglar employed a jemmy to get in.

   **Source synonyms:** apply; use; utilize

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To make busy; to preoccupy.

   **Source synonyms:** occupy; busy
