### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any word that has a magical effect when uttered.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One of the words please, thank you, or hello.

   **Example:** If you learn to use magic words when you are little, it would be easy in your adulthood.

   **Example:** What's the magic word?

   **Source context (verbatim):** in teaching or training polite words to children

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any word that is considered very significant in a certain situation or context.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** When someone offers you drugs, the magic word is no.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A string of text having some special function or purpose.

   **Example:** One of the magic words used in the MediaWiki software is \_\_NOTOC\_\_, which hides the table of contents that would normally be on the page.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences
