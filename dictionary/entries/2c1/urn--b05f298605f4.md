**Source sense R1.S1**

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A metal vessel for serving tea or coffee.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

   **Usage:** figuratively

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

   **Usage:** Roman; historical
