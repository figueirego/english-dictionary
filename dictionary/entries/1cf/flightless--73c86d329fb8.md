### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Unable to fly.
   1. Describing kinds of birds that normally cannot fly, such as the penguin, ostrich, and emu.

      **Usage:** not-comparable; usually

      **Example:** The indigenous fauna of Australia and New Zealand featured an especially interesting array of flightless birds.

      **Source synonyms:** nonflying; nonvolant; apterous; wingless

      **Source antonyms:** flying; volant

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Unable to fly.
   1. Describing an individual bird that normally would be able to fly but in this case cannot, such as an injured sparrow.

      **Usage:** not-comparable; uncommon

      **Example:** The animal shelter has a flightless sparrow named Shiny, who seems to make up for his disability by being an avid runner.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Unable to fly.
   1. Suddenly deprived of a scheduled (airline) flight by its cancellation.

      **Usage:** humorous; not-comparable; uncommon

      **Example:** Oh great, some wind is blowing in Chicago, so now I'm flightless in Seattle, because this airline can't cope!
