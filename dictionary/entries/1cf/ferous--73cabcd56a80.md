### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Used to form adjectives from nouns, with the sense “bearing an entity or entities as specified by the noun”.

   **Usage:** morpheme

   **Example:** flagellum + -i- + -ferous → flagelliferous (“bearing a flagellum”)

   **Example:** flos, floris + -i- + -ferous → floriferous (“bearing flowers”)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used to form adjectives from nouns, with the sense “producing a material as specified by the noun”.

   **Usage:** morpheme

   **Example:** nectar + -i- + -ferous → nectariferous (“producing nectar”)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used to form adjectives from nouns, with the sense “containing a material as specified by the noun”.

   **Usage:** morpheme

   **Example:** nickel + -i- + -ferous → nickeliferous (“containing nickel”)

   **Example:** cadmium + -i- + -ferous → cadmiferous (“containing cadmium”)
