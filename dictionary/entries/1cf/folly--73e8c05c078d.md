### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Foolishness that results from a lack of foresight or lack of practicality.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** It would be folly to walk all that way, knowing the shops are probably shut by now.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Thoughtless action resulting in tragic consequence.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The purchase of Alaska from Russia was termed Seward's folly.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A fanciful building built for purely ornamental reasons.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** A luncheonette in the shape of a coffee cup is particularly conspicuous, as is intended of an architectural duck or folly.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** architecture
