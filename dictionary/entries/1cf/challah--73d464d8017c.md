### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A traditional bread eaten by Ashkenazi Jews, usually braided for the Sabbath and round for a yom tov.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The commandment to separate a portion of bread or bread dough for the cohanim (Numbers 15&#58;17–21); in contemporary practice, the portion is burned until inedible.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The portion separated in fulfillment of the above.

   **Usage:** countable
