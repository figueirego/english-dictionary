### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The liquid said to flow in place of blood in the veins of the gods.

   **Usage:** Greek; countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; mysticism; mythology; philosophy; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The blood of human beings or animals; also (obsolete) the clear, fluid portion of blood; blood plasma, plasma.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; poetic; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A blood-like fluid.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; figuratively; poetic; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A fluid believed to seep out from magma and cause rock to turn into granite.

   **Usage:** archaic; broadly; countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; geology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A fetid, watery discharge from a sore; pus.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable; obsolete; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; pathology; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** sanies
