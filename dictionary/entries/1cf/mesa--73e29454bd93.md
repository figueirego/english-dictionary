### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A flat area of land or plateau higher than other land, with one or more clifflike edges.

   **Example:** A few more miles of hot sand and gravel and red stone brought us around a low mesa to the Little Colorado River.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. a structure with components rising above the insulating substrate that surrounds it

   **Example:** The ohmic contacts were deposited at the edge of the mesa.
