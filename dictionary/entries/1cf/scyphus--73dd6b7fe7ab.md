### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A kind of large drinking cup used in Ancient Greece and Ancient Rome, especially by poor people.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The cup of a narcissus, or a similar appendage to the corolla in other flowers.
