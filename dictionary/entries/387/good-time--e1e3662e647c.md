## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A period of time spent doing some pleasant, enjoyable, or entertaining activity.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** Let the good times roll.

   **Example:** A good time was had by all.

   **Example:** Q&#58; Did you have a good time at your friend's wedding reception? A&#58; Yes, it was a good time for everyone.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The reduction of a prisoner's sentence for good conduct while imprisoned.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A suitable time; a suitable occasion.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** When is a good time for you?

   **Example:** Is this a good time?

   **Example:** There's never a good time for \[X\].

   **Example:** It's always a good time for \[X\].
