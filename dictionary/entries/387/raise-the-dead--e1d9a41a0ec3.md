<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To resurrect a dead person, or dead people in general; to reanimate the corpse of someone, rendering them undead.

   **Usage:** literally

   **Source context (verbatim):** in some belief systems or in fiction

   **Source synonyms:** necromance

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To raise one's voice to a very high level; to be noisy or disruptive.

   **Usage:** derogatory; figuratively; slang

   **Source synonyms:** wake the dead; raise hell; raise Cain
