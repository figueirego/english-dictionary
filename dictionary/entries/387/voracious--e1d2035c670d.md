### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Wanting or devouring great quantities of food.

   **Example:** He is voracious by suppertime.

   **Example:** His appetite is voracious by suppertime.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a person or their appetite

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Having a great appetite for anything; eager.

   **Example:** a voracious reader
