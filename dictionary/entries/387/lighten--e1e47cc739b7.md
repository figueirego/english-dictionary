**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to lighten an apartment with lamps or gas; to lighten the streets

   **Source synonyms:** light up

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Usage:** archaic; intransitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

   **Usage:** transitive
