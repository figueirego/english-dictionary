### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A formula for preparing or using a medicine; a prescription; also, a medicine prepared from such instructions.

   **Usage:** archaic

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any set of instructions for preparing a mixture of ingredients.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A plan or procedure to obtain a given end result; a prescription.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** His new approach is definitely a recipe for success.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Now especially, a set of instructions for making or preparing food dishes.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A set of conditions and parameters of an industrial process to obtain a given result.

   **Example:** Stepper recipes.
