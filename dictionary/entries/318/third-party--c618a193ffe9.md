### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Someone not directly involved in a transaction; an entity beyond the seller (first party) and customer (second party).

   **Example:** A seller may employ a third party to perform specific services to augment the value of a product, such as packing and distribution.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; commerce

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Someone only incidentally or tangentially connected to an incident or dispute; someone other than the principals; a bystander or independent witness.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A political party in opposition to the main parties in a two-party system.

   **Usage:** US

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; politics

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A person or party not directly involved with an arrangement, in contrast to active parties, characteristically the first party (producer) and second party (consumer).
