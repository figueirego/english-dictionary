### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A spiderweb, especially a large one, an old abandoned one covered with debris and dust or an asymmetrical one woven with an irregular pattern of threads.

   **Source synonyms:** spiderweb; web

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A spiderweb, especially a large one, an old abandoned one covered with debris and dust or an asymmetrical one woven with an irregular pattern of threads.
   1. One of a spiderweb's filaments; gossamer; anything flimsy and gauzy, intricately interconnected or otherwise like a cobweb.

      **Example:** cobweb model, cobweb theory

      **Source synonyms:** spiderweb; web

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A spiderweb, especially a large one, an old abandoned one covered with debris and dust or an asymmetrical one woven with an irregular pattern of threads.
   1. An intricate plot to catch the unwary; something entangling or confusing.

      **Usage:** figuratively

      **Example:** I was confused by the cobwebs of contract law.

      **Source synonyms:** spiderweb; web; intrigue; web

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A spiderweb, especially a large one, an old abandoned one covered with debris and dust or an asymmetrical one woven with an irregular pattern of threads.
   1. Something thin and unsubstantial, or flimsy and worthless; valueless remainder.

      **Usage:** figuratively

      **Example:** blow the cobwebs away

      **Source synonyms:** spiderweb; web

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Fuzzy inexact memories; confusion.

   **Usage:** informal; plural-normally

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Fuzzy inexact memories; confusion.
   1. A web page that either has not been updated for a long time, or one that is rarely visited.

      **Usage:** Internet; figuratively; informal; plural-normally; rare

      **Source synonyms:** cobweb site

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. The European spotted flycatcher, Muscicapa striata.
