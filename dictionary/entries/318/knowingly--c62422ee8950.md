## Adverb

### Forms

- more knowingly — comparative
- most knowingly — superlative

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In the manner of one who knows.

   **Example:** She smiled knowingly, but kept the secret.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. With knowledge of all relevant facts.

   **Example:** She would never knowingly pour any pollutant down the drain.

   **Example:** We keep our prices low and are never knowingly undersold.

   **Source synonyms:** awarely; consciously

   **Source antonyms:** unknowingly; obliviously; unawarely; unconsciously; subconsciously
