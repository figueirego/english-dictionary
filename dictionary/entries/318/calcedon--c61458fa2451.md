### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any common mineral, such as chalcedony or calcite, that is found as an intrusion or vein in another stone, especially in a precious stone.

   **Usage:** dated

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The bass lute, an instrument used by baroque composers (notably Telemann).
