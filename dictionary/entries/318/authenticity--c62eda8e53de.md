### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The quality of being genuine or not corrupted from the original, of having the same origin (or attribution, commitment, intention, etc.) as claimed.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** I hereby certify the authenticity of this copy.

   **Example:** The authenticity of this painting is questionable.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The quality of being authentic (of established authority).

   **Usage:** obsolete; uncountable; usually
