## Conjunction · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Provided that, if, assuming (depending upon some condition or requirement).

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I don't mind if he stays there, as long as he cleans up after himself when he's done.

   **Source synonyms:** so long as; provided

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. For the entire duration that; while; since.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** As long as you're here, you may as well help me with the garden.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see as, long.

   **Example:** It took three times as long as I expected (= three times longer than I did).
