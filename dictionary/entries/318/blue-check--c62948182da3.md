### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A Twitter user whose account was verified by Twitter as authentic (i.e. not a parody or imposter account) and deemed to be of public interest prior to this system's replacement by a paid verification scheme in early 2023.

   **Usage:** Internet; derogatory; sometimes

   **Source synonyms:** blue check mark; blue tick

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An online troll, provocateur, or grifter who uses a paid account to attract greater attention after the early 2023 change on Twitter.

   **Usage:** Internet; derogatory
