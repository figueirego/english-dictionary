### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In the International System of Units and other metric systems of units, multiplying the unit to which it is attached by 10⁹ (short scale billion or long scale milliard).

   **Usage:** morpheme

   **Example:** giga- + watt → gigawatt ("one billion watts")

   **Source antonyms:** nano-

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Multiplying the unit to which it is attached by 2³⁰ or 1,073,741,824.

   **Usage:** morpheme; proscribed; sometimes

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** gibi-; mul&#58;Gi-

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Augmenting an adjective; intensely, extremely, or to an exceptionally high degree.

   **Usage:** morpheme; slang

   **Example:** Wow, I am giga-jealous right now.

   **Source synonyms:** super-; supra-; hyper-; ultra-; uber-; mega-

   **Source antonyms:** mini-; micro-; nano-

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Used to augment nouns; denoting an invididual who is a paragon of something.

   **Usage:** Internet; morpheme

   **Example:** giga- + Chad → gigachad

   **Example:** giga- + Stacy → gigastacy
