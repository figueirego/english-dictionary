### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To place; to set in a particular spot or position.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To find out where something is located.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I really can't locate the sever files.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To designate the site or place of; to define the limits of (Note&#58; the designation may be purely descriptive&#58; it need not be prescriptive.)

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The council must locate the new hospital.

   **Example:** to locate a mining claim

   **Example:** to locate (the land granted by) a land warrant

   **Source synonyms:** site

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To place oneself; to take up one's residence; to settle.

   **Usage:** colloquial; intransitive
