### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A distinctive smell.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** the scent of flowers / of a skunk

   **Example:** to give off / release / exude a scent

   **Example:** to breathe in / inhale a scent

   **Source synonyms:** aroma; bouquet; fragrance; nosegay; odor; perfume; redolence; smell

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A smell left by an animal that may be used for tracing.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The dogs picked up / caught the scent but then quickly lost it.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The sense of smell.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** I believe the bloodhound has the best scent of all dogs.

   **Source synonyms:** olfaction

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A substance (usually liquid) created to provide a pleasant smell.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** a scent shop

   **Example:** a scent bazaar

   **Source synonyms:** aftershave; cologne; eau de toilette; perfume; toilet water

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Any trail or trace that can be followed to find something or someone, such as the paper left behind in a paperchase.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

   **Example:** The minister's off-hand remark put journalists on the scent of a cover-up.

   **Example:** The tip put the detectives on a false scent / the wrong scent.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Sense, perception.

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

## Verb · Record 2

### Pronunciation

- enPR: sĕnt — variety unspecified in the source labels
- IPA: /sɛnt/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Forms

- scents — present; singular; third-person
- scenting — participle; present
- scented — participle; past
- scented — past
- sent — alternative; obsolete

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To detect the scent of; to discern by the sense of smell.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The hounds scented the fox in the woods.

   **Source synonyms:** smell

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To inhale in order to detect the scent of (something).

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Source synonyms:** sniff; smell

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To have a suspicion of; to detect the possibility of (something).

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** I scented trouble when I saw them running down the hill towards me.

   **Source synonyms:** detect; discern; perceive; sense

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. To impart an odour to, to cause to have a particular smell.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Scent the air with burning sage before you begin your meditation.

   **Source synonyms:** perfume; odorize

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. To have a smell; (figuratively) to give an impression (of something).

   **Usage:** intransitive; obsolete

   **Source synonyms:** smell

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. To hunt animals by means of the sense of smell.
