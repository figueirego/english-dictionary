### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make public or known; to communicate to the public; to tell (information, especially a secret) so that it may become generally known.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I will never divulge that secret to anyone.

   **Source synonyms:** disclose

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To indicate publicly; to proclaim.

   **Usage:** transitive
