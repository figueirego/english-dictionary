### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To expel from the lungs, throat, stomach, etc. by coughing.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He was coughing up blood.

   **Example:** He was coughing up a lung.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To reluctantly or unwillingly give.
   1. Of money&#58; to pay or disburse reluctantly.

      **Usage:** idiomatic; informal; transitive

      **Example:** Do you think he'll be able to cough up the three grand by Tuesday?

      **Source synonyms:** shell out; fork out; fork over

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To reluctantly or unwillingly give.
   1. Of other objects&#58; to hand over, give.

      **Usage:** idiomatic; informal; transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To reluctantly or unwillingly give.
   1. To confess; to give up information.

      **Usage:** idiomatic; informal; intransitive; slang; transitive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To lose a competition by one's own mistakes, usually near the end of the contest.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** That team had the game won, but they coughed it up in the end.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To spill, to fumble.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; transitive
