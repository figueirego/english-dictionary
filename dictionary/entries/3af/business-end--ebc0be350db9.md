### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The part of a day, event, or place (such as a city or district) used for business activities.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The part of an object (chiefly a tool or weapon) that is physically used for its operation, rather than some other part such as the part by which it is held.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** staring down the business end of a \[calibre of pistol here\]

   **Example:** The business end of a hammer is known as the head.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The part of an activity, period of time, etc., which is most important and produces the significant result.

   **Usage:** broadly; idiomatic
