### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A test of the stability of a vehicle, which determines the maximum speed it can attain and still be able to steer clear of an obstacle (such as a child or a large animal running in front of the car) without going out of control.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** automotive; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; transport; vehicles

   **Source synonyms:** elk test

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any stringent test of the quality of a product.

   **Usage:** also; figuratively

   **Example:** He passed the moose test.
