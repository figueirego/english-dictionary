### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A form of address to a man, usually a stranger or a superior.

   **Usage:** Ireland; London; UK; informal

   **Example:** Spare two quid, guv? I 'aven't eaten since yesterday.

   **Example:** Right away, guv... I mean, sarge.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A male prison officer.

   **Usage:** UK; informal

   **Example:** We cover up the window so the guvs can't see us.

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** boss; guv'nor; mate — to a stranger of comparable status
