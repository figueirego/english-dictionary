### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A woman who has never been married, especially one past the typical marrying age according to social traditions.

   **Usage:** derogatory; sometimes

   **Source synonyms:** old maid

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. One who spins (puts a spin on) a political media story so as to give something a favorable or advantageous appearance; a spin doctor, spin merchant or spinmeister.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Someone whose occupation was spinning thread.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A woman of evil life and character; so called from being forced to spin in a house of correction.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A spider; an insect (such as a silkworm) which spins thread.

   **Usage:** dialectal; rare
