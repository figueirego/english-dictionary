### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A geographic region in East Asia, consisting of two countries, commonly known as South Korea and North Korea; formerly a single country.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A geographic region in East Asia, consisting of two countries, commonly known as South Korea and North Korea; formerly a single country.
   1. South Korea&#58; A country in East Asia, comprising the southern part of the Korean Peninsula.

      **Usage:** countable; informal; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A geographic region in East Asia, consisting of two countries, commonly known as South Korea and North Korea; formerly a single country.
   1. North Korea&#58; A country in East Asia, whose territory consists of the northern part of Korea.

      **Usage:** countable; rare; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A peninsula in East Asia, containing the countries of North Korea and South Korea; in full, Korean Peninsula.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
