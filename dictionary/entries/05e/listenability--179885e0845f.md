## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A measure of the ease or pleasure in listening to something, such as music.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** The listenability of the music would be improved if you played it on a piano instead of a kazoo.

   **Source antonyms:** unlistenability
