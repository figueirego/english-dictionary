### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To drive together, round up.

   **Usage:** archaic; literally; transitive

   **Example:** The shepherds compelled the stray sheep into the fold as night began to fall.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To overpower; to subdue.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To force, constrain, or coerce.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Logic compels the wise, while fools feel compelled by emotions.

   **Example:** Congratulations, your courage compels respect.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To forcefully or powerfully motivate (a course of action).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** necessitate (formal)

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To have a strong, irresistible force (on someone or something).

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To exact, extort, (make) produce by force.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To force to yield; to overpower; to subjugate.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To gather or unite in a crowd or company.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. To call forth; to summon.

   **Usage:** obsolete
