## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A town in Highland council area, in northern Scotland (OS grid ref NH8856).

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A historical county of Scotland absorbed into Highland Region in 1975. Also known as Nairnshire.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A former local government district in Highland Region from 1975 until abolition in 1996.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A river in Highland council area, which flows into the Moray Firth at the town of Nairn; in full, the River Nairn.
