## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A member of a species of poisonous perennial flowering plants, Toxicoscordion venenosum (formerly Zigadenus venosus), easily confused with the edible camas, whose bulbs (rhizomes) look, but do not smell, like onion.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A member of any of various similar species, also called star lilies, of tribe Melanthieae in family Melanthiaceae, and formerly grouped into the genus Zigadenus (only Zigadenus glaberrimus remains there).

   **Usage:** broadly; uncountable
