### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Insolent and shameless audacity.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** We even had the effrontery to suggest that he should leave the country.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An act of insolent and shameless audacity.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** Any refusal to salute the president shall be counted as an effrontery.
