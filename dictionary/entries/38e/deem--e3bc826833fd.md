### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To hold in belief or estimation; to adjudge as a conclusion; to regard as being; to evaluate according to one's beliefs; to account.

   **Usage:** ditransitive

   **Example:** She deemed his efforts insufficient.

   **Source synonyms:** consider

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To think, judge, or have or hold as an opinion; to decide or believe on consideration; to suppose.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To judge, to pass judgment on; to doom, to sentence.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** adjudge; condemn; rule on; adjudge; adjudicate; arbitrate; deem (obsolete); doom (obsolete); hear; judge; rule on; settle; try; adjudge; convict; condemn; damn; deem (obsolete); doom; judge (obsolete); sentence

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To adjudge, to decree.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** declare; ordain

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To dispense (justice); to administer (law).

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** judge
