### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who wishes to be seen as a member of the upper classes or of some other elite (such as the masters of a certain craft) and who looks down on those perceived to have inferior or unrefined tastes, skills, etc.

   **Usage:** derogatory; informal

   **Example:** an insufferable snob

   **Example:** a wine snob

   **Example:** a food snob

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A cobbler or shoemaker.

   **Usage:** colloquial

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A member of the lower classes; a commoner.

   **Usage:** dated

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A workman who works for lower wages than his fellows, especially one who will not join a strike (a scab).

   **Usage:** archaic

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A townsman, as opposed to a gownsman.

   **Usage:** slang; Cambridge University

   **Source synonyms:** cad
