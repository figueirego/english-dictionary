## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An interpretation of quantum mechanics that claims to resolve all of the paradoxes of quantum theory by allowing every possible outcome to every event to define or exist in its own history or world.

   **Usage:** quantum mechanics

   **Source synonyms:** multi-history interpretation; MWI
