### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Said as a request to repeat information.

   **Usage:** Canada; US

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Said, as though as a request to repeat information, in order to indicate disbelief or indignation.

   **Usage:** Canada; US

   **Example:** It was so hot I took off my clothes and sat by the window to cool off. Excuse me?

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Said as a request for an individual's attention.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Said as a request to pass somebody.

   **Example:** Excuse me&#58; can I get through please?

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Sorry, as an apology.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Said as an expression of unexpected or early farewell.

   **Usage:** formal

   **Example:** Oh, excuse me, I hadn't realized how late it was. I need to get home and make dinner.

### Related words

**Source synonyms:** I beg your pardon? — request to repeat; pardon?; I'm sorry?; come again?; say again?; sorry? (Ireland; UK)
