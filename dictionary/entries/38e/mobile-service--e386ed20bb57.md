## Noun

### Forms

- mobile services — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A type of business or service that is provided on-site at various locations rather than at a fixed retail location, typically using a vehicle such as a van or truck.

   **Example:** "The mobile service offers convenient car repairs at your home or office."

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Services related to mobile technology, such as cellular phone services.

   **Example:** "Mobile service providers compete to offer the best coverage and data plans."
