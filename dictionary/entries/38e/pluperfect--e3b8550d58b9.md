### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. More than perfect, utterly perfect, ideal.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Pertaining to action completed before another action or event in the past, past perfect.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** grammar; human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Being or relating to a certain type of graph that complies with a theorem ("pluperfect graph theorem") discovered by D. R. Fulkerson in 1970, satisfying even more constraints than a perfect graph.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** graph-theory; mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Synonym of multiperfect.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** multiperfect (synonym; synonym-of)

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Used as an intensifier in various interjections.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** What in the pluperfect hell is going on here?!
