## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The state or quality of being stony.
   1. The state or quality of being full of stones.

      **Usage:** literally; uncountable; usually

      **Example:** the stoniness of this soil

      **Source synonyms:** rockiness

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The state or quality of being stony.
   1. The state or quality of being like stone&#58; immovable, unpersuadable, emotionally cold, or stoic.

      **Usage:** figuratively; uncountable; usually

      **Source synonyms:** coldness; iciness; stoicism
