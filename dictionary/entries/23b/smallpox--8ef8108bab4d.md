### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An acute, highly infectious often fatal disease caused by Variola virus of the family Poxviridae. It was completely eradicated in the 1970s, but still exists in laboratories. Those who survived were left with pockmarks.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Example:** The Europeans brought new diseases such as smallpox, measles, dysentery, influenza, syphilis and leprosy.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** medicine; pathology; sciences
