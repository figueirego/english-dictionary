### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Lacking dexterity in the use of the hands or of tools.

   **Example:** John was awkward at performing the trick. He'll have to practice to improve.

   **Source synonyms:** clumsy; ungainly; unhandy; gawky; lubberly

   **Source antonyms:** dexterous; graceful; handy; skillful

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Not easily managed; embarrassing.

   **Example:** That was an extremely awkward moment. Everyone was watching.

   **Example:** An awkward silence had fallen.

   **Example:** Things got awkward when my boss tried a cheesy pick-up line on me.

   **Source synonyms:** recalcitrant

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Lacking social skills, or uncomfortable with social interaction.

   **Example:** I'm very awkward at parties.

   **Source synonyms:** maladroit; gauche

   **Source antonyms:** amiable; cool

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Perverse; adverse; difficult to handle.

   **Example:** He's a right awkward chap.

   **Example:** These cabinets are going to be very awkward when we move.

   **Source synonyms:** cantankerous; ungainly; unwieldy
