### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The process of answering practical questions by means of interpretation of rules, or of cases that illustrate such rules, especially in ethics; case-based reasoning.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** casuistics

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An intricate argument, particularly
   1. Hairsplitting, argument with quibbling detail.

      **Usage:** countable; derogatory; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An intricate argument, particularly
   1. Sophistry, a specious argument designed to defend an action or feeling.

      **Usage:** countable; derogatory; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. An intricate argument, particularly
   1. Legalism, argument concerning the text of a written law against all other factors.

      **Usage:** countable; derogatory; uncountable
