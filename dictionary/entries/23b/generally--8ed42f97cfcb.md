### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Popularly or widely.

   **Usage:** broadly; comparative

   **Example:** It is generally known that the Earth is round.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. As a rule; usually; typically; in most cases.

   **Usage:** broadly; comparative

   **Example:** I generally have a walk in the afternoon.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Without reference to specific details.

   **Usage:** broadly; comparative

   **Example:** Generally speaking …

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. In the general case, without exception; in all cases.

   **Usage:** broadly; comparative

   **Example:** It is generally true that the angles of a triangle sum to 180 degrees.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Collectively; as a whole; without omissions.

   **Usage:** broadly; comparative; obsolete
