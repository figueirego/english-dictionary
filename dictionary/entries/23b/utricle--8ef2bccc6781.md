### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A small cell, sac, or bladder-like protuberance in an animal or plant&#58;
   1. The larger of the two fluid-filled cavities forming part of the membranous labyrinth in the vertebrate inner ear (the other being the saccule) and into which the semicircular canals open. It contains hair cells and otoliths which send signals to the brain concerning the orientation of the head.
