### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To feign death; to remain quiet and still to escape attention or remain undetected; to lie low.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** Thinking fast, we played possum, hoping the bear wouldn't bother us.

   **Example:** The soldier played possum, fooling the sniper.

   **Example:** To keep the focus away from his client, the lawyer basically played possum during the entire complex trial, and his tactic paid off with an acquittal.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To feign sleep, illness, etc.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** When we used to get home late at night, I would play possum so my daddy would carry me inside and put me in bed.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To dissemble or to feign ignorance; to disguise or conceal something in order to deceive.

   **Usage:** dated; idiomatic
