### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of several (not closely related) plants which react when touched.
   1. The jewelweed, Impatiens noli-tangere, Impatiens capensis, Impatiens parviflora.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source synonyms:** noli me tangere

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of several (not closely related) plants which react when touched.
   1. The sensitive plant (Mimosa pudica).

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source synonyms:** noli me tangere

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of several (not closely related) plants which react when touched.
   1. The squirting cucumber (Ecballium elaterium).

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source synonyms:** noli me tangere

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Lupus.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A queer person, typically a lesbian, who does not want to receive sexual pleasure.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable; LGBTQ slang

   **Source synonyms:** stone
