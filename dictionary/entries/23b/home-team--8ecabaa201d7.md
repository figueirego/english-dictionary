## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The team that is playing in the usual area that they play in, as opposed to their opponents, the visiting team.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** The home team really pulled off a surprise in the bottom of the ninth. It made for some really exciting baseball for all these lucky fans out here today.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; sports

   **Source antonyms:** away team; visitor team; visitor

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The ingroup (in whatever context).

   **Usage:** figuratively; idiomatic

   **Example:** Let's have a round of applause for the home team, as these quarterly production numbers are a big win.

   **Example:** Dang, I wish she played for the home team, because I would love to date her. \[Said by one straight person to another in reference to a gay person.\]
