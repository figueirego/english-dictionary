## Noun

### Forms

- soundalikes — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A sound that audibly resembles another.
   1. A music recording with this trait. (Often distinguished from plagiarism by themes of covering, pastiche (tribute), or parody, but sometimes inviting copyright disputes).

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A sound that audibly resembles another.
   1. A word that is either homophonous or sounds similar enough to another that confusion of the two is known to happen.

      **Example:** Tall-man lettering is sometimes used to reduce risk around soundalikes and lookalikes \[medication names\].
