## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A small piece of printed paper stuck on an item to be mailed, indicating that postage has been paid.

   **Example:** The postage stamps on the letters from overseas were almost as interesting as the letters themselves.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A very small area.

   **Usage:** colloquial; figuratively

   **Example:** We have just a postage stamp of a back garden.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**
