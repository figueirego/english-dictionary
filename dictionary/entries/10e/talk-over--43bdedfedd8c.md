### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To discuss; to converse about a matter or issue.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Come home early, we have a lot to talk over.

   **Source synonyms:** bespeak; betalk; conversate; debate; treat; bechat; confer; bespeak (archaic; formal); betalk (dialectal); chat; discuss; moot; talk about; talk over; treat

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To persuade (someone); to talk around.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I didn't like the idea at first, but they eventually talked me over.

   **Example:** She is an expert at talking others over to her views/side.

   **Source synonyms:** talk around; win over; bring round; convict; convince; draw; gain over (US); persuade; pimp (US; slang); prevail upon (idiomatic); procure; push; suade (obsolete); talk around; talk over; wash with; win over (idiomatic)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To verbally interrupt (someone); to speak at the same time as someone else.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** She rudely talked over her opponent in the debate.
