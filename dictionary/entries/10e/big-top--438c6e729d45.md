## Noun

### Forms

- big tops — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A circus tent, especially the largest in a given circus, where the central attractions take place.

   **Example:** As a trapeze performer, she generally worked in the big top, but due to her injury, she was stuck working concessions.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A large tent of the style used for circusses.

   **Usage:** broadly

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The circus; the circus lifestyle.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** He had been a big top performer in his youth, but in a twist of fate he had run away to become an investment banker.
