**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Merchandise that has not yielded any use yet&#58; from the view of the businessperson, one that has failed to be sold or processed and is now stowed away for possible sale or manufacturing at a later date, from the view of the consumer, a piece that has been obtained but not found application and is now—perhaps even with original labelling or packaging—stored for future ideas.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
