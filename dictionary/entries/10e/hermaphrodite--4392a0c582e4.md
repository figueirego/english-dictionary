## Noun · Record 1

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /hɝˈmæfɹədaɪt/ — source labels: General-American

### Forms

- hermaphrodites — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person possessing ambiguous sexual organs or characteristics.

   **Usage:** dated; offensive

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Cosexual&#58; an organism possessing both types of gonads; a flower possessing both stamens and pistils.

   **Example:** An earthworm is a hermaphrodite.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Dichogamous&#58; an organism which begins its life as one sex and changes its sex over its life cycle.

   **Example:** Comb jellies are sequential hermaphrodites.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** biology; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A person or thing possessing two opposing qualities.

   **Usage:** dated; offensive

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A hermaphrodite brig.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A farm wagon convertible to multiple purposes.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. An armoured fighting vehicle having features of both male tanks and female tanks.

   **Usage:** World-War-I; historical

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war
