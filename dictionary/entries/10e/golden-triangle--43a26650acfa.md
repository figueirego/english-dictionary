### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any region of prosperity, influence, etc. bounded by three points.
   1. A geographic region of Thailand, Laos and Myanmar, known for its production of opium and heroin.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any region of prosperity, influence, etc. bounded by three points.
   1. A region of Victoria, Australia, where the Victorian Gold Rush occurred.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any region of prosperity, influence, etc. bounded by three points.
   1. A region of New South Wales, Australia, where prime agricultural land occurs, particularly for cereal crops, and from which golden sheaves of wheat are harvested.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Any region of prosperity, influence, etc. bounded by three points.
   1. An area of Paris, France, located between Avenue Montaigne, Avenue des Champs-Elysées, and Avenue George V.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Any region of prosperity, influence, etc. bounded by three points.
   1. A region of Cheshire, United Kingdom, especially known for a large population of Premier League footballers and other wealthy entertainers and professionals.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Any region of prosperity, influence, etc. bounded by three points.
   1. A region of North Yorkshire and West Yorkshire, United Kingdom, located between Harrogate, Leeds and York.
