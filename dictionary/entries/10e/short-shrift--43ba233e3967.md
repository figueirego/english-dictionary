### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A rushed sacrament of confession given to a prisoner who is to be executed very soon.

   **Usage:** countable; historical; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Catholicism; Christianity; Roman-Catholicism

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Speedy execution, usually without any proper determination of guilt.

   **Usage:** broadly; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A short interval of relief or time.

   **Usage:** broadly; countable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Sometimes preceded by the&#58; a quick dismissal or rejection, especially one which is impolite and undertaken without proper consideration.

   **Usage:** figuratively; uncountable

   **Example:** The bank gave me short shrift when I applied for a loan.

   **Example:** The proposal got short shrift.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Something dealt with or overcome quickly and without difficulty; something made short work of.

   **Usage:** dated; figuratively; uncountable
