### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To sow seeds again; to apply seed to (a plot of land) again.

   **Usage:** intransitive; transitive

   **Example:** When the birds ate the seeds he had planted, the farmer was forced to reseed the field.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** agriculture; business; lifestyle

   **Source synonyms:** resow

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To produce seeds to ensure the following generation without human intervention; to self-seed.

   **Usage:** intransitive; reflexive

   **Example:** The marigolds had reseeded themselves in the flower box, so he didn't have to buy new ones this year.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** agriculture; business; lifestyle

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a non-perennial plant

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To reset the input of an algorithm so as to ensure different results.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The game reseeded its random number algorithm with a time-based value each time the game was restarted.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences
