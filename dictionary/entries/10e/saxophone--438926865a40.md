### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A single-reed instrument musical instrument of the woodwind family, usually made of brass and with a distinctive loop bringing the bell upwards.

   **Example:** I played the saxophone in high school.

   **Source synonyms:** sax; gobble-pipe; saxomaphone

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Someone who plays the saxophone; a saxophonist.

   **Example:** The saxophones typically sit behind the flutes in a concert band.
