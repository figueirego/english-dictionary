## Noun · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of speaking a language one does not know, or speaking elaborate but apparently meaningless speech, while in a trance-like state (or, supposedly, under the influence of a deity or spirits); glossolalia.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Christianity; Protestantism

   **Source synonyms:** glossolalia; speaking with tongues; tongues-speaking

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The ability of speaking fluently a foreign language without prior learning or knowledge, supposedly under the influence of God; glossolalia.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Catholicism; Christianity
