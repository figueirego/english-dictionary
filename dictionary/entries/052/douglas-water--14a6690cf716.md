## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A river in Glen Douglas, Argyll and Bute council area, Scotland, which flows into Loch Lomond.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A river in Argyll and Bute council area which flows into Loch Fyne.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A river in South Lanarkshire council area, Scotland, which joins the Clyde. Douglas in South Lanarkshire is also on this river.
