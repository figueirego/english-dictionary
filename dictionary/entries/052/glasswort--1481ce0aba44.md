## Noun

### Forms

- glassworts — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any plant of the salt-tolerant genus Salicornia (syn. Sarcocornia), once burned to produce the ash used to make soda glass.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Other salt-tolerant plants, especially those used to produce such ash.
   1. Species of the Australian genus Tecticornia

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Other salt-tolerant plants, especially those used to produce such ash.
   1. Arthrocnemum subterminale (Parish's glasswort)

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Other salt-tolerant plants, especially those used to produce such ash.
   1. Eriogonum salicornioides (glasswort buckwheat)

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Other salt-tolerant plants, especially those used to produce such ash.
   1. Kali turgidum

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Other salt-tolerant plants, called samphire.
   1. rock samphire (Crithmum maritimum)

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Other salt-tolerant plants, called samphire.
   1. golden samphire (Inula crithmoides, now Limbarda crithmoides)

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable
