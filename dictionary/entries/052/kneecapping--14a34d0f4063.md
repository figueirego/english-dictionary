### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of injuring the knees of (a person), usually by shooting at the knees; often a punishment carried out by criminals or terrorists.

   **Usage:** countable; gerund; uncountable

   **Example:** The mafiosi punished the informer by giving him a kneecapping.

   **Example:** Five kneecappings have been reported this month.
