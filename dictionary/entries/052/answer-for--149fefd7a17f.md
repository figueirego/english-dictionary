## Verb

### Forms

- answers for — present; singular; third-person
- answering for — participle; present

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To be held responsible for; to take the blame for something.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive

   **Example:** The mayor will be answering for his crimes for many years.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To guarantee.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** I will answer for his debt, if he can't pay on the day.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To vouch for (someone); to attest to the character of (someone).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** She's known me for years, and she's always answered for me!

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see answer, for.

   **Example:** I don't know the answer to that, so I'm going to let my lawyer answer for me.
