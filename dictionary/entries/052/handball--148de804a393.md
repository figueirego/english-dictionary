## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

- handballs — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A ball.
   1. A ball held in, or thrown with, the hand, especially one used for one of the various games in later senses, below.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A ball.
   1. The hollow rubber ball squeezed to produce spray from a perfume bottle or other atomizer.

      **Usage:** archaic; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A game.
   1. Any of various traditional ball games played outside between two goals often at a considerable distance from each other.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A game.
   1. Any of several games similar to fives, in which a ball is hit by the hand against a wall or within a walled court (the chief variants now being Gaelic handball and American handball).

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A game.
   1. A sport played on a rectangular court between two teams of seven players each, using a small ball struck only with the hands; team handball.

      **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A game.
   1. A schoolyard game in which a bouncy ball is struck with the hand, played on an improvised court on the asphalt or pavement.

      **Usage:** Australia; uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. An action.
   1. An act of passing a football by holding it with one hand and hitting it with the other.

      **Usage:** countable; Australian rules football

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. An action.
   1. The offence of a player other than the goalkeeper touching the ball with the hand or arm on the field during play.

      **Usage:** countable
