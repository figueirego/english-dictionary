### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A cross consisting of a vertical bar intersected by a shorter horizontal one above its midpoint, and again by another horizontal bar half the length of the first, intersecting the vertical bar midway between the larger horizontal bar and the top of the vertical bar&#58; ⟨☨⟩.

   **Source synonyms:** Lorraine cross; cross of Anjou
