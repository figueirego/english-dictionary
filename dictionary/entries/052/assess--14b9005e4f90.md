### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To determine, estimate or judge the value of; to evaluate; to estimate.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** He assessed the situation.

   **Example:** An insurance adjuster will assess the damage.

   **Source synonyms:** esteem; rate; value; appraise; apprise (archaic); apprize (archaic); assess; esteem (dated); evaluate; judge; price; prize (obsolete); rate; valuate; value; weigh (figuratively); worthen

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To impose or charge, especially as punishment for an infraction.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The referee assessed a penalty for delaying the game.

   **Example:** A $10.00 late fee will be assessed on all overdue accounts.

   **Example:** We were assessed $750 in court costs.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To calculate and demand (the tax money due) from a person or entity.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Once you've submitted a tax return, the Tax Department will assess the amount of tax you still owe.
