## Adverb

### Pronunciation

- IPA: /ˈlaɪkˌwaɪz/ — variety unspecified in the source labels

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In a similar manner.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** Public transportation is virtually inaccessible in this country; likewise, its hospitals are also not very user-friendly.

   **Example:** Come at six, and I’ll do likewise.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** manner

   **Source synonyms:** thuswise

   **Source antonyms:** otherwise

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. also; moreover; too.

   **Usage:** conjunctive; not-comparable

   **Example:** Margaret enjoys playing tennis on Saturdays, Jeremy likewise.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The same to you; used as a response.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** It was very nice meeting you, Samantha. ― Likewise, Mr Thompson.

   **Example:** ‘Let me know if you need any help.’ ‘Likewise.’
