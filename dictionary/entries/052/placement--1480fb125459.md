### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of placing or putting in place; the act of locating or positioning; the state of being placed.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A location or position.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** It seems to me that the placement of that poster could be better.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The act of matching a person with a job.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The agency does not guarantee placement, but they work on commission.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A temporary posting of a person to a particular workplace to enable them to gain work experience.

   **Usage:** UK; countable; uncountable

**Source sense R1.S5**
