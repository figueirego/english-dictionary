### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To induct (someone) into a dignity or office with a formal ceremony.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** inaugur

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To dedicate (a building, monument, etc.) for public use by a formal ceremony.

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To initiate or usher in (something, as a (significant) course of action, development, organization, or period of time) with a formal ceremony or in a ceremonious manner; also (loosely), to begin or commence (something); to start.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** handsel

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To cause (something) to be auspicious or good-omened; also, to declare or make (something) holy; to consecrate, to sanctify.

   **Usage:** archaic; rare; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** auspicate; avow; behallow; bless; canonize; consecrate; dedicate; hallow; inaugurate (archaic; rare); martyr; sacralize; sacrate (obsolete); saint; sanctify

   **Source antonyms:** corrupt; defile; desecrate; profanate; profane; profanize; pollute; sully; tarnish; violate
