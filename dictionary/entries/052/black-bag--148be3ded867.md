## Noun · Record 2

### Forms

- black bags — plural

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A black plastic sack produced for the disposal of household waste.

   **Usage:** Ireland; UK

   **Example:** The road was full of black bags awaiting the arrival of the dustbin men.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. The pouch or case traditionally carried by medical doctors, especially when making house calls, and containing medical instruments and supplies.

   **Usage:** US

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. A pouch or case containing burglary tools.
