## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A river in the Great Glen, Highland council area, Scotland, which flows from Loch Lochy to Loch Linnhe at Inverlochy.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A river in Argyll and Bute council area, Scotland, that joins the River Orchy at Inverlochy, near Dalmally.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A river in Otago, South Island, New Zealand, which flows into Lake Wakatipu; in full, Lochy River.
