### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Joint sovereignty over a territory by two or more countries.

   **Usage:** international law

   **Example:** The Anglo-Egyptian Sudan was a condominium ruled jointly by Egypt and the United Kingdom.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Joint sovereignty over a territory by two or more countries.
   1. A region or territory under such rule.

      **Usage:** international law

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A building or complex of buildings that provides multiple residential units each of which is owned separately but whose grounds, structure, etc. (if any) are owned jointly.
   1. The system of ownership by which such condominiums operate.

      **Usage:** Canada; Philippines; US

      **Source synonyms:** condo building

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A building or complex of buildings that provides multiple residential units each of which is owned separately but whose grounds, structure, etc. (if any) are owned jointly.
   1. An individual unit (such as an apartment) in such a complex.

      **Usage:** Canada; Philippines; US

      **Source synonyms:** condo building; condo

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A building or complex of buildings that provides multiple residential units each of which is owned separately but whose grounds, structure, etc. (if any) are owned jointly.
   1. The legal tenure involved.

      **Usage:** Canada; Philippines; US

      **Source synonyms:** condo building
