### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To act optimistically or as an optimist.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To make (something) optimal.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** We need to optimize the supply chain to reduce costs.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To make (something) more efficient, such as a computer program.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The website was redesigned to optimize user experience.

   **Example:** He used software to optimize the performance of his computer.

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To become optimal.

   **Usage:** intransitive
