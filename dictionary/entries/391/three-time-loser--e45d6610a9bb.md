### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A person who has been sentenced to prison for a third time, especially for a major crime.

   **Usage:** derogatory; slang

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Someone with bad luck or poor skills, and who consistently loses.

   **Usage:** derogatory; figuratively; slang

   **Example:** He'll never make anything of himself. He's just a three-time loser.
