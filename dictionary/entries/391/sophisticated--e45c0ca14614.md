## Adjective · Record 1

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of a person&#58; having obtained worldly experience, and lacking naiveté; cosmopolitan, worldly-wise.

   **Source synonyms:** [sophisticate](../15c/sophisticate--5715e496ab40.md); worldly; urbane

   **Source antonyms:** provincial

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of art or other things&#58; appealing to the tastes of an intellectual or sophisticated (sense 1) person; cerebral; also, cultured, elegant, refined.

   **Source synonyms:** [sophisticate](../15c/sophisticate--5715e496ab40.md)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Complicated, especially of complex technology.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Mixed with a foreign or inferior substance; not genuine or pure; unrefined, adulterated, impure.

   **Usage:** obsolete

   **Source synonyms:** polluted; tainted; adulterated; common (obsolete); contaminated; corrupted; mixed; polluted; sophisticated; tainted

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Of a thing&#58; having its meaning changed in a deceptive or misleading way.

   **Usage:** obsolete
