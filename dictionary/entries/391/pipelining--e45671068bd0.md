### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. (computer architecture) Overlapping the execution of successive instructions by dividing processing into stages that operate concurrently on different instructions.

   **Extractor diagnostics (not usage labels):** no-gloss

**Supplemental definition (evidence reviewed; source gap preserved):** The source has a blank noun meaning and computing derivatives metapipelining and superpipelining. The university architecture material supplies the staged-overlap mechanism explicitly, without claiming every processor has five stages.
