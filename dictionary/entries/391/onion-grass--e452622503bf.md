### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Species of Melica, especially Melica bulbosa and Melica subulata.

   **Usage:** US; uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of several species of non-native grasses of the genus Romulea, including Romulea rosea.

   **Usage:** Australia; uncountable; usually

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Wild or feral chives, garlic, or other species with grass-like leaves in the genus Allium, particularly Allium vineale, a wild herb native to Europe and western Asia, naturalised elsewhere; a common lawn weed.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually
