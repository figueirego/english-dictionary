## Noun

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Given a body B, the distance from a given axis of rotation at which a point mass of the same mass as B would have the same moment of inertia about said axis.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; physics
