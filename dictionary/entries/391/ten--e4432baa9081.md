### Forms

- tens — plural

### Senses

<a id="r2-s1"></a>

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. A set or group with ten elements.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** We divided the chocolates into tens to hand out to Hallowe'en visitors.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. An inexact quantity, typically understood to be between 20 and 100.

   **Usage:** countable; in-plural; uncountable

   **Example:** Our houses are tens of meters apart, so we don't have to worry about noise from our neighbours.

   **Example:** tens of thousands of voters

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. A card in a given suit with a value of ten.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** card-games; games

<a id="r2-s4"></a>

**Source sense R2.S4**

4. A denomination of currency, such as a banknote, with a value of ten units.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** Can you give me two tens for this twenty?

   **Source synonyms:** tenner

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. A perfect specimen, (particularly) a physically attractive person.

   **Usage:** US; countable; slang

   **Source synonyms:** dime piece

<a id="r2-s6"></a>

**Source sense R2.S6**

6. A high level of intensity.

   **Usage:** US; countable; slang

<a id="r2-s7"></a>

**Source sense R2.S7**

7. The act of rowing ten strokes flat out.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** hobbies; lifestyle; rowing; sports
