### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A psychostimulant, a dopamine reuptake inhibitor that affects the central nervous system and is used to treat ADHD. It is judged to have a high potential for abuse (Schedule II in the US). Chemical formula&#58; C₁₄H₁₉NO₂.
