### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of various insectivorous ground-feeding birds, mainly of the genus Phoenicurus. Many of the species have a red tail.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A species of warbler native to the Americas, Setophaga ruticilla, only distantly related to Phoenicurus.
