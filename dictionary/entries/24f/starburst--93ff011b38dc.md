## Noun · Record 1

### Forms

- starbursts — plural

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A violent explosion, or the pattern (likened to the shape of a star) supposed to be made by such an explosion.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A symbol similar to an asterisk, but with additional rays&#58; ✺.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** media; publishing; typography

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A region of space with an unusually high rate of star formation.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astronomy; natural-sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A period in time during which a region of space experiences an unusually high rate of star formation.

   **Example:** The Milky Way will see a starburst in approximately 200,000,000 years.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** astronomy; natural-sciences
