### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To bow (gesture of deference).

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To accept praise, as performers in a theater at the end of their act.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To end a performance; to leave.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To take credit or blame for a particular outcome or circumstance.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; sarcastic

   **Example:** The years we spent together were the worst years of my life. You can take a bow.
