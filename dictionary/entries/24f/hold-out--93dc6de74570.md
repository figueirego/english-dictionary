### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To hold (something) out; to extend (something) forward.

   **Usage:** literally; transitive

   **Example:** He held out his hand, and I grabbed it.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To offer, present (a hope, possibility, opportunity etc.)

   **Usage:** figuratively

   **Example:** The prospectus held out the promise of enormous profits to be made.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To wait, or refuse in hopes of getting something better (from a negotiation, etc.)

   **Usage:** idiomatic; often

   **Example:** I am holding out for more money.

   **Example:** How long has he been holding out?

   **Source context (verbatim):** with for

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To survive, endure.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** How long can they hold out without water?

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To withhold something.

   **Usage:** idiomatic; usually; with-on

   **Example:** You've got a key! Why have you been holding out on me?

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To set aside something or save it for later.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Pack the boxes, but hold out a few blue ones for later.
