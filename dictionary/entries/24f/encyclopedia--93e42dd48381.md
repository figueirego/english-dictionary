### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A comprehensive reference work (often spanning several printed volumes) with articles (usually arranged in alphabetical order, or sometimes arranged by category) on a range of subjects, sometimes general, sometimes limited to a particular field.

   **Example:** I only use the library for the encyclopedia, as we’ve got most other books here.

   **Example:** My old encyclopedia has been gathering dust on the shelf for the past decade.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Similarly comprehensive works in other formats.

   **Example:** Wikipedia is an online encyclopedia that anyone can edit.

   **Example:** The ancient Mesopotamians never got to write an encyclopedia on stone.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. The circle of arts and sciences (see Etymology); a comprehensive summary of knowledge, or of a branch thereof.

   **Usage:** dated; specifically

   **Example:** His life's work is a four-volume encyclopedia of aviation topics.

   **Example:** I have no idea how to build this model railroad, so I'm gonna look it up on Trainpedia, the encyclopedia for all things model train.
