### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In a military or martial manner; not peaceably.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. By way of military or otherwise belligerent means.

   **Example:** The borders of the empire were expanded militarily over the course of many wars of conquest.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. In a manner relating to the military or military matters.
