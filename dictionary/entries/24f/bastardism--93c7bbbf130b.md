### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The condition of being born out of wedlock; bastardy.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A tendency to produce bastards; lack of chastity.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Racial impurity; that state or quality of being mixed-race.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Sexual reproduction involving different species.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An unnatural combination; a mixture of things that do not belong together.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Ideological concern with whether someone is related by blood.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. An act or quality of being bastardly; contemptibleness, cruelty, or lack of proper behavior.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
