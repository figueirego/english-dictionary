**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make a short, loud, explosive noise with the vocal organs (said of animals, especially dogs).

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** The neighbour's dog is always barking.

   **Example:** The seal barked as the zookeeper threw fish into its enclosure.

   **Source synonyms:** give tongue; latrate (rare)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

   **Usage:** intransitive

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To speak sharply.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The sergeant barked an order.
