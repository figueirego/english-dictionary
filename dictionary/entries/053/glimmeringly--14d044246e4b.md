### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. In a glimmering manner.

   **Example:** Lamplight was reflected glimmeringly from the wet street.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. By way of vague, imprecise notions or suspicions.

   **Example:** The truth came to her glimmeringly.
