### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To enter something quickly, usually a mode of transport.

   **Example:** I jumped in the car, and we sped off to the meeting.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To join in on an activity quickly.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To interrupt someone while they are speaking.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To initiate into an organization, usually a gang, with violence.

   **Usage:** US; slang

   **Source antonyms:** jump out

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To play a card that matches the top card in the discard pile out of turn.

   **Usage:** Uno

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see jump, in.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To ambush (someone).

   **Usage:** dated; slang
