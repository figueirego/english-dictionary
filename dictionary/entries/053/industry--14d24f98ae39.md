## Noun

### Pronunciation

### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The tendency to work persistently.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Over the years, their industry and business sense made them wealthy.

   **Source synonyms:** industriousness; diligence; hardworkingness

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Businesses of the same type, considered as a whole; trade.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** The software and tourism industries continue to grow, while the steel industry remains troubled.

   **Example:** The steel industry has long used blast furnaces to smelt iron.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; economics; sciences

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Businesses that produce goods as opposed to services.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** economics; sciences

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The sector of the economy consisting of large-scale enterprises.

   **Usage:** countable; singular; uncountable

   **Example:** There used to be a lot of industry around here, but now the economy depends on tourism.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** economics; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Automated production of material goods.

   **Usage:** Europe; countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; intellectual-property; law; mathematics; natural-sciences; patent-law; physical-sciences; sciences; software

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A typological classification of stone tools, associated with a technocomplex.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** archaeology; history; human-sciences; sciences
