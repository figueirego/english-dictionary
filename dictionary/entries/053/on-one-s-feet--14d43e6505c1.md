## Prep\_phrase

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. While standing up or walking, running, etc.

   **Example:** He's very quick on his feet.

   **Example:** She's so light on her feet that we couldn't hear her coming.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Able to stand; hence, healthy, well, especially after some previous illness.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** Welcome back! It's good to see you back on your feet.

   **Example:** He’s so exhausted he can’t stay on his feet.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. In a satisfactory (nonphysical) condition; happy financially, emotionally, etc.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** The organization provides training and assistance to help the unemployed get back on their feet.

   **Example:** It took some time for the lady to get back on her feet after the death of her husband.

   **Source synonyms:** on one's legs; upon one's legs

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see on, feet.
