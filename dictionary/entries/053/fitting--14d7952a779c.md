   1. An act of trying on an item of clothing to adjust or inspect how well it fits.

      **Usage:** countable; uncountable

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; clothing; fashion; lifestyle; manufacturing; textiles

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. A (generally small) component or part of a device or machine, especially one that is standardized and replaceable; specifically, a standardized part of a piping system used to connect sections of pipe together, such as a coupling.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; engineering; manufacturing; mechanical; mechanical-engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

**Source sense R2.S4**

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** business; engineering; manufacturing; mechanical; mechanical-engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences

<a id="r2-s5"></a>

**Source sense R2.S5**

5. A removable item (such as a carpet, a movable piece of furniture, or a picture) in a house or other building, which can be taken with one when moving out.

   **Usage:** UK; countable; in-plural; often

   **Example:** the fittings of a church or study
