### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A certain kind of sequence of prime numbers, with applications in cryptography.
   1. A Cunningham chain of the first kind of length n is a sequence of prime numbers (p₁, ..., pₙ) such that for all 1 ≤ i &lt; n, pᵢ₊₁ = 2pᵢ + 1.

      **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

   1. A Cunningham chain of the second kind of length n is a sequence of prime numbers (p₁,...,pₙ) such that for all 1 ≤ i &lt; n, pᵢ₊₁ = 2pᵢ − 1.
