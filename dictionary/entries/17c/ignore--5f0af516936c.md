### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To deliberately not listen or pay attention to.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** A problem ignored is a problem doubled.

   **Source synonyms:** disregard; neglect

   **Source antonyms:** notice; recognize; watch; attend (archaic); clock (slang); consider; give heed; heed; let in on; mark; mind (rare); notice; pass (obsolete); pay attention; pay heed; pick up what someone is putting down; privy; regard; take heed; take note; take notice

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To pretend to not notice someone or something.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source synonyms:** overlook; turn a blind eye to; wink at; connive

   **Source antonyms:** notice; observe

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Fail to notice.

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** overlook; ignore; misheed; misregard; miss; overlook; overpass; oversee (obsolete)

**Source sense R1.S4**

   **Usage:** obsolete; transitive

   **Source synonyms:** be ignorant of

   **Source antonyms:** know
