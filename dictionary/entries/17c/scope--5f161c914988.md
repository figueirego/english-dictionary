**Source sense R1.S1**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Environmental impacts lie outside the scope of this report.

   **Example:** Such transactions fall within the scope of VAT.

   **Source synonyms:** compass; ambit

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** telescopic sight

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Potential range of action; degree of freedom; opportunity.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** My job doesn't give me much scope for personal development.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The region of program source code in which a given identifier is meaningful, or a given object can be accessed.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; programming; sciences

**Source sense R1.S5**

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; logic; mathematics; philosophy; sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** the scope of an adverb

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences
