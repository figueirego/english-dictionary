### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A select body of (usually eminent) justices of the peace, every member of which had to be present to constitute a deciding body; a member of this body. Later more generally&#58; all justices collectively.

   **Usage:** historical

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The minimum number of members required for a group to officially conduct business and to cast votes, often but not necessarily a majority or supermajority.

   **Example:** We can discuss the issue tonight but cannot vote until we have a quorum.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Distinguished or essential members of any body; a select company.

   **Usage:** archaic

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. The minimum number of votes that a distributed transaction has to obtain in order to be allowed to perform an operation in a distributed system.

   **Usage:** distributed systems

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** computing; engineering; mathematics; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; sciences
