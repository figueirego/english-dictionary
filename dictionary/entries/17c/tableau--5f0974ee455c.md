### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A striking and vivid representation or scene; a picture.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A vivid graphic scene of a group of people or objects arranged as in a painting or bas relief sculpture.

   **Source synonyms:** tableau vivant

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An arrangement of actors in static positions on stage, having the effect of pointing up a particular moment in the drama, conventionally revealed by opening tableau curtains (known as "tabs").

   **Usage:** UK; broadly; dated

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A two-dimensional array or table of data, usually numbers, of various specific kinds.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A table that shows constraint violations of a list of candidates given an input and a constraint ranking.

   **Usage:** optimality theory

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; linguistics; sciences

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Mostly in solitaire card games, but also in other card and board games, the main area, where random cards can be arranged.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** card-games; games

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A semantic tableau.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; logic; mathematics; philosophy; sciences

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A unit of a play, an opera, or a ballet with change of stage setting.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; theater
