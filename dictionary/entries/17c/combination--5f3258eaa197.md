### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of combining, the state of being combined or the result of combining.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** combinations of colors

   **Example:** These guidelines should be followed in combination with those given last week.

   **Example:** She took a dangerous combination of alcohol and heroin.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. An object formed by combining.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The chef experimented with new flavor combinations.

   **Example:** Certain drug combinations can be dangerous.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A sequence of numbers or letters used to open a combination lock.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The combination to their safe was the date of birth of their first child.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** mathematics; sciences
