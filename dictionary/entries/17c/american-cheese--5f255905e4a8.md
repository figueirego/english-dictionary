### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A common processed cheese, yellow or white in color and mild in flavor, with a medium-firm consistency.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** Many of you other brothers can't deny that American cheese on white bread with yellow mustard is a guilty pleasure that reminds us of our childhood.

   **Source synonyms:** store cheese; rat-trap cheese; rat cheese; government cheese

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see American, cheese&#58; any cheese made in America.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** The opinions on importation of American wheat and steel did not seem as negative as those against importation of American cheese and beer.
