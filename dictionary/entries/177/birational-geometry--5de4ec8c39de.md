## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A field of algebraic geometry in which the aim is to determine under what conditions two algebraic varieties are isomorphic outside lower-dimensional subsets.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** Birational geometry amounts to the study of mappings given by rational functions rather than polynomials.
