**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Trees in a forest regarded as a source of wood.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** collect timber

   **Example:** cut down timber

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Wood that has been pre-cut and is ready for use in construction.

   **Usage:** US; uncountable; outside Canada

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A heavy wooden beam, generally a whole log that has been squared off and used to provide heavy support for something such as a roof.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** the timbers of a ship

**Source sense R1.S4**

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable
