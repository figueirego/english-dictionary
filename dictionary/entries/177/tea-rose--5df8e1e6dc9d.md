### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A plant of a Chinese rose species with tea-scented flowers, Rosa odorata.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A flower from such a plant.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A plant of one of the classes of hybrid roses developed from Rosa odorata, usually including crosses with the repeat-blooming China rose, Rosa chinensis in their ancestry.

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A flower from such a plant.
