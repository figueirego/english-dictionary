### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To be organized; to have one's affairs in order; specifically, to have a multiperson effort coordinated towards the exact same goal.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** Wouldn't it be nice to have our ducks in a row and not have to search for the papers every time we needed them?
