### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. simple past and past participle of have

   **Usage:** form-of; participle; past

   **Example:** This morning I had an egg for breakfast.

   **Example:** A good time was had by all.

   **Form of:** [have](../064/have--193c45b52819.md)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Used to form the past perfect tense, expressing an action that took place prior to a reference point that is itself in the past.

   **Usage:** auxiliary

   **Example:** I felt sure that I had seen him before.

   **Example:** When I'd (already) done some exercise, I had a cappuccino.

   **Source context (verbatim):** followed by a past participle

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. As past subjunctive&#58; would have.

   **Usage:** archaic; auxiliary

   **Example:** Had I not known you were coming this evening, I'd have left at midday.
