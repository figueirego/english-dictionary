### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A set of pieces that work together in unison as a mechanism or device.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** In order to change the bearing, you must first remove the gearbox assembly.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The act or process of putting together a set of pieces, fragments, or elements.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** instructions for assembly

   **Example:** some assembly required

   **Example:** assembly line

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A congregation of people in one place for a purpose.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** school assembly

   **Example:** freedom of assembly

   **Source synonyms:** gathering

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A legislative body.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** the General Assembly of the United Nations

   **Example:** New York State Assembly

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; politics

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A beat of the drum or sound of the bugle as a signal to troops to assemble.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** government; military; politics; war
