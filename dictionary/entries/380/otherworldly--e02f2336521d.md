### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of, concerned with, or preoccupied with a world different from the tangible world, especially a fantasy, imaginary, or mystical world.

   **Source synonyms:** ethereal; transcendental; unworldly

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of, concerned with, or preoccupied with a world different from the tangible world, especially a fantasy, imaginary, or mystical world.
   1. Odd and unfamiliar; strange, uncanny, weird.

      **Usage:** figuratively

      **Source synonyms:** ethereal; transcendental; unworldly; alien; other-worldish

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of, concerned with, or preoccupied with spiritual matters.

   **Source synonyms:** unworldly

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

   **Usage:** broadly; obsolete; rare
