### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

   1. the buckthorn family (Rhamnaceae),
      1. especially genus Rhamnus,

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of several, often thorny shrubs or small trees, especially
   1. the buckthorn family (Rhamnaceae),
      1. especially genus Rhamnus,
         1. especially common buckthorn or purging buckthorn (Rhamnus cathartica).
