### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Having a strong desire for power.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** The accused politician was a power-hungry man who broke the law so he could help his buddies.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of a device, using or requiring a large amount of electrical power.

   **Example:** The latest model is also the most power-hungry in the lineup.
