### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To lower in value or social position.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** Fred degrades himself by his behaviour.

   **Source synonyms:** abase; debase; demean; abase; avile (obsolete); debase; debauch; degrade; depreciate; devalue; devaluate; dirty; elevate (Latinism; obsolete); vitiate; abase; bring down to size (idiomatic); cut down to size (idiomatic); debase (archaic); defame; degrade; demean; demote; depress; discredit; disgrace; dishonour; disparage; downgrade; embase (archaic); give a bad name; humble; humiliate; knock down to size (idiomatic); put someone in their place (idiomatic); reduce; relegate; sink (figuratively); slur; trim down to size (idiomatic); whittle down to size (idiomatic)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To reduce in quality or purity.

   **Usage:** ergative; intransitive

   **Example:** The DNA sample has degraded.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To reduce in altitude or magnitude, as hills and mountains; to wear down.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** geography; geology; natural-sciences
