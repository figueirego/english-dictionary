### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Darkness where light, particularly sunlight, is blocked.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** The old oak tree gave shade in the heat of the day.

   **Example:** Temps rose to a sweltering 40 degrees Celsius in the shade.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Something that blocks light, particularly in a window.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** Close the shade, please&#58; it's too bright in here.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A variety of a color, in particular one obtained by adding black (compare tint).

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** I've painted my room in five lovely shades of pink and chartreuse.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A subtle variation in a concept.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; uncountable

   **Example:** shades of meaning

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. An aspect that is reminiscent of something.

   **Usage:** countable; figuratively; in-plural; uncountable

   **Example:** shades of Groucho Marx

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. A very small degree of a quantity, or variety of meaning

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Example:** That is a shade too close.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. A ghost or specter; a spirit.

   **Usage:** countable; literary; uncountable

   **Example:** Too long have I been haunted by that shade.

   **Example:** The adventurer was attacked by a shade.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** fantasy

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. A postage stamp showing an obvious difference in colour/color to the original printing and needing a separate catalogue/catalog entry.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. Subtle insults.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** throw shade

   **Example:** Why did you paint your room chartreuse? No shade; I'm genuinely curious.

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

   **Usage:** countable

**Source sense R2.S1**

1. To shield (someone or something) from light.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The old oak tree shaded the lawn in the heat of the day.

<a id="r2-s2"></a>

**Source sense R2.S2**

2. To shield oneself from light.

   **Usage:** intransitive; rare

   **Example:** We shaded under a huge oak tree.

<a id="r2-s3"></a>

**Source sense R2.S3**

3. To alter slightly.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** You'll need to shade your shot slightly to the left.
