### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A French surname from Breton.

   **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A French surname from Breton.
   1. Yves-Joseph de Kerguelen-Trémarec (13 February 1734 – 3 March 1797) was a French explorer and naval officer.

      **Usage:** countable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A placename
   1. Ellipsis of Kerguelen Plateau&#58; a microcontinent and large igneous province in the Southern Ocean.

      **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; ellipsis

      **Alternative form of:** Kerguelen Plateau

5. A placename
   1. Ellipsis of Kerguelen Islands.
