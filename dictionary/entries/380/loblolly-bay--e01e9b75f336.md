### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of species Gordonia lasianthus of elegant white-flowered evergreen shrubs or small trees, growing in the maritime parts of the southern United States. Its bark is sometimes used in tanning.
