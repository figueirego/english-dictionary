**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A multisystem pregnancy disorder, defined by hypertension arising after twenty weeks of gestation, usually accompanied by proteinuria, and associated with placental dysfunction and maternal organ involvement.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** gynaecology; medicine; obstetrics; sciences
