### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of telephone numbers dialed in North America, having an additional charge assessed to the caller, part of which is returned to the owner of the number.

   **Usage:** Canada; US; not-comparable

   **Example:** 976-5555 is a 1-976 number that you have to pay for.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** communications; electrical-engineering; engineering; natural-sciences; physical-sciences; telecommunications; telephony
