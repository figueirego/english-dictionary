### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To alternate between two opposite extremes, such as enthusiasm and disinterest or success and failure.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** Henrietta's feelings for Delbert run hot and cold—one minute she's hopelessly in love with him, and the next she can't stand the sight of him.

   **Example:** The business runs hot and cold; some months our firm doesn't earn enough to cover expenses and other months, we net 400% profits.
