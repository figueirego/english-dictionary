### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A raucous and ardent choral hymn sung in ancient Athens in honor of the god Dionysus.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A poem or oration in the same style.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An impassioned speech; a rant.

   **Source synonyms:** admonition; condemnation; diatribe; dithyramb; harangue; impugnation; j'accuse; jeremiad; philippic; polemic; rant; screed; slanging match; tirade
