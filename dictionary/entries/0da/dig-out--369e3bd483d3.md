### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To remove something by digging.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The archaeologist dug out a Saxon dagger.

   **Example:** Houdini not only got out of the ropes&#58; he also dug himself out of the hole he had been buried in.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To find or retrieve something buried.

   **Usage:** figuratively; sometimes; transitive

   **Example:** I shall try to dig out my old textbooks.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To make something by digging.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** We had to dig out our foxhole while under fire.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To decamp; to leave a place hastily.

   **Usage:** US; intransitive; slang

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To have penetrative sexual intercourse with someone.

   **Usage:** slang; transitive

   **Example:** I'd like to dig her out.
