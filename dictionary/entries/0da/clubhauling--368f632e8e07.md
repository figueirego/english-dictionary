### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A risky maneuver whereby a sail-boat tacks (turns into and through the wind) by dropping one of the front anchors, the intent being to turn the boat in a tighter space than would be possible using the rudder and sails alone, especially when difficult conditions such as light winds or heavy swell make tacking more difficult than usual.

   **Usage:** uncountable; usually

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** nautical; transport
