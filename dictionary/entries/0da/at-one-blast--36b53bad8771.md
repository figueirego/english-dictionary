### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. At once, at the same moment in time.

   **Usage:** figuratively; not-comparable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. With one firing of a cannon or firearm; especially one whose ammunition is shotgun shot or grape shot, such that the multiple pieces of shot from one firing hit multiple birds or multiple soldiers.

   **Usage:** literally; not-comparable
