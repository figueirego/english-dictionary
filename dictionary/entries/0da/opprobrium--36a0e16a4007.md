### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A cause, object, or situation of disgrace or shame.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable

   **Source synonyms:** opprobry (obsolete)

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Disgrace or bad reputation arising from exceedingly shameful behaviour; ignominy.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** obloquy; opprobry (obsolete)

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Scornful contempt or reproach; (countable) an instance of this.

   **Usage:** countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** blame; castigation; censure; derision; invective; opprobry (obsolete); contempt; despect; despiciency (obsolete); despisal; despisement; disdain; scorn

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Behaviour which is disgraceful or shameful.

   **Usage:** archaic; countable; uncountable
