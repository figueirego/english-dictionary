### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Contraction of had, as an auxiliary verb.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; clitic; contraction

   **Example:** He’d done his best, but it hadn't been enough.

   **Example:** You’d seen it before anyone else had.

   **Example:** I’d better not.

   **Example:** They baby’d been crying all night.

   **Alternative form of:** [had](../380/had--e02a753f0c06.md); as an auxiliary verb

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Contraction of had, as a main verb, but not a phrasal verb.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; clitic; contraction

   **Example:** I'd no reason to doubt him.

   **Alternative form of:** [had](../380/had--e02a753f0c06.md); as a main verb; but not a phrasal verb

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Contraction of would or should.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; clitic; contraction

   **Example:** I’d like to help, but I have no time.

   **Example:** John’d prefer not to go out tonight.

   **Example:** If I knew his name, I'd tell you.

   **Source synonyms:** 'ld

   **Alternative form of:** [would](../318/would--c6323a211701.md); [should](../3e1/should--f86b1f62a855.md)

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Contraction of did.

   **Usage:** abbreviation; alt-of; clitic; colloquial; contraction

   **Example:** Hey, where’d everybody go? Why’d they take off?

   **Example:** Why the hell'd you do that?

   **Example:** y'all'dn't've

   **Alternative form of:** [did](../1d7/did--75ca3ca2ece2.md)
