## Prep\_phrase · Record 1

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Immediately when sighted.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** I recognized him on sight.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Immediately upon coming to one's attention.

   **Usage:** broadly

   **Example:** He dismissed the idea on sight.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

   **Usage:** broadly; slang
