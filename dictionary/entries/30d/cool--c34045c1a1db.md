### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Of a mildly low temperature.

   **Example:** I like cool weather the most 'cause it's not too hot to wear a jacket but I won't be too cold in my shorts.

   **Source synonyms:** chilly

   **Source antonyms:** lukewarm; tepid; warm

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Allowing or suggesting heat relief.

   **Example:** Linen has made cool and breathable clothing for millennia.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Of a color, in the range of violet to green.

   **Example:** If you have a reddish complexion, you should mainly wear cool colors.

   **Source antonyms:** warm

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Not showing emotion; calm and in control of oneself.

   **Example:** Be cool. There's no need to panic.

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a person

   **Source synonyms:** distant; phlegmatic; standoffish; unemotional

   **Source antonyms:** passionate

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Unenthusiastic; lukewarm; skeptical.

   **Example:** His proposals had a cool reception.

   **Source synonyms:** chilly; lukewarm; tepid

   **Source antonyms:** warm

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. Calmly audacious.

   **Usage:** obsolete

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Applied facetiously to a sum of money, commonly as if to give emphasis to the largeness of the amount.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. Knowing what to do and how to behave; behaving with effortless and enviable style and panache; considered popular by others.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a person

   **Source antonyms:** awkward; uncool

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

9. Fashionable; trendy; hip.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Source synonyms:** à la mode; fashionable; in fashion; modish; stylish; happening; hip; in; trendy

   **Source antonyms:** démodé; old hat; out; out of fashion

<a id="r1-s10"></a>

**Source sense R1.S10**

10. All right; acceptable; good.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** Is it cool if I sleep here tonight?

   **Source synonyms:** acceptable; all right; OK

   **Source antonyms:** not cricket (UK); not on; unacceptable

<a id="r1-s11"></a>

**Source sense R1.S11**

11. Very interesting or exciting.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** I think astronomy is really cool.

   **Source synonyms:** awesome; neat

<a id="r1-s12"></a>

**Source sense R1.S12**

12. Followed by with&#58; able to tolerate.

   **Usage:** informal

   **Example:** I'm completely cool with my girlfriend leaving me.

   **Source synonyms:** easy; fine; not bothered; not fussed

   **Source antonyms:** bothered; upset

<a id="r1-s13"></a>

**Source sense R1.S13**

   **Usage:** informal
