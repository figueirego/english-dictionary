**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To make a rude or obscene gesture (at somebody); particularly, to extend the middle finger.

   **Usage:** ambitransitive; colloquial; idiomatic

   **Example:** I accidentally bumped into him, and he flipped me the bird.

   **Source synonyms:** flick off; flip off; give the finger; give the bird
