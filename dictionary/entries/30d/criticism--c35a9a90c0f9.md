### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of criticising; a critical judgment passed or expressed.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Example:** The politician received a lot of public criticism for his controversial stance on the issue.

   **Example:** Criticism has been leveled at a number of prominent figures.

   **Source synonyms:** censure; dispraise; panning

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A critical observation or detailed examination and review.

   **Usage:** countable

   **Example:** The politician received several detailed criticisms of his stance on the issue.

   **Example:** I was stung by her harsh criticisms.

   **Source synonyms:** [critique](../076/critique--1dba1d0b2fb2.md); animadversion; stricture; animadversion; criticism; criticization; [critique](../076/critique--1dba1d0b2fb2.md); dressing-down (idiomatic); handbagging (UK; humorous); slam; stricture
