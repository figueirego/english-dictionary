### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To come to rest, usually briefly, with one of one's knees on the ground.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Of any player with the ball, to kneel and place the ball on the ground, thereby ending the play and indicating they do not intend to advance with the ball.

   **Usage:** Canadian

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** American-football; ball-games; football; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To cease performing customary activities or duties and kneel on one knee, sometimes as a form of protest.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see take, knee.

   **Example:** During the play he took a knee to the helmet.
