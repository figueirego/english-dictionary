### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. Any of various devices that use a powerful fan to clear an area of smoke.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Any of various devices that use a powerful fan to clear an area of smoke.
   1. A device to clear a gun turret on a naval ship of the smoke and gasses from discharging the gun.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Any of various devices that use a powerful fan to clear an area of smoke.
   1. A device to clear the engine of a coal-powered locomotive of the smoke and cinders resulting from burning coal.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. A small fire chamber with attached bellows used to blow smoke on a hive to pacify the bees before the beekeeper works on it.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** agriculture; beekeeping; business; lifestyle

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. A diagnostic tool that blows smoke through pipes or channels.

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. One who blows smoke; a liar or bullshit artist.

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. Used other than figuratively or idiomatically&#58; see smoke, blower; A person who blows smoke, especially one who blows smoke rings.
