<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The figure who introduced Islam, and the man to whom the Qur'an was revealed; considered a prophet by Muslims and Baháʼís.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Islam; lifestyle; religion

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The 47th sura (chapter) of the Qur'an.

   **Usage:** uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** Islam; lifestyle; religion

**Source sense R1.S3**

   **Usage:** countable
