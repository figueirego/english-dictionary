### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To check obsessively out of excessive caution or fear of a perceived danger.

   **Usage:** idiomatic

   **Example:** This is a cutthroat industry. You have to constantly look over your shoulder.
