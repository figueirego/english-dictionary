### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To survive, get by (with), or use whatever is available (due to lack of resources).

   **Usage:** idiomatic; informal; intransitive

   **Example:** There is barely enough money, so we will have to make do with what we have.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To put into action.

   **Usage:** informal; transitive

   **Example:** Brandon’s makin’ the grill do so we can get to eatin’.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To use for one's purpose something worn, defective, or intended for another purpose.

   **Usage:** informal; transitive
