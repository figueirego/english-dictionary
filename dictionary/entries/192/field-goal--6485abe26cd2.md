### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A kick of the ball through the uprights and over the crossbar (not after a touchdown). It scores 3 points.

   **Usage:** Canadian

   **Example:** He kicked a 33 yard field goal just before the half.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** American-football; ball-games; football; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A shot that is scored while the ball is in play (i.e. not a free throw).

   **Example:** He got six field goals in the first half, two from three-point land.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; basketball; games; hobbies; lifestyle; sports

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. A goal scored where a ball that is in play but on the ground is kicked through the uprights and over the crossbar.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** ball-games; games; hobbies; lifestyle; rugby; sports
