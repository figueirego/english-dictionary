### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To bite (something) very hard and usually for a purpose (like to hold it in one's mouth).

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** Biting down on my wallet, I had two hands free to open the door.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To clench with one's teeth.

   **Usage:** intransitive

   **Example:** He bit down to force himself not to say anything.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To repress or bite back an (emotion, impulse or reaction).

   **Usage:** figuratively; transitive

   **Example:** She bit down on the urge to strike the woman who had wronged her.
