### Senses

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. At no time; on no occasion; in no circumstance.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

   **Example:** I finally finished, and I never do wanna do it again.

   **Example:** We were at the same college, but happened never to meet.

   **Example:** I'll never not like you.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. Not at any other time; not on any other occasion; not previously.

   **Usage:** not-comparable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. Negative particle (used to negate verbs in the simple past tense; also used absolutely).

   **Usage:** colloquial; not-comparable

   **Example:** The police say I stole the car, but I never did it.

   **Example:** You said you were going to mow the lawn today. – I never!
