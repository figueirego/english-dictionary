### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To be ranked at (something), based on polling.

   **Usage:** UK; literally; transitive

   **Example:** The Tories are on twenty-five percent in that constituency.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To be performative or funny in a wearying manner.

   **Usage:** intransitive; literally

   **Example:** You don't have to be on all the time.

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To be responsible for (something).

   **Usage:** literally; transitive

   **Example:** That task is on you.

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To be up to; to be engaged in or scheduled to do imminently.

   **Usage:** transitive; African-American Vernacular

   **Example:** What are you on today?
