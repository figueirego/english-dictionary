### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. The act of abridging; reduction or deprivation

   **Usage:** US; countable; uncountable

   **Source synonyms:** diminution; lessening; shortening

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. The state of being abridged or lessened.

   **Usage:** US; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. An epitome or compend, as of a book; a shortened or abridged form; an abbreviation.

   **Usage:** US; countable; uncountable

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. That which abridges or cuts short; hence, an entertainment that makes the time pass quickly

   **Usage:** countable; obsolete; uncountable

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. Any of various brief statements of case law made before modern reporting of legal cases.

   **Usage:** countable; dated; uncountable

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law
