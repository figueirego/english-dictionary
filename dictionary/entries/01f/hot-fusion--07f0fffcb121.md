## Noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. nuclear fusion using the conventional method of very high temperatures; in contrast to cold fusion.

   **Usage:** uncountable

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A method of synthesizing superheavy elements, where the product nucleus has a relatively high excitation energy; typically this means that an actinide is bombarded with calcium-48.

   **Usage:** uncountable
