## Proper noun

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A former colony of the United Kingdom; a name used to refer to the area now called Zimbabwe, officially introduced in 1901. A self-governing colony of Britain from 1923, its government began to refer to itself simply as Rhodesia in 1964, when Northern Rhodesia became Zambia. The name Zimbabwe was adopted in 1980.

   **Usage:** historical
