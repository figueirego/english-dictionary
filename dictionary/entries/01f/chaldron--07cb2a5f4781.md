### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. An old English dry measure, containing four quarters. At London, 36 bushels heaped up, or its equivalent weight, and more than twice as much at Newcastle. Now used exclusively for coal and coke.

   **Usage:** archaic
