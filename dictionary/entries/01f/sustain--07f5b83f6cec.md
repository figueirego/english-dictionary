### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. To maintain, or keep in existence.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The professor had trouble sustaining students’ interest until the end of her lectures.

   **Example:** The city came under sustained attack by enemy forces.

   **Example:** Sam managed to sustain his erection for two straight hours.

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. To provide for or nourish.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** provisions to sustain an army

   **Source synonyms:** hold

<a id="r1-s3"></a>

**Source sense R1.S3**

3. To encourage or sanction (something).

   **Usage:** transitive

<a id="r1-s4"></a>

**Source sense R1.S4**

4. To experience or suffer (an injury, etc.).

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** The building sustained major damage in the earthquake.

<a id="r1-s5"></a>

**Source sense R1.S5**

5. To confirm, prove, or corroborate; to uphold.

   **Usage:** transitive

   **Example:** to sustain a charge, an accusation, or a proposition

<a id="r1-s6"></a>

**Source sense R1.S6**

6. To allow, accept, or admit (e.g. an objection or motion) as valid.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** law

   **Source context (verbatim):** of a judge

   **Source antonyms:** overrule

<a id="r1-s7"></a>

**Source sense R1.S7**

7. To keep from falling; to bear; to uphold; to support.

   **Example:** A foundation sustains the superstructure; an animal sustains a load; a rope sustains a weight.

<a id="r1-s8"></a>

**Source sense R1.S8**

8. To aid, comfort, or relieve; to vindicate.

<a id="r1-s9"></a>

**Source sense R1.S9**

   **Example:** I like how long that drum sustains.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** entertainment; lifestyle; music
