### Forms

### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A craftsperson or company who makes and/or maintains clocks.

   **Example:** There were hundreds of clockmakers in London during that era.

   **Source synonyms:** clocksmith

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. God in His role as the creator of the clockwork universe.

   **Usage:** capitalized; often

   **Example:** We have the great clockmaker to thank for these glorious sunrises and sunsets.

   **Source topics (extractor hierarchy; not curated):** human-sciences; lifestyle; philosophy; religion; sciences

   **Source synonyms:** watchmaker
