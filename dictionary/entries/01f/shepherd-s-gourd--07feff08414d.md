### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A traditional, often hourglass-shaped container for carrying water, originally made from a hollowed-out, dried gourd; later copied in clay and ceramic, featuring a wide neck for tying a rope, serving as an iconic symbol for travelers, especially pilgrims on the Way of Saint James in Spain and France.

   **Source synonyms:** pilgrim's gourd; shepherd's jug; pumpkin jug; pilgrim's barrel; pilgrim's pumpkin
