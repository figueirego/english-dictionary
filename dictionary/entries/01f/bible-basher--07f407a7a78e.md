### Senses

<a id="r1-s1"></a>

**Source sense R1.S1**

1. A fundamentalist Christian preacher, or other fervent Christian, who is seen to take every opportunity to talk about Christianity and attempt to convert those around them.

   **Usage:** derogatory

   **Example:** I don't ever go to my local church any more&#58; it's become filled with stupid, fuddy-duddy Bible bashers.

   **Source synonyms:** Bible thumper (US); Bible pounder; Bible puncher; God botherer (UK); God squad (UK; US); Bible beater

<a id="r1-s2"></a>

**Source sense R1.S2**

2. A person who finds fault with the Bible, Christianity, or Christian teaching.

   **Source synonyms:** anti-Christian
